## Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order, 1988 {#satellites-and-satellite-subsystems-remission-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-532
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1988-10-18

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Satellites and
Satellite Subsystems for Testing and on Goods for Use in the Manufacture
of Satellites and Satellite Subsystems

P.C. 1988-2374 1988-10-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, is pleased hereby, on
the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of customs duties on satellites and
satellite subsystems for testing and on goods for use in the manufacture
of satellites and satellite subsystems](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-532).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1987, c. 49
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914032 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-532).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914034 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on satellites and satellite
subsystems for testing and on goods for use in the manufacture of
satellites and satellite subsystems, imported on or after January 1,
1988.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914036 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 2 is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made
to the Minister of National Revenue within three years of the date of
importation.
