## Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism) the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-industry-to-the-minister-of-state-small-business-and-tourism-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-canadian-tourism-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-82
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Transferring from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of
State (Small Business and Tourism) the Powers, Duties and Functions
Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act

P.C. 2025-493 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers to the Minister of State (Small Business and Tourism) the
powers, duties and functions under the [Canadian Tourism Commission
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746825003){#fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746825003-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to the Minister of Industry by Order in Council
P.C. 2025-420 of March 14, 2025[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746842331){#fn_81000-3-2908_hq_1746842331-ID0EBEAA}.
