## [Publication on the Internet]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704883 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The information concerning
foreign companies that is required to be published quarterly in the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) pursuant to section 585 of
the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) may, instead of being
published in that way, be published on the Web site of the Office of the
Superintendent of Financial Institutions.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704885 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
