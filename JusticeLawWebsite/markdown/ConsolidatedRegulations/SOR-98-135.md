## Order Respecting the Remission of Anti-Dumping Duties on Vitreous Type I Cold-Rolled Steel {#order-respecting-the-remission-of-anti-dumping-duties-on-vitreous-type-i-cold-rolled-steel .Title-of-Act}

:::: section

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-98-135_e_hq_5562){#fn_SOR-98-135_e_hq_5562-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed Order Respecting the Remission of Anti-Dumping
Duties on Vitreous Type I Cold-Rolled Steel.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018202 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the anti-dumping duties paid or payable
under the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) on imports of
vitreous type I cold-rolled steel produced by the open coil anneal
process to cover the period commencing on June 1, 1997 and ending on
October 31, 1997.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018204 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
pursuant to section 1 on condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the day on which this Order
  comes into force; and,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as National Revenue
  may require that the subject steel is vitreous type I cold-rolled
  steel produced using the open coil anneal process.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018208 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on February 26, 1998.
