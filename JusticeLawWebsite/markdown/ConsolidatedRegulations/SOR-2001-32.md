## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672614 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Defence Production
    Act](/eng/acts/D-1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international student]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual
    who is authorized by a study permit or by the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) to
    engage in studies in Canada and who is not an officer, director or
    employee of a person registered under these Regulations. ([étudiant
    étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 2(1) of the Immigration Act. ([résident
    permanent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a partnership or other
    business enterprise. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[temporary worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer, director
    or employee of a person registered under these Regulations who is
    not

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen ordinarily resident in Canada;
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident ordinarily resident in
      Canada. ([travailleur temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[visitor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[visitor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual --- other than
    an international student --- who is not an officer, director or
    employee of a person registered under these Regulations and who is
    not

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen ordinarily resident in Canada;
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident ordinarily resident in
      Canada. ([visiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-201, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672629 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** Part 2 of the Act does
not apply to members of the following classes of persons who act in good
faith in the course of their duties and employment:

- [a)]{.lawlabel} public officers as defined in subsection 117.07(2) of
  the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} elected or appointed officials of the federal or a
  provincial government; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} members of a visiting force as defined in section 2
  of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2).

- SOR/2004-132, s. 1

### [Eligibility for Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672639 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person is eligible to
apply for registration if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, they carry on business
  in Canada, give their consent to a security assessment and are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen ordinarily resident in Canada,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident ordinarily resident in
    Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, partnership or any
  other business enterprise, it is either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} incorporated under federal or provincial law, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} authorized by federal or provincial law to carry
    on business in Canada.

### [Applications for Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672647 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person who is eligible
may apply for registration by sending to the Minister on a form supplied
by the Minister an application that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant\'s legal name, business name, address,
  telephone numbers for each place of business in Canada and, if
  applicable, electronic mail and facsimile numbers for each place;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the legal status of the applicant\'s
  business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, residential address and position of each
  director, officer, partner or proprietor of the business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of any owner of 20% or more of
  the outstanding voting shares or interests of the business and the
  percentage that each owns;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, date of birth, position, telephone number
  and, if applicable, facsimile number and email address of the
  individual who is proposed to be appointed as the designated official;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-201, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the controlled goods that the
  applicant intends to examine, possess or transfer and the addresses of
  the locations where those goods will be kept;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the addresses of the locations where the records
  required by these Regulations will be kept; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement signed and dated by an individual
  authorized for that purpose, stating that the information contained in
  the application is accurate and complete.

### [Factor for Consideration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672666 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In deciding whether to
register an applicant, the Minister shall assess, based on the security
assessments referred to in section 3.1 and any other information
obtained in respect of the applicant, the extent to which the applicant
poses a risk of transferring a controlled good to a person who is
neither registered nor exempt from registration.

### [Period of Validity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672678 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The registration of a
person is valid for a period not exceeding five years from the date of
approval by the Minister.

### [Non-assignability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672680 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The registration of a
person is not assignable.

### [Conditions of Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672688 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every registration of a
person is subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the person keep and maintain, during the period
  of registration and for a period of five years after the day on which
  the person ceases to be registered, records that contain

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of any controlled goods received by
    the person, the date of their receipt and an identification of the
    person from whom they were transferred,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any controlled goods transferred
    by the person, the date of their transfer and the identity and
    address of the person to whom they were transferred, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the manner and date of
    disposition of the controlled goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the person keep records of the most recent
  security assessment in respect of each of their officers, directors,
  employees, temporary workers, international students and visitors who
  examine, possess or transfer controlled goods and maintain those
  records --- as well as supporting documentation --- for a period of
  two years after the day on which the individual in question ceases to
  act in that capacity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the person keep a copy of the evidence referred
  to in subsection 16(2) for a period of two years after the day on
  which the individual who is exempt ceases to have access to the
  controlled goods of the registered person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the person meets the requirements set out in
  section 11 and subsection 12(1);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the person establish and implement a security
  plan in respect of each place of business in Canada where controlled
  goods are kept that contains written measures that set out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the procedures used by the person to control the
    examination, possession and transfer of controlled goods,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for reporting and investigating
    security breaches in relation to controlled goods,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the description of the responsibilities of the
    person\'s security organization and the identity of individuals who
    are responsible for the security of controlled goods, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the contents of security briefings and training
    programs given to visitors, officers, directors, employees and
    temporary workers, as the case may be;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} that the person provide training programs in respect
  of the secure handling of controlled goods for officers, directors,
  employees and temporary workers who are authorized to possess or
  examine those goods;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} that the person provide briefings in respect of the
  secure handling of controlled goods by visitors who are authorized to
  examine those goods;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} that the person advise the Minister of any actual or
  potential security breach in relation to controlled goods within three
  days after the day on which they discover the breach;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the person make available, at any reasonable
  time, to the Minister the records referred to in paragraph (a), the
  records and documents referred to in paragraph (b) and the copy of the
  evidence referred to in paragraph (c); and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} that the person submit to the Minister, every six
  months, the name of each individual in respect of whom the designated
  official conducted a security assessment during the previous six
  months, as well as the individual's date of birth and an indication of
  the extent to which they were authorized to access controlled goods.

### [Duties of Designated Officials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672721 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every registered person
shall ensure that the designated official

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each officer, director and employee who
  is not a temporary worker of the registered person and who requires in
  the course of their duties access to controlled goods,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} conducts, at least once every five years and with
    the consent of the individual concerned, including their consent to
    the disclosure of their personal information to --- and its use by
    --- Canadian government entities and credit reporting agencies for
    the purpose of the assessment, a security assessment in accordance
    with section 15,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} determines, on the basis of the security
    assessment, the extent to which the individual concerned poses a
    risk of transferring a controlled good to a person who is neither
    registered nor exempt from registration,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the designated official is of the opinion that
    the individual concerned poses a high risk, requests that the
    Minister conduct his or her own security assessment in respect of
    the individual concerned and provides to the Minister, for the
    purpose of carrying out that assessment, all information and
    evidence obtained by the designated official under subsections
    15(2), (4) and (5),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} considers any recommendation provided by the
    Minister under subsection 15.1(2), and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} decides the extent to which the registered person
    ought to authorize the individual concerned to examine, possess or
    transfer controlled goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} verifies the information provided to them by
  temporary workers, international students and visitors for the purpose
  of applications for exemption submitted under section 18; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} obtains and maintains any certification required by
  the Minister.

## [Security Assessments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672739 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall conduct a security assessment in respect of all
  proposed designated officials and all individuals referred to in
  paragraphs 3(d) and (i), and the designated official shall conduct a
  security assessment in respect of all officers, directors and
  employees referred to in paragraph 13(a), having regard to the
  information provided under subsection (4).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In conducting the security assessment, the Minister
  may also have regard to other information provided by any person and
  the designated official may have regard to other information provided
  by the Minister if the Minister or the designated official, as the
  case may be, has reasonable grounds to believe that the information is
  necessary to determine the extent of the risk referred to in
  subsection (3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the security assessment is to
  determine the extent to which the individual being assessed poses a
  risk of transferring a controlled good to a person who is neither
  registered nor exempt from registration.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The individual who is the subject of the security
  assessment shall provide the Minister or the designated official, as
  the case may be, with the following information and, on request, any
  additional information or evidence that the Minister or the designated
  official requires to verify it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name and any previous names;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all citizenships and any permanent resident status;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} personal references; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description, in respect of the five-year period
    immediately before the day on which the individual consents to the
    assessment, of their

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} criminal history,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} places of residence,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} education and employment history,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} financial history,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} history of travel outside of Canada and the
      United States, including any denial of entry into another country,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} significant personal and professional
      associations and relationships that could affect the extent to
      which the individual poses a risk of transferring a controlled
      good to a person who is neither registered nor exempt from
      registration, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} any security clearance held, denied, suspended
      or revoked.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An individual who is or has been the subject of a
  security assessment shall advise the Minister or the designated
  official, as the case may be, of any change concerning their criminal
  history within five business days after the day on which the change
  occurs.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or the designated official, as the case
  may be, shall reconduct a security assessment if new information is
  received or if there are other reasonable grounds for doing so.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672766 .Part}

### [Class of Exempt Individuals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672767 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  individual is exempt from registration if they are a director, an
  officer or an employee of a person registered to access controlled
  goods under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations, Title 22,
  Parts 120-130 of the Code of Federal Regulations (United States).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual is exempt from registration from the
  day on which they provide to the person registered under these
  Regulations from whom they will obtain access to the controlled goods

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the individual\'s status as a director,
    an officer or an employee of the person registered under the
    regulations referred to in subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the registration and eligibility of
    that person under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the eligibility of the individual under
    the International Traffic in Arms Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual is also exempt from registration if
  they are an officer, employee or elected or appointed official of the
  United States federal government or of a state or territorial
  government of the United States and act in good faith in the course of
  their duties and employment.

- SOR/2004-132, s. 2

### [Applications for Exemption from Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672781 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A registered person may
apply to exempt a temporary worker, international student or visitor
from registration by sending to the Minister, on a form supplied by the
Minister, an application that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the registered person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, any previous names, date of birth and
  citizenships of the temporary worker, international student or
  visitor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a visitor, their address and the name
  and address of their employer, if applicable;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description, including the country of origin if
  other than Canada, of the controlled goods that the temporary worker,
  the international student or the visitor will examine, possess or
  transfer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the expected duration of the temporary worker's
  employment or of the international student's placement with the
  registered person or the expected duration and purpose of the visit;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement signed and dated by the designated
  official, stating that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual for whom the exemption is being
    sought meets the requirements of the definition of temporary worker,
    international student or visitor, as the case may be,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a temporary worker or international
    student, the designated official has contacted their personal
    references, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in and accompanying the
    application is accurate and complete.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-201, s. 12
:::

  credit reporting agencies for the purpose of the security assessment,
  as well as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} copies of all identity documents issued to them by
    the Government of Canada or any province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that they are authorized to work or study
    in Canada, as the case may be, and, in the case of an international
    student, a document from the academic institution at which they are
    authorized to study indicating that the work they are doing for the
    registered person is integral to those studies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} personal references;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description, in respect of the five-year period
    immediately before the day on which they consent to the assessment,
    of their

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} criminal history,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} places of residence,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} education and employment history, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} history of international travel outside of
      Canada and the United States, including any denial of entry into
      another country; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the original results of a country-wide criminal
    record check conducted by the relevant authorities in every country
    other than Canada in which they resided during the period referred
    to in paragraph (d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The temporary worker and the international student
  shall, on request, provide the designated official with any additional
  information or evidence that the designated official requires to
  verify the information provided under subsection (2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-201, s. 12

### [Security Assessments for Temporary Workers, International Students and Visitors]{.HTitleText2} {#h-672809 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall conduct a security assessment in respect of all
  temporary workers, international students and visitors in respect of
  whom an application for exemption from registration has been made
  under section 18, having regard to the information provided under
  section 18.1 and any other information that the Minister has
  reasonable grounds to believe is necessary to make a decision under
  section 20.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the security assessment is to
  determine the extent to which the individual being assessed poses a
  risk of transferring a controlled good to a person who is neither
  registered nor exempt from registration.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The registered person shall advise the Minister of
  any change in any of the information contained in or accompanying an
  application for exemption within five business days after the day on
  which the change occurs.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall reconduct a security assessment if
  new information is received or if there are other reasonable grounds
  for doing so.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-201, s. 12

## [Suspension and Revocation of Registrations and Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672837 .Part}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that a registered person or
  an exempt individual poses an undue risk of transferring a controlled
  good to a person who is neither registered nor exempt from
  registration, the Minister shall suspend the registration or the
  exemption.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall revoke the registration or the
  exemption if he or she determines that the registered person or the
  exempt individual poses an undue risk of transferring a controlled
  good to a person who is neither registered nor exempt from
  registration and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a material fact was, with the intent to mislead,
    deliberately omitted from or misstated in the application for
    registration or exemption or in connection with a security
    assessment referred to in section 3.1, 12 or 19; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registered person or the exempt individual is
    bankrupt.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall reinstate the registration or the
  exemption if, within 30 days after the date of the notice of
  suspension or revocation, the registered person makes representations
  to the Minister that satisfy him or her

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that there are no longer grounds for the
    suspension; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the revocation was unfounded.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the registered person does not, in the case of a
  suspension, meet the requirements of subsection (3), the Minister
  shall revoke the registration or the exemption.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall provide to the registered person
  notice with reasons of any decision to suspend or to revoke a
  registration or exemption, or not to reinstate a revoked registration
  or exemption despite representations being made, as well as notice of
  any decision to reinstate a registration or exemption.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The registered person shall, within one business day
  after the day on which they receive a notice from the Minister
  concerning the exemption of a temporary worker, international student
  or visitor, provide to that individual a copy of the notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 41, s. 39
- SOR/2016-201, s. 16
