## Order Designating Saskatchewan for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-saskatchewan-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-204
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2011-09-30

Order Designating Saskatchewan for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest
Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2011-1108 2011-09-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Saskatchewan has legislative measures that protect recipients of
payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing
under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Saskatchewan has
requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the
purposes of section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EEABA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBABA};

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Saskatchewan for the
Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal
Code](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-204).

## [Province Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774255 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Saskatchewan is designated
for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-774257 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_star){#fn_star-ID0EBEA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m. Central Time
or --- in that part of the province in the Mountain Time zone ---
Mountain Time, on the first day on which the following are all in force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 5, 18 and 19 of The Payday Loans Act,
  S.S. 2007, c. P-4.3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 14 of The Payday Loans Regulations, R.R.S.
  c. P-4.3 Reg. 1.
