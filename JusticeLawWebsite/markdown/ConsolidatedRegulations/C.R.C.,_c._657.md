## Canadian Egg Pricing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#canadian-egg-pricing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 657
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Regulations Respecting the Pricing and Marketing in the Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Eggs Produced in Canada
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559657 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Egg Pricing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._657).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559659 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Egg Marketing
    Agency; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means egg of a hen;
    ([oeuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hen of any class of the domestic
    chicken belonging to the species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}. ([poule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559664 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and export trade.

## [Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559666 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall sell or offer to sell eggs at less than the minimum price
  from time to time determined by the Agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall buy eggs at less than the minimum
  price from time to time determined by the Agency.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/80-891, s.
1\]]{.Repealed}
