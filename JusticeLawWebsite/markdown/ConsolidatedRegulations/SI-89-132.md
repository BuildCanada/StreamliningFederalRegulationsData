## Aluminerie de Bécancour Remission Order {#aluminerie-de-bécancour-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-132
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-05-24

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Aluminum Smelting
Equipment of Aluminerie de Bécancour Inc.

P.C. 1989-796 1989-05-04
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 23 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed [Order respecting the
remission of customs duties on aluminum smelting equipment of Aluminerie
de Bécancour Inc.](/eng/regulations/SI-89-132)
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642068 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Aluminerie de Bécancour Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-89-132).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642070 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[purchase order number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[purchase order number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number that
    is assigned by Aluminerie de Bécancour Inc. in respect of the
    purchase of equipment for its smelter in Bécancour, Quebec. ([numéro
    de bon de commande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642073 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted to Aluminerie de Bécancour Inc. of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) by Aluminerie de Bécancour Inc. on smelting
equipment set out in an item of the schedule.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642075 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted by
section 3 is on condition that a claim for remission is made to the
Minister of National Revenue by Aluminerie de Bécancour Inc. on or
before December 31, 1989.
