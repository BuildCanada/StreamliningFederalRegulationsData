## St. Andrews Airport Zoning Regulations {#st.-andrews-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-948
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1981-11-20

Regulations Respecting Zoning at St. Andrews Airport

P.C. 1981-3283 1981-11-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 6 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the revocation by the
Minister of Transport of the [St. Andrews Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-948), C.R.C., c. 110, and to
approve the annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at St. Andrews
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-948) made by the Minister of Transport,
in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872714 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [St. Andrews Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-948).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872716 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the St. Andrews Airport, in
      the Rural Municipality of St. Andrews, in the Province of
      Manitoba; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part III of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the assigned
  elevation of the airport reference point is 757 feet above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872727 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport that consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of an approach
  surface that extends beyond

the outer limits described in Part II of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872732 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point, any

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} approach surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transitional surface.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872737 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or the occupier of the land on which that
object is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872739 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a point at the intersection of a line
drawn parallel to and distant 500 feet measured southwesterly at right
angles from the centre line of runway 12R-30L and a line drawn parallel
to and distant 500 feet measured northwesterly at right angles from the
centre line of runway 04-22.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown outlined in blue on Department of Transport
Plan E.1539, are surfaces abutting each end of the strips associated
with the runways designated 04-22, 12R-30L, 18R-36L, 12L-30R and
18L-36R, and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 04,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 22,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 12R,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 30L,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 18R,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 36L,

consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of one foot measured
vertically to 50 feet measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
the strip, 200 feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end
of the strip, and 10,000 feet measured horizontally from the end of the
strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 2,000 feet
from the projected centre line, and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 12L,

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 30R,

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 18L,

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 36R,

consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of one foot measured
vertically to 40 feet measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
the strip, 207.5 feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end
of the strip, and 8,300 feet measured horizontally from the end of the
strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 955 feet
from the projected centre line.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown outlined in yellow on Department of Transport
Plan E.1539, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 150 feet above the assigned
elevation of the airport reference point except where that common plane
is less than 30 feet above the surface of the ground, the imaginary
surface is located at 30 feet above the surface of the ground.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Department of Transport Plan E.1539, are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 04-22 is 1,000 feet
  in width, 500 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 5,500 feet in length;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12R-30L is
  1,000 feet in width, 500 feet being on each side of the centre line of
  the runway and 3,400 feet in length;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 18R-36L is
  1,000 feet in width, 500 feet being on each side of the centre line of
  the runway and 5,100 feet in length;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12L-30R is 250 feet
  in width, 125 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 3,200 feet in length;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 18L-36R is 250 feet
  in width, 125 feet being on each side of the centre line of the runway
  and 3,150 feet in length.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown outlined in red on Department of
Transport Plan E.1539,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} associated with runways 04-22, 12R-30L and 18R-36L is
  a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of one
  foot measured vertically to seven feet measured horizontally at right
  angles to the centre line and centre line produced of each strip and
  extending upward from the lateral limits of each strip and its
  approach surface to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} associated with runways 12L-30R and 18L-36R is a
  surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of one foot
  measured vertically to five feet measured horizontally at right angles
  to the centre line and centre line produced of each strip and
  extending upward from the lateral limits of each strip and its
  approach surface to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
