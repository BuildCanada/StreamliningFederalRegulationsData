## Divestiture of Service Transitional Coverage Regulations {#divestiture-of-service-transitional-coverage-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-446
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 1998-08-28

Divestiture of Service Transitional Coverage Regulations

T.B. 826504 1998-08-25

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(v.5)[[Footnote

7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-446).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024069 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person or body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person or body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or body to
    whom the administration of a service is transferred or otherwise
    divested under subsection 40.1(1) of the Act.
    ([cessionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Maximum Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024073 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 40.1(2)(a) of the Act, the maximum period during which a
person or body may form part of the Public Service is three years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-213, s. 1
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024077 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** As a condition of remaining
part of the Public Service in accordance with a direction of Treasury
Board made under paragraph 40.1(2)(a) of the Act, a person or body shall
make monthly payments into the Superannuation Account or the Public
Service Pension Fund of the amounts determined in accordance with
section 9 of the [Public Service Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-213, s. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024081 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on August 28, 1998.
