::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556698 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Protection of Privacy
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._440).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556700 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 178.23(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the
Attorney General of a province who gave a notice required to be given by
that subsection, or the Solicitor General of Canada where the notice was
given by him, shall certify to the court that issued the authorization
that such notice was given by filing with a judge of the court a
certificate signed by the person who gave the notice specifying

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person who was the object
  of the interception;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the authorization and any renewal
  thereof expired;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if any delay for the giving of notice was granted
  under section 178.23 or subsection 178.12(3) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the period of such delay; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date, place and method of the giving of the
  notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-859, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/81-859, s.
2\]]{.Repealed}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A certificate filed
pursuant to section 2 shall be treated as a confidential document, shall
be placed in a packet, sealed by the judge with whom it is filed and
kept with the packet sealed pursuant to section 178.14 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46) that relates to the authorization to which the
certificate relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-859, s. 3
:::
