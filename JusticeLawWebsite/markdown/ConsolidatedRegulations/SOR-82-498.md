## Small Manufacturers or Producers Exemption Regulations {#small-manufacturers-or-producers-exemption-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-498
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1982-05-13

Regulations Exempting Certain Classes of Small Manufacturers or
Producers From Payment of Consumption or Sales Tax

P.C. 1982-1419 1982-05-13
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant
to subsection 31(2) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations exempting certain classes of
small manufacturers or producers from payment of consumption or sales
tax](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-498).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873888 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Small Manufacturers or Producers Exemption
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-498).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873890 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[motorhome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-propelled recreational
camping and travel vehicle with built-in kitchen and sleeping
facilities. ([roulotte motorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-866, s. 1
:::

## [Exemption for Small Manufacturers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873894 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following classes of small manufacturers or producers are exempt from
  payment of consumption or sales tax on goods manufactured or produced
  by them in the operations referred to in this section:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufacturers, other than those who elect to
    operate under a licence, who sell goods of their own manufacture
    that are otherwise subject to consumption or sales tax or who
    manufacture goods for their own use that are otherwise subject to
    consumption or sales tax, if the value of such goods sold or
    manufactured for their own use does not exceed \$50,000 per calendar
    year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} manufacturers in any of the following classes who
    sell goods of their own manufacture that are manufactured to the
    requirements of each individual customer and that are otherwise
    subject to consumption or sales tax:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} blacksmiths,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} opticians,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} plumbers,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} cider millers who produce cider for a customer
      from the customer's apples,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} manufacturers of show cards and signs that are

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} made of cardboard, cotton or paper or other
        non-durable materials, and,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} drawn or lettered by hand, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} portrait photographers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} barristers, solicitors and notaries engaged in the
    practice of law, accountants engaged in the practice of accountancy
    and engineers engaged in the practice of engineering to the extent
    that they manufacture printed matter in connection with the
    rendering of their professional services to the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} persons who manufacture original engineering and
    planning design models of ships, buildings, industrial plants or
    construction layouts, excluding persons who manufacture merchandise
    display models or replica models;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} persons who manufacture arrangements of artificial
    or natural flowers or foliage in a retail establishment for the
    purpose of sale in such retail establishment directly and
    exclusively to users;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} persons who manufacture field coils solely for
    their own use as parts in the repair of motors and transformers for
    their customers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/85-619, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} operators of radio and television stations that
    manufacture discs, film and slides, magnetic tapes, photographs and
    props and sets;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} picture framers who frame pictures, photographs,
    diplomas or similar goods that are the property of their customers,
    using frames that they manufacture themselves or purchase from
    others for such operations;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} persons engaged in microfilming operations;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} theatre arts groups who manufacture props and sets
    solely for their own use and not for resale;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} persons who manufacture potting soil or potting
    soil mixtures;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} persons who design and produce goods, from basic
    materials and through the skillful use of their hands and hand tools
    only, but not including goods that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are made wholly or in part of precious metals,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are made wholly or in part of precious or
      semi-precious stones, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are made in a repetitive manner through the use
      of jigs, templates, moulds, dies or other similar devices, except
      that such devices may be used to produce the original or first
      unit;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} persons who produce steam other than those
    manufacturers or producers who produce steam for their own use in
    the manufacture or production of goods;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} persons who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} purchase or import new motor vehicles designed
      for highway use, or the chassis therefor, other than vehicles
      referred to in section 1 of Part XVII of Schedule III to the Act,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have the vehicles or chassis further
      manufactured on their behalf

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} by licensed manufacturers who are liable to pay
        sales tax on the charge made for the further manufacture and any
        applicable excise tax, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for their own use or for sale or lease to
        consumers or users; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} persons described in the definition of
    [[manufacturer or producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    paragraph 2(1)(g) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sell directly and exclusively to consumers or
      users, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rent, lease or take for their own use

    motorhomes that they have imported into Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When the value of the sales of a manufacturer or
  producer who is exempt from obtaining a licence under paragraph (1)(a)
  exceeds \$50,000 during any calendar year, the exemption granted by
  subsection (1) ceases to apply.
