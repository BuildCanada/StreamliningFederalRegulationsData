## Members of Committees Regulations {#members-of-committees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-71
:::

[SPECIAL IMPORT MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-15)

Registration 1988-12-30

Regulations Respecting the Candidates for Selection to, and the Members
of, Extraordinary Challenge Committees under Chapter Nineteen of the
Free Trade Agreement Between Canada and the United States Entered into
Between the Government of Canada and the Government of the United States
and Signed on January 2, 1988

P.C. 1988-2938 1988-12-30
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 1 of Annex 1901.2 and paragraph 1 of
Annex 1904.13 to Chapter Nineteen of the Free Trade Agreement between
Canada and the United States entered into between the Government of
Canada and the Government of the United States and signed on January 2,
1988, the Parties to the Agreement have consulted in developing a roster
of judges or former judges to serve as members of extraordinary
challenge committees;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister for International Trade and the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 77.27(c)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-89-71_e_hq_6307){#fn_SOR-89-71_e_hq_6307-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the candidates for selection
to, and the members of, extraordinary challenge committees under Chapter
Nineteen of the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the United
States entered into between the Government of Canada and the Government
of the United States and signed on January 2,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-71).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920820 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Members of Committees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-71).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920822 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[committee roster]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the roster established by
section 3. (*liste de candidats pour les comités*)

## [Committee Roster]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920824 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of carrying out and giving effect to paragraph 1 of Annex
  1904.13 to Chapter Nineteen of the Free Trade Agreement, the committee
  roster set out in the schedule is established.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The candidates named in the committee roster are
  selected to be on the roster

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a period of three years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during good behaviour.

## [Selection of Committee Candidates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920830 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where under paragraph 1 of
Annex 1904.13 to Chapter Nineteen of the Free Trade Agreement a
candidate is to be selected to serve as a member of a committee, the
candidate shall be selected from the committee roster by a board that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is chaired by the Minister; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} consists of the Minister of Finance and such other
  ministers of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada as may be designated
  from time to time by the Prime Minister.

## [Appointment of Members of Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920834 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a candidate has been
selected in accordance with paragraph 1 of Annex 1904.13 to Chapter
Nineteen of the Free Trade Agreement to serve as a member of a committee
to review the decision of a panel in respect of a definitive decision,
the Minister shall appoint the candidate as a member of the committee.
