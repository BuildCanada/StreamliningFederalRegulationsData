[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

NAFTA Marking Determination, Re-determination and Further
Re-determination Regulations

P.C. 1997-2021 1997-12-29
::::

::: {.section .order}
Whereas the proposed NAFTA Marking Determination, Re-determination and
Further Re-determination Regulations give effect, in part, to a public
announcement made on October 27, 1997 and are therefore, by virtue of
paragraph 164(4)(a.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EEABA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EBABA}, not required
to be published under subsection 164(3) of that Act;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsections 57.01(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EKFAA} and
61(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EIFAA} and
paragraph 164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e){#footnotee_e-ID0EGFAA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDFAA}, hereby makes
the annexed NAFTA Marking Determination, Re-determination and Further
Re-determination Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024801 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
(*Loi*)

## [Manner of Making a Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024803 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An officer who is
designated by the Minister for the purposes of subsection 57.01(1) of
the Act shall determine whether goods imported from a NAFTA country have
been marked in the manner referred to in section 35.01 of the Act by
reviewing, analyzing or examining any of the following material:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} advance rulings given in respect of the goods
  pursuant to section 43.1 of the Act, or by the customs administration
  of a NAFTA country other than Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determinations, re-determinations or further
  re-determinations, made under the Act, in respect of marking, tariff
  classification or origin of goods imported from a NAFTA country;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} decisions made by the Canadian International Trade
  Tribunal, the Federal Court or the Supreme Court of Canada in respect
  of goods imported from a NAFTA country;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} reports prepared by an officer at the time the goods
  were examined in accordance with subsection 99(1) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} samples of the goods taken by an officer in
  accordance with subsection 99(1) of the Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} information provided by the person who accounted for
  the goods pursuant to section 32 of the Act; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other material provided by the importer, exporter
  or producer of the goods in respect of the marking of the goods.

## [Class of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024812 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The officer referred to in
section 2 shall give notice of the marking determination made in respect
of goods imported from a NAFTA country to the importer, exporter and
producer of those goods.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 61(2) of the Act, the Deputy Minister shall give notice of
any re-determination or further re-determination of a marking
determination made pursuant to paragraph 61(1)(b) of the Act in respect
of goods imported from a NAFTA country to the importer, exporter and
producer of those goods.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024815 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024817 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
