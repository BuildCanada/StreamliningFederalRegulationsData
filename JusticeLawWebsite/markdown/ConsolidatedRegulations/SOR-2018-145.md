## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851200 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions --- Regulations

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approved cultivar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approved cultivar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any variety of
      industrial hemp set out in the List of Approved Cultivars,
      published by the Government of Canada on its website, as amended
      from time to time. ([cultivar approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[competent laboratory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent laboratory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a laboratory
      that is owned or operated by a holder of a licence for analytical
      testing issued under subsection 62(1) of the Act, or a laboratory
      outside Canada that is recognized as a qualified laboratory, for
      the application of the United Nations' Single Convention on
      Narcotic Drugs, 1961, as amended from time to time, by the
      competent authorities of the country in which it is
      located. ([laboratoire compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the obtaining of
      industrial hemp by cultivating it. ([culture]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([bureau
      de douane]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[customs officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[customs officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by the definition [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
      2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([agent des
      douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under
      subsection 62(1) of the Act that authorizes the exportation of
      seed or grain. ([permis d'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to industrial hemp,
      means the whole plant, flowering heads, leaves, branches, seed,
      grain or fibre, as the case may be. ([forme]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[grain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means achene of industrial hemp that
      is not represented, sold or used to grow a
      plant. ([grain]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under
      subsection 62(1) of the Act that authorizes the importation of
      seed or grain. ([permis d'importation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under
      subsection 62(1) of the Act in relation to industrial
      hemp. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[partnership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[partnership]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an
      incorporated entity. ([société de personnes]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"} )

  [[pedigreed status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pedigreed status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 2(2) of the [Seeds
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1400). ([qualité
      Généalogique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plant breeder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plant breeder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is
      recognized by the Canadian Seed Growers' Association as a plant
      breeder, under the Canadian Regulations and Procedures for Breeder
      Seed Crop Production, published by the Canadian Seed Growers'
      Association, as amended from time to time. ([sélectionneur de
      plantes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[propagation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[propagation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the obtaining of
      industrial hemp by propagating it for the purpose of developing a
      new variety. ([multiplication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means achene of industrial hemp that
      is represented, sold or used to grow a
      plant. ([graine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[THC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[THC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinol. ([THC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[variety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[variety]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(2) of the [Seeds
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1400). ([variété]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition --- Act and Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act and these Regulations,
  [[industrial hemp]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cannabis plant --- or
  any part of that plant --- in which the concentration of THC is 0.3%
  w/w or less in the flowering heads and leaves.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of THC concentration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The determination of the THC concentration must take
  into account the potential to convert delta-9-tetrahydrocannabinolic
  acid into THC.

## [Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851236 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized activities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A holder
  of a licence is authorized to conduct any of the following activities
  that are authorized by the licence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to sell industrial hemp;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to import or export seed or grain;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to cultivate industrial hemp;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a plant breeder, to propagate
    industrial hemp;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to possess seed or grain for the purposes of
    cleaning it;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} to possess grain for the purpose of processing it;
    or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to obtain seed by preparing it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence that authorizes an activity
  referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(c) to (g) is also authorized to
  offer to conduct that activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ancillary activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence is authorized, to the extent
  necessary to conduct the activity authorized by the licence, to
  possess industrial hemp, to obtain or offer to obtain industrial hemp
  by harvesting it, and to transfer, transport, send or deliver
  industrial hemp.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence and the person referred to in
  subsections 71(1) to (3) of the Act are exempt from the application of
  Subdivisions A to D of Division 2 of Part 1 of the Act in relation to
  the conduct of the activity authorized by the licence or the conduct
  of the ancillary activity authorized under subsection (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence --- content

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The licence must set out
the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and mailing address of the holder of the
  licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the activity that is authorized, the address of each
  place, with the exception of the cultivation site, where the activity
  is authorized, and the form of industrial hemp authorized for each
  activity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licence that authorizes propagation,
  the variety of industrial hemp or the name or number of the germplasm
  of industrial hemp that will be used in the propagation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the licence; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry of the licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence amendment

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence must
submit an application for an amendment to the licence if they propose to
make any of the following changes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to the mailing address of the holder of the
  licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licence that authorizes propagation,
  a change to the variety of industrial hemp or the name or number of
  the germplasm of industrial hemp that is set out in the licence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change to the authorized activity that is set out
  in the licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification --- changes

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A holder
  of a licence must notify the Minister of any of the following changes,
  by submitting a description of the change, within 30 days after the
  change or of becoming aware of the change:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to the name of the holder of the licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change to the partnership name, corporate name,
    or any other name registered with the federal government or with the
    government of a province, under which the licence holder intends to
    identify themself or conduct the activity authorized by the licence
    or, in the case of a partnership, corporation or cooperative, a
    change to the name of a partner, officer or director;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change to the telephone number or email address
    of the holder of the licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership, corporation or
    cooperative, the addition, departure or replacement of a partner,
    officer or director;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change to the address of the place where an
    activity is authorized;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a change to the address of the place for document
    retention as provided in the licence application or the address
    previously provided under this paragraph;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a change in the ownership of the land used to
    cultivate industrial hemp;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the cessation of the activity authorized by the
    licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the notification is in respect of a change
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the holder of the licence must
  provide the Minister with a copy of the document filed with the
  federal government or with the government of a province that states
  the name of the holder and any other registered name under which the
  holder intends to identify themself or conduct the activity authorized
  by the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition or replacement of a partner,
  officer or director

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the notification is in respect of the addition or
  replacement of a partner, officer or director, the holder of the
  licence must provide the Minister with the name and date of birth of
  the new partner, officer or director.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change in ownership

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the notification is in respect of a change in the
  ownership referred to in paragraph (1)(g), the holder of the licence
  must provide the Minister with a declaration signed and dated by the
  new owner of the land indicating that the new owner consents to the
  land being used to cultivate industrial hemp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation --- other circumstances

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 65(h) of the Act, other circumstances for the
  revocation of a licence are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence has requested, in
    writing, the revocation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence has changed their name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership, a partner has been
    added, has departed or has been replaced;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address of the place where the activity is
    authorized, with the exception of the cultivation site, has changed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the licence has sowed seed that is
    not of pedigreed status or of an approved cultivar;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence that authorizes cultivation
    for seed is no longer a member of the Canadian Seed Growers'
    Association;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a licence that authorizes propagation
    is no longer a plant breeder; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a holder of a licence that
    authorizes the obtaining of seed by preparing it, the registration
    referred to in Part IV of the [Seeds
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1400) has been cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(e) does not apply to a holder of a
  licence that authorizes propagation.

## [Import Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851297 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit for each shipment

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the importation of seed
or grain, in addition to holding a licence that authorizes that
activity, a holder of a licence must hold an import permit for each
shipment that is imported.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Import permit --- content

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The import permit must set
out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and mailing address of the holder of the
  permit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit number and the number of the licence that
  authorizes the importation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the exporter and their address in the
  country of export;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2025-43, s. 70\]]{.Repealed}

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} each mode of transportation used;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether seed or grain is being imported and the
  quantity, in kilograms, of each form imported;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the variety of industrial hemp from which the seed or
  grain was harvested or, in the case of germplasm, its name or number;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of THC, expressed as a percentage
  w/w, in the flowering heads and leaves of the variety of industrial
  hemp from which the seed or grain was harvested;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the permit; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry of the permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal --- other ground

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 62(7)(h) of the Act, another ground for the refusal to issue
an import permit is that the applicant does not hold a licence that
authorizes importation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An import permit is valid
until the earliest of the following dates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the shipment is imported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry of the permit or the date of its
  revocation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry of the licence that authorizes
  importation or the date of its revocation; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date of revocation of the permit for exportation
  issued by the competent authority of the country of export that
  pertains to the shipment.

## [Importation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851345 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seed

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An importer of seed must
import only

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} seed of pedigreed status that is of an approved
  cultivar and is recognized under the Organisation for Economic
  Co-operation and Development Seed Schemes or by the Association of
  Official Seed Certifying Agencies; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a plant breeder, seed of a variety of
  industrial hemp that is set out in their licence, or the germplasm
  whose name or number is set out in their licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grain

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An importer of grain must
import only grain from a country that participates in the Organisation
for Economic Co-operation and Development Seed Schemes or a country that
has an agency that is a member of the Association of Official Seed
Certifying Agencies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accompanying document

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** An importer of seed or
grain must ensure that each shipment is accompanied by a document,
issued by the responsible authority of the country of exportation, that
establishes that the seed or grain is industrial hemp and that it
originated from that country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of copy of import permit

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** An importer of seed or
grain must provide a copy of the import permit to the customs office at
the time of importation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information and documents

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** An importer must, within
20 days after the date of release of a shipment of seed or grain in
Canada, provide the Minister with the following information and
documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, the number of the licence that authorizes
  the importation and the import permit number issued in respect of the
  shipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of release of the shipment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of each form imported;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the shipment consists of seed, documents
  establishing that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the seed is of pedigreed status that is of an
    approved cultivar, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a plant breeder, the seed is of a
    variety of industrial hemp set out in their licence or the germplasm
    is the one whose name or number is set out in their licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purpose of verifying whether an importation of seed or grain complies
with these Regulations, provide to a customs officer any information
provided in the import permit application or referred to in sections 9
and 20 and inform that customs officer whether the import permit has
been suspended or revoked.

## [Exportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851366 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of copy of export permit

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** An exporter of seed or

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** An exporter must, within
20 days after the date of exportation of a shipment of seed or grain,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, the number of the licence that authorizes
  the exportation and the export permit number issued in respect of the
  shipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of exportation of the shipment; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of each form exported.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purpose of verifying whether an exportation of seed or grain complies
with these Regulations, provide to a customs officer any information
provided in the export permit application or referred to in sections 13
and 23, and inform that customs officer whether the export permit has
been suspended or revoked.

## [Possession of grain for processing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851376 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Processing

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence
that authorizes the possession of grain for the purpose of processing it
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} render it non-viable; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the processing results in the obtaining of
  non-viable grain,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have it tested for viability at a laboratory that
    is designated as an accredited laboratory under section 2.1 of the
    [Seeds Act](/eng/acts/S-8), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} retain records that demonstrate that the grain is
    non-viable.

## [Cultivation and Propagation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851383 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seed of pedigreed status of approved
cultivar

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a licence that authorizes cultivation, other than as a plant
  breeder, must sow only seed of pedigreed status that is of an approved
  cultivar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized variety or germplasm

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A plant breeder who holds a licence that authorizes
  cultivation or propagation must cultivate or propagate only the
  variety of industrial hemp that is set out in their licence or the
  germplasm whose name or number is set out in their licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a licence that authorizes cultivation, other than for the
  purpose of developing a new variety of industrial hemp, must provide
  the Minister with the following information within 30 days after
  seeding:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the variety of industrial hemp cultivated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of hectares cultivated for each form of
    industrial hemp set out in the licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Global Positioning System coordinates of each
    cultivation site;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if any part of the site is cultivated for seed, the
    Global Positioning System coordinates of that part of the site; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that the holder of the licence is the
    owner of the land used for cultivation or a declaration signed and
    dated by the owner of the land indicating that they consent to that
    use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the holder of a licence is a partnership,
  corporation or cooperative, the declaration referred to in paragraph
  (1)(e) must be signed and dated by the partner, officer or director
  who has the authority to bind the holder of the licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale --- flowering heads, leaves and
branches

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence
that authorizes sale may only sell the flowering heads, leaves and
branches to a holder of a licence, or a
[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of the [Cannabis
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a licence that authorizes cultivation for seed must, for the
  purpose of determining the concentration of THC in the flowering heads
  and leaves

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a representative sample of the flowering heads
    and leaves collected and dried; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a portion of the representative sample tested
    at a competent laboratory using validated test methods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the licence must submit the test
  results expressed as a percentage w/w, together with the name of the
  approved cultivar to which the test relates, to the Minister within 15
  days after receiving the results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of representative sample

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A portion of the sample referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a), in an amount that is sufficient to be tested, must be retained
  for at least one year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance of equipment

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence
that authorizes cultivation must maintain all equipment that is used to
sow or harvest industrial hemp in a manner that prevents its
contamination and avoids the inadvertent dissemination of the seed.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851412 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence
that authorizes sale must store flowering heads, leaves or branches that
are intended for sale under section 28 under conditions that maintain
their quality.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport, send and deliver

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a licence
that authorizes sale must transport, send or deliver flowering heads,
leaves or branches that are intended for sale under section 28 in a
manner that maintains their quality.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851417 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a licence must retain documents that contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the form in which the industrial hemp is imported,
    exported, purchased or sold, and its variety, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of each form imported,
    exported, purchased or sold;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which each shipment of industrial hemp
    is sent or received;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a holder that transports, sends or
    delivers industrial hemp, the name of the person that conducts these
    activities on behalf of the holder, if applicable; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of any tests required by these
    Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence that authorizes sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence that authorizes sale must also
  retain documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and licence number of the holder
    of a licence or a [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the
    meaning of the [Cannabis Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144)
    to which any flowering heads, leaves, branches, seed or grain is
    sold; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name and licence number of the
    holder of a licence from which the flowering heads, leaves,
    branches, seed or grain was purchased, the date on which it was
    purchased and the quantity, in kilograms, of each form that was
    purchased.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence that authorizes importation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence that authorizes importation
  must also retain documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and licence number of the holder
    of a licence, or a [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the
    meaning of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144), to which the seed or
    grain is sold; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the exporter and their address in the
    country of export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence that authorizes exportation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence that authorizes exportation
  must also retain documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the importer and their address in the
    country of import; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name and licence number of the
    holder of a licence from which the seed or grain was purchased, the
    date on which it was purchased and the quantity, in kilograms, of
    each form that was purchased.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence that authorizes cultivation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a licence that authorizes cultivation
  must also retain documents that contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of seed, in kilograms, of each
    approved cultivar that is sown or, in the case of a plant breeder,
    of each variety, and the quantity of seed harvested, in kilograms;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of the plant breeder,
    confirmation of the pedigreed status of the seed sown, including the
    [[official tag]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(2)
    of the [Seeds Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1400).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place and period of retention

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subsections (1) to (5)
  must be retained at the address of the place for document retention as
  provided in the licence application, or at the address provided under
  paragraph 6(1)(f), for at least one year after the day on which they
  were prepared or received.
