## Saskatchewan Broiler Chicken Order {#saskatchewan-broiler-chicken-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 268
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Broiler Chickens Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537481 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Broiler Chicken
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._268).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537483 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broiler chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broiler chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chicken

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} produced from the egg of a domestic hen other
      than a chicken grown to a live weight of more than 5 1/2 pounds or
      to an age of more than six months,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not raised or used for egg production, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} produced by a licensed producer; ([poulet à
      griller]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan
    Broiler Chicken Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to
    the Act; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Broiler Chicken
    Producers' Marketing Plan established by Order 587/66 of March 18,
    1966 made by the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Saskatchewan
    pursuant to the Act. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537492 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of broiler chickens in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Saskatchewan,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation
to the marketing of broiler chickens locally within that Province under
sections 2 and 22 of the Plan, excepting the powers exercisable under
paragraphs 22(j) and (k) of the Plan.
