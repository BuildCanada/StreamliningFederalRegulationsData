## Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations {#single-use-plastics-prohibition-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-138

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2022-06-20

Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations

P.C. 2022-723 2022-06-17

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4516_hq_30603){#fn_81000-2-4516_hq_30603-ID0EICDA}
of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote

copy of the proposed Single-use Plastics Prohibition Regulations,
substantially in the annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity
to file comments with respect to the proposed Regulations or to file a
notice of objection requesting that a board of review be established and
stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 93(3) of that Act, the National Advisory
Committee has been given an opportunity to provide its advice under
section 6[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4516_hq_30605){#fn_81000-2-4516_hq_30605-ID0EBBCA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote

  :::

And whereas, in accordance with subsection 93(4) of that Act, the
Governor in Council is of the opinion that the proposed Regulations do
not regulate an aspect of a substance that is regulated by or under any
other Act of Parliament in a manner that provides, in the opinion of the
Governor in Council, sufficient protection to the environment and human
health;

recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Health, pursuant to subsection 93(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354004 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[single-use plastic checkout bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic checkout bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    plastic manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic,
    that is formed in the shape of a bag that is designed to carry
    purchased goods from a business and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose plastic is not a
      [[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as defined in section 2 of the
      [Textile Labelling Act](/eng/acts/T-10); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose plastic is a [[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      as defined in section 2 of the [Textile Labelling
      Act](/eng/acts/T-10) that will break or tear if the bag is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} used to carry 10 kg over a distance of 53 m 100
        times, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} washed in accordance with the washing
        procedures specified for a single domestic wash in the
        International Organization for Standardization standard ISO
        6330, entitled Textiles --- Domestic washing and drying
        procedures for textile testing, as amended from time to
        time. ([sac d'emplettes en plastique à usage
        unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic cutlery]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic cutlery]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plastic
    manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic, that is
    formed in the shape of a fork, knife, spoon, spork or chopstick and
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains polystyrene or polyethylene; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} changes its physical properties after being run
      through an electrically operated household dishwasher 100
      times. ([ustensile en plastique à usage unique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic flexible straw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic flexible straw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    single-use plastic straw that has a corrugated section that allows
    the straw to bend and maintain its position at various
    angles. ([paille flexible en plastique à usage
    unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic foodservice ware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic foodservice ware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    plastic manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic,
    that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is formed in the shape of a clamshell container,
      lidded container, box, cup, plate or bowl;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is designed for serving or transporting food or
      beverage that is ready to be consumed; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contains expanded polystyrene foam, extruded
      polystyrene foam, polyvinyl chloride, a plastic that contains a
      black pigment produced through the partial or incomplete
      combustion of hydrocarbons or an oxo-degradable
      plastic. ([récipient alimentaire en plastique à usage
      unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic ring carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic ring carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    plastic manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic,
    that is formed in the shape of a series of deformable rings or bands
    that are designed to surround beverage containers in order to carry
    them together. ([anneaux en plastique à usage unique pour emballage
    de boissons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic stir stick]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic stir stick]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    plastic manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic,
    that is designed to stir or mix beverages or to prevent a beverage
    from spilling from the lid of its container. ([bâtonnet à mélanger
    en plastique à usage unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[single-use plastic straw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[single-use plastic straw]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plastic
    manufactured item, made entirely or in part from plastic, that is
    formed in the shape of a drinking straw and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains polystyrene or polyethylene; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} changes its physical properties after being run
      through an electrically operated household dishwasher 100
      times. ([paille en plastique à usage unique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Non-application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354025 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transit

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply in respect of plastic manufactured items
  referred to in section 1 that are in transit through Canada, from a
  place outside Canada to another place outside Canada, and are
  accompanied by written evidence establishing that the items are in
  transit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 8 and 9, these Regulations do not
  apply in respect of plastic manufactured items referred to in section
  1 that are manufactured, imported or sold for the purpose of export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waste

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of plastic
  manufactured items referred to in section 1 that are waste.

## [Single-use Plastic Ring Carriers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354033 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacture or import

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import single-use plastic ring carriers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not sell single-use plastic ring
  carriers.

## [Single-use Plastic Straws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354039 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacture or import

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person must not
manufacture or import single-use plastic straws, other than single-use
plastic flexible straws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- sale

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (6), a person must not sell single-use plastic
  straws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- sale in certain settings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may sell single-use plastic flexible straws
  in a non-commercial, non-industrial and non-institutional setting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- business to business
  sales

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A business may sell a package of 20 or more
  single-use plastic flexible straws to another business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- retail sales

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A retail store may sell a package of 20 or more
  single-use plastic flexible straws to a customer if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customer requests straws; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the package is not displayed in a manner that
    permits the customer to view the package without the help of a store
    employee.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2022-138, s. 11\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- sale in care institutions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A hospital, medical facility, long-term care facility
  or other care institution may sell single-use plastic flexible straws
  to patients or residents.

## [Other Plastic Manufactured Items]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354057 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacture or import

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import single-use plastic checkout bags,
  single-use plastic cutlery, single-use plastic foodservice ware or
  single-use plastic stir sticks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not sell single-use plastic checkout
  bags, single-use plastic cutlery, single-use plastic foodservice ware
  or single-use plastic stir sticks.

## [Analysis]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354063 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, any analysis performed to determine the
  physical characteristics of single-use plastic checkout bags,
  single-use plastic cutlery, single-use plastic flexible straws,
  single-use plastic foodservice ware or single-use plastic straws must
  be performed by a laboratory that meets the following conditions at
  the time of the analysis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
      Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
      requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
      laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
      International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
      Recognition Arrangement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environment Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the scope of its
    accreditation includes the analysis of the physical characteristics
    of single-use plastic checkout bags, single-use plastic cutlery,
    single-use plastic foodservice ware, single-use plastic flexible
    straws and single-use plastic straws.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of good practice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no method has been recognized by a standards
  development organization in respect of the analysis performed to
  determine the physical characteristics of the plastic manufactured
  items referred to in subsection (1) and the scope of the laboratory's
  accreditation does not therefore include that analysis, the analysis
  must be performed in accordance with standards of good scientific
  practice that are generally accepted at the time that it is performed.

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354073 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- export

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any person that
manufactures for the purpose of export, or that imports for the purpose
of export, a plastic manufactured item to which these Regulations apply
must keep records containing the following information and documents for
each type of plastic manufactured item that was manufactured for the
purpose of export or imported for the purpose of export:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that manufactures for the
  purpose of export,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the common or generic name and the trade name, if
    any, of the item,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the item manufactured at each
    manufacturing facility,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of manufacture of the item,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date the item was exported and the quantity
    exported or, if it has not yet been exported, the date on which it
    is intended to be exported and the quantity intended to be exported,
    and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name of the entity, if any, to which the item
    is sold in Canada for subsequent export; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that imports for the purpose
  of export,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the common or generic name and the trade name, if
    any, of the item,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity imported of the item,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date the item was imported,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the copies of the bill of lading, invoice and all
    documents submitted to the Canada Border Services Agency respecting
    the importation of the item,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date the item was exported and the quantity
    exported or, if it has not yet been exported, the date on which it
    is intended to be exported and the quantity intended to be exported,
    and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the name of the entity, if any, to which the item
    is sold in Canada for subsequent export.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of records

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that is required to keep records under section 8 must keep the records
  at the person's principal place of business in Canada or at any other
  place in Canada where they can be inspected, for at least five years
  after the date on which they are made. If the records are not kept at
  the person's principal place of business, the person must provide the
  Minister with the civic address of the place where they are kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records moved

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the records are moved, the person must notify the
  Minister in writing of the civic address in Canada of the new location
  within 30 days after the day of the move.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1354100 .Part}
