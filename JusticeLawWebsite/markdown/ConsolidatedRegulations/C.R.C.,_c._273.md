## Saskatchewan Hog Licensing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#saskatchewan-hog-licensing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 273
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Licensing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Persons Engaged in The Marketing of Hogs Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537640 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatchewan Hog Licensing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._273).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537642 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing
    Act, 1972 of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means premises to which
    producers may deliver their hogs; ([parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assembly yard operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner or
    operator of an assembly yard; ([exploitant d'un parc de
    groupage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog
    Marketing Commission established pursuant to the Act and the Plan;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[order buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person buying sows at
    a public auction in an assembly yard for the purpose of reselling
    to, or on behalf of another person; ([courtier en
    truies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog Marketing
    Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing hogs or using hogs in manufacturing;
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged in producing or feeding hogs
      and making them available for slaughter and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, under any lease or agreement is
      entitled to a share of the hogs produced or fed by a person
      referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who takes possession of any hogs from
      any person under any form of security for a debt;
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person purchasing hogs
    in the province, other than a producer, a processor, a consumer or a
    livestock dealer licensed in Saskatchewan and includes an order
    buyer; ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who operates a
    vehicle for the transport of hogs on a continued commercial basis.
    ([camionneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537658 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated in the Province of Saskatchewan.

## [Licensing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537660 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall act as a purchaser, a processor, an assembly yard
  operator or a trucker unless he holds a licence issued by the
  Commission authorizing such an action.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence referred to in subsection (1) shall expire
  on December 31st next following the day of issue.

## [Fees and Financial Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537664 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A licence referred to in
section 4 shall be issued upon payment of a fee of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$20 in the case of a purchaser;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$20 per plant in the case of a processor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$20 per assembly yard, in the case of an assembly
  yard operator; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$10 per vehicle, in the case of a trucker.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No purchaser, processor,
assembly yard operator or trucker shall hold a licence referred to in
section 4 unless he is properly bonded and gives to the Commission
adequate proof of financial responsibility.

## [Requirements for Assembly Yards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537671 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No licence shall be issued
to an assembly yard operator unless each assembly yard that he proposes
to operate provides the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} adequate protection from wind and sun;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concrete floor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} bedding;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} water readily available;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} adequate pens;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} adequate loading facilities during all weather
  conditions; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} tattooing equipment capable of producing tattoos that
  are clearly legible.
