## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679654 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[retail deposit-taking branch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retail deposit-taking branch]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a branch
    or office in Canada of a financial institution at which the
    financial institution, through a natural person, opens retail
    deposit accounts and disburses cash to customers. ([bureau de dépôt
    de détail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rural area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rural area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any territory in Canada
    located outside an urban area. ([zone rurale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[urban area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[urban area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a geographic area in Canada

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is defined as an urban area in the census
      dictionary published by Statistics Canada for the purpose of the
      most recent general census whose results have been published; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that has a minimum population of 10,000 persons,
      on the basis of that census. ([zone urbaine]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- SOR/2016-142, s. 11

## [Notice Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679665 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed time of giving notice

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, a notice is deemed to be given by a
  member association to a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day recorded by a server of the member
    association as the time of sending the notice to the person, if it
    is sent by electronic means;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the day recorded by a fax machine of the member
    association as the time of sending the notice to the person, if it
    is sent by fax and the person has consented to receive it by fax;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} five days after the postmark date on the notice, if
    it is sent to the person by the member association by mail; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} when the notice is received by the person, if it is
    given to the person by the member association in any other way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be given

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A member association that
is required to give notice under subsection 385.27(1) of the Act before
closing a branch or having it cease to carry on an activity referred to
in that subsection must give notice of the proposed closure of the
branch or cessation of the activity in accordance with sections 4 to 6.

- SOR/2014-273, s. 17(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Commissioner

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice must be given to the Commissioner in writing no later than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} four months before the date proposed for the
    closure of the branch or the cessation of the activity, if the
    branch is in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an urban area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a rural area where there is a retail
      deposit-taking branch within a travelling distance of 10 km from
      the branch; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} six months before the date proposed for the closure
    of the branch or the cessation of the activity, if the branch is in
    a rural area where there is no retail deposit-taking branch within a
    travelling distance of 10 km from the branch.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be included

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of the branch;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date proposed for the closure of the branch or
    the cessation of the activity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alternative sites where, after that date, customers
    of the branch may obtain services similar to the financial services
    that are provided at the branch, or a telephone number that
    customers may call to be informed of those sites;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the measures, if any, that the member association
    is taking to maintain any financial services that are available in
    the area that is served by the branch, to the extent that that
    information is available; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} how the member association may be contacted in
    respect of the proposed closure of the branch or cessation of the
    activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-70, s. 6(F)
- SOR/2014-273, s. 17(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to customers and public

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  branch is in an urban area, or in a rural area where there is a retail
  deposit-taking branch located within a travelling distance of 10 km
  from the branch, the notice must be given to the customers of the
  branch and to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Four months notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given no later than four months
  before the date proposed for the closure of the branch or the
  cessation of the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} posting it in a conspicuous place in a public area
    of the branch; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it to each customer of the branch

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by mail, either included with a regular account
      statement or in a separate mailing, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by electronic means, if the customer regularly
      receives material from the member association by electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of the branch;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date proposed for the closure of the branch or
    the cessation of the activity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alternative sites where, after that date, customers
    of the branch may obtain services similar to the financial services
    that are provided at the branch, or a telephone number that
    customers may call to be informed of those sites;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address of the branch to which the member
    association will transfer the customers' accounts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the measures, if any, that the member association
    is taking to maintain any financial services that are available in
    the area that is served by the branch, to the extent that that
    information is available;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} how the member association and the Commissioner may
    be contacted in respect of the proposed closure of the branch or
    cessation of the activity; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the Commissioner may require the
    member association to convene and hold a meeting between
    representatives of the member association, representatives of the
    Agency and interested parties in the vicinity of the branch, in
    order to exchange views about the proposed closure of the branch or
    cessation of the activity, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the member association has not consulted the
      community in the area affected by the closure of the branch or the
      cessation of the activity well enough to ascertain the views of
      interested persons in the community with regard to the closure of
      the branch or the cessation of the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual or a community representative from
      the area affected by the closure of the branch or the cessation of
      the activity submits to the Commissioner a written request for the
      meeting, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the request is not frivolous or vexatious.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-70, s. 7(F)
- SOR/2014-273, s. 17(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to customers, public and mayor

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  branch is in a rural area and there is no retail deposit-taking branch
  within a travelling distance of 10 km from the branch, the notice must
  be given to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customers of the branch;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the public; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the chairperson, mayor, warden, reeve or other
    similar chief officer of the municipal or local government body or
    authority for the area in which the branch is located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Six months notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given no later than six months
  before the date proposed for the closure of the branch or the
  cessation of the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of giving notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} posting it in a conspicuous place in a public area
    of the branch;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it to each customer of the branch

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by mail, either included with a regular account
      statement or in a separate mailing, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by electronic means, if the customer regularly
      receives material from the member association by electronic means;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} publishing it in a newspaper in general circulation
    at or near the place where the branch is located; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} delivering it to the chairperson, mayor, warden,
    reeve or other similar chief officer of the municipal or local
    government body or authority for the area in which the branch is
    located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of the branch;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date proposed for the closure of the branch or
    the cessation of the activity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} alternative sites where, after that date, customers
    of the branch may obtain services similar to the financial services
    that are provided at the branch, or a telephone number that
    customers may call to determine those sites;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address of the branch to which the member
    association will transfer the customers' accounts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the measures, if any, that the member association
    is taking to maintain any financial services that are available in
    the area that is served by the branch, to the extent that that
    information is available;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} how the member association and the Commissioner may
    be contacted in respect of the proposed closure of the branch or
    cessation of the activity; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the Commissioner may require the
    member association to convene and hold a meeting between
    representatives of the member association, representatives of the
    Agency and interested parties in the vicinity of the branch, in
    order to exchange views about the proposed closure of the branch or
    cessation of the activity, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the member association has not consulted the
      community in the area affected by the closure of the branch or the
      cessation of the activity well enough to ascertain the views of
      interested persons in the community with regard to the closure of
      the branch or the cessation of the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an individual or a community representative from
      the area affected by the closure of the branch or the cessation of
      the activity submits to the Commissioner a written request for the
      meeting, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the request is not frivolous or vexatious.

## [Exceptions to Notice Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679748 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances for exemption from notice
requirement

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A member association is not
required to give notice under subsection 385.27(1) of the Act before
closing a branch or having a branch cease to carry on an activity
referred to in that subsection in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is temporary and is caused
  by events beyond the control of the member association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is not expected to continue
  for more than 15 business days;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from the sale by the
  member association of the branch's assets and liabilities to another
  financial institution, that other financial institution proposes to
  operate a retail deposit-taking branch at the same site and the
  financial services that have been provided to the public from that
  site are not expected to be interrupted as a result of the sale for
  more than 15 business days;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from a relocation of
  the branch or a consolidation of the branch with one or more branches
  into another branch, and the travelling distance from the site of the
  relocated or consolidated branch to the site of the closed branch is
  less than 500 m;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is required in order to
  comply with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a prudential agreement entered into between the
    member association and the Superintendent under section 438.1 of the
    Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a direction of the Superintendent under subsection
    439(1) of the Act, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an order of a court under section 441 of the Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from a decision of
  the Superintendent under subsection 442(2) of the Act after the
  Superintendent has taken control of the member association under
  subparagraph 442(1)(b)(iii) of the Act;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from actions
  directed towards the voluntary liquidation of the member association

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after the Minister has approved an application
    under section 328 of the Act for letters patent dissolving the
    member association, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the supervision of a court under an order
    made by the court under subsection 331(1) of the Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from a winding-up
  order in respect of the member association made under section 10 or
  10.1 of the [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from the termination
  or cancellation of the policy of deposit insurance of the member
  association under the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from the making of
  an order under subsection 39.13(1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance
  Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3) in respect of the member association;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is part of a restructuring
  transaction carried out under subsection 39.2(1) of the [Canada
  Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3);

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the branch was acquired within the preceding year by
  a purchaser in a restructuring transaction carried out under
  subsection 39.2(1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3); or

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the member association has posted in a conspicuous
  place in a public area of the branch a notification of the closure or
  cessation before the coming into force of these Regulations and the
  closure or cessation occurs within

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} six months after the coming into force of these
    Regulations, if the branch is in a rural area where there is no
    retail deposit-taking branch within a travelling distance of 10 km
    from the branch,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} four months after the coming into force of these
    Regulations, in any other cases.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-70, s. 9(F)
- SOR/2014-273, s. 17(F)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner may exempt from or vary
notice requirement

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  circumstances set out in subsection (2), the Commissioner may, at the
  request of a member association,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exempt the member association from the requirement
    to give notice under subsection 385.27(1) of the Act in relation to
    the closure of a branch or the cessation of an activity referred to
    in that subsection; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vary the manner and time in which notice is
    otherwise required to be given under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances in which subsection (1)
  applies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies in the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is in response to a risk
    to the safety of the personnel of the branch or the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation is caused by the member
    association's right to use the premises as a retail deposit-taking
    branch being terminated by a person other than the member
    association or an affiliate of the member association and that
    person has not given the member association enough notice of the
    termination to allow the member association to comply with the
    notice requirements of these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the closure or cessation results from a relocation
    of the branch, and the travelling distance from the new location to
    the former location is more than 500 m but not great enough to
    substantially affect either the customers served by the branch or
    the nature of the business of the branch; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the giving of notice under subsection 385.27(1) of
    the Act in the manner and time otherwise required by these
    Regulations would cause undue prejudice to the member association.

## [Circumstances in Which the Commissioner Shall Require a Member Association to Convene and Hold a Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679786 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2009-42, s. 2

\]
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances in which Commissioner shall
require a meeting

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 385.27(2) of the Act, the following circumstances are
prescribed as circumstances in which the Commissioner shall require a
member association to convene and hold a meeting referred to in that
subsection:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member association has not consulted the
  community in the area affected by the closure of the branch or the
  cessation of the activity well enough to ascertain the views of
  interested persons in the community with regard to the closure of the
  branch, the cessation of the activity, alternate service delivery by
  the member association or measures to help the member association's
  customers adjust to the closing or cessation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual or a community representative from the
  area affected by the closure of the branch or the cessation of the
  activity submits to the Commissioner a written request for the
  meeting; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the request is not frivolous or vexatious.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-42, s. 3
- SOR/2014-273, ss. 17(F), 18(F)
