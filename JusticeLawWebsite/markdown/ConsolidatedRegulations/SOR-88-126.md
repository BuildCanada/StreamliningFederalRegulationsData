## Cranbrook Airport Zoning Regulations {#cranbrook-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-126
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1988-02-04

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Cranbrook Airport

P.C. 1988-188 1988-02-04
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Cranbrook Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on August 22nd and 29th,
1987, and in two successive issues of the Cranbrook Daily Townsman on
June 22nd and 23rd, 1987, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of Transport with respect thereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 4.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting Zoning at Cranbrook
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-126).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911040 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cranbrook Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-126).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911042 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Cranbrook Airport at
      Cranbrook, in the East Kootenay Regional District and in the
      Kootenay Land District, in the Province of British Columbia;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 924.5 m above sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-169, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911055 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-169, s. 2(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911061 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-169, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911068 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-169, s. 4
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911072 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-169, s. 5(F)
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Cranbrook Airport Zoning Plan
No. B.C. 1640 (Z) dated January 12, 1987, is situated at a point distant
197.83 m measured westerly and perpendicular to the centre line of
runway 16-34 from a point on the said centre line distant 1 089.58 m
north of the southern end of the strip.

The airport reference point is marked by a brass plate set in a concrete
catch basin and is identified as Department of Transport Monument
Number 126 + 22.50.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Cranbrook Airport Zoning Plan
No. B.C. 1640 (Z) dated January 12, 1987, are surfaces abutting each end
of the strip associated with the runway designated 16-34 and are
described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 16 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 34 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1.25 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to
  an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 450 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Cranbrook Airport Zoning Plan No. B.C. 1640
(Z) dated January 12, 1987, is an imaginary surface located at a common
plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of
the airport reference point, except that, where that common plane is
less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is an
imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 16-34, shown on Cranbrook Airport
Zoning Plan No. B.C. 1640 (Z) dated January 12, 1987, is described as
being 300 m in width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the
runway, and 1 948.853 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Cranbrook Airport Zoning Plan
No. B.C. 1640 (Z) dated January 12, 1987, is a surface consisting of an
inclined plane having a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected centre
line of each strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of each strip and its approach surface to an intersection with
the outer surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
