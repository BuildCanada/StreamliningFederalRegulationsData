## Tributyltetradecylphosphonium Chloride Regulations {#tributyltetradecylphosphonium-chloride-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-66
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2000-02-24

Tributyltetradecylphosphonium Chloride Regulations

P.C. 2000-209 2000-02-24
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on May 15, 1999, a copy of
the proposed [Tributyltetradecylphosphonium Chloride
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-66), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file a notice of
objection requesting that a board of review be established under section
89 of the Act and stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA){#footnotea_e}R.S., c. 16
  (4th Supp.)
  :::

Whereas more than 60 days have elapsed since the date of publication and
no notices of objection to the proposed Regulations were filed with the
Minister of the Environment pursuant to subsection 48(2) of the Act;

And whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of the Act, the proposed Regulations do not regulate,
in respect of any substance, an aspect of the substance that is
regulated by or under any other Act of Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
section 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA} of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act^a^, on the recommendation of the
Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health and after the
federal-provincial advisory committee has been given an opportunity to
provide its advice under section 6 of the Act, hereby makes the annexed
[Tributyltetradecylphosphonium Chloride
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-66).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 1992, c.
  1, s. 144 (Sch. VII, s. 15)
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664465 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
"substance" means tributyltetradecylphosphonium chloride that has the
molecular formula C~26~H~56~P·Cl.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664467 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of the manufacture, use, processing, sale, offering for
sale or importation of the substance as a laboratory analytical
standard, or any aspect of the substance that is regulated by or under
the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664469 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use,
process, sell, offer for sale or import the substance.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall manufacture
the substance unless the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactures it for export only;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has notified the Minister, in writing, of their
  intent to manufacture it, 60 days before the start date of the
  manufacturing process; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} manufactures it using a fully contained process by
  which the containment of the substance is ensured by various pollution
  control or recovery technologies, including emergency containment, in
  order to prevent any release into the environment.

## [Manufacturer's Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664477 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person who manufactures
the substance for export shall maintain books and records certifying the
quantities of the substance manufactured during a given year. The books
and records shall be kept at the person's facility in Canada for five
years.

## [Export]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664479 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer who exports
the substance shall maintain a copy of the Canada Customs declaration of
export, if applicable, at their facility in Canada for five years.

## [Incineration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664481 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A manufacturer who sends
the substance or a material that contains the substance for incineration
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} proceed in accordance with the *National Guidelines
  for Hazardous Waste Incineration Facilities --- Design and Operating
  Criteria*, Volume 1, Canadian Council of Ministers of the Environment
  (CCME), March 1992, as amended from time to time; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain for five years at their facility in Canada a
  copy of the documents certifying the incineration.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-664485 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
