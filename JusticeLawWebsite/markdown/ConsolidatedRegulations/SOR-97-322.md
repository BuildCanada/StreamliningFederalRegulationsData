## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005993 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 25.7(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), goods are
entitled to the benefit of the Chile Tariff where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped from Chile, without shipment
  through another country, to Canada

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, where requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods that are shipped to Canada from
  Chile through another country, the importer provides, where requested
  by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    demonstrate that the goods remained under customs control while in
    that other country.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006001 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-97-322_e_hq_5745){#fn_SOR-97-322_e_hq_5745-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 51 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.6), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes
into force.
