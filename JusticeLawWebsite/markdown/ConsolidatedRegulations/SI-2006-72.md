## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Industry the Control and Supervision of the National Science Advisor Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-industry-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-national-science-advisor-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-72
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-05-17

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Industry the Control and Supervision of the National Science Advisor
Secretariat

P.C. 2006-354 2006-05-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Industry the control and supervision of the portion of the federal
public administration within the Privy Council Office known as the
National Science Advisor Secretariat, effective May 8, 2006.
