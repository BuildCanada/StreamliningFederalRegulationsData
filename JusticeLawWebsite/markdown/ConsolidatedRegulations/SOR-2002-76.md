## Marine Activities in the Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park Regulations {#marine-activities-in-the-saguenay-st.-lawrence-marine-park-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Marine Activities in the Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park Regulations

P.C. 2002-201  2002-02-20

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 17 of the
[Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3)[[Footnote

the annexed [Marine Activities in the Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-76).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699137 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine
    Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Aeronautics
    Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a juvenile cetacean measuring no
    more than one-half the size of an adult. ([veau]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cargo ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cargo ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial ship used to
    transport goods in various forms including composite units, as
    defined in subsection 1(1) of the [Collision
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416), and barge
    combinations. ([navire de charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a marine tour business whose
    activities take place on a vessel, other than a human-powered
    vessel, and include directed marine mammal watching tours. ([classe
    1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a marine tour business whose
    activities take place on a vessel, other than a human-powered
    vessel, and consist of activities other than directed marine mammal
    watching tours. ([classe 2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a marine tour business whose
    activities take place on a human-powered vessel. ([classe
    3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-257, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a passenger vessel
    offering overnight accommodations for at least 100 persons exclusive
    of crew accommodations. ([navire de croisière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[disturbance of a marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disturbance of a marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-257, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[endangered marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[endangered marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2016-257, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Fjord]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Fjord]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2016-257, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine mammal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any cetacean or
    pinniped. ([mammifère marin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marine tour business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine tour business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any trade,
    industry, employment or service, whether or not for profit, relating
    to the observation from the water or air of plants, animals or the
    landscape or seabed of the park or cultural resources in the park,
    other than such trade, industry, employment or service when carried
    out by a person on behalf of the superintendent for the purposes of
    the management of the park, and includes excursions, cruises,
    fishing, diving, kayaking and flights over the park in the course of
    that trade, industry, employment or service. ([entreprise
    d'excursions en mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[observation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[observation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area consisting of
    two or more overlapping or contiguous observation zones. ([(secteur
    d'observation)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[observation mode]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[observation mode]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    vessel, the mode that describes the state of the vessel when the
    operator, for the purpose of observing a cetacean, permits the
    vessel to approach the cetacean within a distance of less than
    400 m. ([mode d'observation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[observation zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[observation zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a moving circular
    zone that exists around a vessel while it is in observation mode in
    the park and that has a radius of one-half nautical mile
    (926 m). ([zone d'observation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vessel, means the
    person who has the command or control of the
    vessel. ([pilote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following permits
    issued by the Minister under subsection 10(1) of the Act:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a class 1, 2 or 3 permit;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shuttle service permit;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a scientific research permit;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a cruise ship permit; or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a special activity
      permit. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shuttle service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shuttle service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shuttle service that
    is operated for the purpose of carrying passengers by water for
    compensation. ([service de navette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a planned, temporary
    activity or event held in the park, and includes a parade, regatta,
    show, film production or promotion, sports event or an activity
    involving flying at an altitude of less than 609.6 m
    (2,000 feet). ([activité spéciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means speed over the
    ground. ([vitesse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a watercraft of any description
    that is used or capable of being used for navigation, without regard
    to its method of propulsion or whether it lacks a means of
    propulsion, and includes air cushion
    vehicles. ([bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of 12 consecutive months
    beginning on April 1. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699170 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  3 and 14 to 26 do not apply to the superintendent, a park warden, an
  enforcement officer or a peace officer while engaged in the discharge
  of their duties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3(2) and sections 14 to 26 do not apply to
  any person acting under an agreement with or instructions from the
  Minister for the purpose of protecting marine mammals or the
  environment or for the purpose of public safety or park management.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} other than subsection 14(2) and sections 19 and 20,
    do not apply to the holder of any of the following licences issued
    by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans when the holder is engaged
    in fishing for the species for which or in any other activity in
    respect of which the licence is issued, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued for the purpose of commercial
      fishing, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an aboriginal communal fishing licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} other than subsection 14(2) and sections 15 and 19
    to 22, do not apply to the holder of a licence to fish for seal
    issued by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans when the holder is
    engaged in fishing for the species for which or in any other
    activity in respect of which the licence is issued; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other than sections 14, 14.2 and 19, do not apply
    to the operator of a cargo ship.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 15(1) to (3) and sections 15.1 and 21 to
  25 do not apply to the operator of vessel operating under a scientific
  research permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the research involves marine mammals; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is necessary for the purposes of the research to
    approach marine mammals closer than the minimum distance required
    under those provisions.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699185 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699186 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to operate a marine tour business, a cruise
  ship or a shuttle service in the park unless the person is a permit
  holder in respect of that business or service or is authorized in
  writing by the permit holder to do so on their behalf.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person to conduct scientific
  research or to hold a special activity in the park unless the person
  is a permit holder in respect of that research or activity or is
  authorized in writing by the permit holder to do so on their behalf.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a permit
shall ensure that every vessel authorized to operate under the permit is
operated in a manner that does not contravene these Regulations.

### [Limitations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699193 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A class
  1 or 2 permit, a shuttle service permit or a cruise ship permit shall
  authorize the operation of only one vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A class 1, 2 or 3 permit and a shuttle service permit
  shall be issued only to the owner of the business or service in
  respect of which the permit is sought.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A maximum of 53 vessels may be authorized to operate
  in the park under class 1 permits.

### [Application for Permit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699200 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to the Minister for a permit shall be in writing and
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the applicant
    and, if they are different, those of the applicant's business;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the type of permit being sought;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information respecting the equipment that the
    applicant uses or intends to use, including the number of vessels,
    if any, and their registration or listing numbers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any area of the park in which the
    applicant operates or intends to operate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any documentation required by law to
    prove that any vessel that is the subject of the application is
    authorized to carry out, and is capable of carrying out, the
    activity for which the permit is sought;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a class 1, 2 or 3
    permit, a shuttle service permit, or a cruise ship permit, the types
    of goods or services that the applicant intends to offer;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a scientific
    research permit, a description of the proposed scientific research,
    its expected duration, the objectives to be attained, the
    methodology and the equipment to be used, the dates on which and the
    times and places at which the research is to be conducted and, in
    the case of research involving marine mammals, a statement
    indicating whether it is necessary for any vessel operating under
    the permit to approach marine mammals closer than the minimum
    distance required under subsections 15(1) to (3) and sections 15.1
    and 21 to 23;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a special
    activity permit, a description of the proposed special activity, its
    expected duration, the objectives to be attained and the dates on
    which and the times and places at which it is to be held; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cruise ship permit, the dates of
    the intended cruise.

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-257, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a permit shall be accompanied by
  the applicable fee fixed by the Minister under section 24 of the
  [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit holder shall notify the Minister in writing
  as soon as feasible of any change in the information that was provided
  in the application.

### [Issuance of Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699217 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When deciding whether to
issue a permit under section 10 of the Act, the Minister shall take into
consideration the following objectives:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protection, control and management of the park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection of ecosystems, and any elements of
  ecosystems, in the park, in particular by decreasing the number of
  vessels authorized to operate in the park;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the cultural resources submerged in
  the park;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the protection, health and safety of the public in
  the park; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the encouragement of the use of the park for
  educational, recreational and scientific purposes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 6
:::

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699226 .Subheading}

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
specify conditions in a permit to fulfil the objectives set out in
paragraphs 7(a) to (d), including conditions regarding one or more of
the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrictions, in terms of time and place, on the
  conduct of the activity authorized by the permit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuelling practices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} practices for the safe conduct of the activity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information that shall be provided to passengers;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the methods of communication used by operators of
  vessels operating under class 1 permits, in accordance with subsection
  26(2);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the use of required equipment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the requirement to have on board the vessel a park
  warden, enforcement officer or any other person specified in the
  permit;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the reporting of information to the park
  administration, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of uses made annually of the vessel,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a class 3 permit, a report of any
    incidents affecting ecosystems or the health or safety of clients,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a scientific research permit, a
    report on the scientific research activities performed under the
    terms of the permit;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of clients allowed for each guide
  operating under a class 3 permit;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the qualifications required for guides operating
  under a class 3 permit; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a permit for scientific research
  involving marine mammals, in the event that it is necessary for a
  vessel to approach marine mammals closer than the minimum distance
  required under subsections 15(1) to (3) and sections 15.1 and 21 to
  25, restrictions on the conduct of the research in order to minimize
  any disturbance of the marine mammals.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 6
:::

- **[[7.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may amend any condition of a permit to fulfil the objectives
  set out in paragraphs 7(a) to (d).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of the amendment shall be sent to the permit
  holder and the amendment is effective on the day after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day recorded by the park administration's
    server as the day on which the notice is sent, if the permit holder
    consents to receiving it by electronic means;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day recorded by the park administration's fax
    machine as the day on which the notice is sent, if the permit holder
    consents to receiving it by fax;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the notice is received, if it is
    sent to the permit holder by registered mail; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the notice is received by the
    permit holder, if it is delivered personally.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notification is part of the permit and the permit
  holder shall attach it to the permit on receipt.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 6
:::

### [Suspension and Cancellation of Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699254 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  grounds for the suspension of a permit by the Minister under
  section 10 of the Act are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the permit has failed to comply with
    a condition specified in the permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
    permit holder has contravened these Regulations, the Act or any
    other applicable Acts or regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the permit has failed to inform the
    Minister of any change in the information provided in the
    application for the permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The grounds for the reinstatement of a permit by the
  Minister under section 10 of the Act are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the breach that led to the suspension has been
    corrected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a period of 30 days has elapsed since the date of
    the suspension and proceedings in respect of the alleged
    contravention were not instituted before the end of that period; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has been found not guilty of
    contravening these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The grounds for the cancellation of a permit by the
  Minister under section 10 of the Act are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has been found guilty of
    contravening these Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except where the permit was reinstated under
    paragraph (2)(b) or (c), the permit has been suspended three times
    during the period for which it was issued.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not cancel a permit without
  first giving the permit holder an opportunity to be heard.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a permit that has been suspended is not
  eligible to be issued any permit during the suspension period.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a permit that has been cancelled is not
  eligible to be issued any permit during the 12-month period following
  the date of the cancellation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 7

### [Transfer and Expiry of Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699272 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall transfer a
permit except in accordance with section 11.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A permit expires on the
earliest of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date stated in the permit, including any
  instance when the ownership of a marine tour business or a shuttle
  service is sold or otherwise transferred to another person, if the
  permit in respect of that business or service, as the case may be, is
  transferred in accordance with section 11,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of cancellation, if any, of the permit, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the ownership of a marine tour business or
  shuttle service is sold or otherwise transferred to another person,
  the date of that transfer, if the permit in respect of that business
  or service, as the case may be, is not transferred to that other
  person in accordance with section 11.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the ownership of a marine tour business or shuttle service is
  transferred to another person, any permit in respect of the business
  or service may be transferred to that other person if the permit
  holder

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notifies the Minister, in writing, of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the person to
      whom the permit is to be transferred,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date of the transfer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any change in the name of the business or the
      name and registration or listing number of the vessel to be
      operated by the new permit holder under the permit after the
      transfer, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any changes in any other information that was
      provided under subsection 6(1) in the application for the permit;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtains the Minister's approval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall approve the transfer of the permit
  if the proposed changes will not affect the conditions of the permit.

- SOR/2016-257, s. 8

### [Marine Activities Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699289 .Subheading}

- **[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a class 1, 2 or 3
  permit, a scientific research permit involving marine mammals or a
  special activity permit involving marine mammals shall ensure that the
  operator or guide conducting an activity under the permit holds a
  marine activities certificate issued by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall issue a certificate to an operator
  or guide who has passed a training course approved by the Minister.
  The operator or guide shall pass an annual examination to renew the
  certificate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator or guide shall pay the applicable fee
  for the certificate as fixed by the Minister under section 24 of the
  [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator or guide shall carry the certificate at
  all times when engaged in the activities referred to in the permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 9
:::

### [Suspension and Cancellation of Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699297 .Subheading}

- **[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The ground for suspension of a
  marine activities certificate by the Minister under section 10 of the
  Act is that there are reasonable grounds to believe that the operator
  or guide has contravened the Act, these Regulations or other
  applicable Acts or regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The grounds for cancellation of a certificate by the
  Minister under section 10 of the Act are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the operator or guide has been convicted three
    times of contravening the Act, these Regulations or other applicable
    Acts or regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the certificate has been suspended three
    times.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not issue a certificate to an
  operator or guide whose certificate has previously been cancelled.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not cancel a certificate without
  first giving the operator or guide an opportunity to be heard.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 9
:::

### [Log Book]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699307 .Subheading}

- **[[11.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The permit holder of a class 1 or 2
  permit, a shuttle service permit, a scientific research permit or a
  cruise ship permit shall ensure that the log book of a vessel
  operating under the permit is maintained.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The log book shall contain the following information
  for each use of the vessel under the permit:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the point of departure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the operator;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the time of departure and return;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of passengers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the operator's signature; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a note of any incidents affecting ecosystems or the
    health or safety of passengers.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The log book shall be presented to a park warden or
  enforcement officer on request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 9
:::

## [Prohibited Conduct and Controlled Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699339 .Part}

### [Disturbance of Marine Mammals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699340 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall engage in behaviour in the park that may kill or injure a
  marine mammal or cause the disturbance of a marine mammal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Disturbance of a marine mammal includes, but is not
  limited to, the following activities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} feeding or touching a marine mammal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entering the water with the intention of swimming
    or otherwise interacting with marine mammals;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} playing whale songs or calls, or making any other
    noise that may resemble whale songs or calls, under water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} separating members of a group of marine mammals or
    going between an adult marine mammal and its calf;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} trapping a cetacean or group of cetaceans between a
    vessel and the shore or between a vessel and other vessels; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any undue interruption, alteration or disruption of
    the normal behaviour of a marine mammal, in particular its social,
    swimming, breathing, diving, resting, feeding, nursing or
    reproductive behaviour.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1), in respect of behaviour that may
  injure or cause the disturbance of marine mammals, does not apply to a
  person conducting scientific research involving marine mammals under a
  scientific research permit if the injury or disturbance is necessary
  for the research.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel that is involved in an
  incident in which a marine mammal is killed or injured or that
  collides with a marine mammal shall report the incident immediately to
  a park warden or an enforcement officer and provide the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the operator and contact information
    that would permit prompt communication;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location, date and time of the incident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the species involved;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances of the incident;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the condition of the animal before and after the
    incident, if known;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the animal's direction of travel after the
    incident;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the weather and sea conditions; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 10
:::

### [Temporary Exclusion Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699362 .Subheading}

**[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
establish a temporary exclusion area if it is necessary for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protection, control or management of the park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection of ecosystems or any elements of
  ecosystems, in the park;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the cultural resources submerged in
  the park; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the protection, health or safety of the public in the
  park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11
:::

**[[14.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.2 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for
any person to enter a temporary exclusion area during the time that it
is in force unless that person is authorized by the Minister to do so
for purposes related to the reason for the establishment of the
exclusion area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11
:::

- **[[14.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall determine the
  geographic limits of the temporary exclusion area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall determine the period during which
  the temporary exclusion area will remain in force, the duration of
  which shall not exceed 60 days.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may establish further periods not
  exceeding 60 days each if it is necessary for the reasons set out in
  section 14.1.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, as soon as feasible, have the
  Department of Fisheries and Oceans communicate in a Notice to Shipping
  or a Notice to Mariners, the decision to establish the temporary
  exclusion area and the decisions made under subsections (1) to (3).
  The Minister shall also communicate by fax or other electronic means
  the same to all permit holders and any marinas from which vessels may
  navigate into the park and post notices in conspicuous places where
  the notices are most likely to come to the attention of any person who
  will enter the area.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The temporary exclusion area exists as of the time
  that the decision establishing it is communicated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11
:::

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699381 .Subheading}

**[[14.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited, in
the park,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to use a personal watercraft, as defined in
  subsection 1(1) of the [Small Vessel
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1487);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to use an air cushion vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to conduct a water sport activity using a vessel or
  any other motorized system as a method of traction; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to offer any commercial services related to hunting
  migratory birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11
:::

### [Distance Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699389 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections 15.1(1) and
  (2), the operator of a vessel shall not, by means of the vessel's
  motors or under the force of the winds, waves, currents or by any
  other means, permit the vessel to approach a cetacean within a
  distance of less than 100 m, if the vessel is operating under a class
  1 permit, or within a distance of less than 200 m, in the case of any
  other vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections 15.1(1) and (2), the operator
  of a vessel shall not place the vessel in the path of a cetacean in
  such a manner that the cetacean will pass within a distance of less
  than 100 m, if the vessel is operating under a class 1 permit, or
  within a distance of less than 200 m, in the case of any other vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a cetacean, other than a beluga, approaches within
  a distance of less than 100 m from a vessel that is operating under a
  class 1 permit, or less than 200 m from any other vessel, the operator
  of the vessel shall place the engine in neutral gear until the
  cetacean has dived toward the seabed or moved more than 100 m or 200 m
  from the vessel, as the case may be.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 15.1(1), the operator of a
  vessel operating under a class 1 permit shall not permit the vessel to
  approach within a distance of less than 200 m from a cetacean
  accompanied by a calf or from a resting cetacean.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11
:::

- **[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel shall
  maintain a minimum distance of 400 m between the vessel and any marine
  mammal of a species or of a population of a species that is listed in
  any of Parts 1 to 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel shall not place the vessel
  in the path of a marine mammal referred to in subsection (1) in such a
  manner that the marine mammal will pass within a distance of less than
  400 m.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), if the operator of a
  vessel is unable to maintain a minimum distance of 400 m from a
  beluga, the operator shall maintain course until the vessel is more
  than 400 m from any beluga.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if a vessel is in observation
  mode when a beluga approaches within a distance of less than 400 m,
  the operator of the vessel shall place the vessel engine in neutral
  gear or move the vessel away in accordance with sections 23 and 24.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 11

Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416) prevail to the extent of

### [Observation Zones and Observation Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699442 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a vessel shall not keep the vessel in observation mode for
  more than one hour or operate the vessel in the observation zone of
  another vessel or in an observation area for more than one hour.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel shall not permit the vessel
  to re-enter the observation zone of another vessel or an observation
  area until one hour has elapsed after leaving that observation zone or
  observation area, as the case may be.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-257, s. 15
:::

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a vessel operating under a class 1 permit shall not permit
  the vessel to approach a cetacean within a distance of between 100 and
  200 m

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for more than two periods of 30 minutes each during
    each excursion; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than once in the same observation zone or
    observation area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a vessel operating under a class 1
  permit shall --- when the vessel is placed in observation mode, enters
  an observation zone or leaves an observation zone --- indicate this to
  all other vessels nearby by the means of communication indicated in
  the permit under which the vessel is authorized to operate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 25, the operator of a vessel
  operating under a class 2 permit shall not place the vessel in
  observation mode or enter the observation zone of any vessel.

- SOR/2016-257, s. 15

1927 (NAD27).

The area of the park bounded

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the south by a line approximately 3.65 nautical

    having the geographical coordinates 48˚05′52″ N latitude and
    69˚42′26″ W longitude,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} then to a point near Îlet-aux-Alouettes, having
    the geographical coordinates 48˚06′27″ N latitude and 69˚41′02″ W
    longitude,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} then to a point near buoy S7, having the
    geographical coordinates 48˚07′11″ N latitude and 69˚40′30″ W
    longitude,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} then to a point near buoy S8, having the
    geographical coordinates 48˚07′30″ N latitude and 69˚40′17″ W
    longitude, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} ending at a point near Pointe-aux-Vaches, having
    the geographical coordinates 48˚08′52″ N latitude and 69˚39′58″ W
    longitude; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the north by a line approximately 0.77 nautical

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} commencing at a point near Baie-Sainte-Catherine
    ferry wharf, having the geographical coordinates 48˚07′35″ N
