## [Form and Manner of Publishing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-627133 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) is to continue to be published
electronically in Portable Document Format (PDF).

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-627135 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-627137 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on April 1, 2014.
