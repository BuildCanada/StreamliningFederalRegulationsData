## Eggplants and Tomatoes Production (Central Saanich) Restriction Regulations {#eggplants-and-tomatoes-production-central-saanich-restriction-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-448
:::

[PLANT PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-14.8)

Registration 1982-04-30

Regulations Providing for the Restricting of Property Located in the
Municipality of Central Saanich in the Province of British Columbia that
ss Infested With the Golden Nematode from the Production of Eggplants
and Tomatoes

P.C. 1982-1279 1982-04-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 4 of the Plant
Quarantine Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations
providing for the restricting of property located in the Municipality of
Central Saanich in the Province of British Columbia that is infested
with the Golden Nematode from the production of eggplants and tomatoes.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873673 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Eggplants and Tomatoes Production (Central Saanich)
Restriction Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-448).

## [Restriction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873675 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, no
person shall plant from seed or by transplant, grow, produce or harvest
any eggplants or tomatoes in that portion of the Municipality of Central
Saanich in the Province of British Columbia east of the West Saanich
Road, other than property located in the South Saanich land district in
the Province of British Columbia, namely, all the property included in
Plans 617R, 642R and 643R, Parcel "A" north of Wallace Drive in the
western half of Section 10 and all of Sections 11 and 12 in Range 2
East.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873677 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply to
any person who plants from seed or by transplant, grows, produces or
harvests eggplants or tomatoes if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the eggplants or tomatoes are for his own
  consumption, within the limits of the property on which they were
  harvested and if the property was not used, at any time prior to the
  coming into force of these Regulations, for the production of
  potatoes, eggplants or tomatoes; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the eggplants or tomatoes are produced in artificial
  medium or sterilized soil in a greenhouse located on property that was
  not used, at any time prior to the coming into force of these
  Regulations, for the field production of potatoes, eggplants or
  tomatoes.
