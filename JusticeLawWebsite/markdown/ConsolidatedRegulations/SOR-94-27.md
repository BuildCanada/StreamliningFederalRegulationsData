## Order According Reciprocal Protection to Switzerland under the Act {#order-according-reciprocal-protection-to-switzerland-under-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-27
:::

[INTEGRATED CIRCUIT TOPOGRAPHY ACT](/eng/acts/I-14.6)

Registration 1994-01-11

Order According Reciprocal Protection to Switzerland under the Act
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, pursuant to
subparagraph 4(1)(c)(iii)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5251){#fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5251-ID0EECCA}
of the [Integrated Circuit Topography Act](/eng/acts/I-14.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5252){#fn_SOR-94-27_e_hq_5252-ID0EBCCA},
hereby certifies that Switzerland is a country that confers protection
that is substantially equal to the protection conferred by that Act on
nationals of Canada and individuals or legal entities that have in
Canada a real and effective establishment for the creation of
topographies or the manufacture of integrated circuit products.
