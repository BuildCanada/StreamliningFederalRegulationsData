## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838610 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/C-21). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Member Advisory Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Member Advisory Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Council
    established under section 21.4 of the Act. ([comité consultatif des
    membres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who,
    or an entity that, supports the operation, maintenance and
    development or enhancement of payment systems that directly or
    indirectly interface with the national clearing and settlement
    systems. ([fournisseur de services de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system or arrangement
    for the exchange of messages effecting, ordering, enabling or
    facilitating the making of payments or transfers of value that are
    subsequently cleared and settled as payment items through the
    systems operated by the Association. ([système de
    paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stakeholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stakeholder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, or an entity
    that, is a user or a payment service provider or represents the
    interests of a group of users or payment service providers, but is
    not a member or entitled to be a member under subsection 4(2) of the
    Act. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Stakeholder Advisory Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Stakeholder Advisory Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Council established under section 21.2 of the Act. ([comité
    consultatif des intervenants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Members of the Association]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838619 .Part}

### [Banks and Authorized Foreign Banks]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838620 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration as member

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association must register every bank and every authorized foreign bank
  as a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every bank, on the day on which it comes into
  existence, and every authorized foreign bank, on the effective date of
  the order permitting it to establish a branch in Canada under
  subsection 524(1) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), must provide
  the Association with the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name and the date on which and method by which
    it came into existence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it intends to commence
    operations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the address of its head office or another address
    --- including an email address --- designated by it as its address
    for the delivery of notices;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of its president or chief
    executive officer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of its directors and senior
    officers;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of its incorporating instrument or
    letters patent;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of its by-laws;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of its audited financial statements for the
    last five fiscal years, or for all of its fiscal years if it has
    existed for less than five years;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its authorization for the Association to contact
    and to receive information about it from the Office of the
    Superintendent of Financial Institutions and, in the case of an
    authorized foreign bank, the regulatory authority in the foreign
    country in which it was incorporated or formed; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} its agreement to provide any other information
    about it that may be required by the Association in connection with
    the objects, business or affairs of the Association.

### [Application for Membership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838636 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every application for
membership in the Association by an applicant that is entitled to be a
member under subsection 4(2) of the Act must include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and the date on which and method
  by which it came into existence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of its head office or another address ---
  including an email address --- designated by it as its address for the
  delivery of notices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of its president or chief
  executive officer;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of its directors and senior
  officers;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of the resolution of its board of
  directors or executive committee authorizing the application;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of its audited financial statements for the
  last five fiscal years, or for all of its fiscal years if the
  applicant has existed for less than five years;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of its charter, letters patent or
  incorporating instrument;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of its by-laws; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} its agreement to provide any other information about
  it that may be required by the Association in connection with the
  objects, business or affairs of the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The applicant must also
provide to the Association,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is subject to a regulatory authority, the name
  of the regulatory authority and the applicant's authorization for the
  Association to contact and receive information about the applicant
  from that regulatory authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is a securities dealer, evidence of its
  membership in one of the organizations referred to in section 2 of the
  [Canadian Payments Association Membership Requirements
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-476) and evidence of
  registration as a securities dealer under provincial laws;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is a qualified corporation, on behalf of its
  money market mutual fund, a copy of the most recent version of its
  prospectus, a copy of the prospectus receipt from a securities
  commission, a copy of its most recently published list of investments
  and a legal opinion certifying that the applicant meets all of the
  membership requirements set out in the Act, the regulations and the
  by-laws;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is a trustee of a qualified trust, a certified
  copy of its trust agreement, a copy of the most recent version of its
  prospectus, a copy of the prospectus receipt from a securities
  commission, a copy of its most recently published list of investments
  and a legal opinion certifying that the applicant meets all of the
  membership requirements set out in the Act, the regulations and the
  by-laws;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if it is a central, a trust company or a loan
  company, a declaration setting out that it accepts deposits
  transferable by order and, if applicable, its confirmation of a
  clearing arrangement; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if it accepts deposits transferable by order, a
  declaration setting out that it accepts deposits transferable by
  order, evidence that the deposits made with the applicant are insured
  or guaranteed under a federal or provincial statute and, if
  applicable, its confirmation of a clearing arrangement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of dues

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The applicant is liable to
pay dues to the Association on the day on which its membership
commences.

### [Change in Circumstances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838658 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification --- change in circumstances

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every member must notify
the Association without delay of any change in circumstances that would
affect their entitlement to be a member under subsection 4(2) of the
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bridge institution

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** A member that is a
[[federal institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2
of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), and is
designated as a bridge institution for another member by an order made
under paragraph 39.13(1)(c) of that Act is deemed, as of the date and
time specified in the order for it to assume that other member's deposit
liabilities, to have, as between itself and all of the other members
except that other member for whom it has been designated as a bridge
institution, acquired all of that other member's rights under the
by-laws and assumed all of that other member's obligations to those
other members under the by-laws, in respect of the exchange, clearing or
settlement of payments.

### [Suspension of Member's Rights]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838661 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  the Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 6 --- Compliance, the
  Board may suspend one or more of the rights of a member if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member has failed on more than one occasion to
    comply with any provision of the Act, the by-laws or the rules;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a liquidator, a trustee in bankruptcy, a receiver,
    a receiver-manager or a sequestrator is appointed by an authorized
    person or a court of competent jurisdiction to take possession of
    all, or any part of, the member's assets or undertakings; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member proposes any arrangement or composition
    with its creditors, invokes any statute in that regard or commits
    any act of bankruptcy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Board must not suspend a member's rights under
  paragraph (1)(b) or (c) if the member is the subject of an order made
  under subsection 39.13(1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to member --- suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must, as soon as feasible, notify the
  member in writing of its decision to suspend the member's rights and
  the reasons for the suspension and, if the Board considers it
  necessary, must notify at the same time the other members in writing
  of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the member requests a review within five days
  after receiving the notice, the Board must review its decision and
  provide the member with an opportunity to make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to member --- review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After the review, the Board must, as soon as
  feasible, notify the member in writing of its decision and the reasons
  for it. The Board must notify at the same time the other members in
  writing of its decision and reasons if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the members were previously notified of the
    suspension under subsection (2) and the suspension was terminated;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the members were not previously notified of the
    suspension under subsection (2) and the suspension was confirmed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to other members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board must notify the other members in writing of
  the suspension if no request for a review is received within the time
  period specified in subsection (3) and the Board did not previously
  notify the other members under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survival of obligation to pay

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite the suspension of a member's rights, the
  member continues to be liable to pay dues and fees to the Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of By-law No. 6

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The suspension of a
member's rights under section 7 does not affect the operation of
Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 6 --- Compliance in respect of

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Board must reinstate

member applies for reinstatement and the reasons for the suspension no
longer exist.

## [Stakeholder Advisory Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838684 .Part}

### [Appointment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838685 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of members

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Stakeholder Advisory Council is to consist of no more than 20 persons,
  of which at least one but no more than two must be elected directors
  of the Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition --- criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 12 persons who are representative of
    users, of which at least two must be representative of consumers, at
    least one must be representative of the retail sector, at least two
    must be representative of the federal and provincial governments and
    at least one must be representative of the treasury and cash
    management services sector; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least one person who is representative of
    payment service providers.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person appointed to
the Stakeholder Advisory Council, other than an elected director of the
Association, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} represent the interests of a particular stakeholder;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} not be a director, officer or employee of a member or
  of a person entitled to be a member under subsection 4(2) of the Act;

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of nominees

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  consultation with the Minister of Finance, the Board must evaluate the
  nomination of every person who is eligible to be appointed to the
  Council and, in doing so, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into consideration whether the stakeholder
    whose interests would be represented by the nominee has other
    established consultative processes for directly articulating that
    stakeholder's perspectives to the Association or to any of its
    working groups or committees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give preference to nominees who represent
    stakeholders whose interests are national in scope;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to nominees who are representative of
    users, give preference to those nominees who represent associations,
    groups, organizations or other entities that represent the interests
    of a broad constituency or, in the absence of such nominees, to
    nominees who represent the interests of a prominent corporation
    within their industry; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to nominees who are representative of
    payment service providers, give preference to nominees who represent
    associations, groups, organizations or other entities that represent
    the common interests of the majority of participants in a particular
    industry or, in the absence of such nominees, to nominees who
    represent the interests of a prominent corporation within their
    industry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after each nominee is evaluated in accordance
  with the requirements of sections 10 and 11 and subsection (1), the
  number of nominees who are qualified exceeds the number of vacancies
  on the Council, the Board may consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the stakeholder whose interests would be
    represented by the nominee is already adequately represented on the
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the business of the stakeholder whose interests
    would be represented by the nominee and its degree of reliance on
    the payment system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the level of research and development with regard
    to payment systems that has been undertaken by the stakeholder whose
    interests would be represented by the nominee;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nominee's affiliations or experience with
    international organizations that have an interest relating to
    payment systems and the extent of those affiliations and that
    experience; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the skills, expertise and experience that the Board
    considers necessary to permit the Council to carry out its
    responsibilities effectively.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term and reappointment

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members of the Stakeholder Advisory
  Council, other than elected directors of the Association, are to be
  appointed for a term of no more than three years and may be
  reappointed for any number of additional terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Council who is an elected director of
  the Association must be appointed for a term fixed by the Board and
  may be reappointed for any number of additional terms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** On the recommendation of
the chairperson of the Stakeholder Advisory Council, and after
consultation with the Minister of Finance, the Board may remove a
Council member, other than an elected director of the Association, from
office if the Council member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been absent without just cause from three
  consecutive meetings of the Council;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} represents the interests of a stakeholder that has
  ceased to exist or is no longer a stakeholder; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the eligiblity criteria that are set
  out in the Act or this By-law.

### [Cessation of Membership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838721 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When membership ceases

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A person ceases to be a
member of the Stakeholder Advisory Council when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their term expires;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are unable to act due to an extended incapacity
  or illness;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are removed from office under section 14;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they cease to be an elected director of the
  Association; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} they resign, in which case their resignation takes
  effect on the later of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which their resignation is given to the
    Board, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day specified in their resignation.

### [Vacancy on Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838731 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If a vacancy occurs
because an elected director of the Association ceases to be a member of
the Stakeholder Advisory Council before their term would have expired,
and if no other Council member is an elected director, the Board must
appoint an elected director to the Council for the remainder of that
term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Council members

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  vacancy occurs because a member of the Council, other than an elected
  director of the Association, is removed under paragraph 14(a) or (c)
  or ceases to be a member under paragraph 15(b) or (e),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the vacancy occurs three months
    or more before the day on which their term would have expired, the
    stakeholder whose interests were represented by that Council member
    may nominate a substitute for the remainder of that term; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the vacancy must not be filled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No stakeholder

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a vacancy occurs because a member of the Council
  is removed under paragraph 14(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the vacancy occurs one year or
    more before the day on which their term would have expired, the
    Board may appoint a substitute for the remainder of that term; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the vacancy must not be filled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitute appointed by Board

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the stakeholder mentioned in paragraph (1)(a)
  fails to nominate a substitute within the period specified in the
  notice of vacancy, the Board may appoint a substitute.

## [Member Advisory Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838745 .Part}

### [Appointment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838746 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of members

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Member Advisory Council must consist of no more than 20 members, of
  which number no more than two may be elected directors of the
  Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition --- criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Council must include at least six members who are
  representative of direct participants, at least two members who are
  representative of group clearers and at least four members who are
  representative of indirect participants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in subsection (2).

  [[direct participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[direct participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[direct clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
        defined in section 1 of the Canadian Payments Association By-law
        No. 3 --- Payment Items and Automated Clearing Settlement
        System;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-182, s. 56\]]{.Repealed}

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
        defined in section 1 the Canadian Payments Association By-law
        No. 9 --- Lynx, except a group clearer; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a party to any clearing and settlement system,
        payment system or system or arrangement that is established or
        operated by the Association, except

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [[Automated Clearing Settlement
          System]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
          Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 3 --- Payment Items
          and Automated Clearing Settlement System,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-182, s.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility --- criteria

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person appointed to the Member Advisory Council, other an elected
  director of the Association, must be a director, officer or employee
  of a member and must represent the interests of that member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The members of the Council must have, as a group, the
  skills, expertise and experience that the Board considers necessary to
  permit the Council to carry out its responsibilities effectively.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term and reappointment

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members of the Member Advisory
  Council must be appointed for a term of no more than three years and
  may be reappointed for any number of additional terms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a member of the Council who
  is an elected director of the Association must be appointed for a term
  fixed by the Board and may be reappointed for any number of additional
  terms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** On the recommendation of
the chairperson of the Member Advisory Council, the Board may remove a
Council member, other than an elected director of the Association, from
office if the Council member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been absent without just cause from three
  consecutive meetings of the Council; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the eligibility criteria that are set
  out in the Act or in this By-law.

### [Cessation of Membership]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838774 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When membership ceases

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A person ceases to be a
member of the Member Advisory Council when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their term expires;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they are unable to act due to an extended incapacity
  or illness;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they are removed from office under section 21;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they cease to be an elected director of the
  Association;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} they cease to be a director, officer or employee of
  the member whose interests they represent; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} they resign, in which case their resignation takes
  effect on the later of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which their resignation is given to the
    Board, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day specified in their resignation.

### [Vacancy on Council]{.HTitleText2} {#h-838785 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elected directors

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If a vacancy occurs
because an elected director of the Association ceases to be a member of
the Member Advisory Council before their term would have expired, the
Board may appoint an elected director to the Council for the remainder
of that term.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Council members

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  vacancy occurs because a member of the Council, other than an elected
  director of the Association, ceases to be a member under section 22,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the vacancy occurs three months
    or more before the day on which their term would have expired, the
    member whose interests were represented by that Council member may
    nominate a substitute for the remainder of that term; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the vacancy must not be filled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitute appointed by Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the member mentioned in paragraph (1)(a) fails to
  nominate a substitute within the period specified in the notice of
  vacancy, the Board may appoint a substitute.
