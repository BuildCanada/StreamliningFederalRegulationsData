## Order Transferring to the Department of Supply and Services the Control and Supervision of the Government Telecommunications Agency and the Translation Bureau and Transferring to the Minister of Supply and Services the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Public Works and Amalgamating and Combining the Department of Supply and Services and the Department of Public Works Under the Minister of Supply and Services {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-supply-and-services-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-government-telecommunications-agency-and-the-translation-bureau-and-transferring-to-the-minister-of-supply-and-services-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-public-works-and-amalgamating-and-combining-the-department-of-supply-and-services-and-the-department-of-public-works-under-the-minister-of-supply-and-services .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-138
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring to the Department of Supply and Services the Control
and Supervision of the Government Telecommunications Agency and the
Translation Bureau and Transferring to the Minister of Supply and
Services the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Public
Works and Amalgamating and Combining the Department of Supply and
Services and the Department of Public Works Under the Minister of Supply
and Services

P.C. 1993-1484  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to transfer

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Department of Supply and Services the
    control and supervision of the following portions of the public
    service, namely

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the Government Telecommunications Agency in the
      Department of Communications, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the Translation Bureau, consisting of those
      portions of the Office of the Assistant Under-Secretary of State
      (Official Languages --- Translation) and the Office of the
      Assistant Under-Secretary of State (Social Development and
      Regional Operations), in the Department of the Secretary of State
      of Canada relating to translation and related services, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Supply and Services all of the
    powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Public Works under
    any Act of Parliament; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, to amalgamate
  and combine the Department of Supply and Services and the Department
  of Public Works under the Minister of Supply and Services and under
  the Deputy Minister of Supply and Services.
