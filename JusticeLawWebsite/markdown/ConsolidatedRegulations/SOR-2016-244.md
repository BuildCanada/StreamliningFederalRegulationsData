Definition of [[Conference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Conference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
conference known as the Global Fund Replenishment Conference to fight
AIDS, Tuberculosis and Malaria, to be held in Montréal, Quebec from
September 15 to 17, 2016.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileges and Immunities ---
representatives

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} From
  September 15 to 17, 2016, representatives of foreign states that
  participate in the Conference shall have in Canada, to the extent
  required for the exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to
  the Conference, the privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs
  11(b) and (e) and Sections 14 to 16 of Article IV of the Convention on
  the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in
  Schedule III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
  Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileges and Immunities --- heads of
  state

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of foreign states that participate in
  the Conference and who are acting in their official capacity as a head
  of state, a head of government or a minister shall have in Canada,
  during their journey to and from the Conference, the privileges and
  immunities set out in paragraph 11(d) of Article IV of the Convention
  on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in that
  Schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileges and immunities --- heads of
  international organizations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Heads of international organizations that participate
  in the Conference shall have in Canada, during their journey to and
  from the Conference, the privileges and immunities set out in
  paragraph 18(d) of Article V of the Convention on the Privileges and
  Immunities of the United Nations set out in that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into Force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
