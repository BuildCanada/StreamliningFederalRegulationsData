## Calculation of Interest Regulations {#calculation-of-interest-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-631
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 1987-10-29

Regulations Prescribing the Manner of Determining Interest for the
Purpose of Subsections 64.1(1) and (2) of the Indian Act

P.C. 1987-2235 1987-10-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 64.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
prescribing the manner of determining interest for the purpose of
subsections 64.1(1) and (2) of the Indian
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-631), effective November 1, 1987.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910161 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Calculation of Interest
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-631).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910163 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[principal amount outstanding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[principal amount outstanding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    amount received by a person under paragraph 15(1)(a) of the Act, as
    it read immediately prior to April 17, 1985, or under any former
    provision of the Act relating to the same subject-matter as that
    paragraph, less the sum of one thousand dollars and less the amounts
    withheld under subsection 64.1(1) of the Act and the amounts repaid
    under subsection 64.1(2) of the Act in respect of that person;
    ([capital à rembourser]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reinstatement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reinstatement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entering of the name
    of a person referred to in subsection 64.1(1) or (2) of the Act in a
    Band List. ([réintégration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910168 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
persons referred to in subsections 64.1(1) and (2) of the Act who make
an application for reinstatement.

## [Calculation of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910170 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsections 64.1(1) and (2) of the Act, interest shall be calculated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the principal amount outstanding only;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} quarterly on the average month-end balance of the
  principal amount outstanding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at a rate equal to the quarterly average of the
  interest rates applied by the Bank of Canada to Government of Canada
  Treasury bills of terms of six months; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} from the date referred to in section 5.

## [Accrual of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910176 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  reinstatement of a person referred to in paragraph 11(1)(c) or
  11(2)(a) of the Act is made, interest accrues from the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} November 1, 1987; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the date of signature on
    the application for reinstatement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is a difference of more than 30 days
  between the date of signature on an application for reinstatement and
  the date on which the application is received by the Registrar,
  interest shall accrue from the date on which the application is
  received by the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where reinstatement of a person is made pursuant to
  membership rules made pursuant to section 10 of the Act, interest
  accrues from the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} November 1, 1987; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of reinstatement of the person.

## [Method of Applying Moneys Withheld or Repaid]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910185 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an amount is withheld
under subsection 64.1(1) of the Act or is repaid under
subsection 64.1(2) of the Act, the amount shall be applied first against
the principal amount outstanding, and second against accrued interest.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
6, the effective date on which an amount is applied shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount is withheld pursuant to
  subsection 64.1(1) of the Act, the date that the person would have
  been entitled to receive the amount but for that subsection; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the amount is repaid pursuant to
  subsection 64.1(2) of the Act, the date that the amount is received by
  the band.
