## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784205 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784207 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Jordan are considered to originate in that country and are
entitled to the benefit of the Jordan Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
  marking laws of Jordan and indicates that the goods are the product of
  Jordan or Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and there is no
  indication that the goods are not the product of Jordan or Canada.
