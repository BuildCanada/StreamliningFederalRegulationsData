## Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3 {#privacy-act-extension-order-no.-3 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-174
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 2021-07-14

Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 3

P.C. 2021-725 2021-07-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada in Council,
on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection
12(3) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4561_hq_29053){#fn_81000-2-4561_hq_29053-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Privacy Act Extension Order, No.
3](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-174).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of right of access

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The right to be given
access to personal information under subsection 12(1) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21) is extended to include all individuals outside
Canada to whom that right has not been extended previously.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the first anniversary of the day on which it is made.
