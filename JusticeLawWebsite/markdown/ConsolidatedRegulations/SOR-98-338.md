P.C. 1998-1015  1998-06-11

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 115 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022530 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[coasting trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coasting trade]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means coasting trade as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Coasting Trade
    Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3). ([cabotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[coasting trade licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coasting trade licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Coasting Trade
    Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3). ([licence de cabotage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Alaska Marine Lines, Inc.
    ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel as defined in section
    2 of the [Vessel Duties Reduction or Removal
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304).
    ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022536 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to the importer in an amount equal to 97 per
cent of the customs duties paid or payable pursuant to the [Vessel
Duties Reduction or Removal Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304) on
the temporary importation of the vessels engaged in coasting trade, for
up to a maximum of 15 months.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022538 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
section 2 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is operating under a coasting trade
  licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is imported into Canadian waters during
  the period beginning October 1, 1996 and ending December 31, 1997;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the day on which this Order
  comes into force; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides to the Minister of National
  Revenue evidence or information that demonstrates that the importer is
  entitled to a remission under this Order.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022544 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on June 11, 1998.
