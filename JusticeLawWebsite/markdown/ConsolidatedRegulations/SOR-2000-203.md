## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656745 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656746 .Subheading}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[absorbed dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[absorbed dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quotient, in gray,
      obtained by dividing the energy absorbed through exposure to
      radiation by the mass of the body or part of the body that absorbs
      the radiation. ([dose absorbée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
      Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[balance of the pregnancy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[balance of the pregnancy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period
      from the moment a licensee is informed, in writing, of the
      pregnancy to the end of the pregnancy. ([reste de la
      grossesse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[caregiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[caregiver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who willingly and
      voluntarily --- and not as an occupation --- helps in the support
      and comfort of a person who has been administered a nuclear
      substance for therapeutic purposes as directed by a medical
      practitioner who is qualified to give such direction under the
      applicable provincial legislation. ([personne
      soignante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[committed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[committed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a dose of
      radiation, received by an organ or tissue from a nuclear substance
      during the 50 years after the substance is taken into the body of
      a person 18 years old or older or during the period beginning at
      intake and ending at age 70, after it is taken into the body of a
      person less than 18 years old. ([engagée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[dosimeter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dosimeter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device for measuring a
      dose of radiation that is worn or carried by an individual.
      ([dosimètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum of the
      products, in sievert, obtained by multiplying the equivalent dose
      of radiation received by and committed to each organ or tissue set
      out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 1 by the weighting factor
      set out in column 2 of that item. ([dose
      efficace]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the product, in
      sievert, obtained by multiplying the absorbed dose of radiation of
      the type set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 2 by the
      weighting factor set out in column 2 of that item. ([dose
      équivalente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exemption quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207). ([quantité
      d'exemption]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[five-year dosimetry period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[five-year dosimetry period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      period of five calendar years beginning on January 1 of the year
      following the year in which these Regulations come into force, and
      every period of five calendar years after that period. ([période
      de dosimétrie de cinq ans]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
      described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (f) of the Act that a
      licence authorizes the licensee to carry on. ([activité
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
      carry on an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a) to (f)
      of the Act. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[one-year dosimetry period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[one-year dosimetry period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period
      of one calendar year beginning on January 1 of the year following
      the year in which these Regulations come into force, and every
      period of one calendar year after that period. ([période de
      dosimétrie d'un an]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[radon progeny]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[radon progeny]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following
      radioactive decay products of radon 222: bismuth 214, lead 214,
      polonium 214 and polonium 218. ([produit de filiation du
      radon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the layer of cells within the
      skin that are 7 mg/cm^2^ below the surface.
      ([peau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work
      that is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[working level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[working level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-237, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[working level month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[working level month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2020-237, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of the definition [[dosimetry
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act, a dosimetry
  service is a facility that is licensed by the Commission to measure
  and monitor radiation doses.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of the definition [[nuclear energy
  worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act, the prescribed
  limit for the general public is 1 mSv per calendar year.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656768 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply generally for the purposes
  of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a licensee in
  respect of a dose of radiation received by or committed to a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the course of the person's examination,
    diagnosis or treatment, as directed by a medical practitioner who is
    qualified to examine, diagnose or treat the person under the
    applicable provincial legislation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a result of the person's voluntary participation
    in a biomedical research study supervised by a medical practitioner
    who is qualified to provide such supervision under the applicable
    provincial legislation; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} while the person is acting as a caregiver.

## [Obligations of Licensees and Nuclear Energy Workers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656777 .Part}

### [Administration of Nuclear Substance for Medical Purposes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656778 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** When a nuclear substance is
administered to a person for therapeutic purposes, the licensee must
inform the person of methods for reducing the exposure of others ---
including the caregiver or anyone else providing care and assistance ---
to radiation from the person.

### [Radiation Protection Program]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656782 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee must
implement a radiation protection program and must, as part of that
program,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep the effective dose and equivalent dose received
  by and committed to persons as low as reasonably achievable, taking
  into account social and economic factors, through the implementation
  of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} management control over work practices,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} personnel qualification and training,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} control of occupational and public exposure to
    radiation, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} planning for unusual situations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ascertain the quantity and concentration of any
  nuclear substance released as a result of the licensed activity

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by direct measurement as a result of monitoring, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the time and resources required for direct
    measurement as a result of monitoring outweigh the usefulness of
    ascertaining the quantity and concentration using that method, by
    estimating them.

### [Ascertainment and Recording of Doses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656792 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of keeping a record of doses of radiation in accordance with
  section 27 of the Act, every licensee must ascertain and record the
  magnitude of exposure to radon progeny of each person referred to in
  that section, as well as the effective dose and equivalent dose
  received by and committed to that person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must ascertain the magnitude of exposure
  to radon progeny and the effective dose and equivalent dose

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by direct measurement as a result of monitoring; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the time and resources required for direct
    measurement as a result of monitoring outweigh the usefulness of
    ascertaining the amount of exposure and doses using that method, by
    estimating them.

### [Action Levels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656798 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[action level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specific dose of
  radiation or other parameter that, if reached, may indicate a loss of
  control of part of a licensee's radiation protection program and
  triggers a requirement for specific action to be taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a licensee becomes aware that an action level
  referred to in the licence for the purpose of this subsection has been
  reached, the licensee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct an investigation to establish the cause for
    reaching the action level;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and take action to restore the
    effectiveness of the radiation protection program implemented in
    accordance with section 4; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the Commission within the period specified
    in the licence.

### [Provision of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656805 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee must inform each nuclear energy worker, in writing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the fact that the worker is a nuclear energy
    worker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the risks associated with radiation to which the
    worker may be exposed in the course of their work;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of the applicable effective dose limits and
    equivalent dose limits prescribed by sections 13 to 15;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} of the worker's radiation dose levels, received on
    an annual basis; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} of the worker's responsibilities during an
    emergency and the risks associated with radiation to which the
    worker may be exposed during the control of an emergency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee must inform each female nuclear energy
  worker, in writing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the risks associated with the exposure of
    embryos and fetuses to radiation and the risks to breastfed infants
    from the intake of nuclear substances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the importance of informing the licensee, as
    soon as feasible, in writing, that the female nuclear energy worker
    is pregnant or breastfeeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of the rights of a pregnant nuclear energy worker
    and the rights of a breastfeeding nuclear energy worker under
    section 11; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} of the applicable effective dose limits for
    pregnant nuclear energy workers prescribed by section 13.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee must obtain from each nuclear energy
  worker who is informed of the matters referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
  and (b) and subsection (2) a written acknowledgement that the worker
  has received the information.

### [Requirement to Use Licensed Dosimetry Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656814 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee must use a licensed dosimetry service to measure and monitor
  the doses of radiation received by and committed to nuclear energy
  workers who have a reasonable probability of receiving

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an effective dose that is greater than 5 mSv in a
    one-year dosimetry period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an equivalent dose to the skin, or the hands and
    feet, that is greater than 50 mSv in a one-year dosimetry period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee referred to in subsection (1) must
  provide the following information to the licensed dosimetry service
  with respect to each nuclear energy worker referred to in subsection
  (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the worker's given names, surname and any previous
    surname;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the worker's Social Insurance Number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the worker's gender;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the worker's job category; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date, province and country of birth of the
    worker.

### [Pregnant and Breastfeeding Nuclear Energy Workers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656827 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  being informed by a female nuclear energy worker, in writing, that she
  is pregnant, the licensee must, in order to comply with section 13,
  make any accommodation that will not result in costs or business
  inconvenience constituting undue hardship to the licensee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On being informed by a female nuclear energy worker,
  in writing, that she is breastfeeding an infant, the licensee must, in
  order to limit intakes of nuclear substances by the worker, make any
  accommodation to the working conditions that will not result in costs
  or business inconvenience constituting undue hardship to the licensee.

### [When Dose Limit Exceeded]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656896 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** When a licensee becomes
aware that a dose of radiation received by or committed to a person or
an organ or tissue may have exceeded an applicable dose limit prescribed
by section 13 or 14, the licensee must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately notify the person and the Commission of
  the dose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the person to leave any work that is likely
  to add to the dose if the person may have or has received a dose that
  exceeds a dose limit for a nuclear energy worker;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} conduct an investigation to determine the magnitude
  of the dose and to establish the causes of the exposure;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} identify and take any action required to prevent the
  occurrence of a similar incident; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} within 21 days after becoming aware that the dose
  limit has been exceeded, report to the Commission the results of the
  investigation or the progress that has been made in conducting it.

## [Labelling and Signs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656926 .Part}

### [Labelling of Containers and Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-656927 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall possess a container or device that contains a nuclear
  substance unless the container or device is labelled with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the radiation warning symbol set out in Schedule 3
    and the words "[RAYONNEMENT]{.otherLang lang="fr"} --- DANGER ---
    RADIATION"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity, date of measurement and form of
    the nuclear substance in the container or device.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  container or device

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is an essential component for the operation of
    the nuclear facility at which it is located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is used to hold nuclear substances for current
    or immediate use and is under the continuous direct observation of
    the licensee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in which the quantity of nuclear substances is less
    than or equal to the exemption quantity; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that is used exclusively for transporting nuclear
    substances and labelled in accordance with the [Packaging and
    Transport of Nuclear Substances Regulations,
    2015](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-145); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that contains a radium luminous compound, provided
    that radium is the only nuclear substance in the device and the
    device is intact and has not been tampered with.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in respect of a
  container that is used to temporarily hold nuclear substances.
