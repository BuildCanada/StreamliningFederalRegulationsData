## Chatham Airport Zoning Regulations {#chatham-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-173
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1991-02-28

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Chatham Airport

P.C. 1991-349 1991-02-28
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EHACA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning
Regulations respecting Chatham Airport, substantially in the form set
out in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on July 30th and
August 6th, 1988, and in two successive issues of the Chatham Daily News
on September 2nd and 3rd, 1988, and in two successive issues of the
Blenheim News-Tribune on August 31st and September 7th, 1988, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4^\*^
of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Chatham
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-173).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EHACA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 33
  (1st Supp.)

:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933087 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Chatham Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-173).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933089 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chatham Airport, in the
      Township of Raleigh, in the Province of Ontario;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the Airport Reference Point is 196.3 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933100 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933102 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Aeronautical Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933107 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used or developed in a manner that causes interference with
any signals or communications to and from any aircraft or to and from
any facilities used to provide services relating to aeronautics.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-673, s. 1
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933111 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require
that the owner or lessee of the land on which that object is growing
remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-673, s. 2
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933115 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-673, s. 3
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Chatham Airport Zoning Plan
No. 36-011 86-08, Sheet 5, dated January 28, 1987, is a point lying
perpendicular distant 50 m northwesterly from a point on the centre line
of runway 05-23 and distant 761.75 m northeasterly along the said centre
line from the southwesterly end of the said runway.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Chatham Airport Zoning Plan No. 36-011
86-08, Sheets 4, 5 and 6, dated January 28, 1987, are surfaces abutting
each end of the strip associated with the runway designated 05-23 and
are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 05 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 800 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 345 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 45 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 23 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 792 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 343.8 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 44.8 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Chatham Airport Zoning Plan No. 36-011
86-08, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, dated January 28, 1987, is an imaginary
surface located at a common plane established at a constant elevation of
45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point, except that,
where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above
the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 05-23, shown on Chatham Airport Zoning
Plan No. 36-011 86-08, Sheet 5, dated January 28, 1987 is 150 m in
width, 75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
1 644 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Chatham Airport Zoning Plan
No. 36-011 86-08, Sheets 4, 5 and 6, dated January 28, 1987, is a
surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and projected centre line of the strip and extending upward
and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface.
