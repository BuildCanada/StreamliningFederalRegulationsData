## Tariff of Costs {#tariff-of-costs .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-308
:::

[EXPROPRIATION ACT](/eng/acts/E-21)

Registration 1999-07-09

Tariff of Costs

P.C. 1999-1311  1999-07-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works and Government Services and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to subsection 10(9) of the [Expropriation
Act](/eng/acts/E-21), hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 1980-3/2414 of
  September 5, 1980; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prescribes the annexed [Tariff of
  Costs](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-308).
:::

## [Amounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031429 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 10(9) of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), the
following maximum amounts are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each day that counsel, representing the objector,
  is reasonably required to attend the hearing, \$600;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the reasonable accommodation and travelling
  expenses of the objector for each day that he or she is reasonably
  required to attend the hearing, \$140; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the actual and reasonable costs of written or
  prepared materials, if any, used by the objector or his or her counsel
  in the assertion of the objection at the hearing, \$200.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031434 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Tariff comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
