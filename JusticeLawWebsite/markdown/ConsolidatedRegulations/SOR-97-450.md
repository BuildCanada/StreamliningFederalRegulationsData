## Commission for Environmental Cooperation Privileges and Immunities in Canada Order {#commission-for-environmental-cooperation-privileges-and-immunities-in-canada-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-450
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1997-09-23

Commission for Environmental Cooperation Privileges and Immunities in
Canada Order

P.C. 1997-1346 1997-09-23
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Minister of Finance, pursuant to
paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (d) and (f) to (h) and subsections
5(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCAA}
and (3) of the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Commission for Environmental Cooperation Privileges and
Immunities in Canada Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-450).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009379 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Foreign Missions and
    International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Headquarters Agreement
    between the Government of Canada and the Commission that was signed
    by Canada at Gatineau on October 5, 2020 and by the Commission at
    Ottawa on October 5, 2020. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission for
    Environmental Cooperation that was established by Canada, the United
    States and the United Mexican States on January 1, 1994 and is
    continued under the Agreement on Environmental Cooperation among the
    Governments of Canada, the United States of America, and the United
    Mexican States, that was signed by Canada at Ottawa on December 18,
    2018 and that came into force on July 1,
    2020. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the Act. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretariat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretariat of the
    Commission. ([Secrétariat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the Act.
    ([Convention de Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009387 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and, to the extent specified in Articles 3 to 7 of the
  Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Articles II and
  III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of foreign states who are members of
  the Council of the Commission shall have in Canada, to the extent
  specified in Article 8 and paragraph 1 of Article 13 of the Agreement,
  the privileges and immunities comparable to those accorded to
  diplomatic representatives in Canada under the Vienna Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Executive Director and the Directors of the
  Secretariat, and members of their families forming part of their
  households, shall have in Canada, to the extent specified in Article 9
  and paragraph 2 of Article 13 of the Agreement, privileges and
  immunities comparable to those accorded in Canada under the Vienna
  Convention to diplomatic agents and members of their families forming
  part of their households.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Other officials of the Secretariat shall have in
  Canada, to the extent specified in Article 10 and paragraph 2 of
  Article 13 of the Agreement, the following privileges and immunities
  set out in Section 18 of Article V of the Convention:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from legal process in respect of words
    spoken or written and all acts performed by them in their official
    capacity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} exemption from taxation on the salaries and
    emoluments paid to them by the Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immunity from national service obligations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} together with their spouses and relatives dependent
    on them, immunity from immigration restrictions and alien
    registration;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the same privileges in respect of exchange
    facilities as are accorded to the officials of comparable ranks
    forming part of diplomatic missions; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the right to import free of duty their furniture
    and effects at the time of first taking up their post in Canada.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Commission shall
  have in Canada, to the extent specified in Article 11 and paragraph 3
  of Article 13 of the Agreement, the following privileges and
  immunities set out in Article VI of the Convention:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from personal arrest or detention and from
    seizure of their personal baggage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of words spoken or written and acts done
    by them in the course of the performance of their mission, immunity
    from legal process of every kind, which immunity shall continue to
    be accorded notwithstanding that the persons concerned are no longer
    employed on missions for the Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} inviolability for all papers and documents; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the same immunities and facilities in respect of
    their personal baggage as are accorded to diplomatic envoys.
