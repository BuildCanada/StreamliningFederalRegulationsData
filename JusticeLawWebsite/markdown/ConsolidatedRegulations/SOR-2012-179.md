## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784171 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the Hashemite Kingdom of
Jordan, signed on June 28, 2009, have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4-1 and 4-2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Article 4-3, paragraph 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4-4 to 4-13;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Article 4-15; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Annex 4-1.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784178 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndCE58_hq_13231){#fn_IndCE58_hq_13231-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 32 of the [Canada--Jordan Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.45), chapter 18 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012,
comes into force.
