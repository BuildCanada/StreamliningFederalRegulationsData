## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[methenamine tablet test standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[methenamine tablet test standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Canadian General Standards Board standard CAN/CGSB-4.2 No.
    27.6-2015, entitled Textile Test Methods -- Flame Resistance ---
    Methenamine Tablet Test for Textile Floor Coverings, published in
    September 2015. ([norme sur l'essai à la tablette de
    méthénamine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[removal of non-permanent flame-retardant treatments standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[removal of non-permanent flame-retardant treatments
    standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian General Standards
    Board standard CAN/CGSB-4.2 No. 30.2-M90, entitled Textile Test
    Methods -- Procedure for the Removal of Non-Permanent
    Flame-Retardant Treatments on Textile Floor Coverings, published in
    January 1990. ([norme sur le mode de suppression des agents
    ignifuges non permanents]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sampling plans standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sampling plans standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
    General Standards Board standard CAN/CGSB-4.155-M88, entitled
    Flammability of Soft Floor Coverings --- Sampling Plans, published
    in April 1988 and reaffirmed in November 2013. ([norme sur les plans
    d'échantillonnage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile floor covering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile floor covering]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a
    one-of-a-kind or Oriental rug, or an underlay, that is made of
    textile fibres. ([revêtement de sol textile]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831754 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carpet tile or large textile floor
covering --- indoor only, or indoor and outdoor

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  cumulative number of specimen failures that occur when a textile floor
  covering that is intended for indoor use only, or indoor and outdoor
  use, and that is either a carpet tile or has a surface area greater
  than 2.16 m^2^ or a linear dimension greater than 1.8 m is tested in
  accordance with subsection (4) or (5), must not be equal to or greater
  than the number set out in the last column of Table 1, entitled Normal
  Sequential Sampling Plan, to the sampling plans standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carpet tile or large textile floor
  covering --- outdoor only

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A textile floor covering that is intended for outdoor
  use only and that is either a carpet tile or has a surface area
  greater than 2.16 m^2^ or a linear dimension greater than 1.8 m must,
  at the time it is advertised or sold, have a label that carries the
  following warning if the cumulative number of specimen failures that
  occur when it is tested in accordance with subsection (4) or (5) is
  equal to or greater than the number set out in the last column of
  Table 1, entitled Normal Sequential Sampling Plan, to the sampling
  plans standard:

  Caution --- Flammable --- Do not use in locations exposed to open
  flame or sources of intense heat. Do not use indoors.

  [Attention --- Inflammable --- Tenir éloigné de la flamme ou d'une
  source de chaleur intense. Ne pas utiliser à l'intérieur.]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small textile floor covering --- indoor
  only, indoor and outdoor, or outdoor only

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A textile floor covering that is intended for indoor
  use only, indoor and outdoor use or outdoor use only and that has a
  surface area equal to or less than 2.16 m^2^ and linear dimensions
  that are all equal to or less than 1.8 m must, at the time it is
  advertised or sold, have a label that carries the warning set out in
  subsection (2) or the following warning if the cumulative number of
  specimen failures that occur when it is tested in accordance with
  subsection (4) or (5) is equal to or greater than the number set out
  in the last column of Table 1, entitled Normal Sequential Sampling
  Plan, to the sampling plans standard:

  Caution --- Flammable --- Do not use in locations exposed to open
  flame or sources of intense heat.

  [Attention --- Inflammable --- Tenir éloigné de la flamme ou d'une
  source de chaleur intense.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test --- not treated with flame
  retardant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A textile floor covering that has not been treated
  with a flame retardant must be tested using a sample that consists of
  48 specimens, in accordance with the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Normal Sequential Sampling Plan set out in the
    sampling plans standard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the methenamine tablet test standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test --- treated with flame retardant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A textile floor covering that has been treated with a
  flame retardant must be tested using a sample that consists of 48
  specimens, in accordance with the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal of non-permanent flame-retardant
    treatments standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Normal Sequential Sampling Plan set out in the
    sampling plans standard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the methenamine tablet test standard.

## [Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831775 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The warnings referred to in
subsections 2(2) and (3) must be clearly and prominently displayed and
be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} printed in letters that are easily legible and of
  equal size and prominence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set apart from any other information or visual images
  on the label.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaged textile floor covering

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a textile
floor covering that is packaged, the required label must be on its
package, unless the label is affixed to the textile floor covering
itself and it is packaged in such a manner that the warning is clearly
visible to the consumer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may import a textile floor covering that is not labelled in accordance
  with these Regulations if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is imported solely for the purpose of resale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or before the date of the importation, the
    person sends to the inspector referred to in subsection (2) a notice
    containing the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement to the effect that they are the
      importer,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of the importation,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the nature and quantity of the textile floor
      covering being imported, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the address of the premises where the textile
      floor covering will be labelled in accordance with these
      Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prior to reselling the textile floor covering, they
    notify the inspector in writing that it has been labelled in
    accordance with these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the notice must be
  sent to the inspector who is located at the place where the textile
  floor covering is to be labelled in accordance with these Regulations
  or, if there is no inspector, to the inspector who is located nearest
  to that place.
