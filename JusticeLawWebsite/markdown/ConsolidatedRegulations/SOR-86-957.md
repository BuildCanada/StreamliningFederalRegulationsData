## Defence Controlled Access Area Regulations {#defence-controlled-access-area-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-957
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1986-09-11

Regulations Respecting Access to, Exclusion from and Safety and Conduct
of Persons in, on or about any Defence Establishment, Work for Defence
or Materiel

P.C. 1986-2078 1986-09-11
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, is pleased hereby, pursuant to
section 12 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), to revoke the
Defence Establishment Trespass Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1047 and,
pursuant to section 231.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA} of the said
Act, to make the annexed [Regulations respecting access to, exclusion
from and safety and conduct of persons in, on or about any defence
establishment, work for defence or
materiel](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-957), in substitution therefor.

  26, s. 64

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899292 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Defence Controlled Access Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-957).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899294 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[controlled access area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled access area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any defence
    establishment, work for defence or materiel and includes any
    restricted area within such place or materiel; ([secteur d'accès
    contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister, the
    Chief of the Defence Staff or the officer in command or person in
    charge of a controlled access area; ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization to enter a
    controlled access area in accordance with section 5;
    ([laissez-passer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security guard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a peace officer,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Corps of Commissionaires,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an officer or non-commissioned member, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an employee or other person engaged directly or
      indirectly by the Canadian Forces or the Department

    to whom a designated authority has assigned duties relating to the
    enforcement of these Regulations. ([garde de
    sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899305 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all persons except those who are subject to the Code of Service
Discipline.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Access to and Exclusion from a Controlled Access Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899307 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every person is prohibited
from entering, exiting or remaining in or on any controlled access area
except in compliance with these Regulations.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall enter a controlled access area without first obtaining a
  pass where a designated authority requires that a pass be obtained
  prior to entry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a designated authority or a security guard
  authorized by the designated authority to grant passes has reasonable
  grounds to believe that it is necessary for the purpose of maintaining
  security or preserving the routine, administration or order in, on or
  about a controlled access area, the designated authority or the
  security guard may delay the issue of or refuse to grant a pass and
  may withhold or revoke a pass.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision
  of these Regulations, where a designated authority has reasonable
  grounds to believe that it is necessary for the purpose of maintaining
  security or preserving the routine, administration or order in, on or
  about a controlled access area, the designated authority may prohibit
  any person from entering a controlled access area by notice in writing
  to the person specifying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period for which the prohibition is in effect;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason or reasons for the prohibition.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall enter a controlled access area during
  any period that the person has been prohibited from doing so under
  subsection (1).

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A pass shall be valid only
for the person to whom it is granted and for the purpose, period and
specific controlled access area for which it is granted.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person to whom a pass
is granted, other than a person to whom a pass is granted orally, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} retain it in his possession at all times while in or
  on the controlled access area except when required to surrender it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce it on the demand of a security guard as often
  as may be required while remaining in or on the controlled access
  area;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} surrender it to any security guard and immediately
  leave the controlled access area on the demand of the security guard;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} surrender it in the manner provided for by the
  designated authority on its revocation or expiration.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-686, s. 1(F)
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter and exit a controlled access area only by way
  of an established entrance or exit or such other way as may be
  authorized by a designated authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} comply with every direction given by or under the
  authority of a designated authority, including every written or
  printed direction contained in a pass and any written or printed
  notice, direction, rule, regulation or order displayed in, on or about
  a controlled access area; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately leave a controlled access area on the
  demand of a security guard.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every person found in or
on a controlled access area in contravention of these Regulations may be
removed therefrom by a security guard, but the security guard shall use
only such force as is necessary and that removal shall be without
prejudice to any other proceedings that may be taken.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Search]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899330 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** As a condition of being
given access to any defence establishment, work for defence or materiel,
every person shall, on the demand of a security guard, submit to a
search of his person or personal property while entering or exiting any
such place or materiel or any restricted area within such place or
materiel.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a person refuses to
submit to a search of his person or personal property when required to
do so pursuant to section 11, the person may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person is seeking entry, be denied access; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is exiting, have his person and his
  personal property searched by a security guard who shall use only such
  force as is necessary for that purpose.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Except where there are
reasonable grounds to believe that an immediate search is necessary to
maintain security or to prevent danger to the safety of any person, a
search of a person shall be carried out only by a person of the same
sex.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A security guard may
search without warrant any personal property about a controlled access
area where the security guard has reasonable grounds to believe that the
personal property is or may contain anything that is likely to endanger
the safety of any person within the controlled access area.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Safety and Conduct of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899337 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall break
down, damage, weaken or destroy any gate, fence, post, barrier, building
or structure in or on a controlled access area.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall remove,
obliterate, deface or destroy any written or printed sign, notice,
direction, rule, regulation or order that is posted, attached or affixed
to or on any gate, fence, post, barrier, building or structure in or on
a controlled access area.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Except with the prior
consent of a designated authority, no person shall attach or affix
anything to or on any gate, fence, post, barrier, building or structure
in or on a controlled access area.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall cause or
participate in any disturbance while in, on or about a controlled access
area.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Except with the prior
consent of a designated authority, no person shall convey or cause to be
conveyed alcoholic beverages into, within or from a controlled access
area.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be in an
intoxicated condition in or on a controlled access area.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Except with the prior
consent of a designated authority, no person shall bring into or have on
any controlled access area any photographic equipment or any recording
or transmitting device, whether such device records or transmits images,
sounds, data or other information of any type whatsoever.
