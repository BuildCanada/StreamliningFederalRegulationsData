## Electronic Payments Regulations {#electronic-payments-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-129
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1998-02-23

Electronic Payments Regulations

T.B. 825983-3 1998-02-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 10(f) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Electronic Payments Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-129).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018078 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chargeback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chargeback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to adjust the amount
    payable to or claimed by a financial institution as a result of the
    refusal by the Receiver General to redeem an electronic instruction
    for payment. ([débit compensatoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[digital signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[digital signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the result of the
    transformation of a message by means of a cryptosystem using keys
    such that a person who has the initial message can determine

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the transformation was created using the
      key that corresponds to the signer's key; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the message has been altered since the
      transformation was made. ([signature numérique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[electronic instruction for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic instruction for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    instruction for payment referred to in subsection 35(2) of the Act,
    issued electronically on media or by on-line transfer, to a
    financial institution to credit the account of a particular payee
    with a specific payment. ([ordre de paiement
    électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an institution,
    corporation or other entity, incorporated, continued or formed by or
    under an Act of Parliament or the legislature of a province or under
    the laws of a foreign state or a political subdivision of a foreign
    state, that holds deposits and honours cheques and other payment
    instructions on behalf of its clients and includes a bank, a trust
    company and a cooperative credit society. ([institution
    financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means magnetic tapes, disks, diskettes
    or any other electronic physical storage device.
    ([support]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a payment is to
    be made by means of an electronic instruction for payment.
    ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payment date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which payment
    is to be made to the payee. ([date de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[revoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to issue an instruction to a
    financial institution countermanding a particular electronic
    instruction for payment. ([annuler]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018091 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every payment made out of the Consolidated Revenue Fund by means of an
electronic instruction for payment.

## [Issuing Electronic Instructions for Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018093 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every electronic
instruction for payment must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be issued by or under the direction and control of
  the Receiver General;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the payment reference number,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the payee,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the payee's financial institution number, branch
    number and account number, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the payment date; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} when issued by on-line transfer, be authorized by a
  digital signature.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Receiver General shall
take all necessary measures to ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security of the system used for the transmission
  of electronic instructions for payment to financial institutions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the confidentiality, authenticity and integrity of
  the data while it is under the control of the Receiver General or
  being transmitted to a financial institution; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the security, integrity and safekeeping of the media
  used to issue an electronic instruction for payment while the media
  are under the control of the Receiver General or in transit to a
  financial institution for processing.

## [Authentication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018107 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  media used to issue an electronic instruction for payment must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain internal labels that identify the media by
    setting out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the originating data centre,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the processing data centre,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the file creation date and file number,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of release of the media, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the system, file identification number, volume
      serial number and sequence of the originator's file.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when delivered to a financial institution for
    processing, be accompanied by a transmittal document, signed by the
    Deputy Receiver General or by a person authorized by the Deputy
    Receiver General in writing, that contains the information described
    in subparagraphs (a)(i) to (v).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy Receiver General shall provide to a
  financial institution the names and specimen signatures of all persons
  authorized to sign a transmittal document addressed to that financial
  institution.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every electronic instruction for payment issued by
  on-line transfer shall be acknowledged by the financial institution
  that receives it and every electronic authorization shall be verified
  by the financial institution to ensure the integrity of the
  instruction.

## [Acceptance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018119 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A financial institution
that accepts an electronic instruction for payment shall make the funds
available for withdrawal or other use by the payee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} not later than the opening of business on the payment
  date, if the electronic instruction for payment is received by the
  financial institution before the payment date; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of the electronic instruction for payment,
  if it is received on or after the payment date.

## [Replacement Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018123 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where it has been confirmed
with the applicable financial institution that a payment to be made by
means of an electronic instruction for payment has not been credited by
the payment date to the account designated by the payee, in accordance
with the procedures set out in the agreement with the financial
institution for such designation, a replacement payment may be issued to
the payee in the same amount as the original payment if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the electronic instruction for payment is revoked;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount already paid to the financial institution,
  if any, is recovered through chargeback or by other means.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018127 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018129 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on February 23, 1998.
