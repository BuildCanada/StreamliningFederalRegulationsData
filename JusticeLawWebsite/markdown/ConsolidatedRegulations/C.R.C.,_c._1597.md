## Visiting Forces Attachment and Serving Together Regulations {#visiting-forces-attachment-and-serving-together-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1597
:::

[VISITING FORCES ACT](/eng/acts/V-2)

Regulations Respecting the Attachment and Serving Together of Personnel
under Part VI of the Visiting Forces Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531342 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Visiting Forces Attachment and Serving Together
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1597).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531344 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations
\"Act\" means the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2).

## [Attachment and Serving Together]{.HTitleText1} {#h-531346 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every member of the
Canadian Forces shall, for the purpose of Part VI of the Act, unless the
Chief of the Defence Staff otherwise directs, be placed at the disposal
of the service authorities of any country to which Part VI of the Act
has been declared to be applicable, for the purpose of being attached
temporarily to an armed force of that country, for the period during
which he is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under appropriate arrangements, on the staff of or
  attending any school or training unit,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the staff of or a patient in any hospital or
  convalescent establishment,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appointed to or detailed for duty in any ship,
  vessel, formation, unit, detachment or establishment, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in transit, or awaiting passage, in any ship, vessel
  or other conveyance,

that is comprised in or under the control of an armed force of that
country serving either within or without that country.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every member of an armed
force of any country to which Part VI of the Act has been declared to be
applicable, who is placed at the disposal of the Governor in Council by
the service authorities of that country for the purpose of temporary
attachment to the Canadian Forces shall for the purposes of Part VI of
the Act, unless those authorities otherwise direct, be attached
temporarily to the Canadian Forces, whether serving within or without
Canada, for the period during which he is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under appropriate arrangements, on the staff of or
  attending any school or training unit,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the staff of or a patient in any hospital or
  convalescent establishment,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appointed to or detailed for duty in any formation,
  station, unit or detachment, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in transit, or awaiting passage, in any ship, vessel
  or other conveyance,

that is comprised in or under the control of the Canadian Forces.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The statutes relating to
the Canadian Forces shall apply to every member of another force
attached to the Canadian Forces pursuant to Part VI of the Act and these
Regulations, except that any provision of any such statute respecting
superannuation, pensions or veterans benefits shall not apply to such
member.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where any component, unit
or other element of the Canadian Forces is serving in the same place or
in company with any part of an armed force of any country to which Part
VI of the Act has been declared to be applicable, the component, unit or
other element shall be deemed to be serving together with that part of
the armed forces of that country.
