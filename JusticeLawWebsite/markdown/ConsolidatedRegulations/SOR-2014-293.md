## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Standing Orders.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lounge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lounge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility located in a
    building occupied by the Force where alcohol may be served to any
    person under the Commissioner's jurisdiction, individuals undergoing
    police training and their guests. ([bar]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mess]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place that is operated and
    administered to provide services to members of a mess
    association. ([mess]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police Regulations,
    2014](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-281). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Duties and Functions of Members Who Are Peace Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809817 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional duties and functions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the duties
prescribed by the Act and the Regulations, members who are peace
officers are required to perform duties and functions of an operational,
administrative, scientific or technical nature, including duties in
support of those duties and functions.

## [Messes and Lounges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809820 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mess establishment and dissolution

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the approval of the Commissioner, members may establish, determine
  the membership structure and dissolve a Force mess.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may approve the establishment,
  dissolution and membership structure of Force messes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mess association

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members
  of a mess must establish a mess association, consisting of members of
  the Force organized on the basis of geographical area and rank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mess constitution

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each mess association must establish a mess
  constitution that provides for the operation of the mess and includes
  a provision for the election of a mess committee that is responsible
  for the operation and administration of the mess.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mess membership

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  regular member, other than a special constable, is a member of the
  mess established for the member's rank in the geographical area where
  the member is physically posted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every mess member must observe the mess constitution,
  and pay any fees that it requires, for the mess of which they are a
  member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other members of mess

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Special constable members, civilian members and
  civilian employees may become members of a mess under the terms and
  conditions established by the mess constitution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lounge

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may establish lounges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lounge committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commanding Officer for the division in which a
  lounge is located must establish a lounge committee and appoint its
  members.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Committee responsibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The lounge committee is responsible for directing the
  operation of the lounge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Liability insurance must be
maintained for messes and lounges.

## [Representation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing representation or assistance

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** With the exception of a
Member Representative or a Conduct Authority Representative as defined
in section 29 of the [Commissioner's Standing Orders
(Conduct)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-291), or a staff relations
representative, as referred to in subsection 56(2) of the Regulations,
and subject to subsection 9(1), a person under the Commissioner's
jurisdiction may provide representation or assistance to a member under
subsection 47.1(1) of the Act only if the person has been authorized to
do so by the person who holds the first position of officer or its
equivalent in the person's chain of command.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 47.1(3)(b) of the Act, a person must not
  represent or assist a member under subsection 47.1(1) of the Act in
  any of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is involved as a party, witness,
    participant or interested person in the grievance, in the proceeding
    before a board or in the alleged misconduct that led to the decision
    giving rise to the appeal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could result in a
    conflict of interest;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any representation or assistance could impair the
    efficiency, administration or good government of the Force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to provide representation or
  assistance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who wants to represent or assist a member
  must make a request in writing to the first position of officer or its
  equivalent in the person's chain of command and a decision in response
  to that request must be rendered in writing as soon as feasible. The
  person who renders the decision must cause a copy of it to be served
  on the person who made the request and the member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who represents or assists a member must
  immediately advise the person who holds the first position of officer
  or its equivalent in the representative's chain of command if one of
  the circumstances set out in subsection (1) exists.

## [Redress]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809860 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal of decision on representation or
assistance

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member seeking to be represented or assisted who has been aggrieved by
  the decision referred to in subsection 9(2) may seek redress by means
  of an appeal of the written decision in accordance with the
  [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Grievances and
  Appeals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-289).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redress for other decisions, acts or
  omissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member who is aggrieved by any decision, act or
  omission in the process leading to the decision referred to in
  subsection 9(2) may seek redress by means of an appeal of the written
  decision in accordance with the [Commissioner's Standing Orders
  (Grievances and Appeals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-289).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of appeal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any appeal filed under this section does not suspend
  the grievance, the proceeding before a board or the appeal under
  subsection 45.11(1) or (3) of the Act.
