## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002424 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation or
    other body to whom an airport is sold, leased or otherwise
    transferred and includes a person who acts for or on behalf of that
    corporation or other body. ([administration
    aéroportuaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/98-232, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002431 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), these Regulations apply to a person who
  ceases to be employed in the public service and becomes employed by an
  airport authority as a result of the transfer of an airport to an
  airport authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  subsequently becomes re-employed by the airport authority referred to
  in subsection (1) or after ceasing to be employed by the airport
  authority becomes employed by another airport authority.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who makes
  an election pursuant to paragraph 5(1)(e) of the [Airport Transfer
  (Miscellaneous Matters) Act](/eng/acts/A-10.4) on or after the coming
  into force of these Regulations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 11 do not apply to a person who has
  exercised an option under subsection 3(2).

- SOR/98-232, s. 2
- SOR/2016-203, s. 50(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002439 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12, 13, 13.01 and 16 and subsections 17(1) and (3) of the Act only
  apply to a person on and after the date on which that person ceases to
  be employed by an airport authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), where, on or after April 1,
  1998, a person would be eligible, were it not for these Regulations,
  to exercise an option under subsection 12(3), paragraph 13(7)(a)
  or (b) or section 13.01 of the Act, the person may exercise such an
  option

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in paragraphs 13(7)(a)
    and (b) of the Act, no later than June 20, 1998; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in subsection 12(3) and
    section 13.01 of the Act, no later than the later of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} April 1, 1999, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one year after the date on which the person
      ceases to be employed in the public service and becomes employed
      by the airport authority.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a person exercises an option under subsection
  (2), for the purposes of subsection 12(3), paragraphs 13(7)(a) and (b)
  and section 13.01 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of pensionable service is the period of
    pensionable service to that person's credit at the date on which the
    person ceases to be employed in the public service; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the age of the person is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who ceases to be employed
      in the public service on or after April 1, 1998, the age of that
      person on the date on which the person ceases to be employed in
      the public service, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the age of that person on
      April 1, 1998.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a person exercises an option under subsection
  (2), for the purposes of section 13.01 of the Act and sections 83 to
  99 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who ceases to be employed
    in the public service on or after April 1, 1998, [[valuation
    day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the later of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person ceases to be
      employed in the public service, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person exercises an option
      for a transfer value; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, [[valuation
    date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which the person
    exercises an option for a transfer value.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who has exercised an option under
  subsection (2) is not entitled to exercise an option under
  subsection (1) when the person ceases to be employed by an airport
  authority.

## [Surviving Spouse and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002460 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(8) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by an airport authority are entitled to a
death benefit equal to a return of contributions.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(3) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by an airport authority are entitled to
an allowance as described in paragraphs 12(4)(a) and (b) of the Act,
subject to the limitations set out in subsections 12(4) and (5) of the
Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person during the period that
begins on the date on which the person ceases to be employed in the
public service and that ends on the date on which that person ceases to
be employed by an airport authority is entitled to an allowance under
Part I of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 50(E)

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002466 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act shall begin on the date on which the
person ceases to be employed by an aiport authority.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 and 13 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002468 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with an airport authority that begins on the date on which
the person ceases to be employed in the public service and that ends on
the date on which the person ceases to be employed by that airport
authority.
