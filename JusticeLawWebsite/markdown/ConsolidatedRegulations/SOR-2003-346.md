## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705414 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[ACSS cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ACSS cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    immediately after the final adjustment time on a given business day
    and ending with the final adjustment time on the next business
    day. ([cycle du SACR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Automated Clearing Settlement System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Automated Clearing Settlement System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[ACSS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the system into which direct
    clearers, group clearers, clearing agents and the Bank of Canada
    make entries for the purpose of clearing. ([système automatisé de
    compensation et de règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [SACR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day other than an
    ACSS holiday specified in the rules. ([jour
    ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the reconciliation of payment
    items that were exchanged and the calculation of the clearing
    balances. ([compensation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearing agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearing agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the direct clearer or
    group clearer appointed under subsection 33(1) that, on behalf of an
    indirect clearer, exchanges payment items and either effects
    clearing and settlement or makes entries into the ACSS. ([agent de
    compensation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearing balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearing balance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total of the net
    amounts owing to or by a member as a result of clearing. ([solde de
    compensation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member, other than
    the Bank of Canada, appointed under section 26 that, on its own
    behalf, exchanges payment items and makes entries into the
    ACSS. ([adhérent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the delivery and receipt of
    payment items. ([échange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([coopérative de
    crédit fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[final adjustment time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final adjustment time]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the time, set
    out in the rules, prior to which the correction of errors affecting
    the calculation of the clearing balance of a direct clearer or group
    clearer for the current ACSS cycle must be made. ([heure finale de
    rajustement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[final exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[final exchange]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the last exchange of
    payment items at each regional exchange point for the current ACSS
    cycle. ([échange final]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group described in section 28
    and includes the group clearer. ([groupe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[group clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member that, on its
    own behalf or on behalf of the entities belonging to the group in
    respect of which it is appointed in accordance with section 28,
    exchanges payment items and either effects clearing and settlement
    or makes entries into the ACSS. ([adhérent-correspondant de
    groupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indirect clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indirect clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member on behalf of
    which a clearing agent exchanges payment items and either effects
    clearing and settlement or makes entries into the
    ACSS. ([sous-adhérent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grant to the Bank of Canada
    of security in collateral to guarantee an advance of funds that the
    Bank of Canada may make for the purposes described in section 34.2
    and includes a grant of security in which the Bank of Canada does
    not take possession of the
    collateral. ([nantissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regional exchange point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional exchange point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means each location
    in Canada designated in the rules for the exchange of payment items
    among direct clearers, group clearers and clearing agents, or
    between one of them and the Bank of Canada, and includes the
    National Electronic Settlement Region within the meaning of the
    rules. ([point régional d'échange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the payment of the clearing
    balance. ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Payment Items]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705442 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705443 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- exchanges

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies to the following exchanges:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of payment items giving rise to clearing
    through the ACSS,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exchange among direct clearers, group
      clearers and clearing agents, or between one of them and the Bank
      of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exchange between indirect clearers through
      their respective clearing agent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of payment items not giving rise to
    clearing through the ACSS,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exchange between an indirect clearer and its
      clearing agent,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exchange between indirect clearers that are
      represented by the same clearing agent, through that agent,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the exchange between a group clearer and an
      entity belonging to the group, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the exchange among the entities belonging to a
      group, through the group clearer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- clearing and settlement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part also applies to the clearing of payment
  items that are the object of an exchange referred to in subsection (1)
  and to the resulting settlement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation --- non-member entities

payment items and either effects clearing and settlement or makes
entries into the ACSS on behalf of a local referred to in subparagraph

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Payment Items Acceptable for Clearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705467 .Subheading}

#### [Classes of Payment Items]{.HTitleText3} {#h-705468 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approved payment items

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Paper-based payment items and
  electronic payment items that are specified in the rules and that
  conform to the following requirements may be exchanged for the purpose
  of clearing and settlement:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are drawn on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a member, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a local that belongs to a central or a
      cooperative credit association that is a member; and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-43, s. 56\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are payable on demand,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are identified in accordance with the rules, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} conform to the requirements set out in the
      rules, including those relating to value and value date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment items specified in the rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paper-based payment items and electronic payment
  items that do not conform to the requirements in subsection (1) but
  are specified in the rules and conform to the requirements set out in
  the rules may also be exchanged for the purpose of clearing and
  settlement.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Exchange, Clearing and Settlement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705497 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange irrevocable and irreversible

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every exchange of a
payment item shall become irrevocable and irreversible at the time
specified in the rules or, failing such time, when settlement is
effected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with by-laws and rules

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every member shall
exchange payment items in accordance with the by-laws and the rules and
ensure that its payment items comply with the provisions of the by-laws
and the rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement account --- indirect clearers

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  indirect clearer shall maintain a settlement account with each of its
  clearing agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every indirect clearer shall establish a loan
  facility with each of its clearing agents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement account and loan facility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An indirect clearer may maintain a settlement account
  and establish a loan facility with a central that has been designated
  in accordance with subsection 33(2).

### [DIVISION 5]{.HLabel2}[Return of Payment Items]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705511 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Returns in accordance with the rules

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 14,
payment items, the exchange of which becomes irrevocable and
irreversible when settlement is effected, may be returned after
settlement in accordance with, and for the reasons set out in, the
rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acceptance of returned payment items

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Every member shall accept
payment items returned to it in accordance with the rules.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Automated Clearing Settlement System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705516 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705517 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to the
exchange of payment items giving rise to clearing through the ACSS, and
to the clearing of those payment items and the resulting settlement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of liability

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Neither the Association
nor a director, an officer or an employee is liable for any loss or
damage suffered by a member for anything done or omitted to be done,
honestly and in good faith, in relation to the ACSS, in the
administration or discharge of any powers or duties that under this
By-law are intended or authorized to be executed or performed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Association owns the
ACSS.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}ACSS operated by Association

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The operation, management
and ongoing maintenance of the ACSS form part of the business of the
Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation of ACSS

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 46 and
except in case of a national failure of the ACSS within the meaning of
the rules, the Association shall ensure that the ACSS is operational on
all business days.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Access to information
relating to clearing and to the operation of the ACSS shall be governed
by the rules.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Participation in the ACSS]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705533 .Subheading}

#### [Participants]{.HTitleText3} {#h-705534 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Only direct clearers,
group clearers, clearing agents and the Bank of Canada may make entries
into the ACSS.

#### [Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-705537 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct Clearers Appointment

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 32,
the Board may, on completion by a member of the application procedures
set out in the rules, appoint the member to act as a direct clearer if
the member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintains a settlement account with the Bank of
  Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has established a loan facility with the Bank of
  Canada and has entered into any agreements that may be required by the
  Bank of Canada governing the settlement of the member's clearing
  balance, the administration of the loan facility, the provision by the
  Bank of Canada of advances for ACSS purposes and the pledging of
  collateral to secure advances that may be made under the loan
  facility; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-167, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} meets the technical, financial and other requirements
  set out in the rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Groups and Group Clearers Composition of
group

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  group may consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a central or cooperative credit association that
    has been appointed to be the group clearer and one or more of the
    following, including any combination of them:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} centrals,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit associations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} federal credit unions,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} locals that belong to the group clearer, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} locals that belong to another central or
      cooperative credit association if that central or cooperative
      credit association is also a member of the group; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two or more members from one of the following
    categories that have appointed a member of the category to be the
    group clearer:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} authorized foreign banks and banks, excluding
      federal credit unions,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} trust companies and loan companies,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} securities dealers, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} other members, excluding centrals, cooperative
      credit associations, federal credit unions, qualified
      corporations, trustees of qualified trusts and life insurance
      companies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Central may act for locals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if two or more locals belong
  to two centrals, one of which belongs to the other central, one of the
  centrals may appoint the other central to act as group clearer for
  itself and the locals.

#### [Pledging Collateral]{.HTitleText3} {#h-705609 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of necessary collateral

- **[[34.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Association shall calculate, in
  accordance with the rules, the required amount of the ACSS collateral
  pool and the amount of each direct clearer and group clearer's
  collateral pool pledge. The Association shall periodically recalculate
  these amounts, in accordance with the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pledging collateral

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each direct clearer and group clearer shall pledge
  collateral to the Bank of Canada, in an amount determined by the
  Association in accordance with the rules, within the time and in the
  manner set out in the rules. If the amount of the collateral pool
  pledge increases as a result of a recalculation under subsection (1),
  the direct clearer or group clearer shall pledge the recalculated
  amount, within the time and in the manner set out in the rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valuation by Bank of Canada

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any collateral pledged by a direct clearer or group
  clearer for ACSS purposes is subject to valuation by the Bank of
  Canada at the time the pledge is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insufficient collateral

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, on valuation of the collateral by the Bank of
  Canada, the value assigned by the Bank of Canada to the collateral
  pledged is less than the amount that is required to be pledged, the
  direct clearer or group clearer shall pledge additional collateral.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceeding collateral requirement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the value of the direct clearer or group clearer's
  collateral pledge exceeds the amount that is required to be pledged,
  the direct clearer or group clearer shall, on request for a release
  made to the Bank of Canada, obtain a release of the excess collateral
  from the pledge.

- SOR/2018-16, s. 3
