## Order No. 2 Designating the Tuvaijuittuq Marine Protected Area {#order-no.-2-designating-the-tuvaijuittuq-marine-protected-area .Title-of-Act}

[OCEANS ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.4)

Order No. 2 Designating the Tuvaijuittuq Marine Protected Area
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas this Order designates the Tuvaijuittuq Marine Protected Area in
a manner that is not inconsistent with a land claims agreement that has
been given effect and has been ratified or approved by an Act of
Parliament;

No. 2 Designating the Tuvaijuittuq Marine Protected
Area](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-165) under subsection 35.1(2)[[Footnote

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1486473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land claims agreement
    between the Inuit of the Nunavut Settlement Area and Her Majesty the
    Queen in right of Canada, signed on May 25, 1993, and tabled in the
    House of Commons for the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
    Development on May 26, 1993, and includes any amendments to that
    agreement made pursuant to the agreement. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of the
    sea that is designated in section 2. ([zone de protection
    marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  of the sea in the Arctic Ocean consisting of the waters off northern
  Ellesmere Island, as described in plan number FB42596, certified on
  July 16, 2019 and depicted in plan number CLSR 108395, which plans are
  deposited in the Canada Lands Surveys Records, is designated as the
  Tuvaijuittuq Marine Protected Area. 

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of five metres and the water column, including the
  sea ice, each of which is below the low-water line.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ongoing activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 35.1(2)(a) of the Oceans Act, the following classes of
activities are ongoing activities in the Marine Protected Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} national defence activities carried out by the
  Department of National Defence; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} marine scientific research activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited in the Marine Protected Area to carry out any activity ---
  other than those set out in paragraphs 3(a) and (b) --- that disturbs,
  damages, destroys or removes from the Marine Protected Area any unique
  geological or archeological features or any living marine organism or
  any part of its habitat, or is likely to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the following activities may
  be carried out in the Marine Protected Area:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marine navigation by a foreign national, foreign
    ship or foreign state or an entity incorporated or formed by or
    under the laws of a country other than Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the laying, maintenance and repair of cables and
    pipelines by a foreign state.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application -- Nunavut Agreement

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
with respect to the wildlife harvesting rights of the Inuit as provided
for in the [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}.
