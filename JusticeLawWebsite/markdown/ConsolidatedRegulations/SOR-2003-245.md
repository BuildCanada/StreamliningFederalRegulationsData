[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 453(5) and (6)
of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) do not apply to
a company's acquisition of control of, or a company's acquisition or
increase of a substantial investment in, an entity referred to in
paragraph 453(4)(b) of that Act if the financial intermediary activities
of the entity that expose the entity to material market or credit risk
are limited to providing services exclusively to the company or members
of the company's group.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
