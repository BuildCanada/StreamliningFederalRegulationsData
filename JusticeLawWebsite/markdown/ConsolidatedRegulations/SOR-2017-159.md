## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841084 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Broadcasting
    Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any commercial
    message or programming that promotes a station, network or program
    but it does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a station or network identification;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the announcement of an upcoming program that is
      voiced over credits; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a promotion for a Canadian program or a Canadian
      feature film, even if a sponsor is identified in the title of the
      program or film or as a sponsor of that program or film, as long
      as the identification is limited to the sponsor's name and does
      not include a description, representation or attribute of the
      sponsor's products or services. ([matériel
      publicitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period that begins
    on September 1 of one year and ends on August 31 of the following
    year. ([année de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which a [[Canadian film or video
      production certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      section 125.4 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) has been
      issued; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that qualifies as a Canadian program in
      accordance with the criteria established by the Commission in

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Appendices 1 and 2 to Broadcasting Regulatory
        Policy CRTC 2023-90, dated 23 March 2023 and entitled Change to
        the treatment of stock footage costs as part of the evaluation
        of applications for Canadian program certification, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2023-216, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 128 to 130 of Broadcasting
        Regulatory Policy CRTC 2015-86, dated March 12, 2015, entitled
        Let's Talk TV: The way forward -- Creating compelling and
        diverse Canadian programming. ([émission
        canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an advertisement
    that is intended to sell or promote goods, services, natural
    resources or activities, including by mentioning or displaying in a
    list of prizes the name of a person that is selling or promoting the
    goods, services, natural resources or activities. ([message
    publicitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exempt distribution undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exempt distribution undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    distribution undertaking whose operator is exempt from one or more
    of the requirements of Part II of the Act by an order of the
    Commission made under subsection 9(4) of the Act. ([entreprise de
    distribution exemptée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[key figure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[key figure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a figure formed by a
    combination of alphanumeric characters set out in column 2 of
    Schedule 1 that corresponds to the description of the program set
    out in column 1. ([chiffre clé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means licensed by the
    Commission. ([autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that is licensed to
    carry on a discretionary programming undertaking or a discretionary
    services network. ([titulaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new programming service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a programming
    service that has not been previously distributed in Canada and
    includes a high definition version or a new multiplex of an existing
    programming service. ([nouveau service de
    programmation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program that falls into a
    category set out in item 6, column 1, of Schedule
    1. ([émission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything that is
    broadcast, but does not include visual images, whether or not
    combined with sounds, that consist predominantly of alphanumeric
    characters. ([programmation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Canadian Programs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841107 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to broadcast Canadian programs

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and except as otherwise provided under a condition
  of its licence, a licensee shall devote at least 35% of the time that
  it devotes to broadcasting in a broadcast year to the broadcast of
  Canadian programs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensee that provides third language
  programming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee that provides a third language service shall
  devote at least 15% of the time that it devotes to broadcasting in a
  broadcast year to the broadcast of Canadian programs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of time devoted to broadcasting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the time
  devoted to the broadcasting of a program includes any time devoted to
  advertising material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[third language
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), [[third language
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a programming service that
  provides at least 90% of its programming each broadcast week, the
  first day of which falls on a Sunday, in a language other than English
  or French exclusive of secondary audio programming and subtitles.

## [Programming Content]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841117 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- broadcasting of
programming

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A licensee shall not
broadcast programming that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} anything that contravenes an Act of Parliament or of
  the legislature of a province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any abusive comment or abusive pictorial
  representation that, when taken in context, tends to or is likely to
  expose an individual or a group or class of individuals to hatred or
  contempt on the basis of race, national or ethnic origin, colour,
  religion, sex, sexual orientation, age or mental or physical
  disability; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any false or misleading news.

## [Commercial Messages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to comply with technical
requirements

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee shall ensure that
every commercial message that it broadcasts in a break within a program
or between programs complies with the technical requirements set out in
ATSC Recommended Practice A/85: Techniques for Establishing and
Maintaining Audio Loudness for Digital Television, published by the
Advanced Television Systems Committee Inc., as amended from time to
time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alcoholic beverages

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee may broadcast a commercial message directly or indirectly
  advertising an alcoholic beverage if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sponsor is not prohibited from advertising the
    alcoholic beverage by the laws of the province in which the
    commercial message is broadcast;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the commercial message is not designed to promote
    the general consumption of alcoholic beverages; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the commercial message

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} does not attempt to influence non-drinkers of any
      age to drink or to purchase an alcoholic beverage,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not directed at persons under the legal
      drinking age, does not associate an alcoholic beverage with youth
      or youth symbols and does not portray persons under the legal
      drinking age or persons who could reasonably be mistaken for such
      persons in a context where any such product is being shown or
      promoted,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} does not portray an alcoholic beverage in the
      context of, or in relation to, an activity that is attractive
      primarily to people under the legal drinking age,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} does not contain an endorsement of the alcoholic
      beverage, personally or by implication, either directly or
      indirectly, by any person, character or group who is or is likely
      to be a role model for minors because of their past or present
      position of public trust, special achievement in any field of
      endeavour, association with charities or advocacy activities
      benefiting children, or reputation or exposure in the mass media,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} does not attempt to establish an alcoholic
      beverage as a status symbol, a necessity for the enjoyment of life
      or an escape from life's problems or attempt to establish that
      consumption of the product should take precedence over other
      activities,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} does not imply directly or indirectly that
      social acceptance, social status, personal success, or business or
      athletic achievement may be acquired, enhanced or reinforced
      through consumption of alcohol,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} does not imply directly or indirectly that the
      presence or consumption of alcohol is, in any way, essential to
      the enjoyment of an activity or an event,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} does not portray an alcoholic beverage, or its
      consumption, in an immoderate way,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} does not exaggerate the importance or effect of
      any aspect of an alcoholic beverage or its packaging,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} does not show or use language that suggests, in
      any way, product misuse or product dependency, compulsive
      behaviour, urgency of need or urgency of use,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} does not use imperative language to urge people
      to purchase or consume an alcoholic beverage,

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} does not introduce an alcoholic beverage in
      such a way or at such a time that it may be associated with the
      operation of any vehicle or conveyance requiring skill,

    - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} does not introduce an alcoholic beverage in
      such a way or at such a time that it may be associated with any
      activity requiring a significant degree of skill, care or mental
      alertness or involving an obvious element of danger,

    - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} does not contain inducements to prefer an
      alcoholic beverage because of its higher alcohol content,

    - [(xv)]{.lawlabel} does not refer to the feeling and effect caused
      by alcohol consumption or show or convey the impression, by
      behaviour or comportment, that the people depicted in the message
      are under the influence of alcohol,

    - [(xvi)]{.lawlabel} does not portray any person with an alcoholic
      beverage in situations in which the consumption of alcohol is
      prohibited, and

    - [(xvii)]{.lawlabel} does not contain scenes in which an alcoholic
      beverage is consumed or scenes that give the impression, visually
      or in sound, that it is being or has been consumed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, paragraph (1)(b) does not
  apply in order to prohibit industry, public service or brand
  preference advertising.

## [Political Broadcasts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841151 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation --- allocation of broadcasting
time

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  during an election period, a licensee provides time on its programming
  service for the broadcast of programs, advertisements or announcements
  of a partisan political character, the licensee shall allocate the
  time on an equitable basis to all accredited political parties and
  rival candidates represented in the election or referendum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[election
  period]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[election
  period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a federal or provincial election or
    a federal, provincial or municipal referendum, the period that
    begins on the day on which the announcement of the election or
    referendum is made and that ends on the day on which the election or
    referendum is held; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a municipal election, the period
    that begins two months before the day on which the election is to be
    held and that ends on the day on which the election is held.

## [Non-Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841159 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure obligation ---
distribution of programming services

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee whose programming services are distributed by a licensed
  distribution undertaking or that is negotiating terms of carriage with
  such an undertaking for its programming services, including new
  programming services, shall sign and provide to the licensee of the
  distribution undertaking an agreement that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reproduces the CRTC non-disclosure provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains its consent to comply with the CRTC
    non-disclosure provisions for the benefit of the licensee of the
    distribution undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure obligation ---
  broadcasting of programs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee whose programs are broadcast by a licensed
  video-on-demand undertaking or that is negotiating terms of carriage
  with such an undertaking for its programs shall sign and provide to
  the licensee of the video-on-demand undertaking an agreement that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reproduces the CRTC non-disclosure provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains its consent to comply with the CRTC
    non-disclosure provisions for the benefit of the licensee of the
    video-on-demand undertaking.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[CRTC non-disclosure
  provisions]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[CRTC non-disclosure
  provisions]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} means the non-disclosure provisions
  set out in the appendix to Broadcasting Regulatory Policy CRTC
  2013-578, dated October 31, 2013 and entitled Standard clauses for
  non-disclosure agreements.

## [Logs and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841171 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations --- log or record

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep a program log or record of its programming in
    a form that is acceptable to the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} retain the log or record for a period of one year
    after the day on which the programming was distributed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cause the following information to be entered in
    the log or record each day:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an identification of the licensee or the service
      provided by the licensee,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the time at which advertising material that it
      broadcasts in a break within a program or between programs begins,
      its duration and, in the case of a commercial message, the name of
      the person that is selling or promoting goods, services, natural
      resources or activities,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in relation to each program other than a music
      video clip,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its title and any additional information that
        is to be included in accordance with the appropriate subitem of
        Schedule 1,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the key figure that describes the program,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the time at which the program begins and ends,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the code set out in column 1 of
        Part A, C or D of Schedule 2 that indicates the language, type
        or group of the program described in column 2,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the code set out in column 1 of
        Part B of Schedule 2 that indicates the accessibility of the
        program described in column 2, and

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} if it is required by a condition of the
        licence, a brief description of the content of the program,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in relation to each music video clip,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the title of the clip,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the name of and language used by the performer,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} an indication as to whether the clip is a
        Canadian music video clip, as defined in section V of Appendix I
        to Public Notice 2000-42, dated March 17, 2000, entitled
        Certification for Canadian Programs --- A revised approach,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the key figure that describes the clip, and

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the code set out in column 1 of
        Part B of Schedule 2 that indicates the accessibility of the
        clip described in column 2, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} if the licensee distributes its programming in a
      multi-hour block, the time at which each block begins and ends;
      and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the last day of each month,
    provide to the Commission the log or record of its programming for
    the month and a certificate attesting to the accuracy of the
    contents of the log or record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If more than one subitem applies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of clauses (1)(c)(iv)(B) and
  (1)(c)(v)(D), if more than one subitem of Schedule 1 applies to the
  program, a licensee may, in respect of that program, cause to be
  entered in its program log or record the key figures indicating the
  subitems that apply to each segment of the program, in the order in
  which the segments are distributed, and the starting time and duration
  of each segment of the program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to keep recording of
  programming

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall retain a clear and intelligible
  audio-visual recording of all of its programming

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a period of four weeks after the day on which
    the programming is distributed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period of eight weeks after the day on which
    the programming is distributed, if the Commission receives a
    complaint from a person regarding any programming, or for any other
    reason wishes to investigate the programming, and notifies the
    licensee of the investigation before the end of the four-week
    period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to provide recording to
  Commission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission requests a clear and intelligible
  audio-visual recording of a licensee's programming from the licensee
  before the end of the applicable period referred to in paragraph
  (3)(a) or (b), the licensee shall, without delay, provide the
  recording to the Commission.

## [Requests for Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841204 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to file statement of accounts

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before November 30 of each year, a licensee shall file with the
  Commission, on the annual return form issued by the Commission, a
  statement of accounts for the previous broadcast year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to respond to complaint or
  request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the Commission's request, a licensee shall respond
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a complaint or request for resolution of a dispute
    filed by a person or a request for information regarding the
    programming that is originated by or is distributed by the licensee
    or regarding the licensee's technical operations, subscribership,
    financial affairs or ownership; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a request for information regarding the licensee's
    adherence to the conditions of its licence, the Act, these
    Regulations, industry standards, practices or codes or any other
    self-regulatory mechanism of the industry.

## [Transfer of Ownership or Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841212 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used to indicate a
      relationship with a person, includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a partner of the person;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust or an estate in which the person has a
        substantial beneficial interest or in which the person serves as
        a trustee or in a similar capacity;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person's spouse or common-law partner;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a child of the person or of their spouse or
        common-law partner, including a child adopted in fact by the
        person, spouse or common-law partner;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the child;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a relative of the person, or of the person's
        spouse or common-law partner, if that relative has the same
        residence as the person;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which the person alone has, or
        the person together with one or more associates described in
        this definition have, directly or indirectly, control of 50% or
        more of the voting interests;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which an associate, as
        described in this definition, of the person has, directly or
        indirectly, control of 50% or more of the voting interests; and

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person with which the person has entered into
        an arrangement, a contract, an understanding or an agreement in
        respect of the voting of shares of a licensee corporation or of
        a corporation that has, directly or indirectly, effective
        control of a licensee corporation, except when that person
        controls less than 1% of all issued voting shares of a
        corporation whose shares are publicly traded on a stock
        exchange. ([lien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
      is cohabiting with a person in a conjugal relationship having done
      so for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint de
      fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the shares that
      represent the residual equity in the earnings of a corporation,
      and includes securities that are convertible into such shares at
      any time at the option of the holder and the preferred shares to
      which are attached rights to participate in the earnings of the
      corporation with no upper limit. ([actions
      ordinaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual,
      partnership, joint venture, association, corporation, trust,
      estate, trustee, executor or administrator, or a legal
      representative of any of them. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation with share capital, means the
        vote attached to a voting share;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation without share capital, means an
        interest that entitles the owner to voting rights similar to
        those enjoyed by the owner of a voting share;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partnership, trust, association or joint
        venture, means an ownership interest in its assets that entitles
        the owner to receive a share of its profits, to receive a share
        of its assets on dissolution and to participate directly in its
        management or to vote on the election of the persons that are to
        be entrusted with the power and responsibility to manage it; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit partnership, trust,
        association or joint venture, means a right that entitles the
        owner to participate directly in its management or to vote on
        the election of the persons that are to be entrusted with the
        power and responsibility to manage it. ([intérêt avec droit de
        vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share in the capital
      of a corporation to which one or more votes are attached that are
      exercisable at meetings of shareholders of the corporation, either
      under all circumstances or under a circumstance that has occurred
      and is continuing, and includes a security that is convertible
      into such a share at any time at the option of the
      holder. ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of voting interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, control of a voting
  interest by a person includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is, directly or indirectly, the
    beneficial owner of the voting interest; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person, by means of an arrangement, contract,
    understanding or agreement, determines the manner in which the
    interest is voted but the solicitation of proxies or the seeking of
    instructions with respect to the completion of proxies in respect of
    the exercise of voting interests is not considered to be such an
    arrangement, contract, understanding or agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective control of licensee

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, effective control
  of a licensee or its undertaking includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls a majority of the voting
    interests of the licensee directly or indirectly, other than by way
    of security only;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person has the ability to cause the licensee or
    its board of directors to take a course of action; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commission, after a public hearing of an
    application for a licence or in respect of an existing licence,
    determines that a person has effective control of the licensee or
    its undertaking and sets that determination out in a decision or
    public notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to obtain Commission's prior
  approval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall obtain the Commission's prior approval of
  any act, transaction or agreement that, directly or indirectly, would
  result in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change by any means of the effective control of
    its undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person that alone

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 30% of the voting interests of
      the licensee having control of 30% or more of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 30% of the voting interests
      of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee having control of 30% or more of those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} owns less than 50% of the issued common shares
      of the licensee owning 50% or more of those shares but not having,
      directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} owns less than 50% of the issued common shares
      of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee owning 50% or more of those shares but not having,
      directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person together with an associate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 30% of the voting
      interests of the licensee having control of 30% or more of those
      interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 30% of the voting
      interests of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee having control of 30% or more of those
      interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that own less than 50% of the issued common
      shares of the licensee owning 50% or more of those shares but not
      having, directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that own less than 50% of the issued common
      shares of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee owning 50% or more of those shares but not
      having, directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to notify Commission

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall notify the Commission, within 30
  days after the day on which the act or transaction occurs or the
  agreement is entered into, of the occurrence of any act or transaction
  or the entry into any agreement that, directly or indirectly, results
  in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person that alone

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 20% of the voting interests of
      the licensee having control of 20% or more but less than 30% of
      those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 20% of the voting interests
      of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee having control of 20% or more but less than 30% of
      those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 40% of the voting interests
      of the licensee having control of 40% or more but less than 50% of
      those interests but not having, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} controls less than 40% of the voting interests
      of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective control of
      the licensee having control of 40% or more but less than 50% of
      those interests but not having, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person together with an associate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 20% of the voting
      interests of the licensee having control of 20% or more but less
      than 30% of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 20% of the voting
      interests of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee having control of 20% or more but less
      than 30% of those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 40% of the voting
      interests of the licensee having control of 40% or more but less
      than 50% of those interests but not having, directly or
      indirectly, effective control of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that control less than 40% of the voting
      interests of a person that has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 40% or more but less
      than 50% of those interests but not having, directly or
      indirectly, effective control of the licensee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notification

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The notification is to contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person and, if applicable, of the
    associate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of the voting interests controlled
    by the person or by the person and the associate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a complete description of the act, transaction or
    agreement or a copy of the transaction or agreement.

## [Undue Preference or Disadvantage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841274 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- undue preference or
disadvantage

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee shall not give an undue preference to any person, including
  itself, or subject any person to an undue disadvantage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Burden of proof

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a proceeding before the Commission, the burden of
  establishing that any preference or disadvantage is not undue is on
  the licensee that gives the preference or subjects the person to the
  disadvantage.

## [Tied Selling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee shall not offer
its programming service for distribution as part of a package with other
programming services unless it also makes its programming service
available on a stand-alone basis.

## [Availability of New Programming Services for Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841283 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation --- distribution of new
programming service

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee that is ready to
launch a new programming service shall make that programming service
available for distribution by all licensed broadcasting distribution
undertakings or operators of exempt distribution undertakings, despite
the absence of a commercial agreement.

## [Dispute Resolution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841286 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral of dispute to Commission

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  there is a dispute between a licensee and the operator of a licensed
  distribution undertaking or an exempt distribution undertaking
  concerning the carriage or terms of carriage of programming that
  originates from the licensee, including the wholesale rate and the
  terms of any audit referred to in section 15.1 of the [Broadcasting
  Distribution Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555), one or both of
  the parties to the dispute may refer the matter to the Commission for
  dispute resolution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mediation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission accepts a referral of a matter for
  dispute resolution, the parties to the dispute shall participate in a
  mediation with a person who is appointed by the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the dispute resolution process, the person who
  is appointed may require additional information from the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedural requirements, rates, terms
  and conditions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a licensed distribution undertaking or an exempt
  distribution undertaking distributes the licensee's programming
  service in the absence of a commercial agreement and the matter
  proceeds before the Commission for dispute resolution,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dispute shall be resolved in accordance with
    the procedural requirements established by the Commission in
    Broadcasting and Telecom Information Bulletin CRTC 2019-184, dated
    May 29, 2019 and entitled Practices and procedures for dispute
    resolution; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rates, terms and conditions established by the
    Commission apply as of the day on which the programming service was
    first made available to the distributor in the absence of a
    commercial agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rates, terms and conditions --- new
  programming service

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the dispute relates to the rates, terms or
  conditions that relate to a new programming service that is being
  distributed in the absence of a commercial agreement and the matter
  proceeds before the Commission for dispute resolution, the parties
  will be bound by the rates, terms and conditions established by the
  Commission for the duration of the contractual term established by the
  Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rates, terms and conditions ---
  agreement

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (4) and (5), the parties may
  reach an agreement that sets out rates, terms or conditions that
  differ from those established by the Commission.

## [Obligations During Dispute]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841302 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation --- rates, terms and
conditions

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  a dispute between a licensee and a person that is licensed to carry on
  a distribution undertaking or the operator of an exempt distribution
  undertaking concerning the carriage or terms of carriage of
  programming that originates from the licensee or concerning any right
  or obligation under the Act, the licensee must continue to provide its
  programming services to the distribution undertaking at the same rates
  and on the same terms and conditions as it did before the dispute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of dispute

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a dispute begins
  when written notice of the dispute is provided to the Commission and
  is served on the other undertaking that is a party to the dispute and
  ends when an agreement settling the dispute is reached by the
  concerned undertakings or, if no such agreement is reached, when the
  Commission renders a decision concerning any unresolved matter.

## [Transmission of Programming Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841308 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations --- transmission of
programming service

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided under a condition of its licence, a licensee shall, in respect
of each programming service that is required to be distributed under
section 18 of the [Broadcasting Distribution
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555), by the Commission under
paragraph 9(1)(h) of the Act or by order of the Commission made under
subsection 9(4) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure the transmission of the programming service
  from its production facilities to each broadcasting distribution
  undertaking's head end located within the area for which the licensee
  is licensed or to a satellite uplink centre located within that area;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear the costs of the transmission.
