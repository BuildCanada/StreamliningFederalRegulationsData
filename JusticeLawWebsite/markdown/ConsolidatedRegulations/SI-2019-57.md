## Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Accessibility Standards Development Organization for the purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-employment-and-social-development-as-the-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-canadian-accessibility-standards-development-organization-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-57
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2019-07-24

Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development as
the appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Accessibility
Standards Development Organization for the purposes of that Act

P.C. 2019-1044 2019-07-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1642-E_hq_22294){#fn_81000-3-1642-E_hq_22294-ID0EGCAA}
of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
section 2 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1642-E_hq_22295){#fn_81000-3-1642-E_hq_22295-ID0EBCAA},
designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development as the
appropriate Minister with respect to the Canadian Accessibility
Standards Development Organization for the purposes of that Act,
effective July 11, 2019.
