[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the applicant for

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate under subsection 182(1) of the Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an order under subsection 214(1) of the Act;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certificate under subsection 262(1) of the Act;
      or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an authorization under subsection 298(1) of the
      Act. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission referred to
    in section 26 of the Act. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of periods --- subsections
183(5) and 214(5) of the Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 183(5) and 214(5) of the Act, the circumstances in which
periods may be excluded from the calculation of the time limit under
subsection 183(4) or 214(4) of the Act are any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted to the Commission a
  written request for the exclusion of a period from the calculation of
  the time limit relating to the report under subsection 183(1) of the
  Act or the order under subsection 214(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking of studies or the collection of
  information relating to a change made by the applicant in the design,
  the construction or the operation of a pipeline, and the resulting
  effects of the change, if the Commission is of the opinion that there
  is not sufficient information available to it for the purpose of
  submitting the report under subsection 183(1) of the Act or making the
  order under subsection 214(1) of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has not paid the fees, levies or
  charges imposed under section 87 of the Act and any interest payable
  on them in accordance with any of the regulations made under
  subsection 87(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of periods --- subsections
262(6) and 298(6) of the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 262(6) and 298(6) of the Act, the circumstances in which
periods may be excluded from the calculation of the time limit under
subsection 262(4) or 298(4) of the Act are any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted to the Commission a
  written request for the exclusion of a period from the calculation of
  the time limit relating to the application for a certificate under
  subsection 262(1) of the Act or for an authorization under subsection
  298(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the undertaking of studies or the collection of
  information relating to a change made by the applicant in the design,
  the construction or the operation of an international or
  interprovincial power line referred to in subsection 262(1) of the Act
  or a proposed work or activity referred to in subsection 298(1) of the
  Act and the resulting effects of the change, if the Commission is of
  the opinion that there is not sufficient information available to it
  for the purpose of issuing the certificate or the authorization, as
  the case may be; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has not paid the fees, levies or
  charges imposed under section 87 of the Act and any interest payable
  on them in accordance with any of the regulations made under
  subsection 87(1) of the Act.
