## Prospector's Assistance Terms and Conditions Order {#prospectors-assistance-terms-and-conditions-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-145
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1979-80

Registration 1981-02-16

Order Respecting Terms and Conditions for the Providing of Assistance to
Prospectors in the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1981-341 1981-02-12
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, is pleased
hereby to revoke the Prospectors' Assistance Regulations, C.R.C., c. 338
and pursuant to Vote 35 of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1979-80[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA}, to make the
annexed [Order respecting terms and conditions for the providing of
assistance to prospectors in the Yukon Territory and the Northwest
Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-145), effective April 1, 1981.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870358 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Prospector's Assistance Terms and Conditions
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-145).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870360 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[assisted prospector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assisted prospector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prospector to
    whom assistance is provided under this Order; ([prospecteur
    subventionné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to matters pertaining to assistance
      to prospectors prospecting or intending to prospect in the Yukon
      Territory, the Prospectors' Assistance Board established under
      section 6, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to matters pertaining to assistance
      to prospectors prospecting or intending to prospect in the
      Northwest Territories, the Prospectors' Assistance Board
      established under section 7; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director of the Northern
    Non-Renewable Resources Branch of the Department;
    ([Directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[field work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[field work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the work performed in the
    search for occurrences of mineral deposits other than placer;
    ([(travail sur le terrain)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    and including the 1st day of April in any year and ending on and
    including the 31st day of March in the year following. ([exercice
    financier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870370 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies only to
prospectors prospecting or intending to prospect in the Yukon Territory
or the Northwest Territories.

## [Application for Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870372 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  prospector requesting financial assistance under this Order, shall,
  prior to June 1 of the fiscal year for which he is requesting
  assistance, submit to the Mining Recorder for the area in which he
  intends to prospect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application in the form set out in Schedule I
    containing the information required by that form; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sketch map of the area or areas in which the
    applicant intends to prospect.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each Mining Recorder shall forward all applications
  submitted to him pursuant to subsection (1) to the Board with his
  recommendations.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No prospector under the age
of 21 years is eligible for assistance under this Order unless he
intends to carry out field work in the company of an experienced
prospector who is 21 years of age or older.

## [Prospectors' Assistance Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870379 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A Prospectors' Assistance
Board is hereby established for the Yukon Territory consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Supervising Mining Recorder for the Yukon
  Territory, who shall be Chairman of the Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Regional Mining Engineer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Regional Geologist; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not more than two other members, to be appointed by
  the Director from among the nominees of mining or prospecting
  associations that are active in the Yukon Territory and acceptable to
  the Director.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A Prospectors' Assistance
Board is hereby established for the Northwest Territories consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Supervising Mining Recorder for the Northwest
  Territories, who shall be Chairman of the Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Regional Mining Engineer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Regional Geologist; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not more than two other members, to be appointed by
  the Director from among the nominees of mining or prospecting
  associations that are active in the Northwest Territories and
  acceptable to the Director.

## [Duties of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870390 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consider each application for assistance and
    determine the amount of financial aid required by each applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where it deems necessary, require an applicant to
    take a simple test to establish his knowledge of prospecting; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recommend the approval or rejection of any
    application, as it sees fit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall advise each applicant of the
  disposition of his application and, in the case of a successful
  applicant, advise him of the amount of assistance that it has
  recommended and whether it has recommended the payment of an advance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In considering each application for assistance the
  Board shall give preference to Canadian citizens and permanent
  residents as defined in the Immigration Act, 1976.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  calculating the amount of assistance to be recommended in respect of a
  prospector, the Board shall allow

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for all expenses, other than travelling expenses, a
    sum not exceeding \$1,500; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for travelling expenses, a sum not exceeding
    \$2,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may allow a per diem allowance of \$25 to
  be included in the allowable expenses under paragraph (1)(a) as a
  personal labour cost in respect of the period spent by a prospector in
  field work.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Chairman of the Board
shall, pursuant to the recommendation of the Board, authorize the
granting of assistance to a prospector

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the beginning of the prospecting season, of the
  whole or a portion of the recommended amount; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the end of the prospecting season, of an amount
  not exceeding the balance of the amount recommended by the Board, or
  such part thereof as may be sufficient, in the opinion of the
  appropriate Review Committee, to reimburse the prospector for his
  field work expenses.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall arrange
for a Mining Engineer of the Department or the Regional Geologist or
both to visit each assisted prospector in the field and provide
supervision and technical assistance to each such prospector.

## [Review Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870407 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Chairman of the
Board, the appropriate Regional Mining Engineer and the appropriate
Regional Geologist constitute a Review Committee in their respective
territories and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} review all statements of expenditures, diaries or
  other information submitted by each assisted prospector as evidence of
  his prospecting activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommend reimbursement to any assisted prospector at
  the end of the prospecting season if such reimbursement, in its
  opinion, is necessary; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintain records of all payments to prospectors made
  in each fiscal year and all statements of expenditures together with
  supporting invoices and documents.

## [Requirements for Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870412 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Each assisted prospector
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} spend not less than 60 days prospecting within the
  area described in his application for assistance during the fiscal
  year for which he applied for assistance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a daily diary of his activities in a form
  prescribed by the Director;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} keep sketches and maps showing mineral occurrences,
  location of trenches and drill holes and any other type of field work
  performed within the area prospected by him; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} submit to the appropriate Review Committee at the end
  of the prospecting season the diary, sketches and maps described in
  paragraphs (b) and (c) and a statement of expenditures in the form set
  out in Schedule II, together with supporting invoices and other
  documents.

## [Failure to Comply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870418 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  shall be a condition of every grant of assistance to a prospector
  under this Order that if he fails to comply with paragraph 13(a), any
  advance of moneys received by him pursuant to paragraph 10(a) shall,
  subject to subsection (2), be repaid by him to Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Committee is satisfied that 60 days
  prospecting could not be completed by an assisted prospector owing to
  reasons beyond his control, the Committee may approve the retention by
  the prospector of any amount already paid to him and the payment of
  such further amount as the Committee may deem fit.
