## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858188 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Foreign Missions and
    International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the Act. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[G7 meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[G7 meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following ministerial meetings:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} "Preparing for jobs of the future / Employment
        and Innovation Ministers", to be held in Montreal, Quebec, on
        March 27 and 28, 2018,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} "Building a more peaceful and secure world /
        Foreign Affairs and Security Ministers", to be held in Toronto,
        Ontario, from April 22 to 24, 2018,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} "Investing in growth that works for everyone
        / Finance and Development Ministers", to be held in Whistler,
        British Columbia, from May 31 to June 2, 2018,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} "Working together on climate change, oceans
        and clean energy / Environment and Energy Ministers", to be held
        in Halifax, Nova Scotia, and

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} all other ministerial meetings that are part of
        Canada's official program of its G7 Presidency, to be held in
        Canada during the relevant period; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the G7 Leaders' Summit, to be held in La Malbaie,
      Quebec, on June 8 and 9, 2018. ([rencontres du
      G7]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on the day on which this Order comes into force and ending on
    December 31, 2018. ([période visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858202 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives of foreign states

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the relevant period, representatives of a foreign state that
  participates in the G7 meetings shall have, to the extent required for
  the exercise of their functions in relation to the G7 meetings, the
  privileges and immunities set out in sections 11, 12 and 14 to 16 of
  Article IV of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Heads and senior officials of
  international organizations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, heads of an international
  organization, as well as their alternates and other senior officials
  of an international organization, shall have, to the extent required
  for the exercise of their functions in relation to the G7 meetings,
  the privileges and immunities comparable to the privileges and
  immunities accorded to diplomatic agents under the Vienna Convention
  on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other officials

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, other officials of an
  international organization shall have, to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in relation to the G7 meetings, the
  privileges and immunities set out in section 18 of Article V of the
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, experts who perform
  missions for an international organization shall have, to the extent
  required for the exercise of their functions in relation to the G7
  meetings, the privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the
  Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858212 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
