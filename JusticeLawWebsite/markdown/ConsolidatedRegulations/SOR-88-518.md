## Eldorado Nuclear Limited Exemption Order {#eldorado-nuclear-limited-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-518
:::

[ELDORADO NUCLEAR LIMITED REORGANIZATION AND DIVESTITURE
ACT](/eng/acts/E-1.7)

Registration 1988-09-29

Order Respecting the Exemption of Certain Property, Rights, Interests,
Obligations and Liabilities of Her Majesty from the Application of
Subsection 4(4) of the Eldorado Nuclear Limited Reorganization and
Divestiture Act

P.C. 1988-2285 1988-09-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, pursuant to
subsection 4(8) of the [Eldorado Nuclear Limited Reorganization and
Divestiture Act](/eng/acts/E-1.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the exemption of certain property,
rights, interests, obligations and liabilities of Her Majesty from the
application of subsection 4(4) of the Eldorado Nuclear Limited
Reorganization and Divestiture Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-518).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1988, c. 41
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913904 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Eldorado Nuclear Limited Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-518).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913906 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this
Order,[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Eldorado Nuclear Limited
Reorganization and Divestiture Act](/eng/acts/E-1.7). (*Loi*)

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913908 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All the property, rights
and interests of Her Majesty held on September 30, 1988 by Eldorado and
all the obligations and liabilities of Her Majesty incurred by Eldorado
and existing on September 30, 1988, except the shares of the corporation
that is the successor corporation by way of amalgamation to Eldorado
Resources Limited, Eldor Resources Limited and 119371 Canada Limited,
are hereby exempt from the application of subsection 4(4) of the Act.
