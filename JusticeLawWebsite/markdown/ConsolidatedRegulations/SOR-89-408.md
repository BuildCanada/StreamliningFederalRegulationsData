## Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order, 1989 {#lawrence-bay-airways-ltd.-exemption-order-1989 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-408
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1989-08-10

Order Respecting the Exemption of Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. from the
Application of Subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of the National Transportation
Act, 1987
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 73 of the National Transportation Act,
1987[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBDA}, the
Minister of Transport considers it necessary or advisable in the public
interest that a domestic licence be issued to Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd.
who, under subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of that Act, is not qualified to
obtain the licence.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EBBDA){#fn_1e}R.S., c. 28 (3rd Supp.)
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 73 of the
National Transportation Act, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBCA}, hereby revokes the
Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order, 1988, made on December 31,
1988[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDBCA}, and
makes the annexed [Order respecting the exemption of Lawrence Bay
Airways Ltd. from the application of subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of the
National Transportation Act, 1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-408), in
substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e-ID0EDBCA){#fn_2e}SOR/89-79, 1989
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 883
  :::

Ottawa, August 9, 1989

::: FlushRight
BENOÎT BOUCHARD
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Transport*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920011 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order,
1989](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-408).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920013 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd.
is hereby exempted from the application of subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of
the National Transportation Act, 1987.
