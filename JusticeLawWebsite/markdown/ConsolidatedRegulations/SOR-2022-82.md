## Joint Notice Extending the Prohibition Period for Certain Activities on Georges Bank {#joint-notice-extending-the-prohibition-period-for-certain-activities-on-georges-bank .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-82
:::

[CANADA-NOVA SCOTIA OFFSHORE PETROLEUM RESOURCES ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-7.8)

Registration 2022-04-13

Joint Notice Extending the Prohibition Period for Certain Activities on
Georges Bank
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Federal Minister and the Provincial Minister, pursuant to
subsection 141(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514-ID0EHDEA}
of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515-ID0EEDEA},
issued the [Joint Notice Specifying the Prohibition Period For Certain
Activities on Georges Bank](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-70)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30509){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30509-ID0EBDEA}
on April 11, 2016 which imposed a prohibition period that ends on
December 31, 2022;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514-ID0EHDEA){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514}S.C.
  2015, c. 39, s. 3
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515-ID0EEDEA){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515}S.C.
  1988, c. 28
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30509-ID0EBDEA){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30509}SOR/2016-70
  :::

And whereas, after having reviewed the environmental and socio-economic
impact of exploration and drilling activities in that portion of the
offshore area described in Schedule IV to that Act and all other
relevant factors, the Ministers wish to extend, for a period of 10
years, the prohibition period set out in that Joint Notice;

Therefore, the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of Natural
Resources and Renewables for Nova Scotia, pursuant to subsection
141(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30514-ID0EGACA}
of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515){#fn_81000-2-4762_hq_30515-ID0EDACA},
make the annexed [Joint Notice Extending the Prohibition Period for
Certain Activities on Georges Bank](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-82).

Ottawa, April 11, 2022

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 141(3) of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum
Resources Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8), the exploration
and drilling for and the production, conservation and processing of
petroleum in that portion of the offshore area described in Schedule IV
to that Act and the transportation of petroleum produced in that portion
of the offshore area are prohibited for a period beginning on January 1,
2023 and ending on December 31, 2032.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343082 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}January 1, 2023

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Joint Notice comes
into force on January 1, 2023.
