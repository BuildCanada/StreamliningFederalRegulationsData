## Defence Materiel Loan or Transfer for Test and Evaluation Regulations {#defence-materiel-loan-or-transfer-for-test-and-evaluation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 691
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Under Section 52 of the Financial Administration Act
Respecting Loans or Transfers of Defence Materiel for Test and
Evaluation Purposes
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560572 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Defence Materiel Loan or Transfer for Test and Evaluation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._691).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560574 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[materiel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[materiel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all movable public property,
    other than money, provided for the Canadian Forces or the Defence
    Research Board or for any other purpose under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5), and includes any vessel, vehicle, aircraft,
    animal, missile, arms, ammunition, clothing, stores, provisions or
    equipment so provided; ([matériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means materiel given to another country
    on condition of its return; ([prêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the granting of all right,
    title and interest in and to materiel to another country.
    ([transfert]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560580 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister, where he
considers that the Canadian Forces and national defence would benefit,
is authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to lend or transfer materiel for test and evaluation
  purposes, subject to the following conditions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all loans or transfers of materiel under these
    Regulations shall be restricted to friendly countries,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all transfers of materiel shall be restricted to
    consumable items and such other items as are to be tested to
    destruction,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all loans or transfers of materiel under these
    Regulations shall be made gratuitously to the recipient country,
    unless the Minister decides otherwise;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to accept gratuitously or otherwise material loaned
  or transferred by friendly countries for test and evaluation purposes;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to pay from public fund such incidental costs as he
  may consider appropriate, in respect of all or any loans or transfers,
  including the acceptance thereof, made pursuant to these Regulations;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to enter into agreements to give effect to the
  foregoing, the said agreements to contain such other terms and
  conditions as he may consider appropriate.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560589 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall report
annually to the Treasury Board any major item such as a vessel or an
aircraft or any item valued at more than \$25,000 that has been on loan
to a friendly government for more than one year or that has been
transferred.
