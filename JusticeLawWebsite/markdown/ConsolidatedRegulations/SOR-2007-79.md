## Fort McKay First Nation Oil Sands Regulations {#fort-mckay-first-nation-oil-sands-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-79
:::

[FIRST NATIONS COMMERCIAL AND INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT
ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.64)

Registration 2007-04-19

Fort McKay First Nation Oil Sands Regulations

P.C. 2007-583 2007-04-19
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas in accordance with paragraph 5(a) of the [First Nations
Commercial and Industrial Development Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBCA} the Minister of Indian and
Northern Affairs has received a resolution of the council of the Fort
McKay First Nation requesting that the Minister recommend to the
Governor in Council the making of the annexed [Fort McKay First Nation
Oil Sands Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-79);

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBCA){#fna_e}S.C.
  2005, c. 53
  :::

And whereas in accordance with paragraph 5(b) of that Act the Fort McKay
Oil Sands Intergovernmental Agreement has been concluded between the
Minister, the Province of Alberta and the council of the Fort McKay
First Nation for the administration and enforcement of the Regulations
by the provincial officials and bodies specified in the Regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to section 3 of the [First Nations Commercial and
Industrial Development Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Fort McKay First Nation Oil Sands
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-79).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741625 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[federal Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[federal Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Indigenous Services. ([ministre fédéral]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fort McKay First
      Nation. ([Première Nation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the laws of
      Alberta specified in Schedule 1, as amended from time to time,
      with the adaptations specified in Schedule 2. ([texte législatif
      incorporé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lands in Alberta
      in Theoretical Township 96, Range 9, West of the 4th Meridian that
      are part of the Fort McKay Indian Reserve No. 174C and are shown
      on the Plan Showing Survey of Fort McKay Settlement (Oil Sands
      Lands) recorded in the Canada Lands Surveys Records under number
      90264 and registered at the Land Titles Office in Edmonton under
      number 052 2726, containing 3381.48 hectares (8355.8 acres) more
      or less and more particularly described as follows:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Lot 1, Block 1 1304.11 ha (3222.5 ac);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Lot 1, Block 2 830.57 ha (2052.4 ac);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Lot 2, Block 2 1109.11 ha (2740.7 ac); and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Lot 3, Block 2 137.69 ha (340.2 ac). ([terres
        du projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, for the purposes of

      time. ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The incorporated laws that apply with respect to the

  the Interpretation Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. I-8, as amended from

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enactment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Interpretation Act of Alberta

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of enforcing these Regulations, a
  reference to "enactment" in provincial offences procedures laws is to

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741638 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to implement with respect to the project lands a legal
regime that is harmonized with the legal regime of Alberta that governs
oil sands mining and related activities.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741641 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the incorporated laws apply with respect to the
  project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- laws in force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A provision of an incorporated law applies only if it
  is in force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} nothing in these Regulations confers any legislative
  power on any person or body;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or body that has a power, duty or function
  --- other than a legislative power --- under the laws of Alberta
  specified in Schedule 1 has the same power, duty or function under
  these Regulations, subject to the adaptations specified in Schedule 2;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a body or fund referred to in these Regulations is
  the same body or fund constituted under the applicable law of Alberta
  specified in Schedule 1;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} nothing in these Regulations affects the application
  or interpretation of agreements between Canada and Alberta regarding
  cooperative environmental assessments;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where a law that applies with respect to the project
  lands under section 3 refers to a law that is adapted by these
  Regulations, the reference shall be read as a reference to the law as
  adapted; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in Schedule 1, a reference to regulations made under
  an Act includes all regulations made under that Act after the coming
  into force of these Regulations, unless excepted in Schedule 1 or 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Council of First Nation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The First Nation shall act
through its band council in respect of any power, duty or function of
the First Nation under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of [Indian Oil and Gas
Act](/eng/acts/I-7)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The [Indian Oil and Gas
Act](/eng/acts/I-7) does not apply with respect to the project lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevalence of these Regulations

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations prevail
over all regulations made under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), to the
extent of any conflict or inconsistency between them.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 1(1) and section 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Adaptations]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1436147 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations Applicable to Incorporated Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1adaptations-applicable-to-incorporated-laws .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation in French version

  **1** An English term that is printed in a combination of parentheses
  and quotation marks in a provision of the French version of this
  Schedule is defined in the law that is adapted by that provision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of incorporated laws

  **2** Incorporated laws are to be read without reference to provisions
  authorizing any person, provincial official or provincial body to
  expropriate any interest in lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee or charge

  **3** Any obligation to pay a fee or charge under any incorporated law
  does not apply to His Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No liability of His Majesty

  - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any obligation or liability of an owner or a
    registered owner of land, buildings, structures or fixtures under
    any incorporated law does not apply to His Majesty in right of
    Canada.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of owner

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a consent, authorization or other approval of an
    owner or a registered owner of land is required under any
    incorporated law, it may only be given by the First Nation.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to federal Minister and First
    Nation

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a notice or document is required to be given to
    an owner or a registered owner of land under any incorporated law,
    it must be given to both the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial requirements under lease

  **5** If the incorporated laws require a cash deposit or other
  financial security to be given, this requirement applies in addition
  to any other requirements that may apply to the project lands in
  relation to cash deposits or other financial security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on searches and inspections

  - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A power to search or make inspections under
    an incorporated law, including the power to enter a place, does not
    include a power to enter or search a federal government office, or
    to inspect anything in that office, without the consent of the
    person who is or appears to be in charge of that office.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on production of documents

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} A power to seize, remove or compel the production
    of documents under an incorporated law does not include a power to
    seize, remove or compel the production of a document in the
    possession of the federal government without the consent of the
    person in possession of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person indebted

  **7** A reference to "person who is indebted to the Government" or
  "person who is indebted to the Crown" under any incorporated law is to
  be read as to include a reference to a person who is indebted to the
  Government of Alberta, the Crown in right of Alberta, His Majesty in
  right of Canada or the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person responsible

  **8** A reference to "person responsible" under any incorporated law
  does not include His Majesty in right of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surface Rights Act exclusions

  **9** Any provision referring to the Surface Rights Act of Alberta,
  RSA 2000, c. S-24, or to rights under that Act does not apply with
  respect to the project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public Lands Act exclusions

  **10** Any provision referring to the Public Lands Act of Alberta, RSA
  2000, c. P-40, or to rights under that Act does not apply with respect
  to the project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency response plan

  **11** Where an emergency response plan is required to be filed,
  submitted or otherwise made available to any person or body under any
  incorporated law, the person having the obligation to do so must,
  without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide confirmation to the federal Minister and
    the First Nation that the obligation has been met; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if requested, provide a copy of the plan to the
    federal Minister and the First Nation.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Administrative Procedures and Jurisdiction Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2adaptations-to-the-administrative-procedures-and-jurisdiction-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[[Statutory
  power]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  **12** A reference to "statute" in the definition [[statutory
  power]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in clause 1(c) of the Administrative
  Procedures and Jurisdiction Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. A-3, is to be
  read as including a reference to any statute of Alberta that applies
  with respect to the project lands by virtue of these Regulations.

### [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Alberta Utilities Commission Act]{.HTitleText1} {#part-3adaptations-to-the-alberta-utilities-commission-act .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **13** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta
  Utilities Commission Act, SA 2007, c. A-37.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affecting rights

  **14** A Commission decision or order specific to the project lands
  referred to in section 9 of the Act is deemed to directly and
  adversely affect the rights of the federal Minister and the First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person

  **15** A reference to "person" in sections 9 and 10 and in subsection
  24(2) of the Act includes the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitled to file

  **16** The federal Minister and the First Nation are deemed to be
  persons entitled to file applications for the purposes of section 10
  and subsection 24(2) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- His Majesty

  **17** The Commission cannot direct that His Majesty in right of
  Canada pay any costs, money, expenses or penalties under sections 21
  and 25 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registered order --- lien

  **18** In subsection 26(2) of the Act, the reference to "interest in
  land" is to be read as a reference to "leasehold interest in land".

### [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act of Alberta and to the Regulations Made Under It]{.HTitleText1} {#part-4adaptations-to-the-environmental-protection-and-enhancement-act-of-alberta-and-to-the-regulations-made-under-it .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  **19** The following definitions apply in this Part:

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Environmental Protection and
      Enhancement Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. E-12.
       ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Conservation and
      Reclamation Regulation of Alberta, AR
      115/1993. ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

#### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Environmental Protection and Enhancement Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText2} {#division-1adaptations-to-the-environmental-protection-and-enhancement-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person directly affected

  **20** A reference to "any person who is directly affected" in the Act
  is to be read as including the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person responsible

  **21** A reference to "person responsible" or "person responsible for
  the contaminated site" in the Act is to be read as excluding the First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Programs for use of economic
  instruments

  **22** No programs or other measures established under section 13 of
  the Act apply with respect to the project lands without the agreement
  of the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Local authority

  - **23** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A reference to "local authority" in sections
    27 and 28, subsections 110(3) and 115(3), sections 126, 130 and 220,
    subsection 233(1) and section 249 of the Act is to be read as
    including the First Nation and the Regional Municipality of Wood
    Buffalo.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Band council

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference to "council" in subsection 233(1) of
    the Act is to be read as including the band council of the First
    Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Minister

  **24** The reference to "Minister" in subsection 99(1) of the Act is
  to read as including a reference to the federal Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective on agreement

  **25** For the purposes of subsection 100(1) of the Act, an order of
  the Minister is effective on the agreement in writing of the federal
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports and notices

  **26** A report or notice required under subsection 110(1), 110(3) or
  115(3) or section 130 of the Act is to also be given without delay to
  the federal Minister and to the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforceablility of charge

  **27** The reference to "mortgage or other security on land" in
  section 216 of the Act, is to be read as a reference to "mortgage or
  other security on a leasehold interest in land".

#### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Conservation and Reclamation Regulation of Alberta]{.HTitleText2} {#division-2adaptations-to-the-conservation-and-reclamation-regulation-of-alberta .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspectors

  **28** Any designation of a member of the band council of the First
  Nation or the council of the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo as
  an inspector under the Regulation has no effect with respect to the
  project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Local authority

  **29** A reference to "local authority" in subsection 4(2) and in
  section 17.1 of the Regulation is to be read as including the First
  Nation and the Regional Municipality of Wood Buffalo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

  - **30** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 24 of the Regulation is to be read
    without clause (2)(b) or subsection (4) or (7).

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of reference

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference to subsection 24(4) of the Regulation
    is to be read as a reference to subsection 24(3).

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation to subsection 24(3)

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 24(3) of the Regulation is to be read as
    follows:

    > ::: ReadAsText
    > - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Alberta Energy Regulator shall use security
    >   forfeited under subsection (1) to carry out the conservation and
    >   reclamation of the specified land in accordance with the Act and
    >   the regulations.
    > :::

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation to subsection 24(5)

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} The reference to "Minister" in subsection 24(5) of
    the Regulation is to be read as a reference to "Alberta Energy
    Regulator".

### [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Geothermal Resource Development Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-5adaptations-to-the-geothermal-resource-development-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **31** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Geothermal
  Resource Development Act of Alberta, SA 2020, c. G-5.5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  **32** An order or direction of the Regulator made under section 5 of
  the Act must also be approved by the federal Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry, examination or investigation

  **33** For the purposes of section 11 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the federal Minister or the First Nation may also
    require the Regulator to inquire into, examine or investigate any
    matter related to the purpose of the Act referred to in section 3 of
    the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an inquiry, examination or investigation is
    required by the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Alberta, the
    federal Minister or the First Nation, the Regulator must report its
    findings to both the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- His Majesty

  **34** The Regulator cannot direct that His Majesty in right of Canada
  pay costs or expenses under section 17 or subsection 21(3) or 24(4) of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of lien

  **35** The reference to "interests in land" in subsection 23(2) of the
  Act is to be read as a reference to "leasehold interests in land".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry on land

  **36** Any regulation made under the Act with respect to entry on a
  person's land without the person's agreement does not apply with
  respect to the project lands.

### [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Historical Resources Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-6adaptations-to-the-historical-resources-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **37** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Historical
  Resources Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. H-9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement of federal Minister

  **38** Before exercising, or giving notice of an intention to
  exercise, any power with respect to historic resources, archaeological
  resources or palaeontological resources under sections 16, 20, 30, 33,
  34 and 37 of the Act within the project lands, the Minister must
  obtain the agreement of the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing rights

  **39** The Act does not apply to affect the property in any
  archaeological resource or palaeontological resource within the
  project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Property in records

  **40** For greater certainty, the property in any record relating to
  historic resources prepared by the Crown in right of Alberta is vested
  in the Crown in right of Alberta.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of resources

  **41** Any regulation made under the Act respecting the sale, lease,
  exchange or disposition of archaeological resources or
  palaeontological resources does not apply with respect to the project
  lands.

### [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Hydro and Electric Energy Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-7adaptations-to-the-hydro-and-electric-energy-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **42** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Hydro and
  Electric Energy Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. H-16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  **43** The Act applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transmission lines that are used for oil sands
    mining activities on the project lands; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} power plants on the project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public highway

  **44** The definition [[public highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  clause 1(1)(l) of the Act is to be read without reference to the words
  "owned by the Crown or a local authority".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of federal Minister

  **45** Only the federal Minister or the First Nation may give an
  approval under subsection 34(2) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry on land

  **46** Any regulation made under the Act with respect to entry on a
  person's land without that person's agreement does not apply with
  respect to the project lands.

### [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Oil and Gas Conservation Act of Alberta and to the Regulations Made Under It]{.HTitleText1} {#part-8adaptations-to-the-oil-and-gas-conservation-act-of-alberta-and-to-the-regulations-made-under-it .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **47** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Oil and Gas
  Conservation Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. O-6.

#### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Oil and Gas Conservation Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText2} {#division-1adaptations-to-the-oil-and-gas-conservation-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective on agreement

  **48** For the purposes of subsection 18(2) of the Act, a direction of
  the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Alberta is effective on the
  agreement in writing of both the federal Minister and the First
  Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of lien

  **49** A reference to a "debtor" in section 103 of the Act does not
  include His Majesty in right of Canada or the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- His Majesty

  **50** The Regulator cannot direct that His Majesty in right of Canada
  pay costs or expenses under subsection 104(3) or 105(4) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Enforcement of orders

  **51** An enforcement action under section 105 of the Act with respect
  to land can only be taken with respect to the leasehold interest in
  the project lands.

#### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Oil and Gas Conservation Rules of Alberta]{.HTitleText2} {#division-2adaptations-to-the-oil-and-gas-conservation-rules-of-alberta .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alberta Land Surveyor

  **52** A reference to "Alberta Land Surveyor" in clause 2.020(3.1)(c)
  of the of Alberta, AR 151/1971, is to be read as a reference to a
  [[Canada Lands Surveyor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section
  2 of the [Canada Lands Surveyors Act](/eng/acts/L-5.8).

### [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Oil Sands Conservation Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-9adaptations-to-the-oil-sands-conservation-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **53** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Oil Sands
  Conservation Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. O-7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lieutenant Governor in Council

  **54** A reference to "the Lieutenant Governor in Council" in
  subsection 9(1) and in sub-clause 17(a)(ii) of the Act is to be read
  as a reference to the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Alberta, the
  federal Minister or the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply with orders

  **55** For the purposes of section 14 of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the federal Minister may direct that the Regulator
    conduct an inquiry relating to the compliance with a term or
    condition of an authorization of the Lieutenant Governor in Council
    of Alberta, and the Regulator must then comply with the procedure
    set out in subsection 14(2) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an inquiry is directed by the Lieutenant
    Governor in Council of Alberta or the federal Minister, the
    Regulator must report its findings to both of them; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a direction or other order made by the Lieutenant
    Governor in Council of Alberta under subsection 14(4) of the Act is
    effective on the agreement in writing of the federal Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  - **56** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The notice under clause 15(3)(a) of the Act
    to rectify a default must also be given to the First Nation.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice and opportunity to be heard

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice and the opportunity to be heard under
    clause 15(3)(b) of the Act must also be given to the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scheme --- project lands

  - **57** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A scheme referred to in section 18 of the
    Act cannot include lands that are not entirely within the project
    lands.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective on agreement

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 18 of the Act, a scheme
    is effective on the agreement in writing of both the federal
    Minister and the First Nation.

### [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Pipeline Act of Alberta and to the Regulations Made Under It]{.HTitleText1} {#part-10adaptations-to-the-pipeline-act-of-alberta-and-to-the-regulations-made-under-it .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **58** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Pipeline
  Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. P-15.

#### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Pipeline Act]{.HTitleText2} {#division-1adaptations-to-the-pipeline-act .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  **59** The Act applies to pipelines that are used for, or in
  connection with, oil sands mining activities on the project lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[[Road]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  **60** The definition [[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in clause 1(1)(y)
  of the Act is to be read as meaning land that is used or surveyed for
  use as a public road, street, lane or other public way, but does not
  include a highway.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs --- His Majesty

  **61** The Regulator cannot order that His Majesty in right of Canada
  pay any costs under subsection 33(2) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  **62** A report under subsection 35(5) of the Act must also
  immediately be given to the federal Minister and the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective on agreement

  **63** For the purposes of section 38 of the Act, an approval of the
  Minister set out in the section is effective on the agreement in
  writing of the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Local authority

  **64** A reference to "the local authority concerned" in section 39 of
  the Act is to be read as a reference to the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entry on land

  **65** Any regulation made under the Act with respect to entry on a
  person's land without that person's agreement does not apply with
  respect to the project lands.

#### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Adaptations to the Pipeline Rules of Alberta]{.HTitleText2} {#division-2adaptations-to-the-pipeline-rules-of-alberta .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surveys Act

  **66** The reference to "Surveys Act" in subsection 4(1) of the
  Pipeline Rules of Alberta, AR 91/2005 is to be read as a reference to
  the [Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6).

### [PART 11]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Responsible Energy Development Act of Alberta and to the Regulations Made Under It]{.HTitleText1} {#part-11adaptations-to-the-responsible-energy-development-act-of-alberta-and-to-the-regulations-made-under-it .SchedHeadL1}

- Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  **67** In this Part, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Responsible
  Energy Development Act of Alberta, SA 2012, c. R-17.3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limited incorporation

  **68** With respect to the acts and regulations listed in the
  definitions [[energy resource enactment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[specified enactment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 1(1) of
  the Act, only those that are expressly set out in Schedule 1 of these
  Regulations are incorporated under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Landowners

  **69** The reference to "landowners" in section 15 of the Act is to be
  read to include the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crown

  **70** A reference to "Crown" in subsection 50(3) of the Act is to be
  read as including a reference to "Crown in right of Canada".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person affected

  **71** Any reference to "person who may be directly and adversely
  affected" or "person who is directly and adversely affected" or
  "person who believes that the person may be directly and adversely
  affected" in the Act or in the regulations made under it is to be read
  as including the federal Minister and the First Nation.

### [PART 12]{.HLabel1}[Adaptations to the Safety Codes Act of Alberta]{.HTitleText1} {#part-12adaptations-to-the-safety-codes-act-of-alberta .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  **72** A written notice under clauses 55(1)(b) and 56(1)(b) of the
  Safety Codes Act of Alberta, RSA 2000, c. S-1, must be given to the
  First Nation.
