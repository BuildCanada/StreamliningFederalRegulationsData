## Vessel Duties Reduction or Removal Regulations {#vessel-duties-reduction-or-removal-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-304
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1990-05-24

Regulations Respecting the Reduction or Removal of Customs Duty on
Certain Vessels

P.C. 1990-939 1990-05-24
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to Supplementary Note No. 1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-304_e_hq_6125){#fn_SOR-90-304_e_hq_6125-ID0EGCAA}
to chapter 89 of Schedule I to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-90-304_e_hq_6126){#fn_SOR-90-304_e_hq_6126-ID0EDCAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the
reduction or removal of customs duties on certain vessels.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925847 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Vessel Duties Reduction or Removal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925849 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Canadian customs waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian customs waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the waters
    in the territorial sea, internal waters, and the waters above the
    continental shelf of Canada within which a vessel falls under
    customs jurisdiction by virtue of the [Customs and Excise Offshore
    Application Act](/eng/acts/C-53); ([eaux douanières
    canadiennes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[continental shelf of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[continental shelf of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seabed
    and subsoil of those submarine areas that extend beyond the
    territorial sea throughout the natural prolongation of the land
    territory of Canada to the outer edge of the continental margin or
    to a distance of two hundred nautical miles from the inner limits of
    the territorial sea, whichever is the greater, or that extend to
    such other limits as are prescribed by the Governor in Council
    pursuant to subsection 2(3) of the [Customs and Excise Offshore
    Application Act](/eng/acts/C-53); ([plateau continental du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a passenger vessel having
    overnight accommodations for at least 100 persons, exclusive of crew
    accommodations, but does not include a ship engaged in scheduled
    passenger or cargo ferry service; ([navire de
    croisière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/98-28, s.
    9\]]{.Repealed}

[[duty-paid vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duty-paid vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that has
    been released in accordance with section 32 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([navire dédouané]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[inland waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inland waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the rivers, lakes
    and other fresh waters in Canada and includes the St. Lawrence River
    as far seaward as the straight lines drawn

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from Cap-des-Rosiers to the westernmost point of
      Anticosti Island, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from Anticosti Island to the north shore of the
      St. Lawrence River along the meridian of longitude sixty-three
      degrees west; ([eaux internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[internal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[internal waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any areas of the sea that are on the landward
      side of the baselines of the territorial sea and any areas of the
      sea, other than the territorial sea, in respect of which Canada
      has a historic or other title of sovereignty, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inland waters; ([eaux
      intérieures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Public Safety
    and Emergency Preparedness; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[modifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[modifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work done on a
    vessel, other than repairs; ([modifications]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[repairs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[repairs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the restoration of a vessel to
    the condition of the vessel at the time it last departed from
    Canadian customs waters, including any parts, materials and labour
    incurred in the making of the
    repairs; ([réparations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[storage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[storage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the safekeeping of a vessel
    that has been demobilized in a Canadian
    port; ([entreposage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[territorial sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[territorial sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the territorial sea of
    Canada as determined in accordance with the Territorial Sea and
    Fishing Zones Act; ([mer territoriale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good of heading 89.01, of
    subheading 8902.00, of tariff item No. 8903.91.00, 8903.92.00,
    8903.99.90 or 8904.00.00, of heading 89.05, of heading 89.06 or of
    tariff item No. 8907.90.90. ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Reduction or Removal of Customs Duty on Temporary Importation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925870 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 8 and 10, in the case of a vessel that is temporarily
  imported for use in Canadian customs waters, the customs duty on the
  vessel shall be reduced or removed in accordance with section 4, 5 or
  6 for a period not exceeding twelve consecutive months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 8 and 10, where the vessel
  referred to in subsection (1) remains in Canadian customs waters on
  the expiration of the period for which the customs duty is reduced or
  removed, the customs duty on the vessel shall be reduced or removed in
  accordance with section 4, 5 or 6 for an additional period or periods
  not exceeding 12 consecutive months.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [General Customs Duty Reduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925878 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** With the exception of the
removal of customs duty in accordance with section 5 or 6, the reduction
of customs duty on a vessel shall be an amount equal to the difference
between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the vessel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on 1/120 of the value for duty of
  the vessel for each month or part of a month during which the vessel
  remains in Canadian customs waters.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Cruise Ships Customs Duty Removal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925886 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a cruise
ship, the customs duty on the vessel shall be removed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Intercoastal Vessels Customs Duty Removal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925892 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
used to transport cargo between a point in Canada west of the meridian
of longitude ninety-five degrees and a point in Canada east of the
meridian of longitude ninety-five degrees, with the exception of
movements between points in Canada north of the meridian of latitude
sixty degrees, the customs duty on the vessel shall be removed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Customs Duty Removal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925898 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 9 and 10, in the case of a vessel that is placed in
  storage in a Canadian port facility, the customs duty on the vessel
  shall be removed for a period not exceeding 12 consecutive months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 8 and 10, where the vessel
  referred to in subsection (1) remains in Canadian customs waters on
  the expiration of the period for which the customs duty is removed,
  the customs duty on the vessel shall be removed in accordance with
  section 4, 5 or 6 for an additional period or periods not exceeding 12
  consecutive months.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Conditions for Customs Duty Reduction or Removal on Temporary Importation and Storage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925906 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The reduction or removal of
customs duty under section 3 shall be granted on condition that,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is not a duty-paid
  Canadian-registered vessel, no suitable duty-paid or domestically
  built Canadian-registered vessel is available to perform the service
  specified in the application referred to in paragraph 10(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is not a duty-paid
  foreign-registered vessel, no suitable Canadian-registered vessel is
  available to perform the service specified in the application referred
  to in paragraph 10(a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is not a duty-paid
  vessel and that engages in any activity referred to in subsections
  3(2.1) to (2.4) of the [Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3), that
  activity is specified in the application referred to in paragraph
  10(a).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
- SOR/2017-180, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The removal of customs duty
pursuant to section 7 shall be granted on condition that the importer
deposits with the Minister security, in a form that is satisfactory to
the Minister, in an amount up to that of the customs duty that, but for
these Regulations, would be payable on the vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The reduction or removal
of customs duty on a vessel pursuant to section 3 or 7 shall be granted
on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for the reduction or removal of
  customs duty for the purpose of temporary importation or storage is
  made to the Minister in a form that is satisfactory to the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application referred to in paragraph (a) is
  accompanied by documentary evidence that is satisfactory to the
  Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is temporarily imported,
  the vessel is used solely for the purpose for which the reduction or
  removal of customs duty is granted;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the expiration of the period for which the
  reduction or removal is granted, the vessel is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} exported from Canada or, in the case of a vessel
    conditionally exempted from customs duty under the [Customs and
    Excise Offshore Application Act](/eng/acts/C-53), removed beyond the
    territorial sea,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} released in accordance with section 32 of the
    [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} authorized by the Minister to be placed in a
    Canadian facility for repairs, overhaul or adjustment; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the vessel is readmitted into Canada at any
  time other than the time set out in section 3 or 7 or pursuant to any
  regulations made under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
  customs duty is paid in an amount calculated pursuant to the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) and the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
  with no deduction for any amount of customs duty previously reduced or
  removed under these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-234, s. 2(E)
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where the conditions set
out in section 8, 9 or 10 have not been met, the reduction of customs
duty pursuant to section 3 or 7 shall be,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel otherwise qualifying for a
  reduction of customs duty in accordance with section 4, an amount
  equal to the difference between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the vessel, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on 1/50 of the value for duty of
    the vessel for each month or part of a month during which the
    conditions set out in section 8, 9 or 10 are not met; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel otherwise qualifying for the
  removal of customs duty in accordance with section 5, 6 or 7, an
  amount equal to the difference between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the vessel, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on 1/100 of the value for duty of
    the vessel for each month or part of a month during which the
    conditions set out in section 8, 9 or 10 are not met.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Reduction or Removal of Customs Duty on Repaired or Modified Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925938 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 18,
the reduction or removal of customs duty, as determined in accordance
with section 13, 14, 15, 16 or 17, is granted on a Canadian-built or
duty-paid vessel that returns to Canadian customs waters after having
had repairs or modifications done abroad, except for a vessel that was
exported for the purpose of repair or modification.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Reduction of Customs Duty on Repairs Within 12 Months]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925944 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
that returns to Canadian customs waters within 12 months after having
had repairs done abroad, where the repairs were done during the course
of the vessel's operations abroad, except for a vessel having had
repairs done in accordance with section 15, the reduction of customs
duty on the vessel shall be equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the vessel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount calculated by applying the same rate of
  customs duty as would be applied to determine the customs duty on the
  vessel to the value of the repairs done to the vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Reduction of Customs Duty on Modifications Within Three Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925952 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
that returns to Canadian customs waters within three years after having
had modifications done abroad, where such modifications were done during
the course of the vessel's operations abroad, the reduction of customs
duty on the vessel shall be equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duty on the vessel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount calculated by applying the same rate of
  customs duty as would be applied to determine the customs duty on the
  vessel, to the value of the modifications done to the vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Removal of Customs Duty on Essential Repairs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925960 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
that returns to Canadian customs waters after having had repairs done
abroad, where the repairs resulted from an unforeseen contingency that
occurred outside Canadian customs waters and were necessary to ensure
the seaworthiness or safety of the vessel, or to enable the vessel to
safely reach its port of destination or return to Canadian customs
waters, the customs duty on the vessel shall be removed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Removal of Customs Duty on Repairs over 12 Months]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925966 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
that returns to Canadian customs waters more than 12 months after having
had repairs done abroad, the customs duty on the vessel shall be
removed, provided the vessel has not returned to Canadian customs waters
in the interim.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Removal of Customs Duty on Modifications over Three Years]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925972 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a vessel
that returns to Canadian customs waters more than three years after
having had modifications done abroad, the customs duty on the vessel
shall be removed, provided the vessel has not returned to Canadian
customs waters in the interim.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::

## [Conditions for Customs Duty Reduction or Removal on Repaired or Modified Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-925978 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)

\]
:::

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The reduction or removal
of customs duty under section 12 shall be granted on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all repairs or modifications are reported to the
  Minister on the return of the vessel in Canadian customs waters;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owners or operators of the vessel provide, in
  accordance with the request of the Minister, any invoice, work order
  log, report or other information relevant to the repair or
  modification of the vessel abroad; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any application for a reduction or removal of customs
  duty is made to the Minister within two years after the date the
  vessel returns to Canadian customs waters.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 10(E)
:::
