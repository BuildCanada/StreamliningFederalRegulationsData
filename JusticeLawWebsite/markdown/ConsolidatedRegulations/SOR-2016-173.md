## [Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831610 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Face protectors for ice
hockey and box lacrosse players must meet the requirements of standard
CAN3-Z262.2-M78, entitled Face Protectors for Ice Hockey and Box
Lacrosse Players, published by the Canadian Standards Association in
English in December 1978 and in French in June 1979.
