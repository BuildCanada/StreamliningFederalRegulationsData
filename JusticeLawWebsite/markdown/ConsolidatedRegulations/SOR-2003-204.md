## Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations Regulations {#continuation-of-amalgamated-or-merged-corporations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-204
:::

[EXCISE ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)

Registration 2003-06-05

Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations Regulations

P.C. 2003-858 2003-06-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(o) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-204).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  22
  :::
:::

## [Prescribed Purposes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702717 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
213 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), the purpose of applying
any provision of Parts 5 and 6, other than section 213, of that Act is a
prescribed purpose.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702719 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
