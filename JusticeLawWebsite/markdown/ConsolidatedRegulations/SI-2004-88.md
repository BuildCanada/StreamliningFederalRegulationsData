## Order Transferring from the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Office of Indian Residential Schools Resolution of Canada and Ordering the Deputy Prime Minister to Preside Over the Office {#order-transferring-from-the-president-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-to-the-deputy-prime-minister-and-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-indian-residential-schools-resolution-of-canada-and-ordering-the-deputy-prime-minister-to-preside-over-the-office .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-88
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Transferring from the President of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Office of
Indian Residential Schools Resolution of Canada and Ordering the Deputy
Prime Minister to Preside Over the Office

P.C. 2004-851 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers
  from the President of the Queen\'s Privy Council for Canada to the
  Solicitor General of Canada, styled Deputy Prime Minister and Minister
  of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the control and
  supervision of the portion of the public service known as the Office
  of Indian Residential Schools Resolution of Canada, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Solicitor General of Canada, styled
  Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness shall preside over the Office of Indian Residential
  Schools Resolution of Canada,

effective July 20, 2004.
