## National Capital Commission Traffic and Property Regulations {#national-capital-commission-traffic-and-property-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1044
:::

[NATIONAL CAPITAL ACT](/eng/acts/N-4)

Regulations Respecting the Property of the National Capital Commission
and the Operation of Vehicles Thereon
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483370 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Capital Commission Traffic and Property
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1044).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483372 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commercial vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has attached thereto a truck or delivery body
        or other attachment for use in the delivery of goods,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is specially designed or equipped for the
        delivery of goods,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bears any sign or lettering advertising the
        business or wares of any person or organization or displays the
        name or device of any person or organization by whom the vehicle
        is owned or on whose behalf the vehicle is operated, other than
        the name or device of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, a
        Department of the Government of Canada or of any corporation
        established to perform any function or duty on behalf of the
        Government of Canada,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a tractor, or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is a bus, taxicab or other vehicle used or
        designed for the carrying of passengers for hire; ([véhicule
        commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Capital
      Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[driveway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[driveway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of the property
      of the Commission designed and intended for the use of vehicles;
      ([promenade]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[liquor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[liquor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer, wines, spirits or any
      other alcoholic beverages; ([boisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle that is
      capable of being propelled or driven otherwise than by muscular
      power but does not include an over-snow vehicle; ([véhicule
      automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the driver or the person
      who is in actual physical control of the vehicle;
      ([conducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[over-snow vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[over-snow vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle that is
      capable of being propelled or driven otherwise than by muscular
      power, that runs on tracks or skis or on tracks and skis and that
      is designed for operation on snow or ice; ([véhicule de type
      auto-neige]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the standing of a vehicle,
      whether occupied or not, otherwise than momentarily or under
      circumstances that are not under the control of the person
      operating the vehicle; ([laisser en
      stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a peace officer as
      defined in the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) and any person
      authorized by the Commission to enforce or carry out these
      Regulations; ([agent de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device in, or by means of,
      which a person or property is or may be transported upon a
      highway, except any such device used exclusively upon stationary
      rails or tracks. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, a trailer is a
  separate vehicle and does not form part of the vehicle by which it is
  drawn.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in these Regulations shall be held to
  constitute a dedication of any driveway to the public.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-389, s. 1(F)
:::

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483394 .Part}

### [Registration and Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483395 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
vehicle on a driveway unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he holds all the licences and permits that he is
  required to hold in order to operate the vehicle in the province and
  municipality in which the driveway is situated; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is registered and equipped as required by
  the laws of the province and municipality in which the driveway is
  situated.

### [Compliance with Provincial and Municipal Laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483399 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a vehicle, or cause or permit a vehicle that he
  has the right to control to be operated, on a driveway otherwise than
  in accordance with the laws of the province and municipality in which
  the driveway is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a vehicle on a driveway except
  in compliance with the laws of the province and municipality in which
  the driveway is situated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the expression "laws of the province
  and municipality" does not include laws that are inconsistent with or
  repugnant to any of the provisions of these Regulations.

### [Traffic Control, Parking and Speed Limit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483404 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a vehicle on a driveway at a rate of speed, in
  kilometres per hour, that is greater than the speed posted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where no rate of speed is posted on a driveway, no
  person shall operate a vehicle on that driveway at a rate of speed
  greater than 60 kilometres per hour.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) and (2), where
  traffic on a driveway is controlled by a peace officer, every person
  operating a vehicle on that driveway shall do so at the rate of speed
  indicated by that peace officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-99, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate or
park a vehicle, or permit a vehicle under his control to remain parked,
on property of the Commission otherwise than in accordance with the
directions set out on a traffic sign or device erected under the
authority of these Regulations and applicable to such person.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Except as expressly
authorized by the Commission, no person shall park a vehicle on any
property of the Commission other than in an area designated by a sign or
device erected under the authority of these Regulations as an area in
which parking is permitted.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Sections 5, 6 and 7 do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} operators of police vehicles engaged in the chase of
  persons charged with or suspected, on reasonable grounds, of being
  violators of the law; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} operators of police vehicles, fire vehicles or
  ambulances who believe, on reasonable grounds, that the conduct that
  would otherwise be an offence is justified by an emergency.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a vehicle
and the person in control of a parked vehicle on any property of the
Commission shall obey all reasonable instructions given by any peace
officer.

### [Pedestrians]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483417 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No pedestrian shall
proceed onto property of the Commission at a place where a sign or
device has been erected under the authority of these Regulations
otherwise than in accordance with the instructions indicated by such
sign or device.

### [Commercial Vehicles]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483419 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a commercial vehicle on a driveway without the
  consent of the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in the delivery or collection of
    goods or persons to or from any property adjoining a driveway to
    which there is no other means of entry or exit if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the commercial vehicle operated by such person
      enters and leaves the driveway by the intersecting street that is
      nearest to that property, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the commercial vehicle on the
      driveway occurs between midnight and noon;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a commercial vehicle in the
    course of the business of the Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a taxicab;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a commercial vehicle that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is being used as a camper van, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has no advertising displayed thereon;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a light van or pick-up truck
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is used for personal transportation only,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a gross registered weight not exceeding
      5,000 pounds, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has no advertising, or lettering, other than
      the name of the owner thereof, displayed thereon; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person operating a commercial vehicle that has a
    gross registered weight not exceeding 8,000 pounds over the Portage
    Bridge between the City of Hull and Wellington Street in the City of
    Ottawa.

### [Reporting of Accidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483436 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every operator, owner or
person in charge of a vehicle that is involved in an accident on
property of the Commission shall report the accident forthwith to a
member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and furnish him with such
information concerning the accident as he may require.

### [General Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483438 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motor vehicle on any property of the Commission
  other than a driveway unless authorized by the Commission; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an over-snow vehicle on any property of the
  Commission that is not designated by a sign or device erected by order
  of the Commission as an area in which the operation of an over-snow
  vehicle is permitted.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall construct any private road, entranceway, gate or other
  structure or facility as a means of access to any driveway except with
  the consent in writing of the Commission and in accordance with such
  conditions as are specified by the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person operating a vehicle shall enter upon a
  driveway except at a place at which public access to the driveway is
  provided.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to prohibit entrance
  upon a driveway from any private road or entranceway constructed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on or before September 30, 1960; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after September 30, 1960 with the consent in
    writing of the Commission.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2002-165, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall ride a bicycle on property of the Commission other than a
  driveway or on a bicycle path set aside by the Commission for the
  purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall ride a bicycle on a driveway abreast
  of another bicycle or vehicle.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized by the
Commission, no person shall race any vehicle or animal on property of
the Commission.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use any
blasphemous or indecent language, or behave in an offensive manner, upon
any property of the Commission.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall throw,
deposit or leave on a driveway any glass, nails, tacks or scraps of
metal or other material that may be injurious to the tires of vehicles
using the driveway.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-483455 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A peace officer, upon
finding a vehicle parked contrary to these Regulations, may, at the
expense of the owner thereof, cause that vehicle to be moved or taken to
and placed or stored in a suitable place.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided by
these Regulations, no person shall remove, alter or deface any traffic
sign or device erected by the Commission.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairman, General Manager, Chief Engineer or Secretary of the
  Commission may order the erection on or removal from property of the
  Commission of traffic signs and devices.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent of Gatineau Park may order the
  erection or removal of traffic signs and devices within the area of
  the Park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any traffic sign or device erected by order of a
  person mentioned in subsections (1) and (2) shall be deemed to be
  erected by order of the Commission.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any traffic sign or device erected on property of the
  Commission shall, unless the contrary is established, be presumed to
  have been erected by order of the Commission.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/95-445,
s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Protection of Commission Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483464 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No person shall enter on
property of the Commission other than property on which the public is
permitted entry.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** No person shall throw,
deposit or leave any refuse or debris on property of the Commission
other than in such places as are specifically designated for that
purpose.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without
the authority of the Commission,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cut, break, injure, deface or defile any building,
  fence, bridge, sign, light or other work that is the property of the
  Commission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cut, break, injure, deface or defile any rock, tree,
  shrub, plant, flower or turf on property of the Commission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} remove any thing described in paragraphs (a) and (b)
  or any soil, gravel or other part of the property of the Commission,
  or cause or permit such removal; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have in his possession any property of the
  Commission.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No person shall disturb
or injure, or cause or permit to be disturbed or injured, any wild
animal or any bird, bird's nest or bird's eggs on property of the
Commission.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No person shall throw
stones or fire or discharge any missile, firearm, airgun, B.B. gun,
fireworks or torpedo on any property of the Commission except in areas
specifically designated by the Commission for that purpose.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No person, other than a
peace officer engaged in the discharge of his duties, shall, without the
written approval of the commission, carry any firearm or other weapon or
any trap on property of the Commission otherwise than in a vehicle.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-260, s. 1
:::

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** No person shall make,
light or feed a fire on any property of the Commission not specifically
designated for that purpose and no person shall leave a fire unattended.

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** No person shall bathe in
any waters on property of the Commission except as permitted by the
Commission, and no person shall make use of any property of the
Commission for the purpose of bathing in waters not on property of the
Commission, except in such places as are specifically designated for
such use.

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell or
offer or expose for sale any drink, goods or wares, or post or display
any signs, placards, flags or advertising devices, or solicit
subscriptions or contributions on or in any property of the Commission
without first obtaining permission in writing from the Commission to do
so.

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No person shall expose on
or in any property of the Commission any cards, dice, table, wheel or
other device upon, with or by which any game of chance may be played,
and no person shall play any such game upon, with or by such device on
or in any property of the Commission.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** No person shall play at
golf, tennis, baseball, football or soccer on any property of the
Commission except in those parts thereof that have been designated by
the Commission for that purpose.

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall hunt, take or kill any game or fish on property of the
  Commission except in those parts thereof that have been designated by
  the Commission for that purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall hunt, take or kill any game, or fish
  for any fish or engage in any activity relating to hunting or fishing
  in any designated area referred to in subsection (1) otherwise than in
  accordance with the laws of the province in which that area is
  situated.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** No person shall have
liquor in his possession on or in the property of the Commission other
than in accordance with the laws of the province in which the Commission
property is situated.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Except with the written
approval of the Commission, no person shall obstruct any stream or body
of water on the property of the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-389, s. 2
:::

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** No person shall camp,
picnic or erect a tent on any property of the Commission not
specifically designated by the Commission for that purpose.

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[Commission land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means real property or
      immovables owned by the Commission or under the control and
      management of the Commission. ([terrain de la
      Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[domestic animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of a
      species of vertebrates, other than fish, that has been
      domesticated by humans so as to live in a tame condition and
      depend on humans for survival. ([animal
      domestique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner of a domestic
      animal or the person having charge of the animal, except where the
      owner or the person is a minor, in which case
      [[keeper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the father or mother of the
      minor or another adult responsible for the minor.
      ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[leased recreational property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[leased recreational property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      Commission land, of which the Commission is the lessor, that is
      leased for recreational purposes to a municipality. ([terrain
      récréatif loué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no person who is the
  keeper of a domestic animal shall have the animal on Commission land
  except in accordance with the bylaws of the municipality in which the
  Commission land is situated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Commission land that is, by virtue of section 2 of
    the [National Capital Commission Animal
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-164), subject to those
    Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} leased recreational property.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-165, s. 2
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483502 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-445, s. 2

\]
:::

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes a provision of these Regulations is liable on summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding \$500 or to imprisonment for a term
not exceeding six months, or to both.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-389, s. 3
:::
