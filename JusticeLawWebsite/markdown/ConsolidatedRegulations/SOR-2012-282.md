## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788208 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Housing
      Act](/eng/acts/N-11). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[credit score]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[credit score]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a score that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issued by a credit reporting agency that is
        incorporated by or under an Act of Parliament or of the
        legislature of a province; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} based on a scale that is substantially
        equivalent to a scale used on June 26, 2011 by a credit
        reporting agency that was at that time incorporated by or under
        an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
        province. ([pointage de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      property consisting of one to four family housing units, together
      with any associated property interests or real rights, structures
      and facilities. ([immeuble résidentiel
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[first-time home buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first-time home buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual
      who, at the time of purchasing an eligible residential property,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has never before purchased an eligible
        residential property in Canada;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not, in the calendar year in question or at
        any time in the four preceding calendar years, occupied as a
        principal place of residence an eligible residential property in
        Canada that was owned, whether jointly with another person or
        otherwise, by

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their current spouse or common-law partner;
          or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is an individual who

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is living separate and apart from their
          spouse or common-law partner because of a breakdown of their
          marriage or common-law partnership,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been living separate and apart from
          their spouse or common-law partner for a period of at least 90
          days, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} began living separate and apart from their
          spouse or common-law partner in the calendar year in question
          or at any time in the four preceding calendar
          years. ([acheteur d'une première habitation]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[funded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[funded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a housing
      loan, that money under the loan has been advanced to the
      borrower. ([financé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gross debt service ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gross debt service ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      percentage of a borrower's gross annual income that is required to
      cover the annual payments associated with the eligible residential
      property against which a housing loan is secured. ([coefficient
      d'amortissement brut de la dette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[high ratio loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[high ratio loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a housing loan that
      is secured by an eligible residential property and whose principal
      amount, together with the outstanding balance of any loan having
      an equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property,
      is greater than 80% of the value of the eligible residential
      property at the time the loan is approved. ([prêt à ratio
      élevé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loan for the addition of family housing units]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loan for the addition of family housing
      units]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a housing loan that is secured
      by an eligible residential property, whose purpose is the
      improvement, conversion or development of the eligible residential
      property to increase the number of family housing units that it
      contains and whose only other purpose may be the discharge of the
      outstanding balance of a prior loan against the property. ([prêt
      pour l'ajout de logements familiaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[low ratio loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[low ratio loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a housing loan that
      is secured by an eligible residential property and whose principal
      amount, together with the outstanding balance of any loan having
      an equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property,
      is less than or equal to 80% of the value of the eligible
      residential property at the time the loan is approved. ([prêt à
      faible ratio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[newly built]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[newly built]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an eligible
      residential property, means that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property has never been occupied for
        residential purposes; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a condominium unit or a private
        portion of a divided co-ownership, the property has never been
        occupied for residential purposes other than during a period in
        which the borrower assumed occupancy on an interim basis before
        the registration of the condominium declaration or declaration
        of co-ownership or before taking possession. ([nouvellement
        construit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any period of three
      consecutive months beginning on January 1, April 1, July 1 or
      October 1. ([trimestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[total debt service ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[total debt service ratio]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      percentage of a borrower's gross annual income that is required to
      cover their annual payments associated with housing and all other
      debts. ([coefficient d'amortissement total de la
      dette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[value of the eligible residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[value of the eligible residential
      property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value that is ascribed to
      an eligible residential property by a lender or the Corporation
      for the purpose of granting or insuring a housing loan and that is
      verified using a method that is generally accepted by prudent
      lenders, insurers or professional residential property appraisers.
      If the purpose of the loan includes the purchase of the property,
      the value must not exceed

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purchase price of the property; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purchase price of the property plus the
        estimated cost to the borrower of planned improvements to the
        property, if the purpose of the loan also includes those
        improvements. ([valeur de l'immeuble résidentiel
        admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of value

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[value of the
  eligible residential property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1),
  generally accepted methods of verifying value include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use of a statistically reliable and up-to-date
    valuation model that assesses the value's reasonableness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fair-market-value appraisal by a professional
    residential property appraiser who is independent of the borrower;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a drive-by appraisal; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a review of the value of comparable properties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principal amount

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the principal
  amount of a loan does not include any housing loan insurance premiums.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788236 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No pre-existing contract

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every housing loan that is not insured under a contract of insurance
that the Corporation and an approved lender entered into before the
coming into force of these Regulations.

## [Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788239 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurability

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (4) to (6), a housing loan must meet the criteria set
  out in section 4 and, as applicable, section 5, 6 or 6.1 for the
  Corporation to be able to provide insurance against risks relating to
  it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of security and increased
  balance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if an insured housing loan is
  modified to both replace its security with a new eligible residential
  property and increase its outstanding balance, the increased portion
  is to be considered, for the purposes of these Regulations, as a new
  loan approved on the day on which the increase is approved, which must
  meet the criteria that apply on that day for the Corporation to be
  able to provide insurance against risks relating to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other modifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If any other modification is made to an insured
  housing loan that requires the payment of an additional housing loan
  insurance premium, the loan is to be considered, for the purposes of
  these Regulations, as a new loan approved on the day on which the
  modification is approved, which must meet the criteria that apply on
  that day for the Corporation to be able to provide insurance against
  risks relating to it. Any modification that does not require the
  payment of an additional housing loan insurance premium does not
  affect the Corporation's ability to provide insurance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan workout

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may provide insurance against risks
  relating to a housing loan that is made or modified in relation to a
  loan workout whose purpose is to reduce or avoid losses on a real or
  potential housing loan insurance claim on an outstanding insured
  housing loan if it meets the criteria set out in paragraph 4(a) and
  does not require the payment of an additional housing loan insurance
  premium.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan discharge

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may provide insurance against risks
  relating to a housing loan whose purpose is to discharge the
  outstanding balance of a prior insured housing loan if the new loan
  meets the criteria to which the prior loan was subject at the time of
  the discharge and does not require the payment of an additional
  housing loan insurance premium.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particular categories of loan

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may provide insurance against risks
  relating to a housing loan if the loan is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made to a borrower that is owned, otherwise
    guaranteed or subsidized by one of the following entities, and the
    purpose of the loan, guarantee or subsidy is to carry out a
    government social housing program:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a provincial or municipal government,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an agency or an agent corporation of the federal
      Crown, other than the Corporation, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an agency, agent corporation, mandatary
      corporation or housing corporation of a provincial or municipal
      government;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to a project that is owned, otherwise
    guaranteed or subsidized by an entity referred to in paragraph (a),
    and the purpose of the loan, guarantee or subsidy is to carry out a
    government social housing program;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} secured by a property consisting of more than four
    family housing units, together with any associated property
    interests or real rights, structures and facilities; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} secured by a property situated on a reserve as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General criteria

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A housing loan must be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} underwritten and administered by an approved lender
  or held in a registered retirement savings plan or a registered
  retirement income fund and administered by an approved lender; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} secured in first or second priority position by an
  eligible residential property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}High ratio loans

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A high
  ratio loan must meet the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, its principal
    amount, together with the outstanding balance of any loan having an
    equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property
    against which the loan is secured, must be less than or equal to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 95% of the value of the eligible residential
      property, if its value is no more than \$500,000, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$475,000 plus 90% of the value of the eligible
      residential property in excess of \$500,000, if its value is
      greater than \$500,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the loan must either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} include the purchase of the eligible residential
      property against which it is secured, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be the discharge of the outstanding balance of a
      prior uninsured low ratio loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (1.1), the loan must be
    scheduled to amortize over a period that does not exceed 25 years;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the value of the eligible residential property
    against which the loan is secured must be less than \$1,500,000;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the loan agreement allows for fluctuations in
    the amortization period as a result of a variable rate of interest
    during the term of the loan, the loan payment must be recalculated
    at least once every five years to conform to the original
    amortization schedule;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the loan agreement must establish scheduled
    principal and interest payments that will begin reducing the
    outstanding principal in accordance with the overall amortization
    schedule agreed to at the making of the loan, commencing on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the loan is funded,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the agreement of purchase and
      sale closes, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the improvement, conversion or
      development of the eligible residential property is completed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, at least one of
    its borrowers or guarantors must have a credit score that is greater
    than or equal to 600;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, the gross debt
    service ratio and the total debt service ratio must not exceed 39%
    and 44%, respectively;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the eligible residential property against which the
    loan is secured must contain at least one family housing unit that
    will be occupied by the borrower or by a person related to the
    borrower by marriage, common-law partnership or any legal
    parent-child relationship;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, it must be
    reasonably likely to be repaid, having regard to the borrower's
    capacity to make the loan payments while paying their other debts
    and meeting their other obligations over the term of the loan, based
    on reasonable assumptions as to what the highest loan payment over
    the term of the loan will be; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is part of a pool of loans on the
    direct basis of which marketable securities are issued, any
    securities issued on the direct basis of the pool after July 1, 2016
    must be guaranteed under subsection 14(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amortization period not exceeding 30
  years

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The loan may be scheduled to amortize over a period
  that exceeds 25 years, but does not exceed 30 years, if any of the
  borrowers is a first-time home buyer or if the eligible residential
  property against which the loan is secured is newly built.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credit score exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The criterion set out in paragraph (1)(g) does not
  apply if no more than 3% of the lender's high ratio loans and low
  ratio loans that were approved for insurance and funded during one of
  the following periods were loans in respect of which no borrower or
  guarantor had a credit score of at least 600:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding five
    quarters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding six
    quarters; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding seven
    quarters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt service ratio calculations ---
  certain loans

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(h), the gross debt
  service ratio and total debt service ratio are to be calculated using
  the annual payments, in respect of the loan and any other loan with an
  equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property, that
  would be required to conform to the amortization schedule agreed to by
  the borrower and the lender if the interest rate were the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest rate set out in the loan agreement
    plus 2%, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 5.25%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable likelihood of repayment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A high ratio loan does not meet the criterion set out
  in paragraph (1)(j) unless the lender or the Corporation has made
  reasonable efforts to verify the borrower's income and employment
  status or, if the borrower is self-employed, to assess the
  plausibility of the income reported by the borrower.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Low ratio loans

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A low
  ratio loan must meet the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan agreement must establish scheduled
    principal and interest payments that will begin reducing the
    outstanding principal in accordance with the overall amortization
    schedule agreed to at the making of the loan, commencing on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the loan is funded,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the agreement of purchase and
      sale closes, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the improvement, conversion or
      development of the eligible residential property is completed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2017-271, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is part of a pool of loans on the
    direct basis of which marketable securities are issued, any
    securities issued on the direct basis of the pool after July 1, 2016
    must be guaranteed under subsection 14(1) of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is not part of a pool of loans on the
    direct basis of which marketable securities are issued,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be insured on an individual basis
      on either the day on which it is funded or the day on which
      additional money is advanced to the borrower as part of the loan's
      refinancing,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any given day, for at least one day in the
      six-month period prior to that day, the loan must have been part
      of a pool of loans that meets the criterion set out in
      paragraph (c) or have not been insured,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the loan must have been in arrears, have been
      insured when it fell into arrears and since remained insured, and,
      as a result, not be eligible to be part of a pool of loans,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the loan must belong for insurance purposes to a
      portfolio of loans with the Corporation and at least 95% of all
      portfolio insured loans of the lender with the Corporation must
      meet the criterion set out in paragraph (c) or subparagraph (ii)
      or (iii), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be held or will be held in a
      registered retirement savings plan or a registered retirement
      income fund of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} any partnership that does not deal at arm's
        length, within the meaning of section 251 of the [Income Tax
        Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), with the borrower, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a [[connected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
        as defined in subsection 4901(2) of the [Income Tax
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945), to the borrower;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the loan must either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} include the purchase of the eligible residential
      property against which it is secured, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be the discharge of the outstanding balance of a
      prior low ratio loan;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the outstanding balance of the loan at any time
    over the term of the loan is not to be increased to exceed the
    balance that would have been outstanding at that time under the
    lender's original amortization schedule;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the amortization schedule is not to be extended
    over the term of the loan and is not to exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is for the purchase of the eligible
      residential property against which it is secured, 25 years, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is for the discharge of the
      outstanding balance of a prior low ratio loan, the lesser of 25
      years and the remaining amortization period of the prior low ratio
      loan;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} at either the time of the initial approval of the
    loan by an approved lender or discharge of the outstanding balance
    of a prior low ratio loan by the approved lender, the value of the
    eligible residential property against which it is insured must be
    less than \$1,000,000;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the loan agreement allows for fluctuations in
    the amortization period as a result of a variable rate of interest
    during the term of the loan, the loan payment must be recalculated
    at least once every five years to conform to the lender's original
    amortization schedule;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the mortgage or hypothecary
    insurance application, at least one of its borrowers or guarantors
    must have a credit score that is greater than or equal to 600;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the approved lender's initial
    approval of the loan or discharge of the outstanding balance of a
    prior low ratio loan, as the case may be, the gross debt service
    ratio and total debt service ratio must not exceed 39% and 44%,
    respectively;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the approved lender's initial
    approval of the loan or discharge of the outstanding balance of a
    prior low ratio loan, as the case may be, if the eligible
    residential property against which the loan is secured contains only
    one family housing unit, that unit will be occupied by the borrower
    or by a person related to them by marriage, common-law partnership
    or any legal parent-child relationship; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the mortgage or hypothecary
    insurance application, the loan must be reasonably likely to be
    repaid, having regard to the borrower's capacity to make the loan
    payments while paying their other debts and meeting their other
    obligations over the term of the loan, based on reasonable
    assumptions as to what the highest loan payment over the term of the
    loan will be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credit score exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The criterion specified in paragraph (1)(j) does not
  apply if no more than 3% of the lender's high ratio loans and low
  ratio loans that were approved for insurance and funded during one of
  the following periods were loans in respect of which no borrower or
  guarantor had a credit score of at least 600:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding five
    quarters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding six
    quarters; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding seven
    quarters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt service ratio calculations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(k), the gross debt
  service ratio and total debt service ratio must be calculated using
  the annual payments, in respect of the loan and any other loan with an
  equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property, that
  would be required to conform to the amortization schedule agreed to by
  the borrower and the lender if the interest rate were the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest rate set out in the loan agreement
    plus 2%, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 5.25%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt service ratio exception

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} The criterion specified in paragraph (1)(k) does
  not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan is for the discharge of the outstanding
    balance of a prior low ratio loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lender of the loan being discharged is a bank
    to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies, an association to
    which the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)
    applies or a company to which the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) or the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the housing loan insurance application in respect
    of the loan is received on or after December 16, 2024.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable likelihood of repayment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A low ratio loan does not meet the criterion set out
  in paragraph (1)(m) unless the approved lender or the Corporation has
  made reasonable efforts to verify the borrower's income and employment
  status or, if the borrower is self-employed, to assess the
  plausibility of the income reported by the borrower.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan for the addition of family housing
units

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  loan for the addition of family housing units may meet the following
  criteria instead of those set out in section 5 or 6:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, the borrower must
    be the owner of the eligible residential property against which the
    loan is secured;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, its principal
    amount, together with the outstanding balance of any loan having an
    equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property
    against which the loan is secured, must be less than or equal to 90%
    of the estimated value of the eligible residential property after
    the completion of the improvement, conversion or development that
    will increase the number of family housing units;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the principal amount of the loan
    that exceeds the outstanding balance, at the time the loan is
    approved, of any prior loan against the eligible residential
    property must not exceed the estimated cost to the borrower of the
    improvement, conversion or development that will increase the number
    of family housing units;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the added family housing units must not be rented
    for any period of less than 90 consecutive days;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the loan must be scheduled to amortize over a
    period that does not exceed 30 years;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated value of the eligible residential
    property against which the loan is secured, after the completion of
    the improvement, conversion or development that will increase the
    number of family housing units, must be less than \$2,000,000;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the eligible residential property against which the
    loan is secured must contain at least one family housing unit that,
    at the time the loan is approved, is occupied by the borrower or by
    a person related to them by marriage, common-law partnership or any
    legal parent-child relationship and at least one family housing unit
    that will be occupied as such after the completion of the
    improvement, conversion or development;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the loan agreement allows for fluctuations in
    the amortization period as a result of a variable rate of interest
    during the term of the loan, the loan payment must be recalculated
    at least once every five years to conform to the original
    amortization schedule;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the loan agreement must establish scheduled
    principal and interest payments that will begin reducing the
    outstanding principal in accordance with the overall amortization
    schedule agreed to at the making of the loan, commencing on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the loan is last funded, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the improvement, conversion or
      development of the eligible residential property is completed;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, at least one of
    its borrowers or guarantors must have a credit score that is greater
    than or equal to 600;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, the gross debt
    service ratio and the total debt service ratio must not exceed 39%
    and 44%, respectively;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} at the time the loan is approved, it must be
    reasonably likely to be repaid, having regard to the borrower's
    capacity to make the loan payments while paying their other debts
    and meeting their other obligations over the term of the loan, based
    on reasonable assumptions as to what the highest loan payment over
    the term of the loan will be; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if the loan is part of a pool of loans on the
    direct basis of which marketable securities are issued, those
    securities must be guaranteed under subsection 14(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credit score exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The criterion set out in paragraph (1)(j) does not
  apply if no more than 3% of the lender's high ratio loans and low
  ratio loans that were approved for insurance and funded during one of
  the following periods were loans in respect of which no borrower or
  guarantor had a credit score of at least 600:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding five
    quarters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding six
    quarters; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the first four quarters of the preceding seven
    quarters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt service ratio calculations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(k), the gross debt
  service ratio and total debt service ratio are to be calculated using
  the annual payments, in respect of the loan and any other loan with an
  equal or prior claim against the eligible residential property, that
  would be required to conform to the amortization schedule agreed to by
  the borrower and the lender if the interest rate were the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the interest rate set out in the loan agreement
    plus 2%, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 5.25%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable likelihood of repayment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A loan does not meet the criterion set out in
  paragraph (1)(l) unless the lender or the Corporation has made
  reasonable efforts to verify the borrower's income and employment
  status or, if the borrower is self-employed, to assess the
  plausibility of the income reported by the borrower.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- applications before
  January 15, 2025

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} This section applies only to loans in respect of
  which the housing loan insurance application is received on or after
  January 15, 2025.
