## Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order, 1986 {#energy-administration-act-sections-53-to-65-non-application-order-1986 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1049
:::

[ENERGY ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/E-6)

Registration 1986-10-30

Order Declaring Sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act To Be
Non-Applicable in Respect of any Producer-Province

P.C. 1986-2427 1986-10-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to
subsection 52(1.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_ea){#footnote_ea-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Energy Administration Act](/eng/acts/E-6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote_eb){#footnote_eb-ID0EDCAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Order declaring sections 53 to 65 of the
Energy Administration Act to be non-applicable in respect of any
producer-province, effective November 1, 1986.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_ea-ID0EGCAA){#footnote_ea}S.C.
  1986, c. 39, s. 5
  :::

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888514 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order,
1986.

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888516 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 53 to 65 of the
[Energy Administration Act](/eng/acts/E-6) do not apply in respect of
any producer-province.
