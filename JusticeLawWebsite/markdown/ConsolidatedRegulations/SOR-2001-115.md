## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-666457 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means employment with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a surface lease to mine or process
      any uranium-bearing material granted pursuant to The Provincial
      Lands Act, R.S.S. 1978, c. P-31, The Forest Act, R.S.S. 1978,
      c. F-19, or The Forest Resources Management Act, S.S. 1996,
      c. F-19.1; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who operates a uranium mine or mill that
      mines or processes uranium-bearing material for or on behalf of a
      person mentioned in paragraph (a). ([emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[uranium mine or mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[uranium mine or mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a uranium mine or
    uranium mill in the Province of Saskatchewan that is subject to the
    [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([mine d'uranium
    ou usine de concentration d'uranium]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-666463 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Employment on or in
connection with a uranium mine or mill is hereby excluded from the
application of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 122 to 158 of the [Canada Labour
  Code](/eng/acts/L-2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Non-Smokers' Health Act](/eng/acts/N-23.6),
  except section 8.2.

## [Application of Saskatchewan Legislation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-666467 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
following acts and regulations, as amended from time to time, apply in
relation to employment on or in connection with a uranium mine or mill:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} The Boiler and Pressure Vessel Act, R.S.S. 1978,
  c. B-5, except section 39, and the following regulations made under
  that Act, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Regulations Respecting The Design, Construction,
    Installation and Use of Boilers and Pressure Vessels, SR 262/57,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Regulations Respecting Compressed Gas Pressure
    Vessels, SR 99/70,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Regulations Respecting Examinations and
    Certificates of Engineers and Firemen, SR 311/77, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Regulations Respecting the Welding of Boilers,
    Pressure Vessels and Pressure Piping, SR 61/78;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} The Electrical Inspection Act, 1993, S.S. 1993,
  c. E-6.3, and the following regulations made under that Act, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} The Electrical Inspection Regulations,
    Chapter E-6.3, Reg 1, except sections 19 to 21,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} The Use of Electricity in Mines Regulations, 1996,
    Chapter E-6.3, Reg 4,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Electrical Code (Adoption)
    Regulations, 1999, Chapter E-6.3, Reg 5, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Electrical Code (Saskatchewan
    Amendments) Regulations, 1999, Chapter E-6.3, Reg 6;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} The Electrical Licensing Act, S.S. 1988-89, c. E-7.2;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} The Gas Inspection Act, 1993, S.S. 1993, c. G-3.2,
  and The Gas Inspection Regulations, Chapter G-3.2, Reg 1;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} The Gas Licensing Act, S.S. 1988-89, c. G-4.1, and
  The Gas Licensing Regulations, Chapter G-4.1, Reg 1, except
  sections 10, 12 and 13;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} The Occupational Health and Safety Act, 1993, S.S.
  1993, c. O-1.1, and the following regulations made or continued under
  that Act, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} The Occupational Health and Safety Regulations,
    1996, Chapter O-1.1, Reg 1, except section 84 and Parts XXVII, XXIX
    and XXXI, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} The Mines Regulations, SR 284/78, except
    Part XXVII;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} The Passenger and Freight Elevator Act, R.S.S. 1978,
  c. P-4, and The Passenger and Freight Elevator Regulations, 1982,
  Chapter P-4, Reg 1; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} The Radiation Health and Safety Act, 1985, S.S.
  1984-85-86, c. R-1.1, except section 18 and subsection 19(1), and The
  Radiation Health and Safety Regulations, Chapter R-1.1, Reg 1, except
  subsections 21(6) and (7), sections 24 to 28, 32, and 40 to 46,
  clause 47(1)(c), sections 48 to 50, subclauses 51(1)(b)(i) and 2(b)(i)
  and Forms A and B of Part III of the Appendix.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "notwithstanding section 29 of The
  Summary Offences Procedure Act, 1990" in clause 59(1)(a) of The
  Occupational Health and Safety Act, 1993 shall be read as a reference
  to "notwithstanding subsection 787(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46)";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 4(1) of The Boiler and Pressure Vessel Act
  shall be read as if the reference to "that are subject to the
  legislative authority of the province" did not appear;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reference to "C.R.C." wherever it occurs in the
  Regulations Respecting Compressed Gas Pressure Vessels, SR 99/70,
  shall be read as a reference to the Canadian Transportation Agency;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 3 of the Passenger and Freight Elevator Act
  shall be read as if the reference to ", or subject to, the legislative
  authority of" did not appear.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In case of an inconsistency
between any of the provisions of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and any regulations made under that Act and the
provisions of these Regulations and the provisions of any act or
regulation as incorporated into section 3 of these Regulations, the
provisions of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) and
its regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
