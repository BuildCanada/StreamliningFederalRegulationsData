## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804493 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written statement
    confirmed by oath or a solemn
    declaration. ([affidavit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that files an
    application with the Agency. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document that is filed
    to commence a proceeding before the Agency under any legislation or
    regulations that are administered in whole or in part by the
    Agency. ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that the Agency is
    ordinarily open for business. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dispute proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dispute proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any contested
    matter that is commenced by application to the Agency. ([instance de
    règlement des différends]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any information that is
    recorded in any form. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose request to
    intervene filed under section 29 has been
    granted. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant, a respondent or a
    person that is named by the Agency as a
    party. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a partnership and an
    unincorporated association. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any matter that is
    commenced by application to the Agency, whether contested or
    not. ([instance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that is named as a
    respondent in an application and any person that is named by the
    Agency as a respondent. ([défendeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804508 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispute proceedings

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, these Rules apply to dispute proceedings other than a matter that is
the subject of mediation.

## [All Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804511 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In all proceedings, one
member constitutes a quorum.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Principle of proportionality

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Agency is to conduct
all proceedings in a manner that is proportionate to the importance and
complexity of the issues at stake and the relief claimed.

## [Dispute Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804516 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804517 .Subheading}

#### [Interpretation and Dispensing with Compliance]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804518 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of Rules

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Rules are to be interpreted in a manner that facilitates the most
  expeditious determination of every dispute proceeding, the optimal use
  of Agency and party resources and the promotion of justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency's initiative

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Anything that may be done on request under these
  Rules may also be done by the Agency of its own initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing with compliance and varying
rule

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may, at the
request of a person, dispense with compliance with or vary any rule at
any time or grant other relief on any terms that will allow for the just
determination of the issues.

#### [Filing of Documents and Sending of Copy to Parties]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  document filed under these Rules must be filed with the Secretary of
  the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency's public record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All filed documents are placed on the Agency's public
  record unless the person filing the document files, at the same time,
  a request for confidentiality under section 31 in respect of the
  document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to parties

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person that files a
document must, on the same day, send a copy of the document to each
party or, if a party is represented, to the party's representative,
except if the document is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a confidential version of a document in respect of
  which a request for confidentiality is filed under section 31;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a position statement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of transmission

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Documents may be filed with
the Agency and copies may be sent to the other parties by courrier,
personal delivery, email, facsimile or other electronic means specified
by the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facsimile --- cover page

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person that files or
sends a document by facsimile must include a cover page indicating the
total number of pages transmitted, including the cover page, and the
name and telephone number of a contact person if problems occur in the
transmission of the document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic transmission

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is sent by email, facsimile or other electronic means is
  considered to be filed with the Agency and received by the other
  parties on the date of its transmission if it is sent at or before
  5:00 p.m. Gatineau local time on a business day. A document that is
  sent after 5:00 p.m. Gatineau local time or on a day that is not a
  business day is considered to be filed with the Agency and received by
  the other parties on the next business day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Courier or personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is sent by courier or personal
  delivery is filed with the Agency and received by the other parties on
  the date of its delivery if it is delivered to the Agency and the
  other parties at or before 5:00 p.m. Gatineau local time on a business
  day. A document that is delivered after 5:00 p.m. Gatineau local time
  or on a day that is not a business day is considered to be filed with
  the Agency and received by the other parties on the next business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing after time limit

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not file a document after the end of the applicable time
  limit for filing the document unless a request has been filed under
  subsection 30(1) and the request has been granted by the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing not provided for in Rules

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not file a document whose filing is not
  provided for in these Rules unless a request has been filed under
  subsection 34(1) and the request has been granted by the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to comply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is filed in contravention of
  subsection (1) or (2) will not be placed on the Agency's record.

#### [Language of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804553 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}English or French

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  document filed with the Agency must be in either English or French.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person files a document that is in a language
  other than English or French, they must at the same time file an
  English or French translation of the document and the information
  referred to in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Treated as original

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The translation is treated as the original for the
  purposes of the dispute proceeding.

#### [Amended Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804561 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substantive amendment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person proposes to make a substantive amendment to a previously filed
  document, they must file a request under subsection 33(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that files a document that amends a
  previously filed document, whether the amendment is substantive or
  not, must ensure that the amendment is clearly identified in the
  document and that the word "AMENDED" appears in capital letters in the
  top right corner of the first page.

#### [Verification by Affidavit or by Witnessed Statement]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804567 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of contents

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Agency considers it just and reasonable, the Agency may, by notice,
  require that a person provide verification of the contents of all or
  any part of a document by affidavit or by witnessed statement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of verification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The verification by affidavit or by witnessed
  statement must be filed within five business days after the date of
  the notice referred to in subsection (1) and must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 2 or Schedule 3, respectively.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to file verification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may strike the document or the part of the
  document in question from the Agency's record if the person fails to
  file the verification.

#### [Representation and Change of Contact Information]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804575 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative not a member of the bar

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A person that is
represented in a dispute proceeding by a person that is not a member of
the bar of a province must authorize that person to act on their behalf
by filing the information referred to in Schedule 4.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of contact information

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A person must, if the
contact information they provided to the Agency changes during the
course of a dispute proceeding, provide their new contact information to
the Agency and the parties without delay.

### [Pleadings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804580 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804581 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of application

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  application filed with the Agency must include the information
  referred to in Schedule 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application complete

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application is complete, the parties are
  notified in writing that the application has been accepted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incomplete application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the application is incomplete, the applicant is
  notified in writing and the applicant must provide the missing
  information within 20 business days after the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closure of file

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant fails to provide the missing
  information within the time limit, the file is closed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An applicant whose file is closed may file a new
  application in respect of the same matter.

#### [Answer]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804593 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of answer

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A respondent may file an
answer to the application. The answer must be filed within 15 business
days after the date of the notice indicating that the application has
been accepted and must include the information referred to in
Schedule 6.

#### [Reply]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804596 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of reply

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant may file a reply to the answer. The reply must be filed
  within five business days after the day on which they receive a copy
  of the answer and must include the information referred to in
  Schedule 7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new issues

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reply must not raise issues or arguments that are
  not addressed in the answer or introduce new evidence unless a request
  has been filed to that effect and the request has been granted by the
  Agency.

#### [Intervention]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804602 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of intervention

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  intervener may file an intervention. The intervention must be filed
  within five business days after the day on which their request to
  intervene is granted by the Agency and must include the information
  referred to in Schedule 8.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An intervener's participation is limited to the
  participation rights granted by the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to intervention

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** An applicant or a
respondent that is adverse in interest to an intervener may file a
response to the intervention. The response must be filed within five
business days after the day on which they receive a copy of the
intervention and must include the information referred to in Schedule 9.

#### [Position Statement]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804610 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of position statement

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interested person may file a position statement. The position
  statement must be filed before the close of pleadings and must include
  the information referred to in Schedule 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No participation rights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that files a position statement has no
  participation rights and is not entitled to receive any notice in the
  dispute proceeding.

#### [Written Questions and Production of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804616 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may, by notice, request that any party that is adverse in
  interest respond to written questions that relate to the matter in
  dispute or produce documents that are in their possession or control
  and that relate to the matter in dispute. The notice must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 11 and must be filed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of written questions, before the close
    of pleadings; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the production of documents, within
    five business days after the day on which the party becomes aware of
    the documents or before the close of pleadings, whichever is
    earlier.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party to which a notice has been given must,
  within five business days after the day on which they receive a copy
  of the notice, file a complete response to each question or the
  requested documents, as the case may be, accompanied by the
  information referred to in Schedule 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party wishes to object to a question or to
  producing a document, that party must, within the time limit set out
  in subsection (2), file an objection that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a clear and concise explanation of the reasons for
    the objection including, as applicable, the relevance of the
    information or document requested and their availability for
    production;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document that is relevant in explaining or
    supporting the objection; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information or document that is in the
    party's possession or control and that would be of assistance to the
    party making the request.

#### [Expedited Process]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804629 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision to apply expedited process

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may, at the request of a party under section 28, decide that an
  expedited process applies to an answer under section 19 and a reply
  under section 20 or to any request filed under these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits for filing --- answer and
  reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an expedited process applies to an answer under
  section 19 and a reply under section 20, the following time limits
  apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the answer must be filed within five business days
    after the date of the notice indicating that the application has
    been accepted; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reply must be filed within three business days
    after the day on which the applicant receives a copy of the answer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits for filing --- request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an expedited process applies to a request filed
  under these Rules, the following time limits apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any response to a request must be filed within two
    business days after the day on which the person who is responding to
    the request receives a copy of the request; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reply to a response must be filed within one
    business day after the day on which the person who is replying to
    the response receives a copy of the response.

#### [Close of Pleadings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804641 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Normal process

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), pleadings
  are closed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if no answer is filed, 20 business days after the
    date of the notice indicating that the application has been
    accepted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an answer is filed and no additional documents
    are filed after that answer, 25 business days after the date of the
    notice indicating that the application has been accepted; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if additional documents are filed after an answer
    is filed, the day on which the last document is to be filed under
    these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expedited process

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Under the expedited process, pleadings are closed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if no answer is filed, seven business days after
    the date of the notice indicating that the application has been
    accepted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an answer is filed and no additional documents
    are filed after that answer, 10 business days after the date of the
    notice indicating that the application has been accepted; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if additional documents are filed after an answer
    is filed, the day on which the last document is to be filed under
    these Rules.

### [Requests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804653 .Subheading}

#### [General Request]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804654 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of request

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may file a request for a decision on any issue that arises
  within a dispute proceeding and for which a specific request is not
  provided for under these Rules. The request must be filed as soon as
  feasible but, at the latest, before the close of pleadings and must
  include the information referred to in Schedule 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any party may file a response to the request. The
  response must be filed within five business days after the day on
  which they receive a copy of the request and must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person that filed the request may file a reply to
  the response. The reply must be filed within two business days after
  the day on which they receive a copy of the response and must include
  the information referred to in Schedule 15.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new issues

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The reply must not raise issues or arguments that are
  not addressed in the response or introduce new evidence unless a
  request has been filed to that effect and the request has been granted
  by the Agency.

#### [Specific Requests]{.HTitleText3} {#h-804664 .Subheading}

##### [Request for Expedited Process]{.HTitleText4} {#h-804665 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expedited process

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may file a request to have an expedited process applied to an
  answer under section 19 and a reply under section 20 or to another
  request filed under these Rules. The request must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justification for request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party filing the request must demonstrate to the
  satisfaction of the Agency that adherence to the time limits set out
  in these Rules would cause them financial or other prejudice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for filing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The request must be filed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the request is to have an expedited process
    apply to an answer and a reply,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an applicant, at the time that the
      application is filed, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a respondent, within one business
      day after the date of the notice indicating that the application
      has been accepted; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the request is to have an expedited process
    apply to another request,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person filing the other request,
      at the time that that request is filed, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person responding to the other
      request, within one business day after the day on which they
      receive a copy of that request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any party may file a response to the request. The
  response must be filed within one business day after the day on which
  they receive a copy of the request and must include the information
  referred to in Schedule 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The party that filed the request may file a reply to
  the response. The reply must be filed within one business day after
  the day on which they receive a copy of the response and must include
  the information referred to in Schedule 15.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new issues

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The reply must not raise issues or arguments that are
  not addressed in the response or introduce new evidence unless a
  request has been filed to that effect and the request has been granted
  by the Agency.

##### [Request to Intervene]{.HTitleText4} {#h-804685 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to intervene

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that has a substantial and direct interest in a dispute
  proceeding may file a request to intervene. The request must be filed
  within 10 business days after the day on which the person becomes
  aware of the application or before the close of pleadings, whichever
  is earlier, and must include the information referred to in
  Schedule 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Agency grants the request, it may set limits
  and conditions on the intervener's participation in the dispute
  proceeding.

##### [Request to Extend or Shorten Time Limit]{.HTitleText4} {#h-804691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extend or shorten

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may file a request to extend or shorten a time limit that
  applies in respect of a dispute proceeding. The request may be filed
  before or after the end of the time limit and must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any party may file a response to the request. The
  response must be filed within three business days after the day on
  which they receive a copy of the request and must include the
  information referred to in Schedule 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person that filed the request may file a reply to
  the response. The reply must be filed within one business day after
  the day on which they receive a copy of the response and must include
  the information referred to in Schedule 15.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No new issues

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The reply must not raise issues or arguments that are
  not addressed in the response or introduce new evidence unless a
  request has been filed to that effect and the request has been granted
  by the Agency.

##### [Request for Confidentiality]{.HTitleText4} {#h-804701 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidential treatment

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may file a request for confidentiality in respect of a document
  that they are filing. The request must include the information
  referred to in Schedule 17 and must be accompanied by, for each
  document identified as containing confidential information,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one public version of the document from which the
    confidential information has been redacted; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one confidential version of the document that
    identifies the confidential information that has been redacted from
    the public version of the document and that includes, at the top of
    each page, the words: "CONTAINS CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION" in capital
    letters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency's record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for confidentiality and the public
  version of the document from which the confidential information has
  been redacted are placed on the Agency's public record. The
  confidential version of the document is placed on the Agency's
  confidential record pending a decision of the Agency on the request
  for confidentiality.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for disclosure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any party may oppose a request for confidentiality by
  filing a request for disclosure. The request must be filed within five
  business days after the day on which they receive a copy of the
  request for confidentiality and must include the information referred
  to in Schedule 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to request for disclosure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The person that filed the request for confidentiality
  may file a response to a request for disclosure. The response must be
  filed within three business days after the day on which they receive a
  copy of the request for disclosure and must include the information
  referred to in Schedule 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency's decision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Agency determines that the document is not
    relevant to the dispute proceeding, decide to not place the document
    on the Agency's record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Agency determines that the document is
    relevant to the dispute proceeding and that no specific direct harm
    would likely result from its disclosure or that any demonstrated
    specific direct harm is not sufficient to outweigh the public
    interest in having it disclosed, decide to place the document on the
    Agency's public record; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Agency determines that the document is
    relevant to the dispute proceeding and that the specific direct harm
    likely to result from its disclosure justifies confidentiality,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} decide to confirm the confidentiality of the
      document or any part of it and keep the document or part of the
      document on the Agency's confidential record,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} decide to place a version of the document or any
      part of it from which the confidential information has been
      redacted on the Agency's public record,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} decide to keep the document or any part of it
      on the Agency's confidential record but require that the person
      requesting confidentiality provide a copy of the document or part
      of the document in confidence to any party to the dispute
      proceeding, or to certain of their advisors, experts and
      representatives, as specified by the Agency, after the person
      requesting confidentiality has received a signed undertaking of
      confidentiality from the person to which the copy is to be
      provided, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} make any other decision that it considers just
      and reasonable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of undertaking of
  confidentiality

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The original copy of the undertaking of
  confidentiality must be filed with the Agency.

##### [Request to Require Party to Provide Complete Response]{.HTitleText4} {#h-804724 .Subheading}

  not satisfied with the response to the notice or if they wish to
  contest an objection to their request, file a request to require the
  party to which the notice was directed to provide a complete response.
  The request must be filed within two business days after the day on

  referred to in Schedule 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency's decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may do any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require that a question be answered in full or in
    part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require that a document be provided;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require that a party submit secondary evidence of
    the contents of a document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require that a party produce a document for
    inspection only;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} deny the request in whole or in part.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} approve the request in whole or in part and, if the

### [Case Management]{.HTitleText2} {#h-804779 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formulation of issues

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may formulate
the issues to be considered in a dispute proceeding in any of the
following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the documents filed do not clearly identify the
  issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the formulation would assist in the conduct of the
  dispute proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the formulation would assist the parties to
  participate more effectively in the dispute proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary determination

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may, at the
request of a party, determine that an issue should be decided as a
preliminary question.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joining of applications

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may, at the
request of a party, join two or more applications and consider them
together in one dispute proceeding to provide for a more efficient and
effective process.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conference

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may, at the request of a party, require the parties to attend a
  conference by a means of telecommunication or by personal attendance
  for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} encouraging settlement of the dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} formulating, clarifying or simplifying the issues;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining the terms of amendment of any document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} obtaining the admission of certain facts or
    determining whether the verification of those facts by affidavit
    should be required;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establishing the procedure to be followed in the
    dispute proceeding;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} providing for the exchange by the parties of
    documents proposed to be submitted;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establishing a process for the identification and
    treatment of confidential information;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} discussing the appointment of experts; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} resolving any other issues to provide for a more
    efficient and effective process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written submissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The parties may be required to file written
  submissions on any issue that is discussed at the conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minutes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Minutes are prepared in respect of the conference and
  placed on the Agency's record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agency decision or direction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may issue a decision or direction on any
  issue discussed at the conference without further submissions from the
  parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay of dispute proceeding

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may, at the request of a party, stay a dispute proceeding in
  any of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision is pending on a preliminary question in
    respect of the dispute proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decision is pending in another proceeding or
    before any court in respect of an issue that is the same as or
    substantially similar to one raised in the dispute proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a party to the dispute proceeding has not complied
    with a requirement of these Rules or with a procedural direction
    issued by the Agency;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Agency considers it just and reasonable to do
    so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay of decision or order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may, at the request of a party, stay a
  decision or order of the Agency in any of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a review or re-hearing is being considered by the
    Agency under section 32 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review is being considered by the Governor in
    Council under section 40 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application for leave to appeal is made to the
    Federal Court of Appeal under section 41 of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Agency considers it just and reasonable to do
    so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stay --- terms and conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In staying a dispute proceeding or a decision or
  order, the Agency may impose any terms and conditions that it
  considers to be just and reasonable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to dismiss
application

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may, by notice to the applicant and before considering the
  issues raised in the application, require that the applicant justify
  why the Agency should not dismiss the application if the Agency is of
  the preliminary view that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Agency does not have jurisdiction over the
    subject matter of the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dispute proceeding would constitute an abuse of
    process; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application contains a fundamental defect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must respond to the notice within
  10 business days after the date of the notice, failing which the
  application may be dismissed without further notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to comment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may provide any other party with an
  opportunity to comment on whether or not the application should be
  dismissed.
