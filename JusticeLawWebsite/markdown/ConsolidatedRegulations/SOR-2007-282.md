## Directive to the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Regarding the Health of Canadians {#directive-to-the-canadian-nuclear-safety-commission-regarding-the-health-of-canadians .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-282
:::

[NUCLEAR SAFETY AND CONTROL ACT](/eng/acts/N-28.3)

Registration 2007-12-10

Directive to the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Regarding the Health
of Canadians

P.C. 2007-1892 2007-12-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to subsection 19(1) of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_nscadirective3_hq_3401){#fn_nscadirective3_hq_3401-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Directive to the Canadian Nuclear Safety
Commission Regarding the Health of
Canadians](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-282).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_nscadirective3_hq_3401-ID0EDBAA){#fn_nscadirective3_hq_3401}S.C.
  1997, c. 9
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In regulating the
production, possession and use of nuclear substances in order to prevent
unreasonable risk to the health of persons, the Canadian Nuclear Safety
Commission shall take into account the health of Canadians who, for
medical purposes, depend on nuclear substances produced by nuclear
reactors.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Directive comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
