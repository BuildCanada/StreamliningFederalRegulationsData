## Access to Information Act Extension Order, No. 1 {#access-to-information-act-extension-order-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-207
:::

[ACCESS TO INFORMATION ACT](/eng/acts/A-1)

Registration 1989-04-13

Order Extending the Right To Be Given Access under Subsection 4(1) of
the Access to Information Act to Records under the Control of a
Government Institution

P.C. 1989-619 1989-04-13
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 4(2) of the [Access to
Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Order extending the right to be given access under subsection 4(1) of
the Access to Information Act to records under the control of a
government institution](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-207).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918840 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Access to Information Act Extension Order,
No. 1](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-207).

## [Extension]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918842 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The right to be given
access under subsection 4(1) of the [Access to Information
Act](/eng/acts/A-1) to records under the control of a government
institution is hereby extended to include all individuals who are
present in Canada but who are not Canadian citizens or permanent
residents within the meaning of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) and all corporations that are present in Canada.
