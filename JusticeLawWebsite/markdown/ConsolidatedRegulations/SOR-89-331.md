Regulations Respecting the Management and Operation of the Marine
Railway Dry Dock at Selkirk, Manitoba

P.C. 1989-1198 1989-06-22

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
sections 24, 28 and 29 of the Public Works Act, is pleased hereby to
revoke the Selkirk Marine Railway Dry Dock Regulations, made by Order in
Council P.C. 1982-1309 of April 29, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, and
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the management and operation
of the Marine Railway Dry Dock at Selkirk,
Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-331), in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919426 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Selkirk Marine Railway Dry Dock Regulations,
1989](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-331).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919428 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who makes an
    application in respect of a vessel; ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application for the use
    of the dry dock, in the form set out in Schedule I;
    ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of the calculation of
    a dock charge, any period of 24 consecutive hours;
    ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dock charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dock charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any tolls or dues set out
    in column II of an item of Schedule II; ([droits de cale
    sèche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dry dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dry dock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Marine Railway Dry Dock
    at Selkirk, Manitoba; ([cale sèche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dry dock property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dry dock property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land at Selkirk,
    Manitoba, on which the dry dock is situated that is under the
    management, charge and direction of the Minister and includes all
    other property of the Government of Canada that is used, or is for
    use, in connection with the dry dock, other than property that is
    under lease to any third party; ([terrain de la cale
    sèche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entry book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entry book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a book for recording
    reservations for the dry dock and other information in respect of
    the dry docking of vessels; ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Gas Hazard Control Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Gas Hazard Control Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Standard for the Control of Gas Hazards in Vessels to be Repaired or
    Altered, published in March 1984 by the Ship Safety Branch of the
    Department of Transport, having reference number TP 3177E; ([Normes
    pour la protection contre les dangers des gaz]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[gross tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gross tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a vessel,
    the gross tonnage measured in accordance with the International
    Convention on Tonnage Measurement of Ships, 1969 or, in the case of
    a vessel that is registered under the tonnage measurement in force
    before that Convention, a gross registered ton; ([jauge
    brute]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of the Department
    at Edmonton, Alberta, who is responsible for the operation and
    maintenance of the dry dock; ([gestionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine chemist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    the Gas Hazard Control Standards; ([chimiste de la
    marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of the
    Department at Selkirk, Manitoba, who is responsible for the dry dock
    property; ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[winter term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[winter term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period commencing on
    November 1 in any year and ending on May 14 in the following year.
    ([saison d'hiver]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Responsibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919443 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where a vessel uses the dry
dock or dry dock property the agent shall ensure that the vessel does
not enter, remain on or leave the dry dock except in accordance with
these Regulations.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Dry Docking of Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919445 .Part}

### [Entry into the Dry Dock]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919446 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  vessel shall enter the dry dock unless authorized to do so by the
  Superintendent in accordance with subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may authorize a vessel to enter
  the dry dock if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is completed in respect of the
    vessel and submitted to the Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agent and the Superintendent agree on the date
    of entry of the vessel into the dry dock and the estimated duration
    of its stay in the dry dock;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the date of entry and
    estimated duration referred to in paragraph (b) and the name of the
    vessel are recorded in the entry book;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the agent signs the entry book;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the dock charge set out in column II of item 1 of
    Schedule II is paid to the Superintendent; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any cash deposit or bond required under
    subsection (3) is deposited with the Superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may, before recording the name of
  a vessel in the entry book, require the agent to give a cash deposit
  or post a bond, with two sureties, in an amount that is sufficient to
  pay the dock charges for the period for which application is made for
  that vessel.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where a vessel fails to enter the dry dock on the
  date agreed on pursuant to paragraph 4(2)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the vessel shall be deleted from the
    entry book;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the vessel may not enter
    the dry dock on a subsequent date pursuant to the authorization
    obtained pursuant to subsection 4(2); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the agent shall, within three days after the date
    agreed on, pay to the Superintendent

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the dock charge set out in column II of item 2 of
      Schedule II for each day the dry dock is vacant by reason of the
      vessel having failed to enter the dry dock on that date, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the applicable dock charge set out in column II
      of an item of Schedule II payable for any service provided in
      connection with the preparation of the dry dock for entry by the
      vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  that fails to enter the dry dock on the date agreed on where that
  failure is a result of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permission given by the Superintendent under
    section 6 in respect of another vessel; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} weather conditions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may, in respect of the entry into
  the dry dock of a vessel referred to in subsection (1) or (2),
  re-record in the entry book the name of the vessel and a subsequent
  date agreed on by the Superintendent and the agent if the dry dock is
  available on that subsequent date.

### [Priorities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919468 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent may permit a vessel to enter the dry dock before other
  vessels, the names of which are recorded in the entry book, if the
  vessel appears to be in a condition that necessitates its immediate
  entry into the dry dock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Superintendent permits a vessel to enter
  the dry dock before other vessels pursuant to subsection (1), the
  Superintendent shall report the reasons therefor to the Manager.

### [Damage to Dry Dock]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919472 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where damage to the dry
dock or the dry dock property is caused by a vessel, the crew of the
vessel, the agent, an employee of the agent, an independent contractor
or an employee of the independent contractor, or by the failure of any
equipment owned or operated by the agent, by an employee of the agent,
by an independent contractor or by an employee of the independent
contractor, the agent shall deposit with the Superintendent an amount of
money that is sufficient to indemnify the Crown for the damage or shall
post a bond in that amount, with two sureties.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-303, s. 2(F)
:::

### [Inspection of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919476 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a vessel enters the
dry dock, the agent shall, within three days after the dry dock is
pumped out, inspect the vessel and, if necessary, revise the estimated
duration of stay recorded in the entry book pursuant to
paragraph 4(2)(c) and report that revision to the Superintendent for
recording in the entry book.

### [Lighting of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919478 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel that is in the
dry dock shall, between sunset and sunrise, display adequate lighting at
each end of every gangway of the vessel.

### [Protection of Dry Dock and Dry Dock Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919480 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent shall
manage and protect the dry dock and dry dock property.

### [Movement of Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919482 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a vessel enters or
leaves the dry dock, the agent shall have available the number and kind
of tugboats necessary for the proper handling of the vessel.

### [Duration of Stay in Dry Dock]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919484 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No vessel shall remain in
the dry dock longer than the estimated or revised duration of stay
recorded in the entry book pursuant to paragraph 4(2)(c) or section 8,
and any further time that may be permitted by the Superintendent
pursuant to section 13.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where repairs to a vessel
in the dry dock cannot be completed within the estimated or revised
duration of stay recorded in the entry book pursuant to
paragraph 4(2)(c) or section 8, the Superintendent may permit that
vessel to remain in the dry dock for such further time as is necessary
to complete the repairs if no other vessel's name is recorded in the
entry book for entry into the dry dock during that time.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to section 12, a vessel leaves the dry dock before the
  repairs to the vessel have been completed, the agent shall, on any
  re-entry of the vessel to complete those repairs, pay to the
  Superintendent the dock charge set out in column II of item 3 of
  Schedule II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agent shall pay the dock charge set out in
  column II of item 2 of Schedule II in respect of the installation of
  keel and bilge blocks on any re-entry of the vessel into the dry dock.

### [Cleaning of Dry Dock]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919490 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Before a vessel leaves
the dry dock, the agent shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove from the dry dock and adjacent dry dock
  property all equipment, machinery other than machinery that is
  permanently affixed to the dry dock and adjacent dry dock property,
  and materials and scaffolding used in any repairs to the vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove from the dry dock and dry dock property,
  including gutters, water tunnels, sumps, valves, pumps and other dock
  equipment contained therein, any oil and any residue that have escaped
  from the vessel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} remove from the dry dock and dry dock property all
  traces of sand from sandblasting operations and all other refuse
  produced during any repairs to the vessel; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} do any other cleaning of the dry dock and dry dock
  property that is required as a result of the dry docking of the
  vessel.

### [Explosive Material and Oil Carried on Board Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919496 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  where a vessel that has been authorized to enter the dry dock in
  accordance with subsection 4(2) carries any explosive material on
  board, the vessel shall not enter the dry dock until the explosive
  material has been removed from the vessel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where a vessel that has
  been authorized to enter the dry dock in accordance with
  subsection 4(2) carried on its last voyage any oil with a flash-point
  below 23°C, the vessel shall not enter the dry dock until the agent
  submits to the Superintendent a certificate issued by a marine chemist
  in accordance with the Gas Hazard Control Standards.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  carrying, in a bunker or tank, oil intended for consumption on board
  the vessel if no work is to be done to the bunker or tank or to the
  fuelling or ventilation pipes of the bunker or tank or in the area
  immediately surrounding the bunker or tank or the fuelling or
  ventilation pipes of the bunker or tank.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where, as a result of a
delay caused by compliance with section 16, a vessel enters the dry dock
after the date agreed on pursuant to paragraph 4(2)(b), the agent shall
pay to the Superintendent the applicable dock charge set out in
column II of item 5 of Schedule II for each day the dry dock is kept
vacant while awaiting the entry of the vessel.

### [Use of Dry Dock and Dry Dock Property]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919502 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use keel blocks, bilge blocks or other dry dock property
  without obtaining the authorization of the Superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent shall authorize the use of keel
  blocks, bilge blocks or other dry dock property if that property is
  available.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall damage or
set adrift any blocks, poles, shores, planks, machines, stores, water
cisterns, scaffolding, pipes, tackle or other dry dock property.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person shall throw any
object or material from the dry dock cradle or walls.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2003-303, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  vessel shall be bunkered in the dry dock without the authorization of
  the Superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may authorize a vessel to be
  bunkered in the dry dock if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fire watch is kept at the fuelling connection on
    the vessel; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all work on the exterior of the vessel is suspended
    for the duration of the bunkering operations.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Any vessel lying afloat
in the dry dock or moored at a wharf or to the dry dock entrance, or to
any part of the dry dock property other than the dry dock, shall be kept
ready to move immediately on request by the Superintendent.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person, other than a person authorized by the Superintendent pursuant
  to subsection (2), shall load, unload or place on the dry dock
  property any shipment of marine equipment or cargo that is destined
  for or originates from any vessel or any shipment that is intended for
  transshipment at one of the Government of Canada wharves on the dry
  dock property.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may authorize a person to load,
  unload or place on the dry dock property any equipment, cargo or
  shipment described in subsection (1) where the only space available is
  on the dry dock property.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-303, s. 4(E)
:::

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall store on the dry dock property any cargo, building
  materials, equipment or machinery that is destined for or originates
  from a vessel using any part of the dry dock property or that belongs
  to or is in the custody or control of the agent except as authorized
  by the Superintendent pursuant to subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent may authorize a person to store
  the cargo, building materials, equipment or machinery referred to in
  subsection (1), where the cargo, building materials, equipment or
  machinery are necessary for repairing or building a vessel.

### [Refuse]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919523 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Where the crew of a
vessel live on board the vessel while the vessel is in the dry dock, the
agent shall remove from the dry dock property all sewage, garbage,
sweepings, ashes or other refuse from the vessel.

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a vessel is in the dry dock, the agent shall keep the entrances to the
  water-closets and urinals of that vessel locked or fastened.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use the water-closets or urinals of a
  vessel while it is in the dry dock.

### [Winter Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-919528 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Where the temperature
approaches the freezing point, the Superintendent shall advise the agent
to launch the vessel immediately or pay the dock charge set out in
column II of item 6 or 7 of Schedule II.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Winter Term]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919530 .Part}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No vessel may remain in
the dry dock for a winter term except in accordance with this Part.

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent may authorize a vessel to remain in the dry dock for a
  winter term if the agent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has complied with subsection 4(2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pays to the Superintendent the applicable dock
    charges set out in column II of items 2 to 7 of Schedule II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Superintendent authorizes a vessel to use a
  dry dock for a winter term, and no repairs are to be done to the
  vessel during that term, the agent shall, before the vessel enters the
  dry dock, pay to the Superintendent the dock charge set out in
  column II of item 6 or 7 of Schedule II.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel remains in the dry dock after the end
  of a winter term, the agent shall pay to the Superintendent the
  applicable dock charge set out in column II of item 5 of Schedule II
  for each day the vessel is in the dry dock after the end of the winter
  term.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Dock Charges Payable for Services and Use of the Dry Dock and Dry Dock Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919538 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the dock charges payable for use of the dry dock or of dry
dock property or for services provided in respect of a vessel,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel is docked when it has been hauled out of the
  water on the dry dock cradle and is undocked when the dry dock and the
  dry dock property are cleaned as required by section 15 and the vessel
  leaves the dry dock cradle;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), where a vessel has two
  gross tonnages on entry into the dry dock, dock charges shall be
  calculated in respect of the gross tonnage that is the greater; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a vessel is lengthened or modified while in the
  dry dock in a manner that results in a change in the gross tonnage of
  the vessel, dock charges shall be calculated in respect of the average
  of the gross tonnage of the vessel immediately before it entered the
  dry dock and the gross tonnage of the vessel immediately after the
  lengthening or modification.

<!-- -->

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a vessel uses the dry dock or dry dock property or is provided with a
  service set out in column I of an item of Schedule II, the agent shall
  pay to the Superintendent the applicable dock charge set out in that
  Schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 34 and subsection 35(2), the dock
  charge set out in column II of item 5 of Schedule II is payable in
  respect of a vessel to the extent that it exceeds the amount of the
  dock charge set out in column II of item 1 of that Schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel leaves the dry dock five hours or less
  after the beginning of a day, the dock charge for that day shall be
  one half of the applicable dock charge set out in column II of item 5
  of Schedule II that is payable in respect of the vessel for that day.

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Where, by arrangement
with the Superintendent, a vessel is dry docked or launched on a
Saturday or holiday, the amount representing the overtime differential
wages of all employees and related costs shall be added to the
applicable dock charges set out in column II of items 2, 3 and 4 of
Schedule II.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** No dock charges are
payable in respect of a vessel during a period in which the vessel is
detained on the dry dock by weather conditions if during that period no
work is done that would require the dry docking of the vessel.

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Superintendent may detain a vessel in the dry dock when repairs to
  another vessel in the dry dock have not been completed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No dock charges are payable in respect of a vessel
  detained pursuant to subsection (1) if, during that detention, no
  repairs are effected that would require the dry docking of the vessel.

<!-- -->

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 35(2), where a
  vessel is not removed from the dry dock within 48 hours after the day
  on which the stay in respect of which an agreement referred to in
  paragraph 4(2)(b) was made was estimated to end pursuant to that
  agreement, an additional charge as set out in column II of item 8 of
  Schedule II is payable to the Superintendent in respect of that
  vessel, after the expiration of the period of 48 hours unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no other vessel is waiting to use the dry dock; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the delay in the removal of the vessel was caused
    by circumstances that were beyond the control of the agent and that
    the agent could not have anticipated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No vessel shall be kept in the dry dock more than
  14 days after the stay in respect of which an agreement referred to in
  paragraph 4(2)(b) was made was estimated to end pursuant to that
  agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-303, s. 5(F)
:::

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall remove a vessel from the dry dock until the dock charges
  owing in respect of the vessel have been paid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where dock charges owing in respect of a vessel are
  not paid and the vessel is in the dry dock, the Minister may seize the
  vessel and its cargo and, after giving the agent 60 days notice in
  writing, may sell the vessel or its cargo or both.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Fines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919562 .Part}

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes any provision of these Regulations is liable to a fine in
the amount of \$400.
