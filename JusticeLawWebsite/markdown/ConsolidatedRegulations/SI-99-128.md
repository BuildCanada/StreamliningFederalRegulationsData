## Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage as Minister for purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-canadian-heritage-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/99-128
:::

[FOREIGN PUBLISHERS ADVERTISING SERVICES ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.6)

Registration 1999-11-10

Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage as Minister for
purposes of the Act

P.C. 1999-1863  1999-10-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Foreign Publishers
Advertising Services Act](/eng/acts/F-29.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-99-128_e_hq_6453){#fn_SI-99-128_e_hq_6453-ID0EBBAA},
hereby designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
that Act.
