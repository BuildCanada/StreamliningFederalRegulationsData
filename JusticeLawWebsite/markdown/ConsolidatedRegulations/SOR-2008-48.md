## Returnable Beverage Container (GST/HST) Regulations {#returnable-beverage-container-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-48
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 2008-02-28

Returnable Beverage Container (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 2008-408 2008-02-28
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 226(2)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EHCAA} and section 277[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EFCAA} of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), hereby makes the annexed [Returnable Beverage
Container (GST/HST) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-48).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EHCAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  2007, c. 18, s. 28(2)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e-ID0EFCAA){#fnb_e}S.C.
  1993, c. 27, s. 125(1)
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed Acts

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 226(2)(b) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
following Acts are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Environment Act, S.N.S. 1994-95, c. 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Beverage Containers Act, R.S.N.B. 2011, c. 121;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Beverage Containers Act, R.S.P.E.I. 1988, c.
  B-2.1; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Environmental Protection Act, S.N.L. 2002, c.
  E-14.2.
