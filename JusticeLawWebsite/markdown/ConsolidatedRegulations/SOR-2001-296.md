Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 29.9(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2001-296_e_hq_5480){#fn_SOR-2001-296_e_hq_5480-ID0EFAAA}

the Copyright Board of the annexed Educational Program, Work and Other
Subject-matter Record-keeping Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Copyright
    Act](/eng/acts/C-42). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collective society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collective society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a collective
    society that carries on the business of collecting the royalties
    referred to in subsection 29.6(2) or 29.7(2) or (3) of the Act under
    a tariff that has been certified as an approved tariff pursuant to
    paragraph 73(1)(d) of the Act. ([société de
    gestion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[copy identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[copy identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number or other
    reference code assigned to the copy of a program, work or
    subject-matter in accordance with section 3. ([code d'identification
    de l'exemplaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[educational institution identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[educational institution identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    number or other reference code assigned to an educational
    institution in accordance with section 4. ([code d'identification de
    l'établissement d'enseignement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an educational institution
    or a person acting under its authority.
    ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672198 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} copies of news programs and news commentary programs
  that are made pursuant to paragraph 29.6(1)(a) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} copies of works and other subject-matter that are
  made pursuant to paragraph 29.7(1)(a) of the Act.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672202 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An institution shall assign
a number or other reference code to every copy of a program, work or
subject-matter that it makes.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A collective society may
assign a number or other reference code to an educational institution.

## [Marking of Copy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672205 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An institution that makes a
copy of a program, work or subject-matter shall mark on the copy, or on
its container, the copy identifier and, if applicable, the educational
institution identifier.

## [Recording of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672207 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an institution that makes a copy of a program, work
  or subject-matter shall complete, in a legible manner, an information
  record in the form set out in the schedule regarding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the copying of the program, work or subject-matter;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all performances in public of the copy for which
    royalties are payable under subsection 29.6(2) or 29.7(2) or (3) of
    the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destruction of the copy.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the copy of a
  program made pursuant to paragraph 29.6(1)(a) of the Act if the copy
  is destroyed, in a manner that complies with section 7, within 72
  hours after the making of the copy.

## [Destruction of Copy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672214 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Destruction of a copy of a
program, work or subject-matter shall be accomplished by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} destroying the medium onto which the program, work or
  subject-matter was copied; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} erasing the copy of the program, work or
  subject-matter from the medium.

## [Sending of Information Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672218 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an institution shall send to each collective
  society

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the date on which the Board
    first certifies a tariff as an approved tariff pursuant to paragraph
    73(1)(d) of the Act, a copy of every information record on which
    entries have been made during the period between the date on which
    these Regulations come into force and the date on which the tariff
    was certified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after that, on or before January 31, May 31 and
    September 30 in each year, a copy of every information record on
    which entries have been made during the four months preceding the
    month in which the record is sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Once a copy of a program, work or subject-matter has
  been destroyed, the institution may send the original information
  record in respect of the copy to a collective society.

## [Retention of Information Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672224 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An institution shall retain
the original information record in respect of a copy of a program, work
or subject-matter until two years after the copy is destroyed unless,
during that time, the institution sends the original information record
to a collective society.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672226 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the 30th day after the day on which they are registered.
