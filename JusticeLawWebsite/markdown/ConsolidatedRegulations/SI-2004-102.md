## Order Transferring from the Minister of the Environment to the Minister of State (Infrastructure and Communities) the Control and Supervision of the Office of Infrastructure of Canada and Ordering the Minister to Preside Over the Office {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-the-environment-to-the-minister-of-state-infrastructure-and-communities-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-infrastructure-of-canada-and-ordering-the-minister-to-preside-over-the-office .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-102
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Transferring from the Minister of the Environment to the Minister
of State (Infrastructure and Communities) the Control and Supervision of
the Office of Infrastructure of Canada and Ordering the Minister to
Preside Over the Office

P.C. 2004-868 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers
  from the Minister of the Environment to the Minister of State to be
  styled Minister of State (Infrastructure and Communities) the control
  and supervision of the portion of the public service known as the
  Office of Infrastructure of Canada, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of State to be styled
  Minister of State (Infrastructure and Communities) shall preside over
  the Office of Infrastructure of Canada,

effective July 20, 2004.
