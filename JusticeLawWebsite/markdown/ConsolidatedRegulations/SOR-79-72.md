## Import of Chickens Permit {#import-of-chickens-permit .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-72
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1979-01-12

General Import Permit No. 2
::::

:::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Acting Prime Minister, pursuant to section 8 of the Import Permit
Regulations, hereby issues the annexed General Import Permit No. 2,
effective January 15, 1979.

Dated at Ottawa, this 5th day of January, 1979

::: FlushRight
L.S. MARCHAND
:::

::: FlushRight
*Acting Prime Minister*

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866406 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the [Import of Chickens Permit](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-72).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-116, Sch. I, s. 1
- SI/83-181, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866410 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, under
the authority of this General Import Permit, import into Canada from any
country any of the goods described in item 19 of the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604) if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not exceed 9 kg in net weight for each
  importation and are imported into Canada for the personal use of the
  importer or a member of the importer's household; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are chickens contained in a jar or can.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/83-181, s. 1
- SI/89-138, s. 1
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where completion and
validation of a customs entry form is required with respect to any
shipment of goods referred to in section 1 that are imported under the
authority of this General Import Permit, that form shall be endorsed
"Imported under the authority of General Import Permit No. 2" or
"Importé selon la Licence générale d'importation n^o^ 2."

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SI/83-181, s.
2\]]{.Repealed}
