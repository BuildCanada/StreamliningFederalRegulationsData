## [Introduction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381061 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of these Rules

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules set out the
procedure for appeals before the Tribunal. The Rules aim to help parties
understand the appeal process and their roles and responsibilities. The
Rules and the appeal process are meant to promote access to justice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}About the Tribunal

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal is an independent administrative tribunal. It consists of the
  members of the General Division and Appeal Division. The members are
  appointed under Part 5 of the [Department of Employment and Social
  Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7). The Tribunal is supported by the
  Tribunal Secretariat. The Secretariat consists of employees of the
  Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada. The Secretariat
  carries out some administrative tasks related to these Rules for the
  Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Division

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal's General Division decides appeals from
  reconsideration decisions made under each of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8);

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Disability Benefit
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-35);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal's Appeal Division decides appeals from
  General Division decisions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Overview of these Rules

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules are set out in
the order that they normally apply, but the order may be different in
some appeals. Not every rule applies in every appeal. The Rules are
divided into the following Parts:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part 1 sets out how to interpret the Rules;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part 2 sets out general rules about what the Tribunal
  can do and how it conducts the appeal process;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Part 3 sets out the roles and responsibilities of
  participants and the Tribunal;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Part 4 sets out how to file documents with the
  Tribunal and how the Tribunal handles documents;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Part 5 sets out how to appeal, how the Tribunal
  handles late appeals, what language the appeal can be in and how the
  Tribunal adds parties;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Part 6 sets out various procedures that may apply in
  the appeal process;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Part 7 sets out specific rules for Income Security
  appeals at the General Division and Appeal Division;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} Part 8 sets out specific rules for Employment
  Insurance appeals at the General Division and Appeal Division; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} Part 9 sets out specific rules for hearings and
  decisions.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381085 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 1 is about

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out
definitions for terms and expressions used in these Rules. This Part
also sets out how the Tribunal interprets and applies the Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules. To understand the Rules, it is important to first
read the definitions and then refer back to them as needed.

[[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal under Part 5 of the
    [Department of Employment and Social Development
    Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7). It includes an application for permission to
    appeal to the Appeal Division. ([appel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who appeals a
    reconsideration decision to the General Division or a General
    Division decision to the Appeal Division. At the Appeal Division,
    the appellant may also be the Minister or the
    Commission. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[argument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[argument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a submission that sets out a
    party's point of view about what the Tribunal should do in an
    appeal. A party may file arguments with the Tribunal or make
    arguments at an oral hearing. ([argument]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
    Saturday, Sunday or holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Employment
    Insurance Commission. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an address;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a phone number, if there is one; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an email address, if there is
      one. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Employment Insurance appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Employment Insurance appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal
    from a reconsideration decision that the Commission made under the
    [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6). ([appel en
    assurance-emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[evidence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[evidence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means something that a party
    presents to the Tribunal to prove a fact. It includes documents,
    photographs, video recordings and sound recordings that a party may
    file with the Tribunal. It also includes testimony given at an oral
    hearing. ([élément de preuve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Income Security appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Income Security appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal from
    a reconsideration decision that the Minister made under the [Canada
    Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), the [Canada Disability Benefit
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-35) or the [Old Age Security
    Act](/eng/acts/O-9). It includes an appeal from a General Division
    Income Security decision to the Appeal Division. ([appel en sécurité
    du revenu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Employment
    and Social Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parties; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} representatives, support persons, witnesses and
      interpreters who take part in an
      appeal. ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appellant and the Minister, for Income
      Security appeals at the General Division;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appellant and the Commission, for Employment
      Insurance appeals at the General Division;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the appellant and any other party to the appeal
      at the General Division, for appeals at the Appeal Division; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person added as a party under section
      33. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permission to appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permission to appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means leave to appeal a
    General Division decision to the Appeal Division under section 58 or
    58.1 of the [Department of Employment and Social Development
    Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7). ([permission de faire appel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reconsideration decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reconsideration decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a decision that the Minister made under section
      81 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), including a
      decision about more time to make a reconsideration request and a
      decision to rescind or amend a decision based on new facts;

    - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a decision that the Minister made under section
      18 or 19 of the [Canada Disability Benefit
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-35), including a decision
      about more time to make a reconsideration request;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decision that the Minister made under section
      27.1 of the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), including a
      decision about more time to make a reconsideration request; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a decision that the Commission made under section
      112 of the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), including
      a decision about more time to make a reconsideration
      request. ([décision de révision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reconsideration request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reconsideration request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a request for
    the Minister or the Commission to reconsider a decision under

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 81(1) or (1.1) of the [Canada Pension
      Plan](/eng/acts/C-8);

    - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} section 18(1) or (2) of the [Canada Disability
      Benefit Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-35);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 27.1(1) or (1.1) of the [Old Age Security
      Act](/eng/acts/O-9); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} section 112 of the [Employment Insurance
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6). ([demande de révision]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How the Tribunal interprets and applies
these Rules

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must interpret
and apply these Rules so that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appeal process is simple, quick and fair;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal considers the parties' particular
  circumstances;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} every party can participate in the appeal process,
  even if they do not have a representative; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the appeal process is flexible and appropriate for
  what the Tribunal must decide in each appeal.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[General Rules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381126 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 2 is about

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} how the Tribunal conducts the appeal process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} what happens if the Tribunal cannot reach a party;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} how to ask for an accommodation.

### [Conducting the Appeal Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381132 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making the appeal process simple, quick
and fair

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal must make sure that the appeal process is as simple and quick
  as fairness allows.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using active adjudication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal uses active adjudication as described in
  section 17(2) to help parties participate fully in the appeal process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a party wants the Tribunal to decide
  on a procedure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party may ask the Tribunal to do or decide
  something as part of the appeal process. To do this, the party must
  file a request with the Tribunal. The request must set out what the
  party wants and why.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adapting these Rules

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may adapt these Rules or decide that a
  party does not need to follow one or more rules if it is in the
  interest of justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If these Rules do not cover something

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may decide the procedure for anything
  not in these Rules or the [Social Security Tribunal Regulations,
  2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-255).

### [Continuing the Appeal Process Without a Party]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381144 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information on file

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal uses the contact information it has on file when it contacts
  a party or sends a document to a party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If the Tribunal cannot reach a party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may continue the appeal process even if
  it cannot reach a party using the contact information the party gave
  it. The Tribunal may do this without giving further notice to the
  party.

### [Asking for an Accommodation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381150 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
  arrangement to remove a barrier so a person can participate fully in
  the appeal process. An accommodation must relate to one of the grounds
  set out in section 3 of the [Canadian Human Rights
  Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a participant needs an accommodation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A participant must notify the Tribunal as soon as
  possible if they need an accommodation.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Roles and Responsibilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381156 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 3 is about

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out the
roles and responsibilities of participants and the Tribunal.

### [All Participants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381159 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants' responsibilities

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Participants must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be respectful and help maintain a safe environment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be honest; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not act in any way that goes against these Rules, the
  [Social Security Tribunal Regulations,
  2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-255) or the appeal process.

### [Parties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381165 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How parties participate

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Parties are responsible for
  participating in the appeal process. This may include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preparing documents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filing documents with the Tribunal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} presenting evidence or arguments;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} attending a conference or a hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} questioning witnesses; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} responding to another party's arguments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Following Rules and directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Parties must follow these Rules and the Tribunal's
  directions. This includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communicating with the Tribunal and responding to
    its requests; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meeting the deadlines the Tribunal sets out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Keeping contact information up to date

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Parties must notify the Tribunal as soon as possible
  of any changes to their contact information.

### [Representatives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381181 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What a representative does

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party chooses to have a representative under section 63.1 of the
  [Department of Employment and Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7), the representative

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acts on behalf of the party by presenting their
    position;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may represent the party for all or part of the
    appeal process; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not need to be a lawyer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How a representative participates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A representative is responsible for participating in
  the appeal process in the same way that a party participates as
  described in section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sharing information with a party

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal communicates directly with a
  representative. The representative must share information they receive
  from the Tribunal with the party they represent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Keeping a representative's contact
  information up to date

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A representative must notify the Tribunal as soon as
  possible of any changes to their contact information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a representative stops representing
  a party

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A representative must notify the Tribunal as soon as
  possible if they stop representing a party.

### [Support Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381196 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[support
person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section and in Part 1, [[support person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means a person who helps a party at an oral hearing by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} organizing their documents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} taking notes for the party; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} giving moral and emotional support.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How a support person participates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A support person does not represent the party, but
  they may attend the oral hearing as an observer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sharing information with a support
  person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal does not communicate directly with a
  support person outside an oral hearing. A party is responsible for
  sharing information from the Tribunal with their support person if
  they want the support person to have the information.

### [Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381207 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What a witness does

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  witness testifies at an oral hearing to give relevant evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How a witness participates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A witness may be questioned by a party, a
  representative or the Tribunal. A witness must answer all questions
  truthfully.

### [Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381213 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What the Tribunal does

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal hears appeals in a way that allows parties to participate
  fully in the appeal process. The Tribunal considers all of the
  parties' evidence and arguments and gives a decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How the Tribunal actively adjudicates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal actively adjudicates appeals. Active
  adjudication may include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deciding what issues need to be addressed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deciding what procedures are appropriate in the
    circumstances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} helping parties, representatives and witnesses
    understand the appeal process and these Rules;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} providing information about the laws that apply to
    the appeal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing information about evidence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} deciding the order for parties to present evidence
    or arguments at an oral hearing; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} asking parties, representatives and witnesses
    questions at an oral hearing.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381226 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 4 is about

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} how to file documents with the Tribunal and what the
  Tribunal does with them;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} what the Tribunal can do with electronic documents;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} when the Tribunal considers that a party has received
  a document.

### [Documents Filed with the Tribunal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381232 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How to file documents

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that needs to file a document with the Tribunal must file the
  document in one of the following ways:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by email, using the address set out on the
    Tribunal's website;

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Procedures that Apply in Certain Situations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381359 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 6 is about

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out

### [Joining Appeals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381362 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When the Tribunal may join appeals

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may deal
with two or more appeals together if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appeals raise a common question; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} joining the appeals is not unfair to the parties.

### [Limiting Public Access to the Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381367 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a party wants to limit public access

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may ask the Tribunal to limit public access to an oral hearing
  or the appeal record. To do this, the party must file a request with
  the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What information to include with the
  request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party's request must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the serious risk described in section 3 of the
    [Social Security Tribunal Regulations,
    2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-255) that is leading the party to
    ask the Tribunal to limit public access;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information or documents they want to limit
    public access to; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why reasonable alternative measures
    cannot adequately reduce the serious risk.

### [Holding Conferences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381376 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What a conference may cover

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may hold a conference with the parties to talk about the
  appeal. A conference may include discussions about any matter, such as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the step the appeal is at and the next steps in the
    appeal process;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issues in the appeal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Tribunal's authority to do or decide something;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the deadlines for filing documents; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} ways to settle the appeal without a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How a conference takes place

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A conference may take place

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by phone;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by video or other type of telecommunication; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a conference is a settlement
conference

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  conference is a settlement conference, documents related to the
  conference and discussions at the conference must not be disclosed.
  The documents are not part of the appeal record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If all parties agree, the documents and discussions
  may be disclosed and the documents may be included in the appeal
  record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If there is a hearing after a
  settlement conference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal member who holds a settlement conference
  must not hear the appeal unless all parties agree.

  parties the new deadlines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Two-year limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must not set a filing deadline that
  falls more than two years after the day the appellant filed the notice
  of appeal unless there are exceptional circumstances.

### [Income Security Appeals at the Appeal Division]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381480 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asking for more information ---
permission to appeal

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may ask an appellant for more information before it decides
  whether to grant permission to appeal a General Division decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deciding without arguments from other
  parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal decides whether to grant permission to
  appeal without asking the other parties for arguments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending filing deadlines

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If it
  grants permission to appeal, the Tribunal must send the parties the
  deadlines for filing evidence, arguments or any other documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a party is filing evidence,
  arguments or other documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Parties must file any evidence, arguments or other
  documents by the filing deadlines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing the filing deadlines

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may change the filing deadlines if it
  considers the change necessary. The Tribunal must then send the
  parties the new deadlines.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Employment Insurance Appeal Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381493 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 8 is about

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out
specific rules for Employment Insurance appeals at the General Division
and Appeal Division.

### [Employment Insurance Appeals at the General Division]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381496 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing the reconsideration file

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission must file the reconsideration file with the Tribunal within
  seven business days after the day the Commission receives a copy of
  the notice of appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What to include with the
  reconsideration file

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reconsideration file must include a copy of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reconsideration request;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reconsideration decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all other relevant documents; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a document that sets out the Commission's
    arguments, if it has any.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If a party is filing evidence

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** Parties must file any
evidence before the hearing ends.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asking the Commission to investigate and
report

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may ask the
Commission to investigate and report on any question related to a claim
for benefits. The Tribunal may do this anytime before giving its
decision.

### [Employment Insurance Appeals at the Appeal Division]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1381510 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Asking for more information ---
permission to appeal

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may ask an appellant for more information before it decides
  whether to grant permission to appeal a General Division decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deciding without arguments from other
  parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal decides whether to grant permission to
  appeal without asking the other parties for arguments.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for filing arguments

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Tribunal grants permission to appeal, the parties must file any
  arguments no more than 45 days after the day the Tribunal grants
  permission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for filing arguments ---
  hearing in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When there is a hearing in writing, the Tribunal must
  give parties 15 days to file any arguments in response to another
  party's position.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Hearing and Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1381521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}What Part 9 is about

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out
specific rules for hearings and decisions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing details

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** If there will be a
hearing, the Tribunal must send the parties a notice that sets out the
hearing details.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When an oral hearing may take place
without a party

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** An oral hearing may take
place without a party if the Tribunal is of the opinion that the party
received the notice of hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When the Tribunal gives its decision

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal must give
its decision as soon as possible after the hearing. The Tribunal must
give reasons for its decision.
