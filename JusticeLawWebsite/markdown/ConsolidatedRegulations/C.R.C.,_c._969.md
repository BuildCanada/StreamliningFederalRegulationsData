## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600149 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Enterprise Development
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._969).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600151 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan, an application referred
        to in section 21, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of insurance, an application
        referred to in section 38; ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/83-708, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any contribution
      authorized under a Department of Industry, Trade and Commerce vote
      of an Appropriation Act; ([contribution]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[letter of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[letter of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/79-335, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, firm or
      corporation in Canada engaged in a manufacturing or processing
      activity; ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      an activity whereby any goods, products, commodities or wares

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are made, fabricated, processed or refined out
        of any raw material or other substance or combination thereof,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are converted or rebuilt, but not repaired, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are made by causing any raw material or other
        substance to undergo a significant chemical, biochemical or
        physical change including change that preserves or improves the
        keeping qualities of that raw material or other substance but
        excluding change by growth or decay; ([travaux de fabrication ou
        de transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
      Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lender other than

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
        paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively
        controlled by any such government or any agency thereof, or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation; ([prêteur
        privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change that, in the
      opinion of the Minister, is significant in the operations of a
      manufacturer or other person eligible for assistance under these
      Regulations with respect to its or the person's products, methods
      of production, markets or management procedures and includes, if
      directly related to such operations, the acquisition of working
      capital or the acquisition, construction or conversion of
      machinery, equipment, buildings, land or other facilities.
      ([restructuration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/79-335, s. 1
  - SOR/83-708, s. 1
  :::

  to maximize the potential economic gains to Canada arising from any
  contribution administered by him pursuant to subsection (1).

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600189 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 19 and 20, the
  Minister may make a loan to a manufacturer for the purpose of
  assisting the manufacturer in restructuring his manufacturing or
  processing activity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer was engaged in a manufacturing or
    processing activity prior to the first reduction of tariffs or first
    modification of non-tariff barriers by Canada resulting from the
    Tokyo Round agreements; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of the Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer requires a loan in order to
      restructure his manufacturing or processing activity for the
      purpose of improving his position in international competition,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer is seriously injured or
      threatened with serious injury by reason of increased imports as a
      direct result of the Tokyo Round agreements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 19 and 20, the Minister may make
  a loan to a manufacturer for the purpose of assisting the manufacturer
  to adjust to changes in conditions affecting his access to foreign
  markets if, in the opinion of the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer requires a loan for such purpose;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the changes are attributable to the imposition by a
    country other than Canada of an import surtax or to the taking by
    such country of other actions having the same effect.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-335, s. 14
- SOR/83-708, s. 9
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 19
and 20, where, pursuant to these Regulations, the Minister has
authorized the provision of insurance on a loan not exceeding \$200,000
made by a private lender to a manufacturer or other person in Canada,
the Minister may make a loan in an amount not exceeding, in the
aggregate, 50 per cent of the amount of the loan to the manufacturer or
other person on the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loan may be made pursuant to this section only when
  a private lender has agreed to make the loan on which the Minister has
  authorized the provision of insurance on the terms and conditions
  under which the Minister authorized such insurance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan made pursuant to this section shall be subject
  to interest at a rate of not less than the aggregate of the interest
  to be charged by the private lender on the insured loan and the
  insurance fee thereon;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan may be made pursuant to this section only
  where, in the opinion of the Minister, such loan is essential to
  prevent a serious delay in implementing the restructuring program for
  which the loan by the private lender was obtained;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a loan made pursuant to this section shall be repaid
  on the date of the first disbursement of the loan advanced by the
  private lender or on such earlier date as the Minister may determine;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall specify the date on which the loan
  made pursuant to this section shall be repaid in the event that a
  disbursement to repay the loan has not been made by the private lender
  within a reasonable time as established by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-335, s. 15
- SOR/83-708, s. 9
:::

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 19

authorized by law to carry on the business of such manufacturer or other
person, has previously obtained assistance by way of a loan or insurance
on a loan or other financial obligation administered by the Minister
pursuant to subsection 4(1), a loan may be made by the Minister to the
manufacturer or other person or to the trustee or receiver for the
purpose of protecting the Crown's interest in respect of the loan
previously made or the loan or other financial obligation previously
insured.

### [Condition of a Loan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-600212 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall grant
a loan pursuant to sections 16 to 18 only where the manufacturer or
other person is unable to obtain sufficient financing on reasonable
terms from other sources for the purposes set out in those sections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-335, s. 16
- SOR/83-708, s. 9
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where a loan is to be
made by the Minister pursuant to these Regulations to a manufacturer
enabling the manufacturer to make adjustments to his operations that
will result in 20 or more of his employees being laid off for a period
of two or more months, the making of that loan shall be on condition
that the manufacturer agree to give at least three months notice of the
lay-off to the Minister and to each employee who is to be laid off.

- SOR/79-335, s. 16
- SOR/83-708, s. 9

### [Application for a Loan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-600219 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  manufacturer or other person who requires a loan in accordance with
  these Regulations shall make application therefor to the Minister and
  shall provide such information relating to the application as the
  Minister may require.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an application for a loan is approved by the
  Minister, the applicant shall enter into a loan agreement with Her
  Majesty and that agreement shall, subject to these Regulations, be in
  such form and contain such provisions as the Minister considers
  necessary.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a loan made pursuant to these
  Regulations shall be advanced to the manufacturer or other person on
  the requisition of the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-335, s. 16
- SOR/83-708, s. 9
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600245 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-600246 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 33 and 34, the
  Minister may provide insurance on a loan made by a private lender to a
  manufacturer, individual, firm or corporation in Canada, as the case
  may be, if, in the opinion of the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a manufacturer, the manufacturer
    requires a loan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to restructure his operations in order to improve
      his position in international trade competition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to adjust to changes affecting his access to
      foreign markets, which changes are attributable to the imposition
      by a country other than Canada of an import surtax or to the
      taking by such country of other actions having the same effect, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to engage in the manufacture or production of
      products that will be subject to international trade competition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, firm or corporation
    about to engage in a manufacturing or processing activity in Canada,
    the individual, firm or corporation requires a loan to engage in the
    manufacture or production of products that will be subject to
    international trade competition; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual, firm or corporation requires a loan
    to acquire a corporation or group of corporations engaged in a
    manufacturing or processing activity and the acquisition will result
    in a significant increase in the manufacturing or processing
    activity in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 33 and 34 and subsection (3), the
  Minister, with the approval of the Treasury Board, may provide
  insurance on any loan made by a private lender to an individual, firm
  or corporation engaged or about to engage in the business of general
  contracting or special trades contracting in Canada, if, in the
  opinion of the Minister, the individual, firm or corporation requires
  a loan to restructure his or its operations in order to improve his or
  its position in international trade competition and the restructuring
  will improve his or its capability to undertake foreign turnkey and
  other capital projects that would result in significant benefits to
  Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate amount of insurance that may be
  provided by the Minister pursuant to subsection (2) shall not exceed
  \$12 million at any time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding any other provision of these
  Regulations, the Minister shall not, without the approval of the
  Treasury Board, authorize the amendment of the terms and conditions of
  any loan insured pursuant to subsection (2) or exercise any of his
  powers under subsection 24(2) in respect of security taken for the
  loan except, only, where the Minister is satisfied that such amendment
  or the exercise of such powers will not substantially increase the
  risk of any loss to Her Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote

- SOR/79-635, s. 1
- SOR/79-778, s. 1
- SOR/83-708, ss. 5, 9

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 33

lender to a manufacturer or to a trustee or receiver authorized by law

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's products are subject to

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sale will be effected within a reasonable
    period of time, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the sale will enhance the prospects for the

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} would not proceed unless the loan required is
      insured by the Minister, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} would not proceed unless the private lender and
      the Minister agree to limit the liability of the individual, firm
      or corporation acquiring the assets, as the case may be, to the
      lender to 10 per cent of the amount of the loan to be provided.

### [Insurance to the Footwear or Tanning Industry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-600301 .Subheading}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** In this section and
section 32,

[[footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include footwear, the main
    component of which is canvas; ([chaussure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual, firm or corporation or a division
      thereof in Canada that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on January 1, 1974 was engaged in a
        manufacturing or processing activity in the footwear or tanning
        industry, or both, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} acquires from an individual, firm or
        corporation or division thereof referred to in subparagraph (i),
        the whole or a significant portion of its manufacturing or
        processing activity,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corporation restructuring by means of the
      acquisition of one or more corporations or divisions thereof
      referred to in paragraph (a),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation formed after January 1, 1974 by the
      amalgamation or merger of two or more corporations or divisions
      thereof referred to in paragraph (a), or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation or partnership formed by two or
      more individuals, firms, corporations or divisions thereof
      referred to in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) for the purpose of
      undertaking and performing an activity directly related to their
      manufacturing or processing activities;
      ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change that, in the
    opinion of the Minister, is significant in the operations of a
    manufacturer with respect to his products, methods of production,
    markets or management procedures and includes, if directly related
    to such operations,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition, amalgamation or merger of one or
      more manufacturers described in the definition "manufacturer" or
      the formation of a corporation or partnership described in
      paragraph (d) thereof,

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Stock Options]{.HTitleText1} {#h-600403 .Part}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may obtain a stock option to purchase capital stock of a
  company on behalf of Her Majesty, where, in the opinion of the
  Minister, it is advisable to obtain the stock option as a condition of
  or in connection with a loan or insurance administered by the Minister
  pursuant to subsection 4(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a stock option to purchase capital stock of a
  company is acquired by the Minister pursuant to subsection (1) or is
  administered by the Minister pursuant to subsection 4(1), the Minister
  may exercise or assign the stock option on behalf of Her Majesty if,
  in the opinion of the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the capital stock of the company has
    increased as a result of the assistance provided; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exercise or assignment of such option would
    permit Her Majesty to benefit from the increased value of the
    capital stock of the company.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where capital stock of a company is acquired by the
  Minister pursuant to subsection (2) or is administered by the Minister
  pursuant to subsection 4(1), the Minister shall, subject to the
  following conditions, sell or dispose of the capital stock as soon
  thereafter as it is in the interest of Her Majesty to do so:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capital stock shall be sold at such price and
    on such terms as will, in the opinion of the Minister, provide
    maximum benefit to Her Majesty; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the Minister receives an acceptable offer to
    purchase any such capital stock he shall notify the person from whom
    the capital stock was obtained and that person shall have the
    assignable right, during the next seven business days following the
    day the notice was given, to elect to purchase the capital stock at
    the price and on the terms and conditions specified in the offer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister intends to assign a stock option
  acquired pursuant to subsection (1) or administered pursuant to
  subsection 4(1) the Minister, prior to assigning the stock option,
  shall give notice of any intended assignment thereof to the person
  from whom the option was acquired and that person shall have the
  assignable right, during the next seven business days following the
  day the notice was given, to elect to purchase the stock option at the
  same price and on the same terms and conditions as the intended
  assignment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-335, s. 16
- SOR/83-708, s. 7
