Registration 1989-04-13

Export Control List

P.C. 1989-614 1989-04-13
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section 6 of
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), is pleased hereby
to revoke the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202),
C.R.C., c. 601, and, pursuant to section 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA} of that Act, to establish
the annexed [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) in
substitution therefor.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918657 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this List.

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means A Guide to Canada's Export
    Control List, published by the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade
    and Development, as amended from time to
    time. ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Wassenaar Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Wassenaar Arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the List of
    Dual-Use Goods and Technologies and Munitions List that was adopted
    at the Plenary Meeting of the Wassenaar Arrangement on Export
    Controls for Conventional Arms and Dual-Use Goods and Technologies
    in Vienna, Austria, held on July 11 and 12, 1996, as amended from
    time to time. ([Accord de Wassenaar]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918663 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following goods and
technology, when intended for export to the destinations specified, are
subject to export control for the purposes set out in section 3 of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Groups 1, 2, 6,
  and 7 of the schedule, except for goods and technology set out in
  items 2-1, 2-2.a. and 2-2.b., 2-3, 2-4.a., 6-1, 6-2, 7-2, 7-3, 7-12
  and 7-13 of the Guide, that are intended for export to any destination
  other than the United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Groups 3, 4 and 9
  of the schedule and goods and technology set out in items 2-1, 2-2.a.,
  2-2.b., 2-3, 2-4.a., 6-1, 6-2, 7-3 and 7-13 of the Guide that are
  intended for export to any destination;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Group 5 of the
  schedule that are intended for export to any destination referred to
  in the item of the schedule in which the goods or technology is
  described; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology set out in items 7-2 and 7-12 of
  the Guide that are intended for export to any destination referred to
  in the item of the Guide in which the goods or technology is
  described.

### [GROUP 1]{.HLabel1}[Dual Use]{.HTitleText1} {#group-1dual-use .SchedHeadL1}

Goods and technology, as described in Group 1 of the Guide, the export
of which Canada has agreed to control in accordance with the Wassenaar
Arrangement.

### [GROUP 2]{.HLabel1}[Munitions]{.HTitleText1} {#group-2munitions .SchedHeadL1}

The following goods and technology:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology, as described in Group 2 of the
  Guide, the export of which Canada has agreed to control in accordance
  with the Wassenaar Arrangement, other than [[antique
  firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 84(1) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), other than [[antique
  firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 84(1) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), that are:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} set out in item 1 of Part 1 of the Schedule to the
    [Regulations Prescribing Certain Firearms and other Weapons,
    Components and Parts of Weapons, Accessories, Cartridge Magazines,
    Ammunition and Projectiles as Prohibited or
    Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} designed to discharge a projectile at a muzzle
    velocity exceeding 152.4 m/s or at a muzzle energy exceeding 5.7
    joules; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} smooth-bore weapons, firearms, automatic weapons,
  weapons or armaments and projectors, as described in items 2-1 and
  2-2.a of the Guide, the export of which Canada has agreed to control
  in accordance with the Inter-American Convention Against the Illicit
  Manufacturing of and Trafficking in Firearms, Ammunition, Explosives
  and other Related Materials, that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are used for hunting or sporting purposes,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are manufactured earlier than 1938 and are not
    [[antique firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
    84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} use non-centre fire cased ammunition.

### [GROUP 3]{.HLabel1}[Nuclear Non-proliferation]{.HTitleText1} {#group-3nuclear-non-proliferation .SchedHeadL1}

- 3-01
  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods and technology, as described in Group 3 of
    the Guide, the export of which Canada has agreed to control

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the Treaty on the Non-Proliferation of
      Nuclear Weapons that was signed by Canada at London and Washington
      on July 23, 1968 and at Moscow on July 29, 1968 and that came into
      force for Canada on March 5, 1970; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the procedures referred to in
      the Guidelines for Nuclear Transfers (INFCIRC/254/Rev.14/Part 1),
      issued by the Nuclear Suppliers Group, as amended from time to
      time.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not include goods and
    technology described in Group 3 of the Guide that contain

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} four grams or less of natural uranium or depleted
      uranium when contained in a sensing component in instruments;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} alloys containing less than 5% thorium;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ceramic products containing thorium, which have
      been manufactured for non-nuclear use;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} medicinal substances containing source material;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} trace amounts of source material or special
      fissionable material found on contaminated items such as clothing,
      shielding or packaging;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} source material which the Minister has determined
      will be used only in civil non-nuclear applications, such as
      shielding, packaging, ballasts, counter-weights or the production
      of alloys and ceramics;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} four effective grams or less of special
      fissionable material when contained in a sensing component in
      instruments; or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} plutonium 238 that is contained in heart
      pacemakers.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (2)(g), weight in
    effective grams is equal to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of plutonium isotopes and
      uranium-233, the isotope weight in grams;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of uranium enriched to 1% or greater
      in the isotope uranium-235, the element weight in grams multiplied
      by the square of its enrichment expressed as a decimal weight
      fraction; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of uranium enriched to below 1% in
      the isotope uranium-235, the element weight in grams multiplied by
      0.0001.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (2)(d) to (g),
    [[source material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[special fissionable
    material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in the
    Guidelines for Nuclear Transfers referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

- [3-02]{.lawlabel} Deuterium, heavy water (deuterium oxide) and any
  other deuterium compound in which the ratio of deuterium to hydrogen
  atoms exceeds 1:5,000 if the Minister has determined that it is
  destined for use in a nuclear reactor that is capable of operating so
  as to maintain a controlled self-sustaining fission chain reaction.

### [GROUP 6]{.HLabel1}[Missile Technology Control Regime]{.HTitleText1} {#group-6missile-technology-control-regime .SchedHeadL1}

Goods and technology, as described in Group 6 of the Guide, the export
of which Canada has agreed to control under bilateral arrangements
concluded on April 7, 1987, in accordance with the Guidelines for
Sensitive Missile-Relevant Transfers, issued by the Missile Technology
Control Regime to control the export of missile equipment and technology
referred to in the MTCR/TEM/2019/Annex of October 11, 2019, as amended
from time to time.

### [GROUP 7]{.HLabel1}[Chemical and Biological Weapons Non-proliferation]{.HTitleText1} {#group-7chemical-and-biological-weapons-non-proliferation .SchedHeadL1}

Goods and technology, as described in Group 7 of the Guide, the export
of which Canada has agreed to control

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the Guidelines for Transfers of
  Sensitive Chemical or Biological Items and the Common Control Lists,
  as amended from time to time, that are issued by the Australia Group
  to control the export of chemical and biological weapons; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under the Convention on the Prohibition of the
  Development, Production, Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and
  on Their Destruction, signed at Paris, France, on January 13, 1993, as
  amended from time to time.

### [GROUP 9]{.HLabel1}[Arms Trade Treaty]{.HTitleText1} {#group-9arms-trade-treaty .SchedHeadL1}

The goods referred to in items 9-1 to 9-9, whether or not included
elsewhere in this List, the export of which Canada has agreed to control
in accordance with its obligations under the Arms Trade Treaty.

- [9-1]{.lawlabel} Battle tanks that are tracked or wheeled
  self-propelled armoured fighting vehicles weighing at least 16.5 t
  unladen, with a direct fire main gun of at least 75 mm calibre.

- [9-2]{.lawlabel} Armoured combat vehicles as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} tracked, semi-tracked or wheeled self-propelled
    vehicles with armoured protection and cross-country capability
    having any of the following characteristics:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designed or modified and equipped to transport a
      squad of four or more infantry soldiers,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} armed with an integral or organic weapon of at
      least 12.5 mm calibre,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} equipped with a missile launcher,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} equipped with organic technical means for
      observation, reconnaissance and target indication and designed to
      perform reconnaissance missions,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} equipped with integral or organic technical means
      for command of troops,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} equipped with integral or organic electronic and
      technical means designed for electronic warfare; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} armoured bridge-launching vehicles.

- [9-3]{.lawlabel} Large-calibre artillery systems as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} guns, howitzers, mortars -- and artillery pieces
    that combine the characteristics of a gun or howitzer -- that are
    capable of engaging surface targets by delivering indirect fire and
    have

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a calibre of at least 75 mm but not greater than
      155 mm, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a calibre greater than 155 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} multiple-launch rocket systems that are capable of
    engaging surface targets by delivering indirect fire and have a
    calibre of at least 75 mm; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gun carriers specially designed for towing
    artillery.

- [9-4]{.lawlabel} Military aircraft and related systems as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manned fixed-wing or variable-geometry wing
    aircraft that are designed, equipped or modified to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engage targets by employing guided missiles,
      unguided rockets, bombs, guns, cannons or other weapons of
      destruction, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} perform reconnaissance, command-of-troops,
      electronic warfare, suppression of air-defence systems, refuelling
      or airdrop missions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unmanned fixed-wing or variable-geometry wing
    aircraft that are designed, equipped or modified to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engage targets by employing guided missiles,
      unguided rockets, bombs, guns, cannons or other weapons of
      destruction, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} perform reconnaissance, electronic warfare or
      suppression of air-defence systems missions; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} systems for the control and receiving of
    information from the unmanned aircraft referred to in paragraph (b).

- [9-5]{.lawlabel} Military helicopters and related systems as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manned rotary-wing aircraft that are designed,
    equipped or modified to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engage targets by employing guided or unguided
      anti-armour, air-to-surface, air-to-subsurface or air-to-air
      weapons for which the aircraft are equipped with an integrated
      fire control and aiming system, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} perform reconnaissance, electronic warfare,
      target acquisition (including anti-submarine warfare),
      communications, command-of-troops or minelaying missions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unmanned rotary-wing aircraft that are designed,
    equipped or modified to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engage targets by employing guided or unguided
      anti-armour, air-to-surface, air-to-subsurface or air-to-air
      weapons for which the aircraft are equipped with an integrated
      fire control and aiming system, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} perform reconnaissance, electronic warfare or
      suppression of air-defence systems missions; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} systems for the control and receiving of
    information from the unmanned aircraft referred to in paragraph (b).

- [9-6]{.lawlabel} Vessels and submarines that are armed and equipped
  for military use and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a standard displacement equal to or greater
    than 150 t; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a standard displacement of less than 150 t and
    are equipped for launching missiles or torpedoes with a range of 25
    km or greater.

- 9-7
  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Missiles and missile launchers, as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} guided or unguided rockets and ballistic or
      cruise missiles that are capable of delivering a warhead or weapon
      of destruction to a range of 25 km or greater;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} launchers that are specially designed or modified
      for launching the missiles or rockets referred to in paragraph
      (a), if not included in any of items 9-1 to 9-6; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} man-portable air-defense systems (MANPADS).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) includes remotely piloted vehicles
    that have the characteristics of the missiles or rockets described
    in that subsection.

- 9-8
  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Small arms that are destined for police or military
    end-use by individual members, as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} handguns that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are automatic or converted automatic in
        function,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are semi-automatic in function, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are revolvers;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rifles other than those specified in paragraph
      (d);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} submachine guns that are automatic or converted
      automatic in function;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} assault rifles that are automatic or converted
      automatic in function; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} light machine guns that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are automatic or converted automatic in
        function, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have a calibre of 12.7mm or less,

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} firearms that are specially designed for dummy or
      blank ammunition and are incapable of discharging a projectile or
      marking cartridges;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} firearms that are specially designed for marking
      or for force-on-force type training ammunition;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} firearms that are specially designed to launch
      tethered projectiles having no high-explosive charge or
      communications link to a range of 500 m or less; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[antique firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
      defined in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- 9-9
  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Light weapons destined for use by individual
    members of armed or security forces or by several members serving as
    a crew and delivering primarily direct fire as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} heavy machine guns, other than the grenade
      launchers specified in paragraph (b), that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are automatic or converted automatic in
        function, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have a calibre greater than 12.7 mm;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hand-held grenade launchers, under-barrel-mounted
      grenade launchers and mounted grenade launchers;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} portable anti-tank guns;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} recoilless rifles;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} portable anti-tank missile launchers and rocket
      systems; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} mortars that have a calibre less than 75 mm.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not include [[antique
    firearms]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 84(1) of
    the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
