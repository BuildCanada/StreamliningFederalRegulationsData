## Domestic Wine Spirits Remission Order {#domestic-wine-spirits-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 759
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Duty on Spirits for the
Treatment of Domestic Wine
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561632 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Domestic Wine Spirits Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._759).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561634 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission of the excise
duty set out in subsection 1(1) of Part I of the schedule to the [Excise
Act](/eng/acts/E-14) imposed and levied pursuant to section 187 of that
Act is hereby granted on the spirits used for the treatment of domestic
wine after September 18, 1975, in any bonded manufactory.
