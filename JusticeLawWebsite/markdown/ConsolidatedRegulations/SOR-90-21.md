## [Designated Smoking Rooms and Designated Smoking Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921750 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2007-233,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An employer may designate
the following rooms or areas in a work space under the control of the
employer as designated smoking rooms or designated smoking areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any living accommodation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any motor vehicle, lighthouse, crane cab, caboose,
  locomotive or other room or area in the work space

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is not incorporated within any other work
    space,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that does not share a ventilation system with any
    other work space, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to which only one person normally has access
    during a shift; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any room on a ship to which only one person normally
  has access during a shift.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-233, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employer shall ensure that all designated smoking areas in a work
  space under the employer's control are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipped with ashtrays or non-combustible covered
    receptacles for the disposal of wastes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identified as a designated smoking area by a sign
    that meets the requirements set out in subsection 6(2) and is
    clearly visible to any person who approaches the area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply in respect of a room
  or area described in paragraph 4(b) or (c).

## [Notice of Smoking Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921766 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employer shall, by notice in writing or by posting clearly visible
  signs, inform all employees in a work space under the control of the
  employer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that smoking is prohibited in all work spaces
    except in designated smoking rooms and designated smoking areas, if
    any; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of the location of all such rooms and areas.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The signs referred to in subsection (1) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in writing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} substantially in the form of an appropriate symbol
    depicted in Schedule I, in the applicable colours referred to in
    that Schedule.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where an employer is in control of a work space to
  which the public is admitted, the employer shall, by means of a
  clearly visible sign that meets the requirements set out in subsection
  6(2) and that is posted at each entrance to the work space used by the
  public, inform the members of the public who enter the work space
  that, depending on the case,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} smoking is prohibited in the work space; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} smoking is permitted in the work space only in
    designated smoking rooms or designated smoking areas.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the work space referred to in subsection (1) is
  an aircraft, the employer shall orally inform all persons on board the
  aircraft that, depending on the case,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} smoking is prohibited on the aircraft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} smoking is permitted on the aircraft only in
    designated smoking areas.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-242, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Trains]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921783 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An employer who operates a
train that carries passengers may designate any bedroom, roomette or
drawing room on a sleeping car or baggage crew car of the train as a
designated smoking room.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this section,

  [[corridor service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corridor service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of rail
      passenger transportation service provided at least once a day that
      links major cities between Quebec City and Windsor; ([service du
      corridor]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regional service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regional service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of rail
      passenger transportation service linking small communities to one
      another or to major cities that provides at least three departures
      per week with many local stops; ([service
      régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remote service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remote service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of rail
      passenger transportation service providing rail access to small
      communities in remote areas that have no other year-round means of
      transport for at least a portion of the route served; ([desserte
      de localités éloignées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tourism service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tourism service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of rail
      passenger transportation service provided on a seasonal basis that
      is primarily marketed as a tourist attraction; ([service
      touristique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transcontinental service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transcontinental service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of
      rail passenger transportation service providing a connection
      between Halifax and Montreal with six departures per week and
      between Toronto and Vancouver with three departures per week.
      ([service transcontinental]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer who operates a train used in providing a
  corridor service, regional service, remote service, tourism service or
  transcontinental service may designate any passenger seating area as a
  designated smoking area provided that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more that one third of the passenger seating
    accommodation on the train is so designated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the passenger seating accommodation that is so
    designated is located on not more than one third of the passenger
    cars of the train.

## [Ships]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921795 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An employer who operates
a ship may designate any area on the ship that is used by employees for
leisure or recreational activities as a designated smoking area.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An employer who operates
a passenger ship may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate any passenger cabin on the ship as a
  designated smoking room; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designate any area intended for the use of passengers
  on the ship, other than a passenger cabin, as a designated smoking
  area, if the total surface area of all areas so designated does not
  exceed 30 per cent of the total surface area of all enclosed areas on
  the ship that are intended for the use of passengers, excluding
  passenger cabins.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-605, s. 1
- SOR/92-367, s. 1(E)
:::

## [Aircraft]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921802 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/95-344,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An employer who operates
an aircraft that is registered as a private aircraft may, for any flight
of the aircraft, designate any area on the aircraft or all of the
aircraft as a designated smoking area.

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  [[monthly quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[monthly quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a month that falls within the
        period from July 1, 1990 to June 30, 1991, 75 per cent of seat
        allocation,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a month that falls within the
        period from July 1, 1991 to June 30, 1992, 50 per cent of seat
        allocation,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a month that falls within the
        period from July 1, 1992 to June 30, 1993, 25 per cent of seat
        allocation, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a month that falls within the
        period from July 1, 1993 to June 30, 1994, 15 per cent of seat
        allocation; ([quota mensuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[seat allocation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[seat allocation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the percentage of
      passenger seats, regardless of class, included within designated
      smoking areas that were designated by an employer in respect of
      all of the employer's specified international flights during May
      1990, or, in the case of a Canadian air carrier who commenced
      offering specified international flights after May 1990, 20 per
      cent of all passenger seats on all such flights;
      ([contingent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specified international flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specified international flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
      respect of flights operated by a Canadian air carrier,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any segment of a flight between Canada and a
        point outside Canada that has a scheduled duration of more than
        six hours,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any segment of a flight that takes place wholly
        outside Canada, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any segment of a flight that takes place wholly
        within Canada if

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the flight originated outside Canada,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the only passengers on board the aircraft
          are those who boarded the aircraft outside Canada, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} smoking was permitted on board the aircraft
          during the immediately preceding segment of the flight. ([vol
          international déterminé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), an employer who operates
  an aircraft for hire or reward on a specified international flight may
  designate any area on the aircraft as a designated smoking area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No employer shall designate, in respect of all of the
  employer's specified international flights in any month, designated
  smoking areas that include within them, in total, a number of
  passenger seats that, when expressed as a percentage of the number of
  passenger seats on all of these flights, is greater than the
  employer's monthly quota.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} This section shall cease to have effect on June 30,
  1994.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-70, s. 1
- SOR/90-335, s. 1
- SOR/91-605, s. 2
- SOR/93-368, s. 1
:::

- **[[13.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  employer who operates an aircraft for hire or reward between Canada
  and Japan may designate areas for smoking on the aircraft.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer may not designate during any month, on
  aircraft operated between Canada and Japan, areas for smoking that
  include more than 15 per cent of the total number of passenger seats
  made available by the employer during that month for flights between
  Canada and Japan.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section shall cease to have effect on August 31,
  1994.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-487, s. 1
:::
