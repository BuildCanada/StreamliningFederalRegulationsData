[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-02-18

Order Prescribing the Fees and Charges To Be Paid for the Provision of
Satellite Remote Sensing Imagery, Tapes, and Services
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to Order in
Council P.C. 1981-239 of January 29, 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_ea){#footnote_ea-ID0EJCCA} made pursuant

the annexed Order prescribing the fees and charges to be paid for the
provision of satellite remote sensing imagery, tapes and services, in
substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910798 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Satellite Remote Sensing Services Fees Order,
1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-96).

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910800 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person who is provided by
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources with a satellite remote
sensing imagery, tape or service described in Column I of an item of the
schedule shall pay to the Minister the fee or charge set out in Column
II of that item.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section,

  [[ground station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ground station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      owns or controls the facilities for the direct reception of
      satellite remote sensing data and is authorized by the satellite
      operator to receive and distribute these data; ([opérateur de
      station terrestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[satellite operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[satellite operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who owns
      or controls the right to the primary acquisition and distribution
      of satellite remote sensing data. ([opérateur de
      satellite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding section 2, a satellite operator or a
  ground station operator who is provided, during a twelve-month period,
  by the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources with a satellite remote
  sensing imagery, tape or service described in Column I of any of items
  6, 7, 15, and 16 of the schedule shall pay to the Minister the
  following percentages of the fee or charge set out in Column II of
  that item:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 91 per cent, for up to 150 separate scenes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 88 per cent, for up to 250 additional separate
    scenes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 85 per cent, for any additional separate scenes.
