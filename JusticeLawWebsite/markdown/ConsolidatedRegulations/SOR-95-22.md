## Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-1 {#safeguard-surtax-regulations-1995-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-22
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1994-12-20

Regulations Prescribing Agricultural Goods in Respect of any Country for
the Purposes of Imposing a Safeguard Surtax, 1995-1

P.C. 1994-2152  1994-12-20
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.01(7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-22_e_hq_6327){#fn_SOR-95-22_e_hq_6327-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-22_e_hq_6328){#fn_SOR-95-22_e_hq_6328-ID0EDCAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing
agricultural goods in respect of any country for the purposes of
imposing a safeguard surtax, 1995-1, effective on January 1, 1995.
