## Proposed Acquisition of Increased Interest in Greyhound Lines of Canada Ltd. Exemption Order {#proposed-acquisition-of-increased-interest-in-greyhound-lines-of-canada-ltd.-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-65
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1993-02-04

Order Exempting the Proposed Acquisition of an Increased Interest in
Greyhound Lines of Canada Ltd. from the Application of Part VII of the
National Transportation Act, 1987

P.C. 1993-198  1993-02-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the National Transportation Agency,
pursuant to subsection 253(6) of the National Transportation Act,
1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-65_e_hq_6246){#fn_SOR-93-65_e_hq_6246-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to approve the annexed [Order exempting the proposed
acquisition of an increased interest in Greyhound Lines of Canada Ltd.
from the application of Part VII of the National Transportation Act,
1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-65), made by the National Transportation
Agency on December 29, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958448 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Proposed Acquisition of Increased Interest in Greyhound Lines of
Canada Ltd. Exemption Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-65).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958450 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The proposed acquisition of
an increased interest wherein the minority public shareholders of
Greyhound Lines of Canada Ltd. may increase the percentage of their
voting shares in Greyhound Lines of Canada Ltd. from 31 per cent to 46
per cent is hereby exempted from the application of Part VII of the
National Transportation Act, 1987.
