## Repayment of Receipts Regulations, 1997 {#repayment-of-receipts-regulations-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-127
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1998-02-23

Repayment of Receipts Regulations, 1997

T.B. 825983-1 1998-02-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to section 20 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed [Repayment
of Receipts Regulations, 1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-127).
:::

## [Excess Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018003 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  money has been received by a public officer as a deposit to ensure the
  doing of any act or thing and paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund,
  the money must, where the act or thing has been done or where the
  deposit is otherwise no longer required, be repaid to the person who
  paid it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where money that is received by a public officer as a
  deposit to ensure the doing of any act or thing and paid into the
  Consolidated Revenue Fund exceeds the amount prescribed for that
  purpose or, where no such amount has been prescribed, the amount
  required for that purpose, the excess amount must be repaid to the
  person who paid it.

## [Purpose Not Fulfilled]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018007 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where money has been paid
to a public officer for any purpose that has not been fulfilled and the
money has been paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund, the money paid,
less the sum referred to in subsection 20(2) of the Act, must be repaid
to the person who paid it, unless that person cannot be identified or
located.

## [Non Public Money]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018009 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where money paid to the
credit of the Receiver General is not public money, the money must be
repaid to the person who paid it, unless that person cannot be
identified or located.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018011 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1018013 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on February 23, 1998.
