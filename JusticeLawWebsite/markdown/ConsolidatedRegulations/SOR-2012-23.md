## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784812 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 116\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 116\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a foreign company, as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust
      and Loan Companies
      Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[optional product or service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[optional product or service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product
    or service that is offered or provided to a person by an
    institution, an affiliate that it controls or an agent or
    representative of the institution or affiliate for an additional fee
    and is available only with an agreement for a primary financial
    product or service provided by the institution. ([produit ou service
    optionnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary financial product or service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary financial product or service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does
    not include a product or service that is offered or provided by a
    company or a foreign company as defined in the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) to insure a risk. ([produit ou service
    financier de base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784825 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only in respect of natural persons who subscribe, or apply to subscribe,
to any of an institution's products or services for non-business
purposes.

## [Consent for New Products or Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784827 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  providing a person with a new primary financial or optional product or
  service, an institution must first obtain the person's express consent
  to do so, either orally or in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the consent is provided orally, the institution
  must provide the person with confirmation in writing of their express
  consent for the new product or service.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The use by the person of the new product or service,
  or any product or service related to the new product or service, does
  not constitute express consent for the purpose of subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any communication from an institution seeking a
  person's express consent must be made in language, and presented in a
  manner, that is clear, simple and not misleading.

## [Disclosure --- Optional Products and Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784833 .Part}

### [Manner and Content of Disclosure Statement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-784834 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  disclosure statement that is required to be provided by an institution
  under these Regulations must be made in language, and presented in a
  manner, that is clear, simple and not misleading.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any disclosure statement that is sent by mail is
  considered to have been provided on the fifth business day after the
  postmark date.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before a
  person provides their express consent to receive an optional product
  or service from an institution, the institution must provide them,
  orally or in writing, with an initial disclosure statement that
  contains the information referred to in paragraphs 6(a) to (d) or a
  summary of that information.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution that provides the initial disclosure
  statement orally must also provide it in writing.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Unless it has previously
disclosed in writing the information required under this section, an
institution that enters into an agreement with a person for an optional
product or service must, within 30 days after entering into the
agreement, provide the person with a subsequent disclosure statement
containing all relevant information about the product or service,
including:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the product or service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the term of the agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the charges for the product or service or the method
  for determining the charges and an example to illustrate the method;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the conditions under which the person may cancel the
  product or service;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date from which the product or service is
  available for use and, if different, from which charges apply; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the steps required to use the product or service.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any disclosure statement
made in relation to an optional product or service that is provided on
an ongoing basis, other than one provided in relation to a credit
agreement, must specify that the person may cancel the product or
service by notifying the institution that it is to be cancelled, that
the cancellation will be effective as of the last day of the billing
cycle or 30 days after the notification is received, whichever is
earlier, and that on receipt of the notice, the institution must refund
or credit the person with the amount of any charges paid by the person
for any part of the product or service that is unused as of the day the
cancellation takes effect, to be calculated in accordance with the
formula set out in section 9.

### [Notice of Changes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-784849 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution that makes any changes to the terms and conditions that
  apply in respect of an agreement for an optional product or service
  must, not less than 30 days before the day on which the changes take
  effect, disclose in writing to any person who subscribes to the
  product or service the changes to the information that was required to
  be disclosed in the initial disclosure statement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person agrees to a promotional, preferential,
  introductory or special offer for an optional product or service, the
  institution must disclose to the person in a subsequent disclosure
  statement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not less than 30 days before the expiry of an offer
    that comes to an end after a set period of time, the date on which
    the offer will come to an end and the charges that will be imposed
    for use of the product or service after that date; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after the last use that is subject to an offer that
    expires after a set amount of use, the fact that the offer has come
    to an end and the method of imposing charges for subsequent use of
    the product or service.

## [Cancellation of Optional Products or Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784857 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An institution that
receives from a person a notice of cancellation of an optional product
or service that is provided on an ongoing basis, other than one provided
in relation to a credit agreement, must refund or credit the person with
the amount of any charges paid by the person for any part of the product
or service that is unused as of the day the cancellation takes effect,
calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#784859 .Section}
A × (B -- C) / B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the amount of the charges;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the period between the imposition of the charges and the time
    when the services were, before the cancellation, scheduled to end;
    and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the period between the imposition of the charges and the
    cancellation.
:::
