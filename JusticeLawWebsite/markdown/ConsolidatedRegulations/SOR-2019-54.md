## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1115386 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who, in the
    regulated area, is engaged in the slaughtering of
    turkeys. ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who, in the
    regulated area, is engaged in the production of
    turkeys. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Producer Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1115392 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of levies

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Each producer who is in the
regulated area must pay, on each kilogram live weight of turkey that
they market in inter-provincial or export trade, the following levies:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the production levy listed in column 2 of Schedule 1
  that corresponds to the province of the producer set out in column 1;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the marketing levy listed in column 2 of Schedule 2
  that corresponds to the province of the producer set out in column 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance of levies

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a producer who markets turkeys to a person who is not a
  processor, the producer must remit the levies referred to in section 2
  to the Commodity Board of the relevant province no later than 60 days
  after the last day of the month in which the producer markets the
  turkeys.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance of levies --- processor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a producer who markets turkeys to a
  processor, the processor must deduct the levies from the total amount
  they pay the producer for the turkeys and remit the levies to the
  Commodity Board of the relevant province no later than 60 days after
  the last day of the month in which the producer markets the turkeys.

## [Processor Levy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1115402 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of levy

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each processor who is in a
province set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 and who slaughters turkeys
marketed to them by a producer must pay, on each kilogram live weight of
turkey that they market in interprovincial or export trade, the
marketing levy set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance of levy

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Each processor must remit
the levy referred to in section 4 to the Commodity Board of the relevant
province no later than 60 days after the last day of the month in which
the processor markets the turkeys.

## [Commodity Boards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1115407 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection on behalf of the Agency

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Agency, under
subsection 10(4) of Part II of the schedule to the [Canadian Turkey
Marketing Agency Proclamation](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._647),
appoints the Commodity Boards for the regulated area, with their
respective concurrence, to collect, on behalf of the Agency, the levies
imposed by this Order on producers and processors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance --- concurrence

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  province's Commodity Board concurs, in accordance with section 6 with
  its appointment to collect levies, it must remit to the Agency all
  levies it collects under sections 3 and 5 no later than the last day
  of the month in which it collects them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance --- without concurrence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 3 and 5, if a province's Commodity
  Board does not concur with its appointment to collect levies, all
  producers and processors must remit to the Agency on the day that it
  directs the levies that must be paid under sections 2 and 4.

## [Expiration of Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1115415 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 2(a) ceases to have effect
  on March 31, 2026.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marketing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 2(b) and section 4 cease to have effect on
  December 31, 2028.
