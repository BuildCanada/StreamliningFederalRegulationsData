## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673204 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673207 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed international agencies

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[commercial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 490(1)
of the Act, the prescribed international agencies are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the International Bank for Reconstruction and
  Development;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the International Finance Corporation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Inter-American Development Bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Asian Development Bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the African Development Bank;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the European Investment Bank;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Caribbean Development Bank;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Nordic Investment Bank;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development;
  and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Council of Europe Development Bank.

Definition of [[widely distributed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the definition [[commercial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 490(1) of the Act, [[widely
  distributed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of debt obligations the distribution of
    which is exempt from the requirement to file a prospectus under the
    laws of Canada, a province or a jurisdiction outside Canada, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least 90% of the maximum authorized principal
      of the debt obligations is held by one or more persons other than
      the company and its subsidiaries, and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the debt obligations were issued to at least 25
        persons other than the company and its subsidiaries within six
        months after the day on which the first of the debt obligations
        was issued, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligations are issued on a
        continuous basis, there are on average at least 25 holders other
        than the company and its subsidiaries, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of their initial distribution, the
      debt obligations met at least three of the following criteria:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their initial term was less than one year,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} they were rated by a rating agency,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed through a person
        authorized to trade in securities, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed in accordance with an
        offering circular or memorandum, or a similar document relating
        to the distribution of securities; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other securities, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they are listed and posted for trading on a
      recognized stock exchange, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a prospectus relating to the issuance of the
      securities was filed under the laws of Canada, a province or a
      jurisdiction outside Canada.

- Definition of [[maximum authorized principal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subparagraph (1)(a)(i) [[maximum authorized
  principal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to any debt
  obligation, the maximum amount of debt that may be issued under the
  terms of the debt obligation or any agreement relating to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiaries of a life company

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 503 and 504 of the Act, all subsidiaries of a
  life company are prescribed subsidiaries of the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiaries of a property
  and casualty company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 505 of the Act, a
  prescribed subsidiary of a property and casualty company is a
  subsidiary of the company that is not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company or an insurance holding
    company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution, other than
    where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial institution only
    because it is a subsidiary of the company; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a bank holding company or an
    insurance holding company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
505 of the Act, the prescribed percentage is 5%.

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a life company

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of section 503 of the Act, [[total
  assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a life company, at a
  particular time, means the total of the assets that would be reported
  on the balance sheet of the company prepared as at that time in
  accordance with the accounting principles and specifications of the
  Superintendent referred to in subsection 331(4) of the Act.

- Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a property and
  casualty company and a marine company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 505 of the Act, [[total
  assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a property and casualty
  company or a marine company, at a particular time, means the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#673253 .Subsection}
  A - B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the assets that would be reported on the balance
      sheet of the company prepared as at that time in accordance with
      the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
      referred to in subsection 331(4) of the Act; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the assets included in the value of A that are
      assets of a subsidiary of the company that is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company or an insurance holding
        company;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution, other
        than where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial
        institution only because it is a subsidiary of the company; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a bank holding company or an
        insurance holding company.
  :::

## [Societies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiaries of a society

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
562 of the Act, a prescribed subsidiary of a society is a subsidiary of
the society that is not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a financial institution, other than
  where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a financial institution only
  because it is a subsidiary of the society.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
562 of the Act, the prescribed percentage is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the society has twenty-five million dollars or
  less of capital, 5%; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the society has more than twenty-five million
  dollars of capital, 100%.

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a society

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
562 of the Act, [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
society, at a particular time, means the amount determined by the
formula

::: {#673274 .Section}
A - B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the assets that would be reported on the balance
    sheet of the society prepared as at that time in accordance with the
    accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
    referred to in subsection 331(4) of the Act; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the assets included in the value of A that are
    assets of a subsidiary of the society that is a financial
    institution or that are assets of a subsidiary of a financial
    institution other than where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a
    financial institution only because it is a subsidiary of the
    society.
:::

## [Insurance Holding Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673278 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiaries of an insurance
holding company

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
sections 979 and 980 of the Act, all subsidiaries of an insurance
holding company are prescribed subsidiaries of the insurance holding
company.

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an insurance
holding company

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 979 of the Act, [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
of an insurance holding company, at a particular time, means the total
of the assets that would be reported on the balance sheet of the
insurance holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with
the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
referred to in subsection 887(4) of the Act.

## [Foreign Life Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673283 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 616(1) and (2) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a foreign life company the value of
  whose assets in Canada for the classes of insurance referred to in
  those subsections exceeds the aggregate of its liabilities in Canada
  and the margin of assets in Canada over liabilities in Canada referred
  to in subsection 608(1) of the Act in respect of those classes by 25
  million dollars or less, the prescribed percentage of the value of
  those assets is five per cent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a foreign life company the value of
  whose assets in Canada for the classes of insurance referred to in
  those subsections exceeds the aggregate of its liabilities in Canada
  and the margin of assets in Canada over liabilities in Canada referred
  to in subsection 608(1) of the Act in respect of those classes by more
  than 25 million dollars, the prescribed percentage of the value of
  those assets is 100 per cent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-296, s. 28
:::

## [Foreign Property and Casualty Companies and Foreign Marine Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673290 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

**[[11.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 617 of the Act, the prescribed percentage of the value of the
assets in Canada of a foreign property and casualty company or a foreign
marine company is five per cent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-296, s. 28
:::
