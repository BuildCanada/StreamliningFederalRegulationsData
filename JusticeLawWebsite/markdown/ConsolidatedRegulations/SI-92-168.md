## Order Transferring to the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission from the Department of External Affairs the Control and Supervision of the Consular, Immigration and Passport Affairs Branch and the Immigration Sections of the Programs Division {#order-transferring-to-the-canada-employment-and-immigration-commission-from-the-department-of-external-affairs-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-consular-immigration-and-passport-affairs-branch-and-the-immigration-sections-of-the-programs-division .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-168
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1992-09-23

Order Transferring to the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission
from the Department of External Affairs the Control and Supervision of
the Consular, Immigration and Passport Affairs Branch and the
Immigration Sections of the Programs Division

P.C. 1992-1992  1992-09-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission,
effective August 31, 1992, the control and supervision of the following
portions of the public service of Canada in the Department of External
Affairs, namely

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Consular, Immigration and Passport Affairs
  Branch, except the Passport Office and those portions of the Consular
  and Immigration Affairs Bureau and the Consular, Immigration and
  Refugee Policy Bureau related to the provision of consular services;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the immigration sections of the Programs Divisions,
  and their related units at missions abroad, of the Africa and Middle
  East Branch, the Asia and Pacific Branch, the Europe Branch, the Latin
  America and Caribbean Branch, and the United States Branch.
