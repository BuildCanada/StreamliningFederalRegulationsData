## [Prescribed Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734998 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subparagraph 427(7)(b)(ii) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the
prescribed amount is the amount determined by the formula

::: {#735000 .Section}
A × B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the value of the most recent annual Farm Product Price Index for
    Canada (the "annual Index") published by Statistics Canada at the
    time the bankruptcy order or claim is made; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is equal to
    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,322, if the annual Index has not been
      adjusted or re-established on a revised base after April 25, 2001,
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each adjustment or re-establishment of the
      annual Index on a revised base after April 25, 2001, the amount,
      rounded up to the nearest dollar, determined by the formula

      ::: {#735006 .NestedFormula}
      C × D/E

      where

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is, for the first adjustment or re-establishment, \$1,322, and
          for each subsequent adjustment or re-establishment, the value
          that was determined for B for the previous adjustment or
          re-establishment,

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the most recent annual Index immediately
          before the adjustment or re-establishment, and

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the most recent annual Index immediately
          following the adjustment or re-establishment.
      :::
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735011 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on October 1, 2007.
