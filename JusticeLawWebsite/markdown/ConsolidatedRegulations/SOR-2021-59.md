Definition of [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

- **[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fees set out in these Regulations are to be adjusted in each fiscal
  year on April 1 by the percentage change over 12 months in the April
  All-items Consumer Price Index for Canada, as published by Statistics
  Canada under the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for the previous
  fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fee referred to in subsection (1) is not adjusted
  in a fiscal year if the percentage change for that fiscal year is less
  than 1%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment --- subsequent fiscal year

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (2)‍ applies with respect to a fiscal
  year, the percentage change is carried forward to the subsequent
  fiscal year. If the combined percentage change is less than 1%, the
  fee is not adjusted for that subsequent fiscal year and the combined
  percentage change is carried forward to the next subsequent fiscal
  year.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Marine Cargo Inspection Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449773 .Part}

### [Definitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1449780 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[Certificate of Readiness to Load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Certificate of Readiness to Load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate referred to in subsection 119(3), 128(2) or 140(3) of
    the [Cargo, Fumigation and Tackle
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128). ([certificat de navire
    prêt à charger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Fitness to Proceed Certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    certificate referred to in subsection 120(2), 129(2) or 141(2) of
    the [Cargo, Fumigation and Tackle
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128). ([certificat d'aptitude
    au transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Concentrates, Grains and Timber]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1290895 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A fee of \$750 is imposed
on a vessel for each day that the Minister, following receipt of an
application to issue a Certificate of Readiness to Load or a Fitness to
Proceed Certificate to the vessel, takes one or more of the following
actions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspects the vessel to establish that the
  requirements for the issuance of the certificate are met;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} travels to or from the vessel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is, at the vessel's request, made available for an
  inspection referred to in paragraph (a).

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Environmental Protection Inspection Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449788 .Part}

\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  master of a vessel requests an exemption from a tank prewash operation
  under subsection 63(2) of the [Vessel Pollution and Dangerous
  Chemicals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69), a fee of \$3,075
  is imposed on the vessel for the first 3.75 hours or fraction of this
  time that a marine safety inspector requires to assess the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than 3.75 hours

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the marine safety inspector requires more than
  3.75 hours to assess the request, the following additional fees are
  imposed on the vessel for time in excess of the first 3.75 hours:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$105 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    assesses the request from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday,
    other than on a holiday;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$307 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    assesses the request

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before 8:00 a.m. or after 5:00 p.m., Monday to
      Friday,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a Saturday, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on a holiday, other than a Sunday; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$336 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    assesses the request on a Sunday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tank prewash operation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  marine safety inspector is present for a vessel's tank prewash
  operation under subsection 63(5) of the [Vessel Pollution and
  Dangerous Chemicals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69), a fee
  of \$3,075 is imposed on the vessel for the first 3.75 hours or
  fraction of this time that the inspector is present.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than 3.75 hours

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the marine safety inspector is present at the tank
  prewash operation for more than 3.75 hours, the following additional
  fees are imposed on the vessel for time in excess of the first 3.75
  hours:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$105 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    is present from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., Monday to Friday, other than
    on a holiday;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$307 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    is present

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before 8:00 a.m. or after 5:00 p.m., Monday to
      Friday,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a Saturday, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on a holiday, other than a Sunday; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$336 per hour, calculated on a pro-rated basis for
    each quarter hour or fraction of a quarter hour that the inspector
    is present on a Sunday.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Foreign Vessel Inspection Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449792 .Part}

\[

- SOR/2024-29, s. 4

\]

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection following detention order

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If, on request by a
foreign vessel's authorized representative, a marine safety inspector
carries out an inspection of the foreign vessel for the purpose of
verifying that measures indicated in a notice referred to in subsection
222(6) of the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) have been
taken, a fee of \$4,200 is imposed on the vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of deficiencies

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If, on request by a
foreign vessel's authorized representative, a marine safety inspector
carries out an inspection of the foreign vessel for the purpose of
verifying that deficiencies identified in relation to a requirement
under section 18 or 19 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) that did not warrant the
issuance of a detention order have been corrected, a fee of \$1,870 is
imposed on the vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small Vessel Register

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel of 15 gross tonnage or less, other than a pleasure craft
  referred to in paragraph 15(1)(e) or a vessel referred to in
  paragraphs 15(1)(b) to (d), must be registered or listed in the Small
  Vessel Register.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications or requests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application or request for a service related to
  the Small Vessel Register must be presented in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a single vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fleet of two or more identical vessels, including
    government vessels, that are owned by the same owner and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are human-powered,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are 8.5 m in length or less and are propelled by
      sail alone, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are equipped with one or more primary engines
      that have an aggregate propulsion power of less than 7.5 kW; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a group of two or more vessels, other than
    government vessels, of 5 gross tonnage or less that are owned by the
    same owner and that are registered at the same time under different
    certificates of registry.
