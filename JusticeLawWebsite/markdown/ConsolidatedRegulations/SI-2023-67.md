## Supplemental Benefits Received by Three Governor in Council Appointees Within the Department of Employment and Social Development Remission Order {#supplemental-benefits-received-by-three-governor-in-council-appointees-within-the-department-of-employment-and-social-development-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2023-11-08

Supplemental Benefits Received by Three Governor in Council Appointees
Within the Department of Employment and Social Development Remission
Order

P.C. 2023-1069 2023-10-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of certain supplemental benefits received in error is
unreasonable and unjust, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board,
the Minister of Employment and Social Development and the Minister of
Families, Children and Social Development makes the annexed
[Supplemental Benefits Received by Three Governor in Council Appointees
Within the Department of Employment and Social Development Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2023-67) under subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission of the
supplemental benefits received due to administrative error by three
Governor in Council appointees within the Department of Employment and
Social Development is granted to those appointees with respect to the
following positions and the following periods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson and the Member with particular
  responsibilities for children's issues, since their appointment in
  April 2021 as full-time members of the National Advisory Council on
  Poverty and during their prior tenure as Ministerial appointees under
  the [Department of Employment and Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7) from September 2019 to March 2021; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Accessibility Officer, since her
  appointment to the full-time position in May 2022.
