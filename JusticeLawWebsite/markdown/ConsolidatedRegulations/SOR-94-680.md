## Guitar Parts Remission Order, 1994 {#guitar-parts-remission-order-1994 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-680
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1994-11-01

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Certain Guitar Parts

P.C. 1994-1836  1994-11-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-680_e_hq_6306){#fn_SOR-94-680_e_hq_6306-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
customs duties on certain guitar parts](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-680).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966009 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Guitar Parts Remission Order, 1994](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-680).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966011 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on certain parts
of guitars.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966013 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the guitar parts are imported into Canada during the
  period commencing on September 23, 1993 and ending on March 10, 1994;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the guitar parts are for use in the manufacture of
  guitars; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years of the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.
