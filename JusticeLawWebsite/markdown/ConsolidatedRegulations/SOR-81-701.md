## Tariff Item No. 9805.00.00 Exemption Order {#tariff-item-no.-9805.00.00-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-701
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1981-09-04

Order Respecting the Exemption of Certain Goods from Certain
Requirements Specified in Tariff Item No. 9805.00.00

P.C. 1981-2406 1981-09-03
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to tariff item 70320-1 of
Schedule A to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), is pleased
hereby to revoke the Returning Residents Special Exemption Regulations,
C.R.C., c. 545, and to make the annexed Order respecting the exemption
of certain goods pursuant to tariff item 70320-1 of Schedule A to the
Customs Tariff.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871875 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Tariff Item No. 9805.00.00 Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-701).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-84, Part III
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871879 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[bride's trousseau]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bride's trousseau]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods acquired for
    use in the household of a newly married couple, but does not include
    vehicles, vessels or aircraft; ([trousseau de
    mariée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wedding presents]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wedding presents]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods of a
    non-commercial nature received by a person as personal gifts in
    consideration of that person's recent marriage or the anticipated
    marriage of that person within three months of the person's return
    to Canada. ([cadeaux de noces]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871883 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following goods are
exempt from the six-month ownership, possession or use requirements set
out in tariff item No. 9805.00.00 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} alcoholic beverages owned by, in the possession of
  and imported by a person who has attained the minimum age at which a
  person may lawfully purchase alcoholic beverages in the province in
  which the customs office where the alcoholic beverages are imported is
  situated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} tobacco products owned by and in the possession of
  the importer;

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} vaping products owned by and in the possession of
  the importer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a bride's trousseau owned by, in the possession of
  and imported by a recently married person or a bride-to-be whose
  anticipated marriage is to take place within three months of the date
  of her return to Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} wedding presents owned by, in the possession of and
  imported by the recipient thereof;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any goods imported by a person who has resided abroad
  for at least five years immediately prior to returning to Canada and
  who, prior to the date of return, owned, was in possession of and used
  the goods; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} goods acquired as replacements for goods that, but
  for their loss or destruction as the result of fire, theft, accident
  or other unforeseen contingency, would have been classified under
  tariff item No. 9805.00.00 of the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), on condition that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the goods acquired as replacements are of a similar
    class and approximately of the same value as the goods they
    replaced,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods acquired as replacements were owned by,
    in the possession of, and used by a person prior to the person's
    return to Canada, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} evidence is produced at the time the goods are
    accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) that the goods they replaced were lost or
    destroyed as the result of fire, theft, accident or other unforeseen
    contingency.
