## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767523 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[experts performing missions for the Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[experts performing missions for the
    Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means governmental or
    intergovernmental experts who are invited by the Organization to
    attend the Meeting and the preparatory meetings. ([experts en
    mission pour l'Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meeting of the
    Organization to be held in Toronto, Ontario from June 26 to
    27, 2010. ([réunion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
    conference of the 20 leading industrialized countries, also known as
    the G20. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meetings of
    the personal representatives of the Heads of State and Government of
    the G20 member countries, known as sherpas, to be held in Ottawa,
    Ontario from March 18 to 19, 2010, in Calgary, Alberta from May 24
    to 25, 2010 and in Toronto, Ontario from June 22 to 24, 2010, or any
    other meetings of those representatives to be held in Canada during
    the period beginning on March 11, 2010 and ending on June 25, 2010.
    ([réunions préparatoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767530 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the period beginning on March 11, 2010 and ending on July 4, 2010, the
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacity of a body
  corporate and the privileges and immunities set out in sections 2 to 5
  of Article II of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning on March 11, 2010 and
  ending on July 4, 2010, representatives of states and governments that
  are members of or participate in the Organization shall have in
  Canada, to the extent required for the exercise of their functions in
  Canada in relation to the preparatory meetings, the privileges and
  immunities set out in section 11(d) of Article IV of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning on June 15, 2010 and
  ending on July 4, 2010, representatives of states and governments that
  are members of or participate in the Organization shall have in
  Canada, to the extent required for the exercise of their functions in
  Canada in relation to the Meeting, the privileges and immunities set
  out in sections 11, 12 and 14 to 16 of Article IV of the Convention.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning on March 11, 2010 and
  ending on July 4, 2010, experts performing missions for the
  Organization shall have in Canada, to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to the Meeting and
  the preparatory meetings, the privileges and immunities set out in
  Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767536 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
