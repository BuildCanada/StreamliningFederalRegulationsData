## Regulations Maintaining Certain Reciprocal Transfer Agreements {#regulations-maintaining-certain-reciprocal-transfer-agreements .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-376
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 2000-10-02

Regulations Maintaining Certain Reciprocal Transfer Agreements

T.B. 828458 2000-09-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 40.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2000-376_e_hq_5181){#fn_SOR-2000-376_e_hq_5181-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph
7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby
makes the annexed [Regulations Maintaining Certain Reciprocal Transfer
Agreements](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-376).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2000-376_e_hq_5181-ID0EHBAA){#fn_SOR-2000-376_e_hq_5181}S.C.
  1996, c. 18, s. 33
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Reciprocal Transfer
Agreement between Her Majesty in right of Canada and the employer set
out in column 1 of an item of the Schedule, made on the date set out in
column 2 of that item, is excluded from the application of subsection
40.3(2) of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
