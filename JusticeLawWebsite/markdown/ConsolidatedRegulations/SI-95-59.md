## Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#solicitor-general-of-canada-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-59
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-05-17

Solicitor General of Canada Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1995-698  1995-04-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-59_e_hq_6610){#fn_SI-95-59_e_hq_6610-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Solicitor General of Canada to prescribe by order the
fee to be paid by any user to whom any pardon services are provided by
the National Parole Board.
