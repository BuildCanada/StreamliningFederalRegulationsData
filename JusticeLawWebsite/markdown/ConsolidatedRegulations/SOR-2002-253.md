Application of Regulations made under paragraph 33(1)(m) or (n) of the

Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal

## [Non-application of Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-693230 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** All Regulations made under
paragraph 33(1)(m) or (n) of the [Northwest Territories Waters
Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3) cease to apply in relation to Nunavut.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-693232 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
