## Shirting Fabrics Remission Order, 1998 {#shirting-fabrics-remission-order-1998 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-87
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Shirting Fabrics Remission Order, 1998

P.C. 1997-2055 1997-12-29
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Shirting Fabrics Remission Order,
1998](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-87).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025575 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[finished]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[finished]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a fabric, that
    the fabric has undergone all processes required to convert it from a
    greige fabric into a fabric sold to a shirt manufacturer;
    ([fini]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greige]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greige]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a fabric, that
    the fabric has come directly from the loom and has not undergone any
    process required to convert it into a finished fabric and includes
    an unfinished fabric woven from coloured or dyed yarns;
    ([écru]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shirting fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shirting fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a broadwoven textile
    fabric, other than a fabric used for interlining or interfacing,
    used in the manufacture of tailored collar shirts; ([tissu pour
    chemises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tailored collar shirt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tailored collar shirt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a man's or boy's
    fabric shirt of heading No. 62.05 of the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), with a collar that consists of one or
    more pieces of material that are cut and sewn or fused and that is
    designed with two pointed or round ends; ([chemise à col
    façonné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[yarn-dyed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[yarn-dyed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a fabric,
    that the fabric is woven from at least one yarn that is dyed in a
    colour, other than the colour of the base, and is distributed in a
    predetermined and repetitive pattern in the warp direction, whether
    or not any dyed yarns have been distributed in the weft direction.
    ([à fils teints]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-73, s. 12(F)
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025584 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
under the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) to a
tailored collar shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect of
yarn-dyed shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the manufacturer
during the period beginning on January 1, 1998 and ending on December
31, 2004 for use in the manufacture of tailored collar shirts.

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4
and 5, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), to a tailored collar
shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect of yarn-dyed
shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the manufacturer and to a
producer of finished shirting fabrics set out in Schedule B in respect
of greige shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the producer during
the period beginning on January 1, 2005 and ending on December 31, 2006
for use in the manufacture of tailored collar shirts.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-9, s. 3
:::

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4
and 5, remission is hereby granted of 75% of the customs duties paid or
payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), to a tailored
collar shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect of yarn-dyed
shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the manufacturer and to a
producer of finished shirting fabrics set out in Schedule B in respect
of greige shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the producer during
the period beginning on January 1, 2007 and ending on December 31, 2007
for use in the manufacture of tailored collar shirts.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-9, s. 3

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4
and 5, remission is hereby granted of 50% of the customs duties paid or
payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), to a tailored
collar shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect of yarn-dyed
shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the manufacturer and to a
producer of finished shirting fabrics set out in Schedule B in respect
of greige shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the producer during

::: HistoricalNote

**[[2.3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections
4 and 5, a further remission of 25% of the customs duties paid or
payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is hereby granted
to a tailored collar shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect

and to a producer of finished shirting fabrics set out in Schedule B in

collar shirt manufacturer set out in Schedule A in respect of yarn-dyed
shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the manufacturer and to a

5, remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
under the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) to a
producer of finished shirting fabrics set out in Schedule B in respect
of greige and finished shirting fabrics, imported into Canada by the
producer during the period beginning on January 1, 1998 and ending on
December 31, 2004 for use in the manufacture of tailored collar shirts.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-73, s. 13
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
this Order in respect of imports during any calendar year shall not
exceed the total amount of customs duties remitted to the producer under
the Shirting Fabrics Remission Order, 1988 in respect of greige or
yarn-dyed shirting fabrics imported during 1995.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National
Revenue within five years after the day on which the finished and greige
shirting fabrics are imported into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-315, s. 3
:::
