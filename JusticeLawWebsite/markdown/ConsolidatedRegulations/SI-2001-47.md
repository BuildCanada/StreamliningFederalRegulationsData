## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605173 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the First Nation of Nacho
    Nyak Dun Self-Government Agreement signed on May 29, 1993, as it
    read on April 1, 2001. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605177 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission of tax under Part
IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) that was paid by a person
during the period beginning on October 1, 1997 and ending on March 31,
2001 is hereby granted to the person to the extent and in the manner
that a refund of tax is provided for in sections 15.7 to 15.11 of the
agreement, on condition that no refund of that tax is payable under
section 18.1 of the [Yukon First Nations Self-Government
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605179 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Order comes into force on April 2, 2001.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not come into force unless the
  amendment that adds sections 15.7 to 15.11 to the First Nation of
  Nacho Nyak Dun Self-Government Agreement has been consented to in
  accordance with section 6.2 of that Agreement on or before April 1,
  2001.
