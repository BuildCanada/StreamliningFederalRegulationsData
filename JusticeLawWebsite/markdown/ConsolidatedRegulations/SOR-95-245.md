## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973150 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Foreign
    Affairs and International Trade; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973155 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not
apply to export permits issued pursuant to subsection 7(1) of the Act in
respect of softwood lumber products that are described in item 5104 of
Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export Control
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).

## [Fees Payable]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973159 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (1.1), (2) and (3), the fee to be paid by a person to
  whom the Minister issues

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an import permit pursuant to section 8, 8.1, 8.2 or
    8.3 of the Act is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the import permit is delivered by a person
      who is not employed in the public service of Canada but who is
      authorized by the Minister to make the delivery, the fee set out
      in column II of an item of the schedule that corresponds to the
      total value of the goods in respect of which the import permit is
      issued set out in column I of that item, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the import permit is delivered by the
      Export and Import Controls Bureau of the Department, the fee set
      out in column III of an item of the schedule that corresponds to
      the total value of the goods in respect of which the import permit
      is issued set out in column I of that item; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an export permit pursuant to section 7, 8.1 or 8.2
    of the Act or a certificate pursuant to section 9.01 or 9.2 of that
    Act is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$9, where the export permit or certificate is
      delivered by a person who is not employed in the public service of
      Canada but who is authorized by the Minister to make the delivery,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$14, where the export permit or certificate is
      delivered by the Export and Import Controls Bureau of the
      Department.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The fee referred to in paragraph (1)(a) does not
  apply in respect of an import permit issued in respect of goods
  included in items 80 and 81 of the [Import Control
  List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(i) or
  (b)(i) does not include any fee for services incidental to the
  delivery of the permit or certificate that are provided by an
  authorized person referred to in that subparagraph.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fee referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) does
  not apply in respect of an export permit issued in respect of goods
  included in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Groups 1 to 4 of the schedule to the [Export
    Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} items 5000, 5200, 5400, 5401 and 5500 of Group 5 of
    the schedule to that List; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Groups 6 and 7 of the schedule to that List.
