## [Prescribed Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663015 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed fee that is
to accompany an application for deposit insurance made by a provincial
institution under subsection 17(1.1) of the [Canada Deposit Insurance
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3) is \$20,000 and is non-refundable.
