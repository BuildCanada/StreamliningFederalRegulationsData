## Pembroke Airport Zoning Regulations {#pembroke-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-125
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1988-02-04

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Pembroke Airport

P.C. 1988-187 1988-02-04
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Pembroke Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on October 17th and 24th,
1987, and in two successive issues of the Pembroke Observer on
October 24th and 26th, 1987, and two successive issues of [Le
Droit]{.otherLang lang="fr"} on October 2nd and 9th, 1987, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 4.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting Zoning at Pembroke
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-125).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910928 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Pembroke Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-125).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910930 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Pembroke Airport at
      Petawawa, in the Township of Petawawa, in the Province of Ontario;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 160 m above sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-170, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910943 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-170, s. 2(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910947 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910952 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-170, s. 3
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910956 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-isection-2description-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Pembroke Airport Zoning Plan
No. 47-074 84-131, Sheet 5, dated August 21, 1986, is a point which may
be located by:

Commencing at Button 17 being the northerly end of Runway 17-35 at said
Airport;

Thence southerly along the centre line of said Runway 917.82 m to a
point;

Thence westerly and perpendicular to the said Runway centre line
196.26 m to the airport reference point having universal transverse
mercator co-ordinates of 5 081 022.677 N and 325 079.673 E.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iisection-2description-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, as shown on Pembroke Airport Zoning Plan
No. 47-074 84-131, Sheets 4, 5, 6, 10, 11, 12 and 13, dated August 21,
1986, are surfaces abutting each end of the strip associated with the
runway designated 17-35 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 17 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 35 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiisection-2description-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, as shown on Pembroke Airport Zoning Plan No. 47-074
84-131, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, 11 and 12, dated August 21, 1986, is an
imaginary surface located at a common plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point,
except that, where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface
of the ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m
above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivsection-2description-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 17-35, as shown on Pembroke Airport
Zoning Plan No. 47-074 84-131, Sheet 5, dated August 21, 1986, is 300 m
in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
1 643.41 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Description of the Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vsection-2description-of-the-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The transitional surface associated with runway 17-35, as shown on
Pembroke Airport Zoning Plan No. 47-074 84-131, Sheets 4, 5, 6, 11 and
12, dated August 21, 1986, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane
rising at a ratio of a 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected centre
line of the strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.
