## Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees or Charges Order {#minister-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-authority-to-prescribe-fees-or-charges-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/87-93
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-05-13

Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees
or Charges Order

P.C. 1987-844 1987-04-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of
Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fees or
charges to be paid by private sector firms using data from the
Department of Consumer and Corporate Affairs for sale to the public.
