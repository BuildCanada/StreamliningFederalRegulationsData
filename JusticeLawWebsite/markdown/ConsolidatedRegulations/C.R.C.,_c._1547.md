## Fishing Zones of Canada (Zones 1, 2 and 3) Order {#fishing-zones-of-canada-zones-1-2-and-3-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1547
:::

[OCEANS ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.4)

Order Prescribing as Fishing Zones of Canada Certain Areas of the Sea
Adjacent to the Coast of Canada
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527742 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Fishing Zones of Canada (Zones 1, 2 and 3)
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1547).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527744 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[C.H.S. Chart]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[C.H.S. Chart]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian Hydrographic
    Service Chart; ([carte S.H.C.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[geographical coordinates of points]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[geographical coordinates of points]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    latitude and longitude of points determined by reference to Columns
    III and IV in the description of Zones set out in the schedule.
    ([coordonnées géographiques des points]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527748 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The areas of the sea
adjacent to the coast of Canada and described in the schedule are hereby
prescribed as fishing zones of Canada.
