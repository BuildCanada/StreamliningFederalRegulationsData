[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-2
:::

[INTERNATIONAL DEVELOPMENT RESEARCH CENTRE ACT](/eng/acts/I-19)

Registration 2015-02-11

Order Designating the Minister for International Development as the
Minister for the purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-22 2015-01-22
::::

:::: section

the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2[[Footnote

Act](/eng/acts/I-19)[[Footnote
