## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Dolomite Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-dolomite-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-80
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2012-11-07

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Dolomite Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2012-1374 2012-10-18
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1398-E_hq_12717){#fn_81000-2-1398-E_hq_12717-ID0EBBAA},
orders that any portion of the lands described in the annexed schedule
lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the ordinary high water mark
along the shoreline of Dolomite Lake, near the Town of Inuvik, in the
Northwest Territories, be included in any grant of those lands.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1398-E_hq_12717-ID0EBBAA){#fn_81000-2-1398-E_hq_12717}R.S., c.
  T-7
  :::
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-624391 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that portion of Lot 5, Block 103, near the Town of Inuvik, in the
Northwest Territories, as that lot is shown on the plan of survey number
95538 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is
filed in the Land Titles Office for the Northwest Territories Land
Registration District at Yellowknife under number 4233.
