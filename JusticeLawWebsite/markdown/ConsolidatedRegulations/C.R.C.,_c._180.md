## Ontario Chicken Order {#ontario-chicken-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 180
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Chickens Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536097 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Chicken Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._180).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536099 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chicken]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chicken or any class or part
    thereof produced from the egg of a domestic hen where the chicken is
    less than six months of age and was produced in Ontario;
    ([poulet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Chicken
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    chickens established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to
    the Plan. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536106 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of chickens in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of chickens locally within that Province
under the Act and the Plan.
