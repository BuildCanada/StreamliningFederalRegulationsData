## Minister Responsible for the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-responsible-for-the-canada-mortgage-and-housing-corporation-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/81-81
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1981-06-10

Minister Responsible for the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation
Authority to Prescribe Fees Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board and the Minister responsible for the Canada Mortgage
and Housing Corporation, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the
Minister responsible for the said Corporation to prescribe by order the
fees to be charged for services provided by Her Majesty in respect of
the issuance by the Corporation of certificates of multiple-unit
residential building construction starts.
