## Revenue Trust Account Regulations {#revenue-trust-account-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 730
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Respecting Revenue Trust Accounts
::::

::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561144 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Revenue Trust Account
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._730).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561148 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any local cooperative credit society that is a
      member of a central cooperative credit society having membership
      in the Canadian Payments Association, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any fiscal agent or financial institution
      designated by the Minister pursuant to subsection 17(2) of the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([institution
      financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Revenue Trust Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Revenue Trust Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account
    established in a financial institution pursuant to these Regulations
    for the deposit of money paid to the credit of the Receiver General
    and not being public money. ([compte de recettes en
    fiducie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 3
- SOR/94-402, s. 4
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided,
these Regulations, notwithstanding the Repayment of Receipts Regulations
and the Receipt and Deposit of Public Money Regulations, apply in any
circumstances in which a Revenue Trust Account has been established.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where money has been paid
to a public officer in the Northwest Territories or the Yukon Territory
or in such other area for which and for a purpose for which the Minister
of Finance has authorized the establishment of a Revenue Trust Account,
the money may be paid into that account.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Excess Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561163 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  money has been received by a public officer as a deposit to ensure the
  doing of any act or thing and paid into the Revenue Trust Account, the
  public officer shall, where the act or thing has been done or where
  the deposit is otherwise no longer required, repay the money to the
  person who paid it or to the person's legal representative.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where money received by a public officer as a deposit
  to ensure the doing of any act or thing and paid into the Revenue
  Trust Account exceeds the amount prescribed for that purpose or, where
  no such amount has been prescribed, the amount required for that
  purpose, the excess amount shall be repaid to the person who paid it
  or to the person's legal representative.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
- SOR/93-258, s. 2
:::

## [Purpose Not Fulfilled]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561169 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where money has been paid
into a Revenue Trust Account for any purpose and the appropriate
Minister is of opinion that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the money has been paid has not
  been fulfilled, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no service has been rendered by or on behalf of Her
  Majesty,

the amount so paid may be repaid to the person who paid it or to his
legal representatives.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where money has been paid
into a Revenue Trust Account for any purpose and the appropriate
Minister is of opinion that the purpose for which the money has been
paid has been fulfilled, the amount so paid less any money in excess of
that required for the purpose shall be deposited to the credit of the
Receiver General at least once each week in accordance with the Receipt
and Deposit of Public Money Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  money has been paid into a Revenue Trust Account for any purpose and
  the appropriate Minister is of opinion that the purpose for which the
  money has been paid has not been fulfilled but that a service has been
  rendered by or on behalf of Her Majesty, the appropriate Minister
  shall refer the matter to the Treasury Board with a statement of the
  relevant facts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a reference is made to the Treasury Board,
  pursuant to subsection (1), the Treasury Board shall determine the
  amount, if any, that is to be retained by Her Majesty in respect of
  any service rendered and the amount so determined shall be deposited
  to the credit of the Receiver General in accordance with the Receipt
  and Deposit of Public Money Regulations, and the balance, if any,
  shall be repaid to the person who paid it or his legal
  representatives.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-829, s. 2(F)
:::
