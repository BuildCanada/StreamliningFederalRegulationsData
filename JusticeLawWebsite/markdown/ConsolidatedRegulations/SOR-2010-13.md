## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757766 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[experts performing missions for the Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[experts performing missions for the
    Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means governmental or
    intergovernmental experts who are invited by the Organization to
    attend the Meeting and the preparatory meetings. ([experts en
    mission pour l'Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meeting of the
    Organization to be held in Huntsville, Ontario from June 25 to
    27, 2010. ([réunion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
    conference of the eight leading industrialized countries, also known
    as the G8. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[preparatory meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the political directors of the G8
      member countries to be held in Quebec City, Quebec from January 21
      to 22, 2010, in Victoria, British Columbia from March 1 to 2,
      2010, in Ottawa, Ontario on March 29, 2010, in Halifax, Nova
      Scotia from June 7 to 8, 2010 and in Huntsville, Ontario from June
      22 to 24, 2010;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the personal representatives of
      the Heads of State and Government of the G8 member countries,
      known as sherpas, to be held in Yellowknife, Northwest Territories
      from February 4 to 5, 2010, in Vancouver, British Columbia from
      April 15 to 16, 2010, in Toronto, Ontario from May 27 to 28, 2010
      and in Huntsville, Ontario from June 22 to 24, 2010;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the Ministers of Finance of the
      seven leading industrialized countries to be held in Iqaluit,
      Nunavut from February 5 to 6, 2010;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the personal representatives of
      the Ministers of Foreign Affairs of the G8 member countries, known
      as sous-sherpas, to be held in Quebec City, Quebec from February 8
      to 9, 2010, in Calgary, Alberta from March 22 to 23, 2010, in
      Vancouver, British Columbia from May 10 to 11, 2010, in Toronto,
      Ontario from May 29 to June 1, 2010 and in Huntsville, Ontario
      from June 22 to 24, 2010;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the G8 Accountability Senior
      Level Working Group to be held in Ottawa, Ontario from March 17 to
      18, 2010 and from April 28 to 29, 2010;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the Ministers of Foreign Affairs
      of the G8 member countries to be held in Gatineau, Quebec from
      March 29 to 30, 2010;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the African Partnership Forum to
      be held in Ottawa, Ontario from April 13 to 15, 2010;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the meetings of the Africa Personal
      Representatives to be held in Ottawa, Ontario from April 13 to 15,
      2010, in Toronto, Ontario from June 1 to 2, 2010 and in
      Huntsville, Ontario from June 22 to 24, 2010; or

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other meetings of any of the participants
      referred to in paragraphs (a) to (h) to be held in Canada during
      the period beginning on January 15, 2010 and ending on June
      24, 2010. ([réunions préparatoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757782 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada during the period beginning on
  January 15, 2010 and ending on July 4, 2010 the legal capacity of a
  body corporate and the privileges and immunities set out in sections 2
  to 5 of Article II of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of or participate in the Organization shall have in Canada

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on January 29, 2010 and
    ending on February 13, 2010, the period beginning on March 22, 2010
    and ending on April 6, 2010 and the period beginning on June 15,
    2010 and ending on July 4, 2010, to the extent required for the
    exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to the Meeting and
    the preparatory meetings of the Ministers of Finance and the
    Ministers of Foreign Affairs, the privileges and immunities set out
    in sections 11, 12 and 14 to 16 of Article IV of the Convention; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on January 15, 2010 and
    ending on July 4, 2010, to the extent required for the exercise of
    their functions in Canada in relation to the other preparatory
    meetings, the privileges and immunities set out in section 11(d) of
    Article IV of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall have in Canada during the period beginning on January 15, 2010
  and ending on July 4, 2010, to the extent required for the exercise of
  their functions in Canada in relation to the Meeting and the
  preparatory meetings, the privileges and immunities set out in Article
  VI of the Convention.
