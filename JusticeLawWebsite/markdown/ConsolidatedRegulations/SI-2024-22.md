## Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in Nunavut (Kivalliq area) Order {#withdrawal-from-disposal-of-certain-tracts-of-territorial-lands-in-nunavut-kivalliq-area-order .Title-of-Act}

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in
Nunavut (Kivalliq area) Order

P.C. 2024-508 2024-05-09
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Northern Affairs, makes the annexed [Withdrawal from
Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in Nunavut (Kivalliq
area) Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2024-22) under paragraph 23(a) of the
[Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5301_hq_1697506415){#fn_81000-2-5301_hq_1697506415-ID0EBBAA}.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Order
is to withdraw certain tracts of territorial lands in Nunavut from
disposal in order to facilitate the conclusion of Indigenous land
agreements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands withdrawn from disposal

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The tracts of territorial
lands set out in the schedule, including the surface and subsurface
rights to the lands, are withdrawn from disposal for a period of 10
years beginning on the day on which this Order is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- disposal of material

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply in
respect of the disposal of any [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
defined in section 2 of the [Territorial Quarrying
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- existing rights and
interests

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
section 2 does not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recording of a mineral claim for which an
  application to record was made before the day on which this Order is
  made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a lease under the [Nunavut Mining
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-69) to a holder of a recorded
  claim, if the lease covers an area in the recorded claim;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a significant discovery licence under
  the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of
  an exploration licence that was issued before the day on which this
  Order is made, if the significant discovery licence covers an area
  that is subject to the exploration licence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a production licence under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of a
  significant discovery licence that is referred to in paragraph (c), if
  the production licence covers an area that is subject to the
  significant discovery licence;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a surface lease under the
  [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) to a holder of a recorded claim
  under the [Nunavut Mining Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-69)
  or of an interest under the [Canada Petroleum Resources
  Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), if the surface lease is required to allow the
  holder to exercise rights under the claim or interest; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the renewal of an interest.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The [Withdrawal from
Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in Nunavut (Kivalliq
area) Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2024-22)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-5301_hq_1697552083){#fn_81000-2-5301_hq_1697552083-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Tracts of Territorial Lands Withdrawn from Disposal (Kivalliq Area)]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1451948 .scheduleLabel}
:::

In Nunavut, all those tracts of territorial lands that are shown as
"surface and subsurface" or "subsurface only" on the following 1:250 000
Territorial Resource Base Maps, dated 2023, as agreed to among the
representatives and negotiators from [Ghotelnene K'odtįneh
Dene]{.otherLang lang="iu"}, the [Athabasca Denesųłiné]{.otherLang
lang="iu"}, Nunavut Tunngavik Inc., the Kivalliq Inuit Association, the
Government of Nunavut and the Government of Canada, copies of which have
been deposited with the Regional Manager of Land Administration at the
Nunavut Regional Office in Iqaluit, Nunavut:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 55D;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 65A;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 65B;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 65C;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} 65F;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} 65G; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} 65H.
