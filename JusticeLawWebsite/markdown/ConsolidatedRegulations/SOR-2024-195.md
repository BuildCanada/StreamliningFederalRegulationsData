## Critical Habitat of the Spotted Gar (Lepisosteus oculatus) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-spotted-gar-lepisosteus-oculatus-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-195
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2024-09-25

Critical Habitat of the Spotted Gar (Lepisosteus oculatus) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Spotted Gar ([Lepisosteus oculatus]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061){#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061-ID0EBBGA){#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a place
referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559074){#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559074-ID0EBBEA}
of that Act and, under subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must
be excluded from the annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559074-ID0EBBEA){#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559074}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas, under subsection 58(5) of that Act, the Minister of
Fisheries and Oceans has consulted with the Minister responsible for the
Parks Canada Agency, namely the Minister of the Environment, with
respect to the annexed Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Spotted Gar (Lepisosteus oculatus)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-195) under subsection 58(4) and
paragraph 58(5)(a) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061){#fn_81000-2-5159_hq_1685559061-ID0EBACA}.

Ottawa, September 20, 2024

## [Critical Habitat]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1488331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Spotted Gar ([Lepisosteus oculatus]{.otherLang lang="la"}) ---
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry --- other than the
portion of that critical habitat that is in a place referred to in
subsection 58(2) of that Act.
