## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657566 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657567 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[concentrate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[concentrate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an extracted product that
    contains uranium and that results from the physical or chemical
    separation of uranium from ore. ([concentré]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2007-208, s.
    13\]]{.Repealed}

[[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equivalent dose]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned to
    that term by subsection 1(1) of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([dose
    équivalente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[excavation site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[excavation site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place at which
    uranium is moved by means of underground activities for the purpose
    of evaluating a potential orebody. ([site
    d'excavation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[federal authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[federal authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Minister of the Crown in right of Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agency of the Government of Canada or a parent
      Crown corporation, as defined in subsection 83(1) of the
      [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or any other body
      established under an Act of Parliament that is ultimately
      accountable through a Minister of the Crown in right of Canada to
      Parliament for the conduct of its affairs;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any department or departmental corporation that
      is set out in Schedule I or II to the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other body that is set out in Schedule 1 to
      the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
      2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21); and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Executive Council of --- or a minister,
      department, agency or body of the government of --- Yukon, the
      Northwest Territories or Nunavut.

      It does not include a council of the band as defined in subsection
      2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), Export Development Canada
      or the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board. It also does not
      include a Crown corporation that is a wholly-owned subsidiary, as
      defined in subsection 83(1) of the [Financial Administration
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or a not-for-profit corporation that enters
      into an agreement under subsection 80(5) of the [Canada Marine
      Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), that is not set out in Schedule 1 to the
      [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21).
      ([autorité fédérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance, other
    than a nuclear substance, that is used or produced in the course of
    carrying on a licensed activity and that may pose a risk to the
    environment or the health and safety of persons. ([substance
    dangereuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[jurisdiction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a federal authority;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any agency or body that is established under an
      Act of Parliament and that has powers, duties or functions in
      relation to an assessment of the environmental effects of the
      preparation of a site for a uranium mine or mill or its
      construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the government of a province;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any agency or body that is established under an
      Act of the legislature of a province, and that has powers, duties
      or functions in relation to an assessment of the environmental
      effects of the preparation of a site for a uranium mine or mill or
      its construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any body that is established under a land claims
      agreement referred to in section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
      1982](/eng/Const/), and that has powers, duties or functions in
      relation to an assessment of the environmental effects of the
      preparation of a site for a uranium mine or mill or its
      construction, operation, decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a governing body that is established under
      legislation that relates to the self-government of Indians and
      that has powers, duties or functions in relation to an assessment
      of the environmental effects of the preparation of a site for a
      uranium mine or mill or its construction, operation,
      decommissioning or abandonment;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a government of a foreign state or of a
      subdivision of a foreign state, or any institution of such a
      government; and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an international organization of states or any
      institution of such an organization. ([instance]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    described in paragraph 26(e) of the Act that a licence authorizes
    the licensee to carry on in relation to a uranium mine or mill.
    ([activité autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
    carry on an activity described in paragraph 26(e) of the Act in
    relation to a uranium mine or mill. ([titulaire de
    permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility at which ore is
    processed and treated for the recovery of uranium concentrate,
    including any tailings-handling and water treatment system
    associated with the facility. ([usine de
    concentration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an excavation site and a
    removal site. ([mine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mineral or chemical aggregate
    containing uranium in a quantity and of a quality that makes mining
    and extracting the uranium economically viable.
    ([minerai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[removal site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[removal site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place at which uranium
    is removed from its place of natural deposit by means of surface
    activities for the purpose of evaluating a potential orebody. ([site
    d'extraction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste management system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste management system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system for
    collecting, transporting, receiving, treating, processing, storing
    or disposing of the wastes that are produced as a result of the
    licensed activity at a uranium mine or mill. ([système de gestion
    des déchets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work that
    is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[workers' representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[workers' representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is a member of the workers' safety
      and health committee;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the workers' safety and health representative;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b), the workers' collective bargaining agent; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where there is no person referred to in paragraph
      (a), (b) or (c), a worker. ([représentant des
      travailleurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[work place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any area within a uranium
    mine or mill where a worker could reasonably be expected to be in
    the course of performing work. ([lieu de travail]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-208, s. 13
- SOR/2012-288, s. 3
- SOR/2015-67, s. 2

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657607 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of uranium mines and mills.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of uranium
  prospecting or surface exploration activities.

## [Licence Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657611 .Part}

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657612 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence in respect of a uranium mine or mill, other than a licence to
abandon, shall contain the following information in addition to the
information required by section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and
Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the plan and description of the mine
  or mill,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the site evaluation process and of
    the investigations and preparatory work to be done at the site and
    in the surrounding area,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a surface plan indicating the boundaries of the
    mine or mill and the area where the activity to be licensed is
    proposed to be carried on,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a plan showing the existing and planned
    structures, excavations and underground development,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of the mine or mill, including the
    installations, their purpose and capacity, and any excavations and
    underground development,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a description of the site geology and mineralogy,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a description of any activity that may have an
    impact on the development of the mine or mill, including any
    mining-related activity that was carried on at the site before the
    date of submission of the application to the Commission,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the design of and the
    maintenance program for every eating area,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plan for the decommissioning of the
    mine or mill, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed emergency power
    systems and their capacities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the activity to be licensed,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of and the schedule for the planned
    activity,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed methods for carrying
    on the activity,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list of the categories of material proposed to
    be mined and a description of the criteria used to determine those
    categories,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated duration of the activity, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proposed management system for the activity,
    including measures to promote and support safety culture;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the environment and waste management,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the program to inform persons living in the
    vicinity of the mine or mill of the general nature and
    characteristics of the anticipated effects of the activity to be
    licensed on the environment and the health and safety of persons,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the program to determine the environmental
    baseline characteristics of the site and the surrounding area,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment that may result
    from the activity to be licensed, and the measures that will be
    taken to prevent or mitigate those effects,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the proposed positions for and qualifications and
    responsibilities of environmental protection workers,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proposed environmental protection policies and
    programs,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the proposed effluent and environmental monitoring
    programs,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location, the proposed maximum
    quantities and concentrations, and the anticipated volume and flow
    rate of releases of nuclear substances and hazardous substances into
    the environment, including their physical, chemical and radiological
    characteristics,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control releases of
    nuclear substances and hazardous substances into the environment,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} a description of the anticipated liquid and solid
    waste streams within the mine or mill, including the ingress of
    fresh water and any diversion or control of the flow of
    uncontaminated surface and ground water,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to prevent or mitigate the
    effects of accidental releases of nuclear substances and hazardous
    substances on the environment, the health and safety of persons and
    the maintenance of security, including measures to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} assist off-site authorities in planning and
      preparing to limit the adverse effects of an accidental release,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} notify off-site authorities of an accidental
      release or the imminence of an accidental release,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} report information to off-site authorities during
      and after an accidental release,

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} assist off-site authorities in dealing with the
      adverse effects of an accidental release, and

    - [(E)]{.lawlabel} test the implementation of the measures to
      control the adverse effects of an accidental release,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated quantities, composition and
    characteristics of backfill, and

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed waste management
    system;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in relation to health and safety,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the health and safety of persons
    that may result from the activity to be licensed, and the measures
    that will be taken to prevent or mitigate those effects,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed program for selecting, using and
    maintaining personal protective equipment,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed worker health and safety policies
    and programs,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the proposed positions for and qualifications and
    responsibilities of radiation protection workers,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the proposed training program for workers,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control the spread of any
    radioactive contamination,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed ventilation and dust control methods
    and equipment for controlling air quality, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the proposed level of effectiveness of and
    inspection schedule for the ventilation and dust control systems;
    and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in relation to security, the proposed measures to
  alert the licensee to acts of sabotage or attempted sabotage at the
  mine or mill.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-199, s. 6

### [Requirement for Code of Practice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657660 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[action level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a specific dose of
  radiation or other parameter that, if reached, may indicate a loss of
  control of part of a licensee's radiation protection program or
  environmental protection program, and triggers a requirement for
  specific action to be taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence in respect of a uranium
  mine or mill, other than a licence to abandon, shall contain a
  proposed code of practice that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any action level that the applicant considers
    appropriate for the purpose of this subsection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action that the applicant will
    take if an action level is reached; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reporting procedures that will be followed if
    an action level is reached.

### [Licence to Prepare Site and Construct]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657667 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence to prepare a site for and construct a
  uranium mine shall contain the following information in addition to
  the information required by section 3 and subsection 4(2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed design of the mine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed construction program, including its
    schedule;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the components, systems and
    equipment proposed to be installed at the mine, including their
    design operating conditions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program for the
    design of the mine;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of a process-hazard analysis and a
    description of how those results have been taken into account;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed design, construction
    and operation of the waste management system, including the measures
    to monitor its construction and operation, the construction
    schedule, the contingency plans for construction and the measures to
    control the movement of water in existing waterways;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed disposition of the
    ore;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated quantities and grade of ore and
    waste rock that will be removed, their proposed storage location,
    and the proposed method, program and schedule, for their removal and
    disposal;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed mining methods and programs; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed commissioning plan for the components,
    systems and equipment to be installed at the mine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence to prepare a site for
  and construct a uranium mill shall contain the following information
  in addition to the information required by section 3 and subsection
  4(2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed design of the mill;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed construction program, including its
    schedule;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the components, systems and
    equipment proposed to be installed at the mill, including their
    design operating conditions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program for the
    design of the mill;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of a process-hazard analysis and a
    description of how those results have been taken into account;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed design, construction
    and operation of the waste management system, including the measures
    to monitor its construction and operation, the construction
    schedule, the contingency plans for construction and the measures to
    control the movement of water in existing waterways;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed milling methods and programs;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of all proposed laboratory facilities
    and programs; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed commissioning plan for the components,
    systems and equipment to be installed at the mill.

### [Licence to Operate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657690 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence to operate a uranium mine shall contain the
  following information in addition to the information required by
  section 3 and subsection 4(2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of any commissioning work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the structures, components,
    systems and equipment at the mine, including any changes to their
    design and their design operating conditions as a result of the
    commissioning;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed policies, methods and programs for
    operating and maintaining the mine; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods for handling, storing, loading
    and transporting nuclear substances and hazardous substances.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence to operate a uranium
  mill shall contain the following information in addition to the
  information required by section 3 and subsection 4(2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of any commissioning work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the structures, components,
    systems and equipment at the mill, including any changes to their
    design and their design operating conditions as a result of the
    commissioning;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed policies, methods and programs for
    operating and maintaining the mill;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods for handling, storing and
    loading concentrates and uranium-bearing material, both solid and
    liquid;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed operating schedule;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the daily and annual design capacity of the mill,
    and the expected recovery and composition of mill feed, concentrates
    and tailings; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed operation of the
    waste management system.

### [Licence to Decommission]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657705 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to decommission a uranium mine or mill shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3 and subsection 4(2):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of and the proposed schedule for the
  decommissioning work, including the proposed starting date and the
  expected completion date of the decommissioning work and the rationale
  for the schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the land, buildings, structures, components, systems,
  equipment, nuclear substances and hazardous substances that will be
  affected by the decommissioning;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures, methods and programs for
  carrying on the decommissioning; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the planned state of the site upon
  completion of the decommissioning work.

### [Licence to Abandon]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657711 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to abandon a uranium mine or mill shall contain the following
information in addition to the information required by sections 3 and 4
of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the program to inform persons living in the vicinity
  of the site of the mine or mill of the general nature and
  characteristics of the anticipated effects of the abandonment on the
  environment and the health and safety of person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the decommissioning work; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the results of the environmental monitoring programs.

## [Time Lines: Application for Licence to Prepare Site and Construct]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657716 .Part}

### [Compliance Verification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657717 .Subheading}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall,
within 60 days after the day on which an application for a licence to
prepare a site for and construct a uranium mine or mill is received,
determine whether the application contains sufficient detailed
information for the Commission to commence its review.

### [Time Periods --- Review of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657721 .Subheading}

**[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall,
within five days after the day on which it determines that an
application contains sufficient detailed information for it to commence
its review, give notice of the commencement of its review

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing notice in writing to this effect by mail
  or email to the applicant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by posting notice to this effect on its Internet
  site.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-288, s. 4
:::

- **[[8.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall render its decision in respect of an application
  within a time period of 24 months from the day on which the notice is
  posted in accordance with paragraph 8.2(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following are excluded from the 24-month time
  period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any period granted by the Commission for the
    preparation and submission of information requested by the
    Commission, which in the opinion of the Commission is necessary to
    complete the review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any period, not to exceed 30 days following the
    Commission's receipt of a response to the request for information
    referred to in paragraph (a), that the Commission requires to
    determine whether the information requested has been provided and is
    adequate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any period that is required by any jurisdiction to
    respond to an offer to consult and cooperate made by the Commission
    under section 18 of the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
    2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) with respect to the environmental
    assessment of the proposed preparation of the site for, and
    construction of, the uranium mine or mill, or its operation,
    decommissioning or abandonment, and, if the offer is accepted by any
    jurisdiction, any period that is required for consultation and
    cooperation with that jurisdiction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any period that is required to conduct, and render
    a decision on, an environmental assessment of the proposed
    preparation of the site for, and construction of, the uranium mine
    or mill, or its operation, decommissioning or abandonment, by any
    jurisdiction that is obligated by law to conduct that assessment and
    render a decision; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any period during which the licence application
    review was adjourned under section 14 of the [Canadian Nuclear
    Safety Commission Rules of
    Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-211).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall give notice of the beginning and
  end of any period that is excluded from the 24 months

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by providing notice in writing to this effect by
    mail or email to the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by posting notice to this effect on its Internet
    site.

- SOR/2012-288, s. 4

## [Obligations of Licensees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657740 .Part}

### [Posting of Code of Practice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657741 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall post a
copy of the code of practice referred to in the licence at a location
within the uranium mine or mill that is accessible to all workers and
where it is most likely to come to their attention.

### [Operating Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657743 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish, implement and maintain written operating
  procedures for the licensed activity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} train its workers to perform their work in accordance
  with the operating procedures; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} audit its workers for the purpose of verifying
  compliance with the operating procedures.

### [Ventilation Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657748 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall,
with respect to the ventilation systems established in accordance with
the licence,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that each main fan is equipped with a device
  that provides a warning signal when the main fan is not functioning
  properly;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that a person is designated to receive and
  respond to a warning signal provided by a device referred to in
  paragraph (a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} implement measures to prevent any person or activity
  from interfering with the proper operation of the ventilation systems.

### [Malfunction of Ventilation System]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657753 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a ventilation system in a work place is not functioning in accordance
  with a licence, the licensee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} implement alternative measures to protect the
    health and safety of its workers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that only the work necessary to restore that
    system is performed in the work place.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a worker performs any work that is necessary
  to restore a ventilation system, the licensee shall inform the worker
  of the protective measures that have been taken and are to be taken in
  connection with the work.

### [Use of Respirators]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657759 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall rely on
the use of a respirator to comply with the [Radiation Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203) unless the use of the
respirator

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is for a temporary or unforeseen situation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is permitted by the code of practice referred to in
  the licence.

### [Gamma Radiation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657763 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} post signs at all entrances to each area where the
  dose rate of gamma radiation exceeds 25 µSv/h, designating the area as
  a radiation area and indicating the dose rate of gamma radiation in
  that area; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide every worker who is to enter an area where
  the dose rate of gamma radiation exceeds 100 µSv/h with a
  direct-reading dosimeter.

### [Training Program]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657767 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a worker successfully completes the training program in basic
  radiation health and safety referred to in a licence, the licensee
  shall issue to the worker a certificate indicating that the worker has
  completed a training program in basic radiation health and safety that
  is acceptable to the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall provide a copy of the training
  program referred to in the licence to a workers' representative.

## [Records to Be Kept and Made Available]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657771 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its operating and maintenance procedures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its mine plans showing the actual and planned mine
    workings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the schedules for all of its planned mining
    operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the plans of every tailings-containment structure
    and area and every diversion structure and system associated with
    the waste management system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the design of the uranium mine or mill and of the
    components and systems installed at the mine or mill;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the method and relevant data used to ascertain the
    doses of radiation received by the workers at the uranium mine or
    mill and the intake of radioactive nuclear substances by those
    workers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any measurement made in accordance with the licence
    or the regulations made under the Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the inspections and maintenance carried out in
    accordance with the licence or the regulations made under the Act;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of air delivered by each main fan;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the performance of each dust control system; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the training received by each worker.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall make the records referred to in
  subsection (1) available at the uranium mine or mill to the workers
  and a workers' representative.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall retain a record of the training
  referred to in paragraph (1)(k) for the period that the worker is
  employed at the uranium mine or mill.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall post, at a location within the
  uranium mine or mill that is accessible to all workers and where it is
  most likely to come to their attention, a record of the measurements
  made in respect of every work place in accordance with the licence and
  these Regulations.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657788 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are approved by the Governor in
Council.
