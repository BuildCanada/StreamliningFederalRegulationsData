## Critical Habitat of the Northern Madtom (Noturus stigmosus) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-northern-madtom-noturus-stigmosus-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-81
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2016-05-02

Critical Habitat of the Northern Madtom (Noturus stigmosus) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Northern Madtom ([Noturus stigmosus]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b-ID0EBBGA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2) of that Act;

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that
the annexed Order would affect a reserve or any other lands that are set
apart for the use and benefit of a band and, pursuant to subsection
58(7) of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development and the band with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Northern Madtom (Noturus stigmosus)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-81).

Ottawa, April 21, 2016

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838312 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Northern Madtom ([Noturus stigmosus]{.otherLang lang="la"}),
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838314 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
