## Allocation Method Order -- Softwood Lumber Products {#allocation-method-order-softwood-lumber-products .Title-of-Act}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[current softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[current softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    products described in item 5104, Group 5, of the schedule to the
    Export Control List. ([produits de bois d'oeuvre
    courants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning set out in section
    6.4 of the Act. ([exporté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[former softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    products described in item 5105, Group 5, of the schedule to the
    Export Control List as it existed during the reference
    period. ([anciens produits de bois d'oeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    current softwood lumber products that may be exported from Manitoba
    to the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour le Manitoba]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    current softwood lumber products that may be exported from Ontario
    to the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour l'Ontario]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who produced
    or produces former, current or Quebec softwood lumber products from
    softwood sawlogs. ([entreprise de première
    transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the former
    softwood lumber products that the primary producer produced at any
    time and that were not remanufactured in Canada. ([produits d'une
    entreprise de première transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    current softwood lumber products that may be exported from Quebec to
    the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
    Act. ([quantité pour le Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    softwood lumber products produced in Quebec that were reported to
    the Minister of Natural Resources and Wildlife under the Forest Act,
    R.S.Q., c. F-4.1. ([produits de bois d'oeuvre du
    Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on April 1, 2001 and ending on December 31, 2005. ([période de
    référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    remanufactured or remanufactures --- within the meaning of
    subsection 13(1) of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
    2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55) --- former or current softwood lumber
    products. ([entreprise de seconde transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the former
    softwood lumber products that the remanufacturer remanufactured at
    any time and that were not further remanufactured in
    Canada. ([produits d'une entreprise de seconde
    transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
    current softwood lumber products that may be exported from
    Saskatchewan to the United States during a month under subsection
    6.3(2) of the Act. ([quantité pour la Saskatchewan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order establishes the
method for allocating the monthly quantity of current softwood lumber
products that may be exported from Ontario, Quebec, Manitoba and
Saskatchewan in 2007 for the purposes of paragraph 6.3(3)(a) of the Act.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Ontario]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734766 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Ontario quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies for
an export allocation is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#734769 .Section}
OQ × (HP/THP)

where

[OQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Ontario quantity;

[HP]{.dfn}
:   is the primary producer's or remanufacturer's percentage share as
    determined under section 4; and

[THP]{.dfn}
:   is the total of all primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    percentage shares as determined under section 4.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Best 12-month period

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[best 12-month period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
  primary producer, means the 12-month period set out in the description
  of PPBEV in paragraph (2)(a) and, in respect of a remanufacturer,
  means the 12-month period set out in the description of RBEV in
  paragraph (3)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producer's percentage share

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The percentage share of a primary producer is the
  higher of the percentages calculated under paragraphs (a) and (b):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the percentage calculated in accordance with the
    formula

    ::: {#734780 .Paragraph}
    (PPBEV/TBEV) × 100

    where

    [PPBEV]{.dfn}
    :   is the highest volume of the primary producer's products that
        were exported from Ontario to the United States under export
        permits during a period of 12 consecutive months within the
        reference period, and

    [TBEV]{.dfn}
    :   is the total volume of the primary producers' and
        remanufacturers' products exported from Ontario to the United
        States under export permits during their respective best
        12-month periods, excluding the volume of primary producers and
        remanufacturers who indicate in writing to the Minister before
        December 14, 2006 that they relinquish their allocation of the
        Ontario quantity for 2007;
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage calculated in accordance with the
    formula

    ::: {#734785 .Paragraph}
    (PPEO/TEO) × 100

    where

    [PPEO]{.dfn}
    :   is the volume of the primary producer's products that were
        exported from Ontario to the United States under export permits
        during the reference period, and

    [TEO]{.dfn}
    :   is the total volume of the primary producers' and
        remanufacturers' products exported from Ontario to the United
        States under export permits during the reference period,
        excluding the volume of primary producers and remanufacturers
        who indicate in writing to the Minister before December 14, 2006
        that they relinquish their allocation of the Ontario quantity
        for 2007.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remanufacturer's percentage share

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The percentage share of a remanufacturer is the
  higher of the percentages calculated under paragraphs (a) and (b):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the percentage calculated in accordance with the
    formula

    ::: {#734792 .Paragraph}
    (RBEV/TBEV) × 100

    where

    [RBEV]{.dfn}
    :   is the highest volume of the remanufacturer's products that were
        exported from Ontario to the United States under export permits
        during a period of 12 consecutive months within the reference
        period, and

    [TBEV]{.dfn}
    :   is the total volume of the primary producers' and
        remanufacturers' products exported from Ontario to the United
        States under export permits during their respective best
        12-month periods, excluding the volume of primary producers and
        remanufacturers who indicate in writing to the Minister before
        December 14, 2006 that they relinquish their allocation of the
        Ontario quantity for 2007;
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage calculated in accordance with the
    formula

    ::: {#734797 .Paragraph}
    (REO/TEO) × 100

    where

    [REO]{.dfn}
    :   is the volume of the remanufacturer's products exported from
        Ontario to the United States under export permits during the
        reference period, and

    [TEO]{.dfn}
    :   is the total volume of the primary producers' and
        remanufacturers' products exported from Ontario to the United
        States under export permits during the reference period,
        excluding the volume of primary producers and remanufacturers
        who indicate in writing to the Minister before December 14, 2006
        that they relinquish their allocation of the Ontario quantity
        for 2007.
    :::

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Quebec]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734801 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers with an export history

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If some
  or all of a primary producer's products were exported from Quebec to
  the United States under export permits during the reference period and
  the primary producer applies for an export allocation, the primary
  producer must choose to receive an allocation of the Quebec quantity
  under the method described in section 6 (which is based on their
  historic export volume) or the method described in subsection 7(1)
  (which is based on their historic production volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers without an export
  history

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If none of a primary producer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and the primary producer produced Quebec softwood
  lumber products during the period beginning on January 1, 2003 and
  ending on December 31, 2005 and applies for an export allocation, the
  primary producer may only receive the allocation under the method
  described in subsection 7(1) (which is based on their historic
  production volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remanufacturers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If some or all of a remanufacturer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and the remanufacturer applies for an export
  allocation, the remanufacturer may only receive the allocation under
  the method described in section 9 (which is based on their historic
  export volume).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic export volume

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based on their historic
export volume is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#734811 .Section}
QQ × (PPE/TQE) × 94%

where

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[PPE]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's products exported from
    Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period; and

[TQE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of former softwood lumber products exported from
    Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic production volume

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  allocation of the Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based
  on their historic production volume is calculated in accordance with
  the formula

  ::: {#734819 .Subsection}
  RP × (PPPV/TPV)

  where

  [RP]{.dfn}
  :   is the reserve pool calculated in accordance with section 8;

  [PPPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of Quebec softwood lumber products that the primary
      producer produced during the period beginning on January 1, 2003
      and ending on December 31, 2005; and

  [TPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of Quebec softwood lumber products produced
      during the period beginning on January 1, 2003 and ending on
      December 31, 2005 of all primary producers whose allocations are
      based on their historic production volume.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of production volume

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The volume of Quebec softwood lumber products
  produced by a primary producer is based on records provided, with the
  consent of the primary producer, by the Government of Quebec to the
  Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve pool

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  reserve pool is calculated in accordance with the formula

  ::: {#734829 .Subsection}
  A + B + C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of former softwood lumber products determined in
      accordance with subsection (2);

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of former softwood lumber products determined in
      accordance with subsection (3); and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of former softwood lumber products determined in
      accordance with subsection (4).
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of A

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The volume of former softwood lumber products for the
  description of A in subsection (1) is calculated in accordance with
  the formula

  ::: {#734836 .Subsection}
  QQ × (TPPE/TQE) × 6%

  where

  [QQ]{.dfn}
  :   is the Quebec quantity;

  [TPPE]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of primary producers' products that were
      exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
      during the reference period in respect of primary producers who
      have chosen to receive an allocation of the Quebec quantity under
      the allocation method described in section 6; and

  [TQE]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of former softwood lumber products exported
      from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
      reference period.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of B

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The volume of former softwood lumber products for the
  description of B in subsection (1) is calculated in accordance with
  the formula

  ::: {#734843 .Subsection}
  QQ × (TPPEPV/TQE)

  where

  [QQ]{.dfn}
  :   is the Quebec quantity;

  [TPPEPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of primary producers' products exported from
      Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
      reference period in respect of primary producers who have chosen
      to receive an allocation of the Quebec quantity under the
      allocation method described in subsection 7(1); and

  [TQE]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of former softwood lumber products exported
      from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
      reference period.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description of C

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The volume of former softwood lumber products for the
  description of C in subsection (1) is calculated in accordance with
  the formula

  ::: {#734850 .Subsection}
  QQ × (TNPE/TQE)

  where

  [QQ]{.dfn}
  :   is the Quebec quantity;

  [TNPE]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of primary producers' and remanufacturers'
      products exported from Quebec to the United States under export
      permits during the reference period in respect of primary
      producers and remanufacturers who do not apply for an export
      allocation before December 14, 2006; and

  [TQE]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of former softwood lumber products exported
      from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
      reference period.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to remanufacturers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Quebec quantity to a remanufacturer that is based on their historic
export volume is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#734857 .Section}
QQ × (RE/TQE)

where

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[RE]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the remanufacturer's products exported from Quebec
    to the United States under export permits during the reference
    period; and

[TQE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of former softwood lumber products exported from
    Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period.
:::

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Manitoba]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734862 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Manitoba quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies
for an export allocation is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#734865 .Section}
MQ × (EM/TEM)

where

[MQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Manitoba quantity;

[EM]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
    that were exported from Manitoba to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period; and

[TEM]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products exported from Manitoba to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of primary
    producers and remanufacturers who indicate in writing to the
    Minister before December 14, 2006 that they relinquish their
    allocation of the Manitoba quantity for 2007.
:::

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Saskatchewan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734870 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Saskatchewan quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer is based
on the order of applying for the export allocation, with each applicant
receiving the volume applied for until the Saskatchewan quantity is
fully allocated.

have come into force on October 12, 2006.
