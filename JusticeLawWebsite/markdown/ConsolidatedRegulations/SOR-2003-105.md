## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699479 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Marine
    Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allied petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allied petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single
    hydrocarbon or a mixture of hydrocarbons other than a petroleum
    product. It includes alcohols, glycols, thinners, solvents, organic
    chemicals and inks. ([produit pétrolier apparenté]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization given
    under Part 3. ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area designated by
    sign or in writing by the Manager in respect of an activity set out
    in column 1 of the schedule. ([endroit désigné]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fee referred to in
    subsection 92(1) or (2) of the Act. ([droit]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hot work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work that uses flame or
    that may produce a source of ignition, such as heating, cutting or
    welding. ([travail à chaud]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The St. Lawrence Seaway
    Management Corporation. ([gestionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petroleum product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single hydrocarbon
    or a mixture of at least 70% hydrocarbons, refined from crude oil,
    with or without additives, that is used or could be used as a fuel,
    lubricant or power transmitter. It includes gasoline, diesel fuel,
    aviation fuel, kerosene, naphtha, lubricating oil, fuel oil and
    engine oil, including used oil; it excludes propane, paint and
    solvents. ([produit pétrolier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Seaway property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Seaway property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land and other
    property managed, operated or used by the Manager in connection with
    the Seaway. ([biens de la voie maritime]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699490 .Part}

### [Where Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699491 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
the Seaway and on Seaway property.

### [Where Regulations Do Not Apply --- [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699493 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of that aspect of a work, within the meaning of the
[Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22), that involves the
issuance of an approval or an opinion under Part I of that Act to a
person other than the Manager or a person acting on the Manager's
behalf.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Safety and Order in the Seaway and on Seaway Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699497 .Part}

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699498 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise authorized
under these Regulations, no person shall, by act or omission, do
anything or permit anything to be done in the Seaway or on Seaway
property that has or is likely to have any of the following results:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to jeopardize the safety or health of persons in the
  Seaway or on Seaway property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with navigation in the Seaway;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to obstruct or adversely effect any part of the
  Seaway or Seaway property;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with an activity that is authorized
  under section 26 or 30 in the Seaway or on Seaway property;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to divert the flow of a river or stream, cause or
  affect currents, cause silting or the accumulation of material or
  otherwise reduce the depth of the waters of the Seaway;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to cause a nuisance;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} to cause damage to ships or other property;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} to adversely affect sediment, soil, air or water
  quality; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} to adversely affect Seaway operations or Seaway
  property.

### [Removal --- Property or Waters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699560 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise authorized under these Regulations, every person who drops,
  deposits, discharges or spills, in the Seaway or on Seaway property,
  refuse or a substance that pollutes, an object that interferes with
  navigation or any cargo or ship's gear shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately make every technically and economically
    feasible effort to remove it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the Manager of the incident without delay
    and provide a description of what was dropped, deposited, discharged
    or spilled and its approximate location and the efforts made by the
    person, if any, to remove it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, submit to the Manager a
    detailed written report of the incident, including the information
    referred to in paragraph (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not immediately remove the refuse,
  substance, object, cargo or ship's gear, the Manager may have it
  removed and, if the refuse, substance, object, cargo or ship's gear
  interferes with navigation, the Manager may have it removed at the
  person's expense.

### [Fire Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699569 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every person in the
Seaway or on Seaway property shall follow the fire protection and
prevention measures reasonably necessary for the safety of persons and
property, taking into account the activities and goods in the Seaway and
on Seaway property.

### [Dangerous Situations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699571 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Every person who by act
or omission causes a dangerous situation in the Seaway or on Seaway
property shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} take one or both of the following precautions:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} post the notices, deploy the lights and erect the
    fences, barricades or other devices that are necessary to prevent
    accidents and to protect persons and property, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} station a person at the site of the dangerous
    situation to warn persons of the danger;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} take appropriate measures to prevent injury to
  persons or damage to property; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the Manager without delay as to the nature of
  the dangerous situation and the precautions that have been taken and
  their location.

- SOR/2014-102, s. 10(F)

### [Emergencies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699580 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of these Regulations, a person may, in the Seaway or on Seaway
property, conduct an activity set out in column 1 of the schedule
without having a contract, lease or licence or an authorization given by
the Manager or complying with the conditions of an authorization for the
duration of an emergency situation if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activity is required as a result of an emergency
  situation that jeopardizes the safety of persons or threatens to cause
  damage to property or the environment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} without delay, the person conducting the activity
  submits a written report to the Manager describing the activity and
  explaining why the situation was regarded as an emergency.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If a situation causes or
is likely to cause death, bodily injury or any other emergency situation
in the Seaway or on Seaway property or if the situation causes or is
likely to cause damage to property or the environment, every person
directly involved in the situation and, in the case of an activity
conducted under a contract, lease, licence or authorization, the person
authorized to conduct the activity shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, notify the Manager that there is an
  emergency situation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a detailed written report of the emergency
  situation to the Manager as soon as possible after it begins; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Manager, submit with the report
  a copy of each report of the emergency situation that the person makes
  to municipal, provincial and federal authorities.

### [Accidents and Incidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699590 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A person who does
anything in the Seaway or on Seaway property that results in an incident
involving material loss or damage or an explosion, fire, accident,
grounding, stranding or incident of pollution shall submit a detailed
written report of the incident to the Manager without delay.

### [Precautionary Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699592 .Subheading}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If, in the Seaway or on
Seaway property, a person conducts an activity that is likely to have
any of the results prohibited under section 5 and for which no
authorization under these Regulations is required, the Manager may
instruct the person to cease the activity or to take the precautions
necessary to mitigate or prevent the result.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Activities of the Manager]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699596 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699597 .Subheading}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If the Manager is the
proponent of an activity that is set out in column 1 of the schedule and
that is likely to have any of the results prohibited under section 5, it
shall take appropriate measures designed to mitigate or prevent the
result, if technically and economically feasible, taking into account

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety of persons and property in the Seaway and
  on Seaway property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the environmental protection of the Seaway and Seaway
  property; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the management of the marine infrastructure of the
  Seaway and services in connection with the operation of the Seaway in
  a commercial manner.

### [Works within the Meaning of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22)]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699602 .Subheading}

#### [Authority to Build]{.HTitleText3} {#h-699603 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any agreement
between the Minister and the Manager entered into under subsection 80(5)
of the Act, any agreement between Canada and the United States in
respect of the Seaway and section 25, the Manager or a person acting on
behalf of the Manager may build, place, rebuild, repair or alter in, on,
over, under, through or across the navigable waters of the Seaway a work
within the meaning of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
Act](/eng/acts/N-22) that may interfere with navigation.

#### [Assessments and Reports]{.HTitleText3} {#h-699607 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  carrying out an activity referred to in section 24, the Manager shall
  conduct an assessment of the impact of the work on navigation in the
  Seaway.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Manager shall ensure that a written report that
  summarizes the assessment is prepared before the activity in respect
  of the work is begun.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Manager shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the assessment indicates that the work would be
    likely to have an adverse effect on safety in respect of navigation
    in the Seaway, take appropriate measures designed to mitigate the
    effect, if technically and economically feasible; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the assessment indicates that the work would be
    likely to interfere with any other aspect of navigation, take
    measures to ensure that the work is consistent with the objective
    declared in section 4 of the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7).

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Authorizations and Instructions for Activities in the Seaway or on Seaway Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699616 .Part}

### [Activities under Contracts, Leases and Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699617 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A person may, in the
Seaway or on Seaway property, conduct an activity set out in column 1 of
the schedule if authorized to do so in writing expressly or by necessary
implication under a contract or lease entered into with, or a licence
granted by, the Manager.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If, by entering into a
contract or lease or granting a licence, the Manager authorizes an
activity set out in column 1 of the schedule that has or is likely to
have any of the results prohibited under section 5, the Manager shall
stipulate as a condition of the contract, lease or licence that the
person with whom the contract or lease is made or to whom the licence is
granted shall take measures designed to mitigate or prevent the result,
if technically and economically feasible.

**\[28 and 29 reserved\]**

### [Authorizations to Persons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699621 .Subheading}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Manager may give a written authorization under this section to a
  person to conduct, in the Seaway or on Seaway property, an activity
  set out in column 1 of the schedule if an "X" is set out in column 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for an authorization, along
  with payment of the applicable fee, if any, and the information
  required under subsection 31(2), the Manager shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give its authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuse to give its authorization if the results of
    the conduct of the activity are uncertain, or if the conduct of the
    activity is likely to have any of the results prohibited under
    section 5 and the results cannot be mitigated or prevented;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the conduct of the activity is likely to have
    any of the results prohibited under section 5, give its
    authorization subject to conditions that are designed to mitigate or
    prevent the results; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} refuse to give its authorization if the person's
    insurance coverage is inadequate for the conduct of the activity.

    policy is amended or cancelled; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if any of the results prohibited under section 5

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The Manager may cancel an

an authorization is subject if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of the activity has a result that is
  prohibited under section 5 or, because of a change in circumstances,
  it becomes likely to have such a result or the result of it becomes
  unclear;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance coverage, performance security or
  damage security obtained by the person in respect of the conduct of
  the activity becomes inadequate for the activity or is cancelled;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorization was based on incorrect or
  misleading information; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the authorization is given or any
  person covered by the authorization does not comply with a condition
  of the authorization.

<!-- -->

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authorization given under section 30 is cancelled, the Manager shall
  give notice of the cancellation to the person to whom the
  authorization was given.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cancellation is effective at the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the end of the fifth day after the notice of
    cancellation is sent by registered mail to the address provided in
    the application for the authorization,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two hours after a facsimile or electronic
    transmission of the notice of cancellation is sent to the address
    provided in the application for the authorization, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately on service of the notice of
    cancellation at the address provided in the application for the
    authorization.

**[[33.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33.1 .sectionLabel}** The Manager shall
give a temporary written or verbal authorization under this section to a
person to not obey an instruction on a sign or device installed under
the authority of the Manager if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is carrying out an activity that is
  necessary for the operation of the Seaway or Seaway property; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} obeying the instruction is likely to have any of the
  results prohibited under section 5.

### [Instructions to Cease, Remove, Return and Restore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-699659 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Manager may instruct a person to take any of the actions prescribed
  under subsection (2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person conducts an activity that is prohibited
    under section 6;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person conducts an activity for which an
    authorization is required under section 30 without first obtaining
    the authorization or without being covered by one;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person or any person covered by an
    authorization fails to comply with a condition of the authorization;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the authorization to conduct the activity is
    cancelled under section 32.

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-102, s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The actions are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to cease the activity or comply with the conditions
    for conducting the activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is instructed to cease the activity,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to remove anything brought into the Seaway or
      onto Seaway property in connection with the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to return to the Seaway or Seaway property
      anything that was removed from it in connection with the activity,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to restore the property affected by the
      activity to its former state.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not immediately remove a thing
  referred to in subparagraph (2)(b)(i), the Manager may have it removed
  or stored and, if the thing interferes with navigation, the Manager
  may have it removed or stored at the person's expense.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-102, s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

- SOR/2014-102, s. 17
