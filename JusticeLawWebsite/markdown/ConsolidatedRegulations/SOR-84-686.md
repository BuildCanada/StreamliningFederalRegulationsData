## N.H.A. Designated Areas Order {#n.h.a.-designated-areas-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-686
:::

[NATIONAL HOUSING ACT](/eng/acts/N-11)

Registration 1984-08-23

Order Designating Certain Areas of the Provinces of Newfoundland, Nova
Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba,
Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia and Certain Areas of the
Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories as Designated Areas

P.C. 1984-2917 1984-08-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works, pursuant to section 60 and to the
definition of [[designated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA} in section 2 of the
[National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order designating certain areas of the Provinces of
Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Quebec,
Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan, Alberta and British Columbia and
certain areas of the Yukon Territory and the Northwest Territories as
designated areas for the purposes of subsections 34.121(2), 34.161(2),
34.17(1) and 55(7) of the said Act.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1983-84, c. 26
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884560 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [N.H.A. Designated Areas Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-686).

## [Designation of Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884562 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** All that portion of the
Province of Newfoundland outside the boundaries of an incorporated city,
metropolitan area, town or village that has a population in excess of
5,000, based on the most recent decennial census taken pursuant to the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), is a designated area for the purposes
of subsections 34.121(2), 34.161(2), 34.17(1) and 55(7) of the [National
Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11).
