## Government Employees Compensation Place of Employment Regulations {#government-employees-compensation-place-of-employment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-791
:::

[GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES COMPENSATION ACT](/eng/acts/G-5)

Registration 1986-07-24

Regulations for Determining the Place Where a Government Employee is
Usually Employed

P.C. 1986-1666 1986-07-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 10 of the [Government
Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5), is pleased hereby to approve
the annexed [Regulations for determining the place where a government
employee is usually employed](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-791), made by the
Minister of Labour.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898009 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Government Employees Compensation Place of Employment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-791).

## [Place of Employment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898011 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5), the place where
an employee is usually employed is the place where the employee is
appointed or engaged to work.
