## [General Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511916 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized activities and transactions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any person in Canada or
Canadian outside Canada may, under this General Permit, carry out any of
the following activities and transactions that are otherwise prohibited
under the [Special Economic Measures (Syria)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-114) if the activities and
transactions are aimed at the democratization and stabilization of Syria
or the delivery of humanitarian assistance to or within Syria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service to or for
  the benefit of an entity listed in the schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire financial or other related
  services to, from or for the benefit of or on the direction or order
  of Syria or any person in Syria.

## [Validity Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511921 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}360 days

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This General Permit ceases
to have effect on the 360th day after the day on which it comes into
force.
