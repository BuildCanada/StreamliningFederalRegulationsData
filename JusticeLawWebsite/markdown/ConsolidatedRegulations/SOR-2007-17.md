## Conditions for Exempted Persons Regulations {#conditions-for-exempted-persons-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-17
:::

[SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)

Registration 2007-02-01

Conditions for Exempted Persons Regulations

P.C. 2007-112 2007-02-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Minister for International
Trade, pursuant to subsection 16(1), paragraph 100(1)(b) and section 107
of the [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_609223-E_hq_1205){#fn_609223-E_hq_1205-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Conditions for Exempted Persons
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-17).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Softwood Lumber Products
    Export Charge Act, 2006. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reference price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 12(5) or (6) of the Act and is rounded in accordance with
    subsection 12(7) of the Act. ([prix de référence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of persons whose names are set out in the schedule to the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export limit

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person's export limit of softwood lumber products for a calendar year
  is the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#734893 .Subsection}
  BMP × M

  where

  [BMP]{.dfn}
  :   is the person's base average monthly production; and

  [M]{.dfn}
  :   is number of months in that year that the reference price was not
      more than US\$355 per thousand board feet.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base average monthly production

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person's base average monthly production is the
  total production of softwood lumber products for the following years
  as certified by the province in which the person is located, divided
  by 24:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of  9157--9516 Québec inc. (Scierie
    Nord-Sud inc.), 2002 and 2003; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, 2004 and 2005.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products from certain mills

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total production of softwood lumber products in
  respect of the following persons is based on the products produced
  from the following mills:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of J.D. Irving Ltd., its mill at
    Pohénégamook in Quebec; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Matériaux Blanchet inc., its mill at
    Saint-Pamphile in Quebec.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actual exports

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person's actual exports
of softwood lumber products are determined at the end of the calendar
year by adding the person's exports of those products during the months
when the reference price was not more than US\$355 per thousand board
feet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction of export limit

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If, in a calendar year, a
person's actual exports of softwood lumber products exceed their export
limit by more than 0.5%, the person's export limit for the following
calendar year is reduced by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the excess, the first time an excess
  occurs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} twice the amount of the excess, the second time an
  excess occurs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} three times the amount of the excess, the third time
  an excess occurs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of exemption

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 16(1) of the Act, a person is exempt from the charge referred
to in section 10 of the Act on the condition that the person's actual
annual exports of softwood lumber products do not exceed the person's
annual export limit by more than 0.5% in any four calendar years. The
exemption ends on December 31 of the year that the fourth excess occurs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 12, 2006.
