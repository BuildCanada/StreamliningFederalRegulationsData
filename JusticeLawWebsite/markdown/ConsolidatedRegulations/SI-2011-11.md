## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessment-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-11
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2011-02-16

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Act

P.C. 2011-43 2011-02-03
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, hereby acknowledges receipt, on the
making of this Order, of the assessment done pursuant to
subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBAA} by the Committee on the
Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada (COSEWIC) with respect to the
species set out in the annexed schedule.

  2002, c. 29
