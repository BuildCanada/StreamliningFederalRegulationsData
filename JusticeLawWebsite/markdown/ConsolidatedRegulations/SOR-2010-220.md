## Postage Meter Regulations, 2010 {#postage-meter-regulations-2010 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-220

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 2010-10-21

Postage Meter Regulations, 2010

P.C. 2010-1267 2010-10-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, in accordance with subsection 20(1) of the [Canada Post
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612543-E_hq_8546){#fn_612543-E_hq_8546-ID0EFBBA},
a copy of the proposed [Postage Meter Regulations,
2010](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-220), substantially in the form set out
in the annexed regulations, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on May 8, 2010 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect to the
proposed regulations;

recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to

the annexed [Postage Meter Regulations,
2010](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-220), made by the Canada Post
Corporation on August 4^th^, 2010.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765347 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[ascending register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ascending register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the device in a
    postage meter that records the total accumulated value of postage
    that has been printed by the meter. ([totalisateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[descending register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[descending register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the device in a
    postage meter that records the value of the remaining postage.
    ([soustracteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an item of mailable matter.
    ([version anglaise seulement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lessee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is party to a
    lease with a supplier authorizing the use of a postage meter.
    ([locataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[postage meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[postage meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a machine that contains
    an ascending and a descending register, has the capacity to connect
    remotely to a supplier's system for setting the descending register
    and exchanging information, is used to print a postage meter
    impression and may be used to print other words and symbols as well.
    ([machine à affranchir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[postage meter impression]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[postage meter impression]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an impression
    that is printed by a postage meter and shows a barcode or data
    matrix code, an amount of postage, the serial number of the meter
    and the supplier's identifying mark. ([empreinte de machine à
    affranchir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is party to an
    agreement with the Corporation for the installation of postage
    meters in Canada. ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Installation of Postage Meters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765356 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** No person shall install a
model of postage meter for use in Canada unless they

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are a supplier;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have obtained written approval from the Corporation
  to install that particular model of meter; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have entered into a lease for the use of the meter
  with the person in control of the premises where it is to be
  installed.

## [Setting w Descending Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765361 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person, other than a
supplier, shall set a descending register.

## [Using a Postage Meter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765363 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a
postage meter unless they are a supplier or lessee of the meter or use
it under the authority of a supplier or lessee.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person who uses a postage
meter to print a postage meter impression on an item shall print the
impression in fluorescent red ink

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly on the mailing cover of the item;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a postage meter tape affixed to the item; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a label approved by the Corporation that is
  affixed or attached to the item.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person may print a
postage meter impression that bears the words "RETURN POSTAGE ---
PREPAID/PORT DE RETOUR PAYÉ" on an item provided that it is a
pre-addressed reply card, label or envelope that is to be mailed by
someone else.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person may use a postage
meter to print words or symbols on an item, including advertisements,
slogans or a return address, provided that the words and symbols do not
infringe on the area reserved for the postage meter impression.

## [Posting Items Printed with a Postage Meter Impression]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765371 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall post an item on a date other than the date shown in the
  postage meter impression.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if an item cannot be posted on the date
  shown in the postage meter impression, a person may print a second
  impression bearing another date and a zero dollar postage value and
  may post the item on that other date. The second impression must not
  be placed so as to infringe on the original.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** When an item bears a
postage meter impression with a postal code, no person shall post the
item from a location outside the municipality to which the postal code
is assigned unless the location has been agreed to in writing by the
Corporation.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the amount of postage
shown in the postage meter impression on an item appears to have been
altered, the item shall be dealt with as undeliverable mail in
accordance with the [Undeliverable and Redirected Mail
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1298).

## [Lessee's Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765377 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A lessee who intends to
move the postage meter to a new address shall inform the supplier of the
new address and the date on which the meter will be moved.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a lessee becomes aware
of a malfunction in the meter or suspects that the meter has been
tampered with, they shall immediately inform the supplier, cease to use
the meter and make it available to the supplier for inspection.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A lessee shall
immediately inform the supplier if the meter is lost or stolen.

## [Inspection of Postage Meters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765381 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** After receiving
reasonable notice from the Corporation, a supplier or lessee shall make
any postage meter in its possession available for inspection by the
Corporation or a person designated by the Corporation.

## [Return of Postage Meters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765383 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** When the lease of a
postage meter expires or is terminated, the lessee shall immediately
return the meter to the supplier.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation, or a
person designated by the Corporation, may require the supplier of a
postage meter to immediately repossess the meter if the Corporation has
reasonable grounds to believe that the meter has a malfunction or has
been tampered with.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765386 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765388 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
