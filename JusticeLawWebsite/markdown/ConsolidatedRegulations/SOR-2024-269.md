## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501540 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Reduction of Carbon
Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired Generation of Electricity Regulations
do not apply in Saskatchewan.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501543 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of agreement

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order ceases to have
effect on the day on which the agreement between the Minister of the
Environment and the Government of Saskatchewan, entitled "An agreement
on the equivalency of federal and Saskatchewan regulations for the
control of greenhouse gas emissions from electricity producers in
Saskatchewan, 2025" terminates or is terminated in accordance with
subsection 10(8) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501546 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Order Declaring that
the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired Generation of
Electricity Regulations Do Not Apply in Saskatchewan[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-5467_hq_1714398214){#fn_81000-2-5467_hq_1714398214-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.
