## Canadian Egg Anti-dumping Pricing Regulations {#canadian-egg-anti-dumping-pricing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 654
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Regulations Respecting the Anti-Dumping Pricing of Eggs in
Interprovincial Trade
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559639 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Egg Anti-dumping Pricing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._654).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559641 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the egg of a hen;
    ([oeuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hen of any class of the domestic
    chicken belonging to the species [Gallus Domesticus]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}; ([poule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to eggs, means selling
    and offering for sale and buying, pricing, assembling, packing,
    processing, transporting, storing and reselling, whether in whole or
    in processed form. ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559646 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall market eggs
in a province other than the province in which the eggs are produced at
a price that is less than the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the price charged at or about the same time for eggs
  of an equivalent variety, class or grade in the province or other
  geographical area in which the eggs are produced; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reasonable transportation charges therefor to the
  place where such eggs are marketed and that are incurred by the person
  marketing the eggs.
