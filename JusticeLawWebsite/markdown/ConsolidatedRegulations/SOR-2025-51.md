## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515265 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Online News
Act](/eng/acts/O-9.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}News revenue

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The news revenue of an
operator for a calendar year is the gross Canadian revenue that the
operator generates, directly or indirectly, from making news content
available through any digital news intermediary that it operates in that
year, excluding any amount received from another operator to which these
Regulations apply, established on the basis of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual return filed by the operator; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the operator has not filed an annual return for
  the year or the return is inaccurate or incomplete, an estimate by the
  Commission based on the following, where relevant:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information provided by the operator,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the trends of the market in which the digital news
    intermediary is operated,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the previous financial performance of the
    operator, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the business plan of the operator.

## [Annual Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515276 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}April 30

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On or before April 30 of
each year, an operator must file with the Commission a return with
respect to the preceding calendar year that is in the form provided by
the Commission and contains the information required by the Commission,
including information respecting the operator's news revenue with
respect to each digital news intermediary that it operated.

## [Cost Recovery Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515279 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of charge

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The cost
  recovery charge for an operator for a fiscal year is the sum of the
  base assessment amount determined in accordance with subsection (2)
  and the annual adjustment amount for the previous fiscal year
  determined in accordance with subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The base assessment amount is the result of the
  following formula

  ::: {#1515285 .Subsection}
  (A ÷ B) × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the operator's news revenue for the immediately preceding
      calendar year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate news revenue of all operators for that calendar
      year; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated costs incurred by the Commission in the fiscal
      year that are attributable to the Commission exercising its powers
      and carrying out its duties and functions under the Act and that
      will not be recovered under regulations made under subsection
      79(1) of the Act.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual adjustment amount is the result of the
  following formula

  ::: {#1515292 .Subsection}
  (A ÷ B) × (D − E)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the operator's news revenue for the immediately preceding
      calendar year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate news revenue of all operators for that calendar
      year;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the actual costs incurred by the Commission in the fiscal year
      that are attributable to the Commission exercising its powers and
      carrying out its duties and functions under the Act as set out in
      Part III of the Estimates of the Government of Canada and that are
      not recovered under regulations made under subsection 79(1) of the
      Act; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount determined for C in subsection (2) for the fiscal
      year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery charge negative

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the amount of the recovery charge for the fiscal
  year is negative, that amount is not repaid to the operator but is
  instead deducted from the amount of the recovery charge that the
  operator is to pay for the following fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication --- estimated costs

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must, in
each fiscal year, publish a notice in the [Canada
Gazette](https://gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, setting out the estimated
costs to be incurred by the Commission in that fiscal year that are
attributable to the Commission exercising its powers and carrying out
its duties and functions under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment within 30 days

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An operator must pay a cost
recovery charge to the Commission within 30 days after the day on which
the invoice respecting that charge was sent.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515304 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}News revenue for 2024

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An operator must report its
news revenue for 2024 to the Commission, in the form required by the
Commission, within 60 days after the day on which these Regulations come
into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515307 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}April 1, 2025

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 2025, but, if they are registered after that day, they
come into force on the day on which they are registered.
