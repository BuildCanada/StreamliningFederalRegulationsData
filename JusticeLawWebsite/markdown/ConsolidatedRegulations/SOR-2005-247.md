  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
      Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a part of an animal, but
      does not include an animal or a part of an animal that exists
      primarily as a single cell and is without the organization that
      characterizes tissues or organs. ([animal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[anionic polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[anionic polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a polymer that has
      one or more monomer units that are covalently bound and bear a net
      negative charge. ([polymère anionique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[biochemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biochemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance, other than
      a polymer, that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is produced by a micro-organism; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a protein or nucleic acid that is derived
        from a plant or an animal. ([substance
        biochimique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[biopolymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biopolymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a polymer that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is produced by a micro-organism; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a protein or nucleic acid that is derived
        from a plant or an animal. ([biopolymère]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the

      Abstracts Service Division of the American chemical
      Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[cationic polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cationic polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a polymer that has
      one or more monomer units that are covalently bound and bear a net
      positive charge. ([polymère cationique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that is not a
      polymer. ([substance chimique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[consumed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[consumed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a substance, means
      destroyed or completely converted to another
      substance. ([consommée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contained]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contained]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a site-limited
      intermediate substance or an export-only substance, means an
      absolute release limit of 1 kg per day per site to the aquatic
      environment after wastewater
      treatment. ([confinée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[DSL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[DSL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Domestic Substances List

      lang="fr"})

  [[material safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[material safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2015-19, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[micro-organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[micro-organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a microscopic
      organism that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} classified in the Bacteria, the Archaea, the
        Protista, which includes protozoa and algae, or the Fungi, which
        includes yeasts;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a virus, virus-like particle or sub-viral
        particle;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cultured cell of an organism not referred to
        in paragraph (a) or (b), other than a cell used to propagate the
        organism; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any culture other than a pure
        culture. ([micro-organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[monomer unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[monomer unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the reacted form of a
      monomer in a polymer. ([unité monomère]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[NDSL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[NDSL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Non-domestic Substances
      List maintained by the Minister under subsection 66(2) of the Act,
      as amended from time to time. ([liste extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a part of a plant, but does

      single cell and is without the organization that characterizes
      tissues or organs. ([végétaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that consists of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} molecules characterized by the sequence of one
        or more types of monomer units;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} greater than 50% by weight of molecules having
        three or more monomer units that are covalently bound to one or
        more other monomer units or reactants;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} less than 50% by weight of molecules of the
        same molecular weight; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} molecules distributed over a range of molecular
        weights whose differences in molecular weights are primarily
        attributable to differences in the number of monomer
        units. ([polymère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reactant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reactant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a polymer, means a
      substance that is used in the manufacture of the polymer and
      becomes part of its chemical composition, and includes a
      monomer. ([réactif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reactive functional group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reactive functional group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means atoms or

      lang="fr"})

  [[reduced regulatory requirement polymer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to determine the technical viability or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to evaluate the substance prior to its

        et au développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a

      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[site-limited intermediate substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[site-limited intermediate substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a substance that is consumed in a chemical reaction used for the
      manufacture of another substance and that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactured and consumed at the site of
        manufacture;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} manufactured at one site and transported to a
        second site where it is consumed; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported and transported directly to the site
        where it is consumed. ([intermédiaire limitée au
        site]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[test marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[test marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a product,
      means the exploration of its market capability in a competitive
      situation where the creation or improvement of the product is not
      the primary objective. ([test de marché]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Purpose and Scope]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717813 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations set out the information that a person must provide to the
  Minister of the Environment under subsection 81(1) of the Act before
  manufacturing or importing a chemical or polymer that is not on the
  DSL. The information is required so that the Minister may determine
  whether the chemical or polymer is toxic or capable of becoming toxic
  within the meaning of section 64 of the Act. These Regulations set out
  the periods within which the Minister of the Environment and the
  Minister of Health must assess the information received and the
  conditions under which the Minister of the Environment must add a
  chemical or polymer to the DSL under section 87 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Regulations are divided into 4 Parts: Parts 1 and
  2 set out the information requirements; Part 3, administrative
  matters; Part 4, the obligations of the Ministers. Schedules 1 to 6
  and 9 to 11 set out information to be provided, Schedule 7, the types
  of polymers, Schedule 8, reactants and Schedule 12, flowcharts giving
  an overview of all the information requirements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoiding regulatory duplication

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, these Regulations do not apply in respect of a
  substance that is manufactured or imported for a use that is regulated
  under any other Act or regulations listed in Schedule 2 to the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations also do not apply in respect of a
  substance that is loaded on a carrier outside Canada and moved through
  Canada to a location outside Canada, whether or not there is a change
  of carrier during transit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum exempt quantities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 81(6)(e) of the Act, a substance is exempt from the
application of subsection 81(1) of the Act if it is manufactured or
imported in a quantity that does not exceed the quantity that first
triggers a requirement to provide information under these Regulations.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Required Information for Research and Development, Contained Site-limited Intermediate or Contained Export-only Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717826 .Part}

### [Chemicals and Biochemicals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717827 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity greater than 1 000 kg

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person that manufactures or imports a chemical referred to in one of
  the following paragraphs must provide to the Minister the information
  specified in Schedule 1 at least 30 days before the day on which the
  quantity of the chemical exceeds 1 000 kg in a calendar year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a chemical that is a research and development
    substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a chemical that is a contained site-limited
    intermediate substance; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a chemical that is a contained export-only
    substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biochemical research and development
  substance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the chemical is a biochemical research and
  development substance, the person must provide, with the Schedule 1
  information, the information specified in items 1 and 2 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biochemical site-limited intermediate
  or export-only substance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the chemical is a biochemical contained
  site-limited intermediate substance that is not manufactured and
  consumed at the site of manufacture or is a biochemical contained
  export-only substance, the person must provide, with the Schedule 1
  information, the information specified in items 1 to 4 of Schedule 2
  and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the biochemical is a nucleic acid, the
    information specified in items 5 and 6 of Schedule 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the biochemical possesses enzymatic capability,
    the information specified in items 7 to 13 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biochemical site-limited intermediate
  substance manufactured and consumed at the site of manufacture

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the chemical is a biochemical contained
  site-limited intermediate substance that is manufactured and consumed
  at the site of manufacture, the person must provide, with the Schedule
  1 information, the information specified in items 1, 2 and 4 of
  Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity greater than 10 000 kg

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person must notify the Minister at least 30 days
  before the day on which the quantity of the chemical manufactured or
  imported exceeds 10 000 kg in any calendar year and, at that time,
  update the information previously provided under this section or
  indicate that there has been no change in the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity greater than 10 000 kg

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is a research and development

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is a contained site-limited

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is a contained export-only

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biopolymer research and development

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the polymer is a biopolymer research and

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Biopolymer site-limited intermediate or

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the polymer is a biopolymer contained site-limited

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Required Information for Chemicals and Polymers other than Research and Development, Contained Site-limited Intermediate and Contained Export-only Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717857 .Part}

### [Chemicals and Biochemicals on the NDSL]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717858 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture or import: quantities greater
than 1 000 kg and 10 000 kg

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person that manufactures or imports a chemical that is on the NDSL ---
  other than a chemical referred to in section 5 --- must provide to the
  Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 30 days before the day on which the
    quantity of the chemical exceeds 1 000 kg in a calendar year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information specified in Schedule 4, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the chemical is a biochemical, the
      information specified in items 1 to 3 of Schedule 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 60 days before the day on which the
    quantity of the chemical exceeds 10 000 kg in a calendar year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information specified in Schedule 5, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the chemical is a biochemical, the
      information specified in items 1 to 4 of Schedule 2 and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the biochemical is a nucleic acid, the
        information specified in items 5 and 6 of Schedule 2, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the biochemical possesses enzymatic
        capability, the information specified in items 7 to 13 of
        Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity greater than 50 000 kg and
  exceeding 3 kg released per day

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the quantity of the chemical exceeds 50 000 kg in
  a calendar year --- and the chemical is released to the aquatic
  environment in a quantity exceeding 3 kg per day, per site, averaged
  monthly and after wastewater treatment --- the person must, in
  addition to the information referred to in subsection (1), provide to
  the Minister the following information in respect of the chemical, at
  least 75 days before the day on which the quantity exceeds 50 000 kg:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for chemicals having a water solubility of greater
    than or equal to 200 µg/L,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} adsorption-desorption screening test data, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the hydrolysis rate as a function of pH and, if
      known, an identification of the products of the hydrolysis; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the data from a repeated-dose mammalian toxicity
    test of the chemical of at least 28 days duration, using the most
    significant route of potential human exposure to the chemical,
    namely, oral, dermal or inhalation, plus

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the age, sex, number, species, strain and source
      of the animals tested,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the route by which the chemical is administered
      and the conditions under which the test is conducted, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the dose of the chemical, the vehicle by means
      of which the chemical is administered and its concentration in
      that vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity greater than 50 000 kg and
  significant exposure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the quantity of the chemical exceeds 50 000 kg in
  a calendar year and if the public may be significantly exposed to the
  chemical in a product, the person must, in addition to the information
  referred to in subsection (1), provide to the Minister the following
  information in respect of the chemical, at least 75 days before the
  day on which the quantity exceeds 50 000 kg:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the data from a repeated-dose mammalian toxicity
    test of the chemical of at least 28 days duration, using the most
    significant route of potential human exposure to the chemical,
    namely, oral, dermal or inhalation, plus

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the age, sex, number, species, strain and source
      of the animals tested,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the route by which the chemical is administered
      and the conditions under which the test is conducted, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the dose of the chemical, the vehicle by means
      of which the chemical is administered and its concentration in
      that vehicle; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the data obtained from an [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} test, with and without metabolic activation, for
    chromosomal aberrations in mammalian cells or the data from a
    previously existing [in vivo]{.otherLang lang="la"} mammalian test
    for chromosomal aberrations that, together with data substantiating
    that the tissue investigated was exposed to the chemical or its
    metabolites, permits an assessment of [in vivo]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} clastogenicity.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least 75 days before the day on which the

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the chemical is a biochemical, the

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

### [Reduced Regulatory Requirement Polymers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717907 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A reduced regulatory
requirement polymer is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is not one of the types listed in
  items 1 to 4 of Schedule 7 and that has a number average molecular
  weight greater than 10 000 daltons, with less than 2% of its
  components having molecular weights of less than 500 daltons and less
  than 5% of its components having molecular weights of less than 1 000
  daltons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is not one of the types listed in
  Schedule 7 and that has a number average molecular weight greater than

## [SCHEDULE 7]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 2(2) and paragraphs 9(a) and (b))]{.OriginatingRef}[Types of Polymers]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-718227 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** A cationic polymer or a polymer that is reasonably expected to
  become cationic in a natural aquatic environment, except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a polymer whose cationic group has a combined
    equivalent weight greater than 5 000 daltons; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a polymer that is a solid material, that is not
    soluble or dispersible in water and that will be used only in the
    solid phase, such as polymers that can be used as ion exchange
    beads.

- **2** A polymer that is designed, or can be expected, to substantially
  degrade, decompose or depolymerize, including polymers that could
  substantially degrade, decompose or depolymerize after manufacture and
  use, even though they are not intended to do so. Degradation,
  decomposition and depolymerization refer to the types of changes that
  convert a polymeric substance into simpler, smaller substances,
  through processes including but not limited to oxidation, hydrolysis,
  attack by solvents, heat, light and microbial action.

- **3** A polymer that has, as an integral part of its composition, only
  one or none of the following atomic elements: carbon, hydrogen,
  nitrogen, oxygen, silicon and sulphur.

- **4** A polymer that has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any atomic elements other than carbon, hydrogen,
    nitrogen, oxygen, silicon, sulphur, fluorine, chlorine, bromine or
    iodine covalently bound to carbon;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any monoatomic counterions other than chlorine ion,
    bromine ion, iodine ion, sodium ion, divalent magnesium, trivalent
    aluminium, potassium ion or divalent calcium; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 0.2% or more by weight of any atomic element or
    combination of the following atomic elements: lithium, boron,
    phosphorus, titanium, manganese, iron, nickel, copper, zinc, tin or
    zirconium.

- **5** A polymer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that has reactive functional groups other than
    carboxylic acid groups, aliphatic hydroxyl groups, unconjugated
    olefinic groups that are considered "ordinary"[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotestar_e){#footnotestar_e-ID0EBACAAA},
    butenedioic acid groups, blocked isocyanates including
    ketoxime-blocked isocyanates, thiols, unconjugated nitrile groups,
    halogens excluding reactive halogen groups such as benzylic or
    allylic halides, and conjugated olefinic groups present in naturally
    occurring fats, oils and carboxylic acids, in combined equivalent
    weights of less than 5 000 daltons; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the only reactive functional groups
    present are part of acid halides, acid anhydrides, aldehydes,
    hemiacetals, methylol-amides, methylol-amines, methylol-ureas,
    alkoxysilanes with alkoxy greater than C2-alkoxysilanes, allyl
    ethers, conjugated olefins, cyanates, epoxides, imines,
    unsubstituted positions ortho or para to phenolic hydroxyl, in
    combined equivalent weights of less than 1 000 daltons.

    group, such as a vinyl ether, or by other activating influences, for

## [SCHEDULE 9]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 2(2), paragraphs 10(a) and 18(2)(b), subparagraph 18(2)(d)(i) and paragraph 18(2)(e))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information Respecting Reduced Regulatory Requirement Polymers and Other Polymers and Biopolymers (1 000 kg)]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-718250 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The type of polymer:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reduced regulatory requirement polymer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a polymer on the NDSL;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a polymer with all of its reactants on the DSL or
    the NDSL; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a polymer with one or more reactants not on either
    the DSL or NDSL.

- **2** The new substances pre-notification consultation number if it
  has been assigned and if known.

- **3** The chemical name of the polymer, established in accordance with
  the chemical nomenclature rules of the International Union of Pure and
  Applied Chemistry or the Chemical Abstracts Service.

- **4** The trade names of the polymer and the synonyms of its chemical
  name, if known.

- **5** The CAS registry number of the polymer, if such a number can be
  assigned.

- **6** The molecular formula of the polymer.

- **7** The structural formula of the polymer, if possible, or else a
  partial structural formula.

- **8** The reaction scheme if the polymer is a reduced regulatory
  requirement polymer, unless it is a polymer referred in paragraph 9(c)
  of these Regulations.

- **9** The following physical and chemical data in respect of the
  polymer:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its number average molecular weight (Mn); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the maximum concentrations, expressed as a
    percentage, of all residual constituents having molecular weights of
    less than 500 daltons and of all residual constituents having
    molecular weights of less than 1 000 daltons.

- **10** The known impurities present and their concentration by weight.

- **11** The composition of the polymer including constituents --- such
  as monomers and other reactants, additives, stabilizers and solvents
  --- which constituents are present when the polymer is tested, and
  their concentration by weight.

- **12** A safety data sheet in respect of the polymer, if available.

- **13** The following exposure information respecting the polymer:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated annual quantity to be manufactured,
    if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated annual quantity to be imported, if
    applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated uses within Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the polymer is not a reduced regulatory
    requirement polymer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated concentration of the polymer in
      products and, if known, in end-use products,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated degree of direct human exposure
      to the polymer, including concentration, duration, frequency and
      circumstances of exposure and factors that may limit direct human
      exposure,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whether the polymer is anticipated to be used
      in products intended for use by or for children, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if known, the three sites in Canada where the
      greatest quantity of the polymer, manufactured or imported by the
      person, is anticipated to be used or processed and the estimated
      quantity by site.

- **14** A summary of all other information and test data in respect of
  the polymer that are in the possession of the manufacturer or importer
  and that permit the identification of hazards to the environment and
  human health and the degree of environmental and public exposure to
  the polymer.

- **15** The identification of the other government agencies, either
  outside or within Canada, that the person has notified of the
  manufacture or importation of the polymer and, if known, the agency's
  file number, the outcome of the assessment and the risk management
  actions imposed by those agencies.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its octanol-water partition coefficient; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if water extractability is determined to be greater
    than 2%, its hydrolysis rate as a function of pH and, if known, an
    identification of the products of the hydrolysis.

  than or equal to 2%, an acute toxicity test of the polymer for the
  most sensitive species: fish, daphnia or algae or, if the sensitivity
  of these three species is unknown, an acute algae toxicity test. ^\*^

  and the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the age, sex, number, species, strain and source of
    the animals tested;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the route by which the polymer is administered and
    the conditions under which the test is conducted; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dose of the polymer, the vehicle by means of
    which the polymer is administered and the concentration of the
    polymer in the vehicle. ^\*^

- **5** The following exposure information respecting the polymer:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the expected modes for its
    transportation and storage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the size and type of container
    used for its transportation and storage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its anticipated releases into municipal wastewater
    systems;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods recommended for its
    destruction or disposal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its historical and other likely uses;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any factors that may limit environmental exposure;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether it is released to the aquatic environment
    in a quantity exceeding 3 kg per day, per site, averaged monthly and
    after wastewater treatment and, if the release is less than or equal
    to 3 kg per day, per site, the data substantiating the quantity
    released; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether the public is anticipated to be
    significantly exposed to the polymer in a product taking into
    account factors including the concentration of the polymer,
    duration, frequency and circumstances of exposure and factors that
    may limit direct human exposure and, if not, information
    substantiating that the public is not anticipated to be
    significantly exposed.

- **6** A summary of all other information and test data in respect of
  the polymer that are in the possession of the manufacturer or importer
  or to which they may reasonably be expected to have access and that
  permit the identification of hazards to the environment and human
  health and the degree of environmental and public exposure to the
  polymer.

  Note: The asterisks (\*) appearing at the end of certain provisions
  indicate that laboratory practices to be followed in developing data
  for the test referred to in that provision must comply with those
  practices set out in the "Principles of Good Laboratory Practice". See
  subsection 15(2) of the [New Substances Notification Regulations
  (Chemicals and Polymers)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-247).

## [SCHEDULE 12]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 2(2))]{.OriginatingRef}[Overview of Information Requirements]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-718357 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The information required under the [New Substances Notification
  Regulations (Chemicals and Polymers)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-247)
  is divided into three flowcharts according to the type of substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} research and development, contained site-limited
    intermediate and contained export-only substances --- Flowchart 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} chemicals and biochemicals other than research and
    development, contained site-limited intermediate and contained
    export-only substances --- Flowchart 2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} polymers and biopolymers other than research and
    development, contained site-limited intermediate and contained
    export-only substances --- Flowchart 3.

- **2** Choose the appropriate flowchart according to the type of
  substance. Each flowchart identifies the information to be provided
  and the quantity that triggers the regulatory obligation to provide
  it.

- **3** References in the flowcharts are to provisions of the
  Regulations, in italics, and to schedules to the Regulations. Note
  that certain words and expressions used in the flowcharts are defined
  in section 1 of the Regulations.

- **4** The shapes used in the flowcharts distinguish their contents as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ovals identify the type of substance referred
    to in the flowchart, as more particularly described in the
    flowchart's title;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the diamonds identify the timeline and quantity
    trigger; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rectangles identify the required information.

- **5** Shapes outlined with a broken line signal that information is
  required only in certain circumstances.

- **6** Additional information is set out in footnotes to each
  flowchart.

- **7** The Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health must
  assess the information within the same number of days as are afforded
  to the manufacturer or importer for provision of that information ---
  see section 16 of the Regulations. For example, if a manufacturer or
  importer is required to provide information at least 30 days before
  the day on which a certain quantity is exceeded, then the Ministers
  must assess that information within 30 days after receiving it.
