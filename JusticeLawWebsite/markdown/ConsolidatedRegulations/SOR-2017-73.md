TO ALL TO WHOM these presents shall come or whom the same may in any way
concern,

GREETING:

## [A Proclamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-843462 .Part}

Whereas section 4 of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2), chapter
V-2 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985, provides that the Governor
in Council may, by proclamation, designate any country as a designated
state for the purposes of that Act, declare the extent to which that Act
is applicable in respect of any designated state and designate civilian
personnel as a civilian component of a visiting force;

Now Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2017-260 of March 24, 2017, do
by this Our Proclamation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate the Republic of Montenegro as a designated
  state for the purposes of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare that the [Visiting Forces
  Act](/eng/acts/V-2), with the exception of Part VI, is applicable in
  respect of the Republic of Montenegro; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designate, as a civilian component of a visiting
  force from the Republic of Montenegro, the civilian personnel in the
  employ of that force in Canada who are not stateless persons,
  nationals of any state that is not a designated state or citizens or
  residents of Canada.

Of all which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these presents may
concern are required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

IN TESTIMONY WHEREOF, We have caused this Our Proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be affixed to it.

WITNESS:

Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved David Johnston, Chancellor and
Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada, Chancellor and Commander of
Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of
Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of
Canada.

AT OUR GOVERNMENT HOUSE, in Our City of Ottawa, this eleventh day of
April in the year of Our Lord two thousand and seventeen and in the
sixty-sixth year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushLeft
John Knubley
:::

::: FlushLeft
Deputy Registrar General of Canada
