## [Qualifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025479 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, pursuant
to section 44 of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), designate a person as an analyst if the person
has the following qualifications:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a degree in a science related to the work to be
  carried out, awarded by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian university, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a foreign university recognized by a Canadian
    university or professional association; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a combination, sufficient to perform the duties of
  the position, of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} practical experience in the area of responsibility,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} formal education in a science related to the work
    to be carried out or other training related to that work.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025487 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
