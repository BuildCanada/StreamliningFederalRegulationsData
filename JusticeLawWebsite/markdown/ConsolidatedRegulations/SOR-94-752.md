## Alberta Equivalency Order {#alberta-equivalency-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-752
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1994-12-06

Order Declaring that the Provisions of Certain Regulations Made under
Subsection 34(1) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act do not
Apply in the Province of Alberta

P.C. 1994-2007  1994-12-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of the Environment and the government of the
Province of Alberta have agreed in writing dated June 1, 1994, that
there are in force by or under the laws of the Province of Alberta

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are equivalent to the provisions of
  certain regulations made under subsection 34(1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6286){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6286-ID0EECBDA}
  of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287-ID0EBCBDA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6286-ID0EECBDA){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6286}S.C.
    1989, c. 9, s. 2
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287-ID0EBCBDA){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287}R.S., c.
    16 (4th Supp.)
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are similar to sections 108 to 110 of
  that Act for the investigation of alleged offences under Alberta's
  environmental legislation;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 34(7) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287-ID0EDACA},
the Minister of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on July 23, 1994, a copy of
the said agreement;

And Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of that Act, the Minister of
the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on July 23, 1994, a copy of
the proposed [Order declaring that the provisions of certain regulations
made under subsection 34(1) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act
do not apply in the Province of Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-752),
substantially in the form annexed hereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 34(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6289){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6289-ID0EGBAA}
of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287){#fn_SOR-94-752_e_hq_6287-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order declaring that the
provisions of certain regulations made under subsection 34(1) of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act do not apply in the Province of
Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-752), effective on the date of
registration thereof by the Clerk of the Privy Council.

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966380 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Equivalency Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-752).

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966382 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the
following regulations that are made under subsection 34(1) of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act do not apply in the Province of
Alberta:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Vinyl Chloride Release Regulations, SOR/92-631;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [Secondary Lead Smelter Release
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-155), SOR/91-155;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [Pulp and Paper Mill Effluent Chlorinated Dioxins and
  Furans Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-267), SOR/92-267; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsections 4(1), 6(2), paragraph 6(3)(b), sections 7
  and 9 of the [Pulp and Paper Mill Defoamer and Wood Chip
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-268), SOR/92-268.
