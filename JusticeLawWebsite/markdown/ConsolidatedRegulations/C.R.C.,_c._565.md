## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558013 .Part}

cited as the [Brewery Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._565).

## [Production Day]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558030 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A brewer
  shall establish a production day in respect of the brewer\'s brewery
  and shall, in writing, notify the appropriate superior officer of the
  time of commencement and the duration of the production day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A brewer may change the time of commencement and the
  duration of the production day by notifying the appropriate superior
  officer of the change, in writing, before the change is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-45, s. 2
- SOR/91-128, s. 2
- SOR/96-132, s. 1
:::

## [Refunds on Destroyed Beer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558036 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A refund
  of excise duty paid on beer that has been destroyed shall be made to
  the brewer, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in advance of the destruction of the beer the
    brewer informs the Minister in writing of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed site and method of destruction, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the method to be used for determining the
      quantity of the beer destroyed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} following the destruction of the beer the brewer
    determines the quantity of the beer destroyed in accordance with the
    brewer\'s written information to the Minister referred to in
    subparagraph (a)(ii); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the brewer makes an application for a refund to the
    Minister setting out the quantity of the beer destroyed accompanied
    by such other evidence as is necessary to demonstrate to the
    Minister that the brewer is entitled to the refund.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The brewer shall keep records documenting evidence of
  the destruction of the beer for which a refund of excise duty is
  claimed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An excise officer may be present to supervise the
  destruction of the beer on which excise duty has been paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-259, s. 1
:::

## [Excise Duty Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558048 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the duty imposed under the Act in respect of beer
  produced during a particular month shall be paid not later than the
  last day of the month following the particular month.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensed brewer is authorized by the Minister to
  make returns for six-month periods under subsection 36.1(2) of the
  Act, the duty imposed under the Act in respect of beer produced during
  a six-month period shall be paid not later than the last day of the
  month following the period.

## [Loss Allowance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558054 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The amount of allowance
that shall be made to a brewer pursuant to subsection 170(2) of the Act
in respect of beer produced by that brewer shall be an amount equal to
the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duty that would have been payable under the Act
  if the duty had been assessed on the quantity of beer transferred to a
  tank before being packaged,

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the duty paid under the Act by the brewer in respect
  of beer produced by that brewer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-132, s. 3
:::

## [Information on Containers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558061 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  bottle, can, keg, sphere or barrel shall show the name and address of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brewer who packaged the beer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for whom the beer is packaged.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a name and address is shown pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(b), the brewer who packaged the beer shall provide a
  method of identifying the beer as his product.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-45, s. 4
:::

## [Exports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558069 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Beer shall be deemed to be
an export where it is delivered by a brewer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for use as ships stores under the conditions
  described in the Ships Stores Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a base leased to the United States where a receipt
  for the beer is given by the commanding officer of the base or a
  person acting on the commanding officer\'s behalf;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to a duty free shop licensed as such under the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), where the beer is for sale to persons
  who are about to leave Canada; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to an accredited representative for the accredited
  representative\'s use, where the beer is not sold or otherwise
  disposed of by the accredited representative.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-471, s. 2
:::
