## Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act {#order-specifying-the-royal-canadian-mounted-police-for-the-purposes-of-the-employment-equity-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-422
:::

[EMPLOYMENT EQUITY ACT](/eng/acts/E-5.401)

Registration 2002-11-21

Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of
the Employment Equity Act

P.C. 2002-1958 2002-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA}, the Treasury
Board has consulted the Solicitor General of Canada concerning the
application of that Act to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnote1_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  44
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Labour and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the
Purposes of the Employment Equity Act.
:::

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698559 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Royal Canadian Mounted
Police is specified under paragraph 4(1)(d) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401) as a portion of the public sector employing one
hundred or more employees to which that Act applies.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698561 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
