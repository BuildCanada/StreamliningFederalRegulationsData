## CIFTA Verification of Origin Regulations {#cifta-verification-of-origin-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-75
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1996-12-30

CIFTA Verification of Origin Regulations

P.C. 1996-2087 1996-12-30
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the proposed [CIFTA Verification of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-75)implement a provision of CIFTA
and are therefore, by virtue of paragraph 164(4)(a.03)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EEABA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EBABA}, not required
to be published under subsection 164(3) of that Act;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section
42.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EKEAA}, subsection
42.6(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EIEAA} and paragraph
164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EGEAA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDEAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [CIFTA Verification of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-75).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012543 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the competent
    authority that is responsible under the law of Canada or Israel or
    another CIFTA beneficiary for the administration of customs laws and
    regulations. ([administration douanière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under CIFTA is claimed.
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire to be completed by the exporter or producer of goods
    that requests information with respect to the origin of goods that
    are the subject of a verification of origin. ([questionnaire de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into a
    place or premises for the purposes of conducting a verification of
    origin of goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-337, s. 1
:::

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012552 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods may be conducted
by means of a verification questionnaire.

## [Prescribed Premises]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012554 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The premises of an exporter
or producer of goods in Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary are
prescribed premises for the purposes of a verification visit.

## [Conditions for Conducting a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012556 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer or any person acting on behalf of an officer, as designated by
  the Minister under subsection 42.1(1) of the Act, may conduct a
  verification visit on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer sends written notice of the intention
    to conduct the verification visit to the customs administration of
    Israel or the CIFTA beneficiary in whose territory the verification
    visit is to occur;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the officer or the person acting on behalf of the
    officer, sends written notice of the intention to conduct the
    verification visit to the person whose premises are the subject of
    the verification visit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are the subject of the
    verification visit provides written consent to the verification
    visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in paragraph (1)(b) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration on whose
    behalf the notice is being sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are the
    subject of the verification visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of the verification visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the verification visit and
    shall include specific reference to the goods that are the subject
    of the verification of origin;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer or person
    conducting the verification visit; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the verification visit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-337, s. 2
:::

## [Postponement of a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012571 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  customs administration that receives a notice referred to in paragraph
  4(1)(a), may postpone the verification visit by sending a written
  request for postponement within 15 days after receiving the notice to
  the officer who sent the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A postponement of a verification visit shall be for a
  period not exceeding 60 days beginning on the date on which the notice
  was received, or for a longer period, if the longer period is agreed
  to by the customs administration of Canada and the customs
  administration of Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary in whose
  territory the verification visit will take place.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-111, s. 1
:::

## [Observers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012577 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who receives a notice under paragraph 4(1)(b) may designate two
  observers to be present during the verification visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An observer designated under subsection (1) may not
  participate in the verification visit in a manner other than as an
  observer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates an observer under
  subsection (1) shall identify that observer to the officer or person
  conducting the verification visit.

## [Verification Questionnaires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012582 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  officer or a person acting on behalf of an officer sends a
  verification questionnaire to an exporter or producer of goods in
  Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary, the officer or person shall also
  send a copy of the verification questionnaire to the customs
  administration of Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A verification questionnaire shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration on whose
    behalf the verification questionnaire is being sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods that are the subject of
    the verification of origin;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the verification
    questionnaire must be completed and returned, which period shall be
    not less than 30 days after the date on which the verification
    questionnaire was sent; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer or person acting
    on behalf of the officer sending the questionnaire.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-111, s. 2(F)
:::

## [Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under CIFTA]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012592 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, preferential tariff treatment under CIFTA
may be withdrawn from goods that are the subject of the verification of
origin where an exporter or producer of goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not provide written consent to a verification
  visit within 30 days after receiving a notice referred to in paragraph
  4(1)(b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not complete the verification questionnaire and
  return it to an officer, or a person acting on behalf of an officer,
  within the time period referred to in paragraph 7(2)(c), or fails to
  provide sufficient information in response to the questionnaire; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} who is required to maintain records of the goods in
  accordance with the applicable laws of the country in which the
  verification of origin is conducted,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fails to maintain those records in accordance with
    those laws, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} denies the officer or person conducting the
    verification of origin access to those records.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-337, s. 3
:::
