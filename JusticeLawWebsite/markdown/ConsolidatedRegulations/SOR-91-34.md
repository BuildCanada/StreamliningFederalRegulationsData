## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934498 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[general sales tax rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[general sales tax rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a province means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a participating province, the tax rate for
        the participating province,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for Quebec, the rate of tax set out in the
        first paragraph of section 16 of An Act respecting the Québec
        sales tax, R.S.Q., c. T-0.1,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for Manitoba, the rate of tax set out in
        subsection 2(1) of The Retail Sales Tax Act, C.C.S.M., c. R130,

      - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} for British Columbia, the rate of tax set out
        in subsection 34(1) of the Provincial Sales Tax Act, S.B.C.
        2012, c. 35,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-44, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for Saskatchewan, the rate of tax set out in
        subsection 5(1) of The Provincial Sales Tax Act, R.S.S. 1978,
        c. P-34.1; ([taux général de la taxe de vente]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[specified tax rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specified tax rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a province means the
      rate that is the greater of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 12%, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general sales tax rate of the province plus
        4%. ([taux de taxe déterminé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to an Act of the
  legislature of a province is deemed to be a reference to that Act as
  amended.

## [Prescribed Taxes, Duties and Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934514 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following taxes, duties
and fees are prescribed for the purposes of section 154 of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a tax, duty or fee in respect of a transfer of real
  property where that tax, duty or fee is imposed by or under any of the
  following, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Land Transfer Tax Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. L.6,

  - [(i.1)]{.lawlabel} Chapter 760 of the City of Toronto Municipal
    Code, made under Part X of the City of Toronto Act, 2006, S.O. 2006,
    c. 11, Sched. A, if the tax, duty or fee would have applied to that
    transfer under that chapter as it read on February 1, 2008,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Land Transfer Duties Act, R.S.Q., c. D-17,

  - [(ii.1)]{.lawlabel} Part III.7 of the Taxation Act, R.S.Q., c. I-3,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} An Act respecting duties on transfers of
    immovables, R.S.Q., c. D-15.1,

  - [(iv) to (viii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-273,
    s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} Part V of the Municipal Government Act, S.N.S.
    1998, c. 18,

  - [(ix.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-273, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the Real Property Transfer Tax Act, S.N.B. 1983,
    c. R-2.1,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} Part III of The Tax Administration and
    Miscellaneous Taxes Act, C.C.S.M., c. T2,

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} the Property Transfer Tax Act, R.S.B.C. 1996,
    c. 378,

  - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} The Land Titles Act, 2000, S.S. 2000, c. L-5.1,

  - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} the Land Titles Act, R.S.A. 2000, c. L-4,

  - [(xv)]{.lawlabel} the Real Property Transfer Tax Act, S.P.E.I. 2005,
    c. 49,

  - [(xvi)]{.lawlabel} the Registration of Deeds Act, R.S.N.L. 1990,
    c. R-10, and

  - [(xvii)]{.lawlabel} the City of St. John's Municipal Taxation Act,
    S.N.L. 2006, c. C-17.1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a tax imposed by the legislature of a province, under
  an Act referred to in the definition [[general sales tax
  rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1), in respect of an item
  of property or a service if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the tax is calculated as a percentage of the value
    or price of the property or service,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the tax is payable by the recipient of a supply of
    the property or service,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the tax is not included in the value or price of
    the property or service for purposes of calculating any other tax
    under that Act in respect of that property or service, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the total of the rates of all taxes that are
    imposed under that Act in respect of the property or service and
    calculated as a percentage of the value or price of the property or
    service does not exceed the specified tax rate of the province; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a tax in respect of a service or an item of property
  imposed by or under any of the following:

  - [(i) to (v)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-273,
    s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(v.1) and (vi)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-273,
    s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-152, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} section 330 of The Municipal Act, C.C.S.M.,
    c. M225,

  - [(viii.1)]{.lawlabel} subsection 442(1) of The City of Winnipeg
    Charter, S.M. 2002, c. 39,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2010-152, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(ix.1)]{.lawlabel} section 36 of The Power Corporation Act, R.S.S.
    1978, c. P-19,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} The Liquor Consumption Tax Act, S.S. 1979,
    c. L-19.1,

  - [(x.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2006-280, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} section 316 of The Municipalities Act, S.S. 2005,
    c. M.36.1,

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} subsection 2(1) of the Tourism Levy Act, R.S.A.
    2000, c. T-5.5, and

  - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-248, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} section 2 of the Liquor Tax Act, R.S.Y. 2002,
    c. 141,

  where

  - [(xv)]{.lawlabel} the tax is calculated as a percentage of the value
    or price of the property or service,

  - [(xvi)]{.lawlabel} the tax is payable by the recipient of a supply
    of the property or service,

  - [(xvii)]{.lawlabel} no tax in respect of the property or service is
    imposed by the legislature of a province under an Act referred to in
    the definition [[general sales tax rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 2(1), and

  - [(xviii)]{.lawlabel} the total of the rates of all taxes, each of
    which is

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} imposed by or under an Act of the legislature of
      the province in respect of the property or service,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} calculated as a percentage of the value or price
      of the property or service, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} payable by the recipient of a supply of the
      property or service,

    does not exceed the specified tax rate of the province.
