## Kenora Airport Zoning Regulations {#kenora-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-524
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1993-12-02

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Kenora Airport

P.C. 1993-1936 1993-12-02
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed [Regulations respecting
zoning at Kenora Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-524), substantially in
the form set out in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive
issues of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on
June 19th and 26th, 1993, and in two successive issues of The Daily
Miner and News on July 21st and 22nd, 1993, and a reasonable opportunity
was thereby afforded to interested persons to make representations to
the Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Kenora
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-524).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955455 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Kenora Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-524).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955457 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Kenora Airport, in the
      Town of Jaffray and Melick, in the Province of Ontario;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary plane
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      plane is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
      ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which portion is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly described in Part V of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 403.8 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955467 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the land, including public road allowances, that is adjacent to or
in the vicinity of the airport, which land is more particularly
described in Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955469 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Aeronautical Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955474 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit any part of that land
to be used or developed in a manner that causes interference with any
signals or communications to and from any aircraft or to and from any
facilities used to provide services relating to aeronautics.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955476 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth that is on any land to which these Regulations apply grows to a
height that exceeds in elevation at the location of the object any of
the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require that the
owner or lessee of the land remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955478 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit any part of that land
to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or attractive
to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Kenora Airport Zoning Plan
No. 60-014 89-021, Sheet 10, dated 4 December l990, is a point which may
be located by:

COMMENCING at the threshold of runway 07 being the westerly end of
runway 07-25 at Kenora Airport, having the Universal Transverse Mercator
coordinates of 401 029.18 E and 5 515 592.14 N;

THENCE easterly along the centre line of said runway 07-25, 1 016.25 m
to a point;

THENCE northerly and perpendicular to the centre line of the said
runway 07-25, 81.08 m to the airport reference point, which point has
the Universal Transverse Mercator coordinates of 402 008.73 E and
5 515 873.71 N.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Kenora Airport Zoning Plan
No. 60-014 89-021, Sheets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 9, 10, 11, 15 and 16, dated
4 December 1990, are planes abutting each end of the strip associated
with runway 07-25, and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 07 having a ratio of l m
  measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 25 having a ratio of l m
  measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant l5 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Kenora Airport Zoning Plan No. 60-014
89-021, Sheets 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14, dated 4 December 1990,
is an imaginary plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above
the elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that
plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer
surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip, shown on Kenora Airport Zoning Plan No. 60-014 89-021,
Sheet 10, dated 4 December 1990, is described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 07-25 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 888 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Kenora Airport Zoning Plan
No. 60-014 89-021, Sheets 9, 10 and 11, dated 4 December 1990, is an
inclined plane rising at a ratio of l m measured vertically to 7 m
measured horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected
centre line of the strip, extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.
