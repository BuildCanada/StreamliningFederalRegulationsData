## [Maritime Traffic]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506220 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the course of a voyage of a vessel to one of the ports referred to
  in subsection (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the crew or a passenger on board the
    vessel has

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} died,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} had a temperature of 38°C (100°F) or greater
      that persisted for two days or more or was accompanied or followed
      by a rash, jaundice or glandular swelling, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} suffered from diarrhea severe enough to
      interfere with that person\'s work or normal activity,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person in charge of the vessel is, during the
    period

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of four weeks preceding the estimated time of
      arrival of the vessel, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} since the person last submitted a declaration of
      health,

    whichever is the lesser, aware of any instance of illness among the
    crew or passengers that he suspects is of an infectious nature and
    may lead to the spread of disease,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within 14 days of its estimated time of arrival
      in Canada, been in a country that, in the opinion of a quarantine
      officer, is infected or suspected of being infected with smallpox,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within 60 days of its estimated time of arrival
      in Canada been in a country that, in the opinion of a quarantine
      officer, is infected or suspected of being infected with the
      plague, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a certificate establishing that the vessel has been
    de-ratted or exempted from de-ratting procedures has expired or is
    about to expire

  the person in charge of the vessel shall, by radio at least 24 hours
  prior to the vessel\'s estimated time of arrival at its port of
  destination and between the hours of 9 o\'clock in the forenoon and 5
  o\'clock in the afternoon, notify the quarantine officer at the
  quarantine station designated in subsection (3) for that port of the
  occurrence and provide him with the information described in
  subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information to be provided to the quarantine
  officer pursuant to subsection (1) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and nationality of the vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ports called at during the voyage of the
    vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the cargo on board the vessel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons comprising the crew of the
    vessel;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of passengers on board the vessel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the port of destination of the vessel and the name
    of the vessel\'s owner or, if the owner is not in Canada, the name
    of the vessel\'s agent in Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the condition of all persons on board the vessel
    and details of any death or illness occurring during the voyage;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether the body of any person is being carried on
    the vessel;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time of arrival of the vessel at the
    port of destination;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons on board the vessel who are
    not in possession of valid evidence of immunization to smallpox; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of issuance of any de-ratting
    certificate or de-ratting exemption certificate applicable to the
    vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the quarantine
  station for vessels bound for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a port in the Province of Nova Scotia or a port in
    the Province of Prince Edward Island, is Quarantine Station,
    Halifax, Nova Scotia;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a port in the Province of New Brunswick, is
    Quarantine Station, Saint John, New Brunswick;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a port in the Province of Newfoundland, is
    Quarantine Station, St. John\'s, Newfoundland;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a port in the Province of Quebec or any Canadian
    port via the St. Lawrence River, is Quarantine Station, Montreal,
    Quebec;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a port in the Province of British Columbia, is
    Quarantine Station, Vancouver, British Columbia; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a port on Hudson Bay, is Quarantine Station,
    Churchill, Manitoba.

## [Air Traffic]{.HTitleText1} {#h-506263 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person in charge of any aircraft arriving in Canada from a place
  outside Canada wishes to land at any of the airports listed in
  subsection (2),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he shall, prior to arrival, except in the case of
    emergency or other circumstances in which it is impossible to
    communicate with the airport send by radio to the quarantine officer
    at such airport information concerning

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any illness among the persons on board the
      aircraft, other than air sickness, or resulting from any accident
      that might have occurred during the flight, with details of such
      illness including the existence of fever, skin rash, headache,
      backache, jaundice, diarrhea, vomiting, chills or abnormal
      behaviour, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the death of any person on board the aircraft
      during the flight; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} he may, where no illness described in
    subparagraph (a) (i) has become apparent and no death has occurred
    during the flight, send by radio to the quarantine officer at such
    airport a message that all on board appear to be healthy.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies in respect of the following
  airports:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Gander International;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Goose Bay;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} St. John\'s International;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Halifax International;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Sydney; ;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Montreal International (Dorval);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Montreal International (Mirabel);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} Quebec, Jean Lesage International;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Hamilton;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} Toronto, Lester B. Pearson International;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} Ottawa, Macdonald-Cartier International;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} Windsor;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} London;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} Winnipeg International;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} Saskatoon, John G. Diefenbaker International;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} Regina;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} Edmonton International;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} Calgary International;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} Vancouver International;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} Victoria International; and

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} Kelowna.
