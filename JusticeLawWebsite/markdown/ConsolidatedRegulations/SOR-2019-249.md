## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [International Financial
    Assistance Act](/eng/acts/I-19.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[OECD DAC List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[OECD DAC List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Organisation for
    Economic Co-operation and Development's Development Assistance
    Committee List of Official Development Assistance Recipients, as
    amended from time to time. ([liste établie par le CAD de
    l'OCDE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Sovereign Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions to make a loan

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The competent minister must
not make a loan under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the competent minister is of the opinion that the
  recipient or guarantor of the loan is creditworthy, taking into
  consideration World Bank and International Monetary Fund debt
  sustainability assessments; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recipient or guarantor of the loan is the
  government of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a country or territory that is included in the OECD
    DAC List, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a borrowing member of the International Bank for
    Reconstruction and Development of the World Bank Group.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign state limit

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The sum of all outstanding
loans made by the competent minister under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act
to the government of a single foreign state and all outstanding loans
made by the competent minister under that paragraph that are guaranteed
under subsection 3(2) of the Act by the government of that foreign state
must not exceed \$120 million or the value of 20% of all outstanding
loans made by the competent minister under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act,
whichever is greater.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum loan term

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The maximum term of a loan
made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act is 10 years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest rate

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The interest rate
applicable to a loan made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act is a fixed
rate that equals the Government of Canada's cost of borrowing as
determined by reference to the zero-coupon yield curve for Government of
Canada bonds published by the Bank of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initiation fee

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The initiation fee for a
loan made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act is the lesser of 0.15% of
the amount of the loan and \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commitment fee

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a loan made
under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act that is to be disbursed in a single
disbursement, the per annum commitment fee is 0.25% of the amount of the
loan for the period beginning on the day that is 60 days after the
effective date of the loan and ending on the day on which the recipient
draws the loan, payable on a semi-annual basis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions of repayment

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), principal and interest payments related to a loan
  made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act are payable by the recipient
  on at least an annual basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grace period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The competent minister may grant the recipient a
  grace period during which the recipient is not required to make
  principal or interest payments, or both, on a loan made under
  paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act if there is an imbalance between the
  recipient's cash inflows and outflows.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Sections 2, 4 and 8 of
these Regulations do not apply to a loan made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of
the Act as part of debt restructuring.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Currency

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A loan made under
paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act may only be made in the currency of Canada,
the United States, the European Monetary Union, the United Kingdom,
Japan or another currency identified by the International Monetary Fund
as an official foreign exchange reserve currency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval --- Minister of Finance

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The competent minister
must not carry out any of the following transactions without the
approval of the Minister of Finance:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} making a loan under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amending a financial term of a loan made under
  paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation --- Minister of Finance

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The competent minister
must consult with the Minister of Finance before a debt or obligation
relating to a loan made under paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act is written
off or forgiven.

## [Innovative Financing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179917 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons or entities

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Under paragraph 4(a) of
the Act, the competent minister must not guarantee an obligation
undertaken by a person or entity unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the guarantee supports international assistance in a
  country or territory included in the OECD DAC List; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the competent minister obtains from the recipient of
  the guarantee an undertaking to make reasonable efforts to recover
  losses related to the obligation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum guarantee exposure

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum amount of outstanding guarantees provided under paragraph 4(a)
  of the Act must not exceed \$500 million.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recipient limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount of outstanding guarantees provided
  under paragraph 4(a) of the Act to a particular recipient must not
  exceed \$100 million.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial guarantee

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If the competent minister
provides a partial guarantee under paragraph 4(a) of the Act, the
competent minister must not guarantee more than 50% of the obligation
undertaken by a person or entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initiation fee

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 18 of
these Regulations, the initiation fee for a guarantee provided under
paragraph 4(a) of the Act is the lesser of 0.15% of the amount of the
guarantee and \$100,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 18 of
these Regulations, the fee to obtain a guarantee provided under
paragraph 4(a) of the Act is an amount equal to the net present value of
the expected financial loss to the Government of Canada of the
guarantee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees --- guarantees with co-guarantor

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If the competent minister
provides a guarantee under paragraph 4(a) of the Act with a
co-guarantor, the initiation fee and the fee to obtain the guarantee are
the amounts charged for similar fees by the co-guarantor on a prorated
basis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances of dealing with shares

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  competent minister must not under paragraph 4(f) of the Act acquire,
  hold, assign, exchange, sell or otherwise dispose of shares except in
  respect of an investment that supports international assistance in a
  country or territory included in the OECD DAC List.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of dealing with shares

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The competent minister must not seek to acquire or
  hold a controlling or majority interest in a corporation when making
  the investment referred to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval --- Minister of Finance

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The competent minister
must not carry out any of the following transactions without the
approval of the Minister of Finance:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing a guarantee under paragraph 4(a) of the
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} executing a transaction under any of paragraphs 4(b)
  to (f) of the Act, except if the transaction is executed under
  paragraph 4(f) of the Act through a transfer payment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} amending a financial term of a transaction that was
  executed under any of paragraphs 4(a) to (f) of the Act, except if the
  transaction is executed under paragraph 4(f) of the Act through a
  transfer payment.

## [Program for Climate Change]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The competent minister
must not, under section 5 of the Act, seek to acquire or hold a
controlling or majority interest in a corporation.
