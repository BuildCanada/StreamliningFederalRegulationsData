## Repair Abroad of Canadian Civil Aircraft, Canadian Aircraft Engines and Flight Simulators Remission Order {#repair-abroad-of-canadian-civil-aircraft-canadian-aircraft-engines-and-flight-simulators-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-131
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-07-14

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the Excise Tax Act on Canadian Civil Aircraft, Canadian
Aircraft Engines, Canadian Flight Simulators and Parts Thereof, Repaired
Abroad

P.C. 1982-1994 1982-06-30
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to revoke the Repair of Canadian Civil Aircraft, Canadian Aircraft
Engines and Flight Simulators Remission Abroad Order made by Order in
Council P.C. 1981-649 of 6th March, 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA} and to make the
annexed Order respecting the remission of sales tax on Canadian civil
aircraft, Canadian aircraft engines, Canadian flight simulators and
parts thereof, repaired abroad, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote_e}SI/81-44, 1981
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 985
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638601 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Repair Abroad of Canadian Civil Aircraft, Canadian Aircraft Engines
and Flight Simulators Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-82-131).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638603 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Canadian aircraft engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian aircraft engine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft
    engine

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was manufactured in Canada, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on which duty and taxes were paid under the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([moteur d'aéronef
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian civil aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian civil aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a civil
    aircraft

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was manufactured in Canada, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on which duty and taxes were paid under the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([aéronef civil canadien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Canadian flight simulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian flight simulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a flight
    simulator system

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was manufactured in Canada, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on which duty and taxes were paid under the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([simulateur de vol
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian aircraft engines,
    Canadian civil aircraft, Canadian flight simulators and parts
    thereof; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a part that can be classified
    under tariff item No. 9967.00.00 and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was manufactured in Canada, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on which customs duties and taxes were paid under
      the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes maintenance, rebuilding,
    modification and conversion; ([réparation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[value of the repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value of the repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of
    goods, the cost shown on a repair bill for a repair less the cost of
    direct labour and employee benefits when those costs are shown
    separately on the repair bill and are acceptable to the Minister of
    National Revenue. ([valeur des réparations]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/92-203, s. 2(F)
- SI/98-6, s. 13
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638622 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the tax payable under Division III of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on goods returned to Canada after having been
exported therefrom for the purpose of repair in an amount equal to the
difference between the tax paid or payable on the duty paid value of the
goods and the tax paid or payable on the value of the repair where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods were exported under customs supervision;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no drawback has been paid in respect of the goods
  temporarily exported; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no claim for relief of the tax imposed under Division
  III of Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) has been
  applied for under section 101 of the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the goods temporarily
  exported.
