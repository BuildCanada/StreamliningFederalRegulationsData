[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 3, remission is granted of the customs duties paid or
  payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of
  goods that originate in Ukraine, except for goods subject to the
  General Tariff and those classified under one of the tariff items
  whose number is listed in the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods that originate in Ukraine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, goods originate in
  Ukraine if their last production process, other than a minimal
  operation, occurred in Ukraine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[minimal
  operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[minimal
  operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an operation to ensure the preservation of a good
    in good condition for the purposes of transport and storage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} packaging, re-packaging, breaking up of
    consignments or putting up a good for retail sale, including placing
    a good in bottles, cans, flasks, bags, cases or boxes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} mere dilution with water or another substance that
    does not materially alter the characteristics of the good;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collection of goods intended to form sets,
    assortments, kits or composite goods; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any combination of operations referred to in
    paragraphs (a) to (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- [Special Import Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-15)

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 3, remission is granted of the duties paid or payable under
  the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) in respect of goods
  that originate in Ukraine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods that originate in Ukraine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, goods originate in
  Ukraine if they acquired their physical and technical characteristics
  in Ukraine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a remission granted under subsection
  1(1), the goods are imported during the period beginning on June 9,
  2022 and ending on June 9, 2026;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a remission granted under subsection
  2(1), the goods are either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} imported during the period beginning on June 9,
    2022 and ending on June 9, 2025, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in transit to Canada before June 10, 2025; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer makes a claim for remission to the
  Minister of Public Security and Emergency Preparedness within two
  years of the date of importation.
