## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766351 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3 of the
    [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1). ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant, respondent or
    intervener. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Telecommunications
    Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that is adverse in
    interest to an applicant. ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766358 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  if they provide otherwise, these Rules apply to all proceedings before
  the Commission other than a proceeding arising from an application
  listed in Schedule 1 or from a contravention of a prohibition or
  requirement of the Commission for which a person is liable to an
  administrative monetary penalty under any of sections 72.001 to 72.19
  of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), unless the penalty
  is imposed in the course of a proceeding referred to in section 72.003
  of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Rules do not apply to proceedings before the
  Commission under sections 6 to 46 of [An Act to promote the efficiency
  and adaptability of the Canadian economy by regulating certain
  activities that discourage reliance on electronic means of carrying
  out commercial activities, and to amend the Canadian Radio-television
  and Telecommunications Commission Act, the Competition Act, the
  Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act and the
  Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/E-1.6).

## [Matters Before the Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766366 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How matters are brought before Commission

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A matter may be brought
before the Commission by an application or complaint or on the
Commission's own initiative.

*Note: Under section 12 of the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), the Governor in Council may refer back to the
Commission for reconsideration a decision by the Commission, and under
section 14, it may require the Commission to make a report on any matter
within the Commission's jurisdiction under that Act or any special Act.
Under section 15 of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), the
Governor in Council may request the Commission to hold hearings or make
reports on any matter within the jurisdiction of the Commission under
that Act, and under section 28, the Governor in Council may refer back
to the Commission for reconsideration and hearing of the matter a
decision by the Commission to issue, amend or renew a licence.*

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to Broadcasting and Telecommunications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766370 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-766371 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Sections 30 to 32,
subsection 33(4) and section34 apply to a proceeding arising from an
application listed in Schedule 1.

### [General Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-766374 .Subheading}

#### [Powers of the Commission]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766375 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to act

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may exercise any of its powers under these Rules at the
  request of a party or interested person or on its own initiative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters not provided for

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may provide for any matter of practice
  and procedure not provided for in these Rules by analogy to these
  Rules or by reference to the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) and the rules of other tribunals
  to which the subject matter of the proceeding most closely relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information bulletins

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may issue
information bulletins regarding matters within its jurisdiction,
including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application of these Rules and the Commission's
  regulatory policies and decisions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the format and numbering of documents to be filed
  with the Commission, the software to be used to file them and the
  procedure for their filing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing with or varying Rules

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the Commission is of the
opinion that considerations of public interest or fairness permit, it
may dispense with or vary these Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of application or complaint

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If an application or
complaint does not comply with a requirement of these Rules, the
Commission may return the application or the complaint to the applicant
or the complainant so that the deficiencies may be remedied or it may
close the file.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defect in form

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must not
dismiss an application or complaint by reason solely of a defect in
form.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other powers

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is of the opinion that the circumstances or
  considerations of fairness permit, adjourn a proceeding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is of the opinion that the circumstances or
  considerations of fairness permit, combine two or more proceedings;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} decide whether to admit a document as evidence;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} order to be amended or struck out a document or part
  of a document that, in its opinion, might prejudice a party or delay
  the hearing of the matter on the merits;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide an opportunity for the parties to make
  written or oral representations; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the event of a reference to the Federal Court,
  stay the whole or any part of a proceeding pending the decision of the
  Court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relief

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In broadcasting matters,
the Commission may approve the whole or any part of an application or
grant any relief in addition to or in substitution for the relief
applied for.

*Note: Section 60 of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)
provides in part for the same rule in relation to telecommunications
matters.*

#### [Time]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766402 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of time

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 26 to 29 of the
  [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21) apply to the computation of a
  time period set out in these Rules or a decision, notice of
  consultation, regulatory policy or information bulletin, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Saturday is considered to be a holiday;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a time period for the filing of a document with the
    Commission ends at 5:00 p.m., Vancouver time; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period beginning on December 21 in one year and
    ending on January 7 in the following year must not be included in
    the computation of a time period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calendar days

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A time period is computed in calendar days.

#### [Filing and Service of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766411 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of documents

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document must be filed with the Commission

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application, by sending the
    document to the Office of the Secretary General by any electronic
    means that permits its intelligible reception, as set out in
    Broadcasting and Telecom Information Bulletin CRTC 2010-453, as
    amended from time to time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other document, by delivering it
    by hand to the Office of the Secretary General, or sending it to the
    Office by mail or any electronic means that permits its intelligible
    reception.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing at public hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a public hearing of the Commission is in
  progress, a document may also be filed with the Commission by
  delivering the document by hand to the secretary of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of transmission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a document is sent by electronic means, the sender
  must keep proof of the sending and the receipt of the document for 180
  days after the day on which it is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative format

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person with a disability or their designated representative may
  request that a party whose document has been posted on the
  Commission's website file the document with the Commission in the
  alternative format specified by the person or representative. The
  request must be served on the party within five days after the day on
  which the document was posted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within five days after the day on which a party was
  served with the request, the party must file with the Commission and
  serve on the person or representative

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the document in the specified alternative format or
    in an alternative format that was agreed to by the person or
    representative; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why the party cannot file the document
    in an alternative format.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within five days after the day on which the person or
  representative was served with the document or the reasons, they may
  file a reply with the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request of Commission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Failing an agreement between the person or
  representative and the party, the Commission may request the party to
  file the document with the Commission in the alternative format
  specified by the Commission and serve it on the person or
  representative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing day

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document is filed with the Commission

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is delivered by hand, on the day on which it
    is delivered; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by mail or electronic means, on the
    day on which it is received by the Office of the Secretary General.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Holiday

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document received on a holiday is considered to be
  received on the next business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of documents

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may order a person that has filed a document with it to
  verify its content by affidavit and, in case of refusal, may strike
  out the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds of belief

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an affidavit is made as to belief, the grounds on
  which the belief is based must be set out in the affidavit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for service

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A document that is
required to be served must be served, along with the documents that
accompany it, on or before the day on which it is filed with the
Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A document must be served
on a person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by delivering a copy of the document by hand

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the person, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a partnership, body corporate or
    unincorporated organization, to a partner, officer or director, or
    to their designated representative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the document by mail to the last
  known address of the person or their designated representative; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by sending the document by any electronic means that
  permits its intelligible reception to the person or their designated
  representative, in which case the sender must keep proof of the
  sending and the receipt of the document for 180 days after the day on
  which it is filed with the Commission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service day

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Service of a document is
effected

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is delivered by hand, on the day on which it is
  delivered; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by mail or electronic means, on the day
  on which it is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  Commission's request, proof of service, or, if there is none, an
  affidavit in lieu of proof, must be filed with the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of proof

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service or an affidavit must include or be
  accompanied by the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email
    address of the person that served the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the document was delivered by hand
    or sent by mail or electronic means and, if it was sent by
    electronic means, the date and time it was sent and received; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the document was served by facsimile, the total
    number of pages transmitted including the cover page, the facsimile
    number from which it was sent and the name of the person that sent
    it.

#### [Notice of Consultation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766465 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of consultation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  matter is brought before the Commission on the Commission's own
  initiative, the Commission must post a notice of consultation on its
  website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the matters to be considered and the
    deadline for intervening in the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any deadline for filing a reply with the
    Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the Commission will request any parties to
    appear before it, the date and time of the commencement of the
    public hearing and the place of the hearing, which in
    telecommunications matters is fixed by the Chairperson of the
    Commission; and

    *Note : Subsection 18(4) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)
    grants the Chairperson of the Commission the power to designate the
    place of a public hearing in relation to broadcasting matters.*

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in telecommunications matters, any permission
    granted by the Commission for a party to make a request for
    information from another party and the deadlines referred to in
    sections 73 to 76.

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766476 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and service of application

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} filed with the Commission;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} served on any respondent and any other persons that
    the Commission directs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by a list of the persons on whom the
    application is served and the email address of each, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application must be made using the appropriate
  form listed in Broadcasting and Telecom Information Bulletin CRTC
  2010-453, as amended from time to time. If none of the forms listed in
  the Bulletin is appropriate, the application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the name, address and email address of the
    applicant and any designated representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the applicant's website address or, if the
    application is not posted on their website, the email address where
    an electronic copy of the application may be requested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be divided into parts and consecutively numbered
    paragraphs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} identify the statutory or regulatory provisions
    under which the application is made;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} contain a clear and concise statement of the
    relevant facts, of the grounds of the application and of the nature
    of the decision sought;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} set out any amendments or additions to these Rules
    proposed by the applicant; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} include any other information that might inform the
    Commission as to the nature, purpose and scope of the application,
    and be accompanied by any supporting documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting of application

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Commision must post
on its website all applications that comply with the requirements set
out in section 22.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An applicant must not
amend an application or file any supplementary documents related to the
application with the Commission after the application has been posted on
the Commission's website.

#### [Answer to Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766496 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for filing answer

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent may file an answer with the Commission within 30 days after
  the day on which the Commission posts the application on its website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of answer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The answer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the name, address and email address of the
    respondent and any designated representative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be divided into parts and consecutively numbered
    paragraphs;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} admit or deny the facts alleged in the application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} contain a clear and concise statement of the
    relevant facts, of the grounds of the answer and of the nature of
    the decision sought;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} include any other information that might inform the
    Commission as to the nature, purpose and scope of the answer, and be
    accompanied by any supporting documents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a list of the persons on whom the
    answer is served and the email address of each, if any; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} be served on the applicant, any other respondent
    and any other persons that the Commission directs.

#### [Intervention]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766509 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for intervening

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  interested person may intervene in a proceeding in writing within 30
  days after the day on which the application is posted on the
  Commission's website or, if a notice of consultation is posted by the
  Commission on its website, within the time period set out in the
  notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The document of the interested person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state that the person wishes to be considered as an
    intervener in the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the name, address and email address of the
    person and any designated representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be divided into parts and consecutively numbered
    paragraphs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} admit or deny the facts alleged in the application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} clearly state whether the person supports or
    opposes the application and the nature of the decision sought;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} contain a clear and concise statement of the
    relevant facts and of the grounds for the person's support for or
    opposition to the application and the reasons for the decision
    sought;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} state whether the person wishes to participate in
    any future public hearing in person;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} state any reasonable accommodation required to
    enable the person to participate in any future public hearing;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} include any other information that might inform the
    Commission as to the nature, purpose and scope of the intervention,
    and be accompanied by any supporting documents;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a list of the persons on whom the
    document is served and the email address of each, if any; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} be served on the applicant and any other persons
    that the Commission directs.

#### [Reply]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for filing reply

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant may file a reply to an answer or to the document of an
  intervener with the Commission within 10 days after the deadline for
  the filing of the answer or the deadline for intervening in the
  proceeding, as the case may be, or, if a notice of consultation is
  posted by the Commission on its website, within the time period set
  out in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reply must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be restricted to the points raised in the answer or
    the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} admit or deny the facts alleged in the answer or
    the document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the grounds of objection or opposition, if
    any, to points raised in the answer or the document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a list of the persons on whom the
    reply is served and the email address of each, if any; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be served on the respondents and the interveners to
    whom the applicant is replying and any other persons that the
    Commission directs.

#### [Request for Information or Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766537 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement for information and
representations

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may require a party

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide information, particulars or documents
    that it considers necessary to enable the Commission to reach a full
    and satisfactory understanding of the subject matter of the
    proceeding; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make written or oral representations on any
    matter related to the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to represent other person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission may also require the representative of
  a person to file with it evidence of their authority to represent the
  person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for documents

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may request in writing that any other party produce for the
  requesting party's inspection a copy of any document that has been
  referred to in a document that the other party has filed with the
  Commission and permit the requesting party to make copies of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and service of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requesting party must file its request with the
  Commission and serve it on the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to produce document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party that fails to produce a copy of the document
  within 10 days after the day on which the request is filed must not
  rely on the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic version or link

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In order to comply with the request, the party may
  produce an electronic version of the document or provide an electronic
  link to the document, where it may be accessed free of charge.

#### [Confidential Information]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766554 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents public

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In broadcasting matters,
the Commission must make available for public inspection any information
submitted to the Commission in the course of proceedings before it to
the extent that the information is not designated as confidential.

*Note: Section 38 of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)
provides for the same rule in relation to telecommunications matters.*

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation subject to filing

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  broadcasting matters, a party may designate information referred to in
  paragraphs 39(1)(a) to (c) of the [Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) as confidential if they file it with the
  Commission.

  *Note: Subsection 39(1) of the [Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) provides for the same rule in relation to
  telecommunications matters.*

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must make the designation at the time that
  they file the document that contains the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons for designation

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  party that designates information as confidential must provide
  reasons, as well as any supporting documents, why the disclosure of
  the information would not be in the public interest, including why the
  specific direct harm that would be likely to result from the
  disclosure would outweigh the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abridged version

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must either file with the Commission an
  abridged version, intended to be made available to the public, of the
  document that contains the information or provide reasons, as well as
  any supporting documents, why an abridged version cannot be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for disclosure

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that files a request with the Commission for the disclosure of
  information that has been designated as confidential must provide
  reasons, as well as any supporting documents, why the disclosure would
  be in the public interest, including how the information is relevant
  to the Commission's regulatory responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requesting party must serve the request on the
  author of the designation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The author of the designation may file a reply with
  the Commission within 10 days after the day on which the request is
  filed, and must serve the reply on the requesting party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply to request from Commission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Commission requests disclosure of the
  information, the author of the designation may file a reply with the
  Commission within 10 days after the day on which they receive the
  request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure criterion

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  broadcasting matters, the Commission may disclose or require the
  disclosure of information designated as confidential if it is of the
  opinion that the disclosure is in the public interest.

  *Note: Subsection 39(4) of the [Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) provides in part for the same rule in relation
  to telecommunications matters.*

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information not admissible in evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the author of the designation is an applicant and
  confirms the designation, the Commission must not disclose or require
  the disclosure of the information and the information is not
  admissible in evidence.

### [Rules Applicable to Public Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-766584 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of applicant

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  public hearing is held in respect of an application, the applicant
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no later than five days after the day on which the
    Commission posts the notice of consultation on its website, post the
    notice or an electronic link to it on a page of their own website
    that is accessible from the homepage of the website and keep it
    posted until the deadline for intervening in the proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give notice of the notice of consultation in any
    manner that the Commission directs, including through broadcast over
    the applicant's facilities or by service to any person that the
    Commission directs, which notice must set out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the matters to be considered,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the deadline for intervening in the proceeding,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the commencement of the
      hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of licensees and
  telecommunications service providers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a public hearing is held in respect of a
  regulatory policy, the Commission must determine, if it is in the
  public interest to do so, which licensees and telecommunications
  service providers must fulfill the obligations set out in subsection
  (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appearance

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  later than 10 days before the date of the commencement of the public
  hearing, the Commission must notify in writing any party that the
  Commission requests to appear before it in person or by
  videoconference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of persons appearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No later than seven days before the date of the
  commencement of the public hearing, the party must file with the
  Commission a list of the persons who will represent or accompany the
  party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparatory conference

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
request the parties to appear prior to a public hearing before one of
its members, as defined in section 2 of the [Canadian Radio-television
and Telecommunications Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-22), or any other
person designated by the Commission, on a day and at a time and place
specified by the Commission, for the purpose of formulating the issues
and considering

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the simplification of the issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the necessity or desirability of amending the
  application, answer, intervention or reply;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the making of admissions of certain facts, the proof
  of certain facts by affidavit or the use by a party of matters of
  public record;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedure to be followed at the hearing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the mutual exchange by the parties of documents and
  exhibits that the parties intend to submit at the hearing; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other matters that might aid in the
  simplification of the evidence and disposition of the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission may, at the request of a party or on its own initiative,
  order a public hearing to be held in whole or in part [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} to discuss information that the party
  has designated, or may wish to designate, as confidential under
  subsection 39(1) of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) or
  section 31.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only the party, the party's representatives and the
  Commission's representatives may participate in the [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} discussions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transcript of discussions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Only the party that participated in the [in
  camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} discussions is to be provided with a
  transcript of the discussions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of confidential information

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections31 to 34 apply, to the
  extent provided, to the designation of information as confidential in
  the transcript of the [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} discussions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of transcript and abridged
  version

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In broadcasting and in telecommunications matters, a
  party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file the transcript and the abridged version with
    the Commission within the time period established by the Commission;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify any information contained in the
    transcript that they have already designated as confidential in
    another document and, separately or distinctively, identify other
    information contained in the transcript that they are designating as
    confidential.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of appearance

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The parties must be heard
in the following order at a public hearing:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} applicants;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respondents;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} interveners; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} applicants, in reply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Only evidence submitted
in support of statements contained in an application, answer,
intervention or reply, or in documents or supporting material filed with
the Commission, is admissible at a public hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administration of oaths

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may
require a person who appears before it to be sworn or affirmed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simultaneous sittings

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
relation to one proceeding, hold sittings simultaneously in more than
one location.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format of subpoena

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** A subpoena, which the
Commission may refuse to issue, must be in the form set out in Schedule
2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of complaint

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A consumer complaint that

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The person against whom a
complaint is made may file a response with the Commission within 20 days
after the day on which they receive a copy of the complaint and they
must serve the response on the complainant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** If the Commission is not
satisfied with a response, it may take any measures that it considers
necessary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy placed on licensee's file

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may place
a copy of a complaint against a licensee and the licensee's response on

renewal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency telecommunications complaint

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  complaint seeking relief on an emergency basis in relation to a
  telecommunications matter may be made orally to a designated employee
  of the Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interim [ex parte]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an interim settlement cannot be reached, the
  Commission may issue an interim [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  order, in which case the complainant must file their complaint in
  writing with the Commission within five days after the day on which
  the order is issued.

### [Application to Award Costs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-766698 .Subheading}

#### [Interim Costs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766699 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for interim costs

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** A party that considers
that they do not have sufficient financial resources to participate
effectively in a proceeding may file an application with the Commission
for interim costs to be awarded under section 56 of the
[Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  application, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate that they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have, or represent a group or a class of
      subscribers that has, an interest in the outcome of the
      proceeding,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} can assist the Commission in developing a better
      understanding of the matters to be considered, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} do not have sufficient financial resources to
      participate effectively in the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertake to participate in the proceeding in a
    responsible way;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indicate the amount of costs requested, with
    receipts or detailed estimates; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} identify the respondents that should pay the costs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must serve the application on all other
  parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Answer

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The other parties may
file an answer with the Commission within 10 days after the day on which
the application for interim costs is filed and they must serve their
answer on all parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for awarding interim costs

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must
determine whether to award interim costs and the amount that is to be
awarded on the basis of the following criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant has, or is the representative
  of a group or a class of subscribers that has, an interest in the
  outcome of the proceeding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the extent to which the applicant can assist the
  Commission in developing a better understanding of the matters to be
  considered;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant has sufficient financial
  resources to participate effectively in the proceeding; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant undertook to participate in the
  proceeding in a responsible way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for final costs

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** A party that has been
awarded interim costs is required to file an application for final costs
with the Commission.

#### [Final Costs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766724 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for filing application for final

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** An application for final

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application for final costs

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  application, the applicant must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} demonstrate that they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} have, or represent a group or a class of
      subscribers that has, an interest in the outcome of the
      proceeding,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assisted the Commission in developing a better
      understanding of the matters that were considered, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} participated in the proceeding in a responsible
      way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify the respondents that should pay the costs;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if interim costs were awarded to them, provide an
    explanation of any difference between those interim costs and the
    final costs for which they are applying.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be attached

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must attach to the application the
  appropriate taxation forms listed in Broadcasting and Telecom
  Information Bulletin CRTC 2010-453, as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must serve the application on all other
  parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Answer

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The other parties may
file an answer with the Commission within 10 days after the day on which
the application for final costs is filed and they must serve their
answer on all parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for awarding final costs

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** The Commission must
determine whether to award final costs and the maximum percentage of
costs that is to be awarded on the basis of the following criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant had, or was the representative
  of a group or a class of subscribers that had, an interest in the
  outcome of the proceeding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the extent to which the applicant assisted the
  Commission in developing a better understanding of the matters that
  were considered; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant participated in the proceeding
  in a responsible way.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxing officer

#### [Fixing and Taxing of Costs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-766749 .Subheading}

  addressed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that is served with a request for a further response must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respond fully and adequately to each interrogatory;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the party contends that the request for a
    further response is not relevant or that the information necessary
    to respond is not available, set out the reasons in support of that
    contention and, in the case of the unavailability of the
    information, provide any available information that they consider
    might be of assistance to the party requesting the further response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responding party must file a response with the
  Commission on or before the deadline established by the Commission and
  they must serve the response on the requesting party.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef}[Broadcasting Applications That Give Rise to a Proceeding to Which the Rules Do Not Apply]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-766798 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The following applications, if they do not raise any concerns with
respect to Commission policies or regulations or conditions of licence:

- [1]{.lawlabel} Application for the extension of a deadline for the
  implementation of an authority to provide a new service.

- [2]{.lawlabel} Application for the extension of a deadline for the
  filing of documentation or other information in response to a
  requirement made by the Commission in a decision.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Application for changes to the authorized contours of
  over-the-air programming undertakings in cases where the revised
  contours do not enter a new market and the proposal will not result in
  a change of the operating class of a low-power station.

- [4]{.lawlabel} Application for changes to the authorized areas of
  licensed broadcasting distribution undertakings.

- [5]{.lawlabel} Application for amendments to conditions of licence to
  implement a regulatory policy of the Commission or to incorporate a
  standard condition.

- [6]{.lawlabel} Application by a licensee for revocation of the
  licensee's licence.

- [7]{.lawlabel} Share transfer application referred to in paragraph 9.
  of Broadcasting Circular CRTC 2008-8, dated November 21, 2008.
