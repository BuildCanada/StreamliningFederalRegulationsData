## [Remission --- Ten-year Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605249 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount set out in section 2 to every person who has a leasehold
interest in public lands that are situated in a national park set out in
Schedule I to the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) if the
amount of rent payable in respect of those lands has been or is due to
be fixed in the year 2000 for a ten-year period beginning in that year,
in accordance with the terms of the applicable lease and the National
Parks Lease and Licence of Occupation Regulations (1991).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 1 shall be equal to the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the second year of the ten-year
  period beginning in the year 2000

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the last year of the ten-year
  period ending in the year 2000.

## [Remission --- Two-year Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605255 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount set out in section 4 to every person who has a leasehold
interest in public lands that are situated in a national park set out in
Schedule I to the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) if the
amount of rent payable in respect of those lands has been or is due to
be fixed in the year 2000 for a two-year period beginning in that year,
in accordance with the terms of the applicable lease and the National
Parks Lease and Licence of Occupation Regulations (1991).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 3 shall be equal to the sum of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the rental rate for the first
  year of the two-year period beginning in the year 2000 exceeds the
  rental rate for the second year of the two-year period ending in the
  year 2000, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the rental rate for the second
  year of the two-year period beginning in the year 2000 exceeds the
  rental rate for the second year of the two-year period ending in the
  year 2000.
