## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-83
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2021-11-10

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2021-938 2021-10-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29972){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29972-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29996){#fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29996-ID0EEDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Health to the Minister of Mental Health
and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Health under the [Canadian Centre on
Substance Abuse Act](/eng/acts/C-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29981){#fn_81000-3-2264_hq_29981-ID0EBDAA}.
