## Name Use (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#name-use-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-260
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-05-07

Regulations Permitting the Use of the Name of an Association in a
Document in Connection with a Transaction Related to Securities

P.C. 1992-909  1992-05-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 463 and subsection 465(4)
of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations permitting the use of the name of an
association in a document in connection with a transaction related to
securities](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-260), effective June 1, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942178 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Name Use (Cooperative Credit Association) Regulations.

## [Permitted Use of Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942180 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person may use the name
of an association in a prospectus, an offering memorandum, a takeover
bid circular, an advertisement for a transaction related to securities
or in any other document in connection with a transaction related to
securities where the use is required by law or the association has given
its express written permission for the use.
