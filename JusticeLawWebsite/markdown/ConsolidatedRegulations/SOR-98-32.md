## Regulations Respecting the Customs Duty Payable on Woollen Fabrics Originating in Commonwealth Countries {#regulations-respecting-the-customs-duty-payable-on-woollen-fabrics-originating-in-commonwealth-countries .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-32
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Regulations Respecting the Customs Duty Payable on Woollen Fabrics
Originating in Commonwealth Countries

P.C. 1997-2003  1997-12-29
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 17(2) and paragraph
132(1)(c) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Regulations Respecting the Customs Duty Payable on Woollen Fabrics
Originating in Commonwealth Countries](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-32).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA){#fn_ae}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022383 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[British country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[British country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the United Kingdom of
    Great Britain and Northern Ireland, any British colony, possession
    or protectorate, or any territory under British trusteeship. ([pays
    britannique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commonwealth country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commonwealth country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country listed
    in the schedule. ([pays du Commonwealth]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022387 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the rate of customs duty set under paragraph 1(b) of
Supplementary Note 1 to Section XI of the List of Tariff Provisions to
woollen fabrics imported from Commonwealth countries.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022389 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods
  imported under tariff item Nos. 5111.11.90, 5111.20.18, 5111.20.91,
  5111.30.18, 5111.30.91, 5111.90.28, 5111.90.91, 5112.11.90,
  5112.19.91, 5112.20.91, 5112.30.91, 5112.90.91 and 5803.90.19 from
  Commonwealth countries are eligible for the rate of customs duty set
  under paragraph 1(b) of Supplementary Note 1 to Section XI of the List
  of Tariff Provisions on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not less than 50% of the cost of the production of
    the goods was incurred by the industries of one or more Commonwealth
    countries or the industry of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods were finished in a Commonwealth country
    in the form in which they were imported into Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof of origin of the goods is given in accordance
    with the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 4, the goods are shipped
    directly to Canada, with or without transhipment, from a
    Commonwealth country.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the cost of production for the purpose
  of paragraph (1)(a), the costs of the following shall not be included:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} outside packing and expenses related to it,
    required for the transportation of the goods, not including packing
    in which the goods are ordinarily sold for consumption;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gross profit of the manufacturer or exporter and
    the profit or remuneration of any trader, broker or other person
    dealing in the article in its finished manufactured condition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} royalties;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} customs or excise duty or tax paid or payable on
    imported materials;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} carriage, insurance and other charges incurred to
    transfer the goods from the place of production or manufacture in
    the country of origin to the port of shipment; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other costs or charges incurred or likely to be
    incurred after the completion of the manufacture of the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-325, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding paragraph
3(1)(d), goods that originate in Lesotho, Botswana or Swaziland are
deemed to be shipped directly to Canada from the country in which they
originate if they are shipped directly on a through bill of lading from
South Africa to a consignee in Canada with or without transhipment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-325, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/98-325, s.
3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022409 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
