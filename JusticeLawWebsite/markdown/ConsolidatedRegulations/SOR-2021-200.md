## Exemptions from and Modifications to Hours of Work Provisions Regulations {#exemptions-from-and-modifications-to-hours-of-work-provisions-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-200
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Registration 2021-08-12

Exemptions from and Modifications to Hours of Work Provisions
Regulations

P.C. 2021-870 2021-08-11
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
\
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the application
of certain provisions of Division I of Part III of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862-ID0EBAGA},
without modification, to certain classes of employees who are employed
in or in connection with the operation of certain industrial
establishments would be or is unduly prejudicial to the interests of the
employees in those classes or would be or is seriously detrimental to
the operation of those industrial establishments;

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that certain provisions
of Division I of Part III of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862-ID0EBAFA}
cannot reasonably be applied to certain classes of employees;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to paragraphs
175(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27864){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27864-ID0EKAEA}
and (b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_28025){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_28025-ID0EIAEA}
and subsection 270(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_29443){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_29443-ID0EGAEA}
of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862){#fn_81000-2-4139-E_hq_27862-ID0EDAEA},
makes the annexed [Exemptions from and Modifications to Hours of Work
Provisions Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-200).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1315627 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Road Transportation Sector and Postal and Courier Sector]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1315630 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
persons who are employed in the road transportation sector or the postal
and courier sector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport of goods

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following employees in
the road transportation sector who operate a motor vehicle that is used
to transport goods and that has a gross combination weight rating in
excess of 4 500 kg are exempt from the application of sections 169.1,
169.2, 173.01 and 173.1 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[city motor vehicle
  operators]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._990); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[highway motor vehicle
  operators]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of those
  Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transport of mail or parcels

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following employees in the postal and courier sector who operate a
  motor vehicle that is used to transport mail or parcels are exempt
  from the application of section 173.1 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[city motor vehicle
    operators]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Motor Vehicle Operators Hours of Work
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._990); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[highway motor vehicle
    operators]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of those
    Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification --- subsection 169.1(1) of
  the Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With respect to employees referred to in subsection
  (1), subsection 169.1(1) of the Act is modified as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Break
  >
  > - **169.1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every employee is entitled to and shall
  >   be granted an unpaid break of at least 30 minutes for every period
  >   of five consecutive hours of work. The employer may grant this
  >   break at any time during the work period or shift and it may be
  >   divided into periods of at least 15 minutes. If the employer
  >   requires the employee to be at their disposal during the break
  >   period, the employee is to be paid for the break.
  > :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motor coach operators

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Motor
  coach operators who are not employed by a municipal or provincial
  transit authority are exempt from the application of sections 169.1,
  169.2, 173.01 and 173.1 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[motor
  coach]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, [[motor
  coach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bus that is designed for intercity
  passenger transport and that is not equipped with features to
  accommodate standing passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Armoured car crew members

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Armoured car crew members
are exempt from the application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warehouse workers, shippers or receivers

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following employees are
exempt from the application of section 173.1 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} warehouse workers who handle, move, load and unload
  materials by hand or by means of material handling equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shippers; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} receivers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispatchers

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Dispatchers are exempt from
the application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mechanics

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Mechanics are exempt from
the application of section 173.1 of the Act.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Grain Sector]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1315774 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
persons who are employed in the grain sector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevator operators

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Elevator operators,
inland terminal elevator operators and port terminal elevator operators
who are employed in grain handling facilities are exempt from the
application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Railcar spotters, warehouse workers and
grain receivers

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Railcar spotters,
warehouse workers and grain receivers who are employed in grain handling
facilities are exempt from the application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Millwrights, electricians, power
engineers and welders

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Millwrights,
electricians, power engineers and welders who are employed in grain
handling or grain milling facilities are exempt from the application of
section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laboratory technicians and supervisors

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Laboratory technicians
and laboratory supervisors who are employed in grain handling or grain
milling facilities are exempt from the application of section 173.1 of
the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grading, quality assurance and inspection
of grain

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Employees who are
involved in the grading, quality assurance and inspection of grain and
who are employed in grain handling facilities are exempt from the
application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grain cleaning operators

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Grain cleaning operators
who are employed in grain milling facilities are exempt from the
application of section 173.1 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Millers, assistant millers and other
operators

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Millers, assistant
millers, packing equipment operators and bulk load out operators who are
employed in grain milling facilities are exempt from the application of
section 173.1 of the Act.
