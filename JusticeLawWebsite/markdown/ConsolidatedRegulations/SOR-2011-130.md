## Expiry of the Application of Section 12.1 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations {#expiry-of-the-application-of-section-12.1-of-the-softwood-lumber-products-export-charge-act-2006-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-130
:::

[SOFTWOOD LUMBER PRODUCTS EXPORT CHARGE ACT, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)

Registration 2011-06-23

Expiry of the Application of Section 12.1 of the Softwood Lumber
Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations

P.C. 2011-732 2011-06-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 102 of the
[Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1592_hq_10830){#fn_1592_hq_10830-ID0EDBAA}
hereby makes the annexed [Expiry of the Application of Section 12.1 of
the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-130).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1592_hq_10830-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1592_hq_10830}S.C.
  2006, c. 13
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 12.1 of the
[Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55)
ceases to be in force on July 1, 2011.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 1, 2011.
