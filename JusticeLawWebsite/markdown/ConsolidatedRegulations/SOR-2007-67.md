## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741488 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations
    set out in Schedule III to the [Foreign Missions and International
    Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention des Nations
    Unies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Egmont Group of
    Financial Intelligence Units. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the
    [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention de Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741493 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and the privileges and immunities set out in Articles II and
  III of the United Nations Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization shall have in Canada,
  to the extent that may be required for the exercise of their functions
  in relation to the Organization, the privileges and immunities set out
  in section 18 of Article V of the United Nations Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the privileges and immunities accorded
  to officials under subsection (2), the Executive Secretary of the
  Secretariat of the Organization, together with the members of his or
  her family forming part of his or her household, shall have in Canada
  privileges and immunities comparable to the privileges and immunities
  accorded to diplomatic agents and the members of their families
  forming part of their households under the Vienna Convention.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The privileges and
immunities referred to in subsections 2(2) and (3) do not apply to
Canadian citizens or permanent residents of Canada in respect of their
liability for taxes or duties imposed by any law in Canada.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not
restrict the capacity of the Government of Canada to refuse officials of
the Organization entry to Canada or to exclude them from Canada, for
reasons linked to national security.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741500 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
