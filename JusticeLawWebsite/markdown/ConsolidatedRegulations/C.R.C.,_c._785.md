[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Representational Gifts Remission Order

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562011 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Order,

[[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person acting in his capacity
    as a Head of State, Head of Government or representative of a
    government or public body of a foreign country or of a political
    subdivision thereof; ([personnalités étrangères]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[representational gift]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representational gift]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any article
    presented or to be presented by a donor. ([présent
    officiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562015 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
additional duties paid or payable under sections 21.2 and 21.3 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), of the duties paid or payable
under the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1) and of the taxes paid or
payable under any Parts of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) other
than Part IX in respect of the importation of a representational gift
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is presented to a donee in the course of their
  official visit outside Canada if they are acting in the capacity of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Governor General,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Prime Minister of Canada,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a minister of the Government of Canada,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Senate or House of Commons,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a provincial premier, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a municipal mayor; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is to be presented by a donor in the course of their
  official visit to Canada and is subsequently so presented.
