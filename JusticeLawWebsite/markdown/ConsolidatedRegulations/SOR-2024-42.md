## [Definitions and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1447831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who gives security
    under the Act or the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([débiteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[security agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    between a debtor and a security provider under which the security
    provider guarantees payment of an amount that the debtor owes under
    the Act or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([contrat de
    garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    guarantees payment of amounts that a debtor owes under the Act or
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([fournisseur de
    garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
security that is required under the Act or the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1447840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nature of security

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 166(1)(b) of the Act, security that is required to be given
must be a deposit or a written security agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security agreement --- deemed terms and
conditions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following terms and conditions are deemed to be an integral part of
  every security agreement and take precedence in the case of any
  conflict or inconsistency with any other provision of the security
  agreement:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the debtor and the security provider represent and
    warrant that the security agreement constitutes a valid and legally
    binding obligation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the debtor has failed to pay an amount that the
    debtor owes under the Act or the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) by the day on which it is payable, the
    security provider must pay to His Majesty in right of Canada, in
    accordance with section 9, the lesser of the amount that the debtor
    owes, subject to the limit set out in subsection 8(5), and the
    amount of the security entered or confirmed by the security provider
    under paragraph 5(2)(e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security provider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The security provider that is a party to a security
  agreement must be one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a company or society that, by an order of the
    Superintendent of Financial Institutions made under subsection 53(1)
    of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), is approved to
    commence and carry on the fidelity or surety class of insurance
    business in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is licensed or otherwise authorized
    under the laws of a province to carry on the fidelity or surety
    class of insurance business in that province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association
    referred to in section 4 of the [Canadian Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/C-21);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits insured by the
    Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Autorité des marchés
    financiers to the maximum permitted by the statutes under which
    those institutions were established;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a [[credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
    in subsection 137(6) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits from the
    public, if repayment of the deposits is guaranteed by His Majesty in
    right of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic means

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), all security must be given by means of the
  electronic system specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security agreement --- information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a security agreement, the security is
  given when the security provider enters or confirms the following
  information by means of the electronic system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a number or any other identifier for the agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the relevant Agency program;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicable business number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the security;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legislative authority under which the security
    is given; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the validity period of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require that security be given by
  any other means that is made available or specified by the Minister
  for that purpose if the Minister determines that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the infrastructure is inadequate or incompatible
    with the electronic system specified by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural disaster, national crisis or any other
    exceptional circumstance prevents or impedes the use of the
    electronic system or makes using it unreliable; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is impracticable for a debtor, due to
    circumstances beyond their control, to give security by means of the
    electronic system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of security agreement

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** On the Minister's request,
a debtor or security provider must provide a copy of the security
agreement to the Minister by means of the electronic system specified by
the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of security agreement

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a security agreement is
to be terminated, the security provider must notify the Minister of the
termination date by means of the electronic system specified by the
Minister at least 30 days before that date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of security

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) to (5), the Minister may demand that the security
  provider pay the security given in accordance with subsection 5(2) if
  the debtor has failed to pay an amount that the debtor owes under the
  Act or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) by the day on which it
  is payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demand

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The demand must be sent to the security provider in
  writing and contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment demanded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information that the security provider entered
    or confirmed under subsection 5(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period or prescription

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a security agreement that has expired
  or been terminated, a demand under subsection (1) may only be made
  within one year after the day on which the security agreement expired
  or was terminated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The demand must be for an amount equal to the lesser
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount that the debtor owes under the Act or
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the security entered or confirmed by
    the security provider under paragraph 5(2)(e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit --- amount

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (4)(a), the amount that
  the debtor owes includes only those amounts that have accrued to the
  debtor under the Act or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
  during the validity period referred to in paragraph 5(2)(g) and that
  are payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment by security provider

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  60 days after the day on which a demand is sent to a security provider
  under subsection 8(2), the security provider must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay to His Majesty in right of Canada the amount
    referred to in paragraph 8(2)(a); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister with information to rebut the
    demand.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the security provider provides information under
  paragraph (1)(b), the Minister must review the information, determine
  whether to continue with, amend or withdraw the demand and must send
  the security provider notice of that determination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment after determination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the notice referred to in subsection (2) indicates
  that an amount must be paid, the security provider must pay that
  amount to His Majesty in right of Canada on the day on which the
  notice is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disputes --- security provider and
  debtor

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a security provider is
  required to make a payment to His Majesty in right of Canada under
  this section despite any dispute between the security provider and the
  debtor with respect to any matter related to the security or the
  security agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other amounts owed

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
the payment of an amount by a security provider to His Majesty in right
of Canada under paragraph 9(1)(a) or subsection 9(3) does not relieve
the debtor or any other person from liability to pay any amount
otherwise owed.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1447905 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of security

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to security, other than security given under the [Accounting for
Imported Goods and Payment of Duties
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062), that has been given under
the Act or the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and that is valid
immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into force.
The security continues to be valid until the day on which it expires or
is terminated.
