:::

[TOBACCO AND VAPING PRODUCTS ACT](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

Registration 2000-06-26

Tobacco Reporting Regulations

P.C. 2000-1040 2000-06-21

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 42.1 of the Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Health laid a copy of the proposed [Tobacco Reporting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-273), substantially in the
annexed form, before the House of Commons on May 12, 2000 and the House
of Commons concurred on June 8, 2000 in a report from the Standing
Committee on Health approving the proposed Regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to sections 7 and 33
of the Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Tobacco Reporting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-273).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-662450 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[accredited representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accredited representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    is entitled to the tax exemptions specified in Article 34 of the
    Convention set out in Schedule I to the [Foreign Missions and
    International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4) or Article 49 of
    the Convention set out in Schedule II to that Act. ([représentant
    accrédité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
    Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[brand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all of the brand elements that
    as a whole are used by a manufacturer to identify to a consumer a
    tobacco product made by the manufacturer. ([marque]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the identification
    number that is assigned to a chemical substance by the Chemical
    Abstracts Service Division of the American Chemical
    Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a roll or tubular construction
    intended for smoking, other than a little cigar, that contains a
    filler composed of natural or reconstituted tobacco, and that has a
    wrapper, or a wrapper and a binder, composed of natural or
    reconstituted tobacco. ([cigare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any roll or tubular
    construction that contains tobacco and is intended for smoking,
    other than a bidi, cigar, kretek or little
    cigar. ([cigarette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an address in Canada, the unit number, civic
      number, street name, municipality name, province name and postal
      code; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an address outside Canada, the unit number,
      civic number, street name, municipality name, state or province
      name, postal or ZIP code and country name. ([adresse
      municipale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constituent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constituent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a constituent listed in
    column 1 of Schedule 1. ([constituant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumer tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following types of tobacco product that is for use by a consumer:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bidis;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cigarettes;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cigarette tobacco;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} cigars;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} kreteks;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} little cigars;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} pipe tobacco;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} smokeless tobacco. ([produit du tabac pour
      consommation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means either of
    the following types of consumer tobacco product:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cigarettes;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} kreteks. ([produit du tabac
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duty free shop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duty free shop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([boutique
    hors taxes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in the case of an emission
    contained in mainstream smoke, an emission listed in column 1 of
    Schedule 2 and, in the case of an emission contained in sidestream
    smoke, an emission listed in column 1 of Schedule
    3. ([émission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equivalent unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equivalent unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-64, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[identical products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[identical products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2019-64, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-64, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-64, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[mainstream smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mainstream smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the smoke that is
    drawn through the port of a smoking machine when a tobacco product
    is placed in the machine and combusted. ([fumée
    principale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an individual
    or entity that only packages, only labels or only distributes
    tobacco products on behalf of a
    manufacturer. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new brand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new brand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brand of consumer tobacco product that a
      manufacturer sells in a year and that was not referred to in a
      report submitted under section 13 for any portion of the preceding
      year; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brand of cigarette papers, tubes or filters
      that a manufacturer sells in a year and that was not referred to
      in a report submitted under section 11 for the preceding year.

    It does not include any product that was mentioned under a different
    brand name in a report referred to in paragraph (a) or
    (b). ([nouvelle marque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sidestream smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sidestream smoke]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the smoke, other than
    mainstream smoke, that leaves a tobacco product when the product is
    placed in a smoking machine and combusted. ([fumée
    latérale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[smokeless tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[smokeless tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means chewing tobacco,
    nasal snuff and oral snuff. ([tabac sans fumée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[type of package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[type of package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes each of the
    following types of packages as well as each of their sizes:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} slide and shell package;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} slide and shell package with lateral moving
      slide;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} flip-top package;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} soft package;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} pouch;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} bag;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} can;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} tub;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tube;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} flip-top box;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} bundle;

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} plastic or metal container;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} shoulder box. ([type
      d'emballage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[type of tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[type of tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the types of
    tobacco leaves commonly known as

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Virginia flue-cured;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Maryland;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Burley; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Oriental. ([type de tabac]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-64, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    year. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-662551 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations prescribe the information that manufacturers of tobacco
  products are required to submit to the Minister, as well as the manner
  in which it is to be submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information required for each type
  of report set out in these Regulations, every report must set out the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer on whose behalf the
    report is submitted, as well as the telephone number, email address
    and civic address of its principal place of business in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    individual who prepared the report and, if that individual works for
    an entity other than a manufacturer on whose behalf the report is
    submitted, the name of the entity, as well as the telephone number,
    email address and civic address of its principal place of business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the period covered by the report; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the section of these Regulations under which the
    report is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The individual who prepared the report must attach to
  the report an attestation that states that the information in the
  report is true and complete to the best of their knowledge and belief
  and is provided in good faith.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand name

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The manufacturer must use a unique brand name to
  identify each consumer tobacco product in a report submitted under
  section 13 and must use it in a uniform manner in all other reports
  submitted in respect of the same consumer tobacco product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to brand name

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} However, a manufacturer may change the brand name
  used to identify a consumer tobacco product in a report by submitting
  a report under section 13 that identifies the brand name that was
  previously used, as well as the changed brand name.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- export

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The reports referred to in sections 10 to 12, 14, 15
  and 17 to 24 are not required for consumer tobacco products that are
  manufactured solely for export.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1117825 .Part}

### [Manufacturer Profile Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-662577 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of report

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  profile report on a manufacturer of tobacco products must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of every type of tobacco product that the
    manufacturer sells;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each type of tobacco product that the
    manufacturer sells, other than a consumer tobacco product, a list of
    brand names used to identify the brands of tobacco products that the
    manufacturer sells; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of each warehouse in
    Canada that the manufacturer uses to store tobacco products for
    distribution and sale.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Digital images

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include, for each brand of consumer
  tobacco product that the manufacturer sells, a digital image of the
  following items:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each type of package, showing all of the sides that
    are visible when the package is closed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any promotion that is on an interior side of the
    package or that accompanies the package;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any overwrap on which a promotion is displayed,
    that is used over a package or carton or that is used as a carton;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any brand element displayed on a unit of the
    consumer tobacco product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Digital image characteristics

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The digital image of an item must be taken on a white
  background and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be clear and display colours that are as close as
    possible to the colours of the item;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} display any text that appears on the item, in
    legible characters;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include the brand name associated with the item;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a graphical scale in millimetres.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- new manufacturer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must be submitted on or before the day on
  which a manufacturer begins to sell tobacco products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- existing brands

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} However, in the case of a brand of consumer tobacco
  product that is sold on the day on which this subsection comes into
  force, or that was sold at any time during the preceding year or the
  portion of the year before that day, the report must be submitted on
  or before the 180th day after the day on which this subsection comes
  into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- changes to information

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If any of the information referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(a) to (c) is changed by the manufacturer, the manufacturer must
  advise the Minister of the change by submitting, on or before January
  31 of the following year, a description of the information that was
  previously submitted, as well as of the change made to the
  information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- changes to items

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the appearance of any package, promotion, overwrap
  or brand element referred to in subsection (2) is changed by the
  manufacturer, the manufacturer must, within 30 days after the day on
  which the consumer tobacco product is sold using that changed item,
  advise the Minister of the change by submitting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the digital image previously submitted, as well as
    a description of the change made to the item; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a digital image of the changed item.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- new brand

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer must, on or before the day on which
  the manufacturer first sells a new brand of consumer tobacco product
  in a year, submit the digital images referred to in subsection (2).

### [Report on Manufacturing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-662595 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacturing processes

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  report on manufacturing must set out, by brand, the following
  information in respect of the manufacturing processes for each
  consumer tobacco product that a manufacturer sells, as well as for any
  cigarette papers, filters or mouthpieces or tips used in the
  manufacture of those consumer tobacco products:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description and schematic of the
    sequential steps that are involved in the manufacture of the
    consumer tobacco product, cigarette paper, filter or mouthpiece or
    tip, including any steps where additives, such as processing aids,
    are used; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the conditions and
    parameters relating to each of those steps.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Processing aids

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must set out the common, chemical and
  brand name of each processing aid and, if applicable, its CAS registry
  number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Product specifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must include, for each brand of
  cigarettes, kreteks and little cigars that the manufacturer sells,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of cigarette paper used in the product,
    and its specifications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of any filter used in the product, and its
    specifications and pressure drop, as well as, for those brands
    analyzed in accordance with subsection 14(13), its efficiency with
    respect to nicotine, determined in accordance with Official Method
    T-106, entitled Determination of Filter Efficiency in Mainstream
    Tobacco Smoke, prepared by the Department of Health, dated December
    31, 2017; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an engineering drawing of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- existing brands

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a brand of consumer tobacco product
  that is sold on the day on which this subsection comes into force, or
  that was sold at any time during the preceding year or the portion of
  the year before that day, the report must be submitted on or before
  the 180th day after the day on which this subsection comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- new brand

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer must, on or before the day on which
  the manufacturer first sells a new brand of consumer tobacco product
  in a year, submit a report in respect of the new brand.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- sales volume of cigars

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} However, in the case of a brand of cigars, the
  manufacturer is not required to submit the report until the
  manufacturer's total sales of that brand in a year is at least 1 000
  000 units. In that case, the report must be submitted on or before
  January 31 of the following year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- identical manufacturing
  processes

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A manufacturer is not required to submit a report in
  respect of a particular brand of consumer tobacco product if the
  manufacturer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses identical manufacturing processes to
    manufacture a consumer tobacco product that is sold under more than
    one brand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submits a report under this section in respect of
    one of those brands; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identifies in the report all other brands that are
    manufactured using those identical manufacturing processes,
    including the particular brand.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of bidis, cigars, kreteks or little
    cigars, the procedures referred to in paragraph (a), modified in the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of pipe tobacco or smokeless tobacco,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of sidestream smoke, the applicable

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2019-64, s. 41

  - ###### Definitions {#definitions .MarginalNote}

    [41]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section and
    sections 42 to 44.

    [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco
        Reporting Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-273), as they
        read immediately before the day on which these Regulations come
        into force. ([règlement antérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quarter, month
        or semi-annual or annual period that is referred to in
        subsection 11(4), 13(4), 15(1), 15(3) or 16(1) of the former
        Regulations and in respect of which a report is required to be
        submitted to the Minister under those Regulations. ([période de
        déclaration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- --- SOR/2019-64, s. 42

  - ###### Sections 12, 14 and 14.2 {#sections-12-14-and-14.2 .MarginalNote}

    [42]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 6, 10 and 11 of these Regulations,
    a manufacturer may report to the Minister, in accordance with the
    former Regulations, any data obtained in accordance with section 12,
    14 or 14.2 of the former Regulations from the analysis of tobacco
    products performed during the period beginning on January 1, 2019
    and ending on December 31, 2019.

- --- SOR/2019-64, s. 43

  - ###### Sections 11, 13 and 15 {#sections-11-13-and-15 .MarginalNote}

    [43]{.lawlabel} Despite these Regulations, every manufacturer must
    report to the Minister, in accordance with the former Regulations,
    the information required under sections 11, 13 and 15 of the former
    Regulations in respect of the reporting period during which these
    Regulations come into force. For greater certainty, any analyses
    performed under subparagraph 11(3)(b)(ix) of the former Regulations
    for the purpose of reporting in respect of a filter may be performed
    after the day on which these Regulations come into force.

- --- SOR/2019-64, s. 44

  - ###### Sections 16 to 24 --- if these Regulations come into force during the first half of 2019 {#sections-16-to-24-if-these-regulations-come-into-force-during-the-first-half-of-2019 .MarginalNote}

    - [44]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite these Regulations, every
      manufacturer must report to the Minister, in accordance with the
      former Regulations, the information required under sections 16 to
      24 of the former Regulations in respect of any reporting period
      that occurs during the portion of 2019 beginning on January 1 and
      ending on June 30 if these Regulations come into force during that
      portion of the year.

    - ###### Sections 16 to 24 --- if these Regulations come into force during the second half of 2019 {#sections-16-to-24-if-these-regulations-come-into-force-during-the-second-half-of-2019 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite these Regulations, every manufacturer
      must report to the Minister, in accordance with the former
      Regulations, the information required under sections 16 to 24 of
      the former Regulations in respect of any reporting period that
      occurs during the portion of 2019 beginning on July 1 and ending
      December 31 if these Regulations come into force during that
      portion of the year.

- --- SOR/2019-64, s. 45

  - ###### Paragraph 10(3)(b) {#paragraph-103b .MarginalNote}

    [45]{.lawlabel} Despite section 4 of these Regulations, during the
    period beginning on the day on which these Regulations come into
    force and ending on December 31, 2019, a manufacturer may determine
    filter efficiency with respect to nicotine as required under
    paragraph 10(3)(b) of the [Tobacco Reporting
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-273) by using Official Method
    T-106, entitled Determination of Filter Efficiency in Mainstream
    Tobacco Smoke, prepared by the Department of Health, dated December
    31, 1999.
