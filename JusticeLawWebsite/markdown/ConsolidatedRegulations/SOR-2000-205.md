## Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment Regulations {#class-ii-nuclear-facilities-and-prescribed-equipment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-205
:::

[NUCLEAR SAFETY AND CONTROL ACT](/eng/acts/N-28.3)

Registration 2000-05-31

CLASS II NUCLEAR FACILITIES AND PRESCRIBED EQUIPMENT REGULATIONS

P.C. 2000-785  2000-05-31
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section 44 of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)^a^, hereby approves
the annexed [Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-205) made by the Canadian Nuclear
Safety Commission on May 31, 2000.

## [Interpretation and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657226 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657227 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[brachytherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brachytherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2008-119, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

[[brachytherapy remote afterloader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[brachytherapy remote afterloader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    device that is designed to place, by remote control, a sealed source
    inside or in contact with a person for therapeutic purposes and to
    remove, by remote control, the sealed source once a preset dose of
    radiation has been delivered or after a preset time has elapsed.
    ([appareil de curiethérapie à projecteur de source
    télécommandé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document issued by the
    Commission or by a designated officer authorized under
    paragraph 37(2)(a) of the Act, indicating that a model of Class II
    prescribed equipment is certified, or authorized under
    paragraph 37(2)(b) of the Act, indicating that a person is
    certified. ([homologation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
    [accréditation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means certified by the Commission
    under paragraph 21(1)(h) or (i) of the Act or by a designated
    officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(a) or (b) of the Act.
    ([homologué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et
    [accrédité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility
    that includes Class II prescribed equipment. ([installation
    nucléaire de catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an irradiator that uses more than 10^15^ Bq of a
      nuclear substance;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an irradiator that requires shielding which is
      not part of the irradiator and that is designed to deliver a dose
      of radiation at a rate exceeding 1 cGy/min at a distance of 1 m;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a radioactive source teletherapy machine;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a particle accelerator that is capable of
      producing nuclear energy and has a beam energy of less than 50 MeV
      for beams of particles with a mass equal to or less than 4 atomic
      mass units;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a particle accelerator that is capable of
      producing nuclear energy and has a beam energy of no more than
      15 MeV per atomic mass unit for beams of particles with a mass
      greater than 4 atomic mass units; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a brachytherapy remote afterloader. ([équipement
      réglementé de catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance, other
    than a nuclear substance, that is used or produced in the course of
    carrying on a licensed activity and that may pose a risk to the
    environment or the health and safety of persons. ([substance
    dangereuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[irradiator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is designed
    to contain a nuclear substance and to deliver controlled doses of
    radiation from that substance to any target except persons.
    ([irradiateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    described in paragraph 26(a), (c) or (e) of the Act that a licence
    authorizes the licensee to carry on in relation to a Class II
    nuclear facility or Class II prescribed equipment. ([activité
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is licensed to
    carry on an activity described in any of paragraphs 26(a), (c)
    or (e) of the Act in relation to a Class II nuclear facility or
    Class II prescribed equipment. ([titulaire de
    permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiation survey meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiation survey meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument
    that is capable of measuring radiation dose rates.
    ([radiamètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radioactive source teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radioactive source teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a teletherapy machine that is designed to deliver doses of radiation
    produced by a nuclear substance. ([appareil de téléthérapie à source
    radioactive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sealed source]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radioactive nuclear
    substance in a sealed capsule or in a cover to which the substance
    is bonded, where the capsule or cover is strong enough to prevent
    contact with or the dispersion of the substance under the conditions
    for which the capsule or cover is designed. ([source
    scellée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[servicing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[servicing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of Class II prescribed
    equipment, means any maintenance of the equipment, including
    installation, repair or dismantling, other than any installation,
    repair or dismantling that constitutes routine operating procedures

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as indicated in the manufacturer's operating
      manual for the equipment; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as authorized in the licence issued in respect of
      the possession or use of the equipment.
      ([entretien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[teletherapy machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is
    designed to deliver controlled doses of radiation in a collimated
    beam for therapeutic purposes. ([appareil de
    téléthérapie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who performs work that
    is referred to in a licence. ([travailleur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 5
- SOR/2010-107, s. 1

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657256 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of Class II nuclear facilities and Class II prescribed
equipment.

## [Licence Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657258 .Part}

### [Class II Nuclear Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657259 .Subheading}

#### [Licence to Construct]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657260 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to construct a Class II nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by
section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed name and location of the nuclear
  facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the applicant is the owner of the
  proposed site or has authority from the owner of the site to construct
  the nuclear facility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the Class II prescribed equipment
  that is proposed to be used, including its design operating
  conditions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control access within the
  nuclear facility and any other safety-related features, including the
  schematics of the devices involved and their wiring;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plans, elevations and drawings of the
  nuclear facility, showing its layout, location, the location of its
  components and the location of adjacent areas that may be occupied by
  persons;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed purpose of the adjacent areas, including
  a description of their uses and the estimated levels of occupancy;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location, type, composition, thickness
  and density of shielding material, including the method that will be
  used to verify the composition and density and the calculations that
  will be used to determine the adequacy of the shielding;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed location and dimensions of voids in the
  shielding, including access ways and service ducts;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed ventilation system,
  including the ventilation flow rate, air circulation and location of
  intake and discharge points in respect of any irradiator or particle
  accelerator;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program for the design
  and construction of the nuclear facility;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the type and energy of radiation produced by any
  particle accelerator to be encompassed by the licence;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated maximum dose of radiation that may be
  received by any person as a result of the commissioning of and during
  the operation of the nuclear facility;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the direction of the direct beam of any teletherapy
  machine to be encompassed by the licence and a description of the
  physical means that may be used to limit the direction of that beam;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the expected and maximum radiological workloads
  during the commissioning and operation of the nuclear facility, per
  week in grays at 1 m, for any teletherapy machine to be encompassed by
  the licence;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated number of hours per week that the
  Class II prescribed equipment in the nuclear facility will be operated
  for the purpose of treatment, dosimetry, servicing or research;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the activity to be licensed;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} the proposed responsibilities of and qualification
  requirements and training program for workers during the operation of
  the nuclear facility;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} the program to inform persons living in the vicinity
  of the site of the general nature and characteristics of the
  anticipated effects on the environment and the health and safety of
  persons that may result from the nuclear facility; and

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plan for the decommissioning of the
  nuclear facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 6
:::

#### [Licence to Operate]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657283 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to operate a Class II nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by
section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and location of the nuclear facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed commissioning plan;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the results of any commissioning
  work;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the applicant is the owner of the site
  or has authority from the owner of the site to operate the nuclear
  facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the components, systems and
  equipment proposed to be used, including their design operating
  conditions;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures, policies, methods and
  procedures for operating and maintaining the nuclear facility;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed procedures for
  handling, storing, loading and transporting nuclear substances and
  hazardous substances;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the proposed quality assurance program;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type and energy of radiation produced by any
  particle accelerator to be encompassed by the licence;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to ensure that the type of beam
  producible by, and the maximum output energy, limits to the beam
  orientation and maximum radiation dose rate of, the Class II
  prescribed equipment cannot be inadvertently altered;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the proposed method for measuring the effectiveness
  of the shielding of the nuclear facility and any results of those
  measurements;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed portable radiation
  detection equipment, including its type, sensitivity, range and
  accuracy and the methods and procedures for calibrating it;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods, procedures and equipment for
  conducting the leak tests required by these Regulations;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} a description of any proposed area radiation
  monitoring system;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated maximum dose of radiation that may be
  received by any person as a result of the commissioning of the nuclear
  facility;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} the expected and maximum radiological workloads, per
  week in grays at 1 m, for any teletherapy machine to be encompassed by
  the licence;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated number of hours per week that the
  Class II prescribed equipment in the nuclear facility will be operated
  for the purpose of treatment, dosimetry, servicing or research;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods, procedures and equipment for
  conducting radiation surveys, including the frequency of the surveys
  and the location of radiation survey points;

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} the proposed responsibilities of and qualification
  requirements and training program for workers; and

- [(t)]{.lawlabel} the proposed plan for the decommissioning of the
  nuclear facility.

#### [Licence to Decommission]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657305 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to decommission a Class II nuclear facility shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by
section 3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and location of the nuclear facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed nature of and schedule for the
  decommissioning;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the land, buildings, structures, components, systems,
  equipment, nuclear substances and hazardous substances that will be
  affected by the decommissioning;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature, quantity and activity of any radioactive
  nuclear substances at the nuclear facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of any radioactive
  contamination at the nuclear facility, including radiation dose rates;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed measures to control releases of
  radioactive nuclear substances and hazardous substances into the
  environment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated maximum dose of radiation that may be
  received by any person as a result of the decommissioning;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantities and concentrations of nuclear
  substances and hazardous substances that may be released into the
  environment;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the effects on the environment and the health and
  safety of persons that may result from the decommissioning, and the
  measures that will be taken to prevent or mitigate those effects;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed responsibilities of and qualification
  requirements for workers; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the planned state of the site upon
  completion of the decommissioning.

### [Class II Prescribed Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657318 .Subheading}

#### [General Requirement]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657319 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence in respect of Class II prescribed equipment, other than a
licence to service, shall contain a copy of or the number of any
certificate relating to the equipment in addition to the information
required by section 3 and, where applicable, section 4 of the [General
Nuclear Safety and Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202).

#### [Licence to Service]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657321 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence to service Class II prescribed equipment shall contain the
following information in addition to the information required by section
3 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, model number and characteristics of the
  equipment or the number of the certificate relating to the equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the servicing proposed
  to be carried on;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed methods, procedures and equipment for
  carrying on the servicing;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed qualification requirements and training
  program for workers; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed procedures to be followed after
  completion of the servicing to confirm that the equipment is safe to
  use.

## [Exemptions from Licence Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657328 .Part}

### [Activities in Relation to Class II Nuclear Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657329 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person may carry on any
of the following activities without a licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a site for a Class II nuclear facility;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} construct, operate, modify, decommission or abandon a
  Class II nuclear facility that includes a geophysical logging particle
  accelerator; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} decommission a Class II nuclear facility that
  includes a brachytherapy remote afterloader.

### [Application for Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657347 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or a designated officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(a)
  of the Act may certify a model of Class II prescribed equipment after
  receiving an application that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant\'s name and business address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and business address of the manufacturer
    of the equipment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the brand name and model number of the equipment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the design of the equipment and its components,
    including any standards used in the design;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the intended use of the equipment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, quantity in becquerels and form of the
    nuclear substance to be incorporated into the equipment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the method of incorporating the nuclear substance
    into the equipment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the expected radiation dose rates around the
    equipment in all modes of operation, including the method,
    calculations and measurements used to establish them;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum radiation dose rate that the equipment
    can deliver;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} instructions for the use, transportation and
    storage of the equipment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} instructions for conducting leak tests on the
    equipment;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} instructions for dealing with accidents involving
    the equipment;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the labelling of the equipment;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the quality assurance program that was followed
    during the design of the equipment and that will be followed during
    the production of the equipment;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the type of package for and the procedures for
    packaging and transporting any equipment that may contain a nuclear
    substance, including the emergency response plan to be followed in
    case of a transportation accident involving the equipment;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the recommended inspection and servicing program
    for the equipment;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} the type of beam producible by, and the maximum
    output energy and limits to the beam orientation of, any particle
    accelerator or radioactive source teletherapy machine;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} the estimated maximum photon and neutron leakage
    during the use of any teletherapy machine; and

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} the half-lives of activated components of any
    particle accelerator and the radiation dose rate at 30 cm from those
    components.

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-119, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission or the designated officer may request
  any other information that is necessary to enable the Commission or
  the designated officer to determine whether the equipment model in
  question poses an unreasonable risk to the environment, the health and
  safety of persons or national security and whether certification of
  the equipment model would be in conformity with measures of control
  and international obligations to which Canada has agreed.

### [Refusal to Certify]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657373 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or a designated officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(a)
  of the Act shall notify a person who has applied for the certification
  of a model of Class II prescribed equipment of a proposed decision not
  to certify the model, as well as the basis for the proposed decision,
  at least 30 days before refusing to certify it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include a description of the
  person\'s right to be provided with an opportunity to be heard in
  accordance with the procedure referred to in section 14.

### [Decertification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657377 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission or a designated officer authorized under paragraph 37(2)(a)
  of the Act shall notify a person to whom a certificate for a model of
  Class II prescribed equipment has been issued, and any licensee who is
  licensed in respect of that model, of a proposed decision to decertify
  the model, as well as the basis for the proposed decision, at least 30
  days before decertifying it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include a description of the
  person\'s and the licensee\'s right to be provided with an opportunity
  to be heard in accordance with the procedure referred to in section
  14.

### [Opportunity To Be Heard]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657381 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person referred to in section 12 or 13 or a licensee referred to in
  section 13 has received a notice and has requested, within 30 days
  after the date of receipt of the notice, an opportunity to be heard
  either orally or in writing, the person or the licensee shall be
  provided with such an opportunity in accordance with the request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a hearing held in accordance with
  subsection (1), every person and licensee who was notified in
  accordance with section 12 or 13 shall be notified of the decision and
  the reasons for it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where neither a person referred to in section 12 or
  13 nor a licensee referred to in section 13 requests an opportunity to
  be heard within the period referred to in subsection (1), they shall
  be notified of the decision and the reasons for it.

## [Radiation Protection Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657386 .Part}

### [Class II Nuclear Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-657387 .Subheading}

#### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657388 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to licensees who operate Class II nuclear facilities
  other than facilities that include brachytherapy remote afterloaders
  that incorporate pure beta emitters as their only nuclear substances
  or facilities that include particle accelerators used for geophysical
  logging.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each entrance door to a room in which Class II
  prescribed equipment is located shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipped with a device that stops the equipment
    when the door is opened and prevents the equipment from being used
    when the door is open;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipped with a device that prevents the equipment
    from being used until a person activates the device from inside the
    room, leaves the room and closes the door within a preset time; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designed to prevent any person from being locked
    inside the room.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each entrance, other than a door, to a room in which
  Class II prescribed equipment is located shall be equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a device that stops the equipment when a person
    passes through the entrance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a device that prevents the equipment from being
    used until a person activates the device from inside the room and
    leaves the room within a preset time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every room in which Class II prescribed equipment
  that is used on persons is located shall be equipped with a viewing
  system that allows the operator to view the interior of the treatment
  room during operation of the equipment.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each entrance to a room in which Class II prescribed
  equipment is located shall be equipped with a readily visible display
  that indicates the irradiation state of the equipment.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every room in which Class II prescribed equipment ---
  other than a particle accelerator --- is located shall be equipped
  with an area radiation monitoring system that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the Class II prescribed
    equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produces an audible alarm when a person enters the
    room while the equipment is delivering a dose of radiation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has an independent back-up power supply.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every room in which Class II prescribed equipment
  that is not used on persons is located shall be equipped with a device
  that, before the irradiation begins, provides a continuous audible
  alarm of sufficient duration to enable a person inside the room to
  operate one of the emergency stop buttons or other emergency stop
  devices referred to in subsection (8).

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every room in which Class II prescribed equipment is
  located shall be equipped with emergency stop buttons or other
  emergency stop devices located as specified in subsections (9)
  and (10) that, when any one of them is operated, cause all Class II
  prescribed equipment in the room to automatically revert to a safe
  state until the safety circuit is reset from inside that room and a
  switch on the control console of the equipment is operated.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The emergency stop buttons or other emergency stop
  devices shall be unobstructed, accessible and located at a minimum in
  each of the following places:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the control console of all Class II prescribed
    equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} near each entrance to the room in which Class II
    prescribed equipment is located; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on both sides of all Class II prescribed equipment
    --- other than a brachytherapy remote afterloader --- or on the wall
    on both sides of the equipment.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a teletherapy machine, the emergency
  stop buttons or other emergency stop devices shall be unobstructed,
  accessible and located in accordance with paragraphs (9)(b) and (c) in
  places that are not in the machine's direct beam.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall post and keep posted at every
  entrance to a Class II nuclear facility a visible, durable and legible
  sign that indicates the name or job title and the telephone number of
  a person who can initiate any required emergency procedure and who can
  be contacted 24 hours a day.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Class II prescribed equipment shall be equipped with
  a key switch or code-operated device that prevents persons who are not
  authorized by the licensee from operating the equipment.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} After a device or system referred to in subsection
  (2), (3), (5), (6), (7) or (8) is serviced, the licensee shall not use
  the Class II prescribed equipment until the licensee performs a test
  or an inspection that establishes that the device is functioning as
  prescribed by those subsections.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (2), (3) and (7) and paragraphs (9)(b)
  and (c) do not apply in respect of a particle accelerator that meets
  at least one of the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its radiation dose rate at 30 cm is not greater
    than 200 µSv per hour when it is being operated in the manner that
    produces the maximum dose rate as limited either by its
    characteristics or by its interlocks, and it is located in a room
    that is equipped with a lock and that can be unlocked and entered
    only by persons who have been authorized by the licensee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its radiation dose rate at 30 cm is not greater
    than 25 µSv per hour when it is being operated in the manner that
    produces the maximum dose rate as limited either by its
    characteristics or by its interlocks.

- [(15)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (2)(b), (3)(b) and (9)(b) do not apply in
  respect of a brachytherapy remote afterloader that contains a total
  quantity of nuclear substance that, if exposed, would produce a
  radiation dose rate in air of less than 10 mGy per hour at a distance
  of 1 m.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-119, s. 10
- SOR/2010-108, s. 1(F)

#### [Sealed Source Installation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657499 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who installs a sealed source in any Class II prescribed
  equipment other than a pool-type irradiator shall, after installing
  the source, take measurements of radiation dose rates when the
  equipment is not in the irradiation mode and notify the Commission in
  writing as soon as practicable if the dose rate at any location that
  is 1 m from any sealed source in its shielded position exceeds the
  manufacturer's specifications.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who possesses a radioactive source
  teletherapy machine shall, after the installation of a sealed source
  in the machine, take measurements of radiation dose rates at all
  accessible locations outside the room in which the machine is located,
  with the machine in the irradiation mode and operating under
  conditions that will result in the maximum dose rate at each location.

#### [Radiation Survey Meters]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657506 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who uses, decommissions or services Class II prescribed
  equipment shall make available to each worker a radiation survey meter
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been calibrated within the 12 months preceding
    its use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is capable of measuring the gamma, X-ray and, if
    applicable, neutron radiation from the sealed source and the Class
    II prescribed equipment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indicates whether the power level of its batteries
    is sufficient for its operation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use, for the purposes of the Act, the
  regulations made under the Act or an order or a licence, a radiation
  survey meter that has not been calibrated within the 12 months
  preceding its use.

#### [Leak Tests]{.HTitleText3} {#h-657515 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee who possesses or uses, in or for Class II prescribed
  equipment, either a sealed source containing 50 MBq or more of a
  nuclear substance or a nuclear substance as shielding shall, at the
  following times, conduct leak tests on the sealed source or shielding
  using instruments and procedures that enable the licensee to detect a
  leakage of 200 Bq or less of the nuclear substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the sealed source or shielding is used after
    being stored for 12 or more consecutive months, immediately before
    using it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the sealed source or shielding is being stored,
    every 24 months;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if an event that may have damaged the sealed source
    or shielding has occurred, immediately after the event; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the sealed source or shielding is located
      inside Class II prescribed equipment, every 12 months, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the sealed source or shielding is not located
      inside Class II prescribed equipment, every six months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a licensee, in the course of conducting a leak
  test on a sealed source or on shielding, detects the leakage of 200 Bq
  or more of a nuclear substance, the licensee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} discontinue using the sealed source or shielding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discontinue using the Class II prescribed equipment
    in which the sealed source or shielding is located or may have been
    located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take measures to limit the spread of radioactive
    contamination from the sealed source or shielding; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately after complying with paragraphs (a) to
    (c), notify the Commission that the leakage has been detected.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a sealed
  source that is used or stored underwater in a Class II nuclear
  facility that consists of a pool-type irradiator equipped with a
  device capable of detecting water-borne contamination of 200 Bq or
  less of a nuclear substance.

- SOR/2008-119, s. 15

## [Records to Be Kept and Retained]{.HTitleText1} {#h-657534 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall keep a record of each measurement of the radiation dose
  rates required by the licence and retain the record for the period
  ending three years after the earlier of the expiry date and the date
  of revocation, if any, of the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who uses Class II prescribed equipment
  shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the daily output of radiation resulting from the
    operation of the equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the training received by each worker, including the
    date and subject of the training; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any inspection, verification, servicing,
    measurement or test required by the Act, the regulations made under
    the Act or the licence, including a description of the actions
    performed, the date on which they were performed and the results.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall retain a record of the training
  referred to in paragraph (2)(b) for the period that the worker is
  employed by the licensee.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who transfers Class II prescribed
  equipment shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transfer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence number of the person to whom the
    equipment was transferred; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the model and serial number of the equipment.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall keep a record of each leak test
  conducted on a sealed source or on shielding in accordance with
  section 19 and that person shall retain the record for the period
  ending three years after the date on which it is made.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall keep a record of each
  measurement of the radiation dose rates made under subsections 17(1)
  and (2) and retain the record for the period ending three years after
  the earlier of the expiry date and the date of revocation, if any, of
  the licence.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee who holds a licence to service Class
  II prescribed equipment shall keep a record of the following
  information in respect of each servicing performed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the client for whom the
    servicing was performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence number of the client for whom the
    servicing was performed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the brand name, model number and serial number of
    the prescribed equipment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the work and the date on which the
    servicing was performed.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Every person who is required to keep a record
  referred to in subsection (7) shall retain the record for the period
  ending three years after the earlier of the expiry date and the date
  of revocation, if any, of the licence.

- SOR/2008-119, s. 16
