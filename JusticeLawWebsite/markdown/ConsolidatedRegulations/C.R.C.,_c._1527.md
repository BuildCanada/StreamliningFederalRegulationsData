## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527314 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to territorial lands under the control, management and
administration of the Minister.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** In Nunavut, a permit or
lease authorizing the quarrying of carving stone on Crown lands for
carving purposes, or for the disposal of carving stone for carving
purposes, may only be granted to a Designated Inuit Organization, in
accordance with the Agreement Between the Inuit of the Nunavut
Settlement Area and Her Majesty the Queen in Right of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-131, s. 4
:::

## [Staking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527319 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who desires to obtain a lease of territorial lands for the purpose of
  taking material from those lands shall stake those lands in the manner
  prescribed in this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of loam, the area staked shall not exceed
  8.1 hectares, and in the case of any other material, the area shall
  not exceed 64.8 hectares.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The length of the area staked shall not exceed
  twice its width.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The area staked shall be rectangular in form except
  where a boundary of a previously staked tract is adopted as common to
  both areas.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The land shall be marked by the applicant with posts
  firmly fixed in the ground, one at each corner; in areas where there
  is no timber, rock cairns may be used in lieu of posts.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each post shall be at least 10 centimetres square and
  when firmly planted shall be at least 1.2 metres above the ground.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Each post shall bear markings showing the number of
  the post, the name of the applicant, the date of staking and the type
  of material to be removed.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a rock cairn is used, it shall be well constructed
  and at least 60 centimetres high and 60 centimetres in diameter at the
  base. A metal container shall be built into the cairn and shall
  contain a document bearing the number of the cairn, the name of the
  applicant, the date of staking and the type of material to be removed.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} In a timbered area, the lines between the posts shall
  be clearly marked.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall post a written or printed notice
  on a post or in a cairn setting out their intention to apply for a
  lease within the time prescribed by these Regulations.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} If two or more persons apply for the same area, the
  person who first staked the area in accordance with these Regulations
  shall be entitled to priority in respect of the issuance of a lease.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-131, s. 5

## [Leases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527334 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Territorial lands
containing material may be leased by the Minister for the sole purpose
of quarrying or removing material that is specified in the lease.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-131, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a lease shall be filed with the territorial land agent
  of the land district in which the land is situated within 30 days from
  the date upon which it was staked.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every lease application shall be accompanied by the
  application fee set out in Schedule I and the rental for the first
  year of the lease at the rate set out in Schedule II.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every application for a lease shall be in duplicate
  and shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description by metes and bounds of the land
    applied for;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of material that the applicant desires to
    remove from the area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a sketch showing clearly the position of the parcel
    in relation to a survey monument, prominent topographical feature or
    other known point and showing in its margin, copies of the markings
    on the posts or cairns; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit by the applicant setting forth

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that they have complied with all the provisions
      of these Regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the land contains material of the type
      applied for in merchantable quantities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-112, s. 1
- SOR/2016-131, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The term of a lease shall
not exceed 10 years.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A lessee shall, within such
time from the date of the lease as the Minister may fix, commence the
removal of the material or materials in merchantable quantities from the
area under lease and shall continue the removal of materials to an
extent and in a manner satisfactory to the Minister.

## [Renewal of Lease]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527352 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where, in the opinion of
the Minister, the lessee has complied with the terms of their lease and
these Regulations, the Minister may renew the lease for a further term
not exceeding 10 years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-131, s. 8(E)

## [Loam, Sand, Gravel and Stone for Use of Residents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527356 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), any
  person resident in the Northwest Territories or Nunavut may, without
  any charge or permit, take from territorial lands in any calendar year
  not more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 10 cubic metres of loam; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 40 cubic metres of sand, gravel or stone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no interest in the surface rights of the affected
    lands has been licensed, leased or otherwise disposed of by the
    Crown; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the material taken is for the taker's own use but
    not for barter or sale.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 18
- SOR/2016-131, s. 9

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527367 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may apply for a permit to take material from territorial lands
  by filing with a territorial land agent an application that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in the form prescribed by the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), is accompanied by
    payment of the application fee set out in Schedule I and the
    royalties payable under section 14.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There is no application fee and no royalty payable by
  any of the following persons or entities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a department of the Government of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of the Northwest Territories;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner of Nunavut;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal district in the Northwest Territories
    or Nunavut;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any educational, religious or charitable
    institution or hospital in the Northwest Territories or Nunavut.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application referred to in
  subsection (1), the territorial land agent shall issue to the
  applicant a permit for a period based on the estimated dates of
  commencement and completion as set out in the permit application but
  not exceeding three years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2.1), a permit expires on the day
  on which the quantity of material mentioned in the permit has been
  quarried and removed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A permit shall not be assigned.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a permittee has not complied with these
  Regulations or the conditions of their permit, the Minister may cancel
  the permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-112, s. 2
- SOR/2003-116, s. 20
- SOR/2016-131, s. 10

## [Reservations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527384 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the area under a lease or permit issued pursuant to these Regulations
  is subject to an oil and gas permit or lease, or a recorded mineral
  claim, the lease or permit shall not authorize entry upon such area
  without first obtaining the permission of the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may grant such permission subject to
  such conditions for the protection of the holder of the terminable
  grant as may be considered necessary.

## [Fees and Royalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527388 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in
these Regulations, material taken by a lessee or permittee from lands
described in a permit or lease is subject to the payment of royalties at
the rates set out in Schedule II.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-112, s. 3
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The fee set out in
column II of an item of Schedule I is payable for the service set out in
column I of that item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-112, s. 3
:::
