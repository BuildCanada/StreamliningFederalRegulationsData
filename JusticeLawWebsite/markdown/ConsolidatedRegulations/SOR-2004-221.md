## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Iraq {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-on-iraq .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-221
:::

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2004-10-19

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Iraq

P.C. 2004-1176 2004-10-19
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under
Article 41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Security
Council Resolution 1483 (2003) on May 22, 2003, Security Council
Resolution 1511 (2003) on October 16, 2003, Security Council Resolution
1518 (2003) on November 24, 2003, and Security Council Resolution 1546
(2004) on June 8, 2004;

And whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to
make regulations for enabling the measures set out in those resolutions
to be effectively applied;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 2
of the [United Nations Act](/eng/acts/U-2), hereby makes the annexed
[United Nations Iraq Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-221).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712849 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    weapon, ammunition, military vehicle or equipment, including their
    spare parts. ([armes et matériel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen within the meaning
    of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or a body corporate
    incorporated or continued by or under the laws of Canada or a
    province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Committee of the Security Council established
      by Resolution 661 (1990) of August 6, 1990 and modified by
      Resolution 1483 (2003) of May 22, 2003; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Committee of the Security Council established
      by Resolution 1518 (2003) of November 24, 2003.
      ([Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Development Fund for Iraq]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Development Fund for Iraq]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fund
    identified by paragraph 12 of Resolution 1483 (2003) of
    May 22, 2003. ([Fonds de développement pour l'Iraq]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[individual or entity identified by the Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[individual or entity identified by the
    Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or entity
    described in paragraph 23(b) of Resolution 1483 (2003) of May 22,
    2003 that is identified by the Committee pursuant to paragraph 19 of
    that resolution. ([individu ou entité recensé par le
    Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a body
    corporate, a trust, a partnership, a fund, an unincorporated
    association or organization or a foreign state.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[previous Government of Iraq]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[previous Government of Iraq]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Government of Iraq and the governments of its political
    subdivisions, including their state bodies, corporations and
    agencies, that existed prior to May 22, 2003. ([Gouvernement
    iraquien précédent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means property of every description
    and documents relating to or evidencing the title or right to
    property, or giving a right to recover or receive money or goods,
    and includes any funds, financial assets or economic resources.
    ([bien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public entity identified by the Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public entity identified by the Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an entity described in paragraph 23(a) of Resolution
    1483 (2003) of May 22, 2003 that is identified by the Committee
    pursuant to paragraph 19 of that resolution. ([entité publique
    recensée par le Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Resolution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a resolution adopted by the
    Security Council. ([résolution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security Council
    of the United Nations. ([Conseil de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712863 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712865 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No legal
  proceeding --- and no attachment, garnishment or execution --- shall
  lie against

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any petroleum, petroleum product or natural gas
    originating in Iraq until the title or right to the petroleum,
    petroleum product or natural gas passes to an initial purchaser;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds or obligations from the initial sale
    of any petroleum, petroleum product or natural gas originating in
    Iraq; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Development Fund for Iraq, including its
    property wherever located.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the
  enforcement of a final judgement arising out of a contractual
  obligation entered into after June 28, 2004 by the Government of Iraq,
  a government of a political subdivision of Iraq or their state bodies,
  corporations or agencies.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if recourse to the
  proceeds or obligations referred to in paragraph (1)(b) is necessary
  to satisfy liability for damages assessed in connection with an
  ecological accident, including an oil spill, that occurs after May 22,
  2003.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712873 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside of Canada shall knowingly export, sell, supply or send
to any person in Iraq arms and related material unless they are required
by the Government of Iraq, or by a multinational force under unified
command, to serve the purposes of Resolution 1546 of June 8, 2004.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person in Canada and no Canadian outside of Canada shall knowingly

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal directly or indirectly in any property in
    Canada that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was owned on May 22, 2003, and continues to be
      owned, by the previous Government of Iraq or any public entity
      identified by the Committee, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is owned or controlled by or held on behalf of
      any individual or entity identified by the Committee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly,
    any financial transaction related to a dealing in property referred
    to in paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial services or any other
    services in respect of any property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not prohibit dealings in property
  that are undertaken by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada in compliance with
    Resolution 1483 (2003) of May 22, 2003, Resolution 1511 (2003) of
    October 16, 2003, or Resolution 1518 (2003) of November 24, 2003; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person in compliance with subsection 7(3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not apply
  to the property of diplomatic missions and consular posts of Iraq in
  Canada.

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 does not
prohibit the provision, processing or payment of funds, other financial
assets or economic resources or the provision of goods and services that
are necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance
or to support other activities that support basic human needs in Iraq by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds and
  other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies and
  related organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
  observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members of
  those humanitarian organizations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded non-governmental
  organizations that are participating in any United Nations
  humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other United
  Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
  organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
  Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
  grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
  non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other person authorized for that purpose by a
  United Nations committee established by the Security Council acting
  within its mandate.

## [Disclosure and Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712888 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada and every Canadian outside of Canada who is knowingly
  in possession or control of any property described in paragraph
  5(1)(a) must, without delay, notify the Minister in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value, location and nature of the property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    person in possession or control;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person came into possession
    or control;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the property is described in
    subparagraph 5(1)(a)(i), the name of the owner;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the property is described in
    subparagraph 5(1)(a)(ii), the name of the individual or entity
    identified by the Committee that owns or controls the property or on
    whose behalf the property is held; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the person has knowledge that the property is
    subject to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision or
    measure made prior to the coming into force of these Regulations, a
    description of the decision or measure.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless notified by the Minister within 45 working
  days after sending the notice, the person must, at the end of that
  period, transfer without delay to the account of the Development Fund
  for Iraq at the Federal Reserve Bank of New York any cash, currency or
  securities, negotiable instruments or other financial instruments
  described in the notice that are not required to satisfy any decision
  or measure referred to in paragraph (2)(f).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) and no civil proceedings lie against a person for
  a disclosure or transfer made in good faith under this section.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712899 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under these
  Regulations may, before doing so, apply to the Minister in writing for
  a certificate to exempt the activity from the application of the
  Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that such an activity be prohibited or
  if the Security Council or the Committee has approved the activity in
  advance.

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of an
  individual or entity identified by the Committee and that claims not
  to be that individual or entity may apply to the Minister in writing
  for a certificate stating that they are not that individual or entity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 45 working days after receiving the
  application, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is established that the applicant is not the
    individual or entity identified by the Committee, issue the
    certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not so established, provide notice to the
    applicant of his or her determination.

## [Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712903 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No claim in respect of any
contract or transaction the performance of which has been affected by
reason of the measures imposed by these Regulations shall lie against
Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province or against a person in
Canada at the instance of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the previous Government of Iraq, a public entity
  identified by the Committee or an individual or entity identified by
  the Committee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person in Iraq; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person claiming through or for the benefit of any
  person or body referred to in paragraph (a) or (b).
