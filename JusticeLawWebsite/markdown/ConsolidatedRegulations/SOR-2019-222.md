[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation is an affiliate of another corporation
  if one of them is controlled by the other or both are controlled by
  the same corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation is controlled by another corporation if
  the other corporation is able to exercise control in fact of the
  corporation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following activities do
not constitute brokering for the purposes of the [Export and Import
Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brokering of a transaction between a corporation
  and its affiliates if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transaction relates to the movement of a good,
    other than a good referred to in Group 9 of the schedule to the
    [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the good is for end-use by an affiliate of the
    corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the brokering of a transaction by a Canadian citizen
  or a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) if the Canadian citizen or permanent resident is
  outside of Canada and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transaction relates to the movement of a good,
    other than a good referred to in Group 9 of the schedule to the
    [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the brokering is done on behalf of the employer of
    the Canadian citizen or permanent resident and the employer is not a
    Canadian citizen or a permanent resident or an organization that is
    incorporated, formed or otherwise organized under the laws of Canada
    or a province, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian citizen or permanent resident does
    not have the ability to exercise control in fact of the employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2019-222_e_transform_hq_25375){#fn_SOR-2019-222_e_transform_hq_25375-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
subsection 12(2) of An Act to amend the Export and Import Permits Act
and the Criminal Code (amendments permitting the accession to the Arms
Trade Treaty and other amendments), chapter 26 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2018, comes into force, but if they are registered after that
day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
