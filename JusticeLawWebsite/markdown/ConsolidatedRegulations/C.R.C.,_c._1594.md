[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1594

[VETERANS' LAND ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.5)

Regulations Made Pursuant to the Veterans' Land Act
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530561 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Veterans' Land
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1594).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530563 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Veterans' Land
      Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[authorized officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorized officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer of the
      Director authorized by the Director to examine applications to
      participate in the benefits of the Act and to recommend as to the
      qualifications of a veteran and the suitability of property for
      settlement purposes; ([fonctionnaire autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[certified veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certified veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a veteran to whom
      a subsisting qualification certificate has been issued; ([ancien
      combattant titulaire d'un certificat
      d'admissibilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Director, The Veterans'
      Land Act; ([Directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[district manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[district manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer in
      charge of a district office of the Director and includes any
      person authorized by the Director to act on behalf of that
      officer; ([gérant de district]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
      Affairs; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Provincial Advisory Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Provincial Advisory Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      provincial advisory board appointed pursuant to section 21 of the
      Act; ([conseil consultatif provincial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[qualification certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualification certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      certificate certifying that a veteran is qualified to participate
      in the benefits of the Act; ([certificat
      d'admissibilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Regional Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regional Advisory Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      regional advisory committee appointed pursuant to section 44 of
      the Act; ([comité consultatif régional]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[regional director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regional director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of the
      Director in a region who is charged with the administration of the
      several districts comprising the region and includes any person
      authorized by the Director to act on behalf of that officer.
      ([directeur régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act and these Regulations,

  [[agricultural value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[agricultural value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to land,
      means the market value of the land or such lesser amount as the
      Director may determine taking into account the income of the
      veteran occupying the land; ([valeur agricole]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[basic herd livestock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[basic herd livestock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means female cattle,
      sheep or swine of a breeding age with or without an appropriate
      complement of male stock and may include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cattle, up to a normal two-year
        progeny, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of sheep, up to a normal one-year
        progeny; ([animaux de ferme d'un troupeau de
        base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[economic farm unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[economic farm unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a farm enterprise
      that will, in the opinion of the Director, provide sufficient
      income after operating expenses are paid to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a reasonable livelihood for a veteran
        and those members of his family living with him, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} repay the indebtedness of the veteran to the
        Director in accordance with the terms of the contract between
        them; ([unité agricole économique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[farm equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[farm equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the work horses,
      tools, equipment, implements and machinery normally used in the
      operation of a farm that are not affixed to the land; ([outillage
      agricole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[farm land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[farm land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land suitable for
      agriculture and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} buildings affixed to the land,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} apparatus, appliances and machinery normally
        used in the operation of a farm that are affixed to the land,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a revenue-producing wood lot of a size that may
        be operated as part of a farm unit, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permanent plantings of fruit trees and
        grapevines. ([terre ou bien-fonds agricole]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraphs 53(a)(iii) and (iv)
  of the Act, "circumstances beyond the control of the veteran" means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of the veteran's place of employment
    to another area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inability of the veteran to secure employment
    in the area of his place of residence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ill-health of the veteran or his family;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the land having been required and taken for public
    purposes; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} existing housing accommodation that is, in the
    opinion of the Director, not suitable for the veteran and his
    family.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 55(3) of the Act,
  "circumstances beyond the control of the veteran" means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of the veteran's place of employment
    to another area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the inability of the veteran to secure employment
    in the area of his place of residence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ill-health of the veteran;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ill-health of the veteran's family necessitating
    the transfer of residence to another area;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} deficiencies or other disadvantages disclosed or
    developing subsequent to the commencement of construction that, in
    the opinion of the Director, rendered the land unsuitable; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the land having been required and taken for public
    purposes.

## [Application for Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530603 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A veteran who wishes to be
certified as qualified to participate in the benefits of the Act shall
submit to the Director, through the nearest office of the Director, an
application for certification in writing, in such form and containing
such information as the Director may from time to time determine.

## [Eligibility for Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530605 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the eligibility of a veteran with service in the Western
  hemisphere only to be certified, the 12 months active service required
  by paragraph (b) of the definition [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  section 2 of the Act shall be calculated in days and 365 days active
  service constitutes 12 months active service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Time spent by a veteran in professional training in
  the faculty of medicine or dentistry in any university or in any
  hospital prior to appointment to a unit as a commissioned officer
  pursuant to Canadian Army Routine Orders Nos. 3202 and 3343, as
  amended, shall not be counted as active service under the Act.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  veteran is eligible for benefits under the Act in respect of service
  in the naval, army or air forces of Her Majesty subsequent to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day of his acceptance as a member of the
    regular forces, if he was so accepted after March 31, 1946;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} March 31, 1946, if on that day he was a member of
    the regular forces serving on active service; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} March 31, 1946, if he volunteered and was accepted
    for service in the naval, army or air forces of Canada for a special
    period terminating on or after September 30, 1947, unless he was
    serving on overseas service on August 31, 1945, and remained
    continuously on the strength of an establishment, unit or ship on
    overseas service, in which case he is eligible for benefits in
    respect of all such service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A veteran eligible for benefits under the Act is
  eligible for benefits under the Act in respect of all of his full-time
  service, if he was not accepted as a member of the regular forces or
  was not accepted for service in the naval, army or air forces of
  Canada for a special period terminating on or after September 30,
  1947.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), "overseas
  service" has the same meaning as it has in the [War Service Grants
  Act](/eng/acts/W-2.5).

## [Qualification of Veteran]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530616 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a
qualification certificate shall submit reasonable evidence that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he is personally fit and able to carry on the
  occupation by which he proposes to gain his livelihood;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by reason of his character, habits, knowledge and
  experience, he is capable of carrying on that occupation successfully;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} he understands the financial responsibility required
  by the Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in all other respects, including availability of
  initial capital, he is qualified to participate in the benefits of the
  Act.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A qualification certificate
shall not be issued to a veteran who intends to carry on full-time
farming operations unless, in the opinion of the district
superintendent, the veteran has had two years satisfactory farming
experience, prior to the date of his application, in the operation and
management of the type of farm the veteran proposes to operate.

## [Consideration of Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530623 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
qualification certificate shall be considered by an authorized officer
or a Regional Advisory Committee, and where it is first considered by an
authorized officer, it may be referred to a Regional Advisory Committee
for further consideration.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Upon request, an applicant
and his spouse shall appear in person before the authorized officer or
the Regional Advisory Committee to furnish such further or other
information as may be required.

## [Recommendation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530626 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the opinion of an authorized officer or a Regional Advisory
  Committee, a veteran is qualified to participate in the benefits of
  the Act, that officer or the Regional Advisory Committee shall
  recommend to the regional director that a qualification certificate be
  issued to the veteran and indicate the type of establishment for which
  the veteran is qualified.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, in the opinion of an authorized officer or a
  Regional Advisory Committee, a veteran is not qualified to participate
  in the benefits of the Act, that officer or Regional Advisory
  Committee shall so inform the regional director stating in his
  recommendation the reasons why the veteran is not so qualified and any
  conditions under which application for a qualification certificate
  will be reconsidered.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Director may, upon
the recommendation of a regional director, authorize a district manager
to receive the recommendation of an authorized officer or a Regional
Advisory Committee and to act on the recommendation in lieu of a
regional director.

## [Qualification Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530631 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A qualification
certificate, in the form prescribed by the Director, indicating the
province or region to which it applies and the type of settlement to
which it relates may be issued to any veteran who is eligible and
qualified to participate in the benefits of the Act.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Director may at any time review a qualification certificate and may
  cancel that qualification certificate if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in his opinion,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances have altered to such an extent
      that had those altered circumstances existed at the time the
      veteran applied for a qualification certificate, the certificate
      would not have been issued, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} circumstances that existed at the time the
      certificate was issued are such that had they been known to the
      Director at that time the certificate would not have been issued;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the veteran informs the Director in writing that he
    no longer wishes to retain his certificate; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application by the veteran for a sale, advance,
    loan or grant under Part I of the Act was not received by the
    Director on or before March 31, 1975 and the veteran has not a
    subsisting contract with the Director on March 31, 1975.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A qualification certificate becomes void upon a
  notification of cancellation being mailed to the veteran at his last
  address known to the Director.

## [Application for Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530641 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certified veteran may apply for assistance under the Act by filing an
  application therefor in writing in the form prescribed by the Director
  with the office of the Director nearest the land in respect of which
  the assistance is sought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for assistance shall be supported by
  the full cash deposit required by the Act.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An authorized officer or
Regional Advisory Committee may require an applicant for assistance and
his spouse to appear in person before him or it, whether or not the
veteran and his spouse have already appeared before a different
authorized officer or Regional Advisory Committee for examination.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every applicant for
assistance under the Act shall complete a declaration in writing that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he or his agent has personally inspected and
  thoroughly examined the land with respect to which the application is
  made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he is satisfied that such land is suitable for the
  purpose for which it is intended to be used; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} he understands and agrees that approval to purchase
  does not constitute a warranty or guarantee of any kind whatsoever by
  the Director as to the condition of the property or the suitability of
  the property for the purpose for which it is intended to be used.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An application made under
Part I or III of the Act that involves acquisition of land by the
Director may, after inspection and appraisal by an officer of the
Director of the land to which the application for assistance relates, be
referred to the Regional Advisory Committee for consideration.

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  determining whether the assistance applied for is intended for the
  purposes authorized by the Act, and taking into account the inspection
  and appraisal report and the suitability of the land for the
  settlement of the veteran, an authorized officer or Regional Advisory
  Committee shall make a recommendation to the regional director as to
  whether, in the opinion of the authorized officer or the Committee,
  the application should be approved or rejected, together with such
  other recommendations as the authorized officer or the Committee may
  see fit to make.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A recommendation referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be sent to the regional director together with the application form,
  the report of inspection and appraisal and all other relevant
  documents.

## [Approval of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530654 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  approval of purchase or sale of property and the approval of
  construction authorized by the Act shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given in writing, in the form prescribed by the
    Director, over the signature of the regional director; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify the amounts approved, the purposes for
    which the amounts are approved and the terms of repayment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In no event shall the total loan and advances
  approved on the veteran's behalf exceed the veteran's capacity to
  repay as estimated by the Director.

## [Establishment of Land Value]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530660 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  establishing the value of land for the purposes of section 17 of the
  Act, the Director may take into account the value that permanent
  improvements to be effected under that section may add to the value of
  the land.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In establishing the market value of land, the
  Director may take into account the value that permanent improvements
  effected by the Director may add to the value of the land.

## [Standard Dates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530664 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The standard dates for
payments are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} November 1st in each year in the case of annual
  instalments;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} May 1st and November 1st in each year in the case of
  semi-annual instalments; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the 1st or the 15th of each month as may be
  determined by the Director, in the case of monthly instalments.

## [Execution of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530669 .Part}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** No assistance shall be
provided under the Act except under the supervision of the Director or
an officer authorized to act for him and unless the veteran has executed
such documents as may be required by the Act or by the Director.

## [Minimum Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530671 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), financial
  assistance for the establishment of a veteran under section 11 of the
  Act may be approved only in respect of land that is at least 1/2 acre
  in area and that, in the opinion of the Director, is suitable for
  part-time farming purposes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director, in his discretion, may waive the
  requirements of subsection (1) in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a qualified applicant who is in receipt of a
    disability pension of 50 per cent or more or who, in the opinion of
    a medical officer of the Canadian Pension Commission, has a
    permanent impairment of his health that may reasonably be equated
    with a pensionable disability of 50 per cent or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a commercial fishing establishment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a lot of less than 1/2 acre in area forming part of
    lands subdivided by the Director for the establishment of veterans
    under Part I of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a lot that could be approved under subsection (1)
    except that it is smaller than 1/2 acre by not more than 20 per
    cent.

## [Cancellation of Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530679 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Director may at any
time review an approval for assistance and may cancel that approval for
assistance at any time before the whole of the assistance has been
disbursed if,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in his opinion,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances have altered to such an extent
    that, had those altered circumstances existed at the time approval
    for assistance was given to the veteran, that approval would not
    have been given, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} circumstances that existed at the time approval
    for assistance was given to the veteran are such that, had they been
    known to the Director at that time, that approval would not have
    been given; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the veteran informs the Director that he no longer
  wishes to receive such assistance.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Where, subsequent to the
date of approval by the Director of an application for assistance by a
veteran and before the contract related thereto is entered into, there
is a decline in the interest rate in effect for the purpose of
subsection 76(1) of the Act, the Director may cancel that approval and
may approve the application at any lesser rate of interest in effect at
any time between the original date of approval and the date the contract
is entered into.

## [Livestock, Farm Equipment or other Chattels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530686 .Part}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall not
sell to a veteran or make advances to a veteran for livestock, farm
equipment or other chattels for which there is not a reasonable need.

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Director shall not approve the disbursement of more public funds than
  \$100 per tillable acre for the purchase of livestock or farm
  equipment for a veteran certified as qualified to participate in
  part-time farming.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where deletions have been allowed in a contract for
  the construction of a house for a veteran described in subsection (1),
  the Director shall not approve the disbursement of any public funds
  for the purchase of livestock or farm equipment for the veteran.

## [Tree and Bush Fruits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530691 .Part}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Assistance or advances
for tree and bush fruit nursery stock, and seed and roots of hardy
perennials to be planted for commercial purposes, shall be expended as
permanent improvements assistance or advances.

## [Motor Vehicles and Electrical Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530693 .Part}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No advances shall be made
and no contract shall be entered into for the purchase or sale of motor
vehicles, bicycles, washing machines or sewing machines or component
parts of any of them except as may be required for the purposes of
paragraph 71(2)(g) of the Act.

## [Commercial Fishing Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530695 .Part}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A contract for the sale
of commercial fishing equipment may include seaworthy boats of such size
and construction as may be approved by the Director, fishing nets,
floats, weights, anchors, internal combustion motors, sails, blocks and
tackle, necessary cable and cordage, navigating equipment and galley
stoves.

## [Joint Enterprise]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530697 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Commercial fishing
equipment for use in one commercial fishing enterprise may not be sold
on a joint basis to more than two veterans.

## [Buildings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530699 .Part}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  veteran shall not, except with the consent of the Director, erect
  additional buildings or make alterations to existing buildings on any
  land that the veteran has contracted to purchase from the Director.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a veteran or other person who has a subsisting
  contract of sale of land made pursuant to Part I or III of the Act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} wishes to obtain a guaranteed home improvement loan
    under Part V of the [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11) for the
    purpose of financing repairs, alterations and additions to his home
    on the land to which the contract relates in such an amount and
    subject to such repayment terms as would require an owner, under
    ordinary circumstances, to give to the approved lender a mortgage or
    assignment of his interest in the land, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wishes to obtain a loan under Part VI of the
    [National Housing Act](/eng/acts/N-11) for the purpose of assisting
    in the repair, rehabilitation and improvement of his home on the
    land to which the contract relates in such an amount and subject to
    such repayment terms as would require an owner, under ordinary
    circumstances, to give to Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation a
    mortgage or assignment of his interest in the land,

  and gives to the Director a non-revocable direction and undertaking to
  give a mortgage, charge or hypothec in favour of the approved lender
  or Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation, as the case may be, if
  requested by the lender or corporation when title is transferred from
  the Director to the veteran or other person, the Director may grant
  permission to obtain the loan.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the Director grants permission to a veteran
  under subsection (2) to obtain a loan, the direction and undertaking
  of the veteran forthwith becomes a part of the subsisting contract of
  sale.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a veteran or other person who has a subsisting
  contract of sale with the Director under section 11, 13, 23, 26 or 27
  of the Act

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} wishes to obtain, in respect of the land to which
    that contract applies, a loan or grant under the Provincial Home
    Acquisition Act of the Province of British Columbia, being chapter
    39 of the Statutes of British Columbia 1967, as amended, of such
    amount and on such repayment terms that an owner would be required,
    in the case of a loan, to give to the Province a second mortgage of
    that land and, in the case of a grant, to allow the Province to file
    against that land a certificate of grant,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gives to the Director a non-revocable direction and
    undertaking that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan, he will give to the
      Province a mortgage of the land to which that contract applies, if
      required by the Province, when title to the land is transferred
      from the Director to the veteran or other person, or will repay to
      the Province the amount of the loan prior to the transfer to title
      by the Director to the veteran or other person, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a grant, he will allow the
      Province to file against the land a certificate of grant when
      title to the land is transferred from the Director to the veteran
      or other person or to repay to the Province the amount of grant
      prior to the transfer of title from the Director to the veteran or
      other person, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} agrees in writing that the direction and
    undertaking given pursuant to paragraph (b) is a condition of the
    subsisting contract of sale,

  the Director may grant permission to obtain the loan or grant.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} To facilitate the making of a loan or grant to a
  veteran or other person by the Province of British Columbia under the

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the veteran may deliver to the order of the
    Director additional grain from the specified and additional acres;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where, in accordance with the terms of the

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} payment of the amortized amounts due in that year

## [Disposal of Surplus Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530882 .Part}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Director wishes to sell any lands to a person other than a
  certified veteran, he shall first advertise them for sale by public
  tender subject to the condition stated in the advertisement that no
  offer received by the Director as a result of such advertising will
  necessarily be accepted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to lands that may be
  required for the special purposes specified in section 28 of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the time limited in an advertisement published
  pursuant to subsection (1) has expired and no satisfactory offer has
  been received, the Director may negotiate with any person who made an
  offer or with any other person for the purpose of obtaining a binding
  offer to recommend for acceptance under the provisions of the Act.

## [Disposal of Repossessed Chattels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530887 .Part}

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Where the Director has
repossessed chattels theretofore held by a veteran under any agreement
with the Director and the nature of the chattels is such that in the
opinion of the Director they are likely to deteriorate in value in his
hands or prove costly to keep and care for until they can be used for
the ordinary purposes of the Act, or are not likely to be required for
the ordinary purposes of the Act, the Director may sell those chattels
forthwith

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by public auction for cash, after due advertising;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} privately at a cash price agreed to in writing by the
  veteran from whom the chattels have been repossessed; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} privately, after an appraisal of the chattels by an
  independent qualified person, at a cash price at or near the appraised
  value.
