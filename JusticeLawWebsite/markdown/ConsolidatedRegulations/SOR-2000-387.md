## National Parks of Canada Wilderness Area Declaration Regulations {#national-parks-of-canada-wilderness-area-declaration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-387
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 2000-10-19

National Parks of Canada Wilderness Area Declaration Regulations

P.C. 2000-1620 2000-10-19
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to subsection 5(8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2000-387_e_hq_5182){#fn_SOR-2000-387_e_hq_5182-ID0EFBAA}
of the National Parks Act, hereby makes the annexed National Parks
Wilderness Area Declaration Regulations.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2000-387_e_hq_5182-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR-2000-387_e_hq_5182}R.S., c.
  39 (4th Supp.), s. 3(2)
  :::
:::

## [Wilderness Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663764 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The regions shown on the
administrative map plans, the numbers of which are set out in column 2
of an item of the schedule, that exist in a natural state or that are
capable of returning to a natural state are declared to be wilderness
areas within the national park of Canada set out in column 1 of that
item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-99, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-1-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 1"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663768 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
