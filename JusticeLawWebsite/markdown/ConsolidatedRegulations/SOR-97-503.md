## Les Collections Shan Remission Order, 1997 {#les-collections-shan-remission-order-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-503
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-11-20

Les Collections Shan Remission Order, 1997

P.C. 1997-1668  1997-11-20
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Les Collections Shan Remission Order,
1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-503).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010284 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[co-ordinated beachwear and co-ordinated accessories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[co-ordinated beachwear and co-ordinated
    accessories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes dresses, skirts, pants,
    shorts, cover-ups, wraps, handbags and other accessories
    manufactured in the same prints or in matching colours as those used
    to produce the swimsuits with which they are to be sold; ([vêtements
    de plage coordonnés et accessoires coordonnés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[knitted fabrics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[knitted fabrics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means knitted fabrics of
    subheading No. 6002.30, 6002.42 or 6002.43 or of tariff item No.
    6002.92.90 or 6002.93.00; ([étoffes de bonneterie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[narrow woven fabrics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[narrow woven fabrics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means narrow woven
    fabrics of heading No. 58.06; ([rubanerie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[nonwovens]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nonwovens]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means nonwovens of subheading No.
    5603.92, 5603.93 or 5603.94; ([nontissés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[padding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[padding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means padding of tariff item No.
    5903.20.20 or 5906.91.20; ([bourrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[textile products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textile products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means knitted fabrics,
    narrow woven fabrics, nonwovens, padding, tulles, woven fabrics of
    cotton or woven fabrics of man-made filaments and man-made staple
    fibres, excluding fabrics of a uniform solid colour of black or
    white; ([produits textiles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tulles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tulles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tulles of heading No. 58.04;
    ([tulles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[woven fabrics of cotton]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[woven fabrics of cotton]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means woven fabrics
    of cotton of subheading No. 5208.31, 5208.32, 5208.33, 5208.39,
    5208.41, 5208.42, 5208.43, 5208.49, 5208.51, 5208.52, 5208.53,
    5208.59, 5210.31, 5210.32, 5210.39, 5210.41, 5210.42, 5210.49,
    5210.51, 5210.52, 5210.59, 5212.13, 5212.14, 5212.15, 5212.23,
    5212.24 or 5212.25; ([tissus de coton]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[woven fabrics of man-made filaments and man-made staple fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[woven fabrics of man-made filaments and man-made staple
    fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means woven fabrics of man-made
    filaments and man-made staple fibres of subheading No. 5407.10,
    5407.42, 5407.43, 5407.44, 5407.52, 5407.53, 5407.54, 5407.61,
    5407.69, 5407.72, 5407.73, 5407.74, 5407.82, 5407.83, 5407.84,
    5407.92, 5407.93, 5407.94, 5408.22, 5408.23, 5408.24, 5408.32,
    5408.33 or 5408.34 or of heading No. 55.12, 55.13, 55.14, 55.15 or
    55.16; ([tissus de filaments synthétiques ou artificiels et de
    fibres synthétiques ou artificielles discontinues]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010295 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to Les Collections Shan Inc., Laval, Quebec,
of the customs duties paid or payable under Schedule I to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the textile products set out in column I
of the schedule to this Order up to the annual quantity limit shown
opposite that textile product in column II for the five year period set
out therein.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the textile products are used by Les Collections Shan
  Inc. in the manufacture of women's swimsuits with the *Shan* label;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the textile products that are imported by Les
  Collections Shan Inc. for use in the manufacture of women's
  co-ordinated beachwear and co-ordinated accessories with the *Shan*
  label have a similar or complementary pattern and colour to the
  textile products used by Shan in the manufacture of women's swimsuits
  and are made by the same supplier; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years of the date of importation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1010301 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on November 20, 1997.
