## Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins {#regulations-for-the-redemption-of-one-cent-coins .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-264
:::

[CURRENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-52)

Registration 2012-12-07

Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins

P.C. 2012-1622 2012-12-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 9.01(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Currency
Act](/eng/acts/C-52)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDCAA}, makes the annexed
[Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent
Coins](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-264).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is authorized
to redeem one cent coins that are or that have at any time been current
in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day of their registration.
