## Order transferring from the Prime Minister to the President of the Treasury Board the control and supervision of The Leadership Network {#order-transferring-from-the-prime-minister-to-the-president-of-the-treasury-board-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-leadership-network .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-51
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2001-04-25

Order transferring from the Prime Minister to the President of the
Treasury Board the control and supervision of The Leadership Network

P.C. 2001-609 2001-04-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Prime Minister to the President of the Treasury Board
the control and supervision of that portion of the public service known
as The Leadership Network.
