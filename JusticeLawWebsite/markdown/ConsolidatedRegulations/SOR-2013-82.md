## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803975 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Department of Employment and
    Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian citizen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means citizen as set out in
    section 3 of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29). ([citoyen
    canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).  ([résident permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means registration with the
    Commission under section 28.1 or 28.2 of the
    Act. ([enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means status under the laws governing
    Canadian citizenship and immigration to Canada.
     ([statut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- 2013, c. 40, s. 236

## [Application for Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803985 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information required

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any application to register
a person must be submitted in the manner and form required by the
Commission and must include supporting documents that allow for the
identification of the person and their status and contain the
information specified in at least one of the following paragraphs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's full name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their name at birth, if it differs from their name at
  the time of the application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} their place of birth; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the full name at birth of their parents.

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-136, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission and signature

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), an application to register a person must
  be made and signed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person is 12 years of age or older, by that
    person, by one of their parents or by any other person who is
    legally authorized to act in their name or on their behalf; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is under 12 years of age, by one of
    their parents or by any other person who is legally authorized to
    act in their name or on their behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement between Commission and
  province

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an agreement providing for registration at birth
  has been made between the Commission and the government of the
  province where the person was born, an application to register the
  person must be made and signed by an official responsible for
  registering births in that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unable to sign

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person who applies to be registered is unable to
  sign their name, they may attest the application by making their mark
  in the presence of two witnesses who sign the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of signature or mark

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an application for the registration of a person
  has been made in accordance with section 2, the Commission may
  register the person and assign them a Social Insurance Number despite
  the absence of the signature or mark.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to apply

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a person who is required
by law to have a Social Insurance Number refuses to apply for
registration, the Commission may register the person and assign a Social
Insurance Number to that person if it has information that establishes
their identity and status.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lost card

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  whose Social Insurance Number Card has been lost, destroyed or defaced
  may apply to the Commission for a replacement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for replacement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application for a replacement must be made in the
  form and manner required by the Commission and must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information and supporting documents required
    by section 2; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's Social Insurance Number or, if the
    number is not known, a statement that the person was previously
    assigned a Social Insurance Number.

## [Persons Referred to in Section 90 of the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804021 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information required

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application to register
a person who is not a Canadian citizen or permanent resident and in
respect of whom the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration has given a
direction to the Commission under section 90 of the [Immigration and
Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) to issue a Social Insurance
Number must include, in addition to the documents and information
required by section 2, the purpose for which the Social Insurance Number
is being applied for.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of number

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A Social Insurance Number
assigned to a person described in section 7 must begin with the number
"9".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity --- applicant outside
Canada

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A Social
  Insurance Number that begins with "9" and is assigned to a person who
  was not in Canada when they applied to be registered has a period of
  validity that expires five years after the day on which the number was
  assigned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity --- applicant in
  Canada

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Social Insurance Number that begins with "9" and is
  assigned to a person who was in Canada when they applied to be
  registered has a period of validity that expires on the earlier of the
  following days:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the period authorized for their
    stay in Canada ends, as determined under subsection 183(4) of the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day that is two years after the day on which
    the number is assigned.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to extend period of validity

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has been assigned a Social Insurance Number that begins
  with "9" may, at any time following that assignment, apply to the
  Commission to extend its period of validity or obtain a new period of
  validity that is equivalent to the period set out in section 9 that is
  applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in the form and manner
  required by the Commission and must include, in addition to the
  documents and information required by section 2, the purpose for which
  the Social Insurance Number subject to this application has been
  applied for, as set out in the application for registration in
  accordance with section 7.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-10-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 10"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Card issued without expiry date

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
any Social Insurance Number Card that was issued without an expiry date
to a person who was not a Canadian citizen or permanent resident expired
on April 3, 2004.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2018-136, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-12-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification --- status and number

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has been assigned a Social Insurance Number that begins
  with "9" and has since become a Canadian citizen or permanent resident
  must notify the Commission of that change and of that previously
  assigned number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Old number voided --- new number
  assigned

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After the notification under subsection (1) is
  received by the Commission, the Commission must void the previously
  assigned number and assign a new Social Insurance Number to the
  Canadian citizen or permanent resident.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-136, s. 8
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-13-20130430.html "Link to previous version of section 13"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number voided --- not beginning with 9

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If a person who is not a
Canadian citizen or permanent resident applies for a replacement card
under section 5 or informs the Commission of a change of name in
accordance with section 28.3 of the Act or section 139 of the
[Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) and their Social Insurance
Number does not begin with "9", the Commission must void the previously
assigned number and assign a new Social Insurance Number that begins
with "9".
