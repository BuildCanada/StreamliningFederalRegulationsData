## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954718 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Programming Undertaking
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-436).

## [Programming Undertaking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954720 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 3(1.4) of the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42), [[programming
undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a network, other than a network
within the meaning of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01),
consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who transmits by telecommunication all or
  part of the person's programs or programming directly or indirectly to
  the person referred to in paragraph (b); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who communicates all or part of the programs
  or programming referred to in paragraph (a) to the public by
  telecommunication.
