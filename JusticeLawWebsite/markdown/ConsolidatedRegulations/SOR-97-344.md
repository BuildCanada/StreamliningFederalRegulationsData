## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008885 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods referred to in Chapters
    61 and 62 of Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
    that are cut or knit to shape and are sewn or otherwise assembled in
    Chile from fabric or yarn produced or obtained outside the free
    trade area. ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cotton or man-made fibre fabric and cotton or
      man-made fibre made-up textile goods referred to in Chapters 52 to
      55 of Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
      other than goods containing 36% or more by weight of wool or fine
      animal hair, and in Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of Schedule I to the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are woven or knit in
      Chile from yarn produced or obtained outside the free trade area
      or that are knit in Chile from yarn spun in Chile from fibre
      produced or obtained outside the free trade area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods of subheading No. 9404.90 of Schedule I to
      the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are finished and cut
      and sewn or otherwise assembled from fabrics referred to in any of
      subheading Nos. 5208.11 to 5208.29, 5209.11 to 5209.29, 5210.11 to
      5210.29, 5211.11 to 5211.29, 5212.11, 5212.12, 5212.21, 5212.22,
      5407.41, 5407.51, 5407.71, 5407.81, 5407.91, 5408.21, 5408.31,
      5512.11, 5512.21, 5512.91, 5513.11 to 5513.19, 5514.11 to 5514.19,
      5516.11, 5516.21, 5516.31, 5516.41 and 5516.91 of Schedule I to
      the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are produced or
      obtained outside the free trade area; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wool fabric and wool made-up textile goods
      referred to in Chapters 51 to 55 of Schedule I to the [Customs
      Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), containing 36% or more by weight of
      wool or fine animal hair, and in Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of
      Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are
      woven or knit in Chile from yarn produced or obtained outside the
      free trade area or that are knit in Chile from yarn spun in Chile
      from fibre produced or obtained outside the free trade area.
      ([tissus et articles confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada and Chile. ([zone de libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cotton or man-made fibre
    yarns referred to in any of heading Nos. 52.05 to 52.07 and 55.09 to
    55.11 of Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that
    are spun in Chile from fibre referred to in any of heading Nos.
    52.01 to 52.03 and 55.01 to 55.07 of Schedule I to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that is produced or obtained outside the
    free trade area. ([filés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Extension of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008894 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, the
benefit of the Chile Tariff is extended to apparel, fabric and made-up
goods and spun yarn, imported into Canada from Chile, in an amount not
exceeding the annual quantitative limits for each of those goods set out
in Schedules 5.B.1, 5.B.2 and 5.B.3 to Appendix 5.1 of Annex C-00-B to
Chapter C of the Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The benefit referred to in
section 2 is extended on the condition that the importer or owner
provides to the officer, at the request of the officer,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the apparel, fabric and made-up
  goods or spun yarn referred to in that section are accounted for under
  subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or
  are the subject of an application for a refund under paragraph
  74(1)(d) of that Act, a written declaration, in English or French,
  indicating that the certification referred to in paragraph (b) is in
  the possession of the importer or owner; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the apparel, fabric and made-up
  goods or spun yarn referred to in that section are released, or at any
  other time after the importer or owner provides a declaration referred
  to in paragraph (a) in respect of the goods,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certification of the exporter, in English, French
    or Spanish, containing the statement set out in, and the information
    requested by, the form set out in the schedule, accompanied by a
    copy of the applicable invoice or sales agreement, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the certification referred to in
    subparagraph (i) is in Spanish, at the further request of the
    officer, a translation of the certification into English or French.

attached invoice or sales agreement, hereby certify that those goods
