## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750433 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who holds a credit
    card or has applied to an institution to become a holder of a credit
    card. ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an agreement for a
    line of credit, a credit card or any kind of loan. ([convention de
    crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit card]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit card]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a credit card issued to a
    natural person other than for business purposes. ([carte de
    crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person who has
    entered into a credit agreement with an institution other than for
    business purposes and who owes a debt to that institution.
    ([débiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 106\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 106\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a retail association, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a foreign company, as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a company, as defined in section 2 of the [Trust
      and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8).
      ([institution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750446 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
institutions, to the affiliates that they control and to the agents and
representatives of those institutions and affiliates.

## [Minimum Grace Period for New Purchases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750448 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  statement of account in respect of a billing cycle for a credit card
  must be sent by an institution to the borrower after the last day of
  that billing cycle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not require a minimum payment in
  respect of the outstanding balance owing on a credit card account for
  a particular billing cycle to be made by the borrower on a day earlier
  than 21 days after the last day of that billing cycle.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the due date for a minimum payment in respect of
  the outstanding balance owing on a credit card account falls on a
  Saturday or a holiday, the institution must consider a payment made on
  the next business day as being made on time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not charge interest on purchases
  of goods or services made on a credit card during a particular billing
  cycle if the borrower pays the outstanding balance owing on the credit
  card account in full on or before the due date.

## [Allocation of Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750454 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  different interest rates apply to different amounts owing for a
  particular billing cycle on a credit card account, the institution
  must allocate any payment made by the borrower that is greater than
  the required minimum payment for that billing cycle among those
  amounts using one of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by allocating that payment first to the amount with
    the highest interest rate and then allocating any remaining portion
    of the payment to the other amounts in descending order, based on
    their applicable interest rates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by allocating that payment among those amounts in
    the same proportion as each amount bears to the outstanding balance
    owing on the credit card account.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), if the payment
  that the institution allocates to an amount owing on a credit card
  account contains a fraction of a dollar, the institution may round up
  that amount to the nearest dollar if the fraction of the dollar is
  equal to or more than 50 cents, round down that amount to the nearest
  dollar if the fraction is less than 50 cents and, if necessary, make
  corresponding adjustments to the other amounts that are being
  allocated.

## [Over-the-limit Fees Due to Holds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750460 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an institution may not charge a borrower an amount
  for surpassing their credit limit as a result of a hold on their
  credit card.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the borrower would,
  in any case, have surpassed the credit limit during the period in
  which the hold was in effect.

## [Consent for Increases in Credit Limits and to Provide Credit Card Cheques to Borrower]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750464 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution may not increase the credit limit on a borrower's credit
  card account without first obtaining the borrower's express consent to
  do so.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower's consent to the increase is given
  orally, the institution must, not later than the date of the first
  statement of account that is provided after the date of that consent,
  provide confirmation of that consent to the borrower in writing, in
  paper or electronic form.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-188, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution must not provide credit card cheques to a borrower issued
  on a credit card account without first obtaining the borrower's
  express consent to do so.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower's consent to receive credit card
  cheques is given orally, the institution must, not later than the date
  of the first statement of account that is provided after the date of
  that consent, provide confirmation of that consent to the borrower in
  writing, in paper or electronic form.

use of the credit card, does not constitute express consent for the
purpose of sections 6 and 6.1.

## [Debt Collection Practices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750479 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  institution that communicates with a debtor in order to collect
  payment of a debt from the debtor must inform them of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the details of the debt, such as the amount owed
    and the type of debt; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of, or a unique identifier for, any
    person who is attempting to collect the payment on behalf of the
    institution and their relationship with the institution.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not communicate or attempt to
  communicate with a debtor, any member of a debtor's family or
  household, any relative, neighbour, friend or acquaintance of the
  debtor or the debtor's employer by any means, or in any manner or with
  a frequency that constitutes harassment, including:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use of threatening, profane, intimidating or
    coercive language;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of undue pressure; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} making public, or threatening to make public, a
    debtor's failure to pay.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except for the sole purpose of obtaining a debtor's
  address or telephone number, an institution may not contact or attempt
  to contact any member of the debtor's family or household or any
  relative, neighbour, friend or acquaintance of the debtor unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that person has guaranteed to pay the debt and is
    being contacted in relation to that guarantee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the debtor has given their express consent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the consent referred to in paragraph (3)(b) is
  given orally by the debtor, the institution must provide confirmation
  of that consent to the debtor in writing, in paper or electronic form.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise authorized in writing by the debtor,
  an institution may contact a debtor's employer solely for the purpose
  of confirming that the debtor is employed, the nature of their
  employment and their business title and business address.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not contact a debtor at the
  debtor's place of employment unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the institution does not have the home address or
    home telephone number of the debtor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} attempts by the institution to contact the debtor
    at their home telephone number have failed; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the institution obtains written authorization from
    the debtor to do so.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Except with the written consent of the debtor, an
  institution may not contact a debtor, any member of the debtor's
  family or household, any relative, neighbour, friend or acquaintance
  of the debtor or the debtor's employer or guarantor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a Sunday, except between the hours of 1:00 p.m.
    and 5:00 p.m. local time for the person being contacted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on any other holiday; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on any other day, except between the hours of
    7:00 a.m. and 9:00 p.m. local time for the person being contacted.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Except if the debtor or any other person referred to
  in subsection (7) has provided a cellular telephone number as a
  contact number, an institution may not knowingly communicate or
  attempt to communicate with the debtor or that person for the purpose
  of collecting, negotiating or demanding payment of a debt by a means
  that renders the charges or costs incurred for the communication
  payable by the debtor or that person, as the case may be.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} An institution that has communicated with a debtor in
  respect of the collection of a debt may not communicate with the
  debtor again in the course of that collection

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a means other than in writing, if the debtor
    makes a written request by registered mail to the institution to
    communicate with the debtor only in writing in that regard and
    provides an address at which they may be contacted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by a means other than through the debtor's legal
    advisor, if the debtor makes a written request to the institution to
    communicate with the debtor in that regard only through the debtor's
    legal advisor and provides a telephone number and an address for the
    legal advisor; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} without the debtor's consent, if the debtor
    notifies the institution by registered mail that the debt is in
    dispute and that they intend to take the matter before a dispute
    resolution body or that they are prepared for the institution to
    take the matter to court.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not misrepresent the purpose of a
  communication in respect of the collection of a debt with any person
  or give, directly or indirectly, by implication or otherwise, any
  false or misleading information in the course of that communication.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Despite any agreement to the contrary between a
  debtor and an institution, any charges made or incurred by the
  institution in collecting a debt, other than charges referred to in
  section 18 of any of the following regulations, are not considered to
  be a part of the amount owing by the debtor and may not be recovered
  from the debtor by the institution:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 107\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-181, s. 107\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [Cost of Borrowing (Trust and Loan Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-104);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [Cost of Borrowing (Retail Associations)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-263);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [Cost of Borrowing (Canadian Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-102); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [Cost of Borrowing (Foreign Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-103).

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not collect or attempt to collect
  payment in respect of a debt from any person who is not liable for the
  debt.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not directly or indirectly
  threaten or state an intention to proceed with any legal action if it
  does not actually intend to do so.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} An institution may not, for the purpose of
  attempting to collect a debt, use any document that falsely purports
  to originate from any court within or outside Canada.
