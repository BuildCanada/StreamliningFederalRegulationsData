## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674938 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a joint venture;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of an insurance company or
      securities dealer. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    that is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or a
    province. ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a real property entity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was formed by an insurance holding company,
      or by a designated entity controlled by an insurance holding
      company, and one or more other persons for the purpose of a
      specific business undertaking;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the insurance holding company or
      designated entity has a substantial investment; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the persons who formed it
      have agreed on joint control, regardless of the distribution of
      their equity. ([coentreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by an insurance holding company,
    that is held by a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the insurance holding company; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the insurance holding
      company. ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares of a body corporate that is primarily
      engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with real
      property, including shares of a body corporate that is another
      real property entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests in an unincorporated entity
      that is primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise
      dealing with real property, including ownership interests in an
      unincorporated entity that is another real property
      entity. ([entité immobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    an insurance holding company, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity, other than a designated
      entity controlled by the insurance holding company, in which the
      insurance holding company or a designated entity controlled by the
      insurance holding company beneficially owns sufficient shares or
      ownership interests to cause the insurance holding company or
      designated entity to have a substantial investment in the real
      property entity; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in paragraph (a). ([entité
      immobilière apparentée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the
    Act. ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an insurance
    holding company, means a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the insurance holding company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the insurance
      holding company; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
      holding company. ([tierce partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Book value of interest in real property

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 8 to 11, the book value of an asset that is an interest of an
insurance holding company in real property, at a particular time, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is real property, the gross book value
  of the real property, less any accumulated depreciation on the real
  property, that would be reported on a balance sheet of the insurance
  holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with the
  accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
  referred to in subsection 887(4) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is a security or loan, the book value of
  the security or loan that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
  insurance holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with
  the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
  referred to in subsection 887(4) of the Act.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674973 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt bank holding companies

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 981 to 983 of the
Act do not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a widely held insurance holding company with equity
  of five billion dollars or more; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance holding company with equity of five
  billion dollars or more that is controlled by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank holding company,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a widely held insurance holding company,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an eligible financial institution, as defined in
    section 370 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), other than a
    foreign bank as defined in section 2 of that Act, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a foreign bank, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), that is widely held as defined in
    section 2.3 of that Act.

## [Prescribed Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674982 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiary

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 981 to 983 of the Act, a prescribed subsidiary of an insurance
holding company is a subsidiary of the insurance holding company other
than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of an insurance company or securities
  dealer.

## [Amount Determined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-674988 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount determined --- real property and
equity

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 981 and 982 of the Act, the amount determined in
  respect of an insurance holding company is 70% of the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#674992 .Subsection}
  A - B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the regulatory capital of the insurance holding company; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the sum of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is an amount of
        shareholders' equity or subordinated indebtedness of an
        insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the
        insurance holding company, or of an entity controlled by an
        insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the
        insurance holding company, that is included in the amount of
        regulatory capital of the insurance holding company,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of a
        minority interest in an insurance company or securities dealer
        controlled by the insurance holding company, or in an entity
        controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
        controlled by the insurance holding company, that is included in
        the amount of regulatory capital of the insurance holding
        company,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of an
        investment, other than shareholders' equity or subordinated
        indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), of the insurance
        holding company or a designated entity controlled by the
        insurance holding company in an insurance company or securities
        dealer controlled by the insurance holding company, or in an
        entity controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
        controlled by the insurance holding company, if the investment
        is part of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the regulatory capital, within the meaning of
          the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance Companies)
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529), of an insurance
          company to which the Act applies, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an amount that would be treated as the
          capital of any other insurance company, or of a securities
          dealer, by a regulatory body that regulates the insurance
          company or securities dealer, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of a
        loan, other than subordinated indebtedness referred to in
        paragraph (a), made by the insurance holding company or by a
        designated entity controlled by the insurance holding company to
        an insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the
        insurance holding company, or to an entity controlled by an
        insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the
        insurance holding company, if the loan is part of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the regulatory capital, within the meaning of
          the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance Companies)
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529), of an insurance
          company to which the Act applies, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an amount that would be treated as the
          capital of any other insurance company, or of a securities
          dealer, by a regulatory body that regulates the insurance
          company or securities dealer.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount determined --- aggregate limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 983 of the Act, the amount
  determined in respect of an insurance holding company is 100% of the
  amount determined by the formula in subsection (1).

## [Valuation of Equity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675006 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value equals book value

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 982 and 983 of the Act, the value of the participating shares
and ownership interests referred to in those sections that are
beneficially owned by an insurance holding company and its subsidiaries
prescribed by section 4 is equal to their book value as reported on the
consolidated balance sheet of the insurance holding company.

## [Interests of an Insurance Holding Company in Real Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675009 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675010 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition and value of interest in real
property

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of Division 9 of Part XVII of the Act, other than
  subsection 978(3) of the Act, sections 8 to 12 define the interests of
  an insurance holding company in real property and set out how those
  interests are to be valued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 978(3) of the Act, the
  interests of an insurance holding company in real property means real
  property referred to in paragraph 11(1)(a) and shares and ownership
  interests referred to in paragraph 11(1)(b) that become beneficially
  owned by the insurance holding company, or by a designated entity
  controlled by the insurance holding company, as a result of the
  realization of a security interest on a loan or debt obligation
  referred to in subsection 11(1).

### [Direct Interests --- Real Property and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675016 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct interests

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an insurance holding company in real
  property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by the insurance
    holding company or by a designated entity controlled by the
    insurance holding company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation, for which the insurance holding
    company or a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
    company is liable, that was issued for the purpose of acquiring or
    improving real property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of direct interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an insurance holding
  company in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), any amount by which the book value of the debt
    obligation exceeds the book value of the real property referred to
    in that paragraph.

### [Indirect Interests --- Real Property, Shares and Ownership Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675026 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an insurance holding company in real
  property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a particular time, real property

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is beneficially owned by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
        holding company that is a joint venture, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which an entity described in
        clause (A) has a substantial investment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
      insurance holding company prepared as at that time in accordance
      with the accounting principles and specifications of the
      Superintendent referred to in subsection 887(4) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares and ownership interests beneficially owned
    by the insurance holding company, or by a designated entity
    controlled by the insurance holding company, in any related real
    property entity of the insurance holding company other than a
    related real property entity referred to in clause (a)(i)(A).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an insurance holding
  company in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of shares and ownership interests
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the book value of the shares and
    ownership interests.

### [Indirect Interests --- Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675040 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an insurance holding company in real
  property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a related real property
    entity of the insurance holding company and beneficially owned by
    the insurance holding company or by a designated entity controlled
    by the insurance holding company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the insurance holding company, or by
    a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding company, to
    a related real property entity of the insurance holding company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the insurance holding company, or by
    a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding company, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity in which a financial
      institution controlled by the insurance holding company has a
      substantial investment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in subparagraph (i);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a real property entity
    described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) and beneficially owned by
    the insurance holding company or by a designated entity controlled
    by the insurance holding company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issued by a related real property entity of the
      insurance holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beneficially owned by a third party, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} guaranteed by the insurance holding company or
      by a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
      company; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by a third party to a related real
    property entity of the insurance holding company and guaranteed by
    the insurance holding company or by a designated entity controlled
    by the insurance holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an insurance holding
  company in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or (d), the book value of the debt obligation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) or (c), the book value of the loan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guaranteed debt obligation referred
    to in paragraph (1)(e) or a guaranteed loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(f),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is issued by, or the loan
      is made to, an entity that is a related real property entity of
      the insurance holding company and the entity beneficially owns
      real property that is an interest of the insurance holding company
      in real property under paragraph 9(1)(a), any amount by which the
      face value of the guarantee exceeds the value of the real property
      determined in accordance with paragraph 9(2)(a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the face value of the
      guarantee.

### [Indirect Interests --- Secured Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675062 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  insurance holding company or a designated entity controlled by an
  insurance holding company makes a loan to, or beneficially owns or
  guarantees the debt obligation of, a third party, the loan or debt
  obligation is an interest of the insurance holding company in real
  property if it is secured by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by a third party
    in conjunction with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insurance holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the designated entity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
      holding company,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer or insurance company
      controlled by the insurance holding company,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by a securities dealer or
      insurance company referred to in subparagraph (iv), or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity described in
      subparagraph 10(1)(c)(i) or (ii); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by
    a third party in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that beneficially owns real property in
      conjunction with the insurance holding company, a related real
      property entity of the insurance holding company or a designated
      entity controlled by the insurance holding company, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
      holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an insurance holding
  company in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by real property referred to in paragraph (1)(a), the lesser
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in the real property at the time that the security
      interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#675081 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the insurance
            holding company or a designated entity controlled by the
            insurance holding company or the debt obligation is
            beneficially owned by the insurance holding company or a
            designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
            company, the book value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            insurance holding company or a designated entity controlled
            by the insurance holding company, the face value of the
            guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation;
      :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i), the
    lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#675090 .Subparagraph}
      A - (B - (C × D/E))

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the insurance
            holding company or a designated entity controlled by the
            insurance holding company or the debt obligation is
            beneficially owned by the insurance holding company or a
            designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
            company, the book value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            insurance holding company or a designated entity controlled
            by the insurance holding company, the face value of the
            guarantee,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of all security interests
          that were given for the loan or debt obligation,

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the net realizable value of the entity's interest in the
          real property referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i),

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the shares or ownership interests that are
          given as the security interest, and

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of the outstanding shares or ownership
          interests in the entity; and
      :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in a related real property entity referred to in
    subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#675102 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the insurance
            holding company or a designated entity controlled by the
            insurance holding company or the debt obligation is
            beneficially owned by the insurance holding company or a
            designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
            company, the book value of the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            insurance holding company or a designated entity controlled
            by the insurance holding company, the face value of the
            guarantee, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation.
      :::

### [Other Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-675108 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other interests

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of an insurance holding company in real
  property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee given by the insurance holding company,
    or by a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
    company, to an entity other than the insurance holding company or
    designated entity for the purpose of completing the development of
    real property that is beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insurance holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the insurance
      holding company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
      holding company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement made by the insurance holding company,
    or by a designated entity controlled by the insurance holding
    company, to support a third party's cost of operating or financing
    real property that is beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the insurance holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the insurance
      holding company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the insurance
      holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of an insurance holding
  company in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the estimated cost of completing the development
    of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agreement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the amount of any funds advanced by the insurance
    holding company, or by a designated entity controlled by the
    insurance holding company, under the agreement.
