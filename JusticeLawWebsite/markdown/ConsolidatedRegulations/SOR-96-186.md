## Allocation Method Order (Beef and Veal) {#allocation-method-order-beef-and-veal .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-186
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1996-03-29

Order Establishing the Method for Allocating the Import Access Quantity
for Beef and Veal
::::

::::: {.section .order}

6.2(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EJCCA} of the

Allocation Method Order --- Beef and Veal, made on April 19,
1995[[Footnote

the annexed [Order establishing the method for allocating the import
access quantity for beef and veal](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-186), in
substitution therefor.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979782 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a resident of Canada who
    applies for an import allocation. ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[beef and veal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beef and veal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods set out in the
    schedule. ([boeuf et veau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a distributor that is not
    a commission broker and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} buys beef and veal and re-sells them to other
      businesses; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintains or rents a warehouse and trucks, or
      purchases warehousing or transportation services in carrying on
      its trade. ([distributeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[FTA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[FTA country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country from which
    imports of beef and veal are not subject to the tariff rate quota in
    accordance with a free trade agreement (FTA) with Canada. ([pays
    ALÉ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to substantially change the
    appearance or nature of beef and veal, including to de-bone, slice,
    comminute, thermally process, preserve, dehydrate, ferment, render,
    fractionate, defibrinate or add to the product, but does not include
    to dress, trim, refrigerate, freeze, package or defrost.
    ([transformer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has processed
    beef and veal at their own facilities during the 12-month period
    ending on September 30 in the calendar year before the calendar year
    for which the person applies for an import allocation.
    ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retailer-processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retailer-processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a retailer who has
    processed beef and veal at their own facilities during the 12-month
    period ending on September 30 in the calendar year before the
    calendar year for which the retailer applies for an import
    allocation. ([détaillant-transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[under-utilization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[under-utilization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an import
    allocation, means utilization of less than 90% of the quantity
    allocated to an applicant. ([sous-utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

- SOR/2014-294, s. 2

## [Method of Allocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979797 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2014-294, s. 3(F)

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) and (4), the method for allocating the import
  access quantity for beef and veal that may be imported into Canada in
  each calendar year is as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} applicants who are processors and
    retailer-processors shall receive a share of 75% of the import
    access quantity, in proportion to the quantity of beef and veal that
    did not originate in a FTA country and that was processed at their
    own facilities during the 12-month period ending on September 30 in
    the calendar year before the calendar year for which the import
    allocation applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} applicants who are distributors shall receive a
    share of 25% of the import access quantity, in proportion to their
    imports of beef and veal that did not originate in a FTA country
    during the 12-month period ending on September 30 in the calendar
    year before the calendar year for which the import allocation
    applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1) and subject to subsections (3)
  and (4), the method for allocating the import access quantity for beef
  and veal that may be imported into Canada in the 2015 calendar year is
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} applicants who are processors and
    retailer-processors shall receive a share of 75% of the import
    access quantity, in proportion to the quantity of beef and veal that
    did not originate in a FTA country and that was processed at their
    own facilities during either of the following base periods that an
    applicant identifies in an application for allocation:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the 16-month period ending on April 30, 2003
      (prorated to a 12-month period), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the 12-month period beginning on August 1, 2013
      and ending on July 31, 2014; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} applicants who are distributors shall receive a
    share of 25% of the import access quantity, in proportion to their
    imports of beef and veal that did not originate in a FTA country
    during the base period referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any given calendar year, if an applicant that has
  been granted an allocation of 100 000 kg or more in accordance with
  subsection (1) or (2) has used 20% or less of their allocation by
  July 1, their remaining allocation will be reduced by 50% for that
  calendar year.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an applicant under-utilizes their import
  allocation in any given calendar year, the import allocation for which
  they may be eligible for the following calendar year is the import
  allocation in accordance with subsection (1) or (2) reduced by the
  percentage of their import allocation not utilized in that given
  calendar year.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply if the total amount of
  the applicant's import allocation not utilized is less than 9 000 kg
  of beef and veal.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, on or before October 1 in a given calendar year
  an applicant that has been granted an allocation in accordance with
  subsection (1) or (2) informs the Minister, in writing, that they are
  returning a specified portion of their allocation, that portion will
  be considered as having been used for the purposes of subsection (4).

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-979817 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Carcasses and half-carcasses of bovine animals, fresh,
  chilled or frozen, that do not originate in a FTA country and are
  classified under tariff item No. 0201.10.10 or 0202.10.10 in the List
  of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [2]{.lawlabel} Cuts of meat of bovine animals, fresh, chilled or
  frozen, with bone in, that do not originate in a FTA country and are
  classified under tariff item No. 0201.20.10 or 0202.20.10 in the List
  of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [3]{.lawlabel} Boneless meat of bovine animals, fresh, chilled or
  frozen, that does not originate in a FTA country and is classified
  under tariff item No. 0201.30.10 or 0202.30.10 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).
