## Seasonal Residents' Remission Order, 1991 {#seasonal-residents-remission-order-1991 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-84
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-06-19

Order Respecting the Remisson of Taxes Imposed under Division III of
Part IX and any other Part of the Excise Tax Act, Paid or Payable on the
Importation of Goods by Seasonal Residents

P.C. 1991-985  1991-05-30
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 23 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to revoke the Seasonal Residents' Remission Order,
C.R.C., c. 788, and to make the annexed Order respecting the remission
of duties, including the tax imposed under Division III of Part IX of
the Excise Tax Act, paid or payable on the importation of goods by
seasonal residents, in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644119 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644121 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[ \[Repealed, SI/98-20, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[household effects]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[household effects]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means furniture and goods
    that are found in a home, and tools and equipment that are used in
    the maintenance of a home, but does not include construction
    material, electrical fixtures or any other goods that are
    permanently attached or incorporated into a home; ([effets
    domestiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[seasonal resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[seasonal resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is not
    a resident of Canada and who leases for not less than three years or
    who owns, for seasonal use, a residence in Canada, other than a
    time-sharing residence or a mobile home. ([résident
    saisonnier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/98-20, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644128 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, remission is hereby granted of the
  taxes imposed under Division III of Part IX and any other part of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), paid or payable on the importation
  of household effects by a seasonal resident where the household
  effects

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are imported for the personal use of the seasonal
    resident or the seasonal resident's family and are not for any
    commercial, industrial, occupational or other purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are owned by the seasonal resident or the seasonal
    resident's family and have been in the possession of and use of the
    seasonal resident or the seasonal resident's family before the
    seasonal resident's first arrival in Canada to occupy the seasonal
    residence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are not sold or otherwise disposed of in Canada for
    at least one year after their importation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A seasonal resident is entitled to only one remission
  under this Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/98-20, s. 3
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a seasonal resident's household effects shall be
  imported at the time of the first arrival in Canada of the seasonal
  resident and shall be listed on a customs accounting document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where household effects that are listed on a customs
  accounting document referred to in subsection (1) are not imported at
  the time of the first arrival in Canada of the seasonal resident, the
  household effects shall be identified on that customs accounting
  document as household effects that are to follow.
