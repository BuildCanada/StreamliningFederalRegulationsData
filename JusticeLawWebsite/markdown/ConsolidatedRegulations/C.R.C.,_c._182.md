## Ontario Egg Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-egg-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 182
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Egg Producers in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536140 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Egg Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._182).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536142 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total number of dozens
    of eggs that a producer is entitled to market in intraprovincial,
    interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the Canadian Egg
    Marketing Agency Quota Regulations and the orders made by the
    Commodity Board under the Plan; ([production
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Egg
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means eggs of a domestic hen produced
    in Ontario; ([oeufs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Egg Producers'
    Marketing Plan, established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act, and any Regulations made pursuant to the Act to give
    effect to the Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of eggs in the Province of Ontario.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536150 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in addition to the service
  charges set out under the Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.05 per dozen
  of eggs sold by him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in
  addition to the service charges set out under the Plan, levies at the
  rate of \$0.35 per dozen of eggs sold by him that is in excess of his
  allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536154 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator of an egg grading station who buys eggs from a producer shall
  deduct from the moneys payable to the producer for the eggs all levies
  payable by the producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and
  shall pay such levies to the Commodity Board at its office, 5799 Yonge
  Street, Willowdale, Ontario, not later than 10 days from the last day
  of the week in which the eggs were bought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office, 5799 Yonge Street, Willowdale, Ontario, all levies payable
  by him under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the
  Commodity Board in the manner prescribed by subsection (1) in respect
  of eggs sold by him in any month not later than the 15th day of the
  month next following.
