## Import of Eggs Permit {#import-of-eggs-permit .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 625
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

General Import Permit No. 8
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559093 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the Import of Eggs Permit.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559095 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, under the
authority of this General Import Permit, import into Canada from any
country

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} eggs not exceeding two dozen for each non-commercial
  importation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SI/89-139, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} eggs encased in mud or other material that preserves
  their quality for specialty foods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-116, s. 1
- SI/83-186, s. 1
- SI/89-139, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where completion and
validation of a customs entry form is required in respect of any eggs
that are imported under the authority of this General Import Permit,
that form shall be endorsed \"Imported under the authority of General
Import Permit No. 8\" or \"Importé en vertu de la Licence générale
d\'importation n^o^ 8\".
