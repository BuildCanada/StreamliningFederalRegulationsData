## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[contamination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contamination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the presence of
    chemical, physical, radiological or microbiological parameters in
    water or the addition of such parameters to water --- other than for
    disinfection of the water, a potable water system or a potable water
    container --- in a quantity or concentration that renders or may
    render the water non-potable. ([contamination]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[disinfection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disinfection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical, physical or
    radiological process, or a series of such processes, that is part of
    the decontamination process and that is intended to remove or
    inactivate microbiological parameters that are human pathogens such
    as viruses, bacteria and protozoa. ([désinfection]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who carries on a
    business of transporting passengers. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who travels on
    board a conveyance under a contract, but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the master, the pilot or the driver of the
      conveyance; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of the crew working on board the
      conveyance. ([passager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[potable water container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[potable water container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a refillable
    container that is used for water and that meets the requirement set
    out in subsection 16(1), but does not include a bottle of
    prepackaged water. ([contenant d'eau potable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[potable water system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[potable water system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any equipment
    that is used on board a conveyance for handling, treating, storing
    or distributing water that is intended to be used for any purpose
    set out in section 3, but does not include a potable water container
    or a bottle of prepackaged water. ([réseau d'alimentation en eau
    potable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any boat, ship or other means
    of transportation by water. ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Scope]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837837 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator who is authorized, under a law of Canada or of another
  country, to transport at least 25 persons on board any of the
  following conveyances must ensure that the requirements of these
  Regulations, except for the requirement set out in section 18, are met
  if the operator provides water, to be used for a purpose set out in
  section 3, on board the conveyance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an aircraft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel used for interprovincial or international
    transportation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a bus used for interprovincial or international
    transportation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a train used for interprovincial or international
    transportation or used on a railway declared to be a work for the
    general advantage of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded conveyances

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), these Regulations do not
  apply to a conveyance that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is used for urban transit purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a point of departure and a point of destination
    outside Canada and travels through Canada without making any stops
    for the embarkation or disembarkation of passengers; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a vessel that has a point of departure and a
    point of destination in the same province and travels through
    another province or another country without making any stops for the
    embarkation or disembarkation of passengers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 12, paragraph 13(c) and sections 19 and 22 do
  not apply to a foreign operator in respect of a conveyance that, while
  it is in Canada, makes only one stop for the embarkation or
  disembarkation of passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purposes

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of water that is intended to be used

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for drinking, handwashing or oral hygiene by
  passengers;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the preparation of food for passengers, including
  the cleaning of surfaces used in food preparation, the washing of
  utensils --- including kitchenware, tableware or other items used in
  the handling, preparation, storage, transportation or consumption of
  food, ice or potable water --- and handwashing by persons who prepare
  or handle food, ice or potable water on behalf of the operator; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the form of ice, for the contact refrigeration of
  food for passengers or to be added to beverages for passengers.

## [Potable Water]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837857 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  water that is provided for a purpose set out in section 3 must be
  potable and in sufficient quantity for the number of passengers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The water must be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by way of a potable water system that meets the
    requirements of these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from a potable water container that meets the
    requirements of these Regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} from a bottle of prepackaged water.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Water that is provided by
way of a potable water system or from a potable water container must not
exceed the maximum acceptable concentration for [Escherichia
coli]{.otherLang lang="la"} ([E. coli]{.otherLang lang="la"}) of none
detectable per 100 mL.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Free from contamination

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Measures must be taken to
ensure that water that is to be provided by way of a potable water
system or from a potable water container is free from contamination when
it is taken from the water supply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loading of contaminated water

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section
  6 does not apply to contaminated water that is loaded on board a
  vessel while the vessel is making way if the water is subsequently
  decontaminated by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of salt water, reverse osmosis or
    distillation, or both of those processes, and, if necessary, any
    other decontamination process; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of fresh water, filtration and
    disinfection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded waters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), water must not be loaded on
  board a vessel from the waters within a harbour or in a port.

## [Ice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837877 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ice made on board

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Ice that is made on board a
conveyance must be made from water that meets the requirements of
sections 4 to 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ice supply

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Ice that is not made on
board a conveyance may be used if measures are taken to ensure that it
is free from contamination when it is taken from the supplier.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ice made or loaded on board

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Ice
  that is made or loaded on board a conveyance must meet the guidelines
  referred to in section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loading, moving and storage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Measures must be taken to prevent the risk of
  contamination of the ice when it is loaded, moved or stored on board
  the conveyance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Measures, such as the use of an ice scoop or tongs,
  must be taken to prevent the risk of contamination of the ice when it
  is handled.

## [Potable Water System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837889 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General requirements

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  potable water system must be designed, constructed and operated in a
  manner that prevents the risk of contamination through the
  implementation of measures

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to protect the system during the loading of water;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to prevent backflow;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to protect filling connections and cross
    connections; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to protect the system from tampering.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The system's components must be readily identifiable
  in order to avoid confusion with those of other systems and each
  storage tank and filling connection must be identified through the use
  of a readily visible and legible label.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routine sampling

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  each type of conveyance set out in column 1 of the schedule, water
  samples must be taken from the sampling sites set out in column 2 at a
  frequency set out in column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The samples must be analyzed for the purpose of
  verifying that the water is free from [E. coli]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  in accordance with a method for the analysis of potable water set out
  in Part 9000 of Standard Methods for the Examination of Water and
  Wastewater, published jointly by the American Public Health
  Association, the American Water Works Association and the Water
  Environment Federation, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrective measures

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If the water in a potable
water system does not meet the guidelines referred to in section 5 or if
there are reasonable grounds to believe that the water has been
contaminated, the following corrective measures must be taken:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the system must be disinfected, decontaminated,
  repaired or replaced, as the case may be, and flushed so as to ensure
  the water is free from contamination before it is used for any purpose
  set out in section 3;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cause of the contamination must be investigated;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the other conveyances in the operator's fleet must be
  assessed to determine whether their potable water systems have been
  contaminated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disinfection and flushing

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  potable water system must be disinfected and flushed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the frequency, if any, set out in column 3 of
    the schedule for the type of conveyance set out in column 1, that
    relates to the frequency of sampling conducted under section 12;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before it is placed in service or before it is
    returned to service after the conveyance has been taken out of
    operation for a season; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} before the system is returned to service after an
    activity, such as a repair or cleaning, that might lead to
    contamination of the water in the system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part of system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an isolated part of a potable water system is
  returned to service after an activity, such as a repair or cleaning,
  only that part of the system is required to be disinfected and flushed
  before it is returned to service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Water sampling after certain activities

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  each type of conveyance set out in column 1 of the schedule, water
  samples must be taken from the sampling sites set out in column 2
  after the activities referred to in paragraph 13(a) have been
  completed or a potable water system has been disinfected and flushed
  in accordance with paragraph 14(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The samples must be analyzed for the purpose of
  verifying that the water in the potable water system is free from
  contamination.

## [Potable Water Containers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Potable water containers must be
  readily moveable by one person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention of contamination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Measures must be taken to prevent the risk of
  contamination when a potable water container is loaded, stored, moved
  or handled on board a conveyance, and to ensure that the container is
  maintained in a sanitary condition.

## [Dispensing Water]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837928 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prevention of contamination

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Measures, such as the use of
  single-use drinking cups, must be taken to prevent the risk of
  contamination of water when it is dispensed in drinking cups.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Large container

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Measures, such as the use of a tap, must be taken to
  prevent the risk of contamination of water when it is dispensed from a
  container that has a capacity of 5 L or more.

## [Inspection Results]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837934 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Health
must inform the operator of the results of an inspection and of an
analysis of a water sample taken by an inspector for the purposes of
these Regulations.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-837937 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routine sampling

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record must be kept that identifies the conveyance and specifies the
  following information respecting each routine water sample taken under
  section 12:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sampling site and the location of the tap;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the sample is taken; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the results of the analysis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routine disinfection and flushing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A record must be kept that identifies the conveyance
  and specifies the date and time when any routine disinfection and
  flushing is conducted under paragraph 14(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period --- sampling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The record referred to in subsection (1) must be
  retained for a period of 12 months after the day on which the sample
  is taken or, if samples are taken on board a train at a frequency of
  once every 24 or 36 months, the record must be retained until the next
  sample is taken under section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period --- disinfection and
  flushing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The record referred to in subsection (2) must be
  retained for a period of 12 months after the day on which the
  disinfection and flushing is completed or, if samples are taken on
  board a train at a frequency of once every 24 or 36 months, the record
  must be retained for a period of 24 or 36 months, as the case may be,
  after that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities relating to contamination

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record must be kept that identifies the conveyance and specifies the
  date and time when its potable water system is disinfected,
  decontaminated, flushed, repaired or replaced in accordance with
  paragraph 13(a) or paragraph 14(1)(b) or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must also specify the following
  information respecting each water sample taken under section 15:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sampling site and the location of the tap;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the sample is taken;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the type of analysis conducted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the type of parameter that is the object of the
    analysis; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of the analysis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Information that is in the record and is referred to
  in subsection (1) must be retained for a period of 12 months after the
  day on which the relevant activity is completed and information that
  is in the record and is referred to in subsection (2) must be retained
  for a period of 12 months after the day on which the related sample is
  taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrective measures

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record must be kept that identifies the conveyance and specifies the
  following information respecting any corrective measure taken in
  respect of the results of an inspection conducted for the purposes of
  these Regulations or an analysis of a water sample taken during such
  an inspection:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measure taken; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the measure is taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must be retained for a period of 12 months
  after the day on which the corrective measure is completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record must be kept that identifies the conveyance and specifies the
  following information respecting the investigation and the assessment
  that are required by paragraphs 13(b) and (c):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the steps taken in the course of the investigation
    and the assessment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results obtained;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any corrective measure taken; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the corrective measure is
    completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must be retained for a period of 12 months
  after the day on which the corrective measure is completed or, if no
  corrective measure is taken, the day on which the results of the
  investigation and the assessment are known.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The records that are
required to be kept and retained under these Regulations must be readily
accessible for examination by an inspector in the course of the
enforcement of these Regulations.
