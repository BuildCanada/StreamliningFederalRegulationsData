## Family Support Orders and Agreements Garnishment Regulations {#family-support-orders-and-agreements-garnishment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-181
:::

[FAMILY ORDERS AND AGREEMENTS ENFORCEMENT ASSISTANCE
ACT](/eng/acts/F-1.4)

Registration 1988-03-17

Regulations Respecting the Garnishment of Federal Moneys to Satisfy
Support Orders and Support Provisions

P.C. 1988-473 1988-03-17
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 61 of the [Family Orders
and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar_e){#fnstar_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the garnishment of federal
moneys to satisfy support orders and support
provisions](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-181), effective on the day Part II
of the said Act comes into force.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911278 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Family Support Orders and Agreements Garnishment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-181).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911280 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Family Orders and Agreements
Enforcement Assistance Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4). (*Loi*)

## [Designation for the Purposes of the Definition "Garnishable Moneys"]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911282 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following Acts and
their provisions and programs are designated for the purposes of the
definition [[garnishable moneys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 23(1) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 164 and 216 of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) as they relate to the personal return of income
  of the taxpayer for a particular taxation year, excluding
  subsection 164(1.4) of that Act when it applies with respect to an
  amount payable pursuant to provincial income tax legislation that
  specifically excludes that amount from garnishment for the purposes of
  the Act;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} section 253 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
  as it relates to the rebate payable to employees and partners;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-101, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} section 7 of the [Department of Employment and
  Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7) as it relates to grants and
  contributions payable under the Skills Link program, the
  Apprenticeship Incentive Grant program or the Apprenticeship
  Completion Grant program;

- [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} the [Wage Earner Protection Program
  Act](/eng/acts/W-0.8), excluding the provisions relating to fees or
  expenses paid to a trustee or receiver under subsection 22(2) of that
  Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6),
  excluding the provisions relating to benefits paid on behalf of a
  beneficiary to a province or municipality;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), excluding
  the provisions relating to a benefit paid to a child of a disabled
  contributor under paragraph 44(1)(e) of that Act or to an orphan of a
  deceased contributor under paragraph 44(1)(f) of that Act and the
  provisions relating to amounts deducted from a benefit and paid to the
  government of a province in accordance with subsection 65(2) of that
  Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} sections 3, 11, 19 and 21 of the [Old Age Security
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9), excluding the provisions relating to amounts
  deducted from a benefit and paid to the government of a province in
  accordance with subsection 36(2) of that Act; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} section 7 of the [Department of Social Development
  Act](/eng/acts/S-11.5) as it relates to grants and contributions
  payable under the Opportunities Fund for Persons with Disabilities
  program.

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-189, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Order of Priority for Garnishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911296 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  two or more garnishable moneys become payable under any Acts, or any
  provisions thereof or programs thereunder, referred to in any
  provisions of section 3 on the same day to the same judgment debtor,
  those moneys shall be garnisheed according to the order in which those
  provisions are set out in section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to moneys that are
  already being garnisheed when additional garnishable moneys become
  payable to the judgment debtor and the moneys that are already being
  garnisheed shall continue to be garnisheed in priority to any other
  garnishable moneys.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-417, s. 2
:::

## [Period During Which Her Majesty Is Not Bound]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911306 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purposes of section 29 of the Act, the

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911312 .Part}

  purposes of sections 33 and 34 of the Act, service of documents on the
  Minister shall be effected, either in accordance with the law of the
  province of the court that issued the garnishee summons or by
  registered mail, at

  The Department of Justice,

  Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance Unit,

  Ottawa, Ontario

  K1A 0H8.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of documents by a provincial enforcement
  service may also be effected by the means of electronic communication
  that has been agreed on by it and the Department of Justice.

## [Time Limit for Response to Garnishee Summons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911322 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 41 of the Act, the prescribed time within which the Minister
shall respond to a garnishee summons on behalf of Her Majesty is 20 days
after the date of service of the documents referred to in section 28 of
the Act.

## [Notice to Judgment Debtor]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911324 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 45 of the Act, a notice from the Minister to the judgment debtor
named in a garnishee summons shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2
and shall be sent, within 20 days after the date of service of the
documents referred to in section 28 of the Act on the Minister, to each
address of the judgment debtor that is provided in the application to
garnishee referred to in section 5.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a fee in respect of
the processing of a garnishee summons, or any part of that fee, remains

Arrears owing \$![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-solid} as of

Any moneys that are payable to you by the Government of Canada under
Acts, funds or programs designated in the regulations made under the
[Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4) may be garnisheed to pay the judgment creditor
named in the summons.

If you have questions about the amount of money due, or if you wish to
dispute the garnishee summons, **please contact the court or the
provincial or territorial entity that issued the garnishee summons**.

After sufficient moneys are garnisheed to pay the judgment creditor, a
fee of up to \$38 for each year during which Her Majesty is bound by the
garnishee summons will be deducted from the moneys payable to you.

The garnishment of funds and the collection of the fee are authorized by
the [Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).
