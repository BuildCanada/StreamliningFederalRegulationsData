## African Development Fund Privileges and Immunities Order {#african-development-fund-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1304
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities of the African
Development Fund in Canada
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503134 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [African Development Fund Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1304).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503136 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the African Development
    Fund. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503140 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and shall, to such extent as may be required for the
  performance of its functions, have the privileges and immunities set
  forth in Articles II and III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of the Organization shall have in Canada, to such extent as
  may be required for the performance of their functions, the privileges
  and immunities set forth in Article IV of the Convention for
  representatives of Members.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization shall have in Canada,
  to such extent as may be required for the performance of their
  functions, the privileges and immunities set forth in Article V of the
  Convention for officials of the United Nations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall have in Canada, to such extent as may be required for the
  performance of their functions, the privileges and immunities set
  forth in Article VI of the Convention for experts on missions for the
  United Nations.
