## Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Licensing Regulations {#canadian-broiler-hatching-egg-and-chick-licensing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-516
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 1987-08-14

Regulations Respecting the Establishment of a System of Licensing of
Persons Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial or Export Trade of
Broiler Hatching Eggs or Chicks Produced for Chicken Production
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the Canadian Broiler Hatching
Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141-ID0EEAFA},
established the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant
to subsection 17(1) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8142){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8142-ID0EBAFA};

And Whereas the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency has been
empowered to implement a marketing plan pursuant to the Canadian Broiler
Hatching Egg Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141-ID0EBAEA};

Therefore, the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant
to paragraph 23(1)(f) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8142){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8142-ID0EGADA}
and section 7 of the schedule to the Canadian Broiler Hatching Egg
Marketing Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141-ID0EDADA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the establishment of a
system of licensing of persons engaged in the marketing in
interprovincial or export trade of broiler hatching eggs or chicks
produced for chicken production](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-516), effective
October 1, 1987.

Ottawa, Ontario, June 30, 1987

The National Farm Products Marketing Council, pursuant to paragraph
7(1)(e) of the Farm Products Marketing Agencies Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141){#fn_Document1_e_hq_8141-ID0EDABA},
being satisfied that the annexed Regulations are necessary for the
administration of the marketing plan that the Canadian Broiler Hatching
Egg Marketing Agency is authorized to implement, hereby approves the
annexed [Regulations respecting the establishment of a system of
licensing of persons engaged in the marketing in interprovincial or
export trade of broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced for chicken
production](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-516), made by the Canadian Broiler
Hatching Egg Marketing Agency on June 30, 1987.

Ottawa, Ontario, August 13, 1987.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907497 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to persons engaged in the marketing in
  interprovincial or export trade of broiler hatching eggs or chicks
  produced in a signatory province for chicken production; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to persons engaged in the interprovincial marketing
  from a non-signatory province into a signatory province of broiler
  hatching eggs or chicks produced in the non-signatory province for
  chicken production.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Buyers referred to in subsection (1) must not
  knowingly engage in the marketing in interprovincial trade of chicks
  produced for chicken production except with a person who holds an
  appropriate licence issued by the Agency under these Regulations and,
  in the case of chicks produced in a non-signatory province, unless the
  person holds an orderly marketing quota issued under the [Canadian

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907515 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No buyer
  shall engage in the marketing in interprovincial or export trade of
  broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced in a signatory province for
  chicken production unless the buyer holds a buyer's licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No seller shall engage in the marketing in
  interprovincial or export trade of broiler hatching eggs or chicks
  produced in a signatory province for chicken production unless the
  seller holds a seller's licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No buyer shall engage in the interprovincial
  marketing from a non-signatory province into a signatory province of
  broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced for chicken production unless
  the buyer holds a buyer's licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No seller shall engage in the interprovincial
  marketing from a non-signatory province into a signatory province of
  broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced for chicken production unless
  the seller holds a seller's licence.

## [Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-907521 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8, the
Agency shall issue a licence to any person who applies for one.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-580, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A licence

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expires on December 31 of the year for which it is
  issued; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not transferable.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A licence is subject to the
following terms and conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom a licence is issued

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at all times during the term of the licence
    complies with the orders, regulations or requirements of the Agency
    or the National Farm Products Council that are applicable to that
    person,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at all times during the term of the licence
    complies with the orders, regulations or directives made pursuant to
    a provincial broiler hatching egg marketing plan referred to in the
    Federal-Provincial Agreement for Broiler Hatching Eggs, authorized
    by Order in Council P.C. 1986-2653[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_star){#fn_star-ID0EBBCCA}, that are
    applicable to that person,

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_star-ID0EBBCCA){#fn_star}Not published
      in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part II.
      :::

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} submits to the Agency in respect of the period
    set out in the licence a report containing the information set out
    in the schedule, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} keeps complete and accurate books and records of
    all matters related to the marketing in interprovincial or export
    trade of broiler hatching eggs or chicks produced for chicken
    production by the person and retains the books and records for a
    period of not less than six years after the day of the last entry
    therein; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom a licence is issued does not
  knowingly engage

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the marketing in interprovincial trade of chicks
    except with a person who holds an appropriate licence or who is a
    buyer referred to in subsection 3.1(1),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the marketing in interprovincial or export
    trade of broiler hatching eggs produced in a signatory province,
    except for broiler hatching eggs that have been produced by a
    producer who has been allotted an interprovincial quota or export
    quota pursuant to the [Canadian Hatching Egg Producers Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-209), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the interprovincial marketing from a
    non-signatory province into a signatory province of broiler hatching
    eggs produced in the non-signatory province, except for broiler
    hatching eggs that have been marketed by a person who has been
    allotted an orderly marketing quota pursuant to the [Canadian
    Broiler Hatching Egg and Chick Orderly Marketing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-283).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-580, s. 2
- SOR/2008-11, s. 2
- SOR/2008-12, s. 2
- SOR/2013-254, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may refuse to issue a licence to an applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is a producer, if the applicant has not been
    allotted a quota by the Agency pursuant to the Canadian Broiler
    Hatching Egg and Chick Orderly Marketing Quota Regulations or the
    [Canadian Hatching Egg Producers Quota
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-209);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant does not comply with any order,
    regulation or requirement of the Agency or the National Farm
    Products Council that is applicable to the applicant; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant does not comply with any order,
    regulation or directive made pursuant to a provincial broiler
    hatching egg marketing plan referred to in the Federal-Provincial
    Agreement for Broiler Hatching Eggs, authorized by Order in Council
    P.C. 1986-2653[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#a){#a-ID0EBBACA},
    that is applicable to the applicant.

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#a-ID0EBBACA){#a}Not
      published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part
      II.
      :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person to whom a licence was issued fails to
  comply with any term or condition to which the licence is subject, the
  Agency may suspend, cancel or refuse to renew the licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-11, s. 2
- SOR/2013-254, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20071221.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Agency intends to refuse to issue or renew a licence or intends to
  suspend or cancel a licence, the Agency shall give notice of its
  intention to the applicant or the person to whom the licence was
  issued by personal service or registered mail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall set
  out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the Agency bases its
    intention; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which and the time and place at which
    the applicant or the person to whom the licence was issued may show
    cause why the licence should be issued or renewed or should not be
    suspended or cancelled.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The day referred to in paragraph (2)(b) shall be not
  less than 30 days after the day of the service or mailing of the
  notice.
