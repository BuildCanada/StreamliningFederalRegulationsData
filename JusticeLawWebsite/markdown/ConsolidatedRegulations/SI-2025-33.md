## Order Transferring from the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada {#order-transferring-from-the-president-of-the-kings-privy-council-for-canada-to-the-minister-of-industry-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-pacific-economic-development-agency-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the President of the King's Privy Council for
Canada to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in
Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada

P.C. 2025-411 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2‍(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753247){#fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753247-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753277){#fn_81000-3-2868_hq_1741753277-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada to
the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions of the
President of the King's Privy Council for Canada in relation to the
Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada.
