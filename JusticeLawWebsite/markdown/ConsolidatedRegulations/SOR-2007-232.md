## Westbank First Nation Land Registry Regulations {#westbank-first-nation-land-registry-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-232
:::

[WESTBANK FIRST NATION SELF-GOVERNMENT ACT](/eng/acts/W-6.2)

Registration 2007-10-25

Westbank First Nation Land Registry Regulations

P.C. 2007-1638 2007-10-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 13 of the [Westbank First Nation Self-Government
Act](/eng/acts/W-6.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_609513-E_hq_2736){#fn_609513-E_hq_2736-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Westbank First Nation Land Registry
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-232).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who submits an
    application for registration or recording.
     ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[general abstract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[general abstract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a reserve,
    means an electronic abstract of registered interests and recorded
    documents that affect all lands on the reserve. ([répertoire
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Land Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Land Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the office referred to
    in section 2. ([bureau d'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[official plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a graphical description
    of boundaries of land prepared from field notes of a survey
    confirmed under section 29 of the [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6). ([plan officiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parcel abstract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parcel abstract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an electronic abstract
    of registered interests and recorded documents that affect an
    individual parcel of Westbank lands. ([répertoire de
    lot]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Register]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Westbank Lands
    Register. ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer in the
    Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development responsible
    for the management of the Land
    Registry. ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registration plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registration plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a graphical
    description of the boundaries of land made by the Surveyor General
    under section 31 of the [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6). ([plan d'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[textual description]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[textual description]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a metes and bounds
    description of Westbank lands or any other description of Westbank
    lands that does not refer exclusively to one or more complete
    parcels on a registration plan or official plan. ([description
    textuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Operation of Registry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735292 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registry location

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Register shall be
maintained in an office located in the National Capital Region and known
as the Westbank First Nation Land Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Office hours

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Land
  Registry shall be open to the public from Monday to Friday, except on
  holidays, from 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. EST or EDST, as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic registration or recording

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a document may be transmitted
  for registration or recording in accordance with section 12 at any
  time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on activities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Neither the Registrar nor
any employee of the Land Registry shall express an opinion about any
interest or licence in Westbank land, including whether

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a document submitted for registration or recording is
  validly made; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document has been registered, as opposed to having
  been recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of Register

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, during the
hours referred to in subsection 3(1), inspect at the Land Registry the
electronic image of any document that is registered or recorded in the
Register.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** On request, the Registrar
shall provide a copy or certified copy of a document registered or
recorded in the Register.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registrar's certificate

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** On request, the Registrar
shall issue a certificate indicating all the interests registered and
other documents recorded on the abstract of a specified parcel of
Westbank land.

## [Abstracts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735310 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abstracts

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The following abstracts
shall be included in the Register:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each reserve of the Westbank First Nation, a
  general abstract; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each parcel of Westbank land, a parcel abstract.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall not
create a parcel abstract unless the parcel abstract refers exclusively
to a lot described on an official plan or registration plan.

## [Application to Register or Record Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735317 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to register or record

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may apply for the registration or recording in the Register of
  a document that affects Westbank land

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in person, by submitting the document and a
    completed application for registration or recording to the Registrar
    at the address set out in section 11;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by mailing the document and a completed application
    for registration or recording to the Registrar at the address set
    out in section 11; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} electronically, in the manner set out in
    section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for registration or recording shall
  contain the following information in relation to the document
  submitted for registration or recording:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all parties to the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person who completed
    the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the type of document;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the date of execution of the
    document or, if there is more than one date indicated, the most
    recent date;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the province where the Westbank land is located;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the Westbank First Nation, the name of
    the reserve and the lot number, if any, associated with the land;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the land description;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of any supporting documents accompanying the
    document submitted for registration or recording; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if two or more documents that are to be registered
    or recorded are submitted together by mail, directions regarding the
    order to be respected in registering or recording each document
    relative to each other.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission by mail

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A document to be
registered or recorded and a completed application for registration or
recording may be mailed or delivered in person to the Registrar at

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic transmission

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document to be registered or recorded may be submitted electronically
  to the Registrar

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by completing an application for registration or
    recording online at the Registry Internet website at
    [www.ainc-inac.gc.ca](http://www.ainc-inac.gc.ca); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} attaching the document as an electronic file in a
    form referred to in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}File format

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document transmitted electronically to the
  Registrar must be in the form of one or more images contained in a
  single file, scanned from the original document at a resolution of at
  least 200 dpi, in Adobe Acrobat (.pdf) format or tagged image file
  (.tif) format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fax transmissions

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Documents shall not be
transmitted for registration or recording by fax.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tracking number

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of an application and document that were transmitted
  electronically in accordance with section 12, the Registrar shall
  transmit to the applicant by e-mail an acknowledgement of receipt and
  a tracking number and provide a copy of the acknowledgement and
  tracking number to the Westbank First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tracking number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application and document that were
  submitted for registration or recording by mail, the Registrar shall
  mail an acknowledgement of receipt and a tracking number to the
  applicant and provide a copy of the acknowledgement and tracking
  number to the Westbank First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original document

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The following documents
may be submitted for registration or recording instead of an original
document:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a judgment or court order, certified by the
  court;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a document registered in a provincial
  registry or land titles system, certified by the appropriate
  provincial registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a death certificate, marriage certificate,
  name change certificate, power of attorney, will, approval or probate
  of will or appointment of administrator, certified by the person who
  has custody of the original;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a certificate of amalgamation or change of
  name of a corporation, certified by the agency responsible for
  recording the amalgamation or change of name;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of an order in council, certified by the Clerk
  of the Privy Council;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a ministerial order; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a document issued by the Westbank First
  Nation, certified by the person who has custody of the original.

## [Grounds for Refusing Registration or Recording]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735365 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Formal requirements

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall not
register or record a document if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is not dated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is not legible;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it does not identify each party to the document;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in the application for
  registration or recording conflicts with the information contained in
  the document; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the land description does not meet the requirements
  of subsection 17(1) or section 18 or 19.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survey requirements

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  document that purports to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} grant an interest or licence in Westbank land for a
    term of 10 or more years, other than an interest or licence granted
    to a member of the Westbank First Nation,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} grant a strata title, a condominium interest or any
    similar interest,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} allocate or transfer lawful possession of Westbank
    land to a member, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} expropriate an interest in Westbank land for a
    community purpose under section 105 of the Agreement,

  shall be registered or recorded unless the land description contained
  in the document consists exclusively of a reference to one or more
  complete parcels on a registration plan or official plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal or extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The term of a document referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) includes the period of any right of renewal or
  extension of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to easements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for land descriptions

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a document purports to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exchange Westbank land for land that is to become
  Westbank land in accordance with sections 92 to 95 of the Agreement,
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} define the exterior boundaries of Westbank land,

the Registrar shall not register or record the document unless the land
description in the document is derived from an official plan or
registration plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Utilities

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall not
register or record a document that contains a textual description unless
the document grants an interest in unencumbered land, or a licence to a
utility company in respect of unencumbered land, for the purpose of
providing service to the residents of a reserve.

## [Registration or Recording of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735392 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of registration or recording

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 21, the Registrar
  shall register or record each document submitted for registration or
  recording that accords with the requirements of these Regulations in
  the order that it is received by the Land Registry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of registration or recording

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document is considered to have been registered or
  recorded

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the document was transmitted electronically, at
    the time and date indicated on the electronic acknowledgement of
    receipt; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the document was sent by mail, at the time and
    date of receipt stamped on the document by the Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple submissions

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If two or more documents
affecting the same parcel of Westbank land are submitted for
registration or recording at the same time by the same person and the
documents meet the requirements of these Regulations for registration or
recording, the Registrar shall register or record them in the order
indicated on the applications for registration or recording.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registry information

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  document submitted for registration or recording meets the
  requirements of these Regulations, the Registrar shall record the
  following information in the appropriate abstract:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of all parties to the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Westbank land affected;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the date of execution of the
    document or, if there is more than one date indicated, the most
    recent date;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the tracking number assigned to the document at the
    time of receipt; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of registration or recording.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General abstract

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document submitted for registration or recording
  affects a reserve of the Westbank First Nation as a whole, the
  Registrar shall register or record in the general abstract for the
  reserve the information required under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parcel abstract

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a document submitted for registration or recording
  affects a particular parcel of Westbank land, the Registrar shall
  register or record in the parcel abstract for that parcel the
  information required under subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** On the registration or
recording of a document referred to in section 15, the Registrar shall
notify the applicant and Westbank First Nation of the registration or
recording without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration or recording refused

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  document submitted for registration or recording does not meet the
  requirements of these Regulations, the Registrar shall return to the
  applicant the application for registration or recording and all
  supporting documents accompanying it, and provide written reasons for
  the refusal to register or record the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall provide a copy of the reasons for
  refusal to the Westbank First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic storage

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall store
in electronic form every document registered or recorded under these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of registered document

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** After a document that was
submitted to the Registrar in paper form has been registered or
recorded, the Registrar shall return the original document to the
applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors in registration or recording

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If the Registrar becomes
aware of an omission or error in the registration or recording of a
document, the Registrar shall immediately make the necessary
corrections, add a notation to the Register identifying the corrections
and notify all persons who may be adversely affected by the corrections.

## [Effect of Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 31, interests
  registered under these Regulations that affect the same parcel of
  Westbank land have priority according to the time and date of their
  registration and not according to the time and date of their
  execution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No priority to licences and other
  documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not apply
  to a document that does not grant an interest in Westbank land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unregistered interests

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A registered interest
affecting a parcel of Westbank land is entitled to priority over an
unregistered interest affecting the same parcel of Westbank land.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revolving mortgages

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A registered mortgage has
priority over a subsequently registered interest that affects the same
parcel of Westbank land, to the extent of the money actually advanced
under the mortgage, to a maximum of the amount secured by the mortgage,
even if all or part of the money was advanced after the registration of
the subsequently registered interest, unless the mortgagee had actual
notice of the registration of the subsequently registered interest
before making the advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deferred priority

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a registered interest, or a person applying to register an
  interest, may apply to record a postponement agreement that gives
  priority over the registered interest to a specified interest that
  was, or is to be, subsequently registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority on recording

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the recording of a postponement agreement,
  priority shall be accorded to the interests referred to in the
  agreement in the manner provided for in the agreement.

## [Conflicts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735443 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations paramount

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a
conflict between these Regulations and any Westbank law, these
Regulations prevail to the extent of the conflict.

## [Transition to Priority System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735446 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior registered interest

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interest that was registered in the Register before the day on which
  these Regulations come into force has priority over an interest
  registered under these Regulations on or after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No relative priorities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) shall not be interpreted to confer a
  priority on one interest that was registered in the Register before
  the day on which these Regulations come into force over any other
  interest that was also registered in the Register before that day.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-735452 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force date

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on November 5, 2007.
