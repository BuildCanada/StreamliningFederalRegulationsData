- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare that the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2)
  is applicable

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in whole to the United Kingdom of Great Britain and
    Northern Ireland,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with the exception of Parts III and V thereof, to
    the Commonwealth of Australia and the Dominion of New Zealand, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} with the exception of Part VI thereof, to all the
    other designated states; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designate as a civilian component of a visiting force
  from one of the countries listed hereinafter the civilian personnel
  accompanying that visiting force who are in the employ of that force
  and who are not

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} stateless persons,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} nationals of any state that is not a designated
    state, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} citizens of or ordinarily resident in Canada:
