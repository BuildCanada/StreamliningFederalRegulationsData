## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Life Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activities

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 495(2)(f) of the Act, the following activities
  are prescribed in relation to an entity and a life company that
  acquires control of, or acquires or increases a substantial investment
  in, the entity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of safety and risk prevention
    services and services respecting risk management and claims
    adjustment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operation of rehabilitation and training and
    development centres;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision of computer systems to independent
    insurance brokers and agents;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the provision of support to independent insurance
    brokers and agents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the operation of repair and appraisal centres; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other activities that are reasonably ancillary
    to the business of insurance carried on by the life company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed term or condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 495(2)(f) of the Act, a
  prescribed term or condition is that the activities referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (e) must be reasonably ancillary to the business
  of insurance carried on by the life company.

## [Societies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673178 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 554(2)(f) of the Act in relation to an entity and a society
that acquires control of, or acquires or increases a substantial
investment in, the entity, prescribed activities are activities that are
reasonably ancillary to the society's insurance business.

## [Insurance Holding Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673181 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activities

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 971(2)(f) of the Act, the following activities
  are prescribed in relation to an entity and an insurance holding
  company that acquires control of, or acquires or increases a
  substantial investment in, the entity:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of safety and risk prevention
    services and services respecting risk management and claims
    adjustment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operation of rehabilitation and training and
    development centres;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision of computer systems to independent
    insurance brokers and agents;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the provision of support to independent insurance
    brokers and agents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the operation of repair and appraisal centres; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other activities that are reasonably ancillary
    to the business of insurance carried on by a company controlled by
    the insurance holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed term or condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 971(2)(f) of the Act, a
  prescribed term or condition is that the activities referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (e) must be reasonably ancillary to the business
  of insurance carried on by a company controlled by the insurance
  holding company.
