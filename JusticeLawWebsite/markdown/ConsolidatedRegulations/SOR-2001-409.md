## [Permitted Use of Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675771 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of name of bank or bank holding
company

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A person may use the name
of a bank or of a bank holding company in a prospectus, offering
memorandum, takeover bid circular, advertisement for a transaction
related to securities or in any other document in connection with a
transaction related to securities if the use is required by law or if
the bank or bank holding company, as the case may be, has given its
written permission for the use.
