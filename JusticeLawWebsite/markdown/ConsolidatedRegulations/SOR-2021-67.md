[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force of law

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Protocol on Rules of Origin and Origin Procedures of the
Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement, as
incorporated by reference in the Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity
Agreement, have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 1 and 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 1 to 2C and 3 of Article 3;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4 to 17; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Annexes 1, 4, 5, 5‑A and 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2021-67_e_transform_hq_28950){#fn_SOR-2021-67_e_transform_hq_28950-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 37 of the [Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.56), chapter 1 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2021, comes into force, but if they are registered after that
day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
