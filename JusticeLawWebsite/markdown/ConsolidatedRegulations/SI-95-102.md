## Order Transferring to the Minister of National Revenue, the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Industry under the Tax Rebate Discounting Act {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-national-revenue-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-industry-under-the-tax-rebate-discounting-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-102
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-09-20

Order Transferring to the Minister of National Revenue, the Powers,
Duties and Functions of the Minister of Industry under the Tax Rebate
Discounting Act

P.C. 1995-1481  1995-08-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the expression "Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs" in
the definition [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of
the [Tax Rebate Discounting Act](/eng/acts/T-3) was replaced by the
expression "Minister of Industry" pursuant to paragraph 62(1)(t) of the
Department of Industry Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6600){#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6600-ID0EDCBA},
which Act was brought into force by Order in Council P.C. 1995-536 of
March 29, 1995[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6601){#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6601-ID0EBCBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6600-ID0EDCBA){#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6600}S.C.
  1995, c. 1
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6601-ID0EBCBA){#fn_SI-95-102_e_hq_6601}SI/95-48,
  1995 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1013
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased hereby to transfer from the Minister of
Industry to the Minister of National Revenue the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Industry under the [Tax Rebate Discounting
Act](/eng/acts/T-3).
