## Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-111
:::

[CANADA FOUNDATION FOR SUSTAINABLE DEVELOPMENT TECHNOLOGY
ACT](/eng/acts/C-5.5)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating the Minister of Industry as the Minister for the
purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-1258 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canada Foundation
for Sustainable Development Technology Act](/eng/acts/C-5.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223){#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223-ID0EBDAA},
effective November 4, 2015,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223-ID0EBDAA){#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223}S.C.
  2001, c. 23
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2002-365 of March 14,
  2002[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217){#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217}SI/2002-58
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Industry, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
  the [Canada Foundation for Sustainable Development Technology
  Act](/eng/acts/C-5.5)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223){#fn_81000-3-1111-E_hq_16223-ID0EBAAAA}.
