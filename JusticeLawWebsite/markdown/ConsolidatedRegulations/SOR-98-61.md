Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-61) give effect, in part, to a
public announcement made on October 27, 1997 and are therefore, by
virtue of paragraph 164(4)(a.2)[[Footnote

subsection 12(2) and paragraph 164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EJEAA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote

133(f) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EDEAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Returning Persons Exemption
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-61).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025498 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[exemption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the benefit of free rates of
customs duty given to goods that are classified under heading No. 98.04
where the value of the goods does not exceed the applicable amount set
out in a tariff item under heading No. 98.04. (*exemption*)

## [Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025500 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), any person returning to Canada shall, at
  the time of the person's return, report in writing the goods in
  respect of which an exemption is claimed and express the value of
  those goods in Canadian dollars.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person returning to Canada may make an oral
  report in respect of the goods referred to in subsection (1) if the
  goods are in the actual possession of the person, or form part of the
  person's baggage carried on board the same conveyance as the person,
  and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is returning to Canada on board a
    non-commercial passenger conveyance or a bus; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is returning to Canada on board a
    highway conveyance at a land border crossing at a designated customs
    office and is authorized under the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323) to present themself in
    an alternate manner under subparagraph 11(d)(ii) of those
    Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the goods referred to in subsections (1)
  and (2) are intended for the exclusive use or benefit of a child who
  is too young to make a report, the report may be made on behalf of the
  child by the parent or guardian of the child.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-388, s. 1
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025509 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is a
  condition of importing goods that are classified under tariff item No.
  9804.10.00, 9804.20.00 or 9804.30.00 and that have a combined value in
  excess of the amount of the exemption that the exemption be applied to
  those goods that are subject to the highest rate of customs duties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exemption does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alcoholic beverages imported by a person who has
    not attained the minimum age at which a person may lawfully purchase
    alcoholic beverages in the province where the customs office through
    which the alcoholic beverages are imported is located; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} tobacco or vaping products (other than a [[vaping
    product drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
    [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)) imported by a person who has
    not attained 18 years of age.
