## IAC Limited Shareholders Order {#iac-limited-shareholders-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-798
:::

[AN ACT TO INCORPORATE CONTINENTAL BANK OF CANADA](/eng/acts/C-38.5)

Registration 1981-10-09

Order Dispensing with the Submission of a Proposed Amalgamation
Agreement to the Shareholders of Iac Limited

P.C. 1981-2727 1981-10-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 10(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFABA} of An Act to
Incorporate Continental Bank of Canada, is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order dispensing with the submission of a proposed amalgamation
agreement to the shareholders of IAC
Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-798).

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872247 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [IAC Limited Shareholders Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-798).

## [Dispensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872249 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any
provision of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the proposed amalgamation
agreement between Continental Bank of Canada and IAC Limited need not be
submitted to the shareholders of IAC Limited.
