## Public Service Official Languages Exclusion Approval Order {#public-service-official-languages-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-118
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2005-12-14

Public Service Official Languages Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2005-2040 2005-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply the
provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBDA} respecting
official language proficiency to certain persons described in the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBDA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, s. 12 and 13
  :::

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)^a^, hereby makes the
annexed [Public Service Official Languages Exclusion Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2005-118).

Ottawa, October 14, 2005

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)^a^, hereby approves the
annexed [Public Service Official Languages Exclusion Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2005-118) made by the Public Service
Commission.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611615 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Employment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agreement to become bilingual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement to become bilingual]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    agreement in writing by which a person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} undertakes to attain the level of official
      language proficiency required for a bilingual position, through
      language training at public expense, within a period of two years
      beginning on the later of the date of the written agreement of
      appointment to the bilingual position and the effective date of
      the appointment; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} agrees that if, at the end of the two-year
      period, the person has not attained the level of language
      proficiency required for the bilingual position, the person will
      be appointed or deployed on an indeterminate basis to a position
      for which the person meets the essential qualifications referred
      to in paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act and that is of a similar level
      and salary as the bilingual position. ([engagement de devenir
      bilingue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bilingual position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bilingual position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a position
    identified by the deputy head as one for which the work to be
    performed requires proficiency in both official languages. ([poste
    bilingue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-imperative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-imperative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    appointment to a bilingual position, means that the appointment is
    for an indeterminate period and the bilingual position has been
    identified by the deputy head as not requiring, at the time of the
    appointment, occupation by a person who meets the required level of
    proficiency in both official languages. ([nomination non
    impérative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unilingual person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unilingual person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a
    bilingual position, a person who meets the required level of
    proficiency in only one of the official languages. ([personne
    unilingue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Non-Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611625 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** No person is excluded by
this Order from any requirement to meet any qualifications with respect
to shorthand, typing, translation, editing, proofreading, revising,
writing or interpretation or to meet any specialized or expert
proficiency requirements in one or both official languages.

## [Agreement to Become Bilingual]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611628 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from paragraph 30(2)(a) of the
Act with respect to official language proficiency

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A unilingual person who
enters into an agreement to become bilingual in order to be appointed on
a non-imperative basis to a bilingual position is excluded from the
operation of paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act with respect to the official
language proficiency required for the appointment.

## [Medical Grounds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611631 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from paragraph 30(2)(a) of the
Act with respect to official language proficiency

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a unilingual person has
a long-term or recurring physical, mental or learning impairment that
makes the person unable to attain, through language training, the
official language proficiency required for a bilingual position, the
person is excluded from the operation of paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act
with respect to that requirement for a non-imperative appointment to
that position.

## [Eligibility for an Immediate Annuity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from paragraph 30(2)(a) of the
Act with respect to official language proficiency

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If a unilingual person is
eligible for an immediate annuity under the [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) within two years after a
non-imperative appointment to a bilingual position and the person
submits an irrevocable resignation to take effect no later than the end
of that two-year period, the person is excluded from the operation of
paragraph 30(2)(a) of the Act with respect to the official language
proficiency required for that appointment.
