## National Parks Land Rents Remission Order {#national-parks-land-rents-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-23
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-04-12

National Parks Land Rents Remission Order

P.C. 2000-451 2000-03-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[National Parks Land Rents Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-23).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
amount set out in section 2 to every person who holds a leasehold
interest in a public land that is situated in a national park set out in
Schedule I to the National Parks Act if the amount of rent payable in
respect of that land has been or is due to be fixed in the year 2000 for
a ten-year period beginning in that year, in accordance with the terms
of the applicable lease and the National Parks Lease and Licence of
Occupation Regulations (1991).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 1 shall be equal to the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the first year of the ten-year
  period beginning in 2000

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the last year of the ten-year
  period ending in 2000.
