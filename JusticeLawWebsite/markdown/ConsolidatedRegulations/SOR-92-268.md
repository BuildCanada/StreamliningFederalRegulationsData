## Pulp and Paper Mill Defoamer and Wood Chip Regulations {#pulp-and-paper-mill-defoamer-and-wood-chip-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-268
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1992-05-07

Regulations Respecting the Manufacture, Import, Offering for Sale, Sale
and Use of Defoamers Containing Dibenzofuran or Dibenzo-Para-Dioxin at
Pulp and Paper Mills Using Chlorine Bleaching Processes and of Wood
Chips Containing Polychlorinated Phenols at All Pulp and Paper Mills

P.C. 1992-960 1992-05-07
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EGACA}, the
Minister of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on December 14, 1991, a copy
of the proposed Regulations respecting the manufacture, import, offering
for sale, sale and use of defoamers containing dibenzofuran or
dibenzo-para-dioxin at pulp and paper mills using chlorine bleaching
processes and of wood chips containing polychlorinated phenols at all
pulp and paper mills, and amending the List of Toxic Substances in
Schedule I to the Canadian Environmental Protection Act^\*^,
substantially in the form annexed hereto;

And Whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act^\*^, the
annexed Regulations do not regulate an aspect of any substance that is
regulated by or under any other Act of Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
section 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0ELCAA} of
the Canadian Environmental Protection Act^\*^, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of National Health and
Welfare and after the federal-provincial advisory committee has been
given an opportunity to provide its advice under section 6 of the
Act^\*^, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting
the manufacture, import, offering for sale, sale and use of defoamers
containing dibenzofuran or dibenzo-para-dioxin at pulp and paper mills
using chlorine bleaching processes and of wood chips containing
polychlorinated phenols at all pulp and paper mills, and amending the
List of Toxic Substances in Schedule I to the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act^\*^.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942292 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Pulp and Paper Mill Defoamer and Wood Chip
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-268).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942294 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[chlorine bleaching process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chlorine bleaching process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a process
    using chlorine, chlorine dioxide or chlorine bleach to bleach pulp
    in a mill; ([procédé de blanchiment au chlore]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[defoamer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[defoamer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any product that contains
    dibenzofuran or dibenzo-para-dioxin and that is added to the
    water-pulp mixture during the manufacture of pulp in a mill to
    prevent the production of foam or reduce the amount of foam that
    would otherwise be produced; ([additif antimousse]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dibenzofuran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dibenzofuran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the dibenzofuran
    described in item 15 of the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1
    to the Act; ([dibenzofuranne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dibenzo-para-dioxin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dibenzo-para-dioxin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    dibenzo-para-dioxin described in item 14 of the List of Toxic
    Substances in Schedule 1 to the Act;
    ([dibenzo-para-dioxine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plant that produces pulp,
    paper, paperboard, hardboard, insulating or building board;
    ([fabrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person in charge of the
    operation of a mill; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ppb]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ppb]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means parts per billion, being parts per
    10^9^ parts by weight; ([ppb]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[polychlorinated phenols]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[polychlorinated phenols]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means phenol and
    salts of phenol in which at least two phenyl hydrogen atoms are
    replaced by chlorine atoms; ([phénols polychlorés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wood chips]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood chips]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes sawdust, shavings and
    other wood residues used to manufacture pulp in a mill. ([copeaux de
    bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-102, s. 12
:::

## [Owner of a Mill]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942308 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a mill shall
ensure that the operator operates the mill in accordance with these
Regulations and any person working at the mill complies with these
Regulations.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942310 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use a defoamer at a mill that uses a chlorine bleaching
  process, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an analysis of a sample of each batch of the
    defoamer indicates that the defoamer contains

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 40 ppb or less by weight of dibenzofuran, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10 ppb or less by weight of dibenzo-para-dioxin;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certificate provided by the manufacturer of the
    defoamer indicates in respect of a batch of that defoamer that the
    batch contains

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 40 ppb or less by weight of dibenzofuran, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10 ppb or less by weight of dibenzo-para-dioxin.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, import, offer for sale
  or sell, for use in a mill in Canada that uses a chlorine bleaching
  process, a defoamer in which the concentration of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dibenzofuran exceeds 40 ppb; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dibenzo-para-dioxin exceeds 10 ppb.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall import, offer for sale or sell for
  use in a mill in Canada, or use in a mill in Canada, wood chips that
  have been made from wood that has been treated with polychlorinated
  phenols.

## [Analyses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942323 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, concentrations of dibenzofuran and dibenzo-para-dioxin
shall be measured in accordance with the Reference Method for the
Determination of Dibenzofuran and Dibenzo-p-dioxin in Defoamers,
published by the Department of the Environment, Report 1/RM/20, 1991, as
amended from time to time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-102, s. 13
:::

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942327 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), every person in Canada who manufactures, imports,
  offers for sale or sells defoamers for use in a mill in Canada that
  uses a chlorine bleaching process shall submit to the Minister, for
  each quarter of a given year, not later than 60 days after the last
  day of the quarter, a report containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the manufacturer, importer
    or vendor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of defoamer in a batch and the batch
    number of each batch of defoamer manufactured, imported or sold
    during that quarter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the concentrations of dibenzofuran and
    dibenzo-para-dioxin in each batch of defoamer manufactured,
    imported, offered for sale or sold in that quarter; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, telephone number and signature of
    the person who provides the information on behalf of the
    manufacturer, importer or vendor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), every operator that uses a
  chlorine bleaching process in which defoamers are used shall submit to
  the Minister, for each quarter of a given year, not later than 60 days
  after the last day of the quarter, a report containing the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the mill;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of defoamer in, and the batch number
    of, each batch of that defoamer, and the name of the manufacturer,
    importer or vendor from which each batch of defoamer was received
    during the quarter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the analysis referred to in
    paragraph 4(1)(a) or of the certificate referred to in
    paragraph 4(1)(b); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, telephone number and signature of
    the person who provides the information on behalf of the mill.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding paragraphs (1)(c) and (2)(c), where
  the concentrations of dibenzofuran and dibenzo-para-dioxin set out in
  reports in respect of four consecutive quarters that are submitted by
  a person pursuant to this section have not exceeded the concentrations
  set out in subsection 4(1), the person may, for the next quarter,
  submit to the Minister the following information in respect of the
  concentrations of dibenzofuran and dibenzo-para-dioxin:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the person is a manufacturer, importer or
    vendor of defoamers, the concentrations of dibenzofuran and
    dibenzo-para-dioxin in each type of defoamer manufactured, imported
    or sold during the quarter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person is an operator that uses a
    chlorine bleaching process in which defoamers are used, a copy of an
    analysis of a sample of each type of defoamer used or a copy of a
    certificate provided by the manufacturer of each defoamer that
    indicates that the type of defoamer contains

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 40 ppb or less by weight of dibenzofuran, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10 ppb or less by weight of dibenzo-para-dioxin.

## [Maintenance of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-942344 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every operator that uses a
chlorine bleaching process and that receives a defoamer for use in the
mill shall record the information set out in Schedule I and keep the
record for a period of five years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-364, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
manufactures, imports, offers for sale or sells a defoamer for use in a
mill in Canada that uses a chlorine bleaching process shall record the
information set out in Schedule II and keep the record for a period of
five years.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every operator shall keep
dated records in respect of each delivery to the mill of wood chips that
originated from a wood processing facility where polychlorinated phenols
are used to treat wood, showing the quantity of wood chips delivered,
the date of delivery and the name of the wood processing facility from
which the wood chips originated, and shall keep those records for a
period of five years.
