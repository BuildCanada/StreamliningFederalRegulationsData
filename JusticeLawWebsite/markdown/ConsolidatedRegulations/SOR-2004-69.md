## Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations, No. 2 {#canada-customs-and-revenue-agency-regulations-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-69
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2004-03-30

Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations, No. 2

P.C. 2004-353 2004-03-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 36(1)(b) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), hereby makes the
annexed [Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations, No.
2](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-69).
:::

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714160 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 37.3(1) and (2)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) apply to the
portion of the Public Service known as the Canada Customs and Revenue
Agency.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714162 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations cease to
have effect at 00:00:03, April 1, 2004.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714164 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force at 00:00:01, April 1, 2004.
