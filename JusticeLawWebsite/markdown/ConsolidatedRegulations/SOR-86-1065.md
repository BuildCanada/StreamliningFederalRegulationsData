## Customs Sufferance Warehouses Regulations {#customs-sufferance-warehouses-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Customs Sufferance Warehouses Regulations

P.C. 1986-2485 1986-11-06
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFABA}, a copy of
proposed [Regulations respecting customs sufferance
warehouses](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1065), substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on April 26, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of National Revenue with respect
hereto;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
section 30, subsection 37(1), paragraphs 164(1)(i) and (j) and
section 166 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EGBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting customs sufferance
warehouses](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1065), effective the day that
section 30, subsection 37(1), paragraphs 164(1)(i) and (j) and
section 166 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EBBAA} come into
force.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Licensing of Sufferance Warehouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889116 .Part}

### [Issuance of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889117 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 24 of the Act and subject to subsection (3), the
  Minister may issue a licence to any person who makes an application
  for a licence in accordance with subsection (2) and gives the security
  required under section 4.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence must be submitted to the
  Minister in the prescribed form, together with a detailed plan of the
  proposed sufferance warehouse.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} The plan referred to in subsection (2) must
  indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the place proposed to be operated as a
    sufferance warehouse exists or is to be constructed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of construction of the place, whether or
    not it already exists; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the area, within the place, that is to be used for
    the storage of goods.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not issue a licence to an
  applicant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is of good character;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/95-177, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has sufficient financial resources to
    enable him to provide the facilities, equipment, personnel and
    services required under sections 11 to 13 and to lease or purchase
    the proposed sufferance warehouse;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the volume and nature of business in the area in
    which the applicant proposes to operate a sufferance warehouse is
    such that a sufferance warehouse is needed to serve the importers in
    that area;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the site of the proposed sufferance warehouse is
    within a reasonable distance from major transportation routes and a
    customs office;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed sufferance warehouse contains adequate
    space for the storage of imported goods;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the structure of the proposed sufferance warehouse
    will be suitable for the operation of a sufferance warehouse; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is able to provide customs services with
    respect to the proposed sufferance warehouse.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/95-177, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

### [Security]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889137 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant must, before a licence is issued to the applicant, give
  security in accordance with the requirements of the [Financial
  Security (Electronic Means) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-42)
  and in an amount determined by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may at any time require a licensee to
  increase the amount of security given under subsection (1) to an
  amount sufficient to ensure the payment of any duties payable in
  respect of goods stored in the sufferance warehouse.

### [Amendment of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889163 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may amend a licence only for the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to change a restriction specified in the licence
      regarding the classes of goods that may be received in the
      sufferance warehouse in respect of which the licence was issued,
      or to specify such a restriction, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to change the circumstances specified in the
      licence in which goods may be received in the sufferance
      warehouse, or to specify such circumstances; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to change the name of the licensee, where the name
    of the licensee is changed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may amend a licence for the purposes set
  out in subparagraph (1)(a)(i) or (ii) only where he gives to the
  licensee 90 days notice of the proposed amendment.

### [Cancellation or Suspension of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889171 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
licence where the licensee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no longer owns or leases the place that is licensed
  as a sufferance warehouse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requests the Minister in writing to cancel the
  licence; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 9, the Minister may suspend or cancel a licence where the
  licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a receivership in respect of his
    debts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, or any
    regulation made pursuant thereto, that prohibits, controls or
    regulates the importation or exportation of goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/95-177, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has, in the course of his operation of the
    sufferance warehouse, acted dishonestly in his business dealings
    with customs brokers, importers, carriers, Her Majesty or servants
    of Her Majesty; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has been incompetent in the operation of the
    sufferance warehouse.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 9, the Minister may cancel a
  licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the volume of goods being received in the
    sufferance warehouse is no longer sufficient to warrant the
    continued operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no longer a need for a sufferance
    warehouse in the area in which the sufferance warehouse is located;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is no longer able to provide customs
    services with respect to the sufferance warehouse.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-177, s. 4
- SOR/2005-211, s. 6
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, immediately after suspending a licence, give to the
  licensee a notice confirming the suspension and providing all relevant
  information concerning the grounds on which the Minister has suspended
  the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may, within 90 days after the day on
  which the licence is suspended, make representations to the Minister
  regarding why the licence should be reinstated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, before cancelling a licence under
  section 8, give the licensee 90 days notice of the proposed
  cancellation and provide the licensee with all relevant information
  concerning the grounds on which the Minister proposes to cancel the
  licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may, within 90 days after the day on
  which the notice referred to in subsection (3) is given, make
  representations to the Minister regarding why the licence should not
  be cancelled.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-177, s. 5
- SOR/96-152, s. 3
:::

### [Reinstatement of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889196 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
reinstate a suspended licence where the Minister is satisfied that the
cause for the suspension no longer exists.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Sufferance Warehouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889198 .Part}

### [Facilities, Equipment and Personnel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889199 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/96-38, s. 3

\]
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall provide, at the sufferance warehouse in respect of
  which his licence was issued

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} washroom facilities and offices for the use of
    officers, and the heat, light and cleaning services necessary for
    those facilities and offices, where so requested by the chief
    officer of customs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adequate space for the examination of imported
    goods by officers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the personnel and equipment necessary to ensure
    that the goods to be examined by an officer are made available to
    the officer for examination;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detention compound or parking area for the
    storage of imported goods that are held in a conveyance, where so
    requested by the chief officer of customs; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} facilities, equipment and personnel sufficient to
    control access to the sufferance warehouse and provide secure
    storage of the goods stored in it, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} doors and other building components of sturdy
      construction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} secure locks on doors and windows,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} signs that indicate the security requirements
      applicable to the premises, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where the sufferance warehouse will be used for
      the storage of designated goods, such additional facilities and
      equipment as may be required to ensure the secure storage of those
      goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a sufferance warehouse is restricted by the
  terms and conditions of its licence to the receipt of goods arriving
  by motor vehicles used for commercial purposes, the licensee may lease
  space to any carrier who has requested space for his exclusive use in
  the operation of a separate sufferance warehouse.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-177, s. 6
- SOR/96-38, s. 4
:::

### [Operation and Maintenance Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889216 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall ensure that the goods received in the sufferance
  warehouse are stored safely and securely in the area designated for
  that purpose in the plan referred to in subsection 3(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than the licensee, an employee of
  the licensee or an employee of a carrier engaged in the delivery of
  goods to or the removal of goods from the sufferance warehouse, shall
  enter any place in it where goods are stored, without the written
  authorization or the attendance of an officer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall have in place

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures to maintain the security of, and
    restrict access to, the sufferance warehouse; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procedures to ensure that personnel working in the
    sufferance warehouse are aware of and follow the procedures referred
    to in paragraph (a).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-38, s. 5
- SOR/2005-211, s. 2(F)
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall make a schedule of the charges payable for the services
  offered by the licensee and shall make the schedule available for
  examination by any person who requests to see it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any facilities, services, personnel and equipment
  provided by a licensee to an officer under any of paragraphs 11(1)(a)
  to (c) shall be provided free of charge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-177, s. 7

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall acknowledge the receipt of goods in the sufferance
  warehouse by electronic means.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the goods were transported to Canada by
  or on behalf of a courier and will be released under subsection 32(4)
  of the Act before the accounting required under subsection 32(1) of
  the Act and the payment of duties, the licensee shall acknowledge the

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} endorsing the bill of lading, waybill or similar

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} endorsing the customs document on which the goods
    were reported under the [Reporting of Imported Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-873); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issuing a transfer document to the carrier.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2),
  [[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2

### [Time Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889246 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4.1),
  if goods in a sufferance warehouse have not been removed from the
  sufferance warehouse within 40 days after the day on which the goods
  were reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may, at the end
  of that limitation period, be deposited in a place of safe-keeping as
  provided for in subsection 37(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where perishable goods in a sufferance warehouse have
  not been removed therefrom within four days after the day on which
  they were reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may be
  deposited in a place of safe-keeping as provided for in
  subsection 37(1) of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If goods in a sufferance warehouse are [[nuclear
  substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
  [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) or [[prescribed
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
  [General Nuclear Safety and Control
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202) and have not been removed
  from that warehouse within 14 days after the day on which the goods
  are reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may be deposited
  in a place of safe-keeping as provided for in subsection 37(1) of the
  Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39.1(1) of the Act,
  firearms, prohibited ammunition, prohibited devices, prohibited or
  restricted weapons, tobacco products and vaping products are goods of
  a prescribed class that are forfeit if they are not removed from a
  sufferance warehouse within 14 days after the day on which they were
  reported under section 12 of the Act.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39.1(1) of the Act,
  spirits are goods of a prescribed class that are forfeit if they are
  not removed from a sufferance warehouse within 21 days after the day
  on which they were reported under section 12 of the Act.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee shall provide the Agency with a list
  of all goods not removed from the sufferance warehouse within the time
  limit prescribed in subsection (1), (2), (3), (4) or (4.1), as the
  case may be, on the first business day following the end of that
  period.

### [Alteration of Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889257 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall
ensure that goods are manipulated, unpacked, packed, altered or combined
with other goods while in a sufferance warehouse only for the purpose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} stamping the goods, if the goods consist of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} imported raw leaf tobacco or imported tobacco
    products that are placed in the sufferance warehouse in accordance
    with section 39 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} imported vaping products that are placed in the
    sufferance warehouse in accordance with section 158.51 of the
    [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} marking the goods, if the goods consist of special
  containers of spirits or wine, imported by an excise warehouse
  licensee, that are placed in the sufferance warehouse in accordance
  with section 80 or 85 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} marking the goods, if the goods consist of goods in
  respect of which any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(a) of the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) apply.
