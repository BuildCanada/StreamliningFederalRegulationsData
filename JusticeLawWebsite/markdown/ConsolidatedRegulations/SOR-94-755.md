the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology and the Minister of
Communications, pursuant to subsection 67.2(1.2)[[Footnote

## [Small Cable Transmission System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-966905 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4) and section 4, [[small cable transmission
  system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cable transmission system that
  transmits a signal, with or without a fee, to not more than 2,000
  premises in the same service area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), where a cable
  transmission system is included in the same unit as one or more other
  cable transmission systems, the number of premises to which the cable
  transmission system transmits a signal is deemed to be equal to the
  total number of premises to which all cable transmission systems
  included in that unit transmit a signal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (2), a cable
  transmission system is included in the same unit as one or more other
  cable transmission systems where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are owned or directly or indirectly controlled
    by the same person or group of persons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their service areas are each less than 5 km
    distant, at some point, from at least one other among them, and
    those service areas would constitute a series of contiguous service
    areas, in a linear or non-linear configuration, were it not for that
    distance.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a cable transmission
  system that was included in a unit on December 31, 1993.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-148, s. 4
- SOR/2014-80, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The definition set out in
subsection 3(1) does not include a cable transmission system that is a
master antenna system if it is located within the service area of
another cable transmission system that transmits a signal, with or
without a fee, to more than 2,000 premises in that service area.
