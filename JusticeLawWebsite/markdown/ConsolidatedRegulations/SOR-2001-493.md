## Property Assessment and Taxation (Railway Right-of-Way) Regulations {#property-assessment-and-taxation-railway-right-of-way-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-493
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2001-11-08

Property Assessment and Taxation (Railway Right-of-Way) Regulations

P.C. 2001-2081  2001-11-08
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the taxation of property interests within reserves is an
important element of self-government;

Whereas, to facilitate the implementation of property taxation on
reserve land, the First Nations set out in Schedule 1 to the annexed
Regulations wish to provide railway companies occupying reserve land
with levels of property taxation that are predictable and comparable to
levels of property taxation under provincial laws, and have requested
that the Governor in Council make those Regulations;

And whereas the Indian Taxation Advisory Board was established by Canada
to, among other things, advise and assist the Minister of Indian Affairs
and Northern Development on policy issues related to section 83 of the
[Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), to foster harmony between taxation by First
Nations and taxation by other authorities and to ensure compliance with
the principles of equity, natural justice and fairness;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to subsection 83(5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5), hereby makes the annexed Property Assessment and
Taxation (Railway Right-of-Way) Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677154 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Indian
    Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adjacent area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjacent area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a First Nation,
    means the incorporated or unincorporated area bordering a majority
    of those reserves of the First Nation that contain right-of-way
    areas. ([zone adjacente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adjustment factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjustment factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the adjustment
    factor set out in the Railway Corporations Assessment Regulation,
    B.C. Reg. 51/2016, as amended from time to time. ([facteur de
    rajustement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the council of a First
    Nation. ([conseil de bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[by-law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a by-law made under subsection
    83(1) of the Act. ([règlement administratif]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canadian Pacific Railway Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian Pacific Railway Company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    any successor to the Canadian Pacific Railway Company. ([Compagnie
    de chemin de fer Canadien Pacifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fibre optic improvements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fibre optic improvements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fibre optic
    fibres, sheaths, wrapping, conduits, cables and other related
    improvements. ([équipement de fibres optiques]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian band set out in
    column 1 of Schedule 1. ([première nation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[incorporated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incorporated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area incorporated
    as a municipality under the Local Government Act of British
    Columbia. ([zone constituée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a right-of-way, any other
    right or interest in lands, or
    improvements. ([propriété]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial taxation laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial taxation laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the laws and
    regulations of British Columbia respecting property taxation,
    including the Assessment Act, Railway Corporations Assessment
    Regulation, Hospital District Act, School Act, Local Government Act
    and Taxation (Rural Area) Act, as amended from time to time. ([lois
    fiscales provinciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[right-of-way area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[right-of-way area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a First
    Nation set out in column 1 of Schedule 1, means the lands described
    in column 2. ([zone d'emprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[track in place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[track in place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    [[track in place of a railway corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 21(15) of the Assessment Act of British Columbia, as
    amended from time to time. ([voie ferrée existante]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
by-laws made by a band council in respect of taxation of property in a
right-of-way area.

## [Taxation By-laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First Nations Tax Commission review

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A band council shall refer
a draft of each by-law proposed to be made in respect of taxation of
property in a right-of-way area to the First Nations Tax Commission for
review and recommendations prior to making the by-law.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-278, s. 1
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of assessable value

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a by-law in respect of taxation of property in a
  right-of-way area shall provide that an assessor must determine the
  assessable value of the following types of property using the
  assessment rates, adjustments, exceptions, inclusions and exclusions
  that would be applied if the property were subject to provincial
  taxation laws:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} track in place of a railway company, including
    fibre optic improvements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rights-of-way for track referred to in paragraph
    (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bridges of a railway company;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fibre optic improvements of a non-railway company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} utility systems of a non-railway company, including
    pipeline, cable, telephone, power, sewer, gas and related
    facilities; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} other improvements lawfully located in a
    right-of-way area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Adjustment factors do not apply to the determination
  of assessable values of property under a by-law.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-278, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum tax rates for railway companies

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), the tax rate for a taxation year established under
  a by-law enacted by a First Nation set out in column 1 of Schedule 2
  in respect of property of a railway company in a right-of-way area
  shall not exceed the rate equal to the sum of the products, determined
  for each of the tax bases set out in column 2, of

  ::: {#677196 .Subsection}
  A × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the tax rate established for that taxation year under
      provincial taxation laws for that tax base; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the adjustment factor for that tax base.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum tax rates for non-railway
  companies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the tax rate for a
  taxation year established under a by-law enacted by a First Nation set
  out in column 1 of Schedule 2 in respect of improvements of a company
  other than a railway company in a right-of-way area shall not exceed
  the rate equal to the sum of the tax rates, established for that
  taxation year under provincial taxation laws, for the tax bases set
  out in column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions and inclusions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A tax rate referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  incorporate the same exemptions, exceptions, inclusions and exclusions
  that would be applicable to the property if the property were subject
  to the provincial taxation laws applicable to the adjacent area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the adjustment
  factors applicable to the determination of tax rates for the Boothroyd
  Indian Band, Nicomen Indian Band, Siska Indian Band and Skuppah Indian
  Band shall be the adjustment factors that apply to property in
  incorporated areas.
