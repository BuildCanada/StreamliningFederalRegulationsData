## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675879 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by an insurance company or a
      securities dealer. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    that is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province. ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by a bank holding company, that is
    held by a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the bank holding
      company. ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
    Act. ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Regulatory Capital]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675892 .Part}

Definition of [[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 3, the regulatory capital of a bank
  holding company, at any time, is the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#675896 .Subsection}
  A - B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of the amounts of the shareholders' equity, minority
      interests and subordinated indebtedness that would be reported in
      the financial statements of the bank holding company prepared as
      at that time in accordance with the accounting principles and
      specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
      subsection 840(4) of the Act; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of goodwill that would be included in those
      financial statements.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction relating to security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of regulatory capital under
  subsection (1), an amount may be included in respect of a security
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security is, by its terms, subordinate in right
    of payment to all liabilities of the entity that issued the security
    other than liabilities that, by their terms, rank equally with, or
    are subordinate to, that security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security is issued and fully paid up; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subordinated indebtedness or a
    preferred share, the security

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has an initial minimum term of five years or more
      or has no term, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cannot be redeemed or purchased for cancellation
      in the first five years after it is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition for investment limits

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 938 to 940 of the Act, the regulatory capital of
  a bank holding company is the amount calculated under section 2, less
  the total of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of
    shareholders' equity or subordinated indebtedness of an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the bank holding company,
    or an entity controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the bank holding company, that is included in the
    amount of regulatory capital of the bank holding company under
    section 2,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of an
    investment, other than shareholders' equity or subordinated
    indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), of the bank holding
    company or a designated entity controlled by the bank holding
    company in an insurance company or securities dealer controlled by
    the bank holding company, or in an entity controlled by an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the bank holding company,
    if the investment is part of the capital of the insurance company or
    securities dealer,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of a loan,
    other than subordinated indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a),
    made by the bank holding company or by a designated entity
    controlled by the bank holding company to an insurance company or
    securities dealer controlled by the bank holding company, or to an
    entity controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the bank holding company, if the loan is part of the
    capital of the insurance company or securities dealer, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of a
    minority interest in an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the bank holding company, or in an entity controlled
    by an insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the bank
    holding company, that is included in the amount of regulatory
    capital of the bank holding company under section 2.

- Definition of [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (1)(b) and (c),
  [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an insurance company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, its regulatory
    capital within the meaning of the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other insurance company or a
    securities dealer, an amount that would be treated as its capital by
    a regulatory body that regulates the insurance company or securities
    dealer.
