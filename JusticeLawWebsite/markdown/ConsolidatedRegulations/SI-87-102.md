## Goods Imported for Certification Remission Order {#goods-imported-for-certification-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/87-102
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-06-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX and Under any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act and Customs
Duties Imposed Under Section 21 of the Customs Tariff, Paid or Payable
on Goods Imported into Canada to be Tested or Examined for Certification
by an Accredited Organization

P.C. 1987-1044 1987-05-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
17[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of the customs duty and sales and excise
taxes paid or payable on goods imported into Canada to be tested or
examined for certification by an accredited organization.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641270 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Goods Imported for Certification Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-87-102).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641272 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[accredited
organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organization accredited by
the Standards Council of Canada to certify that goods tested or examined
by it meet the standards set by it in respect of those goods.
(*organisme accrédité*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641274 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the taxes imposed under Division III of
Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
and customs duties imposed under section 21 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), paid or payable on goods that are imported
and used solely for testing or examination for certification by an
accredited organization.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-15, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641278 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 3
is granted on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is made to the Minister
  of National Revenue within two years after the date of importation of
  the goods for which remission is claimed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported on or after July 1, 1986 by or
  on behalf of an accredited organization, for testing or examination by
  that organization;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the goods are neither sold nor given to any person
  other than an accredited organization in Canada by or on behalf of the
  importer, and on completion of the testing or examination the goods
  are either exported or destroyed by or on behalf of the importer; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant provides such other evidence as is
  necessary to demonstrate to the Minister of National Revenue that the
  applicant is entitled to the remission.
