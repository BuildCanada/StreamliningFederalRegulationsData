## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024286 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba, C.C.S.M., c. N20. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) of any kind, grade
    or class. ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of hogs in the Province of Manitoba.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Producer Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Producer Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Pork est.,
    continued pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the Manitoba Hog Producers'
    Marketing Plan Regulation made under the Act. ([organisation de
    producteurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024292 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Producer Board, in respect of any hogs marketed by the producer in
interprovincial or export trade from Manitoba, a levy of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.20 per head for each weanling hog; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.85 per head for each other hog.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024296 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The levies imposed pursuant
to section 2 shall be paid by the producer to the Producer Board within
seven days after the receipt by the producer of an invoice from the
Producer Board.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024298 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Manitoba Hog Marketing
Administration Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBA} is repealed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024300 .Part}

on September 16, 1998.
