## Order Transferring to the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons the powers, duties and functions of the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada under the Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety Board Act {#order-transferring-to-the-leader-of-the-government-in-the-house-of-commons-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-president-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-under-the-canadian-transportation-accident-investigation-and-safety-board-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-29
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-04-10

Order Transferring to the Leader of the Government in the House of
Commons the powers, duties and functions of the President of the Queen's
Privy Council for Canada under the Canadian Transportation Accident
Investigation and Safety Board Act

P.C. 2013-302 2013-03-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EEDAA},
transfers to the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons from
the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada the powers, duties
and functions of the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada
under the [Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety
Board Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-643-e_hq_14103){#fn_81000-3-643-e_hq_14103-ID0EBDAA}.
