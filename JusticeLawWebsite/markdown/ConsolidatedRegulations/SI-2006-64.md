## Order Designating the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as a Department and the Executive Director as Deputy Head {#order-designating-the-public-appointments-commission-secretariat-as-a-department-and-the-executive-director-as-deputy-head .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-64
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2006-05-03

Order Designating the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as a
Department and the Executive Director as Deputy Head

P.C. 2006-230 2006-04-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBACAA}, designates
  the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as a department for the
  purposes of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)^a^, designates the Executive
  Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as the
  deputy head of that Secretariat for the purposes of that Act.
