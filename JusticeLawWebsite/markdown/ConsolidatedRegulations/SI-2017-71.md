## Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Permit Authorization Order {#justice-for-victims-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-permit-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-71
:::

[JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT (SERGEI MAGNITSKY
LAW)](/eng/acts/J-2.3)

Registration 2017-11-15

Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Permit Authorization
Order

P.C. 2017-1348 2017-11-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsection 4(4) of the
[Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei Magnitsky
Law)](/eng/acts/J-2.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20732){#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20732-ID0EBCCA},
authorizes the Minister of Foreign Affairs to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue to any person in Canada or any Canadian outside
  Canada a permit to carry out a specified activity or transaction, or
  class of activity or transaction, that is restricted or prohibited
  under the Act or the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-233)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733){#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733-ID0EBABCA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue a general permit allowing any person in Canada
  or any Canadian outside Canada to carry out a class of activity or
  transaction that is restricted or prohibited under the Act or the
  [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-233)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733){#fn_81000-2-32XX_hq_20733-ID0EBAACA}.
