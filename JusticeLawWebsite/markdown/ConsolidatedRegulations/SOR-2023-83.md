## Associate Judges (Federal Court) Regulations {#associate-judges-federal-court-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-83
:::

[FEDERAL COURTS ACT](/eng/acts/F-7)

Registration 2023-05-01

Associate Judges (Federal Court) Regulations

P.C. 2023-372 2023-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, makes the annexed [Associate Judges (Federal
Court) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-83) under subsections
12(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGACA} and
(5)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EEACA} of the
[Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBACA}.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of associate judges

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(2) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), the number
of associate judges that may be appointed under subsection 12(1) of that
Act is fixed at nine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workload --- supernumerary associate
judges

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(5) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), the
workload of a supernumerary associate judge is fixed at a minimum of 50%
of the workload of an associate judge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
