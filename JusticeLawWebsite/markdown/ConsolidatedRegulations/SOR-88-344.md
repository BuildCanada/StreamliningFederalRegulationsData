## Office of the Registrar General Fees for Services Order {#office-of-the-registrar-general-fees-for-services-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-344
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-06-27

Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid by Persons for Services in Respect
of the Issuance of Documents by the Office of the Registrar General of
Canada
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to paragraph
13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and Order in
Council P.C. 1988-672 of April 14, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-88-344_e_hq_6241){#fn_SOR-88-344_e_hq_6241-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid by
persons for services in respect of the issuance of documents by the
Office of the Registrar General of Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-344),
effective July 1, 1988.

  :::

::: FlushRight
HARVIE ANDRE

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912346 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Office of the Registrar General Fees for Services
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-344).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912348 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fees to be paid for the
issuance of documents by the Office of the Registrar General of Canada
are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Certificate of Authentication ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} each \$ 100

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Certificate of Deposit pursuant to the Railway Act,
  the Canadian National Railway Act and the [Bell Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/B-3.6)

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first certificate ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} 100

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each related certificate ![blank
    line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} 35

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Copies of documents deposited under the Railway Act,
  the Canadian National Railway Act and the [Bell Canada
  Act](/eng/acts/B-3.6), per page ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} each 1

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Copy of any other document ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} each 10

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Certified copy of any document ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} each 35
