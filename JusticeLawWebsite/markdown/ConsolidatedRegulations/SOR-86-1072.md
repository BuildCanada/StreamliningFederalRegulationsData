## Duty Free Shop Regulations {#duty-free-shop-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1072
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-11-06

Duty Free Shop Regulations

P.C. 1986-2530 1986-11-06
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFABA}, a copy of
proposed [Regulations respecting duty free
shops](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1072), substantially in the form annexed
hereto, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
Part I on April 26, 1986 and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of National Revenue with respect thereto;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
section 30, subsection 37(1), paragraphs 164(1)(i) and (j) and
section 166 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting duty free
shops](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1072), effective on the day that
section 30, subsection 37(1), paragraphs 164(1)(i) and (j) and
section 166 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) come into force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889891 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who applies for a
    licence; ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer or malt liquor as defined in
    section 4 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([bière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[border crossing point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[border crossing point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place on the
    international border between Canada and the United States at which
    there is a customs office; ([poste frontalier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a
    duty free shop or a proposed duty free shop, means the manager of
    the customs office or customs offices that serve the area in which
    the duty free shop is located or is proposed to be located; ([agent
    en chef des douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2002-218,
    s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[excisable goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[excisable goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means spirits, wine, beer or
    tobacco products; ([marchandises assujetties à
    l'accise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[excise warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[excise warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([entrepôt
    d'accise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intoxicating liquor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intoxicating liquor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the [Importation of Intoxicating Liquors Act](/eng/acts/I-3);
    ([boisson enivrante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence to operate a place
    as a duty free shop; ([agrément]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom a licence
    has been issued; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the Immigration Act, 1976. ([résident permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Licensing of Duty Free Shops]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889907 .Part}

### [Issuance of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889908 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 24 of the Act and subject to subsection (6), a
  licence may be issued to any qualified person who makes an application
  in accordance with subsection (2) and who gives the security required
  under section 4.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must submit the application for a licence
  in the prescribed form by means of the electronic system specified by
  the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A corporation is qualified to operate a duty free
  shop at a border crossing point if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporation is incorporated in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all the shares of the corporation are beneficially
    owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian citizen or permanent resident,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} another corporation that is incorporated in
      Canada all of whose shares are beneficially owned by a Canadian
      citizen or permanent resident, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} both a Canadian citizen or permanent resident
      and that other corporation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian citizen or permanent resident referred
    to in paragraph (b)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is of good character,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} maintains their principal residence in Canada,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} resided in Canada for at least 183 days of the
      year before the year in which the corporation makes the
      application for the licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person other than a corporation is qualified to
  operate a duty free shop at a border crossing point if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a Canadian citizen or a permanent resident;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of good character;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintains his principal residence in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} resided in Canada for at least 183 days of the year
    preceding the year in which the person makes the application for the
    licence.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person is qualified to operate a duty free shop at
  an airport or at a border crossing point if the person has been
  granted a lease or other right to occupy the place proposed as the
  duty free shop.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not issue a licence to an
  applicant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant meets the qualifications referred to
    in subsection (3) or (4) and subsection (5) in the case of a duty
    free shop at a border crossing point, or subsection (5) in the case
    of a duty free shop at an airport;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-153, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-259, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-259, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an adequate range of goods is not available in, or
    adequate service is not provided by, any duty free shop already
    located in the area in which the proposed duty free shop is to be
    located;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the establishment of a duty free shop will not
    impede the flow of traffic in the vicinity in which the proposed
    duty free shop is to be located; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is able to provide customs services with
    respect to the proposed duty free shop.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-153, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

### [Security]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889939 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant must, before a licence is issued to them, give security in
  accordance with the requirements of the [Financial Security
  (Electronic Means) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-42) and in
  an amount that is determined by the Minister and that is not less than
  \$10,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may at any time require a licensee to
  increase the amount of security given under subsection (1) to an
  amount sufficient to ensure the payment of any duties payable in
  respect of the goods in the duty free shop.

### [Term of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889956 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a licence is valid for a period beginning on the
  date indicated on the licence as being the date on which the licence
  becomes effective and ending on the date indicated on the licence as
  being the date on which the licence expires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No licence shall be valid for more than 10 years.

### [Amendment of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889960 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may amend a licence only for the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to change a restriction specified in the licence
      as to the classes of goods that may be received in the duty free
      shop, or to specify such a restriction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to change the circumstances specified in the
      licence in which goods may be received in the duty free shop, or
      to specify such circumstances; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to change the name of the licensee, where the name
    of the licensee is changed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may amend a licence for the purposes set
  out in subparagraph (1)(a)(i) or (ii) only where he has given 90 days
  notice of the proposed amendment.

### [Cancellation or Suspension of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-889968 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
licence where the licensee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no longer owns or leases the place that is licensed
  as a duty free shop;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requests the Minister in writing to cancel the
  licence; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-153, s. 3
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 10, the Minister may suspend or cancel a licence where the
  licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a receivership in respect of his
    debts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, or any
    regulation made pursuant thereto, that prohibits, controls or
    regulates the importation or exportation of goods, or that relates
    to customs and excise;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-153, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has, in the course of his operation of the duty
    free shop, acted dishonestly in his business dealings with
    customers, suppliers, customs brokers, carriers, Her Majesty or
    servants of Her Majesty; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has failed to carry out, within a reasonable time
    and in a reasonable manner, any proposal made in the application for
    the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 10, the Minister may suspend or
  cancel a licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the volume of goods sold in the duty free shop is
    no longer sufficient to warrant the continued operation of the duty
    free shop;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no longer a need for a duty free shop in
    the area in which the duty free shop is located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Agency is no longer able to provide customs
    services with respect to the duty free shop; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee has altered the structure of the duty
    free shop in a manner that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} contravenes a requirement related to the storage
      or sale of goods set out in the Act or in these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} impedes the flow of traffic in the vicinity in
      which the duty free shop is located, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} detrimentally affects the immediate export of
      goods.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 10, the Minister may cancel a
  licence where the licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has made a misrepresentation in the application for
    the licence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no longer qualifies under these Regulations for a
    licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-153, s. 4
- SOR/2002-218, s. 4
- SOR/2005-182, s. 2
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall, immediately after suspending a licence, give to the
  licensee a notice confirming the suspension and providing all relevant
  information concerning the grounds on which the Minister has suspended
  the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may, within 30 days after the day on
  which the licence is suspended, make representations to the Minister
  regarding why the licence should be reinstated.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, before cancelling a licence under
  section 9, give the licensee 90 days notice of the proposed
  cancellation and provide the licensee with all relevant information
  concerning the grounds on which the Minister proposes to cancel the
  licence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee may, within 90 days after the day on
  which the notice referred to in subsection (3) is given, make
  representations to the Minister regarding why the licence should not
  be cancelled.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-153, s. 5
:::

### [Reinstatement and Renewal of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-890002 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may
reinstate a suspended licence where the Minister is satisfied that the
cause for the suspension no longer exists.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may renew a
licence on the expiration of its term on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a duty free shop at a border crossing
  point, the licensee submits an application in the prescribed form to
  the Commissioner at least 90 days before the date on which the licence
  is to expire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a duty free shop at an airport, the
  licensee has been granted a new lease, an extension of the existing
  lease or a new right to occupy the place operated as a duty free shop;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no grounds exist for the Minister to suspend or
  cancel the licence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-218, s. 5
- SOR/2005-182, s. 3

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Duty Free Shops]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890010 .Part}

### [Facilities, Services and Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-890011 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall, at the duty free shop in respect of which his licence
  was issued,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-259, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-259, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide service in English and French if the duty
    free shop is located in an area in which customs services are
    provided in English and French; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} display notices in English and French indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the goods sold in the duty free shop are for
      immediate exportation only and must be reported under the Act if
      they are returned to Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances in which goods imported into
      the United States by individuals are exempt from duties, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances in which goods imported into
      Canada by individuals are exempt from duties.

  - [(e) and (f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-153, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a duty free shop forms only part of a building,
  the licensee shall keep the duty free shop separate from the remainder
  of the building by a partition or other suitable structure.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall
ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-259, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods received in the duty free shop are held in
  an area designated by the chief officer of customs until, in the case
  of imported goods, those goods have been accounted for under the Act
  or, in the case of domestic goods, those goods have been approved by
  an officer for entry into the licensee's inventory;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the duty free shop may be locked and sealed by an
  officer where the chief officer of customs requests that the duty free
  shop be locked and sealed, for the purpose of enabling an officer to
  check the goods therein against the licensee's records of inventory;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the duty free shop may be locked and sealed by an
  officer in the event that the licensee's licence expires or is
  cancelled or suspended and may be kept locked and sealed until

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods in it other than excisable
    goods, the duties and taxes payable on the goods have been paid or
    the goods have been disposed of in accordance with the laws relating
    to customs and excise, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods in it that are excisable
    goods, the goods have been disposed of in accordance with the laws
    relating to customs and excise or, in the case of intoxicating
    liquor, in accordance with the laws relating to intoxicating liquor
    of the province in which the duty free shop is located; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the duty free shop is kept suitable for the
  safe-keeping of the goods stored in it.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  intoxicating liquor shall be sold in a duty free shop in a province
  unless the licensee has obtained written approval to sell intoxicating
  liquor from the board, commission or agency that is authorized by the
  laws of the province to sell or to authorize the sale of intoxicating
  liquor in that province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a licensee has obtained written approval for
  the transfer from the board, commission or agency described in
  subsection (1), an intoxicating liquor shall not be transferred within
  the same province

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of intoxicating liquors, from a duty
    free shop to another duty free shop;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of beer, from a duty free shop to a
    bonded warehouse; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wine or spirits,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from a duty free shop to a bonded warehouse or an
      excise warehouse, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from an excise warehouse to a duty free shop.

      accounts for inventory.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before any goods are taken into a duty free shop, the
  licensee must provide to the Minister any documents required under the
  Act or any of its regulations.

### [Transfer of Ownership of Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-890065 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  the ownership of goods in a duty free shop may be transferred only by
  sale to persons who are about to leave Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ownership of goods in a duty free shop may be
  transferred to the person who sold them to the licensee, on condition
  that the goods are dealt with in accordance with the laws relating to
  customs and excise.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ownership of goods in a duty free shop may be
  transferred to a person having a licence in respect of another duty
  free shop, on condition that the goods are dealt with in accordance
  with any law relating to customs and excise, and, in the case of
  intoxicating liquor, in accordance with section 15.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a licensee's licence is about to expire or be
  cancelled and a new licensee is to be licensed to operate a duty free
  shop on the same premises as the premises of the licensee, the
  ownership of the goods in the duty free shop may be transferred to the
  proposed new licensee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-153, s. 7
- SOR/2005-212, s. 4(F)
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall sell,
give or in any other manner convey any tobacco product to a person under
the age of eighteen years.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-519, s. 5
:::
