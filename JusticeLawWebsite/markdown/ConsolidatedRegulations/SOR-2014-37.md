## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810159 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Transportation
    Accident Investigation and Safety Board
    Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act,
    1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01). ([marchandises
    dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fracture of any bone, except simple fractures
      of fingers, toes or the nose;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lacerations that cause severe hemorrhage or
      nerve, muscle or tendon damage;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an injury to an internal organ;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} second or third degree burns, or any burns
      affecting more than 5% of the body surface;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a verified exposure to infectious substances or
      injurious radiation; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an injury that is likely to require
      hospitalization. ([blessure grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[UN number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[UN number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    1.4 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286). ([numéro
    ONU]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-810172 .Part}

### [Mandatory Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-810173 .Subheading}

#### [Aviation Occurrences]{.HTitleText3} {#h-810174 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Board

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner, operator, pilot-in-command, any crew member of the aircraft and
  any person providing air traffic services that have direct knowledge
  of an occurrence must report the following aviation occurrences to the
  Board if they result directly from the operation of an aircraft:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an accident

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person is killed or sustains a serious injury
      as a result of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} being on board the aircraft,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} coming into direct contact with any part of the
        aircraft, including parts that have become detached from the
        aircraft, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} being directly exposed to jet blast, rotor down
        wash or propeller wash,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft sustains structural failure or
      damage that adversely affects the aircraft's structural strength,
      performance or flight characteristics and would normally require
      major repair or replacement of any affected component, except for

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} engine failure or damage, when the damage is
        limited to the engine, its cowlings or accessories, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} damage limited to propellers, wing tips,
        antennae, tires, brakes, fairings or small dents or puncture
        holes in the aircraft's skin, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is missing or inaccessible; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an incident involving an aircraft
    having a maximum certificated take-off weight greater than 2 250 kg,
    or of an aircraft being operated under an air operator certificate
    issued under Part VII of the [Canadian Aviation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an engine fails or is shut down as a
      precautionary measure,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a power train transmission gearbox malfunction
      occurs,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} smoke is detected or a fire occurs on board,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} difficulties in controlling the aircraft are
      encountered owing to any aircraft system malfunction, weather
      phenomena, wake turbulence, uncontrolled vibrations or operations
      outside the flight envelope,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft fails to remain within the intended
      landing or take-off area, lands with all or part of the landing
      gear retracted or drags a wing tip, an engine pod or any other
      part of the aircraft,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a crew member whose duties are directly related
      to the safe operation of the aircraft is unable to perform their
      duties as a result of a physical incapacitation which poses a
      threat to the safety of persons, property or the environment,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} depressurization of the aircraft occurs that
      requires an emergency descent,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} a fuel shortage occurs that requires a
      diversion or requires approach and landing priority at the
      destination of the aircraft,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is refuelled with the incorrect
      type of fuel or contaminated fuel,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a collision, a risk of collision or a loss of
      separation occurs,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} a crew member declares an emergency or indicates
      an emergency that requires priority handling by air traffic
      services or the standing by of emergency response services,

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} a slung load is released unintentionally or as
      a precautionary or emergency measure from the aircraft, or

    - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} any dangerous goods are released in or from
      the aircraft.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type, model, nationality and registration marks
    of the aircraft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the owner, operator, pilot-in-command
    and, if applicable, hirer of the aircraft;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the last point of departure and the intended
    destination of the aircraft, including the date and time of the
    departure;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the occurrence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person providing air traffic
    services related to the occurrence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of crew members, passengers and other
    persons involved in the occurrence and the number of those who were
    killed or sustained serious injuries as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the location of the occurrence by reference to an
    easily defined geographical point, or by latitude and longitude;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the occurrence and the extent of
    any resulting damage to the environment and to the aircraft and any
    other property;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any dangerous goods carried on board or
    released from the aircraft, including the shipping name or UN number
    and consignor and consignee information;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the aircraft is missing or inaccessible

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the last known position of the aircraft by
      reference to an easily defined geographical point, or by latitude
      and longitude, including the date and time that the aircraft was
      at that position, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the actions taken or planned to locate or gain
      access to the aircraft;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action taken or planned to
    protect persons, property and the environment;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the person making the report
    and the phone number and address at which they can be reached; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any information specific to the occurrence that the
    Board requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person making the report must send to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible and by the quickest means
    available, all the information required under subsection (2) that is
    available at the time of the occurrence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remainder of that information as soon as it
    becomes available within 30 days after the occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board and any person mentioned in subsection (1)
  may enter into an agreement regarding a format and time frame for
  reporting aviation occurrences that are not likely to require
  immediate Board response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person making a report has sent any information
  described in subsection (2) to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board may exempt other persons from the
    requirement to send that information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a member of a company, any other
    person from that company is exempt from the requirement to send that
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[air traffic services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[air traffic services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in subsection 101.01(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433). ([services de la
      circulation aérienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an impact, other than an
      impact associated with normal operating circumstances, between
      aircraft or between an aircraft and another object or
      terrain. ([collision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[loss of separation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[loss of separation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a situation in
      which the distance separating two aircraft is less than the
      minimum established in the Canadian Domestic Air Traffic Control
      Separation Standards, published by the Department of Transport, as
      amended from time to time. ([perte d'espacement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the activities for which
      an aircraft is used from the time any person boards the aircraft
      with the intention of flight until they
      disembark. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 101.01(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433). ([utilisateur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 101.01(1) of the [Canadian Aviation
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433). ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a situation in
      which an aircraft comes so close to being involved in a collision
      that a threat to the safety of any person, property or the
      environment exists. ([risque de collision]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

#### [Marine Occurrences]{.HTitleText3} {#h-810239 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Board

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of the ship, other than a pleasure craft, whether or not they
  are the owner, the master, the ship's pilot, any crew member of the
  ship and the harbour master, that have direct knowledge of a marine
  occurrence must report the following marine occurrences to the Board:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is killed or sustains a serious injury as
    a result of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} boarding, being on board or falling overboard
      from the ship, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} coming into direct contact with any part of the
      ship or its contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person falls overboard from the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a crew member whose duties are directly related to
    the safe operation of the ship is unable to perform their duties as
    a result of a physical incapacitation which poses a threat to the
    safety of persons, property or the environment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the ship

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sinks, founders or capsizes,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is involved in a collision or a risk of a
      collision,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sustains a fire or an explosion,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} goes aground,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} makes unforeseen contact with the bottom without
      going aground,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} sustains damage that affects its seaworthiness
      or renders it unfit for its purpose,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} is anchored, grounded or beached to avoid an
      occurrence,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} is missing or abandoned,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} fouls a utility cable or pipe, or an underwater
      pipeline,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} sustains a total failure of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the navigation equipment if the failure poses a
        threat to the safety of any person, property or the environment,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the main or auxiliary machinery, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the propulsion, steering, or deck machinery if
        the failure poses a threat to the safety of any person, property
        or the environment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all or part of the ship's cargo shifts or falls
    overboard; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} there is an accidental release on board or from the
    ship which results in any of the events listed in subsection 8.4(2)
    of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name or identification number, nationality and
    type of the ship and, if applicable, a general description of the
    cargo on board the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the occurrence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the operator, owner and agents of the
    ship and, if applicable, the name of the authorized representative
    referred to in subsection 14(1) of the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the master of the ship and, if
    applicable, the name of the ship's pilot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the technical specifications of the ship such as
    its tonnage, length and type of propulsion;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the ship is equipped with a voyage data recorder
    or a simplified voyage data recorder,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type of recorder, including its make and
      model, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any action taken or planned to save the data on
      the recorder;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the last point of departure and the intended
    destination of the ship, including the date and time of the
    departure;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the number of crew members, passengers and other
    persons on board at the time of the occurrence;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of crew members, passengers and other
    persons involved in the occurrence who were killed or sustained
    serious injuries as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the local weather, sea and, if applicable, ice
    conditions at the time of the occurrence;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the location of the occurrence by reference to an
    easily defined geographical point, or by latitude and longitude;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the occurrence and the extent of
    any resulting damage to the ship, the environment and any other
    property;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a list of any dangerous goods
    released on board or from the ship, including the shipping name or
    UN number of the dangerous goods;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} if the ship is missing or inaccessible,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the last known position of the ship by reference
      to an easily defined geographical point, or by latitude and
      longitude, including the date and time that the ship was at that
      position, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the actions taken or planned to locate or gain
      access to the ship;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action taken or planned to
    protect persons, property and the environment;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the person making the report
    and the phone number and address at which they can be reached; and

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} any information specific to the occurrence that the
    Board requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person making the report must send to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible and by the quickest means
    available, all the information required under subsection (2) that is
    available at the time of the occurrence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remainder of that information as soon as it
    becomes available within 30 days after the occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board and any person mentioned in subsection (1)
  may enter into an agreement regarding a format and time frame for
  reporting marine occurrences that are not likely to require immediate
  Board response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person making a report has sent any information
  described in subsection (2) to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board may exempt other persons from the
    requirement to send that information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a member of a company, any other
    person from that company is exempt from the requirement to send that
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to radio ship reporting station

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A report made to a radio ship reporting station is
  considered to have been made to the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Towing ship

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a reference to an operator, whether
  or not they are the owner, or a master of a ship includes, in the case
  of a ship being towed by another ship, the operator, whether or not
  they are the owner, and master of the towing ship.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an impact, other than an
      impact associated with normal operating circumstances, between
      ships or between a ship and another
      object. ([collision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person who is in command
      or charge of a ship, but does not include a licensed marine pilot
      while the pilot is performing pilotage duties under the [Pilotage
      Act](/eng/acts/P-14). ([capitaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the activities for which a
      ship is used at any time other than when the ship is in dry dock
      or laid-up. ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pilot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pilot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who is not a member
      of the ship's crew and who has the conduct of the
      ship. ([pilote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship that is used
      for pleasure and not for a commercial purpose. ([embarcation de
      plaisance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[radio ship reporting station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[radio ship reporting station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      Canadian Coast Guard radio station, a Marine Communications and
      Traffic Services Centre, a Canadian marine radio station operated
      by the St. Lawrence Seaway Management Corporation or a Canadian
      harbour radio station. ([station de radiocommunications
      maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a situation in
      which a ship comes so close to being involved in a collision that
      a threat to the safety of any person, property or the environment
      exists. ([risque de collision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

#### [Pipeline Occurrences]{.HTitleText3} {#h-810312 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- pipeline occurrences

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must report any of the following pipeline occurrences to the
  Board:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline sustains damage that affects the safe
    operation of the pipeline as a result of another object coming into
    contact with it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized third party activity affects the
    structural integrity of the pipeline;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a geotechnical, hydrotechnical or environmental
    activity poses a threat to the safe operation of the pipeline.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- occurrences caused by
  operation

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The operator must report any of the following
  pipeline occurrences to the Board if they result directly from the
  operation of the pipeline:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person sustains a [[serious
    injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
    [National Energy Board Onshore Pipeline
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-294) or is killed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is a fire, ignition or explosion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} affects the safe operation of the pipeline, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} poses a threat to the safety of any person,
      property or the environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} there is an occurrence that results in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an unintended or uncontrolled release of
      hydrocarbon gas,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an unintended or uncontrolled release of HVP
      hydrocarbons,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an unintended or uncontrolled release of LVP
      hydrocarbons in excess of 1.5 m^3^, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an unintended or uncontrolled release of a
      commodity other than hydrocarbon gas, HVP hydrocarbons or LVP
      hydrocarbons;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} there is a release of a commodity from the line
    pipe body;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline is operated beyond design limits or
    any operating restrictions imposed by the National Energy Board;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the pipeline restricts the safe operation of any
    mode of transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the occurrence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the unique identifier of the pipeline or portion of
    pipeline, such as its name or number;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific pipeline components that malfunctioned
    or failed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location of the occurrence by reference to a
    specific designation point such as the operator's facility or the
    pipeline's kilometre post location;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the closest city, town or village to the occurrence
    site;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons who were killed or sustained
    serious injuries as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of any commodity contained in or released
    from the pipeline and an estimate of the volume of commodity
    released and recovered;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the actual or anticipated duration of any
    interruption of the operation of the pipeline or a portion of the
    pipeline;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the occurrence, the events leading
    up to it and the extent of any damage, including the consequences on
    the pipeline or portion of the pipeline and on any other property
    and the environment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action taken or planned to
    address the consequences of the occurrence;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action taken or planned to
    protect persons, property and the environment, including any
    evacuation as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the person making the report
    and the phone number and address at which they can be reached; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any information specific to the occurrence that the
    Board requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person making the report must send to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible and by the quickest means
    available, all the information required under subsection (2) that is
    available at the time of the occurrence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remainder of that information as soon as it
    becomes available within 30 days after the occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board and the operator of the pipeline may enter
  into an agreement regarding a format and time frame for reporting
  pipeline occurrences that are not likely to require immediate Board
  response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[CSA Z662]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[design limits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[HVP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [[LVP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

#### [Railway Occurrences]{.HTitleText3} {#h-810365 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Board

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of the rolling stock, the operator of the track and any crew
  member that have direct knowledge of a railway occurrence must report
  the following railway occurrences to the Board:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is killed or sustains a serious injury as
    a result of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} getting on or off or being on board the rolling
      stock, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} coming into direct contact with any part of the
      rolling stock or its contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rolling stock or its contents

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are involved in a collision or derailment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sustain damage that affects the safe operation
      of the rolling stock,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cause or sustain a fire or explosion, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} cause damage to the railway that poses a threat
      to the safe passage of rolling stock or to the safety of any
      person, property or the environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a risk of collision occurs between rolling stock;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an unprotected main track switch or subdivision
    track switch is left in an abnormal position;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a railway signal displays a less restrictive
    indication than that required for the intended movement of rolling
    stock;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock occupies a main track or subdivision
    track, or track work takes place, in contravention of the Rules or
    any regulations made under the [Railway Safety
    Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock passes a signal indicating stop in
    contravention of the Rules or any regulations made under the
    [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} there is an unplanned and uncontrolled movement of
    rolling stock;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a crew member whose duties are directly related to
    the safe operation of the rolling stock is unable to perform their
    duties as a result of a physical incapacitation which poses a threat
    to the safety of persons, property or the environment; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} there is an accidental release on board or from a
    rolling stock which results in any of the events listed in
    subsection 8.4(2) of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the train's number, direction, tonnage, length and
    authorized speed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of loaded cars and empty cars on each
    train and cut of cars;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the operator of the rolling stock and
    the operator of the track;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the occurrence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of crew members, passengers and other
    persons involved in the occurrence and the number of those who were
    killed or sustained serious injuries as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of rolling stock or intermodal platforms
    that are damaged or have derailed and their reporting marks;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each rolling stock that is damaged or has
    derailed, whether the rolling stock is loaded, empty or contains
    residue;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for each damaged or derailed rolling stock a list
    of all the dangerous goods on board the rolling stock, including the
    shipping name or UN number of the dangerous goods;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if dangerous goods are released,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the shipping name or UN number of each dangerous
      good,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reporting marks of each rolling stock from
      which the dangerous goods were released,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of each of the means of
      containment from which the dangerous goods were released,
      including the specification of the means of containment,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the condition of each of
      the means of containment from which the dangerous goods were
      released,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the dangerous goods on board each
      rolling stock or in each means of containment prior to the
      occurrence, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each dangerous good that is
      known or suspected to have been released;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the local weather conditions at the time of the
    occurrence and any climatic conditions such as snow, ice, wind, fog,
    dust and severe heat;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the location of the occurrence, including the mile,
    the subdivision and the track designation;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the occurrence and the extent of
    any resulting damage to the environment and to the rolling stock,
    the railway and other property;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of any action taken or planned to
    protect persons, property and the environment, including any
    evacuation as a result of the occurrence;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the person making the report
    and the phone number and address at which they can be reached; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any information specific to the occurrence that the
    Board requires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person making the report must send to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible and by the quickest means
    available, all the information required under subsection (2) that is
    available at the time of the occurrence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remainder of that information by the end of the
    calendar month following the month of the occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board and any person mentioned in subsection (1)
  may enter into an agreement regarding a format and time frame for
  reporting railway occurrences that are not likely to require immediate
  Board response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a person making a report has sent any information
  described in subsection (2) to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board may exempt other persons from the
    requirement to send that information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a member of a company, any other
    person from that company is exempt from the requirement to send that
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an impact, other than an
      impact associated with normal operating circumstances, between

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock and a person or vehicle; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock and an object or animal, if the
        rolling stock is damaged or
        derailed. ([collision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[derailment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[derailment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any instance where one or
      more wheels of rolling stock have come off the normal running
      surface of the rail. ([déraillement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[main track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[main track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
      Rules. ([voie principale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[risk of collision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a situation in
      which rolling stock comes so close to being involved in a
      collision that a threat to the safety of any person, property or
      the environment exists. ([risque de collision]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Rail Operating
      Rules, as amended from time to time and approved by the Minister
      of Transport in accordance with subsection 19(1) of the [Railway
      Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2). ([Règles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[subdivision track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[subdivision track]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in the Rules. ([voie de subdivision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Keeping and Preservation of Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-810436 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person having possession of or control over evidence relating to a
  transportation occurrence must keep and preserve the evidence unless
  the Board provides otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) is not to be construed as preventing
  any person from taking the necessary measures to ensure the safety of
  any person, property or the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person who takes the measures referred to in
  subsection (2) must, to the extent possible in the circumstances and
  before taking those measures, record the evidence by the best means
  available and advise the Board of their actions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may exempt any person from the requirement
  to keep and preserve evidence if another person has already kept and
  preserved that evidence.

  interview.

  investigator must be taken in a manner so that a complete and usable
  record of the statement is obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy

### [Public Inquiries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-810514 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public hearing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  public inquiry conducted under subsection 21(1) of the Act must
  proceed, for the purpose of ascertaining the facts and circumstances
  relating to the transportation occurrence, by way of a public hearing
  during which witnesses may be examined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presiding officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, under subsection 21(1) of the Act, the
  Chairperson designates a person to conduct a public inquiry, that
  person is to act as the presiding officer or, if more than one person
  is designated to conduct a public inquiry, the Chairperson must
  designate one of those persons to act as the presiding officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}File

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board must open and maintain a file relating to
  each public inquiry conducted and that file must contain all of the
  records relevant to the inquiry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment of technical panel

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Director of
Investigations (Air), the Director of Investigations (Marine) or the
Director of Investigations (Rail and Pipelines), as the case may be,
must establish a technical panel for the purposes of a public inquiry
and designate investigators or persons referred to in subsection 9(2) of
the Act to be members of the panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants in public inquiry

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Board may permit any
person having an interest in the matter to participate in a public
inquiry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-inquiry conference

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding officer may hold a pre-inquiry conference at a time and
  place determined by the presiding officer and is to give notice of the
  pre-inquiry conference to the participants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extent of public inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the pre-inquiry conference and after consulting
  with the participants, the presiding officer must determine the extent
  of the public inquiry and the facts and the safety matters to be
  addressed, including the witnesses to be examined, the order in which
  the witnesses may be examined, the areas in respect of which the
  witnesses may be examined and the exhibits to be produced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The presiding officer is
to determine the time and place for a public inquiry and give notice of
the public inquiry to the participants.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of presiding officer

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding officer is responsible for resolving questions of
  admissibility of evidence, disposing of procedural matters and
  adjourning the public inquiry from time to time and from place to
  place, compelling the attendance of witnesses and the production of
  documents and objects by issuing subpoenas, keeping appropriate
  records of the proceedings and taking any other appropriate action for
  the orderly conduct of the public inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The presiding officer may receive evidence that is
  relevant to the inquiry and that will advance the investigation of the
  transportation occurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena issued to a witness must be as set out in
  Form 6 of the schedule and served personally on them or, if personal
  service is impractical, by registered mail at their last known
  address, at least 10 days before the date set for their attendance, if
  they reside not more than 500 km from the required place of
  attendance, or at least 30 days before the date set for their
  attendance, if they reside more than 500 km from the required place of
  attendance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena sent by registered mail

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena sent by registered mail is deemed to have
  been served on the witness five clear days after the postmarked date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  a public inquiry is completed, the presiding officer must report to
  the Board respecting the facts and circumstances with respect to which
  evidence was gathered during the public inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must consider the report in making its
  final findings as to the causes and contributing factors of a
  transportation occurrence, in identifying any safety deficiencies and
  in making any recommendations in the interests of transportation
  safety.

### [Witness Fees and Allowances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-810547 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees and allowances

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any person attending

witness attending at a public inquiry may be paid a fee and allowance
for travel and living expenses in accordance with the Travel Directive
of the National Joint Council and the Treasury Board of Canada
Secretariat, as amended from time to time, or Tariff A of the Federal
