## Canada Post Systems Management Limited Authorization Order, 1990 {#canada-post-systems-management-limited-authorization-order-1990 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-377
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-06-21

Order Authorizing Canada Post Systems Management Limited to Acquire
Shares

P.C. 1990-1176  1990-06-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that Canada
Post Systems Management Limited, as a corporation incorporated under the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44), is empowered to
undertake the transaction described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Honourable Harvie Andre and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 91(1)(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order
authorizing Canada Post Systems Management Limited to acquire
shares](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-377).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926208 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canada Post Systems Management Limited Authorization Order,
1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-377).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926210 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Canada Post Systems
Management Limited is hereby authorized to acquire shares, at an
aggregate cost of \$120,000, in G.D. Net B.V., a corporation
incorporated under the laws of the Netherlands and having its head
office located in Amsterdam, the Netherlands.
