## Territorial Lands Regulations {#territorial-lands-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1525
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Territorial Lands Regulations
::::

:::::::::::: section

s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527191 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Territorial Lands
    Act](/eng/acts/T-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[land agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[land agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee of the
    Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development designated by
    the Minister as a land agent for an area of the Northwest
    Territories; ([agent des terres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent of
    Resources for the Northwest Territories.
    ([Surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 14
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527199 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to territorial lands under the control, management and
administration of the Minister.

## [Application to Purchase or Lease Territorial Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527201 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every application to lease
or purchase territorial lands shall be made to the land agent for the
area in which the land is situated and shall be accompanied by the
application fee set out in the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-111, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where application is made
to a land agent to lease or purchase territorial lands that are not
surveyed, the land agent shall accompany the applicant to the site of
the lands he wishes to purchase or lease, prepare a sketch of the lands,
and, if he deems it necessary, mark off on the ground the boundary
limits of the land.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person who wishes to
purchase territorial lands shall enter into an agreement for sale with
the Minister containing such terms and conditions as the Minister may
deem necessary.

## [Sale of Territorial Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527207 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  agreement for sale and every grant of territorial lands other than
  surveyed territorial lands in a townsite shall be deemed to contain
  the following reservations and conditions in addition to those
  prescribed by the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reservation of such part or parts of the land as
    may from time to time be appropriated by Her Majesty in right of
    Canada for the purpose of a public road; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the land sold has an area in excess of 10
    acres, the condition that if the owner subdivides the lands or any
    portion thereof into townsite lots, one-third of the lots in the
    land so subdivided shall revert to the Crown.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Lots to which the Crown is entitled under paragraph
  (1)(b) shall be selected as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner shall first select two lots, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the land agent shall then select one lot for the
    Crown,

  and this process shall be repeated until all lots are selected.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  territorial lands have been inspected and appraised by a valuator
  appointed by the Minister and the value of the lands is less than
  \$32,000, the lands may be sold by the Minister for not less than the
  greater of the appraised value and \$200.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Payment for territorial lands shall be made by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment in full at the time of sale; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payment of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not less than 20 per cent of the purchase price
      at the time of sale and the balance of the purchase price in equal
      consecutive annual instalments over a period not exceeding five
      years, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} interest at the rate prescribed by the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-111, s. 2
- SOR/2002-313, s. 1
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  survey of all unsurveyed territorial lands shall be in accordance with
  the instructions of the Surveyor General and the cost thereof shall be
  paid by the purchaser.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Letters patent for territorial lands shall not be
  issued until a plan of survey thereof has been approved and confirmed
  by the Surveyor General and registered in the appropriate land titles
  office.

## [Leasing of Territorial Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527228 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every lease of
territorial lands shall be for a term of not more than 30 years, but the
Minister may grant a renewal of the lease for a further term not
exceeding 30 years.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annual rental payable under any lease other than a grazing lease shall
  be not less than 10 per cent of the appraised value of the lands
  leased.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rental payable under a grazing lease shall be not
  less than the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.25 per hectare per year, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$50 per year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rental payable under any lease other than a
  grazing lease shall be not less than \$150 per year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-111, s. 3
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every lease of
territorial lands shall contain, in addition to such terms and
conditions as the Minister may deem necessary, a reservation of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all mines and of all minerals whether solid, liquid
  or gaseous which may be found to exist within, upon, or under such
  lands together with the full powers to work the same and for that
  purpose to enter upon, use and occupy the lands or so much thereof and
  to such an extent as may be necessary for the effectual working and
  extracting of the said minerals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rights of the recorded holders of mineral claims
  and any other claims or permits affecting the lands;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all timber that may be on the land;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the right to enter upon, work and remove any rock
  outcrop required for public purposes;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} such right or rights-of-way and of entry as may be
  required under regulations in force in connection with the
  construction, maintenance and use of works for the conveyance of water
  for use in mining operations; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the right to enter upon the lands for the purpose of
  installing and maintaining any public utility.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  lease is cancelled or expires and there are no arrears of rent or
  taxes, the lessee may, within three months thereof, remove any
  buildings or other structures owned by him that may be on the lands or
  the portion thereof withdrawn from the lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a lessee described in subsection (1) does not
  remove his buildings or other structures within three months of the
  expiration or cancellation of a lease, a land agent for the area in
  which the lands leased are located shall make an appraisal of the
  buildings or other structures that have been left on the lands by the
  lessee and the Superintendent may direct the sale of the same by
  public auction.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the buildings or other structures described in
  subsection (2) have been offered for sale by public auction and have
  not been sold, the Superintendent may authorize the disposal thereof
  by private sale.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} From the proceeds realized from the sale of a
  building or any other structure under this section, the land agent
  shall, after deducting any expenses of sale and any arrears of rent
  and taxes, pay to the former lessee the balance remaining from that
  sale.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lessee who wishes to assign his lease shall pay all outstanding rent
  and furnish the Department with a properly executed unconditional
  assignment, in duplicate, of the lease together with the registration
  fee set out in the schedule and a certificate from the proper official
  of the Territorial Government, municipality or other local authority
  that all taxes on the territorial lands covered by the assignment have
  been paid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No assignment of a lease that is not approved by the
  Superintendent is binding on the Crown.

## [Grazing Leases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527253 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A lease of territorial
lands for grazing purposes shall contain a covenant that the lessee
shall not during the term of the lease use or allow to be used any part
of the lands leased to him for any purpose other than grazing purposes.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who is a resident in a Territory to which these Regulations
  apply may each year, without permission of the Superintendent, cut on
  territorial lands not more than five tons of hay for each head of
  livestock owned by him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Hay cut pursuant to subsection (1) shall not be sold
  or bartered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 15
:::

Column II of the schedule are payable for the services set out in Column
I thereof.
