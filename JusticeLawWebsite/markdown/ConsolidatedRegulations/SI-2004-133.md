## Northern Pipeline Agency Cost Recovery Charge Remission Order, 2004 {#northern-pipeline-agency-cost-recovery-charge-remission-order-2004 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-133
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2004-10-20

Northern Pipeline Agency Cost Recovery Charge Remission Order, 2004

P.C. 2004-1104 2004-09-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the President
of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EFBAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed [Northern
Pipeline Agency Cost Recovery Charge Remission Order,
2004](/eng/regulations/SI-2004-133).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to Foothills Pipe Lines Ltd. of the portion of the charge under
subsection 29(1) of the [Northern Pipeline Act](/eng/acts/N-26), and any
related interest, paid or payable in respect of the invoices to be
issued on September 30, 2004 and December 31, 2004.
