## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian vessels

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations, other than Part 2, apply in respect of the following
  Canadian vessels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} passenger vessels that are Safety Convention
    vessels;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} passenger vessels that are not Safety Convention
    vessels and that are 24 m or more in length; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cargo vessels that are 24 m or more in length.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian vessels --- Part 2

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Part 2 applies in respect of Canadian vessels that
  are required to be registered or listed under Part 2 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign vessels

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Section 117 applies in
respect of foreign vessels in the internal waters of Canada that
navigate in frazil ice or ice slush conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drilling and other activities

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of vessels referred to in sections 4 and 5 that are capable of
engaging in the drilling for, or the production, conservation or
processing of, oil or gas, except vessels that are on location and
engaged in one of those activities in an area referred to in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 3 of the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
  Act](/eng/acts/O-7);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 8(1) of the [Canada--Newfoundland and
  Labrador Atlantic Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.5); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 8(1) of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore
  Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[fishing vessels,]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
  in subsection 1(1) of the [Marine Personnel
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-115); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} wooden vessels of primitive build.

## [Grandfathered Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430895 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessels constructed before coming into
force

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A vessel that is one of the
following vessels and that is constructed before the day on which this
section comes into force may comply with the requirements respecting
construction and equipment under the Act that applied to the vessel on
the day before that day instead of the requirements set out in these
Regulations and, if it does so, is considered to be a grandfathered
vessel for the purposes of sections 9 to 11:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that has never flown the flag of a foreign
  state; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that holds a certificate issued under the
  [Vessel Certificates Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-31) or the
  [Vessel Safety Certificates
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) on the day on which this
  section comes into force or that held such a certificate at any time
  during the five years before that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on grandfathering ---
immediate

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 8, a
grandfathered vessel must comply with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 103, but only in respect of the requirements
  set out in regulations 20 and 22.3 of Chapter III of SOLAS; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 301(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on grandfathering --- first
anniversary

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 8, a
grandfathered vessel must comply with the following requirements
beginning on the first anniversary of the day on which section 8 comes
into force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that has a mechanical means
  of propulsion, regulations 3-4.2, 3-8, 5 and 5-1 of Chapter II-1 of
  SOLAS instead of subsections 12(5) and (6) and sections 80 and 81 of
  the [Hull Construction Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1431);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a passenger vessel that is not a
  Safety Convention vessel, regulations 6 to 8 of Chapter II-1 of SOLAS
  instead of sections 24 and 25 of the [Hull Construction
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1431); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulations 1.5, 7.2.1, 7.2.2, 9 and 17-1 of Chapter
  III of SOLAS.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on grandfathering --- parts
of a vessel

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 8, the authorized
  representative of a grandfathered vessel must ensure that the
  following parts, machinery, systems and equipment comply with the
  requirements of these Regulations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parts of the vessel that, after the coming into
    force of section 8, undergo repairs, alterations or modifications
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} substantially alter the vessel's dimensions or
      its passenger accommodation spaces, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} substantially increase the vessel's service life
      or the life of the vessel's outfitting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the machinery, systems and equipment related to the
    parts of the vessel described in paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the parts of the vessel that are replaced after the
    day of the coming into force of section 8; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the machinery, systems and equipment of the vessel
    that are replaced after the day of the coming into force of section
    8.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of grandfathering --- entire
  vessel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A grandfathered vessel whose intended service changes
  in such a manner that the vessel can no longer comply with any one of
  the requirements respecting construction and equipment under the Act
  that were in effect on the day before the day on which section 8 comes
  into force is no longer a grandfathered vessel and must comply with
  the requirements respecting construction and equipment of these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of grandfathering ---
  certification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A grandfathered vessel that ceases to hold a
  certificate issued under the [Vessel Safety Certificates
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) for a period of more than
  five years is no longer a grandfathered vessel and must comply with
  the requirements respecting construction and equipment of these
  Regulations.

## [Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed construction date

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A vessel that is one of
the following vessels and that was constructed more than 10 years before
the day of its most recent registration under Part 2 of the Act must
comply with these Regulations as if it were constructed not more than 10
years before that day:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that has never held a certificate issued
  under the [Vessel Certificates
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-31); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that has held a certificate issued under the
  [Vessel Safety Certificates
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) but did not hold such a
  certificate at any time during the five years before the day on which
  this section comes into force.

## [Substitute Safety Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equivalent level of safety

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that there are circumstances in which equipment
  other than the safety equipment required by these Regulations provides
  a level of safety at least equivalent to that of the required safety
  equipment, that other equipment may be substituted for the required
  safety equipment in those circumstances if it bears a mark or label
  indicating that it conforms to the recommended practices and standards
  applicable to that type of equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To determine the level of safety provided by the
  other equipment in those circumstances, the Minister must assess the
  following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the environmental conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the risks to which persons on board
    are exposed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific characteristics of the equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the recommended practices and standards to which
    the equipment conforms;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the equipment will be used; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the equipment to protect a person
    from injury.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430941 .Part}

### [Construction --- Structure, Subdivision and Stability, Machinery and Electrical Installations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1430942 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}SOLAS Chapter II-1 --- mechanical
propulsion

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel that has a mechanical means of propulsion must comply with the
  requirements set out in Chapter II-1 of SOLAS, except those set out in
  the following regulations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cargo vessel that engages only on
    inland voyages, sheltered waters voyages or near coastal voyages,
    Class 2, regulations 3-6 and 6 to 7-3;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a passenger vessel that engages only
    on inland voyages, sheltered waters voyages or near coastal voyages,
    Class 2, regulation 8-1;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cargo vessel, other than a tanker,
    that is not a Safety Convention vessel and that is less than 500
    gross tonnage, regulation 9; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} regulation 19-1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}SOLAS Chapter II-1 --- no mechanical
  propulsion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that does not have a mechanical means of
  propulsion must comply with the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those set out in regulations 3-1, 3-8, 5 and 5-1 of
    Chapter II-1 of SOLAS; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that carries a person, the
    vessel must be outfitted with at least one anchor and be fitted with
    recovery devices of suitable sizes in accordance with the equipment
    number calculation of a recognized organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}2008 IS Code --- Part B

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel must comply with the recommendations set out in part B of the
  2008 IS Code and those recommendations are to be interpreted as
  expressing an obligation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}2008 IS Code --- Canadian modifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a reference to a
  recommendation in the 2008 IS Code is to be read as a reference to
  that recommendation as modified by the publication entitled Canadian
  Modifications to the International Code on Intact Stability, 2008, TP
  7301, published by the Department of Transport in December 2023.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage stability --- mechanical
propulsion

- **[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 100(1), a
  passenger vessel that has a mechanical means of propulsion and that is
  not a Safety Convention vessel may comply with the requirements of the
  publication entitled Damage Stability Standards for Non-Convention
  Passenger Vessels, TP 10943, published by the Department of Transport
  in December 2023, instead of complying with regulations 6 to 8 of
  Chapter II-1 of SOLAS.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage stability --- no mechanical
  propulsion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A passenger vessel that does not have a mechanical
  means of propulsion must comply with the requirements set out in the
  publication referred to in subsection (1).

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel must comply with the requirements set out in Chapter III of
  SOLAS, except those set out in regulations 8, 10, 19, 27, 30 and 37.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}LSA Code --- Canadian modifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a reference in
  Chapter III of SOLAS to a provision of the LSA Code is to be read as a
  reference to that provision as modified by Part A of TP 14475.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}LSA Code --- testing and approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a life-saving appliance or arrangement must comply

### [Tankers Carrying Deck Cargo]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1431020 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bulk liquid --- flash point exceeding
60°C

**[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** A tanker that does not
have a mechanical means of propulsion and that transports liquid in bulk
with a flash point of more than 60°C must not carry deck cargo unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the structural strength of the tanker is adequate for
  its intended loadings;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} precautions are taken to avoid any risk of fire or
  explosion when loading or unloading the deck cargo, and during the
  voyage;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the deck is properly dunnaged to prevent any chafing
  of metal parts; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} all cargo tank openings and vents on the vessel can
  be closed during the handling of the deck cargo.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bulk liquid --- flash point of 60°C or
less

- **[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a tanker
  that does not have a mechanical means of propulsion and that
  transports liquid in bulk with a flash point of 60°C or less must not
  carry deck cargo unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a risk assessment is submitted to the Minister by
    the authorized representative and is approved by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's design and operations comply with the
    recommendations of the risk assessment in order to reduce the
    identified hazards to the level of risk "As Low As Reasonably
    Practicable" according to a methodology referred to in subsection
    (2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} safety measures proposed to mitigate any
    intolerable risks that are identified in the risk assessment are
    submitted to the Minister by the authorized representative and are
    approved by the Minister;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's design and operations comply with the
    measures referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is a vessel to which the [Safety
    Management Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-348) apply and its
    safety management system takes into account the approved safety
    measures referred to in paragraph (c); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is maintained in class with a recognized
    organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by Minister --- risk
  assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must approve the risk assessment
  referred to in paragraph (1)(a) if it complies with the principles of
  the Formal Safety Assessment methodology set out in IMO Circular
  MSC.1/Circ.1023, Guidelines for Formal Safety Assessment (FSA) for use
  in the IMO rule-making process, as amended from time to time, or an
  equivalent methodology, and the risk assessment includes an assessment
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the structural strength of the vessel in relation
    to the intended loadings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the protection of the deck from damage or sparking,
    by dunnage or other means;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the risk of fire or explosion when loading or
    unloading the deck cargo, and during the voyage; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the locations of all openings and vents and the
    proposed methods for closing them prior to the handling of any deck
    cargo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to risk assessment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a tanker that does
  not have a mechanical means of propulsion and that carries deck cargo
  and transports oil in bulk with a flash point of more than 38°C but
  not more than 60°C does not have to submit the risk assessment if the
  vessel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complies with the requirements of section 113; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a vessel to which the [Safety Management
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-348) apply and its safety
    management system takes into account fire prevention and protection
    measures specific to such an operation.

### [Towing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1431082 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[301]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-301 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel that is constructed or converted for the purposes of towing
  must comply with the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its towing equipment must be accompanied by

    indicate the safe working load of the equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the towing winch, towing hook, towline emergency
    release and the towline must be selected with an adequate safety
    factor taking into account the appropriate design load specified in
    the recommended practices and standards for the towing operations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its towing equipment and towline emergency release
    must be maintained in accordance with the manufacturer's
    instructions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the towing equipment manuals must be kept on board;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the towing equipment must have clear operating
    instructions, in English or French, depending on the working
    language of the vessel, located near all control positions;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the towline emergency release must have clear
    operating instructions, in English or French, depending on the
    working language of the vessel, located near it;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel on which a towline is
    attached to a towing winch or to a hook, the vessel must have a
    towline emergency release

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that can operate in all conditions,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that can be activated at each local control
      position and can be remotely operated at each steering position,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is independent of the main source of
      electrical power, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the controls for which are immediately
      identifiable;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the local control and steering positions referred
    to in subparagraph (g)(ii) must be well illuminated and compatible
    in design and configuration with the towline emergency release
    mechanism;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each steering position must be connected to the
    towing winch control position by a two-way voice communication
    system;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if the towing winch can be remotely controlled from
    more than one position, the vessel must be fitted with an
    arrangement that prevents more than one position from exercising
    control at any one time;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} each towing winch control must be fitted with a
    safeguard to prevent the inadvertent operation of the control;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a towing winch must not be fitted with a ratchet
    device that could prevent the winch from paying out the towline;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if the towline is attached to a bollard or to
    bitts, the vessel must have a mechanical arrangement that is able to
    sever or release the towline immediately;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel that is fitted with towing
    pins on the counter, the pins must be power-operated; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the control position for any power-operated towing

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is less than 45 m in length, one fire

  and serviceable use of the axe in the case of an emergency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is 45 m in length or more, two fire
  axes located in parts of the vessel that enable the most convenient
  and serviceable use of the axes in the case of an emergency;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a power-operated fire pump with an apparatus allowing
  it to generate at least one powerful jet of water that can be rapidly
  directed into any part of the vessel; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tanker, a water fog applicator
  fitted with a fixed water fog nozzle or capable of being fitted with a
  water spray nozzle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessels equipped with machinery

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a receptacle containing at least 0.1 m^3^ of sand

    for distributing the material;

  engines

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a fixed fire-extinguishing system, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a foam fire extinguisher, if the power of the
    engines does not exceed 373 kW, and with an additional foam fire
    extinguisher for each additional 746 kW or fraction of that power,
    with a maximum requirement of four extinguishers;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any cargo pump-room

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a fixed fire-extinguishing system operated from a
    readily accessible position outside the pump room, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a foam fire extinguisher;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any cargo space, two foam fire extinguishers
  located where most convenient in case of emergency; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in any control station, one powder fire
  extinguisher.
