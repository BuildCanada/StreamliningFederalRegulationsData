## Quebec Family Allowances (1988) Remission Order {#quebec-family-allowances-1988-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-246
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-12-21

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Taxes Payable in Respect of
Quebec Family Allowances Received in 1988

P.C. 1988-2744 1988-12-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
17[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of income taxes payable in respect of
Quebec family allowances received in 1988](/eng/regulations/SI-88-246).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 4
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641822 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Family Allowances (1988) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-88-246).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641824 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
(*Loi*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641826 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby
granted, to every individual who in 1988 received family allowances
under the Family Allowances Act of the Province of Quebec, of an amount
equal to the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties payable by the
  individual under Parts I and I.1 of the Act for the 1988 taxation year

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties that would have
  been payable by the individual under Parts I and I.1 of the Act for
  the 1988 taxation year if no amount, in respect of family allowances
  in that year under the Family Allowances Act of the Province of
  Quebec, were included in computing the individual's income for that
  year.
