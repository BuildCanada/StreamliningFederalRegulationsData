## Water Supply and Sewage Disposal Services Charges Order (Peace River-Liard Regional District) {#water-supply-and-sewage-disposal-services-charges-order-peace-river-liard-regional-district .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-349
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1984-05-01

Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid by the Peace River-Liard Regional
District (British Columbia) for the Water Supply and Sewage Disposal
Services Provided to that Regional District by and from the Fort
St. John Airport
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and Order in Council P.C. 1983-3759
of 30 November, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBCA}, hereby makes the annexed
Order prescribing the charges to be paid by the Peace River-Liard
Regional District (British Columbia) for the water supply and sewage
disposal services provided to that Regional District by and from the
Fort St. John Airport.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBCA){#fn_1e}SI/83-217, 1983 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4387
  :::

Ottawa, May 1, 1984

::: FlushRight
LLOYD AXWORTHY
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Transport*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882685 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Water Supply and Sewage Disposal Services Charges Order (Peace
River-Liard Regional District)](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-349).

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882687 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Peace River-Liard
Regional District shall pay for the water supply and sewage disposal
services provided by and from the Fort St. John Airport to that Regional
District, in accordance with the agreement entered into by the Regional
District and Her Majesty in Right of Canada pursuant to Order in Council
P.C. 1983-3643 of 17 November, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1en){#fn_1en-ID0EBBA} the fees set out in the
schedule.

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1en-ID0EBBA){#fn_1en}Not
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II
