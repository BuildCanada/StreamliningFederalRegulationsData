## Saskatoon Airport Zoning Regulations {#saskatoon-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-706
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1987-12-10

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Saskatoon Airport

P.C. 1987-2466 1987-12-10
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning regulations
respecting Saskatoon Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 16th and 23rd, 1987
and in two successive issues of the Star-Phoenix on July 2nd and 6th,
1987, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested
persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport with
respect thereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 4.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Saskatoon Airport Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-706),
C.R.C., c. 108, and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting zoning
at Saskatoon Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-706) in substitution
therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910535 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatoon Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-706).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910537 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatoon Airport, in
      the vicinity of Saskatoon, in the Province of Saskatchewan;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport including the runway prepared for the
      take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
      strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the schedule;
      ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 495.30 m above sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-668, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910550 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-668, s. 2(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910556 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-668, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910563 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require
that the owner or lessee of the land on which that object is growing
remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-668, s. 4
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-910567 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-668, s. 5
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Saskatoon Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1754 dated June 12, 1984, is described as follows:

Premising that the bearings hereinafter mentioned are astronomical and
are referred to the centre line of strip 08R-26L of the Saskatoon
Airport, Saskatoon, Saskatchewan as South 79°01′20″ East; Commencing at
the northeast corner of the southeast quarter of Section 13,
Township 37, Range 6, West of the 3rd Meridian; thence southerly along
the easterly boundary of the said southeast quarter of Section 13 a
distance of 198.27 m more or less to an intersection with the centre
line of strip 08R-26L; thence South 79°01′20″ East along the said centre
line a distance of 940.25 m more or less to an intersection with the
centre line of strip 14-32; thence South 49°01′20″ East a distance of
822.96 m to a point designated as the Airport Reference Point.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Saskatoon Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1754
dated June 12, 1984, are surfaces abutting each end of the strips
associated with the runways designated 14-32, 08R-26L and 08L-26R and
are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 14 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 240 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 316.48 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 304.8 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 32 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 240 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 316.48 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 304.8 m measured vertically, above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 08R consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 240 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 316.48 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 304.8 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 26L consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 240 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 316.48 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 304.8 m measured vertically, above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 08L consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 529.84 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 283.46 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 101.19 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 26R consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 529.84 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 283.46 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 101.19 m measured vertically, above the
  elevation at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Saskatoon Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1754
dated June 12, 1984, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the
airport reference point, except that, where that common plane is less
than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is an
imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-each-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strips shown on Saskatoon Airport Zoning Plan No. E.1754 dated
June 12, 1984 are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 08R-26L being
  365.76 m in width, 182.88 m on each side of the centre line of the
  runway and 3 383.28 m in length;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 14-32 being 365.76 m
  in width, 182.88 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  3 139.44 m in length; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 08L-26R being
  60.96 m in width, 30.48 m on each side of the centre line of the
  runway and 1 036.32 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The transitional surfaces, shown on Saskatoon Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1754 dated June 12, 1984, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces associated with
  runways 08R-26L and 14-32 are surfaces consisting of an inclined plane
  rising at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
  horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected centre
  line of the strips extending upward and outward from the lateral
  limits of the strips and their approach surfaces to an intersection
  with the outer surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with
  runway 08L-26R is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at
  a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at
  right angles to the centre line and projected centre line of the strip
  extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
  its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or
  another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
