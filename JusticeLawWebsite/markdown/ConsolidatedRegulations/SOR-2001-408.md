## [Prescribed Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675744 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed entities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 983(5.1) of the Act, the following entities are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a bank has a substantial
  investment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an authorized foreign bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other foreign bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is affiliated with a bank or a bank
  holding company;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a bank holding company has a
  substantial investment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an entity associated with a foreign bank;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a foreign bank has a substantial
  investment;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which an entity associated with a
  foreign bank has a substantial investment;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} an entity that has a substantial investment in a bank
  or a bank holding company; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is affiliated with an entity that has
  a substantial investment in a bank or a bank holding company.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-107, s. 1
- SOR/2008-157, s. 4
- SOR/2010-71, s. 3
:::
