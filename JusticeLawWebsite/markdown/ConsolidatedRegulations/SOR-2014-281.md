## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808305 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
    Act](/eng/acts/R-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civilian member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civilian member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    appointed to a level in the Force under subsection 7(1) of the
    Act. ([membre civil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual in
    a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
    least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place where a member is
    assigned, either permanently or
    temporarily. ([poste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regular member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regular member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    appointed to a rank in the Force and includes a special
    constable. ([membre régulier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police Reserve established under subsection 11(1) of the
    Act. ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reservist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reservist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is appointed to
    the Reserve under subsection 7(2). ([réserviste]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[special constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who was
    appointed before June 30, 1988 to the rank of special
    constable. ([gendarme spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special constable member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special constable member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    is appointed, on or after June 30, 1988, to the rank of special
    constable member. ([membre spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Organization and Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808317 .Part}

### [Divisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808318 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of divisions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Force is to be divided by the Minister into divisions. In addition to
  the Commanding Officer, who may be designated by the Governor in
  Council, each division is composed of members and personnel as the
  Commissioner directs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division headquarters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The headquarters of a division is to be located where
  the Minister directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Force headquarters

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
administration, the headquarters of the Force and of the divisions are
to be organized as the Commissioner directs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Organizational components

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct that a division be divided into organizational
  components consisting of the members and personnel that are necessary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish the name of each organizational component
  and direct where its headquarters is to be located; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct that divisions or any of their organizational
  components be grouped together.

### [Command]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808331 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of Commanding Officer

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Standards and procedures
for the Commissioner's recommendation to the Minister for the
designation of a Commanding Officer are subject to approval by the
Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Command precedence

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of the
person in command or in charge of a post, the command or charge of a
post is, unless the Commissioner directs otherwise, to be determined in
accordance with the order of precedence set out in section 12.

### [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Reserve]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808336 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Royal Canadian Mounted Police Reserve is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Organization of Reserve

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Reserve is to be organized as the Commissioner
  directs and is to consist of those persons that the Commissioner
  appoints, up to a maximum number as determined by Treasury Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may appoint reservists for a period
  of not more than three years and may revoke their appointment at any
  time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person may be appointed
as a reservist only if they are of good character and meet any other
qualifications for appointment to the Reserve as determined by the
Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training or duty

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner may call up a reservist for training or duty when the
  Commissioner considers it to be necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pay and allowances

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reservist who is called up for training or duty is
  to be paid the pay and allowances determined by Treasury Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties of reservist

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A reservist who has been
designated as a peace officer when called up for duty has the duties set
out under section 18 of the Act and section 14.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reservist may resign by giving the Commissioner notice in writing of
  their resignation, and they cease to be a reservist on the date
  specified by the Commissioner in writing on accepting the resignation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may refuse to permit a reservist to
  resign and must notify them, in writing, of the reasons for refusing
  to permit the resignation.

### [Ranks and Levels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808358 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precedence of ranks and levels

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the Commissioner directs otherwise, precedence for regular members,
  other than special constables, is to be taken in the following order
  of rank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Commissioner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Deputy Commissioner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Assistant Commissioner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Chief Superintendent;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Superintendent;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Inspector;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Corps Sergeant-Major;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} Sergeant-Major;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Staff Sergeant-Major;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} Staff Sergeant;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} Sergeant;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} Corporal;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} Constable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precedence within ranks and levels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Precedence within the ranks and levels for members is
  to be determined by the date on which a member is appointed or
  promoted to a rank or level.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of precedence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must determine the order of
  precedence between the ranks of special constables, between the ranks
  of special constable members and between the levels of civilian
  members.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of rank, level or category

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a member, other than a
Deputy Commissioner, requests a reversion to a lower rank or level or a
change from the member's present category to that of regular member,
special constable member or civilian member, the Commissioner may
approve that request if there is an appropriate vacancy.

### [Duties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808381 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to the duties set out in the Act, it is the duty of members
  who are peace officers to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enforce all Acts of Parliament and regulations and
    render assistance to departments of the Government of Canada as the
    Minister directs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain law and order in Yukon, the Northwest
    Territories, Nunavut, national parks and other areas that the
    Minister designates;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintain law and order in those provinces and
    municipalities with which the Minister has entered into an
    arrangement under section 20 of the Act and carry out the other
    duties that are specified in those arrangements;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} guard and protect any property of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or under Her Majesty's control or custody that the
    Minister designates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} protect, inside or outside Canada, whether or not
    there is an imminent threat to their security,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Governor General,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Prime Minister of Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} judges of the Supreme Court of Canada,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} ministers of the Crown in right of Canada, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any other Canadian citizen or permanent resident,
      as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), who is designated by the
      Minister for the period designated by the Minister;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} protect, inside Canada, whether or not there is an
    imminent threat to their security,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any person who qualifies as an [[internationally
      protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as defined in section 2 of
      the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other foreign national who is designated by
      the Minister for the period designated by the Minister; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} ensure, in accordance with any agreement between
    the Commissioner and the Clerk of the Privy Council, the security
    for the proper functioning of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any meeting of the first ministers of the
      provinces and the Prime Minister of Canada that is convened by the
      Prime Minister of Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any meeting of Cabinet that is not held on
      Parliament Hill.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment by Force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The duties described in paragraphs (1)(e) and (f) are
  to be carried out in accordance with the Force's assessment of the
  threat or risk to the security of the person.

### [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808403 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is required to be served under the Act must be served as
  soon as feasible. It may be served personally, by mail, by courier or
  by electronic means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal service of a document on an individual is
  effected by delivering it by hand

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the individual or to a person who has attained
    the age of majority, who is not under a legal disability and who is
    residing at the individual's place of residence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is a minor or under a legal
    disability, to a person who has attained the age of majority, who is
    not under a legal disability and who is residing at the individual's
    place of residence, or to the person who is acting on the
    individual's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by mail or courier

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document on an individual by mail or a
  courier is effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by sending it by ordinary mail to the individual's
    last known address, accompanied by an acknowledgement of receipt
    card;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending it by registered mail to the
    individual's last known address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by delivering the document by courier to the
    individual's last known address; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is a minor or under a legal
    disability, by sending it by registered mail or delivering the
    document by courier to the last known address of the person who is
    acting on the individual's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by electronic means

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document on an individual by electronic
  means is effected by sending it to the individual's last known email
  address or, if the individual is a minor or under a legal disability,
  by sending it to the last known email address of the person who is
  acting on the individual's behalf.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of a document on an individual is
  demonstrated by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by ordinary mail, an
    acknowledgement of receipt card signed by the individual or by a
    person who has attained the age of majority, who is not under a
    legal disability and who is acting on the individual's behalf;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by registered mail, a post
    office receipt signed by the individual or by a person who has
    attained the age of majority, who is not under a legal disability
    and who is acting on the individual's behalf;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by courier, an
    acknowledgement of receipt card signed by the individual or by a
    person who has attained the age of majority, who is not under a
    legal disability and who is acting on the individual's behalf;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case referred to in paragraph (3)(d), a post
    office receipt or an acknowledgement of receipt card signed by the
    person who is acting on the individual's behalf;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service by electronic means, an
    electronic acknowledgement of receipt from the individual or from a
    person who is acting on the individual's behalf; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in all cases, an affidavit of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed service

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of proof of service, the document is
  deemed to have been served on the seventh day after the day on which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it was mailed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the party sending the document gave it to the
    courier to deliver; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it was electronically transmitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal to accept service

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the individual refuses to accept a document
  required to be served personally, personal service is deemed to have
  been effected at the time of the refusal, if the person attempting
  service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} records the refusal on the document; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} leaves a copy of the document with the individual
    by any reasonable means.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late service

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the individual establishes that they were acting
  in good faith but, for reasons beyond their control, did not receive
  the document on the date on which it was served, a person required
  under the Act to make a decision may determine a different date for
  service or extend the time for service of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative service

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner may permit alternate methods of
  service when a document is required to be served personally but
  personal service cannot feasibly be effected.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Grievances and Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescription for subsection 31(3) of Act

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 31(3) of the Act, each position that reports to the
Commissioner either directly or through one other person is prescribed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Committee

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Before an adjudicator, as
defined in section 36 of the [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Grievances
and Appeals)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-289), who is seized of any of
the following appeals considers the appeal, the adjudicator must,
subject to section 50 of those Standing Orders, refer it to the
Committee:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an appeal by a complainant of a written decision
  referred to in subsection 6(1) and paragraph 6(2)(b) of the
  [Commissioner's Standing Orders (Investigation and Resolution of
  Harassment Complaints)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-290);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an appeal of a written decision revoking the
  appointment of a member under section 9.2 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an appeal of a written decision discharging or
  demoting a member under paragraph 20.2(1)(e) of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an appeal of a written decision discharging or
  demoting a member under paragraph 20.2(1)(g) of the Act on the
  following grounds:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} disability, as defined in the [Canadian Human
    Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} being absent from duty without authorization or
    having left an assigned duty without authorization, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} conflict of interest;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an appeal of a written decision ordering the stoppage
  of a member's pay and allowances under paragraph 22(2)(b) of the Act.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Code of Conduct]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808453 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Code of Conduct

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** All members must conduct
themselves in accordance with the Code of Conduct set out in the
schedule.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Deputy Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808456 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If a Deputy Commissioner
has signified an intention to resign, the Commissioner may forward a
recommendation to accept the resignation to the Governor in Council and
the resignation is irrevocable once accepted by the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation for discharge

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A recommendation for the
discharge of a Deputy Commissioner under paragraph 20.2(1)(d), (f) or
(j) of the Act must be forwarded to the Governor in Council for
decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation for dismissal

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A recommendation for the
dismissal of a Deputy Commissioner made under subsection 45(4) of the
Act in respect of which there has been no appeal under subsection
45.11(1) of the Act --- or the appeal decision is to uphold the
recommendation for the dismissal --- must be forwarded to the Governor
in Council for decision.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808463 .Part}

### [Resignation by Member]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808464 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation is irrevocable

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The resignation of a
member, other than a Deputy Commissioner, is irrevocable once accepted
by the Commissioner.

### [Reinstatement of Member]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808467 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a member
  who is suspended from duty under section 12 of the Act must be
  reinstated if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct authority in respect of the member
    finds that the member

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} did not contravene the Code of Conduct set out in
      the schedule; or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} did contravene the Code of Conduct and a conduct
      measure has been imposed under subsection 42(1) of the Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conduct board established in respect of the
    member finds that the member

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} did not contravene the Code of Conduct, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} did contravene the Code of Conduct and the
      conduct measure imposed is not one set out in paragraph 45(4)(a)
      or (b) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retroactive reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reinstatement is retroactive to the date of the
  member's original suspension from duty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of reinstatement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The conduct authority must inform the member, in
  writing, of the reinstatement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of Commissioner

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner must determine whether to reinstate
  a member if the conditions of any of paragraphs (1)(a) or (b) are met
  and the member remains the subject of another proceeding under Part IV
  of the Act or is the subject of a charge for an offence under an Act
  of Parliament or of the legislature of a province.

### [Educational Courses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808483 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undertaking

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  member selected to undertake at government expense a course of studies
  offered through a university, school, college or other place of study,
  of more than six months' duration, with the exception of official
  languages training, must, before commencing the course, sign an
  undertaking agreeing to continue to serve in the Force for the
  duration of the course and for a period after that of two months of
  service for each month of the course.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member defaults on the undertaking, or otherwise
  induces discharge or dismissal from the Force, the member may be
  required to pay all, or the portion that the Commissioner directs, of
  any amount paid to enable the member to attend the course.

### [Certificate of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808489 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A Certificate of Service
in the Force must be issued by the Commissioner, in a form approved by
the Commissioner, to each former member who ceased to be a member as a

### [Benefit Trust Fund]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808608 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monies payable to Benefit Trust Fund

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  money paid to the Benefit Trust Fund under subsections 23(1) and (2)
  of the Act must be paid into the Consolidated Revenue Fund to the
  account of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Benefit Trust Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Interest is credited to the Benefit Trust Fund at a
  rate that is equal to 90% of the simple arithmetic mean of the
  accepted weekly three-month Treasury bill tender rates for the
  immediately preceding month.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management of Benefit Trust Fund

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner must nominate an advisory committee, consisting of three
  officers and one other member, to be approved by the Minister, to
  assist in the management of the Benefit Trust Fund.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advisory committee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The advisory committee must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consider all applications for grants and loans; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry out the daily management of the Fund.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Grants
  may be made out of the Benefit Trust Fund to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member who is in financial distress due to causes
    that are beyond the member's control;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member in recognition of the member's outstanding
    contribution in bringing an important investigation to a successful
    conclusion;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a member in recognition of any act of special
    endurance or bravery or any act demonstrating outstanding
    professional ethics or integrity in the performance of the member's
    duties;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a member, to the extent that the Government of
    Canada or the government of a province is unwilling or unable to
    reimburse the member for loss or damage to the member's personal
    effects or property in the performance of the member's duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a member who is discharged from the Force while
    still in need of medical treatment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a former member or dependants of a deceased former
    member who are in need of financial assistance;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Royal Canadian Mounted Police Veterans'
    Association to maintain a program to seek out and assist former
    members and their dependants under terms and conditions set out by
    the advisory committee;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner and the
    dependants, parents, brothers and sisters of a member, public
    service employee, reservist or auxiliary constable who is killed on
    duty, for expenses they incurred while travelling to a Force
    memorial service, approved by the Commissioner in honour of the
    deceased, in the manner and amount determined by Treasury Board; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner and the
    dependants, parents, brothers and sisters of a member, public
    service employee, reservist or auxiliary constable who is killed on
    duty, for expenses they incurred while travelling to a criminal
    trial, public inquiry or parole review in relation to that death.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grants for certain purposes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Grants may be made out of the Benefit Trust Fund for
  the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} covering the reasonable costs of a burial for a
    former member who dies without leaving sufficient funds for their
    burial and in respect of whom there is no other source from which
    the burial expenses can be paid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchasing wreaths in honour of deceased former
    members;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} covering costs associated with engraving memorial
    plaques and creating an honour roll page at the RCMP Academy,
    "Depot" Division to honour a member, public service employee,
    reservist or auxiliary constable who is killed on duty;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} covering the reasonable funeral reception expenses
    if the death of a member is duty-related;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} purchasing and installing an honour roll or
    memorial plaque in the division in which a member was posted at the
    time that the member was killed on duty;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} covering the reasonable costs of a memorial service
    held at the RCMP Academy, "Depot" Division to honour a member,
    public service employee, reservist or auxiliary constable who is
    killed on duty;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} paying travel expenses incurred by persons while
    travelling on Benefit Trust Fund business, including those incurred
    by persons who are requested by the advisory committee to appear
    before the committee, in the manner and amount determined by
    Treasury Board;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} covering the costs of improving and maintaining
    real property or immovables within Canada used as recreational areas
    by members, former members, spouses or common-law partners and their
    dependants;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} covering the costs of providing a member at the
    time of retirement with the identification badge of the member;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} supporting members participating in competitions of
    marksmanship; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} further assisting members, former members, spouses
    or common-law partners and their dependants, in amounts authorized
    by the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loans

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Loans may be made out of the Benefit Trust Fund to
  members referred to in paragraphs (1)(a), (d), and (e) and for the
  purpose referred to in paragraph (2)(k).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recreational areas

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A grant may be made out of the Benefit Trust Fund for
  the purpose of acquiring real property or immovables within Canada to
  be used as recreational areas by members, former members, spouses or
  common-law partners and their dependants.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monthly deductions

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a loan is made to a member out of the Benefit Trust Fund, the member

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unpaid loan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member to whom a loan is made out of the Benefit

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advisory committee, if the amount does not

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner, if the amount exceeds \$20,000

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, if the amount exceeds \$50,000.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payments made in error

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Commissioner or the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion to grant

### [Referral to Victim Services Organization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808691 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[57.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this section and in sections 57.2 to 57.4.

[[referral information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[referral information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    person, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, age, gender and language preference;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their phone number, home address or any other
      contact information necessary to facilitate contact with them; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the general nature of the crime,
      offence or other incident in question. ([renseignements
      d'aiguillage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[victim services organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[victim services organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    organization that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is certified by the director or manager of victim
      services in the province in which it operates as complying with
      that province's requirements in respect of security clearances and
      the handling, transmission, transportation, storage and
      destruction of personal information; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is recognized by the Commanding Officer of the
      division of the Force that is located in that province as an
      organization that may receive information for the purposes of
      section 57.2, having regard to the provincial certification
      referred to in paragraph (a) and any other information about the
      organization of which the Commanding Officer may have become aware
      in the course of the performance of his or her duties in that
      province. ([organisme d'aide aux victimes]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-101, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on disclosure

- **[[57.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  the course of the performance of their duties under paragraph 18(a) of
  the Act or at common law, a member who is a peace officer may disclose
  referral information in respect of a person to a victim services
  organization only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the member has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the person has suffered --- or is at risk of suffering --- physical
    or emotional harm or economic loss as a result of a crime, offence
    or other incident that has been investigated by the Force or in
    respect of which the Force's assistance was provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the support of a victim services organization is necessary to
    preserve the peace, to prevent commission of a crime or an offence,
    to prevent physical or emotional harm to the person or to protect
    the person from economic loss;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is for the sole purpose of referring
    the person to the organization; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the disclosure is limited to the amount of referral
    information in respect of the person that is necessary to refer them
    to the organization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure about third parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the context of the disclosure of a person's
  referral information, the member may disclose to the victim services
  organization information in respect of any third party who was
  involved in the crime, offence or other incident, including their
  identity and presumed location, but only to the extent necessary to
  effect the referral of the person without endangering anyone.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-101, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing referred person

**[[57.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57.3 .sectionLabel}** The Force shall make
reasonable efforts to ensure that a person whose referral information is
disclosed for the purpose of referring them to a victim services
organization is made aware of the disclosure.

- SOR/2016-101, s. 1

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-derogation

**[[57.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57.4 .sectionLabel}** For greater
certainty, nothing in section 57.2 or 57.3 is intended

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to prevent a member from disclosing any personal
  information in respect of a person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a victim services organization with the consent
    of the person, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to victim services personnel who are employed by
    the Force or who are otherwise under its supervision and direction;
    or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to derogate from the common law powers of a peace
  officer except to the extent that those sections restrict a member's
  exercise of those powers in respect of the member's disclosure of
  referral information to a victim services organization.

- SOR/2016-101, s. 1

### [Disclosure --- Intimate Partner Violence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1291032 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

definitions apply in this section and in sections 57.6 and 57.7.

[[Clare's Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Clare's Law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the legislation of a
    province regarding the provision by a police service or police force
    of disclosure information relating to the risk of intimate partner
    violence. ([loi de Clare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disclosure information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disclosure information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the information
    that is to be disclosed under the Clare's Law that is applicable in
    the province in which the disclosure is to be made. ([renseignements
    visés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Statement of Objectives]{.HTitleText1} {#statement-of-objectives .SchedHeadL1}

Maintaining the confidence of Canadians in the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police is essential.

Members of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police are responsible for the
promotion and maintenance of good conduct in the Force.

This Code of Conduct sets out responsibilities, consistent with section
37 of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10), that
reinforce the high standard of conduct expected of members of the Force.
