## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888020 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Currency Exchange for Customs Valuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-900).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888022 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National Revenue.

## [Determination of Exchange Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888024 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the rate of exchange used by the
Minister for determining the value in Canadian dollars of a currency
other than Canada shall be the rate prevailing on the date of direct
shipment to Canada of the goods whose value in Canadian currency is to
be determined.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), the rate of exchange used
by the Minister for determining the value in Canadian dollars of a
currency of a country other than Canada shall be the rate prevailing on
the date of sale to a Canadian importer or the date of direct shipment
to Canada, whichever date is applicable according to the [Special Import
Measures Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-927), of the goods whose
value in Canadian currency is to be determined.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 3 and 4, [[rate prevailing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} on a date
means, in respect of a currency of any country other than Canada, the
rate of exchange for that currency, as communicated to the ports of
entry by the Minister for that date.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, the rate of exchange for any currency to be communicated by the
Minister to the ports of entry shall be established on the basis of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the latest rate of exchange for that currency quoted
  to the Minister by the Bank of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no rate of exchange is quoted as described in
  paragraph (a), the latest rate of exchange for that currency quoted to
  the Minister by any Canadian chartered bank selected by the Minister;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if no rate of exchange is quoted as described in
  paragraph (a) or (b), the latest rate of exchange for that currency
  quoted by the Financial Times of London, Great Britain.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where more than one rate of
exchange for a currency is quoted as described in paragraph 6(a), (b) or
(c), excluding any rate of exchange for that currency commonly known as
a tourist rate, the rate of exchange for that currency shall be
established on the basis of the average of the exchange rates so quoted,
excluding any tourist rate, weighted by the percentage value in terms of
Canadian currency of all the goods in the same category as the goods in
question that, according to the records of Statistics Canada, were
imported from the country of export during the latest six month period
for which such records are available prior to the establishment of that
rate of exchange.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where no rate of exchange
for a currency is quoted as described in paragraph 6(a), (b) or (c), the
Minister shall establish a rate of exchange for that currency on the
basis of the information available to him as to the value of that
currency in Canadian currency.
