## Regulations Prescribing Public Officers {#regulations-prescribing-public-officers .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-466
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 1998-09-16

Regulations Prescribing Public Officers

P.C. 1998-1666  1998-09-16
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraph 117.07(2)(g)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Prescribing
Public Officers](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-466).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EFBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1995, c. 39, s. 139
  :::
:::

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  of any of the following classes of persons, if employed in the public
  service of Canada or by the government of a province or municipality,
  is a public officer for the purposes of paragraph 117.07(2)(g) of the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employees who are responsible for the examination,
    inventory, storage, maintenance or transportation of court exhibits
    and evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employees of police forces or other public service
    agencies who are responsible for the acquisition, examination,
    inventory, storage, maintenance, issuance or transportation of
    firearms, prohibited weapons, restricted weapons, prohibited
    devices, prohibited ammunition or explosive substances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} technicians, laboratory analysts and scientists who
    work at forensic or research laboratories;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} armourers and firearms instructors who work at
    police academies or similar institutions designated under
    subparagraph 117.07(2)(e)(ii) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or are employed by a federal or provincial
    department of natural resources, fisheries, wildlife, conservation
    or the environment, or by Revenue Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} park wardens and other employees of a federal or
    provincial department who are responsible for the enforcement of
    laws and regulations dealing with natural resources, fisheries,
    wildlife, conservation or the environment;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} immigration officers;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} security personnel employed by the House of Commons
    or the Senate or by the Service, as defined in section 79.51 of the
    [Parliament of Canada Act](/eng/acts/P-1); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} aircraft pilots employed by the Department of
    Transport or other public service agencies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the expression
  [[public service agencies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
  as in section 1 of the [Public Agents Firearms
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).
