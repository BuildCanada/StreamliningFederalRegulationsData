## Order Amalgamating and Combining the Department of International Trade and the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade under the Minister and the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs {#order-amalgamating-and-combining-the-department-of-international-trade-and-the-department-of-foreign-affairs-and-international-trade-under-the-minister-and-the-deputy-minister-of-foreign-affairs .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-11
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Amalgamating and Combining the Department of International Trade
and the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade under the
Minister and the Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs

P.C. 2006-40 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDEABA} of the
  [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDEABA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

  <!-- -->

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the Department of International Trade to the
    Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade the control
    and supervision of all of the portions of the federal public
    administration within the Department of International Trade, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the Minister for International Trade to the
    Minister of Foreign Affairs the powers, duties and functions
    received by the Minister for International Trade under paragraph (b)
    of Order in Council P.C. 2003-2049 of December 12, 2003, and

  <!-- -->

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of the [Public Service
    Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
    amalgamates and combines the Department of International Trade and
    the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade under the
    Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Deputy Minister of Foreign
    Affairs,

effective February 6, 2006.
