## [Microsd Cards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784800 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Memory cards in microSD
form factor, including microSD, microSDHC and microSDXC cards, are
excluded from the definition [[audio recording
medium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 79 of the [Copyright
Act](/eng/acts/C-42).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784802 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
