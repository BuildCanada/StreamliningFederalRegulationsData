## Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service (Cambodia) {#order-placing-members-of-the-canadian-forces-on-active-service-cambodia .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-43
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1992-03-11

Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service
(Cambodia)

P.C. 1992-355  1992-02-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 717 of
October 16, 1991, established a United Nations Advance Mission in
Cambodia to assist in the initial implementation of the Agreements on a
Comprehensive Political Settlement of the Cambodia Conflict, signed in
Paris on October 23, 1991;

And Whereas it appears advisable that Canada participate in that
Mission;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to
paragraph 31(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-43_e_hq_6498){#fn_SI-92-43_e_hq_6498-ID0EDEAA}
of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), is pleased hereby to place
on active service beyond Canada those

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-43_e_hq_6498-ID0EDEAA){#fn_SI-92-43_e_hq_6498}R.S., c.
  31 (1st Supp.), s. 60 (Sch. I, item 14)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the regular
  force of the Canadian Forces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the reserve
  force of the Canadian Forces

who are part of, or serve in immediate support of, the United Nations
Advance Mission in Cambodia.
