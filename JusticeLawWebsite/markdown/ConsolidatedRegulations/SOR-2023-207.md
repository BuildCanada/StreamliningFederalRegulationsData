## [Withdrawal of Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424392 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the General Preferential Tariff is withdrawn in respect of all goods
that originate in the following countries:

Armenia, Belize, British Virgin Islands, Fiji, Georgia, Guatemala,
Guyana, Iraq, Marshall Islands, Moldova, Nauru, Paraguay, Tonga,
Turkmenistan, Tuvalu and Vietnam.

## [Extension of Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424395 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the General Preferential Tariff is extended in respect of all goods
that originate in the following countries:

Lebanon and Tunisia.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424398 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 1 does not apply to
goods that were in transit to Canada before January 1, 2025.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424404 .Part}
