## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005612 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Province of Quebec is
hereby designated for the purposes of the definition [[applicable
guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce
Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005614 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4), the following
legislative texts are the laws that constitute the comprehensive
guidelines for the Province of Quebec:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} An Act to amend the Civil Code of Québec and the Code
  of Civil Procedure as regards the determination of child support
  payments, S.Q. 1996, c. 68;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Regulation respecting the determination of child
  support payments, made by Order 484-97 of April 9, 1997;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Title Three of Book Two of the Civil Code of Quebec,
  S.Q. 1991, c. 64; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Chapter VI.1 of Title IV of Book V of the Code of
  Civil Procedure, R.S.Q., c. C-25.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005620 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on May 1, 1997.
