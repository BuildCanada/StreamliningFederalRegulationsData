## Benzene in Gasoline Regulations {#benzene-in-gasoline-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-493
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1997-11-06

Benzene in Gasoline Regulations

P.C. 1997-1621 1997-11-06
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBCA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on May 3, 1997, a copy of
the proposed [Benzene in Gasoline
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493), substantially in the annexed
form, and an opportunity was given to persons to file a notice of
objection to the proposed Regulations with the Minister of the
Environment pursuant to subsection 48(2) of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBCA){#footnotea_e}R.S., c. 16
  (4th Supp.)
  :::

And Whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of that Act, the proposed Regulations do not regulate
any aspect of a substance that is regulated by or under any other Act of
Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
sections 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA} and 87 of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act^a^, on the recommendation of the
Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health and after the
federal-provincial advisory committee has been given an opportunity to
provide its advice under section 6 of that Act, hereby makes the annexed
[Benzene in Gasoline Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493).

  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 1992, c.

  :::

## [Application --- Types and Uses of Gasoline]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009767 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 7 and 9 to 11, these Regulations do not apply to gasoline
  for use in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} aircraft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} competition vehicles, provided that the gasoline
    has an antiknock index of at least 100; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} scientific research in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 9 to 11, these Regulations do not
  apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gasoline in transit through Canada, from a place
    outside Canada to another place outside Canada, and accompanied by
    written evidence establishing that the gasoline is in transit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline produced or sold for export and
    accompanied by written evidence establishing that the gasoline will
    be exported.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3(1) and sections 4, 16 and 17 do not
  apply to U.S. reformulated gasoline or California gasoline.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 7 and 9 to 13 and subsections
  18(4) and 19(3), these Regulations do not apply to gasoline-like
  blendstock.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any batch that is supplied during the summer but is
  intended for use during the winter in the northern supply area is
  considered to be supplied during the winter if the primary supplier
  identifies the batch under subsection 9(1) as northern winter
  complying gasoline.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to gasoline that has
  been stored in the northern supply area for the purpose of providing
  gasoline to persons in emergency situations if the gasoline was
  supplied before July 1, 1999 and stored before July 1, 2000.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Requirements Pertaining to Benzene in Gasoline]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009782 .Part}

### [Benzene --- Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1009783 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Effective July 1, 1999 and subject
  to section 15, no primary supplier shall supply gasoline that contains
  benzene at a concentration that exceeds 1.0% by volume.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell or offer for sale gasoline that
  contains benzene at a concentration that exceeds 1.5% by volume

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the northern supply area, effective July 1,
    2000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all areas identified in an application under
    paragraph 16(3)(b) in respect of which the Minister has authorized
    the use of a temporary alternative limit, effective April 1, 2000;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other area in Canada not described in
    paragraph (a) and (b), effective October 1, 1999.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-204, s. 3
:::

### [Benzene Emissions Number --- Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1009792 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Effective July 1, 1999 and
subject to section 15, no primary supplier shall supply gasoline that
has a benzene emissions number that exceeds

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 71, in the case of a batch supplied during the
  summer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 92, in the case of a batch supplied during the
  winter.

### [Reference Methods for Sampling and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1009796 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 6(1), all samples must be taken in accordance with any
  one of the ASTM International sampling methods specifically set out in
  section 7 of the National Standard of Canada standard
  CAN/CGSB-3.5-2016, Automotive gasoline.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of benzene and that of aromatics in
  gasoline referred to in sections 3 and 16 and Schedule 1 must be
  measured in accordance with the National Standard of Canada method
  CAN/CGSB-3.0 No. 14.3-2016, Methods of testing petroleum and
  associated products Standard test method for the identification of
  components in automotive gasoline using gas chromatography.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 6(2), the concentration of
  olefins in gasoline referred to in Schedule 3 must be measured in
  accordance with the National Standard of Canada method CAN/CGSB-3.0
  No. 14.3-2016, Methods of testing petroleum and associated products
  Standard test method for the identification of components in
  automotive gasoline using gas chromatography.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of sulphur in gasoline referred to
  in Schedule 1 must be measured in accordance with the ASTM
  International method D5453 - 16e1, Standard Test Method for
  Determination of Total Sulfur in Light Hydrocarbons, Spark Ignition
  Engine Fuel, Diesel Engine Fuel, and Engine Oil by Ultraviolet
  Fluorescence.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The vapour pressure of gasoline at 37.8°C (100°F)
  referred to in Schedule 1 must be measured in accordance with the ASTM
  International method D5191 - 15, Standard Test Method for Vapor
  Pressure of Petroleum Products (Mini Method) and converted to "dry
  vapor pressure equivalent" as described in that method.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The evaporative fractions of gasoline at 93.3°C
  (200°F) and 148.9°C (300°F) referred to in Schedule 1 must be measured
  in accordance with ASTM International method D86 - 17, Standard Test
  Method for Distillation of Petroleum Products and Liquid Fuels at
  Atmospheric Pressure.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of oxygen in gasoline referred to
  in Schedule 1 must be measured in accordance with the National
  Standard of Canada method CAN/CGSB-3.0 No. 14.3-2016, Methods of
  testing petroleum and associated products Standard test method for the
  identification of components in automotive gasoline using gas
  chromatography.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of benzene and that of aromatics in
  oxygenates referred to in the definition [[commercially pure
  oxygenate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 1(1) must be measured
  in accordance with the National Standard of Canada method CAN/CGSB-3.0
  No. 14.3-2016, Methods of testing petroleum and associated products
  Standard test method for the identification of components in
  automotive gasoline using gas chromatography.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of benzene and that of aromatics in
  butane referred to in the definition [[commercially pure
  butane]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 1(1) must be measured in
  accordance with ASTM International method D2163 - 14e1, Standard Test
  Method for Determination of Hydrocarbons in Liquefied Petroleum (LP)
  Gases and Propane/Propene Mixtures by Gas Chromatography.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of sulphur in oxygenates referred
  to in the definition [[commercially pure
  oxygenate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 1(1) must be measured
  in accordance with the ASTM International method D5453 - 16e1,
  Standard Test Method for Determination of Total Sulfur in Light
  Hydrocarbons, Spark Ignition Engine Fuel, Diesel Engine Fuel, and
  Engine Oil by Ultraviolet Fluorescence.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of sulphur in butane referred to
  in the definition [[commercially pure butane]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  in subsection 1(1) must be measured in accordance with the ASTM
  International method D6667 - 14, Standard Test Method for
  Determination of Total Volatile Sulfur in Gaseous Hydrocarbons and
  Liquefied Petroleum Gases by Ultraviolet Fluorescence.

### [Equivalent Methods for Sampling and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1009811 .Subheading}

### [Procedures for Sampling and Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1010038 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Effective July 1, 1999 and subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), every primary supplier must take a sample
  from each batch supplied by the primary supplier using a method
  specified in subsection 5(1) or 6(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier receives gasoline for one or
  more cargo tankers or railway cars from a single batch at a storage
  facility and the batch is not blended, mixed with other gasoline or
  otherwise modified during the period in which the cargo tankers or
  railway cars receive gasoline from the storage facility, the primary
  supplier may take a sample from the batch instead of taking samples
  from each of the cargo tankers or railway cars if the primary supplier
  describes, in the compliance plan required by section 21, how it will
  demonstrate that the sample represents the gasoline in the cargo
  tankers or railway cars.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a primary supplier imports, or dispatches from its
  refinery, to a blending facility gasoline-like blendstock for the
  purpose of manufacturing complying gasoline by blending with a
  commercially pure oxygenate or commercially pure butane, the primary
  supplier may take a sample of the gasoline-like blendstock instead of
  taking a sample from the resulting gasoline if the primary supplier

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is using a combined yearly pool average for the
    facilities under subsection 18(4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} analyses each sample in accordance with subsection
    (4);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} analyses or calculates the effect of blending the
    gasoline-like blendstock with the commercially pure oxygenate or
    commercially pure butane on the model parameters of the sample of
    gasoline-like blendstock analysed under subsection (4);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} computes the model parameters of the resulting
    gasoline according to the effect calculated under paragraph (c) on
    the model parameters of the gasoline-like blendstock analysed under
    paragraph (b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} maintains a record of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the values of the model parameters of the
      gasoline-like blendstock,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the volumes of the commercially pure oxygenate
      and commercially pure butane added to the gasoline-like
      blendstock,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the volumes of the gasoline-like blendstock and
      the resulting gasoline,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the calculations performed under paragraph (c),
      and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the model parameters computed under paragraph
      (d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} reports the volume of the resulting gasoline and
    the adjusted model parameters and adjusted benzene emissions number
    in the report required by section 8.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Effective July 1, 1999, for each batch supplied by a
  primary supplier, the primary supplier must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each sample taken in accordance with this
    section,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} analyse the sample for each of the model
      parameters, using one or more laboratories that have a quality
      control program, and calculate the benzene emissions number, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (5), combine it with other
      samples, thoroughly mix the composite sample, analyse the
      composite sample for each of the model parameters, using one or
      more laboratories that have a quality control program, and
      calculate the composite value of the benzene emissions number; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain records of analysis, as described in
    subsection 8(3), for each of the model parameters and calculate the
    benzene emissions number.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Samples may only be combined under subparagraph
  (4)(a)(ii) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the samples are the same grade of gasoline;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the samples are either summer or winter gasoline
    but not both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the samples are mixed in the proportion of the
    volumes of the batches;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the primary supplier chooses whichever of the
    following compositions that, when combined, requires the most
    frequent analysis, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the composition that represents all the batches
      supplied by the primary supplier for a continuous period of up to
      30 days, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the composition that represents batches supplied
      by the primary supplier that have a combined volume of up to 1 000
      m^3^; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the samples are collected, handled and stored in a
    manner that minimizes changes in gasoline composition of the sample.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Effective January 1, 2002, a primary supplier may use
  a statistical quality assurance program to replace the requirements of
  paragraph (4)(a) for a model parameter if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a report is sent to the Minister at least 60 days
    before the use of the statistical quality assurance program,
    containing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the statistical quality
      assurance program,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} laboratory and volumetric data on the model
      parameter of the gasoline supplied by the primary supplier for the
      previous two years, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an analysis of the results of the statistical
      quality assurance program compared to the results obtained under
      paragraph (4)(a) for the two years referred to in subparagraph
      (ii); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the analysis required by subparagraph (a)(iii)
    demonstrates that the statistical quality assurance program, for the
    range of values of the model parameter for individual batches and
    the value of the yearly pool average,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provides equivalent results compared to the
      results obtained under paragraph (4)(a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} projects a higher concentration of benzene and a
      higher value for the benzene emissions number compared to that
      obtained under paragraph (4)(a).

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No primary supplier shall continue to use the
  statistical quality assurance program referred to in subsection (6) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mix of crude oil used to manufacture the
    gasoline is changed in a way that would result in the requirements
    of paragraph (6)(b) not being met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any equipment is installed or modified at the
    refinery or blending facility that would result in the requirements
    of paragraph (6)(b) not being met; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the primary supplier is convicted of an offence
    under the Act in respect of these Regulations.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Effective July 1, 1999, every primary supplier must
  retain in Canada

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any sample collected for the purposes of these
    Regulations until whichever of the following dates the primary
    supplier chooses, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the 30th day after the date on which the sample
      was analysed, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the primary supplier has
      sampled and supplied 20 batches after the sample was analysed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 1.7 L of the sample so that analysis for
    each model parameter can be performed by the Minister.

### [Calculation of Benzene Emissions Number during the Summer]{.HTitleText1} {#calculation-of-benzene-emissions-number-during-the-summer .SchedHeadL1}

- **4** The benzene emissions number of a batch supplied during the
  summer must be calculated using the following formula:

  ::: {#1010159 .Section}
  BENSUM = \[6.73272 × exp(b1)\] + \[5.0784 × exp(b2)\] + b3

  where

  [b1 = ]{.dfn}
  :   (0.0006197 × SUL) - (0.003376 × E200) + (0.02655 × ARO) + (0.22239
      × BZ);

  [b2 = ]{.dfn}
  :   (-0.096047 × OXY) + (0.000337 × SUL) + (0.011251 × E300) +
      (0.011882 × ARO) + (0.222318 × BZ); and

  [b3 = ]{.dfn}
  :   10 × BZ × {\[(0.004775 × RVP^2^ - 0.05872 × RVP + 0.21306) ×
      (-0.029 × MTBE - 0.080274 × RVP + 1.3758)\] + \[(0.006078 ×
      RVP^2^ - 0.07474 × RVP + 0.27117) × (-0.0342 × MTBE - 0.080274 ×
      RVP + 1.4448)\] + \[(0.016169 × RVP^2^ - 0.17206 × RVP + 0.56724)
      × (-0.0342 × MTBE - 0.080274 × RVP + 1.4448)\] + \[(0.004767 ×
      RVP + 0.011859) × (-0.0296 × MTBE - 0.081507 × RVP + 1.3972)\]}
  :::

### [Calculation of Benzene Emissions Number during the Winter]{.HTitleText1} {#calculation-of-benzene-emissions-number-during-the-winter .SchedHeadL1}

- **5** The benzene emissions number of a batch supplied during the
  winter must be calculated using the following formula:

  ::: {#1010166 .Section}
  BENWIN = \[11.3998 × exp(b1)\] + \[7.68148 × exp(b2)\]

  where

  [b1 and b2]{.dfn}
  :   have the same meaning as in section 4.
  :::

### [Calculation of the Yearly Pool Average of the Benzene Emissions Number]{.HTitleText1} {#calculation-of-the-yearly-pool-average-of-the-benzene-emissions-number .SchedHeadL1}

- **6** The yearly pool average of the benzene emissions number for
  gasoline supplied during a year must be calculated using the following
  formula:

  [BENANN =]{.lawlabel} {The sum of (BENSUM × VOLBAT) for each batch
  supplied during the summer plus the sum of (BENWIN × VOLBAT) for each
  batch supplied during the winter} divided by VOLANN

  where

  BENSUM has the same meaning as in section 4; and

  BENWIN has the same meaning as in section 5.

### [Notes for Schedule 3]{.HTitleText1} {#notes-for-schedule-3 .SchedHeadL1}

- [A]{.lawlabel} *This Report on Composition of Gasoline must be
  submitted separately for each refinery, blending facility and province
  of importation, or any combination of them described under section 18
  of the [[Benzene in Gasoline
  Regulations]{.Regular}](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493).*

- [B]{.lawlabel} *For Note A, the name and location for cargo tankers,
  railway cars, boats, marine vessels or other mobile blending
  facilities are replaced by the type of mobile facilities, their number
  and the province of operation, or the name and location of the
  non-mobile facility with which they are grouped.*.

- [C]{.lawlabel} *The average benzene emissions number is the
  volume-weighted average of the benzene emissions numbers for each
  batch; it is not calculated from the average model parameters.*.

- [D]{.lawlabel} *Under subsection 13(2) of the [[Benzene in Gasoline
  Regulations]{.Regular}](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493), for each batch
  of gasoline-like blendstock dispatched or imported by the primary
  supplier during the period covered by this Report, the primary
  supplier must report to the Minister, in an annex to this Report, the
  name and address of the purchaser or receiver, the date of dispatch or
  importation and the volume.*.

- [E]{.lawlabel} *Under subsection 2(2) of Schedule 1 to the [[Benzene
  in Gasoline Regulations]{.Regular}](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-493), the
  primary supplier must report to the Minister, in an annex to this
  Report, each occurrence of a model parameter that is outside the
  acceptable range, the reason for each occurrence and the volume of
  gasoline affected.*
