**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules shall be
liberally construed to secure the just, least expensive and most
expeditious determination of every appeal on its merits.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930374 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These rules apply to
appeals brought under subsection 103(1) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-8, s. 4

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

## [Filing a Notice of Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930378 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2008-304, s. 2

\]
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal by an appellant from a decision on an appeal to the Minister
  shall be instituted within the time period set out in
  subsection 103(1) of the Act, which is 90 days after the decision is
  communicated to the appellant, or within such longer time as the Court
  may allow on application made to it within 90 days after the
  expiration of those 90 days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a decision referred to in subsection (1) is
  communicated by mail, the date of communication is the date it is
  mailed and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, the date of
  mailing is the date specified on the decision.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An appeal referred to in subsection (1) shall be made
  in writing and set out, in general terms, the reasons for the appeal
  and the relevant facts, but no special form of pleadings is required.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An appeal referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  instituted by filing a notice of appeal, which may be in the form set
  out in Schedule 5, using one of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} depositing the notice in the Registry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by mail to the Registry; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it by fax or by electronic filing to the
    Registry.

- [(5) to (8)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2008-304, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Filing of other Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930392 .Part}

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, a
document other than a notice of appeal may be filed using one of the
following methods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} depositing it with the Registry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by mail to the Registry; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it by fax or by electronic filing to the
  Registry.

## [Filing Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930399 .Part}

**[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, the
date of filing of a document is deemed to be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document filed with the Registry or
  sent by mail or by fax, the date shown by the date received stamp
  placed on the document by the Registry at the time it is received; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document filed by electronic filing,
  the date shown on the acknowledgment of receipt issued by the Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-304, s. 4

## [Electronic Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930405 .Part}

- **[[5.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in
  these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, when a
  document is filed by electronic filing, the copy of the document that
  is printed by the Registry and placed in the Court file is deemed to
  be the original version of the document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who files a document by electronic filing
  shall, if required by these rules or at the request of a party or the
  Court, provide a paper copy of the document and file it with the
  Registry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Registry has no record of the receipt of a
  document, it is deemed not to have been filed, unless the Court
  directs otherwise.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-304, s. 4

## [Extension of Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930412 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an order extending the time within which an appeal may
  be instituted may be in the form set out in Schedule 6.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application under subsection (1) shall be made by
  filing with the Registrar, in the manner provided in section 5.1,
  three copies of the application accompanied by three copies of the
  notice of appeal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No application shall be granted under this section to
  an applicant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is made within 90 days after the
    expiration of 90 days after the day on which the Minister
    communicated his or her decision to the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant demonstrates that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within the initial 90-day period specified in
      paragraph (a), the applicant

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} was unable to act or to instruct another to act
        in the applicant's name, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} had a good faith intention to appeal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} given the reasons set out in the application and
      the circumstances of the case, it would be just and equitable to
      grant the application,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the application was made as soon as
      circumstances permitted it to be made, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} there are reasonable grounds for appealing the
      decision.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} After having given the Minister an opportunity to
  make representations, the Court shall dispose of the application on
  the basis of the representations contained in it and any additional
  information, if any, that the Court may require.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar, on being informed of the decision of
  the Court in respect of the application, shall inform the applicant
  and the Minister of the decision.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The application pursuant to subsection (1) is deemed
  to have been filed on the date of its receipt by the Registry, even if
  the application is not accompanied by the notice of appeal referred to
  in subsection (2), provided that the notice of appeal is filed within
  30 days after that date or within any reasonable time that the Court
  establishes.

## [Intervention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930449 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  affected by a decision of the Minister may intervene as a party in an
  appeal by filing with the Registry a notice of intervention that may
  be in the form set out in Schedule 9.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of intervention shall be filed within
  45 days after the day on which the notice of appeal was served on the
  intervener under section 8.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An intervener may state in the notice of intervention
  that the intervener intends to rely on the reasons set out in the
  notice of appeal received by the intervener or the reasons set out in
  the notice of intervention of another intervener.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve the Minister and the
  appellant with a copy of any notice of intervention received by the
  Registrar.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The notice of intervention may be served personally,
  and personal service on the Commissioner of Revenue is deemed to be
  personal service on the Minister, or by mail addressed to the
  Minister, and if served by mail, the date of service is the date it is
  mailed and, in the absence of evidence to the contrary, the date of
  mailing is that date appearing on the communication from the Registrar
  accompanying the notice of intervention.

## [Consolidation of Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930458 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there are two or more appeals commenced in accordance with these
  rules, the Court may, upon application by the Minister or an appellant
  or by an intervener, direct that the appeals be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consolidated on such terms as it may direct,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} heard at the same time,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} heard consecutively, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} stayed until the determination of any other appeal,

  if there is a common question of law or fact in both or all of the
  appeals, or it is desirable in the interests of justice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  filed in or mailed to the Registry in which the notices of appeal were
  filed or to which they were mailed and shall be served on the other
  parties to the appeals.

## [Consolidation of Interventions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930467 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where there are two or
more notices of intervention served under these rules, the Court may
direct that one notice of intervention be filed and served on behalf of
all interveners if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is a common question of law or fact in both or
  all notices of intervention, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is desirable in the interests of justice.

## [Reply]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930471 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall reply in writing to every notice of appeal or notice of
  intervention filed in or mailed to a Registry under subsection 5(4) or
  9(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} (a) The Minister shall file the reply at the Registry
  and serve it on the appellant or intervener, or both, as the case may
  be, within 60 days from the day on which the notice of appeal or
  notice of intervention was served on the Minister, or within such
  longer time as the Court, on application made to it within those
  60 days, may allow.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} The reply shall be filed in the Registry in which
    the notice of appeal or notice of intervention was filed or to which
    it was mailed, and it may be served on the appellant or intervener
    personally or by mail.

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} A reply may be filed under paragraph (b) by
    mailing it to the appropriate Registry described in that paragraph.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} If the reply is served by mail the date of service
    is the date it is mailed and, in the absence of evidence to the
    contrary, the date of mailing is that date appearing on the
    communication from the Minister accompanying the reply.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The reply referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} admit or deny the facts alleged in the notice of
    appeal or notice of intervention, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain a statement of any further allegations of
    fact on which the Minister intends to rely.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application for an extension of time under
  paragraph (2)(a) shall be made by serving at the Registry an
  application setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the notice of appeal or notice of
    intervention was served on the Minister,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the additional time required, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons therefor.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The application to extend time may be served by
  filing it in the Registry in which the notice of appeal or notice of
  intervention was filed or to which it was mailed, or by sending a
  letter, telegram, telex or fax to that Registry.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the application to extend time is served by
  telegram, telex or fax, the date of service is the date that the
  telegram, telex or fax is transmitted and, if the application is
  served by mail, the date of service is the date stamped on the
  envelope at the post office and, if there is more than one such date,
  the date of service shall be deemed to be the earliest date.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Court shall dispose of the application on the
  basis of the representations contained in it and such additional
  information, if any, as the Court may require and after having given
  the appellant or intervener an opportunity to make representations.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar, on being informed of the decision of

## [Discovery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930527 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the time limited for replying under section 12 has expired the Court
  may, on application by any party to an appeal, direct

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any other party to the appeal to make discovery on

    possession, control or power relevant to any matter in question
    between or among them in the appeal,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant is authorized to examine on
    oath, for the purposes of discovery, any other party to the appeal,
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that there shall be both discovery of documents and
    examination for discovery.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court may specify the form of affidavit to be
  used for the purpose of discovery of documents.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person to be examined for discovery shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the other party is an individual, that
    individual,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (d), if the other party is a
    corporation or any body or group of persons empowered by law to sue
    or to be sued, either in its own name or in the name of any officer
    thereof or any other person, any member or officer of such
    corporation, body or group,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the other party is the Minister, any
    departmental or other officer of the Crown nominated by the Deputy
    Attorney General of Canada,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the other party is the Commission, any officer
    thereof nominated by the Deputy Attorney General of Canada, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who has been agreed upon by the examining
    party and the party to be examined with the consent of such person.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Court may designate the person before whom the
  examination for discovery is to be conducted and direct the manner in
  which it shall be conducted.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} All evidence given at an examination for discovery
  shall be recorded by a court reporter.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any party may, at the hearing of an appeal, use in

  party, the Court may direct that any other part of the examination,
  that in the opinion of the Court, is so connected with the part to be
  used that the last-mentioned part ought not to be used without such
  other part, be put in evidence by the party seeking to use such
  examinations.

## [Hearing of Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930545 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Court may, on
application by the Minister or an appellant or by an intervener or of
its own motion, fix the date, time and place for the hearing of an
appeal.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** When the Court has fixed

before that date, send by registered mail to all parties, or have served

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Court may, on
application by the Minister or an appellant or by an intervener or of
its own motion, adjourn an appeal on such terms as in its opinion the
circumstances of the case require.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Court, upon the
application of a party to an appeal or of its own motion and after
giving every party an opportunity to be heard, may, at any stage of the
appeal, give directions for the further conduct of the appeal.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** All parties to an appeal
may appear in person or may be represented by counsel or an agent.

## [Subpoena]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930551 .Part}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who requires the attendance of a person as a witness at a
  hearing may serve the person with a subpoena requiring the person to
  attend the hearing at the time and place stated in the subpoena and
  the subpoena may also require the person to produce at the hearing the
  documents or other things in the person's possession, control or power
  relating to the matters in question in the appeal that are specified
  in the subpoena.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the request of a party or of counsel, the
  Registrar, or some other person authorized by the Chief Justice, shall
  sign, seal and issue a blank subpoena and the party or counsel may
  complete the subpoena and insert the names of any number of witnesses.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena shall be served on a witness personally
  and, at the same time, witness fees and expenses in accordance with
  subsection (4) shall be paid or tendered to the witness.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A witness, other than a witness who appears to give
  evidence as an expert, is entitled to be paid by the party who
  arranged for his or her attendance \$75 per day, plus reasonable and
  proper transportation and living expenses.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An amount is not payable under subsection (4) in
  respect of an appellant, a respondent or a person who has intervened
  under section 9 unless the appellant, respondent or person has been
  called upon to testify by another party to the appeal.

## [Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930560 .Part}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party to an appeal may, prior to the hearing thereof or at any time
  during the hearing, apply to the Court for a direction permitting all
  facts or any particular fact or facts to be proved by other than oral
  evidence and the Court may give such direction as in its opinion the
  circumstances of the case require.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All evidence given at the hearing of an appeal shall
  be recorded in a manner approved by the Registrar.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person who swears an affidavit to be used in an
  appeal may be required to appear before a person appointed by the
  Court for that purpose to be cross-examined thereon.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a party intends to call an expert witness at
  the hearing of an appeal, that party shall, as soon as practicable and
  not later than 20 days before the date of the hearing of the appeal,
  serve at the Registry and on every other party a copy of the report
  signed by that expert containing the expert's name, address and
  qualifications and a statement of the substance of that expert's
  proposed testimony.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a report by an expert witness shall be
  served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by filing it in the Registry in which the notice of
    appeal was filed or to which it was mailed or by sending the report
    by mail or fax to that Registry, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on every other party to an appeal by personal
    service or by sending the report to that party by mail or fax

  and, if a copy of the report is served by fax, the date of service is
  the date of the transmission of the fax, or if it is served by mail,
  the date of service is the date stamped on the envelope at the post
  office and, if there is more than one such date, the date of service
  shall be deemed to be the earliest date.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A party who has failed to comply with subsection (4)
  is not permitted to call an expert witness without leave of the Court.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (4) and (6) do not apply to evidence in
  rebuttal.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Court may, with the consent of all parties,
  receive in evidence at the hearing of the appeal a report served under
  subsection (4) without requiring the expert to attend and give oral
  evidence.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where it is impracticable or inconvenient for an
  expert witness to attend at the hearing of an appeal, the party
  intending to call the witness may, with leave of the Court or the
  consent of the parties, examine the witness under oath prior to the
  hearing of the appeal before a court reporter appointed by the
  Registrar for the purpose of having the evidence of that expert
  available for use at the hearing of the appeal.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} An expert witness who is examined under
  subsection (9) may be examined, cross-examined by a party adverse in
  interest, or re-examined in the same manner as a witness at the
  hearing of an appeal and, if any dispute arises during the course of
  the examination, any party to the appeal may make application to the
  Court to resolve the dispute.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Where the evidence of an expert witness has been
  taken under subsections (9) and (10), that witness shall not be called
  to give evidence at the hearing of the appeal, except with leave of
  the Court or unless the Court requires the attendance of that witness
  at the hearing of the appeal.

- SOR/2008-304, s. 8

## [Contempt of Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930613 .Part}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is guilty of contempt of court who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a hearing of the Court fails to maintain a
    respectful attitude, remain silent or refrain from showing approval
    or disapproval of the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wilfully disobeys a process or order of the Court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acts in such a way as to interfere with the orderly
    administration of justice or to impair the authority or dignity of
    the Court;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is an officer of the Court and fails to perform his
    or her duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is a sheriff or bailiff and does not execute a writ
    forthwith or does not make a return thereof; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} contrary to these rules and without lawful excuse,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to obey a subpoena or to
      attend at the time and place appointed for his or her examination
      for discovery,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refuses to be sworn or to affirm or to answer
      any question put to him or her,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to produce or permit to be
      inspected any document or other property, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to answer interrogatories or
      to make discovery of documents.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), before a person may be
  found in contempt of court, the person alleged to be in contempt shall
  be served with an order, made on the motion of a person who has an
  interest in the proceeding or at the Court's own initiative, requiring
  the person alleged to be in contempt

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to appear before a judge at a time and place
    stipulated in the order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be prepared to hear proof of the act with which
    the person is charged, which shall be described in the order with
    sufficient particularity to enable the person to know the nature of
    the case against the person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to be prepared to present any defence that the
    person may have.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A motion for an order under subsection (2) may be
  made [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order may be made under subsection (2) if the
  Court is satisfied that there is a [prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  case that contempt has been committed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (2) shall be personally
  served, together with any supporting documents, unless otherwise
  ordered by the Court.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} In a case of urgency, a person may be found in
  contempt of court for an act committed in the presence of a judge in
  the exercise of his or her functions and condemned at once, provided
  that the person has first been called on to justify his or her
  behaviour.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A finding of contempt shall be based on proof beyond
  a reasonable doubt.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person alleged to be in contempt may not be
  compelled to testify.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where the Court considers it necessary, it may
  request the assistance of the Attorney General of Canada or any other
  person in relation to any proceedings for contempt.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is found to be in contempt, a judge
  may order, in addition to any other order made in respect of the
  proceedings, any or all of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the person be imprisoned for a period of less
    than two years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the person pay a fine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the person do or refrain from doing any act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the person's property be sequestered; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the person pay costs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-104, s. 6
