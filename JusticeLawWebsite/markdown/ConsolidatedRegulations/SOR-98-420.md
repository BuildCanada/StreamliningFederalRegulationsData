## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023385 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following goods in the
    List of Tariff Provisions that are both cut or knit to shape and are
    sewn or otherwise assembled in Mexico or the United States from
    fabric or yarn produced or obtained outside the free trade area:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods referred to in Chapters 61 and 62; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods referred to in heading 96.19 of textiles
      other than of wadding. ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cotton or man-made fibre fabric and cotton or
      man-made fibre made-up textile goods referred to in Chapters 52 to
      55 of the List of Tariff Provisions, other than goods containing
      36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair, and in Chapters
      58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions, that are woven or
      knitted in Mexico or the United States from yarn produced or
      obtained outside the free trade area, that are woven or knitted in
      Mexico or the United States from yarn produced in the free trade
      area from fibre produced or obtained outside the free trade area,
      or that are knitted in Mexico or the United States from yarn spun
      in the free trade area from fibre produced or obtained outside the
      free trade area; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods of subheading 9404.90 that are finished and
      cut and sewn or otherwise assembled from fabrics referred to in
      any of subheadings 5208.11 to 5208.29, 5209.11 to 5209.29, 5210.11
      to 5210.29, 5211.11 to 5211.20, 5212.11, 5212.12, 5212.21,
      5212.22, 5407.41, 5407.51, 5407.71, 5407.81, 5407.91, 5408.21,
      5408.31, 5512.11, 5512.21, 5512.91, 5513.11 to 5513.19, 5514.11 to
      5514.19, 5516.11, 5516.21, 5516.31, 5516.41 and 5516.91 that are
      produced or obtained outside the free trade area. ([tissus et
      articles confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada, Mexico and the United States. ([zone de
    libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cotton or man-made fibre
    yarns referred to in any of headings 52.05 to 52.07 and 55.09 to
    55.11 that are spun in Mexico or the United States from fibre
    referred to in any of headings 52.01 to 52.03 and 55.01 to 55.07
    that is produced or obtained outside the free trade area and, for
    imports from the United States, also includes goods of heading 56.05
    that are formed in the United States from fibres that are obtained
    outside the free trade area. ([filés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023393 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is granted of the customs duty paid or payable at the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff rates of customs duty on apparel, fabric and
made-up goods and spun yarn imported from the United States or Mexico.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023397 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  remission granted under section 2 applies only to an amount not
  exceeding the annual quantitative limits for each of the goods
  referred to in that section that are set out in Appendices 1 to 3 of
  Annex 6--A to Chapter 6 of the Canada--United States--Mexico
  Agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission under section 2 is granted on the condition
  that the importer or owner of the goods provides to a customs officer,
  at the request of the officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a certificate issued under the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19) and the [Import Certificate
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._603), identifying the
    quantity available for purposes of remission or refund under Annex
    6--A to Chapter 6 of the Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a written declaration, in English or French, indicating that
    the certification referred to in subparagraph (c)(i) is in the
    possession of the importer or owner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are released, or at any
    other time after the importer or owner provides the declaration
    referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certification of the exporter, in English,
      French or Spanish, in the form set out in the schedule,
      accompanied by a copy of the applicable invoice or sales
      agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the certification is in Spanish, an English
      or French translation of the certification.
