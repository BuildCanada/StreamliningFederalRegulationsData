## Dominion Succession Duty (1957) Regulations {#dominion-succession-duty-1957-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/57-216
:::

[DOMINION SUCCESSION DUTY ACT](/eng/acts/Z-0.98)

Registration 1957-06-13

Regulations Respecting Section 12 of the Dominion Succession Duty Act

P.C. 1957-814 1957-06-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 12 of the [Dominion
Succession Duty Act](/eng/acts/Z-0.98), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations Respecting section 12 of the Dominion Succession
Duty Act.
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Dominion Succession Duty (1957)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-57-216).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Province of Ontario and
the Province of Quebec are hereby prescribed for the purposes of
section 12 of the [Dominion Succession Duty Act](/eng/acts/Z-0.98).
