## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744899 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744902 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 983(4)(b) and
  subsection 983(5.3) of the Act apply to an entity that is affiliated
  with a bank, and subsections 983(8) and (9.1) of the Act apply to an
  entity that is affiliated with a bank holding company, if the entity
  complies with the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entity does not, in Canada, engage in the
    business of accepting deposit liabilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the entity does not, in Canada, represent to the
    public that any instrument issued by it is a deposit or that any
    liability incurred by it is a deposit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the entity carries on as part of its business
    the provision of financial services --- and borrows money in Canada
    from the public through the issue of instruments in denominations of
    less than \$150,000 or by borrowing from a person in an amount of
    less than \$150,000, the entity discloses that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not a member institution of the Canada
      Deposit Insurance Corporation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the liability incurred by it through the
      borrowing is not a deposit, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it is not regulated as a financial institution
      in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of disclosure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure referred to in paragraph (1)(c) must
  be in a prospectus, information circular or other offering document
  related to the borrowing or in a similar document related to the
  borrowing or, if there is no such document, in a statement delivered
  to the lender.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The conditions set out in subsection (1) do not apply
  to an entity that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a trust or loan corporation incorporated under a
    federal or provincial law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an association to which the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative credit society incorporated or
    formed, and regulated, by or under a provincial law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The condition set out in paragraph (1)(c) does not
  apply to an entity that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company or fraternal benefit society
    incorporated or formed by or under a federal or provincial law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance holding company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} controlled by an insurance holding company or in
    which an insurance holding company has a substantial investment; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} incorporated or formed by or under a federal or
    provincial law and that is primarily engaged in dealing in
    securities, including portfolio management and investment
    counselling.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent use by bank or bank holding
  company

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The conditions set out in subsection (1) do not apply
  in respect of the use by an entity of a name that, before being used
  by the bank or bank holding company, as the case may be, was already
  used by the entity, or by an entity affiliated with it, in the
  corporate name of the entity or affiliate or in a name under which the
  entity or affiliate carries on business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent use by bank or bank holding
  company

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The conditions set out in subsection (1) do not apply
  in respect of the use by an entity of an identifying mark that, before
  being used by the bank or bank holding company, as the case may be,
  was already used by the entity, or by an entity affiliated with it, in
  carrying on the business of the entity or affiliate.
