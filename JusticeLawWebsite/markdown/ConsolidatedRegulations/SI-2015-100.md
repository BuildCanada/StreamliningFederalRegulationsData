## Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services as the Minister for the purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-public-works-and-government-services-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-100
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating the Minister of Public Works and Government Services
as the Minister for the purposes of the Act

P.C. 2015-1243 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Post
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222){#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222-ID0EBDAA},
effective November 4, 2015,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222-ID0EBDAA){#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222}R.S., c.
  C-10
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2006-67 of February 6,
  2006[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217){#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-2-1107-E_hq_16217}SI/2006-28
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Public Works and
  Government Services, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada,
  as the Minister for the purposes of the [Canada Post Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222){#fn_81000-3-1110-E_hq_16222-ID0EBAAAA}.
