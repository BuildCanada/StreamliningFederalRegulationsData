## Prince Edward Island Potato Order {#prince-edward-island-potato-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 233
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Potatoes Produced in Prince Edward Island
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537129 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Prince Edward Island Potato
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._233).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537131 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island Natural
    Products Marketing Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Potato Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    potatoes established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[potatoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any potatoes produced in the
    Province of Prince Edward Island. ([pommes de
    terre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-819, s. 1
- SOR/88-276, s. 1
- SOR/90-596, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537139 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of potatoes in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Prince Edward Island, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of potatoes locally within that Province under the Act and the
Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537141 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it under section
  3 with respect to the marketing of potatoes in interprovincial and
  export trade,

to make orders fixing levies or charges and imposing them upon and
collecting them from persons described in section 3 who are engaged in
the marketing of potatoes, and for such purposes classifying such
persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the
creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting from
the sale or disposal of potatoes, and the equalization or adjustment
among producers of potatoes of moneys realized from the sale thereof
during such period or periods of time as it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-90
