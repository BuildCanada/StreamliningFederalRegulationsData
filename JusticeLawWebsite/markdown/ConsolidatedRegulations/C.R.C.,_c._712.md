## Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-agriculture-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 712
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Pursuant to Section 13 of the Financial Administration Act
Authorizing the Minister of Agriculture to Prescribe Certain Fees
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560969 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Minister of Agriculture Authority to Prescribe Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._712).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560971 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Agriculture
is authorized to prescribe by order the fees to be charged for services
provided by Her Majesty with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the inspection of milk production of dairy cattle and
  dairy goats in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the performance testing of beef cattle, sheep and
  swine in Canada.
