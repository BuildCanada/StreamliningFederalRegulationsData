## Order Transferring from the Department of Employment and Immigration to the Department of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of those Portions of the Public Service in the Income Security Programs Branch relating to Child Tax Benefit Program and Special Allowances under the Children's Special Allowances Act {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-employment-and-immigration-to-the-department-of-national-revenue-the-control-and-supervision-of-those-portions-of-the-public-service-in-the-income-security-programs-branch-relating-to-child-tax-benefit-program-and-special-allowances-under-the-childrens-special-allowances-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-35
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-03-22

Order Transferring from the Department of Employment and Immigration to
the Department of National Revenue the Control and Supervision of those
Portions of the Public Service in the Income Security Programs Branch
relating to Child Tax Benefit Program and Special Allowances under the
Children's Special Allowances Act

P.C. 1995-342  1995-02-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement
and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), the control and supervision
of the Income Security Programs Branch were transferred from the
Department of National Health and Welfare to the Department of
Employment and Immigration by Order in Council P.C. 1993-1488 of
June 25, 1993[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-35_e_hq_6605){#fn_SI-95-35_e_hq_6605-ID0EBBBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-35_e_hq_6605-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SI-95-35_e_hq_6605}SI/93-142,
  1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3234
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased hereby to transfer, effective
August 28, 1995, to the Department of National Revenue the control and
supervision of those portions of the public service in the Income
Security Programs Branch of the Department of Employment and Immigration
relating to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Child Tax Benefit Program, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} special allowances under the [Children's Special
  Allowances Act](/eng/acts/C-28.5).
