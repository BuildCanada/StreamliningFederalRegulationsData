## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029001 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Status of the Artist
    Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creation of a production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creation of a production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the creation
    of a production in the performing arts, music, dance and variety
    entertainment, film, radio and television, video, sound-recording,
    dubbing or the recording of commercials. ([création d'une
    production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Professional Categories]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029005 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in relation to the creation of a production, the
  following professional categories comprising professions in which the
  practitioner contributes directly to the creative aspects of the
  production by carrying out one or more of the activities set out in
  paragraph (a), (b), (c), (d) or (e), respectively, are prescribed as
  professional categories for the purposes of subparagraph 6(2)(b)(iii)
  of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} category 1 --- camera work, lighting and sound
    design;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} category 2 --- costumes, coiffure and make-up
    design;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} category 3 --- set design;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} category 4 --- arranging and orchestrating; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} category 5 --- research for audiovisual
    productions, editing and continuity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The professional categories prescribed by
  subsection (1) do not include any profession in which the practitioner
  of the profession

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carries out, in connection with an activity
    referred to in subsection (1), the activities of accounting,
    auditing, legal, representation, publicity or management work or
    clerical, administrative or other support work; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a person referred to in subparagraph 6(2)(b)(i)
    of the Act or carries out an activity referred to in subparagraph
    6(2)(b)(ii) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1029016 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 22, 1999.
