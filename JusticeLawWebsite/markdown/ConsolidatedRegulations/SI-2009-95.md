## Certain Fees Relating to Export Certificates Remission Order {#certain-fees-relating-to-export-certificates-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2009-95
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2009-09-30

Certain Fees Relating to Export Certificates Remission Order

P.C. 2009-1598 2009-09-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of certain fees relating to export certificates is
unreasonable and unjust, on the recommendation of the Minister of
Agriculture and Agri-Food and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612612-E_hq_7746){#fn_612612-E_hq_7746-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612612-E_hq_7747){#fn_612612-E_hq_7747-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Certain Fees Relating to Export Certificates
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2009-95).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614872 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
portion of the fee that exceeds \$75.00 paid or payable under
paragraph 21(4)(a) or (6)(a), subparagraph 21(6)(b)(i), subitem 22(3) or
(5), 23(2) or (5), 24(4) or (5), 25(3), (4) or (7) or 26(3) or item 29
of the table to Part 11 of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency Fees
Notice for an export certificate issued during the period beginning on
October 1, 2009 and ending on September 30, 2011.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614874 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that the inspection on the basis of which the export
certificate is issued is carried out by an accredited veterinarian, as
defined in section 2 of the [Health of Animals
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._296).
