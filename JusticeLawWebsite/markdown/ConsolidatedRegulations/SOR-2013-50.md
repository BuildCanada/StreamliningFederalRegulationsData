## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803204 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and the Republic of Panama,
signed on May 14, 2010, have the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.01 to 3.04;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Article 3.05, paragraph 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 3.06 to 3.15; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Annex 3.02.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803210 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind6795_hq_14176){#fn_Ind6795_hq_14176-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 38 of the [Canada--Panama Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.95), chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012,
comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
