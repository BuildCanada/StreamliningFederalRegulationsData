## [Expression Defined]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022336 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
68.1 of the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42), [[wireless transmission
system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system operated by a terrestrial
radio station that transmits in analog or digital mode a signal
containing performers' performances of musical works or sound recordings
embodying the performers' performances, in analog or digital form,
whether in the A.M. or F.M. frequency band or in any other range
assigned by the Minister under section 5 of the [Radiocommunication
Act](/eng/acts/R-2), without artificial guide for free reception by the
public.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022338 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on May 28, 1998.
