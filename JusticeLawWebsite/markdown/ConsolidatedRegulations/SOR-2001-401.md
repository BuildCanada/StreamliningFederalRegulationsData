## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675430 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity referred to in any of
      paragraphs 930(1)(a) to (j) of the Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes one or more of
      the activities referred to in paragraph 930(2)(a) of the Act and
      that engages, as part of its business, in any financial
      intermediary activity that exposes the entity to material market
      or credit risk, including a factoring entity, a finance entity or
      a financial leasing entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes an activity
      referred to in paragraph 930(2)(b) of the Act, including a
      specialized financing entity, other than an entity in which a bank
      holding company is permitted to acquire or increase a substantial
      investment under subparagraph 930(4)(d)(iii) of the Act. ([entité
      désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Regulatory Capital (Bank Holding Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-420). ([capital
    réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a share, membership share,
      ownership interest or loan held by a bank holding company at a
      particular time, the book value of the share, membership share,
      ownership interest or loan that would be reported on the balance
      sheet of the bank holding company prepared as at that time in
      accordance with the accounting principles and specifications of
      the Superintendent referred to in subsection 840(4) of the Act;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee, the face value of the
      guarantee. ([valeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 7
- SOR/2012-269, s. 5

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675444 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted substantial investments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraphs 930(4)(a)(ii),
  (b)(ii), (c)(ii) and (d)(ii) of the Act, a bank holding company may
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in a designated entity;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of paragraph 930(11)(a) of the Act,
  if a bank holding company controls a designated entity, the bank
  holding company may give up control of the designated entity while
  keeping a substantial investment in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning investments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, a bank holding company must not acquire or increase a substantial
investment in a designated entity under paragraph 2(a) or give up
control of a designated entity while keeping a substantial investment in
it under paragraph 2(b) if, after the acquisition, increase or giving up
of control, the total value of the following would exceed 50% of the
bank holding company's regulatory capital:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all shares, membership shares and ownership interests
  beneficially owned by the bank holding company, and all shares,
  membership shares and ownership interests beneficially owned by
  entities controlled by the bank holding company, in designated
  entities in which the bank holding company has a substantial
  investment but over which it does not exercise control,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all loans held by the bank holding company, and all
  loans held by entities controlled by the bank holding company, that
  were made to designated entities in which the bank holding company has
  a substantial investment but over which it does not exercise control,
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all outstanding guarantees given by the bank holding
  company, and all outstanding guarantees given by entities controlled
  by the bank holding company, on behalf of designated entities in which
  the bank holding company has a substantial investment but over which
  it does not exercise control.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-269, s. 6
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning loans

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, a bank holding company that has a substantial investment in a
designated entity over which it does not exercise control must not make
a loan to, or give a guarantee on behalf of, the designated entity, or
permit entities controlled by it to do so, if, after the making of the
loan or the giving of the guarantee, the total value of the shares,
membership shares, ownership interests, loans and guarantees referred to
in paragraphs 3(a) to (c) would exceed 50% of the bank holding company's
regulatory capital.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-269, s. 7(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In paragraphs 3(a) to (c)
and section 4, any reference to a substantial investment that a bank
holding company has does not include a substantial investment acquired
by the bank holding company under regulations made under paragraph
936(a) of the Act other than these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of "total value" in section 3
or 4

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the total value referred to in section 3 or 4 in respect of
a bank holding company, no amount may be included in respect of shares,
membership shares or ownership interests acquired under sections 933 to
935 of the Act or acquired by a bank controlled by the bank holding
company under subsection 193(12) or (13) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), chapter B-1 of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
1985.
