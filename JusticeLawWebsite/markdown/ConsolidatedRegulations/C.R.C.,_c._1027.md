## [Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476507 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Rye, grain corn, No. 1 feed
screenings and sample feed grain are hereby designated as feed grain for
the purposes of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-521, s. 3
- SOR/90-52, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All that area of Ontario
lying west of the meridian passing through the eastern boundary of the
former city of Port Arthur as it existed on December 31, 1969 is hereby
designated as a part of Eastern Canada for the purposes of the Act.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** All classes of animals,
including birds and fish, that are raised for food, for agricultural
purposes or for the purpose of fur production are hereby designated as
livestock for the purposes of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-678, s. 1
- SOR/89-521, s. 4
:::

## [Payments in Respect of Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476515 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  feed grain set out in the heading to column III of Schedule I, II or
  III is transported during the period beginning on January 1, 1993 and
  ending on March 31, 1993 to a destination set out in column II of an
  item of that Schedule in an area set out in column I of that item, the
  payment related to the cost of transportation of that feed grain shall
  be made by the Minister at the rate set out in column III of that
  item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a feed grain set out in the heading to column
  III of Schedule I, II or III is transported after March 31, 1993 to a
  destination set out in column II of an item of that Schedule in an
  area set out in column I of that item, the payment related to the cost
  of transportation of that feed grain shall be made by the Minister at
  ninety per cent of the rate set out in column III of that item.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), where a feed grain
  set out in the heading to column III of Schedule IV is transported
  after January 31, 1994 to a destination set out in column II of an
  item of that Schedule in an area set out in column I of that item, the
  payment related to the cost of transportation of that feed grain shall
  be made by the Minister at the rate set out in column III of that
  item.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), where a feed grain
  set out in the heading to column III of Schedule V is transported
  after December 31, 1994 to a destination set out in column II of an
  item of that Schedule in an area set out in column I of that item, the
  payment related to the cost of transportation of that feed grain shall
  be made by the Minister at the rate set out in column III of that
  item.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), where a feed grain
  set out in the heading to column III of Schedule VI is transported
  after March 31, 1995 to a destination set out in column II of an item
  of that Schedule in an area set out in column I of that item, the
  payment related to the cost of transportation of that feed grain shall
  be made by the Minister at the rate set out in column III of that
  item.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (2) to (5), no payment
  related to the cost of transportation of a feed grain shall be made
  under these Regulations in respect of a feed grain transported after
  January 9, 1996.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No payment referred to in subsections (2) to (6)
  shall be made unless a request for the payment is received by the
  Minister before May 31, 1996.

## [Payments in Respect of Storage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476536 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  payment related to the cost of feed grain storage in Eastern Canada
  shall be made by the Minister exclusively with respect to feed grain
  grown in Canada and stored in a vessel in Eastern Canada, except at
  the City of Thunder Bay.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rate of payment related to the cost of feed grain
  storage referred to in subsection (1) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$2.95 per tonne for wheat;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$5.20 per tonne for oats; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$3.69 per tonne for barley.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No payment related to the cost of feed grain storage
  in Eastern Canada shall be made by the Minister if the feed grain
  could be stored in another available storage facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-846, s. 4
- SOR/84-678, s. 3
- SOR/89-521, ss. 6, 9(F)
- SOR/90-52, s. 3(F)
- SOR/92-727, s. 5
:::

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476546 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every payment related to
the cost of feed grain transportation or storage shall be made on the
condition that the person who receives the payment

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keeps books of account, records, bills of lading and
  invoices respecting the purchase and sale of feed grain;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} retains all such books of account, records, bills of
  lading and invoices for six years after they are established; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes all such books of account, records, bills of
  lading and invoices available for inspection and audit by an
  authorized representative of the Minister during normal business
  hours.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-521, s. 7
- SOR/92-727, s. 5
:::

## [Conditions of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-476553 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), every
  payment related to the cost of feed grain transportation shall be made
  on the condition that the feed grain has been or will be fed to
  livestock at the destination in the area in respect of which the
  payment is made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} feed grain has been or will be fed to livestock at
    a destination in an area other than the destination in the area in
    respect of which the payment referred to in subsection (1) is made,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of transportation to the first-mentioned
    destination is less than the rate for the destination in respect of
    which that payment is made,

  the payment shall be considered to be an overpayment to the extent of
  the difference between the cost of transportation and that rate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-521, s. 9(F)
- SOR/92-727, s. 3
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every payment related to
the cost of feed grain storage shall be made on the condition that the
feed grain has been or will be fed to livestock in Eastern Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-727, s. 3
:::
