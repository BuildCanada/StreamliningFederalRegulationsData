## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-134
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2019-12-25

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2019-1379 2019-12-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to
the Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Intergovernmental Affairs the
control and supervision of that portion of the federal public
administration known as the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference
Secretariat.
