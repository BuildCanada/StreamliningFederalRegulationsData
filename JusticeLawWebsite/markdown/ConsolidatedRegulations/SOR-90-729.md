## Kemano Completion Project Guidelines Order {#kemano-completion-project-guidelines-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-729
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF THE ENVIRONMENT ACT](/eng/acts/E-10)

Registration 1990-10-12

Order Establishing the Kemano Completion Project Guidelines

P.C. 1990-2252 1990-10-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 6 of the
[Department of the Environment Act](/eng/acts/E-10), is pleased hereby
to approve the annexed [Order establishing the Kemano Completion Project
Guidelines](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-729), made by the Minister of the
Environment on October 4, 1990.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930760 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Kemano Completion Project Guidelines
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-729).

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930762 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Environmental
Assessment and Review Process Guidelines Order does not apply to the
project known as the Kemano Completion Project and, in particular, to
any decisions made as a result of the Settlement Agreement entered into
by Her Majesty in right of Canada, Her Majesty in right of the Province
of British Columbia and Alcan Aluminum Limited on September 14, 1987 and
approved by the Governor in Council by Order in Council P.C. 1987-2481
of December 10, 1987.
