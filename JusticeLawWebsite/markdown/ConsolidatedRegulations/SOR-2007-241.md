## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736358 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[assessable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assessable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means reserve land, an
    interest in reserve lands or a right to occupy, possess or use
    reserve lands, that is subject to assessment under a property
    assessment law. ([bien sujet à évaluation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person designated by a
    First Nation to conduct assessments of assessable
    property. ([évaluateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who commences an
    appeal under section 7. ([plaignant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the complainant, the assessor or
    the tax administrator. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property assessment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property assessment law]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a law made
    under subparagraph 5(1)(a)(i) of the [First Nations Fiscal
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67). ([texte législatif relatif à
    l'évaluation foncière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    responsible for the administration of a First Nation's property
    assessment laws. ([administrateur fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736369 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A property assessment law
shall incorporate the procedures set out in sections 3 to 13 or
procedures that are the same as those set out in the laws in respect of
assessment appeals of the province in which the assessable property is
situated.

## [Reconsideration of Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736372 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration procedure

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  property assessment law shall include a procedure by which a person
  named on the assessment roll in respect of an assessable property may
  request that the assessor reconsider the assessment of that property
  and provide a period of at least 21 days to conduct the
  reconsideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modified assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If after a reconsideration the assessor modifies an
  assessment, the assessor shall send notice of the modified assessment
  to the tax administrator and to any other person who received the
  original notice of assessment.

## [Appeal Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736380 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment appeal

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any person may appeal an
assessment or a reconsideration of an assessment of the assessable
property to an assessment review board established by the council of the
First Nation.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2024-277, s. 7(E)](#1502744-1503356){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1502744-1503356.html"
  role="button"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20071101.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment review board

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  assessment review board shall be composed of at least three members,
  one of whom shall be designated as the chairperson of the board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least one member of an assessment review board is
  to be a lawyer who is a practising or non-practising member in good
  standing of the law society of the province in which the assessable
  property is situated and at least one other member is to have
  experience in assessment appeals in the province in which the
  assessable property is situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person shall not serve as a member of an assessment
  review board if the person has a personal or financial interest in the
  assessable property that is the subject of the appeal or if the person
  is a chief or a member of the council of the First Nation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Membership in First Nation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (3), membership in the
  First Nation does not in itself constitute a personal or financial
  interest in assessable property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum appeal period

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If a property assessment
law establishes a period beyond which assessments are not to be
appealed, that period shall end not less than 45 days after the day on
which the notice of the assessment is mailed to the person named on the
assessment roll.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appeal

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal is commenced by delivery of a notice of appeal to the assessor
  at the address set out in the assessment notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal shall be accompanied by any fee
  required by the property assessment law, and shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and mailing address of the complainant and
    of any representative acting on behalf of the complainant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of the assessable property,
    including any assessment roll number, set out in the assessment
    notice; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the appeal.

- SOR/2016-29, s. 12

## [Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736408 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scheduling of hearing

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  delivery of a notice of appeal to the assessor, the chairperson of the
  assessment review board, in consultation with the assessor, shall
  schedule a hearing of the appeal and, at least 10 days before the day
  on which the hearing is scheduled to begin, shall deliver a written
  notice of the date, time and place of the hearing to the parties and
  to each person named on the assessment roll in respect of the
  assessable property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assessment review board shall commence the
  hearing within 45 days after the notice of appeal to the assessor is
  delivered, unless all parties consent to a later date.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-29, s. 13
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20071101.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing deferred

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a proceeding with
respect to liability to pay taxes in respect of the assessable property
that is the subject of an appeal is brought before a court of competent
jurisdiction

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the hearing is to commence, the hearing shall
  be deferred until the matter is decided by the court;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the hearing, the hearing shall be adjourned
  until the matter is decided by the court; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} after the hearing has concluded but before a decision
  on the appeal is given, the decision shall be deferred until the
  matter is decided by the court.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combined hearing

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The assessment review
board may conduct a single hearing of two or more appeals related to the
same assessment roll if the matters on appeal in each hearing are
addressing the same assessable property or substantially the same
issues.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery of documentation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The chairperson shall
without delay deliver a copy of any document submitted by a party in
relation to an appeal to all other parties.

## [Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736427 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  earliest opportunity after the day on which a hearing is completed,
  the assessment review board shall deliver a written decision on the
  appeal to all parties, and the assessor shall modify the assessment
  roll accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a property assessment law may
  establish a period during which the assessment review board is to
  render its decision. The period shall end not less than 90 days after
  the day on which the hearing is completed.

- SOR/2016-29, s. 15

## [Delivery of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736435 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of delivery

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document may be made
  personally or by sending it by registered mail, fax or e-mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal delivery of a document is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, by leaving the
    document with that individual or with a person at least 18 years of
    age residing at that individual's place of residence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation, by leaving the
    document with the person apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of the First Nation's administrative office, or with the
    First Nation's legal counsel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by leaving the
    document with the person apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of the corporation's head office or branch office, or with
    an officer or director of the corporation, or with the corporation's
    legal counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of delivery

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a document shall be
  considered to have been delivered

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if delivered personally, at the time that personal
    delivery is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if sent by registered mail, on the fifth day after
    it is mailed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if sent by fax, at the time indicated on the
    confirmation of transmission; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if sent by e-mail, at the time indicated in the
    electronic confirmation that the e-mail has been opened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document delivered on a non-business day or after
  17:00 local time on a business day shall be considered to have been
  delivered at 09:00 on the next business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal lies to a court of competent jurisdiction from a decision of
  the assessment review board on a question of law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appeal must be made within 30 days after the day
  on which the decision is delivered.
