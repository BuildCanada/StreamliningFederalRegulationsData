## P.E.I. Vegetable Directed Sales (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#p.e.i.-vegetable-directed-sales-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 237
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Directed Sales Flow in Interprovincial and
Export Trade of Vegetable Produced in Prince Edward Island
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537225 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [P.E.I. Vegetable Directed Sales (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._237).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537227 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island
    Vegetable Growers Cooperative Association and includes any
    sub-agents thereof; ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Vegetable Commodity Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any bag, box or other
    receptacle in which vegetable is or can be packed, transported or
    marketed; ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who produces
    vegetable and includes a producer as defined in the Plan;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual, firm,
    partnership, association or corporation that grows or markets
    vegetable and includes any servants or agents thereof;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island
    Vegetable Marketing Plan established pursuant to the Prince Edward
    Island Natural Products Marketing Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who changes the
    nature of vegetable by mechanical or other means and markets the
    processed vegetable; ([conditionneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sub-agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sub-agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agent of the Agency;
    ([sous-agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transport permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transport permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a valid permit in a
    form approved by and issued under the authority of the Commodity
    Board authorizing the movement or transportation of vegetable;
    ([permis de transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who holds or is
    required by law to hold a public or limited freight licence issued
    by the Public Utilities Commission of Prince Edward Island, but does
    not include a grower who uses his own truck for the sole purpose of
    hauling vegetable that he has grown; ([camionneur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turnip produced in the
    Province of Prince Edward Island; ([légumes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[warehouser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[warehouser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who stores
    vegetable for another person; ([entreposeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person other than the
    Agency who sells or offers for sale vegetable to any person other
    than directly to the consumer, but does not include a jobber or
    broker or a person who has no facilities that are customarily
    employed in his business to store or transport vegetable.
    ([grossiste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537242 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of vegetable in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated in the Province of Prince Edward
Island.

## [Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537244 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise ordered by
the Commodity Board, vegetable shall be marketed only through the
Agency.

## [Exclusive Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537246 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  grower shall, in order to market vegetable, deal with any person other
  than the Agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No wholesaler shall purchase or accept vegetable from
  any person other than the Agency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No processor shall market vegetable except in a
  processed or manufactured form.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No processor shall purchase or accept vegetable from
  any person other than the Agency or a wholesaler licensed pursuant to
  the [P.E.I. Vegetable Licensing (Interprovincial and Export)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._238).

## [Duties of Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537252 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Agency shall, in respect of the marketing of
  vegetable, collect from each grower such charges as are from time to
  time agreed upon by the Agency and the Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Agency sells vegetable directly to a
  processor it may, in addition to any charge referred to in subsection
  (1), charge the processor an amount equal to the normal wholesale
  mark-up at the time of the sale.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may, through its duly authorized officers,
  employees, servants or sub-agents, do anything that it is authorized
  or required to do by these Regulations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board may designate any person as a
  sub-agent.

## [Pooling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537258 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may, for such periods of time as it determines, conduct pools
  for the distribution of proceeds from sales by it of vegetable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In each pool conducted pursuant to subsection (1),
  the Agency shall distribute the proceeds from sales by it of
  vegetable, after deducting therefrom the charges referred to in
  section 6, in such a manner that each grower receives his share of the
  total proceeds in the pool in proportion to the quantity, and taking
  into account the variety, size and grade of the vegetable delivered by
  him to the Agency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No grower is entitled to a sale price for the
  vegetable delivered by him to the Agency that would result in his
  receiving an amount in excess of his share of the total proceeds in a
  pool, as described in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No distribution of the proceeds referred to in
  subsection (2) shall be made without the approval of the Commodity
  Board.

## [Quality]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537264 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency may refuse to accept or market any vegetable delivered to it
  that, in its opinion, is not of marketable quality.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, in any manner, market or dispose of
  any vegetable that the Agency has refused to accept or market, except
  under an order of the Commodity Board and in accordance with the terms
  of the order.

## [Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537268 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall market any vegetable unless it is
  in a container that is approved by the Commodity Board and has
  attached thereto an appropriate label or tag in a form approved by the
  Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of the
  shipping of any vegetable to the Agency.

<!-- -->

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall transport any vegetable in any vehicle unless a transport permit
  in respect of the vegetable has been issued to him and is carried by
  him in the vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may transport any vegetable in a vehicle if
  a transport permit in respect of the vegetable has been issued in his
  name and has been mailed to his address.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall transport any vegetable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any quantity,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to or from any place, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on any day or during any period

  that is not authorized by the transport permit issued to him in
  respect of the vegetable.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall issue or use, in respect of any vegetable, any licence,
  transport permit, container, label or tag that has not been authorized
  by the Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No licence, transport permit, container, label or tag
  authorized by the Commodity Board in respect of any vegetable shall be
  issued or sold except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by a person who is a member of, or an employee duly
    authorized by, the Commodity Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the name and on behalf of the Commodity Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any licence, transport permit, container, label
  or tag authorized by the Commodity Board in respect of any vegetable
  is required to be signed, it may be signed by any person authorized
  for that purpose by the Commodity Board.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall use in respect of any vegetable any licence, transport
  permit, container, label or tag unless it was issued to him by the
  Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall remove, alter, mutilate or destroy
  any label or tag issued by the Commodity Board and attached to a
  container that contains any vegetable.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use or permit to be used any label or
  tag issued by the Commodity Board in respect of any vegetable for any
  purpose other than that for which it was intended.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use any label or tag issued by the
  Commodity Board in respect of any vegetable if the label or tag has
  previously been used.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, without the written permission of
  the Agency, use, for a purpose other than marketing vegetable, any
  container intended for such marketing.
