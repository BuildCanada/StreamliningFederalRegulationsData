## Contaminated Fuel Regulations {#contaminated-fuel-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-486
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1991-08-14

Contaminated Fuel Regulations

P.C. 1991-1433 1991-08-13
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EHBDA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on March 16, 1991, a copy of
the proposed Regulations respecting the import and export of
contaminated fuel and amending the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule
I to the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, substantially in the
form annexed hereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EHBDA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 16
  (4th Supp.)
  :::

Whereas more than 60 days have elapsed from the date of publication and
no notices of objection to the proposed Regulations were filed with the
Minister of the Environment pursuant to subsection 48(2) of the Canadian
Environmental Protection Act^\*^;

And Whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, the
regulations do not regulate any aspect of any substance that is
regulated by or under any other Act of Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
section 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EIBAA} of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act^\*^, on the recommendation of the
Minister of the Environment and the Minister of National Health and
Welfare and after the federal-provincial advisory committee has been
given an opportunity to provide its advice under section 6 of that Act,
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the import
and export of contaminated fuel and amending the List of Toxic
Substances in Schedule I to the Canadian Environmental Protection Act.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EIBAA){#footnote2_e}S.C.
  1989, c. 9, s. 2
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937043 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[contaminated fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any fuel described in
item 13 of the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1 to the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-102, s. 6
:::

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937047 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections 4(1) and (2.1) and section 5, it is prohibited for any
  person to import contaminated fuel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections 4(2) and (2.1), it is
  prohibited for any person to export contaminated fuel.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937051 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may import into Canada contaminated fuel for destruction, disposal or
  recycling in accordance with applicable federal or provincial law
  regarding the destruction, disposal or recycling of hazardous wastes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may export contaminated fuel to a country if
  the appropriate authority in that country authorizes or permits the
  importation.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} A person does not contravene section 3 if the
  contaminated fuel is in transit through Canada, from a place outside
  Canada to another place outside Canada, and there is written evidence
  establishing that the fuel is in transit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), [[appropriate
  authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the authority, body or person,
  of a country that is competent under the laws of that country to
  authorize the import of contaminated fuel into that country.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Maintenance of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937058 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who imports contaminated fuel into Canada shall maintain, for each
  shipment of contaminated fuel imported, records that contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of contaminated fuel imported;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each type of contaminated fuel
    imported;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the contaminated fuel was
    imported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry through which the contaminated
    fuel passed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the commodity code for each type of contaminated
    fuel imported;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the importer number for the shipment of
    contaminated fuel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the contaminated
    fuel, if known;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name of the supplier of the contaminated fuel,
    if different from that of the manufacturer;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} locations to which the contaminated fuel is to be
    delivered;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of all purchasers of the
    contaminated fuel;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the class and name of each dangerous good that
    contaminates the fuel; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the contaminated fuel was
    imported.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who exports contaminated fuel out of Canada
  shall maintain, for each shipment of contaminated fuel exported, a
  document that provides proof that the importation into the country of
  destination is authorized or permitted by that country in accordance
  with subsection 4(2).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any record referred to in subsection (1) and any
  document referred to in subsection (2) shall be kept by the importer
  or exporter, as the case may be, for a period of five years after the
  day on which the record or document, as the case may be, is made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-11, s. 4

## [Production of Records and Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-937077 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An importer or exporter of
contaminated fuel shall, on request of an enforcement officer, produce
any records or documents that contain the information referred to in
section 5.
