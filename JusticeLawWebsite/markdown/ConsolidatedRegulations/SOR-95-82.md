## Scientific or Exploratory Expeditions Remission Order {#scientific-or-exploratory-expeditions-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-82
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1995-01-31

Scientific or Exploratory Expeditions Remission Order

P.C. 1995-132  1995-01-31
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 101 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-82_e_hq_6330){#fn_SOR-95-82_e_hq_6330-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of
duties on certain goods imported into Canada by scientific or
exploratory expeditions.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977204 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scientific equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scientific equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means instruments,
    apparatus, photographic equipment, machines or their accessories,
    used for the purposes of conducting experiments or gathering
    information during scientific or exploratory expeditions; ([matériel
    scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scientific or exploratory expedition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scientific or exploratory expedition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    an expedition

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conducted or sponsored by a scientific or
      cultural organization, an institution of learning or a foreign
      government,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the participants in which are non-residents of
      Canada, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sponsors of which have undertaken to make
      available to the Government of Canada all of the information
      obtained in Canada as a result of the expedition's field studies;
      ([expédition scientifique ou explorative]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[support equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[support equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[ \[Repealed, SOR/98-60, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

[[tools]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tools]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tools specially designed for the
    maintenance, checking, gauging or repair of scientific equipment.
    ([outils]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-60, s. 3
- SOR/99-233, s. 4
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977217 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the taxes imposed under Division III of Part IX of the Act and under
any other Part of the Act paid or payable on foodstuffs and other
consumable goods, other than alcoholic beverages and tobacco products,
imported on or after January 1, 1991, by a scientific or exploratory
expedition for its exclusive use while conducting field studies in
Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-60, s. 4
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6,
remission is hereby granted of the taxes imposed under Division III of
Part IX of the Act and under any other Part of the Act paid or payable
on scientific equipment, spare parts of scientific equipment and tools
imported on or after January 1, 1991, by a scientific or exploratory
expedition for use in conducting field studies in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-60, s. 4
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/98-60, s.
4\]]{.Repealed}

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977225 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted pursuant to
  section 4 on the condition that the scientific equipment, spare parts
  and tools are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} described in a document, on importation, in the
    form authorized by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used exclusively by the scientific or exploratory
    expedition in conducting field studies in Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} destroyed in Canada under the supervision of a
    customs officer at the expense of the importer or exported from
    Canada

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within two years after the date on which the
      goods were accounted for under the [Customs
      Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where an extension is granted pursuant to
      subsection (2), within two years after the date on which that
      extension expires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the two-year period referred
  to in subparagraph (1)(c)(i) by one or more periods, not exceeding two
  years each, where the importer has presented evidence indicating that
  the extension is necessary to allow the scientific or exploratory
  expedition to complete its field studies in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-60, s. 5
