## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-669875 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted to Orion Bus Industries Ltd. of Mississauga,
Ontario, in the amount of \$993,907.56 representing the customs duties
paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on buses
imported during the period commencing on August 1, 1996, and ending on
December 31, 1997.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-669877 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 1 on condition that a claim for remission is made to
the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the date on
which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-669879 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
