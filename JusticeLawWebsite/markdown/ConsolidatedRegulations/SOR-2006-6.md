      complaint. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anyone who has a right to be

  Human Rights Commission is a participant in a proceeding in relation
  to a complaint if the Canadian Human Rights Commission has given
  notice under subsection 20(3) that it intends to make submissions
  regarding an issue raised by the complainant involving the
  interpretation or the application of the [Canadian Human Rights
  Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility Commissioner

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the
  Accessibility Commissioner is a participant in a proceeding in
  relation to a complaint if the Accessibility Commissioner has given
  notice under subsection 20.1(3) that they intend to make submissions
  regarding an issue raised by the complainant involving the
  contravention of a provision of regulations made under subsection
  117(1) of the [Accessible Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6).

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When notice considered received

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A notice that is sent to a
party, an intervenor, the Canadian Human Rights Commission or the
Accessibility Commissioner is considered to have been received

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the notice is sent by electronic mail, fax, or
  other electronic means, on the day on which it is sent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by courier or delivered by hand, on the
  day on which it is received; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by mail, on the day that is six days
  after the date of the postmark or of the postage meter impression
  authorized by the Canada Post Corporation, or if both the postmark and
  postage meter impression appear on the envelope, on the later of the
  dates indicated.

## [Making a Complaint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728365 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for making complaint

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  complaint must be received by the Board no later than 15 days after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the complainant receives notice of
    the lay-off, revocation, appointment or proposed appointment to
    which the complaint relates; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date specified in the notice, if it is a public
    notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of complaint

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A complaint is considered to have been received by
  the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the complaint is sent by electronic mail, fax or
    other electronic means, on the day on which it is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by courier, on the day on which it is
    sent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is delivered by hand, on the day on which it
    is received; or

  - [d)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by mail, on the day that is the date
    of the postmark or of the postage meter impression authorized by the
    Canada Post Corporation, or if both the postmark and postage meter
    impression appear on the envelope, on the later of the dates
    indicated.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of complaint

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A complaint must be filed
with the Board in writing and must include

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the complainant or their authorized
  representative; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the date of the complaint.

## [Alternate Dispute Resolution Processes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728406 .Part}

### [Mediation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-728407 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in mediation

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must schedule mediation for a complaint unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complainant informs the Board, no later than 25
    days after the date of the letter by which the Board acknowledges
    receipt of the complaint, that the complainant does not wish to
    participate in mediation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the respondent informs the Board, no later than 25
    days after they receive a copy of the complaint, that they do not
    wish to participate in mediation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to mediate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The complainant or respondent may, before the date of
  the hearing and with the agreement of the other party, request the
  Board to refer the complaint to mediation.

### [Exchange of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-728417 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange of information

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  facilitate the resolution of the complaint, the complainant and
  respondent must, as soon as feasible after the complaint has been
  filed, exchange all relevant information regarding the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for the exchange

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exchange of information must be completed no
  later than 25 days after the date of the letter by which the Board
  acknowledges receipt of the complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to exchange information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the complainant or respondent does not complete
  the exchange of all relevant information as required by
  subsections (1) and (2), the Board may order the parties to complete
  the exchange within the time specified by the Board.

### [Order to Provide Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-728427 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for order to provide information

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party refuses to provide information, the complainant or respondent
  may, after the time provided for the exchange of information, request
  the Board to order that the information be provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for an order to provide information must
  be in writing and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the party
    making the request;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the complaint to which
    the request relates;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list specifying the documents or information
    requested;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed explanation as to why each requested
    item is relevant to the complaint; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any time that is specified in these Regulations in
  relation to a complaint, notice or document is suspended until the
  Board renders a decision respecting the request for an order to
  provide information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to provide information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board must order that the complainant or
  respondent be provided with the information if the Board determines
  that the information may be relevant and that its provision will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} threaten national security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} threaten any person's safety; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} affect the validity or continued use of a
    standardized test, or any part of such a test, or distort the
    results of a standardized test by giving an unfair advantage to any
    individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board may make the order to provide information
  subject to any conditions that it determines are necessary, including
  any conditions that are necessary to prevent any of the risks referred
  to in paragraphs (4)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of conditions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The conditions apply before and after the hearing or
  after any other resolution or disposition of the complaint.

## [Intervenors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728456 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to intervene

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Anyone
  with a substantial interest in a proceeding before the Board may apply
  to the Board for permission to intervene in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be in writing and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information that are to be
    used for sending documents to the applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-116, s. 11\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the applicant's
    authorized representative, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the complaint that is
    the subject of the application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for intervention and the interest of
    the applicant in the matter;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the contribution that the applicant expects to make
    if allowed to intervene;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or their authorized
    representative; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board must give the parties and, if they are
  participants, the Canadian Human Rights Commission and the
  Accessibility Commissioner, the opportunity to make submissions in
  respect of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowing the application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may allow the applicant to intervene after
  considering the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant is directly affected by the
    proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant's position is already
    represented in the proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the public interest or the interests of
    justice would be served by allowing the applicant to intervene; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the input of the applicant would assist the
    Board in deciding the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to intervenor

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In allowing the application, the Board may issue
  directions regarding the role of the intervenor, including any matter
  relating to the procedure to be followed by the intervenor.

## [Notice to the Canadian Human Rights Commission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728482 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issue

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  complainant raises an issue involving the interpretation or
  application of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6) in a
  complaint made under subsection 65(1) or 77(1) of the Act, the
  complainant must notify in writing the Canadian Human Rights
  Commission under subsection 65(5) or section 78 of the Act. The notice
  must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the complainant
    that can be disclosed to all parties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    complainant's authorized representative, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the issue involving the
    interpretation or the application of the [Canadian Human Rights
    Act](/eng/acts/H-6) and of the alleged discriminatory practice or
    policy;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited ground of discrimination involved;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the corrective action sought;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the complainant or the
    complainant's authorized representative; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies to be provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The complainant must give a copy of the notice to
  each of the other parties, to the Board and to each of the
  intervenors, if any. Those copies do not need to include copies of the
  complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intention to make submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Human Rights Commission must, no later
  than 15 days after receiving the notice, notify the Board whether or
  not it intends to make submissions regarding the issue referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Board to provide copies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board must give a copy of the notice from the
  Canadian Human Rights Commission to each of the parties and each of
  the intervenors, if any.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-116, s. 12
- SOR/2014-250, s. 7

## [Notice to the Accessibility Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1380521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issue

- **[[20.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the complainant raises an issue involving the contravention of a
  provision of regulations made under subsection 117(1) of the
  [Accessible Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6) in a complaint made under
  subsection 65(1) or 77(1) of the Act, the complainant must notify in
  writing the Accessibility Commissioner under subsection 65(9) or
  section 78.1 of the Act. The notice must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the complainant
    that can be disclosed to all parties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    complainant's authorized representative, if any;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the issue;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the corrective action sought;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the complainant or their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of the notice --- complainant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The complainant must give a copy of the notice to
  each of the other parties, to the Board and to each of the
  intervenors, if any. Those copies do not need to include copies of the
  complaint.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intention to make submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Accessibility Commissioner must, no later than 15
  days after receiving the notice, notify the Board whether or not the
  Commissioner intends to make submissions regarding the issue referred
  to in paragraph (1)(d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of the notice --- Board

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board must give a copy of the Commissioner's
  notice to each of the parties and each of the intervenors, if any.

## [Time Period for Raising Timeliness Objection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any
  of the following persons wishes to object on the ground that the
  complaint was not made within the period required under section 10,
  they must do so before the end of the period for exchanging
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the respondent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person appointed or proposed for appointment;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one of the employees referred to in subsection
    65(3) of the Act, other than the complainant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of objection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The objection must be in writing and must include

    party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the objecting
    party's authorized representative, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the complaint;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the facts and any documents on which the objecting
    party relies in making the objection; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of the objection.

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-116, s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

## [Allegations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728516 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  10 days after the end of the period for exchanging information, the
  complainant must provide their allegations to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the other parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the intervenors, if any; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Human Rights Commission and the
    Accessibility Commissioner, if they are participants.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of allegations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The allegations must be in writing and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the complainant
    that can be disclosed to all parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    complainant's authorized representative, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number for the complaint;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the allegations on which
    the complainant intends to rely and full particulars of the relevant
    facts; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-116, s. 14\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide allegations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the complainant fails to provide allegations, the
  Board may dismiss the complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

  give notice that the complaint has been withdrawn and the file closed
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the other parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the intervenors, if any; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Human Rights Commission and the
    Accessibility Commissioner, if they are participants.
