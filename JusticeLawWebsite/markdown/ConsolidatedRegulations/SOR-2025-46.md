## [Scope of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515346 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings under [National Defence
Act](/eng/acts/N-5)

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply to all
proceedings under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) that are
presided over by a military judge.

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a barrister or advocate with
    standing at the bar of a province. ([avocat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Court Martial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Court Martial Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    appointed under section 165.18 of the Act. ([administrateur de la
    cour martiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the prosecutor, the accused
    person or any other person who is designated as a party to the
    proceeding. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Queen's Regulations and Orders]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Queen's Regulations and Orders]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Queen's Regulations and Orders for the Canadian
    Forces. ([Ordonnances et règlements royaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Citation of Statutes, Regulations and Other Authorities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515357 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorities to be provided

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  who intends to rely on statutes, regulations or other authorities in
  any oral or written argument in a proceeding must provide a copy of
  them to every other party and to the presiding military judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a party is not required to
  provide a copy of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), the
  [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5) or the
  [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indicating relevant portions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party must indicate the relevant portions of each
  statute, regulation or authority by highlighting them or by placing a
  vertical line in the margin next to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on electronic database

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the party intends to rely on a decision that is
  publicly available on an electronic database, every reference to that
  decision in their arguments must be to the decision as published on
  that database.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515367 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1515368 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Service of a document must
be effected by personal service, registered mail, fax or email.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When service is effective

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Service of a document is
effective

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is served by personal service, on the day on
  which the document is left with the person being served or their
  authorized representative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is served by registered mail, on the day of
  delivery that is indicated on the post office delivery receipt;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is served by fax, on the day of transmission
  that is indicated on the transmission confirmation receipt; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it served by email, on the day on which the
  document is received by the person being served.

### [Service by Fax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1515377 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by fax must be printed on letter size paper.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Over 50 pages

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A document that is more
than 50 pages in length must not be served by fax without the
recipient's prior consent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cover sheet

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by fax must have a cover sheet that sets out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the party
  who is serving the document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including the
  cover page; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to be
  contacted in the event of a transmission problem.

### [Service by Email]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1515388 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent required

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A party must not serve a
document by email until the recipient gives consent in accordance with
section 10 or after the recipient withdraws that consent in accordance
with section 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to service by email

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a party
  consents to the service of documents by email by providing the Court
  Martial Administrator with a consent to service by email as set out in
  Form 1 of the schedule and serving it on every other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel appointed by either the Director of Military
  Prosecutions or the Director of Defence Counsel Services is deemed to
  have given prior consent to be served with documents by email,
  however, that consent may be withdrawn in accordance with section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When consent is effective

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The consent is effective on the day on which the Form
  1 is served.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of consent

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party withdraws their consent to the service of documents by email by
  providing the Court Martial Administrator with a withdrawal of consent
  to service by email as set out in Form 2 of the schedule and serving
  it on every other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When withdrawal is effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The withdrawal of consent is effective on the day on
  which the Form 2 is served.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A document that is served
by email must be in PDF (Portable Document Format) and the email to
which the document is attached must set out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email address
  of the party who is serving the document;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party who is being served with the
  document and, if applicable, their counsel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the email transmission; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the title of each document that is being served, the
  number of attachments and, for each attachment, the number of pages.

### [Proof of Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1515409 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When proof of service required

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A party must provide
proof of service of a document in accordance with subsection 14(1) if it
is expressly required to be provided by these Rules or if the presiding
military judge, having determined that it is necessary for the party to
prove that service of the document occurred, directs the party to
provide proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is proven by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of service as set out in Form 3 of
    the schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit or other document demonstrating
    service that, in accordance with the laws of the jurisdiction in
    which the service was effected, is provided by the person who
    effected the service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgment of service provided by the
    person served or their counsel; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgement or acceptance of service
    provided by the person served or their counsel while in open court
    before the presiding military judge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery and confirmation receipts

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document is served by registered mail, fax or
  email, a certificate of service for that document must be accompanied
  by one of the following documents, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the post office delivery receipt that
    indicates the day on which the document was delivered by registered
    mail; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transmission confirmation receipt that
    indicates the date and time of the transmission of the document by
    fax or email.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No further proof required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite these Rules, a party is not required to
  provide proof of service for a document if an acknowledgment or
  acceptance of service of the document is provided under paragraph
  1(d).

## [Custody Review Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative of the Canadian Forces

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** In sections 16 to 18,
[[representative of the Canadian Forces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
same meaning as in paragraph 105.27(1) of the Queen's Regulations and
Orders.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of release by custody review
officer

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If the custody review
officer referred to in section 158.2 of the Act does not direct that the
person in custody be released, the representative of the Canadian Forces
must notify the Court Martial Administrator of that decision as soon as
practicable after it is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided by custody review
officer

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of a
hearing referred to in section 159 of the Act, the custody review
officer must, by the most rapid and practical means, provide the
representative of the Canadian Forces, the person in custody and the
Court Martial Administrator with the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the service number, rank and name of the person in
  custody;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list setting out each charge that has been laid
  against the person in custody and, for each charge, information
  indicating whether it is a [[designated
  offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 153 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time when the person was committed to
  custody;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} information indicating whether the person in custody
  has retained or has requested counsel or whether they have chosen not
  to be represented by counsel;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location where the person is being retained in
  custody;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the rank, name and telephone number of the person who
  is responsible for the person in custody;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the service number, rank, name and telephone number
  of the custody review officer;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the custody review officer's review
  of the report of custody and the accompanying documents was completed
  for the purposes of subsection 158.2(1) of the Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rank, name and telephone number of the
  representative of the Canadian Forces and information that indicates
  that they have been notified of the request for a custody review
  hearing; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the location of the video conference facility that is
  nearest to the location where the person is being retained in custody
  and the name and telephone number of the person to be contacted to
  reserve the facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate means of hearing ---
information and documents

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  military judge directs that the custody review hearing be held, in
  whole or in part, by means of a telecommunications device, the parties
  must provide information and documents to the Court Martial
  Administrator as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each party must provide the evidence that the party
    intends to present at the hearing and a copy of all statutes,
    regulations and other authorities on which they intend to rely at
    the hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} together with the information and documents
    referred to in paragraph (a), the representative of the Canadian
    Forces must provide a copy of the report of custody referred to in
    subsection 158.1(1) of the Act and the accompanying documents
    referred to in subsection 158.1(5) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information and documents must be provided no
  later than the day before the day on which the hearing is scheduled to
  be held.

## [Coordinating Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515449 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be discussed

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a court martial there must be a coordinating conference, held
  between the parties and the military judge that is assigned for that
  purpose, in order to schedule the trial and to discuss any matters
  that may impact its duration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scheduling the conference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court Martial Administrator must contact the
  parties to schedule the coordinating conference no later than 45 days
  after the day on which the Court Martial Administrator receives the
  charge sheet that is referred to in subsection 165(2) of the Act.
  However, if the proceeding is one in which the accused person may
  choose the type of court martial under section 165.193 of the Act, the
  time period begins on the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the Court Martial Administrator
    receives the charge sheet that is referred to in subsection 165(2)
    of the Act, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the Court Martial Administrator
    receives the completed confirmation of delivery in the form referred
    to in paragraph 111.023(3) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Holding the conference

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The coordinating conference must be held no later
  than 15 days after the day on which the parties are contacted under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The coordinating conference is to be held by
  telephone unless, after consultation with the parties, the military
  judge directs that it be held by means of any other telecommunications
  device or in person.

## [Pre-trial Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding military judge may, at the request of a party or on his or
  her own initiative, direct that a pre-trial conference be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be discussed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At a pre-trial conference, the parties must be
  prepared to discuss matters of law or evidence that are at issue in
  the trial or any other matter that could facilitate the trial.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A pre-trial conference is to be held by telephone
  unless, after consultation with the parties, the presiding military
  judge directs that the conference be held by means of any other
  telecommunications device or in person.

## [Interpreter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515469 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A request by a party
under the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01) for an interpreter
at the hearing of a court martial or any other proceeding before a
military judge must be provided in writing to the Court Martial
Administrator as soon as is practicable before the hearing begins.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  each application that is made under the Act and each notice that is
  given in respect of an application referred to in paragraph 112.04(1)
  of the Queen's Regulations and Orders must be made as set out in Form
  4 of the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plea of guilty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a plea of guilty under subsection
  189.1(2) of the Act must be made as set out in Form 5 of the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of counsel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application to withdraw as counsel for the accused
  person must be made in accordance with section 29.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications --- general

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application, other than one for which notice is given under paragraph
  112.04(1) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders, must contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sufficient detail of the nature of the application
    and of the relief sought to enable the opposing party to respond
    without adjournment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the documentary, affidavit or other evidence that
    the applicant intends to present at the hearing of the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the time required to present the
    application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed time, date and location for the
    hearing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed means for conducting the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be served on every other party
  and a copy of the application, together with proof of service, must be
  provided to the Court Martial Administrator no later than five days
  before the day on which the hearing is proposed to be held.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications referred to in Queen's
Regulations and Orders

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notice
  that is given in respect of an application referred to in paragraph
  112.04(1) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders must contain the
  following information in addition to that required under paragraph
  112.04(2) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed time, date and location for the
    hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed means for conducting the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Court Martial Administrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3) and in addition to the
  requirements of paragraph 112.04(1) of the Queen's Regulations and
  Orders, a copy of the notice must be provided to the Court Martial
  Administrator no later than five days before the day on which the
  hearing is proposed to be held.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plea of guilty

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements of paragraph
  112.04(1) of the Queen's Regulations and Orders, notice that is given
  in respect of an application for a plea of guilty under subsection
  189.1(2) of the Act must be served on the prosecutor and a copy of it
  provided to the Court Martial Administrator no later than five days
  before the day on which the accused person is ordered to appear before
  the court martial.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reply to application

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent who makes a written reply to an application must make the
  reply as set out in Form 6 of the schedule and set out the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the respondent's position with respect to the
    matters raised in the application, indicating the matters that are
    at issue, the grounds of argument and those matters which the
    respondent does not dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the documentary, affidavit or other evidence that
    the respondent intends to present at the hearing of the application;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the time required to present the
    reply to the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reply must be provided to the Court Martial
  Administrator and every other party no later than the day before the
  day on which the application is scheduled to be heard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of application

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** An applicant who seeks to
withdraw their application before it is heard must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify that application in a notice of withdrawal
  as set out in Form 7 of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the notice of withdrawal to every
  other party and the Court Martial Administrator before the day on
  which the application is scheduled to be heard.

## [Substitution of Prosecutor]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515511 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice required

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If the Director of
Military Prosecutions substitutes the prosecutor in a proceeding, the
Director must provide the Court Martial Administrator with written
notice of the substitution as soon as possible after it occurs.

## [Withdrawal of Counsel for Accused Person]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** If counsel for the
accused withdraws from the proceeding before the court martial is
convened, they must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve notice of their withdrawal, as set out in Form
  8 of the schedule, on the accused person and on the Director of
  Military Prosecutions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the notice to the Court Martial
  Administrator, together with proof of service, no later than five days
  after the day on which service of the notice is effected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to withdraw

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the presiding military judge directs otherwise, if counsel for the
  accused person seeks to withdraw from the proceeding after the court
  martial is convened, they must provide the Court Martial Administrator
  with an application to withdraw as counsel for the accused person, as
  set out in Form 9 of the schedule, that sets out the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the court martial was convened;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason why counsel seeks to withdraw from the
    proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the documentary, affidavit or other evidence that
    the applicant intends to present at the hearing of the application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the time required to present the
    application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed time, date and location for the
    hearing; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed means for conducting the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel for the accused person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve the application on the following persons no
    later than five days before the day on which the application is
    proposed to be heard:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the accused person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Director of Military Prosecutions,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if counsel for the accused person was appointed
      by the Director of Defence Counsel Services, the Director of
      Defence Counsel Services, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} every other person who is designated as a party
      to the proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the application to the Court
    Martial Administrator, together with proof of service, no later than
    the day before the day on which the application is proposed to be
    heard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reply to application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A respondent who makes a written reply to an
  application referred in this section must make the reply in accordance
  with section 25.

## [Public Access to Exhibits, Documents and Other Things]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1515538 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access request --- ongoing proceeding

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of the public may make a request to the presiding military
  judge for access to an exhibit, document or other thing connected with
  an ongoing proceeding, other than information relating to a warrant
  that is subject to section 196.25 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to court reporter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made as set out in Form 10 of the
  schedule and be provided to the court reporter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Granting access

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any conditions that the presiding military
  judge considers just, the judge must grant access to an exhibit,
  document or other thing requested under subsection (1) if, after
  taking the public interest in open and accessible court proceedings
  into account, the judge concludes that access will not subvert the
  ends of justice or unduly impair its proper administration.
