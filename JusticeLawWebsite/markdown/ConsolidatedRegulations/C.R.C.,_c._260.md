## Quebec Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#quebec-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 260
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Turkey Producers in Quebec
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537458 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._260).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537460 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Quebec; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fédération des
    producteurs de volailles du Québec, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of
    turkey meat that a producer is entitled to market in
    intraprovincial, interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the
    Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota Regulations and the regulations
    enacted by the Commodity Board pursuant to the Plan; ([allocation de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Quebec Poultry Producers'
    Joint Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act and any regulations made thereunder to give effect to the Plan;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production or growing of turkeys in the Province of Quebec and
    subject to the Plan; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey produced or grown in
    the Province of Quebec for the purpose of slaughter.
    ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537468 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the charges
set out under the Plan, levies are payable to the Commodity Board by
every producer at the rate of \$0.05 per pound, live weight, on every
turkey marketed by him that is in excess of his market allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537470 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who buys turkeys from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the turkeys all levies payable by the
  producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and shall pay such
  levies to the Commodity Board at its office, 515 Viger Street East,
  Montreal, Quebec, not later than the 15th day of the month next
  following the month in which the levies were deducted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office, 515 Viger Street East, Montreal, Quebec, all levies
  payable by him under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the
  Commodity Board in the manner prescribed in subsection (1), not later
  than the 15th day of the month next following the month in which the
  turkeys in respect of which the levies are payable were sold.
