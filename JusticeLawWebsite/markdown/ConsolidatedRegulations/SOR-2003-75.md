## Canadian Egg Marketing Levies Order {#canadian-egg-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-75
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 2003-02-20

Canadian Egg Marketing Levies Order
::::

Agency Proclamation[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGDFA}, established

22(1)(f) of that Act;

  [[Return to footnote

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)^d^ of that Act, the National
Farm Products Council has approved the proposed Order after being
satisfied that the proposed Order is necessary for the implementation of
the marketing plan that that Agency is authorized to implement;

Therefore, the Canadian Egg Marketing Agency, pursuant to paragraph
22(1)(f) of the [Farm Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)^c^ and
section 10 of Part II of the schedule to the Canadian Egg Marketing
Agency Proclamation^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Canadian Egg Marketing
Levies Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-280).

Ottawa, Ontario, February 20, 2003

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708132 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Egg Marketing
    Agency. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Ontario Egg Producers;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Fédération des producteurs d\'oeufs de
      consommation du Québec;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Nova Scotia Egg Producers;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the New Brunswick Egg Marketing Board;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Manitoba Egg Producers;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the British Columbia Egg Producers;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Egg Producers of Prince Edward Island;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Saskatchewan Egg Producers;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Alberta Egg Producers Board;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Egg Producers of Newfoundland and Labrador;
      and

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the Northwest Territories Egg Producers\' Board.
      ([office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[egg]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the egg of a hen.
    ([oeuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grading station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grading station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    washes, grades, packs or markets eggs and includes, when applicable,
    a Commodity Board and a producer-vendor. ([(responsable d\'un poste
    de classement)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hen of any class of the domestic
    chicken belonging to the species [Gallus domesticus]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}. ([poule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the marketing plan the terms of
    which are set out in Part II of the schedule to the Canadian Egg
    Marketing Agency Proclamation. ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is engaged in
    the production of eggs in Canada and includes a producer-vendor.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer-vendor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer-vendor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer who is a
    grading station operator in respect of all or any portion of the
    eggs produced by him or her. ([producteur-vendeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708153 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 3(1) does not
apply in respect of eggs marketed under a special temporary market
requirement quota that is allotted under the [Canadian Egg Marketing
Agency Quota Regulations, 1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-8).

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708157 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  producer shall pay, on each dozen of eggs marketed by the producer in
  interprovincial or export trade, a levy of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Ontario, \$0.5445;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Quebec, \$0.5205;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Nova Scotia, \$0.5195;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of New Brunswick, \$0.5555;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Manitoba, \$0.5595;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of British Columbia, \$0.5579;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Prince Edward Island, \$0.5295;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Saskatchewan, \$0.5710;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Alberta, \$0.5905;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the Province of Newfoundland and Labrador,
    \$0.5295; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the Northwest Territories, \$0.5535.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) ceases to have effect on March 27,
  2026.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-287, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2017-207, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} Each producer shall pay a levy of \$0.80 for each
  dozen of eggs marketed by the producer in interprovincial or export
  trade in excess of the special temporary market requirement quota that
  is allotted to the producer under the Canadian Marketing Agency Quota
  Regulations, 1986, or marketed in a manner contrary to the conditions
  respecting that quota.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Each producer shall pay a levy of \$0.50 for each
  dozen of eggs marketed by the producer in interprovincial or export
  trade in excess of a federal quota allotted to the producer under the
  [Canadian Egg Marketing Agency Quota Regulations,
  1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-8), or marketed in a manner contrary to
  the conditions respecting that quota.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (3) to (5) apply in addition to any other
  measures or sanctions under the [Farm Products Agencies
  Act](/eng/acts/F-4) or under any order or regulation of the Agency.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If eggs are sold or
otherwise disposed of by a producer to a grading station operator, the
grading station operator shall deduct the amount of the levy payable
under section 3 in respect of those eggs from the moneys payable by the
grading station operator to the producer for the eggs.

## [Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708180 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Agency, having obtained
the concurrence of each Commodity Board, hereby appoints each Commodity
Board, under subsection 10(4) of the Plan, to collect the levy payable
under section 3 on behalf of the Agency.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A levy
  payable under section 3 shall be remitted to the Commodity Board of
  the province where the producer on whom the levy is imposed is engaged
  in the production of eggs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remittance shall be made at the address and at
  the times that the Commodity Board directs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in section 4, by the
    grading station operator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other circumstances, by the producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each Commodity Board shall send to the Agency all the
  levies received by it under subsection (1) during a week at the end of
  that week.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The levy referred to in
subsection 3(3) may be deducted by the Agency from the moneys payable to
the producer.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708189 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
