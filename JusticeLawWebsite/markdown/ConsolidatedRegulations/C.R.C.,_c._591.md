## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558190 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Deductions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558194 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where by
  the Act a person is entitled

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to make a deduction from tax payable by him,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a refund of tax paid, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to receive a payment from the Minister in an amount
    equal to tax paid,

  and circumstances exist that render it difficult to determine the
  exact amount of such deduction, refund or payment by the Minister, the
  amount of the deduction, refund or payment by the Minister shall,
  where the person consents, be determined in the manner set out in
  subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exact amount of deduction, refund or payment by
  the Minister, determined for the purpose of subsection (1), shall be
  equal to the tax that would have been paid at the time of imposition
  of the tax on the goods on a price or value determined by reducing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale price of the goods in the transactions in
    respect of which the deduction, refund or payment by the Minister is
    applied for, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contract price where the goods were used in
    carrying out a contract and no sale price of the goods in the
    transactions in respect of which the deduction, refund or payment by
    the Minister is applied for can be established,

  by a percentage thereof determined by the Minister after taking into
  account the class of the goods and the nature of and parties to the
  transaction that resulted in the application for a deduction, refund
  or payment by the Minister.
