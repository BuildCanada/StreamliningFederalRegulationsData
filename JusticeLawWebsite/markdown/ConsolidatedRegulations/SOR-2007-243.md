## First Nations Taxation Enforcement Regulations {#first-nations-taxation-enforcement-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Registration 2007-11-01

First Nations Taxation Enforcement Regulations

P.C. 2007-1668 2007-11-01

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 5(1)(e), subsection 5(7) and paragraph 36(1)(a) of the First
Nations Fiscal and Statistical Management Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_607158-E_hq_1554){#fn_607158-E_hq_1554-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [First Nations Taxation Enforcement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-243).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736524 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Fiscal
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debtor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is liable for
    unpaid taxes, interest or penalties imposed under a property
    taxation law. ([débiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[taxable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[taxable property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means reserve land, an
    interest in reserve lands, or a right to occupy, possess or use
    reserve lands, that is subject to taxation under a property taxation
    law. ([bien imposable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    responsible for the administration of a First Nation's property
    taxation laws. ([administrateur fiscal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax roll]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax roll]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a list of persons liable to
    pay tax on taxable property. ([rôle d'imposition]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Applicable Conditions and Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736534 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Enforcement measures set
out in a property taxation law are subject to the conditions, and shall
be carried out in accordance with the procedures, prescribed in sections
3 to 22.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of notices

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Copies of notices referred
to in these Regulations shall be delivered

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the notice is in respect of taxable property, to
  all persons named on the tax roll in respect of that taxable property;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the notice is in respect of personal property, to
  all holders of security interests in the personal property registered
  under the laws of the province in which the personal property is
  located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of interest and penalties

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  property taxation law that provides for the imposition of interest or
  a penalty in respect of unpaid taxes shall include the rate of
  interest and the manner in which the amount of the penalty is
  calculated, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum rate of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rate of interest on unpaid taxes shall not exceed
  15% per year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total penalties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The total of any penalties imposed in respect of
  unpaid taxes shall not exceed 10% of the amount of the unpaid taxes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tax arrears certificate

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  taking any enforcement measures referred to in sections 9 to 21, a tax
  administrator shall issue a tax arrears certificate and deliver it to
  every person named on the tax roll in respect of the property, at the
  address indicated on the tax roll.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A tax arrears certificate shall not be issued for at
  least six months after the day on which the taxes became due.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-29, s. 21
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A tax arrears certificate
shall include a description of the taxable property and set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of unpaid taxes;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any penalty incurred and of any future
  penalty to which the debtor may become liable;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any interest payable to date and the
  rate of any interest payable in the future on the unpaid taxes; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date, if any, by which all amounts owing may be
  paid without incurring further interest and penalties.

- SOR/2016-29, s. 22

## [Liens]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of liens

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The tax administrator shall
maintain a list of all liens created under a property taxation law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of discharge

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  property taxation law that provides for the creation of a lien shall
  set out the manner in which the lien may be discharged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of discharge

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receiving payment of the taxes, interest and
  penalties in respect of which a lien was created, the tax
  administrator shall register a discharge of the lien without delay.

## [Assignment of Taxable Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736571 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of seizure

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a property taxation law
provides for the seizure and assignment of taxable property for unpaid
taxes, interest or penalties, and taxes, interest or penalties remain
unpaid more than nine months after a tax arrears certificate was issued,
the tax administrator may deliver to the debtor a notice describing the
proceedings --- referred to in sections 10 to 16 --- that may be taken
in respect of the taxable property if the amounts owing are not paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of sale

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections 15(1) and
  (3), not less than six months after delivery to the debtor of the
  notice referred to in section 9, a right to an assignment of the
  taxable property may be sold by public tender or auction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of the sale by public tender or auction
  shall be published in the local newspaper with the largest circulation
  at least once in each of the four weeks preceding the date of the
  sale, and shall be posted in a prominent place on the reserve not less
  than 10 days before that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of public tender or auction shall set out
  the upset price and any conditions that are attached to the acceptance
  of a bid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Upset price

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The upset price shall not
be less than the total of the amount of taxes, interest and penalties
payable, calculated to the end of the redemption period described in
subsection 14(1), plus 5% of that total.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase by First Nation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If no bid is equal to or
greater than the upset price, the First Nation is deemed to have
purchased the right to an assignment of the taxable property for the
amount of the upset price.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Minister

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The tax administrator
shall without delay notify the Minister in writing of the sale of a
right to an assignment of taxable property that has been made in
accordance with these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redemption period

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  three months after the holding of a public tender or auction in
  respect of taxable property, the debtor may redeem the taxable
  property by paying to the First Nation the amount of the upset price
  plus 3%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment and notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On redemption of the taxable property

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the right to an assignment was sold to a bidder
    in accordance with section 10, the First Nation shall without delay
    repay to that bidder the amount of the bid; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax administrator shall notify the Minister in
    writing of the redemption.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of assignment

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  assignment of the taxable property shall be made until the end of the
  redemption period described in subsection 14(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of taxable property

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), at the end of the
  redemption period, the First Nation shall assign the taxable property
  to the highest bidder in the public tender or auction held under
  subsection 10(1), or to itself as the deemed purchaser under section
  12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction on assignment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Taxable property shall not be assigned to any person
  or entity that would not have been entitled under the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5) or the [First Nations Land Management
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.8), as the case may be, to obtain the interest or
  right constituting the taxable property.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration of assignment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The tax administrator shall register an assignment of
  any taxable property assigned in accordance with these Regulations in
  every registry in which the taxable property is registered at the time
  of the assignment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** At the end of the
redemption period, the proceeds from the sale of a right to assignment
of taxable property shall be paid to any holders of registered interests
in the property and to the First Nation in order of their priority at
law, and any remaining proceeds shall be paid to the debtor.

## [Seizure and Sale of Personal Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736605 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of seizure

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  property taxation law provides for the seizure and sale of personal
  property to pay for unpaid taxes, interest or penalties, and taxes,
  interest or penalties remain unpaid more than 30 days after a tax
  arrears certificate was delivered to the debtor, the tax administrator
  may deliver to the debtor a notice of seizure and sale in respect of
  any personal property of the debtor that is located on the reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on seizure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal property of a debtor that would be exempt
  from seizure under a writ of execution issued by a superior court of
  the province in which the property is located is exempt from seizure
  under a property taxation law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of seizure and sale of personal property
  shall state that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if, within seven days after delivery of the notice,
    the debtor does not pay the unpaid taxes, interest and penalties,
    the tax administrator may seize the personal property described in
    the notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the debtor does not pay the unpaid taxes,
    interest and penalties and the cost of seizure of the property, the
    tax administrator may sell the seized personal property at any time
    after the publication requirements of section 18 have been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of seizure

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if the taxes, interest and
  penalties remain unpaid more than seven days after delivery of the
  notice of seizure and sale of the personal property, the tax
  administrator may request a sheriff, bailiff or by-law enforcement
  officer to seize any personal property described in the notice that is
  in the possession of the debtor and is located on the reserve.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt for personal property

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person who seizes personal property shall provide
  to the debtor a receipt for the personal property seized.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of sale

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sale
  of personal property shall be by public auction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The tax administrator shall publish a notice of the
  sale by auction of the personal property in two consecutive issues of
  the local newspaper with the largest circulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiting period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The first publication of the notice shall not occur
  until at least 60 days after the personal property was seized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale by auction

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), at any time after the
  second publication of the notice, the seized property may be sold by
  auction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Court challenge

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If at any time before the seized property is sold a
  challenge to the seizure is made to a court of competent jurisdiction,
  the sale shall be postponed until after the court rules on the
  challenge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The application of
sections 17 and 18 in respect of personal property subject to a
registered security interest is subject to any laws regarding the
seizure and sale of such property in the province in which the property
is located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds of sale

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The proceeds from the
sale of seized personal property shall be paid to any holders of
registered security interests in the property and to the First Nation in
order of their priority under the laws applicable in the province in
which the property was seized, and any remaining proceeds shall be paid
to the debtor.

## [Discontinuance of Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736634 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preconditions

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a First Nation may discontinue a service that it provides to the
  taxable property of a debtor if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revenues from a property taxation law are used to
    provide that service to taxpayers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property taxation law provides for the
    discontinuance of services to the taxable property for unpaid taxes,
    interest or penalties; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} taxes, interest or penalties remain unpaid by a
    debtor more than 30 days after a tax arrears certificate was
    delivered to the debtor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Essential services

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A First Nation shall not discontinue

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fire protection or police services to the taxable
    property of a debtor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} water or garbage collection services to taxable
    property that is a residential dwelling; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} electrical or natural gas services to taxable
    property that is a residential dwelling during the period from
    November 1 in any year to March 31 in the following year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}30-day notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At least 30 days before discontinuing any service,
  the tax administrator shall deliver to the debtor a notice of
  discontinuance specifying each service provided to the taxable
  property that is to be discontinued and the projected date of
  discontinuance.

## [Delivery of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-736648 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of delivery

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document may be made
  personally or by sending it by registered mail.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal delivery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Personal delivery of a document is made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, by leaving the
    document with that individual or with a person at least 18 years of
    age residing at that individual's place of residence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a First Nation, by leaving the
    document with the individual apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of the main administrative office of the First Nation, or
    with the first nation's legal counsel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by leaving the
    document with the individual apparently in charge, at the time of
    delivery, of its head office or one of its branch offices, or with
    an officer or director of the corporation or the corporation's legal
    counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of delivery

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document shall be considered to have been delivered

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if delivered personally, on the day that personal
    delivery is made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if sent by registered mail, on the fifth day after
    it is mailed.
