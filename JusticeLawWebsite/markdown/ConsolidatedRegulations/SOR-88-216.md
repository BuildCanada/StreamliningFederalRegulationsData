## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911379 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following substances
are designated as oil products:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} methanol; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} methyl tertiary butyl ether.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911383 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Methanol and methyl
tertiary butyl ether are exempt from the operation of Part 9 of the
[Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1).
