Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) the
Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act

P.C. 2024-1183 2024-11-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725647994){#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725647994-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725648015){#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1725648015-ID0EEDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
to the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
under section 7 of the [Emergency Management
Act](/eng/acts/E-4.56)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1726161363){#fn_81000-3-2768_hq_1726161363-ID0EBDAA}.
