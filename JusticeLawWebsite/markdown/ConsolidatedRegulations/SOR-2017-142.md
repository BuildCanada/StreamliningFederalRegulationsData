## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840922 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means qualifying as originating in
the territory of a Party under the rules of origin set out in Chapter
Three (Rules of origin and origin procedures) of the Canada--Ukraine
Free Trade Agreement.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840924 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
originating goods exported from Ukraine are entitled to the benefit of
the Ukraine Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada without shipment
  through another country either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, when requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada through another
  country and the importer provides, when requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
    that country.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840932 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndC5AE_hq_20406){#fn_IndC5AE_hq_20406-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 36 of the [Canada--Ukraine Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.54), chapter 8 of the Statutes of Canada, 2017,
comes into force, but if they are registered after that day, they come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
