## Edmonton Garrison Heliport Zoning Regulations {#edmonton-garrison-heliport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-86
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 2004-04-22

Edmonton Garrison Heliport Zoning Regulations

P.C. 2004-431 2004-04-22
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 5.5(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHBCA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed [Edmonton
Garrison Heliport Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-86),
substantially in the annexed form, was published in two successive
issues of a newspaper serving the area to which the proposed Regulations
relate, namely on March 28 and 29, 2002, and in two successive issues of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on March 30 and
April 6, 2002, and a reasonable opportunity was afforded to interested
persons to make representations to the Minister of National Defence with
respect to the proposed Regulations;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EHBCA){#footnotea_e}R.S., c. 33
  (1st Supp.), s. 1
  :::

And whereas the proposed Regulations would prevent lands adjacent to or
in the vicinity of the Edmonton Garrison Heliport from being used or
developed in a manner that is, in the opinion of the Minister of
National Defence, incompatible with the safe operation of an airport or
aircraft;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to
paragraph 5.4(2)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFAAA} of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), hereby makes the annexed [Edmonton
Garrison Heliport Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-86).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714190 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
    inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the heliport
    boundary or from each end of a strip and are described in Part 3 of
    the Schedule. ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bird hazard area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bird hazard area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area adjacent to
    or in the vicinity of the heliport the outer limits of which are
    described in Part 5 of the schedule. ([zone de péril
    aviaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heliport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heliport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Edmonton Garrison
    Heliport, in the Municipal District of Sturgeon, in the Province of
    Alberta. ([héliport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heliport zoning reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heliport zoning reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    point that is 677 m above sea level and is described in Part 1 of
    the schedule. ([point de repère du zonage de
    l'héliport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Public Works
    and Government Services Plan, Edmonton Garrison Heliport,
    No. E.3104, dated December 31, 2001. ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the heliport, including the runway, that is prepared
    for the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction
    and is described in Part 2 of the schedule.
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714198 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of all lands, other than lands that form part of the heliport
but including public road allowances, that are adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the heliport, the outer limits of which lands are described
in Part 4 of the schedule.

## [Building Restriction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714200 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place,
erect or construct, or permit to be placed, erected or constructed, on
any lands in respect of which these Regulations apply any building,
structure or object, or an addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which would exceed an approach surface
in elevation at the location of the building, structure or object.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714202 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
lands in respect of which these Regulations apply shall permit an object
of natural growth on the lands to exceed in elevation an approach
surface that projects immediately over and above the surface of the
lands at the location of the object.

## [Electronic Communications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714204 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
lands in respect of which these Regulations apply shall permit the lands
or any part of them to be used in a manner that may cause interference
with aeronautical communications.

## [Bird Hazards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714206 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in order to reduce bird hazards to aviation, no
  owner or lessee of any lands in the bird hazard area shall permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands or any part of them, other than any lands
    described in Division 1 of Part 6 of the schedule, to be used as a
    site for sanitary land fill;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands or any part of them to be used as a site
    for food garbage disposal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lands or any part of them, other than lands
    described in Division 2 of Part 6 of the schedule, to be used as a
    site for a sewage lagoon; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the lands or any part of them, other than lands
    described in Divisions 3 and 4 of Part 6 of the schedule, to be used
    as a site for an open water reservoir.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner or lessee of lands in the bird hazard area,
  other than lands described in Divisions 3 and 4 of Part 6 of the
  schedule, may permit the lands or any part of them to be used as a
  site for an open water reservoir if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands are used as an open water reservoir for a
    period of 48 hours or less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands are used as an open water reservoir for a
    period of more than 48 hours, the owner or lessee ensures that all
    reasonable measures are taken in the design, construction and use of
    the reservoir, as well as in all other respects, to minimize bird
    hazards to aviation and the design and construction of the reservoir
    and the manner in which it is to be used as an open water reservoir
    have been approved as minimizing bird hazards to aviation by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the National Defence Airport and Heliport Zoning
      Manager, where the total surface area of the reservoir is greater
      than 2.5 ha but less than or equal to 6 ha, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, where the total surface area of
      the reservoir is greater than 6 ha; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the open water reservoir consists of dry ponds, the
    owner or lessee ensures that all reasonable measures are taken to
    minimize bird hazards to aviation and, where the lands are used as
    an open water reservoir for a period of more than 48 hours more than
    once per calendar year, the measures have been approved by the
    Minister as minimizing bird hazards to aviation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
lands described in Part 7 of the schedule shall modify or improve the
lands or any part of them in a manner that increases their
attractiveness to birds.

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Heliport Zoning Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1description-of-the-heliport-zoning-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The heliport zoning reference point, shown on Sheet No. 54 of the Plan,
is a point located at the intersection of the centre line of
runway 11-29 with the centre line of runway 02-20.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2description-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips located within the heliport and shown on the Plan are
described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 02-20 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  2,192.8 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 11-29 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  4,387.3 m in length.

### [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-3description-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces associated with the heliport and shown on the Plan
are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an imaginary inclined plane extending upward and
  outward from the intersection of imaginary vertical planes passing
  through the heliport boundary, with imaginary horizontal planes lying
  2 m above the general ground level of the heliport boundary at any
  point thereon, to a maximum elevation of 792 m above sea level at the
  outer limit of the approach surface, being 115 m, more or less, above
  the assigned elevation of the heliport zoning reference point, such
  outer limit being a circle of radius 5,000 m centred on the heliport
  zoning reference point;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 11 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  intersection with the outer limit of the approach surface described in
  paragraph (a), thence said approach surface slopes upward at the ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15,000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2,400 m from the projected centre line, said imaginary
  horizontal line being 379.4 m measured vertically above the assigned
  elevation at the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 29 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  intersection with the outer limit of the approach surface described in
  paragraph (a), thence said approach surface slopes upward at the ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15,000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2,400 m from the projected centre line, said imaginary
  horizontal line being 389 m measured vertically above the assigned
  elevation at the end of the strip;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 02 consisting of an inclined plane, having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising
  to an intersection with the outer limit of the approach surface
  described in paragraph (a), thence said approach surface slopes upward
  at the ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally,
  rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the
  projected centre line of the strip and distant 15,000 m measured
  horizontally from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the
  imaginary horizontal line being 2,400 m from the projected centre
  line, said imaginary horizontal line being 403.3 m measured vertically
  above the assigned elevation at the end of the strip; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 20 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  intersection with the outer limit of the approach surface described in
  paragraph (a), thence said approach surface slopes upward at the ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15,000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2,400 m from the projected centre line, said imaginary
  horizontal line being 430.9 m measured vertically, above the assigned
  elevation at the end of the strip.
