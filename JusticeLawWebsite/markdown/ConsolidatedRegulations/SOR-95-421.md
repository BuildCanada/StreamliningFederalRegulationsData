## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975873 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Nordion and Theratronics Employees Pension Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-421).

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975875 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the
regulations made under that Act apply to a person who has made an
election under paragraph 9(1)(d) of the [Nordion and Theratronics
Divestiture Authorization Act](/eng/acts/N-23.7) before May 1, 1998.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-309, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 12(4), (6) and
(7) and 13(2) and (3) of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36) do not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surviving spouse of an employee of Nordion or
  Theratronics, as the case may be, who became a spouse of that employee
  on or after the date on which shares of Nordion or Theratronics, as
  the case may be, were first sold or otherwise disposed of, unless the
  employee had again become a contributor under the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a child of an employee of Nordion or Theratronics, as
  the case may be, who was conceived by, adopted by, or became the
  stepchild of, that employee on or after the date on which shares of
  Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be, were first sold or
  otherwise disposed of, unless the employee had again become a
  contributor under the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

## [Adapted Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975882 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
9 of the [Nordion and Theratronics Divestiture Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/N-23.7),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 10(9)(b)(i) and (ii) of the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the year in which a
  person ceased to be employed in the Public Service is the year in
  which the person ceased to be employed by Nordion or Theratronics, as
  the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subsections 12(1) to (3) and (6) to (8) and
  section 13 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of years of pensionable service and the
    number of years of service of an employee of Nordion or
    Theratronics, as the case may be, includes any period of service
    with Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be, during the period
    beginning on the day following the date on which shares of Nordion
    or Theratronics, as the case may be, are first sold or otherwise
    disposed of and ending on the day preceding the day on which the
    person ceases to be employed by Nordion or Theratronics, as the case
    may be, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a person ceases to be employed in
    the Public Service is the day on which that person ceases to be
    employed by Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 69(3) of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the retirement year or retirement
  month of a person who is an employee of Nordion or Theratronics, as
  the case may be, is the year or month, as the case may be, in which
  the shares of Nordion or Theratronics, as the case may be, are first
  sold or otherwise disposed of.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An option under section 12
or 13 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)
exercised by an employee of Nordion shall be deemed to be null and void
and the benefits to which that employee is entitled under that Act and
the regulations made pursuant to that Act shall be determined as if
sections 2 to 4 of these Regulations applied, with such modifications as
the circumstances require, to that employee on the day on which that
employee made that option, where the employee is a person who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the criteria set out in paragraphs 9(1)(a)
  and (d) of the [Nordion and Theratronics Divestiture Authorization
  Act](/eng/acts/N-23.7);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ceased to be employed by Nordion and exercised that
  option on or after so ceasing to be employed but prior to the date on
  which these Regulations are published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in exercising that option, acted upon erroneous or
  misleading advice received from a person employed in the Public
  Service whose ordinary duties included the giving of advice respecting
  an option under the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), which information led the employee to believe
  that the exercising of that option would not disentitle the employee
  from making an election to have the terms of these Regulations apply
  in the determination of the benefits to which the employee would
  become entitled under that Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975893 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations shall be
deemed to have come into force on November 9, 1991.
