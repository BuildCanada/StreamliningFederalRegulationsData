## Regional Advisory Committee Regulations {#regional-advisory-committee-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1593
:::

[VETERANS' LAND ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.5)

Regulations Respecting Regional Advisory Committees to Advise the
Director, The Veterans' Land Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530536 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Regional Advisory Committee
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1593).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530538 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Veterans' Land
    Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a regional advisory
    committee appointed pursuant to section 44 of the Act;
    ([comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Director, The Veterans'
    Land Act. ([Directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Regional Advisory Committee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530543 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A regional advisory
committee shall consist of three members.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Chairman of a Committee
shall be an officer of the Director appointed by the Governor in Council
to be Chairman of the Committee.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A Chairman of a Committee
may, with the approval of the Director, act as the Chairman of any other
Committee in the event that the Chairman of that Committee is absent or
unable to act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Chairman or acting
chairman and one other member of a Committee constitute a quorum of the
Committee.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairman or acting chairman of a Committee shall receive no salary or
  remuneration other than that which he receives as an employee of the
  Director.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each member of a Committee, other than the Chairman,
  shall hold office during pleasure and shall be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$10 for each half day or portion thereof during
    which the Committee meets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$10 for each half day or portion thereof that the
    member necessarily spends in travel to and from Committee meetings;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the actual and reasonable disbursements necessarily
    made by him in the discharge of his duties as a member of the
    Committee.
