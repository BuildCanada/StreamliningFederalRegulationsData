## Delegation of Powers (VLA) Regulations {#delegation-of-powers-vla-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1588
:::

[VETERANS' LAND ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.5)

Regulations Delegating Powers Conferred by Parts I and II of the
Veterans' Land Act upon the Director, the Veterans' Land Act
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530492 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Delegation of Powers (VLA)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1588).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-530494 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The persons named in the
schedule may sign the name and affix or cause to be affixed the seal of
The Director, The Veterans' Land Act, to all documents that, under Parts
I and II of the [Veterans' Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5), require execution
by the Director in his corporate capacity.
