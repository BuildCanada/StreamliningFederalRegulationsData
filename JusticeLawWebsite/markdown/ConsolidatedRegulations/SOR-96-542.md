## Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Deposit Insurance Information By-law {#canada-deposit-insurance-corporation-deposit-insurance-information-by-law .Title-of-Act}

[CANADA DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-3)

Registration 1996-12-05

Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Deposit Insurance Information
By-law
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation,
pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(f)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFACA} of the [Canada
Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), hereby makes the
annexed Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Deposit Insurance
Information By-law.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002496 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this By-law.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CDIC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CDIC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation. ([SADC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic business site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic business site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a website or
    other electronic site that is used by a member institution to carry
    on business and at which a person may make a deposit or commence a
    transaction to open a deposit account with or without the assistance
    of a representative of the member institution, and, for greater
    certainty, includes an automated teller machine. ([site d'affaires
    électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[place of business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[place of business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
    in Canada where a member institution carries on business, where a
    person may make a deposit or commence a transaction to open a
    deposit account with the assistance of a representative of the
    member institution and where the majority of the deposit-taking
    activities relate to deposits eligible to be insured by the
    Corporation, but does not include an automated teller
    machine. ([lieu d'affaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2017-215, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[Return of Insured Deposits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Return of Insured Deposits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the form of
    return of insured deposits established by the Corporation in
    accordance with subsection 22(1) of the Act. ([Déclaration des
    dépôts assurés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002506 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** No person shall make any
false, misleading or deceptive representation with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} what constitutes, or does not constitute, a deposit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} what constitutes, or does not constitute, a deposit
  that is insured by the Corporation; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} who is a member institution.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-334, s. 2

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  member institution makes representations with respect to any of the
  matters referred to in paragraphs 2(a) to (c), the representations
  shall be made in accordance with sections 4 to 11.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member institution whose policy of deposit
  insurance is terminated or cancelled shall immediately remove from
  each of its places of business, each of its electronic business sites
  and each of its other websites and electronic sites all references to
  its status as a member institution and to the deposit insurance
  provided by the Corporation, including displays of the membership
  sign, brochure and badge.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a member institution ceases to use a location as
  a place of business, it shall remove from the location all references
  to its status as a member institution and to the deposit insurance
  provided by the Corporation, including displays of the membership
  sign, brochure and badge.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a member institution is required to display or
  elects to display a membership sign or badge in digital form, changes
  may be made to its overall size, but only if the proportions of the
  sign or badge, as supplied by the Corporation, are maintained and the
  content is clearly visible and legible.

## [Representations About Membership]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002521 .Part}

### [Representations in Advertisements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002522 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  member institution makes a representation about its status as a member
  institution in its advertising, other than by displaying the
  membership sign or badge, it shall use one of the following
  representations, or a representation in substantially the same words
  as any of the following representations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "Member of the Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation" (or "A Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation member");

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Member of the CDIC" (or "A CDIC member");

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "(name of the member institution) is a member of
    the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation"; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} "(name of the member institution) is a member of
    the CDIC".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The representations may appear anywhere in the
  advertising of a member institution, if the location at which and the
  manner in which they are displayed do not give the impression that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is a member institution when they are not
    a member institution; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deposit is insured by the Corporation when it is
    not eligible to be insured by the Corporation.

## [Representations Concerning Deposits and Deposit Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002546 .Part}

### [Display and Provision of Brochure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002547 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  institution shall prominently display a brochure in physical form,
  supplied by the Corporation for that purpose, at each of its places of
  business and shall also make copies of the brochure available there to
  depositors and all other persons.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member institution shall, as part of the account
  opening process for a deposit that is eligible to be insured by the
  Corporation, provide a depositor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if all account opening documents are provided only
    in digital form, with the digital form of a brochure supplied by the
    Corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if all account opening documents are provided only
    in physical form, with a printed version of the digital form of the
    brochure; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, with either the digital form of
    the brochure or a printed version of that digital form.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The physical and digital form of the brochures shall
  contain the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} general information about the Corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contact information for the Corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation's logo or other identifiers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information as to what constitutes a deposit that
    is eligible to be insured by the Corporation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the maximum amount of deposit insurance coverage
    provided by the Corporation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information as to what a depositor needs to know
    when the Corporation is obliged to make an insurance payment.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a member institution, or any
  other person, may make representations about the subject matter of the
  brochure if the representation is not false, misleading or deceptive.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A member institution shall ensure that the location
  at which and the way in which the brochure is displayed does not give
  the impression that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person is a member institution when they are not
    a member institution; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deposit is insured by the Corporation when it is
    not eligible to be insured by the Corporation.

## [Federal Credit Unions: Statement of Transitional Coverage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002584 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  federal credit union shall prepare a statement of transitional
  coverage that contains the following information which has been
  verified by the Corporation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the statement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the deposit insurance coverage
    provided by the Corporation that applies to the pre-existing
    deposits of the federal credit union during the transition period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the deposit insurance coverage
    referred to in paragraph (b) expires; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the deposit insurance coverage
    provided by the Corporation that will apply to the deposits of the
    federal credit union after the transition period expires.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A federal credit union shall, beginning on the day on
  which it becomes a member institution and ending on the day on which
  the transition period expires,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the statement of transitional coverage
    available by way of a hyperlink on every page of its website that
    refers to deposit products; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a copy of the statement available to
    depositors at each of its places of business.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A federal credit union shall, for a period of 180
  days after the day on which it becomes a member institution,
  prominently display, at each of its places of business, a sign that
  refers depositors and all other persons to the statement of
  transitional coverage and indicates how they can obtain a copy of that
  statement.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), a federal credit union
  shall, not earlier than 12 weeks but not later than 4 weeks before the
  end of the transition period, provide to a depositor in respect of any
  pre-existing deposit that exceeds \$100,000 and is to be repaid on a
  fixed day, a copy of the statement of transitional coverage or an
  indication of how they can obtain a copy of that statement.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A federal credit union that has provided to a
  depositor a copy of the statement of transitional coverage in respect
  of a pre-existing deposit, or given to a depositor an indication of
  how that copy can be obtained, is not required to provide to the same
  depositor in respect of any other pre-existing deposit a copy of the
  statement or an indication of how the copy can be obtained.

## [Warning Statements for Ineligible Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002599 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if a member institution issues an instrument to a
  person evidencing that the member institution has received or is
  holding money that constitutes a deposit that is not eligible to be
  insured by the Corporation, the member institution shall include on
  the face of the instrument a warning statement in substantially the
  same words as one of the following statements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "Not insured by the CDIC";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Not insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation"; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "The deposit evidenced by this instrument does not
    constitute a deposit that is insured under the [Canada Deposit
    Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3)".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a member institution issues an instrument to a
  person evidencing that the member institution has received or is
  holding money that constitutes both a deposit that is eligible to be
  insured by the Corporation and a deposit that is not eligible to be
  insured by the Corporation, the member institution shall include on
  the face of the instrument, in the location specified, a warning
  statement in substantially the same words as one of the following
  statements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-186, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "The following deposits evidenced by this
    instrument do not constitute deposits that are insured under the
    [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3):" before
    the list of deposits that are not eligible to be insured by the
    Corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Not insured by the CDIC" beside the reference to
    each deposit that is not eligible to be insured by the Corporation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} "Not insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation" beside the reference to each deposit that is not
    eligible to be insured by the Corporation; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} "Not insured by the CDIC" in a footnote the
    reference mark for which is located beside the reference to each
    deposit that is not eligible to be insured by the Corporation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a person communicates by telephone with a member
  institution to make a deposit or commence a transaction to open a
  deposit account referred to in subsection (1) or (2), the member
  institution shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make an oral representation in substantially the
    same words as one of the statements set out in subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue to the person in writing, in paper or
    electronic form, a notice that includes one of the statements set
    out in subsection (2).

## [Lists of Trade Names and Financial Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002625 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A member institution
shall not, in the course of its deposit-taking activities relating to
deposits eligible to be insured by the Corporation, use one of its trade
names in its representations about who is a member institution, what
constitutes a deposit or what constitutes a deposit that is insured by
the Corporation, unless the member institution

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepares and maintains an up-to-date list of those
  trade names;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes the list available on its website in a location
  that a depositor would be reasonably expected to look for information
  about the member institution's trade names;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides the Corporation with a copy of the list with
  its Return of Insured Deposits;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides the Corporation with not less than 15 days'
  notice of any new trade name to be used in the course of its
  deposit-taking activities relating to deposits eligible to be insured
  by the Corporation and the date of the intended first public use of
  that trade name by the member institution; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} permits the Corporation to make the contents of the
  list, and any updates to the list, available to the public.
