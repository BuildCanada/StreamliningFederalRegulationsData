## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605424 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[personalized application form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  an application form on which the Registrar of Firearms sets out the
  applicant's name, a return date and a bar code.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission of the fee for a registration certificate
  under subsection 10(3) of the [Firearms Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204) is hereby granted to any
  individual who pays the fee before receiving in the mail a
  personalized application form for the registration certificate from
  the Registrar of Firearms.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605428 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that the fee is paid during the period beginning on
October 1, 1998 and ending on June 30, 2002.
