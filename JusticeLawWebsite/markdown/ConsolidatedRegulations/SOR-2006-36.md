## Transfer of the Crown Corporation Secretariat Regulations (Royal Canadian Mint and Canada Post Corporation) {#transfer-of-the-crown-corporation-secretariat-regulations-royal-canadian-mint-and-canada-post-corporation .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2006-36
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2006-02-06

Transfer of the Crown Corporation Secretariat Regulations (Royal
Canadian Mint and Canada Post Corporation)

P.C. 2006-68 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Transfer of the Crown Corporation Secretariat Regulations
(Royal Canadian Mint and Canada Post
Corporation)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-36).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, ss. 12, 13
  :::
:::

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727464 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 132(1) and (2)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBA} apply to all
persons employed or engaged in the portion of the federal public
administration within the Canada Revenue Agency known as the Crown
Corporation Secretariat, relating to the Royal Canadian Mint and the
Canada Post Corporation.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBA){#footnote1_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, ss. 12, 13
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-727466 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on February 6, 2006.
