## Nova Scotia Share of Offshore Revenue Interim Period Payment Regulations {#nova-scotia-share-of-offshore-revenue-interim-period-payment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-848
:::

[CANADA-NOVA SCOTIA OFFSHORE PETROLEUM RESOURCES ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-7.8)

Registration 1984-11-01

Regulations Respecting the Interim Payment to Her Majesty in Right of
Nova Scotia of the Nova Scotia Share of Offshore Revenue Under Part III
of the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and Gas Agreement Act

P.C. 1984-3486 1984-11-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 70(2) of the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and
Gas Agreement Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDACA}, a copy of
the proposed [Nova Scotia Share of Offshore Revenue Interim Period
Payment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-848) , in the form set out
in the schedule hereto, was furnished to the Minister of Finance for
Nova Scotia and to the Minister of Mines and Energy for Nova Scotia on
October 18, 1984;

And Whereas the Minister of Finance for Nova Scotia and the Minister of
Mines and Energy for Nova Scotia have agreed to waive the thirty day
requirement as provided for in subsection 58(3), as that subsection
applies by virtue of subsection 70(2) of the said Act.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the joint
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Minister of Energy,
Mines and Resources, pursuant to subparagraph 70(1)(a)(i) of the
Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and Gas Agreement Act and on the recommendation
of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources pursuant to subparagraph
70(1)(b)(ii) of that Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations respecting the Interim Payment to Her Majesty in Right of
Nova Scotia of the Nova Scotia Share of Offshore Revenue Under Part III
of the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and Gas Agreement Act.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884739 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Nova Scotia Share of Offshore Revenue Interim Period
Payment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-848).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884741 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada-Nova Scotia Oil and Gas
    Agreement Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Energy, Mines
    and Resources. ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Transfer to Revenue Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884745 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where pursuant to
subsection 63(6) of the Act the Minister of Finance has estimated the
amount referred to in paragraph (e) of the definition [[offshore
revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 63(1) of the Act in respect
of a fiscal year during the term of the Agreement before Part VI of the
Act came into force, the Minister shall transfer such amount to the
Revenue Fund.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where pursuant to
subsection 63(6) of the Act the Minister of Finance has estimated the
amount referred to in paragraph (f) of the definition [[offshore
revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}in subsection 63(1) of the Act in respect
of a fiscal year during the term of the Agreement before Part II of the
Act came into force, the Minister shall transfer such amount to the
Revenue Fund.

## [Payment to Nova Scotia]{.HTitleText1} {#h-884748 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where any amount has been
transferred to the Revenue Fund pursuant to section 3 or 4, the Minister
shall forthwith pay such amount to Her Majesty in right of Nova Scotia.
