## Exemption List Regulations {#exemption-list-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-13
:::

[MACKENZIE VALLEY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/M-0.2)

Registration 1998-12-16

Exemption List Regulations

P.C. 1998-2265  1998-12-16
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 143(1) of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDACA}, the Minister
of Indian Affairs and Northern Development has consulted with the
Minister of Resources, Wildlife and Economic Development of the
Northwest Territories and with the first nations with regard to the
annexed [Exemption List Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-13);

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to paragraph 143(1)(c) of the [Mackenzie Valley
Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDABA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Exemption List Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-13).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027673 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).
    ([produits antiparasitaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dugout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dugout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an excavation designed to hold
    water for the maintenance of public roads or consumption by
    livestock. ([étang-réservoir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[footprint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[footprint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of land occupied by
    a structure at ground level. ([superficie au sol]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[hazardous material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a toxic substance
    within the meaning of section 64 of the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([matières
    dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heritage building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heritage building]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a building that has
    been designated as a heritage building by a regulatory authority.
    ([bâtiment du patrimoine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hook-up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hook-up]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure or line that
    connects a building to a main gas, sewer, water, electrical
    transmission or telecommunication line.
    ([raccordement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international electrical transmission line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international electrical transmission line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an electrical transmission line constructed or operated for
    the purpose of transmitting electricity from a place in Canada to a
    place outside Canada or to a place in Canada from a place outside
    Canada. ([ligne internationale de transport
    d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[irrigation structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[irrigation structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a buried
    pipeline, a pipe, a pump, a pump house, a reservoir, a drain or a
    canal lined with asphalt, wood, concrete or other material, that is
    used for irrigating agricultural land. ([structure
    d'irrigation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[limited expansion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[limited expansion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an increase of 10
    per cent or less in the exterior dimensions or production capacity
    of a structure. ([agrandissement de faible
    envergure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[modification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[modification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a structure,
    means a change, other than an expansion, that does not alter the
    purpose or function of the structure.
    ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national historic site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place that is
    marked or commemorated under paragraph 3(a) of the [Historic Sites
    and Monuments Act](/eng/acts/H-4) and land set apart as a national
    historic site under subsection 42(1) of the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([lieu historique
    national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a park described in
    Schedule 1 to the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) and
    any other park established under a federal-territorial agreement.
    ([parc national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[national park reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[national park reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reserve
    described in Schedule 2 to the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) and any other reserve established under a
    federal-territorial agreement. ([réserve foncière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Northwest Territories territorial park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Northwest Territories territorial park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
    the meaning assigned by the definition [[Territorial
    Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 1 of the Territorial Parks
    Act, R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. T-4. ([parc territorial des Territoires du
    Nord-Ouest]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil and gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil and gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pipeline that
    is used, or is intended to be used, for the transmission of
    hydrocarbons alone or with any other commodity. ([pipeline
    d'hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inland water body in a
    liquid or frozen state, including the portion of a reservoir and a
    wetland below its high-water mark, but does not include a sewage or
    waste treatment lagoon, dugout or mine tailings pond. ([plan
    d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027692 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Proposed or existing
developments set out in Schedule 1 that are situated outside a national
park, national park reserve or national historic site are developments
for which preliminary screenings are not required by reason that their
impact on the environment of the Mackenzie Valley is insignificant.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Proposed or existing
developments set out in Schedule 2 that are situated in a national park,
national park reserve, national historic site or Northwest Territories
territorial park are developments for which preliminary screenings are
not required by reason that their impact on the environment of the
Mackenzie Valley is insignificant.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027695 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which Part 5 of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) comes into force.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Exempted Developments Not Situated in National Parks, National Park Reserves or National Historic Sites]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1027698 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1general .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The operation or maintenance of, or repair to, a structure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) or the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18.

- **2** A development, or part of a development, for which renewal of a
  permit, licence or authorization is required and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been modified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has fulfilled the requirements of any environmental
    assessment process established by the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
    Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) or any other federal act.

- **2.1** A development, or part of a development, for which a permit,
  licence or authorization is required and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was part of a development that fulfilled the
    requirements of the environmental assessment process established by
    the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not been modified since the development
    referred to in paragraph (a) fulfilled the requirements of the
    environmental assessment process established by the [Mackenzie
    Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2).

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The construction or installation of a
    building with a footprint of less than 100 m^2^ and a height of less
    than 5 m on land outside the boundaries of a local government that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will be carried out at a distance greater than 30
      m from a water body; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
      body.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The construction or installation of a building with
    a footprint of less than 1000 m^2^ on land within the boundaries of
    a local government that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will be carried out at a distance greater than 30
      m from a water body; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
      body.

- **4** The modification or limited expansion of a building, or anything
  affixed thereto, that will not entail the deposit of waste into a
  water body.

- **5** The construction, installation or modification of an
  environmental scientific data collection instrument, or its housing or
  enclosure, with a combined footprint of less than 100 m^2^, that will
  not entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **6** The construction, repair or maintenance of a sidewalk, boardwalk
  or parking lot with a capacity of 30 or fewer automobiles that will
  not entail the deposit of waste into a water body, where the
  construction, repair or maintenance will be carried out at a distance
  greater than 30 m from a water body.

- **7** The construction of a fence where the construction will not
  entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **8** The construction, installation, limited expansion or
  modification of a hydrant or hook-up, where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hydrant or hook-up is or will be part of a farm
    or municipal system of distribution; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the construction, installation, limited expansion
    or modification will not involve the crossing of a water body, other
    than an aerial crossing by a telecommunication or electrical
    transmission line.

- **9** The construction, installation, limited expansion or
  modification of a sign, no surface of which has or will have an area
  of more than 25 m^2^.

- **10** The construction, installation or modification of a
  radio-communication antenna and its supporting structure, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will be carried out at a distance greater than 30 m
    from a water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is affixed to a building,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is situated at a distance of less than 15 m from
      a building, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has, together with any supporting lines, a
      combined footprint of not more than 25 m^2^; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a structure referred to in
    subparagraph (c)(iii), does not require a land use permit under the
    [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **11** The construction, installation, limited expansion or
  modification of a geodesic benchmark with a footprint of less than 25
  m^2^ that will not entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **12** The construction, operation, modification or removal of a
  temporary field camp that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act , S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **13** The operation and maintenance of a highway, as defined in the
  Northwest Territories Motor Vehicles Act, R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. M-16, or
  its associated culverts, that is carried out on the highway
  right-of-way and does not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lengthen the highway;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} widen the highway by more than 15 per cent; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **14** The demolition of a building with a floor area of less than
  1000 m^2^ that will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entail the deposit of waste into a water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be carried out within 30 m of another building; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} involve the handling of hazardous materials.

- **15** Engineering tests undertaken to define the elements of a
  development, or alternatives necessary to determine the environmental
  impact of a development, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} do not require a land use permit or a water licence
    pursuant to the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **16** Research, scientific studies or surveys that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not include the capture of wildlife including
    fish, mammals and birds; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} do not require a land use permit or water licence
    under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **17** The cutting of not more than 5000 m^3^ of timber burned by a
  forest fire, or not more than 1100 m^3^ of any other timber, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will be carried out at a distance greater than 30 m
    from a water body;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **18** The setting of a fire, with a footprint of not more than 25
  m^2^, to clear land or remove flammable material during the closed
  season under a permit issued under subsection 11(1) of the Northwest
  Territories Forest Protection Act, R.S.N.W.T. 1988, c. F-10.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Agriculture]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2agriculture .SchedHeadL1}

- **19** The modification of an irrigation structure that will not
  entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **20** The construction, limited expansion or modification of a
  domestic or farm water supply well, pump house, water-tank loading
  facility or dugout on agricultural land, that will not entail the
  deposit of waste into a water body.

- **21** The construction, limited expansion or modification of a centre
  pivot or side roll sprinkler on agricultural land that will not entail
  the deposit of waste into a water body.

### [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Defence]{.HTitleText1} {#part-3defence .SchedHeadL1}

- **22** Military field exercises and field training, involving fewer
  than 275 persons and fewer than 40 vehicles, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

### [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Electrical Energy]{.HTitleText1} {#part-4electrical-energy .SchedHeadL1}

- **23** The construction or installation of an electrical transmission
  line, other than an international electrical transmission line, with
  an operating design voltage of not more than 130 kV, where the
  construction or installation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not be carried out beyond an existing
    right-of-way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} will not involve the placement in or on a water
    body of the supporting structures; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **24** The limited expansion or modification of a telecommunication or
  electrical transmission line, other than an international electrical
  transmission line, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not lengthen the line by more than 10 per
    cent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not be carried out beyond an existing
    right-of-way;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} will not involve the placement in or on a water
    body of supporting structures; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **25** The construction or installation of a switching station that is
  associated with a telecommunication or electrical transmission line
  with an operating design voltage of not more than 130 kV, other than a
  international transmission line, where the construction or
  installation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not be carried out beyond an existing
    right-of-way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not be carried out within a distance of 30 m
    of a water body;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **26** The modification or limited expansion of a switching station
  associated with a telecommunication or electrical transmission line,
  where the modification or limited expansion

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not be carried out beyond an existing
    right-of-way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

### [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Aquatic]{.HTitleText1} {#part-5aquatic .SchedHeadL1}

- **27** The modification or demolition of a structure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} located on land,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} associated with fishing or the use of small
    pleasure craft, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with a footprint of less than 100 m^2^ and a height
    of less than 5 m,

  that will not entail the deposit of waste into a water body or involve
  the handling of hazardous materials.

- **28** The modification of a fish habitat improvement structure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not require heavy machinery; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit or a water
    licence under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **29** The modification of a wharf, other than a floating wharf, or of
  a breakwater that is accessible by land, where the modification will
  not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be carried out below the high-water mark of the
    wharf or breakwater;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} involve dredging; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **30** The re-installation or modification of a floating wharf that
  will not entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **31** The demolition of a wharf that will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} involve the use of explosives; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

### [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#part-6transportation .SchedHeadL1}

- **32** The modification or limited expansion of a paved or gravel area
  within the boundary of an aerodrome, as defined in subsection 3(1) of
  the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not require a land use permit under the
    [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2) or the
    [Territorial Land Use
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **33** The modification of aircraft visual or navigation aids.

- **34** The construction, installation, limited expansion or
  modification of an automatic warning structure at a railway level
  crossing.

- **35** The construction, installation, limited expansion or
  modification of a railway traffic control signal structure on an
  existing railway right-of-way.

- **36** The modification of the portion of an oil and gas pipeline,
  sewer or drain that crosses under a railway or road and is within the
  railway or road right-of-way.

- **37** The operation, maintenance or modification of the portion of a
  culvert that crosses under a railway or road and is within the railway
  or road right-of-way, where the operation, maintenance or modification
  does not require a land use permit or a water licence under the
  [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the
  Waters Act, S.N.W.T. 2014, c. 18 or the [Territorial Land Use
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1524).

- **38** The modification, other than a deviation, of a railway track or
  bed that will not entail the deposit of waste into a water body.

- **39** The modification of a road crossing, as defined in subsection
  4(1) of the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2), that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will be carried out on the railway right-of-way;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} will not entail the deposit of waste into a water
    body.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 3)]{.OriginatingRef}[Exempted Developments Situated in National Parks, National Park Reserves, National Historic Sites and Northwest Territories Territorial Parks]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1027704 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The modification, maintenance or repair of a physical work other
  than a road, including internal fixed physical works and scientific
  data collection instruments, that will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} increase the footprint or height of the physical
    work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} involve a heritage building;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} involve a change in the method of sewage disposal,
    or an increase in the amount of sewage, waste or emissions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} involve any excavation beyond the footprint of the
    physical work;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} create a need for additional related facilities,
    such as parking spaces; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} entail the release of waste into the environment.

- **2** [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-47, s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

- **3** [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-47, s. 13\]]{.Repealed}

- **4** The maintenance or repair of a road, including a pull-off area,
  that will be carried out on the road right-of-way and that will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entail the deposit of waste into a water body; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} involve the application of a dust control product
    or salt to the road or of a control product to the areas adjacent to
    the road.

- **5** [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-47, s. 14\]]{.Repealed}

- **6** The construction or installation of an interpretive display or
  exhibit associated with a physical work, road, pull-off area or trail,
  where the construction and installation will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the expansion of any associated facilities;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be carried out in a special preservation area or a
    wilderness area set out in a management plan for a park tabled in
    each House of Parliament under subsection 11(1) of the [Canada
    National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

- **7** The construction, installation, modification, maintenance or
  repair of a handrail or guardrail associated with a physical work.

- **8** The maintenance or repair of a fire tower.

- **9** A development, or part of a development, for which renewal of a
  permit, licence, lease or authorization is required and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been modified; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has fulfilled the requirements of a preliminary
    screening, environmental assessment or environmental impact review
    under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
    Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2).

- **10** A development, or part of a development, for which a permit,
  licence, lease or authorization is required and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was part of a development that fulfilled the
    requirements of a preliminary screening, environmental assessment or
    environmental impact review under the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
    Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not been modified since the development
    referred to in paragraph (a) fulfilled the requirements referred to
    in that paragraph.

- **11** The construction, installation, expansion or demolition of a
  physical work with a footprint of less than 25 m^2^ and a height of
  less than 5 m that will not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} entail the release of waste into the environment;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} involve the handling of hazardous materials.

- **12** Research, scientific studies or surveys authorized under the
  [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) or the [Species at Risk
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3).
