## Residents of India Remission Order {#residents-of-india-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-137
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-10-23

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax, Interest and Penalties on
Royalties or Fees Received from Canada by Residents of India for
Technical Services

P.C. 1991-1953  1991-10-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, on the recommendation of the Minister of
Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-137_e_hq_6484){#fn_SI-91-137_e_hq_6484-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
income tax, interest and penalties on royalties or fees received from
Canada by residents of India for technical
services](/eng/regulations/SI-91-137).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643804 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Residents of India Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-91-137).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643806 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the *Agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of India for the Avoidance
    of Double Taxation and the Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect
    to Taxes on Income* set out in Schedule III to chapter 7 of the
    Statutes of Canada, 1986; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by paragraph
    1(c) of Article 3 of the Agreement. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643811 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to any person who is a resident of India, within the meaning of the
Agreement, for any amount paid or credited to that person in respect of
royalties in relation to a right or property that is granted after
December 12, 1988 or in respect of fees for technical services under a
contract that is signed after that date, of an amount equal to the
amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the taxes, interest and penalties
  payable by that person under the Act with respect to the amount so
  paid or credited

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the taxes, interest and penalties
  that would have been payable by that person under the Act with respect
  to the amount so paid or credited, if the reference to a rate of 30
  per cent in paragraph 2 of Article 13 of the Agreement were read as a
  reference to a rate of 20 per cent.
