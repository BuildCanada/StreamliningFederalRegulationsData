## Certified Seed Potatoes Regulations {#certified-seed-potatoes-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 517
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Regulations Respecting Entry of Certified Seed Potatoes Imported for Use
Exclusively for Propagation Purposes
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557554 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Certified Seed Potatoes
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._517).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557556 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Certified seed potatoes,
imported under tariff item 7120-1, shall have attached to each bag or
other container a CERTIFIED SEED POTATOES tag issued by the proper
authority of the state or district in which the potatoes were grown,
certifying that they have been grown and approved especially for use as
seed in accordance with the official rules and regulations of the
government of the country of production.
