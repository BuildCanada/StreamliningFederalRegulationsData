## Asian Long-horned Beetle Compensation Regulations {#asian-long-horned-beetle-compensation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-113

Registration 2004-05-10

Asian Long-horned Beetle Compensation Regulations

P.C. 2004-608 2004-05-10

:::::::::::::: section

the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to paragraph 47(q)
of the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708904 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[host tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[host tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any tree of the genera
    [Acer]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [Aesculus]{.otherLang lang="la"},
    [Albizia]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [Betula]{.otherLang lang="la"},
    [Celtis]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [Cercidiphyllum]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}, [Koelreuteria]{.otherLang lang="la"},
    [Platanus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [Populus]{.otherLang lang="la"},
    [Salix]{.otherLang lang="la"}, [Sorbus]{.otherLang lang="la"} or
    [Ulmus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, that was or could have been infested
    or that constitutes or could have constituted a biological obstacle
    to the control of the Asian Long-horned Beetle ([Anoplophora
    glabripennis]{.otherLang lang="la"}). ([arbre hôte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[infested]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[infested]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a host tree, means
    that the Asian Long-horned Beetle is present in the tree.
    ([parasité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned or had possession, care or control of a
      host tree; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is named in a notice referred to in subsection
      2(1). ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2016-71, s. 2

## [Payment of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708913 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (4) to (5), the Minister may order that compensation be
  paid under subsection 39(1) of the [Plant Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) to a person who has received a notice, issued
  by an inspector under the [Plant Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-212) during the period beginning
  on April 1, 2013 and ending on March 31, 2019, to dispose of one or
  more host trees, if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has lost one or more host trees from a property as
    a result of the disposal and has planted on the same property one or
    more trees to replace the host tree or trees lost; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submits an application for compensation to the
    Minister on or before December 31, 2020.

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-71, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Compensation must not be paid in respect of a host
  tree that was disposed of if a host tree is planted to replace it in a
  place where host trees have been destroyed because they were or could
  have been infested or they constituted or could have constituted a
  biological obstacle to the control of the Asian Long-horned Beetle.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Compensation must not be paid in respect of any host
  tree disposed of on any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} natural or wild areas, other than land used for
    woodlots, parks, sportsfields, recreation trails or similar
    recreational areas, golf courses, educational institutions or
    hospitals;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} drainage ditches; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} utility or railway right-of-ways.

## [Amount of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708926 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The compensation to be paid
to a person for the loss of host trees referred to in paragraph 2(1)(a)
is the equivalent of the direct costs incurred by the person to plant
trees to replace the host trees that were disposed of, including the
cost of acquiring the replacement trees, to a maximum amount of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$300 per host tree disposed of, if the location of
  the host tree disposed of is in an area that is zoned municipally for
  residential, commercial or industrial use, or for use for an
  educational institution or a hospital, and that is not referred to in
  paragraph (b) or (c);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$150 per host tree disposed of, if the location of
  the host tree disposed of is on public land, including public land
  that is used as a park, sportsfield, recreation trail or similar
  recreational area and that is not referred to in paragraph (c); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$40 per host tree disposed of, if the location of
  the host tree disposed of is in a woodlot in an area zoned municipally
  for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} commercial or industrial use, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} livestock or vegetation-based agricultural use.

- SOR/2016-71, s. 4(E)

## [Application for Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708935 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application for compensation must be completed on a form provided by
  the Minister and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address and telephone number
    and the applicant's facsimile number and e-mail address, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a corporation, the names,
    addresses and telephone numbers of the directors of the corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the amount paid by the applicant to
    replace the host trees that were disposed of;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the locations where the host trees were before they
    were disposed of;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} copies of all documents received by the applicant
    that relate to the disposition of host trees;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} evidence to substantiate the number of host trees
    that were disposed of and the genus of those trees; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} evidence, by receipts or solemn declaration, to
    substantiate the number of trees that were purchased by the
    applicant and that have been planted to replace the host trees that
    were disposed of and to substantiate the locations where the
    replacement trees were planted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for compensation may be amended at any
  time on or before the date referred to in paragraph 2(1)(b).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for compensation may be submitted
  after the date referred to in paragraph 2(1)(b) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there were exceptional circumstances beyond the
    applicant's control that prevented the applicant from submitting an
    application on or before that date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is submitted within 14 days after
    the day on which those circumstances ceased to exist.

## [Other Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708949 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The payment of compensation
to a person is subject to the condition that the person must keep any
books and records that are necessary to substantiate the information
contained in the application for at least three years after the date on
which the replacement trees are planted.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708951 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
