## Sheshatshiu Innu First Nation Band Order {#sheshatshiu-innu-first-nation-band-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-414
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2002-11-21

Sheshatshiu Innu First Nation Band Order

P.C. 2002-1947  2002-11-21
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph (c) of the definition [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 2(1) and to subsection 73(3) of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5), hereby makes the annexed [Sheshatshiu Innu First
Nation Band Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-414).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697907 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[adoption]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes adoption in accordance with
Innu custom.

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697909 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The body of Indians
described in section 3 is hereby declared to be a band for the purposes
of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), to be known as the Sheshatshiu Innu
First Nation.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Sheshatshiu Innu First
Nation is the body of Indians consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the persons identified in Schedule 1; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every other person of Canadian Innu ancestry who, on
  November 19, 2002, was not registered in the Indian Register and who,
  on that day,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was ordinarily resident in the location known as
    Sheshatshiu, as described in Schedule 2,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} had been absent from the location for a period of
    not more than 12 months but was ordinarily resident in the location
    before that absence,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} had been absent from the location for a period of
    more than 12 months for the purpose of education, employment,
    incarceration or for health reasons, including medical treatments,
    but was ordinarily resident in the location before that absence,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} was absent from the location because of adoption
    or placement into foster care or a similar arrangement, but who was,
    or whose parents were, ordinarily resident in the location at the
    time of the adoption or placement, or

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} was, whether by birth or adoption, the parent,
    grandparent, great-grandparent, child, grandchild, great-grandchild,
    brother, sister, half-brother, half-sister, aunt, uncle, niece,
    nephew or first cousin of a person referred to in any of
    subparagraphs (i) to (iv).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697919 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
