## Related Party Transactions (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#related-party-transactions-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-277
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1996-05-28

Related Party Transactions (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations

P.C. 1996-759 1996-05-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 488 and 531 of the [Trust
and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-277_e_hq_6067){#fn_SOR-96-277_e_hq_6067-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing
transactions that a company may enter into with a related party of the
company](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-276).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981556 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([banque
    étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-82, s. 2
:::

## [Prescribed Related Party Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981562 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
488 of the Act, a company may, directly or indirectly, enter into any of
the following transactions with a related party of the company:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an allocation of an amount by or to the company made
  pursuant to subsection 18(2.3), 125(3), 127(10.3), 181.5(2), 190.15(2)
  or 191.1(3) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the company agrees to pay all or a portion of the
    tax of the related party, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the related party agrees to pay the company an
    amount at least equal to the additional costs incurred by the
    company, including additional taxes payable under the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as a result of the company entering into the
    agreement or amended agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which the related party agrees to pay all or a portion of the tax of
  the company;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issue by or to the company of conversion
  privileges, options or rights to acquire securities the issuance of
  which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which a security is being or has been issued;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, for the purpose of cancellation, of any
  shares or debt obligations issued by or to the company the purchase of
  which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which the shares or debt obligations were issued;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the redemption of any redeemable shares or debt
  obligations issued by or to the company the redemption of which is not
  otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions pursuant to which
  the shares or debt obligations were issued; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the payment or delivery of money or property by or to
  the company as a consequence of a reduction of the stated capital of
  the company or related party.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-82, s. 3
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 488 of the Act, a company that is controlled by a
  foreign bank may enter into the following transactions with the
  foreign bank that controls it, or with a financial institution
  controlled by that foreign bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transaction that forms an integral part of
    financial services provided to customers of the company, of the
    foreign bank or of the financial institution; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deposit by the company with the foreign bank or
    the financial institution

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a term not longer than 30 days,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for short-term liquidity management purposes,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in an amount that, added to the aggregate of
      the amounts of all other deposits of the company with its related
      parties, does not exceed 50 per cent of the regulatory capital of
      the company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The class of transactions referred to in paragraph
  (1)(a) includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} interest rate, currency, equity and commodity
    swaps;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} foreign exchange forwards, options and spot
    transactions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forward rate agreements;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} options on interest rates, futures, commodities,
    equity indices, shares and ownership interests;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} futures on interest rates, currencies and
    commodities;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} transactions involving money market instruments;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} loan syndications and asset sales;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} transactions involving commodity forwards;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} repurchase agreements and other transactions in
    respect of securities; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} derivative transactions of transactions referred to
    in any of paragraphs (a) to (i).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-82, s. 4
:::
