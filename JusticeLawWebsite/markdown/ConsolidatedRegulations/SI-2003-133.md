## Camp Ipperwash Indian Settlement Remission Order, 2003 {#camp-ipperwash-indian-settlement-remission-order-2003 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-133
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Camp Ipperwash Indian Settlement Remission Order, 2003

P.C. 2003-989  2003-06-18

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2003-133_e_hq_5145){#fn_SI-2003-133_e_hq_5145-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed [Camp Ipperwash Indian Settlement Remission Order,
2003](/eng/regulations/SI-2003-133).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607077 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the settlement
    named, and constituting the lands described, in the schedule.
    ([établissement indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
    ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607083 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of the Indian Settlement until lands constituting that Indian
settlement are set apart as a reserve by an order of the Governor in
Council.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Income Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607085 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-607086 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tax imposed under
  Part I, I.1 or I.2 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other words and expressions not otherwise defined
  in section 1 have the same meaning as in the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

### [Remission of Income Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-607090 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to an Indian, or a band, with income situated on the Indian Settlement,
in respect of each taxation year or fiscal period beginning during or
after the calendar year 1985, of the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties paid or payable by
  the Indian or band, as the case may be, for the taxation year or
  fiscal period

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties that would have
  been payable by that Indian or band for the taxation year or fiscal
  period if the Indian Settlement had been a reserve throughout that
  taxation year or fiscal period.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607095 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-607096 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and
  services tax imposed under subsection 165(1) of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other words and expressions not otherwise defined
  in section 1 have the same meaning as in Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

### [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-607100 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, remission is hereby granted to an Indian or a band that is the
recipient of a taxable supply made on or delivered to the Indian
Settlement on or after the day on which this Order comes into force in
the case of an Indian, and January 1, 1991 in the case of a band, of the
amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the recipient

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the recipient
  if the Indian Settlement had been a reserve at the time the supply was
  made or delivered.

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-607105 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission granted to an
Indian under section 6 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable has not otherwise been
  rebated, credited, refunded or remitted under Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) or under the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of tax paid, a written claim for the
  remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years
  after the day on which the tax was paid.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission granted to a band
under section 6 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable has not otherwise been
  rebated, credited, refunded or remitted under Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) or under the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of tax paid on or after January 1, 1991
  but before the day on which this Order comes into force, a written
  claim for the remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue
  within two years after the day on which this Order comes into force;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of tax paid on or after the day on which
  this Order comes into force, a written claim for the remission is made
  to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the day on
  which the tax was paid.
