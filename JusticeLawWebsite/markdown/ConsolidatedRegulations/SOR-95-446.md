## B.C. Place Replacement Turf Remission Order, 1995 {#b.c.-place-replacement-turf-remission-order-1995 .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1995-09-13

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Artificial Turf and
Rubber Backing for British Columbia Place

P.C. 1995-1528  1995-09-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-446_e_hq_6373){#fn_SOR-95-446_e_hq_6373-ID0EDBAA},

customs duties on artificial turf and rubber backing for British
Columbia Place](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-446).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975991 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [B.C. Place Replacement Turf Remission Order,
1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-446).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975993 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on artificial
turf and cyclized rubber sheets of tariff item Nos. 5603.00.10 or
5701.90.10, respectively, imported by or on behalf of British Columbia
Pavilion Corporation to replace and/or repair the existing field in B.C.
Place.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975995 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on the condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the artificial turf and cyclized rubber sheets are
  imported into Canada during the period commencing on February 1, 1993
  and ending on December 31, 1997; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within three years of the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.
