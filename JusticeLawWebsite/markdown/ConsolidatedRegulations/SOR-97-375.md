[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-375

Confederation Bridge Area Provincial (P.E.I.) Laws Application

P.C. 1997-1031  1997-07-25
::::

:::: section

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 27(1) of the [Oceans
Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-375_e_hq_5753){#fn_SOR-97-375_e_hq_5753-ID0EFBBA},
the Minister of Justice published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on April 12, 1997, a copy
of the proposed [Confederation Bridge Area Provincial (P.E.I.) Laws
Application Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-375), substantially in
the annexed form, and an opportunity was given to interested persons and
provinces to make representations with respect to the proposed
Regulations;

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008940 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Confederation Bridge Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area, not
within any province, that is within the lands and the lands covered by
water described in Schedule B, as amended from time to time, to the
lease document registered on October 5, 1993 as document 3562 in the
Prince County Registry Office of the Province of Prince Edward Island.

## [Application of the Laws of Prince Edward Island]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008942 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The laws of the Province of
Prince Edward Island, except the Highway Traffic Act, apply in the
Confederation Bridge Area.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008944 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 28, 1997.
