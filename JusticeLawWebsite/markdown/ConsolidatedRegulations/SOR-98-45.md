## Verification of Origin (Non-Free Trade Partners), Tariff Classification and Value for Duty of Imported Goods Regulations {#verification-of-origin-non-free-trade-partners-tariff-classification-and-value-for-duty-of-imported-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

Registration 1997-12-29

Verification of Origin (Non-Free Trade Partners), Tariff Classification
and Value for Duty of Imported Goods Regulations

P.C. 1997-2017  1997-12-29

Whereas the proposed Verification of Origin, Tariff Classification and
Value for Duty of Imported Goods Regulations (Non-Free Trade Partners)
give effect, in part, to a public announcement made on October 27, 1997
and are therefore, by virtue of paragraph 164(4)(a.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EECBA} of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EBCBA}, not required to be
published under subsection 164(3) of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EECBA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1992, c. 28, s. 30(3)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e-ID0EBCBA){#fnb_e}R.S., c. 1 (2nd Supp.)
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section
42.01[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fnc_e){#fnc_e-ID0EICAA} and
paragraph 164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fnd_e){#fnd_e-ID0EGCAA} of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes the annexed
Verification of Origin, Tariff Classification and Value for Duty of
Imported Goods Regulations (Non-Free Trade Partners).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024112 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Least Developed Country Tariff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Least Developed Country Tariff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Least Developed Country Tariff set out in the List of Tariff
    Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([tarif des pays les moins
    développés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a verification conducted
    under section 42.01 of the Act. ([vérification]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information with respect to the origin, tariff
    classification or value for duty of goods that are the subject of a
    verification. ([lettre de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information with respect to the origin,
    tariff classification or value for duty of goods that are the
    subject of a verification. ([questionnaire de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2008-77, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Prescribed Manner of Verification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024122 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a verification in respect of goods may be conducted
  in a manner set out in one or more of the following paragraphs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a review of a verification questionnaire completed
    by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the importer or owner of the goods, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person who accounted for the goods under
      subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review of a written response received from a
    person referred to in paragraph (a) to a verification letter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a review of any record or information or an
    inspection of any goods or component of goods received from a person
    referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collection, from the premises prescribed under
    subsection 3(1), and review of information that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was requested in a verification questionnaire or
      verification letter but was not provided, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is needed to verify information from a completed
      verification questionnaire or written response to a verification
      letter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A verification in respect of goods of tariff item
  numbers set out in the schedule to the [General Preferential Tariff
  and Least Developed Country Tariff Rules of Origin
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-165) for which the benefit of
  the Least Developed Country Tariff is claimed may be conducted in a
  manner set out in one or more of the following paragraphs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a review of a verification questionnaire completed
    by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the importer or owner of the goods,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the person who accounted for the goods under
      subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material that is
      used in the production of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review of a written response received from a
    person referred to in paragraph (a) to a verification letter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a review of any record or information or an
    inspection of any goods or component of goods received from a person
    referred to in paragraph (a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collection, from the premises prescribed under
    section 3, and review of information that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was requested in a verification questionnaire or
      verification letter but was not provided, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is needed to verify information from a completed
      verification questionnaire or written response to a verification
      letter.

## [Prescribed Premises]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024147 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are prescribed premises for the purposes of section 42.01 of
  the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premises of the importer or owner of the goods,
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the premises of the person who accounted for goods
    under subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following additional premises --- located in a
  country that is a beneficiary of the Least Developed Country Tariff
  --- are prescribed premises in respect of goods of tariff item numbers
  set out in the schedule to the [General Preferential Tariff and Least
  Developed Country Tariff Rules of Origin
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-165) for which the benefit of
  that Tariff is claimed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premises of the exporter or producer of the
    goods, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the premises of a producer or supplier of a
    material that is used in the production of the goods.
