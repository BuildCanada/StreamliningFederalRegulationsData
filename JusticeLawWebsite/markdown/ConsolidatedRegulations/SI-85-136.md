## IRSTD System Remission Order {#irstd-system-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-136
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-08-07

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Goods Imported for
the Infra-Red Search and Target Designation Project

P.C. 1985-2296 1985-07-24
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of customs duty on goods imported for the
Infra-Red Search and Target Designation Project.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 4
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639673 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [IRSTD System Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-136).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639675 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} materials, components and parts that are used in
      the production of an IRSTD System and that form or will form part
      of the system,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} materials, other than fuel or plant equipment,
      that are consumed directly in the production of an IRSTD System or
      its parts,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} equipment and parts thereof, for testing,
      maintaining or operating an IRSTD System or its parts or for
      training personnel to test, maintain or operate an IRSTD System or
      its parts,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} manuals and revisions thereof, and materials
      containing technical and other data for use as an aid in

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} testing, maintaining or operating an IRSTD
        System, or training personnel to test, maintain or operate such
        a System, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the procurement of an IRSTD System, and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} installation and checkout kits for an IRSTD
      System; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IRSTD System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IRSTD System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Engineering
    Development model or part thereof to be built under the Infra-Red
    Search and Target Designation Project approved by Treasury Board
    Minute TB 789702 dated October 6, 1983, as amended by Treasury Board
    Minute TB 794850 dated August 1, 1984; ([système infra-rouge de
    détection et de désignation d'objectifs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means development, design,
    manufacture, testing, repair, maintenance, rebuilding, modification
    and conversion. ([production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/89-248, s. 1
:::

## [Remission of Customs Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639689 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of goods imported
during the period beginning on April 1, 1984 and ending March 31, 1992,
for the production of an IRSTD System.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/89-248, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639693 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
section 3 is subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within three years after the date of importation of
  the goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an importer of goods submits to that Minister any
  reports or information the Minister may require for the administration
  of this Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/89-248, s. 3(F)
