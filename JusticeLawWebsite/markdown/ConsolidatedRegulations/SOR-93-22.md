## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951015 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2015-20, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal referred to in
    section 10.7 or paragraph 22.2(d) of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or paragraph 74(d) of the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([appel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[application for judicial review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application for judicial review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    application referred to in section 22.2 of the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or section 74 of the Immigration and Refugee
    and Protection Act. ([demande de contrôle
    judiciare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application for leave]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application for leave]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application
    referred to in section 22.1 of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29)
    or section 72 of the Immigration and Refugee and Protection Act.
    ([demande d'autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as the circumstances require,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Court of Appeal, including, in
      respect of a motion, a single judge of that court; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Federal Court, including a prothonotary
      acting within his or her jurisdiction. ([Cour]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registry within the meaning
    of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7).
    ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or body who has
    disposed of a matter, referred to in subsection 22.1(1) of the
    [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or subsection 72(1) of the
    Immigration and Refugee and Protection Act, that is the subject of
    an application for leave or an application for judicial review.
    ([tribunal administratif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[written reasons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[written reasons]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a transcript of
    reasons given orally. ([motifs écrits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951029 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply to the
following applications and appeals under the [Citizenship
Act](/eng/acts/C-29) and the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} applications for leave;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} applications for judicial review; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appeals to the Federal Court of Appeal from judgments
  of the Federal Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-20, s. 3
:::

[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  to the extent that they are inconsistent with the [Citizenship
  Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), as the case may be, or these Rules, Parts 1 to
  3, 5.1, 6, 7, 10 and 11 and rules 302 and 383 to 385 of the [Federal
  Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) apply to applications for
  leave, applications for judicial review and appeals.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Rule 133 of the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) does not apply to the service of
  an application for leave or an application for judicial review.

## [Deemed Receipt of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1304856 .Part}

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is required to be sent under these Rules is deemed to be
  received by the recipient

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by ordinary mail, on the tenth day
    after the day on which it is mailed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by registered mail or courier, on the
    day of delivery that is indicated on the post office or courier
    delivery receipt, as the case may be; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by electronic transmission or fax, on
    the day on which it is sent, as indicated on the electronic
    transmission or the fax transmission slip, as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the day on which the document is deemed
  to be received is a holiday or, in the case of a document that is sent
  by electronic transmission or fax, the document is sent after 5 p.m.
  at the recipient's local time, the document is deemed to be received
  on the next day that is not a holiday.

## [Form of Application for Leave]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951041 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for leave shall be in accordance with Form IR-1 as set out
  in the schedule and shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the full names of the parties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and the details of the matter --- the
    decision, determination or order made, measure taken or question
    raised --- in respect of which relief is sought and the date on
    which the applicant was notified of or otherwise became aware of the
    matter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the tribunal and, if the tribunal was
    composed of more than one person, the name of each person who was on
    the tribunal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the tribunal's file number, if any;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the precise relief to be sought on the application
    for judicial review;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the relief is sought,
    including a reference to any statutory provision or Rule to be
    relied on;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed place and language of the hearing of
    the application for judicial review;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the applicant has received the
    written reasons of the tribunal;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant consents to the electronic service
    of documents, the applicant's electronic address as set out in Form
    141A of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of any
    person who, for a fee or other consideration, prepared the
    application for leave; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the signature, name, address and telephone number
    of the applicant's solicitor or, if the applicant acts in person,
    his or her signature, name, address for service in Canada and
    telephone number.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless he or she is the applicant, the respondent to
  an application for leave is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a matter under the [Citizenship
    Act](/eng/acts/C-29), the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a matter under the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), each Minister who is
    responsible for the administration of that Act in respect of the
    matter for which leave is sought.

## [Notice of Appearance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951074 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent who is served with an application for leave shall serve a
  notice of appearance in accordance with Form IR-2 as set out in the
  schedule on the applicant and the tribunal, and file it, together with
  proof of service, within 10 days after service of the application for
  leave.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A respondent who has failed to file a notice of
  appearance in accordance with subrule (1) shall not be entitled to any
  further notice or service of any further document in the proceeding.

## [Anonymity Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1304870 .Part}

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party to an application for leave may make a written request, in Form
  IR-5 as set out in the schedule, that the court make an order that all
  documents that are prepared by the Court and that may be made
  available to the public be amended and redacted to the extent
  necessary to make the party's identity anonymous.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who opposes the request may, in that Form
  IR-5, make a written objection to the request.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request or an objection to a request, as the case
  may be, shall be served and filed and set out the grounds for the
  request or objection.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request shall be determined at the same time, and
  on the basis of the same materials, as the application for leave.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Court may make an order under subrule (1) if,
  after taking the public interest in open and accessible court
  proceedings into account, the Court is satisfied that the party's
  identity should be made anonymous.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} An order under this rule continues in effect until
  the Court orders otherwise, including for the duration of any appeal
  of the proceeding and after final judgment.

## [Obtaining Tribunal's Decision and Reasons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951080 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  application for leave sets out that the applicant has not received the
  written reasons of the tribunal, the Registry shall, without delay,
  send the tribunal a request in Form IR-3 as set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After receiving the request the tribunal shall,
  without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} send a copy of the decision or order at issue and
    the written reasons for it, duly certified by an appropriate officer
    to be correct, to each of the parties, and two copies to the
    Registry; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send a written notice to each of the parties and to
    the Registry indicating that no reasons were given or reasons were
    given but not recorded, as the case may be.

## [Perfecting Application for Leave]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951090 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant shall perfect an application for leave by complying with
  subrule (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the application sets out that the applicant
    has received the tribunal's written reasons, within 30 days after
    filing the application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the application sets out that the applicant
    has not received the tribunal's written reasons, within 30 days
    after receiving either the written reasons, or the notice under
    paragraph 9(2)(b), as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve on every respondent who has filed and served
    a notice of appearance, a record containing the following, on
    consecutively numbered pages, and in the following order:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the application for leave,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the decision or order, if any, in respect of
      which the application is made,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the written reasons given by the tribunal, or
      the notice under paragraph 9(2)(b), as the case may be,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the request, if any, for an anonymity order
      under rule 8.1,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} one or more supporting affidavits that verify the
      facts relied on by the applicant in support of the application or
      a request for an anonymity order under rule 8.1, if any,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of argument that contains concise
      submissions of the facts and law relied upon by the applicant for
      the relief proposed in the event that leave is granted, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the hearing will
      be in English or French, or partly in English and partly in
      French, and whether the materials for the hearing will be in
      English and French, or partly in English and partly in French; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the record, together with proof of service.

## [Respondent's Affidavits and Memorandum of Argument]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951104 .Part}

## [Affidavits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951111 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Affidavits filed in connection with
  an application for leave shall be confined to such evidence as the
  deponent could give if testifying as a witness before the Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless a judge for special reasons so orders, no
  cross-examination of a deponent on an affidavit filed in connection
  with an application is permitted before leave to commence an
  application for judicial review is granted.

## [Reply Memorandum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951117 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where a respondent serves
a memorandum of argument, an applicant may serve a memorandum of
argument in reply thereto, and shall file it, together with proof of
service, within 10 days after the day of service of the respondent's
memorandum of argument.

## [Disposition of Application for Leave]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951121 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any party has failed to serve and file any document
    required by these Rules within the time fixed, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's reply memorandum has been filed, or
    the time for filing it has expired,

  a judge may, without further notice to the parties, determine the
  application for leave on the basis of the materials then filed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the judge considers that documents in the
  possession or control of the tribunal are required for the proper
  disposition of the application for leave, the judge may, by order,
  specify the documents to be produced and filed and give such other
  directions as the judge considers necessary to dispose of the
  application for leave.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registry shall, without delay after an order is
  made under subrule (2), send a copy of the order to the tribunal.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Upon receipt of an order under subrule (2), the
  tribunal shall, without delay, send a copy of the materials specified
  in the order, duly certified by an appropriate officer to be correct,
  to each of the parties, and two copies to the Registry.

## [Obtaining Tribunal's Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951145 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Upon receipt of an order
under Rule 15, a tribunal shall, without delay, prepare a record
containing the following, on consecutively numbered pages and in the
following order:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decision or order in respect of which the
  application for judicial review is made and the written reasons given
  therefor,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all relevant documents that are in the possession or
  control of the tribunal,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any affidavits, or other documents filed during any
  such hearing, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a transcript, if any, of any oral testimony given
  during the hearing, giving rise to the decision or order or other
  matter that is the subject of the application for judicial review,

and shall send a copy, duly certified by an appropriate officer to be
correct, to each of the parties and two copies to the Registry.

## [Disposition of Application for Judicial Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951154 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a judge renders judgment in respect of an application for judicial
  review, the judge shall provide the parties with an opportunity to
  request that he or she certify that a serious question of general
  importance, referred to in paragraph 22.2(d) of the [Citizenship
  Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or paragraph 74(d) of the [Immigration and
  Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), as the case may be, is
  involved.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who requests that the judge certify that a
  serious question of general importance is involved shall specify the
  precise question.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-232, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-232, s. 9
- SOR/2015-20, s. 9

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951161 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/98-235,
s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal to the Federal Court of Appeal shall be commenced by filing a
  notice of appeal in Form IR-4 as set out in the schedule within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the pronouncement of the judgment
    under appeal; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} such further time as may be ordered by a judge of
    the Federal Court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of appeal shall be served on all parties and
  proof of service filed within 15 days after the notice of appeal is
  filed.

## [Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951176 .Part}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** No costs shall be awarded
to or payable by any party in respect of an application for leave, an
application for judicial review or an appeal under these Rules unless
the Court, for special reasons, so orders.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-232, s. 11

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-951180 .Part}

notice of appeal.
