## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536777 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Milk Act, 1965 of Ontario;
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Milk
    Marketing Board or the Ontario Cream Producers' Marketing Board,
    established pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Milk Commission of
    Ontario; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk or cream from cows, produced
    for consumer use in liquid form in Canada; ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    milk established from time to time pursuant to the Act and any
    regulation made by the Commission pursuant to the Act.
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 8
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536786 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of milk produced in the Province of Ontario and milk referred to
in paragraph 3(1)(b) produced in the Province of Quebec.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 9
:::

## [Interprovincial Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536790 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2001-16, s. 10

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Commission and the Commodity Board are each
  authorized to regulate the marketing of milk in interprovincial trade
  and for such purposes may exercise all or any powers like the powers
  exercisable by them in relation to the marketing of milk locally
  within the Province of Ontario under the Act and the Plan, with
  respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons and property situated within the Province
    of Ontario, except persons who ship milk produced in that province
    to the Province of Quebec; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons situated within the Province of Quebec who
    ship milk produced in that province to the Province of Ontario.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authority to regulate the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade does not include any power that is exercised by
  the Canadian Dairy Commission under the [Canadian Dairy Commission
  Act](/eng/acts/C-15) in relation to the marketing of milk in
  interprovincial trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-16, s. 11
:::

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536800 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it under section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose levies or charges on milk or any
  component of milk and collect those levies or charges from persons
  described in subsection 3(1) who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of milk and for that purpose may classify those persons into
  groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members of the
  different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of
  expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of milk and
  the equalization or adjustment among producers of milk of moneys
  realized from the sale of milk during such period or periods of time
  as the Commodity Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-742, s. 1
- SOR/2001-16, s. 12
