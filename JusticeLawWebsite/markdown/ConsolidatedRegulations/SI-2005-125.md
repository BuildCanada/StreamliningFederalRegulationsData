## Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage for Purposes of Section 110 of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-canadian-heritage-for-purposes-of-section-110-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-125
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2005-12-14

Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage for Purposes of
Section 110 of the Act

P.C. 2005-2047 2005-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 110(1) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBAA}, hereby
designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage for the purposes of section
110 of that Act, effective December 31, 2005.

  [[Return to footnote

  22, ss. 12 and 13
