## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2016-39
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2016-07-13

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2016-655 2016-06-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_4360_hq_18539){#fn_4360_hq_18539-ID0EHDAA} of
the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_4360_hq_18540){#fn_4360_hq_18540-ID0EEDAA},
transfers to the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada from
the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons the powers, duties
and functions of the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons
under the [Canadian Transportation Accident Investigation and Safety
Board Act](/eng/acts/C-23.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_4360_hq_18541){#fn_4360_hq_18541-ID0EBDAA}.
