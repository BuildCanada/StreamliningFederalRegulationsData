Deep Panuke Offshore Production Platform Remission Order, 2007

P.C. 2007-1929 2007-12-13

:::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed

2007](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-299).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737964 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of the floating production
platform, classified under tariff item No. 8905.20.20 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), imported into Canada for the Deep Panuke
Offshore Gas Development Project off the southeast coast of Nova Scotia.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737966 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the conditions that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness prior to December
  31, 2012.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737970 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
