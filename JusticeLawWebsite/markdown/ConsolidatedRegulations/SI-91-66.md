## Standard Trust Depositors Remission Order {#standard-trust-depositors-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-66
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-05-22

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax and Penalties Payable by
Standard Trust Depositors

P.C. 1991-812  1991-05-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, the President of the Treasury Board and Minister of
State (Finance) and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of income tax and
penalties payable by Standard Trust
depositors](/eng/regulations/SI-91-66).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644078 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Standard Trust Depositors Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-91-66).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644080 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[trust
company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Standard Trust Company or Standard
Loan Company. (*société de fiducie*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-644082 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted, to a taxpayer who is dependent on the
payment, by the trust company, of the taxpayer's deposit insured by
deposit insurance under the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
Act](/eng/acts/C-3) for the payment of that taxpayer's income tax,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of income tax in an amount equal to the amount of
  interest that is payable thereon by the taxpayer under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) and that has accrued from April 18, 1991 to the
  day of payment of the amount of deposit insured by deposit insurance
  under the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3);
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} of an amount equal to the penalties payable by the
  taxpayer, that have accrued from April 18, 1991 to the day of payment
  of the amount of deposit insured by deposit insurance under the
  [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), for failure
  to remit any amounts required to be remitted under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), Part I of the [Canada Pension
  Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) and Part III of the Unemployment Insurance Act.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 3 is granted in respect of income tax and remittances, the
payment of which by the taxpayer is dependent on the taxpayer's
accessibility to the aggregate deposits made by the taxpayer with the
trust company that do not exceed \$60,000.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in paragraph 3(b) is granted on condition that the taxpayer remits to
the Receiver General the amounts required to be remitted under the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), Part I of the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) and Part III of the Unemployment Insurance Act when
the amount of deposit insured becomes available.
