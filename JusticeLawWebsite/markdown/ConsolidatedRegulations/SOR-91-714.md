## Petro-Canada Limited Pension Protection Regulations {#petro-canada-limited-pension-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-714
:::

[PETRO-CANADA PUBLIC PARTICIPATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-11.1)

Registration 1991-12-05

Regulations Respecting Pension Protection for Employees of Petro-Canada
Limited

P.C. 1991-2406  1991-12-05
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of State (Finance and Privatization) and the President of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17 of the [Petro-Canada Public
Participation Act](/eng/acts/P-11.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-714_e_hq_6177){#fn_SOR-91-714_e_hq_6177-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting pension
protection for employees of Petro-Canada Limited.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939862 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Petro-Canada Limited Pension Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-714).

## [Applicable Enactments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939864 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and the [Supplementary Retirement
Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the regulations made under those Acts
apply to a person who makes an election under paragraph 17(1)(d) of the
Petro-Canada Limited Public Participation Act except as provided in
sections 3 and 4.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::

## [Adapted Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939868 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
17 of the Petro-Canada Limited Public Participation Act, subparagraphs
10(9)(b)(i) and (ii) and sections 12 and 13 of the [Public Service
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) are adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 10(9)(b)(i) and (ii) of that Act,
  the year in which a person ceased to be employed in the Public Service
  is the year in which the person ceased to be employed by Petro-Canada
  Limited; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in sections 12 and 13 of that Act

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} except for the purpose of subsection 12(4) of that
    Act, the number of years of pensionable service and any period of
    employment of a person includes any such service or employment with
    Petro-Canada Limited during the period beginning on the day that the
    person ceased to be employed in the Public Service and ending on the
    day preceding the day on which the person ceased to be employed by
    Petro-Canada Limited, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} except for the purposes of subparagraph (i), the
    day on which a person ceased to be employed in the Public Service is
    the day on which that person ceased to be employed by Petro-Canada
    Limited.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
17 of the Petro-Canada Limited Public Participation Act, subsection 4(5)
and subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and (ii) of the [Supplementary Retirement
Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) are adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 4(5) of that Act, the retirement year
  or retirement month of a person is 1991 or July 1991, as the case may
  be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and (ii) of that Act, the
  year in which a person ceased to contribute in respect of current
  service to the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account is the year
  in which that person ceased to be employed by Petro-Canada Limited.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1991, c. 10, s. 19
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939881 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations shall
come into force, or be deemed to have come into force, on November 29,
1991.
