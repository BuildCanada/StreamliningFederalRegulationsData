## Cost Recovery Regulations {#cost-recovery-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-146
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT ACT, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21)

Registration 2012-07-06

Cost Recovery Regulations

P.C. 2012-971 2012-07-05
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 83 of the [Canadian
Environmental Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1269-E_hq_12106){#fn_81000-2-1269-E_hq_12106-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Cost Recovery
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-146).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782842 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 59(1) of the
[Canadian Environmental Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) ("the
Act") does not apply to the following proponents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a federal authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner in Council of the Northwest
  Territories, the Legislature of Yukon and an agency or body of those
  territories;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the council of a band, as defined in subsection 2(1)
  of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a provincial government, except in the case of a
  provincial Crown corporation.

## [Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782849 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The services provided by a
third party that are set out in Part 1 of the schedule are prescribed
for the purposes of paragraph 59(1)(b) of the Act.

## [Amounts to Be Paid]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782852 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of the Agency

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amounts referred to in
column 2 of Part 2 of the schedule are prescribed for the expenses
referred to in column 1 for the purposes of paragraph 59(1)(b) of the
Act in relation to the exercise of the responsibilities of the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of a review panel

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amounts referred to in
column 2 of Part 3 of the schedule are prescribed for the expenses
referred to in column 1 for the purposes of paragraph 59(1)(b) of the
Act in relation to the exercise of the responsibilities of the members
of a review panel.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782857 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}S.C. 2012, c. 19

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndE9AE_hq_12998){#fn_IndE9AE_hq_12998-ID0EBCA}[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 52 of the [Jobs, Growth and Long-term Prosperity
Act](/eng/acts/J-0.8), chapter 19 of the Statutes of Canada, 2012, comes
into force.
