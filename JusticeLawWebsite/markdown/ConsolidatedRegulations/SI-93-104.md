## Order Designating the Secretary of State of Canada as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Archives, the National Film Board, the National Library, the National Gallery, the National Museum of Science and Technology, the Canadian Museum of Civilization, and the Canadian Museum of Nature {#order-designating-the-secretary-of-state-of-canada-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-national-archives-the-national-film-board-the-national-library-the-national-gallery-the-national-museum-of-science-and-technology-the-canadian-museum-of-civilization-and-the-canadian-museum-of-nature .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-104
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Designating the Secretary of State of Canada as Appropriate
Minister with Respect to the National Archives, the National Film Board,
the National Library, the National Gallery, the National Museum of
Science and Technology, the Canadian Museum of Civilization, and the
Canadian Museum of Nature

P.C. 1993-1450 1993-06-25
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 3(1.1) of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to amend Schedule I.1 of that Act
  by striking out the reference to the Minister of Communications in
  column II thereof opposite the names of the National Archives of
  Canada, the National Film Board and the National Library in Column I
  thereof and by substituting therefor a reference to the Secretary of
  State of Canada in column II thereof opposite those names.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SI/2015-112\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2015-112.
