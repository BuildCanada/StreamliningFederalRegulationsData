Registration 2007-05-10

Load Line Regulations

P.C. 2007-721 2007-05-10

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, Infrastructure and Communities, pursuant to
paragraphs 35(1)(d) and 120(1)(h) of the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Load Line Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-99).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742234 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[1966 Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[1966 Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
      Convention on Load Lines, 1966. ([Convention de
      1966]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Marine Technical Review
      Board established under section 26 of the
      Act. ([Bureau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the American
      Bureau of Shipping, Bureau Veritas, Det Norske Veritas, Lloyd's
      Register of Shipping, Germanischer Lloyd, Registro Italiano Navale
      and Nippon Kaiji Kyokai. ([société de
      classification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
      Convention on Load Lines, 1966, as modified by the
      Protocol. ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[domestic voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[domestic voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage that is
      from a place in Canada to another place in Canada. ([voyage
      intérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[freeboard deck]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[freeboard deck]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a vessel,
      means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's uppermost complete deck that is
        exposed to weather and sea and has permanent means of closing
        all openings in its exposed parts, and below which all openings
        in the shell of the vessel are fitted with permanent means of
        watertight closing; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permanent deck that is lower than the deck
        referred to in paragraph (a), is continuous in a fore and aft
        direction at least between the machinery space and peak
        bulkheads and continuous athwartships if the vessel's owner
        requests that lower deck. ([pont de franc-bord]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[HSC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[HSC Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Code of
      Safety for High Speed Craft, 2000, (2000 HSC CODE), published by
      the IMO, as amended from time to time. ([recueil
      HSC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Maritime
      Organization. ([OMI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
      internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([eaux internes du
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[open-hopper dredge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[open-hopper dredge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-propelled
      dredge with an open hold or hopper in the hull that receives
      dredged material and is fitted with bottom doors that can quickly
      jettison the dredged material. ([marie-salope]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a port; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any vessel or place that is used for loading or
        unloading vessels. ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 1988 Protocol relating
      to the International Convention on Load lines, 1966, as amended on
      January 1, 2005. ([Protocole]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
      abritées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a decked structure on
      the freeboard deck of a vessel extending from side to side of the
      vessel or with the side plating not being more than four per cent
      of the breadth inboard of the shell plating, and includes that
      part of the hull extending above the freeboard deck if the
      freeboard deck is a lower deck. ([superstructure]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[watertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[watertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means designed to withstand a
      static head of water without any leakage. ([étanche à
      l'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, a vessel is
  constructed on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which its keel is laid,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which construction identifiable with a
    specific vessel begins, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which assembly of the vessel reaches the
    lesser of 50 tonnes and one per cent of the estimated mass of all
    structural material.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), for the purpose of these
  Regulations, every reference to "Administration" in a document
  incorporated by reference in these Regulations means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Canadian vessel, the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign vessel, the government of
    the state whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, every reference
  to "Administration" in articles 6 and 8 of the 1966 Convention or the
  Convention means, in the case of a Canadian vessel, the Board.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of interpreting a document
  incorporated by reference in these Regulations, "should" shall be read
  to mean "shall".

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Circle Load Lines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742272 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742273 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[amidships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amidships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the middle of the length of
    a vessel. ([milieu du bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deck line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deck line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a horizontal line that is
    300 mm long and 25 mm wide marked amidships on the outer surface of
    the shell on each side of the vessel. ([ligne de
    pont]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[existing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[existing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is not a
    new vessel. ([bâtiment existant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage, other
    than an inland voyage, between a place in Canada and a place not in
    Canada or between places not in Canada. ([voyage
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a new vessel, has the same meaning
      as in article 2(8) of Annex A to the Convention; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an existing vessel, has the same
      meaning as in subsection 1(2) of Schedule I to the
      Rules. ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian vessel

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that was constructed on or after April 14, 1970
        and is engaged on an international voyage,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that was constructed on or after April 14,
        1973 and is not engaged on an international voyage, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that was constructed before April 14, 1970
        and is engaged on an international voyage, or that was
        constructed before April 14, 1973 and is not engaged on an
        international voyage, if the authorized representative makes a
        request in writing to the Minister for the vessel to be assigned
        freeboards as a new vessel; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a foreign vessel that was constructed on or after
      the date on which the 1966 Convention came into force in the state
      whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly. ([bâtiment
      neuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[North American Great Lakes Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[North American Great Lakes Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Lake
    Ontario, Lake Erie, Lake Huron (including Georgian Bay), Lake
    Michigan and Lake Superior, the waters connecting those lakes, the
    St. Lawrence Seaway and the St. Lawrence River west of the Victoria
    Bridge in Montréal. ([zone des Grands Lacs de l'Amérique du
    Nord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [General Load Line
    Rules](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1425), C.R.C., c. 1425, as they
    read on January 1, 2006. ([Règles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sailing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sailing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that has
    sufficient sail area for navigation under sails alone, whether or
    not fitted with mechanical means of
    propulsion. ([voilier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[St. Lawrence River Seasonal Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[St. Lawrence River Seasonal Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that
    part of the St. Lawrence River bounded by the Victoria Bridge in
    Montréal, a straight line drawn from Cap-des-Rosiers to West Point,
    Anticosti Island, and a line drawn along the meridian of longitude
    63°W from Anticosti Island to the north shore of the St. Lawrence
    River. ([région saisonnière du fleuve
    Saint-Laurent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel specially constructed
    for the carriage of liquid cargoes in
    bulk. ([bâtiment-citerne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[timber deck cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber deck cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cargo of timber
    carried on an uncovered part of a freeboard deck or superstructure
    deck. ([chargement de bois en pontée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[West Coast Treaty Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[West Coast Treaty Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Puget Sound in the State of
      Washington;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waters lying between Vancouver Island and the
      mainland and east of a line from a point one nautical mile west of
      the city limits of Port Angeles in the State of Washington to Race
      Rocks on Vancouver Island and of a line from Hope Island, British
      Columbia, to Cape Calvert, Calvert Island, British Columbia;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the waters lying east of a line from Cape Calvert
      to Duke Point on Duke Island in the State of Alaska;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the waters lying north of Duke Island and east of
      Prince of Wales Island, Baranof Island and Chichagof Island in the
      State of Alaska;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Peril, Neva and Olga Straits as far
      south as Sitka in the State of Alaska; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the waters lying east of a line from Port Althorp
      on Chichagof Island to Cape Spencer in the State of Alaska. ([zone
      d'application du Traité --- côte ouest]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742303 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Part applies in respect of Canadian vessels
  everywhere and foreign vessels in Canadian waters.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels ordinarily engaged in catching, attempting
    to catch or harvesting fish, whales, seals, walrus or other living
    resources of the sea;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} high-speed craft that have been certified in
    accordance with the HSC Code and meet the requirements of that Code;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} new vessels of less than 24 m in length;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} existing vessels of less than 150 gross tonnage;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} vessels that are engaged on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a voyage solely within the limits of the inland
      waters of Canada or an inland voyage and holding a certificate
      issued under Part 2 or similar load line regulations made under
      the laws of the United States,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a sheltered waters voyage, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a domestic voyage, if the vessels are not
      carrying passengers or cargo;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Canadian vessels or vessels registered in the
    United States that are engaged on an international voyage wholly
    within the West Coast Treaty Zone;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} new vessels that do not have means of
    self-propulsion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are engaged on a domestic voyage wholly within
      the West Coast Treaty Zone and are not carrying oil as cargo or
      passengers, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are engaged on a domestic voyage outside the
      West Coast Treaty Zone and are not carrying oil as cargo,
      passengers or crew; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} existing vessels that do not have means of
    self-propulsion, are engaged on a domestic voyage and are not
    carrying passengers or crew.

### [Exceptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1436048 .Subheading}

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs 3(2)(f) and (g),
  a vessel that is constructed or converted for the purpose of towing on
  or after the day on which the [Vessel Construction and Equipment
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-257) come into force and that
  is engaged on a voyage beyond the limits of an inland voyage shall
  meet the conditions of assignment set out in Chapter II of Annex I of
  Annex B of Part 3 of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs 3(2)(f) to (i), a vessel that is
  constructed on or after the day on which the [Vessel Construction and
  Equipment Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-257) come into force,
  that carries at least one person and that is engaged on a voyage
  beyond the limits of an inland voyage shall comply with regulations 24
  and 25 of Chapter II of Annex I of Annex B of Part 3 of the
  Convention.

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742323 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  Canadian vessel shall depart on a domestic voyage unless it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an International Load Line Certificate, a
    Local Load Line Certificate or an International Load Line Exemption
    Certificate issued under section 5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is marked in accordance with the certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No Canadian vessel shall depart on an international
  voyage unless it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an International Load Line Certificate or an
    International Load Line Exemption Certificate issued under section
    5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is marked in accordance with the certificate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No foreign vessel shall depart from a place in Canada
  unless it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an International Load Line Certificate or an
    International Load Line Exemption Certificate issued by the
    government of the state whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly or,
    at the request of that government, by another government; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is marked in accordance with the certificate.

### [Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742334 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by the authorized representative of a Canadian vessel that
  is a new vessel and was constructed before the day on which this
  section comes into force, the Minister shall issue an International
  Load Line Certificate to the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of assignment set out in Chapter II
    of Annex I to the 1966 Convention are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is maintained in accordance with the
    requirements of a classification society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been assigned freeboards that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are determined in accordance with Chapter III of
      Annex I to the 1966 Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the freeboards determined under
      subparagraph (i) are not adequate because of the general
      structural strength of the vessel, are adequate for the general
      structural strength of the vessel; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is marked in accordance with Chapter I
    of Annex I to the 1966 Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On application by the authorized representative of a
  Canadian vessel that is a new vessel and was constructed on or after
  the day on which this section comes into force, the Minister shall
  issue an International Load Line Certificate to the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of assignment set out in Chapter II
    of Annex I to the Convention are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is maintained in accordance with the
    requirements of a classification society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been assigned freeboards that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are determined in accordance with Chapter III of
      Annex I to the Convention, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the freeboards determined under
      subparagraph (i) are not adequate because of the general
      structural strength of the vessel, are adequate for the general
      structural strength of the vessel; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is marked in accordance with Chapter I
    of Annex I to the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On application by the authorized representative of a
  Canadian vessel that is an existing vessel, the Minister shall issue
  an International Load Line Certificate to the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of assignment set out in Part I of
    Schedule I to the Rules are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is maintained in accordance with the
    requirements of a classification society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has been assigned freeboards determined
    in accordance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel other than a sailing
      vessel or tanker, Part II of Schedule I to the Rules and, if the
      vessel is carrying timber deck cargo, Part IV of that Schedule,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sailing vessel, Part III of
      Schedule I to the Rules, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tanker, Part V of Schedule I
      to the Rules; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is marked in accordance with Part VI of
    Schedule I to the Rules.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On application by the authorized representative of a
  Canadian vessel that is a new vessel, the Minister shall issue a Local
  Load Line Certificate to the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intended operation of the vessel provides an
    equivalent or greater level of safety than one or more of the
    requirements set out in subsection (1) or (2), as the case may be;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other requirements set out in subsection (1) or
    (2), as the case may be, are met.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} On application by the authorized representative of
  a Canadian vessel that is an existing vessel, the Minister shall issue
  a Local Load Line Certificate to the vessel if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intended operation of the vessel provides an
    equivalent or greater level of safety than one or more of the
    requirements set out in subsection (3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other requirements set out in subsection (3)
    are met.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} On application by the authorized representative of a
  Canadian vessel, the Minister shall issue an International Load Line
  Exemption Certificate to the vessel if it has been granted an
  exemption by the Board under section 13 and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to the terms of that exemption, the
    requirements of subsection (1), (2) or (3), as the case may be, are
    met; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any design-, contruction- or equipment-related
    safety requirements set by the Board under that section are met.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A Local Load Line Certificate that is issued to an
  open-hopper dredge shall include a dredging load line if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it has been assigned a dredging freeboard that is
    62.5 per cent of the assigned summer freeboard or 150 mm, whichever
    is greater; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is marked with the dredging load line placed
    directly below the deck line so that its upper edge marks the
    assigned dredging freeboard and the letters "WD" are marked forward
    of this line.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (1)(d), (2)(d) and (3)(d), the
  Minister may issue a certificate if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking is placed so that the freeboards are
    greater than those required by paragraph (1)(d), (2)(d) or (3)(d),
    as the case may be; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a passenger vessel, it is marked in
    accordance with the [Vessel Construction and Equipment
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-257) or paragraph 22(2)(c) of
    the [Hull Construction
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1431), as the case may be.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (6)(b), the Minister may issue a
  certificate that includes a dredging load line if the dredging load
  line is placed so that the dredging freeboard is greater than that
  required by that paragraph.

### [Authorized Representative's Duties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742376 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a vessel that holds an International Load
  Line Certificate or a Local Load Line Certificate shall ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions of assignment that were required to
    be met for the issuance of the certificate are met and the vessel is
    maintained in accordance with the requirements of a classification
    society;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is marked as it was required to be
    marked for the issuance of the certificate or as it may be marked
    under subsection 5(7) or (8);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the certificate is endorsed as required by the
    Convention;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} no material alterations take place in the hull or
    superstructure of the vessel that would necessitate the assignment
    of an increased freeboard; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is operated only as intended, in the
    case of a Local Load Line Certificate.

### [Adjustments to Load Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742402 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  vessel is in fresh water of a specific gravity of 1.0, the applicable
  load line is adjusted by the fresh water allowance that is specified
  in the vessel's load line certificate and determined in accordance
  with the 1966 Convention, the Convention or the Rules, as the case may
  be. However, if the vessel is in water of a specific gravity that is
  greater than 1.0 but less than 1.025, the applicable load line is
  adjusted by the following formula:

  ::: {#742405 .Subsection}
  \[(1.025 -- A)/0.025\] × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   equals the actual specific gravity; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   equals the fresh water allowance.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new vessel that is in the North American Great
    Lakes Zone; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an existing vessel that is in the North American
    Great Lakes Zone during the period starting on September 16 in any
    year and ending on April 30 in the next year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When a vessel departs from a place situated on a
  river or on inland waters to the sea, the applicable load line is
  raised to a level that accounts for the weight of fuel and all other
  materials required for consumption between the point of departure and
  the sea.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in the North American Great Lakes Zone; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in the St. Lawrence River Seasonal Area, if the
    vessel is engaged on a voyage wholly within the limits of the North
    American Great Lakes Zone and the St. Lawrence River Seasonal Area.

### [Dredging Load Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-742416 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The dredging load line on
an open-hopper dredge is the applicable load line when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dredge is operating not more than 20 nautical
  miles from the mouth of a harbour of safe refuge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the height of waves in the area of operation is not
  more than 3 m or the wind velocity in the area is not more than
  65 km/h; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dredge is carrying dredged material with a
  specific gravity that is not greater than the highest specific gravity
  of dredged material that the dredge is designed to dredge.

        openings to a single cargo space. ([bâtiment du type
        A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Type B vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Type B vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is not a
      Type A vessel. ([bâtiment du type B]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[weathertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[weathertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means designed to prevent
      water from penetrating a vessel in any sea condition. ([étanche
      aux intempéries]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Stability and other Information]{.HTitleText1} {#stability-and-other-information .SchedHeadL1}

- **2** A vessel that carries bulk or liquid cargo shall carry the
  information required by regulation 7.2 of Chapter VI of the
  International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974 and the
  Protocol of 1988 relating to that Convention, as amended from time to
  time.

### [Access Openings]{.HTitleText1} {#access-openings .SchedHeadL1}

- **3** Access openings in bulkheads at the ends of enclosed
  superstructures shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be fitted with efficiently constructed weathertight
    doors that are permanently attached to the bulkhead so that the
    whole structure is at least equivalent in strength to a bulkhead
    with no openings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have means for securing the doors weathertight that
    are permanently attached to the bulkhead or the doors and are
    operable from both sides of the bulkhead; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have sills at least 300 mm above the deck.

### [Hatchways]{.HTitleText1} {#hatchways .SchedHeadL1}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Hatchways shall have efficiently constructed
    coamings

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 460 mm above the deck, in the case of
      coamings in Position 1; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 300 mm above the deck, in the case of
      coamings in Position 2.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the hatchway is
    fitted with a watertight cover.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except for open-hopper dredges, hatchways in
    Position 1 or Position 2 shall be fitted with efficiently
    constructed weathertight hatchway covers.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} If weathertight covers on hatchways are made of
    mild steel, the maximum allowable stress shall be calculated using
    the following loads:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels that are 110 m in length
      or over, a load not less than 12 kPa on hatchways in Position 1
      and not less than 9.6 kPa on hatchways in Position 2;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels that are 24 m in length, a
      load not less than 9.6 kPa on hatchways in Position 1 and not less
      than 7.2 kPa on hatchways in Position 2; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels of intermediate length, a
      load obtained by interpolation from the loads referred to in
      paragraphs (a) and (b).

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The maximum allowable stress calculated for the
    loads in subsection (4) shall not exceed the minimum ultimate
    strength of the material using a safety factor of 4.25.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Hatchway covers in Position 1 or Position 2 shall
    be so designed as to limit deflection to not more than 0.0028 times
    the span under the loads referred to in subsection (4). Mild steel
    plating that forms the tops of the covers shall not be thinner than
    one per cent of the spacing between stiffeners or 6 mm, whichever is
    greater.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Coamings and hatchway covers of exposed hatchways
    on decks above the superstructure deck shall be efficiently
    constructed.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} The strength and stiffness of hatchway covers made
    of materials other than mild steel shall be at least equivalent to
    those of mild steel.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} The means for securing and maintaining the
    weathertightness of hatchway covers shall be such that the
    weathertightness can be maintained in any sea conditions.

### [Cargo Ports and other Similar Openings]{.HTitleText1} {#cargo-ports-and-other-similar-openings .SchedHeadL1}

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cargo ports and other similar openings in the
    shell of a vessel below the freeboard deck shall be fitted with
    watertight doors that are as strong as the structure to which they
    are attached.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Cargo ports and other similar openings above the
    freeboard deck shall be fitted with weathertight doors that are as
    strong as the structure to which they are attached.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The lower edges of cargo ports and other similar
    openings shall not be below a line drawn parallel to the freeboard
    deck at side that has the upper edge of the uppermost load line at
    its lowest point.

### [Machinery Space Openings]{.HTitleText1} {#machinery-space-openings .SchedHeadL1}

- - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Machinery space openings in Position 1 or
    Position 2 shall be enclosed by efficiently constructed steel
    casings.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Access openings in the casings required by
    subsection (1) shall

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be fitted with efficiently constructed
      weathertight covers that are permanently attached and operable
      from both sides of the opening and have their lower edge at least
      300 mm above the deck;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the opening is a doorway, meet the
      requirements set out in section 3; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the opening is a funnel or machinery space
      ventilator that needs to be kept open for the essential operation
      of the vessel, be fitted with a coaming at a height above the deck
      of at least

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 3.8 m in Position 1, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1.8 m in Position 2.

### [Air Pipes]{.HTitleText1} {#air-pipes .SchedHeadL1}

- - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If air pipes to tanks extend above the
    freeboard deck or superstructure decks, the exposed parts of the
    pipes shall be efficiently constructed and the height from the deck
    to the point on the pipe where water may downflood shall be at least
    760 mm on the freeboard deck, 600 mm on raised quarter-decks and
    300 mm on other superstructure decks.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Air pipes shall be fitted with permanently attached
    means of closing.

### [Ventilators]{.HTitleText1} {#ventilators .SchedHeadL1}

- - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Ventilators and their coamings in Position 1
    or Position 2 serving spaces below freeboard decks, decks of
    enclosed superstructures or trunk decks shall be efficiently
    constructed.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height of ventilator coamings shall be at least
    760 mm above the deck in Position 1 and at least 600 mm above the
    deck in Position 2.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Ventilator openings shall have permanently attached
    weathertight means of closing.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of
    ventilators in Position 1 with coamings that extend 3.8 m or more
    above the deck or to ventilators in Position 2 with coamings that
    extend 1.8 m or more above the deck.

### [Side Scuttles]{.HTitleText1} {#side-scuttles .SchedHeadL1}

- - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Side scuttles to spaces below the freeboard
    deck or to spaces within enclosed superstructures shall be fitted
    with hinged inside deadlights so that they can be closed watertight.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sill of each side scuttle shall be above a line
    that is drawn parallel to the freeboard deck at side and has its
    lowest point 2.5% of the breadth above the summer fresh water load
    line or 500 mm above that line, whichever is the greater distance.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The side scuttles and deadlights shall be
    efficiently constructed.

### [Miscellaneous Openings in Freeboard, Superstructure and Trunk Decks]{.HTitleText1} {#miscellaneous-openings-in-freeboard-superstructure-and-trunk-decks .SchedHeadL1}

- - **10** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Manholes and flush scuttles in Position 1 or
    Position 2 or within a superstructure other than an enclosed
    superstructure shall have watertight covers.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Openings in freeboard decks other than hatchways,
    machinery space openings, manholes and flush scuttles shall be
    protected by

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an enclosed superstructure; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an efficiently constructed and weathertight
      deckhouse or companionway.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Openings in an exposed part of a superstructure
    deck or in the top of a deckhouse on the freeboard deck that give
    access to a space below the freeboard deck or within an enclosed
    superstructure shall be protected in accordance with subsection (2).

### [Freeing Ports]{.HTitleText1} {#freeing-ports .SchedHeadL1}

- - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This section applies in respect of wells
    that could collect water and are formed by bulwarks on the exposed
    parts of the freeboard deck or superstructure decks.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the sheer in way of the well is standard sheer
    or greater than standard sheer, drainage shall be provided by way of
    freeing ports with an area on each side of the vessel of at least

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "A" for each well on the freeboard deck or on the
      raised quarter-deck; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one-half of "A" for each well on superstructure
      decks other than raised quarter-decks.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The value of "A" is calculated in square metres as
    follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the length of the bulwark in way of the well
      is 20 m or less, 0.7 plus 0.035 times the length of that bulwark;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 0.07 times the lesser of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the length of the bulwark in way of the well,
        and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 70 per cent of the length of the vessel.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The freeing port area required by subsection (2)
    shall be increased by 0.04 m^2^ per metre of length of the well for
    each metre that the height of the bulwark exceeds

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 600 mm, in the case of vessels that are 73 m in
      length or less;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 1 200 mm, in the case of vessels that are 146 m
      in length or more; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels that are of intermediate
      length, the height obtained by linear interpolation between the
      heights set out in paragraphs (a) and (b).

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of vessels greater than 146 m in length
    with an average height of bulwark of less than 900 mm, the freeing
    port area required by subsections (2) and (4) shall be decreased by
    0.04 m^2^ per metre of length of well for each metre by which the
    average height of the bulwark is less than 900 mm.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} The freeing port area required by subsections
    (2), (4) and (5) shall be increased by

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels with no sheer, 50 per
      cent;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels with standard sheer, zero
      per cent; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of vessels with less than standard
      sheer, the percentage obtained by linear interpolation between the
      percentages set out in paragraphs (a) and (b).

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel does not have guardrails in way of a
    trunk on an exposed part of the freeboard deck or has continuous
    hatchway side coamings between detached superstructures, the freeing
    port area shall be at least

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20 per cent of the total area of the bulwarks if
      the breadth of the trunk or hatchways is 40 per cent or less of
      the breadth of the vessel;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10 per cent of the total area of the bulwarks if
      the breadth of the trunk or hatchways is 75 per cent or more of
      the breadth of the vessel; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of the total area of the bulwarks
      obtained by linear interpolation between the percentages set out
      in paragraphs (a) and (b) if the breadth of the trunk or hatchways
      is more than 40 per cent but less than 75 per cent of the breadth
      of the vessel.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} In superstructures that are open at either or both
    ends, the minimum freeing port area in way of wells shall be
    determined in accordance with the recommendation set out in
    interpretation LL.60 of the Unified interpretations of the
    provisions of the International Convention on Load Lines, 1966.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} The lower edges of the freeing ports shall be as
    near the deck as practicable.

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} Two thirds of the required freeing port area for
    each well shall be located in the half of the well nearest the
    lowest point of the sheer curve.

  - [(11)]{.lawlabel} Shutters that are fitted to freeing ports shall
    have

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ample clearance to prevent jamming; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hinges that have pins or bearings of material
      that is resistant to corrosion.

### [Scuppers, Drains, Inlets and Discharges]{.HTitleText1} {#scuppers-drains-inlets-and-discharges .SchedHeadL1}

- - **12** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every discharge pipe passing through the
    shell from spaces below the freeboard deck shall have

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an automatic non-return valve fitted at the shell
      with a positive means of closing that is operable

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from above the freeboard deck, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from a readily accessible location if the
        discharge originates in a space that is crewed or equipped with
        a means of continuously monitoring the level of bilge water; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two automatic non-return valves, one of which is
      fitted at the shell and one inboard that is accessible for
      examination when the vessel is in service.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every discharge pipe that passes through the shell
    from within an enclosed superstructure, or from within a deckhouse
    or companionway required by subsection 10(2), shall

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in paragraph (1)(a)
      or (b); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an automatic non-return valve fitted at the
      shell, if the discharge originates in a space that is regularly
      visited by the crew.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every scupper, drain or discharge pipe that passes
    through the shell above the summer fresh water load line at a
    distance that is less than the greater of 5 per cent of the breadth
    and 600 mm shall have an automatic non-return valve fitted at the
    shell.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of a
    scupper, drain or discharge pipe that originates above the freeboard
    deck if the part of the pipe that is between the shell and the
    freeboard deck is efficiently constructed.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every scupper pipe that leads from a superstructure
    other than an enclosed superstructure, a deckhouse or a companionway
    required by subsection 10(2) shall drain overboard.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} In crewed machinery spaces, every main and
    auxiliary sea inlet and discharge necessary for the operation of
    machinery shall have a valve with a positive means of closing that
    can be controlled locally.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} The valves required by this section to have
    positive means of closing shall have indicators at the operating
    position to show whether the valve is open or closed.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} The pipes to which this section refers shall be
    efficiently constructed.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} The shell fittings and the valves required by this
    section shall be efficiently constructed.

### [Protection of the Crew]{.HTitleText1} {#protection-of-the-crew .SchedHeadL1}

- - **13** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The deckhouses used for the accommodation of
    the crew shall be efficiently constructed.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} All exposed parts of the freeboard and
    superstructure decks shall be fitted with guardrails or bulwarks
    that are at least 900 mm in height.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Guardrails shall be fitted

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in at least three courses in which the space
      between the lowest course and the deck does not exceed 230 mm and
      the other courses are not spaced more than 380 mm apart; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the sheer strake projects at least 200 mm
      above the deck, in at least two courses in which the space between
      the lower course and the sheer strake or the upper course does not
      exceed 380 mm.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Guardrail supports shall be placed on the flat of
    the deck on vessels with rounded gunwales.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Vessels shall have lifelines, gangways or underdeck
    passages for the protection of the crew while passing to and from
    their accommodation spaces, the machinery space and all other spaces
    used in the normal operation of the vessel.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} Whenever bulkhead openings are closed, other access
    shall be provided for the crew to reach accommodation spaces or
    machinery or other working spaces in enclosed superstructures that
    are bridges or poops.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} If an exposed part of a freeboard deck is in way of
    a trunk, guardrails that meet the requirements of subsection (3)
    shall be fitted for at least half the length of the exposed part.

### [Special Conditions of Assignment for Type A Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#special-conditions-of-assignment-for-type-a-vessels .SchedHeadL1}

- **14** Type A vessels shall meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} machinery casings shall have bulkheads with no
    openings on all sides on the freeboard deck unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the casings are protected by an enclosed poop or
      bridge or efficiently constructed deckhouse of at least standard
      height, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the openings meet the requirements set out in
      section 3 and lead to a space or passageway that is as strong as
      the casing and from which a second interior access opening that
      meets the requirements set out in section 3 is provided for access
      to the engine room;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless there is fore and aft access below the
    freeboard deck, a permanent fore and aft gangway shall be fitted at
    the superstructure deck level between the poop and all other
    deckhouses used in the normal operation of the vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hatchways on the exposed freeboard or forecastle
    deck shall have efficiently constructed watertight covers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} vessels shall have guardrails fitted for at least
    half the length of the exposed parts of the weather deck; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if superstructures are connected by trunks,
    guardrails shall be fitted for the whole length of the exposed parts
    of the freeboard deck.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#interpretation-1 .SchedHeadL1}

- - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this
    Schedule.

    [[after perpendicular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[after perpendicular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
        perpendicular taken at the after end of the length of a
        vessel. ([perpendiculaire arrière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[depth for freeboard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[depth for freeboard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
        [[D]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the distance equal to the
        moulded depth amidships plus the thickness of the stringer
        plate, with no allowance for sheathing. However, in the case of
        a vessel that does not have right-angled gunwales or that has
        rounded gunwales with a radius greater than four per cent of the
        breadth, that distance shall be adjusted so that the area of the
        topside section is equal to that of a vessel with right-angled
        gunwales and with the same round of beam. ([creux de
        franc-bord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [D]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[effective length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[effective length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
        [[E]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an enclosed superstructure that
          is not a raised quarter-deck,

          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of Hs or more, the
            length of the superstructure, or

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of less than Hs, the
            length of the superstructure times the quotient of the
            height of the superstructure divided by Hs;

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an enclosed superstructure that
          is a raised quarter-deck, the lesser of 0.6 L and

          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of 2/3 Hs or more, the
            length of the superstructure, or

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of less than 2/3 Hs,
            the length of the superstructure times the quotient of the
            height of the superstructure divided by Hs; and

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a trunk,

          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of Hs or more, the
            length of the trunk times the quotient of the mean breadth
            of the trunk divided by the breadth, or

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it has a height of less than Hs, the
            length of the trunk times the quotient of the mean breadth
            of the trunk divided by the breadth times the quotient of
            the height of trunk divided by Hs. ([longueur
            effective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
            [E]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[length of the superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[length of the superstructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
        [[S]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a superstructure of a
        vessel, means the length of the part of the superstructure that
        is between the forward perpendicular and the after perpendicular
        of the vessel. ([longueur de la superstructure]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"} ou [S]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[summer draught]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[summer draught]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the distance
        measured from the top of the keel of a vessel to the upper edge
        of the load line that would mark the vessel's summer freeboard
        referred to in subsection 8(1). ([tirant d'eau
        d'été]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[trunk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[trunk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an efficiently constructed
        structure that is on a freeboard deck of a vessel and has a
        breadth of less than 92 per cent of the breadth of the
        vessel. ([trunk]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Schedule, all length and
    height measurements shall be in metres and taken to three decimal
    places.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Words and expressions used in this Schedule and
    defined in section 1 of Schedule 1 have the same meaning as in that
    section.

### [Basic Freeboard Calculation]{.HTitleText1} {#basic-freeboard-calculation .SchedHeadL1}

- - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The basic freeboard in millimetres shall be

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 850 × p~1~ × D, in the case of a Type A vessel;
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 1000 × p~1~ × D, in the case of a Type B vessel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), p~1~ is equal to p + (L/D --
    L/D~s~) × A for vessels that are 122 m or less in length and equal
    to p for vessels that are more than 122 m in length where

    [p]{.dfn}
    :   is the value set out in column 2 of an item of Table 1 to this
        section for a length set out in column 1 or determined for a
        length that is intermediate to two lengths set out in column 1
        of consecutive items to Table 1 by linear interpolation between
        those lengths;

    [L/D]{.dfn}
    :   is the ratio of length to depth for freeboard except that it
        shall not be more than
        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 15 if L is 122 m or less;

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 21 if L is 214 m or more; or

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ratio obtained for intermediate lengths
          by linear interpolation between the ratios set out in
          paragraphs (a) and (b);

    [L/D~s~]{.dfn}
    :   is the value set out in column 2 of an item of Table 2 to this
        section for a length set out in column 1 or determined for a
        length that is intermediate to two lengths set out in column 1
        of consecutive items to Table 2 by linear interpolation between
        those lengths; and

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the value set out in column 2 of an item of Table 3 to this
        section for a length set out in column 1 or determined for a
        length that is intermediate to two lengths set out in column 1
        of consecutive items to Table 3 by linear interpolation between
        those lengths.

### [Correction for Type B Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#correction-for-type-b-vessels .SchedHeadL1}

- **3** The basic freeboard for a Type B vessel that is less than 153 m
  in length and has enclosed superstructures with an effective length of
  not more than a quarter of the length of the vessel shall be increased
  by b millimetres where

  ::: {#742733 .Section}
  b = 2.5 × (153 -- L) × (0.25 -- E/L)
  :::

### [Correction for Superstructures and Trunks]{.HTitleText1} {#correction-for-superstructures-and-trunks .SchedHeadL1}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the sum of the effective lengths of the
    enclosed superstructures equals L, the basic freeboard shall be
    reduced by x millimetres where

    ::: {#742738 .Subsection}
    x = (Hs × 500)
    :::

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the sum of the effective lengths of the enclosed
    superstructures and trunks in respect of which the requirements set
    out in subsection (3) are met is less than L, the basic freeboard
    shall be reduced by x millimetres where

    ::: {#742741 .Subsection}
    x = (E/2 L) × (1 + E/L) × (Hs × 500)
    :::

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The requirements referred to in subsection (2) are
    the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trunk shall be at least as strong as an
      enclosed superstructure;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no hatchways shall be in the freeboard deck in
      way of the trunk other than small access openings with watertight
      covers;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the trunk deck shall be a permanent working
      platform fitted with guardrails;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the trunk is detached, it shall be connected
      to the adjacent superstructures by permanent gangways;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} guardrails shall be fitted in way of the trunk on
      the exposed parts of the freeboard deck for at least half the
      length of exposed parts of the trunk;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the trunk shall have a breadth at least 60 per
      cent of the breadth of the vessel; and

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if there is no superstructure on the vessel, the
      length of the trunk shall be at least 60 per cent of its length.

### [Correction for Sheer]{.HTitleText1} {#correction-for-sheer .SchedHeadL1}

- **5** Correction in the basic freeboard for sheer shall be determined
  in accordance with regulation 38 of the 1966 Convention. However, for
  the purpose of the determination, L shall be the lesser of the length
  of the vessel and 153 m.

### [Correction for Bow Height]{.HTitleText1} {#correction-for-bow-height .SchedHeadL1}

- - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this
    section.

    [[assumed bow height]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[assumed bow height]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
        vessel, means the vertical distance at the forward perpendicular
        between

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the top of the exposed deck at side; and

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the water line that corresponds to the basic
          freeboard, as corrected in accordance with sections 3 to 6,
          where applicable, including any designed trim. ([hauteur
          d'étrave présumée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[standard bow height]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[standard bow height]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 49.417 L × (1 -- L/500) mm for a vessel of
          less than 168 m in length; or

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 8677 -- 18.917 L mm for a vessel of 168 m or
          more in length. ([hauteur d'étrave normale]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if the assumed bow
    height is less than the standard bow height, the basic freeboard
    shall be increased by the difference.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of the definition [[assumed bow
    height]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, the top of the exposed deck at side is
    deemed not to include sheer or the height of a superstructure unless

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of sheer, it extends for at least 15
      per cent of the length of the vessel measured from the forward
      perpendicular; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the height of a superstructure,
      the superstructure is enclosed and extends from the stem to a
      point at least 0.06 L abaft the forward perpendicular.

### [Correction for Deck Line]{.HTitleText1} {#correction-for-deck-line .SchedHeadL1}

- - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the position of the upper edge of the deck
    line is placed in accordance with paragraph 2(b) of Schedule 3 above
    the position established by paragraph 2(a) of that Schedule, the
    basic freeboard shall be increased by the difference between those
    positions.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the position of the upper edge of the deck line
    is placed in accordance with paragraph 2(b) of Schedule 3 below the
    position established by paragraph 2(a) of that Schedule, the basic
    freeboard shall be decreased by the difference between those
    positions.

### [Fresh Water Freeboards]{.HTitleText1} {#fresh-water-freeboards .SchedHeadL1}

- - **8** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The summer freeboard that is applicable
    during the summer season in fresh water shall be the freeboard
    determined in accordance with sections 2 to 7.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The midsummer freeboard that is applicable during
    the midsummer season in fresh water shall be determined by deducting
    y millimetres from the summer freeboard where y is equal to the
    summer draught in metres multiplied by 25.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The intermediate freeboard that is applicable
    during the intermediate season in fresh water shall be determined by
    adding y millimetres to the summer freeboard where y is equal to the
    summer draught in metres multiplied by the quotient of 2540 divided
    by the greater of 122 m and L.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The winter freeboard that is applicable during the
    winter season in fresh water shall be determined by adding y
    millimetres to the summer freeboard where y is equal to the summer
    draught in metres multiplied by the quotient of 5080 divided by the
    greater of 122 m and L.

### [Salt Water Freeboards]{.HTitleText1} {#salt-water-freeboards .SchedHeadL1}

- - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Salt water freeboards that are applicable to
    vessels that operate in salt water shall be determined by adding y
    millimetres to the corresponding fresh water freeboards where y is
    equal to the displacement in fresh water in tonnes at the summer
    load waterline divided by the product of 4.1 times the tonnes per
    centimetre immersion in fresh water at the summer load waterline.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the displacement in fresh water in tonnes at the
    summer load waterline cannot be determined, one forty-eighth of the
    summer draught shall be added to the corresponding fresh water
    freeboards.

### [Minimum Freeboard]{.HTitleText1} {#minimum-freeboard .SchedHeadL1}

- **10** Despite sections 8 and 9, the freeboard other than the
  open-hopper dredge freeboard shall not be less than 50 mm.

### [Open-hopper Dredges]{.HTitleText1} {#open-hopper-dredges .SchedHeadL1}

- - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The dredging freeboard for open-hopper
    dredges shall be 62.5 per cent of the summer freeboard for a type B
    vessel or 150 mm, whichever is greater.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The dredging freeboard for an open-hopper dredge is
    applicable only when

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dredge is operating not more than 20 nautical
      miles from the mouth of a harbour of safe refuge;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the height of waves in the area of operation is
      not more than 3 m or the wind velocity in the area is not more
      than 65 km per hour; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dredge is carrying dredged material with a
      specific gravity that is not greater than the highest specific
      gravity of dredged material that the dredge is designed to dredge.

## [SCHEDULE 3]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 17(1)(d))]{.OriginatingRef}[Load Line Marks --- Diamond Load Lines]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-742792 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#interpretation-2 .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** Words and expressions used in this Schedule and defined in
  section 1 of Schedule 1 have the same meaning as in that section.

  ### [Deck Line]{.HTitleText1} {#deck-line .SchedHeadL1}

- **2** The upper edge of the deck line shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pass through the point where the continuation
    outwards of the upper surface of the freeboard deck intersects the
    outer surface of the shell, as illustrated in Figure 1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be placed above or below the position established
    in paragraph (a).

### [Load Line Diamond]{.HTitleText1} {#load-line-diamond .SchedHeadL1}

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A right-angled diamond as illustrated in
    Figure 2 shall be marked amidships below the deck line on each side
    of the vessel.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The diamond shall be

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marked with lines 25 mm wide with an outside
      diagonal measurement of 380 mm;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} intersected by a horizontal line that is 540 mm
      long and 25 mm wide and that has the midpoint of its upper edge
      coinciding with the midpoint of the diamond; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} placed so that its centre is at a distance below
      the upper edge of the deck line equal to the summer freeboard
      referred to in subsection 8(1) of Schedule 2.

### [Load Lines]{.HTitleText1} {#load-lines .SchedHeadL1}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Horizontal lines, to be known as fresh water
    load lines, indicating the assigned fresh water freeboards, shall be
    marked as illustrated in Figure 2. Each line shall be 230 mm long
    and 25 mm wide and extend forward of and at right angles to a
    vertical line that is 25 mm wide.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The vertical line referred to in subsection (1)
    shall be placed 660 mm forward of the vertical diagonal of the load
    line diamond.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The midsummer fresh water load line shall be placed
    so that its upper edge marks the assigned midsummer fresh water
    freeboard and the letters "MS" shall be marked forward of this line.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The summer fresh water load line shall be placed so
    that its upper edge marks the assigned summer fresh water freeboard
    and the letter "S" shall be marked forward of this line.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The intermediate fresh water load line shall be
    placed so that its upper edge marks the assigned intermediate fresh
    water freeboard and the letter "I" shall be marked forward of this
    line.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} The winter fresh water load line shall be placed so
    that its upper edge marks the assigned winter fresh water freeboard
    and the letter "W" shall be marked forward of this line.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} If an open-hopper dredge has been assigned a
    dredging freeboard, the dredging fresh water load line shall be
    placed directly below the deck line so that its upper edge marks the
    assigned dredging fresh water freeboard and the letters "WD" shall
    be marked forward of this line.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel has been assigned salt water
    freeboards, salt water load lines shall be marked as illustrated in
    Figure 2. Each line shall be 230 mm long and 25 mm wide, extend
    abaft the vertical line, be placed so that its upper edges mark the
    appropriate assigned salt water freeboards and be marked with
    letters in the same fashion as for the fresh water load lines but
    placed abaft the salt water load lines.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} If salt water load lines are marked, the letters
    "SW" shall be marked above these lines and the letters "FW" shall be
    marked above the fresh water load lines.

### [Details of Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#details-of-marking .SchedHeadL1}

- **5** All load line marks shall be permanently marked on both sides of
  the vessel in white or yellow on a dark background or in black on a
  light background.
