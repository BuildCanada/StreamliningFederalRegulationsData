## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726877 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01).

## [Record Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726879 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 145(1) of the Act, except paragraphs 145(1)(b)
  and (c), the prescribed period for the record date is not more than 60
  days before the day on which the particular action is to be taken.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 145(1)(b) and (c) of
  the Act, the prescribed period for the record date is not less than 21
  days and not more than 60 days before the day of the meeting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 145(3) of the Act, the
  prescribed period for the notice of the record date to be given is not
  less than seven days before the date fixed.

## [Notice of Meeting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726884 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 146(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the notice to be
sent is not less than 21 days and not more than 60 days before the day
of the meeting.

## [Member Proposals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726886 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 152(2) of the Act, the proposal and the statement in support
of it together are not to exceed 500 words.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 152(3)(a) of the Act, the prescribed number of days is 90
days.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 152(3)(c) of the Act, the prescribed period is two years.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 152(3)(d) of the Act, the prescribed minimum
  amount of support for a proposal is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 3% of the total number of votes cast if the
    proposal was introduced at an annual meeting;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 6% of the total number of votes cast at its last
    submission if the proposal was introduced at two annual meetings;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the total number of votes cast at its last
    submission if the proposal was introduced at three or more annual
    meetings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 152(3)(d) of the Act,
  the prescribed period is five years.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 153(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the person to be
notified is 21 days after the day on which the association receives the
proposal.

## [Electronic Voting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726897 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 160(6)(a) of the Act, when a vote is held at a meeting, the
voting may be carried out by means of a telephonic, electronic or other
communication facility, if the facility

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} enables the votes to be gathered in a manner that
  permits their subsequent verification; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permits the tallied votes to be presented to the
  association without it being possible for the association to identify
  how each member or shareholder or group of members or group of
  shareholders voted.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726901 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
