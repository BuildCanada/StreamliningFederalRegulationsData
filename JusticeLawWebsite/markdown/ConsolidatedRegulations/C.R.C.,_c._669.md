## Accountable Education and Travel Advance Regulations (Dependants of Members of the Canadian Forces) {#accountable-education-and-travel-advance-regulations-dependants-of-members-of-the-canadian-forces .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 669
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Respecting the Making of Accountable Advances of Public
Funds to Officers and Men of the Canadian Forces for Education and
Travel Expenses of Dependants
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560224 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Accountable Education and Travel Advance Regulations
(Dependants of Members of the Canadian
Forces)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._669).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560226 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
officers and men of the Canadian Forces.

## [Authorization of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560228 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Chief of the Defence
Staff may authorize the making of accountable advances of public funds
to officers and men of the Canadian Forces for the expenses of travel
and education of dependants when the expenses are authorized within
existing regulations.

## [Limitations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560230 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Accountable advances made
under these Regulations shall be limited to amounts sufficient to meet
the estimated permissible expenses.

## [Settlement of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560232 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsections
31(2), (3) and (4) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the Chief of the Defence Staff may issue such
orders as are required to provide for the accountability and recovery of
advances issued pursuant to these Regulations.
