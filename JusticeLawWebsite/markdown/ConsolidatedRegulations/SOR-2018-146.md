## [Qualifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851464 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysts

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An individual must have the
following qualifications in order to be designated as an analyst under
section 130 of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a degree in a science related to the work to be
  carried out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} awarded by a Canadian university, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if awarded by a foreign university, that is
    recognized by a Canadian university or a Canadian professional
    association; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a combination, sufficient to perform the duties of
  the position, of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} work experience relevant to the duties of an
    analyst, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} formal education in a science related to the work
    to be carried out or other training related to that work.
