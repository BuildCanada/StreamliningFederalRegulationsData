## Credit Information (Insurance Companies) Regulations {#credit-information-insurance-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-11
:::

[INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/I-11.8)

Registration 1996-12-19

Regulations Respecting the Use by a Company or Foreign Company of
Information Supplied by its Customers

P.C. 1996-1914  1996-12-19

a company or foreign company of information supplied by its
customers](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-11).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003288 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Credit Information (Insurance Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-11).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003290 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit or charge card-related insurance,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} creditors' disability insurance,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creditors' life insurance,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} creditors' loss of employment insurance,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} creditors' vehicle inventory insurance,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} export credit insurance,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} mortgage insurance, or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} travel insurance; ([assurance
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit customer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit customer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person who
    has at any time applied for or received a loan from a company or
    foreign company, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loan from a company or foreign company to
      assist the person to purchase a policy, or a loan made on the
      security of a policy, that is issued by the company or foreign
      company,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan referred to in section 88 of the Canadian
      and British Insurance Companies Act, as that section read on May
      31, 1992, and, in the case of a loan referred to in paragraph (a)
      of that section, read without reference to the limits established
      in sections 94, 95 and 96 of that Act,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan, from a foreign company that, under the
      Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, was a British
      company, that the foreign company would have been authorized to
      vest in trust pursuant to subsection 209(2) of that Act and
      sections 1 to 3 of Schedule II to that Act, as those provisions
      read on May 31, 1992, subject to the limits referred to in
      paragraphs 5(a) and (b) of that Schedule,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a loan from a foreign company that the foreign
      company would have been authorized to vest in trust pursuant to
      subsection 28(2) of the Foreign Insurance Companies Act and
      sections 1 to 3 of the schedule to that Act, as those sections
      read on May 31, 1992, subject to the limits referred to in
      paragraphs 5(a) and (b) of that schedule, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a loan under the [National Housing
      Act](/eng/acts/N-11) or the Small Business Loans Act from a
      company or foreign company that is an approved lender under that
      Act; ([client du crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information in
    respect of a customer of a company or foreign company who is
    resident in Canada, an employee of such a customer who is resident
    in Canada, where the customer is an entity with members, any member
    of such a customer who is resident in Canada or where the customer
    is a partnership, any partner of such a customer who is resident in
    Canada, which information the company or foreign company has
    obtained

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by reason that the customer has applied for or
      received a loan from the company or foreign company, other than

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a loan from the company or foreign company to
        assist the person to purchase a policy, or a loan made on the
        security of a policy, that is issued by the company or foreign
        company,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a loan referred to in section 88 of the
        Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, as that section
        read on May 31, 1992 and, in the case of a loan referred to in
        paragraph (a) of that section, read without reference to the
        limits established in sections 94, 95 and 96 of that Act,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a loan, from a foreign company that, under
        the Canadian and British Insurance Companies Act, was a British
        company, that the foreign company would have been authorized to
        vest in trust pursuant to subsection 209(2) of that Act and
        sections 1 to 3 of Schedule II to that Act, as those provisions
        read on May 31, 1992, subject to the limits referred to in
        paragraphs 5(a) and (b) of that Schedule,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a loan from a foreign company that the foreign
        company would have been authorized to vest in trust pursuant to
        subsection 28(2) of the Foreign Insurance Companies Act and
        sections 1 to 3 of the schedule to that Act, as those sections
        read on May 31, 1992, subject to the limits referred to in
        paragraphs 5(a) and (b) of that schedule, or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a loan under the [National Housing
        Act](/eng/acts/N-11) or the Small Business Loans Act from a
        company or foreign company that is an approved lender under that
        Act,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by reason that the customer has applied for or
      uses a credit or charge card issued by the company or foreign
      company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} from a trust or loan corporation incorporated by
      or under an Act of the legislature of a province; ([renseignements
      sur le crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company or foreign company, means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to the holder of a credit
    or charge card issued by the company or foreign company as a feature
    of the card, without request and without an individual assessment of
    risk,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against loss of, or damage to, goods purchased
      with the card,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which the insurance company undertakes to
      extend a warranty provided by the manufacturer of goods purchased
      with the card, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} against any loss arising from a contractual
      liability assumed by the holder when renting a vehicle, when the
      rental is paid for with the card; ([assurance carte de crédit ou
      de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
    of a company or foreign company, means a group policy that will pay
    all or part of the amount of a debt to the company or foreign
    company, or to a loan company or bank that is an affiliate of the
    company or foreign company, in the event of bodily injury to, or
    illness or disability of,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a director
      or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the company, foreign company, loan
      company or bank; ([assurance-invalidité de
      crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    company or foreign company, means a group policy that will pay to
    the company or foreign company, or to a loan company or bank that is
    an affiliate of the company or foreign company, all or part of the
    amount of a debt or, where a debt is in respect of a small business
    or a farm, fishery or ranch, all or part of the amount of the credit
    limit of a line of credit, in the event of the death of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a director
      or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the company, foreign company, loan
      company or bank; ([assurance-vie de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company or foreign company, means a policy of an
    insurance company that will pay to the company or foreign company,
    or to a loan company or bank that is an affiliate of the company or
    foreign company, without an individual assessment of risk, all or
    part of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the company, foreign
    company, loan company or bank in the event that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      becomes involuntarily unemployed, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt becomes involuntarily unemployed; ([assurance
      crédit en cas de perte d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company or foreign company, means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance against direct and
    accidental loss of, or damage to, vehicles that are held in stock
    for display and sale purposes by a debtor of the company or foreign
    company, some or all of which have been financed by the company or
    foreign company; ([assurance crédit pour stocks de
    véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to an exporter of goods or
    services against a loss incurred by the exporter as a result of
    non-payment for exported goods or services; ([assurance crédit des
    exportateurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an insurance
    company that provides insurance severally in respect of a group of
    identifiable persons who individually hold certificates of
    insurance; ([police d'assurance collective]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company or foreign
    company or any other entity that is approved, registered or
    otherwise authorized to insure risks under an Act of Parliament or
    of the legislature of a province; ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commitment on the
    part of a company or foreign company to lend one or more amounts to
    a debtor, without a predetermined repayment schedule, where the
    aggregate amount outstanding does not exceed a predetermined credit
    limit, which limit does not exceed the reasonable credit needs of
    the debtor; ([marge de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body corporate to which
    the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies or a
    loan or trust corporation incorporated by or under an Act of the
    legislature of a province; ([société de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company
    or foreign company, means a policy that provides insurance to the
    company or foreign company, or to a loan company or bank that is an
    affiliate of the company or foreign company, against loss caused by
    a default on the part of a debtor who is a natural person under a
    loan from the company, foreign company, loan company or bank that is
    secured by real property or by an interest in real property;
    ([assurance hypothèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
    policy that provides insurance to a natural person whereby an
    insurance company undertakes to pay

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one or more sums of money in the event of bodily
      injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused by an
      accident, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certain sum for each day that the person is
      hospitalized in the event of bodily injury to the person that is
      caused by an accident or in the event of an illness or disability
      of the person; ([assurance accidents corporels]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business that is or,
    if it were incorporated, would be a small business corporation
    within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a policy of an insurance company that provides
      insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
      away from the place where the person ordinarily resides, without
      an individual assessment of risk, against

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss that results from the cancellation or
        interruption of the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss of, or damage to, personal property that
        occurs while on the trip, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} loss that is caused by the delayed arrival of
        personal baggage while on the trip, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group policy that provides insurance to a
      natural person in respect of a trip by the person away from the
      province in which the person ordinarily resides

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from an illness or disability of the person that
        occurs while on the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from bodily injury to, or the death of, the person
        that is caused by an accident that occurs while on the trip,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to
        pay one or more sums of money in the event of an illness or
        disability of the person that occurs while on the trip or in the
        event of bodily injury to, or the death of, the person that is
        caused by an accident that occurs while on the trip,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred by the person for
        dental care necessitated by an accident that occurs while on the
        trip, or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the person dies while on the
        trip, against expenses incurred for the return of that person's
        remains to the place where the person was ordinarily resident
        before death or for travel expenses incurred by a relative of
        that person who must travel to identify that person's remains.
        ([assurance voyage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Use of Credit Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003357 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No company or foreign
company shall use credit information to promote, in Canada, an insurance
company or insurance agent or broker unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company, foreign company, insurance company or
  insurance agent or broker deals only in authorized types of insurance;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside the offices of the
  company or foreign company and is directed to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued by
    the company or foreign company who receive regularly mailed
    statements of account,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all credit customers of the company or foreign
    company who receive regularly mailed statements of account, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  company or foreign company shall use credit information to promote, in
  Canada, a policy of an insurance company or of an insurance agent or
  broker, or a service in respect thereof, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policy is of an authorized type of insurance or
    the service is in respect of such a policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is a personal accident insurance policy,
    or the service is in respect of such a policy, and the promotion
    takes place outside the offices of the company or foreign company;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside the offices of
    the company or foreign company and is directed to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all holders of credit or charge cards issued by
      the company or foreign company who receive regularly mailed
      statements of account,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all credit customers of the company or foreign
      company who receive regularly mailed statements of account, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) and section 3, a
  company or foreign company may exclude from a promotion referred to in
  paragraph (1)(c) or 3(b) persons

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the promotion would contravene
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who have notified the company or foreign company in
    writing that they do not wish to receive promotional material from
    the company or foreign company; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who are holders of a credit or charge card that was
    issued by the company or foreign company and in respect of which the
    account is not in good standing.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  company or foreign company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly or indirectly provide an insurance company
    or insurance agent or broker with any credit information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit any of its subsidiaries to directly or
    indirectly provide an insurance company or insurance agent or broker
    with any credit information that it receives from the company or
    foreign company; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any of its affiliates that are not
    subsidiaries of the company or foreign company with credit
    information unless the company or foreign company ensures that the
    affiliate does not directly or indirectly provide the information to
    an insurance company or insurance agent or broker.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a company
  or foreign company where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company or foreign company has informed the
    insurance company or insurance agent or broker that the information
    is credit information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company or foreign company has established
    procedures to ensure that the credit information will not be used by
    the insurance company or insurance agent or broker to promote, in
    Canada, the insurance business of the insurance company or insurance
    agent or broker or to promote, in Canada, an insurance policy or a
    service in respect thereof; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company or insurance agent or broker
    has given an undertaking to the company or foreign company in a form
    acceptable to the Superintendent, that the credit information will
    not be used to promote, in Canada, the insurance business of the
    insurance company or insurance agent or broker or to promote, in
    Canada, an insurance policy or a service in respect thereof.
