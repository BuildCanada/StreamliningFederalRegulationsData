## Airport Traffic Regulations {#airport-traffic-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 886
:::

[GOVERNMENT PROPERTY TRAFFIC ACT](/eng/acts/G-6)

[DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORT ACT](/eng/acts/T-18)

Regulations Respecting the Control of Motor Vehicles, Pedestrians,
Aircraft and Equipment at Airports
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581066 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Airport Traffic
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._886).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581068 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area of land, water
    (including the frozen surface thereof) or other supporting surface
    used, designed, prepared, equipped or set apart for use either in
    whole or in part for the arrival, departure, movement or servicing
    of aircraft and includes any buildings, installations and equipment
    used in connection therewith; ([aérodrome]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any machine used or designed
    for navigation in the air but does not include a machine designed to
    derive support in the atmosphere from reactions against the earth's
    surface of air expelled from the machine;
    ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aerodrome in respect of
    which a Canadian aviation document issued pursuant to the
    [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) is in force;
    ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person in charge
    of an airport or the authorized representative of that person;
    ([directeur d'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any domestic animal and
    includes poultry; ([animal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of an airport, other
    than the manoeuvring area, intended to accommodate the loading and
    unloading of passengers and cargo, the refuelling, servicing,
    maintenance and parking of aircraft and the movement of aircraft,
    vehicles and pedestrians to allow execution of those functions;
    ([aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[apron traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apron traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all aircraft, vehicles,
    pedestrians and equipment utilizing the apron area of the airport;
    ([trafic de l'aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial passenger vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial passenger vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any taxi,
    bus or other vehicle used or intended for use in the transportation
    of persons for compensation; ([véhicule commercial pour
    passager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of a provincial or municipal police
      force, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person or a member of a class of persons
      authorized by the Minister to enforce these Regulations;
      ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cross-walk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cross-walk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any portion of a road, apron or other area
      designated by a sign or surface marking as a pedestrian crossing,
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that part of a road at an intersection that is
      included within the connections of the lateral lines of the
      sidewalks on opposite sides of the road or, in the absence of
      curbs, from the edges of the road; ([passage pour
      piétons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
    Transport; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is driving or is
    in actual physical control of a motor vehicle or bicycle;
    ([conducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intersection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intersection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area embraced within
    the prolongation or connection of the lateral curb lines or, in the
    absence of curb lines, of the lateral boundary lines of two or more
    roads that join one another at an angle whether or not one road
    crosses the other; ([carrefour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[land side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[land side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land side as defined in
    section 1 of the [Traffic on the Land Side of Airports
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-102); ([côté
    ville]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[loading area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loading area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any portion of an airport
    designated by a sign and made available to the public for loading or
    unloading; ([aire d'embarquement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manoeuvring area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manoeuvring area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of an
    airport ordinarily used for the take-off and landing of aircraft and
    for the movement of aircraft associated with the take-off and
    landing, but does not include the apron; ([aire de
    manoeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an automobile, over-snow
    vehicle, truck, bus or any self-propelled vehicle or device in, upon
    or by which a person or property is or may be transported, carried
    or conveyed upon land and includes a machine designed to derive
    support in the atmosphere from reactions against the earth's surface
    of air expelled from the machine; ([véhicule à
    moteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[over-snow vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[over-snow vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle that is
    capable of being propelled or driven otherwise than by muscular
    power, that runs on tracks or skis or on tracks and skis and that is
    designed for operation on snow or ice; ([autoneige]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a motor vehicle,
    means a person who holds legal title to it or a person in whose name
    it is or is required to be registered by the laws of a province and
    includes a conditional purchaser, lessee or mortgagor who is
    entitled to or is in possession of the motor vehicle;
    ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the standing of a motor vehicle
    whether occupied or not, except for the purpose of and while
    actually engaged in loading or unloading passengers or goods;
    ([laisser en stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parking area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a part of an airport
    property that is designated by a sign or by surface markings as an
    area for parking motor vehicles; ([parc de
    stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parking meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parking meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/92-120, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[parking permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parking permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued by the
    airport manager that authorizes the permit holder to park a motor
    vehicle in a specific parking area in accordance with the terms of
    the permit; ([permis de stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parking space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parking space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a space for parking
    motor vehicles that is located in a parking area and that, in most
    cases, is delineated by surface markings; ([place de
    stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pay-in-advance parking control system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pay-in-advance parking control system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a system of parking control whereby the person who parks a motor
    vehicle in a parking space is required, immediately before or after
    parking the motor vehicle, to pay a fee in respect of the period of
    time during which the person anticipates that the motor vehicle will
    remain in that parking space; ([péage anticipé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[pedestrian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pedestrian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person on foot and
    includes an invalid or child in a wheelchair or carriage;
    ([piéton]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area at an
    aerodrome that is designated by a sign as an area to which access is
    restricted to persons authorized by the airport manager; ([zone
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any highway, street or place
    designed and intended for or used for the passage of motor vehicles;
    ([route]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sidewalk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sidewalk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of a road or apron
    that is constructed for the use of pedestrians;
    ([trottoir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special parking permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special parking permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued
    by the airport manager that authorizes the permit holder to park a
    motor vehicle in such place and in accordance with such terms as are
    specified by the airport manager. ([permis spécial de
    stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[valid identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[valid identification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document issued
    or approved by the airport manager authorizing the holder to have
    access to a restricted area of the airport. ([pièce d'identité
    valable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

respect of an area other than the land side of an airport set out in
Schedule I or II.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581113 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may authorize
any person or class of persons to act as a constable to enforce these
Regulations.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Motor Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581115 .Part}

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581116 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a motor vehicle on an airport unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he holds all licences and permits that he is, by
    the laws of the province and the municipality in which the airport
    is situated, required to hold in order to operate the motor vehicle
    in that province and municipality; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is registered and equipped as
    required by the laws of the province and the municipality in which
    the airport is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, a provincial
  certificate of motor vehicle registration shall be [prima
  facie]{.otherLang lang="la"} proof of ownership of the motor vehicle.

### [Compliance with Provincial and Municipal Laws]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581122 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a motor vehicle on an airport otherwise than in
  accordance with the laws of the province and the municipality in which
  the airport is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Part, the expression "laws of the province
  and the municipality" does not include laws that are inconsistent with
  or repugnant to any of the provisions of the [Government Property
  Traffic Act](/eng/acts/G-6) or these Regulations.

### [Traffic Signs and Devices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581126 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may mark or erect or cause to be marked or erected on any
  airport traffic signs or devices

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribing the rate of speed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulating or prohibiting parking and designating
    parking, loading or restricted areas;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribing load limits for any motor vehicle or
    class of motor vehicles;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting or regulating the use of any road or
    place by any motor vehicle or class of motor vehicles, or by persons
    or animals;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} designating any road as a one-way road;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} stopping motor vehicles;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} regulating pedestrian traffic; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} directing or controlling in any other manner
    traffic on the airport.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as authorized by subsection (1), no person
  shall mark or erect any traffic sign or device on any airport.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 3(F)
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any traffic sign or device
on any airport bearing the words "Department of Transport", "Ministère
des Transports", or an abbreviation thereof, or purporting to have been
erected by or under the authority of the Minister, shall be deemed
[prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"} to have been erected pursuant to
this Part.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The driver of a motor
vehicle on an airport shall obey the instruction of any traffic sign or
device applicable to that driver or motor vehicle.

### [Traffic Direction and Control]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581142 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, on an
airport, drive

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a motor vehicle other than an over-snow vehicle,
  except on a road; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an over-snow vehicle except with the permission of
  the airport manager.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  driver of a motor vehicle on an airport shall comply with any traffic
  directions given to him by a constable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A constable may, for the purpose of ensuring the
  orderly movement of all traffic on an airport, direct or control such
  traffic.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every person on an
airport shall produce to a constable upon demand

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any permit issued to him under this Part;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any licence or permit he holds authorizing him to
  drive a motor vehicle; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any certificate of registration referred to in
  subsection 5(2).

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every driver of a motor
vehicle who is directly or indirectly involved in an accident on an
airport shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} report the accident forthwith as required by the laws
  of the province in which the accident occurred; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if any property of Her Majesty is damaged by the
  accident, report the accident forthwith to a member of the Royal
  Canadian Mounted Police or the airport manager.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate
or park a motor vehicle

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on an apron unless he has the permission of the
  airport manager; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any area designated by a sign as a restricted area
  unless he is in possession of valid identification.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where an apron traffic
control unit, as defined in Part IV, has been established at an airport,
the operator of a motor vehicle on the apron shall acknowledge and obey
any instruction received from the unit.

### [Parking]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581160 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
motor vehicle in any area designated by a sign as an area in which
parking is prohibited.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, without
the permission of the Minister, park a motor vehicle on any portion of
an airport that is grassed or that is not intended for the use of motor
vehicles.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
motor vehicle in a place designated by a sign as a loading area.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No driver of a commercial
passenger vehicle shall load or unload passengers at an airport except
in an area designated by a sign as an area in which passenger loading or
unloading is permitted.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where an area is
designated by a sign as an area in which parking is reserved for persons
who hold a parking permit or special parking permit, as the case may be,
no person shall park a motor vehicle in that area unless the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a parking permit or special parking permit that
  authorizes the person to park in that area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an identifying label or ticket has been issued
  with the parking permit or special parking permit, affixes the label
  to the lower corner of the driver's side of the windshield of the
  motor vehicle, or displays the ticket on the driver's side of the
  dashboard of the motor vehicle in such a manner that it is clearly
  visible; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} parks the motor vehicle in accordance with the terms
  of the parking permit or special parking permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 4
:::

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where an area is
designated by a sign as an area in which parking is permitted for a
period of time, no person shall park a motor vehicle in that area for
any greater period of time than that indicated on the sign.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where an area is
designated by a sign as an area in which parking is prohibited during
certain hours, no person shall park a motor vehicle in that area during
the hours when parking is prohibited.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Where a parking area is
designated by a sign or surface markings as an area in which parking is
permitted only in parking spaces, no person shall park a motor vehicle
in a parking space in the parking area unless the person pays for the
use of the parking space in accordance with the system of parking
control in effect in that part of the parking area in which the motor
vehicle is to be parked.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No person who has parked
a motor vehicle in a parking space that is governed by a pay-in-advance
parking control system shall allow the motor vehicle to remain parked in
that parking space for a period of time greater than the period of time
for which a fee has been paid in respect of that parking space.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an area is designated by a sign as an area in which parking is
  reserved for handicapped persons, no person shall park a motor vehicle
  in that area unless the motor vehicle is identified by a sign as a
  motor vehicle used for the transport of a handicapped person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an area is designated by a sign as an area in
  which parking is reserved for certain persons or a particular class of
  persons, other than handicapped persons, no person shall park a motor
  vehicle in that area unless the person is one of those persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
motor vehicle at an airport in such a manner as to obstruct traffic.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 28,
where a parking area is designated by a sign or surface markings as an
area in which parking is permitted only in delineated parking spaces, no
person shall park a motor vehicle in that area unless the motor vehicle
is parked wholly within a delineated parking space.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Where a person parks a
motor vehicle that is longer or wider than one delineated parking space
so that it wholly or partially occupies more than one delineated parking
space, the person shall pay the fee that is required in respect of each
parking space wholly or partially occupied by the motor vehicle.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
:::

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a parking area contains delineated parking spaces for normal sized
  vehicles and an area designated by a sign for the parking of oversized
  vehicles, any person who parks a motor vehicle that is longer or wider
  than a delineated parking space shall park it in the designated area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who contravenes subsection (1) shall be
  considered to have parked a motor vehicle in such a manner as to
  obstruct traffic.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 5
- SOR/95-536, s. 6(F)
- SOR/2002-356, s. 1
:::

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/95-536,
s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue or authorize the issue of permits and labels for
  the purposes of this Part.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or a person authorized by him may at any
  time revoke a permit issued under this Part.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless sooner revoked, a permit issued under this
  Part is valid only for the period stated thereon and a label furnished
  with the permit is valid only during the period that the permit is
  valid.

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A constable who finds a
motor vehicle parked in contravention of this Part may, at the expense
of the owner, remove the vehicle and, if he deems it necessary to
protect the vehicle or the interests of the owner, store it in a
suitable place.

### [Speed]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581204 .Subheading}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No person shall drive a
motor vehicle on a road at a rate of speed in excess of the speed limit
indicated for that road by a sign.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
authorized by the airport manager, no person shall drive a motor vehicle
on an apron at a rate of speed in excess of 15 mph (25 km/h).

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** No person shall drive a
motor vehicle on an apron or manoeuvring area in a manner that is
dangerous to persons, aircraft, vehicles or equipment, having regard to
all the circumstances including the amount of traffic thereon or
reasonably expected to be thereon.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of this
Part relating to motor vehicles apply with such modifications as the
circumstances require to a bicycle or any cycle regardless of the number
of wheels it may have.

### [Animals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581209 .Subheading}

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** No person shall allow an
animal owned by him or under his control to be at large on an airport.

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A constable or person in
charge of an airport may, at the expense of the owner of the animal,
cause any animal found at large on the airport to be confined or driven
off the airport or impounded in accordance with the laws of the province
and municipality in which the airport is situated.

### [Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581212 .Subheading}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3)
  and subsection 40(4), every person who contravenes a provision of this
  Part is liable on summary conviction to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fine not exceeding an amount equal to the lesser
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum fine, if any, prescribed under the
      highway traffic laws of the province in which the offence was
      committed, as amended from time to time, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$500;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a term of imprisonment not exceeding six months; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} both.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person who contravenes any provision of these
  Regulations governing the parking of motor vehicles shall be liable on
  summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$50.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is convicted of driving a motor
  vehicle in contravention of this Part, the convicting court or judge
  may, in addition to any other penalty imposed, make an order
  prohibiting that person from driving any motor vehicle on any lands
  belonging to or occupied by Her Majesty in right of Canada for any
  period not exceeding one year from the date of the conviction.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every person who drives a motor vehicle contrary to
  an order made pursuant to subsection (3) is liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$500 or to imprisonment for a term
  not exceeding six months or to both.

- SOR/2002-356, s. 2

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Pedestrians]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581259 .Part}

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581260 .Subheading}

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** No person who is not in
possession of valid identification shall enter upon or remain in any
area that is designated by a sign or device as a restricted area unless
authorized to do so by the airport manager.

- **[[41.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person, being the holder of valid identification designed to be worn
  on outer clothing, shall enter on or remain in a restricted area
  unless that identification is at all times visibly displayed on his
  person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every holder of valid identification who fails to
  comply with the terms of issue of that identification shall surrender
  the identification to the airport manager.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-663, s. 2
:::

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Every person found on or
in a restricted area in contravention of section 41 or subsection
41.1(1) may be removed therefrom by a constable but such removal shall
be without prejudice toward any other proceeding that may be taken.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-663, s. 3
:::

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** Where a portion of a road
or apron has been designated as a cross-walk, no pedestrian shall
proceed to cross the road or apron except within the designated
cross-walk.

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Where a sidewalk or path
is located beside a road or apron, a pedestrian shall, at all times when
reasonable and practicable to do so, use the sidewalk or path and shall
not walk or remain on the road or apron.

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Where there is no
sidewalk or path located beside a road, a pedestrian walking along or
upon the road shall, at all times when practicable to do so, walk on the
left side or shoulder of the road facing on-coming traffic.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No pedestrian on an apron
shall impede, interfere with or obstruct in any way the free movement of
other apron traffic except in the course of his or her employment
relating to the control of such traffic.

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** Where no cross-walk has
been designated, every pedestrian crossing a road shall yield the
right-of-way to motor vehicles using the road.

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** A pedestrian shall obey
the instruction of any traffic sign or device and comply with any
traffic directions given by a constable to him.

### [Smoking and Littering]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581276 .Subheading}

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} smoke, carry or deposit lighted cigars, cigarettes,
  pipes or matches or carry a naked flame

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on an apron or any open deck gallery or balcony
    contiguous to and overlooking an apron,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any area where smoking is specifically
    prohibited by a sign, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other place at an airport under such
    circumstances as may or might be likely to endanger persons or
    property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} throw, deposit or knowingly leave on a road, apron or
  manoeuvring area any glass, nails, tacks, scraps of metal, chemical
  substance or other material that may damage an aircraft or motor
  vehicle; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} throw, deposit or knowingly leave on any airport any
  form of trash or garbage except in containers provided for that
  purpose.

### [Penalties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581284 .Subheading}

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes section 41, subsection 41.1(1) or (2) or section 49 is
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$400.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Registration of Mobile Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581304 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581305 .Subheading}

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any motor vehicle or mobile device used in the
      direct fuelling or defuelling of aircraft, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other motor vehicle or mobile device of a
      specialized nature for use in the maintenance, repair and
      servicing of an aircraft on the ground, including testing
      equipment and cargo and passenger handling equipment;
      ([matérial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gross weight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gross weight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to any equipment,
    means the total weight of the equipment as specified by its
    manufacturer; ([poids brut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registration year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registration year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period
    commencing on January 1st in any year and ending on December 31st in
    that year. ([année d'immatriculation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581312 .Subheading}

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Subject to this Part, the
owner of equipment used at airports shall register that equipment each
year in accordance with this Part.

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate
any equipment at an airport unless that equipment has been registered in
accordance with this Part.

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Sections 53 and 54 do not
apply to equipment operated exclusively on airport areas leased from the
Department by the owner of the equipment.

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Applications for the registration of
  equipment shall be made in the form prescribed by the Minister and
  delivered together with the fees prescribed by this Part to the
  airport manager at the airport at which the equipment is to be used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the application and fees described in
  subsection (1), the airport manager may, if he is of the opinion that
  the equipment for which registration is applied for can be safely
  operated at the airport, issue a registration certificate for that
  equipment.

### [Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581319 .Subheading}

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  annual registration fee payable in respect of any motor vehicle or
  mobile device used in the direct fuelling or defuelling of aircraft
  shall be \$15 or the amount obtained by multiplying the gross weight
  of the equipment, calculated in kilograms, by \$0.033, whichever is
  the greater amount.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any equipment is registered after June 30th in
  any registration year, the fee shall be one-half of the fee prescribed
  by subsection (1).

<!-- -->

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  equipment registered at any airport is replaced by other equipment of
  a similar kind, there shall be paid to the airport manager of that
  airport

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the other equipment is of an equivalent or lower
    gross weight than the equipment replaced, a registration fee of \$5;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the other equipment is of a greater gross weight
    than the equipment being replaced, a registration fee equal to the
    amount obtained by multiplying the difference between the gross
    weight of the equipment being replaced and the other equipment,
    calculated in kilograms, by \$0.033.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registration certificate for any equipment that
  is being replaced at an airport shall be surrendered to the airport
  manager of that airport.

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** Where equipment
registered under this Part is sold or transferred, the new owner of the
equipment shall, before operating the equipment at an airport, notify
the airport manager of that airport, and the airport manager may,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on payment of a fee of \$5 by the new owner,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on surrender of the registration certificate
  previously issued for that equipment to the previous owner, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} upon being satisfied that the equipment may be safely
  operated at the airport,

issue a new registration certificate for the equipment.

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** Where a certificate of
registration for any equipment is lost, a duplicate certificate may be
issued by the airport manager on payment of a fee of \$5.

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or airport
manager may exempt any equipment or class of equipment from the
provisions of this Part.

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** Certificates of
registration for any equipment shall be carried on the equipment at all
times and shall be produced for examination on request by a constable or
by an authorized representative of the airport manager.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-120, s. 7
:::

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** Any person who
contravenes any provision of this Part is guilty of an offence and is
liable on summary conviction to a fine not exceeding \$100.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Control of Aircraft on Aprons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581339 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581340 .Subheading}

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[apron traffic control clearance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apron traffic control clearance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    authorization by an apron traffic control unit for an aircraft to
    proceed on a controlled apron; ([autorisation du contrôle de la
    circulation sur l'aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[apron traffic control instruction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apron traffic control instruction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    directive issued by an apron traffic control unit for airport apron
    traffic and gate control purposes; ([instruction du contrôle de la
    circulation sur l'aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[apron traffic control unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apron traffic control unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means staff at an
    airport that provides apron traffic control service at the airport
    and includes the staff of a mobile control vehicle; ([organe du
    contrôle de la circulation sur l'aire de trafic]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[controlled apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled apron]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an apron at an
    airport where apron traffic control service is provided; ([aire de
    trafic contrôlée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an aircraft, means
    the person in possession of the aircraft, whether as owner, lessee,
    hirer or otherwise, and in section 68 includes the person in whose
    name the aircraft is registered; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the Air
    Regulations; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the standing of an aircraft,
    whether occupied or not; ([laisser en
    stationnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pilot-in-command]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pilot-in-command]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pilot responsible
    for the operation and safety of an aircraft. ([pilote commandant de
    bord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-581350 .Subheading}

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The operator or
pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a continuous watch on the radio frequencies
    designated for apron communications, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a continuous radio watch is not possible, a
    watch for such instructions as may be issued by visual means from an
    apron traffic control unit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} observe other apron traffic for the purpose of
  avoiding collision; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where an apron traffic control unit is in operation,
  obtain, either by radio or by visual signal, authorization for his
  movements from that unit.

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate
or park an aircraft on an apron negligently, recklessly or in any other
manner that endangers or is likely to endanger life or property.

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall move or park an aircraft on a controlled apron except in
  compliance with an apron traffic control clearance or an apron traffic
  control instruction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator or pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall
  immediately, upon receipt of an apron traffic control clearance or an
  apron traffic control instruction, acknowledge that he has received
  that clearance or instruction.

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park an
aircraft on a controlled apron beyond the time limit set in the last
apron traffic control instruction that was received by the operator or
pilot-in-command before the aircraft was parked, or that is later issued
by the apron traffic control unit and delivered to the owner or operator
of the aircraft or the person in whose name the aircraft is registered.

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport manager may order that an aircraft parked at an airport be
  moved.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator or pilot-in-command of an aircraft shall
  comply with any instructions or orders given to him by the airport
  manager pursuant to subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an aircraft is parked at an airport

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} beyond the time limit set by an apron traffic
    control unit,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an unauthorized place, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that obstructs traffic

  the airport manager may, at the expense of the operator of the
  aircraft, cause the aircraft to be moved and if he deems it necessary
  for the protection of the aircraft, store it in a suitable place.

<!-- -->

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), any
  person who contravenes any provision of this Part is liable on summary
  conviction to a fine not exceeding \$400.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a contravention of this Part relates to the
  parking of an aircraft, the fine shall not exceed \$50.
