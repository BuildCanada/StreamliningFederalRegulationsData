## Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Regulations {#prohibition-on-the-purchase-of-residential-property-by-non-canadians-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-250
:::

[PROHIBITION ON THE PURCHASE OF RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY BY NON-CANADIANS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-25.2)

Registration 2022-12-02

Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians
Regulations

P.C. 2022-1259 2022-12-02
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Housing and Diversity and Inclusion after consultation
with the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed Prohibition on the
Purchase of Residential Property by Non-Canadians Regulations under
section 8 of the [Prohibition on the Purchase of Residential Property by
Non-Canadians Act](/eng/acts/P-25.2)[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Prohibition on the Purchase
    of Residential Property by Non-Canadians
    Act](/eng/acts/P-25.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[census agglomeration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[census agglomeration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a census
    agglomeration within the meaning of the Statistics Canada document
    entitled Standard Geographical Classification (SGC)
    2021. ([agglomération de recensement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[census metropolitan area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[census metropolitan area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a census
    metropolitan area within the meaning of the Statistics Canada
    document entitled Standard Geographical Classification (SGC)
    2021. ([région métropolitaine de recensement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a corporation or
    entity, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct or indirect ownership of shares or
      ownership interests of the corporation or entity representing 10%
      or more of the value of the equity in it, or carrying 10% or more
      of its voting rights; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} control in fact of the corporation or entity,
      whether directly or indirectly, through ownership, agreement or
      otherwise. [(contrôle)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-Canadian --- prescribed entities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph (d) of the definition [[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
in section 2 of the Act, the following entities are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity formed otherwise than under the laws of
  Canada or a province; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity formed under the laws of Canada or a
  province -- whose shares or ownership interests are not listed on a
  stock exchange in Canada for which a designation under Section 262 of
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is in effect -- and controlled
  by an entity referred to in paragraph (a) or controlled by a person
  referred to in paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of the definition
  [[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residential property --- exclusion

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the portion of the definition [[residential
  property]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act before
  paragraph (a), a property that is located in an area of Canada that is
  not within either a census agglomeration or a census metropolitan area
  is a prescribed real property or immovable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purchase

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the Act, the acquisition, with or without conditions, of a
  legal or equitable interest or a real right in a residential property
  constitutes a purchase.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a purchase referred to in subsection (1)
  does not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition by an individual of an interest or
    a real right resulting from death, divorce, separation or a gift;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rental of a dwelling unit to a tenant for the
    purpose of its occupation by the tenant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transfer under the terms of a trust that was
    created prior to the coming into force of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the transfer resulting from the exercise of a
    security interest or secured right by a secured creditor; or\

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition by a non-Canadian of residential
    property for the purposes of development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary residents --- prescribed
conditions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 4(2)(a) of the Act, the temporary resident must satisfy one of
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if they are enrolled in a program of authorized study
  at a [[designated learning institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in section 211.1 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they filed all required income tax returns under
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for each of the five taxation
    years preceding the year in which the purchase was made,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they were physically present in Canada for a
    minimum of 244 days in each of the five calendar years preceding the
    year in which the purchase was made,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the purchase price of the residential property
    does not exceed \$500,000, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} they have not purchased more than one residential
    property; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they hold a [[work permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), or are authorized to work
  in Canada under section 186 of those Regulations,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they have 183 days or more of validity remaining on
    their work permit or work authorization on the date of purchase, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they have not purchased more than one residential
    property.

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2023-66, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- persons

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 4(2)(d) of the Act, the following classes of persons are
prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} foreign nationals who hold a passport that contains a
  valid diplomatic, consular, official or special representative
  acceptance issued by the Chief of Protocol for the Department of
  Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} foreign nationals, with valid temporary resident
  status, whose temporary resident visa was issued, or temporary
  resident status was granted, following an exemption provided under
  section 25.2 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), if the Minister is of the opinion that the
  exemption was justified based on public policy considerations to
  provide safe haven to those fleeing conflict; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons that have made a claim for refugee protection
  in accordance with subsection 99(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), if that claim has been found
  eligible and referred to the Refugee Protection Division under
  subsection 100(1) of that Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An order
  referred to in subsection 7(1) of the Act may only be made if the
  following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the non-Canadian is the owner of the residential
    property at the time the order is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notice has been given to every person who may be
    entitled to receive proceeds from the sale; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the superior court of the province is satisfied
    that the impact of the order would not be disproportionate to the
    nature and gravity of the contravention, the circumstances
    surrounding the commission of the contravention and the resulting
    conviction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The order made under subsection 7(1) of the Act must
  provide that the proceeds of the sale are distributed in the following
  order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment of the costs of the sale, including the
    costs incurred by the Minister in bringing the application for the
    order and any unpaid fines by the non-Canadian under the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment of those, other than the non-Canadian,
    who are entitled to receive the proceeds of the sale in amounts and
    according to priorities that the superior court may determine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the repayment of the non-Canadian of an amount that
    is not greater than the purchase price they paid for the residential
    property; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the payment of any amount remaining to the Receiver
    General for Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- circumstances

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 4(3) of the Act, subsection 4(1) of the Act does not apply if
it is incompatible with the rights recognized and affirmed by section 35
of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repeal

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
repealed on the day on which section 236 of the [Budget Implementation
Act, 2022, No. 1](/eng/acts/B-9.8595), chapter 10 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2022, comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on January 1 2023, but if they are registered after that day,
they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
