## National Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act Deputy Heads of the Federal Public Administration Order {#national-security-and-intelligence-review-agency-act-deputy-heads-of-the-federal-public-administration-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-51
:::

[NATIONAL SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE REVIEW AGENCY
ACT](/eng/acts/N-16.62)

Registration 2024-10-23

National Security and Intelligence Review Agency Act Deputy Heads of the
Federal Public Administration Order

P.C. 2024-1075 2024-10-10
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph (e) of the definition [[deputy
head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [National Security and
Intelligence Review Agency Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1723229184){#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1723229184-ID0EDBAA}
and paragraph 55(b) of that Act, makes the annexed [National Security
and Intelligence Review Agency Act Deputy Heads of the Federal Public
Administration Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2024-51).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1723229184-ID0EDBAA){#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1723229184}S.C.
  2019, c. 13, s. 2
  :::
:::

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489799 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The person holding the
position set out in column 2 of the schedule is designated, for the
purposes of the [National Security and Intelligence Review Agency
Act](/eng/acts/N-16.62), as the deputy head of that corresponding
portion of the federal public administration set out in column 1.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489801 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [Canadian Security
Intelligence Service Act Deputy Heads of the Public Service of Canada
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-93-81)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1728409669){#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1728409669-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1728409669-ID0EBBA){#fn_81000-3-2728_hq_1728409669}SI/93-81
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489803 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on October 15, 2024, but if it is made after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is made.
