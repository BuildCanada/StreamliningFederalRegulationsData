## Canadian Agricultural Loans Regulations {#canadian-agricultural-loans-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-122
:::

[CANADIAN AGRICULTURAL LOANS ACT](/eng/acts/F-2.7)

FARM IMPROVEMENT AND MARKETING COOPERATIVES LOANS ACT

Registration 1999-03-11

Canadian Agricultural Loans Regulations

P.C. 1999-401  1999-03-11
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, the Minister of Finance and
the Treasury Board, pursuant to sections 4, 6, 10, 12 and 15[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-122_e_hq_5577){#fn_SOR-99-122_e_hq_5577-ID0EGCAA}

]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-99-122_e_hq_5578){#fn_SOR-99-122_e_hq_5578-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed Farm Improvement and Marketing Cooperatives
Loans and Fees Regulations, 1998.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027061 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Agricultural Loans
      Act](/eng/acts/F-2.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a farmer or a farm products
      marketing cooperative. ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[guaranteed farm improvement loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[guaranteed farm improvement
      loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2014-39, s.
      2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The definitions in this subsection apply for the
  purposes of the Act.

  [[additions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[additions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the installation and
      construction of foundations for buildings and the purchase of
      materials for that purpose, and the purchase and installation of
      equipment for, or systems of plumbing, heating, ventilating, air
      conditioning, sewage disposal or water supply or parts thereof to
      an existing structure or as part of an increase to an existing
      structure. ([ajout]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alteration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a building,
      means a structural modification to the exterior or interior of any
      structure that is designed to improve, modernize or increase the
      usefulness of a structure and includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purchase of material for that purpose;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the relocation of any equipment; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the modification of any system of plumbing,
        heating, ventilating, air conditioning, sewage disposal, water
        supply or electrical system, or any parts thereof.
        ([modification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[repair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the painting of any
      structure and the purchase of the material and services required
      for a repair. ([réparation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[works for drainage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[works for drainage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means works related to
      the making of a drainage ditch, tiling and drainage installations
      and pumping and diking installations and includes works for the
      prevention of soil erosion by water. ([travaux de
      drainage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-39, s. 2

## [Other Prescribed Purposes for Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027077 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 4(1)(h) of the Act, the other purposes for loans made to
farmers are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clearing, breaking, irrigating and reclaiming of
  land;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conservation of soil and the prevention of soil
  erosion by the planting of trees and shelter belts;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, movement to and installation on a farm
  of complete or partially complete structures and, if necessary, the
  completion of the installed structures;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the cost is \$2,000 or more, a repair or overhaul
  of fencing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the cost is \$2,000 or more, the purchase and
  planting of trees for syrup production and the purchase and planting
  of fruit trees, Christmas trees and ginseng;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the construction of a road or driveway on a farm;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the costs of land transfer taxes and survey,
  appraisal and legal costs relating to the purchase of additional land;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the purchase of a crop storage condominium;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost of obtaining security on existing assets;
  and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the cost of a fee or charge referred to in section
  17.

- SOR/2014-39, s. 3

subparagraph 4(1)(c)(iv) of the Act, other prescribed animals are game

## [Nature of Interest of the Borrower in Farming Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027099 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 4(3)(b) and 6(2)(b) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan in respect of property that
  does not form part of or is not affixed to real or immovable property,
  the borrower shall own the property outright or hold rights under a
  conditional sales contract or term contract; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan in respect of property that
  forms part of or is affixed to real or immovable property, the
  borrower shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} own the real or immovable property,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} lease the real or immovable property pursuant to
    the [Prairie Farm Rehabilitation Act](/eng/acts/P-17) or the
    [Veterans' Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5), R.S.C. 1970, c. V-4, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} hold a lease in real or immovable property with
    the lease term extending at least two years beyond the term of the
    loan.

- SOR/2014-39, s. 6

## [Conditions of Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027108 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-39,
s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every lender shall, at the
time of making a loan, require the borrower to give the lender, in
addition to any other security referred to in section 15, a written
promise to repay the loan signed by the borrower, as a separate document
or as an integral part of the lender's loan document that sets forth the
principal amount of the loan, the rate of interest payable on the loan
and the repayment terms.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No initial loan
disbursement may be made for a purchase made more than 60 days before
the making of the disbursement.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Repayment of a loan, other
than repayment that is scheduled to be made more frequently by agreement
between the borrower and the lender, shall be made in instalments that
are payable annually.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Full or partial
prepayment of a loan shall be made in accordance with the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a loan has a remaining
term of fewer than the number of years permitted under paragraph 4(3)(d)
or 6(2)(d) of the Act for that type of loan, the lender may renew the
loan for additional terms at the rate of interest at the time of renewal
if the combined terms do not exceed the maximum term allowed under the
Act in respect of that type of loan.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In the event of actual or
impending default in the repayment of a loan, the lender may, with the
approval of the Minister and the borrower, alter or revise any of the
terms of the loan, or any agreement in connection with the loan, and
that alteration or revision shall not discharge the liability of the
Minister in respect of the loan under the Act.

## [Rate of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027121 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum rate of interest payable per annum by a borrower in respect of
  a loan is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the rate of interest on the loan is not fixed,
    the prime lending rate of the lender plus 1%, as that prime rate is
    fixed daily during the term of the loan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the rate of interest is fixed, the residential
    mortgage loan rate of the lender for a comparable term plus 1%, as
    that rate is fixed on the date the funds are disbursed or on an
    earlier date agreed to in writing by the lender and the borrower.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a loan with a fixed rate of interest is to be
  extended before the expiry of its term, the interest rate on the loan
  may be blended, merged or combined with the interest rate fixed on the
  day of extension for the balance of the proposed term in accordance
  with the lender's current practice for that type of loan.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027127 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A lender shall take
security for the repayment of a loan in one or more of the following
forms, as appropriate:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} security under section 427 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} security registered in accordance with the applicable
  personal or movable property legislation in the province;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a pledge, hypothecation or movable hypothec;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a mortgage or hypothec on real or immovable property;
  or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an assignment of any rights or interest of the
  borrower under an agreement for sale.

- SOR/2014-39, s. 10

## [Registration of Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027136 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender shall register every loan with the Minister, in a form approved
  by the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a loan made for any purpose set out
    in any of paragraphs 4(1)(a) to (c), (g) and (h) and 6(1)(a), (d)
    and (e) of the Act, within 365 days after the date on which the
    first disbursement is advanced; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of loans made for any purpose set out
    in paragraph 4(1)(d), (e) or (f) or 6(1)(b) or (c) of the Act,
    within 365 days after the date on which the first disbursement is
    advanced.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall extend the time within which a
  lender may register the loan on the written application of the lender
  if the application is received before the expiration of the time set
  out in paragraph (1)(a) or (b), as the case may be, and the borrower
  is not in default.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-39, s. 11

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027144 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender shall pay to the Minister at the time of registration of a loan
  a fee in the amount of 0.85% of the amount of every loan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraphs 4(3)(e)(i) and
  6(2)(e)(i) of the Act, the charge is the administration charge in
  respect of a loan charged by the lender up to a maximum amount of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of 0.25% of the loan principal or \$250,
    if the loan is for less than \$250,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.1% of the loan principal, if the loan is for
    \$250,000 or more.

## [Procedure on Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027153 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, the default date is the day after the day on which a
  payment under a loan was scheduled to have been made but was not made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a borrower is in default in respect of a payment
  on a loan made for a purpose set out in subsection 4(1) or 6(1) of the
  Act, the lender shall register, for a period of 10 years, the security
  referred to in section 15 that the lender holds with respect to the
  loan and take any of the following measures that will minimize the
  loss sustained by the lender in respect of the loan or that will
  maximize the amount recovered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collect the principal and interest outstanding on
    the loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fully realize any security, guarantee or
    suretyship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} realize on any insurance policy under which the
    lender is the beneficiary;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fully implement a compromise settlement with the
    borrower or with a guarantor or surety or any other person on behalf
    of the borrower, guarantor or surety that has been approved by the
    Minister; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} take legal proceedings, including the enforcement
    of any resulting judgment, if the estimated cost of the proceedings
    is not greater than the estimated amount that may be recovered.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a claim for loss has been submitted in
  accordance with section 20, the lender shall submit a Report on
  Defaulted Loan to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes set out in subsection 4(1) of the
    Act, within six months after the default date; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes set out in subsection 6(1) of the
    Act, within 15 days after the default date.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-39, s. 14

## [Procedure for Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027167 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lender shall take all of the measures described in subsection 19(2)
  that are applicable before submitting to the Minister a claim for loss
  sustained as a result of a loan.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claim for loss shall be submitted within 18 months
  of the default date or, if an extension has been granted by the
  Minister, then within that extended time period.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on the written request of the
  lender, extend the period of 18 months so that a lender can continue
  to carry out the collection measures prior to submitting its claim for
  loss, if the request is received within 18 months of the default date.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claim for any loss shall be submitted to the
  Minister in the form approved by the Minister, together with a copy of
  the borrower's application form and proof of registration of the
  security referred to in section 15, if applicable.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The lender shall provide the Minister with any other
  documentation that the Minister may require to justify payment to the
  lender.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The payment is to be made within 60 days after the
  Minister approves the claim for loss.

- SOR/2014-39, s. 15

## [Determination of Loss]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027177 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The amount of loss
sustained by a lender as a result of a loan that the Minister is liable
to repay the lender is 95% of the total of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unpaid principal of the loan,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the accrued interest that was not paid by the
  borrower before the due date of the first unpaid instalment,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the uncollected interest accrued after the date
  referred to in paragraph (b) and outstanding at the time the claim is
  approved for payment by the Minister, at the rate of interest
  specified in the written promise to repay the loan, for a maximum
  period of 365 days after that date;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the legal fees, costs and disbursements
  that have been assessed and allowed under section 13 of the Act and
  actually incurred by the lender, with or without litigation, in
  collecting or endeavouring to collect the loan or in protecting the
  interests of the Minister, less any costs recovered by the lender; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any other costs or disbursements
  actually incurred by the lender in collecting or endeavouring to
  collect the loan or in protecting the interests of the Minister.
