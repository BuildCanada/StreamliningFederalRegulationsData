## Saskatchewan Hog Order {#saskatchewan-hog-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 271
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Hogs Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537551 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Hog Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._271).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537553 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Agri-Food Act of Saskatchewan;
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means SPI Marketing Group;
    ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/95-557, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in the
    Province of Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    hogs established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-557, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537562 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agency is authorized to
regulate the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and
for such purposes may, with respect to persons and property situated
within the Province of Saskatchewan, exercise all or any powers like the
powers exercisable by it in relation to the marketing of hogs locally
within that Province under the Act and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-557, s. 2
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537566 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Agency is authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated within the Province of Saskatchewan who are engaged in
the production or marketing of hogs and for such purpose classifying
such persons into groups and fixing levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts, and the Agency may
use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation of
reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or
disposal of hogs and the equalization or adjustment among producers of
hogs of moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or
periods of time as it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-90
- SOR/95-557, s. 2
