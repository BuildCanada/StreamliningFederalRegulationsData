## Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 2 {#privacy-act-extension-order-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-206
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1989-04-13

Order Extending the Right To Be Given Access to Personal Information
under Subsection 12(1) of the Privacy Act

P.C. 1989-618 1989-04-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 12(3) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21), is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order
extending the right to be given access to personal information under
subsection 12(1) of the Privacy Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-553).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918827 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Privacy Act Extension Order, No. 2.

## [Extension]{.HTitleText1} {#h-918829 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The right to be given
access to personal information under subsection 12(1) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21) is hereby extended to include all individuals
present in Canada to whom that right has not been extended previously.
