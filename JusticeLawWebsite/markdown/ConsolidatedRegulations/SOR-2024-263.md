## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1500119 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to protect the environment and human health from the
threat of climate change by establishing a regime that prohibits
excessive carbon dioxide (CO~2~) emissions from the use of fossil fuel
to generate electricity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Planned unit

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A unit is planned if its
commissioning date is within the period that begins on January 1, 2025
and ends on December 31, 2034 and if the Minister is satisfied that the
unit is substantially the same on its commissioning date as the unit in
relation to which the following criteria were met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on or before December 31, 2025,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all information required to initiate any impact
    assessment or environmental assessment required in relation to the
    unit under a federal or provincial law has been submitted to the
    relevant authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the proponent responsible for the development of
    the unit owns or has a lease for the land on which the unit is
    located,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all information required to initiate the process
    to obtain any permit required to begin construction at the site
    where the unit is located has been submitted to the relevant
    authority, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} contracts with a value of at least \$10 million
    have been entered into for the purchase of any equipment referred to
    in the definition [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1)
    for use in the unit; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or before December 31, 2027, construction has
  begun at the site where the unit is located.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1500184 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations begin to apply in respect of a unit on the day on which
  the unit meets the following criteria and, subject to subsection
  43(2), continue to apply until the responsible person for the unit
  submits the notice of permanent cessation of electricity generation in
  accordance with subsection 43(1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit has an electricity generation capacity of
    at least 25 MW;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the unit generates electricity using fossil fuel;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the unit is connected, directly or indirectly, to
    an electricity system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sum of electricity generation capacity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a unit that has an
  electricity generation capacity of less than 25 MW is deemed to meet
  the criterion set out in paragraph (1)(a) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit's commissioning date is on or after
    January 1, 2025; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sum of the electricity generation capacity of
    all units, other than planned units, that are located at the
    facility where the unit is located and that have commissioning dates
    on or after January 1, 2025 is at least 25 MW.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- planned units

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a
  planned unit that has an electricity generation capacity of less than
  25 MW.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electricity generation capacity

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (6), a unit's electricity generation capacity is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unit's maximum gross power, measured at the
    electrical terminals of the unit's generators and determined in
    accordance with subsections (2) to (4); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the responsible person has not determined the
    unit's maximum gross power, the unit's most recent maximum
    continuous rating.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Performance test

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To determine a unit's maximum gross power, the
  responsible person must conduct, in the presence of the performance
  test verifier referred to in subsection (3), a performance test that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is conducted on or after January 1, 2025;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a continuous test that lasts at least two hours;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not compromise the continued operation of the
    unit by exceeding the operating specifications for temperature,
    pressure or electrical conductivity recommended by the manufacturer
    of the equipment that is part of the unit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not require that the planned maintenance
    schedule of the unit be altered; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has results that are adjusted to standard
    conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Performance test verifier

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The performance test verifier is an individual who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is independent of the responsible person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has demonstrated knowledge of the performance
    testing of units that generate electricity using fossil fuel and at
    least five years' experience with respect to such testing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Performance test report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A responsible person that conducts a performance test
  must submit to the Minister a performance test report prepared by the
  performance test verifier that contains the information set out in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report submitted after registration

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A responsible person that submits a performance test
  report for a unit after the registration report for the unit is
  submitted under subsection 7(1), paragraph 7(2)(a) or subsection 8(2)
  must submit to the Minister, no later than 60 days after the day on
  which the test is conducted, the performance test report along with
  the updated registration report referred to in subsection 7(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of electricity generation
  capacity

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, during a calendar year in which a unit is subject
  to an emission limit under subsection 9(1), the maximum gross power or
  maximum continuous rating of the unit changes, the unit's electricity
  generation capacity for that calendar year is determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#1500219 .Subsection}
  <figure>
  <div class="imageGroupcenter">
  <img src="/images/SOR-2024-263img/SOR-2024-263_ef001.JPG"
  alt="The sum of the products of Ci and Di divided by N for each period “i” of a calendar year." />
  </div>
  </figure>

  where

  [i]{.dfn}
  :   is the i^th^ period of the calendar year, where "i" goes from 1 to
      *n* and where *n* is the number of periods in the calendar year
      during which the unit's electricity generation capacity differs
      from the preceding or subsequent period;

  [C~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the electricity generation capacity of the unit during the
      i^th^ period of the calendar year;

  [D~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the length of the i^th^ period of the calendar year, expressed
      in days; and

  [N]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of days during the calendar year that the unit's
      electricity generation capacity is greater than zero.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The electricity generation capacity determined under
  subsection (2) or (6) is to be rounded to the nearest whole number or,
  if equidistant between two consecutive whole numbers, to the higher
  number.

## [Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1500228 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 8, the responsible person for a unit that meets the
  criteria set out in section 5 must submit to the Minister a
  registration report for the unit that contains the information set out
  in Schedule 2 by the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} December 31, 2025, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the 60th day after the day on which the unit meets
    the criteria set out in section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modification of a unit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a unit for which a registration report has been
  submitted under subsection (1) is modified, such as by adding or
  removing a piece of equipment or changing how equipment is physically
  connected, and that modification results in the creation of one or
  more units that meet the criteria set out in section 5, the
  responsible person must submit to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registration report for each unit that was
    created that contains the information set out in Schedule 2 within
    60 days after the day on which the unit was created; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice of permanent cessation of electricity
    generation referred to in subsection 43(1) for the original unit or
    the updated registration report referred to in subsection (4) for
    that unit.

    Regulations;

    gaseous fuel, including an indication of whether a direct measuring
    device was used to determine that value;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's instructions for any measuring
    device used to determine any value or quantity under these
    Regulations;

    out in section 36 have been met;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} documents confirming that the CEMS certification
      requirement set out in subsection 18(2) has been met, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each quality assurance plan developed
      for the CEMS and information demonstrating that the requirements
      set out in section 6 of the CEMS Protocol for the quality
      assurance plan have been met;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the results of the analysis of every sample taken
    for the purposes of subsections 20(1) or (4), as well as the date on
    which each sample was taken and an indication of the standards that
    were used to take representative samples of the fuel;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if a supplier of hydrogen, ammonia or steam has
    provided the values of E~k~ and P~k~ under subsection 24(2), the

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A record must be made no later than 30 days after the

  available.

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  responsible person must keep the records that are required to be made
  under these Regulations with respect to the unit, along with the
  supporting documents, for the following periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a registration report submitted
    under subsection 7(1), paragraph 7(2)(a) or subsection 8(2) and any
    supporting documents related to that registration report, the period
    that begins on the day on which the registration report is submitted
    to the Minister and ends on the day that is seven years after the
    day on which the notice referred to in subsection 43(1) is submitted
    in respect of the unit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, a period of seven years after
    the later of the day on which the record is made and the day on
    which the information is submitted to the Minister.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records and supporting documents must be kept at
  the responsible person's principal place of business in Canada or at

  of business, the responsible person must provide the Minister with the
  civic address of the place where they are kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Relocation of records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the records and supporting documents are moved,
  the responsible person must notify the Minister of the civic address
  of the new location within 30 days after the day of the move.
