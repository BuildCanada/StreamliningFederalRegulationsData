## Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office Privileges and Immunities Order {#hong-kong-economic-and-trade-office-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-207
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1996-04-19

Order Granting Privileges and Immunities to the Hong Kong Economic and
Trade Office, and Persons Connected Therewith, in Canada
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to paragraph 6(1)(a) of the
[Foreign Missions and International Organizations
Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-207_e_hq_6179){#fn_SOR-96-207_e_hq_6179-ID0EHCCA},
is pleased hereby to repeal the [Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office
Privileges and Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-207) made on
January 21, 1994[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-96-207_e_hq_6180){#fn_SOR-96-207_e_hq_6180-ID0EDCCA},
and to make the annexed Order granting privileges and immunities to the
Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office, and to persons connected therewith,
in Canada, in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980431 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-207).

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980433 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The privileges and
immunities accorded to consular posts under section 3 of the [Foreign
Missions and International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4) are
hereby granted to the Hong Kong Economic and Trade Office, and persons
connected therewith, in Canada including members of their families
forming part of their households in Canada, who are not nationals or
permanent residents of Canada.
