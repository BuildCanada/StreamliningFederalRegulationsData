## Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of National Defence the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Coast Guard and the Canadian Coast Guard Support Services Group {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-fisheries-and-oceans-to-the-department-of-national-defence-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-coast-guard-and-the-canadian-coast-guard-support-services-group .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-96
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-09-24

Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the
Department of National Defence the Control and Supervision of the
Canadian Coast Guard and the Canadian Coast Guard Support Services Group

P.C. 2025-639 2025-09-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department
of National Defence the control and supervision of those portions of the
federal public administration in the Department of Fisheries and Oceans
known as the Canadian Coast Guard and the Canadian Coast Guard Support
Services Group.
