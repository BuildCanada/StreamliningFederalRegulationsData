Wage Earner Protection Program Regulations

P.C. 2008-1317 2008-07-04

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 41 of the [Wage Earner
Protection Program Act](/eng/acts/W-0.8)[[Footnote

hereby makes the annexed [Wage Earner Protection Program
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-222).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745996 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Wage Earner Protection
    Program Act](/eng/acts/W-0.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Labour.
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Wages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746000 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following amounts are
prescribed for the purposes of the definition
[[wages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} gratuities accounted for by the employer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} disbursements of a travelling salesperson properly
  incurred in and about the business of a bankrupt or the business of a
  person subject to a receivership; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} production bonuses and shift premiums.

- SOR/2016-258, s. 1

## [Termination of Employment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746007 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An individual's employment
has ended for the purposes of paragraph 5(a) of the Act if it has ended
for any of the following reasons:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual resigned or retired;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual's employment has terminated; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the term of the individual's employment has expired.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 2, s. 348

## [Foreign Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1328249 .Part}

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 5(2) of the Act, a court may determine whether the foreign
proceeding is in respect of a former employer that has terminated all of
its employees in Canada other than any retained to wind down its
business operations.

## [Proceedings Under Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act or Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1328253 .Part}

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 5(5) of the Act, a court may determine whether the former
employer is the former employer all of whose employees in Canada have
been terminated other than any retained to wind down its business

## [Controlling Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746014 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 6(b) of the Act, an individual had a controlling interest in
the business of their former employer if the individual owned

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than 40% of the voting shares in the business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a block of voting shares that is large enough such
  that no one shareholder or coalition of shareholders can block a
  motion; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} enough shares in the business to control the
  business's policy.

## [Excluded Managers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746019 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 6(c) of the Act, an individual occupied a managerial position
with their former employer if the responsibilities of the individual
included making binding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} financial decisions affecting the business of the
  former employer; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} decisions with respect to the payment or the
  non-payment of wages by the former employer.

## [Offsets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746023 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any amount that an
individual has received in respect of eligible wages or in respect of
the termination of employment that is paid by the former employer or
from any other source, excluding any amounts received through other
federal or provincial programs, after the date of the bankruptcy or
receivership, or the day on which a court determines that the former
employer meets the criteria set out in section 3.1 or 3.2, as the case
may be, is the amount for the purposes of subsection 7(1.1) of the Act.

## [Allocation of Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746030 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Payments to an individual
under the Act are to be allocated in the following order:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} firstly, to wages other than those referred to in
  paragraphs (b) to (e);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} secondly, to disbursements of a travelling
  salesperson properly incurred in and about the business of a bankrupt
  or the business of a person subject to a receivership to the extent
  referred to in subsections 81.3(3) and 81.4(3) of the [Bankruptcy and
  Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} thirdly, to vacation pay;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} fourthly, to termination pay; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} lastly, to severance pay.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2009, c. 2, s. 351

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746039 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for payment shall be made within 56 days after the latest
  of the following days:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the bankruptcy or receivership of the
    applicant's former employer,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the applicant's employment ends
    for any of the reasons referred to in section 3, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a court determines that the former
    employer meets the criteria set out in section 3.1 or 3.2, as the
    case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application may be submitted after the expiry of
  the 56-day period if circumstances beyond the control of the applicant
  prevented them from submitting the application during that period.

## [Information to Be Provided to the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746057 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of paragraph 21(1)(d) of the Act, the trustee or receiver
  shall provide the Minister with the following information in the form
  provided by the Minister:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of bankruptcy or receivership, or the day
    on which a court determines that the former employer meets the
    criteria set out in section 3.1 or 3.2, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, social
    insurance number, employee number and job title of the individual;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which wages, other than severance pay
    or termination pay, were earned and the basis upon which they were
    calculated;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} the date on which any employment in respect of
    which severance pay or termination pay is owing ended;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to whether or not the individual
    delivered a proof of claim for wages owing under section 124 of the
    [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names of the employer's officers, directors and
    owners and of the person responsible for the employer's payroll.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The trustee or receiver shall provide the information
  within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 45 days after the date of bankruptcy or after the
    first day on which there was a receiver in relation to the former
    employer, as the case may be, unless circumstances beyond the
    control of the trustee or receiver justify a longer period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the trustee or receiver requests the information
    under subsection 21(3) or (4) of the Act, 15 days after receiving
    the information.

## [Information to Be Provided to an Individual]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746071 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of paragraph 21(1)(d) of the Act, the trustee or receiver
  shall provide each individual with the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of bankruptcy or receivership, or the day
    on which a court determines that the former employer meets the
    criteria set out in section 3.1 or 3.2, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement informing the individual of their
    requirement under section 124 of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) to deliver a proof of claim for wages owing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the information and documents that they
    provided to the Minister with respect to the individual; and

    Program.

  within 45 days after the date of bankruptcy or after the first day on
  which there was a receiver in relation to the former employer, as the
  case may be, unless circumstances beyond the control of the trustee or
  receiver justify a longer period.

## [Fees and Expenses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746087 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of section 22.1 of the Act, the Minister shall, on
  application by the trustee, pay the fees and expenses if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the trustee has submitted a claim in the form
    approved by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claim indicates a deficit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any third-party deposits and
    guarantees in respect of the fees and expenses is less than the
    deficit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount payable is equal to the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the deficit exceeds the amount
    of any third-party deposits and guarantees; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

    ::: {#1328285 .Paragraph}
    A + B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount obtained by adding \$1,000, as adjusted, to
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$150, as adjusted, for each of the first 10
          claims, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$100, as adjusted, for each additional
          claim; and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is, the total of the fees and expenses to a maximum of \$6,000,
        as adjusted, not including applicable sales taxes, incurred
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for taking possession of the property, making
          an inventory and securing and insuring the property,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for mail-outs to creditors to advise them of
          the meeting of creditors and the discharge hearing of the
          trustee,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for publishing a newspaper notice of the
          bankruptcy,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for the official receiver and the registrar,
          and

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for all of the other items that may be
          allowed by the court on the taxation of the statement of
          receipts and disbursements, to a maximum of \$1,000, as
          adjusted.
    :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the amount determined for B
  in paragraph (2)(b) is equal to 0 if the amount determined in
  accordance with the following formula is more than \$10,000, as
  adjusted:

  ::: {#1328297 .Subsection}
  X -- Y

  where

  [X]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to the total value of the former employer's assets; and

  [Y]{.dfn}
  :   is the total value of the claims that have priority over the fees
      and expenses of the trustee.
  :::

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may request that the trustee provide a
  copy of the final statement of receipts and disbursements.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2021-196, s. 8](#1314880-1328303){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1314880-1328303.html"
  role="button"}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amounts, as indicated in subsections 18(2) and (3), that are to be
  adjusted are to be done so on January 1 of each year in accordance
  with the percentage increase to the consumer price index for the year
  ending on September 30 of the previous year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The consumer price index is the annual average
  all-items consumer price index for Canada (not seasonally adjusted)
  published by Statistics Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjusted amounts are to be rounded

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of those set out in the description of
    A in paragraph 18(2)(b), to the nearest dollar;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), in the case of those set
    out in the description of B in paragraph 18(2)(b) and in subsection
    18(3), to the nearest \$500 increment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of those set out in subparagraph (v) of
    the description of B in paragraph 18(2)(b), to the nearest \$100
    increment.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of rounding, an amount set out in the
  description of B in subsection (2) remains the same as it was for the
  previous year, the unrounded adjusted amount is to be used for the
  purposes of the adjustment for the following year.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The notice referred to in
subsections 36(1.1) and (1.2) of the Act is to be provided to the
Minister within 30 days after the day on which the individual becomes
aware of the action, proceeding, decision or order, as the case may be,
and include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and social
  insurance number of the individual;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice referred to in subsection
  36(1.1),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date the action or proceeding was commenced,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the action or proceeding,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who commenced the action
    or proceeding and their contact information; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice referred to in subsection
  36(1.2), the date the decision or order was made and, if a payment has
  been received as a result of the decision or order,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment broken down by the
    components of wages to which it relates and by recipient,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the contact information of the recipients,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the time period for which the payment relates,
    and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the source of the payment.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2009, c. 2, s. 357

  - ###### Application {#application .MarginalNote}

    [357]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the [Wage Earner Protection
    Program Act](/eng/acts/W-0.8) and the [Wage Earner Protection
    Program Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-222) as amended by
    sections 342 to 354 apply

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of wages owing to an individual by an
      employer who becomes bankrupt after January 26, 2009; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of wages owing to an individual by an
      employer any of whose property comes under the possession or
      control of a receiver within the meaning of subsection 243(2) of
      the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) after January
      26, 2009.

- --- SOR/2021-196, s. 9

  - [9]{.lawlabel} The [Wage Earner Protection Program
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-222), as they read
    immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
    force, continue to apply where the date of the bankruptcy or the
    first day on which there was a receiver in relation to the former
    employer occurs before the day on which these Regulations come into
    force.
