## Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2006) {#order-declaring-an-amnesty-period-2006 .Title-of-Act}

Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2006)

P.C. 2006-385 2006-05-17

:::::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 117.14(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby makes the annexed [Order

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728810 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm other
    than a prohibited firearm or a restricted firearm. ([arme à feu sans
    restriction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 1 of the [Public Agents Firearms
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203). ([agence de services
    publics]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Non-restricted Firearms --- Individuals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-728814 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for an individual who,
  at any time during the amnesty period,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a non-restricted firearm, holds
    a licence to possess firearms or a licence to possess and acquire
    firearms but does not hold a registration certificate for the
    firearm; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in possession of a non-restricted firearm, does
    not hold a registration certificate for the firearm and will have
    held a licence to possess firearms or a licence to possess and
    acquire firearms

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that expired during the period beginning on
      January 1, 2004 and ending on May 16, 2006, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that will have expired during the period
      beginning on May 17, 2006 and ending on May 16, 2015.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to permit the
  individual to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual described in paragraph
    (1)(a), obtain the registration certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual described in paragraph
    (1)(b), obtain the licence and registration certificate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deactivate the firearm so that it is no longer a
    firearm;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} export the firearm in accordance with all
    applicable legal requirements, including the legal requirements of
    the country to which the firearm is exported;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} turn in the firearm to a police officer or a
    firearms officer for destruction or other disposal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} sell or give the firearm to a public service
    agency, to a business, including a museum, that holds a licence
    authorizing the acquisition of firearms or to an individual who
    holds a possession and acquisition licence for firearms; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} possess the firearm before doing one of the things
    described in paragraphs (a) to (f).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on May 17, 2006 and ends on
  May 16, 2015.
