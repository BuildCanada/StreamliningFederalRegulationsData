## North Battleford Airport Zoning Regulations {#north-battleford-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-648
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1992-11-19

Regulations Respecting Zoning at North Battleford Airport

P.C. 1992-2320 1992-11-19
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting North Battleford Airport, substantially in the form set out
in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on March 16 and 23,
1991 and in two successive issues of the News Optimist on June 26 and
July 3, 1991, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to
interested persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport
with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Acting Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at North Battleford
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-648).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949018 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [North Battleford Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-648).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949020 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the North Battleford
      Airport, in the vicinity of North Battleford, in the Province of
      Saskatchewan; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 54l.9 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949031 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949033 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949038 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949040 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on North Battleford Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.2745 dated November 30, 1989 is described as follows:

Premising that the bearings hereinafter mentioned are astronomical and
are referred to the centre line of runway 12-30 of the North Battleford
Airport, North Battleford, Saskatchewan, as South 44°10′06″ East;

Commencing at the threshold of runway 12; thence South 44°10′06″ East a
distance of 738.8 m; thence South 45°49′54″ West a distance of 203.4 m
to the airport reference point.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on North Battleford Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2745 dated November 30, 1989, are surfaces abutting each end of
the strips associated with runways designated 06-24 and 12-30, and are
described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 06 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of l m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 280 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 125 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 24 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of l m measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 280 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 125 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 12 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of l m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being
  525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal line
  being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 30 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on North Battleford Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2745 dated November 30, 1989, is an imaginary surface located at a
common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that common
plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer
surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on North Battleford Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2745
dated November 30, 1989, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 06-24 is 60 m in
  width, 30 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  842 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12-30 is 150 m in
  width, 75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  l 644 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The transitional surfaces, shown on North Battleford Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2745 dated November 30, 1989, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces associated with
  runway 06-24 are inclined planes rising at a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at right angles to the centre
  line and projected centre line of the strip and extending upward and
  outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach surfaces
  to an intersection with the outer surface or with another transitional
  surface of an adjoining strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces associated with
  runway 12-30 are inclined planes rising at a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the centre
  line and projected centre line of the strip and extending upward and
  outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach surfaces
  to an intersection with the outer surface or with another transitional
  surface of an adjoining strip.
