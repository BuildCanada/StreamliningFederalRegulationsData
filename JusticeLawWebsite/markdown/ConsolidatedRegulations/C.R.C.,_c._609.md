## Export of Consumable Stores Supplied to Vessels and Aircraft Permit {#export-of-consumable-stores-supplied-to-vessels-and-aircraft-permit .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 609
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

General Export Permit No. Ex. 3
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558973 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the Export of Consumable Stores Supplied to Vessels and Aircraft Permit.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558975 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,
[[consumable stores]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes fuel oils,
lubricants, provisions and supplies required for a voyage or flight and
medical and surgical supplies.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558977 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 15 of
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), any person may
supply from Canada to vessels and aircraft departing from Canada usual
and reasonable quantities of consumable stores where such stores are
intended for use by or on the vessels and aircraft taking them on board.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where completion and
validation of a customs entry form is required with respect to any
consumable stores that are exported under the authority of this Permit,
that form shall be endorsed \"Exported under the authority of General
Export Permit No. Ex. 3\" or \"Exporté en vertu de la Licence générale
d\'exportation n^o^ Ex. 3\".
