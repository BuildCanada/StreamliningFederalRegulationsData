## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197542 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada any goods
described in item 83 of the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If any goods imported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
must contain the statement *"Imported under the authority of General
Import Permit No. 83 --- Aluminum Products"* or *"[Importé en vertu de
la Licence générale d'importation n^o^ 83 --- Produits
d'aluminum]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"*.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197545 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
imports goods under the authority of this Permit must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 10 days after receipt of a request from the
  Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development, provide to the
  Department the documents and records referred to in section 4 in
  respect of any import made during the period specified in the request;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at all reasonable times

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} make the documents and records referred to in
    section 4 available for inspection by any persons authorized by the
    Minister, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provide to those persons all reasonable assistance
    to facilitate the inspection of those documents and records; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Department any documents and records
  necessary for making a determination on the country of origin, import
  value, or quantity of imported aluminum products, within the time
  period specified by the Department.

## [Documents and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197552 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada who
imports goods under this Permit must retain, for a period of six years
after the year in which the import is made, documents and records
containing the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the importer or consignee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof of Canadian residency;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of entry of the goods into Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, expressed in kilograms, of the goods;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country from which the goods are imported;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin of the goods;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the shipping document with freight and other
  transportation costs indicated separately;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the tariff classification of the goods indicated in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the import value in Canadian dollars; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the goods.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197564 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Permit comes into
force on September 1, 2019, but if it is registered after that day, it
comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
