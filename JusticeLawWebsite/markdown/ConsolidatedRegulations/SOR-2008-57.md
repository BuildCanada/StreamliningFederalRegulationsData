## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747762 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[delivery shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[delivery shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means shares issued by a
    bank or a bank holding company to a particular subsidiary for the
    purpose of an acquisition made under subsection 72.1(1) or 716.1(1)
    of the Act, respectively. ([actions remises]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[particular subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[particular subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a subsidiary
    body corporate referred to in subsections 72.1(1) and 716.1(1) of
    the Act. ([filiale visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Corporate Interrelationships]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747767 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsections 72.1(1) and 716.1(1) of the Act, the prescribed conditions
are that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the consideration received by the bank or the bank
  holding company for the delivery shares is equal to the fair market
  value of those shares at the time of their issuance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the class of shares of which the delivery shares are
  a part is widely held and shares of that class are actively traded on
  any of the following stock exchanges in Canada, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Venture Exchange,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} The Montreal Exchange, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Toronto Stock Exchange;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sole purpose of effecting the acquisition by the
  particular subsidiary of delivery shares is to transfer them, as set
  out in paragraph 3(b), to the shareholders of another body corporate;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the acquisition of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary, the other body corporate and its
  shareholders deal at arm's length, to be determined in accordance with
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), with the bank or the bank
  holding company and the particular subsidiary; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the acquisition of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary, the particular subsidiary and the
  other body corporate are not resident in Canada, for the purposes of
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsections 72.1(2) and 716.1(2) of the Act, the prescribed conditions
are that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particular subsidiary does not acquire a
  beneficial interest in the delivery shares as a result of its
  acquisition of those shares and the beneficial interest is acquired by
  the shareholders of the other body corporate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition by the particular subsidiary of the
  delivery shares is followed immediately by a transfer of the delivery
  shares by the particular subsidiary to shareholders of the other body
  corporate;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately after the transfer of the delivery shares
  to the shareholders of the other body corporate, the particular
  subsidiary and the other body corporate are not resident in Canada,
  for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} after the transfer of the delivery shares to the
  shareholders of the other body corporate, the other body corporate is
  a subsidiary body corporate of the particular subsidiary.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsections 72.1(3) and 716.1(3) of the Act, the prescribed requirements
are that, within 30 days after one of the conditions described in
section 2 or 3 is not met or ceases to be met, the bank or bank holding
company shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cancel the delivery shares on the condition that, if
  the by-laws limit the number of authorized shares, the delivery shares
  may be restored to the status of authorized but unissued shares;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} return the consideration received by the bank or bank
  holding company for the delivery shares to the particular subsidiary;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} cancel the entry for the consideration in the stated
  capital account of the bank or bank holding company.
