## Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review Committee Security and Confidentiality Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-external-review-committee-security-and-confidentiality-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

[ROYAL CANADIAN MOUNTED POLICE ACT](/eng/acts/R-10)

Registration 1988-07-21

Regulations Respecting the Security and Confidentiality of Information
to which Members of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review
Committee and Persons Employed by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
External Review Committee May Have Access in the Performance of their
Duties

P.C. 1988-1478 1988-07-21

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912692 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review Committee
Security and Confidentiality Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-397).

## [Security Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912694 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every member of the
Committee and every person employed by the Committee who is required to
receive or obtain information relating to any matter under the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10) shall, in the performance
of their duties, comply with any security and confidentiality
requirements applicable to individuals who normally have access to and
use of that information and shall take the oath of secrecy or the
affirmation of secrecy set out in the schedule.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-912697 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Oath of Secrecy]{.HTitleText1} {#oath-of-secrecy .SchedHeadL1}

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-solid}, solemnly swear that
I will not, without due authority, disclose or make known to any person
not legally entitled thereto any information acquired by me by reason of
the duties performed by me on behalf of or under the direction of the
Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review Committee or by reason of
any office or employment held by me pursuant to the [Royal Canadian
Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10). So help me God.

reason of the duties performed by me on behalf of or under the direction
of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police External Review Committee or by
reason of any office or employment held by me pursuant to the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Act](/eng/acts/R-10).
