the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed [United States Surtax

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- public health, public
safety and national security

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable under the United States
Surtax Order (2025-1), the [United States Surtax Order (Steel and
Aluminum 2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-95) or the [United States
Surtax Order (Motor Vehicles 2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-118) in
respect of goods imported by or on behalf of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a government health research organization or clinical
  health research organization;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an organization that produces or stores medical
  countermeasures, including pharmaceuticals or medical devices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the office of a [[public health
  official]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection C.10.001(1)
  of the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an organization that provides ambulance or other
  emergency response services;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a firefighting service;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a law enforcement agency;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a federal or provincial correctional service;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Department of National Defence;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Forces; or

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Security Intelligence Service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- health care

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable under the United States
Surtax Order (2025-1), the [United States Surtax Order (Steel and
Aluminum 2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-95) or the [United States
Surtax Order (Motor Vehicles 2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-118) in
respect of goods imported

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for use in the provision of medically necessary
  health care services, including services provided at

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a hospital,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a health care or dental clinic,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a medical, dental or diagnostic laboratory, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a long-term care facility; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by or on behalf of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that provides products or services
    related to blood, cells, tissues or organs for medically necessary
    health care, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a federal, provincial, local or Indigenous health
    authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- manufacture, processing or
packaging

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable under the United States
Surtax Order (2025-1) or the [United States Surtax Order (Steel and
Aluminum 2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-95) in respect of goods
imported for use, in Canada, in the manufacture or processing of any
good or the packaging of a food product or beverage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- goods referred to in
Schedule 1

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable under the United States
Surtax Order (2025-1) in respect of the goods referred to in column 1 of
Schedule 1 that are classified under a tariff classification number set
out in column 2.

**[[4.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable under the [United
States Surtax Order (Motor Vehicles
2025)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-118) in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} motor vehicles that are classified under a tariff
  classification number set out in Schedule 5; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all-terrain vehicles, including utility terrain
  vehicles and side-by-side vehicles.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on the
following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the good is imported into Canada before

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} October 16, 2025, in the case of a good in respect
    of which remission is granted under any of sections 1 to 4, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} March 13, 2026, in the case of a good referred to
    in Schedule 3;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a good referred to in column 3 of
  Schedule 4 that is classified under the tariff classification number
  set out in column 2, it is imported into Canada by a person whose
  business number is set out in column 1 during the period, if any,
  specified in column 4 and any conditions set out in column 5 are met;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no other claim for relief of the surtax has been
  granted under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of
  the good; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer makes a claim for remission to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the date of importation.
