## Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety Regulations {#motor-vehicle-restraint-systems-and-booster-seats-safety-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-90
:::

[MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/M-10.01)

Registration 2010-04-29

Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety Regulations

P.C. 2010-545 2010-04-29
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 11(3) of the [Motor Vehicle Safety
Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA}, a copy of the
proposed [Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-90), substantially in the annexed
form, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/),
Part I, on October 10, 2009, and a reasonable opportunity was afforded
to interested persons to make representations to the Minister of
Transport with respect to the proposed Regulations;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1993, c.
  16
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport pursuant to subsection 11(1)
of the [Motor Vehicle Safety Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-90).
:::

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-768329 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768330 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Motor Vehicle Safety
      Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[booster seat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[booster seat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a removable device
      designed to be used in a vehicle for seating a person whose mass
      is at least 18 kg, to ensure that the seat belt assembly fits
      properly. ([siège d'appoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[car bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[car bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a restraint system for an
      infant with special needs that is designed to restrain the infant
      in a supine or prone position on a continuous flat surface.  ([lit
      d'auto]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose mass is more
      than 10 kg and not more than 30 kg.  ([enfant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CMVSS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CMVSS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canada Motor Vehicle Safety
      Standard.  ([NSVAC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[custom restraint system for a disabled person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[custom restraint system for a disabled
      person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a restraint system, other than
      a mass-produced restraint system, designed for a specific disabled
      person. ([ensemble de retenue sur mesure pour personne
      handicapée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disabled person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disabled person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, other than
      an infant with special needs, who, for orthopaedic reasons or
      because of the person's build or other physical characteristics,
      is unable to use an infant restraint system, a child restraint
      system, a booster seat, a built-in device referred to in item
      213.4 of Schedule III to the [Motor Vehicle Safety
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038), or a vehicle seat
      belt. ([personne handicapée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[infant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is unable to
      walk unassisted and whose mass is not more than 10 kg.
       ([bébé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[infant with special needs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[infant with special needs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an infant
      who is unable to use an infant restraint system and

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who was born at less than 37 weeks' gestation;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose mass at birth was less than 2.2 kg; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who has special breathing needs. ([bébé qui a
        des besoins spéciaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lower connector system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lower connector system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system
      consisting of two connectors that each fit inside a checking
      device having the envelope dimensions shown in Figure 9 of
      Schedule 7, that are attached to the lower part of a restraint
      system or booster seat in a manner that does not allow for their
      removal without the use of tools, and that allow the restraint
      system or booster seat to be securely attached to a lower
      universal anchorage system of a vehicle.  ([système d'attaches
      inférieures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lower universal anchorage system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lower universal anchorage system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      device, other than a vehicle seat belt, that is designed to secure
      the lower portion of a restraint system or booster seat to a
      vehicle, and that transfers the load from the restraint system or
      booster seat and its occupant to the vehicle structure or a
      vehicle seat structure. ([dispositif universel d'ancrages
      d'attaches inférieurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mass-produced]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mass-produced]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means manufactured, in
      whatever quantity, using only standard or uniform
      parts. ([fabriqué en série]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restraint system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restraint system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a removable device
      designed to be used together with the seat of a vehicle in order
      to restraint an infant, an infant with special needs, a child or a
      disabled person, but does not include a booster seat or a vehicle
      seat belt.  ([ensemble de retenue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[seat orientation reference line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[seat orientation reference line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[SORL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the horizontal line passing
      through the plane of symmetry of the standard seat assembly and
      passing through the Z point as shown in Figures 3 and 4 of
      Schedule 7. ([ligne repère d'orientation du siège ou
      LROS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard seat assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard seat assembly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seat that
      is specified in the drawing package entitled Standard Seat
      Assembly Specifications for Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and
      Booster Seats Safety Regulations Compliance Testing (January 1,
      2010), published by the Department of Transport, and that has seat
      belt anchorage points and a lower universal anchorage system
      located as indicated in Figures 3 and 4 of Schedule 7,
      respectively. ([siège normalisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tether strap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tether strap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is fitted
      with a tether strap hook and secured to the rigid structure of a
      restraint system or booster seat, and that transfers the load from
      the restraint system or booster seat and its occupant to the
      user-ready tether anchorage. ([courroie
      d'attache]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tether strap hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tether strap hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is
      used to attach a tether strap to a user-ready tether anchorage and
      that has an interface profile shown in Figure 1 of Schedule 7 or,
      in the case of a device with integrated adjustment hardware,
      Figure 2 of Schedule 7.  ([crochet de la courroie
      d'attache]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[torso]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[torso]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the body of an
      anthropomorphic test device or an occupant, excluding the thighs,
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the device or occupant is seated in a
        restraint system other than a car bed or in a booster seat, lies
        between the top of the restraint system seating surface or
        booster seat seating surface and the top of the shoulders; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the device or occupant is seated in a car
        bed, lies between the top of the continuous flat surface of the
        car bed and the top of the shoulders. ([torse]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[TSD 209]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[TSD 209]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Technical Standards Document
      No. 209, Seat Belt Assemblies, published by the Department of
      Transport, as amended from time to time. ([DNT
      209]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[TSD 302]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[TSD 302]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Technical Standards Document
      No. 302, Flammability of Interior Materials, published by the
      Department of Transport, as amended from time to time. ([DNT
      302]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[user-ready tether anchorage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[user-ready tether anchorage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device
      that transfers the tether strap load from a restraint system or
      booster seat and its occupant to the vehicle structure or a
      vehicle seat structure, and that is designed to accept a tether
      strap hook directly, without requiring the installation of any
      other device. ([ancrage d'attache prêt à
      utiliser]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-307, s. 31\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of vehicles

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, "classes of vehicles" refers to
  those classes of vehicles prescribed by section 4 of the [Motor
  Vehicle Safety Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1038) and set
  out in Schedule III to those Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- restraint system or
  booster seat

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a reference in
  TSD 209 to webbing, a belt buckle, a tether strap attachment or a
  related piece of adjustment hardware that is part of a Type 1 seat
  belt assembly is to be read as a reference to webbing, a belt buckle,
  a tether strap attachment or a related piece of adjustment hardware
  that is part of a restraint system or booster seat, as the case may
  be.

### [National Safety Mark]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768367 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National Safety Mark

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes these Regulations, the symbol set out in Schedule 2 is
  prescribed as the national safety mark.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization by Minister

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 3(2) of the Act, on
  application by a company, the Minister may, in the form set out in
  Schedule 1, authorize the company to apply the national safety mark to
  a restraint system or booster seat and to any accompanying
  documentation or any packaging.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company that intends to apply the national safety
  mark to a restraint system or booster seat must apply to the Minister
  to obtain the authorization referred to in subsection (1.1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}National safety mark

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company that applies the national safety mark to a
  restraint system or booster seat must reproduce the national safety
  mark as shown in Schedule 2, including the following information, in
  the locations indicated in that schedule:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authorization number assigned to the company by
    the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number or numbers of the CMVSS to which the
    restraint system or booster seat conforms, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 213, in the case of a child restraint system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 213.1, in the case of an infant restraint
      system,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 213.2, in the case of a booster seat,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 213.3, in the case of a mass-produced restraint
      system for disabled persons,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} 213.3, in the case of a custom restraint system
      for a disabled person that is designed to be used only in school
      buses, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} 213.5, in the case of a restraint system for
      infants with special needs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The national safety mark must be at least 50 mm in
  diameter and be stitched onto the restraint system or booster seat,
  indelibly moulded into or onto it, or indelibly printed on a label
  affixed to it in a permanent manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visibility of national safety mark

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The national safety mark must, when applied to a
  restraint system or booster seat, be fully visible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of national safety mark ---
  removable base

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a restraint system is manufactured with a
  removable base and the seating component of the restraint system is
  designed to be used in a vehicle with or without the base, the
  national safety mark must be applied to the seating component.

### [Prescribed Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768394 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CVMSS 213

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every child restraint system must
  conform to the applicable standards set out in Part 2, CMVSS 213 ---
  Child Restraint Systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CVMSS 213.1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every infant restraint system must conform to the
  applicable standards set out in Part 3, CMVSS 213.1 --- Infant
  Restraint Systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CVMSS 213.2

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every booster seat must conform to the applicable
  standards set out in Part 4, CMVSS 213.2 --- Booster Seats.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CVMSS 213.3

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for disabled persons must
  conform to the applicable standards set out in Part 5, CMVSS 213.3 ---
  Restraint Systems for Disabled Persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CVMSS 213.5

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for infants with special needs
  must conform to the applicable standards set out in Part 6, CMVSS
  213.5 --- Restraint Systems for Infants with Special Needs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable CVMSS

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system that is designed to be used as
  more than one type of restraint system or as a restraint system and
  booster seat must conform to the standards set out in Parts 2 to 6
  that are applicable to each type of restraint system or booster seat
  for which it is designed to be used.

### [Restraint System and Booster Seat Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768408 .Subheading}

#### [National Safety Mark]{.HTitleText3} {#h-768409 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No importation without national safety
mark

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** A company must not
import into Canada a restraint system other than a custom restraint
system for a disabled person that is not designed to be used only in
school buses, or a booster seat, unless the company has applied the
national safety mark to the restraint system or booster seat.

#### [Lower Universal Anchorage System Symbol]{.HTitleText3} {#h-768412 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lower universal anchorage system symbol

**[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** Every restraint system
or booster seat that is equipped with a lower connector system must bear
the lower universal anchorage system symbol shown in Schedule 3, on a
background of contrasting colour, on or near the lower connector system,
and the symbol must be fully visible to a person installing the
restraint system or booster seat when the restraint system or booster
seat is positioned in the vehicle.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768415 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must maintain --- for each restraint system or booster seat to
  which it applies the national safety mark or that it imports into
  Canada --- the records referred to in paragraph 5(1)(g) of the Act and
  retain those records, in paper form or in readily readable electronic
  form, for a period of at least five years after the day on which the
  restraint system or booster seat is manufactured or imported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the records referred to in subsection (1) are
  maintained by a person on behalf of the company, the company must keep
  the name and address of the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request from inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On request in writing from an inspector, a company
  must send to that inspector a copy, in either official language, of
  the records referred to in subsection (1) within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 working days after day on which the request is
    mailed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the records must be translated, 45 working days
    after the day on which the request is mailed.

### [Registration Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768423 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information card

- **[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of maintaining the registration system referred to in
  paragraph 5(1)(h) of the Act, a company must provide to each person
  who purchases a restraint system or booster seat an information card,
  in both official languages, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permits the purchaser to provide to the company or
    to a duly authorized representative of the company, at no cost, the
    purchaser's name, mailing address and email address, the model name
    and number of the restraint system or booster seat, the date of
    purchase and the date of manufacture; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} includes a safety message concerning the importance
    of providing the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be included in
  registration system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The registration system maintained by a company in
  accordance with paragraph 5(1)(h) of the Act must consist of the
  information provided to the company under paragraph (1)(a), and that
  information must be used only for the purposes of paragraph 5(1)(h) of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information in the registration system maintained
  by a company in relation to a restraint system or booster seat must be
  kept for at least five years after the day on which the restraint
  system or booster seat is purchased.

### [Importation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768433 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-768434 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act, a company that imports into Canada a
restraint system or booster seat must make, at the nearest customs
office that is open for business, a declaration signed by the company's
duly authorized representative that contains the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the restraint system
  or booster seat;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the company importing the
  restraint system or booster seat;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2013-117, s. 14\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the manufacturer or its duly
  authorized representative that the restraint system or booster seat
  conforms to the prescribed standards applicable on the date of
  manufacture;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the model name and number of the restraint system or
  booster seat;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of restraint systems and the number of
  booster seats imported at the same time; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the restraint system or booster
  seat was imported.

#### [Temporary Importation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-768446 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed purposes

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 7(1)(a) of the Act, the prescribed purposes
  for which a restraint system or booster seat may be imported
  temporarily are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exhibition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} demonstration;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evaluation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} testing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} further manufacturing prior to export.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declaration referred to in paragraph 7(1)(a) of
  the Act must be filed with the Minister, be signed by the person
  importing the restraint system or booster seat and contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the restraint
    system or booster seat;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer's name, mailing address, telephone
    number, facsimile number and email address and, if the importer is a
    company, the contact information of a contact person at the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the make and the model name or number of the
    restraint system or booster seat;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the restraint system or booster
    seat is to be presented for importation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the restraint system or
    booster seat is being imported and a statement that it will be used
    only for that purpose;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the restraint system or
    booster seat will remain in Canada for a period of not more than one
    year or the period specified by the Minister, as applicable;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the restraint system or
    booster seat will be exported or destroyed before the end of the
    one-year period or the period specified by the Minister, as
    applicable; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the declaration is signed by a representative, a
    statement by the importer indicating that the representative is
    authorized to sign.

### [Notice of Defect]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768449 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed person

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 10(1) of the Act, a person, other than the
  first retail purchaser, who obtained a restraint system or booster
  seat from a company is a prescribed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and language

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of defect that is required to be given under
  subsection 10(1) of the Act must be in writing, in either paper or
  electronic form, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice given to the Minister, be
    in either official language; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice given to the current owner
    of the restraint system or booster seat or a prescribed person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be in the person's official language of choice,
      if it is known, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be in both official languages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must give the notice of defect to the
  current owner of the restraint system or booster seat and to the
  prescribed person as soon as feasible, but not later than 60 days
  after the day on which the company gives the notice of defect to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- content

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice of defect that is given to the Minister
  must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name and its contact information for
    the purpose of correspondence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the restraint
    system or booster seat;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification that is
    assigned by the company to the notice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each restraint system or booster seat that may
    contain the defect, its model name and number, the prescribed class
    of equipment and any other information that is necessary to permit
    its identification;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the restraint systems or
    booster seats were manufactured;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of restraint systems or
    booster seats that could potentially contain the defect;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the estimated percentage of the restraint systems
    or booster seats referred to in paragraph (f) that contain the
    defect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the defect,
    including the causes and contributing factors, if known, and a
    description of the location of the defect;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the systems or components of the restraint system
    or booster seat that may be affected by the defect;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led to
    the determination of the existence of the defect;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} all relevant information, including a summary of
    warranty claims, field reports and service reports, with their dates
    of receipt, that the company used to determine the existence of the
    defect;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety risk to persons arising
    from the defect;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the corrective measures to be
    taken in respect of the defect and how they are to be implemented;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any precautions that may be taken to minimize the
    safety risk until the corrective measures are implemented; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the estimated date on which the notice of defect
    will be sent to the current owner of the restraint system or booster
    seat and the estimated date on which the notice of defect will be
    sent to a prescribed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unavailable information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The company is not required to provide the Minister
  with the information referred to in paragraphs (4)(j), (k), (m)
  and (n) if it is not available on the day on which the notice is given
  but must provide that information as soon as it is available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to owner --- content

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A notice of defect that is given to the current owner
  of the restraint system or booster seat must contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the model name and number of the restraint system
    or booster seat;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following statements:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} "This notice is sent to you in accordance with
      the requirements of the [Motor Vehicle Safety
      Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01).", and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} "This is to inform you that your restraint
      system or booster seat may contain a defect that could affect the
      safety of a person.";

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification that is
    assigned by the company to the notice;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle safety recall number that is
    issued by the Department of Transport;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the defect,
    including the causes, and a description of the location of the
    defect;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the systems or components of the restraint system
    or booster seat that may be affected by the defect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} operating conditions or other factors that may
    cause a malfunction of the system or component of the restraint
    system or booster seat;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the warning signs, if any, of any malfunction that
    could arise as a result of the defect;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety risk to persons arising
    from the defect;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the type of injury that may result from the defect;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the corrective measures to be
    taken in respect of the defect and how they are to be implemented,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the work involved,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time required in order to take the
      corrective measures,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the company will cover the
      costs of the corrective measures or an estimate of the cost of the
      corrective measures to the current owner of the restraint system
      or booster seat, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} information identifying the persons who can
      implement the corrective measures; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any precautions that may be taken to minimize the
    safety risk until the corrective measures are implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unavailable information

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The company is not required to provide the current
  owner with the information referred to in paragraph (6)(l) if it is
  not available on the day on which the notice is given but must provide
  that information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as it is available; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the same time as the company provides the
    information required under subsection 10.4(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required wording

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The words "SAFETY",  "RECALL", "[RAPPEL]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}" and "[SÉCURITÉ]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" are required to be
  clearly visible

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the envelope, or through a window of the
    envelope, in upper case and in a font size that is larger than the
    one used for the recipient's address, if the notice of defect is
    given to the current owner in paper form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the subject line of the communication, in upper
    case, if the notice of defect is given to the current owner in
    electronic form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to prescribed person --- content

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A notice of defect that is given to a prescribed
  person must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each restraint system or booster seat that may
    contain the defect, its model name and number and any other
    information that is necessary to permit its identification;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification that is
    assigned by the company to the notice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle safety recall number issued by
    the Department of Transport;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the defect,
    including the causes, and a description of the location of the
    defect;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} operating conditions or other factors that may
    cause a malfunction of the system or component of the restraint
    system or booster seat;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety risk to persons arising
    from the defect;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the corrective measures to be
    taken in respect of the defect and how they are to be implemented;
    and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any precautions that may be taken to minimize the
    safety risk until the corrective measures are implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unavailable information

### [Notice of Non-compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1190968 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed person

- **[[110.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110.01
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of
  subsection 10.1(1) of the Act, a person, other than the first retail
  purchaser, who obtained a restraint system or booster seat from a
  company is a prescribed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and Language

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of non-compliance that is required to be
  given under subsection 10.1(1) of the Act must be in writing, in
  either paper or electronic form, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice given to the Minister, be
    in either official language; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice given to the current owner
    of the restraint system or booster seat or a prescribed person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be in the person's official language of choice,
      if it is known, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be in both official languages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless the notice of non-compliance contains a
  statement under subparagraph (4)(k)(i), a company must give the notice
  of non-compliance to the current owner of the restraint system or
  booster seat and to the prescribed person as soon as feasible, but not
  later than 60 days after the day on which the company gives the notice
  of non-compliance to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- statement denied

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} If the notice of non-compliance contains a
  statement under subparagraph (4)(k)(i) and the Minister advises the
  company that the Minister has determined that the non-compliance is
  not inconsequential to safety, the company must give the notice of
  non-compliance to the current owner of the restraint system or booster
  seat and to the prescribed person as soon as feasible after the day on
  which the company receives the Minister's determination, but not later
  than 60 days after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- content

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A notice of non-compliance that is given to the
  Minister must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company's name and its contact information for
    the purpose of correspondence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer of the restraint
    system or booster seat;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification that is
    assigned by the company to the notice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each restraint system or booster seat that may
    be non-compliant, the model name and number, the prescribed class of
    equipment and any other information that is necessary to permit its
    identification;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the restraint systems or
    booster seats were manufactured;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of restraint systems or
    booster seats that could potentially be non-compliant;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the estimated percentage of the restraint systems
    or booster seats referred to in paragraph (f) that are
    non-compliant;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the non-compliance, including the
    applicable regulatory requirement, the causes and contributing
    factors, if known;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the systems or components of the restraint system
    or booster seat that may be affected by the non-compliance;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a chronology of the principal events that led to
    the determination of the non-compliance, including the test results,
    observations, inspections and any other relevant information;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} as the case may be,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the non-compliance is
      inconsequential to safety, including detailed information to
      support the statement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety risk to persons
      arising from the non-compliance;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the corrective measures to be
    taken in respect of the non-compliance and how they are to be
    implemented;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any precautions that may be taken to minimize the
    safety risk until the corrective measures are implemented; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the estimated date on which the notice of
    non-compliance will be sent to the current owner of the restraint
    system or booster seat and the estimated date on which the notice of
    non-compliance will be sent to a prescribed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unavailable information

### [Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1191048 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial report --- notice to current
owners

- **[[110.02]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110.02
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within five working days after the
  day on which a company begins sending a notice of defect or
  non-compliance to current owners, the company must provide the
  Minister with a report containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sample of the envelope used to mail the notice;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the company began sending
    notices;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the company finished, or expects
    to finish, sending notices; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of restraint systems or booster seats
    that are subject to the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), a company is not required
  to provide a sample of the envelope if it uses an envelope that has
  previously been provided as a sample to the Minister and the report
  includes the date on which the sample was provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial report --- notice to prescribed
  persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within five working days after the day on which a
  company begins sending a notice of defect or non-compliance to
  prescribed persons, the company must provide to the Minister a report
  containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no notices are sent to current owners, the
    number of restraint systems or booster seats that are subject to the
    notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Follow-up reports

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For a period of five years beginning on the 60th day
  after the day on which a company gives a notice to the Minister under
  subsection 10(1) or 10.1(1) of the Act, the company must provide the
  Minister, within five working days after the day on which they are
  sent to their recipients, a copy of any of the following
  communications, with the date on which they were sent to their
  recipients:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} communications sent to more than one current owner
    relating to the defect or non-compliance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communications sent to more than one prescribed
    person relating to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in subsection 110(9) or
      110.01(9), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the defect or non-compliance.

### [Publication of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1506014 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on website

- **[[110.04]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110.04
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A company that gives a notice of
  defect or non-compliance to the Minister under subsection 10(1) or
  10.1(1) of the Act must publish the following information, in both
  official languages, on the website that the company uses to
  communicate information directed at the Canadian market:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the notice was given to the
    Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the model name and number of the restraint system
    or booster seat referred to in the notice and any other information
    that is necessary to permit its identification;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number, title or other identification that is
    assigned by the company to the notice;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle safety recall number that is
    issued by the Department of Transport;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the defect, including the causes of
      the defect, and its location, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the non-compliance, including the causes;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the safety risk to persons arising
    from the defect or non-compliance;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the type of injury that may result from the defect
    or non-compliance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the corrective measures to be
    taken in respect of the defect or non-compliance and how they are to
    be implemented;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any precautions that may be taken to minimize the
    safety risk until the corrective measures are implemented;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the parts and facilities that are
    necessary to correct the defect or non-compliance are available or,
    if they are not, the earliest date by which they are expected to be
    available;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a statement indicating that
    information in respect of notices that are given before a specified
    date is not available on the website and how to obtain the
    information;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} on each web page displaying the information
    referred to in paragraphs (a) to (k), instructions on how to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} contact the company concerning any questions
      related to the notice,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} report a safety concern relating to a restraint
      system or booster seat, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} inform the company of a transfer of ownership
      of a restraint system or booster seat or a change of address; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the information was last updated
    on each web page displaying the information referred to in
    paragraphs (a) to (k).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must publish the information referred to
  in subsection (1) as soon as feasible, but not later than 60 days
  after the day on which the company gives the notice to the Minister,
  and must update the information within seven days after the day on
  which there is any change to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if the information referred
  to in paragraph (1)(d), (h) or (j) is not available on the day on
  which it is to be published, the company must publish the information
  within seven days after the day on which it becomes available.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum period of availability of
  information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that the information referred
  to in subsection (1) remains displayed on the website for at least 10
  years beginning on the day on which the company gives the notice of
  defect or non-compliance to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hyperlink

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subparagraphs
  (1)(l)(i) to (iii) may be made available by way of a hyperlink.

### [Contactable Surfaces]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768551 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contactable surfaces

**[[209]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209 .sectionLabel}** Every child restraint
system must provide

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the support of the child's back, a continuous
  surface that is flat or concave and has an area of not less than
  54 800 mm^2^; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the support of the sides of the child's torso,
  continuous surfaces that are flat or concave and have an area of not
  less than 30 500 mm^2^ each.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[210]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-210 .sectionLabel}** A child restraint
system must not have any surface directly in front of the child, unless
the surface is designed to limit the forward movement of the child.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-sections of surface

**[[211]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-211 .sectionLabel}** Every horizontal
cross-section of a surface of a child restraint system that is designed
to limit the forward movement of a child must be flat or concave, and
every vertical longitudinal cross-section of that surface must be flat
or convex with a radius of curvature of the underlying structure of not
less than 50 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rigid structural elements

**[[212]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-212 .sectionLabel}** Any rigid structural
element underlying a contactable surface of a child restraint system
must not have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a protrusion, with any padding or flexible overlay
  material removed, of more than 9.5 mm; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exposed edge with a radius of less than 6.4 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surface contactable by head

**[[213]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-213 .sectionLabel}** Every surface of a
child restraint system that is contactable by the head of an
anthropomorphic test device positioned in the restraint system in
accordance with subsection 4.4.2 or 4.5.2 of Test Method 213 must be
covered with slow-recovery, energy-absorbing material that, when tested
in accordance with section 6 of Test Method 213, has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a resistance of not less than 4 kPa but not more than
  70 kPa at 25% of compression-deflection resistance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a thickness of not less than 12 mm, if the material
  has a resistance of not less than 12 kPa but not more than 70 kPa at
  25% of compression-deflection resistance; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a thickness of not less than 19 mm, if the material
  has a resistance of not less than 4 kPa but less than 12 kPa at 25% of
  compression-deflection resistance.

### [Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-768569 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inversion testing

**[[214]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-214 .sectionLabel}** A child restraint
system that is subjected to an inversion test in accordance with section
7 of Test Method 213 must not fall out of the aircraft passenger seat
belt, and the anthropomorphic test device must not fall out of the
restraint system, at any time during the rotation or three-second
immobilisation period referred to in that section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dynamic testing

- **[[215]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-215 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  child restraint system that is subjected to a dynamic test in
  accordance with section 4 of Test Method 213 must, when in any
  adjustment position for which there is no warning under subparagraph
  218(1)(e)(iii),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exhibit no complete separation of any load-bearing
    structural element, and no partial separation exposing a surface
    with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a protrusion of more than 9.5 mm, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a radius of less than 6.4 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remain in the same adjustment position during the
    test as it was in immediately before the test began;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of a restraint system tested
    with the anthropomorphic test device specified in subpart S, part
    572, chapter V, title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations of the
    United States (revised as of October 1, 2012), limit the resultant
    acceleration at the location of the accelerometer mounted in the
    upper thorax of the anthropomorphic test device to not more than 60
    g, except for intervals of not more than 3 ms;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of a restraint system tested
    with the anthropomorphic test device specified in subpart S, part
    572, chapter V, title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations of the
    United States (revised as of October 1, 2012), limit the resultant
    acceleration of the centre of gravity of the head of the
    anthropomorphic test device during the movement of the head towards
    the front of the vehicle to not more than 80 g, except for intervals
    of not more than 3 ms, unless it is established that any resultant
    acceleration above 80 g is caused by another part of the
    anthropomorphic test device striking its head;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 216(2), limit the movement of
    the head of the anthropomorphic test device towards the rear of the
    restraint system by means of a continuous seat back that is an
    integral part of the restraint system; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 216(2), limit the rotation of
    the head of the anthropomorphic test device towards the rear of the
    restraint system, in its midsagittal plane, by means of a continuous
    seat back that is an integral part of the restraint system, so that
    the angle between the head and the torso is at no time during the
    test more than 45° as compared to the angle between the head and the
    torso prior to the test.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuous seat back

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The continuous seat back referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(e) and (f) must have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a height

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of at least 500 mm, in the case of a child
      restraint system recommended by the manufacturer for use by a

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of at least 560 mm, in the case of a child
      restraint system recommended by the manufacturer for use by a
      child whose mass is more than 18 kg; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a width of at least 200 mm, measured in the
    horizontal plane at the height specified in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement of height --- paragraph
  (2)(a)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The height referred to in paragraph (2)(a) must be
  measured in a plane parallel to the surface of the seat back of the
  child restraint system and orthogonal to the vertical longitudinal
  plane passing through the longitudinal centreline of the restraint
  system, from the lowest point of the restraint system's seating
  surface that is contacted by the buttocks of the seated
  anthropomorphic test device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (2)(b), if the child restraint
  system provides surfaces for the support of the sides of the torso,

  surface of the portion of the restraint system provided for the

  system may have a continuous seat back width of not less than 150 mm,
  measured in the horizontal plane at the height referred to in
  paragraph (2)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Belt

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every belt that is part of a child restraint system
  and that is designed to restrain a child in the restraint system must
  not, when subjected to a dynamic test in accordance with section 4 of
  Test Method 213, impose on the anthropomorphic test device any loads
  that result from the mass of the restraint system or the mass of the
  seat back of the standard seat assembly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint of torso and crotch

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrain the upper torso by means of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a forward-facing restraint system,

    system at the pelvic restraint attachment points, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a fixed or movable surface that conforms to the

  restrain the crotch by means of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a crotch belt that is connectable to the pelvic
    restraint or to any other device used to restrain the lower torso,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a fixed or movable surface that conforms to the
    requirements of section 514.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with a lower

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint system designed to face the

**[[601]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-601 .sectionLabel}** Every restraint system

vehicle, except that a car bed must be designed to rest on the vehicle's

vertical longitudinal plane passing through the longitudinal axis of the
vehicle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Torso restraint

**[[602]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-602 .sectionLabel}** Every restraint system
for infants with special needs must, when the anthropomorphic test
device is positioned in the restraint system in accordance with, in the
case of a car bed, subsection 4.5.2 or 4.6.2 of Test Method 213.5 or, in
the case of all other restraint systems for infants with special needs,
subsection 4.5.3 or 4.6.3 of Test Method 213.5,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrain the upper torso by means of belts passing
  over each shoulder; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} restrain the lower torso.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of securing restraint system

- **[[603]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-603
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), every
  restraint system for infants with special needs must be designed to be
  secured to a vehicle

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of a vehicle seat belt in such a manner
    that the belt will not impose directly on the infant any loads that
    result from the mass of the restraint system, and without using any
    other means of attachment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with a lower
    connector system, by means of the lower connector system, without
    using any other means of attachment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint system with tether strap

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a restraint system for infants with special needs
  is equipped with a tether strap and the manufacturer recommends its
  use, the restraint system must be designed to be secured to a vehicle

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of the tether strap together with a
    vehicle seat belt in such a manner that the belt will not impose
    directly on the infant any loads that result from the mass of the
    restraint system, and without using any other means of attachment;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with a lower
    connector system, by means of the tether strap together with the
    lower connector system, without using any other means of attachment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint system with removable base

**[[604]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-604 .sectionLabel}** If a restraint system
for infants with special needs is equipped with a lower connector system
and is manufactured with a removable base, and the seating component of
the restraint system is designed to be used in a vehicle with or without
the base, the restraint system must be equipped with the lower connector
system on the base.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Car bed

**[[605]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-605 .sectionLabel}** Every car bed must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide restraint against the sideways movement of
  the infant's head towards the front of the vehicle by means of a head
  restraint; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have, in the shell of the car bed, a means of
  minimizing, when the car bed is installed in a vehicle in accordance
  with the manufacturer's instructions, any loads to the top of the
  infant's head in case of a side impact.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint system to be adjustable

**[[606]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-606 .sectionLabel}** Every part of a
restraint system for infants with special needs that is designed to
restrain an infant must be adjustable to snugly fit an infant whose mass
and height are within the ranges indicated in the statement referred to
in paragraph 616(1)(d), when the infant is positioned in the restraint
system in accordance with the instructions referred to in paragraph
618(1)(b) and the restraint system is adjusted in accordance with the
instructions referred to in paragraph 618(1)(c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audible or visible indication

**[[607]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-607 .sectionLabel}** A restraint system for
infants with special needs that is equipped with a lower connector
system must provide a clear, audible indication when each connector in a
lower connector system is securely attached to the lower universal
anchorage system or a clear, visual indication that each connector is
securely attached to the lower universal anchorage system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flammability

**[[608]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-608 .sectionLabel}** Every restraint system
for infants with special needs must be constructed only of materials
that conform to the requirements of TSD 302.

### [Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769176 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dynamic testing

- **[[615]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-615 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  restraint system for infants with special needs that is subjected to a
  dynamic test in accordance with section 4 of Test Method 213.5 must,
  if the adjustment position of each component of the restraint system
  is in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exhibit no complete separation of any load-bearing
    structural element, and no partial separation exposing a surface
    with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a protrusion of more than 9.5 mm, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a radius of less than 6.4 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a restraint system other than a car
    bed, remain in the same adjustment position during the test as it
    was in immediately before the test began, except that, if the
    restraint system has a means of automatically repositioning the
    seating surface to allow the anthropomorphic test device to move
    from a reclined position to a more upright position and back to a
    reclined position during the test, the seating surface does not have
    to remain in the same adjustment position during the test as it was
    in immediately before the test began;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a restraint system other than a car
    bed, retain the torso of the anthropomorphic test device within the
    restraint system, and not allow any portion of the target points on
    either side of the device's head, located on the transverse axis
    passing through the centre of mass of the device's head and
    perpendicular to the head's midsagittal plane, to pass at any time,
    during or immediately after the test, through the transverse
    orthogonal planes formed by the extension of the seat back frontal
    surface plane of the restraint system and by the plane that passes
    through the uppermost point of the restraint system, as shown in
    Figure 7 of Schedule 7, or through the vertical transverse plane
    passing through point X on the standard seat assembly, as shown in
    Figure 8 of Schedule 7;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a restraint system other than a car
    bed, not allow the angle between the vertical and the back and head
    support surface, measured 240 mm above the seating surface, to be
    more than 70° at any time during the test;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a restraint system other than a car
    bed, limit the movement of the head of the anthropomorphic test
    device towards the rear of the restraint system by means of a
    continuous seat back that is an integral part of the restraint
    system;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a restraint system other than a car
    bed, limit the rotation of the head of the anthropomorphic test
    device towards the rear of the restraint system, in its midsagittal
    plane, by means of a continuous seat back that is an integral part
    of the restraint system, so that the angle between the head and the
    torso is at no time during the test more than 45° as compared to the
    angle between the head and the torso prior to the test; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a car bed, retain the head, neck and
    torso of the anthropomorphic test device within the confines of the
    car bed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dynamic testing --- levelling device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A restraint system for infants with special needs
  that is equipped with a levelling device and that is subjected to a
  dynamic test in accordance with section 4 of Test Method 213.5 must
  conform to the requirements of paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) if the
  adjustment position of each component of the restraint system, except
  the levelling device, is in accordance with the manufacturer's
  instructions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuous seat back

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The continuous seat back referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(e) and (f) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a height of at least 500 mm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a width of at least 200 mm, measured in the
    horizontal plane at the height specified in paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} not impose any loads on the top of the head of the
    anthropomorphic test device during the dynamic test referred to in
    subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement of height --- paragraph
  3(a)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The height referred to in paragraph (3)(a) must be
  measured in a plane parallel to the surface of the seat back of the
  restraint system for infants with special needs and orthogonal to the
  vertical longitudinal plane passing through the longitudinal
  centreline of the restraint system, from the lowest point of the
  restraint system's seating surface that is contacted by the buttocks
  of the seated anthropomorphic test device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (3)(b), if the restraint system for
  infants with special needs provides surfaces for the support of the
  sides of the torso, and those surfaces extend at least 100 mm forward
  from the padded surface of the portion of the restraint system
  provided for the support of the head of the anthropomorphic test
  device, the restraint system may have a continuous seat back width of
  not less than 150 mm, measured in the horizontal plane at the height
  specified in paragraph (3)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint system with means of
  automatic repositioning

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a restraint system for infants with special needs
  that has a means of automatically repositioning the seating surface is
  subjected, in any adjustment position, to a dynamic test in accordance
  with section 4 of Test Method 213.5, an opening that is exposed and
  larger than 6.4 mm before the test must not become smaller during the
  test as a result of the movement of the seating surface relative to
  the other parts of the restraint system.

### [Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-769202 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[616]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-616
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for infants
  with special needs must have stitched onto it, indelibly moulded into
  or onto it, or indelibly printed on a label affixed to it in a
  permanent manner, the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and principal place of business of the
    company that manufactured, imported or sold the restraint system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the model name and number of the restraint system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of manufacture of the restraint system, as
    shown in Schedule 5, with the year, month and day above the
    corresponding wording in both official languages;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating --- in units based on the
    International System of Units followed by the corresponding imperial
    units in parentheses --- the mass and height range of the infants
    for whom the manufacturer recommends the restraint system;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a warning indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the restraint system must be used only in a
      forward-facing seating position equipped with a vehicle seat belt
      or, if the restraint system is equipped with a lower connector
      system, in a forward-facing seating position equipped with a
      vehicle seat belt or a lower universal anchorage system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the restraint system must be in a
      rear-facing position, except that a car bed must be used in a flat
      position along the vehicle's rear bench seat with the head of the
      infant towards the centre of the vehicle,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with a
      lower connector system, that the restraint system must be secured
      to the vehicle as shown in the installation instructions by either
      of the following means and, if the restraint system is equipped
      with a tether strap and the manufacturer recommends its use, by
      means of the tether strap:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the lower connector system, if the restraint
        system is installed in a seating position that is equipped with
        a lower universal anchorage system, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle seat belt, if the restraint system is
        installed in a seating position that is not equipped with a
        lower universal anchorage system,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is not equipped with a
      lower connector system, that the restraint system must be secured
      to the vehicle as shown in the installation instructions by means
      of a vehicle seat belt and, if the restraint system is equipped
      with a tether strap and the manufacturer recommends its use, by
      means of the tether strap, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with belts
      for restraining an infant, that the belts must be snugly adjusted
      around the infant; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an installation diagram that shows the restraint
    system

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} installed as recommended by the manufacturer in a
      seating position that is equipped only with a lap belt and secured
      to the vehicle by means of the belt and, if the restraint system
      is equipped with a tether strap and the manufacturer recommends
      its use, by means of the tether strap,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} installed as recommended by the manufacturer in
      a seating position that is equipped only with a continuous-loop
      lap and shoulder belt, and secured to the vehicle by means of the
      belt and, if the restraint system is equipped with a tether strap
      and the manufacturer recommends its use, by means of the tether
      strap, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system is equipped with a
      lower connector system, installed as recommended by the
      manufacturer in a seating position that is equipped with a lower
      universal anchorage system, and secured to the vehicle by means of
      the lower connector system and, if the restraint system is
      equipped with a tether strap and the manufacturer recommends its
      use, by means of the tether strap.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official languages and print size

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must be
  in both official languages and in characters of at least 10 points,
  except for the words "year/année", "month/mois" and "day/jour" under
  the date of manufacture, which may be in characters of at least 8
  points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visibility of information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraphs (1)(d)
  to (f) must be fully visible at all times, even when the restraint
  system is occupied, whether the restraint system is installed with or
  without a removable base.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warning --- air bag

- **[[617]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-617
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for infants
  with special needs must bear the air bag warning label shown in
  Schedule 6, and the label must conform to the requirements of that
  schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The label referred to in subsection (1) must be
  affixed in a permanent manner at either of the following locations and
  be fully visible to a person installing the restraint system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the side of the restraint system that will face
    the right front passenger door when the restraint system is facing
    rearward; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the location where the infant's head would rest
    or adjacent to that location.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Installation instructions

- **[[618]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-618
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for infants
  with special needs must be accompanied by printed instructions, in
  both official languages, that set out a step-by-step procedure,
  including diagrams, for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} installing and securing the restraint system in a
    vehicle;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} positioning an infant with special needs in the
    restraint system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adjusting every part of the restraint system that
    is designed to restrain the infant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The instructions referred to in paragraph (1)(a) must
  state that the restraint system, even when unoccupied, must be firmly
  secured to the vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Idem

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The instructions must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the classes of vehicles, the seating
    positions and the types of vehicle seat belts with which the
    restraint system may or may not be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specify whether the restraint system may be used
    with a lower universal anchorage system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the restraint system has a means of
    automatically repositioning the seating surface, specify that the
    ability of the restraint system to change position must not be
    impeded; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} explain the primary consequences of not following
    the warnings appearing on the restraint system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of instructions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every restraint system for infants with special needs
  must have a place for the storage of instructions.
