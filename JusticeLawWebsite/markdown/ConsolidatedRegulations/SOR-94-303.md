## Seized Property Disposition Regulations {#seized-property-disposition-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-303
:::

[SEIZED PROPERTY MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.3)

Registration 1994-04-14

Regulations Respecting the Disposition of Seized Property

P.C. 1994-561 1994-04-14
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services and the Minister of Justice,
pursuant to subsection 5(2), paragraph 13(2)(a) and section 19 of the
[Seized Property Management Act](/eng/acts/S-8.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_f){#footnote1_f-ID0EDABA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the disposition of
seized property](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-303).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963767 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Seized Property Disposition
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-303).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963769 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Seized Property Management
    Act](/eng/acts/S-8.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contract for services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract for services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract for
    services in respect of any property that is in the possession or
    under the control of the Minister, whether the property has been
    forfeited to Her Majesty or not; ([marché de
    services]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who enters into
    a contract for services with the Minister, referred to in section 18
    of the Act; ([entrepreneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means property referred to in any
    of subsections 4(1) to (3) of the Act that is forfeited to Her
    Majesty. ([bien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Disposition of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963775 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Property
  may be disposed of in any manner including public tender or public
  auction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All property disposed of under these Regulations
  shall be disposed of on an "as-is/where-is" basis.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-190, s. 1
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  property is being disposed of by means of public tender, all tenders
  shall be in writing and shall be submitted in sealed envelopes marked
  "tender".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The envelopes referred to in subsection (1) shall
  remain sealed until the designated closing date, after which time the
  envelopes shall be opened in the presence of at least two persons.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel the
sale of any property offered for sale by public auction or public tender
where no bid or tender is received, no bid or tender meets the minimum
bid or tender set before the sale, or the Minister does not consider any
of the bids or tenders acceptable.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where two or more tenders
in respect of property are in the same amount, the first tender received
shall be given precedence.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall give
notice, in writing, to the person whose bid or tender in respect of
property has been verbally or otherwise accepted, setting out the period
within which the sale must be completed.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a person whose bid or
tender in respect of property has been accepted does not complete the
sale within the period set out in the notice referred to in section 7,
the Minister may cancel that sale and accept the next-best acceptable
bid or tender.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may require a deposit from a person who submits a bid or
  tender in respect of property to guarantee completion of a sale.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person whose bid or tender in respect of
  property has been accepted does not complete the sale within the
  period set out in the notice referred to in section 7, the person's
  deposit in respect of that property shall be forfeited to Her Majesty,
  deposited in the Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited to the
  Proceeds Account.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister
disposes of property that is real property, the Minister shall do so in
accordance with, in addition to sections 3 to 9 of these Regulations,
the Federal Real Property Act, the [Federal Real Property
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-502) and any other regulations made
under that Act on or after May 1, 1994.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister
considers property to be unsuitable for sale or of insufficient value to
justify a sale, the Minister may dispose of that property in any other
manner including the execution of a release or discharge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-190, s. 2
:::

## [Net Proceeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963795 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  net proceeds of the disposition of property referred to in paragraph
  13(2)(a) of the Act shall be calculated within 60 days after the
  disposition of the property.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts to be deducted from the proceeds of the
  disposition of property for the purposes of calculating the net
  proceeds are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount to be paid, in relation to the property,
    pursuant to paragraph 462.42(6)(b) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount paid, in relation to the property, as a
    consequence of an order under subsection 17(4) of the Narcotic
    Control Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any expenses incurred by the Minister in respect of
    the management and disposition of the property; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} advances made by the Minister pursuant to paragraph
    9(b) of the Act.

## [Indemnification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963803 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  indemnification that may be granted pursuant to section 18 of the Act
  includes any amount that may be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to settle an action, subject to the prior approval
    of the amount by the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to satisfy a judgement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a contractor has complied with section 14 and
  the Minister is satisfied that the contractor acted honestly and in
  good faith and, in the case of any criminal or administrative action
  or proceeding that is enforced by a monetary penalty, believed on
  reasonable grounds that the contractor's conduct was lawful, the
  Minister shall pay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount to be paid under the indemnification
    granted pursuant to section 18 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contractor's reasonable costs, charges and
    expenses in connection with a claim in respect of which
    indemnification has been granted pursuant to section 18 of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In no event shall any indemnification apply to any
  civil, criminal or administrative action or proceeding instituted by
  Her Majesty in right of Canada.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  contractor who becomes aware of a possible or actual claim in respect
  of anything done, or omitted to be done, by the contractor under a
  contract for services shall, within 15 days after becoming aware of
  the possible or actual claim, give by registered mail a written notice
  of the claim, together with any relevant documents, to the Minister at
  the address specified in the contract for services.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A contractor who proposes to settle a claim referred
  to in subsection (1) shall, at least 15 days before accepting any
  terms of settlement, give by registered mail a written notice of the
  proposed settlement, together with any relevant documents, to the
  Minister at the address specified in the contract for services.

## [Report of Location of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963815 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall file
the report referred to in subsection 5(2) of the Act identifying the
location of property, in the form set out in the schedule, within seven
days after changing the location of the property.
