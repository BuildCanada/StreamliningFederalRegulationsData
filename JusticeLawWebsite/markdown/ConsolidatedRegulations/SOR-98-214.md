## Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations (Businesses) {#importation-and-exportation-of-firearms-regulations-businesses .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-214
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Importation and Exportation of Firearms Regulations (Businesses)

P.C. 1998-489 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed Authorization to Export or Import
Firearms Regulations (Businesses), substantially in the annexed form,
laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996, which date is
at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to sections 44 to
47, paragraphs 117(a), (b) and (i), subparagraph 117(k)(iii) and
paragraph 117(w) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby
makes the annexed Authorization to Export or Import Firearms Regulations
(Businesses).
:::

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 46(d) of the Act, any of the purposes described in section 22

::::

### [Information to be Provided]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020497 .Subheading}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 46(f) of the Act, the information that a business that is
importing goods is to provide to the Registrar is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the business' representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business' licence number and date of expiry;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the point of entry of the goods imported into Canada
  and their expected import date;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the last country the goods were in before
  their importation into Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of non-restricted firearms and restricted
  firearms, the purpose for importing them and, in the case of other
  goods, the prescribed purpose under section 5 for importing them;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of firearms, in respect of each firearm,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the firearm identification number, if any,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whether the firearm is a frame or receiver only,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the make,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer if different from the
    make,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the model,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the serial number, subject to subsection 6(2),

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the type,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the action,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the calibre or gauge,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of ammunition that the cartridge
    magazine can contain, and

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the barrel length;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of other goods, a description of them;
  and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the final destination of the goods in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-271, s. 6
:::
:::::

### [Mandatory Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020521 .Subheading}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  business applies for an authorization to import goods described in
  section 43 of the Act, the Registrar shall attach to the authorization
  issued in respect of those goods the condition that the goods be
  accompanied by the bill of lading for those goods and a copy of the
  authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If at the time of making the application the serial
  number of the firearm that is the subject of the application does not
  exist or is unknown, the business shall provide the serial number, if
  any, to the Registrar, without delay after it becomes known to the
  business.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020539 .Part}

### [Notice of Refusal or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020540 .Subheading}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of a decision to refuse to issue an authorization to export or import
  goods or to revoke such an authorization is sufficiently given if the
  notice is addressed to the last known address of the business that
  applied for or is the holder of the authorization and the notice is,
  as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivered personally during normal business hours
    of the business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by registered mail or by courier;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a notice of a decision to revoke,
    delivered personally to the person who produces the authorization at
    the customs office.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of delivery, if it is delivered
    personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the fifth working day, excluding Saturdays and
    holidays, after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the postmark date, if it is sent by mail, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment on the waybill, if it is
      sent by courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day of transmission, if it is sent by
    electronic means.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-271, s. 9
:::

### [Revocation of Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020555 .Subheading}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may revoke an
authorization for the export or import of goods at any time before the
authorization is confirmed by a customs officer pursuant to subsection
45(2) or 47(2) of the Act.
::::

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall
revoke an authorization, issued to a business, for the export or import
of goods before the goods are exported or imported, as the case may be,
if the Registrar has reason to believe that any of the following, as
applicable, is no longer valid:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence referred to in paragraph 44(c) of the
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence referred to in paragraph 46(a) of the
  Act; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the export permit issued to the business in
  accordance with the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19).
::::

### [Notice of Refusal to Confirm Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020561 .Subheading}

::::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a customs officer
refuses to confirm an authorization to export or import under subsection
45(2) or 47(2) of the Act, the customs officer shall notify the business
and the Registrar of the reasons for the refusal and, if the goods are
detained under subsection 45(3) of the Act, the reasons for detaining
the goods.
