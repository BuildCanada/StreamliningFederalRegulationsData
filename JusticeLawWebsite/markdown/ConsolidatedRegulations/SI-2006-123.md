## [A Proclamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612331 .Part}

Whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2005-1899 of November 1, 2005, the
Governor in Council declared that, in accordance with Article 28 of the
Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of
Lithuania, signed at Vilnius on July 5, 2005, the Agreement shall enter
into force on the first day of the fourth month following the final day
of the month in which the Parties shall have exchanged written notices
through the diplomatic channel confirming that their respective legal
requirements for the entry into force of this Agreement have been
completed;

Whereas that Order in Council was laid before Parliament on November 4,
2005 and, as Parliament was dissolved before the expiry of the
thirty-sitting days period required under the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order in Council was laid before Parliament
again on May 2, 2006;

Whereas, before the twentieth sitting day after the Order had been laid
before Parliament, no motion for the consideration of either House to
the effect that the Order be revoked was filed with the Speaker of the
appropriate House;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 42(2) of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order came into force on the thirtieth sitting
day after it had been laid before Parliament, being June 20, 2006;

Whereas the exchange of the written notices was completed on July 14,
2006;

Whereas the Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the
fourth month following the final day of the month in which the Parties
exchanged written notices, being November 1, 2006;

And whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2006-809 of August 29, 2006, the
Governor in Council directed that a proclamation do issue giving notice
that the Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of
Lithuania shall enter into force on November 1, 2006;

Now know you that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our proclamation give notice that the Agreement on
Social Security between Canada and the Republic of Lithuania, signed at
Vilnius on July 5, 2005, a copy of which is annexed, shall enter into
force on November 1, 2006.

Of all which Our loving subjects and all others whom these presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In testimony whereof, We have caused this Our proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness:
Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved Michaëlle Jean, Chancellor and
Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada, Chancellor and Commander of
Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of
Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of
Canada.

At our government house, in Our City of Ottawa, this second day of
October in the year of Our Lord two thousand and six and in the
fifty-fifth year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushRight
RICHARD DICERNI
:::

::: FlushRight
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*

### [Agreement on Social Security Between Canada and the Republic of Lithuania]{.HTitleText1} {#agreement-on-social-security-between-canada-and-the-republic-of-lithuania .SchedHeadL1}

#### [ARTICLE 3]{.HLabel2}[(Persons to Whom the Agreement Applies)]{.HTitleText2} {#article-3persons-to-whom-the-agreement-applies .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement shall apply to any person who is or who has been subject
to the legislation of the Republic of Lithuania or Canada, and to the
dependants and survivors of such a person within the meaning of the
applicable legislation of either Party.

#### [ARTICLE 4]{.HLabel2}[Equality of Treatment]{.HTitleText2} {#article-4equality-of-treatment .SchedHeadL2}

Any person who is or who has been subject to the legislation of a Party,
and the dependants and survivors of such a person, shall be subject to
the obligations of the legislation of the other Party and shall be
eligible for the benefits of that legislation under the same conditions
as citizens of the latter Party.

  subject to any reduction, modification, suspension or cancellation by
  reason only of the fact that the person is in the territory of the

#### [ARTICLE 7]{.HLabel2}[Detachments]{.HTitleText2} {#article-7detachments .SchedHeadL2}

An employed person who is subject to the legislation of a Party and who
works in the territory of the other Party for the same or related
employer shall, in respect of that work, be subject only to the
legislation of the first Party as though that work is performed in its
territory. In the case of an assignment, this coverage may not be
maintained for more than 60 months without the prior consent of the
competent authorities of both Parties.

##### [ARTICLE 12]{.HLabel3}[Periods Under the Legislation of a Third State]{.HTitleText3} {#article-12periods-under-the-legislation-of-a-third-state .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is not eligible for a benefit on the basis of the creditable
periods under the legislation of the Parties, totalized as provided in
Article 11, the eligibility of that person for that benefit shall be
determined by totalizing these periods and creditable periods completed
under the legislation of a third State with which both Parties are bound
by social security instruments which provide for the totalizing of
periods.

##### [ARTICLE 13]{.HLabel3}[Minimum Period]{.HTitleText3} {#article-13minimum-period .SchedHeadL3}

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, if the total
duration of the Creditable periods accumulated by a person under the
legislation of a Party is less than one year and if, taking into account
only those periods, no right to a benefit exists under the legislation
of that Party, the competent institution of that Party shall not be
required to pay a benefit to that person in respect of those periods by
virtue of this Agreement.

    contributions to the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) in
    relation to the minimum qualifying period required under that Plan
    to establish eligibility for that benefit, but in no case shall that
    fraction exceed the value of one.
    :::
    :::::

#### [CHAPTER 3]{.HLabel2}[Pensions Under the Legislation of the Republic of Lithuania]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-3pensions-under-the-legislation-of-the-republic-of-lithuania .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE 16]{.HLabel3}[Calculating the Amount of Pension Payable]{.HTitleText3} {#article-16calculating-the-amount-of-pension-payable .SchedHeadL3}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  If a person is eligible for a pension solely through the application
  of the totalizing provisions of Chapter 1, the competent institution
  of the Republic of Lithuania shall calculate the amount of pension
  payable exclusively on the basis of the creditable periods completed
  under the legislation of the Republic of Lithuania.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  For the purpose of determining the amount of a disability pension, the
  number of years left until the person reaches pensionable age shall be
  determined by the ratio of the creditable periods completed
  exclusively under the legislation of the Republic of Lithuania in
  proportion to the mandatory creditable period required under that
  legislation for a full pension.
  :::
  :::::

##### [ARTICLE 17]{.HLabel3}[Entitlement and Payment of Benefits]{.HTitleText3} {#article-17entitlement-and-payment-of-benefits .SchedHeadL3}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Subject to paragraph 2, a person described in Article 3 who resides in
  Canada shall be entitled to benefits under the legislation of the
  Republic of Lithuania under the same terms and conditions as if that
  person were residing in the Republic of Lithuania.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  When determining the right to a benefit under the legislation of the
  Republic of Lithuania for a person residing in Canada, creditable
  periods completed before June 1, 1991, in the territory of the former
  Soviet Union shall be taken into account only if that person has
  fulfilled the minimum creditable period required under the legislation
  of the Republic of Lithuania while working in a Lithuanian enterprise,
  office or organization, and without recourse to the totalizing
  provisions of Chapter 1.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel

  :::

#### [ARTICLE 19]{.HLabel2}[Exchange of Information and Mutual Assistance]{.HTitleText2} {#article-19exchange-of-information-and-mutual-assistance .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1

  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    shall, to the extent permitted by the legislation which they
    administer, communicate to each other any information necessary for
    the application of that legislation;
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    shall lend their good offices and furnish assistance to one another
    for the purpose of determining eligibility for, or the amount of,

    this Agreement applies, as if the matter involved the application of
    their own legislation;
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(c)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    shall communicate to each other, as soon as possible, all
    information about the measures taken by them for the application of
    this Agreement or about changes in their respective legislation in
    so far as these changes affect the application of this Agreement.
    :::
    :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The assistance referred to in sub-paragraph 1(b) shall be provided
  free of charge, subject to any provision contained in an
  administrative arrangement concluded pursuant to Article 18 for the
  reimbursement of certain types of expenses.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Unless disclosure is required under the laws of a Party, any
  information about a person which is transmitted in accordance with
  this Agreement to that Party by the other Party is confidential and
  shall be used only for purposes of implementing this Agreement and the
  legislation to which this Agreement applies.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 20]{.HLabel2}[Exemption or Reduction of Taxes, Dues and Charges]{.HTitleText2} {#article-20exemption-or-reduction-of-taxes-dues-and-charges .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Any exemption from or reduction of taxes, legal dues and
  administrative charges for which provision is made in the legislation
  of a Party in connection with the issuing of any certificate or
  document required to be produced for the application of that

  be produced for the application of the legislation of the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Any documents of an official nature required to be produced for the
  application of this Agreement shall be exempt from any authentication
  by diplomatic or consular authorities and similar formality.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 21]{.HLabel2}[Language of Communication]{.HTitleText2} {#article-21language-of-communication .SchedHeadL2}

For the application of this Agreement, the competent authorities and
competent institutions of the Parties may communicate directly with one
another in any official language of either Party.

#### [ARTICLE 22]{.HLabel2}[Submitting a Claim, Notice or Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#article-22submitting-a-claim-notice-or-appeal .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Claims, notices and appeals concerning eligibility for, or the amount
  of, a benefit under the legislation of a Party which should, for the
  purposes of that legislation, have been presented within a prescribed
  period to a competent authority or competent institution of that
  Party, but which are presented within the same period to a competent
  authority or competent institution of the other Party, shall be
  treated as if they had been presented to the competent authority or
  competent institution of the first Party. The date of presentation of
  claims, notices and appeals to the competent authority or competent
  institution of the other Party shall be deemed to be the date of their
  presentation to the competent authority or competent institution of
  the first Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Subject to the second sentence of this paragraph, a claim for a
  benefit under the legislation of a Party made after the date of entry
  into force of this Agreement shall be deemed to be a claim for the
  corresponding benefit under the legislation of the other Party,
  provided that the applicant at the time of application:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    requests that it be considered an application under the legislation
    of the other Party, or
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText2
    provides information indicating that creditable periods have been
    completed under the legislation of the other Party.
    :::
    :::::

  The preceding sentence shall not apply if the applicant requests that
  his or her claim to the benefit under the legislation of the other
  Party be delayed.

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  In any case to which paragraph 1 or 2 applies, the competent authority
  or competent institution to which the claim, notice or appeal has been
  submitted shall transmit it without delay to the competent authority
  or competent institution of the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 23]{.HLabel2}[Payment of Benefits]{.HTitleText2} {#article-23payment-of-benefits .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The competent institution of a Party shall discharge its obligations
  under this Agreement in the currency of either Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Benefits shall be paid to beneficiaries free from any deduction for
  administrative expenses that may be incurred in paying the benefits.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  In the event that a Party imposes currency controls or other similar
  measures that restrict payments, remittances or transfers of funds or
  financial instruments to persons who are outside its territory, that
  Party shall, without delay, take suitable measures to ensure the
  payment of any amount that must be paid in accordance with this
  Agreement to persons described in Article 3 who reside in the
  territory of the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 24]{.HLabel2}[Resolution of Difficulties]{.HTitleText2} {#article-24resolution-of-difficulties .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The competent authorities of the Parties shall resolve, to the extent
  possible, any difficulties which arise in interpreting or applying
  this Agreement according to its spirit and fundamental principles.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The Parties shall consult promptly at the request of either Party
  concerning matters which have not been resolved by the competent
  authorities in accordance with paragraph 1.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Any dispute between the Parties concerning the interpretation of this
  Agreement which has not been resolved or settled by consultation in
  accordance with paragraph 1 or 2 shall, at the request of either
  Party, be submitted to arbitration by an arbitral tribunal.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [4]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  Unless the Parties mutually determine otherwise, the arbitral tribunal
  shall consist of three arbitrators, of whom each Party shall appoint
  one and the two arbitrators so appointed shall appoint a third who
  shall act as president; provided that if either Party fails to appoint
  its arbitrator or if the two arbitrators fail to agree, the competent
  authority of the other Party shall invite the President of the
  International Court of Justice to appoint the arbitrator of the first
  Party or the two appointed arbitrators shall invite the President of
  the International Court of Justice to appoint the president of the
  arbitral tribunal.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [5]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  If the President of the International Court of Justice is a citizen of
  either Party, the function of appointment shall be transferred to the
  Vice-president or the next most senior member of the Court who is not
  a citizen of either Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [6]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The arbitral tribunal shall determine its own procedures, but it shall
  reach its decisions by a majority of votes.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [7]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1
  The decision of the arbitral tribunal shall be final and binding.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE 25]{.HLabel2}[Understandings with a Province of Canada]{.HTitleText2} {#article-25understandings-with-a-province-of-canada .SchedHeadL2}

The relevant authority of the Republic of Lithuania and a province of
Canada may conclude understandings concerning any social security matter
within provincial jurisdiction in Canada in so far as those
understandings are not inconsistent with the provisions of this
Agreement.

#### [ARTICLE 28]{.HLabel2}[Entry into Force]{.HTitleText2} {#article-28entry-into-force .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the fourth
month following the final day of the month in which the Parties shall
have exchanged written notices through the diplomatic channel confirming
that their respective legal requirements for the entry into force of
this Agreement have been completed. The date of the exchange of the
written notices shall be the date of the delivery of the last notice.
