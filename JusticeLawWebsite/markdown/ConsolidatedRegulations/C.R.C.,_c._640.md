## Expropriation Act Basic Rate Order {#expropriation-act-basic-rate-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 640
:::

[EXPROPRIATION ACT](/eng/acts/E-21)

Order Prescribing a Basic Rate for the Purposes of Section 33 of the
Expropriation Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559136 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Expropriation Act Basic Rate Order.

## [Basic Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559138 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
33 of the [Expropriation Act](/eng/acts/E-21), the basic rate shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any period ending on or before September 30,
  1973, six per cent per annum; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any quarterly period commencing after September
  30, 1973

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} six per cent per annum, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a rate per annum

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} equal to the average yield of Government of
      Canada treasury bills for the period of four weeks preceding the
      quarterly period, where that average yield does not contain a
      fraction other than a multiple of one-quarter, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} calculated to the nearest quarter of one per cent
      above the average yield described in clause (A), where that
      average yield contains a fraction other than a multiple of
      one-quarter,

  whichever is greater.
