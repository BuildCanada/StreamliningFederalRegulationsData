## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384765 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[ADR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ADR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means alternative dispute
    resolution. ([MARL]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who makes an appeal
    to the Division. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a person other than a Minister's
      counsel, the person's name, postal address and telephone number
      and, if any, their fax number and email address;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the Minister's counsel, their
      postal address, telephone number and email address and, if any,
      their fax number; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a person referred to in any of
      paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act who is representing or
      advising a party in an appeal, in addition to the information
      referred to in paragraph (a), the name of the body of which the
      person is a member and the membership identification number issued
      to them. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Immigration Appeal
    Division of the Board. ([Section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister's appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister's appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal made under
    subsection 63(5) of the Act against a decision of the Immigration
    Division in an admissibility hearing. ([appel du
    ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    officer by the Minister under subsection 6(1) of the
    Act. ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the appellant or the
    respondent. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a hearing, conference,
    ADR conference, application or proceeding in
    writing. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business office
    designated as a registry office by the
    Division. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[removal order appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[removal order appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal made
    under subsection 63(2) or (3) of the Act against a decision to make
    a removal order. ([appel d'une mesure de renvoi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[residency obligation appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[residency obligation appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal
    made under subsection 63(4) of the Act against a decision made
    outside Canada on the residency obligation under section 28 of the
    Act. ([appel sur l'obligation de résidence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister or, if the
    Minister is appealing a decision of the Immigration Division, the
    person who was the subject of the Immigration Division admissibility
    hearing. ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sponsorship appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sponsorship appeal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appeal made
    under subsection 63(1) of the Act against a decision not to issue a
    permanent resident visa to a foreign national. ([appel en matière de
    parrainage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
    Saturday, Sunday or other day on which the Board offices are
    closed. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General principle

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Rules are to be
interpreted and applied so as to permit the resolution of every appeal
as informally and quickly as the circumstances and the considerations of
fairness and natural justice permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No applicable rule

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of a
provision in these Rules dealing with a matter raised during an appeal,
the Division may do whatever is necessary to deal with the matter in
order to resolve the appeal effectively, completely and fairly.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Division

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Division may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} after giving the parties notice and an opportunity to
  object, act on its own initiative, without a party having to make an
  application or request to the Division;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a requirement of a rule;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} excuse a person from a requirement of a rule;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} extend or shorten a time limit before it has passed;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} extend any time limit after it has passed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to follow Rules

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Unless proceedings are
declared invalid by the Division, a failure to follow any requirement of
these Rules does not make the proceedings invalid.

## [Communicating with the Division]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384800 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating with Division

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** All communication with the
Division must be directed to the registry office.

## [Contact Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384803 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information --- party other than
Minister

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  other than the Minister must provide their contact information and
  that of their counsel, if any, in writing to the Division and the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information --- Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide the contact information of
  their counsel in writing to the Division and the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The contact information must be received by the
  Division and the other party

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the notice of appeal, if the party is the
    appellant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no later than 20 days after the day on which the
    party receives the notice of appeal, if the party is the respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information --- counsel
  retained after time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a party other than the Minister retains counsel
  after providing the notice of appeal or after the time limit set out
  in paragraph (3)(b), as the case may be, they must provide the
  counsel's contact information to the Division and the Minister in
  writing without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to contact information

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  other than the Minister must notify the Division and the other party
  in writing without delay of any changes to the party's contact
  information or their counsel's contact information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's counsel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the Division and the other
  party in writing without delay of any changes to the contact
  information of the Minister's counsel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written statement --- unpaid counsel

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a party other than the
Minister retains counsel who is not a person referred to in any of
paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act, both the party and their counsel
must provide to the Division in writing without delay the information
and written statements referred to in the schedule to these Rules.

## [Counsel of Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Becoming counsel of record

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** As soon as counsel, other
than counsel for the Minister, provides a document to the Division on
behalf of a party, the counsel becomes counsel of record for the party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to be removed

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To be
  removed as counsel of record, counsel for a party other than the
  Minister must first provide to the person represented and the Minister
  a written request to be removed. The counsel must then provide the
  written request to the Division no later than three working days
  before the date fixed for the next proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is not possible for counsel to make the request
  in accordance with subrule (1), they must appear on the date and at
  the time fixed for the proceeding and make the request orally at the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division's permission required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Counsel remains counsel of record unless the Division
  grants the request to be removed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counsel of record --- stay of removal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Division stays a removal order in accordance
  with section 68 of the Act, counsel remains counsel of record for the
  party unless the counsel notifies the Division in writing that they
  have ceased to be counsel of record for the party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removing counsel of record

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party other than the Minister wants to remove their counsel of record,
  they must provide the Division, their counsel of record and the
  Minister with written notice removing the counsel as counsel of
  record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal takes effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel ceases to be counsel of record when the
  Division receives the notice.

## [Filing an Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384839 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appeal --- person

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  file an appeal of a decision, a person must provide to the Division a
  notice of appeal together with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sponsorship appeal, the officer's
    decision and the written reasons, if any, for the refusal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a removal order appeal, the removal
    order; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a residency obligation appeal, the
    officer's decision and the written reasons, if any, for the
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may also provide any other information
  that may assist the Division in resolving the appeal as quickly as
  possible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice provided to Immigration Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Division is provided under paragraph (1)(b)
  with a notice of appeal of a removal order made at an admissibility
  hearing, the Division must provide the notice of appeal to the
  Immigration Division without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appeal --- admissibility
hearing

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subrule 13(1), a person may
  file an appeal against a removal order made at an admissibility
  hearing by providing a notice of appeal at the end of the
  admissibility hearing to the Immigration Division member who made the
  removal order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appeal provided to Division

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Immigration Division must provide the notice of
  appeal and the removal order to the Division without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appeal --- Minister

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  file a Minister's appeal, the Minister must provide a notice of appeal
  to the respondent, the Immigration Division and the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written statement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal provided to the Division under
  subrule (1) must be accompanied by a written statement indicating how
  and when the Minister provided the notice of appeal to the respondent
  and the Immigration Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice of appeal --- grounds

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of appeal, the Minister must indicate
  the grounds of the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Unless a notice of appeal
is provided to the Immigration Division member under subrule 14(1), the
notice of appeal and accompanying documents must be received by the
Division no later than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sponsorship appeal, 30 days after
  the day on which the appellant receives the officer's decision and the
  written reasons, if any, for the refusal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a removal order appeal, 30 days after
  the day on which the appellant receives the removal order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a residency obligation appeal, 60 days
  after the day on which the appellant receives the officer's decision
  and the written reasons, if any, for the decision; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Minister's appeal, 30 days after the
  day on which the Minister receives the Immigration Division's
  decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents provided to Minister

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Division must provide
the following documents to the Minister without delay:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the notice of appeal is provided to the Division
  under rule 13, the notice of appeal and the documents referred to in
  paragraph 13(1)(a), (b) or (c), as the case may be; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the notice of appeal is provided to the
  Immigration Division member under subrule 14(1), the notice of appeal
  and the removal order.

## [Language of the Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384872 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Choice of language

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party other than the Minister must indicate in their notice of appeal
  their choice of either English or French as the language of the
  appeal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language --- Minister's appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a Minister's appeal, the language of
  the appeal is the language chosen by the party other than the Minister
  in the proceedings relating to the decision being appealed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A party other than the
Minister may change the language of the appeal by notifying the Division
and the Minister in writing no later than 45 days before the date fixed
for the next proceeding.

## [Appeal Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record --- sponsorship

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a sponsorship appeal, the Minister must prepare an appeal
  record that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application for a permanent resident visa that
    was refused;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sponsorship application and the sponsor's
    undertaking;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any document that the Minister has that is relevant
    to the applications, the reasons for the refusal or any issue in the
    appeal; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the written reasons for the refusal, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record --- admissibility hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an appeal of a removal order made at
  an admissibility hearing or a Minister's appeal, the Immigration
  Division must prepare an appeal record that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the removal order, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transcript of the admissibility hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any document accepted as evidence at the
    admissibility hearing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the written reasons, if any, for its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record --- examination

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an appeal of a removal order made at
  an examination, the Minister must prepare an appeal record that
  contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the removal order;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any document that the Minister has that is relevant
    to the removal order or to any issue in the appeal; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any written reasons for the Minister's decision to
    make the removal order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record --- residency obligation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a residency obligation appeal, the
  Minister must prepare an appeal record that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document that the Minister has that is relevant
    to the decision on the residency obligation or any issue in the
    appeal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the officer's decision and written reasons, if any,
    for the decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister provides appeal record

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a written request from the Division, the Minister must
  provide the appeal record referred to in subrule 20(1), (3) or (4) to
  the appellant and the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof record provided to appellant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appeal record provided to the Division under
  subrule (1) must be accompanied by a written statement indicating how
  and when the Minister provided the appeal record to the appellant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immigration Division provides appeal
  record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Immigration Division must provide the appeal
  record referred to in subrule 20(2) to the parties and the Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** An appeal record provided
under rule 21 must be received no later than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the Minister receives
  a request referred to in subrule 21(1), in the case of a sponsorship
  appeal or a residency obligation appeal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the day on which the Immigration
  Division receives the notice of appeal, in the case of an appeal of a
  removal order made at an admissibility hearing or a Minister's appeal;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the day on which the Minister receives
  a request referred to in subrule 21(1), in the case of an appeal of a
  removal order made at an examination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late appeal record

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** If the Division does not
receive the appeal record within the time limit set out in rule 22, the
Division may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} require the Minister or the Immigration Division, as
  the case may be, to explain why the appeal record is late and give
  reasons why the appeal record should be accepted late; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} schedule and start the hearing and decide the appeal
  without the appeal record or with only part of the appeal record.

## [Disclosure of Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384923 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of evidence

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party wants to use a document in a proceeding, the party must provide
  the document to the other party and the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement --- no documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party does not intend to use any documents in a
  proceeding, the party must provide a written statement to the Division
  indicating that intention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Documents provided to the
Division for use in a proceeding must be accompanied by a written
statement indicating how and when they were provided to the other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- 60 days

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Documents provided under
subrule 24(1) and the statement provided under subrule 24(2) must be
received no later than 60 days after the day on which the party receives
the appeal record.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- response to evidence

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A document that is
provided in response to evidence presented by the other party must be
received no later than 30 days before the date fixed for the proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for reconsideration -- stay

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a hearing
that is held for the reconsideration of the appeal of a removal order
that has been stayed, documents provided under subrule 24(1) for use in
the hearing must be received no later than 30 days before the date fixed
for the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit not met

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who does not meet the time limits set out in rules 26 to 28 in
  respect of a document may not use the document at the hearing unless
  the Division allows them to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to allow the party to use the
  document, the Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevance and probative value of the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the party, with reasonable effort, could
    have met the time limits set out in rules 26 to 28 in respect of the
    document;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any prejudice to the other party; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the request to be allowed to use the
    document was made in a timely manner and the justification for any
    delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consequence --- failure to disclose

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If a party does not
provide either a document or a written statement within the time limit
set out in rule 26, the Division may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if that party is the appellant, determine that the
  appeal has been abandoned in accordance with subsection 168(1) of the
  Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix a date and time for the proceeding and start the
  proceeding on the basis of the materials provided;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} suspend the fixing of a date and time for the
  proceeding until it determines that the appeal is ready to proceed; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} take any other action that it deems appropriate.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1384952 .Part}

### [Form and Language of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1384953 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents prepared by party

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A document prepared by a
party for use in a proceeding must be legible, in 12-point Times New
Roman, Arial or Tahoma font and on one or both sides of consecutively
numbered 21.5 cm by 28 cm (8½ in. by 11 in.) pages.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one document

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If a party provides more
than one document for use in a proceeding, the documents must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have consecutively numbered pages as if they were one
  document; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a list identifying each of the
  documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of documents

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  documents used by a party other than the Minister in a proceeding must
  be in English or French or, if in another language, be provided
  together with an English or French translation and a written statement
  signed by the translator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of Minister's documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All documents used by the Minister in a proceeding
  must be in the language of the appeal or be provided together with a
  translation in the language of the appeal and a written statement
  signed by the translator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translator's statement

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** In a written statement
referred to in rule 33, the translator must include their name, the
language and, if any, dialect translated and a statement that the
translation is accurate.

### [Providing a Document]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1384967 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Rules 36 to 41 apply to
any document, including a notice, request, application or statement in
writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to Division

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is to be provided to the Division must be provided to
  the registry office that the Division specifies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents in public proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All documents provided to the Division in a
  proceeding conducted in public are placed on the Division's public
  record unless the person providing a document makes an application
  under subrule 93(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is to be provided to the Minister
  must be provided to the Minister's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to person other
  than Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is to be provided to a person other
  than the Minister must be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the person's counsel or, if they do not have
    counsel, to the person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the person's designated representative, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of providing documents

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document may be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by email or other electronic means, if the Division
    allows;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by regular or registered mail;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by courier or priority post;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax, if the document is no more than 20 pages
    long or the recipient consents to receiving more than 20 pages; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by hand.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing electronic document

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  electronic document, including an affidavit or statutory declaration,
  that meets the requirements specified by the Division is deemed to
  have been provided in accordance with subrules 36(1), (3) and (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must publish or post notice of the
  requirements referred to in subrule (1) in a manner that will allow
  public access to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An electronic document provided by the Division is
  considered to be the original version of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of paper document

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who provides to the Division an electronic
  document whose original version is a paper document must retain that
  original version for the duration of the appeal and must, on request,
  provide it to the Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic signature

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  requirement under these Rules that a document be signed is satisfied,
  in the case of an electronic document, by an electronic signature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[electronic
  signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subrule (1), [[electronic
  signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} means a signature that consists of
  one or more letters, characters or other symbols in digital form that
  are incorporated in, attached to or associated with the document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- inability to provide
document

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party is unable to provide a document in accordance with rule 36, the
  party may make an application to the Division to be allowed to provide
  the document in another way or to be excused from providing the
  document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowing application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may allow the application only if it is
  of the opinion that the party has made reasonable efforts to provide
  the document to the person to whom the document must be provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of document by Division

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document provided to the Division is considered to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a paper document, on the date
    indicated by the date stamp that the registry office places on the
    document; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an electronic document, on the date
    and at the time indicated by the electronic means used to provide
    it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of document by party ---
  regular mail

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is provided to a party by regular
  mail is considered to be received seven days after the day it was
  mailed, or 20 days after the day it was mailed if the document was
  sent to or from a place outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of document by party ---
  electronic

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is provided electronically to a party
  is considered to be received at the date and time indicated by the
  electronic means used to provide it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limit --- next working
day

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** When the time limit for
providing a document falls on a day that is not a working day, the time
limit is extended to the next working day.

## [Designated Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385018 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- Immigration Division

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** If the Immigration
Division designated a representative for a person in the proceedings
relating to the decision being appealed, the representative is deemed to
have been designated for the appeal, unless the Division decides
otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of counsel to notify --- minor

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** If counsel for a party
believes that the Division should designate a representative for a
person because the person is under 18 years of age, the counsel must
notify the Division in writing without delay, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appeal is being heard together with the appeal of
  the person's parent, guardian or tutor and the parent, guardian or
  tutor is 18 years of age or older; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appeal is against a removal order made at an
  admissibility hearing and the Immigration Division has designated a
  representative for the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to notify --- inability to
appreciate proceedings

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  counsel for a party believes that the Division should designate a
  representative for a person because the person is unable to appreciate
  the nature of the proceedings, counsel must notify the Division in
  writing without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel is not required to notify the Division under
  subrule (1) if the appeal is against a decision made at an
  admissibility hearing and the Immigration Division designated a
  representative for the person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of counsel's notice

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of a notice
under rule 43 or 44 is to assist the Division in determining whether, in
accordance with subsection 167(2) of the Act, it must designate a
representative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A notice provided under
rule 43 or 44 must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether counsel is aware of a person in
  Canada who meets the requirements to be designated as a representative
  and, if they are aware, provide the person's contact information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate the reasons why counsel believes that a
  representative should be designated; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by any available documents supporting
  the designation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** When determining whether
a person is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings, the
Division must consider any relevant factors, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the person can understand the reason for the
  proceeding and can instruct counsel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's statements and behaviour at the
  proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} expert evidence, if any, on the person's intellectual
  or physical faculties, age or mental condition; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has had a representative
  designated for them for a proceeding in a division other than the
  Immigration Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria for designated representative

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Before designating a
person as a representative, the Division must be satisfied that the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is 18 years of age or older;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} understands the nature of the proceedings;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is willing and able to act in the best interests of
  the person they are to represent;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not have interests that conflict with those of
  the person they are to represent; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} has been informed of the responsibilities of a
  designated representative and is willing and able to fulfill them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of designated
representative

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** The responsibilities of a
designated representative include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deciding whether to retain counsel and, if counsel is
  retained, instructing counsel or assisting the represented person in
  instructing counsel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} making decisions regarding the appeal or assisting
  the represented person in making those decisions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} informing the represented person about the various
  stages of the appeal

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting the represented person in gathering
  evidence to support their case and in providing evidence and, if
  necessary, being a witness at the hearing

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} protecting the interests of the represented person
  and putting forward the best possible case to the Division; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} informing and consulting the represented person to
  the extent possible when making decisions about their case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of designation

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Division may, on its
own initiative or on the request of a party, terminate a designation if
the Division is of the opinion that the representative is no longer
required or suitable. The Division may designate a new representative if
required.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of designation --- 18 years of age

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** The designation of a
representative for a person who is under 18 years of age ends when the
person reaches 18 years of age unless the representative has also been
designated because the person is unable to appreciate the nature of the
proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of designation

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** The designation of a
representative for a person ends when the Division makes a decision
allowing or dismissing the appeal or a decision that stays the removal
order.

## [Interpreters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385064 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- witness

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's oath

an oath or make a solemn affirmation to interpret accurately.

## [Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385074 .Part}

### [Witness Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385075 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing witness information

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants to call a witness must provide the following
  information in writing to the other party and the Division:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the witness's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of the purpose and substance of
    the witness's testimony;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time needed for the witness's testimony;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the party's relationship to the witness;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the party wants the
    witness to testify remotely and, if so, the means of live
    telecommunication by which they will testify; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an expert witness,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the expert witness's
      qualifications, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a brief summary of the testimony to be given,
      signed by the expert witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided to the Division under this rule
  must be accompanied by a written statement indicating how and when
  they were provided to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit --- 30 days

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  no later than 30 days before the date fixed for the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide witness information

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** If a party does not
provide the witness information in accordance with rule 55, the Division
may decide to allow the witness to testify after considering any
relevant factors, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reason why the information was not provided;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the proposed testimony is relevant and has
  probative value; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a party other than the Minister,
  whether the party has counsel.

### [Summons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385096 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requesting summons

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants the Division to order a person to testify at a hearing
  must make a request to the Division for a summons, either orally
  during a proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must consider any relevant factors
  before deciding to issue a summons, including whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the testimony of the person is necessary to a full
    and proper hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is able to give the testimony; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has agreed to be summoned as a witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using summons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} To use a summons, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the summons to the summoned person by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the summons to the Division,
    together with a written statement indicating the name of the person
    who provided the summons and the date, time and place that it was
    provided by hand; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay or offer to pay the summoned person the
    applicable witness fees and travel expenses as determined in
    accordance with Tariff A of the [Federal Courts
    Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancelling summons

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** To cancel a summons, the
person who is summoned to appear as a witness must make an application
in writing to the Division in accordance with rule 85, but is not
required to provide an accompanying affidavit or statutory declaration
under subrule 85(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrest warrant

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person does not obey a summons to appear as a witness, the party who
  requested the summons may make a request to the Division, orally or in
  writing, to issue a warrant for the person's arrest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written request must provide
  supporting evidence in an accompanying affidavit or statutory
  declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for issue of arrest
  warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may issue an arrest warrant only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person summoned was provided the summons by
    hand or the person is avoiding being provided the summons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid or offered the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses as determined in accordance with
    Tariff A of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not appear at the hearing as
    required by the summons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's testimony is still needed for a full
    and proper hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of arrest warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the Division issues an arrest warrant, the
  warrant must include directions concerning detention and release.

## [Notice to Appear]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385125 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must notify the parties in writing of the date, time and
  location fixed for any proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes to date, time or location

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any changes are made to the date, time or
  location, the Division must notify the parties in writing without
  delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date fixed for hearing

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** The hearing must take
place at least 30 days after the day on which the parties receive the
notice to appear, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hearing has been adjourned or postponed; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the parties consent to an earlier date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Party in custody

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The Division may order a
person who holds a party other than the Minister in custody to bring
that party to a proceeding at the location specified by the Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** If a party fails to
appear at a proceeding, the Division may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine that the appeal has been abandoned in
  accordance with subsection 168(1) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold the proceeding in the absence of the party;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prohibit the party from presenting further evidence,
  questioning witnesses, or making submissions to the Division;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} decide the issues in the proceeding on the basis of
  the evidence provided; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} take any other action that it considers appropriate.

## [Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385144 .Part}

### [Informal Resolution Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385145 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in informal resolution
process

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** The Division may require
the parties to participate in an informal resolution process in order to
encourage them to resolve an appeal without a hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of parties and counsel

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In any
  informal resolution process, the parties must be prepared to resolve
  the appeal and their counsel must have authority to do so. The parties
  and their counsel must also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} participate in good faith;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} follow the directions given by the Division with
    respect to the process, including the manner of participation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide to each other and to the Division any
    document that the Division requires them to prepare or provide.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document has been provided to the other party
  and the Division under paragraph (1)(c) for the purpose of an informal
  resolution process, it is deemed to have been provided in accordance
  with rule 26 for the rest of the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality of discussions ---
informal resolution process

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** Any information about a
matter discussed in an informal resolution process, including at an ADR
conference, is confidential and must not be used later in the appeal or
otherwise disclosed to a non-party unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information could be obtained in a way that is
  not part of the informal resolution process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the matter relates to an offence under the Act or a
  breach of these Rules; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person who gave the information agrees to its
  disclosure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document not confidential

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** A document provided for
the purposes of an informal resolution process, including an ADR
conference, is not confidential and may be used for the rest of the
appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to responsible authorities

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** Disclosure under
paragraph 66(b) may be made only to the authorities responsible for
enforcement of the Act or these Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}ADR conference

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division may decide to hold an ADR conference on its own initiative or
  on receipt of a request made by a party that contains a statement of
  the reasons the party believes the appeal can be resolved through such
  a conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request provided to other party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide the request made under
  subrule (1) to the other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of ADR conference facilitator

- **[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must assign a member of the Division or an employee of the
  Board as ADR conference facilitator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Member must not hear appeal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member of the Division who facilitates an ADR
  conference must not hear the appeal, unless the parties agree.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement in ADR conference

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  agreement to resolve an appeal that is reached through an ADR
  conference must be confirmed by the parties or their counsel and
  approved in writing by the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreement not confidential

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The agreement is not confidential.

### [Conferences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385180 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to participate in conference

- **[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division may, on its own initiative or on receipt of a request made by
  a party, require the parties to participate in a conference, other
  than an ADR conference, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} try to resolve some or all of the issues;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} narrow the issues in order to simplify the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} discuss issues, relevant facts and any other matter
    that would make the appeal as fair and efficient as possible; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give information to help the Division fix a date
    for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information or documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may require the parties to provide any
  information or document at or before the conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written record

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** The Division must make a
written record of any decision or agreement made at a conference.

### [Conduct of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385192 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must, in any hearing before it, give the parties the
  opportunity to present evidence, question witnesses and make
  submissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issues and evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Taking into account the nature and complexity of the
  issues and the relevance of the evidence, the Division may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} narrow the issues to be addressed at the hearing,
    including by identifying facts that are not in dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establish the order in which the issues are to be
    addressed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct and limit the evidence to be presented; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} limit the number and the length of questioning of
    witnesses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questioning witnesses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may question any witness at any point
  during the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional evidence or witnesses

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division may notify the parties if additional
  evidence or witnesses are likely to assist the Division in deciding
  the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded witness

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Unless allowed to do so
by the Division, a person must not communicate to a witness excluded
from a hearing any evidence given while the witness was excluded until
the witness has finished testifying.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral submissions

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Submissions must be made orally at
  the end of a hearing unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits on submissions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After all the evidence has been heard, the Division
  may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set time limits for submissions, taking into
    account the complexity of the issues and the amount of relevant
    evidence heard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate what issues need to be addressed in the
    submissions.

### [Proceeding in Writing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385215 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeding in writing

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Instead of holding a hearing, the
  Division may require the parties to proceed in writing if doing so
  would not be unfair to any party and a hearing is not otherwise
  required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may make a decision in a proceeding in
  writing without being provided with the appeal record under rule 21.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subrule (1) does not apply to a residency obligation
  appeal unless the parties agree to resolve the appeal without a
  hearing.

### [Stay of Removal Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385223 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reconsider appeal

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Division has stayed a removal order, a party who makes an application
  to reconsider the appeal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must make the application in accordance with rule
    85, but is not required to provide an accompanying affidavit or
    statutory declaration under subrule 85(3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must provide with their application a written
    statement indicating whether the conditions of the stay have been
    complied with.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The other party must respond to the application in
  accordance with rule 86 and provide with their response a written
  statement of whether the conditions of the stay have been complied
  with.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconsideration on Division's own
initiative

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Division reconsiders an appeal on its own initiative under subsection
  68(3) of the Act, it must notify the parties in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for providing statement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each party must provide to the Division and the other
  party, within the time limit specified by the Division, a written
  statement indicating whether the conditions of the stay have been
  complied with.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation of stay

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** If a stay of removal is
cancelled under subsection 68(4) of the Act, the Minister must provide
the Division and the other party with written notice of the cancellation
stating

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person convicted;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of conviction;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offence and the relevant provision of an Act of
  Parliament; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the offence is not punishable by a maximum term of
  imprisonment of at least 10 years, the term of imprisonment that was
  imposed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof document was provided

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must,
together with the notice under rule 80, provide a written statement
indicating how and when the notice was provided to the other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation of stay

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister provides
written notice of the cancellation under rule 80, the Division must
process the notice in the same manner as an application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to notice of cancellation of
stay

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** A party responding to a
notice provided under rule 80 must respond in accordance with rule 86.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385248 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385249 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** Unless these Rules
provide otherwise

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants the Division to make a decision on
  any matter in a proceeding, including the procedure to be followed,
  must make an application to the Division in accordance with rule 85;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to respond to the application must
  respond in accordance with rule 86; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to reply to a response must reply
  in accordance with rule 87.

### [Making an Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385255 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application and time limit

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  these Rules provide otherwise, an application must be made in writing
  without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state what decision the party wants the Division to
    make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the views of the other party are known, state
    whether the other party agrees to the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit or statutory declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless these Rules provide otherwise, any evidence
  that the party wants the Division to consider with a written
  application must be given in an affidavit or statutory declaration
  that accompanies the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written application must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, the application and any
    accompanying affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the application and any
    accompanying affidavit or statutory declaration, together with a
    written statement indicating how and when the party provided the
    documents to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Division may allow a party to make an application
  orally at a proceeding if the party demonstrates that, with reasonable
  effort, they could not have made a written application before the
  beginning of the proceeding.

### [Written Response and Reply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding to written application

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response to a written application must be in writing, state what
  decision the party wants the Division to make and give reasons why the
  Division should make that decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in written response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (3), any evidence that the party
  wants the Division to consider with the written response must be given
  in an affidavit or statutory declaration that accompanies the
  response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence not required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the party who made the application was not
  required to give evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration
  together with the application, then evidence is not required to be
  given in an affidavit or statutory declaration that accompanies the
  written response unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party who responds to a written application must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, the response and any
    accompanying affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the response and any accompanying
    affidavit or statutory declaration, together with a written
    statement indicating how and when the party provided the documents
    to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (4) must be received
  no later than seven days after the party receives the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replying to written response

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reply to a written response must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subrules 86(2) to (4) apply to the reply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (2) must be received
  no later than five days after the day on which the party receives the
  response.

### [Changing Location]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385293 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who makes an application to the Division to change the location
  of a proceeding must make the application in accordance with rule 85,
  but is not required to provide an accompanying affidavit or statutory
  declaration under subrule 85(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  no later than 30 days before the date fixed for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the proceeding at
  the location fixed and be ready to start or continue the proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** In deciding the
application for a change of location, the Division must consider any
relevant factors, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the party resides in the location where they
  request that the proceeding be held;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would allow the appeal
  to be full and proper;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would likely delay the
  proceeding;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the parties;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location is necessary to
  accommodate the vulnerabilities of a person;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether a hearing may be conducted by a means of live
  telecommunication with the parties; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the operational requirements of the Division.

### [Changing Date or Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385310 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who makes an application to the Division to change the date or
  time of a proceeding must make the application in accordance with rule
  85, but is not required to provide an accompanying affidavit or
  statutory declaration under subrule 85(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be received by the Division at least three working
    days before the date fixed for the proceeding, unless the
    application is made for medical reasons or other emergencies, in
    which case it must be made without delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate at least six dates and times that are
    within the period specified by the Division and at which the party
    is available to start or continue the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of period specified by Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must publish or post notice of the
  period referred to in paragraph (2)(b) in a manner that will allow
  public access to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the party is unable to make the application in
  accordance with paragraph (2)(a), the party must appear on the date
  and at the time fixed for the proceeding and make the application
  orally at the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the proceeding on
  the date and at the time fixed and be ready to start or continue the
  proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptional circumstances

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** The Division may allow
the application only if it determines that there are exceptional
circumstances and must take into consideration any relevant factors
including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
  and, if it was not, the justification for the delay;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any previous change in the date or time of the
  proceeding;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rights and interests of the parties;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the need to change the date or time of the proceeding
  to accommodate the vulnerabilities of a person;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the matter to be heard;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the operational requirements of the Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

**[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** If the party made a
previous application to change the date or time of a proceeding and that
application was denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the
denial and may allow the subsequent application only if the subsequent
application is based on exceptional circumstances supported by new
evidence.

### [Proceeding Conducted in Absence of the Public]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385334 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

- **[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make an application to the Division in accordance with rule
  85 to have a proceeding conducted in the absence of the public, or to
  request that the Division take any other measure to ensure the
  confidentiality of the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to respond to application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person may make a written request to the Division
  to be allowed to respond to an application to have a proceeding held
  in the absence of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of response --- rule 86

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is allowed by the Division to respond to
  the application must respond in accordance with rule 86.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality measures

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division may take any measures it considers
  necessary to ensure the confidentiality of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An application made under this rule must be received
  no later than 20 days before the date fixed for the proceeding.

### [Withdrawing an Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385346 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abuse of process

- **[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 168(2) of the Act, withdrawal of an appeal
  is an abuse of process if withdrawal would likely have a negative
  effect on the integrity of the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No substantive evidence accepted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  appeal, withdrawal of the appeal is not an abuse of process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if no substantive evidence
  accepted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If no substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  appeal, a party may withdraw their appeal by notifying the Division
  either orally at a proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if substantive evidence
  accepted

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  appeal, a party who wants to withdraw their appeal must make an
  application to the Division in accordance with rule 85.

### [Reinstating an Appeal After Withdrawal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385356 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reinstate withdrawn appeal

- **[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appellant may apply to the Division to reinstate an appeal that was
  withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant must make the application in accordance
  with rule 85 and include in the application their contact information
  and, if represented by counsel, their counsel's contact information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

**[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** The Division may allow
the application if it is established that there was a failure to observe
a principle of natural justice or if it is otherwise in the interests of
justice to allow the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

**[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97 .sectionLabel}** If the appellant has made
a previous application to reinstate an appeal and that application was
denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the previous denial
and may allow the subsequent application only if there are exceptional
circumstances supported by new evidence.

### [Application to Reopen Appeal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1385366 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

- **[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to reopen an appeal must be made in accordance with rule
  85 and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made by a party other
    than the Minister, the contact information of the party and, if they
    are represented by counsel, of their counsel; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application made by the Minister,
    the contact information of the Minister's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allegations against former counsel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party other than the Minister alleges in their
  application that their former counsel provided inadequate
  representation, the party must provide the application

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to their former counsel before providing it to the
    Division; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, accompanied by a written statement
    indicating how and when it was provided to their former counsel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  deciding the application to reopen an appeal, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and, if not, the justification for the delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant did not make an application for
    leave to apply for judicial review or an application for judicial
    review, the reasons why an application was not made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Natural justice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an application that is made other than
  under section 71 of the Act, the Division must not allow the
  application unless it is established that there was a failure to
  observe a principle of natural justice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

**[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** If the appellant has
made a previous application to reopen an appeal and that application was
denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the previous denial
and must not allow the subsequent application unless there are
exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

## [Notice of Constitutional Question]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385385 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of constitutional question

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants to challenge the constitutional validity,
  applicability or operability of a legislative provision must complete
  a notice of constitutional question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be completed using Form 69 of the
  [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) or any other form
  that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the party's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Division file number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific legislative provision that is being
    challenged;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the material facts relied on to support the
    constitutional challenge; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the legal argument to be made in
    support of the constitutional challenge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide the notice to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Attorney General of Canada and the attorney
    general of every province, in accordance with section 57 of the
    [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other party; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice provided to Division

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The notice that is provided to the Division must be
  accompanied by a written statement indicating how and when the notices
  were provided to the recipients referred to in paragraphs (3)(a)
  and (b) and proof that they were provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  no later than 10 days before the day on which the constitutional
  question is to be argued.

## [Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385406 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** When the Division makes
a decision, other than an interlocutory decision, it must provide a
written notice of decision to the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reasons

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the Division makes a decision on a sponsorship appeal or stays a
  removal order, it must provide the notice of decision together with
  the written reasons for the decision to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for written reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request referred to in paragraph 169(e) of the Act
  for reasons for a decision, other than a decision referred to in
  subrule (1) or an interlocutory decision, must be in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision takes effect

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** A decision, other than
an interlocutory decision, takes effect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is made in writing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by a single member, when the member signs and dates
    the reasons for the decision; or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by a panel of three members, when all of the
    members of the panel sign and date their reasons for the decision;
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is given orally at a hearing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by a single member, when the member states the
    decision and gives the reasons; or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by a panel of three members, when all of the
    members of the panel state their decision and give their reasons.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1385422 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) to (4),
  these Rules apply to all proceedings filed before the day on which
  these Rules come into force, including any proceeding that a court
  refers back to the Division for redetermination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister or the Immigration Division receives
  a notice of appeal before the day on which these Rules come into
  force, the appeal record must be provided in accordance with the time
  limits set out in the [Immigration Appeal Division
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-230) as they read immediately before
  that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality --- ADR

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subrule 20(4) of the [Immigration Appeal Division
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-230), as they read immediately before
  the day on which these Rules come into force, continues to apply to
  any confidential information, statement or document given in an ADR
  conference for which the date of the notice to appear is before that
  day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an appeal is filed before the day on which these
  Rules come into force,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the statement referred to in subrule 24(2) of these
    Rules is not required in respect of the appeal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time limits for providing or receiving
    documents set out in subrules 30(3) and (4) and 37(3) of the
    [Immigration Appeal Division Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-230),
    as they read immediately before that day, continue to apply.
