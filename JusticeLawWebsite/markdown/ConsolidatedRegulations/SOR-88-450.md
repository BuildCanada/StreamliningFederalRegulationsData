## Wiarton Airport Zoning Regulations {#wiarton-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-450
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1988-08-25

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Wiarton Airport

P.C. 1988-1717 1988-08-25
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Wiarton Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 26th, 1987, and
on January 2nd, 1988, and in two successive issues of the Wiarton Echo
on January 27th and February 3rd, 1988, and in two successive issues of
the Owen Sound Sun Times on January 28th and 29th, 1988, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 4.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Wiarton
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-450).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913209 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Wiarton Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-450).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913211 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Wiarton Airport, in the
      Township of Keppel, County of Grey, in the Province of Ontario;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport:
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 219 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913222 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-178, s. 1(F)
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913226 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913231 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-178, s. 2
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913235 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste edible by or attractive
to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-178, s. 3(E)
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Wiarton Airport Zoning Plan
No. 42-062 84-87, Sheet 5, dated April 2, 1986, is a point distant
247.5 m measured southeasterly at right angles from the centre line of
runway 05-23, from a point distant 495.4 m measured southwesterly along
the said centre line from the northeastern end of the said runway 05-23.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Wiarton Airport Zoning Plan No. 42-062
84-87, Sheets 2 to 8 inclusive and 10 to 13 inclusive, dated April 2,
1986 are surfaces abutting each end of the strips associated with
runways designated 05-23 and 11-29 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 05 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 23 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 11 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 075 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 137.5 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 43 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 29 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 100 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 140 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 44 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Wiarton Airport Zoning Plan No. 42-062
84-87, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive and 11 and 12, dated April 2, 1986, is an
imaginary surface located at a common plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point,
except that, where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface
of the ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m
above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips shown on Wiarton Airport Zoning Plan No. 42-062 84-87, Sheet
5 dated April 2, 1986 are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 05-23 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 621.36 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 11-29 is 60 m in
  width, 30 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 173.32 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Wiarton Airport Zoning Plan
No. 42-062 84-87, Sheets 2 to 8 inclusive and 11 and 12, dated April 2,
1986, is described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with
  runway 05-23, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right
  angles to the centre line and projected centre line extending upward
  and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
  surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with
  runway 11-29, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at right
  angles to the centre line and projected centre line extending upward
  and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
  surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
