## Regulations Prescribing a Provincial Tax {#regulations-prescribing-a-provincial-tax .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-288
:::

[FEDERAL-PROVINCIAL FISCAL ARRANGEMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/F-8)

Registration 1988-05-19

Regulations Prescribing a Tax for the Purposes of Paragraph (c) of the
Definition "Provincial Tax or Fee" in Subsection 34(1) of the
Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements and Federal Post-Secondary
Education and Health Contributions Act, 1977

P.C. 1988-938 1988-05-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 34(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EGCAA} and section 43 of the
Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements and Federal Post-Secondary
Education and Health Contributions Act, 1977[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDCAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations prescribing a tax for the purposes of
paragraph (c) of the definition "provincial tax or fee" in
subsection 34(1) of the Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements and
Federal Post-Secondary Education and Health Contributions Act, 1977.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911693 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Regulations Prescribing a Provincial
Tax](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-288).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911695 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Federal-Provincial Fiscal
Arrangements and Federal Post-Secondary Education and Health
Contributions Act, 1977. (*Loi*)

## [Provincial Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911697 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Quebec provincial tax
payable on insurance premiums pursuant to Chapter III of the Retail
Sales Tax Act, R.S.Q. c. I-1, is a provincial tax within the meaning of
paragraph (c) of the definition of provincial tax or fee in
subsection 34(1) of the Act.
