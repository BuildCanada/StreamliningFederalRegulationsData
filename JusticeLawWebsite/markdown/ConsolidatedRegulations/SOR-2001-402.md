## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity referred to in any of
      paragraphs 468(1)(a) to (j) of the Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes one or more of
      the activities referred to in paragraph 468(2)(a) of the Act and
      that engages, as part of its business, in any financial
      intermediary activity that exposes the entity to material market
      or credit risk, including a factoring entity, a finance entity or
      a financial leasing entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose business includes an activity
      referred to in paragraph 468(2)(b) of the Act, including a
      specialized financing entity, other than an entity in which a bank
      is permitted to acquire or increase a substantial investment under
      subparagraph 468(4)(d)(iii) of the Act. ([entité
      désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulatory capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 3 of the [Regulatory Capital (Banks)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-531). ([capital
    réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a share, membership share,
      ownership interest or loan held by a bank at a particular time,
      the book value of the share, membership share, ownership interest
      or loan that would be reported on the balance sheet of the bank
      prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting
      principles and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in
      subsection 308(4) of the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee, the face value of the
      guarantee. ([valeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 8(F)
- SOR/2012-269, s. 9
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675491 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted substantial investments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subparagraphs 468(4)(a)(ii),
  (b)(ii), (c)(ii) and (d)(ii) of the Act, a bank may acquire or
  increase a substantial investment in a designated entity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of paragraph 468(11)(a) of the Act,
  if a bank controls a designated entity, the bank may give up control
  of the designated entity while keeping a substantial investment in it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning investments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, a bank must not acquire or increase a substantial investment in a
designated entity under paragraph 2(a) or give up control of the
designated entity while keeping a substantial investment in it under
paragraph 2(b) if, after the acquisition, increase or giving up of
control, the total value of the following would exceed 50% of the bank's
regulatory capital:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all shares, membership shares and ownership interests
  beneficially owned by the bank, and all shares, membership shares and
  ownership interests beneficially owned by entities controlled by the
  bank, in designated entities in which the bank has a substantial
  investment but over which it does not exercise control,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all loans held by the bank, and all loans held by
  entities controlled by the bank, that were made to designated entities
  in which the bank has a substantial investment but over which it does
  not exercise control, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all outstanding guarantees given by the bank, and all
  outstanding guarantees given by entities controlled by the bank, on
  behalf of designated entities in which the bank has a substantial
  investment but over which it does not exercise control.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-269, s. 10
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction concerning loans

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, a bank that has a substantial investment in a designated entity over
which it does not exercise control must not make a loan to, or give a
guarantee on behalf of, the designated entity, or permit entities
controlled by it to do so, if, after the making of the loan or the
giving of the guarantee, the total value of the shares, membership
shares, ownership interests, loans and guarantees referred to in
paragraphs 3(a) to (c) would exceed 50% of the bank's regulatory
capital.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-269, s. 11(E)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In paragraphs 3(a) to (c)
and section 4, any reference to a substantial investment that a bank has
does not include a substantial investment acquired by the bank

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under regulations made under paragraph 474(a) of the
  Act, other than these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 466(3.1) of the Act, as that
  subsection read before the coming into force of section 127 of the
  [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), S.C.
  2001, c. 9; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by way of an investment of a specialized financing
  entity controlled by the bank.

Calculation of [[total value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3 or 4

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
calculating the total value referred to in section 3 or 4, no amount may
be included in respect of shares, membership shares or ownership
interests acquired under sections 471 to 473 of the Act or
subsection 193(12) or (13) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01),
chapter B-1 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985.
