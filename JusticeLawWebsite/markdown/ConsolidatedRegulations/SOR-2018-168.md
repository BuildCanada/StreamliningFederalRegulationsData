## Proclamation Declaring that the Government Corporations Operation Act is Applicable to Project Deliver II Ltd. {#proclamation-declaring-that-the-government-corporations-operation-act-is-applicable-to-project-deliver-ii-ltd. .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-168
:::

[GOVERNMENT CORPORATIONS OPERATION ACT](/eng/acts/G-4)

Registration 2018-07-27

Proclamation Declaring that the Government Corporations Operation Act is
Applicable to Project Deliver II Ltd.
::::

:::::::: section
::::::: {.section .order}
Julie Payette

\[L.S.\]

Canada

ELIZABETH THE SECOND, by the Grace of God of the United Kingdom, Canada
and Her other Realms and Territories QUEEN, Head of the Commonwealth,
Defender of the Faith.

::: FlushLeft
Nathalie G. Drouin

::: FlushLeft
Deputy Attorney General

Great Seal of Canada

TO ALL TO WHOM these presents shall come or whom the same may in any way
concern,

GREETING:

## [A proclamation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-852168 .Part}

Whereas section 6 of the [Government Corporations Operation
Act](/eng/acts/G-4) provides that the Act applies to a corporation only
from the date of issue of a proclamation by the Governor in Council
declaring the Act to be applicable to that corporation;

Whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 2018-0682 of June 1, 2018, the
Governor in Council directed that a proclamation do issue declaring that
the [Government Corporations Operation Act](/eng/acts/G-4) is applicable
to 10804142 CANADA INC.;

Whereas, on June 12, 2018, the corporate name of 10804142 CANADA INC.
was changed to Project Deliver II Ltd.;

Whereas Project Deliver II Ltd. is a corporation incorporated under the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) and a wholly-owned
subsidiary of Project Deliver I Ltd.;

And whereas, pursuant to section 3 of the [Government Corporations
Operation Act](/eng/acts/G-4), Project Deliver I Ltd. is for all its
purposes an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada and therefore, in
accordance with the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), all
of the issued shares of Project Deliver II Ltd. are owned by Her Majesty
in right of Canada;

Now know you that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our proclamation declare that the [Government
Corporations Operation Act](/eng/acts/G-4) is applicable to Project
Deliver II Ltd.

Of all which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these presents may
concern are required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In testimony whereof, We have caused this Our Proclamation to be
published and the Great Seal of Canada to be affixed to it.

WITNESS:

Our Right Trusty and Well-beloved Julie Payette, Chancellor and
Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada, Chancellor and Commander of
Our Order of Military Merit, Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of
Merit of the Police Forces, Governor General and Commander-in-Chief of
Canada.

AT OUR GOVERNMENT HOUSE, in Our City of Ottawa, this eighteenth day of
July in the year of Our Lord two thousand and eighteen and in the
sixty-seventh year of Our Reign.

BY COMMAND,

::: FlushLeft
John Knubley
:::

::: FlushLeft
Deputy Registrar General of Canada
