## Submission of Samples of Fabrics for Importation Regulations {#submission-of-samples-of-fabrics-for-importation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-872
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-08-14

Regulations Respecting the Submission of Samples of Fabrics for
Importation

P.C. 1986-1841 1986-08-13
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 22, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
paragraph 164(1)(d) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-86-872_e_hq_6389){#fn_SOR-86-872_e_hq_6389-ID0EFBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
submission of samples of fabrics for
importation](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-872), effective on the coming into
force of paragraph 164(1)(d) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898346 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Submission of Samples of Fabrics for Importation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-872).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898348 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of the imported fabrics listed in the schedule.

## [Sample]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898350 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every person importing
fabrics into Canada shall ensure that there is attached to the
accounting document required pursuant to subsection 32(1) of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) a sample of every quality or style of
the imported fabrics.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A sample of fabric referred
to in section 3 must be of a size sufficient to identify the design or
pattern, if any, woven into or imprinted on the fabric and must be at
least 10 × 10 cm.
