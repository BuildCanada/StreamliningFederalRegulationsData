## [Notice to Be Given Before Opening a Deposit Account]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747936 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice required under subsection 378.2(1) of the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) shall be given either as part of
  the account agreement or as a separate document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the notice is given to a person on premises that
  the retail association shares with a member institution within the
  meaning of section 2 of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the notice shall contain an additional statement
    indicating that the retail association's business is separate and
    distinct from that of the member institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the retail association shall verbally explain all
    the information contained in the notice to the person requesting the
    account and obtain from them a signed declaration stating that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person has been given and has read the
      notice,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the retail association has verbally explained
      the information contained in the notice, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the person understands that deposits with the
      retail association are not insured by the Canada Deposit Insurance
      Corporation.

## [Notices in Branches and on Websites]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747945 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice required to be posted under paragraph 378.2(2)(a) of the
  [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) shall be in
  the following form:

  ### [**NOTICE**]{.HLabel1} {#notice .SchedHeadL1}

  Deposits with (*name of retail association*) are **not insured** by
  the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice in a branch shall be prominently displayed
  in such a manner that it is clearly visible in the area of the branch
  that is open to the public. The notice shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be 27.94 cm in height and 43.18 cm in width; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} display letters having a font size of 120 points in
    the heading and a font size of 50 points in the remainder of the
    text.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice on a website shall be prominently displayed
  in such a manner that it is clearly visible to the person accessing
  the website.

## [Notices in Advertisements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747954 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The notice required under
paragraph 378.2(2)(b) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) shall be in the form set out in subsection 2(1)
and shall appear prominently in advertisements in which the retail
association offers deposit facilities or solicits deposits.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747956 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind15F8_hq_4190){#fn_Ind15F8_hq_4190-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 160 of An Act to amend the law governing financial institutions
and to provide for related and consequential matters, chapter 6 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2007, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
