## Demonstration Aircraft Remission Order {#demonstration-aircraft-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 756
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting Remission of Sales Tax on Domestically Manufactured
Aircraft Used for Demonstration to Prospective Customers
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561570 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Demonstration Aircraft Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._756).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561572 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, "aircraft"
includes helicopters.

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561574 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the sales tax payable under the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on any aircraft manufactured in Canada at the
time of its first use in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/86-58, s. 1
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561578 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 3 is granted on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is used only by the manufacturer of the
  aircraft for the purpose of demonstrating the aircraft to prospective
  customers of the manufacturer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer keeps complete records of the use of
  the aircraft; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer produces the records for inspection
  when requested to do so by an officer of the Department of National
  Revenue administering or enforcing the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/86-58, s. 1
:::

**[[5 and 6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5_and_6 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked,
SI/86-58, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}
