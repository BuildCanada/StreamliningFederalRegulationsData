## Order Transferring from Privy Council Office to the Department of Canadian Heritage the Control and Supervision of the Official Languages Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-canadian-heritage-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-official-languages-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-45
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Transferring from Privy Council Office to the Department of
Canadian Heritage the Control and Supervision of the Official Languages
Secretariat

P.C. 2006-86 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Canadian Heritage the control and supervision of the portion of the
federal public administration within the Privy Council Office in the
Intergovernmental Policy Branch known as the Official Languages
Secretariat, effective February 6, 2006.
