## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931333 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of coal mines subject to the Act.

## [Records, Reports, Plans and Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931337 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  record, report, plan or procedure referred to in these Regulations, or
  any copy thereof, shall be kept by the employer above ground at the
  coal mine in respect of which it applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in such a manner that it is readily available for
    examination by a safety officer and by the safety and health
    committee or the safety and health representative for the work place
    in respect of which it applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections 69(5) and 143(6), for a
    period of at least two years after the last entry therein.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every record, report, plan or procedure referred to
  in these Regulations shall be dated and signed by the person who made
  the record, report, plan or procedure.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements of subsection (2),
  every plan referred to in these Regulations shall be countersigned by
  the mine manager of the coal mine in respect of which the plan
  applies.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where the employer submits
plans or procedures in respect of a coal mine to the Coal Mining Safety
Commission for approval, the employer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay after the submission, notify the safety
  and health committee or the safety and health representative in
  writing of the submission; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the approved plans or procedures
  readily available at the coal mine for examination by the employees.

## [Inconsistent Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931347 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
inconsistency between any standard incorporated by reference in a
provision of these Regulations and any other provision of these
Regulations, that other provision shall prevail to the extent of the
inconsistency.

## [Qualifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931349 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Before an employee is
employed in a position of mine manager or is appointed to a position of
underground manager or overman, the employee shall have obtained the
qualifications required for all positions junior to that position.

## [Appointments and Supervision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931351 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer shall employ a separate mine manager for each coal mine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where a mine manager dies,
  resigns or is otherwise permanently prevented from discharging the
  responsibilities of mine manager, the employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify in writing a person who holds a first
    class certificate as a mine official to perform the duties of the
    mine manager;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify in writing an underground manager to
    perform the duties of the mine manager; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} stop the operation of the mine.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 90 days after an identification made under
  paragraph (2)(b), the employer shall comply with paragraph (2)(a) or
  (c).

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every coal mine shall be
under the daily supervision of at least one underground manager and one
overman.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Explosives and Detonators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931360 .Part}

### [Storage and Handling]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931361 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No explosive or
detonator, other than an explosive or detonator set out in Schedule II,
shall be used in a coal mine.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  explosive or detonator shall be stored underground in a coal mine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Explosives and detonators that are intended for use
  underground shall be stored in a structure above ground that has been
  approved by the Coal Mining Safety Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The maximum quantity of explosives and detonators
  that are stored in accordance with subsection (2) shall be not more
  than the quantity required for use underground during the 24-hour
  period immediately following its storage.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Detonators shall be stored separately from explosives
  in a room that has a wall of masonry not less than 150 mm thick
  between it and any room in which explosives are stored.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  explosives and detonators that are transported underground, other than
  explosives and detonators referred to in subsection 28(3) or 29(4),
  shall be in secure cases or canisters with the detonators in separate
  cases or canisters from the explosives.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No case or canister referred to in subsection (1)
  shall contain more than 5 kg of explosive unless approval for the
  transportation of the explosive in bulk has been given by the Coal
  Mining Safety Commission.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  employee who receives, transports or uses explosives or detonators
  shall return any explosive or detonator that is not used during the
  employee's shift to the surface structure in which it had been stored.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an explosive or detonator is transported
  underground, it shall be kept in its secure case or canister until it
  is used.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), each case or canister
  referred to in subsection (2) shall be kept separate from every other
  case or canister and from all equipment that is likely to cause the
  ignition of the explosive or the detonator, by as great a distance as
  is practicable in the area where the explosive or detonator is kept.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The distance referred to in subsection (3) shall be
  not less than 300 mm.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  qualified person shall have control of the storage, issue and receipt
  of all explosives and detonators.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The qualified person referred to in subsection (1)
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} test each detonator for electrical continuity
    before issuing it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue detonators to no person other than a
    shotfirer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} receive and store unused explosives and detonators
    returned from underground.

### [Care of Blasting Machines]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931382 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  qualified person shall, at least once every three months, clean and
  thoroughly overhaul all blasting machines that are in service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A record of the work performed pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be made by the qualified person who performed the
  work.

### [Shotfirers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931386 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person other than a shotfirer shall fire a shot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No shotfirer shall fire a shot unless the shotfirer
  has been authorized to fire that type of shot.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The handling of
explosives and detonators in the preparation underground of a shot or
shot hole shall be under the direct supervision and control of a
shotfirer.

### [Shotfiring Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931391 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where shotfiring is to be
done underground during a shift, the shotfirer shall verify that no more
explosives or detonators than are required for the shotfiring are kept
in the danger area referred to in paragraph 22(1)(a) in which the
shotfiring is to be done.

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  shotfirer shall use

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} only one class, grade or quality of explosive in a
    shot;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explosive that is in its original wrapping;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no more explosive in any shot hole than the amount
    that is necessary for the work to be performed or the maximum amount
    of that type of explosive that is set out in column II of Part I of
    Schedule II, whichever is the lesser;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} tamping rods and stemmers that are constructed of
    non-metallic materials; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a cleaner and crack detector that is capable of
    cleaning the full length of the shot hole and detecting transverse
    and longitudinal cracks of 3 mm or more in the shot hole.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No shot shall be fired

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in broken coal or in a support pillar; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an area where the concentration of flammable gas
    in the air exceeds 1.25 per cent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every coal-cutting machine shall be stopped before
  shots are fired at the return brushing face in a longwall section.

### [Preparations Before Charging a Shot Hole]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931404 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Before initiating the
preparations for charging a shot hole referred to in section 21, a
shotfirer shall verify that all broken coal is removed from the area of
the shot hole and shall allow sufficient time for dust in the air to
settle.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A shotfirer shall,
immediately before charging a shot hole underground,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 27(3), verify, using a
  methanometer and a locked-flame safety lamp, that the concentration of
  flammable gas in the general body of the air in the area of the shot
  hole and in any area contiguous thereto does not exceed 1.25 per cent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} examine the shot hole to verify that it has been
  thoroughly cleaned;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} verify that the shot hole does not intersect any
  cracks or crevices greater than 3 mm;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} verify that the shot hole is at least 3 mm larger
  than the diameter of the cartridge of the explosive to be used;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} place a plug of stemming in the back of the shot
  hole; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} verify that any dust in the area of the shot hole has
  been treated with stone dust to a minimum of three 25 kg bags of stone
  dust per metre of advance.

### [Precautions Before Firing a Shot]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931413 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  firing a shot, a shotfirer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine a danger area that extends at least 30 m
    from the shot hole;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evacuate all persons from the danger area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} post sentries at the limits of the danger area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sentries referred to in paragraph (1)(c) shall
  prevent any person, other than the shotfirer, from entering the danger
  area until the shotfirer verifies that the area is safe to enter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than a shotfirer, shall enter the
  danger area referred to in subsection (1) until the person has been
  informed by the shotfirer or a sentry that it is safe to do so.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Immediately before firing
a shot, a shotfirer shall repeat the test referred to in
paragraph 21(a).

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Where a shot or round of
shots is fired, the shotfirer shall make an examination of the effect of
the shot or round of shots before the next shot or round of shots is
fired.

### [Firing with a Blasting Machine]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931423 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a shot is to be fired with a blasting machine, the shotfiring cable
  shall be not less than 30 m long.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than a shotfirer, shall connect a
  shotfiring cable to detonator wires or to a blasting machine.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a shot is to be fired with a blasting machine,
  the shotfirer shall, in the following sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} twist the ends of the shotfiring cable together at
    the blasting machine and isolate them from ground;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} check the shotfiring cable and the detonator
    circuit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} connect the shotfiring cable to the shot;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} check the total circuit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} verify that all persons in the danger area referred
    to in paragraph 22(1)(a) have been evacuated from that area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} connect the shotfiring cable to the blasting
    machine, verifying that the shotfirer alone has possession and
    control of the blasting machine;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} shout "FIRE" or "[TIR]{.otherLang lang="fr"}";

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} verify that the danger area referred to in
    paragraph 22(1)(a) is clear of persons;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} take cover outside the danger area referred to in
    paragraph 22(1)(a);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} fire the shot;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} subject to sections 28 and 29, wait at least five
    minutes before the shotfirer returns to the area of the shot hole to
    satisfy himself that no dangerous condition has resulted from firing
    the shot; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} measure the concentration of flammable gas in the
    general body of the air in the area of the shot hole.

### [Single Shots]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931440 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 27,
each shot hole shall be charged separately and each shot shall be fired
singly.

### [Multi-shots]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931442 .Subheading}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  rounds of shots may be fired in shafts, development drivages,
  cross-measure drivages, intake and return brushings of longwall faces
  and repair brushings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Not more than 12 shots in a round may be fired
  simultaneously using the shortest millisecond-delay detonators.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A round of shots using different millisecond-delay
  detonators shall not be fired unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of flammable gas in the general
    body of the air in the area of the shot holes and in any area
    contiguous thereto does not exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the period between the detonation of the
      first shot and the detonation of the last shot is calculated not
      to exceed 0.2 second, 1 per cent, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the period between the detonation of the
      first shot and the detonation of the last shot is calculated to
      exceed 0.2 second but not to exceed 0.75 second, 0.8 per cent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mine manager has issued to the shotfirer for
    each area of firing a copy of the shotfiring pattern that shows

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the position and direction of every shot hole in
      the round,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity of explosive to be used in
      each shot hole, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum length of each shot hole; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the maximum period that may elapse between the
    detonation of the first shot and the detonation of the last shot is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the shots are fired in a development
      drivage that contains no seam of coal exceeding 300 mm and that is
      more than 5 m from a waste or fault, 0.75 second or less, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, 0.2 second or less.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the shotfiring pattern for each area at
  which multi-shot firing is carried out in a mine shall be kept
  available above ground at the coal mine for examination by employees.

### [Misfired Shots]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931458 .Subheading}

#### [Single Shots]{.HTitleText3} {#h-931459 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a single shot misfires, the shotfirer shall, in the following
  sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove the handle or key from the blasting machine
    and disconnect the blasting machine from the shotfiring cable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} twist the ends of the shotfiring cable together at
    the blasting machine and isolate them from ground;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wait 15 minutes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} examine the shotfiring cable and connections for
    any defects and remedy any that are found; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make a further attempt to fire the shot using
    either the same blasting machine or another blasting machine that is
    suitable for the purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the attempt to fire the shot referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e) fails, the shotfirer shall, in the following
  sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove the handle or key from the blasting machine
    and disconnect the blasting machine from the shotfiring cable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} twist the ends of the shotfiring cable together at
    the blasting machine and isolate them from ground;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wait 15 minutes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} mark the location of the detonator lead wires of
    the misfired shot;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cause a new shot hole to be drilled at a distance
    of not less than 300 mm from the loaded shot hole and parallel to
    and equal in depth to that shot hole; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} fire a shot in the newly drilled shot hole.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A shotfirer shall wait for five minutes after firing
  the shot referred to in paragraph (2)(f) and then, where practicable,
  sort the debris by hand, recover all explosives and detonators from
  the debris and take them to the structure referred to in
  subsection 11(2).

#### [Rounds of Shots]{.HTitleText3} {#h-931475 .Subheading}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  all of a round of shots misfire, the shotfirer shall, in the following
  sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove the handle or key from the blasting machine
    and disconnect the shotfiring cable from the blasting machine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} twist the ends of the shotfiring cable together at
    the blasting machine and isolate them from ground;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wait 15 minutes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} test the circuit for electrical continuity; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the test shows that there is electrical
    continuity, attempt to fire the round using either the same blasting
    machine or another blasting machine that is suitable for the
    purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the test referred to in paragraph (1)(e) shows
  that there is no electrical continuity, or where the attempt referred
  to in that paragraph fails, the shotfirer shall, in the following
  sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remove the handle or key from the blasting machine
    and disconnect the shotfiring cable from the blasting machine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} twist the ends of the shotfiring cable together at
    the blasting machine and isolate them from ground;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wait 15 minutes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} disconnect the shotfiring cable and detonator lead
    wires at each shot hole;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} test for electrical continuity in the shotfiring
    cable and detonator lead wires;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} test the blasting machine; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the tests show that there is electrical
    continuity, the detonator lead wires and the shotfiring cable shall
    be reconnected and tested for electrical continuity and a further
    attempt shall be made to fire the round.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any of the tests referred to in
  paragraphs (2)(e) to (g) do not produce the desired result, the
  shotfirer shall install barricades and post a warning sign in
  accordance with subsection 31(1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a single shot in a round misfires and is
  recovered, or where a part of a round of shots misfires and is
  recovered, the shotfirer shall sort the debris by hand, recover all
  explosives and detonators from the debris and take them to the
  structure referred to in subsection 11(2).

### [Unrecovered Misfired Shots]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931493 .Subheading}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a single shot in a round misfires and is not recovered, the procedures
  set out in subsections 29(1) and (2) shall be carried out.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a part of a round of shots misfires and is not
  recovered, the procedures set out in subsection 29(2) shall be carried
  out and groups of shots with the same millisecond-delay shall be fired
  in turn, in increasing order of delay.

<!-- -->

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a misfired shot is not recovered, the shotfirer shall cause

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} barricades to be installed to prevent persons from
    entering the danger area for the shot hole determined in accordance
    with paragraph 22(1)(a); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a warning sign to be conspicuously posted on each
    barricade referred to in paragraph (a) with the words "MISFIRED SHOT
    --- KEEP OUT" and "[COUP DE MINE RATÉ --- ENTRÉE
    INTERDITE]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} After having carried out the procedures set out in
  subsection (1), the shotfirer shall report orally to the underground
  manager or mine manager the misfired shot and the procedures carried
  out.

### [Flamed Shots]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931502 .Subheading}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a shot flames on firing, the shotfirer shall, in the following
  sequence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately take steps to extinguish the flame;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} verify that the section of the coal mine in which
    the shot was fired is guarded by an employee and left unworked,
    except for making the section safe, until inspected by a safety
    officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} without delay, report the flamed shot orally to the
    shotfirer's supervisor or the underground manager; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} report the flamed shot in writing to the mine
    manager.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a mine manager is informed of a flamed shot in
  accordance with paragraph (1)(d), the mine manager shall, without
  undue delay, make a report of the flamed shot to a safety officer at
  the district office.

### [Reports and Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931510 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  end of a shotfirer's shift, the shotfirer shall report in writing to
  the shotfirer's immediate supervisor every misfired shot and flamed
  shot for which the shotfirer was responsible during the shift.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an immediate supervisor is informed of a
  misfired shot or flamed shot in accordance with subsection (1), the
  supervisor shall, without undue delay, report in writing to the
  underground manager or mine manager the misfired shot or flamed shot.

<!-- -->

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  end of a shotfirer's shift, the shotfirer shall record in a book that
  is kept for that purpose all the shots the preparation and firing of
  which the shotfirer was responsible for during the shift.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record referred to in subsection (1) shall
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of detonators used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of explosive used, expressed in grams;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each shot or round of shots,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the time of firing of the shot or round of shots,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of flammable gas in the air
      before and after each shot or round of shots was fired,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of detonators used, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the amount of explosive used, expressed in
      grams;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of misfired shots and whether they were
    recovered; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of flamed shots.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Safe Occupancy of the Work Place]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931526 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931527 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mine manager shall, in respect of each underground portion of a coal
  mine,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop for employees procedures for the safe entry
    to, exit from and occupancy of that portion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} develop emergency procedures that include

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an emergency evacuation plan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedures to be followed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the location of the emergency equipment
      provided by the employer, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an up-to-date plan of the coal mine; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} develop and implement safe operating procedures for
    each employee occupation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep a copy of the procedures
  referred to in subsection (1) readily available for examination by
  employees at the coal mine in respect of which the procedures apply.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall instruct and train all employees
  in the procedures referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

<!-- -->

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer shall provide every person granted access to an underground
  portion of a coal mine with an electric safety lamp.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall provide every person who carries
  out an inspection that is required to be carried out under these
  Regulations by a person who holds a certificate as a mine examiner
  with a locked-flame safety lamp.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Not more than 15 persons
shall be present at one time in a place underground, unless there are at
least two separate means of exit to the surface.

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall excavate a shaft at a distance of less than 30 m from another
  shaft.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A shaft may be connected to another shaft by a
  passageway if the passageway is not less than 1.2 m in width and 1.2 m
  in height.

<!-- -->

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  qualified person shall, every day, inspect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vertical shafts by which employees descend or
    ascend in a coal mine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all shaft equipment that is used in the
    transportation system for employees in the vertical shafts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The qualified person referred to in subsection (1)
  shall make a written report of the inspection in a book kept for that
  purpose.

### [Overmen's Sections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931551 .Subheading}

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A mine
  manager shall define on a plan, on a scale of not less than 1:10,000,
  the limits of each section of the mine for which an overman is
  responsible in such a manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every working face, other than an area where work
    is being carried out for the purpose of repairing or enlarging a
    roadway, is included within a section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the section is of a size that permits a pre-shift
    inspection to be completed in two hours or less.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mine manager shall designate a meeting station that
  is located at the entrance to each overman's section referred to in
  subsection (1) and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mark each meeting station clearly on the plan; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause a notice to be posted at each meeting station
    identifying it as a meeting station.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than a person carrying out an
  inspection or a person accompanying that person, shall pass beyond a
  meeting station referred to in subsection (2) unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the section has been inspected and reported to be
    safe by the person who carried out the inspection referred to in
    subsection 41(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is instructed to pass beyond the meeting
    station by the overman responsible for the section.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No overman shall instruct any person to pass beyond
  the meeting station of the section for which the overman is
  responsible unless the overman has information indicating that it is
  safe to pass beyond the meeting station.

### [Pre-Shift Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931563 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  overman's section shall be inspected by a mine examiner within the
  four-hour period preceding the beginning of work by each shift in that
  section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there are persons present in an overman's
  section, a mine examiner shall carry out the inspections of that
  section at intervals not exceeding eight hours.

### [Inspections During Shifts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931567 .Subheading}

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  overman who is responsible for a section shall inspect every part of
  the section at least once during every shift at such times that no
  place at which an employee works remains uninspected by the overman
  for more than four hours after the timewhen the shift of that employee
  began work in that section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspection referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  carried out to ascertain the conditions in relation to ventilation,
  strata control and general safety.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At least once during every shift, a mine examiner
  shall inspect every part of the overman's section that is allotted to
  the mine examiner for inspection purposes.

### [Inspections Outside an Overman's Section]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931572 .Subheading}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  beginning of every shift and at least once during every shift, a mine
  examiner shall inspect the following places that are not included in
  an overman's section:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every place at which mineral is being worked for
    repairing or enlarging a roadway;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every place from which machinery, equipment, tools
    or supports are being removed or salvaged; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every place at which employees may work and through
    which employees do not regularly pass.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mine examiner shall inspect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every roadway or place through which employees
    regularly pass, at intervals not exceeding 24 hours; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every airway, at least once per week.

### [General Duties]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931581 .Subheading}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  overman or mine examiner who carries out an inspection under any of
  sections 41 to 43 shall, in the course of the inspection,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspect the machinery and equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} report to the underground manager any machinery or
    equipment found by the employee to be unsafe; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} post at the appropriate meeting station a record of
    the report referred to in paragraph (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall use any machinery or equipment that
  is the subject of a record referred to in paragraph (1)(c) until such
  time as it has been reported to be safe.

### [Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931588 .Subheading}

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  mine examiner who carries out an inspection referred to in section 41
  or subsection 42(2) shall make a report of the inspection in a book
  kept for that purpose, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the state of the roof support;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the state of the ventilation and all matters
    affecting ventilation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of flammable gases; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any thing or circumstance that is likely to be
    hazardous to the safety or health of employees.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every overman or mine examiner who carries out an
  inspection referred to in subsection 42(1) or section 43 shall make a
  report of the inspection in a book kept for that purpose, including
  all information relevant to the safety or health of employees.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an inspection discloses a dangerous condition
  in an underground portion of a coal mine, the employee who carried out
  the inspection shall forthwith report the condition to the overman who
  is responsible for that portion of the coal mine or to the underground
  manager.

### [Dangerous Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931597 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an overman who is responsible for an underground portion of a coal
  mine becomes aware of a dangerous condition in that portion of the
  coal mine, the overman shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evacuate all persons, other than an employee
    referred to in subsection (3), from the portion affected by the
    dangerous condition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} post a sign in a conspicuous place as close as
    possible to the dangerous condition but outside the danger area
    created by the dangerous condition, to warn persons of the dangerous
    condition; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} report the existence of the dangerous condition
    orally to the mine manager or the underground manager.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a sign is posted in accordance with
  paragraph (1)(b), no person shall enter the danger area referred to in
  that paragraph.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an employee whose
  presence is necessary to correct the dangerous condition referred to
  in subsection (1).

### [Inspection on Behalf of Employees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931605 .Subheading}

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employees employed in a coal mine may, for the purpose of an
  inspection and test for gas on behalf of the employees, be represented
  by any of the following persons identified by them for that purpose:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who holds a certificate as a mine
    examiner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two persons who belong to one of the following
    categories, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} two employees who are employed in the coal mine,
      at least one of whom holds a certificate as a mine examiner, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} two persons each of whom holds a certificate as
      a coal miner and has at least five years' experience of work
      underground in a mine from which coal is extracted, and at least
      one of whom holds a certificate as a mine examiner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least once every month, the employer shall permit
  the representatives referred to in subsection (1) to inspect every
  part of the coal mine, including the machinery and equipment therein,
  and to test for gas therein.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of an inspection or test on behalf
  of employees, the mine manager and the employees in the coal mine
  shall give every assistance necessary to the representatives referred
  to in subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The employer, the mine manager or an officer of the
  coal mine chosen by the employer or mine manager may accompany the
  representatives who carry out an inspection or test referred to in
  subsection (2).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The results of an inspection or test referred to in
  subsection (2) shall be reported in writing to the employer and to a
  safety officer at the district office.

### [Guards, Fences and Barricades]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931616 .Subheading}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Where a machine, piece of
equipment, tool, fixed ladder, fixed landing or lighting system is
likely to be hazardous to the safety or health of an employee, a guard
or fence shall be installed for the protection of the employees.

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Where live tests are
performed on electrical equipment in an area, a guard or fence shall be
installed for the protection of the employees, and no unauthorized
person shall enter the area unless the electrical equipment is locked
out.

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  flammable gases are discharged from a methane drainage system in an
  area, a barricade shall be installed in a location where the
  concentration of flammable gas does not exceed 2 per cent, to prevent
  access to the area by unauthorized persons.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Warning signs shall be posted on the barricade
  referred to in subsection (1), indicating that access by unauthorized
  persons is prohibited.

### [Mechanical Equipment and Electrical Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931622 .Subheading}

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** No electricity shall be
supplied or used in any part of a coal mine unless the electrical system
and electrical equipment and the use thereof have been approved by the
Coal Mining Safety Commission.

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mine manager shall prepare plans, including written instructions, for
  the installation, inspection, testing and maintenance of all
  mechanical equipment, electrical equipment, machinery and tools in use
  in the coal mine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The plans referred to in subsection (1) and any
  changes in those plans shall be certified by an engineer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The chief mechanic shall supervise the implementation
  of the mechanical aspects of the plans referred to in subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The chief electrician shall supervise the
  implementation of the electrical aspects of the plans referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The mechanical work and the electrical work required
  by the plans referred to in subsection (1) shall be carried out by
  mine mechanics and mine electricians, respectively.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every mine mechanic or mine electrician who carries
  out any work provided for in the plans referred to in subsection (1)
  shall make a written report of that work to the mine manager.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every report referred to in subsection (6) shall be
  read and countersigned by the chief mechanic and the chief electrician
  who shall take action immediately to correct any defect that is known
  by or reported to either of them and that is likely to affect the
  safety or health of employees.

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** At least once every
24 hours of operation of a coal mine, a mine mechanic shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspect the external parts of every conveyor and belt
  line in use in the coal mine; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a record of the inspection referred to in
  paragraph (a) in a book kept for that purpose.

### [Accumulations of Water or Concentrations of Gas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931635 .Subheading}

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any workings in a coal mine are advancing towards or are within 50 m
  of an area that contains or is likely to contain a dangerous
  accumulation of water or a dangerous concentration of flammable gas,
  the working face shall be not more than 5 m in width or 4 m in height.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where any workings in a coal mine are advancing
  towards or are within 50 m of an area that contains or is likely to
  contain a dangerous accumulation of water or a dangerous concentration
  of flammable gas, boreholes shall be bored for the purpose of locating
  the dangerous accumulation of water or dangerous concentration of gas,
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least one borehole shall be bored near the
    centre of the working face

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is not more than 2.5 m in
      width or height, to a depth of at least 5 m in advance of the
      working face, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is more than 2.5 m in
      width or height and not more than 5 m in width or 4 m in height,
      to a depth of at least 20 m in advance of the working face;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least two boreholes shall be bored near each
    side of the working face

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is not more than 2.5 m in
      width or height, to a depth of at least 3 m in advance of the
      working face, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is more than 2.5 m in
      width or height and not more than 5 m in width or 4 m in height,
      to a depth of at least 12 m in advance of the working face; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the seam of a working face is 3.5 m thick or
    more, a borehole shall be bored at an angle of not less than 20° up
    from the roof of the area where the working face is located

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is not more than 2.5 m in
      width or height, to a depth of at least 5 m in advance of the
      working face, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the working face is more than 2.5 m in
      width or height and not more than 5 m in width or 4 m in height,
      to a depth of at least 20 m in advance of the working face.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a boring referred to in subsection (2) makes
  contact with a dangerous accumulation of water or a dangerous
  concentration of flammable gas, a qualified person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} report the accumulation or concentration to the
    overman;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} while any work is being performed at the working
    face, monitor the accumulation or concentration using a locked-flame
    safety lamp and a methanometer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take appropriate measures to dissipate the
    accumulation or concentration.

### [Solid Measure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931652 .Subheading}

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** No coal mine shall be
worked below the sea bottom or below a body of water or material that
may flow, except under the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a solid barrier of unworked mineral of 50 m or more
  shall be left between the workings of a submarine lease and any other
  submarine lease;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), where a coal seam or
  stratified deposit is worked, there shall be a cover of 55 m or more
  of solid measure; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a passageway is driven, there shall be a cover
  of 30 m or more of solid measure.

### [Geological Faults]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931657 .Subheading}

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least one exploring drift shall be driven in advance of the working
  face of workings in a coal mine that are advancing towards an area

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is less than 300 m below the sea bottom or a
    body of water or material that may flow; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there is likely to be a geological fault at a
    distance of 50 m or less from the working face.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the throw or dislocation of a geological fault
  exceeds 10 m or where the faces of the fault plane are separated by
  material more than 600 mm thick, no coal face shall be worked within
  11 m of that fault.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a solid measure below a sea bottom is less than
  150 m, soundings shall be taken to a distance of at least 300 m in
  advance of any workings referred to in subsection (1) for the purpose
  of determining the depth of the water, and levels shall be taken at
  the working face at least once every three months for the purpose of
  determining the depth of the solid measure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The location of the soundings and the levels referred
  to in subsection (3) shall be marked on a plan of the underground
  workings that is kept readily available at the coal mine in respect of
  which it applies for examination by employees.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Underground Transportation and Hoisting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931673 .Part}

### [Underground Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931674 .Subheading}

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mine manager shall prepare in writing and implement safe procedures
  for the operation of cages, trips and mobile equipment underground.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The procedures referred to in subsection (1) shall
  specify the conditions under which persons may be transported.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall submit a copy of the procedures
  referred to in subsection (1) and any changes in those procedures to
  the Coal Mining Safety Commission for approval at least 30 days before
  the implementation of the procedures or changed procedures.

<!-- -->

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Transportation underground by trips
  or mobile equipment shall be subject to the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electric lamp with a red light shall be affixed
    to the approaching end of the trip or mobile equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} switches, derails, stop blocks, frogs and other
    track safety devices shall be installed in a manner that provides
    for the safe operation of trips on the track and for the safety of
    employees walking in the roadway;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a clearance of not less than 300 mm shall be
    provided on one side of the trip or mobile equipment and of not less
    than 600 mm on the other side;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a clearance of not less than 300 mm shall be
    provided

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} above the top of any load on the trip or mobile
      equipment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the trip or mobile equipment is covered,
      above the top of the cover;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} manholes shall be provided at intervals not
    exceeding 50 m in all roadways in which trips or mobile equipment
    are moved by mechanical means, other than by locomotives;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in all roadways in which trips or mobile equipment
    are moved by locomotives, the maximum interval between manholes
    shall be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 100 m in roadways that have no curve and where
      the gradient does not exceed 3.5 per cent, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 30 m in all other roadways and on curves; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the gradient of a roadway referred to in
    paragraph (e) exceeds 5.2 per cent, the employer shall set out in
    writing and keep at the entrance to the roadway procedures that
    specify the speed limits and safety control measures for trips and
    mobile equipment on that roadway.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a roadway that is used for transportation
  intersects a roadway that is used by employees, a fence shall be
  installed and a warning sign posted at the intersection.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every manhole referred to in subsection (1) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} securely supported;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 1.3 m in depth and 0.9 m in width; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 1.5 m in height or the height of the roadway,
    whichever is the higher.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every manhole shall be located on the side of the
  roadway that has the greater clearance.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every manhole shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept clear of material;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} kept free of obstacles to entry; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} clearly marked with an identifying number.

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Where more than
10 employees normally use a roadway, the roadway shall be not less than
1.5 m in height and, where a conveyor is in service in the roadway, have
a clearance of not less than 600 mm between one side of the conveyor and
the side of the roadway.

### [Communications on Roadways]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931703 .Subheading}

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** Where a trip that is used
by persons is operated in a roadway underground that is more than 30 m
in length, a means of signalling and communication between all regular
stopping places designated in the procedures referred to in
subsection 59(1) and the hoist operator or the locomotive operator shall
be provided.

### [Surface Hoist Operation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931705 .Subheading}

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  surface hoist shall be operated by a hoist operator who is a qualified
  person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than an authorized person, shall
  enter a hoist room.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A hoist operator shall be in attendance in the hoist
  room at all times when a person is in an underground portion of the
  coal mine that is normally entered by means of a hoist.

<!-- -->

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  hoist operator shall operate a surface hoist above ground that is used
  for transporting employees unless a physician has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} examined the hoist operator

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the three-month period prior to the operation
      of the hoist, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on the hoist operator's return to work after an
      illness or injury for which the hoist operator received medical
      attention; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} certified that the hoist operator is mentally and
    physically fit to operate a surface hoist.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep the physician's certificate
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b) at the coal mine in which the hoist
  operator who is the subject of the certificate works.

### [Surface Hoist Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931717 .Subheading}

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  surface hoist shall meet the following standards:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the engine shall be installed on a concrete or
    other rigid foundation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hoisting drums on which the rope is coiled shall be
    provided with flanges or horns and, where a hoisting drum is
    conical, with such other safety devices as will prevent the rope
    from slipping;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the hoist shall be equipped with a device that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} clearly indicates to the hoist operator the
      location of the cage or trip, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a trip may be used on more than one track,
      is of a type that indicates on which track the trip is being
      hoisted;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the hoist shall be equipped with a braking system
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} stops cages or trips within the deceleration
      rates set out in the procedures referred to in subsection 59(1)
      for both the descending and ascending modes,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is automatically applied in the event of a power
      failure, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} may be applied by the hoist operator in the
      event of an emergency;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the hoist shall be equipped with an overwind
    preventor and a speed controller that shut off the power and apply
    the braking system when the cage or trip travels

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} beyond the top or bottom of the run, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at a speed in excess of the maximum speed for
      the cage or trip set out in the procedures referred to in
      subsection 59(1);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} unless the overwind preventor and speed controller
    referred to in paragraph (e) are in full and fixed engagement with
    the hoisting engine, the overwind preventor and speed controller
    shall be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designed to be fully engaged automatically or by
      the hoist operator whenever persons are to be transported, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provided with an automatic device that indicates
      to the hoist operator and all persons responsible for the loading
      of the cage or trip that the overwind preventor and speed
      controller have been fully engaged; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} where used for transporting persons, the hoist
    shall be equipped with at least two brakes that meet the
    requirements of subparagraphs (d)(i) to (iii).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every device referred to in paragraph (1)(c) shall be
  tested for effective functioning after every adjustment to the length
  of the hoist rope.

### [Surface Hoist Tests and Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931737 .Subheading}

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  each shift, a hoist operator shall test the surface hoist, including
  the safety devices connected to it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where tests are performed in accordance with
  subsection (1), the hoist operator shall test

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the overwind preventor to verify that the cage or
    trip does not travel more than 0.6 m past the top or the bottom of
    the run; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the speed controller with the cage midway in the
    shaft or with the trip part of the way along the run.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), where a surface hoist has
  not been operated for more than four hours, before persons are
  transported on that hoist, a trial wind shall be carried out over the
  lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complete run of the cage or trip, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 400 m.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except in the case of an emergency, where a surface
  hoist has not been operated for any period because of an accident or
  defect, a full trial wind shall be carried out before lowering or
  hoisting is resumed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A qualified person shall test

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least once every 24 hours, the overwind
    preventor, speed controller and other safety devices of a surface
    hoist that is used only for transporting persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least once every week, the overwind preventor,
    speed controller and other safety devices of a surface hoist that is
    used for hoisting materials; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least once every month, the braking system of
    every surface hoist, including the emergency brake, overwind
    preventor, speed controller, other safety devices and the devices
    referred to in paragraph 65(1)(c) and subparagraph 65(1)(f)(ii).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The hoist operator or qualified person who carries
  out the tests referred to in subsections (1) to (5) shall make a
  record of those tests in a book kept for that purpose.

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** At least once every
24 hours, a mine mechanic shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspect the external parts of all surface hoists,
  including cages, mine cars, tackling equipment, head gear, ropes,
  sockets, rope paths and couplings, that are in use in the coal mine;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a record of the inspection referred to in
  paragraph (a) in a book kept for that purpose.

### [Hoist Rope Standards and Tests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931755 .Subheading}

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** Every hoist rope shall be
made of steel and have a factor of safety of not less than six.

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  hoist rope shall be placed in service unless it has been tested for
  breaking strength by a rope-testing laboratory.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep, for every hoist rope, every
  test certificate issued in respect of the rope by a rope-testing
  laboratory and a certificate from the manufacturer of the rope.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certificates referred to in subsection (2) shall
  contain the following information in respect of the hoist rope:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the manufacturer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's rope number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of manufacture;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the diameter, expressed in millimetres;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the mass per unit length, expressed in kilograms
    per metre;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of strands;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the class of core;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the percentage by mass of lubricant in the core;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the trade name of the interior rope lubricant;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number of wires in a strand;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the diameter of the wires, expressed in
    millimetres;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the breaking strength of steel from which the wire
    is made, expressed in kilopascals;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the results of the standard torsion test of the
    wires;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the actual breaking strength; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the extension of a test piece when tested to
    destruction.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For every hoist rope, the following information shall
  be recorded in a record book:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the company from which the rope was
    purchased;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of purchase;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's rope number;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the rope is installed in or
    removed from a particular location;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mass of the cage or mine car in respect of
    which the rope is installed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the maximum load for which the rope is designed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the maximum length of rope in service below the
    sheave;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum mass of rope in service below the
    sheave;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the factor of safety of the rope in each location
    where it is installed;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which rope samples are taken;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the dates and results of tests for breaking
    strength; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the date of permanent removal
    from service and the reasons therefor.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep the record book referred to
  in subsection (4) at the coal mine in which the rope is in service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period in which the rope is in service;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the five-year period immediately following
    the day on which the rope is permanently removed from service.

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** No spliced hoist rope,
other than an endless hoist rope, shall be used for transporting
persons.

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a cage or trip is at its lowest point on the run, the drum shall have
  remaining on it at least five turns of hoist rope.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The diameter of a hoist rope sheave shall be not less
  than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a rope 25 mm or more in diameter, 80 times the
    rope diameter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a rope less than 25 mm in diameter, 60 times
    the rope diameter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every hoist rope sheave shall be machined to fit the
  rope that is used on it.

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** No direct hoist rope
shall be reversed on the drum of a hoist.

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  hoist rope shall be kept lubricated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lubricant that is used on a hoist rope shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suitable for the conditions under which the rope is
    used; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used in accordance with the recommendations of the
    manufacturer of the rope.

<!-- -->

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A rope
  sample shall be taken at least once every month from every hoist rope
  in service in a coal mine, except where the rope is used in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an endless hoist rope system; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an auxiliary hoist that is used for localized
    movement of material and for which no rope sample is required by the
    plans referred to in subsection 52(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rope sample referred to in subsection (1) shall
  consist of at least 4 m of hoist rope cut from the end at which the
  hoist rope is attached to the cage or trip.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A test piece of the rope sample referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cut from the end of the rope sample that was
    attached to the cage or trip;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least 2 m in length; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} tested by a rope-testing laboratory.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The ends of the rope sample referred to in
  subsection (1) and of the test piece referred to in subsection (3)
  shall be bound with soft wire to prevent them from unravelling.

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Endless hoist ropes that
are more than 19 mm in diameter or that are used to transport persons on
grades exceeding 4 per cent shall be non-destructively tested at least
once every three months.

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** A hoist rope shall be
removed from service where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the extension of a test piece of the rope, when
  tested to destruction, has decreased to less than 60 per cent of its
  original extension;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of broken wires in any section of the rope
  that is equal to the length of one lay of the rope exceeds six;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} marked corrosion of the rope has occurred;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the core of the rope is ineffectually lubricated; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the breaking strength of the rope has decreased to 85
  per cent of the breaking strength for the rope set out in the
  manufacturer's certificate referred to in subsection 69(2).

### [Hoist Attachments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931823 .Subheading}

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Detaching hooks of a hoist shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} installed between the socket and safety chains on
    all cages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cleaned, refitted and tested by a qualified person
    at least once every month.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The detaching plate of a hoist shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} placed as high as is practicable above the decking
    level;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} capable of holding the mass of the cage if
    overwinding occurs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} tested once every month by a qualified person using
    calipers or gauges.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The clearance between the head sheave and the top of
  a cage that is stopped at the top of the run shall be not less than
  5 m.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The qualified person referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  or (2)(c) shall record the results of the tests in a hoisting
  machinery record book kept for that purpose.

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  hoist ropes, other than endless hoist ropes, shall be connected to

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Hoist ropes shall, at least once every month, be

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The drum end of a hoist rope shall be fastened to the

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No hoist rope referred to in subsection (1) shall be

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Ventilation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931995 .Part}

### [Airways]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931996 .Subheading}

**[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** Where reasonably
practicable, main intake and return airways shall be separated by not
less than 30 m of natural strata.

### [Ventilation Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-931998 .Subheading}

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  underground portions of a coal mine shall be ventilated in such a
  manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dangerous concentrations of flammable or noxious
    gases are prevented;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of respirable dust in the air
    does not exceed the levels referred to in section 111;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as far as is practicable, air is not recirculated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the oxygen in the general body of the air exceeds
    19 per cent; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of air circulating dilutes the
    concentration of carbon dioxide to less than 1.25 per cent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the concentration of flammable gas in the
  general body of the air in the intake airway to a working face,
  measured at a location not more than 100 m from that face, exceeds 0.5
  per cent, the employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} report that fact to a safety officer at the
    district office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stop all activities in the section until the
    concentration is reduced to less than 0.5 per cent.

### [Levels of Respirable Dust Underground]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932009 .Subheading}

- **[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section and section 112, the concentration of
  respirable dust in the air shall be measured from respirable dust
  samples taken by a MRDE Cassella 113A respirable dust sampling
  instrument or an instrument that has been calibrated to duplicate the
  results of the MRDE Cassella 113A.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of respirable dust in the air
  underground shall not exceed the following levels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a longwall section,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 3 mg/m^3^, measured at any location 70 m from the
      longwall face in the intake airway, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 6 mg/m^3^, measured at any location 70 m from
      the longwall face in the return airway; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a development roadway,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 5 mg/m^3^, where the coal extracted is greater
      than 70 per cent of the total material extracted, measured at any
      location at the face of the roadway, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 3 mg/m^3^, where the non-coal material extracted
      is greater than 30 per cent of the total material extracted,
      measured at any location at the face of the roadway.

- **[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  qualified person shall measure and record the concentration of
  respirable dust in the air at the locations referred to in
  subsection 111(2) at least once every month.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall, once every month, send a copy of

### [Fans --- Basic Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932032 .Subheading}

- **[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every main fan shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipped with devices that are capable of reversing
    the airflow controlled by the main fan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} located above ground in a fan house constructed of
    non-flammable materials; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} offset from all shafts or connections to the
    underground portions of the coal mine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every fan house referred to in paragraph (1)(b) shall

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No combustible material shall be stored in a fan
  house referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

### [Safe Operating Procedures for Booster Fans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932040 .Subheading}

- **[[115]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-115 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  booster fan shall be installed unless a ventilation survey is carried
  out to demonstrate the necessity for the booster fan, the expected
  performance range while in operation, the location of the booster fan
  and its effect on the remainder of the ventilation network.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Ninety days before the installation of a booster fan,
  the employer shall submit to the Coal Mining Safety Commission for
  approval a copy of the survey referred to in subsection (1) and a plan
  for the operation and maintenance of the booster fan.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every booster fan shall be located in such a manner
  that, if it stops, it will not restrict the free passage of air
  delivered by the main fan.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The booster fan system shall be designed so that
  routine inspection and maintenance may be carried out without stopping
  the booster fan or interfering with the provision of ventilation by
  the booster fan.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The power to a booster fan shall be automatically cut
  off if the percentage of flammable gases in the intake air passing
  through the fan drive room exceeds 0.5 per cent.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every booster fan shall have an automatic fire
  suppression system to suppress fires that may occur in the fan drive
  room or within 15 m on either side of the booster fan.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall provide a means to control
  recirculation of air through the booster fan.

### [Safe Operating Procedures for Fans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932049 .Subheading}

- **[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a main fan, booster fan or
  auxiliary fan stops for any reason, every person who is in an area
  that is affected by the stoppage shall be evacuated to a place that is
  ventilated in accordance with subsection 110(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A qualified person shall, before any other person
  enters an area that has been evacuated pursuant to subsection (1),
  inspect the area to determine whether it is ventilated in accordance
  with subsection 110(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a main fan or booster fan stops for any reason
  for more than 30 minutes, the mine manager shall, without delay,
  submit a written report of the circumstances under which it stopped to
  a safety officer at the district office.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an auxiliary fan stops, no person shall restart
  the auxiliary fan unless a qualified person has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspected the area underground that is serviced by
    the auxiliary fan and has tested for flammable gases; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} informed the person that it is safe to restart the
    auxiliary fan.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The mine manager shall prepare procedures to be
  followed in the event of the stoppage of an auxiliary fan and shall
  post a copy of those procedures in a conspicuous place at the surface
  of the coal mine.

**[[117]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-117 .sectionLabel}** All main fans and
booster fans and associated equipment shall be inspected by a qualified
person at intervals of not more than 30 minutes.

- **[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every qualified person who inspects
  a main fan, a booster fan or any associated equipment shall be
  authorized to do so and shall hold a certificate as a mine mechanic or
  a mine electrician.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every qualified person who inspects a main fan, a
  booster fan or any associated equipment shall record the results of
  the inspection in a book kept for that purpose.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Explosion and Fire Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932098 .Part}

### [Flammable Gas Tests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932099 .Subheading}

- **[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mine manager shall designate the location and frequency of tests
  underground for flammable gas in such a manner that dangerous
  concentrations of gas are detected in time for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} corrective measures to be taken; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where necessary, employees to be evacuated safely
    and expeditiously.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee is required under these Regulations
  to test for flammable gas, the employer shall provide the employee
  with a methanometer that is capable of testing for flammable gas in
  layers, cracks, crevices and other locations that are normally beyond
  arm's reach.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every employee who tests for flammable gas shall make
  a record of each test in a book kept for that purpose.

### [Flammable Gas Exceeding 0.5 Per Cent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932106 .Subheading}

- **[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where six consecutive measurements,
  taken in accordance with the procedures for ventilation surveys
  referred to in subsection 119(1) at a location underground where
  electrical machinery or electrical equipment is used, show a
  concentration of flammable gas that exceeds 0.5 per cent, a
  methanometer shall be installed at the location.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an electrical machine or electrical equipment
  is operated at a longwall face, a methanometer shall be installed at
  each end of the face and one of those methanometers shall be installed
  as near to the return brushing face as is practicable.

### [Flammable Gas Exceeding 0.8 Per Cent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932110 .Subheading}

**[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** Where the concentration
of flammable gas exceeds 0.8 per cent in a location underground where
electrical equipment or a diesel engine is operated or where a source of
ignition is present, a test for gas shall be made in that location,
using a methanometer, at least once every eight hours for as long as the
concentration of flammable gas exceeds 0.8 per cent.

### [Flammable Gas Exceeding 1.25 Per Cent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932112 .Subheading}

**[[129]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-129 .sectionLabel}** Where the concentration
of flammable gas at the return end of a longwall face may exceed 1.25
per cent, the employer shall provide, at appropriate locations, a
methanometer that automatically displays a visual indication of the
concentration and sounds an audible alarm to warn employees working at
or near the longwall face when the concentration exceeds 1.25 per cent.

**[[130]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-130 .sectionLabel}** Where the concentration
of flammable gas in the air in an underground portion of a coal mine
exceeds 1.25 per cent, the following procedures shall be followed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period in which the concentration exceeds
  1.25 per cent, the operation of all diesel engines and of all
  electrical equipment that is not intrinsically safe, other than
  electric safety lamps, shall be stopped in that portion of the coal
  mine; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employer shall, within seven days, make a written
  report of that concentration of flammable gas to a safety officer at
  the district office.

### [Flammable Gas Exceeding 2 Per Cent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932117 .Subheading}

- **[[131]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-131
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where the concentration of flammable
  gas in the air in an area underground exceeds 2 per cent, all persons
  shall forthwith be evacuated from the area to a place that is
  ventilated in accordance with subsection 110(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an area has been evacuated in accordance with
  subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a warning sign bearing the words "DO NOT ENTER" and
    "[ENTRÉE INTERDITE]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" shall be posted in a
    conspicuous place at the entrance to the area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no person, other than an authorized person, shall
    enter the evacuated area unless the authorized person has informed
    the person that it is safe to enter.

### [Methanometers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932123 .Subheading}

- **[[132]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-132
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Methanometers at the intake and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} measure the concentration of flammable gas in the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} display the concentration of flammable gas; and

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record referred to in paragraph (1)(c) shall be
  displayed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the concentration is recorded remotely, for a

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, for a period of at least two

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where remote monitoring of flammable gas is carried
  out, there shall be a qualified person at the remote monitoring

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The mine manager shall establish and keep at the
  remote monitoring station procedures to be followed by a qualified

### [Dust Reduction Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932134 .Subheading}

- **[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every area underground shall be kept
  free from accumulations of coal dust.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Dry areas underground in which coal dust is produced
  shall be systematically wetted down with water.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} To reduce coal dust underground,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where dry coal is cut by a coal-cutting machine, a
    jet of water shall be directed over the picks of the machine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mined coal shall be kept wet during handling.

### [Dusting Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932141 .Subheading}

- **[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every roadway underground shall be
  treated with incombustible dust in such a way that the dust on the
  floor, roof and sides of the roadway contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the concentration of flammable gas in the air
    in the roadway does not exceed 1 per cent, not less than 75 per cent
    by weight of incombustible dust; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the concentration of flammable gas in the air
    in the roadway exceeds 1 per cent, not less than 80 per cent by
    weight of incombustible dust.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the incombustible dust
  referred to in subsection (1) shall contain not less than 70 per cent
  by weight of fine material that is capable, when dry, of passing
  through a sieve of 200 mesh.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a larger percentage of incombustible dust than
  the percentage referred to in subsection (1) is used, the percentage
  of fine material referred to in subsection (2) may be reduced in
  proportion to the increase in the amount of incombustible dust, but in
  no case shall it be less than 25 per cent.

<!-- -->

- **[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least once every month, samples of dust shall be taken from the floor,
  roof and sides along the length of each roadway underground and shall
  be analysed to determine the percentage of combustible material
  therein.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall, in respect of the analysis
  referred to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep a record of the analysis; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a written report of the results of the
    analysis to a safety officer at the district office before the
    15th day of the month following the analysis.

**[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136 .sectionLabel}** Not less than 20 bags
of incombustible dust, each weighing not less than 25 kg, shall be
stored for emergency use within 150 m of each working face in the intake
airway and within 40 m of each working face in the return airway.

### [Explosion Protection Barriers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932154 .Subheading}

- **[[137]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-137 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  each intake airway leading to a working face, a stone-dust barrier or
  a water barrier shall be placed within 300 m of the working face.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A stone-dust barrier referred to in subsection (1)
  shall hold not less than 100 kg of stone dust per square metre of area
  of the roadway that it serves.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A water barrier referred to in subsection (1) shall
  contain not less than 200 L of water per square metre of area of the
  roadway that it serves.

### [Sources of Ignition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932159 .Subheading}

**[[138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-138 .sectionLabel}** All electrical
equipment that is used underground shall be intrinsically safe or shall
be flameproof.

- **[[139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-139
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee uses a
  locked-flame safety lamp, the employee shall inspect the lamp to
  determine that it is securely locked and is safe for use underground

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the employee takes the lamp underground; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at regular intervals during the employee's shift
    underground.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employee who holds a certificate as a mine
  examiner shall inspect every locked-flame safety lamp before it is
  taken underground to determine that it is securely locked and is safe
  for use underground.

**[[140]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-140 .sectionLabel}** Where a locked-flame
safety lamp is damaged underground, the flame shall be extinguished and
the lamp shall not be relit underground.

- **[[141]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-141 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  welding, soldering, brazing or other open-flame or heating work shall
  be carried out underground or at a location above ground that is
  within 30 m of a shaft or airway unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the employer has given not less than 24 hours
    notice of the work to a safety officer at the district office;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the air in the area where the work is carried out
    is tested before and during the work and the concentration of
    flammable gas in the air is found to be not more than 0.25 per cent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the area that is within 8 m of the location where
    the work is carried out is cleared of all combustible substances and
    materials that are not necessary for the operation of the coal mine
    and is thoroughly wetted down;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} guards are installed to prevent sparks from
    escaping from the area where the work is carried out;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} two portable fire extinguishers that have a rating
    of 10A:60B:C and that meet the standards set out in National
    Standard of Canada standard CAN4-S508-M83, Rating and Fire Testing
    of Fire Extinguishers, dated June 1983, are readily available at the
    location where the work is carried out; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the work is under the constant supervision of a
    qualified person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where welding, soldering, brazing or other open-flame
  or heating work is carried out underground, an employee who holds a
  certificate as a mine examiner shall remain in attendance at the
  location where the work was carried out for a period of 24 hours after
  the work ceases.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All conveyor belts underground shall be so
  constructed that they are fire-retardant and anti-static.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where practicable, lubricants and hydraulic fluids
  that are used in machinery underground shall be fire-retardant.

### [Alcohol and Drugs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932178 .Subheading}

- **[[142]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-142
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall go or attempt to go underground in a coal mine if the person has
  in the person's possession

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alcohol or drugs; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a match, a cigarette lighter, a cigarette, a pipe,
    a cigar, tobacco or any other article for use in smoking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), paragraph (1)(a) does not
  apply to an employee who is in possession of a drug that has been
  prescribed for the employee by a physician or any other drug the sale
  of which is authorized under the laws of Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall enter a coal mine if the person is
  under the influence of alcohol or drugs to such an extent as to
  constitute a danger to any employee.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warning sign of durable construction shall be
  posted in a conspicuous place at the lamp house of every coal mine and
  shall bear the following, in white letters not less that 65 mm high on
  a red background:

  "No alcohol, drugs, smoking, matches, lighters or other articles for
  use in smoking" and "[Défense de fumer ou d'avoir en sa possession des
  spiritueux, une drogue, une allumette, un briquet ou un autre article
  pour fumer]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"

  "Random searches of persons going underground may be carried out for
  alcohol, drugs and articles for use in smoking." and "[Les personnes
  qui pénètrent dans la partie souterraine peuvent faire l'objet de
  fouilles au hasard pour les spiritueux, les drogues et les articles
  pour fumer.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"

### [Random Searches]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932188 .Subheading}

- **[[143]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-143 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an employee referred to in
    paragraph (1)(c), after the employee leaves the wash house and
    before the employee goes underground, or at any time when the
    employee is underground.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The mine manager or the underground manager and at

  referred to in subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is selected as the subject of a search

  search himself in the presence of the witnesses referred to in
  subsection (3) and two witnesses of the person's own choosing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who has entered a coal mine is found
  to have in the person's possession any thing referred to in
  subsection 142(1), the person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be instructed to leave the coal mine as soon as
    this may be done without jeopardizing the safety of others; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately follow the instruction referred to in
    paragraph (a).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A complete record of the finding of any thing
  referred to in subsection 142(1) in the possession of any person shall

    superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in paragraph (1)(b) shall be
  countersigned by the mine rescue station superintendent.

### [Stoppings and Barricades]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932263 .Subheading}

- **[[155]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-155
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before stoppings or barricades are
  installed for fire protection in an area of a coal mine, all persons,
  other than those persons required to install the stoppings or
  barricades, shall be evacuated from the area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall enter an area referred to in
  subsection (1) for a period of 24 hours after stoppings or barricades
  have been installed, except for the purpose of saving life, preventing
  injury or relieving human suffering.

**[[156]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-156 .sectionLabel}** Where coal is left
unmined as a barrier against fire or flooding or for any other safety
purpose, no person shall remove the coal.

**[[157]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-157 .sectionLabel}** All openings to any
underground area that is not being worked or developed shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} stopped off; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} posted with a warning sign that states "DO NOT ENTER"
  and "[ENTRÉE INTERDITE]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Plans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932271 .Part}

### [Pre-development Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932272 .Subheading}

- **[[158]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-158 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer shall submit to the Coal Mining Safety Commission for
  approval a notice in writing of the employer's intention to carry out
  any of the following activities in a coal mine, at least 90 days
  before beginning any of those activities, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the development, construction or alteration of a
    portion of a coal mine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the introduction or alteration of mining methods or
    technology;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of new methods of construction, operation
    or installation of equipment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the introduction or alteration of any main fan,
    booster fan, machine, equipment or instrument;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the introduction or alteration of transportation
    equipment or a transportation system for employees or materials;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the construction underground of a bulkhead, dam or
    permanent stopping;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the construction of a tailing dam, a water storage
    reservoir or a structure above ground for the storage of explosives;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the introduction of any electrical equipment or
    electrical system that has not been previously approved, or the
    alteration of any electrical equipment or electrical system; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bulk transportation of explosives.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall
  contain pertinent detailed drawings, plans and specifications.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the drawings, plans and specifications referred
  to in subsection (2) are in respect of a submarine area, they shall
  indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mining method to be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the area from which coal is to be extracted;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sections of the strata between the sea bottom and
    the workings;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location of any faults or other geological
    disturbances; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the calculated strain on the sea bottom.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), a submarine area
  includes an area below any body of water or material that may flow.

### [Strata Control Systems]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932292 .Subheading}

- **[[159]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-159 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  employer shall prepare a plan of strata control for any proposed
  underground workings of a coal mine that is designed to prevent the
  collapse of the roof and sides of those workings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the plan referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be posted at every meeting station in such a manner that it is
  readily available for examination by employees.

### [Mine Survey Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932296 .Subheading}

- **[[160]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-160 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mine surveyor shall prepare a plan of the coal mine using a scale of
  not less than 1:2,500.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The plan referred to in subsection (1) shall identify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the coal mine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of all surveys of that coal mine carried
    out by a mine surveyor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the coal mine;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all working faces, shafts, rooms, pillars, longwall
    sections, meeting stations and passageways;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the position, direction and extent of every fault
    and the displacement of the fault;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the direction and rate of dip of the coal seam;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the depth of every shaft;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the elevation of the floor of the coal seams and
    roadways at intervals of 30 m;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} vertical sections of each coal seam;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the airways and direction of air flow;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} roadways;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} barriers between adjacent coal mines in the same
    seam; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} all stoppings.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A mine surveyor shall prepare additional plans of the
  coal mine to show details of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ventilation system, including airways and
    airway crossings, doors, air ducts, brattice sheets, stoppings,
    methane drainage pipes and holes, auxiliary fans and booster fans;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} compressed air lines;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the water circuit, including the position of
    hydrants, stop valves and tanks and the diameter of the pipes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the electrical equipment and control devices,
    including cables, substations, pump rooms, telephones and
    environmental monitoring equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} roadways and manholes; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the transportation system for employees and
    materials.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The plan referred to in subsection (1) and the
  additional plans referred to in subsection (3) shall be brought up to
  date as of January 1, April 1, July 1 and October 1 of every year and
  a copy of the up-to-date plans shall be submitted to a safety officer
  at the district office not later than on the 20th day of the month for
  which they are brought up to date.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the plans and the additional plans, brought
  up to date in accordance with subsection (4), shall be posted at the
  lamp house.

### [Notices of Closure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932322 .Subheading}

- **[[161]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-161 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least 90 days before closing a coal mine or any portion of a coal
  mine, the employer shall submit a notice of the employer's intention
  by registered mail to the Coal Mining Safety Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  accompanied by a plan of the coal mine prepared in accordance with
  subsection 160(1) and revised to show the boundaries of the mine and
  the limits of all workings as they exist on the day the notice is
  mailed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the time when a coal mine or a portion of a coal
  mine is closed, the employer shall submit to the Coal Mining Safety
  Commission, by registered mail, a final plan of the coal mine or
  portion of the coal mine as it exists on the day of closure.

## [PART VII]{.HLabel1}[Hazardous Occurrences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932327 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1282810 .Subheading}

**[[161.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-161.1 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not
apply in respect of occurrences of harassment and violence in the work
place.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2020-130, s. 48](#1251046-1282813){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1251046-1282813.html"
  role="button"}
:::

### [Reports by Employees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932328 .Subheading}

**[[162]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-162 .sectionLabel}** Where an employee
becomes aware of an accident or other occurrence arising in the course
of or in connection with the employee's work that has caused or is
likely to cause injury to that employee or to any other person, the
employee shall, without delay, report the accident or other occurrence
to the employer, orally or in writing.

### [Investigations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932330 .Subheading}

**[[163]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-163 .sectionLabel}** Where an employer
becomes aware of an accident, occupational disease or other hazardous
occurrence affecting any of the employees in the course of employment,
the employer shall, without delay,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
  investigation of the hazardous occurrence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the safety and health committee or the safety
  and health representative, if any, of the hazardous occurrence and of
  the name of the person appointed to investigate it; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take necessary measures to prevent a recurrence of
  the hazardous occurrence.

### [Reports by Employer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-932335 .Subheading}

- **[[164]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-164
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a hazardous occurrence
  referred to in section 163 results in a death, a fire or an explosion,
  the employer shall forthwith report the hazardous occurrence to a
  safety officer at the district office by telephone or telex.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall submit a written report to a
  safety officer at the district office of any hazardous occurrence
  referred to in section 163 that had one of the following results, as
  soon as possible but not later than 48 hours after becoming aware of
  that result, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an injury for which a person is hospitalized;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a death;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fire or an explosion; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} damage to a hoist, ventilation system or other
    equipment that is essential to the safety or health of employees.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall, as soon as the investigation
  referred to in paragraph 163(a) is completed, submit a written report
  of the findings of the investigation to a safety officer at the
  district office.

**[[165]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-165 .sectionLabel}** Where the employer
becomes aware of an emergency or an unusual dangerous condition in the
mine, the employer shall, without delay, report the emergency or
condition and any action the employer has taken or proposes to take in
respect thereof to a safety officer at the district office by telephone.

**[[166]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-166 .sectionLabel}** The employer shall, not
later than on the 15th day of every month, submit to a safety officer at
the district office a written report of all accidents, occupational
diseases and other hazardous occurrences referred to in section 163 that
occurred in the previous month.

## [PART VIII]{.HLabel1}[Entering a Closed Mine]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932346 .Part}

**[[167]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-167 .sectionLabel}** If an employer proposes
to enter a mine that has been closed but does not intend to
significantly disturb the ground, the employer shall, in accordance with
paragraph 125(1)(v) of the Act, adopt and implement a safety code that
contains information relevant to the health and safety of employees
entering the mine and that is approved by the Senior Director,
Occupational Health and Safety and Injury Compensation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-138, s. 1
:::

**[[168]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-168 .sectionLabel}** The Senior Director,
Occupational Health and Safety and Injury Compensation, shall approve
the safety code if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is filed at least 30 days before the proposed day
  of entry into the mine;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it contains provisions having substantially the same
  purpose and effect as these Regulations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it includes

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and geographical location of the proposed
    entry,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the work to be done and its
    duration,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of safety and control measures that
    will be used when entering the mine, including a description of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} how

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} the mine will be ventilated,

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} ground control will be managed,

      - [(III)]{.lawlabel} electrical and mechanical safety will be
        ensured, and

      - [(IV)]{.lawlabel} water will be handled,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} mine rescue resources and communication,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} air quality and air quantity testing in the
      ventilated part, and

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the type of personal protective equipment that
      will be used,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a list of equipment to be used,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a description of the health and safety
    responsibilities of the employer and the employees,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a list of individuals who will be entering the
    mine, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the mine will be sealed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-138, s. 1
:::

**[[169]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-169 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 to 166 do
not apply to employment in a mine that is entered after having been
closed if the employer has adopted and implemented the safety code
referred to in section 167 and as long as the employer does not
significantly disturb the ground.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-138, s. 1
