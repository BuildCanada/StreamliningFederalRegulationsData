## Foreign Organizations Remission Order, 1983 {#foreign-organizations-remission-order-1983 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/84-50
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1984-04-04

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX and Under Any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act on Goods Imported
for Meetings in Canada of Foreign Organizations

P.C. 1984-867 1984-03-15
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to make the annexed remission order, is pleased
hereby, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue and
the Treasury Board and pursuant to section 17 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to revoke the Foreign Organizations
Remission Order, C.R.C., c. 766, and to make the annexed Order
respecting the remission of customs duty, excise tax and sales tax on
goods imported for meetings in Canada of foreign organizations, in
substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639474 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Foreign Organizations Remission Order,
1983](/eng/regulations/SI-84-50).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639476 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[advertising matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertising matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information
    bulletins, booklets, programs and memoranda relating to a meeting or
    convention or to products displayed at a meeting or convention;
    ([imprimés publicitaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[carnet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carnet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an A.T.A. ([Admission
    Temporaire]{.otherLang lang="fr"} --- Temporary Admission) Carnet as
    set out in the Annex to the Customs Convention on the A.T.A. Carnet
    for the Temporary Admission of Goods; ([carnet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[convention materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[convention materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} banners, flags, papers, shields, stand
      decorations, backdrops and other decorations,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stationery, paper clips, pens, pencils and
      similar items but does not include office machines,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identification badges, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} printing plates, including rolls and cylinders,
      matrices, moulds, exposed positive or negative films and other
      similar goods necessary to produce advertising matter relating to
      a meeting or convention; ([articles de congrès]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[foreign organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    whose head office is outside Canada or an association that is not
    incorporated and none of whose members are residents of Canada but
    does not include a Canadian branch of any such association;
    ([organisation étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official paraphernalia]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official paraphernalia]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means mugs,
    jewellery, pens, scarves, t-shirts, badges and similar items, each
    bearing the official registered symbol of a foreign organization;
    ([accessoires officiels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[souvenirs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[souvenirs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means lapel buttons, billfolds,
    keycases, pens, pencils, corsages, t-shirts, scarves and similar
    items. ([souvenirs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639491 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies only to
meetings or conventions held in Canada by foreign organizations.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639493 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7,
remission is hereby granted of all taxes paid or payable under Division
III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on or in respect of the following goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any convention materials imported for use in the
  conduct of a meeting or convention; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any advertising matter or souvenirs, the individual
  value of which does not exceed \$25, imported for free distribution to
  persons attending a meeting or convention.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-13, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, remission is hereby granted of all taxes paid or payable under
Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on or in respect of the following goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any projector, camera, sound and lighting equipment,
  audio-visual equipment, typewriter or other office machine that is the
  property of a foreign organization and is imported for use in the
  conduct of a meeting or convention, on condition that such goods are
  exported forthwith on conclusion of the meeting or convention; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any souvenirs, the individual value of which exceeds
  \$25, that are imported for free distribution to persons attending a
  meeting or convention, on condition that all such souvenirs are
  exported forthwith on conclusion of the meeting or convention.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-13, s. 3
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, remission of all taxes paid or payable under Division III of Part IX
and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on
official paraphernalia imported by a foreign organization for sale at a
meeting or convention and sold at the meeting or convention is hereby
granted in an amount equivalent to the percentage of non-residents
officially in attendance at the meeting or convention on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the foreign organization maintains records of
  attendance at and registration for the meeting or convention and
  produces the records for inspection at any reasonable time on request
  of an officer employed in the administration of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any official paraphernalia imported by the foreign
  organization for sale at the meeting or convention that is not sold is
  exported forthwith on conclusion of the meeting or convention; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the meeting or convention is not open to the Canadian
  public at large.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-13, s. 4
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
sections 4 to 6 on condition that an application therefor is submitted
to the Minister of National Revenue within two years of the date of
importation of the goods for which remission is claimed.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  goods referred to in section 5 or 6 are imported into Canada for a
  temporary period and are not accompanied by a valid carnet, remission
  is hereby granted in respect of the goods on the condition that the
  importer of those goods, on request of the chief officer of customs,
  posts security in an amount not exceeding the aggregate of the taxes
  imposed under Division III of Part IX and under any other Part of the
  [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) that would be payable if this Order
  did not apply.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Security given under subsection (1) shall be
  deposited with the chief officer of customs and shall be in the form
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cash;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transferable bond issued by the Government of
    Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a bond issued by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is licensed or otherwise
      authorized under the laws of Canada or of a province to carry on
      the fidelity or surety class of insurance business and that is
      recommended to the Treasury Board by the Office of the
      Superintendent of Financial Institutions as an entity whose bonds
      may be accepted by the Government of Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association
      referred to in section 4 of the Canadian Payments Association Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits insured by
      the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or by the [Régie de
      l'assurance-dépôts du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"} to the
      maximums permitted by the statutes under which those institutions
      are established,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a credit union, as defined in subsection 137(6)
      of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits from the
      public, if repayment of the deposits is guaranteed by Her Majesty
      in right of a province.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Security given pursuant to subsection (1) in respect
  of goods referred to in section 5 or 6 shall be refunded or cancelled
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are destroyed, where the destruction is
    certified by a customs officer, police officer or fire marshall;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are exported on conclusion of the meeting
    or convention in respect of which they were imported, where the
    exportation is certified by a customs officer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all taxes payable under Division III of Part IX and
    under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) are
    paid.

- SI/96-45, s. 1
- SI/98-13, s. 5
