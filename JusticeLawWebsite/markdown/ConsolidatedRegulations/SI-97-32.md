A Proclamation

Whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 1995-138 of January 31, 1995, the
Governor in Council declared that, in accordance with Article XXIII of
the Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of the
Philippines, signed at Winnipeg on September 9, 1994, the Agreement
shall enter into force on the first day of the fourth month following
the month in which each Party shall have received written notification
from the other Party that it has complied with all statutory
requirements for the entry into force of this Agreement;

Whereas the Order was laid before Parliament on February 15, 1995;

Whereas, before the twentieth sitting day after the Order had been laid
before Parliament, no motion for the consideration of either House to
the effect that the Order be revoked was filed with the Speaker of the
appropriate House;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 42(2) of the [Old Age Security
Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the Order came into force on the thirtieth sitting
day after it had been laid before Parliament, being April 5, 1995;

Whereas instruments of ratification were exchanged on November 23, 1996;

And whereas the Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the
fourth month following the month in which the instruments of
ratification were exchanged, being March 1, 1997;

And whereas, by Order in Council P.C. 1997-193 of February 11, 1997, the
Governor in Council directed that a proclamation do issue giving notice
that the Agreement on Social Security between Canada and the Republic of
the Philippines is in force as of March 1, 1997;

Now Know You that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada, do by this Our Proclamation give notice that the Agreement on
Social Security between Canada and the Republic of the Philippines,
signed at Winnipeg on September 9, 1994, a copy of which is annexed
hereto, is in force as of March 1, 1997.

Of All Which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these Presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In Testimony Whereof, We have caused these Our Letters to be made Patent
and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness: Our Right
Trusty and Well-beloved Roméo A. LeBlanc, a Member of Our Privy Council
for Canada, Chancellor and Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada,
Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of Military Merit, Governor
General and Commander-in-Chief of Canada.

At Our Government House, in Our City of Ottawa, this twenty-fourth day
of February in the year of Our Lord one thousand nine hundred and
ninety-seven and in the forty-sixth year of Our Reign.

By Command,

::: FlushLeft
KEVIN G. LYNCH
:::

::: FlushLeft
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*

#### [ARTICLE III]{.HLabel2}[**Persons to Whom the Agreement Applies**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-iiipersons-to-whom-the-agreement-applies .SchedHeadL2}

This Agreement shall apply to any person who is or who has been subject
to the legislation of Canada or the Republic of the Philippines, and to
the dependants and survivors of such a person within the meaning of the
applicable legislation of either Party.

#### [ARTICLE IV]{.HLabel2}[**Equality of Treatment**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-ivequality-of-treatment .SchedHeadL2}

Any person who is or who has been subject to the legislation of a Party,
and the dependants and survivors of such a person, shall be subject to
the obligations of the legislation of the other Party and shall be
eligible for the benefits of that legislation under the same conditions
as nationals of the latter Party.

##### [ARTICLE IX]{.HLabel3}[**Periods under the Legislation of a Third State**]{.HTitleText3} {#article-ixperiods-under-the-legislation-of-a-third-state .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is not entitled to the payment of a benefit on the basis of
the creditable periods under the legislation of the Parties, totalized
as provided in Article VIII, the entitlement of that person to the
payment of that benefit shall be determined by totalizing these periods
and creditable periods under the legislation of a third State with which
both Parties are bound by social security instruments which provide for
totalizing periods.

##### [ARTICLE X]{.HLabel3}[**Minimum Period Required for Totalization**]{.HTitleText3} {#article-xminimum-period-required-for-totalization .SchedHeadL3}

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Agreement, if the total
duration of the creditable periods accumulated by a person under the
legislation of a Party is less than one year, the competent institution
of that Party shall not be required to award benefits to that person in
respect of those periods by virtue of this Agreement.

#### [**Chapter 3**]{.HLabel2}[**Benefits Under the Legislation of the Republic of the Philippines**]{.HTitleText2} {#chapter-3benefits-under-the-legislation-of-the-republic-of-the-philippines .SchedHeadL2}

##### [ARTICLE XIII]{.HLabel3}[**Calculating the Amount of Benefit Payable**]{.HTitleText3} {#article-xiiicalculating-the-amount-of-benefit-payable .SchedHeadL3}

If a person is not entitled to the payment of a benefit solely on the
basis of the creditable periods under the legislation of the Republic of
the Philippines, but is entitled to the payment of that benefit through
the application of the totalizing provisions of Chapter 1, the competent
institution of the Republic of the Philippines shall calculate the
amount of benefit payable to that person in the following manner:

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  it shall first determine the amount of the theoretical benefit which
  would be payable under the legislation of the Republic of the
  Philippines solely on the basis of the minimum creditable periods
  required under that legislation;
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  it shall then multiply the theoretical benefit by the ratio that the
  creditable periods actually completed under the legislation of the
  Republic of the Philippines represent in relation to the minimum
  creditable periods required under that legislation.
  :::
  :::::

  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The competent authorities of the Parties shall establish, by means of
  an administrative arrangement, the measures necessary for the
  application of this Agreement.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The liaison agencies of the Parties shall be designated in that
  arrangement.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XV]{.HLabel2}[**Exchange of Information and Mutual Assistance**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xvexchange-of-information-and-mutual-assistance .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The competent authorities and institutions responsible for the
  application of this Agreement:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText1-5
    shall, to the extent permitted by the legislation which they
    administer, communicate to each other any information necessary for
    the application of this Agreement;
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText1-5
    shall lend their good offices and furnish assistance to one another
    with regard to the determination or payment of any benefit under
    this Agreement, or the legislation to which this Agreement applies,
    as if the matter involved the application of their own legislation;
    and
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(c)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText1-5
    shall communicate to each other, as soon as possible, all
    information about the measures taken by them for the application of
    this Agreement or about changes in their respective legislation
    insofar as these changes affect the application of this Agreement.
    :::
    :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The assistance referred to in subparagraph 1(b) shall be provided free
  of charge, subject to any provision contained in an administrative
  arrangement concluded pursuant to Article XIV for the reimbursement of
  certain types of expenses.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Unless disclosure is required under the laws of a Party, any
  information about a person which is transmitted in accordance with
  this Agreement to that Party by the other Party is confidential and
  shall be used only for purposes of implementing this Agreement and the
  legislation to which this Agreement applies.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XVI]{.HLabel2}[**Exemption or Reduction of Taxes, Dues, Fees or Charges**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviexemption-or-reduction-of-taxes-dues-fees-or-charges .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Any exemption from or reduction of taxes, legal dues, consular fees or
  administrative charges for which provision is made in the legislation
  of a Party in connection with the issuing of any certificate or
  document required to be produced for the application of that
  legislation shall be extended to certificates or documents required to
  be produced for the application of the legislation of the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Any documents of an official nature required to be produced for the
  application of this Agreement shall be exempt from any authentication
  by diplomatic or consular authorities and similar formality.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XVII]{.HLabel2}[**Language of Communication**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviilanguage-of-communication .SchedHeadL2}

For the application of this Agreement, the competent authorities and
institutions of the Parties may communicate directly with one another in
any official language of either Party.

#### [ARTICLE XVIII]{.HLabel2}[**Submitting Claims, Notices or Appeals**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xviiisubmitting-claims-notices-or-appeals .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Any claim, notice or appeal concerning the determination or payment of
  a benefit under the legislation of a Party which should, for the
  purposes of that legislation, have been presented within a prescribed
  period to a competent authority or institution of that Party, but
  which is presented within the same period to an authority or
  institution of the other Party, shall be treated as if it had been
  presented to the competent authority or institution of the first
  Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Subject to the second sentence of this paragraph, a claim for benefit
  under the legislation of a Party made after the date of entry into
  force of this Agreement shall be deemed to be a claim for the
  corresponding benefit under the legislation of the other Party,
  provided that the applicant:
  :::
  :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(a)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText1-5
    requests that it be considered an application under the legislation
    of the other Party, or
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    [(b)]{.lawlabel} 
    :::

    ::: listItemText1-5
    provides information at the time of application indicating that
    creditable periods have been completed under the legislation of the
    other Party.
    :::
    :::::

  - ::::: listItemBlock1-5
    ::: listItemLabel
    :::

    ::: listItemText0
    The foregoing sentence, however, shall not apply if the applicant
    requests that his or her claim to the benefit of the other Party be
    delayed.
    :::
    :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  In any case to which paragraph 1 or 2 applies, the authority or
  institution to which the claim, notice or appeal has been submitted
  shall transmit it without delay to the authority or institution of the
  other Party.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XX]{.HLabel2}[**Resolution of Difficulties**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xxresolution-of-difficulties .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The competent authorities of the Parties shall resolve, to the extent
  possible, any difficulties which arise in interpreting or applying
  this Agreement according to its spirit and fundamental principles.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The Parties shall consult promptly at the request of either Party
  concerning matters which have not been resolved by the competent
  authorities in accordance with paragraph 1.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Any dispute between the Parties concerning the interpretation of this
  Agreement which has not been resolved or settled by consultation in
  accordance with paragraph 1 or 2 shall, at the request of either
  Party, be submitted to arbitration by an arbitral tribunal.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [4]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  Unless the Parties mutually determine otherwise, the arbitral tribunal
  shall consist of three arbitrators, of whom each Party shall appoint
  one and the two arbitrators so appointed shall appoint a third who
  shall act as president; provided that if the two arbitrators fail to
  agree, the President of the International Court of Justice shall be
  requested to appoint the president.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [5]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The arbitral tribunal shall determine its own procedures.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [6]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  The decision of the arbitral tribunal shall be final and binding.
  :::
  :::::

#### [ARTICLE XXIII]{.HLabel2}[**Entry into Force and Termination**]{.HTitleText2} {#article-xxiiientry-into-force-and-termination .SchedHeadL2}

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [1]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  This Agreement shall enter into force on the first day of the fourth
  month following the month in which each Party shall have received from
  the other Party written notification that it has complied with all
  statutory requirements for the entry into force of this Agreement.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [2]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  This Agreement shall remain in force without any limitation on its
  duration. It may be denounced at any time by either Party giving 12
  months' notice in writing to the other Party.
  :::
  :::::

- ::::: listItemBlock0
  ::: listItemLabel
  [3]{.lawlabel} 
  :::

  ::: listItemText1-5
  In the event of the termination of this Agreement, any right acquired
  by a person in accordance with its provisions shall be maintained and
  negotiations shall take place for the settlement of any rights then in
  course of acquisition by virtue of those provisions.
