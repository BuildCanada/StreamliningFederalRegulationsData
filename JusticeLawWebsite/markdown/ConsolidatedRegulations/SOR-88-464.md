## Social Insurance Number Replacement Card Fees Order, 1988 {#social-insurance-number-replacement-card-fees-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-464
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-08-25

Order Prescribing the Fee or Charge To Be Paid by the Holder of a Social
Insurance Number Card for the Replacement of that Card
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Employment and Immigration, pursuant to Order in Council
P.C. 1988-1734 of August 25, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHCCA}, herey revokes the Social
Insurance Number Replacement Card Fee or Charge Order, made on June 27,
1985[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDCCA}, and
makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee or charge to be paid by the
holder of a Social Insurance Number Card for the replacement of that
Card](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-464), in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913509 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Social Insurance Number Replacement Card Fees Order,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-464).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913511 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee or charge to be
paid by the holder of a Social Insurance Number Card for the replacement
of that Card is \$10 per Card, except where the replacement is made as a
result of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a legal change in the name of the holder;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an error made by an official of the Government of
  Canada in the exercise of the official's duties; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a natural disaster.
