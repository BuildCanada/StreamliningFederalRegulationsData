## Letter Definition Regulations {#letter-definition-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-481
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 1983-05-26

Regulations Prescribing a Definition for the Word Letter

P.C. 1983-1566 1983-05-26
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of proposed [Regulations prescribing a definition for the
word letter](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-481), substantially in the form of
the annexed regulations was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on February 12, 1983 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Honourable André Ouellet with respect
thereto.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Honourable André Ouellet, pursuant to section 17
of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed [Regulations prescribing a definition for the word
letter](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-481), made by the Canada Post
Corporation on April 21, 1983.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878391 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Letter Definition
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-481).

## [Definition of Letter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878393 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10) and any regulation made
thereunder,

[[letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more messages or
    information in any form, the total mass of which, if any, does not
    exceed 500 g, whether or not enclosed in an envelope, that is
    intended for collection or for transmission or delivery to any
    addressee as one item, but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an item carried incidentally and delivered to the
      addressee thereof by a sender who is a friend of the addressee;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an item of an urgent nature carried by an
      employee where the average salary cost to the employer for
      delivery of that item is at least equal to an amount that is three
      times the regular rate of postage payable for delivery in Canada
      for a similar item weighing 50 g;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an item having no further address than
      "householder", "boxholder", "occupant", "resident", or other
      similar expression;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an item relating to the official functions of a
      diplomatic or consular mission or covered by the privileges and
      immunities provisions of international conventions to which Canada
      is a party;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a cheque, money order or Receiver General payment
      instrument;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a tape, microfilm, disc or similar mass storage
      device, on which a message or information is recorded by
      electronic or optical means;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a newspaper, magazine, book, catalogue, blank
      form, manuscript or musical score;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an invoice or other document relating to

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an item in paragraph (g),

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a specimen for medical or scientific analysis,
        or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} goods

      when delivered therewith by the individual delivering such item,
      specimen or goods;

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an invoice or other document relating to the
        supply of goods or services that is delivered by

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the supplier to the recipient at the supplier's
        normal business address,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the recipient at

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution indicated by the
          supplier, or

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the supplier's normal business address, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the individual who performed the services;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an invoice or other document prepared for
      delivery to an addressee from information obtained at the location
      of such addressee at the time of its delivery;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a bill of exchange, promissory note, draft,
      settlement voucher, money order, Receiver General warrant or any
      other payment order or instruction for the payment of money, or
      any similar or related document, in transit in or between

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one or more institutions or any branch or
        office thereof that accept deposits transferable by order to a
        third party,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Payments Association or any
        branch or office thereof, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any of the places referred to in
        subparagraphs (i) and (ii)

      for the purpose of processing, exchange, clearing or settlement;

      - [(l)]{.lawlabel} an order or instruction given to any
        institution referred to in subparagraph (k)(i) for the payment
        of money, arising out of any transaction in which a payment,
        debit, credit, charge or account access card is used;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} an instrument, certificate or other document
      evidencing or relating to an interest in a security or commodity,
      while in transit in or between any

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} securities or commodities exchange, clearing
        house, or member thereof, or any branch or office thereof,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} body regulating trading in securities or
        commodities, or member or registrant thereof, or any branch or
        office thereof,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} bank or other financial institution or any
        branch or office thereof, or

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any of the places referred to in
        subparagraph (i), (ii) or (iii). ([lettre]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-199, s. 13
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, a transmission by electronic or optical means begins with
the receipt of a message or information at the point of origin of the
transmission and ceases with the display or printing or both at the
point of destination.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Regulation shall come
into force on September 1, 1983.
