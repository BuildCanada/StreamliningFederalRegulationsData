## Forfeited Property Sharing Regulations {#forfeited-property-sharing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-76
:::

[SEIZED PROPERTY MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.3)

Registration 1995-01-31

Regulations Respecting the Sharing of the Proceeds of the Disposition of
Forfeited Property, the Sharing of the Amounts Paid or Recovered on
Account of Certain Fines and the Sharing of Certain Moneys Transferred
to Canada by the Governments of Foreign States

P.C. 1995-125 1995-01-31
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services, the Minister of Justice and the
Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to paragraph 9(f), section 11,
paragraphs 13(2)(a) and (c) and sections 18 and 19 of the [Seized
Property Management Act](/eng/acts/S-8.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0ECBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the sharing of
the proceeds of the disposition of forfeited property, the sharing of
the amounts paid or recovered on account of certain fines and the
sharing of certain moneys transferred to Canada by the governments of
foreign states.

## [Agreements with the Governments of Foreign States Respecting Sharing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977132 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No sharing under the Act
and these Regulations shall take place with the government of a foreign
state unless the government of that foreign state has entered into an
agreement pursuant to section 11 of the Act.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  agreement with the government of a foreign state, entered into
  pursuant to section 11 of the Act, shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide that sharing will be between the Government
    of Canada and the government of that foreign state;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide that the sharing of the proceeds of
    disposition of property in Canada and the sharing of fines in Canada
    will be in accordance with Part I of these Regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide that there will be no conditions in respect
    of the use of any moneys received under the agreement; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} state the title of the official to whom the amount
    of any share is to be paid and sent or provide that a central
    authority will designate to whom the amount of any share is to be
    paid and sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement referred to in subsection (1) shall not
  be inconsistent with the Act or these Regulations.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Forfeited Property and Fines in Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977141 .Part}

### [Determination of the Amount Available for Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977142 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The amount that is
available for sharing shall be determined by subtracting, from the sum
of, first, the amount of the net proceeds of all of a person's property
that has been forfeited in connection with one or more offenses by the
person and, second, the amount of any fine ordered to be paid by the
person in connection with the offence or offenses, the following
amounts:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prorated portion of the amount of the operating
  expenses referred to in paragraph 13(3)(a) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a prorated portion of the amount reserved for

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} future losses,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 15, s. 316\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} payments arising from indemnifications granted
    pursuant to section 18 of the Act.

### [Time of Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977149 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The sharing of an amount
determined pursuant to section 5, shall take place no later than 90 days
after the end of the fiscal year in which the following amounts are paid
into the Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited to the Proceeds Account:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where only one property referred to in
  section 5 has been forfeited, the amount of the net proceeds of the
  property;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where more than one property referred to
  in section 5 has been forfeited, the amount of the net proceeds of the
  property that was disposed of last;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case where a fine referred to in section 5 has
  been ordered, the amount paid or recovered on account of the fine; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case where property referred to in section 5
  has been forfeited and a fine, referred to in section 5 has been
  ordered, the amount of the net proceeds of the property and the amount
  paid or recovered on account of the fine.

### [Contribution of the Government of Canada and Jurisdictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977155 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the sharing of an amount determined pursuant to section 5,
  the Attorney General shall assess the contribution of the Government
  of Canada and of each of the relevant jurisdictions on the basis of
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of information provided by the agencies
    of the Government of Canada and each jurisdiction, and the
    importance of that information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the participation by the agencies of the Government
    of Canada and each jurisdiction in the investigation and prosecution
    that lead to forfeiture or the imposition of a fine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General may, for the purposes of an
  assessment referred to in subsection (1), consult with the relevant
  agencies and jurisdictions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General shall assign a percentage
  representing the contribution of the Government of Canada and of each
  relevant jurisdiction, as compared with the contribution of another
  jurisdiction or group of jurisdictions, as the case may be, determined
  as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the contribution of the Government of Canada
    or a jurisdiction constitutes the predominant portion of the total
    contribution, it shall be considered to be 90 per cent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the contribution of the Government of Canada
    or a jurisdiction constitutes a significant portion of the total
    contribution, it shall be considered to be 50 per cent; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the contribution of the Government of Canada
    or a jurisdiction constitutes a minimal portion of the total
    contribution, it shall be considered to be 10 per cent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Attorney General shall determine the
  contribution, expressed as a percentage in accordance with subsection
  (3), of the Government of Canada and of each of the following
  jurisdictions and groups of jurisdictions, in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the contribution of the Government of
    Canada and the contribution of the group of all relevant
    jurisdictions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the contribution of the jurisdiction that
    has the highest level of contribution from among the group of other
    jurisdictions referred to in paragraph (a), and the contribution of
    the group of remaining jurisdictions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repeat the method set out in paragraph (b), for the
    group of remaining jurisdictions, until only two jurisdictions
    remain; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where only two jurisdictions remain, determine the
    contribution of each of those jurisdictions.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The percentage that represents the contribution of
  the Government of Canada shall in no case be less than 10 per cent.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Attorney General shall
provide to the Minister the percentages that represent the respective
contributions, assigned pursuant to section 7, of the Government of
Canada and the relevant jurisdictions and groups of jurisdictions.

### [Calculation and Payment of Shares]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977172 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall, in
accordance with the percentages referred to in section 8, calculate the
Government of Canada's share and each relevant jurisdiction's share of
an amount determined in accordance with section 5, as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} calculate the share of the Government of Canada by
  multiplying the amount available for sharing by the percentage that
  represents the contribution of the Government of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subtract the product obtained in the preceding
  paragraph from the amount available for sharing, the remainder being
  the first residual amount available for sharing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is only one remaining jurisdiction, the
  share of that jurisdiction is the residual amount referred to in
  paragraph (b), otherwise calculate the share of the jurisdiction that
  has the highest level of contribution as determined under paragraph
  7(4)(b) by multiplying the first residual amount available for sharing
  obtained in accordance with the preceding paragraph by the percentage
  that represents the contribution of that jurisdiction;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subtract the product obtained in the preceding
  paragraph from the first residual amount available for sharing in the
  preceding paragraph, the remainder being the new residual amount
  available for sharing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the method set out in paragraphs (c) and (d),
  with such modifications as the circumstances require, until such time
  as the shares of only two jurisdictions remain to be calculated; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the shares of only two jurisdictions remain to
  be calculated, calculate the shares of those jurisdictions by
  multiplying the new residual amount available for sharing obtained in
  accordance with the preceding step by the percentages that represent
  the respective contributions of those jurisdictions.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall pay to
each relevant jurisdiction the amount of the share, calculated in
accordance with section 9, for that jurisdiction by sending that amount
to the government of a relevant province or to the appropriate official
as specified in an agreement referred to in section 3.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a government of a
province signs a memorandum of understanding with the Government of
Canada respecting the allocation by the province of a portion of a share
received under section 10, the allocation of that portion shall be
consistent with the memorandum of understanding.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Sharing of Moneys Transferred to Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977182 .Part}

### [Determination of the Amount Available for Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977183 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the moneys
transferred to Canada by the government of a foreign state under an
agreement entered into pursuant to section 11 of the Act that is
available for sharing between the Government of Canada and the
government of the relevant provinces shall be determined by subtracting,
from the moneys transferred, the amount referred to in paragraph 5(a).

### [Manner of Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977185 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Sections 7 to 11 apply to
the sharing of the amount determined in accordance with section 12,
except that any contribution of the government of a foreign state shall
not be taken into account.

### [Time of Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-977187 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Sharing shall take place
no later than 90 days after the end of the fiscal year in which the
amounts received from the government of a foreign state are paid into
the Consolidated Revenue Fund and credited to the Proceeds Account.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Within 120 days after the
end of each fiscal year, all amounts credited to the Proceeds Account
that are not shared pursuant to sections 10 and 11 of the Act less such
amounts reserved in accordance with section 16 of the Act shall be
calculated for that fiscal year and paid into the Seized Property
Non-Tax Revenue Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-191, s. 1
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** On or before March 31,
2000, all amounts credited to the Proceeds Account during any of fiscal
years 1993-94 to 1996-97 that have not been shared pursuant to sections
10 and 11 of the Act, less any amounts reserved in accordance with
section 16 of the Act, shall be calculated and paid into the Seized
Property Non-Tax Revenue Account.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-191, s. 1
- SOR/2000-115, s. 2
:::
