## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749612 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
Annex C to the version of the Free Trade Agreement between Canada and
the States of the European Free Trade Association (Iceland,
Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland), signed on January 26, 2008, have
the force of law in Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 1 to 8;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Article 9, paragraph 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 10 to 12;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Article 14; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Appendix I.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749619 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 1, 2009, but if they are registered after that day, they
come into force on the day they are registered.
