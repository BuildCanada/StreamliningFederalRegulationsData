## Energy Monitoring Regulations, 2006 {#energy-monitoring-regulations-2006 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-160
:::

[ENERGY MONITORING ACT](/eng/acts/E-8)

Registration 2007-06-22

Energy Monitoring Regulations, 2006

P.C. 2007-1041 2007-06-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to the definitions [[energy
commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[energy
enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) and sections 4 to
6[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBAA} and 41 of the
[Energy Monitoring Act](/eng/acts/E-8), hereby makes the annexed [Energy
Monitoring Regulations, 2006](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-160).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734686 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Energy Monitoring
    Act](/eng/acts/E-8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CICA Handbook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CICA Handbook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the handbook prepared
    and published by the Canadian Institute of Chartered Accountants, as
    amended from time to time. ([Manuel de l'ICCA]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734690 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[energy commodity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1)
of the Act, the following products resulting from the processing or
refining of oil or gas are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} aviation fuels;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} motor gasoline and motor gasoline blending
  components;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} stove oil and kerosene;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} diesel fuel;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fuel oils;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} semi-refined feedstreams for the manufacture of any
  of the products set out in paragraphs (a) to (e);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} feedstreams, not in themselves petrochemicals, used
  in the manufacture of petrochemicals;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} slack wax;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} asphalt;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} petroleum coke;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} feedstreams for refining into lubricants, process
  oils or hydrocarbon solvents;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} liquified petroleum gases, including propane, butane,
  ethane and mixtures of those products; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} sulphur.

## [Determination of Gross Annual Revenues and Assets in Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734705 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 4(a) of the Act, the gross annual revenues of an
  energy enterprise shall be determined in accordance with the
  recommendations set out in the CICA Handbook without deducting or
  allowing for any expenses other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those expenses or charges normally deducted or
    allowed in accordance with the CICA Handbook for the purpose of
    determining gross revenues; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} royalties or taxes collected on behalf of Canada, a
    province or a municipal authority.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 4(b) of the Act, the
  assets of an energy enterprise related to its activities referred to
  in the definition [[energy enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 2(1) of the Act shall be determined in accordance with the
  recommendations set out in the CICA Handbook.

## [Exemption from Filing a Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734711 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every energy enterprise is
exempted from the requirement of filing a return under sections 5 and 6
of the Act.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734713 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734715 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
