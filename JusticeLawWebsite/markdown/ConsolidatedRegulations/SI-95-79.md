## Order Designating the Appropriate Authority for a Province with Respect to the Act {#order-designating-the-appropriate-authority-for-a-province-with-respect-to-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-79
:::

[CANADA STUDENT FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE ACT](/eng/acts/S-22.7)

Registration 1995-08-09

Order Designating the Appropriate Authority for a Province with Respect
to the Act
::::

:::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Employment and Immigration, pursuant to subsection 3(1)
of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-79_e_hq_5157){#fn_SI-95-79_e_hq_5157-ID0EBBCA},
hereby designates, for the purposes of that Act, the appropriate
authority for a province as set out in the schedule hereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-79_e_hq_5157-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-95-79_e_hq_5157}S.C.
  1994, c. 28
  :::

Ottawa, July 17, 1995

::: FlushRight
LLOYD AXWORTHY
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Employment and Immigration*
:::
