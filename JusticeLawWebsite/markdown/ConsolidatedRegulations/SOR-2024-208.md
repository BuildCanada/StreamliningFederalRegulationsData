## Shipment by Post of Certain Prohibited Firearms and Prohibited Devices by Certain Businesses Regulations {#shipment-by-post-of-certain-prohibited-firearms-and-prohibited-devices-by-certain-businesses-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-208
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 2024-10-16

Shipment by Post of Certain Prohibited Firearms and Prohibited Devices
by Certain Businesses Regulations

P.C. 2024-1112 2024-10-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, under section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174){#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174-ID0EDBBA},
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness had the
proposed [Shipment by Post of Certain Prohibited Firearms and Prohibited
Devices by Certain Businesses
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-208) laid before each House of
Parliament at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174-ID0EDBBA){#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174}S.C.
  1995, c. 39
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, makes the annexed [Shipment by Post of Certain Prohibited
Firearms and Prohibited Devices by Certain Businesses
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-208) under paragraph
117(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1728656306){#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1728656306-ID0EEBAA}
of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174){#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1691176174-ID0EBBAA}.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1728656306-ID0EEBAA){#fn_81000-2-5240_hq_1728656306}S.C.
  2023, c. 32, s. 45(1.1)
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[specified device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specified device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited device
      referred to in item 4 of Part 4 of the schedule to the
      [Regulations Prescribing Certain Firearms and Other Weapons,
      Components and Parts of Weapons, Accessories, Cartridge Magazines,
      Ammunition and Projectiles as Prohibited or
      Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462). ([dispositif
      visé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specified firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specified firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited
      firearm referred to in any of paragraphs 83(a) to (p) or any of
      items 87 to 96 of Part 1 of the schedule to the [Regulations
      Prescribing Certain Firearms and Other Weapons, Components and
      Parts of Weapons, Accessories, Cartridge Magazines, Ammunition and
      Projectiles as Prohibited or
      Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462), other than one that is
      referred to in item 2-2 of A Guide to Canada's Export Control
      List, published by the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and
      Development, as amended from time to time, as adapted by paragraph
      3(2)(a) of the schedule to the [Defence Production
      Act](/eng/acts/D-1). ([arme à feu visée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, all other
  words and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in the [Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other
  Weapons by Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shipment of specified firearms

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 15 of the
[Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by
Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), a business that is
a person referred to in paragraph 2(1)(a) or (b) of the [Order Declaring
an Amnesty Period (2020)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-97) may ship, during
the period set out in subsection 2(3) of that Order, a specified firearm
to a person located in Canada by posting it if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the business has entered into an agreement with the
  Government of Canada regarding the destruction of the specified
  firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addressee is a person referred to in paragraph
  2(1)(g) of that Order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specified firearm is posted using the most secure
  means of transmission by post that includes the requirement to obtain
  a signature on delivery; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specified firearm is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unloaded,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} packaged in a container that

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} is made of an opaque material and is of such
      strength, construction and nature that it cannot readily be broken
      open or into or accidentally opened during transportation,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} does not contain any ammunition or prohibited
      ammunition, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} does not have any markings on its exterior that
      could indicate that it contains a weapon, a prohibited device or
      ammunition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shipment of specified devices

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A business that is a person
referred to in paragraph 2(1)(c) of the [Order Declaring an Amnesty
Period (2020)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-97) may ship, during the period
set out in subsection 2(3) of that Order, a specified device to a person
located in Canada by posting it if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the business has entered into an agreement with the
  Government of Canada regarding the destruction of the specified
  device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addressee is a person referred to in paragraph
  2(1)(g) of that Order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the specified device is posted using the most secure
  means of transmission by post that includes the requirement to obtain
  a signature on delivery; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specified device is packaged in a container that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} does not contain any ammunition or prohibited
    ammunition, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not have any markings on its exterior that
    could indicate that it contains a weapon, a prohibited device or
    ammunition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transmission by post

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The [Storage, Display and
Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210) do not apply to the storage
and transportation of a specified firearm or specified device in the
course of transmission by post within Canada from the time the specified
firearm or specified device is posted to the time it is delivered to the
addressee, within the meaning of subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Post
Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10), or returned to the sender.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
