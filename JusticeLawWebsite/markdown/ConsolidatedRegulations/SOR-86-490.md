## Cree-Naskapi Long-Term Borrowing Regulations, 1986 {#cree-naskapi-long-term-borrowing-regulations-1986 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-490
:::

[NASKAPI AND THE CREE-NASKAPI COMMISSION ACT](/eng/acts/C-45.7)

CREE-NASKAPI (OF QUEBEC) ACT

Registration 1986-05-01

Regulations Respecting Long-Term Borrowing by the Cree Bands and the
Naskapi Band

P.C. 1986-1050 1986-05-01

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 98 and subsection 198(2) of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec)
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EGACA}, is pleased
hereby to revoke the Cree-Naskapi Long-Term Borrowing Regulations, made
by Order in Council P.C. 1985-793 of 14th March, 1985[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EDACA}, and to
make the annexed Regulations respecting long-term borrowing by the Cree
bands and the Naskapi band, in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897177 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cree-Naskapi Long-Term Borrowing Regulations,
1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-490).

## [By-law Content]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897179 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A by-law made under
subsection 97(1) of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act authorizing a
long-term borrowing shall, in addition to the information required to be
specified pursuant to that subsection, specify the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the lender;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest, commissions, premiums and other similar
  costs related to the long-term borrowing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the source of revenue and the portion of the revenue
  from that source from which the long-term borrowing is to be repaid
  and the costs related thereto are to be paid; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the security, if any, given by the band for the
  repayment of the long-term borrowing and the payment of the costs
  related thereto.

## [Repayment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897185 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A band shall apply
sufficient moneys from the revenue received from the source specified
pursuant to paragraph 2(c) to repay the long-term borrowing and to pay
the costs related thereto.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897187 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No council member, officer,
employee or agent of a band shall knowingly apply or assist in the
application of any moneys received by the band from the portion of the
revenue specified pursuant to paragraph 2(c) for the repayment of a
long-term borrowing and the payment of the costs related thereto to
anything other than the repayment of the long-term borrowing and the
payment of the costs related thereto prior to the time the long-term
borrowing is repaid and the costs related thereto are paid.

## [Maximum Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897189 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
198 of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act in respect of a contravention of
section 4 of these Regulations, the maximum fine stipulated is two
thousand dollars and the maximum term of imprisonment stipulated is six
months.
