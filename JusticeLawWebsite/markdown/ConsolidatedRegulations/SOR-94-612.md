- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, pensionable
  service to the credit of a member under the member's pension plan
  accrued during the period subject to division includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all pensionable service that is current service
    during the period subject to division, less any period of absence on
    leave without pay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any period of elective service, other than a period
    described in paragraph (e), to the credit of the member at
    separation day in the proportion that the contributions in respect
    of the elective service paid by or on behalf of the member during
    the period subject to division bears to the total amount of the
    contributions required to be paid for that elective service;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any period of service during which the member was
    absent on leave without pay in the proportion that the amount of
    contributions made by or on behalf of the member during the period
    subject to division bears to the total amount required to be paid by
    the member for the purpose of considering the total period of leave
    without pay as pensionable service;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any period of pensionable service credited to the
    member before valuation day by reason of a transfer of funds from
    another pension plan or pursuant to a reciprocal transfer agreement
    or a pension transfer agreement in the proportion that the period
    credited to the member under that other plan in respect of the
    period subject to division bears to the total period of pensionable
    service to the credit of the member under the other plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any period of service credited to the member before
    valuation day by reason of an election made under clause
    6(1)(b)(iii)(M) of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), in the proportion that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period of pensionable service that accrued
      during the period subject to division that was included in the
      determination of the transfer value referred to in that clause

    bears to

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total period of pensionable service
      represented by the payment of that transfer value.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, except section
  18, if a member of a pension plan referred to in paragraph (a) of the

## [Vested]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964845 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
these Regulations, a member is considered vested if the member is
entitled to receive a pension or would be entitled to receive a pension
if he or she ceases to be employed.

- SOR/2003-408, s. 2
- SOR/2007-298, s. 1

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964849 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 4(4)(a) of the Act, an application shall contain
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the member,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the member's name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the member's most recent address that is known
      to the applicant,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the member's date of birth,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the most recent place of employment of the
      member in the public service of Canada that is known to the
      applicant,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the member has ceased to be
      employed in the public service of Canada, where applicable, if
      that day is known to the applicant, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the member's employee identification number,
      regimental number or pension number, as the case may be, if that
      number is known by the applicant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the spouse, former spouse or former
    common-law partner of the member,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that person's name, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the most recent address of that person known to
      the applicant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 4(4)(b) of the Act, if
  the court order or agreement does not specify the period of
  cohabitation or the period subject to division, the application shall
  be accompanied by the following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the interested parties are or were married to
    each other, an original or certified true copy of the certificate of
    marriage and a statutory declaration by the applicant stating the
    date on which they ceased to cohabit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the interested parties were not married to each
    other, a statutory declaration by the applicant stating the date on
    which the member and the spouse or former common-law partner began
    to cohabit in a relationship of a conjugal nature and the date on
    which that cohabitation ceased.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 3
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the applicant making or proceeding with the application is acting on
  behalf of an interested party, the original or a certified true copy
  of the document that authorizes the applicant to act for the
  interested party shall, in addition to the documents that may be
  required pursuant to subsection 3(2), be submitted to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 6, where the applicant making or
  proceeding with the application is the personal representative of a
  deceased interested party, the following documents shall, in addition
  to the documents that may be required pursuant to subsection 3(2), be
  submitted to the Minister:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original or a certified true copy of the
    document that authorizes the applicant to act in that capacity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the original or a certified true copy of the death
    certificate of the interested party; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the terms of the court order or
    agreement remain in force.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 4
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person may make an
application on behalf of an interested party or may act on behalf of an
interested party in prosecuting an application where the interested
party is incapable of managing their own affairs.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the spouse, former
spouse or former common-law partner of a deceased member makes an
application, he or she shall submit to the Minister a statutory
declaration stating that the terms of the court order or agreement have
not been or are not being satisfied by other means.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 5
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may, by notice
in writing to the Minister, withdraw the application at any time before
a transfer is effected pursuant to paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The notice referred to in
subsections 5(1) and 8(7) of the Act shall be sent by the Minister by
registered mail.

## [Information to the Spouse, Former Spouse, Common-law Partner or Former Common-law Partner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964881 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for information referred to in subsection 13(2) of the Act
  shall be in writing and be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is a court order or agreement,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certified true copy of the court order or
      agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the following circumstances, a statutory
      declaration by the spouse or former spouse or common-law partner
      or former common-law partner that meets the requirements set out
      in subsection (2):

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the court order or agreement does not specify
        the period of cohabitation or the period subject to division, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an appeal or review referred to in paragraph
        6(2)(c) of the Act has been commenced in respect of the period
        of cohabitation or the period subject to division;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is no court order or agreement, a
    statutory declaration by the spouse or former spouse or common-law
    partner or former common-law partner that meets the requirements set
    out in subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statutory declaration must state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a spouse or former spouse, the date
    of marriage and the date on which he or she ceased to cohabit with
    the member or, if the spouse and member have not ceased to cohabit,
    that it is his or her intention to cease cohabiting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a common-law partner or former
    common-law partner, the date on which he or she began to cohabit
    with the member in a relationship of a conjugal nature and the date
    on which that cohabitation ceased or, if the common-law partner and
    member have not ceased to cohabit, that it is his or her intention
    to cease cohabiting.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 6
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 6
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the member

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will be eligible to receive a pension that is

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period subject to division;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the member is not vested on the day on which the

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid by the member during the period

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any period of pensionable service credited to the

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any minimum death benefit payable on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the supplementary death benefit

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the amount that would be determined in accordance

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any period of service credited to the member by

    [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), determined in
    accordance with paragraph 2(3)(e).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), if the period
  subject to division cannot be determined under paragraph 8(2)(a) of
  the Act, the period subject to division begins on the day of marriage
  or, in the case of common-law partners, on the day on which they began
  to cohabit in a relationship of a conjugal nature, and ends

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which the parties ceased to cohabit,
    as stated in the statutory declaration referred to in subsection
    9(2); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the last day of the month preceding the month in
    which the request for information is made, if the parties have not
    ceased to cohabit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following is the information to be provided by
  the Minister in response to a request under subsection 13(2) of the
  Act made after the division of the member's pension benefits under the
  plan in respect of which the request is made:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period subject to division;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the member was not vested on valuation day,
    the amount of contributions made by the member in respect of
    pensionable service accrued during the period subject to division
    and the interest on the contributions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid by the contributor during the
    period subject to division in respect of any period of elective
    service and the period of service included in the period subject to
    division, determined in accordance with paragraph 2(3)(b);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the member's division annuity on
    valuation day;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any period of pensionable service credited to the
    member under a reciprocal transfer agreement or a pension transfer
    agreement in respect of the period subject to division, determined
    in accordance with paragraph 2(3)(d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any minimum death benefit that, on
    valuation day, would have been payable on the death of the member in
    respect of the period subject to division, determined as though
    there were no surviving spouse, surviving common-law partner or
    dependent children;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the amount that, but for subsection 8(4) of the
    Act, would have been transferred in accordance with paragraph
    8(1)(a) of the Act; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any period of service credited to the member by
    reason of an election made under clause 6(1)(b)(iii)(M) of the
    [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), determined in
    accordance with paragraph 2(3)(e).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 7
:::

## [Refusal of Request]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964928 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse
to provide information in response to a request for information made by
a spouse, former spouse, common-law partner or former common-law partner
under subsection 13(2) of the Act or section 12 if the Minister has
responded within the preceding 12 months to a similar request made by
that person, unless, since that previous request,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the parties have ceased to cohabit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the member or his or her spouse, former spouse,
  common-law partner or former common-law partner began proceedings in
  relation to separation, divorce or annulment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the parties entered into an agreement; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the member's pensionable service included in the
  period subject to division has been adjusted.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 8
:::

## [Supplementary Death Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964936 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a court in Canada of
competent jurisdiction has ordered that a spouse, former spouse,
common-law partner or former common-law partner be designated as the
beneficiary of the supplementary death benefit provided under Part II of
the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or Part II of
the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17), or the parties
have agreed in writing to such a designation, the Minister shall, at the
written request of the spouse, former spouse, common-law partner or
former common-law partner, inform that person whether he or she is the
designated beneficiary and the amount of the supplementary death benefit
that would be payable to him or her.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 9
:::

## [Valuation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964940 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 8 of the Act, the value of pension benefits that have accrued to
a member during the period subject to division is equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the member is vested at valuation day, the
  actuarial present value on valuation day of the member's pension
  benefits accrued during the period subject to division, determined in
  accordance with sections 14 and 15; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the member is not vested at valuation day, the
  amount determined under section 16.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  accrued pension benefits of a member shall be determined in accordance
  with the member's pension plan and, where applicable, the
  [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24), subject to
  the following rules:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any reference in the member's pension plan to an
    annuity shall be replaced by a reference to a division annuity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reference in the member's pension plan to the
    CPP deduction shall be replaced by a reference to the member's
    division annuity deduction;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the member's minimum death benefit, if any, shall
    be determined as if the member's annuity were the member's division
    annuity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any benefits that are or may become payable to the
    member's spouse, former spouse, common-law partner or children on
    the death of the member are to be excluded; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} supplementary benefits, including supplementary
    benefits payable under the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/S-24), are determined on the basis of the benefit
    indices established under that Act that would have applied had the
    member ceased to be employed on the earlier of separation day and
    termination day, increased to take into account the period beginning
    on January 1st of the year in which valuation day occurs and ending
    on valuation day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where on valuation day, a member is in receipt of a
  pension by reason of disability, the member's accrued pension benefits
  to be valued are the pension benefits that the member would be
  entitled to if the member were not entitled to a pension by reason of
  disability.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a member dies before a transfer is effected
  pursuant to paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act, the calculation of the
  member's pension benefits shall be made as if the member had not died
  and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the member was employed on the date of death,
    termination day shall be deemed to be the day before the date of
    death; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the member was in receipt of a pension on the
    day before the date of death, the member's accrued pension benefits
    shall be determined as of the day before the member's death.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-408, s. 10
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining the actuarial present value of a member\'s accrued pension
  benefits, the following actuarial assumptions are to be used:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the member is employed on valuation day

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the termination and retirement rates for current
      contributors, used in the preparation of the last actuarial
      valuation report for the member\'s pension plan laid before
      Parliament, modified to reflect any special conditions that apply
      to the tenure of office or service or to special benefits in
      respect of an occupational group of the members of the pension
      plan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the mortality rates for current and former
      contributors, including mortality projection factors, used in the
      preparation of the report referred to in subparagraph (i), or the
      mortality rates, including mortality projection factors, referred
      to in the section entitled "Pension Commuted Values" of the
      Standards of Practice -- Practice-Specific Standards for Pension
      Plans (the "Standards"), published by the Canadian Institute of
      Actuaries, as amended from time to time, whichever will produce
      the greater actuarial present value of a member's accrued pension
      benefits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the member ceased to be employed before
    valuation day, the mortality rates for former contributors, other
    than disabled contributors, including mortality projection factors,
    used in the preparation of the report referred to in subparagraph
    (a)(i), or the mortality rates, including mortality projection
    factors, referred to in the Standards, whichever will produce the
    greater actuarial present value of a member's accrued pension
    benefits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a zero probability of a member becoming entitled to
    a pension by reason of disability; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interest rates determined in accordance with
    the Standards, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of periods during which the member is
      a recipient, the interest rates for fully indexed pensions,
      adjusted to reflect an annual basis of payment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of periods during which the member is
      not a recipient, the interest rates for unindexed pensions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The last actuarial valuation report for the pension
  plans referred to in subparagraphs (a)(iii) and (v) of the definition
  [[pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act shall be
  the last actuarial valuation report in respect of the pension plans
  referred to in subparagraphs (a)(ii) and (iv), respectively, of that
  definition.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The last actuarial valuation report for the pension
  plans referred to in subparagraphs (a)(vi), (vii) and (viii) of the
  definition [[pension plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  Act shall be the last actuarial valuation report in respect of the
  pension plan laid before Parliament under the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the last actuarial
  valuation report is the most recent actuarial valuation report laid
  before Parliament in accordance with section 9 of the [Public Pensions
  Reporting Act](/eng/acts/P-31.4) or, if the actuarial valuation report
  has been tabled in the month in which valuation day occurs or in the
  preceding month, the immediately previously tabled report.

will become payable.

## [Adjustment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964985 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this Part, [[division factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is 0.5, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount transferred is a lump sum amount
    referred to in subsection 8(4) of the Act, the division factor is
    equal to the product of

    ::: {#964989 .Paragraph}
    (A ÷ B) × 0.5

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount determined under subsection 8(4) of the Act, and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount determined under these Regulations that, but for
        subsection 8(4) of the Act, would have been transferred pursuant
        to paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act; and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the member was not vested at valuation day
    and is entitled to a pension under the member's pension plan on the
    day the pension benefits are adjusted, the division factor is equal
    to the product of

    ::: {#964994 .Paragraph}
    (C ÷ D) × 0.5

    where

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount transferred under paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act,
        and

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount that, but for subsection 8(4) of the Act, would
        have been transferred pursuant to paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act
        had the member been vested on valuation day.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining the indexed division
  annuity, if separation day occurs before termination day, the division
  annuity of a member is to be increased on termination day by the
  benefit indices established under the [Supplementary Retirement
  Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) that would apply if the member had
  ceased to be employed on separation day, and further increased to take
  into account the period beginning on January 1 of the year in which
  termination day occurs and ending on termination day.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-420, s. 3
:::

  under a transfer agreement entered into under section 24.1 of the
  [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/R-11).

  the first day of the month following valuation day.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the member's reduction, in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month after valuation day; and

    pension plan.

21, where a transfer has been effected pursuant to paragraph 8(1)(a) of
the Act and the member is entitled to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pension by reason of disability, the reduction of
  the pension benefits shall begin on the first day of the month
  following the month during which the member would first be entitled to
  receive a pension that would not be reduced on account of age;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pension that is reduced on account of age before or
  after becoming entitled to a pension by reason of disability, the
  reduction of the pension benefits shall be determined as if the member
  were not entitled to a pension by reason of disability; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} supplementary benefits by reason of disability, the
  member shall not be considered a recipient for the purposes of
  subsection 21(2) until the day the member would have become a
  recipient by reason of age or by reason of age and pensionable
  service.

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the return of contributions the member would have
  been entitled to receive in respect of the period subject to division,

  contributions, had there not been a transfer of the member's pension
  benefits pursuant to paragraph 8(1)(a) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the division factor.
