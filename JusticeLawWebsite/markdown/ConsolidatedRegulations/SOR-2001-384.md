## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673663 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 390(3)(d)(ii) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), subsections 390(4) to (6) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether an association would be permitted to
acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 388(2),
paragraph 388(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 388(4) or 390(1) or (2) of the
Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6006){#fn_SOR-2001-384_e_hq_6006-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
paragraphs 396(a) and (b) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), as enacted by section 314 of the [Financial
Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
