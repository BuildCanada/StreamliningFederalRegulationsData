## Firearms Fees Remission Order {#firearms-fees-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-54
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-07-05

Firearms Fees Remission Order

P.C. 2000-1027  2000-06-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Justice and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA}of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Firearms Fees Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-54).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission in the amount of
\$35 is hereby granted to any individual who made an application for a
possession licence under the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) during the
period beginning on December 1, 1999 and ending on June 9, 2000 and who
paid \$45 for the licence under column 2 of item 1 of Part 1 of Schedule
I to the [Firearms Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204).
