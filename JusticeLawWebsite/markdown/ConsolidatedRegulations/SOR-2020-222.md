## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262309 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[host company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[host company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a railway company that
    authorizes a passenger company to operate on its
    railway. ([compagnie hôte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[passenger company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[passenger company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company whose
    operations include the transport of passengers by
    railway. ([compagnie de transport de voyageurs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[small passenger company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small passenger company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a passenger
    company that transported fewer than 60,000 passengers in one of the
    two previous calendar years. ([petite compagnie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[train]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[train]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the pieces of railway
    equipment that are joined together and that move as a unit for the
    transport of passengers. ([rame ferroviaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Security Awareness Training]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security awareness training program

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company and host company must have a security awareness
  training program that promotes a culture of vigilance with respect to
  passenger rail transportation security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Program training topics

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The security awareness training program must cover
  the following topics:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the main security risks related to
    passenger rail transportation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any potential threats and other security concerns
    related to passenger rail transportation and how to recognize them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the actions to be taken to deal with potential
    threats and other security concerns; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any measures of the passenger company or host
    company that are designed to enhance passenger rail transportation
    security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must ensure
  that any person employed by, and any person acting on behalf of, the
  company and who have any of the following duties relating to the
  transport of passengers, as well as the direct supervisors of those
  persons, undergo security awareness training:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating, maintaining or inspecting railway
    equipment or railway works;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} controlling the dispatch or movement of railway
    equipment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the security of railway equipment and
    railway works;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} loading or unloading goods to or from railway
    equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} interacting with the public for the purposes of
    railway transportation; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensuring compliance with its security processes,
    including those set out in their security plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of training

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must ensure
  that security awareness training is provided to the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the day on which this section
    comes into force, unless the person has received equivalent security
    awareness training before that day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the day on which the person
    initially assumed any of the duties referred to in subsection (3)
    with that company, if the duties were assigned to that person after
    the day on which this section comes into force; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a recurrent basis at least once every three
    years after the day on which the person completed their previous
    training, including any equivalent security awareness training
    received before the day on which this section comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must ensure
  that, until the person undergoes the security awareness training, the
  person performs their duties under the supervision of a person who has
  undergone that training.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training records

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must ensure
  that a training record is kept for each person who has undergone
  security awareness training and that the record

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is kept up to date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} includes the person's name and details of their
    most recent training, including the date, duration and the title of
    the training, the delivery method and the name of the provider of
    the training;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} includes the title and the date of any previous
    security awareness training taken by the person; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is retained for at least two years after the day on
    which the person ceases to be employed by, or ceases to act on
    behalf of, the company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of training materials

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must ensure
  that a copy of the most recent awareness training materials is kept.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Coordination and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rail security coordinator

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company and host company must have, at all times, a rail
  security coordinator or an acting rail security coordinator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company and host company must provide
  the Minister with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and job title of the rail security
    coordinator or acting rail security coordinator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the 24-hour contact information for the rail
    security coordinator or acting rail security coordinator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties --- passenger company

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company must ensure that the rail
  security coordinator or acting rail security coordinator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} coordinates security matters within the passenger
    company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acts as the principal contact between the passenger
    company and the Minister with respect to security matters; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} coordinates communications between the passenger
    company, the host company, law enforcement and emergency response
    agencies with respect to security matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties --- host company

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The host company must ensure that the rail security
  coordinator or acting rail security coordinator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acts as the principal contact between the host
    company and the Minister with respect to security matters related to
    passenger rail transportation on its railway; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} coordinates communications between the host
    company, passenger companies that operate on its railway, law
    enforcement and emergency response agencies with respect to security
    matters related to passenger rail transportation on its railway.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security Reporting

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company or host company must report to the Transport Canada
  Situation Centre, by any direct means of communication established and
  communicated by the Centre, any threat or other security concern that
  results or may result in an unlawful interference with passenger rail
  transportation. The report must be made as soon as feasible but no
  later than 24 hours after the occurrence of the threat or other
  security concern.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Threats and other security concerns

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Threats and other security concerns include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any interference with the work of a train crew or
    service personnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any bomb threats, either specific or non-specific;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any report or discovery of a suspicious item;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any suspicious activity observed on, inside or near
    railway equipment or railway works used by that company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the discovery, seizure or discharge of a weapon,
    explosive substance or incendiary device on, inside or near railway
    equipment or railway works used by that company;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any sign of tampering with railway equipment or
    railway works;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any information relating to the possible
    surveillance of railway equipment or railway works; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any suspicious person, circumstance or object that
    the passenger company or host company considers to be a threat or
    other security concern.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information provided must include, to the extent
  known, the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the passenger company's or host company's name and
    contact information, including its telephone number and email
    address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who is making the report on
    behalf of the passenger company or host company and the person's
    title and contact information, including their telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information that identifies any train that is
    affected by the threat or other security concern, including its
    itinerary and line or route position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any information that identifies any railway
    equipment or railway works that is affected by the threat or other
    security concern;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the threat or other security
    concern, including the date and time that the passenger company or
    host company became aware of it;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names of the persons involved in the threat or
    other security concern, and any other information related to those
    persons, if the disclosure of those names and that information is
    permitted; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the source of any threat information or other
    security concern, if its disclosure is permitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Follow-up information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company must provide to
  the Transport Canada Situation Centre any information referred to in
  subsection (3) that was not previously reported, as soon as it becomes
  known.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Avoidance of double reporting --- other
  company

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company or host company is not required
  to make a report under this section if the same threat or other
  security concern has been reported by another company under this
  section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information --- passenger
  company

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company that operates on a host company's
  railway must, as soon as feasible, notify the host company if the
  passenger company becomes aware of a threat or other security concern
  that could impact the operations of the host company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information --- host
  company

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A host company must, as soon as feasible, notify a
  passenger company that operates on its railway if the host company
  becomes aware of a threat or other security concern that could impact
  the operations of the passenger company.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Security Inspections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262417 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security inspections

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this section, [[car]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a piece of
  railway equipment that is used for passenger rail transportation and
  includes a baggage car, a dining car, a sleeping car, a lounge car, an
  observation car, the locomotive and any freight car that can be
  subjected to a walk-through inspection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Process

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company must establish and document a
  process with respect to security inspections, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a procedure for conducting security inspections;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a method for determining whether security has been
    compromised;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a method for determining whether additional
    security inspections are necessary if, having regard to the
    circumstances, security could be compromised;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a method for addressing the situation, if security
    has been compromised, before the train enters into service; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a method for preventing unauthorized interference
    with the train after the inspection and until passengers board the
    train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In order to ensure that there are no security
  concerns related to passenger rail transportation, a passenger company
  must ensure that security inspections consist of both a ground-level
  visual inspection of the exterior of the train and an inspection of
  the interior of each car and are carried out in accordance with the
  process set out in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of inspection

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company must ensure that a security
  inspection is carried out before the train enters service for the day.
  In all cases, the inspection must be carried out before passengers
  board the train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional inspections

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company must ensure that additional
  security inspections are carried out after the train enters service
  for the day, if it is determined, in accordance with the method
  referred to in paragraph (2)(c), that they are necessary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection of train

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} When the security inspection is carried out before
  passengers board the train, the passenger company must ensure that the
  train is protected from unauthorized interference from the start of
  the security inspection until passengers board the train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signs of tampering, suspicious objects
  and other things

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If signs of tampering or the presence of a suspicious
  object or any other thing that raises security concerns is discovered
  during the security inspection, the passenger company must determine
  whether security has been compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compromise of security

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If it is determined that security has been
  compromised, the passenger company must ensure the situation is
  resolved before allowing the train to enter into service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company must keep a record of each
  security inspection and ensure that the record contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time and date of the inspection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information identifying the train that was
    inspected;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who conducted the
    inspection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the details of any signs of tampering, suspicious
    objects or other things that raise security concerns; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the measures taken to resolve the situation, if the
    company determined that security was compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The passenger company must retain the record for
  three years after the day on which the security inspection is
  conducted.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Security Risk Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262453 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security risk assessment

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company, other than a small passenger company, must conduct
  a security risk assessment of its network and operations that are
  related to passenger rail transportation in Canada that identifies,
  describes, assesses and prioritizes security risks and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is based on the following elements:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} current security threats, including security
      threat information received from a federal department or agency
      and threats or immediate threats identified in an instrument made
      by an inspector under section 31 of the [Railway Safety
      Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) or by the Minister under section 33 or 39.1
      of that Act;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operations, railway equipment, railway works and
      other assets that are deemed critical and that most require
      protection from acts and attempted acts of unlawful interference
      with passenger rail transportation;

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} security vulnerabilities, including those
      identified during daily operations, in security reports made under
      section 4, during security inspections carried out under section 5
      and during security exercises carried out under section 9; and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} potential impacts, including a decrease in
      public safety and security, loss of life, damage to property or
      the environment, disruption of rail transportation and financial
      and economic loss;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifies, for each risk, the likelihood that the
    risk will occur and the severity of the impact that it could have if
    it occurs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identifies potential safeguards intended to
    mitigate the risks identified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The security risk assessment must be documented in a
  report within 30 days after the day on which the assessment is
  completed and the report must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate the date of completion of the assessment;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain all the information referred to in
    subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent risk assessments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must conduct a new security risk assessment within three
  years after the date of completion of the current security risk
  assessment, or any assessment that is carried out before the day on
  which this section comes into force and that meets the requirements of
  this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must review its security risk assessment within seven days
  after the day on which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is a change in circumstances that is likely
    to adversely affect passenger rail transportation security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an instrument that identifies a threat or an
    immediate threat to passenger rail transportation security that is
    not described in the assessment is made by an inspector under
    section 31 of the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2) or by the
    Minister under section 33 or 39.1 of that Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the company identifies a significant security
    vulnerability that is not described in the assessment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic review

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must review its security risk assessment at least once every
  12 months. A new risk assessment conducted under subsection (3) or a
  review conducted under subsection (4) is a review for the purposes of
  this subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for review

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} As part of a review referred to in subsection (4)
  or (5) --- with the exception of a new risk assessment referred to in
  subsection (5) --- a passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify, describe, assess and prioritize any new
    security risks in accordance with subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} document the review in the report on the current
    security risk assessment, including the date of the review, the
    reason for the review under subsection (4) or (5) and any new risks
    that have been identified, their priority level and the potential
    security safeguards, if applicable.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Security Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262490 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan --- objectives

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company, other than a small passenger company, must have and
  implement a security plan that contains measures to be taken to
  prevent, detect, mitigate, respond to and recover from acts or
  attempted acts of unlawful interference with passenger rail
  transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strategy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to meet the objectives of subsection (1),
  the security plan must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a risk management strategy that addresses the risks
    prioritized as medium or higher in the company's most recent
    security risk assessment and all other risks that require remedial
    action; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} additional safeguards that are intended to mitigate
    heightened risk conditions in a graduated manner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The security plan must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify, by job title, a senior manager
    responsible for the plan's overall development, approval and
    implementation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} describe the organizational structure, identify the
    departments that are responsible for implementing the plan or any
    portion of it and identify each position whose incumbent is
    responsible for implementing the plan or any portion of it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} describe the security duties of each identified
    department and position;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} set out a process for notifying each person who is
    responsible for implementing the plan or any portion of it when the
    plan or that portion of it must be implemented;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} set out a program for the security awareness
    training required under section 2 and the components of the security
    plan training referred to in section 8, including a method to ensure
    that persons who undergo the security plan training acquire the
    knowledge and skills required under subsection 8(3);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} set out a process with respect to security risk
    assessments required under section 6, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a procedure for conducting security risk
      assessments, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a method for assessing and prioritizing the
      risk;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} set out a process with respect to remedial actions
    that are part of the risk management strategy referred to in
    subsection (2), including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a method for identifying security risks that
      require remedial action, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a method for implementing remedial actions and
      for evaluating their effectiveness;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} set out a process for selecting and implementing
    additional safeguards required under paragraph (2)(b);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} describe the remedial actions, including their
    effectiveness in reducing or eliminating the risks, and the
    additional safeguards that are part of the risk management strategy
    referred to in subsection (2);

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} set out the process with respect to security
    inspections referred to in subsection 5(2);

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} set out a process with respect to security
    exercises referred to in section 9, including procedures for
    conducting security exercises;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} set out a process for responding to threats and
    other security concerns, including procedures for communicating and
    coordinating with the host company, if applicable;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} set out a process for reporting threats and other
    security concerns;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} set out a process for reviewing the security plan;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} include the report on the most recent security risk
    assessment required under section 6; and

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} set out a policy on limiting access to
    security-sensitive information and set out measures for the sharing,
    storing and destruction of that information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation --- remedial actions and
  safeguards

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must implement the remedial actions and additional safeguards
  referred to in subsection (2), in accordance with the security plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timelines --- remedial actions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must establish timelines for implementing each remedial
  action and for evaluating its effectiveness in reducing or eliminating
  the risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effectiveness --- remedial actions

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must evaluate the effectiveness of each remedial action that
  has been implemented in reducing or eliminating the risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New remedial action

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the remedial action is not effective in reducing
  or eliminating some of the risks, the passenger company must identify
  additional remedial actions or a new remedial action to address those
  risks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan management

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make available to each person who is responsible
    for implementing the security plan the portions of the security plan
    that are relevant to the duties of that person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} review the security plan at least once every 12
    months after the day on which this section comes into force;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} amend the security plan if it does not reflect the
    most recent security risk assessment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} amend the security plan if deficiencies that could
    adversely impact the security of passenger rail transportation are
    identified in the security plan, including those identified during
    the security exercises;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} conduct a comprehensive review of the security plan
    within three years after the day on which this section comes into
    force and subsequently within three years from the date of
    completion of the last comprehensive review;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} notify the persons referred to in paragraph (a) of
    any amendments to the relevant portions of the security plan; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the security plan to the Minister
    within 30 days after the day on which this section comes into force
    or after a comprehensive review is conducted under subsection (e),
    and a copy of the amended portions of the security plan within 30
    days after an amendment is made under paragraph (c) or (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan training

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company, other than a small passenger company, must ensure
  that the following persons employed by, or acting on behalf of, the
  company undergo training on the components of the security plan
  referred to in paragraphs 7(3)(c) to (e), (g), (m), (n) and (q), and
  any other components that are relevant to the person's duties:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons responsible for the development and
    implementation of the plan or any portion of it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person with duties referred to in
    paragraph 7(3)(d) and for whom the training is considered necessary
    to ensure the effective implementation of the security plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of training

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure that the training is provided to those persons

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the day on which this section
    comes into force, unless those persons have received equivalent
    training on the security plan before that day;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 90 days after the day on which those persons
    initially assume the duties referred to in subsection (1), if their
    duties are assigned after the day on which this section comes into
    force; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a recurrent basis at least once every three
    years after the day on which those persons completed their previous
    training, including any equivalent training on the security plan
    received before the day on which this section comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Knowledge and skills

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure that persons, on completion of the training, have
  acquired the knowledge and skills required to carry out the duties
  referred to in subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure that, until those persons complete the training,
  they perform their duties under the close supervision of a person who
  has completed the training on the overall security plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training on amended plan

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, that amends its security plan in a way that significantly
  affects the security duties of a person referred to in subsection (1)
  must ensure that, within 30 days after the day on which the amendments
  are implemented, the person is provided with training on the
  amendments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training records

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must keep a training record for each person who has undergone
  the security plan training and must ensure that the record

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is kept up to date;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains the person's name and details of the most
    recent training they received under subsections (1) and (5),
    including the date, duration and title of the training and the
    components of the security plan that were covered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contains the title and the date of any previous
    security plan training taken by the person; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is retained for at least two years after the day on
    which the person ceases to be employed by, or ceases to act on
    behalf of, that company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of training materials

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure that a copy of the most recent training materials
  is kept.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Security Exercises]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262564 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operations-based security exercise

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  passenger company, other than a small passenger company, must carry
  out, at least once every five years after the day on which section 7
  comes into force or, in the case of a passenger company created after
  the day on which section 7 comes into force, within five years after
  the day of its creation, an operations-based security exercise that is
  designed to address acts or attempted acts of unlawful interference
  with passenger rail transportation and that tests

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effectiveness of the security plan and its
    implementation with respect to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the elements of the risk management strategy that
      are relevant to the scenario selected for the exercise,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the additional safeguards set out in the
      security plan that are relevant to the scenario selected for the
      exercise, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the process for responding to threats and other
      security concerns; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proficiency of persons in performing security
    duties that are relevant to the scenario selected for the exercise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed --- security exercises

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The implementation of safeguards in response to a
  heightened risk condition may be considered an operations-based
  security exercise if it tests

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the effectiveness of the security plan and its
    implementation with respect to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the elements of the risk management strategy that
      are relevant to the heightened risk condition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the additional security safeguards set out in
      the security plan that are relevant to the heightened risk
      condition, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the process for responding to the heightened
      risk condition; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proficiency of persons in performing security
    duties that are relevant to responding to the heightened risk
    condition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must give the Minister 45 days' notice of any
  operations-based security exercise that it plans to carry out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discussion-based security exercise

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must carry out a discussion-based security exercise at least
  once every year after the day on which section 7 comes into force or,
  in the case of a passenger company created after the day on which
  section 7 comes into force, from the day of its creation, in order to
  address acts or attempted acts of unlawful interference with passenger
  rail transportation and that tests the effectiveness of the security
  plan with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elements of the risk management strategy that
    are relevant to the scenario selected for the exercise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the additional safeguards set out in the security
    plan that are relevant to the scenario selected for the exercise;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the process for responding to security threats or
    other security concerns.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must give the Minister 45 days' notice of any
  discussion-based security exercise that it plans to carry out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} An operations-based security exercise carried out
  under subsection (1) or referred to in subsection (2) is considered to
  be a discussion-based security exercise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participants

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure, to the extent possible, that persons with
  security duties that are relevant to the scenario selected for the
  exercise participate in the exercise referred to in subsection (1) or
  (4). In addition, the company must invite host companies and law
  enforcement and emergency response agencies to participate in the
  exercise, if their participation is relevant to the scenario.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must create a record of each exercise carried out under
  subsections (1), (2) and (4) within 30 days after the date of the
  exercise and must ensure that the record contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the exercise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of participants;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of the companies and agencies that were
    invited;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the exercise scenario or, in the
    case of the exercise referred to in subsection (2), a description of
    the heightened risk condition;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the results of the exercise with
    respect to the elements tested in accordance with subsection
    (1), (2) or (4), as applicable; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of potential actions to address
    deficiencies that were identified during the exercise and that could
    adversely impact the security of passenger rail transportation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must ensure the record of each exercise is retained for three
  years after the date of the exercise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} A passenger company, other than a small passenger
  company, must implement any action to address deficiencies that were
  identified during the exercise and that could adversely impact the
  security of passenger rail transportation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1262605 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  these Regulations come into force on the day on which they are
  registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Three months after registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 2 and 5 come into force on the day that, in
  the third month after the month in which these Regulations are
  registered, has the same calendar number as the day on which they are
  registered or, if that third month has no day with that number, the
  last day of that third month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nine months after registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 6 and 7 come into force on the day that, in
  the ninth month after the month in which these Regulations are
  registered, has the same calendar number as the day on which they are
  registered or, if that ninth month has no day with that number, the
  last day of that ninth month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fifteen months after registration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 8 and 9 come into force on the day that, in
  the fifteenth month after the month in which these Regulations are
  registered, has the same calendar number as the day on which they are
  registered or, if that fifteenth month has no day with that number,
  the last day of that fifteenth month.
