## Solicitations by Mail Regulations {#solicitations-by-mail-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1295
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Regulations Respecting Solicitations for the Order of Goods or Services
::::

::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502373 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Solicitations by Mail
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1295).

## [Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502375 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where a letter or other
mailable matter that is not a bill, invoice or statement of account due
is in such a form that it has the general appearance of a bill, invoice
or statement of account due, it shall have endorsed on its face the
following notice:

"This is a solicitation for the order of (*goods, services or goods and
services, as the case may be*) and not a bill, invoice or statement of
account due. You are under no obligation to make any payments on account
of this offer unless you accept this offer."

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-285, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The notice referred to in
section 2 shall be printed in boldface capital letters in such a manner
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the print of such notice is no less conspicuous than
  the print of any other word on the letter or other mailable matter;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the size of the print of such notice is not smaller
  than the larger of the print of any other word on the letter or other
  mailable matter or 12 point type.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-285, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be a clear space of not less than 6 mm immediately surrounding
  the notice referred to in section 2 that is printed on a letter or
  other mailable matter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in section 2 shall be printed
  on a letter or other mailable matter in such a manner that the
  contrast between the background and the print of that notice is not
  less than the contrast between the background and the print of any
  other word on the face of the letter or other mailable matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-191, s. 1
- SOR/2008-285, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No letter or other mailable
matter referred to in section 2 shall state that it has been approved by
the Canada Post Corporation or that it conforms to any federal statute
or regulation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-840, s. 1
- SOR/2008-285, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No letter or other mailable
matter referred to in section 2 that does not comply with these
Regulations shall be sent by post.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-199, s. 8(F)
- SOR/2008-285, s. 2(F)
:::
