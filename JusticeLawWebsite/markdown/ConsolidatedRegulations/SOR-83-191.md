## National Energy Board Substituted Service Regulations {#national-energy-board-substituted-service-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-191
:::

[NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/N-7)

Registration 1983-02-25

Regulations Respecting Substituted Service of Notices Required to be
Served Under Sections 34, 87 and Subsection 104(2) of the National
Energy Board Act

P.C. 1983-528 1983-02-24
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to section 75.29
of the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed Regulations respecting substituted service of
notices required to be served under sections 29.1 and 75.26 of the
National Energy Board Act, made by the Minister of Energy, Mines and
Resources.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-877931 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Energy Board Substituted Service
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-191).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-877933 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[notice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[notice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a notice referred to in

[[personal service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [Substituted Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-877940 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where a company has been unable to effect personal
  service of a notice on a person after having made reasonable attempts
  to do so, the Board may, on application by the company, order
  substituted service of the notice on the person by one or more of the
  methods referred to in subsection 5(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not order substituted service of a
  notice on a person unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board is satisfied that personal service of the
    notice on the person is impractical in the circumstances; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information provided in accordance with
    paragraph 4(c) discloses that there is a reasonable possibility that
    substituted service of the notice on the person will bring the
    notice to the attention of that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-240, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for an order
under section 3 shall be made by filing with the Board five copies of a
written application, with evidence by affidavit disclosing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made to effect personal service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prejudice to any person that would result from
  further attempts at personal service; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the last known address of the person on whom a notice
  is required to be served, the address of the residence or place of
  business of the person or any other place thought to be frequented by
  the person, the names and addresses of any persons who may be in
  communication with the person, or any other information respecting
  where the person might be found.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-240, s. 2
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Substituted service of a notice may
  be effected by one or more of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} leaving the notice with an adult person at the
    residence or place of business of the person or at any other place
    thought to be frequented by the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} leaving the notice with any other adult person who
    may be in communication with the person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending the notice by registered mail to the last
    known address of the person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} publishing the notice in one or more publications
    in general circulation in the area where the person was last known
    to be or is thought to be; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other method that appears to the Board more
    likely to bring the notice to the attention of the person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/93-240, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-240, s. 2
:::

## [Service of Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-877964 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Unless the Board otherwise
directs, when a notice is served by substituted service it shall be
accompanied by a true copy of the order of the Board ordering such
service.

## [Notice Deemed Received]{.HTitleText1} {#h-877966 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A notice that is served by
the method or methods of substituted service ordered by the Board
pursuant to subsection 3(1) shall be deemed to have been received by the
person entitled thereto on the day following the day on which all the
methods of substituted service ordered by the Board have been carried
out or on such later day as the Board may fix in the order for
substituted service of that notice.
