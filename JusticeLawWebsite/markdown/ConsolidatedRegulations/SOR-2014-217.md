## [Rules of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806390 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions of
the Canada--Honduras Free Trade Agreement have the force of law in
Canada:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4.1 to 4.3;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 1 of Article 4.4;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Articles 4.5 to 4.13; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Annexes 4.1 and 4.5.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806396 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind9748_hq_14611){#fn_Ind9748_hq_14611-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 43 of the Canada --- Honduras Economic Growth and Prosperity
Act, chapter 14 of the Statues of Canada, 2014, comes into force, but if
these Regulations are registered after that day, they come into force on
the day on which they are registered.
