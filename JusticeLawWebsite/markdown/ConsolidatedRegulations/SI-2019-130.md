## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-130
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2019-12-11

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2019-1368 2019-11-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26069){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26069-ID0EECGA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26070){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26070-ID0EBCGA},
transfers from the Minister of La Francophonie to the Minister of
Economic Development and Official Languages

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
  La Francophonie under the [Canadian Tourism Commission
  Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26071){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26071-ID0EDABGA}
  that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C.
  2018-1016 of July 18, 2018[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26072){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26072-ID0EBABGA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
  La Francophonie under the [Official Languages
  Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26081){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26081-ID0EDAAGA}
  that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C.
  2018-1017 of July 18, 2018[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26082){#fn_81000-3-1911_hq_26082-ID0EBAAGA}.
