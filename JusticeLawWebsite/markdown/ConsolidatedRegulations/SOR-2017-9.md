## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Fees for the Examination of an Application in Respect of a Pest Control Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-843512 .Part}

### [Non-Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-843513 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 2

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application filed by a user of a pest control
  product or by a user group, to amend the registration of the pest
  control product by adding a minor use of the product to the label, and
  that meets the following criteria:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} some of the information that is required to
    accompany the application is generated either by the applicant or
    for the applicant by another person who is not the registrant, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by a statement by
    the registrant that they agree to add the new use to the label of
    the pest control product if the application is approved;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a request for a determination of equivalency made
  under section 38 of the [Pest Control Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-124);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application for an authorization to use a foreign
  product under subsection 41(1) of the [Pest Control Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-124); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of a type of application set out
  in Schedule 2 or 3, an application to register, or to amend the
  registration of, a pest control product whose active ingredient is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an organism that is not a microbial agent, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any of the following substances referred to in the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870):

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a food additive that is set out in column 1 of
      the [[Lists of Permitted Food Additives]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
      as defined in subsection B.01.001(1) of those regulations,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a nutritive substance that is used, recognized or
      commonly sold as food or as an ingredient of food,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a vitamin, mineral nutrient or amino acid,

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a flavouring preparation, natural extractive,
      oleoresin, seasoning or spice,

    - [(E)]{.lawlabel} a food packaging material or any substance of
      which such a material is composed, or

    - [(F)]{.lawlabel} a drug for veterinary use in animals that may be
      used as food for human consumption.

### [Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-843530 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration or amendment --- Schedule 1

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The fee
  for the examination by the Minister of an application to register a
  pest control product --- other than a type of application set out in
  Schedule 2 or 3 --- or to amend such a registration that includes a
  component set out in column 1 of Schedule 1 is the fee set out in
  column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one component --- Schedule 1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application referred to in subsection (1)
  includes more than one component set out in column 1 of Schedule 1,
  the fee is the sum of the applicable fees set out in column 2 for all
  of the included components.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Semiochemicals and microbial agents ---
  Schedule 2

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fee for the examination by the Minister of an
  application in respect of a pest control product that is a
  semiochemical or microbial agent for each type of application set out
  in column 1 of Schedule 2 is the fee set out in column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications filed together ---
  Schedule 2

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If more than one type of application set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 2 is filed in respect of the same pest control
  product at the same time, the fee for the examination by the Minister
  is the highest of the applicable fees set out in column 2 in respect
  of those applications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other applications --- Schedule 3

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The fee for the examination by the Minister of an
  application in respect of a pest control product for each type of
  application set out in column 1 of Schedule 3 is the fee set out in
  column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications filed together ---
  Schedule 3

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If more than one type of application set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 3 is filed in respect of the same pest control
  product at the same time, the fee for the examination by the Minister
  is the highest of the applicable fees set out in column 2 in respect
  of those applications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Processing

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The fee for the processing
by the Minister of any of the following applications, other than an
application to renew a registration, is \$1,133:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application in respect of a pest control product
  that is described in paragraph 1(d);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application that includes a component set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 1; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an application in respect of a pest control product
  that does not include any component set out in Schedule 1, that is not
  mentioned in Schedule 2 or 3 and for which no information other than
  what is already available to the Minister is required to conduct an
  evaluation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications not mentioned in schedules

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The fee for the examination
by the Minister of an application in respect of a pest control product,
other than an application to renew a registration, that does not include
any component set out in Schedule 1, that is not mentioned in Schedule 2
or 3 and that does not require an evaluation is \$247.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The fee for the examination
by the Minister of an application to renew the registration of a pest
control product is \$80.

### [Annual Adjustment of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-843553 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee adjustment

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every fee set out in this
Part is to be increased on April 1 of each year by 2%, rounded up to the
nearest dollar.

### [Timing of Payment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-843556 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaning of [[preliminary
review]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[preliminary review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a review by
  the Minister of the information that is submitted with an application
  to determine whether the information is sufficient to enable the
  Minister to conduct an evaluation of the pest control product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- \$2,500 or less

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the total fee for an application under this Part
  is \$2,500 or less, it must be paid when the application is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- more than \$2,500

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} With the exception of fees for applications made in
  respect of paragraphs 1(a), 1(b), and 3(a) of Schedule 3, which must
  be paid in full at the time the application is filed, when the total
  fee for an application under this Part is more than \$2,500,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 10% must be paid when the application is filed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 25% is payable on receipt of a notice from the
    Minister that the application has been accepted for a preliminary
    review; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 65% is payable on receipt of a notice from the
    Minister that the application has been accepted for evaluation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary review and evaluation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The preliminary review and the evaluation begin on
  receipt of the relevant payments.

### [Fee Reduction]{.HTitleText2} {#h-843569 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[actual gross revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[actual gross revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount that
      the person who makes an application to register a pest control
      product referred to in subsection 2(1) collects during the fee
      verification period from the sales in Canada of that
      product. ([recettes brutes réelles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[anticipated gross revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[anticipated gross revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
      that the person who makes an application to register a pest
      control product referred to in subsection 2(1) expects to collect
      during the fee verification period from the sales in Canada of
      that product. ([recettes brutes prévues]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[fee verification period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fee verification period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      pest control product for which an application for a fee reduction
      is approved under subsection (3), means the period that begins on
      the day on which the pest control product is first sold in Canada
      as a registered pest control product or as an active ingredient in
      a registered pest control product and that ends three years after
      that day. ([période de vérification des droits]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[sales in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sales in Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application to register an
        active ingredient, the sales in Canada of all pest control
        products that contain that active ingredient and that became
        registered during the fee verification period;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application to register a
        pest control product that is not an active ingredient, the sales
        in Canada of that product during the fee verification period;
        and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of more than one application that
        includes a common component set out in Schedule 1 and that is
        filed at the same time to register pest control products that
        contain the same active ingredient, the sales in Canada of those
        pest control products during the fee verification
        period. ([ventes au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for fee reduction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who files an application to register a pest
  control product referred to in subsection 2(1) may apply at the same
  time to the Minister for a reduction of the fee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must approve a fee reduction if the
  following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person provides to the Minister with their
    application

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement signed by an individual who is
      designated by the person for the purpose that indicates the
      person's anticipated gross revenue and that certifies that the fee
      is greater than 10% of that anticipated gross revenue, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} information that establishes that that fee is
      greater than 10% of the person's anticipated gross revenue; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines, on the basis of the
    information provided under paragraph (a) and any other information
    that is available to the Minister, that the fee is likely to be
    greater than 10% of the person's actual gross revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recalculation of fee

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister approves a fee reduction, the fee
  that is payable at the end of the fee verification period is the
  lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total fee payable under this Part, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the higher of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the fee referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the actual gross revenue, based on the
      records of the sales in Canada provided to the Minister in
      accordance with subsection (6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment when application is filed

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person who applies for a fee reduction under
  subsection (2) must, at the time of filing the application, pay, in
  accordance with subsection 7(2) or (3), the higher of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 10% of their anticipated gross revenue, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10% of the total fee payable under this Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of sales in Canada

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Within 60 days after the end of the fee verification
  period, the person must provide the Minister with the records of the
  sales in Canada, prepared in accordance with generally accepted
  accounting principles and certified by the individual responsible for
  the person's financial affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconciliation --- balance owing

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the records of the sales in Canada establish that
  the amount that the person paid under subsection (5) is less than the
  amount of the fee calculated in accordance with subsection (4), the
  person must pay the balance within 60 days after the day on which the
  fee verification period ends.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconciliation --- overpayment

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the records of the sales in Canada establish that
  the amount that the person paid under subsection (5) is more than the
  amount of the fee calculated in accordance with subsection (4), the
  Minister must repay the amount of the overpayment to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audited records

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines, on the basis of any
  information that is available to him or her, that the certified
  records of the sales in Canada are not adequate to determine the
  person's actual gross revenue, the Minister may require that the
  person provide records of the sales in Canada that have been audited
  by a qualified independent auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When records inconsistent

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} If there is any inconsistency between the certified
  records and the audited records, the fee is to be based on whichever
  records show the higher amount of sales in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When records not submitted

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not submit the records in
  accordance with subsections (6) and (9), the fee payable is the total
  fee calculated under subsections 2(1) and (2) and section 3, minus any
  portion of the fee that the person paid under subsection (5).

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Annual Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-843610 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payable annually

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  registrant must pay each year, in respect of every pest control
  product that is registered in their name on April 1 of the year, an
  annual charge that is the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$3,600, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 4% of the actual gross revenue during the
    registrant's preceding fiscal year, but not less than \$100.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual charge is payable

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of a notice from the Minister requiring
    payment; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in four equal quarterly payments, the first of
    which is payable on receipt of a notice from the Minister requiring
    payment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the annual charge is paid under paragraph
  (1)(b), the registrant must, when paying it in full or when making the
  first quarterly instalment, provide the Minister with the records of
  the sales in Canada for the fiscal year referred to in that paragraph,
  prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles
  and certified by the individual responsible for the registrant's
  financial affairs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audited records

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines, on the basis of any
  information available to him or her, that the certified records are
  not adequate to calculate the annual charge, the Minister may require
  that the registrant provide records of the sales in Canada that have
  been audited by a qualified independent auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When records inconsistent

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If there is any inconsistency between the certified
  records and the audited records, the calculation of the annual charge
  is to be based on whichever records show the higher amount of sales in
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When records not submitted

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the registrant does not submit the records in
  accordance with subsections (3) and (4), the annual charge that is
  payable by the registrant is \$3,600.
