## Hunter Douglas Canada Remission Order {#hunter-douglas-canada-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-353
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2005-11-21

Hunter Douglas Canada Remission Order

P.C. 2005-2066 2005-11-21
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, pursuant to
section 115 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Hunter Douglas Canada Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-353).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  36
  :::
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721099 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is granted to Hunter Douglas Canada of the amounts of
anti-dumping duties set out in column 3 of the schedule, totalling
\$103,527.64, that were paid or are payable under the [Special Import
Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) in respect of certain wood slats from the
People's Republic of China that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} were processed in the United States before being
  imported into Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} were accounted for on the dates set out in column 2
  of the schedule under the accounting document numbers set out in
  column 1 of the schedule.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721103 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the day on
which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721105 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
