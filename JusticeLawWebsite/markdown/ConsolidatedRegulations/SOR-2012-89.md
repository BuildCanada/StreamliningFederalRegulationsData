## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792147 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Permit.

[[eligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible destination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Argentina,
    Australia, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, the Czech Republic, Denmark,
    Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Ireland, Italy, Japan,
    Luxembourg, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Poland, Portugal,
    the Republic of Korea, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey, Ukraine,
    the United Kingdom or the United States. ([destination
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
    of the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).
    ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792151 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 3 and 4, any resident of Canada may, under this Permit,
  export from Canada to any eligible destination any good or technology
  referred to in Group 3 of the schedule to the [Export Control
  List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) with the exception of any good or
  technology referred to in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any other Group of that schedule; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} item 3-2.1.1 or 3-2.1.2 of the Guide.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply if the export of the
  good or technology referred to in any other Group of the schedule to
  the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) is authorized
  by a permit issued under subsection 7(1.1) of the [Export and Import
  Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Permit does not
authorize

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the export of goods or technology to any country
  listed in the [Area Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the export of goods or technology that are not
  intended for end-use in an eligible destination.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of this
Permit that the exporter

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before making their first export in a calendar year
  under this Permit, provide in writing to the Export Controls Division
  of the Department of Foreign Affairs and International Trade their
  name, address, telephone number and any facsimile number and
  electronic mail address and, in the case of a corporation, the name of
  a contact person and their address, telephone number and any facsimile
  and electronic mail address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} present to the Canada Border Services Agency, before
  or at the time of export, a licence issued by the Canadian Nuclear
  Safety Commission pursuant to the [Nuclear Safety and Control
  Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) that authorizes the export of the goods or
  technology;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} retain, for a period of six years after the year in
  which an export is made under this Permit, the following records:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the item in the Guide in which the good or
    technology exported is described,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of each good or technology
    exported,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of export,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the eligible destination to which the good or
    technology is being exported, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person to whom the good
    or technology is being exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Export Controls Division, within 15
  days after the receipt of its request, the following information in
  respect of any exports made under this Permit during the period
  specified in the request:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of each good or technology exported
    and the item in the Guide in which it is described, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of each good or technology
    exported by country of destination; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} insert the term "GEP-43" or "LGE-43" in the
  appropriate field of the form prescribed under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) if the good exported under this Permit is
  required to be reported under that Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-792173 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Permit comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
