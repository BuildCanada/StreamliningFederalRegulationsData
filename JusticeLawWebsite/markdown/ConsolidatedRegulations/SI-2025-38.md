## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-emergency-preparedness-to-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-section-7-of-the-emergency-management-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

[EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/E-4.56)

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness)
to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Powers,
Duties and Functions Under Section 7 of the Emergency Management Act

P.C. 2025-418 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of State (Emergency Preparedness) to the
Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the powers, duties
and functions under section 7 of the [Emergency Management
Act](/eng/acts/E-4.56)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C.
2024-1183 of November 8, 2024[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2844_hq_1741802881){#fn_81000-3-2844_hq_1741802881-ID0EBEAA}.
