### Rules of provincial and territorial courts made under sections 482, 482.1 and 745.64 of the Criminal Code

The Rules of the Court of Appeal of Quebec in Criminal Matters
(SI/2018-96) were replaced with the Rules of the Court of Appeal of
Quebec in Criminal Matters pursuant to sections 482 and 482.1 of the
Criminal Code (R.S.C. 1985, c. C-46) (Cr.C.), by the judges of the Court
of Appeal, and came into force on March 11, 2024. These new Rules are
published on the Court of Appeal of Quebec website. The previous Rules
of the Court of Appeal of Quebec in Criminal Matters (SI/2018-96)
continue nevertheless to apply to certain ongoing proceedings pursuant
to section 88 of the new Rules of the Court of Appeal of Quebec in
Criminal Matters. If you wish to consult previous versions of the rules
published on the Justice Laws Website, please see [Previous
Versions](PITIndex.html).

As of September 19, 2019, the Department of Justice Canada no longer
publishes on its Justice Laws Website the rules made, by provincial and
territorial courts, under sections 482 and 482.1 of the Criminal Code.
In addition, the Statutory Instruments Act no longer applies to rules
established for the purposes of sections 745.6 and 745.63 of the
Criminal Code. For more information, please consult [hyperlinks to legal
information websites](/eng/Court/).
