## Order Designating the Prime Minister as Presiding Minister for the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat {#order-designating-the-prime-minister-as-presiding-minister-for-the-public-appointments-commission-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-62
:::

Registration 2006-05-03

Order Designating the Prime Minister as Presiding Minister for the
Public Appointments Commission Secretariat

P.C. 2006-227 2006-04-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby orders that the Prime Minister shall preside
over the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat.
