## [Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Spontaneous reignition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A candle must not reignite
spontaneously after it is extinguished.
