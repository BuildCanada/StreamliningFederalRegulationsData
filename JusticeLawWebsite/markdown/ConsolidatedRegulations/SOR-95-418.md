## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975794 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Persons Authorized to Account for Casual Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-418).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975796 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has
    obtained an authorization pursuant to subsection 3(3); ([personne
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any goods imported into
    Canada other than goods imported for sale or for any commercial,
    industrial, occupational, institutional or other like use;
    ([marchandises occasionnelles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial carrier that is
    engaged in scheduled international transportation of shipments of
    goods other than goods imported as mail;
    ([messager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy Minister
    or a Director General of the Department of National Revenue. ([agent
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization of Couriers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975803 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  courier is authorized to account for casual goods released in
  accordance with subsection 32(4) of the Act in lieu of the importer or
  owner thereof on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the courier is a bonded carrier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the courier has given the security required under
    paragraph 7.3(b) of the [Accounting for Imported Goods and Payment
    of Duties Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the courier has obtained an authorization pursuant
    to subsection (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any courier, except a courier that has had an
  authorization revoked pursuant to paragraph 4(b) or section 5 within
  the previous 12 months, may make a written application to the Minister
  or a designated officer for the issuance of an authorization to
  account for casual goods and shall include the following information
  with the application:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the courier is a bonded carrier; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking given by the courier to the Minister
    or the designated officer that the courier will give the security
    required under paragraph 7.3(b) of the [Accounting for Imported
    Goods and Payment of Duties
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or a designated officer shall, after
  reviewing an application made under subsection (2), issue to the
  courier an authorization to account for casual goods released in
  accordance with subsection 32(4) of the Act in lieu of the importer or
  owner thereof.

## [Revocation of Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975813 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister or a
designated officer shall revoke an authorization issued under subsection
3(3) where the authorized person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requests in writing that the authorization be
  revoked; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, the
Minister or a designated officer may revoke an authorization issued
under subsection 3(3) where the authorized person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has made a misrepresentation in an application made
  under subsection 3(2);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has contravened the provisions of an Act of
  Parliament, or any regulation made pursuant thereto, that prohibits,
  controls or regulates the importation or exportation of goods; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has made a misrepresentation as an authorized person
  in business dealings with an importer, an importer\'s agent, Her
  Majesty or servants of Her Majesty.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or a designated officer shall, before revoking an
  authorization under section 5, give to the authorized person 90 days
  notice of the proposed revocation and provide the authorized person
  with all relevant information concerning the grounds on which the
  proposed revocation of the authorization is based.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized person may, within 90 days after the
  day on which the notice referred to in subsection (1) is given, make
  representations to the Minister or a designated officer regarding why
  the authorization should not be revoked.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975824 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every authorized person
shall, in accordance with subsection 40(3) of the Act, in respect of
casual goods, keep, for a period of six years following the importation
of the casual goods, all records that relate to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the origin, importation and value of the casual
  goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of casual goods that have not been
  delivered to the importer or owner,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the destruction of the casual goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exportation of the casual goods; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any application made by the authorized person for an
  advance ruling under subsection 43.1(1) of the Act in respect of the
  casual goods.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to in
section 7 may be kept on microfilm by means of any photographic or
microphotographic process that is in accordance with National Standard
of Canada CAN2-72.11-79, Microfilm as Documentary Evidence, published by
the Canadian General Standards Board in August 1979, as amended from
time to time.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The records referred to in
section 7 may be kept on machine-sensible data media if the media can be
related back to the supporting source documents and are supported by a
system capable of producing accessible and readable copy.

## [Notification of Changes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975835 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every authorized person
shall, within two weeks after the change, notify in writing the Minister
or a designated officer of any change in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the address of that authorized person\'s business
  office;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that authorized person\'s legal or business name; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the ownership of the business.
