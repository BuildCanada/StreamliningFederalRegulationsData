## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767244 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Chemical Weapons Convention
      Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-27.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CAS registry number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      identification number assigned to a chemical substance by the
      Chemical Abstracts Service, a division of the American Chemical
      Society. ([numéro d'enregistrement CAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the address,
      telephone number and, if applicable, fax number and e-mail
      address. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[discrete organic chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[discrete organic chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      chemical that belongs to the class of chemical compounds
      consisting of all compounds of carbon except for carbon monoxide,
      carbon dioxide, carbon disulfide, carbonyl sulfide and metal
      carbonates. Also excluded from this definition are oligomers and
      polymers --- whether or not they contain phosphorus, sulfur or
      fluorine --- and chemicals that contain only carbon and
      metal. ([produit chimique organique défini]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[IUPAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IUPAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Union of
      Pure and Applied Chemistry. ([UICPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[other facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[other facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plant site, plant
      or unit, other than the single small-scale facility, where
      activities authorized under the [Schedule 1 Chemicals Regulations
      (Chemical Weapons Convention)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-155) are
      carried out. ([autre installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[PSF chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[PSF chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a discrete organic
      chemical that contains phosphorus, sulfur or fluorine. ([produit
      PSF]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Schedule 1 chemica1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Schedule 1 chemica1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a toxic chemical
      or precursor listed in Schedule 1 of the Schedules of Chemicals
      set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention. ([produit
      chimique du tableau 1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Schedule 2 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Schedule 2 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a toxic chemical
      or precursor listed in Schedule 2 of the Schedules of Chemicals
      set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention. ([produit
      chimique du tableau 2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Schedule 3 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Schedule 3 chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a toxic chemical
      or precursor listed in Schedule 3 of the Schedules of Chemicals
      set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention. ([produit
      chimique du tableau 3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[single small-scale facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[single small-scale facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      single small-scale facility referred to in paragraph 19(1)(a) of
      the [Schedule 1 Chemicals Regulations (Chemical Weapons
      Convention)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-155) or any facility
      replacing it or intended to replace it. ([installation unique à
      petite échelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, all words and
  expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning as in the
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic transmission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any notice given or declaration made under these
  Regulations may be given or made by electronic means.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Schedule 1 Chemicals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767263 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767264 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to any
activity authorized under the [Schedule 1 Chemicals Regulations
(Chemical Weapons Convention)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-155).

### [Initial Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767267 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single small-scale facility

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the information has already been submitted to the National Authority,
  any person that, at the time of the coming into force of these
  Regulations, is carrying out an activity at the single small-scale
  facility under an authorization referred to in subsection 8(1) of the
  Act must make, within 90 days after the day on which these Regulations
  come into force, an initial declaration to the National Authority that
  is signed and dated by the declarant or, in the case of a corporation,
  by its authorized representative and that contains the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
    information of the authorized representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the facility and its address or, if
    there is no address, a description of its precise location; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed technical description of the facility,
    including an inventory of equipment and detailed diagrams.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declarant must notify the National Authority in
  writing, and within the following time frames, of any change to the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), no
    later than 7 days after the day of the change; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that referred to in paragraphs (1)(c) and (d), not
    less than 200 days before the change is to be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proposed facility

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that intends to carry out an activity at the single small-scale
  facility or an other facility under an authorization referred to in
  subsection 8(1) of the Act must make, not less than 200 days before
  operations are to begin, an initial declaration to the National
  Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or, in the case of
  a corporation, by its authorized representative and that contains,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the single small-scale facility, the
    information set out in paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (d); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an other facility, the information
    set out in paragraphs 3(1)(a) to (c), a detailed technical
    description of the facility and the purposes for which it will be
    operated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declarant must notify the National Authority in
  writing, and within the following time frames, of any change to the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that referred to in paragraphs 3(1)(a) and (b), no
    later than 7 days after the day of the change; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that referred to in paragraphs 3(1)(c) and (d), the
    detailed technical description of an other facility and the purposes
    for which an other facility will be operated, not less than 200 days
    before the change is to be made.

### [Annual Declaration of Past Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767288 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single small-scale facility or other
facility

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any person that, during a
calendar year, carries out an activity at the single small-scale
facility or an other facility under an authorization referred to in
subsection 8(1) of the Act must make, no later than February 28 of the
following year, an annual declaration of the calendar year's activities
to the National Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or,
in the case of a corporation, by its authorized representative and that
contains the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the facility and its address or, if there
  is no address, a description of its precise location;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical referred to in the
  declaration,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number or, if no
    CAS registry number has been assigned, its structural formula,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the quantity produced and, in the
    case of the single small-scale facility or an other facility that is
    licensed to produce Schedule 1 chemicals for protective purposes,
    the production methods employed,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the quantity consumed and the
    purposes for which it was consumed,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, for each receipt of a Schedule 1
    chemical from the single small-scale facility or from one of the
    persons referred to in section 11 of the [Schedule 1 Chemicals
    Regulations (Chemical Weapons
    Convention)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-155) or each shipment of a
    Schedule 1 chemical to that facility or to one of those persons, as
    the case may be, the quantity received or shipped, the purposes for
    which it was received or shipped and the facility or person from
    which it was received or to which it was shipped,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the maximum quantity stored at any time during the
    year, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the quantity stored at the end of the year;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for each precursor listed in Schedule 1, 2 or 3 of
  the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the
  Convention that was used for the production of the Schedule 1
  chemical,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number or, if no
    CAS registry number has been assigned, its structural formula, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its quantity consumed;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any changes at the facility compared to,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the single small-scale facility, its
    detailed technical description, including the inventory of equipment
    and detailed diagrams, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an other facility, its detailed
    technical description; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the declarant no longer carries out activities
  under an authorization referred to in subsection 8(1) of the Act, the
  date of the cessation of activities.

### [Annual Declaration of Anticipated Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767308 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single small-scale facility or other

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the facility and its address or, if there
  is no address, a description of its precise location;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 1 chemical referred to in the
  declaration,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number or, if no
    CAS registry number has been assigned, its structural formula, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity that is anticipated to be produced
    and the purposes for which it will be produced and, in the case of
    an other facility, the calendar yearly quarter during which the
    production is anticipated to take place; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any changes that are anticipated at the facility

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the single small-scale facility, its

### [Notice and Declaration Regarding Exports and Imports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767320 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any person that anticipates
exporting or importing a Schedule 1 chemical under an authorization
referred to in subsection 8(2) of the Act must notify the National
Authority in writing not less than 45 days before the export or import.
The notice must be signed and dated by the person that gives the notice
or, in the case of a corporation, by its authorized representative and
must contain the following information:

  gives the notice;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the IUPAC name of the Schedule 1 chemical and its CAS
  registry number or, if no CAS registry number has been assigned, its
  structural formula; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as the case may be, each of the quantities of each

  expressed in grams or in kilograms, the country of final destination
  or the country from which the chemical will be imported, the name and
  address of the facility in question that is located in that country,
  the purposes for which the chemical will be exported or imported and
  the date of the export or import.

authorized representative and that contains the following information:

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the IUPAC name of the Schedule 1 chemical and its CAS
  registry number or, if no CAS registry number has been assigned, its
  structural formula; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as the case may be, each of the quantities of each

  the chemical was imported, the name and address of the facility in

  chemical was exported or imported and the date of the export or
  import.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Schedule 2 Chemicals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767333 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767334 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  9 to 12 apply to plant sites that consist of one or more plants where,
  during a calendar year, an activity referred to in section 11 of the
  Act, other than export or import, is carried out with respect to any
  Schedule 2 chemical described below in excess of the following
  applicable quantities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 100 grams of any Schedule 2 chemical marked by an
    asterisk (\*) in Part A of Schedule 2 of the Schedules of Chemicals
    set out in the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention, with the
    exception of those that constitute less than 0.5% of the total
    weight of a mixture;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 10 kg of any other Schedule 2 chemical listed in
    Part A of Schedule 2 of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the
    Annex on Chemicals in the Convention, with the exception of those
    that constitute less than 0.5% of the total weight of a mixture; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 100 kg of any Schedule 2 chemical listed in Part B
    of Schedule 2 of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in the Annex on
    Chemicals in the Convention, with the exception of those that
    constitute less than 10% of the total weight of a mixture.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the quantities referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) relate only to the weight of a Schedule 2
  chemical contained in a mixture and not to the weight of the mixture
  itself.

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of its owner or of the corporation or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the number of plants within the

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each plant referred to in section 9 that is
  located within the plant site,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, precise location within the plant site

    in which it is located,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of its owner or of the corporation or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} of whether it is dedicated to those activities or
      is multi-purpose, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} of whether it carries out other activities with

      indication of whether it was sold or transferred to another

      production, processing or consumption.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Schedule 3 Chemicals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767392 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767393 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Sections 15 to 18 apply
to plant sites that consist of one or more plants where, during a
calendar year, more than three tonnes of a Schedule 3 chemical are
produced, with the exception of those chemicals that constitute less
than 25% of the total weight of a mixture.

### [Initial Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767396 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Unless the information
has already been submitted to the National Authority, any person that,
during a calendar year that falls within the period beginning on January
1, 1996 and ending at the end of the calendar year before the coming
into force of these Regulations, produced a Schedule 3 chemical at a
plant site must make, within 90 days after the day on which these
Regulations come into force, an initial declaration to the National
Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or, in the case of a
corporation, by its authorized representative and that contains, for
each calendar year referred to in this section during which such
production took place, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each plant site,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, address and precise location,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of its owner or of the corporation or
    enterprise operating it, as the case may be, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the number of plants within the
    plant site that are referred to in a declaration made under Part 3;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each plant referred to in section 15 that is
  located within the plant site,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, precise location within the plant site
    and, if applicable, the name or number of the building or structure
    in which it is located,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of its owner or of the corporation or
    enterprise operating it, as the case may be, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the main activities that are carried out; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for each Schedule 3 chemical referred to in section
  15 that is produced in a quantity above the threshold set out in that
  section,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number or, if no
    CAS registry number has been assigned, its structural formula,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity produced, expressed in tonnes,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the purposes for which it was produced.

### [Annual Declaration of Past Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767413 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that, during a calendar year, produces a Schedule 3 chemical at
  a plant site must make, no later than February 28 of the following
  year, an annual declaration of the calendar year's activities to the
  National Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or, in
  the case of a corporation, by its authorized representative and that
  contains the information set out in paragraphs 16(a) to (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of production activities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the declarant no longer produces Schedule 3
  chemicals, the declarant must make their declaration to the National
  Authority within 30 days after the cessation of production activities.
  In addition to the information referred to in subsection (1), the
  declaration must contain the date of the cessation.

### [Annual Declaration of Anticipated Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767419 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that anticipates producing, during the following calendar year,
  more than three tonnes of a Schedule 3 chemical at a plant site must
  make, no later than September 15 of the current year, an annual
  declaration of the activities that are anticipated to be carried out
  during the calendar year to the National Authority that is signed and
  dated by the declarant or, in the case of a corporation, by its
  authorized representative and that contains, in addition to the
  information set out in paragraphs 16(a) to (d), the following
  information for each Schedule 3 chemical referred to in the
  declaration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its IUPAC name and CAS registry number or, if no
    CAS registry number has been assigned, its structural formula;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity that is anticipated to be
    produced, expressed in tonnes; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purposes for which it will be produced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of additional production
  activities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declarant must notify the National Authority in
  writing of any additional production activity that is anticipated to
  be carried out during the year referred to in the declaration not less
  than 15 days before the production activity begins.

### [Declarations Regarding Exports and Imports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767428 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial declaration

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Unless the information
has already been submitted to the National Authority, any person that,
during a calendar year that falls within the period beginning on January
1, 1996 and ending at the end of the calendar year before the coming
into force of these Regulations, exported or imported any quantity of a
Schedule 3 chemical referred to in section 15 must make, within 90 days
after the day on which these Regulations come into force, an initial
declaration to the National Authority that is signed and dated by the
declarant or, in the case of a corporation, by its authorized
representative and that contains, for each calendar year referred to in
this section during which an activity referred to in this section was
carried out, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} as the case may be, each of the quantities of each
  Schedule 3 chemical exported or imported, expressed in kilograms or in
  tonnes, and the country of final destination or the country from which
  the chemical was imported.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual declaration of past activities

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Any person that, during a
calendar year, exports or imports any quantity of a Schedule 3 chemical
referred to in section 15 must make, no later than February 28 of the
following year, an annual declaration of the calendar year's activities
to the National Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or,
in the case of a corporation, by its authorized representative and that
contains the information set out in paragraphs 19(a) to (c).

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Discrete Organic Chemicals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767436 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767437 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), this Part
  applies to plant sites

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where, during a calendar year, more than 200 tonnes
    of discrete organic chemicals are produced by synthesis; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that consist of one or more plants where, during a
    calendar year, more than 30 tonnes of a PSF chemical are produced by
    synthesis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply to plant sites where a
  person produces exclusively explosives, hydrocarbons or alcoholic
  beverages.

### [Initial Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767445 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Unless the information
has already been submitted to the National Authority, any person that,
during a calendar year that falls within the period beginning on January
1, 1996 and ending at the end of the calendar year before the coming
into force of these Regulations, produced by synthesis a discrete
organic chemical at a plant site must make, within 90 days after the day
on which these Regulations come into force, an initial declaration to
the National Authority that is signed and dated by the declarant or, in
the case of a corporation, by its authorized representative and that
contains, for each calendar year referred to in this section during
which such production took place, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
  information of the authorized representative;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each plant site,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, address and precise location,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of its owner or of the corporation or
    enterprise operating it, as the case may be,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the main activities that were carried out, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of plants within the plant site where a
    discrete organic chemical was produced by synthesis;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the approximate aggregate quantity of discrete
  organic chemicals that were produced by synthesis during the calendar
  year, expressed in one of the following ranges:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} more than 200 tonnes but less than 1 000 tonnes,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1 000 tonnes or more but less than or equal to 10
    000 tonnes, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 000 tonnes; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if a PSF chemical was produced by synthesis,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of plants within the plant site where a
    PSF chemical was produced by synthesis, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each of those plants, the approximate
    aggregate quantity of PSF chemicals that were produced by synthesis
    there during the calendar year, expressed in one of the following
    ranges:

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} more than 30 tonnes but less than 200 tonnes,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} 200 tonnes or more but less than 1 000 tonnes,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} 1 000 tonnes or more but less than or equal to 10
      000 tonnes, or

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 000 tonnes.

### [Annual Declaration of Past Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767466 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that, during a calendar year, produces by synthesis a discrete
  organic chemical at a plant site must make, no later than February 28
  of the following year, an annual declaration of the calendar year's
  activities to the National Authority that is signed and dated by the
  declarant or, in the case of a corporation, by its authorized
  representative and that contains the information set out in paragraphs
  22(a) to (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of production activities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the declarant no longer produces by synthesis
  discrete organic chemicals, the declarant must make their declaration
  to the National Authority within 30 days after the cessation of
  production activities. In addition to the information referred to in
  subsection (1), the declaration must contain the date of the
  cessation.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Riot Control Agents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767472 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767473 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not apply
to riot control agents that are intended to be used by an individual for
personal defence.

### [Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-767476 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the information has already been submitted to the National Authority,
  any person that holds a riot control agent for riot control purposes
  must make, within 90 days after the day on which these Regulations
  come into force or of the beginning of the holding, as the case may
  be, a declaration to the National Authority that is signed and dated
  by the declarant or, in the case of a corporation, by its authorized
  representative and that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the declarant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, position and contact
    information of the authorized representative; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each riot control agent, its IUPAC name and CAS
    registry number or, if no CAS registry number has been assigned, its
    structural formula.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The declarant must notify the National Authority in
  writing of any changes to the information contained in their
  declaration within 30 days after the day of the change.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Retention of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767485 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the application of paragraph 11(b) of the Act, the prescribed
  documents are those related to the carrying out of any of the
  activities referred to in section 11 of the Act, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inventory and accounting records;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} operational records;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} chemical waste disposal records;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} records of receipt and inventory of raw materials
    and catalysts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} batch records or cards and quality control reports;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} records of transfers of toxic chemicals or their
    precursors listed in one of the Schedules of Chemicals set out in
    the Annex on Chemicals in the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of retention

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents must be kept and maintained for at
  least five years after the day on which they are created.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
