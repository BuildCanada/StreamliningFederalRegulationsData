## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826897 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    chlorofluorocarbon. ([CFC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a use of methyl bromide
    that conforms to Decision IX/6 set out in the document entitled
    Report of the Ninth Meeting of the Parties to the Montreal Protocol
    on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer, published by the Ozone
    Secretariat, United Nations Environment Programme. ([utilisation
    critique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a decision adopted at a
    meeting of the Parties held under Article 11 of the
    Protocol. ([Décision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[emergency use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a use of up to 20 tonnes
    of methyl bromide, in response to an emergency event, that conforms
    to Decision IX/6 set out in the document entitled Report of the
    Ninth Meeting of the Parties to the Montreal Protocol on Substances
    that Deplete the Ozone Layer, published by the Ozone Secretariat,
    United Nations Environment Programme. ([utilisation
    d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[essential use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[essential use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a use, other than a
    laboratory or analytical use, that conforms to Decision IV/25 set
    out in the document entitled Report of the Fourth Meeting of the
    Parties to the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the
    Ozone Layer, published by the Ozone Secretariat, United Nations
    Environment Programme. ([utilisation essentielle]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[feedstock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feedstock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that is used ---
    and the molecular structure of which is transformed --- in the
    manufacture of a chemical substance. ([matière
    première]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foaming agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foaming agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical that is added

    that gas cells are formed throughout the plastic. ([agent de
    gonflement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[HBFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HBFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    hydrobromofluorocarbon. ([HBFC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[HCFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HCFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    hydrochlorofluorocarbon. ([HCFC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[HFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HFC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    hydrofluorocarbon. ([HFC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[laboratory or analytical use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[laboratory or analytical use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a use
    that is agreed to be a laboratory or analytical use through a
    Decision of the Parties. ([utilisation en laboratoire ou à des fins
    d'analyse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a State that has ratified the
    Protocol or that meets the conditions referred to in paragraph 8 of
    Article 4 of the Protocol. ([Partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plastic foam]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plastic foam]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plastic the weight per
    unit of volume of which is decreased substantially by the use of a
    foaming agent during the manufacturing process. ([mousse
    plastique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pre-shipment application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pre-shipment application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treatment
    with methyl bromide, within 21 days prior to export, of a commodity
    or a product that is to be entirely exported to another country, or
    of a means of conveyance, in order to meet a requirement of the
    importing country or a requirement of Canadian law. ([traitement
    préalable à l'expédition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Montreal Protocol on
    Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer, published by the United
    Nations Environment Programme and signed by Canada on September 16,
    1987, in its most recent version. ([Protocole]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[quarantine application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quarantine application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treatment
    with methyl bromide of a commodity, product, facility or means of
    conveyance, when the treatment is intended to prevent the spread of,
    or to control or eradicate, pests of quarantine significance in
    order to meet a requirement of the importing country or a
    requirement of Canadian law. ([traitement en
    quarantaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reclaimed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reclaimed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a substance,
    recovered and then reprocessed and upgraded through a process such
    as filtering, drying, distillation or chemical treatment to restore
    the substance to industry-accepted reuse
    standards. ([régénérée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recovered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recovered]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a substance
    used and subsequently collected. ([récupérée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[recycled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recycled]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a substance,
    recovered, cleaned through a process such as filtering or drying and
    reused, including reused to recharge
    equipment. ([recyclée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[rigid foam product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[rigid foam product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product
    containing or consisting of any of the following types of foam:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} closed-cell rigid polyurethane foam, including
      one- and two-component froth, pour, spray, injected or
      bead-applied foam and polyisocyanurate foam;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} closed-cell rigid polystyrene boardstock foam;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} closed-cell rigid phenolic foam;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} closed-cell rigid polyethylene foam that is
      suitable in shape, thickness and design to be used as a product
      that provides thermal insulation in heating, plumbing or
      refrigeration systems or industrial processes. ([produit en mousse
      rigide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of Canada's obligations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to implement Canada's obligations under the Protocol by
setting out rules concerning certain ozone-depleting substances and
certain products containing or designed to contain ozone-depleting
substances. These Regulations also set out rules concerning halocarbon
alternatives.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826928 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} substances --- including their isomers --- whether
  existing alone or in a mixture, that are set out in Schedule 1 and
  specified on the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1 to the Act;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} products containing or designed to contain those
  substances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a substance if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is produced incidentally in the manufacture of
    substances that are not regulated under these Regulations, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is incidentally present in a mixture, product
    or equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of a substance that is left in a
  container after it has been emptied and that does not exceed 10% of
  the total capacity in weight of the container for that substance;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a substance that is in transit through Canada from a
  place outside Canada to another place outside Canada, or a substance
  that is in transit through another country from a place in Canada to
  another place in Canada, if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the address of the destination is known on the date
    of entry into Canada or on the date of exit from Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} while in transit, the substance is not stored ---
    other than in the normal course of transport --- repackaged, sorted,
    altered in any manner or sold; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an automobile that is in transit through Canada from
  a place outside Canada to another place outside Canada.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[CFCs, Bromofluorocarbons, Bromochlorodifluoromethane, Tetrachloromethane, 1,1,1-Trichloroethane, HBFCs and Bromochloromethane]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826945 .Part}

### [Exporting Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-826946 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- exporting substance
without permit

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to export a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1 without a
permit issued under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of exporting

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  permit may only be issued to export the substance to a Party for one
  of the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its destruction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its disposal if the substance was imported by
    mistake;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a use set out in column 3 of Table 1 of Schedule 1
    if the substance was manufactured or imported for a use set out in
    that column;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its reclamation, if the substance is a CFC, a
    bromofluorocarbon or bromochlorodifluoromethane that is recovered,
    recycled or reclaimed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other purpose that complies with the laws of
    the importing Party, if the substance is a CFC, a bromofluorocarbon
    or bromochlorodifluoromethane.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exporting --- regardless of purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permit may also be issued to export, regardless of
  purpose, any of the following substances, if they are recovered,
  recycled or reclaimed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bromochloromethane;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an HBFC;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any reclaimed CFC, tetrachloromethane or
    1,1,1-trichloroethane.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation with respect to paragraph
6(2)(c)

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a permit
issued under subsection 6(2) must export any substance set out in
paragraph 6(2)(c) to the country of origin of the substance within six
months after its importation date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refilling or servicing --- foreign ship

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 does not apply to
a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1 that is sold to a foreign
ship for the refilling or servicing of its refrigeration,
air-conditioning or fire-extinguishing equipment in a quantity that does
not exceed the total capacity of that equipment.

### [Exporting Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-826966 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- exporting product without
permit

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to export to a Party referred to in
  paragraph 1 of Article 5 of the Protocol a product containing or
  designed to contain any CFC, bromofluorocarbon,
  bromochlorodifluoromethane, tetrachloromethane or
  1,1,1-trichloroethane set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1 without a
  permit issued under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- fire-extinguishing
  equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to fire-extinguishing
  equipment for use in aircraft, military ships or military vehicles.

### [Importing Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-826972 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- importing substance
without permit

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to import a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1 without a
permit issued under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of importing

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  permit may only be issued to import the substance from a Party for one
  of the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its destruction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a use set out in column 3 of Table 1 of Schedule 1;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its reclamation, if the substance is a CFC,
    tetrachloromethane, 1,1,1-trichloroethane, an HBFC or
    bromochloromethane that is recovered, recycled or reclaimed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importing --- regardless of purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A permit may also be issued to import, regardless of
  purpose, a bromofluorocarbon or bromochlorodifluoromethane that is
  recovered, recycled or reclaimed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to re-export in certain
circumstances

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The holder of the permit
must ensure

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the permit is for a substance set out in paragraph
  11(1)(c), that the substance is reclaimed and re-exported to its
  country of origin within six months after its importation date;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the permit is for a substance set out in
  subsection 11(2), that the substance is re-exported to a Party, for
  any use that complies with the laws of that Party, within six months
  after its importation date; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the holder is unable to comply with paragraph (a)
  or (b), that the substance is sent for destruction, no later than
  three months after the end of the six-month period following the
  importation date, to a facility that is operated in accordance with
  the Handbook for the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the
  Ozone Layer, published by the Ozone Secretariat, United Nations
  Environment Programme, or that the substance is exported for
  destruction within that time.

### [Importing Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-826988 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- importing product

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to import a product containing or designed
  to contain a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- miscellaneous products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 1 does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fire-extinguishing equipment containing or designed
    to contain a bromofluorocarbon or bromochlorodifluoromethane for use
    in aircraft, military ships or military vehicles if the equipment is
    imported from a Party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an aircraft, ship or vehicle manufactured before
    January 1, 1999;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a personal or household effect for the person's
    personal use; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a product that contains a CFC supplied in a
    container of 3 L or less and that is used for a laboratory or
    analytical use.

### [Manufacture, Use and Sale of a Substance or a Product]{.HTitleText2} {#h-826998 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacturing substance

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to manufacture a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacturing product

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to manufacture a product containing or designed to contain a
substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- using or selling
substance

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to use or sell a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1,
unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the substance is recovered, recycled or reclaimed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the substance is sold to be destroyed;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the substance was manufactured or imported before the
  following date, whichever applies:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of tetrachloromethane, January 1, 1995,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of 1,1,1-trichloroethane, January 1,
    1996,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a CFC, January 1, 1996,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of bromochlorodifluoromethane,
    bromotrifluoromethane and dibromotetrafluoroethane, July 1, 1994,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of bromofluorocarbons other than those
    set out in subparagraph (iv), January 1, 1994,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an HBFC, January 1, 1996, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of bromochloromethane, January 1,
    2002;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the substance was manufactured or imported for one of
  the uses set out in column 3 of that Table and it is used or sold for
  that same use; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the substance is tetrachloromethane that was
  manufactured or imported in 1995 and it is used

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in chlor-alkali plants as a diluent for nitrogen
    trichloride to prevent explosions, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as feedstock or for a laboratory or analytical
    use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CFCs, bromofluorocarbons,
bromochlorodifluoromethane, tetrachloromethane or 1,1,1-trichloroethane

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to use, for any other use, a CFC, a bromofluorocarbon,
bromochlorodifluoromethane, tetrachloromethane or 1,1,1-trichloroethane
that is recovered from a product in which that substance was used for
one of the uses set out in column 3 of Table 1 of Schedule 1, or to sell
that substance for any other use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substance no longer needed

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A person in possession of
a substance set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1 that was imported under a
permit issued under these Regulations and that is no longer needed for
the use set out in that permit must, within six months after the day on
which it is no longer needed,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that it is sent for destruction to a facility
  referred to in paragraph 12(c); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that it is exported for destruction or for a
  use set out in column 3 of that Table.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products, containers and packaging
material containing CFCs

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to sell

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pressurized container that contains 10 kg or less
    of a CFC set out in Table 1 of Schedule 1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a container or packaging material for food or
    beverages that is made of a plastic foam in which a CFC set out in
    Table 1 of Schedule 1 is used as a foaming agent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- metered-dose inhalers and
  products containing a CFC

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} metered-dose inhalers --- including those
    containing a mixture of active ingredients --- other than nasal
    sprays and metered-dose inhalers whose active ingredient is
    salbutamol; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} products containing a CFC set out in Table 1 of
    Schedule 1 that is supplied in a container of 3 L or less for a
    laboratory or analytical use.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Methyl Bromide]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part, [[methyl bromide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes products
containing or designed to contain methyl bromide.

### [Exporting Methyl Bromide]{.HTitleText2} {#h-827037 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- exporting methyl bromide
without permit

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to export methyl bromide without a permit issued under these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of exporting

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The permit may only be
issued to export methyl bromide to a Party for one of the following
purposes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its destruction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its disposal if the methyl bromide was imported by
  mistake; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a use set out in column 3 of Table 2 of Schedule 1 if
  the methyl bromide was manufactured or imported for a use set out in
  that column.

### [Importing Methyl Bromide]{.HTitleText2} {#h-827045 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- importing methyl bromide
without permit

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to import methyl bromide without a permit issued under these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of importing

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The permit may only be
issued to import methyl bromide from a Party for one of the following
purposes:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its destruction; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a use set out in column 3 of Table 2 of Schedule 1.

### [Manufacture, Use and Sale of Methyl Bromide]{.HTitleText2} {#h-827052 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- manufacturing methyl
bromide

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to manufacture methyl bromide.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- using or selling methyl
bromide

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to use or sell methyl bromide, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is sold for destruction;

### [Consumption Allowance for HFCs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-827402 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of consumption allowance for
HFCs

- **[[65.06]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65.06
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The annual consumption allowance for
  an HFC set out in Table 4 of Schedule 1 to which a person is entitled
  is determined as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each calendar year that falls within the period
    that begins on January 1, 2019 and ends on December 31, 2023, by
    multiplying the base consumption granted to that person by 90%;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each calendar year that falls within the period
    that begins on January 1, 2024 and ends on December 31, 2028, by
    multiplying the base consumption granted to that person by 60%;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each calendar year that falls within the period
    that begins on January 1, 2029 and ends on December 31, 2033, by
    multiplying the base consumption granted to that person by 30%;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each calendar year that falls within the period
    that begins on January 1, 2034 and ends on December 31, 2035, by
    multiplying the base consumption granted to that person by 20%; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} as of January 1, 2036, by multiplying the base
    consumption granted to that person by 15%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of base consumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The base consumption granted to a person is
  determined as follows:

  ::: {#827413 .Subsection}
  C/D × E

  where

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the person's average HFC consumption for 2014 and 2015,
      expressed in tonnes of CO~2~ equivalent;

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the average Canadian HFC consumption for 2014 and 2015,
      expressed in tonnes of CO~2~ equivalent; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is 18 008 795 tonnes of CO~2~ equivalent.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent or temporary transfer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a transfer of a portion of the consumption
  allowance is approved in accordance with subsection 65.08(4), the
  transferred portion is subtracted from or added to the person's annual
  consumption allowance, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permanent transfer, for every
    calendar year following the year of the transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a temporary transfer, for the
    calendar year of the transfer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must inform the person in writing of
  their consumption allowance.

  [GWP]{.dfn}
  :   is the global warming potential of the HFC;

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the quantity imported during the calendar year; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the quantity exported during the calendar year.
  :::

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 67(1))]{.OriginatingRef}[Notice of Shipment in Transit --- Information Required]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-827588 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Information respecting the person providing the notice:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
    number and, if any, email address and fax number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, title, civic and postal
    addresses, telephone number and, if any, email address and fax
    number of the person authorized to act on their behalf; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, their business number assigned by
    the Minister of National Revenue.

- **2** Information respecting the substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its CAS registry number, if such a number can be
    assigned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity in transit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the estimated date of entry into Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated date of exit from Canada; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether it is recovered, recycled
    or reclaimed.

- **3** The name, civic and postal addresses, telephone number and, if
  any, email address and fax number of each carrier of the substance.

- **4** The name, civic and postal addresses, telephone number and, if
  any, email address and fax number of the customs broker in Canada.

- **5** Information respecting the source of the substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the countries through which it has transited;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry into Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
    number and, if any, email address and fax number of the sender.

- **6** Information respecting the destination of the substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the port of exit from Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the country of destination; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
    number and, if any, email address and fax number of the recipient.

- **7** If known at the time the notice is provided, information
  respecting the storage in Canada of the substance:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the civic address of the location of the storage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
    number and, if any, email address and fax number of the person
    responsible for the storage; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expected duration of the storage.

    calculated level, of each substance used as feedstock;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the substance is sent for destruction, the name,
    civic and postal addresses, telephone number and, if any, email
