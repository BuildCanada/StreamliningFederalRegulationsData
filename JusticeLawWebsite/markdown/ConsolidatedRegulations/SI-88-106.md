## Pasta Remission Order 1988 {#pasta-remission-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-106
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-07-06

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Pasta

P.C. 1988-1203  1988-06-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-88-106_e_hq_6457){#fn_SI-88-106_e_hq_6457-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the
annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duties on
pasta](/eng/regulations/SI-88-106).

- ::: Footnote

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641605 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Pasta Remission Order 1988](/eng/regulations/SI-88-106).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641607 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on pasta containing only flour
and water and imported under tariff item 1902.19.90 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) during the period beginning January 1, 1988
and ending July 31, 1995.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/95-89, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on pasta containing only flour
and water and imported under tariff item 1902.19.91 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on or after August 1, 1995.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/95-89, s. 1
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641614 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in sections 2 and 3 is granted on condition that a claim for remission
is made to the Minister of National Revenue within three years after the
date of importation of the pasta.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/95-89, s. 1
:::
