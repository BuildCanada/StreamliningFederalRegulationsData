## Transitional Assistance Benefit Regulations {#transitional-assistance-benefit-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/65-410
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

Registration 1965-09-01

Regulations Providing for Transitional Assistance to Workers in
Automotive Manufacturing and Parts Industries

P.C. 1965-1589 1965-09-01
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to any enactment of the Parliament of
Canada for defraying the several charges and expenses of the public
service from and after the first day of April, 1965, that provides for
payments of Transitional Assistance to workers in automotive
manufacturing and parts industries who have become unemployed as a
result of the operation of the Canada-United States agreement on
automotive products, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
providing for Transitional Assistance to workers in Automotive
Manufacturing and Parts Industries](/eng/regulations/SOR-65-410).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860301 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Transitional Assistance Benefit
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-65-410).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860303 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Automotive Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Automotive Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada-United
    States Agreement on Automotive Products signed on January 16, 1965,
    by the President of the United States and the Prime Minister of
    Canada; ([accord de l'automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[average weekly wages and salaries in the industries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[average weekly wages and salaries in the
    industries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the average wages and
    salaries of employees in the Motor Vehicles and Motor Vehicle Parts
    and Accessories Industries weighted according to the number of
    persons employed in each industry for the latest twelve months for
    which figures are published by the Dominion Bureau of Statistics and
    effective the first of the month following the publication thereof;
    ([traitements et salaires hebdomadaires moyens dans les
    industries]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the General Adjustment
    Assistance Board established by the [General Adjustment Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._971);
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board of Referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board of Referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a board of referees
    appointed pursuant to the Unemployment Insurance Act; ([conseil
    arbitral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate issued to
    the Department of Labour by the Board in accordance with section 12
    of the [Automotive Manufacturing Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._966);
    ([certificat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Unemployment Insurance
    Commission established pursuant to the Unemployment Insurance Act;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person considered a
    dependant under the provisions of subsection (3) of section 47 of
    the Unemployment Insurance Act; ([personne à
    charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[effective date of the lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective date of the lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day
    stated by the Board in a certificate to be the effective date of the
    lay-off of the employees named in the certificate; ([date effective
    du débauchage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employer of employees
    named in a certificate issued by the Board and any other employer
    who, in the opinion of the Minister of Industry, is engaged in
    producing products to which the Automotive Agreement applies;
    ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee named in a
    certificate issued by the Board; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[insurance officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer or
    employee appointed or employed pursuant to the Unemployment
    Insurance Act and authorized by the Commission to be an insurance
    officer for the purposes of that Act; ([fonctionnaire de
    l'assurance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum benefit entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum benefit entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of an
    employee means the weekly sum of money established under section 10;
    ([allocation maximum attribuable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Labour;
    ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[previous earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[previous earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the gross weekly
    wages of an employee for a normal full week of employment without
    overtime by an employer immediately preceding the effective date of
    the lay-off; ([gains antérieurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a week

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for which a contribution in respect of earnings
      of at least twenty dollars of an insured person is payable under
      the Unemployment Insurance Act and has been paid by an employer,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in which an employee for whom contributions are
      not payable under the Unemployment Insurance Act has worked for an
      employer and has earned at least twenty dollars, if the fact of
      his having so worked is established to the satisfaction of an
      insurance officer; ([semaine ouvrant droit à
      prestations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplemental unemployment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplemental unemployment benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    benefits under a private supplemental unemployment benefit plan that
    has been approved by the Commission pursuant to the Unemployment
    Insurance Act; and ([prestations supplémentaires de
    chômage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar week.
    ([semaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860326 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Transitional assistance
benefits may be paid by the Commission of moneys appropriated by
Parliament in amounts calculated in accordance with these Regulations to
an employee where an insurance officer is satisfied that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transitional assistance benefit period has been
  established in respect of such an employee; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other conditions of these Regulations have been
  complied with.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A transitional assistance
benefit period in respect of an employee is established when, upon
filing an application for transitional assistance benefits, he proves
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period of fifty-two weeks immediately
  preceding the Sunday before the day stated by the Board to be the
  effective date of the lay-off, he had at least sixteen qualifying
  weeks and the effective date of the lay-off is after November 17, 1968
  but not after June 30, 1973; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he has an established benefit period under the
  Unemployment Insurance Act or had a benefit period established under
  that Act that terminated subsequent to the effective date of the
  lay-off.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-13, s. 1
- SOR/68-410, s. 1
- SOR/69-30, s. 2
- SOR/70-275
- SOR/70-390
- SOR/71-322, s. 2
- SOR/71-501
- SOR/71-593
- SOR/73-358, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No transitional assistance
benefits may be paid to an employee for any week in respect of which he
is paid supplemental unemployment benefits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/69-30, s. 3
:::

## [Transitional Assistance Benefit Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860338 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  transitional assistance benefit period established under these
  Regulations in respect of an employee shall commence with and include
  the week in which the effective date of the lay-off falls and shall
  terminate on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date that the employee exhausts the
    transitional assistance benefits provided under these Regulations in
    respect of that period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one hundred and fifty-six weeks from the effective
    date of the lay-off.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies in respect of an employee only
  if the effective date of the lay-off occurred or occurs after April 2,
  1966.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding paragraph (b) of subsection (1) but
  subject to paragraph (a) thereof, no transitional assistance benefit
  period established in respect of an employee that commenced between
  April 2, 1966, and December 31, 1966, shall be terminated prior to
  December 27, 1969.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-13, s. 2
- SOR/68-410, s. 2
- SOR/69-30, s. 4
- SOR/73-358, s. 2
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transitional assistance benefit period has been
  established in respect of an employee, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during that period, the employee is employed by an
  employer and, subsequent to the termination of that employment, is
  laid off so that there is an effective date of the lay-off within the
  meaning of paragraph (h) of section 2,

there may be established in respect of the employee, at the termination
of the transitional assistance benefit period mentioned in
paragraph (a), a further transitional assistance benefit period in
respect of the lay-off mentioned in paragraph (b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-13, s. 2
:::

## [Duration of Transitional Assistance Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860353 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the effective date of the lay-off of an employee occurred or occurs
  after November 17, 1968, that employee may, in respect of any
  transitional assistance benefit period, be paid benefits for a number
  of weeks not exceeding the difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one week for each two of his qualifying weeks in
    the one hundred and four weeks immediately preceding the most recent
    Sunday before the effective date of the lay-off; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of weeks for which the employee is
    disqualified under section 14 from receiving transitional assistance
    benefits.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a certificate indicates the proportion of a
  lay-off that is due to the Automotive Agreement, the number of weeks
  determined pursuant to subsection (1) shall be reduced in the same
  proportion, except that the number of weeks remaining after the
  reduction shall not be less than four.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a fraction of a
  week shall be deemed to be a full week.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-13, s. 2
- SOR/69-30, s. 5
:::

## [Calculation of Transitional Assistance Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860362 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  transitional assistance benefit of an employee is, subject to
  subsection (2) of this section and to sections 14 and 17, a weekly sum
  of money equal to the amount, if any, by which the maximum benefit
  entitlement of the employee exceeds the greatest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the weekly unemployment insurance benefit to which
    the employee is entitled under the Unemployment Insurance Act and
    the Supplementary Unemployment Insurance Benefit Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the weekly training allowance to which the employee
    is entitled under the provisions of the Adult Occupational Training
    Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the unemployment insurance
    benefit, the supplementary unemployment insurance benefit and the
    training allowance to which the employee is entitled in respect of
    the week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to section 58 of the Unemployment
  Insurance Act, moneys are recovered out of unemployment insurance
  benefits payable to an employee during a transitional assistance
  benefit period, the amount of transitional assistance benefit payable
  shall not be greater than the amount that would have been paid if the
  employee had been entitled to the money so recovered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-296, s. 1
- SOR/71-12, s. 1
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The maximum benefit
entitlement of an employee in respect of whom a transitional assistance
benefit period has been established is a weekly sum of money equal to
the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sixty-two per cent of the previous earnings of the
  employee together with two and one-half per cent of such earnings for
  each dependant of the employee at the beginning of his transitional
  assistance benefit period, up to a maximum of seventy-five per cent of
  the employee's previous earnings; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sixty-five per cent of the average weekly wages and
  salaries in the industries.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/69-30, s. 6
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A maximum benefit
entitlement calculated under section 10 is fixed for the duration of the
transitional assistance benefit period for which it is calculated.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The maximum benefit
entitlement of an employee calculated under section 10 shall be rounded
to the nearest whole dollar and a half dollar shall be rounded to a full
dollar.

## [Disqualification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860378 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, the
  provisions of the Unemployment Insurance Act and the Regulations made
  thereunder relating to disqualification of an insured person from
  receiving benefits under the Act apply [mutatis mutandis]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} to employees applying for transitional assistance benefits
  under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employee shall not be disqualified from receiving
  transitional assistance benefits

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by virtue of paragraph (b) of subsection (2) of
    section 54 or paragraph (a), (b) or (c) of subsection (1) of
    section 59 of the Unemployment Insurance Act if the suitable
    employment referred to therein is not comparable employment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by virtue of paragraph (a) of subsection (2) of
    section 54 of the Unemployment Insurance Act during any period in
    respect of which he receives a training allowance under the Adult
    Occupational Training Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by virtue of paragraph (d) of subsection (1) of
    section 59 of the Unemployment Insurance Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 66 of the Unemployment Insurance Act shall
  apply [mutatis mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} in respect of an
  applicant for transitional assistance benefits where the waiting
  period provided for in section 16 has been completed and the
  provisions of section 55 of the Unemployment Insurance Act have been
  fulfilled or are not required, for the purposes of a claim under that
  Act, to be fulfilled.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-296, s. 2
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in relation to an application for transitional assistance benefits, an
  employee or any person on his behalf makes a statement or
  representation that he knows to be false or misleading, an insurance
  officer may declare the employee to be disqualified from receiving
  transitional assistance benefits for not more than the first six of
  the weeks described in subsection (2) that occur after such day as the
  insurance officer may determine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a week described
  in this subsection is a week in respect of which an employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes a claim for transitional assistance benefits
    in the prescribed manner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would, but for this section, be entitled to receive
    transitional assistance benefits.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to section 65 of the Unemployment
  Insurance Act, an employee is disqualified from receiving unemployment
  insurance benefits in any week during a transitional assistance
  benefit period, then notwithstanding the provisions of section 9, the
  amount of transitional assistance benefits payable shall not be
  greater than the amount that would have been paid if the
  disqualification had not been imposed and the unemployment insurance
  benefits had been fully paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/67-13, s. 3
:::

## [Payment of Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860396 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to these Regulations, where
  an employee in respect of whom a transitional assistance benefit
  period has been established proves that he was unemployed and not
  entitled to a retirement pension under the [Canada Pension
  Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or the Quebec Pension Plan during any week in the
  transitional assistance benefit period, he is entitled, commencing
  with the week in which he makes an application for transitional
  assistance benefits and makes a claim for unemployment insurance
  benefits, if an unemployment insurance benefit period is then
  established with respect to him, to be paid transitional assistance
  benefits in respect of his unemployment during that week.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an unemployment insurance benefit period of an
  employee terminates prior to the termination of a transitional
  assistance benefit period of the employee, transitional assistance
  benefits continue to be payable to him in accordance with these
  Regulations but shall not be paid if the employee is able to establish
  a new unemployment insurance benefit period and fails to do so.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of section 57 of the Unemployment
  Insurance Act and the Regulations relating thereto apply [mutatis
  mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} to these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/72-12, s. 1
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An employee shall not be
paid transitional assistance benefits for the first week of his
transitional assistance benefit period.

## [Deduction of Earnings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860404 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be deducted from the transitional assistance benefits payable to
  an employee for any week an amount equal to the amount by which the
  aggregate of the employee's earnings for that week added to his
  maximum benefit entitlement exceeds the lesser of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seventy-five per cent of his previous earnings; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate derived by adding to his maximum
    benefit entitlement the earnings that, under the Schedule to
    section 56 of the Unemployment Insurance Act, are not deducted from
    his weekly benefit under that Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/69-30, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Determination and allocation of earnings for purposes
  of this section shall be made on the basis prescribed in sections 172
  and 173 of the Unemployment Insurance Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/69-30, s. 7
:::

## [Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860413 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall
administer these Regulations on behalf of the Minister and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive applications from employees named in a
  certificate for the purpose of determining eligibility of the
  employees to receive transitional assistance benefits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the eligibility of such employees to
  receive transitional assistance benefits, and the amount and duration
  of the transitional assistance benefits payable to each such employee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay transitional assistance benefits and, subject to
  these Regulations, make recovery of overpayments of transitional
  assistance benefits and of amounts paid pursuant to these Regulations
  to any person not entitled thereto;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide information to the Board when required; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make such reports to the Minister concerning the
  administration of these Regulations as he may require.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Sections 145 to 148 of
the Unemployment Insurance Regulations apply [mutatis
mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} to applications made under these
Regulations.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where an employee submits
an application for transitional assistance benefits on a day later than
the day on which he was first qualified to make the application and
shows good cause for the delay, the application may, in the manner and
to the extent prescribed in section 150 of the Unemployment Insurance
Regulations, be regarded as having been submitted on a day earlier than
the day on which it was submitted.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** All claims for
transitional assistance benefits and all questions arising in connection
therewith shall be submitted to an insurance officer.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  insurance officer shall consider applications submitted to him under
  these Regulations and,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if he is of the opinion that a transitional
    assistance benefit period has been established, he shall so declare,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if he is of the opinion that a transitional
    assistance benefit period has not been established, he shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} declare that a transitional assistance benefit
      period has not been established on the ground that one or more of
      the requirements of these Regulations have not been complied with,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refer the application, if practicable, within
      fourteen days from the day on which the application was submitted
      to him to a Board of Referees for its decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding that a transitional assistance
  benefit period has been established, if an insurance officer is not
  satisfied that an employee has fulfilled all the other conditions of
  qualification for transitional assistance benefits or if he is of the
  opinion that the employee is or was disqualified from receiving
  transitional assistance benefits, he shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declare the employee to be disqualified from
    receiving transitional assistance benefits for such days as he may
    determine, on the ground that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the employee is disqualified under these
      Regulations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the employee does not fulfil one or more of any
      of the conditions or requirements of these Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer the application, if practicable, within
    fourteen days from the day on which the application was submitted to
    him, to a Board of Referees for its decision.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee has been declared disqualified
  under paragraph (a) of subsection (2) for any days, there shall be
  deducted from the transitional assistance benefits otherwise payable
  to him in respect of the week in which such days fall, an amount equal
  to one-sixth of the product obtained by multiplying the total number
  of such days in the week by the weekly transitional assistance benefit
  of that employee, but if the amount so calculated is not a multiple of
  one dollar, the amount shall be rounded to the nearest dollar and a
  half dollar shall be rounded to a full dollar.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860435 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Board of Referees has power to consider and determine appeals from
  decisions made under these Regulations by insurance officers and
  matters referred to it under these Regulations by insurance officers
  and an appeal lies from decisions of a Board of Referees on any such
  appeal or reference to the Umpire or any Deputy Umpire appointed under
  the Unemployment Insurance Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 70 to 82 of the Unemployment Insurance Act
  and sections 177 to 187 of the Unemployment Insurance Regulations
  apply [mutatis mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} to these Regulations.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860439 .Part}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an employee has received transitional assistance benefits for any
  period in respect of which he is disqualified or to which he is
  otherwise not entitled, he is liable to repay an amount equal to the
  amount so received by him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), section 175 of the
  Unemployment Insurance Regulations applies [mutatis
  mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} to transitional assistance benefits
  paid to employees not entitled thereto.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Transitional assistance
benefits are not capable of being assigned, charged, attached,
anticipated or given as security and any transaction purporting to
assign, charge, attach, anticipate or give as security any transitional
assistance benefits is void, except that any amounts payable or
repayable under these Regulations by any employee may be recovered out
of any transitional assistance benefits payable to that employee,
without prejudice to any other mode of recovery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/69-30, s. 8
:::

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Where not inconsistent
with these Regulations, the relevant provisions of the Unemployment
Insurance Act and the Unemployment Insurance Regulations apply [mutatis
mutandis]{.otherLang lang="la"} to these Regulations.
