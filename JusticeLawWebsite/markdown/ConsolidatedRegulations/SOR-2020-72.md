## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1243830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dairy year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dairy year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 12-month period
    beginning on August 1 of each year and ending on July 31 of the
    following year. ([année laitière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a class of
    product. ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Export Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1243836 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export permits --- subsection 7(1) of Act

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a permit issued under subsection 7(1) of the Act who
  exports, under that permit, a product that is set out in column 1 of
  the schedule must pay an export charge in an amount determined in
  accordance with subsection (2) if the export access quantity for the
  product in respect of the current dairy year, determined under
  subsection 6.2(1.1) of the Act, is less than the total of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of the product to be
    exported under the permit,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of the product
    previously authorized to be exported under all permits issued to
    residents of Canada under subsection 7(1) of the Act since the
    beginning of the dairy year, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, in kilograms, of the product
    allocated for export in respect of the dairy year to residents of
    Canada under paragraph 6.2(2)(b) of the Act, including any quantity
    that has been allocated but not yet exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Export charge calculation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The export charge is calculated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the total of the quantities referred to in
    paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) is greater than the export access quantity
    referred to in subsection (1), by the formula

    ::: {#1243846 .Paragraph}
    A x B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the quantity referred to in paragraph (1)(a), and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the rate set out in the applicable item, in column 3, of the
        schedule; and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the total of the quantities referred to in
    paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) is less than or equal to the export access
    quantity, by the formula

    ::: {#1243851 .Paragraph}
    (A + B + C -- D) x E

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the quantity referred to in paragraph (1)(a),

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the quantity referred to in paragraph (1)(b),

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the quantity referred to in paragraph (1)(c),

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the export access quantity for that product in respect of
        that dairy year, determined under subsection 6.2(1.1) of the
        Act, and

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the rate set out in the applicable item, in column 3, of the
        schedule.
    :::

section 40 of the [Canada--United States--Mexico Agreement
