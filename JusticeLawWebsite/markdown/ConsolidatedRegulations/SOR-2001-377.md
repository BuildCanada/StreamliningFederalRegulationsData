## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673492 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible Canadian financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible Canadian financial
      institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 370(1) of the Act. ([institution financière canadienne
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible foreign institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 370(1) of the Act. ([institution
      étrangère admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
      corporate to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
      Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies or a trust or loan corporation
      incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a
      province. ([société de fiducie et de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial statement equity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the financial
  statement equity of an entity is the sum of the shareholders' and
  members' equity of the entity, and the minority interests in entities
  controlled by the entity, as they appear in the consolidated financial
  statements of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acting in concert

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If two or more persons are deemed under section 9,
  9.1, 9.2 or 371 of the Act, for the purposes of Part VII or Division 7
  of Part XV of the Act, to be a single person who is acquiring
  beneficial ownership of the aggregate number of shares or membership
  shares of a bank, shares of a bank holding company or shares or
  ownership interests of an entity that are beneficially owned by them,
  those persons are deemed, for the purposes of these Regulations, to be
  a single person who has acquired beneficial ownership of the aggregate
  number of shares or membership shares of the bank, shares of the bank
  holding company or shares or ownership interests of the entity that
  are beneficially owned by them.

## [Determination of the Equity of a Bank or a Bank Holding Company]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673506 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where an entity's equity is its financial
statement equity

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act, the equity of an entity is its financial statement equity if the
entity is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank or any other bank that is not
  controlled by any person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank holding company or any other bank
  holding company that is not controlled by any person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a bank controlled by a widely held bank holding
  company;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company controlled by a widely held
  bank holding company;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a bank controlled by a widely-held insurance holding
  company;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company controlled by a widely-held
  insurance holding company;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a bank controlled by an eligible Canadian financial
  institution;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company controlled by an eligible
  Canadian financial institution;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a bank that is not controlled by any person other
  than an eligible foreign institution or a subsidiary of the eligible
  foreign institution; or

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company that is not controlled by any
  person other than an eligible foreign institution or a subsidiary of
  the eligible foreign institution.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bank's equity by reference to its group

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act, if the equity of a bank is not determined under section 2, the
equity of the bank is the sum of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statement equity of the bank, if the
  bank is not controlled by a bank, bank holding company or trust and
  loan company, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the financial statement
  equity of an affiliate of the bank that is a bank, bank holding
  company or trust and loan company and that is not controlled by a
  bank, bank holding company or trust and loan company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bank holding company's equity by
reference to its group

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
Act, if the equity of a bank holding company is not determined under
section 2, the equity of the bank holding company is the sum of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial statement equity of the bank holding
  company, if the bank holding company is not controlled by a bank, bank
  holding company or trust and loan company, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the financial statement
  equity of an affiliate of the bank holding company that is a bank,
  bank holding company or trust and loan company and that is not
  controlled by a bank, bank holding company or trust and loan company.
