Rules of Practice and Procedure of the Tax Court of Canada in Respect of
Appeals Under Part IX of the Excise Tax Act (Informal Procedure)

registration under the Statutory Instruments Act the annexed Tax Court
of Canada Rules of Procedure Respecting the Excise Tax Act (Informal
Procedure).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945523 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These rules may be cited as
the Tax Court of Canada Rules of Procedure Respecting the Excise Tax Act
(Informal Procedure).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these rules,

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945536 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These rules apply to
appeals brought under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15),
other than appeals to which the [Tax Court of Canada Rules (General
Procedure)](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-688a) apply.

## [Associate Judges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1418675 .Part}

- **[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  associate judge of the Court has all the powers of a judge of the
  Court under these Rules.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any associate judge may sit and act at any time and
  at any place in Canada for the transaction of the business of the
  Court, and, where the associate judge so sits or acts, that associate
  judge constitutes the Court.

## [Filing a Notice of Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945538 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An appeal referred to in
section 3 shall be instituted by filing a notice of appeal, which may be
in the form set out in Schedule 4, using one of the following methods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} depositing the notice with the Registry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by mail to the Registry; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it by fax or by electronic filing to the
  Registry.

## [Filing of other Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945545 .Part}

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, a
document other than a notice of appeal may be filed using one of the
following methods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} depositing it with the Registry;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending it by mail to the Registry; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending it by fax or by electronic filing to the
  Registry.

## [Filing Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945552 .Part}

**[[4.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.2 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, the
date of filing of a document is deemed to be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document filed with the Registry or
  sent by mail or by fax, the date shown by the date received stamp
  placed on the document by the Registry at the time it is received; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document filed by electronic filing,
  the date shown on the acknowledgment of receipt issued by the Court.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-300, s. 2
:::

## [Electronic Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945558 .Part}

- **[[4.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in
  these rules and unless otherwise directed by the Court, when a
  document is filed by electronic filing, the copy of the document that
  is printed by the Registry and placed in the Court file is deemed to
  be the original version of the document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who files a document by electronic filing
  shall, if required by these rules or at the request of a party or the
  Court, provide a paper copy of the document and file it with the
  Registry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Registry has no record of the receipt of a
  document, it is deemed not to have been filed, unless the Court
  directs otherwise.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-300, s. 2
:::

## [Appellant's Address for Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945565 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2004-103, s. 4

\]
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of appeal shall also include the appellant's address for
  service of documents.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appellant's address for service may be the
  address of the appellant, the appellant's counsel or agent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A written notice of any change in the appellant's
  address for service shall forthwith be given at the Registry by the
  appellant, the appellant's counsel or agent, which address shall
  thereafter be that party's address for service.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Until notice of a change of the appellant's address
  for service is received at the Registry, any service required to be
  made on the appellant of any documents pertaining to his appeal shall
  be made by mail to the address contained in the notice of appeal and
  shall constitute good and sufficient service on the appellant.

## [Reply to Notice of Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945573 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  reply to a notice of appeal shall contain a statement of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts that are admitted,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the facts that are denied,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the facts of which the respondent has no knowledge
    and puts in issue,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the findings or assumptions of fact made by the
    Minister when making the assessment,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other material facts,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issues to be decided,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the statutory provisions relied on,

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the reasons the respondent intends to rely on, and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the relief sought.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within five days after a reply is filed, the Minister
  shall serve a copy of it by registered mail addressed to the
  appellant's address for service of documents.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-100, s. 2
:::

## [Expert Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945588 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  who intends to call an expert witness at the hearing of an appeal
  shall, not less than 10 days before the commencement of the hearing,
  file at the Registry and serve on the other parties a report, signed
  by the expert, setting out the expert's name, address and
  qualifications and the substance of the expert's testimony.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An expert witness may not testify, except with leave
  of the presiding judge, if subsection (1) has not been satisfied.

## [Discontinuance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945592 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  who instituted an appeal in the Court may, at any time, discontinue
  that appeal by written notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Such discontinuance may be in the form set out in
  Schedule 8.

## [Pronouncing and Entering of Judgments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945596 .Part}

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Court shall dispose of an appeal or an interlocutory or other
  application that determines in whole or in part any substantive right
  in dispute between or among the parties by rendering a judgment and
  shall dispose of any other interlocutory or other application by
  issuing an order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A judgment shall be dated on the day it is signed and
  that day is the date of the pronouncement of the judgment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A judgment and the reasons relating to it, if any,
  shall be deposited with the Registry without delay.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-26, s. 44
:::

## [Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945603 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Court may determine the amount of the costs of all parties involved in
  any proceeding, the allocation of those costs and the persons required
  to pay them.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Court may award costs not exceeding the amounts
  listed in section 10 to the respondent only if the actions of the
  appellant have unduly delayed the prompt and effective resolution of
  the appeal.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Court may direct the payment of costs in a fixed
  sum, in lieu of any taxed costs.

::: HistoricalNote

- SOR/2008-300, s. 3

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** On the taxation of party
and party costs the following fees may be allowed for the services of
counsel

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the preparation of a notice of appeal or for
  advice relating to the appeal, \$185;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for preparing for a hearing, \$250;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the conduct of a hearing, \$375 for each half day
  or part of a half day; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the taxation of costs, \$60.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-103, s. 6
:::

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
directed by the Court, where an appellant is represented or assisted by
an advisor other than counsel, disbursements in respect of services
referred to in section 10 may be allowed on the taxation of party and
party costs in an amount not to exceed one half of the amounts listed in
section 10.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-103, s. 7
:::

- **[[10.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Such other disbursements may be
  allowed as were essential for the conduct of the appeal, if it is
  established that the disbursements were made or that the party is
  liable for them.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There may be allowed all services, sales, use or
  consumption taxes and other like taxes paid or payable on any counsel
  fees and disbursements allowed if it is established that such taxes
  have been paid or are payable and are not otherwise reimbursed or
  reimbursable in any manner whatever, including, without restriction,
  by means of claims for input tax credits in respect of such taxes.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-103, s. 7
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  witness, other than a witness who appears to give evidence as an
  expert, is entitled to be paid by the party who arranged for his or
  her attendance \$75 per day, plus reasonable and proper transportation
  and living expenses.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An amount is not payable under subsection (1) in
  respect of an appellant unless the appellant is called upon to testify
  by counsel for the respondent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} There may be paid to a witness who appears to give
  evidence as an expert a reasonable payment, not to exceed \$300 per
  day unless the Court otherwise directs, for the services performed by
  the witness in preparing himself to give evidence and giving evidence.

against the person in respect of whom the order has been made.

## [Removal of an Appeal from the Informal Procedure to the General Procedure or from the General Procedure to the Informal Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945646 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An application by the
Attorney General of Canada to remove an appeal from the informal
procedure to the general procedure shall be by motion and the Court may
give such directions as are necessary for the subsequent conduct of the
appeal. There shall be no additional filing fee for proceeding in the
general procedure, unless the Court so directs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-103, s. 10
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person who has appealed under Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) has not elected in the notice of appeal that
  section 18.3001 and sections 18.3003 to 18.302 (Informal Procedure) of
  the Act shall apply, that person may, within 90 days from the date on
  which the reply is served or within such additional time as the Court
  may, on motion for special reasons, allow, make such an election.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An election under subsection (1) may be in the form
  set out in Schedule 15.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-100, s. 3
- SOR/2004-103, s. 11

## [Application for Extension of Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945655 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has made an application to the Minister to extend the time
  for the filing of a notice of objection or the making of a request in
  writing to the Minister for an assessment in respect of an avoidance
  transaction may apply to the Court to have the application granted
  after either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has refused the application, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90 days have elapsed after service of the
    application and the Minister has not notified the person of the
    Minister's decision,

  but no application under this section may be made after the expiration
  of 30 days after the day on which notification of the Minister's
  decision was mailed to the person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subsection (1) may be in
  the form set out in Schedule 16(1) --- OBJECTION or 16(2) --- REQUEST,
  as the case may be.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application under subsection (1) shall be made by
  filing with the Registrar, in the manner provided in section 4.1,
  three copies of the application made to the Minister accompanied by
  three copies of the notice of objection or the request, as the case
  may be, and three copies of the Minister's decision, if any.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Court may dispose of an application made under
  subsection (1) by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismissing it, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} granting it,

  and, in granting it, may impose such terms as it deems just or order
  that the notice of objection or the request be deemed to be a valid
  objection or request as of the date of the order.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No application shall be granted under this section to
  a person unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application is made within one year after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the expiration of the time specified by the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) for filing a notice of
      objection\*, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the lapse of 180 days after the day of mailing
      of a notice under subsection 274(6) of the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person demonstrates that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within the applicable time specified in
      subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii), the person

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} was unable to act or instruct another to act in
        the person's name, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} had a [bona fide]{.otherLang lang="la"}
        intention to object to the assessment or make the request,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} given the reasons set out in the application and
      the circumstances of the case, it would be just and equitable to
      grant the application, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the application to extend time was made to the
      Minister as soon as circumstances permitted it to be made.

  <!-- -->

  - [ \*]{.lawlabel} Subsection 301(1.1) of the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15) provides:

    - ["(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has been assessed and who
      objects to the assessment may, within ninety days after the day
      notice of the assessment is sent to the person, file with the
      Minister a notice of objection in the prescribed form and manner
      setting out the reasons for the objection and all relevant facts."

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The application pursuant to subsection (1) is deemed
  to have been filed on the date of its receipt by the Registry, even if
  the application is not accompanied by all of the documents listed in
  subsection (3), provided that those documents are filed within 30 days
  after that date or within any reasonable time that the Court
  establishes.

## [Contempt of Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945724 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person is guilty of contempt of court who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a hearing of the Court fails to maintain a
    respectful attitude, remain silent or refrain from showing approval
    or disapproval of the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} wilfully disobeys a process or order of the Court;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acts in such a way as to interfere with the orderly
    administration of justice or to impair the authority or dignity of
    the Court;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is an officer of the Court and fails to perform his
    or her duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is a sheriff or bailiff and does not execute a writ
    forthwith or does not make a return thereof; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} contrary to these rules and without lawful excuse,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to obey a subpoena or to
      attend at the time and place appointed for his or her examination
      for discovery,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refuses to be sworn or to affirm or to answer
      any question put to him or her,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to produce or permit to be
      inspected any document or other property, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} refuses or neglects to answer interrogatories or
      to make discovery of documents.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), before a person may be
  found in contempt of court, the person alleged to be in contempt shall
  be served with an order, made on the motion of a person who has an
  interest in the proceeding or at the Court's own initiative, requiring
  the person alleged to be in contempt

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to appear before a judge at a time and place
    stipulated in the order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be prepared to hear proof of the act with which
    the person is charged, which shall be described in the order with
    sufficient particularity to enable the person to know the nature of
    the case against the person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to be prepared to present any defence that the
    person may have.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A motion for an order under subsection (2) may be
  made [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An order may be made under subsection (2) if the
  Court is satisfied that there is a [prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  case that contempt has been committed.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An order under subsection (2) shall be personally
  served, together with any supporting documents, unless otherwise
  ordered by the Court.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} In a case of urgency, a person may be found in
  contempt of court for an act committed in the presence of a judge in
  the exercise of his or her functions and condemned at once, provided
  that the person has first been called on to justify his or her
  behaviour.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A finding of contempt shall be based on proof beyond
  a reasonable doubt.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A person alleged to be in contempt may not be
  compelled to testify.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where the Court considers it necessary, it may
  request the assistance of the Attorney General of Canada or any other
  person in relation to any proceedings for contempt.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is found to be in contempt, a judge
  may order, in addition to any other order made in respect of the
  proceedings, any or all of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the person be imprisoned for a period of less
    than two years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the person pay a fine;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the person do or refrain from doing any act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the person's property be sequestered; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that the person pay costs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-103, s. 14
