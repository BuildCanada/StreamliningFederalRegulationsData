## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827860 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    governmental authority that is responsible under the law of Canada
    or of Panama, as the case may be, for the administration of its
    customs laws and regulations. ([administration
    douanière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under the CPAFTA is
    claimed. ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good, and includes a part or
    ingredient. ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information regarding the origin of goods that are the
    subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information regarding the origin of
    goods that are the subject of a verification of
    origin. ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} verification visit means
    the entry into premises or a place for the purpose of conducting a
    verification of origin of goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the
    Act. ([visite de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827870 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods in addition to verification visit

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods may be conducted
by reviewing the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification questionnaire completed by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written response received from a person referred to
  in paragraph (a) to a verification letter; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information received from a person referred
  to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification letters and verification
questionnaires

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the customs administration and specify the
  name and title of the officer who is sending the letter or
  questionnaire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe the goods or materials that are the subject
  of the verification of origin; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the period within which the letter must be
  answered or the questionnaire must be completed and returned, and
  which must be a minimum of 30 days beginning on the day on which the
  letter or the questionnaire is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of period

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who receives a
verification letter or verification questionnaire may, on a single
occasion and within the period specified in the letter or questionnaire,
make a written request asking for an extension of that period to the
customs administration. The extension may be granted for a further
period of not more than 30 days.

## [Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premises

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The premises in Panama of
the following persons are prescribed premises for the purposes of a
verification visit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an exporter or producer of goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may only conduct a verification visit if the following
  conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer sends a written notice of their
    intention to conduct the visit to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are to be the subject
      of the visit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the customs administration of Panama, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if requested by Panama, the Embassy of Panama
      that is located in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are to be the subject of
    the visit consents in writing to it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice of the verification visit must
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration on whose
    behalf the notice is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer who is to conduct
    the visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are to be the
    subject of the visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and the place of the visit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the visit, including a
    description of the goods or materials that are the subject of the
    verification of origin; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the visit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who receives a notice under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) may, on a single
  occasion and within 15 days after the day on which the notice is
  received request a postponement of the verification visit by sending a
  written request for a postponement to the officer who sent the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The postponement must not be for a period exceeding
  60 days beginning on the day on which the notice is received, or for
  any longer period that is agreed to by the customs administration of
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A verification visit is postponed if, within 15 days
  after the day on which the customs administration receives a notice
  under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(ii), the customs administration of Panama
  sends a written request for the postponement to the officer who sent
  the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum period

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The postponement must not be for a period exceeding
  60 days beginning on the day on which the notice is received, or for
  any longer period that is agreed to by Canada and Panama.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who receives a notice under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) may designate two
  observers to be present during the verification visit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of observers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The observers' participation is limited to
  observation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of observers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates observers must identify them
  to the officer who is conducting the verification visit.

## [Denial or Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment under CPAFTA]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827922 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- exporter and producer

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, the exporter and the
  producer of goods are, in relation to a verification of origin under
  paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act, required to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain, in accordance with the applicable laws of
    the country in which the verification of origin is conducted,
    records relevant to the determination of origin with respect to the
    goods that are required to be maintained under those laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the officer who is conducting the
    verification of origin with access to the records; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reply to each verification letter or complete and
    return each verification questionnaire to the officer within the
    period specified under paragraph 3(c) or section 4, as the case may
    be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent to verification visit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act,
  consent to the verification visit must be provided in writing by the
  exporter or producer within 30 days after the day on which the notice
  sent in accordance with subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of re-determination of origin

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer must send a written notice of the intent to re-determine the
  origin of goods under subsection 59(1) of the Act with a statement
  that is provided to an exporter or producer of goods under subsection
  42.2(1) of the Act and that states that the goods that are the subject
  of a verification of origin are not eligible for preferential tariff
  treatment under the CPAFTA.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice's

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice must specify the date from which
  preferential tariff treatment under the CPAFTA may be denied or
  withdrawn in respect of the goods that are the subject of the
  verification of origin and the period during which the exporter or
  producer of the goods may provide written comments or additional
  information regarding the origin of those goods.

## [Communication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827936 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of communication

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter, a
verification questionnaire and the notices referred to in paragraph
6(1)(a) and section 10 must be sent by any means that produces
confirmation of receipt.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827939 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}April 1, 2013

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on April 1, 2013.
