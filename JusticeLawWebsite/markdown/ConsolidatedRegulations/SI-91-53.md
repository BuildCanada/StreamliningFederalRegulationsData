## Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and Papua New Guinea Came into Force on December 21, 1989 {#order-giving-notice-that-a-tax-agreement-between-canada-and-papua-new-guinea-came-into-force-on-december-21-1989 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-53
:::

[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](/eng/acts/Z-02)

CANADA-PAPUA NEW GUINEA INCOME TAX AGREEMENT ACT, 1989

Registration 1991-04-10

Order Giving Notice that a Tax Agreement Between Canada and Papua New
Guinea Came into Force on December 21, 1989
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 18 of the Canada-Papua New
Guinea Income Tax Agreement Act, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-53_e_hq_6214){#fn_SI-91-53_e_hq_6214-ID0EBBCA},
that the Agreement between Canada and Papua New Guinea for the Avoidance
of Double Taxation and the Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect to
Taxes on Income came into force on December 21, 1989.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-53_e_hq_6214-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-91-53_e_hq_6214}S.C.
  1989, c. 20, Part III
  :::

Ottawa, February 2, 1991

::: FlushRight
MICHAEL WILSON
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Finance*
