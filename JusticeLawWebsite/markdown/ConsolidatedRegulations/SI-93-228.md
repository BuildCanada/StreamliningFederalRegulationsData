## Order Designating the Minister for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-228
:::

[CULTURAL PROPERTY EXPORT AND IMPORT ACT](/eng/acts/C-51)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Minister for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 1993-1980 1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI_93-228_EN_hq_12211){#fn_SI_93-228_EN_hq_12211-ID0EFDAA}His
Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the
Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Cultural Property
Export and Import Act](/eng/acts/C-51), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1453 of June 25,
  1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-228_e_hq_6541){#fn_SI-93-228_e_hq_6541-ID0EBBCAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-228_e_hq_6541-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_SI-93-228_e_hq_6541}SI/93-107,
    1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3199
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Communications, a member
  of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the
  purposes of that Act.

<!-- -->

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI_93-228_EN_hq_12211-ID0EFDAA){#fn_SI_93-228_EN_hq_12211}\[Note:
  S.C. 1995, c. 11, s. 46, provided that every reference to "Minister of
  Communications" be read as "Minister of Canadian Heritage", effective
  July 12, 1996.\]
  :::
