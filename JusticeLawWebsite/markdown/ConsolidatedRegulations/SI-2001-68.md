## Hampton Place and Taylor Way Remission Order {#hampton-place-and-taylor-way-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-68
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Hampton Place and Taylor Way Remission Order

P.C. 2001-895  2001-05-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2001-68_e_hq_5130){#fn_SI-2001-68_e_hq_5130-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed [Hampton Place and Taylor Way Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2001-68).

## [Remission of Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605284 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted to the purchaser of a condominium unit
located at a municipal address set out in column 1 of the schedule, on a
leased strata lot indicated in column 2, of an amount equal to the tax
paid under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of
the purchase of that condominium unit and for which no tax was payable.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605286 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount applied for has not been otherwise
  rebated, credited or remitted; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written claim for the remission is made to the
  Minister of National Revenue within two years after the date of this
  Order.
