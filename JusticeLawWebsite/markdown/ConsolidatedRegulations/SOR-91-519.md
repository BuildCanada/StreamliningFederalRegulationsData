## National Battlefields Park By-Law {#national-battlefields-park-by-law .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-519
:::

[NATIONAL BATTLEFIELDS AT QUEBEC ACT](/eng/acts/N-3.4)

Registration 1991-09-05

By-Law Respecting the National Battlefields Park

P.C. 1991-1626  1991-09-05
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 4 of the National
Battlefields at Quebec Act, 1914[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-519_e_hq_6171){#fn_SOR-91-519_e_hq_6171-ID0EFBAA},
is pleased hereby to confirm the revocation, by the National
Battlefields Commission, of the [National Battlefields Park
By-law](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-519), C.R.C., c. 1042, and to confirm
the making, by the National Battlefields Commission, of the annexed
By-law respecting the National Battlefields Park, in substitution
therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-519_e_hq_6171-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR-91-519_e_hq_6171}S.C.
  1914, c. 46
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-938975 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This By-law may be cited as
the [National Battlefields Park By-law](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-519).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-938977 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this By-law,

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The National Battlefields
    Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lands owned by, or falling
    under the jurisdiction, control or care of, the Commission;
    ([parc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[ \[Repealed, SOR/2002-186, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[sports field]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sports field]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that portion of the Park
    that is bounded on the north by the Grande Allée, on the west by
    avenue Montcalm, on the south by avenue George VI and on the east by
    avenue Wolfe-Montcalm. ([terrain de sport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-186, s. 1
:::

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-938985 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person in the Park shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} displace, deface, soil, pollute or otherwise damage
    any property of the Commission; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pollute water, including water in any fountain, or
    throw or deposit any object, bathe in or permit any animal that the
    person has brought into the Park to be in any water.

  - [(c) to (i)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-186, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-186, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-437, s. 1
- SOR/2002-186, s. 2
:::

## [Sports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-938994 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person in the Park shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ride a bicycle, other than on a public road or on a
    path designated for that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ride on a skateboard, roller skates, in-line
    skates, roller skis or any other similar device, except

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a path designated for that purpose, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other area designated for that purpose;
      or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} engage in any organized sport or game, other than
    on the sports field or in any other area designated for that
    purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-186, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-296, s. 1
- SOR/2002-186, s. 3
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-186,
s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

## [Animals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939006 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who brings an animal into the Park shall keep it restrained by
  a leash that is not more than 3 m in length or confine it in a box,
  cage or vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person brings an animal into the Park and it
  defecates, the person shall collect the excrement and deposit it in a
  waste receptacle.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-186, s. 5
:::

SOR/2002-186, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}
