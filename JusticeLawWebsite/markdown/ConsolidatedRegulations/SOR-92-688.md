## Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 14 (CSIS) {#exempt-personal-information-bank-order-no.-14-csis .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-688
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1992-11-26

Order Respecting the Designation of the Canadian Security Intelligence
Service Investigational Records, Treasury Board Secretariat Registration
No. 002872, as an Exempt Personal Information Bank

P.C. 1992-2412  1992-11-26
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the President of the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the designation of
the Canadian Security Intelligence Service Investigational Records,
Treasury Board Secretariat Registration No. 002872, as an exempt
personal information bank](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-688).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949743 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 14
(CSIS)](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-688).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949745 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian Security
Intelligence Service Investigational Records, Treasury Board Secretariat
Registration No. 002872, a personal information bank under the control
of the Canadian Security Intelligence Service, is hereby designated as
an exempt bank on the basis of section 21 and paragraphs 22(1)(a) and
(b) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(3)(b) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), the laws
concerned, in relation to those files that are contained in the exempt
bank designated by section 2 that consist predominantly of personal
information described in subparagraph 22(1)(a)(ii) of that Act, are the
[Foreign Interference and Security of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5)
and the [Security Offences Act](/eng/acts/S-7).
