## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749629 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749631 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Iceland

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Iceland and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Iceland Tariff if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
    marking laws of Iceland and indicates that the goods are the product
    of Iceland or Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and nothing indicates
    that the goods are not the product of Iceland or Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Norway and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Norway Tariff if the marking of the
  goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Iceland and indicates
  that the goods are the product of Norway; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Switzerland or
  Liechtenstein and are entitled to the benefit of the Switzerland --
  Liechtenstein Tariff if the marking of the goods is in accordance with
  the marking laws of Iceland and indicates that the goods are the
  product of Switzerland or Liechtenstein.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Norway

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Norway and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Norway Tariff if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
    marking laws of Norway and indicates that the goods are the product
    of Norway or Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and nothing indicates
    that the goods are not the product of Norway or Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Iceland and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Iceland Tariff if the marking of the
  goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Norway and indicates
  that the goods are the product of Iceland; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Switzerland or
  Liechtenstein and are entitled to the benefit of the Switzerland --
  Liechtenstein Tariff if the marking of the goods is in accordance with
  the marking laws of Norway and indicates that the goods are the
  product of Switzerland or Liechtenstein.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Switzerland or Liechtenstein

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Switzerland or
  Liechtenstein and are entitled to the benefit of the Switzerland --
  Liechtenstein Tariff if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
    marking laws of Switzerland or Liechtenstein and indicates that the
    goods are the product of Switzerland or Liechtenstein or Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and nothing indicates
    that the goods are not the product of Switzerland or Liechtenstein
    or Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Iceland and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Iceland Tariff if the marking of the
  goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Switzerland or
  Liechtenstein and indicates that the goods are the product of Iceland;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are considered to originate in Norway and are
  entitled to the benefit of the Norway Tariff if the marking of the
  goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Switzerland or
  Liechtenstein and indicates that the goods are the product of Norway.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749650 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 1, 2009, but if they are registered after that day, they
come into force on the day they are registered.
