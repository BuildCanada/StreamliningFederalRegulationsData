[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General circumstances

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 112(1)(b) of the [Employment Insurance
  Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) and subject to subsection (2), the Commission
  may allow a longer period to make a request for reconsideration of a
  decision if the Commission is satisfied that there is a reasonable
  explanation for requesting a longer period and the person has
  demonstrated a continuing intention to request a reconsideration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Particular circumstances

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission must also be satisfied that the
  request for reconsideration has a reasonable chance of success, and
  that no prejudice would be caused to the Commission or a party by
  allowing a longer period to make the request, if the request for
  reconsideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is made after the 365-day period after the day on
    which the decision was communicated to the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is made by a person who submitted another
    application for benefits after the decision was communicated to the
    person; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is made by a person who has requested the
    Commission to rescind or amend the decision under section 111 of the
    [Employment Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 2013.
