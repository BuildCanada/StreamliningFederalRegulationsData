## Exclusion Approval Order for Certain Former Members of the Canadian Forces {#exclusion-approval-order-for-certain-former-members-of-the-canadian-forces .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-20
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2025-02-26

Exclusion Approval Order for Certain Former Members of the Canadian
Forces

P.C. 2025-98 2025-02-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada, under section 20
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776-ID0EDEEA},
approves the exclusion, by the Public Service Commission, from the
application of section 35.11[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133-ID0EBEEA}
of that Act of persons who

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776-ID0EDEEA){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12, 13
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133-ID0EBEEA){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133}S.C.
  2015, c. 5, s. 4
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on November 13, 2020 and
  ending on January 18, 2024,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} completed their third year of service in the
    Canadian Forces, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} were honourably released within the meaning of
    regulations made under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041-ID0EBBABEA};
    and

    - ::: Footnote
      [[Return to footnote
      ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041-ID0EBBABEA){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041}R.S., c.
      N-5
      :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} created an account in the Public Service Resourcing
  System on or before January 18, 2024.

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
section 35.11[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133-ID0EECDA}
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776-ID0EBCDA}
to persons who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on November 13, 2020 and
  ending on January 18, 2024,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} completed their third year of service in the
    Canadian Forces, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} were honourably released within the meaning of
    regulations made under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041-ID0EBAABDA};
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} created an account in the Public Service Resourcing
  System on or before January 18, 2024;

And whereas, in accordance with subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776-ID0EBACA},
the Public Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, under section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721920776-ID0EDCBA},
excludes from the application of section 35.11[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1721921133-ID0EBCBA}
of that Act persons who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on November 13, 2020 and
  ending on January 18, 2024,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} completed their third year of service in the
    Canadian Forces, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} were honourably released within the meaning of
    regulations made under the [National Defence
    Act](/eng/acts/N-5)[[Footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041){#fn_81000-2-5564_hq_1731690041-ID0EBAABBA};
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} created an account in the Public Service Resourcing
  System on or before January 18, 2024.

Gatineau, December 23, 2024
