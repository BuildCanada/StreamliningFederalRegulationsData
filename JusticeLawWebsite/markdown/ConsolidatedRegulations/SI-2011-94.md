## Order Transferring from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to the Minister of Health the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency and Ordering the Minister of Health to Preside over the Agency {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-to-the-minister-of-health-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-northern-economic-development-agency-and-ordering-the-minister-of-health-to-preside-over-the-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-94
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2011-12-07

Order Transferring from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development to the Minister of Health the Control and Supervision of the
Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency and Ordering the Minister
of Health to Preside over the Agency

P.C. 2011-1290 2011-11-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EEADAA} of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBADAA}, transfers from the
  Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to the Minister of
  Health the control and supervision of the portion of the federal
  public administration known as the Canadian Northern Economic
  Development Agency; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Health shall preside over
  the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency.
