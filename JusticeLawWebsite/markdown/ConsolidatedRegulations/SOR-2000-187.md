## [Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656223 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following are
designated as violations that may be proceeded with in accordance with
the Act, namely

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of a provision of the [Health of
  Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3), the [Plant Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) or the [Safe Food for Canadians
  Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1), or of a regulation made under one of those
  Acts, that is set out in column 1 of any Division of any Part of
  Schedule 1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of an order made by the Minister
  under a provision of the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) that
  is set out in column 1 of Division 1 of Part 2 of Schedule 1; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the refusal or neglect to perform any duty imposed by
  or under a provision of the [Health of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3),
  the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) or the [Safe Food for
  Canadians Act](/eng/acts/S-1.1), or of a regulation made under one of
  those Acts, that is set out in column 1 of any Division of any Part of
  Schedule 1.

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656234 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The classification of a
violation as a minor, serious or very serious violation of a provision
set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 1 is as set out in column 3
of that item.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656236 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the penalty in respect of a violation that is committed by
  an individual otherwise than in the course of a business and that is
  not committed to obtain a financial benefit is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$500, for a minor violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$800, for a serious violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$1300, for a very serious violation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the penalty in respect of a minor
  violation that is committed by a person in the course of a business or
  in order to obtain a financial benefit is \$1,300.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the penalty in respect of a violation
  that is committed by a person in the course of a business or in order
  to obtain a financial benefit is \$6,000 for a serious violation and
  \$10,000 for a very serious violation, with adjustments, if any,
  determined for each total gravity value, as established in accordance
  with section 6, that is set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 in
  accordance with the calculation set out in column 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-215, s. 1
- SOR/2016-226, s. 3(E)

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The total gravity value in
respect of each serious or very serious violation referred to in
subsection 5(3) shall be established by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} considering the following criteria:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the history of the person who committed the
    violation in respect of prior violations or convictions as described
    in column 2 of an item of Part 1 of Schedule 3,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the degree of intention or negligence on the part
    of the person who committed the violation as described in column 2
    of an item of Part 2 of Schedule 3, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the harm done or that could be done by the
    violation as described in column 2 of an item of Part 3 of Schedule
    3;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ascribing, for each of the criteria referred to in
  subparagraphs (a)(i), (ii) and (iii), the applicable gravity value
  that is set out in column 1 of an item of Parts 1, 2 and 3 of Schedule
  3; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} adding the values obtained under paragraph (b).

## [Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656255 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), where the Minister agrees to enter into a
  compliance agreement and the terms and conditions of the agreement
  include monetary expenditures to be made by a person, the amount of
  the penalty shall be reduced by one dollar for every two dollars spent
  by the person, with a maximum reduction to nil.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount set out in a notice of default served
  pursuant to subsection 10(4) of the Act shall be paid by certified
  cheque or money order made payable to the Receiver General for Canada
  within 15 days after service of the notice.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656259 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service
  of a notice of violation or any document originating from the Minister
  on an individual named in the notice or document may be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personally, by leaving a copy of it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with the individual at any place, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it is impracticable to find the individual,
      with someone who appears to be an adult member of the same
      household at the last known address or usual place of residence of
      the individual. The day on which the document is left with that
      person is deemed to be the day on which the document is served; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail or
    courier to the last known address or usual place of residence of the
    individual, or by sending a copy of it to the individual by fax or
    other electronic means.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of a notice of violation or any document
  originating from the Minister on a person other than an individual may
  be effected by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by fax, registered mail or
    courier to the head office or place of business of the person or to
    the person's agent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it at the person's head office or
    place of business, with an officer or other individual who appears
    to be in control or management of the head office or place of
    business or with the person's agent; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by electronic means other than
    by fax to any individual referred to in paragraph (b).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a notice of violation or any document originating
  from the Minister is sent by fax or by other electronic means, a copy
  of the document shall be sent either by courier or by registered mail
  within 48 hours following the original transmission.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656281 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection 9(1) of the Act, and subject to subsection
  (2), a person named in a notice of violation shall pay the amount of
  the penalty by certified cheque or money order made payable to the
  Receiver General for Canada within 30 days after the day on which the
  notice is served.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person named in a notice of violation may pay an
  amount equal to one half of the penalty, in lieu of the penalty,
  within 15 days after the day on which the notice is served.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), payment
  is deemed to be made:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated in the postmark stamped on
    the envelope, where the amount is sent by regular mail; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the date indicated in the receipt issued by the
    post office or the courier, where the amount is sent by registered
    mail or courier.

## [Review and Compliance Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656290 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person named in a notice of violation that contains a warning
  requests, pursuant to subsection 8(1) of the Act, a review of the
  facts of the violation by the Minister or the Tribunal, the request
  shall be made in writing within 30 days after the day on which the
  notice is served.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person named in a notice of violation that
  contains a penalty requests, pursuant to subsection 9(2) of the Act, a
  review of the facts of the violation by the Minister or the Tribunal
  or, if the penalty is \$2,000 or more, to enter into a compliance
  agreement with the Minister, the request shall be made in writing
  within 30 days after the day on which the notice is served.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), a request to
  enter into a compliance agreement shall include a proposal outlining
  in detail the corrective action that will be taken to ensure future
  compliance.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a person is notified
that the Minister refuses to enter into a compliance agreement, the time
within which the person may pay the amount of the penalty or request a
review by the Tribunal of the facts of the violation is 30 days after
the day on which the notice is served. The request must be in writing.
