## Canadian Computer Reservation Systems (CRS) Regulations {#canadian-computer-reservation-systems-crs-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-275
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1995-06-06

Regulations Respecting Computer Reservation Systems (CRS) Operated in
Canada for the Purpose of Displaying or Selling Air Services

P.C. 1995-887 1995-06-06
::::

:::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Government wishes to promote aeronautics, in accordance with
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), by ensuring that air carriers
offering passenger air services that are displayed or sold in Canada are
guaranteed fair and neutral presentation of those services in any
computer reservation system operated in Canada for the purpose of
displaying or selling air services;

And Whereas the Government wishes to promote aeronautics by ensuring
that travel agents and consumers have access to comprehensive, neutral
information about passenger air services that are displayed or sold in
Canada;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection
4.3(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EHCAA} and
section 4.9[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EFCAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting computer reservation systems (CRS)
operated in Canada for the purpose of displaying or selling air
services](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-275).

    ([transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    affrété]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[charterer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a travel organizer or

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973257 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of systems that are operated in Canada for the purpose of
displaying or selling air services, irrespective of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the legal status or nationality of the system vendor;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the source of the information used or the location of
  the relevant data processing centre; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the air services are provided.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of systems that are used by a carrier and its
affiliates or a charterer, in their own offices and at their own sales
counters, or to carrier Internet websites.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations exempts any person from the operation of the [Competition
Act](/eng/acts/C-34) or the [Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 2
:::

**[[6. to 8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6._to_8 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2004-91, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Display Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973269 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A system
  vendor shall ensure that all displays in a system respecting
  information provided by the participating carrier about the schedules,
  fares, rules or availability of that participating carrier meet the
  requirements of these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall ensure that, except in response
  to requests for information limited to specific carriers, these
  displays are comprehensive, neutral and non-discriminatory.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 4
:::

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall construct and order the information in the
  displays referred to in section 9 in a manner that is applied
  consistently to all participating carriers and to all city-pair
  markets within each display.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In ordering the information in these displays, a
  system vendor shall not use any factor that relates, directly or
  indirectly, to carrier identity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall, in constructing and ordering
  flights to or from a city with more than one airport, ensure that
  there will be no discrimination on the basis of airport served when
  that city is requested as the origin or destination.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall ensure that flights involving
  stops en route, changes of aircraft, carrier or airport or segments
  carried out by other modes of transportation are clearly identified.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), a system vendor shall
  include charter air services in these displays and shall clearly
  indicate that they are charter air services.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where more than one charterer is offering charter air
  service on the same flight in a city-pair market, a system vendor
  shall ensure that the service appears only once in these displays and
  that the charterers who are offering this service are clearly
  indicated.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall ensure that tourism products
  are not included in these displays.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall not create or maintain any
  function that automatically prefers one or more participating carriers
  over other participating carriers.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where a system vendor chooses to display, for any
  city-pair market, information about the schedules or fares of
  non-participating carriers, the information shall be displayed in the
  same manner for all the non-participating carriers that the system
  vendor chooses to display.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall provide to any person, on
  request, the current criteria used in constructing and ordering
  flights for these displays and the weight given to each criterion.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 5
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall allow a subscriber, from time to time and at no
  additional cost, to select one of the displays conforming with these
  Regulations to be the default display, and shall not override that
  selection.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A default display shall be at least as useful to
  subscribers, in terms of the basic functions or service enhancements
  offered and the ease with which those functions or service
  enhancements can be performed or implemented, as any other display
  offered by the system vendor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, in response to a stated
  consumer preference, another display may be requested for each
  transaction without the default display appearing.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 6
- SOR/2009-167, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-11-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 11"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a system vendor
provides information limited to fares, the fares shall be displayed in a
neutral and non-discriminatory manner and shall include at least the
fares provided to the system vendor by the carrier or charterer for each
class of service offered by that carrier or charterer for flights
included in the displays.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall not intentionally or negligently display
  incomplete, inaccurate or misleading information in its system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a system vendor knows that a carrier has
  provided inaccurate or misleading information about its air services
  or about the identity of the carriers operating its air services, the
  system vendor shall inform the carriers and the subscribers.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  participating carrier shall ensure that the information that it has
  agreed to supply to a system vendor is accurate and non-misleading.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every participating carrier shall ensure that flights
  involving stops en route, changes of aircraft, carrier or airport or
  segments carried out by other modes of transportation are clearly
  identified to the system vendor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where flights are operated by a carrier other than
  the carrier identified by the carrier designator code, the carrier
  whose designator code is being used shall ensure that the carrier
  operating the flight is, except in the case of short-term [ad
  hoc]{.otherLang lang="la"} arrangements, clearly indicated to the
  system vendor.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A charterer who provides information respecting
  charter air services to a system vendor shall provide the information
  in accordance with subsections (1) to (3).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A participating carrier or a charterer may provide
  information referred to in subsections (1) to (3) directly or through
  an intermediary acceptable to the participating carrier and system
  vendor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 7
:::

## [Constructing Connecting Flights]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973307 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall not use any factors that relate, directly or
  indirectly, to carrier identity in constructing connecting flights for
  a display.

- [(2) to (4)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall provide to all subscribers and
  participating carriers, on request, current information about

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all connecting points for each market;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all criteria used to select connecting points;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all criteria used to edit connecting flights; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the weight given to each criterion referred to in
    paragraphs (b) and (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 8
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91,
s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

## [Additional Obligations of System Vendors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973319 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall allow any carrier the opportunity to participate
  in its distribution facilities and service enhancements, subject to
  any technical constraints that are outside the control of the system
  vendor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall not require, as a condition for
  participation in its system, the purchase or sale of any other goods
  or services.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 10
- SOR/2009-167, s. 3(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-17-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 17"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall apply the same standards of care and promptness in
  loading the information of each participating carrier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a system vendor provides special loading
  capability to any participating carrier, it shall offer the same
  capability to all other participating carriers as soon as this is
  technically feasible.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall provide on request by any
  participating carrier all current database update procedures and data
  formats related to schedule and fare processing.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A system vendor is not
required to display information provided by a carrier that will not
enter into a contract with the system vendor in accordance with these
Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 11
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91,
s. 11\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  billing for the services of a system shall be in a form and of
  sufficient detail to allow a participating carrier to verify promptly
  and exactly which services have been used and the fees for those
  services.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The billing referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  provided by an electronic medium that is compatible with the
  electronic medium used by the participating carrier and shall include
  the following information for each billable segment:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of CRS booking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the (booking) status code;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the booking/cancellation indicator;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the PNR record locator number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the passenger name;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the IATA/ARC/BSP agency identification code, or
    other information identifying the agency;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the pseudo-city code;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the CRS transaction date (booking date);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the city-pair or segment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the flight date;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the flight number; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the class of service.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 12
:::

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  system vendor shall ensure that its distribution facilities are
  separated by software, either physically or logically, in a clear and
  verifiable manner from any hosting services that are provided to a
  carrier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall ensure equality of treatment of
  hosted and non-hosted carriers in respect of procedures, protocols,
  inputs and outputs.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A system vendor shall offer to participating carriers
  interfaces that are compatible with the relevant, generally accepted
  industry standards.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91,

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A system vendor shall
display the complete unedited text of these Regulations in the reference
information maintained in its system.

## [Service Enhancements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973357 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Where a system vendor
adds any improvement to distribution facilities, service enhancements or
the equipment used to provide distribution facilities or service
enhancements, it shall offer, and provide information about, that
improvement to all participating carriers, subject to any technical
constraints that are outside the control of the system vendor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-91, s. 14
- SOR/2009-167, s. 4(F)

make available to subscribers the internal reservations system display
of a participating carrier unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all participating carriers are offered the capability
  to make their internal reservations system displays available to
  subscribers; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a subscriber may see the internal reservations system

## [Obligations of Subscribers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973367 .Part}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A subscriber shall
provide to a consumer all the information provided by participating
carriers and charterers pursuant to section 14 respecting each service
in which the consumer expresses an interest

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} orally, at the time the consumer expresses an
  interest and at the time of reservation or sale; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in writing, at the time of reservation, when
  requested by the consumer, and at the time the ticket is issued.

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** In any direct oral
communication with a consumer concerning a flight that is part of a
code-sharing arrangement or long-term wet lease, a subscriber shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the consumer, before booking transportation,
  that the transporting carrier is not the carrier whose designator code
  will appear on the ticket and shall identify the transporting carrier;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time of sale, provide the consumer with
  written notice of the carrier that will be operating the service or
  segment of an itinerary.

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** When a transaction
between a subscriber and a consumer takes place in an alternative
communications medium where there is no oral exchange or written record,
the subscriber may maintain evidence of having met the requirements of
section 29 by including a notation in the passenger name record (PNR)
that the information was communicated to the consumer.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973375 .Part}

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** No carrier shall require,
directly or indirectly, a subscriber or potential subscriber to use a
system as a condition for obtaining any benefit, commission, rebate,
discount or other incentive for the sale of, or as a condition for
obtaining access to, air services provided by it or its affiliates.

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No system vendor
operating in Canada shall use its sales force responsible for marketing
the system to promote, directly or indirectly, any carrier to
subscribers.

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** No carrier shall use its
sales force responsible for marketing its air services in Canada to
promote, directly or indirectly, any system or system vendor to
subscribers.

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** No member of a sales
force responsible for marketing a system of a system vendor and no
member of a sales force responsible for marketing the air services of a
carrier shall make or participate in joint marketing calls or
presentations to subscribers.

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-91,
s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

## [Order Requiring Equivalent Treatment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-973381 .Part}

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister of
Transport is of the opinion that the treatment given to Canadian
participating carriers by a system vendor operating in a foreign country
is not equivalent to the treatment given to foreign participating
carriers with regard to any matter contained in these Regulations, the
Minister may, by order, require all system vendors operating in Canada
to treat carriers of the foreign country in a manner that is equivalent
to the treatment given to Canadian participating carriers in that
foreign country.
