## Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone Regulations {#eastern-canada-vessel-traffic-services-zone-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-99
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Registration 1989-02-09

Regulations Respecting the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone

P.C. 1989-147 1989-02-09
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 635.2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Canada Shipping
Act](/eng/acts/S-9), a copy of the proposed Regulations respecting the
Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zones, substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on July 2, 1988 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to ship owners, masters,
seamen and other interested persons to make representations to the
Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1987, c. 7, s. 75
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Acting Minister of Transport, pursuant to
sections 635.15[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0ENBAA}
and 635.16[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0ELBAA} of
the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), is pleased hereby to revoke
the Eastern Canada Traffic Zone Regulations, made by Order in Council
P.C. 1978-2590 of August 16, 1978[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EFBAA}, and to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic
Services Zone](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-99), in substitution therefor,
effective on the day on which sections 73 to 76 of An Act to amend the
Canada Shipping Act and to amend the Arctic Waters Pollution Prevention
Act, the Maritime Code Act and the Oil and Gas Production and
Conservation Act in consequence thereof, being chapter 7 of the Statutes
of Canada, 1987, come into force.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e-ID0EFBAA){#fn_2e}SOR/78-669, 1978
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3459
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921243 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-99).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921245 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a wharf, pier, lock,
    anchorage or mooring buoy; ([poste]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with reference to dangerous goods,
    has the same meaning as in section 2 of the [Dangerous Goods
    Shipping Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-951);
    ([classe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 654 of the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9).
    ([polluant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-215, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921252 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every ship of 500 tons gross tonnage or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every ship that is engaged in towing or pushing a
  vessel where the combined tonnage of the ship and the vessel being
  towed or pushed is 500 tons gross tonnage or more; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} every ship carrying a pollutant or dangerous goods,
  or engaged in towing or pushing a vessel carrying a pollutant or
  dangerous goods.

## [Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921257 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Eastern Canada Vessel
Traffic Services Zone shall consist of the Canadian waters on the east
coast of Canada south of the sixtieth parallel of north latitude and in
the St. Lawrence River east of 66°00′ west longitude except the waters
within Ungava Bay and the waters within the Vessel Traffic Services
Zones referred to in the Vessel Traffic Services Zone Regulations.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921259 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (6), the master of a ship shall ensure that a report is
  made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is about to enter the Eastern Canada
    Vessel Traffic Services Zone,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 24 hours before entering that Zone, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as soon as practicable before entering that
      Zone, where the estimated time of arrival of the ship at that Zone
      is less than 24 hours after the time the ship departed from the
      last port of call;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is departing from a berth within the
    Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone, two hours before the
    ship departs from the berth;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the ship is within the Eastern Canada Vessel
    Traffic Services Zone and has been stranded or has stopped as a
    result of a breakdown in the main propulsion systems or steering
    systems, or has been involved in a collision, immediately before the
    ship gets underway;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immediately before the ship crosses the seaward
    boundary of the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the ship enters that Zone, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the ship departs from that Zone; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on the arrival of the ship at a berth within the
    Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report required in the case referred to in
  paragraphs (1)(a) and (b) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the master of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the time the ship arrived at the position;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the course of the ship, if any;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the speed of the ship, if any;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the prevailing weather conditions;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case referred to in paragraph (1)(a), the
    estimated time that the ship will enter the Eastern Canada Vessel
    Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the
    estimated time that the ship will depart the berth;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the ship;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the estimated time of arrival of the ship at the
    destination;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the route the ship intends to take through the
    Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone to arrive at the
    destination;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the name of the last port of call of the ship;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the draft of the ship;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} any dangerous goods, listed by class, or pollutant,
    that is carried on board the ship or a vessel being towed or pushed
    by the ship;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/96-215, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} any defect in the ship's hull, main propulsion
    systems or steering systems, radars, compasses, radio equipment,
    anchors or cables;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} any discharge, or threat of discharge, of a
    pollutant from the ship into the water, and any damage to the ship
    that may result in the discharge of a pollutant from the ship into
    the water;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} the name of the Canadian or United States agent of
    the ship; and

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiration of a certificate referred to
    in Article VII of the International Convention on Civil Liability
    for Oil Pollution Damage, 1969, the International Oil Pollution
    Prevention Certificate, the International Pollution Prevention
    Certificate for the Carriage of Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk,
    the Certificate of Fitness and the Certificate of Compliance, if
    any, issued to the ship.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A report required in the case referred to in
  paragraph (1)(c) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any matter referred to in paragraph (1)(c).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A report required in the case referred to in
  paragraph (1)(d) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the position of the ship;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the time the ship will cross the seaward boundary
    of the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the prevailing weather and ice conditions.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A report required in the case referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the ship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radio call sign of the ship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the port where the berth is located
    within a port; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the time of the arrival of the ship at the berth.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A report required to be made pursuant to
  subsection (1) is not required to be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a case referred to in paragraph (1)(a), where
    the ship is on a voyage between two ports within the Eastern Canada
    Vessel Traffic Services Zone;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a case referred to in paragraph (1)(b), where
    the ship is proceeding to another berth in the same port; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a case referred to in paragraph (1)(d), where
    the seaward boundary of the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services
    Zone constitutes the seaward boundary of a Vessel Traffic Services
    Zone referred to in the Vessel Traffic Services Zone Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-215, s. 2
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The master of a ship that
is within or about to enter the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services
Zone shall make a report as soon as practicable where the master becomes
aware of any of the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the occurrence on board the ship of any fire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the involvement of the ship in a collision, grounding
  or striking;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any defect in the ship's hull, main propulsion
  systems or steering systems, radars, compasses, radio equipment,
  anchors or cables;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} another ship in apparent difficulty;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any obstruction to navigation;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any aid to navigation that is functioning improperly,
  damaged, off-position or missing;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any ice and weather conditions that are detrimental
  to safe navigation; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the presence of any pollutant in the water.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-215, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The master of a ship that
is within or about to enter the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services
Zone shall ensure that a report is made describing any significant
change in the information contained in a report made pursuant to these
Regulations, except where the report was made pursuant to
subparagraph 5(1)(d)(ii).

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  report made pursuant to these Regulations shall specify the time at
  which the report is made in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) using the
  24 hour clock system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report made pursuant to these Regulations shall be
  addressed to ECAREG CANADA.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-215, s. 4
:::
