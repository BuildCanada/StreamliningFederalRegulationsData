## Associate Judges (Tax Court of Canada) Regulations {#associate-judges-tax-court-of-canada-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-84
:::

[TAX COURT OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/T-2)

Registration 2023-05-01

Associate Judges (Tax Court of Canada) Regulations

P.C. 2023-373 2023-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, makes the annexed [Associate Judges (Tax Court
of Canada) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-84) under subsections
11.1(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGACA} and
(6)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EEACA} of the [Tax
Court of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBACA}.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of associate judges

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 11.1(2) of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-2), the
number of associate judges that may be appointed under subsection
11.1(1) of that Act is fixed at two.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Workload --- supernumerary associate
judges

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 11.1(6) of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-2), the
workload of a supernumerary associate judge is fixed at a minimum of 50%
of the workload of an associate judge.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
