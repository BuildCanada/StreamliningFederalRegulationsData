## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on the Suppression of Terrorism {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-on-the-suppression-of-terrorism .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-360
:::

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2001-10-02

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on the
Suppression of Terrorism

P.C 2001-1716 2001-10-02
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under section
41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Security Council
Resolution 1373 (2001) on September 28, 2001;

And whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to
make regulations for enabling the measures set out in that resolution to
be effectively applied;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to sections
2 and 3 of the [United Nations Act](/eng/acts/U-2), hereby makes the
annexed United Nations Suppression of Terrorism Regulations.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672907 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is a
    citizen within the meaning of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29),
    or a body corporate incorporated or continued by or under the laws
    of Canada or a province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Committee of the Security Council of the United Nations established
    by Resolution 1267 (1999) of October 15, 1999, adopted by the
    Security Council of the United Nations. ([Comité du Conseil de
    sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body corporate, trust,
    partnership or fund or an unincorporated association or
    organization. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Justice of the Federal
    Court or a judge of that Court designated by the Chief Justice.
    ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[listed person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[listed person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose name is
    listed in the schedule in accordance with section 2, with the
    exception of the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the entities referred to in the [Regulations
      Establishing a List of Entities](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-284);
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any [[person associated with the
      Taliban]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
      Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on
      Taliban, ISIL (Da'esh) and Al-Qaida. ([personne
      inscrite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an entity.
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means real and personal property of
    every description and deeds and instruments relating to or
    evidencing the title or right to property, or giving a right to
    recover or receive money or goods, and includes any funds, financial
    assets or economic resources. ([bien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672920 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  whose name is listed in the schedule is a person who the Governor in
  Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that
  there are reasonable grounds to believe

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has carried out, attempted to carry out,
    participated in or facilitated the carrying out of a terrorist
    activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is controlled directly or indirectly by any person
    conducting any of the activities set out in paragraph (a); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is acting on behalf of, or at the direction of, or
    in association with any person conducting any of the activities set
    out in paragraph (a).

- [(2) and (3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2006-165, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  listed person may apply in writing to the Minister to request to be
  removed from the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written application, the Minister
  shall decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend to the
  Governor in Council that the applicant be removed from the schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not make a decision on the
  application within 60 working days after receipt of the application,
  the Minister is deemed to have decided to recommend that the applicant
  remain a listed person.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall give notice without delay to the
  applicant of any decision taken or deemed to have been taken
  respecting the application.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A listed person may not make another application
  under subsection (1) unless there has been a material change in
  circumstances since the time the person made their last application.

## [Judicial Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672937 .Part}

- **[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within 60 working days after receipt
  of the notice referred to in subsection 2.1(4), the applicant may
  apply to a judge for judicial review of the decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an application is made, the judge shall without
  delay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 98\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, 2024, c. 16, s. 98\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide the applicant with a reasonable opportunity
    to be heard; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} determine whether the decision is reasonable on the
    basis of the information available to the judge and, if found not to
    be reasonable, order that the applicant no longer be a listed
    person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The judge may receive into evidence anything that, in
  the opinion of the judge, is reliable and appropriate, even if it
  would not otherwise be admissible under Canadian law, and may base his
  or her decision on that evidence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall cause to be published, without
  delay, in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) notice of a
  final order of a court that the applicant no longer be a listed
  person.

## [Providing or Collecting Funds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672960 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside Canada shall knowingly provide or collect by any means,
directly or indirectly, funds with the intention that the funds be used,
or in the knowledge that the funds are to be used, by a listed person.

## [Freezing Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672964 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person in Canada and no
Canadian outside Canada shall knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal directly or indirectly in any property of a
  listed person, including funds derived or generated from property
  owned or controlled directly or indirectly by that person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate, directly or indirectly, any
  transaction related to a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or other related service in
  respect of the property referred to in paragraph (a); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any property or any financial or other related
  service available, directly or indirectly, for the benefit of a listed
  person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-165, s. 6(F)

## [Causing, Assisting or Promoting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672975 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do anything
that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to cause,
facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 3 and 4.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672977 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property owned, held or controlled by or on behalf of a
listed person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada, and banks regulated by that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance activities in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity referred to in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity set
  out in section 7 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the Canadian
  Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or
    controlled by or on behalf of a listed person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) and no civil proceedings lie against a person for
  a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672995 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under these
  Regulations may, before doing so, apply to the Minister in writing for
  a certificate to exempt the activity from the application of the
  Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council of the United Nations did not intend that such an
  activity be prohibited or if the Security Council of the United
  Nations or the Committee of the Security Council has approved the
  activity in advance.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose property is affected by the application of section 4 may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the
