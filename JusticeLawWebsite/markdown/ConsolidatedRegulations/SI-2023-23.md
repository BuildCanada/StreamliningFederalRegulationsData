## [Remission and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1411512 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition of permanent resident status
fee

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted, to
persons whose application for permanent residency was processed under
one of the following temporary public policies, of the fees paid or
payable under subsection 303(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee
Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the
acquisition of permanent resident status:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Temporary public policy for the issuance of
  permanent resident visas for families of Afghan nationals who came to
  Canada under previous public policies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Amended temporary public policy to facilitate
  permanent residence for certain citizens of Afghanistan issued
  temporary resident permits;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Temporary public policy to facilitate permanent
  residence for certain citizens of Afghanistan;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Temporary public policy for the issuance of
  permanent resident visas for families of Afghan nationals who came to
  Canada under previous public policies --- intake cap increase;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Temporary public policy for extended families of
  former language and cultural advisors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted, to persons who
  meet the conditions set out in subsection (2), of the fees paid or
  payable under subsection 31(1) of the [Citizenship
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246) for an application for
  certificate of citizenship, under section 315 of the [Immigration and
  Refugee Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), and
  under subsection 2(1) of the [Passport and Other Travel Document
  Services Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253) for the
  services set out in any of items 9, 10 and 14 of the schedule to those
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission is granted on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application for the services was made abroad
    during the period beginning on August 25, 2021 and ending on
    December 31, 2023;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is a Canadian citizen or permanent
    resident of Canada who was in Afghanistan or in another country en
    route to Canada and who urgently needed to travel as a result of the
    fall of Kabul in August 2021; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the person was in a country other than
    Afghanistan when they applied for the services referred to in
    subsection (1), their urgent travel was facilitated by Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee for consular services

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted, to persons who
  meet the conditions set out in subsection (2), of the fees paid or
  payable under section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538) for the issuance of a
  temporary passport.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission is granted on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on August 25, 2021 and
    ending on December 31, 2023, the person made, at a mission abroad,
    an application for a temporary passport; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person urgently needed to travel as a result of
    the fall of Kabul in August 2021.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1411535 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day made

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
