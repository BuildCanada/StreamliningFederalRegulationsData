## Exclusion Approval Order for Certain Employees and Certain Positions (Air Traffic Control Group), 1989 {#exclusion-approval-order-for-certain-employees-and-certain-positions-air-traffic-control-group-1989 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-125
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33)

Registration 1989-05-10

Order Approving the Exclusion by the Public Service Commission from the
Operation of Section 10 and Subsection 21(1) of the Public Service
Employment Act of Certain Persons and Positions

P.C. 1989-724 1989-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided it is not practicable
nor in the best interests of the Public Service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to qualifications other than language
  skills, to apply section 10 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to training positions in the Department of
  Transport to which are appointed employees of that Department who
  participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for air traffic
  control training or in the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down
  Program,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to qualifications other than language
  skills, to apply section 10 of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to operational positions in the Department of
  Transport to which will be appointed trainees who have successfully
  completed their training in the Selection by Seniority Program for air
  traffic control training or in the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer
  Down Program,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment to training positions in the Department of Transport of
  certain employees of that Department who participate in the Selection
  by Seniority Program for air traffic control training or in the Air
  Traffic Controllers Transfer Down Program,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment of operational positions in the Department of Transport of
  trainees who have successfully completed their training in the
  Selection by Seniority Program for air traffic control training or in
  the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down Program,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to apply subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to all persons who, but for this
  Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection against the
  appointment to positions in the Department of Transport of trainees
  who have not successfully completed or have terminated without
  completing their training in the Selection by Seniority Program for
  air traffic control training or in the Air Traffic Controllers Traffic
  Down Program,

during the period beginning on May 1, 1989 and ending on April 30, 1991.

And Whereas the Public Service Commission recommends that, pursuant to
subsection 41(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), the Governor in Council approve the exclusion
by the Public Service Commission from the operation of section 10 and
subsection 21(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) of certain persons and positions.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Secretary of State, pursuant to subsection 41(1)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order approving the exclusion by the Public
Service Commission from the operation of section 10 and subsection 21(1)
of the Public Service Employment Act of certain persons and
positions](/eng/regulations/SI-89-125).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642035 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exclusion Approval Order for Certain Employees and Certain
Positions (Air Traffic Control Group),
1989](/eng/regulations/SI-89-125).

## [Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642037 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of section 10 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), in relation to
qualifications other than language skills, of training positions in the
Department of Transport to which will be appointed employees of that
Department who participate in the Selection by Seniority Program for air
traffic control training or in the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down
Program, during the period beginning on May 1, 1989 and ending on
April 30, 1991, is hereby approved.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of section 10 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), in relation to
qualifications other than language skills, of operational positions in
the Department of Transport to which will be appointed trainees who have
successfully completed their training in the Selection by Seniority
Program for air traffic control training or in the Air Traffic
Controllers Transfer Down Program, during the period beginning on May 1,
1989 and ending on April 30, 1991, is hereby approved.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment to training positions in the Department of
Transport of certain employees of that Department who participate in the
Selection by Seniority Program for air traffic control training or in
the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down Program, during the period
beginning on May 1, 1989 and ending on April 30, 1991, is hereby
approved.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment to operational positions in the Department of
Transport of trainees who have successfully completed their training in
the Selection by Seniority Program for air traffic control training or
in the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down Program, during the period
beginning on May 1, 1989 and ending on April 30, 1991, is hereby
approved.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The exclusion by the Public
Service Commission, from the operation of subsection 21(1) of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), of all persons who,
but for this Order, would have a right of appeal under that subsection
against the appointment to positions in the Department of Transport of
trainees who have not successfully completed or have terminated without
completing their training in the Selection by Seniority Program for air
traffic control training or in the Air Traffic Controllers Transfer Down
Program during the period beginning on May 1, 1989 and ending on
April 30, 1991, is hereby approved.
