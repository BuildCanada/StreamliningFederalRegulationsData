## Aircraft (International Service) Remission Order {#aircraft-international-service-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/79-2
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1979-01-10

Order Respecting the Partial Remission of Customs Duties, Sales and
Excise Taxes Paid on Parts, Equipment and Other Items for Use by
Canadian Air Carriers Providing International Commercial Air Service

P.C. 1978-3762 1978-12-14
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 17 of the Financial Administation Act, is pleased hereby to
revoke the Aircraft (Combined Services) Remission Order made by Order in
Council P.C. 1970-356 of 24th February, 1970[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFCAA}, as
amended[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EDCAA}, and to make
the annexed Order respecting the partial remission of Customs duty,
sales and excise taxes paid on parts, equipment and other items for use
by Canadian air carriers providing international commercial air service.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634347 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Aircraft (International Service) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-79-2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634349 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[aircraft parts and equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft parts and equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means aircraft
    parts, engine parts or equipment for incorporation into an aircraft
    or aircraft engine, and includes safety equipment for use aboard an
    aircraft; ([pièces et matériel d'aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[available ton miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[available ton miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the revenue miles
    flown by an aircraft multiplied by the payload capacity in tons of
    that aircraft; ([tonnes-milles disponibles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[commissary and passenger convenience item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commissary and passenger convenience item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an item for use aboard an aircraft

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the preparation and serving of food and drink,
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the comfort or convenience of passengers;
      ([vivres et objets pour le confort des passagers]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[eligible carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial air
    carrier incorporated under the laws of Canada and licensed by the
    Canadian Transport Commission to provide international air service
    to the public; ([transporteur admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all qualifying aircraft owned or
    leased by an eligible carrier and that have been used by the carrier
    at any time to provide commercial air service; ([matériel
    volant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any flight other
    than a flight originating and terminating in Canada; ([vol
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international usage percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international usage percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    percentage that the available ton miles flown by a fleet on
    international flights during a year is of the total available ton
    miles flown by the fleet during that year; ([pourcentage
    d'utilisation internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[materials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means materials for incorporation
    into an aircraft or aircraft engine; ([matières]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[payload capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payload capacity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a flight,
    means the maximum take-off weight on wheels as prescribed by the
    Department of Transport, of an aircraft, less

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual weight of the aircraft, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the weight of the fuel on board the aircraft at
      the commencement of the flight; ([capacité de la charge
      payante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fixed wing
    aircraft having a maximum take-off weight on wheels, as prescribed
    by the Department of Transport, that is greater than 75,000 pounds;
    ([aéronef admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revenue miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revenue miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means miles flown by an
    aircraft in respect of which consideration is received for the
    carriage of passengers or cargo by the carrier operating the
    aircraft; ([milles payants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar year.
    ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634367 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to an eligible carrier of a portion, determined in accordance with
section 5, of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of aircraft parts and equipment
that, on or after January 1, 1975, are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased in Canada by the carrier, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported into Canada by the carrier

for use in qualifying aircraft used to provide commercial air service.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to an eligible carrier of a portion, determined in accordance with
section 5, of the customs duties, sales tax and excise tax paid or
payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of materials and commissary and
passenger convenience items that, on or after January 1, 1975, are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased in Canada by the carrier, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported into Canada by the carrier

for use in qualifying aircraft used to provide commercial air service.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The portion of the duty and
taxes referred to in section 3 or 4 is that percentage of the duty and
taxes equal to the international usage percentage of the fleet of the
eligible carrier during the year in which the carrier purchased the
goods or imported the goods into Canada.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634381 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
pursuant to section 3 or 4 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application is made by the eligible carrier, in a
  form satisfactory to the Deputy Minister of National Revenue for
  Customs and Excise, not later than four years after the end of the
  year in which the carrier purchased the goods or imported the goods
  into Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application required pursuant to paragraph (a)
  contains a declaration whereby the eligible carrier certifies that the
  goods for which remission is being claimed were

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} purchased in Canada by the carrier, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} imported into Canada by the carrier

  for use in qualifying aircraft used to provide commercial air service.
