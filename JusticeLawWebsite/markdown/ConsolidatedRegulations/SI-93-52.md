## Order Transferring to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology under Sections 9 and 15 of the Department of Industry, Science and Technology Act Respecting the Hibernia Development Project {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-energy-mines-and-resources-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-under-sections-9-and-15-of-the-department-of-industry-science-and-technology-act-respecting-the-hibernia-development-project .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-52
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-04-07

Order Transferring to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, the
Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Industry, Science and
Technology under Sections 9 and 15 of the Department of Industry,
Science and Technology Act Respecting the Hibernia Development Project

P.C. 1993-557  1993-03-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, for
the purposes of the Hibernia Development Project, as described in the
Hibernia Development Plan approved by the Canada-Newfoundland Offshore
Petroleum Board Decision 86.01, as amended by Decision 90.01, the
powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Industry, Science and
Technology under sections 9 and 15 of the Department of Industry,
Science and Technology Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-52_e_hq_6515){#fn_SI-93-52_e_hq_6515-ID0EBBAA}
to develop a project of special assistance in respect of the Hibernia
Development Project and to implement the project through loan agreements
with the owners of the Hibernia Development Project.
