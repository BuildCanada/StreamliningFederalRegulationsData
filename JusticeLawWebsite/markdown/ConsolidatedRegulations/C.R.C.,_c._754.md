## Customs Accounting Document Error Remission Order {#customs-accounting-document-error-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 754
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties Imposed under the
Customs Tariff and Taxes Imposed under Division III of Part IX and under
any other Part of The Excise Tax Act, Underpaid Due to Error on Customs
Accounting Document
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561534 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Customs Accounting Document Error Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._754).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561538 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount by which the customs duties imposed under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the taxes imposed under Division III of
Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
on goods imported into Canada were underpaid in error, if the amount
payable does not amount to more than \$7.50 in respect of any one
customs accounting document, other than an interim document.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
:::
