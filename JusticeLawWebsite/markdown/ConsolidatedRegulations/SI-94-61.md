## Order Designating the National Capital Region as the Place in Canada at which the Principal Office of the National Round Table on the Environment and the Economy Shall be Located {#order-designating-the-national-capital-region-as-the-place-in-canada-at-which-the-principal-office-of-the-national-round-table-on-the-environment-and-the-economy-shall-be-located .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-61
:::

[NATIONAL ROUND TABLE ON THE ENVIRONMENT AND THE ECONOMY
ACT](/eng/acts/N-16.4)

Registration 1994-05-18

Order Designating the National Capital Region as the Place in Canada at
which the Principal Office of the National Round Table on the
Environment and the Economy Shall be Located

P.C. 1994-695  1994-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 14 of the [National Round Table
on the Environment and the Economy Act](/eng/acts/N-16.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-94-61_e_hq_6559){#fn_SI-94-61_e_hq_6559-ID0EBBAA},
is pleased hereby to designate the National Capital Region as the place
in Canada at which the principal office of the National Round Table on
the Environment and the Economy shall be located.
