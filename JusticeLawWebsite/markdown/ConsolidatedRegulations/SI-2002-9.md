## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-great-slave-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2002-9
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2002-01-02

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2001-2304  2001-12-13
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby
orders that any portion of the lands described in the schedule hereto
lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet), of the ordinary high water mark
along the shore line of Great Slave Lake, in the Northwest Territories,
be included in the grant of these said lands.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#606009 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-606010 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lots numbered 137, 149 and a road, located within the
Community of Fort Resolution, in the Northwest Territories, as said lots
and road are shown on a plan of survey of record number 82896 in the
Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the
Land Titles Office for the Northwest Territories at Yellowknife under
number 3446.
