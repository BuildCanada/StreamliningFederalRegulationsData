## Export Permits Regulations {#export-permits-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-204
:::

Export Permits Regulations

P.C. 1997-583 1997-04-15

the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to paragraphs 12(a)[[Footnote

annexed [Export Permits Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-204).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005233 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a resident of Canada who
    applies for a permit or an amendment to a permit.
    ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[controlled goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2023-118, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([bureau de
    douane]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[DPA controlled goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DPA controlled goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    [[controlled goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 35
    of the [Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1). ([marchandises
    contrôlées de la LPD]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods as described in the Guide that are intended
      for export to a destination specified in respect of those goods in
      section 2 of the List; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are intended for export to a country
      included in the [Area Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543).
      ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
    of the List. ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202). ([Liste]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
    ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2023-118, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an export permit issued
    pursuant to subsection 7(1) of the Act. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[United States export authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[United States export authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    copy of any of the following approvals issued by the United States
    under the International Traffic in Arms Regulations, Title 22, Parts
    120-130 of the Code of Federal Regulations (United States):

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an export licence;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Warehousing and Distribution Agreement;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Technical Assistance Agreement;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Manufacturing Licence Agreement;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a re-export authorization letter; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a U.S. export licence exemption. ([autorisation
      d'exportation des États-Unis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005256 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply to the exportation of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} softwood lumber products that are described in item
    5104 of Group 5 of the schedule to the List; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any goods that are referred to in items 5200 to
    5210 of Group 5 of the schedule to the List.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) ceases to have effect on the day
  item 5104 of Group 5 of the schedule to the List is repealed.

## [Application for a Permit: Strategic and Military Goods and Technology]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005265 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section does not apply to the exportation of goods referred to in
  subsection 4(1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a permit must submit to the Minister
  a duly completed and signed application form, provided by the
  Minister, containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the application form is
    completed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, telephone number,
    email address and any facsimile number and any identifier number
    assigned by the Minister and, if the applicant is a corporation, the
    name and telephone number of a contact person familiar with the
    application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is applying for a permit on behalf
    of, or for the use of another person or corporation who will export
    or transfer the goods or technology, the name, address, telephone
    number, email address and any facsimile number of the other person
    or corporation and any identifier number assigned by the Minister
    and the name and telephone number of a contact person familiar with
    the application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, email address
    and any facsimile number of each consignee and the name and
    telephone number of a contact person associated with the consignee
    who is familiar with the application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country in which the goods or technology are to
    be used or the country of final destination;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for each type of separately identifiable goods or
    technology,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their country of origin and, if any portion of
      them is of United States origin and is included in item 5400 of
      Group 5 of the schedule to the List, their proportion to the total
      cost of the goods or technology, expressed as a percentage,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are included in the schedule to the
      List, their corresponding item number in the Guide,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods and technology
      including, as applicable, their purpose and technical
      specifications, with sufficient detail to disclose their true
      identity and in terms that avoid the use of trade names, technical
      names or general terms that do not adequately describe them,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, unit value and total market value
      of the goods or technology,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the factory where the goods or technology were
      made or the first shipping point in Canada, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the approximate mass, net weight or volume of
      the goods or technology;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the means by which the permit
    should be sent to the applicant or the exporter; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information to establish that the export or
    transfer of the goods or technology is consistent with the purpose
    for which the export or transfer of the goods or technology is
    controlled, consisting of one or more of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an International Import Certificate,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an End-use Certificate,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an End-use Statement,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the contract of sale between the
      applicant or the exporter and the person from whom the applicant
      or the exporter purchased the goods or technology,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the contract of sale between the
      applicant or the exporter and the person to whom the applicant
      sold the goods or technology for export or transfer,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the commercial invoice that relates to
      the export or transfer,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a letter of credit or other financial
      documentation which identifies the Canadian and foreign financial
      institutions involved in the export or transfer,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person from whom
      the applicant or exporter acquired the goods or technology,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the intended end-use of the goods or technology
      by their consignee,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the intended end-use location of the goods or
      technology, if different from the location of the consignee,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} an import permit issued by the government of the
      country for which the goods or technology are destined, or

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} an in-transit authorization.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the application form, the applicant
  must submit to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a declaration that, to the best of their knowledge,
    the goods or technology will enter into the economy of the country
    under paragraph (2)(e) that is identified in the application form,
    and will not be transhipped or diverted from that country;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods or technology referred to in
    Group 2, Group 6 or item 5504 of Group 5 of the schedule to the
    List:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they are DPA controlled goods

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} proof of registration or exemption from
        registration under the [Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1)
        and the [Controlled Goods
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-32); or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} proof that the applicant or the exporter is a
        person that Part 2 of the [Defence Production
        Act](/eng/acts/D-1) does not apply to, under section 36 of that
        Act; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are not DPA controlled goods, a
      declaration attesting that the applicant has reviewed the [Defence
      Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1) and determined that they are not
      exporting or transferring DPA controlled goods; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a United States export authorization in respect of
    the following goods

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any DPA controlled goods that are of United
      States origin,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any goods incorporating DPA controlled goods of
      United States origin, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any goods manufactured in Canada using any DPA
      controlled goods of United States origin.

## [Application for a Permit: Certain Forest Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1402331 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to the exportation of goods that are referred to in
  items 5101 to 5103 of Group 5 of the schedule to the List.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a permit must submit to the Minister
  a duly completed and signed application form, provided by the
  Minister, containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the application form is
    completed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, telephone number,
    email address and any facsimile number and any identifier number
    assigned by the Minister, and if the applicant is a corporation, the
    name and telephone number of a contact person who is familiar with
    the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is applying for a permit on behalf
    of, or for the use of another person or corporation who will export
    the goods, the name, address, telephone number, email address and
    any facsimile number of the other person or corporation and any
    identifier number assigned by the Minister, and the name and
    telephone number of a contact person who is familiar with the
    application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in British Columbia, the customs
    office at which the logs will be exported;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number, email address
    and any facsimile number of each consignee, and the name and
    telephone number of a contact person associated with the consignee
    who is familiar with the application;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country in which the goods are to be consumed
    or the country of final destination;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested outside of Canada, the country
    of origin of the logs;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in Canada, the province in which
    the logs have been harvested;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods, including species,
    total quantity, total volume or net weight and total value of the
    goods;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in British Columbia, the sort,
    grade and scale of the logs and any other identifier marks,
    including timber mark, boom number and load slip number;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in British Columbia, the name of
    the transportation company and means of transport;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in British Columbia, their
    anticipated date of export;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the means by which the permit
    should be sent to the applicant or the exporter;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested in British Columbia, any
    documentation of authorization, recommendation or consent from the
    applicable authority; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} for logs harvested on provincial lands in British
    Columbia,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is not the permit holder listed
      on the provincial permit, written confirmation from the provincial
      permit holder that the applicant is authorized to submit the
      application on their behalf,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the consignee identified under paragraph (e)
      differs from the consignee listed on the provincial permit,
      written confirmation from the provincial permit holder that the
      consignee listed on the application is accurate, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a detailed summary of the scale of the logs.

## [Shipping Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005306 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** At the time of exportation
of the goods, including goods containing controlled technology, the
exporter must provide the permit to an officer at the customs office,
within the reporting time frames specified in the [Reporting of Exported
Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-23).

compare the information in the permit with that contained in the

## [Amendments to Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005311 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A permit holder may, in
writing, before the expiry date shown on the permit, make a request to
the Minister to amend the permit. The request must state the terms or
conditions of the permit in respect of which the amendment is being
requested and the reasons for the amendment.
