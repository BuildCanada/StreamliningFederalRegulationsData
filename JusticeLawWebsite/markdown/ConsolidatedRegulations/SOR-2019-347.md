## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206636 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Energy Regulator
      Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization
      referred to in paragraph 275(3)(b) of the
      Act. ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of a permit or
      certificate issued under Part 4 of the Act in respect of an
      international or interprovincial power
      line. ([titulaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[international or interprovincial power line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international or interprovincial power
      line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility referred to in
      subsection 271(1) of the Act. ([ligne internationale ou
      interprovinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day that is not a
      Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of Documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, if a document that is available
  in both official languages is incorporated by reference as amended
  from time to time, any amendment to that document is incorporated only
  when the amendment is available in both official languages.

## [Prescribed Area]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206647 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 273(1) of the Act, the prescribed area means
  the strip of land on which an international or interprovincial power
  line is located and that corresponds to the right of way of that line.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Right of way

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The right of way is the land registered or recorded
  as the right of way in the land registry office or other office where
  title to land is registered for the locality in which that land is
  situated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of right of way

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there is no right of way for the international or
  interprovincial power line, the prescribed area is the strip of land
  30 m on each side from the centre of the line.

## [Ground Disturbance --- Specified Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206655 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities not included

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph (a) of the definition [[ground
disturbance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act, in respect
of an international or interprovincial power line, the following
activities are specified:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cultivation to a depth of less than 45 cm below the
  surface of the ground; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other activity to a depth of less than 30 cm and
  that does not result in a reduction of the earth cover over any
  underground portion of a line.

## [Ground Disturbance --- Temporary Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206660 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If the holder, after having
received a locate request from a person that intends to engage in an
activity that causes a ground disturbance within a prescribed area,
indicates an area that is situated in the vicinity of an international
or interprovincial power line and that may extend beyond the prescribed
area, the ground disturbance is prohibited within that area during the
period referred to in subsection 276(1) of the Act.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206663 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Locate request --- timing

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that intends to construct a facility across, on, along or under an
  international or interprovincial power line or engage in an activity
  that causes a ground disturbance within a prescribed area, must make a
  locate request at least three working days before the day on which the
  construction or activity is to start.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Locate request --- making

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The locate request must be made to a one-call centre
  if there is one within the area where the intended construction or
  activity takes place and if there is none in the area, to the holder
  directly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an unexpected situation that could
  endanger life or cause substantial property or environmental damage
  and that requires immediate action, the period set out in
  subsection (1) does not apply and the locate request must be made as
  soon as possible before the construction or activity starts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One-call centre

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A one-call centre is an organization that, for the
  purposes of protecting the underground infrastructures of its members
  from damage and ensuring public safety,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receives locate requests from persons within a
    defined geographical area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notifies its members that may be affected by any
    proposed construction or by any proposed activity that would cause a
    ground disturbance, if that construction or activity is the subject
    of a locate request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any person that intends to
construct a facility across, on, along or under an international or
interprovincial power line, engage in an activity that would cause a
ground disturbance within a prescribed area or operate a vehicle or
mobile equipment across an international or interprovincial power line
must, before the construction, activity or operation is to start, inform
all persons working on their behalf, including contractors and
subcontractors, of the obligations under these Regulations.

## [Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206677 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of facility

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  construction of a facility --- in an area other than an offshore area
  --- across, on, along or under an international or interprovincial
  power line is authorized if the person that intends to construct the
  facility

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains written authorization from the holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} agrees with the holder in writing to a procedure
    and schedule for the work;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes a locate request in accordance with section
    5; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meets the following requirements:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} complies with the conditions set out in the
      authorization,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of overhead crossings, ensures that
      the construction is designed and executed in accordance with CSA
      Group standard C22.3 No. 1, Overhead systems, as amended from time
      to time, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of underground crossings, ensures
      that the construction is designed and executed in accordance with
      CSA Group standard C22.3 No. 7, Underground systems, as amended
      from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the authorization is suspended, the construction
  must cease for the duration of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person that is undertaking the construction of a
  facility must ensure that the construction is carried out in
  accordance with the technical details that are set out in the person's
  request for authorization and that have been accepted by the holder,
  as well as with the conditions set out in the holder's authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ground disturbance activity

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  activity --- in an area other than an offshore area --- that would
  cause a ground disturbance within a prescribed area is authorized if
  the person that intends to engage in the activity

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains written authorization from the holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} agrees with the holder in writing to a procedure
    and schedule for the work;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes a locate request in accordance with section
    5; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} complies with the conditions set out in the
    authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the authorization is suspended, the activity must
  cease for the duration of the suspension.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any person that is engaged in an activity that causes
  a ground disturbance within a prescribed area must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the activity is carried out in
    accordance with the conditions set out in the holder's
    authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that there is no additional earth or other
    material within the prescribed area that could reduce the ground to
    conductor clearance of the overhead line;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately notify the holder of any contact with
    any underground portion of the holder's international or
    interprovincial power line; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notify the holder at least 24 hours before
    backfilling over any underground portion of the holder's
    international or interprovincial power line that is exposed, unless
    the holder and the person that is engaged in the activity agree
    otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operation across a power line

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The operation of a vehicle
or mobile equipment across an international or interprovincial power
line is authorized if the person that intends to operate the vehicle or
mobile equipment obtains authorization from the holder.

## [Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206709 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction of international or
interprovincial power line

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 272(1)(c) of the Act, a person may construct an international
or interprovincial power line that passes on, over, along or under a
facility in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of overhead crossings, the line is
  designed and constructed in accordance with CSA Group standard C22.3
  No. 1, Overhead systems, as amended from time to time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of underground crossings, the line is
  designed and constructed in accordance with CSA Group standard C22.3
  No. 7, Underground systems, as amended from time to time;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the line does not extend beyond any terminal of that
  line;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the line is designed and constructed in accordance
  with any applicable provincial and federal legislation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person obtains written permission from the owner
  of the facility; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person and the owner of the facility agreed in
  writing to a construction procedure and schedule for the work.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206718 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[coming-into-force
day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In sections 12 to 15,
[[coming-into-force day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day on which the
[Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1) comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any leave granted by the
holder before the coming-into-force day, under subsection 58.31(2) of
the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), as it read immediately
before that date, to operate a vehicle or mobile equipment across an
international or interprovincial power line, remains in force until the
expiry date set out in the leave.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave --- excavation or construction of
facility

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any excavation or the
construction of a facility that was started before the coming-into-force
day and for which leave of the National Energy Board was not necessary
under paragraph 58.33(c) of the [National Energy Board
Act](/eng/acts/N-7), as it read immediately before that day, may be
continued after that day if it is carried out in conformity with the
applicable conditions set out in section 3 of the [Power Line Crossing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-500), as it read immediately before
that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Construction --- international or
interprovincial power line

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any construction of an
international or interprovincial power line that passes on, over, along
or under a facility for which leave was granted before the
coming-into-force day, under subsections 58.28(1) and (4) of the
[National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), as it read immediately
before that date, continues to be authorized if the construction is
carried out in conformity with the applicable circumstances set out in
section 4 of the [Power Line Crossing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-500), as it read immediately before
that day.
