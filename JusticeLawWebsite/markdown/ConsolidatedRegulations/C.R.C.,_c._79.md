## Cartierville Airport Zoning Regulations {#cartierville-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 79
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Cartierville Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562063 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cartierville Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._79).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562065 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Cartierville Airport, in the
      Province of Quebec; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane the lower end of which is a horizontal line at
      right angles to the centre line of a strip and passing through a
      point at the strip end on the centre line of the strip; ([surface
      d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      horizontal plane centering on and located 150 feet above the
      assigned elevation of the airport reference point; ([surface
      horizontale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401,
      s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, 700 feet in width, including the
      runway, especially prepared for the take-off and landing of
      aircraft in a particular direction; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane extending upward and outward from the outer lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surface to an intersection with
      the horizontal surface or other transitional surfaces. ([surface
      de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding the definition [[horizontal
  surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection (1), for the purposes of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that part of lot 365 to which these Regulations
    apply, lot 365-1 and that part of lot 367, West of the Canadian
    National Railway right-of-way, to which these Regulations apply, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lots 369-2, 370-6, 367-110 and 2660,

  which lie in the parish of St. Laurent, [[horizontal
  surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary horizontal plane
  centering on and located 196 feet above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-401, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 104 feet above
sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-562082 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, as more particularly described in Part II of
the schedule.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

Premising that the bearings hereinafter mentioned are astronomical and
are referred to the centre line of strip 10-28, Cartierville Airport,
Province of Quebec, as South eighty-six degrees forty-nine minutes West
(S. 86°49′ W)

BEGINNING at the threshold point being distant nine hundred and fifty
feet (950′) Southwesterly along the centre line of strip 10-28 from the
Northeasterly end thereof; THENCE, continuing along the said centre
line, South eighty-six degrees forty-nine minutes West (S. 86°49′ W.) a
distance of three thousand feet (3,000′) to a point thereon; THENCE,
North three degrees eleven minutes West (N. 03°11′ W.) a distance of two
hundred and fifty feet (250′) to a point henceforth designated as the
airport reference point, said airport reference point being also at one
thousand seven hundred and eighty-three feet and eight tenths (1,783.8′)
measured along a line having a bearing of North six degrees fifteen
minutes twenty seconds East (N. 06°15′20″ E.) from an iron pin at the
intersection of the Northeasterly boundary of lot 212 with the lot line
between lots 213 and 214, the said Airport Reference Point having an
assigned elevation of one hundred and four feet (104′) above sea level.
