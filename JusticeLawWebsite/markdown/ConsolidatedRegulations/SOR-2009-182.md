## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749088 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[acceptable rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acceptable rating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rating given
      by a credit rating agency to an issuer at the time of the issuance
      or renewal of a letter of credit that is at least equal to one of
      the following ratings:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} A, from Dominion Bond Rating Service Limited;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} A, from Fitch Ratings;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} A2, from Moody's Investors Service; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} A, from Standard & Poor's Ratings Services.
        ([note acceptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
      Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank or authorized foreign
      bank, as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).
      ([banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member or former
      member of a plan or any person who is entitled to pension benefits
      under the plan except

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former member who has transferred or has
        chosen to transfer their pension benefit credit under section 26
        of the Act; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a former member for whom the administrator has
        purchased an immediate or deferred life annuity.
        ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[beneficiary representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[beneficiary representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a union
      representative or court-appointed representative of a beneficiary.
      ([représentant des bénéficiaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cooperative credit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cooperative credit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      cooperative credit society to which the [Cooperative Credit
      Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies or a cooperative
      credit society that is incorporated and regulated by or under an
      Act of the legislature of a province. ([coopérative de
      crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Crown Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Crown Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Crown
      corporation that is an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada in
      respect of which employment has not been excepted from included
      employment by a regulation made under subsection 4(6) of the Act.
      ([société d'État]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[default]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the occurrence of one of the
      following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the written notification to the Superintendent
        that the administrator intends to terminate or wind up the whole
        plan under subsection 29(5) of the Act;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amendment of the plan, resolution by the
        employer or coming into force of any other measure that effects
        the termination of the whole plan;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Superintendent's declaration under
        subsection 29(2) or (2.1) of the Act that terminates the whole
        plan;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the filing of any application or petition by
        the employer, or against the employer, under the [Companies'
        Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36), the [Bankruptcy and
        Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or the [Winding-up and
        Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11);

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the termination of the whole plan;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the non-renewal of a letter of credit referred
        to in Part 3 for its full face amount unless

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it has been replaced by another letter of
          credit for the same face amount at least 30 days before the
          beginning of the following plan year,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the face amount of the
          letter of credit has been remitted to the pension fund at
          least 30 days before the beginning of the following plan year,
          or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the face amount of the letter of credit has
          been reduced in accordance with section 28; or

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the failure by an employer to comply with a
        direction issued by the Superintendent under section 11 of the
        Act with respect to the face amount of the letters of credit
        required by subsection 21(3). ([défaut]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a 2008 solvency
      deficiency or a solvency deficiency referred to in subsection 5(1)
      or 21(1) or (2). ([déficit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trust company that is
      licensed to carry on business in Canada and that has entered into
      a trust agreement with the employer or, if the employer is not the
      administrator, with the employer and the administrator.
      ([détenteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[issuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bank or cooperative credit
      society that is given an acceptable rating by two credit rating
      agencies and that is not the employer or affiliated with the
      employer within the meaning of subsection 2(2) of the [Canada
      Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).
      ([émetteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[solvency shortfall]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[solvency shortfall]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount,
      calculated as at the 2008 plan year end, by which the liabilities
      of the plan, determined on the basis that the plan is terminated
      or on a basis that is certified by an actuary to be reasonably
      approximate to a termination basis, and that takes into account
      any significant increases or decreases in benefits to the plan
      members as a result of the termination, exceed the total of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the value of the assets of the
        plan, determined on the basis of market value or of a value
        related to the market value by means of a method using market
        values over a period of not more than five years to stabilize
        short-term fluctuations, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the present value of a special payment in
        respect of a solvency deficiency that emerged before the 2008
        plan year end and that was calculated, as the case may be, in
        accordance with section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
        Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), section 9 or 13
        of the [Air Canada Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174) or section 5, 6, 7
        or 19 of the [Solvency Funding Relief
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275). ([déficit à
        combler]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a payment or one of
      a series of payments that is determined in accordance with section
      9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
      1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), section 9 or 13 of the [Air
      Canada Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174), section 5, 6, 7 or 19
      of the [Solvency Funding Relief
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-275) or section 5 or 21 of
      these Regulations. ([paiement spécial]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[2008 solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[2008 solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      solvency deficiency of a plan that emerged on the date on which
      the valuation that identified the deficiency was performed, as
      reported in the first actuarial report filed with the
      Superintendent after the coming into force of these Regulations,
      and that values the plan as at a date that is in the period
      beginning on November 1, 2008 and ending on October 31, 2009.
      ([déficit de solvabilité pour l'année 2008]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[2008 plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[2008 plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan year that
      ends no later than October 31, 2009. ([exercice
      2008]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[2009 plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[2009 plan year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan year that
      ends no later than October 31, 2010. ([exercice
      2009]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided, words and expressions
  used in these Regulations have the same meaning as in the [Pension
  Benefits Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749128 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the funding of a defined benefit plan and, except
  as otherwise provided, the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) also apply to the funding of a plan
  under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a 2008
  solvency deficiency shall be calculated in accordance with the
  definition [[solvency deficiency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection
  9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) and that definition shall be
  interpreted, in its paragraph (d), as including the present value of
  any special payments that are due in the next 10 years and that have
  been determined before the emergence of the 2008 solvency deficiency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), these Regulations prevail.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a solvency deficiency that emerges as the result of Air Canada
electing to fund a plan in accordance with Part 2 of the [Air Canada
Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174) other than in respect of a
2008 solvency deficiency that emerges as at the end of the 2008 plan
year, despite section 21 of those Regulations.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Plans
  may only be funded under these Regulations if all of the payments that
  are owed to the pension fund before the day on which the deficiency
  emerges, as required by subsection 9(14) of the [Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), have been
  made as at the filing date of the actuarial report that shows the
  emergence of that deficiency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 8 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), the funding of a plan
  shall be considered to meet the standards for solvency if the funding
  is in accordance with Part 1, 2 or 3 of these Regulations.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749137 .Part}

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749138 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  subsection 9(4) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) and section 13 of the [Air Canada
  Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174), a solvency deficiency, as
  defined in subsection 9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), of a plan that emerged
  at the end of the 2008 plan year may be funded in accordance with
  section 9 of those Regulations but the remittance to the pension fund
  of a portion of the special payments determined in accordance with
  those Regulations may be made in accordance with subsection (2) or
  (3), as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the actuarial report that values the plan as at
  the end of the 2008 plan year indicates that there is a 2008 solvency
  deficiency and that there is a solvency deficiency as defined in
  subsection 9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), a portion of the special payments
  determined in accordance with subsection 9(4) of those Regulations or
  section 13 of the [Air Canada Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174) may be paid to the pension
  fund as if the 2008 solvency deficiency were funded by special
  payments sufficient to liquidate the 2008 solvency deficiency by equal
  annual payments over a period not exceeding 10 years from the day on
  which the 2008 solvency deficiency emerged.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the actuarial report that values the plan as at
  the end of the 2008 plan year indicates that there is no 2008 solvency
  deficiency but that there is a solvency deficiency as defined in
  subsection 9(1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), the special payments determined in
  accordance with subsection 9(4) of those Regulations or section 13 of
  the [Air Canada Pension Plan Solvency Deficiency Funding
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-174) may be paid to the pension
  fund provided that those special payments result in the solvency
  shortfall being funded and liquidated over a period not exceeding 10
  years from the day on which the solvency shortfall was calculated.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 8(1) of the Act, if
  the 2008 solvency deficiency or solvency shortfall, as the case may
  be, is calculated by valuing the assets of a plan in excess of 110% of
  market value as at the date on which the valuation that identified the
  2008 solvency deficiency or solvency shortfall was performed, the
  amount by which the aggregate amount of special payments calculated
  using a value of assets equal to 110% of market value on that date
  exceeds the aggregate amount of special payments calculated using a
  value of assets in excess of 110% of market value on that date shall
  be considered to be an amount accrued to the pension fund.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The amount that was accrued as a result of
  subsection (4) is considered to have been paid to the plan at the end
  of the fifth plan year after the end of the 2009 plan year by special
  payments that have been calculated in accordance with these
  Regulations and section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) provided that all those
  payments have been made to the fund by the end of that plan year.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the funding is for a deficiency of a
  multi-employer pension plan and if the annual amount of the payments
  required to be made to the pension fund in accordance with
  subsection (2) or (3), as the case may be, is less than the aggregate
  amount of the payments that are required to be made to the pension
  fund, excluding the normal cost and the special payments required to
  liquidate an unfunded liability, under all applicable collective
  agreements, the amount of the payments required to be made to the
  pension fund in accordance with subsection (2) or (3), as the case may
  be, shall be the aggregate amount of the payments required to be made
  to the pension fund under all applicable collective agreements and
  subsection (4) shall not apply.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Continuation of 10-year Funding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749161 .Part}

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749162 .Subheading}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite paragraph 9(4)(c) of the Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985, if there is a solvency deficiency, a plan
  shall be funded in each plan year by annual solvency special payments
  equal to the amount by which the solvency deficiency divided by 5
  exceeds the amount of going concern special payments --- other than
  those referred to in paragraph 14(1)(c) --- that are payable during
  the plan year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[unfunded liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the going concern deficit of a plan as determined
    on the date that the plan was established;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which an increase in the going
    concern liabilities of a plan resulting from an amendment to the
    plan exceeds the going concern excess of the plan as determined on
    the day before the effective date of the amendment; or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the going concern deficit of
    a plan determined at the valuation date exceeds the sum of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
      payments established in respect of periods after the valuation
      date, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the present value of special payments referred to
      in paragraph 14(1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-149, s. 19
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  deficiency of a plan may continue to be funded in accordance with Part
  1 after the 2009 plan year only if less than one third of the members
  and less than one third of the beneficiaries excluding members object
  before the date indicated in the statement referred to in paragraph
  10(1)(j).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any objection expressed by a beneficiary
  representative on behalf of the persons that they represent shall be
  counted as a separate objection for each person that they represent.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the

  beneficiaries:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the solvency ratio of the plan as at the day on
    which the deficiency funded in accordance with section 5 emerged;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the deficiency to be funded in
    accordance with this Part;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the extent to which the
    beneficiaries' benefits would be reduced if the plan were fully
    terminated and wound up with the solvency ratio referred to in
    paragraph (a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that extending the period
    for funding the deficiency as permitted by this Part may result in a
    lower value of the plan assets during the funding period than would
    be the case if the deficiency were funded over a period not
    exceeding five years and that the longer funding period may also
    extend the period during which the plan assets are less than the
    plan liabilities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the special payments that would have been made
    during the first plan year covered by the actuarial report referred
    to in paragraph 6(b) if the deficiency were to be funded in
    accordance with section 9 of the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the special payments in respect of the deficiency
    that will be made during the 2009 plan year;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-60, s. 43\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the plan may continue
    to be funded in accordance with this Part following the 2009 plan
    year end only if less than one third of the members object and less
    than one third of the beneficiaries excluding members object;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the Superintendent's
    approval is not required to fund the deficiency in accordance with
    this Part;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the beneficiaries may
    object to the proposal to fund the plan in accordance with this Part
    by sending an objection to the administrator at the address and by
    the date indicated in the statement, which date shall not be less
    than 30 days after the day on which the other information required
    to be provided under this subsection is provided by the
    administrator;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that amendments to the plan
    that increase the pension benefits have been restricted during the
    2009 plan year and that, if the deficiency of the plan is funded in
    accordance with this Part, those benefits will continue to be
    restricted for the following four plan years of funding following
    the 2009 plan year end; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a statement setting out the right of access to the
    documents described in paragraph 28(1)(c) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a beneficiary is represented by a beneficiary
  representative, the administrator shall provide the information set
  out in subsection (1) to the beneficiary representative.

### [General Funding Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749252 .Subheading}

**[[20.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} despite paragraph 9(4)(c) of the Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985, if there is a solvency deficiency, a plan
  shall be funded in each plan year by annual solvency special payments
  equal to the amount by which the solvency deficiency divided by 5
  exceeds the amount of going concern special payments --- other than
  those referred to in paragraph 29(1)(c) --- that are payable during
  the plan year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [[unfunded liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the going concern deficit of a plan as determined
    on the date that the plan was established;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which an increase in the going
    concern liabilities of a plan resulting from an amendment to the
    plan exceeds the going concern excess of the plan as determined on
    the day before the effective date of the amendment; or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the going concern deficit of
    a plan determined at the valuation date exceeds the sum of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the present value of going concern special
      payments established in respect of periods after the valuation
      date, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the present value of special payments referred to
      in paragraph 29(1)(b).

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 9(4) of the

### [Letter of Credit]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749270 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  letter of credit required by this Part shall be an irrevocable,
  unconditional standby letter of credit that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in accordance with the rules of International
    Standby Practices 1998 (publication No. 590 of the International
    Chamber of Commerce), as amended from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is payable only in Canadian currency;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is issued or confirmed by an issuer who is a member
    of the Canadian Payments Association; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit is made out to the holder's
      benefit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the issuer will pay the face amount of the
      letter of credit on demand from the holder without inquiring
      whether the holder has a right to make the demand,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the bankruptcy of the employer shall have no
      effect on the rights and obligations of the issuer and the holder
      set out in the letter of credit,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit will expire on the day on
      which the plan's year ends,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit will automatically be
      renewed for the full face amount for further one-year periods on
      the expiry date referred to in subparagraph (iv) unless the issuer
      notifies the holder, in writing, of the non-renewal not less than
      90 days before the expiry date, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the letter of credit may not be amended, except
      to increase the face amount, during the term of the letter of
      credit and may not be assigned except to another holder.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The initial letter of credit for funding under this
  Part shall be obtained by the end of the 2009 plan year and each
  subsequent letter of credit shall be obtained at least 30 days before
  the beginning of each subsequent plan year that is covered by it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The letter of credit shall immediately be provided to
  the holder.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 5(4) shall not apply in respect of a plan
  if the letters of credit are obtained in accordance with this Part.

  amount of the letters of credit obtained to fund the plan under this
  Part is included as a solvency asset as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the Pension Benefit Standards Regulations, 1985.
