## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Specifications.

[[auxiliary equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[auxiliary equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment that is
    connected to, or used in conjunction with, a timber dimension
    measuring device. ([matériel auxiliaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[default temperature range]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[default temperature range]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect
    of a timber dimension measuring device, a temperature range of -10°C
    to +40°C. ([plage de températures par défaut]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[diameter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diameter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a log, its
    theoretical diameter, in centimetres or metres, calculated by
    measuring its actual circumference and dividing that value by pi
    (π). ([diamètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disturbance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disturbance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a phenomenon or event ---
    such as a short time power reduction or an electrostatic discharge,
    electrical burst or electromagnetic or electrostatic field --- to
    which a timber dimension measuring device may be exposed and the
    characteristics of which are inconsistent with the operating
    parameters of the device. ([perturbation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[indicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indicator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a digital device that is an
    integral part of, or is connected to, a measuring element and that
    displays measurement registrations. ([indicateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[influence factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[influence factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a phenomenon or event
    to which a timber dimension measuring device or any of its auxiliary
    equipment may be exposed and that may affect the performance of the
    device. ([facteur d'influence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[d]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the value, expressed in units of length, of the difference between
    two consecutively indicated values for any axis of measurement of a
    timber dimension measuring device. ([échelon]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"} ou [d]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a log, the
    maximum distance of a straight line, in centimetres or metres, that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is measured as closely as possible along the
      longitudinal axis of the log; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is between two planes that are perpendicular to
      that axis and located at each end of the
      log. ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[measurement registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[measurement registration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a displayed,
    printed or recorded representation of the measurement information
    for a log that has been measured by a timber dimension measuring
    device, including the dimension measured, the measurement value and
    the unit of measurement. ([enregistrement d'une
    mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[measuring element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[measuring element]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the parts of a
    timber dimension measuring device, other than the indicator, that
    together produce the measurement of a log. ([élément
    mesureur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[metrological function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[metrological function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a function of a
    timber dimension measuring device that is necessary for the
    measurement process and that may affect measurement registrations or
    the validity of transactions, including the sensing of the measured
    object, the transmission, processing, storage, correction or
    adjustment of measurement signals or values and the display or
    printing of measurement values. ([fonction
    métrologique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ready condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ready condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    timber dimension measuring device, the condition in which it is
    ready to take a measurement. ([état prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[timber dimension measuring device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber dimension measuring device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    measuring machine that measures the length and diameter of
    logs. ([appareil de mesure des dimensions du bois]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489349 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specifications prevail

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a conflict
between these Specifications and an instruction issued by the
manufacturer or importer, these Specifications prevail to the extent of
the conflict.

## [Performance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489352 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits of error

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The acceptance limits of
error and the in-service limits of error that apply to a timber
dimension measuring device are ±1 d for the axis and measuring range in
use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Influence factors

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must perform within the applicable limits of error
referred to in section 3 when, under controlled conditions, the device
is tested while it is exposed to an influence factor, such as:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the device is powered by alternating current, a
  voltage that is within the range of -15% to +10% of the nominal
  voltage of the device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the device is powered by direct current,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a voltage that is less than the nominal voltage of
    the device and at which voltage the device can produce a measurement
    registration, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a voltage that is up to 10% greater than the
    nominal voltage of the device;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a temperature that is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the temperature range specified
    for the device by the manufacturer is a range that spans at least
    30°C and is entirely within the default temperature range, that
    specified temperature range, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the default temperature range;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a humidity level of up to 85% relative humidity and a
  temperature that is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the temperature range specified
    for the device by the manufacturer is a range that spans at least
    30°C and is entirely within the default temperature range, that
    specified temperature range, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the default temperature range;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the device measures using optical principles, an
  ambient light level that is within the range of 100 lx to 100 000 lx;
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the device measures using acoustic principles, an
  acoustic interference of up to 100 dB at the nominal centre frequency
  of the device's ultrasonic transducers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eccentric measurements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must perform within the applicable limits of error
referred to in section 3 when, under controlled conditions, the device
is tested while it measures a log in an off-center position.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disturbances

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When a timber dimension
measuring device is exposed to a disturbance, it must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a measurement registration that is ±1 d from
  the measurement registration that would be provided without the
  disturbance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a blank measurement registration and prevent
  the measurement information from being displayed, transmitted, printed
  or stored;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide an error message and prevent the measurement
  information from being displayed, transmitted, printed or stored; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide a measurement registration that is so
  unstable that it cannot be interpreted as representing the correct
  measurement values.

## [Design, Composition and Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489377 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accuracy and fraud prevention

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be designed, composed and constructed in a manner
that, under normal conditions of use, enables it to measure accurately
and does not facilitate the perpetration of fraud.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination and testing

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be designed, composed and constructed in a manner
that enables it to be examined and enables physical test standards or
test objects to be applied to it and recovered before they are processed
by any other device.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervals

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Each interval of a timber
dimension measuring device must be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} displayed or printed in decimal form, with the
  decimal submultiple of the unit of length being set apart by a decimal
  point or comma; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} equal to 1 × 10^n^, 2 × 10^n^ or 5 × 10^n^ units of
  length, where "n" is an integer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding measurement values

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a measurement value
for a log falls between successive intervals, the timber dimension
measuring device must round the value to the nearest interval or, if the
value is equidistant between successive intervals, round it up to the
nearest interval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Software identification

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be equipped with a feature that identifies the
name and version of the software that it uses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indicator

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an indicator that has digits, letters or symbols that
  are of a consistent design, number and size and that permit
  measurement registrations to be clearly displayed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the indicator displays information provided by the
  device and any other source, a feature that displays measurement
  registrations separately from the information provided by the other
  source.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portable second indicator

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  timber dimension measuring device must be equipped with a portable
  second indicator if the device is installed such that, during an
  examination of the device by an inspector, the indicator required
  under section 12 cannot be readily observed by the inspector when they
  are at the measuring element.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The portable second indicator must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be readily connectable to all of the measuring
    elements without affecting their performance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be configured to display the same information as
    the indicator required under section 12; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} display information that is in exact agreement with
    the information displayed by the indicator required under section
    12.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Error handling

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be equipped with a mechanism or feature, or be
connected to auxiliary equipment that is equipped with a mechanism or
feature, that prevents a log from being processed after the device
provides an error message or blank measurement registration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Devices with multiple measuring elements

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device that is installed such that multiple measuring elements
are connected to one indicator or printer must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a feature that automatically identifies the measuring
  element that is in use and is providing information to the indicator
  or printer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a feature that prevents a measuring element from
  being used while another measuring element is in use and is providing
  information to the indicator or printer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test mode for display

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must have a test mode that shows the functional status
of all display elements and segments of its indicator if failure of any
single element or segment will result in the display of incorrect
information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic ready condition

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must automatically enter a ready condition when an
object is not on or in the measuring element and, when the device is in
that condition, that fact must be displayed on its indicator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ready condition before measurement
registration

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must not display, transmit, print or store a
measurement registration if it was not in a ready condition before
taking the measurement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tare function prohibited

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must not use a process, mechanism or feature that
reduces or adjusts the gross measured value of a log by a tare value,
including a process, mechanism or feature that permits bark or other
undesirable material to be subtracted from the measurement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement registration elements

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  words, names, symbols and abbreviations that are part of a measurement
  registration must be displayed or marked on the timber dimension
  measuring device in a location in which they will not be obliterated
  or rendered illegible under the normal conditions of use of the device
  and that is clearly visible to the device's operator and any customer
  who is present when a measurement is taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Displayed or printed measurement
  registrations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A measurement registration that is displayed or
  printed by a timber dimension measuring device must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be clear and unambiguous; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the measurement registration is printed, be
    indelible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement registrations --- auxiliary
equipment

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A measurement
registration that is displayed or printed by auxiliary equipment must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} agree exactly with the measurement registration
  provided by the timber dimension measuring device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be clear and unambiguous; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the measurement registration is printed, be
  indelible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions --- measurements and
measurement registrations

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must not

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} measure or calculate a log's area or volume or take
  any other measurement that is calculated from the log's length or
  diameter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a measurement registration for a measurement
  or calculation referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide a measurement registration that has a
  negative measurement value; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide a measurement registration for a log that has
  a length or diameter that is less than 10 d for the measuring range in
  use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measuring range --- minimum measurable
value

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  minimum measurable value for each measuring range of a timber
  dimension measuring device must not be less than 10 d.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervals for measuring ranges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a timber dimension measuring device has more than
  one measuring range,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the measuring ranges must be consecutive; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interval of each measuring range must be
    smaller than the interval of the immediately superior measuring
    range.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Error message or blank measurement
registration

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must provide an error message or a blank measurement
registration if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it measures a log that is not within the limits of
  the dimensions marked on the device or on a plate that is affixed to
  the device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it measures a log that is not within the detection
  limits of the device's sensing component and the measurement value is
  not within the in-service limits of error referred to in section 3; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} its sensing component is obstructed when a
  measurement is taken and the measurement value is not within the
  in-service limits of error referred to in section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be recorded and stored

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  timber dimension measuring device must record and store the following
  information, or be connected to auxiliary equipment that records and
  stores that information, when a customer is not present at the time
  their logs are measured:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each log measured, its length and each
    measurement of its diameter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the logs are separated into lots, the lot
    identifier for, and number of logs in, each lot.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided to customer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be recorded and stored in a manner that allows it
    to be provided to the customer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be stored for a period of 90 days after the day on
    which it was recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Auxiliary equipment interface

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device that is equipped with an interface that permits the
connection of auxiliary equipment must be designed in a manner that
prevents the metrological functions of the device from being adversely
affected if the connected auxiliary equipment is exposed to an influence
factor or a disturbance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustable components

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A component of a timber
dimension measuring device that is physically or electronically adjusted
must maintain that adjustment until any further adjustment is made to
that component.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seals

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The metrological
functions and adjustable components of a timber dimension measuring
device must be protected by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} physical seals that are readily accessible and
  observable and that make any access to those functions or components
  apparent; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} electronic seals that make any access to those
  functions or components apparent, such as an audit trail that counts
  the number of changes made to the calibration or configuration
  parameters of the device or records the values related to these
  changes.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking --- general

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following information must be permanently marked in a clearly visible
  location on a timber dimension measuring device or on a descriptive
  plate that is affixed to the device:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approval number assigned to the class, type or
    design of the device in accordance with paragraph 17(c) of the
    [Weights and Measures
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1605);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the device's manufacturer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the device's model designation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the device's serial number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for each axis of measurement that the device is
    capable of measuring in the location where it is installed,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the minimum and maximum dimensions for each
      measuring range on that axis, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the interval for each measuring range on that
      axis;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of scan heads installed on the device;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the device's minimum and maximum operating speeds
    as installed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the minimum distance, in centimetres or metres,
    between each log measured; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the temperature range specified for the device by
    the manufacturer, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking --- temperature range

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(i), a temperature range
  specified for the device by the manufacturer must not be marked on it
  or the descriptive plate unless the range spans at least 30°C and is
  entirely within the default temperature range.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking --- other

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The following information
must be permanently marked on a timber dimension measuring device, or be
posted near the device, in a location where the information is clearly
visible to the device's operator and any customer who is present when a
measurement is taken:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all restrictions, limitations or conditions on the
  use of the device; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all special applications or uses of the device.

## [Installation and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1489483 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  timber dimension measuring device must be installed and used in a
  manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensures accurate measurement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respects the parameters, restrictions and
    limitations set out in the notice of approval issued in respect of
    the device under section 3 of the [Weights and Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/W-6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 2, is in accordance with the
    instructions issued by its manufacturer or importer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not adversely affect the performance of the
    device;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} does not facilitate the perpetration of fraud; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensures that any auxiliary equipment will not
    prevent the device from meeting the requirements of paragraphs (a)
    to (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination and testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A timber dimension measuring device must be installed
  in a manner that enables it to be examined and enables physical test
  standards or test objects to be applied to it and recovered before
  they are processed by any other device.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use --- general

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must only be used

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that is consistent with its design,
  composition and construction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to measure logs that have a naturally occurring
  cross-section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use --- mobile equipment

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device that is an integral part of, or is attached or secured
to, the frame, chassis, lifting mechanism or bed of mobile equipment ---
such as a truck, tractor, trailer or forklift --- must not be supported
by the wheels or treads of that equipment when the device is making a
measurement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visibility of measurement registrations

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** A timber dimension
measuring device must be installed in a manner that permits the
operator, and any customer who is present when a measurement is taken,
to observe the taking of the measurement and easily read the device's
indicator.
