## Indians and Bands on Certain Indian Settlements Remission Order (1997) {#indians-and-bands-on-certain-indian-settlements-remission-order-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/97-127
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1997-11-12

Indians and Bands on Certain Indian Settlements Remission Order (1997)

P.C. 1997-1529  1997-10-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-97-127_e_hq_5160){#fn_SI-97-127_e_hq_5160-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed [Indians and Bands on Certain Indian Settlements Remission Order
(1997)](/eng/regulations/SI-97-127).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649927 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area that is
    named and described in column 2 of the schedule. ([établissement
    indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
    ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649933 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies to any
Indian Settlement until the time when the area of that Indian Settlement
is set aside, in whole or in part, as a reserve by Order of the Governor
in Council.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Income Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649935 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-649936 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tax imposed
  under Parts I, I.1 and I.2 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 1, all other words and expressions
  used in this Part have the same meaning as in the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

### [Remission of Income Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-649940 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a taxpayer who is an Indian whose income is situated on an Indian
Settlement, in respect of the taxation year set out in column 3 of the
schedule in relation to that Indian Settlement and each taxation year
after that year, of the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties paid or payable by
  the taxpayer for the taxation year under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3)

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties that would have
  been payable by the taxpayer for the taxation year under that Act if
  the Indian Settlement were a reserve throughout the year.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649945 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-649946 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and
  services tax imposed under Division II of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 1, all other words and expressions
  used in this Part have the same meaning as in Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

### [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-649950 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission of the tax paid or payable is hereby granted to an individual
who is an Indian who resides on an Indian Settlement and who is the
recipient of a taxable supply made on or after the date for remission of
the tax set out in column 4 of the schedule, in an amount equal to the
amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the individual

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the
  individual if the Indian Settlement were a reserve.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9,
remission of the tax paid or payable is hereby granted to a band that is
established on an Indian Settlement and is the recipient of a taxable
supply made on or after the date for remission of the tax set out in
column 5 of the schedule, in an amount equal to the amount, if any, by
which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the band

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the band if
  the Indian Settlement were a reserve.

### [Condition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-649959 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 6
in respect of tax paid is granted on the condition that an application
in writing for the remission be submitted to the Minister of National
Revenue within two years after the date on which the tax was paid.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 7
in respect of tax paid is granted on the condition that an application
in writing for the remission be submitted to the Minister of National
Revenue

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for tax paid on or after the date for remission of
  the tax set out in column 5 of the schedule but before the date for
  remission of the tax set out in column 4, within two years after the
  latter date; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for tax paid on or after the date for remission of
  the tax set out in column 4 of the schedule, within two years after
  the date on which the tax was paid.
