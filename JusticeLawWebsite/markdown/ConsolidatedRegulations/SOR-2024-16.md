## Locally Engaged Staff Employment Regulations, 2024 {#locally-engaged-staff-employment-regulations-2024 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2024-16
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2024-02-02

Locally Engaged Staff Employment Regulations, 2024

P.C. 2024-84 2024-02-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada and of the Public
Service Commission, under section 21 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4386_hq_28533){#fn_81000-2-4386_hq_28533-ID0EDBAA},

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441233 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[locally engaged staff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[locally engaged staff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means persons who
      are excluded from the application of the [Public Service
      Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) under section 20 of that Act
      and who are

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appointed and employed outside Canada;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} performing duties that are directly related to
        diplomatic or consular operations or a military support unit of
        the Government of Canada located outside Canada; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to the laws of the host country where
        they are employed. ([personnel embauché sur
        place]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development or the Department of
      National Defence. ([administration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any reference to a deputy head in these Regulations
  is to be read as a reference to the deputy head of the Department of
  Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development in respect of a diplomatic or
  consular operation and to the deputy head of the Department of
  National Defence in respect of a military support unit of the
  Government of Canada located outside Canada for which they are
  responsible.

## [Appointments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441244 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers or functions of deputy head

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A deputy
  head is responsible for the appointment of locally engaged staff and
  matters related to their employment under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exercise of powers or functions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deputy head may authorize any person within their
  organization to exercise or perform any of the deputy head's powers or
  functions under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment on basis of merit

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appointment of any person as locally engaged staff must be made on the
  basis of merit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Merit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appointment is made on the basis of merit if the
  deputy head is satisfied that the person to be appointed meets the
  qualifications established for the work to be performed before the
  start date of their appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of deputy head

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A deputy head may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish the qualifications for the work to be
    performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use an advertised or non-advertised appointment
    process;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use any assessment method they consider appropriate
    to determine if a person meets the qualifications established for
    the work to be performed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} make an appointment for a specified or
    indeterminate period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is not necessary to consider more than one person
  for an appointment to be made on the basis of merit.

## [Temporary Employment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In order
  to meet short-term, immediate staffing needs, a deputy head may
  appoint a person as temporary staff for a maximum of 125 working days
  in a calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provisions of these Regulations, other than this
  section and sections 1, 2 and 8, do not apply to temporary staff.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At any time during the period for which a person is
  appointed as temporary staff, the deputy head may provide a written
  notice to them ending their appointment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The written notice must be given at least one day
  before the effective date of the end of appointment provided for in
  the notice or in accordance with the laws of the host country where
  they are employed, whichever results in the longer notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of temporary employment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The employment of a person appointed as temporary
  staff ends at the end of the period for which they were appointed or
  on the effective date specified in the written notice.

## [Termination of Employment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441275 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lay-off

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A deputy
  head may lay off a person who is appointed as locally engaged staff
  under these Regulations due to a lack of work, the discontinuance of a
  function or the transfer of work or a function outside a diplomatic or
  consular operation or a military support unit of the Government of
  Canada located outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deputy head must give written notice of a lay-off
  to the person one month before the effective date of the lay-off
  provided for in the notice or in accordance with the laws of the host
  country where they are employed, whichever results in the longer
  notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resignation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person who is appointed
as locally engaged staff under these Regulations may resign by giving
notice in accordance with any applicable laws of the host country and by
giving written notice of their intention to resign to the deputy head
who will determine the effective date of the termination of employment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of employment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The employment of a person
appointed as locally engaged staff ends on one of the following dates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an appointment for a specified period
  under section 3, at the end of that period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a lay-off under section 5, the date
  specified in the written notice provided by the deputy head; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a resignation under section 6, the date
  specified in writing by the deputy head.

## [Oversight and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441288 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oversight

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A deputy
  head must monitor compliance with these Regulations, including the
  manner in which any person authorized under subsection 2(2) to
  exercise or perform the deputy head's powers or functions complies
  with the requirements of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A deputy head must report the results of their
  monitoring to the Commission within three years after the day on which
  these Regulations come into force and at least every five years after
  that.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1441294 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[former
Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 10 to 15 of these Regulations, [[former
Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Locally-Engaged Staff
Employment Regulations as they read immediately before these Regulations
come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priorities

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person who has a
priority for appointment under subsection 11(3) of the former
Regulations on the day on which these Regulations come into force
continues to have priority for appointment for the period provided for
under the former Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pending competitions and appointments

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  competition or other selection process that is being conducted under
  the former Regulations on the day on which these Regulations come into
  force continues to be conducted under the former Regulations as if
  these Regulations had not come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The results of those competitions or other selection
  processes remain valid for six months after the day on which these
  Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of lay-off

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If an employee was
served, prior to the day on which these Regulations come into force,
with a written notice under subsection 11(1) of the former Regulations
that they would be laid off but the applicable notice period has not
ended, the provisions of the former Regulations continue to apply to
that employee until the end of that period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employees on probation and rejection

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any employee who was
considered to be on probation under section 10 of the former Regulations
immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into force
continues to be on probation until the end of the period established
under the former Regulations and subsections 10(2) to (4) of the former
Regulations continue to apply to that employee until the end of that
period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency employment

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any employee who occupied
a position under subsection 8(1) of the former Regulations continues to
be subject to those Regulations after the day on which these Regulations
come into force until they are served with a written notice under
subsection 8(4) of the former Regulations and they cease to be an
employee under subsection 8(5) of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of appointment

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Any inquiry commenced
under the former Regulations that has not been completed by the day on
which these Regulations come into force must be dealt with and disposed
of in accordance with the former Regulations.
