## Order Transferring from the Department of Industry to the Privy Council Office the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public administration in the Department of Industry known as the Internal Trade Directorate {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-industry-to-the-privy-council-office-the-control-and-supervision-of-that-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-department-of-industry-known-as-the-internal-trade-directorate .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2018-106
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2018-12-12

Order Transferring from the Department of Industry to the Privy Council
Office the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public
administration in the Department of Industry known as the Internal Trade
Directorate

P.C. 2018-1420 2018-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1734-E_hq_23182){#fn_81000-3-1734-E_hq_23182-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1734-E_hq_23183){#fn_81000-3-1734-E_hq_23183-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Department of Industry to the Privy Council Office
the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public
administration in the Department of Industry known as the Internal Trade
Directorate.
