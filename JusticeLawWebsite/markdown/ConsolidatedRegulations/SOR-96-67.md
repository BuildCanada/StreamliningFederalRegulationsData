## Veterans Review and Appeal Board Regulations {#veterans-review-and-appeal-board-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-67
:::

[VETERANS REVIEW AND APPEAL BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/V-1.6)

Registration 1996-01-04

Regulations Prescribing the Procedure in Respect of Applications and
Appeals to the Veterans Review and Appeal Board

P.C. 1996-17 1996-01-04
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Veterans Affairs, pursuant to section 45 of the
[Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHCAA}, is pleased
hereby to repeal the Veterans Appeal Board Regulations, made by Order in
Council P.C. 1987-2146 of October 15, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA}, and to
make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the procedure in respect of
applications and appeals to the Veterans Review and Appeal
Board](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-67), in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002776 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Veterans Review and Appeal Board
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-67).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002778 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Veterans Review and Appeal
    Board Act](/eng/acts/V-1.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Veterans Review and Appeal
    Board established by section 4 of the Act.
    ([Tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application for Review or Reconsideration and Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002782 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In any proceeding under the
Act, other than an application for a compassionate award under
subsection 34(1) of the Act, the applicant or appellant shall inform the
Board whether they intend to proceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by written submission;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by appearing personally, with or without making a
  written submission; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by having a representative appear, with or without
  making a written submission.

## [Reconsiderations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002787 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The review panel, appeal
panel or Board, before convening a hearing to confirm, amend or rescind
a decision, pursuant to subsection 23(1), 32(1) or 34(7) of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall inform the applicant or appellant of the facts
  and allegations in its possession; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall provide the applicant or appellant with an
  opportunity to respond in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-172, s. 1(E)
:::

## [Notice and Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002793 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When the
  Board receives an application for review, an application for
  reconsideration referred to in subsection 32(1) or 34(7) of the Act, a
  notice of appeal or an application for a compassionate award under
  subsection 34(1) of the Act, or initiates a reconsideration pursuant
  to subsection 23(1), 32(1) or 34(7) of the Act, the Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} retrieve from the Department of Veterans Affairs'
    records

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the decision, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any relevant material, including

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all evidence submitted in the proceedings, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} any recording or transcript of the proceedings.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall notify the applicant or appellant of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its receipt of the material from the Department of
    Veterans Affairs' records; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the review, reconsideration or
    appeal shall be heard.

## [Questions of Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002805 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  question of interpretation is raised by an appellant pursuant to
  section 30 of the Act, or is referred to the Board pursuant to section
  37 of the Act, the appellant or the person or organization shall
  submit to the Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the question; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the facts and arguments to be relied on at the
    hearing of the question.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where it is of the opinion that the question is not
  trivial, frivolous or vexatious, the Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a question raised pursuant to
    section 30 of the Act, stay the appeal proceedings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the persons and organizations named in
    section 2 of the [Prescribed Persons and Organizations
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-68), other than those who
    referred the question to the Board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} annex to the notice a copy of the question and
    arguments raised by the appellant or referred by the person or
    organization.

## [Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002814 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every decision of the Board
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the issue on review, reconsideration or appeal,
  or the question of interpretation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} state the reasons for the decision;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} state the names of the members who took part in the
  decision;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} include the signature of at least one of the members
  who took part in the decision;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a review panel decision, inform the
  applicant that the applicant has the right to appeal to the Board and
  that, in pursuing this right of appeal, the applicant may be
  represented, free of charge, by the Bureau of Pensions Advocates or a
  service bureau of a veterans' organization or, at the applicant's own
  expense, by any other representative;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal panel decision, annex to the
  decision the reasons for dissent, if any, signed by the dissenting
  members of the appeal panel.

## [Notice of Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1002822 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall send
written notice of its decision

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for review or an
  application for reconsideration pursuant to subsection 32(1) of the
  Act, to the applicant and to the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an appeal, to the appellant and to the
  Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a question of interpretation raised by
  the appellant,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the persons referred to in paragraph (b), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the persons and organizations named in section
    2 of the [Prescribed Persons and Organizations
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-68);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a referral of a question of
  interpretation to the Board

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the person or organization who referred the
    question to the Board, and to the Minister, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the persons and organizations named in section
    2 of the [Prescribed Persons and Organizations
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-68), other than the person or
    organization referred to in subparagraph (i);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a compassionate
  award under subsection 34(1) of the Act or an application for
  reconsideration pursuant to subsection 34(7) of the Act, to the
  applicant and to the Minister; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a reconsideration by the Board on its
  own motion pursuant to subsection 23(1), 32(1) or 34(7) of the Act, to
  the applicant or appellant and to the Minister.
