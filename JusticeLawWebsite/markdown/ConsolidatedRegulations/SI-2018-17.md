## List of Wildlife Species at Risk (referral back to COSEWIC) Order {#list-of-wildlife-species-at-risk-referral-back-to-cosewic-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2018-17
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2018-02-21

List of Wildlife Species at Risk (referral back to COSEWIC) Order

P.C. 2018-53 2018-02-01
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsections 27(1.1) and
(1.2) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EADAA}

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EADAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  29
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} refers the assessment of the status of the Yukon
  Draba ([Draba yukonensis]{.otherLang lang="la"}) back to the Committee
  on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada (COSEWIC) for further
  consideration; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} approves that the Minister of the Environment include
  in the public registry established under section 120 of that Act the
  statement that is attached as the annex to this Order and that sets
  out the reasons for the referral back to COSEWIC.

## [ANNEX]{.scheduleLabel}[Statement Setting Out the Reasons for Referring the Assessment of the Yukon Draba ([Draba yukonensis]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Back to COSEWIC]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-632001 .scheduleLabel}
:::

Yukon Draba is a small herbaceous mustard plant. At the time of
COSEWIC's assessment in 2011, the only occurrence known worldwide was in
a single meadow complex in southwestern Yukon. The meadow complex is
under threat from industrial activities, nearby human habitation,
invasive species, trampling by humans and forest encroachment. Given the
rarity of suitable habitat within the range of natural dispersal, the
restricted range and extreme population fluctuations as understood at
the time of the assessment, COSEWIC proposed that the Yukon Draba be
designated as endangered. However, since the publication of the
assessment, COSEWIC has advised that a reassessment is warranted based
on new information regarding the species range in Canada and its
population dynamics.
