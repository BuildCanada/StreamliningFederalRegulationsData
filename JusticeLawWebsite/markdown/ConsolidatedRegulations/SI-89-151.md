## Order Assigning to the Minister of the Environment the Administration, Management and Control of Certain Public Lands {#order-assigning-to-the-minister-of-the-environment-the-administration-management-and-control-of-certain-public-lands .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-151
:::

[CANADA WILDLIFE ACT](/eng/acts/W-9)

Registration 1989-07-05

Order Assigning to the Minister of the Environment the Administration,
Management and Control of Certain Public Lands

P.C. 1989-1047 1989-06-01
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the public lands
described in the schedule hereto are required for wildlife research,
conservation or interpretation.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 4(1) of the [Canada Wildlife Act](/eng/acts/W-9), is pleased
hereby to assign to the Minister of the Environment the administration,
management and control of the public lands described in the schedule
hereto.
:::

Portobello Creek

Being all those lots in the Province of New Brunswick, in the County of
Sunbury, in the Parishes of Maugerville and Sheffield, identified as
lots 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 13, 15, 17, 23, 24, 26, 27, 32, 33, 36, 36A, 38,
41, 42, 44, 45, 46, 49, 54, 56, 59, 60, 61, 62, 66, 68A, 71, 74, 84, 86,
87, 89, 89A and 94; all according to Plan 71087, Canada Lands Surveys
Records, Ottawa, containing together about 1,769.5 hectares.
