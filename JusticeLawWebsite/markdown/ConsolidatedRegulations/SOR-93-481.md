## Royal Canadian Mounted Police Fingerprinting for Visa, Licensing or Security Clearance Purposes Fee Regulations {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-fingerprinting-for-visa-licensing-or-security-clearance-purposes-fee-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-481
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-09-23

Regulations Prescribing the Fee for Fingerprinting for the Purposes of a
Visa, Licence or Security Clearance

P.C. 1993-1818  1993-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-481_e_hq_6265){#fn_SOR-93-481_e_hq_6265-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the fee for fingerprinting
for the purposes of a visa, licence or security
clearance](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-481).

  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955081 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Fingerprinting for Visa,
Licensing or Security Clearance Purposes Fee Regulations.

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955083 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee to be paid by an
individual for fingerprinting for the purposes of obtaining a visa,
licence or security clearance is \$25.
