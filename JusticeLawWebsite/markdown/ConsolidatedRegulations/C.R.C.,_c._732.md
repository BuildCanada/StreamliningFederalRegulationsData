## Search, Reproduction and Certification of Documents Regulations {#search-reproduction-and-certification-of-documents-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 732
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Prescribing the Fees for the Search, Reproduction and
Certification of Documents
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561210 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Search, Reproduction and Certification of Documents
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._732).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561212 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reproduction in any form of a
    document contained in the files of the Department or an extract
    therefrom; ([copie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of National
    Revenue; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an invoice, entry or other
    customs form relating to a specific importation or exportation;
    ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[search]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the act of obtaining a document
    or information from a document contained in the files of the
    Department in response to a request by an importer or an exporter or
    any person lawfully acting on behalf thereof.
    ([compulsation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561218 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A fee of \$0.75 shall be
paid

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each search required to obtain a document for
  perusal by an importer or an exporter or any person lawfully acting on
  behalf thereof or to confirm the accuracy of information appearing on
  copies of a document submitted for certification; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for certification of each document.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-217, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the fees
specified in section 3, a fee of \$3.00 shall be paid for the
reproduction of a document by the Department.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-217, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding sections 3
and 4, no fee shall be paid for a search or for reproduction or
certification of a document where that search, reproduction or
certification is performed at the request of a foreign government.
