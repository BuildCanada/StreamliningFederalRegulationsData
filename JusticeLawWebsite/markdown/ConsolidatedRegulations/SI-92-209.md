## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645262 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Foreign Aircraft Servicing Equipment Remission Order,
1992](/eng/regulations/SI-92-209).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645264 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on machinery and equipment imported into
Canada, on or after September 17, 1969, for use exclusively in servicing
aircraft registered in a foreign country or territory while at
international airports in Canada if it is established that, at the time
of the importation, the foreign country or territory grants a similar
privilege in respect of aircraft registered in Canada.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on the goods in respect of which customs duties are
remitted by this Order, in an amount equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax paid or payable on the goods,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax that would have been paid or
  would be payable on the goods if the duty paid value used to calculate
  the sales tax were reduced by the amount of the remission of customs
  duties granted under this Order.
