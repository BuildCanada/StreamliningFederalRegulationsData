## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604697 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of one half of the tax that was paid or
became payable under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) by
the Nova Scotia Public Service Long Term Disability Plan Trust Fund (the
"Fund") during the period beginning on July 21, 1993 and ending on March
26, 1998.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604699 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that the Fund make an application for the remission in writing
to the Minister of National Revenue no later than December 31, 2000 and
that the tax has not otherwise been rebated, refunded or remitted.
