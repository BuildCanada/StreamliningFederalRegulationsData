## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Controlled Drugs and
    Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[listed substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[listed substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance set out
    in Schedule 1 to the [Benzodiazepines and Other Targeted Substances
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-217), the schedule to Part G
    of the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870)
    or the schedule to the [Narcotic Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041), and includes
    anything that contains the substance.  ([substance
    inscrite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[midwife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[midwife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is registered and
    entitled under the laws of a province to practise midwifery and who
    is practising midwifery in that
    province. ([sage-femme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    registered and entitled under the laws of a province to practise as
    a nurse practitioner or an equivalent designation and who is
    practising as a nurse practitioner or an equivalent designation in
    that province. For the purpose of this definition, a designation is
    equivalent when it designates a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a registered nurse;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possesses additional educational preparation and
      experience related to health care;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} can autonomously make diagnoses, order and
      interpret diagnostic tests, prescribe drugs and perform other
      specific procedures under the laws of a province; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is practising their profession in accordance
      with, for example, the following provincial laws, as amended from
      time to time:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Extended Practice Regulation, Man. Reg.
        43/2005, made under The Registered Nurses Act of Manitoba,
        C.C.S.M., c. R40,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Regulation 275/94, made under the
        Nursing Act, 1991 of Ontario, S.O. 1991, c. 32, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Regulation respecting [Ordre des
        infirmières et infirmiers du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
        classes of specialities related to the performance of acts
        contemplated in section 36.1 of the Nurses Act, R.R.Q., c.
        I-8, r. 8, made under the Nurses Act of Quebec, R.S.Q., c.
        I-8. ([infirmier praticien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is registered
    and entitled under the laws of a province to practise podiatry or
    chiropody and who is practising podiatry or chiropody in that
    province. ([podiatre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [New Classes of Practitioners Prescribed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784889 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of
the Act, the following classes of persons are prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} midwives;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} nurse practitioners; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} podiatrists.

## [Permitted Activities and Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784895 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, a
midwife, nurse practitioner or podiatrist, as a practitioner, may
prescribe or possess a listed substance, or conduct an activity with a
listed substance, in accordance with the [Benzodiazepines and Other
Targeted Substances Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-217), Part G
of the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or
the [Narcotic Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041) if
they are permitted to prescribe, in their practice under the laws of the
province in which they are registered and entitled to practise, that
substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions --- midwife or podiatrist

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  respect of a midwife or podiatrist, a listed substance excludes a
  substance set out in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the definition [[designated
    drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection G.04.001(1) of the [Food
    and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} item 1 of Part III of the schedule to Part G of the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of subitems 1(1) and (10), 2(1), 5(4) and 10(1)
    of the schedule to the [Narcotic Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions --- nurse practitioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a nurse practitioner, a listed
  substance excludes a substance set out in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} item 1 of Part III of the schedule to Part G of the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), except
    for subitem (40); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subitem 1(1) or 2(1) of the schedule to the
    [Narcotic Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041).

## [Inconsistency with other Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-784912 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the event of any
inconsistency between these Regulations and the [Benzodiazepines and
Other Targeted Substances Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-217),
Part G of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or the [Narcotic Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041), these Regulations
prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.
