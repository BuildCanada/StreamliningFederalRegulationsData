## Direction to the CRTC (Ineligibility of Non-Canadians) {#direction-to-the-crtc-ineligibility-of-non-canadians .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-192
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 1997-04-08

Direction to the CRTC (Ineligibility of Non-Canadians)

P.C. 1997-486 1997-04-08
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 26(4) of the [Broadcasting
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Canadian Heritage has consulted with the Canadian Radio-television
and Telecommunications Commission with regard to the annexed Direction
to the CRTC (Ineligibility of Non-Canadians);

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to
subsection 26(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby issues
the annexed Direction to the CRTC (Ineligibility of Non-Canadians).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  11
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005140 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Direction.

[[acquiring corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[acquiring corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    referred to in paragraph 17(2)(a) of the [Canadian
    Telecommunications Common Carrier Ownership and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-667) as they read on October
    25, 1994, registered as SOR/94-667. ([acquéreur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[affiliate corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliate corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to
    a qualified successor,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian carrier referred to in subsection
      16(2) of the [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) or any of
      its subsidiary corporations;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an acquiring corporation or any of its subsidiary
      corporations; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that controls the corporations
      referred to in paragraphs (a) and (b), or any of its subsidiary
      corporations. ([société affiliée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a citizen within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
      of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) who is ordinarily
      resident in Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident within the meaning of
      subsection 2(1) of the Immigration Act who is ordinarily resident
      in Canada and has been ordinarily resident in Canada for not more
      than one year after the date on which that person first became
      eligible to apply for Canadian citizenship;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian government, whether federal,
      provincial or local, or an agency thereof, subject to the
      [Direction to the CRTC (Ineligibility to Hold Broadcasting
      Licences)](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-627);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a corporation without share capital where a
      majority of its directors are appointed or designated, either by
      their personal names or by their names of office, by one or more
      of the following, namely,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a federal or provincial statute or any
        regulation made thereunder,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Governor in Council or the lieutenant
        governor in council of a province, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a minister of the Crown in right of Canada or
        a province;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a qualified corporation;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a qualified mutual insurance company;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a qualified pension fund society;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a qualified cooperative; or

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a qualified successor

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of holding a broadcasting
        distribution undertaking licence, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of beneficially owning,
        directly or indirectly, 50 per cent or less of all the issued
        and outstanding voting shares, and 50 per cent or less of the
        votes, of a qualified corporation that holds a broadcasting
        licence for a distribution undertaking only.
        ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means control in any manner that
    results in control in fact, whether directly through the ownership
    of securities or indirectly through a trust, an agreement or
    arrangement, the ownership of a corporation or otherwise.
    ([contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is a member of
    the board of directors of a corporation or, where the corporation
    has no directors, a person performing functions that are similar to
    the functions performed by directors.
    ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[independent member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[independent member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is not
    an officer or employee of, or a contractor who provides goods or
    services to, a qualified successor or any of its affiliate
    corporations, who is not a director of any affiliate corporations of
    the qualified successor, and in respect of whom there are no
    considerations that could reasonably be anticipated to interfere
    with the person's ability to act in the best interests of the
    qualified successor. ([membre indépendant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or entity that
    is not a Canadian. ([non-Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified cooperative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified cooperative]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cooperative,
    not less than 80 per cent of the members of which are Canadians,
    that is established under an Act of Parliament or under any
    provincial legislation that relates to the establishment of
    cooperatives. ([coopérative qualifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    incorporated or continued under the laws of Canada or a province,
    where

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the chief executive officer or, where the
      corporation has no chief executive officer, the person performing
      functions that are similar to the functions performed by a chief
      executive officer, and not less than 80 per cent of the directors
      are Canadians;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation having share
      capital, Canadians beneficially own and control, directly or
      indirectly, in the aggregate and otherwise than by way of security
      only, not less than 80 per cent of all the issued and outstanding
      voting shares of the corporation and not less than 80 per cent of
      the votes; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation that is a subsidiary
      corporation,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the parent corporation is incorporated or
        continued under the laws of Canada or a province,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Canadians beneficially own and control,
        directly or indirectly, in the aggregate and otherwise than by
        way of security only, not less than 66 2/3 per cent of all of
        the issued and outstanding voting shares of the parent
        corporation and not less than 66 2/3 per cent of the votes, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the parent corporation or its directors do
        not exercise control or influence over any programming decisions
        of the subsidiary corporation where

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} Canadians beneficially own and control,
          directly or indirectly, in the aggregate and otherwise than by
          way of security only, less than 80 per cent of the issued and
          outstanding voting shares of the parent corporation and less
          than 80 per cent of the votes,

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the chief executive officer of the parent
          corporation or, where the parent corporation has no chief
          executive officer, the person performing functions that are
          similar to the functions performed by a chief executive
          officer is a non-Canadian, or

        - [(C)]{.lawlabel} less than 80 per cent of the directors of the
          parent corporation are Canadian. ([personne morale
          qualifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified mutual insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified mutual insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    mutual insurance company, the head office and principal place of
    business of which are in Canada and not less than 80 per cent of the
    board of directors and of each committee of the directors of which
    are Canadians. ([société mutuelle d'assurance
    qualifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified pension fund society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified pension fund society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    pension fund society, not less than 80 per cent of the board of
    directors of which and of each committee of the directors of which
    are Canadians, and that is established under An Act to incorporate
    the Guarantee and Pension Fund Society of the Dominion Bank, S.C.
    1887, c. 55, An Act to incorporate the Pension Fund Society of the
    Bank of Montreal, S.C. 1885, c. 13, the [Pension Fund Societies
    Act](/eng/acts/P-8), R.S., 1985, c. P-8, or under any provincial
    legislation that relates to the establishment of pension fund
    societies. ([société de caisse de retraite
    qualifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified successor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified successor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    referred to in paragraph 17(2)(b) or (c) of the [Canadian
    Telecommunications Common Carrier Ownership and Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-667) as they read on October
    25, 1994, registered as SOR/94-667, incorporated or continued under
    the laws of Canada or a province and directly controlled by a
    Canadian carrier referred to in subsection 16(2) of the
    [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), or by its acquiring
    corporation, where

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control of the Canadian carrier and its
      acquiring corporation has remained unchanged since the date of the
      coming into force of this Direction;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the chief executive officer of the corporation
      or, where the corporation has no chief executive officer, the
      person performing functions that are similar to the functions
      performed by a chief executive officer, and all its directors are
      Canadians;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all the voting shares of the corporation that are
      not beneficially owned and controlled by the Canadian carrier or
      its acquiring corporation are beneficially owned and controlled by
      Canadians;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation referred to in
      subparagraph (i)(ii) of the definition
      [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, all the voting shares of the
      qualified corporation that are not beneficially owned by the
      corporation are beneficially owned and controlled by Canadians;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the corporation operates only in the operating
      territory of the Canadian carrier;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the corporation does not beneficially own,
      directly or indirectly, voting shares of a corporation that holds
      a broadcasting distribution undertaking licence and that operates
      outside of the operating territory of the Canadian carrier;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the directors of the corporation and its officers
      have complete and exclusive control over all programming decisions
      and

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least 33 1/3 per cent of the directors are
        independent members, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a quorum at any meeting of the directors or of
        any committee of the directors must include at least one
        independent member; and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} no parent corporation or affiliate corporation of
      the corporation exercises any control or influence over any
      programming decisions of the corporation. ([ayant droit
      qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidiary corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidiary corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation
    that is controlled by another corporation.
    ([filiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share of any class of
    shares of a corporation carrying voting rights under all
    circumstances or by reason of any event that has occurred and is
    continuing or by reason of a condition that has been fulfilled, and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a security that is convertible into such a share
      at the time a calculation of the percentage of shares owned and
      controlled by Canadians is made; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an option or a right to acquire such a share, or
      the security referred to in paragraph (a), that is exercisable at
      the time the calculation referred to in that paragraph is made.
      ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Direction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005196 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian
Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission is hereby directed
that no broadcasting licence may be issued, and no amendments or
renewals thereof may be granted, to an applicant that is a non-Canadian.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where the Canadian
Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission determines that an
applicant is controlled by a non-Canadian, whether on the basis of
personal, financial, contractual or business relations or any other
considerations relevant to determining control, other than the
beneficial ownership and control of the voting shares of a qualified
successor by a Canadian carrier or its acquiring corporation, the
applicant is deemed to be a non-Canadian.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005199 .Part}
