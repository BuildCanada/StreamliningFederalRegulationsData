::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484370 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [National Energy Board Order No.
MO-62-69](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1055).

## [Application of part IV of the act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484372 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The National Energy Board,
pursuant to subsection 97(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBA} of the
[National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), hereby orders that Part IV
of the said Act applies to every person who was operating a pipeline on
November 2, 1959.
