## Firearms Fees Remission Order (Licenses) {#firearms-fees-remission-order-licenses .Title-of-Act}

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Firearms Fees Remission Order (Licenses)

P.C. 2006-387 2006-05-17

:::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
Firearms Fees Remission Order (Licences).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613046 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted to an
  individual who had been issued, on or after December 1, 1998, a
  possession licence for firearms (other than a possession licence for
  firearms for individuals who are less than 18 years old) or a
  possession and acquisition licence for firearms of the fee the
  individual paid under the [Firearms Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204) for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the renewal of one of those licences;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of another possession and acquisition
    licence for firearms; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a possession and acquisition
    licence for firearms if the previously held licence was a possession
    licence for firearms.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fee paid for a licence issued to an individual if
    that licence or the licence previously held by the individual had
    been revoked in circumstances other than those described in
    subsection 72(1.1) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) and that
    revocation had not been cancelled; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fee paid for a licence if the renewal or issuance
    of the licence has been refused and the refusal has not been
    overturned.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission of the fee paid
under the [Firearms Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204) by an
individual for the renewal of a possession licence for firearms for
individuals who are less than 18 years old is granted to the individual.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613056 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 1 or 2 is granted on the condition that the fee was paid
during the period beginning on December 1, 1998 and ending on the day
before the day on which this Order comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613058 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
