## National Parks of Canada Businesses Regulations {#national-parks-of-canada-businesses-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-455
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1998-09-15

National Parks of Canada Businesses Regulations

P.C. 1998-1575  1998-09-15
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(p) of the
National Parks Act, hereby makes the annexed National Parks Businesses
Regulations, 1998.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024165 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any trade, industry,
    employment, occupation, activity or special event carried on in a
    park for profit, gain, fund raising or commercial promotion, and
    includes an undertaking carried on in a park by a charitable
    organization, or by an organization or individual on a non-profit
    basis. ([commerce]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[charitable organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[charitable organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a charitable
    organization within the meaning of subsection 149.1(1) of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and includes an organization that
    is a registered charity as defined in subsection 248(1) of that Act.
    ([oeuvre de bienfaisance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 2(1) of the [Parks Canada Agency
    Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4). ([directeur général]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued by the
    superintendent under section 4.1, or issued or reinstated under
    subsection 10.1(2), as the case may be. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of a valid
    licence. ([titulaire de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a planned temporary
    activity conducted for recreation, entertainment or promotional
    purposes, and includes a parade, concert, theatrical or musical
    performance, sports event, fair, circus, contest and show.
    ([événement spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 12-month period beginning on
    April 1st in each year and ending on March 31st in the next year.
    ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024177 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to parks and, subject to
  sections 40 and 41 of the Act, to park reserves as if they were parks.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in the Town of Banff
  or in the Town of Jasper.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 3
- SOR/2010-23, s. 15

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024183 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall carry on,
in a park, any business unless that person is the holder of a licence or
an employee of a holder of a licence.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who wishes to carry on a business in a park, or their agent authorized
  in writing to act on their behalf, shall apply for a licence to the
  superintendent and shall provide the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of goods and services
    that the applicant proposes to offer in the business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of equipment that the
    applicant proposes to use in the business;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address, if any, at which, or a description of
    the area in the park in which, the applicant proposes to carry on
    the business;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any documentation relevant to the
    applicant's ability to carry on the business; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a licence to
    carry on a guiding business, a summary of the education, skills and
    experience that qualify the applicant and each of the applicant's
    employees to carry on the business safely.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application must be accompanied by the applicable
  fee fixed under section 24 of the [Parks Canada Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, ss. 4, 10(F)
:::

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may,
on application by a person in accordance with section 4, and having
regard to the matters to be considered under subsection 5(1), issue a
licence to that person to carry on the business indicated in the
application.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 5
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining whether to issue a licence and under what terms and
  conditions, if any, the superintendent shall consider the effect of
  the business on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the natural and cultural resources of the park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety, health and enjoyment of persons
    visiting or residing in the park;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the safety and health of persons availing
    themselves of the goods or services offered by the business; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the preservation, control and management of the
    park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent must set out as terms and
  conditions in a licence

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the types of goods and services that will be
    offered by the business; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address, if any, at which, or a description of
    the area in the park in which, the business is to be carried on.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Depending on the type of business, the superintendent
  may, in addition to the terms and conditions mentioned in
  subsection (2), set out in a licence terms and conditions that specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hours of operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the equipment that shall be used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the health, safety, fire prevention and
    environmental protection requirements; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that is necessary for the
    preservation, control and management of the park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 10(F)
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Before issuing a licence
for any business, the superintendent may require the applicant to
furnish a certificate from a medical health officer or sanitary
inspector, or both, certifying that the premises in which the business
is to be carried on are in a sanitary condition.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 10(F)
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend a licence where the licensee fails to
    comply with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of the licence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of a lease or licence
      of occupation issued in respect of the business under the National
      Parks Lease and Licence of Occupation Regulations (1991); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reinstate a suspended licence when the failure that
    gave rise to the suspension has been remedied.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may revoke a licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee is convicted of a contravention of
    these Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence has been suspended three times in the
    year in which it was issued.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 6(F)
:::

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** If the superintendent
refuses to issue a licence in respect of an application referred to in
section 4.1, or suspends or revokes a licence under section 7, the
superintendent shall, as soon as possible after making a decision,
provide written notice of the decision, including reasons, to the
applicant or licensee, as applicable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 7
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The licensee shall permit
the superintendent, any park warden or any enforcement officer to
inspect, for the purposes of these Regulations, at any reasonable time,
any premises or place used by the licensee in carrying on the licensee's
business.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 8
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The licensee shall post
their licence in a conspicuous place on the premises or at the place
where the licensee is carrying on their business, and shall produce the
licence on the request of the superintendent, a park warden or any
enforcement officer during an inspection of those premises or that place
under section 8.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 8
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A licence expires on the
earliest of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of expiry stated in the licence,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of revocation, if any, of the licence, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sale of the business.

## [Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024243 .Part}

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any person to whom the
  superintendent has refused to issue a licence in respect of an
  application referred to in section 4.1, or whose licence has been
  suspended or revoked by the superintendent under section 7, may
  request a review of the superintendent's decision by submitting a
  request in writing to the Chief Executive Officer within 30 days
  following receipt by that person of the notice referred to in
  section 7.1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Upon receipt of a written request for review, the
  Chief Executive Officer shall require that the superintendent issue or
  reinstate the licence if the Chief Executive Officer determines that
  the superintendent's decision was incorrect with regard

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under section 4.1,
    to the requirements set out in section 4 or the matters to be
    considered under subsection 5(1), or both; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a decision made under section 7, to
    the reasons for suspension and revocation set out in
    subsections 7(1) and (2), respectively.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer shall provide written
  notice of his or her decision, including reasons, to the person that
  requested the review.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 9
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024252 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every person to whom a
licence to sell intoxicating beverages has been issued shall keep a
record of all the person's purchases of spirits, wine, beer, ale and
lager and shall produce that record for inspection upon request by the
superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-370, s. 10(F)
:::
