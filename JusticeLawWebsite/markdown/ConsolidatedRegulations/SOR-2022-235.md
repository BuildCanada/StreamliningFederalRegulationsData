## Critical Habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-black-redhorse-moxostoma-duquesnei-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-235
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-11-08

Critical Habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Black Redhorse ([Moxostoma duquesnei]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185-ID0EBBFA){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954411){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954411-ID0EBBDA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954411-ID0EBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954411}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that
the annexed Order would affect a reserve or other lands that are set
apart for the use and benefit of a band and, under subsection
58(7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954639){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954639-ID0EBBCA}
of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indigenous Services and
the band in question with respect to the Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954639-ID0EBBCA){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954639}S.C.
  2019, c. 29, subpar. 375(1)(f)(ii)
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Black Redhorse (Moxostoma duquesnei)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-235) under subsections 58(4) and (5) of
the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185){#fn_81000-2-4898_hq_1658954185-ID0EBABA}.

Ottawa, November 4, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Black Redhorse ([Moxostoma duquesnei]{.otherLang lang="la"}),
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
