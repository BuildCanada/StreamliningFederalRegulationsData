## Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United States and Families Duty and Tax Relief Privileges Order {#administrative-and-technical-staff-of-the-embassy-of-the-united-states-and-families-duty-and-tax-relief-privileges-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-391
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1993-07-21

Order Extending Duty and Tax Relief Privileges to Members of the
Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United States,
and Members of their Families, in Canada

P.C. 1993-1544 1993-07-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the joint
recommendation of the Secretary of State for External Affairs and the
Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 4(2) of the Foreign Missions
and International Organizations Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Order extending duty and tax relief
privileges to members of the administrative and technical staff of the
Embassy of the United States, and members of their families, in Canada.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 41
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953800 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United
States and Families Duty and Tax Relief Privileges
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-391).

## [Privileges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953802 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the duty and tax relief privileges accorded to
  diplomatic agents under section 3 of the [Foreign Missions and
  International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4) shall extend to the
  members of the administrative and technical staff of the Embassy of
  the United States, and to the members of their families forming part
  of their households, in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The privileges referred to in subsection (1) shall
  not extend to members of the administrative and technical staff, or to
  the members of their families forming part of their households, who
  are nationals or permanent residents of Canada.
