[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Patent
  Act](/eng/acts/P-4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[authorization for
  sale]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations and for the purposes of section
  104 of the Act, [[authorization for sale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
  an authorization under the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), or
  any predecessor enactment relating to the same subject matter, that
  permits the sale of a drug in Canada, but does not include an interim
  order permitting the sale of a drug under section 30.1 of that Act, a
  certificate issued under section C.08.015 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), an exemption under
  subsection C.10.002(1) or C.10.008(1) of those Regulations or an
  authorization under section C.05.006, C.05.008 or C.08.010 or
  subsection C.11.003(1) or C.11.014(1) of those Regulations, section 67
  or 71 of the [Natural Health Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196) or section 21 or
  subsection 24(2) of the Clinical Trials for Medical Devices and Drugs
  Relating to COVID-19 Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 105(3) and (4) of the Act, the prescribed variations are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a variation in any appendage within the molecular
  structure of a medicinal ingredient that causes it to be an ester,
  salt, complex, chelate, clathrate or any non-covalent derivative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a variation that is an enantiomer, or a mixture of
  enantiomers, of a medicinal ingredient;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a variation that is a solvate or polymorph of a
  medicinal ingredient;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an [in vivo]{.otherLang lang="la"} or [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} post-translational modification of a
  medicinal ingredient; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any combination of the variations set out in
  paragraphs (a) to (d).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible patents --- requirement

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 106(1)(a) of the Act, the prescribed requirement
  is that the patent must be in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible patents --- manners of
  pertinence to medicinal ingredients

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 106(1)(c) of the Act,
  the prescribed manners in which a patent may pertain to a medicinal
  ingredient or combination of medicinal ingredients are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the patent contains a claim for the medicinal
    ingredient or combination of all the medicinal ingredients contained
    in a drug for which the authorization for sale set out in the
    application for a certificate of supplementary protection was
    issued;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the patent contains a claim for the medicinal
    ingredient or combination of all the medicinal ingredients as
    obtained by a specified process and contained in a drug for which
    the authorization for sale set out in the application for a
    certificate of supplementary protection was issued; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the patent contains a claim for a use of the
    medicinal ingredient or combination of all the medicinal ingredients
    contained in a drug for which the authorization for sale set out in
    the application for a certificate of supplementary protection was
    issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization for sale

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 106(1)(c) of the Act, the prescribed kind of authorization for
sale is a notice of compliance issued under section C.08.004 or
C.08.004.01 of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with Patent Office

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may consult
with the officers or employees of the Patent Office about any matter
relating to a patent that is set out in a certificate of supplementary
protection or in an application for a certificate of supplementary
protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Countries and period

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 106(1)(f) of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed countries are

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the European Union and any country that is a
      member of the European Union,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the United States of America,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Australia,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Switzerland,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Japan, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the United Kingdom; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed period for filing the application
    for the authorization for sale is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 24 months, if the application for a certificate
      of supplementary protection was filed no later than the first
      anniversary of the day on which section 59 of the
      [Canada--European Union Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement
      Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-4.8) comes into force, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 12 months, in any other case.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- filing application for
  certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 106(3) of the Act, the
  prescribed period for filing the application for a certificate of
  supplementary protection is 120 days.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for a certificate of supplementary
  protection must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and contact information in
    Canada, including their complete address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the filing date of the application for the patent,
    the date on which the patent was granted and the date on which the
    term referred to in section 44 of the Act will expire;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's attestation, as the case may be,
    that the applicant is the patentee and is recorded as an owner of
    the patent in the Patent Office or that they are the manufacturer
    who is authorized under section 8 to file the application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's attestation that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} when the application was filed for the
      authorization for sale referred to in paragraph 106(1)(c) of the
      Act, no application for a marketing approval, equivalent to an
      authorization for sale, with respect to the medicinal ingredient
      or combination of medicinal ingredients, as the case may be, set
      out in the application for the certificate of supplementary
      protection had been submitted in a country prescribed by paragraph
      (1)(a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if one or more of those applications for a
      marketing approval had been submitted in one or more of those
      countries, the application for the authorization for sale referred
      to in paragraph 106(1)(c) of the Act was filed before the end of
      the prescribed period referred to in paragraph (1)(b) that begins
      on the day of submission of the first of those marketing approval
      applications; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the method of payment used to pay
    the fee prescribed under subsection 9(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing date

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The filing date of an
application for a certificate of supplementary protection is the date on
which the information required by paragraphs 106(5)(a) and (b) of the
Act and the content required by subsection 6(3) of these Regulations are
received by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application by manufacturer

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person recorded in the
Patent Office as the owner of a patent may authorize a manufacturer to
file on their behalf an application for a certificate of supplementary
protection that sets out the patent if the authorization for sale in
relation to which the certificate of supplementary protection is sought
was issued to that manufacturer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee --- application for certificate

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The fee
  payable on filing an application for a certificate of supplementary
  protection is \$9,011. Beginning on April 1, 2018, the fee increases
  annually by an amount equal to 2% of the fee payable in the previous
  year, rounded up to the nearest dollar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee must be paid in Canadian currency to the
  account of the Receiver General for Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- commencement of proceeding

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 110(2) of the Act, the prescribed period for commencing a
proceeding referred to in that subsection is 90 days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- expiry of pending applications

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 111(1) of the Act, the prescribed period for the expiry of
pending applications for a certificate of supplementary protection is 90
days. However, if any proceedings have been commenced under section 110
of the Act and two or more applications are pending at the end of the
90-day period, the prescribed period for the expiry of all of the
pending applications is 30 days after the day on which the last of those
proceedings has been finally disposed of.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of application

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
certificate of supplementary protection is withdrawn on the day on which
the Minister receives a notice of withdrawal from the applicant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Register

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
maintain an electronic register of applications for certificates of
supplementary protection and certificates of supplementary protection
that contains the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application, the number of the
  patent as recorded in the Patent Office, the date on which the term
  referred to in section 44 of the Act expires, the medicinal ingredient
  or combination of medicinal ingredients and the number of the
  authorization for sale set out in the application, along with an
  indication of whether the authorization for sale relates to human use
  or veterinary use; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a certificate of supplementary
  protection, the number of the patent as recorded in the Patent Office,
  the medicinal ingredient or combination of medicinal ingredients and
  the number of the authorization for sale set out in the certificate of
  supplementary protection, the number of the certificate of
  supplementary protection, the day on which its term begins and on
  which its term ends, along with an indication of whether it relates to
  human use or veterinary use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period --- new patent notice

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 122(1) of the Act, the prescribed period that begins on the
day on which a new patent is issued under section 47 of the Act is 30
days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of errors --- application

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  request of an applicant for a certificate of supplementary protection
  or with their consent, the Minister may, before the certificate of
  supplementary protection is issued, amend the application to correct
  any obvious error, including an obvious omission. However, after the
  expiry of the period prescribed by section 10, the Minister must not
  correct an error in the patent number that is set out in the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of errors --- certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may correct any obvious error, including
  an obvious omission, in a certificate of supplementary protection, on
  the basis of information that the Minister had, or could have
  obtained, on the day of its issuance. However, the Minister may also
  correct such an error that concerns how a medicinal ingredient, or a
  combination of medicinal ingredients, is set out on the basis of
  information that the Minister obtains after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of correction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A corrected application or certificate of
  supplementary protection has the same effect as if it were originally
  filed or issued in its corrected form.
