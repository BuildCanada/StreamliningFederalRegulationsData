Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of the
Environment is designated as the Minister responsible for the
administration and enforcement of subsections 36(3) to (6) of the Act
for all purposes and subject-matters, except the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction, operation, modification and
  decommissioning of, and other activity in relation to, an aquaculture
  facility, and any resulting effects of those activities on the waters
  frequented by fish; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the control or eradication of any aquatic invasive
  species or aquatic species that constitute a pest to the fisheries.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
administering and enforcing subsections 36(3) to (6) of the Act, for the
purposes and in relation to the subject-matters set out in section 2,
any reference to the Minister in the following provisions of the Act is
a reference to the Minister of the Environment:
