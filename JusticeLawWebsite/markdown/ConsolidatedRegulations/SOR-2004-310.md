## Manitoba Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations {#manitoba-sex-offender-information-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-310
:::

[SEX OFFENDER INFORMATION REGISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.7)

Registration 2004-12-13

Manitoba Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations
::::

::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Lieutenant Governor in Council of Manitoba, pursuant to subsection
18(1) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), S.C. 2004, c. 10, makes the attached [Manitoba
Sex Offender Information Registration
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-310).

December 8, 2004

::: FlushLeft
John Harvard
:::

::: FlushLeft
Lieutenent Governor of Manitoba
:::
:::::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[aboriginal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aboriginal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a first
    nations police service designated under paragraph 44(c) of The Law
    Enforcement Review Act, Continuing Consolidation of the Statutes of
    Manitoba, c. L75, as amended from time to time.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[MSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[MSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Sex Offender
    Information Registration Centre, located in RCMP Division "D"
    headquarters at 1091 Portage Avenue, Winnipeg. ([Bureau
    d'inscription]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713619 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in the Province of Manitoba
  may make any report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or
  section 4.3 of the Act, or provide any notification required under
  section 6 of the Act, by telephone at 1-877-700-1222.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not remove
  the requirement for the sex offender to report in person under
  paragraph 4.1(c) of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713623 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Manitoba to collect information for the
purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a person employed at the MSOIRC whose duties
  include the collection of information under the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer or special constable appointed to,
  or employed by, a municipal police service; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a special constable appointed to, or employed by, an
  aboriginal police service.

- SOR/2007-8, s. 1

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713631 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Persons employed at the
MSOIRC whose duties include the registration of information are
authorized in the Province of Manitoba to register that information for
the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713633 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  places set out in the schedule are designated as registration centres
  in the Province of Manitoba.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated area of service of each registration
  centre is the entire Province of Manitoba.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713637 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar_e){#fnstar_e-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
the [Sex Offender Information Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7),
chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes into force.
