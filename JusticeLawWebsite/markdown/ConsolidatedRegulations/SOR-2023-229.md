## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1426464 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Retail Payment Activities
    Act](/eng/acts/R-7.36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an entity,
    means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of its board of directors who is also
      one of its full-time employees;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its chief executive officer, chief operating
      officer, president, chief risk officer, secretary, treasurer,
      controller, chief financial officer, chief accountant, chief
      auditor or chief actuary, or any person who performs functions
      similar to those normally performed by someone occupying one of
      those positions; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other officer who reports directly to its
      board of directors, chief executive officer or chief operating
      officer. ([cadre dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Non-application of Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1426472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Securities-related transactions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-2 .sectionLabel}** A transaction in
relation to securities is a prescribed transaction for the purpose of
paragraph 6(b) of the Act if it is performed by an individual or entity
that is regulated, or exempted from regulation, under Canadian
securities legislation as defined in National Instrument 14-101
Definitions, as amended from time to time, of the Canadian Securities
Administrators.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incidental retail payment activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-3 .sectionLabel}** A retail payment
activity that is performed as a service or business activity that is
incidental to another service or business activity is, unless that other
service or business activity consists of the performance of a payment
function, a prescribed retail payment activity for the purpose of
paragraph 6(d) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}SWIFT

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Society for
Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication (SWIFT) is a prescribed
entity for the purpose of paragraph 9(k) of the Act.

## [Risk Management and Incident Response]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1426479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Framework

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  risk management and incident response framework required under
  subsection 17(1) of the Act must be in writing and must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the following among its objectives:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the payment service provider is
      able to perform retail payment activities without reduction,
      deterioration or breakdown, including by ensuring the availability
      of the systems, data and information involved in the performance
      of those activities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} preserving the integrity and confidentiality of
      those activities, systems, data and information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out clearly defined and measurable reliability
    targets for the ability to perform the retail payment activities and
    for the availability of the systems, data and information referred
    to in subparagraph (a)(i), as well as indicators for assessing
    whether each of the objectives referred to in paragraph (a) is met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identify the human and financial resources that are
    required to implement and maintain the framework, including, with
    respect to human resources, their skills and training, as well as
    the measures that the payment service provider must take to ensure
    timely and reliable access to those resources, whether from internal
    or external sources;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} allocate specific roles and responsibilities in
    respect of the implementation and maintenance of the framework ---
    both in the normal course of business and when detecting, responding
    to and recovering from incidents --- including, unless the payment
    service provider is an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} responsibility for challenging and overseeing the
      exercise of each of those roles and responsibilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to a senior officer, responsibility for
      overseeing the payment service provider's compliance with sections
      6 to 10 of these Regulations and subsection 17(1), section 18 and
      subsection 19(3) of the Act and for overseeing material decisions
      that relate to the payment service provider's identification and
      mitigation of, and response to, operational risks and incidents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} identify the assets --- including systems, data and
    information --- and business processes that are associated with the
    payment service provider's performance of retail payment activities
    and classify them according to their sensitivity and their
    criticality to the performance of those activities;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} identify, and describe the potential causes of, the
    payment service provider's operational risks, including those
    relating to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} business continuity and resilience,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cybersecurity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fraud,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} information and data management,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} information technology,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} human resources,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} process design and implementation,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} product design and implementation,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} change management,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} physical security of persons and assets, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} third parties;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} describe the systems, policies, procedures,
    processes, controls and any other means that the payment service
    provider must have in place to mitigate its operational risks and
    protect the assets and business processes referred to in paragraph
    (e);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} describe the systems, policies, procedures,
    processes, controls and any other means that the payment service
    provider must have in place to ensure the continuous monitoring of
    the following for the purpose of promptly detecting incidents,
    anomalous events that could indicate emerging operational risks and
    lapses in the implementation of the framework:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider's retail payment
      activities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the systems, data and information involved in
      the performance of those activities, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the systems, policies, procedures, processes,
      controls and other means referred to in paragraph (g);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} set out a plan for responding to --- including
    recovering from --- incidents, including those involving or detected
    by an agent or mandatary or a third-party service provider, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} contains clearly defined policies, processes and
      procedures for implementing the plan and for escalating the
      response to an incident, taking into account the incident response
      procedures of any third-party service provider from which the
      payment service provider receives services and the need to
      coordinate its response with that of the third-party service
      provider,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} identifies the measures to be taken to mitigate
      the impact of an incident, including manual processes or other
      alternate solutions that the payment service provider could resort
      to if primary systems relating to the provision of retail payment
      activities were unavailable, and indicates how quickly those
      measures could be implemented,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} requires the payment service provider, on
      becoming aware of an incident, to immediately investigate it to
      determine

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the incident's root causes,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} its possible or verified impact on retail
        payment activities,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} its possible or verified impact on end users,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} its possible or verified impact on other
        payment service providers or on [[clearing
        houses]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} of [[clearing and settlement
        systems]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} that are designated under
        subsection 4(1) of the [Payment Clearing and Settlement
        Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4), as those expressions are defined in
        section 2 of that Act, and

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} its possible or verified impact on systems,
        data or information involved in the performance of retail
        payment activities,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} requires the payment service provider, while an
      investigation is underway, to take immediate measures to prevent
      or mitigate any further damage, including to the integrity,
      confidentiality or availability of systems, data or information,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} requires the payment service provider to take
      measures as soon as feasible to address the identified root causes
      of the incident,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} sets out policies and procedures for reporting
      incidents to and coordinating incident response with relevant
      internal stakeholders --- including any senior officer referred to
      in subparagraph (d)(ii) and relevant agents and mandataries ---
      and relevant external stakeholders, that address, among other
      things,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the timing of the reporting and coordination,
        and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the information that is to be reported and
        shared for the purpose of coordination,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} addresses how the payment service provider will
      promptly identify the status of all transactions at the time of
      any service reduction, deterioration or breakdown, recover lost or
      corrupted data and correct any data integrity issues, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} requires the payment service provider to keep,
      in respect of each incident, a record of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in clauses (iii)(A)
        to (E), as determined by the investigation,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the measures taken in accordance with
        subparagraphs (ii), (iv) and (v),

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which it reported the incident
        and coordinated the incident response, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the status of all transactions identified, the
        manner in which the status of those transactions was identified
        and the manner in which the payment service provider recovered
        any lost or corrupted data and corrected any data integrity
        issues; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} set out a plan for responding to anomalous events
    or lapses referred to in paragraph (h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proportionality

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All aspects of the risk management and incident
  response framework --- including all objectives, targets, systems,
  policies, procedures, processes and controls --- must be proportionate
  to the impact that a reduction, deterioration or breakdown of the
  payment service provider's retail payment activities could have on end
  users and other payment service providers, having regard to factors
  including the payment service provider's ubiquity and connectedness,
  as established using the information referred to in subparagraph
  19(4)(a)(i) or paragraph 19(4)(b), as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third-party service providers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a payment service provider receives services
  related to a payment function from one or more third-party service
  providers, the risk management and incident response framework must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} address the means by which the payment service
    provider will --- no less than once a year in respect of each of its
    third-party service providers and before entering into, renewing,
    extending or substantially amending a contract with a third-party
    service provider for the provision of a service related to a payment
    function --- assess

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the third-party service provider's ability to
      protect data and information that they obtain from the payment
      service provider or in the course of performing services for it,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the security of the third-party service
      provider's connections to and from the payment service provider's
      systems,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the third-party service
      provider will consult or inform the payment service provider prior
      to making changes to the services that they provide, the manner in
      which they provide them or their practices for managing
      operational risk,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the third-party service
      provider's performance may be monitored, including the time and
      manner in which the third-party service provider will inform the
      payment service provider of any detected breach of the payment
      service provider's or the third-party service provider's data,
      information or systems and of any other deterioration, reduction
      or breakdown in the services provided to the payment service
      provider, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the third-party service provider's risk
      management practices in relation to the services that they provide
      to the payment service provider;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the payment service provider to keep a
    record of the dates, scope and findings of the assessments referred
    to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} clearly allocate responsibilities between the
    payment service provider and the third-party service provider,
    including in relation to the ownership, integrity, confidentiality
    and availability of data and information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agents and mandataries

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a payment service provider intends to have agents
  or mandataries perform retail payment activities, the risk management
  and incident response framework must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out criteria in relation to the management of
    operational risk that those agents or mandataries must satisfy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prohibit the payment service provider from having
    an agent or mandatary perform retail payment activities on its
    behalf if the agent or mandatary does not satisfy those criteria;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} address the means by which the payment service
    provider must, at least once a year, assess the extent to which its
    agents and mandataries satisfy those criteria and the agents' and
    mandataries' practices for managing operational risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} require the payment service provider to keep a
    record of the date and findings of each assessment referred to in
    paragraph (c); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} clearly allocate responsibilities between the
    payment service provider and its agents and mandataries, including
    in relation to the ownership, integrity, confidentiality and
    availability of data and information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third party roles and responsibilities

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the risk management and incident response
  framework allocates, under paragraph (1)(d), any roles or
  responsibilities to a third party, including a third-party service
  provider or an agent or mandatary, the framework must set out systems,
  policies, procedures, processes, controls or other means for
  overseeing the third party's fulfillment of those roles and
  responsibilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The risk management and incident response framework
  must be approved

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the senior officer referred to in subparagraph
    (1)(d)(ii), if any, at least once a year and following each material
    change that is made to the framework; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the payment service provider's board of
    directors, if any, at least once a year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability of framework

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-6 .sectionLabel}** A payment service
provider must ensure that its risk management and incident response
framework remains available to all persons who have a role in
implementing or maintaining it and must take all reasonable precautions
to prevent its unauthorized deletion, destruction or amendment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information and training

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-7 .sectionLabel}** A payment service
provider must ensure that all employees and other persons who have a
role in establishing, implementing or maintaining its risk management
and incident response framework are provided with the information and
training that are necessary to carry out that role.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider must review its risk management and incident
  response framework

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least once a year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before making any material change to its operations
    or its systems, policies, procedures, processes, controls or other
    means of managing operational risk.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The review must evaluate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the risk management and incident response
    framework's conformity with section 5;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider's effectiveness at
    meeting the objectives referred to in paragraph 5(1)(a), having
    regard to the targets and indicators referred to in paragraph
    5(1)(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the adequacy of the payment service provider's
    human and financial resources for ensuring implementation of the
    framework.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must, in respect of each
  review, keep a record of the date on which it is conducted and its
  scope, methodology and findings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report and approval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must ensure that the
  findings of each review are reported to the senior officer referred to
  in subparagraph 5(1)(d)(ii), if any, for their approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider must establish and implement a testing
  methodology, for the purpose of identifying gaps in the effectiveness
  of, and vulnerabilities in, the systems, policies, procedures,
  processes, controls and other means provided for in its risk
  management and incident response framework, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is proportionate to the impact that a reduction,
    deterioration or breakdown of the payment service provider's retail
    payment activities could have on end users and other payment service
    providers, having regard to factors including the payment service
    provider's ubiquity and connectedness, as established using the
    information referred to in subparagraph 19(4)(a)(i) or paragraph
    19(4)(b), as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is designed taking into account both
    high-likelihood and high-impact operational risks;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provides for the use of tests that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} involve relevant internal stakeholders, including
      agents or mandataries, decision-makers and individuals responsible
      for the payment service provider's operational risk management,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} take into account the payment service provider's
      reliance on external stakeholders, including third-party service
      providers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} sets out the frequency and scope of testing; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provides for testing before the adoption of any
    material change to the systems, policies, procedures, processes,
    controls or other means --- or to any of the payment service
    provider's operations that will affect them --- for the purpose of
    evaluating the effects of the change.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must, in respect of each
  test that it carries out, keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the test is carried out;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its methodology, including a summary of how the
    test satisfies the requirements of subparagraphs (1)(c)(i) and (ii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its results; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any measures taken or to be taken to address those
    results.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to senior officer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must ensure that the
  record is provided to the senior officer referred to in subparagraph
  5(1)(d)(ii), if any.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independent review

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider that has an internal or external auditor must
  ensure that, at least once every three years, a sufficiently skilled
  individual who has had no role in establishing, implementing or
  maintaining the payment service provider's risk management and
  incident response framework carries out an independent review of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conformity of each element of the payment
    service provider's risk management and incident response framework
    with the applicable requirements of section 5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider's compliance with each
    of its obligations under sections 6 to 9.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must obtain a record
  that sets out the independent reviewer's name --- or, if the
  independent reviewer carried out the review on behalf of an entity
  other than the payment service provider, that entity's name --- and
  the date of the review and describes the review's scope, methodology
  and findings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must report any gaps and
  vulnerabilities that are identified by the independent review, and any
  measures being taken to address them, to the senior officer referred
  to in subparagraph 5(1)(d)(ii), if any.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of incident --- Bank

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice that must be given to the Bank under section 18 of the Act must
  be submitted using the electronic system provided by the Bank for that
  purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider's name, the name of an
    individual who may be contacted regarding the incident and that
    individual's telephone number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the incident and its material
    impact on the individuals or entities referred to in paragraphs
    18(1)(a) to (c) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures taken by the payment service provider
    to respond to the incident.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of incident --- individual or
entity

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice that must be given under section 18 of the Act to an individual
  or entity referred to in any of paragraphs 18(1)(a) to (c) of the Act
  must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provided to each materially affected individual or
    entity using the most recent contact information provided by them to
    the payment service provider; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} posted on the payment service provider's website if
    contact information is not available for every materially affected
    individual or entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment service provider's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the incident, including when it
    began, and the nature of its material impacts on the individuals or
    entities; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any corrective measures that could be taken by the
    individuals or entities.

## [Safeguarding of Funds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1426618 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounts

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-13 .sectionLabel}** A payment service
provider that holds end-user funds in accordance with paragraph 20(1)(a)
or (c) of the Act must ensure that the account in which they are held is
provided by an entity that is referred to in one of paragraphs 9(a) to
(d) or (f) to (h) of the Act or by a foreign financial institution that
is regulated by a regulatory regime that imposes standards in respect of
capital, liquidity, governance, supervision and risk management that are
comparable to those that apply to those entities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insurance or guarantee

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider that holds end-user funds in accordance with
  paragraph 20(1)(c) of the Act must ensure that the insurance or
  guarantee referred to in that paragraph is provided by an entity that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is referred to in one of paragraphs 9(a) to (h) of
    the Act or is a foreign financial institution that is regulated by a
    regulatory regime that imposes standards in respect of capital,
    liquidity, governance, supervision and risk management comparable to
    those that apply to those entities; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not affiliated with the payment service provider
    within the meaning of section 3 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from the insurance or guarantee will
    not form part of the payment service provider's estate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from the insurance or guarantee will
    be payable for the benefit of end users as soon as feasible
    following an event referred to in subsection (3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the insurance or guarantee will survive the payment
    service provider's insolvency, as well as any compromise or
    arrangement with the payment service provider's creditors and any
    extinguishment of the payment service provider's obligations to end
    users, including those resulting from restructuring; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Bank is notified at least 30 days before any
    cancellation or termination of the insurance or guarantee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Events

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (2)(b), the events are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bringing by the payment service provider of an
    insolvency proceeding in respect of itself;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the consent by the payment service provider to the
    bringing of an insolvency proceeding in respect of it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the passage of 30 days after the day on which an
    insolvency proceeding is brought in respect of the payment service
    provider by another individual or entity, unless that insolvency
    proceeding is discontinued or dismissed in that time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[insolvency
  proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (3), [[insolvency
  proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any proceeding, action,
  application, case or legal process relating to bankruptcy, insolvency,
  liquidation, dissolution or winding-up that is commenced in respect of
  a payment service provider under the law of any jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safeguarding-of-funds framework

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment service provider that holds end-user funds must establish,
  implement and maintain a written safeguarding-of-funds framework that
  conforms to subsections (2) to (5) for the purpose of ensuring that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} end users have reliable access without delay to the
    end-user funds that are being held by the payment service provider;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if an event referred to in subsection 14(3) occurs
    in respect of the payment service provider, those end-user funds, or
    proceeds of the insurance or guarantee referred to in paragraph
    20(1)(c) of the Act, are paid to end users as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The safeguarding-of-funds framework must describe the
  payment service provider's systems, policies, processes, procedures,
  controls and other means for meeting the objectives referred to in
  subsection (1), including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those in relation to the payment service provider's
    use of liquidity arrangements and its holding of end-user funds in
    the form of secure and liquid assets;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a requirement to keep a ledger, which is to be
    identified and classified as an asset in accordance with paragraph
    5(1)(e), that sets out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of each end user
      whose funds are held by the payment service provider, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of funds belonging to each of those
      end users that is held by the payment service provider at the end
      of each day; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the objective referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the means by which it will be ensured that the
      insolvency or bankruptcy administrator or trustee or other person
      appointed to carry out [[insolvency
      proceedings]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
      14(4), or the insurance or guarantee provider, as the case may be,
      is able to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} access all relevant records or documentation in
        relation to end-user funds,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} contact end users as soon as feasible, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} identify any errors or deficiencies in the
        payment service provider's ledger of end-user funds and address
        any shortfall in the funds to be returned to each end user,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed to return funds to
      end users, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the role of any of the payment service
      provider's agents, mandataries or third-party service providers in
      facilitating the execution of the tasks referred to in
      subparagraphs (i) and (ii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal risks and operational risks

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The safeguarding-of-funds framework must identify
  legal risks and operational risks that could hinder the meeting of the
  objectives referred to in subsection (1) and the means of mitigating
  those risks, including having regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the jurisdictions in which the payment service
    provider, its end users, the providers of the accounts in which it
    holds end-user funds and, if applicable, its insurance or guarantee
    providers are located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the payment service provider's
    account providers and, if applicable, its insurance or guarantee
    providers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the payment service provider's trust
    arrangements with its end users, if applicable; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the payment service provider's
    insurance policies or guarantees, if applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of senior officer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The safeguarding-of-funds framework must, unless the
  payment service provider is an individual, identify a senior officer
  who is responsible for overseeing the payment service provider's
  practices for safeguarding end-user funds and for ensuring the payment
  service provider's compliance with sections 13 to 17 of these
  Regulations and subsection 20(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The safeguarding-of-funds framework must be approved

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by the senior officer, if any, at least once a year
    and following each material change that is made to the framework;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by the payment service provider's board of
    directors, if any, at least once a year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of framework

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The payment service provider must review, at the

  framework's conformity with subsections (2) to (5) and its
  effectiveness at meeting the objectives referred to in subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least once a year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after any change to the means, among those set out
    in paragraphs 20(1)(a) to (c) of the Act, by which the payment
    service provider safeguards end-user funds; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} after any of the following changes, if they could
    reasonably be expected to have a material impact on the manner in
    which end-user funds are safeguarded:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the opening or closure of any account in which
      the payment service provider holds end-user funds,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a change in the entity that provides any account
      in which the payment service provider holds end-user funds,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a change to the terms of the account agreement

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a payment service provider that

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-49 .sectionLabel}** The amount payable as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the harm that is done by the violation and the harm
  that could have been done by it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the history of the individual or entity that
  committed the violation with respect to any prior violation committed
  by them within the five-year period immediately before the violation;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the degree of intention or negligence on the part of
  the individual or entity that committed the violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional penalty

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                            s-50 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 82(1)(b) of the Act, the additional penalty is equal to the
amount of the penalty set out in the notice of violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#
                              s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  notice that is to be served under Part 5 of the Act must be served by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with the individual,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with someone who appears to
      be an adult member of the same household at the individual's last
      known address or usual place of residence,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail or
      courier to the individual's last known address or usual place of
      residence,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it to the individual's last
      known email address, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} making a copy of it available to the individual
      through an electronic system maintained for that purpose by the
      Bank and advising the individual, by email to their last known
      email address, of the availability of the notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service on an entity,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with an individual who
      appears to manage or be in control of the head office or place of
      business of the entity or of the entity's authorized
      representative,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail or
      courier to the head office or place of business of the entity or
      of the entity's authorized representative,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it to the entity's last known
      email address, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} making a copy of it available to the entity
      through an electronic system maintained for that purpose by the
      Bank and advising the entity, by email to its last known email
      address, of the availability of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice is deemed to be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which it is left with an individual
    in accordance with subparagraph (1)(a)(i) or (ii) or (b)(i);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the 10th day after the date indicated in the
    receipt issued by the postal or courier service, in the case of
    service by registered mail or courier; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which the email referred to in
    subparagraph (1)(a)(iv) or (v) or (b)(iii) or (iv) is delivered.

  34(1) of the Act begins on the day on which the Minister is provided

  transition period; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed period for the purpose of section 36
  of the Act begins on the day on which the Minister decides to review
  the application and ends on the later of 180 days after that day and
  180 days after the last day of the transition period.

                            s-53 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed period

the day on which section 29 of the Act comes into force and ends on the
later of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day that is 14 days after the day on which
  section 29 of the Act comes into force, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day that is 60 days before the first day during
  the transition period on which the payment service provider plans to
  perform retail payment activities.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address, telephone number and email address of
  the applicant's place of business, as well as their website address,
  if any.
