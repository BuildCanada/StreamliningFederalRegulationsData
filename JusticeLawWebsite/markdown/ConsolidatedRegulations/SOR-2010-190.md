## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761443 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Inuvialuit Final
    Agreement as approved, given effect and declared valid by the
    [Western Arctic (Inuvialuit) Claims Settlement
    Act](/eng/acts/W-6.7). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Tarium Niryutait Marine
    Protected Areas. ([zones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the seabed and subsoil below
    the waters to a depth of five metres. ([eaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761448 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Areas consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Niaqunnaq Marine Protected Area designated under
  section 3;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Okeevik Marine Protected Area designated under
  section 4; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Kittigaryuit Marine Protected Area designated
  under section 5.

### [Niaqunnaq Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-761453 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The area of the sea in
Mackenzie Bay consisting of the waters within the boundaries described
in plan number FB36305, certified on February 19, 2009 and depicted in
plan number CLSR 91991, Sheet 2, which plans are deposited in the Canada
Lands Surveys Records, is designated as the Niaqunnaq Marine Protected
Area.

### [Okeevik Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-761455 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  of the sea in the Mackenzie River Estuary consisting of the waters
  within the boundaries described in plan number FB36305, certified on
  February 19, 2009 and depicted in plan number CLSR 91991, Sheet 3,
  which plans are deposited in the Canada Lands Surveys Records, is
  designated as the Okeevik Marine Protected Area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Okeevik Marine Protected Area is comprised of
  Special Management Zones 1 and 2 and the Primary Protection Zone as
  described in plan number FB36305, certified on February 19, 2009 and
  depicted in plan number CLSR 91991, Sheet 3, which plans are deposited
  in the Canada Lands Surveys Records.

### [Kittigaryuit Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-761459 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The area of the sea in the
Mackenzie River Estuary consisting of the waters within the boundaries
described in plan number FB36305, certified on February 19, 2009 and
depicted in plan number CLSR 91991, Sheet 4, which plans are deposited
in the Canada Lands Surveys Records, is designated as the Kittigaryuit
Marine Protected Area.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761461 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy in the Areas, or remove
  from them, any living marine organism or any part of its habitat; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity in the Areas --- including
  depositing, discharging or dumping any substance, or causing any
  substance to be deposited, discharged or dumped --- that is likely to
  result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a living
  marine organism or any part of its habitat.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761465 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
may be carried out in the Areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing in accordance with the Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dredging

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act and the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and their regulations, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing in accordance with the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and its regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a scientific activity that is carried out in
  accordance with the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and its
  regulations or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out for the purpose of managing
    the Areas or for monitoring the effectiveness of conservation
    measures implemented in the Areas;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a geophysical operation, as defined in section 2 of
  the [Canada Oil and Gas Geophysical Operations
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-117),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out on, through or under the ice
    cover of the Areas,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act, [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
    their regulations, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} exploratory drilling for oil or gas in the Special
  Management Zones of the Okeevik Marine Protected Area

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out on, through or under the ice
    cover of the Areas,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act, [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
    their regulations, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} oil or gas production in the Special Management Zones
  of the Okeevik Marine Protected Area,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act, [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
    their regulations, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the construction or decommissioning of an oil or gas
  pipeline

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out on, through or under the ice
    cover of the Areas,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act, [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
    their regulations, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance of an oil or gas pipeline,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has been recommended in accordance with the
    Agreement and authorized by a competent government authority,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is carried out in accordance with the
    Navigable Waters Protection Act, [Species at Risk
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3), [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
    [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
    their regulations, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that does not result in and is not likely to
    result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a
    marine mammal;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} any movement or other activity of a ship, submarine
  or aircraft if the movement or other activity is carried out for the
  purpose of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} public safety, law enforcement or national security
    or for the exercise of Canadian sovereignty and the ship, submarine
    or aircraft is owned or operated by or on behalf of Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or by a foreign military force acting in cooperation
    with, or under the command or control of, the Canadian Forces, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an emergency response under the direction, command
    or control of the Canadian Coast Guard; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any activity carried out for the purpose of public
  health and safety.

## [Reporting of Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761503 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is
involved in an accident that is likely to result in any disturbance,
damage, destruction or removal prohibited under section 6 shall, within
two hours after its occurrence, report the accident to the Canadian
Coast Guard.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-761505 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
