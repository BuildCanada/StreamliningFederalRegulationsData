## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-department-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-aboriginal-affairs-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-86
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Department of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development the Control and Supervision of
the Aboriginal Affairs Secretariat

P.C. 2004-849 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Department of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development the control and supervision of the
portion of the public service within the Intergovernmental Affairs
Branch in the Privy Council Office known as the Aboriginal Affairs
Secretariat, effective July 20, 2004.
