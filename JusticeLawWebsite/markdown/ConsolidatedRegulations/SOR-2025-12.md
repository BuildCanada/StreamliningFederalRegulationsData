## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1508548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted of surtaxes paid or payable
  under the [China Surtax Order (2024)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-187)
  in respect of the goods referred to in Schedule 1 or 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission referred to in subsection (1) is granted on
  the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a good referred to in Schedule 1

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not the subject of an [[order or
      finding]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15), on the date of
      importation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is imported into Canada during the period
      beginning on October 22, 2024 and ending on December 31, 2025;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a good referred to in column 3 of
    Schedule 2 that is classified under the tariff item listed in column
    2, it is imported into Canada by an importer listed in column 1
    during the period specified in column 4, and any other applicable
    condition listed in column 5 is met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no other claim for relief of the surtax has been
    granted under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of
    the good;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the good is not subsequently exported to the United
    States in the same condition in which it was imported; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the importer makes a claim for remission to the
    Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
    years after the date of importation.
