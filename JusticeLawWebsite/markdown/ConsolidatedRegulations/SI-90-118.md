## Spruce Falls Remission Order {#spruce-falls-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-118
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-10-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax to Spruce Falls Power and
Paper Company, Limited

P.C. 1990-2094  1990-09-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of income tax to Spruce Falls Power and
Paper Company, Limited](/eng/regulations/SI-90-118).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642484 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Spruce Falls Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-90-118).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642486 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Power Rights Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Power Rights Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement
    dated October 31, 1989, entered into between Spruce Falls and
    Ontario Hydro for the supply of electrical power by Ontario Hydro to
    Spruce Falls at no charge; ([Convention d'approvisionnement en
    électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Spruce Falls]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Spruce Falls]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Spruce Falls Power and
    Paper Company, Limited; ([Spruce Falls]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Water Power Lease Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Water Power Lease Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    agreement entered into between Her Majesty The Queen in Right of
    Ontario and Spruce Falls dated January 1, 1983, as amended by an
    amendment dated March 17, 1989, pursuant to which certain lands were
    leased by Her Majesty to Spruce Falls for the development of water
    power. ([bail pour l'exploitation de ressources
    hydro-électriques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642492 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is granted to Spruce Falls, in respect of each of the 1989 and
subsequent taxation years, of an amount equal to the amount, if any, by
which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the income tax payable under Part I
  of the Act by Spruce Falls for the year and the interest and penalties
  in respect thereof payable by it under the Act for the year

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the income tax that would be payable
  under Part I of the Act by Spruce Falls for the year and the interest
  and penalties in respect thereof that would be payable by it under the
  Act for the year, if subsections 13(4) and (4.1) and section 44 of the
  Act were to apply to the assignment by Spruce Falls of the Water Power
  Lease Agreement to Ontario Hydro and the acquisition by Spruce Falls
  of the Power Rights Agreement as if the Water Power Lease Agreement
  were a former business property and the Power Rights Agreement were
  depreciable property acquired as a replacement for that former
  business property, as those terms are defined for the purposes of the
  Act, and as if Spruce Falls had elected under subsection 13(4) of the
  Act as and when required for that subsection to apply with respect to
  the disposition of the Water Power Lease Agreement and the acquisition
  of the Power Rights Agreement.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642497 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
this Order on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in calculating the profit or loss (other than on
  account of capital) of Spruce Falls in respect of each of the 1989 and
  subsequent taxation years, no deduction in respect of the cost of the
  Power Rights Agreement to Spruce Falls resulting from the lease
  assignment is claimed by Spruce Falls;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is a disposition of the Power Rights
  Agreement by Spruce Falls,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} subsections 13(4) and 44(1) of the Act are treated
    by Spruce Falls as applying in respect of the assignment by Spruce
    Falls of the Water Power Lease Agreement to Ontario Hydro and the
    acquisition by Spruce Falls of the Power Rights Agreement as if the
    Water Power Lease Agreement were a former business property and the
    Power Rights Agreement were depreciable property acquired as a
    replacement for that former business property, as those terms are
    defined for the purposes of the Act, and as if Spruce Falls had
    elected under subsection 13(4) of the Act as and when required for
    that subsection to apply with respect to the disposition of the
    Water Power Lease Agreement and the acquisition of the Power Rights
    Agreement, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 13(1) of the Act,
    the Power Rights Agreement is treated by Spruce Falls for income tax
    purposes as property included in Class 14 in Schedule II to the
    [Income Tax Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._945); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all conditions precedent contained in the Power
  Rights Agreement are satisfied by the parties to that Agreement before
  1996.
