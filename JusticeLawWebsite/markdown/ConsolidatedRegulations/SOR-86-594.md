## Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Rules {#transportation-appeal-tribunal-of-canada-rules .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-594
:::

[TRANSPORTATION APPEAL TRIBUNAL OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/T-18.5)

Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Rules

P.C. 1986-1265 1986-05-29

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 29(3)[[Footnote

Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve effective June 1, 1986
the annexed Rules governing the practice and procedure in connection
with matters dealt with by the Civil Aviation Tribunal, made by the
Civil Aviation Tribunal.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897407 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Transportation Appeal
    Tribunal of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-18.5); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to a
    proceeding; ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a request for a review, an
    appeal or an application that is before the
    Tribunal; ([instance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registrar of the Tribunal,
    and includes a deputy registrar; ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the principal office of the
    Tribunal in the National Capital Region or such other offices as the
    Tribunal may establish from time to time. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897416 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requests for a review or appeals brought before the
  Tribunal under a statute referred to in subsection 2(2) or (3) of the
  Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} applications referred to in section 10.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-202, s. 4
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897422 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a procedural matter not
provided for by the Act, by statutes referred to in subsection 2(2) or
(3) of the Act or by these Rules arises during the course of any
proceeding, the Tribunal may take any action it considers necessary to
enable it to settle the matter effectively, completely and fairly.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-202, s. 5
:::

## [Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897426 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Service of a document,
other than a summons referred to in section 14, shall be effected by
personal service or by registered mail.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where service of a document
is effected by registered mail, the date of service is the date of
receipt of the document.

## [Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897429 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a party is required
or authorized to file a document with the Tribunal, the document may be
filed by depositing it in the registry personally, by mailing it or
sending it by courier to the registry or by transmitting it to the
registry by telex, facsimile or other electronic means of communication
if the registry has the necessary facilities for accepting transmission
in such manner.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The date of filing of a
document with the Tribunal is the date of receipt of the document at the
registry, as evidenced on the document by means of the filing stamp of
the Tribunal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Holiday]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897434 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a time limit prescribed
by these Rules falls on a Saturday or a holiday, the time limit is
extended to the next following business day.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 3(E)
- SOR/2017-202, s. 6

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897438 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to any application for any relief or order brought
  before the Tribunal under a statute referred to in subsection 2(2)
  or (3) of the Act, other than a request for a review or an appeal.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An application shall be in writing and filed with
  the Tribunal unless, in the opinion of the Tribunal, circumstances
  exist to allow the application to be brought in some other manner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application shall fully set out the grounds on
  which it is based and shall specify the relief or order requested.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), where a party makes an
  application, the Tribunal shall serve notice of the application on
  each other party and shall afford each other party a reasonable
  opportunity to make representations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may dispose of an application on the
  basis of the material submitted by each party or, if in its opinion
  there exist exigent circumstances, on the basis of the material
  submitted by the applicant only.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal, upon considering the material submitted
  to it, shall render its determination of an application in writing and
  shall serve on each party a copy of the determination forthwith after
  the determination has been rendered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 4
- SOR/2017-202, s. 7
:::

## [Extending or Abridging Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897448 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may extend
or abridge a time prescribed by or pursuant to these Rules for
performing any act or doing any thing on such terms, if any, as seem
just.

## [Preliminary Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897450 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may, orally
or in writing, direct that the parties appear before a member of the
Tribunal at a specified date, time and place for a conference, or
consult each other and submit suggestions in writing to the Tribunal,
for the purpose of assisting it in the consideration of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the admission or proof of certain facts;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any procedural matter;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exchange between the parties of documents and
  exhibits proposed to be submitted during a proceeding;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the need to call particular witnesses; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that may aid in the simplification
  of the evidence and disposition of the proceeding.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 5(E)
:::

## [Adjournments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897459 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** At any time, the Tribunal
may, on the application of any party or on its own motion, adjourn a
proceeding on such terms, if any, as seem just.

## [Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897461 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  request of a party, the registrar shall issue a summons in blank for a
  person to appear as a witness before the Tribunal and the summons may
  be completed by the party requesting it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A summons shall be served personally on the person to
  whom it is directed at least 48 hours before the time fixed for the
  attendance of the person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the time of service of a summons on a person, the
  party requesting the appearance of the person shall pay witness fees
  and travel expenses to the person in accordance with Rule 42 of the
  [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-202, s. 8
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-14-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 14"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person has been summoned to appear as a witness before the Tribunal
  and does not appear, the party that requested the issuance of the
  summons may apply to the Tribunal for a warrant directing a peace
  officer to cause the person who failed to appear to be apprehended
  anywhere in Canada and, subsequent to the apprehension, to be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} detained in custody and immediately brought before
    the Tribunal until their presence as a witness is no longer
    required; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} released on a recognizance, with or without
    sureties, conditional on the person's appearance at the date, time
    and place specified therein to give evidence at a proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application made pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  contain information indicating that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person named in the summons

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was served with the summons in accordance with
      subsection 14(2),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was paid or offered witness fees and travel
      expenses in accordance with subsection 14(3), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} failed to attend or remain in attendance before
      the Tribunal in accordance with the requirements of the summons;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the presence of the person named in the summons is
    material to the proceeding.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 6(E)
- SOR/2017-202, s. 9
:::

## [Proceeding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897480 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Witnesses at a proceeding shall be
  subject to examination and cross-examination orally on oath or solemn
  affirmation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may order a witness at a proceeding to
  be excluded from the proceeding until called to give evidence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, with the consent of each party,
  order that any fact be proved by affidavit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may inspect any property or thing for
  the purpose of evaluating the evidence.

## [Argument]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897486 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may direct a
party to submit written argument in addition to oral argument.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897488 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal to the Tribunal shall be commenced by filing a request for
  appeal in writing with the Tribunal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for appeal shall include a concise
  statement of the grounds on which the appeal is based.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a request for appeal shall be served by the
  Tribunal on each other party within ten days after filing the request.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 7
- SOR/2017-202, s. 10
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-18-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 18"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Where a request for
appeal has been filed with the Tribunal, the Tribunal shall serve on the
parties to the appeal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice of the date, time and place of the hearing
  of the appeal; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the record referred to in section 20 of the
  Act, respecting the matters to which the appeal relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-346, s. 8
- SOR/2017-202, s. 11

## [Determination or Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897500 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2017-202, s. 12(E)

\]
:::

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal shall render its determination or decision in writing at the
  conclusion of a proceeding or as soon as is feasible after a
  proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of calculating the period within
  which a party may appeal a determination, the determination is deemed
  to be made on the day on which it is served on the party.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal shall serve on each party a copy of the
  determination or decision immediately after it has been rendered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-202, s. 13(E)
:::
