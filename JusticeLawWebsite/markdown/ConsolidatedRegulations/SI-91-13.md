## GST Federal Government Departments Remission Order {#gst-federal-government-departments-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-13
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-01-30

GST Federal Government Departments Remission Order

P.C. 1990-2854  1990-12-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 23 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to remit the tax that is paid or
payable by a department under Part IX[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-13_e_hq_6476){#fn_SI-91-13_e_hq_6476-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
