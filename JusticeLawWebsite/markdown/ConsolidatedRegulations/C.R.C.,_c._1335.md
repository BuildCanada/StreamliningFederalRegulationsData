## Defence Research Board Employees Regulations {#defence-research-board-employees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1335
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Regulations Respecting the Application of the Public Service Employment
Act to Employees of the Defence Research Board
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503852 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Defence Research Board Employees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1335).

## [Application of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503854 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding paragraph
53(a) of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), all the provisions
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) apply to the
professional, scientific, technical, clerical and other employees
required to carry out efficiently the duties of the Defence Research
Board.
