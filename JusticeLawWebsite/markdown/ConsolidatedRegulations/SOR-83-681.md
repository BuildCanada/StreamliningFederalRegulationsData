## Excise Tax Indexing Ratio Regulations {#excise-tax-indexing-ratio-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-681
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1983-08-24

Regulations Respecting the Ratio Used in the Indexing of Excise Taxes on
Wines, Cigarettes and Manufactured Tobacco

P.C. 1983-2591 1983-08-24
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant
to clause 24(2)(b)(i)(B)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHCAA} of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and clause 3(b)(i)(B)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDCAA} of Schedule II to the
said Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting
the ratio used in the indexing of excise taxes on wines, cigarettes and
manufactured tobacco](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-681).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880994 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Excise Tax Indexing Ratio
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-681).

## [Alteration of Ratio]{.HTitleText1} {#h-880996 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
adjusting the excise taxes imposed by subsection 24(1) of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the ratio determined according to
clause 24(2)(b)(i)(B) of that Act is altered by multiplying the ratio

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by 1.35559 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1983; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by 1.99924 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1984.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-729, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
adjusting the excise tax rates enumerated in section 1 of Schedule II to
the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the ratio determined according to
clause 3(b)(i)(B) of that schedule is altered by multiplying the ratio

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by 1.37078 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1983; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by 1.88464 for the adjustment on the first day of
  September, 1984.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-729, s. 2
:::
