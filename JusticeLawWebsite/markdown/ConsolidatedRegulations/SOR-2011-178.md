## Promotion of Tobacco Products and Accessories Regulations (Prohibited Terms) {#promotion-of-tobacco-products-and-accessories-regulations-prohibited-terms .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-178
:::

[TOBACCO AND VAPING PRODUCTS ACT](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

TOBACCO ACT

Registration 2011-09-22

Promotion of Tobacco Products and Accessories Regulations (Prohibited
Terms)

P.C. 2011-926 2011-09-22

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 42.1 of the Tobacco Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDADA}, the Minister of Health laid
a copy of the proposed [Promotion of Tobacco Products and Accessories
Regulations (Prohibited Terms)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-178),
substantially in the annexed form, before the House of Commons on
June 9, 2011 and the House of Commons concurred on June 22, 2011 in a
report from the Standing Committee on Health approving the proposed
Regulations;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to
section 33[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EGACA} of the
Tobacco Act[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA},
hereby makes the annexed [Promotion of Tobacco Products and Accessories
Regulations (Prohibited Terms)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-178).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773807 .Part}

Definition of [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any roll or tubular
construction that contains tobacco and is intended for smoking, other
than a bidi, cigar, kretek or tobacco stick.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of prohibitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The prohibitions in these
Regulations that apply with respect to the terms "light" and "mild" also
apply with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any variations in the spelling of those terms as well
  as the spelling of other parts of speech and grammatical forms of
  those terms; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any modifiers of those terms, including "extra" and
  "ultra", as well as any abbreviation of those terms or modifiers.

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773815 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773816 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No promotion if terms on product

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not promote a bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar or tobacco
  stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or filters if the
  tobacco product displays the term "light" or "mild" or promote such a
  tobacco product by affixing to it the term "light" or "mild".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No promotion if terms on packaging

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not promote a bidi, cigarette, kretek,
  little cigar or tobacco stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers,
  tubes or filters if the package of the tobacco product displays the
  term "light" or "mild".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No promotion if terms on accessories

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person must not promote

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an accessory that displays a brand element of a
    bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar or tobacco stick or cigarette
    tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or filters if that accessory
    displays the term "light" or "mild"; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar or tobacco
    stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or filters by
    affixing to an accessory a brand element of such a tobacco product
    if that accessory displays the term "light" or "mild".

### [Advertising]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773826 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No advertising of product with terms

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person must not promote a
bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar or tobacco stick, or cigarette
tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or filters by using the term "light" or
"mild" in an advertisement of the tobacco product.

### [Packaging]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773829 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No packaging of products with terms

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person must not package a
bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar or tobacco stick or cigarette
tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or filters, or have the tobacco product
packaged by a third party, in a package that displays the term "light"
or "mild".

### [Sale]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773832 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No sale of accessories with terms

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person must not sell an
accessory that displays a brand element of a bidi, cigarette, kretek,
little cigar or tobacco stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers,
tubes or filters if that accessory displays the term "light" or "mild".

### [Displaying at Retail]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773835 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No retail display of products with terms

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not display, at retail, a bidi, cigarette, kretek, little cigar
  or tobacco stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes or
  filters if the term "light" or "mild" is displayed on the tobacco
  product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No retail display of accessories with
  terms

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not display, at retail, an accessory
  that displays a brand element of a bidi, cigarette, kretek, little
  cigar or tobacco stick or cigarette tobacco, cigarette papers, tubes
  or filters if that accessory displays the term "light" or "mild".

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773841 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations come into force on the day on
  which they are registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}After registration --- four months

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 6 and 7 come into force on the day that is
  four months after the day on which these Regulations are registered.
