## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785834 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Basis of Claim Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Basis of Claim Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the form in which
    a claimant gives the information referred to in
    Schedule 1. ([Formulaire de fondement de la demande
    d'asile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with respect to a
    person,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, postal address and telephone
      number, and their fax number and email address, if any; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of counsel for a claimant or
      protected person, if the counsel is a person referred to in any of
      paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act, in addition to the
      information referred to in paragraph (a), the name of the body of
      which the counsel is a member and the membership identification
      number issued to the counsel. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Refugee Protection
    Division. ([Section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as an
    officer by the Minister under subsection 6(1) of the
    Act. ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a claim for refugee protection,
      the claimant and, if the Minister intervenes in the claim, the
      Minister; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application to vacate or to
      cease refugee protection, the protected person and the
      Minister. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a conference, an
    application or a hearing. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business office of
    the Division. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vulnerable person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vulnerable person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has
    been identified as vulnerable under the Guideline on Procedures with
    Respect to Vulnerable Persons Appearing Before the IRB issued under
    paragraph 159(1)(h) of the Act. ([personne
    vulnérable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include Saturdays,
    Sundays or other days on which the Board offices are closed. ([jour
    ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Communicating with the Division]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785852 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating with Division

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** All communication with the
Division must be directed to the registry office specified by the
Division.

## [Information and Documents to Be Provided]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785855 .Part}

### [Claims for Refugee Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-785856 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fixing date, time and location of hearing

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as a claim for refugee protection is referred to the Division, or as
  soon as possible before it is deemed to be referred under
  subsection 100(3) of the Act, an officer must fix a date, time and
  location for the claimant to attend a hearing on the claim, within the
  time limits set out in the Regulations, from the dates, times and
  locations provided by the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date fixed by officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph 3(b), the officer must select
  the date closest to the last day of the applicable time limit set out
  in the Regulations, unless the claimant agrees to an earlier date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In fixing the date, time and location for the
  hearing, the officer must consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claimant's preference of location; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} counsel's availability, if the claimant has
    retained counsel at the time of referral and the officer has been
    informed that counsel will be available to attend a hearing on one
    of the dates provided by the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing information to claimant in
  writing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The officer must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the claimant in writing by way of a notice
    to appear

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the date, time and location of the hearing of
      the claim; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of the date, time and location of any special
      hearing on the abandonment of the claim under subrules 65(2) and
      (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless the claimant has provided a completed Basis
    of Claim Form to the officer in accordance with subsection 99(3.1)
    of the Act, provide to the claimant the Basis of Claim Form; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide to the claimant information in writing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} explaining how and when to provide a Basis of
      Claim Form and other documents to the Division and to the
      Minister,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} informing the claimant of the importance of
      obtaining relevant documentary evidence without delay,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} explaining how the hearing will proceed,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} informing the claimant of the obligation to
      notify the Division and the Minister of the claimant's contact
      information and any changes to that information,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} informing the claimant that they may, at their
      own expense, be represented by legal or other counsel, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} informing the claimant that the claim may be
      declared abandoned without further notice if the claimant fails to
      provide the completed Basis of Claim Form or fails to appear at
      the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing information in writing and
  documents to Division

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} After providing to the claimant the information set
  out in subrule (4), the officer must without delay provide to the
  Division

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written statement indicating how and when the
    information set out in subrule (4) was provided to the claimant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the completed Basis of Claim Form for a claimant
    referred to in subsection 99(3.1) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each notice to appear provided to the
    claimant in accordance with paragraph (4)(a);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any identity and travel documents of the
    claimant that have been seized by the officer;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice of seizure of any seized
    documents referred to in paragraph (e); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any other relevant documents that are in
    the possession of the officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing copies to claimant

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The officer must provide to the claimant a copy of
  any documents or information that the officer has provided to the
  Division under paragraphs (5)(d) to (g).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claimant's contact information

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  claimant must provide their contact information in writing to the
  Division and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claimant's contact information must be received
  by the Division and the Minister no later than 10 days after the day
  on which the claimant receives the information provided by the officer
  under subrule 3(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to contact information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant's contact information changes, the
  claimant must without delay provide the changes in writing to the
  Division and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information concerning claimant's
  counsel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A claimant who is represented by counsel must without
  delay, on retaining counsel, provide the counsel's contact information
  in writing to the Division and to the Minister and notify them of any
  limitations on the counsel's retainer. If that information changes,
  the claimant must without delay provide the changes in writing to the
  Division and to the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- counsel not representing
or advising for consideration

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If a claimant retains
counsel who is not a person referred to in any of paragraphs 91(2)(a) to
(c) of the Act, both the claimant and their counsel must without delay
provide the information and declarations set out in Schedule 3 to the
Division in writing.

### [Basis of Claim Form]{.HTitleText2} {#h-785901 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claimant's declarations

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  claimant must complete a Basis of Claim Form and sign and date the
  declaration set out in the form stating that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information given by the claimant is complete,
    true and correct; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claimant understands that the declaration is of
    the same force and effect as if made under oath.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form completed without interpreter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant completes the Basis of Claim Form
  without an interpreter's assistance, the claimant must sign and date
  the declaration set out in the form stating that they can read the
  language of the form and understand what information is requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant completes the Basis of Claim Form
  with an interpreter's assistance, the interpreter must sign and date
  the declaration in the form stating that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are proficient in the language and dialect, if
    any, used, and were able to communicate effectively with the
    claimant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the completed Basis of Claim Form and all attached
    documents were interpreted to the claimant; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the claimant indicated that the claimant understood
    what was interpreted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing Basis of Claim Form --- inland
claim

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  claimant referred to in subsection 99(3.1) of the Act must provide the
  original and a copy of the completed Basis of Claim Form to the
  officer referred to in rule 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing Basis of Claim Form --- port
  of entry claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claimant other than a claimant referred to in
  subsection 99(3.1) of the Act must provide the original and a copy of
  the completed Basis of Claim Form to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be attached

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The claimant must attach to the original and to the
  copy of the completed Basis of Claim Form a copy of their identity and
  travel documents, genuine or not, and a copy of any other relevant
  documents in their possession. The claimant does not have to attach a
  copy of a document that has been seized by an officer or provided to
  the Division by an officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents obtained after providing
  Basis of Claim Form

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant obtains an identity or travel
  document after the Division has received the completed Basis of Claim
  Form, they must provide two copies of the document to the Division
  without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing Basis of Claim Form --- port
  of entry claim

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Basis of Claim Form provided under subrule (2)
  must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} received by the Division within the time limit set
    out in the Regulations, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provided in any of the following ways:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by hand,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by courier,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by fax if the document is no more than 20 pages
      long, unless the Division consents to receiving more than
      20 pages, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} by email or other electronic means if the
      Division allows.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original Basis of Claim Form

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A claimant who provides the Basis of Claim Form by
  fax must provide the original to the Division at the beginning of the
  hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  claimant who makes an application for an extension of time to provide
  the completed Basis of Claim Form must make the application in
  accordance with rule 50, but the claimant is not required to give
  evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be received by the Division no
  later than three working days before the expiry of the time limit set
  out in the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for medical reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant makes the application for medical
  reasons, other than those related to their counsel, they must provide,
  together with the application, a legible, recently dated medical
  certificate signed by a qualified medical practitioner whose name and
  address are printed or stamped on the certificate. A claimant who has
  provided a copy of the certificate to the Division must provide the
  original document to the Division without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The medical certificate must set out the particulars
  of the medical condition, without specifying the diagnosis, that
  prevent the claimant from providing the completed Basis of Claim Form
  in the time limit referred to in paragraph 7(5)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide medical certificate

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant fails to provide a medical certificate
  in accordance with subrules (3) and (4), the claimant must include in
  their application

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} particulars of any efforts they made to obtain the
    required medical certificate, supported by corroborating evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the medical reasons for the
    application, supported by corroborating evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the medical condition
    prevents them from providing the completed Basis of Claim Form in
    the time limit referred to in paragraph 7(5)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing Basis of Claim Form after
  extension granted

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If an extension of time is granted, the claimant must
  provide the original and a copy of the completed Basis of Claim Form
  to the Division in accordance with subrules 7(2) and (3), no later
  than on the date indicated by the Division and by a means set out in
  paragraph 7(5)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes or additions to Basis of Claim
Form

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To make
  changes or add any information to the Basis of Claim Form, the
  claimant must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Division the original and a copy of
    each page of the form to which changes or additions have been made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sign and date each new page and underline the
    changes or additions made; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sign and date a declaration stating that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information given by the claimant in the
      Basis of Claim Form, together with the changes and additions, is
      complete, true and correct, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the claimant understands that the declaration is
      of the same force and effect as if made under oath.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subrule (1) must be
  provided to the Division without delay and must be received by it no
  later than 10 days before the date fixed for the hearing.

### [Conduct of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-785959 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard order of questioning

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In a
  hearing of a claim for refugee protection, if the Minister is not a
  party, any witness, including the claimant, will be questioned first
  by the Division and then by the claimant's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of questioning --- Minister's
  intervention on exclusion issue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a hearing of a claim for refugee protection, if
  the Minister is a party and has intervened on an issue of exclusion
  under subrule 29(3), any witness, including the claimant, will be
  questioned first by the Minister's counsel, then by the Division and
  then by the claimant's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of questioning --- Minister's
  intervention not on exclusion issue

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a hearing of a claim for refugee protection, if
  the Minister is a party but has not intervened on an issue of
  exclusion under subrule 29(3), any witness, including the claimant,
  will be questioned first by the Division, then by the Minister's
  counsel and then by the claimant's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of questioning --- application to
  vacate or cease refugee protection

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In a hearing into an application to vacate or to
  cease refugee protection, any witness, including the protected person,
  is to be questioned first by the Minister's counsel, then by the
  Division and then by the protected person's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of order of questioning

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not vary the order of questioning
  unless there are exceptional circumstances, including that the
  variation is required to accommodate a vulnerable person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limiting questioning of witnesses

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Division may limit the questioning of witnesses,
  including a claimant or a protected person, taking into account the
  nature and complexity of the issues and the relevance of the
  questions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral representations

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Representations must be made orally at the end of a
  hearing unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral decision and reasons

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A Division member must render an oral decision and
  reasons for the decision at the hearing unless it is not practicable
  to do so.

### [Documents Establishing Identity and Other Elements of the Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-785977 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The claimant must provide
acceptable documents establishing their identity and other elements of
the claim. A claimant who does not provide acceptable documents must
explain why they did not provide the documents and what steps they took
to obtain them.

### [Application to Vacate or to Cease Refugee Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-785980 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If an application to
vacate or to cease refugee protection is made, the protected person must
without delay notify the Division and the Minister in writing of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any change in their contact information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} their counsel's contact information and any
  limitations on the counsel's retainer, if represented by counsel, and
  any changes to that information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- counsel not representing
or advising for consideration

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a protected person
retains counsel who is not a person referred to in any of
paragraphs 91(2)(a) to (c) of the Act, both the protected person and
their counsel must without delay provide the information and
declarations set out in Schedule 3 to the Division in writing.

## [Counsel of Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785987 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Becoming counsel of record

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), as soon as
  counsel for a claimant or protected person agrees to a date for a
  proceeding, or as soon as a person becomes counsel after a date for a
  proceeding has been fixed, the counsel becomes counsel of record for
  the claimant or protected person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on counsel's retainer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant or protected person has notified the
  Division of a limitation on their counsel's retainer, counsel is
  counsel of record only to the extent of the services to be provided
  within the limited retainer. Counsel ceases to be counsel of record as
  soon as those services are completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to be removed as counsel of
record

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To be
  removed as counsel of record, counsel for a claimant or protected
  person must first provide to the person represented and to the
  Minister, if the Minister is a party, a copy of a written request to
  be removed and then provide the written request to the Division, no
  later than three working days before the date fixed for the next
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is not possible for counsel to make the request
  in accordance with subrule (1), counsel must appear on the date fixed
  for the proceeding and make the request to be removed orally before
  the time fixed for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Division's permission required

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Counsel remains counsel of record unless the request
  to be removed is granted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removing counsel of record

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  remove counsel as counsel of record, a claimant or protected person
  must first provide to counsel and to the Minister, if the Minister is
  a party, a copy of a written notice that counsel is no longer counsel
  for the claimant or protected person, as the case may be, and then
  provide the written notice to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ceasing to be counsel of record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Counsel ceases to be counsel of record as soon as the
  Division receives the notice.

## [Language of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786005 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Choice of language --- claim for refugee
protection

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  claimant must choose English or French as the language of the
  proceedings at the time of the referral of their claim for refugee
  protection to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claimant may change the language of the proceedings
  that they chose under subrule (1) by notifying the Division and the
  Minister in writing. The notice must be received by the Division and
  the Minister no later than 10 days before the date fixed for the next
  proceeding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Choice of language --- application to
vacate or cease refugee protection

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  language that is chosen under rule 17 is to be the language of the
  proceedings in any application made by the Minister to vacate or to
  cease refugee protection with respect to that claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A protected person may change the language of the
  proceedings by notifying the Division and the Minister in writing. The
  notice must be received by the Division and the Minister no later than
  10 days before the date fixed for the next proceeding.

## [Interpreters]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- claimant

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  claimant needs an interpreter for the proceedings, the claimant must
  notify an officer at the time of the referral of the claim to the
  Division and specify the language and dialect, if any, to be
  interpreted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing language of interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claimant may change the language and dialect, if
  any, that they specified under subrule (1), or if they had not
  indicated that an interpreter was needed, they may indicate that they
  need an interpreter, by notifying the Division in writing and
  indicating the language and dialect, if any, to be interpreted. The
  notice must be received by the Division no later than 10 days before
  the date fixed for the next proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- protected
  person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a protected person needs an interpreter for the
  proceedings, the protected person must notify the Division in writing
  and specify the language and dialect, if any, to be interpreted. The
  notice must be received by the Division no later than 10 days before
  the date fixed for the next proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Need for interpreter --- witness

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If any party's witness needs an interpreter for the
  proceedings, the party must notify the Division in writing and specify
  the language and dialect, if any, to be interpreted. The notice must
  be received by the Division no later than 10 days before the date
  fixed for the next proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's oath

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The interpreter must take an oath or make a solemn
  affirmation to interpret accurately.

## [Designated Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786028 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of counsel or officer to notify

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  counsel for a party or if an officer believes that the Division should
  designate a representative for the claimant or protected person
  because the claimant or protected person is under 18 years of age or
  is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings, counsel or the
  officer must without delay notify the Division in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subrule (1) does not apply in the case of a claimant
  under 18 years of age whose claim is joined with the claim of their
  parent or legal guardian if the parent or legal guardian is 18 years
  of age or older.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether counsel or the officer is aware of a person
    in Canada who meets the requirements to be designated as a
    representative and, if so, the person's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any available supporting documents; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why counsel or the officer believes
    that a representative should be designated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for being designated

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} To be designated as a representative, a person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be 18 years of age or older;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} understand the nature of the proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be willing and able to act in the best interests of
    the claimant or protected person; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} not have interests that conflict with those of the
    claimant or protected person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} When determining whether a claimant or protected
  person is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings, the
  Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the person can understand the reason for
    the proceeding and can instruct counsel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's statements and behaviour at the
    proceeding;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expert evidence, if any, on the person's
    intellectual or physical faculties, age or mental condition; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has had a representative
    designated for a proceeding in another division of the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation applies to all proceedings

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a representative for a person who
  is under 18 years of age or who is unable to appreciate the nature of
  the proceedings applies to all subsequent proceedings in the Division
  with respect to that person unless the Division orders otherwise.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of designation --- person reaches
  18 years of age

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The designation of a representative for a person who
  is under 18 years of age ends when the person reaches 18 years of age
  unless that representative has also been designated because the person
  is unable to appreciate the nature of the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of designation

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Division may terminate a designation if the
  Division is of the opinion that the representative is no longer
  required or suitable and may designate a new representative if
  required.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation criteria

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Before designating a person as a representative, the
  Division must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assess the person's ability to fulfil the
    responsibilities of a designated representative; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the person has been informed of the
    responsibilities of a designated representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of representative

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} The responsibilities of a designated representative
  include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deciding whether to retain counsel and, if counsel
    is retained, instructing counsel or assisting the represented person
    in instructing counsel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} making decisions regarding the claim or application
    or assisting the represented person in making those decisions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} informing the represented person about the various
    stages and procedures in the processing of their case;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting in gathering evidence to support the
    represented person's case and in providing evidence and, if
    necessary, being a witness at the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} protecting the interests of the represented person
    and putting forward the best possible case to the Division;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} informing and consulting the represented person to
    the extent possible when making decisions about the case; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} filing and perfecting an appeal to the Refugee
    Appeal Division, if required.

## [Disclosure of Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786070 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of information from another
claim

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), the Division
  may disclose to a claimant personal and other information that it
  wants to use from any other claim if the claims involve similar
  questions of fact or if the information is otherwise relevant to the
  determination of their claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to another claimant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the personal or other information of another
  claimant has not been made public, the Division must make reasonable
  efforts to notify the other claimant in writing that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it intends to disclose the information to a
    claimant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other claimant may object to that disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for disclosure

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In order to decide whether to object to the
  disclosure, the other claimant may make a written request to the
  Division for personal and other information relating to the claimant.
  Subject to subrule (5), the Division may disclose only information
  that is necessary to permit the other claimant to make an informed
  decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to claimant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the personal or other information of the claimant
  has not been made public, the Division must make reasonable efforts to
  notify the claimant in writing that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it intends to disclose the information to the other
    claimant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claimant may object to that disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information not to be disclosed

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not disclose personal or other
  information unless it is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is not a serious possibility that disclosing
    the information will endanger the life, liberty or security of any
    person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} disclosing the information is not likely to cause
    an injustice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information from joined claims

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Personal or other information from a joined claim is
  not subject to this rule. If claims were once joined but were later
  separated, only personal or other information that was provided before
  the separation is not subject to this rule.

## [Exclusion, Integrity Issues, Inadmissibility and Ineligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786116 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister of possible exclusion
before hearing

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Division believes, before a hearing begins, that there is a
  possibility that section E or F of Article 1 of the Refugee Convention
  applies to the claim, the Division must without delay notify the
  Minister in writing and provide any relevant information to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister of possible
  exclusion during hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Division believes, after a hearing begins,
  that there is a possibility that section E or F of Article 1 of the
  Refugee Convention applies to the claim and the Division is of the
  opinion that the Minister's participation may help in the full and
  proper hearing of the claim, the Division must adjourn the hearing and
  without delay notify the Minister in writing and provide any relevant
  information to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to claimant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the claimant a copy of
  any notice or information that the Division provides to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of hearing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division must fix a date for the resumption of
  the hearing that is as soon as practicable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister responds to the notice referred to
    in subrule (2), after receipt of the response from the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister does not respond to that notice, no
    earlier than 14 days after receipt of the notice by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister of possible integrity
issues before hearing

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Division believes, before a hearing begins, that there is a
  possibility that issues relating to the integrity of the Canadian
  refugee protection system may arise from the claim and the Division is
  of the opinion that the Minister's participation may help in the full
  and proper hearing of the claim, the Division must without delay
  notify the Minister in writing and provide any relevant information to
  the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister of possible
  integrity issues during hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Division believes, after a hearing begins,
  that there is a possibility that issues relating to the integrity of
  the Canadian refugee protection system may arise from the claim and
  the Division is of the opinion that the Minister's participation may
  help in the full and proper hearing of the claim, the Division must
  adjourn the hearing and without delay notify the Minister in writing
  and provide any relevant information to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Integrity issues

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this rule, claims in which the
  possibility that issues relating to the integrity of the Canadian
  refugee protection system may arise include those in which there is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} information that the claim may have been made under
    a false identity in whole or in part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a substantial change to the basis of the claim from
    that indicated in the Basis of Claim Form first provided to the
    Division;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information that, in support of the claim, the
    claimant submitted documents that may be fraudulent; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} other information that the claimant may be directly
    or indirectly misrepresenting or withholding material facts relating
    to a relevant matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to claimant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the claimant a copy of
  any notice or information that the Division provides to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resumption of hearing

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Division must fix a date for the resumption of
  the hearing that is as soon as practicable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister responds to the notice referred to
    in subrule (2), after receipt of the response from the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister does not respond to that notice, no
    earlier than 14 days after receipt of the notice by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of possible inadmissibility or
ineligibility

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must without delay notify the Minister in writing and provide
  the Minister with any relevant information if the Division believes
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claimant may be inadmissible on grounds of
    security, violating human or international rights, serious
    criminality or organized criminality;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is an outstanding charge against the claimant
    for an offence under an Act of Parliament that is punishable by a
    maximum term of imprisonment of at least 10 years; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the claimant's claim may be ineligible to be
    referred under section 101 or paragraph 104(1)(c) or (d) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to claimant

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the claimant a copy of
  any notice or information that the Division provides to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of proceeding

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, within 20 days after receipt of the notice
  referred to in subrule (1), the Minister does not notify the Division
  that the proceedings are suspended under paragraph 103(1)(a) or (b) of
  the Act or that the pending proceedings respecting the claim are
  terminated under section 104 of the Act, the Division may continue
  with the proceedings.

### [Providing a Document]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786215 .Subheading}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allowing application

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When document received by Division

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limit --- next
  working day

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the time limit for providing a document ends on
  a day that is not a working day, the time limit is extended to the

## [Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786265 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing witness information

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party wants to call a witness, the party must provide the following
  witness information in writing to the other party, if any, and to the
  Division:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the witness's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brief statement of the purpose and substance of
    the witness's testimony or, in the case of an expert witness, the
    expert witness's brief signed summary of the testimony to be given;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time needed for the witness's testimony;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the party's relationship to the witness;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an expert witness, a description of
    the expert witness's qualifications; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the party wants the witness to testify by
    means of live telecommunication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof witness information provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The witness information provided to the Division must
  be accompanied by a written statement indicating how and when it was
  provided to the other party, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 10 days before the date fixed for
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide witness information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a party does not provide the witness information,
  the witness must not testify at the hearing unless the Division allows
  them to testify.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to allow a witness to testify,
  the Division must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevance and probative value of the proposed
    testimony; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason why the witness information was not
    provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requesting summons

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants the Division to order a person to testify at a hearing
  must make a request to the Division for a summons, either orally at a
  proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to issue a summons, the Division
  must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the necessity of the testimony to a full and proper
    hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's ability to give that testimony; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has agreed to be summoned as a
    witness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using summons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party wants to use a summons, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the summons to the person by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the summons to the Division,
    together with a written statement indicating the name of the person
    who provided the summons and the date, time and place that it was
    provided by hand; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay or offer to pay the person the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of the [Federal
    Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancelling summons

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person who is summoned to appear as a witness wants the summons
  cancelled, the person must make an application in writing to the
  Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must make the application in accordance
  with rule 50, but is not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrest warrant

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person does not obey a summons to appear as a witness, the party who
  requested the summons may make a request to the Division orally at the
  hearing, or in writing, to issue a warrant for the person's arrest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written request for a warrant
  must provide supporting evidence by affidavit or statutory
  declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for issue of arrest
  warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not issue a warrant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was provided the summons by hand or the
    person is avoiding being provided the summons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid or offered the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of the [Federal
    Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not appear at the hearing as
    required by the summons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's testimony is still needed for a full
    and proper hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued by the Division for the arrest of a
  person must include directions concerning detention or release.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded witness

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** If the Division excludes
a witness from a hearing room, no person may communicate to the witness
any evidence given while the witness was excluded unless allowed to do
so by the Division or until the witness has finished testifying.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786318 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786319 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** Unless these Rules
provide otherwise,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants the Division to make a decision on
  any matter in a proceeding, including the procedure to be followed,
  must make an application to the Division in accordance with rule 50;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to respond to the application must
  respond in accordance with rule 51; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a party who wants to reply to a response must reply
  in accordance with rule 52.

### [How to Make an Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786325 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written application and time limit

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  these Rules provide otherwise, an application must be made in writing,
  without delay, and must be received by the Division no later than
  10 days before the date fixed for the next proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow a party to make an
  application orally at a proceeding unless the party, with reasonable
  effort, could not have made a written application before the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless these Rules provide otherwise, in a written
  application, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision the party wants the Division to
    make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is another party and the views of that
    party are known, state whether the other party agrees to the
    application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit or statutory declaration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Unless these Rules provide otherwise, any evidence
  that the party wants the Division to consider with a written
  application must be given in an affidavit or statutory declaration
  that accompanies the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application to other party
  and Division

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written application must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, if any, a copy of the
    application and a copy of any affidavit or statutory declaration;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original application and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with a
    written statement indicating how and when the party provided a copy
    to the other party, if any.

### [How to Respond to a Written Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786342 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding to written application

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response to a written application must be in writing and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision the party wants the Division to
    make; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in written response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any evidence that the party wants the Division to
  consider with the written response must be given in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration that accompanies the response. Unless the
  Division requires it, an affidavit or statutory declaration is not
  required if the party who made the application was not required to
  give evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration, together with
  the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who responds to a written application must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the response and a
    copy of any affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original response and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with a
    written statement indicating how and when the party provided a copy
    to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (3) must be received
  by their recipients no later than five days after the date on which
  the party receives the copy of the application.

### [How to Reply to a Written Response]{.HTitleText2} {#h-786356 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replying to written response

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reply to a written response must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any evidence that the party wants the Division to
  consider with the written reply must be given in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration that accompanies the reply. Unless the Division
  requires it, an affidavit or statutory declaration is not required if
  the party was not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration, together with the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing reply

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who replies to a written response must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the reply and a copy
    of any affidavit or statutory declaration; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original reply and the
    original of any affidavit or statutory declaration, together with a
    written statement indicating how and when the party provided a copy
    to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under subrule (3) must be received
  by their recipients no later than three days after the date on which
  the party receives the copy of the response.

## [Changing the Location of a Proceeding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786368 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to change location

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to change the location
  of a proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must make the application in accordance
  with rule 50, but is not required to give evidence in an affidavit or
  statutory declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 20 days before the date fixed for
  the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the party is residing in the location where
    the party wants the proceeding to be held;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would allow the
    proceeding to be full and proper;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would likely delay the
    proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the
    Division's operation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the parties;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location is necessary to
    accommodate a vulnerable person; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether a hearing may be conducted by a means of
    live telecommunication with the claimant or protected person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the proceeding at
  the location fixed and be ready to start or continue the proceeding.

## [Changing the Date or Time of a Proceeding]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application in writing

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), an
  application to change the date or time of a proceeding must be made in
  accordance with rule 50, but the party is not required to give
  evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made without delay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be received by the Division no later than three
    working days before the date fixed for the proceeding, unless the
    application is made for medical reasons or other emergencies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include at least three dates and times, which are
    no later than 10 working days after the date originally fixed for
    the proceeding, on which the party is available to start or continue
    the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If it is not possible for the party to make the
  application in accordance with paragraph (2)(b), the party must appear
  on the date fixed for the proceeding and make the application orally
  before the time fixed for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), the Division must not allow
  the application unless there are exceptional circumstances, such as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the change is required to accommodate a vulnerable
    person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an emergency or other development outside the
    party's control and the party has acted diligently.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counsel retained or availability of
  counsel provided after hearing date fixed

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, at the time the officer fixed the hearing date
  under subrule 3(1), a claimant did not have counsel or was unable to
  provide the dates when their counsel would be available to attend a
  hearing, the claimant may make an application to change the date or
  time of the hearing. Subject to operational limitations, the Division
  must allow the application if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claimant retains counsel no later than five
    working days after the day on which the hearing date was fixed by
    the officer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the counsel retained is not available on the date
    fixed for the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application is made in writing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application is made without delay and no later
    than five working days after the day on which the hearing date was
    fixed by the officer; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the claimant provides at least three dates and
    times when counsel is available, which are within the time limits
    set out in the Regulations for the hearing of the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for medical reasons

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant or protected person makes the
  application for medical reasons, other than those related to their
  counsel, they must provide, together with the application, a legible,
  recently dated medical certificate signed by a qualified medical
  practitioner whose name and address are printed or stamped on the
  certificate. A claimant or protected person who has provided a copy of
  the certificate to the Division must provide the original document to
  the Division without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The medical certificate must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the medical condition, without
    specifying the diagnosis, that prevent the claimant or protected
    person from participating in the proceeding on the date fixed for
    the proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the claimant or protected person
    is expected to be able to participate in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide medical certificate

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant or protected person fails to provide a
  medical certificate in accordance with subrules (6) and (7), they must
  include in their application

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} particulars of any efforts they made to obtain the
    required medical certificate, supported by corroborating evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the medical reasons for the
    application, supported by corroborating evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the medical condition
    prevents them from participating in the proceeding on the date fixed
    for the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the party made a previous application that was
  denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the denial and must
  not allow the subsequent application unless there are exceptional
  circumstances supported by new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the proceeding at
  the date and time fixed and be ready to start or continue the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New date

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} If an application for a change to the date or time
  of a proceeding is allowed, the new date fixed by the Division must be
  no later than 10 working days after the date originally fixed for the
  proceeding or as soon as possible after that date.

## [Joining or Separating Claims or Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786426 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claims automatically joined

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division must join the claim of a claimant to a claim made by the
  claimant's spouse or common-law partner, child, parent, legal
  guardian, brother, sister, grandchild or grandparent, unless it is not
  practicable to do so.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications joined if claims joined

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Applications to vacate or to cease refugee protection
  are joined if the claims of the protected persons were joined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to join

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to join claims or
  applications to vacate or to cease refugee protection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to separate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may make an application to the Division to
  separate claims or applications to vacate or to cease refugee
  protection that are joined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application and providing
  application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes an application to join or separate
  claims or applications to vacate or to cease refugee protection must
  do so in accordance with rule 50, but the party is not required to
  give evidence in an affidavit or statutory declaration. The party must
  also

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the application to any person who
    will be affected by the Division's decision on the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Division a written statement
    indicating how and when the copy of the application was provided to
    any affected person, together with proof that the party provided the
    copy to that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 20 days before the date fixed for
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application to join or separate, the
  Division must consider any relevant factors, including whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claims or applications to vacate or to cease
    refugee protection involve similar questions of fact or law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing the application to join or separate would
    promote the efficient administration of the Division's work; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} allowing the application to join or separate would
    likely cause an injustice.

## [Proceedings Conducted in Public]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786448 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister considered party

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of this rule, the Minister is considered to be a party
  whether or not the Minister takes part in the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes an application to the Division to
  have a proceeding conducted in public must do so in writing and in
  accordance with this rule rather than rule 50.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow a person to make an
  application orally at a proceeding unless the person, with reasonable
  effort, could not have made a written application before the
  proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision they want the Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state whether they want the Division to consider
    the application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should consider the
    application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if they want the Division to hear the application
    orally, give reasons why the Division should do so; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that they want the Division to
    consider in deciding the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person must provide the original application
  together with two copies to the Division. The Division must provide a
  copy of the application to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to application

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A party may respond to a written application. The
  response must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision they want the Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state whether they want the Division to consider
    the application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should consider the
    application in public or in the absence of the public;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if they want the Division to hear the application
    orally, give reasons why the Division should do so; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that they want the Division to
    consider in deciding the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide a copy of the response to the
  other party and provide the original response and a copy to the
  Division, together with a written statement indicating how and when
  the party provided the copy to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing response to applicant

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide to the applicant either a
  copy of the response or a summary of the response referred to in
  paragraph (12)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply to response

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or a party may reply in writing to a
  written response or a summary of a response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing reply

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} An applicant or a party who replies to a written
  response or a summary of a response must provide the original reply
  and two copies to the Division. The Division must provide a copy of
  the reply to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} An application made under this rule must be received
  by the Division without delay. The Division must specify the time
  limit within which a response or reply, if any, is to be provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(12)]{.lawlabel} The Division may take any measures it considers
  necessary to ensure the confidentiality of the proceeding in respect
  of the application, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing a summary of the response to the
    applicant instead of a copy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Division holds a hearing in respect of the
    application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} excluding the applicant or the applicant and
      their counsel from the hearing while the party responding to the
      application provides evidence and makes representations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} allowing the presence of the applicant's counsel
      at the hearing while the party responding to the application
      provides evidence and makes representations, upon receipt of a
      written undertaking by counsel not to disclose any evidence or
      information adduced until a decision is made to hold the hearing
      in public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summary of response

  [(13)]{.lawlabel} If the Division provides a summary of the response
  under paragraph (12)(a), or excludes the applicant and their counsel
  from a hearing in respect of the application under
  subparagraph (12)(b)(i), the Division must provide a summary of the
  representations and evidence, if any, that is sufficient to enable the
  applicant to reply, while ensuring the confidentiality of the
  proceeding having regard to the factors set out in paragraph 166(b) of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of decision on application

  [(14)]{.lawlabel} The Division must notify the applicant and the
  parties of its decision on the application and provide reasons for the
  decision.

## [Observers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786494 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application under rule 57 is not necessary if an observer is a member
  of the staff of the Board or a representative or agent of the United
  Nations High Commissioner for Refugees or if the claimant or protected

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must allow the attendance of an observer

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Observers --- confidentiality of

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may take any measures that it considers
  necessary to ensure the confidentiality of the proceeding despite the
  presence of an observer.

## [Withdrawal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786502 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abuse of process

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of subsection 168(2) of the Act, withdrawal of a claim or
  of an application to vacate or to cease refugee protection is an abuse
  of process if withdrawal would likely have a negative effect on the
  Division's integrity. If no substantive evidence has been accepted in
  the hearing, withdrawal is not an abuse of process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if no substantive evidence
  accepted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  hearing, a party may withdraw the party's claim or the application to
  vacate or to cease refugee protection by notifying the Division orally
  at a proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if substantive evidence
  accepted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  hearing, a party who wants to withdraw the party's claim or the
  application to vacate or to cease refugee protection must make an
  application to the Division in accordance with rule 50.

## [Reinstating a Withdrawn Claim or Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reinstate withdrawn claim

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make an application to the Division to reinstate a claim
  that was made by the person and was withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must make the application in accordance
  with rule 50, include in the application their contact information
  and, if represented by counsel, their counsel's contact information
  and any limitations on counsel's retainer, and provide a copy of the
  application to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice or it is otherwise in the interests of justice to
  allow the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including whether the application was
  made in a timely manner and the justification for any delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the person made a previous application to
  reinstate that was denied, the Division must consider the reasons for
  the denial and must not allow the subsequent application unless there
  are exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reinstate withdrawn
application to vacate or to cease refugee protection

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make an application to the Division to reinstate an
  application to vacate or to cease refugee protection that was
  withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must make the application in accordance
  with rule 50.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice or it is otherwise in the interests of justice to
  allow the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including whether the application was
  made in a timely manner and the justification for any delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister made a previous application to
  reinstate that was denied, the Division must consider the reasons for
  the denial and must not allow the subsequent application unless there
  are exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

## [Reopening a Claim or Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786533 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reopen claim

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before the Refugee Appeal Division or the Federal Court has made
  a final determination in respect of a claim for refugee protection
  that has been decided or declared abandoned, the claimant or the
  Minister may make an application to the Division to reopen the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in accordance with
  rule 50 and, for the purpose of paragraph 50(5)(a), the Minister is
  considered to be a party whether or not the Minister took part in the
  proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant makes the application, they must
  include in the application their contact information and, if
  represented by counsel, their counsel's contact information and any
  limitations on counsel's retainer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allegations against counsel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If it is alleged in the application that the
  claimant's counsel in the proceedings that are the subject of the
  application provided inadequate representation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claimant must first provide a copy of the
    application to the counsel and then provide the original application
    to the Division, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application provided to the Division must be
    accompanied by a written statement indicating how and when the copy
    of the application was provided to the counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of notice of appeal or pending
  application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a copy of any
  notice of pending appeal or any pending application for leave to apply
  for judicial review or any pending application for judicial review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factor

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and the justification for any delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a party who had the right of appeal to the
      Refugee Appeal Division did not appeal, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a party did not make an application for leave to
      apply for judicial review or an application for judicial review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the party made a previous application to reopen
  that was denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the denial
  and must not allow the subsequent application unless there are
  exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other remedies

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If there is a pending appeal to the Refugee Appeal
  Division or a pending application for leave to apply for judicial
  review or a pending application for judicial review on the same or
  similar grounds, the Division must, as soon as is practicable, allow
  the application to reopen if it is necessary for the timely and
  efficient processing of a claim, or dismiss the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to reopen application to
vacate or to cease refugee protection

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time before the Federal Court has made a final determination in
  respect of an application to vacate or to cease refugee protection
  that has been decided or declared abandoned, the Minister or the
  protected person may make an application to the Division to reopen the
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in accordance with
  rule 50.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a protected person makes the application, they
  must include in the application their contact information and, if
  represented by counsel, their counsel's contact information and any
  limitations on counsel's retainer, and they must provide a copy of the
  application to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allegations against counsel

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If it is alleged in the application that the
  protected person's counsel in the proceedings that are the subject of
  the application to reopen provided inadequate representation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protected person must first provide a copy of
    the application to the counsel and then provide the original
    application to the Division, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application provided to the Division must be
    accompanied by a written statement indicating how and when the copy
    of the application was provided to the counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of pending application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a copy of any
  pending application for leave to apply for judicial review or any
  pending application for judicial review in respect of the application
  to vacate or to cease refugee protection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factor

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Division must not allow the application unless it
  is established that there was a failure to observe a principle of
  natural justice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the application was made in a timely manner
    and the justification for any delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a party did not make an application for leave to
    apply for judicial review or an application for judicial review, the
    reasons why an application was not made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsequent application

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the party made a previous application to reopen
  that was denied, the Division must consider the reasons for the denial
  and must not allow the subsequent application unless there are
  exceptional circumstances supported by new evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other remedies

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If there is a pending application for leave to apply
  for judicial review or a pending application for judicial review on
  the same or similar grounds, the Division must, as soon as is
  practicable, allow the application to reopen if it is necessary for
  the timely and efficient processing of a claim, or dismiss the
  application.

## [Applications to Vacate or to Cease Refugee Protection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786582 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of application

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to vacate or to cease refugee protection made by the
  Minister must be in writing and made in accordance with this rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the Minister must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contact information of the protected person and
    of their counsel, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given by the Department
    of Citizenship and Immigration to the protected person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and file number of any Division decision
    with respect to the protected person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person whose application for
    protection was allowed abroad, the person's file number, a copy of
    the decision and the location of the office;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the decision that the Minister wants the Division
    to make; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the reasons why the Division should make that
    decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing application to protected
  person and Division

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the application to the protected person;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the original of the application to the registry
    office that provided the notice of decision in the claim or to a
    registry office specified by the Division, together with a written
    statement indicating how and when a copy was provided to the
    protected person.

## [Abandonment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786598 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to explain

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  determining whether a claim has been abandoned under subsection 168(1)
  of the Act, the Division must give the claimant an opportunity to
  explain why the claim should not be declared abandoned,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately, if the claimant is present at the
    proceeding and the Division considers that it is fair to do so; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by way of a special hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special hearing --- Basis of Claim Form

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The special hearing on the abandonment of the claim
  for the failure to provide a completed Basis of Claim Form in
  accordance with paragraph 7(5)(a) must be held no later than five
  working days after the day on which the completed Basis of Claim Form
  was due. At the special hearing, the claimant must provide their
  completed Basis of Claim Form, unless the form has already been
  provided to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special hearing --- failure to appear

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The special hearing on the abandonment of the claim
  for the failure to appear for the hearing of the claim must be held no
  later than five working days after the day originally fixed for the
  hearing of the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to consider

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Division must consider, in deciding if the claim
  should be declared abandoned, the explanation given by the claimant
  and any other relevant factors, including the fact that the claimant
  is ready to start or continue the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical reasons

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the claimant's explanation includes medical
  reasons, other than those related to their counsel, they must provide,
  together with the explanation, the original of a legible, recently
  dated medical certificate signed by a qualified medical practitioner
  whose name and address are printed or stamped on the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of certificate

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The medical certificate must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the medical condition, without
    specifying the diagnosis, that prevented the claimant from providing
    the completed Basis of Claim Form on the due date, appearing for the
    hearing of the claim, or otherwise pursuing their claim, as the case
    may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the claimant is expected to be
    able to pursue their claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to provide medical certificate

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a claimant fails to provide a medical certificate
  in accordance with subrules (5) and (6), the claimant must include in
  their explanation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} particulars of any efforts they made to obtain the
    required medical certificate, supported by corroborating evidence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} particulars of the medical reasons included in the
    explanation, supported by corroborating evidence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the medical condition
    prevented them from providing the completed Basis of Claim Form on
    the due date, appearing for the hearing of the claim or otherwise
    pursuing their claim, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Start or continue proceedings

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} If the Division decides not to declare the claim
  abandoned, other than under subrule (2), it must start or continue the
  proceedings on the day the decision is made or as soon as possible
  after that day.

## [Notice of Constitutional Question]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786623 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of constitutional question

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants to challenge the constitutional validity,
  applicability or operability of a legislative provision must complete
  a notice of constitutional question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must complete the notice as set out in
  Form 69 of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) or
  any other form that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the party's name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Division file number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific legislative provision that is being
    challenged;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the material facts relied on to support the
    constitutional challenge; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the legal argument to be made in
    support of the constitutional challenge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the Attorney General of
    Canada and to the attorney general of each province of Canada, in
    accordance with section 57 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the other party, if any;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the original notice to the Division, together with
    a written statement indicating how and when the copies of the notice
    were provided under paragraphs (a) to (c), and proof that they were
    provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 10 days before the day on which the
  constitutional argument is made.

## [Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786643 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision and reasons

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the Division makes a decision, other than an interlocutory decision,
  it must provide in writing a notice of decision to the claimant or the
  protected person, as the case may be, and to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written reasons

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must provide written reasons for the
  decision together with the notice of decision

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if written reasons must be provided under
    paragraph 169(1)(d) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister was not present when the Division
    rendered an oral decision and reasons allowing a claim for refugee
    protection; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when the Division makes a decision on an
    application to vacate or to cease refugee protection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for written reasons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request under paragraph 169(1)(e) of the Act for
  written reasons for a decision must be made in writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision of single member takes
effect

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  decision made by a single Division member allowing or rejecting a
  claim for refugee protection, on an application to vacate or to cease
  refugee protection, on the abandonment of a claim or of an application
  to vacate or to cease refugee protection, or allowing an application
  to withdraw a claim or to withdraw an application to vacate or to
  cease refugee protection takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if given orally at a hearing, when the member
    states the decision and gives the reasons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if made in writing, when the member signs and dates
    the reasons for the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision of three member panel
  takes effect

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A decision made by a panel of three Division members
  allowing or rejecting a claim for refugee protection, on an
  application to vacate or to cease refugee protection, on the
  abandonment of a claim or of an application to vacate or to cease
  refugee protection, or allowing an application to withdraw a claim or
  to withdraw an application to vacate or to cease refugee protection
  takes effect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if given orally at a hearing, when all the members
    state their decision and give their reasons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if made in writing, when all the members sign and
    date their reasons for the decision.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-786663 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No applicable rule

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of a
provision in these Rules dealing with a matter raised during the
proceedings, the Division may do whatever is necessary to deal with the
matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Division

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** The Division may, after
giving the parties notice and an opportunity to object,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} act on its own initiative, without a party having to
  make an application or request to the Division;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a requirement of a rule;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} excuse a person from a requirement of a rule; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} extend a time limit, before or after the time limit
  has expired, or shorten it if the time limit has not expired.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to follow rule

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** Unless proceedings are
declared invalid by the Division, a failure to follow any requirement of
these Rules does not make the proceedings invalid.
