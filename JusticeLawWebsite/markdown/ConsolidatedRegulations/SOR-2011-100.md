## [Prescribed Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771395 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 385.131 and 385.241 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) and sections 434.1 and 442.1 of the [Trust and
Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the following products are
prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deposit type instruments as defined in the [Deposit
  Type Instruments Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-98); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} principal protected notes as defined in the
  [Principal Protected Notes
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-180).
