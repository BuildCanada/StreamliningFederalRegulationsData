## Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-national-revenue-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-8
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-01-27

Minister of National Revenue Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1992-2597  1992-12-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-8_e_hq_6510){#fn_SI-93-8_e_hq_6510-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of National Revenue to prescribe, by order,
the fees or charges to be paid by persons who request that publications
of the Department of National Revenue be distributed to them.
