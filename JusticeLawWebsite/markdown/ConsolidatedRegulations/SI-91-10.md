## Passover Products Remission Order {#passover-products-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-10
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-01-30

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed under Division III of
Part IX and under any other Part of the Excise Tax Act, Payable on
Passover Foods and Products of a Class not Available in Canada

P.C. 1990-2849  1990-12-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, on the recommendation of the Minister of
National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
revoke Order in Council P.C. 1966-19/2220 of December 1, 1966 and to
make the annexed Order respecting the remission of customs duties
imposed under the Customs Tariff and taxes imposed under Division III of
Part IX and under any other part of the Excise Tax Act, payable on
Passover foods and products of a class not available in Canada, in
substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643260 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Passover Products Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-91-10).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643262 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of customs taxes imposed under Division III
of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), payable on Passover foods and products of a class
not available in Canada, that are for use during the Passover holiday
and that are imported during the period beginning two months before the
eve of the first day of that holiday and ending on the last day of that
holiday.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/95-25, s. 1
- SI/98-17, s. 2
:::

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SI/98-17,
s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643267 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
section 2 on condition that a claim for remission is made to the
Minister of National Revenue within two years after the final accounting
for the foods and products under section 32 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
