## Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals Regulations {#storage-display-transportation-and-handling-of-firearms-by-individuals-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-209
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals
Regulations

P.C. 1998-484 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Storage, Display, Transportation and
Handling of Firearms by Individuals
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996 and
October 30, 1997, which dates are at least 30 sitting days before the
date of this Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs
117(h), (i) and (o) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby
makes the annexed [Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of
Firearms by Individuals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019904 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[muzzle-loading firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[muzzle-loading firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a
    handgun. ([arme à feu à chargement par la bouche]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that
    is neither a prohibited firearm nor a restricted firearm. ([arme à
    feu sans restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10).
    ([poster]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handgun under
    paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
    firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([arme de poing prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[secure locking device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secure locking device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that can only be opened or released by the use of
      an electronic, magnetic or mechanical key or by setting the device
      in accordance with an alphabetical or numerical combination; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that, when applied to a firearm, prevents the
      firearm from being discharged. ([dispositif de verrouillage
      sécuritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transmit by post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transmit by post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10). ([transmission postale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[unattended]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unattended]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vehicle, means
    that the vehicle is not under the direct and immediate supervision
    of a person who is 18 years of age or older or to whom a licence has
    been issued under the Act. ([non surveillé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[unloaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unloaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a firearm, means
    that any propellant, projectile or cartridge that can be discharged
    from the firearm is not contained in the breech or firing chamber of
    the firearm nor in the cartridge magazine attached to or inserted
    into the firearm. ([non chargée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any conveyance that is used
    for transportation by water, land or air.
    ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-277, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019920 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply to members of any of the following classes of
  persons who are acting in the course of their duties or for the
  purposes of their employment:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} peace officers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} members of the Canadian Forces or of the armed
    forces of a state other than Canada who are attached or seconded to
    any of the Canadian Forces;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons training to become police officers or peace
    officers under the control and supervision of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a police force, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a police academy or similar institution
      designated by the Attorney General of Canada or the lieutenant
      governor in council of a province;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} members of a visiting force, within the meaning of
    section 2 of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2), who are
    authorized under paragraph 14(a) of that Act to possess and carry
    explosives, ammunition and firearms;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} persons or members of a class of persons employed
    in the public service of Canada or by the government of a province
    or municipality who are prescribed by the regulations made by the
    Governor in Council under Part III of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) to be public officers; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} chief firearms officers or firearms officers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to the transportation
  or handling of a restricted firearm or a prohibited handgun by an
  individual in accordance with an authorization issued under section 20
  of the Act, including any conditions attached to the authorization,
  who is carrying it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to protect the life of that individual or of other
    individuals; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for use in connection with his or her lawful
    profession or occupation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-277, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 10 and 14 do not apply to firearms that are
  used or handled by an individual in the course of hunting from a
  vehicle in a location in circumstances in which it is lawful for that
  individual to hunt from that vehicle in that location.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5 to 13 do not apply to antique firearms.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-277, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to the storage and transportation of a non-restricted firearm,
restricted firearm or prohibited handgun in the course of transmission
by post within Canada from the time the firearm or handgun is posted to
the time it is delivered to the addressee, within the meaning of
subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10), or
returned to the sender.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-277, s. 3
:::

s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Storage of Non-Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019943 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual may store a non-restricted firearm only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
      device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by the removal of the bolt
      or bolt-carrier, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} stored in a container, receptacle or room that
      is kept securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot
      readily be broken open or into; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is not readily accessible to ammunition, unless
    the ammunition is stored, together with or separately from the
    firearm, in a container or receptacle that is kept securely locked
    and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken open or
    into.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to any individual who
  stores a non-restricted firearm temporarily if the individual
  reasonably requires it for the control of predators or other animals
  in a place where it may be discharged in accordance with all
  applicable Acts of Parliament and of the legislature of a province,
  regulations made under such Acts, and municipal by-laws.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) do not apply to an
  individual who stores a non-restricted firearm in a location that is
  in a remote wilderness area that is not subject to any visible or
  otherwise reasonably ascertainable use incompatible with hunting.

## [Storage of Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019954 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An individual may store a
restricted firearm only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device and stored in a container, receptacle or room that is kept
    securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be
    broken open or into, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} stored in a vault, safe or room that has been
    specifically constructed or modified for the secure storage of
    restricted firearms and that is kept securely locked; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is not readily accessible to ammunition, unless
  the ammunition is stored, together with or separately from the
  firearm, in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a container or receptacle that is kept securely
    locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken
    open or into, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a vault, safe or room that has been specifically
    constructed or modified for the secure storage of restricted
    firearms and that is kept securely locked.

## [Storage of Prohibited Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019963 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An individual may store a
prohibited firearm only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device and stored in a container, receptacle or room that is kept
    securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be
    broken open or into, and, if the prohibited firearm is an automatic
    firearm that has a removable bolt or bolt-carrier, the bolt or
    bolt-carrier is removed and stored in a room that is different from
    the room in which the automatic firearm is stored, that is kept
    securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be
    broken open or into, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} stored in a vault, safe or room that has been
    specifically constructed or modified for the secure storage of
    prohibited firearms and that is kept securely locked; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is not readily accessible to ammunition, unless
  the ammunition is stored, together with or separately from the
  firearm, in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a container or receptacle that is kept securely
    locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken
    open or into, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a vault, safe or room that has been specifically
    constructed or modified for the secure storage of prohibited
    firearms and that is kept securely locked.

## [Display of Non-Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019972 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An individual may display a
non-restricted firearm only if it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unloaded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
  device or is in a container, receptacle or room that is kept securely
  locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken
  open or into; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not displayed with and is not readily accessible
  to ammunition that can be discharged from it.

## [Display of Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019977 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual may display a restricted firearm or a prohibited firearm in
  a dwelling-house only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the restricted firearm or prohibited firearm is
    unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the restricted firearm or prohibited firearm is
    rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the restricted firearm or prohibited firearm is
    securely attached to a non-portable structure in such a manner that
    it cannot readily be removed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the restricted firearm or prohibited firearm is not
    displayed with and is not readily accessible to ammunition that can
    be discharged from it; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an automatic firearm that has a
    removable bolt or bolt-carrier, the bolt or bolt-carrier is removed
    and stored in a room that is different from the room in which the
    automatic firearm is displayed, that is kept securely locked and
    that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken open or
    into.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may display a restricted firearm in a
  place other than a dwelling-house only if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is, subject to subsection (4), securely attached to
    a structure on which it is displayed by a chain, metal cable or
    similar device in such a manner that the restricted firearm cannot
    readily be removed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not displayed with and is not readily accessible
    to ammunition that can be discharged from it, unless the ammunition
    is displayed in a container or receptacle that is kept securely
    locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken
    open or into.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual may display a prohibited firearm in a
  place other than a dwelling-house only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited firearm is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited firearm is rendered inoperable by
    means of a secure locking device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited firearm is, subject to subsection
    (4), securely attached to a structure on which it is displayed by a
    chain, metal cable or similar device in such a manner that the
    prohibited firearm cannot readily be removed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited firearm is not displayed with and is
    not readily accessible to ammunition that can be discharged from it,
    unless the ammunition is displayed in a container or receptacle that
    is kept securely locked and that is constructed so that it cannot
    readily be broken open or into; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an automatic firearm that has a
    removable bolt or bolt-carrier, the bolt or bolt-carrier is removed
    and stored in a room that is different from the room in which the
    automatic firearm is displayed, that is kept securely locked and
    that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken open or
    into.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (2)(c) and (3)(c) do not apply if the
  firearm is detached from the structure so that the firearm may be
  handled by a person under the direct and immediate supervision of the
  individual displaying it.

## [Transportation of Non-Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019997 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual may transport a non-restricted firearm only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of a muzzle-loading firearm that
    is being transported between hunting sites, it is unloaded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a muzzle-loading firearm that is
    being transported between hunting sites, its firing cap or flint is
    removed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), an individual may
  transport a non-restricted firearm in an unattended vehicle only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the non-restricted
    firearm is in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment
    is securely locked; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the non-restricted
    firearm is not visible from outside the vehicle and the vehicle, or
    the part that contains the non-restricted firearm, is securely
    locked.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, in a remote wilderness area that is not subject
  to any visible or otherwise reasonably ascertainable use incompatible
  with hunting, an individual is transporting a non-restricted firearm
  in an unattended vehicle that is not equipped with a trunk or similar
  compartment that can be securely locked, and the vehicle or the part
  of it that contains the non-restricted firearm cannot be securely
  locked, the individual shall ensure that the non-restricted firearm

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not visible; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is rendered inoperable by a secure locking device,
    unless the individual reasonably requires the non-restricted firearm
    for the control of predators.

## [Transportation of Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020008 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An individual may
transport a restricted firearm only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is rendered inoperable by means of a secure
  locking device;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is in a locked container that is made of an opaque
  material and is of such strength, construction and nature that it
  cannot readily be broken open or into or accidentally opened during
  transportation; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is in a container described in paragraph (c)
  that is in an unattended vehicle,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the container is in
    that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is securely
    locked, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or the
    part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely locked
    and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle.

## [Transportation of Prohibited Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020016 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An individual may
transport a prohibited firearm only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is rendered inoperable by means of a secure
  locking device;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is an automatic firearm that has a bolt or
  bolt-carrier that is removable with reasonable facility, the bolt or
  bolt-carrier is removed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is in a locked container that is made of an opaque
  material and is of such strength, construction and nature that it
  cannot readily be broken open or into or accidentally opened during
  transportation; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} when it is in a container described in paragraph (d)
  that is in an unattended vehicle,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or similar
    compartment that can be securely locked, the container is in that
    trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is securely
    locked, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or the
    part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely locked
    and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle.

## [Transportation of Replica Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020025 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An individual may
transport a replica firearm only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle in which it is being transported is
  equipped with a trunk or similar compartment that can be securely
  locked, the replica firearm is in that trunk or compartment and the
  trunk or compartment is securely locked; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle in which it is being transported is
  not equipped with a trunk or similar compartment that can be securely
  locked, the replica firearm is not visible from outside the vehicle
  and the vehicle, or the part of the vehicle that contains the replica
  firearm, is securely locked.

## [Antique Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020029 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual may store, display or transport an antique firearm only if
  it is unloaded.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may transport an antique firearm in an
  unattended vehicle only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the antique firearm
    is in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is
    securely locked; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
    similar compartment that can be securely locked, the antique firearm
    is not visible from outside the vehicle and the vehicle, or the part
    that contains the antique firearm, is securely locked.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An individual may transport an antique firearm that
  is a handgun only if it is in a locked container that is made of an
  opaque material and is of such strength, construction and nature that
  it cannot readily be broken open or into or accidentally opened during
  transportation.

## [Handling of Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020036 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An individual may load a
firearm or handle a loaded firearm only in a place where the firearm may
be discharged in accordance with all applicable Acts of Parliament and
of the legislature of a province, regulations made under such Acts, and
municipal by-laws.

## [Shipping by Post]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020038 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An individual may ship a
firearm by posting it only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is a non-restricted firearm, restricted
  firearm or prohibited handgun;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the destination is within Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is posted using the most secure means of
  transmission by post that is offered by the Canada Post Corporation
  that includes the requirement to obtain a signature on delivery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-277, s. 4
:::

## [Offence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020045 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 117(o) of the Act, it is an offence to contravene section 13.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020047 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on December 1, 1998.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-471, s. 19.
