## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765646 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Exemption Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765648 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 1047 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the
exemption does not prejudice any of the shareholders or policyholders
entitled to vote or the public interest.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765650 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on June 1, 2011.
