[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006240 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who grows, mines,
    harvests, fishes, traps, hunts, manufactures, processes or assembles
    a good. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Certification Criteria for a Non-Producing Exporter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006244 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 97.1(1) of the Act, where the exporter of goods to a free
trade partner, for which preferential tariff treatment under a free
trade agreement will be claimed in accordance with the laws of that free
trade partner, is not the producer of the goods, the certificate shall
be completed and signed by the exporter on the basis of the following
criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's knowledge that the goods meet the
  applicable rules of origin;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's reasonable reliance on the written
  representation of the producer that the goods meet the applicable
  rules of origin; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported or to be exported from
  Canada to a NAFTA country, Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Jordan,
  Panama or Honduras, a certificate --- completed and signed by the
  producer and provided voluntarily to the exporter --- stating that the
  goods meet the applicable rules of origin.

## [Language of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006251 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The certificate referred to
in section 97.1 of the Act shall be completed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported or to be exported from
  Canada to the United States or to an EFTA state, in English or French;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported or to be exported from
  Canada to Mexico, Chile, Costa Rica, Peru, Colombia, Panama or
  Honduras in English, French or Spanish;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported or to be exported from
  Canada to Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary, in English, French,
  Hebrew or Arabic; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods exported or to be exported from
  Canada to Jordan, in English, French or Arabic.
