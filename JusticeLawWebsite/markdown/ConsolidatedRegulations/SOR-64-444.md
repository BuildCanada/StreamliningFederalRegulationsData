## Canadian Forces Compulsory Early Superannuation Regulations {#canadian-forces-compulsory-early-superannuation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/64-444
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 7, 1964

Registration 1964-10-29

Canadian Forces Compulsory Early Superannuation Regulations

P.C. 1964-1669 1964-10-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to Vote 56a of the
Appropriation Act No. 7, 1964, is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Canadian Forces Compulsory Early Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-64-444).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Forces Compulsory Early Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-64-444).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations all
words and expressions have the same meaning as in the [Canadian Forces
Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A contributor who, having
served in the forces for ten or more years, is compulsorily retired from
the forces before reaching retirement age during the period commencing
on the 7th day of May, 1964 and ending two years thereafter due to a
reduction in the total number of members of the forces shall, in the
event that the Service Pension Board determines that the said retirement

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was, in fact, by reason of a reduction in the total
  number of members of the forces arising out of reductions in
  establishments of the regular forces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} was not by reason of inefficiency in the performance
  of duties, misconduct or pursuant to a request made by the
  contributor,

be deemed for the purpose of subsection (3) of section 10 of the
[Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) to have served in
the forces for twenty or more years if, except for these Regulations,
the contributor would upon his retirement be entitled to a reduced
annuity pursuant to clause (A) of subparagraph (ii) of paragraph (c) of
subsection (3) of section 10 of that Act.
