## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838111 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 141(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-3-1148-1-E_hq_16565){#fn_81000-3-1148-1-E_hq_16565-ID0EECA}
of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_81000-3-1148-1-E_hq_16566){#fn_81000-3-1148-1-E_hq_16566-ID0EBCA},
the exploration and drilling for and the production, conservation and
processing of petroleum in that portion of the offshore area described
in Schedule IV to that Act, and the transportation of petroleum produced
in that portion of the offshore area are prohibited for a period
beginning on the date on which this Joint Notice comes into force and
ending on December 31, 2022.
