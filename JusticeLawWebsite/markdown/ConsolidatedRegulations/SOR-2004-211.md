## Potato Wart Compensation Regulations, 2003 {#potato-wart-compensation-regulations-2003 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-211
:::

[PLANT PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-14.8)

Registration 2004-09-30

Potato Wart Compensation Regulations, 2003

P.C. 2004-1092 2004-09-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to paragraph 47(q)
of the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the
annexed [Potato Wart Compensation Regulations,
2003](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-211).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712288 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[potato wart]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the potato disease caused by
the presence of the soil-borne fungal pathogen, [Synchytrium
endobioticum]{.otherLang lang="la"}.

## [Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712290 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), if a notice has been issued by the Canadian Food
  Inspection Agency after December 31, 2001, for any treatment,
  prohibition, restriction or disposition referred to in the [Plant
  Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) or the [Plant Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-212) as a result of the presence
  of potato wart, compensation shall be paid to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has suffered a loss as a result of the treatment,
    prohibition, restriction or disposition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has reported taxable business or farm income in the
    year immediately preceding the year or during the year in which the
    notice was issued or the year in which the loss occurred;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has taken all reasonable measures to mitigate the
    loss, including,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} selling crops, other than potatoes, that have
      been grown at a place that is subject to a restriction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} selling potatoes infested or suspected of being
      infested with potato wart for purposes other than the purpose for
      which they were grown,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} applying for compensation to another department
      or organization, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} applying for compensation under an insurance or
      service contract; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} submits an application for compensation to the
    Minister within three years after the date of issuance of the
    notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may amend an application for compensation
  at any time before the expiry of the period referred to in paragraph
  (1)(d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may submit an application after the expiry
  of the period referred to in paragraph (1)(d) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there were exceptional circumstances beyond the
    person's control that prevented the person from submitting an
    application during that period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is submitted within 14 days after
    the day on which those circumstances cease to exist.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No compensation shall be awarded under these
  Regulations for a loss referred to in paragraph (1)(a) for which
  compensation has already been awarded under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Potato Wart Compensation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-451);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other compensation legislation or measure,
    including regulations made under the [Plant Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8) for the purposes of compensating for losses
    resulting from the presence of potato wart; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other compensation program including, but not
    limited to, an insurance contract.

## [Amount of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712310 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The compensation to be paid
to a person for a loss referred to in paragraph 2(1)(a) shall be
equivalent to the direct costs that were paid by the person in respect
of a loss suffered as a result of a measure described in column 1 of the
table to this section, but shall not exceed the amount set out in column
2.

## [Application for Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712318 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for
compensation must be on a form provided by the Minister and signed by
the applicant and must include the following information and documents,
together with any other information and documents that are necessary for
the Minister to determine whether the application meets the requirements
of these Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address and telephone number
  and, if applicable, the applicant's facsimile number and e-mail
  address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a legal description of the place in respect of which
  the applicant is making the application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of every notice issued in respect of the place
  referred to in paragraph (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant is a sole proprietorship,
  corporation, partnership, cooperative, association or organization and
  the names, addresses and telephone numbers and, if applicable, the
  facsimile numbers and e-mail addresses of its owners or directors, as
  the case may be;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence to substantiate the amount of the loss,
  including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's costs of obtaining a substitute
    place for the production of potatoes and a copy of every agreement,
    if any, with respect to the substitute place that was in effect
    during the time that potato production on the original place was
    restricted,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the direct costs paid by the applicant for the
    disposition of potatoes or potato by-products,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an itemized list of the equipment and places,
    including the potato processing and storage facilities, in respect
    of which treatment was required,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of times that the treatment referred to
    in subparagraph (iii) occurred, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the direct costs paid by the applicant for the
    treatment referred to in subparagraph (iii); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the applicant has made every reasonable
  effort to mitigate the loss.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712331 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
