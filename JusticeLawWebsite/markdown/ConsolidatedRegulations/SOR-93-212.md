## Integrated Circuit Topography Regulations {#integrated-circuit-topography-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-212
:::

[INTEGRATED CIRCUIT TOPOGRAPHY ACT](/eng/acts/I-14.6)

Registration 1993-04-27

Regulations Respecting the Protection of Integrated Circuit Topographies

P.C. 1993-831  1993-04-27
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to sections 16, 18, 19, 21 and 27 of the [Integrated Circuit
Topography Act](/eng/acts/I-14.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1208_hq_8211){#fn_1208_hq_8211-ID0EDBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the
protection of integrated circuit
topographies](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-212), effective May 1, 1993.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1208_hq_8211-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1208_hq_8211}S.C.
  1990, c. 37
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950867 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Integrated Circuit Topography
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-212).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950869 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Integrated Circuit Topography
    Act](/eng/acts/I-14.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or firm appointed by an
    applicant pursuant to section 10; ([mandataire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the creator of a topography
    or, where the topography has been transferred, the successor in
    title thereto who applies for registration of a topography pursuant
    to section 16 of the Act; ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application for
    registration of a topography made pursuant to section 16 of the Act;
    ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drawing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drawing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a plot;
    ([dessin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Office of the Registrar of
    Topographies; ([Bureau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representative for service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative for service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or
    firm in Canada appointed by an applicant or the owner of a
    registered topography pursuant to section 11. ([représentant aux
    fins de signification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Communications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950878 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence intended for the
  Registrar or the Office shall be addressed to the "Registrar of
  Topographies".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Registrar may be
  physically delivered to the Office during ordinary business hours of
  the Office and is considered to be received by the Registrar on the
  day of the delivery.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), if the
  correspondence is physically delivered to the Office outside of its
  ordinary business hours, it is considered to have been delivered to
  the Office during ordinary business hours on the day when the Office
  is next open for business.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Registrar may be
  physically delivered to an establishment that is designated by the
  Registrar in the Canadian Patent Office Record as an establishment to
  which correspondence addressed to the Registrar may be delivered,
  during ordinary business hours of that establishment, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the delivery is made to the establishment on a
    day that the Office is open for business, the correspondence is
    considered to be received by the Registrar on that day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the delivery is made to the establishment on a
    day that the Office is closed for business, the correspondence is
    considered to be received by the Registrar on the day when the
    Office is next open for business.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (4), if the
  correspondence is physically delivered to an establishment outside of
  ordinary business hours of the establishment, it is considered to have
  been delivered to that establishment during ordinary business hours on
  the day when the establishment is next open for business.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence addressed to the Registrar may be sent
  at any time by electronic or other means of transmission specified by
  the Registrar in the Canadian Patent Office Record.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (6), if, according to
  the local time of the place where the Office is located, the
  correspondence is delivered on a day when the Office is open for
  business, it is considered to be received by the Registrar on that
  day.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (6), if, according to
  the local time of the place where the Office is located, the
  correspondence is delivered on a day when the Office is closed for
  business, it is considered to be received by the Registrar on the day
  when the Office is next open for business.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), all communications relating to an application or a
  registered topography shall be made in writing or by electronic
  transmission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, where the circumstances require,
  have regard to an oral communication made in relation to an
  application or a registered topography.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), all correspondence addressed to the Registrar shall
  deal with only one application or registered topography.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  correspondence relating to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transfer of an interest or grant of a licence
    affecting a registered topography, referred to in subsection 21(1)
    of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change in the name or address of an owner of more
    than one registered topography;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change in the name or address of an applicant for
    the registration of more than one topography;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a change in the name or address of the
    representative for service of an owner of more than one registered
    topography; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change in the name or address of the
    representative for service or the agent of an applicant for the
    registration of more than one topography.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every person who is required by the Act or these
  Regulations to furnish an address shall furnish a complete postal
  address, including a street name and number where applicable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person referred to in subsection (1) may furnish,
  in addition to the required postal address, another address to which
  correspondence may be mailed.

<!-- -->

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence relating to an
  application shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application number, if one has been assigned;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicant; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the title or titles of the topography.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence relating to a registered topography
  shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the topography;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the owner of the topography; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the title or titles of the topography.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), correspondence relating to an application shall be
  conducted with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant, where there is only one applicant;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there is more than one applicant,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant authorized by the other applicant
      or applicants to act on their behalf, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the first applicant named in the application,
      where no applicant has been authorized in accordance with
      subparagraph (i).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Correspondence relating to an application shall be
  conducted with an agent where the agent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has signed the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has transmitted the application to the Office; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has notified the Office of the agent's appointment.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No regard shall be had to
any correspondence relating to an application that is received from any
person or firm other than the person or firm with whom correspondence on
the subject of the application is being conducted.

## [Appointment of Agent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950926 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant may appoint a person or firm as an agent to act on behalf of
  the applicant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the appointment of an
  agent need not be made in writing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may, where the circumstances require,
  request that an agent file a written appointment within a period that
  the Registrar deems appropriate in the circumstances.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an agent fails to file a written appointment as
  requested pursuant to subsection (3), the Registrar shall give notice
  to the agent that any further correspondence will be conducted with
  the applicant until a written appointment is filed.

## [Appointment of Representative for Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950932 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant or the owner of a registered topography may appoint a person
  or firm in Canada as a representative for service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice sent to or a proceeding served on a
  representative for service has the same effect as if the notice were
  sent to or the proceeding were served on the applicant or the owner of
  the registered topography, as the case may be.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950936 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application and any amendment thereto shall be in one of the official
  languages and shall bear the signature of the applicant or the
  applicant's agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A separate application shall be made for each
  topography.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An application shall
contain, in addition to the information and material required by
paragraphs 16(2)(a) to (d) of the Act, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the applicant has no office or place of
  business in Canada, the name and address of a representative for
  service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where an agent has been appointed, the name and
  address of the agent;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the material filed, including, where
  the topography consists of layers and contains confidential
  information, the number of layers and the number of layers in relation
  to which section 15 or 16 has been relied on; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature or function of the
  topography.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to these Regulations, an
  application shall contain, in addition to the information and material
  required by paragraphs 16(2)(a) to (d) of the Act, a complete set of
  overlay sheets, drawings or photographs of the topography.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The material referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  sufficiently magnified so that the design of the topography is clearly
  visible to the naked eye.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the material referred to in subsection (1)
  consists of more than one sheet, drawing or photograph, the sheets,
  drawings or photographs shall be numbered consecutively.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where a topography that
consists of more than two layers contains confidential information, an
application may contain, instead of a complete set of overlay sheets,
drawings or photographs as required by section 14, a set that contains
the same total number of sheets, drawings or photographs, but that
includes a selected number of sheets, drawings or photographs on which
is blocked out up to 50 per cent of the total area covered by those
sheets, drawings or photographs, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the selected sheets, drawings or photographs are
  clearly indicated in the application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of sheets, drawings or photographs
  selected does not exceed 50 per cent of the total number of sheets,
  drawings or photographs, said total number having been reduced by one
  where it is an odd number; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application contains topography design data in
  printed form for the areas that are blocked out; however, up to 50 per
  cent of these data may be blocked out, if the application contains
  four or more integrated circuit products incorporating the topography.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Where a topography
contains confidential information and has not been commercially
exploited at the filing date of an application, the application may
contain, instead of the material referred to in section 14,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the topography design data in printed form, of which
  up to 50 per cent may be blocked out; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a composite drawing or photograph of the topography,
  on which up to 50 per cent of each layer of the topography is blocked
  out.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Any topography design
data in printed form or integrated circuit products shall be filed at
the time of or subsequent to the filing of the other material referred
to in section 14, 15 or 16 and on or before the date of registration of
the topography.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** All material filed
pursuant to section 14, 15, 16 or 17 shall be identified by a title
consisting of letters of the Roman alphabet, Arabic numerals or a
combination thereof.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** All material filed
pursuant to section 14, 15 or 16 and all topography design data in
printed form referred to in section 17 shall be in a storable size,
either folded or otherwise, and shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than 21.5 cm x 28 cm (8½ inches x 11
  inches); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in A4 format.

## [Amendment of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950961 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  applicant may, at any time prior to the registration of a topography,
  on submitting any necessary information and material and on payment of
  the applicable fee set out in the schedule, request that the Registrar
  amend the applicant's application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall not make any amendment to an
  application that would substantially alter the topography to which the
  application relates.

## [Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950965 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
information required by the Act to be entered in the register, the
Registrar shall enter in the register

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all information filed pursuant to section 13; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all material filed pursuant to section 14, 15, 16 or
  17.

## [Amendment of Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950969 .Part}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 21(2)(c) of the Act, the Registrar may amend the register to
reflect any change in the name or address of a representative for
service.

## [Certificate of Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950971 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A certificate of
registration issued in respect of a topography shall include, in
addition to the particulars required by subsection 19(2) of the Act, the
following particulars:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the registered owner of the
  topography;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title of the topography;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature or function of the
  topography;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the topography has been commercially exploited,
  the date on which and place at which the topography was first
  commercially exploited;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of registration of the topography; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the topography.

## [Transfer of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950979 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A person to whom an
application or an interest affecting a registered topography is
transferred shall, if the person has no office or place of business in
Canada, provide the Registrar with the name and address of a
representative for service.

## [Public Inspection and Copies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950981 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** An application shall not
be available for public inspection until it has been assigned an
application number.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Except with the written
consent of the applicant or the owner of a registered topography, as the
case may be, no person shall, by any means, make or provide a copy of
any material filed pursuant to section 14, 15, 16 or 17.

## [Transmission to Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950984 .Part}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If an application has
been made to the Federal Court under subsection 24(1) of the Act, the
Registrar shall, at the request of any of the parties, transmit to that
Court all material on file in the Office relating to the application.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-950988 .Part}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The fees to be paid for
acts or services rendered by the Registrar are as set out in the
schedule and the appropriate fees shall be paid in Canadian funds to the
Receiver General at the time any act or service is requested.
