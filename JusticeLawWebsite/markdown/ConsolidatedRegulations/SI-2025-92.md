## Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Appropriate Minister for the Canada Lands Company Limited for the Purposes of the Financial Administration Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-infrastructure-and-communities-as-the-appropriate-minister-for-the-canada-lands-company-limited-for-the-purposes-of-the-financial-administration-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-92
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2025-08-13

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the
Appropriate Minister for the Canada Lands Company Limited for the
Purposes of the Financial Administration Act

P.C. 2025-575 2025-07-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph (d) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143){#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143-ID0EDDAA}
and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 83(1) of that Act,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143-ID0EDDAA){#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143}R.S., c.
  F-11
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2011-585 of May 18,
  2011[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752160954){#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752160954-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752160954-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752160954}SI/2011-50
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Infrastructure and
  Communities, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  appropriate Minister for the Canada Lands Company Limited for the
  purposes of the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143){#fn_81000-3-2949_hq_1752161143-ID0EBAAAA}.
