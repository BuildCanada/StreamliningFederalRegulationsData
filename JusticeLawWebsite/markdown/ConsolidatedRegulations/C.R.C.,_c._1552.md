## Timber Marking Rules {#timber-marking-rules .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1552
:::

[TIMBER MARKING ACT](/eng/acts/T-11)

Rules Respecting the Marking of Timber
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528695 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules may be cited as
the [Timber Marking Rules](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1552).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528697 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application to register
a timber mark shall be in duplicate in Form 1, shall be prosecuted by
correspondence and shall be addressed to "The Commissioner of Patents,
Ottawa, Canada".

## [Correspondence and Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528699 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Correspondence on the
subject of an application, except correspondence concerning assignments
or other documents of title,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall relate to that application only; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be conducted only by the applicant or his agent
  duly authorized in writing.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  document, other than a drawing relating to a timber mark, shall be
  clearly and legibly typewritten or printed on sheets of white paper of
  a quality satisfactory to the Commissioner, eight inches wide and 13
  inches long, with a clear margin of one inch on the left-hand side.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} One of the copies of a typewritten document submitted
  to the Commissioner shall be the ribbon copy.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Drawings
  deposited shall be on sheets of good quality pure white paper or
  tracing cloth that is eight inches wide and 13 inches long, and views
  shall be clearly drawn in black lines on a sufficiently large scale to
  be easily read.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Neither the title of the timber mark nor any
  descriptive matter shall appear on any part of a drawing sheet, but
  each sheet may bear in the lower right-hand corner the signature of
  the applicant or his agent.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  assignment presented for registration against a registered timber mark
  shall be the original document or a typed or printed copy thereof
  certified before a notary public to be a true copy.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an assignment is accompanied by a duplicate or a
  notarially certified copy thereof, the duplicate or copy shall be
  returned by the Commissioner with a certificate of registration, but
  if an assignment is not so accompanied the Commissioner shall notify
  the person who presented it for registration of the number and date
  under which and of the timber mark against which it has been
  registered.
