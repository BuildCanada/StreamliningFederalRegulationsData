## [Oath or Solemn Affirmation of Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-785811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text of the oath or affirmation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The oath or solemn
affirmation of office to be taken by the Chairperson and other members
of the Immigration and Refugee Board under section 152.1 of the
[Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) is to be in
the following form:

I, ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader-solid}, do swear (or
solemnly affirm) that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} I will, during the term of my appointment, and any
  re-appointments, faithfully, impartially and to the best of my
  knowledge and ability, properly carry out the duties of a (full-time
  or part-time) (*title*) of the Immigration and Refugee Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} I will comply with the Code of Conduct for Members of
  the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada, as amended or replaced
  from time to time; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} I will not disclose or make known, unless authorized,
  any matter that comes to my knowledge because I am holding that
  office.

(*In the case of an oath, add*: So help me (*Name of Deity*).)
