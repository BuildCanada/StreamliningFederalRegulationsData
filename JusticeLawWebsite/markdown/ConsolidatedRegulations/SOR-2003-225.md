## Saskatchewan Canola Order {#saskatchewan-canola-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-225
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 2003-06-12

Saskatchewan Canola Order

P.C. 2003-918 2003-06-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to
section 2[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes the
annexed [Saskatchewan Canola Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-225).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703136 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Agri-Food Act of the Province
    of Saskatchewan. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[canola]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[canola]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means canola, rapeseed or oilseed, of
    the genus [Brassica]{.otherLang lang="la"}, that is produced in
    Saskatchewan. ([colza]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Canola
    Development Commission. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Saskatchewan Canola
    Development Plan Regulations made under the Act.
    ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703142 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
authorized to regulate the marketing of canola in interprovincial and
export trade and for that purpose may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Saskatchewan, exercise all or
any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of canola locally within the Province under the Act and the
Plan.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703144 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
relation to the powers granted to it under section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose, by Order, and collect levies or
  charges from persons referred to in that section who are engaged in
  the production or marketing of canola, and for those purposes may
  classify those persons into groups and fix the levies or charges
  payable by the members of the different groups in different amounts;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commission, including the creation of reserves, the payment of
  expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of canola and
  the equalization or adjustment among the producers of canola of money
  realized from the sale of canola during any period that the Commission
  may determine.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703148 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703150 .Part}

on the day on which it is registered.
