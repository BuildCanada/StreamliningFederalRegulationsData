## I.O.S. Income Tax Remission Order {#i.o.s.-income-tax-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 770
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax to I.O.S. Ltd. and to
Certain Other Taxpayers
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561823 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [I.O.S. Income Tax Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._770).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561825 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Order,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[I.O.S.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[I.O.S.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} I.O.S. Ltd.,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corporation, trust, legal or other entity
        directly or indirectly controlled by or affiliated with I.O.S.
        as of July 29, 1973, or subsequent thereto or hereafter,
        including the following:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Fund of Funds Limited,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} I.O.S. Growth Fund Limited,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Fund of Funds Sterling Limited,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} I.I.T., an International Investment Trust,
          and

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} Investment Properties International Limited,
          and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any corporation, trust, legal or other entity
        now existing or hereafter created by law or otherwise that
        acquires the assets of any corporation, trust or affiliate or
        other entity referred to in paragraph (b);
        ([I.O.S.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[liquidator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person appointed under the laws of Canada
        or a province as a liquidator, receiver or trustee of I.O.S.,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person, including a director, officer,
        employee or agent of a corporation, trust, legal or other entity
        referred to in paragraphs (b) and (c) of the definition
        "I.O.S.", appointed by a liquidator who acts as agent or trustee
        on behalf of any liquidator, receiver or trustee referred to in
        paragraph (a), and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person, ordinarily resident in Canada, who
        acts as agent or trustee on behalf of any liquidator, receiver
        of trustee of I.O.S. other than a liquidator, receiver or
        trustee referred to in paragraph (a).
        ([liquidateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided in this Order, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the [Income
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561843 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to I.O.S., any shareholder or unitholder of I.O.S. or to any other
person, before payment thereof has been made and before liability
therefor arises, of all taxes exigible under the Act that otherwise have
or may become payable by I.O.S. or by that shareholder or unitholder or
by that person, by reason only of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appointment of a liquidator to manage I.O.S.; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exercise of control over I.O.S. or the taking of
  any other action in respect of I.O.S. by a liquidator.
