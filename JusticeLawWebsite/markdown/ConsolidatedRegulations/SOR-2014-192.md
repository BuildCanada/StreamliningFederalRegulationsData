[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 169.31(1)(a) of the Act

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 169.31(1)(a) of the Act, a term constitutes an
  operational term when it sets out an obligation of the railway company
  towards the shipper respecting the performance of an action related to
  the receiving, loading, carrying, unloading and delivering of traffic.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examples

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A term that deals with any of the following matters
  is an operational term referred to in subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the furnishing of cars to the shipper, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of cars to be furnished,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the schedule for furnishing them,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the place where they are to be furnished,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their condition,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their type and specifications, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed by the railway
      company for the processing of the shipper's order for those cars
      and for their delivery by the railway company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the handling of cars that are furnished by the
    shipper to the railway company, including the cycle time for
    handling them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the furnishing of locomotives or other motive power
    sources, of other equipment or of train crews;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the pick-up of cars by the railway company,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the schedule for picking them up, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the place where they are to be picked up;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number and scheduling of switches;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the transit time for delivery of the shipped
    traffic ;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the route to be used for that delivery;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a performance standard --- expressed as a quantity,
    frequency, percentage or other metric --- for measuring the railway
    company's compliance with an operational term that deals with a
    matter referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (g) or any other
    operational term referred to in subsection (1), including any term
    that establishes the railway company's responsibility for collecting
    and sharing data and for reporting in respect of the performance
    standard;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances that would make it impossible for
    the railway company to comply with an operational term that deals
    with a matter referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (g) or any
    other operational term referred to in subsection (1), including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a superior force, including a flood, fire or
      other natural disaster,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a war or insurrection,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a riot, strike or lock-out,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a derailment,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a blockage of rail lines due to an accident,
      demonstration, natural cause or other cause,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} any condition related to the loading of cars,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} a failure of the shipper to comply with
      conditions that are associated with the performance by the railway
      company of service obligations under section 113 of the Act,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the inability of the railway company to access
      a terminal or a delay in accessing it,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the inability of the railway company to transfer
      the shipped traffic to another railway company or a delay in
      transferring it, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a breakdown in a component of the railway.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 169.31(1)(b) of the Act

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 169.31(1)(b) of the Act, a term constitutes an
  operational term when it sets out an obligation of recovery that the
  railway company must comply with if it fails to comply with an
  operational term that deals with a matter referred to in any of
  paragraphs 2(2)(a) to (g) or any other operational term referred to in
  subsection 2(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An obligation of recovery must set out steps that the
  railway company must take to minimize the consequences for the shipper
  of its non-compliance and to ensure that compliance, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} preparing a recovery plan for those purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} taking reasonable steps to secure whatever
    assistance may be required to carry out those purposes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} limiting any prejudicial consequences related to
    the non-compliance; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} implementing the recovery plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 169.31(1)(c) of the Act

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 169.31(1)(c) of the Act, a term constitutes an
  operational term when it sets out an obligation of the shipper towards
  the railway company respecting the performance of an action related to
  an operational term referred to in section 2, including a term that
  deals with any of the following matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the furnishing of cars to the railway company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the handling of cars that are furnished by the
    railway company to the shipper;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loading and unloading of cars, including the
    scheduling of and the procedures for the release of those cars;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number and scheduling of switches; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the provision of access to the shipper's
    facilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of recovery

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 169.31(1)(c) of the
  Act, a term constitutes an operational term when it sets out an
  obligation that the shipper must comply with in relation to an
  obligation of recovery referred to in section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication protocols

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 169.31(1)(a) to (c) of the Act, a term constitutes an
operational term when it sets out a communication protocol for the
railway company and shipper to use to communicate information in respect
of their compliance with an operational term, including a protocol for
communicating information about

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the shipped traffic, including its position, state
  and expected time of delivery at any particular time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any circumstance referred to in paragraph 2(2)(i);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an obligation of recovery or any obligation of the
  shipper related to an obligation of recovery, including reporting on
  the implementation of any recovery plan;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any failure of the railway company or the shipper to
  comply with an operational term;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the billing for services in relation to an
  operational term referred to in paragraph 169.31(1)(a) of the Act; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the authorization of a terminal.
