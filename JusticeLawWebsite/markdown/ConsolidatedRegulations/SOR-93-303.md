## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952726 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
    Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2); (Loi)

[[groundwater]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[groundwater]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all water in a zone of
    saturation beneath the land surface, regardless of its origin; ([eau
    souterraine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[undertaking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undertaking in respect
    of which water is to be used or waste is to be deposited, of a type
    set out in Schedule II; ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[watercourse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural watercourse,
    body of water or water supply, whether usually containing water or
    not, and includes groundwater, springs, swamps and gulches. ([cours
    d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2016-130, s. 3

## [Water Use or Waste Deposit Without a Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952740 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may use water and deposit waste without a licence if the proposed use
  or deposit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has no potential for significant adverse
    environmental effects;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would not interfere with existing rights of other
    water users or waste depositors; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} satisfies the criteria set out

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an industrial undertaking, in
      column II of Schedule IV;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a mining and milling undertaking,
      column II of Schedule V;

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a municipal undertaking, in
      column II of Schedule VI;

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a power undertaking, in column II
      of Schedule VII; and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agricultural, conservation,
      recreational or miscellaneous undertaking, in column II of
      Schedule VIII.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual may, without a licence, deposit waste
  that is sewage from a residential building, in accordance with the
  General Sanitation Regulations of the Northwest Territories.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vessel, as defined in section 2 of the [Canada
  Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), may deposit waste without a
  licence if the deposit of that waste is not prohibited under Part 9 of
  that Act.

## [Applications for Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952755 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence or for the amendment or renewal of a licence
  shall be the form set out in Schedule III and shall contain the
  information identified therein and be accompanied by a deposit equal
  to any water use fee that would be payable under subsection 9(1) in
  respect of the first year of the licence that is being applied for.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application referred to in subsection (1) shall
  also include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the proposed undertaking consists of a dam,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a plan showing the length, height, cross-sections
      and elevations of the dam and the location and preliminary designs
      of spillways, canals, sluice pipes and any other outlet works, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} data respecting the type and composition of the
      material to be used in the construction of the dam;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the proposed undertaking consists of a
    storage reservoir,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the number of hectares of land to
      be flooded, the surface area, in hectares, of the reservoir when
      filled and the contemplated total storage capacity of the
      reservoir, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a plan showing representative cross-sections of
      the reservoir;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the proposed undertaking consists of a
    watercourse crossing,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a plan of the crossing showing cross-sections and
      elevations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the existing bed and banks of
      the watercourse, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any available data on the water flow of the
      watercourse;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the water is proposed to be used for a
    municipal undertaking in respect of a camp or lodge or in respect of
    a municipality or settlement,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a plan showing the location of the camp or lodge
      or the location, area and boundaries of the municipality or
      settlement,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the approximate capacity of the
      camp or lodge or population of the municipality or settlement, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a plan of the intended water or sewage system,
      showing cross-sections and elevations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the water is proposed to be used for an
    industrial or mining and milling undertaking, a description of the
    undertaking and of all wastes produced and chemicals used in the
    operation of the undertaking;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the proposed undertaking involves a deposit
    of waste,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location, rate, timing, frequency and
      duration of deposit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated constituents of the deposit and
      the concentration thereof,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the methods proposed for the storage and
      treatment thereof, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the qualitative and
      quantitative effects on the waters into which the waste is to be
      deposited;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} where the undertaking involves the handling or
    storage of petroleum products or hazardous materials,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a plan for the safe handling, storage and
      disposal thereof, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a contingency plan for their containment and for
      the clean-up thereof in the event of a spill; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in any case, plans for the abandonment, or any
    temporary closing, of the proposed undertaking.

## [Licensing Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952787 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a licence issued under subsection 72.03(1) of the
  Act shall be a type B licence for one or more uses of water or
  deposits of waste set out in column I of any of Schedules IV to VIII,
  if any one of those uses or deposits

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets a criterion set out in column III thereof; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets a criterion set out in column II thereof, but
    does not meet the requirements of paragraphs 5(1)(a) and (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence issued under subsection 72.03(1) of the Act
  shall be a type A licence for one or more uses of water or deposits of
  waste set out in column I of any of Schedules IV to VIII, if any one
  of those uses or deposits meets a criterion set out in column IV
  thereof.

## [Water Use Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952795 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (4) and (5), the fee payable by a licensee for the
  right to the use of water, calculated on an annual basis, is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agricultural undertaking, the
    greater of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$30, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$0.15 for each 1 000 m^3^ authorized by the
      licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an industrial, mining and milling or
    miscellaneous undertaking, the greater of \$30 and the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first 2 000 m^3^ per day that is
      authorized by the licence, \$1 for each 100 m^3^ per day,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any quantity greater than 2 000 m^3^ per day
      but less than or equal to 4 000 m^3^ per day that is authorized by
      the licence, \$1.50 for each 100 m^3^ per day, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for any quantity greater than 4 000 m^3^ per
      day that is authorized by the licence, \$2 for each 100 m^3^ per
      day; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a power undertaking,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 0 power undertaking, nil,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 1 power undertaking, \$1,500,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 2 power undertaking, \$4,000,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 3 power undertaking, \$10,000,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 4 power undertaking, \$30,000,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 5 power undertaking, \$80,000, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} for a Class 6 power undertaking, \$90,000 for
      the first 100 000 kW of authorized production and \$1,000 for each
      1 000 kW of authorized production in excess of 100 000 kW.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), where a licence
  authorizes the use of water on a basis other than a daily basis, the
  licence fee payable shall be calculated by converting the rate of
  authorized use to an equivalent daily rate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the volume of water is specified in a licence
  to be total watercourse flow, the licence fee will be calculated using
  the mean daily flow of the watercourse, calculated on an annual basis.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Licence fees are payable only for the portion of the
  year during which the licence is in effect.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No fees are payable under subsection (1) in respect
  of a diversion of water where the water is not otherwise used.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Licence fees shall be paid or, in the case of an
  initial payment, deducted from the deposit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence for a term of one year or
    less, at the time the licence is issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a licence for a term of more than one
    year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the first year of the licence, at the time
      the licence is issued, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each subsequent year of the licence, or for
      any portion of the final year of the licence, in advance, on the
      anniversary of the date of issuance of the licence.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the licence fee payable under this section is
  less than the amount of the deposit remitted under subsection 6(1),
  the difference shall be refunded accordingly.

## [Applications for Assignment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952823 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorization of a board for the assignment of a licence referred to
  in section 72.14 of the Act may be obtained by submitting an
  application, accompanied by the fee set out in section 7, to the board
  established for the relevant water management area not less than 45
  days before the date on which the applicant proposes to assign the
  licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  signed by the assignor and the assignee and shall include the name and
  address of the assignee.

## [Applications for Cancellation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952829 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An application for
cancellation of a licence shall be in writing and set out the reason for
the requested cancellation and a description of the measures taken or
proposed to be taken, prior to cancellation, for abandonment of the
appurtenant undertaking.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952831 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  board may fix the amount of security required to be furnished by an
  applicant under subsection 72.11(1) of the Act in an amount not
  exceeding the aggregate of the costs of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} abandonment of the undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restoration of the site of the undertaking; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any ongoing measures that may remain to be taken
    after the abandonment of the undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In fixing an amount of security under subsection (1),
  a board may have regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the applicant, licensee or
    prospective assignee to pay the costs referred to in that
    subsection; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the past performance by the applicant, licensee or
    prospective assignee in respect of any other licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Security referred to in subsection (1) shall be in
  the form of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note guaranteed by a bank in Canada
    and payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque drawn on a bank in Canada and
    payable to the Receiver General;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-130, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an irrevocable letter of credit from a bank in
    Canada; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cash.

## [Public Register]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952849 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  register referred to in section 68 of the Act shall be in the form of
  one or more files in respect of each licence application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each file referred to in subsection (1) shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the application and of all supporting

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records from any public hearing held in

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any licence issued in respect of the

    issuance; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all correspondence and documents submitted to the
    board in respect of compliance with the conditions of any licence

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952859 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall maintain accurate and detailed books and records and
  shall submit a report each year to the board that issued the licence,
  on or before the anniversary of the date of issuance of the licence,
  setting out the quantity of water used under the licence and the
  quantity, concentration and type of any waste deposited under the
  licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report submitted pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  be signed by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee, where the licensee is an individual;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authorized agent of the licensee, where the
    licensee is not an individual.

- SOR/2016-130, s. 12

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2014, c. 2, s. 252

  - ###### Northwest Territories Waters Regulations {#northwest-territories-waters-regulations .MarginalNote}

    [252.]{.lawlabel} The provisions of the Northwest Territories Waters
    Regulations, that are made under the [Northwest Territories Waters
    Act](/eng/acts/N-27.3), chapter 39 of the Statutes of Canada, 1992,
    that are in force immediately before the day on which section 182
    comes into force, are deemed, as of that day, to have been made
    under the other Act and shall remain in force, to the extent that
    they are not inconsistent with the other Act, as it reads on that
    day, until they are repealed or replaced.
