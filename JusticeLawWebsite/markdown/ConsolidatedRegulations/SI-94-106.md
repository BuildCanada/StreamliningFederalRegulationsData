## Minister of Communications (National Archives of Canada) Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-communications-national-archives-of-canada-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-106
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-08-24

Order Authorizing the Minister of Communications to Prescribe By Order
the Fees to be Paid by Users or Classes of Users of a Service or
Facility of the National Archives of Canada

P.C. 1994-1346 1994-08-04
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBCA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBCA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 6
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke the [Minister of Communications (National
  Archives of Canada) Authority to Prescribe Fees
  Order](/eng/regulations/SI-94-106), made by Order in Council P.C.
  1989-1595 of August 24, 1989[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBBBA}; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnote2_e}SI/89-181,
    1989 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4000
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make the annexed Order authorizing the Minister of
  Communications to prescribe by order the fees to be paid by users or
  classes of users of a service or facility of the National Archives of
  Canada, in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647606 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Minister of Communications (Library and Archives of Canada)
Authority to Prescribe Fees Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2004, c. 11, subpar. 52(5) and (6)
:::

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647610 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of
Communications is hereby authorized to prescribe by order the fees to be
paid by users or classes of users of a service or facility of the
Library and Archives of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- 2004, c. 11, subpar. 52(5) and (6)
:::

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-647614 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The fees referred to in
section 2 shall be determined in accordance with the Treasury Board
Policy on External User Charges for Goods, Services, Property Rights and
Privileges, issued in August 1986 and revised in December 1989.
