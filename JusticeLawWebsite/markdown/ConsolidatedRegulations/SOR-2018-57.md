## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858223 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debt obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([titre de
    créance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-viability contingent capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-viability contingent capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    share or liability of a federal member institution that, by
    operation of its terms, may be converted into common shares of the
    institution as a result of a public announcement related to the
    institution's viability that is made by the Superintendent or Her
    Majesty in right of Canada or a province. ([fonds propres d'urgence
    en cas de non-viabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subordinated indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subordinated indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([titre
    secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Shares and Liabilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858230 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed shares and liabilities

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (7), the following shares and liabilities are
  prescribed for the purposes of subsection 39.2(2.3) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any debt obligation, other than subordinated
    indebtedness, that is issued by a domestic systemically important
    bank and that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is perpetual, has an original or amended term to
      maturity of more than 400 days, has one or more explicit or
      embedded options that, if exercised by or on behalf of the issuer,
      would result in a maturity date that is more than 400 days from
      the date of issuance of the debt obligation or has an explicit or
      embedded option that, if exercised by or on behalf of the holder,
      would by itself result in a maturity date that is more than 400
      days from the maturity date that would apply if the option were
      not exercised,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is unsecured or is only partially secured at the
      time of issuance, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been assigned a Committee on Uniform
      Security Identification Procedures (CUSIP) number, International
      Securities Identification Number (ISIN) or other similar
      designation that identifies a specific security in order to
      facilitate its trading and settlement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any share or subordinated indebtedness that is
    issued by a domestic systemically important bank and that is neither
    a common share nor non-viability contingent capital.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a reference to a debt
  obligation or to subordinated indebtedness in this section includes a
  reference to any debt obligation or subordinated indebtedness that is
  due but remains unpaid on the day on which the order under paragraph
  39.13(1)(d) of the Act is made or becomes due after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A share or liability is prescribed only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the share or liability is issued on or after the
    day on which these Regulations come into force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a liability issued before the day on
    which these Regulations come into force, the terms of the liability
    are, on or after that day, amended to increase its principal amount
    or to extend its term to maturity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partially secured

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a debt obligation, other than subordinated
  indebtedness, is only partially secured at the time of issuance, only
  the portion of the principal amount and accrued and unpaid interest of
  the debt obligation that exceeds the value of the collateral,
  determined at the time that the debt obligation is issued, is a
  prescribed liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following, as well as any liabilities that arise
  from any of them, are not prescribed shares or liabilities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any [[covered bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in section 21.5 of the [National Housing
    Act](/eng/acts/N-11);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any [[eligible financial
    contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 39.15(9)
    of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any structured note;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any conversion or exchange privilege that is
    convertible at any time into shares;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any option or right to acquire shares or any
    privilege referred to in paragraph (d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any share of a series that was created before
    January 1, 2013 and issued as a result of the exercise of a
    conversion privilege under the terms of another series of shares
    that was created before January 1, 2013.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structured note

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (5)(c), a structured
  note is a debt obligation that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifies that the obligation's stated term to
    maturity, or a payment to be made by its issuer, is determined in
    whole or in part by reference to an index or reference point,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the performance or value of an entity or asset,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the market price of a security, commodity,
      investment fund or financial instrument,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an interest rate, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the exchange rate between two currencies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains any other type of embedded derivative or
    similar feature.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- structured note

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (6), the following debt
  obligations are not structured notes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation in respect of which the stated
    term to maturity, or a payment to be made by its issuer, is
    determined in whole or principally by reference to the performance
    of a security of that issuer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} specifies that the return on the debt obligation
      is determined by a fixed or floating interest rate or a fixed
      spread above or below a fixed or floating interest rate,
      regardless of whether the return is subject to a minimum interest
      rate or whether the interest rate changes between fixed and
      floating,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has no other terms affecting the stated term to
      maturity or the return on the debt obligation, with the exception
      of the right of the issuer to redeem the debt obligation or the
      right of the holder or issuer to extend its term to maturity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is payable in cash.

## [Conditions for Conversion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858270 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 4 and 5, [[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to non-viability contingent capital,
  conversion of the shares and liabilities in accordance with their
  terms; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to other shares and liabilities,
  conversion under subsection 39.2(2.3) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion amount

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In carrying out a
conversion, the Corporation must take into consideration the
requirement, under subsection 485(1) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), for banks to maintain adequate capital.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of conversion

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Corporation must use its best efforts to ensure that a prescribed
  share or liability is converted only if all subordinate prescribed
  shares and liabilities and any subordinate non-viability contingent
  capital have previously been converted or are converted at the same
  time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same proportion --- equal rank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation must use its best efforts to ensure
  that the converted part of the liquidation entitlement of a prescribed
  share, or the converted part of the principal amount and accrued and
  unpaid interest of a prescribed liability, is converted on a pro rata
  basis for all prescribed shares or liabilities of equal rank that are
  converted during the same restructuring period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority of instruments

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In a conversion under subsection 39.2(2.3) of the
  Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holders of prescribed shares or liabilities must
    receive a greater number of common shares per dollar of the
    converted part of the liquidation entitlement of their shares, or
    the converted part of the principal amount and accrued and unpaid
    interest of their liabilities, than holders of any subordinate
    prescribed shares or liabilities that are converted during the same
    restructuring period or of any subordinate non-viability contingent
    capital that is converted during the same restructuring period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} holders of prescribed shares or liabilities of
    equal rank that are converted during the same restructuring period
    must receive the same number of common shares per dollar of the
    converted part of the liquidation entitlement of their shares or the
    converted part of the principal amount and accrued and unpaid
    interest of their liabilities; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} holders of prescribed shares or liabilities must
    receive, if any non-viability contingent capital of equal rank to
    the shares or liabilities is converted during the same restructuring
    period, a number of common shares per dollar of the converted part
    of the liquidation entitlement of their shares, or the converted
    part of the principal amount and accrued and unpaid interest of
    their liabilities, that is equal to the largest number of common
    shares received by any holder of the non-viability contingent
    capital per dollar of that capital.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ranking

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a share or liability of the federal
  member institution is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subordinate to another share or liability of the
    institution if, in the event that the institution is wound up, the
    share or liability would rank subordinate in right of payment to
    that other share or liability; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equal in rank to another share or liability of the
    institution if, in the event that the institution is wound up, the
    share or liability would rank equally in right of payment to that
    other share or liability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[liquidation
  entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[liquidation
  entitlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount to which the holder
  of a share of a federal member institution is entitled to be paid, in
  the event that the institution is wound up, in priority to any amount
  to be paid to a holder of a subordinate share.
