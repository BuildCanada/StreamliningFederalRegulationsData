## Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Public Service Commission to the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of Canada {#order-transferring-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-commission-to-the-public-service-human-resources-management-agency-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-44
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-04-21

Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Public Service Commission to
the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of Canada

P.C. 2004-369  2004-03-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Public Service Commission to the Public Service Human
Resources Management Agency of Canada, effective April 1, 2004, the
control and supervision of those portions of the public service in the
Public Service Commission known as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Accelerated Executive Development Directorate,
  which is comprised of the Assistant Deputy Minister Prequalification
  Process and the Accelerated Executive Development Program,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Career Assignment Program,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Management Trainee Program,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Accelerated Economist Training Program,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Interchange Canada,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Demographics within the Research Directorate, and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Labour Market Studies within the Research
  Directorate,

except for those portions carrying out the powers, duties and functions
of the Public Service Commission under paragraph 5(a) and sections
6[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EJEAAA}, 8,
35[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EHEAAA},
37[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fnc_e){#fnc_e-ID0EFEAAA} and
41[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fnd_e){#fnd_e-ID0EDEAAA} of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).
