## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Licensing of Bonded Warehouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001020 .Part}

### [Issuance of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001021 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness may issue a licence to any person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes an application in accordance with subsection
    (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides such security as may be required under
    subsection 91(4) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and in
    accordance with section 4.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2015-70, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence must be submitted to the
  Minister in the prescribed form, together with a detailed plan of the
  proposed bonded warehouse.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The plan referred to in subsection (2) must indicate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the place proposed to be operated as a
    bonded warehouse exists or is to be constructed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of construction of the place, whether or
    not it already exists; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the area, within the place, that is to be used for
    the storage of goods.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness shall not issue a licence to an applicant unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is of good character;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the site of the proposed bonded warehouse is within
    an area served by a customs office;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has sufficient financial resources

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to meet the requirements set out in sections 11
      and 12, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to lease or purchase the place proposed to be
      operated as a bonded warehouse;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicant will provide conditions suitable for
    the safekeeping of goods; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Border Services Agency is able to
    provide customs services with respect to the proposed bonded
    warehouse.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The terms and conditions under which a licence may be
  issued include the extent to which and circumstances in which, in
  accordance with section 20, goods may be manipulated, unpacked,
  packed, altered or combined with other goods while in bonded
  warehouses.

### [Cancellation or Suspension of Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001061 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness may cancel a licence where the
licensee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no longer owns or leases the place that is licensed
  as a bonded warehouse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} makes a request in writing to that Minister that the
  licence be cancelled; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is bankrupt.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 9, the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
  may suspend or cancel a licence where the licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of a receivership in respect of the
    licensee's debts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails to comply with any Act of Parliament, or any
    regulation made pursuant to an Act of Parliament, that prohibits,
    controls or regulates the importation or exportation of goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has, in the course of operating the bonded
    warehouse, acted dishonestly in business dealings with customs
    brokers, importers, carriers, Her Majesty or servants of Her
    Majesty;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has not met any of the requirements set out in
    sections 11 and 12; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has been incompetent in the operation of the bonded
    warehouse.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 9, the Minister of Public Safety
  and Emergency Preparedness may cancel a licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the volume of goods being received in the bonded
    warehouse is no longer sufficient to warrant the continued operation
    of the bonded warehouse;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no longer a need for a bonded warehouse in
    the area in which the bonded warehouse is located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Border Services Agency is no longer able
    to provide customs services with respect to the bonded warehouse;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensee manipulates, unpacks, packs, alters or
    combines the goods with other goods while in the bonded warehouse
    other than in accordance with the terms and conditions set out in
    the licence; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee contravenes section 20.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a licence is suspended, an officer may lock and
  seal the bonded warehouse and keep it locked and sealed during the
  period of suspension.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Operation of Bonded Warehouses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001095 .Part}

### [Facilities, Equipment and Personnel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001096 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall provide at the bonded warehouse in respect of which the
  licence was issued

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such facilities, equipment and personnel as are
    sufficient to control access to the bonded warehouse premises and
    provide secure storage of the goods in it, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} doors and other building components of sturdy
      construction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} secure locks on doors and windows,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} signs that indicate the security requirements
      applicable to the premises, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where the bonded warehouse will be used for the
      storage of designated goods, such additional facilities and
      equipment as may be required to ensure the secure storage of those
      goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} adequate space for the examination of goods by
    officers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the personnel and equipment necessary to ensure
    that the goods to be examined by an officer are made available to
    the officer for examination; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the personnel necessary to furnish information, for
    audit purposes, to an officer with respect to the bonded warehouse
    operations and inventory system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a bonded warehouse forms only part of a
  building, the licensee shall, if so requested by the chief officer of
  customs, keep the bonded warehouse separate from the remainder of the
  building by a partition or other structure.

### [Operation and Maintenance Standards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001108 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licensee shall ensure that the goods received in the bonded warehouse
  are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stored safely and securely in the area designated
    for that purpose in the plan referred to in subsection 3(2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identified in such a manner so as to enable an
    officer to locate the goods and check them against the appropriate
    documentation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than the licensee, an employee of
  the licensee or an employee of a carrier engaged in the delivery of
  goods to or the removal of goods from the bonded warehouse, shall
  enter any place in it where goods are stored, without the written
  authorization or the attendance of an officer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every licensee of a bonded warehouse shall have in
  place

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures to maintain the security of, and
    restrict access to, the bonded warehouse; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procedures to ensure that personnel working in the
    bonded warehouse are aware of and follow the procedures referred to
    in paragraph (a).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A bonded warehouse may be locked and sealed by an
  officer where the chief officer of customs requests that the bonded
  warehouse be locked and sealed, for the purpose of verifying the goods
  received or the warehouse documentation.

### [Restrictions on Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001118 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall receive
in or transfer from a bonded warehouse in a province intoxicating liquor
unless the licensee has obtained written approval to receive or transfer
the intoxicating liquor from the board, commission or agency authorized
by the laws of that province to sell or authorize the sale of
intoxicating liquor in that province.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No licensee shall receive
the following domestic products into a bonded warehouse:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cigars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} packaged raw leaf tobacco;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} manufactured tobacco that is not stamped;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} non-duty paid packaged or bulk spirits;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} non-duty paid packaged or bulk wine;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} non-duty paid specially denatured alcohol; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} vaping products that are not stamped.

### [Receipt of Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001138 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acknowledge receipt of imported goods into the bonded
  warehouse in respect of which their licence was issued by endorsing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transportation document presented to the
    licensee by the carrier, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the form that is referred to in subsection 19(2)
    of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) and is presented to the
    licensee by the importer or owner of the goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acknowledge the receipt of any other goods that have
  been received in the bonded warehouse by completing the delivery
  document in the manner described in the prescribed form.

### [Prescribed Class of Goods and Period of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001144 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 37(2) and 39.1(2) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),
tobacco products, packaged spirits and vaping products are a prescribed
class of goods and are forfeit if they have not been removed from the
bonded warehouse within five years of the day on which the goods are
described in the form prescribed under subsection 19(2) of that Act.

### [Manipulation, Alteration and Combination of Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001152 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2002-130, s. 3

\]
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every licensee shall
ensure that goods are not manipulated, altered or combined with other
goods while in the bonded warehouse except for the purpose of or in the
course of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disassembling or reassembling goods that have been
  assembled or disassembled for packing, handling or transportation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} displaying;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} inspecting;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} marking, labelling, tagging or ticketing;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} packing, unpacking, packaging or repackaging;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} removing from the warehouse, for the sole purpose of
  soliciting orders for goods or services, a small quantity of material,
  or a portion, a piece or an individual object, that represents the
  goods;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} storing;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} testing; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any of the following that do not materially alter the
  characteristics of the goods:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cleaning,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} complying with any applicable law of Canada or of
    a province,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} diluting,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} normal maintenance and servicing,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} preserving,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} separating defective goods from prime quality
    goods,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} sorting or grading, and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} trimming, filing, slitting or cutting.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-130, s. 4
:::

### [Transfer and Removal of Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001175 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where there is a transfer
of ownership of goods stored in a bonded warehouse, the importer or
owner of the goods shall submit a transfer document in the prescribed
form to an officer at the customs office where the goods were described
under subsection 19(2) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where the importer or
owner of goods stored in a bonded warehouse wants the goods removed from
the bonded warehouse in smaller units than those described under
subsection 19(2) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the importer or
owner shall submit to the chief officer of customs

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are to be released, an amended
  accounting in the prescribed form; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are not to be released, an amended
  description in the prescribed form.
