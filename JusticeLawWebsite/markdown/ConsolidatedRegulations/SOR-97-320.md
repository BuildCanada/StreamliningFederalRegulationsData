## Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Fruit and Vegetable Aggregate Quantity Limit Order {#canada-chile-free-trade-agreement-fruit-and-vegetable-aggregate-quantity-limit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-320
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-07-05

Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Fruit and Vegetable Aggregate Quantity
Limit Order

P.C. 1997-951  1997-07-04
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 25.8[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes
the annexed Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Fruit and Vegetable
Aggregate Quantity Limit Order.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EGCAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  14, s. 51
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA){#footnoteb_e}R.S., c. 41
  (3rd Supp.)
  :::
:::

## [Tariff Rate Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005948 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The quantity limit of goods
of tariff item No. 0703.10.92 that are entitled to the Chile Tariff is,
for the period set out in column 1 of item 1 of the schedule, the
applicable quantity limit set out in column 2 of that item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 30
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate quantity
limit of goods of tariff item Nos. 0810.10.11 and 0810.10.92 that are
entitled to the Chile Tariff is, for the period set out in column 1 of
item 2 of the schedule, the applicable aggregate quantity limit set out
in column 2 of that item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 30
:::
