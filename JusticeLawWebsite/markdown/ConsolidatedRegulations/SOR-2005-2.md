British Columbia Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations

::::::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Lieutenant Governor in Council of British Columbia, pursuant to
subsection 18(1) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), S.C. 2004, c. 10, hereby makes the annexed
[British Columbia Sex Offender Information Registration
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-2).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716935 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[BCSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[BCSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Sex
    Offender Information Registration Centre administered by RCMP "E"
    Division. ([BIRDSCB]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716940 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A sex offender whose main
residence is located in the Province of British Columbia may report
under any of paragraphs 4.1(1)(a) to (b.2) or section 4.3 of the Act or
provide any notification required under section 6 of the Act by
telephone to the BCSOIRC.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716942 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of British Columbia to collect information
for the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member or reservist of the RCMP;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a "provincial constable", "municipal constable" or
  "designated constable", within the meaning of section 1 of the Police
  Act, chapter 367 of the Revised Statutes of British Columbia, 1996, as
  amended from time to time; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a "special municipal constable", within the meaning
  of section 1 of the Police Act, chapter 367 of the Revised Statutes of
  British Columbia, 1996, as amended from time to time, who has
  previously been a member of the RCMP or a constable referred to in
  paragraph (b).

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716946 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person employed at a
registration centre whose duties include the registration of information
are authorized in the Province of British Columbia to register
information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716948 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  places set out in the schedule are designated as registration centres
  in the Province of British Columbia.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated area of service of each registration
  centre is the entire Province of British Columbia.
