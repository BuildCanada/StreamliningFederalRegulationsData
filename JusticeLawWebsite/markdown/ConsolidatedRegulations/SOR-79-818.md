## British Columbia Tree Fruit Order {#british-columbia-tree-fruit-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-818
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1979-11-09

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Tree Fruit in
British Columbia

P.C. 1979-3011 1979-11-08
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[British Columbia Tree Fruit Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-818),
C.R.C., c. 145, and to make the annexed [Order granting authority to
regulate the marketing of tree fruit in British
Columbia](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-818) in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866508 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [British Columbia Tree Fruit Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-818).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866510 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing
    (British Columbia) Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia
    Tree Fruit Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Tree Fruit
    Marketing Scheme established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give
    effect to the Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tree fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tree fruit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tree fruit grown and
    produced in the Province of British Columbia. ([fruit de
    verger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-262, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866518 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of tree fruit in interprovincial
and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of British Columbia, exercise all
or any powers like the power exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of tree fruit locally within that province under the Act and
the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1106, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866522 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with respect to the
marketing of tree fruit in interprovincial and export trade,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose by order and collect levies or charges
  from persons situated in the Province of British Columbia who are
  engaged in the production or marketing of tree fruit and for that
  purpose classify those persons into groups and, by order, fix the
  levies or charges payable by the members of the different groups in
  different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, and the payment
  of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal of tree
  fruit, and the equalization or adjustment among producers of tree
  fruit of moneys realized from the sale of tree fruit during such
  period or periods of time as the Commodity Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1106, s. 2
