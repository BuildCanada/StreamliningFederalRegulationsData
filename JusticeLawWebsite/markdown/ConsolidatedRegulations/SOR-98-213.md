## Firearms Records Regulations {#firearms-records-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-213
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Firearms Records Regulations

P.C. 1998-488 1998-03-24
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Firearms Records
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-213), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996, which
date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraph
83(1)(f), section 84 and paragraphs 87(1)(d) and 117(m), (s) and (w) of
the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Firearms Records Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-213).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020418 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian Firearms Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian Firearms Registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    registry established by the Registrar under section 83 of the Act.
    ([Registre canadien des armes à feu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Canadian Firearms Registry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020422 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 83(1)(f) of the Act, records shall be kept in the Canadian
Firearms Registry of the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every application for a licence, registration
  certificate or authorization that is issued or revoked by the
  Registrar, as well as all information accompanying the application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information provided to the Registrar concerning
  firearms that are taken as samples or seized under the Act or any
  other Act of Parliament;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of the individuals who are designated as
  chief firearms officers or firearms officers within the meaning of
  subsection 2(1) of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} information concerning prohibition orders made under
  section 147.1 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names of the individuals who are approved
  verifiers within the meaning of the [Conditions of Transferring
  Firearms and other Weapons Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-202);
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} information concerning any other matter relevant to
  the Registrar's responsibilities under the Act, that is required to be
  collected under the Act or any other Act of Parliament.

## [Records of Chief Firearms Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020430 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 87(1)(d) of the Act, a chief firearms officer shall keep
records of the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information concerning every approved shooting club
  or shooting range within the province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information provided to the chief firearms
  officer concerning any recognizance referred to in section 810 or
  810.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), bail condition,
  probation order condition or condition of parole, that contains a
  prohibition or limitation on the possession of any weapon or other
  device referred to in subsection 109(1) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} information concerning the restoration of an
  authorization, a licence or a registration certificate under section
  117.06 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names of the instructors in the province who are
  designated for the purpose of giving a course or test referred to in
  section 7 of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the names of the individuals who have met the
  requirements of section 7 of the Act, including the date and place of
  any course or test;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} every certification issued under paragraph 7(4)(a) of
  the Act; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the maximum inventory levels for prohibited firearms,
  prohibited weapons, prohibited devices or prohibited ammunition set
  out in accordance with subsection 24(3) of the [Firearms Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-199).

## [Destruction of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020439 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purpose of section 84 of the Act, a record
  kept in the Canadian Firearms Registry shall not be destroyed until
  after the expiration of 10 years after the date of the last
  administrative action taken regarding the information in the record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A record, kept in the Canadian Firearms Registry
  under paragraph 83(1)(a) of the Act, of a registration certificate
  that is issued or revoked shall not be destroyed.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 87(2) of the Act, a record kept by a chief firearms officer
shall not be destroyed until after the expiration of 10 years after the
date of the last administrative action taken regarding the information
in the record.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 5, the following records shall not be destroyed until after
  the death of the individual in respect of whom they are kept:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the record attesting to the fact that the
    individual has met the requirements of section 7 of the Act,
    including the date and place of any course or test; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} records of every certification issued under
    paragraph 7(4)(a) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 4 and 5, records of prohibition
  orders kept under paragraph 87(1)(c) of the Act and records of
  information concerning prohibition orders made under section 147.1 of
  the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5) shall not be destroyed until
  after the death of the individual in respect of whom the record is
  kept unless the individual meets the requirements of subsection 7(3)
  of the Act.

## [Amendment of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020449 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Records
  kept in the Canadian Firearms Registry under section 83 of the Act
  shall be amended only by the Registrar.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Records kept under section 87 of the Act by a chief
  firearms officer shall be amended only by the chief firearms officer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall inform every chief firearms
  officer of any amendment made under subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A chief firearms officer shall inform the Registrar
  and all other chief firearms officers of any amendment made under
  subsection (2).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} An individual who wants personal information that is
  contained in a record about himself or herself to be amended shall
  submit an application in writing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a record in the Canadian Firearms
    Registry, to the Registrar; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a record kept under section 87 of
    the Act, to the chief firearms officer in respect of the province in
    which the record was originally created.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020458 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 1, 1998.
