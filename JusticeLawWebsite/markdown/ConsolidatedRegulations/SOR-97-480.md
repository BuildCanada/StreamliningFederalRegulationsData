## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009665 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 2 and
3, remission is hereby granted of the anti-dumping duties paid or
payable on imports of certain hot-dipped galvannealed steel sheet,
coating weight 45/45 g/m^2^, 1.0 mm thick by 101 mm wide by coil, 390
MPa/mm^2^ hi-tensile, with a minimum elongation of 40%, for use in the
manufacture of non-exposed motor vehicle parts.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009667 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted
pursuant to section 1 on condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods on which remission is granted are imported
  on or after July 1, 1997;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the day on which this Order
  comes into force; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as National Revenue
  may require that the subject steel has been, or is to be, used in the
  manufacture of motor vehicle parts.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009672 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on October 23, 1997.
