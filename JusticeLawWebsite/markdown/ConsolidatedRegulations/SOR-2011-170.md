## Public Servants Disclosure Protection Tribunal Rules of Procedure {#public-servants-disclosure-protection-tribunal-rules-of-procedure .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVANTS DISCLOSURE PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-31.9)

Registration 2011-08-30

Public Servants Disclosure Protection Tribunal Rules of Procedure
::::

:::::::::::::: section
:::::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 21(4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EIAFA} of the [Public Servants
Disclosure Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EFAFA}, a copy of the proposed
[Public Servants Disclosure Protection Tribunal Rules of
Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-170), substantially in the annexed
form, was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/),
Part I, on May 21, 2011 and interested persons were given a reasonable
opportunity to make representations with respect to the proposed Rules;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 21(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAEA} of that Act, the Chairperson
of the Public Servants Disclosure Protection Tribunal has consulted with
the Royal Canadian Mounted Police and has ensured that the proposed
Rules take that organization's security and confidentiality needs into
account;

Therefore, the Chairperson of the Tribunal, pursuant to subsection
21(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGADA} of the
[Public Servants Disclosure Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDADA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Public Servants Disclosure Protection Tribunal Rules of
Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-170).

Ottawa, August 29, 2011

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773305 .Part}

apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Servants Disclosure
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-31.9). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

:   [[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to file with the
    registrar. ([déposer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    Rules. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773314 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liberal interpretation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Rules must be
liberally interpreted, with the aim of ensuring that the proceedings are
conducted informally and expeditiously and that the rights of the
parties set out in subsection 21.6(1) of the Act are respected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing with or varying Rules

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may vary a
Rule, or dispense with compliance with a Rule, if doing so advances the
aims set out in Rule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a time limit under these
Rules or under an order of the Tribunal ends on a Saturday, or a holiday
as defined in subsection 35(1) of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21), it is extended to end on the next day that is not
a Saturday or a holiday.

## [Application by the Commissioner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application by the
Commissioner under subsection 20.4(1) of the Act is made when the
application is filed. An application must contain the following
information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identification of the orders referred to in
  paragraphs 20.4(1)(a) and (b) of the Act that the Commissioner is
  seeking in the event that the Tribunal determines that a reprisal was
  taken against the complainant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the basis for the Commissioner's opinion that an
  application to the Tribunal in relation to the complaint is warranted;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the complaint and a summary of its
  contents;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, telephone number, fax
  number and email address of each party to the proceedings and of their
  representative, if any;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location at which the Commissioner believes the
  hearing should be conducted;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the language in which the Commissioner believes the
  proceedings should be conducted; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} identification of any special arrangements, including
  interpreters and equipment, that the Commissioner believes will be
  necessary to conduct the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** After the application has
been filed by the Commissioner, the registrar must provide each party
with notice of the filing.

## [Filing and Service]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service before filing

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is required to be filed under these Rules may be filed
  only if a copy of that document has been served on each party and each
  interested person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subrule (1), an application by the
  Commissioner under subsection 20.4(1) of the Act must be served on
  each person and entity that will be a party to the application under
  subsection 21.4(2) or 21.5(2) of the Act, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on representative

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party or interested person is represented,
  documents must be served on their representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a document referred to in subrule (1) is filed,
  it must be accompanied by proof of service that meets the requirements
  of Rule 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of filed documents

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A document written in a
language that is neither English nor French may be filed only if it is
accompanied by a translation of that document into English or French and
an affidavit attesting to the accuracy of the translation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original documents

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** If a document is filed
by electronic filing, any electronic version of it is, in the absence of
evidence to the contrary, considered to be the original of the document.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-107, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic version

**[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal creates
an electronic version of a document that is filed by hand, fax or by
mail, the electronic version is, in the absence of evidence to the
contrary, considered to be the original version of the document.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-107, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Service of a document is
effected by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivering a copy of the document by hand to the
  person or, if the person is a partnership, corporation or
  unincorporated organization, to a partner, officer or director; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of the document to the person by email
  or fax or by mail to the person's last known address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective day of service

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is effective
  on the day on which it is delivered. However, service of a document by
  ordinary mail is effective on the day that is 10 days after the day on
  which it is mailed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service after 17:00 hours

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by email or fax after 17:00
  hours at the recipient's local time is deemed to have been delivered
  on the next day that is not a Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is proved by
  any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written statement that is signed by the person
    who effected the service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the admission of the person served.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If service of a document is proved under paragraph
  (1)(a) or (b), the written statement or affidavit of service must be
  accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the document is served by email
    or fax, a receipt that confirms the successful transmission of the
    document; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the document is served by
    registered mail or courier, a receipt that identifies the day on
    which the document was delivered.

## [Addition of Interested Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773372 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that wishes to be added to the proceedings as an interested
  person may make a motion for that purpose in accordance with Rules 13
  to 18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether or not to add the person to the
  proceedings, the Tribunal must consider whether

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have a substantial interest in the
    proceedings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their position is already represented in the
    proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their intervention would serve the public interest
    or the interests of justice; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} their participation would assist the Tribunal in
    its determination of the issues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal adds the person to the proceedings,
  the Tribunal must provide directions to them regarding their role in
  the proceedings.

## [Motions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773384 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773385 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of question

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may, by motion, submit any procedural or evidentiary question to
  the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeliness

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except for a motion referred to in subrule 15(1), a
  party must make a motion as soon as feasible after it determines that
  there is a need to submit the question to the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may provide directions to the parties
  regarding the time and manner in which it will hear their arguments
  and receive their evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of defects

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If the content or service
of a document that is filed is defective, the party that filed the
document may make a motion to correct the defect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality order

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make a motion for an order that information contained in a
  filed document, or that the entire document, be kept confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The motion must be made in writing at the time the
  document is filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the notice of motion, the party must identify the
  information or document for which the order is requested and the harm
  that would result from its disclosure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Redacted document

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal orders that information contained in
  a document is to be kept confidential, the party must provide the
  registrar with a version of the document that is redacted to reflect
  the terms of the order and that is marked with the word
  "NON-CONFIDENTIAL" in bold-faced and capitalized letters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking filed document

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal orders that information contained in
  a document, or that the entire document, is to be kept confidential,
  the registrar must mark the filed copy of that document with the word
  "CONFIDENTIAL" in bold-faced and capitalized letters.

### [Oral Motions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773406 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave required

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Leave
  of the Tribunal is required before a motion can be made orally.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to oral motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal must provide each party that wishes to
  respond to an oral motion with an opportunity to do so.

### [Written Motions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773412 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A written motion is made
by filing a notice of motion that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the relief requested by the party and the
  grounds for the motion; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicates which other parties, if any, have consented
  to the relief requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Within 10 days after the
day on which they are served with a notice of motion, a party may file a
response that sets out their position regarding the relief sought by the
moving party.

## [Discovery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773419 .Part}

### [Statement of Particulars]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773420 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-773421 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement for each application

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A party must file a
statement of particulars for each of the following applications made by
the Commissioner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for a determination of whether or not
  a reprisal was taken against the complainant and for an order
  respecting a remedy under paragraph 20.4(1)(a) or (b) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Tribunal has determined that a reprisal was
  taken against the complainant, an application for an order respecting
  disciplinary action under paragraph 20.4(1)(b) of the Act.

#### [Content]{.HTitleText3} {#h-773426 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}All parties

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  statement of particulars must contain the following information and
  documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the party's position regarding the legal issues
    raised in the application and regarding the remedy or disciplinary
    action sought, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the material facts that the party intends to prove
    in the proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} regarding documents that are relevant to a matter
    at issue in the proceedings and that are in the party's power,
    possession or control,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} those documents that the party intends to produce
      in the proceedings,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a list and description of the documents for
      which the party claims privilege, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list and description of the documents that
      are not otherwise referred to in subparagraphs (i) and (ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each document listed under subparagraph
    (c)(ii), the grounds for the privilege claimed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} regarding documents that are relevant to a matter
    at issue in the proceedings and that are no longer in the party's
    power, possession or control,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list and description of the documents, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for each document listed, a description of how
      the party lost power, possession or control of it and, to the best
      of the party's knowledge, its current location;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names of the witnesses, other than expert
    witnesses, that the party intends to call; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the party intends to call an expert witness, a
    summary of the issues that will be the subject of the witnesses'
    testimony.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A statement of particulars filed by the Commissioner
  must also set out the remedy or disciplinary action requested, as the
  case may be, and the basis for that request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complainant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A statement of particulars filed by the complainant
  in relation to an application for remedy may include a request for a
  remedy that is different than that requested by the Commissioner, and
  if so, the statement must also include the basis for that request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employer or former employer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A statement of particulars filed in relation to an
  application for remedy by the complainant's employer or by the person
  or entity that was the complainant's employer at the time the alleged
  reprisal was taken, as the case may be, must --- for each material
  fact raised in the statements of particulars filed by the Commissioner
  and the complainant --- contain a statement indicating whether they
  admit, deny or have no knowledge of that fact.

#### [Time Limits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-773448 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remedy applications

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In relation to an
application for an order respecting a remedy, a party must file its
statement of particulars within the following number of days after the
date of the registrar's notice under Rule 6:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner, within 20 days after
  the date of the notice;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the complainant, within 35 days after
  the date of the notice;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the complainant's employer or the
  person or entity that was the complainant's employer at the time the
  alleged reprisal was taken, as the case may be, within 50 days after
  the date of the notice;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the person or persons identified in
  the application as being the person or persons who may have taken the
  alleged reprisal, within 50 days after the date of the notice; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a party that is added to the
  proceedings under subsection 21.4(3) of the Act,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they are added before the date of the notice,
    within 50 days after the date of the notice, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are added after the date of the notice,
    within the later of 50 days after the date of the notice and 20 days
    after the day on which they are added to the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disciplinary applications --- registrar

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Commissioner makes an application for an order respecting disciplinary
  action under paragraph 20.4(1)(b) of the Act and the Tribunal
  determines that a reprisal was taken against the complainant, the
  registrar must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve each party referred to in subsection 21.5(5)
    of the Act with a copy of the reasons issued by the Tribunal under
    subsection 21.5(3) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after that is completed, provide each party with
    notice that all parties have been served with the reasons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disciplinary applications --- time
  limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In relation to an application for an order respecting
  disciplinary action, a party must file its statement of particulars
  within the following number of days after the date of the registrar's
  notice under paragraph (1)(b):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner, within 20 days
    after the date of the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person designated by the Tribunal
    under subsection 21.5(5) of the Act, within 35 days after the date
    of the notice; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the person against whom the
    disciplinary action would be taken, within 50 days after the date of
    the notice.

### [Commissioner's Reply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773468 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and time limits

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner may file
a reply that sets out the arguments that he or she intends to make in
rebuttal to facts or issues that were raised in another party's
statement of particulars. The reply must be filed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for a remedy, within 65
  days after the date of the registrar's notice under Rule 6; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for disciplinary
  action, within 65 days after the date of the registrar's notice under
  paragraph 22(1)(b).

### [Complainant's Reply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773473 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and time limit

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** In relation to an
application for an order respecting a remedy, the complainant may file a
reply that sets out the arguments that he or she intends to make in
rebuttal to facts or issues that were raised in another party's
statement of particulars. The reply must be filed within 65 days after
the date of the registrar's notice under Rule 6.

### [Supplementary Statement of Particulars]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773476 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content and time limit

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Within 20 days after the
day on which they are served with a statement of particulars, a party
may file a supplementary statement of particulars that sets out any
facts or issues that were not raised in their statement of particulars
and that are necessary to address those raised in another party's
statement of particulars.

### [Expert Reports]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773479 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A party that intends to
call an expert witness at the hearing must file a report prepared for
them by the expert. The report must be signed and dated by the expert
and include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the expert's opinion;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the expert's professional
  qualifications; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the expert's business address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits --- remedy applications

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** In relation to an
application for an order respecting a remedy, a party must file the
expert's report

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner, at least 60 days
  before the day on which the hearing begins;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the complainant, at least 40 days
  before the day on which the hearing begins; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other party, at least 20 days
  before the day on which the hearing begins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits --- disciplinary action
applications

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** In relation to an
application for an order respecting disciplinary action, a party must
file the expert's report

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner, at least 60 days
  before the day on which the hearing begins;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the person designated by the Tribunal
  under subsection 21.5(5) of the Act, at least 40 days before the day
  on which the hearing begins; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the person against whom the
  disciplinary action would be taken, at least 20 days before the day on
  which the hearing begins.

## [Pre-Hearing Conferences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773507 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may schedule
a pre-hearing conference to resolve any procedural or evidentiary
matters related to the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modes

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may conduct
a pre-hearing conference in person, by teleconference, video conference
or any other electronic means of communication.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal schedules
a pre-hearing conference, the registrar must provide each party with
notice of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mode in which the conference will be conducted;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conference's date, time and place; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any motions that the Tribunal intends to hear at the
  conference.

## [Summoning of Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Following a written
request made by a party or an interested person to the registrar, the
Tribunal must issue a subpoena for the attendance of a witness or for
the production of a document at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance in blank

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may issue a
subpoena in blank and the party or interested person to whom it is
issued must complete it and may include any number of names.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attendance

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A witness is not required
to attend a hearing, or to produce a document at the hearing, under a
subpoena that is served on them less than 10 days before the day on
which the hearing begins.

## [Hearing and Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773524 .Part}

### [Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773525 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remote hearings

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may order
that a hearing be conducted in whole or in part by teleconference, video
conference or any other electronic means of communication. If so, the
Tribunal may provide directions to facilitate the conduct of the hearing
using that means of communication.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of hearing

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The registrar must
provide each party and interested person with notice of the date, time
and place of the hearing at least 65 days before the day on which the
hearing begins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Special arrangements

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A party or interested
person that requires an interpreter or any other special arrangements at
the hearing must provide the registrar with written notice of those
requirements at least 10 days before the day on which the hearing
begins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of party or interested person

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** If a party or interested
person fails to appear at the hearing, the Tribunal may proceed with the
hearing in their absence if it is satisfied that they received notice of
the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjournment of hearing

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may adjourn
a hearing, and if so, it must provide directions to each party and
interested person as to the date, time, place and terms of its
continuance.

### [Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-773536 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limits

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Except with leave of the
Tribunal, a party is not permitted to do any of the following at the
hearing:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} raise a position, seek to prove a material fact or to
  introduce a document that was not disclosed, or call a witness ---
  other than an expert witness --- who was not named, in its statement
  of particulars, reply or supplementary statement of particulars;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} introduce an expert report into evidence or call the
  expert as a witness if the expert report was not provided in
  accordance with Rules 26 to 28; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner or the complainant,
  request any remedy or disciplinary action, as the case may be, that
  was not raised in its statement of particulars.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination outside hearing

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that wishes to examine a person who is unable to attend the
  hearing may make a motion to the Tribunal for an order to examine that
  person outside the hearing and to introduce that person's evidence at
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal grants the party's motion, the
  Tribunal must provide directions regarding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time, place and manner of the examination and
    cross-examination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notices to be given to the person being
    examined, the parties and the interested persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the attendance of parties; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the production of any documents requested by the
    examining party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may order that a witness be excluded from the hearing room
  until they are called to give evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Tribunal cannot order the exclusion of
  any witness who is a party or any witness whose presence is essential
  to instruct the party's representative, but the Tribunal may require
  the witness to give evidence before any other witnesses are called to
  give evidence on behalf of that party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication with excluded witnesses
prohibited

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** If the Tribunal orders
that a witness be excluded from the hearing room, it is prohibited for
any person to communicate with the witness regarding the evidence given
during their absence from the hearing room until the witness has been
called and has finished giving evidence.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773558 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) to (4) and
  except for Rules 5, 6 and 21, these Rules apply to the proceedings in
  respect of an application that is made to the Tribunal by the
  Commissioner under subsection 20.4(1) of the Act before the day on
  which these Rules come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The time limits referred to in paragraph 23(a) and
  Rule 24 do not apply to the proceedings in respect of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal directions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal provides a party or interested person
  with directions regarding any matter of procedure that is governed by
  these Rules before the day on which these Rules come into force, they
  must continue to act in accordance with those directions and the Rules
  that would otherwise govern the matter do not apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a Rule contains a time limit that requires the
  doing of a thing within a prescribed number of days before the day on
  which the hearing begins, the time limit does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the day on which these Rules come into
    force, the Tribunal provides the parties with directions regarding
    the time for the doing of the thing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which these Rules come into force,
    there are no more than the prescribed number of days remaining
    before the day on which the hearing begins.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-773570 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
