## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1118684 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[base amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[base amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a cannabis
    product and a listed specified province,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the cannabis product is delivered or made
      available to a person that obtains it otherwise than by means of a
      purchase, the fair market value of the cannabis product; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the amount determined by the
      formula

      ::: {#1118691 .Paragraph}
      \[(A − B) − C\] × \[100%/(100% + D)\]

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the total determined for A in paragraph (a) of the
          definition [[dutiable amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
          section 2 of the Act in respect of the cannabis product,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total determined under section 1 of Schedule 7 to the
          Act in respect of the cannabis product,

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Ontario, the amount determined under section 1 of Schedule 1
            in respect of the cannabis product,

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is New
            Brunswick, the amount determined under section 1 of Schedule
            4 in respect of the cannabis product,

          - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Prince Edward Island, the amount determined under section 1
            of Schedule 6 in respect of the cannabis product,

          - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Saskatchewan, the amount determined under section 1 of
            Schedule 7 in respect of the cannabis product,

          - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Alberta, the amount determined under section 1 of Schedule 8
            in respect of the cannabis product,

          - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Newfoundland and Labrador, the amount determined under
            section 1 of Schedule 9 in respect of the cannabis product,
            and

          - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Nunavut, the amount determined under section 1 of Schedule
            12 in respect of the cannabis product, and

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Ontario, the percentage set out in section 5 of Schedule 1,

          - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is New
            Brunswick, the percentage set out in section 5 of Schedule
            4,

          - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Prince Edward Island, the percentage set out in section 5 of
            Schedule 6,

          - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Saskatchewan, the percentage set out in section 5 of
            Schedule 7,

          - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Alberta, the percentage set out in section 5 of Schedule 8,

          - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Newfoundland and Labrador, the percentage set out in section
            5 of Schedule 9, and

          - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} if the listed specified province is
            Nunavut, the percentage set out in section 5 of Schedule
            12. ([montant de base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
      :::

[[listed specified province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[listed specified province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following specified provinces:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Ontario;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Prince Edward Island;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Saskatchewan;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Alberta;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Newfoundland and Labrador; and

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Nunavut. ([province déterminée
      désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Cannabis Product Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1118719 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cannabis product --- exclusions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
the Act, the following substances, materials or things are not included:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  subsection 1(2) of the [Cannabis
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144), for which a registration
  number has been issued, but not cancelled, under those Regulations;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference standard, being a highly purified and
  standardized form of a given substance used as a measurement base to
  confirm the identity, strength, quality or purity of a substance,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that contains a known quantity or concentration of
    a chemical component of cannabis, such as cannabidiol, cannabidiolic
    acid, THCA or THC,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is designed to be used during the course of a
    chemical or analytical procedure for a medical, laboratory,
    industrial, educational, law administration or enforcement, or
    research purpose, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is not intended to be consumed or
    administered.

  equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of dried cannabis, fresh cannabis,
    cannabis plants and cannabis plant seeds, the total of

      the cannabis product or used in the production of the cannabis
      product,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$0.225 per gram of non-flowering material
      included in the cannabis product or used in the production of the
      cannabis product,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} \$0.75 per viable seed included in the cannabis
      product or used in the production of the cannabis product, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} \$0.75 per vegetative cannabis plant included in
      the cannabis product or used in the production of the cannabis
      product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, \$0.0075 per milligram of the
    total THC of the cannabis product.

- **2** Any cannabis product produced in Canada: the amount obtained by
  multiplying the dutiable amount for the cannabis product by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of dried cannabis, fresh cannabis,
    cannabis plants and cannabis plant seeds, 7.5%; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 0%.

- **3** Any imported cannabis product: the amount obtained by
  multiplying the value of the cannabis product by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of dried cannabis, fresh cannabis,
    cannabis plants and cannabis plant seeds, 7.5%; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 0%.

- **4** Any cannabis product taken for use or unaccounted for: the
  amount obtained by multiplying the fair market value of the cannabis
  product by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of dried cannabis, fresh cannabis,
    cannabis plants and cannabis plant seeds, 7.5%; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 0%.

- **5** Any cannabis product produced in Canada: the amount obtained by
  multiplying the base amount for the cannabis product and for Ontario
  by 3.9%.

- **6** Any cannabis product produced in Canada: the amount obtained by
  multiplying the dutiable amount for the cannabis product by 3.9%.

- **7** Any imported cannabis product: the amount obtained by
  multiplying the value of the cannabis product by 3.9%.

- **8** Any cannabis product taken for use or unaccounted for: the
  amount obtained by multiplying the fair market value of the cannabis
  product by 3.9%.
