## Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2020) {#order-declaring-an-amnesty-period-2020 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2020-97
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2020-05-01

Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2020)

P.C. 2020-299 2020-05-01
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 117.14(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3863_hq_24630){#fn_81000-2-3863_hq_24630-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3863_hq_24631){#fn_81000-2-3863_hq_24631-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Order Declaring an Amnesty Period
(2020)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-97).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[specified device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited device
    referred to in item 4 of Part 4 of the schedule to the [Regulations
    Prescribing Certain Firearms and Other Weapons, Components and Parts
    of Weapons, Accessories, Cartridge Magazines, Ammunition and
    Projectiles as Prohibited or
    Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462). ([dispositif
    visé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[specified firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited firearm
    referred to in any of paragraphs 83(a) to (p) or any of items 87 to
    96 of Part 1 of the schedule to the [Regulations Prescribing Certain
    Firearms and Other Weapons, Components and Parts of Weapons,
    Accessories, Cartridge Magazines, Ammunition and Projectiles as
    Prohibited or Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462). ([arme à feu
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on May 1, 2020, owned or possessed a specified
      firearm and held a licence that was issued under the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of the specified firearm,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold
      the licence while in possession of the specified firearm, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the specified firearm was, on April 30, 2020,
      a restricted firearm,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} held on that day a registration certificate for
        the specified firearm that was issued under the [Firearms
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} was issued, on or after May 1, 2020, a
        registration certificate for the specified firearm that they
        would have been eligible to hold under section 13 of the
        [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) had the firearm remained a
        restricted firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on May 1, 2020, held a licence that was issued
      under the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on or before May 1, 2020, entered into an
      agreement for the transfer, within the meaning of section 21 of
      the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), to them of a specified
      firearm that was, on April 30, 2020, a restricted firearm,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on or after May 1, 2020, was issued a
      registration certificate for the specified firearm that they would
      have been eligible to hold under section 13 of the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) had the firearm remained a restricted
      firearm,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of the specified firearm, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold the
      licence while in possession of the specified firearm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on May 1, 2020, owned or possessed a specified
      device, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of the specified device;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm for the sole purpose of storing
      it on behalf of an owner referred to in paragraph (a) or (b), if
      storage by them is a condition of that owner's licence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence that was issued under the
      [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) that would have permitted them to
      possess the specified firearm according to its classification on
      April 30, 2020;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm in the course of their duties as
      an employee of the Bank of Canada who is responsible for the
      security of the Bank's facilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence that was issued under the
      [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) that would have permitted them to
      possess the specified firearm according to its classification on
      April 30, 2020;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is, for
      the sole purpose of carrying out a repair or adjustment referred
      to in subparagraph (2)(d)(i), in temporary possession of a
      specified firearm referred to in that subparagraph that is owned
      by a person referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence that was issued under the
      [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) that would have permitted them to
      possess the specified firearm according to its classification on
      April 30, 2020;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm or specified device for the sole
      purpose of destroying it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has entered into an agreement with the
      Government of Canada to destroy specified firearms or specified
      devices or is acting in the course of their duties, including as
      an employee or an agent or mandatary, on behalf of a person who
      has entered into such an agreement;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm or specified device for the sole
      purpose of delivering it to a person referred to in paragraph (g)
      to be destroyed, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an organization, within the meaning of
        paragraph (a) of the definition
        [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the
        [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), that is authorized under an Act
        of Parliament to transport firearms, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an employee, a contractor or an agent or
        mandatary of such an organization --- or a subcontractor engaged
        by any such contractor --- if they are acting in the course of
        their duties or under a contract; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm owned by a person referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) for the sole purpose of deactivating it so
      that it is no longer a firearm, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is a [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), that holds a licence issued under that Act
      authorizing the business to possess prohibited firearms for the
      purpose of deactivating them or any employee of such a business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in any of paragraphs
    (1)(a) to (c) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} deactivate the specified firearm so that it is no
      longer a firearm or deactivate the specified device so that it is
      no longer a prohibited device,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm or specified
      device to a police officer for destruction or other disposal,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is not the owner of the specified
      firearm or specified device, deliver it to its owner,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} export the specified firearm or specified device
      in accordance with all applicable legal requirements, including
      the legal requirements of the country to which it is exported,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a
      [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} return the specified firearm or specified
        device to the manufacturer,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm or specified
        device to a person referred to in paragraph (1)(h) for the
        purpose of shipping the specified firearm or specified device to
        be destroyed, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm or specified
        device to a [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
        subsection 2(1) of that Act, for the purpose of shipping the
        specified firearm or specified device to be destroyed or, in the
        case of a specified firearm, to be deactivated,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm or specified
      device by vehicle, for the purpose of doing any of the things
      described in subparagraphs (i) to (v), by a route that, in all the
      circumstances, is reasonably direct, as long as, during
      transportation,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm, it is unloaded and no
        ammunition is present in the vehicle,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the firearm or device is in the trunk of the
        vehicle or, if there is no trunk, the firearm or device is not
        visible from outside the vehicle, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is not left unattended,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in
      paragraph (1)(a) or (b), transport the specified firearm by
      vehicle, in the manner referred to in subparagraph (vi), for the
      purpose of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} storing it in accordance with subparagraph (x),
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} having it stored by or, following storage,
        retrieving it from a person referred to in paragraph (1)(d), if
        the owner first notifies the chief firearms officer for the
        province in which they reside of the transport,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in
      paragraph (1)(a), if the specified firearm was, on April 30, 2020,
      a non-restricted firearm, use it to hunt in the exercise of a
      right recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution
      Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) or to sustain the person or their family,
      until they are able to obtain another firearm for that use,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in paragraph
      (1)(a), transport the specified firearm whose use is permitted
      under subparagraph (viii) in accordance with section 10 of the
      [Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by
      Individuals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209) for the
      purpose of doing the thing described in that subparagraph or
      giving temporary possession of the firearm to a person referred to
      in paragraph (1)(f), and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm or specified device
      before doing any of the things described in subparagraphs (i) to
      (ix), as long as, in the case of a specified firearm, it is stored
      in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} section 5 or 6 of the [Storage, Display,
        Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209), according to the
        classification of the firearm on April 30, 2020, if the person
        is an individual, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} section 5 of the [Storage, Display and
        Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), if the person is a
        [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
        of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(d) to
    do the things described in subparagraphs (a)(iii) and (x);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(e) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} use the specified firearm in the course of their
      duties,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm in accordance
      with section 12 of the [Storage, Display and Transportation of
      Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210) for the purpose of using
      it or giving temporary possession of it to a person referred to
      paragraph (1)(f), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm before doing
      either of the things referred to in subparagraphs (i) and (ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(f) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carry out repairs or adjustments, as necessary
      for safety reasons, of the specified firearm whose use is
      permitted under subparagraph (a)(viii) or (c)(i), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} do the things described in subparagraphs
      (a)(iii) and (x);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(g) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm or specified
      device for the purpose of destroying it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm or specified
      device before transporting it under subparagraph (i) or destroying
      it, as long as, in the case of a specified firearm, it is stored
      in accordance with section 6 of the [Storage, Display and
      Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(h) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm or specified device
      to a person referred to in paragraph (1)(g) for the purpose of
      destroying it,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm or specified
      device for the purpose of delivering it in accordance with
      subparagraph (i), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm or specified
      device before doing any of the things described in
      subparagraph (i) or (ii); and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} permit a business referred to in paragraph (1)(i)
    to possess the specified firearm before deactivating it, as long as
    it is stored in accordance with section 6 of the [Storage, Display
    and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on May 1, 2020 and ends on
  October 30, 2025.
