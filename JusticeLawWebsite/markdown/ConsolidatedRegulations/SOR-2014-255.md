## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806915 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions --- Act and Regulations

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in the Act and in these Regulations.

  [[acknowledgment of liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acknowledgment of liability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written promise to pay the money owing,
        signed by the borrower or his or her agent or other
        representative;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written acknowledgement of the money owing,
        signed by the borrower or his or her agent or other
        representative, whether or not a promise to pay can be implied
        from it and whether or not it contains a refusal to pay;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a part payment by the borrower or his or her
        agent or other representative of any money owing; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of the money owing made by
        the borrower, his or her agent or other representative or the
        trustee or administrator in the course of proceedings under the
        [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) or any other
        legislation dealing with the payment of debts. ([reconnaissance
        de responsabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} other than in section 5, a person who

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a Canadian citizen, a person registered as
          an Indian under the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), a [[permanent
          resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
          of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
          Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a protected person as described in
          subsection 95(2) of that Act,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not a high school student, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is registered with a province as an
          apprentice in an eligible trade; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in section 5, a person who is described in
        subparagraphs (a)(i) and (ii) and who is enrolled in a program
        of study that is mandatory in order to enable the person to
        register as an apprentice with a
        province. ([apprenti]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apprentice loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a debt obligation
      incurred by an eligible apprentice on entering into an apprentice
      loan agreement that is owed to Her Majesty in right of Canada, as
      represented by the Minister. ([prêt aux
      apprentis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom an
      apprentice loan is made under the
      Act. ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible apprentice]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an apprentice
      who is enrolled at a technical training provider for technical
      training in an eligible trade and whose primary occupation during
      the technical training period is to participate in that technical
      training. ([apprenti admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[severe permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[severe permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive,
      learning, communication or sensory impairment --- or a functional
      limitation --- that prevents a person from performing the daily
      activities necessary to participate in the labour force in a
      manner that is [[substantially gainful]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in section 68.1 of the [Canada Pension Plan
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._385), and is expected to
      remain with the person for their expected life. ([invalidité grave
      et permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[technical training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means formal
      instruction or training that is offered by a technical training
      provider and that is determined by a province to be an essential
      element of an apprenticeship program in that province and required
      for the practice of an eligible trade. ([formation
      technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[technical training period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[technical training period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      continuous period of technical training that begins on the first
      day of the month in which technical training begins and ends on
      the last day of the month in which the training ends. ([période de
      formation technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions --- Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Apprentice Loans
      Act](/eng/acts/A-11.9). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[apprentice loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apprentice loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      agreement entered into between an eligible apprentice and the
      Minister under section 4 of the Act that is in the form determined
      by the Minister and includes the social insurance number of the
      apprentice. ([contrat de prêt aux apprentis]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[apprenticeship program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[apprenticeship program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program of
      practical experience, technical training and certification
      examination that must be undertaken --- as determined by the
      province --- by an apprentice in an eligible trade. ([programme
      d'apprentissage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      borrower, means a person who is cohabiting with the borrower in a
      conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confirmation of continued apprenticeship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confirmation of continued
      apprenticeship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means proof that a borrower
      is still registered with a province as an apprentice in an
      eligible trade. ([confirmation d'apprentissage
      continu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[confirmation of enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[confirmation of enrolment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means proof that
      an apprentice is enrolled in technical training with a technical
      training provider. ([confirmation d'inscription]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[family income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[family income]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the aggregate income
      --- including income from employment, social programs, investments
      and monetary gifts --- of the borrower and their spouse or
      common-law partner, as the case may be. ([revenu
      familial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[financial assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
      financial aid provided under the Act, including apprentice
      loans. ([aide financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the one-year period that
      begins on April 1 in any year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[guaranteed student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[guaranteed student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Student Financial
      Assistance Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329). ([prêt
      garanti]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[interest suspension period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[interest suspension period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      period set out in section 8.2 of the Act. ([période de suspension
      des intérêts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanent disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any impairment,
      including a physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive, learning,
      communication or sensory impairment --- or a functional limitation
      --- that restricts the ability of a person to perform the daily
      activities necessary to participate in an apprenticeship program
      or in the labour force and that is expected to remain with the
      person for the person's expected life. ([invalidité
      permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[persistent or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[persistent or prolonged disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      any impairment, including a physical, mental, intellectual,
      cognitive, learning, communication or sensory impairment --- or a
      functional limitation --- that restricts the ability of a person
      to perform the daily activities necessary to participate in an
      apprenticeship program or in the labour force and has lasted, or
      is expected to last, for a period of at least 12 months but is not
      expected to remain with the person for the person's expected life.
      ([invalidité persistante ou prolongée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[provincial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329). ([prêt
      provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[student loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329). ([prêt
      d'études]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Schedule 1 --- Eligible trades

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 2(1) of the Act,
  eligible trades are those that are listed in Schedule 1 to these
  Regulations.

## [Obtaining an Apprentice Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806955 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, the
Minister may make an apprentice loan to an eligible apprentice if the
following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the apprentice has entered into an apprentice loan
  agreement for a technical training period on or before the last day of
  technical training;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} confirmation of enrolment is submitted to the
  Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the event that interest started accruing prior to
  the first day of the current technical training period, the apprentice
  pays to the Minister the unpaid interest accrued under their
  apprentice loan agreement to the day before the first day of that
  technical training period; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the event that interest started accruing prior to
  the day on which the apprentice enters into an apprentice loan
  agreement for the current technical training period that began on the
  day on which interest started accruing, the apprentice

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pays to the Minister the unpaid interest accrued
    under their apprentice loan agreement to the day before the day on
    which the apprentice enters into the current apprentice loan
    agreement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} directs the addition of the interest referred to
    in subparagraph (i) to the outstanding principal.

## [Payment of Principal and Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806977 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment-deferred period

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 8(2) of the Act, the period during which no
  amount on account of principal or interest in respect of an apprentice
  loan is required to be paid by the borrower starts on the day on which
  the loan is made and ends on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the 19th month after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the event that no interest has accrued prior
      to the first day of a technical training period for which
      confirmation of enrolment is submitted to the Minister, the last
      day of that period,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the event that interest started accruing
      prior to the first day of a technical training period for which
      confirmation of enrolment is submitted to the Minister, the last
      day of that period, if the apprentice pays to the Minister the
      unpaid interest accrued to the day before the first day of that
      period,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the event that interest started accruing
      prior to the day on which confirmation of enrolment is submitted
      to the Minister for a technical training period that began prior
      to the day on which interest started accruing, the last day of
      that period if the apprentice

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} pays to the Minister the unpaid interest
        accrued under their apprentice loan agreement to the day before
        the day on which confirmation of enrolment is submitted to the
        Minister, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} directs the addition of the interest referred
        to in subparagraph (i) to the outstanding principal,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the event that no interest has accrued prior
      to the day on which confirmation of continued apprenticeship is
      submitted to the Minister, the day on which the confirmation of
      continued apprenticeship is submitted, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that interest started accruing prior
      to the day on which confirmation of continued apprenticeship is
      submitted to the Minister, the day on which the confirmation of
      continued apprenticeship is submitted, if the apprentice

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} pays to the Minister the unpaid interest
        accrued under their apprentice loan agreement to the day before
        the day on which confirmation of continued apprenticeship is
        submitted, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} directs the addition of the interest referred
        to in subparagraph (i) to the outstanding principal; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the month in which an [[applicable
    day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in any of paragraphs 6(2)(a)
    to (l), occurs in relation to the borrower.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan received erroneously

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 1(b), if an event described in
  paragraph 6(2)(a) occurs in respect of an apprentice loan made to a
  borrower and, subsequent to that event, the borrower erroneously
  receives an apprentice loan, the period during which no amount on
  account of principal or interest in respect of an apprentice loan is
  required to be paid by the borrower ends on the last day of the
  technical training period for which the apprentice loan was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- continuation of
  payment-deferred period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), if, before the last day of
  the apprenticeship program in which the borrower is registered at the
  time the event occurs, an event described in any of paragraphs 6(2)(b)
  to (f) occurs in respect of an apprentice loan made to a borrower, the
  period during which no amount on account of principal or interest in
  respect of an apprentice loan is required to be paid ends on the
  earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the apprenticeship program, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the 36th month after the applicable
    day for the event or, if that day falls during a technical training
    period, the last day of that period.

## [Special Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806980 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount determined by Minister

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 7 of the Act, the special payment amount for a
  province as determined by the Minister after consultation with the
  Chief Statistician of Canada is the amount for the fiscal year that is
  obtained by multiplying the total costs for the fiscal year for
  provinces where apprentices registered with those provinces are able
  to enter into apprentice loan agreements for the fiscal year, by the
  ratio of the estimated number of persons in the province in question
  who are at least 16 years of age but less than 65, to the estimated
  number of persons of those ages in provinces where apprentices
  registered with those provinces are able to enter into apprentice loan
  agreements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, total costs for a fiscal year means
  the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#806986 .Subsection}
  A + B - C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated aggregate of all amounts paid by the Minister in
      that fiscal year to service providers under the Act and to
      collection agencies in respect of apprentice loans;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated aggregate of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of interest calculated at the rate
        of interest referred to in subsection (3) in that fiscal year in
        respect of outstanding apprentice loans referred to in the
        description of A made under an apprentice loan agreement,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which the outstanding principal
        in respect of all apprentice loans referred to in paragraph (a)
        has been reduced in that fiscal year in accordance with these
        Regulations,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the outstanding principal and
        interest in respect of all apprentice loans referred to in
        paragraph (a) for which the borrower's obligations have
        terminated in that fiscal year as a result of the death or
        severe permanent disability of the borrower, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the outstanding principal and
        interest in respect of all apprentice loans referred to in
        paragraph (a) for which the Minister has commenced collection
        action in that fiscal year, minus the amount of the outstanding
        principal and interest in respect of apprentice loans referred
        to in that paragraph for which the Minister has ended collection
        action in that fiscal year due to the occurrence of an event
        described in section 7 that resulted in the removal of
        restrictions to financial assistance; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated aggregate of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of interest received by or on behalf
        of Her Majesty in right of Canada in that fiscal year in respect
        of apprentice loans, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amounts received by or on behalf of Her
        Majesty in right of Canada in that fiscal year pursuant to
        collection action taken by the Minister in respect of apprentice
        loans.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rate of interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rate of interest for the purposes of
  paragraph (a) of the description of B in subsection (2) is the average
  percentage yield, during the fiscal year in question, of Benchmark
  Bonds issued by the Government of Canada maturing in 10 years, as
  published by the Bank of Canada.

## [Restrictions on Obtaining Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of loan

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  apprentice loan is to be denied to an eligible apprentice who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the 36 months before the day on which they make
    their application for an apprentice loan, has been in default --- in
    at least three instances --- for over 90 days in paying an
    instalment under at least three loans or other debts each in an
    amount over \$1000; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is in receipt of a student loan for the same
    technical training period.

- Definition of [[applicable day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, [[applicable
  day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the Minister is informed that the borrower has
    failed to make a payment within the two-month period after the day
    on which the payment is required under their apprentice loan
    agreement or these Regulations and they do not fulfil the conditions
    of section 2, the day following the day on which the borrower must
    begin to repay the principal amount of any apprentice loan made to
    them, and any interest, in accordance with section 4;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower makes an assignment under the
    [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3) that is filed and not
    cancelled, is deemed under that Act to have made an assignment, or
    is the subject of a receiving order, the earlier of the day on which
    a receiving order is made or the assignment is filed with the
    official receiver;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower makes a proposal under Division I
    of Part III of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3)
    that is approved by a court under that Act, the day on which that
    proposal is approved;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower makes a consumer proposal under
    Division II of Part III of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3) that is approved or deemed approved by a court
    under that Act, the day on which the consumer proposal is approved
    or deemed approved;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower applies for a consolidation order
    under Part X of the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3)
    that includes an apprentice loan, the day on which that order is
    issued;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower seeks relief under a provincial law
    relating to the orderly payment of debts that includes an apprentice
    loan, the day on which the document seeking relief is filed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower is, by reason of his or her conduct
    in obtaining or repaying an apprentice loan, found guilty of an
    offence under any Act of Parliament, the day of the finding of
    guilt;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower fails to comply with subsection
    11(1) or 13(1), section 15 or subsection 17(3), the 30th day after
    the last day of the applicable repayment assistance period;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the borrower has been granted repayment
    assistance under subsection 13(2), the day on which the repayment
    assistance period begins;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if no amount on account of principal or interest in
    respect of an apprentice loan was required to be paid by a borrower
    for a cumulative period of six years, the day after the last day of
    that period;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if any of paragraphs 15(1)(b) to (i) of the [Canada
    Student Financial Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-329) apply to the borrower, the
    applicable day set out in the paragraph in question;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if a measure is taken in respect of the borrower
    under subsection 20(1) of the Act or under section 17.1 of the
    [Canada Student Financial Assistance Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7), the day
    after the day on which 60 days' notice given under subsection 20(2)
    of the Act or subsection 17.1(3) of the [Canada Student Financial
    Assistance Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7) expires;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if five disbursements have already been made to the
    borrower under these Regulations, the day after the day on which the
    borrower received the last disbursement under an apprentice loan
    agreement; or

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the obligations of the borrower
    are terminated under section 10 of the Act or under section 11 or
    11.1 of the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
    Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Denial of loan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (5) and (7), if an event
  described in any of paragraphs (2)(a) to (n) occurs, the Minister may,
  effective on the applicable day for that event, deny the borrower a
  new apprentice loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of repayment assistance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an event described in paragraph 2(g) or (h)
  occurs, the Minister must terminate any repayment assistance granted
  to the borrower under section 10 or 12 and refuse to grant further
  repayment assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan received erroneously

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an event described in paragraph (2)(a) occurs in
  respect of an apprentice loan made to a borrower and, subsequent to
  that event, the borrower erroneously receives an apprentice loan, the
  borrower is entitled to the apprentice loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New loan

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If, before the last day of the apprenticeship program
  in which the borrower is registered at the time the event occurs, an
  event described in any of paragraphs (2)(b) to (f) occurs in respect
  of an apprentice loan made to a borrower, the borrower is entitled, if
  otherwise eligible, to a new apprentice loan for that apprenticeship
  program.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection (3) measure delayed

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If the borrower receives a new apprentice loan under
  subsection (6), the measure referred to in subsection (3) takes effect
  on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the apprenticeship program, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day that is three years after the applicable
    day for the event or, if that day falls during a technical training
    period, the last day of that period.

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month that is six months before
  the day on which the borrower applies for the assistance, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first day of the month on which the borrower must
  begin to repay the principal amount of any apprentice loan made to
  them, and any interest, under section 4.

### [Condition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807159 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unpaid accrued interest

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If any accrued interest
remains unpaid on the day on which a repayment assistance period begins,
the borrower must, on or before 30 days after the day on which the
repayment assistance period ends,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the interest to the Minister; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they have not already done so, enter into a
  revised apprentice loan agreement for the payment of up to three
  months of the interest accrued and pay the Minister any remaining
  interest accrued.

### [Reconsideration of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807164 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, on the written request of the borrower and based on
  documentary evidence provided by the borrower, reconsider a borrower's
  application for repayment assistance if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application was rejected for the sole reason
    that the borrower did not meet the condition set out in paragraph
    10(1)(c) or 12(1)(c); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unforeseen and unavoidable circumstances beyond the
    control of the borrower or their spouse or common-law partner have
    led to the borrower incurring extraordinary expenses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide notice of his or her
  determination to the borrower.

### [Borrower Error]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807172 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation or reduction of repayment
assistance

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  repayment assistance is granted based on an error by the borrower in
  their application for the assistance or for reconsideration, the
  Minister may cancel the assistance or reduce the amount of assistance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide notice of the cancellation
  or reduction to the borrower, specifying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the repayment assistance is to be
    cancelled or reduced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation of borrower

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The borrower must, within 30 days after the date of
  the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} repay to the Minister the amount of repayment
    assistance that the borrower was not entitled to receive; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into a revised apprentice loan agreement for
    repayment of the amount referred to in paragraph (a).

### [Effect on Apprentice Loan Agreement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807184 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If repayment assistance
is provided to a borrower, the apprentice loan agreement in effect
between the borrower and the Minister on the day on which the borrower
applies for that assistance is suspended until the earlier of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which repayment assistance is terminated
  in accordance with section 6, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day after the last day of the repayment
  assistance period.

## [Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807189 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of measures

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are measures for the purposes of subsection 20(1) of the
  Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} denial of financial assistance for a specified
    period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2023-273, s. 25\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} denial, for a specified period, of deferral of
    payments of principal or interest, or termination of any such
    deferral;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} denial, for a specified period, of any agreement to
    allow repayment of an apprentice loan on an income-contingent basis
    as provided for in sections 10 and 12, or termination of any such
    agreement; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} imposition of a requirement for repayment, without
    delay, of any outstanding amount of an apprentice loan obtained by
    reason of a false statement or misrepresentation or the provision of
    false or misleading information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the following are the
  specified periods for the purposes of paragraphs (1)(a), (c) and (d):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person is awarded an amount of financial
    assistance to which they were not entitled,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one year, if the amount is less than \$4,000,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} two years, if the amount is \$4,000 or more and
      less than \$6,000,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} three years, if the amount is \$6,000 or more
      and less than \$8,000,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} four years, if the amount is \$8,000 or more and
      less than \$10,000, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} five years, if the amount is \$10,000 or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is not an apprentice, five years; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a measure described in subsection (1) has
    already been taken in respect of a person under subsection 20(1) of
    the Act, five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified period --- five years

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If more than one period applies to a person under
  subsection (2), the specified period is five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation or prescription --- measures

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not take any measure under
  subsection (1) later than six years after the day on which he or she
  becomes aware of the false statement or misinterpretation or the false
  or misleading information.
