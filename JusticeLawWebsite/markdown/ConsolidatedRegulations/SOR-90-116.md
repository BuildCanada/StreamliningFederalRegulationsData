## Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Police Information Retrieval System Fees Order {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-police-information-retrieval-system-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-116
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-02-02

Order Prescribing the Fee or Charge To Be Paid by a Province or
Municipality for the Use, by a Police Force for which the Province or
Municipality is Responsible, of the Police Information Retrieval System
Owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1989-1899 of September 21, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-90-116_e_hq_5205){#fn_SOR-90-116_e_hq_5205-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee or charge to be paid
by a province or municipality for the use, by a police force for which
the province or municipality is responsible, of the Police Information
Retrieval System owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-116).

  [[Return to footnote

  :::

Ottawa, January 26, 1990

::: FlushRight
PIERRE BLAIS
:::

::: FlushRight
*Solicitor General of Canada*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921502 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police, Police Information Retrieval System
Fees Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-116).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-921504 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A province or municipality
shall pay to the Receiver General for the use, by a police force for
which the province or municipality is responsible, of the Police
Information Retrieval System owned by the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
a fee of \$450 per year in respect of each sworn police officer in the
police force.
