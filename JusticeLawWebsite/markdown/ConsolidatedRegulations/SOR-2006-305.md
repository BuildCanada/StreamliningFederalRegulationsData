## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726569 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

## [Distributing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-726571 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of the Act and subject to subsection (2), any one of the
  following constitutes a distributing company or a distributing
  insurance holding company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a company or an insurance holding company that is a
    reporting issuer under any legislation that is set out in column 2
    of the schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company or an insurance holding
    company that is not a reporting issuer referred to in paragraph (a),
    a company or an insurance holding company

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that has filed a prospectus or registration
      statement under provincial or foreign legislation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any of the securities of which are listed and
      posted for trading on a stock exchange in or outside Canada, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is involved in, is formed for, results
      from or is continued after an amalgamation, a reorganization, an
      arrangement or a statutory procedure, if one of the participating
      bodies corporate is an entity referred to in subparagraph (i) or
      (ii).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company or an insurance holding company that is
  subject to an exemption under provincial securities legislation, or to
  an order of the relevant provincial securities regulator that provides
  that the company or insurance holding company is not a reporting
  issuer for the purposes of the applicable legislation, is not a
  distributing company or a distributing insurance holding company for
  the purpose of subsection (1).
