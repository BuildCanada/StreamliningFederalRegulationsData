## Residential Detectors Regulations {#residential-detectors-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-190
:::

[CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/C-1.68)

Registration 2016-06-22

Residential Detectors Regulations

P.C. 2016-617 2016-06-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the [Canada Consumer
Product Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2016-190_e_hq_18685){#fn_SOR-2016-190_e_hq_18685-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Residential Detectors
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-190).

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832802 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residential detectors

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A residential detector set
out in column 1 of the table to this section must comply with the
standard set out in column 2, without regard to any other standard to
which it refers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official languages

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The information required
under these Regulations to be provided on the label of a residential
detector or in instructions intended for consumers must appear in
English and in French.
