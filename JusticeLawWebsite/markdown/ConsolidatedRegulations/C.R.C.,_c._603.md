## Import Certificate Regulations {#import-certificate-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 603
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Regulations Respecting Import Certificates
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558534 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Import Certificate
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._603).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558536 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to any import certificate that is issued by or on
  behalf of the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce concerning
  goods for which such a certificate is required by the country of
  export before it will allow their export to Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an applicant has applied for an import
  certificate, it shall be deemed, for the purpose of these Regulations
  and the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), that the
  import certificate is required by the exporting country he has named
  in his application before it will allow the export of the goods to
  Canada.

## [Application for Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558540 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an import certificate shall be made only by a resident
  of Canada, that is to say, if the applicant is a natural person, he
  shall be a person who ordinarily resides in Canada and, if the
  applicant is a corporation, it shall be a corporation having its head
  office in Canada or operating a branch office in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall apply for an import certificate
  unless he intends to import the goods into Canada.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an import certificate shall be made on a form provided
  by the Department of Industry, Trade and Commerce and procurable from
  the Export and Import Permits Division of that Department.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant shall furnish all information required
  in the application form and, without restricting the generality of the
  foregoing, shall, in particular,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the goods concerned in sufficient detail
    as to disclose their true identity and, in so doing, avoid the use
    of trade names, technical names or general terms that do not
    adequately describe the goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} truthfully certify that he intends to import those
    goods into Canada and that he will not cause or assist in their
    disposal or diversion to any other person during transit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant shall furnish such other information, in
  addition to that given in the application form, as may be required by
  or on behalf of the Chief of the Export and Import Permits Division.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The completed application form, together with such
  other information as may be required, shall be sent to the Export and
  Import Permits Division, Department of Industry, Trade and Commerce,
  Ottawa, Canada.

## [Issue of Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558551 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When an
  application for an import certificate has been signed by the Chief of
  the Export and Import Permits Division or otherwise signed by or on
  behalf of the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, the
  application form, with all information and certifications by the
  applicant appearing therein, becomes an import certificate and shall
  not, thereafter, be altered except by or on behalf of the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The import certificate and a copy thereof shall be
  sent to the applicant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In any proceedings under the [Export and Import
  Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), a document, purporting to be certified
  by the Chief of the Export and Import Permits Division to be a true
  copy of an import certificate, is [prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  evidence of the original document of which it purports to be a copy
  and that the same was issued at the time stated in the certificate and
  is signed, certified, attested or executed by the persons by whom and
  in the manner in which the same purports to be signed, certified,
  attested or executed as shown or appearing from such certified copy.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558556 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise authorized
by the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce or on his behalf by the
Chief of the Export and Import Permits Division or other person
authorized by the Minister, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to goods for which an import certificate
  has been issued and prior to their being received by the person to
  whom the certificate was issued, cause or assist in the disposal or
  diversion of such goods to any person other than the person to whom
  the certificate was issued;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use an import certificate in any manner other than in
  accordance with the information and certifications appearing in the
  import certificate; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} being the person to whom an import certificate was
  issued, refuse or otherwise fail to make any report or provide any
  information required by or under these Regulations.

## [Duration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558561 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An import certificate is
not valid beyond the expiry date specified therein.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558563 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The following reports
shall, where applicable, be made, in writing, to the Chief of the Export
and Import Permits Division by a person to whom an import certificate
has been issued:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an immediate report, with full particulars, of any
  information he receives that the certificate or any part of the goods
  to which it relates has been or will be dealt with by the exporter or
  any other person otherwise than in accordance with the terms of the
  certificate or the requirements of these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a report of the arrival of the goods in Canada,
  together with a true copy of the customs entry document covering them
  and bearing the stamp of the collector of customs at the Canadian port
  of entry;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a report of any proposal to export the goods from
  Canada; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the goods have not arrived in Canada before the
  expiry date of the certificate, a report of that fact together with
  such information as he may have as to the reasons for the delay and
  the whereabouts of the goods.

## [Delivery Verification Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558569 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Delivery
  verification certificates of the delivery in Canada of goods imported
  into Canada may be granted by or on behalf of the Minister of
  Industry, Trade and Commerce to any person who requests such
  certificate in order to comply with or assist his exporter in
  complying with the requirements of the country of export of the goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Delivery verification certificates shall not be
  issued unless a report of the arrival of the goods in Canada, together
  with a true copy of the customs entry documents covering them and
  bearing the stamp of the collector of customs at the Canadian port of
  entry, has been made, in writing, to the Chief of the Export and
  Import Permits Division.
