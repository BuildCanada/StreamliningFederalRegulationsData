## Maximum Amounts for Informal Procedure Regulations {#maximum-amounts-for-informal-procedure-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-295
:::

[TAX COURT OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/T-2)

Registration 1993-06-08

Regulations Increasing the Maximum Amounts that Apply in Respect of
Appeals under the Informal Procedure

P.C. 1993-1205  1993-06-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 18.27[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-295_e_hq_6257){#fn_SOR-93-295_e_hq_6257-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Tax Court of Canada Act](/eng/acts/T-2), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations increasing the maximum amounts that apply
in respect of appeals under the informal
procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-295).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952581 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Maximum Amounts for Informal Procedure
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-295).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952583 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tax Court of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/T-2). (*Loi*)

## [Maximum Amounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952585 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of \$7,000
referred to in paragraph 18(1)(a), section 18.1, subsections 18.11(2)
and (3) and sections 18.12 and 18.13 of the Act is increased to
\$12,000.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of \$14,000
referred to in paragraph 18(1)(b) and sections 18.1, 18.12 and 18.13 of
the Act is increased to \$24,000.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952588 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Sections 3 and 4 apply in
respect of appeals instituted on or after September 1, 1993.
