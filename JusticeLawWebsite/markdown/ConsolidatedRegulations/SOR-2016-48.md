## Ste. Anne's Hospital Divestiture Regulations {#ste.-annes-hospital-divestiture-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-48
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 2016-03-17

Ste. Anne's Hospital Divestiture Regulations
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0ELEAA} and subsection
42.1(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EJEAA} of the
[Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EGEAA} and paragraph 7(2)(a) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d){#fn_d-ID0EDEAA}, makes the annexed [Ste.
Anne's Hospital Divestiture Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-48).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to a person who, by reason of an agreement between
  the Government of Canada, the Government of Quebec and the [Centre
  intégré universitaire de santé et de services sociaux de
  l'Ouest-de-l'Île-de-Montréal]{.otherLang lang="fr"} (the "Centre"),
  dated April 16, 2015, ceases to be employed in the public service and
  becomes employed by the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- person re-employed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, these Regulations do not apply to a person
  who is re-employed by the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- survivor and children

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Moreover, sections 2, 3 and 7 do not apply to the
  survivor and children of a person who has received a return of
  contributions or has exercised an option under subsection 6(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 10(5) of Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the [Public Service Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/P-36) (the "Act"), the one-year period referred to in
paragraph (a) of that subsection begins on the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits --- survivor and children

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and children
of a person who dies while employed by the Centre are entitled to one of
the following benefits to which they would have been entitled if the
person had been employed in the public service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death benefit under subsection 12(8) or 12.1(8)
  of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allowances referred to in subsection 13(3) or
  13.001(3) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections 12 to 13.001 of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.001 of the Act, a person's age when they cease to be
employed in the public service is their age on the day on which they
cease to be employed by the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service that begins on the day on which a person ceases to be
employed in the public service and ends on the day on which they cease
to be employed by the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When certain provisions are applicable

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12 to 13.01 of the Act only apply to a person beginning on the day on
  which they cease to be employed by the Centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if on or after April 1, 2016 the person
  would be entitled to a return of contributions under subsection 12(3)
  or 12.1(4) of the Act, were it not for these Regulations, they may
  request, in writing, the return of contributions no later than one
  year after the day on which they cease to be employed in the public
  service and become employed by the Centre, and if, in the same
  circumstances, they would be entitled to exercise an option under
  section 13.01 of the Act, they may exercise the option within the same
  period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 26(2) of Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a person is deemed to cease to be employed
in the public service on the day on which they cease to be employed by
the Centre.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 2016.
