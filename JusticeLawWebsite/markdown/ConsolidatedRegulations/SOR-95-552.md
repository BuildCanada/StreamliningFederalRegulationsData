## Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1995, No. 3 {#government-employees-land-acquisition-order-1995-no.-3 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-552
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1995-11-23

Order Authorizing a Certain Employee of the Government of Canada to
Acquire an Interest in Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1995-1949  1995-11-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 29(1)(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing a certain employee
of the Government of Canada to acquire an interest in territorial lands
in the Northwest Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-478).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976462 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1995, No.
3](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-552).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976464 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The employee of the
Government of Canada named in the schedule is hereby authorized to
acquire an interest in territorial lands in the Northwest Territories as
described in the schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#976466 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-976467 .scheduleLabel}
:::

To Frank Albert Tsetso, of the Village of Fort Simpson in the Northwest
Territories, Peace Officer, a member of the Royal Canadian Mounted
Police, to lease, for a trapper's cabin, the whole of a parcel of land
situated on the northerly shore of Cli Lake, at approximately 61°59′
North Latitude and 123°17′ West Longitude, in Quad numbered 95 G/14 in
the Northwest Territories, as said parcel is shown outlined in red on a
sketch plan on file 33-1-12 in the Land Management Division of the
Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development at Ottawa, saving
and excepting therefrom and reserving thereout all mines and minerals,
including hydrocarbons, whether solid, liquid or gaseous and the right
to work them.
