## Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee Order {#canadian-heraldic-authority-fee-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-168
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-02-28

Order Prescribing the Fee for Processing Applications Submitted to the
Canadian Heraldic Authority for the Grant of Coats of Arms
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Prime Minister, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1990-2224 of
October 11, 1990[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-168_e_hq_5227){#fn_SOR-91-168_e_hq_5227-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fee for processing

of coats of arms](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-168).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932960 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-168).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932962 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable by an
applicant for the processing of an application submitted to the Canadian
Heraldic Authority for the grant of a coat of arms is \$435.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The fee referred to in
section 2 shall be paid by cheque payable to the Receiver General for
Canada and shall be included with a letter acknowledging receipt of
notice from the Canadian Heraldic Authority that a warrant has issued
authorizing the grant of a coat of arms to the applicant.
