## Critical Habitat of the Northern Bottlenose Whale (Hyperoodon ampullatus) Scotian Shelf Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-northern-bottlenose-whale-hyperoodon-ampullatus-scotian-shelf-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-157
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2018-07-06

Critical Habitat of the Northern Bottlenose Whale (Hyperoodon
ampullatus) Scotian Shelf Population Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Northern Bottlenose Whale ([Hyperoodon
ampullatus]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Scotian Shelf population is a
wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of
Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBFA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a
place referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBDA} of that Act and, under
subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBDA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Northern Bottlenose Whale (Hyperoodon ampullatus) Scotian
Shelf Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-157).

Ottawa, July 3, 2018

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Northern Bottlenose Whale ([Hyperoodon ampullatus]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Scotian Shelf population --- which is identified in the
recovery strategy for that species that is included in the Species at
Risk Public Registry --- other than the portion of that critical habitat
that is in a place referred to in subsection 58(2) of that Act, more
specifically, in Zone 1 of the Gully Marine Protected Area as depicted
in Schedule 2 to the [Gully Marine Protected Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-112).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into Force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
