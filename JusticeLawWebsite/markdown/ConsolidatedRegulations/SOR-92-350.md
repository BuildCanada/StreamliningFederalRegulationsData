## Protection of Assets (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#protection-of-assets-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-350
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-06-04

Regulations Respecting the Protection and Maintenance of Assets of
Companies

P.C. 1992-1179  1992-06-04
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 531(g) of the [Trust and
Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBAA}, is pleased hereby to
revoke the Protection of Securities (Loan Companies) Regulations,
C.R.C., c. 1031 and the Protection of Securities (Trust Companies)
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1568, and to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the protection and maintenance of assets of
companies](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-353) in substitution therefor.

  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944986 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Protection of Assets (Trust and Loan Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-350).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944988 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract of insurance by which
    one party agrees to indemnify another party for loss arising out of
    the act of a third party; ([caution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company, means a
    negotiable security owned or held by the company or held by the
    company in trust, but does not include a security issued by the
    company; ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a purchase, sale,
    redemption, exchange, transfer or assignment of, or other
    transaction in respect of, a security. ([opération sur
    titres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944993 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a company

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is carrying on business in a jurisdiction in
  compliance with a law of that jurisdiction, to the extent that that
  law is inconsistent with these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the extent that they are inconsistent with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a power conferred or a duty imposed on the company
    in respect of the administration of an estate or trust, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the discharge of the company's responsibilities in
    acting as an agent.

## [Safeguarding of Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944999 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A company shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a record in writing of procedures to be
  followed in the handling and safeguarding of assets owned or held by
  the company; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform every director, officer, employee and agent of
  the company who has access to, or is involved in the handling and
  safeguarding of, assets owned or held by the company of those
  procedures.

## [Safeguarding of Securities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945003 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A company shall maintain an
up-to-date record that identifies every security.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 7, a company shall ensure that every
  security is kept securely, in a manner that prevents unauthorized
  access to the security, in the custody of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is authorized to act as a custodian
    of securities or as a depository or clearing agency for securities
    by a law of the jurisdiction in which the entity is carrying on
    business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the control of the government of a
    jurisdiction in which the company is carrying on business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pledged as collateral for the indebtedness or
    potential indebtedness of the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} loaned to a person pursuant to a written agreement;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in transit.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A company shall not place a
security in the custody of an entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b)
unless the company has entered into a written custodial agreement with
that entity.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A company shall, on a daily
basis, deposit any net amount received by the company as a result of any
security transaction in an account kept by the company

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a financial institution that is authorized to
  accept deposits by a law of the jurisdiction where the financial
  institution is carrying on business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with a trust company that is authorized to hold money
  in trust by a law of the jurisdiction where the trust company carries
  on business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} with the government of the jurisdiction in which the
  company is carrying on business, or with an agency thereof that is
  authorized to act as a custodian of securities; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} with CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc.

## [Registration of a Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945023 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a company shall ensure that every security
  is registered in the company's name in the register of the entity that
  issued the security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cannot be registered in the company's name for any
    reason that is beyond the control of that company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is under the control of the government of a
    jurisdiction in which the company is carrying on business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is held by the company as collateral or for
    safekeeping;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is registered in the name of a nominee of the
    company or of an entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is held under a book entry certificateless or
    immobilization system; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is held temporarily by an agent of the company, a
    liquidator, a trustee or the issuer of the security for purposes of
    reorganization, amalgamation, liquidation or voting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a security is otherwise protected against loss,
  fraud, theft and destruction, the company may hold the security

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in bearer form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in registered form in a name other than the
    company's name.

## [Bonding and Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945036 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall acquire and at all times maintain one or more bonds
  issued by an entity authorized to insure risks under the laws of the
  jurisdiction in which the entity carries on business to indemnify the
  company for any loss in respect of assets owned or held by that
  company arising out of a dishonest or criminal act of an officer or
  employee of that company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall, in respect of assets owned or held
  by the company, acquire and at all times maintain one or more
  insurance policies to indemnify that company for loss arising out of
  damage to, or the destruction or mysterious disappearance of, those
  assets or out of any other usual contingency.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall
provide that the bond or insurance policy shall not be cancelled or
terminated by the insurer or the insured until at least 30 days after
the receipt by the Superintendent of a written notice from the insurer
or the insured, as the case may be, of its intention to cancel or
terminate the bond or insurance policy.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall be in
an amount that is established by the directors of the company, having
regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature and value of the assets owned or held by
  the company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arrangements and procedures applicable to the
  handling and safeguarding of the assets owned or held by the company;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other factors that might affect the extent of any
  loss that the company might sustain.
