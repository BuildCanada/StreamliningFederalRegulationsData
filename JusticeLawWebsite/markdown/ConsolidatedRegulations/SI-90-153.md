## Prime Minister Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Canadian Heraldic Authority) {#prime-minister-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order-canadian-heraldic-authority .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-153
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-10-24

Prime Minister Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Canadian Heraldic
Authority)

P.C. 1990-2224  1990-10-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of
services provided by the Canadian Heraldic Authority in respect of the
processing of applications for the grant of coats of arms should be
borne by the person to whom they are provided;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
is pleased hereby to authorize the Prime Minister to prescribe, by
order, the fee for processing applications submitted to the Canadian
Heraldic Authority for the grant of coats of arms.
