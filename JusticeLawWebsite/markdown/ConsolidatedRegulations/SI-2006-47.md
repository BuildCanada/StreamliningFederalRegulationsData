## Order Tranferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Control and Supervision of the Office of Indian Residential Schools Resolution of Canada {#order-tranferring-from-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-the-minister-of-canadian-heritage-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-indian-residential-schools-resolution-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-47
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Tranferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Control and
Supervision of the Office of Indian Residential Schools Resolution of
Canada

P.C. 2006-89 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBBA} of the
  [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and
  Emergency Preparedness to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the
  control and supervision of the portion of the federal public
  administration known as the Office of Indian Residential Schools
  Resolution of Canada, and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Canadian Heritage shall
  preside over the Office of Indian Residential Schools Resolution of
  Canada,

effective February 6, 2006.
