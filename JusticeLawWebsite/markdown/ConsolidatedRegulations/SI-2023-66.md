## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessment-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-66
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2023-11-08

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessment Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Act

P.C. 2023-1054 2023-10-20
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, acknowledges receipt, on the making of
this Order, of the assessment done pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4640-E_hq_26007){#fn_81000-2-4640-E_hq_26007-ID0EBBAA}
by the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada
(COSEWIC) with respect to the status of the species set out in the
annexed schedule.
