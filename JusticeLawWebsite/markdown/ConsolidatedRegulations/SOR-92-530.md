## Regulatory Capital (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#regulatory-capital-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-530
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-08-31

Regulations Defining the Regulatory Capital of a Company

P.C. 1992-1849  1992-08-27
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to the definition [[regulatory
capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 and section 531 of the [Trust
and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations defining the regulatory capital of a
company](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 45
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946708 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Regulatory Capital (Trust and Loan Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-530).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946710 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by an insurance company or a
      securities dealer; ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    that is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province; ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by a company, that is held by a
    person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the company;
      ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act.
    ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Regulatory Capital]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946722 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, the regulatory capital of a company,
  at a particular time, is the aggregate of the amounts of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shareholders equity,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} minority interests, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subordinated indebtedness

  that would be reported in the financial statements of the company
  prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
  and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in subsection
  313(4) of the Act, less the amount of goodwill that would be included
  in those financial statements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of regulatory capital under
  subsection (1), an amount may be included in respect of a security
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security is, by its terms, subordinate in right
    of payment to all deposit liabilities and all other liabilities of
    the entity that issued the security, other than liabilities that, by
    their terms, rank equally with, or are subordinate to, that
    security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security is issued and fully paid-up; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subordinated indebtedness or a
    preferred share, the security

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has an initial minimum term of five years or more
      or has no term, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cannot be redeemed or purchased for cancellation
      in the first five years after it is issued.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-64, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 464 to 466 of the Act, the regulatory capital of
  a company is the amount calculated under section 3, less the aggregate
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amounts of shareholders' equity and
    subordinated indebtedness of an insurance company or securities
    dealer controlled by the company, or an entity controlled by such an
    insurance company or securities dealer, that are included in the
    amount of regulatory capital of the company calculated pursuant to
    section 3,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of investments, other than shareholders'
    equity or subordinated indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), of
    the company or a designated entity controlled by the company in an
    insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the company, or
    in an entity controlled by such an insurance company or securities
    dealer, where the investments are part of the capital of the
    insurance company or securities dealer,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of loans, other than subordinated
    indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), made by the company or by
    a designated entity controlled by the company to an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the company, or to an
    entity controlled by such an insurance company or securities dealer,
    where the loans are part of the capital of the insurance company or
    securities dealer, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of a minority interest in an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the company, or in an
    entity controlled by such an insurance company or securities dealer,
    that is included in the amount of regulatory capital of the company
    calculated pursuant to section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (1)(b)and (c),
  [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an insurance company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, regulatory
    capital within the meaning of the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other insurance company or a
    securities dealer, an amount that would be treated as capital by a
    regulatory body that regulates that insurance company or securities
    dealer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-425, s. 1
