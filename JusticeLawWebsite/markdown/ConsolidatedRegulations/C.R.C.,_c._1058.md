## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484383 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-50, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[book cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[book cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of plant, the
    amount that is recorded in a plant account without any deduction for
    accumulated depreciation or amortization or for any other
    purpose; ([valeur comptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission referred to
    in subsection 26(1) of the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1); ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contingent assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contingent assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means assets without known
    value to the company until fulfilment of conditions regarded as
    uncertain; ([actif éventuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contingent liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contingent liabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means liabilities
    that may, under certain conditions, become obligations of the
    company but are not direct or assumed obligations on the date of the
    balance sheet; ([passif éventuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to the
    accounts of a company, the calendar year or the fiscal year approved
    by the Commission for the accounts of the company; ([exercice
    financier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minor item of plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor item of plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an associated part
    or item included within a plant unit; ([article d'installation
    accessoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net salvage value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net salvage value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means salvage value minus
    any removal costs; ([valeur nette de récupération]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company authorized by the
    Commission to construct or operate an oil pipeline that is owned in
    undivided interest; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[other plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[other plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any installation or
    property that is entirely distinct from and is not operated in
    connection with the transportation plant of the company; ([autre
    installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an oil pipeline
    company, any installation or property the cost of which is to be
    recorded in the Plant Accounts listed in
    Schedule II; ([installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plant retired]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plant retired]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a unit of plant, whether it is replaced or not,
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} plant classified as a minor item of plant that is
      not replaced,

    that is sold, abandoned, demolished, dismantled or otherwise
    withdrawn from transportation service; ([installation
    réformée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plant unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plant unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a complete structure,
    apparatus or item of equipment that constitutes a component of any
    installation or property that could properly be included in the
    plant accounts listed in Schedule II and includes a part of any
    structure or apparatus where such part is a physically distinct part
    of such structure or apparatus and the value of such part is
    material; ([unité d'installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[salvage value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[salvage value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount received for
    plant retired, including insurance proceeds, and includes any amount
    received for material salvaged from plant retired where the material
    is sold; ([valeur de récupération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transportation plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transportation plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means plant used for
    any aspect of pipeline operations or plant held for use under a
    definite plan for future oil pipeline operations; ([installation de
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[undivided interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[undivided interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a beneficial
    ownership in common of the assets, liabilities, revenues and
    expenses of an oil pipeline by two or more companies or
    persons. ([copropriété indivise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484404 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to a company authorized by the Commission to construct or operate
an oil pipeline.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  Group 1 company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep separate books of account in Canada in a
    manner consistent with generally accepted accounting principles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise authorized or instructed by the
    Commission, keep accounts in the manner set out in these
    Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} keep a system of accounts as prescribed in these
    Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every Group 2 company

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall keep separate books of account in Canada in a
    manner consistent with generally accepted accounting principles
    until the expiration of one year after such time as the Commission
    grants leave to abandon the operation of the pipeline;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall file a set of audited financial statements
    with the Regulator within one hundred and twenty (120) days after
    the end of each fiscal year of the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} shall comply with subsections 6(1), (7), (8) and
    (9); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is exempt from complying with these Regulations
    except as prescribed in paragraphs (a) to (c).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section,

  [[Group 1 company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Group 1 company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company listed in
      Schedule VII; and ([compagnies du groupe 1]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Group 2 company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Group 2 company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any company, other
      than a Group 1 company, that constructs or operates an oil
      pipeline. ([compagnies du groupe 2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484421 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company's accounting records shall provide sufficient particulars to
  show fully the facts pertaining to all entries made in the accounts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall maintain the applicable accounts
  listed in Schedules I to VI and may, in addition, keep subsidiary
  accounts for its own purposes.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The account numbers listed in Schedules I to VI shall
  be set out in the descriptive headings of the applicable ledger
  accounts, computer print-outs or other accounting records.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All transactions applicable to a month shall be
  recorded in the accounts for that month and, at the end of that month,
  trial balances of the accounts shall be prepared.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A company shall close its accounts at the end of each
  fiscal year.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Final accounting entries for each month shall be made
  not later than 30 days after the last day of the month, except that
  the final entries for the last month of each fiscal year may be made
  within a period not exceeding 90 days from the end of that last month.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The books, accounts and records referred to herein
  include not only accounting records in a limited technical sense, but
  all records such as minute books, stock books, reports,
  correspondence, memoranda, computer print-outs, tapes and card decks
  that may be useful in determining the history of or facts pertaining
  to any transaction.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The books, accounts and records shall be readily
  accessible for examination by representatives of the Regulator.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where an oil pipeline is owned in undivided interest,
  the operator shall maintain all books, accounts and records in a
  manner that permits ready identification to the books, accounts and
  records of the beneficial owners of the pipeline.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} All accounts required to be maintained pursuant to
  subsection (2) shall be retained until the expiration of one year
  after leave to abandon the operation of the pipeline has been granted
  by the Commission.

## [Account Grouping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484435 .Part}

### [Balance Sheet Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484436 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Balance
  sheet accounts, set out in Schedule I as account series 1 to 93,
  shall, in respect of a company, disclose the company's financial
  position at the balance sheet date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The balance sheet accounts referred to in
  subsection (1) shall record assets, liabilities, capital stock, and
  retained earnings or deficit, as the case may be.

### [Plant Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484440 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Plant
  accounts, set out in Schedule II as account series 100, are subsidiary
  to balance sheet accounts 30 (Transportation Plant) and 38
  (Transportation Plant Leased to Others), and shall be used to classify
  a company's transportation plant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Plant accounts shall be supported by subsidiary
  accounts that record the costs, by location, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each major facility including each pumping station,
    main pipeline, receiving, storage and delivery facility; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other facility that is separately located.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any installation or property not used in pipeline
  operations shall be recorded in balance sheet account 34 (Other
  Plant).

### [Retained Earnings Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484447 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Retained earnings accounts,
set out in Schedule III as account series 300, shall show a summary of
all changes in balance sheet account 92 (Retained Earnings) for each
year and shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the balance transferred from income for the year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prior period adjustments;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} miscellaneous retained earnings adjustments;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} dividend appropriations; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} income tax applicable to retained earnings
  adjustments.

### [Income Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484454 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Income
  accounts, set out in Schedule IV as account series 400, shall show a
  summary of a company's revenue, expenses and extraordinary items.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall inform the Regulator of all
  extraordinary items and shall record them in account 402
  (Extraordinary Income) or account 422 (Extraordinary Income
  Deductions), as applicable.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company's net balance of income or loss shall be
  transferred to retained earnings at the end of each fiscal year.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[extraordinary
  items]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means material gains or losses that are
  not typical of a company's normal business activities, are not
  expected to occur regularly over a period of years and would not be
  considered as recurring factors in any evaluation of the ordinary
  operating process of the company.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the following shall
  be applied in determining materiality:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} items of a similar nature shall be considered in
    the aggregate and dissimilar items shall be considered individually;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to qualify for inclusion as an extraordinary item,
    the item should exceed the greater of one per cent of the total
    operating revenue and 10 per cent of the balance transferred from
    income to account 302 for the year.

### [Operating Revenue Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484465 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Operating revenue
accounts, set out in Schedule V as account series 500, shall be used to
classify all revenue of a company from pipeline operations and from
operations incident thereto.

### [Operating Expense Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484467 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Operating expense accounts, set out
  in Schedule VI as account series 600 and 700, shall show cost elements
  making up a company's maintenance, transportation and general expenses
  pertaining to pipeline operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operating expense accounts shall include a
  separate series of accounts for the following company service
  functions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the gathering function; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trunk function.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The gathering function series of accounts referred to
  in paragraph (2)(a) shall include amounts in respect of the gathering
  and collection of oil from the oil fields, refineries or other sources
  and transportation of oil to the point of connection to meters or
  storage tanks or the intake side of the manifold at the trunk
  receiving site or station.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The trunk function series of accounts referred to in
  paragraph (2)(b) shall include amounts in respect of the
  transportation of oil from the receiving stations on the trunk line to
  the point of connection with the facilities of other pipelines and
  consignees.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Expenses common to the gathering and trunk accounts
  referred to in subsection (2) shall be equitably apportioned between
  them and supported by records showing the basis of the apportionment.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The operating expense accounts under the maintenance
  and transportation subheadings of Schedule VI shall be supported by
  subsidiary accounts recording the expenses, by location, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each major facility including each pipeline
    district, pumping station, oil tank farm and warehouse; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other facility that is separately located.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The operating expense accounts under the general
  subheading of Schedule VI shall be supported by subsidiary accounts
  recording the expenses by location where practicable or by department.

### [Clearing Accounts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484480 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  it is not possible to record expenditures directly to a particular
  expense or plant account, a company may maintain clearing accounts for
  recording the expenditures.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Amounts recorded in clearing accounts shall be
  regularly distributed to the appropriate expense or plant accounts on
  an equitable basis.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any balances in the clearing accounts shall be
  substantially distributed before the end of each fiscal year except
  for any items held therein that relate to a future period.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The net monthly balance in clearing accounts shall be
  included in account 45 (Other Deferred Debits) or account 78 (Other
  Deferred Credits), as applicable.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an operator maintains clearing accounts for the
  purpose of distributing monthly balances to the beneficial owners of a
  pipeline, the accounts shall contain sufficient particulars to permit
  ready identification with the accounts of the beneficial owners.

## [Interpretation of Lists of Items]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484487 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  item that appears in a representative list in any account in the
  schedules shall be included in the corresponding account of a company
  if the inclusion of the item is consistent with the text of the
  account.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A representative list shall be considered only as
  representative and not as excluding from an account any analogous
  component or item that is omitted from the list.

## [Plant Acquired or Constructed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484491 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484492 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall record in the plant accounts the costs incurred to
  acquire or construct any plant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the consideration given for plant referred to
  in subsection (1) is other than money, the money value of the
  consideration on the day the contract for the acquisition or
  construction comes into force shall be debited to the applicable
  accounts and a record of the actual consideration, in sufficient
  detail to identify it, shall be retained by the company.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a company purchases all or part of the existing
  facilities of another company, the purchasing company shall prepare
  and submit to the Commission for approval details of the proposed
  method of accounting for such purchase together with the company's
  reasons for the proposed method of accounting.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the facilities referred to in subsection (3)
  are purchased from an affiliated company, the original cost of the
  facilities to the affiliated company and the accumulated depreciation
  applicable to the facilities to the date of purchase, as shown in the
  accounts of the affiliated company, shall be recorded in the accounts
  of the purchasing company.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Balance sheet account 39 (Transportation Plant Under
  Construction) shall be supported by subsidiary records that record
  separately each transaction for each project involving plant
  additions, replacements or relocations.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where plant acquired by a company is constructed by
  or for that company, the costs to be recorded in the plant accounts
  shall include the elements of cost set out in sections 16 to 27 and
  section 29.

### [Cost of Labour]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484500 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cost
  of labour for construction of plant shall include the amount paid for
  labour, including employee benefits, performed by the company's
  employees.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the employees referred to in subsection (1) are
  specially assigned to construction work, their pay while so engaged
  shall be included in the cost of the work.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No charge shall be made to plant accounts for the pay
  of the company's employees whose services in connection with
  construction are merely incidental, except as provided in section 26.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The allocation of travelling and other incidental
  expenses of the company's employees shall be made to accounts on the
  same basis as the distribution of the pay of those employees.

### [Cost of Materials and Supplies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484506 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cost
  of materials and supplies shall include the purchase price of
  materials and supplies at the point of free delivery, the cost of [ad
  valorem]{.otherLang lang="la"} taxes, inspection, transportation and,
  where appropriate, a suitable proportion of store expenses.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the cost of materials and supplies,
  suitable allowance shall be made for all discounts allowed and
  realized on the purchase thereof.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In debiting the plant accounts with materials and
  supplies used, allowance shall be made for the value of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unused portions and other salvage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the materials recovered from temporary pipe,
    scaffolding and other temporary structures used in construction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} small tools recovered and usable for other
    purposes.

### [Cost of Special Machine and Heavy Work Equipment Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484514 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cost
  of special machine and heavy work equipment service shall include the
  cost of labour, material, depreciation, supplies and other costs
  incurred in maintaining and operating power shovels, scrapers, pile
  drivers, dredgers, ditchers, material loaders and similar equipment,
  and any amount paid to others for rent, operation and maintenance of
  such equipment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a construction project necessitates the
  purchase of equipment to be used exclusively on the project until the
  project is completed, the cost of the equipment shall be debited to
  the accounts representing the cost of the project and while the cost
  remains so debited no debit shall be made to expenses for depreciation
  on the equipment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount realized from any subsequent sale of
  equipment referred to in subsection (2), or the appraised value of any
  equipment retained after the completion of the project for which it
  was purchased, shall be credited to the accounts debited with the
  equipment's cost.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The appraised value of equipment retained after the
  completion of the project for which it was purchased shall be debited
  to the appropriate plant account and, thereafter, for the purposes of
  accounting, that appraised value shall be considered as the cost of
  the equipment.

### [Cost of Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484520 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cost
  of transportation incurred in the construction of plant shall include
  the cost of transporting men, equipment and material and supplies used
  for construction purposes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of transportation of construction material
  to the point where material is received by the company shall be
  included, if practicable, in the cost of the material.

### [Cost of Contract Work]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484524 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Cost of contract work
shall include any amount paid for work performed under contract by other
companies and individuals.

### [Cost of Insurance, Injuries and Damages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484526 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cost
  of insurance, injuries and damages shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premiums paid for insuring plant during
    construction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs incurred for protection against fire and
    wilful destruction of plant during construction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs incurred in respect of injuries to
    persons, damage to property of others and damage to plant incidental
    to construction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Insurance recovered or recoverable for compensation
  for injuries to persons injured during the construction of plant shall
  be credited to the account or accounts to which such compensation is
  debited.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Insurance recovered or recoverable in respect of
  property damage incidental to the construction of plant shall be
  credited to the account or accounts debited with the cost of the
  damage.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), any injury or damage
  incidental to the removal of a structure or part thereof shall be
  debited to the account recording the retirement costs of the
  structure.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any injury or damage incidental to the removal of a
  structure or part thereof that is an encumbrance on newly acquired
  land by a company shall be included in the cost of that land.

### [Cost of Overhead During Construction]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484544 .Subheading}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only actual and reasonable overhead costs shall be

### [Allowance for Funds Used During Construction]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484549 .Subheading}

### [Major Renewals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484599 .Subheading}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[cost of renewals]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of material, other than old parts
    remaining in a rebuilt plant unit, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of labour used in the rebuilding process,

  but does not include the cost of dismantling and repairing old parts
  that are re-used.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), where the cost of renewals
  to parts of a plant unit exceeds 50 per cent of the new replacement
  cost of a plant unit of the same kind and class, the plant unit shall
  be considered as rebuilt.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to the renewal of a
  plant unit where the new replacement cost of that unit would not
  exceed \$120,000.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A rebuilt plant unit shall be accounted for as an
  addition and the old plant unit that the rebuilt plant unit replaced
  shall be accounted for as retired from service.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), the charge to the
  appropriate plant account for a rebuilt plant unit shall be the
  aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of old parts remaining in the rebuilt
    plant unit; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of labour and material applied.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The cost of dismantling and repairing old parts used
  in the renewal of plant shall be debited to maintenance expense.

## [Depreciation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484689 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484690 .Subheading}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** In sections 49 to 57,

[[group system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system by which a
    weighted average rate of depreciation is calculated for a particular
    group of plant accounts, a plant account, or a group of assets
    within a plant account, and established in recognition of the fact
    that some part of the investment in a group of assets may be
    recovered through salvage realization and that there will be
    variations in the service lives of the assets constituting the
    group, even among assets of the same class; ([système
    d'amortissement par catégorie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period of time
    between the placement of plant in service and its retirement for
    accounting purposes; ([durée d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[service value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the book cost of plant
    minus the estimated net salvage value of that plant. ([valeur de
    service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Under
  the group system, in the case of an ordinary retirement of an
  individual asset in a group of assets, the accumulated depreciation
  attributable to the asset shall, for the purposes of these
  Regulations, be considered to be equal to the cost of the asset minus
  any amount that may reasonably be recoverable through salvage
  realization, whether or not the actual service life of the asset is
  shorter or longer than the anticipated average service life for the
  group.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Individual assets, within a group of assets,
  remaining in use after reaching their average service life expectancy
  shall not be regarded as fully depreciated until actual retirement or
  until the group is fully depreciated, whichever is earlier.

<!-- -->

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Charges for depreciation with
  respect to accounts that are classed by section 51 as being accounts
  covering assets that are depreciable shall be computed in conformity
  with the group system.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall charge depreciation by using such one
  of the following methods as is chosen by the company and approved by
  the Commission for use by the company:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the straight line method;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use or unit of production method;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the diminishing value method; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other systematic method consistent with
    generally accepted accounting principles.

### [Depreciation Charges]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484709 .Subheading}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be debited each month to expenses or other appropriate accounts
  and credited to the accounts for accumulated depreciation amounts that
  will allocate, in a systematic and rational manner, the service value
  of plant over its estimated service life.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Monthly depreciation charges under the straight line
  method shall be computed by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} applying the annual percentage rate of depreciation
    to the depreciation base as of the first of each month and dividing
    the result by 12; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the prior approval of the Commission, applying
    the annual percentage rate of depreciation to the depreciation base
    at the beginning of the company fiscal year and dividing the result
    by 12.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Monthly depreciation charges under the use or unit of
  production method shall be computed by applying the appropriate rate
  of use or production per unit for the year to the number of units of
  use or production for the month.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Monthly depreciation charges under the diminishing
  value method shall be computed by applying the annual percentage rate
  to the depreciation base as of the first of each month and dividing
  the result by 12.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A company shall compute depreciation in accordance
  with subsection 28(1).

  in account 30 (Transportation Plant) or account 38 (Transportation

- **[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of these Regulations, one company is affiliated with

  are subsidiaries of the same company or each of them is controlled by
  the same person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a company is a
  subsidiary of another company if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is controlled by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that other company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that other company and one or more companies
      each of which is controlled by that other company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} two or more companies each of which is
      controlled by that other company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is a subsidiary of a company that is subsidiary
    to that other company.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a company is
  controlled by another company or person or by two or more other
  companies if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shares of the company carrying more than 50 per
    cent of the votes for the election of directors are held, otherwise
    than by way of security only, by or for the benefit of that other
    company or person or by or for the benefit of such other companies;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the votes carried by shares referred to in
    paragraph (a) are sufficient, if exercised, to elect a majority of
    the board of directors of the company.

## [Oil Accounting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-484912 .Part}

### [Allowance Oil Accounting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484913 .Subheading}

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall record any amounts earned as a result of its tariff
  relating to oil allowances covering losses due to shrinkage or other
  factors.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Oil allowances referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  valued at not more than the market value at point of delivery and
  shall be debited to account 9 (Oil Inventory) and concurrently shall
  be credited to account 505 or 555 (Allowance Oil Revenue).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Gains in oil allowances resulting from pumping,
  temperature corrections or other factors shall be debited to account 9
  (Oil Inventory) and credited to account 505 or 555 (Allowance Oil
  Revenue).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Shortages in oil allowances shall be debited to
  account 505 or 555 (Allowance Oil Revenue) and credited to account 9
  (Oil Inventory).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, at balance sheet date, the debits to
  account 505 or 555 (Allowance Oil Revenue) exceed the credits, the net
  debit shall be debited to account 620-8 or 720-8 (Oil Loss).

### [General Oil Accounting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-484920 .Subheading}

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the operations of a company have consumed oil that has been accounted
  for by a debit to account 9 (Oil Inventory), that account shall be
  credited and account 620-2 or 720-2 (Operating Fuel and Power) shall
  be debited with the oil at the value of the inventory of oil carried
  in account 9.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Oil lost through line breaks or other extraordinary
  circumstances shall be accounted for by debiting account 620-8 or
  720-8 (Oil Loss) and crediting account 9 (Oil Inventory), and
  purchases of oil to replenish shortages or losses shall be accounted
  for by debiting account 9.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a company requires part of its oil inventory to
  provide an operating oil supply, account 33 (Operating Oil Supply)
  shall be debited and account 9 (Oil Inventory) shall be credited with
  the value of the oil so required and the oil shall be priced at the
  value at which it is carried in inventory.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sales of oil shall be accounted for by a credit to
  account 9 (Oil Inventory) or account 33 (Operating Oil Supply), as
  applicable, and the difference between the selling price and the value
  at which the oil is recorded in account 9 or 33 shall be debited or
  credited, as applicable, to account 620-8 or 720-8 (Oil Loss).

    utilities, or unemployment insurance commissions.

- [4]{.lawlabel} *Accounts Receivable --- Trade*

  This account shall include amounts receivable for the transportation,
  storage and delivery of oil, exclusive of such amounts receivable from
  affiliated companies.

- [5]{.lawlabel} *Accounts Receivable --- Other*

  This account shall include the following items when the amounts
  involved are considered collectable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} advances to officers, employees and agents as
    working funds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company's estimate of current liabilities in
    respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} injuries to persons, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss and damage claims.

##### [Deferred Credits and Appropriations]{.HTitleText3} {#deferred-credits-and-appropriations-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- [70]{.lawlabel} *Welfare and Pension Appropriations*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the credit balances that represent the liability
      of the company for any amount contributed by employees and by the
      company, through debits to expenses or other accounts approved for
      the purpose by the Commission, for

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pensions,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} accidents and death benefits,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} savings,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} relief, and

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} hospital, or other provident purposes,

    whether or not a special fund has been established to meet such
    liabilities. (*See* Regulations, section 62 and account 23
    (Miscellaneous Special Funds)); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the credit balances representing the liability of
      the company for amounts provided by debits to expenses in respect
      of deferred compensation payments under an employees' profit
      sharing plan.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Separate subaccounts shall be maintained for each
    kind of appropriation created pursuant to subsection (1).

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Disbursements shall be debited to this account and
    any excess of the disbursements over the amounts appropriated shall
    be transferred to account 630-13 or 730-13 (Employee Benefits) or to
    account 630-11 or 730-11 (Injuries to Persons and Property Damage),
    as applicable.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Current amounts payable relative to funded welfare,
    pension and other plans with insurance companies or trustees shall
    be transferred to account 52 (Accounts Payable and Accrued).

- [71]{.lawlabel} *Accumulated Tax Reductions Applicable to Future
  Years*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a company does not follow the taxes payable
    or flow-through basis of accounting for income taxes, this account
    shall include the amount by which a company's provision for income
    taxes for a particular year differs from the amount of income taxes
    payable for that year.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a company does not follow the method of
    accounting for accumulated tax reductions applicable to future
    years, the company shall show the amount of its accumulated tax
    reductions in its annual financial report to Statistics Canada
    pursuant to the Statistics Act.

- [72]{.lawlabel} *Insurance Appropriations*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the credit balances that
    represent insurance appropriations concurrently debited to expenses
    to cover self-insurance against losses through accident, fire,
    flood, burglary or other hazards and re-insurance recoveries from
    insurance companies. (*See* Regulations, sections 61 and 62).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} To the extent that a loss or damage sustained is
    covered by this account, the amount of that loss or damage shall be
    debited to this account.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Separate subaccounts shall be maintained for each
    kind of insurance reserve created.

- [73]{.lawlabel} *Maintenance Expense Equalization*

  This account shall include the differences between actual and budgeted
  monthly maintenance expenses. (*See* Regulations, section 47).

- [74]{.lawlabel} *Allowance for Loss in Value of Investments*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include all the credit balances
    maintained by a company where the credit balances represent
    reductions in the gross book value of investments carried in
    account 20 (Investments in Affiliated Companies) and account 21
    (Other Investments). (*See* account 415 (Provision for Loss in
    Valuation of Investments)).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Debits to this account that represent reductions in
    the allowance for loss in value of investments carried in accounts
    20 or 21 shall be made only after approval of the Commission. (*See*
    account 404 (Investment Valuation Adjustment)).

- [75]{.lawlabel} *Allowance For Doubtful Accounts*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the total of the
    balances maintained by a company for the purpose of providing for
    reductions in the value of current assets recorded in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} account 4 (Accounts Receivable --- Trade),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} account 5 (Accounts Receivable --- Other),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} account 6 (Accounts Receivable --- Affiliated
      Companies), or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} account 12 (Other Current Assets),

  and the corresponding debits shall be made to accounts 630-15 or
  730-15 (Bad Debts), as applicable.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Collections on accounts that were previously
    debited to this account shall be credited to this account.

- [76]{.lawlabel} *Unamortized Premium On Long Term Debt*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the unamortized premiums
    received from the issuance of long term debt and shall include the
    total of the credit balances in the subaccounts maintained for each
    series of each class of long term debt. (*See* Regulations,
    section 71).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts recorded in this account shall be
    amortized in accordance with sections 72 or 73 of the Regulations,
    as applicable.

- [78]{.lawlabel} *Other Deferred Credits*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit items that cannot be disposed of until
    further information is received;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undistributed balances in clearing accounts; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} credit items of a deferred nature, not provided for
    in other accounts under the Regulations, that are to be amortized
    over future periods.

##### [Long Term Liabilities]{.HTitleText3} {#long-term-liabilities-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- [80]{.lawlabel} *Long Term Debt*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total par value of unmatured debt maturing
      more than one year from the date of the balance sheet, where the
      debt is issued and not retired or cancelled; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total par value of any unmatured debt similar
      to the debt referred to in paragraph (a) of any other company, the
      payment of which has been assumed by the company.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts in this account shall be divided to
    show the par value of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} certificates or other evidences of long term
      debt, pledged and unpledged, held in the company's treasury, by
      its agents or trustees, or otherwise subject to the company's
      control; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} certificates or other evidences of long term debt
      issued and outstanding, and not held by the company, its agents or
      trustees, or subject to the company's control.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amounts included in this account shall be
    further divided to show the amount of each of the following classes
    of long term debt:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mortgage bonds --- bonds that are secured by lien
      on physical property and not included in any other subdivisions of
      this account;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} collateral trust bonds --- bonds and notes that
      are secured by a lien on securities or other negotiable paper and
      stock trust certificates that are similar in character to
      collateral trust bonds;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} income bonds --- bonds that are a lien on a
      company's revenue alone, or bonds that, while being a lien on the
      company's property and franchises, provide for payment of interest
      only if the interest is earned;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} convertible bonds --- bonds that may be converted
      into capital stock of the company according to the agreement under
      which they are issued; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} miscellaneous obligations --- all other long term
      obligations that mature more than one year from the date of the
      balance sheet.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each of the classes listed in subsection (3) shall
    be divided into subclasses according to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} differences in security given therefor;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rates of interest;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} interest dates; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} dates of maturity.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Long term debt securities are considered to be
    issued when they have been sold to a *bona fide* purchaser for
    valuable consideration and the purchaser holds them free from all
    control by the accounting company.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} All long term debt securities that are issued, not
    reacquired and are held by or for the company are considered to be
    outstanding.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Long term debt that is to be repaid in a foreign
    currency shall be included in this account in accordance with
    generally accepted accounting practice.

- [85]{.lawlabel} *Advances from Affiliated Companies*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the par value of bonds, debentures, and long term
      notes payable,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the par value of non-negotiable notes,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the par value of matured long term bonds,
      debentures or notes payable where,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bonds, debentures or notes of the company
        are held by an affiliated company,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there is no agreement for an extension as to
        time of payment, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the collection of the principal is not
        enforced,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} credit balances in open accounts with affiliated
      companies, other than credit balances in current accounts, and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} interest accrued on any matter referred to in
      paragraphs (a) to (d)

  owed to an affiliated company and not subject to settlement within one
  year from the date of the balance sheet.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Separate subaccounts shall be kept for each of the
    items listed in subsection (1). (*See* Regulations, section 80).

- [86]{.lawlabel} *Other Long Term Debt*

  This account shall include amounts payable more than one year from the
  date of the balance sheet if such amounts have not been provided for
  elsewhere and loans payable authorized by subsection 76(2) of the
  Regulations.

##### [Capital Stock and Surplus]{.HTitleText3} {#capital-stock-and-surplus-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- [90]{.lawlabel} *Capital Stock*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total par value of par value stock, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total amount received from the issuance of
      stock without par value, excluding amounts that have been properly
      allocated to account 91 (Contributed Surplus),

    if the stock has been issued to *bona fide* purchasers and has not
    been reacquired and cancelled, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} appropriations of retained earnings that have
      been transferred to this account.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Premiums received from the issue of par value stock
    shall be credited to account 91 (Contributed Surplus).

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where capital stock is reduced or cancelled, this
    account shall be debited with the appropriate amount applicable to
    the particular series or class of stock involved.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where capital stock having par value is exchanged
    for capital stock without par value, any amounts with respect
    thereto in account 91 (Contributed Surplus) shall be transferred to
    this account.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} This account shall be subdivided in a manner that
    shows the total amount credited to this account with respect to each
    series or class of stock issued and outstanding and carrying
    different conditions.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} An appropriate record shall be maintained showing
    the number of shares issued and outstanding for each series or class
    referred to in subsection (5).

- [91]{.lawlabel} *Contributed Surplus*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} surplus arising from transactions relating to the
      company's share capital; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} capital contributions and donations from
      government and other sources.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of
    contributed surplus to be included in this account: (*See*
    Regulations, section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} premiums received on the issue of par value
      shares;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proceeds from the sale of donated shares;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} credits resulting from redemption or conversion
      of shares at less than the amount recorded as share capital;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} other contributions made by shareholders in
      excess of par or stated value of shares;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the proceeds from the issue of no
      par value shares that has been properly allocated to this account;
      and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} capital contributions in the form of subsidies,
      building sites and other such contributions.

- [92]{.lawlabel} *Retained Earnings*

  This account shall include the balance, debit or credit, of the
  amounts included in the Retained Earnings accounts listed in Schedule
  III.

- [93]{.lawlabel} *Excess of Appraised Value of Fixed Assets Over
  Depreciated Cost*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall be credited with the increase in
    value of fixed assets of the company as a result of an appraisal,
    where the appraisal is associated with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an actual purchase and sale of fixed assets as in
      the acquisition of an affiliated company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reorganization of the accounting company; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a substantial issue of securities, whether equity
      shares or otherwise.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The crediting of this account in the manner
    referred to in subsection (1) and the disposition of any amounts
    included therein shall be subject to the prior approval of the
    Commission.

### [Text Pertaining to Plant Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#text-pertaining-to-plant-accounts .SchedHeadL1}

#### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#general .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The total of the balances in the plant accounts shall
  equal the total of the balances in balance sheet accounts 30
  (Transportation Plant) and 38 (Transportation Plant Leased to Others).

- [2]{.lawlabel} Where the same account title is used for more than one
  group of plant accounts, the account text instructions are given in
  detail only for the accounts under the gathering lines group, except
  in the case of account 153 (Pipelines).

#### [Gathering Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#gathering-lines .SchedHeadL2}

- [101]{.lawlabel} *Land*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of land owned in fee simple and used for
      the construction and operation of pipelines; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of removing the property of others from
      the land and relocating the property.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of land not used in transportation service
    or for which there is no definite plan for use in pipeline
    operations, shall be debited to account 34 (Other Plant).

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Net proceeds from the sale of timber, minerals or
    improvements that were part of the land cost shall be credited to
    this account.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the approval of the Commission, a
    company shall credit the amount of any excess of net proceeds
    referred to in subsection (3) over the original cost of the timber,
    minerals or improvements included in land cost to account 402
    (Extraordinary Income) unless the amount of the excess is not
    material, in which case the company shall credit that amount to
    account 410 (Other Income).

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of
    land to be included in the account: (*See* Regulations, section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} abstract;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appraisal;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} arbitrator in expropriation cases;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} commissions paid to others;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} compensation and expenses of outside land agents
      if specifically assigned to acquisition of land except arbitrary
      apportionments for incidental services;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} cost of clearing, filling and levelling submerged
      or low land;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} compensation for damages to property of others;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} cost of a ditch for waterway when it is part of
      consideration for purchase;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} engineering and survey expenses in connection
      with the purchase of land in fee-simple;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} expropriation expenses, including court costs and
      special counsel fees;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} judgments and decreed costs to clear or defend
      titles;

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} legal and notarial fees;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} plats;

    - [(n)]{.lawlabel} premiums on expropriation bonds;

    - [(o)]{.lawlabel} cost of registering, filing and depositing title
      documents and plans;

    - [(p)]{.lawlabel} payments for release and discharge of
      encumbrances and charges against the land;

    - [(q)]{.lawlabel} payments for release from restrictive provisions
      of original title documents and for other rights;

    - [(r)]{.lawlabel} removal and relocation of buildings and other
      structures on the land that are not purchased;

    - [(s)]{.lawlabel} rent of land where it is part of the
      consideration for purchase; and

    - [(t)]{.lawlabel} taxes accrued on the land and assumed at time of
      purchase.

- [102]{.lawlabel} *Land Rights*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include amounts paid for
    registered land rights or easements and the costs incurred in
    connection with obtaining the land rights.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Temporary land rights or easements used for
    construction purposes only and the cost of clearing, levelling or
    grading land for which the company has acquired land rights both
    before and after the construction of facilities thereon shall be
    included in the plant account provided for the facilities
    constructed.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company shall credit to this account the net
    proceeds from the sale of timber, minerals or improvements that were
    part of the cost of land rights.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the approval of the Commission, a
    company shall credit the amount of any excess of net proceeds
    referred to in subsection (3) over the original cost of the timber,
    minerals or improvements included in the cost of land rights to
    account 402 (Extraordinary Income) unless the amount of the excess
    is not material, in which case the company shall credit that amount
    to account 410 (Other Income).

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of
    land rights to be included in the account: (*See* Regulations,
    section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} abstract expenses;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} engineering and survey expenses;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expropriation expenses;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fees and costs of registering, filing and
      depositing title documents and plans;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} judgments and decreed costs of clearing or
      defending titles;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} legal and notarial fees;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} outside land right agents' compensation;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} plats;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} premiums on expropriation bonds;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} payments for release and discharge of
      encumbrances and charges against the land affected by the land
      rights;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} payments for tenants' consents, options and
      easements; and

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} servitudes.

- [103]{.lawlabel} *Pipelines*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the cost of installed
    line pipe and line pipe fittings laid in the construction of
    gathering lines and shall be maintained to show separately the costs
    of line pipe, line pipe fittings and pipeline construction.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of pipeline
    fittings to be included in the account: (*See* Regulations,
    section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} flange;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} scraper trap; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} valve.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of
    work and material to be included in the account: (*See* Regulations,

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total revenue derived from operation of, or
      income arising from, assets included in account 34 (Other Plant);
      and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total revenue from transportation plant
      leased to others in the form of operating units or systems, where
      the lessee has exclusive possession.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of
    revenue to be included in the account: (*See* Regulations,
    section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} revenue from commercial power plants;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} revenue from land and buildings not used in
      pipeline service;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} revenue from land and property acquired and held
      in anticipation of an indefinite future use; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} revenue from mineral and timber land.

- [404]{.lawlabel} *Investment Valuation Adjustment*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} With the approval of the Commission, this account
    shall be credited with amounts debited to account 74 (Allowance for
    Loss in Value of Investments) in respect of reductions in the
    allowance for loss in value of investments carried in account 20
    (Investments in Affiliated Companies) and account 21 (Other
    Investments): (*See* texts of accounts 74 and 415, and Regulations,
    section 68).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the approval of the Commission, this account
    shall include credits arising under the terms of agreements or
    contracts that provide that any surplus resulting from the operation
    by others of properties of other companies having a separate
    corporate existence but whose operations are considered to be an
    integral part of the company's oil transportation system, is to be
    paid in whole or in part to the company.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount receivable under
    subsection (2), consideration shall be given not only to the
    revenues and expenses but also to other items of income or deduction
    that affect that amount.

- [405]{.lawlabel} *Income from Affiliated Companies*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include income from a company's
    investment in stocks, securities and other types of indebtedness
    that are issued or assumed by affiliated companies and in respect of
    which the income is the property of the accounting company, whether
    such stocks, securities and other types of indebtedness are owned by
    the accounting company and held in its treasury or deposited in
    trust or are otherwise controlled: (*See* Regulations, section 80).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts included in this account shall be
    divided to show separately the following types of income:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dividends;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other income.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Income from affiliated companies shall not be
    credited to this account until such time as it is recorded on the
    books of the affiliated company.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the option of the company, there may be included
    in this account the applicable portion of discount or premium on any
    investments recorded in account 20: (*See* Regulations, section 63).

- [406]{.lawlabel} *Income from Investments*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} interest and dividends from investments recorded
      in account 2 (Temporary Cash Investments) and account 21 (Other
      Investments);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest on bank balances, open accounts and
      special deposits, where the interest is the property of the
      company; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the amounts involved are not material,
      profits and losses on sales or amounts required to provide
      allowance for the decrease in market value of temporary cash
      investments: (*See* Regulations, sections 64 and 65).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the option of the company, there may be included
    in this account any applicable portion of discount or premium on
    investments recorded in accounts 2 and 21: (*See* Regulations,
    section 63).

- [407]{.lawlabel} *Income from Sinking and Other Funds*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the income accrued on cash, securities and other
      assets (not including securities issued or assumed by the company)
      recorded in account 22 (Sinking Funds) and account 23
      (Miscellaneous Special Funds); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immaterial gains realized and losses sustained in
      the sale of securities recorded in account 22 (Sinking Funds) and
      account 23 (Miscellaneous Special Funds): (*See* Regulations,
      section 62).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the option of the company, there may be included
    in this account the applicable portion of the discount or premium on
    any securities recorded in account 22 (Sinking Funds) and account 23
    (Miscellaneous Special Funds): (*See* Regulations, section 62).

- [408]{.lawlabel} *Release of Premium on Long Term Debt*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include, during each fiscal
    period, such portion of the premium on outstanding long term debt as
    may be applicable to the period.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The portion referred to in subsection (1) shall be
    determined in accordance with section 71, 72 or 73 of the
    Regulations, as applicable.

- [409]{.lawlabel} *Gain on Foreign Exchange*

  This account shall include gains on foreign exchange during the fiscal
  year, including realized gains as well as gains due to the conversion
  to the Canadian dollar equivalent, on assets and liabilities held in
  foreign currencies.

- [410]{.lawlabel} *Other Income*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gains realized from the sale of materials and
    supplies not purchased for resale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immaterial gains realized from reacquisition at
    less than net book value of debt securities issued by the company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immaterial gains arising from the sale of land,
    other plant or investments carried in account 20 (Investments in
    Affiliated Companies) and account 21 (Other Investments);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the excess, if any, of capitalized interest during
    construction over the debits to accounts 416 (Interest on Long Term
    Debt) or 417 (Other Interest); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all other income items not provided for in other
    accounts under the Regulations.

- [410.1]{.lawlabel} *Allowance for funds used during construction
  (Credit)*

  This account shall be credited with amounts concurrently debited to
  account 189 (Allowance for funds used during construction): (*See*
  Regulations, section 27 and account 189).

#### [Debits]{.HTitleText2} {#debits-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- [411]{.lawlabel} *Operating Expenses*

  This account shall include the total of the balances contained in the
  operating expense accounts listed in the 600 and 700 series of
  accounts.

- [412]{.lawlabel} *Other Expenses*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all expenses, including depreciation but
      excluding income taxes, arising from the operation of other plant
      the cost of which is included in account 34 (Other Plant); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all expenses, other than depreciation, of

      systems while the lessee has exclusive possession.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of

    section 14)

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} expenses of commercial power plants;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} expenses of land and buildings not used in
      pipeline service;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} expenses of land and property acquired and held
      in anticipation of an indefinite future use; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenses of mineral and timber land.

- [413]{.lawlabel} *Provision for Income Taxes*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include provision for federal,
    provincial or other government income taxes levied on the basis of a
    company's operations for the fiscal year, except income taxes that
    are debited to account 318 (Income Tax Applicable to Retained
    Earnings Adjustments).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} This account shall be subdivided as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} current income taxes applicable to income before
      extraordinary items;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} current income taxes and reductions of current
      income taxes applicable to extraordinary items; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} deferred income taxes.

- [414]{.lawlabel} *Depreciation*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include debits for the
    depreciation of plant included in

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} account 30 (Transportation Plant);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} account 38 (Transportation Plant Leased to
      Others); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} account 36 (Improvements to Leased Facilities).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company's subsidiary records shall be
    maintained to show separately the debits for depreciation for each
    plant account.

- [415]{.lawlabel} *Provision for Loss in Valuation of Investments*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall be debited with amounts
    concurrently credited to account 74 (Allowance for Loss in Value of

    carried in account 20 (Investments in Affiliated Companies) or
    account 21 (Other Investments): (*See* text of account 74 and
    Regulations, sections 67 and 68).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} With the approval of the Commission, this account

    that provide that the deficits resulting from the operation by
    others of properties of other companies having a separate corporate
    existence but whose operations are considered to be an integral part
    of the company's oil transportation system, are to be paid in whole
    or in part by the company.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} In considering the amount payable under
    subsection (2), consideration shall be given to the revenues and
    expenses and to other items of income or deductions that affect that
    amount.

- [416]{.lawlabel} *Interest on Long Term Debt*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include current accruals of

    Debt) and account 57 (Long Term Debt Due Within One Year), less
    current accruals on the debt included in account 24 (Company Long
    Term Debt Owned).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} This account shall be maintained to show separately
    the interest on each class of long term debt.

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all interest except that debited to account 416
    (Interest on Long Term Debt) or to account 421 (Interest on Amounts
    Due Affiliated Companies); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all discounts, premiums and expenses on short term

    date of the balance sheet.

  obligations that is applicable to that period and such portion shall
  be determined in accordance with section 71, 72 or 73 of the
  Regulations, as applicable.

  This account shall include losses on foreign exchange during the
  fiscal year, including realized losses as well as losses due to the
  conversion to the Canadian dollar equivalent of assets and liabilities
  held in foreign currencies.

- [420]{.lawlabel} *Other Income Deductions*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immaterial losses through reacquisitions at greater
    than net book value of debt securities issued by the company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immaterial losses arising from the sale of land,
    other plant or investments carried in account 20 (Investments in
    Affiliated Companies) and account 21 (Other Investments); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all other income deductions not provided for in
    other accounts under the Regulations.

- [421]{.lawlabel} *Interest on Amounts Due Affiliated Companies*

  This account shall include the current accruals of interest on all
  classes of debt included in account 85 (Advances from Affiliated
  Companies) or account 53 (Accounts Payable --- Affiliated Companies).

- [422]{.lawlabel} *Extraordinary Income Deductions*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} A company shall include in this account all
    material losses constituting extraordinary items. (See Regulations,
    subsections 10(2) and (4)).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following is a representative list of items of

      Regulations, section 44);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} loss on sale of assets included in Sinking Funds
      or Miscellaneous Special Funds: (*See* Regulations, section 62);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} loss on sale of Temporary Cash Investments:
      (*See* Regulations, section 64);

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} reduction in the value of Temporary Cash
      Investments: (*See* Regulations, section 65);

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} loss on sale of Investments in Affiliated
      Companies or Other Investments: (*See* Regulations, section 66);

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} reduction in the value of Investments in
      Affiliated Companies or Other Investments: (*See* Regulations,
      section 67);

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss on redemption of long term debt: (*See*
      Regulations, section 73);

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} loss on reacquisition of long term debt: (*See*
      Regulations, section 73); and

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} payments for options to purchase land for use in
      contemplated projects not proceeded with: (*See* text of
      account 44).

- [423]{.lawlabel} *Amortization*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} debits for the amortization of plant included in

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} account 30 (Transportation Plant), and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} account 38 (Transportation Plant Leased to
        Others),

    if a change from depreciation accounting to amortization accounting
    has been approved by the Commission: (*See* Regulations, section
    60); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} debits for the amortization of Improvements to
      Leased Facilities: (*See* text of account 36).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsidiary records shall be maintained to show
    separately the amortization expense for each

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} group of plant accounts;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} plant account; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} group of assets within a plant account that

- [425]{.lawlabel} *Income Transferred to Other Companies*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the whole or any portion

    company under the terms of an agreement or contract that contains no
    obligation for reimbursement by that other company to the accounting
    company.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the amount payable by an accounting
    company under subsection (1), consideration shall be given to
    revenues and expenses and other items of income or deduction that
    affect that amount.

  oil products.

  the basis of tariff charges and demurrage charges incident to the
  failure of consignees to receive shipments promptly.

  (Transportation Plant Leased to Others).

  provided for in other accounts under the Regulations.

  tariff relating to oil allowances covering loss due to shrinkage and
  other causes: (*See* Regulations, section 81).

  This account shall include revenue earned on the basis of tariff
  charges for receiving, delivering, unloading and loading at the
  company's receiving and delivery facilities.

### [Text Pertaining to Operating Expense Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#text-pertaining-to-operating-expense-accounts .SchedHeadL1}

#### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#general-5 .SchedHeadL2}

- [1]{.lawlabel} The total of the balances in the operating expense
  accounts shall equal the balance in income account 411 (Operating
  Expenses).

- [2]{.lawlabel} The following account text instructions are given in
  detail only for the accounts in the gathering lines group.

#### [Gathering Lines]{.HTitleText2} {#gathering-lines-2 .SchedHeadL2}

- [610]{.lawlabel} *Maintenance*

  This account shall include the total of the balances recorded in
  accounts 610-1 to 610-7.

- [610-1]{.lawlabel} *Salaries and Wages*

  This account shall include the salaries and wages, including holiday
  and sick leave pay, for personnel directly engaged in the maintenance
  of transportation plant.

- [610-3]{.lawlabel} *Materials and Supplies*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the cost of materials
    and supplies used in the maintenance of transportation plant.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The salvage value of materials recovered in
    maintenance work shall be credited to this account.

- [610-4]{.lawlabel} *Outside Services*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the cost of maintenance
    services provided by persons other than company employees, under
    contract, agreement or other arrangement.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of services performed by affiliated
    companies shall be segregated within this account.

- [610-5]{.lawlabel} *Other Expenses*

  This account shall include maintenance expenses not provided for in
  other accounts under the Regulations.

- [610-7]{.lawlabel} *Equalization --- Plant*

  This account shall include the difference between actual and budgeted
  maintenance expenses: (*See* Regulations, section 47).

- [620]{.lawlabel} *Transportation*

  This account shall include the total of the balances recorded in
  accounts 620-1 to 620-8.

- [620-1]{.lawlabel} *Salaries and Wages*

  This account shall include the salaries and wages, including holiday
  and sick leave pay, for employees of the company directly engaged in
  transportation operations.

- [620-2]{.lawlabel} *Operating Fuel and Power*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of fuel and power consumed in operations;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost of normal utilities services where the
    cost is directly attributable to operations.

- [620-3]{.lawlabel} *Materials and Supplies*

  This account shall include the cost of materials and supplies consumed
  in transportation operations.

- [620-4]{.lawlabel} *Outside Services*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include the cost of
    transportation services provided by persons other than company
    employees, under contract, agreement or other arrangement.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost of services performed by affiliated
    companies shall be segregated within this account.

- [620-5]{.lawlabel} *Other Expenses*

  This account shall include transportation expenses not provided for in
  other accounts under the Regulations.

- [620-8]{.lawlabel} *Oil Loss*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of oil and oil products lost in the
    course of transportation or during storage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gain or loss on sale of oil that is recorded in
    account 9 (Oil Inventory) or account 33 (Operating Oil Supply); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the net debits, if any, at balance sheet date in
    account 505 or 555 (Allowance Oil Revenue): (*See* Regulations,
    sections 81 and 82).

- [630]{.lawlabel} *General*

  This account shall include the total of the balances recorded in
  accounts 630-1 to 630-16.

- [630-1]{.lawlabel} *Salaries and Wages*

  This account shall include the salaries and wages, including holiday
  and sick leave pay, for executives, general officers, general office
  personnel and other employees whose salaries and wages cannot be
  directly allocated to maintenance or transportation accounts.

- [630-3]{.lawlabel} *Materials and Supplies*

  This account shall include the cost of materials and supplies used for
  administration and general services by the company.

- [630-4]{.lawlabel} *Outside Services*

  This account shall include the fees and expenses of professional
  consultants and other outside parties for general services.

- [630-5]{.lawlabel} *Other Expenses*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} expenses of aircraft and vehicles used for general
    purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} travel and other expenses of general employees and
    officers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} utility services; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all other incidental general expenses not provided
    for in other accounts under the Regulations.

- [630-9]{.lawlabel} *Law Expenses*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pay and expenses of the law department of the
    company not provided for in other accounts under the Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fees, costs and expenses of lawsuits; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the payments of special law fees.

- [630-10]{.lawlabel} *Rent*

  This account shall include rents payable in respect of leased plant
  used in pipeline service and not provided for in other accounts under
  the Regulations.

- [630-11]{.lawlabel} *Injuries to Persons and Property Damage*

  This account shall include all expenses, except law expenses, incurred
  as a result of injury to or the death of an employee or other person
  or damage to or destruction of the property of others.

- [630-13]{.lawlabel} *Employee Benefits*

  This account shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost to the company of annuities and pensions
    for active or retired employees, their beneficiaries or designees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contributions to health or welfare funds or
    payments for similar benefits to or on behalf of employees; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} premiums, to the extent borne by the company, for
    group life, health, accident and other beneficial insurance for
    employees: (*See* text of account 70 (Welfare and Pension
    Appropriations)).

- [630-14]{.lawlabel} *Insurance*

  - [(1)]{.lawlabel} This account shall include premiums payable to
    insurance companies against any loss that may be sustained by the
    company.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the company provides self-insurance, the
    reserve accrual shall be debited to this account: (*See*
    Regulations, section 61).

- [630-15]{.lawlabel} *Bad Debts*

  This account shall be debited with amounts receivable and determined
  to be uncollectable.

- [630-16]{.lawlabel} *Taxes Other Than Income Taxes*

  This account shall include taxes levied under the laws of Canada or
  any province other than income taxes and [ad valorem]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} taxes on material.
