## Pensionable Service Order {#pensionable-service-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/65-262
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 5, 1961

Registration 1965-06-18

Pensionable Service Order

P.C. 1965-1116 1965-06-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Associate Minister of National Defence, pursuant to Vote 545 of the
Appropriation Act No. 5, 1961, is pleased hereby to revoke the order
made by Order in Council P.C. 1962-761 of 28th May, 1962[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^(1)^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make
the annexed [Pensionable Service Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-65-262) in
substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^(1)^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}SOR/62-196,
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, Vol. 96, No. 11,
  June 13, 1962
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Pensionable Service Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-65-262).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where a person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has elected to come under Part V of the Militia
  Pension Act or the Defence Services Pension Act, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has, while subject to Parts I and IV of either of
  those Acts, made a payment to the Receiver General that purported to
  be a deduction under any of those Parts in respect of a period of
  service,

that period of service shall be deemed to be pensionable service to his
credit for the purposes of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/C-17), if

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the payment referred to in paragraph (b) is
  sufficient to pay in full the amount of contributions required by
  section 3; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the payment referred to in paragraph (b) is not
  sufficient to pay in full the amount of contributions required by
  section 3, the person has paid the difference between the amount of
  contributions required by section 3 and the payment referred to in
  paragraph (b) in a lump sum to the Receiver General to the credit of
  the *Canadian Forces Superannuation Account*.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of contributions
required in respect of a period of service described in section 2 is
five per cent of the aggregate amount of pay, as defined in
paragraph (g) of section 36 of the [Defence Services Pension
Continuation Act](/eng/acts/D-1.3), received by the contributor during
the period of service.
