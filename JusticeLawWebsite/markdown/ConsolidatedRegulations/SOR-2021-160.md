## Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission Accessibility Reporting Regulations {#canadian-radio-television-and-telecommunications-commission-accessibility-reporting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-160
:::

[ACCESSIBLE CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/A-0.6)

Registration 2021-06-23

Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission
Accessibility Reporting Regulations
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, a copy of the proposed Canadian Radio-television and
Telecommunications Commission Accessibility Reporting Regulations,
substantially in the annexed form, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on March 6, 2021, and a
reasonable opportunity was given to regulated entities and other
interested persons to make representations to the Canadian
Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission with respect to the
proposed Regulations;

Therefore, the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission, pursuant to sections 45 and 54 of the [Accessible Canada
Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4366_hq_29071){#fn_81000-2-4366_hq_29071-ID0EDBCA},
makes the annexed Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission Accessibility Reporting Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1302367 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Accessible Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[broadcasting entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[broadcasting entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity or
      person that is a member of one of the classes established under
      subsection 2(1).  ([entité de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian
      Radio-television and Telecommunications
      Commission. ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person employed by a
      regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the
      Act and includes a [[dependent contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
      as defined in subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2), but does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person employed under a program designated by
        the employer as a student employment program; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a student employed solely during their vacation
        periods. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[regulated broadcasting entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[regulated broadcasting entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a

  :   [[regulated telecommunications entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  :   [[telecommunications entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity

      subsection 18(1). ([entité de télécommunication]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Web Content Accessibility
      Guidelines, published by the World Wide Web Consortium, as amended
      from time to time. ([WCAG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of document incorporated
  by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, if a document that is available
  in both official languages is incorporated by reference as amended
  from time to time, any amendment to that document is incorporated only
  when the amendment is available in both official languages.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Broadcasting Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1302383 .Part}

### [Classes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302384 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes --- broadcasting entity

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Part, the following classes are established:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class B1 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in any of paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (c) of
    the Act that carries on a broadcasting undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class B2 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that carries on a broadcasting undertaking and is not a member of
    any other class;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class B3 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that carries on a broadcasting undertaking and has submitted an
    attestation to the Commission that it has 10 or more employees but
    fewer than 100 employees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Class B4 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that carries on a broadcasting undertaking and has submitted an
    attestation to the Commission that it has fewer than 10 employees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated broadcasting entity submits an
  attestation that it employs a quantity of employees that qualifies it
  as a member of a particular class, that entity is deemed to have
  always been a member of that class and has the obligations of entities
  in that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations before deemed change

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if a regulated broadcasting
  entity published an accessibility plan while it was a member of a
  particular class, it must fulfill its obligations relating to the
  feedback process and progress report with respect to that
  accessibility plan as though it were still a member of that class.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day fixed --- broadcasting entity

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purposes of subsection 42(1) of the Act,
  the day fixed is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class B1 regulated entity, June 1,
    2022;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class B2 regulated entity, June 1,
    2022; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class B3 regulated entity, June 1,
    2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New broadcasting entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an entity or person qualifies as a Class B1, Class
  B2 or Class B3 broadcasting entity on a day after the day fixed under
  subsection (1) for that class, the day fixed for the purposes of
  subsection 42(1) of the Act in respect of that entity or person is
  June 1 of the calendar year after the calendar year in which the
  entity or person qualified.

### [Accessibility Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302404 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A regulated broadcasting
entity must include a header in its accessibility plan for each element
of the plan that is required under subsections 42(1), (5) and (9) of the
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of accessibility plan

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A regulated broadcasting
entity must electronically publish its most recent accessibility plan in
clear, simple and concise language

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
  operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
  the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a conspicuous manner that makes the plan
  accessible on the digital platform either directly on the homepage or
  by way of a hyperlink on that homepage; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
  conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of updated accessibility plan

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A regulated broadcasting
entity must prepare and publish an updated version of its accessibility
plan no later than the third anniversary of the day on which the plan
was last required to be published.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Commission

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A regulated broadcasting
entity must, by electronic means, notify the Commission of the
publication of each version of its accessibility plan within 48 hours of
publication and include in the notice a hyperlink to the URL of the
plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative formats

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may make a request that a regulated broadcasting entity make its
  accessibility plan available to them in print, large print, braille,
  audio format, electronic format that is compatible with adaptive
  technology that is intended to assist persons with disabilities, or
  any other format that the person and the entity agree upon and for
  which there is proof of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of requests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must make its
  accessibility plan available to the person in the requested format as
  soon as feasible after the request is received, but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in braille or
    an audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which the request
    is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in any other
    format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class B1 and B2 regulated entities, on the
      15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class B3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

### [Feedback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302431 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feedback process

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 43(1) of the Act, a regulated broadcasting
  entity must allow the receipt of feedback that is provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of feedback;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of feedback;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of feedback.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anonymous feedback

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must allow feedback
  to be provided anonymously.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person designated to receive feedback

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must designate and
  publicly identify a person to be responsible for receiving feedback on
  behalf of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of feedback

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must acknowledge
  receipt of feedback, other than anonymous feedback.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the [Personal Information Protection and
  Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) and the [Privacy
  Act](/eng/acts/P-21), the regulated broadcasting entity must ensure
  that the personal information of a person who provides feedback
  remains confidential, unless the person consents to the disclosure of
  their personal information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of feedback process

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulated broadcasting entity must, for the purposes of subsection
  43(2) of the Act, electronically publish a description of its process
  for receiving feedback, in clear, simple and concise language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a conspicuous manner that makes the description
    accessible on the digital platform either directly on the homepage
    or by way of a hyperlink on that homepage; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
    conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must publish the
  description of its feedback process before the end of the day fixed
  under section 3 in respect of that entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated feedback process

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated broadcasting entity updates its
  feedback process, it must publish an updated description of its
  feedback process in the manner set out in subsection (1) as soon as
  feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative formats

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make a request that a regulated broadcasting entity make
  the description of its feedback process available to them in print,
  large print, braille, audio format, electronic format that is
  compatible with adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons
  with disabilities, or any other format that the person and the entity
  agree upon and for which there is proof of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of requests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated broadcasting entity must make the
  description of its feedback process available to the person in the
  requested format as soon as feasible after the request is received,
  but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in
    braille or an audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which
    the request is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in any
    other format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class B1 and B2 regulated entities, on the
      15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class B3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Commission

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A regulated broadcasting
entity must, by electronic means, notify the Commission of the
publication of the description of its feedback process or the
publication of an updated description of its feedback process within 48
hours of publication and include in the notice a hyperlink to the URL of
the description or updated description.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Telecommunications Entities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1302501 .Part}

### [Classes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302502 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes --- telecommunications entity

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Part, the following classes are established:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class T1 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in any of paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (c) of
    the Act that is a Canadian carrier or telecommunications service
    provider;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class T2 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that is a Canadian carrier or telecommunications service provider
    and is not a member of any other class;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class T3 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that is a Canadian carrier or telecommunications service provider
    and has submitted an attestation to the Commission that it has 10 or
    more employees but fewer than 100 employees, other than an entity
    that is a member of Class T5;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Class T4 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that is a Canadian carrier or telecommunications service provider
    and has submitted an attestation to the Commission that it has fewer
    than 10 employees, other than an entity that is a member of Class
    T5;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Class T5 is a class that consists of every
    regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
    that is a telecommunications service provider whose
    telecommunications service is limited to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a service that is offered without an explicit
      charge,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a service that is offered on a temporary basis
      only to individuals located on the entity's premises, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a service that does not allow individuals to
      engage autonomously in two-way voice telecommunications or to
      access the Internet autonomously.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated telecommunications entity submits an
  attestation that it employs a quantity of employees that qualifies it
  as a member of a particular class, that entity is deemed to have
  always been a member of that class and has the obligations of entities
  in that class.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations before deemed change

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if a regulated
  telecommunications entity published an accessibility plan while it was
  a member of a particular class, it must fulfill its obligations
  relating to the feedback process and progress report with respect to
  that accessibility plan as though it were still a member of that
  class.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day fixed --- telecommunications entity

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), for the
  purposes of subsection 51(1) of the Act, the day fixed is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class T1 regulated entity, June 1,
    2022;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class T2 regulated entity, June 1,
    2022; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a Class T3 regulated entity, June 1,
    2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New telecommunications entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an entity or person qualifies as a Class T1, Class
  T2 or Class T3 telecommunications entity on a day after the day fixed
  under subsection (1) for that class, the day fixed for the purposes of
  subsection 51(1) of the Act in respect of that entity or person is
  June 1 of the calendar year after the calendar year in which the
  entity or person qualified.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make a request that a regulated telecommunications entity
  make its accessibility plan available to them in print, large print,
  braille, audio format, electronic format that is compatible with
  adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons with
  disabilities, or any other format that the person and the entity agree
  upon and for which there is proof of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of requests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must make its
  accessibility plan available to the person in the requested format as
  soon as feasible after the request is received, but at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in braille or
    an audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which the request
    is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in any other
    format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class T1 and T2 regulated entities, on the
      15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class T3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

### [Feedback]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302553 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feedback process

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 52(1) of the Act, a regulated
  telecommunications entity must allow the receipt of feedback that is
  provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of feedback;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of feedback;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of feedback.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anonymous feedback

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must allow
  feedback to be provided anonymously.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person designated to receive feedback

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must
  designate and publicly identify a person to be responsible for
  receiving feedback on behalf of the entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgement of feedback

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must
  acknowledge receipt of feedback, other than anonymous feedback.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to the [Personal Information Protection and
  Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) and the [Privacy
  Act](/eng/acts/P-21), the regulated telecommunications entity must
  ensure that the personal information of a person who provides feedback
  remains confidential, unless the person consents to the disclosure of
  their personal information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of feedback process

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulated telecommunications entity must, for the purposes of
  subsection 52(2) of the Act, electronically publish a description of
  its process for receiving feedback, in clear, simple and concise
  language

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a conspicuous manner that makes the description
    accessible on the digital platform either directly on the homepage
    or by way of a hyperlink on that homepage; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
    conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for publication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must publish
  the description of its feedback process before the end of the day
  fixed under section 19 in respect of that entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated feedback process

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the regulated telecommunications entity updates
  its feedback process, it must publish an updated description of its
  feedback process in the manner set out in subsection (1) as soon as
  feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative formats

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may make a request that a regulated telecommunications entity
  make the description of its feedback process available to them in
  print, large print, braille, audio format, electronic format that is
  compatible with adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons
  with disabilities, or any other format that the person and the entity
  agree upon and for which there is proof of the agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and manner of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
    address designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by completing the online form, if any, that is
    available on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
    operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
    the public; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other means that is designated by the entity
    for the receipt of requests.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated telecommunications entity must make the
  description of its feedback process available to the person in the
  requested format as soon as feasible after the request is received
  but, at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in
    braille or an audio format, on the 45th day after the day on which
    the request is received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a description in any
    other format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for Class T1 and T2 regulated entities, on the
      15th day after the day on which the request is received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for Class T3 regulated entities, on the 20th day
      after the day on which the request is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Commission

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A regulated
telecommunications entity must, by electronic means, notify the
Commission of the publication of the description of its feedback process
or the publication of an updated description of its feedback process
within 48 hours of publication and include in the notice a hyperlink to
the URL of the description or updated description.

### [Progress Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1302596 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A regulated
telecommunications entity must include a header in its progress report
for each element set out in subsections 51(1) and 53(4) and (5) of the
Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of progress report

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** A regulated
telecommunications entity must, for the purposes of subsection 53(1) of
the Act, electronically publish its most recent progress report in
clear, simple and concise language

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the entity owns,
  operates or controls and that it uses to communicate information to
  the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a conspicuous manner that makes the progress
  report accessible on the digital platform either directly on the
  homepage or by way of a hyperlink on that homepage; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements of Level AA
  conformance that are set out in the WCAG.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative formats

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  make its progress report available to them in print, large print,
  braille, audio format, electronic format that is compatible with
  adaptive technology that is intended to assist persons with
  disabilities, or any other format that the person and the entity agree
  upon and for which there is proof of the agreement.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by calling the main telephone number that the
    entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the telephone
    number designated by the entity for the receipt of requests;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending an email to the main email address that
    the entity uses to communicate with the public or, if any, the email
