## Industrial Product Price Indices Adjustment Regulations {#industrial-product-price-indices-adjustment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-09-27

Regulations Prescribing the Manner of Adjusting the Industrial Product
Price Indices for Motor Gasoline and Diesel Oil

P.C. 1990-2069  1990-09-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 50.1(3)(b)[[Footnote

annexed [Regulations prescribing the manner of adjusting the Industrial
Product Price Indices for Motor Gasoline and Diesel
Oil](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-672).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-927222 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Industrial Product Price Indices Adjustment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-672).

## [Adjustment of the Industrial Product Price Index for Motor Gasoline]{.HTitleText1} {#h-927224 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 50.1(2)(a) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
Industrial Product Price Index for Motor Gasoline released by Statistics
Canada for each of the twelve months immediately preceding October 1985
shall be adjusted by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the index by 0.8804; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} rounding the result obtained under paragraph (a) to
  the nearest one-tenth or, if the result obtained is equidistant from
  two one-tenths, to the greater thereof.

## [Adjustment of the Industrial Product Price Index for Diesel Oil]{.HTitleText1} {#h-927228 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 50.1(2)(a) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
Industrial Product Price Index for Diesel Oil released by Statistics
Canada for each of the twelve months immediately preceding October 1985
shall be adjusted by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} multiplying the index by 0.9681; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} rounding the result obtained under paragraph (a) to
  the nearest one-tenth or, if the result obtained is equidistant from
  two one-tenths, to the greater thereof.
