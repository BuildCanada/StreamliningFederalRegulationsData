## Red Tape Reduction Regulations {#red-tape-reduction-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[RED TAPE REDUCTION ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.5)

Registration 2015-07-23

Red Tape Reduction Regulations

P.C. 2015-1125 2015-07-22

:::: section

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to sections 7 and 10 of
the [Red Tape Reduction Act](/eng/acts/R-4.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1995-E_hq_13458){#fn_81000-2-1995-E_hq_13458-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Red Tape Reduction
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-202).

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Red Tape Reduction
Act](/eng/acts/R-4.5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity that is
      necessary to demonstrate compliance with a
      regulation. ([activité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an interval of time during
      which an activity is expected to be completed by a business in
      accordance with a regulation. ([période]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total number of periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section, the total number of
  periods is determined by multiplying the number of times per year that
  the activity is expected to be completed by 10, which number is the
  number of years for which the calculation is being made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of administrative burden

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 5 of the Act, the cost of
  the administrative burden imposed by a regulation is the sum of the
  cost of each activity that is expected to be completed during the
  first 10 years after the regulation is registered and is calculated in
  accordance with the following formula:

  ::: {#822165 .Subsection}
  A × 0.142378 ÷ 1.07^B\ -\ 2012^

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the activity cost that is the sum of the cost for each activity
      for each period, and is calculated in accordance with the
      following formula:
      ::: {#822168 .NestedFormula}
      C × D × E ÷ 1.07^F\ ÷\ G^

      where

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the estimated hourly cost of labour, adjusted to 2012 price
          levels using the Consumer Price Index set out by Statistics
          Canada in CANSIM Table 326-0021, as amended from time to time,
          that is required in a period so that a business is able to
          complete the activity within that period;

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the estimated number of hours required in a period so that
          the business is able to complete the activity within that
          period;

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the estimated number of businesses that are required in a
          period to complete the activity within that period;

      [F]{.dfn}
      :   is the specific period, out of the total number of periods
          determined in accordance with subsection (2), for which the
          calculation is being made; and

      [G]{.dfn}
      :   is the number of times per year that the activity is required
          to be completed; and
      :::

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the year the regulation is registered.
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}24-month deadline

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A regulation must be
amended or repealed in accordance with section 5 of the Act within 24
months after the day on which a regulation that imposes a new
administrative burden is registered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offset

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  regulation has been amended or repealed before a regulation that
  imposes a new administrative burden is made and the prior repeal or
  amendment offsets the cost of the new administrative burden that is
  imposed, one or more regulations do not need to be amended or repealed
  in accordance with subsection 5(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repealed regulation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulation has been repealed before a new
  regulation that imposes an administrative burden is made, a regulation
  does not need to be repealed in accordance with subsection 5(2) of the
  Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of section 5 of Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 of the Act
applies to any regulation that is made, amended or repealed after March
31, 2012.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may
exempt a regulation from the application of section 5 of the Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the regulation is related to tax or tax
  administration;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if Her Majesty in right of Canada has no discretion
  regarding the requirements that must be included in the regulation due
  to international or legal obligations, including the imposition of
  international sanctions or the implementation of Supreme Court of
  Canada decisions; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in emergency, unique or exceptional circumstances,
  including if compliance with that section would compromise public
  health, public safety or the Canadian economy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of annual report

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The annual report referred
to in section 9 of the Act must contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the increases and decreases in the cost
  of administrative burden that results from regulatory changes that are
  made in accordance with section 5 of the Act within the 12-month
  period ending on March 31 of the year in which the report is made
  public; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of regulations that are amended or
  repealed as a result of regulatory changes that are made in accordance
  with section 5 of the Act within that 12-month period.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-822194 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
