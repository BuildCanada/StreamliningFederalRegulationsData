## Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#security-certificate-transfer-fee-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-256
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-05-07

Regulations Prescribing the Maximum Fee that a Company May Charge for a
Security Certificate Issued in Respect of a Transfer

P.C. 1992-905  1992-05-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 88(2) and section 531 of
the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations prescribing the maximum fee that a company
may charge for a security certificate issued in respect of a
transfer](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-256), effective June 1, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 45
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-941707 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Trust and Loan
Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-256).

## [Maximum Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-941709 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A company may charge a fee
not exceeding five dollars for a security certificate issued in respect
of a transfer.
