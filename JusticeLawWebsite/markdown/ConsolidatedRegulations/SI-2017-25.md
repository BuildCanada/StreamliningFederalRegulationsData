## Office of the Governor General's Secretary Exclusion Approval Order {#office-of-the-governor-generals-secretary-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-25
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2017-05-17

Office of the Governor General's Secretary Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2017-458 2017-05-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, pursuant to
section 20 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBDHA}, approves

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBDHA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application of the provisions of that Act to
  the positions excluded pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
  2010-1113[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824-ID0EBBBHA}
  and to the persons occupying them; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824-ID0EBBBHA){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824}SI/2010-73
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion of positions designated by the position
  numbers 00025450 to 00025460 in the Office of the Governor General's
  Secretary and the persons occupying those positions from the
  application of that Act, with the exception of the definitions
  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[public
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of sections 17
  to 21, 54 and 55, 68 to 72, subsection 111(1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214-ID0EBBAHA},
  sections 112 to 116, section 118, sections 120 to 122, and section 134
  and 135 of that Act.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214-ID0EBBAHA){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214}S.C.
    2014, c. 2, s. 55
    :::

Whereas the Public Service Commission, having decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EHAGA} to positions designated by
the position numbers 00025450 to 00025460 in the Office of the Governor
General's Secretary and to the persons occupying those positions, has
decided, pursuant to subsection 20(1) of that Act, to exclude those
positions and persons from the application of the Act, with the
exception of the definitions [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
[[public service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of
sections 17 to 21, 54 and 55, 68 to 72, subsection 111(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214-ID0EBAGA},
sections 112 to 116, section 118, sections 120 to 122, and section 134
and 135 of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAFA}, the Public Service
Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBCEA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reapplies the provisions of that Act to the positions
  excluded pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2010-1113[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_18824-ID0EBABEA}
  and to the persons occupying them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes positions designated by the position numbers
  00025450 to 00025460 in the Office of the Governor General's Secretary
  and the persons occupying those positions from the application of that
  Act with the exception of the definitions
  [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[public
  service]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of sections 17
  to 21, 54 and 55, 68 to 72, subsection 111(1)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214){#fn_81000-2-2836_hq_19214-ID0EBAAEA},
  sections 112 to 116, section 118, sections 120 to 122, and section 134
  and 135 of that Act.

Ottawa, February 21, 2017
