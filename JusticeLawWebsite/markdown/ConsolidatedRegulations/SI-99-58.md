## Order Designating the Minister of Foreign Affairs, the Minister of National Defence and the Minister of Public Works and Government Services as the Ministers for Purposes of Certain Sections of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-the-minister-of-national-defence-and-the-minister-of-public-works-and-government-services-as-the-ministers-for-purposes-of-certain-sections-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/99-58
:::

[ANTI-PERSONNEL MINES CONVENTION IMPLEMENTATION ACT](/eng/acts/A-11.5)

Registration 1999-06-09

Order Designating the Minister of Foreign Affairs, the Minister of
National Defence and the Minister of Public Works and Government
Services as the Ministers for Purposes of Certain Sections of the Act

P.C. 1999-952 1999-05-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 5 of the [Anti-Personnel Mines
Convention Implementation Act](/eng/acts/A-11.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBDA}, hereby
designates

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBDA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  33
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Foreign Affairs, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
  all provisions of that Act, other than sections 8, 9, 10 and 22;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of National Defence, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
  sections 8, 9, 10 and 18 of that Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Public Works and Government Services,
  a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for
  the purposes of sections 18 and 22 of that Act.
