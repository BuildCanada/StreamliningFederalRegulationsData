## Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Order {#ontario-flue-cured-tobacco-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 183
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Flue-Cured Tobacco Produced in Ontario
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536165 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._183).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536167 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured
    Tobacco Growers\' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco
    Growers\' Marketing Plan established and amended from time to time
    pursuant to the Act, and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to
    give effect to the Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means unmanufactured flue-cured
    tobacco produced in Ontario. ([tabac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536174 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of tobacco in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation
to the marketing of tobacco locally within that province under the Act
and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1105, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536178 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board may, in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with
respect to the marketing of tobacco in interprovincial and export trade,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix and impose by order and collect levies or charges
  from persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in
  the production or marketing of tobacco and for that purpose classify
  those persons into groups and, by order, fix the levies or charges
  payable by the members of the different groups in different amounts;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Board and the Commodity Board, including the creation of reserves, and
  the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or disposal
  of tobacco, and the equalization or adjustment among tobacco producers
  of moneys realized from the sale of tobacco during such period or
  periods of time as the Board or the Commodity Board may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1105, s. 2
