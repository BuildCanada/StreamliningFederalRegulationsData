## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634314 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Diplomatic, Consular and International Organizations' Property
Grants Order](/eng/regulations/SI-79-19).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634316 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[diplomatic property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diplomatic property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property in Canada owned by a foreign
      government or the head of the diplomatic mission of a foreign
      government and used as

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices of the diplomatic
        mission, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of the
        mission

    of that government,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} real property in Canada owned by a foreign
      government and used as

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices of a consular post or
        trade commission, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of a
        consular post or trade commission

    of that government, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} real property in Canada owned by an international
      organization and used as

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the chancery or offices, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the official residence of the head of a
        mission

    of that international organization; ([propriété
    diplomatique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    organization within the meaning of the Privileges and Immunities
    (International Organizations) Act. ([organisation
    internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Grants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634332 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, on
receipt of an application from a municipality in a form prescribed by
the Minister of Finance, the Secretary of State for External Affairs may
pay to the municipality, in respect of diplomatic property,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} grants in lieu of real estate taxes and local
  improvement costs in the amount that might be paid pursuant to the
  Municipal Grants Act if the diplomatic property were federal property
  as defined in that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} grants in lieu of development or redevelopment taxes
  of a type and in the amount that might be paid pursuant to the
  [Development Tax and Redevelopment Tax Grant
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._322) if those Regulations
  applied to the diplomatic property.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, on
receipt of an application from a province in a form prescribed by the
Minister of Finance, the Secretary of State for External Affairs may pay
to the province, in respect of diplomatic property, a grant in lieu of
real estate taxes in the amount that might be paid pursuant to the [Real
Property Grants Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._343) if the
diplomatic property were federal property as defined in those
Regulations.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where any real estate
taxes, local improvement costs or development or redevelopment taxes
referred to in section 3 or 4 are payable over a period of more than one
year and the person liable to pay those taxes or costs has the option to
pay them in annual instalments together with interest at a rate
prescribed by a municipality or province, the Secretary of State for
External Affairs may pay to the municipality or province the grants
payable under section 3 or 4 in annual instalments together with
interest at the rate prescribed by the municipality or province.
