## Order Transferring to the Department of National Health and Welfare the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service in the Social Service Programs Branch of the Department of Employment and Immigration {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-national-health-and-welfare-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-in-the-social-service-programs-branch-of-the-department-of-employment-and-immigration .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-167
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-08-25

Order Transferring to the Department of National Health and Welfare the
Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service in the
Social Service Programs Branch of the Department of Employment and
Immigration

P.C. 1993-1667  1993-08-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement
and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), the control and supervision
of certain portions of the public service were transferred from the
Department of National Health and Welfare to the Department of
Employment and Immigration by Order in Council P.C. 1993-1488 of
June 25, 1993[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-167_e_hq_6519){#fn_SI-93-167_e_hq_6519-ID0EBBBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-167_e_hq_6519-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SI-93-167_e_hq_6519}SI/93-142,
  1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3234
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Department of
National Health and Welfare the control and supervision of those
portions of the public service in the Social Service Programs Branch of
the Department of Employment and Immigration known as the Family
Violence Prevention Division, the Children's Bureau and attendant
Brighter Futures programs, the Community Action Program for Children,
HIV/AIDS Research and Development and the Seniors Independence Research
Program.
