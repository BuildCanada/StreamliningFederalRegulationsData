## [Equity of a Company]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equity

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), the equity of a
company is the sum of the shareholders' equity of the company and the
minority interests in entities controlled by the company, as they appear
in the consolidated financial statements of the company.

Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), as enacted by section 569 of the [Financial
