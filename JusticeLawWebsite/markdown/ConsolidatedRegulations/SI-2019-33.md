## Order Designating Certain Members of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to be Minister for the Purposes of this Act and Certain Sections of that Act in Certain Circumstances: (1) the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness; (2) the Minister of Transport; and (3) the Minister of Foreign Affairs {#order-designating-certain-members-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-to-be-minister-for-the-purposes-of-this-act-and-certain-sections-of-that-act-in-certain-circumstances-1-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-2-the-minister-of-transport-and-3-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-33
:::

[PRECLEARANCE ACT, 2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32)

Registration 2019-06-12

Order Designating Certain Members of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to be Minister for the Purposes of this Act and Certain Sections
of that Act in Certain Circumstances: (1) the Minister of Public Safety
and Emergency Preparedness; (2) the Minister of Transport; and (3) the
Minister of Foreign Affairs

P.C. 2019-619 2019-05-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 4(1) of the [Preclearance Act,
  2016](/eng/acts/P-19.32)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-378X_hq_24417){#fn_81000-2-378X_hq_24417-ID0EBBCAA},
  designates the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, a
  member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the responsible
  Minister for the purposes of that Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-378X_hq_24417-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_81000-2-378X_hq_24417}S.C.
    2017, c. 27
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsections 4(2) and (3) of that Act,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Public Safety and
    Emergency Preparedness, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for
    Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of sections 6 to 8 of that
    Act in circumstances concerning highways, roads, bridges, tunnels
    and paths, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Transport, a member of
    the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the
    purposes of sections 6 to 8 of that Act in circumstances concerning
    marine navigation or infrastructure, aerodromes and railways and
    railway terminals; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 4(2) of that Act, designates
  the Minister of Foreign Affairs, a member of the Queen's Privy Council
  for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of section 41 of that
  Act.
