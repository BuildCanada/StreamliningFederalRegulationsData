## [Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832836 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Restraint systems and
booster seats for motor vehicles described in column 1 of the table to
this section must meet the requirements set out in the Parts of the
[Motor Vehicle Restraint Systems and Booster Seats Safety
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-90) that are mentioned in
column 2.
