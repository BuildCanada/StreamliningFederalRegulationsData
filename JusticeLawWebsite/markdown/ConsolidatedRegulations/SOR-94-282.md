## Ocean Data and Services Fees Order, 1993 {#ocean-data-and-services-fees-order-1993 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-282
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-04-13

Order Prescribing the Fees to be Paid for the Provision of Ocean Data
and Services
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1988-440 of March 10, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-282_e_hq_5262){#fn_SOR-94-282_e_hq_5262-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid for the

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963527 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ocean Data and Services Fees Order,
1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-282).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963529 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[CPU]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CPU]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means central processing
    unit; ([UCT]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TWL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TWL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Tides and Water
    Levels. ([MNE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963533 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Department of Fisheries and Oceans provides a person with any
  ocean data or analyses set out in column I of an item of Part I of the
  schedule, that person shall pay to the Minister of Fisheries and
  Oceans, in addition to any fees required by subsection (3), the fees
  set out in columns II and III of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Department of Fisheries and Oceans provides
  a person with a service set out in column I of an item of Part II of
  the schedule that is necessary to provide any data or analyses not set
  out in Part I of the schedule, that person shall pay to the Minister
  of Fisheries and Oceans, in addition to any fees required by
  subsection (3), the fee set out in column II of that item.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Department of Fisheries and Oceans provides
  a person with ocean data or analyses, that person shall pay to the
  Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, in addition to any fees required by
  subsection (1) or (2), for any processing or materials set out in
  column I of an item of Part III of the schedule, the fee set out in
  column II of that item.
