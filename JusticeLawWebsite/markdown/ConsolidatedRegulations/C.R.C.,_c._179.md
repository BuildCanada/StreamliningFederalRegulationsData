## Ontario Cheese Order {#ontario-cheese-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 179
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Cheese Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536079 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Cheese Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._179).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536081 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Milk Act, 1965 of Ontario;
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cheese]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cheese]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cheese of every variety
    produced in Ontario; ([fromage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Milk Commission of
    Ontario; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Milk
    Marketing Board established by Order in Council of the Lieutenant
    Governor of Ontario pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Cheese-Marketing Regulations, being O. Reg. 44-66 of February 17,
    1966, made by the Commission pursuant to the Act. ([règlement
    provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536088 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission and the
Commodity Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of cheese
in interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with
respect to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by the Commission
under sections 3, 4 and 8 to 12 of the Provincial Regulations and by the
Commodity Board under sections 5 to 7 and subsection 8(3) of the
Provincial Regulations in relation to the marketing of cheese locally
within that Province.
