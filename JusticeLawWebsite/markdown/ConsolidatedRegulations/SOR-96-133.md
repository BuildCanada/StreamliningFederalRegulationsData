## Fort Frances Airport Zoning Regulations {#fort-frances-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-133
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1996-02-26

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Fort Frances Airport

P.C. 1996-259 1996-02-26
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EHBBA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed
[Regulations respecting zoning at Fort Frances
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-133), substantially in the form annexed
hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on September 30 and
October 7, 1995, and in two successive issues of the Fort Frances Times
on November 8 and 15, 1995, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of Transport with respect thereto;

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EHBBA){#footnote1star_e}R.S., c.
  33 (1st Supp.), s. 1
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2star_e){#footnote2star_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Fort Frances
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-133).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979075 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fort Frances Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-133).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979077 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fort Frances Airport, in
      the Township of Miscampbell, in the District of Rainy River, in
      the Province of Ontario; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de référence de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary plane
      located above and in the  immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      plane is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
      ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, that is
      prepared for the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular
      direction, which rectangular portion is more particularly
      described in Part IV of the schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and the approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly  described in Part V of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 340.9 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979087 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all land, including public road allowances, that is adjacent to or in
the vicinity of the airport, which land is more particularly described
in Part VI of the schedule.

## [Building Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979089 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Aeronautical Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979094 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit any part of that land
to be used or developed in a manner that causes interference with any
signals or communications to and from

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any aircraft; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any facilities used to provide services relating to
  aeronautics.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979098 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth that is on any land to which these Regulations apply grows to a
height that exceeds in elevation at the location of the object any of
the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister of Transport may
require that the owner or lessee of the land remove the excess growth.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Fort Frances Airport Zoning Plan
No. 59-011 91-60, dated January 14, 1993, is a point which may be
located by:

commencing at the threshold of runway 30, being the southeastern end of
runway 12-30 at Fort Frances Airport, having Universal Transverse
Mercator (UTM) coordinates of E 468 118.65 and N 5 388 517.56;

thence northwesterly along the centre line of said runway 12-30 684.45 m
to a point;

thence southwesterly and perpendicular to the centre line of said
runway 12-30 40 m to the airport reference point, which point has the
UTM coordinates of E 467 542.09 and N 5 388 888.00.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Fort Frances Airport Zoning Plan
No. 59-011 91-60, dated January 14, 1993, are planes abutting each end
of the strips associated with runways 12-30 and 03-21 and are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 12 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 740 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 336 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 43.5 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 30 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 800 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 345 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 45 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 03 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 890 m measured horizontally from
  the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line
  being 119 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal
  line being 44.5 m above the elevation at the end of the strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 21 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 20 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 858 m measured horizontally from
  the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line
  being 115.8 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 42.9 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Fort Frances Airport Zoning Plan No. 59-011
91-60, dated January 14, 1993, is an imaginary plane established at a
constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference
point, except that, where that plane is less than 9 m above the surface
of the ground, the outer surface is located at 9 m above the surface of
the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Fort Frances Airport Zoning Plan No. 59-011 91-60,
dated January 14, 1993, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 12-30 is 150 m in
  width, 75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 488.9 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 03-21 is 60 m in
  width, 30 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  860.05 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The transitional surfaces, shown on Fort Frances Airport Zoning Plan
No. 59-011 91-60, dated January 14, 1993, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces associated with
  runway 12-30 are inclined planes rising at a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the centre
  line and projected centre line of the strip, extending upward and
  outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach surfaces
  to an intersection with the outer surface or with another transitional
  surface of an adjoining strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces associated with
  runway 03-21 are inclined planes rising at a ratio of 1 m measured
  vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at right angles to the centre
  line and projected centre line of the strip, extending upward and
  outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach surfaces
  to an intersection with the outer surface or with another transitional
  surface of an adjoining strip.
