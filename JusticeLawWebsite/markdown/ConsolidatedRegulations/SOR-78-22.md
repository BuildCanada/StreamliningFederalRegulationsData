## Indian Economic Development Direct Loan Order {#indian-economic-development-direct-loan-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-22
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1970

Registration 1977-12-29

Terms and Conditions Respecting the Making of Direct Loans for the
Purpose of the Economic Development of Indians

P.C. 1977-3609 1977-12-22
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indians Affairs and Northern Development and the
Treasury Board, pursuant to Vote L53b of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970,
is pleased hereby to approve the annexed [Terms and conditions
respecting the making of direct loans for the purpose of the economic
development of Indians](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-22).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861865 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These terms and conditions
may be cited as the [Indian Economic Development Direct Loan
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-22).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861867 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Indian Economic
    Development Account in the Consolidated Revenue Fund of Canada
    established pursuant to Vote L53b of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970;
    ([Compte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means as applicant under section 3
    for a direct loan; ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant who has received
    a direct loan pursuant to this Order; ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, regardless of a
    change in ownership or control,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any business carried on in Canada for gain or
      profit,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative association incorporated under a
      law of the Parliament of Canada or the legislature of a province,
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business of a profession recognized as such
      under a law of the Parliament of Canada or the legislature of a
      province,

    but does not include

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a business having as its object the furtherance
      of a charitable or religious purpose, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a business carried on for the purpose of money
      lending, insurance or finance; ([entreprise
      commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan from the Account;
    ([prêt direct]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct loan agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement by
    the Minister to make a direct loan; ([convention de prêt
    direct]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian as defined in the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means simple interest;
    ([intérêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861884 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any individual,
corporation, partnership or Indian band whose activities contribute or
may contribute to the economic development of Indians may apply for a
direct loan by submitting an application in a form satisfactory to the
Minister, signed by the applicant and containing such information as
will enable the Minister to satisfy himself in respect of the matters
referred to in subsection 4(1) or (2).

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may enter into a direct loan agreement with an applicant
  where the Minister is satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the direct loan is required to finance a business
    enterprise that will or may contribute to the economic development
    of Indians or to provide equity in a business enterprise that will
    or may contribute to the economic development of Indians;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business enterprise other than
      the business of a profession, is unable to obtain the required
      financing on reasonable terms and conditions except under this
      Order, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the business of a profession, is
      unable to obtain a loan, except under this Order, by reason only
      that he is prevented by section 89 of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5) from providing satisfactory security;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the direct loan is not for the purpose of
    refinancing an existing indebtedness except where the refinancing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is incidental to the main purpose of the direct
      loan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of the Minister, improves the
      security to be obtained by the Minister in respect of the direct
      loan,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing direct loan or a
      direct loan made under this Order, is, in the opinion of the
      Minister, desirable, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is approved by the Minister of Finance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of the direct loan, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amounts outstanding in respect of all direct
      loans

    does not exceed the total amount authorized by any Act to be
    outstanding from the Account at the time the direct loan was made;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of the direct loan,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed
      amount in respect of all other direct loans made pursuant to Vote
      L53b of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed
      amount of all guaranteed loans made pursuant to Vote L53b of
      Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} grants, contributions or other payments made or
      authorized by the Minister for the purposes of Indian economic
      development,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed amount
      in respect of all loans, grants, contributions and development
      incentives made under statutory authority vested in the Minister
      of Regional Economic Expansion, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} loans made or guaranteed under statutory
      authority by any federal Department, Crown Corporation or Agency

    to or on behalf of the applicant or in respect of the business
    enterprise for the purpose of which the direct loan is required
    exceeds \$500,000 or may, in the opinion of the Minister, be
    reasonably expected to exceed \$500,000, the direct loan has been
    approved by the Treasury Board; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is able to contribute from his own
    assets at least 20 per cent of the estimated cost of the entire
    business enterprise for which the direct loan is to be made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may enter into a direct loan agreement
  with an applicant who is unable to make the contribution specified in
  paragraph (1)(f) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that the conditions set
    out in paragraphs (1)(a) to (e) have been met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is an Indian, a group of Indians, an
    Indian band or a corporation controlled by one or more Indians; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the direct loan is, in the opinion of the Minister,
    warranted by the circumstances.

## [Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861914 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A borrower shall pay
interest on a direct loan, both before and after maturity,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the aggregate of the principal amount of the
  loan and the amounts outstanding in respect of all direct loans to the
  borrower does not exceed \$75,000, at a rate of interest equal to one
  per cent greater than the simple average prime commercial lending
  rate, to the nearest one-quarter of one per cent, of the seven major
  Canadian chartered banks, established at the close of business on the
  last banking day of December, March, June or September, whichever such
  day is the nearest preceding day to the day the loan agreement is
  signed by or on behalf of the Minister; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the aggregate of the principle amount of the
  loan and the amounts outstanding in respect of all direct loans to the
  borrower exceeds \$75,000, at a composite of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the rate of interest prescribed by paragraph (a),
    in respect of the first \$75,000, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a rate of interest two per cent greater than the
    rate of interest prescribed by paragraph (a), in respect of the
    amount in excess of \$75,000.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-327, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A borrower shall make
repayments on the principal amount of a direct loan not less frequently
than annually.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A borrower shall repay a
direct loan within 15 years from the payment thereof or within such
longer period as is approved by the Treasury Board.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861924 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may require that a borrower give as security for a direct
  loan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mortgage on real property or a chattel mortgage
    on personal property;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee specifically listing the interest rate
    and the repayment terms;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note specifically listing the interest
    rate and the repayment terms; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any security in place of or in addition to that
    referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c) that the Minister deems
    appropriate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The security required by the Minister under
  subsection (1) may be of whatever rank and may be subject to whatever
  charges the Minister deems appropriate from time to time.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A borrower shall not,
without the consent of the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell, lease or otherwise dispose of or encumber any
  security given for the direct loan or any property purchased with the
  proceeds of the direct loan; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make capital expenditures other than those authorized
  by the direct loan agreement.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861935 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A borrower shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep and maintain books and accounting records in a
  form satisfactory to the Minister and permit the Minister or any
  person authorized by him to examine those books and records at any
  reasonable time until the direct loan is repaid in full; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide invoices, receipts, cancelled cheques or
  other documents satisfactory to the Minister as evidence that the
  proceeds of the direct loan will be or were used for the purpose set
  out in the application therefor.

## [Revision of Loan Terms and Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861939 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, at the
request of a borrower, accept repayment of all or any part of a direct
loan at a date earlier than the date required by the direct loan
agreement or any revision or alteration thereof.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 13,
where a borrower is in default in respect of any repayment or condition
of a direct loan, the entire amount of the balance outstanding on the
loan shall, at the option of the Minister, become due and repayable.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where a borrower is in
default in respect of the repayment of a direct loan or informs the
Minister that any of the terms of the loan agreement are such that the
borrower may have to default thereon, and the Minister is of the opinion
that a revision or alteration of any of the terms of the agreement may
enable the borrower to meet his obligations thereunder, the Minister
may, with the consent of the borrower, revise or alter the terms of the
agreement by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} increasing or decreasing the periods between
  repayments, except that repayments shall be due no less frequently
  than annually;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} extending the time within which the direct loan is to
  be repaid, but no such extension shall be granted to a date that is
  more than 15 years from the date of the loan agreement without the
  approval of the Treasury Board; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} reducing the amount of each repayment or increasing
  that amount where the period between each repayment is being
  increased.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where a borrower is in
default in respect of any repayment on a direct loan and the entire
amount of the balance outstanding on the loan becomes repayable pursuant
to section 12, the Minister may take such steps, whether by legal
proceedings or otherwise, as he considers necessary to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} effect collection of the balance outstanding;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain additional security;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} realize on any or all of the security he has taken;
  or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} effect any compromise with, or grant any concession
  to, any person other than the borrower.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861951 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
maintain a registry for the purpose of recording loans made under these
Regulations.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall in
January of each year prepare a report with respect to the administration
of this Order during the immediately preceding fiscal year and shall
include that report in the report submitted by him to Parliament
pursuant to section 7 of the [Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development Act](/eng/acts/I-6).
