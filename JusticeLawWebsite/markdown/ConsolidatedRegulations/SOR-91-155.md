## Secondary Lead Smelter Release Regulations {#secondary-lead-smelter-release-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-155
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1991-02-21

Regulations Respecting the Release into the Ambient Air of Lead from
Secondary Lead Smelters

P.C. 1991-303 1991-02-21
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBCA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on September 15, 1990, a copy
of the proposed [Regulations respecting the release into the ambient air
of lead from secondary lead smelters](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-155),
substantially in the form annexed hereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFBCA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 16
  (4th Supp.)
  :::

And Whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of that Act, the annexed Regulations do not regulate an
aspect of a substance that is regulated by or under any other Act of
Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EIBAA} of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA}, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
National Health and Welfare, and after the federal-provincial advisory
committee has been provided an opportunity to render its advice under
section 6 of that Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the Secondary Lead
Smelter National Emission Standards Regulations, C.R.C., c. 412, and to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the release into the ambient
air of lead from secondary lead smelters](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-155),
in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EIBAA){#footnote2_e}S.C.
  1989, c. 9, s. 2
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932850 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Secondary Lead Smelter Release
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-155).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932852 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[ambient air]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ambient air]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the atmosphere surrounding
    the earth but does not include the atmosphere within a structure or
    within any underground space; ([air ambiant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a condition that does not include
    uncombined water vapour; ([à sec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lead alloy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lead alloy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an alloy of lead that
    contains forty per cent or more of lead by weight; ([alliage de
    plomb]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[normal cubic metre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[normal cubic metre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[norm.
    m^3^]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a gas, the quantity
    of gas occupying a volume of one cubic metre at 25°C and at a
    pressure of 760 mm of mercury; ([mètre cube normal]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"} or [m^3^ norm]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[particulate matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[particulate matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means particulate matter
    containing lead or lead in combination with any other substance;
    ([particules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reverberatory furnace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reverberatory furnace]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a stationary,
    rotating or rocking and tilting furnace; ([four à
    réverbère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[secondary lead smelter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secondary lead smelter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plant or
    factory in which lead-bearing scrap or lead-bearing materials, other
    than lead-bearing concentrates derived from a mining operation, are
    processed by metallurgical or chemical process into refined lead,
    lead alloys or lead oxide; ([fonderie de plomb de seconde
    fusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[undiluted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[undiluted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a condition that does not
    include air or other gases in excess of the quantity necessary for
    processing requirements at a secondary lead smelter. ([non
    dilué]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Release of Lead]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932862 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of
particulate matter that the owner or operator of a secondary lead
smelter releases into the ambient air shall not exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0.046 grams per normal cubic metre, in the case of
  operations involving the use of blast furnaces, cupolas or
  reverberatory furnaces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.023 grams per normal cubic metre, in the case of
  operations involving the use of holding furnaces, kettle furnaces or
  lead oxide production units or involving scrap handling and material
  handling, crushing, furnace tapping, furnace slagging, furnace
  cleaning or casting, whether released separately or in combination
  with releases from other sources,

measured dry and undiluted in accordance with the method described in
Standard Reference Methods for Source Testing: Measurement of Emissions
of Particulate Matter and Lead from Secondary Lead Smelters, Department
of the Environment Report EPS-1-AP-78-3 dated June 1979, as amended from
time to time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-364, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, the
concentration of lead in particulate matter that the owner or operator
of a secondary lead smelter releases into the ambient air from the
operations referred to in paragraphs 3(a) and (b) shall not exceed
sixty-three per cent by weight of the particulate matter measured by the
method described in Standard Reference Methods for Source Testing:
Measurement of Emissions of Particulate Matter and Lead from Secondary
Lead Smelters, Department of the Environment Report EPS-1-AP-78-3 dated
June 1979, as amended from time to time.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of lead
in particulate matter that is released from the operations referred to
in paragraphs 3(a) and (b) may exceed sixty-three per cent by weight of
the particulate matter where the quantity of that particulate matter
released from those operations is reduced so that the quantity of lead
released is equivalent to sixty-three per cent by weight of a
particulate matter release of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0.046 grams per normal cubic metre, in the case of
  the operations referred to in paragraph 3(a); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.023 grams per normal cubic metre, in the case of
  the operations referred to in paragraph 3(b).

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No particulate matter shall
be released into the ambient air from the storage of lead-bearing scrap
or lead-bearing material in or about a secondary lead smelter, other
than as a result of handling.

## [Malfunction or Breakdown]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932874 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding sections 3
and 4, the concentrations of particulate matter and of lead in
particulate matter set out in those sections may be exceeded in the
event of a malfunction or breakdown in the air pollution control
equipment or the process equipment associated with the operations
referred to in section 3 where the time during which the release
standards are exceeded does not exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a cupola or blast furnace, a total of
  two hours per month;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a reverberatory furnace, a total of one
  hour per month; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a holding furnace, kettle furnace or
  lead oxide production unit,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 15 minutes for a single malfunction or breakdown,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a total of one hour per month.

## [Information and Samples]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932881 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may request
the owner or operator of a secondary lead smelter to submit to the
Minister information and samples respecting the operation of the smelter
or the malfunction or breakdown of its air pollution equipment or
process equipment.

## [Release Measurement Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932883 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to section 8, the Minister requests information respecting
  the operation of a secondary lead smelter, the owner or operator of
  the smelter shall submit to the Minister a duly completed Release
  Measurement Report in the form set out in Schedule I.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall
  contain information that is obtained from release measurements and
  subsequent analysis of samples taken from the appropriate sources at
  the secondary lead smelter during the sixty days preceding the date on
  which the report is required to be submitted to the Minister pursuant
  to subsection (3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  submitted to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the operations referred to in
    paragraph 3(a), at intervals of six months, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the operations referred to in
    paragraph 3(b), at intervals of twelve months,

  the intervals to commence three months after the request is made by
  the Minister.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), at the request of
  the Minister, release measurements shall be carried out in the
  presence of an enforcement officer.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The analysis referred to in subsection (2) shall be
  carried out in accordance with the appropriate methods described in
  sections 3 and 4 and all samples shall be taken

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during normal operating conditions of the secondary
    lead smelter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of furnace operation sampling, during
    representative periods of furnace operation including charging and
    tapping.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-364, s. 2(E)
- SOR/2000-102, s. 4
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The release measurements
referred to in section 9 shall consist of at least three valid test runs
and the release measurement result shall be the arithmetic average of
the results of these valid test runs.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 10, a [[valid test run]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a release
measurement

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the result of which does not vary by more than
  twenty-five per cent from the arithmetic average of the results of all
  release measurements made for the tested operation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is made using a sample taken over a minimum
  continuous operating period of 120 minutes.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where the detailed
calculations of the test data set out in the report referred to in
subsection 9(1) are not appended to the report, the owner or operator of
a secondary lead smelter shall, within thirty days after submitting the
report, submit those detailed calculations in writing to the Minister.

## [Malfunction or Breakdown Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932902 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
section 8, the Minister requests information respecting a malfunction or
breakdown in the air pollution control equipment or the process
equipment associated with the operations referred to in section 3, the
owner or operator of the secondary lead smelter shall submit to the
Minister a duly completed Malfunction or Breakdown Report in the form
set out in Schedule II on a monthly basis commencing two months after
the request is made by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-364, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Signing of Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932906 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Any report submitted
under section 9 or 13 by a corporation shall be signed by an officer
authorized by the corporation to sign the report on its behalf.

## [Submission of Samples and Related Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932908 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to section 8, the Minister requests samples of gas streams
  released into the ambient air from a secondary lead smelter and such
  related information as will enable the Minister to cause analysis to
  be made of the concentration of particulate matter in those gas
  streams, the owner or operator of the secondary lead smelter shall
  take the samples at six month intervals, in accordance with the method
  described in Standard Reference Methods for Source Testing:
  Measurement of Emissions of Particulate Matter and Lead from Secondary
  Lead Smelters, Department of the Environment Report EPS-1-AP-78-3
  dated June 1979, as amended from time to time and shall forthwith
  submit the samples and related information to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), at the request of
  the Minister, the samples shall be taken in the presence of an
  enforcement officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-102, s. 4
:::
