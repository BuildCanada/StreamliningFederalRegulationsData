## N.R.C. Sales and Loans Regulations {#n.r.c.-sales-and-loans-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-403
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1981-05-22

Regulations Respecting Sales and Loans by the National Research Council
of Canada

P.C. 1981-1335 1981-05-21
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister designated for the purpose of the [National Research
Council Act](/eng/acts/N-15) and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 52 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting sales and
loans by the National Research Council of
Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-403).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871151 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [N.R.C. Sales and Loans
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-403).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871153 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Research Council
    of Canada;

[[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means scientific equipment that is
    owned by the Council and that has been specially manufactured by or
    for the Council or that is otherwise not readily available to the
    borrower; and

[[non-surplus research material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-surplus research material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means material
    that is part of the inventory or stores of the Council.

## [Sales]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871158 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the conditions
set out in Schedule I, the Council may sell non-surplus research
material to persons that are engaged in doing research or development
work, at the invitation of the Council, on property that is owned by the
Council.

## [Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871160 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the conditions
set out in Schedule II, the Council may make gratuitous loans of
equipment to Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province, municipal
corporations, hospitals, universities or other educational institutions
or persons for the purpose of teaching, research or development.

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-871163 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Each sale shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be authorized by a Divisional Director of the
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be made at a price sufficient to recover the cost
    to the Crown of the non-surplus research material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be authorized only if the material is to be used in
    the research or development work of the person to whom the material
    is to be sold;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be limited to a maximum value of \$1,000; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be made by means of a bill of sale indicating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who authorized the sale,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the authorization,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the sale,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the description and quantity of the material
      sold,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the sale,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the sale price, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person to whom the material is
      sold.

## [SCHEDULE II]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-871179 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Each loan shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be authorized by a Divisional Director of the
    Council;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be directly related to teaching, research or
    development; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be made by means of a written loan agreement that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} indicates the period of the loan, which in no
      case shall be in excess of two years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} reserves to the Council the right to require the
      temporary return of the equipment at the request of the Council
      and to terminate the loan, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} specifies that the borrower shall

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} be responsible for all costs associated with
        the loan including, but not limited to, the transportation,
        handling, packing, installation, removal, operation,
        replacement, maintenance, repair and insurance,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where the loan is made to municipal
        corporations, hospitals, universities or other educational
        institutions or persons for the purpose of teaching, research or
        development, be responsible for and shall indemnify and save
        harmless Her Majesty from and against all claims or liens
        arising in the course of the loan or attributable to the loan or
        based on the equipment or the use thereof,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} sign a statement, at the time of the
        commencement of the loan, describing the condition of the
        equipment, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} on termination of the loan, return the
        equipment in the condition described, fair wear and tear
        excepted.

- **2** The maximum value of any equipment for each loan to any borrower
  shall not exceed one million dollars unless approval by Treasury Board
  has been obtained.
