## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793620 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3).

## [Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793623 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contravention of a provision of the Act or any of its regulations that
  is set out in column 1 of the schedule is designated as a violation
  that may be proceeded with in accordance with sections 65.01 to 65.21
  of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Short-form descriptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of a discrepancy between the short-form
  descriptions in the schedule and the provision to which it pertains,
  the provision prevails.

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The classification of a
violation of a provision that is set out in column 1 of the schedule as
a Category A, Category B or Category C violation is as set out in column
3.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793632 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Individual

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount payable as the penalty in respect of a violation that is
  committed by an individual is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$300 to \$3,000, for a Category A violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$300 to \$10,000, for a Category B violation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$300 to \$25,000, for a Category C violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person other than an individual

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount payable as the penalty in respect of a
  violation that is committed by a person other than an individual is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000 to \$12,000, for a Category A violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000 to \$40,000, for a Category B violation;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000 to \$100,000, for a Category C violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of amount

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The amount of a penalty is
determined by the Commission having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the compliance history of the person who committed
  the violation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the degree of intention or negligence on the part of
  the person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the harm that resulted or could have resulted from
  the violation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the person derived any competitive or
  economic benefit from the violation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the person made reasonable efforts to
  mitigate or reverse the violation's effects;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the person provided all reasonable assistance
  to the Commission; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} whether the person brought the violation to the
  attention of the Commission.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-793653 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of service

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of violation referred to in section 65.05 of the Act and a
  determination referred to in section 65.13 of the Act shall be served
  by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with the individual,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with someone who appears to
      be an adult member of the same household at the individual's last
      known address or usual place of residence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail,
      courier, fax or other electronic means to the individual's last
      known address or usual place of residence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail, courier
      or fax to the person's head office or place of business or to the
      person's agent;

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it at the person's head office
      or place of business with an individual who appears to manage or
      be in control of the head office or place of business or with the
      person's agent; or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by electronic means, other
      than by fax, to any person referred to in subparagraph (ii).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation or determination --- other
  than when personally served --- is considered to be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy that is left with an adult
    referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(ii), on the day on which it is
    left with the adult;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy that is sent by registered
    mail or courier, on the tenth day after the date indicated in the
    receipt issued by the postal or courier service; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy sent by fax or other
    electronic means, on the day on which it is transmitted.
