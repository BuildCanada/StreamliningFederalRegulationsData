## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451050 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** **(1)** The following
definitions apply in this Order.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Bank for International Settlements on
    the Establishment of an Office of the Bank in Canada for the
    Operation of the BIS Innovation Hub Toronto Centre, done at Ottawa
    on January 31, 2024. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning asssigned by the
    definition [[BISIH Toronto Centre]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    sub-paragraph (b) of Article 1 of the
    Agreement. ([Centre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    assigned by the definition [[Official
    Activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in sub-paragraph (j) of Article 1
    of the Agreement. ([activités officielles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning assigned
    by the definition [[BIS]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in sub-paragraph (a)
    of Article 1 of the Agreement. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451059 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacities, privileges and
immunities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Organization has in
Canada, while carrying out its official activities, the legal capacities
of a body corporate and, to the extent specified in Articles 2 and 4 to
10 of the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Articles
II and III of the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Members and representatives

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Members
  of the Organization's Board of Directors and representatives of the
  central banks and monetary authorities that are members of the
  Organization referred to in Article 11 of the Agreement have in
  Canada, while carrying out official activities and to the extent
  specified in that Article, the privileges and immunities set out in
  Article IV of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Manager and Deputy General
  Manager

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The General Manager of the Organization and the
  Deputy General Manager of the Organization have in Canada, while
  carrying out official activities, to the extent specified in
  sub-paragraphs (1)(a), (c) and (d) and paragraph 2 of Article 11 of
  the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Article V of
  the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Centre officials

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Officials of the Centre
referred to in paragraph (e) of Article 1 of the Agreement have in
Canada, while carrying out official activities and to the extent
specified in sub-paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) and (e) to (h) and paragraph 2
of Article 14 of the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in
Article V of the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officials of the Organization

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Officials of the
Organization referred to in sub-paragraph (d) of Article 1 of the
Agreement have in Canada, while carrying out official activities and to
the extent set out in subparagraphs (1)(a), (b) and (d) and paragraph 2
of Article 13 of the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in
Article V of the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Experts referred to in
sub-paragraph (f) of Article 1 of the Agreement that perform missions
for the Organization have in Canada, to the extent specified in Article
15 of the Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Article VI
of the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Not for personal benefit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The privileges and
immunities described in sections 2 to 6 are not granted for the personal
benefit of the member, representative, manager, official or expert, as
the case may be, but rather are granted so that they may independently
carry out official activities in connection with the Organization and in
the interests of the Organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Traffic accident or offence

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in this Order exempts any person who has been conferred immunity
by this Order from liability arising from a traffic accident or a
traffic offence imposed by any law in Canada to the extent specified in
Article 17 of the Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taxes or duties

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not exempt
a Canadian citizen or permanent resident from liability for any taxes or
duties imposed by any law in Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1451079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
