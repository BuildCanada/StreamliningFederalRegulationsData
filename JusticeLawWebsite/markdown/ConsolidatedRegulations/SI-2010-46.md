## Student Employment Programs Participants Exclusion Approval Order {#student-employment-programs-participants-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2010-46
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2010-07-07

Student Employment Programs Participants Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2010-775 2010-06-17
::::

:::::::::: section
::::::::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EBBFA}
to participants in the Federal Student Work Experience Program, the
Research Affiliate Program, the Post-Secondary Co-op/Internship Program
or any other student employment program established by the Treasury
Board after consultation with the Public Service Commission, for the
purposes of hiring and during the course of their employment under the
program;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EBBFA){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of that Act, the Public
Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EBCDA},
hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} re-applies the provisions of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EDBBDA}
  to the persons and positions that were excluded by Order in Council
  P.C. 1997-488 of April 8, 1997[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_608227alt_hq_8993){#fn_608227alt_hq_8993-ID0EBBBDA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_608227alt_hq_8993-ID0EBBBDA){#fn_608227alt_hq_8993}SOR/97-194
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes the application of that Act, other than
  section 2, subsections 15(1) and (2), sections 16 to 21, 24, 25, 29,
  34, 54, 55, 66 to 72, 111 to 122, 134 and 135 of that Act to
  participants in the Federal Student Work Experience Program, the
  Research Affiliate Program, the Post-Secondary Co-op/Internship
  Program or any other student employment program established by the
  Treasury Board after consultation with the Public Service Commission,
  for the purposes of hiring and during the course of their employment
  under the program.

Ottawa, May 12, 2010

::: FlushRight
MARIA BARRADOS

*President of the Public Service Commission*

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EBCAA},
hereby approves

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application by the Public Service Commission
  of the provisions of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612020-e_hq_7337){#fn_612020-e_hq_7337-ID0EDABAA}
  to the persons and positions that were excluded by Order in Council
  P.C. 1997-488 of April 8, 1997[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_608227alt_hq_8993){#fn_608227alt_hq_8993-ID0EBABAA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion by the Public Service Commission from
  the application of that Act, other than section 2, subsections 15(1)
  and (2), sections 16 to 21, 24, 25, 29, 34, 54, 55, 66 to 72, 111 to
  122, 134 and 135 of that Act to participants in the Federal Student
  Work Experience Program, the Research Affiliate Program, the
  Post-Secondary Co-op/Internship Program or any other student
  employment program established by the Treasury Board after
  consultation with the Public Service Commission, for the purposes of
  hiring and during the course of their employment under the program.
