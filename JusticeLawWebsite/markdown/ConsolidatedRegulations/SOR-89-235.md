## Airborne Remote Sensing Services Fees Order, 1989 {#airborne-remote-sensing-services-fees-order-1989 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-235
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1989-05-03

Order Prescribing the Fees and Charges to be Paid for the Provision of
Airborne Remote Sensing Services
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to Order in
Council P.C. 1982-1125 of April 8, 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn1star_e){#fn1star_e-ID0EJCCA} made pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
hereby revokes the Airborne Remote Sensing Services Fees Order, 1986
made on September 2, 1986[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn2star_e){#fn2star_e-ID0EDCCA} and makes the
annexed [Order prescribing the fees and charges to be paid for the
provision of airborne remote sensing
services](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-235) in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919017 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Airborne Remote Sensing Services Fees Order,
1989](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-235).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919019 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[CCT]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CCT]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means computer-compatible tape;
    ([BM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[HDDT]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HDDT]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means high density digital tape;
    ([BNHD]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAPL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NAPL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Air Photo Library;
    ([PNA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[SAR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[SAR]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Synthetic Aperture Radar.
    ([ROS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919025 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister of
Energy, Mines and Resources provides a person with an airborne remote
sensing service described in Column I of an item of the schedule, that
person shall pay to the Minister the fees and charges set out in Column
II of that item.
