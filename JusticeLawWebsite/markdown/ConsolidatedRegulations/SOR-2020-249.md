[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information --- paragraph
16.9(2)(d) of Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 16.9(2)(d) of the Act, the following information is
prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who intends to undertake a
  relocation and the name of any child of the marriage who is
  relocating, if applicable;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of any other child of the marriage in
  respect of whom the person has parenting time or decision-making
  responsibility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person's current place of
  residence and their current contact information; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any person who has parenting time,
  decision-making responsibility or contact under a contact order in
  respect of any child of the marriage referred to in paragraph (a) or
  (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of relocation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
16.9 of the Act, a person who intends to undertake a relocation must
give notice of their intention by providing the information set out in
Form 1 of the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information --- paragraph
16.91(2)(d) of Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 16.91(2)(d) of the Act, the following information is
prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who has received the notice
  under section 16.9 of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person's current place of
  residence and their current contact information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection to relocation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of clause
16.91(1)(b)(i)(A) of the Act, a person who intends to object to a
relocation must do so by providing the information set out in Form 2 of
the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information --- subsection
16.96(2) of Act

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 16.96(2) of the Act, the following information is prescribed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who has contact with a child
  of the marriage under a contact order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address of the person's current place of
  residence and their current contact information;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of any child of the marriage specified in
  the contact order; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any person who has parenting time or
  decision-making responsibility in respect of any child of the marriage
  specified in the contact order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- persons with contact

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 16.96(2) of the Act, a person who has contact with a child of
the marriage under a contact order and who intends to change their place
of residence must give notice of their intention by providing the
information set out in Form 3 of the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into Force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2020-249_e_transform_hq_28276){#fn_SOR-2020-249_e_transform_hq_28276-ID0EBCA}[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 12 of An Act to amend the Divorce Act, the Family Orders and
Agreements Enforcement Assistance Act and the Garnishment, Attachment
and Pension Diversion Act and to make consequential amendments to
another Act, chapter 16 of the Statutes of Canada, 2019, comes into
force, but if they are registered after that day, they come into force
on the day on which they are registered.
