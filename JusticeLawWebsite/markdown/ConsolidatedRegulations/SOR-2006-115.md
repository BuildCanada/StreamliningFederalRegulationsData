## Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of the Status of Wildlife Species {#order-extending-the-time-for-the-assessment-of-the-status-of-wildlife-species .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2006-115
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2006-06-01

Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of the Status of Wildlife
Species

P.C. 2006-436 2006-06-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 130(5) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA}, the Minister
of the Environment has consulted with the competent ministers and has
included a statement in the public registry setting out the reasons for
the extension;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  29
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 130(5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)^a^,
hereby makes the annexed [Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of
the Status of Wildlife Species](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-215).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The time provided for the
assessment of the status of the following wildlife species is extended
for one year from June 5, 2006:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "Snake, Lake Erie Water ([Nerodia sipedon
  insularum]{.otherLang lang="la"}) [Couleuvre d'eau du lac
  Érié]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" set out under the heading "REPTILES" in
  Part 1 of Schedule 2 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Cisco, Blackfin ([Coregonus nigripinnis]{.otherLang
  lang="la"}) [Cisco à nageoires noires]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" set out
  under the heading "FISH" in Part 2 of Schedule 2 to that Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Sculpin, Deepwater ([Myoxocephalus
  thompsoni]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Great Lakes population [Chabot de
  profondeur des Grands Lacs]{.otherLang lang="fr"} population des
  Grands Lacs" set out under the heading "FISH" in Part 2 of Schedule 2
  to that Act.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
