## Transfer of Royal Military College Academic Hoods Order {#transfer-of-royal-military-college-academic-hoods-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 736
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Transfer Academic
Hoods to Recipients of Honorary Degrees
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561275 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Transfer of Royal Military College Academic Hoods
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._736).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561277 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence is authorized to transfer academic hoods from time to time to
recipients of honorary degrees conferred by the Royal Military College
of Canada, Kingston, Ontario.
