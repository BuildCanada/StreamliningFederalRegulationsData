## Disclosure of Charges (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#disclosure-of-charges-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-328
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Respecting the Disclosure by Companies of Charges Applicable
to Deposit Accounts and Services

P.C. 1992-1104  1992-05-21
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 429, 431 to 433 and 531 of
the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the disclosure by companies of
charges applicable to deposit accounts and
services](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-328), effective June 1, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944488 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Disclosure of Charges (Trust and Loan Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-328).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944490 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[controlled access automated teller machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled access automated teller machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means an automated teller machine located in a branch or a point of
    service of a company, or in an enclosed area adjacent to a branch or
    a point of service of a company, access to which is controlled by a
    system that permits entry to automated teller machine users.
    ([guichet automatique à accès contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
    to which the public has access and at which a company carries on
    business with the public and opens or initiates the opening of
    retail deposit accounts through natural persons. ([point de
    service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-52, s. 1

## [Personal Deposit Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944496 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A company shall disclose to
its customers and to the public all charges applicable to personal
deposit accounts with the company by means of a written statement,
copies of which shall be displayed and made available to customers and
to the public at each of company's branches where products and services
are offered, at each of the company's points of service and on the
company's websites through which products or services are offered in
Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-687, s. 5(F)
- SOR/2009-52, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a company increases a
charge applicable to personal deposit accounts with the company or
introduces a new charge applicable to personal deposit accounts with the
company, the company shall disclose the increase or new charge, as the
case may be, to each customer in whose name such an account is kept

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a customer to whom a statement of
  account is provided,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by means of a notice in writing provided to the
    customer at least 30 days before the effective date of the increase
    or new charge, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the customer has instructed the company in
    writing to provide such a notice to another person, by means of a
    notice provided to that other person at least 30 days before the
    effective date of the increase or new charge; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a customer to whom a statement of
  account is not provided

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by means of a notice that is displayed, for a
    period of at least 60 days immediately before the effective date of
    the increase or new charge, at each branch of the company where
    products or services are offered, at each of the company's points of
    service, at each controlled access automated teller machines on
    which the name of the company or information identifying the machine
    with the company is displayed and on the company's websites through
    which products or services are offered in Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by means of a notice, electronic or otherwise,
    advising customers of the increase or new charge and the manner in
    which further details can be obtained, which notice shall be
    provided, for a period of at least 60 days immediately before the
    effective date of the increase or new charge, at all automated
    teller machines, other than controlled access automated teller
    machines, on which the name of the company or information
    identifying the machine with the company is displayed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-52, s. 3
:::

## [Deposit Accounts other than Personal Deposit Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944509 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall disclose to its customers and to the public all charges
  applicable to any of the following services provided by the company in
  respect of deposit accounts with the company, other than personal
  deposit accounts, by means of a written statement, copies of which
  shall be displayed and made available to customers and to the public
  at each branch of the company at which deposit accounts are kept, at
  each of the company's points of service and on the company's websites
  through which products or services are offered in Canada:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acceptance of deposits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acceptance of coins, cheques or Bank of Canada
    notes for deposit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} issuance of cheques;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} certification of cheques;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} handling of a cheque presented by a customer that
    is subsequently returned because there are not sufficient funds;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} holding of cheques for deposit;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} handling of cheques drawn in the currency of the
    United States;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} processing of a stop payment on a cheque;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} handling of a cheque issued by a customer that is
    subsequently returned because there are not sufficient funds;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} supply of coins and Bank of Canada notes;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} handling of overdrafts;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} transfers between accounts;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} supply of account statements;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} handling of account confirmations;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} conducting searches related to an account;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} provision of information in regard to an account
    balance;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} closing of an account; and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} handling of unclaimed balances in inactive
    accounts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written statement referred to in subsection (1)
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the statement sets out all charges
    for services provided by the company in respect of deposit accounts
    with the company other than personal deposit accounts; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the statement does not set out all of
    the charges referred to in paragraph (a) and set out the manner in
    which information regarding charges not included in the statement
    can be obtained.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-687, s. 5(F)
- SOR/2009-52, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a company increases a
charge applicable to a service referred to in subsection 5(1), the
company shall disclose the increase

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to customers to whom a statement of
  account is provided,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by means of a notice in writing provided to the
    customer at least 30 days before the effective date of the increase,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the customer has instructed the company in
    writing to provide such a notice to another person, by means of a
    notice in writing provided to that other person at least 30 days
    before the effective date of the increase; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to customers to whom a statement of
  account is not provided, by means of a notice that is displayed for a
  period of at least 60 days immediately before the effective date of
  the increase at each branch of the company where products or services
  are offered, at each of the company's points of service and on the
  company's websites through which products or services are offered in
  Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-52, s. 5
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Section 6 does not apply in
respect of a customer who has agreed in writing that the company will
charge an amount other than an amount required to be disclosed pursuant
to subsection 5(1).

## [List of Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944543 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall maintain, at each of its branches where products or
  services are offered, at each of its points of service and on its
  websites through which products or services are offered in Canada, a
  list of the charges applicable to deposit accounts with the company
  and the usual amount charged by the company for services normally
  provided by the company to its customers and to the public.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall, on request, make the list available
  to its customers and to the public for inspection during business
  hours at each of its branches and points of service referred to in
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-52, s. 6
