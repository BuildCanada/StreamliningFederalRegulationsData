## Privileges and Immunities Accession Order (United Nations) {#privileges-and-immunities-accession-order-united-nations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1317
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting the Accession to Convention on the Privileges and
Immunities of the United Nations
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503349 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Privileges and Immunities Accession Order (United
Nations)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1317).

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503351 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The United Nations shall
have the legal capacities of a body corporate.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The United Nations shall in
Canada have the immunities and privileges set out in sections 2, 3, 4,
5, 7 and 9 of the Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the
United Nations, hereinafter referred to as the \"Convention\".

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of Members to the
  principal and subsidiary organs of the United Nations and to
  conferences convened by the United Nations shall, while exercising
  their functions and during their journey to and from the place of
  meeting, have in Canada the privileges and immunities set out in
  section 11 of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of Members to the principal and
  subsidiary organs of the United Nations and to conferences convened by
  the United Nations shall continue to have the immunities set forth in
  paragraph 11(a) of the Convention in respect of words spoken or
  written and all acts done by them in discharging their duties
  notwithstanding that they are no longer the representatives of
  Members.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the incidence of any form of taxation depends
  upon residence, periods during which the representatives of Members to
  the principal and subsidiary organs of the United Nations and to
  conferences convened by the United Nations are present in Canada for
  the discharge of their duties shall not be considered as periods of
  residence.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1), (2) and (3) do not apply to a
  representative of Canada or to a Canadian citizen.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the expression
  \"representatives\" includes delegates, deputy delegates, advisers,
  technical experts and secretaries of delegations.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the United Nations
  whose names are included in the categories specified by the
  Secretary-General pursuant to section 17 of the Convention shall in
  Canada have the immunities and privileges set forth in section 18 of
  the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary-General and all Assistant
  Secretaries-General, their spouses and minor children shall in Canada
  have the privileges and immunities, exemptions and facilities accorded
  to diplomatic envoys, in accordance with international law.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Experts, other than
officials of the United Nations within the meaning of section 4 of this
Order, performing missions for the United Nations shall in Canada have
the privileges and immunities set forth in paragraphs 22(a), (b), (c),
(d), (e) and (f) of the Convention except in so far as any such
privilege or immunity is waived by the Secretary-General pursuant to
section 23 of the Convention.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order shall
be construed as exempting a Canadian citizen residing or ordinarily
resident in Canada from taxation imposed by any law in Canada on
salaries and emoluments.
