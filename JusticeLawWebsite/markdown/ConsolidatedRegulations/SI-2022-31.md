## Order Transferring from the Minister of Health to the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act and the Tobacco and Vaping Products Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-health-to-the-minister-of-mental-health-and-addictions-and-associate-minister-of-health-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-controlled-drugs-and-substances-act-and-the-tobacco-and-vaping-products-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-31
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2022-06-08

Order Transferring from the Minister of Health to the Minister of Mental
Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health the Powers,
Duties and Functions Under the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act and
the Tobacco and Vaping Products Act

P.C. 2022-549 2022-05-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EKEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EHEAA},
transfers from the Minister of Health to the Minister of Mental Health
and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Health under the [Controlled Drugs and
Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2373_hq_31003){#fn_81000-3-2373_hq_31003-ID0EEEAA}
and the [Tobacco and Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2373_hq_31004){#fn_81000-3-2373_hq_31004-ID0EBEAA}.
