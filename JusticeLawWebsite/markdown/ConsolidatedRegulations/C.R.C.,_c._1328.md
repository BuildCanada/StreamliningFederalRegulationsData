## Rideau Canal Lease Regulations {#rideau-canal-lease-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1328
:::

[FEDERAL REAL PROPERTY AND FEDERAL IMMOVABLES ACT](/eng/acts/F-8.4)

Regulation Authorizing a Lease for a Term of 42 Years of Lands on the
Rideau Canal
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503509 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Regulation may be
cited as the [Rideau Canal Lease
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1328).

## [Lease]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503511 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development may, notwithstanding subsection 4(2) of
the I.A. and N.D. Canal Land Regulations, lease for a term of 42 years
to Galeries Canada Holdings Limited and Olympia & York Developments
Limited, bodies corporate, duly incorporated under the laws of the
Province of Ontario, the lands on the Rideau Canal, in the Regional
Municipality of Ottawa-Carleton, in the Province of Ontario, containing
approximately 61,600 square feet and shown outlined in red on Plan No.
42482 dated November 8, 1974 and on file in the records of the
Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development.
