## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Office of Infrastructure of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Cities Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-office-of-infrastructure-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-cities-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-103
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Office of
Infrastructure of Canada the Control and Supervision of the Cities
Secretariat

P.C. 2004-870 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Office of Infrastructure
of Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the public
service in the Privy Council Office known as the Cities Secretariat,
effective July 20, 2004.
