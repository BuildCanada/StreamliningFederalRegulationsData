## Age Guidelines {#age-guidelines .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/78-165
:::

[CANADIAN HUMAN RIGHTS ACT](/eng/acts/H-6)

Registration 1978-10-25

Guidelines of the Canadian Human Rights Commission Respecting Reasonable
Practices Relating to Age Justifying Different Fares, Rates or Charges
in the Provision of Goods, Services and Facilities Available to the
General Public
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Canadian Human Rights Commission, pursuant to paragraph 14(e) and
subsection 22(2) of the [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6), is
hereby pleased to issue guidelines as set out in the schedule hereto
respecting reasonable practices relating to age justifying different
fares, rates or charges in the provision of goods, services and
facilities available to the general public.

Dated at Ottawa, October 10, 1978
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634142 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Guidelines may be
cited as the [Age Guidelines](/eng/regulations/SI-78-165).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634144 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Guidelines,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Human Rights
Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634146 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where adverse
differentiation in relation to any individual in the provision of goods,
services, facilities or accommodation customarily available to the
general public is based only on a reduction or absence of rates, fares
or charges with respect to children, youths or senior citizens, such
adverse differentiation is reasonable and is not, in the opinion of the
Commission, a discriminatory practice within the meaning of section 5 of
the Act.
