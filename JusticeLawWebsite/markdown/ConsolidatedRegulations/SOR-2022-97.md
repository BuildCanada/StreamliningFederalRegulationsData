## Critical Habitat of the Cerulean Warbler (Setophaga cerulea) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-cerulean-warbler-setophaga-cerulea-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-97
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-05-06

Critical Habitat of the Cerulean Warbler (Setophaga cerulea) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Cerulean Warbler ([Setophaga cerulea]{.otherLang lang="la"})
is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2
of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056){#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056-ID0EBBFA){#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a
place referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26075){#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26075-ID0EBBDA}
of that Act and, under subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must
be excluded from the annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26075-ID0EBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26075}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Cerulean Warbler ([Setophaga cerulea]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-97) under subsections 58(4)
and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056){#fn_81000-2-4043-E_hq_26056-ID0EBACA}.

Gatineau, May 5, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Cerulean Warbler ([Setophaga cerulea]{.otherLang lang="la"}) ---
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry --- that is located on
federal lands within Gatineau Park.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
