## Regulations Designating Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of Enforcement (Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999) {#regulations-designating-regulatory-provisions-for-purposes-of-enforcement-canadian-environmental-protection-act-1999 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-134
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2012-06-20

Regulations Designating Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of
Enforcement (Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999)

P.C. 2012-840 2012-06-19
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EIAEA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EFAEA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on July 2, 2011, a copy of
the proposed [Regulations Designating Regulatory Provisions for Purposes
of Enforcement (Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-134), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the proposed Regulations or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section
286.1[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EGADA} of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDADA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Regulations Designating Regulatory Provisions for Purposes of
Enforcement (Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-134).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EIAEA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2004, c. 15, s. 31
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EFAEA){#fn_b}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c-ID0EGADA){#fn_c}S.C.
  2009, c. 14, s. 80
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The provisions set out in
the schedule are designated for the purposes of paragraph 272(1)(h) of
the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind1D5B_hq_12752){#fn_Ind1D5B_hq_12752-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 80 of the Environmental Enforcement Act, chapter 14 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2009, comes into force, but if they are registered
after that day, they come into force on the day on which they are
registered.
