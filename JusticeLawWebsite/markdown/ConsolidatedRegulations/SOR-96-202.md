## Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1996, No. 2 {#government-employees-land-acquisition-order-1996-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-202
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1996-04-16

Order Authorizing a Certain Employee of the Government of Canada to
Acquire an Interest in Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1996-497  1996-04-16
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 29(1)(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order authorizing a certain employee
of the Government of Canada to acquire an interest in territorial lands
in the Northwest Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-478).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980413 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Government Employees Land Acquisition Order, 1996, No.
2](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-202).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-980415 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The employee of the
Government of Canada named in the schedule is hereby authorized to
acquire an interest in territorial lands in the Northwest Territories as
described in the schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#980417 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-980418 .scheduleLabel}
:::

To Mavis Rachel Michaud, of the Village of Fort Simpson in the Northwest
Territories, Manager, an employee of the Department of Human Resources
Development, to lease, for a traditional use cabin, the whole of a
parcel of land situated near kilometre 510 of the Mackenzie Highway,
N.W.T. Highway No. 1, at approximately 61°59′27″ North Latitude and
121°54′61″ West Longitude, in Quad numbered 95 H/13 in the Northwest
Territories, as said parcel is shown outlined in red on a sketch plan on
file 33-1-12 in the Land Management Division of the Department of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development at Ottawa, saving and excepting
therefrom and reserving thereout all mines and minerals, including
hydrocarbons, whether solid, liquid or gaseous and the right to work
them.
