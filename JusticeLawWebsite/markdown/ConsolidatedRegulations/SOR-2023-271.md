## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Federal Prompt Payment for
    Construction Work Act](/eng/acts/F-7.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a party to a
    dispute. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Proper Invoice and Notice of Non-Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430326 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proper invoice

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to meeting any
requirements under the Act or set in the contract, a proper invoice must
include the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the invoice and the name, street and
  mailing address, telephone number and email address of the contractor
  that performed the construction work;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the materials or services
  were supplied;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contract number or other authorization under
  which the materials or services were supplied;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description, including the quantity, if applicable,
  of the materials or services supplied;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount payable for the services or materials
  supplied and the payment terms; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, street and mailing address,
  telephone number and email address of the person to which payment must
  be made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of non-payment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A notice of non-payment
must indicate, in addition to the information set out in section 13 of
the Act, the amount to be paid that is not in dispute.

## [Adjudicators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430337 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator Authority --- powers, duties
and functions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Adjudicator Authority
has the following powers, duties and functions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop and provide initial training and continuing
  education for adjudicators;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue, renew, suspend or cancel certifications for
  adjudicators;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that adjudicators meet all eligibility and
  criteria set out in these Regulations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} maintain a publicly accessible list of adjudicators,
  including their qualifications;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain a fee schedule that reflects
  the experience of the adjudicator and the complexity of the dispute;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} regulate the conduct of adjudicators, including by
  establishing a code of conduct;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} address complaints against adjudicators respecting
  breaches of the code of conduct, including by establishing a
  complaints procedure;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} consider the circumstances in which an adjudicator is
  not required to determine a dispute under paragraph 5(d) of the
  [Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work Regulations (Criteria,
  Time Limits, Interest and
  Circumstances)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-269); and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if an adjudicator can no longer determine a dispute
  for one of the circumstances set out in section 5 of the [Federal
  Prompt Payment for Construction Work Regulations (Criteria, Time
  Limits, Interest and Circumstances)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-269),
  appoint their replacement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator --- eligibility and
qualifications

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An individual must meet the
following eligibility criteria in order to be designated as an
adjudicator:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they must be an accredited member in good standing
  with the Adjudicator Authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they must have at least 10 years of relevant working
  experience in the construction industry;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} they must not have been convicted of an indictable
  offence in Canada or of a comparable offence outside of Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} they must not be an undischarged bankrupt; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} they must have the appropriate level of security
  clearance to address the dispute.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjudicator --- powers, duties and
functions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  adjudicator has the following powers, duties and functions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine matters in the dispute in an impartial
    and independent manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not have any conflicts of interest in the dispute
    or with the parties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ascertain the relevant facts and law;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} issue directions respecting the scheduling of the
    conduct of the adjudication;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} issue directions respecting any restrictions on the
    length of written or oral submissions;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} request that any party provide any documents
    supporting or supplementing the notice of adjudication;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} meet and question any of the parties and their
    representatives;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} on consent of the parties and subject to any
    third-party consents, make any site visits or inspections;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on consent of the parties and subject to any
    third-party consents, carry out any tests or experiments;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} on consent of the parties, appoint any experts or
    assessors necessary to better determine any fact or issue in the
    dispute;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} draw inferences based on the conduct of the parties
    prior to and during the adjudication; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} issue any other directions so as to promote the
    expeditious resolution of the adjudication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party does not comply with any request or
  direction issued by the adjudicator, the adjudicator may continue the
  adjudication in the absence of that party and make a decision on the
  basis of any available information or evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single adjudicator

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A single adjudicator is to
be appointed to determine each dispute.

## [Adjudication Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1430375 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1430376 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of time

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The following periods are
excluded from the computation of time in these Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any holiday as defined in subsection 35(1) of the
  [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Saturdays;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nine-day period beginning on December 24 and
  ending on January 1; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any construction holiday that is recognized by any
  provincial government.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic provision of documents

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any document required by
these Regulations must be provided by electronic means.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One matter per adjudication

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 11,
each adjudication must address only one matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated adjudication

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  related disputes are the subject of separate adjudications, the
  parties may agree to have those adjudications consolidated and
  determined by a single adjudicator in which case they must inform the
  adjudicators concerned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation required by contractor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if the parties to each of the
  adjudications do not agree to consolidated adjudication, a contractor
  may require the consolidation of disputes by informing the parties and
  the adjudicators concerned.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of adjudicator

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When disputes are consolidated, the following rules
  apply to the appointment of an adjudicator:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an adjudicator appointed to adjudicate a dispute
    that is consolidated is considered to have resigned from the
    adjudication as of the date on which they are informed that it is
    consolidated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an adjudicator who is considered to have resigned
    under paragraph (a) may be appointed or another adjudicator may be
    appointed under section 13, 14 or 15 as the adjudicator for the
    consolidated adjudication.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A party may act in person
or be represented by legal counsel or by another representative.

### [Dispute Resolution]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1430398 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint appointment of adjudicator

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties seeking to jointly appoint an adjudicator to request that they
  determine a dispute must communicate, in writing, with that
  adjudicator within four days after the day on which the notice of
  adjudication referred to in subsection 16(2) of the Act is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent or refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator must provide their consent or refusal
  to the parties, in writing, within four days after the day on which
  the request for adjudication referred to in subsection (1) is
  received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New adjudicator

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  adjudicator refuses their joint appointment under subsection 13(2),
  the parties may agree to make a request, in writing, to another
  adjudicator that they determine the dispute.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent or refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The new adjudicator must provide their consent or
  refusal to the parties, in writing, within four days after the day on
  which the request for adjudication referred to in subsection (1) is
  received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment by Adjudicator Authority

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  parties cannot jointly appoint an adjudicator, either party may
  request, in writing, that the Adjudicator Authority appoint one.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of adjudicator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudicator Authority has five days after the day
  on which the appointment request is received to appoint an
  adjudicator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing parties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudicator Authority must, within two days after
  the day on which the adjudicator is appointed, inform the parties of
  the date of that adjudicator's consent to their appointment, as well
  as their name, street and mailing address, telephone number and email
  address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents sent to adjudicator

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Within the five days
after the day on which consent referred to in subsections 13(2), 14(2)
or 15(3) is received, the party that provided the notice of adjudication
must send the following documents to the adjudicator and to the other
parties:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice of adjudication;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written statement of the facts on which it intends
  to rely; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} copies of all documents on which it intends to rely,
  including if, applicable, all relevant extracts from the construction
  contract.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Steps to follow

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of the
documents referred to in section 16, the adjudicator must inform each
party, in writing, of the steps to be followed in the adjudication
process.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The party to which the
notice of adjudication was provided and that intends to respond has 20
days after the day on which the documents referred to in section 16 are
received to send to the adjudicator and every other party

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written statement of the facts on which it intends
  to rely; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} copies of any material it wishes the adjudicator to
  consider.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of new adjudicator

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** If the adjudicator can no
longer determine the dispute for any of the circumstances set out in
section 5 of the [Federal Prompt Payment for Construction Work
Regulations (Criteria, Time Limits, Interest and
Circumstances)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-269), a new adjudicator must
be appointed in accordance with the procedure established under section
13, 14 or 15 of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Termination of dispute

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The parties may, upon
agreement, at any time following the notice of adjudication but before
the adjudicator makes a determination, terminate the dispute.

### [Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1430431 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information considered

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator must
consider any relevant information submitted by the parties and must make
available to them any other information that the adjudicator considers
in making a determination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for determination

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  adjudicator must make a determination no later than 20 days after the
  day on which the response referred to in section 18 is received or, if
  there is no response, no later than 20 days after the day on which it
  was to be provided under that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The time limit referred to in subsection (1) may be
  extended for up to five days by the adjudicator, or for a longer
  period with the consent of all parties and of the adjudicator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format and content

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator must
render a written determination with reasons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of determination

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator must,
without delay after a determination is made, provide a copy of it to all
the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified copy

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The adjudicator must
also, no later than five days after the day on which a determination is
made, provide a certified copy of it to all parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minor corrections

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  five days after the day on which the determination is provided, an
  adjudicator may, on their own or on the written request of a party,
  make any changes to the determination as may be necessary to correct a
  typographical or similar error.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of corrected determination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An adjudicator who makes a change to a determination
  under subsection (1) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the parties with a copy of the corrected
    determination on the day the change is made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the parties with a certified copy of the
    corrected determination no later than five days after the making of
    the change.
