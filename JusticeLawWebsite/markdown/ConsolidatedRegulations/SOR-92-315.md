## Subsidiaries Holding Company Shares (Insurance Companies) Regulations {#subsidiaries-holding-company-shares-insurance-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-315
:::

[INSURANCE COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/I-11.8)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Permitting Companies to Permit their Subsidiaries to Hold
Shares of the Company

P.C. 1992-1090  1992-05-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 703(h) of the [Insurance
Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations permitting companies to permit their
subsidiaries to hold shares of the
company](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-315), effective June 1, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944188 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Subsidiaries Holding Company Shares (Insurance Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-315).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944190 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means distribution as defined
    in section 296 of the Act; ([mise en circulation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    financial institution whose primary business is dealing in
    securities and that is regulated in its securities dealings by an
    Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province or by the laws
    of a foreign country. ([entité de valeurs mobilières
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Holding of Company's Shares by Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944195 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 74(c) of the Act, subject to section 4, a company may permit a
subsidiary of the company that is a regulated securities entity to hold
shares of the company if the aggregate value of shares of the company
held by all such subsidiaries, other than shares referred to in section
76 of the Act, does not exceed one per cent of the regulatory capital of
the company.

## [Exception for Underwriter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944197 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a subsidiary of a
company is a regulated securities entity and is acting as a securities
underwriter in connection with a distribution of shares of the company,
the company may permit the subsidiary to hold shares of the company in
excess of the limit set out in section 3 until the distribution is
ended.
