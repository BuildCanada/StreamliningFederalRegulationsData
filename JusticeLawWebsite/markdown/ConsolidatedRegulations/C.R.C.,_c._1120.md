**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing with a hook and line
    held in the hand or with a hook, line and rod held in the hand but
    does not include fishing with a set line; ([pêche à la
    ligne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[artificial fly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[artificial fly]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single or double hook
    on a common shank, dressed with silk, tinsel, wool, fur, feathers or
    other material, or any combination thereof, without a spinning
    device whether attached to the hook or line; ([mouche
    artificielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chemical attractant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chemical attractant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical or
    scented substance that is applied inside or onto a lure in order to
    attract fish; ([attractif chimique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    1\]]{.Repealed}

    the internal organs, the head, the fins and the bones, except
    intramuscular or lateral bones, have been
    removed; ([filet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a soft-shell clam ([Mya
    arenaria]{.otherLang lang="la"}); ([poisson]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fish finder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fish finder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a sonar device for
    detecting fish; ([détecteur de poissons]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foul hooking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foul hooking]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing with a hook or
    hooks attached to a line manipulated in such a manner as to pierce a
    fish in any part of its body other than its mouth; ([hameçon
    illicite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gaff-hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gaff-hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hook attached directly

    piercing a fish in any part of its body; ([gaffeau]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-494474 .Part}

**[[2.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.01 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations
apply to parks and, subject to sections 40 and 41 of the Act, to park
reserves as if they were parks.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-54, s. 5
:::

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in
  respect of fishing for commercial purposes for eel, smelt or gaspereau
  in Kouchibouguac National Park of Canada by persons who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in 1967, 1968 or 1969, possessed a commercial
    fishing licence under the authority of the New Brunswick Fishery
    Regulations, C.R.C., c. 844, to fish for eel, smelt or gaspereau
    within the waters of that park; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in 1979, were registered as commercial fishers
    under the authority of the New Brunswick Fishery Regulations,
    C.R.C., c. 844, and fished from the Cap St.-Louis or Loggiecroft
    wharf areas within that park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Fishing for commercial purposes for eel, smelt or
  gaspereau within Kouchibouguac National Park of Canada shall be
  subject to the requirements of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and
  the regulations made under that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-33, s. 1
- SOR/96-245, s. 2
- SOR/2003-54, s. 68
:::

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** With the exception of
paragraphs 15.1(a) and (b), these Regulations do not apply in respect of
fishing in the marine portion of Forillon National Park of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-4, s. 2
- SOR/98-268, s. 2
- SOR/2003-54, s. 69
:::

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** For those parks within
the Inuvialuit Settlement Region, these Regulations do not apply to the
beneficiaries of the Inuvialuit Final Agreement as approved, given
effect and declared valid by the [Western Arctic (Inuvialuit) Claims
Settlement Act](/eng/acts/W-6.7).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-245, s. 3
- SOR/98-268, s. 2
:::

**[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** For those parks within
the Nunavut Settlement Area, these Regulations do not apply to the
beneficiaries of the Nunavut Land Claims Agreement as approved, given
effect and declared valid by the [Nunavut Land Claims Agreement
Act](/eng/acts/N-28.7).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-268, s. 2
:::

**[[2.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.5 .sectionLabel}** For those parks within
the Yukon Territory, south of the Inuvialuit Settlement Region, these
Regulations do not apply to the beneficiaries, within their respective
traditional territories, of the Champagne and Aishihik First Nations
Final Agreement and of the Vuntut Gwitchin First Nation Final Agreement
as approved, given effect and declared valid by the [Yukon First Nations
Land Claims Settlement Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-268, s. 2
- SOR/99-352, s. 2
:::

**[[2.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.6 .sectionLabel}** For the Gwaii Haanas
National Park Reserve of Canada, these Regulations do not apply to
people of the Haida Nation to whom the Gwaii Haanas Agreement,
authorized by Order in Council P.C. 1992-1591 dated July 16, 1992,
applies.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-54, s. 6
:::

## [Fishing Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-494500 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall fish

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in any park waters set out in column I of Schedule
    II unless that person is the holder of a fishing permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for salmon in any waters in Terra Nova National
    Park of Canada unless that person is the holder of a salmon licence
    and is in possession of at least one unused salmon tag;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for lake trout in any waters of Prince Albert
    National Park of Canada unless that person is the holder of a lake
    trout endorsement and a fishing permit issued pursuant to paragraph
    4(a) and is in possession of at least one unused lake trout tag;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for soft-shell clams in any waters in Kouchibouguac
    National Park of Canada unless that person is the holder of a
    soft-shell clam permit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for salmon in any waters in Cape Breton Highlands
    National Park of Canada unless that person is the holder of a salmon
    licence; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for salmon in any waters in Gros Morne National
    Park of Canada unless that person is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a salmon licence and in possession
      of at least one unused salmon tag, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a salmon licence issued under the
      Wild Life Regulations, Consolidated Newfoundland and Labrador
      Regulation 1156/96, made under the Wild Life Act, chapter W-8 of
      the Revised Statutes of Newfoundland, 1990, and in possession of
      at least one unused salmon tag issued under those Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to a person under 16
  years of age who is accompanied by a person 16 years of age or older
  who is the holder of a fishing permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply to a person under 16
  years of age who is accompanied by a person 16 years of age or older
  who is the holder of a salmon licence and is in possession of at least
  one unused salmon tag.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(c) does not apply to a person under 16
  years of age who is accompanied by a person 16 years of age or older
  who is the holder of a lake trout endorsement and is in possession of
  at least one unused lake trout tag.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(d) does not apply to a person under 16
  years of age who is accompanied by a person 16 years of age or older
  who is the holder of a soft-shell clam permit.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(e) does not apply to a person under 16
  years of age who is accompanied by a person 16 years of age or older
  who is the holder of a salmon licence.
