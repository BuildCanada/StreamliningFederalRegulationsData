## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-229
:::

[CANADIAN SPACE AGENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-23.2)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as
Minister for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 1993-1981  1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canadian Space
Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-23.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6542){#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6542-ID0EBDAA},
is pleased hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6542-ID0EBDAA){#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6542}S.C.
  1990, c. 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1672 of
  August 18, 1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6543){#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6543-ID0EBBBAA};

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6543-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_SI-93-229_e_hq_6543}SI/93-172,
    1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3711
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and
  Technology, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of that Act.
:::
