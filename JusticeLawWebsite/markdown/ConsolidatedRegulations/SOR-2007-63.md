## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741048 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document required or authorized to be served on a public office holder
  under sections 53 to 57 of the [Conflict of Interest
  Act](/eng/acts/C-36.65) may be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personally, by delivering a copy of it

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the public office holder at any place, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the public office holder cannot conveniently
      be found, to a person who appears to be at least 18 years of age
      at the public office holder's last known address or usual place of
      residence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by registered mail or courier to the public office
    holder's last known address, usual place of residence or place of
    work; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by electronic transmission, including by fax or
    e-mail, to the public office holder's last known address, usual
    place of residence or place of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When a copy required to be sent by
  registered mail

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a document is served by electronic transmission, a
  copy of it shall also be sent by registered mail.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of service

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The date of service of a
document is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the document is delivered personally, the day on
  which it is delivered;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served by registered mail or by
  courier, the 10th day after the date indicated in the receipt issued
  to the sender by the postal or courier service, as the case may be;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the document is served by electronic transmission,
  the day on which it is sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any of the following
documents constitute proof of service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an acknowledgment of service signed by or on behalf
  of the person served; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit of service stating the name of the
  person who served the document and the date, place and manner of
  service.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-741070 .Part}

enacted by section 2 of Chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2006, comes
into force or, if it is later, on the day on which they are registered.
