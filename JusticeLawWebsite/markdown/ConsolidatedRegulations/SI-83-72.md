## Pari-Mutuel Payments Order {#pari-mutuel-payments-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-72
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 1983-04-13

Order Respecting Payments to Be Made for the Supervision of Pari-Mutuel
Systems of Betting

P.C. 1983-846 1983-03-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 188(4) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), is pleased
hereby to revoke the [Pari-Mutuel Payments
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-72) made by Order in Council P.C.
1980-3512 of 19th December, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make
the annexed Order respecting payments to be made for the supervision of
pari-mutuel systems of betting, effective April 1, 1983.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote_e}SI/81-1, 1981
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 105
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639328 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Pari-Mutuel Payments Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-72).

## [Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639330 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 188(4) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) the fraction
fixed is eight-tenths of one per cent.
