## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Shell Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-shell-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-171
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2003-11-19

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Shell Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2003-1685  2003-10-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby
orders that a strip of land 30.48 m (100 feet) in width, measured from
the ordinary high water mark to the shore of Shell Lake, in the
Northwest Territories, not be deemed to be reserved to the Crown out of
any grant of any of the lots of land described in the schedule hereto.
:::
