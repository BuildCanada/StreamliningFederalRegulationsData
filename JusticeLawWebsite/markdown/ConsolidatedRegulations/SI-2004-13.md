## Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian Officials in Relation to Maher Arar as a Department and Designating the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Commission {#order-designating-the-commission-of-inquiry-into-the-actions-of-canadian-officials-in-relation-to-maher-arar-as-a-department-and-designating-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-commission .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-13
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2004-02-25

Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian
Officials in Relation to Maher Arar as a Department and Designating the
Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Commission

P.C. 2004-72  2004-02-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBBAA} of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), designates the Commission of
  Inquiry into the Actions of Canadian Officials in Relation to Maher
  Arar as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EFBBAA){#fna_e}S.C. 1992, c. 1, s.
    69(3)
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EDBAAA} of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of that Act,
  designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect
  to the Commission referred to in paragraph (a).

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e-ID0EDBAAA){#fnb_e}S.C. 1992, c. 1, s.
    69(1)
    :::
