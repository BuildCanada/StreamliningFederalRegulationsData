Certain Chloroprene Sheets Remission Order

P.C. 2002-1241 2002-07-17

::::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-693479 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on chloroprene sheets, with a
knitted nylon fabric laminated to both sides, that weigh more than
1500g/m^2^ and that contain less than 50% by weight of textile material.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-693481 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the chloroprene sheets were imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 1998 and ending on December
  31, 2005;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within four years of the date that the chloroprene
  sheets are imported.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-95, s. 1
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-693487 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
