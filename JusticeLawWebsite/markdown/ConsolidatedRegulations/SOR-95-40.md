## General Import Permit No. 1 --- Dairy Products for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-1-dairy-products-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-40
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1994-12-30

General Import Permit No. 1 --- Dairy Products for Personal Use
::::

:::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas dairy products have been included on the Import Control list;

And Whereas the Secretary of State for External Affairs has determined
an import access quantity for dairy products pursuant to subsection
6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBDA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBDA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 106
  :::

Therefore, the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to
section 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0ENDCA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby cancels the
[Import of Dairy Products for Personal Use
Permit](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._623), C.R.C., c. 623, and, pursuant
to subsection 8(1.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#footnote3_e){#footnote3_e-ID0EHDCA} and
subsection 8.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#footnote4_e){#footnote4_e-ID0EFDCA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues the
annexed General permit authorizing the importation of dairy products for
personal use, effective on the latest of the days on which sections 74
and 106, subsection 108(1) and sections 109 and 111 of An Act to
implement the Agreement Establishing the World Trade Organization, being
chapter 47 of the Statutes of Canada, 1994, come into force.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0ENDCA){#footnote2_e}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 111
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#footnote3_e-ID0EHDCA){#footnote3_e}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 108(1)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#footnote4_e-ID0EFDCA){#footnote4_e}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 109
  :::

Ottawa, Ontario, December 29, 1994

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975451 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit, [[dairy
products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods set out in the schedule.
(*produits laitiers*)

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975453 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), any resident of Canada may, under the authority of
  this Permit, import into Canada for the personal use of the importer
  and the importer's household dairy products not exceeding \$20 in
  value for each importation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The \$20 limit in value for each importation does not
  apply in respect of the dairy products set out in item 5 of the
  schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-41, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods imported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 1 --- Dairy Products for Personal
Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-40)\" or "[Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 1 --- Produits laitiers pour usage
personnel](/eng/regulations/DORS-95-40)]{.otherLang lang="fr"}\".
