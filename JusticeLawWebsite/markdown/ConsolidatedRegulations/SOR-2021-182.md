## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1314123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/C-21). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Bank of
    Canada. ([Banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    Rules. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collateral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collateral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a form of collateral that
    is identified by the Bank in written communications with members as
    an acceptable form of security for funds loaned or advanced to the
    member by the Bank. ([garantie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conditional release mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conditional release mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    mechanism in Lynx in which settlement instructions are temporarily
    held before being sent to a settlement mechanism or being
    rejected. ([mécanisme de déblocage conditionnel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[entry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an entry to a
    Lynx account or an intraday loan account, an entry on the books of
    the Bank. ([écriture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[finalization window]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[finalization window]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the final window
    of the payments processing cycle. ([période de
    clôture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial message service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial message service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who, or an entity that, provides an electronic message
    service that allows members to exchange financial information, but
    does not include the Association or a member. ([fournisseur de
    service de messagerie financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intraday loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intraday loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan made by the Bank
    to a participant in accordance with subsection 22(1). ([prêt
    intrajournalier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intraday loan account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intraday loan account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account on
    the books of the Bank that a participant has in Lynx and to which
    the Association makes entries respecting the making and repayment of
    the participant's intraday loan. ([compte de prêt
    intrajournalier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lynx]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lynx]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the electronic funds transfer
    system that is owned and operated by the
    Association. ([Lynx]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lynx account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lynx account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account on the books
    of the Bank that a participant has in Lynx and to which the
    Association makes entries respecting the settlement of Lynx payment
    obligations and transfers of funds to and from the account. ([compte
    Lynx]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lynx operating schedule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lynx operating schedule]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the schedule
    for Lynx operations that is set out in the Rules. ([horaire
    d'exploitation de Lynx]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Lynx payment obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Lynx payment obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the obligation
    of a sending participant to pay a fixed amount to a receiving
    participant through Lynx. ([obligation de paiement de
    Lynx]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[overnight advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[overnight advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan that has a
    term of one business day, is on the books of the Bank and is made by
    the Bank to a participant for the purpose of enabling the
    participant to repay its intraday loan. ([avance à un
    jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member who is approved
    to participate in Lynx in accordance with this
    By-law. ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person, including a
    receiving participant, to whom the amount specified in a payment
    message is to be paid or credited, whether or not that person is the
    ultimate beneficiary of the amount. ([bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[payment confirmation reference number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment confirmation reference number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the alpha-numeric confirmation number that is generated by Lynx to
    identify a Lynx payment obligation that has been settled through
    Lynx. ([numéro de confirmation du paiement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[payment message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payment message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an electronic message
    that contains settlement instructions and the information necessary
    for the receiving participant to make the amount of the payment
    finally and irrevocably available to the payee. ([message de
    paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[payments processing cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[payments processing cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion
    of the Lynx operating schedule during which Lynx payment obligations
    may be settled through Lynx, including the finalization
    window. ([cycle de traitement des paiements]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[pledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pledge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grant to the Bank of security
    in collateral for funds loaned or advanced by the Bank, and it
    includes the grant of security in which the Bank does not take
    possession of the collateral. ([nantissement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[real-time settlement mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real-time settlement mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    settlement mechanism that is used to make and repay intraday loans
    and make overnight advances. ([mécanisme de règlement en temps
    réel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[receiving participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[receiving participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a participant
    who receives a payment message from another
    participant. ([participant destinataire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reserved collateral mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserved collateral mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    settlement mechanism that is used only to settle Lynx payment
    obligations that are between the Bank and a participant and for
    which the payee is CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc., a
    corporation incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44). ([mécanisme de garantie
    réservée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rules made by the Board
    respecting Lynx. ([règles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sending participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sending participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a participant who
    sends a payment message to another participant. ([participant
    expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement instructions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement instructions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means information
    contained in a payment message that sets out a Lynx payment
    obligation and the details necessary for it to be settled through
    Lynx. ([instructions de règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement mechanism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mechanism in
    Lynx that is used to settle Lynx payment obligations. ([mécanisme de
    règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1314154 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314155 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  By-law applies to the clearing and settlement of payment messages and
  their associated Lynx payment obligations through Lynx.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each participant must comply with the provisions of
  this By-law and the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation --- rights, liabilities,
  duties and obligations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise expressly provided in this By-law
  or the Rules, nothing in this By-law or the Rules

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} affects the rights and liabilities of a person at
    law; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} imposes, or deems to impose, on a participant or
    the Association a duty, liability or obligation toward another
    person.

### [Association's Responsibilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314165 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association responsible for Lynx

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Association must manage, operate and maintain Lynx and ensure that
  Lynx is capable of performing all activities and functions required by
  this By-law and the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association responsible for contractors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Association enters into a contract with
  another person for the maintenance of Lynx or for performance of an
  activity or function required by this By-law or the Rules, the
  Association remains responsible for ensuring that Lynx is capable of
  performing all activities and functions required by this By-law and
  the Rules.

### [Exclusion of Liability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314171 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Association and Bank

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Neither the Association nor
the Bank, including their respective directors, officers and employees,
is liable for any loss or damage suffered by a member as a result of
anything done or omitted to be done in good faith in the administration
or discharge of any powers or duties that are intended or authorized to
be executed or performed under this By-law or the Rules.

### [Access to Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314174 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access provided

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Association or a
participant, regardless of whether the participant is subject to a
suspension under any of sections 8 to 10, may, in accordance with the
Rules, access information in Lynx relating to the operation of Lynx.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Participation in Lynx]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1314177 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314178 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- participant

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In sections 8 to 13, a
reference to a participant does not include a reference to the Bank.

### [Application to Be Participant]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314181 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A member
  may, in the form and manner set out in the Rules, make an application
  to the President to be a participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval criteria

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President must approve the application if the
  member demonstrates that it has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} met the technical and testing requirements set out
    in the by-laws and the Rules;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paid the applicable fees set out in the by-laws and
    the Rules; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application by a member other
    than the Bank,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} established one Lynx account per settlement
      mechanism and one intraday loan account, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} entered into agreements with the Bank in respect
      of those accounts, the provision of intraday loans and overnight
      advances and the pledging of collateral to secure those loans and
      advances.

### [Suspension and Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314192 .Subheading}

#### [Suspension]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314193 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- access to accounts

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The President must suspend
a participant's permission to participate in Lynx if the Bank notifies
the President that the participant no longer has access to its Lynx
accounts or intraday loan account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- other

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  President may suspend a participant's permission to participate in
  Lynx if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the participant no longer meets the technical or
    testing requirements set out in a by-law or the Rules; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the participant fails to pay an applicable fee set
    out in a by-law or the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notice to Bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a suspension, the President must
  provide the Bank with notice of the intention to make the suspension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- exceptional circumstances

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada, an agent or mandatary of Her
  Majesty in right of a province, a regulator or a supervisory body
  takes control of a participant or any of its assets or makes a
  declaration that a participant is considered to be no longer viable or
  that a participant is unable to meet its liabilities as they become
  due, the President may, after being advised of such an action having
  been taken or such a declaration having been made and with the
  concurrence of the Minister and the Governor of the Bank, suspend the
  participant's permission to participate in Lynx if its further
  participation could adversely affect the efficiency, safety or
  soundness of Lynx.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President may reinstate the participant's
  permission to participate in Lynx if, with the concurrence of the
  Minister and the Governor of the Bank, the President determines that
  the participant's further participation in Lynx will not adversely
  affect the efficiency, safety or soundness of Lynx.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to participants

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** After making a suspension
under this By-law, the President must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, notify the participant that its
  permission to participate in Lynx is suspended; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as feasible, notify all other participants of
  the suspension.

#### [Revocation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314212 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation by Board

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may revoke a member's approval to be a participant in Lynx if
  the participant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no longer has access to its Lynx accounts or
    intraday loan account; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the technical or testing
    requirements set out in a by-law or the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior notice to Bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a revocation, the Board must provide
  the Bank with notice of the intention to make the revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice by President

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} After the Board makes a revocation, the President
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay, notify the member of the revocation;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as feasible, notify all participants of the
    revocation.

### [Reinstatement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314224 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant who has had its permission to participate in Lynx
  suspended under section 8 or 9 or a member who has had its approval to
  be a participant in Lynx revoked under section 12 may, in the form and
  manner set out in the Rules, make an application to the President to
  have that permission or approval reinstated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President must reinstate the permission or
  approval if the application demonstrates that the circumstances giving
  rise to the suspension or revocation no longer exist.

### [Withdrawal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314230 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A participant may cease
to be a participant in Lynx by providing a notice of withdrawal to the
President in the form and manner set out in the Rules.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Lynx Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1314233 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314234 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operate accounts

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Association must
operate the following accounts in Lynx on behalf of the Bank:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each participant, one Lynx account per settlement
  mechanism; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each participant other than the Bank, one
  intraday loan account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operate every business day

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Lynx
  must operate every business day and in accordance with the Lynx
  operating schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lynx access

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All participants whose permission to participate in
  Lynx is not suspended must be provided with access to Lynx every
  business day, including access to all settlement mechanisms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Connecting to Lynx required

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** All participants whose
permission to participate in Lynx is not suspended must connect to Lynx
every business day unless technical difficulties prevent them from
connecting.

### [Liquidity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314246 .Subheading}

#### [Collateral]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314247 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pledge and assessment

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  participant other than the Bank must pledge collateral and the Bank
  must, before the beginning of the payments processing cycle every
  business day, assess the value of that collateral.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the beginning of the payments processing cycle
  every business day, each participant other than the Bank must allocate
  all or part of the assessed value of its pledged collateral as
  security for its intraday loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to allocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A participant may change the allocation of collateral
  any time before the beginning of the finalization window. However, the
  assessed value of the allocated collateral must not be less than the
  outstanding amount of the participant's intraday loan at the time the
  change is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on reassessment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the period beginning when the credit limit
  established for a participant under paragraph 19(1)(a) is provided to
  the Association and ending when the payments processing cycle ends,
  the Bank must not reassess the value of the collateral allocated by
  the participant.

#### [Credit Limit]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314257 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Credit limit established

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the beginning of the payments processing cycle every business day, the
  Bank must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each participant referred to in subsection
    18(2), establish a credit limit that is equal to the assessed value
    of the collateral allocated by the participant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the credit limit to the Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New credit limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a participant changes its allocation in accordance
  with subsection 18(3), the Bank must establish a new credit limit for
  that participant in accordance with paragraph (1)(a) and provide it to
  the Association.

#### [Funding Settlement Mechanisms]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314265 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum amount of funds

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Before the beginning of
the payments processing cycle on a business day, the Association may, in
accordance with the Rules, set the minimum amount of funds that a
participant other than the Bank must apportion to a settlement mechanism
under subsection 21(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Apportion funds

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of funding the settlement of Lynx payment obligations in a
  settlement mechanism, a participant other than the Bank may --- within
  the period before the finalization window set out in the Rules and in
  accordance with the procedures set out in the Rules --- apportion an
  amount of funds from its credit limit to its Lynx account for that
  settlement mechanism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if the Association establishes a minimum
  amount of funds to be apportioned to a settlement mechanism, the
  participant must apportion an amount of funds from its credit limit to
  its Lynx account for that mechanism that is no less than the minimum
  established.

#### [Intraday Loan]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314273 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bank makes intraday loan

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  business day, the Bank must make an intraday loan to each participant
  that is equal to the total amount of funds apportioned to all
  settlement mechanisms by the participant under subsection 21(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions to repay intraday loan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A participant may, within the period before the
  finalization window set out in the Rules, instruct the Association to
  repay all or part of its intraday loan by transferring funds from any
  of its Lynx accounts to its intraday loan account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outstanding amount of intraday loan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the outstanding amount of a
  participant's intraday loan is, at any given time in the payments
  processing cycle, equal to the amount of the intraday loan made under
  subsection (1) less the amount of any repayments made in accordance
  with repayments instructed under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfers and entries

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer the amount apportioned to a settlement
    mechanism by a participant under subsection 21(1) to the Lynx
    account for that settlement mechanism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 24(3), make the transfers
    from the participant's Lynx account to its intraday loan account to
    reflect any repayment instructed under subsection (2); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to those accounts in
    accordance with the procedures set out the Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participant's instructions

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant may, within the period before the finalization window set
  out in the Rules, instruct the Association to transfer funds between
  any of its Lynx accounts --- other than its account for the reserved
  collateral mechanism --- if the transfer will not result in a negative
  balance in any of those accounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfers and entries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 24(3), the Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the transfer as instructed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to those Lynx
    accounts in accordance with the procedures set out in the Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction by Bank to repay

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  directed by the Bank, the Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the partial repayment of a participant's
    intraday loan before the finalization window begins by transferring
    the funds in the participant's Lynx account for the reserved
    collateral mechanism to its intraday loan account; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to those accounts in
    accordance with the procedures set out in the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Bank must not make a direction other than for a
  reason specified in the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instruction to repay or transfer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a direction is made under subsection (1), any
  repayment or transfer instructed by the participant under subsection
  22(2) or 23(1) may be effected by the Association only after the
  partial repayment of the intraday loan has been made.

#### [Liquidity for Lynx Payment Obligations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1314302 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquidity available

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** At any time, the total
amount of liquidity available to a participant to settle Lynx payment
obligations is equal to the amount calculated in accordance with the
following formula:

::: {#1314305 .Section}
A = B + (C -- D)

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the total amount of liquidity available to a participant to
    settle Lynx payment obligations;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the participant's credit limit;

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the total amount of the payments received by the participant from
    the settlement of Lynx payment obligations in all settlement
    mechanisms; and

[D]{.dfn}
:   is the total amount of the payments sent by the participant in
    settlement of Lynx payment obligations that have been settled
    through Lynx in all settlement mechanisms.
:::

### [Payment Messages]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314311 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment items

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[payment item]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of
the Act, payment messages are a class of payment items.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending payment messages

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant may, in accordance with the procedures set out in the
  Rules, send a payment message to another participant for the
  settlement of a Lynx payment obligation through Lynx.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of settlement mechanism

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The participant must designate in the payment message
  the settlement mechanism that is to be used.

### [Settlement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314319 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial message service provider

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The Association must
arrange for a financial message service provider to extract settlement
instructions from payment messages, send those settlement instructions
to Lynx and provide participants with payment messages and payment
confirmation reference numbers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extraction of settlement instructions by
participant

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A sending participant may
extract the settlement instructions from a payment message and send them
to Lynx if, as a result of technical difficulties,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the financial message service provider is unable to
  receive the payment message or extract the settlement instructions; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sending participant is unable to send the payment
  message to the financial message service provider.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement instructions sent to
settlement mechanism

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after receiving
  settlement instructions, Lynx must send them to the settlement
  mechanism designated under subsection 27(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the settlement instructions
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be sent to the conditional release mechanism if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sending participant has directed that
      transfer of the amount specified in the settlement instructions is
      to occur at a specific time later in the payments processing
      cycle,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sending participant or receiving participant
      is temporarily unable to settle the Lynx payment obligation
      through Lynx,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the settlement mechanism designated under
      subsection 27(2) is not open,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} transfer of the amount specified in the
      settlement instructions would exceed the "net send limit", as
      defined in the Rules, set by the sending participant, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Rules require that the settlement
      instructions must be validated by the sending participant before
      the Lynx payment obligation can be settled through Lynx; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be rejected if the payment message is a duplicate
    or the settlement instructions contain an error specified in the
    Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement required

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Association must settle each Lynx payment obligation for which
  settlement instructions are sent to a settlement mechanism under
  subsection 30(1) or from the conditional release mechanism.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- negative balance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the sending participant is the Bank, the
  Association must not settle a Lynx payment obligation if settling it
  would result in a negative balance in the Lynx account for the
  settlement mechanism designated under subsection 27(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement through Lynx

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of this By-law, a Lynx payment obligation is settled
  though Lynx when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Association transfers the amount indicated in
    the settlement instructions from the sending participant's Lynx
    account for the settlement mechanism designated under subsection
    27(2) to the receiving participant's Lynx account for that
    settlement mechanism; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Association makes the corresponding entries to
    those accounts in accordance with the procedures set out in the
    Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment confirmation reference number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After a Lynx payment obligation is settled through
  Lynx, the Association must provide the financial message service
  provider with the payment confirmation reference number that is to
  accompany the payment message that the service provider will provide
  to the participants.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Finality for participants

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** A sending participant's
payment to a receiving participant is final and irrevocable when the
Lynx payment obligation is settled through Lynx in accordance with
subsection 32(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Settlement queues

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  Lynx payment obligation cannot be settled through Lynx immediately
  after the settlement instructions are sent to a settlement mechanism,
  those instructions must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be held in the settlement queue for that settlement
    mechanism, in the sequence set out in the Rules, for processing
    later in the payments processing cycle; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be rejected, if that settlement mechanism does not
    have a settlement queue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from queue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A sending participant may, at any time, remove from a
  settlement queue any settlement instructions that were contained in a
  payment message that it sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rejection of settlement instructions

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** Settlement instructions
must be rejected if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they remain in the settlement queue when the
  settlement mechanism closes; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they remain in the conditional release mechanism when
  the finalization window begins.

### [Payments to Payees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314363 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  a Lynx payment obligation is settled through Lynx and the receiving
  participant has the payment message and the payment confirmation
  reference number, the receiving participant must make the amount of
  the payment finally and irrevocably available to the payee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the payee makes a reasonable
    request for payment before the end of the payments processing cycle,
    as soon as feasible after the request is received, but by no later
    than the end of the payments processing cycle; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by no later than the end of the
    payments processing cycle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service charges

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A receiving participant may deduct the amount of a
  service charge from the amount of a payment and must comply with the
  deduction and disclosure procedures, if any, set out in the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final and irrevocable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a payment is made finally and
  irrevocably available to a payee when the earliest of the following
  actions is taken:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant credits the amount to an
    account of the payee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant lawfully applies the
    amount against a debt of the payee; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant otherwise makes the
    amount of the payment accessible to the payee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation to payee

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  receiving participant must not represent to a payee that a payment it
  has made to the payee is a payment under section 36 unless there is an
  associated Lynx payment obligation that has been settled through Lynx.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Finality for payee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a receiving participant makes a payment available
  to a payee and there is no associated Lynx payment obligation or that
  obligation has not yet been settled through Lynx, the amount of the
  payment is made finally and irrevocably available to the payee if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payee requests that the receiving participant
    provide the payment confirmation reference number; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant provides the payee with a
    number that it represents as being the payment confirmation
    reference number.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptional circumstances

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** If any of the following
exceptional circumstances exist, the receiving participant must comply
with the procedures set out in the Rules for those circumstances instead
of making the amount of the payment finally and irrevocably available to
the payee in accordance with section 36:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment message received by the receiving
  participant contains an error or omission specified in the Rules;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payment message is received by the receiving
  participant after the time specified in the Rules;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the payment to be made available must
  be converted into a currency other than Canadian dollars;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} action is required by the branch or office at which
  the amount of the payment is to be made available to the payee and the
  receiving participant receives the payment message and payment
  confirmation reference number after the time specified in the Rules;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant cannot make the amount of
  the payment available due to circumstances beyond its control;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant cannot make the amount of
  the payment available because of a restriction it has imposed on the
  payee or the account to which the payment is to be credited;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} in order to comply with a law or an order of a court,
  the receiving participant cannot make the amount of the payment
  available to the payee; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the receiving participant agrees to the payee's
  request that the amount of the payment not be made available to them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment confirmation reference number

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A receiving participant
must provide a payee with the payment confirmation reference number if
the receiving participant has the number and it is requested by the
payee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payee identifier

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  receiving participant who receives a payment message that identifies
  the payee both by name and an account number or other identifier
  specified in the Rules is not required to detect any inconsistency
  between them and may, for the purpose of making the amount of the
  payment available to the payee, rely on that account number or other
  identifier.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reliance on payee identifier

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A receiving participant who relies on an account
  number or other identifier under subsection (1) is deemed to have
  complied with section 36 even if that reliance results in the amount
  of the payment being made available to a person other than the payee
  identified by name in the payment message.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations to payee

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The obligations of a
receiving participant under any of sections 36 to 40 are to the payee
and no obligation is owed by the receiving participant to the sending
participant, the payor or any other person solely because of any of
those sections.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements not limited

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The requirements set out
in sections 36 to 41 must not be limited by the Rules or any agreement.

### [Repayment of Intraday Loan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314404 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation of balances

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the beginning of the finalization window, the Association must, for
  each participant, consolidate into the participant's Lynx account for
  the real-time settlement mechanism the balances in all of the
  participant's other Lynx accounts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfers and entries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the necessary transfers to carry out the
    consolidation referred to in subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to the accounts
    referred to in subsection (1) in accordance with the procedures set
    out in the Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  balance in a participant's intraday loan account is greater than zero
  when the finalization window begins, the Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer, from the participant's Lynx account for
    the real-time settlement mechanism to the participant's intraday
    loan account, the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an amount that is equal to the balance in the
      participant's intraday loan account, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the balance in the participant's Lynx account
      for the real time settlement mechanism; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to those accounts in
    accordance with the procedures set out the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Balances provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After making any transfer required under subsection
  (1), the Association must provide the Bank with the balance in the
  participant's Lynx account for the real-time settlement mechanism and
  the participant's intraday loan account.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer to Bank's Lynx account

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Association must transfer the amount of the
  balances consolidated under subsection 43(1), less any amount
  transferred under subsection (1), to the Bank's Lynx account for the
  real-time settlement mechanism and make the corresponding entries to
  those accounts in accordance with the procedures set out in the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and liabilities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the respective rights and
  liabilities of the Bank and a participant in respect of the amount
  transferred to the Bank under subsection (3) are governed in
  accordance with the terms of the agreements between them.

### [Overnight Advances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314425 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance by Bank

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  balance in a participant's intraday loan account remains greater than
  zero after a transfer referred to in paragraph 44(1)(a) has been made,
  the Bank must make an overnight advance to the participant in an
  amount equal to the outstanding balance of the intraday loan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer and entries

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Association must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer the amount of the overnight advance from
    the Bank's Lynx account for the real-time settlement mechanism to
    the participant's Lynx account for that settlement mechanism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer the amount transferred under paragraph (a)
    to the participant's intraday loan account; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} make the corresponding entries to those accounts in
    accordance with the procedures set out in the Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rights and liabilities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the respective rights and
  liabilities of the Bank and a participant in respect of an overnight
  advance are governed in accordance with the terms of the agreements
  between them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intraday loan repaid

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A participant's intraday
loan is repaid when the balance of in its intraday loan account is
reduced to zero by means of a transfer referred to in paragraph 44(1)(a)
or subsection 45(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 36 to 46

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), sections
  36 to 46 apply in respect of a participant who is subject to a
  suspension or revocation under this By-law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment to payee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A participant who is subject to a suspension or
  revocation under this By-law is required to make the amount of a
  payment available to a payee only if the associated Lynx payment
  obligation is settled through Lynx before the suspension or
  revocation, as the case may be.

### [End of Payments Processing Cycle]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314443 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of cycle

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** The payments processing
cycle ends when the Association has made all transfers and entries
required under section 44 and subsection 45(2).

### [Emergencies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1314446 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency condition

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** If communications between
Lynx and a participant are interrupted, the ability of Lynx to receive,
send or otherwise process payment messages or to settle Lynx payment
obligations is impaired, the safe and efficient operation of Lynx is
placed into question or any other emergency affects its operations, the
President, with the prior agreement of the Bank and in accordance with
the procedures set out in the Rules, may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct that the Lynx operating schedule, or the
  conduct of any event in the schedule, be changed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} direct a participant not to send payment messages for
  a specified period of time;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct that any day is not a business day; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} direct the Association or a participant to take any
  other action necessary to ensure

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the safe and efficient operation of Lynx, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the continued processing of payment messages or
    settlement of Lynx payment obligations.
