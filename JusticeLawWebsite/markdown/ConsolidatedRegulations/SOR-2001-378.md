## [Equity of an Association]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673538 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equity

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
[Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), the equity of
an association is the sum of the aggregate book value of the shares of
the association and the aggregate book value of the membership shares of
the association, as they appear in the consolidated financial statements
of the association.
