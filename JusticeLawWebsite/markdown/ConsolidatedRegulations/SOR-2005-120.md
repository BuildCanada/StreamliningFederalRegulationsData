## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715029 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Toronto Port Authority
shall not use, or authorize or permit another person to use, the port to
build a bridge or similar fixed link between the mainland of the City of
Toronto and the Toronto Islands.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715031 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Section 1 does not apply in
respect of a pedestrian tunnel.
