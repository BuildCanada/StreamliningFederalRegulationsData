## Preliminary Screening Requirement Regulations {#preliminary-screening-requirement-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-12
:::

[MACKENZIE VALLEY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/M-0.2)

Registration 1998-12-16

Preliminary Screening Requirement Regulations

P.C. 1998-2264  1998-12-16
::::

:::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 143(1) of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676){#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676-ID0EDACA},
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development has consulted
with the Minister of Resources, Wildlife and Economic Development of the
Northwest Territories and with the first nations with regard to the
proposed [Preliminary Screening Requirement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-12) in the annexed form;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to paragraph 143(1)(b) of the [Mackenzie Valley
Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676){#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676-ID0EDABA},
hereby makes the annexed [Preliminary Screening Requirement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-12).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676-ID0EDACA){#fn_SOR-99-12_e_hq_4676}S.C.
  1998, c. 25
  :::
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1026531 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 124(1) of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), a regulatory authority or a designated regulatory
agency that receives an application for a licence, permit or other
authorization required to carry out a proposed development, under a
provision of a federal Act set out in Part 1 of Schedule 1 or a
provision of federal regulations set out in Part 2 of that Schedule,
shall conduct a preliminary screening of the proposed development before
the issuance of the licence, permit or authorization.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 124(1) of the [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management
Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), a regulatory authority or a designated regulatory
agency that receives an application for a licence, permit or other
authorization required to carry out a proposed development, under a
provision of an Act of the Northwest Territories set out in Part 1 of
Schedule 2 or a provision of regulations of the Northwest Territories
set out in Part 2 of that Schedule, shall conduct a preliminary
screening of the proposed development before the issuance of the
licence, permit or authorization.
