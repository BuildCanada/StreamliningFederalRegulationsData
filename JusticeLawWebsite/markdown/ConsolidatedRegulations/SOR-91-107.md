## Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development Privileges and Immunities Order {#organization-for-economic-co-operation-and-development-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-107
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1991-01-17

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities of the Organization for
Economic Co-operation and Development

P.C. 1991-41  1991-01-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section 4 of
the Privileges and Immunities (International Organizations) Act, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the privileges and
immunities of the Organization for Economic Co-operation and
Development.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932626 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development Privileges
and Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-107).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932628 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement on Privileges
    and Immunities between Canada and the Organization;
    ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Organization for
    Economic Co-operation and Development.
    ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932633 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have in Canada the legal capacities of a body
  corporate and shall, to the extent provided in section II of the
  Agreement, have the privileges and immunities set out in Articles II
  and III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of the Organization shall have in Canada, to the extent
  provided in section III of the Agreement, the privileges and
  immunities set out in Article IV of the Convention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the Organization shall have in Canada,
  to the extent provided in section IV of the Agreement, the privileges
  and immunities set out in Article V of the Convention.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall have in Canada, to the extent provided in section V of the
  Agreement, the privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the
  Convention.
