## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[(Paragraph 67(1)(a) of the Act)]{.OriginatingRef}[Band Elections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-911878 .Part}

### [Number of Positions of Council Member]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911879 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The council of a band shall
consist of one chief and not fewer than four nor more than twelve
councillors, determined on the basis of one councillor for every 100
members of the band.

### [Term of Office of Council Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911881 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The term of office of a
council member shall be for a period of two years commencing on the day
following the day of the election of that member.

### [Calling of Elections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911883 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  council calls a band election, the council shall determine the day on
  which the poll for the election will be held and whether an advance
  poll or travelling poll, or both, will be held.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a Returning Officer calls a band election
  pursuant to section 77 of the Act, the Returning Officer shall
  determine the day on which the poll for the election will be held and
  whether an advance poll or travelling poll, or both, will be held.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a band election is to
be held, the Returning Officer of the band shall determine,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with section 10, on which day the
  nomination meeting for the election will be held and shall decide at
  which hour of the day and at which location within the land of the
  band the meeting will be held;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with section 25, the hours during which
  the poll for the election will be held and shall decide at which
  location within the land of the band the poll will be held;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if necessary, on which day, prior to the day
  determined for the poll, during which hours and at which location
  within the land of the band the advance poll for the election will be
  held; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if necessary, on which day, prior to the day
  determined for the poll, during which hours and at which location
  within or outside the land of the band the travelling poll for the
  election will be held.

### [Electoral List]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911892 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a band election is to
be held, the Returning Officer of the band shall prepare and sign an
electoral list that sets out, in alphabetical order, the names of the
electors of the band.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Returning Officer of a band may, on the application of an elector,
  revise an electoral list of the band to add the name of an elector
  whose name had been omitted from the list, correct the name of an
  elector whose name had been incorrectly set out or delete the name of
  a person who is not an elector.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Returning Officer of a band may, on his own
  initiative, correct any clerical errors in an electoral list of the
  band.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A Returning Officer shall initial each revision and
  correction made to an electoral list pursuant to subsection (1) or
  (2), as the case may be.

### [Notice of Election]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911898 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** At least 15 clear days
prior to the day determined for the poll for a band election, the
Returning Officer of the band shall prominently post, in the head office
of the band and in one or more public places within the land of the
band,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice of election in the form set out
  in Schedule I and signed by the Returning Officer; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the electoral list for the election.

### [Nomination Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911902 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A nomination meeting for
a band election shall be held during the period commencing five clear
days after the posting of the notice of election for the election and
ending five clear days prior to the day determined for the poll for the
election.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be a
candidate for the position of council member unless that person is an
elector who is eligible to be elected to that position.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A nomination may be made
in writing, on a nomination paper, or orally, at a nomination meeting.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A written nomination for
a band election shall be in the form set out in Schedule II and shall be
signed by the candidate nominated and by three other electors of the
band and shall be forwarded to the Returning Officer of the band prior
to the nomination meeting for the election.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** On the day, at the hour
and at the location specified in the election notice for a band
election, the Returning Officer of the band shall declare the nomination
meeting open and announce and post the names of the candidates for the
position of chief or councillor for whom a written nomination has been
received by the Returning Officer pursuant to section 13.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  oral nomination presented at a nomination meeting for a band election
  shall be proposed by an elector of the band and seconded by another
  elector of the band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is nominated orally at a nomination
  meeting for a band election shall indicate his acceptance or refusal
  of the nomination to the Returning Officer of the band prior to the
  close of the meeting and, where he does not so indicate, he shall be
  considered to have refused the nomination.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person who is nominated orally at a
  nomination meeting for a band election is not present at the meeting,
  the person shall indicate his acceptance or refusal of the nomination
  to the Returning Officer of the band by telephone, radio or any other
  means considered appropriate by the Returning Officer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the nomination meeting for a band election, the
  Returning Officer of the band shall indicate the requirements of
  subsections (1) to (3) to the persons at the meeting.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall accept
nominations as a candidate in a band election for both the position of
chief and the position of councillor.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), at the
  nomination meeting for a band election, the Returning Officer of the
  band shall declare the meeting closed when nominations have ceased.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No nomination meeting or session of a nomination
  meeting shall last less than two hours.

<!-- -->

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  immediately before the close of a nomination meeting for a band
  election, there is an insufficient number of candidates to fill all
  vacant positions, the Returning Officer of the band shall declare the
  first session of the meeting adjourned and shall reconvene the meeting
  at a second session two days later.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No nomination meeting shall consist of more than two
  sessions.

### [Election by Acclamation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911920 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  at the close of a nomination meeting for a band election, there is
  only one candidate for the position of chief, the Returning Officer of
  the band shall forthwith proclaim the candidate to be elected by
  acclamation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, at the close of a nomination meeting for a
  band election, the number of candidates for the position of councillor
  is equal to or less than the number of councillor positions to be
  filled, the Returning Officer of the band shall forthwith proclaim the
  candidates to be elected by acclamation.

### [Poll Required]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911924 .Subheading}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Where, at the close of a
nomination meeting for a band election, there is more than one candidate
for the position of chief or the number of candidates for the position
of councillor exceeds the number of councillor positions to be filled at
the election, the Returning Officer of the band shall declare that a
poll will be held in respect of that position or those positions, as the
case may be, on the day specified in the notice of election for that
election.

### [Notice of Poll]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911926 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where a poll is required
at a band election, the Returning Officer of the band shall as soon as
possible sign and prominently post a copy of the notice of poll, in the
form set out in Schedule III, in the head office of the band and in one
or more public places within the land of the band and shall send a copy
of the notice to the band secretary.

### [Preparation for Poll]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911928 .Subheading}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Where a poll is required
at a band election, the Returning Officer of the band shall ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are one or more polling stations within the
  land of the band;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is, at each polling station, one voting
  compartment that is constructed in such a manner that electors can
  mark their ballots in secret;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} there are, at each voting compartment, directions for
  voting and a pen or pencil that is similar to the pen or pencil at
  each of the other voting compartments;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} there are ballots in a sufficient number for the poll
  to be held;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} there is a sufficient number of ballot boxes for the
  poll to be held; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} there is a poll book for each polling station.

<!-- -->

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Ballots for the election of a chief
  and ballots for the election of a councillor shall be in the form set
  out in schedules IV and V respectively.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A ballot shall list, in alphabetical order, the name
  of each candidate and may, if applicable, indicate the name by which a
  candidate is commonly known but shall not indicate any additional
  information regarding a candidate.

<!-- -->

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  ballot box shall be made of durable material, furnished with a padlock
  and constructed in such a manner that any ballots that have been
  deposited in it cannot be withdrawn without unlocking the ballot box.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Returning Officer shall keep count of and supervise
  the ballot boxes to be used at a band election up to the opening of
  the polling stations for the election.

### [Election Method and Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911942 .Subheading}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection
26(1), a poll shall be open for voting continuously for at least eight
hours between the hours of 6 o'clock in the morning and 10 o'clock in
the evening on the day determined for the poll.

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a natural disaster, an electrical blackout or other
  extraordinary circumstance, a Returning Officer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} postpone the closing of a poll for up to two hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} postpone a poll for up to four weeks; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} interrupt a poll and resume the poll within four
    weeks.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Returning Officer of a band postpones a
  poll or the closing of a poll or interrupts a poll, the Returning
  Officer shall take reasonable measures to inform the electors of the
  band of any resulting changes regarding the poll.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** A candidate for a band
election may appoint, in writing,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each polling station provided for the election,
  one person to represent the candidate during the hours the polling
  station is open for voting; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two persons to represent the candidate during the
  counting of votes.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Immediately prior to the
opening of a polling station for a band election for voting, the
Returning Officer of the band shall open each ballot box to be used at
the polling station, call on any persons present to witness that the
ballot boxes are empty and then padlock the ballot boxes.

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an elector appears at the appropriate polling station for the purpose
  of voting in a band election, the Returning Officer of the band shall
  verify that the elector's name is on the electoral list for the band
  and that the elector has not yet voted in the election and shall
  initial the back of a ballot for the election of a chief or a ballot
  for the election of a councillor, or both, as the case may be, and
  hand the ballot or ballots to the elector.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Returning Officer may, and where requested to do
  so, shall, explain the method of voting to an elector.

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** On receiving a ballot or
ballots, an elector shall immediately proceed to a voting compartment to
vote.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-592, s. 2(E)
:::

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), only one
  person shall be in a voting compartment at any one time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An elector who is unable to vote without assistance
  because of an inability to read or to understand the language in which
  the ballots are printed or because of blindness or any other physical
  disability may be assisted in voting by the Returning Officer or,
  where accompanied by a friend or relative, by the friend or relative.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-592, s. 2(E)
:::

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** After marking a ballot,
an elector shall forthwith return the ballot to the Returning Officer
who shall check, without attempting to see how the elector voted,
whether the ballot has been initialled by the Returning Officer.

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the Returning Officer to whom a ballot is returned shall request the
  elector who returned the ballot to deposit it in the ballot box.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a ballot that was not initialled by the
  Returning Officer is returned to the Returning Officer by an elector,
  the Returning Officer shall mark the word "declined" on the back of
  the ballot and deposit the ballot in the ballot box.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a returned ballot is damaged, the Returning
  Officer to whom the ballot is returned shall mark the word "damaged"
  on the back of the ballot and deposit it in the ballot box.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-592, s. 2
:::

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** An elector whose ballot
was marked "declined" pursuant to subsection 33(2) or "damaged" pursuant
to subsection 33(3) shall be entitled to receive a replacement ballot.

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The Returning Officer at
a polling station shall list, in the poll book provided for that polling
station, the name of each elector who receives a ballot at the polling
station and shall record opposite an elector's name any case where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elector receives assistance to vote pursuant to
  subsection 31(2) and the reason for that assistance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the elector after receiving a ballot left the polling
  station without returning the ballot to the Returning Officer.

<!-- -->

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  elector who after receiving a ballot at a polling station leaves the
  polling station without returning the ballot to the Returning Officer
  shall be considered to have voted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an elector referred to in subsection (1) later
  returns a ballot to a Returning Officer, the Returning Officer shall
  mark the word "declined" on the back of the ballot and deposit it in
  the ballot box.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** An elector who is inside
a polling station at the time determined for the closing of the polling
station and who is otherwise entitled to vote at that polling station
shall be entitled to vote before the polling station is closed.

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after a polling station is
  closed, the Returning Officer at the polling station shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} record in the poll book for that polling station
    the number of ballots issued for the polling station, the number of
    ballots used and the number of ballots remaining unused; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place in separate envelopes the unused ballots for
    chief and the unused ballots for councillor and label each envelope
    to indicate its contents.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the duties of the Returning Officer of a band
  at a polling station are performed by a Deputy Returning Officer or an
  Assistant Returning Officer, the Deputy Returning Officer or the
  Assistant Returning Officer, as the case may be, shall, forthwith
  after complying with subsection (1), return the ballot boxes and poll
  books used at the polling station and the envelopes of unused ballots
  to the Returning Officer of the band.

### [Advance Polls and Travelling Polls]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911985 .Subheading}

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Where an advance poll is
held in connection with a band election, an elector who has reason to
believe that he will be unable to vote on the day on which the poll for
the election will be held may vote at the advance poll.

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Where a travelling poll
is held in connection with a band election, an elector who has reason to
believe that he will be unable to vote on the day on which and at the
location at which the poll for the election will be held may vote at the
travelling poll.

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Sections 22 to 38 apply
to an advance poll or travelling poll with such modifications as the
circumstances require.

### [Counting the Votes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-911989 .Subheading}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** Forthwith after the
closing of the poll for a band election, the Returning Officer of the
band shall count the votes, including, if applicable, those at the
advance poll and the travelling poll held in connection with the
election, in the presence of any candidates and representatives who wish
to attend.

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  counting votes at a band election, the Returning Officer of the band
  shall reject any ballot

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was not initialled by the Returning Officer,
    the Deputy Returning Officer or an Assistant Returning Officer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is blank;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on which there appear more votes than there are
    positions to be filled;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on which there appear marks or writing identifying
    the elector; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on which the Returning Officer has marked
    "declined" or "damaged".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In counting votes at a band election, the Returning
  Officer of the band shall reject, on a ballot, any vote that does not
  clearly indicate the elector's intention.

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Where a candidate or a
representative objects to a decision of the Returning Officer concerning
the counting of a vote in a band election, the Returning Officer of the
band shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} decide any questions arising out of the objection;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} number and record the objection in the appropriate
  poll book; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} write on the back of the ballot the word or words
  "allowed", "allowed in part" or "rejected", as the case may be, as
  well as the number of the objection and initial the ballot.

<!-- -->

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the counting of the votes at a band election, the Returning Officer of
  the band shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, separate the ballots provided for
    the election for the position of chief from the ballots provided for
    the election for the position of councillor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sort the ballots provided for the election for the
    position of chief or councillor, as the case may be, into two
    separate lots consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the ballots allowed or allowed in part, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the rejected ballots.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after the counting of the votes at a band
  election, the Returning Officer of the band shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} place each of the lots of ballots referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) in a separate envelope and label each envelope to
    indicate its contents; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} complete and sign a poll report in the form set out
    in Schedule VI and forward a copy of the poll report to each of the
    candidates for the election, the band secretary, the Minister of
    Indian Affairs and Northern Development and, in the case of a Cree
    band election, to the secretary of the Cree Regional Authority.

### [Positions of Council Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912012 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a poll is held for the position of chief, the candidate for the
  position who receives the largest number of votes shall be elected
  chief.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a poll is held for one or more positions of
  councillor, the candidate or candidates, to the number corresponding
  to the number of positions of councillor to be filled, who receive the
  largest number of votes shall be elected councillor.

<!-- -->

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a poll is held at a general election, the candidate who receives the
  largest number of votes for the position of councillor shall be the
  deputy chief.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any circumstances other than the circumstances
  referred to in subsection (1), the council of a band shall designate,
  from among the councillors of the band, one councillor to be the
  deputy chief.

### [Proclamation of Candidates Elected]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912019 .Subheading}

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Forthwith after the
counting of votes in a band election, the Returning Officer of the band
shall proclaim elected the candidates elected in accordance with section
46.

### [Recount of Votes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912021 .Subheading}

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Returning Officer of a band shall recount the votes for a position to
  be filled at a band election where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is an equal number of votes for each of two
    or more candidates for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the position of chief,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the position of deputy chief, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a position of councillor, where the position is
      the only position or the only remaining position to be filled; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Returning Officer receives, within one day
    after the day on which the poll for the band election was held, a
    written request for a recount of votes from a candidate for a
    position to be filled at the election.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 42 to 48 apply, with such modifications as
  the circumstances require, to any recount of votes.

### [Recount Poll]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912030 .Subheading}

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  after a recount of votes there exists an equal number of votes for
  each of two or more candidates for a position referred to in paragraph
  49(1)(a), the Returning Officer of the band shall, within 28 days
  after the day on which the recount was conducted, hold a second poll
  for those candidates for that position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations apply to a poll referred to in
  subsection (1) with such modifications as the circumstances require.

### [Election Results]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912034 .Subheading}

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  five days after the day on which the poll for a band election was
  held, the Returning Officer of the band shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a report of election results in the form
    set out in Schedule VII;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sign the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prominently post a copy of the report in the head
    office of the band and in one or more public places within the land
    of the band; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} forward a copy of the report of election results to
    the persons referred to in paragraph 45(2)(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where all the candidates for a band election have
  been elected by acclamation, the Returning Officer of the band shall
  comply with subsection (1) within five days of the nomination meeting
  held for that election.

### [Contestation of Election Results]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912042 .Subheading}

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A petition prepared
pursuant to subsection 78(3) of the Act shall be in the form set out in
Schedule VIII.

### [Election Materials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912044 .Subheading}

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Returning Officer of a band shall retain and safeguard all the
  election materials used in a band election including the ballots,
  ballot boxes, electoral list, poll books, poll report and report of
  the election results for a period of 14 days after the day on which
  the poll for the band election is held.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), after the expiration of
  the period referred to in subsection (1), the Returning Officer of a
  band shall remit to the band secretary for safekeeping all the
  election materials referred to in subsection (1), except the ballots,
  which shall be destroyed by the Returning Officer in the presence of
  two witnesses.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the election of a council member for a band is
  contested pursuant to section 78 of the Act, the Returning Officer of
  the band shall continue to retain and safeguard the election materials
  referred to in subsection (1) until otherwise directed by the judge
  who is inquiring into the election.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912049 .Subheading}

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Returning Officer of a band shall supervise the conduct of any
  election held by that band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy Returning Officer or an Assistant
  Returning Officer appointed by the Returning Officer pursuant to
  subsection 71(2) of the Act may, under the supervision of the
  Returning Officer, perform the duties of the Returning Officer under
  these Regulations except those referred to in subsections 15(2) and
  (3), sections 26, 42 to 45 and 48, subsections 49(1) and 50(1),
  sections 51 and 53 and this section.

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** All documents referred to
in these Regulations for use in a band election shall be in either the
English or the French language and may also be in the Cree language or
the Naskapi language, as the case may be.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[(Paragraph 67(1)(b) of the Act)]{.OriginatingRef}[Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912054 .Part}

### [Prohibited Acts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912055 .Subheading}

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, at a
poll for a band election, vote or attempt to vote

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than once;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowing that he is not entitled to vote; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} under any name other than his own name.

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** No person shall induce
another person to vote at a poll for a band election knowing that the
other person is not entitled to vote.

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use
bribery, treating, undue influence or any other corrupt practice in a
band election.

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** No person shall cause or
attempt to cause any disturbance at a nomination meeting or at a polling
station.

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** No person shall campaign
or advertise in connection with a band election in a polling station or
within 30 metres of a polling station.

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** No person shall be
present in a polling station except those authorized by a by-law made
under section 64 of the Act or these Regulations and those present for
the purposes of voting.

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized by a
by-law made under section 64 of the Act or by subsection 31(2), no
person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in a voting compartment with an elector;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be in a position from which the person can see for
  whom an elector marks his ballot; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at a polling station, attempt to know how an elector
  intends to vote.

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** No person who assists an
elector to vote pursuant to a by-law made under section 64 of the Act or
to subsection 31(2) shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} influence or attempt to influence the elector in the
  selection of the candidate for whom the elector votes; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the elector's ballot contrary to the elector's
  intention.

<!-- -->

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall interfere with or attempt to interfere with an elector
  who is in a voting compartment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall interfere with or attempt to
  interfere with the counting of votes.

<!-- -->

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} counterfeit or fraudulently alter or deface a
    ballot or the initials of a Returning Officer, Deputy Returning
    Officer or Assistant Returning Officer on a ballot;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fraudulently destroy a ballot;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} without authority, supply a ballot to a person;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fraudulently deposit in a ballot box any paper
    other than the ballot that the person is authorized by a by-law made
    under section 64 of the Act or by these Regulations to deposit in
    the ballot box;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} without authority take a ballot;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} fraudulently take a ballot out of a polling
    station;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} without authority, destroy, damage, take, open or
    otherwise interfere with a ballot box; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} fraudulently print a ballot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall attempt to commit any of the acts
  referred to in subsection (1).

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** No Returning Officer,
Deputy Returning Officer or Assistant Returning Officer shall divulge
any information obtained by that officer in respect of the conduct of an
election

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} except as authorized by a by-law made under section
  64 of the Act or by these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} unless that officer is required to give evidence in
  the course of a legal proceeding respecting the election.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-592, s. 2
:::

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** No Returning Officer,
Deputy Returning Officer or Assistant Returning Officer shall
fraudulently initial or attempt to initial a ballot or any paper
purported to be a ballot.

### [Maximum Punishment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-912092 .Subheading}

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 198 of the Act,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), for the contravention of
  paragraph 56(a) or any of sections 59 to 62, 64 or 66, the maximum
  fine is one hundred dollars and the maximum term of imprisonment is
  three months;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), for the contravention of
  paragraph 56(b) or section 57, 63 or 65, the maximum fine is two
  hundred dollars and the maximum term of imprisonment is six months;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the contravention of paragraph 56(b), section 57,
  62, 63 or any of sections 65 to 67 by a Returning Officer, a Deputy
  Returning Officer or an Assistant Returning Officer, the maximum fine
  is four hundred dollars and the maximum term of imprisonment is six
  months; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the contravention of paragraph 56(c) or section
  58, the maximum fine is five hundred dollars and the maximum term of
  imprisonment is six months.
