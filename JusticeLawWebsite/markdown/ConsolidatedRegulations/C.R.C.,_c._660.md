## Canadian Turkey Licensing Regulations {#canadian-turkey-licensing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 660
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Regulations Respecting the Licensing of Persons who are Engaged in the
Marketing of Turkeys in Interprovincial or Export Trade
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559718 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Turkey Licensing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._660).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559720 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Turkey Marketing
    Agency; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consumer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consumer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who buys turkey
    for consumption by him or his
    household; ([consommateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period set out in
    the heading of the schedule to the [Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota
    Regulations, 1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-231); ([période
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person other than a
    processor or retailer who buys turkeys for
    resale; ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hatcheryman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hatcheryman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    hatching of turkey eggs; ([accouveur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an inspector appointed
    pursuant to section 34 of the Act; ([inspecteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a document issued by the Agency to a person
      certifying that he is a licensee, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list issued by the Agency to a person
      containing his name and the names of other persons and certifying
      that each person is a licensee; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to turkeys, means
    selling and offering for sale and buying, pricing, assembling,
    packing, processing, transporting, storing and reselling, whether in
    whole or in processed form, and includes marketing by or on behalf
    of a producer; ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means changing the nature, size,
    quality or condition of turkeys by mechanical means or otherwise and
    includes killing; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    business of processing turkeys or who packs, markets, grades, offers
    for sale, sells, stores, or transports the packed, processed or
    manufactured turkey product; ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of turkeys in Canada; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer-vendor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer-vendor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who
    produces and markets, offers for sale, sells or stores all or any
    portion of the turkeys produced by
    him; ([producteur-vendeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who sells or
    offers for sale turkey directly and exclusively to
    consumers; ([détaillant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turkey, male or female,
    live or slaughtered, of any size and includes any part of any
    turkey. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-250, s. 1
- SOR/2017-70, s. 1

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559740 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 10, no person shall engage in the marketing of turkeys in
  interprovincial or export trade as a producer, producer-vendor,
  hatcheryman, processor or dealer unless he holds the appropriate
  licence set out in section 4 and pays to the Agency annually the fee
  prescribed by that section for that licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell or offer for sale turkey to any
  person required to be licensed under these Regulations unless that
  person is licensed as required by subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall buy turkeys from any person required
  to be licensed under these Regulations unless that person is licensed
  as required by subsection (1).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The types of licences and
fees payable therefor are as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Producer's licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} Nil

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Producer-Vendor's licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$1.00

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Processor's licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} 1.00

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Dealer's licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} 1.00

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Hatcheryman's licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} 1.00

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a person engages in
marketing turkeys in more than one of the capacities set out in
subsection 3(1) he shall apply to the Agency for a licence in respect of
each capacity.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every licence shall be
issued in respect of a control period and comes into effect on the first
day of the control period and expires on the last day of that control
period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-250, s. 2
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every licence shall be
issued subject to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensee shall, within seven days following the
  last day of each week, forward to the Agency, a report in a form
  approved by the Agency containing the information specified in the
  schedule respecting the marketing of turkeys in interprovincial or
  export trade during that week;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensee shall permit an inspector to enter his
  premises and examine his books, records or other documents in
  accordance with subsection 35(1) of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensee shall comply with all orders and
  regulations of the Agency.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Agency may suspend,
revoke or refuse to issue a licence where the applicant or licensee has
failed to observe, perform or carry out any condition of the licence.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where the Agency intends to
suspend or revoke a licence, the Agency shall give to the licensee by
hand delivery or registered mail addressed to him at his address
recorded in the books of the Agency notice of its intention to suspend
or revoke the licence, as the case may be, and such notice shall fix a
time of not less than 30 days from the delivery or mailing thereof for
the licensee to show cause why the licence should not be suspended or
revoked, as the case may be.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply, in the Province of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Nova Scotia, to a producer who markets less than
  25 turkeys in a year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} New Brunswick, to a producer who markets less than
  26 turkeys in a year;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Ontario, to a producer who markets less than
  50 turkeys in a year;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Manitoba, to any person in any year who, in the year
  immediately preceding that year, marketed less than 100 turkeys raised
  by him, until such time in that year as he does market 100 or more
  turkeys;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Saskatchewan, to a producer who markets less than
  100 turkeys in a year;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Alberta, to a producer who markets less than
  300 turkeys in a year; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} British Columbia, to a producer who markets less than
  25 turkeys in a year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-357, s. 1

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 7)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-559772 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Producer Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#producer-reports .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** Each licensed producer shall provide the Agency with the
  following information respecting the marketing of turkeys in
  interprovincial or export trade:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} names and addresses of all purchasers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number, total weight, category and condition of
    turkeys marketed.

### [Producer-Vendor Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#producer-vendor-reports .SchedHeadL1}

- **2** Each licensed producer-vendor shall provide the Agency with the
  following information respecting the marketing of turkeys in
  interprovincial or export trade:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} names and addresses of all purchasers;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number, total weight, category and condition of
    turkeys marketed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} price and terms of sale of turkeys marketed.

### [Processor Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#processor-reports .SchedHeadL1}

- **3** Each licensed processor shall provide the Agency with the
  following information respecting the marketing of live turkeys in
  interprovincial or export trade:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} names and addresses of all producers of turkeys
    marketed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} total weight and category of turkeys marketed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} price and terms of purchase of turkeys marketed.

### [Dealer Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#dealer-reports .SchedHeadL1}

- **4** No report necessary.

### [Hatcheryman Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#hatcheryman-reports .SchedHeadL1}

- **5** Each licensed hatcheryman shall provide the Agency with the
  following information respecting the marketing of turkeys in
  interprovincial or export trade:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} names and addresses of all purchasers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} number, category and sex (if sexed) of turkey
    poults marketed.
