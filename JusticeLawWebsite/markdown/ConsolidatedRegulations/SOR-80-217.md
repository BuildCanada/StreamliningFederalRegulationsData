## National Parks of Canada Garbage Regulations {#national-parks-of-canada-garbage-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-217
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1980-03-27

National Parks of Canada Garbage Regulations

P.C. 1980-741 1980-03-27
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 7(1) of the
National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the National Parks
Garbage Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1123 and to make the annexed Regulations
respecting garbage in the National Parks of Canada in substitution
therefor.
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2001-449,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868788 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[approved enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an enclosure for
    the storage of containers between collection times that has been
    approved by the superintendent in accordance with section 2.2;
    ([enceinte approuvée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any trade, industry,
    employment or activity carried on for gain or profit in a Park;
    ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the removal of garbage from
    a premises to a disposal location whether such removal is effected
    by the superintendent or a contractor under public or private
    contract, or an owner; ([enlèvement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a type of garbage container
    listed in Schedule I that has been designated by the superintendent
    of a park as being acceptable for use in that park or at specific
    locations in that park; ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[garbage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[garbage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any kind of waste including

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} animal and agricultural wastes, being manures,
      crop residues, animal offal such as carcass waste and entrails and
      other materials obtained from agricultural pursuits, stables,
      kennels, veterinary establishments and other such premises,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bulky wastes, being large items of refuse
      including appliances, furniture, vehicle parts under 35 kg, large
      containers and tree cuttings not exceeding 1 m in length or 10 cm
      in diameter, in bundles not exceeding 35 kg,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} construction and demolition wastes, being waste
      building materials and rubble resulting from construction, repair,
      remodelling or demolition activities,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} liquid waste, being waste materials or substances
      that have sufficient moisture or other liquid contents to be free
      flowing but that are not suitable for disposal through a sewer
      system,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} rubbish, being

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} combustibles, consisting of burnable materials
        such as paper, rags, cartons, boxes, wood excelsior, bedding,
        rubber, leather and plastics, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} non-combustibles, consisting of materials that
        are not burnable such as metal objects and containers, ceramics,
        metal foils and glass,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} solid wastes, being the useless, unwanted or
      discarded solid waste materials resulting from normal human
      activities including semi-liquid or wet wastes with insufficient
      liquid content to be free flowing,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} special wastes, being

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} hazardous wastes, consisting of any waste that
        may present a hazard to persons, flora, fauna or public lands,
        including wastes of a pathological, an explosive, a highly
        flammable, a radioactive or a toxic nature,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sanitary wastes, consisting of any putrescible
        waste that is capable of producing conditions that may present a
        hazard to health,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} natural waste, consisting of tree stumps,
        soil, sand and stone, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} other special wastes, consisting of materials
        so classified by the superintendent,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} trade waste, being petroleum products, scrap
      metal, machinery and vehicles and parts thereof, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} yard rubbish, being prunings, grass clippings,
      weeds, leaves, and general garden wastes, other than solid wastes;
      ([ordures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lot volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lot volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a lot, means
    the result obtained by multiplying the aggregate of the volume
    values for the units on the lot by the number of months of occupancy
    permitted under the lease, permit or licence of occupation for that
    lot divided by 12; ([valeur volumétrique du lot]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, syndicate,
    association, corporation, company, club or organization in charge
    of, or responsible for, a residence or for the operation of any
    accommodation, activity, business, entertainment, church, hospital,
    school, university or other facility within a park, and includes an
    employee, manager, lessee, transferee or partner of such an
    individual, syndicate, association, corporation, company, club or
    organization; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/96-170, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[season]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a park in which
    garbage is not collected throughout the year, the period in the year
    during which garbage is collected; ([saison]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[street]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[street]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that portion of any
    throughfare, road, lane, avenue, parkway, viaduct, alley, square,
    bridge, causeway, trestleway or other place, whether publicly or
    privately owned, that the public is ordinarily entitled or permitted
    to use for the passage or parking of motor vehicles but does not
    include sidewalks, ditches or rights-of-way adjacent to a roadway;
    ([rue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/92-440, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[total operating and maintenance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total operating and maintenance costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the annual operating and maintenance costs incurred in respect of a
    park to collect and dispose of garbage generated by owners of lots
    in the park; ([coût total de fonctionnement et
    d'entretien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[total volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[total volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a park or
    service area, means the aggregate of the lot volume values for all
    lots in that park or service area that receive garbage collection
    and disposal services; ([valeur volumétrique
    totale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a building, or a portion of a
    building, in a park that is designated as a unit by a council or
    committee of residents of the park and representatives of Parks
    Canada; ([unité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unit of garbage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit of garbage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a service
    area, means the amount of garbage held by one self-dumping garbage
    container of the type used in that service area; ([unité
    d'ordures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[volume value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the numerical value
    assigned to a unit in a park, determined by comparing the estimated
    volume of garbage that the unit will generate annually to that
    generated by a building that has been established as the benchmark
    of one volume value for that park; ([valeur
    volumétrique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period commencing on the
    first day of April in any year and ending on the thirty-first day of
    March in the year next following. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-237, s. 1
- SOR/92-440, ss. 1, 5
- SOR/96-170, s. 1
- SOR/2001-449, ss. 3, 12(F)
:::

  they were parks.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in the Town of Banff
  or in the Town of Jasper.

## [Approval of Enclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868830 .Part}

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent
shall approve an enclosure for the storage of containers if the
enclosure is designed and constructed to prevent access to the garbage
in the containers by domestic animals and wildlife.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-449, s. 4
:::

## [Collection of Garbage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868834 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
provide for the collection of garbage by a garbage collector in such
portions of a park and at such times as he deems advisable.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, s. 12(F)
:::

## [Duties of an Owner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868838 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every owner shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide and maintain in a serviceable and sanitary
  condition a sufficient number of containers to hold all garbage
  accumulated on his premises at any time;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraphs (h), (i) and (k), ensure that
  all garbage is placed, and remains, in containers that are of a type
  set out in Column I of an item of Schedule I and that meet the
  specifications set out in Column II of that item;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} drain and wrap all wet garbage before placing it in a
  container;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that containers are set out for collection at
  such places and such times and in such manner as the superintendent
  may designate;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} remove all containers from their designated
  collection location on any street within such period as may be
  specified by the superintendent;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all rigid metal containers and disposable
  plastic bag type containers are securely closed when set out for
  collection;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} between collection times, store garbage

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in Banff National Park of Canada, Glacier National
    Park of Canada, Jasper National Park of Canada, Kootenay National
    Park of Canada, Mount Revelstoke National Park of Canada and Yoho
    National Park of Canada, in self-dumping garbage containers that
    meet the specifications set out in column II of item 4 of Schedule
    I, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other park, in an approved enclosure or in
    steel refuse containers that meet the specifications set out in
    column II of item 2 of Schedule I;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} securely tie in bundles any combustible rubbish set
  out for collection in accordance with any instructions of the
  superintendent;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} prepare yard rubbish for collection in accordance
  with any instructions of the superintendent;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} extinguish all ashes or coals before setting them out
  for collection;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} ensure that bulky wastes are not set out for
  collection except at such times as may be determined by the
  superintendent;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} ensure that liquid wastes, trade wastes, special
  wastes, animal and agricultural wastes, and construction and
  demolition wastes are not set out for collection by the
  superintendent; and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} keep the street, sidewalk and boulevard abutting, in
  front of, flanking and at the rear of the owner's premises, free of
  garbage.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, ss. 5, 12(F)
:::

## [Solid Wastes or Liquid Wastes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868857 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may post a sign at the entrance to a garbage disposal
  area or facility requiring a permit to deliver any solid or liquid
  wastes to that disposal area or facility.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent has posted a sign at the
  entrance to a garbage disposal area or facility pursuant to subsection
  (1), no person shall deliver solid or liquid wastes to that disposal
  area or facility without first obtaining a permit from the
  superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person in possession of a permit issued
  pursuant to subsection (2) shall show such permit at the request of
  the superintendent or the person in charge of the disposal area or
  facility.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, s. 12(F)
:::

## [Escape of Garbage from Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868864 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall convey or cause to be conveyed any garbage in a vehicle
  that is not properly constructed and covered so as to prevent the
  contents thereof from escaping.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an escape of garbage from any vehicle
  the owner of the vehicle shall be responsible for the immediate
  clean-up.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868868 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall deposit any yard rubbish on a
  sidewalk or a street.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent has made an arrangement for
  the collection of yard rubbish on a sidewalk or a street on a day
  specified by the superintendent, a person may deposit yard rubbish on
  that sidewalk or street on that day if the yard rubbish does not
  impede pedestrian or vehicular traffic.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-377, s. 1
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, s. 12(F)
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall discard or
dispose of or deposit garbage anywhere in a park except in such places
and at such times and under such conditions as the superintendent may
authorize.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, s. 12(F)
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no owner shall accumulate or allow to accumulate
  any garbage on his premises.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, on application to the superintendent, an owner
  has been issued a permit to accumulate trade waste, he may, in
  accordance with the permit, accumulate trade waste on his premises for
  the purposes of sale or recycling.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The permit referred to in subsection (2) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature and type of trade waste that may be
    accumulated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location and manner in which the trade waste is
    to be accumulated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which trade waste is to be removed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No permit to accumulate trade waste shall be issued
  where the superintendent has reasonable grounds to believe that the
  accumulation of trade waste could adversely affect

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the appearance, natural resources or amenity of the
    park or of any community within the park; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the enjoyment, safety or comfort of residents or
    visitors to the park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-377, s. 2
- SOR/92-440, s. 5
- SOR/2001-449, s. 12(F)
:::

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868889 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of a lot of a
type set out in column I of an item of Schedule II for which garbage
collection and disposal services are provided shall pay the charge set
out in column II of that item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-440, s. 2
- SOR/95-526, s. 1
- SOR/96-170, s. 2
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of an
establishment, in a park referred to in Schedule III, of a class set out
in column I of an item of that Schedule to whom garbage collection and
disposal services are provided or who delivers or disposes of solid or
liquid wastes in a park garbage disposal area or facility shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the service is provided or the disposal is made
  throughout the year, pay the charge set out in column II of that item;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the service is provided or the disposal is made
  in certain seasons only, pay the charge set out in column III of that
  item.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-170, s. 2
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  owner of a lot located in Banff National Park of Canada, Yoho National
  Park of Canada or Waterton Lakes National Park of Canada shall pay the
  garbage collection and disposal charge determined by the formula

  ::: {#868900 .Subsection}
  A × B/12 × C/D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the volume values of the units on the lot,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of months in the year during which the owner is
      permitted to occupy the lot under the lease, permit or licence of
      occupation for that lot,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total operating and maintenance cost incurred in respect of
      the park, and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume value for the park.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each park referred to in subsection (1), the
  total cost incurred to collect and dispose of garbage generated by
  owners of lots in the park, the lot volume value for each lot in the
  park and the total volume value for the park shall be calculated on
  March 31, 1996 and on that date in every subsequent year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-170, s. 2
- SOR/2001-449, s. 6
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), every
  owner of a lot in Prince Albert National Park of Canada, other than
  owners of lots in the central business district service area, shall
  pay the garbage collection and disposal charge determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#868911 .Subsection}
  (A × B × C) / D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of units of garbage generated annually by the
      service area in which the lot is located,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the cost to collect and dispose of one unit of garbage in that
      service area,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of units on the lot, and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of units in the service area.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cost, per unit of garbage, to provide garbage
  collection and disposal services in Prince Albert National Park and
  the total number of units in each service area in the park shall be
  calculated on March 31, 1996 and on that date in every subsequent
  year.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), every owner of a lot in
  the central business district service area in Prince Albert National
  Park shall pay the garbage collection and disposal charge determined
  by the formula

  ::: {#868919 .Subsection}
  A × B × C/12 × D/E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of units of garbage generated annually by that
      service area,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the cost to collect and dispose of one unit of garbage in that
      service area,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of months of the year during which the owner is
      permitted to occupy the lot under the lease, permit or licence of
      occupation for that lot,

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the volume values of the units on that lot,
      and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume value for that service area.
  :::

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The cost, per unit of garbage, to provide garbage
  collection and disposal services in Prince Albert National Park, the
  lot volume value for each lot in the park and the total volume value
  for the central business district service area shall be calculated on
  March 31, 1996 and on that date in every subsequent year.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/99-297, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-170, s. 2
- SOR/99-297, s. 1
- SOR/2001-449, s. 11
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 12(1), the superintendent of each park
  referred to in that subsection shall, on March 31 of each year,
  provide each owner of a lot in that park with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lot volume value for the lot;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the volume value for each unit on the lot;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total operating and maintenance cost incurred
    in respect of the park; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total volume value for the park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 13(1), the
  superintendent of Prince Albert National Park of Canada shall, on
  March 31 of each year, provide each owner of a lot in that park with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of units of garbage generated
    annually by the service area in which the lot is located;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost to collect and dispose of one unit of
    garbage in that service area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of units on the lot; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of units in that service area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 13(3), the
  superintendent of Prince Albert National Park of Canada shall, on
  March 31 of each year, provide each owner of a lot in the central
  business district service area with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of units of garbage generated
    annually by that service area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost to collect and dispose of one unit of
    garbage in that service area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lot volume value for the lot;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the volume value for each unit on the lot; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total volume value of that service area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-170, s. 2
- SOR/2001-449, s. 11
:::
