## Canadian Retailers Duty Remission Order, 1993 {#canadian-retailers-duty-remission-order-1993 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-430
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1993-08-26

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on Certain Products

P.C. 1993-1678  1993-08-26
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-430_e_hq_6261){#fn_SOR-93-430_e_hq_6261-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
customs duties on certain products](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-430).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954671 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Retailers Duty Remission Order,
1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-430).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954673 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the following
goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Golf bags of tariff item No. 4202.91.10, 4202.92.11
  or 4202.92.91;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Downhill ski-boots of tariff item No. 6401.92.91,
  6401.92.92, 6402.11.10 or 6403.11.10;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Portable butane hair curlers of tariff item No.
  8205.51.00;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Electronic calculating machines of tariff item No.
  8470.10.00, 8470.21.00 or 8470.29.00 and parts thereof of tariff item
  No. 8473.21.00;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Food mixers or juice extractors of tariff item No.
  8509.40.90 and parts thereof of tariff item No. 8509.90.31 or
  8509.90.39;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Can openers of tariff item No. 8509.80.00 and parts
  thereof of tariff item No. 8509.90.31 or 8509.90.39;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Bread makers and fabric steamers of tariff item No.
  8516.79.90 and parts thereof of tariff item No. 8516.90.69;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} Microphones of tariff item No. 8518.10.00;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} Headphones, including earphones, of tariff item No.
  8518.30.90 and parts thereof of tariff item No. 8518.90.40;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} Cassette-players of tariff item No. 8519.91.00 and
  parts thereof of tariff item No. 8522.90.34 or 8522.90.94;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} Tape recorders of tariff item No. 8520.39.90 and
  parts thereof of tariff item No. 8522.90.34 or 8522.90.94;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} Magnetic tape-type video recording or reproducing
  apparatus of tariff item No. 8521.10.90 and parts thereof of tariff
  item No. 8522.90.34 or 8522.90.94;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} Laser video disc players of tariff item No.
  8521.90.00 and parts thereof of tariff item No. 8522.90.35 or
  8522.90.95;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} Pick-up cartridges of tariff item No. 8522.10.00;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} Styli (needles for turntables (record-decks) or
  record-players) of tariff item No. 8522.90.10;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} Audio jack boxes of tariff item No. 8522.90.94;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} Radio remote control apparatus, for use on amateur
  bands for hobby purposes, of tariff item No. 8526.92.90 and parts
  thereof of tariff item No. 8529.90.12 or 8529.90.52, remote control
  apparatus for domestic audio/video equipment and parts thereof of
  tariff item No. 8522.90.94, 8522.90.95, 8529.90.51, 8529.90.52,
  8529.90.53, 8529.90.54, 8543.80.50, 8543.90.15 or 8543.90.95, and
  aerials for use therewith of tariff item No. 8529.10.90;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} Telescopes, not including telescopic sights for

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} Cameras of tariff item No. 9006.59.20 and parts and

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954703 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
under section 2 on the condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported during the period commencing
  on the coming into force of this Order and ending on December 31,
  1997; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date of importation of the
  goods for which remission is claimed.

- SOR/97-26, s. 9(E)
