## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721271 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations
[[substantive level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of an employee,
means the occupational group and level:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at which the employee is being paid; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the employee has received an acting appointment,
  at which the employee was being paid immediately before receiving the
  appointment.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721275 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of organizations named in Schedule I or IV to the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721277 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 51(5) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), [[promotion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  assignment to an employee of the duties of a position for which the
  maximum rate of pay is more than the maximum rate applicable to the
  employee's substantive level immediately before the assignment of the
  duties, by an amount equal to or greater than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the smallest increment on the pay scale for the new
    position, if it has more than one rate of pay; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 4% of the maximum rate of pay for the previous
    position, if the new position has only one rate of pay.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An assignment described in subsection (1) does not
  include the assignment of an employee to the duties of another
  position in the same occupational group and subgroup and at the same
  or a lower level.
