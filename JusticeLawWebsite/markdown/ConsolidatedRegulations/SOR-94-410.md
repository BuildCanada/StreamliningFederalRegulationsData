## Export Development Canada Exercise of Certain Powers Regulations {#export-development-canada-exercise-of-certain-powers-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-410
:::

[EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT](/eng/acts/E-20)

Registration 1994-06-02

Regulations Governing the Disposal by Lease of Certain Property, the
Entering Into of Certain Arrangements and the Acquisition of Certain
Interests by Export Development Canada

P.C. 1994-935 1994-06-02
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 10(8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EFBBA} of the [Export
Development Act](/eng/acts/E-20), a copy of proposed regulations,
substantially in the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on November 6, 1993 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations with respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1-ID0EFBBA){#footnote1}S.C. 1993, c.
  26, s. 4(2)
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister for International Trade and the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 10(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EFAAA} of the [Export
Development Act](/eng/acts/E-20), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Regulations governing the disposal by lease of certain property, the
entering into of certain arrangements and the acquisition of certain
interests by Export Development Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-410).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963941 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export Development
    Act](/eng/acts/E-20); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[domestic financial transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic financial transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    arrangement entered into by the Corporation whereby the Corporation
    provides credit to a person with respect to a transaction that does
    not relate, directly or indirectly, to the carrying on of business
    or other activities outside Canada, but does not include an
    arrangement that has as its purpose the management of the assets and
    liabilities of the Corporation; ([opération de financement sur le
    marché national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[domestic insurance transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic insurance transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    arrangement entered into by the Corporation whereby the Corporation
    provides any insurance, reinsurance, indemnity or guarantee with
    respect to a transaction entered into by another person and that
    does not relate, directly or indirectly, to the carrying on of
    business or other activities outside Canada; ([opération d'assurance
    sur le marché national]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equity interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equity interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an
    entity, an ownership interest in an entity; ([titres de
    participation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[estimated residual value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[estimated residual value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect
    of property to be leased, the estimated value of the leased property
    at the end of the term of the lease, less any amount that the
    Corporation is entitled to receive under any agreement that is in
    effect on the day the lease is entered into and that relates to the
    disposal by the Corporation of its interest in the property, as
    determined by the Corporation at the time the lease is entered into
    by the Corporation; ([valeur résiduelle estimative]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[export transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transaction
    involving:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the export of goods out of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture, sale, leasing, treatment or
      servicing of goods that are to be used by a person in carrying on
      business or other activities outside Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sale or licensing of any right in a patent,
      trademark or copyright to a person that are to be used by a person
      in carrying on business or other activities outside Canada, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the rendering of any service to a person in
      connection with the carrying on of business or other activities
      outside Canada by a person. ([opération
      d'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign project equity interest acquisition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign project equity interest
    acquisition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2008-46, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Circumstances Under Which the Corporation May Exercise Certain Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963956 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Corporation may dispose by lease of property
  acquired by the Corporation for the purpose of such disposal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A disposal by lease of property of the Corporation
  requires the approval of the Minister and the Minister of Finance,
  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property is not to be used outside Canada
    during the term of the lease;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lease is not entered into for the primary
    purpose of extending credit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the lease provides that the Corporation will be
    responsible for installing, promoting, servicing, cleaning,
    maintaining and repairing the property during the term of the lease;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation does not intend to dispose of its
    interest in the property during or at the end of the term of the
    lease;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the capital cost of the property and the financing
    charges related to the capital cost of the property will not be
    recovered by the Corporation; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} no contractual arrangement exists for the final
    disposal of the Corporation's interest in the property to be leased
    and the estimated residual value of the property to be leased,
    together with the estimated residual value of all other property
    that the Corporation has acquired an interest in for the purpose of
    disposal by lease in respect of which no contractual arrangement
    exists for the final disposal of the corporation's interest in such
    property, would be more than 20 per cent of the aggregate of the
    cost of acquisition of all such properties.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Corporation may acquire an equity interest in
  an entity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (5), an acquisition by
  the Corporation requires the approval of the Minister and the Minister
  of Finance, if the value of the equity interest to be acquired by the
  Corporation together with the value of any equity interest already
  held by the Corporation in that entity exceeds 25 per cent of the
  value of all equity interests in the entity at the time of acquisition
  by the Corporation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the value of all
  equity interests in the entity at the time of acquisition by the
  Corporation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is to be calculated on a fully diluted basis and
    shall include the equity interest being acquired by the Corporation
    or by any other person and any equity interest that the Corporation
    or any other person is obligated to acquire; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is to be determined in accordance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the most recent audited financial statements of
      the entity, if available,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any agreements between the entity and the
      Corporation that relate to the acquisition, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any agreements among persons who have acquired,
      are acquiring or are obligated to acquire an equity interest in
      the entity and any agreements between any of them and the entity
      that relate to the acquisition of their respective equity
      interests in the entity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to an acquisition
  resulting from a restructuring, reorganization or other similar
  workout arrangement involving an entity in which the Corporation
  already holds an equity interest or to which it is a creditor.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply where the equity
  interest is acquired by reason of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dividend or other in-kind distribution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exchange or substitution, as a result of an
    amalgamation, merger or other similar action, of an equity interest
    already held by the Corporation for an equity interest in an entity
    that is the result of the action; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the settlement, in whole or in part,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of a debt held by the Corporation in connection
      with any arrangement that has the effect of extending credit or
      providing an undertaking to pay money, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a debt or claim in respect of which the
      Corporation has made a payment pursuant to an arrangement that has
      the effect of providing any insurance, reinsurance, indemnity or
      guarantee.

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** The Corporation must
ensure that domestic financial transactions and domestic insurance
transactions complement the products and services available from
commercial financial institutions, commercial insurance providers and
the Business Development Bank of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-227, s. 1
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Corporation may enter into a domestic financial
  transaction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A domestic financial transaction to be entered into
  by the Corporation requires the approval of the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance if it is entered into with or in respect of a
  person whose annual export transaction and foreign market business
  volume, at the time of the person's request for credit to the
  Corporation, is less than 50 per cent of that person's total annual
  business volume.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the approval of the Minister
  and the Minister of Finance is not required if, within 48 months
  before the person's request for credit, the Ministers approved a
  domestic financial transaction entered into by the Corporation with or
  in respect of that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-227, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the Corporation may enter into a domestic
  insurance transaction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A domestic insurance transaction to be entered into
  by the Corporation requires the approval of the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance if it is entered into with or in respect of a
  person whose annual export transaction and foreign market business
  volume, at the time of the person's request for insurance,
  reinsurance, indemnity or guarantee to the Corporation, is less than
  50 per cent of that person's total annual business volume.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the approval of the
  Minister and the Minister of Finance is not required if, within 48
  months before the person's request for insurance, reinsurance,
  indemnity or guarantee, the Ministers approved a domestic insurance
  transaction entered into by the Corporation with or in respect of that
  person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A domestic insurance transaction to be entered into
  by the Corporation requires the approval of the Minister and the
  Minister of Finance where the domestic insurance transaction is an
  insurance arrangement that is of a class defined in the schedule to
  the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) and that is set out in
  column II of an item of the schedule to these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-227, s. 3

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2009, c. 2, ss. 263(4), (5)

  - ###### Application suspended {#application-suspended .MarginalNote}

    - [263]{.lawlabel} [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 5(2) and 6(2)
      and (3) of the [Export Development Canada Exercise of Certain
      Powers Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-410) do not apply for
      the period beginning on the day on which paragraph 10(1)(a) of the
      Act, as enacted by subsection 260(1), comes into force and ending
      on the day on which that paragraph is repealed.

    - ###### Transactions entered into before repeal {#transactions-entered-into-before-repeal .MarginalNote}

      [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), subsections 5(2) and 6(2)
      and (3) of the [Export Development Canada Exercise of Certain
      Powers Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-410) do not apply to a
      new transaction that Export Development Canada enters into during
      the period that paragraph 10(1)(a) of the Act, as enacted by
      subsection 260(1), is in force, even after that paragraph is
      repealed. Despite the repeal, Export Development Canada may take
      any steps and do anything that it considers necessary or desirable
      to implement the transaction or that it considers related to the
      transaction.
