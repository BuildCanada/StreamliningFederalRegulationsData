## Exemption Regulations (Consumer Products) {#exemption-regulations-consumer-products .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-97
:::

[CANADA CONSUMER PRODUCT SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/C-1.68)

Registration 2015-05-01

Exemption Regulations (Consumer Products)

P.C. 2015-470 2015-04-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 38 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2015-97_e_hq_15709){#fn_SOR-2015-97_e_hq_15709-ID0EDACA},
the Minister of Health caused the proposed [Exemption Regulations
(Consumer Products)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-97), substantially in the
annexed form, to be laid before the House of Commons on March 31, 2014
and the Senate on April 1, 2014 and no appropriate committee of either
House of Parliament made a report with respect to the proposed
Regulations;

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 38(4)(b) of the Act, 90 calendar days
have passed since the proposed Regulations were laid before each House
of Parliament;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection 37(1)
of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2015-97_e_hq_15709){#fn_SOR-2015-97_e_hq_15709-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Exemption Regulations (Consumer
Products)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-97).

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826503 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[donation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of a consumer product
made without consideration.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826505 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  retailer is exempt from the application of subparagraph 13(1)(a)(i) of
  the [Canada Consumer Product Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) in respect
  of a consumer product that they obtained by donation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the consumer product
  was donated by a person who, in the course of business, is or was
  engaged in manufacturing, importing or selling such consumer products.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826509 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
