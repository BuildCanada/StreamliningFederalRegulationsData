## Commercial Loan (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#commercial-loan-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-356
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-06-04

Regulations Defining Certain Expressions and Prescribing Certain
International Agencies in Respect of Commercial Loans

P.C. 1992-1185  1992-06-04
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to the definition [[commercial
loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 386(1) and sections 398 to 400
and 463 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations defining certain expressions and
prescribing certain international agencies in respect of commercial
loans](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-356).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945262 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Commercial Loan (Cooperative Credit Associations)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-356).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945264 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). (*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Subsidiaries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945266 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of sections
398 and 399 of the Act, all of the subsidiaries of the association,
other than those that are financial institutions, are prescribed
subsidiaries.

## [Definition of Widely Distributed]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945268 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of the definition [[commercial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
  subsection 386(1) of the Act, [[widely
  distributed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of debt obligations the distribution of
    which is exempt from the requirement to file a prospectus under the
    laws of Canada, a province or a jurisdiction outside Canada, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at least 90% of the maximum authorized principal
      of the debt obligations is held by one or more persons other than
      the association and its subsidiaries, and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the debt obligations were issued to at least 25
        persons other than the association and its subsidiaries within
        six months after the day on which the first of the debt
        obligations was issued, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligations are issued on a
        continuous basis, there are on average at least 25 holders other
        than the association and its subsidiaries, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of their initial distribution, the
      debt obligations met at least three of the following criteria:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their initial term was less than one year,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} they were rated by a rating agency,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed through a person
        authorized to trade in securities, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} they were distributed in accordance with an
        offering circular or memorandum, or a similar document relating
        to the distribution of securities; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other securities, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they are listed and posted for trading on a
      recognized stock exchange, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a prospectus relating to the issuance of the
      securities was filed under the laws of Canada, a province or a
      jurisdiction outside Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subparagraph (1)(a)(i), [[maximum
  authorized principal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to any
  debt obligation, the maximum amount of debt that may be issued under
  the terms of the debt obligation or any agreement relating to it.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-367, s. 1
:::

## [International Agencies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945286 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[commercial loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 386(1)
of the Act, the prescribed international agencies are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the International Bank for Reconstruction and
  Development;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the International Finance Corporation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Inter-American Development Bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Asian Development Bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the African Development Bank;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the European Investment Bank;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Caribbean Development Bank;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Nordic Investment Bank;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development;
  and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the Council of Europe Development Bank.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-367, s. 2
:::

## [Prescribed Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945300 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 398 and 399 of the Act, [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
in respect of an association, at a particular time, means the amount
determined by the formula

::: {#945302 .Section}
A - B

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the assets that would be reported on the balance
    sheet of the association prepared as at that time in accordance with
    the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
    referred to in subsection 292(4) of the Act; and

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the total of the assets included in the value of A that are
    assets of a subsidiary of the association that is a financial
    institution or that are assets of a subsidiary of a financial
    institution, other than where the subsidiary is a subsidiary of a
    financial institution only because it is a subsidiary of the
    association.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-63, s. 1
- SOR/2001-367, s. 3
:::
