## Miawpukek Band Order {#miawpukek-band-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-533
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 1989-11-02

Order Declaring a Body of Indians at Conne River, Newfoundland, to be a
Band for the Purposes of the Indian Act

P.C. 1989-2206  1989-11-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 73(3) and paragraph (c) of the definition
[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
Act](/eng/acts/I-5), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1984-2273, made on
  June 28, 1984[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBABA}; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make the annexed Order declaring a body of Indians
  at Conne River, Newfoundland, to be a band for the purposes of the
  [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), in substitution therefor.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBABA){#footnote1_e}SOR/84-501,
    1984 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2935
    :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920657 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Miawpukek Band Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-533).

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920659 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The body of Indians
described in section 3, and sometimes known as the Conne River Indian
Band, is a band for the purposes of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) and
is to be known as "Miawpukek Mi'Kamawey Mawio'mi" or, in its abbreviated
form, the "Miawpukek Band".

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Miawpukek Band is that body of Indians comprising every person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is of Canadian Indian ancestry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is not excluded by the operation of section 7
    of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) from entitlement to registration
    as an Indian; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was ordinarily resident in the community of Conne
      River, Newfoundland, on June 28, 1984,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is the child or sibling of a person referred to
      in subparagraph (i), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is the child of a sibling of a person referred
      to in subparagraph (i).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (1)(c)(i) and
  without restricting the generality thereof, a person shall be
  considered to have been ordinarily resident in the community of Conne
  River, Newfoundland, on June 28, 1984, if the person was absent from
  the community

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on June 28, 1984 for reasons of health or
    education, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period not exceeding 12 months during the
    period beginning on June 28, 1983 and ending on June 28, 1984

  and would, but for that absence, have been ordinarily resident in that
  community.
