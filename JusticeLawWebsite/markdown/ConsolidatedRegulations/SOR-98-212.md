## Shooting Clubs and Shooting Ranges Regulations {#shooting-clubs-and-shooting-ranges-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-212
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Shooting Clubs and Shooting Ranges Regulations

P.C. 1998-487 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Shooting Clubs and Shooting
Ranges Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-212), substantially in the
annexed form, laid before each House of Parliament on October 30, 1997,
which date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 29 and
paragraphs 117(d) and (e) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^,
hereby makes the annexed [Shooting Clubs and Shooting Ranges
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-212).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020269 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approved]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means approved under section 29 of
    the Act. ([agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person approved under
    subsection 29(1) of the Act to operate either a shooting club or a
    shooting range. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handgun under
    paragraph (a) of the definition "prohibited firearm" in subsection
    84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).([arme de poing
    prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in the [Public Agents Firearms
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203). ([agence de services
    publics]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[range officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[range officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who
    oversees the shooting activities at the firing line of a shooting
    range. ([officiel de tir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shooting club]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shooting club]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a non-profit
    organization whose activities include target practice or target
    shooting competitions using restricted firearms or prohibited
    handguns at an identified approved shooting range. ([club de
    tir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shooting range]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shooting range]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place that is
    designed or intended for the safe discharge, on a regular and
    structured basis, of firearms for the purpose of target practice or
    target shooting competitions. ([champ de tir]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-268, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020281 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shooting clubs; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections (2) and (3), shooting
    ranges.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A shooting range that is part of the premises of a
  licensed business is exempt from the application of these Regulations
  on the condition that it be used only by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owners, partners, directors and officers of the
    business who hold a licence authorizing the possession of the
    firearms that they use on the range; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employees of the business who hold a licence
    authorizing the possession of the firearms that they use on the
    range or, in the case of prohibited firearms, authorizing the
    acquisition of restricted firearms.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A shooting range that is used only by public officers
  within the meaning of subsection 117.07(2) of the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46) is exempt from the application of these
  Regulations on condition that each public officer uses the shooting
  range only in connection with his or her lawful duties or employment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-268, s. 2
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020292 .Part}

### [Request for Approval of Shooting Range]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020293 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who wishes to establish and operate a shooting range shall submit a
  request for approval to the provincial minister and provide the
  following information in the request:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, phone number and, if
    applicable, facsimile number and electronic mail address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of the shooting range, including road
    directions to reach it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the proposed hours of operation of the shooting
    range; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to each operator, each owner of the
    shooting range, and each employee of the shooting range who handles
    firearms

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his or her name, address and phone number, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of his or her licence to possess
      firearms or, if one does not exist, his or her date of birth.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for approval of a shooting range must be
  accompanied by the following documentation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a survey report, location certificate or other
    similar documents that show the geographical location and layout of
    the shooting range and the portion of the surrounding area that
    could be affected by shooting on the shooting range, as well as the
    land use of that portion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the proposed safety rules;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), evidence of at least
    \$2,000,000 of commercial general liability insurance with coverage
    on an occurrence basis;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} evidence of compliance with applicable zoning laws;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} copies of, and evidence of compliance with, any
    operating licences required by federal, provincial or municipal
    laws;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the design and operation of the
    shooting range meets at least the requirements set out in section 5;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the shooting range complies with any
    federal, provincial or municipal legislation that applies to the
    establishment and operation of such a facility in regard to
    environmental protection.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a shooting range operated by a public
  service agency, the agency shall provide evidence of liability
  indemnification with respect to the shooting range, of at least
  \$2,000,000, from a federal, provincial or municipal government.

### [Request for Approval of Shooting Club]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020311 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who wishes to establish and operate a shooting club shall submit a
  request for approval to the provincial minister and provide the
  following information in the request:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address, phone number and, if
    applicable, facsimile number and electronic mail address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to each member or officer of the club

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his or her name, address and phone number, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of his or her licence to possess
      firearms or, if one does not exist, his or her date of birth; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each approved shooting
    range that the shooting club uses for target practice or target
    shooting competitions.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for an approval of a shooting club must
  be accompanied by the following documentation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the articles of incorporation of the shooting club
    or other documentation sufficient to demonstrate that it is a
    non-profit organization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence of at least \$2,000,000 of commercial
    general liability insurance with coverage on an occurrence basis;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence confirming that the shooting club has
    permission to use at least one shooting range referred to in
    paragraph (1)(c) or a shooting range maintained by the Minister of
    National Defence under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

## [Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020323 .Part}

### [Compliance with Safety Standards and Other Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020324 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The operator of an approved
shooting range shall ensure that the discharge of firearms on the
shooting range does not endanger the safety of persons at the shooting
range or in the portion of the surrounding area described in paragraph
3(2)(a), by taking appropriate measures, including ensuring that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the design and operation of the shooting range

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is such that projectiles discharged from firearms
    will not leave the shooting range if they are discharged there in
    accordance with the safety rules, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} promotes the safety of all persons on the shooting
    range, including by accommodating any adaptation that may be
    appropriate given the nature of the shooting activities that may
    take place and the type and calibre of firearms that may be used
    there;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the shooting range has an adequate warning system to
  warn persons that they are entering a shooting range and to inform
  them, when such is the case, that shooting activities are taking place
  at that time;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appropriate safety rules for the shooting range are
  applied that are consistent with the nature of the shooting activities
  that may take place and the type and calibre of firearms that may be
  used there;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the safety rules are posted in a conspicuous place on
  the shooting range; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if more than one person is simultaneously engaged in
  shooting activities on the shooting range, a person acts as the range
  officer.

### [Requirements for Users]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020333 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person may use a
restricted firearm or prohibited handgun at a shooting range unless the
person is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member or an officer of an approved shooting club;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a guest of a person referred to in paragraph (a); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an individual who ordinarily resides outside of
  Canada who is either a member of a recognized shooting organization or
  a guest described in paragraph (b).

### [Safety Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020338 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a shooting range shall ensure that every person who
  indicates an intention to use the shooting range for the first time is
  informed of the safety rules used at that shooting range.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person may use a shooting range for the first time
  without having received the information referred to in subsection (1).

## [Continuing Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020342 .Part}

### [Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020343 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a shooting
club or a shooting range shall at all times maintain the insurance
coverage referred to in paragraphs 3(2)(c) and 4(2)(b).

### [Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020345 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every five years after the
date on which the approval of a shooting range was granted, the operator
shall submit current copies of the documents set out in paragraphs
3(2)(a) to (c), as well as evidence of continuing compliance with the
requirements referred to in paragraphs 3(2)(d) to (g) and sections 5 and
8, to the chief firearms officer.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief firearms officer may request an operator of a shooting range to
  provide evidence as described in section 9 no more than once in a
  calendar year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the chief firearms officer
  may make a request more frequently if he or she

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has received, in the preceding 12 months

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a personal injury report in accordance with
      section 11, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a change report in accordance with section 12;
      or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has reasonable grounds to believe that the
    continued operation of the shooting range may endanger the safety of
    any person.

## [Reports and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020354 .Part}

### [Personal Injury Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020355 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of an approved shooting club or shooting range shall report
  any personal injury occurring on a shooting range that is caused by
  the discharge of a firearm

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible to the local police; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the personal injury, to the
    chief firearms officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) must include
  the following information relating to the personal injury:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its date, time and location;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the individuals involved;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of any range officer who was on duty at
    the time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether or not medical attention was sought; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the incident, including
    the circumstances in which the personal injury occurred, if known.

### [Change Report]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020366 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The operator of an
approved shooting range who proposes to make a change that affects the
matters set out in the documentation submitted under subsection 3(2)
shall give advance notice of the proposed change to the chief firearms
officer within sufficient time, given the nature and complexity of the
proposed change, to allow the chief firearms officer to evaluate it.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The operator of an
approved shooting range shall report immediately to the chief firearms
officer any change, other than one referred to in section 12,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the shooting range or the portion of the
  surrounding area described in paragraph 3(2)(a) that could endanger
  the safety of any person; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in operating permits or licences issued under
  provincial or municipal laws that may be relevant to the approval of
  the shooting range or its activities.

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020371 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of an approved shooting club shall keep records, with respect
  to the following users of restricted firearms or prohibited firearms,
  that include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a member or officer of the club

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} his or her name, address and phone number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} his or her membership card number, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of his or her licence to possess
      firearms or, if one does not exist, his or her date of birth; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a guest of a member or officer of
    the club

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information required in subparagraph (a)(i),
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of his or her licence to possess
      firearms, if one exists.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every record made under subsection (1) must be
  maintained for at least six years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} At the chief firearms officer's request, the operator
  of the approved shooting club shall submit to the chief firearms
  officer a report containing all or any requested part of the
  information described in subsection (1).

### [Participation of Officers, Members and their Guests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020383 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of an approved shooting club shall, on the request of a chief
  firearms officer, supply a written description of the participation,
  if any, of a current or past member or officer of the shooting club or
  his or her guest, in target practice or target shooting competitions
  within the previous five years, where subsection 67(2) of the Act
  applies to the member, officer or guest.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of an approved shooting club shall, on
  the request of a current or past member or officer of the shooting
  club or his or her guest, supply to the requestor the description
  referred to in subsection (1) that concerns the requestor.

## [Notice of Refusal or Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020387 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice of a decision by a provincial minister to refuse to approve a
  shooting club or shooting range or to revoke an approval pursuant to
  subsection 29(3) of the Act is sufficiently given if the notice is
  addressed to the applicant for or operator of a shooting club or
  shooting range at the address of that person that is set out in the
  request for approval, or, if the person has advised the provincial
  minister of a change of address, at the new address, and the notice is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivered personally

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant or operator is an individual, at
      any time that is reasonable in the circumstances, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant or operator is a business,
      during normal business hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by registered mail or by courier; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of delivery, if it is delivered
    personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the fifth working day, excluding Saturdays and
    holidays, after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the postmark date, if it is sent by mail, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment on the waybill, if it is
      sent by courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by electronic means,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant or operator is an individual, on
      the day of the transmission, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant or operator is a business, on
      the day of the transmission, if that day is a working day, or, if
      that day is not a working day, on the first working day after the
      day of transmission.
