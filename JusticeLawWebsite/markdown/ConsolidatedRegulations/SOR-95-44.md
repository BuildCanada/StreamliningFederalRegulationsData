## General Import Permit No. 14 --- Margarine for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-14-margarine-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-44
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1994-12-30

General Import Permit No. 14 --- Margarine for Personal Use
::::

::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas margarine has been included on the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604);

And Whereas the Secretary of State for External Affairs has determined
an import access quantity for margarine pursuant to subsection
6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5274){#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5274-ID0EDBDA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5274-ID0EDBDA){#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5274}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 106
  :::

Therefore, the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to
subsection 8.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5275){#fn_SOR-95-44_e_hq_5275-ID0EHBCA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues
the annexed General permit authorizing the importation of margarine for
personal use, effective on the latest of the days on which sections 74,
106 and 109 of [An Act to implement the Agreement Establishing the World
Trade Organization](/eng/acts/W-11.8), being chapter 47 of the Statutes
of Canada, 1994, come into force.

  [[Return to footnote

  1994, c. 47, s. 109
  :::

Ottawa, Ontario, December 29, 1994

ANDRÉ OUELLET

*Secretary of State for External Affairs*

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975973 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,
[[margarine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means margarine (excluding liquid
margarine) and substitutes for butter, that are classified under tariff
item No. 1517.10.10 or 1517.90.21 or under heading No. 98.04 or 98.26 in
the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). (*margarine*)

::: HistoricalNote

:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975977 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada for the personal
use of the importer and the importer's household, margarine in a
quantity not exceeding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 3 kg in net weight for each importation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 12 kg in net weight for each importation, where the
  importer has a medical prescription for the margarine.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods imported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 14 --- Margarine for Personal
Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-44)" or "Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 14 --- Margarine pour usage
personnel](/eng/regulations/DORS-95-44)".
