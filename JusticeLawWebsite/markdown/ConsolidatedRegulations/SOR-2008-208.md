## Regulations Respecting Employment Related to Mines at the Donkin Coal Block {#regulations-respecting-employment-related-to-mines-at-the-donkin-coal-block .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-208
:::

[DONKIN COAL BLOCK DEVELOPMENT OPPORTUNITY ACT](/eng/acts/D-3.9)

Registration 2008-06-12

Regulations Respecting Employment Related to Mines at the Donkin Coal
Block

P.C. 2008-1061 2008-06-12
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to subsections 13(1) and (3) of the
[Donkin Coal Block Development Opportunity
Act](/eng/acts/D-3.9)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610343-E_hq_4049){#fn_610343-E_hq_4049-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Respecting Employment Related to
Mines at the Donkin Coal Block](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-208).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Nova Scotia laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Acts and regulations
of Nova Scotia, as amended from time to time, set out in Schedule 1 and,
for greater certainty, includes regulations made under one or more of
those Acts after the coming into force of these Regulations, unless
otherwise provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nova Scotia [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Nova Scotia
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21) (R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 235), as amended
from time to time, applies to the interpretation of the Nova Scotia
laws, with the adaptations set out in Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The employment in or in
connection with the operation of a mine that is wholly or partly at the
Donkin coal block is excluded from the application of Parts I, II and
III of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of Nova Scotia laws

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Nova Scotia laws are
incorporated by reference and apply, with the adaptations set out in
Schedule 2, to the employment in or in connection with the operation of
a mine that is wholly or partly at the Donkin coal block.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-745842 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Adaptation of the Labour Standards Code]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1adaptation-of-the-labour-standards-code .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pregnancy and parental leave

  **1** In subsections 59A(2) and 59G(3), a reference to the Human
  Rights Act (R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 214) shall be read as a reference to the
  [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Death of common-law partner

  **2** An employee is also entitled to leave under clause 60A(a) on the
  death of the employee's [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} as
  defined in clause 60E(1)(a).

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Adaptation of the Trade Union Act]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2adaptation-of-the-trade-union-act .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial Crown bound

  **3** For the purposes of subsection 4(1), the Act applies to Her
  Majesty in right of the Province and to employees of Her Majesty.
