:::

[IMMIGRATION AND REFUGEE PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/I-2.5)

Registration 2002-06-11

Immigration Division Rules

P.C. 2002-999 2002-06-11

The Chairperson of the Immigration and Refugee Board, pursuant to
subsection 161(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDADA} and subject to the
approval of the Governor in Council, in consultation with the Deputy
Chairpersons and the Director General of the Immigration Division,
hereby makes the annexed [Immigration Division
Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-229).

Ottawa, May 7, 2002

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant to subsection
161(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDABA}, hereby approves the
annexed [Immigration Division Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-229),
made on May 7, 2002 by the Chairperson of the Immigration and Refugee
Board, in consultation with the Deputy Chairpersons and the Director
General of the Immigration Division.

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-691321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[admissibility hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[admissibility hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hearing held
    under subsection 44(2) of the Act. ([enquête]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person's name,
    postal address and telephone number and the person's fax number and
    electronic mail address, if any. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[detention review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[detention review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a forty-eight hour
    review, a seven-day review and a thirty-day review. ([contrôle des
    motifs de détention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Immigration
    Division. ([Section]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forty-eight hour review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forty-eight hour review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the review of
    the reasons for continued detention under subsection 57(1) of the
    Act. ([contrôle des quarante-huit heures]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permanent resident or foreign
    national, as the case may be, and the
    Minister. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an admissibility hearing, a
    detention review, a conference or an
    application. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business office of
    the Division. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[seven-day review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[seven-day review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the review of the
    reasons for continued detention required to be held during the seven
    days following a forty-eight hour review, under subsection 57(2) of
    the Act. ([contrôle des sept jours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[thirty-day review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[thirty-day review]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the review of the
    reasons for continued detention required to be held during the
    thirty days following each previous review, under subsection 57(2)
    of the Act. ([contrôle des trente jours]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Communicating with the Division]{.HTitleText1} {#h-691335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communicating with the Division

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** All communication with the
Division must be directed to the registry office specified by the
Division.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to Admissibility Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-691338 .Part}

### [Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691339 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided by the Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** When the Minister requests
the Division to hold an admissibility hearing, the Minister must provide
to the Division and the permanent resident or foreign national, as the
case may be, any relevant information or document that the Minister may
have, including

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and other contact information in Canada of
  the permanent resident or foreign national;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person's date of birth, sex and citizenship;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person is single, married, separated or
  divorced or is a common-law partner;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the inadmissibility report and the Minister's
  referral;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has made a claim for refugee
  protection;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the place of detention, if
  the person is detained;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the language --- English or French --- chosen by the
  person for communicating with the Division;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if an interpreter is required, the language or
  dialect to be interpreted;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the person has counsel, the counsel's contact
  information;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the client identification number given to the person
  by the Department of Citizenship and Immigration;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the names, sex, date of birth, citizenship, and other
  contact information of any family member whose case has been referred
  to the Division, and the client identification number given to them by
  the Department of Citizenship and Immigration;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the Minister makes the request;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the Minister's counsel;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister has made an application for
  non-disclosure of information;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister believes that the person is less
  than 18 years of age or is unable to appreciate the nature of the
  proceedings; and

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} the evidence to be presented by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to contact information

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If the contact information
changes, the permanent resident or foreign national, unless detained,
must without delay provide the changes in writing to the Division and
the Minister.

### [Withdrawing a Request by the Minister for an Admissibility Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691360 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Abuse of process

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Withdrawal of a request for an
  admissibility hearing is an abuse of process if withdrawal would
  likely have a negative effect on the integrity of the Division. If no
  substantive evidence has been accepted in the proceedings, withdrawal
  of a request is not an abuse of process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if no evidence has been
  accepted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  proceedings, the Minister may withdraw a request by notifying the
  Division orally at a proceeding or in writing. If the Minister
  notifies in writing, the Minister must provide a copy of the notice to
  the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal if evidence has been
  accepted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If substantive evidence has been accepted in the
  proceedings, the Minister must make a written application to the
  Division in order to withdraw a request.

### [Reinstating a Request by the Minister for an Admissibility Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691368 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for reinstatement of
withdrawn request

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may make a written application to the Division to reinstate a
  request for an admissibility hearing that was withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division must allow the application if it is
  established that there was a failure to observe a principle of natural
  justice or if it is otherwise in the interests of justice to allow the
  application.

### [Decision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691374 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Favourable decision

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  decision at the conclusion of an admissibility hearing is in favour of
  the permanent resident or foreign national, the member making the
  decision must date and sign a notice of decision and provide a copy to
  the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unfavourable decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the decision is not in favour of the permanent
  resident or foreign national, the member must date and sign an order
  indicating the applicable provisions of the Act and provide a copy to
  the parties. The member must also notify the permanent resident or
  foreign national of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their right to appeal to the Immigration Appeal
    Division; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they do not have the right to appeal, their
    right to file an application for judicial review in the Federal
    Court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision takes effect

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A decision made orally at a hearing takes effect when
  a Division member states the decision. A decision made in writing
  takes effect when the member signs and dates it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for written reasons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request made by a party for written reasons for a
  decision may be made orally at the end of an admissibility hearing or
  in writing. A request in writing must be received by the Division no
  later than 10 days after the decision takes effect.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rules Applicable to Detention Reviews]{.HTitleText1} {#h-691386 .Part}

### [Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691387 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided by the Minister

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  foreign national or a permanent resident is subject to a detention
  review, the Minister must provide the Division and the person detained
  with the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, sex, date of birth and
    citizenship;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the person is single, married, separated or
    divorced or is a common-law partner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has made a claim for refugee
    protection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the language --- English or French --- chosen by
    the person for communicating with the Division;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if an interpreter is required, the language or
    dialect to be interpreted;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the person has counsel, the counsel's contact
    information;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that the person was first placed
    in detention;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the place where the person
    is being detained;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister is seeking a detention review
    after the first forty-eight hour detention or after a seven-day or
    thirty-day review;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the identification number given to the person by
    the Department of Citizenship and Immigration;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the Act under which the review of
    the reasons for continued detention is required;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} whether an application for non-disclosure of
    information has been made; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} whether the Minister believes that the person is
    less than 18 years of age or is unable to appreciate the nature of
    the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be received by the Division and
  the person detained

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a forty-eight hour review, as soon
    as possible; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a seven-day or thirty-day review, at
    least three days before the date fixed for the review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for early review

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  may make a written application to the Division requesting a detention
  review before the expiry of the seven-day or thirty-day period, as the
  case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may allow the application if the party
  sets out new facts that justify an early review of the detention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal before detention review

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must notify
the Division as soon as a permanent resident or foreign national is
removed from Canada prior to a scheduled detention review.

### [Decisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691415 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to the parties

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  conclusion of a detention review, the member must notify the parties
  of the member's decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The member must date and sign an order for detention
  or release indicating the applicable provisions of the Act and provide
  a copy to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When decision takes effect

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A decision made orally at a hearing takes effect when
  a Division member states the decision. A decision made in writing
  takes effect when the member signs and dates it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for written reasons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request made by a party for written reasons for a
  decision may be made orally at the end of a detention review or in
  writing. A request in writing must be received by the Division no
  later than 10 days after the decision takes effect.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Rules that Apply to Both Admissibility Hearings and Detention Reviews]{.HTitleText1} {#h-691425 .Part}

### [Information Relating to Counsel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691426 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counsel's contact information

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A permanent resident or
foreign national who is represented by counsel must, on obtaining
counsel, provide the counsel's contact information in writing to the
Division and the Minister. If that information changes, the permanent
resident or foreign national must without delay provide the changes in
writing to the Division and the Minister.

### [Counsel of Record]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691429 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Becoming counsel of record

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** As soon as counsel for a
permanent resident or foreign national agrees to a date for a
proceeding, or becomes counsel after a date has been fixed, the counsel
becomes counsel of record.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal as counsel of record

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** To withdraw as counsel of
record, counsel must notify the Division and the Minister in writing as
soon as possible. Counsel is no longer counsel of record as soon as the
Division receives the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal of counsel of record

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** To remove counsel as
counsel of record, the permanent resident or foreign national must
notify the Division and the Minister in writing as soon as possible.
Counsel is no longer counsel of record when the Division receives the
notice.

### [Language of Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691436 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changing the language of proceedings

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permanent resident or foreign national may make an application to the
  Division to change the language of the proceedings to English or
  French

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} orally or in writing in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in writing in all other cases.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A written application must be received by the
  Division

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
    hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requesting an interpreter

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party or a party's witness needs an interpreter for a proceeding, the
  party must notify the Division in writing and specify the language or
  dialect of the interpreter. The notice must be received by the
  Division

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpreter's oath

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interpreter must take an oath or make a solemn
  affirmation to interpret accurately.

### [Designated Representatives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691453 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of counsel to notify the Division

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If counsel for a party
believes that the Division should designate a representative for the
permanent resident or foreign national in the proceedings because they
are under 18 years of age or unable to appreciate the nature of the
proceedings, counsel must without delay notify the Division and the
other party in writing. If counsel is aware of a person in Canada who
meets the requirements to be designated as a representative, counsel
must provide the person's contact information in the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for being designated

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** To be designated as a
representative, a person must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be 18 years of age or older;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} understand the nature of the proceedings;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be willing and able to act in the best interests of
  the permanent resident or foreign national; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} not have interests that conflict with those of the
  permanent resident or foreign national.

### [Conference]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691462 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to participate at a
conference

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Division may require the parties to participate at a conference to
  discuss issues, relevant facts and any other matter that would make
  the proceedings more fair and efficient.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information or documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Division may require the parties to give any
  information or document at or before the conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decisions noted

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division must make a written record of any
  decisions and agreements made at the conference or state them orally
  at the hearing.

### [Fixing a Date]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691470 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fixing a date

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The Division must fix the
date for a hearing and any other proceeding relating to the hearing. The
Division may require the parties to participate in the preparation of a
schedule of proceedings by appearing at a scheduling conference or
otherwise providing information.

### [Notice to Appear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691473 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to appear

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The Division must notify
the parties, orally or in writing, of the date, time and location of a
hearing.

### [Permanent Resident or Foreign National in Custody]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691476 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Division may order
the person who holds a permanent resident or foreign national in custody
to bring the permanent resident or foreign national to a hearing at a
location specified by the Division.

### [Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691479 .Subheading}

#### [Form and Language of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691480 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents prepared by party

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document prepared for use by a party in a proceeding must be
  typewritten on one side of 21.5 cm by 28 cm (8½\" x 11\") paper and
  the pages must be numbered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Photocopies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any photocopy provided by a party must be a clear
  copy of the document photocopied and be on one side of 21.5 cm by 28
  cm (8½\" x 11\") paper and the pages must be numbered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Numbered documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party must number consecutively each document
  provided by the party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If more than one document is provided, the party must
  provide a list of the documents and their numbers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of documents

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  documents used at a proceeding must be in English or French or, if in
  another language, be provided with an English or French translation
  and a translator's declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of Minister's documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister provides a document that is not in
  the language of the proceedings, the Minister must provide a
  translation and a translator's declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translator's declaration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A translator's declaration must include the
  translator's name, the language translated and a statement signed by
  the translator that the translation is accurate.

#### [Disclosure of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691497 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of documents by a party

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If a party wants to use a
document at a hearing, the party must provide a copy to the other party
and the Division. The copies must be received

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
  hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the same
  time; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
  hearing.

#### [How to Provide a Document]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691502 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Rules 28 to 31 apply to
any document, including a notice or a written request or application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to the Division

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document provided to the Division must be provided to a Division
  member at a proceeding or to the registry office specified by the
  Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to the Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document provided to the Minister must be provided
  to the Minister's counsel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents to a permanent
  resident or foreign national

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document provided to a permanent resident or
  foreign national must be provided to them or, if they have counsel, to
  their counsel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}How to provide a document

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A document can be
provided in the following ways:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by regular mail or registered mail;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by courier or priority post;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax if the recipient has a fax number and the
  document has no more than 20 pages, unless the recipient consents to
  receiving more than 20 pages; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} by electronic mail if the Division allows.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If document cannot be provided under rule
29

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If a party after making
reasonable efforts is unable to provide a document in a way required by
rule 29, the party may make an application to the Division to be allowed
to provide the document in another way or to be excused from providing
the document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When a document is considered received by
the Division

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document provided to the Division is considered received by the
  Division on the day the document is date stamped by the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When a document provided by regular
  mail is considered received by a party

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document provided by regular mail to a party is
  considered to be received seven days after the day it was mailed. If
  the seventh day is a Saturday, Sunday or other statutory holiday, the
  document is considered to be received on the next working day.

### [Witnesses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691526 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing witness information

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party wants to call a witness, the party must provide in writing to
  the other party and the Division the following witness information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose and substance of the witness's
    testimony or, in the case of an expert witness, a summary of the
    testimony to be given signed by the expert witness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time needed for the witness's testimony;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the party's relationship to the witness;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an expert witness, a description of
    their qualifications;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the party wants the witness to testify by
    videoconference or telephone; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of witnesses that the party intends to
    call.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The witness information must be received by the
  Division and the other party

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
    hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for a summons

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants the Division to order a person to testify at a hearing
  must make an application to the Division for a summons, either orally
  at a proceeding or in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to issue a summons, the Division
  must consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the necessity of the testimony to a full and proper
    hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the person to give that testimony.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using the summons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a party wants to use a summons, the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the summons to the summoned person by hand;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the summons to the Division with
    a written statement of how and when the summons was provided; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay or offer to pay the summoned person the
    applicable witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of
    the Federal Court Rules, 1998.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancelling a summons

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** A person summoned to
appear may make a written application to the Division to cancel the
summons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arrest warrant

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person does not obey a summons to appear, the party who requested the
  summons may make a written application to the Division to issue a
  warrant for the arrest of the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting evidence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide supporting evidence for the
  written application by affidavit or statutory declaration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for issue of arrest
  warrant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Division may issue a warrant if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person was provided the summons by hand or the
    person is avoiding being provided the summons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person was paid or offered the applicable
    witness fees and travel expenses set out in Tariff A of the Federal
    Court Rules, 1998;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not appear at the hearing as
    required by the summons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person's testimony is still needed for a full
    and proper hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of a warrant

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued by the Division for the arrest of a
  person must include directions concerning detention and release.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded witness

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Unless allowed by the
Division, no person shall communicate to a witness excluded from a
hearing room any testimony given while the witness was excluded until
that witness has finished testifying.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691569 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General provision

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Unless these Rules
provide otherwise, a party

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who wants the Division to make a decision on any
  matter in a proceeding, including the procedure to be followed, must
  make an application to the Division under rule 38;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who wants to respond to the application must respond
  under rule 39; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} who wants to reply to a response must reply under
  rule 40.

#### [How to Make an Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691575 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to the Division

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  these Rules provide otherwise, an application must follow this rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit and form of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made orally or in writing,
  and as soon as possible or within the time limit provided in the Act
  or these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure in oral application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For an application made orally, the Division
  determines the applicable procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of written application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision that the party wants the
    Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that the party wants the
    Division to consider in deciding the application; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application that is not specified
    in these Rules, include supporting evidence in the form of a
    statutory declaration or affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A party who makes a written application must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original application, together
    with a written statement of how and when the party provided the copy
    to the other party.

#### [How to Respond to a Written Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691593 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding to a written application

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response to a written application must be in writing. In a response
  the party must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision the party wants the Division to
    make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that the party wants the
    Division to consider when it decides the application; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include supporting evidence in the form of a
    statutory declaration or affidavit, if the response is to an
    application that is not provided for by these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the response

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who responds to a written application must
  provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the response; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original response, together
    with a written statement of how and when the party provided the copy
    to the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, no later than five days after
    the party received a copy of the application.

#### [How to Reply to a Written Response]{.HTitleText3} {#h-691609 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replying to a written response

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  reply to a written response must be in writing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party who replies must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the other party, a copy of the reply; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Division, the original reply, together with
    a written statement of how and when the party provided the copy to
    the other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, no later than three days after
    the party received a copy of the response.

### [Non-disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691621 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to prohibit disclosure

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application made by the Minister for non-disclosure of information
  must be made in writing as soon as possible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion from hearing room

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application is made during a hearing, the
  Division must exclude the permanent resident or foreign national, and
  their counsel, from the hearing room.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing summary to the Minister

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The summary that the Division proposes to provide to
  the permanent resident or foreign national under paragraph 78(h) of
  the Act may be provided to the Minister by any means that ensures its
  confidentiality.

### [Changing the Location of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691629 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to change the location of a
hearing

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to change the location
  of a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would allow the
    hearing to be full and proper;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would likely delay or
    slow the hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the operation
    of the Division;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} how a change of location would affect the parties;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether a change of location would endanger public
    safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear at the hearing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the hearing at the
  location fixed and be ready to start or continue the hearing.

### [Changing the Date or Time of a Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to change the date or time of
a hearing

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to change the date or
  time of a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding the application, the Division must
  consider any relevant factors, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a date and time that was fixed after
    the Division consulted or tried to consult the party, the existence
    of exceptional circumstances for allowing the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the party made the application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the time the party has had to prepare for the
    hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made by the party to be ready to start
    or continue the hearing;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the matter to be
    heard;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether the party has counsel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any previous delays and the reasons for them;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether the time and date fixed for the hearing was
    peremptory; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether allowing the application would unreasonably
    delay the proceedings or likely cause an injustice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to appear at the hearing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Unless a party receives a decision from the Division
  allowing the application, the party must appear for the hearing at the
  date and time fixed and be ready to start or continue the hearing.

### [Joining or Separating Hearings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691659 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to join hearings

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may make an application to the Division to join hearings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to separate hearings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may make an application to the Division to
  separate hearings that are joined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before deciding an application, the Division must
  consider any information provided by the applicant and any other
  relevant information, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the hearings involve similar questions of
    law or fact;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether allowing the application would promote the
    efficient administration of the work of the Division; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether allowing the application would likely cause
    an injustice.

### [Proceedings Conducted in Private]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691670 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for proceeding conducted in
private

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who makes an application to the Division to have a proceeding
  conducted in private must apply in writing and follow this rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the person must state the
  decision that the person wants the Division to make, and may request
  that the hearing of the application be conducted in private.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must provide a copy of the application to
  the parties and the original application to the Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document provided under this rule must be received
  by its recipient

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing of the application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} At the hearing, the person must give reasons why the
  Division should conduct the proceeding in private and present any
  evidence that the person wants the Division to consider in deciding
  the application.

### [Proceeding Conducted in Public]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691684 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for proceeding conducted in
public

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who makes an application to the Division to have a proceeding
  conducted in public must apply in writing and follow this rule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the application, the person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state the decision that the person wants the
    Division to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give reasons why the Division should make that
    decision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} include any evidence that the person wants the
    Division to consider in deciding the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must provide the original application and
  two copies to the Division. The Division must provide a copy of the
  application to the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document provided under this rule must be received
  by the Division

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, in the case of a forty-eight
    hour or seven-day review or an admissibility hearing held at the
    same time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, at least five days before the
    hearing.

### [Notice of Constitutional Question]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691699 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of constitutional question

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who wants to challenge the constitutional validity,
  applicability or operability of a legislative provision must complete
  a notice of constitutional question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide notice using either Form 69,
  "Notice of Constitutional Question", set out in the Federal Court
  Rules, 1998, or any other form that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Division file number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and place of the hearing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific legislative provision that is being
    challenged;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the relevant facts relied on to support the
    constitutional challenge; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the legal argument to be made in
    support of the constitutional challenge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing the notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party must provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice of constitutional question to
    the Attorney General of Canada and to the attorney general of every
    province and territory of Canada, in accordance with section 57 of
    the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice to the other party; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the original notice to the Division, together with
    a written statement of how and when a copy of the notice was
    provided under paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Documents provided under this rule must be received
  by their recipients no later than 10 days before the day the
  constitutional argument will be made.

- 2002, c. 8, s. 182

### [Oral Representations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691720 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral representations

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Representations made by a
party must be made orally at the end of a hearing unless the Division
orders otherwise.

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691723 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No applicable rule

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** In the absence of a
provision in these Rules dealing with a matter raised during the
proceedings, the Division may do whatever is necessary to deal with the
matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of the Division

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Division may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} act on its own, without a party having to make an
  application or request to the Division;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} change a requirement of a rule;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} excuse a person from a requirement of a rule; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} extend or shorten a time limit, before or after the
  time limit has passed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failing to follow a rule

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** Unless proceedings are
declared invalid by the Division, a failure to follow any requirement of
these Rules does not make the proceedings invalid.

### [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText2} {#h-691734 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_cif){#fn_cif-ID0EBCA}[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52
.sectionLabel}** These Rules come into force on the day on which section
161 of the Act comes into force.
