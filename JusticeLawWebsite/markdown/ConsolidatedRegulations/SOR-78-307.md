## Ontario Fresh Grape Information (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-fresh-grape-information-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-307
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1978-04-03

Regulations Respecting the Information Required from Persons Engaged in
the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Fresh Grapes
Produced in Ontario
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Fresh Grape Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to the
Ontario Fresh Grape Order, hereby revokes the [Ontario Fresh Grape
Information (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-307) made on 26 January,
1977[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDACA}, and makes
the annexed [Regulations respecting the information required from
persons engaged in the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of
fresh grapes produced in Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-307).

Dated at the Town of Lincoln, Ontario, this 28th day of March, 1978

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862034 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Fresh Grape Information (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-307).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862036 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[appointed dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    dealer-shipper in possession of a valid certificate of appointment
    issued by the Board pursuant to section 5 of the Ontario Fresh Grape
    Shippers and Dealer-Shippers (Interprovincial and Export)
    Regulations; ([négociant-expéditeur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipper in
    possession of a valid certificate of appointment issued by the Board
    pursuant to section 4 of the Ontario Fresh Grape Shippers and
    Dealer-Shippers (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations;
    ([expéditeur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Fresh Grape Growers'
    Marketing Board; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer-shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells or
    offers to sell, receives, assembles, packs, ships or transports
    fresh grapes but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer, an appointed shipper or an appointed
      dealer-shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports fresh grapes by motor
      vehicle as the agent of a producer;
      ([négociant-expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fresh grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means grapes produced in
    Ontario other than grapes that are used by a processor for
    processing; ([raisin frais]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the manufacture of grape
    products or juice, beverage, spirits or wine from grapes and
    includes bottling, distilling or fermenting with sugar of sulphur
    dioxide or any other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    business of processing grapes; ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of fresh grapes; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who receives,
    assembles, packs, ships or transports fresh grapes but does not
    include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer, an appointed shipper or an appointed
      dealer-shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports fresh grapes by motor
      vehicle as the agent of a producer. ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862053 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of fresh grapes in interprovincial and export
trade and to persons and property situated within the Province of
Ontario.

## [Monthly Sales Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862055 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every person who sells
fresh grapes in interprovincial or export trade shall deliver to the
Board a Monthly Sales Return for each of the months of July, August,
September, October, November and December in each year in the form set
out in Schedule I, not later than the 15th day of the month next
following the month covered by the return, containing for each sale the
information required by the form.

## [Monthly Shipping Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862057 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who ships
fresh grapes in interprovincial or export trade shall deliver to the
Board a Monthly Shipping Return for each of the months of July, August,
September, October, November and December in each year in the form set
out in Schedule II, not later than the 15th day of the month next
following the month covered by the return, containing for each shipment
the information required by the form.

## [Monthly Transportation Return]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862059 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person, other than a
railway company, who transports fresh grapes in interprovincial or
export trade shall deliver to the Board a Monthly Transportation Return
for each of the months of July, August, September, October, November and
December in each year in the form set out in Schedule III, not later
than the 15th day of the month next following the month covered by the
return, containing for each shipment the information required by the
form.

## [Daily Tally Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862061 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper or
dealer-shipper shall deliver to a properly identified representative of
the Board a Daily Tally Return in the form set out in Schedule IV
together with one copy of the shipping manifest, not later than 10:00
a.m. of the day next following the day covered by those documents,
containing the information required by the form.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At the
  time of receiving fresh grapes from a producer, every shipper or
  dealer-shipper shall deliver to the producer a receipt for the fresh
  grapes containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the shipper or
    dealer-shipper;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the producer who delivered the fresh
    grapes to the shipper or dealer-shipper;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of delivery of the fresh grapes to the
    shipper or dealer-shipper;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number and type of containers of each variety;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where fresh grapes are delivered in bulk for
    central packing, the charge made by the shipper or dealer-shipper
    for central packing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A shipper or dealer-shipper may stamp on the receipt
  referred to in subsection (1) words to the effect that the prices are
  subject to change in accordance with any price determination order of
  the Board.

## [Inspection of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862071 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper or
dealer-shipper shall produce for inspection by a properly identified
representative of the Board, at any time during the normal business
hours of the shipper or dealer-shipper after 10:00 a.m. of the day next
following receipt of fresh grapes from a producer, a true copy of the
receipt referred to in subsection 8(1).

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every dealer-shipper
shall produce for inspection by a properly identified representative of
the Board, at any time during the normal business hours of the
dealer-shipper after 10:00 a.m. of the day next following the day on
which the dealer-shipper sold fresh grapes, a true copy of every invoice
covering every sale of fresh grapes sold by him the previous day.

## [Annual List of Producers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862074 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper or
dealer-shipper shall, not later than November 30th in each calendar
year, deliver to the Board a written list showing the names and
addresses of all producers from whom he has received fresh grapes in
that calendar year.
