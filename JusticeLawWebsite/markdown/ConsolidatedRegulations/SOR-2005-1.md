## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714659 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[NWTSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NWTSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Northwest Territories Sex
    Offender Information Registration Centre, administered by RCMP "G"
    Division and located at 5010 49th Avenue, Yellowknife.
    ([BIRDSTNO]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714664 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in the Northwest Territories
  may make any report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or section
  4.3 of the Act, or provide any notification required under section 6
  of the Act, by telephone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not remove
  the requirement for the sex offender to report in person under
  paragraph 4.1(c) of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714668 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every member of the RCMP is
authorized in the Northwest Territories to collect information for the
purposes of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714670 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every person whose duties
include the registration of information at the NWTSOIRC is authorized in
the Northwest Territories to register information for the purposes of
the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714672 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  RCMP detachment in the Northwest Territories is designated as a
  registration centre and the designated area of service of each
  registration centre is the area served by the detachment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The NWTSOIRC is designated as a registration centre
  in the Northwest Territories and its designated area of service is the
  entire Northwest Territories.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714676 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndFB73_hq_16346){#fn_IndFB73_hq_16346-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the later of the
day on which the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes
into force and the day on which these Regulations are registered.
