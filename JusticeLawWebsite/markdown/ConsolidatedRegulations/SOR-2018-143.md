[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or body who, by
    reason of an agreement with Her Majesty in right of Canada, carries
    out the activities that were formerly carried out by a portion of
    the public service. ([nouvel employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to a person who, by reason
  of an agreement between Her Majesty in right of Canada and a new
  employer, ceases to be employed in the public service and becomes
  employed by the new employer after the day on which these Regulations
  come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- person re-employed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  becomes re-employed by the new employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- survivor and
  children

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 6 do not apply to the survivor or
  children of a person who has received a return of contributions or has
  exercised an option in accordance with subsection 3(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When certain provisions are applicable

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12 to 13.01 of the Act only apply to the person on or after the day on
  which they cease to be employed by the new employer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if, on or after the day on
  which these Regulations come into force, a person, were it not for
  these Regulations, would be entitled to a return of contributions
  under subsection 12(3) or 12.1(4) of the Act, the person may request,
  in writing, the return of contributions within one year after the day
  on which they cease to be employed in the public service and become
  employed by the new employer, and, if, in the same circumstances, a
  person is entitled to exercise an option in favour of the transfer
  value referred to in section 13.01 of the Act, they may exercise the
  option within the same period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Benefits for survivor and children

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and children
of a person who dies while employed by the new employer are entitled to
one of the following benefits to which they would have been entitled if
the person had been employed in the public service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death benefit under subsection 12(8) or 12.1(8)
  of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allowances referred to in subsection 13(3) or
  13.001(3) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 26(2) of Act

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a person is deemed to cease to be employed
in the public service on the day on which they cease to be employed by
the new employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 10(5) of Act

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one-year period referred to in
paragraph (a) of that subsection begins on the day on which the person
ceases to be employed by the new employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pensionable service

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service that begins on the day on which the person ceases to be
employed in the public service and that ends on the day on which they
cease to be employed by the new employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sections 12 to 13.01 of Act

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, the age of the person when they cease
to be employed in the public service is their age on the day on which
they cease to be employed by the new employer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
