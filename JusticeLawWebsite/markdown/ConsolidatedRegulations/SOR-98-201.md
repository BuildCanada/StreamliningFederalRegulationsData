## Firearms Registration Certificates Regulations {#firearms-registration-certificates-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-201
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Firearms Registration Certificates Regulations

P.C. 1998-476 1998-03-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Firearms Registration Certificates
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-201), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on October 30, 1997, which
date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraph 14(b),
subsection 61(1), section 72 and paragraphs 117(a), (b) and (w) of the
[Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Firearms
Registration Certificates Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-201).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019074 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approved verifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved verifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is
    designated by the Registrar under section 1.2 for the purpose of
    verifying information under subsection 2(1). ([vérificateur
    autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following classes of
    firearms, namely, prohibited firearms, restricted firearms and
    non-restricted firearms. ([classe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commencement day firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commencement day firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm
    that was in the lawful possession of an individual or a business in
    Canada on December 1, 1998. ([arme à feu de la date de
    référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that
    is neither a prohibited firearm nor a restricted firearm. ([arme à
    feu sans restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handgun under
    paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
    firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([arme de poing prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[specially imported firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specially imported firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm
    imported on a temporary basis by a business that holds a firearms
    licence, as a good under tariff item No. 9993.00.00 of the List of
    Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([arme à feu d'importation
    spéciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sticker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sticker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-adhesive label issued
    by the Registrar under subsection 3(2).
    ([étiquette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verify]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verify]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the act of confirming that the
    information concerning the identification and classification of a
    firearm that is submitted to the Registrar in support of an
    application for a registration certificate is complete and accurate.
    ([vérifier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 1
:::

## [Describing Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019087 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 14(b) of the Act, the manner of describing a firearm is by
referring to its make, class, type, action and calibre or gauge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 2
:::

## [Verification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019091 .Part}

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar may
designate an individual as an approved verifier if the individual has
the required knowledge to identify and classify firearms.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 3
:::

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a registration certificate shall be accompanied by
  evidence that the information submitted in support of the application
  has been verified by an approved verifier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the application does not have
  to be accompanied by the evidence referred to in that subsection if
  the firearm has already been verified, the description of the firearm
  has not changed and the Registrar determines, taking into account the
  previous method of verification, that a new verification is
  unnecessary.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 3
:::

## [Manner of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019100 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 54(1) of the Act, an application for a registration
certificate in respect of a transfer of a firearm may, in addition to
being made in the prescribed form, be made by telephone by either
speaking to a person or to an interactive voice response system.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 3
:::

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 54(1) of the Act, an application for a registration
certificate by a business may, in addition to being made in the
prescribed form, be made by submitting the information set out in
section 2.3 on a compact disc or as an attachment to an e-mail.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 3
:::

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019107 .Part}

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 54(1) of the Act, an application for a registration
certificate that is made in a manner prescribed by section 2.1 or 2.2
shall include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and licence number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a business, the name of its
  representative and the representative's licence number or, if the
  representative does not hold a licence, date of birth;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is an individual and the firearm is
  a prohibited firearm or restricted firearm, the address where the
  firearm will be stored if different from the applicant's residential
  address;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is an individual, the number of the
  existing registration certificate;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a business, the number of the
  existing registration certificate or, if the firearm is newly
  manufactured, the serial number, the make, model, type, action,
  calibre or gauge, barrel length and the quantity of ammunition that
  the cartridge magazine can contain;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the firearm identification number, if any; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is an individual and the firearm is
  a prohibited handgun or restricted firearm, the purpose for which the
  firearm is being acquired.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 3
:::

## [Firearm Identification Number]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019118 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall indicate, on the registration certificate that is
  issued in respect of a firearm, the firearm identification number
  assigned to the firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Registrar has made a determination under
  section 6, and if required for the purposes of paragraph 7(2)(a), the
  Registrar shall issue with the registration certificate a sticker that
  bears the firearm identification number.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019122 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 5, the Registrar shall attach to a registration certificate
  that is issued in respect of a firearm the condition that the holder
  of the certificate shall advise the Registrar, within 30 days after
  the modification, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any modification to the firearm that results in a
    change of class of the firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm registered as a frame or
    receiver only, any modification that makes it capable of discharging
    ammunition;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any modification to an altered automatic firearm;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any modification that results in the firearm
    ceasing to be a firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 5, the Registrar shall attach to a
  registration certificate that is issued in respect of a firearm the
  condition that when the type, action, calibre or gauge of the firearm
  is modified, the holder of the certificate shall advise the Registrar
  of the modification,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the modification is intended to be permanent,
    within 30 days after the modification; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the modification is not intended to be permanent
    but still exists 30 days after it is made, without delay after that
    period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 4
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If the Registrar issues a
registration certificate in respect of a firearm to a business that is
the holder of a licence issued for the purposes prescribed by paragraph
22(f) of the [Firearms Licences
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-199), the Registrar shall attach to
the certificate the condition that the holder advise the Registrar,
within 13 months after the modification, of any modification referred to
in section 4, if the modification still exists one year after it is
made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 5
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Registrar determines that a firearm does not bear a serial number
  sufficient to distinguish it from other firearms in accordance with
  paragraph 14(a) of the Act, the Registrar shall attach to the
  registration certificate that is issued a condition that the firearm
  bear its firearm identification number in accordance with sections 7
  to 9, as applicable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a specially imported
  firearm.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 6
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the holder of a registration certificate for a
  firearm that is subject to a condition under section 6 shall ensure
  that the firearm identification number is permanently and legibly
  stamped or engraved on a visible place on the firearm's frame or
  receiver within 90 days after the issuance of the certificate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a commencement day firearm, the holder
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the sticker that bears the firearm
    identification number is attached to a visible place on the
    firearm's frame or receiver within 60 days after the issuance of the
    certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the firearm identification number is
    permanently and legibly stamped or engraved on a visible place on
    the firearm's frame or receiver within 90 days after the issuance of
    the certificate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 7
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar, on
application by the holder of a registration certificate, shall allow the
holder to attach a sticker to, or to permanently and legibly stamp or
engrave the firearm identification number on, a place on the firearms's
frame or receiver that is not visible without disassembly if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} so doing would be consistent with the current
  practices of the manufacturer of that model of firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the firearm does not provide a visible space suitable
  to attach a sticker or to stamp or engrave the firearm identification
  number;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is rare; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is of a value that is unusually high for
  that type of firearm and that value would be significantly reduced if
  the sticker or firearm identification number were visible without
  disassembly.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 8
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  registration certificate holder referred to in paragraph 7(2)(a) shall
  ensure that the sticker that bears the firearm identification number
  remains attached to the firearm in accordance with section 7 and that
  the number remains legible.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder shall advise the Registrar without delay
  if the sticker bearing the firearm identification number becomes
  detached from the firearm or is obscured or if the number on the
  sticker becomes illegible.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When advised under subsection (2), the Registrar
  shall without delay issue a new sticker bearing the identification
  number of the firearm and the holder shall ensure that the sticker is
  attached to the firearm without delay on receipt.

## [Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019160 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall
revoke a registration certificate if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its holder contravenes any condition attached to it,
  including the conditions referred to in sections 4 to 6; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar is advised under paragraph 4(1)(a) of a
  modification that changes the class of the firearm or is advised under
  paragraph 4(1)(b) or subsection 4(2) of a modification referred to in
  either of those provisions.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 9
:::

## [Notice of Refusal or Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019166 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice of a decision to refuse to issue a registration certificate or
  to revoke one is sufficiently given if it is addressed to the
  applicant or holder at the address given in the application or to
  another address of which the Registrar has been advised and if the
  notice is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personally delivered

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, at any time that is
      reasonable in the circumstances, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business, during normal
      business hours;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by registered mail or by courier; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of delivery, if it is personally
    delivered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the fifth working day, excluding Saturdays and
    holidays,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} after the postmark date, if it is sent by mail,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after the date of shipment on the waybill, if it
      is sent by courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is sent by electronic means

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, on the day of
      transmission, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a business, on the day of
      transmission, if that day is a working day, or, if that day is not
      a working day, on the first working day after the day of
      transmission.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-276, s. 10
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019184 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on December 1, 1998.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-471, s. 3.
