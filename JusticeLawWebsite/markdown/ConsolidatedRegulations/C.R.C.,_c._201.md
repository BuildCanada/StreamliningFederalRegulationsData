## Ontario Grapes-for-Processing Order {#ontario-grapes-for-processing-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 201
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Grapes-for-Processing Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536460 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Grapes-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._201).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536462 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Grape
    Growers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grapes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means grapes produced in Ontario,
    except grapes used for any purpose other than processing by a
    processor; ([raisin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    grapes, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the
    Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the manufacture of grape
    products or juice, beverage, spirits or wine from grapes and
    includes bottling, distilling or fermenting with sugar or sulphur
    dioxide or any other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    business of processing grapes. ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536471 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of grapes in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of grapes locally within that Province
under the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536473 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of grapes in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of grapes, and for such purpose to classify such
persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the members
of the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity Board
may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation
of reserves, and the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the
sale or disposal of grapes and the equalization or adjustment among
producers of grapes of moneys realized from the sale thereof during such
period or periods of time as it may determine.
