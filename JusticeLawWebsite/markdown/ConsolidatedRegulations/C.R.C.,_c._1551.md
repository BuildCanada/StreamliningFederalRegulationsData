## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528131 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Textile Labelling and Advertising
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1551).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528133 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Textile Labelling
    Act](/eng/acts/T-10); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[country of origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[country of origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the country in which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a textile fibre was grown or made, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any textile fibre product not included in
      paragraph (a) was substantially made; ([pays
      d'origine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[narrow fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[narrow fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a textile fibre product
    that is 30 centimetres or less in width and is ordinarily used as
    findings or trimming; ([article de rubanerie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a consumer textile
    article, means prescribed by section 4; ([désigné]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reclaimed textile fibre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reclaimed textile fibre]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a textile
    fibre obtained from a yarn, fabric, consumer textile article or
    plumage product. ([fibre textile récupérée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528144 .Part}

### [Labelling Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528145 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[disclosure label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disclosure label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a representation
    label that complies with the Act and these Regulations as to its
    form and as to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information that it shows with respect to the
      consumer textile article to which it is applied, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which such information is shown,

    and where the information required by section 11 is shown in two
    labels pursuant to subsection 14(3) or 14(4), means both labels;

[[representation label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representation label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label that
    contains any representation as to the textile fibre content of the
    article to which it is applied.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 3(a) of the Act and these Regulations, a prescribed consumer
textile article is any consumer textile article included in Schedule I.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-505, s. 1
- SOR/91-299, s. 1
- SOR/94-247, s. 2
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  representation label that is applied to a consumer textile article
  that is sold, imported into Canada or advertised by a dealer shall
  meet the requirements for a disclosure label unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the article to which it is applied is exempted by
    these Regulations from the application of paragraph 3(b) of the Act;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it meets the requirements set out in subsection
    (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A representation label that is applied to a consumer
  textile article that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is sold, imported into Canada or advertised by a
    dealer, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has applied to it in the prescribed manner another
    representation label that is a disclosure label

  shall not contain any representation that

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} detracts from, qualifies or contradicts any
    representation in the disclosure label.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-253, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-505, s. 2
- SOR/2018-253, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A consumer textile article
is exempt from section 3 of the Act where that article is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} included in Schedule II or is not prescribed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sold or advertised by a dealer if the article was,
  prior to December 1, 1972, received by or in transit to him or any
  other dealer from the dealer who manufactured or made the article;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sold

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a dealer outside Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to or by a duty-free store in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} imported into Canada and labelled before resale in
  accordance with section 8;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} made up for or sold to one of the following entities:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a commercial or industrial enterprise,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a federal, provincial or municipal department or
    agency,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a public utility,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an educational institution,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a religious order or organization, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a health care facility,

  for use by the entity or for use by or resale to its employees or
  students or, in the case of a religious order or organization, for use
  by or resale to members of the order or organization; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} sold by the manufacturer to an employee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-299, s. 2
- SOR/94-247, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The prohibition set out in
paragraph 3(a) of the Act does not apply to the sale of a prescribed
consumer textile article where the article is made up for the use of an
individual or, if the article is a floor covering, cut for the use of an
individual if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the order for the article is placed,
  the individual has the opportunity to examine the textile fibre
  product, or a sample or swatch thereof, from which the article is to
  be made up or cut and the textile fibre product, sample or swatch is
  labelled in accordance with the Act and these Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information required to be shown on a label by
  the Act and these Regulations is shown on the bill of sale or on any
  other document delivered to the individual at the time the article is
  delivered to the individual.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-505, s. 1
- SOR/91-299, s. 3
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A dealer
  may import into Canada a prescribed consumer textile article that does
  not have a disclosure label applied to it if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he supplies to the inspector described in
    subsection (2),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on or before the date on which entry is made, the
      following information:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a statement that he has made or intends to make
        such an importation,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the date and place or the proposed date and
        place of such importation,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the nature and quantity of the article imported
        or to be imported, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the address of the premises where the article
        will be labelled in accordance with the Act and these
        Regulations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} such sample of the product as the inspector may
      reasonably request; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prior to resale of the article,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} he applies in the prescribed manner a disclosure
      label to the article, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} he notifies the inspector described in
      subsection (3) that the article has been labelled in accordance
      with the Act and these Regulations and affords the inspector a
      reasonable opportunity to inspect the article so labelled.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspector referred to in paragraph (1)(a) is the
  inspector located at the port of entry or, where there is no inspector
  located at that port, the inspector who is located nearest to such
  port.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The inspector referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii)
  is the inspector located at the place where the consumer textile
  article has been labelled or, where there is no inspector located at
  such place, the inspector who is located nearest thereto.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A prescribed consumer
textile article that is clearly identified by means of a label as a
second-hand article is exempt from the application of section 3 of the
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-505, s. 1
- SOR/94-247, s. 4
:::

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** The prohibition in
paragraph 3(a) of the Act does not apply to a sale by mail order of any
consumer textile article that is piece goods (including narrow fabrics)
if the information in respect of the article required by
paragraphs 11(1)(a) and (c) is shown in the prescribed manner, at the
time the article is ordered, in the mail order catalogue of the dealer
who sells the article.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-655, s. 1
- SOR/89-505, s. 1
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A consumer textile
article that contains filling, stuffing, padding or interlining for
which the only representation label that is required is provided in
accordance with the laws of a province is exempt from the application of
paragraph 3(b) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-505, s. 2
- SOR/94-247, s. 5

### [Information To Be Shown in Label]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528213 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  representation label that is required to meet the requirements for a
  disclosure label shall show

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the textile fibre content of the article in the
    manner prescribed in Part III;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections (2) and 12(1), the name and
    postal address of the dealer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where there is a representation that the article or
    any fabric or fibre therein is imported, the name of the country of
    origin, unless the representation is made in another label applied
    to the article and the name of the country of origin is shown in
    that other label.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(b) does not apply where a consumer
  textile article is a remnant and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the dealer who manufactured the fabric
    of which the article is a remnant is not known; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the article is sold or displayed for sale with
    other remnants in a manner described in section 18 and not all the
    remnants sold or displayed in such manner are remnants or fabric
    manufactured by the same dealer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information required by paragraph (1)(a) shall be
  shown in the label in both the English and French languages.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply where the consumer
  textile article is or is to be sold to the public in an area where
  only one of the official languages is in use continuously by the
  public for the purpose of making consumer purchases and the
  information required by paragraph (1)(a) is shown in that official
  language.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (4),
  [[area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the geographical area from which the store, shop or
    other trade outlet, in or by which a product is or is to be sold to
    the public, draws most of its customers; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the product is or is to be sold by mail
    order, the geographical area in which reside or are located all the
    persons who have, as part of their postal addresses, the name of the
    same city, town or place as has the consumer to whom the product is
    or is to be sold.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-247, s. 2
- SOR/94-247, s. 6
:::

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name under which the dealer carries on

      and residing in Canada, who is authorized by the dealer to make
      the application,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the address and telephone number of the
      dealer's place of business in Canada, or of the dealer's head
      office where that office is in Canada, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the dealer's postal address in Canada, where the
      address set out under subparagraph (iii) is not appropriate for
      postal delivery; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a fee of \$100.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall assign an identification
  number to a dealer if the dealer meets the requirements set out in
  subsections (1) and (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A dealer may use the identification number on the

  postal address if the information required by paragraph (2)(b) is
  up-to-date.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a dealer transfers the identification number to a
  subsequent dealer, the subsequent dealer may use the identification
  number in place of the subsequent dealer's name and postal address if

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} the subsequent dealer meets the requirements set out
    in subsection (1); and

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} the information required by paragraph 2(b) is
    up-to-date.

### [Manner in Which Information is To Be Shown in Label]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528251 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information that is required by paragraph 11(1)(a) and
  subsection 11(3) to be shown in a representation label shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly and prominently shown;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} easily legible;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in type of equal size and prominence; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 40, set apart from any graphic
    material in the label not required to be shown therein by these
    Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information that is required by
  paragraphs 11(1)(b) and (c) to be shown in a representation label
  shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clearly and prominently shown;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} easily legible; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set apart from any graphic material in the label
    not required to be shown therein by these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-247, s. 5
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information required by section 11 to be shown in a representation
  label that is applied to a consumer textile article, other than the
  information referred to in paragraph 11(1)(c), shall be shown

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} together in one place thereon, where it is
    imprinted on the consumer textile article itself; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsections (2), (3) and (4), in one
    label and on one side thereof, where it is shown on any material
    other than the consumer textile article.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the label referred to in subsection (1) is
  attached at one end to the consumer textile article so that both sides
  of the label are readily accessible, part of the information required
  by section 11 may be shown on one side and the remainder on the other.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where two representation labels are applied to any
  consumer textile article included in Schedule III,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information required by paragraph 11(1)(a) may
    be shown in English on one label and in French on the other label,
    and the information required by paragraph 11(1)(b) may be shown on
    either of the two labels; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the two labels are contiguous, any part of
    the information required by section 11 may be shown on one label and
    the remainder on the other label.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where two representation labels are applied to any
  article included in Schedule I and not included in Schedule III, part
  of the information required by section 11 may be shown on one label
  and the remainder on the other if the two labels are contiguous.

### [Form of Label and Manner of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528275 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  disclosure label shall be applied to a consumer textile article in
  such manner that the article will bear the label at the time that it
  is sold to the consumer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The disclosure label applied to a consumer textile
  article included in Schedule I and not included in Schedule III shall
  be of such material and applied in such a manner that it can be
  reasonably expected to withstand and be legible throughout
  10 cleanings of the article.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-247, s. 7
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A disclosure label shall
be applied

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the consumer textile article itself; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a manner permitted by section 17, 18, 19 or 20 or
  subsection 21(2) where the consumer textile article is one to which
  such application is permitted to be made; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a place readily accessible to a prospective
  purchaser.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where a consumer textile
article is sold in a pair or set and the textile fibre content of each
article in the pair or set

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the same, a disclosure label applied to one

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not the same, a disclosure label applied to one
  article in the pair or set showing the textile fibre content of each
  article separately

shall be, for the purpose of the Act and these Regulations, a disclosure
label applied to each article in the pair or set.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where the consumer

  the remnants in the bundle, bale or package, the information that is
  required by section 11 to be shown, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is displayed for sale together with other remnants
  having the same fibre content and a sign that surmounts or is in
  immediate proximity to such remnants shows, in the prescribed manner,
  in respect of all such remnants, the information that is required by
  section 11 to be shown,

such label or sign shall be, for the purpose of the Act and these
Regulations, a disclosure label applied to such consumer textile
article.

  article.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528319 .Part}

### [Advertising Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528320 .Subheading}

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any representation as to
the textile fibre content of a consumer textile article by a dealer in
advertising the article shall be made in accordance with sections 23 and
24 unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the consumer textile article is included in
  Schedule II or is not prescribed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement appears in a newspaper, magazine or
  other periodical that is published for and distributed to dealers; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the advertising is done by means other than written
  words, depiction, television or radio.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-247, s. 9

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by a dealer who manufactures or supplies the

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is or contains a textile fibre described in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing;

      the textile fibre,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the composition of the textile fibre, including
      any fibre-forming substances and the respective percentages
      thereof,

### [Textile Fibre Content]{.HTitleText2} {#h-528485 .Subheading}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The textile fibre content
of a consumer textile article or constituent part shall be shown in the
following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 32, each textile fibre that is
  present in the article or constituent part in an amount of five per
  cent or more by mass shall be shown

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by its generic name,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 31.1, in order of predominance
    by mass, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (d), section 31.01 and
    subsection 34(2), with the amount in which it is present preceding
    or immediately following the generic name of the fibre;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraphs (c) and (d), each textile fibre
  that is present in the article or constituent part in an amount less
  than five per cent by mass shall be shown

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the following manner, namely,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} as "other fibre",

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} immediately after the fibre or fibres shown in
      accordance with paragraph (a), and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection 34(2), with the amount in
      which it is present immediately preceding or following the words
      "other fibre", or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} as prescribed in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where there is more than one textile fibre present in
  the article or constituent part in an amount less than five per cent
  by mass, all such fibres that are not shown in a manner described in
  paragraph (a) shall be shown in the aggregate as "other fibres"

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} immediately after the fibre or fibres shown in
    accordance with paragraph (a), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 31.01 and subsection 34(2),
    with the aggregate amount immediately preceding or following the
    words "other fibres"; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the article or constituent part contains, as an
  integral part, ornamentation, an elastic yarn or a reinforcement yarn
  that comprises less than five per cent by mass of the article or
  constituent part, the textile fibre content of the article or
  constituent part may be shown exclusive of ornamentation, elastic yarn
  or reinforcement yarn if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the percentage by mass of each textile fibre
    present in the article or constituent part that is not part of the
    ornamentation, elastic yarn or reinforcement yarn is increased
    proportionately so that the total percentage of all such fibres
    equals 100 per cent, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the words "exclusive of ornamentation", "exclusive
    of elastic" or "exclusive of reinforcement", as the case may be, are
    shown immediately after the percentage of fibres referred to in
    subparagraph (i).

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/91-477, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[False or Misleading Representations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-528602 .Part}

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The use of the word

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "all" or "pure" in conjunction with the name of a
  textile fibre to describe a consumer textile article, a constituent
  part thereof or any other textile fibre product that contains a
  textile fibre other than the textile fibre named, unless such use is
  permitted by subsection 29(2), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "virgin" or "new" to describe a textile fibre that
  has been reclaimed,

- [(c) and (d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/94-247, s. 15\]]{.Repealed}

shall be deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to constitute a false or
misleading representation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-791, s. 3
- SOR/79-656, s. 4
- SOR/94-247, s. 15
:::

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The use of the expression

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "hand-knitted", "hand-crafted" or "hand-framed" to
  describe a textile fibre product that has been, in whole or in part,
  knitted by a device or a machine that was not wholly manually or
  pedally operated,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "hand-spun", "hand-woven" or "hand-crafted" to
  describe a textile fibre product that has been, in whole or in part,
  spun or woven by a device or machine that was not wholly manually or
  pedally operated, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "hand-printed" or "hand-crafted" to describe a
  textile fibre product that has been printed by means other than
  manually-operated screens, stencils or blocks

shall be deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to constitute a false or
misleading representation.

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** The use of the linear
measurements of a textile fibre product that has been cut to make a
consumer textile article as the dimensions of the finished article shall
be deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to constitute a false or
misleading representation.

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  use of any expression, word, depiction or symbol that signifies or
  connotes fur, hair or wool, or an animal that bears fur, hair or wool,
  to describe, or otherwise in relation to, any textile fibre product
  that is not made of or does not contain such fur, hair or wool without
  qualifying or explanatory words that make it clear that the product is
  not made of or does not contain such fur, hair or wool shall be
  deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to constitute a false or
  misleading representation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the textile fibre product or part
    thereof described in subsection (1) simulates fur, hair or wool; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} only where the fur, hair or wool signified is a
    fur, hair or wool that is used in or in place of textile fibre
    products of the same class or kind as the textile fibre product in
    respect of which the expression, word, depiction or symbol is used.

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The use of any
expression, word, depiction or symbol that signifies or connotes a bird
where the plumage referred to is not at least 90 per cent by weight from
that bird shall be deemed, unless the contrary is proven, to constitute
a false or misleading representation.
