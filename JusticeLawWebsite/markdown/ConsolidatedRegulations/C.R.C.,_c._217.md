## Ontario Milk Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-milk-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 217
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Certain Milk Producers in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536833 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Milk Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._217).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536835 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Milk
    Marketing Board established by Order of the Lieutenant-Governor in
    Council of Ontario pursuant to The Milk Act of Ontario; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means milk from cows;
    ([lait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer who sells milk to
    the Commodity Board; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quota in pounds of milk fixed
    and allotted by the Commodity Board to a producer in relation to the
    volume of sales of the Commodity Board of Classes 3, 4, 5 and 6 milk
    as established by Ontario Regulation 139/70.
    ([contingent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536841 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence
  fees set out in The Ontario Milk Marketing Plan, levies at the rate of
  \$1.20 for 100 pounds of milk or \$0.3429 per pound of butterfat sold
  by him to the Commodity Board that is not in excess of the quota fixed
  and allotted to him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in
  addition to the licence fees set out in the Ontario Milk Marketing
  Plan, levies at the rate of \$7 for each 100 pounds of milk or \$2 per
  pound of butterfat sold by him to the Commodity Board that is in
  excess of the quota fixed and allotted to him.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to a producer all levies payable by the producer to the
  Commodity Board under subsection (1) or (2).
