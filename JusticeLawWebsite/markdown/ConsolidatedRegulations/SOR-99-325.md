## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031739 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, "Act" means the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to a copy of a work
  is a reference to a copy of all or any substantial part of a work.

## [Newspaper or Periodical]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031743 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 30.2(6) of the Act, "newspaper or periodical" means a
newspaper or a periodical, other than a scholarly, scientific or
technical periodical, that was published more than one year before the
copy is made.

## [Records Kept Under Section 30.2 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031745 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In respect of activities
undertaken by a library, an archive or a museum under subsection 30.2(1)
of the Act, section 4 applies only to the reproduction of works.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a library, an archive or a museum, or a person
  acting under the authority of one, shall record the following
  information with respect to a copy of a work that is made under
  section 30.2 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the library, archive or museum making
    the copy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the request for a copy is made by a library,
    archive or museum on behalf of a person who is a patron of the
    library, archive or museum, the name of the library, archive or
    museum making the request;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date of the request; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} information that is sufficient to identify the
    work, such as

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the title,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the International Standard Book Number,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the International Standard Serial Number,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name of the newspaper, the periodical or the
      scholarly, scientific or technical periodical in which the work is
      found, if the work was published in a newspaper, a periodical or a
      scholarly, scientific or technical periodical,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date or volume and number of the newspaper or
      periodical, if the work was published in a newspaper or
      periodical,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the date or volume and number of the scholarly,
      scientific or technical periodical, if the work was published in a
      scholarly, scientific or technical periodical, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the numbers of the copied pages.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A library, an archive or a museum, or a person acting
  under the authority of one, does not have to record the information
  referred to in subsection (1) if the copy of the work is made under
  subsection 30.2(1) of the Act after December 31, 2003.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A library, an archive or a museum, or a person acting
  under the authority of one, shall keep the information referred to in
  subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by retaining the copy request form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other manner that is capable of reproducing
    the information in intelligible written form within a reasonable
    time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A library, an archive or a museum, or a person acting
  under the authority of one, shall keep the information referred to in
  subsection (1) with respect to copies made of a work for at least
  three years.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A library, an archive or a museum, or a person acting
  under the authority of one, shall make the information referred to in
  subsection (1), with respect to copies made of a work, available once
  a year to one of the following persons, on request made by the person
  in accordance with subsection (7):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the owner of copyright in the work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the representative of the owner of copyright in the
    work; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a collective society that is authorized by the
    owner of copyright in the work to grant licences on their behalf.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A library, an archive or a museum, or a person acting
  under the authority of one, shall make the information referred to in
  subsection (1) available to the person making the request, within 28
  days after the receipt of the request or any longer period that may be
  agreed to by both of them.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A request referred to in subsection (5) must be made
  in writing, indicate the name of the author of the work and the title
  of the work, and be signed by the person making the request and
  include a statement by that person indicating that the request is made
  under paragraph (5)(a), (b) or (c).

## [Patrons of Archives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031772 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person registers as a patron of an archive, the archive shall inform
  the patron in writing at the time of registration that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any copy of a work under section 30.21 of the Act
    is to be used solely for the purpose of research or private study;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any use of that copy for any other purpose may
    require the authorization of the copyright owner of the work.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person requests a copy of a work from an archive
  under section 30.21 of the Act and the person has not registered as a
  patron of the archive, the archive shall inform the person in writing
  at the time of the request

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that any copy is to be used solely for the purpose
    of research or private study; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any use of a copy for a purpose other than
    research or private study may require the authorization of the
    copyright owner of the work in question.

## [Stamping of Copied Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031782 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A library, archive or
museum, or a person acting under the authority of one, that makes a copy
of a work under section 30.2 or 30.21 of the Act shall inform the person
requesting the copy, by means of text printed on the copy or a stamp
applied to the copy, if the copy is in printed format, or by other
appropriate means, if the copy is made in another format,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the copy is to be used solely for the purpose of
  research or private study; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any use of the copy for a purpose other than
  research or private study may require the authorization of the
  copyright owner of the work in question.

## [Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031786 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An educational institution,
a library, an archive or a museum in respect of which subsection
30.3(2), (3) or (4) of the Act applies shall ensure that a notice that
contains at least the following information is affixed to, or within the
immediate vicinity of, every photocopier in a place and manner that is
readily visible and legible to persons using the photocopier:

**"WARNING!**

Works protected by copyright may be copied on this photocopier only if
authorized by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42) for the purpose
  of fair dealing or under specific exceptions set out in that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the copyright owner; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licence agreement between this institution and a
  collective society or a tariff, if any.

*For details of authorized copying, please consult the licence agreement
or the applicable tariff, if any, and other relevant information
available from a staff member.*

*The [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42) provides for civil and criminal
remedies for infringement of copyright*."

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031795 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on September 1, 1999.
