## National Energy Board Export and Import Reporting Regulations {#national-energy-board-export-and-import-reporting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-563
:::

[NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/N-7)

Registration 1995-11-28

Regulations Respecting the Returns and Information To Be Submitted by
Persons Exporting Oil, Gas or Electricity or Importing Gas

P.C. 1995-1992  1995-11-28
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to subsection
129(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-563_e_hq_6369){#fn_SOR-95-563_e_hq_6369-ID0EFBAA}
of the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed [Regulations respecting the returns and information
to be submitted by persons exporting oil, gas or electricity or
importing gas](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-563), made by the National Energy
Board on October 31, 1995.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976623 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Energy Board Export and Import Reporting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-563).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976625 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy Board
    Act](/eng/acts/N-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[border accommodation transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[border accommodation transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    transfer of power or energy for the purpose of providing electricity
    to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person in a foreign country who lacks ready
      access to services from a power system in that country,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an international work, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person in a foreign country who has lost
      service from a power system in that country as a result of an
      emergency; ([transfert en vue d'un service
      frontalier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electricity transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electricity transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a transfer of any
    of the following classes, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sale transfer, being a transfer of power or
      energy under a contract of sale,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an equichange transfer, being an interchange of
      equal quantities of power or energy within a stated period,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a storage transfer, being a transfer of energy
      banked for the time being in the form of water in a reservoir of
      another power system, with the expectation that equivalent energy
      will be returned at a later time,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an adjustment transfer, being a transfer of power
      or energy to adjust energy account balances or to compensate for
      services rendered, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a carrier transfer, being a transfer of power or
      energy wheeled from one power system, through the circuits of
      another power system that acts as a carrier, for delivery to a
      third party or to the original power system; ([transfert
      d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[energy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of energy in
    the form of electricity transmitted over a period of time, expressed
    in units of watt hours or multiples or sub-multiples of watt hours;
    ([énergie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued under Part VI
    of the Act; ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order authorizing the
    exportation, importation, exportation for subsequent importation or
    importation for subsequent exportation of gas or authorizing the
    exportation of oil issued by the Board under the National Energy
    Board Act Part VI (Oil and Gas) Regulations;
    ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit for the exportation of
    electricity issued under Part VI of the Act;
    ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rate of transferring
    electric energy, expressed in units of watts or multiples or
    sub-multiples of watts; ([puissance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[power system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[power system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the generating
    stations, transformers, switching stations, transmission lines,
    substations, distribution lines and circuits necessary for the
    production, transmission and distribution of electricity; ([réseau
    d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[refined petroleum products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[refined petroleum products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} oil recovered by the processing of oil sands,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} gasoline-type fuels for use in internal
      combustion engines,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} oil for use as a component in the blending of
      gasoline-type fuels referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} middle distillates, including the products
      commercially known as kerosene, stove oil, diesel fuel, furnace
      oil, diesel oil, gas oil, distillate heating oil, engine
      distillates and Nos. 1, 2, and 3 fuel oils,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} heavy fuels oils, including Nos. 4, 5 and 6 fuel
      oils, bunker "C" oil, "C" grade oil, residual fuel oil, heavy
      bunker oil, intermediate and thin bunker fuels and any blend of
      heavy fuel oils, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} partially processed oil, whether commingled with
      crude oil or equivalent hydrocarbons or not. ([produits pétroliers
      raffinés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-61, s. 1
:::

## [Keeping Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976653 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A copy of each return
required to be submitted to the Board pursuant to these Regulations
shall be kept by the person submitting the return for a period of three
years from the month to which the return relates.

## [Gas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976655 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/99-243, s. 1(E)

\]
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 and
6, every holder of a licence or an order for the exportation,
importation, exportation for subsequent importation or importation for
subsequent exportation of gas shall submit to the Board, on or before
the last day of each month, a return for the previous month that
contains, for each licence or order, the following information set out
by point of exportation or importation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence number or order number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity exported or imported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the highest quantity exported or imported in any one
  day during the month;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the average heating value of the gas exported or
  imported;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the value or price, at the international border, of
  all gas exported or imported, expressed in Canadian currency;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the export customer of the gas exported
  or the name of the seller of the gas imported;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the province in which the gas was produced for all
  gas exported and the country and state in which the gas was produced
  for all gas imported;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the transportation costs associated with the gas
  exported;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the exportation or importation of gas was
  firm or interruptible;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the geographical region within a country of
  destination to which gas was exported or within Canada for gas that
  was imported; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person who
  prepared the return.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-243, s. 2
:::

## [Propane and Butanes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976672 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of a licence
or an order for the exportation of propane or butanes shall submit to
the Board, on or before the last day of each month, a return for the
previous month that contains, for each licence and order, the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence number or order number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity exported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the export price of the propane and butanes at the
  point of loading or injection into a pipeline, expressed in Canadian
  currency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the province where the exportation occurs;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the country to which the propane or butanes were
  exported and the destination within the importing country;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mode of transport used in the exportation;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} information respecting

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the opening and closing inventory levels of the
    propane and butanes,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the supply sources of the propane and butanes,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the final disposition of the propane and butanes,
    and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} inter-provincial transfers of the propane and
    butanes; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person who
  prepared the return.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-243, s. 3
:::

## [Ethane]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976688 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of a licence
or an order for the exportation of ethane shall submit to the Board, on
or before the last day of each month, a return for the previous month
that contains, for each licence and order, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence number or order number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the province where the exportation occurs;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total revenue generated by the exportation
  calculated at the point of loading or injection into a pipeline,
  expressed in Canadian currency;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the destination of the exportation of the ethane;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mode of transport used in the exportation of the
  ethane; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person who
  prepared the return.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-243, s. 4
:::

## [Oil]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976699 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of a licence
or an order for the exportation of oil shall submit to the Board, on or
before the last day of each month, a return for the previous month that
contains, for each licence and order, the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence number or order number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of oil other than refined petroleum
  products,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the crude oil stream exported,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the consignee and destination of the oil within
    the importing country,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity exported,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the mode of transport used in the exportation,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the point of sale,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the export price of the oil at the point of sale,
    expressed in Canadian currency, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the marine freight cost of the cost, insurance
    and freight (CIF) sales, expressed in Canadian currency;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of refined petroleum products,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type of petroleum product exported,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity exported, expressed in cubic
    metres,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the export price at the point of loading or
    injection into a pipeline, expressed in Canadian currency,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the province where the exportation occurs,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the mode of transport used in the exportation, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the country to which the products were exported
    and the destination within the importing country; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person who
  prepared the return.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-243, s. 5
:::

## [Electricity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976720 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), every holder of a licence or permit for the
  exportation of electricity shall submit to the Board, on or before the
  15th day of each month, a return for the previous month that contains,
  for each licence or permit, the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantities and dollar value, in Canadian
    currency, of electricity exported, by customer, by type (firm or
    interruptible) and by class of electricity transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person who
    prepared the return.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit is issued authorizing, as a border
  accommodation transfer, the exportation of 1 000 kW or less of power
  to each customer served pursuant to the permit, the returns referred
  to in subsection (1) shall be submitted to the Board every six months.

## [Units of Measurement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976726 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations, all gas shall be measured in units of
  measurement that meet the requirements of the [Electricity and Gas
  Inspection Act](/eng/acts/E-4), and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of volume measurement, shall be
    expressed as the number of cubic metres the gas would occupy at the
    standard conditions, namely, at a temperature of 15°C and at an
    absolute pressure of 101.325 kPa; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of thermal measurement, shall be
    computed as the number of joules on a dry basis, where dry gas has a
    moisture content of less than 110 mg/m^3^.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where volume is measured under conditions of
  temperature and pressure other than the standard conditions described
  in paragraph (1)(a), the volume shall be converted to the equivalent
  under the standard conditions, in accordance with the Ideal Gas Laws,
  and shall be corrected for deviations from the Ideal Gas Laws in
  accordance with subsection (3), where the amount of the deviation
  exceeds one per cent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Correction for deviation from the Ideal Gas Laws
  shall be based on the tables published in American Gas Association
  (AGA) Report No. 3, Orifice Metering of Natural Gas and Other Related
  Hydrocarbon Fluids, as amended from time to time.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (1) to (3), propane,
  butanes and ethane may be measured in liquid form, in which case the
  volume measurement shall be computed in cubic metres.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the units of measurement of liquids, other than liquids
determined by the Board to be cryogenic liquids, shall be computed at a
temperature of 15°C.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, power and energy shall be measured in units of measurement
that meet the requirements of the [Electricity and Gas Inspection
Act](/eng/acts/E-4).
