Charges for Services Provided by the Office of the Superintendent of

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 23.1[[Footnote

Superintendent of Financial Institutions Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#c){#c-ID0EDDAA}, hereby makes the annexed [Charges
for Services Provided by the Office of the Superintendent of Financial
Institutions Regulations 2002](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-337).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694736 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The words and expressions
used in these Regulations have the same meaning as in the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), the Insurance Companies Act, and the [Trust and
Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), as the case may be.

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-694738 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The charge to be paid for
the consideration by the Superintendent of an application to obtain a
document described in column 1 of Schedule 1 or for any other service
described in that column, is the amount set out in column 2.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The charge to be paid for a
service described in column 1 of Schedule 2 is the amount set out in
column 2.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2006-74,
s. 1\]]{.Repealed}
