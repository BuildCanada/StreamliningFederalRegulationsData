## Post Office Savings Bank Regulations {#post-office-savings-bank-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"} 1955, p. 2561
:::

CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT

The Post Office Savings Bank Regulations
::::

:::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536275 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Post Office Savings Bank
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._19552561).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536277 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the accounting
  record kept at Head Office of all Bank transactions by a depositor
  with the Bank and of the interest earned by his deposits;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Post Office
  Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[Bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Post Office
  Savings Bank;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} [[deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a deposit of
  money by or on behalf of a depositor in the Bank;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} [[depositor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
  has an account in the Bank;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director
  of Accounting, Canada Post Corporation;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} [[Head Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Central
  Office of the Bank at Ottawa;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} [[pass-book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the book
  issued to a depositor in which a depositor's deposits, withdrawals and
  interest are recorded; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} [[withdrawal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a withdrawal
  of money from the Bank by a depositor;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

and other terms and expressions in these Regulations have the meaning
ascribed to them in the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 1
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 54, s. 65
:::

## [Operation and Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536293 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall
administer and control the activities, transactions and operation of the
Bank and shall maintain the records of accounts thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 2
:::

## [Powers and Business of the Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536297 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Director shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), allow the closing of any
  account upon demand from the depositor or any person legally
  authorized to claim on his behalf;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer to the Receiver General, at the end of each
  fiscal year, unclaimed balances of accounts of less than \$25 in
  respect of which, for a period of thirty years, no transaction has
  taken place and no statement of account has been requested by or on
  behalf of the depositor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintain, indefinitely, accounts of \$25 and over;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} carry out any operation incidental to the actions
  referred to paragraphs (a), (b) and (c).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 3
:::

**[[5 to 9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5_to_9 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/71-176, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

## [Hours of Business]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536306 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The hours of business of
the Bank shall be the hours of business of the Canada Post Corporation
and such exceptional hours as may be prescribed by the Director.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 54, s. 65
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176,
s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

## [Classes of Deposit Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536311 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  the Director shall maintain accounts as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "Individual Personal Accounts" in the names of
    individual depositors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Trust Accounts" in the name of one or more persons
    in trust for some specified person or persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Joint Accounts --- All to Withdraw" in which two
    or more persons have made deposits in their joint names;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} "Joint Accounts --- Any to Withdraw" in which
    deposits have been made in the joint names of two or more persons;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} "Incorporated or Unincorporated Societies'
    Accounts" in which clubs, schools, churches, savings or credit
    unions, cemetery companies, estates or provident or charitable
    institutions have made deposits.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every application for withdrawal from an account
  described in paragraph (c) of subsection (1) shall be made jointly by
  all the persons in whose names the account stands.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every application for withdrawal from an account
  described in paragraph (d) of subsection (1) may be made by any of the
  persons in whose names the account stands.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} At the death of any of the persons in whose names an
  account described in paragraph (c) or (d) of subsection (1) stands,
  control of the account shall be transferred to the survivor or
  survivors upon production of the pass-book together with evidence,
  satisfactory to the Director, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death of the deceased joint depositor, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the survivor or survivors.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/58-282
- SOR/71-176, s. 6
:::

## [Destruction of Old Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536326 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the Director may destroy books, records, documents, vouchers, paid
  instruments and papers relating to any account where they are dated,
  or were in existence or contain entries or writings made more than
  fifteen years prior to their destruction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every signature declaration and signing authority
  relating to any account of which the balance is less than \$25 and the
  Bank's records showing such a balance shall be retained until the
  balance is transferred to the Receiver General pursuant to
  paragraph (b) of section 4.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Director shall not destroy any signature
  declaration or signing authority relating to an account of which the
  balance is \$25 or more or the Bank's records showing such a balance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 6
:::

**[[14 and 15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14_and_15
.sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

## [Change in Name of Depositor]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536334 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** When any change occurs in
a depositor's name he shall notify the change to the Director and submit
such evidence of change of name as the Director may require.

## [Pass-Books]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536336 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  depositor shall, at the request of the Director produce for his
  inspection any pass-book issued to that depositor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A depositor shall, when requesting to close his
  account, send to the Director the pass-book issued to him in respect
  of the account.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of loss of a pass-book, the depositor
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Director without delay of the loss,
    stating where and approximately when the first and last deposits and
    the last withdrawal were made, and giving as much information as
    possible concerning the account and the circumstances of the loss;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sign an undertaking to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} return to the Director the lost pass-book if it
      should be recovered, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} indemnify the Canada Post Corporation against
      any misuse of the lost pass-book.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 7
- 1980-81-82-83, c. 54, s. 65
:::

## [Withdrawal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536347 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  withdrawal may only be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the Head Office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on presentation of a passbook or such other
    evidence of the right to make the withdrawal as may be acceptable to
    the Director.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 8
:::

## [Closing of Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536354 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** An account shall be
closed upon the withdrawal of the full amount of the account.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 8
:::

## [Withdrawals from 'Individual Personal Accounts' and 'Trust Accounts' of Deceased Depositors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536358 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  wife or husband of a deceased depositor whose deposit did not exceed
  \$50 at the date of the depositor's death may withdraw the deposit
  upon the production to the Director of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a signed request for the deposit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deceased depositor's pass-book or a declaration
    of its loss and an undertaking to surrender it, if and when the same
    is recovered, and to indemnify the Bank against any misuse of the
    pass-book;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof satisfactory to the Director of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the depositor's death; and of

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} marital relationship.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person other than the persons specified in
  subsection (3) may withdraw a deposit of a deceased depositor if such
  deposit did not exceed \$500 at the date of the depositor's death,
  upon the production to the Director of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a signed request for the deposit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deceased depositor's pass-book or a declaration
    of its loss and an undertaking to surrender it, if and when the same
    is recovered, and to indemnify the Bank against any misuse of the
    pass-book;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof satisfactory to the Director of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the depositor's death; and of

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the claimant's legal authority to claim the
      deposit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an authenticated or notarial copy of the last will
    of the deceased depositor, if any;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a written statement setting forth that it is not
    proposed to apply for Letters of Probate of the deceased depositor's
    last will, or for Letters of Administration of his estate, or for an
    Act of Curatorship or Tutorship, as the case may be;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statutory declaration and indemnity agreement
    executed in the form and manner prescribed by the Director with
    releases of all claims in respect of the deposit signed by all other
    beneficiaries named in the last will or by all other persons
    entitled to share in the estate on intestacy where there is no will;
    and of

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a bond of indemnity with two sureties for twice the
    amount of the deposit and accrued interest thereon where the
    Director considers it advisable that such further safeguard be
    provided.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The wife, husband, child, father, mother, brother or
  sister of a deceased depositor may withdraw his deposit if such
  deposit did not exceed \$2,500 at the date of the depositor's death
  upon the production to the Director of the documents mentioned in
  paragraphs (a) to (g) of subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The executor, administrator, trustee, tutor or
  curator of the estate of a deceased depositor may withdraw the
  depositor's deposit upon the production to the Director of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a signed request for the deposit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deceased depositor's pass-book or a declaration
    of its loss and and undertaking to surrender it, if and when the
    same is recovered, and to indemnify the Bank against any misuse of
    the pass-book;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof satisfactory to the Director of the
    depositor's death; and of

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} such of the documents specified in subsection (5)
    as may be applicable to the case.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The executor, administrator, trustee, tutor or
  curator, as the case may be, of a deceased depositor's estate, shall
  produce to the Director such of the following documents as the
  Director may require, and any such document may be retained by the
  Director:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an authenticated or notarial copy of the probate of
    the will of the deceased depositor, or Letters of Administration of
    his estate, or of the Act of Curatorship or Tutorship, granted by
    any court in Canada having power to grant the same;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an authentic notarial copy of the will of the
    deceased depositor, if such will is in notarial form according to
    the law of the Province of Quebec; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the deceased depositor died outside of Canada,
    an authenticated or notarial copy of the Probate of his will, or of
    Letters of Administration of his property, or other documents of
    like import satisfactory to the Director, granted by any court or
    authority having the requisite power in such matters;

  and the production of such document or documents shall be sufficient
  justification and authority to the Director for paying such deposit to
  the executor, administrator, trustee, tutor or curator, as the case
  may be.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A deceased depositor's deposits withdrawn under the
  provision of this section

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} may, in any of the cases provided for by
    subsections (1), (2) or (3), be paid by cheque payable to the person
    or persons legally authorized to claim for the same; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall, in the case of subsection (4), be paid by
    cheque payable to the estate of the deceased depositor.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Director may apply the provisions of
  subsections (1) to (6) for the disposal of a deceased depositor's
  deposit notwithstanding the legitimacy or illegitimacy of such
  deceased depositor or of his heirs or of any one of them.

## [Deposits of Incapacitated Depositors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536391 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where a depositor becomes
insane or otherwise incapacitated to act, and such incapacity is proved
to the satisfaction of the Director, and he is satisfied of the urgency
of the case, he may authorize payment, from time to time, out of the
deposits of such depositor, to any person whom he shall judge proper,
and the receipt of such person shall be a good discharge of the same.

## [Persons of Unsound Mind]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536393 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If any
  depositor is of unsound mind or under any other legal disability,
  anything which, under these regulations, is required or authorized to
  be done by or to the depositor shall or may be done by or to the
  committee or other person empowered by law to administer his estate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176, s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The prescribed identification information shall be
  given by such committee or other person who shall specify the capacity
  in which it or he acts.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any application for the withdrawal of the deposits
  shall be made by such committee or other person.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a depositor is of unsound mind and no committee
  or other person empowered by law to administer his estate has been
  appointed, the Director may, when it is proved to his satisfaction
  that it is just and expedient so to do, pay the deposits standing in
  the name of the depositor, or any part thereof, to any person whom he
  shall judge proper to receive the same.

## [Evidence of Identity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536400 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in these Regulations shall
  prejudice the right of the Director to require evidence to be given to
  his satisfaction

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of the identity of any person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title or authority of any person to withdraw
    any deposits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that anything purporting to be done pursuant to
    these Regulations has been duly done; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any other matters on which the due
    exercise of his powers or performance of his duties under these
    Regulations depends.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director may, for the purpose of obtaining any
  evidence referred to in subsection (1), require a statutory
  declaration to be made by any person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/71-176, s. 11
:::

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/71-176,
s. 11\]]{.Repealed}

## [Service Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536411 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Director may charge,
for the keeping of any depositor's account, service charges at such
rates as may be approved by the Canada Post Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-843, s. 1
:::

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/81-843,
s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Application of Regulations to Existing Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536416 .Part}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the provisions
of these regulations, these regulations shall apply to all sums standing
to the credit of any account open in the Bank, provided that anything
had or done by or in accordance with the directions of the Postmaster
General or the Director with respect to deposits made before the coming
into force of these regulations, if had or done in accordance with the
regulations hereby repealed, shall be deemed to have been lawfully had
or done notwithstanding that the provisions of these regulations were
not complied with.
