## Alberta Hog Marketing Levies Order {#alberta-hog-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-190
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1984-02-21

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Hog Producers in the Province of Alberta
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Alberta Pork Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 4 of
the [Alberta Hog Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._130), C.R.C.,
c. 130, hereby makes the annexed [Order providing for the fixing,
imposing and collecting of levies from hog producers in the Province of
Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-190).

Edmonton, Alberta, January 26, 1984
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882157 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Hog Marketing Levies Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-190).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882159 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Marketing of Agricultural
    Products Act of Alberta; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Pork Producers
    Development Corporation established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofia L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} produced in Alberta and known as
    domestic pig; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of hogs in the Province of Alberta.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-573, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882167 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Board, in respect of any hogs sold by the producer in
interprovincial or export trade, a levy in the amount of:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1.00 per hog for every hog that is 50 kg or more
  live weight; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.25 per hog for every hog that is less than 50 kg
  live weight.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-61, s. 1
- SOR/97-573, s. 2
- SOR/2005-331, s. 1
- SOR/2006-170, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882173 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay
the levy described in section 3 to the Board at its office in Edmonton,
Alberta, not later than the 15th day of the month following the month in
which the hogs were sold.
