## Alexander First Nation Treaty Land Entitlement Remission Order {#alexander-first-nation-treaty-land-entitlement-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-124
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2003-07-02

Alexander First Nation Treaty Land Entitlement Remission Order

P.C. 2003-913  2003-06-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed [Alexander
First Nation Treaty Land Entitlement Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2003-124).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607037 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Alexander]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Alexander]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alexander Indian Band
      No. 438, also known as Alexander First Nation.
      ([Alexander]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[settlement agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alexander
      First Nation Treaty Land Entitlement Settlement Agreement executed
      on October 13, 1998, by Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada
      and executed on September 3, 1998, by Alexander. ([accord de
      règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[settlement land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land, and any mines
      and minerals contained in or under it, purchased or acquired by or
      on behalf of Alexander in accordance with the settlement
      agreement. ([terre désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[third party interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[third party interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
      assigned by Article 1.1(cc) of the settlement agreement. ([intérêt
      de tierce partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions not otherwise defined
  in subsection (1) have the same meaning as in subsection 123(1) of the
  Act.

## [Remission of Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607046 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to Alexander of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable under Division II of Part IX
  of the Act on the value of consideration paid or payable by Alexander
  or its agent on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the supply to Alexander or its agent of an amount
    of settlement land not exceeding a cumulative total of 10,000 acres,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the supply to or cancellation in favour of
    Alexander or its agent of a third party interest in settlement land,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the supply to Alexander or its agent of tangible
    personal property that is situated on settlement land at the time
    Alexander or its agent acquires an interest in that land, provided
    that title in the tangible personal property is transferred to
    Alexander or its agent, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} costs incurred by Alexander or its agent in the
    context of any of the transactions described in subparagraphs (i) to
    (iii); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest and penalties paid or payable by Alexander
  or its agent under Part IX of the Act in respect of any transaction
  described in paragraph (a).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-607054 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax, interest and penalties paid or payable under
  Part IX of the Act have not otherwise been rebated, credited, refunded
  or remitted to any person under the Act or the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for the remission is made in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of tax, interest and penalties paid
    before the date of the coming into force of this Order, within two
    years after that date, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of tax, interest and penalties paid on
    or after the date of the coming into force of this Order, within two
    years after the day the tax, interest and penalties were paid; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development
  confirms in writing that the claim for remission is being made with
  respect to settlement land.
