## Order Transferring from the Minister of Finance to the Minister of Industry, the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to Regional Economic Development in Quebec and the Control and Supervision of the Economic Development Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-finance-to-the-minister-of-industry-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-regional-economic-development-in-quebec-and-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-economic-development-agency-of-canada-for-the-regions-of-quebec .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/96-16
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1996-02-07

Order Transferring from the Minister of Finance to the Minister of
Industry, the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to Regional
Economic Development in Quebec and the Control and Supervision of the
Economic Development Agency of Canada for the Regions of Quebec

P.C. 1996-121  1996-01-25
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer from the Minister of Finance to the Minister of
Industry

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the powers, duties and functions referred to in
  paragraph (a) of Order in Council P.C. 1993-1907 of November 4,
  1993[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6555){#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6555-ID0EDCBBA}
  and in Order in Council P.C. 1995-537 of March 29, 1995[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6556){#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6556-ID0EBCBBA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6555-ID0EDCBBA){#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6555}SI/93-206
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6556-ID0EBCBBA){#fn_SI-96-16_e_hq_6556}SI/95-49
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the control and supervision of that portion of the
  public service known as the Economic Development Agency of Canada for
  the Regions of Quebec.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/98-32.
