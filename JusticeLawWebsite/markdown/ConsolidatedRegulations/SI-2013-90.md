## Order Transferring from the Minister of Health to the Minister of the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency the Control and Supervision of the portion of the federal public administration known as the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency and Ordering the Minister of the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency to Preside over the Agency {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-health-to-the-minister-of-the-canadian-northern-economic-development-agency-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-known-as-the-canadian-northern-economic-development-agency-and-ordering-the-minister-of-the-canadian-northern-economic-development-agency-to-preside-over-the-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-90
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-07-31

Order Transferring from the Minister of Health to the Minister of the
Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency the Control and
Supervision of the portion of the federal public administration known as
the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency and Ordering the
Minister of the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency to Preside
over the Agency

P.C. 2013-872 2013-07-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, effective July 15, 2013,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECBAA}
  of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCBAA},
  transfers to the Minister of the Canadian Northern Economic
  Development Agency from the Minister of Health the control and
  supervision of that portion of the federal public administration known
  as the Canadian Northern Economic Development Agency; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECBAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7867}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCBAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7868}R.S., c.
    P-34
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of the Canadian Northern
  Economic Development Agency shall preside over the Canadian Northern
  Economic Development Agency.
:::
