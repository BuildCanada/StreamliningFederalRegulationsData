## Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees (Pleasure Craft) {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-transport-to-prescribe-fees-pleasure-craft .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/94-57
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-05-04

Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees (Pleasure
Craft)

P.C. 1994-642 1994-04-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph
19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_94-57_EN_hq_20089){#fn_SI_94-57_EN_hq_20089-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of Transport to prescribe, by order, the
charges to be paid in respect of pleasure craft for the issue of plates
pertaining to the recommended safe limits of engine power and
recommended gross load capacity and for the issue of notices pertaining
to compliance with construction standards.
