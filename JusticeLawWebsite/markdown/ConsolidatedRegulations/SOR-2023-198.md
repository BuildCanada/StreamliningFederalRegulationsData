## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424268 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[CARICOM]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CARICOM]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Caribbean Community
    established under the Treaty of Chaguaramas, signed at Chaguaramas,
    Trinidad and Tobago on July 4, 1973, including the Secretariat
    referred to in Article 23 of that
    treaty. ([CARICOM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on October 14, 2023 and ending on October 22, 2023. ([période
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Summit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Summit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada-CARICOM Summit to be
    held in Ottawa from October 17 to 19,
    2023. ([Sommet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424275 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CARICOM

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the relevant period, CARICOM has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the legal capacities of a corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the extent necessary for the exercise of its
    functions in relation to the Summit, the privileges and immunities
    set out in sections 2 to 5 of Article II and Article III of the
    Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives of foreign states

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, representatives of a
  foreign state who are official delegates to the Summit have, to the
  extent required for the exercise of their functions in relation to the
  Summit, the privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs 11(a)
  to (f) and section 12 of Article IV of the Convention, to the extent
  set out in sections 14 to 16 of Article IV of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior officials

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, senior officials of
  CARICOM have, to the extent required for the exercise of their
  functions in relation to the Summit, the privileges and immunities
  comparable to the privileges and immunities accorded to diplomatic
  agents under the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations, other than
  duty and tax relief privileges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other officials

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, other officials of
  CARICOM and officials of other international organizations who have
  been invited by Canada to the Summit have, to the extent required for
  the exercise of their functions in relation to the Summit, the
  privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs 18(a), (d) and (e) of
  Article V of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, experts invited by Canada
  to the Summit have, to the extent required for the exercise of their
  functions in relation to the Summit, the privileges and immunities set
  out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424289 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
