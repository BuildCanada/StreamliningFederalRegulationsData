## Remission Order in Respect of Fees for Claims Filed under the Hazardous Materials Information Review Act for Exemption from the Requirement to Disclose Confidential Business Information {#remission-order-in-respect-of-fees-for-claims-filed-under-the-hazardous-materials-information-review-act-for-exemption-from-the-requirement-to-disclose-confidential-business-information .Title-of-Act}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Chief Screening Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief Screening Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 10(1) of the [Hazardous Materials
      Information Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7). ([agent de contrôle en
      chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazardous ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazardous ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in subsection 1(1) of the [Hazardous Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17). ([ingrédient
      dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazardous product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazardous product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Hazardous Products
      Act](/eng/acts/H-3). ([produit dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Hazardous Products
      Act](/eng/acts/H-3). ([fiche de données de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Hazardous Products
      Act](/eng/acts/H-3). ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of meanings in Acts and
  Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions used in this Order
  have the same meaning as in the [Hazardous Products
  Act](/eng/acts/H-3), the [Hazardous Materials Information Review
  Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7), the [Hazardous Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17) or the [Hazardous Materials
  Information Review Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-456), as
  applicable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- fee for claim for exemption

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted to a supplier of an amount
  equal to the fee required under subsection 11(3) of the [Hazardous
  Materials Information Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7) to accompany a
  claim filed by the supplier under that Act for an exemption from the
  requirement to disclose, on the safety data sheet of a hazardous
  product, confidential business information relating to the
  concentration or concentration range of a hazardous ingredient in the
  hazardous product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of an amount of up to \$850,000,
  which represents the total maximum amount to be repaid to all the
  affected suppliers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the supplier is subject to the [Hazardous Products
  Act](/eng/acts/H-3) with respect to the hazardous products that it
  sells in or imports into Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claim for exemption relates to the requirement
  that the supplier disclose confidential business information relating
  to the actual concentration or concentration range of a hazardous
  ingredient in the hazardous product;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the supplier filed the claim for exemption in
  accordance with the [Hazardous Materials Information Review
  Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7) and the [Hazardous Materials Information Review
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-456) on or after February 11,
  2015, but before April 18, 2018, and paid the fee required;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on or before December 14, 2018, the supplier

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} informed the Chief Screening Officer in writing
    that with respect to the requirement to provide on the safety data
    sheet the concentration or concentration range of the hazardous
    ingredient in the hazardous product, it intends to provide one of
    the concentration ranges that is set out in the [Hazardous Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17), as they read on April
    18, 2018; and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} confirmed in writing that it is withdrawing the
    claim for exemption that is referred to in paragraphs (b) and (c)
    and on which a decision has not yet been rendered; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the time of repayment of the amount remitted, the
  information provided by the supplier in the claim for exemption is
  valid and continues to meet the requirements of the [Hazardous
  Materials Information Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7) and the [Hazardous
  Materials Information Review Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-456)
  with respect to the information that must be contained in a claim for
  exemption from the requirement to disclose confidential business
  information on the safety data sheet of a hazardous product.
