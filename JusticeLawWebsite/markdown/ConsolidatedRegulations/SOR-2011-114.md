## Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-syria-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-114
:::

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2011-05-24

Special Economic Measures (Syria) Regulations

P.C. 2011-594 2011-05-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
Syria constitutes a grave breach of international peace and security
that has resulted or is likely to result in a serious international
crisis;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Special Economic Measures (Syria)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-114).

- ::: Footnote

  1992, c. 17

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771498 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is in
    Syria, or is or was a national of Syria who does not ordinarily
    reside in Canada, and whose name is listed in Schedule 1. ([personne
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[luxury goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[luxury goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods such as jewellery,
    gems, precious metals, watches, cigarettes, alcoholic beverages,
    perfume, designer clothing and accessories, furs, sporting goods,
    private aircraft, gourmet foods and ingredients, lobster, computers,
    televisions and other electronic devices. ([articles de
    luxe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Syria]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Syria]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Syrian Arab Republic and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any political subdivision of Syria;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government, and any department, of Syria or
      of a political subdivision of Syria; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agency of Syria or of a political subdivision
      of Syria. ([Syrie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771509 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in Schedule 1 is a person in respect of whom the Governor in
Council, on the recommendation of the Minister, is satisfied that there
are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former or current senior official of the Government
  of Syria;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an associate or family member of a person set out in
  paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf
  of, a person set out in paragraph (a) or (b); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned or controlled by, or acting on behalf
  of, the Government of Syria.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771517 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3.2, it
is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, held by or
  on behalf of a designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make any goods, wherever situated, available to a
  designated person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related service to or for
  the benefit of a designated person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-220, s. 1
- SOR/2011-330, s. 1

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3.2,
it is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside
Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} import, purchase, acquire, carry or ship any goods
  --- other than food for human consumption --- that are exported,
  supplied or shipped from Syria after December 23, 2011;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire financial or other related
  services to, from or for the benefit of or on the direction or order
  of Syria or any person in Syria for the purpose of facilitating the
  importation, purchase, acquisition, carriage or shipment of any
  petroleum or petroleum products, excluding natural gas, from Syria;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make an investment in Syria if that investment
  involves a dealing in any property, wherever situated, held by or on
  behalf of Syria, a person in Syria or a national of Syria who does not
  ordinarily reside in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire financial or other related
  services to, from or for the benefit of or on the direction or order
  of Syria or any person in Syria for the purpose of making an
  investment described in paragraph (c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} export, sell, supply or ship to Syria or any person
  in Syria any goods, including technical data, for use in the
  monitoring of telecommunications;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire financial or other related
  services --- other than those whose provision or acquisition is
  otherwise prohibited by these Regulations --- to, from or for the
  benefit of or on the direction or order of Syria or any person in
  Syria;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} export, sell, supply or ship to Syria or any person
  in Syria any luxury goods;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} export, sell, supply or ship to Syria or any person
  in Syria any of the goods set out in Schedule 2; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} transfer, provide or communicate to Syria or any
  person in Syria technical data related to any of the goods set out in
  Schedule 2.

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** The prohibitions set
out in sections 3 and 3.1 do not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any activity engaged in under an agreement or
  arrangement between Canada and Syria;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any payment made by or on behalf of a designated
  person that is due under a contract entered into before the person
  became a designated person, provided that the payment is not made to
  or for the benefit of a designated person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any goods made available, or services provided, to or
  by any of the following entities for the purpose of safeguarding human
  life, disaster relief, democratization, stabilization or providing
  food, medicine or medical supplies or equipment:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an international organization with diplomatic
    status,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a United Nations agency,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the International Red Cross and Red Crescent
    Movement, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a non-governmental organization that has entered
    into a grant or contribution agreement with the Department of
    Foreign Affairs and International Trade or the Canadian
    International Development Agency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the importation, purchase, acquisition, carrying or
  shipping of petroleum or petroleum products that were exported,
  supplied or shipped from Syria before October 5, 2011;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any goods from the Embassy of Canada in Syria
  destined for Canada or any goods from Syria destined for the Embassy
  of Syria in Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} personal or settlers' effects that are taken or
  shipped by an individual leaving Syria and that are solely for the use
  of the individual or the individual's immediate family;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} personal correspondence, including lettermail,
  printed papers and postcards, of a weight not exceeding 250 g per item
  of correspondence;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} pension payments to any person in Canada, any
  Canadian abroad or any person in Syria;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of the accounts at a
  Canadian financial institution that are used for the regular business
  of the Embassy of Syria or its consular missions in Canada;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of the accounts at a
  Syrian financial institution that are used for the regular business of
  the Embassy of Canada or its consular missions in Syria; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any transactions necessary for a Canadian to transfer
  any existing accounts, funds or investments of a Canadian held with a
  designated person to a non-designated person.

**[[3.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the
matters referred to in section 3.2, the prohibition set out in paragraph
3.1(f) does not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} loan repayments to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian abroad, in respect of loans entered into before March 5,
  2012, enforcement of security in respect of those loans, or payments
  by guarantors guaranteeing those loans;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} financial services that are required to be provided
  or acquired further to a contract entered into before March 5, 2012;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} financial services in respect of non-commercial
  remittances of \$40,000 or less sent to or from Syria, or any person
  in Syria, if the person providing the financial services keeps a
  record of the transaction.

- **[[3.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} import, purchase, acquire, carry or ship any
    chemical weapons and equipment, goods or technology related to
    chemical weapons from Syria, wherever situated, whether held by or
    on behalf of Syria or any person in Syria; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer, acquire or purchase from Syria or any
    person in Syria technical assistance that is related to any of the
    chemical weapons and equipment, goods or technology set out in
    paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to Her Majesty in right
  of Canada or a province if the Security Council of the United Nations
  has authorized, for the purpose of ensuring the elimination of Syria's
  chemical weapons program, a member state of the United Nations to
  acquire, control, transport, transfer and destroy chemical weapons
  identified by the Director-General of the Organisation for the
  Prohibition of Chemical Weapons.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 3.1(a) does not apply after February 3,
  2014 to any of the goods set out in paragraph 3.4(1)(a).

## [Duty to Determine]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771573 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Each of the following
entities must determine on a continuing basis whether it is in
possession or control of property owned or controlled by or on behalf of
a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada or banks to which that Act
  applies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities engaged in the business of insuring risks that are
  regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

## [Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771585 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada and every Canadian outside Canada must disclose
  without delay to the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned or controlled,
    directly or indirectly, by a designated person or by an entity owned
    or controlled by a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [Special Economic Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person
  for a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Application to No Longer Be a Designated Person]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771591 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated person may apply in writing to the Minister to have their
  name removed from Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the application, the Minister must
  decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend to the
  Governor in Council that the applicant's name be removed from Schedule
  1.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must make a decision on the application
  within 90 days after the day on which the application is received.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give notice without delay to the
  applicant of the decision taken.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If there has been a material change in circumstances
  since the last application was submitted, a person may submit another
  application under subsection (1).

- SOR/2012-145, s. 5

## [Application for a Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-771600 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  claiming not to be a designated person may apply to the Minister for a
  certificate stating that they are not the person who has been
  designated under section 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established that the person is not a
  designated person, the Minister must issue a certificate to the
  applicant within 30 days after the day on which the application is
  received.
