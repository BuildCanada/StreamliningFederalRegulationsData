## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Official Languages) to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Official Languages Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-official-languages-to-the-minister-of-canadian-heritage-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-official-languages-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Official Languages) to
the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Powers, Duties and Functions Under
the Official Languages Act

P.C. 2025-421 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the powers, duties and
functions under the [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to the Minister of State (Official Languages) by
Order in Council P.C. 2021-946 of October 26, 2021[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078){#fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078-ID0EBEAA}.
