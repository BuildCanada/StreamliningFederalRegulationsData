## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1125937 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Transportation of Dangerous
      Goods Act, 1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[handling site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[handling site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility connected
      to a railway line where a railway vehicle is placed for the
      loading or unloading of dangerous goods. ([lieu de
      manutention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[railway carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[railway carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has
      possession of dangerous goods for the purposes of transportation
      by railway vehicle on a main railway line, or for the purposes of
      storing them in the course of such transportation. ([transporteur
      ferroviaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[railway loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[railway loader]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person that operates a handling site, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any manufacturer or producer of dangerous goods
        that has possession of dangerous goods at a handling site for
        the purposes of loading them prior to, or unloading them after,
        transportation by rail. ([chargeur ferroviaire]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terminology --- [Transportation of
  Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, all other
  words and expressions used in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in section 1.4 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Rail Security Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1125945 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objective

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out security
requirements for the purposes of section 5 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Potential threats and other security
concerns

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway carrier must immediately report any potential threat or other
  security concern by telephone to the Transport Canada Situation
  Centre. Potential threats and other security concerns include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any interference with a train crew;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any bomb threats, either specific or non-specific;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any reports or discoveries of suspicious items when
    the report or discovery results in a disruption of railway
    operations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any suspicious activities observed on or near a
    railway vehicle, at or near infrastructure used in railway
    operations or at or near a facility or location used in railway
    operations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the discovery, seizure or discharge of a firearm or
    other weapon on or near a railway vehicle, at or near infrastructure
    used in railway operations or at or near a facility or location used
    in railway operations;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any signs of tampering with a railway vehicle if
    the railway carrier determines that security has been compromised;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any information relating to the possible
    surveillance of a railway vehicle, of infrastructure used in railway
    operations or of a facility or location used in railway operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include, if applicable and to the
  extent known, the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the railway carrier's name and contact information,
    including telephone number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who is making the report on
    behalf of the railway carrier and the person's title and contact
    information, including their telephone number and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information that identifies any train that is
    affected by the potential threat or other security concern,
    including its itinerary and line or route position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any information that identifies any railway
    vehicle, infrastructure, facility or location that is affected by
    the potential threat or other security concern;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the classification and quantity of any dangerous
    goods that are involved in the potential threat or other security
    concern; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the potential threat or other
    security concern, including the date and time that the railway
    carrier became aware of it and the date and time of any incident
    linked to it.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Rail Security Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1125966 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1125967 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objective

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out security
requirements for the purposes of section 5 of the Act.

### [Coordinator]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1125970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rail security coordinator

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway carrier must, at all times, have an employee designated as a
  rail security coordinator or an acting rail security coordinator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier must provide the Minister with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and job title of the rail security
    coordinator or acting rail security coordinator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 24-hour contact information for the rail security
    coordinator or acting rail security coordinator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duties

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A railway carrier must
ensure that the rail security coordinator or acting rail security
coordinator

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} coordinates security matters within the railway
  carrier's organization; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acts as the principal contact between the railway
  carrier, law enforcement and emergency response agencies and the
  Minister with respect to security matters.

### [Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1125982 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security inspection --- railway vehicle
accepted

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  railway carrier accepts a railway vehicle that contains dangerous
  goods for transport in a train and a placard is required under Part 4
  of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286), the railway carrier must
  carry out a visual security inspection of the railway vehicle when it
  is accepted for transport and when it is placed in the train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security inspection --- dangerous goods
  accepted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a railway carrier accepts dangerous goods for
  transport in a railway vehicle in a train and a placard is required
  under Part 4 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286), the railway carrier must
  carry out a visual security inspection of the railway vehicle when it
  is placed in the train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tampering or suspicious items

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a railway carrier that is carrying out an
  inspection under subsection (1) or (2) discovers signs of tampering or
  a suspicious item, the railway carrier must take measures to determine
  whether security has been compromised.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compromise of security

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the railway carrier determines that security has
  been compromised, the railway carrier must take measures to address
  the situation before transporting the dangerous goods.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Security Plan and Training]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1125992 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1125993 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed persons

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A railway carrier, or a
person who is employed by or is acting directly or indirectly for a
railway carrier, is a prescribed person for the purposes of section 7.3
of the Act.

### [Security Plan and Security Plan Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1125996 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- security-sensitive
dangerous goods

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  10 to 13 apply only to railway carriers that transport and railway
  loaders that offer for transport or handle any of the
  security-sensitive dangerous goods set out in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, paragraph 10(1)(g) does not
  apply to railway loaders.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway carrier or railway loader is required to implement a security
  plan that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifies, by job title, a senior manager
    responsible for the plan's overall development and implementation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} describes the railway carrier's or railway loader's
    organizational structure, identifies the departments that are
    responsible for implementing the plan or any portion of it and
    identifies every position whose incumbent is responsible for
    implementing the plan or any portion of it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} describes the security duties of each identified
    department and position;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} sets out a process for notifying each person in a
    position referred to in paragraph (b) or (c) and who is responsible
    for implementing the plan or any portion of it that the plan or that
    portion of it must be implemented;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} includes an assessment of the security risks
    associated with the offering for transport, handling or transport of
    the dangerous goods set out in Schedule 1 that the railway carrier
    or railway loader offers for transport, handles or transports;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} sets out a process for security inspections
    referred to in section 7, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a procedure for conducting security inspections,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a method for determining whether security has
      been compromised,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a method for determining whether additional
      security inspections are necessary when, given the circumstances,
      security could be compromised, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a method for addressing the situation, if it is
      determined that security has been compromised;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} sets out measures to prevent access by unauthorized
    persons to the dangerous goods set out in Schedule 1 and to the
    railway vehicles used to transport those dangerous goods;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sets out measures to verify information provided by
    candidates for positions that involve access to the dangerous goods
    set out in Schedule 1;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} sets out a policy on limiting access to
    security-sensitive information and sets out measures for the
    sharing, storing and destruction of that information;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} sets out measures to address other security risks
    identified in the assessment referred to in paragraph (f);

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} sets out a program for the security awareness
    training required under section 14 and the security plan training
    required under section 11; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} sets out measures to respond to a security incident
    and for reporting it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the most recent version of the security plan
    or any portion of it available to each person who is responsible for
    implementing the plan or that portion of it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} review, and if necessary revise, the plan at least
    once a year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} revise the plan if a change in circumstances is
    likely to affect the security risks identified in the assessment
    referred to in paragraph (1)(f);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notify without delay the persons referred to in
    paragraph (a) of any significant revisions to the plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide a copy of the plan to the Minister upon his
    or her request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commensurate measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The measures required under subsection (1) and under
  subsection 7.3(2) of the Act must be commensurate with the security
  risks identified in the assessment referred to in paragraph (1)(f).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  requires a railway carrier or railway loader to develop a security
  plan if it already has a plan that meets the requirements of
  subsections (1) and (3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons required to undergo security plan
training

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is employed by or is acting directly or indirectly for a
  railway carrier or railway loader to which this section applies is
  required to undergo training on the security plan if that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offers for transport, handles or transports by
    railway vehicle, in Canada, any of the dangerous goods set out in
    Schedule 1; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is responsible, in Canada, for implementing the
    security plan or any portion of it but does not perform any of the
    duties referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of training

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must ensure
  that training on the security plan is provided to the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the day on which the person initially
    undertakes the duties referred to in paragraph (1)(a), unless the
    person has, before that date, received training that meets the
    requirements of section 12;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within six months after the later of the day on
    which this subsection comes into force and the day on which the
    person initially undertakes the responsibility referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), unless the person has, before that date, received
    training that meets the requirements of section 12; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a recurrent basis at least once every three
    years after the day on which a person completed their previous
    training, including any training received before the coming into
    force of this subsection that meets the requirements of section 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must ensure
  that, until a person with the duties referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  undergoes training on the security plan, the person performs their
  duties under the supervision of a person who has undergone training on
  the components of the plan that are relevant to the duties of the
  person being supervised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training topics

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Training on the security
plan must cover the following topics:

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the security plan measures that, in the event of a
  security incident, are relevant to the duties of the person undergoing
  the training.

### [Security Awareness Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1126051 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security awareness training

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway carrier or railway loader must ensure that security awareness
  training is provided on the following topics:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security risks that are posed by the dangerous
    goods that the railway carrier or railway loader offers for
    transport, handles or transports;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measures that are designed to enhance rail
    security; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the recognition of and response to potential
    threats and other security concerns.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons required to undergo training

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who is employed by or is acting directly or
  indirectly for the railway carrier or railway loader is required to
  undergo the security awareness training if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} offers for transport, handles or transports
    dangerous goods by railway vehicle, in Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has duties, in Canada, regarding the security of
    the transportation of dangerous goods by railway vehicle but does
    not perform any of the duties referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of training

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must ensure
  that the security awareness training is provided to the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the day on which the person initially
    undertakes the duties referred to in paragraph (2)(a), unless the
    person has, before that date, received an equivalent training;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within six months after the later of the day on
    which this subsection comes into force and the day on which the
    person initially undertakes the duties referred to in paragraph
    (2)(b), unless the person has, before that date, received an
    equivalent training; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a recurrent basis at least once every three
    years after the day on which a person completed their previous
    training, including any equivalent training received before the
    coming into force of this subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must ensure
  that, until a person with the duties referred to in paragraph (2)(b)
  undergoes security awareness training, the person performs their
  duties under the supervision of a person who has undergone that
  training.

### [Training Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1126069 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training records

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway carrier or railway loader must have a training record for each
  person who has undergone training under section 11, 13 or 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of training record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The training record must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name and details of the most recent

    namely the date, the duration, the course title, the delivery
    method, the components of the security plan that were covered, if

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the title and date of each training session that
    the person has previously followed under each section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The railway carrier or railway loader must retain the
  record for at least two years after the day on which the person ceases
  to be employed by or act directly or indirectly for the railway
  carrier or railway loader.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1126079 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Various exemptions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are exempted from all or a portion of
the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) in accordance with one or
more of the provisions of those Regulations that are set out in
Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limited quantities

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are in a limited quantity as
determined in accordance with subsection 1.17(1) of the [Transportation
of Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excepted quantities

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they are in an excepted quantity as determined in
  accordance with subsections 1.17.1(1) and (2) of the [Transportation
  of Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) or are
  in an excepted quantity that is specified in subsection 1.17.1(8) of
  those Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirement set out in subsection 1.17.1(5) of
  those Regulations is met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Samples for classifying, analysing or
testing

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Parts 2 and 3 do not
apply in respect of samples of goods that a railway loader reasonably
believes to be dangerous goods if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the classification or the exact chemical composition
  of the goods is unknown and cannot be readily determined; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conditions set out in paragraphs 1.19.1(a) to (d)
  of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dangerous goods in apparatus, piece of
equipment or piece of machinery

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Part 2 and sections 9 to
13 do not apply in respect of dangerous goods that are exempted from a
portion of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) in accordance with special
provision 167 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flammable liquids

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Parts 2 and 3 do not
apply in respect of dangerous goods that are exempted from a portion of
the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) in accordance with
section 1.33 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engines or machinery containing dangerous
goods

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Parts 2 and 3 do not
apply in respect of dangerous goods that are exempted from a portion of
the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) in accordance with special
provision 154 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Human or animal specimens

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are exempted from a portion of the
[Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) in accordance with section
1.42 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical or clinical waste

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are medical waste or clinical waste
if the conditions set out in paragraphs 1.42.3(a) and (b) of the
[Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Radioactive materials

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Parts 2 and 3 do not
apply in respect of dangerous goods that are radioactive materials
included in Class 7 if the conditions set out in paragraphs 1.43(a) and
(b) of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residue in drum

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), Part 2
  and sections 9 to 13 do not apply in respect of a residue of dangerous
  goods contained in a drum if the conditions set out in
  paragraphs 1.44(a) and (b) of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This exemption does not apply in respect of dangerous
  goods that are included in Packing Group I or that are contained in a
  drum for which a Class 1, 4.3, 6.2 or 7 label is required under the
  [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine pollutants

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Part 2 and sections 9 to
13 do not apply in respect of substances that are classified as marine
pollutants in accordance with subparagraph 2.43(b)(ii) of the
[Transportation of Dangerous Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Life-saving appliances

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are determined, in accordance with
subsections (1) and (2) of special provision 21 of the [Transportation
of Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286), to be
either UN2990, LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES, SELF-INFLATING or UN3072,
LIFE-SAVING APPLIANCES NOT SELF-INFLATING, if the conditions set out in
subsection (3) of that special provision are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Molten sulphur

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are UN2448, MOLTEN SULFUR, MOLTEN
SULPHUR, SULFUR, MOLTEN or SULPHUR, MOLTEN, if the dangerous goods are
transported in a large means of containment and the conditions set out
in paragraphs (a) and (b) of special provision 32 of the [Transportation
of Dangerous Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lithium cells and batteries

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Parts 1 to 3 do not apply
in respect of dangerous goods that are UN3090, LITHIUM METAL BATTERIES
(including lithium alloy batteries), UN3091, LITHIUM METAL BATTERIES
CONTAINED IN EQUIPMENT (including lithium alloy batteries) or LITHIUM
METAL BATTERIES PACKED WITH EQUIPMENT (including lithium alloy
batteries), UN3480, LITHIUM ION BATTERIES (including lithium ion polymer
batteries) or UN3481, LITHIUM ION BATTERIES CONTAINED IN EQUIPMENT
(including lithium ion polymer batteries) or LITHIUM ION BATTERIES
PACKED WITH EQUIPMENT (including lithium ion polymer batteries), if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conditions set out in subsection (1) of special
  provision 34 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286) are met; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cells and batteries that are installed
  in equipment, the requirements of subsections (2) to (4) of special
  provision 34 of those Regulations are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Neutron radiation detectors

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Parts 1 to 3 do not apply in respect
  of a neutron radiation detector, including one with solder glass
  joints, that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not contain more than 1 g of boron trifluoride
    gas;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be transported under the UN number and shipping
    name UN1008, BORON TRIFLUORIDE, in accordance with subsection (1) of
    special provision 145 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is packed in accordance with subsection (2) of
    special provision 145 of those Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Radiation detection systems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Parts 1 to 3 do not apply in respect of a radiation
  detection system that contains a neutron radiation detector, including
  one with solder glass joints, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the neutron radiation detector meets the conditions
    set out in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the radiation detection system is packed in
    accordance with subsection (3) of special provision 145 of the
    [Transportation of Dangerous Goods
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-286).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One month after registration

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  these Regulations come into force on the day that, in the first month
  after the month in which they are registered, has the same calendar
  number as the day on which they are registered or, if that first month
  has no day with that number, the last day of that first month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Three months after registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 7 come into force on the day that, in
  the third month after the month in which these Regulations are
  registered, has the same calendar number as the day on which they are
  registered or, if that third month has no day with that number, the
  last day of that third month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Nine months after registration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 8 to 15 come into force on the day that, in
  the ninth month after the month in which these Regulations are
  registered, has the same calendar number as the day on which they are
  registered or, if that ninth month has no day with that number, the
  last day of that ninth month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First anniversary of registration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Sections 19 and 32 to 34 come into force on the first
  anniversary of the day on which these Regulations are registered.
