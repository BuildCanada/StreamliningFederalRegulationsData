## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734438 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Assisted Human Reproduction
      Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship at the relevant time, having so
      cohabited for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint de
      fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in relation to human reproductive material,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is not the donor of the
          human reproductive material or the spouse or common-law
          partner of that donor, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a couple in respect of whom neither spouse
          or common-law partner is the donor of the human reproductive
          material; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to an [in vitro]{.otherLang
        lang="la"} embryo,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is not the donor of the [in
          vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo under subsection 10(1), or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a couple who is not the donor of the [in
          vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo under subsection 10(1).
          ([tiers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, the term
  [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a person who, at the
  relevant time, lives separate and apart from the person to whom they
  are married because of the breakdown of their marriage.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, the written
  consent of a donor must be signed by the donor and attested by a
  witness.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Consent Given Under Subsection 8(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734452 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of a consent given under subsection 8(1) of the Act to make use
of human reproductive material for the purpose of creating an embryo.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Before a person makes use
of human reproductive material for the purpose of creating an embryo,
the person shall have a document signed by the donor of the material
stating that, before consenting to the use of the material, the donor
was informed in writing that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), the human reproductive
  material will be used in accordance with the donor's consent to create
  an embryo for one or more of the following purposes, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the donor's own reproductive use,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} following the donor's death, the reproductive use
    of the person who is, at the time of the donor's death, the donor's
    spouse or common-law partner,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reproductive use of a third party,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} improving assisted reproduction procedures, or

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} providing instruction in assisted reproduction
    procedures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the human reproductive material is to be removed
  from the donor's body after the donor's death, the material will be
  used in accordance with the donor's consent to create an embryo for
  one or more of the following purposes, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reproductive use of the person who is, at the
    time of the donor's death, the donor's spouse or common-law partner,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} improving assisted reproduction procedures, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} providing instruction in assisted reproduction
    procedures;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the donor wishes to withdraw their consent, the
  withdrawal must be in writing;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is effective only if the person who
  intends to make use of the human reproductive material is notified in
  writing of the withdrawal

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of human reproductive material to be
    used to create an embryo for a purpose mentioned in paragraph (a) or
    (b), other than subparagraph (a)(iii), before the material is used,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of human reproductive material to be
    used to create an embryo for the purpose mentioned in subparagraph
    (a)(iii),

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} before the third party acknowledges in writing
      that the material has been obtained for their reproductive use, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the material to be used has not yet been
      removed or collected but the third party has acknowledged in
      writing that the material is to be obtained for their reproductive
      use, before the removal or collection;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryos created with the human reproductive material may be in excess
  of the immediate reproductive needs of the individual or couple for
  whom they were created;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the human reproductive material is used to create
  [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos for a third party's
  reproductive use and there are [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryos in excess of the third party's reproductive needs, the excess
  [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos will be used in accordance
  with the third party's consent and, if the use is providing
  instruction in assisted reproduction procedures, improving assisted
  reproduction procedures or other research, the consent of the donor in
  accordance with section 4 or 4.1;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the human reproductive material is used to create
  [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos for the reproductive use of
  the person who, at the time of the donor's death, is the donor's
  spouse or common-law partner and there are [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryos in excess of the spouse or common-law partner's
  reproductive needs, the excess [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryos will be used in accordance with the spouse or common-law
  partner's consent and, if the use is providing instruction in assisted
  reproduction procedures, improving assisted reproduction procedures or
  other research, the consent of the donor in accordance with section 4
  or 4.1;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the human reproductive material is used to create
  [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos for the reproductive use of a
  third party who is a couple, along with human reproductive material
  from an individual who is a spouse or common law partner in the
  couple, the use of the [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos will
  be subject to the consent of that individual alone if, prior to the
  use of the [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos, the individual is
  no longer a spouse or common-law partner in the couple; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the donor consents to the human reproductive
  material being used to create an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryo for the purpose of providing instruction in assisted
  reproduction procedures or improving assisted reproduction procedures,
  no additional consent from the donor is required to permit the use of
  the embryo for that purpose.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before a
  person makes use of human reproductive material for the purpose of
  creating an embryo, the person shall have the written consent of the
  donor of the material stating that the material may be used for one or
  more of the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor's own reproductive use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} following the donor's death, the reproductive use
    of the person who is, at the time of the donor's death, the donor's
    spouse or common-law partner;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reproductive use of a third party;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} improving assisted reproduction procedures; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} providing instruction in assisted reproduction
    procedures.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A donor's consent stating that the donor's human
  reproductive material may be used for a purpose mentioned in paragraph
  (1)(b) or (c) shall also state whether any [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryos that are not required for that purpose may be used
  for providing instruction in assisted reproduction procedures,
  improving assisted reproduction procedures or other research.

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 3 and
4, if a person who makes use of human reproductive material for the
purpose of creating an embryo cannot obtain the written consent of the
donor for that use because the material was donated on the condition of
anonymity, the person shall have instead, before making use of that
material, a document signed by the person who originally obtained
consent from the donor, attesting to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the donor provided a signed document stating
  that they had been informed, in writing, of the information set out in
  section 3 prior to consenting to the use of their human reproductive
  material;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the donor gave written consent to the use of
  their human reproductive material for the purpose of creating an
  embryo for the purposes referred to in paragraph (c);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purposes indicated in the written consent of the
  donor; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the donor provided consent to the use of their
  human reproductive material for the purpose of creating embryos for
  the reproductive use of a third party, stating whether the donor
  consented that any [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryos that are
  not required for that purpose may be used for providing instruction in
  assisted reproduction procedures, improving assisted reproduction
  procedures or other research.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Consent Given Under Subsection 8(2) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734487 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of a consent given under subsection 8(2) of the Act to remove
human reproductive material from a donor's body after the donor's death
for the purpose of creating an embryo.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Before a person removes
human reproductive material from a donor's body after the donor's death
for the purpose of creating an embryo, the person shall have a document
signed by the donor stating that, before consenting to the removal, the
donor was informed in writing that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the human reproductive material will be removed in
  accordance with the donor's consent to create an embryo for one or
  more of the following purposes, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reproductive use of the person who is, at the
    time of the donor's death, the donor's spouse or common-law partner,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} improving assisted reproduction procedures, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} providing instruction in assisted reproduction
    procedures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the donor wishes to withdraw their consent, the
  withdrawal must be in writing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is effective only if the person who
  intends to remove the human reproductive material is notified in
  writing of the withdrawal before the removal of the material; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} human reproductive material removed from the donor
  cannot be used for a purpose mentioned in paragraph (a) unless the
  person who intends to make use of the material has the donor's written
  consent under Part 1 respecting the use of the material.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Before a person removes
human reproductive material from a donor's body after the donor's death
for the purpose of creating an embryo, the person shall have the donor's
written consent respecting the removal of the material and the donor's
written consent under Part 1 respecting the use of the material.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  donor wishes to withdraw their consent respecting the removal of human
  reproductive material after their death, the withdrawal must be in
  writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The withdrawal is effective only if the person who
  intends to remove the human reproductive material is notified in
  writing of the withdrawal before the removal of the material.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Consent Given Under Subsection 8(3) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734501 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 15, in this Part,
  [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following individual or
  individuals for whose reproductive use an [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryo is created:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual who has no spouse or common-law
    partner at the time the [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo is
    created, regardless of the source of the human reproductive material
    used to create the embryo; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), the couple who are
    spouses or common-law partners at the time the [in vitro]{.otherLang
    lang="la"} embryo is created, regardless of the source of the human
    reproductive material used to create the embryo.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the donor is a couple, the [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} embryo shall be used only for the purposes to which both
  spouses or common-law partners have consented.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  embryo created using human reproductive material from only one of the
  individuals in the couple that was the donor of the embryo at the time
  it was created, that individual becomes the donor of the embryo under
  paragraph (1)(a) if, before the use of the embryo, the individual is
  no longer a spouse or common-law partner in the couple.

    stated in the consent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the donor wishes to withdraw their consent, the
  withdrawal must be in writing; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the withdrawal is effective only if the person who
  intends to make use of the [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo is
  notified in writing of the withdrawal

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
    embryo to be used for the purpose mentioned in subparagraph (a)(i),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the person acknowledges in writing that the [in

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the person acknowledges in writing that the [in

      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo for the purpose of research,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the creation of a stem cell line using the [in
      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo.

      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo has been obtained for the
      purpose of research,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the beginning of the process of thawing the [in
      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo for the purpose of research,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the creation of a stem cell line using the [in
      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the donor is a couple, the consent of the donor
  may be withdrawn by either spouse or common-law partner.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
sections 12 to 14, in the case of an [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}

embryo and the document and the consent provided under sections 3, 4 and
4.1 in respect of the use of their human reproductive material for the
purpose of creating the embryo constitute, respectively, the document
and the consent required under section 12, subsection 13(1) and section
13.1 with respect to the use of the embryo.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1215024 .Part}

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** A person who makes
use of human reproductive material from a donor under Part 1 shall keep,
for each use of that material, a record of all the documents required
under that Part for a period of 10 years following the day on which that
material is used.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734555 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 3 and 4, in the
  case of human reproductive material obtained before the coming into
  force of these Regulations, a person may make use of the human
  reproductive material to create an embryo for a purpose mentioned in
  any of paragraphs 4(1)(b) to (e) if the person has the written consent
  of the persons whose human reproductive material was used to create
  the embryo, dated before the coming into force of these Regulations,
  stating that the human reproductive material may be used for that
  purpose.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 12, 13 and 13.2, in the case of an
  [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo created before the coming into
  force of these Regulations, a person may make use of the embryo for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose mentioned in paragraph 13(1)(b) if the
    person has the written consent of the donor of the embryo, dated
    before the coming into force of these Regulations, stating that the
    embryo may be used for that purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a purpose mentioned in paragraph 13(1)(c) or (d) if
    the person has

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the embryo having been created for
      that purpose, the written consent of the persons whose human
      reproductive material was used to create the embryo, dated before
      the coming into force of these Regulations, stating that their
      human reproductive material may be used to create an [in
      vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo for that purpose, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the embryo having been created
      for another purpose but not required for the purpose for which it
      was created,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the written consent of the donor of the embryo,
        dated before the coming into force of these Regulations, stating
        that the embryo may be used for the purpose mentioned in
        paragraph 13(1)(c) or (d), as the case may be, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the written consent of the persons whose human
        reproductive material was used to create the embryo, dated
        before the coming into force of these Regulations, stating that
        if the embryo is not required for the purpose for which it was
        created, it may be used for the purpose of providing instruction
        in assisted reproduction procedures or improving assisted
        reproduction procedures, as the case may be, unless those
        persons have already consented to that use as the donor of the
        embryo;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the purpose mentioned in paragraph 13(1)(e), if the
    person has

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the written consent of the donor of the embryo,
      dated before the coming into force of these Regulations, stating
      that the embryo may be used for that purpose, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the written consent of the persons whose human
      reproductive material was used to create the embryo, dated before
      the coming into force of these Regulations, stating that if the
      embryo is not required for the purpose for which it was created,
      it may be used for research, unless those persons have already
      consented to that use as the donor of the embryo.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite sections 3 and 4, in the case of an [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} embryo created after the coming into
  force of these Regulations using human reproductive material obtained
  before the coming into force of these Regulations, a person may make
  use of the embryo for a purpose mentioned in paragraph 13(1)(c), (d)
  or (e) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of sections 12 and 13 are
    satisfied; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has the written consent of the persons
    whose human reproductive material was used to create the embryo,
    dated before the coming into force of these Regulations, stating
    that if the embryo is not required for the purpose for which it was
    created, it may be used for the purpose of providing instruction in
    assisted reproduction procedures, improving assisted reproduction
    procedures or other research, as the case may be, unless those
    persons have already consented to that use as the donor of the
    embryo.
