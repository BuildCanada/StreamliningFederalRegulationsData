## [Maximum Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1290394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Setting of maximum fee

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 3(1) of the [Disability Tax Credit Promoters
  Restrictions Act](/eng/acts/D-2.8), the maximum fee for a disability
  tax credit request made by a promoter is set at

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$100, for a request for a determination of
    disability tax credit eligibility under subsection 152(1.01) of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$100 per taxation year, for a request made in
    respect of a deduction under subsection 118.3(1) or (2) of the
    [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$100 per taxation year, for a request made in
    respect of any deduction or overpayment of tax under the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) that is contingent upon the eligibility for a
    deduction under subsection 118.3(1) or (2) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total fee accepted or charged

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If more than one disability tax credit request is
  made in respect of a claimant for a taxation year under either
  paragraph (1)(b) or (c), or both, the total fee that a promoter
  accepts or charges must not exceed \$100.

## [Adjusted Maximum Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1290403 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inflationary adjusted year

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[inflationary adjusted year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 2025
  and every fifth year after that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum fee adjusted on December 1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the maximum fee
  set out in section 1 is to be adjusted on December 1 of a particular
  inflationary adjusted year so that the maximum fee is equal to the
  greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee determined by the formula

    ::: {#1290410 .Paragraph}
    A × B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the maximum fee on November 30 of the particular inflationary
        adjusted year, and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount determined by the formula in subparagraph (i) or
        (ii), whichever is applicable, rounded to the nearest
        one-thousandth, or, if the amount is equidistant from two
        consecutive one-thousandths, rounded to the higher
        one-thousandth,
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the particular inflationary adjusted year
          is 2025,

          ::: {#1290415 .NestedFormula}
          C/D

          where

          [C]{.dfn}
          :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period ending
              on September 30, 2025, and

          [D]{.dfn}
          :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period that
              ended on September 30, 2019,
          :::

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for any other particular inflationary
          adjusted year,

          ::: {#1290420 .NestedFormula}
          E/F

          where

          [E]{.dfn}
          :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period ending
              on September 30 of the particular inflationary adjusted
              year, and,

          [F]{.dfn}
          :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period ending
              on September 30 of the last inflationary adjusted year in
              which the maximum fee was adjusted; and
          :::
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fee referred to in the description of A in
    paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of adjustment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjustment referred to in subsection (2) is to be
  applied only if the amount determined under that subsection exceeds
  the amount of the maximum fee described in the description of A in
  paragraph (2)(a) by \$5 or more.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rounding

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the adjustment referred to in subsection (2) is
  applied, the maximum fee determined under that subsection is to be
  rounded to the nearest dollar or, if the result is equidistant from
  two consecutive dollar amounts, to the higher dollar amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer Price Index

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section, the Consumer Price Index for any
  12-month period is the result arrived at by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} aggregating the Consumer Price Index for Canada, as
    published by Statistics Canada under the authority of the
    [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for each month in that period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dividing the aggregate obtained under paragraph (a)
    by 12; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rounding the result obtained under paragraph (b) to
    the nearest one-thousandth or, if the result obtained is equidistant
    from two consecutive one-thousandths, to the higher one-thousandth.
