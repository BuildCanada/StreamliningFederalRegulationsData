## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704960 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi ]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization, issued
    by the Minister under section 11.1 of the Act, for a person to
    present themself in an alternative
    manner. ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person to whom the
    Minister has issued an authorization. ([personne
    autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    operates a commercial highway
    conveyance. ([routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods imported into
    Canada for sale or for any commercial, industrial, occupational,
    institutional or other like use. ([marchandises
    commerciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial highway conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial highway conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    conveyance designed for hauling freight on highways. ([moyen de
    transport routier commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    conveyance that is used to carry passengers who have paid for
    passage. ([moyen de transport commercial de
    passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a
    person, an individual who has cohabited with the person in a
    conjugal relationship for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint
    de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[corporate aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[corporate aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft that is
    used for purposes related to the business affairs of a person, that
    does not carry passengers who have paid for passage and that has
    aboard on its arrival in Canada no more than 15 persons, including
    the crew. ([aéronef d'affaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated customs office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a customs
    office designated under section 5 of the Act as a customs office
    where a person may present themself pursuant to section 11 of the
    Act, or in an alternative manner if the person is so
    authorized. ([bureau de douane établi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated holding area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated holding area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area
    designated by the President for the use of persons arriving in
    Canada who are in transit to another place in Canada or to a place
    outside Canada. ([zone d'attente désignée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a person
    who is serving as a member of the Canadian or American armed forces
    in a foreign country or at a Canadian or American diplomatic mission
    or consular post in a foreign country, a spouse, common-law partner
    or dependent who is identified as such in the document confirming
    the mission or post. ([membre de la famille]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[marine pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marine pleasure craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel,
    however propelled, other than a seaplane or other similar
    conveyance, that is used exclusively for pleasure and that does not
    carry passengers who have paid for passage. ([embarcation de
    plaisance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    conveyance that does not carry passengers who have paid for passage,
    and includes corporate aircraft, private aircraft and marine
    pleasure craft. ([moyen de transport non commercial de
    passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a child, means a parent
    who, by operation of law, by virtue of a written agreement or under
    a court order, has custody or parental authority over the
    child. ([parent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent resident of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent resident of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    permanent resident within the meaning of the [Immigration and
    Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([résident permanent du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft other
    than a corporate aircraft that does not carry passengers who have
    paid for passage and that has aboard on its arrival in Canada no
    more than 15 persons, including the crew. ([aéronef
    privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704982 .Part}

### [Exceptions to Presentation under Subsection 11(1) of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-704983 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Persons
  who arrive in Canada aboard a commercial passenger conveyance, who do
  not disembark in Canada and who have as their destination a place
  outside Canada are not required to present themselves in accordance
  with subsection 11(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following persons, in the following circumstances
  and conditions, are not required to present themselves in accordance
  with subsection 11(1) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who arrive in Canada aboard a commercial
    passenger conveyance if they do not disembark at the place of their
    arrival and have as their destination another place in Canada at
    which there is a designated customs office, and on arrival at their
    destination they present themselves without delay at that customs
    office or if the designated customs office is not open for business,
    at the nearest one that is open for business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who arrive in Canada aboard a commercial
    passenger conveyance if they are transferred under customs control
    from a designated holding area to another commercial passenger
    conveyance for departure

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a place outside Canada and they do not leave
      the designated holding area except to board that other commercial
      passenger conveyance, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to another place in Canada and at which there is
      a designated customs office, and on arrival at that place they
      present themselves without delay at that customs office or, if it
      is not open for business, at the nearest designated customs office
      that is open for business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons arriving in Canada aboard a non-commercial
    passenger conveyance at a designated customs office where the person
    in charge of the conveyance may present themself and their
    passengers by radio or telephone, and the person informs an officer
    by radio or telephone of their arrival and, if required to do so by
    the officer, presents themself and their passengers at the time and
    place specified by the officer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any crew member arriving in Canada aboard a freight
    train at a designated customs office where presentation may be done
    by radio or telephone, and the crew member informs an officer by
    radio or telephone of their arrival and, if required to do so by the
    officer, presents themself at the time and place specified by the
    officer; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any person entering Canadian waters, including the
    inland waters, or the airspace over Canada in circumstances in which
    none of paragraphs (a) to (d) applies, and who has as their
    destination a place in Canada at which there is a designated customs
    office, and on arrival at their destination they present themself
    without delay at that office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- answer questions
  truthfully

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, every person who presents
  themself in accordance with any of paragraphs (2)(a) to (e) is
  required to answer truthfully any questions asked by an officer in the
  performance of the officer's duties under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament.

### [Exception to Subsection 11(3) of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-704998 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commercial passenger conveyances

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person in charge of a commercial passenger conveyance arriving in
  Canada is not required to ensure that the following persons, in the
  following circumstances and conditions, are forthwith on arrival
  transported to a customs office as required by subsection 11(3) of the
  Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} passengers and crew who do not disembark in Canada
    and who have as their destination a place outside Canada, if only
    passengers or goods that have come from a designated holding area
    are taken aboard the conveyance while it is in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} passengers and crew who do not disembark at the
    place of arrival in Canada and who have as their destination another
    place in Canada at which there is a designated customs office, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} only passengers or goods that have come from a
      designated holding area are taken aboard the conveyance while it
      is in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on arrival at their destination the passengers
      and crew present themselves without delay at that customs office
      or, if it is not open for business, at the nearest designated
      customs office that is open for business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} passengers and crew who are transferred under
    customs control to another commercial passenger conveyance for
    departure

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a place outside Canada and the passengers and
      crew comply with subparagraph 2(2)(b)(i); or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to another place in Canada at which there is a
      designated customs office, and the passengers and crew comply with
      subparagraph 2(2)(b)(ii); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} passengers and crew who arrive at a designated
    customs office where persons may present themselves by radio or
    telephone, and the person in charge of the commercial passenger
    conveyance

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} notifies an officer by radio or telephone of the
      arrival of the conveyance and advises the officer of the number
      and names of the passengers and crew who disembark at the place,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if required to do so by an officer, ensures that
      the passengers and crew who disembark at the place are transported
      at the time and to the place specified by the officer for the
      purpose of presenting themselves and answering truthfully any
      questions asked by an officer in accordance with subsection 11(1)
      of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Freight trains

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the arrival in Canada of a freight train, the
  person in charge of it is not required to ensure that the crew members
  aboard it are forthwith transported to a customs office as is required
  by subsection 11(3) of the Act if those crew members arrive at a
  designated customs office where persons may present themselves by
  radio or telephone, and the person in charge of the freight train

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notifies an officer by radio or telephone of the
    arrival of the train and advises the officer of the number and names
    of the crew members who disembark at the place; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if required to do so by an officer, ensures that
    the crew members who disembark at the place are transported at the
    time and to the place specified by the officer for the purpose of
    presenting themselves and answering truthfully any questions asked
    by an officer in accordance with subsection 11(1) of the Act.

### [Advance Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705016 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided ---
non-commercial passenger conveyance

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person in charge of a non-commercial passenger conveyance, other than
  a marine pleasure craft, that will arrive in Canada who intends to
  present themselves and all other persons on board the conveyance by
  telephone to an officer at a designated customs office is required
  under subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the
  conveyance's scheduled time and place of arrival in Canada and, if it
  is different, the conveyance's place of final destination in Canada
  and scheduled time of arrival at that destination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of providing
  information

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the conveyance shall give
  the information by telephone to an officer at a designated customs
  office at least two but no more than 48 hours before the conveyance
  arrives in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If required by an officer, the person in charge of a
  non- commercial passenger conveyance shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide any information relating to the other
    persons aboard the non-commercial passenger conveyance; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify an officer of the arrival of the conveyance
    in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change to information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person in charge of a conveyance who gives
  information in the circumstances set out in this section shall, before
  the conveyance arrives in Canada, notify an officer at a designated
  customs office by telephone of any change to the information given
  unless there are emergency circumstances, in which case they shall
  notify an officer at a designated customs office of the change and the
  circumstances when the conveyance arrives in Canada.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Presentation in Alternative Manners]{.HTitleText1} {#h-705030 .Part}

### [Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705031 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CANPASS Air program

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue an authorization to a person to present themself in
  an alternative manner described in paragraph 11(a) if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a citizen or permanent resident of Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a citizen or permanent resident of the United
      States, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a citizen of another country and the following
      conditions are met:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the person is a member of a program in that
        country that allows for an alternative manner of presentation to
        facilitate or expedite entry into that country, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} Canada has a reciprocal arrangement with that
        country, entered into under paragraph 13(2)(a) of the [Canada
        Border Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4), in respect of the
        alternative manner of presentation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of good character;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not inadmissible to Canada under the
    [Immigration and Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or its
    regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provides their consent in writing to the use by the
    Minister of biometric data concerning the person for the purposes
    set out in section 6.3;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has provided true, accurate and complete
    information in respect of their application for the authorization;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), has resided only in one
    or more of the following countries during the three-year period
    before the day on which the application was received and until the
    day on which the authorization is issued:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canada or the United States,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is serving as a member of the
      American armed forces in a foreign country, that foreign country,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a family member of a person
      who is a member of the Canadian or American armed forces serving
      in a foreign country, that foreign country, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a family member of a person who
      is serving at a Canadian or American diplomatic mission or
      consular post in a foreign country, that foreign country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (1)(f) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a citizen of Canada or the United States;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is not a citizen of Canada or the
    United States and who meets the conditions set out in
    subparagraph (1)(a)(iii); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a child who is under 18 years of age and is a
    permanent resident of the following country and on behalf of whom an
    application is made by a person who meets the requirement set out in
    that paragraph:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Canada and was adopted outside Canada by a
      citizen or permanent resident of Canada or born outside Canada to
      a citizen of Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the United States and was adopted outside the
      United States by a citizen or permanent resident of the United
      States or born outside the United States to a citizen of the
      United States.

### [Alternative Manners of Presentation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705153 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative manners

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Persons may present
themselves in one of the following alternative manners if they have been
authorized to do so:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on arrival in Canada aboard a commercial aircraft at
  a commercial airport that is a designated customs office, by means of
  an electronic device;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on arrival in Canada aboard a private aircraft at a
  public airport that is a designated customs office, by means of the
  person in charge of the aircraft if that person presents themself and
  those persons aboard by telephone to an officer at a designated
  customs office and all persons aboard the aircraft are authorized to
  present themselves in that manner;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on arrival in Canada aboard a corporate aircraft at a
  public airport that is a designated customs office, by means of the
  person in charge of the aircraft, if that person presents themself and
  those persons aboard by telephone to an officer at a designated
  customs office and all persons aboard the aircraft are authorized to
  present themselves in that manner;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on arrival in Canada at a land border crossing,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they are commercial drivers who are operating or
    aboard a commercial highway conveyance, by means of an electronic
    device at a designated customs office,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they are operating or aboard a conveyance, by
    means of an electronic device at a designated customs office if
    every person aboard the conveyance is authorized to present themself
    in that manner, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if they are commercial drivers who are operating
    or aboard a commercial highway conveyance, by means of an electronic
    device at a designated customs office if every person aboard the
    conveyance is authorized to present themself in that manner; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} on arrival in Canada aboard a marine pleasure craft,
  by means of the person in charge of the marine pleasure craft if that
  person presents themself and those persons aboard by telephone to an
  officer at a designated customs office and all persons aboard the
  marine pleasure craft are authorized to present themselves in that
  manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carrying of Authorization

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every authorized person,
other than a person whose authorization was issued under section 8,
shall carry their authorization on their person when presenting themself
in an alternate manner, and shall show it to an officer if so requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of authorizations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No authorized person
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfer or assign their authorization;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit another person to use their authorization;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or attempt to use their authorization if it is
  expired, suspended or cancelled; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} use or attempt to use their authorization to present
  themself in a manner that is not authorized.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loss or theft

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every authorized person
whose authorization is lost or stolen shall, without delay, inform the
Minister of the loss or theft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- commercial goods belonging
to person

- **[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorization issued to a person to present themself in an alternative
  manner described in paragraph 11(a) or subparagraph 11(d)(i) or (iii)
  is only valid if, on arrival in Canada, the person does not have any
  commercial goods in their actual possession or in their baggage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- commercial goods on board
  a conveyance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorization issued to a person to present
  themself in an alternative manner described in paragraph 11(b) or (c),
  subparagraph 11(d)(ii) or paragraph 11(e) is only valid if there are
  no commercial goods on board the conveyance that the person uses to
  arrive in Canada.

### [Advance Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705183 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided --- aircraft

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person in charge of a corporate aircraft or private aircraft that will
  arrive in Canada who intends to present themselves and all other
  authorized persons on board the aircraft in an alternative manner
  described in paragraph 11(b) or (c) is required under
  subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the aircraft's
  scheduled time and place of arrival in Canada and, if it is different,
  the aircraft's final place of destination in Canada and scheduled time
  of arrival at that destination.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner of providing
  information

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the aircraft shall give the
  information by telephone to an officer at a designated customs office
  at least two but no more than 48 hours before the aircraft arrives in
  Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If required by an officer, the person in charge of
  the corporate aircraft or private aircraft shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide any information relating to the other
    persons aboard the aircraft; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify an officer of the arrival of the aircraft in
    Canada.

### [Suspensions and Cancellations of Authorizations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-705232 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend or cancel an authorization if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no longer meets the requirements for the issuance
    of the authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has contravened the Act, the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19) or the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15), or any regulations made under any of those
    Acts; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has provided information that was not true,
    accurate or complete for the purposes of obtaining an authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2006-154, s. 15\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of suspension or cancellation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Immediately after cancelling or suspending an
  authorization of a person, the Minister shall send written notice of,
  and the reasons for, the cancellation or suspension to the person at
  their latest known address.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of authorization

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person whose authorization is cancelled or
  suspended shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on receiving a notice under subsection (3),
    immediately and in accordance with it, return to the Minister the
    written authorization and any other thing relevant to the
    authorization that is specified in the notice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on being advised of the suspension or cancellation
    in person by an officer, immediately return to the officer the
    written authorization and any other thing relevant to it that is
    specified by the officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date of suspension or
  cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The suspension or cancellation of an authorization
  becomes effective on the earlier of the day on which an officer
  advises in person of the suspension or cancellation and 15 days after
  the day on which notice of the suspension or cancellation is sent.

- SOR/2005-385, ss. 14, 21
- SOR/2006-154, s. 15
