## Canadian Bill of Rights Examination Regulations {#canadian-bill-of-rights-examination-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 394
:::

[CANADIAN BILL OF RIGHTS](/eng/acts/C-12.3)

Regulations Respecting the Examination of Bills and Regulations Pursuant
to the Canadian Bill of Rights
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553738 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Bill of Rights Examination
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._394).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553740 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Justice.

## [Examination of Bills]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553742 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the case of every Bill
introduced in or presented to the House of Commons by a Minister of the
Crown, the Minister shall, forthwith upon receipt of two copies of the
Bill from the Clerk of the House of Commons,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the Bill in order to determine whether any of
  the provisions thereof are inconsistent with the purposes and
  provisions of the [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3), and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause to be affixed to each of the copies thereof so
  received from the Clerk of the House of Commons a certificate, in a
  form approved by the Minister and signed by the Deputy Minister of
  Justice, stating that the Bill has been examined as required by the
  [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3),

and one each of the copies thereof so certified shall thereupon be
transmitted to the Clerk of the House of Commons and the Clerk of the
Privy Council.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-782, s. 1
:::

## [Examination of Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553749 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Clerk of the Privy
Council shall, on receipt of a regulation transmitted to him for
registration pursuant to the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22)
but not examined as a proposed regulation in accordance with section 3
of that Act, forward a copy thereof to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-782, s. 2
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall,
forthwith upon receipt of a copy of a regulation forwarded to him by the
Clerk of the Privy Council pursuant to section 4,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} examine the regulation in order to determine whether
  any of the provisions thereof are inconsistent with the purposes and
  provisions of the [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3), and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cause to be affixed to the copy thereof so received
  from the Clerk of the Privy Council a certificate, in a form approved
  by the Minister and signed by the Deputy Minister of Justice, stating
  that the regulation has been examined as required by the [Canadian
  Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3),

and the copy so certified shall thereupon be returned to the Clerk of
the Privy Council.

## [Report of the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-553757 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where any of the provisions
of any Bill examined by the Minister pursuant to section 3 or any of the
provisions of any regulation examined by him pursuant to section 5 are
ascertained by the Minister to be inconsistent with the purposes and
provisions of the [Canadian Bill of Rights](/eng/acts/C-12.3), the
Minister shall make a report in writing of the inconsistency and shall
cause such report to be deposited with the Clerk of the House of Commons
in accordance with the Standing Orders of the House of Commons at the
earliest convenient opportunity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-782, s. 3
- SOR/86-41, s. 1
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A copy of every report made
by the Minister pursuant to section 6 shall, where such report relates
to a regulation, be transmitted to the Clerk of the Privy Council
forthwith upon the making thereof.
