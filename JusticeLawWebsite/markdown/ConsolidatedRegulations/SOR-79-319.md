## Toll Information Regulations {#toll-information-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-319
:::

[NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/N-7)

Registration 1979-04-09

Regulations Respecting Information to be Furnished by Companies that
Charge Tolls

P.C. 1979-1116 1979-04-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to section 88 of
the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed [Regulations respecting information to be furnished
by companies that charge tolls](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-319).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865027 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Toll Information
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-319).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865029 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy Board
Act](/eng/acts/N-7).

## [Information to Be Furnished]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865031 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A company that charges
tolls shall, at the end of each three month period of operation, furnish
to the Board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rate of return on the rate base obtained for that
  period compared with the rates of return on the rate base authorized
  by the Board at the company's most recent rate hearing or, in the case
  of a company whose rate of return on the rate base has not been
  authorized by the Board, the rate of return on equity for that period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information explaining material changes between the
  results obtained from charging the tolls and the forecast figures on
  which the tolls were determined; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} calculations showing the reasons for the material
  changes referred to in paragraph (b) in capital, traffic, revenues,
  expenses and rates of return.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where, at the end of a
three month period of operation of a company the Board is satisfied on
application by the company that it has no material changes to explain
under paragraph 3(b), the Board may exempt the company from compliance
with paragraphs 3(a) and (b) and may require the company thereafter to
furnish information to the Board under section 3 at the end of each such
period of operation greater than three months as the Board may consider
requisite for the purposes of the Act.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where the Board considers
that information furnished by a company in accordance with
paragraph 3(b) does not fully explain the causes of the difference
between the actual figures and the forecast figures, the Board shall
require the company to furnish such additional information as may be
required to enable the Board to maintain surveillance over the effect of
the tolls charged by the company.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any company that is
required to furnish information to the Board pursuant to section 3 or 4
shall duly comply with the requirement.
