## [Application for a Refund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025203 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application for a refund under section 110 of
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a document certified by an officer that identifies
    the obsolete or surplus goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the documents required under Part I of
    the [Accounting for Imported Goods and Payment of Duties
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062) that relate to the
    importation of those goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an officer is not available to certify the
  document referred to in paragraph (1)(a), other non-certified
  documentation that contains similar information may be substituted for
  it.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025209 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
