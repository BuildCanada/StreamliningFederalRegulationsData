[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[cannabis revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cannabis revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 1 of the [Cannabis Fees
    Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-198). ([recettes tirées de la vente
    de cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    1(2) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([client]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[named responsible adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[named responsible adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 264(1) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([responsable
    nommé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to a
holder of a licence for sale for medical purposes and any other licence
set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 to the [Cannabis Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-198) of the fee paid or payable by the
holder under subsection 7(1) or 8(2) of the Order if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the cannabis sold during the applicable fiscal
  year under the licences for the same site is sold in Canada to a
  person that is a client or a named responsible adult or to which an
  exemption has been granted under section 140 of the [Cannabis
  Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) for a medical purpose and in relation to the
  cannabis or class of cannabis that is sold; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder submits to the Minister

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the applicable fiscal year ends
    before this Order comes into force, not later than 30 days after
    this Order comes into force,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a written request signed by the holder to remit
      the fee, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the cannabis revenue for the
      applicable fiscal year with respect to those licences, including
      the amount received from the sale of cannabis in Canada and the
      amount paid for the purchase of cannabis that were used to
      determine the cannabis revenue, accompanied by a written document
      confirming that all those sales were made to a person that was, at
      the time of the sale, a client or a named responsible adult or to
      which an exemption had been, at the time of the sale, granted
      under section 140 of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) for a
      medical purpose and in relation to the cannabis or class of
      cannabis that was sold; and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} not later than 30 days after this Order comes
      into force, a written request signed by the holder to remit the
      fee indicating that the holder intends to sell all cannabis during
      the applicable fiscal year only in Canada to a person that is a
      client or a named responsible adult or to which an exemption has
      been granted under section 140 of the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) for a medical purpose and in relation to
      the cannabis or class of cannabis that is sold, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} not later than April 30 following the applicable
      fiscal year, a statement of the cannabis revenue for the
      applicable fiscal year with respect to those licences, including
      the amount received from the sale of cannabis in Canada and the
      amount paid for the purchase of cannabis that were used to
      determine the cannabis revenue, accompanied by a written document
      confirming that all those sales were made to a person that was, at
      the time of the sale, a client or a named responsible adult or to
      which an exemption had been, at the time of the sale, granted
      under section 140 of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) for a
      medical purpose and in relation to the cannabis or class of
      cannabis that was sold.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents and information

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a licence
that submits a request under section 2 must retain the following for not
less than seven years after the day on which they are prepared and in a
manner that will enable an audit to be made in a timely manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} documents or information stating the amount received
  from the sale of cannabis, the amount paid for the purchase of
  cannabis and the name of the person from which cannabis was purchased;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} documents or information confirming that all sales of
  cannabis under the licence were made in Canada to a person that was,
  at the time of the sale, a client or a named responsible adult or to
  which an exemption had been, at the time of the sale, granted under
  section 140 of the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) for a medical
  purpose and in relation to the cannabis or class of cannabis that was
  sold.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 22, 2020.
