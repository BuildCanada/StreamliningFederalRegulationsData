## 14^th^ IAAF World Half Marathon Championships Remission Order {#th-iaaf-world-half-marathon-championships-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-263
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2005-08-31

14^th^ IAAF World Half Marathon Championships Remission Order

P.C. 2005-1507 2005-08-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [14^th^ IAAF World Half Marathon Championships Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-263).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718911 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Championships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Championships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 14^th^ IAAF World
    Half Marathon Championships to be held in Edmonton, Alberta, on
    October 1, 2005. ([Championnats]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Championships family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Championships family member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual, other than an individual
      ordinarily resident in Canada, who is an athlete participating in
      a competitive event of the Championships, a coach, a trainer, an
      official or a judge in the Championships; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual, other than an individual
      ordinarily resident in Canada, who is the holder of an IAAF
      accreditation issued by the LOC and who is a member of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the IAAF, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a sports federation that is a member of the
        IAAF. ([membre de la famille des Championnats]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[[foreign corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a corporation,
    whose head office is located outside Canada, that does not have a
    branch office or a subsidiary corporation located in Canada and that
    has been designated as an official sponsor or supplier of the
    Championships, or granted broadcasting rights for the Championships,
    by the LOC. ([société étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IAAF]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IAAF]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Association of
    Athletics Federations. ([IAAF]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[LOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[LOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the local organizing committee
    known as the Edmonton World Half Marathon Championships 2005 Ltd.
    Local Organizing Committee. ([COL]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718922 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to alcoholic beverages or tobacco products.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718924 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, remission is hereby granted of the excise taxes and the goods and
services tax paid or payable on goods imported temporarily into Canada
by a Championships family member, if the goods are for the exclusive use
of the member in connection with the Championships.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 7 and 8, remission is hereby granted of a portion of the
  goods and services tax paid or payable on the following goods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods for display, including apparatus and
    equipment used to display those goods, imported temporarily into
    Canada by a foreign corporation, or its agent or representative, for
    use exclusively in connection with the Championships; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment imported temporarily into Canada by the
    LOC or a foreign corporation, or its agent or representative, for
    use exclusively at the Championships.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The portion of the goods and services tax remitted
  under subsection (1) is an amount equal to the difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the goods and services tax paid or
    payable on the value of the goods, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the goods and services tax payable on
    1/60 of the value of the goods for each month or a portion of a
    month that the goods remain in Canada.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable on
goods imported into Canada by a foreign corporation, or its agent or
representative, if the goods have a unit value of \$60 or less and are
intended for free distribution at the Championships.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties, the excise taxes and
the goods and services tax paid or payable on goods imported into Canada
by a Championships family member, if the goods have a unit value of \$60
or less and are intended for free distribution as gifts or awards to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} another Championships family member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the LOC or any member of the LOC;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada participating in the
  Championships; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada acting in an official capacity
  in connection with the Championships.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718939 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
sections 3 and 4 on the condition that the goods, on or before December
31, 2005, are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exported from Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} destroyed in Canada under the supervision of a
  customs officer at the expense of the importer.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
this Order on the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported into Canada during the period
  beginning on March 1, 2005 and ending on October 1, 2005;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the
  day on which the goods were accounted for under section 32 of the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer provides the Canada Border Services
  Agency with any evidence or information that demonstrates that the
  importer is entitled to remission under this Order.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/2005-263, err., Vol. 140, No. 4
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718949 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
