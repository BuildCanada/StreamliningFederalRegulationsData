## St. Andrew's Lock Regulations {#st.-andrews-lock-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-444
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF PUBLIC WORKS AND GOVERNMENT SERVICES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-38.2)

Registration 1991-07-10

Regulations Respecting the Management and Proper Use of the St. Andrews
Lock, Dam, Bridge and Related Facilities at Lockport, Manitoba

P.C. 1991-1219 1991-07-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works, pursuant to sections 28 and 29 of the
Public Works Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the Regulations for the
Use and Operation of St. Andrews Lock, made by P.C. 6514 on December 29,
1949[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA}, and
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the management and proper
use of the St. Andrews lock, dam, bridge and related facilities at
Lockport, Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-444), in substitution
therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1star_e}Statutory
  Orders and Regulations, Consolidation, 1949, Volume III p. 3227
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936770 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [St. Andrew's Lock
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-444).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936772 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[approach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of water within a
    radius of 15 m from the lock-gates; ([approche]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[lock-master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lock-master]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person employed by the
    Department who is in charge of the operation of the lock, dam,
    bridge and related facilities; ([éclusier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an owner or an agent of the
    owner of a vessel or the captain or master of a vessel;
    ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person appointed by
    the Minister to be responsible for the lock, dam and bridge;
    ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any ship, boat, raft, canoe or
    other floating craft used or capable of being used as a means of
    transportation on water. ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936779 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the St. Andrews lock, dam, bridge and related facilities at Lockport,
Manitoba.

## [Opening and Closing of the Lock]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936781 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The lock shall be opened
for navigation during the period beginning on the date set by the
lock-master, based on climatic conditions, and ending on October 15 in
any year.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), during each day of the period referred to in
  section 4, the lock shall be opened for any vessel, on request, from
  8:00 a.m. to midnight, with the last locking to begin at 11:30 p.m.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} From noon on Friday until midnight on the following
  Sunday and during holidays, the lock shall be opened for pleasure
  vessels

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of northbound pleasure vessels, every
    hour on the hour; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of southbound pleasure vessels, every
    hour on the half hour.

## [Priority of Passage Through Lock]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936788 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Priority
  of passage through the lock is as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} government vessels;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} passenger vessels and excursion boats; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} vessels principally engaged in carrying freight and
    pleasure boats.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b)
  is within such distance of the lock that it would be delayed if any
  other vessel lying in the tier and over which it has precedence were
  passed through, the lock shall be held for the passage of the vessel
  that has precedence.

## [Requirements for Vessels Using the Lock or Bridge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936795 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), no vessel shall be permitted to enter the approach
  unless the owner of the vessel has given a ship report completed and
  signed by the owner, to the lock-master.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship report referred to in subsection (1) shall
  indicate the name of the vessel, its point of departure, its
  destination and any other particulars that may be required by the
  Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a vessel that measures 40 feet or less
  in length, no such vessel shall be permitted to enter the approach
  unless the owner signed a log book maintained and kept by the
  lock-master and has indicated therein the name of the vessel, its
  point of departure, its destination and any other particulars that may
  be required by the Minister.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of a vessel
shall comply with any order of the lock-master during the vessel's entry
in or passage through the approach or the lock.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel using the
lock, other than a vessel that measures 40 feet or less in length, shall
be accurately gauged and clearly marked at the bow and stern to show its
draught fore and aft.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel using the
lock shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have all protruding gear and equipment stored and
  secured inboard;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure their anchors and stow their cargo in a manner
  that prevents any damage to the lock, dam, bridge and related
  facilities or other vessels;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have all discharge outlets covered with hoods in a
  manner that permits discharge below the lock coping; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} use vertical fenders on both sides of the vessel when
  passing through the lock.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel using the
lock shall be equipped with lights as set out in the [Collision
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1416).

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every vessel using the
lock shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has a gross tonnage of 40 tons or less,
  be equipped with at least two lines or hawsers of adequate strength,
  one at the bow and one at the quarter; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has a gross tonnage of more than 40
  tons, be equipped with lines of sufficient number and attended by a
  sufficient number of crew members

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to check the speed of the vessel while entering the
    lock,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to prevent the vessel from striking against the
    lock gates or other parts of the lock, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to keep the vessel in proper position while the
    lock is being filled or emptied.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every owner of a vessel
using the lock shall obey the following traffic light signals installed
at the lock:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a red light, indicating that the lock is not ready
  for passage;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a flashing red light, indicating that the lock is
  being made ready for passage; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a green light, indicating that the lock is ready for
  passage.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  owner of a vessel shall attempt to enter into or depart from the lock
  until the lock-gate is fully open.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No owner of a vessel shall attempt to overtake
  another vessel while the overtaking vessel is within 300 m of the lock
  gate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vessel's engines shall be stopped while the
  vessel's propeller is passing over the mitre sills of the lock.

<!-- -->

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  owner of a vessel approaching the lock or bridge shall ensure that the
  lock or bridge is ready for passage and shall be prepared to stop the
  vessel in sufficient time to avoid a collision with the lock,
  lock-gate or bridge.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every owner of a vessel approaching the lock or
  bridge shall, while any other vessel proceeding in the opposite
  direction is in or about to leave the lock, stop and moor the vessel
  to the posts placed for that purpose and remain there until that other
  vessel has left the lock.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No vessel shall, while in
the approach or the lock, proceed at a speed not exceeding 10 km per
hour.

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Vessels waiting to enter the lock
  shall lie in a single tier at a distance of not less than 50 m from
  the lock, except where climatic conditions may, in the opinion of the
  Superintendent, otherwise require.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each vessel shall, for the purpose of passing through
  the lock, advance in the order set out in section 6.

<!-- -->

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  approach shall be used only by vessels requesting passage at the next
  available locking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No owner of a vessel shall proceed into the approach
  where the condition of the vessel might delay navigation or cause
  damage to the lock, dam, bridge or related facilities or to another
  vessel.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The owner of any vessel
shall, at the request of the Superintendent, forthwith remove the vessel
from the lock or the approach, as the case may be.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No vessel drawing more
than 2.75 m shall be permitted to enter the lock.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No person shall tie any
line for the purpose of mooring a vessel on any telephone or hydro pole
situated on the lock.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use on a
vessel a searchlight in such a manner that the rays of the searchlight
interfere with the navigation of another vessel or the operation of the
lock.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** While a vessel is passing
through the lock, the owner of the vessel shall, on request of the
lock-master, provide the help of the crew to assist in working the lock.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Where a vessel that is
blown or otherwise held on a lee bank in the approach, its crew members
shall not attempt to work itself off the bank with its engine and wheel,
but shall use lines to the opposite side, and heave out into the
approach with its capstan.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** No person, other than a
person employed in the operation of the lock or dam, shall use the
service or operating floors of the dam.

## [Mooring and Fastening]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936839 .Part}

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lock-master shall assign berths for all vessels where it is necessary
  to do so.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lock-master or Superintendent may change any
  berth assigned under subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every owner of a vessel shall comply promptly with
  any order of the lock-master or Superintendent.

## [Refuse]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936844 .Part}

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No person shall throw or
deposit refuse into the lock, in the approach, or on the banks of the
lock.

## [Explosives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936846 .Part}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No vessel carrying on
board gunpowder, dynamite, nitro- glycerine or any other explosive,
shall pass through the approach or the lock unless written authority is
given for such entry or passage by the Minister, and the conditions of
that written authority are met.

## [Traffic on Bridge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936848 .Part}

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall drive or transport across the bridge any vehicle or
  machinery that exceeds the gross weight posted on the bridge
  approaches.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall drive a motor vehicle across the
  bridge at a speed exceeding the limit posted on the bridge or bridge
  approaches.

## [Interference with Officials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936852 .Part}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** No person shall interfere
with or obstruct the Superintendent or lock-master or any other employee
while in the execution or performance of the duties of the
Superintendent or lock-master or employee.

## [Damages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936854 .Part}

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a vessel does injury to the lock, dam, bridge or related facilities,
  the Superintendent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall estimate the damages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall notify the owner of the vessel in writing, of
    the estimated damages to be paid; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may seize and detain that vessel, at the risk of
    the owner, until the owner has paid those damages or provided
    security to the Superintendent to cover the amount of damages.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a vessel referred to in subsection (1)
  shall, on notification of the estimate of the damages, pay that
  estimate or provide to the Superintendent sufficient security to cover
  the amount of damages.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The provision of security in relation to an estimate
  of damages referred to in subsection (2) shall not relieve the owner
  from liability to pay the amount set out in the notice referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a vessel is seized and detained pursuant to
  subsection (1), and the estimated damages are not paid or security for
  payment for the cost of repair of damage is not made within 30 days
  following the seizure and detention, the vessel seized and detained
  may be sold by public auction and the proceeds applied in payment of
  the damages.

## [Fine]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936863 .Part}

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Every person who violates
any provision of these Regulations is guilty of an offence punishable on
summary conviction and is liable to a fine not exceeding \$400.
