## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020853 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[angling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing with hook and line.
      ([pêche à la ligne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance that attracts fish
      by scent or flavour and includes a lure to which scent or
      flavouring has been added. ([appât]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bait fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bait fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fish of a species set
      out in Part 2 of Schedule 1. ([poisson-appât]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[barbless hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[barbless hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a hook the barbs of
      which are pressed against the shaft of the hook so that the barbs
      are not functional. ([hameçon sans ardillon]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Sustainable Resource Development of the Province.
      ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dip net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dip net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device consisting of a
      bag-like net that is mounted on a ring or frame with or without a
      pole or handle attached. ([épuisette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Director of Fisheries
      Management of the Fish and Wildlife Division of the Department.
      ([directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fish management zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fish management zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area set out in Schedule 2.
      ([zone de gestion du poisson]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} *ou*
      [ZGP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[game fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[game fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fish of a species set
      out in Part 1 of Schedule 1. ([poisson de sport]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[gill net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gill net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a net by which fish are
      caught by being enmeshed but that does not enclose an area of
      water. ([filet maillant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hook]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single or multi-pointed hook
      on a common shaft and includes a hook that is attached to a lure.
      ([hameçon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hook size]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hook size]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the measurement between
      the shaft and the tip of a hook. ([taille de
      l'hameçon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian within the terms of
      Paragraph 12 of the Memorandum of Agreement, known as the Alberta
      Natural Resources Transfer Agreement, being the agreement entered
      into between Canada and Alberta on December 14, 1929 and confirmed
      by the [Constitution Act, 1930](/eng/Const/).
      ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fish, means the

  [[lure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a spoon, plug, jig, fly or

  :   [[mealworms]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the larvae of terrestrial

  [[mesh size]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      measured

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} after the net has been immersed in water for at
        least 30 minutes; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with the mesh extended without straining the
        twine. ([maillage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order issued under
      subsection 3(1) in relation to any waters to which these
      Regulations apply. ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province of Alberta.
      ([province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Fisheries

      provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of

      provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[seine net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[seine net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a net the ends of which
      are brought together or hauled ashore and includes a bar seine,
      beach seine, drag seine, purse seine or other similar type of net.
      ([seine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[snagging]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[snagging]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means attempting to catch or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by intentionally piercing and hooking a fish in

  [[snagging device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[snagging device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an instrument that is designed for the purpose
        of snagging fish; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hooks or lures that are altered to facilitate

        lang="fr"})

  [[sportfishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sportfishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fishing by means of
      angling, bow and arrow, spear, dip net, seine net or minnow trap.

      ([pêche sportive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trap net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trap net]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is designed

  :   [[tributary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a watercourse that flows

  [[trout]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[watershed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area drained by one

      directly connected to the stream. ([bassin
      versant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[watershed unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[watershed unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a subdivision of a fish
      management zone set out in Schedule 2. ([unité de bassin
      versant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} *ou* [unité]{.DefinedTermLink

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reference in these Regulations to a species or

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the boundary

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020900 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply in the Province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing in, or fish taken from, waters to which the
    National Parks Fishing Regulations apply;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing in a fish culture facility; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing for, or the possession of, freshwater
    shrimp.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020909 .Part}

### [Variation of Close Times, Fishing Quotas and Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020910 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  close time, fishing quota or limit on the size or weight of fish is
  fixed in respect of an area under these Regulations, the Director may,
  by order, vary that close time, fishing quota or limit in respect of
  that area or any portion of it.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota or limit is varied
  under subsection (1), the Director shall give notice of the variation
  to the persons affected or likely to be affected by it, by one or more
  of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} broadcasting the notice over a radio station that
    broadcasts in the area or vicinity of the area affected by the
    variation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicating the notice by telephone to those
    persons;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} posting the notice in the area or in the vicinity
    of the area affected by the variation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} having a fishery officer give oral notice of the
    variation to those persons;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} transmitting the notice via electronic means to
    those persons; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} publishing the notice in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a newspaper circulated in the vicinity of the
      area affected by the variation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Alberta Gazette,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Guide to Sportfishing Regulations published
      on behalf of the Department, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Guide to Commercial Fishing Seasons
      published by the Department.

### [Waters Closed to All Fishing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020924 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized by a
Research Licence issued under the provincial Act, no person shall fish
in the waters set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 3 during a
close time set out in column 2 of that item.

### [Possession and Use of Live Fish]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020926 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall possess
live fish unless the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is authorized to do so under the provincial Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in angling, caught the fish in the waters
  in which the person is angling, and the fish are within 5 m of those
  waters.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2012-21, s. 1
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place live
fish in any waters other than the waters from which they were taken
unless the person is authorized to do so by a licence issued under the
provincial Act.

### [Release of Prohibited Fish]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020933 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person who catches a
fish the possession or retention of which is prohibited under these
Regulations or the provincial Act shall immediately return that fish to
the waters from which it was caught, and, if the fish is alive, release
it in a manner that causes it the least harm.

### [Placing of Bait in Waters of the Province]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020935 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place or
cause to be placed in any waters bait of any kind unless it is attached
to a hook.

### [Lights Prohibited]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020937 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use any
light while fishing other than a light that is attached to a hook or
line used in angling.

### [Gear Prohibited]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020939 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall engage in
fishing with a snare, a firearm, a gaff hook or any device that can be
used to pass an electric current through water to attract, stun or kill
fish, unless the method of fishing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is authorized by a licence issued under the
  provincial Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not adversely affect the conservation or
  protection of the fish in the fishery to which the permit applies.

### [Sale of Fish]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020943 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall buy,
sell, trade, barter or offer to buy, sell, trade or barter any fish
unless it was caught and retained under the authority of a licence in
which the sale of fish is authorized and that is issued under the Act or
the provincial Act.

### [Molest, Disturb or Remove Fish]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020945 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No person shall molest or
disturb fish or remove fish from a facility or structure that is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} erected by the Department, or under the authority of
  a research licence issued under the provincial Act, and that is used
  or designed to catch or hold fish; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designed to facilitate the passage of fish.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Authority to Fish]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020949 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fish except as authorized by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provincial Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection (2); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued under subsection (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 18, an Indian may engage in
  sportfishing without a licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The provincial Minister may issue a licence to an
  Indian to engage in fishing solely for the purpose of catching fish
  for food for their personal use or for the use of their immediate
  family.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No fee shall be charged to issue a licence under
  subsection (3).

**[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1 .sectionLabel}** For the proper
management and control of fisheries and the conservation and protection
of fish, the provincial Minister may specify in a licence issued under
subsection 13(3) any condition that is not inconsistent with these
Regulations and in particular, but not restricting the generality of the
foregoing, may specify conditions respecting any of the following
matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the species of fish and the quantities of fish that
  are permitted to be taken or transported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the age, sex, stage of development or size of fish
  that are permitted to be taken or transported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the waters in which fishing is permitted to be
  carried out;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the location from which and to which fish is
  permitted to be transported;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person or persons who may conduct fishing
  operations under the licence;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the period during which fishing or transporting fish
  is permitted to be carried out;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the vessel that is permitted to be used for fishing
  and the persons who are permitted to operate it;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the type, size and quantity of fishing gear and
  equipment that are permitted to be used and the manner in which they
  are permitted to be used;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the specific location at which fishing gear is
  permitted to be set;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the depth of water at which fishing is permitted;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the range of the depth of water within which fishing
  gear may be deployed;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the distance to be maintained between fishing gear;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} information to be reported by the holder of the
  licence to the Department before the start of a fishing trip with
  respect to where and when fishing will be carried out, including the
  means by which, the times at which and the person to whom the report
  is to be made;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} records that the licence holder shall keep of any
  fishing activity carried out under the licence including the manner
  and form in which the records are to be kept, the times at which and
  the person to whom the records are to be produced and the period
  during which the records are to be retained;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the marking or tagging of fish for the purpose of
  identifying the source of the fish;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} the segregation of fish by species before and during
  transport;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} the period within which findings and data obtained as
  a result of fishing are to be forwarded to the Director;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} the species of fish, including fish of particular
  sizes, that must be released and the manner in which they are to be
  released; and

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the release of fish is
  permitted to be carried out.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Sportfishing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020980 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020981 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies only to
sportfishing.

### [Close Times]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020983 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sportfish
in the waters set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 4 during a
close time set out in column 2 of that item.

### [Catch Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020985 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall in any day catch and retain from the waters set out in
  column 2 of an item of Schedule 5 more fish of any species set out in
  column 1 of that item than the quota set out in column 3 of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall catch and retain from the waters set
  out in column 2 of an item of Schedule 5 a fish of any species set out
  in column 1 of that item that is of a prohibited length as set out in
  column 4 of that item.

### [Possession Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020989 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall possess more fish of a species than the daily quota
  determined under subsection 16(1) for that species for the waters in
  which the person is fishing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall at any time possess more fish of a
  species set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 6 than the quota
  set out in column 2 of that item.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** No person shall possess a
lake sturgeon unless authorized by a Sturgeon Fishing Licence issued
under the provincial Act.

### [Angling Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1020994 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall angle
using

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than three hooks attached to a line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a hook with more than three points on a common shaft;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a lure with more than three hooks as part of it;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in open water, more than one line;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in ice-covered water, more than two lines;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a spring-loaded hook; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any fish as bait, other than dead bait fish, dead
  smelt, dead herring, dead shrimp, dead fish eggs or the skin, fins or
  eyes of game fish caught by angling.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-349, s. 2
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fish by snagging;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} possess fish taken by snagging;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} possess a snagging device; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} possess a gaff hook while angling.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No person angling shall
be more than 30 m from any line that the person has in the water.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** No person shall angle
through ice in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any flowing waters in Fish Management Zone 1, except
  the portion of the Lobstick River lying in 53, 54-9, 10-W5; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any beaver pond.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** No person shall fish with
prohibited gear or bait set out in column 1 of an item of Schedule 7 in
waters set out in column 2 of that item during the close time set out in
column 3 of that item.

### [Dip Net, Minnow Trap and Seine Net Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021015 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No person shall fish with
a dip net, seine net or minnow trap

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for any fish other than bait fish or crayfish; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any waters set out in column 2 of items 3 to 6 of
  Schedule 7 during the close times set out in column 3 of those items.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-34, s. 1
:::

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fish with a minnow trap unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is visibly and legibly marked with the person's
    name and address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it does not exceed 60 m in length or 30 cm in
    width, depth or diameter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fish with more than two minnow traps.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall fish with
a seine net that exceeds 3 m in length or 2 m in depth.

### [Bow and Arrow Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021027 .Subheading}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fish with a bow and arrow for Arctic grayling, trout,
  mountain whitefish, lake sturgeon, walleye or northern pike.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fish with a crossbow.

### [Spear Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1021033 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall fish with a spear except when swimming and unless the
  spear is propelled by a spring, an elastic, compressed gas or by hand.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), no person shall fish with a
  spear for Arctic grayling, trout, mountain whitefish, lake sturgeon,
  walleye or northern pike.

- SOR/99-349, s. 4

River watersheds and the Slave River and Lake Athabasca watersheds, and
