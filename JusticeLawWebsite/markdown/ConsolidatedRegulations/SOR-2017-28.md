## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842843 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Border Services
    Agency. ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gypsum board products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gypsum board products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means [[subject
    goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in paragraph 30 of the
    finding of the Canadian International Trade Tribunal issued on
    January 4, 2017 in Inquiry Number NQ-2016-002. ([produits de plaques
    de plâtre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[import value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[import value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value calculated for
    B in the formula set out in paragraph 2(1)(b) as if it were the
    export price in sections 24 and 25 of the Act. ([valeur à
    l'importation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reference value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value calculated
    for A in the formula set out in paragraph 2(1)(b). ([valeur de
    référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842850 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of the
  anti-dumping duties paid or payable under the Act for each gypsum
  board product in the amount by which such duties exceed the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an amount calculated by the following formula:

    ::: {#842855 .Paragraph}
    A -- B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is the reference value that corresponds to the normal value, per
        thousand square feet, calculated at the time of the final
        determination rendered, in respect of the investigation, under
        section 41 of the Act for future shipments of the gypsum board
        product, reduced by 32.17%; and

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the import value per thousand square feet of the gypsum board
        product calculated,
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if paragraph 25(1)(b) of the Act applies,
          under section 25 of the Act, without any deduction for duties
          set out in paragraph 25(1)(c) or (d) of the Act, other than
          those imposed by the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, under section 24 of the
          Act.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the import value calculated for B in the formula
  set out in subsection (1) is less than the reference value calculated
  for A in that formula, the amount calculated under subsection (1) is
  reduced by the difference between the reference value and the import
  value.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section,
  [[investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the investigation that was
  initiated on June 8, 2016 under subsection 31(1) of the Act and that
  led to the finding of the Canadian International Trade Tribunal issued
  on January 4, 2017 regarding the injurious dumping of gypsum board
  products.

## [Revision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842863 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The reference value for
each gypsum board product is indexed annually, beginning on January 1,
2018, according to the Industrial Product Price Index for gypsum product
manufacturing for the 12-month period ending on September 30 of the
immediately preceding calendar year.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842865 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on the
following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the day on which the gypsum board products are accounted for
  under section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request by the Agency, the importer files with the
  Agency the evidence or information that the Agency requires to
  determine eligibility for the remission;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer agrees that it is subject at any time,
  including after the remission, to audit by the Agency for the purpose
  of determining whether the information supplied by the importer under
  paragraph (a) or (b) is accurate and complete and whether the facts on
  which the Agency relied or intends to rely to determine the
  eligibility for remission remain unchanged in all material respects;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} at the time when the Agency conducts the audit
  referred to in paragraph (c), the Agency must be able to conclude that
  the information supplied remains accurate and complete and that the
  facts remain unchanged in all material respects.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842871 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
