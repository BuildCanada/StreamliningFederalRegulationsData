## Wrigley Airport Zoning Regulations {#wrigley-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-507
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1995-10-31

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Wrigley Airport

P.C. 1995-1814 1995-10-31
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1){#fn_1-ID0EHADA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed [Regulations respecting
zoning at Wrigley Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-507), substantially
in the form annexed hereto, was published in two successive issues of
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on June 24 and
July 1, 1995, and in two successive issues of The News of the North on
June 19 and 26, 1995, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded
to interested persons to make representations to the Minister of
Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2){#fn_2-ID0EFACA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Wrigley
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-507).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976302 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Wrigley Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-507).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976304 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Wrigley Airport, in the
      vicinity of Wrigley, in the Northwest Territories;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de référence de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary plane
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      plane is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
      ([surfaceextérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, that is
      prepared for the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular
      direction, which rectangular portion is more particularly
      described in Part IV of the schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly described in Part V of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 146.6 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976314 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all land, including public road allowances, that is adjacent to or in
the vicinity of the airport, which land is more particularly described
in Part VI of the schedule.

## [Building Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976316 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on land to which these Regulations apply, a building,
structure or object or an addition to an existing building, structure or
object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976321 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth that is on land to which these Regulations apply grows to a
height that exceeds in elevation at the location of the object any of
the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister of Transport may
require that the owner or lessee of the land remove the excess growth.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 3)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-976324 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Wrigley Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.3040, dated December 5, 1994, is a point located on the centre
line of runway 10-28 at a distance of 533.4 m from the threshold of
runway 10.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Wrigley Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3040,
dated December 5, 1994, are planes abutting each end of the strip
associated with runway 10-28 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 10 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 83.3 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 28 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally, rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 2 500 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 295 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 83.3 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Wrigley Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3040,
dated December 5, 1994, is an imaginary plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point,
except that, where that plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, the outer surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 10-28, shown on Wrigley Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.3040, dated December 5, 1994, is 90 m in width, 45 m being on
each side of the centre line of the runway, and 1 186.8 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Wrigley Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.3040, dated December 5, 1994, is an inclined plane rising at a
ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right
angles to the centre line and projected centre line of the strip,
extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Land to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-land-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The outer boundary of the land to which these Regulations apply, shown
on Wrigley Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3040, dated December 5, 1994, is a
circle with a radius of 4 000 m centred on the airport reference point.
