## Telecommunications and Electronic Maintenance Services Fees Order {#telecommunications-and-electronic-maintenance-services-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-319
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1981-04-09

Order Respecting Fees for Provision of Telecommunications and Electronic
Maintenance Services Provided by the Department of Transport
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Transport, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-928 of
2 April, 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238){#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing fees or charges to be paid
for the services provided in accordance with any agreement entered into
pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-928 of 2 April, 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238){#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238-ID0EBBCA},
in accordance with schedule hereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238-ID0EDBCA){#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6238}SI/81-61,
  1981 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1229
  :::

Dated in Ottawa, April 8, 1981

::: FlushRight
J. L. PEPIN
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Transport*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870753 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Telecommunications and Electronic Maintenance Services Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-319).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870755 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Fees for telecommunications
and electronic maintenance services provided by the Department of
Transport as a result of any agreement entered into pursuant to Order in
Council P.C. 1981-928 of 2 April, 1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6240){#fn_SOR-81-319_e_hq_6240-ID0EBBA}
shall be charged in accordance with the schedule.

  [[Return to footnote

  1981 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1229
  :::
