## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699000 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order
[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that would have been eligible
for the Canada-Israel Agreement Tariff rate of duty as of December 31,
1997 had they been imported on that date, but are not eligible for that
rate of duty if imported on or after January 1, 2002.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699002 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on goods imported during the
period commencing on January 1, 2002, and ending on December 31, 2002,
in an amount equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties paid or payable at the
  Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff rate or the General Preferential Tariff
  rate of duty for that good, as the case may be, under the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the regulations made under it, as they
  read on January 1, 2002, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties that would be payable at the
  Canada-Israel Agreement Tariff rate of duty for the good, as if that
  good qualified for that rate of duty, in accordance with the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), and the regulations made under it, as
  they read on January 1, 2002.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699006 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of National
Revenue within four years after the day on which the goods are imported.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699008 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
