## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737538 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[adjacent area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjacent area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a First Nation
    set out in Schedule 1, means the incorporated or unincorporated area
    bordering a majority of those reserves of the First Nation that
    contain right-of-way areas. ([zone adjacente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[adjustment factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjustment factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the adjustment
    factor set out in the Railway Corporations Assessment Regulation,
    B.C. Reg. 51/2016, as amended from time to time. ([facteur de
    rajustement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fibre optic improvements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fibre optic improvements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fibre optic
    fibres, sheaths, wrapping, conduits, cables and other related
    improvements. ([équipement de fibres optiques]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[incorporated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incorporated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area incorporated
    as a municipality under the Local Government Act of British
    Columbia. ([zone constituée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a right-of-way, any other
    right or interest in lands, or
    improvements. ([propriété]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial taxation laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial taxation laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the laws and
    regulations of British Columbia respecting property taxation,
    including the Assessment Act, Railway Corporations Assessment
    Regulation, Hospital District Act, School Act, Local Government Act
    and Taxation (Rural Area) Act, as amended from time to time. ([lois
    fiscales provinciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[right-of-way area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[right-of-way area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a First
    Nation set out in column 1 of Schedule 1, means the lands described
    in column 2. ([zone d'emprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[track in place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[track in place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    [[track in place of a railway corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    subsection 21(15) of the Assessment Act of British Columbia, as
    amended from time to time. ([voie ferrée existante]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737551 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
property taxation laws made by the council of a First Nation set out in
Schedule 1 in respect of taxation of property in a right-of-way area.

## [Property Taxation Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-737554 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of assessable value

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a property taxation law in respect of taxation of
  property in a right-of-way area shall provide that an assessor must
  determine the assessable value of the following types of property
  using the assessment rates, adjustments, exceptions, inclusions and
  exclusions that would be applied if the property were subject to
  provincial taxation laws:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} track in place of a railway company, including
    fibre optic improvements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rights-of-way for track referred to in
    paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} bridges of a railway company;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fibre optic improvements of a non-railway company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} utility systems of a non-railway company, including
    pipeline, cable, telephone, power, sewer, gas and related
    facilities; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} other improvements lawfully located in a
    right-of-way area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Adjustment factors do not apply to the determination
  of assessable values of property under a property taxation law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum tax rates for railway companies

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), the tax rate for a taxation year established under
  a property taxation law enacted by a First Nation set out in column 1
  of Schedule 2 in respect of property of a railway company in a
  right-of-way area shall not exceed the rate equal to the sum of the
  products, determined for each of the tax bases set out in column 2, of

  ::: {#737571 .Subsection}
  A×B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the tax rate established for that taxation year under
      provincial taxation laws for that tax base; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the adjustment factor for that tax base.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum tax rates for non-railway
  companies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the tax rate for a
  taxation year established under a property taxation law enacted by a
  First Nation set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 in respect of
  improvements of a company other than a railway company in a
  right-of-way area shall not exceed the rate equal to the sum of the
  tax rates, established for that taxation year under provincial
  taxation laws, for the tax bases set out in column 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions and inclusions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A tax rate referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  incorporate the same exemptions, exceptions, inclusions and exclusions
  that would be applicable to the property if the property were subject
  to the provincial taxation laws applicable to the adjacent area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment factors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the adjustment
  factors applicable to the determination of tax rates shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the Adams Lake Indian Band, Chawathil
    First Nation, Cook's Ferry Indian Band, Kanaka Bar Indian Band,
    Leq'a:mel First Nation, Little Shuswap Lake Indian Band, Matsqui
    First Nation, Neskonlith Indian Band and Shuswap First Nation, the
    adjustment factors that apply to property in incorporated areas; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the Seabird Island Indian Band, the
    adjustment factors that apply in the District of Kent.
