## Domestic Spirits Destroyed Remission Order {#domestic-spirits-destroyed-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/87-116
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-06-24

Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Duty Imposed Under Section 135
of the Excise Act in Respect of Domestic Spirits Destroyed as Being
Unfit for Human Consumption

P.C. 1987-1135 1987-06-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of National Revenue, the Minister of Finance and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of the excise duty imposed under section
135 of the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) in respect of domestic spirits
destroyed as being unfit for human consumption.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641293 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Domestic Spirits Destroyed Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-87-116).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641295 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any board, commission or agency
    of the government that, by the law of a province, is empowered to
    sell or to authorize the sale and delivery of spirits;
    ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distiller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distiller]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person to whom a license
    is granted pursuant to section 129 of the [Excise
    Act](/eng/acts/E-14); ([distillateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[domestic spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[domestic spirits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any material or
    substance that is made in Canada whether in liquid or in any other
    form, containing any proportion by mass or by volume of absolute
    ethyl alcohol (C~2~H~5~OH), other than beer or malt liquor.
    ([eau-de-vie fabriquée au Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641301 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the excise duty paid or payable after
March 1, 1986 by the distiller or agency pursuant to section 135 of the
[Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14) in respect of domestic spirits destroyed as
being unfit for human consumption.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641303 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted
pursuant to section 3 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is made to the Minister
  within two years after the domestic spirits are destroyed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is made in respect of domestic
  spirits that are unfit for human consumption;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application specifies the quantity of domestic
  spirits, as determined by the distiller under the supervision of an
  excise officer, for which the remission is sought;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the domestic spirits are destroyed in a manner
  approved by the Minister; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by such other evidence
  as is necessary to demonstrate to the Minister that the applicant is
  entitled to the remission.
