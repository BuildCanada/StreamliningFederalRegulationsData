## Related Party Transactions (Banks) Regulations {#related-party-transactions-banks-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-309
:::

[BANK ACT](/eng/acts/B-1.01)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Prescribing Certain Transactions  and Classes of
Transactions that a Bank may  Enter into with Related Parties of the
Bank

P.C. 1992-1084 1992-05-21
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 500 and 559 of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing certain transactions
and classes of transactions that a bank may enter into with related
parties of the bank, effective June 1, 1992.

## [Prescribed Related Party Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944108 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 500 of the Act, a bank that is a foreign bank
  subsidiary may enter into the following transactions with the foreign
  bank that controls it, or with a financial institution controlled by
  that foreign bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transaction that forms an integral part of
    banking services provided to customers of the foreign bank
    subsidiary, of the foreign bank or of the financial institution; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a deposit by the foreign bank subsidiary with the
    foreign bank or the financial institution

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a term not longer than 30 days,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for short-term liquidity management purposes,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in an amount that, added to the aggregate of
      the amounts of all other deposits of the foreign bank subsidiary
      with its related parties, does not exceed 50 per cent of the
      regulatory capital of the foreign bank subsidiary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the class of transactions referred to in paragraph (1)(a) includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} interest rate, currency, equity and commodity
    swaps;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} foreign exchange forwards, options and spot
    transactions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forward rate agreements;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} options on interest rates, futures, commodities,
    equity indices, shares and ownership interests;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} futures on interest rates, currencies and
    commodities;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} transactions involving money market instruments;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} loan syndications and asset sales;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} transactions involving commodity forwards;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} repurchase agreements and other transactions in
    respect of securities; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} derivative transactions of transactions referred to
    in any of paragraphs (a) to (i).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
500 of the Act, a bank may, directly or indirectly, enter into any of
the following transactions with a related party of the bank:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an allocation of an amount by or to the bank pursuant
  to subsection 18(2.3), 125(3), 127(10.3), 137(2), (4.1), (4.2) or
  (5.1), 181.5(2), 190.15(2) or 191.1(3) of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bank agrees to pay all or a portion of the tax
    of the related party, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the related party agrees to pay the bank an amount
    at least equal to the additional costs incurred by the bank,
    including additional taxes payable under the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as a result of the bank entering into the
    agreement or amended agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which the related party agrees to pay all or a portion of the tax of
  the bank;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issue by or to the bank of conversion privileges,
  options or rights to acquire securities or membership shares the
  issuance of which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and
  conditions pursuant to which a security or membership share is being
  or has been issued;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, for the purpose of cancellation, of any
  shares, membership shares or debt obligations issued by or to the bank
  the purchase of which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and
  conditions pursuant to which the shares, membership shares or debt
  obligations were issued;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the redemption of any redeemable shares, membership
  shares or any debt obligations issued by or to the bank the redemption
  of which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which the shares, membership shares or debt obligations
  were issued; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the payment or delivery of money or property by or to
  the bank as a consequence of a reduction of the stated capital of the
  bank or related party.
