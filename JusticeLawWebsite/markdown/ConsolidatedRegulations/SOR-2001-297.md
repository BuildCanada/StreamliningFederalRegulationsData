## Order Declaring the Pickering Lands as an Airport Site {#order-declaring-the-pickering-lands-as-an-airport-site .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-297
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 2001-08-01

Order Declaring the Pickering Lands as an Airport Site

P.C. 2001-1406 2001-08-01
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the title to the lands whose land titles are set out in the
schedule hereto is vested in Her Majesty in right of Canada;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to the definition
[[airport site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} in subsection
5.4(1) of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), hereby declares that the
lands whose land titles are set out in the schedule hereto are required
for use as an airport.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}R.S., c. 33
  (1st Supp.), s. 1
  :::
:::

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-672267 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The lands of the Pickering airport site, to which the land titles are
set out in this schedule, excepting the lands described in instruments
registered under the numbers D165716 and D494890 in the Registry
Division of Durham, being lands in the Province of Ontario, are shown
outlined in blue on a plan indexed as Item No. 97-30-1, dated July 30,
1997, on record at Public Works and Government Services Canada at North
York, Province of Ontario.
