## Adjustment Assistance Benefit Regulations (Footwear and Tanning Workers) {#adjustment-assistance-benefit-regulations-footwear-and-tanning-workers .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 317
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 5, 1973

Regulations Respecting Provision for Adjustment Assistance Benefits for
Displaced Workers In The Footwear and Tanning Industries
::::

:::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549158 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Adjustment Assistance Benefit Regulations (Footwear and
Tanning Workers)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._317).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549160 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Unemployment Insurance Act,
    1971; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[average weekly insurable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[average weekly insurable earnings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with
    respect to an employee who is eligible to receive pre-retirement
    benefit, means the average of his weekly insurable earnings, as
    determined pursuant to the Act, from employment by a manufacturer
    for the 20 weeks of employment nearest to the effective date of his
    lay-off; ([rémunération hebdomadaire moyenne
    assurable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Enterprise Development Board
    established by section 4 of the [Enterprise Development
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._969); ([Commission d'aide
    générale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[board of referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board of referees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a board of referees
    established by the Act; ([conseil arbitral]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate issued by
    the Board pursuant to section 3; ([certificat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Employment and
    Immigration Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[effective date of lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[effective date of lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date of
    lay-off of an employee that occurs within a lay-off period; ([date
    d'effet de la mise à pied]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is included in a
    lay-off; ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employer named in a
    certificate; ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[footwear]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of footwear other
    than footwear the main component of which is canvas;
    ([chaussure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lay-off]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the involuntary separation
    from employment for an indefinite period of an employee of a
    manufacturer solely as a result of a reduction by that manufacturer
    in the number of employees employed by him; ([mise à
    pied]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person, firm or corporation or a division
      thereof in Canada engaged in a manufacturing or processing
      activity in the footwear or tanning industries, or both, on
      January 1, 1974,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corporation restructured by means of the
      acquisition of one or more corporations or divisions thereof
      referred to in paragraph (a), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new corporation formed after January 1, 1974 by
      the merger or acquisition of two or more corporations or divisions
      thereof referred to in paragraph (a);
      ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    an activity whereby any footwear or leather

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is made, fabricated, processed or refined out of
      any raw material or other substance or combination thereof, and
      includes the tanning of any raw material or other substance or
      combination thereof, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is made by causing any raw material or other
      substance to undergo a significant chemical, biochemical or
      physical change including any change that preserves or improves
      the keeping qualities of that raw material or other substance but
      excluding any change by growth or decay; ([travaux de fabrication
      ou de traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum pre-retirement benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum pre-retirement benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    meaning assigned by section 9; ([prestation maximale
    d'avant-retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Labour;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pre-retirement benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pre-retirement benefit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    pre-retirement benefit payable under these Regulations; ([prestation
    d'avant-retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying week]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    employee, means a week during which the employee was employed by a
    manufacturer and had insurable earnings as determined pursuant to
    the Act; ([semaine donnant droit à l'aide]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change that, in the
    opinion of the Board, is significant in the operations of a
    manufacturer in respect of the products, methods of production,
    markets or management procedures and, if directly related to those
    operations, includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition, merger or amalgamation of one or
      more manufacturers described in paragraph (a) of the definition
      "manufacturer",

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition of working capital, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition, construction or conversion of
      machinery, equipment, buildings, land or other facilities;
      ([restructuration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[umpire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[umpire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an umpire appointed under Part
    V of the Act. ([juge-arbitre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-786, s. 1
- SOR/79-283, s. 1
- SOR/79-624, s. 1
:::

## [Conditions of Payment of Pre-retirement Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549191 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  pre-retirement benefit shall be paid to any person unless he is a
  person or belongs to a class of persons certified by the Board to the
  Minister in accordance with subsection (3) as eligible to apply for
  that benefit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any labour union or unit thereof, or any other
  representative group of workers, representing any workers employed in
  Canada by a Canadian manufacturer or any Canadian manufacturer may
  apply to the Board, in the manner specified by its rules, for
  certification of those persons or classes of persons who are eligible
  to apply for pre-retirement benefit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not certify persons or classes of
  persons as eligible to apply for pre-retirement benefit unless an
  application has been made pursuant to subsection (2), it has carried
  out such investigation as it considers necessary for the purpose and,
  in its opinion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work force of a Canadian manufacturer has been
    or will be reduced through lay-offs

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by 10 per cent of the employees or 50 employees,
      whichever is the lesser, of the manufacturer, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a period of at least four weeks; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lay-offs referred to in paragraph (a) are
    directly attributable to import competition that resulted in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an increase in the market share in Canada of
      footwear produced by manufacturing or processing activities
      outside Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a decline in the production of footwear by a
      manufacturer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board shall not certify persons or classes of
  persons as eligible to apply for a pre-retirement benefit in respect
  of a lay-off that occurred before January 1, 1976 or after December
  31, 1982 where, in the opinion of the Board, the lay-off was directly
  attributable to import competition that resulted in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an increase in the market share in Canada of
    footwear produced by manufacturing or processing activities outside
    Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a decline in the production of footwear by a
    manufacturer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-283, s. 2
- SOR/79-624, s. 2
- SOR/80-463, s. 1
- SOR/81-59, s. 1
- SOR/81-1020, s. 1
:::

## [Eligibility for Pre-retirement Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549207 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (1.1) and (2), a pre-retirement benefit may be paid by
  the Commission out of monies appropriated by Parliament for the
  purpose in an amount calculated in accordance with these Regulations
  to an employee who is a resident of Canada if the Commission is
  satisfied that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for a benefit under the Act has been
    made and any benefit entitlement thereunder has been exhausted;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a pre-retirement benefit period in respect of the
    employee has been established pursuant to section 5;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employee has not attained the age of 65 years;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the employee is not eligible to receive a
    retirement pension under the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or
    the Quebec Pension Plan.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall not pay a pre-retirement
  benefit in respect of any period prior to April 1, 1979 to any person
  or group of persons whose certification pursuant to subsection 3(3)
  occurred on or after that date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Payments of pre-retirement benefit to an employee
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be suspended for such time as the
  certification referred to in paragraph 5(c) is suspended pursuant to
  section 6, in respect of that employee.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-283, s. 3
- SOR/79-624, s. 3
:::

## [Establishment of Pre-retirement Benefit Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549218 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A pre-retirement benefit
period is established in respect of an employee when, upon filing an
application for pre-retirement benefit, he proves that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} he had been employed by a Canadian manufacturer in at
  least 10 of the 15 years immediately preceding a lay-off and that he
  was paid by a manufacturer for at least 1,000 hours in each of those
  10 years;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} he was not less than 54 years of age but less than 65
  years of age at the date of the lay-off; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} he has been certified by the Commission as a person
  resident in Canada

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for whom there is no present prospect of employment
    with or without further training or relocation assistance, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who has accepted employment with earnings that are
    less than his average weekly insurable earnings.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-786, s. 2
:::

## [Certification Suspended]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549227 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The circumstances of every
employee certified as required by paragraph 5(c) shall be reviewed at
least once every 12 months by the Commission and if, upon review, any
such employee does not meet the requirements of that paragraph the
certification referred to therein shall be suspended.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-786, s. 3
:::

## [Disqualification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549231 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in respect of an application for pre-retirement benefit any employee
  or any person on his behalf makes a statement or representation that
  the employee knows to be false or misleading, the Commission may
  declare the employee to be disqualified from receiving pre-retirement
  benefit for a number of weeks not exceeding the first three of the
  weeks described in subsection (2) that occur after such day as the
  Commission may determine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a week described
  in this subsection is a week in respect of which an employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes a claim for pre-retirement benefit in the
    manner prescribed by the Commission; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would, but for this section, be entitled to receive
    pre-retirement benefit.

## [Duration of Pre-retirement Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549237 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every pre-retirement
benefit period established under these Regulations in respect of an
employee shall commence with the date on which his unemployment
insurance benefit period is terminated and shall terminate when he
attains the age of 65 years, or on the date on which he becomes eligible
to receive retirement pension under the [Canada Pension
Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or the Quebec Pension Plan, whichever is the
earlier.

## [Maximum Pre-retirement Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549239 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  maximum pre-retirement benefit of an employee in respect of whom a
  pre-retirement benefit period has been established under these
  Regulations is a weekly sum of money equal to 66 2/3 per cent of his
  average weekly insurable earnings, which amount shall be rounded to
  the nearest cent in accordance with subsection 12(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the insurable
  earnings of an employee for any week of employment pursuant to an
  agreement under section 37 of the Act shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount the employee would have earned in that
    week if he had worked a full working week for the same employer, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the maximum weekly insurable earnings of an insured
    person under the Act

  whichever is the lesser.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-786, s. 4
- SOR/79-283, s. 4
- SOR/80-88, s. 1
:::

## [Deduction for Earnings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549248 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** There shall be deducted
from pre-retirement benefits payable to an employee an amount equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.66 2/3 for each dollar obtained by the employee
  as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} earnings from employment or self-employment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pension benefits from employer pension plans that
    were earned as a result of employment,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} vacation pay, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any income other than that described in
    subparagraphs (i) to (iii) that is received during the
    pre-retirement benefit period established under these Regulations as
    a result of current or previous employment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$1 for each dollar obtained by the employee as
  unemployment insurance benefit received during a pre-retirement
  benefit period established under these Regulations.

## [Annual Adjustment of Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549256 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a lay-off occurs as a result of which a pre-retirement benefit is
  authorized to be paid to an employee, the monthly amount of that
  benefit shall be adjusted annually so that the pre-retirement benefit
  payable to the employee for a month in any year following the year in
  which the lay-off occurs is the product obtained by multiplying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pre-retirement benefit for that month

  by

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ratio that the Pension Index for the year bears
    to the Pension Index for the year in which the lay-off occurred.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, "Pension Index" has the same meaning
  as in section 43.1 of the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8), and
  the Pension Index for any year means the Pension Index for that year
  calculated in the manner described in that section.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-283, s. 5
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  the maximum pre-retirement benefit is adjusted annually pursuant to
  section 11,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product obtained by multiplying the amount
    referred to in paragraph 11(1)(a) by the ratio referred to in
    paragraph 11(1)(b) shall be adjusted to the nearest cent in
    accordance with subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quotient obtained from the ratio referred to in
    paragraph 11(1)(b) shall be expressed as a decimal fraction in
    accordance with subsection (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the product referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
  contains a fractional part of \$1, that fraction shall be expressed as
  a decimal fraction of three or more digits after the decimal point and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the third and subsequent digits after the decimal
    point shall be dropped if the third digit is less than five; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the second digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the third and subsequent digits shall be
    dropped if the third digit is five or greater than five.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the quotient referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  contains a fraction that is less than one, that fraction shall be
  expressed as a decimal fraction of four digits after the decimal
  point, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fourth digit after the decimal point shall be
    dropped if that digit is less than five; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the third digit after the decimal point shall be
    increased by one and the fourth digit dropped if the fourth digit is
    five or greater than five.

## [Procedure and Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549275 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Commission shall
administer these Regulations on behalf of the Minister and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} receive applications for pre-retirement benefit from
  employees;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the eligibility of employees to
  pre-retirement benefit and the amount and duration of the
  pre-retirement benefit payable to each such employee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pay pre-retirement benefit and, subject to these
  Regulations, make recovery of overpayments of pre-retirement benefit
  and of amounts paid pursuant to these Regulations to any person not
  entitled thereto; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make such reports to the Minister concerning the
  administration of these Regulations as he may require.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Sections 53, 54 and 55 of
the Act apply, with such modifications as the circumstances require, to
applications for pre-retirement benefit under these Regulations.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where an employee submits
an application for pre-retirement benefit on a day later than the day on
which he was first qualified to make the application and shows good
cause for the delay, the application may, in the manner and to the
extent prescribed by section 39 of the Unemployment Insurance
Regulations be regarded as having been submitted on a day earlier than
the day on which it was submitted.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission shall consider all applications submitted to it under these
  Regulations, and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is of the opinion that a pre-retirement
    benefit period has been established, it shall so declare; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is of the opinion that a pre-retirement
    benefit period has not been established, it shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} declare that a pre-retirement benefit period has
      not been established and state which of the conditions or
      requirements of these Regulations have not been complied with, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} refer the application, if practicable, within 14
      days from the day on which the application was submitted to it, to
      a board of referees for its decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding that a pre-retirement benefit period
  has been established, if the Commission is not satisfied that an
  employee has fulfilled all the other conditions or requirements for
  pre-retirement benefit or if it is of the opinion that the employee is
  or was disqualified or disentitled from receiving pre-retirement
  benefit it shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declare the employee to be disqualified or
    disentitled from receiving benefit for such days as it may
    determine, and state

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the grounds on which the employee is disqualified
      or disentitled, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the conditions or requirements that he does not
      fulfil; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer the application, if practicable, within 14
    days from the day on which the application was submitted to it, to a
    board of referees for its decision.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee has been declared disqualified or
  disentitled under paragraph (2)(a) for any day or days, there shall be
  deducted from the pre-retirement benefit otherwise payable to him in
  respect of the week in which such day or days fall, an amount equal to
  1/5 of the product obtained by multiplying the total number of such
  days in the week by the weekly pre-retirement benefit of that
  employee, but if the amount so calculated is not a multiple of \$1,
  the amount shall be rounded to the nearest dollar and \$0.50 shall be
  rounded to a full dollar.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) applies only in respect of days of
  disqualification or disentitlement falling on or after April 24, 1974.

<!-- -->

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  board of referees has power to consider and determine appeals from
  decisions made under these Regulations by the Commission and matters
  referred to it under these Regulations by the Commission and an appeal
  lies from a decision of a board of referees on any such appeal or
  reference to an umpire.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 91 to 99 and 101 to 105 of the Act and 62 to
  70 of the Unemployment Insurance Regulations apply to these
  Regulations with such modifications as the circumstances require.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549299 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an employee has received a pre-retirement benefit for any period in
  respect of which he is disqualified or to which he is otherwise not
  entitled, he is liable to repay an amount equal to the amount so
  received by him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), section 60 of the
  Unemployment Insurance Regulations applies with such modifications as
  the circumstances require to pre-retirement benefits paid to employees
  not entitled thereto.
