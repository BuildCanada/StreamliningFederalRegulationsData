## Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the Virtual Elimination List {#regulations-adding-perfluorooctane-sulfonate-and-its-salts-to-the-virtual-elimination-list .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2009-15
:::

[PERFLUOROOCTANE SULFONATE VIRTUAL ELIMINATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.3)

Registration 2009-01-13

Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the
Virtual Elimination List
::::

:::::::: section
::::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health, pursuant to
subsection 3(1) of the [Perfluorooctane Sulfonate Virtual Elimination
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDBCA}, hereby make the annexed
[Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the
Virtual Elimination List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-15).

## [Addition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748558 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Perfluorooctane sulfonate
and its salts are added to column 1 of the [Virtual Elimination
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-298)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1){#fn_1-ID0EECA}, compiled under subsection
65(2) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_2){#fn_2-ID0EBCA}.

  [[Return to footnote

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-748560 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
