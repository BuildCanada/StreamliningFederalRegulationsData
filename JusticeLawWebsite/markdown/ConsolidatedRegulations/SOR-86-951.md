## Determination of the Tariff Classification of Sugar, Molasses and Sugar Syrup Regulations {#determination-of-the-tariff-classification-of-sugar-molasses-and-sugar-syrup-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-951
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-09-11

Regulations Respecting the Method to be Followed in Determining the
Tariff Classification of Sugar, Molasses and Sugar Syrup

P.C. 1986-2072 1986-09-11
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 15, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
paragraph 164(1)(f) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the method to be
followed in determining the tariff classification of sugar, molasses and
sugar syrup](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-951), effective on the coming into
force of paragraph 164(1)(f) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899263 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Determination of the Tariff Classification of Sugar,
Molasses and Sugar Syrup Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-951).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899265 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[ICUMSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Commission for
Uniform Methods of Sugar Analysis (*ICUMSA*).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-899267 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The tariff classification
for sugar shall be determined from the results of analysis for
polarization obtained by following the method adopted by ICUMSA and
known as Method No. 1, first issued in 1958, as amended from time to
time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-241, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The tariff classification
for molasses and sugar syrup shall be determined from the results of
analysis obtained by following one of the two methods for ascertaining
reducing sugars adopted by ICUMSA set out in the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-533, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Sugar polarimeters,
polarimeter cells, volumetric glassware and other laboratory apparatus
and reagents used in the determination of the tariff classification of
sugar, molasses and sugar syrup shall meet the appropriate ICUMSA
standards.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-533, s. 1
:::
