## Ontario Wheat Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-wheat-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 224
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Wheat Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536943 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Wheat Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._224).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536945 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed by the
    Commodity Board as its agent to purchase or receive wheat from
    producers on its behalf; ([agent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Wheat
    Producers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of wheat. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536950 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply only to the marketing of wheat in interprovincial
  and export trade and to persons and property situated in the Province
  of Ontario.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply where wheat is sold
  directly by one producer to another and used by that other producer on
  his farm.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536954 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer shall sell or offer to sell wheat to any person other than an
  agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person other than an agent shall purchase wheat
  from a producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No agent shall purchase wheat from a producer except
  for the Commodity Board.

## [Transportation and Processing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536959 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no producer shall transport or ship wheat
  to any place other than the premises of an agent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A producer may transport wheat to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a feed mill for processing and use by the producer;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a terminal elevator at the direction of an agent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A producer may transport seed wheat to a seed
  cleaning plant.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall process wheat that has not been sold
  by or through the Commodity Board, except seed wheat processed for a
  producer.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commodity Board shall conduct a pool or pools for the distribution of
  proceeds from sales of wheat.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall distribute the proceeds
  from sales in each pool conducted pursuant to subsection (1), after
  deducting therefrom all the necessary and proper disbursements and
  expenses in respect of the sales, in such a manner that each producer
  receives his share of the total proceeds in the pool in proportion to
  the quantity, and taking into account the variety and grade, of wheat
  delivered by him.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each producer who sells wheat to the Commodity Board
  pursuant to these Regulations is entitled to be paid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on delivery to an agent, an initial payment in an
    amount determined by the Commodity Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} payments, in addition to the payment referred to in
    paragraph (a), in such amounts and at such times as the Commodity
    Board determines, until all of the proceeds referred to in
    subsection (2) received by the Commodity Board from sales of wheat
    have been paid to the producers entitled thereto.

## [Agents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536973 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board may
appoint any person as an agent and shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribe the duties and the terms and conditions of
  employment of every agent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for the remuneration of every agent.

## [Quality Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536977 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** All wheat sold by a
producer to an agent shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be inspected and sold on the basis of grades
  established by subsection 15(1) of the [Canada Grain
  Act](/eng/acts/G-10) and Schedule I to that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be tested for moisture content.

<!-- -->

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When an
  agent receives wheat from a producer, the agent or the producer may
  require a sample of the wheat to be taken for the purpose of checking
  the grade and the moisture content.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every sample of wheat taken pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} weigh not less than two pounds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be accepted by the agent and the producer as a fair
    sample of the wheat from which it was taken;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be placed by the agent in a sealed, moisture-proof
    container with a label thereon containing the name and address of
    the producer and the name of the agent; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if required by an inspector of the Canadian Grain
    Commission for examination and testing, be delivered to the
    inspector.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of accepting a sample of wheat as a
  fair sample pursuant to paragraph (2)(b), any person delivering wheat
  to an agent on behalf of a producer shall be deemed to be the
  authorized agent of the producer.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a dispute arises
between an agent and a producer in respect of the grade, moisture
content or condition of any load of wheat, the matters in dispute shall
be referred to an inspector of the Canadian Grain Commission and the
decision of the inspector shall be final and binding upon the agent and
the producer.
