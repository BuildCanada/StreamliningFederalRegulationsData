## Storage of Goods Regulations {#storage-of-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-991
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-09-18

Storage of Goods Regulations

P.C. 1986-2177 1986-09-18
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on March 22, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations with respect thereto to the Minister of national
Revenue;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(e), subsections 37(1), 38(1), 39(1) and 102(2) and
paragraph 164(1)(i) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_star){#fn_star-ID0EDABA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the storage of
goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-991).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900623 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day on which the
    customs office is open and accepts delivery or removal of commercial
    goods; ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for sale for
    any commercial, industrial, occupational, institutional or other
    similar use that are imported into Canada or exported from Canada;
    ([marchandises commerciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([arme à
    feu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[place of safe-keeping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[place of safe-keeping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place
    designated by the Minister under section 37 of the Act for the
    safe-keeping of goods; ([lieu du dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited ammunition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited ammunition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([munitions prohibées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([dispositif prohibé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([arme
    prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restricted weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted weapon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46); ([arme à
    autorisation restreinte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strike]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strike]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Public Service Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35).
    ([grève]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Time Limit for Removal of Goods Left at a Customs Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900638 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (5), if goods in a customs office have not been
  removed from the customs office within 40 days after the day on which
  the goods were reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may, at
  the end of that limitation period, be deposited in a place of
  safe-keeping as provided for in subsection 37(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where perishable goods in a customs office have not
  been removed therefrom within four days after the day on which they
  are reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may be deposited
  in a place of safe-keeping as provided in subsection 37(1) of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If goods in a customs office are [[nuclear
  substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the
  [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3) or [[prescribed
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
  [General Nuclear Safety and Control
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202) and have not been removed
  from the customs office within 14 days after the day on which the
  goods are reported under section 12 of the Act, the goods may be
  deposited in a place of safe-keeping as provided in subsection 37(1)
  of the Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39.1(1) of the Act,
  firearms, prohibited ammunition, prohibited devices, prohibited or
  restricted weapons, tobacco products and vaping products are goods of
  a prescribed class that are forfeit if they are not removed from a
  customs office within 14 days after the day on which they were
  reported under section 12 of the Act.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39.1(1) of the Act,
  spirits are goods of a prescribed class that are forfeit if they are
  not removed from a customs office within 21 days after the day on
  which they were reported under section 12 of the Act.

## [Storage Charges for Commercial Goods Left at a Customs Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900647 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), commercial goods left at a customs office in
  accordance with paragraph 19(1)(e) of the Act are subject to the
  storage charges set out in the schedule, beginning on the fourth
  business day after the day on which the commercial goods were left at
  the customs office.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No storage charges are payable pursuant to
  subsection (1) on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} commercial goods that are imported by mail; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} commercial goods that cannot be removed from the
    customs office by the owner or importer thereof by reason of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a strike,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a court proceeding,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized act of an officer or the
      omission by an officer to perform a duty imposed on him by the
      Act,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a mistake of a third party, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a natural disaster.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-483, s. 2
- SOR/92-404, s. 1(E)
:::

## [Time Limit for Removal of Goods from a Place of Safe-keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900660 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 39(1) of the Act and subject to subsections (2)
  and (3), goods that have not been removed from a place of safe-keeping
  within 30 days after they were deposited therein are, at the
  termination of that period of time, forfeit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39(1) of the Act,
  perishable goods that have not been removed from a place of
  safe-keeping within 24 hours after they were deposited therein are, at
  the termination of that period of time, forfeit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39(1) of the Act,
  goods that are [[nuclear substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined
  in section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)
  or [[prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  section 1 of the [General Nuclear Safety and Control
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-202) and that have not been
  removed from a place of safe-keeping within 24 hours after they were
  deposited there are, at the termination of that period of time,
  forfeit.

## [Storage Charges for Goods Deposited in a Place of Safe-keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900667 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the storage charges set out in the schedule shall
  be paid for goods deposited in a place of safe-keeping operated by Her
  Majesty in right of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No storage charges are payable on goods deposited in
  a place of safe-keeping operated by Her Majesty in right of Canada if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are delivered to the place of
    safe-keeping in error and are removed therefrom by the owner or
    importer thereof forthwith after notification of the error; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods cannot be removed from the place of
    safe-keeping by the owner or importer thereof by reason of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a strike,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a court proceeding,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an unauthorized act of an officer or the
      omission by an officer to perform a duty imposed on him by the
      Act,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a mistake of a third party, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a natural disaster.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-483, s. 3(F)
- SOR/2005-215, s. 4(E)
