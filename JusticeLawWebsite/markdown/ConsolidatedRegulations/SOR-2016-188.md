## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[child care article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[child care article]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that is
    intended to facilitate the relaxation, sleep, hygiene, feeding,
    sucking or teething of a child under four years of age. ([article de
    puériculture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[toy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[toy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that is intended for use
    by a child under 14 years of age in learning or
    play. ([jouet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832558 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}DEHP, DBP and BBP

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The vinyl in a toy or child
care article must not contain more than 1 000 mg/kg of di(2-ethylhexyl)
phthalate (DEHP), dibutyl phthalate (DBP) or benzyl butyl phthalate
(BBP) when tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good
laboratory practices.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}DINP, DIDP and DNOP

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  vinyl in any part of a toy or child care article that can, in a
  reasonably foreseeable manner, be placed in the mouth of a child under
  four years of age must not contain more than 1 000 mg/kg of diisononyl
  phthalate (DINP), diisodecyl phthalate (DIDP) or di-n-octyl phthalate
  (DNOP) when tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good
  laboratory practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), a part of a toy or
  child care article can be placed in the mouth of a child under four
  years of age if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it can be brought to the child's mouth and kept
    there so that it can be sucked or chewed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one of its dimensions is less than 5 cm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dimensions --- deflated state

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (2)(b), if a part of a
  toy or child care article is inflatable, its dimensions must be
  determined in its deflated state.
