## Canadian Film Certification Regulations {#canadian-film-certification-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 516
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Regulations Respecting the Certification of Canadian Films and Related
Products
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557540 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Film Certification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._516).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557542 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The National Film Board is
hereby charged with the duty of certifying on behalf of Her Majesty
moving picture films, separate sound film track, filmstrips and slides,
videotape and sound recordings, model, wall charts, maps and posters
produced in Canada or produced abroad by persons normally resident in
Canada as being of an international educational, scientific or cultural
character.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where a product referred to
in section 2 is submitted to the National Film Board for certification,
the Board shall certify it as being of an international educational,
scientific or cultural character if, in the opinion of the Board, the
product

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} interprets the life of a country or any part thereof
  in such a way as to promote understanding; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be used for teaching or training purposes to
  contribute to the development of technical efficiencies and is not
  advertising material.
