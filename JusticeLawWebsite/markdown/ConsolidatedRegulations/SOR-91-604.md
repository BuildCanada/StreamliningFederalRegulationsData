## Crown Liability and Proceedings (Provincial Court) Regulations {#crown-liability-and-proceedings-provincial-court-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-604
:::

[CROWN LIABILITY AND PROCEEDINGS ACT](/eng/acts/C-50)

Registration 1991-10-24

Regulations Prescribing the Rules of Practice and Procedure in Respect
of Proceedings Taken in Provincial Courts under Part II of the Crown
Liability and Proceedings Act

P.C. 1991-2030  1991-10-24
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 34[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-604_e_hq_6172){#fn_SOR-91-604_e_hq_6172-ID0EKCAA}
of the [Crown Liability and Proceedings Act](/eng/acts/C-50)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-91-604_e_hq_6173){#fn_SOR-91-604_e_hq_6173-ID0EHCAA},
is pleased hereby to revoke the Crown Liability (Provincial Court)
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 447, and to make the annexed [Regulations
prescribing the rules of practice and procedure in respect of
proceedings taken in provincial courts under Part II of the Crown
Liability and Proceedings Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-604), in
substitution therefor, effective on the day on which An Act to amend the
Federal Court Act, the Crown Liability Act, the Supreme Court Act and
other Acts in consequence thereof, being chapter 8 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1990, comes into force.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939361 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Crown Liability and Proceedings (Provincial Court)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-604).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939363 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Crown Liability and
    Proceedings Act](/eng/acts/C-50); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Attorney General
    of Canada; ([procureur général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[court]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the court having jurisdiction
    under Part II of the Act to hear and determine the claim in respect
    of which the proceedings have been taken;
    ([tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Deputy Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Deputy Attorney General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy
    Attorney General of Canada; ([sous-procureur
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[plaintiff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[plaintiff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a party claiming against
    the Crown in third party proceedings; ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[provincial rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial rules]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rules of practice
    and procedure of the court. ([règles provinciales]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 8 and 10, [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural person
of full age and capacity other than Her Majesty in right of Canada or a
province.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-255, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939374 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
proceedings taken in a provincial court

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under Part II of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} against an agency of the Crown to which section 35 of
  the Act applies.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939378 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2), (5) and (7), service of a document originating any
  proceedings shall be made on the Deputy Attorney General at the office
  of the Deputy Attorney General in Ottawa.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document referred to in subsection (1) has been
  validly served where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it has been served on the Deputy Attorney General
    at the office of the Deputy Attorney General in Ottawa;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has been served on the Director of the
    appropriate Regional Office of the Department of Justice, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      British Columbia, the Vancouver Regional Office,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Alberta, the Edmonton Regional Office,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Saskatchewan, the Saskatoon Regional Office,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Manitoba, the Winnipeg Regional Office,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Ontario, the Toronto Regional Office,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Quebec, the Montreal Regional Office,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Province of
      Nova Scotia, of New Brunswick, of Prince Edward Island or of
      Newfoundland, the Halifax Regional Office,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Yukon
      Territory, the Whitehorse Regional Office, and

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} in respect of proceedings in the Northwest
      Territories, the Yellowknife Regional Office; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has been served on and accepted by an agent or
    mandatary duly authorized to act for the Attorney General in the
    particular proceeding.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where service of a document is made in respect of
  proceedings in the Province of New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or
  Newfoundland, service shall be deemed to have been made within the
  province within which the proceedings are taken.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The address for service on the Crown of any document
  other than a document originating proceedings shall be the address
  designated therefor by or on behalf of the Deputy Attorney General.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document originating any proceedings may
  be effected in the manner provided by section 23 of the Act, without
  special order, even though proceedings are being, or have been, taken
  in a court outside Ontario.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a document may be served by mail under
  provincial rules, service shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be by registered or certified mail; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be effective on the tenth day after the document is
    mailed.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document originating any proceedings on
  the chief executive officer of an agency of the Crown may be made in
  the manner provided by this section.

- SOR/2018-255, s. 2(E)

## [Filing of Defence or Reply and Counterclaim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939403 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Attorney General may file a defence or other reply to a document
  originating proceedings, including a counterclaim, if any, within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the service of the document of
    originating process, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time provided by or under the provincial rules,

  whichever is greater, or within such further time as may be allowed by
  the court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a proceeding is instituted in the Province of
  Quebec, the Attorney General may urge any preliminary exception in the
  manner provided by articles 160, 161 and 162 of the Code of Civil
  Procedure of the Province of Quebec, except that, for the purpose of
  this subsection, articles 160, 161 and 162 shall be read as though the
  period of 5 days provided for therein were 30 days.

## [Judgment or Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939410 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there is a judgment or an order of a court against the Crown for the
  payment of money, the court, or the registrar, clerk or protonotary
  thereof, or a deputy of the registrar, clerk or protonotary, shall,
  unless the judgment or order has been quashed on appeal, at the
  request of the party in whose favour the judgment or order was made,
  after the judgment or order has been formally made a judgment or order
  of the court, certify to the Minister of Finance the tenor and purport
  of the judgment or order

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} forthwith, if no appeal is allowed by law from the
    judgment or order;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the expiration of the time allowed by law for an
    appeal from the judgment or order, if no appeal has then been
    instituted and no application has been made for an extension of time
    for appeal;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the final disposition of the claim, if there has
    been an appeal from the judgment or order;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the dismissal of an application for an extension
    of time for appeal from the judgment or order or on the expiration
    of time granted on such an application without an appeal having then
    been instituted; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at such earlier time as the Attorney General files
    notice that the Attorney General does not intend to appeal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of judgment shall be transmitted to, or
  left at, the office of the Deputy Attorney General in Ottawa or the
  appropriate Regional Office of the Department of Justice referred to
  in paragraph 4(2)(b) by the party in whose favour the judgment or
  order was made.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a certified copy of
  a judgment, or a certified copy of a judgment read with a certified
  copy of a taxed bill of costs, shall be accepted as a certificate of
  judgment.

## [Examination for Discovery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939420 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 37 to
39 of the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5), where, under the
provincial rules, there is provision under which, if an action were an
action between a corporation (other than an agency of the Crown) and
another person, an officer or servant of the corporation could be
examined for discovery, such officer or servant of the Crown or an
agency of the Crown, as the case may be, as may be designated for the
purpose by the Deputy Attorney General or after such designation by
order of the court, may be examined for discovery during an action
subject to the same conditions and with the same effect as would apply
to the examination for discovery of the officer or servant of a
corporation.

## [File List of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939422 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 37 to 39 of the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5),
  where the Attorney General or an agency of the Crown would, if the
  Crown were a person, be required under the provincial rules to file or
  serve a list or an affidavit of documents, the Deputy Attorney General
  must, subject to the same conditions as apply between subject and
  subject, file or serve a list of the documents relating to the matter
  of which the Crown has knowledge within 60 days after the event that
  under the provincial rules gives rise to the obligation to file or
  serve the list or affidavit, or within any additional time that may be
  allowed by the court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, under provincial rules, a party would be
  entitled to obtain production for inspection of any document or a copy
  of any document as against or from the Crown, if the Crown were a
  person, the production for inspection or copy may be had, subject to
  sections 37 to 39 of the [Canada Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5), under
  order of the court after consideration has been given to any objection
  that would be available to the Crown if the Crown were a person.

- SOR/2018-255, s. 3

## [Confession of Judgment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939428 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Attorney General may, at any stage of the proceedings, file in the
  court a confession of judgment either for a part or the whole of the
  claim against the Crown.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The plaintiff may, at any time within 30 days after
  the plaintiff has received notice of a confession filed under
  subsection (1), file, and serve on the Deputy Attorney General, a
  notice in writing of the plaintiff's acceptance or refusal of such
  confession of judgment and, in the event of acceptance, the court or a
  judge may order that judgment be entered accordingly, with such
  provision as to costs as the court or the judge deems just in the
  circumstances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the plaintiff has given notice within the time
  referred to in subsection (2) to the Deputy Attorney General of the
  plaintiff's refusal of a confession of judgment, or if the plaintiff
  has not given any notice under subsection (2) within the period of
  time referred to therein, the matter shall be determined in the
  ordinary manner.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, on the final disposition of an action in which a
  confession of judgment has been made and has not been accepted, the
  plaintiff does not recover a larger sum than the one offered by the
  confession of judgment, the Crown, whatever the result of the action,
  shall be entitled to costs incurred after the date of filing of the
  confession.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No confession of judgment filed under this section
  shall be accepted as evidence against the Crown, either in the action
  in which it was filed or in any other action or suit.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No provision in any provincial rules relating to
  confessions of judgment has any application in proceedings under Part
  II of the Act.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939436 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where, by or under the
provincial rules, the Attorney General or an agency of the Crown would,
if the Crown were a person, be required or permitted to do anything in
relation to any matter not expressly dealt with in these Regulations,
within a certain period of time, a period of 14 days is to be added to
the time otherwise allowed for doing that thing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-255, s. 4
:::

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No order for security for
costs may be made against the Crown.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Rules of the court
relating to taxation of costs between solicitor and client have no
application as between the Attorney General and the agents or
mandataries of the Attorney General.
