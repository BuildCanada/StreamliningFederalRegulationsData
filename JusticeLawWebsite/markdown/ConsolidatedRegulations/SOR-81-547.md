## National Revenue Transfer of Property Regulations {#national-revenue-transfer-of-property-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-547
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1981-07-03

Regulations Respecting the Disposition of Certain Property Under the
Control, Management and Administration of the Minister of National
Revenue

P.C. 1981-1801 1981-07-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 52 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the disposition of certain
property under the control, management and administration of the
Minister of National Revenue](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-547).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871406 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Revenue Transfer of Property
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-547).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871408 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue;

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means clothing goods and uniform
    accoutrements under the control, management and administration of
    the Minister that have been acquired for use in the manufacture of
    uniforms to be worn by persons employed in the administration or
    enforcement of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Sale of Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871412 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on behalf
of the Crown, sell to any person property on condition that the property
is to be used by that person in the manufacture of uniforms for the
Crown under a contract entered into by that person and the Crown.
