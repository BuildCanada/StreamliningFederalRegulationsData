## Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses Regulations {#storage-display-and-transportation-of-firearms-and-other-weapons-by-businesses-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-210
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by
Businesses Regulations

P.C. 1998-485 1998-03-24
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Storage, Display and Transportation of
Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996 and
October 30, 1997, which dates are at least 30 sitting days before the
date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs
117(h), (i) and (o) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby
makes the annexed [Storage, Display and Transportation of Firearms and
Other Weapons by Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020060 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that
    is neither a prohibited firearm nor a restricted firearm. ([arme à
    feu sans restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a business, includes a
    partner in the business. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10).
    ([poster]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[premises]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[premises]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the premises used in the
    course of carrying on a business. ([locaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handgun under
    paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
    firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([arme de poing prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[secure locking device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secure locking device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that can only be opened or released by the use of
      an electronic, magnetic or mechanical key or by setting the device
      in accordance with an alphabetical or numerical combination; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that, when applied to a firearm, prevents the
      firearm from being discharged. ([dispositif de verrouillage
      sécuritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transmit by post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transmit by post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10). ([transmission postale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[unattended]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unattended]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a vehicle, means
    that the vehicle is not under the direct and immediate supervision
    of a person who is 18 years of age or older or to whom a licence has
    been issued under the Act. ([non surveillé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[unloaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unloaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a firearm, means
    that any propellant, projectile or cartridge that can be discharged
    from the firearm is not contained in the breech or firing chamber of
    the firearm nor in the cartridge magazine attached to or inserted
    into the firearm. ([non chargée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any conveyance that is used
    for transportation by water, land or air.
    ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-278, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020077 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to the storage and transportation of a non-restricted firearm,
restricted firearm or prohibited handgun in the course of transmission
by post within Canada from the time the firearm or handgun is posted to
the time it is delivered to the addressee, within the meaning of
subsection 2(2) of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10), or
returned to the sender.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-278, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to the storage of replica firearms that are being stored in
accordance with the Special Authority to Possess Regulations ([Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)).

## [Storage of Non-Restricted Firearms, Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Handguns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020083 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may store a non-restricted firearm, a restricted firearm or a
  prohibited handgun only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is stored

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a securely locked cabinet, vault or safe,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a securely locked location, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in a location that is readily accessible only
      to the owner or an employee of the business; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is stored on premises where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is an electronic burglar alarm system, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} every window that can be opened, and every
      exterior door, can be securely locked.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (1)(b) and (c), a business may
  store a non-restricted firearm, a restricted firearm or a prohibited
  handgun if it is stored in a location the security of which is equal
  or superior to the places, locations and premises described in
  subparagraphs (1)(b)(i) to (iii) and paragraph (1)(c) and that is
  approved in writing by the chief firearms officer who issued a licence
  to the business.

## [Storage of Prohibited Firearms, other than Prohibited Handguns, and their Components and Parts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020095 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may store a prohibited firearm, other than a prohibited
  handgun, or a component or part of a prohibited firearm only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a prohibited firearm, it is
    unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is stored

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a securely locked cabinet, vault or safe,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a securely locked location, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in a location that is readily accessible only
      to the owner or an employee of the business; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is stored on premises where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is an electronic burglar alarm system, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} every window that can be opened, and every
      exterior door, can be securely locked.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (1)(b) and (c), a business may
  store a prohibited firearm, other than a prohibited handgun, or a
  component or part of a prohibited firearm if it is stored in a
  location the security of which is equal or superior to the places,
  locations and premises described in subparagraphs (1)(b)(i) to (iii)
  and paragraph (1)(c) and that is approved in writing by the chief
  firearms officer who issued a licence to the business.

## [Possession of Firearms for the Purpose of Storage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020107 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If firearms are kept for
the purpose of storage in the ordinary course of a business, the
business shall store those firearms

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} apart from other weapons that are kept for the
  purpose of being offered for sale or for carrying out maintenance or
  repairs in the ordinary course of the business; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a place where they are not visible from any part
  of the premises to which persons not employed in the business have
  access.

## [Storage of Restricted Weapons and Prohibited Weapons, Devices and Ammunition and their Components and Parts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020111 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A business may store a
restricted weapon, a prohibited weapon, a prohibited device or
prohibited ammunition, or a component or part thereof, only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is stored

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a vault, safe or room that has been specifically
    constructed or modified for its secure storage and that is kept
    securely locked, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on premises where there is an electronic burglar
    alarm system and every window that can be opened, and every exterior
    door, can be securely locked; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is stored under security measures that are equal
  or superior to those set out in paragraph (a) and that are approved in
  writing by the chief firearms officer who issued a licence to the
  business.

## [Display of Non-Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020117 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business, other than a museum, may display a non-restricted firearm
  only if it is unloaded and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} secured by a chain or metal cable that is passed
    through the trigger guard, with one end of the chain or cable
    attached to a wall or permanent fixture on the premises and the
    other end attached by a lock to the wall or permanent fixture, in
    such a manner as to prevent the removal of the firearm by a person
    other than the owner or an employee of the business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secured by a metal bar, with one end of the bar
    attached to a wall or permanent fixture on the premises and the
    other end attached by a lock to the wall or permanent fixture, in
    such a manner as to prevent the removal of the firearm by a person
    other than the owner or an employee of the business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device and displayed in a locked display case or cabinet;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device and displayed at a location on the premises that is readily
    accessible only to the owner or an employee of the business; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} secured by a method that is equal or superior to
    the method described in paragraph (a) or (b), or rendered inoperable
    by means of a secure locking device and displayed in a place or
    location the security of which is equal or superior to the security
    of the place described in paragraph (c) or the location described in
    paragraph (d), and the method, or the place or location, are
    approved in writing by the chief firearms officer who issued a
    licence to the business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A museum may display a non-restricted firearm only if
  it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unloaded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is displayed under security measures that are equal
    or superior to those set out in subsection (1) and that are approved
    in writing by the chief firearms officer of the province in which
    the museum carries on business.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(a) to (e) and (2)(b) do not apply if
  the non-restricted firearm is detached from the structure so that the
  firearm may be handled by a person under the direct and immediate
  supervision of an employee or owner of the business.

## [Display of Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020129 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business, other than a museum, may display a restricted firearm or a
  prohibited firearm only if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is rendered inoperable by means of a secure locking
    device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is displayed in a locked display case or cabinet;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not displayed in a store window; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a prohibited firearm other than a
    prohibited handgun, is displayed in a location that is readily
    accessible only to the owner or an employee of the business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A museum may display a restricted firearm or a
  prohibited firearm only if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unloaded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is displayed under security measures that are equal
    or superior to those set out in paragraphs (1)(b) to (d) and, in the
    case of a prohibited firearm other than a prohibited handgun,
    paragraph (1)(e), and that are approved in writing by the chief
    firearms officer of the province in which the museum carries on
    business.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paragraphs (1)(b) and (c) and (2)(b) do not apply if
  the restricted firearm or prohibited firearm is being handled by a
  person under the direct and immediate supervision of an employee or
  owner of the business.

## [Transportation of Non-Restricted Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020141 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may transport a non-restricted firearm only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is made of an opaque material and is of such
      strength, construction and nature that it cannot be readily broken
      open or into or accidentally opened during transportation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that, subject to subsection (2), does not have
      any markings on its exterior that could indicate that a weapon, a
      prohibited device or ammunition is in it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when it is in a container described in
    paragraph (b) that is in an unattended vehicle,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the container is
      in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is
      securely locked, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or
      the part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely
      locked and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the only marking on the exterior of the container
    that could indicate that a weapon, a prohibited device or ammunition
    is contained in it is a name or address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the container and its contents are being imported
    into Canada or exported from Canada.

## [Transportation of Restricted Firearms and Prohibited Handguns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020154 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may transport a restricted firearm or a prohibited handgun
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is made of an opaque material and is of such
      strength, construction and nature that it cannot be readily broken
      open or into or accidentally opened during transportation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that, subject to subsection (2), does not have
      any markings on its exterior that could indicate that a weapon, a
      prohibited device or ammunition is in it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when it is in a container described in
    paragraph (b) that is in an unattended vehicle,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the container is
      in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is
      securely locked, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or
      the part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely
      locked and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the only marking on the exterior of the container
    that could indicate that a weapon, a prohibited device or ammunition
    is contained in it is a name or address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the container and its contents are being imported
    into Canada or exported from Canada.

## [Transportation of Prohibited Firearms other than Prohibited Handguns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020167 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may transport a prohibited firearm, other than a prohibited
  handgun, only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is unloaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is made of an opaque material and is of such
      strength, construction and nature that it cannot be readily broken
      open or into or accidentally opened during transportation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is constructed and sealed so as to prevent
      it from being opened without breaking the seal or otherwise
      clearly indicating that it has been opened, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that, subject to subsection (2), does not have
      any markings on its exterior that could indicate that a weapon, a
      prohibited device or ammunition is in it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when it is in a container described in
    paragraph (b) that is in an unattended vehicle,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the container is
      in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is
      securely locked, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or
      the part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely
      locked and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is an automatic firearm that has a bolt or
    bolt-carrier that is removable with reasonable facility, the
    automatic firearm is made inoperable by the removal of the bolt or
    bolt-carrier; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-278, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if it is being transported by a carrier, the
    carrier holds a firearms licence and, with respect to each shipment,
    the business maintains a record of each prohibited firearm, other
    than prohibited handguns, in the shipment.

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} [[\[Repealed, SOR/2004-278, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(b)(iii) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the only marking on the exterior of the container
    that could indicate that a weapon, a prohibited device or ammunition
    is contained in it is a name or address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the container and its contents are being imported
    into Canada or exported from Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(b)(ii) does not apply in respect of
  a prohibited firearm if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is being transported by a business licensed to
    possess it for use in motion picture, television, video or
    theatrical productions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is being transported

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the premises of the business to a place
      where it is required for use in motion picture, television, video
      or theatrical productions, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} between locations of a motion picture,
      television, video or theatrical production for use in that
      production; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has been converted to fire only blank
    cartridges.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-278, s. 3
:::

## [Transportation of Restricted Weapons, Prohibited Weapons, Prohibited Devices and Prohibited Ammunition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020193 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  business may transport a restricted weapon, a prohibited weapon, a
  prohibited device or prohibited ammunition only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is in a container

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is made of an opaque material and is of such
      strength, construction and nature that it cannot be readily broken
      open or into or accidentally opened during transportation,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that is constructed and sealed so as to prevent
      it from being opened without breaking the seal or otherwise
      clearly indicating that it has been opened, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that, subject to subsection (2), does not have
      any markings on its exterior that could indicate that a weapon, a
      prohibited device or ammunition is in it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the container described in paragraph (a) is in
    an unattended vehicle,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the container is
      in that trunk or compartment and the trunk or compartment is
      securely locked, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the vehicle is not equipped with a trunk or
      similar compartment that can be securely locked, the vehicle, or
      the part of the vehicle that contains the container, is securely
      locked and the container is not visible from outside the vehicle;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-278, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if it is being transported by a carrier, the
    carrier holds a firearms licence and, with respect to each shipment,
    the business maintains a record of each restricted weapon,
    prohibited weapon, prohibited device and piece of prohibited
    ammunition in the shipment.

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-278, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subparagraph (1)(a)(iii) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the only marking on the exterior of the container
    that could indicate that a weapon, a prohibited device or ammunition
    is contained in it is a name or address; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the container and its contents are being imported
    into Canada or exported from Canada.

## [Shipping by Post]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020211 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A business may ship a
firearm by posting it only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is a non-restricted firearm, restricted
  firearm or prohibited handgun;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the destination is within Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is posted using the most secure means of
  transmission by post that is offered by the Canada Post Corporation
  that includes the requirement to obtain a signature on delivery.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-278, s. 5
:::

## [Offences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1020218 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 117(o) of the Act, it is an offence to contravene section 6,
with respect to components or parts only, or section 8 or 14.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on December 1, 1998.
