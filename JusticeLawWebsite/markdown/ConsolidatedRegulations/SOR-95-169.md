## Retirement Compensation Arrangements Regulations, No. 2 {#retirement-compensation-arrangements-regulations-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-169
:::

[SPECIAL RETIREMENT ARRANGEMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.2)

Registration 1995-03-31

Regulations Establishing the Public Service Early Retirement
Compensation Arrangement

P.C. 1995-545  1995-03-31
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 10 and
subsection 28(1) of the [Special Retirement Arrangements
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-169_e_hq_5276){#fn_SOR-95-169_e_hq_5276-ID0EDBAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations establishing the
Public Service early retirement compensation
arrangement](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-169), effective April 1, 1995.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-969926 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Retirement Compensation Arrangements Regulations, No.
2](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-169).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-969928 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 11(4) of the [Special Retirement Arrangements
      Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2); ([administrateur général]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Executive Employment Transition Policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Executive Employment Transition Policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means the Executive Employment Transition Policy approved by the
      Treasury Board on August 24, 1992 and effective September 1, 1992,
      as amended from time to time; ([Politique de transition dans la
      carrière pour les cadres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Governor in Council appointee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Governor in Council appointee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who has been appointed by the Governor in Council to a
      position in the Public Service, which position

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person employed in a portion
        of the Public Service referred to in Part I of the schedule, has
        been abolished during the period beginning on April 1, 1995 and
        ending on March 31, 1998, and who occupied the position at the
        time it was abolished, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person employed in a portion
        of the Public Service referred to in Part II of the schedule,
        has been abolished during the period beginning on the day on
        which that portion was added to Part II of the schedule and
        ending on March 31, 1998, and who occupied the position at the
        time it was abolished; ([personne nommée par le gouverneur en
        conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[notice of surplus status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[notice of surplus status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written
      notice given to a person employed in the Public Service by the
      appropriate deputy head, stating that the person's services are no
      longer required by reason of lack of work, the discontinuance of a
      function or the transfer of work or a function to an employer
      outside the Public Service; ([avis de statut
      d'excédentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a surplus employee or
      Governor in Council appointee who meets the conditions set out in
      section 4 or 5, as the case may be;
      ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Public Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Public Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, except in subparagraphs
      4(1)(f)(i) and 5(b)(i) and section 9, means a department or other
      portion of the Public Service, as defined in subsection 3(1) of
      the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), that is
      referred to in Part I or II of the schedule; ([fonction
      publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Superannuation Account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Superannuation Account referred to in section 4 of the [Public
      Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([compte de pension
      de retraite]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[surplus employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[surplus employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person referred
      to in section 2.1. ([fonctionnaire excédentaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Work Force Adjustment Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Work Force Adjustment Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Work Force Adjustment Directive issued on the recommendation of
      the National Joint Council of the Public Service and with the
      approval of the Treasury Board, which came into force on December
      15, 1991, as amended from time to time. ([Directive sur le
      réaménagement des effectifs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Words and expressions used in these Regulations and
  not otherwise defined in the [Special Retirement Arrangements
  Act](/eng/acts/S-15.2) or in these Regulations have the same meaning
  as in the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) or the
  [Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-289, s. 1
- SOR/95-423, s. 1
:::

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4), a
  surplus employee is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person employed in a portion of
    the Public Service referred to in Part I of the schedule, a person
    who has been given a notice of surplus status during the period
    beginning on April 1, 1995 and ending on March 31, 1998; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person employed in a portion of
    the Public Service referred to in Part II of the schedule, a person
    who has been given a notice of surplus status during the period
    beginning on the day on which that portion was added to Part II of
    the schedule and ending on March 31, 1998.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who, at the time the person receives a
  notice referred to in subsection (1), occupies a position in the
  Public Service that was most recently occupied by a surplus employee
  is not a surplus employee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who is given a notice referred to in
  subsection (1) after being deployed, appointed, assigned, seconded or
  otherwise transferred to the position of an employee in the Public
  Service in order to prevent that employee from receiving a notice of
  surplus status is not a surplus employee, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that employee is also transferred to the person's
    position and both transfers are effective on the same day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the difference between the maximum rate of pay for
    the position from which the person is transferred and the maximum
    rate of pay for the position to which the person is transferred is
    not more than four per cent of the lower of those rates.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is subject to the Executive Employment
  Transition Policy and who receives a notice of surplus status is not a
  surplus employee if the person is not eligible to receive benefits
  under that policy because the person does not meet the conditions
  added to the policy by the Treasury Board on August 15, 1995.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-423, s. 2
:::

## [Establishment of Arrangement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-969956 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There is hereby established
a retirement compensation arrangement for the payment of benefits to or
in respect of any surplus employee or Governor in Council appointee who

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on April 1, 1995, was employed in a portion of the
  Public Service referred to in Part I of the schedule and was

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} required to contribute to the Superannuation
    Account pursuant to subsection 5(1) of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contributing to the Superannuation Account
    pursuant to an election made under section 5.2 of that Act, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on leave of absence without pay and could make or
    had made an election under subsection 5.3(1) of that Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} was employed in a portion of the Public Service
  referred to in Part II of the schedule on the day on which that
  portion was added to Part II of the schedule and, on that day, was

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} required to contribute to the Superannuation
    Account pursuant to subsection 5(1) of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contributing to the Superannuation Account
    pursuant to an election made under section 5.2 of that Act, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on leave of absence without pay and could make or
    had made an election under subsection 5.3(1) of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-289, s. 2
:::

## [Benefit Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-969968 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  surplus employee referred to in paragraph 3(a) is entitled to an
  annual benefit calculated in accordance with subsection 7(1) on
  condition that the employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} elects, not later than 60 days after the date of
    the first notice of surplus status given to the employee after April
    1, 1995, to receive that annual benefit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ceases to be employed within a period of six months
    after the date of the first notice of surplus status given to the
    employee after April 1, 1995 or not later than the end of such
    longer period, determined by the deputy head, as is required in
    order to meet operational requirements;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has not, before ceasing to be employed, received

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an employee who is subject to the
      Work Force Adjustment Directive, a reasonable job offer within the
      meaning of that Directive, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an employee who is subject to the
      Executive Employment Transition Policy, an offer of alternative
      employment in a portion of the Public Service referred to in Part
      I of the schedule;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the employee's services are no longer
    required by reason of the transfer of work or a function to an
    employer outside the Public Service, has not received an offer of
    contiguous employment from that employer as a consequence of the
    transfer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has not elected to receive

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an employee referred to in
      subparagraph (c)(i), a payment under a program respecting early
      departure incentives arising from the budget tabled in Parliament
      on February 27, 1995, or a payment under the Civilian Reduction
      Program arising from the budget tabled in Parliament on February
      22, 1994, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an employee referred to in
      subparagraph (c)(ii), a negotiated settlement under the Executive
      Employment Transition Policy; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} at the time the employee ceases to be employed,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been employed in the Public Service, within
      the meaning of subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service
      Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), on a full-time basis as
      described in clause 13(1)(c)(ii)(C) of that Act for a period of or
      for periods totalling at least 10 years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has reached the age of 50 years but has not
      reached the age of 55 years, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has exercised the option to receive an annual
      allowance in accordance with clause 13(1)(c)(ii)(B) or (D) of the
      [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) applies in
  respect of a surplus employee referred to in paragraph 3(a) whose
  surplus status ceases but who, during the period beginning on April 1,
  1995 and ending on March 31, 1998, receives another notice of surplus
  status and, for the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), the period of six
  months referred to in that paragraph shall be calculated from the date
  of the subsequent notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A surplus employee referred to in paragraph 3(b) is
  entitled to an annual benefit calculated in accordance with subsection
  7(1) on condition that the employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} elects, not later than 60 days after the date of
    the first notice of surplus status given to the employee after the
    applicable day referred to in paragraph 3(b), to receive that annual
    benefit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ceases to be employed within a period of six months
    after the date of the first notice of surplus status given to the
    employee after the applicable day referred to in paragraph 3(b) or
    not later than the end of such longer period, determined by the
    deputy head, as is required in order to meet operational
    requirements;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not, before ceasing to be employed, receive an
    offer of alternative employment in the portion of the Public Service
    in which the employee is employed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meets the conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(d)
    and (f).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (3) applies in
  respect of a surplus employee referred to in paragraph 3(b) whose
  surplus status ceases but who, during the period beginning on the
  applicable day referred to in that paragraph and ending on March 31,
  1998, receives another notice of surplus status and, for the purposes
  of paragraph (3)(b), the period of six months referred to in that
  paragraph shall be calculated from the date of the subsequent notice.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-289, s. 3
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A Governor in Council
appointee referred to in section 3 is entitled to an annual benefit
calculated in accordance with subsection 7(1) on condition that the
appointee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ceases to be employed within six months after the day
  on which the appointee's position is abolished;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time the appointee ceases to be employed,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been employed in the Public Service, within the
    meaning of subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36), on a full-time basis as described in clause
    13(1)(c)(ii)(C) of that Act for a period of or for periods totalling
    at least 10 years, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has reached the age of 50 years but has not
    reached the age of 55 years;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} elects, not later than 30 days after ceasing to be
  employed, to receive that annual benefit;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} exercises, not later than 30 days after ceasing to be
  employed, the option to receive an annual allowance in accordance with
  clause 13(1)(c)(ii)(B) or (D) of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} has not received a lump sum termination settlement.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The annual benefit referred
to in sections 4 and 5 is payable to a participant in the same manner
and subject to the same conditions as an annual allowance payable under
the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

## [Amount of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970002 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of the annual benefit to which a participant is entitled is the
  difference between

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the annuity that would be payable to
    the participant under paragraph 11(1)(a) of the [Public Service
    Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) if that paragraph applied to all
    of the participant's pensionable service, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the annual allowance to which the
    participant is entitled under clause 13(1)(c)(ii)(B) or (D) of that
    Act plus the amount of any benefit to which the participant is
    entitled under section 35 of the [Retirement Compensation
    Arrangements Regulations, No. 1](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-785).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The annual benefit shall be paid to a participant as
  of the same day that the annual allowance referred to in subparagraph
  4(1)(f)(iii) or paragraph 5(1)(d) becomes payable.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  participant has made an election under section 13.1 of the [Public
  Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) in respect of a person who
  became the participant's spouse after the participant ceased to be
  employed in the Public Service, the amount of the annual benefit
  determined under subsection 7(1) shall be reduced by the same
  proportion, and for the same period, as the participant's annual
  allowance is reduced under section 68 of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reduction in the amount of the annual benefit
  referred to in subsection (1) shall be effective on the same day as
  the reduction calculated under section 68 of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the surviving spouse of a participant is
  entitled to an immediate annual allowance under subsection 13.1(3) of
  the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the spouse is
  entitled to an annual benefit equal to the amount determined under
  subsection 7(1), multiplied by the percentage of the participant's
  annual allowance, calculated before any reduction is made under
  section 68 of the[Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358), that the spouse will
  receive as an allowance under section 79 of those Regulations.

## [Changes in Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970012 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant receiving an annual benefit under these Regulations shall
  cease to be entitled to receive that benefit on the day on which the
  participant is re-employed in the Public Service, within the meaning
  of subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), and becomes a contributor within the meaning of
  section 64 of that Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a participant's entitlement to an annual
  benefit under these Regulations ceases as described in subsection (1),
  that entitlement will not recommence where the participant
  subsequently ceases to be employed in the Public Service, within the
  meaning of subsection 3(1) of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36).

<!-- -->

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a participant becomes entitled to an immediate annuity under
  subparagraph 13(1)(d)(ii) of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the participant ceases to be entitled to an
  annual benefit under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a participant who ceases to be entitled to an
  annual benefit as described in subsection (1) becomes entitled to a
  deferred annuity under section 28 of the [Public Service
  Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the participant shall again
  become entitled to an annual benefit under these Regulations if the
  participant exercises the option to receive an annual allowance in
  accordance with clause 13(1)(c)(ii)(B) or (D) of that Act.

## [Supplementary Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970019 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Supplementary benefits
shall be paid in respect of periodic payments payable under these
Regulations at the same rates and in the same manner as are
supplementary retirement benefits under the [Supplementary Retirement
Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24).

## [Recovery of Overpayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970021 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where, under these
Regulations, an amount has been paid in error to a person, an amount
equal to the amount paid in error shall be recovered in the same manner
as that specified in section 20 of the [Retirement Compensation
Arrangements Regulations, No. 1](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-785), with such
modifications as the circumstances require.

## [Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970023 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Supply
and Services shall provide all administrative services necessary for the
administration of the retirement compensation arrangement established by
section 3.

## [Time Limitation on Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-970025 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in these Regulations, a participant is not entitled to an annual benefit
under section 4 or 5 unless that participant ceases to be employed in
the Public Service on or before October 1, 1998.

- SOR/97-162, s. 1
