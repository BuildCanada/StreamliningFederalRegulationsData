## Northwest Territories Mining District and Nunavut Mining District Order {#northwest-territories-mining-district-and-nunavut-mining-district-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-53
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2000-07-05

Northwest Territories Mining District and Nunavut Mining District Order

P.C. 2000-1026 2000-06-21
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(g)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby makes the annexed
[Northwest Territories Mining District and Nunavut Mining District
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-53).

## [Districts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604854 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The territorial lands of
the Northwest Territories and Nunavut are hereby divided into two mining
districts, described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Northwest Territories Mining District, comprising
  the area described in Schedule 1; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Nunavut Mining District, comprising the area
  described in Schedule 2.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604858 .Part}

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 1(a))]{.OriginatingRef}[Northwest Territories Mining District]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-604861 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that area that includes all that part of Canada north of the
sixtieth parallel of north latitude and west of the boundary described
in Schedule I to the [Nunavut Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6) that is not within
the Yukon Territory.
::::
:::::

::::: section
:::: {#604863 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 1(b))]{.OriginatingRef}[Nunavut Mining District]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-604864 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that area that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all that part of Canada north of the sixtieth
  parallel of north latitude and east of the western boundary of Nunavut
  described in Schedule I to the [Nunavut Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6) that is
  not within Quebec or Newfoundland; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the islands in Hudson Bay, James Bay and Ungava Bay
  that are not within Manitoba, Ontario or Quebec.
