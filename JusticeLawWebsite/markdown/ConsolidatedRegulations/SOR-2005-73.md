## Natural and Man-made Harbour Navigation and Use Regulations {#natural-and-man-made-harbour-navigation-and-use-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-73
:::

[CANADA MARINE ACT](/eng/acts/C-6.7)

Registration 2005-03-22

Natural and Man-made Harbour Navigation and Use Regulations

P.C. 2005-400 2005-03-22
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 104(1) of the [Canada
Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed Natural and Man-made Harbour Navigation and Use Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722436 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Marine
    Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned in
    section 2 of the [Transportation of Dangerous Goods Act,
    1992](/eng/acts/T-19.01). ([marchandises
    dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[floating structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[floating structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    floathome, houseboat, ship, work or other type of floating craft
    that is used for residential or commercial purposes. ([construction
    flottante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harbour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the navigable waters of a
    natural or man-made harbour that have been designated by the
    Governor in Council under subsection 104(2) of the Act.
    ([port]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[harbour official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[harbour official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a harbour,
    means a traffic control official designated by the Minister under
    section 106 of the Act or an enforcement officer designated by the
    Minister under subsection 108(1) of the Act. ([responsable de
    port]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Binding on Her Majesty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722443 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
binding on Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

## [Safety of Persons and Ships in Harbours]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722445 .Part}

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722446 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise authorized
under these Regulations, no person shall, by act or omission, adversely
affect the use of a harbour, interfere with navigation in a harbour or
jeopardize the safety of persons or ships in a harbour by doing anything
or permitting anything to be done that has or is likely to have any of
the following results in the harbour:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to obstruct or threaten any part of the harbour;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with an authorized activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to divert the flow of a river or stream, cause or
  affect currents, cause silting or the accumulation of material or
  otherwise reduce the depth of the waters;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} to cause a nuisance;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} to cause injury to persons or damage to ships; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} to adversely affect sediment or water quality.

### [Removal from Waters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722454 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise authorized under these Regulations, a person who, adversely
  affects the use of a harbour, interferes with navigation in a harbour
  or jeopardizes the safety of persons or ships in a harbour by
  dropping, depositing, discharging or spilling refuse, a substance that
  pollutes, cargo, ship's gear or any other object must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately make every technically feasible and
    reasonable effort to remove it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify a harbour official of the incident without
    delay and provide a description of what was dropped, deposited,
    discharged or spilled and its approximate location, and of the
    efforts made by the person to remove it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, provide to the harbour
    official a detailed written report containing the information
    mentioned in paragraph (b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person does not remove the refuse, polluting
  substance, cargo, ship's gear or object immediately, the harbour
  official may have it done.

### [Fire Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722461 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person in a harbour
must follow the fire protection and prevention measures established for
the safety of persons and ships in the harbour, taking into account the
activities in the harbour.

### [Dangerous Situations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722463 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person who, by act or
omission, causes a dangerous situation in a harbour must immediately
take appropriate precautionary measures to prevent injury to persons or
damage to ships and notify a harbour official without delay as to the
nature of the dangerous situation and the precautions that have been
taken and their location.

### [Emergencies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722465 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other provision
of these Regulations, a person may, in a harbour, conduct an activity
set out in column 1 of the schedule without having a contract or lease
with, or a licence granted by, the Minister of National Defence or an
authorization from a harbour official or without complying with the
conditions of an authorization for the duration of an emergency
situation if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activity is required as a result of an emergency
  situation that jeopardizes the safety of persons or ships;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person conducting the activity makes every effort
  to notify a harbour official of the activity and the emergency
  situation, if practicable; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person conducting the activity submits a detailed
  written report to the harbour official without delay describing the
  activity and explaining why the situation was regarded as an
  emergency.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If an activity in a harbour
causes or is likely to cause an emergency situation that jeopardizes the
safety of persons or ships, the person conducting the activity and, in
the case of an activity conducted under a contract, lease, licence or
authorization, the person authorized to conduct the activity must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify a harbour official without delay of the
  emergency situation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a detailed written report of the emergency
  situation to the harbour official as soon as possible after the
  situation ceases to exist; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the harbour official, submit with
  the report to the harbour official a copy of each report of the
  emergency situation that the person makes to municipal, provincial and
  federal authorities.

### [Accidents and Incidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722474 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person who does anything
in a harbour that results in an incident involving injury to persons or
damage to ships, including an explosion, fire, accident, grounding, or
stranding must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify a harbour official of the incident without
  delay; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a detailed written report of the incident to
  the harbour official as soon as possible after the incident occurs.

### [Precautionary Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722478 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  a harbour, a person conducts an activity, other than an activity set
  out in the schedule, that is likely to have any of the results
  prohibited under section 3, a harbour official may instruct the person
  conducting the activity to cease the activity or authorize the person
  to conduct the activity on condition that the person takes measures
  reasonably necessary to mitigate or prevent the result.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must immediately comply with the
  instructions of the harbour official.

## [Authorizations and Instructions for Activities in a Harbour]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722482 .Part}

### [Activities under Contracts, Leases and Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722483 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person may, in a
harbour, conduct an activity set out in column 1 of the schedule if
authorized to do so in writing expressly or by necessary implication
under a contract or lease entered into with, or a licence granted by,
the Minister of National Defence.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If, by entering into a
contract or lease or granting a licence, the Minister of National
Defence authorizes an activity set out in column 1 of the schedule that
has or is likely to have any of the results prohibited under section 3,
that Minister may stipulate as a condition of the contract, lease or
licence that the person with whom the contract or lease is made or to
whom the licence is granted must take measures reasonably necessary to
mitigate or prevent the result.

### [Authorizations by Posted Signs, Nautical Charts, Maps or Forms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722486 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a harbour official may, by such means as posted signs, nautical
  charts, maps or forms, give an authorization under this section to
  conduct, in a harbour, an activity set out in column 1 of the schedule
  if an "X" is set out in column 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the conduct of the activity is not likely to have
  a result prohibited under section 3, the harbour official may give the
  authorization without conditions to persons wanting to conduct the
  activity.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the conduct of the activity is likely to have a
  result prohibited under section 3, the harbour official may give the
  authorization for the activity only if the harbour official

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishes conditions designed to mitigate or
    prevent the result; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicates the conditions by posting them in a
    prominent place clearly visible to persons wanting to conduct the
    activity or setting them out on forms that are readily available to
    persons wanting to conduct the activity.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall conduct an activity set out in column 1 of the schedule
  if an "X" is set out in column 2, unless the person complies with the
  conditions, if any, for conducting the activity that are posted or set
  out on forms.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a condition for conducting the activity is to
  complete a checklist, the person conducting the activity must keep the
  checklist readily available for inspection.

### [Authorization to a Person]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722496 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  harbour official may give an authorization under this section to a
  person to conduct, in a harbour, an activity set out in column 1 of
  the schedule if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an "X" is set out in column 3; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an "X" is set out in column 2 and the person or any
    person who would be covered by the authorization is unable to comply
    with the conditions posted or set out on forms under section 13 for
    the conduct of the activity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for an authorization and the
  information required under subsection 16(2), the harbour official must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give their authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the results of the conduct of the activity are
    uncertain or if the conduct of the activity is likely to have any of
    the results prohibited under section 3,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} refuse to give their authorization, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} give their authorization subject to conditions
      designed to mitigate or prevent the results; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the harbour official required that the person
    obtain insurance coverage, performance security or damage security
    in respect of the conduct of the activity and none is obtained or
    that which is obtained is inadequate, refuse to give their
    authorization.

<!-- -->

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a harbour, conduct an activity set out in column 1 of
  the schedule if an "X" is set out in column 3 unless the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtains an authorization under section 15 or is
    covered by an authorization given under that section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} complies with the conditions, if any, of the
    authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who seeks an authorization from a harbour
  official to conduct an activity in a harbour must provide to the
  harbour official

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information relevant to the proposed activity and
    required by the harbour official to assess the likelihood of the
    occurrence of any of the results prohibited under section 3;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if required by the harbour official, proof that the
    person seeking the authorization has an insurance policy that
    provides adequate coverage for the activity, names Her Majesty in
    right of Canada as an additional insured and provides for the
    insurer to notify the harbour official in the event that the policy
    is amended or cancelled; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if required by the harbour official, performance
    security and damage security in respect of the conduct of the
    activity.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A harbour official may
cancel an authorization given under section 15 or change the conditions
to which an authorization is subject if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of the activity has a result prohibited
  under section 3 or, because of a change in circumstances, becomes
  likely to have such a result or the result of the activity becomes
  uncertain;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the authorization was based on incorrect or
  misleading information; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the authorization is given or any
  person covered by the authorization does not comply with a condition
  of the authorization.

<!-- -->

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authorization given under section 15 is cancelled, the harbour
  official must give notice of the cancellation to the person to whom
  the authorization was given.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The cancellation is effective at the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the end of the fifth business day after the notice
    of cancellation is sent by registered mail to the address provided
    in the application for the authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two hours after a facsimile or electronic
    transmission of the notice of cancellation is sent to the address
    provided in the application for the authorization; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately on service of the notice of
    cancellation at the address provided in the application for the
    authorization.

### [Instructions to Cease, Remove, Return and Restore]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722526 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  harbour official may instruct a person to take any of the actions
  referred to in subsection (2) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person conducts an activity for which an
    authorization is required under section 15 without first obtaining
    the authorization or without being covered by one;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person, or any person covered by the
    authorization, fails to comply with a condition of the
    authorization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorization to conduct the activity is
    cancelled under section 17; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an activity for which no
    authorization is required under these Regulations, the conduct of
    the activity has a result prohibited under section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The actions are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to cease the activity or comply with the conditions
    for conducting the activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person is instructed to cease the activity,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to remove anything brought into the harbour in
      connection with the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to return to the harbour anything that was
      removed from it in connection with the activity, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to restore the property affected by the
      activity to its former state.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person must comply immediately with the
  instructions of the harbour official.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the person fails to remove the thing or to restore
  the property immediately, the harbour official may conduct the removal
  or restoration and may store the thing.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722541 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
