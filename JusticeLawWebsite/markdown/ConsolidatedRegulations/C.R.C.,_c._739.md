## Advertising Material Remission Order {#advertising-material-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 739
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Paid or Payable Under Division
III of Part IX and Under any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act on Certain
Kinds of Advertising Material
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561286 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Advertising Material Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._739).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561288 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means catalogues,
price-lists and trade notices.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561290 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of all taxes paid or payable under Division
III of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on advertising material imported into Canada on or
after January 1, 1991, relating to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods offered for sale or rental;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transport services; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} commercial insurance services.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-7, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission mentioned in
section 3 shall be granted only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each importation of advertising material

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} consists of one document,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} consists of more than one document but does not
    include more than one copy of any one document, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} irrespective of the number of documents or
    copies, does not exceed 1 kg (2.205 lbs.) in gross weight; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advertising material clearly indicates the name
  of the foreign supplier of the goods or services, and does not
  indicate any Canadian source of supply for the goods or services.
