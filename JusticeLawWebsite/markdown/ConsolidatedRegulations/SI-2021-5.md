## Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness that Each Listed Entity Remain a Listed Entity {#order-accepting-the-recommendation-of-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-that-each-listed-entity-remain-a-listed-entity .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-5
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2021-02-03

Order Accepting the Recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness that Each Listed Entity Remain a Listed Entity

P.C. 2021-30 2021-02-02
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 83.05(8.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28010){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28010-ID0ELECA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28011){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28011-ID0EIECA},
the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness has completed a
review of each entity set out in the annexed schedule and listed in the
[Regulations Establishing a List of
Entities](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-284)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28089){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28089-ID0EFECA},
made pursuant to subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012-ID0EDECA}
of that Act, and has determined that there are still reasonable grounds,
as set out in subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012-ID0EBECA},
for each entity to remain a listed entity;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  2019, c. 13, s. 141(4)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote

  C-46
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28089-ID0EFECA){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28089}SOR/2002-284
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012-ID0EDECA){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012}S.C.
  2019, c. 13, s. 141(1)
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 83.05(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28012-ID0EBABA}
of that Act, the Administrator in Council is satisfied, on the
recommendation of that Minister, that there are reasonable grounds to
believe that each listed entity set out in the annexed schedule is an
entity that has knowingly carried out, attempted to carry out,
participated in or facilitated a terrorist activity or that has
knowingly acted on behalf of, at the direction of or in association with
such an entity;

Therefore, His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada
in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and
Emergency Preparedness made pursuant to subsection 83.05(8.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28010){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28010-ID0EEAAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28011){#fn_81000-2-4367_hq_28011-ID0EBAAA},
accepts the recommendation that each listed entity set out in the
annexed schedule remain a listed entity.
