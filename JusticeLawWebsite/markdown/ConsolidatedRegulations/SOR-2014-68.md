## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Territorial Lands
      Act](/eng/acts/T-7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[boundary post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[boundary post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a legal post,
      excluding a corner post and a witness post, marking the boundary
      of a claim or a plot of land that is being staked for the purpose
      of making it a claim. ([borne de délimitation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
      Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief of Financial
      Analysis and Royalties Administration Section of the Department of
      Indian Affairs and Northern Development. ([chef]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[contiguous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contiguous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with reference to two or more
      claims, includes claims that were staked in a manner to be
      contiguous. ([contigus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[corner post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corner post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a legal post marking the
      northeast, southeast, southwest or northwest corner of a claim or
      a plot of land that is being staked for the purpose of making it a
      claim. ([borne d'angle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cost of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cost of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means expenses incurred in
      performing work, but excludes all of the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transportation costs outside Canada for persons
        who performed work or for equipment used in work;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} taxes and fees paid to any level of government
        or any institution of public government;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} costs of staking and recording a claim;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} administrative costs. ([coût des
        travaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[depreciable assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[depreciable assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means buildings, plant,
      machinery and equipment. ([actif amortissable]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[environmental baseline studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[environmental baseline studies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      description of selected environmental attributes that existed
      before mineral exploration or mining development and that are used
      to establish a benchmark from which to measure changes to the
      environment. Selected environmental attributes include
      meteorologic, hydrologic and hydro-geologic attributes, surface
      water and groundwater quality, aquatic resources, soil profiling,
      ecosystems, wildlife and wildlife habitat, cultural heritage and
      archaeology. ([études environnementales de base]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[exploration cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exploration cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an expense incurred
      for the purpose of determining the existence, location, extent,
      quality or economic potential of a mineral deposit in the
      Northwest Territories, but does not include an expense incurred
      for the purpose of bringing a mine into production. ([frais
      d'exploration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a mine, means
      the fiscal period of the mine's operator as that period is defined
      in section 249.1 of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[holder of the surface rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder of the surface rights]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      lessee or registered holder of the surface rights to the
      land. ([titulaire des droits de surface]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[legal post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[legal post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a post, tree or mound of
      stones set up in accordance with section 26 to serve as a boundary
      post, corner post or witness post. ([borne
      légale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence to prospect
      referred to in section 3. ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undertaking that produces or
      has produced minerals or processed minerals from lands within the
      Northwest Territories Mining District, and includes the
      depreciable assets that are located in the Northwest Territories
      and used in connection with the
      undertaking. ([mine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any naturally occurring

  [[mining property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recorded claim or a leased claim within the
        boundaries of which a mine or part of a mine is situated; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group of contiguous recorded or leased claims
        within the boundaries of which a mine or part of a mine is
        situated and

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that belong to the same owner, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the mine is operated as a joint venture,
          that are owned exclusively by the members of the joint venture

          or leased claim. ([propriété minière]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[mining reclamation trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining reclamation trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a trust

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of subsection 72.11(1) of the
        [Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2); or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as a condition of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a lease issued under the [Territorial Lands

  [[Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Mining Recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person

      minier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mining royalty valuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mining royalty valuer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person

  :   [[witness post]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a legal post set up in

  :   [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} excavation,

        - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} geological mapping,

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811545 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Northwest Territories Mining District

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the Northwest Territories Mining District, the area of which
is described in Schedule 1 to the [Northwest Territories Mining District
and Nunavut Mining District Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-53).

## [Licence to Prospect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mining Recorder must issue a licence to prospect to a person who has
  applied for one and paid the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 if
  the person is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is 18 years of age or older; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a company that is incorporated or registered under
    the Business Corporations Act, S.N.W.T. 1996, c. 19, or that is
    incorporated under the [Canada Business Corporations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence not transferable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence is not transferable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence valid for one year

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licence is valid from the date of its issue until
  March 31 following the date of its issue or, if renewed before March
  31, for a period of one year beginning on April 1 following the date
  of its renewal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of licence

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensee may, on request and payment of the
  applicable fee set out in Schedule 1, obtain from the Mining Recorder
  a copy of their licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence authorization --- licensee or
agent

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Only a
  licensee or a person authorized to act on behalf of a licensee may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prospect for the purpose of staking a claim; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} undertake the staking of a claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence authorization --- licensee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Only a licensee may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make an application to record a claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an application for a prospecting permit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be issued a written confirmation under subsection
    15(11), a certificate of extension under subsection 42(2) or a
    certificate of work under subsection 47(1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be issued a lease of a recorded claim or a renewal
    of such a lease; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquire, by themself or with another licensee, a
    prospecting permit, recorded claim or lease of a recorded claim.

## [Prohibitions Respecting Prospecting, Staking and Mining]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lands not open for prospecting or staking

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to
prospect or stake a claim on any of the following lands:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands used as a cemetery;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands covered by a prospecting permit, a recorded
  claim or a lease of a recorded claim, unless the prospecting or
  staking is done by the permittee, claim holder or lessee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands for which the minerals have been granted by the
  Crown;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} lands subject to a prohibition on prospecting or
  staking a claim under a land use plan that has been approved under
  federal legislation or under a land claims agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} lands that have been withdrawn from disposal or set
  apart and appropriated by the Governor in Council under paragraphs
  23(a) to (e) of the Act;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} lands that are mentioned in subsections 22(1) and
  52(5), section 56, subsection 67(2) and section 85 that are not open
  for prospecting or staking.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surface rights --- prohibition respecting
entry

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the surface rights to
lands have been granted or leased by the Crown, it is prohibited to go
on the surface of those lands to prospect or stake a claim unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the surface rights has consented to
  entry for the prospecting or staking; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a tribunal competent to deal with surface rights in
  the Northwest Territories has made an order that authorizes entry on
  those lands and that sets the compensation, if any, to the surface
  holder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on removing minerals

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to remove minerals or processed minerals from, or develop a
  mine within, the area of a recorded claim or a leased claim, except in
  the case of the holder of the recorded claim or the lessee.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation respecting holder of a
  recorded claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to remove minerals or processed
  minerals whose gross value exceeds \$100,000 from a recorded claim
  that is not subject to a lease issued under subsection 60(5) or a
  renewal issued under subsection 62(4), except if the removal is for
  the purposes of assay and testing to determine the existence,
  location, extent, quality or economic potential of a mineral deposit
  within the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition on construction and
  disposal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to erect on a recorded claim any
  building to be used as a dwelling or any mill, concentrator or other
  mine building, or to create any tailings or waste disposal area for
  the purpose of the commencement of production from a mine, unless the
  claim holder has been issued a lease of the surface rights to, or a
  grant of, the land covered by the claim.

## [Prospecting Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811592 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prospecting permit zones

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  mining district is divided into prospecting permit zones based on the
  National Topographic System of Canada. Each zone consists of one
  quarter of the area shown on a claim staking sheet and is designated
  as the northeast, southeast, southwest or northwest quarter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[claim staking
  sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[claim staking
  sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} south of 68° north latitude, a map of zones bounded
    on the north and south by 15-minute intervals of latitude and on the
    east and west by 30-minute intervals of longitude; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} north of 68° north latitude, a map of zones bounded
    on the north and south by 15-minute intervals of latitude and on the
    east and west by one-degree intervals of longitude.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for prospecting permit

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee may apply to the Mining Recorder for a prospecting permit for
  the exclusive right to prospect and stake claims in a prospecting
  permit zone specified in the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a prospecting permit must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be received by the Mining Recorder between February
    1 and close of business on the last business day of November before
    the year in which it is to commence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by payment of the applicable fee set
    out in Schedule 1; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} include a description of the work proposed to be
    carried out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of charge

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition, the licensee must pay to the Mining
  Recorder the charge referred to in subparagraph 14(a)(i) or (b)(i), as
  the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a charge has been paid under subsection (3) but no
  prospecting permit is issued with respect to the application, the
  charge is remitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any charge referred to in subparagraph 14(a)(i) or
  (b)(i) that has been paid to the Mining Recorder and that is remitted
  under this section must be repaid by the Minister to the person
  entitled to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Zones excluded

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A prospecting permit must
not be issued in respect of a prospecting permit zone if, at close of
business on the last business day of January in the year the permit is
to be issued, either of the following situations exists:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are recorded claims or leased claims or
  applications to record claims in that zone and the total area covered
  by those claims exceeds 1250 hectares;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are recorded claims or leased claims in that
  zone and the total area covered by those claims is 1250 hectares or
  less and, in the five years preceding that day, either of the
  following has happened with respect to any of those recorded claims or
  leased claims:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} work has been done with respect to any of the
    recorded claims and has been reported on under section 41 of these
    Regulations or under section 41 of the [Northwest Territories and
    Nunavut Mining Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1516),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there has been a transfer recorded under section
    66 of a majority of the interest in the recorded claim or lease to a
    person who was not, immediately before the transfer, recorded as a
    holder of the claim or lease and who is not related to anyone who
    was, immediately before the transfer, a holder of the claim or
    lease.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If two
  or more applications for a prospecting permit are made in respect of a
  prospecting permit zone, the Mining Recorder must, in issuing a
  prospecting permit, give priority to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} firstly, applications presented in person at the
    office of the Mining Recorder on the first business day of November,
    in the order of their receipt;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secondly, applications --- other than those dealt
    with in paragraph (a) --- received by the Mining Recorder before the
    second business day of November, in the order of their drawing from
    a lottery of those applications; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lastly, all other applications received after the
    first business day of November, in the order of their receipt by the
    Mining Recorder, or, if the order of receipt of applications cannot
    be determined, then in the order of their drawing from a lottery of
    those applications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Mining Recorder must issue the prospecting permit
  as soon as possible after January 31 in the year following the receipt
  of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Mining Recorder must assign an identification
  number to each prospecting permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of claim from permit zone

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The area covered by a prospecting permit must be
  modified by removing the area covered by a recorded claim within that
  zone if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claim was staked within the zone before the
    permit was issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application to record the claim was made after
    the permit was issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of prospecting permits

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A prospecting permit
takes effect on the first day of February in the year it is issued and
expires at the end of January

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} three years later, in the case of a permit in respect
  of a zone located south of 68° north latitude; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} five years later, in the case of a permit in respect
  of a zone located north of 68° north latitude.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice respecting prospecting permits

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** On or before the fifth
business day in February, a notice must be posted in the Mining
Recorder's office identifying the zones in respect of which prospecting
permits were issued that year in the mining district.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charges for prospecting permit

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The following charges
must be paid for a prospecting permit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a prospecting permit zone located south of 68°
  north latitude,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before the close of business on the last business
    day of November in the year in which the application for the permit
    is made, an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit
    zone multiplied by \$0.25,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} before the start of the second year of the permit,
    an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit zone
    multiplied by \$0.50, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} before the start of the third year of the permit,
    an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit zone
    multiplied by \$1.00; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a prospecting permit zone north of 68° north
  latitude,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before the close of business on the last business
    day of November of the year in which the application for the permit
    is made, an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit
    zone multiplied by \$0.25,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} before the start of the third year of the permit,
    an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit zone
    multiplied by \$0.50, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} before the start of the fifth year of the permit,
    an amount equal to the number of hectares in the permit zone
    multiplied by \$1.00.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on work to obtain remission

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permittee who has done work in respect of a zone for which they have a
  prospecting permit may request remission of the charges the permittee
  has paid or is obligated to pay for that permit under section 14 by
  submitting a report on the work to the Mining Recorder no later than
  60 days after the expiry of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparation of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must be prepared in accordance with Part 1
  of Schedule 2, but if the report deals only with excavation, sampling
  or the examination of outcrops and surficial deposits --- or any
  combination of them --- and the cost of work is less than \$10,000,
  the report may be a simplified report prepared in accordance with Part
  2 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must be prepared and signed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a simplified report, by the
    individual who performed or supervised the work; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, by a professional geoscientist
    or a professional engineer as those terms are defined in subsection
    1(1) of the Engineering and Geoscience Professions Act, S.N.W.T.
    2006, c. 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The following must be submitted with the report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of work in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work by type of
    work, together with, for each type of work, details of the costs
    sufficient to enable evaluation of the report in accordance with
    subsection (8); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work that is
    attributable to each permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of costs

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In respect of the details of the costs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a permittee uses their own equipment to perform
    work, the cost claimed in relation to that equipment must not be
    more than the cost of leasing similar equipment for the period that
    the equipment was used in performing the work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a permittee personally performs work, the cost
    claimed in relation to that work must not be more than an amount
    equal to the cost of engaging another person to do that work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The permittee must keep all supporting documents used
  to justify the cost of work and make them available on request by the
  Mining Recorder until the permittee has received a confirmation under
  subsection (11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work reported once

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Work reported in one report must not be reported in
  any other report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of report

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Mining Recorder must evaluate the report to
  determine its compliance with Schedule 2 and the cost of work to be
  entered in a written confirmation of the cost of work under subsection
  (11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting documents

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} If the Mining Recorder requests supporting documents
  that justify the cost of work reported, the Mining Recorder must
  notify the permittee in writing and must specify the cost of work for
  which such documents are requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lack of justification

  [(10)]{.lawlabel} If the permittee does not, within four months after
  the day the notification is sent, provide the supporting documents
  requested, the cost of work to which they relate must be considered to
  be unjustified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Justified cost of work and allocation

  [(11)]{.lawlabel} After completing the evaluation of a report, the
  Mining Recorder must provide to the permittee a written confirmation
  of the cost of work that has been justified in the report and, if a
  request for allocation of the cost has been made under subsection
  17(5), of the amounts allocated in accordance with the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for deferral of charge and
extension of permit

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  permittee is unable to do work with respect to a prospecting permit
  zone because the permittee is, for reasons beyond the permittee's
  control, waiting for a public authority to give an authorization or
  decision without which the work cannot proceed, the permittee may
  request an extension of one year for the payment of the charge under
  section 14 and the duration of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements respecting request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made in writing to the
  Supervising Mining Recorder before the next charge under section 14 is
  payable and must be accompanied by documents showing that the
  permittee is waiting for the authorization or decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of extension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsection (2) have been met,
  the Supervising Mining Recorder must record the extension.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping of prospecting permits

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Prospecting permits may be grouped
  for the purpose of allocating the cost of work done with respect to
  them if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there are no more than four permits in the group;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the zones of the grouped permits are wholly or
    partly within a circle having a radius of 32 kilometres.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements respecting request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request to group prospecting permits must be
  submitted in writing to the Mining Recorder. It must be signed by each
  of the permittees and be accompanied by the applicable fee set out in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsections (1) and (2) are
  met, the Mining Recorder must issue a grouping certificate to each of
  the permittees.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of grouping certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A grouping certificate takes effect on the day on
  which the fee is submitted under subsection (2) and ceases to have
  effect on the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the 120th day after the day that any permit in the
    group expires or is cancelled, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a new grouping certificate in
    respect of any permit in the group takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of cost of work within group

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On request by any of the permittees referred to in a
  grouping certificate, the Mining Recorder must allocate, in accordance
  with the request, the cost of work that has been justified in a report
  in respect of any of the prospecting permits referred to in the
  grouping certificate to those permits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on reallocation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The cost of work allocated under subsection (5) must
  not be reallocated to any prospecting permit referred to in another
  grouping certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation not permitted

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The cost of work set out in a confirmation under
  subsection 15(11) with respect to a prospecting permit that was not,
  when the confirmation was provided, referred to in a grouping
  certificate must not be allocated to any other prospecting permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application to record claim in
prospecting permit zone

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permittee may apply to record a claim wholly or partially within the
  permittee's prospecting permit zone only if a confirmation under
  subsection 15(11) respecting that zone has been provided setting out a
  cost of work equal to or greater than the number of hectares in the
  permit zone multiplied by \$0.25.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of claim from permit zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a claim referred to in subsection (1) has been
  recorded, the area covered by the claim no longer forms part of the
  permit zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excess cost of work transferrable to
  claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the cost of work that has been justified in a
  report with respect to a prospecting permit exceeds the charges
  remitted under subsection 19(2), the permittee may request the Mining
  Recorder to allocate the excess cost of work to work required under
  subsection 39(1) on a claim referred to in subsection (1). The request
  must be in the prescribed form and be accompanied by the fee for a
  certificate of work set out in Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of charge

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  confirmation in respect of a prospecting permit is provided under
  subsection 15(11), the portion of the charges paid or payable under
  section 14 that does not exceed the cost of work set out in the
  confirmation is remitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}To whom remission is payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any charge referred to under section 14 that has been
  paid to the Mining Recorder and that is remitted under this section
  must be repaid by the Minister to the person entitled to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of permit on request

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A permittee may submit to
the Mining Recorder a written request for cancellation of their
prospecting permit. The cancellation takes effect at the end of January
after it is requested.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of permit for non-payment

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If any charge payable
under section 14 is not paid when due, the prospecting permit is
cancelled.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of expiration or cancellation of
permit

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lands that were covered by a prospecting permit that has expired or
  has been cancelled are open for prospecting and staking at noon on the
  day following the first business day after the day on which the permit
  expired or was cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary prohibition relating to
  former permittee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For one year after a prospecting permit expires or is
  cancelled, the former permittee and any person related to the former
  permittee are not permitted to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply for a prospecting permit for a zone, or apply
    to record a claim, that covers any part of the area that was covered
    by the expired or cancelled permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire a legal or beneficial interest in a
    prospecting permit or claim referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Claims]{.HTitleText1} {#h-811730 .Part}

### [Size, Boundaries and Marking of a Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811731 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Area of claim --- excluded lands

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  area of a claim must not exceed 1250 hectares and must not include any
  lands referred to in section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shape and boundaries of claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every claim must, as nearly as possible, meet all of
  the following specifications:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is rectangular in shape;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its boundaries run north, south, east and west
    astronomically;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the length of each boundary is 500 metres or a
    multiple of 500 metres;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the length of the longest boundary is not more than
    five times the length of the shortest.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification tags

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  payment of the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1, the Mining
  Recorder must issue a set of four identification tags, with the
  following inscriptions, for use in identifying the corners of a claim
  --- "NE 1" for the northeast corner, "SE 2" for the southeast corner,
  "SW 3" for the southwest corner and "NW 4" for the northwest corner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduced-area tags

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On payment of the applicable fee set out in Schedule
  1, the Mining Recorder must issue a set of four reduced-area tags for
  use in marking the corners of a reduced-area claim referred to in
  section 52.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification number

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each identification tag in a set must contain a
  unique identification number for the set.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking of boundaries

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  boundaries and corners of a claim must be marked by legal posts in
  accordance with sections 26 to 30.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marking in treed area

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a treed area, the boundaries of a claim must be
  marked along as much of the length as it is possible to mark by
  cutting underbrush and flagging or blazing trees.

### [Legal Posts]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811753 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for legal post

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  legal post must be one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a treed area,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a post of sound wood planted firmly in an upright
      position and standing not less than one metre above the ground,
      with at least the upper 30 centimetres squared off so that each
      face of the squared portion is not less than four centimetres in
      width, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a tree found in position and cut off not less
      than one metre above the ground, with at least the upper 30
      centimetres squared off so that each face of the squared portion
      is not less than four centimetres in width;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a treeless area, a post described in
    subparagraph (a)(i) or a mound of stones that is not less than one
    metre in diameter at the base and not less than 50 centimetres in
    height.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements when mounds of stones are
  used

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a legal post is a mound of stones, then

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a tag being secured to that post is
    to be read as a reference to a tag being inserted in a shatterproof
    and waterproof container secured in the apex of the mound; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to information being inscribed on that
    post is to be read as a reference to information being clearly and
    indelibly written on paper or inscribed on durable material and
    inserted in a shatterproof and waterproof container secured in the
    apex of the mound.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundary post

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), boundary
  posts must be erected at intervals of not more than 500 metres along
  the boundaries of a claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstructions in boundary line

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, because of the presence of a body of water or
  other natural obstruction or lands on which entry for staking has not
  been authorized by the holder of the surface rights, it is not
  practicable to erect a boundary post on the boundary line of the
  claim, a boundary post must be erected on the boundary line on each
  side of the body of water or natural obstruction or lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common boundary post

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When two or more claims are staked at the same time
  by or on behalf of the same licensee and have a common boundary, a
  single boundary post may be erected to mark any common interval along
  the boundary.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Numbering of boundary posts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Boundary posts must be numbered consecutively in a
  clockwise direction, commencing at "1" after the northeast corner
  post, and recommencing at "1" after each subsequent corner post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on boundary posts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The name of the claim, the number referred to in
  subsection (4) and the following information must be inscribed on each
  boundary post:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the northern boundary, the letters "NBP" or
    "BLN";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the eastern boundary, the letters "EBP" or
    "BLE";

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the southern boundary, the letters "SBP" or
    "BLS"; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the western boundary, the letters "WBP" or
    "BLO".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corner post

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 29, corner posts
  must be erected at the four corners of a claim, and an identification
  tag marked with the required inscription must be secured to each
  corner post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common corner post

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When two or more claims are staked at the same time
  by or on behalf of the same licensee and have a common corner, a
  single corner post may be used to mark the common corner. The
  identification tag for each claim, marked with the required
  inscription, must be secured to the common corner post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on corner posts

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following information is to be clearly and
  indelibly inscribed on the identification tag of each corner post or,
  if there is insufficient room on the tag, on the post itself:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensee for whom the claim is
    staked;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who erected that post, if
    that person is not the licensee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the claim; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that the post was erected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness post

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  because of the presence of a body of water or other natural
  obstruction or lands on which entry for staking has not been
  authorized by the holder of the surface rights, it is not practicable
  to erect a corner post, a witness post must be erected at one of the
  following locations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a boundary or an extension of a boundary of the
    claim and as near as practicable to the corner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not practicable to erect it at a location
    specified in paragraph (a), at a location as near as practicable to
    the corner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corner post tag on witness post

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The identification tag for the corner post must be
  attached to the witness post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information on witness post

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the information referred to in
  subsection 28(3), the following information must be inscribed on the
  identification tag after the letters "WP": the compass bearing and
  distance in metres, measured in a straight line, from the witness post
  to the location where the corner post would have been erected had it
  been practicable to do so.

### [Staked Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811800 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements to complete staking

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** When the boundaries and
corners of a claim have been marked in accordance with sections 23 to
29, the date and time of completion of the requirements of those
sections and the licence number of the licensee referred to in paragraph
28(3)(a) must be inscribed on the identification tag of the northeast
corner post or of the witness post designating the northeast corner, as
the case may be. The claim is then staked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of staking

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Compliance with the
requirements set out in sections 23 to 30 may be verified by a person
authorized by the Minister to perform any function related to the
administration and enforcement of these Regulations.

### [Moving Legal Posts and Modifying Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811805 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as provided in subsections (2) and (3), it is prohibited

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to remove, displace or destroy a legal post; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to remove, deface or alter an identification tag
    secured to a legal post or an inscription on a legal post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claim holder, the holder of the surface rights or
  a public authority may remove a legal post if the post is interfering
  with their use of the lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the holder of the surface rights or a public
  authority removes a legal post, they must, within 30 days after the
  removal, notify the claim holder and the Mining Recorder of the
  removal.

### [Recording of a Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811815 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Only
  the licensee for whom a claim has been staked may submit an
  application to the Mining Recorder to record the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed form and time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in the prescribed form
  within 60 days after the date on which the staking of the claim is
  completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee and map

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by the applicable
  fee set out in Schedule 1 and a map or sketch at the scale of 1:50 000
  showing all of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location of the claim in relation to permanent
    topographical features in the vicinity of the claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any nearby prospecting permit zones, recorded
    claims and leased claims;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the positions of the corner posts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the positions and numbers of the boundary posts;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the positions of any witness posts.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of claim

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsections (1) to (3) are
  met, the Mining Recorder must record the claim as soon as practicable
  after the 60th day following the day on which the staking is
  completed. The recording date is considered to be the date that the
  application is received at the Mining Recorder's office.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of claim

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless a recorded claim is leased under subsection
  60(5) or its recording is cancelled under subsection 50(2) or 53(3) or
  section 54 or 55, the duration of a recorded claim is 10 years,
  beginning on its recording date, plus any suspensions recorded under
  section 51 and any extension under subsection 60(4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflicting applications

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** If applications have been
submitted to record two or more claims that overlap, the claim that is
staked first in compliance with the staking requirements is the one that
must be recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim recorded under the law of another
province

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If it
  is determined that lands covered by a mining claim that is recorded or
  otherwise recognized under the law regulating the disposition of
  mining interests in another province are wholly or partly in the
  Northwest Territories Mining District, and if the portion in the
  Northwest Territories does not contain lands referred to in section 5,
  the claim holder may, within 90 days after the determination, apply to
  the Mining Recorder to have the portion of the claim within the
  Northwest Territories recorded as a separate claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application to record the claim is not made
  within the period set out in subsection (1), the lands that were
  covered by the claim and that are within the Northwest Territories are
  considered to never have been covered by a claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording date and time

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application made under
  subsection (1) and the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1, the
  Mining Recorder must record the claim as soon as practicable, using
  the date and time when the claim was recorded or otherwise recognized
  under the law regulating the disposition of mining interests in the
  other province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim lying partly in another province

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** If a plan of survey
recorded under section 59 and prepared with respect to a recorded claim
shows that part of the claim is in another province, the Mining Recorder
must reduce the boundaries of the claim in accordance with the plan, and
must, as soon as practicable, notify the claim holder of the reduction.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inquire into the allegations contained in the

  Mining Recorder may

### [Work Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811863 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work required

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  that is equal to or greater than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$10 per full or partial hectare in the claim
    during the two-year period following the day on which the claim is
    recorded; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$5 per full or partial hectare in the claim during
    each subsequent one-year period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work before recording of claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Work that was done during the two years before the
  claim was recorded is considered to be work done on the claim during
  the period referred to in paragraph (1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of work or request for extension

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The claim holder must, no
later than 90 days after the end of the period during which the work
referred to in subsection 39(1) must be done, submit to the Mining
Recorder

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a report of the work that has been done in respect of
  the claim; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an application for a one-year extension to do the
  work.

### [Work Reports and Applications for Extension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811875 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report on work

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  report of the work that has been done in respect of a claim must be
  prepared in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 2, but if the report
  deals only with excavation, sampling or the examination of outcrops
  and surficial deposits --- or any combination of them --- and the cost
  of work is less than \$10,000, the report may be a simplified report
  prepared in accordance with Part 2 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must be prepared and signed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a simplified report, by the
    individual who performed or supervised the work; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, by a professional geoscientist
    or a professional engineer as those terms are defined in subsection
    1(1) of the Engineering and Geoscience Professions Act, S.N.W.T.
    2006, c. 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report must be accompanied by the fee for a
  certificate of work set out in Schedule 1 and the following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of work in the prescribed form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work by type of
    work, together with, for each type of work, details of the costs
    sufficient to enable evaluation of the report in accordance with
    subsection 44(1); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the cost of work that is
    attributable to each claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Claim holder's equipment or work

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a holder of a recorded claim uses their own
  equipment to perform work or personally performs work,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost claimed in relation to that equipment must
    not be more than the cost of leasing similar equipment for the
    period that the equipment is used in performing the work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cost claimed in relation to that work must not
    be more than an amount equal to the cost of engaging another person
    to do that work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a recorded claim must keep all
  supporting documents used to justify the cost of work and make them
  available on request by the Mining Recorder until the holder has
  received a certificate of work under paragraph 47(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Work reported once

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Work reported in one report must not be reported in
  any other report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for extension of time

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application for a one-year extension referred to in paragraph 40(b)
  must be in the prescribed form and be accompanied by the applicable
  fee set out in Schedule 1 and the charge payable for that period under
  subsection 43(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of extension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsection (1) are met, the
  Mining Recorder must issue to the claim holder a certificate of
  extension for a one-year period to do the work, except that not more
  than three certificates of extension are to be issued with respect to
  a claim.

### [Charges to Hold Recorded Claim and Assess Mineral Potential]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811901 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charge

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  assess its mineral potential:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$10 per full or partial hectare in the claim

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$5 per full or partial hectare in the claim for
    each subsequent one-year period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claim holder must pay the charges in accordance
  with subsection 42(1) or 49(1), as the case may be.

### [Evaluation of Report and Cost of Work]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811909 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of report

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mining Recorder must evaluate the report of work that is referred to
  in paragraph 40(a) to determine its compliance with Schedule 2 and the
  cost of work to be entered in a certificate of work under subsection
  47(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Mining Recorder requests supporting documents
  that justify the cost of work reported, the Mining Recorder must
  notify the holder of the recorded claim in writing and must specify
  the cost of work for which the documents are requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lack of justification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the holder of the recorded claim does not, within
  four months after the day the notification is sent, provide the
  supporting documents requested, the cost of work to which they relate
  must be considered to be unjustified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of excess cost of work

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), if a
  recorded claim is not grouped under section 46 and the cost of work
  that has been justified in a report in respect of that claim exceeds
  the cost of work required to be done on it under subsection 39(1) at
  the time the certificate of work respecting the report is ready to be
  issued, the Mining Recorder must allocate the excess cost of work to
  the next period or periods for which work is still required to be done
  on the claim under subsection 39(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unallocated excess cost of work

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At any time before the evaluation of the report has
  been completed, the claim holder may submit a written request to the
  Mining Recorder to not allocate the excess cost of work or to allocate
  it to fewer periods than would be required under subsection (1) and
  the Mining Recorder must comply with the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previous payment of charges

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A claim holder who has paid a charge in respect of a
  period under subsection 43(1) or 49(1) may submit a written request to
  the Mining Recorder that remission be granted of the charge in an
  amount equal to the excess cost of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request re unallocated excess cost of
  work

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If an unallocated excess cost of work is recorded
  with respect to a recorded claim, the claim holder may submit a
  request to the Mining Recorder to allocate that cost as specified in
  the request. The request must be in the prescribed form and be
  accompanied by the fee for a certificate of work set out in Schedule
  1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation of unallocated excess cost
  of work

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the unallocated excess cost of work is sufficient
  to comply with the request, the Mining Recorder must allocate it in
  accordance with the request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping of recorded claims

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Recorded claims may be grouped for
  the purpose of allocating the cost of work done with respect to them
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claims are contiguous;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total area of the group does not exceed 5000
    hectares; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} none of the claims is leased.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for grouping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request to group recorded claims must be submitted
  in the prescribed form to the Mining Recorder. It must be signed by
  each of the claim holders and be accompanied by the applicable fee set
  out in Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsections (1) and (2) are
  met, the Mining Recorder must issue a grouping certificate respecting
  the claims to each of the claim holders.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A grouping certificate takes effect on the day on
  which the fee under subsection (2) is received and ceases to have
  effect on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the recording of any claim in the
    group is cancelled,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a lease of any claim in the group
    takes effect, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a new grouping certificate in
    respect of any claim in the group takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for allocation re grouped
  claims

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} On request by a holder of a recorded claim listed in
  a grouping certificate, the Mining Recorder must allocate, in
  accordance with the request, the cost of work that has been justified
  in a report in respect of any of the claims listed in the grouping
  certificate to any of the other claims listed in the certificate, for
  any of the periods referred to in subsection 39(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of request

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A request must be in the prescribed form and, if it
  is not submitted with the report referred to in section 41, it must be
  accompanied by the fee for a certificate of work set out in Schedule
  1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on reallocation

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The cost of work allocated to a recorded claim
  referred to in a grouping certificate must not be reallocated to any
  recorded claim referred to in another grouping certificate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of certificate of work

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Mining Recorder must issue a certificate of work in the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when evaluation of a report respecting a recorded
    claim has been completed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when allocation of the cost of work under
    subsection 18(3), 45(1), (2) or (4) or 46(5) has been done.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of work respecting claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of work respecting a claim must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allocation of the cost of work.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of charge

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of the charges
  paid or payable in respect of a period referred to in section 43 in an
  amount equal to the cost of work allocated to the recorded claim
  during that period in the certificate of work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission in subsequent periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a holder of a recorded claim pays a charge in
  respect of a period under subsection 43(1) or 49(1), remission is
  granted of the portion of the charge that is equal to the cost of work
  allocated to the claim during any subsequent period that exceeds the
  cost of work required to be done under subsection 39(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repayment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any charge referred to in section 43 that has been
  paid to the Mining Recorder and that is remitted under this section
  must be repaid by the Minister to the person entitled to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insufficient work

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), if a
  certificate of work indicates that the cost of work is less than the
  amount required by subsection 39(1), the holder of the recorded claim
  must pay a charge that is equal to the difference between the charge
  referred to in subsection 43(1) and the cost of work indicated in the
  certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of charge

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claim holder must pay the charge within 60 days
  after the day on which the certificate is issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of recording of claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If three certificates of work are issued in respect
  of a claim that indicate that the cost of work is less than the amount
  required by subsection 39(1), the recording of the claim is cancelled
  on the day that the third certificate of work is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of application of requirements

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  requirements set out in section 39 and subsection 43(1) apply for the
  duration of a recorded claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of recording

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The recording of a claim is cancelled on one of the
  following days, as the case may be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day after the last day on which a report must
    be submitted under section 40, if a report with respect to that
    claim has not been submitted in accordance with section 41 and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the claim holder has not been issued a
      certificate of extension with respect to that claim under section
      42, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a suspension with respect to that claim has not
      been granted under section 51;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 84, the day that is 61 days
    after the day on which a certificate of work with respect to that
    claim is issued under section 47, if the cost of work allocated to
    that claim in the certificate of work is less than the cost of work
    required to be done under subsection 39(1) and the claim holder has
    not paid the difference between the charge referred to in subsection
    43(1) and the cost of work indicated in the certificate.

### [Suspension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811981 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of payment and work
requirements

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  holder of a recorded claim is unable to do work as required under
  subsection 39(1) because the claim holder is, for reasons beyond the
  claim holder's control, waiting for a public authority to give an
  authorization or decision without which the work cannot proceed, the
  claim holder may request a suspension of one year --- beginning on the
  anniversary date of the recording of the claim --- of the work
  requirements under section 39 and the charges under subsection 43(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made in writing to the
  Supervising Mining Recorder no later than 90 days after the end of the
  applicable period under section 39 and subsection 43(1) for which the
  suspension is requested, and must be accompanied by documents showing
  that the claim holder is waiting for the authorization or decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Companies' Creditors Arrangement
  Act](/eng/acts/C-36)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an order under section 11.02 of the [Companies'
  Creditors Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36) has been made with respect
  to a claim holder, the holder of a recorded claim may request a
  suspension of the payment and work requirements under section 39 and
  subsection 43(1) until the anniversary date of the recording of the
  claim that is at least 12 months after the day on which the order has
  ceased to have effect with respect to the claim holder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for request

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The request must be made in writing to the
  Supervising Mining Recorder no later than 90 days after the day on
  which the order was made, and must be accompanied by a certified copy
  of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of suspension

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements of subsections (1) and (2) or (3)
  and (4) are met, the Supervising Mining Recorder must record the
  suspension with respect to the claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of suspension

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A suspension extends the duration of the claim by the
  duration of the suspension.

### [Reduced Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-811995 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for reduced-area claim

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a recorded claim (the original claim) may make an
  application to the Mining Recorder to record, within the area of the
  original claim, a reduced-area claim if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost of work set out in a certificate of work
    with respect to the original claim is at least \$10 per full or
    partial hectare; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reduced-area claim has been staked in
    accordance with sections 23 to 30, using reduced-area tags issued
    under subsection 24(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be made in the prescribed form
  no later than 60 days after the staking of the reduced-area claim, and
  must be accompanied by the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 and
  the map or sketch referred to in subsection 33(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of reduced-area claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements set out in this section are met,
  the Mining Recorder must record the reduced-area claim, effective on
  the next anniversary date of the recording of the original claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of recording

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the recording of a reduced-area claim takes
  effect,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recording date for it is considered to be the
    recording date of the original claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information recorded by the Mining Recorder,
    including the requests and the documents filed with respect to the
    original claim, are considered to have been recorded or presented
    with respect to the reduced-area claim; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the recording of the original claim is cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reopening of lands for prospecting and
  staking

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 56(3), the lands in the
  original claim that are not within the reduced-area claim are open for
  prospecting and staking at noon on the day following the first
  business day after the day on which the recording of the original
  claim was cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} On the day the recording of the reduced-area claim
  takes effect --- or if that day is not a business day, on the first
  business day that follows --- the Mining Recorder must post a notice
  in the Mining Recorder's office specifying when the lands in the
  original claim that are not within the reduced-area claim are open for
  prospecting and staking, and setting out the boundaries of the
  reduced-area claim.

### [Cancellation of Recording of Claims]{.HTitleText2} {#h-812014 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Mining Recorder has information that either of the following
  circumstances apply in respect of a recorded claim, the Mining
  Recorder must immediately send the claim holder a notice that the
  recording of the claim will be cancelled unless, within 60 days, the
  holder can show that the information is not correct:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the claim holder knowingly made a false or
    misleading statement on the application to record the claim;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claim holder has contravened subsection 7(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to claim holder

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must set out the reason for the impending
  cancellation and a summary of the evidence to support the
  cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the claim holder has not shown the Mining Recorder
  that the information is not correct within 60 days after the day on
  which the notice was sent, the recording of the claim is cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of recording or exclusion
  of lands

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If it is determined that a recorded claim includes
  any of the following lands, the Mining Recorder must cancel the
  recording of the claim or, if it is possible to exclude those lands
  from the claim, must amend the boundaries of the recorded claim to do
  so and must notify the claim holder accordingly:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} lands referred to in section 5;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lands that were staked in contravention of section
    6;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} lands to which these Regulations do not apply.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for cancellation of recording

**[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a recorded
claim may request the cancellation of the recording of the claim by
submitting to the Mining Recorder a written request for cancellation
accompanied by the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of cancellation

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  recording of a claim is cancelled on the day that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Mining Recorder receives the request for its
    cancellation and payment of the fee under section 54;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time specified in paragraph 60(2)(b) has ended
    and no application for a lease on the claim has been received; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a lease on the claim has terminated without being
    renewed or was cancelled under subsection 63(2) or section 64.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For one year after the recording of a claim is
  cancelled, the former claim holder and any person related to the
  former claim holder are not permitted to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply for a prospecting permit for a zone, or apply
    to record a claim, that covers any part of the area that was covered
    by the claim the recording of which has been cancelled; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire a legal or beneficial interest in a
    prospecting permit or claim referred to in paragraph (a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reopening of lands

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lands that were covered by a claim the recording of which has been
  cancelled under subsection 50(2), section 55 or subsection 62(3) are
  open for prospecting and staking at noon on the day following the
  first business day after the day on which the recording of the claim
  was cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reopening of lands --- 30-day delay

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 84, the lands that were covered by
  a claim the recording of which has been cancelled under subsection
  49(3) or 53(3) or paragraph 53(4)(b) are open for prospecting and
  staking at noon on the day that is 30 days after the day on which the
  recording of the claim was cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay in reopening lands

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe
  that there is unremedied environmental damage to the lands that were
  covered by a claim the recording of which has been cancelled under a
  provision set out in subsection (1) or (2), or the lands referred to
  in subsection 52(5), the Minister may delay the opening of the lands
  for prospecting and staking.

## [Lease of a Recorded Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812048 .Part}

### [Plan of Survey]{.HTitleText2} {#h-812049 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survey required for lease

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a recorded claim who wants to obtain a lease of a recorded
  claim must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain a plan of survey of the claim prepared in
    accordance with the [Canada Lands Surveys Act](/eng/acts/L-6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a report of any overlap with a boundary of
    any other claim;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} send a copy of the plan of survey, the report and a
    notice in the prescribed form, by registered mail or courier, to the
    holders of adjacent recorded claims and leased claims at their
    addresses on record with the Mining Recorder; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} send to the Mining Recorder a copy of the plan of
    survey, the report, the notice and evidence that the holders of the
    adjacent claims have received copies of those documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Perimeter survey

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If two or more contiguous recorded claims are to be
  included in one lease and their aggregate area, as stated in the
  applications to record them, does not exceed 1250 hectares, one plan
  of survey may be prepared that shows only the perimeter of the
  collection of contiguous claims.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the documents referred to in paragraph
  (1)(d), the Mining Recorder must post a copy of the notice in the
  office of the Mining Recorder for a period of 21 days.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extra charge for additional area --- one
claim

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  surveyed area of one claim exceeds the area stated in the application
  to record that claim, the claim holder must pay to the Mining Recorder
  the charge determined by the formula

  ::: {#812064 .Subsection}
  A × C × D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the excess area in hectares of the claim;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is \$5; and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of years from the date of recording of the claim.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extra charge for additional area ---
  collection of claims

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the surveyed area of contiguous recorded claims
  that are shown in a plan of survey of their perimeter prepared in
  accordance with subsection 57(2) exceeds their aggregate area as
  stated in the applications to record those claims, the claim holder
  must pay to the Mining Recorder for each claim the charge determined
  by the formula

  ::: {#812071 .Subsection}
  (A / B) × C × D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total excess area in hectares of all of the claims;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of claims in the survey;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is \$5; and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of years from the recording date of the claim.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted of the portion of the charge
  paid or payable under subsection (1) or (2), as the case may be, that
  does not exceed the cost of work set out in certificates of work
  issued with respect to the claim or contiguous claims and that has not
  been remitted under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of plan of survey

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The Mining Recorder must
record the plan of survey if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days have elapsed after the end of the period for
  posting of a notice under subsection 57(3) and the Mining Recorder has
  not received any protest with respect to the survey;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the plan of survey has been made by the Surveyor
  General under section 31 of the [Canada Lands Surveys
  Act](/eng/acts/L-6);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 has been
  paid;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of section 57 have been met; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any charge payable under subsection 58(1) or (2) has
  been paid.

### [Requirements Respecting Leases]{.HTitleText2} {#h-812086 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for lease

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a recorded claim who wants to obtain a lease of a recorded
  claim or a collection of contiguous recorded claims must apply for the
  lease in the prescribed form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application fee and limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by the applicable fee set out in
    Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be received by the Mining Recorder at least one
    year before the end of the duration of the claim or of the claim
    that has the earliest recording date of any of the claims in the
    collection of contiguous claims.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other requirements for lease

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before a lease is issued,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an official plan of survey of the claim, or the
    collection of contiguous claims, must have been recorded under
    section 59;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} certificates of work must have been recorded with
    respect to the claim, or with respect to each claim in the
    collection of contiguous recorded claims, that allocate to the
    claim, or to each claim, a cost of work of at least \$25 per
    hectare, of which the total of the costs of the official plan of
    survey and of the construction of any roads, airstrips or docks does
    not exceed \$5 per hectare; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rent for the first year of the lease must have
    been paid to the Mining Recorder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of duration of claim

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a claim holder who has applied for a lease advises
  the Mining Recorder in writing that they cannot, for reasons beyond
  their control, obtain an official plan of survey and record it before
  the end of the duration of the claim to be leased,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the recorded claim is extended for
    one year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements under section 39 and subsection
    43(1) do not apply for that year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application for a lease on the claim remains
    valid for that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of lease

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, before the end of the duration of the recorded
  claim or of the claim that has the earliest recording date of any of
  the claims in the collection of contiguous recorded claims, the
  requirements of subsections (1) to (3) are met, the Minister must
  issue the lease to the claim holder for a term of 21 years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual rent for lease

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annual rent for a lease is \$2.50 per hectare during the first term
  and \$5.00 per hectare during each renewed term.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When rent is due

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rent must be paid to the Mining Recorder before
  the anniversary date of the lease.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for renewal of lease

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  obtain renewal of a lease, the lessee must, at least six months before
  the end of the existing lease, submit to the Mining Recorder a written
  request for renewal accompanied by the applicable fee set out in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction in area of leased claim

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the time of renewal, the Mining Recorder must
  reduce the area specified in the lease if, at least 90 days before the
  end of the existing lease,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lessee requests it and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the lease covers only one recorded claim, that
      claim has been reduced in area in accordance with section 52, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, one or more recorded claims have been
      removed from the lease; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an official plan of survey of the reduced area has
    been prepared in accordance with [Canada Lands Surveys
    Act](/eng/acts/L-6) and has been submitted to the Mining Recorder
    for recording.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of recording of claim

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The recording of a claim that has been removed from a
  lease under subparagraph (2)(a)(ii) is cancelled at the time that the
  renewal of the lease takes effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of renewal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, before the existing lease ends, the lessee pays
  the rent for the first year of the renewal, the Minister must issue
  the renewal for a term of 21 years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of overdue rent

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  rent is not paid within 30 days after the day it is due, the Mining
  Recorder must send the lessee a notice stating the amount of rent due
  and the rate of interest payable on that amount.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of lease for non-payment
  of rent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the rent due, together with interest from the date
  on which the rent was due, is not paid within 60 days after the day
  the notice was sent, the lease is cancelled on the 61st day after the
  notice was sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of lease by lessee

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lease is cancelled if the lessee submits to the Mining Recorder a
  written notice of cancellation. The cancellation takes effect on the
  day the notice is received by the Mining Recorder or, if a later day
  for cancellation is set out in the notice, on that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary prohibition relating to
  former lessee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For one year after a lease is cancelled, the former
  lessee and any person related to the former lessee are not permitted
  to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} apply for a prospecting permit for a zone, or apply
    to record a claim, that covers any part of the area that was covered
    by the cancelled lease; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire a legal or beneficial interest in a
    prospecting permit or claim referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Transfer of a Prospecting Permit, Claim or Lease]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812136 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of permit

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The transfer of a
prospecting permit or an interest in it may be recorded only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transfer is made to a licensee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a request for recording of the transfer is made by
  the permittee in writing to the Mining Recorder and is signed by the
  permittee and by the transferee; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 is paid to
  the Mining Recorder.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for transfer of recorded
claim or lease

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  transfer of a recorded claim or a lease of a recorded claim or an
  interest in either of them may be recorded only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transfer is made to a licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the transfer is of a recorded claim, a request
    for recording of the transfer is made by the claim holder in the
    prescribed form to the Mining Recorder and is signed by the claim
    holder and the transferee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the transfer is of a lease,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a request for recording of the transfer is made
      by the lessee in writing to the Mining Recorder and is signed by
      the lessee and the transferee, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the rent and any interest on it is paid;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fee set out in Schedule 1 is paid to
    the Mining Recorder.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of lease includes claims

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The transfer of a lease includes the transfer of the
  recorded claims to which the lease applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on transfer within mining
  property

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The transfer of a lease or a recorded claim either of
  which is part of a mining property may be recorded only if security in
  the amount of any unpaid royalties in relation to the mining property
  has been deposited with the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of recorded claim or lease

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  recording of a claim is cancelled, or a lease and the recording of the
  claims covered by the lease are cancelled, on the day that any of the
  following events occur:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has realized on a charge or security
    over the real property of the claim holder or the lessee for the
    costs of remedying any environmental condition or environmental
    damage under subsection 11.8(8) of the [Companies' Creditors
    Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36) or subsection 14.06(7) of the
    [Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act](/eng/acts/B-3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interests in territorial lands represented by
    the claim or lease have reverted to the Crown as a result of a court
    order made under the [Companies' Creditors Arrangement
    Act](/eng/acts/C-36) or the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has accepted the claim or lease as a
    security in respect of a debt or other obligation owed to the Crown
    and the Minister has realized on the security under section 156 of
    the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delay in opening lands

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lands that were covered by a claim or a lease the
  recording of which has been cancelled under subsection (1), are not
  open for prospecting and staking until the Minister opens them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition of interests in lands

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), at any time after the
  recording of a claim (the original claim) was cancelled under
  subsection (1), the Minister may, with respect to the lands that were
  covered by the original claim, instruct the Mining Recorder to record,
  in the name of a specified person, a new claim that covers those
  lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New claim considered as transfer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When a new claim is recorded under subsection (3),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the new claim need not be staked;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recording date for the new claim is considered
    to be the recording date of the original claim;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information recorded by the Mining Recorder,
    including the requests and the documents filed with respect to the
    original claim, are considered to have been recorded or presented
    with respect to the new claim;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} despite subsection 33(5), the duration of the new
    claim extends for a period beginning on the day on which the
    Minister instructs that the new claim be recorded (the start day) to
    the next anniversary date of the recording of the original claim
    that is at least "x" days after the start day, where "x" is equal to
    the number of days that were left in the duration of the original
    claim immediately before it was cancelled; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of paragraphs 39(1)(b) and
    43(1)(b) apply with respect to the new claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister may issue new lease

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (7), at any time after a lease
  is cancelled under subsection (1), the Minister may issue a new lease
  that covers the same lands as were covered by the cancelled lease.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New lease considered as transfer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A new lease issued under subsection (5) is considered
  to be a transfer of the previous lease on the same lands, with the
  same duration as was left on the previous lease at the time it was
  cancelled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may only act under subsection (3) or (5)
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands covered by the new claim or lease have
    not been opened for prospecting and staking; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is in the financial interest of the Crown or
    will aid in remedying environmental damage on territorial lands.

## [Royalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812180 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commencement of production of mine

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of these Regulations, the date on which a mine commences
  production is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the mine includes a mill or concentrator, the
    first day of the first 90-day period during which the mill or
    concentrator operates at an average of at least 60% of its rated
    capacity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the day the mine begins to produce
    minerals in reasonable commercial quantities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumptions respecting mineral or
  processed mineral

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a mineral or
  processed mineral is considered to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be produced and be part of the output of a mine if
    the mineral or processed mineral is in a saleable form or has been
    removed from the mine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} form part of the output of the mine if it is
    produced from the reprocessing of tailings from a mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumptions respecting related persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals that have been
    sold by an operator to a person not related to the operator are
    later sold to a person related to the operator, those minerals or
    processed minerals are considered to have been sold by the operator
    to a related person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if minerals or processed minerals that have been
    sold by an operator to a person related to the operator are later
    sold to a person not related to the operator and proof of that sale
    is provided, those minerals or processed minerals are considered to
    have been sold by the operator to a person not related to the
    operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation of value of output

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the value of the

  with the formula

  ::: {#812212 .Provision}
  A + B -- C + D + E + F + G + H -- I + J

  where

  [A]{.dfn}

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from sales, during the fiscal

        to persons not related to the operator, if proof of those sales
        is provided,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the market value of any minerals or processed

        a person related to the operator, or to any other person if the
        proof of that disposition is not provided, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the minerals or processed minerals produced
        from the mine are precious stones that have been cut or polished
        before their sale or transfer, the market value of those
        precious stones before they were cut or polished;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the market value of any inventories of minerals and processed
      minerals produced from the mine, as at the end of the fiscal year,
      determined under subsection (9);

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the market value of any inventories of minerals and processed
      minerals produced from the mine, as at the beginning of the fiscal
      year, determined under subsection (9);

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any payment received during the
        fiscal year that is related to a cost that has been claimed as a
        deduction or allowance under this section, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that cost;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is any excess amount referred to in paragraph 70(5)(b);

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is any amount withdrawn, during the fiscal year, from a mining
      reclamation trust established in respect of lands to which these
      Regulations apply, up to the maximum of the total of the amounts
      contributed to the trust;

  [G]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of any proceeds received, during the fiscal year,
      from insurance on minerals or processed minerals produced from the
      mine;

  [H]{.dfn}

  before the last day of the fourth month after the end of each fiscal
  year of a mine, including the fiscal year during which the mine
  commences production and all subsequent years for which there are any
  amounts that qualify for determining the values of A to H and J in
  subsection 69(4), the operator of the mine must deliver to the Chief a
  mining royalty return, in the prescribed form, setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and a description of the mine;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the operator;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the names of processing plants to which minerals or
    processed minerals have been shipped from the mine for processing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the weight of the minerals or processed minerals
    produced from the mine during the fiscal year;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the weight and value of the minerals or processed
    minerals produced from the mine that were

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sold or transferred during the fiscal year of the
      mine to persons not related to the operator,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sold or transferred during the fiscal year of
      the mine to persons related to the operator,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in inventory at the beginning of the fiscal
      year of the mine, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in inventory at the end of the fiscal year of
      the mine;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any costs, deductions and allowances claimed under
    subsection 70(1);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if exploration costs are claimed as a deduction
    under paragraph 70(1)(g), or if costs are included in the costs
    eligible for a development allowance under paragraph 70(1)(i), the
    recorded claims or leases on which those costs were incurred;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in respect of depreciable assets,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of depreciable assets at
      the beginning of the fiscal year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the cost of additions during the fiscal year to
      depreciable assets,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the proceeds from the disposition during the
      fiscal year of depreciable assets,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of depreciable assets at
      the end of the fiscal year before deduction of a depreciation
      allowance,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of depreciable assets at
      the end of the fiscal year after deduction of a depreciation
      allowance, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of depreciable assets disposed
      of during the fiscal year;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of development allowances,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance at the beginning of the
      fiscal year of costs eligible for a development allowance,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the mining royalty return is delivered for
      the first fiscal year of the mine, the amount of the costs
      determined under subparagraphs 70(1)(i)(i) and (ii),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amounts of each of the costs identified in
      subparagraphs 70(1)(i)(iii) to (v) incurred during the fiscal
      year,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of costs eligible for a
      development allowance at the end of the fiscal year before
      deduction of a development allowance, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of costs eligible for a
      development allowance at the end of the fiscal year after
      deduction of a development allowance;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any mining reclamation trust
    established for lands to which these Regulations apply,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts contributed to the
      mining reclamation trust,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of contributions to the
      mining reclamation trust at the beginning of the fiscal year,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the amounts contributed to the mining
      reclamation trust during the fiscal year,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of contributions to the
      mining reclamation trust at the end of the fiscal year before any
      deduction of a mining reclamation trust contribution allowance,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the undeducted balance of contributions to the
      mining reclamation trust at the end of the fiscal year after
      deduction of a mining reclamation trust contribution allowance,
      and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts withdrawn from the
      mining reclamation trust during the fiscal year and in previous
      fiscal years;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in respect of processing assets,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of the processing assets at the
      beginning of the fiscal year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of any new processing assets
      added to the mine during the fiscal year,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of any processing assets that
      were substituted for other processing assets of the mine during
      the fiscal year,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of any processing assets for
      which other processing assets were substituted during the fiscal
      year,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of any processing assets not
      used, sold, discarded or otherwise disposed of during the fiscal
      year, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the original cost of the processing assets at
      the end of the fiscal year;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any payment received during the fiscal year that is
    related to a cost that has been claimed as a deduction or allowance;
    and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any amount by which the proceeds of disposition of
    assets for which a depreciation allowance has been claimed exceed
    the undeducted balance of depreciable assets at the end of the
    fiscal year in which the assets were disposed of.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to accompany royalty return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every mining royalty return must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} accompanied by the financial statements for the
    mine or, if the mine has no financial statements, the financial
    statements of the operator of the mine, and a reconciliation of
    those financial statements to the mining royalty return; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} signed by the operator of the mine and include a
    statement under oath or solemn affirmation by the operator or, if
    the operator is a corporation, by an officer of the corporation,
    that the financial statements are to that person's knowledge and
    belief complete and correct.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty return --- election under
  subsection 69(7)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a mine's operator makes an election under
  subsection 69(7),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator must deliver the initial mining
    royalty return for the fiscal year using the market value of the
    inventories of minerals or processed minerals and then deliver an
    amended mining royalty return once those inventories have been sold;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator must deliver a mining royalty return
    for the mine's subsequent fiscal years if there are any amounts that
    qualify for determining the values of D to H in subsection 69(4), or
    if the mine recommences production.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty returns for certain joint
ventures

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  mine is operated as a joint venture and each member of the joint
  venture takes its share of the output of the mine in kind and sells
  that share separately and independently from other members of the
  joint venture to purchasers who are not related to any of the members
  of the joint venture,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each member may deliver to the Chief a separate
    mining royalty return for the royalty payable under subsection 69(1)
    on the value of its share of the output of the mine, in lieu of
    including that information in a mining royalty return delivered
    under subsection 73(1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each member, and any person related to that member,
    is liable to pay only those royalties attributable to that member's
    share of the output of the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint venture respecting single mine

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When, under subsection (1), more than one member of a
  joint venture delivers a mining royalty return to the Chief for a
  single mine,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each member of the joint venture is considered to
    be a separate operator for the purposes of these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each member must indicate on the mining royalty
    return the percentage of the output of the mine represented by that
    mining royalty return;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the value of the output on the mining royalty
    return for each member must be calculated in accordance with section
    69 using

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of costs eligible for deductions under
      paragraphs 70(1)(a) to (f),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of costs that have been jointly
        incurred, equal to the percentage of the output of the mine
        received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the costs that have been incurred by that
        member alone,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in determining a deduction for exploration costs
      under paragraph 70(1)(g), the exploration costs incurred by that
      member alone,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a depreciation allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the depreciable assets of the
        mine that are jointly held, equal to the percentage of the
        output of the mine received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the depreciable assets of the mine that are
        held by the member alone,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a development allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the costs referred to in
        subparagraphs 70(1)(i)(i) to (v) that have been jointly
        incurred, equal to the percentage of the output of the mine
        received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the costs referred to in subparagraphs
        70(1)(i)(i) to (v) that have been incurred by that member alone,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a mining reclamation trust contribution allowance
      equal to the amount contributed to a mining reclamation trust in
      respect of lands to which these Regulations apply by that member,
      and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a processing allowance based on

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a percentage of the processing assets of the
        mine that are jointly held equal to the percentage of the output
        of the mine received by that member, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the processing assets of the mine that are held
        by that member alone;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amounts in column 1 of the table to subsection
    69(1) must be adjusted by multiplying each amount by a percentage
    equal to the percentage of the output of the mine received by that
    member; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} each mining royalty return must be based on the
    same fiscal year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of assessment of royalties

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  six years after the end of a particular fiscal year of a mine, the
  Chief must send to the operator of the mine a notice of assessment for
  the amount of the royalty payable for that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of reassessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, during or after the period referred to in
  subsection (1), there are reasonable grounds to believe that the
  operator of a mine or any other person who delivers a mining royalty
  return has made a fraudulent or negligent misrepresentation in
  completing the mining royalty return or in supplying any other
  information under section 73 or 74, the Chief may send a notice of
  reassessment for the amount of the royalty payable for a fiscal year
  in respect of the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When royalties are considered payable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Chief sends an operator a notice of assessment
  or reassessment for the amount of royalty payable for a fiscal year,
  the amount of royalty assessed or reassessed for the fiscal year is
  considered to be payable on the last day of the fourth month after the
  end of that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Royalty returns when ownership of mine
  changes

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the ownership of a mine changes, the operator may
  file a separate mining royalty return for the portion of the fiscal
  year before the change of ownership and the portion of the fiscal year
  after the change of ownership, and each such portion is considered to
  be a fiscal year of less than 12 months for the purposes of subsection
  70(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  operator of a mine must keep at an office in Canada and make available
  to the Chief, to substantiate information required on mining royalty
  returns,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} records, books of account and other documents
    evidencing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the weight of all minerals extracted from the
      mine and of all minerals or processed minerals processed at the
      mine, whether or not they are produced from the mine,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the weight and value of all minerals or
      processed minerals produced from the mine, sold, transferred or
      removed from the mine by the mine's operator,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any amounts received from a processing plant
      and any other amounts received from the sale of minerals or
      processed minerals, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the costs, payments, allowances and other
      deductions referred to in section 70;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the financial statements of the mine and the
    operator;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reconciliation between the documents referred to
    in paragraphs (a) and (b) and the mining royalty return;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the financial statements of an owner or the
    operator of the mine are audited by an external auditor,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the audited financial statements and the
      accompanying signed audit opinion of the external auditor, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any working papers and documentation prepared by
      the external auditor that are in the possession of an owner or the
      operator;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any documents filed by an owner or the operator
    with a stock exchange or securities commission;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any documents related to any internal audits of a
    company that is an owner or the operator; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other documents that contain information
    necessary for ascertaining the amount of royalty payable under
    section 69.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-disclosure of confidential
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to disclose information of a
  confidential nature acquired for the purposes of sections 69 to 77,
  except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the extent necessary to determine the amount of
    royalties payable under section 69;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when required under a land claims agreement
    referred to in section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
    1982](/eng/Const/); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under an agreement entered into by the Minister for
    the purpose of the administration of section 69 with the government
    of a country, province or state, or with an Aboriginal organization
    owning mineral rights, under which the officers of that government
    or Aboriginal organization are provided with the information and the
    Chief is provided with information from the government or Aboriginal
    organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on removal of minerals

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), it is
  prohibited to remove minerals or processed minerals produced from a
  mine, other than for the purposes of assay and testing to determine
  the existence, location, extent, quality or economic potential of a
  mineral deposit in the lands constituting the mining property, until
  the weight and any other information necessary to establish the value
  of those minerals or processed minerals has been ascertained and
  entered in the books of account referred to in subsection 76(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on removal of precious stones

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Precious stones must not be removed from a mine ---
  other than in a bulk sample or in a concentrate for the purposes of
  establishing the grade and the value of the stones in a mineral
  deposit --- or cut, polished, sold or transferred, until they have
  been valued by a mining royalty valuer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities required for valuation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a mine must provide, in the Northwest
  Territories, any facilities and equipment, other than computer
  equipment, necessary for a mining royalty valuer to value any precious
  stones produced from the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities considered part of mine

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, facilities
  referred to in subsection (3) are considered to be part of the mine
  and any transfer of the precious stones from one part of the mine to
  another is not considered to be a removal of the stones from the mine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cleaning of precious stones

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Precious stones must not be presented to the mining
  royalty valuer until the operator of the mine has cleaned the stones
  so as to remove all substances from the stones that are not part of
  them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of precious stones to
  royalty valuer

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} As soon as any precious stones have been processed
  into a saleable form, they must be presented to a mining royalty
  valuer for valuation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate valuation of precious stones

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} An operator who produces precious stones and sells or
  transfers them to persons who are related to the operator must present
  to a mining royalty valuer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all stones that are to be sold or transferred to a
    person related to the operator, for separate valuation before their
    sale or transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all stones that are to be cut or polished by the
    operator or any related party, for separate valuation before their
    being cut or polished.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of diamonds to royalty
  valuer

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (6) and (7), unless
  otherwise agreed on by the operator and the mining royalty valuer, an
  operator must present to the mining royalty valuer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight of 10.8 carats or more,
    separately, together with the weight of each diamond;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight from 2.8 carats to 10.79
    carats, in lots separated according to weight in carats, together
    with the number of diamonds per lot;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight from 0.66 carats to 2.79
    carats, in lots separated according to weight in grainers, from
    which randomly selected samples, accurately representing the
    composition of each lot, have been separated; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} diamonds with a weight of less than 0.66 carats, in
    lots separated according to industry standard DTC sieve sizes, from
    which randomly selected samples, accurately representing the
    composition of each lot, have been separated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Estimate of market value of diamonds

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} Before diamonds are presented to the mining royalty
  valuer under subsection (8), the operator must provide an estimate of
  the market value of each diamond or lot, as the case may be, to the
  Chief.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812516 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recorded claim and lease subject to
public works

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** Every recorded claim,
whether leased or not, is subject to the right of the Crown and of the
Commissioner of the Northwest Territories to construct and maintain
roads or other public works on or over the lands covered by the claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- death or incompetency of
claim holder

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** If the holder of a
recorded claim for which no lease has been issued dies or is declared by
a court of competent jurisdiction to be incapable of managing their
affairs and notice of the death or declaration is filed with the Mining
Recorder within 180 days after the date of the death or declaration, and
if the recording of the claim was not cancelled before the filing of the
notice, the running of a time period within which anything is required
to be done by a claim holder with respect to that claim under these
Regulations is suspended until the anniversary date of the recording of
the claim that is at least 12 months after the day on which the notice
was filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension on account of strike

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** If, as a result of a
strike within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the [Federal Public
Sector Labour Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3), a holder of a
prospecting permit, recorded claim or lease is unable, through no fault
on their part, to do a thing within the time required by these
Regulations, the deadline for doing that thing is extended for a period
ending 15 days after the last day of the strike.

## [Review by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-812548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for ministerial review

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person with a legal or beneficial interest in the subject matter of a
  decision made or an action taken or omitted to be taken under these
  Regulations may request that the Minister review any issue the person
  has with respect to the decision, action or omission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for review must be made in writing within
  30 days after the day on which the decision is made or the action is
  taken or, in the case of an omission to take action, within 30 days
  after the day on which action should have been taken and must specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the requester and their contact
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issue the requester wishes the Minister to
    review;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the decision, act or omission
    took place; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the corrective relief requested.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of or error in information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A review may be undertaken despite a failure to
  specify, or an error in specifying, any information required under
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure for review

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} After receipt of a request for review, the Minister
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the requester and all persons with an
    interest in the issue a reasonable opportunity to be heard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} review all information received respecting the
    issue; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} decide the corrective relief, if any, to be taken
    respecting the issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may request the requester or any other
  person to provide any document or other information that may be
  relevant to the review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision and reasons

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A written statement of the Minister's decision and
  the reasons for it must be sent to the requester and all persons with
  an interest in the issue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final decision

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision under this section is not to
  be the subject of a request for review.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition respecting staking

**[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** If a request for review
is with respect to the cancellation of the recording of a claim under
subsection 53(3) or paragraph 53(4)(b) or the amendment of the
boundaries of a claim under paragraph 53(4)(b), it is prohibited to
stake a claim on the lands covered by the claim the recording of which
was cancelled or the boundaries of which were amended. The prohibition
is in effect from the start of the review until noon on the day
following the first business day after the day on which the Minister's
decision was sent in accordance with subsection 84(6).

  least one year".

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  **1** The following definitions apply in this Schedule.

  [[identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a set of alphabetic,
      numeric or alphanumeric characters used to uniquely identify a
      sample, a prospecting permit zone or an electronic storage
      medium. ([identificateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[map]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[map]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a plan, graphical image or
      three-dimensional model in electronic or paper
      form. ([carte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[preparation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[preparation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes drying, fractioning,
      sieving and panning. ([préparation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a sample fraction,
      processed sample, analyzed sample, duplicate sample, blank sample,
      quality control sample and each of multiple samples from one
      location. ([échantillon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[section]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, except when used to refer to a
      legislative provision, includes a cross-section, inclined section
      and longitudinal section in electronic or paper
      form. ([coupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#part-1general-requirements .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paper or electronic format

  - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A report must be submitted in paper format,
    in electronic format or in a combination of paper and electronic
    formats.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report submitted in electronic format

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The electronic format in which any part of a report
    --- other than the raw data specified in paragraph 4(s) --- is
    submitted must be such that the report can be read using the Mining
    Recorder's computer systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identifying information

  - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following identifying information must be
    provided:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the types of work being reported
      on;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the permittee or of the holder of the
      recorded claim, as the case may be;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of the prospecting permits or recorded
      claims in respect of which the work was done, and setting out for
      each one

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if a prospecting permit, its identification
        number, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a claim, its identification tag number and
        the name associated with it, if any;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of the National Topographic System of
      Canada 1:50 000 map sheet that shows the area covered by the
      permit or claim;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the four values representing either

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum and minimum latitude and longitude
        of the area covered by the permit or claim, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maximum and minimum Northings and Eastings
        UTM coordinates of the area covered by the permit or claim;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name of the individual who prepared and
      signed the report;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each period that work was done in the field,
      the first date and last date that work in the field was done;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a report submitted in paper format in more
      than one volume, the volume number and total number of volumes in
      the report; and

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} for a report submitted on more than one
      electronic medium, the number of media submitted and the
      identifiers for each.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where information is provided

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsection (1) must
    appear

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the parts of a report submitted in paper
      format, on the front cover of each volume of the report; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the parts of a report submitted in electronic
      format,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a label affixed to, or on a sheet of paper
        inserted into, each container submitted, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a file named "Readme - Lisez-moi" stored on
        each electronic medium submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Details of contents

  **4** A report must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table of contents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the electronic media submitted and their
    identifiers;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of the tables, charts, diagrams, maps,
    sections and images presented in the report and the location of each
    in the report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of the permits or claims in respect of which
    the work was done, setting out for each one

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if a prospecting permit, its identification
      number and date of issue,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a claim, its identification tag number,
      recording date and the name, if any, associated with it,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the area in hectares of the lands covered,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of the National Topographic System of
      Canada 1:50 000 map sheet in which it is located, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name of the permittee or holder of the
      recorded claim;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a bibliography setting out the sources and
    references used in the report;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an alphabetized list of definitions for all
    abbreviations used in the report;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the regional geology of the
    area in which the work was done;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the purpose of the work being
    reported on;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the previous work that was done in the
    lands covered by the prospecting permit or claim, and in the lands
    adjacent to those lands, that is relevant to the purpose of the work
    being reported on;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a summary and a detailed description of the work
    being reported on;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if sampling was performed, a description of the
    sampling methodology and a statement of the number of samples
    analyzed;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} identification of quality control samples submitted
    to laboratories, along with the elemental concentration of each
    sample;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of preparation,
    processing and analysis applied to samples and the calculations used
    to arrive at the results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} specification of the cutoff grades used for the
    results of geochemical analyses shown on maps and sections;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} a description of any geophysical or remote sensing
    anomalies and any anomalous results of geochemical analysis, with an
    explanation of the criteria used to define them;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} interpretation of the data collected and of
    observations made during the work;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} conclusions and recommendations resulting from the
    interpretation of the data and of observations made during the work;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} if geochemical analyses were performed, copies of
    all resulting analytical certificates and laboratory reports;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} an electronic copy of the raw data that was
    acquired in electronic format during the work; and

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} if environmental baseline studies were conducted,
    an appendix that sets out the results of the studies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maps or sections

  **5** A report must include the following maps or sections:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a regional geological map that is marked to
    indicate the area in which the work was done and that is published
    by the Geological Survey of Canada, the Northwest Territories
    Geoscience Office, the Canada-Nunavut Geoscience Office or other
    source of geoscientific publications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a map that shows the area in which the work was
    done, the regional infrastructure (including roads, airfields, ports
    and mine sites) and, if the scale of the map permits, the
    communities and boundaries of Northwest Territories;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps on which the following elements
    are indicated:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the recorded claim or
      prospecting permit zone where the work was done and,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the type of work being reported on,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the location of the work with respect to the
        boundaries of the recorded claim or prospecting permit zone
        where the work was done,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the identification number of each prospecting
        permit, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the identification tag number of each claim
        and, if available, the name of each claim or group of claims,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the location and type of the previous work that
      was done in the lands covered by the prospecting permit or claim,
      and the lands adjacent to those lands, that is relevant to the
      purpose of the work being reported on,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all surface and underground workings that are
      not part of the work being reported on,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of each prospecting permit zone
      or recorded claim, whether leased or not, that is adjacent to the
      boundaries of the prospecting permit zone or recorded claim on
      which the work was done, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the infrastructure, hydrography and natural
      landmarks within the boundaries referred to in subparagraphs (i)
      and (iv);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps or sections with a symbol
    indicating every site at which samples or data were collected, and a
    sample identifier for each sample; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} one or more maps or sections indicating any
    geochemical or geophysical anomalies.

#### [Geographic Location and Coordinates]{.HTitleText2} {#geographic-location-and-coordinates .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample sites --- coordinate systems

  - **6** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All coordinates used in a report to locate
    the sites where samples or data were collected --- including those
    in sections, maps, tables and lists --- must be geographic
    coordinates or UTM coordinates.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinate system specified on map

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} The geodetic reference system --- and, for UTM
    coordinates, the zone number --- must be indicated for each map,
    section and table in which that coordinate system is used.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geodetic coordinates

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} The geodetic reference system must be one of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} North American Datum 1927 (NAD 27);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} North American Datum 1983 (NAD 83);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian Spatial Reference System (NAD 83
      (CSRS));

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} World Geodetic System 1984 (WGS 84).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method for determination of location

  **7** For each location where work was done, a report must specify how
  the location was determined for each type of work, using one or more
  of the following methods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a satellite positioning system, indicating the
    geodetic reference system used and, if UTM coordinates are used, the
    zone number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a map, indicating the scale, the geodetic reference
    system used and, if UTM coordinates are used, the zone number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} surveying, together with a description of the
    surveying equipment and method used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevation coordinates

  **8** If elevation coordinates are used with respect to the work,
  those coordinates and their zero reference level must be specified in
  the report.

#### [Maps, Sections and Samples]{.HTitleText2} {#maps-sections-and-samples .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinates for geographic locations

  - **9** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section submitted with a report
    must have the location it represents indicated on it in geographical

    specification of those coordinates that is sufficient to allow the
    location of any point on the map.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements respecting grid lines

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If grid lines were used to perform the work, at

    identify each of the grid lines.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for maps and sections

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section must

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be legible;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have its scale set out on it using a bar scale in
      metric units;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a geographic north indicator;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a legend; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} indicate the units of measure used on it.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevation coordinates

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each map and section must show the relevant
    elevation coordinates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of data collection sites

  **10** Every table or list that contains data with respect to a site
  (including sample data) must set out the location of the data
  collection site specified in geographic or UTM coordinates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cross-referencing of sample identifiers

  - **11** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an identifier used in a report to
    identify a sample, such as in an analytical certificate, is not the
    same as the corresponding sample identifier shown on the sample
    location maps or sections required under paragraph 5(1)(d), a table
    that makes a cross-reference between the two identifiers must be
    provided.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample identifiers

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} Data about samples must set out one of the
    following identifiers for each sample:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sample identifier shown on a map or section
      as required under paragraph 5(1)(d);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identifier cross-referenced, in accordance
      with subsection (1), to the sample identifier mentioned in
      paragraph (a).

#### [Information About Types of Work]{.HTitleText2} {#information-about-types-of-work .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excavation

  **12** Reporting on excavation work must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maps and sections showing the location, dimensions
    and orientation of each of the workings, including stripped areas,
    trenches, open pits, shafts, adits, drifts and ramps;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maps or sections showing any anomalous results of
    geochemical analyses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the excavation methods and the equipment used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the samples or groups of samples collected from
      each of the workings,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the geological observations and any
      geotechnical observations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the results of any geophysical surveys, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any log made respecting excavation of
    overburden or extraction of bedrock.

  **13** Reporting on drilling work must include the following:

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the overburden thickness,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the geological units intersected,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the locations where samples were taken, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} all anomalous results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the drilling methods and the equipment used,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the samples and their down-hole intervals,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the geological observations and any
      geotechnical observations,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the results of any geophysical surveys, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} drill logs setting out for each drill hole

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the hole number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the hole or core diameter,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of drilling, the date logged, the
      geographic or UTM coordinates of the collar, initial azimuth and
      inclination, final hole length and the results of any down-hole
      tests, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the overburden thickness.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geological mapping

  **14** Reporting on geological mapping work must include the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a table setting out the geological formations of
    the area mapped, if the information on those formations is published
    by the Geological Survey of Canada, the Northwest Territories
    Geoscience Office, the Canada-Nunavut Geoscience Office or other
    source of geoscientific publications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maps or sections showing geological units,
    structural data, areas of mineralization, locations of rock outcrops
    and mineralized erratic blocks, anomalous results of geochemical
    analyses, locations of any trenches, stripped areas, drill holes and
    other workings referred to in the report, and, if applicable,
    indicators of the direction of ice movement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the features of geological
    interest, such as overburden, rock types, structures, veins, areas
    of mineralization, mineralized erratic blocks, altered zones,
    anomalous results of geochemical analyses, and, if applicable,
    indicators of the direction of ice movement; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the results of geoscientific investigations,
    including the results of any petrographic studies, and any
    interpretation that has been made of the glacial history based on
    the geological mapping work.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling and geochemistry

  **15** Reporting on sampling and geochemical analysis must include the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complete results of geochemical analyses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a geological description of the samples or group of
    samples;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the sampling methodologies,
    including any pattern of sample collection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the dates the samples or group of samples were
    collected and the dates that any preparation or processing of the
    samples was done in the field or in a laboratory;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of any preparation or processing of
    samples or group of samples that was done in the field or in a
    laboratory;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of geochemical
    analysis used on each sample or group of samples, including a list
    of the elements and compounds for which the analysis was done and
    the limits for their detection;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of any statistical methods used and
    the results obtained;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the results of the geochemistry survey, represented
    on maps or sections; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if any geoscientific investigation of surficial
    deposits was performed, an interpretation of the sources from which
    the indicator minerals came, a description of the methods used to
    determine the direction of movement of ice, water or other transport
    medium for those minerals and any interpretation of the glacial
    history revealed by the investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geophysics or remote sensing work

  - **16** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Reporting on geophysical analysis or remote
    sensing work must be accompanied by the following information and
    documents:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of survey performed;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dates that the survey started and ended;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the work was done using a grid, the distance
      between the lines, the azimuth and the length of the lines
      surveyed and the total distance surveyed;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each report respecting geophysics or
      remote sensing made by a contractor, together with all maps and
      sections produced and data collected in connection with the
      report;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the survey methods, including

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the equipment used,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics of the instruments and
        sensors used, including their precision,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the positioning system used to record the
        location of data collection points, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of any methods used to make
        corrections to the data;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods of analysis applied
      to the data;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a database or spreadsheet containing the
      geographic coordinates of each ground data collection site, the
      raw measurements, the measurements used to correct the raw
      measurements, the corrected measurements and calculated results
      used for interpretations; and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the results of the geophysical or remote sensing
      survey, represented on maps, sections, pseudo-sections or
      profiles.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Down-hole geophysical survey

    [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a down-hole geophysical survey was performed,
    the section required under paragraph (1)(h) must show

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hole number;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date the hole was drilled;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location of the drill hole collar and trace;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the geographic or UTM collar coordinates;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the initial azimuth and inclination of the hole;
      and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the length of the hole.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electromagnetic survey

    [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an electromagnetic survey was performed, the map
    required under paragraph (1)(h) must show the projection of the
    surveyed drill hole to the surface and the location and
    configuration of the transmitter and receiver.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geophysical survey grids

    [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a ground-based, waterborne or airborne
    geophysical survey was performed, it must be presented in the report
    accompanied by a map showing the surveyed lines.

  - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Underground geophysical survey

    [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an underground geophysical survey was performed,
    it must be presented in the report accompanied by a map showing the
    location of the geophysical survey, the surveyed lines and the
    location, dimensions and orientation of the underground workings.

### [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Simplified Report]{.HTitleText1} {#part-2simplified-report .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simplified report

  **17** A simplified report provided for under subsection 15(2) or
  41(1) of these Regulations must be prepared in accordance with
  sections 2 to 11 of this Schedule, but without the requirements set
  out in paragraphs 4(g), (m), (o), (p) and (q) and 5(1)(a) and (e), and
  must contain the following information and documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each sample or group of samples collected,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} methods of preparation, processing and analysis
      applied to samples,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the excavation methods and the equipment used
      for the excavation,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the field observations, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the results of the work performed and of
      geochemical analyses;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maps or sections showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area investigated and the traverses
      performed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the locations of rock outcrops investigated,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the locations of the sampling sites, including
      the locations of any erratic blocks that were sampled,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the locations of any stripped areas and
      trenches, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} features of interest such as significant results
      of geochemical analyses; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} comments respecting follow-up work for the purpose
    of assessing the mineral potential of the area investigated.
