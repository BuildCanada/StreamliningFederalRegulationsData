and Import of Rough Diamonds Act](/eng/acts/E-18.5)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699844 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import of Rough
    Diamonds Act](/eng/acts/E-18.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Harmonized System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Harmonized System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Harmonized
    Commodity Description and Coding System set out in the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([Système harmonisé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[shipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more containers of
    rough diamonds that are transported together for the purpose of
    export or import. ([chargement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application for a Canadian Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699849 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
Canadian Certificate for the export of rough diamonds under section 9 of
the Act must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name and address in Canada and, if
  the applicant is a corporation, the name of the individual submitting
  the application on its behalf;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is not the exporter of the rough
  diamonds, the name and address of the exporter;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person to whom the
  exporter is exporting the rough diamonds;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the participant to which the rough
  diamonds are to be shipped;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the origin of the rough diamonds, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they were mined in Canada, the place and name of
    the mine,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they were recovered during exploration in
    Canada, the longitude and latitude of the exploration site, and the
    place and name of the facility in which they were extracted,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if they were extracted in Canada from mineral
    concentrates that originated outside Canada, the place and name of
    the facility in which they were extracted,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if they were present in Canada at the coming into
    force of section 8 of the Act, documentary evidence establishing
    their presence in Canada at that time, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if they were imported into Canada after the coming
    into force of section 8 of the Act, the serial number of each
    Kimberley Process Certificate that accompanied the rough diamonds;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the mass, measured in carats, of the rough diamonds
  in the shipment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the value, in United States dollars, of the rough
  diamonds in the shipment;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the subheading in the List of Tariff Provisions in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) under which
  the rough diamonds are classified;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of containers in the shipment and a seal
  number for each container; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a declaration signed and dated by the applicant
  stating that the information contained in the application is accurate.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
replacement of a Canadian Certificate for the export of rough diamonds
under section 11 of the Act must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made before the export of the rough diamonds;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain all the information required under section 2
  in respect of any information appearing on the Canadian Certificate
  that is inaccurate or has changed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by the Canadian Certificate in respect
  of which the application is made.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application to the
Minister under section 9 or 11 of the Act must be delivered by hand or
sent by mail or courier or by facsimile or other electronic means.

## [Canadian Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699871 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Canadian Certificate for the export of rough diamonds issued by the
  Minister under section 9 or 11 of the Act must contain the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the exporter and the person
    to whom the exporter is exporting the rough diamonds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the participant to which the rough
    diamonds are to be shipped;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the mass of the rough diamonds, measured in carats,
    for the shipment;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the value of the rough diamonds, in United States
    dollars, for the shipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Harmonized System code for the rough diamonds;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of containers in the shipment and a seal
    number for each container;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of issue and date of expiry; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the name of the participant in which the rough
    diamonds originated, if all diamonds in the shipment originated in a
    single participant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each Canadian Certificate must bear

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title "Kimberley Process
    Certificate/[certificat du Processus de Kimberley]{.otherLang
    lang="fr"}" and the statement "The rough diamonds in this shipment
    have been handled in accordance with the provisions of the Kimberley
    Process international certification scheme for rough diamonds./[Les
    diamants bruts contenus dans ce chargement ont été traités
    conformement aux dispositions du système international de délivrance
    de certificats pour les diamants bruts du Processus de
    Kimberley.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a unique serial number; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the Minister or a person
    authorized under section 6 of the Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 12 of
the Act, a Canadian Certificate is valid until 24:00 Greenwich mean time
of the day that is 60 days after the date of issue.

## [Export and Import Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699889 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An exporter reports an
export of rough diamonds under subsection 13(1) of the Act by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} completing a report, in the form fixed by the
  Minister, in which the exporter provides

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the exporter and the person to whom the
    exporter has exported the rough diamonds,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the serial number of the Canadian Certificate, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of export of the rough diamonds;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} attesting to the accuracy of the information provided
  under paragraph (a), and the information contained in the Canadian
  Certificate on export, by signing the report; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} presenting the completed report to a customs officer
  on export and then sending it to the Minister by mail or courier, or
  delivering it by hand, within seven days after export.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An importer reports the
import of rough diamonds under subsection 16(1) of the Act by presenting
the accompanying Kimberley Process Certificate to a customs officer on
import and then sending it to the Minister by mail or courier, or
delivering it by hand, within seven days after import.

## [Containers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699898 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  container to be used for the export or import of rough diamonds must
  be so constructed that the container, when sealed, cannot be opened
  without showing evidence of having been opened.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A container in which rough diamonds are exported must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be sealed with a seal that bears a seal number
    listed on the accompanying Canadian Certificate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear the serial number of the accompanying Canadian
    Certificate.

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699904 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person who exports or
imports rough diamonds must keep, for a period of three years, the
following records in respect of the rough diamonds:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the serial number of the Canadian Certificate or
  Kimberley Process Certificate that accompanied the rough diamonds;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the rough diamonds were mined in Canada by the
  exporter,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the daily total mill feed for the calendar year in
    which the rough diamonds were mined,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the daily total rough diamond recovery for the
    calendar year in which the rough diamonds were mined, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a record of any change in their mass due to
    handling for the calendar year in which the diamonds were mined;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the rough diamonds were extracted in Canada by the
  exporter from mineral concentrates, a record of the mass of rough
  diamonds extracted from the concentrates;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the rough diamonds were purchased or otherwise
  obtained by the exporter or importer, a copy of the purchase order,
  invoice or similar document; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the rough diamonds are polished and facetted by
  the importer after import into Canada, a record of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the loss of diamond material during the polishing
    and facetting,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the mass of the recovered polished diamonds, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the mass of the remaining rough diamonds.

## [Disposition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699917 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is satisfied that a rough diamond forfeited under the Act
  will not re-enter the international rough diamond trade, the Minister
  must dispose of it in accordance with the [Surplus Crown Assets
  Act](/eng/acts/S-27).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not dispose of a rough diamond
  under subsection (1), the Minister must destroy the rough diamond.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must dispose of things, other than rough
  diamonds, that are forfeited under the Act in accordance with the
  [Surplus Crown Assets Act](/eng/acts/S-27).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On disposition of a rough diamond or other thing
  forfeited under the Act, the Minister shall cause notice of the date
  and manner of disposition to be published in Part I of the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
