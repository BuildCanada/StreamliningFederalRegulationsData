## Additional Legislative Powers Designation Order {#additional-legislative-powers-designation-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1235
:::

[NORTHWEST TERRITORIES ACT](/eng/acts/N-27)

Order Designating Additional Legislative Powers Vested in the
Commissioner in Council
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499737 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Additional Legislative Powers Designation
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1235).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499739 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The making of grants and
loans to individuals and the making of loans to persons are hereby
designated as matters in relation to which the Commissioner in Council
of the Northwest Territories may make Ordinances.
