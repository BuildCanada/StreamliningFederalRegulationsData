## Decision Body Time Periods and Consultation Regulations {#decision-body-time-periods-and-consultation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-380
:::

[YUKON ENVIRONMENTAL AND SOCIO-ECONOMIC ASSESSMENT ACT](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)

Registration 2005-11-28

Decision Body Time Periods and Consultation Regulations

P.C. 2005-2200 2005-11-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development has
consulted with the member of the Executive Council of Yukon designated
by the Commissioner of Yukon as the territorial minister for the
purposes of the [Yukon Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EECBA}, with the
Gwich'in Tribal Council and with the Yukon First Nations, within the
meaning of the Umbrella Final Agreement as defined in section 2 of the
[Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EBCBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EECBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  7
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EBCBA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 1994, c.
  34
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to paragraphs 122(d) and (e) of the [Yukon
Environmental and Socio-economic Assessment
Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Decision Body Time Periods and Consultation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-380).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721808 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Yukon Environmental and
    Socio-economic Assessment Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new recommendation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new recommendation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a recommendation
    made or deemed to be made to a decision body under subsection 77(2)
    of the Act. ([nouvelle recommandation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recommendation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recommendation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a recommendation made
    under

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 56(1)(a), (b) or (c) of the Act by a
      designated office at the conclusion of an evaluation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 58(1)(a), (b) or (c) of the Act by the
      executive committee at the conclusion of a screening;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subsection 72(4) of the Act by a panel of the
      Board or a joint panel at the conclusion of a review; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 73 of the Act in the report of a review
      panel. ([recommandation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[review panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[review panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a review panel referred
    to in section 63 of the Act. ([commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Recommendations of a Designated Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721818 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 75(1) of the Act, the period for issuing a decision document
in respect of a recommendation made to a decision body by a designated
office is 30 days after the date of the report that includes the
recommendation.

## [Recommendations of a Joint Panel or Review Panel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721820 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 75(1) of the Act, the period for issuing a decision document
in respect of a recommendation made to a decision body by a joint panel
or a review panel is 90 days after the date of the report that includes
the recommendation.

## [Recommendations of the Executive Committee or a Panel of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721822 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 76(1)(a) of the Act, the period for issuing a
  decision document accepting a recommendation made to a decision body
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the date of the report that includes
    the recommendation, if the recommendation is made by the executive
    committee; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90 days after the date of the report that includes
    the recommendation, if the recommendation is made by a panel of the
    Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 76(1)(b) of the Act, the
  period for referring a recommendation made to a decision body back for
  reconsideration to the executive committee or a panel of the Board is
  45 days after the date of the report that includes the recommendation.

## [New Recommendations of the Executive Committee or a Panel of the Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721828 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 77(3) of the Act, the period for issuing a decision document
in respect of a new recommendation made or deemed to have been made to a
decision body is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a new recommendation made or deemed to
  have been made by the executive committee, 30 days after

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report that includes the new
    recommendation, if the new recommendation is made rather than deemed
    to have been made, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the period prescribed by rules
    made for the purpose of subsection 77(2) of the Act, if the new
    recommendation is deemed to have been made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a new recommendation made or deemed to
  have been made by a panel of the Board, 60 days after

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the report that includes the new
    recommendation, if the new recommendation is made rather than deemed
    to been have made, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the last day of the period prescribed by rules
    made for the purpose of subsection 77(2) of the Act, if the new
    recommendation is deemed to have been made.

## [Extended Period if Consultation with First Nations Is Required]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721836 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If a decision body
considering a recommendation is required under subsection 74(2) of the
Act to consult a first nation, the period prescribed under a provision
of these Regulations for the decision body to issue the decision
document in respect of the recommendation is extended as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed period of 30 days or 45 days is extended
  by 7 days;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed period of 60 days is extended by 21
  days; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a prescribed period of 90 days is extended by 30
  days.

## [Consultation Among Decision Bodies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721841 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If there are two or more
decision bodies for a project, the decision bodies must cooperate with
each other to collectively determine the manner of consultation with one
another under subsection 78(1) of the Act in respect of the project,
including the determination of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time schedules for the consultations, having
  regard to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the period within which decision documents must be
    issued,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the complexity of the project,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the location of the project, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any other considerations that are relevant to the
    applicable recommendation or new recommendation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which first nations are to be consulted
  by the decision bodies, if consultation with first nations is required
  under subsection 74(2) of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other matters of a procedural nature that are
  relevant to consultation under subsection 78(1) of the Act.

<!-- -->

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If more
  than one federal decision body is required to issue a decision
  document in respect of a recommendation in respect of a project, the
  federal decision bodies must select from among themselves one federal
  decision body to be the lead federal decision body for the project.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The lead federal decision body must, within seven
  days after the date of the report that includes the recommendation,
  provide written notice of its selection to any decision bodies for the
  project that are not federal decision bodies.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The lead federal decision body must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the efforts of the federal decision
    bodies to make the determinations required under section 7;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the consultations between the federal
    decision bodies under subsection 78(1) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} coordinate the consultation between the federal
    decision bodies and any other decision bodies under subsection 78(1)
    of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} represent the federal decision bodies in
    discussions with the other decision bodies in making the
    determinations required under section 7.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the federal decision bodies cannot come to an
  agreement on any determination required under section 7, the lead
  federal decision body must, on behalf of the federal decision bodies,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there are no decision bodies referred to in
    paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition [[decision
    body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act in relation
    to the project, determine the matter; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there are one or more decision bodies referred
    to in paragraph (a) or (b) of the definition [[decision
    body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act in relation
    to the project, determine the position of the federal government to
    propose to those other decision bodies with respect to the
    determination.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721861 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
