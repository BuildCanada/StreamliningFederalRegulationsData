[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, on that person's
    own behalf or as an agent or mandatary for or on behalf of any other
    person, is engaged in the marketing of potatoes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, except in Prince Edward Island;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the United States; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Puerto Rico. ([détaillant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, on that
    person's own behalf or as an agent or mandatary for or on behalf of
    any other person, is engaged in the marketing of potatoes outside of
    Canada, the United States and Puerto
    Rico. ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of potatoes, includes
    buying, selling, promoting, offering for sale, shipping for sale or
    storage, packing, grading, storing, processing and researching as
    well as the production and transportation of potatoes in any manner
    by any person. ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2017-238, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means canning, dehydrating,
    chipping, drying or freezing potatoes, peeling or processing
    potatoes with any chemical or by heat or combining or mixing
    potatoes with one or more other
    vegetables. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, on that
    person's own behalf or as an agent or mandatary for or on behalf of
    any other person, purchases or ships potatoes for processing outside
    of Prince Edward Island. ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Levies --- processor

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  processor in Prince Edward Island must pay to the Commodity Board
  levies at the rate of \$0.09 per 100 lb. on all potatoes that they
  have purchased or shipped for processing outside of that province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Levies --- dealer and exporter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every dealer and exporter in Prince Edward Island
  must pay to the Commodity Board levies at the rate of \$0.13 per
  100 lb. on all potatoes that are sold or shipped in interprovincial or
  export trade by that dealer or exporter, except on potatoes that are
  referred to in subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance of levies --- processor

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  processor referred to in subsection 2(1) must, on or before the 15th
  day of each month,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remit to the Commodity Board the levies imposed on
    that processor under that subsection; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} render to the Commodity Board a statement relating
    to all potatoes that are purchased or shipped by that processor
    during the previous month that includes the name and address of the
    grower and the quantity purchased or shipped.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance of levies --- dealer and
  exporter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every dealer and exporter referred to in subsection
  2(2) must, on or before the 15th day of each month,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} remit to the Commodity Board the levies imposed on
    that dealer or exporter under that subsection; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} render to the Commodity Board a statement relating
    to all potatoes that are sold or shipped by that dealer or exporter
    during the previous month that includes the quantity sold or
    shipped, variety and type of pack of the potatoes.
