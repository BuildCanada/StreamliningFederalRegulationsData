## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817073 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Payments
    Act](/eng/acts/C-21). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[five-year corporate plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[five-year corporate plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan
    referred to in section 23 of the Act. ([plan d'entreprise
    quinquennal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Five-Year Corporate Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817078 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  five-year corporate plan for the Association must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the fiscal year for which the plan is
    submitted, its operating budget and capital expenditures budget;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each of the four fiscal years after the fiscal
    year referred to in paragraph (a), its operating budget forecast and
    capital expenditures budget forecast;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the fiscal year before the fiscal year referred
    to in paragraph (a), its audited financial statements, or, if those
    statements are not available, its updated operating budget and
    updated capital expenditures;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the fiscal year before the fiscal year for
    which information is required under paragraph (c), its audited
    financial statements;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of any major variance in the amounts
    provided under paragraphs (a) to (d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of any major variance between an
    amount provided under paragraphs (a) to (d) for a fiscal year and
    any amount provided in the previous five-year corporate plan for
    that fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must submit the five-year corporate plan to
  the Minister for approval no later than three months after the day on
  which the fiscal year for which the plan is submitted begins.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board must submit any significant amendment to
  the approved five-year corporate plan to the Minister for approval.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817092 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timelines

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The report referred to in
section 24 of the Act must be prepared --- and published on the
Association's Internet site --- no later than five months after the day
on which the fiscal year ends.
