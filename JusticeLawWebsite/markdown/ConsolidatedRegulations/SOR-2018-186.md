## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-852492 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accessory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accessory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product, including a
    change table accessory and a sleep accessory, that is designed or
    advertised to be used with a playpen and that has the following
    characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is designed to support the weight of a child;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it can be placed on or fixed to the playpen; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its primary function is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a change table accessory, to
        allow the changing of a child's diaper or the dressing of a
        child,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sleep accessory that has
        sides to confine the child, to provide sleeping accommodation
        for a child, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to provide a play area for
        a child or an area to enable caregiver-child
        interaction. ([accessoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fastener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fastener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all of the following
    items:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} items that, once installed on a playpen or
      accessory, are capable of being joined or unjoined without the use
      of any tool or equipment, such as

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} string, cords, straps, wire and other similar
        items, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} zippers, buttons, snaps and other similar
        items; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} items that join two objects together on being
      installed on a playpen or accessory, such as screws, bolts,
      hinges, nails and other similar
      items. ([attaches]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[occupant retention area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[occupant retention area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a playpen, the space that is
      bounded by the playing surface and the interior surfaces of the
      playpen's sides; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sleep accessory, the space that
      is bounded by the sleeping surface and the interior surfaces of
      the accessory's sides. ([espace de confinement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[playpen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[playpen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has the
    following characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its function is to provide a play area for a
      child who has a height of 90 cm or less;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} none of its structural components are inflatable;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has sides, at least one of which is made of a
      textile or other pliable material, that are attached to its floor;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it serves to confine the child; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} it can be used as a stand-alone product. ([parc
      pour enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[structural cover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[structural cover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structural
    component of a playpen or sleep accessory that has the following
    characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is made of a textile or other pliable
      material;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it attaches to the frame of the playpen or sleep
      accessory with a zipper, snaps, buttons or other similar
      fasteners;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it completely or partially defines the occupant
      retention area; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is removable. ([recouvrement
      structurel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplied with]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplied with]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an accessory,
    means designed to be used and sold together with a specific playpen
    or accessory that is made by the same
    manufacturer. ([fourni]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-852523 .Part}

### [Requirements for Playpens and Accessories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-852524 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxicity

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every playpen or accessory
that contains a toxic substance must comply with section 25 of the [Toys
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-17) in relation to that
substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stickers, films and surface coating
materials

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sticker, film or other similar material that can be removed, or a
  surface coating material, that is applied to an accessible part of a
  playpen or accessory must not contain, when it is tested in accordance
  with a method that conforms to good laboratory practices,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than 90 mg/kg total lead;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any compound of antimony, arsenic, cadmium,
    selenium or barium if more than 1 000 mg/kg of the compound migrates
    from the material; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 mg/kg total mercury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meanings as in section 1
  of the [Surface Coating Materials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-193).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stitching

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All stitching that forms
part of a playpen or accessory must be lock-stitching.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shearing and pinching

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A playpen or accessory must
not have any part that is accessible to the child and that may cause
injury to the child due to shearing or pinching.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- textile or other pliable
material

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any part of a playpen or
accessory that is made of a textile or other pliable material and that
is permanently affixed to a rigid structural component of the product
must not, when the playpen or accessory is tested in accordance with
Schedule 1, tear or become detached from the component.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mesh --- strength and integrity

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any mesh
  that is made of a textile or other pliable material and that forms
  part of a playpen or accessory must not, when tested in accordance
  with Schedule 2, tear or become detached.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mesh --- size of openings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The openings in mesh that is made of a textile or
  other pliable material and that forms part of a playpen or accessory
  must be of such a size that the hemispherical tip of a probe described
  in Schedule 3 is unable to pass through the openings when tested in
  accordance with that schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flammability

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any part of a playpen or
accessory that is made of a textile or other pliable material must have
a flame spread time greater than seven seconds, when tested in
accordance with the Canadian General Standards Board standard
CAN/CGSB-4.2 No. 27.5, entitled Textile Test Methods: Flame Resistance
--- 45° Angle Test --- One-Second Flame Impingement, as amended from
time to time, in either of the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it does not have a raised fibre surface; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a raised fibre surface and, when tested,
  exhibits ignition or fusion of its base fibres.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posts

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A post of a playpen or
sleep accessory must not extend more than 1.5 mm --- measured within
76 mm from the outermost surface of the post --- above the lowest point
on the upper surface of the higher of the sides that adjoin the post.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Openings --- entrapment

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** When a playpen or sleep
accessory is tested in accordance with Schedule 4, there must not be any
completely bounded opening in the surfaces that define the occupant
retention area through which a solid rectangular block that measures
60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm is capable of passing in any orientation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- wood, plastic or similar hard
material

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  exposed part of a playpen or accessory that is made of wood, plastic
  or a similar hard material must be smoothly finished to eliminate
  sharp edges and points and be free from cracks, burrs and other
  defects.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- metal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every exposed part of a playpen or accessory that is
  made of metal must be smoothly finished to eliminate sharp edges and
  points and be free from burrs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Metal tubing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every cut edge of any metal tubing that is part of a
  playpen or accessory must meet one of the following requirements if
  the cut edge is accessible to the child:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be smoothly finished to eliminate sharp
    edges and points and be free from burrs; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be protected by a cap that remains in place
    when the cap is subjected to a force of 90 N applied in any
    direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bolts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The threaded end of every bolt of a playpen or
  accessory must, if the end is accessible to the child, be covered by
  an acorn nut or a device that offers equivalent protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small parts

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every part of a playpen
or accessory that is accessible to the child and that is small enough to
be totally enclosed in the small parts cylinder illustrated in Schedule
5 must be affixed to the product so that the part does not become
detached when it is subjected to a force of 90 N applied in any
direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- openings

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every slot, notch, groove
or other opening in a wooden, plastic or metal part of a playpen or
accessory --- or in a part of one made of a similar hard material ---
that is accessible to the child must meet one of the following
requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be of such a size and shape that, if it
  admits a probe that is 5.33 mm in diameter, it will also admit a probe
  that is 9.53 mm in diameter; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must have a depth that is not greater than the
  minor span dimension across its opening, if that minor span dimension
  is greater than or equal to 5.33 mm and less than 9.53 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- length --- general

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  cord, strap or other similar item that is attached by only one of its
  ends to a playpen or sleep accessory must not measure more than 188 mm
  in length when it is stretched by the gradual application of a force
  of 22 N.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- length --- change
  table accessory

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a cord, strap or other similar item
  that is attached by only one of its ends to a change table accessory
  that is placed on or fixed to a playpen, the portion of the cord,
  strap or item that extends into the playpen's occupant retention area
  must not measure more than 188 mm in length when it is stretched by
  the gradual application of a force of 22 N.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- loops --- general

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  tested in accordance with section 1 of Schedule 6, a cord, strap or
  other similar item that is attached to a playpen or sleep accessory
  must not be capable of forming either of the following loops:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loop inside the occupant retention area that
    permits the passage of the small head probe illustrated in the
    figure to that schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loop outside the occupant retention area that
    permits the passage of that probe and that, when the loop is pulled
    into the occupant retention area, remains there.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- loops --- change
  table accessories

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule
  6, a cord, strap or other similar item that is designed to restrain a
  child in a change table accessory that is placed on or fixed to a
  playpen must not be capable of forming a loop that can be pulled into
  the occupant retention area of the playpen and that permits the
  passage of the small head probe illustrated in the figure to that
  schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pads

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A floor pad or mattress
pad that is supplied with, or that is designed or advertised to be used
with, a playpen or sleep accessory respectively must, when it is
installed according to the manufacturer's instructions, meet the
following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must not be more than 38 mm thick; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be of such a size that, when it is pushed
  firmly against any side of the playpen or sleep accessory, it does not
  leave a gap of more than 30 mm between the floor pad or mattress pad
  and any part of any other side.

### [Additional Requirements for Playpens]{.HTitleText2} {#h-852584 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stability

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** When a playpen is tested
in accordance with Schedule 7, at least three support points, each of
which is found below or as close as possible to a post and one of which
is not in a straight line with the other two, must remain in contact
with the inclined plywood sheet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Height of sides

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** When the top rails of the
sides of a playpen are placed in the manufacturer's recommended use
position and no load is applied to its playing surface, the upper
surface of the top rail of every side must be at least 508 mm higher
than the playing surface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Side deflection

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The upper surface of the
top rail of every side of a playpen must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when tested in accordance with paragraphs 1(a) to (e)
  of Schedule 8, be at least 508 mm higher than the playing surface; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when tested in accordance with paragraphs 1(f) to (k)
  of Schedule 8, be at least 460 mm higher than the playing surface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strength of sides

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The top rail of every
side of a playpen that has a hinge and the latching or locking
mechanisms of the rail must not, when the playpen is tested in
accordance with section 2 of Schedule 8, break or disengage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rigid top rails --- folding playpen

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A rigid top rail of a
folding playpen that has at least two hinges that latch or lock when the
playpen is placed in the manufacturer's recommended use position and
that permits the top rail to fold vertically downwards from that
position when unlatched or unlocked must meet the following
requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the top rail is tested in accordance with
  section 1 of Schedule 9, it must be possible to insert the probe
  described in that section into the space that is created by the
  folding of the top rail to a depth that is equal to or greater than
  the minimum distance between the opposite folded segments of the top
  rail; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if at least one of the hinges can be unlatched or
  unlocked independently from any other hinge, when the top rail is
  tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule 9, the interior angle
  that is formed by the folding of the top rail must be at least 75°,
  measured within a radius of 76 mm of the hinge that is unlatched or
  unlocked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-rigid top rails

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A non-rigid top rail of a
playpen must not, when tested in accordance with section 3 of Schedule
9, deform so that the surfaces or points that are identified on the
template that is illustrated in the figure to that section and that are
set out in columns 1 and 2 of the table to that section are in
simultaneous contact with the top rail.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Top rails affixed to brackets

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A playpen that has
brackets that are affixed to its top rails and that are located above
its posts must, when the playpen is tested in accordance with Schedule
10, meet the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the brackets must be free from any visible cracks;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the fasteners of each bracket must not pass
  completely through any bracket wall; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any part of a fastener must not completely detach.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latching or locking mechanisms ---
folding playpens

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  folding playpen must have a latching or locking mechanism that
  prevents the playpen from spontaneously folding up or collapsing when
  the playpen is placed in the manufacturer's recommended use position.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latching or locking mechanisms --- top
  rails that fold down

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A latching or locking mechanism that is on the top
  rail of a folding playpen that has a hinge permitting the rail to fold
  downwards must latch or lock automatically when the rail is placed in
  the manufacturer's recommended use position.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Latching or locking mechanisms ---
operation

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every latching or locking
mechanism on a playpen must meet one of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the mechanism requires a single action to latch or
  lock it and requires a force of at least 45 N to unlatch or unlock it;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mechanism requires at least two separate,
  deliberate and simultaneous actions to unlatch or unlock it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance of latching or locking --- top
rails

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** When a playpen is tested
in accordance with Schedule 11, a top rail of a playpen that has a
latching or locking mechanism must fold when the mechanism is unlatched
or unlocked.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entanglement

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** When a playpen is tested
in accordance with Schedule 12, the load illustrated in Figure 2 to that
schedule must not remain caught on any mechanism, fastener or projection
of the playpen that is accessible to the child.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structural integrity

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The playing surface of a
playpen, any component of the playpen that is located below the playing
surface as well as the playpen's rigid structural components must not
exhibit any damage when the playpen is tested in accordance with
Schedule 13.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vertical displacement --- removable floor
pad with panel

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  removable floor pad of a playpen that is composed of a rigid panel or
  a series of rigid panels must not, when the playpen is tested in
  accordance with section 1 of Schedule 14, be displaced upwards more
  than 133 mm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vertical displacement --- removable and
  foldable component

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A removable and foldable component of a playpen that
  is composed of a rigid panel or a series of rigid panels and on which
  a removable floor pad rests must not, when the playpen is tested in
  accordance with section 2 of Schedule 14, be displaced upwards more
  than 133 mm.

### [Additional Requirements for Accessories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-852626 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entrapment --- accessory placed on or
fixed to playpen

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  tested in accordance with section 1 of Schedule 15, any opening that
  is created when an accessory is placed on or fixed to a playpen must
  not permit the passage of the small head probe illustrated in Figure 1
  to that section unless the opening also permits the passage of the
  large head probe illustrated in Figure 2 to that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entrapment --- accessory detached or
  displaced

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule
  15, any opening that is created when an accessory detaches or is
  displaced from a playpen must not permit the passage of the small head
  probe illustrated in Figure 1 to section 1 of that schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply in respect of a
  manufacturer's recommended use position for an accessory if, in that
  position, the playpen's floor pad cannot be used with the playpen.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structural integrity --- sleep and change
table accessories

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Every sleep accessory and
change table accessory must be capable of supporting one of the
following loads at the following locations for 60 seconds without any
damage to the accessory:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sleep accessory, a load of 24 kg
  that has a square base that measures 152 mm × 152 mm applied to the
  geometric centre of the sleeping surface and, if the surface has
  corners, then to each of the corners of the surface successively; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a change table accessory, a load of
  45 kg that has a square base that measures 152 mm × 152 mm applied to
  the geometric centre of the surface that supports the weight of the
  child.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sleep accessories --- angle

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  angle of the sleeping surface of a sleep accessory must not exceed 7°
  from the horizontal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sleep accessories --- angle of foldable
  mattress pad

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The angle of the surfaces adjacent to a fold in a
  foldable mattress pad that is supplied with, or that is designed or
  advertised to be used with, a sleep accessory must not, when the sleep
  accessory is tested in accordance with Schedule 16, exceed 7° from the
  horizontal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sleep accessories that rock or swing ---
angle

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Every sleep accessory
that rocks or swings must meet the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it does not rock or swing beyond a 20° angle from the
  horizontal; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its angle at rest does not exceed 7° from the
  horizontal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sleep accessories --- height of sides

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The upper surface of
every side of a sleep accessory must be at least 191 mm higher than the
sleeping surface when no load is applied to the sleeping surface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sleep accessories --- restraint systems

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A sleep accessory must
not have a child restraint system.

## [Information and Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-852651 .Part}

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-852652 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to [Canada Consumer Product
Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or Regulations

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Information that appears

### [Presentation of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-852657 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation --- general

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The information required
by these Regulations must meet all of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be displayed in both English and French;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be printed on the playpen or accessory, or on
  a label that is permanently affixed to it;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it must be legible and clearly displayed, including
  being printed in characters whose colour contrasts sharply with the
  background; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} it must be sufficiently durable to remain legible or
  visible, as the case may be, throughout the useful life of the playpen
  or accessory under normal conditions of transportation, storage, sale
  and use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Print

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  required information must be printed in a standard sans-serif type
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not compressed, expanded or decorative; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a large x-height relative to the ascender or
    descender of the type, as illustrated in Schedule 17.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height of the type is determined by measuring an
  upper case letter or a lower case letter that has an ascender or a
  descender, such as "b" or "p".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signal words

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  signal words "**WARNING**" and "**[MISE EN GARDE]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}**" must be displayed in boldfaced, upper case type that is
  not less than 5 mm in height.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other information --- height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other required information must be displayed in
  type that is not less than 2.5 mm in height.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 6)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Strength of Parts Made of a Textile or Other Pliable Material that are Permanently Affixed to a Rigid Structural Component]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852765 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the strength of
  playpen or accessory parts that are made of a textile or other pliable
  material and that are permanently affixed to a rigid structural
  component of the product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen or accessory according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} over a period of five seconds, gradually apply a
    force of 130 N using a clamp with a 19-mm diameter clamping surface
    to a part that is made of a textile or other pliable material and
    that is permanently affixed to a rigid structural component, in the
    direction that is most likely to cause the part to tear or become
    detached from the component, and maintain the force for 10 seconds;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (b) for all
    the other playpen or accessory parts that are made of a textile or
    other pliable material and that are permanently affixed to a rigid
    structural component.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 7(1))]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Strength of Mesh and Integrity of Attachment]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852772 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the strength of
  mesh on a playpen or accessory and the integrity of its attachment to
  that product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen or accessory according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the playpen or accessory on a horizontal
    surface on its side so that the side with the mesh panel that is
    being tested is positioned above the opposite side and, if
    necessary, secure it by using any blocking or support that maintains
    its position and that does not act directly on the frame of the side
    that is being tested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} apply a load of 9.18 kg that has a rectangular base
    that measures 150 mm × 75 mm to the geometric centre of the mesh
    panel or, if any exterior framing interferes with the test, as close
    as possible to the geometric centre, so that the 150-mm dimension of
    the load is aligned parallel to the top rail of the side that is
    being tested, in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply the load over a period of five
      seconds,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leave the load in place for 10 seconds,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} gradually remove the load over a period of five
      seconds, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} allow a recovery time of 10 seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (c) nine times
    for a total of 10 applications;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    on the top portion of the mesh panel in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} align the load so that the 75-mm dimension is
      parallel to the top edge of the mesh panel, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply the load to the mesh panel so that the
      centre of the 75-mm dimension of the load is 25 mm below the
      centre of the top edge of the panel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    on the bottom portion of the mesh panel in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} align the load so that the 75-mm dimension is
      parallel to the bottom edge of the mesh panel, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply the load to the mesh panel so that the
      centre of the 75-mm dimension of the load is 25 mm above the
      centre of the bottom edge of the panel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the bottom of the playpen or accessory has a
    mesh panel, place the product upside down on a horizontal surface
    and, if necessary, secure it by using any blocking or support that
    maintains its position and that does not act directly on the frame
    of the bottom of the product, and repeat the steps set out in
    paragraphs (c) to (f); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (g)
    on all the other mesh panels of the playpen or accessory.

## [SCHEDULE 4]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 10)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Openings]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852798 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing completely
  bounded openings that are formed or exposed in the surfaces that
  define the occupant retention area of a playpen or sleep accessory:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen or sleep accessory according
    to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that
    could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inside the occupant retention area, place a solid
    rectangular block that measures 60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm against a
    side of the playpen or sleep accessory in a location where the
    application of force is likely to create or expose a completely
    bounded opening;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply a force of 90 N to the block over a
    period of five seconds and maintain the force for an additional 10
    seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) and (c)
    for all the other locations that are likely to create or expose a
    completely bounded opening in that side;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (d)
    for all the other sides;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (d)
    for the playing surface or sleeping surface, but place the block on
    the surface;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} by gradually applying a force of 90 N over a period
    of five seconds and maintaining the force for an additional 10
    seconds, attempt to pass the block, in all possible orientations,
    through any completely bounded opening that is located --- or that
    was created or exposed as a result of the steps set out in
    paragraphs (b) to (f) --- in each of the surfaces that define the
    occupant retention area;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the playpen or sleep accessory has a structural
    cover, attempt to undo the zippers, snaps or other similar fasteners
    that attach it to the playpen or accessory by applying a force of
    90 N in any direction to each of them; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (g) for all
    the other completely bounded openings that are created or exposed in
    the surfaces that define the occupant retention area.

    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with no load placed on the playing surface,
    gradually apply a vertical force of 220 N downwards to a location
    that measures 50 mm on the upper surface of the top rail of a side
    where the application of the force is likely to cause the top rail
    to deflect, then maintain the force for 10 seconds;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} remove the force and measure the distance between
    the lowest point on the upper surface of the top rail and the
    playing surface;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) and (c)
    at all the other locations on the upper surface of the top rail
    where the application of the force is likely to cause the top rail
    to deflect;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (d)
    for all the other top rails of the playpen;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} identify a location that measures 50 mm on the
    upper surface of a top rail where the application of force is likely
    to cause the top rail to deflect;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} place on the playing surface a wooden block that
    has a square base that measures 152 mm × 152 mm so that the centre
    of the side of the block that is closest to the top rail is aligned
    on a vertical axis with the centre of the identified location;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} place a load of 4.5 kg on the centre of the wooden
    block;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply a vertical force of 130 N downwards
    to the location identified in paragraph (f) and then maintain the
    force for 10 seconds while measuring the distance between the
    playing surface and the identified location;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (f) to (i)
    at all the other locations on the upper surface of the top rail
    where the application of the force is likely to cause the top rail
    to deflect; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (f) to (j)
    for all the other top rails of the playpen.

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing the strength of a
  side of a playpen that has a hinge:

    and towards the interior of the playpen, and maintain the force for
    10 seconds; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (b) for all
    the other top rails of the playpen.

## [SCHEDULE 14]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 29)]{.OriginatingRef}[Tests for Vertical Displacement of Removable Floor Pad and Removable and Foldable Component]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852951 .scheduleLabel}

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the vertical
  displacement of a removable floor pad of a playpen that is composed of
  a rigid panel or a series of rigid panels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} attach a clamp with a 19-mm diameter clamping
    surface as close as possible to a corner of the removable floor pad;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} using the clamp, apply a vertical upwards force of
    2.2 N;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} while maintaining the force, select a fixed
    reference point on the clamp that is near the upper surface of the
    removable floor pad, and another fixed reference point on the
    component on which the floor pad rests, directly below the first
    fixed reference point;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} using the clamp, gradually apply a vertical upwards
    force of 66 N over a period of five seconds;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} while maintaining the force, measure the vertical
    upwards displacement of the removable floor pad between the fixed
    reference points;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (f)
    to the other corners; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the removable floor pad is foldable, repeat the
    steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (f) to other locations on the
    removable floor pad that are most likely to be displaced upwards and
    that are situated along the edges of the removable floor pad that
    are parallel to the axis around which the floor pad folds.

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing the vertical
  displacement of a removable and foldable component of a playpen that
  is composed of a rigid panel or a series of rigid panels and on which
  a removable floor pad rests:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove the removable floor pad;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} attach a clamp with a 19-mm diameter clamping
    surface as close as possible to a corner of the removable and
    foldable component;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} using the clamp, apply a vertical upwards force of
    2.2 N;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} while maintaining the force, select a fixed
    reference point on the clamp that is near the upper surface of the
    removable and foldable component, and another fixed reference point
    on the component on which the floor pad rests, directly below the
    first fixed reference point;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} using the clamp, gradually apply a vertical upwards
    force of 66 N over a period of five seconds;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} while maintaining the force, measure the vertical
    upwards displacement of the removable and foldable component between
    the fixed reference points; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (g)
    to the other corners and other locations on the removable and
    foldable component that are most likely to be displaced upwards and
    that are situated along the edges of the removable and foldable
    component that are parallel to the axis around which the component
    folds.

## [SCHEDULE 15]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 30(1) and (2))]{.OriginatingRef}[Tests for Entrapment in Accessories]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852972 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing openings that are
  created when an accessory is placed on or fixed to a playpen:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen and the accessory or
    accessories according to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting
    any attachments that could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the accessory or combination of accessories
    on the playpen --- or fix it or them to the playpen --- in one of
    the manufacturer's recommended use positions, and identify any
    openings that are created and are likely to permit the passage of
    the small head probe illustrated in Figure 1 to this section but not
    the large head probe illustrated in Figure 2 to this section;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rotate the small head probe to the orientation that
    is most likely to permit its passage through one of the identified
    openings and, from inside the occupant retention area of the
    playpen, gradually apply an upward or outward force of 111 N to the
    probe over a period of five seconds, then maintain the force for 10
    seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the small head probe can pass entirely through
    the opening, repeat the step set out in paragraph (c) using the
    large head probe and attempt to pass it, without forcing it, through
    the opening in any orientation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    for all the other identified openings; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (e)
    for all the other manufacturer's recommended use positions.

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing openings that are
  created by the detachment or displacement of an accessory from a
  playpen:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen and the accessory or
    accessories according to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting
    any attachments that could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the accessory or combination of accessories
    on the playpen --- or fix it or them to the playpen --- in one of
    the manufacturer's recommended use positions, and identify the parts
    of the accessory that are most likely to detach or become displaced
    and so create an opening;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} using a rod that has a circular tip with a diameter
    of 50 mm, gradually apply an upward or outward force of 111 N over a
    period of five seconds to one of the identified locations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if an opening is created, attempt to pass the small
    head probe illustrated in Figure 1 to section 1, without forcing it,
    through the opening in any orientation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    for all the other identified parts of the accessory; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (e)
    for all the other manufacturer's recommended use positions.

## [SCHEDULE 16]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 32(2))]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Angle of Foldable Mattress Pad of Sleep Accessories]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-852993 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the angle of the
  surfaces adjacent to a fold in a foldable mattress pad that is
  supplied with, or that is designed or advertised to be used with, a
  sleep accessory:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the playpen and sleep accessory according
    to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that
    could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the playpen on a horizontal surface in the
    manufacturer's recommended use position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the sleep accessory on, or fix it to, the
    playpen in the manufacturer's recommended use position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the playpen or sleep accessory rocks or swings,
    disable that function;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish a horizontal reference plane by placing
    on the horizontal surface and near the playpen an inclinometer with
    a minimum resolution of 0.5° and set it out to zero;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} place a cylinder that has a mass of 7.6 kg, a
    length of 305 mm and a diameter of 152 mm horizontally on a fold in
    the foldable mattress pad so that the centre of the cylinder is
    above the fold and the cylinder is aligned parallel to that fold,
    and if the cylinder does not remain in that location, place a
    sufficient number of stops to immobilize it, with the total mass of
    the stops not exceeding 0.11 kg;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} place, within 12.7 mm of one of the ends of the
    cylinder, a steel block measuring 152 mm × 101.6 mm, that has a
    thickness of 12.7 mm and a mass of 1.5 kg so that one of the sides
    of the block is placed on the fold parallel to the central axis of
    the cylinder and ignore any contact that results from the block
    sliding and touching the cylinder;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the size of the sleep accessory does not allow
    the steel block to be placed in accordance with paragraph (g), move
    the cylinder off-centre sufficiently so that the block can be placed
    in accordance with that paragraph;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the size of the sleep accessory does not allow
    the steel block to be placed in accordance with paragraph (h), use a
    steel block with a mass of 1.5 kg that is of such a size that it can
    be placed in accordance with paragraph (g);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} place the inclinometer on the centre of the upper
    surface of the block and measure the angle that is formed between
    the upper surface of the block and the horizontal, while ensuring
    that the inclinometer does not come into contact with the upper
    surface of the foldable mattress pad;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if the angle is greater than 7°, repeat the steps
    set out in paragraphs (g) to (j) two times and calculate the average
    of the three measurements;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (g) to (k)
    but move the block horizontally while maintaining the same
    orientation so that the opposite side of the block is placed on the
    same fold, parallel to the central axis of the cylinder;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (f) to (l)
    for all the other folds in the foldable mattress pad; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} remove any removable component of the sleep
    accessory that supports the foldable mattress pad and repeat the
    steps set out in paragraphs (f) to (m).
