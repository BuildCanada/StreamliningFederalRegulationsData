## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496603 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[CBSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CBSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Border Services Agency
    established under subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Border Services
    Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4). ([ASFC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CFIA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CFIA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Food Inspection
    Agency established by section 3 of the [Canadian Food Inspection
    Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5). ([ACIA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial transporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial transporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose
    commercial activity includes the transport of goods for
    others. ([entreprise de transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Fisheries
    and Oceans. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[elver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[elver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an American eel that is less
    than 10 cm in length. ([civelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496611 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the possession of elvers in Canada and in Canadian fisheries waters, and
to the export of elvers from Canada.

## [Possession of Elvers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496614 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- possession without a
licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), it is prohibited for any person to be in possession
  of elvers except under the authority of a licence to possess elvers
  issued under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the following
  persons in relation to the elvers that they possess in the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who is in possession of elvers that the
    person caught under the authority of a licence issued under section
    52 of the [Fishery (General)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) or that another person
    caught under the authority of the same licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is in possession of elvers for the
    purpose of their transfer to an aquaculture facility or their
    release into fish habitat, as authorized under the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14) or under provincial legislation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), a person, other than a
    person referred to in paragraph (a), who is in possession of elvers
    that the person caught under the authority of a licence issued under
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) or that another person caught
    under the authority of the same licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a commercial transporter who is transporting elvers
    in the course of their business and who has no other right or
    interest, directly or indirectly, in the elvers;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who is working at an airport, is engaged
    in the handling of goods intended for air transport and is in
    possession of elvers in the course of their duties;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an operator of an aquaculture facility acting under
    the authority of an [[aquaculture licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
    defined in section 1 of the [Aquaculture Activities
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-177) who is in possession of
    elvers for the purpose of cultivating them to grow to 10 cm or more
    in length;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} personnel of the CBSA, of the CFIA, of the
    Department or of a province, acting in the course of their duties;
    or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a person who obtains possession of elvers as a
    result of a law enforcement officer acting in the course of their
    duties under federal or provincial legislation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The exception set out in paragraph (2)(c) applies
  only while the person is in possession of elvers in the course of
  transporting them from the waters in which they were caught to a
  holding facility that is specified in the licence referred to in that
  paragraph.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obtaining possession

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to obtain possession of elvers from anyone other than a person
who is authorized to possess elvers under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authority of a licence issued under these
  Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exception referred to in any of paragraphs 3(2)(a)
  to (e), (g) or (h).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transferring possession of elvers

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to transfer possession of elvers to anyone
  other than a person who is authorized to possess elvers under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authority of a licence issued under these
    Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exception referred to in any of paragraphs
    3(2)(b) or (d) to (h).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons authorized under the same
  licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a person who possesses elvers
  that they caught under the authority of a licence referred to in
  paragraph 3(2)(a) or (c) --- or that were caught by another person
  under the authority of the same licence --- may transfer possession of
  those elvers to any other person who is authorized to fish under the
  same licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence to possess elvers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a licence authorizing a person to possess elvers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for the proper management and
  control of fisheries and the conservation and protection of fish,
  specify in the licence any condition that is consistent with these
  Regulations, including conditions respecting any of the following
  matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location or the facility to which, manner in
    which and time at which elvers must be brought and stored;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information that the licence holder must
    provide to the Department;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which, time at which and location
    from which the possession of elvers is permitted to be transferred;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the transport of elvers;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the containers that must be used to hold or
    transport elvers and their marking, labelling, sealing and
    unsealing, including by the licence holder, a fishery officer, a
    fishery guardian or personnel of the Department;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the records that a licence holder must keep,
    including records respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the elvers that they possess at any time, the
      persons from whom they obtained possession of them and the persons
      to whom they transferred possession of them, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of elvers that died while in the
      licence holder's possession and the circumstances of the deaths;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the manner and form in which the records are to be
    kept and the times at which and the manner and form in which records
    and information are to be produced to the Department; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the verification, by a fishery officer, a fishery
    guardian or personnel of the Department, of any shipment of elvers,
    including the process of packaging them, the weight of the elvers
    and the total weight of the package.

## [Export of Elvers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496656 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- export without a licence

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for any person to export elvers except under the authority
  of a licence to export elvers issued under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- certain elvers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for any person to export elvers that
  were caught illegally in Canadian fisheries waters or elvers whose
  possession was obtained from a person who was not authorized to
  possess them under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licence to export elvers

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a licence authorizing a person to export elvers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for the proper management and
  control of fisheries and the conservation and protection of fish,
  specify in the licence any condition that is consistent with these
  Regulations, including conditions respecting any of the following
  matters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location and time at which elvers are permitted
    to be packaged for export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information that the licence holder must
    provide to the Department;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information to declare to the CBSA respecting
    each shipment of elvers for export;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the records that an export licence holder must
    keep, including records respecting the commercial transporter to
    which an export shipment has been entrusted, and the tracking number
    or similar information identifying that shipment; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manner and form in which the records are to be
    kept and the times at which and the manner and form in which records
    and information are to be produced to the Department.

## [Containers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496672 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- combining elvers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person must not combine,
in the same container

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} eels that are less than 10 cm in length caught
  outside Canadian fisheries waters with elvers caught in those waters;
  or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} elvers legally caught in Canadian fisheries waters
  with elvers that were illegally caught in those waters.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- seals, sealed containers

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the conditions
of a licence issued under these Regulations, a person must not unseal a
container of elvers that has been sealed for export or tamper with its
seal.

## [Retention of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496679 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Any records that are
required to be kept under the conditions of a licence issued under these
Regulations must be retained for a period of at least five years after
the day on which the licence expires or is cancelled.

## [Application of Fishery (General) Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1496682 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of certain provisions

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions
of the [Fishery (General) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) apply
--- despite section 3 of those Regulations --- for the purposes of these
Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 2, 4, 5, 8 to 11 and 14;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} subsection 15(1);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sections 16, 17 and 21;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} subsections 22(2) to (6);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} section 24;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 25(1)(a); and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Part XI.
