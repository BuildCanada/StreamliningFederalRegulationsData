## Unfinished Leather Remission Order {#unfinished-leather-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-42
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-02-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Unfinished Leather for
Use in the Manufacture of Finished Garment Leathers

P.C. 1982-190 1982-01-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duty on
unfinished leather for use in the manufacture of finished garment
leathers](/eng/regulations/SI-82-42).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638727 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Unfinished Leather Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-82-42).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638729 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National Revenue.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638731 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the difference between the customs duty
paid or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on
leather further finished than tanned imported under tariff item 60405-1
and the customs duty that are otherwise paid or payable under tariff
items 60750-1, 60751-1 or 60752-1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SI_82-42_EN_hq_10880){#fn_SI_82-42_EN_hq_10880-ID0EBBA}.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SI_82-42_EN_hq_10880-ID0EBBA){#fn_SI_82-42_EN_hq_10880}S.C.
  1980-81, c. 67, s. 7
  :::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638733 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No remission shall be
granted under this Order unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the leather is imported during the period commencing
  on July 1, 1984 and ending on June 30, 1985;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the leather is used in the manufacture of finished
  garment leathers within three years of the date of importation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister within
  three years of the date of importation; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the claimant submits such reports and other
  information necessary to the Minister for the administration of this
  Order.
