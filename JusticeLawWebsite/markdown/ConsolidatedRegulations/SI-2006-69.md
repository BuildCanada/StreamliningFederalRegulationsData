## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Hay River, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-hay-river-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-69
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2006-05-17

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Hay River, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2006-246 2006-04-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby
orders that any portion of the lands described in the annexed schedule
lying within 30.48 m (100 feet) of the ordinary high water mark along
the shoreline of Hay River, in the Northwest Territories, be included in
the grant of those lands.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#612976 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-612977 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lot 626, Group 814, in the Town of Hay River, in the
Northwest Territories, as that lot is shown on a plan of survey of
record number 55497 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a
copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office for the Northwest
Territories Land Registration District at Yellowknife under number 497.
