## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-75
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2019-08-07

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2019-1109 2019-07-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25031){#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25031-ID0EEFAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25032){#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25032-ID0EBFAA},
effective July 22, 2019,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25031-ID0EEFAA){#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25031}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25032-ID0EBFAA){#fn_81000-3-1826_hq_25032}R.S., c.
  P-34
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Department of Crown-Indigenous
  Relations and Northern Affairs to the Department of Indigenous
  Services the control and supervision of those portions of the federal
  public administration in the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations
  and Northern Affairs known as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Individual Affairs Branch,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Lands and Economic Development Sector,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Planning, Research and Statistics Branch,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Financial Officer Recruitment and Development
    (FORD) Program,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Information Officer Branch,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Financial Planning, Analysis and Estimates
    Directorate,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the Corporate Accounting and Material Management
    Branch,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the Legislative, Parliamentary and Regulatory
    Affairs Directorate,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the Communications Branch,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the Security and Accommodation Directorate, and

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the Evaluation, Performance Measurement and Review
    Directorate;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Minister of Crown-Indigenous
  Relations to the Minister of Indigenous Services, the powers, duties
  and functions of the Minister of Crown-Indigenous Relations that
  relate to the National Indigenous Economic Development Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Department of Indigenous Services
  to the Department of Crown-Indigenous Relations and Northern Affairs
  the control and supervision of that portion of the federal public
  administration in the Department of Indigenous Services known as the
  Human Resources Planning, Performance Measurement and Systems Unit.
