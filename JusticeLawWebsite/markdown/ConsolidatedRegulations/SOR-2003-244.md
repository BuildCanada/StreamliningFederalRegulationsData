[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- life companies

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 495(7) and (8)
of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) do not apply to a
life company's acquisition of control of, or a life company's
acquisition or increase of a substantial investment in, an entity
referred to in paragraph 495(6)(b) of that Act if the financial
intermediary activities of the entity that expose the entity to material
market or credit risk are limited to providing services exclusively to
the life company or members of the life company's group.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- insurance holding companies

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 971(5) and (6)
of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) do not apply to an
insurance holding company's acquisition of control of, or an insurance
holding company's acquisition or increase of a substantial investment
in, an entity referred to in paragraph 971(4)(b) of that Act if the
financial intermediary activities of the entity that expose the entity
to material market or credit risk are limited to providing services
exclusively to the insurance holding company or members of the insurance
holding company's group.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
