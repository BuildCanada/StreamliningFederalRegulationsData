## Shipbuilding Temporary Assistance Program Regulations {#shipbuilding-temporary-assistance-program-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 349
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 3, 1970

Regulations Respecting the Granting of Assistance to Stimulate
Employment in Shipbuilding
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550409 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Shipbuilding Temporary Assistance Program
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._349).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550411 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an eligible ship, means the
    cost of the eligible ship determined by the Minister pursuant to
    subsection 6(3); ([coût approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an eligible ship,
    means that a certificate of registry has been issued for the ship;
    ([terminé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement in writing
    between a shipbuilder and a shipowner for the construction of one or
    more vessels that provides for the deposit of at least five per cent
    of the contract price or at least five per cent of the price of the
    first vessel in the case where more than one vessel is included in
    the contract; ([contrat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contract date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the later of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which a contract is signed by both
      parties, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which a deposit is paid by a
      shipowner to a shipbuilder; ([date du contrat]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[contract price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contract price]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount payable by a
    shipowner to a shipbuilder under the terms of a contract; ([prix
    contractuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
    declared by the Minister, pursuant to subsection 4(3) to be an
    eligible ship for the purposes of these Regulations; ([navire
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gross tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gross tonnage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9); ([jauge
    brute]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[maximum approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maximum approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an eligible ship
    means the maximum amount determined by the Minister pursuant to
    subsection 6(5); ([coût maximum approuvé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeds of disposition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeds of disposition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned to that phrase in paragraph 13(21)(d) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([produit d\'une
    disposition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen of Canada or a
    company incorporated in Canada that is engaged in Canada in the
    business of building ships in Canada; ([constructeur de
    navires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipbuilding grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipbuilding grant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount payable
    to a shipbuilder in respect of an eligible ship pursuant to these
    Regulations; ([aide à la construction de navires]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any commercial or scientific
    vessel that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} of at least

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 100 tons gross tonnage if self-propelled,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 200 tons gross tonnage if not self-propelled,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 50 tons gross tonnage if a tug,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} two tons gross tonnage if a submersible
        capable of operating at depths of at least 1,000 feet, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} over 75 feet in overall length if a fishing
      vessel. ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Payment of Shipbuilding Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550434 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  money is voted by Parliament for the purpose of shipbuilding grants
  for the construction of vessels, a shipbuilding grant may be paid in
  respect of an eligible ship

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction of which did not commence before
    November 27, 1970; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contract date applicable to which is not later
    than March 31, 1975.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-842, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/78-842, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a shipbuilding grant shall be
  determined in accordance with section 6 and may, subject to these
  Regulations, be paid in accordance with an agreement entered into
  pursuant to section 4.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-842, s. 1
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for a shipbuilding grant is made in respect of a vessel,
  the Minister may, subject to any terms or conditions established by
  the Treasury Board and subject to subsection (2), enter into an
  agreement with the shipbuilder thereof, providing for the payment of a
  shipbuilding grant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No agreement shall be entered into pursuant to
  subsection (1) until the Minister has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declared the vessel referred to in the application
    for a shipbuilding grant to be an eligible ship; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined the maximum amount of the approved cost
    of that eligible ship, pursuant to subsection 6(5).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), where the Minister is
  satisfied that the vessel referred to in the application for a
  shipbuilding grant is to be constructed in Canada by a shipbuilder and
  is to be registered in a country other than Canada, the Minister may
  declare the vessel to be an eligible ship.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before declaring a vessel to be an eligible ship the
  Minister shall consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the benefits to Canada through the use of Canadian
    materials and components;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the shipbuilder to carry out the
    terms of the contract without having to over-expand his facilities
    in relation to anticipated long-term demand;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the continuing availability of facilities to meet
    domestic requirements for ship construction and repair;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the positive effects of the contract on the
    long-term competitive status of the shipbuilder and of the Canadian
    shipbuilding industry; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the market development implications of the
    contract.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
shipbuilding grant shall be in the form prescribed by the Minister and
shall be made within 60 days of the contract date or within such longer
time as the Minister may allow.

## [Amount of Shipbuilding Grant]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550457 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  shipbuilding grant for an eligible ship of not more than 25,000 tons
  gross tonnage shall be an amount not exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 17 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is not later than September 30,
    1971;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 16 1/2 per cent of the approved cost when the
    contract date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period
    commencing October 1, 1971 and ending December 31, 1971;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 16 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period commencing
    January 1, 1972 and ending March 31, 1972;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 15 1/2 per cent of the approved cost when the
    contract date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period
    commencing April 1, 1972 and ending March 31, 1973; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 17 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period commencing
    April 1, 1973 and ending March 31, 1975.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The shipbuilding grant for an eligible ship of more
  than 25,000 tons gross tonnage shall be an amount not exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 14 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is not later than September 30,
    1971;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 13 1/2 per cent of the approved cost when the
    contract date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period
    commencing October 1, 1971 and ending December 31, l971;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 13 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period commencing
    January 1, 1972 and ending March 31, 1972;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 12 1/2 per cent of the approved cost when the
    contract date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period
    commencing April 1, 1972 and ending March 31, 1973; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 17 per cent of the approved cost when the contract
    date applicable to the eligible ship is within the period commencing
    April 1, 1973 and ending March 31, 1975.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the Minister shall, before
  paying the balance of a shipbuilding grant, pursuant to an agreement
  entered into under section 4, determine the cost of the eligible ship
  in respect of which the payment is to be made for the purpose of
  calculating the balance of the grant, which cost shall be the lower of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the maximum approved cost; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount obtained by an audit of costs pursuant
    to paragraph 7(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the whole or any part of the
    proceeds of disposition of any vessel described in subsection 13(15)
    of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is used by a person for
    replacement in accordance with subparagraph (a)(i) of that
    subsection, the approved cost of the replacement shall, if the
    replacement is an eligible ship in respect of which a shipbuilding
    grant is to be paid under these Regulations, be reduced by an amount
    equal to the amount used for replacement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel is acquired as a replacement under such
    circumstances that the Minister may pay out all or any part of a
    deposit under subsection 13(19) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the approved cost of such replacement shall,
    if the replacement is an eligible ship in respect of which a
    shipbuilding grant is to be paid under these Regulations, be reduced
    by the amount that bears the same relationship to the proceeds of
    disposition of the vessel disposed of as the amount that may be paid
    out by the Minister under subsection 13(19) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) bears to the amount of the deposit described
    in that subsection.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, before entering into any
  agreement under these Regulations, determine the maximum amount of the
  approved cost, which maximum amount shall not exceed the amount
  obtained by dividing the contract price of the eligible ship by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0.830 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraphs (1)(a) and (e) and paragraph (2)(e);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0.835 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (1)(b);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 0.840 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (1)(c);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 0.845 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (1)(d);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 0.860 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (2)(a);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} 0.865 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (2)(b);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} 0.870 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (2)(c); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} 0.875 when the shipbuilding grant is to be paid
    under paragraph (2)(d).

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550486 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement entered
into pursuant to these Regulations shall provide for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment of a shipbuilding grant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by progress payments not exceeding in the aggregate
    80 per cent of the shipbuilding grant, calculated on the basis of
    the maximum approved cost, before any eligible ship, the
    construction of which is referred to in the agreement, is completed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where the agreement refers to the construction of
    more than one eligible ship, by a further progress payment after
    completion of each eligible ship not exceeding such amount as would,
    in the opinion of the Minister based on an interim audit made by the
    Audit Services Bureau, Department of Supply and Services, afford
    adequate protection to the interests of Her Majesty, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by the payment of the balance of the shipbuilding
    grant after all eligible ships, the construction of which is
    referred to in the agreement, are completed and an audit has been
    made as provided in the agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the making of an audit and, where the agreement
  refers to the construction of more than one eligible ship, interim
  audits by the Audit Services Bureau, Department of Supply and
  Services, which audits shall exclude profit from the determination of
  approved cost;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking by the shipbuilder to use in the
  construction of the eligible vessel Canadian materials, components and
  equipment when they are available and internationally competitive; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other matters as the Minister considers
  necessary or appropriate to protect the interests of Her Majesty or to
  carry out the purposes and provisions of these Regulations.

## [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550495 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purposes of these Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize persons to sign agreements for him;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} investigate the financial responsibility of a
  shipowner or shipbuilder and such other matters as he considers
  necessary to determine questions relating to eligibility or approved
  cost; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribe forms and make rules relating to an
  application for the payment of shipbuilding grants.
