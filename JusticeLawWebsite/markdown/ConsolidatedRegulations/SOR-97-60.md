His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 58.3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-60_e_hq_5660){#fn_SOR-97-60_e_hq_5660-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-97-60_e_hq_5661){#fn_SOR-97-60_e_hq_5661-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed Order Specifying Limits on the Annual Aggregate
Quantity of Goods of Tariff Item No. 0603.10.21 that are Entitled to the
Benefit of the Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Tariff.

## [Tariff Rate Quota]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011470 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The aggregate quantity of
roses of tariff item No. 0603.10.11 that are entitled to the benefit of
the Canada-Israel Agreement Tariff is limited to 90,000 dozen in a
calendar year.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-27, s. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011474 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind8723_hq_16385){#fn_Ind8723_hq_16385-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which section
45 of the [Canada-Israel Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.4), being chapter 33 of the Statutes of Canada, 1996,
comes into force.
