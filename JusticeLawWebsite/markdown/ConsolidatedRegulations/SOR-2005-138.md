## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715659 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01).

## [Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715661 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 410(1.1) of the Act, a person is not a related party of a
retail association if the person would be a related party of the retail
association only because the person is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the retail association other than a
  member who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} controls, or has a significant interest in, the
    retail association, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has the right to exercise more than 10% of the
    votes that may be cast at an annual meeting of the retail
    association or to appoint or elect more than 10% of the directors of
    the retail association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a member of an association --- other than a member
  that controls that association --- that controls, or has a significant
  interest in, the retail association;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner, or a child who is
  less than 18 years of age, of a person described in paragraph (a) or
  (b); or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is controlled, determined without
  regard to paragraph 3(1)(e) of the Act, by a person described in
  paragraphs (a) to (c).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715669 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
