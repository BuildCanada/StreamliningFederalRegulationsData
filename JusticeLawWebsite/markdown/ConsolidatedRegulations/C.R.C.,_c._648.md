## Agencies' Orders and Regulations Approval Order {#agencies-orders-and-regulations-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 648
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Order Respecting the Classes of Orders and Regulations of Agencies to
which Paragraph 7(1)(d) of the Farm Products Agencies Act is Applicable
and the Procedure for their Approval
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559623 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Agencies' Orders and Regulations Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648).

## [Application of Paragraph 7(1)(d) of the Farm Products Agencies Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559625 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Paragraph 7(1)(d) of the
[Farm Products Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4) applies to the classes of
orders and regulations made by an agency that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish a quota system by which quotas are assigned
  to members of classes of producers of a farm product;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} impose levies or charges on persons engaged in the
  production of a farm product or the marketing thereof; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulate the price at which a farm product is
  marketed in a province other than the province in which the farm
  product is produced.

## [Procedure for Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559630 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Five copies of every order
and regulation of a class described in section 2 that is proposed to be
made by an agency shall be submitted to the Council for approval in both
official languages at least two weeks before the day on which the order
or regulation is proposed to be made.
