## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787933 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Guidelines.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the applicant for an
    approval referred to in section 373 of the
    Act. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 485.1 of the Act. ([cadre dirigeant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787939 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acquisition referred to in section 373 of
the Act

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Guidelines apply for
the purpose of section 377.2 of the Act in respect of an acquisition
referred to in section 373 of the Act.

## [Control in Fact]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In determining whether an
acquisition referred to in section 373 of the Act would, if approved by
the Minister, give a person control, within the meaning of paragraph
3(1)(d) of the Act, of a federal credit union, the policy objectives set
out in section 4 must be considered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policy objectives

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following policy
objectives must be considered for the purpose of a determination
referred to in section 3, taking into account the factors set out in
section 5:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} preserving the benefits of the ownership rules
  applicable to federal credit unions, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} continuing the organization and operation of
    federal credit unions on a cooperative basis,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} maintaining a high degree of transparency and
    member and shareholder oversight over federal credit unions,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} lessening the risk of distortions by federal
    credit unions in their credit allocation that may result from links
    with investors, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} enhancing the safety and soundness of federal
    credit unions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} allowing federal credit unions to manage their
  day-to-day operations and to develop their strategic visions by taking
  into account their best interests, as opposed to the best interests of
  any single member or shareholder, any group of members or
  shareholders, or any person or group of persons with which the federal
  credit unions have a business relationship; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} allowing investors in federal credit unions to
  exercise the degree of influence necessary to gain the benefits of
  their investments, including allowing them to account for their
  investments using the equity accounting method in accordance with
  generally accepted accounting principles.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The factors referred to in
section 4 are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number, type and distribution of securities and
  membership shares of the following entities, and the rights,
  privileges or features attached to those securities and membership
  shares:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the federal credit union referred to in section 3,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any subsidiary of the federal credit union;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value of the equity, the number of membership
  shares and the number and type of securities of any entity referred to
  in paragraph (a) that the applicant has or proposes to acquire, and
  the rights, privileges or features attached to those membership shares
  and securities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the involvement of the applicant or any member or
  shareholder of an entity referred to in paragraph (a) in the business
  of that entity, and their knowledge or expertise in financial services
  or in areas relevant to the operations of the federal credit union;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the relationships, agreements, understandings or
  arrangements

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} among members of one or more of the entities
    referred to in paragraph (a),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} among shareholders of one or more of the entities
    referred to in paragraph (a),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} among members and shareholders of one or more of
    the entities referred to in paragraph (a),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} between the applicant and the members or
    shareholders of any entity referred to in paragraph (a),

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} between the applicant and any person in relation to
    membership shares or securities of the federal credit union, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} between the applicant and any entity referred to
    in paragraph (a);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the composition and structure of the board of
  directors, any committees of the board of directors and any senior
  management committees of any entity referred to in paragraph (a), and
  the voting arrangements of those boards and committees;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether members, shareholders, directors or senior
  officers of any entity referred to in paragraph (a) are also members,
  shareholders, directors or senior officers of the applicant;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the existence of family relationships between the
  applicant's directors and senior officers and the directors and senior
  officers of any entity referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the ability of any person, including the applicant,
  to nominate, appoint or veto the appointment of directors, members of
  committees of the board of directors or senior officers of any entity
  referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the ability of any person, including the applicant,
  in respect of the board of directors, any committee of the board of
  directors or any senior management committee of any entity referred to
  in paragraph (a), to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} require that their consent be obtained before a
    proposal is submitted to that board or committee, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} veto a proposal submitted to that board or
    committee;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the ability of any person, including the applicant,
  to determine, influence or veto the day-to-day operations, business
  plans, significant capital expenditures, patronage allocations,
  dividend policy or issuance of membership shares or securities of any
  entity referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any dependency of any entity referred to in
  paragraph (a) on the applicant or on a member or shareholder of any
  entity referred to in that paragraph, by reason of an agreement or
  other arrangement between them;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} any links between the applicant or a member or
  shareholder of the federal credit union and an entity on which the
  federal credit union has a dependency by reason of an agreement or
  other arrangement between the federal credit union and the entity;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} any representations made to any agency or body that
  regulates or supervises financial institutions about any person's
  control of the federal credit union; and

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant factor that is related to any of
  the policy objectives set out in section 4.
