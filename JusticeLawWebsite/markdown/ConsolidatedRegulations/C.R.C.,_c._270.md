## Saskatchewan Egg Marketing Levies Order {#saskatchewan-egg-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 270
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies From
Certain Egg Producers in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537525 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Egg Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._270).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537527 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Saskatchewan
    Commercial Egg Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to
    the Act; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any shell eggs of the domestic
    hen offered for sale for human consumption and produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([oeufs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[layer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[layer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a female chicken over six months
    of age that may produce eggs; ([pondeuse]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a period of three consecutive
    calendar months commencing on January 1st, April 1st, July 1st or
    October 1st in any year; ([période]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Commercial Egg
    Producers' Marketing Plan established and amended from time to time
    pursuant to the Act; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person operating
    facilities for the production of eggs in the Province of
    Saskatchewan and marketing the production of 300 or more layers at
    any time during the calendar year; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number of layers the eggs of
    which a producer may market pursuant to quota orders made by the
    Commodity Board. ([contingent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537537 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall pay to the Commodity Board, in addition to service
  charges set forth under the Plan, levies at the rate of \$0.0125 per
  period for each layer in each period when the layer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is available for production at any given time
    during that period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not in excess of the quota fixed and allotted to
    him.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No levy is payable during any period for a layer that
  replaces another layer in respect of which a levy referred to in
  subsection (1) is payable or has been paid during that period.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall, in
respect of every period, pay to the Commodity Board, not later than the
15th day of the month following the month in which the period commenced,
the levies referred to in section 3.
