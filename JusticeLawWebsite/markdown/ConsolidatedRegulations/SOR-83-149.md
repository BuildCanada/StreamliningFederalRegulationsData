## Canada Oil and Gas Operations Regulations {#canada-oil-and-gas-operations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-149
:::

[CANADA OIL AND GAS OPERATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/O-7)

Registration 1983-02-04

Regulations Respecting Oil and Gas Operations on Canada Lands

P.C. 1983-334 1983-02-03
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of the proposed [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-149) was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 8, 1982 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations with respect thereto.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, the
Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to section 12 of the Oil and Gas Production and
Conservation Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting oil and gas operations on Canada
lands](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-149).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875157 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-149).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875159 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Oil and Gas Production and
    Conservation Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operating licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operating licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence issued
    pursuant to section 3.2 of the Act. ([licence
    d'opération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Operating Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875163 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an operating licence may be made by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is 18 years of age or over;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is registered with the Registrar
    of Companies pursuant to the Companies Ordinance of the Northwest
    Territories; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is entitled to carry on business
    in any province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every application for an operating licence shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the name and address of the applicant;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be forwarded to the Chief Conservation Officer; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be accompanied by a fee of \$25 made payable to the
    Receiver General.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall assign or
transfer an operating licence and any purported assignment or transfer
of an operating licence is void.

## [Work Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875175 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No holder of an operating
licence shall carry out any geotechnical or engineering feasibility
program, environmental study, geophysical or geological program, diving
program or other work or activity that is required by the Act to be
authorized unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that work or activity is expressly authorized
  pursuant to regulations made under the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder has obtained a written authorization
  pursuant to paragraph 3.2(1)(b) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-350, s. 1
:::

## [Reporting of Spills]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875181 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any person who, pursuant to
subsection 19.1(2) of the Act, reports a spill shall do so by informing
the Chief Conservation Officer forthwith of the circumstances and
relevant details of the spill by the most rapid and practical means.
