## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457217 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Marine Protected
Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  area of the sea that is designated by section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North American Datum
  1983, Canadian Spatial Reference System (NAD83, CSRS).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates for points

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The geographical coordinates of the points referred
  to in sections 2 and 3 are set out in the schedule.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457225 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  of the sea depicted in the schedule that is bounded by the following
  lines is designated as the Tang.[ɢ]{.Underline}wan --- ḥačxʷiqak ---
  Tsi[g]{.Underline}is Marine Protected Area:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a rhumb line drawn from point 1 to point 2, with
    point 2 being located on the western boundary of the Scott Islands
    Protected Marine Area, as described in Schedule 1 to the [Scott
    Islands Protected Marine Area Establishment
    Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2018-44);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a rhumb line drawn southerly following the western
    boundary of the Scott Islands Protected Marine Area, as described in
    Schedule 1 to the [Scott Islands Protected Marine Area Establishment
    Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2018-44), to point 3;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a rhumb line southeasterly following the boundary
    of the Scott Islands Protected Marine Area, as described in Schedule
    1 to the [Scott Islands Protected Marine Area Establishment
    Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2018-44), to point 4;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a series of rhumb lines drawn from point 4 to point
    5 and then to point 6;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a rhumb line to a point on the international
    boundary between Canada and the United States intersecting a rhumb
    line drawn from point 6 to point 7;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a line southwesterly following the international
    boundary between Canada and the United States to point 8, on the
    seaward limit of the exclusive economic zone of Canada; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a line northwesterly following the seaward limit of
    the exclusive economic zone of Canada to a point intersecting a
    rhumb line drawn from point 1 to point 9, and then a rhumb line back
    to point 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 1 000 m and the water column above the seabed.

## [Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457238 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundaries

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Marine Protected Area
consists of the following management zones, each of which is depicted in
the schedule:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Dellwood Zone, which consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 1 000 m and the water column above the seabed
  and is bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn from points 10 to 13
  and then back to point 10;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Union Zone, which consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 1 000 m and the water column above the seabed
  and is bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn from points 14 to 17
  and then back to point 14; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the General Zone, which consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 1 000 m and the water column above the seabed
  that is within the part of the Marine Protected Area that is not
  within the Dellwood Zone or the Union Zone.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457244 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to carry
out any activity in the Marine Protected Area that disturbs, damages,
destroys or removes from the Marine Protected Area any living marine
organism or any part of its habitat or that is likely to do so.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457247 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4, the
following activities may be carried out in the Marine Protected Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following fishing activities when conducted in
  accordance with the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), the [Coastal
  Fisheries Protection Act](/eng/acts/C-33) and the regulations to those
  Acts:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fishing, other than commercial fishing, that is
    authorized under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332) and that does not involve
    the use of fishing gear that contacts the seabed, such as bottom
    trawls, dredges, traps or bottom longlines;

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} commercial or recreational fishing in the General
    Zone carried out by means of a pelagic hook and line or a midwater
    trawl that does not involve the use of that fishing gear at a depth
    greater than 500 m below the sea surface, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commercial or recreational fishing in the
    Dellwood Zone or the Union Zone that is carried out by means of a
    pelagic hook and line that does not involve the use of that fishing
    gear at a depth greater than 100 m below the sea surface;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the laying, maintenance or repair of cables;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the navigation of a vessel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an activity that is carried out for the purpose of
  public safety, national defence, national security or law enforcement
  or to respond to an emergency; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an activity that is part of an activity plan that has
  been approved by the Minister.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1457258 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A person who proposes to
carry out a scientific research or monitoring activity or an educational
activity in the Marine Protected Area must submit to the Minister an
activity plan that contains the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number and
  email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the activity plan is submitted by an institution
  or organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible
  for the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number
  and email address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
  use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
  registration number, its radio call sign and the name, address,
  telephone number and email address of its owner, master and any
  operator;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
  its purpose, the methods or techniques that are to be used to carry
  out the activity and the data to be collected;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographical coordinates of the site of the
  proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
  within the Marine Protected Area;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates and alternative dates on which the
  proposed activity is to be carried out;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the equipment that is to be used, the means
  by which it will be deployed and retrieved and the methods by which it
  is to be anchored or moored;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the types and quantities of samples that
  are to be collected;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited during
  the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Area --- other than
  substances that are authorized under the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) to be deposited in the navigation of a vessel
  --- and the quantity and concentration of each substance;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse environmental effects
  that are likely to result from carrying out the proposed activity and
  of any measures that are to be taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or
  mitigate those effects;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
  monitoring activity or educational activity that the person has
  carried out or anticipates carrying out in the Marine Protected Area;
  and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
  work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity and its
  anticipated date of completion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are not likely to destroy the habitat of any
    living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase knowledge of the habitat
      of any living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area or the
      ecosystems these habitats support in the Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will serve to assist in the management of the
      Marine Protected Area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational activities set in out in the plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not likely to damage, destroy or remove from
      the Marine Protected Area any living marine organism or any part
      of its habitat, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase public awareness of the
      Marine Protected Area; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the approval of the activity plan is necessary to
    be consistent with the United Nations Convention on the Law of the
    Sea, signed by Canada on December 10, 1982 or customary
    international law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, the Minister must not approve an activity
  plan under paragraph 1(a) or (b) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are likely to adversely affect the ecological
    integrity of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Salty Dawg hydrothermal vent, being the area
      of the sea depicted in the schedule that is bounded by a series of
      rhumb lines drawn from points A to D and then back to point A, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the High Rise hydrothermal vent, being the area
      of the sea depicted in the schedule that is bounded by a series of
      rhumb lines drawn from point E to H and then back to point E;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the
    substance is authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with those of any other past and
    current activities carried out in the Marine Protected Area, are
    such that the activity is likely to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} destroy the habitat of any living marine organism
      in the Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} adversely affect the biological, chemical or
      oceanographic processes that maintain or enhance the biodiversity,
      structural habitat or ecosystem function in the Marine Protected
      Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 90 days after the day on which the plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 90 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity report

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister approves an activity plan, the person who submitted it must
  provide the Minister with an activity report within 90 days after the
  last day of the activity and the report must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the data collected during the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type and quantity of any sample that was
    collected, the date of collection and the geographic coordinates of
    the sampling site;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the effectiveness of any measures
    taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or mitigate the adverse
    environmental effects of the activity; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any event that occurred during the
    activity and that was not anticipated in the activity plan, if the
    event could result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or
    removal from the Marine Protected Area of any living marine organism
    or any part of its habitat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, reports or other publications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must also provide the Minister with a copy
  of any study, report or other publication that results from the
  activity and is related to the conservation and protection of the
  Marine Protected Area. The study, report or other publication must be
  provided within 90 days after the day on which it is published.
