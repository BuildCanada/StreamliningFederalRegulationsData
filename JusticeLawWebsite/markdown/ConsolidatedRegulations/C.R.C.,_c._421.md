## Certification of Countries Granting Equal Copyright Protection Notice {#certification-of-countries-granting-equal-copyright-protection-notice .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 421
:::

[COPYRIGHT ACT](/eng/acts/C-42)

Notice Pursuant to Subsection 4(2) of the Copyright Act Certifying that
Certain Countries Grant the Benefit of Copyright Protection
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556519 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Notice may be cited as
the Certification of Countries Granting Copyright Protection Notice.

## [Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556521 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Consumer
and Corporate Affairs hereby certifies that the countries listed in the
schedule grant or have undertaken to grant to citizens of Canada the
benefit of copyright on substantially the same basis as to its own
citizens or copyright protection substantially equal to that conferred
by the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42) and, for the purpose of the
rights conferred by the said Act, such a country shall be treated as if
it was a country to which the said Act extends.
