## International Letter-post Items Regulations {#international-letter-post-items-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-807
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 1983-10-13

Regulations Respecting International Letter-post Items

P.C. 1983-3198 1983-10-13
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of the proposed [International Letter-post Items
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-807), substantially in the form set
out in the schedule hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on Saturday, August 6, 1983,
and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons
to make representations to the Minister responsible for the Canada Post
Corporation with respect hereto.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Honourable André Ouellet, pursuant to paragraphs
17(1)(b) and (c), subparagraph 17(1)(g)(i), paragraph 17(1)(q) and
section 66 of the [Canada Post Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-10) and the
Universal Postal Convention, Rio de Janeiro, 1979, is pleased hereby to
approve the revocation of the International First Class Mail
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1280 and the making by the Canada Post
Corporation on October 7, 1983 of the annexed Regulations respecting
international letter-post items.
:::

## [Limits of Size and Weight]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881543 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  letter-post item shall not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a card or postcard, 235 mm in length
    and 120 mm in width; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 380 mm in length, 270 mm in
    width and 20 mm in thickness.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A card or postcard shall be made of cardboard or any
  other material that is commonly used in printing and shall weigh a
  minimum of 2 g so as to provide the stiffness necessary to withstand
  machine processing.

## [Customs Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881560 .Part}

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  letter-post item that, by reason of its volume, nature or value, may
  be subject to customs duty in the country of destination shall have
  affixed on the address side thereof in the upper left-hand corner,
  under the sender's name and address, where given,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a completed green CN22 label; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a completed green printed facsimile of the label
    referred to in paragraph (a) that is 74 mm in length and 52 mm in
    width.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the value of a letter-post item referred to in
  subsection (1) exceeds \$500 and the item is addressed for delivery to
  the United States, its territories and possessions, the sender shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complete the customs document required by the
    Corporation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} securely affix the customs document to the item on
    the address side where possible.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the value of a letter-post item referred to in
  subsection (1) exceeds \$500 and the item is addressed for delivery to
  a country other than the United States, its territories and
  possessions, the sender shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} complete a Customs Declaration Dispatch Note in the
    form set out in Schedule III; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} securely affix the Customs Declaration Dispatch
    Note referred to in paragraph (a) to the item on the address side
    when possible.

### [General Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-881578 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No letter or postcard
enclosed in an envelope and no self-mailer shall be transmitted by post
unless the envelope or self-mailer is completely sealed and rectangular
in shape.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-199, s. 21(F)
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where the name and address
of the sender of a letter or postcard appear on the address side
thereof, such name and address shall be located in the upper left-hand
corner.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/86-246,
s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

### [Postcards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-881584 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  expression "post card", "postcard" or "carte postale" shall appear on
  the face of all postcards other than illustrated postcards.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following articles shall not be attached to a
  postcard:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} samples of merchandise or similar articles;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} photographs;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cuttings of any kind;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fold-back sheets; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cloth, embroidery, spangles and other decorative
    articles.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No postcard shall have projecting or raised relief
  parts.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-246, s. 4
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any illustration, stamp,
label or address slip may be affixed to a postcard if it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} consists of paper or any other very thin substance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} adheres completely to the postcard;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an illustration or stamp, is affixed
  to the back of the postcard or left-hand portion of the face of the
  postcard; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a label or address slip, is affixed to
  the back of the postcard or the left-hand portion or whole of the face
  of the postcard.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-246, s. 5
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Every postcard shall be
rectangular in shape, or if not rectangular in shape, be enclosed in a
rectangular envelope.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2000-378, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}
