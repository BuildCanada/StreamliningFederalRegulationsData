## [Provincial Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-494966 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate a
motor vehicle on a highway unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person holds all licences, permits and
  certificates required to operate the motor vehicle by the laws of the
  province in which the highway is situated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is registered and equipped in
  accordance with the laws of the province in which the highway is
  situated; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person wears the equipment required to operate
  the motor vehicle by the laws of the province in which the highway is
  situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 6
- SOR/92-546, s. 5(F)
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate a motor vehicle on a highway otherwise than in
  accordance with the laws of the province in which the highway is
  situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), in the event of any
  inconsistency between these Regulations and the laws of a province in
  which a highway is situated, these Regulations prevail to the extent
  of the inconsistency.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No passenger of a motor vehicle shall ride in or on a
  motor vehicle on a highway otherwise than in accordance with the laws
  of the province in which the highway is situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 7
:::

## [Dimensions of Motor Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-494979 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 14
and 16, no person shall operate a bus, truck tractor trailer
combination, truck, or a passenger vehicle and house-trailer combination
on any highway within a park unless the unit conforms with the
dimensions and gross weights approved for the unit by the province in
which the highway is situated.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
issue a single trip permit for the operation of a motor vehicle having
any dimensions or carrying any load, and a person in possession of that
permit may operate that motor vehicle for the single trip authorized by
the permit along such portion of the highway and under such conditions
as the permit specifies.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

## [Traffic Signs and Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-494988 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may mark and erect on or along a highway a traffic sign
  or device that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribes rates of speed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} regulates or prohibits the tethering of horses or
    the stopping or parking of motor vehicles or any class thereof;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribes load limits and dimensions for motor
    vehicles or any class thereof or prohibits the use of the highway by
    a motor vehicle carrying loads in excess of prescribed load limits;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} designates the highway as a one-way highway;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} requires motor vehicles to stop;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} closes the highway to all motor vehicles or any
    class thereof;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} limits the hours during which the highway may be
    used;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} regulates pedestrian traffic;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} limits the use of the highway by horses or by a
    specific class or specific classes of motor vehicles;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} designates a taxi or cab stand; or

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} regulates, directs or controls in any other manner
    the use of the highway by horses, motor vehicles or pedestrians.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person other than the superintendent shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} mark or erect any traffic sign or device on or
    along a highway; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove or deface any traffic sign or device on or
    along a highway.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person using a highway shall obey the
  instructions on every traffic sign or device.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person driving or operating a motor vehicle for
    the park fire protection service when proceeding to a fire; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person driving or operating a police motor
    vehicle, ambulance or motor vehicle that belongs to the Department
    when proceeding to the scene of an accident, or when using the motor
    vehicle for any emergency purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, ss. 9, 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Where any traffic sign or
device erected pursuant to section 16 bears a regulatory symbol, the
symbol has the meaning prescribed for that symbol by the laws of the
province in which the highway is situated.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Any traffic sign or
device in a national park bearing the words "Government of Canada",
"Gouvernement du Canada", "National Parks of Canada", "Parcs nationaux
du Canada" or any abbreviation thereof or purporting to have been
erected by or under the authority of the superintendent shall be deemed
to have been erected pursuant to these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/90-79,
s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may by order prohibit a person from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} parking a motor vehicle on a highway; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} loading or unloading a motor vehicle on a highway.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who receives a copy of an order made by the
  superintendent under subsection (1) shall comply with the order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** The driver of a motor
vehicle on a highway shall, notwithstanding any traffic sign or device,
comply with any traffic directions given to him by a police constable or
any person appointed by the superintendent to direct the movement of
motor vehicles.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Every person operating a
motor vehicle within a park shall, on the request of a park warden,
police constable or peace officer,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} stop the motor vehicle and give to the park warden,
  police constable or peace officer information respecting the motor
  vehicle; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} produce for inspection by the park warden, police
  constable or peace officer any licence, permit, pass or certificate
  that is required by these Regulations or by the laws of the province
  in which the park is situated to operate the motor vehicle in the
  park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 11
- SOR/92-546, s. 6(F)
:::

## [Parking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495031 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may erect a sign that designates an area as

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an area where parking is reserved for persons
    holding parking permits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area where parking is permitted for a period of
    time; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an area where parking is not permitted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle in an area
  designated pursuant to paragraph (1)(a) unless he

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a parking permit authorizing him to park in
    the area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} attaches to and exposes on the motor vehicle the
    label furnished with the parking permit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} parks in accordance with the terms of his parking
    permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle in an area
  designated pursuant to paragraph (1)(b) for any greater period of time
  than that designated by the sign.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle in an area
  designated pursuant to paragraph (1)(c).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle or a trailer on
  a highway for purposes of overnight accommodation except in areas or
  at locations designated for that purpose by the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, ss. 12, 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may issue parking permits and furnish labels for the
  purposes of these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A parking permit issued pursuant to subsection (1) is
  valid for the period stated thereon or until revoked by the
  superintendent and a label furnished with the permit is valid for the
  same period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Where an area in a park
is designated by a sign as an area where parking is reserved for a
category of persons, no person shall park in the area unless he is
included in that category.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} abandon a motor vehicle in a park; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} park a motor vehicle in an area that is not
  designated by a sign pursuant to subsection 23(1) and that is not an
  area in which parking is prohibited, for a greater period of time than
  48 hours, unless he

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has obtained the prior permission of the
    superintendent to park in such area for a greater period than 48
    hours,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is the owner, lessee or licensee of the property,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is authorized by the owner, lessee or licensee of
    the property to park the motor vehicle for a greater period than 48
    hours.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park a
motor vehicle within 6 m of any highway intersection or within 3 m of a
water hydrant or fire plug within a park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 13
:::

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall park a motor vehicle on any highway so as to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obstruct a driveway leading to a private residence
    or business premises; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interfere with a motor vehicle that is being loaded
    or unloaded.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle on a sidewalk
  unless he is permitted to do so by the superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, without permission from the
  superintendent, stop or park a motor vehicle on a highway otherwise
  than in the direction of the vehicular traffic on the side of the
  highway on which he proposes to stop or park, and parallel with, and
  no further distant than 1 foot from, the curb or edge of the highway.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent has permitted the stopping
  or parking of a motor vehicle on a highway in a manner other than the
  manner described in subsection (3), every person stopping or parking a
  motor vehicle on that highway, shall comply with any order of the
  superintendent regarding the manner of stopping or parking the motor
  vehicle.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where the superintendent has designated parking
  spaces on a highway, no person shall park a motor vehicle on that
  highway except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in one of those parking spaces; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the motor vehicle exceeds the length of one
    parking space, in not more than two of those parking spaces.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person shall park a motor vehicle on a sloping
  highway without having set the vehicle's parking brakes and, by
  turning the wheels of the motor vehicle or by other suitable means,
  ensure that in case of brake failure the motor vehicle will not run
  down the slope.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** No person shall stop or
park a motor vehicle in any public lane except while engaged in loading
or unloading chattels to or from the motor vehicle.

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall park a trailer within a park in a location other than a
  location designated by a sign erected by the superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} abandon a trailer in a park; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} park a trailer in a location designated by a sign
    erected by the superintendent for a greater period than is specified
    on the sign.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 14
:::

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A park warden, police
constable or peace officer may, at the expense of the owner of a motor
vehicle or trailer that is parked or left in contravention of these
Regulations, cause the motor vehicle or trailer to be moved or taken to
and stored in a suitable place.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 14

## [Careless Driving Prohibited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495093 .Part}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** No person shall drive or
ride any motor vehicle, bicycle, horse or other animal upon any highway
without due care and attention or without reasonable consideration for
other persons using the highway.

## [Bicycles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495095 .Part}

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Every person riding a
bicycle on a highway shall ride as close as possible to the right-hand
edge or curb of the highway and, when riding with other persons, shall
not ride more than two abreast.

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** No person shall ride a
bicycle on a sidewalk in a park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 15
:::

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** No person shall ride a
bicycle in a park unless the bicycle is equipped in accordance with the
laws of the province in which the park is situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 15
:::

## [Sidewalks and Boulevards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495103 .Part}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an access driveway to a lot within a townsite or subdivision within a
  park is not provided or conveniently located, and a person desires to
  ride or drive a horse or motor vehicle from the highway to the lot, he
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} construct across the drain, gutter or water course
    skirting the place where he intends to enter the lot a good and
    sufficient bridge of planks or other material satisfactory to the
    superintendent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} construct over the sidewalk or boulevard to be
    crossed and of the full width thereof a temporary crossing of planks
    or other material satisfactory to the superintendent sufficient to
    prevent injury to the sidewalk or boulevard.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The temporary crossing described in subsection (1)
  shall be removed forthwith after it has served its purpose.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Except as authorized by
these Regulations, no person shall ride, drive, lead or back any horse
or motor vehicle across or along any sidewalk or boulevard within a
park.

## [Taxi or Cab Stands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495112 .Part}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  chauffeur shall park a motor vehicle for hire at any place within a
  park other than a place designated by the superintendent as a taxi or
  cab stand.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who operates a motor vehicle for hire shall
  not solicit passengers on any highway or sidewalk other than a highway
  or sidewalk at or adjacent to a taxi or cab stand or his place of
  business.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

## [Snow Tires and Chains]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495118 .Part}

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall drive a motor vehicle

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on any highway in Mount Revelstoke National Park,
    Glacier National Park or Yoho National Park,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the Icefield Parkway in Banff National Park and
    Jasper National Park,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the Banff-Windermere Highway in Banff National
    Park and Kootenay National Park, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} on any other highway in Banff National Park, Jasper
    National Park or Kootenay National Park that has been designated by
    the superintendent by means of a notice prominently posted or
    displayed on any such highway

  during the period between November 1 to March 31 or any other period
  during which the highway is covered with snow or ice unless adequate
  snow tires or tire chains have been installed on the motor vehicle.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/90-79, ss. 16, 25(F)
  - SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
  :::

## [Over-snow Vehicles and All-terrain Vehicles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495128 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/90-79, s. 17

\]
:::

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate an over-snow vehicle in a park unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he has the written permission of the
    superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the over-snow vehicle is licensed, registered and
    equipped as required by the laws of the province in which the park
    is situated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} he operates it in accordance with such conditions
    and in such areas as the superintendent may specify; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that person and any passenger on the over-snow
    vehicle are wearing the equipment required to operate the over-snow
    vehicle by the laws of the province in which the park is situated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall operate an all-terrain vehicle in a
  park except for purposes of administration of the park and with the
  permission of the superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/90-79, s. 18\]]{.Repealed}

- SOR/90-79, ss. 18, 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)

## [Miscellaneous]{.HTitleText1} {#h-495141 .Part}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** No person shall leave any
horse unattended on a highway.

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** No person shall, within a
park, coast or slide upon any toboggan or sled along or across a highway
or public place that has not been set aside by the superintendent for
coasting or sliding.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), no person
  shall, without the permission of the superintendent, haul any dead
  animal, offal, nightsoil or other offensive matter or thing on a
  highway in a park during the hours of daylight.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of any animal that dies or has been killed
  on any highway shall forthwith cause the carcass to be removed
  therefrom and suitably disposed of.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 25(F)
- SOR/91-375, s. 3(E)
:::

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** When the load of a
passenger vehicle, truck or trailer consists of loose material or of
material that is likely to slip, swing out, sway, shift, spill, leak,
blow off, fall off or otherwise escape in any direction while being
conveyed, the load shall be placed inside a container or shall be
secured and bound so as to prevent such escape.

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place on
or over any highway or sidewalk any material or commodity of any
description that might cause personal or property damage or in any way
interfere with traffic on the highway or sidewalk.

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall draw or tow by motor vehicle on a highway a sled,
  toboggan, ski, bicycle or any other conveyance on which a person is
  riding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall skate, roller skate, roller blade,
  roller ski or ride a skateboard on any highway or sidewalk in a town,
  visitor centre or resort subdivision.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person who roller blades or roller skis on a
  highway outside a town, visitor centre or resort subdivision shall
  travel as close as possible to the left-hand edge or curb of the
  highway and, when roller blading or roller skiing with other persons,
  shall travel in single file.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 19
:::

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** In every prosecution of
an owner of a motor vehicle in respect of the use or operation of the
vehicle in violation of any provision of these Regulations, the onus of
proving that the motor vehicle was not in his possession or control at
the time of the violation is on the accused.

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** In any violation of these
Regulations where the owner of a motor vehicle is identified through
provincial motor vehicle registration records, the court may accept as
evidence of ownership of the motor vehicle a written statement of
ownership of that motor vehicle that is duly completed in accordance
with the laws of the province in which the park is situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-79, s. 20
:::
