## Eligibility of Certain Former Members of the Canadian Forces Regulations {#eligibility-of-certain-former-members-of-the-canadian-forces-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2025-25
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2025-02-14

Eligibility of Certain Former Members of the Canadian Forces Regulations

P.C. 2025-95 2025-02-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Public Service Commission and the President of the King's Privy
Council for Canada, makes the annexed [Eligibility of Certain Former
Members of the Canadian Forces
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-25) under section 21 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).[[Footnote

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511523 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Employment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not employed in the public service for an
      indeterminate period;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on November 13, 2020
      and ending on January 18, 2024,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} completed their third year of service in the
        Canadian Forces, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was honourably released within the meaning of
        regulations made under the [National Defence
        Act](/eng/acts/N-5); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} created an account in the Public Service
      Resourcing System on or before January 18, 2024. ([personne
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511533 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entitlement

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Until April 1, 2029, an
eligible person may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} participate in an advertised internal appointment
  process as if they were employed in the public service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make a complaint under section 77 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criterion in relation to designated
groups

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Until April 1, 2029, if a
criterion in relation to belonging to any of the [[designated
groups]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 3 of the
[Employment Equity Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401), is established under section
34 of the Act in respect of an advertised internal appointment process,
the eligible person must meet that criterion.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1511540 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
