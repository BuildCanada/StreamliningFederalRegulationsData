## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Species at Risk Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessments-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-species-at-risk-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-48
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2004-05-05

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Species at Risk Act

P.C. 2004-413 2004-04-21
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, hereby acknowledges receipt of the
assessments done pursuant to subsection 23(1) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_N8414_5E_hq_23){#fn_N8414_5E_hq_23-ID0EBBAA} by
the Committee on the Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada (COSEWIC)
with respect to the species enumerated in the Annex to this Order.
