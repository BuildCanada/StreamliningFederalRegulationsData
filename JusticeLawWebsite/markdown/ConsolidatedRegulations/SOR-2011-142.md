## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772719 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[ICCRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ICCRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Immigration Consultants of
    Canada Regulatory Council, incorporated under Part II of the [Canada
    Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) on February 18, 2011.
    ([CRCIC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772723 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 91(2)(c) of the Act, the ICCRC is designated as a body whose
members in good standing may represent or advise a person for
consideration --- or offer to do so --- in connection with a proceeding
or application under the Act.

## [Transitional Measures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772725 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who, on the date on which these Regulations come into force, is
  a member in good standing of the Canadian Society of Immigration
  Consultants, incorporated under Part II of the [Canada Corporations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-1.8) on October 8, 2003, is a member of the ICCRC,
  for the purposes of the Act, for a period lasting until the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 120 days following the date on which these
    Regulations come into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which that person discontinues their
    status as a member of the ICCRC.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the period during which they are a member of the
  ICCRC in accordance with subsection (1), the persons referred to in
  that subsection are not required to pay membership fees to that body.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772731 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndC19A_hq_11037){#fn_IndC19A_hq_11037-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 1 of An Act to amend the Immigration and Refugee Protection Act,
chapter 8 of the Statutes of Canada, 2011, comes into force, but if they
are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which
they are registered.
