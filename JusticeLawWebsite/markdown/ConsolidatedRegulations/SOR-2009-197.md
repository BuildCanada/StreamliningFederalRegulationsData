## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749420 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[automotive refinishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[automotive refinishing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity
    relating to the servicing, maintenance, repair, restoration or
    modification of motor vehicles or mobile equipment, or their parts,
    involving the application of a coating or surface
    cleaner. ([finition automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that forms a film
    when applied to a surface for protective or any other automotive
    refinishing purpose. It excludes products used in carrying out metal
    plating and lacquer topcoats and any oil-based enamel paints used
    for the restoration of motor vehicles or mobile equipment made on or
    before 1985 and their parts. ([revêtement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[excluded compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[excluded compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the compounds that
    are excluded under item 65 of Schedule 1 to the [Canadian
    Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) as well as
    acetic acid, 1,1--dimethylethyl ester (C~6~H~12~0~2~). ([composés
    exclus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mobile equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mobile equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any equipment, other
    than a motor vehicle, that is capable of being pulled on a
    highway. ([équipement mobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any self-propelled
    vehicle, but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an aircraft as defined in subsection 3(1) of the
      [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} rolling stock as defined in section 6 of the
      [Canada Transportation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4); or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a boat, ship or craft designed, used or capable
      of being used solely or partly for navigation in, on, through or
      immediately above water. ([véhicule automobile]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[surface cleaner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[surface cleaner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product used to
    prepare the surface of motor vehicles or mobile equipment by
    removing unwanted matter from the surface before applying a coating.
    It excludes products used for cleaning automotive refinishing
    equipment and hand-held spray bottle spot cleaners used to prepare
    surfaces prior to sanding. ([nettoyant de surface]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[volatile organic compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[volatile organic compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
    [[VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means volatile organic compounds that
    participate in atmospheric photochemical reactions and that are not
    excluded compounds. ([composés organiques volatils]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"} ou [COV]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749433 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply in respect of any product
  containing volatile organic compounds set out in column 1 of the
  schedule, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product is to be used for automotive
    refinishing; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} anywhere on the product's container, or in any
    documentation relating to the product supplied by the product's
    manufacturer, importer or seller or their duly authorized
    representative, it is indicated that the product may be used for
    automotive refinishing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of
  automotive refinishing products set out in column 1 of the schedule
  that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold
    for the purposes of export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used for application in or on the premises of a
    factory or a shop, for purposes other than automotive refinishing,
    on products other than motor vehicles, mobile equipment, or their
    parts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported, offered for sale or sold in a
    non-refillable aerosol spray container or manufactured or imported
    to be packaged in that type of container;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} imported, offered for sale or sold in a container
    with a capacity of 14.8 mL (0.5 fl oz) or less or manufactured or
    imported to be packaged in that size of container;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be applied to motor vehicles or mobile equipment, or their parts,
    during manufacture;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be used as a solvent in a laboratory for analysis;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be used in scientific research;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be used as a laboratory sample or analytical standard;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold
    for use in chemical agent resistant coatings for motor vehicles,
    mobile equipment or their parts, for use in a military operation; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold in
    a container with a capacity of 118.3 mL (4.0 fl oz) or less or
    manufactured or imported to be packaged in that size of container,
    for use in automobile mobile restoration services.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (2)(i), [[military
  operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any operation undertaken to
  protect national security, support humanitarian relief efforts,
  participate in multilateral military or peace-keeping activities under
  the auspices of international organizations or defend a member state
  of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749454 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture or import

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import any automotive refinishing product set
  out in column 1 of the schedule if its VOC concentration exceeds the
  limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that product unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product is required to be diluted before use to
    a VOC concentration equal to or less than that limit set out in
    column 2 and the manufacturer, importer or seller, as the case may
    be, specifies the instructions for dilution on the product's label
    or accompanying documentation, in both official languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person that manufactures or imports has been
    issued a permit under section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sell or offer for sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not sell or offer for sale any
  automotive refinishing product set out in column 1 of the schedule if
  its VOC concentration exceeds the limit set out in column 2 for that
  product unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product is required to be diluted before use to
    a VOC concentration equal to or less than that limit set out in
    column 2 and the manufacturer, importer or seller, as the case may
    be, specifies the instructions for dilution on the product's label
    or accompanying documentation, in both official languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product was manufactured or imported under a
    permit issued under section 5 and the sale or offer to sell occurs
    no later than one year after the day on which the permit expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combination of multiple components

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if the written instructions of
  the manufacturer, importer or seller require the combination of
  multiple components before the product is to be used, the VOC
  concentration in the product resulting from the combination of the
  multiple components must not exceed the VOC concentration limit set
  out in column 2 of the schedule for that product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lowest VOC concentration limit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If anywhere on the container of a coating set out in
  the schedule, or in any documentation relating to the coating supplied
  by the manufacturer, importer, seller or their duly authorized
  representative, it is indicated that the coating may be used for the
  purpose of a different coating set out in the schedule, then the
  lowest VOC concentration limit applies.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749468 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749469 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement for permit

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports a product set out in column 1 of
  the schedule, other than a product referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a),
  or the components of such a product that must be combined together
  before its use in which the VOC concentration exceeds the limit set
  out in column 2 for that product, must hold a permit issued under
  section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a permit must be submitted to the
  Minister and contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the applicant,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
      number and, if any, fax number and e-mail address,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
      telephone number and, if any, fax number and e-mail address of
      their duly authorized representative, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its trade name, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its VOC concentration,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated quantity to be manufactured,
      sold, offered for sale or imported in a calendar year and the unit
      of measurement,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the category of the product as set out in the
      schedule and the information on which the selection of the product
      category was made, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for renewal of a
      permit under subsection 5(3), the number of the existing permit
      that was issued under section 5;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that it is not technically or economically
    feasible for the applicant at the time of the application to reduce
    the VOC concentration in the product if the limit set out in column
    2 of the schedule is not met for that product;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the plan prepared identifying the
    measures that will be taken by the applicant so that the VOC
    concentration in the product to be manufactured or imported will be
    within the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that
    product;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} identification of the period within which the plan
    is to be fully implemented; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the civic and postal addresses of the location
    where information, supporting documents and the certification
    referred to in subsection (3), are kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a
  certification dated and signed by the applicant or by their duly
  authorized representative, stating that the information contained in
  the application is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format for submission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The application and certification may be submitted
  either in writing or in an electronic format that is compatible with
  the format that is used by the Minister, and the documents must bear
  the written or electronic signature, as the case may be, of the
  applicant or their duly authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on receiving an application made
  under this section, require further details that pertain to the
  information contained in the application and that are necessary for
  the application to be processed.

### [Conditions of Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749494 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuing permit

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister must issue the permit if the following
  conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has provided evidence that, at the
    time of the application, it is not technically or economically
    feasible for the applicant to reduce the VOC concentration in the
    product to the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that
    product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has prepared a plan to identify the
    measures that will be taken by the applicant such that the VOC
    concentration in the product for manufacture or import is within the
    limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that product; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the plan is to be fully
    implemented does not exceed four years after the day on which the
    first permit is issued to the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for refusing permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the applicant has provided false or misleading information in
    support of their application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information required under subsections 4(2) or (5)
    has not been provided or is insufficient to enable the Minister to
    process the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry and permit renewal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit expires 24 months after the day on which it
  is issued unless, within the period of 30 days before the day on which
  the permit expires, the applicant submits an application for renewal
  in accordance with section 4. The validity of the first permit may
  only be extended once for an additional 24 months for a given product
  and the same use.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-749509 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for revocation

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must revoke a permit if he or she has reasonable grounds to
  believe that the implementation of the plan referred to in paragraph
  5(1)(b) will not be completed within the specified period or that the
  permit holder has provided false or misleading information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not revoke a permit unless the
  Minister has provided the permit holder with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any written reasons for the revocation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to be heard, by written
    representation, in respect of the revocation.

## [Determination of VOC Concentration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automotive refinishing coating

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The VOC concentration in a
coating as set out in the schedule is determined by the following
equation:

::: {#749520 .Section}
VOC concentration = (Ws -- Ww -- Wec) ÷ (Vm -- Vw -- Vec)

where

[VOC concentration]{.dfn}
:   is the grams of VOC per litre of coating, undiluted or diluted in
    accordance with the written instructions of the manufacturer,
    importer or seller if required before the product is to be used;

[Ws]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of volatiles, in grams;

[Ww]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of water, in grams;

[Wec]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of excluded compounds, in grams;

[Vm]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of coating, in litres;

[Vw]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of water, in litres; and

[Vec]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of excluded compounds, in litres.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surface cleaner

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The VOC concentration in a
surface cleaner as set out in the schedule is determined by the
following equation:

::: {#749531 .Section}
VOC concentration = (Ws -- Ww -- Wec) ÷ Vm

where

[VOC concentration]{.dfn}
:   is the grams of VOC compounds per litre of surface cleaner,
    undiluted or diluted in accordance with the written instructions of
    the manufacturer, importer or seller if required before the product
    is to be used;

[Ws]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of volatiles, in grams;

[Ww]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of water, in grams;

[Wec]{.dfn}
:   is the weight of excluded compounds, in grams; and

[Vm]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of surface cleaner, in litres.
:::

## [Accredited Laboratory]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749538 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any laboratory that
performs an analysis for the purposes of these Regulations must be
accredited under the International Organization for Standardization
standard ISO/IEC 17025:2005, entitled General requirements for the
competence of testing and calibration laboratories, as amended from time
to time, and its accreditation must include the analysis in question
within its scope of testing.

## [Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749541 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture date --- manufacturer or

  offered for sale or sold, the date on which the product was
  manufactured or a code representing that date. If a code is used, the
  manufacturer or importer must provide the Minister, on request, with
  an explanation of the code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture date --- seller

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that offers for sale or sells any product
  set out in the schedule must, if the manufacturer or importer has
  failed to indicate the date or code referred to in subsection (1) on
  the container in which the product is to be offered for sale, or sold,
  indicate that date or that code on the container and must provide the
  Minister, on request, with an explanation of the code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions for dilution ---
manufacturer or importer

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  product set out in column 1 of the schedule requires dilution before
  its use, the manufacturer or importer must ensure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the product's label or the accompanying
    documentation specifies the instructions for the recommended
    dilution in both official languages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any instructions for dilution appearing on the
    product's label or in any accompanying documentation would not
    result in dilution of the product to a concentration greater than
    the VOC concentration limit set out in column 2 for that product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions for combination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a multiple component product requires that
  components be combined before its use, the manufacturer or importer
  must ensure that the product's label or the accompanying documentation
  specifies, in both official languages, the instructions for the
  recommended combinations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions for dilution --- seller

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  product set out in column 1 of the schedule requires dilution before
  its use, the seller must ensure

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the product's label or the accompanying
    documentation specifies the instructions for the recommended
    dilution in both official languages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that any instructions for dilution appearing on the
    product's label or in any accompanying documentation would not
    result in dilution of the product to a concentration greater than
    the VOC concentration limit set out in column 2 for that product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions for combination

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a multiple component product requires that
  components be combined before its use, the seller must ensure that the
  product's label or the accompanying documentation specifies, in both
  official languages, the instructions for the recommended combinations.

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures, imports or sells a product set out in the
  schedule must maintain records containing the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that manufactures,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product manufactured at each
      manufacturing plant,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the trademark and trade name of the manufactured
      product, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the product's manufacture;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that imports,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product imported,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the trademark and trade name of the product
      imported,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry where the product was
      imported,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic or postal address, telephone
      number, and, if any, the fax number and e-mail address of the
      principal place of business of the sender,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date of import of the product,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding
      System number for the product,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the importer number for the product shipped,
      and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the copies of the bill of lading, invoice and
      all documents submitted to the Canada Border Services Agency for
      the product shipped;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that sells to a supplier,
    wholesaler or retailer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product sold,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the trademark and trade name of the product
      sold,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the sale of the product,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the delivery date of the product, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic or postal address of each
      supplier, wholesaler and retailer to whom the product was sold.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping and time limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person that submits the information set out in
  subsection 4(2) must keep a record of that information, supporting
  documents and the certification referred to in subsection 4(3) for a
  period of at least five years after the day on which they are
  submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location and duration of records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The records, supporting documents and the
  certification as referred to in subsection 4(3) must be kept, for a
  period of at least five years after the day on which they are made, at
  the person's principal place of business in Canada or at any other
  place in Canada where they can be inspected. If the records are kept
  at any place other than the person's principal place of business, the
  person must provide the Minister with the civic address of the place
