## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1119138 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[level I air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[level I air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 3(1) of the [Transportation Information
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-334). ([transporteur aérien de
    niveau I]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1119143 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees --- notice

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If a notice is given to the
Minister under subsection 53.71(1) of the Act in respect of a proposed
arrangement involving at least one level I air carrier, the following
fee must be paid by one of the parties to the proposed arrangement:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$28,000, if the proposed arrangement has two
  parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$36,000, if the proposed arrangement has three or
  more parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees --- review process

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a proposed arrangement
involving at least one level I air carrier is subject to the review
process set out in section 53.73 of the Act, the following fee must be
paid by one of the parties to the proposed arrangement:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$252,000, if the proposed arrangement has two
  parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$304,000, if the proposed arrangement has three or
  more parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of fees

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** All fees are to be paid in
full by certified cheque to the Receiver General for Canada as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fees set out in section 2 are payable at the time a
  notice is given under subsection 53.71(1) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fees set out in section 3 are payable within five
  days after the day on which the Minister informs the parties, under
  subsection 53.71(6) of the Act, that the proposed arrangement raises
  significant considerations with respect to the public interest.
