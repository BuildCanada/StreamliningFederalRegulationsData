## Canadian Industrial Renewal Regulations {#canadian-industrial-renewal-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-850
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRY ACT](/eng/acts/I-9.2)

Registration 1981-10-15

Regulations Respecting Canadian Industrial Renewal

P.C. 1981-2916 1981-10-15
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, the Minister of Regional
Economic Expansion and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 7 of the
Department of Industry, Trade and Commerce Act and Vote 1a (Industry,
Trade and Commerce) of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting Canadian Industrial
Renewal](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-850).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872327 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Industrial Renewal
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-850).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872329 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan application, an
    insurance application or a contribution application made pursuant to
    these Regulations; ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/86-718, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an eligible
    manufacturer or a designated business; ([entreprise
    commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any contribution
    authorized under a vote of an Appropriation Act;
    ([contribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person carrying
    on a manufacturing or processing activity or other commercial
    activity in a designated community; ([entreprise
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area
    designated for the purposes of these Regulations pursuant to section
    6 of the Department of Regional Economic Expansion Act; ([communauté
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a manufacturer
    engaged or about to engage in an eligible manufacturing or
    processing activity; ([fabricant admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible manufacturing or processing
    activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity whereby goods or
    services are manufactured or provided by establishments that are
    classified or would normally be classified under any of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 3, Establishments under S.I.C. 1624,
      Rubber Footwear Manufacturers,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 4,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Establishments under S.I.C. 172, Leather
        Tanneries,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Establishments under S.I.C. 174, Shoe
        Factories,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Establishments under S.I.C. 175 that are
        primarily engaged in manufacturing gloves and mittens of
        leather,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 5, Textile Industries, other than
      Establishments under S.I.C. 1899, Miscellaneous Textile
      Industries, n.e.s. that are primarily engaged in the manufacture
      of hygiene products and whose manufacturing of textile materials
      used in the manufacture of hygiene products is solely for the
      purposes of manufacturing their own hygiene products,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 6, Knitting Industries,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 7, Clothing Industries, or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Major Group 20,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Establishments under S.I.C. 3931, Sporting
        Goods Manufacturers, where the manufacture of sporting,
        recreational or athletic footwear is a substantial part of
        production,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Establishments under S.I.C. 3993 that are
        primarily engaged in manufacturing coated fabrics, including oil
        cloth, leatherette and water repellant fabrics other than
        rubberized fabrics,

    of Division 5, Manufacturing Industries of the Standard Industrial
    Classification (S.I.C.) of 1970; ([travaux de fabrication ou de
    transformation admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[external or other business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[external or other business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    that carries on a manufacturing or processing activity or other
    commercial activity at a place other than a designated community or
    a person that intends to carry on a manufacturing or processing
    activity or other commercial activity and that is prepared to carry
    on a manufacturing or processing activity or other commercial
    activity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a designated community, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a community, other than a designated
      community, that has a population of less than 100,000 and is
      located within a Canada Employment Centre area where employment in
      eligible manufacturing accounts for at least 20 per cent of
      manufacturing employment and 5 per cent of total employment and
      that has been severely affected by a major shutdown or layoff in
      an eligible manufacturing or processing activity; ([entreprise
      externe ou autre entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in a
    manufacturing or processing activity in Canada;
    ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturing or processing activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    an activity whereby any goods, products, commodities or wares

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are made, fabricated, processed or refined out of
      any raw material or other substance or combination thereof,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are converted or rebuilt, but not repaired, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} are made by causing any raw material or other
      substance to undergo a significant chemical, biochemical or
      physical change including change that preserves or improves the
      keeping qualities of that raw material or other substance but
      excluding change by growth or decay; ([travaux de fabrication ou
      de transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual, partnership,
    association, body corporate, trustee, executor, administrator or
    legal representative but does not include an individual,
    partnership, association, body corporate, trustee, executor,
    administrator or legal representative who is primarily engaged in
    pulp and paper manufacturing falling under group 271 of Division E,
    Manufacturing Industries of the Standard Industrial Classification
    (S.I.C.) of 1980; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financially sound and
    reliable lender other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by that government or any agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any municipal corporation; ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a change that is
    significant in the operations of a business with respect to its
    products, methods of production, markets or management procedures
    and includes, if directly related to such operations, the
    acquisition of working capital or the acquisition, construction or
    conversion of machinery, equipment, buildings, land or other
    facilities. ([restructuration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  Minister shall administer the loans, insurance and contributions made
  or authorized under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on behalf of Her Majesty, enter
  into and execute any agreement necessary to give effect to these
  Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in respect of any loan, insurance
  or contribution made or authorized under these Regulations, require
  such information and documentation and make such stipulations as may
  be necessary in relation to such loan, insurance or contribution.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, subject to these Regulations, grant
  loans, provide insurance or make contributions under these Regulations
  only where an applicant would likely be unable to implement a project
  without the assistance of the Minister.

  appropriate to facilitate the efficient operation of these
  Regulations, to minimize any loss suffered or threatened to be
  suffered by Her Majesty arising out of any loan or insurance
  administered by the Minister pursuant to these Regulations and to
  maximize the potential economic gains to Canada arising from any
  contribution administered by the Minister pursuant to these
  Regulations.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Loans]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872380 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7 where,
pursuant to these Regulations, the Minister has authorized the provision
of insurance on a loan made by a private lender to a person, the
Minister may make a loan in an amount not exceeding, in the aggregate,
50 per cent of the amount of the private lender's loan to that person on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the private lender has agreed to make the loan for
  which the Minister has authorized the provision of insurance pursuant
  to the terms and conditions set by the Minister in accordance with
  these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loan made by the Minister shall bear interest at
  a rate of not less than the aggregate of interest to be charged by the
  private lender on the insured loan and the insurance fee thereon;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the loan is essential to prevent a serious delay in
  implementing the program for which the loan by the private lender was
  obtained;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the loan shall be repaid on the date of the first
  disbursement of the loan advanced by the private lender or on such
  date as may be set out in the loan agreement between the Minister and
  the applicant; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister shall specify the date on which the loan
  shall be repaid in the event that a disbursement to repay the loan has
  not been made by the private lender within a reasonable time as
  established by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 3(F)
- SOR/86-718, ss. 3, 24
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7, where
a person or a trustee or receiver authorized by law to carry on the
activities of that person has previously obtained assistance by way of a
loan or insurance on a loan made or authorized under these Regulations,
the Minister may make a loan to that person or to the trustee or
receiver for the purpose of protecting the Crown's interest in respect
of the loan previously made or insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  person submits adequate plans, developed with the assistance of a
  qualified consultant, that are based on a feasibility study made by a
  qualified consultant for the merger with or acquisition of a business
  enterprise and the person that submits the plans requires a loan

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to purchase the effective ownership control of the
    business enterprise or the whole or a significant portion of the
    assets of the business enterprise that are related to its activity,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to effect the merger or amalgamation with the
    business enterprise,

  the Minister may make a loan to that person in an amount not exceeding
  \$1.5 million.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where a person
  establishes that the person is capable of undertaking a feasibility
  study and developing plans described in that subsection, the
  feasibility study may be undertaken and the plans developed without
  the assistance of a qualified consultant.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 4(F)
- SOR/86-718, s. 4
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall grant a

### [Application for a Loan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872404 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that requires a loan in accordance with these Regulations shall apply
  therefor to the Minister and shall provide such information as may be
  necessary in order to process the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an appplication for a loan is approved by the
  Minister, the applicant shall enter into a loan agreement with Her
  Majesty.

::: HistoricalNote

:::

### [Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872410 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The rate of interest for
any loan made pursuant to section 5 or 6 shall be determined by the
Minister as of the day the application therefor is approved by the
Minister and shall be not less than the rate of interest charged by the
Government of Canada to Crown corporations for loans of a similar term
nor higher than 3 per cent above the rate.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

### [Term of Loan and Repayment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872414 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A loan
  made pursuant to these Regulations shall be for such term, not
  exceeding 20 years, as may be fixed by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The whole or any part of a loan made pursuant to
  these Regulations may be repaid in advance of the repayment date in
  accordance with the terms of the loan agreement and without notice,
  bonus or penalty.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 5(F)
- SOR/86-718, ss. 6, 24
:::

### [Security]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872420 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  the appropriate security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may surrender, retransfer or reconvey
  any security obtained or held by it pursuant to subsection (1) in
  exchange for other security or amend the provisions thereof.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may designate any trustee, receiver,
  receiver-manager or other person required to be appointed by the
  Minister under the security referred to in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 6(F)
- SOR/86-718, ss. 7, 24
:::

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872427 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872428 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 17,
the Minister may provide insurance on a loan made by a private lender to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a business enterprise if the business enterprise
  requires the loan to restructure its operations in order to improve
  its position in international trade competition;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an eligible manufacturer if the eligible manufacturer
  requires the loan to engage in the manufacture of products that will
  be subject to international trade competition;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a designated business if the designated business is
  about to engage in a manufacturing or processing activity or other
  commercial activity and will be subject to international trade
  competition; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person if the person requires a loan to acquire a
  corporation or group of corporations engaged in an eligible
  manufacturing or processing activity or located in a designated
  community and the acquisition will result in a significant increase in
  manufacturing or processing activities or other commercial activities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 7(F)
- SOR/86-718, s. 8
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 17,
the Minister may provide insurance on a loan made by a private lender to
a business enterprise or a trustee or receiver authorized by law to
carry on the activities of the business enterprise if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the business enterprise is subject to international
  trade competition;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the business enterprise is in financial difficulty
  that threatens the cessation of a significant portion of its
  operations and the layoff of a significant number of its employees;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business enterprise proposes the sale of all or a
  significant portion of its assets or the person that holds effective
  ownership control of the business enterprise proposes the sale of such
  control and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the business enterprise requires a loan to continue
    its operations until the assets are sold or effective ownership
    control of the business enterprise is sold, as the case may be,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sale will be effected within a reasonable
    period of time, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the sale will enhance the prospects for the
    continued employment of a significant number of its employees.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, ss. 9, 24
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the
  Minister may provide insurance on a loan made by a private lender to a
  person if the person requires the loan to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} acquire effective ownership control of a business
    enterprise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acquire from a business enterprise the whole or a
    significant portion of the assets of the business enterprise; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} restructure its operations as a result of the
    acquisition by the person of the whole or a significant portion of
    the assets of a business enterprise.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the Minister may provide
  insurance on a loan made by a private lender to a business enterprise
  in Canada to restructure its operations if the business enterprise
  requires the loan to restructure its operations as a result of the
  acquisition of its effective ownership control by a person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may provide insurance pursuant to
  subsection (1) or (2) on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person referred to in subsection (1) or the
    business enterprise referred to in subsection (2) that requires a
    loan has agreed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to provide or to cause to be provided to the
      private lender an appropriate guarantee for 10 per cent of the
      amount of the loan to be provided, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to provide the private lender with appropriate
      security on the assets acquired or on the assets of the
      corporation the effective ownership control of which has been
      acquired; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the acquisition or restructuring in respect of
    which the loan is required

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is essential to prevent a serious disruption in
      the activity of the corporation acquired or the activity resulting
      from the use of the assets acquired,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will result in the growth, efficiency or
      international competitiveness of a manufacturing or processing
      activity or other commercial activity in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} would not proceed unless the loan required is
      insured by the Minister, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} would not proceed unless the Minister and the
      private lender agree to limit the liability of the person referred
      to in subsection (1) or the business enterprise referred to in
      subsection (2) to the lender to 10 per cent of the amount of the
      loan to be provided.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 8
- SOR/86-718, s. 10
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 17,
the Minister may provide insurance on a loan made by a private lender to
a person engaged or about to engage in a business in Canada that
provides services, directly or indirectly, to a business enterprise in
Canada that is engaged in international trade competition if that person
requires the loan to establish, restructure or improve its operations in
order to improve the position of the business enterprise in
international trade competition.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 10
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 17,
where a person or a trustee or receiver authorized by law to carry on
the activities of that person has previously obtained assistance by way
of a loan or insurance on a loan made or authorized under these
Regulations, the Minister may provide insurance on a loan to that
person, trustee or receiver for the purpose of protecting the Crown's
interest in respect of the loan previously made or insured.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

### [Condition of Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872468 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall grant
insurance pursuant to sections 12, 13, 15 and 16 only where the business
enterprise or person referred to in those sections that requires the
loan is unable to obtain sufficient financing on reasonable terms unless
the loan is insured by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

### [Amount of Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872472 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Insurance provided by the
Minister pursuant to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 12, 14 or 15 shall not exceed 90 per cent, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 13 or 16 shall not exceed 100 per cent

of the amount of the loan in respect of which it is provided.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-643, s. 9(F)
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

### [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872479 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A private lender who
wishes the Minister to provide insurance on a loan in accordance with
these Regulations shall apply therefor to the Minister and shall provide
such information as may be necessary to process the application.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 11
:::

### [Reduction of Insurance and Insurance Fee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872483 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of any insurance provided by the Minister pursuant to these
  Regulations may be reduced at the request of the private lender at
  such times and in such manner as may be agreed to by the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The insurance fee payable by a private lender shall
  be 1 per cent per annum of the amount of insurance in force from time
  to time and shall be paid in advance to the Minister in semi-annual
  installments.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (2), the Minister may, in
  respect of insurance provided pursuant to section 13, 14 or 16, waive
  or reduce the amount and vary the terms of payment of the insurance
  fee accordingly.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the amount of a loan made by a private lender is
  advanced in periodic advances, the Minister may, if required by the
  private lender, provide insurance pursuant to these Regulations on
  such advances.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, ss. 12, 24
:::

### [Payment of Insurance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-872491 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender
has demanded repayment of a loan in respect of which insurance has been
provided pursuant to these Regulations, the amount payable to the
private lender shall not exceed the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such
  demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a loan insured pursuant to section 12 or 15, 90 per
    cent of the loss incurred by the private lender, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a loan insured pursuant to section 13 or 16, 100
    per cent of the loss incurred by the private lender.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
21, where a private lender has demanded repayment of a loan in respect
of which insurance has been provided by the Minister pursuant to
section 14, the amount payable to the private lender shall not exceed
the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such
  demand; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 90 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender excluding any amount recovered or recoverable under the
  guarantee obtained by the private lender in accordance with
  subparagraph 14(3)(a)(i).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Contributions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-872502 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
administer contributions in accordance with the provisions of this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-718, s. 24
:::

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make
contributions to a business enterprise in respect of any of the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs of engaging the services of a qualified
  consultant to develop a proposal to qualify for a loan or loan
  insurance under these Regulations or contributions under paragraph (c)
  or (d);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs of engaging the services of a qualified
  consultant to identify new or improved products or to conduct product
  testing or market testing on such products;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the costs of development and design of a new or
  improved and technologically advanced product, process or service

  technically feasible but represent technical risk;

  mass-produced durable product through an expansion of the industrial
  design program of the business enterprise where the industrial design
  activity is directed by a qualified industrial designer;

  consultant to develop a market strategy or market feasibility study to
  exploit a project in respect of which a loan, loan insurance or
  contribution was made or authorized by the Minister under these
  Regulations;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the costs of engaging the services of a qualified
  consultant to undertake a study to determine the feasibility of
  implementing plans for a project to significantly enhance the
  productivity or efficiency of the project where

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the enhancement project involves a significant

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} there exists a potential for significant
    productivity or efficiency gains and a demonstrable uncertainty as
