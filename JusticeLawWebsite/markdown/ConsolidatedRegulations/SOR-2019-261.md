## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179118 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Special Import Measures
    Act](/eng/acts/S-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[large diameter line pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[large diameter line pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [[subject
    goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in paragraph 4 of the
    Statement of Reasons of the Canadian International Trade Tribunal
    issued on November 4, 2016 in Inquiry Number NQ-2016-001. ([tubes de
    canalisation à gros diamètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remissions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179122 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anti-dumping duties

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted to a company set out in column
  1 of the schedule of the amount of anti-dumping duties, set out in
  column 2, that are paid or payable under the Act, by or on behalf of
  the company, in respect of large diameter line pipe, if the
  importation was recorded under the transaction number set out in
  column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is made to the
    Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
    years after the day on which this Order comes into force; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request by the Canada Border Services Agency,
    the applicant files the evidence or information that is required by
    the Agency to determine eligibility for remission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anti-dumping duties

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted to Trans Mountain Pipeline
  L.P. of the amount of anti-dumping duties that are paid or payable
  under the Act, by or on behalf of the company, in respect of large
  diameter line pipe, up to the amount of anti-dumping duties that are
  paid or payable under the Act in respect of the importation of
  2,963,565 kg of large diameter line pipe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted on the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is made to the
    Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
    years after the day of importation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request by the Canada Border Services Agency,
    the applicant files the evidence or information that is required by
    the Agency to determine eligibility for remission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount claimed in the application is not
    subject to remission under section 2;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the large diameter line pipe is supplied by JFE
    Steel Corporation; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the large diameter line pipe is for use in the
    Trans Mountain Expansion Project.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1179140 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
