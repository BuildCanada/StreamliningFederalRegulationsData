## [Interpretation, Definitions and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814269 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of these Rules is to enable the parties to a dispute to
  achieve a resolution that is commercially fair and reasonable to the
  parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Just, expeditious, inexpensive

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Rules are to be interpreted and applied in a
  manner that facilitates, through arbitration, the just, expeditious
  and inexpensive resolution of the dispute.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day on which the Agency
    is ordinarily open for business. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    day. ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any information recorded
    or saved in any form. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Rules set out the
procedure for an arbitration conducted under Division II of Part IV of
the Act.

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814283 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dispensing and amending

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  arbitrator may dispense with or amend any of these Rules if to do so
  will improve the efficiency and effectiveness of the arbitration
  procedure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator may extend or abridge any time limit
  established under these Rules either before or after the time limit
  expires.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If any information relating
to the arbitration is to be kept confidential, every person who will
have access to the information in the course of the arbitration must
sign a confidentiality agreement before they obtain access to the
information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Last day

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The last day for doing an
act or meeting any other requirement under these Rules applies even if
that day is not a business day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of arbitration --- agreement of
parties

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  parties are in agreement on the official language in which the
  arbitration is to be conducted, they must advise the Agency
  accordingly by submitting a written notice along with each of their
  proposals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determined by arbitrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the parties fail to provide notice to the Agency,
  the language of the arbitration is to be determined by the arbitrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translation services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party that requires translation services in order
  to consult any document produced in either official language by the
  other party must make arrangements to obtain those services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simultaneous translation services

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party that requires simultaneous translation
  services in order to participate in the arbitration, or in order that
  one of their witnesses may present evidence in the language of the
  arbitration, must, at least seven days before the services are
  required, provide notice in writing of the requirement to the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Providing documents

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Documents to be provided to a party
  under these Rules must be delivered or transmitted by email or
  facsimile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delivery --- effective

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Delivery of a document to a party is effective on the
  day on which the document is received at the address of the party or
  of their legal counsel or other authorized representative, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Email or facsimile --- effective

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Email or facsimile transmission of a document is
  effective at the time that the email or facsimile is transmitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irregular transmission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a document that is transmitted by email or
  facsimile is not received or is only partially received, the sender
  must, as soon as feasible after receiving a request from the
  recipient, deliver a paper copy of the document to the recipient.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral communication with arbitrator

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  or their legal counsel or authorized representative must not speak
  with the arbitrator in the absence of the other party or the other
  party's legal counsel or authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written communication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party or their legal counsel or authorized
  representative must not write to the arbitrator without providing a
  copy of the communication to the other party at the same time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitrator's mandatory disclosure

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Within 24 hours after the
day on which a matter is referred for arbitration under
subsection 169.35(1) of the Act, the arbitrator must provide a signed
declaration to the parties that discloses any situation of which he or
she is aware that could place him or her in a conflict of interest or
that could give rise to a reasonable apprehension of bias in the context
of the arbitration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lack of impartiality or conflict of
interest

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party is of the view that an arbitrator is unable to perform his or
  her duties impartially or is in a conflict of interest in the context
  of the arbitration, the party must advise the Agency by written notice
  without delay, setting out the reasons for their claim.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision within three days

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson must decide on the claim and notify
  the parties within three business days after the day on which written
  notice of the claim is received by the Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substitution

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Chairperson must appoint a substitute arbitrator as soon as feasible
  after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the arbitrator becomes unable to continue to
    perform his or her duties;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arbitrator informs the Agency that he or she is
    no longer able to act as arbitrator or considers himself or herself
    unable to perform the duties of arbitrator without giving rise to a
    reasonable apprehension of bias or because of a conflict of
    interest; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Chairperson decides, following written notice
    from a party, that the arbitrator cannot continue to perform his or
    her duties without giving rise to a reasonable apprehension of bias
    or because of a conflict of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings to be repeated

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an arbitrator is replaced, any hearings previously
  held must be repeated.

## [General Powers of Arbitrator]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814331 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Role of arbitrator

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The arbitrator may, among
other things,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} encourage and assist the parties in settling the
  dispute or any of the matters in dispute at all times until the end of
  the first pre-arbitration meeting;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} order the adjournment of the arbitration, if
  required;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} order the inspection of documents, goods or other
  property, including a site visit;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} give directions on questions of procedure that do not
  deal with the substance of the dispute, including those that arise in
  the course of pre-arbitration meetings;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} request further statements to clarify matters in
  dispute;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} in exceptional circumstances and if there is no
  prejudice to the parties, dispense with an oral hearing and order that
  all arguments and evidence be presented in writing; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} as required, appoint one or more independent external
  experts to report on specific issues.

## [Steps Preceding Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814341 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First pre-arbitration meeting

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  four days after the day on which the arbitrator is chosen in
  accordance with subsection 169.35(1) of the Act, he or she must
  convene a pre-arbitration meeting for the purpose, among other things,
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} encouraging settlement by the parties of the
    dispute, or of any matter in dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} clarifying matters in dispute;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining whether there is consensus between the
    parties on any relevant facts, and if so, ordering the parties to
    produce an agreed statement of facts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} determining the time required for the arbitration
    hearing, which should normally not exceed five days;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fixing the date of the arbitration hearing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} establishing a timetable for the completion of all
    pre-arbitration matters, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of arbitration briefs,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of questions referred to in
      subsection 169.36(3) of the Act, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the holding of a second pre-arbitration
      meeting, if the arbitrator considers it appropriate;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establishing the order of proceedings at the
    arbitration hearing; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} deciding any other procedural matters in respect of
    the arbitration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The meeting may be conducted in person or by means of
  electronic communication, such as webcasting, video conferencing or
  teleconferencing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meeting summary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within three days after the day on which the meeting
  takes place, the arbitrator must provide a summary of the meeting to
  the parties that outlines, among other things, any agreements reached
  and decisions rendered, the time, date and place, if applicable, of
  the second pre-arbitration meeting and the time, date and place of the
  arbitration hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange of arbitration briefs

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  party must provide their arbitration brief to the arbitrator and to
  the other party in accordance with the timetable established at the
  first pre-arbitration meeting.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each party's arbitration brief must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the material facts in support of the party's
    position;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the agreed statement of facts ordered by the
    arbitrator, if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that the party intends to
    produce at the arbitration hearing in support of their position and
    that is selected from information exchanged under
    subsection 169.34(3) of the Act, including, in respect of any
    written opinion from an expert who is to give evidence at the
    hearing,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the qualifications of the expert,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the expert's opinion, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the facts upon which the expert's opinion is
      based;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the oral evidence to be presented
    at the arbitration hearing or signed witness statements; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the arguments in support of the party's position,
    set out in concise numbered paragraphs, including references linking
    each argument to the relevant evidence referred to in paragraph (c)
    or (d).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to deliver arbitration brief

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party that fails to deliver an arbitration brief in
  accordance with the established timetable is not entitled to
  participate further in the arbitration and the arbitration continues
  without that arbitration brief.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Optional second pre-arbitration meeting

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  second pre-arbitration meeting is to be held, the arbitrator must
  convene it for the purpose, among other things, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} further clarifying matters in dispute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} confirming the time required for the arbitration
    hearing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} settling an agreed statement of facts, if
    applicable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} determining which witnesses will attend the
    arbitration hearing and the dates and times of their attendance;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} confirming the order of proceedings at the
    arbitration hearing; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} deciding any other procedural matters in respect of
    the arbitration.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type of meeting

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The meeting may be conducted in person or by means of
  electronic communication, such as webcasting, video conferencing or
  teleconferencing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meeting summary

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within three days after the day on which the meeting
  takes place, the arbitrator must provide a summary of the meeting to
  the parties that outlines, among other things, any agreements reached
  and any decisions rendered.

## [Arbitration Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814388 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Place of arbitration hearing

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the arbitration hearing is to be held at the head office of the
  Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic means

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator may, if circumstances justify it,
  conduct all or part of the hearing by means of electronic
  communication, such as webcasting, video conferencing or
  teleconferencing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of location

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} On the request of a party, the arbitrator may conduct
  all or any part of the arbitration hearing at another location for the
  purpose of hearing witnesses, experts or the parties or of inspecting
  documents, goods or other property, if he or she determines that it
  would be more practical or that it is necessary to do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transcript of arbitration hearing

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  transcript of the arbitration hearing is to be taken, unless requested
  by the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to arbitrator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a transcript is requested by the parties, they
  must provide a copy to the arbitrator as soon as it is available.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final oral argument

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Subject to
subsection 15(3) and section 23, each party must be permitted to present
final oral arguments at the arbitration hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Information that has not
been exchanged under subsection 169.34(3) of the Act is not to be
introduced as evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of examination

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  manner in which witnesses are to be examined is to be determined by
  the arbitrator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witness

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the request of a party, the arbitrator may require
  a witness to be absent from the arbitration hearing during the
  testimony of other witnesses.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signed witness statement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If it was included in the party's arbitration brief,
  a signed witness statement may replace the examination in chief of a
  party's witness, in which case the witness is subject only to
  cross-examination and re-examination in respect of the statement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Absence of party

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} All witness testimony is to be given in the presence
  of the arbitrator and, except if a party is voluntarily absent or has
  defaulted in the context of subsection 15(3), in the presence of both
  parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative to witness attendance

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** The arbitrator may, on
the agreement of both parties, accept in evidence a signed witness
statement or the affidavit of a witness in lieu of the attendance of the
witness at the arbitration hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Default of party

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The arbitrator will
continue the arbitration hearing despite the absence of a party if the
party, without sufficient cause and in the absence of notice to the
arbitrator, fails to appear at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Close of hearing

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The arbitrator may close
the arbitration hearing if the parties, after inquiry by the arbitrator,
confirm that they have no further evidence to present or submissions to
make or if the arbitrator determines that, because he or she has
sufficient understanding of the matters and of each party's position, it
is unnecessary to continue the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of arbitration hearing

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The record of the
arbitration consists of the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the written notice referred to in
  paragraph 169.33(1)(a) of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the shipper's submission for arbitration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} each party's proposal;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} each party's arbitration brief;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} exhibits produced at the hearing; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any applicable order made by the Agency under
  section 169.43 of the Act.

## [Arbitrator's Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Arbitration decision

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  arbitrator's decision must be made within seven days after the day on
  which the arbitration hearing ends.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of decision to parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator must provide each party with a signed
  copy of the decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minor corrections

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Within two business days
after the day on which they receive the arbitrator's decision, a party
may apply to the arbitrator to correct

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a clerical or typographical error;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an accidental error, slip, omission or other similar
  mistake; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an error in calculation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-814439 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
