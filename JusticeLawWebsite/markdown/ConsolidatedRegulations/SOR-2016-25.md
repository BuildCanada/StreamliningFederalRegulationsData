## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834408 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
Act](/eng/acts/O-7).

## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834411 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions of Act and regulations

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contravention of a provision of the Act or of a regulation made under
  the Act that is set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1 is
  designated as a violation that may be proceeded with in accordance
  with sections 71.01 to 72.02 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions, requirements, decisions and
  orders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contravention of a direction, requirement,
  decision or order made under the Act is designated as a violation that
  may be proceeded with in accordance with sections 71.01 to 72.02 of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms, conditions and requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The contravention of a term, condition or requirement
  of an operating licence or authorization, or of an approval, leave or
  exemption granted, under the Act is designated as a violation that may
  be proceeded with in accordance with sections 71.01 to 72.02 of the
  Act.

## [Classification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provisions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  contravention of a provision that is set out in column 1 of a Part of
  Schedule 1 is a Type A or Type B violation as set out in column 2 of
  that Part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions, requirements, decisions,
  orders, terms and conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The contravention of a direction, requirement,
  decision or order referred to in subsection 2(2) or of a term,
  condition or requirement referred to in subsection 2(3) is a Type B
  violation.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  penalty for a violation with a total gravity value set out in column 1
  of Schedule 2 is, in the case of a Type A violation, the corresponding
  amount set out in column 2 and, in the case of a Type B violation, the
  corresponding amount set out in column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of total gravity value

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The total gravity value in respect of a violation is
  to be established by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} considering each of the criteria in column 1 of the
    table to this section;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ascribing to each criterion an appropriate gravity
    value as set out in column 2, having regard to the circumstances of
    the violation (with a lower gravity value representing a lower level
    of gravity and a higher gravity value representing a higher level of
    gravity); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} adding the values obtained.

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834440 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of service

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  service of a document that is authorized or required by section 71.06
  or 71.5 of the Act is to be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person to be served is an individual, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with that individual,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it with someone who appears to
      be an adult member of the same household at the individual's last
      known address or usual place of residence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail,
      courier, fax or other electronic means to the individual's last
      known address or usual place of residence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person to be served is not an individual, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it at the person's head office
      or place of business with an officer or other individual who
      appears to manage or be in control of the head office or place of
      business,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail, courier
      or fax to the person's head office or place of business, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by electronic means other
      than by fax to an officer or other individual referred to in
      subparagraph (i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is not personally served is
  considered to be served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy that is left with a person
    referred to in subparagraph (1)(a)(ii), on the day on which it is
    left with that person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy that is sent by registered
    mail or courier, on the 10th day after the date indicated in the
    receipt issued by the postal or courier service; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a copy sent by fax or other
    electronic means, on the day on which it is transmitted.
