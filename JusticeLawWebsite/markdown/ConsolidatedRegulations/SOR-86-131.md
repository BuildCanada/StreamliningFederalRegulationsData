## Electricity and Gas Inspection Regulations {#electricity-and-gas-inspection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-131
:::

[ELECTRICITY AND GAS INSPECTION ACT](/eng/acts/E-4)

Registration 1986-01-16

Regulations Relating to the Inspection of Electricity and Gas Meters and
Supplies

P.C. 1986-116 1986-01-16
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 28(2) of the [Electricity and Gas
Inspection Act](/eng/acts/E-4) a copy of the proposed [Electricity and
Gas Inspection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-131), substantially
in the form annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on August 10, 1985 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations with respect thereto to the Minister of Consumer
and Corporate Affairs.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs,
pursuant to section 28 of the [Electricity and Gas Inspection
Act](/eng/acts/E-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1244_hq_6950){#fn_1244_hq_6950-ID0EDBAA}, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations relating to the
inspection of electricity and gas meters and
supplies](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-131), effective January 25, 1986.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-891035 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[absolute pressure scale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[absolute pressure scale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pressure
      measuring scale the zero of which represents the pressure exerted
      by a total vacuum; ([échelle de pression absolue]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[accreditation audit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accreditation audit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an assessment of
      the facilities, procedures and capabilities of an applicant for
      accreditation as a meter verifier to ascertain whether the
      required standards of meter performance control can be met and
      maintained by the applicant; ([vérification
      d'accréditation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Electricity and Gas
      Inspection Act](/eng/acts/E-4); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[average flowing gas temperature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average flowing gas temperature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      time weighted or the volume weighted average of the temperatures
      of the gas flowing through a meter during the period a volume of
      gas is measured by the meter; ([température moyenne d'écoulement
      du gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average meter pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average meter pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the time
      weighted or the volume weighted average of the pressures exerted
      by the gas within a meter during the period a volume of gas is
      measured by the meter; ([pression moyenne du
      compteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[electricity metering installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[electricity metering installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      installation that consists of more than one electricity meter
      installed at the same location and that is used for the purpose of
      obtaining the basis of a charge for electricity supplied to a
      purchaser; ([installation de mesure de
      l'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gas metering installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gas metering installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      installation that consists of more than one gas meter installed at
      the same location and that is used for the purpose of obtaining
      the basis of a charge for gas supplied to a
      purchaser; ([installation de mesure du gaz]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[gauge pressure scale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gauge pressure scale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pressure
      measuring scale the zero of which represents the pressure exerted
      by the earth's atmosphere at the time and place the measurement is
      taken; ([échelle de pression manométrique]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[measured atmospheric pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[measured atmospheric pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      time weighted or volume weighted average of the atmospheric
      pressures measured at the location of a meter during the period a
      volume of gas is measured by the meter; ([pression atmosphérique
      mesurée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Measurement Canada office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Measurement Canada office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any office
      of the Department of Industry for use by persons employed in the
      administration of the Act; ([bureau de Mesures
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means standard
      temperature and standard pressure; ([conditions
      normales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the International system of units, an
        absolute pressure equal to 101.325 kPa, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Imperial system of units,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 14.73 psia, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 30 in. of mercury at 32°F; ([pression
          normale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard temperature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard temperature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a temperature
      that is equal to

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 15°C in the International system of units, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 60°F in the Imperial system of
        units; ([température normale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[standard volume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[standard volume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a volume of gas
      expressed in units of either the cubic metre or the cubic foot at
      standard pressure and standard temperature as determined in
      accordance with section 35. ([volume normal]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a word or expression is used in these
  Regulations to describe a type of meter, a function or part of a meter
  or a measurement, that term shall have the meaning set out in the
  appropriate specifications, as amended from time to time, made
  available pursuant to sections 12, 18, 19, 44 and 45.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-891061 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/92-438, s. 1

\]
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Instrument transformers that are
  designed and constructed to have fixed, non-adjustable accuracy
  characteristics are exempted from subsection 9(1) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A gas meter with a broken seal is exempt from the
  requirement that it not be continued in use until it has been
  reverified under subsection 15(2) of the Act if all of the following
  conditions are satisfied:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the seal was deliberately broken by an inspector or
    accredited meter verifier for the purpose of installing or removing
    a device that transmits the reading of the meter to a remote
    location;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no components of the meter other than the register
    cover and the register have been removed from the meter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any component that has been removed from the meter
    has been promptly replaced such that the reading of the register is
    unchanged except for, in cases where the register has not been
    removed, increases indicating the volume of gas that passed through
    the meter during the time the seal was broken; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the meter is promptly resealed and restamped,
    relabelled, retagged or otherwise marked by an inspector or
    accredited meter verifier in accordance with the specifications made
    available by the director pursuant to section 18 of these
    Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A meter that was originally sealed using a plastic
  filament of any diameter or a metallic filament with a diameter of
  less than 0.644 mm and that is discovered in service with a broken
  seal is exempt from the requirement of subsection 15(2) of the Act
  that it not be continued in use until it has been reverified if both
  of the following conditions are satisfied:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no visible sign that the broken seal
    resulted from an attempt to alter or affect the working parts or
    adjustments of the meter or in any other way affect the registration
    of the meter; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the meter is resealed by an inspector in accordance
    with the Act and these Regulations.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Units and Measuring Apparatus]{.HTitleText1} {#h-891080 .Part}

### [Units]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891081 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the amount of electricity supplied and the time-related demand for
  electricity supplied are the joint basis of a charge for electricity
  sold to a purchaser, the unit of measurement for the sale of such
  electricity shall be, with respect to the amount of electricity sold,
  a unit referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act and, with respect
  to the time-related demand for electricity sold,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the watt;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the volt-ampere; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the var.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the unit of measurement for the sale of
  electricity is the volt-ampere or volt-ampere hour, vector addition
  shall be used to determine the total number of such units in a
  combination of polyphase circuits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-532, s. 3(F)
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  cubic metre and the cubic foot referred to in subparagraph 3(1)(b)(i)
  of the Act shall be determined at standard pressure and at standard
  temperature.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The British Thermal Unit referred to in subparagraph
  3(1)(b)(ii) of the Act shall be defined as the quantity of energy that
  must be added to one pound avoirdupois of distilled water to raise its
  temperature from 60°F to 61°F under standard pressure and is
  represented in these Regulations by the symbol "Btu~(60.5)~".

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-532, s. 3(E)
:::

### [Measuring Apparatus]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891095 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A measuring apparatus that
is required for measurement of electricity or gas or examination of
meters shall be calibrated in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by testing its accuracy at points throughout the
  measuring range of the apparatus; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by tracing its accuracy at one or more points tested
  in accordance with paragraph (a) to reference standards kept by the
  National Research Council of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-317, s. 2
- SOR/95-532, s. 3
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 5 of the Act, the calibration of a measuring
  apparatus referred to in section 7 shall be certified by the director
  on receipt of written particulars establishing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the apparatus has been calibrated in the
    manner prescribed in section 7;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the proposed manner of installation and use of
    the measuring apparatus is appropriate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that no error at any of the points tested
    throughout the measuring range referred to in section 7 exceeds two
    per cent of the value or reading at that point indicated by the
    reference standard referred to in paragraph 7(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate issued pursuant to subsection (1) shall
  be valid

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} only for use of the measuring apparatus within the
    measuring range referred to in section 7;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} only for the installation and use of the measuring
    apparatus as referred to in paragraph (1)(b); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a measuring apparatus set out in an
    item of column I of the table to this section, for the period set
    out in column II of that item.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Contractors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-891118 .Part}

### [Registration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891119 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  register referred to in subsection 6(1) of the Act shall be maintained
  for the registration of contractors who hold a certificate of
  registration pursuant to subsection 6(2) of the Act and shall contain
  for each of the contractors the information referred to in subsection
  (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A contractor who wishes to register under section 6
  of the Act shall apply in writing for a certificate of registration
  and indicate whether the application concerns the supply of
  electricity or gas and such application shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the contractor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the principal place of business of the contractor;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the geographical area in which the contractor
    intends to operate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The original of a certificate of registration shall
  be sent to the contractor who has applied therefor and a copy thereof
  shall be retained with the register.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where there is any change in the information provided
  by a contractor pursuant to subsection (2), the contractor shall
  forthwith inform the director in writing of any such change and shall
  return to the director the certificate of registration to which the
  change applies.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-532, s. 3
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 6(3) of the Act, a notice referred to in that subsection
shall be sent by registered mail, shall state the date on which the
contractor ceased to sell electricity or gas on the basis of measurement
and shall contain details as to the disposition or intended disposition
of all meters owned, used or possessed by the contractor.

### [Owner's Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891131 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an owner referred to in
      subsection 16(1) of the Act; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[owner's records]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[owner's records]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the records required
      to be kept by an owner pursuant to subsection 16(2) of the
      Act. ([dossier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner's records shall contain the following
  information and documentation if applicable for each verified or
  reverified meter in that owner's use, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type designation, serial number, rating and
    multiplier assigned by the manufacturer,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the purchaser's account number, name and address,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the inspection number assigned by the contractor,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date of the meter's latest reverification,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of the meter's initial verification or
    purchase,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the applicable period referred to in section 12 of
    the Act within which the meter shall be submitted to reverification,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the most recent certificate issued for the meter by
    an inspector or an accredited meter verifier,

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the parent group identification assigned by the
    owner,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the meter's current installation address,

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the date of the meter's installation at its present
    location,

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an in-service unsealed meter, the
    date and nature of any maintenance work, and the date and results of
    any calibration,

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the date or dates on which the meter was sold,
    scrapped or otherwise disposed of, leased or lost, and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} for each billing period, the metering information
    used by the owner in establishing a charge,

  and, in the case of a verified or reverified meter used in respect of
  gas, shall also contain the following information, namely,

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} an indication whether the verified or reverified
    meter corrects for temperature or pressure, or both,

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the metric conversion factor used for the purpose
    of billing,

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the mean atmospheric pressure or the atmospheric
    pressure calculated, declared or measured, as the case may be,
    pursuant to section 36 or 37, and applied to measurement
    calculations,

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} where the pressure exerted by the gas within the
    meter is maintained constant by the use of an approved regulating
    device, the pressure multiplier established for the meter pursuant
    to section 36, and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} where the average meter pressure exceeds the mean
    atmospheric pressure or the atmospheric pressure calculated,
    declared or measured, as the case may be, pursuant to section 36 or
    37, by more than 3.5 kPa in the International system of units or
    0.5 psi in the Imperial system of units,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-pressure converting meter,
      the average meter pressure expressed on the gauge pressure scale
      for each billing period,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a non-temperature converting
      meter, the average flowing gas temperature for each billing
      period,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the average relative density, the average
      carbon dioxide content and the average nitrogen content of gas
      metered for each billing period,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the supercompressibility factor established
      pursuant to section 40 and the method of its determination for
      each billing period, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an orifice meter, the size of the
      orifice plates and the dimensions of the primary element
      components in the orifice meter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An owner's records shall contain, in addition to the
  information referred to in subsection (2), the following information
  on a calendar year basis with respect to the verified or reverified
  meters in use by that owner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of verified or reverified meters
    used to measure electricity or gas supplied

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to residential locations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to industrial or commercial locations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a listing of the inspection numbers and types of
    verified or reverified meters due or overdue for reverification.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an owner installs a gas metering installation
  or an electricity metering installation, the owner's records shall
  contain, in addition to the information referred to in subsection (2)
  for each meter, the following information respecting the installation
  of the meters:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of any apparatus, wiring and piping that
    may affect the accuracy of the meters;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the consumption on a calendar year basis of
    electricity or gas at that location; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an electricity metering
    installation, the overall multiplier.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where an owner has installed a gas metering
  installation or an electricity metering installation, that owner shall
  provide forthwith to the director the information contained in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the records respecting the installed meters as
    referred to in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs (2)(a) to (d),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs (2)(p) and (q), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} subparagraph (2)(r)(v); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the records respecting the installation as referred
    to in paragraphs (4)(a) and (c).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where an owner sells gas on the basis of energy
  units, the owner's records shall contain the following information
  respecting the owner's gas distribution system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of the system, including the location of
    each point of supply of gas thereto;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the method whereby the calorific power of the gas
    sold is determined; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the calorific power of the gas sold and the
    variation thereof.

- [(6.1)]{.lawlabel} An owner's records shall contain the following
  information respecting the determination of a supercompressibility
  factor:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of the system, including the location of
    each point of supply of gas; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the method by which the supercompressibility factor
    of the gas sold is determined.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} An owner shall retain the records containing the
  information referred to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subsection (2) for a period of at least 12
    months after the date the meter ceased to be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subsection (3) for a period of at least 12
    months after the expiration of the calendar year to which the
    records referred to in that subsection apply;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in subsection (4) for a period of at least 12
    months after the date the gas metering installation or electricity
    metering installation, as the case may be, ceased to be used; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in subsection (6) for a period of at least 12
    months after the date the gas distribution system ceased to be used.

### [Application for Permission or Approval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891197 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
permission or an approval referred to in section 9 of the Act shall be
made in writing to the director and shall contain the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and principal place of business of the

### [Sale of Gas by Volume]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891357 .Subheading}

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A volume of gas measured
by any meter that registers in units of volume shall be converted to
standard volume by using the following equation, namely,

::: {#891359 .Section}
V~s~ = V~r~ × P~m~ × T~m~ × (F~pv~)^2^

where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} V~s~ is the standard volume;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} V~r~ is the volume registered by the meter;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} P~m~ is the pressure multiplier established for the
  meter pursuant to section 36;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} T~m~ is the temperature multiplier established for
  the meter pursuant to section 38 or 39, whichever is applicable; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} F~pv~ is the supercompressibility factor established
  for the meter pursuant to section 40.
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-532, s. 3(F)
:::

### [Pressure Multiplier]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891368 .Subheading}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  pressure multiplier for any pressure converting meter shall be
  established by using the following equation, namely,

  ::: {#891371 .Subsection}
  P~m~ = P~b~/P~s~

  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} P~m~ is the pressure multiplier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} P~b~ is the pressure expressed on the absolute
    pressure scale and used as the base pressure for the calibration of
    the pressure converting meter; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} P~s~ is the standard pressure expressed on the
    absolute pressure scale.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The pressure multiplier for any meter other than a
  pressure correcting meter shall be established by using the following
  equation, namely,

  ::: {#891377 .Subsection}
  P~m~ = (P~ga~ + P~a~)/P~s~

  where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} P~m~ is the pressure multiplier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} P~ga~ is the average meter pressure expressed on
    the gauge pressure scale;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} P~a~ is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the mean atmospheric pressure at the meter
      location, expressed to at least four significant digits,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the atmospheric pressure calculated for the
      meter location pursuant to subsection 37(1), expressed to at least
      four significant digits,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the atmospheric pressure declared for the meter
      location pursuant to subsection 37(2), expressed to at least four
      significant digits, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the measured atmospheric pressure at the meter
      location, expressed to at least four significant digits; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} P~s~ is the standard pressure expressed on the
    absolute pressure scale.
  :::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-532, s. 3
:::

### [Atmospheric Pressure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891389 .Subheading}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  atmospheric pressure shall be calculated for a meter location by using
  the following equation, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the International system of units,

    ::: {#891393 .Paragraph}
    P~a~ = 101.560 - (0.0113 × M) kPa, or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Imperial system of units,

    ::: {#891396 .Paragraph}
    P~a~ = 14.73 - (0.0005 × F) psia

    where
    :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} P~a~ is the calculated atmospheric pressure; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} M is the number of metres of elevation of the meter
    location above mean sea level and F is the number of feet of
    elevation of the meter location above mean sea level, in either case
    as determined by a survey carried out by a registered surveyor or as
    determined from the most recent applicable topographical maps
    published by the Department of Energy, Mines and Resources.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The atmospheric pressure declared for a meter
  location shall not differ from the atmospheric pressure calculated for
  that meter location pursuant to subsection (1) by more than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the International system of units,

    ::: {#891402 .Paragraph}
    0.621 + (0.001 × P~ga~) kPa, or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Imperial system of units,

    ::: {#891405 .Paragraph}
    0.090 + (0.001 × P~ga~) psi

    where

    [P~ga~]{.dfn}
    :   is the average meter pressure expressed on the gauge pressure
        scale.
    :::

### [Temperature Multiplier]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891408 .Subheading}

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 39, the
  temperature multiplier for any temperature converting meter shall be
  established by using the following equation, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the International system of units,

    ::: {#891412 .Paragraph}
    T~m~ = 288.15/(T~b~ + 273.15), or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Imperial system of units,

    ::: {#891415 .Paragraph}
    T~m~ = 519.67/(T~b~ + 459.67)

    where
    :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} T~m~ is the temperature multiplier; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} T~b~ is the temperature established by the
    contractor and used as the base temperature for the calibration of
    the temperature converting meter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 39, the temperature multiplier of
  any meter other than a temperature converting meter shall be
  established by using the following equation, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the International system of units,

    ::: {#891421 .Paragraph}
    T~m~ = 288.15/(T~ga~ + 273.15), or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the Imperial system of units,

    ::: {#891424 .Paragraph}
    T~m~ = 519.67/(T~ga~ + 459.67)

    where
    :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} T~m~ is the temperature multiplier; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} T~ga~ is the average flowing gas temperature.

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A temperature multiplier
of one may be used for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meters that are purchased prior to December 31, 1986
  and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that have a capacity that is less than 14 standard
    cubic metres per hour at a differential pressure of 0.125 kPa, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that have a capacity that is less than 500
    standard cubic feet per hour at a differential pressure of 0.5 in.
    of water column;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meters that are installed prior to December 31, 1986,
  where the average meter pressure exceeds the atmospheric pressure by

  the Imperial system of units; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} meters that are installed where the average meter
  pressure does not exceed the atmospheric pressure by more than 3.5 kPa
  in the International system of units or 0.5 psi in the Imperial system
  of units and that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} were most recently sealed prior to December 31,
    1986, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have a capacity that is greater than that stated
    in subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii), whichever is applicable.

### [Supercompressibility Factor]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891438 .Subheading}

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 35(e),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraphs (b) and (c), the
  supercompressibility factor for any meter shall be established based
  on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pressure exerted by the gas within the meter
    and the temperature of the gas flowing through the meter as
    determined by continuous monitoring at the meter location,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the relative density, the carbon dioxide content
    and the nitrogen content of the gas being measured, as determined by

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a gas sampling process, if the process gives
      results that are representative of the gas at the meter location,
      or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} continuous monitoring at the meter location, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the parameters of the gas referred to in
    subparagraphs (i) and (ii) vary, either the time weighted or the
    volume weighted averages of those parameters during the period that
    the volume of the gas is measured by the meter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the supercompressibility factor for any meter for
  which the pressure exerted by the gas within the meter expressed on
  the gauge pressure scale does not exceed 207 kPa in the International
  system of units, or 30 psi in the Imperial system of units, may be
  established based on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pressure exerted by the gas within the meter,
    established and maintained through the use of a pressure factor
    metering regulator that has been approved and verified under section
    9 of the Act,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an assumed flowing gas temperature of 59°F or
    15°C, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the relative density, the carbon dioxide content
    and the nitrogen content of the gas being measured as determined by
    a gas sampling process, if the process gives results that are
    representative of the gas at the meter location; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a supercompressibility factor of one may be used if
  the pressure exerted by the gas within the meter expressed on the
  gauge pressure scale does not exceed 700 kPa in the International
  system of units or 100 psi in the Imperial system of units.

### [Sale of Gas by Energy Units]{.HTitleText2} {#h-891453 .Subheading}

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** Where gas is sold by
energy units, the number of energy units shall be the product obtained
by multiplying

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the calorific power of a unit of mass of the gas by
  the number of the said units of mass of the gas; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the calorific power of a unit of volume of the gas at
  a certain temperature and certain pressure by the number of the said
  units of volume of the gas at the same certain temperature and same
  certain pressure.

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The calorific power of
gas shall be determined by measurement or by calculation based on gas
sampling and shall be expressed in terms of one of the following units:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} joules or multiples thereof, per standard cubic metre
  of gas, free of water vapour;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Btu~(60.5)~, per standard cubic foot of gas, free of
  water vapour; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} joules or multiples thereof, per kilogram of gas,
  free of water vapour.

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In order to obtain the
calorific power of gas in Btu~(60.5)~ per cubic foot of gas free of
water vapour at a pressure P expressed on the absolute pressure scale
and at standard temperature, where the gas has been tested using a
method that gives results in Btu~(60.5)~ per cubic foot of gas saturated
with water vapour at the pressure P and standard temperature, the
results shall be multiplied by a factor equal to

::: {#891462 .Section}
P/(P - 0.25611)
:::

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The director shall set
out specifications, subject to sections 42 and 43, for determination of
the calorific power of gas.
