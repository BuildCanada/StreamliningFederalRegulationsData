## Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in the Yukon Territory (Dawson First Nation, Y.T.) {#order-respecting-the-withdrawal-from-disposal-of-certain-lands-in-the-yukon-territory-dawson-first-nation-y.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/97-93
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1997-08-20

Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Lands in the
Yukon Territory (Dawson First Nation, Y.T.)

P.C. 1997-1063  1997-07-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), hereby
repeals the Withdrawal from Disposal Order (Dawson First Nation, Y.T.),
made by Order in Council P.C. 1995-720 of May 2, 1995[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-97-93_e_hq_5075){#fn_SI-97-93_e_hq_5075-ID0EDBAA},
and makes the annexed Order Respecting the Withdrawal from Disposal of
Certain Lands in the Yukon Territory (Dawson First Nation, Y.T.).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-651115 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Order
is to withdraw certain lands from disposal to facilitate the settlement
of the Aboriginal land claim of the Dawson First Nation, Y.T.

## [Lands Withdrawn from Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-651117 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, the tracts of territorial lands described in the schedule including
all mines and minerals (including hydrocarbons), whether solid, liquid
or gaseous, and the right to work them, are withdrawn from disposal for
the period beginning on the date this Order comes into force and ending
on December 31, 2001.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-651119 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substances or materials that may be disposed of
  under the [Territorial Quarrying
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} forest resources that may be disposed of under the
  Yukon Timber Regulations.

## [Existing Rights and Interests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-651123 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
section 2 does not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} existing recorded mineral claims in good standing
  acquired under the [Yukon Placer Mining Act](/eng/acts/Y-3) or the
  [Yukon Quartz Mining Act](/eng/acts/Y-4);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} existing oil and gas rights and interests granted
  under the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} existing rights acquired under section 8 of the
  [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) or under the [Territorial Lands
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1525).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-651128 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on July 25, 1997.
