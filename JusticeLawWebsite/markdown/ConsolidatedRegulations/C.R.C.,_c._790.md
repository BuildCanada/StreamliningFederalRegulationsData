## Settlers' Effects Acquired with Blocked Currencies Remission Order {#settlers-effects-acquired-with-blocked-currencies-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 790
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Duties, Including the Tax Imposed
Under Division III of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act, Payable on
Settlers' Effects Acquired Abroad with Blocked Currencies by Settlers
who Immigrate into Canada
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563737 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Settlers' Effects Acquired with Blocked Currencies Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._790).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563739 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duties]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means duties as defined in section 2
    of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([droits]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means household and personal effects
    imported by a settler for the settler\'s use but not for use by the
    settler in a business or manufacturing establishment or as a
    contractor\'s outfit; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who enters Canada
    with the intention of establishing, for the first time, a residence
    for a period of not less than 12 months, but does not include a
    person who enters Canada for the purpose of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} employment for a period not exceeding 36 months,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} studying at an educational institution, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} performing preclearance activities on behalf of
      the Government of the United States under the terms of the
      Agreement on Land, Rail, Marine, and Air Transport Preclearance
      between the Government of Canada and the Government of the United
      States of America, done at Washington on March 16,
      2015. ([immigrant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563749 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the duties, including the tax imposed under Division III of Part IX
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), payable on the importation of
goods into Canada by a settler within three years after the settler's
arrival in Canada where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the settler emigrated to Canada from a country that
  applies restrictions on the transfer of capital of emigrants therefrom
  to Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported by the settler from the
  country from which the settler emigrated to Canada where they were
  purchased by the settler or on the settler\'s behalf with funds that
  were on deposit in that country to the settler\'s credit at the time
  the settler emigrated.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563755 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission of duties,
including the tax imposed under Division III of Part IX of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), pursuant to this Order is conditional on the
settler who imports the goods retaining them for the settler's own use
for at least 12 months after the date of importation thereof, and, where
any such goods are sold or otherwise disposed of by the settler within
12 months after the date of importation, duties are forthwith due and
payable by the settler in an amount equal to the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the duties that, but for this Order, would have been
  payable on importation into Canada of the goods sold or otherwise
  disposed of; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the duties payable on the appraised value of the
  goods at the time of the sale or other disposal thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
