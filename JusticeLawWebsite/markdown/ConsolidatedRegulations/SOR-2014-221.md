## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806462 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods referred to in Chapters
    61 and 62 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are cut or knit to
    shape and are sewn or otherwise assembled in Honduras from fabric or
    yarn produced or obtained outside the free trade area.
    ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fabric and made-up textile goods referred to in
      Chapters 51 to 55, 58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions
      that are woven or knitted in Honduras from yarn produced or
      obtained outside the free trade area or that are knitted in
      Honduras from yarn spun in Honduras or Canada from fibre produced
      or obtained outside the free trade area; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods of subheading No. 9404.90 of the List of
      Tariff Provisions that are finished and cut and sewn or otherwise
      assembled in Honduras from fabric or yarn produced or obtained
      outside the free trade area. ([tissus et articles
      confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada and Honduras. ([zone de libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806469 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is granted of the customs duty paid or payable at the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff rates of customs duty on apparel and fabric
and made-up goods imported from Honduras on or after October 1, 2014.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806471 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  remission granted under section 2 applies only to an amount not
  exceeding the annual quantitative limits, for each of the goods
  referred to in that section, that are set out in Section 5 of Annex
  3.1 to Chapter Three of the Canada--Honduras Free Trade Agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted under section 2 on the condition
  that the importer or owner of the goods provides to a customs officer,
  at the request of the officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a permit issued under the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19) and the [Import Certificate
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._603) that identifies the
    quantity available for purposes of remission or refund under Chapter
    Three of the Canada--Honduras Free Trade Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a written declaration, in English or French, indicating that
    the certification referred to in subparagraph (c)(i) is in the
    possession of the importer or owner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are released, or at any
    other time after the importer or owner provides the declaration
    referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certification of the exporter, in English,
      French or Spanish, in the form set out in the schedule,
      accompanied by a copy of the applicable invoice or sales
      agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the certification is in Spanish, an English
      or French translation of the certification.
