## Fishery (General) Regulations {#fishery-general-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-53
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 1993-02-04

Regulations Respecting Fishing and Fish Habitat in General and the
Payment of Penalty and Forfeiture Proceeds Under the Fisheries Act

P.C. 1993-186 1993-02-04
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to sections 8,
43[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#a1_e){#a1_e-ID0ELCAA} and
79.7[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#a2_e){#a2_e-ID0EJCAA} and
subsection 87(2) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), is pleased
hereby to revoke the Fishways Obstructions Removal Regulations, C.R.C.,
c. 814, and the Penalties and Forfeitures Proceeds Regulations, C.R.C.,
c. 827, and to make the annexed Regulations respecting fishing and fish
habitat in general and the payment of penalty and forfeiture proceeds
under the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), in substitution therefor,
effective February 24, 1993.

    Act](/eng/acts/F-14); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-21); ([zone de la
    Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Fisheries
    and Oceans; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955583 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), Parts I to VIII apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing and related activities in Canadian
    fisheries waters off the Atlantic, Pacific and Arctic coasts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing and related activities in the provinces of
    Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, British Columbia, Prince Edward Island
    and Newfoundland and Labrador and in the Yukon Territory and
    Northwest Territories; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing and related activities carried out in
    waters other than Canadian fisheries waters from vessels subject to
    the jurisdiction of Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Parts I to VIII do not apply in respect of fishing
  and related activities in respect of which the [Quebec Fishery
  Regulations, 1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-214) apply.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Parts IX and X apply in respect of Canada and
  Canadian fisheries waters.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where there is an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and any of the following Regulations, the following
  Regulations shall prevail:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Atlantic Fishery Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-21);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [British Columbia Sport Fishing Regulations,
    1996](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-137);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Foreign Vessel Fishing
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._815);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Marine Mammal
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-56);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Management of Contaminated Fisheries
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-351);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Maritime Provinces Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the [Newfoundland and Labrador Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-443);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the [Northwest Territories Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._847);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
    1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the [Yukon Territory Fishery
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._854);

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the [Pacific Aquaculture
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-270);

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the [Aquatic Invasive Species
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-121); and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the [Possession and Export of Elvers
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-237).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Sections 18 to 21, 23 and 26 to 29 do not apply with
  respect to fishing and related activities carried out under the
  authority of a licence issued under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing
  Licences Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Sections 18 to 21, 23, 26 and 29 do not apply in
  respect of Inuit domestic fishing, being the exercise by a person of
  the right to harvest any species or stock of fish for food, social and
  ceremonial purposes in accordance with the Agreement defined in
  section 2 of the [Labrador Inuit Land Claims Agreement
  Act](/eng/acts/L-4.3).

### [Periods of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955610 .Subheading}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2023-147, s. 2

\]

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where a period of time is
set out in any of the Regulations listed in subsection 3(4), the period

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless otherwise specified, begins at 00:00 h on the
  first day set out and ends at 24:00 h on the last day set out; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} begins and ends in each calendar year except that
  where the period is stated to end on a day in a month that occurs
  earlier in a calendar year than the month in which the period begins,
  that period begins in one calendar year and ends in the next calendar
  year.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a reference to time in a provision of any of the
  Regulations listed in subsection 3(4) or a notice given under section
  7 is a reference to local time in effect in the province or portion
  thereof that is closest to the waters in respect of which the
  provision or the notice applies unless, in the case of a notice, it is
  otherwise stated in the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where notice is given under section 7 in respect of
  any close time set out in the [Foreign Vessel Fishing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._815), that time is a
  reference to Greenwich time.

### [Variation Orders]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955617 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  close time, fishing quota, limit on the size or weight of fish or
  fishing gear or equipment is fixed in respect of an area under any of
  the Regulations listed in subsection 3(4), the Regional
  Director-General may, by order, vary that close time, quota, limit,
  gear or equipment in respect of that area or any portion of that area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota or fishing gear or
  equipment for herring or salmon fishing is fixed in respect of an area
  under the [Pacific Fishery Regulations,
  1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-54), a fishery officer may, by order,
  vary that close time, quota, gear or equipment in respect of that area
  or any portion of that area.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota, limit on the size or
  weight of fish or fishing gear or equipment is fixed in respect of an
  area of non-tidal waters for any species of fish other than salmon
  under the [British Columbia Sport Fishing Regulations,
  1996](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-137), the director responsible for
  fisheries management in the ministry responsible for fisheries in the
  Government of British Columbia may, by order, vary that close time,
  quota, limit, gear or equipment in respect of that area or any portion
  of that area.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota, limit on the size or
  weight of fish or fishing gear or equipment is fixed in respect of an
  area of non-tidal waters for any species of fish other than salmon
  under the [Yukon Territory Fishery
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._854), the Director of the
  Fish and Wildlife Branch of the Department of Environment for the
  Yukon Territory may, by order, vary that close time, quota, limit,
  gear or equipment in respect of that area or any portion of that area.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota, limit on the size or
  weight of fish or fishing gear or equipment is fixed in respect of an
  area of the province of New Brunswick under the [Maritime Provinces
  Fishery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55), the director
  responsible for fisheries management in the ministry responsible for
  fisheries in the Government of New Brunswick may, by order, vary that
  close time, quota, limit, gear or equipment in respect of that area or
  any portion of that area for any species of fish listed in Appendix A
  of the Canada--New Brunswick Memorandum of Understanding on
  Recreational Fisheries, dated June 18, 2001, as amended from time to
  time.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a close time, fishing quota, limit on the size or
  weight of fish or fishing gear or equipment is fixed in respect of an
  area of the province of Nova Scotia under the [Maritime Provinces
  Fishery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-55), the director
  responsible for fisheries management in the ministry responsible for
  fisheries in the Government of Nova Scotia may, by order, vary that
  close time, quota, limit, gear or equipment in respect of that area or
  any portion of that area for any species of fish listed in Appendix A
  to the Canada--Nova Scotia Memorandum of Understanding on Recreational
  Fisheries, dated September 17, 2003, as amended from time to time.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Documents and Registrations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955643 .Part}

### [Application for Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955644 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may require an applicant for a document to submit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} such information in addition to that included in
    the application as may reasonably be regarded as relevant; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statutory declaration verifying the information
    given in the application or verifying the information submitted
    under paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application from a corporation for a document
  shall be signed by an officer of the corporation.

### [Signing of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955650 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where it is indicated in a
document that it is not valid unless signed, the holder of the document
shall, on receipt of the document, immediately sign it in ink in the
space provided.

### [Expiration of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955652 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified in a document, a document expires

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where it is issued for a calendar year, on December
  31 of the year for which it is issued; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where it is issued for a fiscal year, on March 31 of
  the year for which it is issued.

### [Carrying and Producing Licences, Registration Cards and Certificates]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955656 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of a
licence, a fisher's registration card or a provincial or territorial
fisher's certificate shall carry it at all times while engaged in any
activity to which it relates and shall produce it on the demand of a
fishery officer or fishery guardian.

### [Amending Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955660 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding paragraph 15(1)(a),
  a fishery officer who is employed by the Department or any employee of
  the Department engaged in the issuance of documents may, on the
  request of the holder of a document,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amend the document and sign and date the amendment;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cancel the document and issue a replacement
    document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a document contains an error or was issued in
  error, a fishery officer or an employee referred to in subsection (1)
  may request the holder of the document to surrender it.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the holder of a document receives a request
  referred to in subsection (2), that person shall immediately comply
  with the request.

### [Alteration and Use of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955667 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alter or deface any document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use or produce any document that has been altered
    or defaced; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use or produce any document issued to another
    person under the pretence of being the holder thereof.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No holder of a fisher's registration card shall
  permit another person to use that registration card.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a licence specifies the person or persons who
  may fish under the licence, the holder of that licence shall not
  permit any person other than the person or persons so specified to use
  that licence.

### [Transfer of Documents and Rights and Privileges]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955675 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document is the property of the Crown and is not transferable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The issuance of a document of any type to any person
  does not imply or confer any future right or privilege for that person
  to be issued a document of the same type or any other type.

### [Change of Name or Address and Replacement of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955679 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person is the holder of a document and has a change of name or
  address, or the document is lost, stolen or destroyed or becomes
  illegible, that person shall notify the Minister in writing within 15
  days after the occurrence of the event and the Minister may issue a
  replacement document showing any applicable changes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a replacement document, the holder
  shall immediately return to the Department the document being replaced
  if it is still in the holder's possession.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A holder of a replacement document who recovers the
  document that was replaced shall immediately return it to the
  Department.

### [Transfer of Ownership or Loss of Vessel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955684 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  there is a transfer of ownership of a registered vessel or a loss,
  destruction, dismantling or other removal of a registered vessel from
  use as a fishing vessel, the person in whose name the vessel is
  registered shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister in writing of the event within
    15 days after the occurrence of the event; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person is still in possession of the
    vessel registration card or any licence issued in respect of the
    vessel, return the document to the Minister with the notification
    required under paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notification of transfer of ownership given under
  paragraph (1)(a) shall include the name and address of the new owner.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Where there is a transfer
of ownership of a registered vessel and the vessel is not registered in
the name of the new owner,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no person shall use that vessel as a fishing vessel;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the new owner of that vessel shall not permit another
  person to use the vessel as a fishing vessel.

### [Identification of Fishing Gear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-955791 .Subheading}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a method to track progress towards achieving the

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a schedule for a periodic review of the plan in

  the day on which the Minister first had knowledge that the major fish

  Department.

  consistent with the rebuilding of the stock above the limit reference
  point.

## [SCHEDULE I]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 31(2)(b) and subsection 31(3))]{.OriginatingRef}[Topside Chafers]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-956101 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Regular Topside Chafer]{.HTitleText1} {#regular-topside-chafer .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** A rectangular piece of netting that

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} is at least 1 ½ times the width of the area of the
    cod-end that is covered, where the width is measured at right angles
    to the long axis of the cod-end;

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} has a mesh size that is not less than the mesh size
    of the cod-end; and

  - [c)]{.lawlabel} is fastened to the cod-end only along the forward
    and lateral edges of the netting in a manner that will permit it to
    extend

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where a splitting strap is used, over not more of
      the cod-end than that part between the fourth mesh forward of the
      cod line mesh and the fourth mesh forward of the splitting strap,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a splitting strap is not used, over not
      more than one-third of the cod-end, measured from not less than
      the fourth mesh forward of the cod line mesh.

### [Modified Polish Topside Chafer]{.HTitleText1} {#modified-polish-topside-chafer .SchedHeadL1}

- **2** A rectangular piece of netting that

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} is made of twine of the same material and size as
    that of the cod-end, or of any single, thick, knotless twine
    material;

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} has a mesh size that is twice as large as the mesh
    size of the cod-end;

  - [c)]{.lawlabel} is attached to the rear portion of the topside of
    the cod-end; and

  - [d)]{.lawlabel} is fastened to the cod-end along the forward,
    lateral and rear edges of the netting in a manner that will cause
    each mesh to exactly overlie four meshes of the cod-end over which
    it extends.

### [Multiple Flap-Type Topside Chafer]{.HTitleText1} {#multiple-flap-type-topside-chafer .SchedHeadL1}

- **3** A series of pieces of netting where

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate length extends less than two-thirds of
    the length of the cod-end; and

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} each piece of netting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is attached to the topside of the cod-end so that
      it overlaps the piece of netting immediately to its rear, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a mesh size that is not less than the mesh
      size of the cod-end,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is at least as wide as the cod-end, where the
      width is measured at right angles to the cod-end,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is not more than 10 meshes long, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} is fastened by its forward edge only across the
      cod-end at right angles to its long axis.
