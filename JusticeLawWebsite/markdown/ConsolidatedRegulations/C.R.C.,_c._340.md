## Rabies Indemnification Regulations {#rabies-indemnification-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 340
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 4, 1973

REGULATIONS RESPECTING THE REIMBURSEMENT OF PROVINCES FOR MONEYS PAID TO
OWNERS OF ANIMALS DYING AS A RESULT OF RABIES
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549925 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Rabies Indemnification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._340).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549927 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
\"Minister\" means the Minister of Agriculture.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549929 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, the Minister may reimburse any province 2/5 of the amount paid by the
province to owners of animals that have died as a result of rabies.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The maximum amount per
animal that the Minister may pay under these Regulations is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for cattle, \$400;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for horses, 200;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for sheep, 80;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for swine, 80; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for goats, 80.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/81-136, s. 1
  :::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No payment shall be made by
the Minister under section 3 in respect of any animal unless the
Minister has first received a certificate signed by a veterinary
inspector under the Animal Disease and Protection Act certifying that
the animal died as a result of rabies.
