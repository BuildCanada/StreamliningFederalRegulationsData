## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985698 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the issuance
    and publication of a tariff, means a manner by which the tariff is
    made available for public inspection electronically. ([forme
    électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[printed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[printed form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the issuance and
    publication of a tariff, means a book, pamphlet, single sheet or
    loose-leaf sheets. ([forme imprimée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Information to Be Included in Tariffs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985703 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following information
shall be included in every traffic or passenger tariff that is issued
and published by a railway company under Part III of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the rates, in dollars and cents, for
  each designated unit of weight or volume, for each type of equipment
  or service rendered or for each passenger;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of any category of traffic or
  passenger that is being transported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the point of origin and point of destination of any
  traffic or passenger;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the route over which a rate applies,
  or a reference to a routing guide to describe that route;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of issuance, of coming into force and of
  expiration of the tariff;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions of the tariff, including
  terms and conditions of the carriage of persons with disabilities, or
  an explanation, with references, of where the terms and conditions can
  be found; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of any symbols or abbreviations used
  in the tariff.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In addition to maintaining
the information specified in section 2, an amended tariff shall include
the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any changes, highlighted, that are made to the tariff
  that was in effect immediately before the changes came into force;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a tariff is in printed form and any changes are
  found in a supplement to the tariff,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a reference to any cancelled supplement and where
    it can be found, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a list of any other supplements to the tariff that
    are in effect on the date of issuance of the supplement; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where a tariff is in electronic form, a reference to
  all previous versions of items that are being amended.
