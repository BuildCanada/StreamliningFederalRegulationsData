## Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order {#statistics-canada-census-related-term-employment-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2010-45
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2010-07-07

Statistics Canada Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval
Order

P.C. 2010-773 2010-06-17
::::

:::::::::: section
::::::::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8010){#fn_612624-e_hq_8010-ID0EOFGA},
hereby approves the exclusion from the application of the definitions
[[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[internal appointment
process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1), paragraphs 22(2)(a) to
(c)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8011){#fn_612624-e_hq_8011-ID0EIFGA}
and sections 40, 41[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8012){#fn_612624-e_hq_8012-ID0EGFGA},
48, 51 to 53[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8013){#fn_612624-e_hq_8013-ID0EEFGA},
57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose
function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to
perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the
population census and the agriculture census as described in the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8014){#fn_612624-e_hq_8014-ID0EBFGA}
and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a
specified term basis, other than other persons mentioned in
subsection 5(1) of that Act.

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8010){#fn_612624-e_hq_8010-ID0EEAFA}
to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is solely to provide
administrative or management support or to perform data processing
duties for the purpose of conducting the population census and the
agriculture census as described in the [Statistics
Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8014){#fn_612624-e_hq_8014-ID0EBAFA}
and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a
specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in
subsection 5(1) of that Act;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8010){#fn_612624-e_hq_8010-ID0EBAEA},
the Public Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8010){#fn_612624-e_hq_8010-ID0EOADA},
hereby excludes from the application of the definitions
[[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[internal appointment
process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1), and of
paragraphs 22(2)(a) to (c)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8011){#fn_612624-e_hq_8011-ID0EIADA},
and sections 40, 41[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8012){#fn_612624-e_hq_8012-ID0EGADA},
48, 51 to 53[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8013){#fn_612624-e_hq_8013-ID0EEADA},
57, 59 and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose
function is solely to provide administrative or management support or to
perform data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the
population census and the agriculture census as described in the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_612624-e_hq_8014){#fn_612624-e_hq_8014-ID0EBADA}
and to the persons appointed or deployed to those positions on a
specified term basis, except for other persons mentioned in
subsection 5(1) of that Act.

This Order may be cited as the Statistics Canada Census-Related Term
Employment Exclusion Approval Order.

Ottawa, May 11, 2010
