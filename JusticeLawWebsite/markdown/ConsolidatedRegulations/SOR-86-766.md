## Delegation of Powers (Customs) Order {#delegation-of-powers-customs-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-766
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-07-15

Order Respecting the Delegation of Powers Conferred on the Minister of
National Revenue by Sections 131 to 133 of the Customs Act
::::

:::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section 134 of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EGBCA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order respecting the delegation of powers conferred on the
Minister of National Revenue by sections 131 to 133 of
the](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-766) [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),
effective on the day that the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCA}, other than
paragraph 99(1)(b), subsections 99(2) to (4) and sections 192 to 194
thereof, comes into force.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EGBCA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1986, c. 1
  :::

Ottawa, July 14, 1986

::: FlushRight
E. M. MacKAY
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of National Revenue*
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897752 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The officers named in the
schedule may exercise the powers conferred on the Minister of National
Revenue by sections 131 to 133 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
