## Listed Airport Regulations {#listed-airport-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-293
:::

[AIR TRAVELLERS SECURITY CHARGE ACT](/eng/acts/A-10.5)

Registration 2004-12-06

Listed Airport Regulations

P.C. 2004-1489 2004-12-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 2 and 84 of the [Air
Travellers Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Listed Airport Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-293).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}L.C. 2002,

  :::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713264 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Air Travellers Security Charge
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5).

## [Prescribed Airports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713266 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following airports are
prescribed airports for the purposes of the definition [[listed
airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Red Deer Regional; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Rivière-Rouge (Mont-Tremblant International).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713270 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 23, 2004.
