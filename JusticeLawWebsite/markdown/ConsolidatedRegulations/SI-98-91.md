## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Yellowknife Bay, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-yellowknife-bay-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/98-91
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1998-09-16

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Yellowknife Bay, N.W.T.)

P.C. 1998-1522  1998-08-26
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order that any portion of the lands described in the
schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the ordinary
high water mark of Yellowknife Bay of the City of Yellowknife in the
Northwest Territories be included in the grant of the said lands.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#653570 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-653571 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lots 1, 2, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15 and 16, Block 19
in the City of Yellowknife in the Northwest Territories as said lots are
shown on a plan of survey of record number 40264 in the Canada Lands
Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land Titles
Office for the Northwest Territories Land Registration District at
Yellowknife under number 66.
