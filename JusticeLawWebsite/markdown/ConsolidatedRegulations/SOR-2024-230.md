## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495274 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada National Parks
      Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Chief Executive Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Parks Canada Agency
      Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4). ([directeur général]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Indigenous peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Indigenous peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
      assigned by the definition [[aboriginal peoples of
      Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 35(2) of the
      [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/). ([peuples
      autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[land use agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[land use agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a lease or
      licence of occupation, a grant, letters patent for public lands or
      any agreement between a person and His Majesty in right of Canada
      in respect of the use of land or a condominium unit in a
      park. ([entente d'utilisation des terres]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[occupancy authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[occupancy authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      authorization referred to in section 17. ([autorisation
      d'occupation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[parcel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[parcel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any land or condominium unit
      that is the subject of a land use
      agreement. ([parcelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued under section
      11. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work or activity that is
      prohibited under subsection 3(1). ([projet]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any temporary or permanent
      physical work, such as any building, facility, fence or
      sign. ([structure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of "park"

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to a "park"
  includes a reference to a "park reserve".

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495289 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- Banff

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in the town of Banff.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495292 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works, activities and land uses

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to do any of the following, other than in accordance with
  an authorization or permit issued by the superintendent:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} change the use of any land or structure, including
    by changing the intensity of that use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} construct, install, alter, move, remove or demolish
    a structure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} excavate the ground or dump fill;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} materially alter the grade of the land or surface
    drainage patterns;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} landscape an area with non‑living materials such as
    paving stones or make alterations to such landscaping;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} plant a non‑native or fruit‑bearing plant species;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} fell, move or damage a tree that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deciduous tree, has a height of
      at least 4 m and, if measured from a height of 1 m above the
      ground, a diameter of at least 6 cm, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a coniferous tree, has a height
      of at least 2.5 m;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} install exterior lights, other than temporarily for
    the purpose of a seasonal event.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Occupancy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited to occupy a building, or any part of
  it, other than in accordance with an occupancy authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3(1) does not apply in
  respect of any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a work or activity that is carried out solely for
    the purpose of maintaining or repairing a structure and that does
    not alter the colour or finish of the structure's exterior;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a work or activity that is carried out solely for
    the purpose of altering the interior of a structure, unless it
    includes a work or activity that, if it were not carried out on
    public lands, would be subject to the requirements set out in any
    building, fire, plumbing, electrical or energy efficiency code that
    applies in the province where the park is located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the moving, removal or demolition of a structure,
    unless it includes a work or activity that, if it were not carried
    out on public lands, would be subject to the requirements set out in
    any building, fire, plumbing, electrical or energy efficiency code
    that applies in the province where the park is located;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a work or activity that is carried out solely for
    the purpose of constructing, installing, maintaining or repairing a
    public roadway or utility;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the installation, for a period of less than 10
    days, of an individual tent or group of tents if the tent or group
    of tents, as the case may be, occupy a total floor area of less than
    10 m^2^, do not contain any commercial cooking equipment and are
    located more than 3 m from any structure other than a sign;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a work or activity that is carried out by the
    superintendent or by a person who is acting on behalf of the
    superintendent in accordance with any conditions specified by the
    superintendent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- occupancy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3(2) does not apply in respect of the
  temporary occupancy of a building by the federal government or a
  provincial or municipal government or the temporary occupancy of a
  building for the purpose of an election, referendum or census.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- public safety or emergency

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of an activity carried out for the purpose of public safety or
in response to an emergency.

## [Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
issue an authorization for any person to construct, install or alter a
structure or to landscape an area with non‑living materials or make
alterations to such landscaping, and may make the authorization subject
to any conditions that the superintendent considers appropriate, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it includes a work or activity that, if it were not
  carried out on public lands, would be subject to the requirements set
  out in any building, fire, plumbing, electrical or energy efficiency
  code that applies in the province where the park is located; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it includes a change to the use of any land or
  structure, including by changing the intensity of that use.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495326 .Part}

### [Application and Consultation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1495327 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who has a land use agreement may apply to the superintendent,
  in the form approved by the superintendent, for a permit to carry out
  a project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by any fee
  payable in respect of that application under the [Parks Canada Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicant and their contact
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the project that the applicant
    proposes to carry out, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its estimated costs,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its location, including its municipal address,
      if any,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the project site that includes
      any existing structures and land features and their current uses,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the type of structures proposed
      to be constructed, if any, and their proposed uses, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any alterations proposed to be made to any
      existing structures, including their removal, and any changes
      proposed to be made to their uses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the proposed project
    is in conformity with the provisions of the Act and these
    Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the proposed project includes a
    work or activity that, if it were not carried out on public lands,
    would be subject to any building, fire, plumbing, electrical or
    energy efficiency code that applies in the province where the park
    is located, information demonstrating that the project meets the
    requirements set out in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any such code, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any code on the same subject matter that is
      published by the National Research Council of Canada or by the
      Canadian Standards Association;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the proposed project
    is in the interests of the park, having regard to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of the project,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the park management plan and, in the case of a
      park community, the community plan,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} principles of environmental stewardship and
      heritage conservation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any potential impact on natural and cultural
      resources in the park, including any proposed mitigation measures;
      and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information concerning any potential impact of the
    proposed project on the rights of Indigenous peoples recognized and
    affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-application meeting

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person who wishes to
apply for a permit to carry out a project may consult with the
superintendent with respect to the land use planning process before
submitting the application, if the proposed project includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction, installation, alteration,
  demolition or removal of a structure;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change to the use of land or of a structure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the installation, alteration or removal of a public
  utility;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the construction, alteration or removal of a public
  roadway, railroad, gondola or ski lift; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a work, activity or use that has any potential impact
  on the rights of Indigenous peoples recognized and affirmed by section
  35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public consultation

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
consult with the public with respect to a proposed project before making
a decision in respect of the issuance of the permit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with Indigenous peoples

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a proposed project has
any potential impact on the rights of Indigenous peoples recognized and
affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/), the
superintendent must consult with the Indigenous peoples who may be
affected before making a decision in respect of the issuance of the
permit.

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1495361 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superintendent

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may, on application, issue or amend a permit that
  authorizes a person to carry out a proposed project if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project is in conformity with the provisions of
    the Act and any Regulations made under it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project is in conformity with any land use
    agreement in respect of the parcel on which the project is to be
    located;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the project is in the interests of the park, having
    regard to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of the project,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the park management plan and, in the case of a
      park community, the community plan,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} principles of responsible environmental
      stewardship and heritage conservation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any potential impact on natural and cultural
      resources in the park, including any proposed mitigation measures;
      and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the superintendent has carried
    out the consultation referred to in section 10, the superintendent
    is satisfied that any potential impact on the rights of Indigenous
    peoples recognized and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution
    Act, 1982](/eng/Const/) has been identified and taken into account
    in the permit application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Impact assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application for a permit concerns a project
  for which an impact assessment is required under the [Impact
  Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75), the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
  Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2), the [Yukon Environmental and
  Socio-economic Assessment Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.2) or the [Nunavut
  Planning and Project Assessment Act](/eng/acts/N-28.75), a permit must
  not be issued until a final determination has been made under the
  applicable Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of permit

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
make a permit subject to any conditions that the superintendent
considers appropriate, including any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that certain types of plans, drawings, specifications
  or studies be prepared in respect of the project;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that certain types of work or activities be carried
  out by persons with a particular professional qualification, such as a
  professional accreditation or membership in a provincial licensing
  body;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that no plans, drawings or specifications that have
  been approved by the superintendent be altered unless the alteration
  is approved in writing by the superintendent and that the project is
  carried out in accordance with the approved plans, drawings and
  specifications;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that no person occupy or use any land or structure,
  or part of a structure, on the parcel where the project is carried out
  until the superintendent issues a certificate of completion in respect
  of the project;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that measures be implemented to mitigate the impact
  of the project.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory condition

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent must
make every permit subject to a condition that the project meet the
requirements set out in any building, fire, plumbing, electrical or
energy efficiency code that applies in the province where the project is
carried out or that is published by the National Research Council of
Canada or by the Canadian Standards Association.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may make a permit subject to a condition that the
  permit holder deposit with the superintendent any security that the
  superintendent considers necessary as a guarantee that the permit
  holder will comply with the conditions of the permit and with these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a permit holder to begin
  carrying out a project before the security specified by the permit has
  been deposited with the superintendent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return of security

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent must return the security to the
  permit holder after the certificate of completion is issued with
  respect to the project.

### [Expiry and Renewal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1495391 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry of permit

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permit expires on the earlier of the second anniversary of the day on
  which the permit is issued or the expiry day specified in the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal of permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, if the conditions referred to
  in paragraphs 11(1)(a) to (d) are met, renew a permit for a period of
  no more than 12 months and may make that permit subject to any
  additional conditions that the superintendent considers appropriate.

## [Certificate of Completion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495397 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent must
issue a certificate of completion with respect to a project if the
superintendent is satisfied that the permit holder has completed the
project and has complied with all of the conditions of the permit and
the requirements of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Occupancy authorization

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
include, in the certificate of completion, an authorization for the
occupancy of any building, or any part of a building, and may make the
authorization subject to conditions, such as that the occupancy be for a
particular purpose.

## [Suspension and Revocation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495402 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may suspend a permit or an occupancy authorization if
  the person who holds the permit or authorization has contravened a
  requirement of these Regulations or any condition of the permit or
  occupancy authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit or occupancy authorization is suspended
  until the day on which the superintendent notifies the person who
  holds the permit or authorization that it is reinstated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent must reinstate a suspended permit
  or occupancy authorization

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the contravention that led to the suspension has
    been corrected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after a period of 30 days from the date of the
    suspension, unless proceedings have been instituted before the end
    of that period in respect of the alleged contravention; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if proceedings have been instituted, after the
    person who holds the permit or occupancy authorization has been
    found not guilty of an offence in respect of the alleged
    contravention or after the proceedings against them have been
    discontinued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may revoke a permit or occupancy authorization if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the superintendent has reasonable grounds to
    believe that the person who holds the permit or occupancy
    authorization has provided false or misleading information to the
    superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person who holds the permit or occupancy
    authorization is convicted of an offence in respect of a
    contravention of any provision of these Regulations or any condition
    of the permit or occupancy authorization; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the permit or occupancy authorization has been
    suspended twice during the period for which it was issued, other
    than in any case where it was subsequently reinstated under
    paragraph 18(3)(b) or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent must not revoke a permit or
  occupancy authorization under paragraph (1)(a) unless the
  superintendent has given the holder of the permit or authorization
  written reasons and an opportunity to be heard, by written
  representation, in respect of the proposed revocation.

## [Notice of Superintendent's Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495421 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** If the superintendent
refuses to issue, amend or renew a permit or refuses to issue an
occupancy authorization, or suspends or revokes a permit or occupancy
authorization, the superintendent must provide notice in writing of
their decision, including the reasons, to the applicant or to the holder
of the permit or authorization, as the case may be.

## [Review of Superintendent's Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495424 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for review

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person whose application for the issuance, amendment or renewal of a
  permit has been refused or whose permit has been suspended or revoked
  by the superintendent may request a review of the superintendent's
  decision no later than the 30th day after the day on which they
  receive the notice referred to in section 20. The request must be
  submitted in writing to the Chief Executive Officer and include the
  reasons for the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Chief Executive Officer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer must direct the
  superintendent to issue or reinstate the permit if, after carrying out
  the review, the Chief Executive Officer reaches a decision that
  differs from the superintendent's decision with respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a refusal to issue, amend or renew a
    permit, the requirements and matters to be considered under section
    11; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a suspension or revocation of a
    permit, the reasons set out in section 18 or 19.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conformity with codes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A decision of the superintendent is not reviewable by
  the Chief Executive Officer under this section if it is with respect
  to the conformity of a structure with any building, fire, plumbing,
  electrical or energy efficiency code that applies in the province
  where the project is carried out or that is published by the National
  Research Council of Canada or by the Canadian Standards Association.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Chief Executive Officer must provide written
  notice of their decision, including the reasons, to the person who
  requested the review.

## [Maintenance Standards and Other Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495436 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Every person who has a
land use agreement for a parcel must ensure that any structure located
on the parcel is maintained in good repair, including by ensuring that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the structure is maintained in a structurally sound
  condition, capable of safely sustaining its own weight and any load to
  which it may be subject;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} materials that have been damaged or that show
  evidence of rot or other deterioration are repaired or replaced;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exterior walls are maintained to prevent
  deterioration due to weather, rot or insects, including by painting,
  restoring or repairing the walls, coping and flashing and by
  weatherproofing joints;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the roof, including its fascia board, soffit, cornice
  and flashing, is maintained in a watertight condition to prevent
  leakage of water into the structure;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} rotted or damaged doors, door frames, window frames,
  sashes and casings, broken glass and missing or defective door and
  window hardware are repaired or replaced;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} outside stairs, porches, patios and decks are
  maintained free from defects that constitute a hazard, and all treads,
  risers and supporting structural members that are rotted or
  deteriorated are repaired or replaced;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} all plumbing, drain pipes, water pipes and plumbing
  fixtures are maintained in good working order and are free from leaks
  and defects;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the electrical wiring and all electrical fixtures are
  maintained in good working order; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} crawl spaces and areas under porches, landings and
  decks are securely enclosed to prevent wildlife access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Every person who has a
land use agreement for a parcel must ensure that the appearance of the
parcel, and of any structure located on the parcel, is compatible with
the natural characteristics of the park in which it is located and the
characteristics of the surrounding area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Outdoor storage

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Every person who has a
land use agreement for a parcel must ensure that no item is stored
outdoors on the parcel other than in a manner that does not detract from
the appearance of the parcel and does not interfere with the use and
enjoyment of nearby lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- section 26

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Section 26 applies in
respect of any lands leased or licensed for the purpose of residential
occupancy in the following areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the visitor centre of Waterton Lakes Park, located in
  Waterton Lakes National Park of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the visitor centre of the Lake Edith Resort
  Subdivision, located in Jasper National Park of Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the visitor centre of Waskesiu, the Lakeview Resort
  Subdivision and the Clare Beach Subdivision, located in Prince Albert
  National Park of Canada, excluding the whole of Blocks 11 to 23, as
  shown on plan No. 93824 filed in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at
  Ottawa, a copy of which has been deposited in the Saskatchewan Land
  Surveys Directory under No. 101942872; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the visitor centre of Wasagaming and the Clear Lake
  Resort Subdivision, located in Riding Mountain National Park of
  Canada, excluding the whole of Block 64, as shown on plan No. 101515
  filed in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which
  has been deposited in the Land Titles Office in Neepawa under No.
  54045.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Floor area of cottages

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who holds a lease or licence of occupation for a parcel must
  ensure that the total floor area of all residential buildings located
  on the parcel does not exceed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 130 m^2^, in the case of the visitor centre of
    Waskesiu, the Lakeview Resort Subdivision and the Clare Beach
    Subdivision, located in Prince Albert National Park of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 130 m^2^, in the case of the visitor centre of
    Wasagaming and the Clear Lake Resort Subdivision, located in Riding
    Mountain National Park of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 150 m^2^, in the case of the visitor centre of
    Waterton Lakes Park, located in Waterton Lakes National Park of
    Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 150 m^2^, in the case of the visitor centre of the
    Lake Edith Resort Subdivision, located in Jasper National Park of
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the floor area
  must be measured between the interior faces of the walls for each
  habitable storey of the building and must include the floor area of
  any roofed porches and roofed terraces.

## [Compliance Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495467 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who has a land use agreement for a parcel may apply for a
  compliance report in respect of a structure located on the parcel or
  in respect of the use of the structure or parcel. The application must
  be accompanied by any fee payable in respect of that application under
  the [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent must, on application, issue a
  compliance report in respect of the conformity of the structure or of
  the use with the requirements of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The compliance report must specify any requirement of
  these Regulations that is deemed to be met with respect to the
  structure or the use by reason of the application of subsection 45(1)
  or section 46, as the case may be.

## [Inspection Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495475 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 30, the
  superintendent may, at any reasonable time and for the purpose of
  verifying compliance with the conditions of an authorization or permit
  or with the provisions of these Regulations, enter any place in a park

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in order to inspect a project carried out in
    accordance with an authorization or permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the superintendent believes on reasonable
    grounds that a project has been, is being or is likely to be carried
    out in that place without an authorization or permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to assist

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or person in charge of the place, and every
  person in the place, must give all assistance that is reasonably
  required for the superintendent to exercise their powers or carry out
  their duties and functions under subsection (1) and must provide the
  superintendent with any information that the superintendent may
  reasonably require for that purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Superintendent's order

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may order the taking of any action in the park that the
  superintendent considers appropriate to counter a threat to public
  health or to remedy the consequences of a contravention of these
  Regulations, including ordering a person to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} stop work on a project;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stop the use or occupation of a structure, in whole
    or in part; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} demolish, remove, repair or replace a structure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking of action by superintendent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 30, if the action ordered by the
  superintendent under subsection (1) is not taken, the superintendent
  may, at any reasonable time and on reasonable notice, enter any place,
  including any structure, in which the action was to be taken, and may
  take that action.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- dwelling‑place

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent must
not enter a dwelling‑place, or any place that is intended to be used and
is being used as a permanent or temporary dwelling‑place, unless the
superintendent has given notice to the occupant at least 24 hours in
advance and obtained their consent to enter.

## [Subdivision of Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495493 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[subdivision]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** In sections 32 to 40,
[[subdivision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the division of a parcel into
two or more parcels, the consolidation of two or more parcels or an
alteration to the boundaries of parcels where they are adjoining.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for preliminary approval

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person who has a land use agreement may apply to the Minister, in the
  form approved by the Minister, for the preliminary approval of a
  subdivision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by any fee
  payable in respect of that application under the [Parks Canada Agency
  Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4) and must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the applicant and their contact
    information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of any project that the applicant
    proposes to carry out on the land subject to the subdivision,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its location, including the municipal address, if
      any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the project site that includes
      a description of any existing structures and land features, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the type of proposed
      structures, if any, and their proposed uses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the proposed
    subdivision is in the interests of the park, having regard to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of any proposed project,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the park management plan and, in the case of a
      park community, the community plan, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} principles of responsible environmental
      stewardship and heritage conservation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed subdivision,
    including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a site plan of the existing parcels and a
      description of their uses,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a site plan of the parcels resulting from the
      subdivision and a description of their proposed uses, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the land subject
      to the subdivision is suitable for the proposed use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-application meeting

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The application for the
preliminary approval of a subdivision may be submitted only after the
applicant has consulted with the superintendent with respect to the land
use planning process.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public consultation

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
consult with the public with respect to an application for the
preliminary approval of a subdivision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation with Indigenous peoples

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** If a proposed subdivision
has any potential impact on the rights of Indigenous peoples recognized
and affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act, 1982](/eng/Const/),
the superintendent must consult with the Indigenous peoples who may be
affected before making a decision in respect of the preliminary approval
of the subdivision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preliminary approval

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may grant a
preliminary approval for a subdivision if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} each parcel that results from the subdivision has
  direct access to a public roadway;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any proposed project referred to in paragraph
  32(2)(b) is in conformity with the provisions of the Act and these
  Regulations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the subdivision is in the interests of the park,
  having regard to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of any proposed project
    referred to in paragraph 32(2)(b),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the park management plan and, in the case of a
    park community, the community plan, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} principles of responsible environmental
    stewardship and heritage conservation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of preliminary approval

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may make the
preliminary approval of the subdivision subject to any conditions that
the Minister considers appropriate, including that the applicant

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} make a request under the [National Parks of Canada
  Lease and Licence of Occupation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-25) to amend or surrender a lease
  or licence of occupation for purposes relating to the subdivision
  before applying for a permit under subsection 7(1) of these
  Regulations for a project that the applicant proposes to carry out on
  the land subject to the subdivision; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} apply for the permit under subsection 7(1) within one
  year after the day on which the preliminary approval is granted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation of preliminary approval

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may revoke
the preliminary approval of the subdivision if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the applicant has provided false or misleading information; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is convicted of an offence in respect
  of a contravention of any provision of these Regulations or any
  condition of the preliminary approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry of preliminary approval

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The preliminary approval
of a subdivision expires on the earlier of the second anniversary of the
day on which the approval is granted by the Minister under section 36 or
the day specified by the Minister in the approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final approval

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, on application, grant a final approval of a subdivision
  that has received a preliminary approval under section 36 and make the
  final approval subject to any conditions that the Minister considers
  appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application for the final approval must be
  accompanied by any fee payable in respect of that application under
  the [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4).

## [Timeshare Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495543 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a person who holds a lease or licence of occupation for
  a parcel to enter into or be a party to a timeshare agreement in
  respect of the parcel or any structure located on the parcel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[timeshare
  agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1), [[timeshare
  agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement entered into on or
  after the day on which these Regulations come into force between a
  person who holds a lease or licence of occupation for a parcel and
  another person that confers rights on the other person with respect to
  the use of the parcel or a structure located on the parcel for one or
  more periods in each year, whether the agreement is for a fixed or
  unfixed number of years.

## [Advisory Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495549 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishment

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent of a
park may establish committees to advise the superintendent on any matter
relating to land use planning.

## [Application to Pre-existing Permits, Structures and Uses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1495552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[former
regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In sections 44 to 46,
[[former regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Town of Jasper
Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1111), the [National
Parks Building Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1114), the
[National Parks Signs Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1130) or
the [National Parks of Canada Cottages
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-398), as they read immediately
before the coming into force of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit under former regulations

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** Every permit issued under
any of the former regulations that is valid immediately before the day
on which these Regulations come into force continues in effect for the
remainder of the period during which it would have been in effect had
these Regulations not come into force, unless it is suspended or revoked
in accordance with these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing structure

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who has a land use agreement for a parcel on which a structure
  was constructed or installed prior to the day on which these
  Regulations come into force is deemed to meet the requirements of
  section 23 and subsection 26(1) with respect to the structure if,
  immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into force,
  the requirements applicable with respect to the structure under the
  former regulations were met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When provision ceases to apply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) ceases to apply with respect to a
  structure if, on or after the day on which these Regulations come into
  force,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the floor area of the structure is increased; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a substantial portion of the structure is rebuilt,
    other than in the case where it is rebuilt because it has been
    damaged or destroyed by a natural or accidental cause and there is
    no increase to the floor area of the structure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing occupancy

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsection 3(2),
if a building was occupied for a particular purpose immediately before
the day on which these Regulations come into force and the occupancy was
permitted under the former regulations, the occupancy is permitted to
continue under these Regulations.
