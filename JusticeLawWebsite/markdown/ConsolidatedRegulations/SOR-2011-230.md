[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed entities, mortgages and
hypothecs

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 10(2)(b) of the [Interest Act](/eng/acts/I-15),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following entities are prescribed:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} partnerships,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} trusts settled for business or commercial
    purposes,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} unlimited liability corporations as defined in
    the Business Corporations Act, R.S.A. 2000, c. B-9,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} unlimited liability companies as defined in the
    Business Corporations Act, S.B.C. 2002, c. 57, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} unlimited companies as defined in the Companies
    Act, R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 81; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prescribed class of mortgages and hypothecs
  consists of those issued after January 1, 2012.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 2012.
