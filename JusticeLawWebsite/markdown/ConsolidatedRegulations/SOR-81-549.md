## Manitoba Egg Order {#manitoba-egg-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-549
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1981-07-03

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing of Eggs in Manitoba

P.C. 1981-1814 1981-07-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Manitoba Egg Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-549), C.R.C., c. 153, and
to make the annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the marketing
of eggs in Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-549) in substitution
therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871423 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Egg Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-549).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871425 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Egg
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eggs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any variety, grade or class of
    eggs of a domestic hen belonging to the species [Gallus
    Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} produced in Manitoba;
    ([oeufs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    eggs established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871431 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, by order, with respect to
persons and property situated within the Province of Manitoba, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of eggs locally within that Province under the Act and the
Plan, other than those powers that are exercised by the Canadian Egg
Marketing Agency pursuant to the Farm Products Marketing Agencies Act
and the proclamation and agreement made thereunder, as amended from time
to time.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871433 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized, in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with
respect to the marketing of eggs in interprovincial and export trade, to
make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges from
persons situated within the Province of Manitoba who are engaged in
production or the marketing of eggs and for such purposes to classify
such persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes including the
creation of reserves and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of eggs and the equalization or adjustment
among producers of eggs of moneys realized from the sale thereof during
such periods or time as it may determine.
