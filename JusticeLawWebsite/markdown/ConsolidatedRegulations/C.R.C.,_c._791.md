## Side Shows and Concessions Remission Order {#side-shows-and-concessions-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 791
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Temporary Entry of Goods Comprising Side Shows and
Concessions
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563768 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Side Shows and Concessions Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._791).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563770 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[concession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[concession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a concession at a circus,
    fair, exhibition or rodeo; ([concession de
    carnaval]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include anything that is
    imported for the purpose of being sold or disposed of in any manner
    in Canada; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 30 consecutive days;
    ([période]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[side show]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[side show]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a side show at a circus,
    fair, exhibition or rodeo. ([attractions foraines]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563779 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, remission is hereby granted of all
  taxes paid or payable under Division III of Part IX and under any
  other part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on goods imported
  into Canada and used as a side show or a concession in excess of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a side show,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$100 for each period or part thereof that the
      side show is used solely at fairs or exhibitions subsidized by the
      Department of Agriculture, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$200 for each period or part thereof that the
      side show remains in Canada and is used for purposes other than
      the purpose referred to in subparagraph (i); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a concession,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$50 for each period or part thereof that the
      concession is used solely at fairs or exhibitions subsidized by
      the Department of Agriculture, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$100 for each period or part thereof that the
      concession remains in Canada and is used for purposes other than
      the purpose referred to in subparagraph (i).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amounts set out in subsection (1) shall be
  reduced by one-half if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is established to the satisfaction of the
    Minister of National Revenue that the goods so imported were in lieu
    of and similar to goods that were not available in the immediate
    vicinity in Canada in which the imported goods were used at the time
    the goods were imported; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are used in Canada for less than two
    months.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-19, s. 1
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 3 shall be granted only

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the owner, at the time the goods to be used as a
  side show or concession are imported, makes an application in writing
  to the chief officer of customs at the customs office where the goods
  were reported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application referred to in paragraph (a) sets
  out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the places in Canada where the goods will be used,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the dates when the goods will commence and cease
    to be used at each place referred to in subparagraph (i);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the chief officer of customs is notified forthwith
  of any changes in respect of any information required by paragraph
  (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported immediately after the date
  they cease to be used as stated in the application under subparagraph
  (b)(ii) or the notification under paragraph (c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are presented to customs for examination
  prior to their exportation

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at the customs office where the goods were exported
    from Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at an inland customs office, where the goods will
    be conveyed to a customs office for exportation by a person
    authorized to transport goods pursuant to the [Transportation of
    Goods Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1064); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are presented to customs for
  examination pursuant to paragraph (e), if the following information is
  provided on a form satisfactory to the Minister:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods being exported,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the customs office where the goods
    were accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the transaction number and the date of the
    customs accounting documents, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the goods being exported were
    imported under the [Side Shows and Concessions Remission
    Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._791).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/90-138, s. 1(E)
- SI/91-8, s. 2(E)
