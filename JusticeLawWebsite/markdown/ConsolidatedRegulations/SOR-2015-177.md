## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820154 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aquaculture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aquaculture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the cultivation of
    fish. ([aquaculture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aquaculture licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aquaculture licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a lease or licence issued or granted by the
      Minister under section 7 or 58 of the Act, or its regulations, for
      the purpose of aquaculture;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lease granted by the government of a province
      under subsection 59(1) of the Act for the cultivation of oysters;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a provincial licence or authorization for the
      operation of an aquaculture facility. ([permis
      d'aquaculture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[barren substrate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[barren substrate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means benthic substrate on
    which there are no visible organisms. ([substrat
    stérile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[biochemical oxygen demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[biochemical oxygen demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    any organic matter that contributes to the consumption of oxygen
    that is dissolved in water or sediment. ([matière exerçant une
    demande biochimique en oxygène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Monitoring Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Monitoring Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Aquaculture
    Monitoring Standard, as amended from time to time, that is produced
    by the Minister and maintained on the Department of Fisheries and
    Oceans website. ([Norme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[soft bottom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soft bottom]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a benthic substrate that
    consists of loose particles such as clay, mud, marl, sand, pebbles,
    gravel, shells or small stones.  ([fond meuble]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Deleterious Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820167 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified substances

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 36(4)(c) of the Act, the following classes of substances
deposited in the operation of an aquaculture facility are specified to
be deleterious substances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} drugs whose sale is permitted or otherwise
  authorized, or whose importation is not prohibited, under the [Food
  and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pest control products that are registered, or whose
  use is authorized, under the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} biochemical oxygen demanding matter.

## [Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions applicable to deposits

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An owner or operator of an
aquaculture facility may, subject to the conditions set out in sections
4 to 14, deposit a deleterious substance specified in section 2 in any
water or place referred to in subsection 36(3) of the Act.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aquaculture facility

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The deleterious substance
must be deposited in the operation of the aquaculture facility and the
facility must be operated under an aquaculture licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drugs

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a deposit of
a drug,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if by or under an Act of Parliament the drug may only
  be sold under a prescription, it must be prescribed by a person who is
  duly authorized to practise veterinary medicine

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the laws of the province in which the
    aquaculture facility is located, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the laws of any province, if the aquaculture
    facility is not located in a province;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner or operator of the facility must take
  measures to minimize the risk of an accidental deposit of the drug;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the drug is deposited to control a pest as defined
  in the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), the owner or
  operator must consider, before depositing the drug, whether there are
  alternatives to the deposit of that drug and make a record of that
  consideration.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pest control products

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a deposit of
a pest control product,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product is registered, the owner
  or operator of the aquaculture facility must use it in compliance with
  any conditions specified under the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), including any conditions relating to the place
  where it may be used and the quantity and concentration that may be
  used;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the pest control product is not registered, it
  must be authorized to be used by the owner or the operator under
  subsection 21(5) or 41(1) of the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) or have been exempted from registration by a
  regulation made under paragraph 67(1)(z.4) of that Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the owner or the operator must consider, before
  depositing the pest control product, whether there are alternatives to
  the deposit of that pest control product and make a record of that
  consideration; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the owner or the operator must notify the Minister,
  at least 72 hours before the deposit, of the product name of the pest
  control product and the time, date and geographic coordinates of the
  deposit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measures to reduce detriment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or the operator of the aquaculture facility must, in depositing
  a deleterious substance referred to in paragraph 2(a) or (b), take
  reasonable measures to minimize detriment to fish and fish habitat
  outside the facility, having regard to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost and effectiveness of the available
    measures;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the degree and nature of the detriment that may
    result from the deposit; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the physical characteristics of the facility and
    the type of aquaculture that is engaged in.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feces and unconsumed feed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an aquaculture facility that
  cultivates finfish and that is operated under an aquaculture licence
  that permits a standing biomass of more than 2.5 t or an annual
  production of more than 5 t, the owner or operator of the facility
  must take reasonable measures to minimize the deposit of fish feces
  and unconsumed feed, having regard to the factors set out in
  paragraphs (1)(a) to (c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information required before depositing

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of an aquaculture facility located in tidal waters for the
  cultivation of finfish that commences operations after the day on
  which these Regulations come into force, the owner or operator of the
  facility must submit the following information to the Minister at
  least 300 days before making a first deposit of a deleterious
  substance in the operation of the facility:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the predicted contours of the footprint of the
    biochemical oxygen demanding matter that will be deposited by the
    facility, calculated in accordance with the Monitoring Standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a survey conducted in accordance with the
    Monitoring Standard that identifies the fish and fish habitat on the
    seabed that is leased for the operations of the facility and in the
    water column above the seabed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the bathymetry of the seabed that is leased for the
    operations of the facility, measured in accordance with the
    Monitoring Standard; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a facility located over a soft
    bottom, the additional information that is specified in the
    Monitoring Standard concerning the seabed that is leased for the
    operations of the facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if an aquaculture facility
  commences operations during the 300 days after the day on which these
  Regulations come into force, the owner or operator of the facility
  must provide the information referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (d)
  within 30 days after the day on which the operations were commenced.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monitoring Standard

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The studies undertaken to obtain the information
  referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (d) must be conducted in
  accordance with the Monitoring Standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to an
  acquaculture facility whose aquaculture licence, if issued or granted,
  permits a maximum standing biomass of 2.5 t or less or a maximum
  annual production of 5 t or less.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications affecting contours of
footprint

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  owner or operator of an aquaculture facility located in tidal waters
  that cultivates finfish makes an application under federal or
  provincial law that, if approved, would authorize any activity that is
  likely to increase the predicted contours of the footprint of the
  biochemical oxygen demanding matter deposited by the facility, the
  owner or the operator must conduct the studies necessary to obtain the
  information referred to in paragraphs 8(1)(a) to (d) and submit the
  information to the Minister within 30 days after the day on which the
  application was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to an acquaculture
  facility whose aquaculture licence, if amended, permits a maximum
  standing biomass of 2.5 t or less or a maximum annual production of 5
  t or less.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substrate sampling and restocking

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of an aquaculture facility that is located over a soft bottom and
  cultivates finfish in tidal waters in or adjacent to Quebec, Nova
  Scotia, New Brunswick, British Columbia, Prince Edward Island or
  Newfoundland and Labrador, the owner or operator of the facility

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must take samples of the benthic substrate in the
    manner and at the times and locations specified in the Monitoring
    Standard and determine the concentration of free sulfide in the
    samples in accordance with that Standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must take additional samples of the benthic
    substrate, in the manner and at the times and locations specified in
    the Monitoring Standard, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a facility located in tidal waters
      in or adjacent to Quebec, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince
      Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador, the mean concentration
      of free sulfide as calculated at the locations specified in the
      Monitoring Standard exceeds 3000 µM, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a facility located in tidal
      waters in or adjacent to British Columbia, the mean concentration
      of free sulfide as calculated at 30 m and 125 m from the structure
      that contains the cultivated fish exceeds 1300 µM and 700 µM,
      respectively; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} must not restock the facility if the concentrations
    of free sulfide exceed the applicable concentration limits set out
    in paragraph (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visual monitoring instead of sampling

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of an acquaculture facility
  must conduct visual monitoring in accordance with section 11 instead
  of the sampling described in subsection (1) if the owner or the
  operator cannot obtain, from each of the locations specified in the
  Monitoring Standard, benthic substrate samples that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain substrate to a depth of at least 5 cm; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a volume of at least 15 ml and undisturbed
    sediment-water interface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visual monitoring of substrate

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to aquaculture facilities that cultivate finfish in
  the waters referred to in subsection 10(1) but

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are not located over a soft bottom; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are located over a soft bottom in respect of which
    subsection 10(2) applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monitoring and restocking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of a facility referred to in
  subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must conduct visual monitoring of the benthic
    substrate in the manner and at the times and locations specified in
    the Monitoring Standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not, if the facility is located in tidal
    waters in or adjacent to Quebec, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince
    Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador, restock the facility if
    the visual monitoring shows the presence of Beggiatoa species or
    similar bacteria, marine worms or barren substrate in more than 70%
    of the locations specified in the Monitoring Standard; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} must not, if the facility is located in tidal
    waters in or adjacent to British Columbia, restock the facility if
    visual monitoring shows that Beggiatoa species or similar bacteria
    or marine worms cover

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 10% or more of any four segments of substrate
      specified in the Monitoring Standard that are within 100 m to 124
      m from the fish containment structure, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 10% or more of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the two contiguous segments of substrate
        specified in the Monitoring Standard that are within 116 m to
        124 m from the fish containment structure, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the two or more contiguous segments of
        substrate specified in the Monitoring Standard that are within
        124 m to 140 m from the fish containment structure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of Minister

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or operator of an aquaculture facility that cultivates finfish
  that is located in tidal waters in or adjacent to Quebec, Nova Scotia,
  New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador must
  notify the Minister if the following occurs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration limits set out in subparagraph
    10(1)(b)(i) are exceeded; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Beggiatoa species or similar bacteria, marine worms
    or barren substrate are present to the extent referred to in
    paragraph 11(2)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification --- British Columbia
  facilities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of an aquaculture facility that
  cultivates finfish that is located in tidal waters in or adjacent to
  British Columbia must notify the Minister if the following occurs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the concentration limits set out in subparagraph
    10(1)(b)(ii) are exceeded; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Beggiatoa species or similar bacteria or marine
    worms are present to the extent referred to in paragraph 11(2)(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time for notification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of an acquaculture facility
  that cultivates finfish that is located in tidal waters must notify
  the Minister within 14 days after the day on which the samples of the
  substrate were taken or the visual monitoring conducted, as the case
  may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fish morbidity or mortality

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  fish morbidity or mortality outside the aquaculture facility is
  observed from any part of the facility within 96 hours after the
  deposit of any drug or pest control product referred to in paragraph
  2(a) or (b), the owner or operator of the facility must immediately
  notify a fishery officer of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the facility and its operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the geographic coordinates of the fish observed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number and, if known, species of the
    fish observed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the product name of the drug or pest control
    product deposited and the date of the deposit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's directions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the owner or operator of an acquaculture facility
  is directed by the Minister to undertake measures under subsection
  36(6) of the Act, and the Minister advises the owner or the operator
  that the direction is related to a notification made by them under
  subsection (1), the owner or the operator must cease depositing the
  drug or pest control product referred to in subsection (1) until they
  have complied with the Minister's directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The owner or operator of
an acquaculture facility must submit an annual report to the Minister,
and retain a copy of it, in accordance with section 16.

## [Section 35 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820266 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed works, undertakings,
activities and conditions

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 35(2)(a) of the Act,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following works, undertakings and activities
    are prescribed:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the installation, operation, maintenance or
      removal of an aquaculture facility, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} measures to control biofouling or the presence
      of fish pathogens or pests in the aquaculture facility; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the following conditions are prescribed for the
    carrying on of those works, undertakings and activities:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aquaculture facility is operated under an
      aquaculture licence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the owner or operator of the facility takes
      reasonable measures to mitigate the risk of serious harm to fish
      outside the facility that are part of a commercial, recreational
      or Aboriginal fishery, having regard to

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the effectiveness of the available measures,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the degree of serious harm that may result from
        the carrying on of the works, undertakings and activities, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the physical characteristics of the facility
        and the type of aquaculture that is engaged in at the facility,
        and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the owner or operator submits an annual report
      to the Minister in accordance with section 16.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- deleterious substances

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The deposit of deleterious substances is not
  prescribed for the purpose of paragraph (1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- [Pacific Aquaculture
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-270)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any work,
  undertaking or activity carried on in any waters or place referred to
  in section 2 of the [Pacific Aquaculture
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-270).

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820284 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  annual report must be submitted in a form acceptable to the Minister
  and contain the following information in respect of the operation of
  the aquaculture facility during the calendar year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each deposit of a drug or pest control product
    during the year,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the product name of the drug or pest control
      product or the common chemical names of its active ingredients,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the deposit,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date, quantity and geographic coordinates
      of the deposit, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the record of consideration of alternatives
      referred to in paragraph 5(c) or 6(c);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of any measures taken under paragraph
    5(b), section 7 and subparagraph 15(1)(b)(ii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a facility referred to in section
    10,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the concentrations of free sulfide referred to in
      subsection 10(1), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if visual monitoring is required under
      subsection 10(2), the visual monitoring data collected; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a fishery officer was notified of fish morbidity
    or mortality in accordance with subsection 13(1), the information
    provided to the officer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due date of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An annual report must be submitted to the Minister on
  or before April 1 of the year following the year that is the subject
  of the report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report to be retained

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An owner or operator of an aquaculture facility must
  retain a copy of the annual report in the facility for a period of two
  years after the day on which it is submitted to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information prior to coming into force

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an owner or operator of an
  aquaculture facility is not required to include information in an
  annual report in respect of any period before the day on which these
  Regulations come into force.

## [Canadian Food Inspection Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820304 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 35(2)(a) of Act

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 35(2)(a) of the Act, the disposal of fish by
  the President of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency for the purposes
  of fish pathogen or pest control and the [Health of Animals
  Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3) is prescribed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit of deleterious substance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The President of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency
  may, for the purposes of fish pathogen or pest control and the [Health
  of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3), deposit a deleterious substance
  referred to in paragraph 2(a) or (b) in any water or place referred to
  in subsection 36(3) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
