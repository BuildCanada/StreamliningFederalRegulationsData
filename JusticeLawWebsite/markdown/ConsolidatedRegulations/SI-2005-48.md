## Order Transferring from the Department of Natural Resources to the Department of the Environment the Control and Supervision of the Large Final Emitters Group {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-natural-resources-to-the-department-of-the-environment-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-large-final-emitters-group .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-48
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2005-06-01

Order Transferring from the Department of Natural Resources to the
Department of the Environment the Control and Supervision of the Large
Final Emitters Group

P.C. 2005-909 2005-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Department of Natural Resources to the Department of
the Environment the control and supervision of the portion of the
federal public administration in the Department of Natural Resources
known as the Large Final Emitters Group.
