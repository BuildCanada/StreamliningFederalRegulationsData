## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707761 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[balance sheet value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the
    shares and ownership interests held by an entity, means the value
    reported on its balance sheet on an unconsolidated basis. ([valeur
    au bilan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[book value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2008-168, s.
    13\]]{.Repealed}

[[member of an association's group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member of an association's group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    same meaning as in subsection 386(2) of the Act. ([membre du groupe
    d'une association]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Purpose or Circumstance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707770 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed purpose or circumstance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 376(1)(h)(iii) of the Act and subject to the approval
required under paragraph 376(1)(h) of the Act, an association may
develop, design, hold, manage, manufacture, sell or otherwise deal with
data transmission systems, information sites, communication devices or
information platforms or portals that are used for a purpose or in a
circumstance that is materially related to the provision of financial
products or services by the association or a member of the association's
group.

## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707773 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 390(2)(f) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), a prescribed activity in relation to an
  entity is developing, designing, holding, managing, manufacturing,
  selling or otherwise dealing with any data transmission system,
  information site, communication device or information platform or
  portal that is used to provide information services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An association may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the sum of the following
  exceeds 5% of the association's regulatory capital:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the association and its subsidiaries,
    whether individually or jointly, would acquire in the entity under
    paragraph 390(2)(f) of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held by the association and its subsidiaries,
    whether individually or jointly, in entities engaging in an activity
    described in subsection (1) that the association holds control of,
    or a substantial investment in, under paragraph 390(2)(f) of the
    Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the association and its subsidiaries, whether individually or
    jointly, to entities engaging in an activity described in
    subsection (1) that the association holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 390(2)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An association may not acquire control of, or hold,
  acquire or increase a substantial investment in, an entity engaging in
  an activity described in subsection (1) if the entity engages in the
  business of accepting deposit liabilities or if the activities of the
  entity include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activities that an association is not permitted to
    engage in under any of sections 378, 382 and 382.1 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, except as may be permitted
    under paragraph 390(2)(e) of the Act or as may be permitted either
    to an association under paragraph 376(1)(f) of the Act or to a
    retail association under subparagraph 376(1)(i)(ii) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dealing in goods, wares or merchandise that an
    association is not permitted to deal in under subsection 376(3) of
    the Act, other than as permitted under subsection (1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} activities that an association is not permitted to
    engage in under section 381 of the Act if the entity engages in the
    activities of a finance entity or of any other entity prescribed
    under paragraph 390(3)(c) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} acquiring control of or acquiring or holding a
    substantial investment in another entity unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is controlled by
      the association, the association itself would be permitted under
      Part X of the Act to acquire a substantial investment in the other
      entity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is not controlled
      by the association, the association itself would be permitted to
      acquire a substantial investment in the other entity under
      subsection 388(2), paragraph 388(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 388(4)
      or 390(1) or (2) of the Act; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any activity prescribed under paragraph 390(3)(e)
    of the Act.

## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707794 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from restrictions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 3(3)(e)(ii), subsections 390(4) to (6) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether an association would be permitted to
acquire a substantial investment in an entity under subsection 388(2),
paragraph 388(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 388(4) or 390(1) or (2) of the
Act.

## [Non-application of Subsection 390(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707797 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 390(5) of the
Act does not apply where, under paragraph 390(2)(f) of the Act, an
association acquires control of, or acquires or increases of a
substantial investment in, an entity whose business is limited to
activities described in subsection 3(1).  Nothing in this section limits
the operation of subsections 3(2) and (3).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707800 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
