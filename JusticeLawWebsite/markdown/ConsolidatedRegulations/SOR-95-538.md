## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976391 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Consular Services Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976393 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[travel document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travel document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    documents that are issued by the Minister for travel purposes:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a passport issued pursuant to the [Canadian
      Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of identity; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} refugee travel papers. ([document de
      voyage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-976399 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A travel document is
subject to the payment of the fee set out in section 4.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The fee for a travel
document that is issued to a person of at least 16 years of age is \$25
and shall be paid when the application for the document is made.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2001-536, s. 2

  - [2]{.lawlabel} Section 4 of the Regulations, as it read immediately
    before the coming into force of these Regulations, continues to
    apply in respect of an application to a passport issuing office for
    a travel document that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made in person before that coming into force; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by mail or courier

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before that coming into force, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within five days after that coming into force
        if the application is accompanied by the fee referred to in that
        section.
