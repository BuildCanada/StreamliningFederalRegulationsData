## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527783 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Fishing Zones of Canada (Zones 4 and 5)
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1548).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527785 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[C.H.S. Chart]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[C.H.S. Chart]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian Hydrographic
    Service Chart; ([carte S.H.C.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Fishing Zone 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[Fishing Zone
    2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[Fishing Zone 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the areas of the sea prescribed as fishing zones in the
    [Fishing Zones of Canada (Zones 1, 2 and 3)
    Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1547) and described respectively
    as "Zone 1", "Zone 2" and "Zone 3" in the schedule thereto; ([zone

    2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} *et* [zone de pêche
    3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    and longitudes listed in Schedules I to IV. ([coordonnées
    géographiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAD83]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    geodetic system. ([NAD83]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescription of Fishing Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527793 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The area of the sea
described in section 4 as "Fishing Zone 4", and the area of the sea
described in section 5 as "Fishing Zone 5", are hereby prescribed as
fishing zones of Canada.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Fishing Zone 4 comprises an
area of the sea adjacent to the Atlantic coast of Canada bounded on its
outer perimeter

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by geodesic lines joining the points determined by
  reference to the geographical coordinates listed in Schedule I,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by arcs of circles circumscribed about points
  determined by reference to the geographical coordinates listed in
  Schedule II, so that each such arc has a radius of two hundred
  nautical miles and terminates at the points where it intersects with
  an immediately adjoining arc or with a geodesic line described in
  paragraph (a) or, in the case of Area 2 in Schedule I, with the arc
  defining the outer limit of the 200 nautical mile fishery conservation
  zone established by the United States of America pursuant to the
  Fishery Conservation and Management Act of the United States of
  America dated March 1, 1977,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} and, in the case of Area 2 in Schedule I, by the arc
  defining the outer limit of the 200 nautical mile fishery conservation
  zone established by the United States of America pursuant to the
  Fishery Conservation and Management Act of the United States of
  America dated March 1, 1977 from its point of intersection referred to
  in paragraph (b) to its point of intersection with a geodesic line
  described in paragraph (a),

but does not include Fishing Zones 1 and 2 and other areas within the
internal waters and territorial sea of Canada, or the waters of the
islands of St. Pierre and Miquelon, delimited by the territorial sea of
Canada between the point of Latitude 47°21′54″N, Longitude 56°29′40″W
(North American Datum 1927, hereinafter referred to as "NAD27") and the
point of Latitude 46°38′46″N, Longitude 55°54′12″W (NAD27), and by the
boundary defined in Schedule V.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-229, s. 1
- SOR/93-50, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Fishing Zone 5 comprises an
area of the sea adjacent to the Pacific coast of Canada bounded on its
outer perimeter

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by geodesic lines joining the points determined by
  reference to the geographical coordinates listed in Schedule III, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by arcs of circles circumscribed about points
  determined by reference to the geographical coordinates listed in
  Schedule IV, so that each such arc has a radius of 200 nautical miles
  and terminates at the points where it intersects with an immediately
  adjoining arc or with a geodesic line described in paragraph (a),

but does not include Fishing Zone 3 and other areas within the internal
waters and territorial sea of Canada.
