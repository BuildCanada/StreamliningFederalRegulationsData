## Cheque Issue Regulations, 1997 {#cheque-issue-regulations-1997 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-240

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1997-04-23

Cheque Issue Regulations, 1997

T.B. 825240 1997-04-17
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to section 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-240_e_hq_5729){#fn_SOR-97-240_e_hq_5729-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005729 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[cheque]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cheque]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Part I, a cheque, bill of exchange or other
      negotiable instrument, other than an instrument in an electronic
      form, issued in Canadian or foreign currency by, or on behalf of,
      the Receiver General; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Parts II and III, a cheque, bill of exchange
      or other negotiable instrument, other than an instrument in an
      electronic form, issued in Canadian or foreign currency by, or on
      behalf of, the appropriate Minister of a department.
      ([chèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[departmental bank account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[departmental bank account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account
    established in a financial institution by the Receiver General under
    Part II. ([compte bancaire d'un ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Deputy Receiver General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Deputy Receiver General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy
    Minister of Public Works and Government Services. ([sous-receveur
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holder for value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holder for value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 53 of the [Bills of Exchange Act](/eng/acts/B-4).
    ([détenteur à titre onéreux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Receiver General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Receiver General]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Public Works and Government Services. ([receveur
    général]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[signing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[signing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of Part I, a person authorized
      in writing by the Receiver General to sign cheques on behalf of
      the Receiver General or on behalf of the Deputy Receiver General,
      as the case may be, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of Part III, a person authorized
      in writing by the appropriate Minister of a department to sign
      cheques on behalf of that Minister. ([signataire
      autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Receiver General Cheques]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005741 .Part}

### [Issuing of Cheques]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005742 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Receiver General is
responsible for the procuring, numbering and safekeeping of the forms
for cheques.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  cheque issued must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 4, bear

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the Receiver General or a
      signing officer, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the Deputy Receiver General or
      a signing officer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear the name of the payee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be for a specified amount; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} bear no alteration in its amount or in the name of
    the payee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), every cheque that is
  issued must bear the date of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is issued;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the payment in respect of which it
    is issued is due; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which that payment is to be made.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Receiver General may authorize the dating of a
  cheque by reference only to the month and year in which the payment in
  respect of which the cheque is issued is due and without reference to
  any day of the month, if the cheque

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not released before the month in which the
    payment is due;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} represents a payment under an Act of Parliament
    that does not specify a day on which the payment must be made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} represents a recurring payment in a fixed amount
    authorized by an Act of Parliament; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is issued in respect of an amount that is due not
    more than once a month.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where authorization of the Receiver General is
  granted under subsection (3), the day of the month may nevertheless be
  shown on the cheque.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where the Receiver General
authorizes the signing of cheques by the use of equipment capable of
reproducing the signatures of the persons referred to in paragraph
3(1)(a), such cheques may be signed in that manner.

### [Replacement Cheques]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005763 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) and (4), where the payee of a cheque reports that
  the cheque has not been received by the payee, or the payee or holder
  for value of a cheque reports that the cheque has been lost, destroyed
  or stolen, the Receiver General or a signing officer shall issue a
  replacement cheque to the payee or holder for value, in the name of
  the payee or holder for value, for the same amount as the original
  cheque if the payee or holder for value provides a form of
  undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The form of undertaking referred to in subsection (1)
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comprise

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the payee or holder for
      value,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount, date of issue and purpose of the
      cheque that was not received or was lost, destroyed or stolen, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking whereby the payee or holder for
      value agrees to return the original cheque to the Receiver General
      if it comes into the possession of the payee or holder for value
      and to indemnify and save harmless Her Majesty in right of Canada
      from any loss or expense incurred in connection therewith;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the payee or holder for value before a
    witness, and by that witness; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be forwarded to the Receiver General.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the payee or holder for value of a cheque
  referred to in subsection (1) is a supplier or the recipient of grants
  or contributions and the original cheque is for an amount greater than
  \$5,000, the Receiver General or a signing officer shall not issue a
  replacement cheque to the payee or holder for value unless the payee
  or holder for value provides, together with the form of undertaking
  referred to in subsection (1), an affidavit or statutory declaration
  sworn or declared before a commissioner for oaths, justice of the
  peace or other person before whom affidavits or declarations may be
  lawfully sworn or declared in Canada or abroad, respecting the
  non-receipt, loss, destruction or theft of the original cheque.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Receiver General or a signing officer shall issue
  a replacement cheque to a payee referred to in subsection (1) without
  the payee having to provide an undertaking if the payee requests in
  writing the issue of a replacement cheque and the cheque represents

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a recurring payment in a fixed amount authorized by
    an Act of Parliament; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a payment made under a program where the payee is
    required to provide an undertaking as part of the terms and
    conditions of the program.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Despite anything in this
Part, the Receiver General or a signing officer shall issue a
replacement cheque, without requiring the provision of any document that
would otherwise be required under this Part, if the Receiver General is
notified by an employee or public officer of a department, the Canada
Post Corporation or another carrier, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police
or a provincial or municipal police force that a cheque cannot be
delivered to the payee of the cheque by reason of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an act of God;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fire;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} theft;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} loss or accidental destruction of the cheque in the
  course of delivery; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} inaccessibility of the cheque that will probably
  result in a delay in delivery beyond the date on which the payment in
  respect of which the cheque was issued is due.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Departmental Bank Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005783 .Part}

### [Establishment by Receiver General --- Authorized Expenditures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005784 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister of a department who is required to make
  expenditures in locations where the normal facilities for the issue of
  Receiver General cheques are not immediately available may apply to
  the Receiver General for the establishment of a departmental bank
  account in the name of that department or a branch or division of that
  department.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the receipt of an application under subsection
  (1), the Receiver General may establish a departmental bank account in
  the name of the department, or branch or division of the department,
  referred to in that subsection, from which only the following classes
  of expenditures may be made:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} emergency salary advances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} accountable travel advances, where the use of a
    travel credit card or participation in the traveller's cheque
    program is not a satisfactory alternative to the appropriate
    Minister;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reimbursement of travel and removal expenses;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} payment of supplies and services acquired under
    local purchase authority where immediate payment is required;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} reimbursement of expenditures made from petty cash
    advances;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} brokerage fees and customs duties payable to an
    independent customs broker;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} postage; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} payment of salaries and wages to part-time and term
    employees who are employed locally under the Locally-Engaged Staff
    Employment Regulations, 1995 and who are not paid through the
    Canadian Forces, Royal Canadian Mounted Police or public service pay
    systems under section 12 of the [Department of Public Works and
    Government Services Act](/eng/acts/P-38.2).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Two signatures are required on any cheque drawn on a
  departmental bank account for the expenditures referred to in
  subsection (2), unless, at the request of the appropriate Minister of
  the department, the Treasury Board authorizes a single signature.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), no expenditure in excess
  of \$5,000 shall be made from a departmental bank account without the
  approval of the Treasury Board.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Expenditures in excess of \$5,000 may be made from a
  departmental bank account without the approval of the Treasury Board
  for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paying locally engaged employees, if the department
    has the authority to make such payments; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issuing an emergency salary advance under paragraph
    4(b) of the [Accountable Advances
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-438).

### [Further Authorization of Expenditures by Treasury Board]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005801 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  departmental bank account has been established by the Receiver
  General, or the establishment of such an account is being applied for,
  under section 7, the appropriate Minister of the department may apply
  to the Treasury Board for authorization to make from that account
  expenditures other than those set out in subsection 7(2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the receipt of an application under subsection
  (1), the Treasury Board may grant an authorization to the appropriate
  Minister, which authorization must be in writing and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the classes of expenditures for which the
    authorization is granted; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of signatures required on any cheque
    drawn on the departmental bank account for those expenditures.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005807 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Despite the establishment
of a departmental bank account by the Receiver General under section 7
or the granting of any authorization in respect of that account by the
Treasury Board under section 8, the Receiver General may direct the
appropriate Minister of the department to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} direct the financial institution in which that
  account is established to refuse payment of any cheque drawn on that
  account;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} withdraw the authority given to any person to draw
  cheques on that account; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} discontinue that account and pay the balance
  remaining in it to the Receiver General.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Departmental Bank Account Cheques]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005812 .Part}

### [Issuing of Cheques]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005813 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Receiver General is
responsible for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} procuring and numbering the forms of cheques to be
  drawn on every departmental bank account; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishing procedures to be followed for the
  operation of departmental bank accounts.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every cheque drawn on a
departmental bank account must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} bear the signature of one or more signing officers,
  as the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear the name of the payee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be for a specified amount;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bear no alteration in its amount or in the name of
  the payee; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} bear the date of the day on which it is issued or on
  which the payment in respect of which it is issued is due.

### [Replacement Cheques]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1005823 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where the
  payee of a cheque drawn on a departmental bank account reports that
  the cheque has not been received by the payee, or the payee or holder
  for value of such a cheque reports that the cheque has been lost,
  destroyed or stolen, one or more signing officers, as the case may be,
  shall issue a replacement cheque to the payee or holder for value, in
  the name of the payee or holder for value, for the same amount as the
  original cheque, if the payee or holder for value provides

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a form of undertaking that meets the requirements
    of subsection 5(2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the original cheque was for an amount greater
    than \$5,000, an affidavit or statutory declaration sworn or
    declared before a commissioner for oaths, justice of the peace or
    other person before whom affidavits or declarations may be lawfully
    sworn or declared in Canada or abroad, respecting the non-receipt,
    loss, destruction or theft of the original cheque.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No replacement cheque shall be issued in accordance
  with subsection (1) unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original cheque has not been cashed; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a stop payment direction has been given to the
    financial institution in respect of the original cheque.
