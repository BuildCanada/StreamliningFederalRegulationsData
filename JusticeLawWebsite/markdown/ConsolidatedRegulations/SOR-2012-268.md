## Federal Credit Union Conversion Regulations {#federal-credit-union-conversion-regulations .Title-of-Act}

P.C. 2012-1627 2012-12-06

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787785 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[board authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[board authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the decision by
      the board of directors of a federal credit union to pursue
      conversion. ([autorisation du conseil]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal credit union
      becoming a bank with common shares as a result of the issuance of
      letters patent by the Minister under section 216.08 of the Act.
      ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[converted credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[converted credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal
      credit union that has been converted into a bank with common
      shares. ([transformée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[converting credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[converting credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a federal
      credit union that is proposing to convert into a bank with common
      shares. ([en transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[market terms and conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[market terms and conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 501(2) of the Act. ([conditions du
      marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[related party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 486 of the Act. ([apparenté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[value of the converting credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[value of the converting credit union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the market value of the converting credit union as of the day on
      which the conversion occurs or such other day as may be specified
      by the Superintendent under paragraph 7(a), including that of its
      membership shares and shares, if any, but excluding

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any capital to be contributed as part of the
        conversion proposal; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any expense to be incurred by the converting
        credit union to effect conversion. ([valeur de la coopérative de
        crédit en transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[valuation expert]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[valuation expert]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person capable of
      providing an independent and fair certification of the value of
      the converting credit union. ([évaluateur expert]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, any reference to an estimated
  value may be read as a reference to a range of estimated values.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787800 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the conversion of a federal credit union into a bank with
common shares under section 216.08 of the Act.

## [Conversion Proposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787802 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  conversion proposal must be written in language that is clear and
  simple and in a manner that is not misleading, and must include all
  information that members and shareholders, if any, of the converting
  credit union would reasonably require or expect to be given to make an
  informed decision about the proposed conversion, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date of conversion or a description of
    the event with which the converting credit union proposes that its
    conversion coincide;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed name of the converted credit union;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the advantages and disadvantages
    of the proposed conversion in relation to the converting credit
    union and to its members and shareholders, if any, as a whole;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the alternatives to conversion
    that the converting credit union's board of directors considered,
    and the reasons why, in their opinion, the conversion is in the best
    interests of the converting credit union and its members and
    shareholders, if any, as a whole;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the converting credit union's
    business plan for the three-year period following the day on which
    the conversion occurs, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the services it intends to
      provide,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any services it no longer
      intends to provide,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if any service is proposed to be provided by
      another entity, the name of that entity and its relationship to
      the converting credit union and its related parties, if any, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of any anticipated changes to the
      geographic area within which the converting credit union intends
      to provide its services;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description, including the value, of any
    securities that are proposed to be issued, or cash that is proposed
    to be paid, as a result of the conversion, to a person that owns a
    membership share or share of the converting credit union;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the number of members and
    shareholders, if any, of the converting credit union that, on the
    day on which the conversion occurs, will become common or other
    shareholders of the converted credit union;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether the converted credit
    union is expected, within the three-year period following the day on
    which the conversion occurs, to be controlled by a shareholder or
    shareholder group or to have any shareholder with a significant
    interest in any class of its shares;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of any proposed changes to the
    following that would arise from conversion:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of directors of the converting credit
      union, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the rights of its members and shareholders, if
      any, with respect to its governance, including their rights to
      participate in meetings and elect directors and the rights of
      shareholders to control the converted credit union;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of any changes to the rights of
    shareholders arising from the requirements of section 5;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} for the one-year period before the day on which the
    board authorization occurs,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of any related party's interest in or
      relationship to the converting credit union and any change in a
      related party's ownership of membership shares or shares of the
      converting credit union, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any transactions between the
      converting credit union and a related party;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a discussion of the fairness of the conversion
    proposal to the converting credit union's members and shareholders,
    if any, as a whole, as well as an independent opinion on the
    fairness of the proposal by a person with the necessary skills and
    experience to provide such an opinion;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the valuation report referred to in
    paragraph 6(k), which must include the certification of the
    reasonableness of the values and assumptions contained in the report
    and the information referred to in subparagraphs 6(k)(iii) to (v);

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} summary pro forma financial statements for the
    converted credit union for the three-year period following the day
    on which the conversion occurs that are based on one of the
    following and prepared in a manner consistent with the annual
    statement, as well as a report or comments on those statements by
    the converting credit union's auditor:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the date of the most recent annual financial
      statement for the converting credit union is more than 120 days
      before the giving of notice in respect of a special resolution
      referred to in paragraph (s), the financial statement for the
      converting credit union for the most recently completed quarter,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the annual statement for the
      most recently completed financial year of the converting credit
      union;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} an indication of the expected capital requirements
    of the converted credit union, based on the summary statements
    referred to in paragraph (n), and the anticipated sources of that
    capital;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of the terms of and date proposed for
    any offering of securities by the converted credit union during the
    three-year period following the day on which the conversion occurs;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} a description of any corporate reorganization that
    is expected to be participated in by the converted credit union
    during the three-year period following the day on which the
    conversion occurs;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} a description of the converted credit union's
    dividends and patronage allocation policy, compared to that of the
    converting credit union;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any special resolution that the members
    and shareholders, if any, of the converting credit union are to
    consider under subsection 216.09(1) of the Act;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any proposed by-law for the converted
    credit union;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} the name of the proposed auditor and any proposed
    transfer agent for the converted credit union;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the board of directors of the
    converting credit union may withdraw the conversion proposal at any
    time before the issuance of letters patent;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the restrictions referred to in
    section 9; and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the tax consequences of conversion for
    members and shareholders, if any, of the converting credit union.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the conversion proposal that has been
  approved by the Superintendent must be provided with the notice of the
  meeting at which the special resolution referred to in
  subsection 216.09(1) of the Act is to be considered.

## [Amendment or Withdrawal of Conversion Proposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787840 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  converting credit union's board of directors may amend the conversion
  proposal at any time before conversion so long as the amended
  conversion proposal is approved by the Superintendent and by the
  members and shareholders, if any, of the converting credit union in
  accordance with paragraph 216.09(1)(a) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The converting credit union's board of directors may
  withdraw the conversion proposal at any time before conversion.

## [By-Law Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787844 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Unless the conversion
proposal provides that the common shares of the converted credit union
will be listed on a public market within the three-year period following
the day on which the conversion occurs, the converted credit union's
by-laws must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a limitation on the number of common shares of the
  converted credit union that may be issued;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a restriction on a shareholder's right to transfer a
  common share if the transfer is not approved by a special resolution
  of the common shareholders and, as a result of the transfer, the
  transferee acquires a significant interest in or control of the
  converted credit union that is not anticipated by the conversion
  proposal; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement that any certificate issued for a common
  share must note conspicuously that the transfer of the share is
  restricted under the by-laws of the converted credit union.

## [Material to Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787849 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The converting credit union
must supply to the Superintendent, along with a copy of its conversion
proposal for the Superintendent's approval, the following items:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed name of the converted credit union;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date of conversion or a description of
  the event with which the converted credit union proposes that its
  conversion coincide;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a certified copy of the resolution of the converting
  credit union's board of directors authorizing conversion, and a
  certificate from the converting credit union indicating that its board
  of directors has approved the conversion proposal as being fair and
  reasonable to its members and shareholders, if any, as a whole;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any special resolution that the members and
  shareholders, if any, of the converting credit union are to consider
  under subsection 216.09(1) of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed letters patent of conversion;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any by-law proposed for the converted
  credit union;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of any information in addition to that
  contained in the conversion proposal that is proposed to be provided
  to the members and shareholders, if any, of the converting credit
  union for review before the date for the making of the special
  resolution;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} details of any information sessions for members and
  shareholders, if any, that have been or will be held by the converting
  credit union and a description of any public advertising proposed by
  the converting credit union to advertise those sessions;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the pro forma financial statements and auditor's
  report or comments referred to in paragraph 3(1)(n) and statements of
  any necessary reconciliations;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the information required in paragraph 3(1)(o);

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a report prepared by a valuation expert setting out
  the following information and certifying that the values and
  assumptions contained in the report are reasonable:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the valuation expert,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated value of the converting credit
    union, the estimated market value of its membership shares and the
    estimated market value of its shares, if any, as of the day on which
    the conversion occurs or such other day as may be specified by the
    Superintendent under paragraph 7(a),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which those estimates have been
    rendered, including an indication of any assumptions relied upon in
    doing so,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the day or days, if any, on which those estimates
    are to be adjusted, and the manner of doing so, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the value of the converting credit
    union, the market value of its membership shares and the market
    value of its shares, if any, as of the day on which the conversion
    occurs will be calculated, and the manner of doing so;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description, including the value, of any securities
  that are proposed to be issued, or cash that is proposed to be paid,
  as a result of the conversion, to a person that owns a membership
  share or share of the converting credit union;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the independent fairness opinion required by
  paragraph 3(1)(l);

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the name of any person that will, as a result of
  conversion, own more than five per cent of the common shares or a
  class of shares of the converted credit union, or that is expected to
  own that amount in accordance with a statement of anticipated sources
  of capital under paragraph 3(1)(o), and an indication of whether that
  person is expected to be a related party; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any offering of securities for public
  subscription, a copy of any offering document for any current offering
  and a copy of any offering document, or the most recent draft of such
  a document, for any offering that is anticipated to occur no later
  than 30 days following the day on which the conversion occurs.

## [Superintendent's Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787871 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent may,
prior to approving the conversion proposal, including an amended
conversion proposal,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} specify the day as of which the value of the
  converting credit union, the market value of its membership shares and
  the market value of its shares, if any, must be estimated for the
  purposes of subparagraph 6(k)(ii);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} require a converting credit union to hold one or more
  information sessions for its members and shareholders, if any, and set
  the rules under which the information sessions must be held;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} direct the converting credit union to take other
  measures to assist its members and shareholders, if any, in forming a
  reasoned judgment on the conversion proposal, including by providing
  additional valuations, fairness opinions and other information further
  to that required elsewhere in these Regulations; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} exempt a converting credit union, on any terms and
  conditions that the Superintendent considers appropriate, from any of
  the requirements of subparagraphs 6(k)(iv) and (v).

## [Restrictions on Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787877 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on the day on which the
  board authorization occurs and ending on the day on which the
  conversion occurs, the converting credit union must not pay any fee,
  compensation or other consideration to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its related parties or employees, except for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} regular fees and compensation on market terms and
      conditions, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the member and shareholder benefits referred to
      in paragraph (b); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its members or shareholders, if any, except for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} interest on deposits and securities on market
      terms and conditions,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} dividends on shares and membership shares on
      market terms and conditions, in accordance with the converting
      credit union's by-laws and section 12.1 of the Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} patronage allocations and rebates of interest
      on loans on market terms and conditions, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any member or shareholder benefits specifically
      provided for in the conversion proposal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Beginning on the day on which the board authorization
  occurs and ending one year following the day on which the conversion
  occurs, the converting credit union must not issue or provide shares,
  share options or rights to acquire shares, other than those issued as
  a result of implementing the conversion proposal, to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any director, officer or employee of the converted
    credit union; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who was a director, officer or employee
    of the converting credit union during the year preceding the day on
    which the conversion occurs.

## [Restriction on Issuance of Membership Shares and Securities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787891 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the converting credit union is subject to an order to increase its
  capital under subsection 485(3) of the Act, it must not issue
  membership shares or securities beginning on the day on which the
  board authorization occurs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the issuance

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a person applying for membership in the
    converting credit union of the minimum number of membership shares
    required for membership under the by-laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} of membership shares in payment of a patronage
    allocation to the converting credit union's members; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} of securities other than shares on market terms and
    conditions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A converting credit union must not accept any new
  applications for membership or issue any new membership shares
  beginning on the thirtieth day before the date of the valuation report
  referred to in paragraph 6(k).

## [Notice of Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787899 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible
following the board authorization, the converting credit union must
publish an announcement at least once a week for a period of four
consecutive weeks in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and
in a newspaper of general circulation in each location in which its
members normally reside, and continuously for four consecutive weeks on
its website, advising members of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed conversion;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the restrictions set out in sections 8 and 9; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} its policy on accepting new members beginning on the
  day on which the board authorization occurs and ending once the
  acceptance of new members is prohibited under subsection 9(2).

## [Application to Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787904 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An application made under
section 216.08 of the Act must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information referred to in paragraphs 6(a) and
  (b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the conversion proposal;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the Superintendent has
  approved the conversion proposal and the date on which it was
  approved;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the special resolutions of the converting credit
  union's members and shareholders, if any, accompanied by a certificate
  from the converting credit union's board of directors indicating the
  results of the votes held in respect of the resolutions;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the material referred to in paragraphs 6(k) to (m);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the converting credit union's response to any
  requirement imposed by the Superintendent under section 7;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the conversion is in the best
  interests of the Canadian financial system, including the cooperative
  financial system;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any by-laws necessary to implement the conversion
  proposal; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed letters patent of conversion.

## [Converting Credit Unions Subject to the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-787915 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A converting credit union
that is a bridge institution within the meaning of section 2 of the
[Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3) or is subject
to an order under paragraph 39.13(1)(a) of that Act

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} need not include the information referred to in
  paragraphs 3(1)(d) to (f), (h) to (r), (w) and (x) in its conversion
  proposal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} need not supply to the Superintendent the information
  referred to in paragraphs 6(c) to (o);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} need not include the information referred to in
  paragraphs 11(c) to (h) in an application under section 216.08 of the
  Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not subject to sections 4, 5 and 7 to 10.
