## General Import Permit No. 7 --- Turkeys and Turkey Products for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-7-turkeys-and-turkey-products-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-38
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1994-12-30

General Import Permit No. 7 --- Turkeys and Turkey Products for Personal
Use
::::

:::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas turkeys and turkey products have been included on the [Import
Control List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604);

And Whereas the Secretary of State for External Affairs has determined
an import access quantity for turkeys and turkey products pursuant to
subsection 6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDADA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

Therefore, the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to
section 10[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EPECA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby cancels the
[Import of Turkeys and Turkey Products
Permit](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-122), issued on February 4,
1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#footnote3_e){#footnote3_e-ID0EJECA}, and,
pursuant to subsection 8.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*\*^](#footnote4_e){#footnote4_e-ID0EHECA} of the
[Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues the
annexed General permit authorizing the importation of turkeys and turkey
products for personal use, effective on the later of the days on which
sections 74, 106, 109 and 111 of [An Act to implement the Agreement
Establishing the World Trade Organization](/eng/acts/W-11.8), being
chapter 47 of the Statutes of Canada, 1994, come into force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975272 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,

[[turkey products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods set out in
    items 2 to 10 of the schedule; ([produits de dindons et
    dindes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkeys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkeys]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods set out in item 1 of
    the schedule. ([dindons et dindes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-53, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975278 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada for the personal
use of the importer and the importer's household

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one turkey; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} turkey products in a quantity not exceeding 10 kg in
  net weight for each importation.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where any goods imported
under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of *General
Import Permit No. 7 --- Turkeys and Turkey Products for Personal Use*"
or ["Importé en vertu de la *Licence générale d'importation n^o^ 7 ---
Dindons et dindes et produits de dindons et dindes pour usage
personnel*"]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-975284 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Live turkeys, weighing more than 185 g, that are classified
  under tariff item No. 0105.99.11 or heading No. 98.04 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **2** Meat and edible offal of turkeys, fresh, chilled or frozen, not
  cut in pieces, that are classified under tariff item No. 0207.24.11,
  0207.24.91, 0207.25.11 or 0207.25.91 in the List of Tariff Provisions
  set out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **3** Cuts of meat and edible offal, including livers, of turkeys,
  fresh, chilled or frozen, that are classified under tariff item No.
  0207.26.10, 0207.27.11 or 0207.27.91 in the List of Tariff Provisions
  set out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **4** Turkey fat (not rendered or otherwise extracted), fresh, chilled
  or frozen, salted, in brine, dried or smoked, that is classified under
  tariff item No. 0209.90.30 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **5** Meat of turkeys, salted, in brine, dried or smoked, that is
  classified under tariff item No. 0210.99.14 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **6** Sausages and similar products, made from meat, meat offal or
  blood of turkeys, and food preparation based on those products (other
  than in cans or glass jars), that are classified under tariff item No.
  1601.00.31 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **7** Prepared or preserved liver paste, made from turkeys (other than
  in cans or glass jars), that is classified under tariff item No.
  1602.20.31 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **8** Prepared meals, made from turkeys (other than specially defined
  mixtures), that are classified under tariff item No. 1602.31.12 in the
  List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **9** Prepared or preserved meat or meat offal of turkeys (other than
  prepared meals and specially defined mixtures and other than in cans
  or glass jars) that is classified under tariff item No. 1602.31.93 in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- **10** Goods that are classified under heading No. 98.04 or 98.26 in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and that are otherwise classifiable under
  a tariff item referred to in any of items 2 to 9 of this schedule.
