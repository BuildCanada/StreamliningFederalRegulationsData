## Order Renaming the Canadian Armed Forces Naval Jack as the Canadian Naval Ensign and Approving its use by the Canadian Forces {#order-renaming-the-canadian-armed-forces-naval-jack-as-the-canadian-naval-ensign-and-approving-its-use-by-the-canadian-forces .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-51
:::

Registration 2013-05-08

Order Renaming the Canadian Armed Forces Naval Jack as the Canadian
Naval Ensign and Approving its use by the Canadian Forces

P.C. 2013-436 2013-04-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} renames the Canadian Armed Forces Naval Jack as the
  Canadian Naval Ensign, which is described as follows:

  Argent a fouled anchor surmounted by an eagle volant affronty head to
  the sinister, all ensigned by a naval coronet Azure, a canton of the
  National Flag of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} approves the Canadian Naval Ensign for use by the
  Canadian Forces as directed by the Minister of National Defence or his
  or her delegate.
