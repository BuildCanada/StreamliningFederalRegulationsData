## Designating the Minister of Justice and the President of the Treasury Board as Ministers for Purposes of Certain Sections of the Act {#designating-the-minister-of-justice-and-the-president-of-the-treasury-board-as-ministers-for-purposes-of-certain-sections-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-108
:::

[ACCESS TO INFORMATION ACT](/eng/acts/A-1)

Registration 1983-06-22

Designating the Minister of Justice and the President of the Treasury
Board as Ministers for Purposes of Certain Sections of the Act

P.C. 1983-1583 1983-06-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition [[designated
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3 of the [Access to
Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1983-431 of 17th
  February, 1983[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCA};

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCA){#footnote1_e}SI/83-48,
    1983 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1036
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Justice, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, to act as the Minister for the
  purposes of paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 3, subsection 4(2), paragraphs
  77(1)(f) and (g) and subsection 77(2) of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to designate the President of the Treasury Board, a
  member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, to act as the Minister
  for all other purposes of the Act.
