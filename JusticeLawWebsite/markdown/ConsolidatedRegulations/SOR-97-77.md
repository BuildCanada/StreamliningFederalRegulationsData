## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Permit.

[[imported from Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported from Israel or another CIFTA
    beneficiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    [Regulations Defining Certain Expressions for the Purposes of the
    Customs Tariff](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-62). ([importé d'Israël ou
    d'un autre bénéficiaire de l'ALÉCI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[roses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[roses]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cut roses and rose buds, fresh,
    suitable for bouquets or ornamental purposes that are classified
    under tariff item No. 0603.10.11 or under heading No. 98.04 or 98.06
    in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([roses]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-76, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1012619 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, a
resident of Canada may, under the authority of this Permit, import from
Israel or another CIFTA beneficiary, roses.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where roses imported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be accounted for in the
prescribed form under subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form shall contain the statement "Imported
under the authority of [General Import Permit No. 193 ---
Roses](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-77)" or "[Importé en vertu de la [Licence
générale d'importation n^o^ 193 ---
Roses](/eng/regulations/DORS-97-77)]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Permit applies only to
roses that are imported and accounted for under subsection 32(1), (3) or
(5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) on or before the day, in a
calendar year, on which the annual quota of 90,000 dozen roses as set
out in the Order Specifying Limits on the Annual Aggregate Quantity of
Roses of Tariff Item No. 0603.10.11 that are Entitled to the
Canada-Israel Agreement Tariff is reached.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-76, s. 2
:::
