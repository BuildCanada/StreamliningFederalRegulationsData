## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673697 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed circumstances

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 453(3)(f)(ii) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), subsections 453(4) to (6) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a company would be permitted to acquire a
substantial investment in an entity under subsection 451(2), paragraph
451(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 451(4) or 453(1) or (2) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673700 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6016){#fn_SOR-2001-386_e_hq_6016-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
paragraphs 459(a) and (b) of the [Trust and Loan Companies
Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8), as enacted by section 550 of the [Financial
Consumer Agency of Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the
Statutes of Canada, 2001, come into force.
