**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cultural Property Export and
    Import Act](/eng/acts/C-51). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any object or class of objects
    described in subsection 4(2) of the Act that is included in the
    Control List. ([objet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any original documentary
    material made by or received by a public authority or the
    predecessor in the territory that is now Canada of that public
    authority that contains information relating to the organization,
    function, procedure, policy or activity of that public authority.
    ([pièce d'archives publiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 3

## [Application for an Export Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557148 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
export permit to export any object shall be made in the form established
by the Minister, signed by the applicant and forwarded to a permit
officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following information
shall be furnished and forwarded with an application referred to in
section 3 in respect of the object to which the application applies:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the applicant is not the owner of the object,
  the name, address and telephone number of the owner;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the shipper
  or consignor;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  consignee;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 6, a description of the object in
  sufficient detail to clearly identify it;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date of export of the object;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} details in respect of the purpose of exporting the
  object;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value in Canada or, if sold, the
  selling price of the object;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any information requested by the permit officer to
  whom the application is made or by any expert examiner to whom the
  application has been referred or by the Minister if, in the opinion of
  one of them, the information furnished by the applicant requires
  clarification, or the identification of the object, is not in
  sufficient detail;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence indicating the proposed
  destination of the object; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any other information that is required.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 5
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) and (4), there shall be included with an
  application for an export permit in respect of an object a photograph
  or, in the case of an object that is a document, a photograph or
  photostatic copy of the object, satisfactory to the Minister, where
  the application is in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an object described in paragraph 2(a), (b) or (e)
    of Group I of the Control List;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an object described in paragraph 3(a), (b), (c)
    or (d) of Group I of the Control List;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an object or collection of objects described in
    item 4 of Group I of the Control List;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an object described in any item in Group II, III,
    IV, V or VI of the Control List;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an object described in paragraph 2(a) or (b), 3(a)
    or (b), 4(a) or (b) or 5(a) or (b) of Group VII of the Control List;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an object, other than a printed book, described in
    item 7 of Group VII of the Control List.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), there shall be included
  with an application for an export permit in respect of a collection of
  objects described in paragraph 2(c) of Group I of the Control List a
  photograph, satisfactory to the Minister, of each specimen in the
  collection.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), where an object
  described in paragraph (1)(e) or (f) contains more than 12 pages, the
  application for an export permit for that object need include only
  photograph or photostatic copies satisfactory to the Minister of
  12 pages that are representative of the object as a whole.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to an
  application for an export permit in respect of the following objects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an object referred to in paragraph 7(a) of the Act
    that was imported into Canada for a temporary purpose, other than
    resale, if the application includes

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a permit or document described in
      subparagraph 6(c)(i),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an A.T.A. Carnet described in
      subparagraph 6(c)(iii), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a declaration described in
      subparagraph 6(c)(iv);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an object referred to in paragraph 7(b) of the Act
    if the application includes the reference number used by the
    institution or public authority to identify or catalogue the object;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an object referred to in paragraph 7(c) of the Act
    that is to be exported by an institution or public authority if the
    application includes the inventory number, accession number or other
    reference number used by the institution or public authority to
    identify or catalogue the object.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When an application is
being made for an export permit in respect of an object to which
section  7 of the Act applies, the object may be identified in the
application by setting out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the object;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the maker of the object; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reference number

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the export permit issued by the government of
    the foreign state if that state authorized the exportation of the
    object into Canada, or the shipping document covering the entry into
    Canada of the object that clearly identifies the object if a copy of
    the export permit or shipping document, as the case may be, or a
    translation of it in one of the official languages, is forwarded
    with the application,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} issued by an institution or public authority in
    Canada to identify or catalogue the object,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} of an A.T.A. Carnet referred to in the [Temporary
    Importation (Tariff Item No. 9993.00.00)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-58) that was issued in respect
    of the object on the last occasion the object was imported into
    Canada, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} of the appropriate import declaration made by the
    person who imported the object on the last occasion the object was
    imported into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When an application is
being made for an export permit in respect of an object to which
paragraph 7(a) of the Act applies, documentary evidence or a signed
declaration by the applicant is required to establish that the object in
respect of which the application is made was imported into Canada within
the 35 years immediately preceding the date of the application and was
not exported from Canada under a permit issued under the Act prior to
that importation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 8
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** When a permit officer
refers an application for an export permit to an expert examiner for
consideration under subsection 8(3) of the Act, the expert examiner may,
for the purpose of making a determination under section 9 of the Act,
require the applicant to produce the object for their examination.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 8

## [Export Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557201 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An export permit shall be
issued in the form established by the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 9
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-9-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 9"){.PITLink}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  export permit shall only be valid for a period equal to 90 days
  calculated from the day the permit was issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a person to whom an export permit has been
  issued requires the reinstatement of the permit when the permit has
  lapsed or has been suspended or cancelled, or an amendment to the
  permit, they shall forward the permit to the Minister with a request
  for reinstatement or amendment, as the case may be, and the reasons
  for it.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557210 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where an export permit
has been issued under the Act, the person to whom the permit was issued
shall, at the request of the Minister, provide any information that may
be necessary to verify any statement made by that person in the
application for the export permit and any information to establish

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the consignee referred to in the
  application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the object referred to in the export
  permit was delivered to the consignee; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the object was sold, the selling price of the
  object.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 11(F)

## [Removal of an Object from Canada Under Paragraph 7(c) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557217 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2015-155, s. 12

\]
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  permit officer shall issue an export permit under paragraph  7(c) of
  the Act if the applicant for the permit certifies and establishes to
  that officer's satisfaction that the object is to be removed from
  Canada for any of the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appraisal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authentication;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conservation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} exhibition;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} on loan;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} processing;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} research;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} restoration or repair; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as personal effects.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The length of time for which an object may be removed
  from Canada under an export permit issued under paragraph  7(c) of the
  Act shall be for a period not exceeding five years.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who applies for an export permit to export
  an object under paragraph  7(c) of the Act shall include in their
  application for the permit a written undertaking that they will return
  the object for which the permit was issued to Canada within the time
  prescribed in the permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 13
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-12-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If an object is removed
from Canada under an export permit issued under paragraph 7(c) of the
Act, the person to whom the permit was issued shall, at any time on the
request of the Minister, forward to the Minister the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} details in respect of the place where the object is
  located or has been located during the period it is removed from
  Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the object is damaged, destroyed or lost during
  the period it is removed from Canada, the details in respect of such
  damage, destruction or loss.

- SOR/2015-155, s. 14

## [Notice of Return of an Object to Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557240 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2015-155, s. 15

\]
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  object has been removed from Canada under an export permit issued
  under paragraph 7(c) of the Act, the person to whom the permit was
  issued shall, within 15 days following the day on which the object is
  returned to Canada, forward to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Notice of Return in the form established by the
    Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof satisfactory to the Minister that the object
    described in the Notice of Return is the object for which the export
    permit was issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following information shall be furnished in the
  Notice of Return referred to in subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's name, address and telephone number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reference number of the export permit issued in
    respect of the object; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence indicating the port of entry
    and the day of entry of the object into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 16

## [Objects Requiring Copies To Be Deposited]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557253 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
objects are prescribed for the purposes of section 14 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any original archive of national historic interest if
  it was made in the territory that is now Canada, made out of the
  territory by a person who at any time normally resided in the
  territory or made out of the territory and relates to the history or
  national life of Canada, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a textual record or document in manuscript form,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a map, cartographic record or document in
    manuscript form,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a map, cartographic record or document in printed
    form made prior to the year 1850,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a design, pictorial record or document in
    manuscript form,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a design, pictorial record or document in printed
    form made prior to the year 1880, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a photograph, photographic record or document made
    prior to the year 1900; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any original public record made or received by a
  public authority that is a

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} document in graphic or textual form,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} photograph,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cinematographic film, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} sound recording.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 17

## [Shipping Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557269 .Part}

object, or their authorized agent or mandatary, shall, before exporting
the object described in the export permit issued in respect of that
object, give the export permit to the collector of customs at the port
of export.

## [Application for a General Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557276 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
general permit referred to in subsection  17(1) of the Act shall be made
in the form established by the Minister, signed by the applicant and
forwarded to the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 19

::: PITLink
[Previous

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The following information
shall be furnished and forwarded with an application referred to in
section 18:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the detailed reasons for the application setting out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area of the applicant's business
    specialization, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the inconvenience, if any, to obtain an export
    permit in respect of the individual export of objects;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list describing any object exported under an export
  permit by the applicant during the six-month period preceding the date
  of the application together with the details in respect of each permit
  issued during that period;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the details of any current general permit held by the
  applicant;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the volume of the applicant's commercial activities
  in respect of the purchase and sale of any objects;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees of the applicant, the
  distance between the applicant's place of business and the nearest
  office of a permit officer;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any information requested by the Minister when, in
  the Minister's opinion, the information furnished by the applicant
  requires clarification; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other information that is required.

## [Cultural Property General Permit Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557296 .Part}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** No person shall export
any object under a general permit issued to them under subsection  17(1)
of the Act unless that person or a person authorized to sign a Cultural
Property General Permit Declaration by the general permit under which
the object is to be exported, before exporting the object, completes a
Cultural Property General Permit Declaration in respect of that object.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 22
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-21-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 21"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Cultural Property General Permit Declaration referred to in section 21
  shall be in the form established by the Minister. The form shall be
  signed by one of the persons referred to in section 21 and shall state
  that the object intended for export from Canada is an object
  authorized to be exported from Canada under a general permit and that
  the conditions set out in that general permit have been met.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When an object is to be exported under a general
  permit issued under subsection 17(1) of the Act, the declaration
  referred to in section 21

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall be given to the postmaster, if the object is
    to be sent by means of Canada Post; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be given to the collector of customs at the
    port of export, if the object is to be sent by means other than
    Canada Post.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 22

## [Lost Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-557307 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2015-155, s. 23(F)

\]
:::

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Where a permit issued
under the Act has been lost or destroyed, the person to whom it was
issued may request from the Minister a certified true copy of the permit
and shall, in so requesting,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} submit a statutory declaration stating whether the
  permit has been lost or destroyed and an explanation of the loss or
  destruction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permit that has been lost, submit an
  undertaking to return to the Minister, without delay, the original
  permit, if it is found.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-155, s. 24(F)
