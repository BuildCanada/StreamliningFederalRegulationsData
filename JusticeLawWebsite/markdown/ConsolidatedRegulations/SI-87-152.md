## Low Level Air Defence System Remission Order {#low-level-air-defence-system-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/87-152
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1987-08-19

Order Respecting the Remission of the Customs Duties and the Partial
Remission of the Sales Tax Paid or Payable on Defence Supplies Imported
into Canada as Part of or for Use in the Manufacture of a Low Level Air
Defence System

P.C. 1987-1534 1987-07-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
17[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
Order respecting the remission of the customs duty and the partial
remission of the sales tax paid or payable on defence supplies imported
into Canada as part of or for use in the manufacture of a Low Level Air
Defence System.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 4
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641319 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Low Level Air Defence System Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-87-152).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641321 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[defence supplies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[defence supplies]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    the [Defence Production Act](/eng/acts/D-1); ([approvisionnements de
    défense]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Low Level Air Defence System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Low Level Air Defence System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
    missile system mounted on an armoured personnel carrier, 35 mm twin
    guns, and a skyguard radar and fire control unit. ([système de
    défense aérienne à basse altitude]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641325 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SI/88-165, s. 2(E)

\]
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of defence supplies
that are imported during the period commencing on April 1, 1986 and
terminating on April 1, 1995, by Werkzeugmaschinenfabrik
Oerlikon-Buehrle AG or Oerlikon Aerospace Inc. or by the agents or
subcontractors of those companies and that are part of, or are used in
the manufacture of, a Low Level Air Defence System pursuant to a
contract with the Department of National Defence.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-165, s. 1
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641331 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
partial remission is hereby granted of the sales tax paid or payable
under the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on the defence supplies in
respect of which customs duties are remitted under section 3, in an
amount equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax paid or payable in respect of
  the defence supplies; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of sales tax that would have been paid or
  that would have been payable in respect of the defence supplies if the
  duty paid value used to calculate the sales tax paid or payable in
  respect of the defence supplies had been reduced by the amount of the
  customs duties remitted under section 3.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-165, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-641337 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted under
sections 3 and 4 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an application for remission is made to the Minister
  of National Revenue within two years after the date that the defence
  supplies receive customs clearance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of Supply and Services certifies that
  the supplies are defence supplies; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the application is accompanied by such other evidence
  as is necessary to demonstrate to the Minister of National Revenue
  that the applicant is entitled to the remission.
