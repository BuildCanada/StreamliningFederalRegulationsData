## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1534910 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[aluminum goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aluminum goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item set
      out in Part 2 of the schedule; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
      Chapter 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are otherwise
      classifiable under a tariff item set out in Part 2 of the
      schedule. ([marchandises en aluminium]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[steel goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[steel goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item set
      out in Part 1 of the schedule; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
      Chapter 99 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are otherwise
      classifiable under a tariff item set out in Part 1 of the
      schedule. ([marchandises en acier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Steel melted and poured in China

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of this Order, goods contain steel melted and poured in China
  if the raw steel, or any portion of the raw steel, contained in the
  goods was first produced in a liquid state in a steel-making furnace
  and poured into its first solid state --- which can take the form of a
  semi-finished or finished steel mill product --- in China.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aluminum smelted and cast in China

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Order, goods contain
  aluminum smelted and cast in China if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the largest volume --- or, if any, second largest
    volume --- of primary aluminum contained in the goods was produced
    in China; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aluminum contained in the goods was most
    recently liquified and cast into a solid state --- which can take
    the form of a semi-finished or finished aluminum product --- in
    China.

## [Surtax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1534926 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- steel goods

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Steel
  goods that contain steel melted and poured in China are subject to a
  surtax in the amount of 25% of the value for duty determined in
  accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Steel goods are deemed to contain steel melted and
  poured in China if the importer fails to provide, when requested by an
  officer, a certificate, report or commercial invoice that demonstrates
  that the goods do not contain steel melted and poured in China.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surtax --- aluminum goods

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Aluminum
  goods that contain aluminum smelted and cast in China are subject to a
  surtax in the amount of 25% of the value for duty determined in
  accordance with sections 47 to 55 of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Aluminum goods are deemed to contain aluminum smelted
  and cast in China if the importer fails to provide, when requested by
  an officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate or report that demonstrates that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the largest volume --- and, if any, second
      largest volume --- of primary aluminum contained in the goods was
      produced in a country other than China, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the aluminum contained in the goods was most
      recently liquified and cast into a solid state in a country other
      than China; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a commercial invoice that demonstrates that the
    goods do not contain aluminum smelted and cast in China.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 3 and 4,
the following steel goods and aluminum goods are not subject to the
surtax:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are accounted for under subsection
  32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) in a single
  declaration and that have a cumulative value for duty of \$5000 or
  less;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are subject to a surtax under the [China
  Surtax Order (2024)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-187);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are [[casual
  goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the [Persons
  Authorized to Account for Casual Goods
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-418);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods that are classified under a tariff item of
  Chapter 98 of the List of Tariff Provisions, even if the goods are
  otherwise classifiable under a tariff item set out in the schedule;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} goods that originate in the United States, as
  determined in accordance with the [Determination of Country of Origin
  for the Purpose of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} goods that are in transit to Canada on the day on
  which this Order comes into force.

## AMENDMENTS NOT IN FORCE {#amendments-not-in-force .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2025-154, s. 6

  - [6]{.lawlabel} Subsection 3(2) of this Order is replaced by the
    following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Deeming {#deeming .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Steel goods are deemed to contain steel melted
      and poured in China if the importer fails to provide, when
      requested by an officer, a certificate that demonstrates that the
      goods do not contain steel melted and poured in China.
    :::
    ::::

- --- SOR/2025-154, s. 7

  - [7]{.lawlabel} Subsection 4(2) of this Order is replaced by the
    following:

    :::: section
    ::: AmendedText
    - ###### Deeming {#deeming-1 .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} Aluminum goods are deemed to contain aluminum
      smelted and cast in China if the importer fails to provide, when
      requested by an officer, a certificate that demonstrates that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the largest volume --- and, if any, second
        largest volume --- of primary aluminum contained in the goods
        was produced in a country other than China; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aluminum contained in the goods was most
        recently liquified and cast into a solid state in a country
        other than China.
    :::
