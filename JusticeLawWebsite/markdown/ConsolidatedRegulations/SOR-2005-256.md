## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an advance ruling on
    the tariff classification of goods given under paragraph 43.1(1)(c)
    of the Act. ([décision anticipée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application for an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718816 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Who may apply

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling in respect of goods proposed to be imported may be made
by any member of the following classes of persons:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} importers of goods in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who are authorized to account for goods under
  paragraph 32(6)(a) or subsection 32(7) of the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporters or producers of those goods outside of
  Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling shall be made not less than 120 days before the proposed
date of importation of the goods.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for an
advance ruling shall be made in English or French.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an officer determines
that the information contained in the application is insufficient to
make a ruling, the officer may request supplementary information from
the applicant and shall specify a period of not less than 30 days within
which the information shall be provided.

## [Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718828 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consistency of rulings

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall give
consistent advance rulings with respect to applications for advance
rulings based on facts and circumstances that are identical in all
material respects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other requirements for ruling

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall give the
applicant an advance ruling in writing, in the same language as the
language of the application for the advance ruling, and shall provide
the reasons for the advance ruling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods affected

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling applies
to goods that are the subject of the advance ruling and are imported on
or after the effective date of the advance ruling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling is
effective on the date on which it is issued, or on such later date as
may be specified in the advance ruling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister bound

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An advance ruling shall
remain in effect and shall bind the Minister if:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no change in the material facts, material
  circumstances or laws of Canada on which the ruling is based;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person to whom the advance ruling was given has
  acted in accordance with the ruling; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has not been revoked.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-40, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponing

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may postpone the issuance of an advance ruling in respect of
  goods where goods, other than those for which an application for an
  advance ruling was made, are the subject of one of the following
  processes, the result of which is likely to affect the advance ruling:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification under section 42.01 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a review, re-determination or further
    re-determination of tariff classification under section 59, 60 or 61
    of the Act, as the case may be; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a hearing before the Canadian International Trade
    Tribunal or any court.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declining or postponing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer may decline or postpone the issuance of an
  advance ruling where the applicant fails to provide supplementary
  information requested under section 5 within the period specified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declining

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An officer may decline the issuance of an advance
  ruling where it is impractical or unreasonable to provide a ruling
  prior to the proposed date of importation.

## [Modification or Revocation of an Advance Ruling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-718854 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** An officer may modify or
revoke an advance ruling given in respect of goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the advance ruling is based on an error of fact or
  in the tariff classification of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to conform with a decision of a Canadian court or
  tribunal or a change in the laws of Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there is a change in the material facts or
  material circumstances on which the advance ruling is based;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the Commissioner revises an advance ruling under
  paragraph 60(4)(b) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-40, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-12-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods affected

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  modification or revocation of an advance ruling applies to goods that
  are the subject of the advance ruling and are imported on or after the
  effective date of the modification or revocation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Goods affected

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The modification or revocation also applies to goods
  imported before the effective date where the modification or
  revocation is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the detriment of the person to whom the advance
    ruling was given and that person has not acted in accordance with
    the advance ruling; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the benefit of the person to whom the advance
    ruling was given.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2014-40, s. 3(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-13-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 13"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Subject to a postponement
under subsection 16(1), a modification or revocation of an advance
ruling is effective on the date on which the modification or revocation
is issued or on such later date as may be specified in the notice given
under section 15.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** An officer shall give
notice in writing of any modification or revocation of an advance ruling
and of the effective date of the modification or revocation to the
person to whom the advance ruling was given.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer shall postpone the effective date of a modification or
  revocation of an advance ruling for a period not exceeding 90 days
  where the person to whom the advance ruling was given demonstrates
  that the person has relied in good faith on that advance ruling to the
  person\'s detriment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An officer shall give, in writing, to the person to
  whom the advance ruling was given, notice of any postponement made
  under subsection (1).
