## Minister of Foreign Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-foreign-affairs-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-66
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-05-31

Minister of Foreign Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1995-783 1995-05-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraphs 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089){#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089-ID0EJCAA}
and 19.1(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089){#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20089-ID0EHCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to repeal Order in Council P.C. 1989-1700 of August 24, 1989[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20103){#fn_SI_95-66_EN_hq_20103-ID0EDCAA}
and to authorize the Minister of Foreign Affairs to prescribe, by order,
the fee to be paid by any person to whom a permit or a certificate is
issued pursuant to the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19).
