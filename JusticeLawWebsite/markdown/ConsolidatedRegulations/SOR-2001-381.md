## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673582 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt classes of foreign banks

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The governments of foreign
countries, or of political subdivisions of foreign countries, that do
not in Canada carry on a business that includes any of the activities
referred to in paragraphs (a) to (g) of the definition [[financial
services entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 507(1) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and that would, but for these Regulations, be
foreign banks are exempt from the application of the definition
[[foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act.

section 43.1 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
