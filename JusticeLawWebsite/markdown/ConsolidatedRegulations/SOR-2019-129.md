## Surtax on the Importation of Certain Steel Goods Refund Order {#surtax-on-the-importation-of-certain-steel-goods-refund-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-129
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 2019-05-09

Surtax on the Importation of Certain Steel Goods Refund Order

P.C. 2019-477 2019-05-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 58 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDABA}, makes the annexed [Surtax
on the Importation of Certain Steel Goods Refund
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-129).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EDABA){#fn_a}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A refund
  is granted of a surtax paid under the [Order Imposing a Surtax on the
  Importation of Certain Steel Goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-206), as
  it read on April 28, 2019, in respect of goods of the following
  classes of goods, as set out in column 1 of Schedule 1 to that Order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} classes of goods 2 to 5 and 7 for goods originating
    in all countries; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} classes of goods 1 and 6 for goods originating in
    Colombia, Korea, Panama and Peru.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The refund referred to in subsection (1) is granted
  of the surtax, or portion of the surtax, for which no other claim for
  relief has been granted under the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
