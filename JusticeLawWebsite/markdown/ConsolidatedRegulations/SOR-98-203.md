## Public Agents Firearms Regulations {#public-agents-firearms-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-203
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Public Agents Firearms Regulations

P.C. 1998-478 1998-03-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Public Agents Firearms
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on October 30, 1997, which
date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs 117(l)
and (m) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the
annexed [Public Agents Firearms
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019266 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agency firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agency firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that is in
    the possession of a public service agency for use by its public
    agents. ([arme à feu d'agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[agency identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agency identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2004-265, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[class]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following classes of
    firearms, namely, prohibited firearms, restricted firearms and
    non-restricted firearms. ([classe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[controlled item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited device, a
    prohibited weapon, a restricted weapon or prohibited ammunition.
    ([article contrôlé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[firearm identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firearm identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a number
    assigned to a firearm under paragraph 7(1)(b). ([numéro
    d'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that
    is neither a prohibited firearm nor a restricted firearm. ([arme à
    feu sans restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protected firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protected firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm that is in
    the possession of a public service agency and that is not an agency
    firearm. ([arme à feu protégée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of the following persons in the course of
      their duties or for the purposes of their employment:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} peace officers,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} persons training to become police officers or
        peace officers under the control and supervision of a police
        force or a police academy or similar institution designated by
        the federal Minister or the lieutenant governor in council of a
        province,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} persons or members of a class of persons
        employed in the public service of Canada or by the government of
        a province or municipality who are prescribed to be public
        officers by the [Regulations Prescribing Public
        Officers](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-466), and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} chief firearms officers and firearms officers,
        and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an individual acting on behalf of, and under the
      authority of, a police force or a department of the Government of
      Canada or of a province. ([agent public]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[public agency identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public agency identification number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the number assigned to a public service agency or unit under
    paragraph 7(1)(a). ([numéro d'identification]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police force,
    a department or agency of the public service of Canada or of a
    province or municipality, a police academy or other public agency
    that employs or otherwise has under its authority public agents.
    ([agence de services publics]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sticker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sticker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a self-adhesive label issued
    by the Registrar under subsection 7(2).
    ([étiquette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of a public service
    agency. ([subdivision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019289 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} public service agencies; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} public agents.

## [Storage of Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019293 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A public
  agent who is assigned an agency firearm or a controlled item shall,
  when the firearm or item is not in use,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that it is stored in a container,
    receptacle, vault, safe or room, controlled by a public service
    agency, that is kept securely locked and is constructed so that it
    cannot readily be broken open or into; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} store it in a dwelling house if authorized by the
    public service agency under whose authority the agent acts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A public agent who stores an agency firearm in a
  dwelling house shall store the firearm

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the [Storage, Display,
    Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with instructions given to the agent
    under subsection (4).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A public agent who stores a controlled item in a
  dwelling house shall store the item

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a container or receptacle that is kept securely
    locked and that is constructed so that it cannot readily be broken
    open or into; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with instructions given to the agent
    under subsection (4).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A public service agency may give written instructions
  respecting the safe storage of its agency firearms and controlled
  items in a dwelling house.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public service agency shall ensure that its agency firearms and
  controlled items that have been assigned to a public agent are stored
  in accordance with subsections 3(1) to (3) when the firearms or items
  are not in use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every public service agency shall ensure that its
  agency firearms and controlled items that have not been assigned to a
  public agent and its protected firearms are stored in a container,
  receptacle, vault, safe or room, controlled by the agency, that is
  kept securely locked and is constructed so that it cannot readily be
  broken open or into.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 2
:::

## [Training]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019312 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every public service agency
shall ensure that each public agent whom it employs, or otherwise has
under its authority, and who stores, handles, transports, possesses or
uses firearms in the course of his or her duties receives training
appropriate to his or her particular duties before storing, handling,
transporting, possessing or using them.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 2
:::

## [Public Agency Identification Number]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019316 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public service agency shall ensure that one or more applications for
  public agency identification numbers have been made to the Registrar
  so that the agency, including all of its units, will be covered by one
  or more of those numbers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application shall include the name, address,
  telephone number and, if applicable, facsimile number and electronic
  mail address of the public service agency or unit making the
  application.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 2
:::

## [Duties of the Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019322 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall assign:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a public agency identification number to any public
    service agency or unit that applies for one under section 6 or
    reports that it has possession of a firearm; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a firearm identification number to an agency
    firearm or a protected firearm reported under subsection 8(1),
    8.1(1), 9(1) or 10(1) or (1.1) in respect of which a firearm
    identification number has not already been assigned.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a protected firearm that does not bear
  either a serial number sufficient to distinguish it from other
  firearms or a firearm identification number, the Registrar shall issue
  a sticker that bears the firearm identification number assigned to the
  firearm.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 3
:::

## [Change of Name or Address]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019330 .Part}

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** Every public service
agency and unit that has been assigned a public agency identification
number shall advise the Registrar of a change of its name or address
within 30 days after the change.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 4
:::

## [Initial Firearm Inventory]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019334 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public service agency shall ensure that on or before October 31, 2009
  the Registrar is provided with one or more reports that set out the
  inventory of all agency firearms and protected firearms in the
  agency's possession on October 31, 2008.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), inventory does
  not include protected firearms in the public service agency's
  possession on October 31, 2008 that the agency intends to dispose of
  on or before October 31, 2009.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} A report shall include the name and the public
  agency identification number of the public service agency or unit
  making the report.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each agency firearm set out in a report referred
  to in subsection (1), the public service agency or unit making the
  report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serial number, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the manufacturer if different from the
    make;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} model, if known;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} type;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} action;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} calibre or gauge;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the barrel length if it is shorter than 470 mm; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of ammunition that the cartridge
    magazine can contain.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For each protected firearm set out in a report
  referred to in subsection (1), the public service agency or unit
  making the report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the serial number, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the barrel length is shorter than 470 mm;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the case reference or file reference respecting the
    firearm, if applicable.

## [Coming into Possession --- Agency Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019362 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public service agency shall ensure that a report of each firearm that
  comes into its possession for use as an agency firearm after October
  31, 2008 is provided to the Registrar without delay.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and public agency identification number of
    the public service agency or unit making the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the firearm, its firearm
    identification number or, if there is none, the information referred
    to in paragraphs 8(2)(a) to (i); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a statement that the firearm was
    imported by the public service agency or unit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply in respect of a
  transaction that is required to be reported to the Registrar under
  subsection 26(1) of the Act or section 13 of these Regulations.

## [Marking or Attaching Firearm Identification Number]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019389 .Part}

- **[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an agency firearm of a public service agency does not bear a serial
  number sufficient to distinguish it from other firearms, the public
  service agency shall, after a firearm identification number is
  assigned, ensure that the number is permanently and legibly stamped or
  engraved on a visible place on the firearm's frame or receiver.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an agency
  firearm kept for the exclusive use of peace officers involved in
  covert operations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 10
:::

**[[10.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.2 .sectionLabel}** If a protected
firearm in the possession of a public service agency has been issued a
sticker under subsection 7(2), the public service agency shall ensure
that the sticker is attached to a visible place on the firearm's frame
or receiver.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 10
:::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the firearm, its firearm

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every public service
agency shall ensure that the Registrar is advised of any modification to
one of its agency firearms that changes the class of the firearm within
30 days after the modification is made.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Transactions Involving Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019410 .Part}

- **[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  a public service agency may only sell, barter, give, lend or rent a
  firearm to another public service agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A public service agency may give or lend a firearm to
  a department, an agency or a police force of a foreign government,
  national or otherwise, for purposes of evidence in judicial
  proceedings.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A public service agency may return an agency firearm
  to the business that supplied it if the firearm is defective.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, nothing in this section
  prevents a public service agency from returning a protected firearm to
  a person who is entitled to it.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 11
:::

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  public service agency that sells, barters, gives, lends or rents a
  firearm or returns a firearm under subsection 12.1(3) shall ensure
  that a report on the transaction is provided to the Registrar

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm that is sold, bartered,
    given or returned, without delay; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a firearm that is loaned or rented,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the firearm is intended to be lent or rented
      for more than 180 days, without delay, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the firearm is not intended to be lent or
      rented for more than 180 days but the period that the firearm is
      lent or rented lasts longer than 180 days, without delay after the
      180 days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A public service agency that lends or rents a firearm
  that is required to be reported under paragraph (1)(b) shall ensure
  that a report of the return of the firearm is provided to the
  Registrar without delay.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A report under subsection (1) or (2) shall include
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties to the transaction and
    their public agency identification numbers, if any; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the firearm, its firearm
    identification number or, if there is none, the information referred
    to in paragraphs 8(2)(a) to (i).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 11
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  public service agency that returns a protected firearm to the person
  entitled to it shall ensure that a report on the transaction is
  provided to the Registrar without delay.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and public agency identification number of
    the public service agency or unit making the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the firearm, its firearm
    identification number or, if there is none, the information referred
    to in paragraphs 8(2)(a) to (i);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of the registration certificate for the
    firearm, if any; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licence number of the person entitled to the
    firearm or, if that person is a non-resident who does not hold a
    licence, the name of the person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 11
:::

## [Disposal of Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019439 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  a public service agency disposes of a firearm, the agency shall offer
  the firearm to the chief firearms officer of the province in which the
  firearm is stored or to the Commissioner of the Royal Canadian Mounted
  Police, for destruction or for any scientific, research or educational
  purpose, or for preservation as a historical firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the offer referred to in subsection (1) is
  refused, the public service agency may only dispose of the firearm by
  having it destroyed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-265, s. 11
:::

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  public service agency shall ensure that a report of each firearm that
  it disposes of is provided to the Registrar within 30 days after the
  disposal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and public agency identification number of
    the public service agency or unit making the report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the firearm, its firearm
    identification number or, if there is none, the information referred
    to in paragraphs 8(2)(a) to (i); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date, place and method of destruction.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  protected firearm that is in the possession of a public service agency
  on October 31, 2008 and that the agency disposes of on or before
  October 31, 2009.
