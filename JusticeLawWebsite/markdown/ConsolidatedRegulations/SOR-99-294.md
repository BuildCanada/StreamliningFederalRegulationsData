Canadian Energy Regulator Onshore Pipeline Regulations

P.C. 1999-1184  1999-06-23

The National Energy Board, pursuant to subsection 48(2) of the [National
Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), hereby makes the annexed Onshore
Pipeline Regulations, 1999.

Calgary, Alberta, May 26, 1999

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to subsection 48(2) of the
[National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), hereby approves the annexed
Onshore Pipeline Regulations, 1999, made by the National Energy Board.

[[abandon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030761 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 2.1 and
3, these Regulations apply in respect of onshore pipelines designed,
constructed, operated or abandoned after the coming into force of these
Regulations.

- SOR/2003-39, s. 56

not apply in respect of a hydrocarbon processing plant that is subject
to the [Canadian Energy Regulator Processing Plant

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030775 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  company designs, constructs, operates or abandons a pipeline, or
  contracts for the provision of those services, the company shall
  ensure that the pipeline is designed, constructed, operated or
  abandoned in accordance with the applicable provisions of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} CSA Z276, if the pipeline transports liquefied
    natural gas;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} CSA Z341 for underground storage of hydrocarbons;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} CSA Z662, if the pipeline transports liquid or
    gaseous hydrocarbons; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} CSA Z246.1 for all pipelines.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without limiting the generality of subsection (1),
  the company shall ensure that the pipeline is designed, constructed,
  operated or abandoned in accordance with the design, specifications,
  programs, manuals, procedures, measures and plans developed and
  implemented by the company in accordance with these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there is an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and a standard referred to in paragraph (1)(b), (c), (d)
  or (e), these Regulations prevail to the extent of the inconsistency.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to require and enable a company to design, construct,
operate or abandon a pipeline in a manner that ensures

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety and security of persons;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety and security of pipelines and abandoned
  pipelines; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the protection of property and the environment.

## [Management System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030801 .Part}

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall establish, implement and maintain a management system
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is explicit, comprehensive and proactive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} integrates the company's operational activities and
    technical systems with its management of human and financial
    resources;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} applies to all the company's activities involving
    the design, construction, operation or abandonment of a pipeline and
    to the programs referred to in section 55;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensures coordination between the programs referred
    to in section 55; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} corresponds to the size of the company, to the
    scope, nature and complexity of its activities and to the hazards
    and risks associated with those activities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall establish its management system
  within 90 days after the day on which the certificate or order
  authorizing it to construct or operate a pipeline is issued under the
  Act.

### [Management System Processes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1030832 .Subheading}

- **[[6.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall, as part of its management system and the programs
  referred to in section 55,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for setting the
    objectives and specific targets that are required to achieve the
    goals established under subsection 6.3(1) and for ensuring their
    annual review;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} develop performance measures for evaluating the
    company's success in achieving its goals, objectives and targets;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for identifying
    and analyzing all hazards and potential hazards;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain an inventory of the
    identified hazards and potential hazards;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for evaluating
    the risks associated with the identified hazards and potential
    hazards, including the risks related to normal and abnormal
    operating conditions;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for developing
    and implementing controls to prevent, manage and mitigate the
    identified hazards, potential hazards and risks and for
    communicating those controls to anyone who is exposed to the risks;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for identifying,
    and monitoring compliance with, all legal requirements that are
    applicable to the company in matters of safety, security and
    protection of the environment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} establish and maintain a list of those legal
    requirements;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for identifying
    and managing any change that could affect safety, security or the
    protection of the environment, including any new hazard or risk, any
    change in a design, specification, standard or procedure and any
    change in the company's organizational structure or the legal
    requirements applicable to the company;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for developing
    competency requirements and training programs that provide employees
    and other persons working with or on behalf of the company with the
    training that will enable them to perform their duties in a manner
    that is safe, ensures the safety and security of the pipeline and
    protects the environment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for verifying
    that employees and other persons working with or on behalf of the
    company are trained and competent and for supervising them to ensure
    that they perform their duties in a manner that is safe, ensures the
    safety and security of the pipeline and protects the environment;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for making
    employees and other persons working with or on behalf of the company
    aware of their responsibilities in relation to the processes and
    procedures required by this section;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for the internal
    and external communication of information relating to safety,
    security and protection of the environment;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for identifying
    the documents required for the company to meet its obligations under
    these Regulations;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for preparing,
    reviewing, revising and controlling those documents, including a
    process for obtaining approval of the documents by the appropriate
    authority;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for generating,
    retaining and maintaining records that document the implementation
    of the management system and the programs referred to in section 55
    and for providing access to those who require them in the course of
    their duties;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} establish and implement a process for coordinating
    and controlling the operational activities of employees and other
    people working with or on behalf of the company so that each person

### [Training Program]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1031063 .Subheading}

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall develop and implement a training program for any
  employee of the company who is directly involved in the operation of
  the pipeline.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The training program shall instruct the employee on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety regulations and procedures applicable to
    the day-to-day operation of the pipeline;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} the security processes, procedures and measures
    applicable to the day-to-day operation of the pipeline;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} responsible environmental practices and procedures
    in the day-to-day operations of the pipeline;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for the proper operation of the
    equipment that the employee could reasonably be expected to use; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the emergency procedures set out in the manual
    developed under section 32 and the procedures for the operation of
    all emergency equipment that the employee could reasonably be
    expected to use.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company shall use reasonable efforts to ensure
  that any employee who attends a training program has a working
  knowledge of the subject-matter of the program at the end of the
  program.

### [Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1031151 .Subheading}

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** A company shall, in
addition to complying with the record retention requirements set out in
the CSA standards referred to in section 4, retain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} until at least one month after the date on which they
  were recorded, the records made under paragraphs 36(c) and 37(b)
  except for leak detection data, which shall be retained for six
  months;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an annual report on the training program developed
  under section 46 that compares the actual training received by
  employees to the planned training;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for at least one year after a pipeline or part of one
  is placed into service, any information with respect to the quality
  assurance program developed under section 15;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the most recent five years of operation or for
  the period covered by the two most recent complete audits, whichever
  period is longer, the records for the audits and inspections required
  by sections 53 to 55;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for as long as the installations referred to in
  section 38 remain on the pipeline, detailed records of those
  installations, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location of the installation,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the type of installation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of installation,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the welding procedure used,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the carbon equivalent of the pipeline,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the results of the non-destructive testing
    performed on the installation, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the planned date of removal of the installation;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} accurate records of the location of all buried
  facilities, until they are removed; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} for at least two years after the operation of a
  pipeline or part of one has been duly abandoned in accordance with all
  applicable requirements

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all records available to the company in respect of
    the procedures used in each stage of the construction of the
    pipeline or part,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the production reports and mill certificates,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the specifications and name-plate data, if any,
    of the pumps, compressors, drivers, storage tanks and other major
    equipment of the pipeline,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the performance curves of all main line pumps and
    compressors of the pipeline,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the reports of all surveillance and monitoring
    programs developed under section 39,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the documentation referred to in section 41 in
    respect of pipeline defects, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the documentation on all incidents reported under
    section 52.
