## Indian Income Tax Remission Order {#indian-income-tax-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-166
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-08-25

Indian Income Tax Remission Order

P.C. 1993-1649  1993-08-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-166_e_hq_6518){#fn_SI-93-166_e_hq_6518-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to remit amounts that would be remitted if the reference to
subsection 87(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5) in the [Indian
Income Tax Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-93-44) included a
reference to a provision similar to that subsection in an Act cited in
that Order.
