## Government Airport Concession Operations Regulations {#government-airport-concession-operations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-373
:::

[DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORT ACT](/eng/acts/T-18)

Registration 1979-04-27

Regulations Respecting the Control of Commercial and Other Operations at
Government Airports

P.C. 1979-1332 1979-04-25
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transports, pursuant to sections 25 and 26 of the
[Department of Transport Act](/eng/acts/T-18), is pleased hereby to
revoke the [Government Airport Concession Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-373) made by Order in Council P.C.
1976-2076 of 19th August, 1976[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR-79-373_e_hq_7904){#fn_SOR-79-373_e_hq_7904-ID0EDBAA}
and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the control of
commercial and other operations at government
airports](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-373), in substitution therefore.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865048 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Government Airport Concession Operations
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-373).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865050 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aerodrome for which an
      airport licence has been issued by the Minister under Part III of
      the Air Regulations; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of
      Transport official in charge of an airport or his duly authorized
      representative; ([directeur d'un aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any group of persons that has
      agreed with the Minister to provide advice in respect of the
      issuance and cancellation of permits;
      ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial passenger vehicle
      operated on a per passenger fare basis and serving a specific
      route or destination; ([autobus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[commercial passenger vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commercial passenger vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      rental motor vehicle or a motor vehicle that is used in the
      transportation of persons for compensation; ([véhicule commercial
      de passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[courtesy vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[courtesy vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor vehicle
      operated by a commercial enterprise for the purpose of
      transporting customers of that enterprise between an airport and
      the place of business of that enterprise; ([véhicule de
      courtoisie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an airport
      designated by the Minister pursuant to subsection 4(1); ([aéroport
      désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a motor vehicle
      of a class specified by the Minister pursuant to subsection 4(2);
      ([véhicule désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dispatch service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dispatch service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a service provided
      to an airport that directs taxicabs or limousines to customers who
      request such vehicles; ([service de réparation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[driver]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a motor vehicle,
      means the person who is driving or has the care or control of that
      motor vehicle; ([conducteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[general vehicle station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[general vehicle station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area at
      an airport described or delineated pursuant to section 6 for the
      parking or standing of a courtesy vehicle or a commercial
      passenger vehicle, other than a taxicab or limousine, used to
      provide transportation for passengers or goods; ([station générale
      de véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ground transportation inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ground transportation inspector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person appointed pursuant to section 5; ([inspecteur du transport
      au sol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[limousine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[limousine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial passenger
      vehicle, other than a bus, that has a seating capacity of not more
      than 10 persons including the driver and that is used for the
      transportation of passengers on a zone fare basis;
      ([limousine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport
      or a person authorized by him to act on his behalf;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[open limousine station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[open limousine station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area at an
      airport described or delineated pursuant to section 6 for the
      parking or standing of any limousine; ([station publique de
      limousines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[open taxicab station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[open taxicab station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area at an
      airport described or delineated pursuant to section 6 for the
      parking or standing of any taxicab; ([station publique de
      taxis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a commercial
      passenger vehicle or courtesy vehicle, means the person who is in
      charge of the vehicle, whether or not he is actually the driver;
      ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit issued pursuant to
      subsection 11(1); ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permit holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permit holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the operator specified
      in the permit; ([détenteur de permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plate, card, sticker or
      other device provided pursuant to subsection 11(2);
      ([plaque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[taxicab]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[taxicab]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial passenger
      vehicle, other than a bus or a limousine, fitted with a meter that
      is used to determine the fee to be paid pursuant to paragraph
      10(c). ([taxi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a commercial passenger vehicle
  does not include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vehicle that is primarily used in the
    transportation of persons to, from or between medical facilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bus that provides a regularly scheduled urban
    transit service primarily to and from destinations other than an
    airport; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a bus that provides an urban transit service for
    persons having disabilities.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/85-255, s. 1
  - SOR/95-228, s. 1
  - SOR/96-88, s. 1
  - SOR/98-123, s. 14(F)
  :::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865080 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every airport that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is under the management and control of the Minister;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} belongs to or is occupied by Her Majesty in right of
  Canada.

## [Permit Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865084 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may designate any airport at which a permit is required to
  operate a commercial passenger vehicle or a courtesy vehicle.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister designates an airport pursuant to
  subsection (1), he may specify the class or classes of commercial
  passenger vehicles or courtesy vehicles that require the operator of
  those vehicles to be issued a permit before operating those vehicles
  at that airport.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A designation referred to in subsection (1) or a
  specification referred to in subsection (2) may be revoked by the
  Minister.

## [Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865089 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport manager may designate any person at the airport as a ground
  transportation inspector to monitor and enforce the provisions of
  these Regulations relating to ground transportation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall obstruct a ground transportation
  inspector engaged in the performance of his duties.

## [Motor Vehicle Stations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865093 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport manager may, by means of a sign or surface marking, describe
  or delineate any area at the airport as an open taxicab station, open
  limousine station or general vehicle station.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an area has been described or delineated
  pursuant to subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as a general vehicle station, an operator of a
    courtesy vehicle or an operator of a commercial passenger vehicle,
    other than a taxicab or limousine, shall not pick up or unload a
    passenger or goods at that airport except at that general vehicle
    station; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as an open taxicab station or open limousine
    station, an operator of a taxicab or limousine shall not pick up or
    unload a passenger or goods at that airport except at that open
    taxicab station or open limousine station, as the case may be.

## [Conditions Respecting Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865099 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 8(1), a person who operates any business or commercial
  undertaking at an airport must have a permit to do so or have entered
  into a lease, licence agreement or other contract with the Minister in
  respect of the operation of that business or undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who fixes, installs or places anything at an
  airport for the purpose of a business or commercial undertaking at the
  airport must have a permit to operate the business or undertaking or
  have entered into a lease, licence agreement or other contract with
  the Minister in respect of the operation of that business or
  undertaking.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-228, s. 2
- SOR/2010-59, s. 1
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 4 and subject to section 10, any person may transport a
  passenger in a commercial passenger vehicle or a courtesy vehicle from
  any place outside an airport to a place at the airport where the
  transportation does not contravene any provincial law relating to
  trespassing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who uses a commercial passenger vehicle or a
  courtesy vehicle to provide transportation to persons from any place
  at an airport that has been designated pursuant to subsection 4(1) to
  any place outside the airport must:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have entered into a licence agreement with the
    Minister that provides for such transportation of persons; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a permit in respect of the vehicle and operate
    the vehicle in accordance with the terms and conditions of the
    permit.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 6
or 7 or subsection 8(2), the airport manager may authorize the operator
of any commercial passenger vehicle to pick up, unload or transport
passengers at an airport

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an emergency, where the requirements of public
  safety, convenience or necessity cannot be met by the ground
  transportation services authorized by these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} during irregular flight operations as a result of
  weather or technical reasons, where the ground transportation services
  authorized by these Regulations are disrupted or inadequate; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at any location that is remote from any place at
  which ground transportation services authorized by these Regulations
  is provided.

## [General Conditions of Operation of Ground Transportation Services at an Airport]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865116 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The operation of any
commercial passenger vehicle at an airport shall be subject to the
following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every person who has transported a passenger in a
  commercial passenger vehicle from a place outside an airport to a
  place at the airport shall leave the airport by the most direct route;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when a commercial passenger vehicle, other than a
  bus, has advanced to the front of a commercial passenger vehicle
  waiting line at any airport, the driver of the vehicle shall not
  refuse to transport any person, who has requested to be transported,
  to a destination within or outside the airport unless that person is,
  in the opinion of a peace officer, as that term is defined in the
  [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), or a ground transportation inspector,
  intoxicated or conducting himself in a disorderly manner;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the only fee that may be charged to a passenger by a
  driver of a commercial passenger vehicle who picks up and transports
  that passenger from a place at an airport to a destination outside the
  airport is the fee authorized to be charged by the applicable
  provincial or municipal tariff or by a tariff approved by the
  Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the driver of every commercial passenger vehicle at
  an airport shall, at the request of a ground transportation inspector
  at that airport,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} stop the vehicle,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} permit the ground transportation inspector to
    inspect the vehicle, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a permit has been issued to the operator of
    the vehicle, produce the permit for examination;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where a permit has been issued pursuant to subsection
  11(1), the operator and driver of the vehicle for which the permit was
  issued shall comply with any terms and conditions under which the
  permit was issued; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the commercial passenger vehicle is a taxicab,
  the operator of that taxicab holds a provincial or municipal licence
  to operate a taxi service.

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** The airport manager
may establish a dispatch service at a designated airport.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-255, s. 2
- SOR/98-123, s. 16
:::

## [Issue of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865130 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 13 and 14, where
  an operator of a commercial passenger vehicle or courtesy vehicle
  requires a permit to operate that vehicle at an airport pursuant to
  subsection 4(1) or (2), the airport manager at the airport may, on
  written application for it, issue a permit to the operator permitting
  the operation of that vehicle at that airport subject to such terms
  and conditions as the airport manager deems necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit has been issued, pursuant to
  subsection (1), the airport manager shall provide a plate to the
  permit holder.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 15 and 17, a permit issued
  pursuant to subsection (1) shall be valid for the period set out in
  the permit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-123, s. 17
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** When a permit expires,
the permit shall be returned by the permit holder to the airport
manager.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The airport manager
shall, on notice to the applicant, refer any application for a permit
received by him to a Board for its advice as to whether the permit, in
the interests of public necessity and convenience, should be issued and,
if issued, as to the terms and conditions under which it should be
issued.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where the Board has
advised the airport manager, pursuant to a referral under section 13,
the airport manager shall consider such advice before issuing or
refusing to issue the permit if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advice is given, pursuant to a hearing by the
  Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant was a party to the hearing; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the airport manager or his representative was given
  an opportunity to be heard at the hearing.

## [Suspension of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865143 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  airport manager may suspend a permit for a period not exceeding 30
  days where he has reason to believe that the permit holder or driver
  of the motor vehicle for which the permit was issued has violated any
  provision of these Regulations or any of the terms and conditions
  under which the permit was issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No permit shall be suspended pursuant to subsection
  (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless written notice of the proposed suspension
    has been delivered by hand or sent by registered mail to the permit
    holder, at the address set out in the permit, stating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the proposed suspension,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period of the proposed suspension, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the permit holder may, within 10 days of
      the date on which the notice was delivered by hand or sent by
      registered mail

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} submit a request to appear before the airport
        manager for the purpose of making representations in respect of
        the proposed suspension, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} submit the reasons, in writing, to the airport
        manager showing cause why the permit should not be suspended;
        and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} until the expiration of the 10-day period specified
    in subparagraph (a)(iii).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where an airport manager has received the
  representations referred to in clause (2)(a)(iii)(A) or the reasons
  referred to in clause (2)(a)(iii)(B), he shall consider the
  representations or reasons, as the case may be, and shall decide
  whether the permit is to be suspended and, if the permit is to be
  suspended, whether it is to be suspended for the proposed period
  referred to in subparagraph 2(a)(ii) or for a lesser period.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the airport manager has made a decision in
  accordance with subsection (3), he shall, without delay, inform the
  permit holder of the decision by written notice delivered by hand or
  sent by registered mail to the permit holder at the address set out in
  the permit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The airport manager shall, within seven days of the
  suspension of any permit, send a written report of the suspension to
  the Minister accompanied by a recommendation as to whether or not the
  permit should be cancelled.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Where a permit is
suspended, the permit holder shall forthwith return the permit to the
airport manager.

## [Cancellation of Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865158 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to this section, where a
  permit holder or driver has violated any provision of these
  Regulations or any of the terms and conditions under which the permit
  was issued, the Minister may, on the recommendation of an airport
  manager, cancel the permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No permit shall be cancelled pursuant to subsection
  (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} unless written notice of the proposed cancellation
    has been delivered by hand or sent by registered mail to the permit
    holder, at the address set out in the permit, stating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the proposed cancellation, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the permit holder may, within 10 days of
      the date on which the notice was delivered by hand or sent by
      registered mail, submit reasons, in writing, to the Minister
      showing cause why the permit should not be cancelled; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} until the expiration of the 10-day period specified
    in subparagraph (a)(ii).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister has received the reasons referred
  to in subparagraph (2)(a)(ii), he shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} consider the reasons and decide whether or not to
    cancel the permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refer the matter with all relevant information to a
    Board for its advice as to whether or not the permit should be
    cancelled.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister has referred the matter to a Board
  pursuant to paragraph (3)(b) and the Board has advised the Minister,
  the Minister shall consider the advice of the Board if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advice is given, pursuant to a hearing by the
    Board,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder was a party to the hearing, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister or his representative was given an
    opportunity to be heard at the hearing

  and shall decide whether or not to cancel the permit.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} When the Minister has made a decision in accordance
  with paragraph (3)(a) or subsection (4), he shall, without delay,
  inform the permit holder of the decision by written notice delivered
  by hand or sent by registered mail to the permit holder at the address
  set out in the permit.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit is cancelled, the permit holder shall
  forthwith return the permit to the airport manager.

## [Plates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865176 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a plate has been provided pursuant to subsection 11(2), the plate
  shall be securely affixed at all times to the exterior of the vehicle
  for which it was provided to identify that vehicle as a vehicle for
  which a permit is in force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every plate shall remain the property of the Minister
  and shall be returned, without delay, by the operator to whom it was
  provided to the airport manager on the expiration, suspension or
  cancellation of a permit issued to that operator.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a plate has been received from an operator,
  pursuant to subsection (2), as a result of the suspension of a permit,
  the plate shall be returned to that operator on the termination of the
  suspension.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No operator to whom a
permit has been issued in respect of a commercial passenger vehicle or a
courtesy vehicle shall operate that vehicle at an airport unless the
vehicle has the plate provided for the vehicle affixed to the vehicle in
accordance with subsection 18(1).

## [Punishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-865182 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes these Regulations is liable on summary conviction to a fine
not exceeding four hundred dollars.
