## [Use of Public Lands or other Public Property Within a Park]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861614 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person may use or occupy
public lands or other public property within a Park if that person does
so in accordance with the Act, the regulations made thereunder and any
agreement made between the Government of Canada and the government of
the province within which the Park is situated.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, from time
to time, arrange to have public lands in a Park surveyed or resurveyed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} into lots in townsites or other subdivisions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for any right-of-way of the type referred to in
  subsection 6(2) of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of schools, hospitals, churches and
  the entertainment of persons visiting the Park; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of a cemetery.

## [Hazardous Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861631 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may require any person to register at the office of the
  superintendent or at such other place as may be specified by the
  superintendent prior to and on completing, in a Park, any activity
  that, in the opinion of the superintendent, may present a hazard to
  the person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall designate any activity in
  respect of which registration is required pursuant to subsection (1)
  by posting notices at the office of the superintendent, at all
  information offices or centres located in a Park and at any place
  specified by the superintendent pursuant to subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, in a Park, undertake any activity
  designated by the superintendent pursuant to subsection (2) without
  registering as required by the superintendent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prior to undertaking any such activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately on completing or returning from such
    activity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an activity has not been designated by the
  superintendent pursuant to subsection (2), a person who wishes to do
  so may, prior to undertaking that activity, register at the office of
  the superintendent or at such other place as may be specified by the
  superintendent, on condition that the person register again
  immediately on completing or returning from that activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)

## [Restricted and Prohibited Activities, Uses and Travel]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861641 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent may, where it is necessary for the proper management of
  the Park to do so, designate certain activities, uses or entry and
  travel in areas in a Park as restricted or prohibited.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notice of a restriction or prohibition referred to in
  subsection (1) shall be posted by the superintendent at park warden
  offices and information bureaus in the Park or at entrances to the
  Park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice posted in accordance with subsection (2)
  shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the activity or use to which the
    restriction or prohibition applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the extent of restriction, where an activity or use
    is being restricted;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the area to which the restriction
    or prohibition of entry or travel in that area applies; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a map of the area in which the restriction or
    prohibition, applies, where that area is not the total area of the
    Park.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall engage in an activity or use or enter
  and travel in an area that has been designated as restricted or
  prohibited pursuant to subsection (1) otherwise than in accordance
  with the terms and conditions prescribed in a permit issued under
  subsection (5).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on application to him by any
  person, in respect of any activity or use restricted or prohibited
  pursuant to subsection (1) or any entry and travel in an area that has
  been restricted or prohibited, pursuant to that subsection, issue to
  that person a permit to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} engage in that activity or use, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter and travel in that area

  on such terms and conditions as the superintendent may prescribe in
  the permit.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, if it is necessary for the
  preservation, control and management of the Park, suspend or revoke a
  permit issued under subsection (5).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, ss. 1(F), 2
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
- SOR/2017-21, s. 10

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861658 .Part}

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section,

  [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pass, licence,
      ticket or other form of permission authorizing the holder to carry
      on any designated activity; ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[designated activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activity in
      a park, including travel, entry in an area of a park and the use
      of any facility in a park, that is designated in accordance with
      subsection (2). ([activité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, where it is necessary for the
  proper management of the park, designate the activities other than
  those referred to in section 7 that may be carried on only by the
  holder of an authorization for that purpose.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall post a list of designated
  activities and their description in the park warden offices and
  information bureaus in the park or at the entrances to the park.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person other than the holder of an authorization
  issued for that purpose in accordance with this section shall engage
  in a designated activity.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on request, issue an
  authorization and may prescribe terms and conditions in any such
  authorization, taking into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the natural and cultural resources of the park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety, health and enjoyment of persons
    visiting or residing in the park; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the preservation, control and management of the
    park.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The holder of an authorization shall, at the request
  of the superintendent or a park warden or other park officer, produce
  the authorization for inspection.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend an authorization, where the holder of it
    fails to comply with any prescribed term or condition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reinstate the authorization, when the failure
    referred to in paragraph (a) has been remedied; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} revoke an authorization, where the holder of it is
    convicted of a contravention of these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-151, s. 1
:::

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  superintendent is authorized, on application, to issue a permit
  authorizing the permit holder to take flora or natural objects for
  scientific purposes from a park, or for the removal and use of natural
  objects for construction purposes within a park, if the applicant
  demonstrates in writing that the performance of those activities will
  not

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a significant adverse environmental impact on
    the park and its natural resources;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} jeopardize any cultural, historical and
    archaeological resources; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pose a danger to public health or public safety.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit shall specify the kind and amount of and
  the location from which flora or natural objects may be removed and
  set out the terms and conditions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where natural objects are removed for the purpose of
  constructing other than a public work within a Park, every person on
  removal of such natural objects shall pay to the superintendent the
  sum of 25 cents for each cubic yard of such natural objects or
  fraction thereof.

## [Use of Water Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861707 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No person shall pollute
any watercourse.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No person shall obstruct
or divert, by means of a pipe or otherwise, any watercourse, except as
authorized by a permit issued by a superintendent.

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  superintendent may issue a permit to any person for a period not
  exceeding 10 years authorizing the person to take water for domestic,
  business or railway water supply purposes within a Park from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any watercourse;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any Park well; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any Park water supply system other than a
    waterworks system for a townsite or subdivision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), a superintendent
  shall not issue a permit to any person to take water unless the person
  submits to the superintendent

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement specifying the purpose for which the
    water is required;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the method to be used to take the
    water;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement specifying the location of any
    equipment to be installed in taking the water;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a document setting out the results of a water
    quality test that show that the quality of the water to be taken is
    suitable for the purpose for which it is required; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of any impairment to the resources of
    the Park that may result from the installation of the equipment
    referred to in paragraph (c).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall revoke a permit issued under
  subsection (1) if its holder is convicted of a contravention of these
  Regulations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On the expiry or revocation of a permit issued under
  subsection (1), the person who was issued that permit shall
  immediately remove any equipment installed in taking the water and
  restore the site of the equipment to the extent possible to its
  original state.

## [Prevention of Nuisances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861759 .Part}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the opinion of the superintendent or of a Park Medical Officer or a
  medical or sanitary inspector, a nuisance exists on any premises in a
  Park, the superintendent may order the owner, lessee, licencee or any
  other occupier of the premises to abate the nuisance and cleanse the
  premises.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the owner, lessee, licencee or any other
  occupier of any premises where a nuisance exists fails, after
  reasonable notice from the superintendent, to abate the nuisance and
  cleanse the premises, the superintendent may take such steps as are
  necessary to abate the nuisance and to cleanse the premises.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The owner, lessee, licencee or other occupier of the
  premises shall pay the costs of abating the nuisance and cleansing any
  premises referred to in subsection (2) that are incurred by the
  superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** No person shall deposit
any snow, leaves, rubbish or any matter of an offensive nature in a Park
except in such places, at such times and under such conditions as the
superintendent specifies.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  earth-pit privy, out-closet or privy vault shall be erected or used in
  any townsite or subdivision in a Park in which a water and sewer
  system has been installed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All earth-pit privies, out-closets or privy vaults on
  property that is not capable of being serviced by a Park water and
  sewer system shall be designed, located and maintained in accordance
  with standards approved by the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  buildings in a Park used for the purpose of residence, business or
  tourist accommodation shall have a plumbing system including an
  adequate supply of potable water and suitable sanitary fixtures,
  except that such buildings in outlying areas not serviced by a Park
  water supply and sewer system shall have sanitary privies, chemical
  closets or other such conveniences as the superintendent may approve.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where there is no Park water supply and sewer system
  in a Park, sewage or water waste shall be drained into a septic tank
  or other disposal system approved by the superintendent, which shall,
  where considered necessary by the superintendent, be equipped to
  chlorinate or otherwise treat the effluent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person occupying or using a public area in a Park shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at all times maintain the area in a satisfactory
    condition; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when vacating the area, restore the area to its
    natural condition as nearly as possible.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where incinerators or trash receptacles are provided
  in a Park, all refuse, waste paper or other material being discarded
  shall be placed therein.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
- SOR/2010-140, s. 12(E)
:::

## [Prohibited Conduct]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861786 .Part}

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall, in a Park,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cause any excessive noise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct or behave in a manner that unreasonably
    disturbs other persons in the Park or unreasonably interferes with
    their enjoyment of the Park; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out any action that unreasonably interferes
    with fauna or the natural beauty of the Park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may remove or have removed from a
  Park any person who by his disorderly conduct, behaviour or action is
  in violation of subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person who has been removed from a Park under
  subsection (2) shall enter or attempt to enter that Park for one year
  following the date of removal unless that person applies for and
  obtains permission from the superintendent to enter the Park.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, ss. 1(F), 7
- SOR/93-167, ss. 5, 6(E)
- SOR/2010-140, s. 14
:::

## [Cleaning of Sidewalks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861810 .Part}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner, lessee, licensee or other occupier of any lot in a townsite in
  a Park on which residence or the operation of a business is permitted
  shall remove snow, leaves or other material that has accumulated on
  any sidewalk bordering the lot in accordance with subsections (2) and
  (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any snow required to be removed by subsection (1)
  shall be removed within a period of 12 hours after it has fallen or
  within such longer period as may be specified by the superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any leaves or other material required to be removed
  by subsection (1) shall be removed whenever such leaves or other
  material interferes with or endangers pedestrian traffic or creates an
  obstructive, dangerous or unsightly condition or whenever removal is
  directed by the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)

## [Explosives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861817 .Part}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), no person
  shall, without an appropriate permit issued by the superintendent
  pursuant to subsection (2), bring into a Park or, in a Park, have in
  his possession, store, use, sell or offer for sale, any explosive.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may, on any conditions that he may
  prescribe, issue a permit to have in possession, store, use or sell
  any explosive in a Park to a person described in subsection (3) who is
  authorized under the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17) to have in
  possession, store, use, make, manufacture or sell such an explosive in
  any other part of Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the following
  persons are described:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in construction or demolition work
    who requires the explosive for his work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person representing a fraternal organization,
    service or other group that is sponsoring recreational or sport
    activities and requires the explosive in order to present a
    fireworks display on the occasion of a public holiday or
    celebration; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who is licensed under the National Parks
    Businesses Regulations to sell small arms ammunition.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person may, without obtaining a permit under this
  section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transport any explosive through a Park by railway
    in a manner authorized by the Railway Act or a regulation or order
    under that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transport explosives through a Park in accordance
    with the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17) or any provincial
    regulation respecting explosives;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be in possession of flares or fuses for use as
    safety equipment for motor vehicles, trains or boats in accordance
    with any provincial or federal requirements; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be in possession of small arms ammunition for his
    personal use.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

## [Closing of Areas and Ways]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861832 .Part}

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the superintendent deems it necessary for the prevention of any
  seasonal or temporary danger to persons, flora, fauna or natural
  objects in a Park, he may by notice in writing close to public use or
  traffic any area in the Park for the period he considers the danger
  will continue.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  displayed on each approach road, trail or other way of access to the
  area in the Park closed to public use or traffic.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall enter any area in a Park during the
  period that it is closed to public use or traffic pursuant to
  subsection (1) except with the permission of the superintendent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/82-949, s. 1(F)
- SOR/93-167, s. 6(E)
:::

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Where the Minister is of
the opinion that any road, street, sidewalk, trail, wharf, bridge or
other way or portion thereof in a Park has been rendered unnecessary for
public use or traffic, he may by order close to public use or traffic
that way or portion thereof.

operation and maintenance in a Park of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} telephone, telegraph, electrical, other than
  hydro-electrical, and natural gas services for use only in the Park;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} hydro-electrical power, pursuant to the [Dominion
  Water Power Act](/eng/acts/W-4), for the use of such power only in the
  Park.
