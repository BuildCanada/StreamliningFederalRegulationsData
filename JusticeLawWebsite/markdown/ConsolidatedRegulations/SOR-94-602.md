## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964701 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Debt Write-off Regulations,
1994](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-602).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964703 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a debt or an obligation due to
    Her Majesty or a claim by Her Majesty; ([créance]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any electronic form that
    is capable of being reproduced in intelligent, written form within a
    reasonable time. ([par écrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964708 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the determination of, or resolution of disputes over,
  what amount of a debt is owing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the process of negotiating and settling claims by Her
  Majesty; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} debts owing to Crown corporations.

## [Authority to Write Off Debts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964713 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 25(2) of the Act and sections 5 and 6 of these
  Regulations, the appropriate Minister of a department, or an officer
  authorized by that Minister in writing, may write off from the
  accounts of the department a debt, or a part of a debt, that has been
  determined to be uncollectible.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 25(2) of the Act and section 5
  of these Regulations, the appropriate Minister of a department, or an
  officer authorized by that Minister in writing, may write off from the
  accounts of the department a debt for which further administrative
  expense or other costs of collecting the debt are not justifiable in
  relation to the amount of the debt or the probability of collection.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 25(2) of the Act, the
  appropriate Minister of a department, or an officer authorized by that
  Minister in writing, may write off from the accounts of the department

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the balance of a future debt or a debt not due that
    remains after payment of the present value of the debt has been
    accepted as full settlement of the debt; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the balance of a debt that remains after a
    compromise settlement has been effected pursuant to an applicable
    authority.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  accountable advances or other debts arising from the overpayment by
  Her Majesty of salaries, wages or employment-related allowances, or
  any part of any of them, shall be written off without the approval of
  the Treasury Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to debts owing by
  former employees that are discovered after their employment has
  terminated and all termination benefits have been paid.

## [Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964723 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No debt, or any part of a
debt, shall be written off from the accounts of a department under
subsection 4(1) unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all reasonable collection action has been taken and
  all possible means of collection have been exhausted;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there is no possibility now or in the foreseeable
  future of collection through set-off; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the appropriate Minister, or the officer referred to
  in section 4, is satisfied on reasonable grounds that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the debtor is not resident in Canada, there are no
    apparent means of collecting the debt and there is no evidence that
    the debtor has a family or business concerns in Canada that could
    lead the debtor to return to Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the debtor cannot be located,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} evidence of the debt has been lost or destroyed
    and the debtor denies that a debt exists,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} legal proceedings are statute-barred or the debt
    is otherwise legally unenforceable, the debtor has refused to pay
    and there are no apparent alternative means of enforcing payment or
    collecting the debt,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the debtor is a corporation and the corporation is
    inoperative and without assets,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the debtor is an undischarged bankrupt corporation
    and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the corporation is without assets and the trustee
      has been discharged, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the trustee has confirmed in writing that the
      trustee does not foresee any further payments to Her Majesty,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the debtor is an undischarged bankrupt individual
    and

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the trustee has been discharged, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the trustee has confirmed in writing that the
      trustee does not foresee any further payments to Her Majesty,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the debtor is deceased and there is no known
    estate, or

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the debtor

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} is incapable of repaying the debt, in whole or in
      part, as evidenced, among other things, by the debtor's family
      income level being below the net monthly income level requiring
      payments from income toward debts, as stated in the guidelines on
      payments required from income issued periodically by the
      Superintendent of Bankruptcy,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} is not reasonably expected to have the capacity
      to repay the debt in the foreseeable future, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} does not own or have an interest in mortgageable
      real or personal property, has not entered into a contract to
      purchase property under the [Veterans' Land Act](/eng/acts/V-1.5)
      and does not own financial assets that could be applied toward
      discharging the debt, in whole or in part.

## [Procedures for the Control and Writing Off of a Debt]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964744 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All debts shall be
controlled within the appropriate department's accounts until they are
either collected or written off.

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister of each department or the deputy head on behalf
  of the Minister shall establish a formal review process for the
  writing off of debts, in whole or in part, under subsections 4(1) and
  (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an amount exceeding \$25,000, or such lower
  amount as may be fixed by the appropriate Minister or the deputy head,
  is proposed to be written off, the matter shall be referred to a
  review committee established pursuant to subsection (1), which
  committee shall make recommendations on the proposed write-off to the
  appropriate Minister or the officer referred to in section 4.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A review committee shall consist of at least three
  public officers, at least one of whom shall not have been involved in
  the creation or establishment of the debt that is proposed to be
  written off or in any action taken to collect it.

## [Retention of Information and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964750 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any information or records
concerning a debt that is written off pursuant to these Regulations
shall be retained until

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all audit procedures are completed and other
  administrative requirements with respect to the debt and its write-off
  are satisfied; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in addition, in the case of a debt referred to in
  subsection 4(1),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is no longer any probability of future
    set-off, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the limitation period for initiating any legal
    action with respect to the determination of the amount or recovery
    of the debt has expired.
