## Firearms Marking Regulations {#firearms-marking-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-275
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 2004-11-29

Firearms Marking Regulations

P.C. 2004-1435 2004-11-29
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBCA}, the Solicitor
General of Canada had the proposed [Firearms Marking
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-275) laid before each House of
Parliament on June 13, 2003, which date is at least 30 sitting days
before the date of this Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBCA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::

And whereas subsection 119(1) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^
provides that no proposed regulation that has been laid pursuant to
section 118 of that Act need again be laid, whether or not it has been
altered;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to section
117[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA}
of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Firearms Marking Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-275).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EGBAA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  8, s. 54
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713184 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specially imported firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specially imported firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm
      imported on a temporary basis by a business that holds a firearms
      licence, as a good under tariff item No. 9993.00.00 of the List of
      Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
      Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([arme à feu d'importation
      spéciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, [[agency
  firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[protected
  firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[public
  agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[public service
  agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meaning as in section 1
  of the [Public Agents Firearms
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, in these Regulations,
  [[transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} means transfer as defined in
  subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
::::

## [Marking of Manufactured Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713191 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every individual or
business that manufactures a firearm shall ensure that the firearm is
marked, at the time of manufacture, in accordance with section 4.
::::

## [Marking of Imported Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713193 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  individual, business or public service agency that imports a firearm
  shall ensure that the firearm is marked in accordance with section 4
  before the 60th day after its release as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or before transferring the
  firearm, whichever occurs first.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a firearm imported by an individual under section
    35 or 35.1 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a specially imported firearm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a protected firearm;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a firearm that was initially exported from Canada
    by an individual or business if the individual or business retained
    ownership of the firearm while the firearm was outside Canada; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a firearm that was initially exported from Canada
    by a public service agency and that was retained by that agency as
    an agency firearm while the firearm was outside Canada.
::::

## [Manner of Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713202 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  firearm shall be marked by permanently stamping or engraving on the
  firearms's frame or receiver the word "Canada" or the letters "CA" and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a manufactured firearm, the name of
    the manufacturer and the firearm's serial number; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an imported firearm, the last two
    digits of the year of the importation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The markings shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be legible;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a depth of at least 0.076 mm and a height of
    at least 1.58 mm; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), be visible without the
    need to disassemble the firearm.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an imported firearm, the Registrar, on
  application by the individual, business or public service agency that
  is importing it, shall grant the applicant an exemption from the
  requirement set out in paragraph (2)(c) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the firearm in a place that is visible only
    by disassembling the firearm is consistent with the current
    practices of the manufacturer of that model of firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the firearm does not provide a visible space
    suitable to stamp or engrave the markings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is rare;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is of a value that is unusually high
    for that type of firearm and that value would be significantly
    reduced if the markings were visible without disassembly; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is imported by a business that holds a
    licence for the purpose of using the firearm in respect of motion
    picture, television, video or theatrical productions or in
    publishing activities.
::::

## [Tampering with Markings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713217 .Part}

:::: nif
::: hiddenMsg
The following provision is not in force.
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall knowingly remove, alter, obliterate or deface a marking
  on a firearm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a public agent
  acting in the course of their duties or for the purposes of their
  employment.
::::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713221 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 1, 2025.
