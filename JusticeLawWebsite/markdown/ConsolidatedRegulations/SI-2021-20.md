[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[passport]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[passport]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} has the same meaning as in section 2
of the [Canadian Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted to any person
  who meets the conditions set out in subsection (2) of fees paid or
  payable under paragraphs 7(a) to (c) and item 14 of the schedule to
  the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253) or under section 4 of the
  [Consular Services Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The conditions are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at some time during the period beginning on July 7
    and ending on September 15, 2017, the person was in, or had their
    residence in, an area affected by a forest fire originating in
    British Columbia;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a passport that was issued to that person was,
    while it was valid, lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered
    inaccessible as a result of the forest fire referred to in paragraph
    (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on July 7, 2017, and
    ending on January 6, 2018, that person, or a person acting on their
    behalf, made an application for a passport with the same expiry date
    as the passport referred to in paragraph (b), in accordance with the
    [Canadian Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that person, or the person acting on their behalf,
    included with the application for a passport a declaration made by
    that person or the person acting on their behalf that states that
    the passport was lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible
    as a result of the forest fire referred to in paragraph (a) and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} proof that, at the time that the passport was
      lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible, that person was
      a resident of an area referred to in paragraph (a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a declaration made by that person or the person
      acting on their behalf that states that, at the time that the
      passport was lost, damaged, destroyed or rendered inaccessible,
      that person was in the area referred to in paragraph (a); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the fees in question have not been remitted under
    section 11 of the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253).
