## Deficient Postage Regulations {#deficient-postage-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-567
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Registration 1985-06-19

Regulations Respecting Mailable Matter Bearing No Postage or
Insufficient Postage

P.C. 1985-1954 1985-06-18
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of the proposed Regulations were published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on April 10, 1985 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister with respect thereto.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 17(1) of the [Canada Post Corporation
Act](/eng/acts/C-10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed [Regulations respecting mailable matter bearing no
postage or insufficient postage](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-567), made by
the Canada Post Corporation, effective September 1, 1985.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1980-81-82-83, c.
  54
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887165 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Deficient Postage
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-567).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887167 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[deficient postage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deficient postage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by which
    the postage payable on mailable matter has not been paid;
    ([affranchissement insuffisant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deficient postage item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deficient postage item]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any item of
    mailable matter having deficient postage. ([envoi insuffisamment
    affranchi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-199, s. 26
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887173 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of mailable matter posted in Canada for transmission by post and
delivery in or outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-158, s. 1
- SOR/87-683, s. 1
- SOR/88-435, s. 1
- SOR/90-11, s. 1
:::

## [General Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887177 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection 5(1),
a deficient postage item shall be returned to the sender.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  deficient postage item shall be forwarded to the addressee where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a charge is payable pursuant to the [Undeliverable
    and Redirected Mail Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1298)
    for redirected mail;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the item bears no return address; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the addressee has agreed, by means of a written
    service agreement, to accept deficient postage items and pay to the
    Corporation the amount referred to in subsection (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a deficient postage item is forwarded to the
  addressee pursuant to paragraph (1)(a) or (b) and accepted by the
  addressee, the deficient postage plus the deficient postage rate set
  out in section 6 are payable by the addressee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to paragraph (1)(c), a deficient
  postage item is forwarded to the addressee, the amount specified in
  the agreement referred to in paragraph (1)(c) plus the deficient
  postage rate set out in section 6 are payable by the addressee.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsections (2) and (3), where a
  deficient postage item is posted in Canada for transmission by post
  and delivery outside Canada, the deficient postage rate shall not
  apply.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-435, s. 2
:::

## [Deficient Postage Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887189 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The rate for a deficient
postage item that is forwarded to the addressee pursuant to section 5 is
the rate established by the Corporation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-435, s. 3
- SOR/90-11, s. 2
- SOR/90-797, s. 1
- SOR/91-623, s. 1
- SOR/91-637, s. 1
:::
