## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455505 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pay Equity
    Act](/eng/acts/P-4.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grouping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grouping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the grouping of ministers'
    offices under section 1 of the [Order Grouping Ministers' Offices
    for the Purpose of a Pay Equity
    Plan](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-116). ([groupement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455510 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grouping

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  sections 6 to 8 of the Act, the grouping becomes subject to the Act
  --- and is deemed to be recognized by the Pay Equity Commissioner as a
  single employer under section 106 of the Act --- on the day on which
  the [Order Grouping Ministers' Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity
  Plan](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-116) comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of new Prime Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if a new Prime Minister is appointed, the
  grouping is deemed to become subject to the Act --- and to be
  recognized by the Pay Equity Commissioner as a single employer under
  section 106 of the Act --- on the date of that appointment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Group of employers

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these Regulations, the provisions of the Act and any
regulations made under it that apply to groups of employers recognized
by the Pay Equity Commissioner as a single employer under section 106 of
the Act apply to the grouping.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pay equity plan --- new Prime Minister

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Upon the appointment of a
new Prime Minister, the grouping's posted pay equity plan ceases to
apply to the ministers whose offices form part of the grouping, and the
obligations of those ministers that arose as a result of the posting of
the plan also cease.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pay equity plan --- new minister

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If a new minister is
appointed without the Prime Minister being replaced, the pay equity plan
posted by the employers of the grouping is deemed to have been posted by
the new minister, and that minister assumes the same obligations as the
other ministers whose offices form part of the grouping.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Section 30, subsections
61(2) and 62(4), section 63, subsection 78(2) and sections 107 and 113
of the Act do not apply to the grouping.

## [Adaptations to the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455524 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- section 17

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
grouping, section 17 of the Act is adapted as follows:

> ::: ReadAsText
> [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to establish pay equity
> committee --- grouping
>
> **17** The grouping must, in respect of the pay equity plan that the
> grouping is required to establish, make all reasonable efforts to
> establish a pay equity committee.
> :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- subsections 68(1) to (3)

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
grouping, subsections 68(1) to (3) of the Act are adapted as follows:

> ::: ReadAsText
> [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to establish pay equity
> committee --- grouping
>
> - **68** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The grouping must, in respect of the pay
>   equity plan that the grouping is required to update, make all
>   reasonable efforts to establish a pay equity committee.
> :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- paragraphs 71(a) and (b)

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
grouping, paragraphs 71(a) and (b) of the Act are adapted as follows:

> ::: ReadAsText
> - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the grouping is considered to have 100 or more
>   employees if the sum of the average of the number of employees of
>   each of the employers in the grouping --- in the fiscal year
>   immediately before the fiscal year in which the notice is posted in
>   accordance with subsection 66(1) in respect of the pay equity plan
>   that the grouping is required to update --- is 100 or more; or
>
> - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grouping is considered to have less than 100
>   employees if the sum of the average of the number of employees of
>   each of the employers in the grouping --- in the fiscal year
>   immediately before the fiscal year in which the notice is posted in
>   accordance with subsection 66(1) or (2), as the case may be, in
>   respect of the pay equity plan that the grouping is required to
>   update --- is less than 100.
> :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- subsection 83(1)

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
grouping, subsection 83(1) of the Act is adapted as follows:

> ::: ReadAsText
> [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final version --- three-year maximum
>
> - **83** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each employer in the grouping must post the
>   final version of the revised pay equity plan and of the document
>   referred to in subsection 79(2) no later than the third anniversary
>   of the day on which the employer posted, as the case may be,
>
>   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the pay equity plan in accordance with section 55
>     or subsection 57(2); or
>
>   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the previous final version of the revised pay
>     equity plan and the previous final version of the document
>     referred to in subsection 79(2), in accordance with this
>     subsection or subsection 85(2).
> :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- subsection 88(2)

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** With respect to the
grouping, subsection 88(2) of the Act is adapted as follows:

> ::: ReadAsText
> - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lump sum
>
>   [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an employee referred to in subsection (1) is
>   entitled, as determined in accordance with the regulations, to a
>   lump sum in respect of a period determined in accordance with the
>   regulations --- which period is to begin on or after the day on
>   which the previous pay equity plan was posted in accordance with
>   section 55, subsection 57(2), section 83 or subsection 85(2), as the
>   case may be, and end no later than the day on which the revised pay
>   equity plan was posted in accordance with section 83 or, if the
>   employer posted it in accordance with subsection 85(2), the third
>   anniversary of the day referred to in subsection 83(1) --- and in an
>   amount determined in accordance with the regulations, the employer
>   is also required to pay to the employee, on the day on which
>   compensation is required to be increased under subsection (4), that
>   lump sum.
> :::

## [Adaptations to the Pay Equity Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455555 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- subsections 57(1) and (2)

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} With
  respect to the grouping, subsections 57(1) and (2) of the [Pay Equity
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-161) are adapted as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Range --- under 100 employees
  >
  > - **57** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The range of penalties in respect of a
  >   violation within a classification set out in column 1 of Part 1 of
  >   Schedule 3 is set out in column 2 for a first violation, in column
  >   3 for a second violation and in column 4 for a third or subsequent
  >   violation, with respect to a violation committed by
  >
  >   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employer with less than 100 employees at the
  >     time the notice of violation is served, if it has not posted a
  >     notice under subsection 65(1) of the Act;
  >
  >   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employer referred to in subparagraph
  >     127(2)(a)(ii) of the Act;
  >
  >   - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grouping, if the sum of the average of the
  >     number of employees of each of the employers in the grouping is
  >     less than 100; or
  >
  >   - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a bargaining agent representing some or all of
  >     the unionized employees of an employer referred to in
  >     paragraph (a) or (b) or of an employer that is in the grouping
  >     referred to in paragraph (c).
  >
  > - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sum of average
  >
  >   [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c),
  >
  >   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the employers in the grouping have posted
  >     one or more notices under subsection 66(1) of the Act at the
  >     time the notice of violation is served, the sum of the average
  >     is the one described in paragraph 71(b) of the Act; and
  >
  >   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the average is deemed to be
  >     the number of employees of each employer in the grouping at the
  >     time the notice of violation is served.
  > :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptation --- subsections 57(5) and
  (6)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} With respect to the grouping, subsections 57(5)
  and (6) of the Regulations are adapted as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average
  >
  >   [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(a) and (4)(a)
  >
  >   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the employer has posted one or more notices
  >     under subsection 65(1) of the Act at the time the notice of
  >     violation is served, the average is the one described in
  >     paragraph 69(a) of the Act in respect of the most recently
  >     posted notice; and
  >
  >   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the average is deemed to be
  >     the number of employees at the time the notice of violation is
  >     served.
  >
  > - [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sum of average
  >
  >   [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (3)(b) and (4)(b),
  >
  >   - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the employers in the grouping have posted
  >     one or more notices under subsection 66(1) of the Act at the
  >     time the notice of violation is served, the sum of the average
  >     is the one described in paragraph 71(a) of the Act; and
  >
  >   - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the average is deemed to be
  >     the number of employees of each employer in the grouping at the
  >     time the notice of violation is served.
  > :::
