## Order Transferring to the Department of Industry, Science and Technology the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service and Transferring to the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and Amalgamating and Combining the Departments of Industry, Science and Technology and the Department of Consumer and Corporate Affairs under the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-industry-science-and-technology-the-control-and-supervision-of-certain-portions-of-the-public-service-and-transferring-to-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-and-amalgamating-and-combining-the-departments-of-industry-science-and-technology-and-the-department-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-under-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-141
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring to the Department of Industry, Science and Technology
the Control and Supervision of Certain Portions of the Public Service
and Transferring to the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology the
Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate
Affairs and Amalgamating and Combining the Departments of Industry,
Science and Technology and the Department of Consumer and Corporate
Affairs under the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology

P.C. 1993-1487  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), to transfer

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the Department of Industry, Science and
    Technology the control and supervision of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the public service in
      Investment Canada known as the Investment Review Division and the
      Investment Research and Policy Division, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} those portions of the public service in the
      Department of Communications known as Automated Applications,
      Telematics and New Media, Spectrum Management Operations,
      Certification and Engineering Bureau, Communications Research
      Centre (CRC), Office of the Assistant Deputy Minister (Quebec),
      Regional Spectrum Services Centre and Canadian Workplace
      Automation Research Centre, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Industry, Science and
    Technology all the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
    Consumer and Corporate Affairs under any Act of Parliament; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, to amalgamate
  and combine the Department of Industry, Science and Technology and the
  Department of Consumer and Corporate Affairs under the Minister of
  Industry, Science and Technology and under the Deputy Minister of
  Industry, Science and Technology.
