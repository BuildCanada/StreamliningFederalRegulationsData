## Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Service Charge (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-greenhouse-vegetable-service-charge-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 211
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Service Charges to be Paid by Producers for
the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Greenhouse
Vegetables Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536649 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Service Charge
(Interprovincial and Export) Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536651 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipper in
    possession of a valid and subsisting certificate of appointment
    issued by the Commodity Board pursuant to the [Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Appointed Shippers' Procedures (Interprovincial and
    Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._210); ([expéditeur
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Producers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tomatoes,
    cucumbers and lettuce produced in the Province of Ontario in a
    greenhouse or any other enclosure under glass, plastic or other
    material used for the purpose of controlling temperature and
    providing protection for growing plants; ([légumes de
    serre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of greenhouse vegetables; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any quantity of greenhouse
    vegetables; ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who offers for
    sale, sells, receives, assembles, packs, ships for sale or
    transports a product but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer or an appointed shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports the product by motor
      vehicle as agent of the producer. ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536662 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of a product in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536664 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer who sells a
product directly to a consumer is, in respect of the product so sold,
exempt from these Regulations.

## [Imposition and Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536666 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the remuneration of the appointed
shippers as determined from time to time by the Commodity Board, a
service charge for the marketing of the product in the amount of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.005 per pound of tomatoes;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.05 per dozen of long, seedless cucumbers; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$0.025 per dozen of cucumbers other than long,
  seedless cucumbers.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536671 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  product is sold through the Commodity Board, the Commodity Board shall
  deduct the service charge imposed by section 5 from the moneys payable
  by the Commodity Board to the producer for the sale of the product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a product is sold through a shipper, the
  shipper shall deduct the service charge imposed by section 5 from the
  moneys payable by him to the producer and remit the amount deducted to
  the Commodity Board within 21 days after the receipt by him of the
  product.
