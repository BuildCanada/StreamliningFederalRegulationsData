## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-91
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2021-11-10

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2021-946 2021-10-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29970){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29970-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29995){#fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29995-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of Economic Development and Official
Languages to the Minister of State (Official Languages) the powers,
duties and functions of the Minister of Economic Development and
Official Languages under the [Official Languages
Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29980){#fn_81000-3-2263_hq_29980-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C.
2019-1368 of November 20, 2019[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29969){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_29969-ID0EBEAA}.
