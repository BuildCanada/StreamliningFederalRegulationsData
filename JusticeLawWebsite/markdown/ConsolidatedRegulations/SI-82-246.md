## Goods for Production of Aircraft Components Remission Order No. 2 {#goods-for-production-of-aircraft-components-remission-order-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-246
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-12-22

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Certain Goods Used in
the Production of Components for Certain Aircraft

P.C. 1982-3822 1982-12-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duty on
certain goods used in the production of components for certain
aircraft](/eng/regulations/SI-82-246).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638677 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Goods for Production of Aircraft Components Remission Order No.
2](/eng/regulations/SI-82-246).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638679 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duty paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on goods, other than goods classified under
tariff items 42700-1, 42700-2, 42700-3, 42700-4, 42700-5, 42700-6,
42700-7, 42700-8, 42700-9, 42701-1 and 42701-2 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), imported during the period commencing
January 1, 1983 and ending December 31, 1985 for use exclusively in the
production of components for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} aircraft of Boeing types 707, 727, 737, 747, 747SP,
  757, 767, CH46 and CH47,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} aircraft of Douglas types DC-8, DC-9, DC-10, CF-18
  and F-18, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} aircraft of Lockheed types L1011, CP-140 and P-3,

on condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
National Revenue within three years of the date of importation.
