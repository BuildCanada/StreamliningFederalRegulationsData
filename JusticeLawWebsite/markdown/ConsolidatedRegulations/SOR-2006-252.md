## Period for Entering into an Agreement for the Purpose of Jointly Establishing a Review Panel Regulations {#period-for-entering-into-an-agreement-for-the-purpose-of-jointly-establishing-a-review-panel-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2006-252
:::

[MACKENZIE VALLEY RESOURCE MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/M-0.2)

Registration 2006-10-19

Period for Entering into an Agreement for the Purpose of Jointly
Establishing a Review Panel Regulations

P.C. 2006-1107 2006-10-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 143[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#a_e){#a_e-ID0EEABA} of the [Mackenzie Valley
Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#b_e){#b_e-ID0EBABA}, the Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development has consulted with the territorial
Minister, first nations, the Tlicho Government and the Review Board
regarding the annexed regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development, pursuant to paragraph 143(1)(g)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#c_e){#c_e-ID0EGDAA} of the [Mackenzie Valley
Resource Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#b_e){#b_e-ID0EDDAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Period for Entering into an Agreement for the Purpose of Jointly
Establishing a Review Panel Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-252).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725269 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Mackenzie Valley Resource
Management Act](/eng/acts/M-0.2).

## [Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725271 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 138.1(4) of the Act, the period is 90 days after the day on
which the proposal is referred to the Minister of the Environment under
paragraph 130(1)(c) of the Act.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 141(4) of the Act, the period is 90 days after the day on
which an environmental impact review of the proposal is ordered under
subparagraph 128(1)(b)(i), paragraph 128(1)(c) or 130(1)(a),
subparagraph 130(1)(b)(ii) or paragraph 131(1)(b) of the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725274 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
