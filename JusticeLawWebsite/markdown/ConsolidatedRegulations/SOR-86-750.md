## Pacific Hake Exemption Notice {#pacific-hake-exemption-notice .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-750
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 1986-07-07

Notice Excepting Certain Pacific Hake from the Operation of Section 29
of the Fisheries Act
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to section 29 of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), hereby makes the annexed Notice
excepting certain Pacific hake from operation of section 29 of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14).

Ottawa, July 3, 1986

::: FlushRight
TOM SIDDON
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897735 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Notice may be cited as
the [Pacific Hake Exemption Notice](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-750).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897737 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Pacific hake caught in
those waters described in the [Pacific Fishery Management Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-215) as Areas 121, 123, 124, 125
and 126 are hereby excepted from the operation of section 29 of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14).
