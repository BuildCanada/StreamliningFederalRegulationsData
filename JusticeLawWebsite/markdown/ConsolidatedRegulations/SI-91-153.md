## Quebec Family Allowances Income Tax Remission Order, 1991 {#quebec-family-allowances-income-tax-remission-order-1991 .Title-of-Act}

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-12-04

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Taxes Payable in Respect of
Quebec Family Allowances for 1990 and 1991

in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection
23(2)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643838 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Family Allowances Income Tax Remission Order,
1991](/eng/regulations/SI-91-153).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643840 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
(*Loi*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643842 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission of income tax is
hereby granted, to every individual who received family allowances in
1990 or who receives family allowances in 1991 under the Act respecting
family assistance allowances of the Province of Quebec, of an amount
equal to the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of taxes, interest and penalties
      payable by the individual under Parts I, I.1 and I.2 of the Act
      for the 1990 or 1991 taxation year,

    exceeds

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the amount, if any, deemed under subsection
      120(2) of the Act to have been paid on account of the individual's
      tax under Part I of the Act for the 1990 or 1991 taxation year,

  exceeds

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the excess amount that would have been determined
    under subparagraph (a)(i) for the 1990 or 1991 taxation year, if no
    amount were included in computing the individual's income for that
    year with respect to family allowances received under the Act
    respecting family assistance allowances of the Province of Quebec in
    that year, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts each of which is an amount
    that would have been deemed under subsection 122.2(1), 122.4(3) or
    122.5(3) of the Act to have been paid by the individual on account
    of the individual's tax under Part I of the Act for the 1990 or 1991
    taxation year, if no amount were included in computing the
    individual's income for that year with respect to family allowances
    received under the Act respecting family assistance allowances of
    the Province of Quebec in that year

  exceeds

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total of all amounts each of which is an
    amount deemed under subsection 122.2(1), 122.4(3) or 122.5(3) of the
    Act to have been paid by the individual on account of the
    individual's tax under Part I of the Act for the 1990 or 1991
    taxation year.
