## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006011 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[casual goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods other than goods
imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or
institutional or other like use.

## [Casual Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006013 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Casual goods that are
acquired in Chile are deemed to originate in Chile and are entitled to
the benefit of the Chile Tariff if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the marking of the goods is in accordance with the
  marking laws of Chile and indicates that the goods are the product of
  Chile or Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods do not bear a mark and there is no evidence
  to indicate that the goods are not the product of Chile or Canada.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006017 .Part}

**[[Footnote

section 9 of the [Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-1.6), chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes
into force.
