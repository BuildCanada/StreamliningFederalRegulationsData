## Transitional Regulations for the Purpose of the National Energy Board Cost Recovery Regulations {#transitional-regulations-for-the-purpose-of-the-national-energy-board-cost-recovery-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-300
:::

[AN ACT TO ENACT THE IMPACT ASSESSMENT ACT AND THE CANADIAN ENERGY
REGULATOR ACT, TO AMEND THE NAVIGATION PROTECTION ACT AND TO MAKE
CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS TO OTHER ACTS](/eng/acts/)

Registration 2019-08-08

Transitional Regulations for the Purpose of the National Energy Board
Cost Recovery Regulations

P.C. 2019-1180 2019-08-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council considers it necessary, as of the day on
which the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24217){#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24217-ID0EJAGA}
comes into force, to provide for the calculation of cost recovery
charges under the [National Energy Board Cost Recovery
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24218){#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24218-ID0EGAGA}
by the Canadian Energy Regulator established by section 10 of that Act
as if it were the National Energy Board established by section
3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24220){#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24220-ID0EEAGA}
of the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24221){#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24221-ID0EBAGA}.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to section
43 of An Act to enact the Impact Assessment Act and the Canadian Energy
Regulator Act, to amend the Navigation Protection Act and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24222){#fn_81000-3-3571_hq_24222-ID0EDAFA},
makes the annexed [Transitional Regulations for the Purpose of the
National Energy Board Cost Recovery
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-300).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[commencement day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commencement day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day on which the
    [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1) comes into
    force. ([date de référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[National Energy Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[National Energy Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National
    Energy Board established by section 3 of the [National Energy Board
    Act](/eng/acts/N-7) as it read immediately before commencement
    day. ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NEB Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NEB Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy
    Board Cost Recovery Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-7) as they
    read immediately before commencement day. ([Règlement de
    l'Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Energy
    Regulator established by section 10 of the [Canadian Energy
    Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}NEB Regulations --- as of commencement
day

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On and
  after commencement day, the Regulator is to apply the NEB Regulations
  as if it were the National Energy Board and, in particular, is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} continue and complete any calculation of cost
    recovery charges, including the determination of costs for the
    purpose of that calculation or the determination of any other
    related matter, that had been commenced by the National Energy
    Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} begin and complete any calculation of cost recovery
    charges, including the determination of costs for the purpose of
    that calculation or the determination of any other related matter,
    that is required to be done under those Regulations and that had not
    been commenced by the National Energy Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of NEB Regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the NEB
  Regulations are to be read with any modifications that the
  circumstances require, including that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the National Energy Board is to be
    read as a reference to the Regulator, if the context of the
    reference relates to a period on or after commencement day; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reference to a cost or a forecast for the purpose
    of the calculation of cost recovery charges, including the
    determination of costs for the purpose of that calculation or the
    determination of any other related matter, is to be read, if the
    context requires, to be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a cost or a forecast that the National Energy
      Board determined, for any relevant period that ends before
      commencement day, to be attributable to its responsibilities under
      the [National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7) or any other Act of
      Parliament or that the Regulator determines, as if it were the
      National Energy Board, to be attributable to those
      responsibilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a cost or a forecast that the Regulator
      determines, for any relevant period that begins on or after
      commencement day, to be attributable to the carrying out of the
      Regulator's mandate under the [Canadian Energy Regulator
      Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1), including costs related to applications
      that are denied or withdrawn, as if the cost or forecast were
      determined by the National Energy Board to be attributable to the
      Board's responsibilities under the [National Energy Board
      Act](/eng/acts/N-7) or any other Act of Parliament.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2019-300_e_transform_hq_25914){#fn_SOR-2019-300_e_transform_hq_25914-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 10 of An Act to enact the Impact Assessment Act and the Canadian
Energy Regulator Act, to amend the Navigation Protection Act and to make
consequential amendments to other Acts, Chapter 28 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2019, comes into force, but if they are registered after that
day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
