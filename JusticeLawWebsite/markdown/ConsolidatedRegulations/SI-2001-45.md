## Certain Taxpayers Remission Order, 2000-3 {#certain-taxpayers-remission-order-2000-3 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-45
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2001-04-11

Certain Taxpayers Remission Order, 2000-3

P.C. 2001-429 2001-03-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
Certain Taxpayers Remission Order, 2000-3.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  24, s. 7(2)
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of amounts payable under the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) by a
person who was a member of the Newfoundland Association of Public
Employees where those amounts would not be payable if the contributions
paid by the person in respect of non-existent service pursuant to
section 32 of the Public Service (Pensions) Act, R.S.N. 1970, c. 319,
and the regulations made under that section, were deductible under the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), and where the following conditions
are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contributions for non-existent service were made
  pursuant to an agreement entered into prior to 1991;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the contributions were made in accordance with the
  pension plan as registered;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contributions have not been deducted from income
  in prior years; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person applies for this relief, in writing, to
  the Minister of National Revenue before January 1, 2002.
