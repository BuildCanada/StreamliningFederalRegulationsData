## Timber Regulations, 1993 {#timber-regulations-1993 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-118
:::

[FORESTRY ACT](/eng/acts/F-30)

Registration 1994-01-20

Regulations Respecting the Cutting and Removal of Timber

P.C. 1994-93 1994-01-20
::::

:::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Forestry, pursuant to section 6[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-118_e_hq_6294){#fn_SOR-94-118_e_hq_6294-ID0EICAA}
of the [Forestry Act](/eng/acts/F-30)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-118_e_hq_6295){#fn_SOR-94-118_e_hq_6295-ID0EFCAA},
is pleased hereby to revoke the Forestry Timber Regulations, C.R.C., c.
874, and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the cutting and
removal of timber](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-118), in substitution
therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958848 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Timber Regulations, 1993](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-118).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958850 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant for a permit
    who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who has reached the age of majority
      as set out under the laws of the province in which the permit is
      to be issued, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, partnership or joint
      venture; ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cutting and removal of timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cutting and removal of timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the
    cutting or removal of timber; ([coupe et enlèvement du
    bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[debris]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[debris]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all debris that results from
    the cutting and removal of timber and includes any garbage that is
    brought into or left in a forest area and any logging slash on roads
    and other rights-of-way; ([débris]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any stumpage or royalty to be
    paid for the cutting and removal of timber in a forest area or
    portion thereof; ([droits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forest area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forest area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lands referred to in
    section 5 of the [Forestry Act](/eng/acts/F-30); ([région
    forestière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[forestry officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[forestry officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated
    as a forestry officer pursuant to section 4; ([agent
    forestier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2017-52, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a corporation,
    a partnership or a joint venture that enters into an agreement under
    section 14; ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[timber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means trees that are standing, that
    have fallen or that have been cut. ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958865 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of the cutting and removal of timber in a forest area.

## [Forestry Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958867 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may designate
as a forestry officer for the purposes of these Regulations any person
who is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} employed by the Government of Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} contracted to perform services for the Government of
  Canada.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958871 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), no person shall cut and remove timber in a forest
  area except under the authority of a permit issued by a forestry
  officer pursuant to section 7 or an agreement entered into with the
  Minister under section 14.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to an employee of the
  Department of Forestry who is engaged in the cutting and removal of
  timber in a forest area in the performance of the employee's duties.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958875 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The fees that are to be
paid for a permit issued under section 7 are those that are imposed by
the province in which the forest area is located and to which the permit
applies.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of issuing a permit for the cutting and removal of timber, a
  forestry officer shall cause a notice to be posted in a public place
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any proposed cutting and removal of timber in a
    particular forest area or portion thereof;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information respecting the cutting and removal
    of timber that is relevant or that an applicant is required to
    supply;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date by which an applicant shall apply for a
    permit; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the forestry officer to
    whom an application for a permit is to be directed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant may apply for a permit in writing or in
  person to the forestry officer referred to in paragraph (1)(d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (5), a forestry
  officer shall issue a permit to an applicant who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant previously cut and removed timber
    under a permit issued or an agreement made pursuant to these
    Regulations, did not contravene the terms or conditions of that
    permit or agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} applies within the time limit therefor; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if two or more applicants meet the criteria set out
    in paragraphs (a) and (b), has been selected by the drawing of lots.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Before a forestry officer issues a permit to an
  applicant, the applicant shall pay to the Receiver General, by cheque
  or cash, the fees calculated by the forestry officer pursuant to
  section 6.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A forestry officer shall not issue a permit to an
  applicant if the aggregate of the fees to be paid by the applicant
  exceeds \$2,000 per fiscal year per forest area.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A permit shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forest area or portion thereof in which the
    timber is to be cut and removed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the approximate amount and the species of timber
    that the permit holder may cut;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the means by which the timber is to be measured;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions respecting the cutting and
    removal of the timber that will allow for the protection of the
    forest area.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 3(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If, under paragraph 7(6)(d)
or 12(1)(b), the forestry officer includes terms or conditions in a
permit or gives instructions for the protection of the forest area,
those terms, conditions or instructions shall be to encourage
regeneration and reforestation, to avoid damage to vegetation or to the
timber that is not covered by the permit, and to avoid damage to the
cutting and removal site and any animal habitats.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-8-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 8"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
these Regulations, a forestry officer shall issue a permit without
requiring the payment of any fees, to any department or agency of the
Government of Canada, for the cutting and removal in its own lands in a
forest area of such timber as that department or agency may require for
its purposes.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), timber
  that is cut under the authority of a permit shall be measured by a
  mill scale or by a scaler who holds a scaling licence that is valid in
  the province in which the timber is cut.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the use of a mill scale or a scaler is not
  appropriate to the forest area or to the species, quantity or product
  of timber being cut, timber that is cut under the authority of a
  permit shall be measured by such method as the forestry officer
  considers appropriate, having regard to that forest area, species,
  quantity or product.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a permit holder
cuts and removes timber in excess of the amount set out in the permit,
the permit holder shall pay to the Receiver General, by cheque or cash,
fees calculated by a forestry officer pursuant to section 6 on the
amount of that timber that exceeds the permitted amount.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  forestry officer shall cancel a permit if the permit holder

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fails to observe any terms or conditions of the
    permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fails or refuses to comply with such instructions
    for the protection of the forest area as have been given by the
    forestry officer who is supervising the cutting and removal of
    timber in the forest area; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fails to observe any municipal, provincial or
    federal law that pertains to forest activities in the forest area to
    which the permit applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A forestry officer who cancels a permit pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall notify the permit holder in writing of the
  cancellation and of the reasons therefor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 21 days after receipt of a notice of
  cancellation referred to in subsection (2), the permit holder may in
  writing appeal the cancellation to the Minister.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after receipt of an appeal under
  subsection (3), the Minister shall render a decision on the appeal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 5
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A permit may not be
assigned or transferred.

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958918 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, after
making a public call for tenders, enter into an agreement for the
cutting and removal of timber in a forest area with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who has reached the age of majority as
  set out under the laws of the province in which the agreement is
  entered into; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, partnership or joint venture.

<!-- -->

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), every
  operator shall provide a security deposit to the Minister with each
  tender to ensure due performance of the agreement by the operator.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the security deposit, that is
  determined by the Minister in the agreement, shall be not less than
  10% of the tender price.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall apply the security deposit
  provided pursuant to subsection (1) to the tender price of the
  agreement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-52, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-15-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 15"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An agreement shall be in
a form approved by the Minister and may contain terms, conditions or
information respecting, but not necessarily limited to,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the payment schedule for the tender price of the
  agreement, including the security deposit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forest area or portion thereof in which the
  operator may cut and remove timber;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity, product or species of timber that the
  operator may cut;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} timber utilization standards;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} scaling and measurement practices;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} protection of the environment;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} silviculture;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the location of and standards for roads, trails,
  landing areas, buildings or other works that are to be constructed by
  the operator;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} default and termination of the agreement; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} indemnity by the operator.

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  permit holder and every operator shall remove from the forest area
  within any period that is specified in the permit or agreement or, if
  no period is specified in it, within 12 months after the expiry of the
  permit or the completion of operations in accordance with the
  agreement,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all buildings and other works owned by the permit
    holder or operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all equipment that was brought into the forest area
    in connection with the cutting and removal of timber; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all debris.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, on the request of the permit holder or
  operator and before the expiry of the period referred to in subsection
  (1), the forestry officer may extend that period by a maximum of 12
  months having regard to the circumstances beyond the permit holder's
  or operator's control that prevent them from removing the things
  referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) to (c) within the specified period.
