## Involuntary Retirements Remission Order {#involuntary-retirements-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 772
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Granting Remission of Taxes Payable in Respect of Persons
Involuntarily Retired from the Public Service
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561881 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Involuntary Retirements Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._772).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561883 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is or was
    employed in the public service of Canada;
    ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an annuity within the meaning
    of the Act; ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an amount received in
    respect of a loss of salary or pension benefits arising from the
    termination of employment of an employee;
    ([paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[termination of employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[termination of employment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, with
    respect to an employee, the termination of his employment pursuant
    to subsection 20(12) of the [Public Service Superannuation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358), as that subsection
    read on October 19, 1972. ([cessation d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561890 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the 1972 or any subsequent taxation year, a settlement was or is
  received by an employee or his legal representative because of the
  termination of employment, remission is hereby granted of the amount
  by which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the income tax payable by him or his legal
    representative for the taxation year

  exceeds

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by him or
      his legal representative for the taxation year if the settlement
      had not been received, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the tax that, if the salary or pension in
      respect of which the settlement was paid had in fact been received
      by him or his legal representative in the taxation years in
      respect of which the settlement was paid, would have been payable
      less the tax that was so paid in respect of those years

  together with the interest, if any, payable on that amount.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies to the widow or child of an
  employee, as the circumstances require.
