## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841565 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any product referred to in items
5200 to 5210 of Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export Control
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).

## [Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841568 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An application under
subsection 7(1) of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19)
or a request under section 8.31 of that Act for a permit to export a
product shall include, in the form provided by the Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email address
  of the exporter and of any agent or mandatary applying on the
  exporter's behalf;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the exporter or agent or mandatary is a
  corporation, the name of a contact person for the corporation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's identifier number assigned by the
  Minister;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the official language selected for communications
  with the agent or mandatary and the exporter;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the exporter and, if
  applicable, the agent or mandatary are residents of Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the product;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the tariff classification of the product set out in,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for a product referred to in item 5201 of Group 5
    of the schedule to the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202), the List of Tariff Provisions
    set out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a product referred to in item 5203 or 5204 of
    Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202), the Harmonized Tariff Schedule
    of the United States (1999) (United States International Trade
    Commission Pub. 2831, 19 U.S.C. § 1202 (1988)), and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for a product referred to in any of items 5205 to
    5210 of Group 5 of the schedule to the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202), Council Regulation (EEC) No
    2658/87 of 23 July 1987 on the tariff and statistical nomenclature
    and on the Common Customs Tariff, made by the European Union, or the
    Tariff of the United Kingdom, established by regulations made under
    section 8 of the Taxation (Cross-border Trade) Act 2018, c. 22, of
    the United Kingdom;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product and the unit of
  measurement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the value in Canadian dollars of the product;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment of the product;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry of the product;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the date of entry of the product;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the importer or consignee;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the product's country of origin; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} any other information requested by the Minister for
  clarification purposes related to the application or request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  export permit holder may, in writing, before the expiry date of the
  permit, make a request to the Minister to amend the permit. The
  request must state the terms and conditions of the permit in respect
  of which the amendment is being requested and the reasons for the
  amendment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the request is approved, the letter of the
  Minister amending the permit, together with the original permit,
  becomes a valid permit.
