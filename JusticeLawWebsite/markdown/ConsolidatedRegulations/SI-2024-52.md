## Order Transferring from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to the Minister of Foreign Affairs the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Great Lakes Fishery Commission {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-fisheries-and-oceans-to-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-great-lakes-fishery-commission .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-52
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2024-11-06

Order Transferring from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to the
Minister of Foreign Affairs the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation
to the Great Lakes Fishery Commission

P.C. 2024-1114 2024-10-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, under section 33 of the Government Organization Act,
1979[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725994925){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725994925-ID0EECBA},
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is responsible for the
administration of the [Great Lakes Fisheries Convention
Act](/eng/acts/F-17)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725995010){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725995010-ID0EBCBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725994925-ID0EECBA){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725994925}S.C.
  1978-79, c. 13
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725995010-ID0EBCBA){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725995010}R.S., c.
  F-17
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647930){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647930-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647952){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647952-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans to the Minister of
Foreign Affairs the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of
Fisheries and Oceans in relation to the Great Lakes Fishery Commission.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647930-ID0EECAA){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647930}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647952-ID0EBCAA){#fn_81000-3-2769_hq_1725647952}R.S., c.
  P-34
