## Firearms Fees Regulations {#firearms-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-204
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Firearms Fees Regulations

P.C. 1998-479 1998-03-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Firearms Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996 and
October 30, 1997, which dates are at least 30 sitting days before the
date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs 117(p)
and (q) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the
annexed [Firearms Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-204).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019473 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-restricted firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a firearm other
    than a prohibited firearm or a restricted firearm. ([arme à feu sans
    restrictions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Individuals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019477 .Part}

### [Fees Payable for Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019478 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 2.1 to
6, the fee payable by an individual for the issuance or renewal, as the
case may be, of a licence referred to in column 1 of Schedule 1 is the
appropriate fee set out in column 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 1
:::

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** If a possession and
acquisition licence is issued for more than one class of firearm, the
fee payable is the greatest of the appropriate fees set out in item 4 of
Schedule 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 1
:::

### [Reduction and Waiver of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019485 .Subheading}

- **[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 3, the fee payable
  by an individual for the renewal of a possession licence that is
  solely for non-restricted firearms or for the issuance or renewal of a
  possession and acquisition license that is solely for non-restricted
  firearms is waived for the period specified in subsection (4) if the
  individual had been issued, on or after December 1, 1998, a possession
  licence for firearms (other than a possession licence for firearms for
  individuals who are less than 18 years old) or a possession and
  acquisition licence for firearms.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a fee
  payable by an individual for a licence if the licence previously held
  by the individual had been revoked in circumstances other than those
  described in subsection 72(1.1) of the Act and that revocation had not
  been cancelled.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fee payable for the renewal of a possession
  licence for firearms for individuals who are less than 18 years old is
  waived for the period specified in subsection (4).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (3), the
  period begins on September 18, 2012 and ends on May 16, 2013.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2006-96, s. 1
- SOR/2008-145, s. 1
- SOR/2009-137, s. 1
- SOR/2010-102, s. 1
- SOR/2011-111, s. 1
- SOR/2012-101, s. 1
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2.2-20120511.html "Link to previous version of section 2.2"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If an individual has held a
possession licence for firearms for three years or less and that
individual makes an application for a possession and acquisition licence
for firearms, the fee payable for that licence is reduced by 50%.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If an individual has held a
possession and acquisition licence that is solely for non-restricted
firearms for three years or less and that individual makes an
application for a possession and acquisition licence for restricted or
prohibited firearms, the fee payable for that licence is reduced by 50%.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 2
- SOR/2012-101, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable for the
issuance or renewal of a possession licence for firearms for individuals
who are less than 18 years old is waived in respect of an individual who
is eligible to hold the licence under subsection 8(2) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-471, s. 6
- SOR/2004-272, s. 2
:::

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2004-272, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable for renewal
of a possession licence that is solely for non-restricted firearms, or
for issuance or renewal of a possession and acquisition licence that is
solely for non-restricted firearms, is waived in respect of individuals
who require firearms to hunt or trap in order to sustain themselves or
their families.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 2
- SOR/2012-101, s. 3
:::

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Businesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019507 .Part}

### [Fees Payable for Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019508 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the fee payable by a business for the issuance or
  renewal of a licence to carry on an activity referred to in column 1
  of Schedule 2 is the appropriate fee set out in column 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there are two or more activities described in
  column 1 of Schedule 2 that are carried on by a business in the same
  place, the fee payable by the business for the issuance or renewal of
  a licence to carry on those activities is the greatest of the
  appropriate fees set out in column 2 for those activities.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 3
:::

### [Waiver of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019514 .Subheading}

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** If the sole activity of
a business consists of the wholesale selling and manufacturing of
ammunition, the fee payable by that business for a licence to carry on
that activity is waived, provided that any factory or magazine that it
uses for that activity is a licensed factory or magazine within the
meaning of section 2 of the [Explosives Act](/eng/acts/E-17).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 3
:::

### [Fees Payable for Authorizations and Confirmations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019520 .Subheading}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 17,
the fee payable for the issuance or renewal, as the case may be, of an
authorization or a confirmation referred to in column 1 of Schedule 3 is
the appropriate fee set out in column 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 4
:::

### [Waiver of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019524 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If an individual has paid
a fee for an authorization to carry for use in connection with a lawful
profession or occupation for a period of one year or less and, within 12
months after its issuance, requests any other authorization to carry for
that use, the fee payable for that other authorization is waived.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-272, s. 5
:::

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019529 .Part}

### [Replacement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019530 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the replacement fee for a lost, stolen or destroyed licence, including
  any document referred to in subsection 120(4) of the Act, or for a
  lost, stolen or destroyed authorization or confirmation, is \$25.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The replacement fee for a lost, stolen or destroyed
  authorization to import or to export is waived.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The replacement fee for a lost, stolen or destroyed
  possession licence for firearms for individuals who are less than 18
  years old is \$10.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), the replacement fee for
  lost, stolen or destroyed registration certificates is \$10 for each
  set of four or fewer certificates replaced at the same time.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The replacement fee for a lost, stolen or destroyed
  registration certificate for a non-restricted firearm or for a licence
  referred to in section 6 is waived in respect of individuals who
  require firearms to hunt or trap in order to sustain themselves or
  their families.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-471, s. 8
- SOR/2004-142, s. 2
- SOR/2004-272, s. 6
:::

**[[21.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2000-259, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}
