## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024421 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([loi
    antérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handgun that is
    referred to in paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
    firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([arme de poing prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public service agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 1 of the [Public Agents Firearms
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-203). ([agence de services
    publics]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibited Handguns --- Individuals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024426 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any individual who, during
the period beginning on December 1, 1998 and ending on December 31,
2005, is in possession of a prohibited handgun, other than a handgun
referred to in subsection 12(6) of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),
that was acquired and registered after February 14, 1995 and before
December 1, 1998, cannot be found guilty of an offence under Part III of
the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) by reason only of being in
possession of that handgun while doing or prior to doing any of the
following things:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deactivating the handgun so that it is no longer a
  firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} changing the barrel length so that the handgun
  becomes a restricted firearm, and turning in the prohibited barrel to
  a police officer or a firearms officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporting the handgun in accordance with all
  applicable legal requirements, including the legal requirements of the
  country to which the handgun is exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} turning in the handgun to a police officer or a
  firearms officer for destruction or other disposal; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} selling or giving the handgun to a business ---
  including a museum --- licensed under subsection 11(2) of the
  [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) to possess such a handgun, or to a
  public service agency.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any individual who is not
an individual referred to in subsection 12(6) of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) and who, during the period beginning on December
1, 1998 and ending on December 31, 2005, is in possession of a
prohibited handgun referred to in that subsection that was acquired by
the individual after February 14, 1995 and before December 1, 1998 and
for which a registration certificate had been issued to or applied for
by another individual under the former Act, cannot be found guilty of an
offence under Part III of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) by reason
only of being in possession of that handgun while doing or prior to
doing any of the following things:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deactivating the handgun so that it is no longer a
  firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} changing the barrel length so that the handgun
  becomes a restricted firearm, and turning in the prohibited barrel to
  a police officer or a firearms officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporting the handgun in accordance with all
  applicable legal requirements, including the legal requirements of the
  country to which the handgun is exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} turning in the handgun to a police officer or a
  firearms officer for destruction or other disposal;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} selling or giving the handgun to a business ---
  including a museum --- licensed under subsection 11(2) of the
  [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) to possess such a handgun, or to a
  public service agency; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} selling or giving the handgun to an individual
  referred to in subsection 12(6) of the [Firearms
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) who is licenced to possess such a handgun.

## [Prohibited Handguns --- Businesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024444 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any person who was the
holder of a permit to carry on a business required under subsection
105(4) of the former Act and who, during the period beginning on
December 1, 1998 and ending on December 31, 2005, is in possession of a
prohibited handgun acquired on or before February 14, 1995 and reported
to the Commissioner or to an authorized person under paragraph
105(1.1)(d) of the former Act, cannot be found guilty of an offence
under Part III of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) by reason only of
being in possession of that handgun while doing or prior to doing any of
the following things:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deactivating the handgun so that it is no longer a
  firearm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} changing the barrel length so that the handgun
  becomes a restricted firearm, and turning in the prohibited barrel to
  a police officer or a firearms officer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporting the handgun in accordance with all
  applicable legal requirements, including the legal requirements of the
  country to which the handgun is exported;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} turning in the handgun to a police officer or a
  firearms officer for destruction or other disposal; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} selling or giving the handgun to a business ---
  including a museum --- licensed under subsection 11(2) of the
  [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) to possess such a handgun, or to a

## [Firearms Possessed by Businesses on Consignment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024460 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for a business that, on
  December 31, 2002, possesses a firearm --- that was unregistered or
  deemed in law to be registered --- for the purpose of sale on
  consignment and holds a licence that authorizes the retail sale of
  firearms, including a sale on consignment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to permit the
  business to sell or otherwise lawfully dispose of the firearm.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on January 1, 2003 and ends
  on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day when the business sells the firearm or, if
    the sale involves the export of the firearm, the day when the
    business exports the firearm in accordance with all applicable legal
    requirements, including the legal requirements of the country to
    which the firearm is exported,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day when the business deactivates the firearm
    so that is no longer a firearm,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day when the business turns in the firearm to a
    police officer or a firearms officer for destruction or other
    disposal, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} December 31, 2003.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-439, s. 2
