## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905369 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Notifiable Transactions
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-348).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905371 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Competition
    Act](/eng/acts/C-34); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asset securitization transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asset securitization transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    transaction or series of related transactions entered into where

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of obtaining funds or credit or
      for related financial purposes, a person, directly or indirectly,
      sells, assigns, transfers, leases or otherwise disposes of
      financial assets to a person or persons or a trust or trusts who
      acquire an undivided co-ownership interest or interests in the
      financial assets or whose business consists solely or primarily of
      acquiring, holding or creating interests in, creating securities
      or debt backed or secured by, or otherwise dealing in, financial
      assets, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after their disposal, the financial assets will
      be administered, serviced and operated by

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person disposing of the financial assets or
        an affiliate of that person,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person that is an agent of or a trustee for
        all persons that own securities or debt backed or secured by, or
        representing an interest in, the financial assets unless there
        is a person that, together with that person's affiliates, owns,
        directly or indirectly, or exercises control or direction over
        more than 10 per cent of the securities or debt backed or
        secured by, or representing an interest in, the financial
        assets, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other person or trust other than,

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a person or persons or trust or trusts that
          acquire any interest in the financial assets unless the
          interest is held by way of security only or is limited to an
          undivided co-ownership interest or interests that, in
          aggregate, are not more than 10 per cent of all the interests,

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an affiliate of a person described in clause
          (A),

        - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a person that, together with that person's
          affiliates, owns, directly or indirectly, or exercises control
          or direction over more than 10 per cent of the securities or
          debt backed or secured by, or representing an interest in, the
          financial assets,

        - [(D)]{.lawlabel} a person that owns, directly or indirectly,

          - [(I)]{.lawlabel} securities carrying more than 10 per cent
            of the voting rights attached to all voting securities for
            the time being outstanding of a person described in clause
            (C), or

          - [(II)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 per cent of the equity
            securities for the time being outstanding of a person
            described in clause (C), or

        - [(E)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which a person described in
          clause (C) owns, directly or indirectly,

          - [(I)]{.lawlabel} securities carrying more than 10 per cent
            of the voting rights attached to all voting securities of
            the corporation for the time being outstanding, or

          - [(II)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 per cent of the equity
            securities of the corporation for the time being
            outstanding; ([transaction de titralisation d'éléments
            d'actif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[audited financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[audited financial statements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means financial
    statements in respect of which a report has been prepared by an
    external professional auditor accredited for that purpose who is a
    member in good standing of any corporation, association or institute
    of professional accountants; ([états financiers
    vérifiés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equity security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equity security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any security that
    carries a residual right to participate in the earnings of the
    issuer of the security and, upon the liquidation and winding up of
    the issuer, in the issuer's assets; ([Titres de toute
    nature]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial asset]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any interest,
    including any related security or collateral, in any

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} debt, receivable, account, claim or other right
      to payment,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contract or obligation that generates or secures
      any thing referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} security (other than a voting share of a
      corporation or an interest in a combination) backed or secured by,
      or representing an interest in, a thing referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or in both paragraphs (a) and (b); ([actif
      financier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reference date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the Commissioner is notified of a proposed
      transaction pursuant to section 114 of the Act, the date on which
      the Commissioner receives the notification; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the notification referred to in
      paragraph (a) is not given to the Commissioner,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposed transaction referred
        to in subsection 110(4) of the Act, the thirtieth day preceding
        the day on which articles of amalgamation in respect of the
        proposed transaction are filed with the appropriate governmental
        or regulatory authority, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proposed transaction referred
        to in subsection 110(2), (3), (5) or (6) of the Act, the
        thirtieth day preceding the day on which beneficial ownership of
        property forming any part of the subject-matter of the
        transaction is to be conveyed, assigned or otherwise
        transferred; ([date de référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2010-22, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[voting security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[voting security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any security that
    carries voting rights under all circumstances or by reason of an
    event that has occurred and is continuing. ([Titres comportant droit
    de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Audited Financial Statements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905404 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Audited financial
statements shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be prepared in accordance with accounting principles
  that are normally used by the person with respect to whom the
  statements were prepared and that are generally accepted for the type
  of business carried on by the person; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include working papers and other records used to
  prepare audited financial statements if reference to the working
  papers and other records is necessary to obtain information required
  for making a determination, pursuant to sections 109 and 110 of the
  Act, of the aggregate value of assets or the gross revenues from
  sales.

## [Determination of Aggregate Value --- General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905410 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 109 and 110 of the Act, in determining the
  aggregate value of assets, the following amounts shall be deducted:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any amount that represents duplication arising from
    transactions between affiliates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount that represents duplication arising from
    an ownership interest of one person in another person, whether or
    not those persons are affiliated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any amount provided for depreciation or diminution
    of value.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 109 and 110 of the Act,
  in determining the aggregate value of assets, no amount shall be
  deducted for liabilities or encumbrances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The aggregate value of assets shall be expressed in
  Canadian dollars.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The conversion into Canadian dollars of the aggregate
  amount of assets reported in foreign currency shall be based on the
  noon exchange rate quoted by the Bank of Canada on the date that the
  aggregate value of assets is to be determined in accordance with these
  Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-8, s. 2
- SOR/2010-22, s. 11
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purposes of sections 109 and 110 of the
  Act, the gross revenues from sales of a person for an annual period
  shall be determined by aggregating the following amounts accruing to
  that person during that period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts accruing from the sale or lease of goods,
    other than amounts that are not properly included in revenue in
    accordance with the accounting principles referred to in paragraph
    3(a), and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts accruing from the rendering of services,

  without deducting any expenses or other amounts incurred or provided
  for in relation to the sale or lease of goods or the rendering of
  services.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In determining the gross revenues from sales, any
  amount that represents duplication arising from transactions between
  affiliates shall be deducted.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Gross revenues from sales shall be expressed in
  Canadian dollars.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The conversion into Canadian dollars of the gross
  revenues from sales reported in foreign currency shall be based on the
  noon exchange rate quoted by the Bank of Canada on the last day of the
  annual period for which the gross revenues from sales are to be
  determined in accordance with these Regulations.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Provisions with Respect to Parties to the Transaction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905439 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 109(1)(a) of the Act, the aggregate value of
  assets in Canada of the parties to a transaction, together with their
  affiliates, shall be determined by aggregating the aggregate values of
  the assets in Canada of each of the parties and each affiliate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each party or affiliate referred to in subsection
  (1), the aggregate value of its assets in Canada shall equal the
  aggregate amount of those assets as stated in the audited financial
  statements referred to in section 6.

## [Determination of Aggregate Value --- Specific Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905478 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  aggregate value of a person's assets cannot reasonably be determined
  in accordance with subsection 8(2) or 9.1(2) or section 10, the
  aggregate value of the assets

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall equal the aggregate amount of the assets as
    stated in the books of the person with such adjustment as may be
    necessary to ensure that the determination is in accordance with the
    accounting principles referred to in paragraph 3(a); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall be determined as of the most recent date that
    the amount can reasonably be determined, provided that that date is
    within three months prior to the reference date.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The determination of the aggregate value of assets
  referred to in subsection (1) is subject to the requirements of
  section 4.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-8, s. 5(F)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall equal the amount of the gross revenues as

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  subsequent to the day or date referred to in section 6 or 12 or the
  annual period referred to in section 7 or 13, as the case may be, any
  party to a proposed transaction or any affiliate of that party was a
  party to or was otherwise affected by a transaction or event the
  consequences of which, if taken into account, would affect the
  determination of whether notification is required to be given under
  section 114 of the Act with respect to the proposed transaction, the
  values or amounts referred to in sections 8 to 13 shall be adjusted to
  reflect that transaction or event.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A transaction or event referred to in subsection (1)

    proposed transaction or their affiliates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any disposition, acquisition or reorganization that
    is likely to have a material effect on the aggregate value of the
    assets of the parties to the proposed transaction or their
    affiliates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any agreement, arrangement, understanding or other
    transaction or event that is likely to have a material effect on the
    aggregate value of the assets or gross revenues from sales of the
    parties to the proposed transaction or their affiliates.

## [Transactions that Are Exempt from Part IX of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905502 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  transaction that is an acquisition of financial assets that is
  undertaken to give effect to an asset securitization transaction is
  exempt from the application of Part IX of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A transaction that is an acquisition of assets other
  than financial assets that is undertaken to give effect to an asset
  securitization transaction is exempt from the application of Part IX
  of the Act, unless any person would, as a result of the transaction or
  transactions, acquire all or substantially all of the assets, other
  than financial assets, of a business or an operating segment of a
  business carried on by the person disposing of the assets.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-8, s. 7
:::

## [Information Required]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905508 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 114(1) of the Act and subject to subsection
  (2), the following information is to be supplied to the Commissioner:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed transaction and the
    business objectives intended to be achieved as a result of it;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each legal document, or the most recent
    draft of that document if it is not yet executed, that is to be used
    to implement the proposed transaction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the foreign competition or antitrust
    authorities that have been notified of the proposed transaction by
    the parties and the date on which each authority was notified;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each party,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its full name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the addresses of its principal offices,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a list of its affiliates that have significant
      assets in Canada or significant gross revenues from sales in, from
      or into Canada and a chart describing the relationships between
      the party and those affiliates, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of its principal businesses and
      the principal businesses of its affiliates referred to in
      subparagraph (iii), including

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} their most recent annual report and, if the
        annual report is not available or if the financial statements
        are different from those contained in the report, audited
        financial statements relating to their principal businesses for
        their most recently completed fiscal year, and financial
        statements for subsequent interim periods,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a summary description of each of the principal
        categories of products, as defined by the party in its
        day-to-day operations, that it produces, supplies or distributes
        and each of the principal categories of products produced,
        supplied or distributed by its affiliates referred to in
        subparagraph (iii),

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} statements identifying, for each of those
        principal categories of products, the twenty most important
        current suppliers and customers, the contact names, the
        telephone numbers and addresses of those suppliers and
        customers, and the annual volume or dollar value of purchases
        from and sales to those suppliers and customers,

      - [(C.1)]{.lawlabel} statements identifying, for each of those
        principal categories of products, the total annual volume or
        dollar value of purchases from and sales to all suppliers and
        customers, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the geographic regions of sales for its
        principal businesses and the principal businesses of its
        affiliates; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each party, and each of its
    affiliates referred to in subparagraph (c)(iii), all studies,
    surveys, analyses and reports that were prepared or received by an
    officer or director of the corporation --- or in the case of an
    unincorporated entity, an individual who serves in a similar
    capacity --- for the purpose of evaluating or analysing the proposed
    transaction with respect to market shares, competition, competitors,
    markets, potential for sales growth or expansion into new products
    or geographic regions and, if not otherwise set out in that
    document, the names and titles of the individuals who prepared the
    document and the date on which it was prepared.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Instead of being supplied with a report or financial
  statement referred to in clause (1)(c)(iv)(A), the Commissioner may be
  supplied with the address of an Internet site from which a copy of
  those documents can be obtained without charge, which is operational
  at the time the address is supplied and which remains operational
  prior to the expiry of the period referred to in subsection 123(1) of
  the Act.
