His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to subsection 55(2) of the
[Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005375 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Controlled Drugs and
    Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appropriate police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the RCMP, the Assistant
      Commissioner of the RCMP in charge of drug enforcement; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other police force, the member
      of the police force who is the most senior officer responsible for
      operations. ([agent de police compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a police force
    other than the RCMP, the senior police officer in charge of the
    police force. ([chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[particular investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[particular investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a primary
    investigation conducted under the Act or any other Act of Parliament
    and includes any investigation that arises from the primary
    investigation. (enquête particulière). ([enquête
    particulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[police force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[police force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police force that is
    designated pursuant to section 2. ([corps policier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a preliminary inquiry,
    trial or other proceeding under the Act or any other Act of
    Parliament. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provincial
    minister responsible for policing in a province. ([ministre
    provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-72, ss. 1, 16(F)

## [Revocation of Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certificate issued under section 8, 11 or 13 is revoked on the
  earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the appropriate police officer
    who issued the certificate revokes it,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the member to whom it was issued
    is no longer an active member of the police force,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the member to whom it was issued
    is no longer acting in the course of the member's responsibilities
    for the purposes of the particular investigation to which the
    certificate relates,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the particular investigation to
    which the certificate relates has been completed, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the certificate expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate police officer shall notify the
  member to whom a certificate was issued of the revocation on the day
  on which the certificate is revoked pursuant to paragraph (1)(a), (c)
  or (d).

## [Detention of Forfeited Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forfeited substances

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate officer shall, as soon as practicable but not
  later than 60 days after a forfeited controlled substance or precursor
  is no longer required for the proceeding in respect of which it was
  seized, where the controlled substance or precursor is required for
  the purposes of conducting investigations under the Act or any other
  Act of Parliament, inform the Minister in writing of that requirement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secure location

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every controlled substance or precursor referred to
  in subsection (1) shall be kept in a secure location while not being
  used for the purposes of conducting investigations under the Act or
  any other Act of Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate police officer of a police
  force is exempt from the application of section 5 of the Act if that
  person transfers any controlled substance or precursor referred to in
  subsection (1) to the chief or appropriate police officer of another
  police force and if the chief or appropriate police officer of that
  other police force requests the transfer for the purposes of a
  particular investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inform Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a transfer is conducted pursuant to subsection
  (3), the chief or appropriate police officer who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes the transfer shall inform the Minister of the
    transfer, as soon as practicable after the request for the transfer
    has been received; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} receives the controlled substance or precursor
    shall inform the Minister of its receipt, as soon as practicable
    after the receipt.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a controlled substance or precursor referred to in
  subsection (1) is no longer required for the purposes of conducting
  investigations under the Act or any other Act of Parliament, the chief
  or appropriate police officer shall seek the directions of the
  Minister and dispose of or otherwise deal with the controlled
  substance or precursor in accordance with the Minister\'s directions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substances not required

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a forfeited controlled substance or precursor is
  no longer required for the proceeding in respect of which it was
  seized and is not required for the purposes of conducting
  investigations under the Act or any other Act of Parliament, the chief
  or appropriate police officer shall, as soon as practicable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in writing seek directions from the Minister
    respecting the disposal of or otherwise dealing with the controlled
    substance or precursor, unless the Minister has previously given
    such directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dispose of or otherwise deal with the controlled
    substance or precursor in accordance with the Minister\'s
    directions.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005568 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate police officer shall submit to the Minister of
  Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness and to the Minister, within
  three months after the end of every calendar year, a report in written
  or electronic format containing the information set out in subsection
  (3), in respect of each of the following controlled substances or
  precursors that came into the possession of the police force in the
  course of a particular investigation completed during the calendar
  year, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a controlled substance or precursor imported or
    exported in accordance with section 11;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a controlled substance produced in accordance with
    section 13; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a forfeited controlled substance or precursor
    referred to in section 21.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate police officer of a police
  force other than the RCMP shall also send a copy of the report
  referred to in subsection (1) to the provincial minister responsible
  for the police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report shall indicate the name and total quantity
  of each controlled substance or precursor and the quantity, in respect
  of each controlled substance or precursor, that was forfeited,
  imported, exported, produced or destroyed, as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate police officer of a police
  force shall also submit, on request, a report in written or electronic
  format to the Minister respecting the controlled substances or
  precursors referred to in subsection (1) as required for the following
  purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the protection of the public against
    potential public health risks caused by the potential misuse or
    diversion of those substances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to collect data required for studies and research;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to meet international obligations of the Government
    of Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for compliance with these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of substances no longer in
possession

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate police officer shall submit a report in written
  or electronic format to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
  Preparedness and the Minister containing the information required by
  subsection (3), respecting every controlled substance or precursor
  referred to in subsection 21(1) that is lost, stolen or otherwise no
  longer in the possession of the police force, as soon as practicable
  after the substance is lost, stolen or no longer in the possession of
  the police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate police officer of a police
  force other than the RCMP shall also send a copy of the report
  referred to in subsection (1) to the provincial minister responsible
  for the police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report shall include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of each controlled substance
    or precursor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of forfeiture, importation or exportation
    of each controlled substance or precursor, or the production of each
    controlled substance, as applicable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which and an explanation of the
    circumstances in which the controlled substance or precursor was
    lost or stolen or ceased to be in the possession of the police
    force.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005597 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transitional

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
in respect of every controlled substance or precursor that was forfeited
to Her Majesty before the coming into force of these Regulations and
that is in the possession of a police force, except that, in respect of
subsection 21(1), the reference to 60 days shall be read as a reference
to 120 days after the coming into force of these Regulations.
