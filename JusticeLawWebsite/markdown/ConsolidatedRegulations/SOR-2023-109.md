## Special Economic Measures (Moldova) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-moldova-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-109

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2023-05-30

Special Economic Measures (Moldova) Regulations

P.C. 2023-475 2023-05-29
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
the Republic of Moldova constitutes a grave breach of international
peace and security that has resulted or is likely to result in a serious
international crisis;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, makes the annexed
[Special Economic Measures (Moldova)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-109) under subsections

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401521 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Moldova]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Moldova]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Republic of Moldova and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government, any of its departments and any
      government and department of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies and any agency of its
      political subdivisions. ([Moldova]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401529 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed person

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person whose name is
listed in the schedule is a person who is in Moldova, or is or was a
national of Moldova who does not ordinarily reside in Canada, and in
respect of whom the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the
Minister, is satisfied that there are reasonable grounds to believe is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person engaged in activities that directly or
  indirectly facilitate, support, provide funding for or contribute to a
  violation or attempted violation of the sovereignty or territorial
  integrity of Ukraine or Moldova or that obstruct the work of
  international organizations in Ukraine or Moldova;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who has participated in gross and systematic
  human rights violations in Moldova;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a current or former senior official of the Government
  of Moldova;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an associate of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (c);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a family member of a person referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (d) and (h);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an entity owned --- or held or controlled, directly
  or indirectly --- by a person referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
  (e);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an entity whose ownership or control was purported to
  be altered by a person who has violated or attempted to violate the
  sovereignty or territorial integrity of Ukraine or Moldova; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a senior official of an entity referred to in
  paragraph (f) or (g).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401540 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited dealings and activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, that is
  owned --- or that is held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by
  a listed person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any goods, wherever situated, to a
  listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services to or for
  the benefit of a listed person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply in
respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment made by or on behalf of a listed person
  that is due under a contract that the listed person entered into
  before they became a listed person, provided that the payment is not
  made to a listed person or to a person acting on behalf of a listed
  person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any transaction necessary for a Canadian to transfer
  to a non-listed person any accounts, funds or investments of a
  Canadian held by a listed person on the day on which that person
  became a listed person;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any dealings with a listed person required with
  respect to loan repayments made to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian outside Canada, for loans entered into with any person other
  than a listed person, and for enforcement and realization of security
  in respect of those loans or payments by guarantors guaranteeing those
  loans;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any dealings with a listed person required with
  respect to loan repayments made to any person in Canada, or any
  Canadian outside Canada, for loans entered into with that listed
  person before they became a listed person, and for enforcement and
  realization of security in respect of those loans or payments by
  guarantors guaranteeing those loans;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any benefit paid under the [Old Age Security
  Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or the
  Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan, CQLR, c. R-9, any
  superannuation, pension or benefit paid under or in respect of any
  retirement savings plan or under any retirement plan, any amount paid
  under or in respect of the [Garnishment, Attachment and Pension
  Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2) or the [Pension Benefits Division
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7) and any other payment made in respect of
  disability to any person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} financial services necessary in order for a listed
  person to obtain legal services in Canada with respect to the
  application of any of the prohibitions set out in these Regulations;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of any account at a
  financial institution held by a diplomatic mission, if the transaction
  is necessary in order for the mission to fulfill its diplomatic
  functions as set out in Article 3 of the Vienna Convention on
  Diplomatic Relations or, if the diplomatic mission has been
  temporarily or permanently recalled, when the transaction is necessary
  in order to maintain the mission premises;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any transaction with any international organization
  with diplomatic status, with any United Nations agency, with the
  International Red Cross and Red Crescent Movement or with any entity
  that has entered into a grant or contribution agreement with the
  Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a transaction by the Government of Canada that is
  provided for in any agreement or arrangement between Canada and
  Moldova.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assisting in prohibited activity

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do
anything that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to
cause, facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property that is owned --- or that is held or controlled,
directly or indirectly --- by a listed person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} banks regulated by the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  and, in respect of their business in Canada, [[authorized foreign
  banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and [caisses populaires]{.otherLang lang="fr"} regulated by a
  provincial Act and associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit
  Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any business described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the business involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity set
  out in section 6 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the Canadian
  Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned --- or that is
    held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by a listed person;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [Special Economic Measures
  Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5) and no civil proceedings lie against a person
  for a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401580 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Removal from list

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A listed
  person may apply to the Minister in writing to have their name removed
  from the schedule.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasonable grounds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of an application, the Minister must
  decide whether there are reasonable grounds to recommend the removal
  to the Governor in Council.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New application

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If there has been a
material change in circumstances since the last application was
submitted, a listed person may submit another application under section
8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of a listed
  person and who claims not to be that person may apply to the Minister
  in writing for a certificate stating that they are not that listed
  person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the day on which the Minister
  receives the application, the Minister must,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is established that the applicant is not the
    listed person, issue the certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not so established, provide notice to the
    applicant of the determination.

## [Application Before Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401595 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply according to their terms before they are
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1401598 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
