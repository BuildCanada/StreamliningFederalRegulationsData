## Passenger Automobile and Light Truck Greenhouse Gas Emission Regulations {#passenger-automobile-and-light-truck-greenhouse-gas-emission-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-201
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2010-09-23

Passenger Automobile and Light Truck Greenhouse Gas Emission Regulations

P.C. 2010-1162 2010-09-23
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EICBA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EFCBA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on April 17, 2010, a copy
of the proposed [Passenger Automobile and Light Truck Greenhouse Gas
Emission Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-201), substantially in
the annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments
with respect to the Regulations or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EICBA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2004, c. 15, s. 31
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EFCBA){#fn_b}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections
160 and 162 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Passenger Automobile and Light Truck Greenhouse Gas Emission
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-201).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent sale

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
these Regulations, a vehicle of a specific model year that is sold in
Canada is considered to be sold concurrently in Canada and in the United
States if a vehicle of that model year that belongs to the same test
group is offered for sale in the United States during the 365 days
preceding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vehicle that is imported into
  Canada, the day on which the vehicle is imported; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vehicle that is manufactured in
  Canada,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the national emissions mark is
    applied to the vehicle, if known, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the day referred to in subparagraph (i) is not
    known, the day on which the main assembly of the vehicle was
    completed.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-764463 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to reduce greenhouse gas emissions from passenger
automobiles and light trucks by establishing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} emission standards and test procedures that are
  aligned with the federal requirements of the United States; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requirements that, beginning in model year 2026, will
  incrementally lead to all new passenger automobiles and light trucks
  being zero-emission vehicles as of model year 2035.

## [Background]{.HTitleText1} {#h-764466 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Background

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations set out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prescribed classes of vehicles for the purposes of
  section 149 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting the conformity of passenger
  automobiles and light trucks with greenhouse gas emission standards
  for the purposes of section 153 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting the conformity of fleets of
  passenger automobiles and light trucks with greenhouse gas emission
  standards and other requirements for carrying out the purposes of
  Division 5 of Part 7 of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a credit system for the purposes of section 162 of
  the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} requirements respecting the conformity of [[combined
  fleets]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 30.1(1), with
  requirements for zero-emission vehicles; and

#### [Calculation of Fleet Average CO~2~ Equivalent Emission Values]{.HTitleText3} {#h-764663 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission
value

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A company must calculate
the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission value for its fleet of
passenger automobiles and its fleet of light trucks of the 2011 model
year and subsequent model years in accordance with the following
formula:

::: {#764666 .Section}
D -- E -- F -- G -- H

where

[D]{.dfn}
:   is the fleet average carbon-related exhaust emission value for each
    fleet, calculated in accordance with subsections 18.1(1) and (2),
    taking into account subsections 18.1(6) and (7);

[E]{.dfn}
:   is the allowance for the reduction of air conditioning refrigerant
    leakage, calculated in accordance with subsection 18.2(1);

[F]{.dfn}
:   is the allowance for the improvement of air conditioning system
    efficiency, calculated in accordance with subsection 18.2(2);

[G]{.dfn}
:   is the allowance for the use of innovative technologies that result
    in a measurable CO~2~ emission reduction, which corresponds to the
    sum of the allowances calculated in accordance with
    subsections 18.3(1), (3) or (3.1), and (5); and

[H]{.dfn}
:   is the CO~2~ allowance for full-size pick-up trucks, calculated in
    accordance with subsection 18.4(1).

#### [CO~2~ Equivalent Emission Credit System]{.HTitleText3} {#h-764822 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}CO~2~ equivalent emission credits

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subparagraph 162(1)(b)(i) of the Act, a company
  obtains CO~2~ equivalent emission credits if the fleet average CO~2~
  equivalent emission value in respect of a fleet of passenger
  automobiles or a fleet of light trucks of a specific model year is
  lower than the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission standard for
  that fleet and model year and the company reports the credits in its
  end of model year report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company incurs deficits if the fleet average CO~2~
  equivalent emission value in respect of a fleet of passenger
  automobiles or a fleet of light trucks of a specific model year is
  higher than the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission standard for
  that fleet and model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3.1) to (3.4), a company must
  calculate, using the following formula, the credits or deficits for
  each of its fleets:

  ::: {#764830 .Subsection}
  ECD = ((A -- B) × C × D) ÷ 1,000,000

  where

  [ECD]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of credits, if the result is positive, or the number
      of deficits, if the result is negative, expressed in megagrams of
      CO~2~ equivalent;

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission standard calculated
      in accordance with section 16 or 17, as the case may be, expressed
      in grams per mile;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission value calculated in
      accordance with section 18, expressed in grams per mile;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passenger automobiles or light trucks in
      the fleet; and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the assumed total mileage of the vehicles in question, namely,
  :::

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 195,264 miles for a fleet of passenger automobiles,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 225,865 miles for a fleet of light trucks.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative standard --- nitrous oxide

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} For each test group in respect of which a company
  uses, for any given model year, an alternative standard for nitrous
  oxide (N~2~O) under subsection 10(1) or 12(1), the company must use
  the following formula, expressing the result in megagrams of CO~2~
  equivalent, and add the sum of the results for each test group to the
  number of credits or deficits calculated in accordance with
  subsection (3) for the fleet to which the test group belongs:

  ::: {#764841 .Subsection}
  (298 × A × (B -- C) × D) ÷ 1,000,000

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of passenger automobiles or light trucks in
      the test group;

  [B]{.dfn}

      part 86, subpart S, of the CFR, for the applicable model year,
      expressed in grams per mile;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the alternative exhaust emission standard for nitrous oxide
      (N~2~O) under subsection 10(1) or 12(1) to which the company
      certifies the test group, expressed in grams per mile; and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the assumed total mileage of the vehicles in question, namely,
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 195,264 miles for a fleet of passenger

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 225,865 miles for a fleet of light trucks.
  :::

- **[[30.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this section, in sections 30.11 to 30.21 and in subsections 33(4.1) to
  (5).

  [[combined fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[combined fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all automobiles of a
      specific model year that a company manufactures in Canada or
      imports into Canada for the purpose of sale of those automobiles
      to the first retail purchaser. ([parc combiné]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      149 of the Act. ([entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ZEV requirement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ZEV requirement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the minimum
      performance required with respect to zero-emission vehicles of a
      company's combined fleet for a given model year, as set out in
      section 30.12. ([exigence VZE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ZEV value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ZEV value]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the actual performance
      with respect to zero-emission vehicles of a company's combined
      fleet for a given model year, calculated in accordance with
      section 30.13(1). ([valeur VZE]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- emergency vehicles and
  fire fighting vehicles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite the definition [[combined
  fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1), a company may, for
  the purposes of sections 30.11 to 30.21, elect to exclude emergency
  vehicles and fire fighting vehicles from its combined fleet of any
  model year, if it reports that election in its end of model year
  report for that model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- automobile being exported

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The definition [[combined
  fleet]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1) does not include any
  automobile that is being exported and that is accompanied by written
  evidence establishing that it will not be sold or used in Canada.

- **[[30.13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A company must calculate, for the
  2026 model year and subsequent model years, the ZEV value of its
  combined fleet using the following formula:

  ::: {#1432555 .Subsection}
  (A ÷ B) + C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of electric vehicles and fuel cell vehicles in
      the combined fleet;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of automobiles in the combined fleet; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is, with respect to plug-in hybrid electric vehicles, the lesser
      of the following results:
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contribution of these plug-in hybrid
        electric vehicles to the actual performance of a company's
        combined fleet with respect to zero-emission vehicles,
        calculated using the following formula:

        ::: {#1432561 .NestedFormula}
        (0.15 × A + 0.75 × B + C + D + 0.75 × E + F + 0.75 × G + H) ÷ I

        where

        [A]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2026 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet with an
            all-electric driving range of at least 35 km and not more
            than 49 km,

        [B]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2026 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet equipped with
            less than seven seats with an all-electric driving range of
            at least 50 km and not more than 64 km,

        [C]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2026 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet equipped with
            seven seats or more with an all-electric driving range of at
            least 50 km and not more than 64 km,

        [D]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2026 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet with an
            all-electric driving range of at least 65 km,

        [E]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2027 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet equipped with
            less than seven seats with an all-electric driving range of
            at least 50 km and not more than 79 km,

        [F]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2027 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet equipped with
            seven seats or more with an all-electric driving range of at
            least 50 km and not more than 79 km,

        [G]{.dfn}
        :   is, for the 2028 model year, the total number of plug-in
            hybrid electric vehicles in the combined fleet with an
            all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not more

        :   is the total number of automobiles in the combined fleet,

#### [Compliance Unit or Deficit System]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1432589 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obtaining units

- **[[30.14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As of the 2026 model year, a company
  obtains compliance units in respect of its combined fleet if its ZEV
  value is greater than the ZEV requirement for the model year in
  question and the company reports the compliance units in its end of
  model year report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} As of the 2026 model year, a company incurs a deficit
  in respect of its combined fleet if its ZEV value is less than the ZEV
  requirement for the model year in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must calculate the compliance units or
  deficit for its combined fleet of a given model year using the
  following formula:

  ::: {#1432597 .Subsection}
  (A − B) × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the ZEV value of its combined fleet of the model year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the ZEV requirement for the model year; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of automobiles in the combined fleet.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of units or deficit

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A company obtains compliance units or incurs a
  deficit in respect of its combined fleet on the day on which the end
  of model year report for the model year in question is submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of units --- time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Compliance units obtained for a combined fleet of any
  of the 2026 to 2034 model years may be used to offset a deficit
  incurred in respect of any combined fleet of up to three model years
  before the model year for which those units were obtained and, at the
  latest, of the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fifth model year after the model year for which
    the units were obtained; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the 2034 model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of validity

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Compliance units are no longer valid after the day on
  which the company submits its end of model year report for the 2035
  model year.

#### [Offsetting Deficits and Use of Units]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1432612 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deficits

- **[[30.15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.15
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a company
  must use compliance units obtained in respect of its combined fleet of
  a given model year to offset a deficit incurred in respect of its
  combined fleet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remaining compliance units

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company may bank all or some of any remaining
  compliance units to offset a future deficit or transfer all or some of
  them to another company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offset

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company may offset a deficit with,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deficit incurred in respect of the
    2026 and 2027 model years, a number of units equal to the deficit,
    consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} compliance units obtained in accordance with
      section 30.14, early compliance units obtained in accordance with
      section 30.16 or charging station units created in accordance with
      section 30.21,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} compliance units or charging station units that
      were transferred to it by another company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a combination of any of these units;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deficit incurred in respect of the
    2028 to 2030 model years, a number of units equal to the deficit,
    consisting of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} compliance units obtained in accordance with
      section 30.14 or charging station units created in accordance with
      section 30.21,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} compliance units or charging station units that
      were transferred to it by another company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a combination of any of these units;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deficit incurred in respect of the
    2031 to 2034 model years, a number of compliance units equal to the
    deficit, consisting of compliance units obtained in accordance with
    section 30.14 or that were transferred to it by another company, or
    a combination of any of these units.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offsetting deficits --- sum of early
  compliance units and charging station units

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The sum of early compliance units and charging
  station units, including those charging station units that were
  transferred to it by another company, that a company uses to offset a
  deficit incurred in respect of the combined fleet of any of the 2026
  to 2030 model years may not exceed, for the model year in question,
  the amount calculated using the following formula:

  ::: {#1432631 .Subsection}
  0.1 × A × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the ZEV requirement for the model year in question; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total number of automobiles in the combined fleet.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offsetting deficits 2026 to 2034 model
  years --- time limit

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any deficit incurred in respect of a combined fleet
  for a given model year must be offset no later than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deficit in respect of the 2026 to
    2031 model years, the day on which the company submits the end of
    model year report of the third model year after the model year for
    which the company incurred the deficit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a deficit in respect of the 2032 to
    2034 model years, the day on which the company submits the end of
    model year report for the 2035 model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No offset --- 2035 model years and
  subsequent model years

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} No deficits incurred in respect of a combined fleet
  for the 2035 model year and subsequent model years can be offset.

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the project targets the installation of charging

    restricting the power output, at all times and at the same price by

    at least five years,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} no financial contribution or other benefit will
    be granted with respect to the project from a government program,
    and

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} no compliance credits, referred to in sections
    102 and 103 of the [Clean Fuel
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-140), will be created with

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Creation of charging station units

- **[[30.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A company may create charging
  station units with respect to any registered charging station project
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all charging stations became operational no earlier
    than on January 1, 2024 and no later than two years after the date
    of registration of the project, but the date must not be later than
    December 31, 2027; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company reports those units in its end of model
    year report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of units --- one investor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a company is the only investor in a registered
  project, the company must calculate the number of charging station
  units it will create using the following formula:

        amounts for each charging station, in accordance with the table
        below; and

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of units --- multiple investors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a company invests in the registered project with
  other companies or third parties, that company must calculate the
  number of charging station units it will create using the following
  formula:

  ::: {#1432741 .Subsection}
  (A ÷ B) × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the lesser of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total investment amount in the project, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total of the maximum allowable investment
        amounts for each charging station, in accordance with the table
        in subsection (2);

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is \$20,000; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the proportion of the company's investment in the project, as
      compared to the total investment in the project, expressed as a
      percentage of the total investment.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of units

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A company creates charging station units with respect
  to a registered project on the day on which the end of model year
  report for the model year in question is submitted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Banking or transfer

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A company may bank all or some of the charging
  station units created with respect to the registered project and
  transfer all or some of them to another company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of charging station units

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must cancel

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each charging station of the
    registered project that does not comply with any of subparagraph
    30.18(f)(ii) to (vi), those charging station units created with
    respect to the station; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the cancellation of the registration
    of a project under section 30.20, all units created with respect to
    that project.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}End of validity

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Charging station units are no longer valid after the
  day on which the company submits its end of model year report for the
  2030 model year.

  also contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of time, in days and hours, during which
    each charging station is non-operational during the calendar year
    that corresponds to the year of the end of model year reportand the
    percentage of that year which that period represented, as well as
    the reason for any service interruptions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total number of kWh that the charging stations
    provided over the course of the calendar year that corresponds to
    the end of model year report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- compliance
  units and charging station units

  [(4.3)]{.lawlabel} The end of model year report must also contain the
  following information with respect to any compliance units and
  charging station units transferred to or from the company since the
  submission of the previous end of model year report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, street address and, if different, the
    mailing address of the company that transferred the units and the
    model year in respect of which that company obtained or created
    those units;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, street address and, if different, the
    mailing address of the company that received those units and the
    model year in respect of which that company obtained or created
    those units; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of units transferred and the date of the
    transfer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- zero-emission vehicles

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The end of model year report for the model year 2026
  and subsequent model years must also contain the following information
  in respect of the company's combined fleet:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a statement that the company has
    elected to exclude emergency vehicles and fire fighting vehicles
    from its combined fleet;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total number of automobiles;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of electric vehicles and fuel cell
    vehicles;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of plug-in hybrid electric
    vehicles, broken down according to their all-electric driving range,
    calculated in accordance with subsection 30.13(2), as follows:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the 2026 model year,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those with at least 35 km and not more than
        49 km,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} those equipped with less than seven seats and
        with an all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not
        more than 64 km,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} those equipped with seven seats or more and
        with an all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not
        more than 64 km, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} those with at least 65 km,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the 2027 model year,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} those equipped with less than seven seats and
        with an all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not
        more than 79 km,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} those equipped with seven seats or more and
        with an all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not
        more than 79 km, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} those with an all-electric driving range of at
        least 80 km,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for the 2028 model year, those with an
      all-electric driving range of at least 50 km and not more than
      79 km, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for the 2028 and subsequent model years, those
      with an all-electric driving range of at least 80 km;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the ZEV value calculated in accordance with section
    30.13 and the actual charge-depleting range, used in the calculation
    referred to in subsection 30.13(2), for each plug-in hybrid electric
    vehicle;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the total number of compliance units or the deficit
    calculated in accordance with subsection 30.14(3);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the total number of compliance units used to offset
    the deficit incurred in respect of the combined fleet for the model
    year in question or a previous deficit incurred in respect of the
    combined fleet and, if applicable, the information referred to in
    subsection (4.3) that applies to those units;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the number of early compliance units that were used
    to offset the deficit incurred in respect of the combined fleet for
    the model year in question or a previous deficit incurred in respect
    of the combined fleet and the model year with respect to which the
    units were obtained;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of charging station units that were used
    to offset the deficit incurred in respect of the combined fleet for
    the model year in question or a previous deficit incurred in respect
    of the combined fleet and, if applicable, the information referred
    to in subsection (4.3) that applies to those units; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an accounting, for each model year, of the
    compliance units, early compliance units, charging station units and
    deficits.

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765181 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- subsection 14(1) or
paragraph 14(1.1)(d)

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 14(1) or paragraph 14(1.1)(d), a company
  must submit a declaration to the Minister, signed by a person who is
  authorized to act on behalf of the company, no later than May 1 of the
  calendar year that corresponds to the model year in respect of which
  the company does not wish to be subject to sections 13 and 17 to 20,
  and must specify in the declaration the total number of passenger
  automobiles and light trucks manufactured or imported for sale in
  Canada for each model year in question.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Declaration --- paragraphs 14(1.1)(a)
  to (c)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs 14(1.1)(a) to (c), a
  company must submit a declaration to the Minister, signed by a person
  who is authorized to act on behalf of the company, no later than May 1
  of the calendar year that corresponds to two, three or four model
  years, as the case may be, following the first model year for which
  the company manufactures or imports passenger automobiles or light
  trucks for sale in Canada, and must specify in the declaration the
  total number of passenger automobiles and light trucks manufactured or
  imported for sale in Canada for the model year in question.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765189 .Part}

### [Evidence of Conformity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765190 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence of conformity

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a vehicle that is covered by an EPA certificate and that is
  either sold concurrently in Canada and the United States or has a
  national emissions mark applied to it, evidence of conformity for the
  purpose of paragraph 153(1)(b) of the Act in respect of a company must
  consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the EPA certificate covering the vehicle;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one or both of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a document demonstrating that the vehicles
      covered by the EPA certificate are sold concurrently in Canada and
      the United States,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a document demonstrating that the vehicle
      covered by the EPA certificate bears the national emissions mark;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the records submitted to the EPA in
    support of the application for the EPA certificate in respect of the
    vehicle and any application for an amendment to that EPA certificate
    and any records submitted to the EPA to maintain that EPA
    certificate; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a U.S. emission control information label that is
    permanently affixed to the vehicle in the form and location set out
    in section 1807 of Title 40, chapter I, subchapter C, part 86,
    subpart S, of the CFR, for the applicable model year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}U.S. control information label

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the U.S. emission
  control information label may be permanently affixed to the vehicle in
  any other form and location that may be specified in Title 40,
  chapter I, subchapter C, part 86, of the CFR.

- SOR/2015-186, s. 58

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vehicles not sold in the United States

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For

  and produce evidence of conformity for a vehicle other than one
  referred to in subsection 36(1) in a form and manner satisfactory to
  the Minister, instead of as specified in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time of submission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a company must submit the
  evidence of conformity to the Minister before importing a vehicle or
  applying a national emissions mark to it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 153(2) of the Act

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, a
company that imports a vehicle or applies a national emissions mark to
it under subsection 153(2) of the Act is not required to provide the
Minister with the evidence of conformity referred to in subsection 37(1)
before importing it or applying a national emissions mark to it, but
must provide that evidence in accordance with subsection 153(2) of the
Act before the vehicle leaves the possession or control of the company
and before it is presented for registration under the laws of a province
or an aboriginal government.

### [Fleet Average Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765211 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- fleets

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must maintain records containing the following information for
  each of its fleets:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the model year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fleet average CO~2~ equivalent
    emission standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fleet average CO~2~ equivalent emission value;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the values and data used in calculating the fleet
    average CO~2~ equivalent emission value, including information
    relating to the calculation of allowances;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a copy of the clarifications or
    additional information referred to in subsection 19(1); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the values used to calculate the CO~2~ emission
    credits, the credits obtained in respect of a temporary optional
    fleet and the early action credits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records --- vehicles

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company must maintain records containing the
  following information for each vehicle in the fleet referred to in
  subsection (1):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the model type and model year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicable fleet average CO~2~ equivalent
    emission standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vehicle covered by an EPA
    certificate, the applicable test group identified in the application
    for the EPA certificate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and street address of the plant where the
    vehicle was assembled;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle identification number;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the applicable carbon-related exhaust emission
    value and the values and data used in calculating that value;

  - [(f.1)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a full-size pick-up truck in
    respect of which an allowance is calculated in accordance with
    subsection 18.4(1) for the use of a mild or strong hybrid electric
    technology, a description of the hybrid electric technology; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and street or mailing address of the first
    purchaser of the vehicle in Canada.

### [Maintenance and Submission of Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-765233 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance of records

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must maintain, for vehicles of each model year, in writing or
  in a readily readable electronic or optical form,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a period of at least eight years after the end
    of the calendar year that corresponds to the model year in question,
    a copy of the reports referred to in sections 31 to 33;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period of at least eight years after the date
    on which the main assembly of the vehicle was completed, the
    evidence of conformity referred to in section 36; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a period of at least eight years after the end
    of the calendar year that corresponds to the model year in question,
    the records referred to in section 39.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records maintained on behalf of a
  company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the evidence of conformity, the records and the
  copy of reports referred to in subsection (1) are maintained on behalf
  of a company, the company must keep a record of the name and street
  address and, if different, the mailing address of the person who
  maintains those records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written request for records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister makes a written request for the
  evidence of conformity or the records referred to in subsections (1)
  and (2), or a summary of any of them, the company must provide the
  Minister with the requested information, in either official language,
  within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 40 days after the request is delivered to the
    company; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the evidence of conformity or records referred
    to in section 36 or 37 must be translated from a language other than
    French or English, 60 days after the request is delivered to the
    company.

## [Information Regarding Suspension or Revocation of EPA Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765246 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be submitted

**[[40.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40.1 .sectionLabel}** If an EPA certificate
referred to in section 12 is suspended or revoked, the company must
submit the following information to the Minister within 60 days after
the day on which the certificate is suspended or revoked:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its name, address and telephone number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the EPA certificate that was suspended or
  revoked;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the EPA decision to suspend or revoke the
  certificate; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the make, model and model year of the vehicles that
  are covered by the EPA certificate.

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The annual rental rate to

prorated on a daily basis for each day that a vehicle is made available,

## [Application for Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A company applying under
section 156 of the Act for an exemption from conformity with any
standard specified under these Regulations must submit in writing to the
Minister the information set out in section 44 of the On-Road Vehicle
and Engine Emission Regulations.

## [Defect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765264 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of defect

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  notice of defect referred to in subsections 157(1) and (4) of the Act
  must be given in writing and must contain the information set out in
  subsection 45(1) of the On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In respect of a notice of defect issued under these
  Regulations, a company must comply with subsections 45(1.1) to (3) of
  the On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standard

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the application of section 157 of the Act, the
  prescribed standard that applies to a vehicle is the product of 1.1
  multiplied by the carbon-related exhaust emission value for the model
  type in question, calculated in accordance with subsection 18.1(2), or
  by the equivalent value in the case of a model type of the 2011 model
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standard ---
  subconfiguration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3), if the
  carbon-related exhaust emission value for a subconfiguration of the
  model type in question was used in the calculation under subsection
  18.1(2), the prescribed standard that applies to a vehicle is the
  product of 1.1 multiplied by the carbon-related exhaust emission value
  for that subconfiguration.
