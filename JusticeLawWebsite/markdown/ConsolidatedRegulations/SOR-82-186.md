## Potato Production and Sale (Central Saanich) Restriction Regulations {#potato-production-and-sale-central-saanich-restriction-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-186
:::

[PLANT PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-14.8)

Registration 1982-01-29

Regulations Providing for the Restricting of Property Located in the
Municipality of Central Saanich in the Province of British Columbia that
is Infested with the Golden Nematode from the Production of Potatoes and
Prohibiting the Sale of Potatoes Produced Therein that Are Infested with
the Golden Nematode

P.C. 1982-295 1982-01-28
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 4 of the Plant
Quarantine Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
providing for the restricting of property located in the municipality of
Central Saanich in the Province of British Columbia that is infested
with the golden nematode from the production of potatoes and prohibiting
the sale of potatoes produced therein that are infested with the golden
nematode](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-186).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873080 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Potato Production and Sale (Central Saanich) Restriction
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-186).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873082 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[sale]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes offering or exposing for sale,
displaying or advertising for sale, having in possession for sale,
bartering, exchanging, disposing for consideration and distributing.

## [Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873084 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} seed, plant, grow, produce or harvest any potatoes,
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sell or dispose of any potatoes seeded, planted,
    grown, produced or harvested

  in that portion of the Municipality of Central Saanich in the Province
  of British Columbia east of the West Saanich Road.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/83-294, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to any person who
  seeds, plants, grows, produces or harvests potatoes for his own
  consumption if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the potatoes are consumed within the limits of the
    property on which they were harvested; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property on which the potatoes were seeded,
    planted, grown, produced or harvested was not used, at any time
    prior to the coming into force of these Regulations, for the
    production of potatoes for sale.
