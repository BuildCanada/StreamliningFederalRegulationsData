## Teleglobe Canada Pension Protection Regulations {#teleglobe-canada-pension-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-379
:::

[TELEGLOBE CANADA REORGANIZATION AND DIVESTITURE ACT](/eng/acts/T-4.2)

Registration 1987-06-25

Regulations Respecting Pension Protection for Employees of Teleglobe
Canada

P.C. 1987-1270 1987-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of State (Privatization) and the Treasury Board, pursuant
to section 25 of the [Teleglobe Canada Reorganization and Divestiture
Act](/eng/acts/T-4.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting pension protection
for employees of Teleglobe Canada](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-379).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905635 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Teleglobe Canada Pension Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-379).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905637 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Teleglobe Canada
    Reorganization and Divestiture Act](/eng/acts/T-4.2);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the new corporation as
    defined in the Act. ([nouvelle société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905641 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All the provisions of the
[Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Supplementary
Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the regulations made under
those Acts as amended apply to persons referred to in paragraph 25(1)(d)
of the Act except to the extent that those provisions are adapted in
accordance with sections 4 and 5.

## [Adapted Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905643 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 25 of the Act, subparagraphs 9(7)(b)(i) and (ii) and sections 11
and 12 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) are
adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 9(7)(b)(i) and (ii), the year in
  which a person ceased to be employed in the Public Service shall be
  considered to be the year in which the person ceased to be employed by
  the new corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in sections 11 and 12,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} except in subsection 11(4), the number of years of
    pensionable service and any periods of employment in respect of the
    person shall be considered to include any such service or employment
    with the new corporation during the period beginning on the day the
    person ceases to be employed in the Public Service and ending on the
    day immediately preceding the day on which the person ceases to be
    employed by the new corporation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} except for the purposes of subparagraph (i) the
    day on which a person ceases to be employed in the Public Service
    shall be considered to be the day on which the person ceases to be
    employed by the new corporation.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 25 of the Act, subsection 4(3) and subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and
(ii) of the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) are
adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 4(3), the retirement year or retirement
  month of a person shall be considered to be 1987 or April 1987, as the
  case may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and (ii), the year in
  which a person ceased to contribute in respect of current service to
  the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account shall be considered to
  be the year in which the person ceased to be employed by the new
  corporation.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply on
and after April 4, 1987.
