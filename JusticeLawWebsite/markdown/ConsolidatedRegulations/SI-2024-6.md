[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[WTO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the World Trade Organization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to the
WTO of the tax paid by the WTO to Canada under the [Income Tax
Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) during the period beginning on January 1, 2000 and
ending on December 31, 2011 in respect of investments made in connection
with the WTO's pension plan for its employees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest and penalties

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of
interest and penalties paid in respect of any amount for which remission
is granted under section 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission of an amount
under section 2 or 3 is granted only to the extent to which the amount
has not otherwise been rebated, remitted, credited or refunded to any
person under the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or any
other Act of Parliament and on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for the remission is filed in writing with
  the Minister of National Revenue not later than two years after the
  day on which this Order is made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if any amount claimed is in respect of tax that has
  been paid by the WTO, the WTO provides the Minister of National
  Revenue with evidence or information that demonstrates that it has
  paid that tax.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
