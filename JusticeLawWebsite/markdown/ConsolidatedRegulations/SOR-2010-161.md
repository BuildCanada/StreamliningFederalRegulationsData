## Regulations Prescribing Certain Offences to be Serious Offences {#regulations-prescribing-certain-offences-to-be-serious-offences .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-161

Regulations Prescribing Certain Offences to be Serious Offences

P.C. 2010-932 2010-07-13

the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 467.1(4)[[Footnote

hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Prescribing Certain Offences to be

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following offences
under the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) are serious offences that are
included in the definition [[serious offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 467.1(1) of that Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keeping a common gaming or betting house (subsection
  201(1) and paragraph 201(2)(b));

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} betting, pool-selling and book-making (section 202);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} committing offences in relation to lotteries and
  games of chance (section 206);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} cheating while playing a game or in holding the
  stakes for a game or in betting (section 209); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} keeping a common bawdy-house (subsection 210(1) and
  paragraph 210(2)(c)).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following offences
under the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8) are
serious offences that are included in the definition [[serious
offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 467.1(1) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} trafficking in any substance included in Schedule IV
  (paragraph 5(3)(c));

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} trafficking in any substance included in Schedule II
  in an amount that does not exceed the amount set out for that
  substance in Schedule VII (subsection 5(4));

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} importing or exporting any substance included in
  Schedule IV or V (paragraph 6(3)(c)); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} producing any substance included in Schedule IV
  (paragraph 7(2)(d)).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-760044 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
