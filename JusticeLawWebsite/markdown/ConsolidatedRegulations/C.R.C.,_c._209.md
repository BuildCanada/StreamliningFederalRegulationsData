## Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Pricing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-greenhouse-vegetable-pricing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 209
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Pricing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Greenhouse Vegetables Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536571 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Pricing (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536573 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appointed shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a shipper in
    possession of a valid and subsisting certificate of appointment
    issued by the Commodity Board pursuant to the Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Appointed Shippers' Procedures(Interprovincial and Export)
    Regulations; ([expéditeur désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Producers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tomatoes,
    cucumbers and lettuce produced in the Province of Ontario in a
    greenhouse or any other enclosure under glass, plastic or other
    material used for the purpose of controlling temperature and
    providing protection for growing plants; ([légumes de
    serre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of greenhouse vegetables; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any quantity of greenhouse
    vegetables; ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who offers for
    sale, sells, receives, assembles, packs, ships for sale or
    transports a product, but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer or an appointed shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports the product by motor
      vehicle as agent of the producer. ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536584 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of a product in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536586 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer who sells a
product directly to a consumer or a consumer who buys a product directly
from a producer is, in respect of that product, exempt from these
Regulations.

## [Minimum Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536588 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or offer to sell a product, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} buy a product

at less than the minimum price as determined from time to time by the
Commodity Board.

## [Conditions of Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536593 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell, offer
to sell or buy, either as principal or agent, any product

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on commission or consignment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in combination with any other commodity at a combined
  price or at a price not applicable to the current prices of the
  commodities individually; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} contemporaneously with or conditional upon

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any gift or sale of anything of value, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any rebate or refund.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No shipper shall, on behalf
of a producer, pay for or take into account in any transaction the cost
of transportation of the product other than the transportation of the
product from the premises of the producer to the premises of the
shipper.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536601 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper shall, within
21 days after receipt by him of a product from a producer, pay to the
producer not less than the full amount of the minimum price determined
by the Commodity Board for the product shipped by him, less only the
following deductions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the service charge imposed by the Commodity Board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration of the appointed shipper as fixed
  from time to time by the Commodity Board;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the product is delivered in bulk for central
  packing by the shipper before shipping, the charges made by the
  shipper for central packing;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any claim for compensation made under
  the Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Information(Interprovincial and
  Export) Regulations received by the shipper if the claim is
  substantiated by the claimant and approved in writing by the Commodity
  Board; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any amount then owing to the shipper by the producer
  for supplies purchased by the producer from him.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No shipper shall require
any producer to deliver a receipt or other similar document in respect
of a product received from that producer before he pays the producer as
required by section 8.

## [Brokerage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536609 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall pay brokerage fees on a product to any produce broker in
  excess of the brokerage rates prevailing in the province in which the
  product is sold.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall pay brokerage fees in the Province of
  Ontario or Quebec to any person other than a licensed produce broker.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall pay brokerage fees in the Province of
  Newfoundland, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island,
  Manitoba, Saskatchewan or Alberta to any person other than a licensed
  produce broker or a central purchasing agent in a province who serves
  more than five wholesale outlets in that province.
