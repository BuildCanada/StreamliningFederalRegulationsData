## Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Sections 131, 132, 134, 136 and 137 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1 {#order-fixing-the-day-for-the-purposes-of-sections-131-132-134-136-and-137-of-the-economic-action-plan-2014-act-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-14
:::

[ECONOMIC ACTION PLAN 2014 ACT, NO. 1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)

Registration 2015-01-30

Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Sections 131, 132, 134, 136 and
137 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1

P.C. 2015-37 2015-01-29
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Health, pursuant to sections 131, 132, 134, 136 and 137
of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDBAA}, makes the annexed [Order
Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Sections 131, 132, 134, 136 and 137
of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No.
1](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-14).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EDBAA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2014, c. 20
  :::
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day for purposes of certain sections

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The day for the purposes of
sections 131, 132, 134, 136 and 137 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014
Act, No. 1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_8656_hq_19931){#fn_8656_hq_19931-ID0EBCA} is
fixed as September 1, 2018.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817177 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind6022_hq_15211){#fn_Ind6022_hq_15211-ID0EBDA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the first day on which
both sections 114 and 115 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No.
1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_Ind6022_hq_15212){#fn_Ind6022_hq_15212-ID0EBCA}
are in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
