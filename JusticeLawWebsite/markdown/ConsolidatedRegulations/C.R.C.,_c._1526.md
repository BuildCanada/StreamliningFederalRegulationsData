## Territorial Lands Act Exclusion Order {#territorial-lands-act-exclusion-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1526
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Order Respecting the Acquisition of Interests in Territorial Lands by
Officers or Employees of or under the Government of Canada
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527279 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Territorial Lands Act Exclusion Order.

## [Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527281 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an officer or employee of or under the Government
  of Canada may be a shareholder in a corporation that purchases,
  acquires or holds territorial land or any interest therein where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the shares held by the officer or employee are of a
    class of shares of the capital stock of the corporation that are
    listed on a stock exchange in Canada that is prescribed for the
    purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sole interest of the officer or employee in the
    corporation is as holder of shares of a class described in paragraph
    (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) shall be construed as
  authorizing an officer or employee of or under the Government of
  Canada to hold an interest as a shareholder in a corporation where
  that interest

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} would enable him to derive any direct or indirect
    benefit or interest from any government contracts over which he can
    influence decisions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would create a conflict with his public duties.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527289 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies to
shares described in subsection 2(1) acquired by an officer or employee
of or under the Government of Canada either before or after August 13,
1975.
