## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732592 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Marine Technical Review
    Board established under section 26 of the Act.
    ([Bureau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Review]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732596 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a vessel that is detained under section
  222 of the Act may, within 30 days after the day on which notice of
  the detention order was served, apply to the Board's National
  Vice-Chair for a review of the order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The National Vice-Chair shall assign the review to a
  Vice-Chair of the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Vice-Chair may confirm, rescind or vary the
  detention order and shall notify the authorized representative of the
  Vice-Chair's decision.

## [Reconsideration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732601 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  30 days after the day on which the authorized representative receives
  notification of the decision made under subsection 2(3), the
  authorized representative may apply to the Board's Chair for a
  reconsideration of the decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chair may confirm, rescind or vary the decision
  and shall notify the authorized representative of the Chair's
  decision.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732605 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fnstar_e){#fnstar_e-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15),
chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes into force.
