## B.C. Tree Fruit Export Regulations {#b.c.-tree-fruit-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-677
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1981-08-31

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Export Trade of Tree Fruit
Produced in British Columbia
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The British Columbia Tree Fruit Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3
of the [British Columbia Tree Fruit
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-818)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA}, hereby revokes the
British Columbia Tree Fruit Export Regulations, C.R.C., c. 146, and
makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the marketing in export trade
of tree fruit produced in British
Columbia](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-677).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/79-818, 1979 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4225
  :::

Dated at Kelowna, British Columbia, this 25th day of August, 1981
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871762 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [B.C. Tree Fruit Export
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-677).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871764 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means B.C. Tree Fruits Limited, a
    company incorporated under the laws of the Province and having its
    chief place of business at the City of Kelowna in the Province;
    ([organisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Tree Fruit
    Marketing Board; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes buying, selling,
    disposing of, offering for sale or other disposition, and the
    transportation in any manner by any person of the regulated product
    for marketing; ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a body corporate;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Province]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Province of British
    Columbia; ([province]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any tree fruit grown
    within the Province; ([produit réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any motor vehicle, wagon,
    railway car, ship, boat or other thing in which the regulated
    product can be transported. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871773 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the marketing of the regulated product in export trade and to persons
and property situated within the Province.

## [Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871775 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency, having been designated by the Board as the agency through
  which all regulated products shall be marketed locally within the
  Province, is hereby authorized as the sole agency through which all
  regulated products shall be marketed in export trade.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the Board may, on
  application from any person, designate that person as an agent to
  market any variety, grade, size or quantity of the regulated product
  in export trade.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person other than the Agency or a person
  designated by the Board under subsection (2) shall market any
  regulated product in export trade.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871780 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person engaged in
producing, packing, transporting, storing or marketing the regulated
product in or for the purpose of export trade shall furnish to the
Board, upon demand by any member or employee of the Board or any other
person authorized by the Board, full and correct information relating to
such production, packing, transporting, storing or marketing, and shall,
at any reasonable time upon request, permit the Board or any member
thereof or any officer or inspector of the Board or any other person
authorized by the Board to inspect his books and premises.

## [Search]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871782 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person in charge of
any vehicle in which the regulated product could be transported for
marketing in export trade shall permit any member or employee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} who is accompanied by a police officer, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a warrant,

to search the vehicle.

## [Seizure and Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871787 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  member of the Board, any inspector appointed by the Board and any
  police constable or any other person designated by the Board is hereby
  authorized and empowered on behalf of the Board to seize and, subject
  to the directions of the Board, to dispose of, any regulated product
  that is marketed in export trade in violation of an order of the
  Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall, without the authority of the Board,
  transport, market, move or interfere with any regulated product that
  has been seized pursuant to subsection (1) while that product remains
  under seizure.

## [Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871791 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person, except the Agency or a person acting under its authority,
  shall transport or cause to be transported any grade, quality or class
  of the regulated product, or offer or accept any grade, quality or
  class of the regulated product for transportation in export trade.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person designated by the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person transporting the regulated product under
    the direction or with the permission of the Board.
