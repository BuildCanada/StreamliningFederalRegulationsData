## Masked Name Regulations {#masked-name-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-261
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1994-03-24

Regulations Respecting the Masking of Names of Substances to Prevent the
Disclosure of Confidential Information

P.C. 1994-486 1994-03-24
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of National Health and
Welfare, pursuant to subsection 32(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the masking of names
of substances to prevent the disclosure of confidential
information](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-261).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963416 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Masked Name Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-261).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963418 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[chemical group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chemical group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an arrangement of
    bonded atoms that is a portion of a molecule and that possesses a
    name consisting of a non-composite radical term established in
    accordance with the chemical nomenclature rules of the International
    Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry or the Chemical Abstracts
    Service; ([groupe chimique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[locant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[locant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a numeral or Greek or Roman
    letter used in the name of a substance to indicate the positions of
    unsaturated bonds or attachments of chemical groups in a molecule;
    ([symbole indicateur de position]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[masked name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[masked name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a name determined in
    accordance with section 3 that conceals the explicit chemical or
    biological name of a substance without misrepresenting the generic
    identity of the substance; ([dénomination
    maquillée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[multiplicative prefix]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[multiplicative prefix]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prefix that
    describes the number of identical chemical groups in a chemical
    substance; ([préfixe multiplicatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-descriptive term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-descriptive term]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a term used in
    the masked name of a substance, in place of a distinctive element of
    the explicit chemical or biological name, that does not misrepresent
    the generic identity of the substance; ([terme non
    descriptif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[parent structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[parent structure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the principal chain
    or ring system from which an explicit chemical name or part of an
    explicit chemical name is derived. ([structure
    parentale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-101, s. 6
:::

## [Masking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963429 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 88 and 113 of the Act, the explicit chemical or
  biological name of a substance shall be masked by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} replacing a single distinctive element of the name
    with a non-descriptive term;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} replacing each of two or more distinctive elements
    of the name with a non-descriptive term;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} removing the locants; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} removing the locants and replacing each of one or
    more other distinctive elements with a non-descriptive term.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The number of distinctive elements in an explicit
  chemical or biological name that are replaced or removed in accordance
  with subsection (1) shall not exceed the minimum number necessary to
  ensure confidentiality.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the removal of a
  stereochemical indicator from an explicit chemical name does not
  constitute the removal of a distinctive element.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-101, s. 7
:::

## [Explicit Chemical Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963440 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, where
all or part of the explicit chemical name of a substance can be
described with a structural diagram, any of the following is a single
distinctive element of that name or part:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a locant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a locant and multiplicative prefix that together
  specify the position and number of a chemical group;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of a chemical group;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the parent structure and the locant of a
  chemical group bonded to the parent structure; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a salt, the name and multiplicative
  prefix of a single cation or anion of the salt.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-416, s. 1(E)
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 6, where the explicit chemical name of a
  substance cannot be described with a structural diagram, either of the
  following is a single distinctive element of that name:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a constituent of the name that describes a complex
    mixture of a known class of chemicals including any substance that
    is recorded in the *Chemical Abstracts Chemical Substance Index* as
    an indefinite or general derivative of a specific chemical
    substance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a constituent of the name that describes a complex
    mixture of an unknown class of chemicals including a substance that
    is recorded or is capable of being recorded in the *Chemical
    Abstracts General Subject Index* and can be named only by using
    descriptive or illustrative terms.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), where a part of
  the explicit chemical name can be described with a structural diagram,
  the masking of that part shall be carried out in accordance with
  section 4.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a substance referred
to in section 4 or 5 contains a chemical group that includes a carbon
atom possessing more than a single valence, the name of the chemical
group shall not be masked where the carbon atom is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} attached directly to an acyclic carbon atom; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} included within a ring system.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The name of a parent
structure of a substance referred to in section 4 or 5 may be masked
only by one of the following non-descriptive terms, as appropriate:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} alkyl or alkane;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} alkenyl or alkene;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} alkynyl or alkyne;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} carbomonocyclic or carbomonocycle;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} carbopolycyclic or carbopolycycle;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} heteromonocyclic or heteromonocycle; or

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} heteropolycyclic or heteropolycycle.

## [Explicit Biological Name]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963465 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where all or any part of
the name of a substance is an explicit biological name, any of the
following is a single distinctive element of that explicit biological
name:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the genus name;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the species name;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strain name;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the common biological name;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the source;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the culture history;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the phenotypic or genotypic characteristics;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the use; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other pertinent descriptive information.
