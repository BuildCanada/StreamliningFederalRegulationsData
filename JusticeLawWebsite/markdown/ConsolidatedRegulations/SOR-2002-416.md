## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698004 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([Code]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Justice.
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698008 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 696.1(2) of the Code, an application for
  ministerial review under Part XXI.1 of the Code shall be in the form
  set out in the schedule and contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the applicant,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, including any alias or
      former name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's address, date of birth and, if
      any, the number assigned to the applicant under the Royal Canadian
      Mounted Police Automated Fingerprint Identification System,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
      person making the application on the applicant's behalf, if any,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} whether the alleged miscarriage of justice
      relates to a conviction on an offence punishable on summary
      conviction or on an indictable offence, or, in the case of a
      finding of dangerous offender or long-term offender under
      Part XXIV of the Code, particulars of the finding, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant is in custody;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to any pre-trial hearings,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the preliminary inquiry, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the court and its address, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number, type and date of any pre-trial
      motions, as well as the court decision on those motions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the trial,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it started,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the court and its address, the plea entered at
      trial, the mode of trial and the date of the conviction and that
      of sentencing,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of all counsel involved
      in the trial, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number, type and date of any motions made,
      as well as the date of the court decision on those motions;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} particulars regarding any subsequent appeals to the
    court of appeal or the Supreme Court of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the application; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the new matters of significance
    that support the application, if any.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by the following
  documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's signed consent authorizing the
    Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to have access to the applicant's personal
      information that is required for reviewing the application, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to disclose to any person or body the
      applicant's personal information obtained in the course of
      reviewing the application in order for the Minister to obtain from
      that person or body any information that is required for reviewing
      the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a true copy of the information or indictment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a true copy of the trial transcript, including any
    preliminary hearings;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a true copy of all material filed by the defence
    counsel and Crown counsel in support of any pre-trial and trial
    motions;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a true copy of all factums filed on appeal;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a true copy of all court decisions; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other document that the applicant considers
    necessary for the review of the application.

## [Review of the Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698041 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an
application completed in accordance with section 2, the Minister shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} send an acknowledgment letter to the applicant and
  the person acting on the applicant's behalf, if any; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct a preliminary assessment of the application.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the preliminary assessment has been completed, the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall conduct an investigation in respect of the
    application if the Minister determines that there may be a
    reasonable basis to conclude that a miscarriage of justice likely
    occurred; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not conduct an investigation if the Minister

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that there is a reasonable basis to
      conclude that a miscarriage of justice likely occurred and that
      there is an urgent need for a decision to be made under
      paragraph 696.3(3)(a) of the Code for humanitarian reasons or to
      avoid a blatant continued prejudice to the applicant, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that there is no reasonable basis
      to conclude that a miscarriage of justice likely occurred.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall send a notice to the applicant and
  to the person acting on the applicant's behalf, if any, indicating
  whether or not an investigation will be conducted under
  subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not conduct an investigation for
  the reason described in subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the notice under
  subsection (2) shall indicate that the applicant may provide further
  information in support of the application within one year after the
  date on which the notice was sent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant fails, within the period prescribed
  in subsection (3), to provide further information, the Minister shall
  inform the applicant in writing that no investigation will be
  conducted.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If further information in support of the application
  is provided after the period prescribed in subsection (3) has expired,
  the Minister shall conduct a new preliminary assessment of the
  application under section 3.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  completing an investigation under paragraph 4(1)(a), the Minister
  shall prepare an investigation report and provide a copy of it to the
  applicant and to the person acting on the applicant's behalf, if any.
  The Minister shall indicate in writing that the applicant may provide
  further information in support of the application within one year
  after the date on which the investigation report is sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant fails, within the period prescribed
  in subsection (1), to provide any further information, or if the
  applicant indicates in writing that no further information will be
  provided in support of the application, the Minister may proceed to
  make a decision under subsection 696.3(3) of the Code.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall provide
a copy of the Minister's decision made under subsection 696.3(3) of the
Code to the applicant and to the person acting on the applicant's
behalf, if any.

## [Annual Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698059 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An annual report submitted
under section 696.5 of the Code shall contain the following information
in respect of the financial year under review in the report:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of applications made to the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of applications that have been abandoned
  or that are incomplete;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of applications that are at the
  preliminary assessment stage;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number of applications that are at the
  investigation stage;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of decisions that the Minister has made
  under subsection 696.3(3) of the Code; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other information that the Minister considers
  appropriate.

## AMENDMENTS NOT IN FORCE {#amendments-not-in-force .scheduleTitleText}

- --- 2024, c. 33, s. 10

  - ###### No consent {#no-consent .MarginalNote}

    - [10]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant, within the time
      limit fixed by the Minister, does not reply in writing to the
      Minister or gives a written refusal of consent to transfer the
      application, subsection (2) or (3) applies.

    - ###### Preliminary assessment completed {#preliminary-assessment-completed .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, before the commencement day, the Minister
      completed the preliminary assessment of the application required
      under paragraph 3(b) of the [Regulations Respecting Applications
      for Ministerial Review --- Miscarriages of
      Justice](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-416), the old scheme continues
      to apply in respect of the application.

    - ###### Preliminary assessment not completed {#preliminary-assessment-not-completed .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister did not complete the preliminary
      assessment before the commencement day, the application is deemed
      not to have been made and the applicant may apply to the
      Commission under the new scheme.

- --- 2024, c. 33, s. 11

  - ###### Late consent {#late-consent .MarginalNote}

    [11]{.lawlabel} An application is deemed to have been made to the
    Commission under the new scheme and the Minister is authorized to
    disclose to the Commission all information pertaining to the
    application that is under the Minister's control if

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant gives, after the time limit fixed
      by the Minister, written consent to transfer the application;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister completed the preliminary assessment
      required under paragraph 3(b) of the [Regulations Respecting
      Applications for Ministerial Review --- Miscarriages of
      Justice](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-416) before the commencement
      day; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister receives the consent before having
      made, under subsection 10(2) of this Act, a decision under
      subsection 696.3(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), as it
      read immediately before the commencement day.

- --- 2024, c. 33, s. 19

  - ###### [SOR/2002-416]{.HistoricalNote} {#sor2002-416 .MarginalNote}

    [19]{.lawlabel} The [Regulations Respecting Applications for
    Ministerial Review --- Miscarriages of
    Justice](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-416) are repealed.
