## Territorial Dredging Regulations {#territorial-dredging-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1523
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Territorial Dredging Regulations
::::

:::::::::::: section
**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-116,
s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526714 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes every natural substance
      including gold and silver that may be recovered from the submerged
      bed of a river by the process commonly known as dredging, but does
      not include peat, bitumen, oil shales, clay, sand and gravel;
      ([minéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
      Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[recorder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated by the
      Minister to perform the duties of a recorder for a mining district
      established under the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) for
      the Northwest Territories; ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a stream of water the bed of
      which is of an average width of 150 feet throughout the portion
      thereof sought to be leased; ([rivière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[river bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[river bed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the bed and bars of the
      river to the foot of the natural banks. ([lit d'une
      rivière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The recorder's decision as to the width of any stream
  on which dredging applications have been made shall be final.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 7
:::

## [Leases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526725 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may issue
leases to any person applying therefor granting the exclusive right to
dredge for minerals in the submerged bed of any river in the Northwest
Territories.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 8
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every location for a
dredging lease shall be marked out by two posts placed on the margin of
such river, above highwater mark, as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the No. 1 post shall be at least four feet above the
  surface of the ground located at the upstream end of the area to be
  covered by the lease and shall be a post not less than four inches in
  diameter, mounded to a height of two feet, such mounding to be
  conically shaped and having a diameter at the base of not less than
  three feet, and this post shall be faced on the downstream side, which
  facing shall contain the following inscription legibly marked thereon:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} post No. 1,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} date and time of staking,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} name of staker,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} distance to No. 2 post,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the letters "DL";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the No. 2 post shall be similar to the No. 1 post and
  placed on the same side of the river as the No. 1 post at the
  downstream end of the location covered by the lease, shall be faced
  and mounded in the manner prescribed for Post No. 1 and the
  inscription thereon shall be as follows:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} post No. 2,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} name of staker,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} distance upstream to No. 1 post, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the letters "DL".

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A portion of a river staked
under these Regulations shall be continuous, and shall in no case exceed
10 miles measured along the middle of the river following its
sinuosities.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of a lease
issued under these Regulations shall have the exclusive right to dredge
the river bed within the length of river leased to him.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Not more than one lease
shall be issued to any one person.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The lessee shall, when so
directed by the Minister, cause a survey to be made, at his own expense
and in accordance with the instructions of the Surveyor General, of the
area of river leased to him, and the returns of such survey shall be
filed in the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development
within six months after receipt by the lessee of such direction and
instructions.

::: HistoricalNote
- 1998, c. 14, s. 101(F)
:::

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lease shall be for a term of 15 years, at the end of which time all
  rights that are vested in or may be claimed by the lessee under his
  lease shall cease and determine.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A lease may be renewed from time to time at the
  discretion of the Minister if it is shown to his satisfaction that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the area of the lease has not been fully dredged;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lessee has, during the term of his lease,
    carried on dredging operations efficiently and has otherwise
    complied with these Regulations.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The lessee shall not
assign, transfer or sublet the demised premises or any portion thereof,
without the consent in writing of the Minister.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  lease of an area in the Northwest Territories issued under these
  Regulations is subject to the rights of any person who, prior to the
  issue of the lease, is entitled to or has obtained a grant of a claim
  under the Northwest Territories Placer Mining Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-116, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 9
:::

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lessee shall, within three years from the date of his lease, have at
  least one dredge, of such capacity as the Minister may deem
  sufficient, in operation upon the area described in his lease, and
  shall in every year thereafter during the continuance of his lease
  dredge from such area not less than 20,000 cubic yards of gravel, sand
  or other substance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the lessee fails to furnish proof yearly, or at
  such times as the Minister may direct, of the efficient operation of
  such dredge, and of the actual work performed, the Minister may cancel
  the lease.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Upon application to that effect being made to him by
  any persons holding adjoining dredging leases, not exceeding five in
  number, the Minister may grant permission to such persons to operate
  each of their respective dredges on any one or more of the areas
  covered by such leases for a term not exceeding 10 years, and to
  perform on any one or more of such areas all the work required to be
  done on each area separately, as required by subsection (1).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526762 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  application for a lease shall be accompanied by a fee of \$5 together
  with the rental for the first year at the rate of \$100 for each mile
  of river to be leased.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The rental for each subsequent year shall be at the
  rate of \$10 for each mile, payable in advance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All fees pertaining to a lease are set out in the
  schedule.

## [Royalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-526767 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2003-116, s.
  10\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On or before April 1st in each year, the lessee shall
  pay to Her Majesty upon all gold recovered from the bed of a river in
  the Northwest Territories under a dredging lease during the year
  ending December 31st immediately preceding a royalty at the rate of 1
  1/4 per cent of its value.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On or before April 1st in each year, the lessee shall
  pay to Her Majesty on all minerals other than gold, including silver,
  recovered from the bed of a river in the Northwest Territories under a
  dredging lease during the year ending on December 31st immediately
  preceding a royalty at the rate of 2 1/2 per cent of their value.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On or before April 1st in each year, every person
  liable to pay royalty in respect of gold, silver or other minerals
  recovered under a lease during the year ending on December 31st
  immediately preceding shall, without notice or demand to that effect,
  in addition to any other statements or returns which may otherwise be
  required, deliver to the Minister or such officer as the Minister may
  name, a sworn statement containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the lessee,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of the lease,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the description of the lands covered by the lease,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the calendar year for which the statement is made,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of gold recovered and the
    amount of royalty thereon, and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the quantity and value of silver or other minerals
    recovered and the amount of royalty thereon.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every lease issued under these Regulations shall
  contain a provision that the lessee shall pay royalty upon gold,
  silver and other minerals obtained from the beds of rivers under the
  lease at such rate as may from time to time be prescribed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 10
:::

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lessee may obtain from the timber agent a permit or permits to cut,
  free of dues, such timber as may be necessary for the purposes of his
  dredging operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit or permits referred to in subsection (1)
  shall contain a description of the tract or tracts within which the
  timber may be cut, and the kind, dimensions and quantities of timber
  to be so cut.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The permit referred to in subsection (1), however,
  shall not give or be deemed to give to the holder thereof any
  exclusive right to the timber on the tract therein described.

<!-- -->

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  lessee shall not interfere in any way with the general right of the
  public to use for navigation or other purposes the river upon which he
  holds a lease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The free navigation of a river shall not be impeded
  by the deposit of tailings, and the current or stream shall not be
  obstructed by the accumulation of tailings.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the lessee fails to comply with the requirements
  of subsections (1) and (2), notice may be posted, by such officer as
  the Minister directs, at the point where the stream has been impeded
  or obstructed or in the vicinity thereof, requiring such defect to be
  remedied, and a copy of such notice shall be served upon the lessee or
  his agent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the lessee fails to remove, within the time set
  out in the notice referred to in subsection (1), the impediment or
  obstruction complained of, the lease may be cancelled by the Minister.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The lease shall provide
that any person who has recorded a claim under the Northwest Territories
Placer Mining Regulations[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^3^](#footnote3_e){#footnote3_e-ID0EBCA} shall be
entitled to run tailings into the river at any point thereof and to
construct all works which may be necessary for properly operating his
claim, but no such person shall construct a dam or wing-dam within 1,000
feet from the place where any dredge is being operated, or obstruct or
interfere in any way with the operation of any dredge.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^3^](#footnote3_e-ID0EBCA){#footnote3_e}Revoked by
  SOR/61-86
  :::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-116, s. 11
:::

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The lease shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reserve all roads, ways, bridges, drains and other
  public works and all duly authorized improvements now existing or
  which may hereafter be made in, upon, or under any part of the river,
  and the right to enter and construct the same,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide that the lessee shall not damage nor obstruct
  any public or duly authorized ways, drains, bridges, works and
  improvements now or hereafter to be made upon, in, over, through or
  under the river, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide that the lessee shall substantially bridge or
  cover and protect all the cuts, flumes, ditches and sluices and all
  pits and dangerous places at all points where they may be crossed by a
  public highway or frequented path or trail to the satisfaction of the
  Minister.
