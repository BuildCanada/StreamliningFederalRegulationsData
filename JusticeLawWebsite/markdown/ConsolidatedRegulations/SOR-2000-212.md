## Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission By-laws {#canadian-nuclear-safety-commission-by-laws .Title-of-Act}

Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission By-laws

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660135 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these By-laws.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Nuclear Safety
    Commission established by section 8 of the Act.
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a meeting of the members of
    the Commission or a panel held for the conduct of the Commission's
    affairs. ([réunion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the Commission
    appointed under section 10 of the Act.
    ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a panel of the Commission
    consisting of one or more members established by the President under
    section 22 of the Act. ([formation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the
    Commission designated under subsection 10(3) of the Act.
    ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of the
    Commission appointed under section 16 of the Act.
    ([secrétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660144 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These By-laws apply to the
management and conduct of the affairs of the Commission, including any
panel, and to the procedures to be followed in proceedings other than
those prescribed in the [Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Rules of
Procedure](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-211).

## [Signing Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660146 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the President is absent
or incapacitated or the office of President is vacant and the Commission
has not designated a replacement under subsection 12(2) of the Act, the
Secretary or, in the absence of the Secretary, a Director General, on
behalf of the Commission, shall sign any transfer, assignment, contract,
certificate, permit, licence, approval, order, direction or other
instrument authorized to be issued or made by the Commission.

## [Secretary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660148 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Secretary shall keep the corporate seal of the Commission and affix it
  to any record that the Commission directs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Secretary shall carry out the duties that are
  required by the President or under these By-laws.

## [Meetings and Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660152 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Meetings are convened by
the Commission or by the President and are held at any time and place
that the convenor determines.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Meetings are held in public
or in closed session.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Notice of the time and
place of a meeting shall be given to each member, and if the meeting is
to be held in public, the notice shall be published in the manner that
the Commission determines.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** When urgent matters of
business require it, a meeting may be convened without prior notice.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a meeting is to be held
in public, the Secretary shall make the meeting agenda publicly
available at least 30 days before the meeting.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Failure to give proper or
any notice of a meeting does not invalidate the meeting.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A meeting may be
adjourned from time to time and from place to place, and those in
attendance shall be notified of the time and place the meeting will be
reconvened.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A member who participates
at a meeting or in a decision of the Commission is deemed to have voted
in the affirmative on any decision or action taken, or resolution or
vote passed, unless the member enters a dissent or an abstention on the
record.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The record of every
meeting shall be approved at the next meeting.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** If a meeting is held in
public, the approved record of the meeting shall be made publicly
available in the manner that the Commission determines.

## [Quorum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660163 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Quorum for a meeting of
the Commission is three members.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Quorum for a meeting of a
panel is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a panel of one or two members, one member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a panel of three or four members, two members;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a panel of more than four members, three members.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660169 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660171 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** These By-laws come into
force on the day on which they are made by the Commission.
