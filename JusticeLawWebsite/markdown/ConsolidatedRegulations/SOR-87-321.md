## Export Permit (Steel Monitoring) Regulations {#export-permit-steel-monitoring-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-321
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1987-06-05

Regulations Respecting Export Permits Issued for Steel Export Monitoring
Purposes

P.C. 1987-1120 1987-06-04
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section 12 of
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed Regulations respecting the issuance of Export
Permits for steel export monitoring purposes.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905264 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Export Permit (Steel Monitoring)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-321).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905266 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a resident of Canada who
    applies for a permit under section 8.1 of the Act;
    ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of External
    Affairs; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who exports steel
    from Canada; ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to export steel issued
    pursuant to section 8.1 of the Act; ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[steel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[steel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of steel or any product
    made of steel that is included on the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) pursuant to section 5.1 of the
    Act. ([acier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905274 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the exportation of steel.

## [Application for Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905276 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may apply
either orally or in writing to the Special Trade Relations Bureau of the
Department, or to any person authorized by the Minister to issue a
permit on the Minister's behalf, by furnishing the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} information as to whether or not the applicant is a
  resident of Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the exporter, if different
  from that of the applicant;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the country of origin of the steel being exported;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the consignee to whom the
  steel will be exported;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the country that is to be the final destination of
  the steel;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian customs office at which the steel will
  exit Canada;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date or proposed date of exportation of the steel
  from Canada;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steel, including the appropriate
  Special Trade Relations Bureau Classification Commodity Code;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the number of units of the steel to be exported and
  the total value of those units in Canadian currency; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any additional information requested by the Minister
  in order to clarify the information furnished by the applicant or to
  provide a more detailed description of the steel.

## [Issuance of Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905289 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A permit
  shall be in the form set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a permit is issued, the Minister shall sign
  each copy of the permit and transmit two copies of the permit to the
  applicant.

## [Exporting Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905293 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every person to whom a
permit has been issued shall, before exporting the steel described in
the permit,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} certify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the information furnished in the application
    for the permit is true, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the person is a resident of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} present a copy of the permit

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at the Canadian customs office located at the
    Canadian port of entry set out in the permit, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at a Canadian customs office authorized by the
    Special Trade Relations Bureau of the Department.

## [Terms and Conditions of Permit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905301 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A permit is issued on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of units of steel exported under the
  permit does not exceed the number of units of steel set out in the
  permit by more than five per cent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the value of the steel exported under the permit does
  not exceed the value of the steel set out in the permit by more than
  five per cent;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exporter keeps at the exporter's place of
  business documents and records in respect of each exportation of steel
  for which a permit is issued, in such form and containing such
  information as will enable the country of origin of the exported steel
  to be determined, and retains every account and voucher necessary to
  verify the information contained in those documents and records;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the exporter at all reasonable times

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} makes the documents and records referred to in
    paragraph (c) available for inspection by any persons authorized by
    the Minister, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provides to those persons every facility necessary
    for the inspection of the documents and records and permits copies
    thereof to be made by those persons; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the documents and records kept by the exporter
  pursuant to paragraph (c) prove, on inspection, to be inadequate for
  the purpose of determining the country of origin of the exported
  steel, the exporter provides the additional documents or records
  necessary for making that determination.

## [Lost or Destroyed Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905310 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where a permit has been
lost or destroyed, the person to whom it was issued may request from the
Special Trade Relations Bureau of the Department a permit to replace it
and shall, in so requesting, submit a statutory declaration containing

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the original permit has been lost or
  destroyed and an explanation of the circumstances of the loss or
  destruction; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a permit that has been lost, an
  undertaking to return the original permit to the Special Trade
  Relations Bureau of the Department without delay, if it is found.

## [General Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905314 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, by order, issue general permits to export steel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A general permit issued pursuant to subsection (1)
  shall be subject to such terms and conditions as are set out in the
  permit.
