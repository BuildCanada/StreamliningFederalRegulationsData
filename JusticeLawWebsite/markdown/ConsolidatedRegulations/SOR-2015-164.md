## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817902 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part II of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [General Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817904 .Part}

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
to any person who is not an employee but who performs for an employer to
which these Regulations apply activities whose primary purpose is to
enable the person to acquire knowledge or experience, and to the
employer, as if that person were an employee of the employer, and every
provision of these Regulations must be read accordingly.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-211, s. 6
:::

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Policy Committees and Work Place Committees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817908 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817909 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of policy committees and work place committees.

### [Selection of Members]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817911 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Employees who are not
represented by a trade union must select members of a committee by a
majority of votes.

### [Qualifications of Members Selected by the Employer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817913 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The members of a committee
selected by the employer must be employees who exercise managerial
functions.

### [Chairpersons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817915 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chairpersons of a committee who are selected in accordance with
  subsection 135.1(7) of the Act must act alternately.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chairpersons have the following responsibilities:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} scheduling the committee meetings and notifying the
    members of those meetings;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} preparing the agenda of each committee meeting;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that each item under discussion at a
    committee meeting concludes with a decision; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the committee carries out its
    functions.

### [Vacancy]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817923 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If a committee member
ceases to be a member and by reason of the vacancy the composition of
the committee fails to meet the requirements of section 135.1 of the
Act, a new member must be selected and appointed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a policy committee, within 60 days
  after the day on which the vacancy occurs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a work place committee, within 30 days
  after the day on which the vacancy occurs.

### [Quorum]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817927 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A quorum of a committee
consists of the majority of members, at least half of which are employee
members and at least one of which is an employer member.

### [Minutes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817929 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} As soon
  as feasible after each committee meeting, the minutes must be provided
  to both chairpersons for their approval and the approval document, if
  any, must be attached to the minutes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after receiving the minutes and
  the approval document, the chairperson selected by the employer
  members of the committee must provide a copy of those documents to the
  employer and each member of the committee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after receiving a copy of the
  minutes and the approval document, the employer must provide a copy of
  them

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the work place committee, in the case of minutes
    of a policy committee meeting; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the policy committee, at its request, in the
    case of minutes of a work place committee meeting.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after receiving a copy of the
  minutes and the approval document, the employer must make a copy of
  the minutes readily available to the employees for a period of one
  month.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The employer must keep a copy of the minutes and the
  approval document at the following locations for a period of two years
  after the day on which the meeting was held:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a policy committee, at the
    employer's head office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a work place committee, at the
    employer's head office or at the work place.

### [Annual Report on Work Place Committee Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817940 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before March 1 each year, the chairperson selected by the employer
  members of the work place committee must submit to the Head of
  Compliance and Enforcement an annual report of the committee's
  activities during the 12-month period ending on December 31 of the
  preceding year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must be in the form set out in the
  schedule, contain the information set out in the form, and be signed
  by both chairpersons.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after the report has been
  submitted, the chairperson selected by the employer members of the
  work place committee must provide a copy of the report to the
  employer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} As soon as feasible after receiving a copy of the
  report, the employer must post a copy of it in the conspicuous place
  or places in which the employer posts the information referred to in
  paragraph 125(1)(z.17) of the Act and keep the copy posted for a
  period of two months.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Health and Safety Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817945 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817946 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of health and safety representatives.

### [Selection of Representatives]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817948 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Employees who are not
represented by a trade union must select their health and safety
representatives by a majority of votes.

### [Term of Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817950 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The term of office of a
health and safety representative is two years.

### [Vacancy]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817952 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If a health and safety
representative ceases to be a representative, the vacancy must be filled
within 30 days after the day on which the vacancy occurred.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Health and Safety Training Program]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817954 .Part}

### [Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-817955 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 125(1)(z.01) of the Act, training for
  members of policy and work place committees and health and safety
  representatives must be developed by the employer after consultation
  with the committees or representatives concerned and must include the
  following aspects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Act and any regulations made under it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the means that allow the committee members and the
    health and safety representatives to fulfill their responsibilities
    under the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rules of each of the committees; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the principles of consensus building regarding
    health and safety issues.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The health and safety training program must be
  reviewed and updated at least once every three years, and whenever
  there is a change of circumstances that may affect the content of the
  training.
