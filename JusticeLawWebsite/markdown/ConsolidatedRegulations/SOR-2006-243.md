:::

[FIRST NATIONS FISCAL MANAGEMENT ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.67)

FIRST NATIONS FISCAL AND STATISTICAL MANAGEMENT ACT

Registration 2006-10-05

First Nations Tax Commissioner Appointment Regulations

P.C. 2006-1078 2006-10-05
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 20(3) and paragraph 140(a) of the First Nations Fiscal and
Statistical Management Act[[Footnote

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-243).

## [Body to Appoint]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725129 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Named body

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 20(3) of the [First Nations Fiscal Management
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67), a commissioner shall be appointed by the
Indigenous Law Centre at the University of Saskatchewan.
