## NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods Regulations {#nafta-prescribed-class-of-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-608
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1993-12-15

Regulations Prescribing a Class of Goods for the Purposes of Subsection
35.02(4) of the Customs Act

P.C. 1993-2109  1993-12-15
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection
35.02(4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6274){#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6274-ID0EKDAA}
and paragraph 164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6275){#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6275-ID0EIDAA}
of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6276){#fn_SOR-93-608_e_hq_6276-ID0EFDAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations prescribing a class
of goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the Customs
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-608), effective on the day on which section
83 of [An Act to implement the North American Free Trade
Agreement](/eng/acts/N-23.8), being chapter 44 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958356 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [NAFTA Prescribed Class of Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-608).

## [Class of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958358 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The goods in respect of
which any regulations made under paragraph 19(1)(a) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) apply are hereby prescribed as a class of
goods for the purposes of subsection 35.02(4) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).
