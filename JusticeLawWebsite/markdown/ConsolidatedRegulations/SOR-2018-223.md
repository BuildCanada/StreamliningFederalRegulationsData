## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-856320 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[minimal operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minimal operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an operation to ensure the preservation of a good
      in good condition for the purposes of transport and storage;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} packaging, re-packaging, breaking up of
      consignments or putting up a good for retail sale, including
      placing a good in bottles, cans, flasks, bags, cases or boxes;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} mere dilution with water or another substance
      that does not materially alter the characteristics of the good;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the collection of goods intended to form sets,
      assortments, kits or composite goods; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any combination of operations referred to in
      paragraphs (a) to (d). ([activité minimale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means qualifying as originating
    in the territory of a Party under the rules of origin set out in the
    [CPTPP Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-221). ([originaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Shipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-856329 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
originating goods exported from a CPTPP country are entitled to the
benefit of a CPTPP tariff set out in section 3 if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada from a CPTPP country
  without shipment through another country that is not a CPTPP country,
  either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, when requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are shipped to Canada through another
  country that is not a CPTPP country and the importer provides, when
  requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the goods, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the goods remained under customs control while in
    that other country.

## [Applicable Preferential Tariff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-856337 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), goods that meet the requirements of section 2 are
  eligible for any of the following tariffs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the CPTPP tariff applicable to the CPTPP country in
    which the last production process, other than a minimal operation,
    occurred;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the highest rate of the CPTPP tariffs applicable to
    the CPTPP country in which a production process occurred; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the highest rate of the CPTPP tariffs applicable to
    any CPTPP country.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The CPTPP tariffs referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (c) are the CPTPP tariffs applicable to a CPTPP country set
  opposite the reference to that country in the column "Tariff Treatment
  / Other" in the List of Countries set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

are registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which
they are registered.
