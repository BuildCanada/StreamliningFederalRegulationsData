## Golden Nematode Order {#golden-nematode-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-260
:::

[PLANT PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-14.8)

Registration 1980-04-08

Order Prohibiting and Restricting the Transportation and Movement of any
Plant or Other Matter that is Likely to Result in the Spread of the
Golden Nematode
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Agriculture, upon the report of an inspector setting
forth a reasonable belief of the existence of the Golden Nematode in
that portion of the municipality of Central Saanich in the Province of
British Columbia east of the West Saanich Road defined in that report,
pursuant to section 8 of the Plant Quarantine Act, hereby makes the
annexed [Order prohibiting and restricting the transportation and
movement of any plant or other matter that is likely to result in the
spread of the Golden Nematode](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-260).

Dated at Ottawa, Ontario, this 3rd day of April, 1980

::: FlushRight
EUGENE F. WHELAN
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Agriculture*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869000 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Golden Nematode Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-260).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869002 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Category A land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category A land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land in the quarantine
    area in which cysts of the Golden Nematode that contain viable eggs
    or larvae have been found; ([terre de catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Category B land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category B land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land in the quarantine
    area exposed to infestation or suspected of being infested by virtue
    of its association with or location relative to Category A land;
    ([terre de catégorie B]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Category C land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Category C land]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all land in the
    quarantine area other than Category A land or Category B land;
    ([terre de catégorie C]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quarantine area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quarantine area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area of land
    described in the schedule; ([zone de quarantaine]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Plant Quarantine
    Regulations; ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restricted movement area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted movement area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the land
    districts of Esquimalt, Goldstream, Highland, Lake, Malahat,
    Metchosin, North Saanich, Otter, Sooke and Victoria including that
    portion of the South Saanich Land District not included in the
    municipality of Central Saanich, and all that portion of the
    Municipality of Central Saanich west of the West Saanich Road.
    ([zone de déplacement restreint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869010 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 4 to 8, the transportation into or from and the movement
  within the quarantine area is hereby prohibited of any plant, soil or
  other matter that is or may be contaminated by the Golden Nematode or
  by soil that is infested with the Golden Nematode and, without
  limiting the generality of the foregoing, of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Golden Nematode including cysts thereof that
    contain viable eggs or larvae;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} soil and sand and media containing soil or sand;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} plants and parts thereof, with soil or with roots,
    including nursery stock, greenhouse plants, bedding plants,
    vegetable transplants and sod;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} eggplant, potato and tomato plants, and parts
    thereof;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} root crops and vegetables in a raw or unprocessed
    state;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} containers and packing material containing soil or
    sand or to which soil or sand adheres;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} hay, straw, plant litter and manure; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} machinery, implements and vehicles containing soil
    or sand or to which soil or sand adheres.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the following plants
  and other things if they are free from soil, namely

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} plants propagated in synthetic growing media such
    as perlite, expanded mica, Baystrat and expanded clay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} unrooted plants or parts thereof, such as Christmas
    trees, holly wreaths, budsticks, seeds and fruits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} aquatic plants, moss or lycopodium;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} peat moss in sealed bags or unbroken bales;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} processed root crops, vegetables or other edible
    plant material;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} containers, machinery, implements and vehicles that
    are new and have not been in contact with the soil; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} all vegetables and unprocessed root crops other
    than potatoes.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to the restrictions
of any other applicable legislation, anything listed in paragraphs
(1)(a) to (g) except soil samples intended for scientific or research
purposes may be moved within the quarantine area or transported from the
quarantine area if an inspector has determined that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it originated in non-infested premises in the
  quarantine area, and has not been exposed to infestation while within
  the quarantine area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has been treated to destroy infestation in
  accordance with any requirement of the inspector; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it was grown, produced, manufactured, stored, or
  handled in such a manner that no infestation would be transmitted by
  such movement or transportation.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Potatoes intended for
marketing or processing may be moved within the quarantine area or
transported from the quarantine area if the potatoes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have been washed and freed of all soil on the
  premises of the producer or at a grading station approved by an
  inspector;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have been transported from the premises of the
  producer to a grading station in a manner that an inspector has
  determined will preclude the spread of the Golden Nematode enroute;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if packed in containers, are packed in new
  containers.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Cull potatoes intended for
use only as livestock feed that meet the requirements of paragraphs 5(a)
and (b) and do not meet any standards for potatoes set out in the [Fresh
Fruit and Vegetable Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._285) or any
equivalent standards of the Province of British Columbia may be
transported from the quarantine area if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of the potatoes is authorized by
  an inspector; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the potatoes are transported in a manner and to
  premises approved by an inspector.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Soil samples intended for
scientific or research purposes may be moved from the quarantine area
under a movement certificate referred to in section 10 of the
Regulations if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} they have been treated to prevent the spread of the
  Golden Nematode; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where not so treated, they are transported in such a
  manner and if such precautions are taken at destinations as to
  preclude the possibility of the spreading of Golden Nematode.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Machinery, implements and
vehicles may be moved as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if they have been used on and are in Category A land,
  they may be moved

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within Category A land without cleaning, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to Category B land or Category C land or
    transported from the quarantine area on condition that

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all plant debris and soil have been removed
      therefrom at the working site prior to movement or transportation
      to such an extent that no plant debris or soil will be
      transported,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where required by an inspector, they are cleaned
      under his supervision to his satisfaction, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} prior notice is given to an inspector that they
      are to be moved;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they have been used on and are in Category B land,
  they may be moved

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to Category A land or Category B land without
    cleaning, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to Category C land or transported from the
    quarantine area on condition that

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all plant debris and soil have been removed
      therefrom at the working site or at a site approved by an
      inspector, prior to movement or transportation, to such an extent
      that no appreciable amounts will be transported, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where required by an inspector, they are cleaned
      under his supervision to his satisfaction, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} prior notice is given to an inspector that they
      are to be moved; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if they have been used on and are in Category C land,
  they may be moved

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within the quarantine area and transported from the
    quarantine area to the restricted movement area without cleaning, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transported from the quarantine area to any other
    area on condition that

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} all plant debris and soil have been removed
      therefrom at the working site, prior to transportation, to such an
      extent that no appreciable amounts will be transported, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where required by an inspector, they are cleaned
      under his supervision to his satisfaction.
