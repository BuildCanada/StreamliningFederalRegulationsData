- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may authorize the payment of interest at the rate fixed from
  time to time by the Minister of Finance with the approval of the
  Governor in Council on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} moneys received by the Department and paid into a
    trust fund, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts of allowances or other moneys payable out
    of an appropriation by Parliament or paid into a trust fund from an
    appropriation by Parliament,

  in respect of a veteran or qualified person and held pursuant to these
  Regulations for a period of not less than three months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Interest shall not be paid on any moneys that are
  held by the Department due to the failure of a veteran or qualified
  person to claim the amount due to him or to notify the Department of
  his address except where that veteran or qualified person is
  physically or mentally incapable of so doing.

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Where on the death of a
veteran or qualified person his account shows a credit resulting from an
award made pursuant to these Regulations or any other Regulations
respecting treatment of a veteran made prior to enactment of these
Regulations, the amount of that credit shall not form part of his estate
but may, where the Deputy Minister so orders, be paid in whole or in
part

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to or on behalf of a dependant of that veteran or
  qualified person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a person who maintained that veteran or qualified
  person or has been maintained by that veteran or qualified person; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} towards the expenses of the last sickness and the
  funeral and burial of that veteran or qualified person, where such
  expenses have not been paid in full from public funds.
