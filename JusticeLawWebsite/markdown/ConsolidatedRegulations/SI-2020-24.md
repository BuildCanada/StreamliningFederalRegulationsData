## Order Designating the Minister of Labour as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-labour-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2020-24
:::

[PAY EQUITY ACT](/eng/acts/P-4.2)

Registration 2020-04-01

Order Designating the Minister of Labour as the Minister for the
Purposes of that Act

P.C. 2020-118 2020-03-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 5 of the [Pay Equity
Act](/eng/acts/P-4.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Labour, a member of the Queen's Privy Council
for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
