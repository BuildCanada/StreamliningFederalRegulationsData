P.C. 2021-532 2021-06-10

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the annexed [Vessel Safety Certificates
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) set out an amendment to the
[Navigation Safety Regulations, 2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216),
which establishes additional or complementary standards to those set out
in the International Convention for the Safety of Life At Sea of 1974
and the Protocol of 1988 relating to the Convention, and the
Administrator in Council is satisfied that those standards meet the
objectives of the Convention and Protocol;

Therefore, His Excellency the Administrator of the Government of Canada
in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the
Minister of Natural Resources with respect to the provisions of the
annexed Regulations other than sections 21 to 27, on the recommendation
of the Minister of National Revenue with respect to that section 21, of
the Minister of Labour with respect to those sections 22 to 24 and of
the Minister of Transport with respect to those sections 25 to 27, makes
the annexed [Vessel Safety Certificates
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) pursuant to

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298587 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage that is not a
    sheltered waters voyage and is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the inland waters of Canada; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the inland waters of Canada together with any
      part of a lake or river forming part of the inland waters of
      Canada that lies within the United States, or on Lake
      Michigan. ([voyage en eaux internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all the
    rivers, lakes and other navigable fresh waters within Canada, and
    includes the St. Lawrence River as far seaward as a straight line
    drawn

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from Cap-des-Rosiers to Pointe Ouest, Anticosti
      Island; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from Anticosti Island to the north shore of the
      St. Lawrence River along longitude 63°W. ([eaux internes du
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not a sheltered waters voyage, an inland
      voyage or a near coastal voyage, Class 2;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is between places in Canada, the United
      States (except Hawaii), Saint Pierre and Miquelon, the West
      Indies, Mexico, or Central America, or on the northeast coast of
      South America, including on any navigable waters connected with
      that coast; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during which a vessel is always

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} north of latitude 6°N, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 200 nautical miles or less from shore or above
        the continental shelf. ([voyage à proximité du littoral, classe
        1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[near coastal voyage, Class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[near coastal voyage, Class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not a sheltered waters voyage or an
      inland voyage; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during which a vessel is always

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 25 nautical miles or less from shore in waters
        contiguous to Canada, the United States (except Hawaii) or Saint
        Pierre and Miquelon, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 100 nautical miles or less from a place of
        refuge. ([voyage à proximité du littoral, classe
        2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage that
    is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada on a river above tidal waters or on a
      lake, where it is impossible for a vessel to be more than 2.5
      nautical miles from the closest shore;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the waters listed in column 1 of Schedule 1
      during the period specified in column 2; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} made by a ferry between two or more points listed
      in column 1 of Schedule 2 during the period specified in column
      2. ([voyage en eaux abritées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention for
    the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, and the Protocol of 1988 relating
    to the Convention, as amended from time to
    time. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage that is not
    a sheltered waters voyage, an inland voyage, a near coastal voyage,
    Class 2 or a near coastal voyage, Class 1. ([voyage
    illimité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of interpreting the provisions of SOLAS that are incorporated
  by reference into these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "should" is to be read as "must" and any
    recommendation or suggestion is to be read as an obligation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Administration" is to be read as "Minister";

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "ship" is to be read as "vessel"; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} "survey" is to be read as "inspection".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inconsistency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between a provision
  in SOLAS and a provision in these Regulations, the provision in these
  Regulations prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

## [Application and Prescribed Class]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298624 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian vessels and foreign vessels

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of Canadian vessels everywhere and
  foreign vessels in Canadian waters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activities relating to oil or gas

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations apply in respect of a vessel
  referred to in subsection (1) that is capable of engaging in the
  drilling for, or the production, conservation or processing of, oil or
  gas.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed class --- section 119 of the
Act

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A Canadian vessel in
respect of which these Regulations apply is a vessel of a prescribed
class for the purposes of section 119 of the Act.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Canadian Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298632 .Part}

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Safety Convention Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1298633 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Division applies in
respect of Canadian vessels that are Safety Convention vessels.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety certificate

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A vessel must hold the
applicable safety certificates issued under section 7 to engage on a
voyage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of safety certificate

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by the authorized representative of a vessel, the Minister
  must issue the applicable safety certificates referred to in SOLAS to
  the vessel if the vessel meets the requirements of regulations made
  under Part 4 of the Act in respect of the following that apply to the
  vessel when it is engaged in its intended service:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's structure and its main and auxiliary
    machinery, boilers and other pressure vessels;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} watertight subdivision arrangements and details;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subdivision load lines;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} structural fire protection, fire safety systems and
    appliances and fire control plans;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} life-saving appliances and equipment for lifeboats,
    life rafts and rescue boats;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} line-throwing appliances and radio installations
    used in life-saving appliances;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} radio installations;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} shipborne navigational equipment, means of
    embarkation for pilots, and nautical charts and publications; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} lights, shapes and means of making distress and
    sound signals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections and endorsements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a vessel that holds
  a safety certificate must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel undergoes all inspections required in
    respect of the applicable certificate under Part B of Chapter I of
    SOLAS; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if Part B of Chapter I of SOLAS requires that the
    certificate be endorsed following the inspection, the certificate is
    endorsed by the Minister, in accordance with that Part, to indicate
    that the requirements for the issuance of the certificate continue
    to be met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption certificate

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** On application by the
authorized representative of a vessel, the Minister must issue an
exemption certificate to the vessel if an exemption has been granted by
the Marine Technical Review Board in accordance with section 28 of the
Act in respect of the requirements that must be met for the issuance of
a safety certificate under section 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuous synopsis record

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by the authorized representative of a vessel for a
  continuous synopsis record referred to in Regulation 5 of Chapter XI-1
  of SOLAS, the Minister must issue the record to the vessel in
  accordance with that Regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information in application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must contain the information referred
  to in Regulation 5.3 of Chapter XI-1 of SOLAS.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update to information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there is a change that affects the information
  that is contained in the continuous synopsis record, the authorized
  representative must, as soon as feasible,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the Minister in writing of the change;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a new application for an updated version of
    the continuous synopsis record; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the information referred to in
    Regulations 5.3.4 to 5.3.12 of Chapter XI-1 of SOLAS, amend the
    continuous synopsis record to reflect the change.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition from altering continuous
  synopsis record

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to paragraph (3)(c), the authorized
  representative must ensure that the continuous synopsis record is not
  altered or defaced and that any information contained in it is not
  modified or removed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel transferred --- continuous
  synopsis record on board

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative must ensure that the
  continuous synopsis record is left on board when the vessel is
  transferred to the flag of another state, is sold or another company
  assumes the responsibility for its operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessel transferred --- information to
  Minister

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel is transferred to the flag of another
  state, the authorized representative must, as soon as feasible after
  the transfer, inform the Minister of the name of the state to allow
  the Minister to forward to that state a copy of each continuous
  synopsis record issued by the Minister to that vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of authorized
representative

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a vessel that holds a safety certificate must ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all safety certificates that are required by section
  6 are kept on board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document that is required to be attached to the
  certificates under SOLAS and the regulations made under Part 4 of the
  Act is kept on board;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any exemption certificate that is referred to in
  section 8 is kept on board; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the authorized representative has applied for an
  updated continuous synopsis record under paragraph 9(3)(b) and the
  authorized representative has not yet received the updated record, a
  copy of the application is kept on board.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Vessels that are not Safety Convention Vessels]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1298678 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Division applies in respect of the following Canadian vessels that are
  not Safety Convention vessels:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} vessels of 15 gross tonnage or less that carry more
    than 12 passengers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels of more than 15 gross tonnage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Division does not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels to which the Special-purpose Vessel
    Regulations apply;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} human-powered vessels; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that does not have a mechanical means of
    propulsion and does not carry persons, dangerous chemicals in bulk
    or oil in bulk on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[dangerous
  chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (2)(d), [[dangerous
  chemical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
  1(1) of the [Vessel Pollution and Dangerous Chemicals
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety certificate

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A vessel must hold the
applicable safety certificates issued under section 13 to engage on a
voyage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of safety certificate

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application by the authorized representative of a vessel, the Minister
  must issue the applicable safety certificates to the vessel if the
  vessel meets the requirements of regulations made under Part 4 of the
  Act in respect of the following that apply to the vessel when it is
  engaged in its intended service:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's structure and its main and auxiliary
    machinery, boilers and other pressure vessels;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} watertight subdivision arrangements and details;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subdivision load lines;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} structural fire protection, fire safety systems and
    appliances and fire control plans;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} life-saving appliances and equipment for lifeboats,
    life rafts and rescue boats;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} line-throwing appliances and radio installations
    used in life-saving appliances;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} shipborne navigational equipment, means of
    embarkation for pilots, and nautical charts and publications; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} lights, shapes and means of making distress and
    sound signals.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections and endorsements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a vessel that holds
  a safety certificate that is valid for more than one year must ensure
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is inspected no later than a date that
    is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} determined by the Minister after considering the
      vessel's type and size and the class of voyage on which the vessel
      may engage, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} indicated on the certificate at the time it is
      issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the certificate is endorsed by the Minister
    following the inspection to indicate that the requirements for the
    issuance of the certificate continue to be met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inland voyage safety certificate

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  vessel that holds a safety certificate for an inland voyage may also
  engage on a sheltered waters voyage that is within the waters
  described in paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition [[inland
  voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Near coastal voyage, Class 2 safety
  certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that holds a safety certificate for a near
  coastal voyage, Class 2 may also engage on a sheltered waters voyage
  and an inland voyage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Near coastal voyage, Class 1 safety
  certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that holds a safety certificate for a near
  coastal voyage, Class 1 may also engage on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sheltered waters voyage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inland voyage; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a near coastal voyage, Class 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unlimited voyage safety certificate

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A vessel that holds a safety certificate for an
  unlimited voyage may also engage on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sheltered waters voyage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inland voyage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a near coastal voyage, Class 2; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a near coastal voyage, Class 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of authorized
representative

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a vessel that holds a safety certificate must ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all safety certificates that are required by section
  12 are kept on board; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any document that is required to be attached to the
  certificates under the regulations made under Part 4 of the Act is
  kept on board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister
determines that terms and conditions on a safety certificate issued to a
vessel are necessary based on the vessel's construction, equipment,
stability and design criteria, the Minister may impose terms and
conditions that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} restrict a vessel's intended service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} limit the voyage on which the vessel may engage after
  considering the following criteria with respect to the vessel's
  intended area of operation:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} wave height,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} wind velocity,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} current,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} visibility,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} proximity to assistance, a place of refuge or
    shore,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} navigation hazards,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} communications coverage,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} average depth of the water,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} water temperature,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} air temperature,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} maximum tide range, and

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} ice conditions.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Reports and Inspections]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1298747 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a Canadian vessel must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if an inspection report indicates that a
    requirement referred to in subsection 7(1) or 13(1) has not been met
    and a period is set out in the inspection report to comply with the
    requirement, submit to the Minister, within that period,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a report, in the form specified by the Minister,
      that specifies the measures taken to comply with the requirement,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} documents demonstrating the measures taken; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a modification or an alteration made to the
    vessel, or damage caused to the vessel, results in the vessel no
    longer meeting all of the requirements referred to in subsection
    7(1) or 13(1), submit to the Minister, without delay,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a report, in the form specified by the Minister,
      that describes the modification, alteration or damage and
      specifies the measures subsequently taken to comply with the
      requirements, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} documents demonstrating the measures taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confirmation from Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Following the submission of the report and the
  documents, the authorized representative must ensure that they receive
  a written confirmation from the Minister that the requirements for the
  issuance of the certificate continue to be met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and inspection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In order to provide the confirmation, the Minister
  must review the report and the documents provided by the authorized
  representative and, if the Minister determines that it is necessary,
  inspect the vessel.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Foreign Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298761 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety Convention vessels

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  authorized representative of a foreign vessel that is a Safety
  Convention vessel must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all safety certificates that are required by
      SOLAS are kept on board,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any document that is required by SOLAS to be
      attached to a safety certificate is kept on board,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any exemption certificate that is referred to
      in Regulation 12(a)(vii) of Part B of Chapter I of SOLAS is kept
      on board, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the authorized representative has applied to
      its flag state for an updated continuous synopsis record and the
      authorized representative has not yet received the updated record,
      a copy of the application is kept on board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be able to demonstrate that the requirements for
    the issuance of the certificates and documents referred to in
    subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) continue to be met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of authorized
  representative

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a foreign vessel
  that holds a safety certificate that is required by SOLAS must ensure
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel undergoes all inspections required in
    respect of the applicable certificate under Part B of Chapter I of
    SOLAS; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if Part B of Chapter I of SOLAS requires that the
    certificate be endorsed following the inspection, the certificate is
    endorsed, in accordance with that Part, to indicate that the
    requirements for the issuance of the certificate continue to be met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessels that are not Safety Convention
vessels

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a foreign vessel that is not a Safety Convention
vessel must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that a document issued by or under the
  authority of the vessel's flag state that indicates that the vessel
  complies with the safety requirements of its flag state is kept on
  board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be able to demonstrate that the requirements for the
  issuance of that document continue to be met; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the vessel's design, construction and
  equipment meet a level of safety that is equivalent to or higher than
  that required by regulations made under Part 4 of the Act for Canadian
  vessels that are of a similar size and type.
