## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566802 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of fishing in the Canadian fisheries
  waters off the Yukon Territory and in the waters of the Yukon
  Territory other than the waters of a National Park of Canada located
  in that Territory.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in these Regulations,
  these Regulations, other than subsections (1) and 9(3) and --- to the
  extent that they relate to a contravention of subsection 9(3) ---
  sections 10 and 11, do not apply with respect to fishing and related
  activities carried out under the authority of a licence issued under
  the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332).

## [Licences and Conservation Cards --- General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566808 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (3), section 4.1 and subsection 5.1(1), no person shall
  fish, engage in fish farming or hold a derby except under the
  authority of a licence issued under these Regulations, the [Fishery
  (General) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) or the [Aboriginal
  Communal Fishing Licences Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 6(14.1), on receipt of an
  application for a licence and, where applicable, a Salmon Conservation
  Catch Card as set out in column I of an item of Schedule III, together
  with the applicable fee set out in column II of that item, the
  Minister may issue the licence or the Salmon Conservation Catch Card.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person under 16 years of age may engage in angling
  without a licence if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a Yukon resident; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is accompanied by a person who is the holder of an
    angling licence or a sport fishing licence issued under these
    Regulations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All fish caught by a person under 16 years of age
  shall be counted as part of the catch of the licence holder referred
  to in paragraph 3(b).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The person under 16 years of age referred to in
  subsection (3) shall, at the request of a fishery officer or fishery
  guardian, produce proof of age.

## [National Fishing Week]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566819 .Part}

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  Yukon resident may engage in angling without a licence

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period that begins on the last Friday in
    June and ends at midnight on the Monday immediately following that
    Friday, if July 1 falls within that period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period that begins on the first Friday
    in July and ends at midnight on the Monday immediately following
    that Friday, if July 1 does not fall within the period referred to
    in paragraph (a).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to angling by a
  participant in a derby.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person engaged in angling without a licence during
  a period referred to in subsection (1) shall, at the request of a
  fishery officer or fishery guardian, produce proof that the person is
  a Yukon resident.

- SOR/2008-152, s. 2

## [Inuvialuit Subsistence Fishery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566829 .Part}

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 4(1), a
  beneficiary may fish for subsistence usage within the Inuvialuit
  Settlement Region without a licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection 9(4) and subject to
  section 12 of the Agreement, a beneficiary may sell to or trade or
  barter with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} another beneficiary, fish or any part thereof taken
    for subsistence usage from the Inuvialuit Settlement Region; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person, the nonedible by-products of fish
    taken from the Inuvialuit Settlement Region by the beneficiary for
    personal use.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than a beneficiary, shall possess
  any edible portions of fish taken for subsistence usage from the
  Inuvialuit Settlement Region.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the Inuvialuit Settlement Region, subsection 9(1)
  does not apply to a beneficiary.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} between December 1st and April 30th, for more than
    72 consecutive hours,

  therein.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566972 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to these Regulations, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} molest, injure or kill any fish;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} trap or pen fish in their spawning grounds or in
    any river or stream leading thereto;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} use torches or artificial lights to attract fish;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} catch or attempt to catch fish by snagging or with
    snares or set lines;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use firearms to kill or injure finfish;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/93-60, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} use a dip net except when landing a fish caught by
    angling;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/93-60, s. 8\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} use a gaff or a spear while fishing; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} use a gill net that contains monofilament in its
    web while fishing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2.1), no person shall, in any
  waters, catch and retain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whitefish, cisco, lake trout or inconnu from
    September 1st to October 31st in any year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} grayling from May 1st to June 15th in any year.

- [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds an angling licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is fishing under the authority of a licence issued
    under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} holds any other licence listed in subsection 4(3)
    if the licence specifies any fish referred to in subsection (2) that
    may be caught and retained during the applicable period set out in
    subsection (2); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a beneficiary who is fishing for subsistence
    usage within the Inuvialuit Settlement Region.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bring into the Yukon Territory for use as bait any
    live fish, salamander or other aquatic organism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} release into any waters of the Yukon Territory any
    live fish unless that fish is released

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the waters from which it was taken, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a licence issued pursuant to
      section 56 of the [Fishery (General)
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} release into any waters of the Yukon Territory any
    live salamander or other live aquatic organism, that is not a fish,
    unless that salamander or other aquatic organism was taken from
    waters of the Yukon Territory;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} possess any live fish for use as bait in the Yukon
    Territory; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} use any live fish as bait in the Yukon Territory.
