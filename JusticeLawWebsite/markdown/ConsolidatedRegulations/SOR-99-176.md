**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of clause
173(1)(d)(vi)(A) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), where a
registrant supplies property or a service and an amount in respect
thereof is required to be included in computing an individual's income
for the purposes of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) for a taxation
year, the prescribed percentage of the total consideration that includes
that amount is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 9% if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual is an employee of the registrant and
    is required under subsection 6(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the last
    establishment of the registrant at which the individual ordinarily
    worked, or to which the individual ordinarily reported, in the year
    in relation to that office or employment is located in Ontario, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the individual is a shareholder of the registrant,
    is required under subsection 15(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the individual is
    resident in Ontario at the end of the year;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} 10% if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual is an employee of the registrant and
    is required under subsection 6(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the last
    establishment of the registrant at which the individual ordinarily
    worked, or to which the individual ordinarily reported, in the year
    in relation to that office or employment is located in Nova Scotia,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the individual is a shareholder of the registrant,
    is required under subsection 15(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the individual is
    resident in Nova Scotia at the end of the year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 11% if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual is an employee of the registrant and
    is required under subsection 6(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the last
    establishment of the registrant at which the individual ordinarily
    worked, or to which the individual ordinarily reported, in the year
    in relation to that office or employment is located in New
    Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and Labrador, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the individual is a shareholder of the registrant,
    is required under subsection 15(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) to so include the amount and the individual is
    resident in New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island or Newfoundland and
    Labrador at the end of the year; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-191, s. 19\]]{.Repealed}

- [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-212, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 3%, in any other case.
