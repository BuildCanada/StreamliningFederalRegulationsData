## Soquip Alberta Inc. (Petroleum and Gas Revenue Tax Act) Remission Order {#soquip-alberta-inc.-petroleum-and-gas-revenue-tax-act-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-163
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-11-21

Order Respecting the Remission of Amounts Payable under the Petroleum
and Gas Revenue Tax Act by Soquip Alberta Inc.

P.C. 1990-2397  1990-11-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of amounts payable
under the Petroleum and Gas Revenue Tax Act by Soquip Alberta
Inc.](/eng/regulations/SI-90-163)
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642721 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Soquip Alberta Inc. (Petroleum and Gas Revenue Tax Act) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-90-163).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642723 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Petroleum and Gas Revenue Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/P-12); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Soquip Alberta Inc.;
    ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of amounts of tax that have been
      assessed under the Act as payable by the Corporation

    exceeds

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of all amounts deemed to have been
      paid by the Corporation under the Act and other similar amounts.
      ([impôt net]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642731 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to the Corporation of the net tax to the extent of the grants paid by
the Corporation or the Province of Quebec to the Receiver General for
Canada within 30 days after the date on which this Order is made.

## [Remission of Interest and Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642733 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to the Corporation of the interest and penalties payable in respect of
the net tax remitted pursuant to section 3.
