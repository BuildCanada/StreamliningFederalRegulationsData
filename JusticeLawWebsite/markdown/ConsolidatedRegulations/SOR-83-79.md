## Carriage by Air Act Gold Franc Conversion Regulations {#carriage-by-air-act-gold-franc-conversion-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-79
:::

[CURRENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-52)

Registration 1983-01-14

Regulations Respecting Conversion of Values Expressed in Gold Francs
into Canadian Dollar Equivalents for Purposes of Subsection 2(6) of the
Carriage by Air Act

P.C. 1983-19 1983-01-13
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 13.1 of the Currency and
Exchange Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations
respecting conversion of values expressed in gold francs into Canadian
dollar equivalents for purposes of subsection 2(6) of the [Carriage by
Air Act](/eng/acts/C-26).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881495 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Carriage by Air Act Gold Franc Conversion Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881497 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[gold francs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gold francs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the francs referred to in
    subsection 2(6) of the [Carriage by Air Act](/eng/acts/C-26);
    ([francs-or]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[S.D.R.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[S.D.R.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the special drawing rights
    issued by the International Monetary Fund.
    ([D.T.S.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-881501 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 2(6) of the [Carriage by Air Act](/eng/acts/C-26), the
equivalent dollar value of gold francs shall be determined as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} gold francs shall be converted into S.D.R.\'s at the
  exchange rate of 15.075 gold francs per S.D.R.; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} S.D.R.\'s shall be converted into Canadian dollars at
  the exchange rate established by the International Monetary Fund for
  S.D.R.\'s and Canadian dollars.
