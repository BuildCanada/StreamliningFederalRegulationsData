## [Recovery of Allowances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011269 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  respecting recovery must be sent to the concerned person setting out
  the amounts referred to in subsection 56(3) and section 63.1 of the
  Act, or the amount of a reduction referred to in subsection 59.1(6) of
  the Act, and the manner in which the amounts may be recovered under
  this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person who receives a notice must elect, within
  45 days after the date of the notice,

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} to pay the specified amounts in a lump sum; or

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} to arrange to pay the specified amounts by monthly
    deductions from their allowance or other benefit payable

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the deductions are made under paragraph
      56(3)(b) or section 63.1 of the Act, over the period required to
      pay the amounts by monthly deductions equal to 10% of the
      aggregate of the gross monthly amounts payable to the person under
      the Act as at the date the notice is sent under subsection (1), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the deductions are made under subsection
      59.1(6) of the Act --- calculated in accordance with the mortality
      rates used in the preparation of the actuarial valuation report
      that was most recently laid before Parliament, in accordance with
      subsection 65(1) of the Act, with interest at the rate of 4%
      compounded annually --- during the shorter of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the lifetime of the person, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the period required to pay the amounts by
        monthly deductions equal to 10% of the aggregate of the gross
        monthly amounts payable to the person under the Act as at the
        date the notice is sent under subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an election is not made within the time limit
  under subsection (2), recovery must be made by monthly deductions as
  set out in subparagraphs (2)(b)(i) or (ii), as applicable.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The deductions referred to in paragraph (2)(b) must
  be made in equal amounts --- with the first to be made in the month
  following the day on which the 45-day period under subsection (2) ends
  and the other deductions every subsequent month --- with the exception
  of the last deduction, which may be less in amount than the preceding
  deductions.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any person who has elected or who is deemed to have
  elected to pay monthly deductions may at any time

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the remaining outstanding amount in one lump
    sum; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrange

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the monthly deductions determined under
      paragraph (2)(b) to be increased, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to pay a lump sum and pay the remaining
      outstanding amount by monthly deductions determined under
      paragraph (2)(b) and payable within the same or a lesser period
      than that originally used.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the reduction that may be recovered
  under subsection 59.1(6) and section 63.1 of the Act must be recovered
  in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from allowances or other benefits payable under
    Part I of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from supplementary benefits payable under Part IV
    of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} from allowances or other benefits payable under
    Part II of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} from allowances payable under Part III of the Act.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where the amount of the monthly deductions made
  pursuant to paragraph (2)(b) causes financial hardship to the person,
  the Minister may reduce that amount but it may not be less than 5 per
  cent of the aggregate of the gross monthly amounts payable to that
  person under the Act as at the date the notice was given.

## [Reconciliation of Amounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1011294 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must perform a reconciliation of amounts of the reduction
  made under subsection 59.1(5) of the Act and make appropriate
  adjustments by way of recovery or refund

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on receipt of the notifications specified in
    paragraph 59.1(3)(b) or (c) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on each anniversary date referred to in subsection
    59.1(5) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the anniversary
  date referred to in subsection 59.1(5) of the Act is not changed if a
  former member commences to hold a federal position or enters into a
  federal service contract within the 12-month period following that
  date.
