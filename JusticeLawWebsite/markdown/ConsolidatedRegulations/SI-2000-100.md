## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604609 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vuntut Gwitchin First
    Nation Self-Government Agreement signed on May 29, 1993, as it read
    on November 2, 2000. ([accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    123(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604613 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a person, to the extent and in the manner that a refund of tax paid
by the person is provided for in sections 15.7 to 15.11 of the
agreement, of tax under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
that was paid by the person during the period beginning on October 1,
1997 and ending on October 31, 2000, on condition that no refund of that
tax is payable under section 18.1 of the [Yukon First Nations
Self-Government Act](/eng/acts/Y-2.6).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604615 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on November 2, 2000.
