Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote

## [Prescribed Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757889 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The circumstances referred
to in section 147 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an actual or anticipated shortage of fuel exists and
  one of the following measures has been made or issued:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a proclamation by the Governor in Council under
    subsection 6(1), 17(1), 28(1) or 38(1) of the [Emergencies
    Act](/eng/acts/E-4.5),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an order by the Governor in Council under
    subsection 15(1) of the [Energy Supplies Emergency
    Act](/eng/acts/E-9), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a declaration of emergency in a province, by a
    responsible provincial authority duly authorized by the applicable
    provincial Act regarding emergency situations or civil protection;
    or

  - [(iv) to (xv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-246, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an actual or anticipated shortage of fuel exists and
  the Minister of National Defence advised the Minister in writing that
  the actual or anticipated shortage of fuel affects or could affect the
  Government of Canada's ability to protect national security, support
  humanitarian relief efforts, participate in multilateral military or
  peace-keeping activities under the auspices of international
  organizations or defend a member state of the North Atlantic Treaty
  Organization.
