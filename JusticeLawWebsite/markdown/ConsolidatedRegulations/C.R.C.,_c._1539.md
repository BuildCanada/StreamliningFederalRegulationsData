## Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Sawmill Bay N.W.T.) from Disposal Order {#withdrawal-of-certain-lands-sawmill-bay-n.w.t.-from-disposal-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1539
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Order Withdrawing from Disposal Certain Lands in the Northwest
Territories for the Purpose of an Airstrip
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Withdrawal of Certain Lands (Sawmill Bay N.W.T.) from Disposal
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1539).

## [Withdrawal from Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-527615 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby ordered that
the surface of the parcel of land described in the schedule be withdrawn
from disposal under the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) for the
purpose of an airstrip.

::::: section
:::: {#527617 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-527618 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that parcel of land at Sawmill Bay, in the Northwest Territories,
required for an airstrip as said airstrip is shown bordered in red on a
sketch plan of record on file 5-2-28 in the Resources Division of the
Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development at Ottawa.
