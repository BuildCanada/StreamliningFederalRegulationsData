## Edmonton International Airport Zoning Regulations {#edmonton-international-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 81
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Edmonton International Airport
::::

:::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565248 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Edmonton International Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._81).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565250 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Edmonton International
    Airport, in the Province of Alberta; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane extending upward and outward from each end of a strip along
    and at right angles to the projected centre line thereof, which
    approach surface is more particularly described in Part II of the
    schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
    located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
    outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
    schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
    take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
    strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the schedule;
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending  upward and outward from the lateral limits
    of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional surface is
    more particularly described in Part V of the schedule. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1005, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 712.3 m above
sea level.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1005, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565264 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport that consist of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of an approach
  surface that extends beyond,

the outer limits described in Part VI of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1005, s. 3
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565271 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point any of the surfaces hereinafter set out that
project immediately over and above the surface of the land at that
location, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565276 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 5, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object is
growing remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-170, s. 1
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565280 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit that land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-1005, s. 4
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point is a point coincidental with Transport
Canada coordinate control survey monument No. 710323 located at latitude
53°18′33.9″ N., longitude 113°34′57″ W.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Edmonton International Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.1874 dated February 5, 1985, are surfaces abutting each end
of the strips associated with the runways designated 01L-19R, 01R-19L,
11L-29R and 11R-29L and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 01L,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 19R,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 01R,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 19L,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 11L,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 29R,

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 11R, and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 29L,

consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of 1 m measured
vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an imaginary
horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected centre line of
the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally from the end of the
strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being 2 400 m
from the projected centre line; said imaginary horizontal line being
300 m measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Edmonton International Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1874 dated February 5, 1985, is an imaginary surface located at a
common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
assigned elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where
that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the
imaginary surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Edmonton International Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.1874 dated February 5, 1985, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 01L-19R, being 300 m
  in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  2 863 m in length;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 01R-19L, being 300 m
  in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  3 473 m in length;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 11L-29R, being 300 m
  in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  3 229 m in length; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 11R-29L being 300 m
  in width, 150 m on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  3 777 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Edmonton International Airport
Zoning Plan E.1874 dated February 5, 1985, is a surface consisting of an
inclined plane having a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and centre line produced
of each strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits
of each strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the
outer surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
