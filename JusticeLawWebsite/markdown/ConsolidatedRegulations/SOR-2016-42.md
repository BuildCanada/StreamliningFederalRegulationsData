## Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations {#order-extending-the-application-of-the-freezing-assets-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-tunisia-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-42
:::

[FREEZING ASSETS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT](/eng/acts/F-31.6)

Registration 2016-03-11

Order Extending the Application of the Freezing Assets of Corrupt
Foreign Officials (Tunisia) Regulations

P.C. 2016-125 2016-03-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 6 of the [Freezing
Assets of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act](/eng/acts/F-31.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16607){#fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16607-ID0EECAA},
extends the application of the [Freezing Assets of Corrupt Foreign
Officials (Tunisia) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-78)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16608){#fn_81000-2-2665-E_hq_16608-ID0EBCAA}
for a period of five years beginning on March 24, 2016.
