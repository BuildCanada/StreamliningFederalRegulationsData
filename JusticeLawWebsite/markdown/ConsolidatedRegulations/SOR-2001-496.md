## Special Duty Area Pension Order {#special-duty-area-pension-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-496
:::

[PENSION ACT](/eng/acts/P-6)

Registration 2001-11-08

Special Duty Area Pension Order

P.C. 2001-2085  2001-11-08
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 91.1(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2001-496_e_hq_5193){#fn_SOR-2001-496_e_hq_5193-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), hereby makes the annexed [Special
Duty Area Pension Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-496).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2001-496_e_hq_5193-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR-2001-496_e_hq_5193}S.C.
  2000, c. 34, s. 40
  :::
:::

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677239 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
[Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), an area set out in column 1 of the
schedule is designated as a special duty area in respect of the period
commencing on the date set out in column 2 and terminating on the date
set out in column 3.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677241 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-677243 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
