## Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service (Somalia) {#order-placing-members-of-the-canadian-forces-on-active-service-somalia .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-169
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1992-09-23

Order Placing Members of the Canadian Forces on Active Service (Somalia)

P.C. 1992-2006  1992-09-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 751 of
April 24, 1992, established a United Nations Operation in Somalia to
promote the process of reconciliation and political settlement in
Somalia and to provide urgent humanitarian assistance;

Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 775 of
August 28, 1992, authorized an increase in the strength, and the
deployment, of the United Nations Operation in Somalia;

And Whereas it appears advisable that Canada participate in that
Operation;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to
paragraph 31(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-169_e_hq_6503){#fn_SI-92-169_e_hq_6503-ID0EDDAA}
of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), is pleased hereby to place
on active service beyond Canada those

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-169_e_hq_6503-ID0EDDAA){#fn_SI-92-169_e_hq_6503}R.S., c.
  31 (1st Supp.), s. 60 (Sch. I, item 14)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the regular
  force of the Canadian Forces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the reserve
  force of the Canadian Forces who are part of, or serve in immediate
  support of, the United Nations Operation in Somalia.
