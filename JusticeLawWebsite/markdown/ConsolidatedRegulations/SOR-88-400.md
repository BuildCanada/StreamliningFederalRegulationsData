## Voyageur Colonial Limited Acquisition Exemption Order {#voyageur-colonial-limited-acquisition-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-400
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1988-07-21

Order Exempting Certain Specified Acquisitions by Voyageur Colonial
Limited from the Application of Part VII of the National Transportation
Act, 1987

P.C. 1988-1481 1988-07-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the National Transportation Agency, pursuant to subsection 253(6) of the
National Transportation Act, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
approve the annexed [Order exempting certain specified acquisitions by
Voyageur Colonial Limited from the application of Part VII of the
National Transportation Act, 1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-400), made by
the National Transportation Agency.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1987, c. 34
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912737 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Voyageur Colonial Limited Acquisition Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-400).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-912739 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The acquisition by Voyageur
Colonial Limited of all shares in C.F. Kingsway Inc. held by Groupe CSL
Inc. is hereby exempted from the application of Part VII of the National
Transportation Act, 1987.
