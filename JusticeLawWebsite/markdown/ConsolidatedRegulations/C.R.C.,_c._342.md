## Railway Passenger Services Adjustment Assistance Regulations {#railway-passenger-services-adjustment-assistance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 342
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1977

Regulations Respecting the Implementation of Adjustment Assistance to
Railway Companies and Employees Affected by Changes in Railway Passenger
Services
::::

:::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549945 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Railway Passenger Services Adjustment Assistance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._342).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549947 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[benefits]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the benefits and expenses
      referred to in subparagraph (c)(ii) of Department of Transport
      Vote 52d, Appropriation Act No. 1, 1977;
      ([bénéfices]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[changes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[changes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means changes in the provision,
      management or operation of selected Railway Passenger Services as
      a result of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the implementation of the provisions of the
        contract between the Minister of Transport and VIA Rail Canada
        Inc., entered into pursuant to Department of Transport Vote 52d
        of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1977, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the discontinuance of a Railway Passenger
        Service; ([changements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the cost incurred by a railway
      company with respect to the provision of benefits;
      ([coûts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[government arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[government arrangement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      arrangement between the Minister of Transport and a railway
      company with respect to the reimbursement of the prescribed
      portion of the cost; ([entente gouvernementale]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[prescribed portion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescribed portion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion
      prescribed from time to time by the Governor in Council;
      ([proportion déterminée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Railway Passenger Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Railway Passenger Service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      passenger train service that does not accommodate principally
      persons who commute between points on the railway; ([service de
      transport ferroviaire de passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
      between a railway company or railway companies and a trade union
      or trade unions with respect to the provision of benefits that
      result from the special agreement process; ([accord
      spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[special agreement process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[special agreement process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      negotiating process between a railway company and a trade union
      commenced with the undertaking between the parties that the
      arbitration provisions set out in these Regulations will apply to
      and be binding upon the parties in the event of the dispute
      between them on any matter arising in respect of the benefits and
      conditions referred to in subsection 5(2); ([processus d'accord
      spécial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trade union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trade union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee organization
      certified or recognized as bargaining agent for employees of a
      railway company. ([syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any word or expression used in these Regulations that
  is not defined in subsection (1) shall have the same meaning as in the
  Railway Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-701, s. 1
- SOR/88-401, s. 1
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549964 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Transport
may reimburse a railway company for the prescribed portion of the cost
incurred by the company for the provision of benefits where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cost results from changes implemented during the
  period beginning on March 29, 1977 and ending on December 31, 1990;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a special agreement exists between the railway
  company and a trade union or trade unions; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a government arrangement exists between the railway
  company and the Minister of Transport.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-25, s. 1
- SOR/85-701, s. 2
- SOR/86-56, s. 1
:::

## [Special Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549971 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In negotiating a special
agreement, the parties to the special agreement process shall, inasmuch
as the following are generally incorporated in their existing job
security agreements, give consideration to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in so far as possible, ensuring continuing employment
  for the employees concerned;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where preferred and to the extent possible, keeping
  employees in gainful employment at the same location;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where necessary, training employees for alternative
  employment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} when required, providing appropriate assistance in
  relocation;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in so far as possible, avoiding loss of employees'
  earnings;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} developing a separation plan for the assistance of
  employees close to or eligible for retirement who wish to leave the
  work force;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} minimizing seniority obstacles for the purpose of
  facilitating

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} continuing employment by Canadian National Railway
    Company, Canadian Pacific Railway Limited, Toronto Terminal Railway
    Company or Toronto, Hamilton and Buffalo Railway Company, where
    mutually agreed to by the parties, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transfer of employees to VIA Rail Canada Inc.;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} where employees are laid off, providing reasonable
  weekly lay-off benefits or severance payments; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} assisting employees unable to maintain their jobs to
  secure employment outside the railway industry.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-735, s. 1
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  special agreement shall provide for the benefits and the terms and
  conditions of those benefits contained in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Railway Job Security, Technological, Operational,
    Organizational Changes Agreements between Canadian National Railway
    Company, Canadian Pacific Railway Limited and non-operating
    shop-craft or other unions; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Railway Material Change Agreements between Canadian
    National Railway Company, Canadian Pacific Railway Limited and
    United Transportation Union and Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A special agreement may provide for benefits and
  conditions in addition to those referred to in subsection (1),
  consistent with the principles referred to in section 4.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A special agreement shall
be the only instrument applicable with respect to benefits and the
words, terms and conditions of such an instrument shall not be amended,
revised or otherwise changed without

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a joint request from the parties to the special
  agreement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the consent, in writing, of the Minister of Labour.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A special agreement shall
not provide for benefits to employees other than those adversely
affected by the implementation of changes.

## [Arbitration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-549995 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in negotiating a special agreement, a settlement has not been reached
  by the parties to the special agreement process (hereinafter referred
  to as "the parties") on any issue arising under subsection 5(2) within
  60 days after the date a railway company has served notice on a trade
  union, either party may inform the Minister of Labour, by notice in
  writing, of their failure to reach a settlement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within a reasonable period after his receipt of a
  notice referred to in subsection (1), the Minister of Labour or a
  representative designated by him shall confer with the parties in
  order to assist them in reaching a settlement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister of Labour or his representative is
  unsuccessful in assisting the parties in reaching a settlement, either
  party may, within seven days following the conference with the
  Minister of Labour or his representative, request, by notice in
  writing to the other party, that the matter be referred to a single
  arbitrator.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where, within seven days of a request referred to in
  subsection (3), the parties cannot agree on the selection of an
  arbitrator, either party may submit to the Minister of Labour an
  application to appoint an arbitrator.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Within a reasonable period after his receipt of an
  application referred to in subsection (4), the Minister of Labour
  shall select and appoint an arbitrator.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} On the appointment of an arbitrator, the parties
  shall prepare a joint statement of any issues arising under subsection
  5(2) that remain in dispute and shall submit that statement to the
  arbitrator prior to the hearing.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Within 21 days of his appointment the arbitrator
  shall convene a hearing of the parties.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Within 60 days of his appointment the arbitrator
  shall make an award.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} An arbitral award shall be final and binding on the
  parties and for the purposes of these Regulations shall be deemed to
  be an integral part of the special agreement.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} An arbitral award, notwithstanding a decision to the
  contrary by the arbitrator as to its duration, shall be the only
  arbitral award applicable in respect of any matter referred to
  arbitration under this section.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Any time limit established pursuant to this section
  may be extended by agreement between the parties.

## [Non-unionized Employees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550008 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  non-unionized employees have been adversely affected by the
  implementation of changes, they shall be provided with benefits
  consistent with existing railway policies and practices and may be
  provided with additional benefits.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every railway company affected by changes in Railway
  Passenger Services shall provide a detailed statement of the benefits
  to be provided to non-unionized employees pursuant to subsection (1)
  to the Minister of Transport and that statement shall, for the
  purposes of these Regulations, be considered to be a special
  agreement.

## [Government Arrangement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550012 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  government arrangement shall not provide for the reimbursement of a
  greater portion of the cost than the prescribed portion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A government arrangement shall not be executed until
  the appropriate special agreement has been signed by the parties to
  the special agreement.

## [Reimbursement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550016 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Reimbursement to a
railway company of the prescribed portion of the cost shall be made in
accordance with the appropriate government arrangement.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550018 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A railway company shall
keep detailed records of all transactions made by the company with
respect to cost and shall make the records available for inspection by
the Minister of Transport or his authorized representative on an agreed
periodic basis.
