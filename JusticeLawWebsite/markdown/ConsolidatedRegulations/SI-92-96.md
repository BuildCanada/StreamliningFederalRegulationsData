## Saskatchewan Liquor Board Employees' Remission Order {#saskatchewan-liquor-board-employees-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-96
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-05-20

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax Payable by Employees of the
Saskatchewan Liquor Board on Strike Pay

P.C. 1992-943  1992-05-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-96_e_hq_6499){#fn_SI-92-96_e_hq_6499-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of income tax
payable by employees of the Saskatchewan Liquor Board on strike
pay](/eng/regulations/SI-92-96).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645720 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Saskatchewan Liquor Board Employees' Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-92-96).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645722 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strike pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strike pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means strike pay that was
    received in respect of the strike against the Saskatchewan Liquor
    Board that began on November 26, 1979 and ended on December 17,
    1979; ([allocation de grève]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[taxpayer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[taxpayer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a taxpayer

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who was a member of the Liquor Board Branch of
      the Saskatchewan Government Employees' Union,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose income, for the purposes of an assessment
      under the Act, included an amount of strike pay that was received
      in 1979, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who did not object to the assessment referred to
      in paragraph (b). ([contribuable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645730 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a taxpayer of taxes, interest and penalties payable under Parts I and
I.1 of the Act by the taxpayer that would not have been payable if
strike pay had not been included in the computation of the taxpayer's
income for the purposes of an assessment under the Act.
