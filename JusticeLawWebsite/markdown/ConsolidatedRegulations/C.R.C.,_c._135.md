## Alberta Turkey Marketing Levies Order {#alberta-turkey-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 135
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collecting of Levies from
Certain Turkey Producers in Alberta
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503939 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Turkey Marketing Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._135).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503941 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Marketing of Agricultural
    Products Act of Alberta; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Turkey
    Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[market allotment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the total quantity of
    turkey meat that a producer is entitled to market in
    intraprovincial, interprovincial and export trade pursuant to the
    Canadian Turkey Marketing Quota Regulations and the Orders made by
    the Commodity Board pursuant to the Plan; ([allocation de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Turkey Growers'
    Marketing Plan, established and amended from time to time pursuant
    to the Act and any Regulations made thereunder to give effect to the
    Plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    production of turkeys in the Province of Alberta;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a turkey of any variety, grade
    or class that is raised or kept for slaughter within the Province of
    Alberta and sold for slaughter. ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503949 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, in addition to the licence fees set out under the
Plan, levies at the following rates:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$0.50 for each broiler turkey sold by him that is in
  excess of his annual or periodic market allotment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$0.77 for each heavy breed hen turkey sold by him
  that is in excess of his annual or periodic market allotment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$1.27 for each heavy breed tom turkey sold by him
  that is in excess of his annual or periodic market allotment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$1.02 for each heavy breed turkey sold by him as
  part of an unsexed flock that is in excess of his annual or periodic
  market allotment.

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503955 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who buys turkeys from a producer shall deduct from the moneys
  payable to the producer for the turkeys all levies payable by the
  producer to the Commodity Board under section 3 and shall pay such
  levies to the Commodity Board at its office at 11826-100th Avenue,
  Edmonton, Alberta, not later than the 15th day of the month next
  following the month in which the levies were deducted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board at
  its office at 11826-100th Avenue, Edmonton, Alberta, all levies
  payable by him under section 3 that were not deducted and paid to the
  Commodity Board in the manner prescribed in subsection (1), not later
  than the 15th day of the month next following the month in which the
  turkeys in respect of which the levies are payable were sold.
