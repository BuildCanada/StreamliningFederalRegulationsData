## Shared Premises Regulations (Trust and Loan Companies) {#shared-premises-regulations-trust-and-loan-companies .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2008-68
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 2008-03-06

Shared Premises Regulations (Trust and Loan Companies)

P.C. 2008-520 2008-03-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 413.3(4)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EGCAA} of the [Trust and Loan
Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Shared Premises Regulations (Trust and Loan
Companies)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2008-68).

Definition of [[member institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[member institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
  meaning as in section 2 of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shared premises

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company to which paragraph 413(1)(b) or (c) of the
  [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies may carry on
  business in Canada on premises that are shared with those of a member
  institution that is affiliated with the company if, on those premises,
  whenever a request is made in person to open a deposit account with
  the company, the company opens the account at a work space that is
  separate and distinct from every work space at which the member
  institution carries on business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind16FE_hq_4193){#fn_Ind16FE_hq_4193-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 358 of An Act to amend the law governing financial institutions
and to provide for related and consequential matters, being chapter 6 of
the Statutes of Canada, 2007, comes into force, but if they are
registered after that day, they come into force on the day on which they
are registered.
