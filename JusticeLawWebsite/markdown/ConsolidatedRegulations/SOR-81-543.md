## Area Control List {#area-control-list .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-543
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1981-07-03

Area Control List

P.C. 1981-1793 1981-07-02
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to sections 4 and
6 of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), is pleased
hereby to revoke the [Area Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543),
C.R.C., c. 600 and to establish the annexed [Area Control
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543) in substitution therefor.
:::
