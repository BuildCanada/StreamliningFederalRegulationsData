## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order, 1986, No. 2 {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-1986-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-461
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1986-04-17

Order Waiving the Reservation to the Crown with Respect to Certain
Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories

P.C. 1986-967 1986-04-17
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 9(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order waiving the reservation to the Crown
with respect to certain territorial lands in the Northwest
Territories](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-856).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897065 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order, 1986,
No. 2](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-461).

## [Waiver]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897067 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The reservation to the
Crown set out in paragraph 9(b) of the [Territorial Lands
Act](/eng/acts/T-7) is hereby waived with respect to a grant of the
territorial lands described in the Schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#897069 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-897070 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lot numbered 278 in the Hamlet of Eskimo Point in the
Northwest Territories, as said lot is shown on a plan of survey of
record number 66305 in the Canada Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a
copy of which is filed in the Land Titles Office for the Northwest
Territories Land Registration District at Yellowknife under number 1341.
