## Protection of Assets (Fraternal Benefit Societies) Regulations {#protection-of-assets-fraternal-benefit-societies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-81

Regulations Respecting the Protection and Maintenance of Assets of
Societies

P.C. 1994-60  1994-01-13
::::

:::::: section

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 703(g) of the [Insurance
Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967669 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Protection of Assets (Fraternal Benefit Societies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-81).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967671 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract of insurance by which
    one party agrees to indemnify another party for loss arising out of
    the act of a third party; ([caution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a society, means a
    negotiable security beneficially owned or held by the society or
    held by the society in trust, but does not include a security issued
    by the society; ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a purchase, sale,
    redemption, exchange, transfer or assignment of, or other
    transaction in respect of, a security. ([opération sur
    titres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967676 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a society

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is carrying on business in a jurisdiction in
  compliance with a law of that jurisdiction, to the extent that that
  law is inconsistent with these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the extent that they are inconsistent with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a power conferred, or a duty imposed, on the
    society in respect of the administration of an estate or trust, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the discharge of the society's responsibilities in
    acting as an agent.

## [Safeguarding of Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967682 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A society shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a record in writing of procedures to be
  followed in the handling and safeguarding of assets owned or held by
  the society; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform every director, officer, employee and agent of
  the society who has access to, or is involved in the handling and
  safeguarding of, assets owned or held by the society of those
  procedures.

## [Safeguarding of Securities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967686 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A society shall maintain an
up-to-date record that identifies each of its securities.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 7, a society shall ensure that every
  security is kept securely, in a manner that prevents unauthorized
  access to the security, in the custody of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the society; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is authorized to act as a custodian
    of securities or as a depository or clearing agency for securities
    by a law of the jurisdiction in which the entity is carrying on
    business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the control of the government of the
    jurisdiction in which the society is carrying on business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pledged as collateral for the indebtedness or
    potential indebtedness of the society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} loaned to a person pursuant to a written agreement;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in transit.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A society shall not place a
security in the custody of an entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b)
unless the society has entered into a written custodial agreement with
that entity.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A society shall, on a daily
basis, deposit any net amount received by the society as a result of any
security transaction in an account kept by the society

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} with the society;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a financial institution that is authorized to
  accept deposits by a law of the jurisdiction in which the financial
  institution is carrying on business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with a trust company that is authorized to hold money
  in trust by a law of the jurisdiction in which the trust company
  carries on business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} with the government of the jurisdiction in which the
  society is carrying on business, or with an agency thereof that is
  authorized to act as a custodian of securities; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} with CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc.

## [Registration of a Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967706 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a society shall ensure that every security
  is registered in the society's name in the register of the entity that
  issued the security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cannot be registered in the society's name for a
    reason that is beyond the control of that society;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is under the control of the government of the
    jurisdiction in which the society is carrying on business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is held by the society as collateral or for
    safekeeping;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is registered in the name of a nominee of the
    society or of an entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is held under a book entry certificateless or
    immobilization system; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is held temporarily by an agent of the society, a
    liquidator, a trustee or the issuer of the security for purposes of
    reorganization, amalgamation, liquidation or voting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a security is otherwise protected against loss,
  fraud, theft and destruction, the society may hold the security

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in bearer form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in registered form in a name other than the
    society's name.

## [Bonding and Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-967719 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  society shall acquire and maintain one or more bonds, issued by an
  entity authorized to insure risks under the laws of the jurisdiction
  in which the entity carries on business, to indemnify the society for
  any loss in respect of assets owned or held by the society that arises
  out of a dishonest or criminal act of an officer or employee of the
  society.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A society shall, in respect of assets owned or held
  by the society, acquire and maintain one or more insurance policies to
  indemnify the society for any loss arising out of damage to, or the
  destruction or mysterious disappearance of, those assets or out of any
  other normal contingency.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall
provide that the bond or insurance policy shall not be cancelled or
terminated by the insurer or the insured until at least 30 days after
the receipt by the Superintendent of a written notice from the insurer
or the insured, as the case may be, of its intention to cancel or
terminate the bond or insurance policy.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall be in
an amount that is established by the supreme governing body of the
society, having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature and value of the assets owned or held by
  the society;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arrangements and procedures applicable to the
  handling and safeguarding of the assets owned or held by the society;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other factors that might affect the extent of any
  loss that the society might sustain.
