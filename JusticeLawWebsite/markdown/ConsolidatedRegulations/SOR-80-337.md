## Customs Drawback on Toy Skins Regulations {#customs-drawback-on-toy-skins-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-337
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1980-05-09

Regulations Respecting a Drawback on Customs Duties Paid on Toy Skins
Used in the Manufacture of Stuffed Toys

P.C. 1980-1214 1980-05-08
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 273 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting a drawback on customs duties paid on toy skins
used in the manufacture of stuffed toys](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-337).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869227 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Customs Drawback on Toy Skins
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-337).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869229 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Canadian fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fabric that has been
    woven or knitted in Canada, whether or not it contains imported
    materials; ([tissu canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drawback period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drawback period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    periods that is selected by a manufacturer who has not before
    selected any part of that period:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the twelve month period before January 1, 1981,
      January 1, 1982, January 1, 1983, January 1, 1984, January 1, 1985
      or January 1, 1986, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the six month period before July 1, 1980, January
      1, 1981, July 1, 1981, January 1, 1982, July 1, 1982, January 1,
      1983, July 1, 1983, January 1, 1984, July 1, 1984, January 1,
      1985, July 1, 1985 or January 1, 1986; ([période de
      drawback]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person engaged in the
    manufacture of stuffed toys in Canada; ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[toy skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[toy skin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unfilled outer covering
    of a stuffed toy used by a manufacturer in the manufacture of the
    stuffed toy. ([revêtement de jouet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-921, s. 1
- SOR/85-178, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869240 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any other
provision in these Regulations, a drawback shall be granted, at the rate
determined under section 5, of the customs duties paid under the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on imported toy skins that have
been used by a manufacturer in the manufacture of stuffed toys during a
drawback period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-76
- SOR/92-685, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A drawback granted under
these Regulations in respect of imported toy skins shall be paid to the
manufacturer.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The rate
  of drawback referred to in section 3 that may be granted under these
  Regulations in respect of a drawback period of a manufacturer shall
  be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a manufacturer's total cost of Canadian
    fabrics purchased during the period for the manufacture of stuffed
    toys is more than 55 per cent of the duty paid value of imported toy
    skins used by that manufacturer during the period, 80 per cent of
    the customs duties plus an amount equal to 2 per cent of those
    customs duties for each percentage point or portion thereof by which
    the manufacturer's total cost of Canadian fabrics purchased during
    the period exceeds 55 per cent of the duty paid value of imported
    toy skins used by that manufacturer during the period;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a manufacturer's total cost of Canadian
    fabrics purchased during the period for the manufacture of stuffed
    toys is not more than 55 per cent and not less than 45 per cent of
    the duty paid value of imported toy skins used by that manufacturer
    during the period, 80 per cent of the customs duties; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the manufacturer's total cost of Canadian
    fabrics purchased during the period for the manufacture of stuffed
    toys is less than 45 per cent of the duty paid value of imported toy
    skins used by the manufacturer during the period, 80 per cent of the
    customs duties minus an amount equal to 2 per cent of the customs
    duties for each percentage point or portion thereof by which the
    manufacturer's total cost of Canadian fabrics purchased during the
    period is less than 45 per cent of the duty paid value of imported
    toy skins used by that manufacturer during the period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), the rate of drawback
  referred to therein shall not exceed 100 per cent of the customs
  duties.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-76
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No drawback shall be paid
under these Regulations in respect of a drawback period of a
manufacturer if the manufacturer's total consumption in square metres of
Canadian fabrics in the manufacture of stuffed toys during the period is
less than its total consumption in square meters of Canadian fabrics in
the manufacture of stuffed toys during the corresponding period in the
calendar year 1978 or, where the manufacturer did not manufacture
stuffed toys during 1978, the corresponding period of the first year
subsequent to 1978 during which the manufacturer manufactured stuffed
toys in Canada.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  drawback shall be paid under these Regulations in respect of any
  imported toy skins unless a claim therefor is filed within a period of
  four years from the date customs duties were paid on the imported toy
  skins.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies to all customs duties paid on
  or after April 1, 1981.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-304, s. 1
- SOR/88-76
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  claim referred to in section 7 shall be made in such form as the
  Minister may prescribe and shall be filed with a collector of customs
  and excise.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require, in respect of any claim
  filed in accordance with subsection (1), the production of such
  information as he deems necessary to establish the validity of the
  claim.
