## Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development To Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Poverty Reduction Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-employment-and-social-development-to-be-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-poverty-reduction-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-43
:::

[POVERTY REDUCTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-16.81)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development To
Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Poverty Reduction Act

P.C. 2025-423 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Poverty Reduction
Act](/eng/acts/P-16.81)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620){#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620-ID0EBDAA},

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620-ID0EBDAA){#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731620}S.C.
  2019, c. 29, s. 315
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2020-123 of March 9,
  2020[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646){#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646-ID0EBBBAA){#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646}SI/2020-28
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Employment and Social
  Development, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, to be
  the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
