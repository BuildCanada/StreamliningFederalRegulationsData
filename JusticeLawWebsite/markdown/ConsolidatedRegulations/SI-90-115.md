## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Tuktoyaktuk Harbour, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-tuktoyaktuk-harbour-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-115
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 1990-10-10

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Tuktoyaktuk Harbour, N.W.T.)

P.C. 1990-1902  1990-08-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7), is
pleased hereby to order that any portion of the lands described in the
schedule hereto lying within 30.48 metres (100 feet) of the Ordinary
High Water Mark of Tuktoyaktuk Harbour in the Northwest Territories be
included in the sale of the said lands.
:::

::::: section
:::: {#642426 .Schedule}
::: {}
## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-642427 .scheduleLabel}
:::

The whole of Lots numbered 1007 to 1024 inclusive in Quad numbered 107
C/8 near Tuktoyaktuk in the Northwest Territories, as said lots are
shown on a plan of survey of record number 71375 in the Canada Lands
Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land Titles
Office for the Northwest Territories Land Registration District at
Yellowknife under number 1941.
