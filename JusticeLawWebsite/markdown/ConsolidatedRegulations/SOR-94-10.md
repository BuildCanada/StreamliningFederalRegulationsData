## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958669 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Marking of Imported Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-10).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958671 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[country of origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[country of origin]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods imported from a CUSMA
      country, the country of origin of the goods determined in
      accordance with the Determination of Country of Origin for the
      Purposes of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries) Regulations, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods imported from any other
      country, the country of origin of the goods determined in
      accordance with the Determination of Country of Origin for the
      Purposes of Marking Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries)
      Regulations; ([pays d'origine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CUSMA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CUSMA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any goods in respect of
    which the [Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of
    Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-23) apply; ([marchandises d'un
    pays ACEUM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any goods in respect of which
    any regulations made pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(a) of the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) apply; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[identical goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[identical goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that are the
    same in all respects, including physical characteristics, origin,
    quality and reputation, except for minor differences in appearance;
    ([marchandises identiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[NAFTA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NAFTA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-83, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[non-CUSMA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-CUSMA goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any goods in respect
    of which the Determination of Country of Origin for the Purposes of
    Marking Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries) Regulations
    apply; ([marchandises d'un pays autre qu'un pays
    ACEUM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[similar goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[similar goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that closely
    resemble each other in respect of their component materials and
    characteristics, are capable of performing the same functions, are
    commercially interchangeable, and were produced in the same country;
    ([marchandises semblables]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sufficiently permanent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sufficiently permanent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of
    a marking, capable of remaining in place until the goods reach the
    ultimate purchaser or, in the case of non-CUSMA goods where there is
    no ultimate purchaser, the ultimate recipient, unless deliberately
    removed; ([suffisamment permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ultimate purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ultimate purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the last person in
    Canada who purchases the goods in the form in which they are
    imported, whether or not that purchaser is the last person to use
    the goods in Canada; ([dernier acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[ultimate recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ultimate recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the last person in
    Canada who receives the goods in the form in which they are
    imported; ([dernier destinataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[usual container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[usual container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the container in which
    goods will ordinarily reach their ultimate purchaser. ([contenant
    usuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958688 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  marking of CUSMA goods shall indicate, to the ultimate purchaser, the
  country of origin of the CUSMA goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The marking of non-CUSMA goods shall indicate, to the
  ultimate purchaser, or where there is no ultimate purchaser, to the
  ultimate recipient, the country of origin of the non-CUSMA goods.

## [General Manner of Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958692 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} CUSMA
  goods shall be marked in English, French or Spanish.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), non-CUSMA goods shall be
  marked in English or French.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Non-CUSMA goods of which the country of origin is
  Honduras shall be marked in English, French or Spanish.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The marking of goods shall
be legible, sufficiently permanent and capable of being seen easily
during normal handling of the goods or their container.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If the word "Canada",
"Canadian" or "canadien" or any abbreviation of it, or the name of any
country or place other than the country of origin of the goods appears
on any goods elsewhere than in the country of origin marking, and the
presence of the word can be expected to mislead the ultimate purchaser
or the ultimate recipient, as the case may be, the country of origin
marking shall be located in close proximity to that word and shall
contain the expression "made in", "produced in" or "printed in" or any
other expression, in any of the languages used in accordance with
section 4 that indicates to the ultimate purchaser or to the ultimate
recipient, as the case may be, the country of origin of the goods.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An abbreviation of a
country name may be used in a country of origin marking if the
abbreviation clearly indicates to the ultimate purchaser or to the
ultimate recipient, as the case may be, the country of origin of the
goods.

## [Specific Manner of Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958704 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
5 and subject to section 9, goods that are iron or steel pipes or tubes
shall be marked

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by paint stencilling, die stamping, cast-in-mould
  lettering, etching or engraving; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of pipes or tubes that have an inside
  diameter of 4.8 cm or less (1.9 inches or less) and are imported in
  bundles, by tagging or labelling each bundle.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Goods that are iron or
steel pipes or tubes and have a critical surface finish may be marked by
tagging or labelling each pipe or tube.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 5, the country of origin marking for goods that are printed or
lithographed paper products shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} of a size not smaller than 1.6 mm (0.06 inch); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a colour that is as bold as any other characters
  or images that are printed or lithographed on the paper product.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A container that is
required to be marked in accordance with the Determination of Country of
Origin for the Purposes of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries) Regulations,
in which is imported an empty usual container, whether or not the usual
container is disposable, shall be marked with the country of origin of
the usual container.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A usual container that is
required to be marked in accordance with the Determination of Country of
Origin for the Purposes of Marking Goods (CUSMA Countries) Regulations,
imported filled, whether or not the usual container is disposable, shall
be marked with the country of origin of its contents unless the contents
are marked with their country of origin and the usual container can be
readily opened for inspection of the contents, or the marking of the
contents is clearly visible through the usual container.

## [Time of Marking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958714 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 14,
goods shall be marked before their importation.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958716 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Goods may be marked in
Canada after their importation but before their release, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are not imported as mail;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice referred to in subsection 35.02(2) of the
  Act in respect of similar goods or identical goods to be imported

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has not been served on the importer of the goods,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been served on the importer of the goods less
    than thirty days or more than two years before the importation of
    the goods;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer of the goods

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} before the importation of the goods, gives written
    notice to an officer that the goods will be marked in Canada by the
    importer, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the importation of the goods, gives
    notice to an officer that the goods will be marked in Canada by the
    importer, where the importer demonstrates that it was not possible
    to give the notice referred to in subparagraph (i) before the
    importation of the goods; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the importer of the goods complies with any
  instructions given pursuant to section 15.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** After receipt of a notice
referred to in paragraph 14(c), an officer shall give the importer of
the goods written instructions indicating

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs office, bonded warehouse or sufferance
  warehouse in Canada where the marking is to be done;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the time within which the marking must be completed;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the customs office, bonded warehouse or sufferance
  warehouse in Canada where the goods are to be made available on the
  completion of the marking for examination by an officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-302, s. 1
:::

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  importer of goods who has given a notice under subparagraph 14(c)(i)
  shall present to an officer a copy of the instructions referred to in
  section 15 at the time the goods

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are reported under section 12 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are accounted for under subsection 32(1) or (2) of
    the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An importer of goods who has given a notice under
  subparagraph 14(c)(ii) shall present to an officer a copy of the
  instructions referred to in section 15 at the time the goods are
  accounted for under subsection 32(1) or (2) of the Act.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An importer of goods that
are required to be marked, who delivers the goods to a bonded warehouse
before they are marked, shall mark the goods in accordance with any
instructions given under section 15 before a transfer document may be
submitted in accordance with section 18 of the Customs Bonded Warehouse
Regulations.
