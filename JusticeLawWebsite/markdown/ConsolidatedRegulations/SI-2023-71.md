## Order Transferring the Powers, Duties and Functions with Respect to the Homelessness Policy Directorate to the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities {#order-transferring-the-powers-duties-and-functions-with-respect-to-the-homelessness-policy-directorate-to-the-minister-of-infrastructure-and-communities .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-71
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-11-22

Order Transferring the Powers, Duties and Functions with Respect to the
Homelessness Policy Directorate to the Minister of Infrastructure and
Communities

P.C. 2023-1063 2023-10-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1690908469){#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1690908469-ID0EGDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1690908488){#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1690908488-ID0EDDAA},
transfers the powers, duties and functions with respect to the
Homelessness Policy Directorate that were transferred to the Minister of
Housing and Diversity and Inclusion by Order in Council P.C. 2021-952 of
October 26, 2021[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1691935352){#fn_81000-3-2609_hq_1691935352-ID0EBDAA}
to the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities.
