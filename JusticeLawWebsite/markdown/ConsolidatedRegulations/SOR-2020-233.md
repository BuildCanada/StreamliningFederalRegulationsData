Order Declaring that the Provisions of the Regulations Respecting
Reduction in the Release of Methane and Certain Volatile Organic

P.C. 2020-826 2020-10-23

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26282){#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26282-ID0EICEA}
of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26283){#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26283-ID0EFCEA},
the Minister of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 6, 2020, a copy of
the proposed [Order Declaring that the Provisions of the Regulations
Respecting Reduction in the Release of Methane and Certain Volatile
Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas Sector) Do Not Apply in
Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-233), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the proposed Order or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26282-ID0EICEA){#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26282}S.C.

- ::: Footnote

  1999, c. 33

Whereas, the Minister and the Government of Alberta have entered into a
written agreement referred to in subsection 10(3) of that Act to the
effect that there are in force by or under the laws of Alberta

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are equivalent to the provisions of
  the [Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release of Methane and
  Certain Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas
  Sector)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-66)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4161_hq_27740){#fn_81000-2-4161_hq_27740-ID0EBBBDA},
  made under subsection 93(1) of that Act, and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4161_hq_27740-ID0EBBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4161_hq_27740}SOR/2018-66
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are similar to sections 17 to 20 of
  that Act for the investigation of alleged offences under environmental
  legislation of Alberta;

Whereas, pursuant to subsections 10(4) and (5) of that Act, the Minister
gave notice of the availability of that agreement in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 6, 2020, and
persons were given an opportunity to file with the Minister comments or
a notice of objection;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 10(6) of that Act, the Minister
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on
September 26, 2020 a notice of availability of a report that summarizes
how any comments or notices of objection were dealt with;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 10(3) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26283){#fn_81000-2-3724_hq_26283-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed [Order Declaring that the Provisions of the
Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release of Methane and Certain
Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas Sector) Do Not Apply in
Alberta](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-233).

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1272419 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Except with respect to a
federal work or undertaking, the following provisions of the
[Regulations Respecting Reduction in the Release of Methane and Certain
Volatile Organic Compounds (Upstream Oil and Gas
Sector)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-66) do not apply in Alberta:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 1 to 25, 28 to 36 and 42 to 56; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 26, 27 and 37 to 41.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1272424 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day agreement terminates

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order ceases to have
effect on the day on which the agreement between the Minister of the
Environment and the Government of Alberta, entitled "Agreement on the
Equivalency of Federal and Alberta Regulations Respecting the Release of
Methane from the Oil and Gas Sector in Alberta, 2020", terminates or is
terminated under subsection 10(8) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1272427 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Order, except paragraph 1(b), comes into force on the day on which it
  is registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}January 1, 2023

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 1(b) comes into force on January 1, 2023.
