## Regulations for the Purposes of the Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act {#regulations-for-the-purposes-of-the-canada-strategic-infrastructure-fund-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-51
:::

[CANADA STRATEGIC INFRASTRUCTURE FUND ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.3)

Registration 2003-01-30

Regulations for the Purposes of the Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund
Act

P.C. 2003-112 2003-01-30
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph 5(a) of the [Canada
Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the
annexed Regulations Prescribing Capital Assets for the Purpose of the
Definition [[Strategic Infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in the
[Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea}S.C. 2002, c.
  9, s. 47
  :::
:::

## [Capital Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707585 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following are
prescribed as other fixed capital assets for the purpose of paragraph
(f) of the definition [[strategic infrastructure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
in section 2 of the [Canada Strategic Infrastructure Fund
Act](/eng/acts/C-10.3):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} advanced telecommunications and high-speed broadband
  infrastructure; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all infrastructure situated in Yukon, the Northwest
  Territories and Nunavut not otherwise prescribed in paragraph (a) of
  this subsection or set out in any of paragraphs (a) to (e) of that
  definition.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-200, s. 2
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707591 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day they are registered.
