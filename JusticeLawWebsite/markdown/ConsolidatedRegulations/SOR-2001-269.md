the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 5[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670472 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[acid reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[acid reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of an
      alkali, expressed in grams of sodium hydroxide, that is required
      to bring 100 mL of a liquid acidic product, or 100 g of an acidic
      product in the form of a solid, paste or gel, to a pH of 4.00 ±
      0.05. ([réserve acide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-24, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[alkali reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[alkali reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of an
      alkali, expressed in grams of sodium hydroxide, that is
      neutralized when 100 mL of a liquid basic product, or 100 g of a
      basic product in the form of a solid, paste or gel, is brought to
      a pH of 10.00 ± 0.05 by the addition of hydrochloric acid or its
      equivalent. ([réserve alcaline]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[aspiration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aspiration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry of a liquid or
      solid chemical product directly through the oral or nasal cavity,
      or indirectly from vomiting, into the trachea or lower respiratory
      system. ([aspiration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[chemical product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[chemical product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product used by a
      consumer that has the properties of one or more of the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a toxic product;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corrosive product;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a flammable product; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a quick skin-bonding adhesive.

      It does not include any of the following:

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a product described in any of paragraphs (a)
        to (d) if it is not possible for a user to be exposed to the
        product or to any of its hazardous ingredients during reasonably
        foreseeable use;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a portable petroleum container that conforms
        with CSA B306 or CSA B376;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a lighter;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a fire extinguisher that conforms with
        ULC-S503, ULC-S504, ULC-S507 or ULC-S512; or

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a container of fuel, such as gasoline, ethanol
        or propane, if the container is permanently attached to an
        internal combustion engine, a gas turbine or an appliance that
        uses the fuel. ([produit chimique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[complex mixture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[complex mixture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a combination of
      chemicals that has a generic name that is not a trade name and is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a substance of natural origin;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fraction obtained by a physical separation
        process from a substance of a natural origin; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a chemical modification of a substance of
        natural origin or of a fraction obtained by a physical
        separation process from a substance of natural origin. ([mélange
        complexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Category 5 pressurized container that is or
        is likely to be used by a consumer, including an empty
        container, as described in Part 5;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an empty container that is destined for use by
        a consumer to store or dispense a chemical product; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other container that is or is likely to be
        used by a consumer to store or dispense a chemical
        product. ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[corrosive product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[corrosive product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical product
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is capable of inducing necrosis or ulceration
        of epithelial tissue;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is capable of causing an erythema or edema of
        the skin, corneal or iris damage or conjunctival swelling or
        redness; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is identified in Part 2 as a Category 2
        corrosive product. ([produit corrosif]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
      surface area of a container on which the information required by
      these Regulations can be displayed. It does not include the
      surface area of the bottom, of any seam or of any concave or
      convex surface near the top or the bottom of a container. ([aire
      d'affichage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means solid airborne particles that
      are mechanically generated. ([poussière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[first aid statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[first aid statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of the hazardous ingredients in the
        chemical product; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the first aid treatment to be
        administered to anyone who has come into contact with a chemical
        product, such as through ingestion, absorption or inhalation, or
        information that may be helpful to someone who is assisting that
        individual. ([énoncé de premiers soins]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[flame projection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flame projection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the flame resulting
      from the ignition of a chemical product discharged from a spray
      container when tested in accordance with the procedure set out in
      Schedule 1. ([projection de la flamme]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[flammable product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flammable product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical product
      that is capable of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} spontaneous combustion;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} becoming flammable when in contact with air; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} having a flash point below 60°C or a flame
        projection greater than 15 cm or exhibiting a
        flashback. ([produit inflammable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a flame
      projection that extends from the point of ignition back towards
      the spray container when a chemical product is tested in
      accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 1. ([retour de
      flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flash point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flash point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the minimum temperature
      at which a substance gives off a vapour in sufficient
      concentration to ignite under test conditions. ([point
      d'éclair]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fume]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means solid particles in the air that
      are generated by condensation from the vapour of a solid
      material. ([fumée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fumes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fumes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in the context of the information
      that must be displayed on a container, means a vapour or a fume or
      both that may be given off by a chemical product under normal
      conditions of use or storage. ([émanations]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the development of test data, conditions
        and procedures similar to those set out in the OECD Test
        Guidelines;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for laboratory practices, practices similar to
        those set out in the OECD Principles of Good Laboratory
        Practice; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for human experience data, a peer-reviewed
        study of clinical cases. ([bonnes pratiques
        scientifiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazard category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazard category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a category into
      which a chemical product or container is classified, in
      particular:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Category 1, toxic products set out in Part 1;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Category 2, corrosive products set out in Part
        2;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Category 3, flammable products set out in Part
        3;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Category 4, quick skin-bonding adhesives set
        out in Part 4; and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Category 5, pressurized containers set out in
        Part 5. ([catégorie de danger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazard symbol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazard symbol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pictograph and its
      frame as set out in Schedule 2. ([pictogramme de
      danger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazardous ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazardous ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a pure chemical product;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an ingredient present in a chemical product in
        a concentration of 1% or more that is taken into consideration
        when classifying the product and that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a chemical product,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the supplier believes on reasonable grounds
          may be harmful to humans,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has toxicological properties that are not
          known to the supplier, or

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} derives from a reaction between precursor
          constituents and the hazards associated with the chemical
          product are not known to the supplier; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a complex mixture present in a chemical product
        in a concentration of 1% or more that is taken into
        consideration when classifying the product and that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a chemical product,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the supplier believes on reasonable grounds
          may be harmful to humans, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has toxicological properties that are not
          known to the supplier. ([ingrédient
          dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[human experience data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[human experience data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means data,
      collected in accordance with good scientific practices, that
      demonstrates that injury to or poisoning of a human has or has not
      resulted from

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exposure to a chemical product; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonably foreseeable use of a chemical
        product or container by a consumer, including, in particular,
        the consumption of the product by a child. ([données de
        l'expérience humaine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[LC~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[LC~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the concentration of a
      substance in air that, when administered by means of inhalation
      over a specified length of time in an animal assay, is expected to
      cause the death of at least 50% of a defined population of
      animals. ([CL~50~]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[LD~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[LD~50~]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the single dose of a
      substance that, when administered by a defined route in an animal
      assay, is expected to cause the death of at least 50% of a defined
      population of animals. ([DL~50~]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of the
      display surface that is displayed or visible under normal
      conditions of sale to the consumer. It includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rectangular container, the
        largest side of the display surface;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cylindrical container, the
        larger of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area of the top, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 40% of the area obtained by multiplying the
          circumference of the container by the height of the display
          surface;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bag, the largest side of the
        bag; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other container, the largest
        surface of the container that is not less than 40% of the
        display surface. ([aire d'affichage principale]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a packager and a
      labeller. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means droplets of liquid suspended in
      air that are produced by the condensation of a vapourized liquid
      or by the dispersion of a liquid by a spray
      container. ([brouillard]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mixture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mixture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a combination of two or more
      products, materials or substances that do not undergo a chemical
      change as a result of their
      interaction. ([mélange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[National Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[National Standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a standard
      recognized by the National Standards System of the Standards
      Council of Canada. ([norme nationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[normal atmospheric pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[normal atmospheric pressure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      absolute pressure of 101.324 kPa at 20°C. ([pression atmosphérique
      normale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[official languages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[official languages]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the English and
      French languages. ([langues officielles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[person responsible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person responsible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2016-170, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[quick skin-bonding adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[quick skin-bonding adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      Category 4 adhesive set out in Part 4 that has properties similar
      to an alkyl cyanoacrylate adhesive and that is capable of bonding
      skin with skin instantly or nearly instantly. ([adhésif qui colle
      rapidement la peau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer, in the case of a chemical
        product or container that is manufactured in Canada; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer, in the case of a chemical product
        or container that is imported. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[single-use container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[single-use container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      non-reclosable container whose contents are to be used in their
      entirety immediately after the container is opened. ([contenant à
      usage unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[spray container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[spray container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a container that
      permits the dispersal of its contents in the form of a mist and
      includes a pressurized container and a pump-spray
      container. ([contenant pulvérisateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sub-category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sub-category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following
      classifications within a hazard category in which a chemical
      product may be classified, in particular:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Category 1 toxic product,
        "very toxic", "toxic" and "harmful";

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Category 2 corrosive product,
        "very corrosive", "corrosive" and "irritant"; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Category 3 flammable product,
        "spontaneously combustible", "very flammable", "flammable" and
        "combustible". ([sous-catégorie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[toxic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[toxic product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical product
      that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is capable of causing a lethal effect on a
        human;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is capable of causing a serious and
        irreversible but non-lethal effect on a human, such as a
        depressed level of consciousness, muscular weakness or
        paralysis, acute renal or hepatic failure, arrhythmia,
        hypotension, dyspnea, respiratory depression, pulmonary edema or
        optic neuritis; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is identified in Part 1 as a Category 1 toxic
        product. ([produit toxique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vapour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vapour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the gaseous form of a
      substance that is found in a solid or liquid state at normal
      atmospheric pressure. ([vapeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670598 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- importation to bring into
compliance or to export

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  may import a chemical product or container that does not comply with a
  requirement of these Regulations for the purpose of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bringing the product or container into compliance
    with the requirement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reselling the product or container to a
    manufacturer in Canada who will bring it into compliance with the
    requirement; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exporting the product or container to another
    country.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who imports a chemical product or a
  container for a purpose described in subsection (1) must, on the
  request of an inspector, provide credible evidence to the inspector
  that it is being brought into compliance with these Regulations or is
  being exported, as the case may be.

## [Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-670610 .Part}

### [Hazard Category, Container and Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670611 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by responsible person

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person, using one or more of the applicable properties,
  data sources or test procedures set out in section 6 or Parts 1 to 5,
  must determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the hazard categories of the chemical product or
    container and, if applicable, its appropriate sub-categories;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of container that is required; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the information that is required to be displayed on
    the container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple hazard categories

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a chemical product and its container fall into
  more than one hazard category, its container must display the
  information that is required for every applicable hazard category.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple sub-categories

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), if a chemical product
  falls into more than one sub-category within a hazard category, the
  responsible person must classify the product in the sub-category that
  represents the greatest hazard within each applicable hazard category.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple sub-categories --- flammable
  products

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a chemical product falls into both the
  sub-category "spontaneously combustible" and another sub-category in
  the hazard category "Category 3, flammable product", it must be
  classified in both sub-categories.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple routes of exposure

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The container of a chemical product to which an
  individual may be exposed by more than one route of exposure must
  display the required information for each route of exposure.

- SOR/2016-170, s. 8(E)

### [Data Sources]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670634 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precedence of data sources

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person must determine the hazards arising from the
  reasonably foreseeable use of the chemical product or container from
  one or more of the following data sources in the following order of
  precedence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} human experience data pertaining to the product or
    container;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} data, set out in peer-reviewed, scientific
    literature, from tests conducted using the product in accordance
    with the OECD Test Guidelines;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if tests using the product have not been conducted
    in accordance with the OECD Test Guidelines,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the peer-reviewed results of tests conducted with
      the product in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a National Standard or an international
        standard recognized by the Standards Council of Canada, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a generally accepted procedure that accorded
        with good scientific practices at the time the tests were
        conducted,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the peer-reviewed results of tests conducted
      with a chemical product that has properties similar to those of
      the product under examination, in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} OECD Test Guidelines,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a National Standard or an international
        standard recognized by the Standards Council of Canada, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a generally accepted procedure that accorded
        with good scientific practices at the time the tests were
        conducted, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} other current, peer-reviewed information about
      the product;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the hazards associated with the product cannot
    be estimated from information referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c),
    data with respect to the hazards associated with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the hazardous ingredients, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a chemical product that has properties similar
      to those of the product under examination; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of tests conducted by the responsible
    person in accordance with a test methodology that conforms with good
    scientific practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Differing data sources

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of differing data sources,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an original literature source must be used in
    preference to literature that refers to the original study; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a source that reports test results that conform
    with good scientific practices and that disclose the greatest hazard
    must be used.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-170, s. 8(E)

### [Child-resistant Containers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670667 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standard

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A child-resistant container
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be constructed so that it can be opened only by
  operating, puncturing or removing one of its functional and necessary
  parts using a tool that is not supplied with the container; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the child test protocol requirements of one of
  CSA-Z76.1, ISO 8317 or 16 CFR 1700.20 or a standard that is at least
  equivalent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintain characteristics

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  child-resistant container must, when in contact with a chemical
  product, maintain its child-resistant characteristics throughout the
  useful life of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person, using good scientific
  practices, must evaluate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the compatibility of the chemical product with its
    child-resistant container, to determine that the chemical or
    physical properties of the product will not compromise or interfere
    with the proper functioning of the container; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the physical wear and stress factors and the force
    required for opening and closing the container, to determine that
    the proper functioning of the container will be maintained for the
    number of openings and closings reasonably foreseeable for the size
    and contents of the container.

- SOR/2016-170, s. 8(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to open and close

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  child-resistant container that meets the requirements of
  paragraph 9(b) must display directions explaining how to open and, if
  applicable, to close the container

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the closure, using either or both of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} words that comply with sections 17 to 19, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a diagram or self-explanatory symbol that
      complies with paragraph 17(b) and section 18; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the container, located after the positive
    instructions for the chemical product and set out in a manner that
    complies with requirements for a positive instruction in sections 17
    to 19, subsection 24(1) and sections 25, 29 and 30.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The directions for opening and, if applicable,
  closing a child-resistant container may be set out on the closure in
  only one official language if they are repeated on the container in
  the other official language in the manner set out in paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person must prepare and maintain documents containing the
  following information and must keep those documents for a period of at
  least three years after the day on which the child-resistant container
  is manufactured in Canada or the day on which it is imported:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a container that comes into direct contact with
    the chemical product, the specifications critical to the
    child-resistant characteristics of the container, which
    specifications must include

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the physical measurements within which the
      container retains its child-resistant characteristics,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the torque that must be applied
      to open or close the container, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the compatibility of the container and its
      closure system with the chemical product that is to be put into
      it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test results that demonstrate that the
    container and its closure system comply with the requirements of a
    standard set out in paragraph 9(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector's request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person for a chemical product that is
  required by these Regulations to be packaged in a child-resistant
  container must provide an inspector with any of the documents that the
  inspector requests within 15 days after the day on which the request
  is received.

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Packaging is exempt from the
  requirements of paragraph 15(1)(b) if the packaging

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is transparent and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the required information displayed on the
      container is legible through the packaging, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transparent packaging does not obscure any
      of the required information on the container; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not transparent and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} encloses

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a container and a product that is not subject
        to these Regulations, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} two or more containers that have different
        information requirements, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} displays the following signal word and primary
      hazard statement, or a statement to the same effect, in the manner
      set out in sections 17 to 20 and 23, subsections 24(1) and (3) and
      sections 25, 26 and 28:

      "CAUTION. CONTAINS PRODUCTS THAT MAY BE HARMFUL. READ WARNINGS ON
      EACH CONTAINER."

      "[ATTENTION. CONTIENT DES PRODUITS QUI PEUVENT ÊTRE NOCIFS. LIRE
      LES MISES EN GARDE SUR CHAQUE CONTENANT]{.otherLang lang="fr"}."

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- packaging not customarily
  displayed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Packaging that is not customarily displayed to the
  consumer, such as a shipping carton, is exempt from the requirements
  of paragraph 15(1)(b).

### [Location and Order --- General Rule]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670805 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location and order

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information required by these Regulations must be displayed on the
  container at the following locations and in the following order:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the main display panel,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a hazard symbol,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the signal word "EXTREME DANGER", "DANGER" or
      "CAUTION", as the case may be, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the primary hazard statement; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on any part of the display surface,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the specific hazard statement,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} negative instructions,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} positive instructions, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the first aid statement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- small container

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1) and subject to Part 4, if the
  main display panel of a container has an area of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} less than 35 cm^2^, only the hazard symbol and the
    signal word need be displayed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 35 cm^2^ or more but less than 70 cm^2^ and more
    than one hazard symbol is required, the required negative and
    positive instructions, other than the positive instructions referred
    to in subsection 11(2), may be omitted.

### [Location on Main Display Panel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670822 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of hazard symbol, signal word
and primary hazard statement

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  information referred to in paragraph 25(1)(a) must be located as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} below the common name or brand name of the chemical
    product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} centred parallel to and near the base of the main
    display panel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (3), immediately below one
    another.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base of collapsible tube

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), the base of the
  main display panel of a collapsible tube is the end from which the
  chemical product is discharged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- short, wide containers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the main display panel has a height that is less
  than 10 cm and a width that is at least twice the height, the signal
  word may be located immediately beside the hazard symbol instead of
  below it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple hazard symbols

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If more than one hazard
symbol is required, the symbols must be grouped immediately beside each
other in a row parallel to the base of the container.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Precedence among signal words

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** If these Regulations
require that more than one signal word be displayed on a container, only
the signal word that discloses the greatest hazard must be displayed on
the container.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} must be different from any other border on the label;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} must comply with paragraph 17(b) and section 18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First aid statement --- order of
information

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The first aid statement
must set out the following information in the following order:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title "**FIRST AID TREATMENT**" and "**[PREMIERS

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a complex mixture, by its generic
  name.

### [Classification of Toxic Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670860 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The responsible person
for a toxic product must determine the appropriate sub-category for the
product from one or more of the following data sources in the following
order of precedence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} human experience data pertaining to the toxic
  product;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a toxic product that contains a
  substance of special concern, the table to subsection 34(1);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a toxic product that poses a risk of
  exposure through an oral, a dermal or an inhalation route, the LD~50~
  or LC~50~ or both, as the case may be, of the product as determined in
  accordance with the applicable table to subsections 34(2) to (4) and
  the data sources and formulas set out in sections 35 to 37; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a toxic product that poses an
  aspiration hazard, the properties set out in subsection 34(5).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sub-category --- aspiration hazard

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A chemical product must be classified in the
  sub-category "toxic" if it has a viscosity of 14 mm^2^/s or less at
  40°C and 10% or more of the product is composed of hazardous
  ingredients that pose an aspiration hazard, including, in particular,
  any of the following substances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an n-primary alcohol with a composition of at least
    3 carbon atoms but not more than 13;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an isobutyl alcohol;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a terpene alcohol;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a ketone with a composition of at least 3 carbon
    atoms but not more than 13;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a hydrocarbon with a composition of at least 3
    carbon atoms but not more than 13; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a substance that has been determined to be an
    aspiration hazard based on its viscosity, surface tension and water
    solubility through the application of generally accepted standards
    of good scientific practices.

### [Determination of Toxicity]{.HTitleText2} {#h-670916 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person for a toxic product must determine, from one or
  more of the following data sources in the following order of
  precedence, its toxicity or, in the case of a mixture that separates,
  the toxicity of each layer of the mixture:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the LD~50~ or LC~50~ or both, as the case may be,
    of the product or of the layer as determined by the peer-reviewed
    results of acute toxicity tests using the product on animals, which
    tests were conducted in accordance with the OECD Test Guidelines for
    acute toxicity testing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if tests on animals using the product have not been
    conducted in accordance with the OECD Test Guidelines for acute
    toxicity testing, the LD~50~ or LC~50~ or both, as the case may be,
    of the product or of the layer as determined by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} peer-reviewed results of acute toxicity tests of
      the product or the layer, which tests were conducted on animals in
      accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a National Standard or an international
        standard recognized by the Standards Council of Canada, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a generally accepted procedure that conformed
        with good scientific practices at the time the tests were
        conducted,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the product is a mixture that does not
      separate, section 36,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} peer-reviewed results of tests of a chemical
      product or a substance that has similar properties to those of the
      product or of the layer under examination, which tests were
      conducted on animals in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} OECD Test Guidelines for acute toxicity
        testing,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a National Standard or an international
        standard recognized by the Standards Council of Canada, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a generally accepted procedure that conformed
        with good scientific practices at the time the tests were
        conducted, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} other current, peer-reviewed information about
      the product or the layer; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the LD~50~ or LC~50~ or both, as the case may be,
    of the product or of the layer as determined by the results of tests
    conducted with the toxic product by the responsible person in
    accordance with a test methodology that conforms with good
    scientific practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Differing data sources

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of differing data sources,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an original literature source must be used in
    preference to a literature source that refers to the original study;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a source that reports test results that conform
    with good scientific practices and that disclose the greatest hazard
    must be used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mixture that separates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a mixture that separates, the toxic
  product must be assigned the LD~50~ or LC~50~ or both, as the case may
  be, of the most toxic layer.

- Definition of [[mixture that separates]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[mixture that
  separates]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chemical product in a liquid
  or semi-liquid state that separates into two or more distinct layers
  if left standing undisturbed for a period of 30 days at 20^o^C.

- SOR/2009-165, s. 10
- SOR/2016-170, s. 8(E)

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additivity formulas --- LD~50~ or LC~50~
of mixtures

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  LD~50~ or LC~50~ of a mixture may be determined from the LD~50~ or
  LC~50~ of its ingredients that are present in a concentration of 1% or
  more, using one of the following additivity formulas, as the case may
  be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a solid or a liquid

    ::: {#670946 .Paragraph}
    LD~50~ = 1/\[(P~a~/LD~50a~)+(P~b~/LD~50b~)+\...+(P~n~/LD~50n~)\]

    where

    [LD~50~]{.dfn}
    :   represents the LD~50~ of the mixture,

    [LD~50a~ to LD~50n~]{.dfn}
    :   represent the LD~50~ of each ingredient that is present in a
        concentration of 1% or more, and

    [P~a~ to P~n~]{.dfn}
    :   represent the proportion by weight of each ingredient that is
        present in a concentration of 1% or more; or
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a gas, vapour, dust, mist or fume

    ::: {#670952 .Paragraph}
    LC~50~ = 1/\[(P~a~/LC~50a~)+(P~b~/LC~50b~)+\...+(P~n~/LC~50n~)\]

    where

    [LC~50~]{.dfn}
    :   represents the LC~50~ of the mixture,

    [LC~50a~ to LC~50n~]{.dfn}
    :   represent the LC~50~ of each ingredient that is present in a
        concentration of 1% or more, and

    [P~a~ to P~n~]{.dfn}
    :   represent the proportion by weight of each ingredient that is
        present in a concentration of 1% or more.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complex mixture

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the additivity formulas set out
  in subsection (1), "ingredient" includes a complex mixture.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When LD~50~ or LC~50~ of ingredient is
  not known but can be estimated

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When the LD~50~ or LC~50~ of one or more ingredients
  present in a chemical product in a concentration of 1% or more is not
  known, the responsible person may, in the additivity formulas set out
  in subsection (1), use an estimated LD~50~ or LC~50~ determined in
  accordance with good scientific practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When LD~50~ or LC~50~ of ingredient is
  not known and cannot be estimated

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} When the LD~50~ or LC~50~ of one or more ingredients
  present in a chemical product is not known and cannot be estimated
  from information referred to in paragraph 35(1)(b) or (c), the
  responsible person, in the additivity formulas set out in subsection
  (1), must substitute for the LD~50~ or LC~50~ of the ingredient, the
  LD~50~ or LC~50~ of the most toxic known ingredient that is present in
  the product at a concentration of 1% or more.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion to a 4-hour LC~50~

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** An LC~50~ obtained during
a duration of exposure of other than four hours must be converted to an
LC~50~ equivalent to a duration of exposure of four hours by using one
of the following formulas, as the case may be:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a gas or vapour

  ::: {#670968 .Paragraph}
  LC~50\ of\ 4\ hours~ = (LC~50y~)(y^½^/2)

  where

  [LC~50\ of\ 4\ hours~]{.dfn}
  :   represents the LC~50~ of the mixture for a duration of exposure of
      four hours,

  [LC~50y~]{.dfn}
  :   represents the LC~50~ of the mixture for a duration of exposure of
      y hours, and

  [y]{.dfn}
  :   represents the actual duration of exposure expressed in hours and
      must be no greater than 10 hours; or
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a dust, mist or fume

  ::: {#670974 .Paragraph}
  LC~50\ of\ 4\ hours~ = (LC~50y~)(y/4)

  where

  [LC~50\ of\ 4\ hours~]{.dfn}
  :   represents the LC~50~ of the mixture for a duration of exposure of
      four hours,

  [LC~50y~]{.dfn}
  :   represents the LC~50~ of the mixture for a duration of exposure of
      y hours, and

  [y]{.dfn}
  :   represents the actual duration of exposure expressed in hours and
      must be no greater than 10 hours.
  :::

### [Child-resistant Containers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-671007 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sub-category "toxic"

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2) and
  section 14, a chemical product that is classified under section 33 in
  the sub-category "toxic" must be in a child-resistant container that
  complies with sections 9 to 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- spray container and
  single-drop dispenser

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a chemical product
  classified under section 33 in the sub-category "toxic" that is in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a spray container that cannot be opened and that
    disperses the product as a mist; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a container that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} dispenses only one drop of the product at a time,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} displays the following primary hazard statement
      in the manner set out in sections 17 to 20, subsections 24(1)
      and (3) and sections 25 and 26:

      "THIS CONTAINER IS NOT CHILD-RESISTANT. KEEP OUT OF REACH OF
      CHILDREN."

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Corrosive Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671019 .Part}

### [Classification of Corrosive Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-671020 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person for a corrosive product must determine the
  appropriate sub-category for the product from one or more of the
  following data sources in the following order of precedence:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), human experience data
    for the corrosive product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the table to subsection 42(1), in the case of a
    corrosive product that contains a substance of special concern;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one or more of the following sources in the
    following order of precedence, in the case of a corrosive product
    that contains one or more acids or one or more bases:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the data sources set out in paragraph
      6(1)(b), (c) or (e), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the pH and, if applicable, the acid reserve or
      the alkali reserve of the corrosive product as set out in the
      tables to subsections 42(2) and (3), determined using the test
      methods set out in section 44;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the table to subsection 42(4), in the case of a
    corrosive product that contains a substance, other than an acid or a
    base, that is capable of inducing necrosis or ulceration of
    epithelial tissue at the site of application determined using the
    data sources set out in subsection 43(1); or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsection 42(5), in the case of a corrosive
    product that contains a substance, other than an acid or a base,
    that is capable, when tested using the appropriate test methods set
    out in subsection 43(2), of causing any of the following at the site
    of application:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an erythema or edema of the skin graded at 2 or
      more,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} corneal damage graded at 2 or more,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} iris damage graded at 1 or more, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} conjunctival swelling or redness graded at 2.5
      or more.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification using human experience
  data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the human experience data for a corrosive product
  demonstrates that the product is capable of causing an effect
  described in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph (1)(d), the product must be classified in
    the sub-category "corrosive"; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of subparagraphs (1)(e)(i) to (iv), the product
    must be classified in the sub-category "irritant".

### [Test Methods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-671080 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination --- necrosis and ulceration

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  capability of a substance in a corrosive product to induce necrosis or
  ulceration of epithelial tissue at the site of application must be
  determined from an applicable data source set out in paragraphs
  6(1)(a) to (c) or (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination --- other effects

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The capability of a substance in a corrosive product
  to cause an erythema or edema of the skin, corneal or iris damage or
  conjunctive swelling or redness at the site of application, to the
  grade specified in paragraph 41(1)(e), must be determined from the
  applicable data source set out in paragraphs 6(1)(a) to (c) or (e),
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Draize Test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an erythema or an edema, OECD No.
    404; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of corneal or iris damage or
    conjunctive swelling or redness, OECD No. 405.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of the pH

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person for a corrosive product must determine the pH of
  the product by using good scientific practices that are in accordance
  with a procedure similar to that described in ASTM D 1293, from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a product in the form of a liquid,
    the product as it is dispensed from its container; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a product in the form of a solid,
    paste or gel, or in a form otherwise unsuitable for direct
    measurement of the pH, a 10% aqueous solution of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of acid reserve or alkali
  reserve

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person for a corrosive product must
  determine, where applicable, the acid reserve or the alkali reserve of
  the product by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} titrating, in accordance with the OECD Principles
    of Good Laboratory Practice,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a product in the form of a liquid,
      a suitable aliquot of the product as it is dispensed from its
      container, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a product in the form of a solid,
      paste or gel, or in a form otherwise unsuitable for direct
      measurement of the pH, a suitable aliquot of a 10% aqueous
      solution of the product; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculating

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an acidic product, the amount of
      an alkali, expressed in grams of sodium hydroxide, that is
      required to bring 100 mL of the product in the form of a liquid,
      or 100 g of the product in the form of a solid, paste or gel, to a
      pH of 4.00 ± 0.05, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a basic product, the amount of an
      alkali, expressed in grams of sodium hydroxide, that is
      neutralized when 100 mL of the product in the form of a liquid, or
      100 g of the product in the form of a solid, paste or gel, is
      brought to a pH of 10.00 ± 0.05 by the addition of hydrochloric
      acid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unstable end point

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the end point of the titration referred to in
  subsection (2) is unstable and exhibits drifting, the pH end point
  reached within 30 seconds after the last addition of titrant is to be
  used as the effective end point for classification purposes.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Flammable Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-671156 .Part}

### [Classification of Flammable Products]{.HTitleText2} {#h-671157 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources and tests

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person for a flammable product must determine the
  appropriate sub-category for the product in accordance with section 49
  using human experience data or the results of tests conducted in
  accordance with the test methods set out in sections 50 to 52 and
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A flammable product that is classified in the
  sub-category "combustible" is exempt from the requirements of this
  Part if it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is composed of 50% or more of water and 50% or less
    of water-miscible solvent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} does not sustain combustion when tested in
    accordance with Test L.2.

### [Test Methods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-671183 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flash point --- liquids

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** A flammable product in
the form of a liquid must be tested for its flash point in accordance
with the following standards:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for liquids that have a viscosity of less than 5.8
  mm^2^/s at 37.8°C, ASTM D 56 or ASTM D 3828; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for liquids that have a viscosity of 5.8 mm^2^/s or
  more at 37.8°C, ASTM D 93.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flash point --- solids, pastes and gels

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** A flammable product in
the form of a solid, paste or gel must be tested for its flash point in
accordance with ASTM D 56.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flame projection and flashback --- spray
container

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A flammable product that
is enclosed in a spray container must be tested for its flame projection
and flashback in accordance with the procedure set out in Schedule 1.

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsections 1(1), 48(1) and 49(1) and section 52)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Determining the Flashback and the Length of the Flame Projection of a Flammable Product Enclosed in a Spray Container]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-671286 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#application .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** This test is to be used to determine the flashback and the
  length of the flame projection of a flammable product enclosed in a
  spray container.

### [Apparatus]{.HTitleText1} {#apparatus .SchedHeadL1}

- **2** The following apparatus is to be used in this test:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a flammability tester, as illustrated in Figures 1
    and 2 to the Schedule, that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is constructed so that the spray container can be
      secured in place by means of a holding device, such as a
      three-pronged clamp affixed to a ring stand, in such a manner that
      the discharge from the spray container is in the horizontal plane,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} may include a remote control device by which

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the valve of a pressurized container can be
        activated, such as a side-pull, caliper-type bicycle hand brake,
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the trigger or plunger of a pump-spray
        container can be pneumatically activated, as illustrated in
        Figures 3 and 4 to the Schedule,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has a vertically mounted burner that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} has an inside diameter of 1.2 mm,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} has a Luer-Lock 16-gauge needle affixed to a
        metal tube or another suitable device, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} is placed at a distance of 15 cm from the
        discharge orifice of the spray container, which distance is to
        be measured horizontally between the vertical planes of the
        discharge orifice and the burner orifice, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} has two support frameworks, placed on the
      opposite side of the burner from the spray container, one at a
      distance of 15 cm from the burner and the other at a distance of
      100 cm from the burner, each of which

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} has an internal open space of 35 cm wide by 45
        cm high,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} is constructed from metal or other
        non-flammable material,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} is mounted in a vertical plane perpendicular to
        the direction of discharge from the spray container, and

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} is adjustable in the vertical plane;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cylinder of chemically pure grade propane fitted
    with a regulator capable of delivering pressure to the burner
    appropriate to maintaining a flame height of 5 cm; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} loosely woven cotton fabric commonly referred to as
    cheesecloth that has, in the bleached state, a mass per unit area of
    not less than 35 g/m^2^ and not more than 65 g/m^2^.

### [Test Specimen]{.HTitleText1} {#test-specimen .SchedHeadL1}

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When there are instructions by the
    manufacturer respecting the shaking of the spray container, a test
    must be conducted as follows, using each of three spray containers
    of the same product and of the same size:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if shaking is applicable, shake and discharge the
      container three times in the manner described in paragraph
      4(9)(a); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if shaking is not applicable, discharge the
      container three times in the manner described in paragraph
      4(9)(b).

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} When there are no instructions by the manufacturer
    respecting the shaking of the spray container, a test must be
    conducted as follows, using each of three spray containers of the
    same product and of the same size:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without shaking the container, discharge it three
      times in the manner described in paragraph 4(9)(b); and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shake and discharge the container a further three
      times in the manner described in paragraph 4(9)(a).

### [Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#procedure .SchedHeadL1}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A test

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be conducted at a room temperature of 22 ±
      2°C in the absence of air currents, with an allowance made for a
      clearance of 50 cm beyond the support framework that is set at a
      distance of 100 cm from the burner; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may be conducted in a fume hood with the exhaust
      fan turned off and the protecting door lowered.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any fume must be removed from the test area and any
    residue cleaned up after each discharge.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before testing, each spray container must be

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintained at a temperature of 22 ± 2°C for at
      least four hours; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} primed by

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pressurized container,
        discharging the container for five seconds, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pump-spray container,
        activating the trigger or pump using each of 18 N, 36 N and 54 N
        of force for each possible nozzle position until

        - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the "stream" position, a
          continuous stream is produced, and

        - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the "spray" position, a mist
          is produced.

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Install the first spray container in the holding
    device and ensure that the burner orifice is 15 cm from the
    discharge orifice of the spray container in the horizontal plane and
    5 cm below it in the vertical plane and that the discharge orifice
    points in the direction of the burner.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Adjust the burner to give a flame height of 5 cm
    and release a single trial discharge from the spray container.

  - [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the operation set out in subsection (5) does not
    produce a flame projection, lower the burner orifice by 5 cm and
    adjust the burner to give a flame height of 12 cm.

  - [(7)]{.lawlabel} Attach the cheesecloth to the flammability tester
    with bulldog clips or in any other manner so as to cover the entire
    internal space of the support framework set at a distance of 15 cm
    from the burner and verify that the cheesecloth is at a proper
    horizontal distance from the vertical plane of the burner orifice on
    the opposite side of the burner from the spray container.

  - [(8)]{.lawlabel} Adjust the height of the support framework so that
    the cheesecloth will intercept the line of flame projection.

  - [(9)]{.lawlabel} Prepare the spray container in accordance with the
    manufacturer's instructions and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if shaking is applicable,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} shake vigorously for five seconds, or for the
        period specified in the manufacturer's instructions,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} install the spray container in the holding
        device, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 15 seconds after the cessation of shaking,
        release the discharge in accordance with subsection (10); or

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-165, s. 20\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if shaking is not applicable, install the spray
      container in the holding device and release the discharge in
      accordance with subsection (10), allowing the spray container to
      stand for at least 60 seconds between discharges.

  - [(10)]{.lawlabel} Discharge the spray container

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pressurized container, for five
      seconds or until the cheesecloth ignites; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pump-spray container, for three
      sprays or until the cheesecloth ignites.

  - [(11)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a pump-spray container, repeat the
    procedure set out in paragraph (10)(b) for each of 18 N, 36 N and 54
    N of force for each possible nozzle position.

  - [(12)]{.lawlabel} If the cheesecloth attached to the support
    framework that is set at a distance of 15 cm from the burner
    ignites, the remaining discharges referred to in section 3 must be
    carried out in accordance with subsections (1) to (11) using a new
    piece of cheesecloth attached to the support framework that is set
    at a distance of 100 cm from the burner.

### [Determination and Reporting of Flame Projection and Flashback]{.HTitleText1} {#determination-and-reporting-of-flame-projection-and-flashback .SchedHeadL1}

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If at any time during the test the
    cheesecloth that is mounted at a distance of 100 cm from the burner
    is ignited, the length of the flame projection is 100 cm or more.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} If at any time during the test the cheesecloth that
    is mounted at a distance of 15 cm from the burner is ignited and, at
    all times during the test, the cheesecloth that is mounted at a
    distance of 100 cm from the burner remains unignited, the length of
    the flame projection is 15 cm or more but less than 100 cm.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} If at any time during the test the cheesecloth
    mounted at a distance of 15 cm from the burner is not ignited but
    there is a flame projection, the length of the flame projection is
    less than 15 cm.

- **6** The following test results must be recorded and kept for a
  period of at least three years after the testing is carried out:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the length of the flame projection

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pressurized container, for each
      discharge, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pump-spray container, for each
      discharge at each nozzle position and each force applied;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lack of flame projection resulting from any of
    the test discharges; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any flashback.

## [SCHEDULE 3]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 7)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test Method for Determining Whether a Container Leaks]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-671376 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  **1** The definitions in this section apply in this Schedule.

  [[seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a container, means any
      device or membrane under the closure that covers the opening of
      the container, including any membrane that is part of the
      container, such as a heat induction seal or a sonic seal. It does
      not include a liner or a mechanism that is an integral part of the
      closure. ([sceau d'étanchéité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[test sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[test sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a container of a chemical
        product, the container filled with the product in the manner in
        which it is sold to the consumer; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an empty container that is
        destined to receive a chemical product, the container filled
        with the product that it is destined to receive. ([échantillon
        d'essai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Test Method]{.HTitleText1} {#test-method .SchedHeadL1}

#### [Preparation of Sample for Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#preparation-of-sample-for-testing .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remove seal

  **2** Except in the case of a single-use container,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} open the test sample;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remove any seal that is present under the closure
    in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions or, if no
    instructions are provided, puncture the seal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reclose the sample as tightly as possible without
    stripping the threads of the closure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bring to room temperature

  **3** Place the test sample in a test location that has a constant
  temperature of 23 ± 2°C for at least four hours to permit the
  container and its contents to reach the temperature of the test
  location.

#### [Testing for Leaks]{.HTitleText2} {#testing-for-leaks .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Position of test sample

  **4** Position the test sample

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} over a clean piece of blotting paper or another
    paper that stains on contact with a liquid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an inverted position at a 45° angle below the
    horizontal; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with the closure in the lowest possible position
    and free of any obstruction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examine paper

  **5** After one hour, remove the test sample and examine the paper
  described in paragraph 4(a) for evidence that any of the contents have
  leaked from the sample.

#### [Pass or Fail]{.HTitleText2} {#pass-or-fail .SchedHeadL2}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fail

  **6** If an examination of the paper described in paragraph 4(a)
  discloses any trace of the contents of the test sample, the sample has
  failed the leakage test and a container of the same type as the test
  sample must not be used for a chemical product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pass

  **7** If no trace of the contents of the test sample is found on the
  paper described in paragraph 4(a), the sample has passed the leakage
  test and a container of the same type as the test sample may be used
  for a chemical product.
