## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Labelling --- Awareness of Health Hazards from Use of Vaping Products]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1206892 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1206893 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Part.

  [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
      surface area of a vaping product or package on which the
      information referred to in this Part can be displayed. It does not
      include the surface area of the bottom, of any seam or of any
      concave or convex surface near the top or the bottom of a vaping
      product or package. ([aire d'affichage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package that
      contains a vaping product and that is displayed or visible under
      normal or customary conditions of sale or use of the vaping
      product. ([emballage extérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[interior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[interior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the innermost
      package of a vaping product, including a blister pack. ([emballage
      intérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package that contains a
      collection of two or more units of vaping
      products. ([trousse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      vaping product or package, the part of the display surface that is
      displayed or visible under normal or customary conditions of sale
      or use of the vaping product. It includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product or package that
        has a rectangular cuboid shape, one of the largest sides of the
        display surface;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping product or package that
        has a cylindrical shape, the larger of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area of the top, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 40% of the area obtained by multiplying the
          circumference of the vaping product or package by the height
          of the display surface;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bag, the largest side of the
        bag; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the largest surface of the
        vaping product or package that is not less than 40% of the
        display surface. ([aire d'affichage principale]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an
      individual or entity that only packages or labels vaping products
      on behalf of a manufacturer. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([dispositif de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      paragraph (c) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([pièce de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by paragraph (d) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([substance de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- package

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Part, a reference to an exterior package or
  interior package does not include package liners or shipping
  containers or any outer wrapping, including a box, that is not
  displayed or visible under normal or customary conditions of sale or
  use of a vaping product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- refill vaping
  product

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this Part, a reference to a vaping product that is
  intended to be used for the purpose of refilling another vaping
  product does not include a reference to a vaping device or vaping
  part.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of meanings in [Tobacco and
  Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions used in this Part
  have the same meaning as in the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
  Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5).

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1206918 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retail sale --- vaping products

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies to every vaping product that is intended for retail sale
  in Canada, as well as to its packaging.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vaping products --- otherwise furnished

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part also applies to every vaping product that
  is intended to be otherwise furnished, in Canada, at a point of sale
  that is a retail establishment where vaping products are ordinarily
  sold, as well as to the packaging of such a vaping product.

### [Purpose]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1206926 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling --- [Tobacco and Vaping
Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 15.1 and 15.2 of the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
  Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5), this Part sets out the labelling requirements
  that manufacturers of vaping products must meet

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of information that is required to be
    displayed about vaping products and their emissions and about the
    health hazards and health effects arising from the use of those
    products and from their emissions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the manner of displaying the
    information referred to in paragraph (a) on vaping products and
    their packages, as well as on leaflets and on any tags attached to
    vaping products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of sections 30.42 and 30.45 of the
  [Tobacco and Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5), this Part also
  sets out the requirements that manufacturers of vaping products must
  meet in respect of the manner of displaying certain non-mandatory
  expressions on vaping products and their packages.

### [Vaping Products Containing Nicotine]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1206934 .Subheading}

#### [Nicotine Concentration Statement]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1206935 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- vaping product containing
nicotine

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every vaping product that
contains nicotine must display a nicotine concentration statement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- nicotine concentration

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The nicotine concentration
statement must set out the concentration of nicotine in the vaping
substance, expressed in milligrams per millilitre. The concentration
must be preceded by "Nicotine --- " and followed by the unit of measure
"mg/mL".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- vaping device and vaping
part

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The nicotine concentration
statement must be displayed, in the case of a vaping device or vaping
part that is not packaged, on the main display panel or on a tag.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- packaged vaping device and
vaping part

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nicotine concentration statement must be displayed, in the case of a
  vaping device or vaping part that is packaged, on the main display
  panel of the exterior package.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple layers of packaging

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a vaping device or vaping part is packaged in
  multiple layers of packaging, the nicotine concentration statement
  must also be displayed on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the main display panel of the vaping device or
    vaping part, as the case may be; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the main display panel of the interior package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- refill vaping product

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nicotine concentration statement must be displayed, in the case of a
  vaping product that is intended to be used for the purpose of
  refilling another vaping product, on the main display panel of that
  refill vaping product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- packaged refill vaping
  product

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the refill vaping product is packaged, the
  nicotine concentration statement must also be displayed on the main
  display panel of the exterior package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- vaping products in kit

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  nicotine concentration statement must be displayed, in the case of a
  vaping device or vaping part that is packaged in a kit, on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the main display panel of the vaping device or
    vaping part, as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the main display panel of the interior package of
    the vaping device or vaping part, as the case may be; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a tag.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kit --- refill vaping product

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The nicotine concentration statement must be
  displayed, in the case of a vaping product that is intended to be used
  for the purpose of refilling another vaping product and that is
  packaged in a kit, on the main display panel of that refill vaping
  product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- kit

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  vaping products that contain nicotine are packaged in a kit, the
  nicotine concentration statement that is required for each of those
  vaping products must be displayed on the main display panel of the
  kit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One nicotine concentration statement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if two or more of the vaping products in the
  kit have the same concentration of nicotine, the nicotine
  concentration statement that is applicable to those vaping products
  must be displayed only once on the main display panel of the kit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- prepackaged product

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a vaping product is a
[[prepackaged product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
2(1) of the [Consumer Packaging and Labelling Act](/eng/acts/C-38), and
its identity is shown, as required under subparagraph 10(b)(ii) of that
Act, in terms of its common or generic name or in terms of its function,
the nicotine concentration statement must be displayed immediately below
that name or function.

### [Vaping Products Without Nicotine]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1207017 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No nicotine

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A vaping product may
display an expression permitted by section 23 if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vaping substance, it does not
  contain nicotine; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other vaping product that contains
  a vaping substance, the product does not contain nicotine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted expressions

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** Only one of the following
expressions is permitted to be displayed on a vaping product:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "Nicotine-free" in the English version of the
  expression and "[Sans nicotine]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" in the French
  version of the expression;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "No nicotine" in the English version of the
  expression and "[Aucune nicotine]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" in the French
  version of the expression; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Does not contain nicotine" in the English version of
  the expression and "[Ne contient pas de nicotine]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}" in the French version of the expression.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An expression permitted
by section 23 must be displayed only on the display surface of a vaping

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Labelling and Packaging --- Protection of Human Health or Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1207113 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1207114 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Part.

  [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[display surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
      surface area of an immediate container or of an exterior package
      on which the information referred to in this Part can be
      displayed. It does not include the surface area of the bottom, of
      any seam or of any concave or convex surface near the top or the
      bottom of the immediate container or exterior package. ([aire
      d'affichage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package, other
      than an immediate container, that contains a vaping product and
      that is displayed or visible under normal or customary conditions
      of sale of the vaping product to a consumer. ([emballage
      extérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazard symbol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazard symbol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the pictograph and its
      frame as set out in Schedule 1. ([pictogramme de
      danger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the container,
      including a vaping device or vaping part, that is in direct
      contact with a vaping substance or which it is reasonably
      foreseeable will be in direct contact with such a
      substance. ([contenant immédiat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}).

  [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[main display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of the
      display surface that is displayed or visible under normal or
      customary conditions of sale to the consumer. It includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rectangular immediate
        container or exterior package, the largest side of the display
        surface;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cylindrical immediate
        container or exterior package, the larger of

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the area of the top, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 40% of the area obtained by multiplying the
          circumference of the immediate container or exterior package
          by the height of the display surface;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bag, the largest side of the
        bag; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the largest surface of the
        immediate container or exterior package, as the case may be,
        that is not less than 40% of the display surface. ([aire
        d'affichage principale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer that, in Canada, manufactures
        a vaping device or vaping part or places a vaping substance in
        its immediate container; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer, in the case of a vaping device or
        vaping part that is imported or a vaping substance that is
        imported in its immediate
        container. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      paragraphs (a) and (b) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([dispositif de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      paragraph (c) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([pièce de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
      Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([produit de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by paragraph (d) of the definition [[vaping
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the [Tobacco and
      Vaping Products Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([substance de
      vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of "should"

#### [Child-Resistant Containers]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1207174 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- child-resistant container

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  immediate container that is a vaping device or vaping part, as well as
  every other immediate container that contains a vaping substance that
  has a nicotine concentration of 0.1 mg/mL or more, must meet the
  requirements set out in section 51 for a child-resistant container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if exposure to a vaping
  substance that is in a form other than an aerosol is impossible during
  the reasonably foreseeable use of the container.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable standard

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  child-resistant container must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be constructed so that it can be opened only by
    operating, puncturing or removing one of its functional and
    necessary parts using a tool that is not supplied with the
    container; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the child test protocol requirements of one of
    the following standards or a standard that is at least equivalent:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Standards Association Standard
      CAN/CSA Z76.1-16, entitled Reclosable child-resistant packages, as
      amended from time to time,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the International Organization for
      Standardization Standard ISO 8317:2015, entitled Child-resistant
      packaging --- Requirements and testing procedures for reclosable
      packages, as amended from time to time, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the United States Code of Federal Regulations,
      Title 16: Commercial Practices, section 1700.20, entitled "Testing
      procedure for special packaging", as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amended standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), if a person has applied the
  child test protocol requirements of one of the standards referred to
  in that paragraph to a child-resistant container, as the standard read
  immediately before the day on which an amended version of that
  standard is published, the person may continue to apply those
  requirements to the child-resistant container during the period of 180
  days after the day on which the amended standard is published.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintain characteristics

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A child-resistant
container must maintain the characteristics of a child-resistant
container throughout the useful life of the vaping substance that is
placed in it, or in the case of a refillable container, throughout its
useful life, under normal conditions of storage, sale and use.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person, using good scientific practices, must evaluate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the compatibility of the vaping substance with its
    child-resistant container to determine that the chemical or physical
    properties of the substance will not compromise or interfere with
    the proper functioning of the container; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the physical wear and stress factors and the force
    required for opening and closing the container to determine that the
    proper functioning of the container will be maintained for the
    number of openings and closings reasonably foreseeable for the size
    and contents of the container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[good scientific practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the development of test data, conditions
        and procedures that are in accordance with or equivalent to
        those set out in the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and
        Development document entitled OECD Guidelines for the Testing of
        Chemicals, as amended from time to time;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for laboratory practices, practices that are in
        accordance with the principles set out in the Organisation for
        Economic Co-operation and Development document entitled OECD
        Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of the OECD
        Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and Compliance
        Monitoring, as amended from time to time; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for human experience data, a peer-reviewed
        study of clinical cases. ([bonnes pratiques
        scientifiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[human experience data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[human experience data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means data,
      collected in accordance with good scientific practices, that
      demonstrates that injury to or poisoning of a human has or has not
      resulted from

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exposure to a vaping substance; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasonably foreseeable use of a vaping
        substance or immediate container by a consumer, including by a
        child. ([données de l'expérience humaine]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person must prepare and maintain documents containing the
  following information and must keep those documents for a period of at
  least three years after the day on which the child-resistant container
  is manufactured or imported:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the specifications critical to the child-resistant
    characteristics of the container, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the physical measurements within which the
      container retains its child-resistant characteristics,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the torque that must be applied
      to open or close the container, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the compatibility of the container and its
      closure system with the vaping substance that is to be placed into
      it; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test results that demonstrate that the
    container and its closure system meet the requirements of a standard
    referred to in paragraph 51(1)(b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspector's request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a vaping substance for which a
  child-resistant container is required, the responsible person must
  provide an inspector with any of the documents that the inspector
  requests within 15 days after the day on which the request is
  received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions for opening and closing

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Directions for opening and, if
  applicable, closing a child-resistant container that meets the
  requirements of paragraph 51(1)(b) must be displayed on the closure
  system of the immediate container or on the display surface of the
  immediate container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- vaping device and vaping
  part

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, in the case of an immediate container that
  is a vaping device or vaping part, the directions may be displayed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a package containing the vaping device or vaping
    part, or on a leaflet that is inserted in the package; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not packaged, on a tag that is attached to
    the vaping device or vaping part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single-use immediate container

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (5),
  the following statement must be displayed on the display surface of a
  child-resistant container that is an immediate container whose
  contents are to be used in their entirety immediately after the
  container is opened:

  "USE ENTIRE CONTENTS ON OPENING. THIS CONTAINER IS NOT CHILD-RESISTANT
  ONCE OPENED."

  ["UTILISER LA TOTALITÉ DU CONTENU APRÈS OUVERTURE. UNE FOIS OUVERT, LE
  CONTENANT N'EST PLUS UN CONTENANT PROTÈGE-ENFANTS."]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exterior package

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the statement must also be
  displayed on the display surface of the exterior package, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- immediate container

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the main display panel of the immediate container
  has an area of less than 45 cm^2^, the statement need not be displayed
  on the display surface of the immediate container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- exterior package

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the main display panel of the exterior package has
  an area of less than 45 cm^2^, the statement must be displayed on a
  tag attached to the immediate container.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- small immediate container
  that is not packaged

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the immediate container has a small size and is
  not packaged, the statement must be displayed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the main display panel has an area greater than
    15 cm^2^ but less than 45 cm^2^, on the display surface of the
    immediate container or on a tag attached to the immediate container;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the main display panel of the immediate
    container has an area equal to or less than 15 cm^2^, on a tag
    attached to the immediate container.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxicity information

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following information must be displayed on the display surface of the
  immediate container of a vaping substance that contains nicotine in a
  concentration of 0.1 mg/mL or more:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an exact reproduction, except with respect to size,
    of the hazard symbol set out in Schedule 1; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the following toxicity warning and first aid
    treatment statement:

    "**POISON:** if swallowed, call a Poison Control Centre or doctor
    immediately."

    "[**POISON :** en cas d'ingestion, appeler immédiatement un centre
    antipoison ou un médecin.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- vaping device and vaping
  part

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the immediate container is a vaping device or
  vaping part and it is not packaged, the information must be displayed
  on a tag attached to the vaping device or vaping part, as the case may
  be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exterior package

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information must also be displayed on the display
  surface of the exterior package, if any.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- exposure to vaping
  substance

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} However, the information is not required if exposure
  to a vaping substance that is in a form other than an aerosol is
  impossible during the reasonably foreseeable use of the container.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vaping device and vaping part

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 56 and
57, the information required by those sections need not be displayed on
the display surface of an immediate container that is a vaping device or
vaping part.

#### [Presentation of Information]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1207251 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Languages, legibility and durability

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The information required
under this Part must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be displayed in both official languages;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be clear and legible and remain so throughout the
  useful life of the vaping substance, or in the case of a refillable
  immediate container, throughout its useful life, under normal
  conditions of transportation, storage, sale and use; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be displayed in black type on a white background.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information in words --- general rules

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  required information is set out in words, the words must be displayed
  in a standard sans-serif type that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not compressed, expanded or decorative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as illustrated in Schedule 2, has a large x-height
    relative to the ascender or descender of the type;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an immediate container or exterior
    package whose main display panel has an area equal to or greater
    than 45 cm^2^, has a minimum height of 3 mm and a minimum body size
    of 8 points; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an immediate container or exterior
    package whose main display panel has an area of less than 45 cm^2^,
    has a minimum height of 2 mm and a minimum body size of 6 points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Measurement of height of type

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The height of the type must be determined in
  accordance with subsection 28(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Characters in text

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each character in the text of the required
  information must have the same font and type size.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of presentation of ingredients

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  ingredients set out in the list of ingredients referred to in section
  47 must,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of ingredients that are present in the
    vaping substance in a concentration of 1% or more, be set out in
    descending order of their proportions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of ingredients that are present in the
    vaping substance in a concentration of less than 1%, be set out in
    any order and immediately following the ingredients referred to in
    paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concentration of ingredient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, when the concentration of an
  ingredient in a vaping substance is expressed as a percentage, the
  percentage represents the ratio of the weight of that ingredient to
  the volume of the vaping substance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concentration --- combination of
  flavours

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of ingredients that are added to a vaping
  substance solely to produce a combination of flavours, the proportion
  of the total concentration of those ingredients must be used to
  determine the order of setting out the indication "flavour" in the
  list of ingredients.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions to open and close

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  directions for opening and, if applicable, closing a child-resistant
  container that are referred to in section 55 must be set out using
  either or both

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} words;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a diagram or self-explanatory symbol.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official languages

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the directions are set out in words, they may be
  displayed in one official language on the closure system of the
  child-resistant container and repeated elsewhere on the display
  surface of the container in the other official language.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diagram or symbol --- colour contrast

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the directions are set out in a diagram or symbol,
  the contrast between the colour used for the information and that used
  for the background must be equivalent to at least a 70% screen of
  black on white.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hazard symbol --- minimum diameter

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The hazard symbol
referred to in paragraph 57(1)(a) must,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an immediate container or exterior
  package whose main display panel has an area equal to or greater than
  45 cm², have a diameter at least as large as the diameter of an
  imaginary circle that has an area equal to 3% of the main display
  panel; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an immediate container or exterior
  package whose main display panel has an area of less than 45 cm², have
  a diameter of at least 6 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxicity warning and first aid treatment
statement

**[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** The toxicity warning and
first aid treatment statement referred to in paragraph 57(1)(b) must be
displayed in such a manner that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the word "**POISON**" is in bold-faced, upper case
  letters; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the statement is located immediately beside or below
  the hazard symbol referred to in paragraph 57(1)(a).
