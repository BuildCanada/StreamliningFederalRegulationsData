## Foreign Claims (Bulgaria) Settlement Regulations {#foreign-claims-bulgaria-settlement-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/66-506
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

Registration 1966-11-03

Regulations Respecting the Settlement of Claims With Bulgaria

P.C. 1966-2062 1966-11-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs and the Minister of Finance,
pursuant to any enactment of the Parliament of Canada for defraying the
several charges and expenses of the Public Service from and after the
1st day of April, 1966 that provides for payments out of the Foreign
Claims Fund, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the settlement of claims with
Bulgaria](/eng/regulations/SOR-66-506).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Foreign Claims (Bulgaria) Settlement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-66-506).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    Government of Canada and the Government of the People's Republic of
    Bulgaria relating to the settlement of financial matters signed at
    Ottawa on June 30, 1966; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Claim]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a claim of the Government of
    Canada, a Canadian citizen or a Canadian juridical person against
    the Bulgarian Government in respect of property, rights, interests
    and debts in Bulgaria which have been affected directly or
    indirectly by Bulgarian measures of nationalization, expropriation
    or other similar measures which have taken effect before June 30,
    1966; ([réclamation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Foreign Claims Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Foreign Claims Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the special
    account in the Consolidated Revenue Fund known as the Foreign Claims
    Fund established by Vote 22a of Appropriation Act No. 7, 1966; and
    ([Fonds pour les réclamations étrangères]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Ministers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ministers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Secretary of State for
    External Affairs and the Minister of Finance.
    ([Ministres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Payment to the Canadian
Government by the Government of the People's Republic of Bulgaria of the
sum of \$40,000 in accordance with Articles I and II of the Agreement
constitutes satisfaction by Bulgaria of its obligation to Canada and
Canadian claimants in respect of any claim subject to the Agreement.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Ministers may make payments out of the Foreign Claims Fund to any
  Canadian claimant who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gave notice of his claim to the Government of
    Canada prior to June 30, 1966; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishes to the satisfaction of the Ministers
    that he is entitled to receive compensation under Articles I and III
    of the Agreement in respect of a claim.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a Canadian claimant has died on or after the
  30th day of June, 1966, the Ministers may pay to the personal
  representative of the Canadian claimant or to such other person as
  appears to the Ministers to be entitled to the assets of the Canadian
  claimant any amount that they would have paid under subsection (1) to
  the Canadian claimant if he had survived.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  payment to any person of an amount that the Ministers designate as a
  final payment in respect of the claim of a Canadian claimant under
  Articles I and III of the Agreement constitutes full satisfaction of
  all claims in respect of the Canadian claimant under those Articles.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the Ministers make a final payment, they shall
  secure from the person to whom the payment is to be made in
  consideration of the payment, a release in the form that they consider
  satisfactory in respect of the claim for which payment is to be made.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  administering these Regulations, the Ministers may refer any matter to
  an adviser to be appointed with the approval of the Treasury Board
  called the "Adviser on Claims under the Agreement" but the Ministers
  are not bound by the findings and recommendations of the adviser.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The adviser shall, if so requested by the Ministers,
  enquire into and make reports and recommendations to the Ministers
  concerning

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the validity of claims by Canadian claimants under
    the Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount to which a Canadian claimant is entitled
    under Articles I and II of the Agreement in respect of a claim;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the division as between the Canadian claimants of
    the moneys to be paid out by the Ministers; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other matter arising out of the administration
    of these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Treasury Board shall determine

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration to be paid to the adviser; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the adviser's remuneration and expenses
    shall be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} paid out of the Foreign Claims Fund,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paid out of moneys provided by Parliament, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} apportioned in such manner as Treasury Board
      may direct between the Foreign Claims Fund and the moneys provided
      by Parliament.
