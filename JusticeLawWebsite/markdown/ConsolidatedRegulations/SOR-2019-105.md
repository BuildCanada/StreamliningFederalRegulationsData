## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123158 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of Marine Protected Area

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  area of the sea that is designated by section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD 83) reference system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates for points

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The geographical coordinates of the points referred
  to in sections 2 and 3 are set out in the schedule.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123166 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  of the sea depicted in the schedule that is bounded by the following
  lines is designated as the Laurentian Channel Marine Protected Area:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a series of rhumb lines drawn from points 1 to 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a geodesic line drawn from point 8 to point 9; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a series of rhumb lines drawn from points 9 to 18
    and then back to point 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of five metres and the water column above the
  seabed.

## [Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Marine Protected Area
consists of the following management zones, each of which is depicted in
the schedule:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1a, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from point 6 to point 7, then to points 19 to 23 and then back
  to point 6;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1b, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from points 24 to 27 and then back to point 24;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2a, which is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from points 1 to 6, then to point 23, then to point 22, then to
  points 14 to 18 and then back to point 1; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2b, which is bounded by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a series of rhumb lines drawn from point 7 to point
    24, then to point 27, then to point 26, then to point 25 and then to
    point 8,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a geodesic line drawn from point 8 to point 9, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a series of rhumb lines drawn from points 9 to
    13, then to point 21, then to point 20, then to point 19 and then
    back to point 7.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123185 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited to carry
out any activity in the Marine Protected Area that disturbs, damages,
destroys or removes from the Marine Protected Area any living marine
organism or any part of its habitat or is likely to do so.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123188 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 4, the
following activities may be carried out in the Marine Protected Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the navigation of vessels provided that there is no
  anchoring in Zone 1a or 1b;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing, other than commercial fishing, that is
  authorized under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the laying, maintenance and repair of cables in Zones
  2a and 2b, provided that it is not likely to destroy the habitat of
  any living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is carried out for the purpose of
  public safety, national defence, national security, law enforcement or
  responding to an emergency; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is part of an activity plan that
  has been approved by the Minister.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123196 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission to Minister

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may submit to the Minister an activity plan for the carrying
  out of scientific research or monitoring or educational activities in
  the Marine Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The activity plan must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the activity plan is submitted by an institution
    or organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible
    for the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number
    and email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
    use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
    registration number, its radio call sign and the name, address,
    telephone number and email address of its owner, master and any
    operator;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
    its purpose, the methods or techniques that are to be used to carry
    out the activity and the data to be collected;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographical coordinates of the site of the
    proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
    within the boundaries of the Marine Protected Area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates and alternative dates on which
    the activity is to be carried out;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the equipment that is to be used, the
    means by which it will be deployed and retrieved and the methods by
    which it is to be anchored or moored;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the type and quantity of samples that are
    to be collected;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited
    during the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Area --- other
    than substances that are authorized under the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) to be deposited in the navigation of a
    vessel --- and the quantity and concentration of each substance;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse environmental effects
    that are likely to result from carrying out the proposed activity
    and of any measures that are to be taken to monitor, avoid, minimize
    or mitigate those effects;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
    monitoring activity or educational activity that the person has
    carried out or anticipates carrying out in the Marine Protected
    Area; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
    work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity and
    its anticipated date of completion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of activity plan

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are not likely to destroy the habitat of any
    living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase knowledge of the habitat
      of any living marine organism or the biodiversity or the ecosystem
      structure and function of the Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will serve to assist in the management of the
      Marine Protected Area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational activities set out in the plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not likely to damage, destroy or remove from
      the Marine Protected Area any living marine organism or any part
      of its habitat, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase public awareness of the
      Marine Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister must not approve
  an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    as defined in subsection 34(1) of the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the substance is
    authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
    activities carried out in the Marine Protected Area, are such that
    the activity is likely to destroy the habitat of any living marine
    organism in the Marine Protected Area or adversely impact the
    biodiversity or the ecosystem structure and function of the Marine
    Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 60 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Post-activity report

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister approves an activity plan, the person who submitted it must
  provide the Minister with a post-activity report within 90 days after
  the last day of the activity. The report must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the data collected during the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type and quantity of any sample that was
    collected, the date of collection and the geographic coordinates of
    the sampling site;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the effectiveness of any measures
    taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or mitigate the adverse
    environmental effects of the activity; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any event that occurred during the
    activity and that was not anticipated in the activity plan, if the
    event could result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or
    removal from the Marine Protected Area of any living marine organism
    or any part of its habitat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, reports or other works

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must also provide the Minister with a copy
  of any study, report or other work that results from the activity and
  is related to the conservation and protection of the Marine Protected
  Area. The study, report or other work must be provided within 90 days
  after the day on which it is completed.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123240 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
