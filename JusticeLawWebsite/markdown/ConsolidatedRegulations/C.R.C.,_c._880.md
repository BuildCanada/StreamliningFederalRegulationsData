Regulations Made Under the Government Employees Compensation Act

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581046 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Government Employees Compensation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._880).

## [Compensation to Employee or Dependants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-581048 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An employee who is disabled
by reason of a disease that is not an industrial disease but is due to
the nature of his employment and peculiar to or characteristic of the
particular process, trade or occupation in which he is employed at the
time the disease was contracted and the dependants of a deceased
employee whose death is caused by reason of such a disease, are entitled
to receive compensation at the same rate as they would be entitled to
receive under the [Government Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5)
if the disease were an industrial disease, and the right to and the
amount of such compensation shall be determined by the same board,
officers or authorities and in the same manner as if the disease were an
industrial disease.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  employee, except an employee engaged locally outside Canada, who is
  disabled by reason of any disease that results from the environmental
  conditions of any place outside Canada to which he was assigned and
  the dependants of a deceased employee whose death is caused by reason
  of such a disease, are entitled to receive compensation at the same
  rate as they would be entitled to receive under the [Government
  Employees Compensation Act](/eng/acts/G-5) if the disease were an
  industrial disease, and the right to and the amount of such
  compensation shall be determined by the same board, officers or
  authorities and in the same manner as if the disease were an
  industrial disease.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining whether an employee
  or his dependants are entitled to receive compensation under this
  section, a certificate of a medical doctor employed by the Department
  of National Health and Welfare certifying that the disease

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is clearly attributable to environmental conditions
    of the place outside Canada to which the employee was assigned, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/86-942, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  shall be accepted as [prima facie]{.otherLang lang="la"} proof that
  the disease results from the environmental conditions of the place
  outside Canada to which the employee was assigned.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-942, s. 1
:::
