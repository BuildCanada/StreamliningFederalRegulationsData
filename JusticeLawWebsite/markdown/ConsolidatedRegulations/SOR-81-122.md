## Import of Turkeys and Turkey Products Permit {#import-of-turkeys-and-turkey-products-permit .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-122
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1981-02-10

General Import Permit No. 7
::::

:::::::: section
:::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to section 10 of
the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby cancels
[General Import Permit No. 7](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-122), C.R.C.,
c. 635, and, pursuant to section 5 of the Import Permit
Regulations[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBCA},
hereby issues the annexed [General Import Permit
No. 7](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-122) in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870336 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the [Import of Turkeys and Turkey Products
Permit](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-122).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870338 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, under the
authority of this General Import Permit, import into Canada from any
country

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one turkey, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} turkey parts not exceeding 10 kg

for each non-commercial importation, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} turkey poults, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} turkey contained in jars or cans.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-116, Sch. II, s. 1
- SI/83-185, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where completion and
validation of a Customs entry form is required in respect of any turkeys
that are imported under the authority of this General Import Permit,
that form shall be endorsed "Imported under the authority of General
Import Permit No. 7" or "[Importé en vertu de la Licence générale
d'importation n^o^ 7]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SI/83-185, s.
2\]]{.Repealed}
