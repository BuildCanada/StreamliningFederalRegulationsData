## [Overview]{.HTitleText1} {#h-752831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of these Regulations is to enhance the security of the
  Canadian ferry transportation system through the establishment of a
  framework for detecting security threats and taking preventive
  measures against security incidents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations are divided into five Parts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Part 1 sets out general matters that apply to the
    entire Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part 2 sets out the security documentation that
    must be obtained from the Minister and the manner in which it must
    be obtained;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Part 3 sets out the responsibilities of the
    operators of domestic ferries and domestic ferry facilities;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Part 4 sets out the roles and responsibilities of
    the personnel involved in the security of domestic ferries and
    domestic ferry facilities; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Part 5 provides a framework for conducting security
    assessments and for establishing, implementing and maintaining
    security plans.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-752842 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Marine Transportation
      Security Act](/eng/acts/M-0.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[certain dangerous cargoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[certain dangerous cargoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
      assigned by the definition [[certain dangerous
      cargoes]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[CDCs]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
      in subsection 1(1) of the [Marine Transportation Security
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144). ([certaines
      cargaisons dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[domestic ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[domestic ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
      entitled to fly the Canadian flag, carries passengers on a regular
      schedule and operates on a route set out in Schedule
      1. ([traversier intérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[domestic ferry facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[domestic ferry facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a marine
      facility set out in Schedule 2. ([installation pour traversiers
      intérieurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[interface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[interface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an interaction between

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a domestic ferry and another vessel or a marine
        facility; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a domestic ferry facility and a
        vessel. ([interface]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device, including a card,
      issued by an operator of a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry
      facility that is designed to allow access to a restricted area in
      a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry
      facility. ([clé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[MARSEC level 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[MARSEC level 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the level for which
      minimum security procedures are maintained as set out in the
      approved security plan. ([niveau MARSEC 1]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[MARSEC level 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[MARSEC level 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the level for which
      security procedures additional to those of MARSEC level 1 are
      maintained for a limited period because of heightened risk of a
      security threat or security incident. ([niveau MARSEC
      2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[MARSEC level 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[MARSEC level 3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the level for which
      security procedures additional to those of MARSEC level 1 and
      MARSEC level 2 are maintained for a limited period when a security
      threat or security incident is probable or imminent, whether or
      not the specific target is identified. ([niveau MARSEC
      3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[passenger]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([passager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[port administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[port administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator of a marine facility that is a
        port authority incorporated under section 8 of the [Canada
        Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator of a public port designated under
        regulations made under section 65 of the [Canada Marine
        Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7); or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a group of marine facilities, in close
        proximity to each other, whose operators agree with each other
        to be subject to sections 362 to 375 of the [Marine
        Transportation Security
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144). ([organisme
        portuaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[restricted area pass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[restricted area pass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document
      issued by an operator of a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry
      facility that entitles the holder to have access, during a
      specified period, to specific restricted areas in domestic ferries
      or domestic ferry facilities. ([laissez-passer de zone
      réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security breach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security breach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a violation of
      security regulations, measures, rules or procedures that does not
      result in a security incident. ([infraction à la
      sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security documentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security documentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more
      of the following documents issued by the Minister:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Security Assessment Letter of Approval in
        respect of a domestic ferry issued under subsection 16(1);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Security Assessment Letter of Approval in
        respect of a domestic ferry facility issued under subsection
        16(2);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an Interim Domestic Ferry Security Certificate
        in respect of a domestic ferry issued under subsection 18(1);

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Security Certificate in respect of a domestic
        ferry issued under paragraph 19(1)(a); and

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a Statement of Compliance in respect of a
        domestic ferry facility issued under paragraph
        19(1)(b). ([documents de sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an incident that
      affects the security of a domestic ferry, a domestic ferry
      facility or an interface. ([incident de sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[security threat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security threat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any suspicious act
      or circumstance that could compromise the security of a domestic
      ferry, a domestic ferry facility or an interface. ([menace contre
      la sûreté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operators

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to an operator
  shall be read as a reference to the operator of a domestic ferry, the
  operator of a domestic ferry facility or both.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-752876 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferries and domestic ferry
facilities

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to domestic ferries and domestic ferry facilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, these Regulations do not apply
  to ferries that are dismantled or laid-up at a domestic ferry
  facility.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-752882 .Part}

### [Overview]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752883 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General matters

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out general
matters that apply to the entire Regulations.

### [Compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752886 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of operators

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a domestic
ferry and the operator of a domestic ferry facility shall ensure that
the requirements of these Regulations are met.

### [MARSEC Level]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752889 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}MARSEC level 1

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The operator of a domestic
ferry and the operator of a domestic ferry facility shall maintain
MARSEC level 1 at all times unless a higher MARSEC level is required by
a security measure formulated by the Minister under section 7 of the
Act.

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752892 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security equipment and systems

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall tamper
with, damage or disrupt the normal operation of any security equipment
or system to which these Regulations apply.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Security Documentation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-752896 .Part}

### [Overview]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752897 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements relating to security
documentation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out the
security documentation that must be obtained from the Minister and the
manner in which it must be obtained. It also sets out the security
documentation that must be carried on board domestic ferries.

### [Required Documentation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752900 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (4),
  no person shall operate a domestic ferry without

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an Interim Domestic Ferry Security Certificate in
    respect of the ferry issued under subsection 18(1) within the
    preceding 90 days; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Security Certificate in respect of the ferry
    issued under paragraph 19(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed compliance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a domestic ferry is deemed to comply
  with the requirements of subsection (1) if any of the following
  documents have been issued in respect of the ferry:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a valid International Ship Security Certificate or
    a valid interim International Ship Security Certificate that is
    issued under subsection 202(1) or (3) of the [Marine Transportation
    Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a valid Canadian Vessel Security Certificate issued
    by the Minister under subsection 202(2) of the [Marine
    Transportation Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144);
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a valid interim Canadian Vessel Security
    Certificate issued by the Minister under subsection 202(3) of the
    [Marine Transportation Security
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- Part 1 of Schedule 1

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- Parts 2 to 4 of Schedule

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply before April 1, 2010 to

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed compliance

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- Parts 2 to 4 of Schedule

### [Procedure for Issuance of Security Documentation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752926 .Subheading}

#### [Overview of the Procedure]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752927 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  procedure for the issuance of security documentation in respect of a
  domestic ferry is the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the ferry submits to the Minister a
    security assessment report in respect of the ferry in accordance
    with subsection 15(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issues to the operator of the ferry a
    Security Assessment Letter of Approval in respect of the ferry in
    accordance with subsection 16(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the ferry submits to the Minister a
    security plan in respect of the ferry in accordance with subsection
    17(1);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issues to the operator of the ferry an
    Interim Domestic Ferry Security Certificate in respect of the ferry
    in accordance with subsection 18(1); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issues to the operator of the ferry a
    Security Certificate in respect of the ferry in accordance with
    paragraph 19(1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The procedure for the issuance of security
  documentation in respect of a domestic ferry facility is the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the ferry facility submits to the
    Minister a security assessment report in respect of the ferry
    facility in accordance with subsection 15(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issues to the operator of the ferry
    facility a Security Assessment Letter of Approval in respect of the
    ferry facility in accordance with subsection 16(2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the ferry facility submits to the
    Minister a security plan in respect of the ferry facility in
    accordance with subsection 17(2); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister issues to the operator of the ferry
    facility a Statement of Compliance in respect of the ferry facility
    in accordance with paragraph 19(1)(b).

#### [Submission of Security Assessment by Operator]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752942 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  order to obtain a Security Certificate in respect of a domestic ferry,
  the operator of the ferry shall submit to the Minister for approval a
  security assessment report in respect of the ferry that contains the
  elements set out in section 70.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In order to obtain a Statement of Compliance in
  respect of a domestic ferry facility, the operator of the ferry
  facility shall submit to the Minister for approval a security
  assessment report in respect of the ferry facility that contains the
  elements set out in section 70.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents signed in ink

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The documents submitted to the Minister under
  subsections (1) and (2) shall be signed in ink by the operator.

#### [Issuance of Letter of Approval by Minister]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752950 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall approve a security assessment report in respect of a
  domestic ferry and issue to the operator of the ferry a Security
  Assessment Letter of Approval in respect of the ferry if the Minister
  is satisfied that the security assessment submitted by the operator
  meets the requirements of sections 66 to 70.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall approve a security assessment
  report in respect of a domestic ferry facility and issue to the
  operator of the ferry facility a Security Assessment Letter of
  Approval in respect of the ferry facility if the Minister is satisfied
  that the security assessment submitted by the operator meets the
  requirements of sections 66 to 70.

#### [Submission of Security Plan by Operator]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752956 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  receiving a Security Assessment Letter of Approval in respect of a
  domestic ferry, the operator of the ferry shall submit to the Minister
  for approval a security plan that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} addresses the analysis and recommendations set out
    in the approved security assessment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of sections 71 to 73.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} After receiving a Security Assessment Letter of
  Approval in respect of a domestic ferry facility, the operator of the
  ferry facility shall submit to the Minister for approval a security
  plan that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} addresses the analysis and recommendations set out
    in the approved security assessment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of sections 71, 72 and 74.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents signed in ink

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The documents submitted to the Minister under
  subsections (1) and (2) shall be signed in ink by the operator.

#### [Approval of Security Plan by Minister]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752968 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall approve a security plan in respect of a domestic ferry
  and issue to the operator of the ferry an Interim Domestic Ferry
  Security Certificate in respect of the ferry if the Minister is
  satisfied that the security plan submitted by the operator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of sections 71 to 73; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sets out adequate systems to address or respond to
    security threats, security breaches and security incidents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall approve a security plan in respect
  of a domestic ferry facility if the Minister is satisfied that the
  security plan submitted by the operator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements of sections 71, 72 and 74;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sets out adequate systems to address or respond to
    security threats, security breaches and security incidents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of validity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A security plan approved by the Minister is valid for
  a period fixed by the Minister, which shall not exceed five years
  after the date on which it is approved. In fixing the period, the
  Minister shall consider the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a domestic ferry,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its operations and the industry in which it
      operates,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its ports of call and usual routes,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the operator's security record, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the complexity of its security plan and the
      details of its procedures; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a domestic ferry facility,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its operations and the industry in which it
      operates,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the operator's security record, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the complexity of its security plan and the
      details of its procedures.

#### [Issuance of Security Certificate and Statement of Compliance]{.HTitleText3} {#h-752989 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Successful inspection

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister is satisfied, on the basis of an inspection conducted under
  section 23 of the Act, that the procedures set out in the approved
  security plan in respect of a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry
  facility are implemented, the Minister shall issue to the operator of
  the ferry or the ferry facility, in English or French,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a domestic ferry, a Security
    Certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a domestic ferry facility, a
    Statement of Compliance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A Security Certificate or a Statement of Compliance
  issued under subsection (1) is valid while the approved security plan
  in respect of the domestic ferry or the domestic ferry facility is
  valid.

### [Requirements for Domestic Ferries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-752997 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents on board

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an approved security plan in respect of the ferry;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Security Certificate issued in respect of the
      ferry, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a Security Certificate has not been issued,

      of the ferry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance with the [Marine
  Transportation Security Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a domestic ferry to which subsection
  12(2) applies, the operator of the ferry shall ensure that the ferry
  carries on board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate referred to in that subsection that
    was issued in respect of the ferry; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel security plan referred to in paragraph
    204(1)(b) of the [Marine Transportation Security
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-144) that was approved in
    respect of the ferry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- Part 1 of Schedule 1

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply before January 15, 2010
  to a domestic ferry operating on a route set out in Part 1 of Schedule
  1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- Parts 2 to 4 of Schedule
  1

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply before April 1, 2010 to

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-753014 .Part}

### [Overview]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753015 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator obligations

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** This Part sets out the
responsibilities of the operators of domestic ferries and domestic ferry
facilities, including specific rules relating to restricted area passes
and keys and the replacement of ferries.

### [Responsibilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753018 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations --- Domestic ferry

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a domestic ferry shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establish an administrative and organizational
    structure for the security of the ferry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide each of its employees having
    responsibilities under these Regulations with the technical
    assistance and the training necessary to fulfil those
    responsibilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} designate a ferry security officer in writing by
    name or by position, other than the position for which the
    designation is being made;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide the ferry security officer with information
    regarding security threats and with other information relevant to
    security;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the security assessment in respect of
    the ferry is carried out and submitted to the Minister for approval;

### [Restricted Area Passes and Keys]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753077 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753078 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility of operator

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator shall issue a restricted area pass or key for controlling
  access to restricted areas established by the operator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An operator may issue a restricted area pass or key
  only to a person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} who requires access to a restricted area in the
    performance of their duties; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is known to the operator or whose identity has
    been confirmed by valid government-issued photo identification.

#### [Content of Restricted Area Pass]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753086 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A restricted area pass
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} show the name, height and eye colour of the person to
  whom the pass has been issued, a clear photograph of the person's head
  and shoulders, and an expiry date that does not exceed five years
  after the date of issue; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain an expiry date or other information to
  indicate the period during which access is required, and sufficient
  information to allow the identification of the pass holder.

#### [Use of Restricted Area Pass]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753091 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pass to be displayed

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a restricted area pass shall, when entering or remaining in
  a restricted area, display the pass on their outer clothing and above
  their waist with their photo visible at all times.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No personal use

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a restricted area pass or key shall not
  use the restricted area pass or key except in the performance of their
  duties.

#### [Restricted Area Pass Restrictions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753097 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restrictions

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide false information for the purpose of
  obtaining a restricted area pass or key;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} loan or give a restricted area pass or key to a
  person other than the individual to whom it was issued;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have or use a restricted area pass or key that was
  issued to another person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} alter or otherwise modify a restricted area pass or
  key;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} use a counterfeit restricted area pass or key; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} reproduce a restricted area pass or key unless
  authorized to do so by the operator who issued the pass or key.

#### [Loss or Theft of Restricted Area Pass or Key]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753106 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report of theft or loss

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a restricted area pass or key that is lost or stolen shall
  immediately report its loss or theft to the operator who issued the
  pass or key.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Blocking of lost or stolen key

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator shall immediately prevent the use of a
  restricted area pass or key upon being notified of its loss or theft.

#### [Return of Restricted Area Pass or Key]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753112 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Return when no longer necessary

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a
restricted area pass or key shall return it immediately to the operator
who issued it when

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder ceases to work on the domestic ferry or in
  the domestic ferry facility; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operator determines that the holder otherwise
  ceases to require the restricted area pass or key.

#### [Administration of Restricted Area Passes and Keys]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753117 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record-keeping

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  operator shall keep a record of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of restricted area passes and keys
    issued and, for each pass and key, the name of the holder, the
    number of the pass or key, the date of issue, the period of validity
    of the pass, and, if applicable, the date of return; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} lost or stolen passes or keys.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safekeeping and communication of
  records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An operator shall ensure that the record referred to
  in subsection (1) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept for at least two years after the date of
    expiry or return of the restricted area passes or keys; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} made available to the Minister on request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Port administration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a domestic ferry facility is located in a port,
  the port administration may issue restricted area passes and keys on
  behalf of the operator of the ferry facility. In this case, the
  operator shall cooperate with the port administration and shall
  provide it with the information required.

### [Replacement of Ferries]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753129 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of replacement

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** If the operator of a

  ferry and the replaced ferry; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ferry facility security officer of each domestic
  ferry facility with which the replacement ferry will interface on that
  route.

### [Master of a Domestic Ferry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753139 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Judgment of master prevails

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in these Regulations permits

  executing any decision that, in the professional judgment of the
  master, is necessary to maintain the safety and security of the ferry,
  including decisions

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to deny access to persons other than the following
    and, where applicable, their goods:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the ferry,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the operator of the domestic ferry facility with
      which the ferry interfaces,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} local law enforcement personnel in the exercise
      of their functions, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} emergency response personnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to refuse to load cargo, including containers or
    other closed cargo transport units; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to coordinate with the operator of a domestic ferry
    facility, and with the port security officer if the ferry facility
    is in a port, crew changes as well as access to the ferry through

    representatives of seafarers' welfare and labour organizations.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a conflict between any safety and security

  operations, the master of the ferry shall give precedence to
  requirements intended to maintain the safety of the ferry and shall,
  in such a case, use any temporary procedures that the master
  determines appropriate under the circumstances and that satisfy, to
  the highest possible degree, the security requirements of the MARSEC
  level in effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary procedures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the master of a domestic ferry uses temporary
  procedures, the master shall inform the Minister as soon as
  practicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master to provide support

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The master of a domestic ferry shall provide the
  ferry security officer with the support necessary to carry out their
  duties on board the ferry.

#### [On-Site Security Assessment]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753304 .Subheading}

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure the performance of all security duties;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} control access through the use of identification
  systems or through other means;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} control the embarkation of personnel and other

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} monitor restricted areas to ensure that only
  authorized persons have access to them;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} monitor the deck areas and the areas adjacent to the

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the security communication systems,
  information and equipment are readily available.

#### [Analysis and Recommendations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753314 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elements to be considered

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who conducts a security assessment shall take into account the
  security assessment information, the on-site security assessment and
  the requirements of these Regulations in order to provide
  recommendations for the security procedures to be established in the
  security plan in respect of the domestic ferry or the domestic ferry
  facility, including recommendations respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} restricted areas;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} response procedures for fire or other emergency
    situations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} security supervision of all persons on a ferry or
    at a ferry facility;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the frequency and effectiveness of security
    patrols;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} access control systems;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} security communication systems;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} security doors, barriers and lighting;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} security and surveillance equipment and systems;
    and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the evaluation of the potential for each identified
    point of access on a ferry, including open decks, to be used by
    individuals who might try to breach security, whether or not those
    individuals legitimately have access to the ferry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Potential threats to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A security assessment in respect of a domestic ferry
  or a domestic ferry facility shall take into account the following
  potential threats:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} damage to or destruction of the ferry or the ferry
    facility by explosive devices, arson, sabotage or vandalism;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} tampering with essential equipment or systems,
    ships' stores or cargo;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the unauthorized access to or use of the ferry or
    the ferry facility, including the presence of stowaways;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the smuggling on the ferry or at the ferry facility
    of weapons, explosives, incendiaries or other dangerous substances
    or devices, including weapons of mass destruction;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the use of the ferry or its equipment, or the use
    of ferry facility equipment, as a weapon or as a means of causing
    damage or destruction;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the hijacking of the ferry, the seizure of the
    ferry facility, and the seizure of any persons on the ferry or at
    the ferry facility;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} attacks on the ferry while it is at berth, at
    anchor or in movement; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the use of the ferry facility or its equipment by
    persons intending to cause a security incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a domestic ferry, the security
  assessment shall consider the security of all persons, operations and
  goods that it is important to protect, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the capacity to maintain safe navigation and to
    respond to any emergency situation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cargo, if any, particularly dangerous goods or
    substances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ships' stores and bunkers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the ferry's security communication and surveillance
    systems; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other security systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a domestic ferry facility, the
  security assessment shall include a vulnerability assessment to
  determine the following, so as to produce an overall assessment of the
  level of risk for which security procedures have to be developed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any particular aspect of the ferry facility,
    including vessel traffic in the vicinity, that might make the ferry
    facility a target of an attack;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the potential consequences of an attack on or at
    the ferry facility in terms of loss of life, damage to property and
    economic disruption, including the disruption of marine
    transportation systems;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the capability and intent of persons likely to
    mount an attack;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the potential types of attack;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} current security procedures, including
    identification systems;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} methods and points of access to the ferry facility;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to protect radio and
    telecommunications equipment, including computer systems and
    networks;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any conflict between safety and security
    procedures;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any implementation or personnel constraints;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} methods of monitoring restricted areas to ensure
    that only authorized persons have access to them;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} areas adjacent to the ferry facility that might be
    exploited during or for an attack;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} current security procedures relating to utilities
    and other services;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any deficiency identified during drills or
    exercises;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} any deficiencies identified during daily
    operations, or following incidents or alerts, reports of security
    concerns, the application of control measures or audits; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the structural integrity of the ferry facility.

### [Security Procedures for Access Control]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753430 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753431 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives

- **[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Security procedures for controlling
  access to a domestic ferry and a domestic ferry facility shall be
  established in their respective security plans for all MARSEC levels,
  as appropriate to their operations, in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevent persons from introducing unauthorized
    weapons, explosives, incendiaries, and other dangerous substances
    and devices on the ferry or at the ferry facility;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure weapons, explosives, incendiaries, and other
    dangerous substances and devices that are authorized to be on the
    ferry or at the ferry facility;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} verify the identity of ferry personnel, ferry
    facility personnel and any other authorized personnel;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} indicate the frequency of access controls,
    particularly if they are carried out on a random or occasional
    basis;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} verify that passengers have valid tickets; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensure that unattended goods do not pose an
    immediate threat to marine security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a domestic ferry,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} security procedures shall be established in the
    security plan in respect of the ferry, for all MARSEC levels, as
    appropriate to its operations, to control access to ladders, access
    gangways, access ramps, access doors, side scuttles, windows and
    ports, hatches, mooring lines, anchor chains and hoisting gear; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at all MARSEC levels, the ferry security officer
    shall, after any period during which the ferry was unattended,
    ensure that security sweeps are performed in order to confirm the
    absence of security threats or dangerous substances or devices
    before the ferry gets under way.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a domestic ferry facility, security
  procedures shall be established in the security plan in respect of the
  ferry facility for all MARSEC levels, as appropriate to its
  operations, to identify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locations at which restrictions or prohibitions
    preventing unauthorized access are to be applied; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the types of restrictions or prohibitions to be
    applied and the means of applying them.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identification of access points that must be

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} methods to prevent unauthorized access to the ferry

### [Security Procedures for Handling Cargo]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753608 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753609 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

  respective security plans for all MARSEC levels, as appropriate to
  their operations, in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deter and detect tampering;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevent cargo that is not meant for carriage from
    being accepted and stored on the ferry or in the ferry facility;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identify cargo that is approved for loading onto
    the ferry;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} control inventory at points of access to the ferry;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} inspect cargo for dangerous substances and devices,
    by one or more of the following means:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} visual examination,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} physical examination,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the use of detection devices such as scanners,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the use of canines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Domestic ferry facility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements in subsection (1),
  cargo handling procedures shall be established in the security plan in
  respect of a domestic ferry facility, as appropriate to its
  operations, in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify cargo that is accepted for temporary
    storage in a restricted area while awaiting loading or pickup;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} release cargo only to the carrier specified in the
    cargo documentation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} coordinate with shippers and other persons
    responsible for cargo;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} create, update and maintain a continuous inventory
    of certain dangerous cargoes, from their receipt to their delivery
    in the ferry facility, that sets out the locations in which they are
    stored; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} check the documentation of cargo entering the ferry
    facility.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ferry security officer or the ferry facility

  applicable, the port security officer, make arrangements with shippers

  inspection, sealing, scheduling, supporting documentation, and similar
  matters. The ferry security officer or the ferry facility security
  officer shall inform the operator and, if applicable, the port
  security officer, of any arrangement.

    Sea, 1972.

### [Security Procedures for Security Threats, Security Breaches and Security Incidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753750 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives

**[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** Security procedures for
responding to security threats, security breaches or security incidents
on a domestic ferry and at a domestic ferry facility shall be
established in their respective security plans for all MARSEC levels, as
appropriate to the operations of the ferry or the ferry facility, in
order to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain critical ferry, ferry facility and interface
  operations by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} prohibiting entry into the affected area,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} denying access to the ferry or the ferry facility,
    except to persons responding to the security threat, security breach
    or security incident,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} implementing the appropriate MARSEC level
    security procedures throughout the ferry or the ferry facility,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} stopping cargo-handling operations, if any, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} notifying shoreside authorities, or other ferries,
    vessels or marine facilities of the security threat, security breach
    or security incident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} evacuate the ferry or the ferry facility in case of
  security threats, security breaches or security incidents;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} report to the Minister, without undue delay, any
  security threats, security breaches or security incidents;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} brief ferry or ferry facility personnel on potential
  security threats and the need for vigilance and their assistance in
  reporting suspicious persons, objects or activities; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} suspend non-critical operations in order to focus
  response on critical operations.

### [Security Drills and Security Exercises]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753763 .Subheading}

#### [General]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753764 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives

**[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** Security drills and
security exercises shall test the proficiency of domestic ferry
personnel and domestic ferry facility personnel in carrying out their
assigned security responsibilities at all MARSEC levels and the
effective implementation of the respective security plans of the ferry
or the ferry facility, and shall enable the ferry security officer or
the ferry facility security officer to identify any related security
deficiencies that need to be rectified.

#### [Security Drills]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753767 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of drills

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry facility shall ensure
  that a security drill is conducted at least once every three months
  except when the ferry or the ferry facility is out of service, in
  which case a drill shall be conducted within one week after the ferry
  or the ferry facility is back in service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate or coordinated drills

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Domestic ferries and domestic ferry facilities may
  hold separate or coordinated drills.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concurrent drills

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A security drill may be conducted with another drill,
  if appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan elements tested

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Security drills shall test individual elements of the
  security plan in respect of a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry
  facility including responses to security threats, security breaches
  and security incidents, and shall take into account, in respect of the
  ferry or the ferry facility, the types of operations, personnel
  changes and other relevant circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equivalency

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A documented response to a security incident is
  considered equivalent to a security drill.

#### [Security Exercises]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753779 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of exercises

- **[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Security exercises shall be
  conducted at least once every calendar year, with not more than 18
  months between them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of exercises

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Security exercises may be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} full-scale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a tabletop simulation or seminar; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} combined with other appropriate exercises.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objectives of exercises

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Security exercises

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall test the relevant security plan, and shall
    include the substantial and active participation of all personnel
    having security responsibilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may include, depending on the scope and the nature
    of the exercises, the participation of government authorities or
    personnel from other vessels, marine facilities or port
    administrations having security responsibilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may involve only a domestic ferry or a domestic
    ferry facility, or may be part of a cooperative program to test the
    security plan of another vessel, marine facility or port; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} shall test, at a minimum, elements of coordination,
    resource availability and response, and the communication and
    notification procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of higher MARSEC level

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a domestic ferry or a domestic ferry facility is
  involved in the implementation of MARSEC level 2 or MARSEC level 3
  following a security incident, the implementation of the MARSEC level
  is equivalent to a security exercise.

    duration and description, and the names of the participants;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} details of security drills and security exercises,
    including the date and description, the names of the participants
    and any best practices or lessons learned that might improve the
    security plan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} details of security threats, security breaches and
    security incidents, including the date, time, location and
    description, the response, and the person to whom they were
    reported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} records of changes in the MARSEC level, including
    the date, the time that notification was received, and the time of
    compliance with the requirements of the new level;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} records of the maintenance, calibration and testing
    of equipment used for security, including the date and time of the
    activity and the equipment involved;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for each annual audit of the security plan, a
    letter certifying the date on which the audit was completed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the security assessment in respect of the
    domestic ferry or the domestic ferry facility and details of each
    periodic review of the assessment, including the date on which it
    was conducted and its findings;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the approved security plan in respect of
    the ferry or the ferry facility and details of each periodic review
    of the plan, including the date on which it was conducted, its
    findings and any recommended amendments to the plan;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the security documentation issued in respect of the
    ferry or the ferry facility;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} details of each amendment to the approved security
    plan in respect of the ferry or the ferry facility, including the
    date of the approval and implementation of the amendment;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a list, by name or by position, of the personnel of
    the ferry or the ferry facility having security responsibilities;
    and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} records of inspections, including the dates on
    which they were conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records management

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), a ferry security officer
  and a ferry facility security officer shall ensure that the records
  set out in subsection (1) in relation to their respective
  responsibilities are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept for at least two years after the date on which
    they are made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} made available to the Minister upon request;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} protected from unauthorized access or disclosure;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} protected from deletion, destruction and revision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for keeping security plans

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ferry security officer and the ferry facility
  security officer shall ensure that copies of the security assessment
  and the approved security plan in respect of the domestic ferry or the
  domestic ferry facility are kept for at least two years after the date
  of expiry of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure prohibited

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall disclose security information
  contained in the records referred to in subsection (1), in the
  security assessments or in the security plans unless the disclosure is
  for the purpose of complying with these Regulations.

### [Coordination and Implementation of Procedures at Each MARSEC Level]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753822 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation before an interface

- **[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before an interface occurs between a
  domestic ferry and a domestic ferry facility, the operator of the
  ferry and the operator of the ferry facility shall each ensure that
  the ferry or the ferry facility is operated in compliance with the
  MARSEC level in effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of master of domestic ferry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When notified of an increase in the MARSEC level at a
  domestic ferry facility with which a domestic ferry is about to
  interface, the master of the ferry shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the ferry complies with the higher
    MARSEC level without undue delay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} report any implementation difficulties to the
    Minister as soon as possible; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} brief all ferry personnel on identified security
    threats, emphasizing reporting procedures and stressing the need for
    increased vigilance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of domestic ferry facility
  operator

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When notified of an increase in the MARSEC level on a
  domestic ferry with which a domestic ferry facility is about to
  interface, the operator of the ferry facility shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the ferry facility complies with the
    higher MARSEC level without undue delay;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} report any implementation difficulties to the
    Minister as soon as possible; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} brief all ferry facility personnel on identified
    security threats, emphasizing reporting procedures and stressing the
    need for increased vigilance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordination with port administration

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the domestic ferry facility referred to in
  subsection (3) is within a port, the operator of the domestic ferry
  and the operator of the ferry facility shall also coordinate with the
  port administration.

### [Audit of Security Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753839 .Subheading}

#### [Domestic Ferry]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753840 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of audit

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a domestic ferry shall ensure that an audit of the
  security plan in respect of the ferry is conducted to determine
  whether the security plan meets the requirements of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a domestic ferry shall ensure that an
  audit of the security plan in respect of the ferry is conducted every
  12 months, and that a new audit is initiated within 30 days after the
  occurrence of any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ferry has a new operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modifications have been made to the physical
    structure of the ferry;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} modifications have been made to the ferry's
    emergency response procedures, security procedures or operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial audit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An audit of the security plan in respect of a
  domestic ferry conducted as a result of modifications to the physical
  structure of the ferry may be limited to the provisions of the
  security plan that are affected by the modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of an audit, it is determined that
  the security plan in respect of a domestic ferry meets the
  requirements of these Regulations, the ferry security officer shall
  attach to the security plan a letter certifying that the plan meets
  those requirements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of an audit, it is determined that
  the security plan in respect of a domestic ferry does not meet the
  requirements of these Regulations, the ferry security officer shall
  submit a security plan amendment to the operator of the ferry within
  30 days after the completion of the audit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for conducting audit

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Persons conducting an audit shall have a knowledge of
  the methods of conducting audits and inspections, and a knowledge of
  access control and monitoring techniques, and shall be independent of
  the security procedures being audited unless that is impracticable
  because of the size and type of ferry.

#### [Domestic Ferry Facility]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753857 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of audit

- **[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  ferry facility security officer shall ensure that an audit of the
  security plan in respect of a domestic ferry facility is conducted to
  determine whether the security plan meets the requirements of these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of audit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A ferry facility security officer shall ensure that
  an audit of the security plan in respect of a domestic ferry facility
  is conducted every 12 months, and that a new audit is initiated within
  30 days after the occurrence of any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ferry facility has a new operator;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} modifications have been made to the physical
    structure of the ferry facility;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} modifications have been made to the ferry
    facility's emergency response procedures, security procedures or
    operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial audit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An audit of the security plan in respect of a
  domestic ferry facility conducted as a result of modifications to the
  physical structure of the ferry facility may be limited to the
  provisions of the security plan affected by the modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of an audit, it is determined that
  the security plan in respect of a domestic ferry facility meets the
  requirements of these Regulations, the ferry facility security officer
  shall attach to the security plan a letter certifying that the plan
  meets those requirements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If, as a result of an audit, it is determined that
  the security plan in respect of a domestic ferry facility does not
  meet the requirements of these Regulations, the ferry facility
  security officer shall submit a security plan amendment to the
  operator of the ferry facility within 30 days after the completion of
  the audit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for conducting audit

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Persons conducting an audit shall have a knowledge of
  the methods of conducting audits and inspections, and a knowledge of
  access control and monitoring techniques, and shall be independent of
  the security procedures being audited unless that is impracticable
  because of the size and type of ferry facility.

### [Amendment to Security Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-753874 .Subheading}

#### [Obligation to Amend]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753875 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments required

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** A security plan shall
be amended if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an audit conducted under subsection 106(2) or 107(2)
  indicates that the security plan no longer meets the requirements of
  these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that an amendment is required
  to address security threats, security breaches or security incidents
  that are not addressed in the plan.

#### [Procedures for Taking Effect]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753880 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments by operator

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  amendment to a security plan shall be submitted to the Minister at
  least 30 days before the day on which it is to take effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment required by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an amendment is required by the Minister under
  paragraph 108(b), the operator shall submit the amendment to the
  Minister within 60 days after the day on which the Minister sends the
  operator written notification of the determination.

#### [Approval of Amendments]{.HTitleText3} {#h-753886 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall approve the amendments to the security plan if they
  meet the requirements of these Regulations, unless approving the
  amendments is likely to adversely affect marine transportation
  security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security plan no longer valid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an amendment to the security plan that is required
  under section 108 is not submitted or approved, the approved security
  plan ceases to be valid on the day on which the operator receives
  notification in writing that the plan is no longer valid.
