## Support Organizations Superannuation Regulations {#support-organizations-superannuation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/62-91
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 5, 1961

Registration 1962-03-08

Support Organizations Superannuation Regulations

P.C. 1962-304 1962-03-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to Vote No. 547
of the Appropriation Act No. 5, 1961, is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Support Organizations Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-62-91).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Support Organizations Superannuation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-62-91).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following organizations
and positions are, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, in support
of the forces specified in clauses (E) and (F) of subparagraph (ii) of
paragraph (b) of section 5 of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation
Act](/eng/acts/C-17);

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} South African Military Nursing Service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Queen Alexandra's Royal Naval Nursing Services;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Women's Royal Naval Service; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a position of full-time service with forces of the
  Royal Navy under T124 or T124X agreements.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Full-time service in an
organization or position designated in section 2 may be counted as
pensionable service by a contributor for the purposes of the [Canadian
Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17) if, while a member of the
regular forces, he elects within one year from the date of coming into
force of these Regulations or within one year of becoming a contributor
under the Act, whichever is the later date, to pay for such service.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of contributions
required in respect of pensionable service elected pursuant to
section 3, shall be the same amount and paid in the same manner as
required for elective service specified in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} clause (E) of subparagraph (ii) of paragraph (b) of
  section 5 of the [Canadian Forces Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/C-17),
  in the case of service during a period of time specified in that
  clause; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} clause (F) of that subparagraph, in the case of
  service during a different period of time.
