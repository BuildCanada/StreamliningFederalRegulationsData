## Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Distribution Direction « No. 2 » {#revised-statutes-of-canada-1985-distribution-direction-no.-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-122
:::

[REVISED STATUTES OF CANADA, 1985 ACT](/eng/acts/R-7.7)

Registration 1989-05-10

Direction Respecting the Distribution of the Revised Statutes of Canada,
1985

P.C. 1989-734 1989-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the [Revised
Statutes of Canada, 1985 Act](/eng/acts/R-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1){#footnote1-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Direction respecting the distribution of the
Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985](/eng/regulations/SI-88-50).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1}S.C. 1987, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642005 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Distribution Direction (No. 2).

## [Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642007 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Four copies of the Revised
Statutes of Canada, 1985 shall, as soon as practicable after they become
available, be distributed without charge to the chairperson of any
committee of the House of Commons, of the Senate or of both Houses of
Parliament that may be established for the purpose of reviewing and
scrutinizing statutory instruments.
