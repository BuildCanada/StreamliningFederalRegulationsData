## National Parks Town, Visitor Centre and Resort Subdivision Designation Regulations {#national-parks-town-visitor-centre-and-resort-subdivision-designation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-8
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1990-12-13

Regulations Respecting the Designation of Towns, Visitor Centres and
Resort Subdivisions in National Parks

P.C. 1990-2680  1990-12-13
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 7(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-8_e_hq_5224){#fn_SOR-91-8_e_hq_5224-ID0EHBAA}
of the National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the National
Parks Townsite and Subdivision Designation Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1133,
and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the designation of
towns, visitor centres and resort subdivisions in national
parks](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-8), in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-8_e_hq_5224-ID0EHBAA){#fn_SOR-91-8_e_hq_5224}R.S., c.
  39 (4th Supp.)
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939944 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Parks Town, Visitor Centre and Resort Subdivision
Designation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-8).

## [Surveyed Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939946 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The surveyed areas shown on
the plans of survey listed in the schedule are designated as towns,
visitor centres or resort subdivisions as set out in the schedule.
