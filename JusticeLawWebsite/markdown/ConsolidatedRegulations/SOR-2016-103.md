## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826519 .Part}

Definition of [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any roll or tubular construction
that contains tobacco, has a wrapper or cover made of paper and is
consumed through the inhalation of the products of combustion. It does
not include a bidi, cigar, little cigar or kretek.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826522 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to cigarettes that are imported by an organization for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} research; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} laboratory testing or analysis.

## [Ignition Propensity Standard]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826527 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Cigarettes of every size of each
  brand must burn their full length no more than 25% of the time when
  tested on 10 layers of filter paper using the International
  Organization for Standardization standard ISO 12863, entitled Standard
  test method for assessing the ignition propensity of cigarettes (ISO
  12863), as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In each test, the cigarettes that are burned in every
  determination must be of the same size and of the same brand.

- Definition of [[determination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section,
  [[determination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in ISO
  12863.
