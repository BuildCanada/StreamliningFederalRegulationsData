## Moosonee Airport Zoning Regulations {#moosonee-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-236
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1989-05-04

Regulations Respecting Zoning At Moosonee Airport

P.C. 1989-775 1989-05-04
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Moosonee Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on July 30th and August 6th,
1988, and in two successive issues of the Timmins Daily Press on
September 1st and 2nd, 1988, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby
afforded to interested persons to make representations to the Minister
of Transport with respect thereto.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}R.S., c. 33 (1st supp.)
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Moosonee
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-236).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919045 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Moosonee Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-236).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919047 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Moosonee Airport, in the
      District of Cochrane in the Province of Ontario;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 7.7 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919058 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919060 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919065 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919067 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Moosonee Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090 85-59, Sheet 5, dated June 30, 1987, is a point which may be
located by:

Commencing at Button 06 being the southwesterly end of runway 06-24 at
Moosonee airport;

Thence measuring northeasterly along the centreline of said
runway 345.662 m to a point;

Thence measuring northwesterly and perpendicular to the said runway
centreline 117.685 m to the airport reference point, said point having
six degree Universal Transverse Mercator coordinates of N 5 682 039.953
and E 527 412.708.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Moosonee Airport Zoning Plan No. 56-090
85-59, Sheets 1 to 8 inclusive and 10 to 13 inclusive, dated June 30,
1987, are surfaces abutting each end of the strips associated with
runways designated 06-24 and 14-32 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 06 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 24 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 14 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 752 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 220.2 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 43.8 m measured vertically above the elevation
  at the end of the strip;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 32 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 800 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 225 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 45 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Moosonee Airport Zoning Plan No. 56-090
85-59, Sheets 1 to 9 inclusive, dated June 30, 1987, is an imaginary
surface located at a common plane established at a constant elevation of
45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point, except that,
where that common plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, the outer surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above
the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips, shown on Moosonee Airport Zoning Plan No. 56-090 85-59,
Sheet 5, dated June 30, 1987, are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 06-24 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 644 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 14-32 is 90 m in
  width, 45 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 339 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Moosonee Airport Zoning Plan
No. 56-090 85-59, Sheets 1 to 8 inclusive, dated June 30, 1987, is a
surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to the
centre line and centre line produced of each strip extending upward and
outward from the lateral limits of each strip and its approach surfaces
to an intersection with the outer surface or another transitional
surface of an adjoining strip.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Lands to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-lands-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits of lands, shown on Moosonee Airport
Zoning Plan No. 56-090 85-59, Sheets 1 to 4 inclusive and 6 to 13

THENCE in an easterly direction following the said circular arc to its
