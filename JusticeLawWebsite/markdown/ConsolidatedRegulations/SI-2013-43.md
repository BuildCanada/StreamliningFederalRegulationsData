## Order Transferring to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Acquisitions Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the End User Devices Procurement Unit {#order-transferring-to-shared-services-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-in-the-acquisitions-branch-of-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services-known-as-the-end-user-devices-procurement-unit .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-43
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-04-24

Order Transferring to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision
of the portion of the Federal Public Administration in the Acquisitions
Branch of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known
as the End User Devices Procurement Unit

P.C. 2013-366 2013-03-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12091-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092){#fn_81000-3-PHAC-PSRTDA-E_hq_12092-ID0EBCAA},
transfers to Shared Services Canada the control and supervision of the
portion of the federal public administration in the Acquisitions Branch
of the Department of Public Works and Government Services known as the
End User Devices Procurement Unit, effective April 1, 2013.
