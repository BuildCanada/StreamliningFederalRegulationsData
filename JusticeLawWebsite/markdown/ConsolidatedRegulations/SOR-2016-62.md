## Personal Health Information Custodians in Nova Scotia Exemption Order {#personal-health-information-custodians-in-nova-scotia-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-62
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2016-03-29

Personal Health Information Custodians in Nova Scotia Exemption Order

P.C. 2016-161 2016-03-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the Personal Health
Information Act, SNS 2010, c. 41, of Nova Scotia, which is substantially
similar to Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413){#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413-ID0EBBBA},
applies to the personal health information custodians referred to in the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413-ID0EBBBA){#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413}S.C.
  2000, c. 5
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph
26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413){#fn_81000-2-2452-E_hq_15413-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed [Personal Health Information Custodians in Nova Scotia
Exemption Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-62).
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838093 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Any personal health
information custodian to which the Personal Health Information Act, SNS
2010, c. 41, of Nova Scotia applies is exempt from the application of
Part 1 of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) in respect of the collection, use and disclosure
of personal health information that occurs in Nova Scotia.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838095 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
