## Order Transferring from the Department of Public Works and Government Services to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the Control and Supervision of the Public Access Programs Sector {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services-to-the-department-of-human-resources-and-skills-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-public-access-programs-sector .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-83
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2005-09-21

Order Transferring from the Department of Public Works and Government
Services to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the
Control and Supervision of the Public Access Programs Sector

P.C. 2005-1637 2005-09-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDFAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Department of Public Works and Government
Services to the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the
control and supervision of the portion of the federal public
administration in the Department of Public Works and Government Services
known as the Public Access Programs Sector, which Sector is responsible
for the following programs:

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDFAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, s. 207
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Site,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Enquiry Centre,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Publiservice, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Gateways and Clusters.
