## Railway Running-Trades Employees Hours of Work Regulations {#railway-running-trades-employees-hours-of-work-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 991
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Regulations Respecting Hours of Work of Employees Engaged in the
Running-Trades in the Railway Industry in Canada
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604551 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Railway Running-Trades Employees Hours of Work
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._991).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604553 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
\"Act\" means Part III of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604555 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
employees, that is to say, yardmasters, assistant yardmasters,
locomotive engineers, locomotive firemen (helpers), hostlers, train
conductors, train baggagemen, brakemen, yard foremen, yardmen, switch
tenders and car retarder operators in those railroads that are within
the legislative authority of Parliament are exempted from the
application of sections 169 and 171 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::

## [Scheduling Hours of Work]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604559 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, hours of work may be scheduled and actually worked without
regard to section 173 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-594, s. 2
:::
