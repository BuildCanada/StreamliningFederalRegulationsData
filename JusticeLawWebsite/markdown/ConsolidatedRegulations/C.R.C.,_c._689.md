## Defence Materiel and Equipment Loaned to Contractors Order {#defence-materiel-and-equipment-loaned-to-contractors-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 689
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Loan of Defence Materiel and Equipment to
Contractors
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560519 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Defence Materiel and Equipment Loaned to Contractors
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._689).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560521 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence is authorized to lend, on such terms and conditions as he may
deem appropriate and in the interests of defence,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} materiel, other than prototypes, sealed samples,
  models and catering contractor's equipment, to the Department of
  Supply and Services for the use of its contractors; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} prototypes, sealed samples, models and catering
  contractor's equipment directly to contractors of the Department of
  Supply and Services engaged in work for national defence.
