## [Rate of Exchange]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030468 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 545(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the rate of
exchange that shall be applied on any day in determining the amount in
Canadian dollars of a deposit with an authorized foreign bank in a
currency of a country other than Canada is the latest exchange rate at
which the authorized foreign bank offers, before the day of the
determination, to buy that currency with Canadian dollars.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030470 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on June 28, 1999.
