## Provision of Materiel and Services to Foreign Ships Order {#provision-of-materiel-and-services-to-foreign-ships-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 721
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Authorizing the Minister of National Defence to Provide Materiel
and Services to Foreign Ships
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561035 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Provision of Materiel and Services to Foreign Ships
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._721).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561037 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence, or such authority as he may designate, is authorized to
provide, on a repayment basis,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} materiel, services and money to naval ships of the
  governments

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the Commonwealth countries,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of the United States,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} of the NATO countries, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} of all other powers on approved visits to Canadian
    ports; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} materiel and services to merchant ships in distress,
  subject to the following qualifications:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} no materiel shall be issued unless the recipient
    agrees to pay to the Receiver General in respect thereof an amount
    not less than the amount that, in the opinion of the said Minister,
    is the cost thereof to the Crown,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a limitation of maximum debit balances is imposed,
    in connection with naval ships, of \$1 million for the United States
    and \$50,000 for all other governments concerned, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cash money is advanced on a repayment basis only
    where reciprocal arrangements are available to Canadian naval ships.
