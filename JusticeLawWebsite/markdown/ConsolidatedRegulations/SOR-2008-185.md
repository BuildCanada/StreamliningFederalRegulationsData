## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745097 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Softwood Lumber
  Products Export Charge Act, 2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other definitions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The expressions [[American softwood lumber
  exports]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[American softwood lumber imports from
  countries other than Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[Canadian
  softwood lumber exports]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meaning as
  in paragraph 1 of Annex 7D of the softwood lumber agreement, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Export and Import Permits
  Act](/eng/acts/E-19), as those expressions are, from time to time,
  amended by the parties or clarified as a result of an arbitral award.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data sources

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The source of data to be
used for calculations under these Regulations is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Statistics Canada, for Canadian softwood lumber
  exports to the United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the United States Census Bureau, for American
  softwood lumber exports and American softwood lumber imports from
  countries other than Canada; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Western Wood Products Association's monthly
  publication "Lumber Track", for American shipments of softwood lumber.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}American consumption

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** American consumption of
softwood lumber is the amount determined by the formula

::: {#745110 .Section}
CE + AI + NAS

where

[CE]{.dfn}
:   is Canadian softwood lumber exports to the United States;

[AI]{.dfn}
:   is American softwood lumber imports from countries other than
    Canada; and

[NAS]{.dfn}
:   is net American shipments of softwood lumber.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Net American shipments

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Net American shipments of
softwood lumber are the amount determined by the formula

::: {#745117 .Section}
AS - AE

where

[AS]{.dfn}
:   is American shipments of softwood lumber calculated as the sum of
    the softwood lumber shipments from the Western (Coast, Inland and
    California Redwood) region, the Southern region and the Northern
    region. Northern region shipments are calculated by taking the sum
    of Western and Southern region shipments and multiplying by 6.25%;
    and

[AE]{.dfn}
:   is American softwood lumber exports from those regions.
:::

## [Expected Monthly American Consumption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745121 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expected monthly American consumption

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 14(3) and (4) of the Act, the expected monthly American
consumption of softwood lumber products is the amount determined by the
formula

::: {#745124 .Section}
(AC/12) × SAF

where

[AC]{.dfn}
:   is American consumption of softwood lumber for the 12--month period
    ending 3 months immediately before the month for which the expected
    monthly American consumption is being calculated; and

[SAF]{.dfn}
:   is the seasonal adjustment factor for the pertinent month set out in
    the schedule.
:::

## [Market Share]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745128 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}American consumption of non-Canadian
imports

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 40(1)(a) of the Act, the share of American consumption of
softwood lumber products from imports not originating in Canada is the
amount determined by the formula

::: {#745131 .Section}
AI/AC

where

[AI]{.dfn}
:   is American softwood lumber imports from countries other than
    Canada; and

[AC]{.dfn}
:   is American consumption of softwood lumber.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}American consumption of Canadian exports

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 40(1)(b) of the Act, the Canadian market share of American
consumption of softwood lumber products is the amount determined by the
formula

::: {#745137 .Section}
CE/AC

where

[CE]{.dfn}
:   is Canadian softwood lumber exports to the United States; and

[AC]{.dfn}
:   is American consumption of softwood lumber.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}American consumption of net American
shipments

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 40(1)(c) of the Act, the American market share of American
consumption of softwood lumber products is the amount determined by the
formula

::: {#745143 .Section}
NAS/AC

where

[NAS]{.dfn}
:   is net American shipments of softwood lumber; and

[AC]{.dfn}
:   is American consumption of softwood lumber.
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745147 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}October 12, 2006

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on October 12, 2006.
