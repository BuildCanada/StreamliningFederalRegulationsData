## Secure Electronic Signature Regulations {#secure-electronic-signature-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-30
:::

[CANADA EVIDENCE ACT](/eng/acts/C-5)

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2005-02-01

Secure Electronic Signature Regulations

P.C. 2005-57 2005-02-01
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the technology or
process prescribed in the annexed [Secure Electronic Signature
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-30) can be proved to meet the
requirements set out in paragraphs 48(2)(a) to (d) of the [Personal
Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBADA};

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of
the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)^a^ and paragraph 31.4(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EFACA} of the [Canada
Evidence Act](/eng/acts/C-5), hereby makes the annexed [Secure
Electronic Signature Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-30).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719302 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Personal Information
    Protection and Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asymmetric cryptography]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asymmetric cryptography]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    cryptographic system that relies on key pairs. ([système de
    chiffrement à clé publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certification authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certification authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or
    entity that issues digital signature certificates and that is listed
    as such on the website of the Treasury Board Secretariat. ([autorité
    de certification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[digital signature certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[digital signature certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    a person, means an electronic document that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifies the certification authority that
      issued it and is digitally signed by that certification authority;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identifies, or can be used to identify, the
      person; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contains the person\'s public key. ([certificat
      de signature numérique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any federal department,
    branch, office, board, agency, commission, corporation or body for
    the administration of the affairs of which a minister of the Crown
    is accountable to Parliament. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hash function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hash function]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an electronic one-way
    mathematical process that converts data contained in an electronic
    document into a message digest that is unique to that data in a way
    that, were that data changed, it would, on conversion, result in a
    changed message digest. ([fonction de hachage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[key pair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[key pair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pair of keys held by or for
    a person that includes a private key and a public key that are
    mathematically related to, but different from, each other.
    ([biclé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a string of data that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is used in asymmetric cryptography to encrypt
      data contained in an electronic document; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is unique to the person who is identified in, or
      can be identified through, a digital signature certificate and
      corresponds only to the public key in that certificate. ([clé
      privée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public key]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a string of data contained
    in a digital signature certificate that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is used in asymmetric cryptography to decrypt
      data contained in an electronic document that was encrypted
      through the application of the private key in the key pair; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} corresponds only to the private key in the key
      pair. ([clé publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Technology or Process]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719322 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[secure electronic signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 31(1) of the Act, a secure electronic signature in respect of
data contained in an electronic document is a digital signature that
results from completion of the following consecutive operations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} application of the hash function to the data to
  generate a message digest;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} application of a private key to encrypt the message
  digest;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} incorporation in, attachment to, or association with
  the electronic document of the encrypted message digest;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} transmission of the electronic document and encrypted
  message digest together with either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a digital signature certificate, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a means of access to a digital signature
    certificate; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} after receipt of the electronic document, the
  encrypted message digest and the digital signature certificate or the
  means of access to the digital signature certificate,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} application of the public key contained in the
    digital signature certificate to decrypt the encrypted message
    digest and produce the message digest referred to in paragraph (a),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} application of the hash function to the data
    contained in the electronic document to generate a new message
    digest,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} verification that, on comparison, the message
    digests referred to in paragraph (a) and subparagraph (ii) are
    identical, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} verification that the digital signature
    certificate is valid in accordance with section 3.

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  digital signature certificate is valid if, at the time when the data
  contained in an electronic document is digitally signed in accordance
  with section 2, the certificate

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is readable or perceivable by any person or entity
    who is entitled to have access to the digital signature certificate;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not expired or been revoked.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements for validity set out
  in subsection (1), when the digital signature certificate is supported
  by other digital signature certificates, in order for the digital
  signature certificate to be valid, the supporting certificates must
  also be valid in accordance with that subsection.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  recognizing a person or entity as a certification authority, the
  President of the Treasury Board must verify that the person or entity
  has the capacity to issue digital signature certificates in a secure
  and reliable manner within the context of these Regulations and
  paragraphs 48(2)(a) to (d) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person or entity that is recognized as a
  certification authority by the President of the Treasury Board shall
  be listed on the website of the Treasury Board Secretariat.

## [Presumption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719343 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** When the technology or
process set out in section 2 is used in respect of data contained in an
electronic document, that data is presumed, in the absence of evidence
to the contrary, to have been signed by the person who is identified in,
or can be identified through, the digital signature certificate.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-719345 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
