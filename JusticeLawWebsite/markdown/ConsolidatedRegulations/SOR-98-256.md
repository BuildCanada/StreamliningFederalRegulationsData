## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Province of New
Brunswick is hereby designated for the purposes of the definition
[[applicable guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the
[Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021193 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4), the laws that
constitute the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of New
Brunswick are sections 1 to 3 of the Child Support Guidelines Regulation
--- Family Services Act, N.B. Reg. 98-27.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021195 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on May 1, 1998.
