## Telesat Canada Financing Remission Order {#telesat-canada-financing-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-117
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-10-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax in Respect of Certain
Amounts Paid or Credited by Telesat Canada

P.C. 1990-2093  1990-09-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 23 of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of income tax in respect of certain
amounts paid or credited by Telesat Canada](/eng/regulations/SI-90-117).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642456 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Telesat Canada Financing Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-90-117).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642458 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[credit agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement for a
    loan by the lenders to Telesat Canada for the purpose of financing
    the launches of certain satellites, signed on July 24, 1987 by
    Telesat Canada and the lenders, as amended to October 30, 1988;
    ([convention de financement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financing entities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financing entities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Banque française pour le commerce extérieur,
      a financing agency of the Government of the French Republic, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Compagnie française d'assurance des crédits à
      l'exportation, an export development and financing agency of the
      Government of the French Republic; ([organismes de
      financement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lenders]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lenders]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following banking
    institutions created under the law of the French Republic

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Banque nationale de Paris,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Imperial Bank of Commerce
      (International) S.A., and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Crédit lyonnais; ([prêteurs]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Protocol to the Taxation
    Convention between the Government of Canada and the Government of
    the French Republic signed on May 2, 1975, which Protocol was signed
    at Ottawa on January 16, 1987, entered into force on October 1, 1988
    and was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)
    Part II on December 7, 1988 in SI/88-237;
    ([avenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Telesat Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Telesat Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the company continued
    under the Telesat Canada Act. ([Télésat Canada]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642470 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to
each of the lenders and financing entities of the amount, if any, by
which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the taxes, interest and penalties
  payable by the lender or financing entity under the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of amounts paid or credited by
  Telesat Canada to the lender or financing entity after October 30,
  1987 under the credit agreement

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the taxes, interest and penalties
  that would have been payable by the lender or financing entity under
  the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of those amounts if
  the Protocol had entered into force on October 30, 1987.
