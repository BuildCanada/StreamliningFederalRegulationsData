## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713494 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[OPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[OPP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Ontario Provincial Police.
    ([PPO]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[police officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a chief of police or
    any other police officer, but does not include a special constable,
    a First Nations Constable, a municipal law enforcement officer or an
    auxiliary member of a police force. ([agent de
    police]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713499 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Ontario to collect information for the
purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a police officer appointed to the OPP or to a
  municipal police force in Ontario;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a First Nations Constable appointed under subsection
  54(1) of the Police Services Act, Revised Statutes of Ontario 1990, c.
  P.15; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an employee of the OPP, a municipal police force in
  Ontario, an aboriginal police service or the Ontario Ministry of
  Community Safety and Correctional Services, whose duties include the
  collection of information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713504 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is
authorized under section 2 to collect information is authorized in the
Province of Ontario to register information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713506 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A place
  set out in an item of the schedule is designated as a registration
  centre in the Province of Ontario.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The designated area of service for a registration
  centre is the area served by the OPP detachment or police service, as
  the case may be, identified in the item.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the designated area of
  service for a registration centre referred to in this subsection is as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the registration centre set out in
    item 76 of the schedule, the area served by the Manitoulin and
    Manitowaning OPP detachments; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the registration centre set out in
    item 136 of the schedule, the area served by the Toronto Police
    Service.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713513 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2004-306_e_hq_8562){#fn_SOR-2004-306_e_hq_8562-ID0EBCA}[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5

day on which the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes
into force and the day on which these Regulations are registered.
