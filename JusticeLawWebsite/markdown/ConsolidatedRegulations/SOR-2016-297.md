## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834951 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Office of the Superintendent
    of Financial Institutions
    Act](/eng/acts/O-2.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approved mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved mortgage insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an approved
    mortgage insurer within the meaning of section 2 of the [Protection
    of Residential Mortgage or Hypothecary Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/P-25.7) or a corporation or company that is treated
    as if it were an approved mortgage insurer in accordance with
    subsection 6(4) or 7(1) of that Act, respectively. ([assureur
    hypothécaire agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([banque
    étrangère autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount determined in
    respect of a financial institution in accordance with section
    4. ([fonds propres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cooperative credit association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cooperative credit association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    association, other than a retail association, to which the
    [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies and
    includes a central cooperative credit society for which an order has
    been made under subsection 473(1) of that Act. ([association
    coopérative de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign fraternal benefit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign fraternal benefit society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
    same meaning as in section 571 of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société de secours
    étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 571 of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société d'assurance-vie
    étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société d'assurance-vie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[minimum assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minimum assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount
    determined in accordance with subsection 3(1) in respect of a
    financial institution, as adjusted in accordance with subsection
    3(2). ([cotisation minimale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    to which the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies and
    whose activities are restricted to insuring risks within the class
    of mortgage insurance. ([société d'assurance
    hypothécaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property and casualty insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property and casualty insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    company, provincial company or foreign company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies --- other than a
    life company, society, foreign life company, foreign fraternal
    benefit society or mortgage insurance company --- or Green Shield
    Canada. ([assureur multirisque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retail association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retail association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([association de détail]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société de secours]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company to
    which the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)
    applies. ([société de fiducie et de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Determination of Assessment Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834968 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 23(3) of the Act and subject to subsection (2),
  the amount assessed by the Superintendent against each financial
  institution in respect of any fiscal year is equal to the aggregate of
  the base assessment amount determined for the financial institution in
  accordance with sections 5 to 9 and any applicable assessment
  surcharge determined for the financial institution in accordance with
  section 10, less any interim assessment prepared against the financial
  institution under subsection 23(4) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No assessment payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No assessment is payable in any fiscal year by a
  financial institution with respect to which, before the beginning of
  that fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Superintendent did not make an order to
    commence and carry on business or, in the case of a foreign company
    to which the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, an
    order to insure in Canada risks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister approved an application for voluntary
    liquidation and dissolution; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a court made a winding-up order under the
    [Winding-up and Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/W-11).

## [Determination of Base Assessment Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834977 .Part}

### [Minimum Assessment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-834978 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applicable amount

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of determining, under sections 5 to 9, the base assessment
  amount for a financial institution, the minimum assessment applicable
  to the financial institution is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, in the case of a bank;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, in the case of an authorized foreign bank
    that is not subject to the restrictions and requirements referred to
    in subsection 524(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$15,000, in the case of an authorized foreign bank
    that is subject to the restrictions and requirements referred to in
    subsection 524(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$15,000, in the case of a trust and loan company
    whose activities are restricted to carrying on any of the fiduciary
    activities referred to in section 412 of the [Trust and Loan
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) and any ancillary activities;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, in the case of a trust and loan company
    not referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, in the case of a retail association;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} \$15,000, in the case of a cooperative credit
    association;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} \$15,000, in the case of a company, a provincial
    company or a foreign company to which the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$2,000, in the case of a society or a foreign
    fraternal benefit society.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (6), each minimum
  assessment referred to in subsection (1) is to be adjusted for
  inflation in respect of each fiscal year in accordance with the
  following formula, with the result being rounded to the nearest
  multiple of 10:

  ::: {#834993 .Subsection}
  A × (B/C)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the minimum assessment that was applicable for the previous
      fiscal year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period that ends on
      December 31 immediately preceding that fiscal year; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the Consumer Price Index for the 12-month period immediately
      preceding the period referred to in the description of B.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No adjustment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the amount determined by dividing B by C in
  subsection (2) is less than 1 in respect of a fiscal year, no
  adjustment is to be made to the minimum assessment applicable for that
  year and the minimum assessment applicable for the preceding fiscal
  year continues to apply.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer Price Index

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (2), the Consumer Price Index for any
  12-month period is the amount determined by aggregating the Consumer
  Price Index for all items for Canada, as published by Statistics
  Canada under the authority of the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19),
  for each month in that period and dividing the aggregate by 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Superintendent must, before the beginning of each
  fiscal year, publish on the Office's website each of the minimum
  assessments adjusted in accordance with subsection (2) that are
  applicable in respect of that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No adjustment in first year

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply in respect of the
  fiscal year that begins on April 1, 2016.

### [Capital]{.HTitleText2} {#h-835006 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
determining, under sections 5 to 9, the base assessment amount for a
financial institution, the capital of the financial institution in
respect of a fiscal year is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a bank, a trust and loan company or a
  retail association, the total risk-weighted assets that it reported
  during the preceding fiscal year in the financial return prepared in
  respect of the fourth quarter of its financial year in accordance with
  section 628 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), section 495 of the
  [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) or section 431 of the
  [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01),
  respectively;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an authorized foreign bank, the
  minimum capital equivalency deposit that it reported during the
  preceding fiscal year in its annual return prepared in accordance with
  section 601 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cooperative credit association, an
  amount equal to 1/20 of the total borrowings that it reported during
  the preceding fiscal year in the financial return prepared in respect
  of the fourth quarter of its financial year in accordance with section
  431 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a company, society or provincial
  company to which the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)
  applies, the minimum required capital that it reported during the
  preceding fiscal year in its annual return prepared in accordance with
  subsection 665(1) of that Act; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a foreign company to which the
  [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, the minimum
  required margin of assets in Canada that it reported during the
  preceding fiscal year in its annual return prepared in accordance with
  subsection 665(2) of that Act.

### [Banks, Authorized Foreign Banks, Trust and Loan Companies and Retail Associations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-835014 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The base assessment amount
for a financial institution that is a bank, an authorized foreign bank,
a trust and loan company or a retail association is, for any fiscal
year, equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the minimum assessment applicable to the institution,
  if it is greater than the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#835018 .Paragraph}
  A/B × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the capital of the institution in respect of that fiscal year,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined for A for all banks,
      authorized foreign banks, trust and loan companies and retail
      associations, other than those referred to in subsection 2(2), and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount by which the amount of expenses, ascertained under
      subsection 23(1) of the Act, incurred for or in connection with
      the administration of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the [Trust
      and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) and the [Cooperative
      Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) and attributable to
      banks, authorized foreign banks, trust and loan companies and
      retail associations in respect of that fiscal year exceeds the
      total of any service charges, assessment surcharges and other
      revenues relating to the administration of those Acts and
      attributable to those institutions in respect of that fiscal year;
      or
  :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the aggregate of the minimum
  assessment and the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#835024 .Paragraph}
  D/E × (C - F)

  where

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is as described in paragraph (a),

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount by which the amount determined by the formula in
      paragraph (a) exceeds the minimum assessment applicable to the
      institution in respect of that fiscal year,

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined for D for all banks,
      authorized foreign banks, trust and loan companies and retail
      associations, other than those referred to in subsection 2(2), and

  [F]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the minimum assessments applicable to all
      banks, authorized foreign banks, trust and loan companies and
      retail associations, other than those referred to in subsection
      2(2).
  :::

## [Assessment Surcharge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-835103 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stage rating assigned

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (4),
  the amount of the surcharge to be assessed for any fiscal year in
  respect of a financial institution that has been assigned a stage
  rating in accordance with the Office's guides to intervention for
  federally regulated financial institutions is equal to the aggregate
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each month during which the institution was
    rated at stage 1, the aggregate of \$3,000 and an amount equal to
    1/12 of 10% of its base assessment amount for the preceding fiscal
    year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each month during which the institution was
    rated at stage 2, stage 3 or stage 4, the aggregate of \$5,000 and
    an amount equal to 1/12 of 15% of its base assessment amount for the
    preceding fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the amount of the
  surcharge to be assessed for any fiscal year in respect of a financial
  institution that has been assigned a stage rating for the sole reason
  that it is an affiliate within the meaning of section 2 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) of another financial institution that has also
  been assigned a stage rating is equal to the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for each month during which the institution was
    rated at stage 1, an amount equal to 1/12 of 10% of its base
    assessment amount for the preceding fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each month during which the institution was
    rated at stage 2, stage 3 or stage 4, an amount equal to 1/12 of 15%
    of its base assessment amount for the preceding fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}If no base assessment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If no base assessment was made against a financial
  institution for the preceding fiscal year, the surcharge referred to
  in subsection (1) or (2) is to be calculated using the minimum
  assessment that would have been applicable to that institution for
  that year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the surcharge to be assessed for any
  fiscal year in respect of a financial institution must not exceed an
  amount equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a surcharge referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a) or (2)(a), 0.2% of the assets of the institution as reported
    during the preceding fiscal year in the financial or annual return
    referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (e); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a surcharge referred to in paragraph
    (1)(b) or (2)(b), 0.4% of the assets of the institution as reported
    during the preceding fiscal year in the financial or annual return
    referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (e).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple stage ratings

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount referred to in paragraph (4)(b) is
  to be applied if a financial institution was assigned more than one
  stage rating during a fiscal year.
