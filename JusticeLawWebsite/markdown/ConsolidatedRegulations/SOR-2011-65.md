Conversion from Analog to Digital Television Regulations

Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779679 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[broadcast day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcast day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a licensee,
    means the period of up to 18 consecutive hours, beginning each day
    not earlier than six o'clock in the morning and ending not later
    than one o'clock in the morning of the following day, as selected by
    the licensee. ([journée de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licensed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means licensed by the Commission
    under paragraph 9(1)(b) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01).
    ([autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a station operator or network
    operator who is authorized to operate an analog transmitter with a
    power greater than 50 watts on the VHF band or greater than
    500 watts on the UHF band. ([titulaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[network operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[network operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    licensed to operate a television network. ([exploitant de
    réseau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[programming]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means anything that is broadcast
    but does not include visual images, whether or not combined with
    sounds, that consist predominantly of alphanumeric text.
    ([programmation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a television programming
    undertaking that transmits sounds and images or a broadcasting
    transmitting undertaking that transmits sounds and images but does
    not include a broadcasting undertaking that only rebroadcasts the
    radiocommunications of another licensed broadcasting undertaking.
    ([station]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[station operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    licensed to operate a station. ([exploitant de
    station]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779688 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every licensee

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that operates in markets that have populations of
  300,000 persons or more;

- [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} that operates in markets that include the national
  capital or a provincial capital;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that operates in markets that are served by more than
  one television station; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whose programming is broadcast on channels 52 to 69
  in any market.

## [Public Service Announcements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779696 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided under a licensee's condition of licence, every
  licensee must, beginning no later than May 1, 2011, broadcast a public
  service announcement at least six times per broadcast day, increasing
  to eight times per broadcast day beginning on August 1, 2011 or one
  month before the licensee ceases to broadcast in analog or changes
  channel, whichever is sooner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 25% of the required announcements per
  broadcast day must be broadcast between 7:00 p.m. and 11:00 p.m.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The broadcast announcements must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a duration of at least 30 consecutive seconds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain the information set out in the schedule;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be closed captioned and contain audio description
    of on-screen information.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensee may use a crawl to fulfil up to 25% of
  their obligations under this section.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A crawl must not be used during the broadcast of
  programming that contains audio description of on-screen information
  or described video.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-105, s. 2

## [Other Means of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779708 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided under a licensee's condition of licence, every licensee must,
beginning no later than May 1, 2011, post and keep posted on their
website

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information regarding the conversion that is set
  out in the schedule;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the steps that an over-the-air
  viewer may need to take to continue receiving and viewing the
  station's over-the-air programming after the conversion;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the channel on which the station will broadcast after
  the conversion;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as soon as they are known, the date and time that the
  station will cease broadcasting in analog;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} as soon as they are known, the date and time that the
  station will begin or began broadcasting on the post-conversion
  channel referred to in paragraph (c); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a description of the discrete
  geographic areas where a loss of service is likely to occur.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-105, s. 3

## [Cessation of Obligation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779718 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A licensee's obligation to
broadcast and post public service announcements ceases when the licensee
completes their conversion and shuts off their analog transmitter.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-779720 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on April 1, 2011.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraphs 3(3)(b) and 4(a))]{.OriginatingRef}[Public Service Announcements]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-779723 .scheduleLabel}
:::

A public service announcement must contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement to the effect that the Canadian
  television system is in the process of converting its over-the-air
  transmitters from analog to digital;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a statement to the effect that some
  viewers may experience a loss of service following the conversion;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement to the effect that only those viewers who
  receive the signal directly over the air may be impacted by the
  conversion;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement to the effect that an over-the-air viewer
  may need to take steps to continue receiving and viewing the station's
  over-the-air programming after the conversion and an indication of
  where further information on those steps can be found; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the licensee's contact information and website
  address.
