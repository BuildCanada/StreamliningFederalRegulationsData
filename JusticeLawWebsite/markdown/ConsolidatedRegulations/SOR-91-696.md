## General Preferential Tariff Reduction Order, No. 1 {#general-preferential-tariff-reduction-order-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-696
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1991-11-28

Order Respecting the Reduction of the Rate of Customs Duty Set Out in
the General Preferential Tariff in Schedule I to the Customs Tariff

P.C. 1991-2350  1991-11-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 35(2)(b) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the reduction of the rate
of customs duty set out in the General Preferential Tariff in Schedule I
to the](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-696) [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 41
  (3rd Supp.)
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939540 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the General Preferential Tariff Reduction Order, No. 1.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-939542 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Schedule I to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is amended in the manner set out in the
schedule.

::::: section
:::: {#939544 .Schedule}

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-939545 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is
  amended by revoking the rate of customs duty set out under the heading
  "General Preferential Tariff" and substituting therefor the rate of
  customs duty "Free" in each of the following tariff item Nos.:
