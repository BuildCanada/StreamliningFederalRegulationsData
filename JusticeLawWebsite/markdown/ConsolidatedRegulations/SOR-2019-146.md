Order Calling in Certain Notes

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notes called in

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Notes issued under the
[Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2) in the following denominations are
called in:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} one dollar;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two dollars;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} twenty-five dollars;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} five hundred dollars; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} one thousand dollars.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 2021.
