## Grosse Isle, P.Q., Prohibited Place Order {#grosse-isle-p.q.-prohibited-place-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1245
:::

[FOREIGN INTERFERENCE AND SECURITY OF INFORMATION ACT](/eng/acts/O-5)

SECURITY OF INFORMATION ACT

Order Declaring that Grosse Isle, P.Q., Is a Prohibited Place
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499875 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
[Grosse Isle, P.Q., Prohibited Place
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1245).

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499877 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Grosse Isle, County of
Montmagny, Province of Quebec is hereby declared to be a prohibited
place within the meaning of the [Foreign Interference and Security of
Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5).
