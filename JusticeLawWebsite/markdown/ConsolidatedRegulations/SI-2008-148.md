## Order Governing the Grant of the Memorial Cross {#order-governing-the-grant-of-the-memorial-cross .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2008-148
:::

Registration 2008-12-24

Order Governing the Grant of the Memorial Cross

P.C. 2008-1924 2008-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence and the Minister of Veterans Affairs,
hereby makes the annexed [Order Governing the Grant of the Memorial
Cross](/eng/regulations/SI-2008-148).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613774 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[1950 Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[1950 Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Order Governing the
    Grant of the Memorial Cross to Mothers and Widows, made by Order in
    Council P.C. 5812 of December 5, 1950. ([décret de
    1950]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[2006 Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[2006 Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Order Governing the
    Grant of the Memorial Cross (Canadian
    Forces)](/eng/regulations/SI-2006-141), made by Order in Council
    P.C. 2006-1449 of November 30, 2006 and registered as SI/2006-141.
    ([décret de 2006]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer or non-commissioned
    member. ([militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representative of the estate or succession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representative of the estate or succession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the person who acts as executor or administrator of the
    estate, or liquidator of the succession, of a member or former
    member or the person who was acting in that capacity when the estate
    was settled. ([représentant de la succession]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Description]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613780 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Memorial Cross is a
cross *pattée* in silver placed on a wreath of laurel, suspended from a
brooch bar in the form of the lateral arms of the Cross; at the end of
the upright, the Royal Crown, at the foot and at the end of either arm,
a maple leaf; and in the centre, the Royal Cypher of the reigning
monarch. The reverse of the Cross is engraved with the service number,
rank, initials and surname of the member or former member who is
commemorated.

## [Service Before October 7, 2001]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613782 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Memorial Cross shall be
granted as a memento of personal loss and sacrifice in respect of the
death of a member or former member, to recipients designated under
section 4 or 5 who survive the member or former member, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member of the Supplementary Reserve,
  the member did not serve in or with the Regular Force, Primary
  Reserve, Cadet Instructors Cadre or Canadian Rangers on or after
  October 7, 2001;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former member of the Canadian
  Forces, Royal Canadian Navy, Canadian Army or Royal Canadian Air
  Force, the former member served before October 7, 2001;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the death is attributable to military service in an
  area of hostilities outside Canada in consequence of any action
  undertaken by Canada under the Charter of the United Nations, the
  North Atlantic Treaty or any other instrument for collective defence
  entered into by Canada; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the death occurs on or after the day on which this
  Order comes into force.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The member or former member
may designate, in a form approved by the Minister of Veterans Affairs, a
maximum of two recipients of the Memorial Cross.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The representative of the
estate or succession may designate recipients of the Memorial Cross, of
which the number shall not exceed, as applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} two, if the designation of recipients referred to in
  section 4 has not been made, a completed form cannot be found or no
  designated recipients survive the member or former member; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} one, if only one Memorial Cross is granted to a
  recipient designated under section 4.

## [Service on or After October 7, 2001]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613792 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Memorial Cross shall be
granted as a memento of personal loss and sacrifice in respect of the
death of a member or former member, to recipients designated under
section 7, 8 or 9 who survive the member or former member, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member or former member of the
  Regular Force, Primary Reserve, Cadet Instructors Cadre or Canadian
  Rangers, the member or former member served on or after October 7,
  2001; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member or former member of the
  Supplementary Reserve, the member or former member served in or with
  the Regular Force, Primary Reserve, Cadet Instructors Cadre or
  Canadian Rangers on or after October 7, 2001; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the death is attributable to an injury or disease
  related to military service.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The member or former member
may designate, in a form approved by the Chief of the Defence Staff, a
maximum of three recipients of the Memorial Cross.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9, the
representative of the estate or succession may designate recipients of
the Memorial Cross, of which the number shall not exceed, as applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} three, if the designation of recipients referred to
  in section 7 has not been made, a completed form cannot be found or no
  designated recipients survive the member or former member;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two, if only one Memorial Cross is granted to a
  recipient designated under section 7; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} one, if two Memorial Crosses are granted to
  recipients designated under section 7.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the death of a member or
former member who served during the period beginning on or after October
7, 2001 and ending on December 31, 2006 has occurred before the day on
which this Order comes into force, the representative of the estate or
succession may designate recipients of the Memorial Cross, of which the
number shall not exceed, as applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} three, if no Memorial Crosses were granted under the
  1950 Order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} two, if only one Memorial Cross was granted under the
  1950 Order; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} one, if two Memorial Crosses were granted under the
  1950 Order.

## [Responsible Ministers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613806 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of National Defence is responsible for the grant of the
  Memorial Cross if a member dies while serving in or with the Regular
  Force, Primary Reserve, Cadet Instructors Cadre or Canadian Rangers.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of Veterans Affairs is responsible for
  the grant of the Memorial Cross if a former member of the Canadian
  Forces, Royal Canadian Navy, Canadian Army or Royal Canadian Air Force
  dies, or a member of the Supplementary Reserve dies while not serving
  with the Regular Force, Primary Reserve, Cadet Instructors Cadre or
  Canadian Rangers.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-613810 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a member or former
member has, before the coming into force of section 14, designated
recipients, in a form approved by the Chief of the Defence Staff for the
purpose of section 6 of the 2006 Order, the designation is deemed to
have been made in the form approved under section 7 of this Order.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A recipient to whom the
Memorial Cross has been granted under the 1950 Order or the 2006 Order
shall not be granted a Memorial Cross under section 6 of this Order.
