## Order Designating Ontario for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-ontario-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2009-277
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2009-10-01

Order Designating Ontario for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate
Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2009-1628 2009-10-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Ontario has legislative measures that protect recipients of
payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing
under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Ontario has requested
that the Governor in Council designate that province for the purposes of
section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EECBA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBCBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EECBA){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365}S.C.
  2007, c. 9, s. 2
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBCBA){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366}R.S., c.
  C-46
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EGAAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EDAAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Ontario for the Purposes of
the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of
the](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-277) [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).
:::

## [Province Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750926 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Ontario is designated for
the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750928 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndE901_hq_8059){#fn_IndE901_hq_8059-ID0EBEA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m., Eastern time
or --- in that part of Ontario in the Central time zone --- Central
time, on the first day on which both of the following are in force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Payday Loans Act, 2008, S.O. 2008, c. 9, except
  for sections 52 and 66 to 74; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Regulation 98/09, except for sections 37 and
  38.
