## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Tourism and Associate Minister of Finance) to the Minister of State (Tourism) the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-tourism-and-associate-minister-of-finance-to-the-minister-of-state-tourism-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-canadian-tourism-commission-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-40
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Tourism and Associate
Minister of Finance) to the Minister of State (Tourism) the Powers,
Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Tourism Commission Act

P.C. 2023-769 2023-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers from the Minister of State (Tourism and Associate Minister of
Finance) to the Minister of State (Tourism) the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of State (Tourism and Associate Minister of
Finance) under the [Canadian Tourism Commission
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2594_hq_1690042139){#fn_81000-3-2594_hq_1690042139-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2021-945
of October 26, 2021[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2594_hq_1690042149){#fn_81000-3-2594_hq_1690042149-ID0EBEAA}.
