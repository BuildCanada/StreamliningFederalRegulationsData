## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247740 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a special
    payment required under paragraph 9(4)(c) or (d) of the [Pension
    Benefits Standards Regulations,
    1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19). ([paiement spécial de
    solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Payment Relief]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247745 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reduction of annual payments

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The solvency special
payments that are required for a plan year are to be reduced by the
total amount of all instalments of those payments in respect of that
plan year that, under paragraph 9(14)(b) of the [Pension Benefits
Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} became due during the period beginning on April 1,
  2020 and ending on the day before the day on which these Regulations
  come into force, unless the plan year ended more than 30 days before
  the day on which these Regulations come into force; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} but for subsection 3(1), would have become due during
  the period beginning on the day on which these Regulations come into
  force and ending on December 30, 2020.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instalments not required

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  paragraph 9(14)(b) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), no solvency special payment
  instalments are required to be paid during the period beginning on the
  day on which these Regulations come into force and ending on December
  30, 2020.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction from other payments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of any solvency special payment instalment
  that becomes due after March 31, 2020 and is paid no later than the
  day on which these Regulations come into force may be deducted from
  the total amount of all instalments that become due to the same plan
  under paragraphs 9(14)(a) and (b) of the [Pension Benefits Standards
  Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) during the period
  beginning on the day on which these Regulations come into force and
  ending on December 30, 2020, and the corresponding contributions and
  going concern special payments required under paragraphs 9(4)(a)
  and (b) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) are to be reduced accordingly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph 10(2)(b) of the [Pension Benefits
  Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), no interest
  is payable in respect of a special solvency payment instalment that
  became due after March 31, 2020 but before the day on which these
  Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No reduction for subsequent plan year

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 9(6) of the
[Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) does not apply in respect of any
portion of an additional payment that, but for these Regulations, would
have been payable as a solvency special payment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Letter of credit --- reduction of face
value

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The face
  value of a letter of credit that was provided or transferred before
  the day on which these Regulations come into force may, in addition to
  any reduction permitted under subsections 9.1(2) to (4) of the
  [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), be reduced by an amount not
  exceeding the total amount of all instalments referred to in
  paragraphs 2(a) and (b) of these Regulations that were to be replaced
  by the letter of credit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deeming

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the face value of a letter of credit is reduced in
  accordance with subsection (1), it is deemed to have been reduced in
  accordance with the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
  1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) and the non-renewal of that letter
  of credit for its full face value does not constitute a default for
  the purpose of section 9.1 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Void amendment --- solvency ratio

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the period beginning on
the day on which these Regulations come into force and ending on
November 30, 2020, the prescribed solvency ratio level for the purposes
of paragraphs 10.1(2)(c) and (d) of the Act is 1.05.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of information

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following information
is prescribed, in addition to the information referred to in subsections
23(1) and (1.1) of the [Pension Benefits Standards Regulations,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19), for the purposes of subparagraphs
28(1)(b)(iv) and (b.1)(ii) of the Act in respect of any plan year for
which a solvency special payment is reduced under section 2 of these
Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the reduced solvency special payments
  for the plan year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the solvency special payments that
  would have been required for the plan year, but for these Regulations.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247770 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
