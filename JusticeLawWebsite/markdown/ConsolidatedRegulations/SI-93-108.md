## Order Designating the Minister for International Trade as Minister for Purposes of the Export Development Act and as Appropriate Minister with Respect to Export Development Canada for Purposes of the Financial Administration Act {#order-designating-the-minister-for-international-trade-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-export-development-act-and-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-export-development-canada-for-purposes-of-the-financial-administration-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-108
:::

[EXPORT DEVELOPMENT ACT](/eng/acts/E-20)

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Designating the Minister for International Trade as Minister for
Purposes of the Export Development Act and as Appropriate Minister with
Respect to Export Development Canada for Purposes of the Financial
Administration Act

P.C. 1993-1454  1993-06-25
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition of
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Export
  Development Act](/eng/acts/E-20), to designate the Minister for
  International Trade, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada,
  as the Minister for the purposes of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and subparagraph
  (a)(ii) of the definition of [[appropriate
  Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 83(1) of that Act, to
  designate the Minister for International Trade, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with
  respect to Export Development Canada for the purposes of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

::: HistoricalNote
- 2001, c. 33, ss. 30, 31(F).
