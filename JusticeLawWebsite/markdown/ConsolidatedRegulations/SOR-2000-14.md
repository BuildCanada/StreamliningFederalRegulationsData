[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1999-12-16

Military Police Professional Code of Conduct

P.C. 1999-2213 1999-12-16

the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to section 13.1[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655972 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Code,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Defence
Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of this Code
shall be interpreted as being in addition to, and not in derogation of,
any power, jurisdiction or authority that may be exercised under the Act
or any other Act of Parliament.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655975 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Code applies to
officers and non-commissioned members who are appointed as military
police under regulations for the purposes of section 156 of the Act.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655977 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No member of the military
police shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowingly, without good cause, supervise or carry out
  an arrest, a detention, a search or surveillance that is unlawful;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowingly, without good cause, fail to report an
  arrest, a detention, a search or surveillance that is, or can
  reasonably be considered, unlawful;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowingly use, or permit or direct the use of,
  excessive force on any person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} while carrying out their duties, act in a
  discriminatory or discourteous manner towards any person;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} intimidate, or retaliate against, any person who
  makes a report or complaint about the conduct of a member of the
  military police;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} unlawfully discharge a weapon;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} use a weapon in a dangerous or negligent manner;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} knowingly suppress, misrepresent or falsify
  information in a report or statement;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} knowingly and improperly interfere with the conduct
  of any investigation;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} use military police information, military police
  resources or their status as a member of the military police for a
  private or another unauthorized purpose;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} disclose military police information unless
  authorized; or

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} engage in conduct that is likely to discredit the
  military police or that calls into question the member's ability to
  carry out their duties in a faithful and impartial manner.

## [Presumed Discredit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655991 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A member of the military
police is presumed to discredit the military police if the member is
convicted of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an offence under the Act, other than an offence tried
  at a summary trial for which a minor punishment or a fine of not more
  than 25% of basic monthly pay has been imposed; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an indictable offence, or an offence punishable on
  summary conviction, under any other Act of Parliament or the
  legislature of a province.

## [Duty to Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655995 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A member of the military
police shall report to their superior in the military police chain of
command any incident for which they have been charged with an offence
under the Act or any other Act of Parliament or the legislature of a
province.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A member of the military
police who believes, or is aware of an allegation, that another member
of the military police has breached this Code shall report the belief or
allegation, as the case may be, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their superior in the military police chain of
  command; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the next superior in the military police chain of
  command, if the superior referred to in paragraph (a) is the subject
  of the belief or allegation.

## [Duty to Cooperate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656000 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  member of the military police is excused from responding to any
  question relating to an investigation into a breach of this Code,
  unless the member is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the subject of the investigation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the assisting officer for another member of the
    military police who is the subject of the investigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response given by a member of the military police
  under subsection (1) shall not be used against the interests of the
  member by the Military Police Credentials Review Board established
  under Chapter 22 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders for the
  Canadian Forces.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-656006 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Code comes into force
on the day on which they are registered.
