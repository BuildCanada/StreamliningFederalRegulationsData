## Order Designating the National Transportation Act Review Commission as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister {#order-designating-the-national-transportation-act-review-commission-as-a-department-and-the-prime-minister-as-appropriate-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-39
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-03-11

Order Designating the National Transportation Act Review Commission as a
Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

P.C. 1992-293  1992-02-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the National
  Transportation Act Review Commission as a department for the purposes
  of that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Prime
  Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the National
  Transportation Act Review Commission.
