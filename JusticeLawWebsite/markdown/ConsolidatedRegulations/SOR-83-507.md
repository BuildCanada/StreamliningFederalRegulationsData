## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878439 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Access to Information Request Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Access to Information Request Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    form prescribed by the designated Minister pursuant to paragraph
    70(1)(b) of the Act for the purpose of requesting access to records
    under the control of a government institution; ([formule de demande
    d'accès à l'information]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Access to Information
    Act](/eng/acts/A-1); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the officer of a
    government institution whose title and address is published pursuant
    to paragraph 5(1)(d) of the Act. ([fonctionnaire
    compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Limitation on Production of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878444 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 4(3) of the Act, a record that does not exist but can be
produced from a machine readable record under the control of a
government institution need not be produced where the production thereof
would unreasonably interfere with the operations of the institution.

## [Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878446 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for access to a record under Part 1 of the Act must be made by
  forwarding to the appropriate officer of the government institution
  that has control of the record, together with the required application
  fee,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a completed Access to Information Request Form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written request that provides sufficient detail
    to enable the officer to identify the record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the information contained in the request is
  insufficient to establish that the person making the request has a
  right of access under section 4 of the Act, the government institution
  must request additional information from the person in order to
  confirm their right of access.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If access is to be given to
a record that contains personal information about the person who
requested access, the government institution must require the person to
provide adequate identification before the personal information is
disclosed unless the person's identity has already been confirmed.

## [Transfer of Request]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878458 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The head
  of a government institution may, within 15 days after a request for
  access to a record is received by the institution, transfer the
  request to another government institution as provided in subsection
  8(1) of the Act, on condition that the head of the other government
  institution consents to process the request within the time limit set
  out for such a request in the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request that has been transferred under
  subsection (1) shall not be transferred to a third government
  institution.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878462 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person who makes a
request for access to a record under Part 1 of the Act must pay an
application fee of \$5 at the time the request is made.

## [Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878484 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  person is given access to a record or part thereof under the control
  of a government institution, the head of the institution may require
  that the person be given an opportunity to examine the record or part
  thereof, rather than a copy of the record or part thereof if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the record or part thereof is so lengthy that
    reproduction of the record or part thereof would unreasonably
    interfere with the operations of the institution; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the record or part thereof is in a form that does
    not readily lend itself to reproduction.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is given access to a record or part
  thereof under the control of a government institution, the head of the
  institution shall provide the person with an opportunity to examine
  the record or part thereof, rather than with a copy of the record or
  part thereof, where providing a copy to that person is prohibited by
  or under another Act of Parliament.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is given access to a record under the
  control of a government institution, the head of the institution may
  require that the person be given a copy of the record, rather than an
  opportunity to examine it if,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the record forms a disclosable part of a record for
    which disclosure may otherwise be refused under Part 1 of the Act
    and from which it cannot reasonably be severed for examination; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the record is in a form that does not readily lend
    itself to examination.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where access to a record under the control of a
  government institution is given in the form of an opportunity to
  examine the record, the head of the government institution shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide reasonable facilities for the examination;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set a time for the examination that is convenient
    both for the institution and the person.

## [Limitations in Respect of Format]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878499 .Part}

- **[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 4(2.1) of the Act, if access to a record
  involves giving a copy of the record, the copy does not have to be
  provided in the requested format if the record does not exist in that
  format within the government institution and the head of the
  government institution considers, taking into account the factors
  described in subsection (3), that the conversion of the record to the
  requested format is unreasonable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the head of the government institution considers
  that the conversion of the record to the requested format is
  unreasonable, the copy of the record must be provided in a format
  chosen by the person making the request

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from a format in which the record already exists
    within the government institution; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from a format the conversion to which the head of
    the government institution considers is reasonable taking into
    account the factors described in subsection (3).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following factors are to be taken into account in
  determining if the conversion to the requested format is reasonable or
  unreasonable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs to the government institution;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the potential degradation of the record;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the person making the request is to be given
    access to only a part of a record, the facility with which the
    record may be severed in the format requested;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the existence of the record within the government
    institution in another format that is useful to the person making
    the request;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the possibility that the record can be converted to
    another format that is useful to the person making the request;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the impact on the operations of the government
    institution;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the availability of the required personnel,
    resources, technology and equipment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-187, s. 1

## [SCHEDULE II]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 10)]{.OriginatingRef}[Classes of Investigations]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-878536 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Investigations by a Fact Finding Board established by the
  Department of Transport to investigate air traffic control where it
  has been alleged that owing to a system deficiency

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} flight safety may have been jeopardized; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} less than the minimum required separation between
    aircraft may have existed.

- **2** Investigations by a Flight Service Station Review Committee
  established by the Department of Transport to investigate reported
  occurrences relating to aviation safety where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures or actions or a lack thereof,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} systems failure, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other causes

  have brought the reliability of a Flight Service Station of the Flight
  Service Station System into question.

- **3** Canadian Forces flight safety accident investigations other than
  those conducted in the form of a board of inquiry or summary
  investigation under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- **4** Investigations by or under the authority of the Canadian Forces
  Fire Marshall for the purpose of determining the cause of a fire,
  other than those conducted in the form of a board of inquiry or
  summary investigation under the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5).

- **5** Investigations by the Special Inquiries Unit of the Inspector
  General's Branch of the Correctional Service of Canada.
