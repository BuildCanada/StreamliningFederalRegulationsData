## Order Transferring from the Minister of National Health and Welfare to the Minister of Labour, the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to Regional Economic Development in Quebec and the Control and Supervision of the Federal Office of Regional Development --- Quebec {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-national-health-and-welfare-to-the-minister-of-labour-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-regional-economic-development-in-quebec-and-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-federal-office-of-regional-development-quebec .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-98
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring from the Minister of National Health and Welfare to
the Minister of Labour, the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to
Regional Economic Development in Quebec and the Control and Supervision
of the Federal Office of Regional Development --- Quebec

P.C. 1993-1395  1993-06-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer from the Minister of National Health and Welfare to
the Minister of Labour:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the powers, duties and functions in relation to
  regional economic development in the Province of Quebec; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the control and supervision of that portion of the
  public service known as the Federal Office of Regional Development ---
  Quebec.
