## [Entity Member of a Bank's Group]{.HTitleText1} {#h-680022 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entity member of bank's group *(Entité
membre du groupe d'une banque)*

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 464(2)(d) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the following
entities are members of a bank's group:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a central for which an order has been made under
  subsection 473(1) of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) that controls the bank;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of a central referred to in paragraph
  (a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which a central referred to in
  paragraph (a) has a substantial investment.
