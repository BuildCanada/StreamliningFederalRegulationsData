[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[passport]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 2 of the [Canadian Passport Order](/eng/regulations/SI-81-86);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[certificate of
  citizenship]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a travel document issued to a permanent resident
  under subsection 31(3) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an emergency travel document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to any
person who meets the conditions set out in section 3 of the fees paid or
payable under

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 7(c), 9(a) and (b) and items 10 and 14 of
  the schedule to the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 4 of the [Consular Services Fees
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-538);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} item 6 of the schedule to the [Citizenship
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-246);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} section 315 of the [Immigration and Refugee
  Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227); or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} subsections 296(1), 298(1), 299(1), 300(1), 303.2(1),
  305(1) and 315.1(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission of the fees set out in
  paragraphs 2(a) to (d) is granted on the condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is a Canadian citizen or a permanent
    resident who was in Ukraine or in another country and was required
    to travel on an urgent basis as a result of the Russian invasion of
    Ukraine, and, in the case of a remission of fees under paragraph
    2(d), the person was travelling to Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period beginning on February 22, 2022
    and ending on March 31, 2023, that person, or a person acting on
    their behalf, submitted an application for the issuance of a
    document either electronically or in person at the Canadian mission
    in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Warsaw, Poland,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Bucharest, Romania,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} Budapest, Hungary, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} Vienna, Austria; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the fees in question have not been remitted under
    section 11 of the [Passport and Other Travel Document Services Fees
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-253).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ukrainian nationals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission of the fees set out in paragraph 2(e) is
  granted on the condition that the application for a document was
  submitted by a Ukrainian national to the Minister of Citizenship and
  Immigration during the period beginning on February 22, 2022 and
  ending on March 14, 2022.
