## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027652 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). (*Loi*)

## [Meaning of "Widely Held"]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027654 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 407(4) of the Act, a life company is widely held at a
particular time if at that time

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no person has a significant interest in any class of
  shares of the life company; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where another company acquired all of the voting
  shares of the life company as a result of the conversion of the life
  company into a company with common shares and holds all of the voting
  shares of the life company at that time, no person has a significant
  interest in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any class of shares of the other company, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any class of non-voting shares of the life
    company.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027660 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 407(5) of the Act, the companies in respect of which
subsection 407(4) of the Act does not apply are life companies whose
total assets in Canada were less than \$7.5 billion on December 31,
1991.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1027662 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
