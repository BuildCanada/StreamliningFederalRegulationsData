## Printed Material for Foreign Carriers Remission Order, 1995 {#printed-material-for-foreign-carriers-remission-order-1995 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-360
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1995-07-26

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX of the Excise Tax Act on Printed Material Imported for Use by
Foreign Carriers

P.C. 1995-1202  1995-07-26
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of customs
duties imposed under the Customs Tariff and the tax imposed under
Division III of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act on printed material
imported for use by foreign carriers.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}R.S., c. 41
  (3rd Supp.)
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975072 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Printed Material for Foreign Carriers Remission Order,
1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-360).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975074 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[printed material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[printed material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods described
    in the annexed schedule; ([imprimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an airline registered
    in a foreign country; ([transporteur étranger]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Canadian carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an airline registered
    in Canada. ([transporteur Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975079 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the tax paid or payable under Division III of Part IX of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on printed material imported into Canada by or
on behalf of a foreign carrier for use exclusively in the promotion and
operation of air services provided by that carrier on condition that, at
the time of importation, the country of the foreign carrier grants a
similar privilege to Canadian carriers.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 23
