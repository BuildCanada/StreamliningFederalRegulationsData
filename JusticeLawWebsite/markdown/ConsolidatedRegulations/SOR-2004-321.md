## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713659 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713663 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in Nunavut may make any
  report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or section 4.3 of the
  Act, or provide any notification required under section 6 of the Act,
  by telephone.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not remove
  the requirement for the sex offender to report in person under
  paragraph 4.1(c) of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713667 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in Nunavut to collect information for the purposes of the
Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person employed by the RCMP whose duties include
  the collection of information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713671 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is
authorized under section 3 to collect information is authorized in
Nunavut to register information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713673 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An RCMP detachment set out
in the schedule is designated as a registration centre in Nunavut, and
its designated area of service is the area served by the detachment.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713675 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2004-321_e_hq_6626){#fn_SOR-2004-321_e_hq_6626-ID0EBCA}[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the later of the
day on which the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), chapter 10 of the Statutes of Canada, 2004, comes
into force and the day on which these Regulations are registered.
