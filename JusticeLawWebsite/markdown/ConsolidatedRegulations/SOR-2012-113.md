## Crown Share Adjustment Payments Regulations {#crown-share-adjustment-payments-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-113
:::

[CANADA-NOVA SCOTIA OFFSHORE PETROLEUM RESOURCES ACCORD IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-7.8)

Registration 2012-06-01

Crown Share Adjustment Payments Regulations

P.C. 2012-744 2012-05-31
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 154(1) of the [Canada-Nova Scotia
Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_10713){#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_10713-ID0EFBCA},
a copy of the proposed [Crown Share Adjustment Payments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-113), substantially in the
annexed form, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I on February 11, 2012 and
interested persons were given an opportunity to make representations to
the Minister of Natural Resources with respect to the proposed
Regulations;

  1988, c. 28

And whereas, pursuant to section 6 of that Act, the Provincial Minister
has been consulted by the Minister of Natural Resources with respect to
the proposed regulations and has approved the making of those
regulations;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources, pursuant to
sections 153[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_11255){#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_11255-ID0EICAA}
and 248[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_11256){#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_11256-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_10713){#fn_81000-2-68-e_hq_10713-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Crown Share Adjustment Payments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-113).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781860 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore
      Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8).
      ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[conversion date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conversion date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day that is 30
      days after the day on which the Board's fundamental decision to
      approve the development plan is implemented. ([date de
      conversion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gross revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gross revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in relation to a project,
      includes oil, natural gas and NGL production revenues as
      determined using the prices of those resources at the project
      boundary, insurance proceeds, asset sale proceeds and revenues
      derived from the provision of a service for another project in
      relation to the production of oil, natural gas and NGL in the
      offshore area. ([recettes brutes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[income tax payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[income tax payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporate
      income tax payable for a year under the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), Chapter 217 of the Revised Statutes of Nova
      Scotia, 1989, on the Provincial Crown share of the project.
      ([impôt sur le revenu à payer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[NGL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[NGL]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means natural gas liquids ---
      including propane, butane, ethane and condensates --- derived from
      the production of natural gas. ([LGN]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[project boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project boundary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point at which
      the product leaves the infrastructure as set out in the
      development plan for the project. ([limite du
      projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Provincial Crown share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Provincial Crown share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 12.5% in the
      case of natural gas and NGL production and 6.25% in the case of
      oil production. ([part de la Couronne
      provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The calculations set out in sections 4 and 6 shall be
  made in respect of each of the oil, natural gas and NGL components of
  a project, if the project consists of at least 2 of those components.

## [Forecasts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781871 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any forecast referred to in
these Regulations, other than one provided in the development plan under
section 7, shall be the average of the forecasts made closest to the
date of conversion by three independent organizations designated jointly
in writing by the Federal Minister and the Provincial Minister.

## [Annual Average Cost of Borrowing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781873 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of the definition [[average annual cost to the Province of
  borrowing money]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 246(1) of the Act,
  the average annual rate of interest is equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the weighted average of the effective yield on all
    outstanding debt instruments, including promissory notes, issued by
    the Province during the 12-month period immediately preceding the
    date of calculation of the rate of return referred to in subsection
    247(2) of the Act adjusted for the effect of any debt swap
    arrangements in effect during that period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Province has issued no debt instruments in
    that period, the average of long-term Government of Canada benchmark
    bond yields for that period.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Provincial Minister shall provide to the Federal
  Minister a list, certified by an independent auditor, of the debt
  instruments that includes their dates of issuance, their dates of
  maturity and their effective yields.

## [Crown Share Adjustment Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781879 .Part}

### [Profit Realized]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781880 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 247(1) of the Act, the profit realized for a year is equal to
the amount determined by the following formula:

::: {#781882 .Section}
A × (B -- C) -- D

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the Provincial Crown share;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the gross revenues of the project for the year;

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the sum, for the year, of the project operating costs, the
    project capital costs and the project royalties payable under the
    Offshore Petroleum Royalty Act;

[D]{.dfn}
:   is the sum, for the year, of the provincial capital loan repayment
    determined under subsection 9(1), the acquisition payment determined
    under subsection 10(1) and the income tax payable .
:::

### [Information Required to Determine Profit Realized]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781888 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a project shall, over the life of a project beginning on
  the day on which the development plan is submitted under subsection
  143(2) of the Act, provide the following information to the Federal
  Minister no later than June 30th of each year for the preceding year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project operating costs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the project capital costs categorized in accordance
    with their tax treatment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Federal Minister, any other
    information necessary to determine the profit in relation to a
    project or to verify the accuracy and completeness of the
    information provided under this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interest holder, as defined in section 49 of the
  Act, shall, over the life of a project beginning on the day on which
  the development plan is submitted under subsection 143(2) of the Act,
  provide the following information to the Federal Minister no later
  than June 30 of each year for the preceding year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the project gross revenues;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information provided under the Offshore
    Petroleum Royalty Act for the purpose of the calculation of the
    royalties payable under that Act in respect of the project; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Federal Minister, any other
    information necessary to determine the profit in relation to a
    project or to verify the accuracy and completeness of the
    information provided under this section.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If information provided for any year is inaccurate or
  incomplete, the Federal Minister shall, as soon as feasible, request
  accurate and complete information.

### [Rate of Return of the Project]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781899 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 247(2) of the Act, the rate of return in
  respect of the project that would have been obtained on behalf of Her
  Majesty in right of the Province is equal to the interest rate that
  would make the net present value of the provincial cash flow,
  calculated over the life of the project beginning on the conversion
  date, equal to zero.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provincial cash flow for a year is equal to the
  amount determined by the following formula:

  ::: {#781903 .Subsection}
  A × (B~1~ -- C~1~) -- D~1~

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the Provincial Crown share;

  [B~1~]{.dfn}
  :   is the gross revenues of the project for the year determined under
      section 8;

  [C~1~]{.dfn}
  :   is the sum, for the year, of the project operating and capital
      costs, as specified in the development plan approved in respect of
      the project under section 143 of the Act converted to current
      dollars using the forecasted Canadian gross domestic product (GDP)
      deflator in that year, and the project royalties that would be
      payable under the Offshore Petroleum Royalty Act; and

  [D~1~]{.dfn}
  :   is the sum, for the year, of the acquisition payment determined
      under subsection 10(1) and the income tax payable.
  :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The calculation made under subsection (2) shall be
  based on the mean production scenario provided in the development
  plan.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Federal Minister determines that the rate of
  return in respect of a project meets the requirements of subsection
  247(2) of the Act, no further determination shall be made under that
  subsection due to an amendment to the development plan unless the
  amendment is in respect of a new pool or field.

### [Information Required in Development plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781911 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 143(3)(b) of the Act, Part II of the development plan shall
include,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a mean production scenario,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a forecast of the annual project operating and
    capital costs over the life of the project in constant dollars and
    categorized in accordance with their income tax treatment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the pre-development costs in constant dollars,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the forecast volume of annual production of NGL
    and crude oil over the life of the project in cubic metres, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the forecast volume of annual production of
    natural gas over the life of the project in gigajoules;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forecast annual difference over the life of the
  project in constant US dollars per cubic metre between

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the crude oil price at the project boundary and the
    West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude oil price at Cushing, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the NGL price at the project boundary and the WTI
    crude oil price at Cushing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the forecast annual difference over the life of the
  project in constant US dollars per gigajoule between the natural gas
  price at the project boundary and the natural gas price at Henry Hub;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the forecast of any other annual gross revenues over
  the life of the project in respect of the project in constant dollars.

### [Project Gross Revenues --- Rate of Return]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781923 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 6(2), the project gross revenue for a year is
  equal to the sum of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forecast volume of the crude oil production in
    the year multiplied by the forecast crude oil price in that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the forecast volume of natural gas production in
    the year multiplied by the forecast natural gas price in that year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the forecast volume of NGL production in the year
    multiplied by the forecast NGL price in that year; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the forecast of any other gross revenues in respect
    of the project in the year multiplied by the forecast Canadian GDP
    deflator for the year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The forecast crude oil, natural gas or NGL price for
  a year is equal to the amount determined by the following formula:

  ::: {#781931 .Subsection}
  (P + E) × I × PFX

  where

  [P]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of NGL or crude oil, the forecast
        WTI crude oil price at Cushing in constant US dollars per cubic
        metre; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of natural gas, the forecast
        natural gas price at Henry Hub in constant US dollars per
        gigajoule;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of NGL or crude oil, the forecast
        difference in constant US dollars per cubic metre between the
        crude oil price or the NGL price, as the case may be, at the
        project boundary and the WTI crude oil price at Cushing; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of natural gas, the forecast
        difference in constant US dollars per gigajoule between the
        natural gas price at the project boundary and the natural gas
        price at Henry Hub;

  [I]{.dfn}
  :   is the forecast US GDP deflator for the year; and

  [PFX]{.dfn}
  :   is the forecast rate for the conversion of a US dollar into a
      Canadian dollar for the year.
  :::

### [Provincial Capital Loan Repayment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781941 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provincial capital loan repayment is equal to the amount determined by
  the following formula:

  ::: {#781944 .Subsection}
  A × (B -- C)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the Provincial Crown share;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the gross revenue of the project for the year; and

  [C ]{.dfn}
  :   is the sum, for the year, of project operating costs, project
      capital costs and the project royalties payable under the Offshore
      Petroleum Royalty Act.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provincial capital loan repayments shall be
  considered during the period beginning in the year in which the cash
  flow first becomes positive and ending in the year in which the sum of
  the repayments considered is equal to the cumulative cash flow, plus
  interest, from the conversion date until the year prior to the year in
  which cash flow first becomes positive. Any years of negative cash
  flow after this period shall not be considered for the purposes of
  determining Crown share adjustment payments.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The interest referred to in subsection (2) shall be
  calculated annually using either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the weighted average of the effective yield on all
    outstanding debt instruments, including promissory notes, issued by
    the Province during the year adjusted for the effect of any debt
    swap arrangements in effect during that year; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Province has issued no debt instruments in
    that year, the average of long-term Government of Canada benchmark
    bond yields for that year.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section "cash flow" means the amount
  determined by the following formula:

  ::: {#781954 .Subsection}
  A × (B -- C) -- D~2~

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the Provincial Crown share;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is gross revenue of the project for the year;

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is equal to, for the purpose of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the description of D in section 4, the sum, for
        the year, of the project operating and capital costs, and the
        project royalties payable under the Offshore Petroleum Royalty
        Act; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of D~1~ in subsection 6(2), the
        sum, for the year, of the project operating and capital costs,
        as specified in the development plan approved in respect of the
        project under section 143 of the Act and converted to current
        dollars using the forecasted Canadian GDP deflator in that year,
        and the project royalties that would be payable under the
        Offshore Petroleum Royalty Act; and

  [D~2~]{.dfn}
  :   is the income tax payable for the year.
  :::

### [Acquisition Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781962 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  acquisition payment for a year is equal to one half of the net revenue
  for the year.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The net revenue is equal to the amount determined by
  the following formula:

  ::: {#781966 .Subsection}
  A × (B~3~ -- C~3~)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the Provincial Crown share;

  [B~3~]{.dfn}
  :   is, for the purposes of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the description of D in section 4, the gross
        revenues of the project for the year; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of D~1~ in subsection 6(2), the
        gross revenues of the project determined under section 8 for the
        year.

  [C~3~]{.dfn}
  :   is, for the purposes of
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the description of D in section 4, the sum, for
        the year, of the project operating and capital costs, and the
        project royalties payable under the Offshore Petroleum Royalty
        Act; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of D~1~ in subsection 6(2), the
        sum, for the year, of the project operating and capital costs,
        as specified in the development plan approved in respect of the
        project under section 143 of the Act and converted to current
        dollars using the forecasted Canadian GDP deflator in that year,
        and the project royalties that would be payable under the
        Offshore Petroleum Royalty Act.
  :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The acquisition payments shall be considered during
  the period beginning in the year in which the cumulative net revenue
  since the conversion date becomes positive and ending the year in
  which the sum of the acquisition payments considered is equal to the
  sum of the incentives --- adjusted in accordance with the following
  formula --- that were paid in respect of the project under the
  Petroleum Incentive Program Act, chapter 107 of the Statutes of
  Canada, 1980--81--82--83, from the conversion date, plus simple
  interest:

  ::: {#781976 .Subsection}
  2.5 × A × H × \[1 + 0.01n\]

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the Provincial Crown share; and

  [H]{.dfn}
  :   is 25% of the eligible costs and expenses in respect of which an
      incentive was made under the Petroleum Incentive Program Act
      chapter 107 of the Statutes of Canada, 1980--81--82--83, and which
      have not already been considered in the calculation of a Crown
      share adjustment payment in respect of another project; and

  [n]{.dfn}
  :   is the number of months in the period beginning in the month
      following the month in which the incentive referred to in element
      H was made and ending in the month in which the conversion date
      falls.
  :::

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Simple interest shall be calculated annually based on
  the annual average of long-term Government of Canada benchmark bond
  yields.

## [Fiscal Incentives and other Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781982 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of paragraph 247(3)(b) of the Act, a copy of any proposed
  fiscal incentive or other benefit shall be provided to the Provincial
  Minister for approval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Provincial Minister has 60 days from the date of
  reception of the copy of the proposed fiscal incentive or other
  benefit to approve it. However, the deadline may, on request, be
  extended by up to 30 days if circumstances beyond the control of the
  Provincial Minister justify the extension. If there is no response
  within the deadline, the Provincial Minister shall be considered to
  have given his or her approval.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If the aggregate amount
of fiscal incentives and other benefits for a project for a fiscal year
is greater than the Crown share adjustment payment for the project for
the year, the difference shall be included in the aggregate amount of
fiscal incentives and other benefits for the following fiscal year.

## [Delay of Crown Share Adjustment Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781987 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** If the information
referred to in subsection 5(1) or (2) is provided after June 30, the
Crown share adjustment payment may be made after the deadline set out in
subsection 247(4) of the Act.

## [Recovery of Overpayment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781989 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The amount of any
overpayment made for a fiscal year shall be subtracted from the Crown
share adjustment payment for subsequent fiscal years until all of the
overpayment has been recovered.

## [Payment of Underpayment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781991 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If the Federal Minister
determines that there has been an underpayment of any Crown share
adjustment payment for a fiscal year, the amount of the underpayment
shall be added to the Crown share adjustment payment for the fiscal year
following the determination.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781993 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
