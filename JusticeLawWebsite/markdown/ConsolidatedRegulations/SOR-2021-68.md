CUKTCA Tariff Preference Regulations

P.C. 2021-239 2021-03-26

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[originating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 24(1)(b) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011),
originating products exported from a CUKTCA beneficiary are entitled to
the benefit of the United Kingdom Tariff if they are shipped to Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} without shipment through another country that is not
  a CUKTCA beneficiary, either

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on a through bill of lading, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without a through bill of lading and the importer
    provides, when requested by an officer, documentary evidence that
    indicates the shipping route and all points of shipment and
    transhipment prior to the importation of the products; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} through another country that is not a CUKTCA
  beneficiary and the importer provides, when requested by an officer,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} documentary evidence that indicates the shipping
    route and all points of shipment and transhipment prior to the
    importation of the products, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the customs control documents that
    establish that the products remained under customs control while in
    that other country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2021-68_e_transform_hq_28951){#fn_SOR-2021-68_e_transform_hq_28951-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 37 of the [Canada--United Kingdom Trade Continuity Agreement
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-10.56), chapter 1 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2021, comes into force, but if they are registered after that
day, they come into force on the day on which they are registered.
