## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804071 .Part}

Definition of [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Marketing Authorization, [[List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the List
  of Maximum Residue Limits (MRLs) for Veterinary Drugs in Foods
  published by the Department of Health on its website, as amended from
  time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Words and expressions used in the List have the same
  meaning as in Part B of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804077 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** When a veterinary drug that
is set out in column I of the List or one of its metabolites --- and
that is analysed as being the substance named in column II --- is
present in a food that is set out in column III, the food is exempt from
the application of paragraphs 4(1)(a) and (d) of the [Food and Drugs
Act](/eng/acts/F-27) in respect of the drug and its metabolites, if the
amount of the substance present in the food does not exceed the maximum
residue limit that is set out in column IV for that food and if any
other condition that is set out in that column is met.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-804080 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Marketing
Authorization comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
