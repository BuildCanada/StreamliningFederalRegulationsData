## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499646 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Navigable Waters Works
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1232).

## [Buoys and Marks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499659 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall build or
place a work in a navigable water unless all lights, buoys and other
marks required in the approval are installed and maintained to the
satisfaction of the Minister.

## [Equipment and Debris]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499661 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall permit any
tools, equipment, vehicles, temporary structures or parts thereof used
or maintained for the purpose of building or placing a work in a
navigable water to remain in such water after the completion of the
project.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a work or a portion
of a work that is being constructed or maintained in a navigable water
causes debris or other material to accumulate on the bed or on the
surface of such water, the owner of that work or portion of that work
shall cause the debris or other material to be removed to the
satisfaction of the Minister.

## [Dams]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499664 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  section, [[work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dam or power plant in a
  navigable water.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a work shall, when required by the
  Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} install, maintain and operate log chutes to permit
    the transport of logs through or over the work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide and maintain roads or foot-ways for the
    free passage of the public by vehicle or foot around the work
    between the upper and lower reaches of the river; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} furnish to the Minister the records of flow,
    elevation of water above and below the work and all plans and other
    material relating to navigation that may be required by the
    Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or his authorized representative shall
  be permitted to measure the discharge of water in the various channels
  and passages through or over a work.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a work shall maintain the limits of flow
  and elevation of water for navigation purposes as required by the
  Minister.

## [Exploration and Development]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499673 .Part}

### [Lights]{.HTitleText2} {#h-499674 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a work built or placed for the purposes of exploration or
  development of natural resources, and the transport, removal or
  handling of such resources from the waters or bed of a navigable water
  shall, whether such work is of a permanent, temporary or floating
  character and whether fixed to the bed or otherwise moored by anchors,
  spuds or cables, install and maintain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a work having a maximum horizontal dimension of
    9 m or less on any one side or through any diameter thereof, one
    light visible in all directions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a work having a maximum horizontal dimension of
    over 9 m but less than 15 m on any one side or through any diameter
    thereof, two lights on diagonally opposite corners or in a position
    satisfactory to the Minister, each visible in all directions and
    mounted on the same horizontal plane; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on a work having a maximum horizontal dimension of
    15 m or more on one side or through any diameter thereof, a light
    visible in all directions at each corner of the work or at the outer
    limits of each quadrant thereof with 90° separation, mounted on the
    same horizontal plane.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A light installed pursuant to subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be white, displaying a quick flash characteristic
    of 60 flashes per minute;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a nominal range of at least 12.8 km;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be powered by a reliable power source and possess
    an auxiliary power source; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} be installed not less than 6 m above the water so
    that at least one light will be visible from any angle of approach
    until within 15 m of the work.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Lights shall be displayed between sunset and sunrise,
  and from sunrise to sunset in periods of restricted visibility, and
  where more than one light is installed the lights shall flash in
  unison.

## [Sound Signals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499688 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a work
referred to in subsection 8(1) shall install and maintain thereon a
sound signal

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is powered by a reliable source of power;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that possesses an auxiliary power source;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that emits a sound of two seconds duration with an
  18-second pause during every period of 20 seconds;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that has its maximum intensity at a frequency between
  100 and 1 000 Hertz and has a usual range of 3.2 km in any horizontal
  direction, measured at a height not exceeding 7.5 m;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} that has not more than eight sound sources;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} that is not less than 3 m or more than 30 m above the
  surface of the water and is so placed that the signal emitted is
  audible, up to and including the required range, in all directions in
  a horizontal plane from the work when there is no wind; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} that is sounded whenever the visibility is less than
  8 km in any direction.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-182, s. 2
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a work
referred to in subsection 8(1) shall install thereon identification
panels displaying block letters for the purpose of identifying his work,
which letters shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} black in colour;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a yellow background;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} not less than 1 m in height; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} outlined in retro-reflectors or retro-reflecting
  material or otherwise illuminated at night.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-182, s. 3
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-499716 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The owner of a work shall
notify the office of the engineer within whose district the work has
been located of any lights or sound signals that have ceased to function
or of any change in the position of a work or any other facts affecting
the safety of navigation.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every person who violates
any provision of these Regulations is liable on summary conviction to a
fine of \$500 or to imprisonment for a term of six months or to both.

- SOR/86-966, s. 4
