## Spirit Destruction Remission Order {#spirit-destruction-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 793
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Excise Duty on Spirits Destroyed by
Accident
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563817 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Spirit Destruction Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._793).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563819 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bonding warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bonding warehouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    assigned to that term by the Act. ([entrepôt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563823 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission of the excise duty imposed pursuant to subsection 1(1) of Part
I of the schedule to the Act and payable pursuant to section 52 and
subsection 54(1) of the Act is hereby granted where spirits are
destroyed due to accidents incurred

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when transporting the spirits in bond; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} while handling the spirits in licensed bonding
  warehouses.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563827 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 3
may be granted on the condition that a request therefor in writing is
forwarded to the Minister of National Revenue together with documentary
evidence that establishes to his satisfaction that the spirits

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have not been illegally removed from the bonding
  warehouse;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} were not destroyed through the failure of the
  shipper, warehouse operator or transportation company to exercise
  reasonable diligence and effective control of his operations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} were destroyed before being released from the control
  of excise officers.
