## Perfluorinated Ion-Exchange Membranes Remission Order {#perfluorinated-ion-exchange-membranes-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 778
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Perfluorinated
Ion-Exchange Membranes
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561920 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Perfluorinated Ion-Exchange Membranes Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._778).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561922 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of all customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on perfluorinated ion-exchange membranes
that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for use in the production of chlorine or sodium
  hydroxide; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported during the period commencing on January 1,
  1979 and ending June 30, 1990.
