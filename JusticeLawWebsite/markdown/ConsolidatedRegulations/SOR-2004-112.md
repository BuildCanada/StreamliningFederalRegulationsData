## Gully Marine Protected Area Regulations {#gully-marine-protected-area-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-112
:::

[OCEANS ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.4)

Registration 2004-05-07

Gully Marine Protected Area Regulations

P.C. 2004-606 2004-05-07
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsection 35(3) and
section 52.1 of the [Oceans Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EDABA}, hereby makes the
annexed [Gully Marine Protected Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-112).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EDABA){#footnotea}S. C. 1996, c. 31
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708807 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, all geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) are
  expressed in the North America Datum 1983 (NAD83) geodetic reference
  system .

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In Schedules 1 and 2, the lines connecting the points
  are rhumb lines.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708811 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The area of the Atlantic
Ocean depicted in Schedule 1 --- consisting of the seabed, the subsoil
to a depth of 15 m and the water column above the seabed --- that is
bounded by a rhumb line drawn from a point 44°13′N, 59°06′W to a point
43°47′N, 58°35′W, then to a point 43°35′N, 58°35′W, then to a point
43°35′N, 59°08′W, then to a point 43°55′N, 59°08′W, then to a point
44°06′N, 59°20′W (which points are shown as points 1 to 6, respectively,
in Schedule 1), and back to the point of origin, is hereby designated as
a marine protected area to be known as the Gully Marine Protected Area.

## [Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708813 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Gully Marine Protected
Area consists of the following management zones:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1, as depicted in Schedule 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2, as depicted in Schedule 2, consisting of the
  area surrounding Zone 1,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 3, as depicted in Schedule 2, consisting of the
  Gully Marine Protected Area, other than Zones 1 and 2.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708820 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 8 to
10, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy in the Gully Marine
  Protected Area, or remove from it, any living marine organism or any
  part of its habitat;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy in the Gully Marine
  Protected Area, or remove from it, any part of the seabed, including
  the subsoil to a depth of 15 m of the seabed; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity --- including depositing,
  discharging or dumping any substance, or causing any substance to be
  deposited, discharged or dumped --- in the Gully Marine Protected Area
  or in the vicinity of that Area that is likely to result in the
  disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of anything referred to in
  paragraph (a) or (b).

- SOR/2008-99, s. 18

## [Plan to Be Submitted]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708827 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 10 and
11, every person who proposes to carry out an activity in the Gully
Marine Protected Area must submit to the Minister for approval, not less
than 60 days before beginning the proposed activity, a plan that
indicates the management zone in which the activity is proposed to be
carried out and includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the purpose of the activity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identity of every ship and aircraft proposed to
  be used during or in connection with the activity;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed period or periods during which the
  activity will take place;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the location of the activity, expressed in latitude
  and longitude;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} two copies of all plans and specifications relating
  to the activity;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} two copies of a report assessing the environmental
  impact of the activity on the Gully Marine Protected Area, including a
  consideration of any cumulative environmental effects that are likely
  to result from the activity in combination with any other past and
  current activities undertaken in or affecting that Area and any other
  anticipated activities that may be undertaken in or may affect that
  Area;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of every licence, permit, authorization or
  consent obtained or applied for in respect of the activity; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number and, if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of the
  contact person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-99, s. 19
:::

::: PITLink

Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister shall, within 30 days after receiving
  a plan submitted in accordance with section 5, approve the plan,
  subject to the following conditions, as applicable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of scientific research or monitoring
    activities in Zone 1, the activities

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are to be carried out for the purpose of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} managing the Gully Marine Protected Area or
        monitoring the effectiveness of the conservation measures being
        implemented in that Area, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} investigating incidents that may have an
        environmental impact on the Gully Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are to be carried out for a purpose other than
      the purposes referred to in subparagraph (i) and will not result
      in any damage or destruction referred to in section 4 in Zone 1 or
      2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of scientific research or monitoring
    activities in Zone 2 or 3, the activities

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are to be carried out for the purpose of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} managing the Gully Marine Protected Area or
        monitoring the effectiveness of the conservation measures being
        implemented in that Area, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} investigating incidents that may have an
        environmental impact on the Gully Marine Protected Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are to be carried out for a purpose other than
      the purposes referred to in subparagraph (i) and

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} will not result in any damage or destruction
        referred to in section 4 in Zone 1 or 2, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} will not result in any damage or destruction
        referred to in section 4 in Zone 3 or will only result in damage
        or destruction referred to in section 4 in Zone 3 that is within
        the natural variation of the ecosystem in which that Zone is
        located; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other activity in the Gully
    Marine Protected Area, the activity

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will be limited to Zone 3,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} will not result in any disturbance, damage,
      destruction or removal referred to in section 4 in Zone 1 or 2,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} will not result in any disturbance, damage,
      destruction or removal referred to in section 4 in Zone 3 or will
      only result in disturbance, damage, destruction or removal
      referred to in section 4 in Zone 3 that is within the natural
      variation of the ecosystem in which that Zone is located.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not approve the plan referred to
  in subsection (1) if the cumulative environmental effects of the
  proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
  activities undertaken in or affecting the Gully Marine Protected Area
  and any other anticipated activities that may be undertaken in or may
  affect that Area, are likely to result in disturbance, damage,
  destruction or removal that exceeds the parameters described in the
  conditions set out in paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c), as applicable.

- SOR/2008-99, s. 20

## [Accidents to Be Reported]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708861 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any person involved in an
accident that is likely to result in any disturbance, damage,
destruction or removal prohibited under section 4 shall, within two
hours after its occurrence, report the accident to the Canadian Coast
Guard.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-99, s. 21
:::

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708865 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Living marine organisms may
be removed from Zone 2 or 3 if they are removed by the holder of a valid
commercial fishing licence issued under subsection 7(1) of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence is for swordfish, tuna or shark and the
  holder of the licence, when fishing for a species of fish authorized
  by the licence, complies with the terms and conditions of the licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licence is for groundfish and the holder of the
  licence, when fishing for halibut, complies with the terms and
  conditions of the licence; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licence is a valid commercial fishing licence,
  other than a licence referred to in paragraph (a) or (b), and the
  holder of the licence, when fishing for a species of fish authorized
  by the licence,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} complies with the terms and conditions of the
    licence,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not cause any damage or destruction referred
    to in section 4 in Zone 2,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} causes only damage or destruction referred to in
    section 4 in Zone 3 that is within the natural variation of the
    ecosystem in which that Zone is located, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} removes living marine organisms from Zone 2 or 3
    only to an extent that is within the natural variation of the
    ecosystem in which Zone 2 or 3, as the case may be, is located.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Paragraph 4(c) does not
apply in respect of an activity carried out in the vicinity of the Gully
Marine Protected Area if the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal
referred to in that paragraph

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is limited to Zone 3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is within the natural variation of the ecosystem in
  which Zone 3 is located.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-99, s. 22
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-9-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 9"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 and 5 do not
apply in respect of any movement or other activity of a ship, submarine
or aircraft if the movement or other activity is carried out for the
purpose of public safety, law enforcement or national security or for
the exercise of Canadian sovereignty and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ship, submarine or aircraft is owned or operated
  by or on behalf of Her Majesty or by a foreign military force acting
  in cooperation with, or under the command or control of, the Canadian
  Forces; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the movement or other activity is carried out for the
  purpose of an emergency response under the direction, command or
  control of the Canadian Coast Guard.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 does not apply
in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing activities carried out in Zone 2 or 3 by the
  holder of a valid commercial fishing licence, issued under subsection
  7(1) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), if the activities are
  carried out in a manner that complies with the terms and conditions of
  the licence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} marine scientific research activities that are
  carried out or sponsored by a foreign government in the Gully Marine
  Protected Area and in respect of which that government has received
  the consent of the Minister of Foreign Affairs under paragraph 3(2)(c)
  of the [Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3), if the activities are
  carried out in a manner that complies with the terms and conditions of
  the consent; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the activities of a ship that is exercising
  international navigational rights in the Gully Marine Protected Area
  and is not contravening the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) or
  any requirements of the International Maritime Organization.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708886 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
