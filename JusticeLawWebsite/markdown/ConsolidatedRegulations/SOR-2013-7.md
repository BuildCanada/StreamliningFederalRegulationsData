## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803880 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next
    year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[National Do Not Call List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[National Do Not Call List]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the national
    do not call list established for the purposes of section 41 of the
    [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4). ([Liste nationale de
    numéros de télécommunication exclus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803885 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that subscribes to the National Do Not Call List must pay to the
  Commission the fees payable by them in accordance with sections 3 and
  4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}For greater certainty --- rates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the fees referred to in
  subsection (1) are in addition to any rates that may be charged by a
  delegate under subsection 41.4(1) of the [Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), including those that may be charged to
  subscribe to the National Do Not Call List.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-199, s. 1(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20130401.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to be paid on subscription

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  subscribing to the National Do Not Call List, a person must pay to the
  Commission, or to a person to whom the Commission has delegated the
  power to collect fees under subsection 41.3(1) of the
  [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4), if applicable, the amount
  set out in column 2 that corresponds to each of the person's
  subscription types set out in column 1 of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Schedule 1, if the person subscribes during the
    period beginning on August 1, 2015 and ending on March 31, 2016;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Schedule 2, if the person subscribes during the
    period beginning on April 1, 2016 and ending on March 31, 2017; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Schedule 3, if the person subscribes on or after
    April 1, 2017.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of fee payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No later than 90 days after the end of each fiscal
  year in which amounts are paid under subsection (1), the Commission or
  person to whom the Commission has delegated the power to collect fees
  under subsection 41.3(1) of the [Telecommunications
  Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine in accordance with subsection 4(1) or
    (2), as the case may be, the fees payable to the Commission by each
    person for that fiscal year; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if those fees payable are less than the amounts
    paid by the person under subsection (1) in that fiscal year, refund
    to them the excess amount in accordance with subsection 4(3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-199, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20130401.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees payable --- amounts paid do not
exceed costs

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  total of all amounts paid under subsection 3(1) in a given fiscal year
  is less than or equal to the Commission's telemarketing regulatory
  costs for that fiscal year, the fees payable by a person for that
  fiscal year are equal to the amounts paid by them under that
  subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees payable --- amounts paid exceed
  costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the total of all amounts paid under subsection
  3(1) in a given fiscal year exceeds the Commission's telemarketing
  regulatory costs for that fiscal year, the fees payable by a person
  for that fiscal year are equal to the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#803910 .Subsection}
  (A / B) × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amounts paid by the person under subsection 3(1) in the
      fiscal year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the total of all amounts paid under subsection 3(1) in the
      fiscal year; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the Commission's telemarketing regulatory costs for the fiscal
      year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The difference between the amounts paid by a person
  under subsection 3(1) in a fiscal year and the fees payable by them
  under subsection 4(2) for that fiscal year is, if it is at least five
  dollars, to be refunded to the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telemarketing regulatory costs

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The telemarketing regulatory costs of the Commission
  for a given fiscal year are the portion of the costs of the
  Commission's activities for that fiscal year, as set out in the
  Commission's Expenditure Plan published in Part III of the Estimates
  of the Government of Canada and, if applicable, the Supplementary
  Estimates of the Government of Canada, that are attributable to the
  Commission's responsibilities under section 41.2 of the
  [Telecommunications Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4) and that are not recovered
  under any regulation made under section 68 of that Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-199, s. 3

## [Public Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-803921 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telemarketing regulatory costs

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commission is to publish, each year in a public notice in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, its telemarketing
  regulatory costs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Total amounts paid

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission is to publish, each year in a public
  notice, the total of all amounts paid under subsection 3(1) in the
  last completed fiscal year.
