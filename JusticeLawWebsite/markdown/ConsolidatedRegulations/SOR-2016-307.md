## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-835137 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Marine Liability
      Act](/eng/acts/M-0.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Administrator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Administrator of
      the Ship-source Oil Pollution
      Fund. ([administrateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[receive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[receive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of contributing oil,
      means to receive the oil into tankage or storage immediately after
      carriage by a ship. ([recevoir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundary of sea (east coast)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the St.
  Lawrence River ends and the sea begins at a straight line drawn

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} from Cap-des-Rosiers to West Point, Anticosti
    Island; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} from Anticosti Island to the north shore of the St.
    Lawrence River along the meridian of longitude 63° W.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Ship-source Oil Pollution Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-835148 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer price index

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 113(3)(b) of the Act, the average of the
  Consumer Price Index for any 12-month period must be calculated by
  dividing by 12 the aggregate of the Consumer Price Indexes, excluding
  the food and energy components, for each month in that 12-month
  period.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fraction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the quotient obtained contains a fraction, the
  fraction must be expressed as a decimal fraction rounded to one digit
  after the decimal point as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the second digit after the decimal point is less
    than five, that digit shall be dropped; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the second digit is five or greater than five,
    the first digit after the decimal point must be increased by one and
    the second digit must be dropped.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[International Fund and Supplementary Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-835156 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies in respect of contributing oil that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been imported by sea into Canada, in bulk as
    cargo on a ship, and is to be unloaded at a terminal installation or
    port in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been shipped by sea to a port in the United
    States, in bulk as cargo on a ship, has subsequently been imported
    into Canada by a mode of transport other than a ship, and is to be
    unloaded at an installation in Canada; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has been shipped by sea from a place in Canada or
    from an offshore installation in the exclusive economic zone of
    Canada, in bulk as cargo on a ship, and is to be unloaded at a
    terminal installation or port in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who receives, in a calendar year,
  contributing oil in a quantity exceeding 150 000 metric tons must file
  with the Administrator, no later than February 28 of the following
  calendar year, an information return in the form established by the
  Minister in respect of that oil.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the quantity of
  contributing oil is the aggregate of the quantity received by the
  person and the quantities received by associated persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information return must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, email address, phone
    number and fax number, if applicable, of the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of contributing oil and the total quantity
    of each type of contributing oil received in that calendar year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the name and address of
    associated persons that have received contributing oil.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[International Hazardous and Noxious Substances Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-835172 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies in respect of contributing cargo that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been imported by sea into Canada and is to be
    unloaded at a terminal installation or port in Canada; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has been shipped by sea from a place in Canada or
    from an offshore installation in the exclusive economic zone of
    Canada, and is to be unloaded at a terminal installation or port in
    Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A receiver who receives, in a calendar year, the
  following types and quantities of contributing cargo must file with
  the Minister, no later than February 28 of the following calendar
  year, an information return respecting that cargo:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} liquefied natural gases referred to in paragraph
    2(b) of Article 16 of the Hazardous and Noxious Substances
    Convention, in any quantity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} liquefied petroleum gases referred to in paragraph
    2(c) of Article 16 of the Hazardous and Noxious Substances
    Convention, in a quantity exceeding 17 000 metric tons; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any hazardous and noxious substances referred to in
    paragraphs 5(a)(ii), (iii), (v), (vi) and (vii) of Article 1 of the
    Hazardous and Noxious Substances Convention, in a quantity exceeding
    17 000 metric tons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-persistent oil

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A receiver who receives, in a calendar year,
  contributing cargo in the form of non-persistent oil in a quantity
  exceeding 17 000 metric tons must file with the Administrator, no
  later than February 28 of the following calendar year, an information
  return in the form established by the Minister in respect of that
  cargo.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated persons

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (2) and (3), the
  quantity of contributing cargo is the aggregate of the quantity
  received by the receiver and the quantities received by associated
  persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The information return referred to in subsections (2)
  and (3) must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, email address, phone
    number and fax number, if applicable, of the receiver;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the type of contributing cargo, and the total
    quantity of each type of contributing cargo received in that
    calendar year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the receiver received contributing cargo as an
    agent or mandatary for a principal or mandator, the name of the
    principal or mandator, and the type and quantity of cargo received;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the receiver received contributing cargo from an
    agent or mandatary, the name of the agent or mandatary and the type
    and quantity of cargo received; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the name and address of
    associated persons that have received contributing cargo.

## [PART 3.1]{.HLabel1}[Exports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1291134 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies in respect of contributing oil and non-persistent oil
  that has been exported by sea from a place in Canada or an offshore
  installation in the exclusive economic zone of Canada, in bulk as
  cargo on a ship, and is to be unloaded at a place outside of Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information return

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person who exports, in a calendar year,
  contributing oil in a quantity exceeding 150 000 metric tons or
  non-persistent oil in a quantity exceeding 17 000 metric tons must
  file with the Administrator, no later than February 28 of the
  following calendar year, an information return in the form established
  by the Minister in respect of that oil.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated persons

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (2), the quantity of
  contributing oil or non-persistent oil is the aggregate of the
  quantity exported by the person and the quantities exported by
  associated persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information return must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, mailing address, email address, phone
    number and fax number, if applicable, of the person;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the types of contributing oil and non-persistent
    oil, and the total quantity of each type of contributing oil and
    non-persistent oil exported in that calendar year;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the name and address of
    associated persons that exported contributing oil or non-persistent
    oil;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the person exported non-persistent oil as an
    agent or mandatary for a principal or mandator in Canada, the name
    of the principal or mandator, and the type and quantity of
    non-persistent oil exported; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the person, as a principal or mandatory in
    Canada, exported non-persistent oil through an agent or mandatary,
    the name of the agent or mandatary and the type and quantity of
    non-persistent oil exported.
