## Canada Gazette (1978) Special Issue Regulations {#canada-gazette-1978-special-issue-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/79-613
:::

[STATUTORY INSTRUMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/S-22)

Registration 1979-08-20

Regulations Respecting a Special Issue of Part II of the Canada Gazette
To Be Published in Conjunction with the Consolidated Regulations of
Canada, 1978

P.C. 1979-2183 1979-08-16
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 13(2) and paragraph
27(m) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting a special issue of
Part II of the Canada Gazette to be published in conjunction with the
Consolidated Regulations of Canada, 1978](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-613).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866307 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canada Gazette (1978) Special Issue
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-79-613).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866309 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[special issue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the issue of Part II of the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) to be published in
conjunction with the Consolidated Regulations of Canada, 1978, on the
order of the Clerk of the Privy Council, made pursuant to section 13 of
the [Statutory Instruments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509).

## [Free Delivery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866311 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding subsection
18(2) of the [Statutory Instruments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509), a copy of the special
issue shall be delivered without charge

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the persons for the time being holding the offices
  specified in Schedule I;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the persons for the time being holding the offices
  specified in Schedule II for the use of those persons while holding
  those offices; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on request, to the persons for the time being holding
  the offices or performing the functions specified in Schedule III for
  the use of those persons while holding those offices or performing
  those functions.

## [Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-866316 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 19
of the [Statutory Instruments
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1509), a copy of each volume of
the special issue may be sold to any person on payment of the sum of
\$34.50.
