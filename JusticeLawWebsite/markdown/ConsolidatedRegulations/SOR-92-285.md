## Resident Canadian (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#resident-canadian-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-285
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-05-14

Regulations Prescribing Classes of Persons the Members of which are
Resident Canadians

P.C. 1992-1040  1992-05-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
[[resident Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 and section 463
of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations prescribing classes of persons the members
of which are resident Canadians](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-282), effective
June 1, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943573 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Resident Canadian (Cooperative Credit Associations)
Regulations.

## [Prescribed Resident Canadians]{.HTitleText1} {#h-943575 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph (b) of the definition [[resident
Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Cooperative Credit
Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01), members of the following classes
of persons are resident Canadians:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canadian citizens not ordinarily resident in Canada
  whose principal reason for residing outside Canada is to act as a
  full-time employee of a body corporate

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of which more than 50 per cent of the voting shares
    are beneficially owned, or over which control or direction is
    exercised, by resident Canadians described in paragraph (a) or (c)
    of that definition,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the directors of which are resident
    Canadians described in paragraph (a) or (c) of that definition, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that is a subsidiary of a body corporate
    described in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Canadian citizens not ordinarily resident in Canada
  who are full-time employees of an international association or
  organization of which Canada is a member; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Canadian citizens not ordinarily resident in Canada
  who were, on their sixtieth birthday, ordinarily resident in Canada
  and who have been residing outside Canada for less than 10 consecutive
  years.
