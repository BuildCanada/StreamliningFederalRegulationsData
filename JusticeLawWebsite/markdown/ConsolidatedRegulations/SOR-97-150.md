## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003932 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).
    ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Auyuittuq Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Auyuittuq Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2004-299,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[diversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diversion]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a forced or precautionary
    landing to address or avert an imminent or potential threat to the
    safety of a pilot, a passenger or a person on the ground if the
    pilot cannot return safely to the take-off point and if continuing
    to the planned destination is not feasible, including a landing
    because of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a medical emergency of the pilot or a passenger;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a mechanical or fuel problem that renders
      continued operation of the aircraft unsafe;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the minimum visual meteorological conditions for
      VFR flight, as set out in Part VI, Subpart 2, Division VI of the
      [Canadian Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433), not
      being met; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the destination airstrip becoming unusable while
      the flight is in progress. ([déroutement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Ellesmere Island Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Ellesmere Island Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2004-299, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[Kluane Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Kluane Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2004-299,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[Nahanni Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Nahanni Reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2004-299,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft access permit
    issued pursuant to subsection 3(1). ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

- SOR/2013-10, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003947 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
to parks and, subject to sections 40 and 41 of the [Canada National
Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01), to park reserves as if they were parks.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 3
:::

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do
not apply in respect of non-motorized aircraft that are incapable of
transporting persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 3
:::

**[[1.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do
not apply in respect of non-motorized paragliders or hang-gliders.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-10, s. 2
:::

## [Aircraft Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003957 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 5, it is prohibited for a person to conduct a take-off or
  landing of an aircraft in a park, other than in a park set out in
  column I of the schedule at a take-off and landing location set out in
  column II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to conduct a take-off
  or landing of an aircraft in a park set out in any of items 1 to 9, 11
  or 12, column I, of the schedule unless that person is the holder of a
  permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to conduct a take-off
  or landing of an aircraft in the park set out in item 13, column I, of
  the schedule, other than

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for non-commercial recreational purposes if the
    person is the holder of a permit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to land in the case of a diversion or other
    emergency situation.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to conduct a take-off
  or landing of an aircraft in the park set out in item 14, column I, of
  the schedule, other than to land in the case of a diversion or other
  emergency situation.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a landing referred to in paragraph
  (3)(b) or subsection (4), the person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the superintendent as soon as feasible after
    landing of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fact that they have landed at a take-off and
      landing location set out in item 13 or 14, column II, of the
      schedule, as the case may be, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the diversion or other emergency
      situation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} obtain the superintendent's authorization before
    take-off.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 4
- SOR/2013-10, s. 3

## [Aircraft Access Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003972 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), the superintendent may issue an aircraft access
  permit to any person who applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application referred to in subsection (1) shall
  be made in the form determined by the superintendent and shall contain
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the owner
    of the aircraft that the applicant proposes to use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of a person,
    other than the applicant, to be contacted in an emergency;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the make, model and registration number of the
    aircraft that the applicant proposes to use;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date or dates or period of time for
    take-off or landing, or both; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the location or locations that the applicant
    proposes to use for take-off or landing, or both.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An operator of a commercial air service that applies
  for a permit shall submit with the application a copy of the licence
  issued to the operator pursuant to section 4.1 or subsection 10.1(2)
  of the [National Parks of Canada Businesses
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-455) that authorizes the operator
  to operate the service in the park in question.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall, before issuing a permit,
  take into account

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the natural and cultural resources of the park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety, health and enjoyment of visitors or
    residents of the park; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the preservation, control and management of the
    park.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall set out in a permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    permit holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the owner
    of the aircraft to be used; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of a person,
    other than the permit holder, to be contacted in an emergency.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent shall set out in a permit terms
  and conditions that specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the make, model and registration number of the
    aircraft to be used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date or dates or period of time authorized by
    the superintendent for take-off or landing, or both;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the location or locations authorized by the
    superintendent for take-off or landing, or both; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that is necessary for the
    preservation, control and management of the park in which the
    landing or take-off is authorized.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} \[Repealed, SOR/2004-299, s. 5\]

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, ss. 5, 9(F)
- SOR/2009-322, s. 21(F)

## [Powers of the Superintendent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003999 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} suspend a permit, where the permit holder fails to
  comply with these Regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reinstate a suspended permit, where the failure that
  gave rise to the suspension has been remedied; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} refuse to issue a permit, for a period not exceeding
  one year, to a person convicted of a contravention of these
  Regulations or the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 9(F)
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent may
authorize the use of a take-off and landing location in a park set out
in any of items 1, 3 to 7 or 10, column I, of the schedule, other than a
location set out in column II, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no other reasonable means of access to that
  area of the park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} such use will not result in any significant adverse
  effects to the ecological integrity or public safety of that area of
  the park; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} such use will not impair the enjoyment of that area
  of the park by other persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 6
- SOR/2013-10, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything in
these Regulations, the superintendent may authorize the take-off and
landing of an aircraft anywhere in a park for the purposes of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} natural or cultural resource management and
  protection directly related to the administration of the park;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other management or control function directly
  related to the administration of the park;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} public safety; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} law enforcement.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-299, s. 7(F)
:::
