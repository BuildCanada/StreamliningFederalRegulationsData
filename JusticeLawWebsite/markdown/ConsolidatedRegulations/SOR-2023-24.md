## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Definitions, Application and Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391869 .Part}

### [Definitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391870 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Copyright
    Act](/eng/acts/C-42). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chair]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chair of the Board appointed
    under subsection 66(1) of the Act. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[intervener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person granted leave by
    the Board to intervene in a proceeding before the
    Board. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member appointed under
    subsection 66(1) of the Act. ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[objector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[objector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that files an
    objection to a proposed tariff. ([opposant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to a matter in
    respect of a proposed tariff filed under section 67 or 83 of the
    Act, the collective society that filed the proposed tariff, an
    objector or an intervener and, in relation to a proceeding in
    respect of an application made under subsection 71(1) of the Act, a
    collective society or user referred to in that subsection or an
    interverner. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391879 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Rules apply to any
matter in relation to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a proposed tariff filed by a collective society with
  respect to rights under section 3, 15, 18, 19 or 21 of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a proposed tariff filed by a collective society with
  respect to royalties referred to in subsections 29.7(2) and (3) and
  paragraph 31(2)(d) of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a proposed tariff filed by a collective society with
  respect to levies referred to in section 82 of the Act; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an application to fix royalty rates or any related
  terms or conditions in individual cases under subsection 71(1) of the
  Act.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391886 .Subheading}

#### [Liberal Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1391887 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying, supplementing or dispensing with
rules

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** As the circumstances and
considerations of fairness permit, the Board may vary or supplement
these Rules, or dispense with compliance with these Rules in whole or in
part, including in order to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal with matters informally and expeditiously;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} allow for meaningful participation of persons with an
  interest in a matter before the Board; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} deal with special circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, on its own
initiative or on the request of a party or interested person, issue
directions in respect of any procedural issue not explicitly addressed
by these Rules or in respect of any uncertainty as to the application of
these Rules.

#### [Calculating Time Limits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1391895 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Days not counted

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The days included in the
period beginning on December 21 in one year and ending on January 7 in
the following year do not count in the calculation of a time limit set
out in these Rules or in an order or a direction of the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[business
day]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules, [[business
day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a Saturday, a holiday
or a day that falls in the period referred to in rule 5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a time limit set out in
these Rules or an order or direction of the Board falls on a day other
than a business day, the time limit is extended to the next business
day.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391902 .Part}

### [Defect in form]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391903 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No document or proceeding
under these Rules is invalid by reason solely of a defect in form or
technical irregularity.

### [Non-compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391906 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a party does not comply
with these Rules, an order or direction of the Board, the Board may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} stay the proceeding until it is satisfied that the
  party has complied with the Rules, the order or the direction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} decide the matter only on the basis of the
  information on the record; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take any measure that it considers fair in the
  circumstances.

### [Quorum]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391912 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quorum

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** In all proceedings before
the Board, the quorum is one member.

### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391915 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of filing

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided
by these Rules, a document to be filed with the Board must be filed by
the electronic means specified by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of filing

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The date of filing of a
document with the Board is the date on which the Board receives the
document. A document filed after 17:00 Ottawa local time is deemed to
have been filed on the next business day.

### [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391920 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of service

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document is effected by
  sending an email that includes the document to the person being served
  or the person's authorized representative. If it cannot be effected by
  email, service may be effected by personal service, by mail to the
  address for service described in subrule (2) or by any other means
  that the Board authorizes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Address for service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The address for service is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the most recent address used by the person or the
    person's authorized representative in their written communication
    with the Board with respect to a proposed tariff or an application
    made under subsection 71(1) of the Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of the address referred to in
    paragraph (a), the most recent address that was used by the Board to
    send any notice or that appears in any notice issued by the Board
    with respect to the proposed tariff or the application made under
    subsection 71(1) of the Act or if no such address has been used or
    appears, the last known address of the person or the person's
    authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date and time of service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document is served on the date on which and at the
  time at which the person being served or the person's authorized
  representative receives the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by email

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of evidence to the contrary, a
  document served by email is presumed to have been received on the date
  and at the time that the email was sent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt after 17:00

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A document received or presumed to have been received
  after 17:00 Ottawa local time is deemed to have been served on the
  next business day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a person serves a document, the person must, at
  the Board's request, file a proof of service of the document with the
  Board, in the form and manner specified by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service --- email

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Absent evidence to the contrary, the email referred
  to in subsection (1) constitutes proof of service of a document.

### [Language]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391938 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents and proceedings

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections 68.1(1) and
  83(3) of the Act, documents used in a proceeding before the Board must
  be in the official language or languages of the parties' choice and
  proceedings must be conducted in the official language or languages of
  the parties' choice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Translation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document written in a language that is other than
  English or French may be filed and served only if it is accompanied by
  a translation of that document into English or French and an affidavit
  attesting to the accuracy of the translation.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Filing of Proposed Tariff]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391944 .Part}

### [Notice of Grounds for Proposed Tariff]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391945 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of notice of grounds

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A collective society that
files a proposed tariff must file with the Board a notice of grounds for
the proposed tariff within seven days after the day on which the
proposed tariff is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The notice of grounds for
the proposed tariff must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} describe the uses covered by the proposed tariff;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the basis for the proposed royalty or levy
  rates; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} include any other information required by the Board.

### [Other Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391953 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of certain documents

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  collective society that files a proposed tariff that covers the same
  or substantially the same uses as those in the last approved tariff
  or, if there is no approved tariff, in the last proposed tariff must
  file with the Board a comparative document that indicates the
  differences between the two tariffs, at the same time that it files a
  notice of grounds for the proposed tariff under rule 15.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must, in the manner that it sees fit,
  publish the notice of grounds for the proposed tariff and the
  comparative document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A collective society may, at the same that it files
  the notice of grounds for a proposed tariff, file additional
  information for the Board's consideration of the proposed tariff.

### [Notice of Grounds for Objection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391961 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of notice of grounds for objection

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A person that objects to
a proposed tariff must file an objection with the Board in accordance
with subsection 68.3(2) of the Act together with a notice of grounds for
objection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice of grounds for
objection

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The notice of grounds for
objection must, as applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} set out the grounds for why the Board should not
  approve the proposed tariff despite any alteration of royalties or
  levies or fixation of terms or conditions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the grounds for objection to the proposed
  tariff; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} include any other information required by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** At the same time a person
files a notice of grounds for objection, the person may also file
additional information for the Board's consideration of the proposed
tariff and of alterations to the royalty rates and to the related terms
and conditions or of the new related terms and conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of reply

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A collective society that
intends to file a reply to an objection under subsection 68.4(1) or
83(7) of the Act must do so within 14 days after the day on which it
receives the notice of grounds for objection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sufficient information

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If the Board concludes
that it has sufficient information to decide a matter before it, the
Board may after giving notice to the collective society and the
objector, decide the matter only on the information provided in the
notice of grounds for the proposed tariff, any notice of grounds for
objection and any reply to an objection and on any additional
information filed under subrule 17(3) or rule 20.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Conduct of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1391975 .Part}

### [Commencement of Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391976 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The Board must, in the
manner it sees fit, publish a notice when it commences a proceeding.

### [Statement of Issues To Be Considered]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391979 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint statement of issues to be
considered

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to an order of the Board or
  case manager, if the Board has commenced a proceeding with respect to
  a proposed tariff and an objection to the proposed tariff has been
  filed, the parties to that proceeding other than an intervener, must
  jointly file with the Board a joint statement of issues to be
  considered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The joint statement of issues to be considered
  referred to in subrule (1) must be filed within 90 days after the day
  on which the proceeding is commenced or within the time limit
  specified by the Board or case manager.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of statement

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The statement of issues
to be considered must contain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the issues to be considered;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a proposal to consolidate the
  proceeding with one or more other proceedings with respect to other
  proposed tariffs filed with the Board or with applications to fix
  royalty rates under subsection 71(1) of the Act, or with both; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lack of agreement

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If the parties are unable
to agree on a joint statement of issues to be considered, each party to
the proceeding, other than an intervener, must file with the Board and
serve on each other party, within the time limit referred to in rule 24,
a separate statement of issues to be considered that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the issues to be considered and the
  party's position regarding each of those issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a proposal to consolidate the
  proceeding with one or more other proceedings with respect to other
  proposed tariffs filed with the Board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration of other Issues

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Board may consider
relevant issues other than those identified by the parties in the
statement of issues to be considered.

### [Case Management]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1391997 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[case
manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of rules
23, 24, 29 to 35 and 53, [[case manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
person assigned by the Chair under subsection 66.504(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of case manager

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  relation to a matter or any step in a matter, the case manager may
  give a direction or make an order that adapts, restricts or excludes
  the application of any provision of these Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation in decision on merits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assignment of a member as the case manager does
  not prevent that member from participating in the decision on the
  merits of the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case management conference

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  case manager may, during the course of a matter, conduct one or more
  case management conferences.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Topics to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During a case management conference, any of the
  following topics may be considered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clarification, simplification and elimination
    of issues to be considered;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} opportunities for an agreement to be concluded on
    any of the issues to be considered;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the issue of whether a question of law should be
    determined by the Board;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the official language the parties will use during
    the proceeding;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issue of whether interrogatories should take
    place and their parameters as applicable;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the filing of evidence;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any questions of confidentiality;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the parameters of any expert testimony, including
    the possibility of experts meeting before or after a hearing to
    answer questions posed by the Board;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a proceeding schedule and the expected duration of
    the hearing;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the documents to be filed with the Board before a
    hearing, including legal briefs and case records;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the information and documents to be submitted to
    the parties at a hearing;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for subsequent case management
    conferences;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} other issues to be resolved.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case management order

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** After a case management
conference, the case manager may make an order relating to any issue
considered at the case management conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for variation of order

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may submit a request to the case manager to vary a case
  management order made under rule 31. The request must be filed with
  the Board and served on each other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request to vary must indicate the reason why the
  request is being made and must include a statement indicating whether
  the other parties have consented to the variation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation of the order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The case manager may vary the order if there are
  compelling reasons to do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Joint request

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  collective society and one or more objectors jointly submit a set of
  royalty rates and related terms and conditions to the Board and
  jointly request that the Board approve a proposed tariff based on
  those royalty rates and terms and conditions, they must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file a submission in support of their request;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file all agreements concluded between the parties
    that relate to the same uses or substantially the same uses as those
    covered by the proposed tariff;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identify each proposed tariff, or a portion of the
    proposed tariff, that is to be approved on the basis of the request;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide any other information required by the
    Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receiving the request, the Board or the case
  manager may, if it or they consider it appropriate to do so, establish
  any procedural steps in the matter or make modifications to any
  schedule of proceeding.

### [Interrogatories]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392041 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interrogatories

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The Board or the case
manager may make an order to permit interrogatories and to specify their
number, type, scope and form, the time limits for their completion and
the person to whom they may be addressed.

### [Case Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392044 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization of filing

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board or the case manager may authorize or require a person to file a
  case record, a response to a case record or a reply to a response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party files a case record, a response to a case
  record, or a reply to a response with the Board, they must serve it on
  each other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of case record or response

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The case record or response to a case record must
  contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement of case setting out the party's
    position and how they intend to support it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the documentary exhibits on which the party intends
    to rely, including any expert reports and sworn statements or solemn
    declarations of lay witnesses, if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of the documentary exhibits, if applicable;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of witnesses the party intends to call and
    an indication of the time required for the examination of each
    witness, if applicable; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of reply

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A reply to a response to a case record must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the documentary exhibits on which the party intends
    to rely, including any expert reports and sworn statements or solemn
    declarations of lay witnesses, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of the documentary exhibits, if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a list of witnesses the party intends to call and
    an indication of the time required for the examination of each
    witness, if applicable; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other information required by the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing in an oral hearing

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise ordered
by the Board or the case manager, in the case of an oral hearing, a
collective society must file a case record and an objector must file a
response to the case record.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing and service

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  case record, any response to the case record and any reply to the
  response must be filed and served within the time limits set by the
  Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paper copies

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Board orders otherwise, in addition to the
  electronic filing referred to in rule 11, two paper copies of any case
  record, response to the case record and reply to the response must be
  filed with the Board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal brief

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, in response to a request of a party or on its own
  initiative and on terms that it considers appropriate, allow a party
  to file a legal brief and allow other parties to file a response to
  that legal brief.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The legal brief and any response to the legal brief
  must be served on each other party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendments

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The Board may grant leave
to amend a statement of issues to be considered, case record, response
to a case record, reply to a response to a case record, legal brief or
response to a legal brief on the terms the Board considers appropriate.

### [Written or Oral Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392077 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written or oral hearing

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** The Board may hold a
written or oral hearing in respect of any matter before it.

### [Oral Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392080 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simultaneous interpretation services

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** A party that requires
simultaneous interpretation services must submit a request to the Board
at least 30 days before the date of the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral hearing open to public

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the Board orders otherwise, oral hearings of the Board on the merits
  of a matter are open to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may submit a request to the Board for all or
  a portion of an oral hearing not to be open to the public. The Board
  may make any order that it considers appropriate in the circumstances.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents received during oral hearing

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** A document that is not
part of a case record, a response to a case record or a reply to a
response to a case record must not be received as evidence at an oral
hearing unless it is to be used only for the purposes of
cross-examination of a witness or the Board permits it to be received as
evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transcript

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The Board may cause a
transcript, recording or other record of an oral hearing on the merits
of a matter to be prepared.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392092 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplemental information

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, at any
time in a proceeding, require a party to provide it with information or
documents to supplement the record.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality order

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time in a proceeding, parties may file a joint request for a
  confidentiality order with the Board. The request must specify the
  manner in which the information to be designated as confidential or
  highly confidential is to be treated and must be accompanied by a
  proposed confidentiality order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents filed before commencement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may file confidential or highly confidential
  information before the commencement of a proceeding only with leave of
  the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disagreement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the absence of an agreement among the parties, a
  party may file its own request for a confidentiality order with the
  Board, together with a proposed confidentiality order and a statement
  indicating the reasons why the parties could not agree.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Once a confidentiality order has been made, a party
  may designate the information that it filed with the Board in the
  proceeding as confidential or highly confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Warranted designation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, in response to a request from a party
  or, after giving the parties notice, on its own initiative, determine
  whether the designation is warranted and make any order that it
  considers appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public record

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Any document that is filed with the Board in relation
  to a matter and any related proceeding is placed on the public record,
  unless the document has been designated as confidential or highly
  confidential.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** The Board may order that
a party request leave before designating any information as confidential
or highly confidential as well as provide an explanation that justifies
the designation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert witness report

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  party who, in a proceeding before the Board, intends to introduce
  evidence given by an expert witness must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} file with the Board and serve on each other party
    an expert witness report that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} contains a summary of the expert's report,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} describes the qualifications of the expert with
      respect to the issues addressed in the report,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} contains the expert's curriculum vitae,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} sets out the issues addressed in the report,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} sets out the expert's opinion respecting each
      issue addressed in the report,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a report provided in response to
      another expert's report, sets out the points of agreement and
      disagreement with the other expert's opinions,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} sets out the grounds supporting each expressed
      opinion,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} describes the facts and assumptions on which
      the opinions in the report are based,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} includes any literature, documents and data
      specifically relied on in support of the opinions expressed,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} sets out a complete description of the
      methodology on which the expert has relied, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} sets out any caveats or qualifications necessary
      to render the report complete and accurate, including those
      relating to any insufficiency of data or research and any issues
      that fall outside the expert's field of expertise;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file with the Board and serve on each other party
    any form that is specified by the Board in relation to the expert
    witness report; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the expert witness is available for
    examination and cross-examination at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal of Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may refuse to consider expert witness
  evidence if that evidence addresses issues not mentioned in any
  document filed under subrule 35(1) or rule 36.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of independent experts

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, at any time, by order, appoint one or more independent
  experts to inquire into and report on any question of fact or give
  their opinion relevant to an issue in a proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report of an independent expert is to be placed
  on the public record of the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any party may make submissions to the Board with
  respect to the terms of the appointment of an independent expert and
  may file a written response to the independent expert's report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, in
response to a request from a party or on its own initiative, issue a
subpoena for the attendance of a witness at the hearing or for the
production of documents.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Parties and Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392138 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Severance and Consolidation

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** At any time the Board
may, on its own initiative or at the request of any party, order that
any proceeding be severed or, that any two or more proceedings be
consolidated, heard together, heard consecutively or severed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for leave to intervene

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person with an interest in a proceeding that is before the Board and
  that is in respect of a proposed tariff or an application under
  subsection 71(1) of the Act, may make a request to the Board for leave
  to intervene.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request for leave to intervene must be filed with
  the Board as soon as feasible after the commencement of a proceeding
  and served on each other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The request for leave to intervene must be in writing
  and must set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the requester and the
    requester's authorized representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of why the intervention is necessary
    and the interest of the requester in the matter;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts on which the
    request is based;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issues that the requester intends to address;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of how the requester wishes to
    participate in the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any party may make submissions with respect to the
  request for leave to intervene by filing them with the Board and
  serving a copy of them on the requester within 15 days after the day
  on which the served request to intervene is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors considered by the Board

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether to grant leave to intervene,
  the Board must consider

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the requester has an interest in the
    proceeding that is sufficient to warrant the intervention;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the requester will present information or
    submissions that are useful and different;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the intervention will prejudice any party
    to the proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the intervention will interfere with the
    fair and expeditious conduct of the proceeding; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other factor that the Board considers
    appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of the Board

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Board may grant or deny the request for leave to
  intervene and impose any condition or restriction that it considers
  appropriate, including a restriction on the scope of the intervention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to parties

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Board must notify the parties to the proceeding
  of its decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any confidentiality order and any
  restrictions imposed by the Board under subrule (6), each party must
  serve on the intervener or its authorized representative any document
  that is filed by a party after the day on which the parties are
  notified of the Board's decision to grant the request for leave to
  intervene.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Letter of comment

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  interested person that does not intend to request leave to intervene
  but wishes to make comments on the proceeding may file with the Board
  a letter of comment before

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day fixed by the Board or the case manager as
    the final day on which any party may present their written or oral
    submission; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other day specified by the Board or case
    manager.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of letter

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The letter of comment must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comments on the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the person's
    interest in the proceeding; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any relevant information that will explain or
    support the person's comments.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies to parties and on public record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board must provide all parties with a copy of any
  letter of comment and must place a copy of the letter on its public
  record of the proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A party may, within 15 days after the day on which
  the party receives a letter of comment, file a response with the Board
  and serve a copy of that response on all parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consideration by the Board

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A letter of comment that is filed with the Board and
  any response to that letter must be considered by the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Status

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person that files a letter of comment with the
  Board does not become a party to the proceeding.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Transitional Provisions and Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1392186 .Part}

### [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392187 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceedings commenced

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Rules apply in respect of all proceedings before the Board whether
  commenced before or after these Rules come into force, except in
  respect of steps already taken before the coming into force of these
  Rules.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directive of the Board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any proceeding commenced or document filed in
  accordance with a directive of the Board before the coming into force
  of these Rules is not invalid merely because the proceeding or
  document does not conform to these Rules.

### [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1392193 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
