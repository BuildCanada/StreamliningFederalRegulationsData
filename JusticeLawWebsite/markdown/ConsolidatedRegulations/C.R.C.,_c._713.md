## Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 713
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Under Section 13 of the Financial Administration Act Authorizing
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to Prescribe
Certain Fees
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560984 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development Authority to
Prescribe Fees Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._713).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560986 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development is authorized to prescribe by order the
fees to be paid by persons provided with microfilm copies of geological,
geophysical and well-history data submitted pursuant to the [Canada Oil
and Gas Land Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1518).
