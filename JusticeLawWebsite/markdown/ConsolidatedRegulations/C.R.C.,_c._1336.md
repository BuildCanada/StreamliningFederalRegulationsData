## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503863 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The portions of the federal
public administration listed in the schedule are designated as parts of
the public service, and the employees in those portions of the federal
public administration who are not otherwise employed in the public
service are deemed, for the purposes of subsection 35(2) of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to be employed in the public
service.
