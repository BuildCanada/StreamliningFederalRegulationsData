## Alberta Hog Order {#alberta-hog-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 130
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Hogs Produced in Alberta
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502791 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Alberta Hog Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._130).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502793 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Marketing of Agricultural
    Products Act of Alberta; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Pork
    Producers Development Corporation established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Alberta Agricultural
    Products Marketing Council established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hog produced within the Province
    of Alberta and sold or offered for sale for slaughter;
    ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    hogs, established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act,
    and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect to the
    Plan. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-872, s. 1
- SOR/97-423, s. 1
:::

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502802 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Council and the
Commodity Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of hogs in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Alberta,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of hogs locally within that Province under
the Act and the Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502804 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing levies or charges and imposing them upon and
collecting them from persons described in section 3 who are engaged in
the marketing of hogs, and for such purposes, classifying such persons
into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the members of
the different groups in different amounts; and the Commodity Board may
use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation of
reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or
disposal of hogs and the equalization or adjustment among producers of
hogs of moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or
periods of time as it may determine.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/80-90
