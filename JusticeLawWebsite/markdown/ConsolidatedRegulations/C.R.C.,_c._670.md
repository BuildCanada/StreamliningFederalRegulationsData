## Accountable Travel and Moving Advance Regulations (Canadian Forces) {#accountable-travel-and-moving-advance-regulations-canadian-forces .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 670
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Regulations Respecting the Making of Accountable Advances of Public
Funds to Officers and Men of the Canadian Forces for Travel and Moving
Expenses
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560241 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Accountable Travel and Moving Advance Regulations
(Canadian Forces)](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._670).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560243 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
officers and men of the Canadian Forces.

## [Authorization of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560245 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Chief of the Defence
Staff may authorize the making of accountable advances of public funds
to officers and men of the Canadian Forces who are required to travel on
duty or move from one location to another, or who are authorized to move
their dependants or furniture and effects, or both, as applicable, from
one location to another, within existing regulations.

## [Limitations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560247 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Accountable advances made
under these Regulations shall be limited to amounts sufficient to meet
estimated permissible expenses.

## [Settlement of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-560249 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsections
31(2), (3) and (4) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), the Chief of the Defence Staff may issue such
orders as are required to provide for the accountability and recovery of
advances issued pursuant to these Regulations.
