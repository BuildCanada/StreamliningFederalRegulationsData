## Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and Promotion Levies Order {#beef-cattle-research-market-development-and-promotion-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-236
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Registration 2016-08-17

Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and Promotion Levies Order
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council has, by the [Canadian Beef Cattle
Research, Market Development and Promotion Agency
Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-48)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGAKA}, established the Canadian
Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and Promotion Agency pursuant
to subsection 39(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EEAKA} of the [Farm Products
Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EBAKA};

Whereas that Agency has been empowered to implement a promotion and
research plan pursuant to that Proclamation;

Whereas the proposed [Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and
Promotion Levies Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-236) is an order of a
class to which paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d){#fn_d-ID0EGAIA} of that Act applies by
reason of section 2 of the [Agencies' Orders and Regulations Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._648)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_e){#fn_e-ID0EDAIA}, and has been submitted to
the National Farm Products Council pursuant to paragraph
42(1)(d)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBAIA} of that
Act;

And whereas, pursuant to paragraph 7(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_d){#fn_d-ID0EBAHA} of that Act, the National
Farm Products Council has approved the proposed Order after being
satisfied that it is necessary for the implementation of the promotion
and research plan that that Agency is authorized to implement;

Therefore, the Canadian Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and
Promotion Agency, pursuant to paragraphs 42(1)(d)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0ELAGA} and (e)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EJAGA} of the [Farm Products
Agencies Act](/eng/acts/F-4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_c){#fn_c-ID0EGAGA} and section 10 of the
schedule to the [Canadian Beef Cattle Research, Market Development and
Promotion Agency Proclamation](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-48)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDAGA}, makes the annexed [Beef
Cattle Research, Market Development and Promotion Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-236).

Calgary, August 9, 2016

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834183 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[beef breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beef breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any breed of beef cattle of
    the species [Bos taurus]{.otherLang lang="la"} or [Bos
    indicus]{.otherLang lang="la"} that is not a dairy breed. ([race de
    boucherie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bob calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bob calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any dairy breed or
    dairy-cross calf that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Ontario and has a live
      weight of 68 kg or less; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Quebec and has a live
      weight of 109 kg or less. ([jeune veau]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[cull cow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cull cow]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any beef breed, dairy breed
    or dairy-cross cow, heifer or bull that has been culled and is
    marketed for slaughter. ([vache de réforme]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dairy breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dairy breed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following
    breeds of cattle: Holstein, Ayrshire, Jersey, Brown Swiss, Milking
    Shorthorn, Guernsey and Canadienne. ([race
    laitière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dairy-cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dairy-cross]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to cattle,
    describes any cattle that result from mating cattle from a dairy
    breed with cattle from a beef breed. ([croisement
    laitier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who acts as an agent
    in the buying or selling of beef cattle and includes auctioneers and
    auction markets. ([négociant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fed cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fed cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any finished beef breed,
    dairy breed or dairy-cross cattle that have been fed for the purpose
    of meat production and are marketed for slaughter. ([bovin
    d'abattage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fed veal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fed veal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any head of dairy breed or
    dairy-cross cattle fed primarily on milk or grain that is marketed
    for slaughter and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Ontario and has a live
      weight of more than 204.1 kg but no more than 348.8 kg; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Quebec and has a live
      weight of more than 109 kg but no more than 348.8 kg. ([veau
      d'abattage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[feeder calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feeder calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2025-162, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[feeder cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[feeder cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any dairy breed, beef
    breed or dairy-cross cattle that are marketed for finishing before
    slaughter but are not bob calves or started calves. ([bovin
    d'embouche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grain-fed calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grain-fed calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2024-142, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[imported beef product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported beef product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a beef product
    that is imported into Canada and classified under one or more of the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) tariff item numbers set out in
    Schedule 2. ([produit de boeuf importé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who imports beef
    cattle or beef products into Canada. ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[milk-fed calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[milk-fed calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2024-142, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[started calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[started calf]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any dairy breed or
    dairy-cross calf that is marketed for finishing before slaughter and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Ontario, is intact and
      has a live weight of more than 68 kg but no more than 204.1 kg; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is sold by a resident of Quebec and has a live
      weight of more than 109 kg but no more than 348.8 kg. ([veau de
      démarrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 7 to 9 and subsection 11(3), if a dealer is involved in the
purchase or sale of beef cattle, the reference to "purchaser" must be
read as a reference to "dealer". If more than one dealer is involved in
the purchase or sale, the reference is to the dealer who first receives
payment of the purchase price.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of levies

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** All levies must be paid to
the Agency through a collector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deduction of levy by purchaser

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The purchaser must deduct
the levy payable from the purchase price and remit it to the collector
for the purchaser's province of residence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The purchaser must, at the
time of the sale, provide the seller with a document setting out the
purchase price and the amount of the levy deducted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance and information

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The purchaser must remit
the levy to the collector within the period established by the laws of
the purchaser's province of residence for remitting levies payable on
sales of beef cattle, together with a statement in a form established by
the Agency that sets out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purchaser's name, mailing address, telephone
  number and, if available, email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of head of beef cattle purchased, broken
  down by the seller's province of residence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of levies deducted and remitted, broken
  down by the seller's province of residence and by the type of beef
  cattle, as set out in the table to section 4; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} when required to be provided under the laws of the
  purchaser's province of residence, the name, mailing address,
  telephone number and, if available, email address of each person who
  sold beef cattle to them if a levy was deducted from the purchase
  price of those cattle during the reporting period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every seller, purchaser
and dealer who is subject to this Order must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep all records relating to the sale -- including
  the records necessary to verify any of the statements provided to the
  Agency and the amounts of levies paid -- for a period of seven years
  after the date on which the beef cattle were sold; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make those records available for examination by the
  Agency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment by seller not required

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  levy is deducted under this Order from the purchase price, the seller
  is not required to pay the levy to the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the request of the Agency, the seller must prove
  that the levy payable under this Order has been deducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to deduct levy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a purchaser fails to deduct the levy as required
  by section 7, the seller must remit the levy payable in respect of the
  sale to the Agency within 90 days after the date of the sale.

## [Import]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834261 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importer's levy

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  importer must pay to the Agency, for each head of beef cattle imported
  or each imported beef product, a levy in the amount set out in
  Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date and method of payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The levy must be paid by negotiable instrument in
  Canadian dollars within 15 calendar days after the date indicated on
  the invoice sent by the Agency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The importer must include with payment of the levy a
  statement that sets out the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer's name, mailing address, telephone
    number and, if available, email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of head of beef cattle imported, broken
    down by delivery destination;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of kilograms of imported beef products,
    broken down by [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) tariff item
    number, as set out in Schedule 2;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levy paid to the Agency, broken
    down between beef cattle and imported beef products; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the levy was paid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refund

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Agency must refund a
levy paid by an importer if the importer proves to the satisfaction of
the Agency that the levy was not required to be paid under subsection
12(1) or that the levy paid was more than \$1.00 per head of beef cattle
or per carcass equivalent of imported beef products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every importer who is
subject to this Order must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep all records relating to the importation --
  including the records necessary to verify any of the statements
  provided to the Agency and the amounts of levies paid -- for a period
  of seven years after the date on which the beef cattle or beef
  products were imported; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make those records available for examination by the
  Agency.
