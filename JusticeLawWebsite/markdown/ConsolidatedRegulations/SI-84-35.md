## Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-consumer-and-corporate-affairs-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/84-35
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1984-02-22

Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees
Order

P.C. 1984-324 1984-02-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of
Consumer and Corporate Affairs to prescribe by order the fees to be paid
for Contractor and Homeowners Registration Courses under the Urea
Formaldehyde Insulation Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EBBAA}.
