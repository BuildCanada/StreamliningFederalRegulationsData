## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919122 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[area of transmission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a terrestrial television station

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for an analog signal, the area within the predicted
    Grade B contour of the station, as determined in accordance with the
    method set out in the schedule, and the area within a radius of
    32 km from that contour, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for a digital signal, the area within the
    noise-limited bounding contour of the station, as determined in
    accordance with the document entitled BPR-10 --- Application
    Procedures and Rules for Digital Television (DTV) Undertakings,
    Issue 1, published by the Department of Industry in August 2010, and
    the area within a radius of 32 km from that contour;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a terrestrial F.M. radio station, the
  area within the predicted 0.5 mV/m field strength contour of the
  station, as determined in accordance with the method set out in the
  schedule; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a terrestrial A.M. radio station, the
  area within a radius of 32 km from the principal studio of the
  station.

## [Local Signal and Distant Signal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-919131 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 31(2) of the [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42),
  [[local signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the entire service area of a cable
    retransmitter, the signal of a terrestrial radio or television
    station the area of transmission of which covers all of that area;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a portion of the service area of a
    retransmitter, the signal of a terrestrial radio or television
    station the area of transmission of which covers all of that
    portion.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 31(2) of the
  [Copyright Act](/eng/acts/C-42), [[distant
  signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a signal that is not a local
  signal.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 1)]{.OriginatingRef}[Method for Determining the Predicted 0.5 Millivolt Per Metre (mV/m) Contour of Terrestrial F.M. Radio Stations and the Predicted Grade B Contour of Terrestrial Television Stations]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-919141 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Height of Antenna Above Average Terrain (Haat)]{.HTitleText1} {#height-of-antenna-above-average-terrain-haat .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** The height of an antenna above average terrain shall be
  determined on a topographical map by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} marking the transmitting site on the map, using the
    geographical coordinates of the site;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} drawing two concentric circles with radii of 3 km
    and 16 km, respectively, from the transmitting site marked under
    paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} starting at true north, drawing eight radials from
    the transmitting site at intervals of 45°;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} drawing, for each radial, a profile graph for the
    segment of terrain between 3 km and 16 km from the transmitting
    site, with the eight profile graphs plotted separately on
    rectangular coordinate paper, the distance in kilometres as the
    abscissa and the elevation in metres above mean sea level as the
    ordinate and reflecting the topography of the terrain;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} obtaining the average elevation of the terrain
    above mean sea level for each segment of terrain between 3 km and
    16 km from the transmitting site by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} using a planimeter,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} dividing the segment in equal sectors and
      averaging their respective median elevations, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} averaging the elevations at a sufficient number
      of equally spaced points to provide a representation of the
      terrain; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} obtaining the HAAT for each radial by subtracting
    the average terrain elevation calculated in accordance with
    paragraph (e) from the height above sea level of the centre of
    radiation of the antenna.

### [Predicted Contours]{.HTitleText1} {#predicted-contours .SchedHeadL1}

- - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For F.M. radio stations, the predicted
    contour is defined by a field strength of 0.5 mV/m.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} For television stations, depending on the channel
    involved, the predicted Grade B contour is defined by the following
    field strength:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 47 dB above 1 µV/m for channels 2 to 6;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 56 dB above 1 µV/m for channels 7 to 13;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 64 dB above 1 µV/m for channels 14 to 69.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The HAAT determined in accordance with section 1
    shall be ascertained for each radial and the effective radiated
    power (ERP) shall be ascertained in the plane of maximum radiation
    (in the case of directional antennas, the ERP value in the direction
    of each radial shall be used).

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate F(50,50) propagation curves
    (Tables I to III) shall be used with the HAAT and the ERP
    ascertained in accordance with subsection (3) to determine the
    distance from the transmitting site to the contour point on each
    radial.

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} The contour points determined under subsection (4)
    shall be joined by a smooth curve to obtain the contour.

    - ::::: listItemBlock0
      ::: listItemLabel
      [Note:]{.lawlabel} 
      :::

      ::: listItemText3
      The 40 dB line is the reference line for an effective radiated
      power (ERP) of 1 kW.
      :::
      :::::
