## Amalgamations and Windings-Up Continuation (GST/HST) Regulations {#amalgamations-and-windings-up-continuation-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-33
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Regulations Prescribing Purposes for Which New Successor Corporations
Shall Be Deemed to Be the Same Corporations as and Continuations of
Predecessor Amalgamated or Wound-Up Corporations

P.C. 1990-2742  1990-12-18
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR_91-33_EN_hq_5507){#fn_SOR_91-33_EN_hq_5507-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations prescribing purposes for which new successor
corporations shall be deemed to be the same corporations as and
continuations of predecessor amalgamated or wound-up corporations.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR_91-33_EN_hq_5507-ID0EFBAA){#fn_SOR_91-33_EN_hq_5507}S.C.
  1990, c. 45, s. 12
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Amalgamations and Windings-Up Continuation (GST/HST)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-33).

- SOR/99-173, s. 1

## [Prescribed Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-934442 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 271 and 272 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15),
"prescribed purpose" means for the purpose of applying any provision of
that Act set out in the schedule.
