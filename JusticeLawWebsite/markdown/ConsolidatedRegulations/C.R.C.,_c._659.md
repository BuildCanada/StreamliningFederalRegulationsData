## Canadian Turkey Anti-dumping Regulations {#canadian-turkey-anti-dumping-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 659
:::

[FARM PRODUCTS AGENCIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-4)

Regulations Respecting the Pricing of Turkeys Iin Interprovincial Trade
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559696 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Turkey Anti-dumping
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._659).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559698 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to turkeys, means
    selling and offering for sale and buying, pricing, assembling,
    packing, processing, transporting, storing, and reselling, whether
    in whole or in processed form, and includes marketing by or on
    behalf of a producer; ([commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[regulated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Provinces of Nova
    Scotia, New Brunswick, Quebec, Ontario, Manitoba, Saskatchewan,
    Alberta and British Columbia; ([région réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[turkey]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turkey, male or female,
    live or slaughtered, of any size and includes any part of any
    turkey; ([dindon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[unregulated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[unregulated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any part of Canada
    not within the regulated area. ([région non
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559704 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Within the regulated area
and with respect to turkeys shipped from the unregulated area into the
regulated area in interprovincial trade and not for export, no person
shall market turkeys in a province other than the province in which the
turkeys are produced at a price that is less than the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the price charged at or about the same time for
  turkeys of an equivalent variety, class or grade in the province or
  other geographical area in which the turkeys are produced; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reasonable transportation charges therefor to the
  place where such turkeys are marketed and that are incurred by the
  person marketing the turkeys.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** With respect to turkeys
from the regulated area shipped into the unregulated area in
interprovincial trade, no person shall market turkeys in a province
other than the province in which the turkeys are produced at a price
that is less than the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the price charged at or about the same time for
  turkeys of an equivalent variety, class or grade in the province or
  other geographical area in which the turkeys are produced; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any reasonable transportation charges therefor to the
  place where such turkeys are marketed and that are incurred by the
  person marketing the turkeys.
