## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618156 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Canadian mission worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian mission worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen of
    Afghanistan who directly supported the Canadian mission in Kandahar
    province, either

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as an employee or independent contractor of a
      department or agency of the federal public administration; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as an employee or independent contractor of a
      person or entity that has entered into a contract with a
      department or agency of the federal public administration.
      ([travailleur de la mission canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 1(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227). ([conjoint de
    fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[JRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[JRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Joint Referral Committee,
    established by the federal public administration and composed of
    senior officials of the federal public administration.
    ([CMR]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is considered to
    be a spouse under the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).
    ([époux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618164 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted, to
persons who meet the conditions set out in section 3 and to their
spouses or common-law partners, of the fees paid or payable under
subsection 303(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the acquisition of
permanent resident status.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618166 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was a Canadian mission worker in Kandahar province
    for at least 12 months, consecutive or not, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at any point between October 9, 2007 and July 31,
    2011, served in Kandahar province as an interpreter, cultural
    advisor or other professional;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on or before September 12, 2011, a form provided by
  the federal public administration had been submitted to the JRC in
  accordance with the Temporary Public Policy Concerning Certain Local
  Staff in Direct Support of the Canadian Mission in Kandahar as of
  October 9, 2009 but the form was not referred to the Department of
  Citizenship and Immigration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person has received a positive recommendation
  from a member of the Canadian Forces or an official of the federal
  public administration;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} at the request of the Department of Citizenship and
  Immigration, the person provides any additional evidence or
  information that is required to demonstrate that they meet the
  conditions of this Order; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Department of Citizenship and Immigration
  assessed the person in accordance with the Temporary Public Policy
  Concerning Certain Local Staff Who Provided Direct Support to the
  Canadian Mission in Kandahar as of February 2012 and approved the
  person's application for permanent residence in Canada.
