## Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of the Status of Wildlife Species {#order-extending-the-time-for-the-assessment-of-the-status-of-wildlife-species .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-215
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2003-06-05

Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of the Status of Wildlife
Species

P.C. 2003-879  2003-06-05
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 130(5) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788){#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788-ID0EBBBA},
the Minister of the Environment has consulted with the competent
ministers and has included a statement in the public registry setting
out the reasons for the extension,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 130(5) of the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788){#fn_SOR-2003-215_e_hq_5788-ID0EDAAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order Extending the Time for the Assessment of
the Status of Wildlife Species](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-215).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The time provided for the
assessment of the status of the wildlife species set out in the schedule
is extended for 3 years from the day on which section 14 of the [Species
at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) comes into force.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on June 5, 2003.
