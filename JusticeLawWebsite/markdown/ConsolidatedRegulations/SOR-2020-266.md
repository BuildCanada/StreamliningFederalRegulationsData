## [Denial of Licences for Family Orders and Agreements Enforcement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1278411 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Family Orders and Agreements
Enforcement Assistance Act](/eng/acts/F-1.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of licence denial application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A licence denial
application referred to in subsection 67(2) of the Act must contain the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and reference number of the provincial
  enforcement service making the application;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the provincial enforcement service
  sent the notice referred to in paragraph 67(3)(c) of the Act to the
  debtor;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a declaration by the provincial enforcement service
  that the information in the application is correct and that the
  information was provided for the purpose of a licence denial under the
  Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the debtor,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their surname and given names,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their last known address,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their phone number and email address, if they are
    known,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} their date of birth,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their gender,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} their social insurance number, if known,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the surnames at birth of their parents, if known,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the city and country of their birth, if known,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} their height, if known,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} their eye colour, if known,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} their employer's name and address, if they are
    known, and

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} the language of their choice for correspondence,
    if known; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the support order,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the court that issued the support
    order,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date the support order was issued,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties set out in the support
    order,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the amount in arrears or the payment periods in
    default, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} whether payments set out in the support order must
    be made every week, every two weeks, twice a month, every month,
    every quarter, twice a year or every year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sending applications and requests to
Minister

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Applications referred to in
subsection 67(2) of the Act and requests referred to in subsection 72(2)
of the Act are to be sent by mail to the Department of Justice, Family
Law Assistance Services, Ottawa, Ontario K1A 0H8, or by the means of
electronic communication that has been agreed on by the provincial
enforcement service and the Minister.
