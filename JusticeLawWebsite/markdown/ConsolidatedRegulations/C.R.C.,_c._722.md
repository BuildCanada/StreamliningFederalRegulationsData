## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561055 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked, SOR/85-911, s.
1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561059 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Indian
Affairs and Northern Development is authorized to execute, on behalf of
Her Majesty, licences, during pleasure, to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} erect, lay, maintain and operate telegraph, telephone
  and power transmission wires and cables and water, sewer, drain and
  oil pipes on, over, under and across canals,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} erect, place and maintain boathouses at sites on a
  canal from time to time designated by the Superintending Engineer of
  the said canal,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} moor or pull ashore at sites providing less than the
  normal navigation draft of the canal concerned, such sites being
  designated from time to time by the Superintending Engineer of the
  said canal,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} draw water from the said canals through pipes not
  exceeding 12 inches in diameter or to an extent not exceeding 1,500
  gallons per minute, for domestic, agricultural and industrial, but not
  water power, purposes,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} draw water from canals for fire protection purposes,
  and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} discharge, into canals, water, drainage and effluents
  provided such effluents are, if and when deemed advisable by the
  General Superintendent of Canals, settled and or purified to his
  satisfaction,

and to determine the rental therefor.
