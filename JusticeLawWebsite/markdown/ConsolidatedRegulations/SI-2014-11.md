## Transfer of Duties Order (Unit Responsible for Federal Bridges in the Region of Montreal) {#transfer-of-duties-order-unit-responsible-for-federal-bridges-in-the-region-of-montreal .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2014-11
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2014-02-26

Transfer of Duties Order (Unit Responsible for Federal Bridges in the
Region of Montreal)

P.C. 2014-144 2014-02-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers to the Office of Infrastructure of Canada from the Department
of Transport the control and supervision of that portion of the federal
public administration in the Department of Transport known as the Unit
Responsible for Federal Bridges in the Region of Montreal, effective
February 13, 2014.
