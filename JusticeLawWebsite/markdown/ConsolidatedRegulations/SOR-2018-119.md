## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-846853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an air vehicle which does
    not have a pilot on board. ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Protected Marine Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Protected Marine Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Scott
    Islands Protected Marine Area established by order of the Governor
    in Council. ([zone marine protégée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a boat, ship or craft that is
    designed, used or capable of being used solely or partly for
    navigation in, on, through or immediately above water, without
    regard to method of propulsion or lack of it, and includes any such
    vessel that is under construction, but does not include a floating
    object of a prescribed class established under the [Canada Shipping
    Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-846859 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity that is likely to disturb,
    damage or destroy wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine
    Area or to remove wildlife or its habitat from the Protected Marine
    Area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dump or discharge any waste material or substance
    that is likely to harm wildlife or degrade the quality of wildlife
    habitat in the Protected Marine Area;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} introduce any living organism that is likely to
    harm wildlife or degrade the quality of wildlife habitat in the
    Protected Marine Area;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fly an aircraft above the flight restriction zone
    described in subsection (2) at an altitude that is below 3500 feet
    above mean sea level;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be within 300 metres of the low water mark of the
    Triangle, Sartine or Beresford Islands; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} anchor a vessel of more than 400 gross tonnes
    within one nautical mile (1852 metres) of the low water mark of the
    Triangle, Sartine or Beresford Islands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flight restriction zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The flight restriction zone depicted in the schedule
  is the part of the Protected Marine Area that is bounded by a line,
  based on the North American Datum 1983, Canadian Spatial Reference
  System (NAD83 CSRS) 1997 epoch, commencing at latitude 50°49′22″ north
  and longitude 128°31′29″ west, then along a rhumb line to a point at
  latitude 50°56′14″ north and longitude 129°05′39″ west, then along a
  rhumb line to a point at latitude 50°50′14″ north and longitude
  129°08′22″ west, then along a rhumb line to a point at latitude
  50°43′22″ north and longitude 128°34′47″ west and then along a rhumb
  line to the point of commencement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- public safety, national
security or emergency

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply in
respect of an activity carried out for the purpose of public safety or
national security or in response to an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- foreign vessels or aircraft

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply in
respect of activities of foreign vessels or aircraft in the part of the
Protected Marine Area situated within the exclusive economic zone of
Canada, unless the application of that section is consistent with
Article 56 of the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea,
signed by Canada on December 10, 1982.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- fishing and navigation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Paragraphs 2(1)(a) and (b)
do not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing carried out in accordance with the [Coastal
  Fisheries Protection Act](/eng/acts/C-33) and the [Fisheries
  Act](/eng/acts/F-14);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the navigation of a vessel in accordance with the
  [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the navigation of a vessel that belongs to a foreign
  military force or that belongs to or is under the command of the
  Canadian Forces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions --- certain federal or
provincial officers or employees

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Paragraphs 2(1)(d) and (e)
do not apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a federal or provincial enforcement officer when they
  are performing their duties or functions or a person who is acting
  under their direction or control; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee or officer of the government of British
  Columbia who, in the course of performing their duties or functions,
  is carrying out an activity for the purpose of wildlife research or
  the conservation or interpretation of wildlife, or a person who is
  acting under the direction or control of that employee or officer.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-846884 .Part}

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-846885 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's power

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite
  section 2, the Minister may, on application, issue a permit
  authorizing any person or government body to carry out an activity
  described in that section if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the proposed activity is to promote
    the conservation of wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine
    Area, the proposed activity is likely to provide benefits for the
    conservation of that wildlife or habitat that outweigh any adverse
    effects that it is likely to have on them and there is no
    alternative to the proposed activity that is likely to provide the
    same or equivalent benefits but that is likely to have less
    significant adverse effects on that wildlife or habitat; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the adverse effects that the proposed activity is
    likely to have on wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine
    Area do not compromise their conservation, or are not likely to do
    so as a result of the applicant taking the measures described in
    paragraph (2)(d), and there is no alternative to the proposed
    activity that allows the applicant to achieve the same outcome but
    that is likely to have less significant adverse effects on that
    wildlife or habitat.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's contact information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} details regarding the activity that the applicant
    proposes to carry out, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the activity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the location where it will be carried out,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the conveyances that will be used,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the types of equipment that will be used, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons who will be carrying out
      the activity and their qualifications;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} details regarding the effects that the activity is
    likely to have on wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine
    Area, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the likelihood that the effects will occur and
      their scope, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the likely effects on birds or their habitat or
      on species that are a significant source of food for birds; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} details regarding the measures that will be taken
    by the applicant to monitor the effects described in paragraph (c)
    and to prevent or, if prevention is not feasible, mitigate any
    adverse effects.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria --- evaluating effects

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must, in
evaluating the likely effects of a proposed activity on wildlife or its
habitat in the Protected Marine Area and determining if the effects are
adverse, consider the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the likelihood that the effects will occur and their
  scope;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the capacity of wildlife to recover or its habitat to
  be restored, if the effects occur;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the likely effects on birds or their habitat or on
  species that are a significant source of food for birds; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative effects of the proposed activity when
  combined with the effects of other activities carried out in the
  Protected Marine Area.

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-846910 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory conditions

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A permit must include the
following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder must notify the Minister of any
  change to any information provided in the application for the permit;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} each individual who carries out an activity
  authorized by the permit must carry a copy of the permit on their
  person at all times when in the Protected Marine Area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other conditions

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A permit may include any
other conditions with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locations, dates, duration and frequency of the
  activity that may be carried out and the types of equipment or
  conveyances that may be used;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and type of any vessel that may be used to
  carry out the activity, the State in which it is registered, its
  registration number, radio call sign and the name and address of its
  owner, master and operator;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the measures that the permit holder must take to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} monitor the effects that the activity is likely to
    have on wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine Area, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} prevent or, if prevention is not feasible,
    mitigate any adverse effects;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reports that the permit holder must provide to
  the Minister with respect to the activity, including its actual and
  likely effects on wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine
  Area; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the minimum or maximum number of persons that may
  carry out the activity and the qualifications they are required to
  possess.

### [Expiry]{.HTitleText2} {#h-846924 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum duration of permit

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A permit expires on the
fifth anniversary of the day on which it is issued or the date of expiry
set out in the permit, whichever is earlier.

### [Suspension and Cancellation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-846927 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend a permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is necessary to do so for the conservation of
    wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine Area, particularly
    in relation to birds or species that are a significant source of
    food for birds; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has failed to comply with any
    condition of the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period of suspension

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The permit is suspended until the day on which the
  Minister notifies the permit holder that the suspension of the permit
  is lifted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lifting of suspension

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must lift the suspension when the
  grounds for the suspension no longer exist or when the permit holder
  has taken the measures that are necessary to remedy the situation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
permit if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is necessary to do so for the conservation of
  wildlife or its habitat in the Protected Marine Area, particularly in
  relation to birds or species that are a significant source of food for
  birds;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are grounds on which to suspend the permit
  under subsection 12(1) and the permit has been suspended at least
  twice before;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the permit has been suspended for at least six
  months; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has provided false or misleading
  information.
