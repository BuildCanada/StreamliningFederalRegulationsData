Whereas, in accordance with paragraph 5(1)(a) of the [First Nations
Commercial and Industrial Development Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453){#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453-ID0EBBFA},
the Minister of Indigenous Services has received a resolution from the
council of the Squamish Nation requesting that the Minister recommend to
the Governor in Council the making of the annexed Regulations;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453-ID0EBBFA){#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453}S.C.
  2005, c. 53
  :::

Whereas the purpose of the annexed Regulations is to ensure that the
laws set out in Schedule 2 to the annexed Regulations apply as federal
law to the project lands, within the limits of federal constitutional
authority;

Whereas, in accordance with paragraph 3(2)(b) of that Act, the annexed
Regulations confer on the Minister of Indigenous Services the
legislative power that the Governor in Council considers necessary to
effectively regulate the commercial or industrial undertakings that are
located on the reserve lands described in the annexed Regulations;

Whereas the annexed Regulations specify provincial officials by whom,
and provincial bodies by which, powers may be exercised or duties are to
be performed;

And whereas, in accordance with paragraph 5(1)(b) of that Act, an
agreement has been concluded between the Minister of Indigenous
Services, the Province of British Columbia and the council of the
Squamish Nation for the administration and enforcement of the annexed
Regulations by those provincial officials and provincial bodies;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Indigenous Services makes the annexed
[Squamish Nation Residential Tenancy
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-135) under sections 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658157679){#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658157679-ID0EEBAA}
and 4 of the [First Nations Commercial and Industrial Development
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453){#fn_81000-2-4894-E_hq_1658156453-ID0EBBAA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407086 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[First Nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to lands
      described in column 2 of Schedule 1, means the First Nation named
      in column 1 of that Schedule. ([Première Nation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the statutes and
      regulations of British Columbia, or the portions of them, that are
      set out in Schedule 2, as amended from time to time and as adapted
      by sections 11 to 31. ([texte législatif
      incorporé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a First
      Nation named in column 1 of Schedule 1, means the reserve lands
      described in column 2 of that Schedule. ([terres du
      projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the FNCIDA Implementation Act, S.B.C. 2012, c.
      21. ([organisme provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[provincial official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
      as in section 1 of the FNCIDA Implementation Act, S.B.C. 2012, c.
      21. ([fonctionnaire provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expanded meaning of undertaking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the [First Nations Commercial and
  Industrial Development Act](/eng/acts/F-11.64), the meaning of the
  expression "commercial or industrial undertaking" is enlarged to
  include a manufactured home park and a residential tenancy
  development.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}British Columbia [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The incorporated laws are
to be interpreted in accordance with the British Columbia
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21), R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 238, as amended
from time to time, and, for that purpose, references to "enactment" in
that Act are to be read to include the incorporated laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
adaptations in sections 11 to 31 are to be interpreted to be part of the
incorporated laws to which they apply.

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407101 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of these
Regulations is to implement with respect to the project lands a legal
regime that is harmonized with the legal regime of British Columbia that
governs the landlord and tenant relationship in relation to manufactured
home parks and residential tenancy developments.

## [Application of Incorporated Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407104 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to fix date of application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 6 and
9, the Minister must, by order, fix and set out in column 3 of Schedule
1, the date on which the incorporated laws begin to apply to project
lands listed in column 2 of that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- incorporated laws in
force

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provision of an incorporated law applies only if the provision of the
  law of British Columbia that it incorporates is in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction --- limits of authority

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an incorporated law applies
  only to the extent that it is within the limits of federal
  constitutional authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and penalties

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If contravention of a law
of British Columbia that is incorporated in these Regulations is an
offence under the laws of British Columbia, contravention of the
incorporated law is also an offence and is subject to the same penalties
as under the laws of British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of procedural matters

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided and subject to any adaptations set out in sections
  11 to 31, the following are to conform to the laws of British
  Columbia, whether or not those laws have been set out in Schedule 2:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the enforcement of incorporated laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prosecution of an offence, or any other
    proceedings, in relation to the contravention of an incorporated
    law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the review or appeal of an action or decision
    taken, or of a failure to take an action that could have been taken,
    under an incorporated law; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any requirements for notice or other procedures in
    relation to an action to be taken under an incorporated law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Related powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a person,
  provincial official or provincial body that has a power, duty or
  function under a law of British Columbia is a person, provincial
  official or provincial body specified to have the same power, duty or
  function in respect of any actions taken under that subsection.

## [Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407123 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to leaseholders and Indians in
lawful possession

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the Minister makes an order under section 5 with respect to any
  project lands, the council of the First Nation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} send notice by mail to the following persons
    registered as leaseholders or Indians in lawful possession, as
    applicable, in a register in which the project lands are registered,
    at the addresses that are indicated in relation to the registrations
    or that may be otherwise known by the First Nation, advising those
    leaseholders and Indians of the intention to apply the incorporated
    laws to the project lands:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any leaseholder in any of the project lands, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any Indian considered to be lawfully in
      possession of any of the lands under section 20 of the [Indian
      Act](/eng/acts/I-5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish a notice one day per week for two
    consecutive weeks, in the local newspaper with the largest
    circulation, advising of the intention to apply the incorporated
    laws to the project lands; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister with written notice of
    compliance with paragraphs (a) and (b).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No invalidity or cause of action

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An order made by the Minister under section 5 is not
  invalid by reason only of the failure of a leaseholder or Indian
  referred to in paragraph (1)(a) to receive notice under subsection (1)
  and no cause of action exists against His Majesty in right of Canada,
  the Minister, His Majesty in right of British Columbia, any provincial
  official, any provincial body, the First Nation or the council of the
  First Nation from such a failure.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} has
  the same meaning as in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notation on the register

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Once the incorporated
laws apply to the project lands, the officer in the Department of
Indigenous Services responsible for the management of the register in
which those project lands are registered must place a notation on the
register indicating that the incorporated laws apply to those project
lands.

## [General Adaptations to Incorporated Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1407138 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statutes and regulations of British
Columbia

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
indicated, the statutes and regulations referred to in sections 16 to 31
are statutes and regulations of British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to "person"

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A reference to a "person"
in an incorporated law is to be read to include a First Nation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of incorporated laws

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Incorporated laws are to be read
  without reference to any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} spent provisions, commencement provisions and
    consequential amendments;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions appointing a person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provisions authorizing the Lieutenant Governor in
    Council to make regulations of general application, except to the
    extent required to make the regulations set out in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment to a position

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b), a person who is appointed
  to a position under an incorporated law is considered to have been
  appointed to the same position for the purposes of these Regulations
  for as long as that person remains in that position under the law of
  British Columbia.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified persons, officials and bodies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person, provincial official
  or provincial body that has a power, duty or function under an
  incorporated law or a law of British Columbia referred to in
  subsection 8(1) that is not set out in Schedule 2, is a person
  specified to have the same power, duty or function under these
  Regulations, subject to the adaptations set out in sections 16 to 31.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to incorporated law

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if a law is adapted by these
  Regulations, a reference to that law in an incorporated law, or in any
  notice, form, instrument or other document issued under an
  incorporated law, is to be read as a reference to that law as adapted
  by these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A provision of an
incorporated law that imposes an obligation, liability or penalty on a
landlord, owner, occupier, public authority, public body or unspecified
person or entity does not apply to His Majesty in right of Canada or
federal ministers or officials.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to seize, remove or compel
production of documents

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A power to seize or
remove a document or record or to compel the production of a document or
record under an incorporated law does not include a power to seize or
remove a document or record that is in the possession of the federal
government or to compel the production by the federal government of a
document or record.

> - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by a person or organization that receives funding
>   from a First Nation, a local government or the government of British
>   Columbia or of Canada for the purpose of providing that
>   accommodation, and
> :::

  >   the operation of residential property with the following:
  > :::

  as follows:

  > - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a municipality or a First Nation;
  > :::

  follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any housing society or non-profit municipal or
  >   First Nation housing corporation that previously had an agreement
  >   regarding the operation of residential property with a person or

  >   renewed.
  > :::
