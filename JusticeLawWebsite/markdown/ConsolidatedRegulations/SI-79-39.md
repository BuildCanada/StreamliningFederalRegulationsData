## Non-Commercial Importations Remission Order {#non-commercial-importations-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/79-39
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1979-03-14

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties and Excise Taxes in
Respect of Non-Commercial Importations in Connection with Warranty on
Guarantee Adjustments

P.C. 1979-395 1979-02-15
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 17 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is
pleased hereby to revoke Order in Counsil P.C. 1963-15/1067 of 16th
July, 1963[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make
the annexed Order respecting the remission of customs duty and excise
taxes in respect of non-commercial importations in connection with
warranty or guarantee adjustments.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634397 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Non-Commercial Importations Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-79-39).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634399 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-commercial imported goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-commercial imported goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods
    imported for a purpose other than for any sale or for any
    commercial, industrial or business use in respect of which the
    aggregate amount of customs duties and excise taxes payable does not
    exceed fifty dollars. ([marchandises importées non
    commerciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634405 .Part}

### [Non-Commercial Goods Exported or Destroyed]{.HTitleText2} {#h-634406 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and excise taxes paid or
payable under the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of
non-commercial imported goods if evidence is submitted by the importer
to a chief officer of customs to show that the goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} are not of the class or description of the goods
  ordered by the importer,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} are defective, or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} are of inferior quality,

and the goods have been

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} destroyed in Canada under a customs officer's
  supervision, or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} exported from Canada to the supplier of the goods

within one year from the day the goods were imported into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

### [Replacement of Non-Commercial Imported Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-634417 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties and excise taxes paid
or payable in respect of non-commercial imported goods if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported to replace non-commercial
  imported goods described in section 3 that have been destroyed in
  Canada or exported from Canada within the time set out in that
  section;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are of a similar class and approximately
  the same value as the non-commercial imported goods that they are
  replacing;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the goods is borne by the supplier of the
  goods;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the goods are imported within six months of the
  destruction or exportation of the non-commercial imported goods that
  they are replacing; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} no remission, refund or drawback of customs duties or
  excise tax has been granted in respect of the non-commercial imported
  goods that they are replacing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
:::

### [Repair of Non-Commercial Imported Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-634426 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties and excise taxes paid
or payable in respect of non-commercial imported goods that were
previously imported into Canada and subsequent to that importation were
exported from Canada for the sole purpose of being repaired, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods were so exported within one year from the
  day the goods were first imported into Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods were returned to Canada within six months
  from the day they were so exported; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the repair to the goods, except any
  shipping charges, communication expenses or other expenses paid by the
  importer in accordance with any warranty or guaranty adjustment
  arrangements, is borne by the foreign supplier under the terms of the
  warranty or guaranty in respect of the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
:::

## [Application for Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634433 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No remission shall be
granted under this Order unless an application is made for remission to
a chief officer of customs at the customs office where the
non-commercial imported goods were imported into Canada,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the remission is in respect of goods described
  in section 3, within ninety days of the destruction or exportation of
  the goods; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the remission is in respect of goods described
  in section 4 or 5, within ninety days from the day the goods are
  entered into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::
