## Vessel Clearance Regulations {#vessel-clearance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-125
:::

[CANADA SHIPPING ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)

Registration 2007-06-07

Vessel Clearance Regulations

P.C. 2007-923 2007-06-07
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, Infrastructure and Communities, pursuant to
paragraph 244(a) of the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_514e_hq_665){#fn_514e_hq_665-ID0EDBAA}, hereby
makes the annexed [Vessel Clearance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-125).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732350 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is not a
    passenger vessel. ([bâtiment de charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chemical tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chemical tanker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that was
    constructed or adapted, and that is used, for the carriage in bulk
    of any liquid product listed in Chapter 17 of the International Code
    for the Construction and Equipment of Ships Carrying Dangerous
    Chemicals in Bulk, published by the IMO. ([bâtiment-citerne pour
    produits chimiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gas carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gas carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a cargo vessel that was
    constructed or adapted, and that is used, for the carriage in bulk
    of any liquefied gas or other products listed in Chapter 19 of the
    International Code for the Construction and Equipment of Ships
    Carrying Liquefied Gases in Bulk, published by the IMO.
    ([transporteur de gaz]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[IMO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Maritime
    Organization. ([OMI]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
    internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[inland waters of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([eaux internes du
    Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[INF cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[INF cargo]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means packaged goods that are
    irradiated nuclear fuel, plutonium or high-level radioactive wastes
    carried as cargo. ([cargaison INF]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage from a
    port in Canada to a place not in Canada. ([voyage
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[passenger vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[passenger vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that carries
    more than 12 passengers. ([bâtiment à passagers]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[place]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a port; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any vessel or place that is used for loading or
      unloading vessels. ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention for
    the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, and the Protocol of 1988 relating
    to the Convention, as amended from time to time.
    ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Section 213 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732370 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section
  213 of the Act applies only to vessels that are departing on an
  international voyage and are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} passenger vessels of 15 gross tonnage or less; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels of more than 15 gross tonnage.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-135, s. 32\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-135, s. 32\]]{.Repealed}

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 213 of the Act does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels in respect of which a direction has been
    given under paragraph 227(1)(c) of the Act.

### [Documents Required for Clearance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732391 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 214 of the Act, the following documents are
  required in respect of a vessel:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if required under the [Load Line
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-99),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Great Lakes and Inland Waters of Canada Load
      Line Certificate or a load line certificate issued under Title 46,
      chapter I, part 45 of the Code of Federal Regulations of the
      United States, as amended from time to time, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an International Load Line Certificate or an
      International Load Line Exemption Certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if required under the [Cargo, Fumigation and Tackle
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-128), a Fitness to Proceed
    Certificate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if required under the [Vessel Pollution and
    Dangerous Chemicals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an International Oil Pollution Prevention
      Certificate,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel carrying noxious liquid
      substances in bulk,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an International Pollution Prevention
        Certificate for the Carriage of Noxious Liquid Substances in
        Bulk,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a Certificate of Fitness for the Carriage of
        Dangerous Chemicals in Bulk or an International Certificate of
        Fitness for the Carriage of Dangerous Chemicals in Bulk, if the
        vessel is a chemical tanker, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} a Certificate of Fitness for an Offshore
        Support Vessel, if the vessel is a vessel to which the
        Guidelines for the Transport and Handling of Limited Amounts of
        Hazardous and Noxious Liquid Substances in Bulk on Offshore
        Support Vessels, IMO Resolution A.673(16), apply,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an International Sewage Pollution Prevention
      Certificate,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an International Air Pollution Prevention
      Certificate,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an International Anti-fouling System Certificate
      or a Declaration on Anti-fouling System, as applicable, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} an International Energy Efficiency Certificate;
      and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Safe Manning Document or an equivalent document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificates referred to in subparagraph
  (1)(c)(ii) are not required in respect of a vessel if the vessel's
  master has a Certificate of Fitness for Offshore Support Vessels.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 214 of the Act, the
  following certificates are required in respect of a passenger vessel,
  or a cargo vessel of 500 gross tonnage or more, that is departing on
  an international voyage other than an inland voyage:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Safety Management Certificate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a passenger vessel, a Passenger Ship
    Safety Certificate and, if applicable, an Exemption Certificate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cargo vessel,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Cargo Ship Safety Certificate and, if
      applicable, an Exemption Certificate, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a Cargo Ship Safety Construction Certificate, a
      Cargo Ship Safety Equipment Certificate, a Cargo Ship Safety Radio
      Certificate and, if applicable, an Exemption Certificate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a gas carrier, a Certificate of
    Fitness for the Carriage of Liquefied Gases in Bulk or an
    International Certificate of Fitness for the Carriage of Liquefied
    Gases in Bulk; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel carrying INF cargo, an
    International Certificate of Fitness for the Carriage of INF Cargo.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The certificates referred to in paragraphs (3)(b)
  and (c) are not required in respect of a vessel to which Chapter X of
  SOLAS applies if the vessel's master has a High-Speed Craft Safety
  Certificate.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 214 of the Act, a safety
  certificate issued under section 13 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) is required in respect of
  a Canadian vessel that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a cargo vessel of less than 500 gross tonnage
    that is departing on an international voyage other than an inland
    voyage; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is departing on an inland voyage.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 214 of the Act, an
  inspection certificate issued by or under the authority of the
  government of a foreign state is required in respect of a foreign
  vessel that is entitled to fly the flag of that state and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a cargo vessel of less than 500 gross tonnage
    that is departing on an international voyage other than an inland
    voyage; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is departing on an inland voyage.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a vessel is entitled to fly the flag of a state
  that has not ratified an international convention or protocol that
  establishes the requirements for issuing one of the certificates
  referred to in subsections (1) to (4), a Certificate of Compliance
  that is issued by or under the authority of the government of that
  state and certifies that the vessel complies with those requirements
  is required in lieu of that certificate in respect of the vessel.
