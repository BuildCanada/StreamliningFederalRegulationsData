## Appointment or Deployment of Alternates Exclusion Approval Order {#appointment-or-deployment-of-alternates-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-74
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2017-12-13

Appointment or Deployment of Alternates Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2017-1437 2017-11-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Public Works and Government Services, pursuant to
section 20 of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147-ID0EBDHA},
approves

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147-ID0EBDHA){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application by the Public Service Commission
  of the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded pursuant to
  Order in Council P.C. 2012-437[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148-ID0EBBBHA}
  of April 5, 2012 and the persons occupying them; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148-ID0EBBBHA){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148}SI/2012-27
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion by the Public Service Commission from
  the application of section 16, paragraph 22(2)(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149-ID0EFDAHA},
  subsection 29(3), sections 30, 31 and 39.1 to 48[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150-ID0EDDAHA},
  subsection 51(4) and section 77[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151-ID0EBDAHA}
  of that Act of an indeterminate employee who is appointed or deployed
  to the position of an indeterminate employee whose work unit is
  relocated and who does not wish to relocate or whose services are no
  longer required by reason of lack of work, the discontinuance of a
  function or the transfer of work or a function outside the public
  service and who has not received a guarantee of a reasonable job
  offer.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149-ID0EFDAHA){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149}S.C.
    2015, c. 5, s. 2
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150-ID0EDDAHA){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150}S.C.
    2006, c. 9, s. 103; S.C. 2008, c. 15, s. 6; S.C. 2015, c. 5, ss. 7
    and 8
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151-ID0EBDAHA){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151}S.C.
    2013, c. 40, par. 414(d)
    :::

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
section 16, paragraph 22(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149-ID0EIAGA},
subsection 29(3), sections 30, 31 and 39.1 to 48[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150-ID0EGAGA},
subsection 51(4) and section 77[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151-ID0EEAGA}
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147-ID0EBAGA}
to an indeterminate employee who is appointed or deployed to the
position of an indeterminate employee whose work unit is relocated and
who does not wish to relocate or whose services are no longer required
by reason of lack of work, the discontinuance of a function or the
transfer of work or a function outside the public service and who has
not received a guarantee of a reasonable job offer;

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of that Act, the Public
Service Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21147-ID0EBCEA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reapplies the provisions of that Act to the positions
  excluded pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 2012-437[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21148-ID0EBABEA}
  of April 5, 2012 and the persons occupying them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes from the application of section 16,
  paragraph 22(2)(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21149-ID0EFAAEA},
  subsection 29(3), sections 30, 31 and 39.1 to 48[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21150-ID0EDAAEA},
  subsection 51(4) and section 77[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151){#fn_SI-2017-74_e_hq_21151-ID0EBAAEA}
  of that Act to an indeterminate employee who is appointed or deployed
  to the position of an indeterminate employee whose work unit is
  relocated and who does not wish to relocate or whose services are no
  longer required by reason of lack of work, the discontinuance of a
  function or the transfer of work or a function outside the public
  service and who has not received a guarantee of a reasonable job
  offer.

Ottawa, July 18, 2017
