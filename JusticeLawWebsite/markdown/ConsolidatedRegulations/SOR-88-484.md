## Montreal Port Warden's Tariff of Fees Order (1988) {#montreal-port-wardens-tariff-of-fees-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-484
:::

[AN ACT TO AMEND AND CONSOLIDATE THE ACTS RELATING TO THE OFFICE OF PORT
WARDEN FOR THE HARBOUR OF MONTREAL](/eng/acts/O-2.6)

Registration 1988-09-15

Order Establishing the Tariff of Fees and Charges to be Paid to the Port
Warden of the Harbour of Montreal for Services Performed by the Port
Warden or Any Deputy Port Warden

P.C. 1988-2023 1988-09-15
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to sections 28 and 33[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn1star_e){#fn1star_e-ID0EFDAA} of An Act to
amend and consolidate the Acts relating to the office of Port Warden for
the Harbour of Montreal[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn2star_e){#fn2star_e-ID0EDDAA}, is pleased
hereby to approve the repeal of the Montreal Port Warden's Tariff of
Fees Order, approved by Order in Council P.C. 1985-3546 of December 5,
1985[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn3star_e){#fn3star_e-ID0EBDAA}, and to
approve the annexed Order establishing the tariff of fees and charges to
be paid to the Port Warden of the Harbour of Montreal for services
performed by the Port Warden or any Deputy Port Warden, made by the
Council of the Board of Trade at the City of Montreal, on the
recommendation of the Board of Examiners, in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913561 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Montreal Port Warden's Tariff of Fees Order
(1988)](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-484).

## [Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913563 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fees and charges set
out in column II of an item of the schedule shall be paid for the
services specified in column I of that item and are due from the master
or owner of a vessel at the time the services are performed.
