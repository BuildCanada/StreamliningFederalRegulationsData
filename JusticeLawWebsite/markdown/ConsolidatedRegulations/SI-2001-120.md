## Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration as the Minister responsible for the administration of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-citizenship-and-immigration-as-the-minister-responsible-for-the-administration-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-120
:::

[IMMIGRATION AND REFUGEE PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/I-2.5)

Registration 2001-12-19

Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration as the
Minister responsible for the administration of that Act

P.C. 2001-2265  2001-12-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [Immigration and
Refugee Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5), assented to on November 1,
2001, being chapter 27 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, hereby
designates the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, a member of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister responsible for the
administration of that Act, effective December 6, 2001.
:::
