[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
4.11 of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), the
Brokering Control List includes the following goods and technology:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Groups 2 and 9 of
  the schedule to the [Export Control
  List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Groups 1, 3, 4, 6
  and 7 and in sub items 5504(2)(d), (e) and (g) of the schedule to the
  [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) if their properties
  and any information made known to the broker would lead a reasonable
  person to suspect that they will be used

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the development, production, handling,
    operation, maintenance, storage, detection, identification or
    dissemination of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} chemical or biological weapons,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} nuclear explosive or radiological dispersal
      devices, or

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} materials or equipment that could be used in such
      weapons or devices,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the development, production, handling,
    operation, maintenance or storage of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} missiles or other systems capable of delivering
      chemical or biological weapons or nuclear explosive or
      radiological dispersal devices, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} materials or equipment that could be used in such
      missiles or systems, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any facility used for any of the activities
    described in subparagraphs (i) and (ii); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods and technology referred to in Groups 1, 3, 4, 6
  and 7 and in sub items 5504(2)(d), (e) and (g) of the schedule to the
  [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) if the Minister has
  determined, on the basis of their properties and any additional
  information relating to such matters as their intended end-use or the
  identity or conduct of their intermediary or final consignees, that
  they are likely to be used in the activities or facilities referred to
  in paragraph (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2019-220_e_transform_hq_25373){#fn_SOR-2019-220_e_transform_hq_25373-ID0EBCA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This List comes into force on the day on which section
5 of An Act to amend the Export and Import Permits Act and the Criminal
Code (amendments permitting the accession to the Arms Trade Treaty and
other amendments), chapter 26 of the Statutes of Canada, 2018, comes
into force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
