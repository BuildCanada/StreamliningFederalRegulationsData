## Indian Band Council Procedure Regulations {#indian-band-council-procedure-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 950
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Respecting Procedure at Indian Band Council Meetings
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598852 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Band Council Procedure
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._950).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598854 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Assistant Deputy Minister, Indian and Eskimo Affairs of the
    Department; ([sous-ministre adjoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the council of a Band elected
    pursuant to section 74 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5);
    ([conseil]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[secretary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person appointed by the
    council of a band to record the minutes of the council meetings;
    ([secrétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent or
    Senior Field Officer of the Indian Affairs Branch in charge of the
    Agency, and includes the Indian Commissioner for British Columbia,
    all Regional Supervisors, all Assistants Indian Agency, and any
    other officer acting under the instructions of the Minister or the
    Assistant Deputy Minister. ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Meetings of the Council]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598862 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  first meeting of the council shall be held not later than one month
  after its election, on a day, hour and place to be stated in a notice
  given to each member of the council, and meetings shall thereafter be
  held on such days and at such times as may be necessary for the
  business of the council or the affairs of the band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No member of a council may be absent from meetings of
  the council for three consecutive meetings without being authorized to
  do so by the chief of the band or superintendent, with the consent of
  the majority of the councillors of the band.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The chief of the band or
superintendent may, at any time, summon a special meeting of the
council, and shall summon a special meeting when requested to do so by a
majority of the members of the council.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The superintendent shall
notify each member of the council of the day, hour and place of the
meeting.

## [Order and Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-598868 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A majority of the whole
council shall constitute a quorum, but where a council consists of nine
or more members five members shall constitute a quorum.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If no quorum is present
within 1 hour after the time appointed for the meeting, the secretary
shall call the roll and take the names of the members then present and
the council shall stand adjourned until the next meeting.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The chief of the band or,
with the consent of the majority of the councillors present at the
meeting, the superintendent shall be the presiding officer.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Upon a
  quorum being present, the presiding officer shall take the chair and
  call the meeting to order.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A chairman shall be chosen

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the absence of the chief, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the superintendent is not chosen the
    presiding officer pursuant to section 8,

  from among the members present who shall preside during the meeting or
  until the arrival of the chief or until the superintendent is chosen
  as the presiding officer.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The presiding officer
shall maintain order and decide all questions of procedure.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The order of business at
each regular meeting shall be as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reading (correction, if any) and adoption of the
  minutes of the previous meeting;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} unfinished business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} presentation and reading of correspondence and
  petitions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} presentation and consideration of reports of
  committees;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} new business;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} hearing deputations;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} adjournment.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Each resolution shall be
presented or read by the mover, and when duly moved and seconded and
placed before the meeting by the presiding officer, shall be open for
consideration.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** After a resolution has
been placed before the meeting by the presiding officer it shall be
deemed to be in the possession of the council, but it may be withdrawn
by consent of the majority of the council members present.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** When any member desires
to speak, he shall address his remarks to the presiding officer and
confine himself to the question then before the meeting.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** In the event of more than
one member desiring to speak at one time, the presiding officer shall
determine who is entitled to speak.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  presiding officer or any member may call a member to order while
  speaking and the debate shall then be suspended and the member shall
  not speak until the point of order is determined.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A member may speak only once on a point of order.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Any member may appeal the
decision of the presiding officer to the council and all appeals shall
be decided by a majority vote and without debate.

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  questions before the council shall be decided by a majority vote of
  the councillors present.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The presiding officer shall not be entitled to vote
  but whenever the votes are equal the presiding officer, other than the
  superintendent, shall cast the deciding vote.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Every member present when
a question is put shall vote thereon unless the council excuses him or
unless he is personally interested in the question, in which case he
shall not be obliged to vote.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A member who refuses to
vote shall be deemed to vote in the affirmative.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Whenever a division of
the council is taken for any purpose, each member present and voting
shall announce his vote upon the question openly and individually to the
council and, when so requested by any member, the secretary shall record
the same.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Any member may require
the question or resolution under discussion to be read for his
information at any period of the debate, but not so as to interrupt a
member who is speaking.

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  regular meetings shall be open to members of the band, and no member
  shall be excluded therefrom except for improper conduct.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The presiding officer may expel or exclude from any
  meeting any person who causes a disturbance at the meeting.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The council may at the
first meeting thereof appoint in lieu of the committee of the whole
council the following standing committees:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Finance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Roads and Bridges; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Welfare.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The council may appoint
special committees on any matters as the interests of the band may
require.

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A majority of the members
of a committee shall be a quorum.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The chief of the band
shall [ex officio]{.otherLang lang="la"} be a member of all committees
and be entitled to vote at all meetings thereof, and other members of
the council may attend meetings of a committee and may with the consent
of the committee take part in the discussion but shall not be entitled
to vote.

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** The general duties of
standing and special committees are,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to report to the council from time to time as often
  as the interests of the band may require, all matters connected with
  the duties imposed on them respectively and to recommend such action
  by the council in relation thereto as they may deem necessary and
  expedient; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to consider and report upon all matters referred to
  them by the council or by the chief of the band.

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Special meetings of
committees shall be called at the request of the chairman or a majority
of the committee or, in the absence of the chairman on request of the
chief of the band or the superintendent.

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Any representative of the
Minister present at a council meeting may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} address the council, and explain to and advise the
  members thereof upon their powers and duties;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} explain to and advise the members thereof upon any
  question of procedure; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} give such information as may be requested by any
  member of the council relating to the administration of the affairs of
  the band.

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The council may make such
rules of procedure as are not inconsistent with these Regulations in
respect of matters not specifically provided for thereby, as it may deem
necessary.
