## Sale of Defence Materiel and Services to United Nations Order {#sale-of-defence-materiel-and-services-to-united-nations-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 731
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Sale of Materiel and Services to the United Nations
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This order may be cited as
the [Sale of Defence Materiel and Services to United Nations
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._731).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561193 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National Defence.

## [Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561195 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is authorized
to sell materiel or provide services under the control of the Department
of National Defence to the United Nations for the support of the
operations which may be established from time to time by the United
Nations and the continued operations of the United Nations Emergency
Force.

## [Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561197 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The price of the materiel
or services provided under the authority of this Order shall be the cost
to the public, as determined by the Minister, of providing that materiel
or those services to the United Nations.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561199 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall report
annually to the Treasury Board individual authorizations made under this
Order where the value thereof is in excess of \$5,000.

## [Consultation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561201 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Departments of National
Defence, External Affairs and Finance shall act in consultation on all
major requests for such materiel.
