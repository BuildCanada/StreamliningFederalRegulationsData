## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458429 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
      2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class 1 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class 1 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel to
      which Chapter IX of SOLAS applies. ([bâtiments de catégorie
      1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class 2 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class 2 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel,
      other than a Class 1 vessel, that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is 500 gross tonnage or more; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is certified to carry 50 passengers or more as
        indicated on the vessel's safety certificate issued under the
        [Vessel Safety Certificates
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135) and is 24 m in
        length or more. ([bâtiments de catégorie 2]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[Class 3 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class 3 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel,
      other than a Class 1 or 2 vessel, that is 24 m in length or
      more. ([bâtiments de catégorie 3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class 4 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class 4 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel,
      other than a Class 1, 2 or 3 vessel, that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is more than 15 gross tonnage; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is 15 gross tonnage or less and is a
        passenger-carrying vessel or a towboat. ([bâtiments de catégorie
        4]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class 5 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class 5 vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian vessel,
      other than a Class 1, 2, 3 or 4 vessel, of 15 gross tonnage or
      less. ([bâtiments de catégorie 5]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ISM Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ISM Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
      Management Code for the Safe Operation of Ships and for Pollution
      Prevention adopted by the International Maritime Organization by
      resolution A.741(18). ([Code ISM]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[passenger-carrying vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[passenger-carrying vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel
      that is used to carry one or more passengers. ([bâtiment
      transportant des passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ship manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ship manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a qualified person who
      is responsible for managing the shore-based and on-board
      operations of a vessel. ([gestionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention
      for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, and the Protocol of 1988
      relating to the Convention. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[towboat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[towboat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      1 of the [Navigation Safety Regulations,
      2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216). ([bâtiment
      remorqueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety management system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A safety management system is a structured set of
  policies and procedures whose purpose is to enable the effective
  implementation of safety and environmental protection policies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents --- as amended from time to
time

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any reference in these
Regulations to a document is a reference to that document as amended
from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents incorporated by reference

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
interpreting a document incorporated by reference into these
Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} "should" is to be read as "must";

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Administration" is to be read as "Minister";

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Company" is to be read as "ship manager";

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} "ship" is to be read as "vessel"; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} "rules" and "regulations" are to be read as
  "regulations made under the Act".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Length and gross tonnage

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any reference in these
Regulations to the length or gross tonnage of a vessel is a reference to
the length or gross tonnage indicated on the certificate of registry
issued to it under the [Vessel Registration and Tonnage
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-126).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458463 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian vessels

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations establish the requirement that the ship manager of a Class
  1, 2, 3, 4 or 5 vessel develop, implement and maintain a documented
  safety management system that addresses the shore-based and on-board
  operations of the vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations establish the requirement that the
  authorized representative of a foreign vessel to which Chapter IX of
  SOLAS applies and that is in Canadian waters operates the vessel in
  accordance with the procedures and practices described in a documented
  safety management system.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458469 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7, these
Regulations apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 vessels and their shore-based
  and on-board operations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} foreign vessels to which Chapter IX of SOLAS applies
  that are operating in Canadian waters.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel subject to the [Fishing Vessel Safety
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1486);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that does not have a mechanical means of
  propulsion and does not carry persons, dangerous chemicals in bulk or
  oil in bulk on board;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a human-powered vessel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a vessel subject to the Special-purpose Vessels
  Regulations; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a pleasure craft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- foreign vessels subject
to SOLAS

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a foreign vessel must not operate the vessel in
Canadian waters unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a safety management certificate or interim safety
  management certificate that was issued in respect of the vessel by the
  government of the State whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly is
  kept on board;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document of compliance or interim document of
  compliance that applies to the vessel and was issued by the government
  of the State whose flag the vessel is entitled to fly is kept on
  board; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorized representative ensures that the vessel
  operates in accordance with the procedures and practices described in
  the documented safety management system in relation to which the
  certificate referred to in paragraph (a) and the document referred to
  in paragraph (b) were issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exchange

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must, on
application by the holder of a Canadian Document of Compliance, exchange
that document for a Canadian Document of Compliance of another class if
the requirements for the issuance of that other Canadian Document of
Compliance are met and the holder is not in contravention of a provision
of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Endorsement

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a Canadian
maritime document issued under these Regulations that is valid for more
than one year must ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the shore-based and on-board operations of each
  vessel to which the document applies are inspected not later than the
  dates determined by the Minister, as indicated on the document at the
  time it is issued; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the document is endorsed by the Minister following
  the inspection to indicate that the requirements for the issuance of
  the document continue to be met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification of ship manager

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a Canadian vessel must, on the request of the
Minister, identify the ship manager in the form and manner specified by
the Minister.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Class 1 Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458495 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- Documents of Compliance

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, on application by a ship manager of a Class 1 vessel,
  issue the following documents to them:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an Interim Document of Compliance, if the Minister
    determines that the ship manager has developed a documented safety
    management system and that the requirements set out in paragraph
    14.1 of Part B of the ISM Code are met; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Document of Compliance, if the Minister
    determines that the ship manager has developed and implemented a
    documented safety management system that meets the requirements of
    Part A of the ISM Code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, when issuing a Document of
  Compliance or an Interim Document of Compliance, specify on the
  document the types of vessels to which the document applies.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- safety management
certificates

**[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must, on
application by a ship manager of a Class 1 vessel, issue the following
documents in respect of the vessel to them:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an Interim Safety Management Certificate, if the
  Minister determines that the ship manager has developed a documented
  safety management system and that the requirements set out in
  paragraphs 14.2 and 14.4 of Part B of the ISM Code are met; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Safety Management Certificate, if the Minister
  determines that the vessel operates in accordance with the procedures
  and practices described in the safety management system and that the
  system meets the requirements for the issuance of a Document of
  Compliance under subsection 100(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents on board --- master

- **[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a Class 1 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Document of Compliance or Interim Document of
    Compliance that was issued to the vessel's ship manager is kept on
    board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Safety Management Certificate or Interim Safety
    Management Certificate that was issued in respect of the vessel to
    the vessel's ship manager is kept on board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    referred to in subsection 100(1) is set out is kept on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager of a Class 1 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Document of Compliance or Interim Document of
    Compliance that was issued to the ship manager is produced on the
    request of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Safety Management Certificate or Interim Safety
    Management Certificate that was issued in respect of the vessel to
    the ship manager is produced on the request of the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    referred to in subsection 100(1) is set out is produced on the
    request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audits and reviews

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  ship manager of a Class 1 vessel must ensure that an internal audit
  and management review is carried out in accordance with section 12 of
  Part A of the ISM Code to verify whether the shore-based and on-board
  operations of the vessel comply with the procedures and practices
  described in the safety management system that is referred to in
  subsection 100(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the audits and
  management reviews are carried out at frequencies determined in
  accordance with paragraph 12.1 of Part A of the ISM Code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to personnel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  audits and management reviews for an area of operation are brought to
  the attention of all personnel that have responsibility in that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrective action plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that a corrective action
  plan to address any deficiency identified during an audit is developed
  and implemented in a timely manner after the day on which the
  deficiency is identified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of corrective action plan

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the corrective
  action plan identifies the cause of any deficiency and specifies a
  process to rectify it and the actions to be taken to prevent a
  recurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results kept and produced

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  audits and management reviews are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept for a period of at least 5 years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produced on the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized representative
and ship manager

- **[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is prohibited for the authorized representative or ship manager of a
  Class 1 vessel to operate the vessel, or permit the vessel to be
  operated, unless the ship manager

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a Document of Compliance or an Interim
    Document of Compliance that applies to the type of vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} holds a Safety Management Certificate or an Interim
    Safety Management Certificate that was issued in respect of the
    vessel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintains the safety management system referred to
    in subsection 100(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized
  representative, ship manager and master

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for the authorized representative,
  ship manager or the master of a Class 1 vessel to operate the vessel,
  or permit the vessel to be operated, unless the vessel is operated in
  accordance with the procedures and practices described in the safety
  management system that is referred to in subsection 100(1).

  on-board operations of the vessel within six months after the day on
  which the Class 3 Canadian Document of Compliance is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- Canadian Safety Management
Certificate

- **[[301]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-301 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, on application by a ship manager of a Class 3 vessel,
  issue a Canadian Safety Management Certificate in respect of the
  vessel to them if the Minister determines that the ship manager has
  developed a documented safety management system that meets the
  requirements of Part A of the ISM Code, other than sections 4 and 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the safety
  management system is implemented in respect of the on-board operations
  of the vessel within six months after the day on which the Canadian
  Safety Management Certificate is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents on board --- master

- **[[302]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-302 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a Class 3 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Document of Compliance that was issued
    to the vessel's ship manager is kept on board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Safety Management Certificate that was
    issued in respect of the vessel to the vessel's ship manager is kept
    on board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    is set out is kept on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager of a Class 3 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Document of Compliance that was issued
    to the ship manager is produced on the request of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Safety Management Certificate that was
    issued in respect of the vessel to the ship manager is produced on
    the request of the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    is set out is produced on the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audits and reviews

- **[[303]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-303 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  ship manager of a Class 3 vessel must ensure that an internal audit
  and management review is carried out to verify whether the shore-based
  and on-board operations of the vessel comply with the procedures and
  practices described in the safety management system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation intervals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the audits and
  management reviews are carried out at intervals not exceeding 12
  months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to personnel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  audits and management reviews for an area of operation are brought to
  the attention of all personnel that have responsibility in that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrective action plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that a corrective action
  plan to address any deficiency identified during an audit is developed
  and implemented in a timely manner after the day on which the
  deficiency is identified.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of corrective action plan

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the corrective
  action plan identifies the cause of any deficiency and specifies a
  process to rectify it and the actions to be taken to prevent a
  recurrence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results kept and produced

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  audits and management reviews are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept for a period of at least 5 years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produced on the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized representative
and ship manager

- **[[304]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-304 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is prohibited for the authorized representative or ship manager of a
  Class 3 vessel to operate the vessel, or permit the vessel to be
  operated, unless the ship manager

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a Class 2 or Class 3 Canadian Document of
    Compliance that applies to the type of vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} holds a Canadian Safety Management Certificate that
    was issued in respect of the vessel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintains the safety management system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized
  representative, ship manager and master

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for the authorized representative,
  ship manager or master of a Class 3 vessel to operate the vessel, or
  permit the vessel to be operated, after the time limit referred to in
  subsection 301(2) unless the vessel operates in accordance with the
  procedures and practices described in the safety management system.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Class 4 Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458638 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- Canadian Document of
Compliance

- **[[400]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-400 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, on application by a ship manager of a Class 4 vessel,
  issue a Class 4 Canadian Document of Compliance to them if the
  Minister determines that the ship manager has developed a documented
  safety management system that meets the requirements set out in
  sections 402 to 404.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, when issuing a Class 4 Canadian
  Document of Compliance, specify on the document the types of vessels
  to which it applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the safety
  management system is implemented in respect of the shore-based and
  on-board operations of the vessel within six months after the day on
  which the Class 4 Canadian Document of Compliance is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- Canadian Safety Management
Certificate

- **[[401]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-401 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must, on application by a ship manager of a Class 4 vessel,
  issue a Canadian Safety Management Certificate in respect of the
  vessel to them if the Minister determines that the ship manager has
  developed a documented safety management system that meets the
  requirements set out in sections 402 to 404.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the safety
  management system is implemented in respect of the on-board operations
  of the vessel within six months after the day on which the Canadian
  Safety Management Certificate is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety management system

**[[402]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-402 .sectionLabel}** The ship manager of a
Class 4 vessel must develop a documented safety management system that
includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a safety and environmental protection policy;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} instructions and procedures to ensure compliance with
  provisions of the Act and its regulations respecting the safe
  operation of the vessel and the protection of the environment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} defined levels of authority and lines of
  communication between, and among, shore-based and on-board personnel;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} procedures for reporting shipping casualties and
  situations of non-compliance with the requirements of the safety
  management system;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} procedures for preparing for and responding to
  emergency situations; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} procedures for internal reviews of the safety
  management system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Instructions and procedures --- paragraph
402(b)

**[[403]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-403 .sectionLabel}** The instructions and
procedures required under paragraph 402(b) must include instructions and
procedures for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipment inspection, maintenance and testing;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the vessel's seaworthiness and stability;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} voyage planning, safe navigation and handling of the
  vessel; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensuring safety at sea and preventing human injuries,
  loss of life and damage to the marine environment and to property.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures --- paragraph 402(e)

**[[404]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-404 .sectionLabel}** The procedures required
under paragraph 402(e) must include procedures for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} preparing for and responding to shipping casualties
  and personnel accidents;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} dealing with equipment failure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} responding to pollution incidents; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} reporting emergency situations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluations

- **[[405]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-405 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  ship manager of a Class 4 vessel must ensure that the effectiveness of
  the safety management system is evaluated in accordance with the
  procedures referred to in paragraph 402(f) to verify whether the
  shore-based and on-board operations comply with the procedures and
  practices described in the safety management system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation intervals

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the evaluations are
  carried out at intervals not exceeding 12 months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to personnel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  evaluation for an area of operation are brought to the attention of
  all personnel that have responsibility in that area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Results kept and produced

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager must ensure that the results of the
  evaluation are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} kept for a period of at least 5 years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} produced on the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents on board --- master

- **[[406]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-406 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a Class 4 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Document of Compliance that was issued
    to the vessel's ship manager is kept on board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Safety Management Certificate that was
    issued in respect of the vessel to the vessel's ship manager is kept
    on board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    is set out is kept on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ship manager of a Class 4 vessel must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Document of Compliance that was issued
    to the ship manager is produced on the request of the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Safety Management Certificate that was
    issued in respect of the vessel to the ship manager is produced on
    the request of the Minister; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the document in which the safety management system
    is set out is produced on the request of the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized representative
and ship manager

- **[[407]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-407 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It
  is prohibited for the authorized representative or ship manager of a
  Class 4 vessel to operate the vessel, or permit the vessel to be
  operated, unless the ship manager

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a Class 2, Class 3 or Class 4 Canadian
    Document of Compliance that applies to the type of vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} holds a Canadian Safety Management Certificate that
    was issued in respect of the vessel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} maintains the safety management system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- authorized
  representative, ship manager and master

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for the authorized representative,
  ship manager or master of a Class 4 vessel to operate the vessel, or
  permit the vessel to be operated, after the time limit referred to in
  subsection 401(2) unless the vessel operates in accordance with the
  procedures and practices described in the safety management system.
