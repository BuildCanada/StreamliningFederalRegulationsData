## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239315 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization
    referred to in paragraph 275(3)(b) of the
    Act. ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 1(1) of the [International and Interprovincial Power Line
    Damage Prevention Regulations ---
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-347). ([titulaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[international or interprovincial power line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international or interprovincial power
    line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility referred to in
    subsection 271(1) of the Act. ([ligne internationale ou
    interprovinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    section 2 of the [International and Interprovincial Power Line
    Damage Prevention Regulations ---
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-347). ([zone
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day that is not a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [One-call Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239324 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to be member

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  holder operates an international or interprovincial power line within
  a geographical area where there is a one-call centre, the holder must
  be a member of that centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One-call centre

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A one-call centre is an organization that, for the
  purposes of protecting the underground infrastructures of its members
  from damage and ensuring public safety,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receives locate requests from persons within a
    defined geographical area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notifies its members that may be affected by any
    proposed construction or by any proposed activity that would cause a
    ground disturbance, if that construction or activity is the subject
    of a locate request.

## [Authorizations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239332 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of holder

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A holder may give
authorizations under paragraph 275(1)(c), (d) or (f) of the Act and may
require that the authorization be subject to any conditions that the
holder considers appropriate.

## [Obligations Following Request to Locate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239335 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a holder receives a
request to locate the holder's international or interprovincial power
line from a person who intends to construct a facility across, on, along
or under an international or interprovincial power line or engage in an
activity that causes a ground disturbance within the prescribed area,
the holder must, within three working days, after the day on which the
request is made, or any longer period agreed to by the holder and that
person,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the person, in writing, of safety practices to
  be followed while working in the vicinity of the holder's
  international or interprovincial power line and, in case of a ground
  disturbance, within the prescribed area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the location of the underground portion of the
  holder's international or interprovincial power line in the vicinity
  of the proposed facility or the prescribed area using markings that
  are clearly visible and distinct from any other markings that may be
  in the vicinity of the proposed facility or the prescribed area; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide information to the person that clearly
  explains the significance of the markings.

## [Inspections by Holder]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239341 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections and field observations

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** To ensure the safety and
security of persons and the protection of property and the environment
in relation to an activity that causes a ground disturbance within the
prescribed area, the holder must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} conduct inspections in accordance with the conditions
  set out in the authorization and in any case in which the holder
  determines that an inspection is necessary;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect any underground portion of the holder's
  international or interprovincial power line that is exposed, to ensure
  that no damage to the line has occurred, before backfilling over it;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any inspection carried out under
  paragraph (a) or (b), make field observations relating to compliance
  with the applicable measures set out in the [International and
  Interprovincial Power Line Damage Prevention Regulations ---
  Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-347).

## [Obligation to Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239347 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Regulator

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Beginning with the year
2021, the holder must, no later than January 31 of each calendar year,
provide the Regulator a report for the previous calendar year that
contains the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of any contravention of the [International
  and Interprovincial Power line Damage Prevention Regulations ---
  Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-347);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} details of any damage to its international or
  interprovincial power lines, including the cause and nature of the
  damage and any related impacts on the reliability of an international
  or interprovincial power line;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any concerns that the holder may have regarding the
  international or interprovincial power line's safety, security or
  reliability as a result of the construction of the facility, the
  activity that causes a ground disturbance or the operation of vehicles
  or mobile equipment across the power line; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any action the holder has taken or intends to take or
  request in relation to the contravention or damage.

## [Management System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239354 .Part}

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1239355 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period --- construction and
activities

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder must maintain a record of all construction of facilities
  across, on, along or under its international or interprovincial power
  line and of all activities that cause a ground disturbance within the
  prescribed area for the life of that line.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must contain, for each facility or each
  activity that causes a ground disturbance,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the written authorization given by the
    holder;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the inspections referred to in
    paragraphs 5(a) and (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person that conducted the
      inspection,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the inspection, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} all findings and observations including any
      field observations referred to in paragraph 5(c); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the details of any abandonment, removal or
    alteration of the facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time period --- certain authorizations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if there is an expiry date
  set out in the authorization, the holder need only retain the record
  that contains the copy of the written authorization for a period of 12
  months from the day on which the authorization expires.

### [Damage Prevention Program]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1239369 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum elements

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder is required to develop, implement and maintain a damage
  prevention program that must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an ongoing public awareness program to inform the
    public

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the location of its international or
      interprovincial power line,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of the locations where the operation of vehicles
      or mobile equipment across the holder's international or
      interprovincial power lines could compromise the safety and
      security of persons or the safety or security of the power lines
      and their components,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} of the safety measures to be implemented at the
      locations referred to in subparagraph (ii) in order to reduce the
      risks of an incident resulting from the crossing of the power line
      at that location,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} on how to work safely near an international or
      interprovincial power line,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} on how to report an unexpected situation related
      to an international or interprovincial power line that could
      endanger life or cause substantial property or environmental
      damage and that requires immediate action,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} on how to report any contact with an
      international or interprovincial power line and if the
      international or interprovincial power line was damaged, the
      damage caused,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} of the services of a one-call centre, if there
      is one within the relevant geographical area,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} on the obligation to obtain an authorization
      before constructing a facility across, on, along or under an
      international or interprovincial power line, engaging in an
      activity that causes a ground disturbance within the prescribed
      area or operating vehicles or mobile equipment across an
      international or interprovincial power line, in the case of a
      vehicle or mobile equipment that is not operated within the
      travelled portion of a highway or public road,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} of the information to be provided in a request
      for authorization to construct a facility across, on, along or
      under an international or interprovincial power line, to engage an
      activity that causes a ground disturbance within the prescribed
      area or to operate a vehicle or mobile equipment across an
      international or interprovincial power line, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} on the obligation to make a locate request and on
      how to make a locate request;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a process to ensure a timely response to locate
    requests;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a process for locating and marking any underground
    portion of an international or interprovincial power line; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a process for managing requests for authorization
    to construct a facility across, on, along or under an international
    or interprovincial power line, to engage in an activity that causes
    a ground disturbance within the prescribed area or to operate a
    vehicle or mobile equipment across the international or
    interprovincial power line.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing damage prevention program

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A holder is not required to establish a new damage
  prevention program if the holder already has one, as required by the
  conditions of the certificate or permit, and that program meets the
  requirements of subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for compliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A holder must establish a damage prevention program
  within 12 months after the day on which the certificate or permit is
  issued or within a time limit set out in the conditions of the
  certificate or permit.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239391 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damage prevention program

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A holder that has been
issued a certificate or permit, authorizing the holder to construct or
operate an international or interprovincial power line, under the
[National Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7) before the coming into force
of these Regulations, must establish a damage prevention program within
12 months after the day on which these Regulations come into force or
within a time limit specified or set out in the conditions in the
certificate or permit.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1239394 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[S.C. 2019, c. 28, s.
10]{.HistoricalNote}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
