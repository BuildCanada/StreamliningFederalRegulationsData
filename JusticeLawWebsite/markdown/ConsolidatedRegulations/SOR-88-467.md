## Ginn and Company (Canada) and GLC Publishers Limited Acquisition of Shares Order {#ginn-and-company-canada-and-glc-publishers-limited-acquisition-of-shares-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-467
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-09-06

Order Authorizing the Acquisition, from Gulf + Western Industries, Inc.
or any of its Subsidiaries, by the Canada Development Investment
Corporation of Shares Representing Fifty-one Per Cent of a Corporation
To Be Created which will own the Businesses of Ginn and Company
(Canada), Formerly a Division of Xerox Canada Inc., and GLC Publishers
Limited

P.C. 1988-1943 1988-09-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Canada Development Investment Corporation is a parent Crown
corporation within the meaning of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

And Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 101(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that the Canada
Development Investment Corporation is otherwise empowered under the
[Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44) to undertake the
transaction described in the annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1984, c. 31, s. 11
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the President of the Privy Council and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to paragraph 101(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0ELBAA} and subsection
101(7)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EJBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
revoke the Ginn and Company (Canada) Acquisition of Shares Order made by
Order in Council P.C. 1988-459 of March 10, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make the
annexed Order authorizing the acquisition, from Gulf + Western
Industries, Inc. or any of its subsidiaries, by the Canada Development
Investment Corporation of shares representing fifty-one per cent of a
corporation to be created which will own the businesses of Ginn and
Company (Canada), formerly a division of Xerox Canada Inc., and GLC
Publishers Limited, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_2e}SOR/88-177, 1988
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1662
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913548 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ginn and Company (Canada) and GLC Publishers Limited Acquisition of
Shares Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-467).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913550 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canada Development
Investment Corporation, a corporation incorporated under the laws of
Canada, is hereby authorized to acquire from Gulf + Western Industries,
Inc. or any of its subsidiaries, shares representing 51 per cent of a
corporation to be created which will own the businesses of Ginn and
Company (Canada), formerly a division of Xerox Canada Inc., and GLC
Publishers Limited.
