## Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to Subsection 23(1) of the Act {#order-acknowledging-receipt-of-the-assessments-done-pursuant-to-subsection-231-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-91
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2011-11-09

Order Acknowledging Receipt of the Assessments Done Pursuant to
Subsection 23(1) of the Act

P.C. 2011-1271 2011-10-27
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, hereby acknowledges receipt, on the
making of this Order, of assessments conducted under subsection 23(1) of
the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBAA} by the Committee on the
Status of Endangered Wildlife in Canada with respect to the species set
out in the annexed schedule.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBAA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::
:::
