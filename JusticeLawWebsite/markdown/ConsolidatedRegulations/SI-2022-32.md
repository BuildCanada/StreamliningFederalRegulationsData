## Certain Emergency Response Benefits Remission Order {#certain-emergency-response-benefits-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-32
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2022-06-22

Certain Emergency Response Benefits Remission Order

P.C. 2022-617 2022-06-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of certain amounts is unreasonable, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board and the Minister of Employment and Social
Development, pursuant to subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1111_hq_30457){#fn_81000-2-1111_hq_30457-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1111_hq_30458){#fn_81000-2-1111_hq_30458-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Certain Emergency Response Benefits Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2022-32).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- Canada emergency response
benefit

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission of the amount determined
  under subsection (2) is granted to any person who meets the following
  conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person received an income support payment under
    the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7) for a
    four-week period in which they were not eligible for the payment,
    and the person has repaid or is to repay the payment under
    subsection 12(1) of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for that four-week period, the person would have
    been eligible for a Canada emergency student benefit under the
    [Canada Emergency Student Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.75) if they had
    not received the income support payment for that period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not receive a Canada emergency
    student benefit for that period;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person applied for the income support payment
    for that period not later than September 30, 2020;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person files with the Minister of National
    Revenue, not later than December 31, 2022, a return of income under
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of each of the 2019
    and 2020 taxation years; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person applies for the remission in the form
    and manner specified by the Minister of Employment and Social
    Development.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount remitted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 3, the amount to be remitted is
  equivalent to the amount of the income support payment received by the
  person for the four-week period referred to in paragraph (1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- employment insurance
emergency response benefit

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission of the amount determined
  under subsection (2) is granted to any person who meets the following
  conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person received an employment insurance
    emergency response benefit under the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6) for a two-week period in which they were not
    eligible for the benefit and, under section 43 of that Act, the
    person has repaid or is to repay the benefit or, under section 44 of
    that Act, has returned or is to return the benefit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during a four-week period that includes at least
    one of the weeks in the two-week period referred to in paragraph
    (a), the person would have been eligible for a Canada emergency
    student benefit under the [Canada Emergency Student Benefit
    Act](/eng/acts/C-3.75) if they had not received an employment
    insurance emergency response benefit for any week in that four-week
    period;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person did not receive a Canada emergency
    student benefit for that four-week period;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person applied for an employment insurance
    emergency response benefit for the two-week period referred to in
    paragraph (a) not later than September 30, 2020;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the person files with the Minister of National
    Revenue, not later than December 31, 2022, a return of income under
    the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) in respect of each of the 2019
    and 2020 taxation years; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person applies for the remission in the form
    and manner specified by the Canada Employment Insurance Commission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount remitted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 3, the amount to be remitted is
  equivalent to the amount of the employment insurance emergency
  response benefit that the person has repaid, is to repay, has returned
  or is to return for the weeks included in a four-week period referred
  to in paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount to be remitted
to a person for a four-week period referred to in paragraph 1(1)(b) or
2(1)(b) must not exceed the amount of the Canada emergency student
benefit that the person would have received for that period if they had
applied for that benefit under section 5 of the [Canada Emergency
Student Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.75) and had not received any income
support payment or employment insurance emergency response benefit for
any part of that period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
