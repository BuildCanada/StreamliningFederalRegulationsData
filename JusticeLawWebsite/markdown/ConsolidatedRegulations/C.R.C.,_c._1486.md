## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521774 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[amidships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amidships]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Part 0.1, the mid-point of the hull length of
      a fishing vessel; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Parts I and II, the mid-point of the length of
      a fishing vessel. ([milieu du bâtiment]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[closed construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[closed construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing
    vessel, means that more than 50 per cent of the length of the vessel
    is covered full width, at or above the gunwale level, by decks or
    permanent enclosures. ([ponté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[existing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[existing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing vessel,
    means that the vessel is not new. ([existant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is used
    or is to be used for commercially catching, harvesting or
    transporting fish or other living marine resources. ([bâtiment de
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing vessel,
    means, in Parts I and II,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the distance from the fore part of the uppermost
      end of the stem to the aft side of the head of the stern post,
      except that if a stern post is not fitted to the vessel, the
      measurement shall be taken to the foreside of the head of the
      rudder stock; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has no rudder stock or has a rudder
      stock situated outside the hull at the stern, the distance from
      the foreside of the foremost permanent structure of the vessel to
      the aft side of the aftermost permanent structure of the vessel,
      not including guards or rubbing
      strakes. ([longueur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing vessel, means,
    in Parts I and II, that construction of the vessel started on or
    after January 6, 1965. This definition applies also to any foreign
    fishing vessel brought under Canadian registry, regardless of the
    date on which its construction started. ([neuf]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[open construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[open construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing
    vessel, means that the vessel is not one of closed
    construction. ([non ponté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TP 127]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TP 127]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Ship Safety Electrical
    Standards, issued by the Department of Transport, as amended from
    time to time. ([TP 127]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521791 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian vessels

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of fishing vessels that are Canadian vessels and that are not
more than 24.4 m in length and not more than 150 gross tonnage.

## [PART 0.1]{.HLabel1}[Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-521796 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[3.01]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.01
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in
  this Part.

  [[breadth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[breadth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum breadth of a
      fishing vessel, measured amidships to the moulded line of the
      frame in the case of a vessel with metal shell plating, and
      measured to the outer surface of the shell plating in any other
      case. ([largeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[classification society]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      classification society that is a member of the International
      Association of Classification Societies (IACS).  ([société de
      classification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[engine space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[engine space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any space that contains
      a permanently installed propulsion engine or auxiliary engine, and
      includes any connected spaces.  ([compartiment
      moteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[EPIRB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[EPIRB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an emergency
      position-indicating radio beacon. ([RLS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[hull length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hull length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing
      vessel, means the distance measured from the forward end of the
      foremost outside surface of the hull shell to the aft end of the
      aftermost outside surface of the hull shell. ([longueur de
      coque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[IMO Resolution MSC.81(70)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IMO Resolution MSC.81(70)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the annex
      to International Maritime Organization Resolution MSC.81(70),
      Revised Recommendation on Testing of Life-Saving
      Appliances. ([résolution MSC.81(70) de l'OMI]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inland voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
      internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[IS Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[IS Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the annex to International
      Maritime Organization Resolution MSC.267(85), International Code
      on Intact Stability, 2008. ([recueil IS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[lifebuoy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lifebuoy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a SOLAS lifebuoy or a small
      vessel lifebuoy. ([bouée de sauvetage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[lifejacket]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lifejacket]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a small vessel
      lifejacket, a standard lifejacket, a Class 1 or Class 2 lifejacket
      or a SOLAS lifejacket. ([gilet de sauvetage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[life raft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[life raft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a SOLAS life raft, a
      reduced capacity life raft or a coastal life raft. ([radeau de
      sauvetage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[LSA Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[LSA Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the annex to International
      Maritime Organization Resolution MSC.48(66), International
      Life-Saving Appliance (LSA) Code. ([recueil LSA]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[machinery space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[machinery space]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any space containing
      propelling machinery, steering gears, boilers, steam and internal
      combustion engines, generators and major electrical machinery, oil
      filling stations, refrigerating, stabilizing, ventilation and
      air-conditioning machinery, and any similar spaces and trunks to
      those spaces. ([tranche des machines]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[near coastal voyage, Class 1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage à proximité
      du littoral, classe 1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[near coastal voyage, Class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[near coastal voyage, Class 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage à proximité
      du littoral, classe 2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[near coastal voyage, Class 2, restricted to 2 nautical miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[near coastal voyage, Class 2, restricted to 2 nautical
      miles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a near coastal voyage, Class 2,
      during which the fishing vessel engaged on the voyage is always
      within 2 nautical miles from shore. ([voyage à proximité du
      littoral, classe 2, limité à 2 milles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing vessel, means
      that construction of the vessel started --- or that a contract was
      signed for the construction of the vessel or that the vessel was
      imported into Canada and registered for the first time in Canada
      --- more than one year after the day on which these Regulations
      come into force. ([neuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permanently installed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanently installed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      object, means securely fastened so that tools must be used for its
      removal. ([fixé à demeure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[power-driven]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[power-driven]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing
      vessel, means that the fishing vessel is propelled by an engine or
      has an engine on board to propel it. ([à propulsion
      mécanique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[product certification body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[product certification body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body
      that is accredited by the Standards Council of Canada, or by any
      other national accreditation organization that is a member of the
      International Accreditation Forum Multilateral Recognition
      Arrangement, to give third-party written assurance that a product
      meets the specified requirements for the product, including
      initial certification of the product and maintenance of that
      certification. ([organisme de certification de
      produits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pyrotechnic distress signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pyrotechnic distress signal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rocket
      parachute flare, a multi-star flare, a hand flare, or a buoyant or
      hand smoke signal. ([signal de détresse
      pyrotechnique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[readily accessible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[readily accessible]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means capable of being
      reached easily and safely under emergency conditions without the
      use of tools. ([facilement accessible]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[reboarding device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reboarding device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ladder, lifting
      harness or other apparatus, not including any part of a fishing
      vessel's propulsion unit, that assists a person to reboard the
      vessel from the water. ([dispositif de remontée à
      bord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[recommended practices and standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[recommended practices and standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the recommended practices and standards for marine use issued by a
      marine classification society, standards development organization,
      industrial or trade organization, government, government agency or
      international body. ([normes et pratiques
      recommandées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[recovery boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[recovery boat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a boat that is
      auxiliary to a fishing vessel and that can be used in an
      emergency. ([embarcation de récupération]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sheltered waters voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage en eaux
      abritées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention
      for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[sound-signalling device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sound-signalling device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pealess
      whistle or a compressed-gas or electric horn. ([dispositif de
      signalisation sonore]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[TP 1332]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[TP 1332]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Construction Standards
      for Small Vessels, published by the Department of Transport. ([TP
      1332]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[TP 14475]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[TP 14475]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Life Saving
      Appliance Standard, published by the Department of Transport. ([TP
      14475]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[unlimited voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the [Vessel Safety Certificates
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2021-135). ([voyage
      illimité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[watertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[watertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a structure,
      means capable of preventing the passage of water through the
      structure in any direction under a head of water for which the
      structure is designed. ([étanche à l'eau]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[weathertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[weathertight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a fishing
      vessel, means that in any sea conditions water will not penetrate
      into the vessel. ([étanche aux intempéries]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

### [Division 1]{.HLabel2}[General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-521849 .Subheading}

#### [Safe Operation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521850 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design, construction and equipment

- **[[3.03]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.03
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a
  fishing vessel shall ensure that the vessel is designed, constructed
  and equipped to operate safely and be seaworthy in its area of
  operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safe operation and seaworthiness

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe
  that the design, construction or equipment of a fishing vessel
  adversely affects its safe operation or seaworthiness in its area of
  operation, the Minister shall request the authorized representative of
  the vessel to establish that the vessel meets the requirements of
  subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance of machinery and equipment

- **[[3.04]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.04 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  fishing vessel, as well as its machinery and equipment, shall be
  maintained to ensure that it is in a safe operating condition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a fishing vessel
  shall maintain records on the maintenance of the vessel and of its
  machinery and equipment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [Prohibition --- Operational Limits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521865 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- freezing spray

- **[[3.05]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.05 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate, or permit another person to operate, a fishing
  vessel in an area for which a freezing spray warning has been issued
  by Environment Canada unless the stability assessment for the vessel
  has demonstrated that the vessel has the capability to operate safely
  in freezing spray conditions when ice accretion is likely to occur.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accumulated ice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a freezing spray warning has been issued by
  Environment Canada for an area in which a fishing vessel is operated
  or is intended to be operated, the vessel shall carry on board a means
  to remove accumulated ice from the vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [General Prohibitions]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521873 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Before first putting into service

- **[[3.06]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.06
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a
  fishing vessel shall not operate, or permit another person to operate,
  the fishing vessel unless, before the vessel is first put into
  service, its authorized representative has informed the Minister of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intention to operate the vessel or permit its
    operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the physical characteristics of the vessel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of its operation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information provided to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of a fishing vessel
  shall provide the Minister, on request, with information respecting
  the physical characteristics of the fishing vessel and the nature of
  its operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceeding design limitations

**[[3.07]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.07 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
operate, or permit another person to operate, a fishing vessel under
circumstances that exceed its design limitations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Careless operation

**[[3.08]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.08 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
operate a fishing vessel in a careless manner, without due care and
attention or without reasonable consideration for other persons.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety of persons on board jeopardized

**[[3.09]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.09 .sectionLabel}** No person shall
operate, or permit another person to operate, a fishing vessel in
environmental conditions or circumstances that could jeopardize the
safety of persons on board unless a lifejacket required by this Part, or
a personal flotation device that meets the requirements of section 3.2,
is worn

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by all persons on board, in the case of a fishing
  vessel that has no deck or deck structure; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by all persons on the deck or in the cockpit, in the
  case of a fishing vessel that has a deck or deck structure.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521898 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Openings closed at sea

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** When a fishing vessel
is at sea, openings on the vessel that are exposed to the weather and to
the sea and that can be closed shall be kept closed unless they must be
kept open for the operation of the vessel, in which case they shall be
closed immediately if there is a danger of water entering the interior
spaces of the hull.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stowage of tools and spare parts

**[[3.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.11 .sectionLabel}** Tools and spare parts
necessary for performing routine maintenance on and minor repairs to
machinery, electrical equipment and installations shall be carried on
board a fishing vessel and securely stowed in a readily accessible
location.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of modifications affecting
stability

**[[3.12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.12 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a fishing vessel shall ensure that a record is kept of
any modification or series of modifications that affects the stability
of the vessel. The record shall be in the form and manner specified by
the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [Fuel]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521911 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Engine space blower

**[[3.13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.13 .sectionLabel}** No person shall start
a gasoline-powered fishing vessel unless the engine space blower has
been operated for a period of not less than four minutes immediately
before the engine is started.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leakage of fuel

- **[[3.14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall permit fuel leakage within or from a fishing vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discharge of fuel or oil

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall permit fuel or oil to be discharged
  from a fishing vessel except in accordance with the provisions
  relating to discharges of oil or oily mixtures in section 5 of Part 1
  and Subdivision 4 of Division 1 of Part 2 of the [Vessel Pollution and
  Dangerous Chemicals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-69).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fuelling

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fuel a gasoline-powered fishing
  vessel that is at dockside or beached unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is equipped with a portable fuel
    tank, the tank is first removed from it; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is equipped with a fixed fuel tank,
    the person fuelling the vessel is the only person on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fuelling --- fixed fuel tank

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fuel a gasoline-powered fishing
  vessel that is equipped with a fixed fuel tank unless all electrical
  equipment is switched off, all doors, windows and ports are closed,
  all engines are shut off and all open flames, including pilot lights,
  are extinguished.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portable container

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall carry liquid fuel on board a fishing
  vessel in a portable container that has not been designed to carry the
  fuel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of portable fuel tank

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A portable fuel tank containing fuel and carried on
  board a fishing vessel shall be stored as far away as practicable from
  heat and ignition sources, machinery spaces and crew's quarters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filling of fixed fuel tank

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fill a fixed fuel tank on board a
  fishing vessel by means of a funnel, nozzle or similar device unless
  continuous contact is maintained between the shipboard filling pipe
  and the filling device immediately prior to and during the fuelling
  operation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portable fuel-burning equipment or
appliance

**[[3.15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.15 .sectionLabel}** Any portable
fuel-burning equipment or appliance used on a fishing vessel shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} used only in a well-ventilated location that is in an
  open space or on an open deck;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} well secured to prevent its movement while in use;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} when not in use, stored in a well-ventilated location
  that is isolated from heat and ignition sources.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [Safety Procedures]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521942 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written safety procedures

- **[[3.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Safety procedures shall be
  established in writing, in English or French or in both, according to
  the needs of the crew, and implemented to familiarize persons on board
  a fishing vessel with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the location and use of all safety equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all the measures that must be taken to protect
    persons on board, in particular measures to prevent persons from
    falling overboard, measures to retrieve persons who have fallen
    overboard, measures to protect limbs from rotating equipment, and
    measures to avoid ropes, docking lines, nets and other fishing
    equipment that may pose a safety hazard to persons on board;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of beam trawling and purse seining
    operations, the quick release of loads that can be activated in an
    emergency;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all the measures that must be taken to prevent
    fires and explosions on the vessel;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel has a deck or deck structure, all the
    measures that must be taken to maintain watertightness and
    weathertightness and to prevent flooding of the interior spaces of
    the hull or, if the vessel has no deck or deck structure, all the
    measures that must be taken to prevent swamping of the vessel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} all the measures that must be taken to ensure safe
    loading, stowage and unloading of fish catches, baits and
    consumables; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the operation of towing and lifting equipment and
    the measures that must be taken to prevent overloading of the
    vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drills on procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Drills on the safety procedures shall be held to
  ensure that the crew is at all times proficient in carrying out those
  procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of drills

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A record shall be kept of every drill.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [Records]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521959 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- maintenance and drills

- **[[3.17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record on the maintenance of a fishing vessel and a record of a drill
  on the safety procedures shall be kept for a period of seven years
  after the day on which it is established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of modifications affecting
  stability

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a fishing vessel that has undergone a
  stability assessment, a record of a modification or series of
  modifications that affects the stability of the vessel shall be kept
  until the vessel undergoes a new stability assessment that takes into
  account the modification or series of modifications.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of ownership

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} When ownership of a fishing vessel is transferred,
  the authorized representative of the vessel shall provide the new
  owner with any records kept in respect of the vessel.

### [Division 2]{.HLabel2}[Safety Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-521969 .Subheading}

#### [Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-521970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3.18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate, or permit another person to operate, a fishing
  vessel unless the safety equipment required by this Division is
  carried on board the vessel and the equipment meets the requirements
  of this Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement of safety equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, equipment that was acquired before the day
  on which this Division comes into force may replace any safety
  equipment required by this Division if the equipment meets the
  requirements of these Regulations as they read before that day and if
  the equipment is in good working order or, in the case of equipment
  that bears an expiry date, that date has not expired.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity in excess --- previously
  acquired equipment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any equipment that exceeds the quantity of safety
  equipment required and that was acquired before the day on which this
  Division comes into force may be carried on board a fishing vessel if
  the equipment meets the requirements of these Regulations as they read
  before that day and if the equipment is in good working order or, in
  the case of equipment that bears an expiry date, that date has not
  expired.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantity in excess --- recently
  acquired equipment

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any equipment that exceeds the quantity of safety
  equipment required and that was acquired on or after the day on which
  this Division comes into force may be carried on board a fishing
  vessel if the equipment meets the requirements of this Division.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other equipment

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Any equipment that is not of a type referred to in
  this Division may be carried on board a fishing vessel if the
  equipment is not likely to be confused with safety equipment.

- SOR/2016-163, s. 2

#### [Substitute Safety Equipment]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522003 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equivalent level of safety

- **[[3.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the Minister determines that there are circumstances in which
  equipment other than the safety equipment required by these
  Regulations provides a level of safety at least equivalent to that
  provided by the required safety equipment, the other equipment may be
  substituted for the required safety equipment in those circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} To determine the level of safety provided by the
  substitute equipment in the circumstances, the Minister shall assess
  the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the environmental conditions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the risks to which persons on board
    are exposed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the specific characteristics of the equipment;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the recommended practices and standards to which
    the equipment conforms;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the equipment will be used; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the ability of the equipment to protect a person
    from injury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mark or label

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The substitute equipment shall bear a mark or label
  indicating that it conforms to the recommended practices and standards
  applicable to that type of equipment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2

#### [First Aid Kit]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522038 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[3.24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.24 .sectionLabel}** A fishing vessel
shall carry on board one of the following first aid kits, which shall be
packed in a waterproof case that is capable of being tightly closed
after use:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a marine emergency first aid kit that contains the
  following:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an up-to-date first aid manual or up-to-date first
    aid instructions, in English and French,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 48 doses of analgesic medication of a non-narcotic
    type,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} six safety pins or one roll of adhesive first aid
    tape,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} one pair of bandage scissors or safety scissors,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} one resuscitation face shield,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} two pairs of examination gloves,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} 10 applications of antiseptic preparations,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} 12 applications of burn preparations,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} 20 adhesive plasters in assorted sizes,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} 10 sterile compression bandages in assorted sizes,

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} 4 m of elastic bandage,

  - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} two sterile gauze compresses,

  - [(xiii)]{.lawlabel} two triangular bandages, and

  - [(xiv)]{.lawlabel} a waterproof list of the contents, in English and
    French; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a first aid kit that meets the requirements of the
  [Maritime Occupational Health and Safety
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-120) or of provincial
  regulations governing workers' compensation, with the addition of a
  resuscitation face shield and two pairs of examination gloves if the
  kit is not required to contain them.

  size for each person on board, but the lifejacket shall not be a Class

  on board a bailer or a manual bilge pump.

  9 m shall carry on board a manual bilge pump.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fitted with or accompanied by a sufficient length
    of piping or hose to enable water to be pumped from the bilge space
    of the fishing vessel over the side of the vessel; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} capable of being operated from a position above the
    deck of the fishing vessel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classes of fires

**[[3.39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.39 .sectionLabel}** In any reference in
these Regulations to the classification of a portable fire extinguisher,
the letters in the classification refer to the following classes of
fires:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class A fires, namely, fires that involve combustible
  materials such as wood, cloth, paper, rubber and plastic;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class B fires, namely, fires that involve inflammable
  liquids, gases and greases;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Class C fires, namely, fires in energized electrical
  equipment, where the electrical non-conductivity of the extinguishing
  media is of importance; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Class K fires, namely, fires in cooking appliances
  that involve combustible cooking media such as vegetable or animal
  oils or fats.

### [Division 3]{.HLabel2}[Stability]{.HTitleText2} {#h-522260 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522261 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3.44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.44
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This Division applies in respect of
  a fishing vessel that is propelled or designed to be propelled by an
  engine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wooden vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Division does not apply in respect of a wooden
  fishing vessel that was designed to be human-powered but has been
  modified for propulsion by an outboard motor and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has no deck or deck structure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not mass produced; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has been constructed following traditional methods
    that have been proven to be effective and reliable over time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

#### [Stability Standards and Demonstration]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522272 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing vessels --- adequate stability

**[[3.45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.45 .sectionLabel}** The stability and, if
applicable, the buoyancy and flotation of an existing fishing vessel
that is not required to undergo a stability assessment shall be adequate
to safely carry out the vessel's intended operations.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New vessels of more than 6 m but not more
than 9 m

- **[[3.46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.46
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The stability of a new fishing
  vessel that has a hull length of more than 6 m and not more than 9 m
  shall conform to recommended practices and standards that are
  appropriate to the type of vessel and that take into account its
  intended operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demonstration of conformity with
  standards

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of the fishing vessel
  shall demonstrate, on the request of the Minister, that the stability
  of the vessel conforms to the selected recommended practices and
  standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Some activities --- consistency with
  good practices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the selected recommended practices and standards
  do not take into account some of the activities of the fishing vessel,
  its authorized representative shall demonstrate, on the request of the
  Minister, that the stability of the vessel is adequate to safely carry
  out those activities, using first principles of naval architecture,
  appropriate testing, or any other method that is consistent with good
  practices for assessing the stability of a fishing vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New vessels of not more than 6 m

- **[[3.47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.47
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The stability of a new fishing
  vessel that has a hull length of not more than 6 m shall conform to
  the standards for buoyancy, flotation and stability that are set out
  in Section 4 of TP 1332.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Demonstration of conformity with TP
  1332

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The authorized representative of the fishing vessel
  shall demonstrate, on the request of the Minister, that the stability
  of the vessel conforms to the standards for buoyancy, flotation and
  stability that are set out in Section 4 of TP 1332.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Some activities --- consistency with
  good practices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If Section 4 of TP 1332 does not contain standards
  respecting some of the activities of the fishing vessel, its
  authorized representative shall demonstrate, on the request of the
  Minister, that the stability of the vessel is adequate to safely carry
  out those activities, using first principles of naval architecture,
  appropriate testing, or any other method that is consistent with good
  practices for assessing the stability of a fishing vessel.

#### [Stability Assessment and Stability Standards]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522295 .Subheading}

##### [Stability Assessment]{.HTitleText4} {#h-522296 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stability assessment required

- **[[3.48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall operate, or permit another person to operate, a fishing
  vessel in the following cases unless the vessel has successfully
  undergone a stability assessment conducted by a competent person:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel has a hull length of more than 9 m and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is new, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it has undergone a major modification or a
      change in activity that is likely to adversely affect its
      stability;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is an existing vessel of closed
    construction, is of more than 15 gross tonnage, is used for catching
    herring or capelin and, during the period beginning on July 6, 1977
    and ending on the day before this Division comes into force,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its keel was laid,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it was registered under Part 2 of the [Canada
      Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), or under Part 1 the
      [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9), chapter S-9 of the Revised
      Statutes of Canada, 1985,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it was converted to herring or capelin fishing,
      or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} it underwent any modifications that adversely
      affected its stability characteristics; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the vessel is fitted with an anti-roll tank.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type of assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fishing vessel may undergo either a full or a
  simplified stability assessment, but shall undergo a full stability
  assessment

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is carrying fish in bulk that exhibit
    free surface effect, unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fish are carried in containers such as pails,
      boxes or tote tanks of which none exceed one third of the breadth
      of the vessel, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fish hold or deck is divided by two
      fishtight longitudinal divisions, secured in place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is carrying fish or liquids that
    exhibit free surface effect in containers such as live wells, tote
    tanks or tanked fish holds of which any exceed one third of the
    breadth of the vessel and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are not designed to be used at maximum capacity
      only,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are not filled before the vessel's departure or
      in calm waters, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are not fitted with an alarm to indicate when
      the tank is not at maximum capacity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is fitted with an anti-roll tank; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the vessel is new and has a hull length of more
    than 18 m.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Major modification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[major
  modification]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a modification or repair, or
  a series of modifications or repairs, that substantially changes the
  capacity or size of a fishing vessel or the nature of a system on
  board a fishing vessel, that affects its watertight integrity or its
  stability.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

##### [Stability Standards]{.HTitleText4} {#h-522324 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Simplified stability assessment

- **[[3.49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.49
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The stability of a fishing vessel
  that undergoes a simplified stability assessment shall conform to
  recommended practices and standards that are appropriate to the type
  of vessel and that take into account its intended operations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Some activities --- consistency with
  good practices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the selected recommended practices and standards
  do not take into account some of the activities of the fishing vessel,
  the impact of those activities on the stability of the vessel shall be
  assessed using first principles of naval architecture, appropriate
  testing, or any other method that is consistent with good practices
  for assessing the stability of a fishing vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Full stability assessment

- **[[3.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  stability of a fishing vessel that undergoes a full stability
  assessment shall conform to the applicable standards set out in
  Chapter 2 of Part A of the IS Code, in sections 2.1.1 to 2.1.4 of
  Chapter 2 of Part B of the IS Code, in Chapters 3, 6 and in sections
  8.1 to 8.4 in Chapter 8 of Part B of the IS Code, and in Annex 1 to
  the IS Code.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Some activities --- consistency with
  good practices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the standards set out in the IS Code do not take
  into account some of the activities of the fishing vessel, the impact
  of those activities on the stability of the vessel shall be assessed
  using first principles of naval architecture, appropriate testing, or
  any other method that is consistent with good practices for assessing
  the stability of a fishing vessel.

#### [Persons and Organizations Competent to Conduct a Stability Assessment]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522339 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory authorization --- full or
simplified stability assessment

**[[3.51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.51 .sectionLabel}** The following persons
and organizations are competent to conduct a full or simplified
stability assessment:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an engineer who is a member in good standing of the
  Ordre des ingénieurs du Québec or an association of professional
  engineers of a province of Canada or a state of the United States;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a classification society; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who has at least three years of
  post-secondary education in the field of naval architecture, who is a
  member in good standing of an order or an association of technologists
  or technicians of a province of Canada, and who has at least five
  years' experience in the marine transportation industry.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory authorization --- simplified
stability assessment

- **[[3.52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.52
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following persons are competent
  to conduct a simplified stability assessment if they have received
  training, from a training institution or any other organization, in
  the application of the standards used to conduct a simplified
  stability assessment and if they have practical experience in the
  application of those standards:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a marine surveyor who is a member in good standing
    of a national association of accredited or certified marine
    surveyors of Canada or of the United States; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a builder, manufacturer or rebuilder of fishing
    vessels.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Training with competent person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who meets the following requirements is also
  competent to conduct a simplified stability assessment:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has received training from a competent
    person in the application of the standards used to conduct a
    simplified stability assessment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has, after receiving the training,
    acquired practical experience in the application of those standards.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation by Minister --- full or

- **[[3.53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.53

  person or class of persons as competent to conduct a full or
  simplified stability assessment if the person or class of persons has
  received training in the application of the standards that will be
  used to conduct the assessment and has the knowledge and experience to
  conduct the assessment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contact information of applicant for
  designation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who applies to the Minister to be designated

#### [Obligations --- Competent Person]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522377 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessment of compliance

**[[3.57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.57 .sectionLabel}** A competent person
who conducts a stability assessment for a fishing vessel shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} verify whether the vessel conforms to the stability
  standards that are applied to the vessel and, if those standards do
  not take into account some of the activities of the vessel, assess the
  impact of those activities on the stability of the vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the authorized representative of the vessel,
  in English or French or in both, according to the needs of the crew,
  with a stability booklet in the case of a full stability assessment,
  or with a record of stability in the case of a simplified stability
  assessment, that sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the stability standards that were applied to the
    vessel,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} information, in the form and manner set out in the
    standards, respecting the stability characteristics of the vessel
    and, if those standards do not take into account some of the
    activities of the vessel, the results of the assessment of the
    impact of those activities on the stability of the vessel,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's safe operating limits, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a signed declaration confirming, on the basis of
    the information provided to the competent person by the authorized
    representative, that the stability characteristics of the vessel
    conform to the standards that were applied to vessel; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prepare a stability notice for the vessel that sets
  out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the stability standards that were applied to the
    vessel for the stability assessment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a graphical representation, including a
    description or legend, of the operational practices necessary to
    operate the vessel within the safe operating limits set out in the
    vessel's stability booklet or record of stability, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the vessel has
    been assessed for operations in freezing spray conditions.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2

#### [Population of Fishing Vessels]{.HTitleText3} {#h-522428 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to Minister --- group of
authorized representatives

- **[[3.63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  group of authorized representatives of fishing vessels may request to
  the Minister that a population of fishing vessels not be required to
  undergo a stability assessment if it demonstrates to the satisfaction
  of the Minister that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each vessel in the population is similar to a
    vessel representative of the population that has successfully
    undergone a full stability assessment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} not requiring a stability assessment for each
    vessel will not decrease the level of safety of the population.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors --- similar vessels

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A fishing vessel in a population of fishing vessels
  is similar to the vessel representative of the population if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is operated or is to be operated in the same
    fishery, in the same environmental conditions and with the same
    fishing gear as the representative vessel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its physical characteristics are similar to those
    of the representative vessel; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its stability characteristics are equivalent to
    those set out in the stability booklet of the representative vessel.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of request

**[[3.64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.64 .sectionLabel}** The request referred
to in section 3.63 shall be submitted in the form and manner established
by the Minister, and shall include the following documents and
information in order to demonstrate to the satisfaction of the Minister
that the conditions set out in paragraphs 3.63(1)(a) and (b) are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} data relating to any accidents and incidents that
  have been reported under the [Transportation Safety Board
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-446) in respect of each vessel in
  the population and in respect of any other fishing vessel similar to
  the representative vessel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the stability booklet of the representative
  vessel;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an analysis of the likelihood that a
  stability-related accident or incident will occur if a stability
  assessment is not conducted for each vessel in the population, which
  analysis shall be based on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the similarity of each vessel in the population to
    the representative vessel,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the risks to which the vessels in
    the population and persons on board are exposed,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the accident and incident history of the vessels
    in the population and any other similar vessels,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the operating parameters of each vessel in the
    population, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in the stability booklet
    of the representative vessel; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that are proposed in
  order to decrease the likelihood, or mitigate the consequences, of a
  stability-related accident or incident identified in the analysis.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-163, s. 2
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister --- assessment of request

- **[[3.65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  determine whether the fishing vessels in a population of fishing
  vessels are not required to undergo a stability assessment, the
  Minister must be satisfied, on the basis of the documents and
  information submitted in the request, and the extent to which the
  operating parameters of each vessel in the population are equivalent
  to those of the representative vessel, that the conditions set out in
  paragraphs 3.63(1)(a) and (b) are met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister determines that the conditions set
  out in paragraphs 3.63(1)(a) and (b) are met, the Minister shall send
  to the authorized representative of each fishing vessel in the
  population of fishing vessels a document informing the authorized

### [Bilge Pumping Arrangements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-522488 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  fishing vessel not exceeding 15.2 m in length shall be provided with
  one bilge power or hand pump having a capacity of not less than 1.14
  L/s.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every fishing vessel exceeding 15.2 m and not
  exceeding 19.8 m in length shall be provided with two bilge pumps,
  each having a capacity of not less than 1.14 L/s, one of which shall
  be a power pump and the other either a power pump or a hand pump, but
  where two power pumps are provided, they are not to be driven by the
  same engine.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), every fishing vessel
  exceeding 19.8 m and not exceeding 24.4 m in length shall be provided
  with at least two bilge pumps, each having a capacity of not less than
  2.28 L/s, one of which shall be a power pump and the other either a
  power pump or a hand pump, but where two power pumps are provided,
  they are not to be driven by the same engine.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Two or more pumps, each with a capacity of not less
  than 1.14 L/s, may, if they can be used simultaneously, be provided on
  a fishing vessel in lieu of one only of the pumps required by
  subsection (3); if the pumps provided under this subsection are power
  pumps, they may be driven from the one engine, but not from the engine
  that is used to drive the mandatory power pump.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (6), bilge piping arrangements
  on a fishing vessel shall be such that each of the bilge pumps
  required by subsection (1), (2), (3) or (4) can draw water from each
  of the bilge suctions required by subsection (7) or (8).

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Bilge piping and pumping arrangements that provide a
  piping and pumping system at least as efficient as those described in
  subsection (5) may be provided on a fishing vessel.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Bilge suctions and means for drainage on a fishing
  vessel that is divided into watertight compartments shall be so
  arranged that any water that enters any main watertight compartment
  can be pumped out, through at least one bilge suction situated in that
  compartment, and all compartments within each main division shall be
  arranged to drain to that bilge suction.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Bilge suctions and means for drainage on a fishing
  vessel that is not divided into watertight compartments shall be so
  arranged that any water that enters the vessel is able to drain to at
  least one bilge suction.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Bilge piping on a fishing vessel shall have an
  internal diameter of not less than 38 mm except in the case of bilge
  piping on a fishing vessel not exceeding 15.2 m in length in which
  fish sludge, scale or offal is not usually pumped, in which case the
  bilge piping shall have an internal diameter of not less than 25 mm.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} Hand bilge pumps on a fishing vessel shall be
  capable of being worked from a position above the load water line.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Each bilge branch suction line on a fishing vessel,
  except hand pumps with only one suction, shall be fitted with a stop
  valve that is readily accessible at all times.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} Where a bilge pump on a fishing vessel is fitted
  with a connection to the sea, a non-return valve or swing check valve
  that is readily accessible shall be fitted between the sea connection
  and the bilge main so that sea water cannot flow into the bilges when
  sea connection and bilge valves are open.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} Bilge suctions that lead into crew spaces or engine
  rooms of a fishing vessel shall be fitted with strainers and the area
  of the openings of such strainers shall not be less than twice the
  cross-sectional area of the bilge pipe.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (15), the piping for bilge
  pumps on a fishing vessel shall be of steel, bronze or other material
  that is suitable for the purpose, and the joints for such piping shall
  be flanged or screwed.

- [(15)]{.lawlabel} Where it is necessary to reduce the effect of
  vibration, short lengths of rubber or plastic hose may be installed
  and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be clearly visible at all times;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be of sufficient strength to withstand collapsing
    due to suction; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have joints made with clamps suitable for the
    purpose.

### [Exhaust Pipes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-522608 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Exhaust pipes from the main engines
  and from auxiliary engines of a fishing vessel shall be permanently
  mounted and shall lead to the open air outside the vessel through the
  uppermost deck or canopy or through the side.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the exhaust pipes referred to in subsection (1)
  pass through the uppermost deck or canopy, they shall be of sufficient
  height to ensure that exhaust gases are prevented from escaping into
  the fishing vessel.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the exhaust pipes referred to in subsection (1)
  pass through the side of a fishing vessel, the connection shall be
  watertight and provision shall be made to prevent the engine from
  being flooded.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All exhaust pipes on a fishing vessel shall be well
  secured, shall be clear of all woodwork and other combustible
  materials, and, if there is a risk of contact with heated surfaces,
  shall be covered with lagging.

  or cocks shall be fitted to all suctions and discharges that pass
  through the hull of a fishing vessel below the weatherdeck.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pump discharges of 38 mm or less in internal
    diameter that are situated above the load water line;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} keel cooling systems; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} scuppers that pass from the weatherdeck to the
    ship's side above the load water line.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The cocks or valves required by subsection (1) shall
  be fitted as close to the side of the fishing vessel as possible.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Suction and discharge valves and cocks on a steel
  fishing vessel shall be attached to the plating of the hull or to the
  plating of fabricated water boxes by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bolts with countersunk heads tapped through the
    plating, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} studs that are screwed into heavy steel pads welded
    or riveted to the plating and that do not penetrate the plating,

  and such valves or cocks shall be fitted with spigots passing through
  the plating.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Suction and discharge valves and cocks on a wooden
  fishing vessel shall be attached to the hull by the methods shown in
  Schedule VII or by any other method that conforms to the recommended
  practices and standards.

### [Shafting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-522665 .Subheading}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where the
  propulsion shafting of a fishing vessel is driven by a diesel or a
  gasoline engine, the intermediate shaft shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be made of material having a tensile strength of
    not less than 372 MPa; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a diameter not less than that obtained by the
    following formula:

    ::: {#522670 .Paragraph}
    d = 106.41 cubic root (H/R) x \[cubic root (414/S) or 0.85,
    whichever is the greater\]

    where

    [d =]{.dfn}
    :   diameter of the intermediate shaft in millimetres,

    [H =]{.dfn}
    :   brake kilowatt power of the engine at continuous rating,

    [R =]{.dfn}
    :   revolutions per minute of the intermediate shaft at continuous
        rating,

    [S =]{.dfn}
    :   tensile strength of the intermediate shaft material in
        megapascals.
    :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a fishing vessel is operated exclusively in
  fresh water, the diameter of the intermediate shaft may be 1.75 per
  cent less than the diameter obtained by the formula set out in
  subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the propulsion shafting of a fishing vessel is
  not driven by a diesel or gasoline engine, the size of the
  intermediate shaft shall conform to the recommended practices and

### [Precautions Against Fire]{.HTitleText2} {#h-522861 .Subheading}

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  permanent fuel tank on a fishing vessel shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} bonded electrically to the fuel piping system and
    engine; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} installed and maintained so as to prevent fuel
    leakage.

- [(2) to (5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2016-163, s. 27\]]{.Repealed}

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Every inboard gasoline-fuelled engine on a fishing
  vessel shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be equipped to prevent fuel leakage into bilges or
    enclosed spaces;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a wire gauze-covered drip pan located below
    the carburetor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the engine is located below deck or in an
    enclosure where fuel vapour will not readily dissipate, have an air
    intake arrangement that will arrest back-fire flame; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the engine is located in a space required by
    subsection (8) to be mechanically ventilated, have a starting switch
    arrangement that will cause the ventilation fan to operate for a
    suitable period prior to engine ignition.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Every space on a fishing vessel in which machinery is
  located shall be ventilated so that in all weather conditions where
  the engines and other fuel-burning appliances are operating at full
  power, an adequate supply of air is maintained to ensure the safety
  and comfort of the crew and the operation of the engines and
  appliances.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} A mechanical ventilation system shall be installed in
  a fishing vessel in accordance with subsection (9) in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every space that is below deck and in which a
    gasoline-fuelled engine is located; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every other space, including bilges, in which
    gasoline fuel vapours may collect and may not readily dissipate.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} The mechanical ventilation system referred to in
  subsection (8) shall meet the following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the system shall include both inlet and exhaust
    ducts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust duct opening shall be located in a low
    position in the space that permits the most efficient ventilation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exhaust duct outlet shall lead to a location on
    the open deck where fumes may safely dissipate;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fan in the exhaust duct shall be spark proof;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the fan motor is located in an area where
    ignitable vapours may be generated or collect, the fan motor shall
    be spark proof.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} An appliance that burns liquid or gas fuel shall be
  so installed that air circulates freely all around the appliance.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} Insulation to prevent combustion shall be placed
  between woodwork or other inflammable material and adjacent surfaces
  of engine exhaust piping, heating appliances or other components that
  may become heated.

ship. The layers shall be not less in thickness than that set out in the
table. The bulkheads shall be efficiently stiffened and connected. To
ensure watertightness felt or canvas shall be fitted between the two
layers of planking.

## [SCHEDULE VIII]{.scheduleLabel}[(s. 24.1)]{.OriginatingRef}[Portable Fish Hold Divisions]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-522955 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** Every portable fish hold division shall be fishtight and shall
  extend from the bottom of the hold to the deckhead, with due regard to
  the loading and unloading of the vessel.

- **2** Provision shall be made to allow water to drain into the bilge,
  and bilge suctions shall be adequately protected to prevent clogging.

- **3** Portable penboards shall be installed in such a manner as to
  prevent dislodgment when fish are being pumped into and out of the
  hold.

- **4** Where wooden stanchions are installed, the penboards shall be
  secured in such a manner as to withstand the loads imposed.

- **5** Every portable fish hold division shall be installed in such a
  manner as to prevent overloading or excessive trim, and to the
  satisfaction of the Inspector.

- **6** Where an aluminum penboard or stanchion is installed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it shall be constructed of a salt water resistant
    alloy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the scantlings shall be compatible with the size of
    the fish hold; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the requirements of paragraphs (a) and (b) shall be
    complied with to the satisfaction of the Inspector.

- - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The scantlings of every wooden fish hold
    division shall be at least in accordance with the formulae set out
    in subsection (2), as illustrated in Figure 1, provided that in the
    case of a fish hold not exceeding 2 m in depth, a 100 mm × 100 mm
    wooden stanchion and a 38 mm penboard may be used.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The formulae for vertical steel uprights and
    horizontal wooden boards are as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} minimum section modulus of every vertical steel
      upright,

      ::: {#522970 .Paragraph}
      z = 4psbh^2^;
      :::

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} minimum actual finished thickness of every
      horizontal wooden board,

      ::: {#522973 .Paragraph}
      t = sqrt 8psb^2^; and
      :::

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the above formulae,

      - [z]{.lawlabel} = section modulus, in cubic centimetres

      - [t]{.lawlabel} = thickness of wooden board, in centimetres

      - [p]{.lawlabel} = density of cargo, in tonnes per cubic metre

      - [s]{.lawlabel} = maximum transverse distance between any two
        adjacent longitudinal divisions or line of supports, in metres

      - [h]{.lawlabel} = maximum vertical span of a column taken to be
        the hold depth, in metres

      - [b]{.lawlabel} = maximum longitudinal distance between any two
        adjacent transverse divisions or line of supports, in metres.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The formulae set out in subsection (2) shall be
    applied subject to the following conditions:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a longitudinal fish hold division is
      athwartship, the formulae shall be modified by interchanging s and
      b;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when it is known that a longitudinal or
      transverse division will always be loaded on both sides, reduced
      scantlings may be accepted;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if a vertical steel upright is permanent and well
      connected at both ends to the structure of the vessel, reduced
      scantlings may be accepted if there is sufficient security
      provided by the end connections;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the timber used shall be of sound durable
      quality, and of a type and grade that has been proved satisfactory
      for a fish hold division.

## [SCHEDULE X]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 3.19(2))]{.OriginatingRef}[Safety Equipment Standards and Tests]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-522995 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Immersion Suits]{.HTitleText2} {#immersion-suits .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The standards and tests for an immersion suit are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those set out in sections 3 to 9 of Canadian
    General Standards Board Standard CAN/CGSB-65.16-2005, Immersion Suit
    Systems; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those set out in section 2.3 of the LSA Code, when
    the immersion suit is tested in accordance with section 3 of Part 1
    of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70) without the use of a lifejacket.

#### [Anti-exposure Suits]{.HTitleText2} {#anti-exposure-suits .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** The standards and tests for an anti-exposure suit are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those set out in sections 9.2 to 9.4 and 9.6 of
    Canadian General Standards Board Standard CAN/CGSB-65.21-95, Marine
    Anti-exposure Work Suit Systems;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those set out in Canadian General Standards Board
    Standard CAN/CGSB-65.7-2007, Life Jackets, in the case of a Class 1
    or Class 2 lifejacket that provides Category IV thermal protection;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} those set out in section 2.4 of the LSA Code, when
    the anti-exposure suit is tested in accordance with section 3 of
    Part 1 of IMO Resolution MSC.81(70).

#### [Recovery Boats]{.HTitleText2} {#recovery-boats .SchedHeadL2}

- **3** The standards for a recovery boat are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those set out in Part B and Part C of TP 14475; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those set out in TP 1332, for non-pleasure craft,
    in the case of a recovery boat that is motorized or built to
    accommodate a motor.

#### [Emergency Boats and Rescue Boats]{.HTitleText2} {#emergency-boats-and-rescue-boats .SchedHeadL2}

- **4** The standards and tests for an emergency boat and a rescue boat
  are those set out in the LSA Code and TP 14475.
