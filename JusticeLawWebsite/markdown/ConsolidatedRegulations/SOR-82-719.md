## Nova Scotia Wheat Order {#nova-scotia-wheat-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-719
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1982-07-23

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Wheat Produced in Nova Scotia

P.C. 1982-2159 1982-07-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Order granting authority to regulate the marketing in
interprovincial and export trade of wheat produced in Nova
Scotia](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-719).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874819 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Nova Scotia Wheat Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-719).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874821 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Nova Scotia; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nova Scotia Winter Grain
    Marketing Board; ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    wheat established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wheat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wheat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means winter wheat or rye produced in
    Nova Scotia. ([blé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874827 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board is authorized to
regulate the marketing of wheat in interprovincial and export trade and
for such purposes may, by order or regulation, with respect to persons
and property situated within the Province of Nova Scotia, exercise all
or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of wheat locally within that province under the Act and the
Plan.
