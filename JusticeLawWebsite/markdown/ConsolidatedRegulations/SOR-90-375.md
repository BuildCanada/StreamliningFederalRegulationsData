## Canadian National Railway Company Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries Incorporation Authorization Order, 1990 {#canadian-national-railway-company-wholly-owned-subsidiaries-incorporation-authorization-order-1990 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-375
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-06-21

Order Authorizing the Canadian National Railway Company to Procure the
Incorporation of Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries

P.C. 1990-1187  1990-06-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 91(6) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that the
Canadian National Railway Company is empowered to undertake the
transactions described in the annexed Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to paragraphs 91(1)(a) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order
authorizing the Canadian National Railway Company to procure the
incorporation of wholly-owned subsidiaries.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian National Railway Company Wholly-Owned Subsidiaries
Incorporation Authorization Order, 1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-375).

## [Authorized Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926193 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Canadian National
Railway Company is hereby authorized to procure the incorporation of two
wholly-owned subsidiaries, 173335 CANADA INC and 173336 CANADA INC,
under the [Canada Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44).

::: HistoricalNote
- 1994, c. 24, s. 34(F)
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The transactions described
in this Order shall be completed not later than December 31, 1990.
