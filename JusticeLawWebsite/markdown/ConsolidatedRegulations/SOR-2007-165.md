## [Direction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734727 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The requirements of
subsections 24(1) and (2) of the [Auditor General Act](/eng/acts/A-17)
apply in respect of the departments set out in the schedule.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734729 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Direction comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
