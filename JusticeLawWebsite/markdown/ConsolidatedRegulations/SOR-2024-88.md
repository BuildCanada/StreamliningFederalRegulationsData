## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453511 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Same meaning

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Words and expressions used
in this Marketing Authorization have the same meaning as in the [Food
and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453514 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Yogurt

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Yogurt
  --- except drinkable yogurt --- that is made from dairy products is
  exempt from the application of paragraphs 4(1)(a) and (d) of the [Food
  and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) and section D.03.002 of the [Food and
  Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), in respect of the
  vitamin D that is present in the food, if the applicable condition in
  subsection (2) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The food must contain the following amount of vitamin
  D:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of plain yogurt, 5 μg of vitamin D per
    100 g; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of fruit or flavoured yogurt, not less
    than 3.8 μg and not more than 5 μg of vitamin D per 100 g.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Drinkable Yogurt

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Drinkable yogurt that is made from
  dairy products is exempt from the application of paragraphs 4(1)(a)
  and (d) of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27) and section
  D.03.002 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), in respect of the
  vitamin D that is present in the food, if the applicable condition set
  out in subsection (2) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The food must contain the following amount of vitamin
  D:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of plain drinkable yogurt, 5.2 μg of
    vitamin D per 100 mL; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of fruit or flavoured drinkable yogurt,
    not less than 3.9 μg and not more than 5.2 μg of vitamin D per
    100 mL.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Kefir

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Kefir
  that is made from dairy products is exempt from the application of
  paragraphs 4(1)(a) and (d) of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27)
  and section D.03.002 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), in respect of the
  vitamin D that is present in the food, if the applicable condition set
  out in subsection (2) is met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The food must contain the following amount of vitamin
  D:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of plain kefir, 2.7 μg of vitamin D per
    100 mL; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of fruit or flavoured kefir, not less
    than 2.3 μg and not more than 2.7 μg of vitamin D per 100 mL.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1453536 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Marketing
Authorization comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
