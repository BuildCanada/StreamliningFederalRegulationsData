## Order Giving Notice that a Tax Convention Between Canada and the United Mexican States Came into Force May 11, 1992 {#order-giving-notice-that-a-tax-convention-between-canada-and-the-united-mexican-states-came-into-force-may-11-1992 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-110
:::

[AGREEMENTS AND CONVENTIONS](/eng/acts/Z-02)

CANADA-MEXICO INCOME TAX CONVENTION ACT, 1991

Registration 1992-06-17

Order Giving Notice that a Tax Convention Between Canada and the United
Mexican States Came into Force May 11, 1992
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Notice is hereby given, pursuant to section 18 of the Canada-Mexico
Income Tax Convention Act, 1991[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-110_e_hq_6217){#fn_SI-92-110_e_hq_6217-ID0EBBCA},
that the Convention between the Government of Canada and the Government
of the United Mexican States For the Avoidance of Double Taxation and
the Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with respect to Taxes on Income came
into force on May 11, 1992.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-110_e_hq_6217-ID0EBBCA){#fn_SI-92-110_e_hq_6217}S.C.
  1992, c. 3, Part III
  :::

Ottawa, June 3, 1992

::: FlushRight
DON MAZANKOWSKI
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Finance*
