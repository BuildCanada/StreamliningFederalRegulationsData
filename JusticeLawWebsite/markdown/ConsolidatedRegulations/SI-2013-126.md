## Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories (Dehcho Region) Order {#withdrawal-from-disposal-of-certain-tracts-of-territorial-lands-in-the-northwest-territories-dehcho-region-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-126

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2013-12-18

Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Tracts of Territorial Lands in the
Northwest Territories (Dehcho Region) Order

P.C. 2013-1306 2013-11-28
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 23(a) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_5716_hq_12435){#fn_5716_hq_12435-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Withdrawal from Disposal of Certain Tracts of
Territorial Lands in the Northwest Territories (Dehcho Region)
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2013-126).

## [Purpose]{.HTitleText1} {#h-626477 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The purpose of this Order
is to withdraw from disposal certain tracts of territorial lands to
facilitate the settlement of Aboriginal land and resource agreements in
the Northwest Territories.

## [Lands Withdrawn from Disposal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-626479 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The tracts of territorial
lands set out in Schedule 1, including the surface and subsurface rights
to the lands, and the tracts of territorial lands set out in Schedule 2,
including the subsurface rights to the lands, are withdrawn from
disposal for a period of two years beginning on the day on which this
Order is made.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-626481 .Part}

### [Disposition of Substances or Materials]{.HTitleText2} {#h-626482 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not apply to
the disposition of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} substances or materials under the [Territorial
  Quarrying Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1527); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} interests in land listed in the Acquisition
  Agreement, dated May 5, 1988 between Her Majesty the Queen in right of
  Canada and the Government of the Northwest Territories, the Northern
  Canada Power Commission and the Northwest Territories Power
  Corporation.

### [Existing Rights and Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-626486 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
section 2 does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the locating of a mineral claim by the holder of a
  prospecting permit that was granted before the day on which this Order
  is made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recording of a mineral claim that is referred to
  in paragraph (a) or that was located before the day on which this
  Order is made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the granting of a lease under the [Northwest
  Territories and Nunavut Mining
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1516) to a person with a
  recorded claim, if the lease covers an area in the recorded claim;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a significant discovery licence under
  the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of
  an exploration licence that was issued before the day on which this
  Order is made, if the significant discovery licence covers an area
  that is subject to the exploration licence;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a production licence under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of a
  significant discovery licence that is referred to in paragraph (d), if
  the production licence covers an area that is subject to the
  significant discovery licence;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a production licence under the
  [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5) to a holder of an
  exploration licence or a significant discovery licence that was issued
  before the day on which this Order is made, if the production licence
  covers an area that is subject to the exploration licence or the
  significant discovery licence;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the issuance of a surface lease under the
  [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7) to a holder of a recorded claim
  under the [Northwest Territories and Nunavut Mining
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1516) or of an interest under
  the [Canada Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), if the surface
  lease is required to allow the holder to exercise rights under the
  claim or interest; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the renewal of an interest.
