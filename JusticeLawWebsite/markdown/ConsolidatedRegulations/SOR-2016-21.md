## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833159 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada--Newfoundland and
    Labrador Atlantic Accord Implementation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-7.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[actual full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[actual full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the full cost
    confirmed by the Board's audited financial statements. ([coût entier
    réel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    activities that are required for the Board to fulfill its regulatory
    responsibilities such as assessing applications, issuing licences,
    granting approvals and authorizations, verifying and enforcing
    compliance with the Act and providing information, products and
    services. ([activités de réglementation directes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[indirect regulatory costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indirect regulatory costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the costs
    that support the Board's direct regulatory activities such as office
    accommodation, supplies and equipment, professional services,
    communications, travel, management, training, administration, human
    resources services, finance, information technology services,
    hardware and software, the preparation of documents (including
    policies, standards, guidelines, procedures and notices) and the
    provision of technical expertise (including any advice relating to
    legislation or regulations) to the Federal Minister or the
    Provincial Minister at that Minister's request. ([coûts de
    réglementation indirects]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the work or the activity
    referred to in paragraph 138(1)(b) of the
    Act. ([projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Part 1]{.HLabel1}[Regulatory Activity Plan Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833167 .Part}

### [Estimated annual charge]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833168 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory activity plan

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For each new project
relating to development, production, abandonment, exploratory drilling
or multi-year or complex seismic programs in respect of petroleum
operations, on receipt of a project description or letter of intent, the
Board must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a regulatory activity plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate the estimated annual charge payable by the
  applicant or the operator for the project by determining the estimated
  full cost, including indirect regulatory costs, associated with the
  implementation of the regulatory activity plan based on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the estimated total number of units of
    time necessary to be spent in that fiscal year on direct regulatory
    activities for the project, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other costs, excluding costs calculated under
    other cost recovery methods; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the applicant or the operator, in writing, of
  the regulatory activity plan and the estimated annual charge payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Existing project

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For each existing project
that was previously under a regulatory activity plan, after approval of
the Board's budget in any given fiscal year following its submission in
accordance with subsection 27(2) of the Act, the Board must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prepare a new regulatory activity plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate the estimated annual charge payable by the
  applicant or the operator for the project by determining the estimated
  full cost, including indirect regulatory costs, associated with the
  implementation of thenew regulatory activity plan based on

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the cost of the estimated total number of units of
    time necessary to be spent in that fiscal year on direct regulatory
    activities for the project, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other costs, excluding costs calculated under
    other cost recovery methods; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the applicant or the operator, in writing, of
  the new regulatory activity plan and the estimated annual charge
  payable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recalculation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If an applicant or operator
proposes changes to its project that are not reflected in the regulatory
activity plan, the Board may recalculate the estimated annual charge for
that project and adjust the payable amount accordingly.

### [Quarterly Invoicing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833185 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Invoice

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must, on a quarterly basis, prepare and send an invoice for an
  amount equal to 25% of the estimated annual charge payable to each
  applicant or operator who has been notified under paragraph 2(c) or
  3(c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment within 30 days

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the date of the invoice, the
  applicant or the operator must pay the amount invoiced.

### [Annual Charge Adjustment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833191 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  year, following the end of the fiscal year, the Board must, for each
  project under a regulatory activity plan,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} calculate the actual full cost associated with the
    implementation of the regulatory activity plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate the charge adjustment, if any, by
    subtracting the estimated annual charge, calculated in accordance
    with paragraph 2(b) or 3(b), from the actual full cost; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the applicant or the operator in writing of
    the amount of the actual full cost and the amount of the charge
    adjustment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effect of adjustment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the actual full cost calculated under paragraph
  (1)(a) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} less than the estimated annual charge, the
    difference is credited to the applicant's or operator's account and
    must be refunded as a credit on the next invoice; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} greater than the estimated annual charge, the Board
    must invoice the applicant or the operator for an amount equal to
    the difference and the applicant or the operator must pay that
    amount to the Board within 30 days after the date of the invoice.

## [Part 2]{.HLabel1}[Formula Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833202 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833203 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} base units of time are the number of units of time
  published by the Board in accordance with section 8 based on the
  Board's estimate of the time required for it to undertake direct
  regulatory activities in relation to each activity set out in the
  tables to section 9;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} variable units of time are the number of units of
  time published by the Board in accordance with section 8 based on the
  Board's estimate of the time required for it to undertake direct
  regulatory activities in relation to the variables set out in column 3
  of the table to subsection 9(3);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective rate is the rate published by the Board
  in accordance with section 8 based on the estimated sum of the costs
  incurred by the Board's undertaking of all direct regulatory
  activities and of the Board's indirect regulatory costs minus the sum
  of the costs incurred by the Board's undertaking of regulatory
  activities that are not recovered by the Board at the request of the
  Federal Minister and the Provincial Minister and divided by the total
  number of units of time spent by the Board for those direct regulatory
  activities; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the heavy burden coefficient is a multiplier that the
  Board may apply to a fee for an activity if the Board is of the
  opinion that the fee must be increased to reflect the additional
  number of units of time spent by the Board to undertake direct
  regulatory activities as a result of non-compliance with the Act,
  negligence or lack of effort by an applicant or operator in responding
  to any of the Board's questions during an application process or
  activity.

### [Publication]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833210 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication by Board

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Each year the Board must
publish, by electronic or other means that is likely to reach applicants
and operators,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the base units of time for each activity set out in
  the tables to section 9;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the variable units of time for each activity set out
  in the table to subsection 9(3); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective rate.

### [Payment of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833257 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees calculated under section 9

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  submission of an application in respect of an activity set out in any
  table to section 9, the applicant must pay to the Board the fee
  determined in accordance with that section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Heavy burden coefficient

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board uses a heavy burden coefficient to
  calculate an additional charge in respect of an activity, the Board
  must invoice the applicant or the operator and the applicant or the
  operator must pay that amount to the Board within 30 days after the
  date of the invoice.

## [Part 3]{.HLabel1}[Geodata Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[daily access
rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In this Part, the [[daily
access rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is the rate established and published
by the Board by electronic or other means that is likely to reach
applicants and operators.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample access fee

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any person, except a
person requesting access for an academic purpose, the Federal Minister
and the Provincial Minister, who accesses a physical sample at the
geodata centre must pay the daily access rate for each day the sample is
accessed.

## [Part 4]{.HLabel1}[Other Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833268 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement of Board costs

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Board may require
reimbursement for 100% of its costs for activities that are not set out
in Parts 1 to 3 and that are related to the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any verification of compliance under the Act
  involving travel to another location by the Board's staff;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Oil and Gas Committee;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any technical analysis or process review that is
  related to a specific project and that is requested by an applicant or
  operator;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any public review, written or oral hearing or inquiry
  that is related to a specific project and that is required or
  initiated by the Board;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a participant funding program that is part of an
  environmental assessment conducted under the [Canadian Environmental
  Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} information, products or services that are requested
  by a person.

## [Part 5]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833277 .Part}

### [Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833278 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compound interest rate of 1.5%

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Interest on an amount
owing to the Board must be calculated and compounded monthly at the rate
of 1.5% and is payable and accrues during the period beginning on the
due date and ending on the day before the day on which the payment is
received by the Board.

### [Remittance of Fees and Charges]{.HTitleText2} {#h-833281 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remittance

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 29.3 of the Act, the fees and charges obtained in accordance
with these Regulations must be remitted on a quarterly basis subject to
the Board's operational requirements.
