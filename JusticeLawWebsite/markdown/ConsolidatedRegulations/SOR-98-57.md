## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025297 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[designated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means designated pursuant to
    paragraph 132(1)(g) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).
    ([désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods described in tariff item
    No. 9810.00.00. ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-233, s. 5
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025303 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where goods are imported by
a designated foreign country, the importer shall furnish to an officer,
at the time of importation, a certificate in the form set out in
Schedule 1, completed in English or French and signed by an official
authorized by the foreign country.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where goods are imported
and consigned to a designated military service agency or institution,
the importer shall furnish to an officer, at the time of importation, a
certificate in the form set out in Schedule 2, completed in English or
French and signed by an official authorized by that agency or
institution.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025306 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on January 1, 1998.
