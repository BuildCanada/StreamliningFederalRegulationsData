## Order Designating the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a Department and the Registrar as Deputy Head {#order-designating-the-office-of-the-registrar-of-lobbyists-as-a-department-and-the-registrar-as-deputy-head .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-18
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Designating the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a
Department and the Registrar as Deputy Head

P.C. 2006-54 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBACBA}, designates
  the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a department for the
  purposes of that Act, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBABBA}, designates
  the Registrar of the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as the
  deputy head of that office for the purposes of that Act,

effective February 6, 2006.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBACBA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12, 13
