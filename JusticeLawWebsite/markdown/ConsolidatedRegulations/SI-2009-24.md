## Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Any Person or Entity that Should Receive those Payments on Behalf of a Deceased Person who was a Chinese Head Tax Payer or who was in a Conjugal Relationship with a Head Tax Payer {#order-respecting-ex-gratia-payments-to-any-person-or-entity-that-should-receive-those-payments-on-behalf-of-a-deceased-person-who-was-a-chinese-head-tax-payer-or-who-was-in-a-conjugal-relationship-with-a-head-tax-payer .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2009-24
:::

Registration 2009-04-15

Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Any Person or Entity that Should
Receive those Payments on Behalf of a Deceased Person who was a Chinese
Head Tax Payer or who was in a Conjugal Relationship with a Head Tax
Payer

P.C. 2009-423 2009-03-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration and the Treasury Board,
hereby makes the annexed Order Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Any
Person or Entity that Should Receive those Payments on Behalf of a
Deceased Person who was a Chinese Head Tax Payer or who was in a
Conjugal Relationship with a Head Tax Payer.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614426 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Citizenship and
Immigration.

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614428 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is hereby
authorized, on application under subsection 3(1), to make an
[ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment of \$20,000 to any person or
entity that, in the opinion of the Minister, should receive the payment
on behalf of a person who, at the time of their death,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} met the criteria set out in section 2 of the Order
  Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Chinese Head Tax Payers[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_612360-E_hq_6794){#fn_612360-E_hq_6794-ID0EBBBA}
  but had not made an application under that Order; or

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_612360-E_hq_6794-ID0EBBBA){#fn_612360-E_hq_6794}SI/2006-109
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} met the criteria set out in section 2 of the Order
  Respecting Ex-Gratia Payments to Persons who were in Conjugal
  Relationships with now Deceased Chinese Head Tax Payers or to
  Designated Beneficiaries[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_612360-E_hq_6795){#fn_612360-E_hq_6795-ID0EBBAA}
  but had not made an application under that Order.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_612360-E_hq_6795-ID0EBBAA){#fn_612360-E_hq_6795}SI/2006-137
    :::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614432 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for an [ex-gratia]{.otherLang lang="la"} payment referred
  to in this Order must have been made to the Minister on or before
  March 31, 2008, in the form approved by the Minister and supported by
  any evidence that the Minister considers necessary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an application was submitted after March 31, 2008
  but on or before March 31, 2009, the application may be accepted by
  the Minister if he or she is satisfied that the applicant was unable
  to submit the application before that date because of facts or
  circumstances beyond the control of the applicant.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614436 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Each payment shall be a
one-time lump sum payment.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-614438 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Payments made under this
Order shall not be construed as an admission of liability on the part of
the Crown.
