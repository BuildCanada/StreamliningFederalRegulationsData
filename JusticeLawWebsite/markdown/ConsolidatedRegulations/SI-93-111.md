## Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Defence Construction (1951) Ltd. {#order-designating-the-minister-of-supply-and-services-as-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-defence-construction-1951-ltd. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-111
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Designating the Minister of Supply and Services as Appropriate
Minister with Respect to the Defence Construction (1951) Ltd.

P.C. 1993-1457  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and subparagraph (a)(ii)
of the definition of [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 83(1) of that Act, is pleased hereby to designate the
Minister of Supply and Services, a member of the Queen's Privy Council
for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Defence
Construction (1951) Ltd. for the purposes of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).
