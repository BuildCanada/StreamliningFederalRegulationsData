## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964252 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Coast Guard Radio Station Communications Charges Order,
1994](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-501).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964254 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Coast Guard Radio Station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Coast Guard Radio Station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    shore-based radio station operated by the Canadian Coast Guard;
    ([station radio de la Garde côtière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[originator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in the case of a communication
    transmitted to a ship, includes the landline company from which the
    communication was received by the Coast Guard Radio Station;
    ([envoyeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a ship, includes the
    operator of the ship. ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964259 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Order applies to every communication
  transmitted to a ship by means of a Coast Guard Radio Station or
  transmitted from a ship to a Coast Guard Radio Station.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an AMVER report forwarded through a Coast Guard
    Radio Station; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a communication

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an aid to navigation, ice, ice
      routing, medical advice, pollution, weather, any navigational
      hazard or the safety of a life, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} addressed to a part of or a member of the
      Canadian Coast Guard that involves a report of a ship movement,
      position or condition.

## [Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964267 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The charge payable for
transmitting a communication of the type set out in column I of an item
of the schedule for the period of time or number of words set out in
column II of that item is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as set out in column III of that item, where the
  communication

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is transmitted from or to a ship registered in or
    licensed by Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is transmitted from Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as set out in column IV of that item, for any other
  communication.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-277, s. 1
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A charge payable pursuant
to section 4 shall be paid to the Receiver General for Canada by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the originator, where the communication is
  transmitted to a ship; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner of the ship, where the communication is
  transmitted from a ship.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Payment
  of a charge payable pursuant to paragraph 4(b) shall be made in United
  States dollars.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), one United States
  dollar is equivalent to 2.5374 gold francs.
