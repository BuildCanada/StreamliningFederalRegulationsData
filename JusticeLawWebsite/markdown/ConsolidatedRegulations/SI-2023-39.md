## Order Transferring from the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-infrastructure-and-communities-to-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-intergovernmental-conference-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-39
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Transferring from the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities
to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the Control
and Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat

P.C. 2023-765 2023-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities to the
Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness the control and
supervision of the portion of the federal public administration known as
the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat.
