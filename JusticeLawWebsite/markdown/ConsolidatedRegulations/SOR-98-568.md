## The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc. Regulations {#the-jacques-cartier-and-champlain-bridges-inc.-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-568
:::

[CANADA MARINE ACT](/eng/acts/C-6.7)

Registration 1998-11-19

The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc. Regulations

P.C. 1998-2042  1998-11-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 26(1) of the St. Lawrence Seaway
Authority Act, The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc. was
incorporated as a subsidiary of The St. Lawrence Seaway Authority for
the purpose of undertaking or carrying out any acts or things that the
Authority was authorized to undertake or carry out under that Act;

Whereas subsection 26(3) of that Act extends the application of certain
provisions of the Act to a corporation incorporated pursuant to section
26 of the Act as though the corporation were The St. Lawrence Seaway
Authority;

Whereas, on the repeal of the St. Lawrence Seaway Authority Act, these
provisions cease to apply in respect of The Jacques-Cartier and
Champlain Bridges Inc.;

Whereas, pursuant to section 141 of the [Canada Marine
Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7), the Governor in Council may, by regulation,
provide that any provision of the St. Lawrence Seaway Authority Act
applies to The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc.;

And whereas the Governor in Council considers it appropriate that
certain provisions of the Act apply in respect of The Jacques-Cartier
and Champlain Bridges Inc.;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 141 of
the [Canada Marine Act](/eng/acts/C-6.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc. Regulations.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025224 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 3(2) and
sections 4, 9, 16, 17, 22, 24 and 25 of the St. Lawrence Seaway
Authority Act, as that Act read on November 30, 1998, apply, in a
modified form, to The Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc. The
modified provisions are set out in the schedule.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025226 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 1, 1998.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 1)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-1025229 .scheduleLabel}

###### [Modified Provisions]{.HTitleText}

###### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText}

- - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this schedule, "Corporation" means The
    Jacques-Cartier and Champlain Bridges Inc.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each section or subsection of this schedule is
    followed by the section or subsection number, in parentheses, of the
    corresponding provision of the St. Lawrence Seaway Authority Act
    (referred to in this schedule as "SLSAA").

###### [Agent of Her Majesty]{.HTitleText}

- **2** Except as provided in section 4, the Corporation is for all
  purposes an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada. (Subsection 3(2),
  SLSAA)

###### [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText}

- **3** Actions, suits or other legal proceedings in respect of any
  right or obligation acquired or incurred by the Corporation on behalf
  of Her Majesty, whether in its name or in the name of Her Majesty, may
  be brought or taken by or against the Corporation in the name of the
  Corporation in any court that would have jurisdiction if the
  Corporation were not an agent of Her Majesty. (Section 4, SLSAA)

###### [Officers and Employees]{.HTitleText}

- **4** The Corporation may employ such officers and employees for such
  purposes and on such terms and conditions as may be determined by it,
  and the officers and employees so employed are not officers or
  servants of Her Majesty. (Section 9, SLSAA)

###### [Fees]{.HTitleText}

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Corporation may, subject to section 6,
    fix fees to be charged by it with respect to

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the use of any building, plant, property or
      facilities under its administration;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any service performed by it; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pedestrians or vehicles entering, passing over or
      leaving a bridge or highway under its administration and
      passengers or goods carried in those vehicles. (Subsection 16(1),
      SLSAA)

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fees that may be charged may be for the use of
    any particular part of the bridges or other works administered by
    the Corporation, for the use of those bridges or works as a whole or
    for any particular service rendered by the Corporation. (Subsection
    16(2), SLSAA)

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} A fee fixed or any amendment to it shall be filed
    with the Canadian Transportation Agency and becomes operative from
    the day of filing. (Subsection 16(3), SLSAA)

  - [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any person interested may at any time file a
    complaint with the Canadian Transportation Agency that there is
    unjust discrimination in an existing fee and the Agency shall then
    consider the complaint and make a finding, which it shall report to
    the Corporation. (Subsection 16(4), SLSAA)

  - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Section 40 of the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4) applies, with such modifications as the
    circumstances require, in respect of every report of the Canadian
    Transportation Agency under subsection (4) as if the report were a
    decision made under that Act. (Subsection 16(5), SLSAA)

###### [Fees to be Fair and Reasonable]{.HTitleText}

- **6** The fees that may be charged by the Corporation shall be fair
  and reasonable and designed to provide a revenue sufficient to defray
  the cost to it of its operations in carrying out the purposes for
  which it is incorporated, which costs shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} payments in respect of the interest on amounts
    borrowed by it to carry out those purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts sufficient to amortize the principal of
    those amounts over a period not exceeding 50 years; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cost of operating and maintaining the bridges
    and other works under its administration, including all its
    operating costs and such reserves as may be approved by the
    Minister. (Section 17, SLSAA)

###### [Retirement of Former Employees in Public Service]{.HTitleText}

- - **7** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a person employed by the Corporation was a
    contributor under the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36) immediately prior to that employment, and that
    employment was entered into with the consent of the Minister of the
    department or branch of the Public Service in which the person was
    employed, the provisions that apply to that person on retirement
    from employment by the Corporation for a reason other than
    misconduct are as follows:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if before being employed by the Corporation the
      person was employed in a position to which the [Public Service
      Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applied, the person may be
      appointed to a position to which that Act applies of a class not
      lower than the class in which the person was so employed; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if before being employed by the Corporation the
      person was employed in a position in the Public Service to which
      the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) did not
      apply, the person may be appointed to a position in the public
      service of Canada to which that Act does not apply of a class not
      lower than the class in which the person was so employed.
      (Subsection 22(1), SLSAA)

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Government Employees Compensation
    Act](/eng/acts/G-5) applies to officers and servants of the
    Corporation and, notwithstanding section 4, for the purposes of that
    Act but not otherwise, those officers and servants shall be deemed
    to be employees in the service of Her Majesty. (Subsection 22(2),
    SLSAA)

###### [Contracts with Her Majesty]{.HTitleText}

- **8** Although the Corporation is an agent of Her Majesty, it may
  enter into contracts with Her Majesty. (Section 24, SLSAA)

###### [Auditor]{.HTitleText}

- **9** The Auditor General of Canada is the auditor of the Corporation.
  (Section 25, SLSAA)
