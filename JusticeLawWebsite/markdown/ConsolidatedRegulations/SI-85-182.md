## Courier Imports Remission Order {#courier-imports-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-182
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-10-16

Order respecting the remission of customs duties, sales and excise taxes
on certain imported goods transported into Canada by courier services

P.C. 1985-2955 1985-10-03
::::

::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board and pursuant to
section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_85-182_EN_hq_10798){#fn_SI_85-182_EN_hq_10798-ID0EJCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to revoke the [Courier Imports Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-182), made by Order in Council P.C.
1982-197 of 21st January 1982[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SI_85-182_EN_hq_10799){#fn_SI_85-182_EN_hq_10799-ID0EDCAA}
and, considering that it is in the public interest to do so, is pleased
to substitute therefor, the annexed [Order respecting the remission of
customs duties, sales and excise taxes on certain imported goods
transported into Canada by courier
services](/eng/regulations/SI-85-182).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639812 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([produit du
    cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial carrier that is
    engaged in scheduled international transportation of shipments of
    goods other than goods imported by mail;
    ([messager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with the exception of a publication
    or book classified under tariff item No. 9812.00.00 in the List of
    Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alcoholic beverages, cannabis products, vaping
      products, cigars, cigarettes and manufactured tobacco;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods classified under tariff item No. 9816.00.00
      in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and goods for which the value
      for duty is reduced by the application of section 85 of the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} books, newspapers, magazines, periodicals and
      other similar publications where the supplier is required to
      register under Subdivision d of Division V of Part IX of the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and is not so
      registered; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([produit de
    vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639822 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported goods that are purchased from a retailer in
  Canada and shipped to the purchaser directly from a place situated out
  of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported goods that are purchased or ordered through
  or from an address, a post office box or a telephone number in Canada;
  or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are imported by a person other than the
  person in Canada who ordered or purchased the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-128, s. 4
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639829 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is granted of the customs duties and excise taxes paid or
payable in respect of imported goods, other than goods imported from
Mexico or the United States, that are transported by courier and have a
value for duty of \$20 or less.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-128, s. 4

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission of the following is granted in respect of goods imported from
Mexico or the United States that are transported by courier:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties paid or payable, if the goods have
  a value for duty of \$150 or less; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the excise taxes paid or payable, if the goods have a
  value for duty of \$40 or less.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SI/2020-34, s. 1](#1243496-1253904){.wb-lbx

  role="button"}

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639833 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If the benefit of remission
is not received at the time of importation, the remission granted under
section 4 or 4.1 is granted on the condition that a claim for remission
is made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the
date of importation of the goods for which remission is claimed.
