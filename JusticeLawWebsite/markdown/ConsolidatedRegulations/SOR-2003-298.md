## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704330 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an interest-bearing
    deposit account. ([compte de dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
    to which the public has access and at which a retail association
    carries on business with the public and opens or initiates the
    opening of deposit accounts through natural persons in
    Canada. ([point de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Deposit Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of disclosure

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a retail association shall disclose to a person who
  requests the retail association to open a deposit account the rate of
  interest applicable to the deposit account and how the amount of
  interest to be paid is to be calculated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a statement of account is provided in respect of
    the deposit account, by means of a written statement delivered to
    that person, at or before the time the deposit account is opened; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no statement of account is provided in respect
    of the deposit account, by means of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a written statement, copies of which are
      available and displayed in each branch of the retail association
      where deposit accounts are kept, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a general notice that is displayed in each of
      the retail association's branches where deposit accounts are kept,
      at each of the retail association's points of service and on the
      retail association's websites through which it offers deposit
      accounts in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opening of account on telephone request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of the opening of a deposit account on a
  telephone request as contemplated by subsection 385.1(3) of the Act, a
  retail association shall, not later than seven business days after the
  account is opened, provide to the customer a written statement that
  sets out the rate of interest applicable to the account and how the
  amount of interest to be paid is to be calculated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required contents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A written statement and a general notice referred to
  in subsection (1) or a written statement referred to in subsection (2)
  shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate of interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the frequency of payment of interest;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner, if any, in which the balance in the
    deposit account affects the rate of interest; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other circumstance that affects the rate of
    interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A written statement referred to in subsection (2)
  that is sent to a customer by mail is deemed to be provided to the
  customer on the fifth day after the postmark date on the statement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of changes

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A retail association shall
disclose any change in the rate of interest applicable to a deposit
account or any change in the manner of calculating the amount of
interest applicable to the deposit account, by means of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written statement delivered to the person in whose
  name the account is kept;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written statement copies of which are available and
  displayed in each branch of the retail association where deposit
  accounts are kept; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a general notice that is displayed in each of the
  retail association's branches where deposit accounts are kept, at each
  of the retail association's points of service and on the retail
  association's websites through which it offers deposit accounts in
  Canada.

## [Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704366 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of disclosure

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A retail association shall,
in all advertisements referred to in section 385.08 of the Act, disclose
how the amount of interest is to be calculated by means of a statement
in the advertisement that clearly sets out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of interest-bearing deposits, the manner,
  if any, in which the balance of a deposit account will affect the rate
  of interest; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other circumstances that will affect the rate of
  interest.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704371 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
