## Order Amalgamating the Department of Social Development and the Department of Human Resources and Skills Development under the Authority of the Minister and the Deputy Minister {#order-amalgamating-the-department-of-social-development-and-the-department-of-human-resources-and-skills-development-under-the-authority-of-the-minister-and-the-deputy-minister .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-10
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Amalgamating the Department of Social Development and the
Department of Human Resources and Skills Development under the Authority
of the Minister and the Deputy Minister

P.C. 2006-37 2006-02-06
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDDBCA} of the
  [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDDBCA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

  <!-- -->

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from the Department of Social Development to the
    Department of Human Resources and Skills Development the control and
    supervision of all of the portions of the federal public
    administration within the Department of Social Development, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the Minister of Social Development to the
    Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development all of the
    powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Social Development
    under any Act of Parliament, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of that Act, amalgamates
  and combines the Department of Social Development and the Department
  of Human Resources and Skills Development under the Minister of Human
  Resources and Skills Development and under the Deputy Minister of
  Human Resources and Skills Development,

effective February 6, 2006.
