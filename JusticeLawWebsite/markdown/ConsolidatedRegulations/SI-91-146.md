## Order Placing Members of the Canadian Force on Active Service (Western Sahara) {#order-placing-members-of-the-canadian-force-on-active-service-western-sahara .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-146
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Registration 1991-11-20

Order Placing Members of the Canadian Force on Active Service (Western
Sahara)

P.C. 1991-2115  1991-10-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations has received and
approved the report of the Secretary General of the United Nations on
the holding of a referendum for self-determination of the people in
Western Sahara;

Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, by Resolution 690 of
April 29, 1991, established a United Nations Mission for the Referendum
in Western Sahara;

And Whereas it appears advisable that Canada participate in that
Mission;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Defence, pursuant to
paragraph 31(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-146_e_hq_6485){#fn_SI-91-146_e_hq_6485-ID0EDEAA}
of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), is pleased hereby to place
on active service beyond Canada those

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-146_e_hq_6485-ID0EDEAA){#fn_SI-91-146_e_hq_6485}R.S., c.
  31 (1st Supp.), s. 60 (Sch. I, item 14)
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the regular
  force of the Canadian forces, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} officers and non-commissioned members of the reserve
  force of the Canadian Forces

who are part of, or serve in immediate support of, the United Nations
Mission for the Referendum in Western Sahara.
