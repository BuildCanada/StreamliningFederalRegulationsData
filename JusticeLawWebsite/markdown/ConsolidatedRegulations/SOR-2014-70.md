## Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in Benefits Order {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-dependants-pension-fund-increase-in-benefits-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-70
:::

[ROYAL CANADIAN MOUNTED POLICE PENSION CONTINUATION
ACT](/eng/acts/R-10.6)

Registration 2014-03-28

Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in
Benefits Order

P.C. 2014-308 2014-03-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears from a report made under section 56 of the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148){#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148-ID0EBBBA}
that the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund is
substantially in excess of the amount required to make adequate
provision for the prospective payments out of it;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148-ID0EBBBA){#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148}R.S.C.,
  1970, c. R-10
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 57(1) of the [Royal Canadian
Mounted Police Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148){#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13148-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed [Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension
Fund Increase in Benefits Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-122).
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following pension
benefits, as provided in Part IV of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13149){#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13149-ID0EBEA},
are to be increased in the following manner:

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13149-ID0EBEA){#fn_81000-2-1893-E_hq_13149}R.S.C.,
  1970, c. R-10
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pensions for current and prospective widows are to be
  increased by 1.2% in each of the 2014, 2015 and 2016 plan years, on
  April 1 of each plan year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lump sum benefits payable on the death of a member
  are to be increased by 1.2% in each of the 2014, 2015 and 2016 plan
  years, on April 1 of each plan year; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} residual amounts payable on the death of a member's
  widow are to be calculated with a deemed increase in the member's
  contributions, without crediting the contributions with interest, of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1,240% if the widow's death occurs in the 2015 plan
    year,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1,256% if the widow's death occurs in the 2016
    plan year, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 1,272% if the widow's death occurs in the 2017
    plan year.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
effect on April 1, 2014.
