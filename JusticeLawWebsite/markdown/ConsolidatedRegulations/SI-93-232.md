## Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Minister for Purposes of Section 47 of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-communications-as-minister-for-purposes-of-section-47-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-232
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Minister for
Purposes of Section 47 of the Act

P.C. 1993-1985  1993-12-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 47[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-232_e_hq_6546){#fn_SI-93-232_e_hq_6546-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), is pleased
hereby to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the
Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
section 47 of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01).
