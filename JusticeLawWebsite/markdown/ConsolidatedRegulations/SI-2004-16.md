## Order Amalgamating and Combining Communication Canada with the Department of Public Works and Government Services {#order-amalgamating-and-combining-communication-canada-with-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-16
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-02-25

Order Amalgamating and Combining Communication Canada with the
Department of Public Works and Government Services

P.C. 2004-100  2004-02-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(b) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
amalgamates and combines Communication Canada with the Department of
Public Works and Government Services under the Minister of Public Works
and Government Services and under the Deputy Minister of Public Works
and Government Services, effective April 1, 2004.
