## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-722575 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Federal Public Sector Labour
    Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-33.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contact information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a telephone
    number, fax number, mailing and electronic
    address. ([coordonnées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
    day. ([jour]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Part 1, has the same meaning as in subsection
      2(1) of the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Part 2, has the same meaning as in section 206
      of the Act. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[initiating document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[initiating document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, as the case may
    be,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a request for an extension, an addition or a
      reduction of time under section 12 if the request is filed before
      the filing of any document referred to in any of paragraphs (b)
      to (x) or (z.1);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a request for the Board to exercise any of its
      powers, duties and functions under section 12 of the Act;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a request for the Board to exercise any of its
      powers under section 43 of the Act;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-251, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an application for certification under section 54
      of the Act;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an application, under section 58 of the Act, for
      a determination of membership of an employee or a class of
      employees in a bargaining unit;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an application, under subsection 79(2) of the
      Act, for a determination of the rights, privileges and duties of
      an employee organization;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an application for certification under section 83
      of the Act;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an application for an order under subsection
      84(1) or section 89 of the Act;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an application, under subsection 86(1) of the
      Act, for leave to give to the other party a notice to bargain
      collectively;

    - [(k)]{.lawlabel} an application for revocation of certification
      under section 94, 98, 99 or 100 of the Act;

    - [(l)]{.lawlabel} an application, under subsection 101(2) of the
      Act, for a determination of the rights or duties of an employee
      organization;

    - [(m)]{.lawlabel} an application for direction under section 102 of
      the Act;

    - [(n)]{.lawlabel} an application, under paragraph 117(b) of the
      Act, for an extension of the time to implement a collective
      agreement;

    - [(o)]{.lawlabel} an application for a determination on a matter
      that may be included in an essential services agreement under
      subsection 123(1) of the Act;

    - [(p)]{.lawlabel} an application to amend an essential services
      agreement under subsection 127(1) of the Act;

    - [(q)]{.lawlabel} an application, under section 131 of the Act, for
      the amendment or suspension of an essential services agreement
      because of an emergency;

    - [(r)]{.lawlabel} an application for an extension of time under
      section 133 of the Act;

    - [(s)]{.lawlabel} a request for arbitration under subsection 136(1)
      of the Act;

    - [(s.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-43, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(t)]{.lawlabel} a request for conciliation under subsection
      161(1) of the Act;

    - [(u)]{.lawlabel} an application for a declaration that a strike
      vote is invalid under subsection 184(2) of the Act;

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a complaint made under section 190 of the Act;

    - [(w)]{.lawlabel} an application for a declaration of unlawful
      conduct under subsection 198(1) of the Act;

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} an application to obtain the consent of the Board
      referred to in section 205 of the Act;

    - [(y)]{.lawlabel} an application for an extension of time referred
      to in subsection 61(b) if the application is filed before the
      notice of a reference to adjudication;

    - [(z)]{.lawlabel} the notice of a reference to adjudication under
      section 223 of the Act; or

    - [(z.1)]{.lawlabel} a request for the filing of an order of an
      adjudicator or the Board in the Federal Court under subsection
      234(1) of the Act. ([document introductif]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a proceeding,
    means any person that has been granted intervenor
    status. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an employee organization, a
    council of employee organizations and an
    employer. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[signature]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a handwritten signature or
    an electronic signature that consists of one or more letters,
    characters, numbers or other symbols in digital form and that is
    incorporated in, attached to or associated with an electronic
    document or electronic information. ([signature]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of document

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A document is considered to
have been received by the Board,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a document is sent by courier, on the day on which
  it is sent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a document is delivered by hand, on the day on
  which it is received;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the document is sent by electronic mail, fax
  transmission or other electronic means, on the day on which it is
  sent; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the document is sent by mail, on the day that is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the postmark or the date of the postage
    meter impression authorized by the Canada Post Corporation, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if both the postmark and postage meter impression
    appear on the envelope, on the later of the dates indicated in them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Computation of time

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If the time limit, under
these Regulations, for the filing or presentation of a document expires
on a Saturday or a holiday, the document may be filed on the day next
following that is not a Saturday or a holiday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  person who initiates a proceeding before the Board, the Chairperson or
  an adjudicator may withdraw the proceeding in person at the hearing or
  by providing the Board with a notice of withdrawal in writing at any
  time before a decision is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing of file

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On being informed by the Chairperson or an
  adjudicator of the withdrawal or on receipt of the notice of
  withdrawal, the Board must close the file and inform the parties, the
  intervenors and, if notice to the Canadian Human Rights Commission was
  given under subsection 210(1), 217(1) or 222(1) of the Act, the
  Canadian Human Rights Commission that the file has been closed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension or reduction of time

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of this Part, the Board may, with respect to any Board matter
referred to in this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the interest of fairness, extend the time
  specified under this Part, or allow for additional time, to do any act
  or file any notice or any document; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a matter is urgent, on notice to all parties,
  reduce the time specified under this Part to do any act or file any
  notice or any document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Addition of party or intervenor

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person with a substantial interest in a proceeding before the Board
  may apply to the Board to be added as a party or an intervenor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, after giving the parties the
  opportunity to make representations in respect of the application, add
  the person as a party or an intervenor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Insufficient information

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may, on its own initiative or at request of a party or an
  intervenor, request that information contained in any document filed
  by any other party or any other intervenor be made more complete or
  specific.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Striking out information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, after giving the party or intervenor
  to whom the request was made the opportunity to reply to the request,
  strike from the document the information that is incomplete or
  insufficiently specific.

### [DIVISION 1]{.HLabel2}[Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722726 .Subheading}

#### [Procedure for Certification]{.HTitleText3} {#h-722727 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for certification

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** An application for
certification under section 54 or 238.13 of the Act must be filed with
the Board and signed by the authorized representative of the applicant
and must contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the applicant and
  its authorized representative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a council of employee
  organizations, the names and contact information of the constituent
  employee organizations and their contact persons;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employer's name and contact information;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description, in both official languages,
  of the group of employees proposed as a unit appropriate for
  collective bargaining;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of employees in the proposed
  bargaining unit;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for which the proposed bargaining unit is
  appropriate for collective bargaining;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if an employee organization is, on the date of the
  application for certification, certified as the bargaining agent for
  employees in the proposed bargaining unit,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of that bargaining
    agent,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the bargaining unit represented,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the term of the collective agreement or arbitral
    award, or both, as the case may be; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application for certification.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2020-43, s. 11](#1231896-1232123){.wb-lbx

  role="button"}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Closing date

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of the
application for certification, the Board must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix a closing date that is a date that will allow
  sufficient time for the employees to be notified and for them to
  respond, considering the number of employees who may be affected by
  the application for certification and the locations at which they are

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-722884 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Despite anything in this
Part, the time prescribed by this Part or provided for in a grievance
procedure contained in a collective agreement for the doing of any act,
the presentation of a grievance at any level of the grievance process,
the referral of a grievance to adjudication or the providing or filing
of any notice, reply or document may be extended, either before or after
the expiry of that time,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by agreement between the parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the interest of fairness, on the application of a
  party, by the Board or an adjudicator, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension of time

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** If the parties avail
themselves of an informal conflict management system established under
section 207 of the Act, the time prescribed in this Part or provided for
in a grievance procedure contained in a collective agreement for the
presentation of a grievance at a level of the grievance process is
suspended until either party gives to the other notice in writing to the
contrary.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rejection for failure to meet a deadline

rejected for the reason that the time limit prescribed in this Part for
the presentation of the grievance at a lower level has not been met,
only if the grievance was rejected at the lower level for that reason.

        arbitral award that are the subject of the individual grievance,

      - [(H)]{.lawlabel} if an adjudicator is named in the collective

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} if the parties have selected an adjudicator,

        adjudication, or

        agent,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the place of work, position title, division and
        section or unit and classification of the grievor, as well as
        the grievor's department,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which the individual grievance was
        presented at the first and final levels of the individual
        grievance process,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the employer provided its
        decision at the final level of the individual grievance process,
        if any,

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} the term of the collective agreement or
        arbitral award, or both, as the case may be, that is the subject
        of the individual grievance,

      - [(G)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the Act under which the
        individual grievance is referred to adjudication,

      - [(H)]{.lawlabel} if an adjudicator is named in the collective
        agreement, the adjudicator's name and contact information,

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} if the parties have selected an adjudicator,
        the adjudicator's name and contact information ,

      - [(J)]{.lawlabel} if the establishment of a board of adjudication
        is requested, the name and contact information of the person
        nominated as a member of the board of adjudication, and

      - [(K)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice of reference to
        adjudication;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a group grievance,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the names and contact information of the
      bargaining agent and its authorized representative,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the aggrieved employees' employer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the description of the bargaining unit to which
      the aggrieved employees belong,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the federal public administration
      in which the aggrieved employees work,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which the group grievance was
      presented at the first and final levels of the group grievance
      process,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the employer provided its
      decision at the final level of the group grievance process, if
      any,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the term of the collective agreement or
      arbitral award, or both, as the case may be, that is the subject
      of the group grievance,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the collective agreement or
      arbitral award that is the subject of the group grievance,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} if an adjudicator is named in the collective
      agreement, the adjudicator's name and contact information,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} if the parties have selected an adjudicator, the
      adjudicator's name and contact information,

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} if the establishment of a board of adjudication
      is requested, the name and contact information of the person
      nominated as a member of the board of adjudication, and

    - [(xii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice of reference to
      adjudication; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a policy grievance,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the names and contact information of the party
      filing notice of reference to adjudication and its authorized
      representative,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the other party to the policy
      grievance,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the bargaining unit,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the policy grievance was
      presented to the other party,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the other party provided a
      decision with respect to the policy grievance,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the term of the collective agreement or arbitral
      award, or both, as the case may be, that is the subject of the
      policy grievance,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the collective agreement or
      arbitral award that is the subject of the policy grievance,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} if an adjudicator is named in the collective
      agreement, the adjudicator's name and contact information,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} if the parties have selected an adjudicator, the
      adjudicator's name and contact information,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} if the establishment of a board of adjudication
      is requested, the name and contact information of the person
      nominated as a member of the board of adjudication, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice of reference to
      adjudication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the notice of reference to adjudication concerns a
  group grievance, the bargaining agent must also provide a copy of the
  consent submitted in accordance with subsection 77(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grievance relating to interpretation or
  application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an individual grievance relates to the
  interpretation or application, in respect of the grievor, of a
  provision of a collective agreement or an arbitral award, the notice
  of the reference to adjudication must contain a declaration by an
  authorized representative of the bargaining agent for the bargaining
  unit to which the collective agreement or arbitral award applies,
  indicating that the bargaining agent is willing to represent the
  grievor in the adjudication proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for reference to adjudication

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), a
  grievance may be referred to adjudication no later than 40 days after
  the day on which the person who presented the grievance received a
  decision at the final level of the applicable grievance process.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no decision at the final level of the applicable
  grievance process was received, a grievance may be referred to
  adjudication no later than 40 days after the expiry of the period
  within which the decision was required under this Part or, if there is
  another period set out in a collective agreement, under the collective
  agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline --- establishment of board of
adjudication

- **[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  party that receives a copy of a notice of a reference to adjudication
  may, no later than 15 days after receipt of the notice,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the notice contains a request for the
    establishment of a board of adjudication, object to the
    establishment of a board or file a document specifying the name of
    their nominee as member of the board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, request the establishment of a
    board of adjudication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline --- establishment of board of
  adjudication

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the party that receives a copy of a notice of a
  reference to adjudication requests the establishment of a board of
  adjudication, the other party may, no later than 15 days after being
  provided with a copy of the request, object to the establishment of
  the board or file a document specifying the name of their nominee as a
  member of the board.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Canadian Human Rights
Commission

- **[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  notice of a human rights issue under subsection 210(1), 217(1) or
  222(1) of the Act must be given to the Canadian Human Rights
  Commission, together with a copy of the grievance and the notice of
  the reference to adjudication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and contents

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be filed with the Canadian Human
  Rights Commission and signed by the party raising the issue or their
  authorized representative, as the case may be, and contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the party
    raising the issue involving the interpretation or application of the
    [Canadian Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6) and of their authorized
    representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the description of the issue involving the
    interpretation or application of the [Canadian Human Rights
    Act](/eng/acts/H-6);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited ground of discrimination involved;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the corrective action sought; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person who gives the notice must send a copy of
  it to the Board, the other party, any intervenors and every person in
  receipt of a copy of the notice of the reference to adjudication by
  virtue of section 4, unless that person has notified the Board in
  writing that the person does not wish to receive a copy of subsequent
  documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline for raising objections

- **[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may, no later than 30 days after being provided with a copy of
  the notice of the reference to adjudication,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} raise an objection on the grounds that the time
    limit prescribed in this Part or provided for in a collective
    agreement for the presentation of a grievance at a level of the
    grievance process has not been met; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} raise an objection on the grounds that the time
    limit prescribed in this Part or provided for in a collective
    agreement for the reference to adjudication has not been met.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection may not be raised

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The objection referred to in paragraph (1)(a) may be
  raised only if the grievance was rejected at the level at which the
  time limit was not met and at all subsequent levels of the grievance
  process for that reason.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Objection raised

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the party raises an objection referred to in
  subsection (1), it must provide a statement in writing giving details
  regarding its objection to the Board.
