## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298293 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Human Rights
    Act](/eng/acts/H-6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chairperson of the
    Tribunal, appointed in accordance with subsection 48.1(1) of the
    Act, who has supervision over and direction of the work of the
    Tribunal. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Human Rights
    Commission established by subsection 26(1) of the
    Act. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[complainant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the individual or group of
    individuals who filed a complaint under subsection 40(1) of the Act
    and any person added as a complainant under an order referred to in
    Rule 28 or 29. ([plaignant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a drawing, photograph,
    film or sound recording. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the member or group of three
    members of the Tribunal assigned by the Chairperson under subsection
    49(2) of the Act to conduct an
    inquiry. ([formation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission, a complainant or
    respondent. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an employee organization,
    employer organization and unincorporated
    entity. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the employee of the
    Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada who is
    responsible for providing registry services to the Tribunal under
    the direction of the Chairperson, or a registry
    officer. ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a complaint,
    a person alleged to be engaging or to have engaged in a
    discriminatory practice and any person added as a respondent under
    an order referred to in Rule 28 or 29. ([intimé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298306 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Rules do not apply to an inquiry for which the request to institute an
  inquiry to the Chairperson under subsection 49(1) of the Act was made
  before the day on which these Rules come into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, these Rules apply to any inquiry referred to
  in subsection (1) on consent of all parties.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298312 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inquiry

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inquiry is instituted by the Chairperson assigning a panel to it and
  concludes when the panel renders a decision under section 53 of the
  Act or when the complaint is settled, abandoned or withdrawn.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of inquiry

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The panel controls the conduct of an inquiry under
  these Rules including holding a case management conference, hearing a
  motion and conducting the hearing on the merits of a complaint.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No panel assigned

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a panel has not yet been
assigned to conduct an inquiry, the Chairperson may exercise the powers
assigned to a panel under these Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General principle

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Rules are to be
interpreted and applied so as to secure the informal, expeditious and
fair determination of every inquiry on its merits.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Needs of participants

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Rules are to be interpreted and applied so as to reasonably
  accommodate the needs of all participants in an inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification to Registrar

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any participant in the inquiry who requires
  accommodation must notify the Registrar as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation services

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any participant in the inquiry who requires the
  services of an interpreter must notify the Registrar as soon as
  feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rules not exhaustive

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A panel may decide any
matter of procedure that is not provided for by these Rules.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying or dispensing with Rules

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A panel may, on the motion
of a party or on its own initiative, vary or dispense with compliance
with a Rule, if doing so achieves the purpose set out in Rule 5.

## [Consequences of Non-compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-compliance with Rules or orders

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a party does not comply
with these Rules, an order of a panel or a time limit established under
these Rules, the panel may, on the motion of another party or its own
initiative, and having regard to the circumstances, order the party to
remedy their non-compliance, proceed with the inquiry, dismiss the
complaint or make any other order to achieve the purpose set out in Rule
5.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vexatious conduct or abuse of process

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A panel may, on the
motion of a party or its own initiative, make any order that it
considers necessary against vexatious conduct or abuse of process.

## [Service and Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298338 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be served and filed

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every document to be
served and filed under these Rules must be served on all parties or, if
a party is represented, on their representative and filed with the
Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Methods of service

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Service may be effected

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by email to an email address provided by a party;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by registered or ordinary mail or by courier;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in person;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} by fax if the document does not exceed 20 pages; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} electronically to an Internet address designated by
  the Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A panel may order that a
party provide proof of service of a document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A document may be filed
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transmitting it to the Internet address designated by
  the Registrar;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivering by hand or mailing two copies to the
  address designated by the Registrar; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} faxing it to the fax number designated by the
  Registrar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language of documents

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Any document that is
filed must be in English or French or be accompanied by a translation in
English or French and an affidavit of the translator attesting to the
accuracy of the translation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representative of record

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  party files or serves a document signed by a representative, that
  representative is the representative of record for the party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may change or remove its representative of
  record or appoint a representative of record by serving and filing a
  written notice to this effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the representative of record is changed or a
  representative is appointed, the notice must set out the name, postal
  address, telephone number, fax number and email address of the new
  representative.

## [Request to Institute Inquiry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298364 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request by Commission

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  request to the Chairperson to institute an inquiry into the complaint
  must be filed by the Commission and be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the complaint; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written notice that sets out the following
    information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal address, telephone number, fax
      number and email address of each complainant and respondent,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal address, telephone number, fax
      number and email address of any representative of the complainant
      or respondent,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the anticipated language of the inquiry, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any special arrangements required for the
      inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Update to information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the information provided by the Commission under
  paragraph (1)(b) is inaccurate or changes, the party in question must
  notify the Registrar and the other parties as soon as feasible.

## [Statement of Particulars]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298376 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of particulars --- complainant

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  complainant must serve and file, within the time limit established by
  the panel, a statement of particulars that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the facts they intend to prove in support of their
    complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issues raised by the complaint and their
    position on each issue, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the prohibited grounds of discrimination referred
      to in section 3 of the Act, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the discriminatory practices referred to in
      sections 5 to 14.1 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of the relationship between the
    prohibited grounds of discrimination raised under subparagraph
    (b)(i) and the discriminatory practices raised under subparagraph
    (b)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any order they are seeking under subsections 53(2)
    to (4) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of each witness, other than expert
    witnesses, whom they intend to call, along with a summary of the
    witness's anticipated testimony; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of all the documents in their possession
    that relate to a fact or issue that is raised in the complaint or to
    an order sought by any of the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list of documents described in paragraph (1)(f)
  must indicate the documents in respect of which privilege is claimed
  and the basis for the privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of particulars --- Commission

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Commission is participating at the inquiry, it must serve and file,
  within the time limit established by the panel, a statement of
  particulars that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its position on the facts on which the complaint is
    based and the issues it raises;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any order it is seeking under subsections 53(2)
    to (4) of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its position on the orders sought, as set out in
    the complainant's statement of particulars;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of each witness, other than an expert
    witness, whom it intends to call along with a summary of the
    witness's anticipated testimony; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of all the documents in its possession that
    relate to a fact or issue that is raised in the complaint or to an
    order sought by any of the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list described in paragraph (1)(e) must indicate
  the documents in respect of which privilege is claimed and the basis
  for the privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response to statements of particulars ---
respondent

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent must serve and file, within the time limit established by
  the panel, a response to the statements of particulars that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its position on the facts on which the complaint is
    based and the issues raised by the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether it intends to rely on any exception set out
    in subsection 15(1) of the Act and, if so, the facts it intends to
    prove to establish that exception;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its position on the orders sought, as set out in
    the statements of particulars;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of each witness, other than an expert
    witness, whom it intends to call, along with a summary of the
    witness's anticipated testimony; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the documents that are in its possession
    that relate to a fact or issue that is raised in the complaint or to
    an order sought by any of the parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list described in paragraph (1)(e) must indicate
  the documents in respect of which privilege is claimed and the basis
  for the privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  of the complainants and the Commission, if it is participating in the
  inquiry, may serve and file a reply, within the time limit established
  by the panel, that sets out any facts, issues, witnesses or documents
  they intend to present in reply to the response provided by the
  respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witnesses or documents in reply

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a reply sets out additional witnesses or
  documents, the reply must include, in the case of the complainant, the
  information set out in paragraphs 18(1)(e) and (f) and subrule 18(2)
  and, in the case of the Commission, the information set out in
  paragraphs 19(1)(c) and (d) and subrule 19(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert witness

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  each expert witness whom a party intends to call, that party must
  serve and file, within the time limit established by the panel, a
  report that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the expert's name and address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} includes the expert's *curriculum vitae* and a
    statement indicating how their training, education and experience
    qualify them to produce the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sets out the substance of their anticipated
    testimony; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is signed by the expert.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An expert witness has a duty to assist the panel in
  an impartial, objective and independent manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of documents

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party that discloses a document in accordance with paragraph 18(1)(f),
  19(1)(e) or 20(1)(e) or subrule 24(1) for which privilege is not
  claimed must provide a copy of that document to the other parties
  within the time limit established by the panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents not to be filed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, documents provided to the
  other parties under subrule (1) must not be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ongoing disclosure

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party must, as soon as feasible, serve and file a list of the
  additional relevant documents that are in its possession if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new fact or issue is raised or a new order is
    sought in a statement of particulars, response or other party's
    reply; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the party discovers that the list of documents
    provided in accordance with paragraph 18(1)(f), 19(1)(e) or 20(1)(e)
    is inaccurate or incomplete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileged documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The list of documents must indicate the documents in
  respect of which privilege is claimed and the basis for the privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of constitutional questions

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** If a party intends to
challenge the constitutional validity, applicability or operability of a
statute or regulation, the party must serve notice in accordance with
subsection 57(2) of the [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7) and Form 69
of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

## [Motions and Adjournments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298438 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the panel agrees to proceed orally, a motion, including a motion for
  adjournment, must be initiated by a notice in writing that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the order sought and the grounds relied
    on; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies any consent obtained from other parties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service and filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be served and filed as soon as
  feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A panel that receives a notice of motion, including a
  notice of motion for adjournment,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must give the other parties an opportunity to
    respond;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may give directions respecting the time, manner and
    form of any response;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} may give directions respecting the presentation of
    arguments and evidence by the parties, including the time at which
    and the manner and form in which the arguments and the evidence must
    be presented; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} may dispose of the motion as it considers
    necessary.

## [Addition of Parties and Interested Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298452 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for interested person status

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that wishes to be recognized as an interested person in respect
  of an inquiry must serve and file a notice of motion for an order to
  that effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of motion must specify the assistance the
  person wishes to provide to the inquiry and the extent to which the
  person wishes to participate in the inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision of panel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the panel grants the motion, it must specify the
  extent to which the interested person is permitted to participate in
  the inquiry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for party status

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A person that wishes to
be recognized by the panel as a party in respect of the inquiry must
serve and file a notice of motion for an order to that effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion by party to add party

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** A party that wishes to
have a person recognized as a party in respect of an inquiry must serve
and file a notice of motion for an order to that effect after having
served the notice of motion on the prospective party. The prospective
party may make submissions on the motion.

## [Case Management Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298464 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scheduling by panel

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel may schedule a case management conference to resolve matters of
  an administrative or procedural nature in respect of the inquiry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A case management conference may be held in person,
  by telephone conference call, video-conference, or any other form of
  electronic communication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Registrar

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party that wishes to raise a matter at a case
  management conference must notify the Registrar as soon as feasible
  before the conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of conference

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** At a case management
conference, the panel may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} hear arguments on a motion or give the directions
  referred to in paragraph 26(3)(b) or (c);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} after hearing the parties on an issue or motion, make
  any order that it considers necessary;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} set dates for the hearing on the merits of the
  complaint;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} establish time limits for complying with the
  obligations set out in rules 18 to 23; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} deal with any other matter relating to the conduct of
  the inquiry.

## [Hearing and Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298479 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hours of hearings

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** Hearings are held on
Monday to Friday, except on holidays, between 9:30 a.m. and 5:00 p.m
local time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of hearing

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), a hearing is
  conducted in person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remote conferencing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The panel may order that a hearing be conducted in
  whole or in part by means of a telephone conference call,
  video-conference or any other form of electronic communication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technological assistance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The panel may give directions to facilitate the
  conduct of a hearing by the use of any electronic or digital means of
  communication or storage or retrieval of information, or any other
  technology it considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence taken outside hearing

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may serve and file a notice of motion for an order to examine an
  individual who is unable to attend a hearing for the purpose of
  introducing that individual's evidence at the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions for taking evidence outside
  hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the motion is granted, the panel must give
  directions respecting

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the time, place and manner of the examination and
    cross-examination;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the notice to be given to the individual being
    examined and the parties;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the attendance of other witnesses; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any documents to be produced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearing in absence of party

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A hearing may proceed
even though a party fails to appear before the panel if the panel is
satisfied that the party received proper notice of the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  party must file, by no later than the 30th day before the first
  scheduled day of the hearing, a list of the documents, other than any
  expert reports referred to under rule 22, that it intends to introduce
  into evidence at the hearing and a copy of each of those documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service on other parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The party must serve the list on the other parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Undisclosed matters

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A party may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} raise an issue at the hearing only if that issue was
  raised under rule 18, 19, 20 or 21;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} call a witness at the hearing only if that witness
  was identified and a summary of their anticipated testimony was
  provided under rule 18, 19, 20 or 21;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (e), introduce a document into
  evidence at the hearing only if that document was disclosed under rule
  18, 19, 20 or 21, provided under rule 23 and filed under subrule
  36(1);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} only make submissions at the hearing about an order
  sought under section 53 of the Act if the party identified that order
  under rule 18, 19 or 20; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} call an expert witness or introduce an expert report
  into evidence at the hearing only if the party complied with rule 22.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admission of documents into evidence

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraph 37(c),
a document is admitted into evidence only when the document is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} introduced at the hearing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designated as an exhibit by the panel.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  panel may order that a witness be excluded from the hearing room until
  they are called to give evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The panel must not order the exclusion of a witness
  who is a party or whose presence is necessary to instruct a party's
  representative, but the panel may require such a witness to give
  evidence before any other witness is called.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication with excluded witnesses

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** Unless authorized by the
panel, a person may not communicate with a witness who has been excluded
under subrule 39(1) regarding evidence given during their absence, or
provide them with access to a recording or transcript of the hearing,
until after they have given their testimony.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication with witnesses during
examination

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The panel may give
directions restricting any communication with a witness during the
examination of that witness.

## [Book of Authorities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298525 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party may serve and file a book of authorities, to be used during
  closing arguments, containing copies of the legislative provisions,
  case law and other legal authorities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Highlighted passages

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The relevant passages in each legal authority must be
  highlighted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronically available case law

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For case law that is accessible to the public
  electronically, a party need only include in the book of authorities

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevant passages, along with the paragraphs
    immediately preceding and following those passages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the case law citation, including the Internet
    address where the case law can be accessed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronically available legislation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For legislation that is accessible to the public
  electronically, a party need only include in the book of authorities

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevant provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the legislative citation, including the Internet
    address where the legislation can be accessed.

## [Time Limit for Decision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final decision

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of a decision under section 53 of the Act, the panel must issue
  its decision no later than six months after the day on which the
  presentation of arguments and evidence by the parties is concluded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision on motion

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a decision on a motion, the panel must
  issue its decision no later than three months after the day on which
  the presentation of arguments and evidence by the parties is concluded

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of extension

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** The panel must give
notice to the parties of an extension to the time limits referred to in
rule 43.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reassignment

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** The Chairperson may,
after consulting the parties, assign an inquiry to a different panel if
the deliberation period is unduly long and a decision cannot be rendered
in the foreseeable future.

## [Interest on Orders to Pay Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298549 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Interest awarded under
subsection 53(4) of the Act must be simple interest that is equivalent
to the bank rate established by the Bank of Canada and must accrue from
the day on which the discriminatory practice occurred until the day on
which the award of compensation is paid.

## [Tribunal's Official Record]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  each inquiry, the Registrar must keep an official record that contains

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the complaint;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the request to institute an inquiry by the
    Commission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the statements of particulars and any responses or
    replies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any motion materials;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any correspondence between the Registrar and the
    parties;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any summaries of case management conferences;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any book of authorities;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any written submissions;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any orders, rulings or decisions;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any exhibits;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any recordings of the hearing and any transcripts
    of those recordings; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} any other documents that are designated by the
    Panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public access

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any confidentiality measures or orders
  under section 52 of the Act, the public may access the Tribunal's
  official record under such terms and conditions as specified by the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Chairperson may establish the retention period
  for the official record in respect of any inquiry that has concluded
  and for which any judicial review or appeal proceedings have been
  exhausted.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1298572 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}30th day after registration

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** These Rules come into
force on the 30th day after the day on which they are registered.
