## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-847412 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
      Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [Medical Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282). ([instrument
      médical]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Natural Health Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([produit de santé
      naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-prescription drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-prescription drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drug for
      human use that meets all of the following criteria:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is neither set out in the prescription drug
        list, as amended from time to time, established under subsection
        29.1(1) of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), nor is part
        of a class of drugs that is set out in it;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it does not contain a [[controlled
        substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1)
        of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8),
        the sale or provision of which is authorized under that Act; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it has been assigned a drug identification
        number under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food and Drug
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870). ([drogue vendue
        sans ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the
  expressions [[drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  [[label]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meanings as in section 2
  of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27).

## [Non-application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-847425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Tobacco and Vaping Products
Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Act does not apply in
respect of the following that are vaping products:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following products that do not contain nicotine:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a non-prescription drug, or a natural health
    product in respect of which a product licence has been issued under
    section 7 of the [Natural Health Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196), that is administered by
    a facial steamer or mask,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a non-prescription drug that is authorized to be
    sold for the treatment of respiratory illness or its symptoms, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a natural health product in respect of which a
    product licence has been issued under section 7 of the [Natural
    Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196) and that
    is authorized to be sold for the treatment of respiratory illness or
    its symptoms;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural health product that is authorized for sale
  under Part 4 of the [Natural Health Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a drug that is authorized for sale under Division 5
  or 7 of Part C of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a drug that is authorized for importation under
  subsection C.10.001(2) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or whose sale is exempt,
  under subsection C.10.002(1) of those Regulations, from the provisions
  of those Regulations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a drug that was sold under subsection C.08.011(1) of
  the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a medical device that was sold under section 43.5 of
  the [Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a medical device in respect of which an authorization
  has been issued under subsection 72(1) or 83(1) of the [Medical
  Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282); and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} cannabis and marihuana referred to in sections 2 and
  3, respectively, of the [Cannabis Exemption (Food and Drugs Act)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-231).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-prescription drugs and natural health
products

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the Act,
other than section 30.71, do not apply in respect of a vaping product
that is a non-prescription drug, or a natural health product in respect
of which a product licence has been issued under section 7 of the
[Natural Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196),
that meets all of the following criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is administered to the body by means of a medical
  device other than an [[active device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in section 1 of the [Medical Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its administration to the body does not depend on a
  source of energy other than energy generated by the human body or
  gravity, such as heat, pressure, chemical reaction, magnetism or
  elastic energy;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no reasonable grounds exist to believe that its
  appearance, shape or other sensory attribute or function could make
  the product appealing to young persons; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} its package and labels do not display an indication
  or illustration, including a brand element, for which there are
  reasonable grounds to believe that the indication or illustration
  could be appealing to young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensed medical devices

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  provisions of the Act --- other than those provided for in subsection
  13(1) of the Act --- do not apply in respect of a vaping product that
  is a licensed medical device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Associated products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), in the case where certain
  provisions of the Act do not apply to a vaping product that is a drug
  that has been assigned a drug identification number under subsection
  C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), or that is a natural
  health product in respect of which a product licence has been issued
  under section 7 of the [Natural Health Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196), those same provisions of
  the Act do not apply in respect of a vaping product that is a licensed
  medical device whose license authorizes its sale for use with the drug
  or natural health product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a licensed
  medical device that meets either or both of the following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reasonable grounds exist to believe that its
    appearance, shape or other sensory attribute or function could make
    the product appealing to young persons;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its package or labels display an indication or
    illustration, including a brand element, for which there are
    reasonable grounds to believe that the indication or illustration
    could be appealing to young persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[licensed medical
  device]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[licensed medical
  device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical device in respect of
  which a licence has been issued under subsection 36(1) of the [Medical
  Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescription vaping products

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The provisions of the Act
--- other than those provided for in subsection 13(1) of the Act --- do
not apply in respect of a prescription vaping product that has been
assigned a drug identification number under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of
the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-prescription drugs and natural health
products

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  7.21, 7.22 and 30.47 and paragraph 30.5(a) of the Act do not apply in
  respect of a vaping product that is a non-prescription drug or a
  natural health product in respect of which a product licence has been
  issued under section 7 of the [Natural Health Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a non-prescription
  drug or natural health product referred to in, as the case may be,
  paragraph 2(a) or section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Name of manufacturer

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 30.3(1) and (2) and
  section 30.4 of the Act do not apply in respect of, as the case may
  be, the promotion, use or display of the name of the manufacturer of a
  vaping product --- in respect of which one of the following has been
  assigned or issued --- in the case where the manufacturer does not
  sell or advertise a vaping product that is manufactured, sold or
  represented for a purpose other than for use in the diagnosis,
  treatment, mitigation or prevention of a disease, disorder or abnormal
  physical state, or its symptoms, in human beings:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a drug identification number assigned for a drug
    under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued in respect of a medical device
    under subsection 36(1) of the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a product licence issued in respect of a natural
    health product under section 7 of the [Natural Health Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In subsection (1),
  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
  following provisions, as the case may be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a drug, by section A.01.010 of the
    [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a medical device, by section 1 of the
    [Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a natural health product, by
    subsection 1(1) of the [Natural Health Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196).
