## Critical Habitat of the Eastern Sand Darter (Ammocrypta pellucida) Ontario Populations Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-eastern-sand-darter-ammocrypta-pellucida-ontario-populations-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-265
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2017-12-04

Critical Habitat of the Eastern Sand Darter (Ammocrypta pellucida)
Ontario Populations Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Eastern Sand Darter ([Ammocrypta pellucida]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Ontario populations is a wildlife species that is listed as
a threatened species in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBGA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a place
referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBEA} of that Act and, under
subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBEA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that
the annexed Order would affect reserves or other lands that are set
apart for the use and benefit of bands and, pursuant to subsection 58(7)
of that Act, has consulted with the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development and the bands in question with respect to the
Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Eastern Sand Darter ([Ammocrypta pellucida]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Ontario Populations Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-265).

Ottawa, November 30, 2017

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Eastern Sand Darter ([Ammocrypta pellucida]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Ontario populations --- which is identified in the recovery
strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public
Registry --- other than the portion of that critical habitat that is in
a place referred to in subsection 58(2) of that Act, more specifically,
in the Long Point National Wildlife Area, as described in Part IV of
Schedule I to the [Wildlife Area
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into Force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
