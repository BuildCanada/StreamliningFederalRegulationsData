## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501609 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organization
    approved by the Regulator under subsection 40(2). ([autorité de
    certification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[control system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any system, station or
    panel used to control the operation and monitor the status of the
    equipment used for an offshore renewable energy project or offshore
    power line. ([système de contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operations site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operations site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a site where
    authorized work or activities are carried on in relation to an
    offshore renewable energy project or offshore power
    line. ([emplacement des opérations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant for or holder of
    an authorization referred to in section 298 of the
    Act. ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[physical and environmental conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[physical and environmental conditions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    any physical, geotechnical, seismic, oceanographic, meteorological
    or ice conditions that might affect authorized work or
    activities. ([conditions physiques et
    environnementales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reportable incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reportable incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the
    following incidents that occur in relation to an offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loss of life;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an injury that prevents an employee from
      reporting for work or from effectively performing all the duties
      connected with the employee's regular work on any day subsequent
      to the day on which the injury occurred, whether or not that
      subsequent day is a working day for that employee;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a fire or explosion;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a collision;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any incident that has an adverse effect on the
      environment, produces debris or introduces into the environment
      any substance or form of energy that is likely to have an adverse
      effect on the environment, unless that adverse effect, production
      or introduction is authorized or explicitly anticipated in an
      application for an authorization;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any incident that results in damage to an
      archaeological site or burial ground;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a significant disruption to authorized work or
      activities due to the carrying on of an activity, or the
      introduction into the environment of a substance, by a person
      unrelated to the project or power line;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} an impairment to the reliability of the power
      system of the project or power line;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the impairment of any facility, equipment or
      system that is critical to safety, security or environmental
      protection or the impairment of a support craft;

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any incident that results in the implementation
      of emergency response procedures. ([incident à
      signaler]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[support craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[support craft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel, vehicle,
    aircraft or other conveyance used to provide transportation or
    assistance to persons at an operations site. ([véhicule de
    service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501636 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carrying on work or activities

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 298(2) of the Act, information submitted in an application
for an authorization must demonstrate that the work or activities will
be carried on in a manner that is safe and secure, that protects
property and the environment, that is in conformity with all applicable
laws in respect of safety, security and environmental protection and
that takes into account the potential impacts of climate change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Level of detail

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The information submitted
in support of an application and all documents and information submitted
to the Regulator to meet a condition of an authorization must provide a
level of detail that is proportionate to the scope, nature and
complexity of the proposed work or activities.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Offshore Renewable Energy --- Wind Energy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1501641 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1501642 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of offshore renewable energy projects that exploit or aim to
exploit the power of the wind to generate electricity and in respect of
the offshore power lines associated with those projects.

### [Application for Authorization and Conditions of Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1501645 .Subheading}

#### [Requirements for All Applications]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1501646 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 298(2) of the Act, every application for authorization to
carry on work or activities in relation to an offshore renewable energy
project or offshore power line must include the following information
and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the authorized
  representative of the operator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development concept for the project or power line
  as a whole, including any plans for overlapping or concurrent
  authorizations, for the proposed work or activities to be undertaken
  in stages or for the overall project or power line to be developed in
  stages;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the work or activities that are the
  subject of the application, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the objectives to be attained,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the scope of the proposed work or activities, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} maps or charts showing the preferred locations of
    all operations sites, as well as any other locations that are being
    considered;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the anticipated use of support
  craft, of any other vessel, vehicle, aircraft or conveyance and of
  associated equipment and systems in carrying on the work or activities
  that are the subject of the application;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an execution plan and a schedule for undertaking the
  work or activities that are the subject of the application, including,
  if applicable, an indication of the need for any overlapping or
  concurrent authorizations or for the work or activities to be
  undertaken in stages;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the results of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} relevant surveys and monitoring programs, including
    those in respect of previously authorized work or activities in
    relation to the project or power line, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} relevant analyses and assessments based on the
    data from such surveys and monitoring programs;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the results referred to in
  paragraph (f) were taken into account in the planning of the work or
  activities that are the subject of the application;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to conform
  with all applicable laws in relation to safety, security or
  environmental protection;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the potential effects that the work
  or activities that are the subject of the application may have on any
  other users of the operations site;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a list of all the permits and other authorizations
  that are required in relation to the work or activities that are the
  subject of the application;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the target levels of safety, security and
  environmental protection that the operator proposes to establish under
  section 30 for the work or activities that are the subject of the
  application; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} if the operator is proposing to carry on the work or
  activities that are the subject of the application in a manner that
  differs from what was proposed in a previous application, a detailed
  description of the proposed changes, the rationale for those changes
  and a description of their effect.

#### [Conditions of Every Authorization]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1501666 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}System and plans

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 298(9) of the Act, every authorization is
  subject to the condition that the operator, before the authorized work
  or activities begin, develop and implement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a management system that meets the requirements set
    out in section 8;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a safety plan that meets the requirements set out
    in section 9;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an environmental protection plan that meets the
    requirements set out in section 10; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an emergency management plan that meets the
    requirements set out in section 11.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission and approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every authorization is also subject to the condition
  that the operator submit the following information and plans to the
  Regulator and obtain the Regulator's approval of the information
  referred to in paragraph (b) and the plans before the authorized work
  or activities begin:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, position and contact information of the
    accountable person designated under subsection 24(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the management system, an
    explanation of how it has been implemented and a statement, signed
    by the accountable person, confirming that it has been implemented;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the safety plan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the environmental protection plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the emergency management plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Management system

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  management system must establish the overall framework for reducing
  risks to safety, security and the environment, including measures for
  ensuring the reliable operation of the facilities, equipment and
  systems related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore
  power line and for ensuring an appropriate response in case of an
  emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Overarching requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The management system must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be commensurate with the scope, nature and
    complexity of the authorized work or activities and the associated
    hazards and risks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cover all of the authorized work or activities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be explicit, comprehensive and proactive; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} foster a culture of safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The management system must identify the management
  system standards on which it is based.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The management system must include overarching
  policies, processes, procedures and protocols related to safety,
  security, environmental protection, the reliability of facilities,
  equipment and systems, and emergency response, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} processes for establishing and maintaining
    measurable goals and associated performance indicators for the
    management system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} processes for ensuring that persons who are
    assigned duties or who carry on authorized work or activities in
    relation to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power
    line have the necessary experience, training, qualifications and
    competence, and receive the supervision that is necessary, for them
    to perform the duties or carry on the work or activities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} policies setting out the roles, responsibilities
    and authority of all persons performing functions under the
    management system, as well as processes for making them aware of
    their roles, responsibilities and authority;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} processes for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identifying hazards that may arise during the
      authorized work or activities, including hazards related to human
      and organizational factors,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assessing the risks associated with the
      identified hazards and determining the measures to be taken to
      minimize those risks,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} establishing an inventory of the identified
      hazards and the measures to be taken to minimize the associated
      risks, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} keeping that inventory up to date;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} processes for internally and externally
    communicating documents and information related to safety, security,
    environmental protection, the reliability of facilities, equipment
    and systems, and emergency response, including processes for
    informing persons who may be exposed to a hazard of the measures
    that will be taken to minimize the risks associated with the hazard;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} processes for coordinating and managing the work of
    the operator, suppliers, service providers and other persons
    involved in carrying on the authorized work or activities, including
    processes for ensuring that the processes and procedures put in
    place by service providers are consistent with those put in place by
    the operator;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} processes for auditing and evaluating the
    management system to ensure that it is functioning and achieving the
    measurable goals and associated performance indicators referred to
    in paragraph (a) and processes for ensuring that, if any
    deficiencies or areas for improvement are identified, the management
    system is updated to correct those deficiencies and make
    improvements;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} processes for inspecting, monitoring, testing and
    maintaining all facilities, equipment and systems related to the
    offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line, including
    those set out in the integrity management program referred to in
    paragraph 18(a), if applicable, and processes for ensuring that, if
    any deficiencies or areas for improvement are identified, measures
    are taken to correct those deficiencies and make improvements;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} processes for ensuring continuous improvement,
    including processes for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} internally reporting hazards, reportable
      incidents and situations that have the potential to result in a
      reportable incident,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} analyzing the hazards, reportable incidents and
      situations and conducting investigations,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} identifying the root cause and causal factors
      of, and contributing factors to, the hazards, reportable incidents
      and situations, including any human or organizational factors,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} analyzing trends in respect of the hazards,
      reportable incidents and situations,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} taking corrective and preventive measures,
      including measures to manage imminent hazards,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} providing feedback to persons who have made a
      report, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} communicating documents and information
      concerning best practices or lessons learned from the hazards,
      reportable incidents and situations that might improve safety,
      security, environmental protection, the reliability of facilities,
      equipment and systems, and emergency response;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} processes for identifying, evaluating and managing
    any changes that could affect safety, security, environmental
    protection, the reliability of facilities, equipment and systems,
    and emergency response;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} processes for receiving information and fielding
    concerns related to safety, security and environmental protection
    from the general public and for reviewing and responding to such
    information and concerns;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} processes for ensuring that all documents
    associated with the management system are approved by a person with
    the necessary authority, reviewed periodically and, if necessary,
    updated;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} processes for ensuring that records associated with
    the management system and records that are necessary to support
    operational requirements or demonstrate compliance with all laws
    related to safety, security and environmental protection that apply
    in respect of the offshore renewable energy project or offshore
    power line are generated, classified by type, organized and retained
    and are readily accessible for consultation and examination by
    persons who require access to them; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} processes for monitoring compliance and preventing
    non-compliance with the conditions of the authorization and all laws
    related to safety, security and environmental protection that apply
    in respect of the offshore renewable energy project or offshore
    power line.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety plan

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safety plan must set out the procedures, practices and resources, as
  well as the sequence of key safety-related activities, that are
  necessary to safely carry on the authorized work or activities and
  must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the safety plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} integrates and reflects the processes and
      principles included in the management system that relate to
      safety, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensures that the obligations set out in these
      Regulations with regard to safety are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the studies and a description of the
    processes used to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identify the safety hazards that may arise during
      the authorized work or activities, including any safety hazards
      that may arise due to activities taking place in the vicinity of
      the operations site, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assess the safety risks associated with the
      identified hazards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the hazards identified under
    subparagraph (b)(i) and the results of the risk assessment under
    subparagraph (b)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    anticipate, monitor, avoid and minimize the safety risks associated
    with the identified hazards, including, if hazards related to ice or
    icing are identified, measures for detecting, anticipating,
    monitoring and reporting those hazards, as well as measures for data
    collection and, if applicable, for ice avoidance or deflection;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the anticipated effectiveness of
    the measures referred to in paragraph (d) and of how they will
    assist in meeting the target levels of safety referred to in section
    30;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods that are to be used to
    communicate with persons who may be directly affected by the
    identified hazards regarding the existence of those hazards and the
    measures that will be taken to minimize the associated safety risks;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of all the facilities, equipment and
    systems that are critical to safety and are to be used during the
    authorized work or activities and a summary of the procedures and
    policies to be put in place for their inspection, testing and
    maintenance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of all support craft and
    other vessels, vehicles, aircraft or conveyances that are to be
    used;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the training required by the
    persons who will carry on the authorized work or activities and the
    experience, qualifications and competencies that they must possess;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the authorized work or activities
    that involve diving operations and the measures to be taken to
    ensure that those operations are carried out safely and in
    accordance with the prevailing industry standards and best practices
    that are appropriate for the water depth and type of diving
    involved;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    monitor compliance with the safety plan and to evaluate performance
    in relation to the plan's objectives, including data collection and
    analysis, inspections and auditing;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and
    the chain of command for ensuring that the authorized work or
    activities are carried on safely; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the processes to be put in place
    to identify, evaluate and manage any changes related to the
    authorized work or activities that could affect safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service providers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator contracts for the provision of
  services with respect to the authorized work or activities, the safety
  plan must also include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the procurement process takes
    into consideration service providers' past performance in relation
    to safety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of service providers' roles and
    responsibilities with respect to safety;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    oversee the work or activities of service providers and to ensure
    that service providers comply with the safety plan; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the operator and service
    providers will communicate with each other regarding any safety
    issues that may arise during the work or activities.

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods to be used to

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of all the facilities, equipment and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and

    activities are carried on in a manner that minimizes environmental

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedure to be followed if an

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    monitor compliance with the environmental protection plan and to

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency management plan

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  emergency management plan must set out the emergency response
  procedures referred to in subsection (2) and any other procedures,
  practices and resources that are necessary to effectively prepare for,
  manage and respond to an emergency and must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the emergency management plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} integrates and reflects the processes and
      principles included in the management system that relate to
      emergency response, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensures that the obligations set out in these
      Regulations with regard to emergency response are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of incidents, in
    relation to the authorized work or activities, that may require an
    emergency response, an assessment of the risks associated with each
    type of incident and a description of the planned emergency response
    for each type of incident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and
    the chain of command for emergency response;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a method for classifying incidents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the incident management system to
    be used for emergency response;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods that are to be used to
    monitor, constantly and in real time, both the facilities used in
    the generation and transmission of electrical energy and the work or
    activities, including at night and during periods of poor

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the necessary experience, training,
    qualifications and competence to perform the duties or carry on the
    work or activities in accordance with these Regulations and in a
    manner that ensures safety, security and environmental protection;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are sufficient in number and receive the
    supervision that is necessary for them to perform the duties or
    carry on the work or activities in a manner that ensures safety,
    security and environmental protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigational safety zones

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must take measures to protect facilities, equipment and
  systems at an operations site against collision with vessels,
  vehicles, aircraft and other conveyances operating in the vicinity of
  the facilities, equipment and systems from the time their construction
  begins until their decommissioning and abandonment is completed,
  including by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing the navigational safety zones referred
    to in paragraph 14(e), 18(d) or 21(b), as the case may be, that are
    approved by the Regulator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the following information is made
    available to other persons in the offshore area and brought to the
    attention of the Canadian Coast Guard, the Department of Transport
    and the Canadian Hydrographic Service of the Department of Fisheries
    and Oceans:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location of the operations site and of the
      facilities, equipment and systems,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the navigational safety zones,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the risks associated with
      navigation in the vicinity of the facilities, equipment and
      systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundaries of navigational safety zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment of the boundaries of a navigational
  safety zone must be informed by the risk assessment referred to in
  paragraph 13(d) or 20(d) as it relates to the risks associated with
  navigation in the vicinity of the facilities, equipment or systems at
  the operations site, but the distance between the outer extremity of
  the facilities, equipment or systems and the perimeter of the safety
  zone must not exceed 500 m in any direction, except as permitted under
  paragraph 5 of Article 60 of Part V of the United Nations Convention
  on the Law of the Sea.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routing systems and measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A navigational safety zone must not interfere with
  the use of established routing systems and routing measures related to
  navigational safety.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design requirements

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must ensure that all facilities, equipment and systems
  related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power
  line are designed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in such a way as to ensure safety, security and
    environmental protection, including by achieving the target levels
    of safety, security and environmental protection established under
    section 30;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} using the best available technologies for
    minimizing adverse effects on persons, infrastructure and the
    environment that are economically feasible to implement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with prevailing industry standards
    and best practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be taken into account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The design of the facilities, equipment and systems
  must take into account their intended use, their location and any
  site-specific physical and environmental conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical and environmental conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that every facility related
  to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line is
  designed to withstand or avoid all reasonably foreseeable
  site-specific physical and environmental conditions, including any
  reasonably foreseeable combination of those conditions, without its
  structural integrity or any associated equipment or systems that are
  critical to safety, security and environmental protection being
  compromised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Target levels

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must establish measurable target levels of safety, security
  and environmental protection for each work or activity carried on in
  relation to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power
  line.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quantitative or qualitative assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The target levels of safety, security and
  environmental protection must be based on a quantitative assessment
  unless quantitative assessment methods are inappropriate or the
  quantitative data is not reliable, in which case the target levels
  must be based on a qualitative assessment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Critical software

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must ensure that any software that is critical to safety,
  security or environmental protection is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} secure, reliable and capable of being updated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} designed, commissioned and updated in a competent
    manner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} demonstrated to be fit for the purposes for which
    it is to be used through a testing and validation process that takes
    into consideration

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all foreseeable operating conditions and
      emergency situations, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} system complexity, the dependencies and
      interactions among systems, software failure modes and the level
      of risk associated with system failure or malfunction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modifications to software features

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The features of the software referred to in
  subsection (1) must not be modified unless the necessary internal
  approvals for the modification are obtained, including the approval of
  the facility manager.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testing and validation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The modified software must not be used until it has
  been tested and validated in accordance with paragraph (1)(c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Software-dependent control systems

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** If the failure or
malfunction of any integrated software-dependent control systems would
cause a hazard to safety, security or the environment, the operator must
ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the control systems are designed, installed,
  commissioned and maintained in such a way as to ensure their
  reliability, availability and security; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} measures are implemented and maintained to protect
  the control systems from any threat, including unauthorized access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescue boat

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that a rescue boat is available and ready for use in the event of an
emergency at all times when personnel are present in the offshore area
for the purposes of carrying on authorized work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for vessels

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that every vessel that is used to carry on authorized work or activities
is suitable for the operations site at which it is used and for the
nature of the work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evacuation systems and equipment

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** In determining the number
of persons that any lifeboat, life raft or marine evacuation system can
accommodate, the operator must take into consideration each person's
weight while the person is wearing an immersion suit, as well as the
space they require while wearing such a suit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Third-party assessment

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that a competent third party verifies all equipment that is installed
temporarily on a vessel that is used in connection with any authorized
work or activities to ensure that the equipment is fit for the purpose
for which it is to be used and is secured in a seaworthy manner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Landing area

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  personnel will be transported by aircraft to or within an operations
  site or between operations sites, the operator must ensure that the
  landing area for the aircraft, or for any person or cargo being raised
  to or lowered from the aircraft, is designed to ensure safety,
  security and environmental protection, including the prevention of
  incidents or damage due to the use of an aircraft.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that the landing area

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not contain any obstacles that may impede the
    safe landing or takeoff of aircraft or the safe disembarkation or
    embarkation of persons or cargo and, if applicable, is oriented
    relative to the prevailing winds;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is able to withstand all functional loads that may
    be imposed on it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has conspicuous markings and signage; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has adequate lighting, including for reduced
    visibility conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specific requirements --- landing and
  takeoff

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that any area used for the
  landing or takeoff of aircraft is capable of accommodating aircraft of
  expected sizes and is equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} emergency response and firefighting equipment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a monitoring system and status light system;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} communication and meteorological equipment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the landing area is on a facility, fuel storage
    tanks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedures and training program

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} procedures with respect to the support of aircraft
    operations, including procedures for emergency response, are
    developed and implemented; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a training program is provided for personnel and
    passengers in respect of those procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aircraft service provider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before any authorized work or activities that require
  the use of an aircraft begin, the operator must ensure that the
  aircraft service provider has accepted in writing to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} comply with all conditions stipulated by the
    operator with respect to the use of the equipment on any landing
    area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} implement the procedures referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all personnel and passengers follow the
    training program referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to documents and information

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that a copy of the following documents and information is readily
accessible at each operations site when personnel are present and at all
times at every associated control centre and emergency response
operations centre:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every authorization issued in respect of work or
  activities related to the offshore renewable energy project or
  offshore power line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all information and documents submitted to the
  Regulator to meet a condition of authorization, including the safety
  plan, environmental protection plan and emergency management plan, as
  well as supporting documentation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all procedures and other documents that are necessary
  to carry on authorized work or activities in a manner that ensures
  safety, security and environmental protection.

### [Certifying Authority and Certificate of Fitness]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1502055 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certifying authority

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must engage the services of an organization to serve as a
  certifying authority.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval by Regulator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The organization engaged by the operator must be
  approved as a certifying authority by the Regulator based on the
  organization's qualifications to assume the responsibilities referred
  to in subsection (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibilities of certifying
  authority

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority is responsible for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conducting an independent assessment of the
    facility design report referred to in paragraph 14(a), including an
    assessment of the design inputs, the methodologies used in the
    design and the results of any technical studies undertaken in
    support of the design, and providing a certification statement to
    the operator that confirms that the proposed design of the
    facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line is in accordance with good
    engineering practices, the authorization and all applicable laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conducting an independent assessment of the
    fabrication and construction report referred to in paragraph 14(b)
    and providing a certification statement to the operator that
    confirms that the proposed fabrication, construction and
    installation of the facilities, equipment and systems related to the
    offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line are in
    accordance with good engineering practices, the authorization and
    all applicable laws;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} conducting an independent assessment of the
    integrity management program referred to in paragraph 18(a) and
    providing a certification statement to the operator that confirms
    that the proposed measures to ensure the integrity of the
    facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line are in accordance with good
    engineering practices, the authorization and all applicable laws;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} monitoring and carrying out inspections related to
    the fabrication, transportation, construction, installation and
    commissioning of the facilities, equipment and systems related to
    the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line,
    including any major maintenance or modifications;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} assessing whether the facilities, equipment and
    systems related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore
    power line have been fabricated, constructed and installed in
    accordance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} good engineering practices,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fabrication and construction report referred
      to in paragraph 14(b), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the authorization;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} verifying that the facilities, equipment and
    systems related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore
    power line are fit for the purposes for which they are to be used
    and can be operated without posing a threat to safety, security or
    the environment;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} issuing a certificate of fitness in respect of the
    facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} periodically performing on-site or remote
    inspections of the facilities, equipment and systems related to the
    offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line to verify
    their continued integrity in accordance with the integrity
    management program referred to in paragraph 18(a) and ensure that
    they remain fit for the purposes for which they are to be used and
    can be operated without posing a threat to safety, security or the
    environment;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} validating the effectiveness of any measures
    implemented by the operator to minimize risks in relation to safety,
    security and the environment that are associated with the
    facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} verifying that the certificate of fitness remains
    valid for as long as the facilities, equipment and systems related
    to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line
    remain in operation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operator's obligations

**[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the certifying authority has, for the purpose of
  fullfilling its responsibilities, access to the facilities, equipment
  and systems related to the offshore renewable energy project or
  offshore power line, as well as to the necessary documentation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the certificate of fitness issued under subsection
  44(1) remains valid for as long as the facilities, equipment and
  systems remain in operation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of work

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The certifying authority
must submit a scope of work to the Regulator for approval and obtain the
Regulator's approval before undertaking the relevant activities referred
to in subsection 40(3) in relation to the construction, operation and
maintenance of the facilities, equipment and systems related to an
offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Performance of functions

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** In performing its
functions, the certifying authority must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out the scope of work that was approved by the
  Regulator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use good engineering judgment and practices; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that any person that is tasked with carrying
  out an activity referred to in subsection 40(3) has the necessary
  experience, training, qualifications and competence to do so.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate of fitness

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certifying authority may, on the basis of the activities referred to
  in subsection 40(3), issue a certificate of fitness in respect of the
  facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
  energy project or offshore power line.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fitness for purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificate of fitness must confirm that the
  facilities, equipment and systems are fit for the purposes for which
  they are to be used and can be operated without posing a threat to
  safety, security or the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitations on operation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certificate of fitness may set out limitations in
  relation to the operation of the facilities, equipment and systems
  that the certifying authority considers necessary to ensure that their
  operation does not pose a threat to safety, security or the
  environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy provided to Regulator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority must provide a copy of the
  certificate of fitness to the Regulator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection plans

- **[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certifying authority must prepare a plan for the periodic inspection
  of the facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore
  renewable energy project or offshore power line during their
  construction and during their operation and maintenance and provide
  those plans to the Regulator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review of operator's records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The inspection plan must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include a schedule for the periodic review of the
    operator's records from the management system to identify any
    changes to the management system that might affect safety, security
    or environmental protection; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be updated as needed to ensure that the facilities,
    equipment and systems are inspected in light of those changes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope and frequency of inspections

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority must establish the scope and
  frequency of the periodic inspections based on a risk assessment
  conducted in accordance with prevailing industry standards and best
  practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes to inspection plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority must, as soon as
  circumstances permit, inform the Regulator of any material changes to
  the inspection plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Independence and impartiality

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certifying authority must not issue a certificate of fitness in
  respect of the facilities, equipment and systems related to the
  offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line unless the
  certifying authority is able to fulfill its responsibilities under
  subsection 40(3) with independence and impartiality, including by
  ensuring that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a separation is maintained between the
    certification functions that it or any of its subsidiaries or
    affiliates perform and any work that it or any of its subsidiaries
    or affiliates undertake with respect to the design, construction,
    transportation, installation, establishment or commissioning of the
    facilities, equipment and systems; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} barriers and processes are in place to prevent
    real, potential or perceived conflicts of interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of conflict of interest

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority must monitor for real,
  potential or perceived conflicts of interest throughout the period
  that it acts as certifying authority for the operator and must,
  without delay, notify the operator and the Regulator of any such
  conflicts that are identified.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of certifying authority

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  operator decides to replace the certifying authority with a new
  certifying authority, the operator must provide the name of the
  organization that the operator proposes to engage as the new
  certifying authority to the Regulator, along with a description of the
  organization's qualifications to assume the responsibilities referred
  to in subsection 40(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Before issuance of certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator engages a new certifying authority
  before the initial certificate of fitness is issued, the new
  certifying authority must carry out its own independent assessment,
  monitoring, inspection, verification and validation activities for the
  purposes of issuing the certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}After issuance of certificate

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the operator engages a new certifying authority
  after a certificate of fitness has been issued, the operator must
  develop and submit to the Regulator a transition plan outlining all of
  the activities to be carried out by the parties before the transition
  from the former to the new certifying authority and demonstrating that
  the transition will not result in any gaps, delays or negative effects
  on the extent and quality of the activities referred to in subsection
  40(3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation of transition plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that the transition plan is
  implemented.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One certificate and one authority

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} There must be no more than one certificate of fitness
  and one certifying authority in relation to an offshore renewable
  energy project or offshore power line at any given time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
end of each calendar year during which authorized work or activities are
carried on in relation to the construction, operation and maintenance of
facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
energy project or offshore power line, the certifying authority must
submit a report for that year to the Regulator that includes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an overview of the activities that it carried out
  during the calendar year in its capacity as certifying authority;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement, signed by the certifying authority,
  confirming that it has maintained the qualifications it possessed when
  it was approved by the Regulator as a certifying authority;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, position, contact information and
  qualifications of the persons that were involved in carrying out the
  activities referred to in subsection 40(3);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a description of the methodologies
  used to conduct the independent assessments referred to in paragraphs
  40(3)(a) to (c) and the assessment referred to in paragraph 40(3)(e)
  and the results of those assessments;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the inspections performed
  during the calendar year, including their scope and the methodology
  used, as well as the results;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the activities that it carried out
  to validate the effectiveness of any measures implemented by the
  operator to minimize risks in relation to safety, security and the
  environment and the results of that validation.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Offshore Renewable Energy --- Other Than Wind Energy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1502133 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1502134 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of offshore renewable energy projects --- other than those that
exploit or aim to exploit the power of the wind to generate electricity
--- and the offshore power lines associated with those projects.

### [Requirements for All Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1502137 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 298(2) of the Act, every application for authorization to
carry on work or activities in relation to an offshore renewable energy
project or offshore power line must include the following information
and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the authorized
  representative of the operator;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development concept for the project or power line
  as a whole, including any plans for overlapping or concurrent
  authorizations, for the proposed work or activities to be undertaken
  in stages or for the overall project or power line to be developed in
  stages;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the work or activities that are the
  subject of the application, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the objectives to be attained,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the scope of the proposed work or activities, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} maps or charts showing the preferred locations of
    all operations sites, as well as any other locations that are being
    considered;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the anticipated use of support
  craft, of any other vessel, vehicle, aircraft or conveyance and of
  associated equipment and systems in carrying on the work or activities
  that are the subject of the application;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an execution plan and a schedule for undertaking the
  work or activities that are the subject of the application, including,
  if applicable, an indication of the need for any overlapping or
  concurrent authorizations or for the work or activities to be
  undertaken in stages;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the results of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} relevant surveys and monitoring programs, including
    those in respect of previously authorized work or activities in
    relation to the project or power line, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} relevant analyses and assessments based on the
    data from such surveys and monitoring programs;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the results referred to in
  paragraph (f) were taken into account in the planning of the work or
  activities that are the subject of the application;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to conform
  with all applicable laws in relation to safety, security or
  environmental protection;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the potential effects that the work
  or activities that are the subject of the application may have on any
  other users of the operations site;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a list of all the permits and other authorizations
  that are required in relation to the work or activities that are the
  subject of the application;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} if the operator is proposing to carry on the work or
  activities that are the subject of the application in a manner that
  differs from what was proposed in a previous application, a detailed
  description of the proposed changes, the rationale for those changes
  and a description of their effect;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a risk assessment in respect of the proposed work or
  activities that addresses the safety and security of persons and
  infrastructure that are at or in the vicinity of an operations site;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} an environmental and socio-economic assessment in
  respect of the proposed work or activities;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} an assessment in respect of the proposed work or
  activities that addresses the factors referred to in paragraphs
  298(3)(d) and (e) of the Act;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} a list of the industry standards and best practices
  to be used in the design of the facilities, equipment and systems that
  are to be used in the generation and transmission of electrical
  energy, as well as any data sets to be used in establishing
  operational conditions and extreme loading conditions;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} a description of the facilities, equipment and
  systems that are to be used in the generation and transmission of
  electrical energy and their proposed location, capabilities and
  limitations, including, in the case of an offshore power line, maps
  that show

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the preferred location of each
    terminal point and each provincial interconnection point for the
    power line, as well as any other locations that are being
    considered,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the preferred route for the power line, as well as
    any other routes that are being considered,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} constraints and hazards that restrict the
    preferred locations referred to in subparagraph (i) or the preferred
    route referred to in subparagraph (ii), and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the width of the proposed corridor for the power
    line, accompanied by the reasons for the selected width; and

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} the proposed approach to the decommissioning and
  abandonment of the facilities, equipment and systems related to the
  project or power line, an estimate of the costs of the decommissioning
  and abandonment and an indication of the manner in which the operator
  will finance or pay for those costs.

### [Conditions of Every Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1502166 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plans

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 298(9) of the Act, every authorization is
  subject to the condition that the operator, before the authorized work
  or activities begin, develop and implement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a safety plan that meets the requirements set out
    in section 52;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an environmental protection plan that meets the
    requirements set out in section 53; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an emergency management plan that meets the
    requirements set out in section 54.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission and approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every authorization is also subject to the condition
  that the operator submit the following information and plans to the
  Regulator and obtain the Regulator's approval of the information
  referred to in paragraph (e) and the plans before the authorized work
  or activities begin:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, position and contact information of the
    accountable person designated under subsection 55(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety plan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the environmental protection plan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the emergency management plan; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the navigational safety zones
    proposed for the purposes of paragraph 57(1)(a) or a justification
    of why navigational safety zones are not needed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety plan

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  safety plan must set out the procedures, practices and resources , as
  well as the sequence of key safety-related activities, that are
  necessary to safely carry on the authorized work or activities and
  must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the safety plan ensures that
    the obligations set out in these Regulations with regard to safety
    are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the studies and a description of the
    processes used to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identify the safety hazards that may arise during
      the authorized work or activities, including any safety hazards
      that may arise due to activities taking place in the vicinity of
      the operations site, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assess the safety risks associated with the
      identified hazards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the hazards identified under
    subparagraph (b)(i) and the results of the risk assessment under
    subparagraph (b)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    anticipate, monitor, avoid and minimize the safety risks associated
    with the identified hazards, including, if hazards related to ice or
    icing are identified, measures for detecting, anticipating,
    monitoring and reporting those hazards, as well as measures for data
    collection and, if applicable, for ice avoidance or deflection;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the anticipated effectiveness of
    the measures referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods to be used to
    communicate with persons who may be directly affected by the
    identified hazards regarding the existence of those hazards and the
    measures that will be taken to minimize the associated safety risks;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of all the facilities, equipment and
    systems that are critical to safety and are to be used during the
    authorized work or activities and a summary of the procedures and
    policies to be put in place for their inspection, testing and
    maintenance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of all support craft and
    other vessels, vehicles, aircraft or conveyances that are to be
    used;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the training required by the
    persons who will carry on the authorized work or activities and the
    experience, qualifications and competencies that they must possess;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the authorized work or activities
    that involve diving operations and the measures to be taken to
    ensure that those operations are carried out safely and in
    accordance with the prevailing industry standards and best practices
    that are appropriate for the water depth and type of diving
    involved;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    monitor compliance with the safety plan and to evaluate performance
    in relation to the plan's objectives, including data collection and
    analysis, inspections and auditing;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and
    the chain of command for ensuring that the authorized work or
    activities are carried on safely; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a description of the processes to be put in place
    to identify, evaluate and manage any changes related to the
    authorized work or activities that could affect safety.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service providers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator contracts for the provision of
  services with respect to the authorized work or activities, the safety
  plan must also include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the procurement process takes
    into consideration service providers' past performance in relation
    to safety;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of service providers' roles and
    responsibilities with respect to safety;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    oversee the work or activities of service providers and to ensure
    that service providers comply with the safety plan; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the operator and service
    providers will communicate with each other regarding any safety
    issues that may arise during the work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental protection plan

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  environmental protection plan must set out the procedures, practices
  and resources to be put in place to manage environmental hazards and
  protect the environment and must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the environmental protection
    plan ensures that the obligations set out in these Regulations with
    regard to environmental protection are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the studies and a description of the
    processes used to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identify the environmental hazards that may arise
      during the authorized work or activities, including any
      environmental hazards that may arise due to activities taking
      place in the vicinity of the operations site, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assess the environmental risks associated with
      the identified hazards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the hazards identified under
    subparagraph (b)(i) and the results of the risk assessment under
    subparagraph (b)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the measures to be taken to anticipate, monitor,
    avoid and minimize the environmental risks associated with the
    identified hazards;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the anticipated effectiveness of
    the measures referred to in paragraph (d);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods to be used to
    communicate with persons who may be directly affected by the
    identified hazards regarding the existence of those hazards and the
    measures that will be taken to minimize the associated environmental
    risks;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of all the facilities, equipment and
    systems that are critical to the protection of the environment and
    are to be used during the authorized work or activities and a
    summary of the procedures and policies to be put in place for their
    inspection, testing and maintenance;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and
    the chain of command for ensuring that the authorized work or
    activities are carried on in a manner that minimizes environmental
    hazards;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the procedure to be followed if an
    archaeological site or a burial ground is discovered during the
    authorized work or activities;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    monitor compliance with the environmental protection plan and to
    evaluate performance in relation to the plan's objectives, including
    data collection and analysis, inspections and auditing; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of the processes to be put in place
    to identify, evaluate and manage any changes related to the
    authorized work or activities that could affect the protection of
    the environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service providers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the operator contracts for the provision of
  services with respect to the authorized work or activities, the
  environmental protection plan must also include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the procurement process takes
    into consideration service providers' past performance in relation
    to environmental protection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of service providers' roles and
    responsibilities with respect to environmental protection;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to
    oversee the work or activities of service providers and to ensure
    that service providers comply with the environmental protection
    plan; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the operator and service
    providers will communicate with each other regarding any
    environmental issues that may arise during the work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency management plan

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  emergency management plan must set out the emergency response
  procedures referred to in subsection (2) and any other procedures,
  practices and resources that are necessary to effectively prepare for,
  manage and respond to an emergency and must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the emergency management plan
    ensures that the obligations set out in these Regulations with
    regard to emergency response are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of incidents, in
    relation to the authorized work or activities, that may require an
    emergency response, an assessment of the risks associated with each
    type of incident and a description of the planned emergency response
    for each type of incident;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the organizational structure and
    the chain of command for emergency response;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a method for classifying incidents;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the incident management system to
    be used for emergency response;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of the methods to be used to monitor,
    constantly and in real time, both the facilities used in the
    generation and transmission of electrical energy and the work or
    activities, including at night and during periods of poor
    visibility;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the frequency and scope of the emergency response
    drills and exercises to be carried out to test and validate
    emergency response procedures and practices; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures to be taken to ensure
    that first responders, the personnel of relevant medical facilities,
    organizations and agencies, and persons that are carrying on
    activities within or in the vicinity of an operations site are
    informed of, and provided with instructions and updates concerning,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location of the facilities that are to be
      used in the generation and transmission of electrical energy,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the potential emergency situations that may
      arise in relation to those facilities, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed in case of an
      emergency.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency response procedures

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The emergency response procedures must cover the
  following subjects:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} emergency response for each type of incident
    identified under paragraph (1)(b), including the emergency equipment
    that is necessary and is to be made available for emergency response
    to each type of incident;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safe shutdown, in the event of an emergency, of
    facilities, equipment and systems to be used in the generation and
    transmission of electrical energy;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evacuation plans for personnel carrying on work or
    activities in the offshore area, including, if applicable,
    evacuation plans for divers engaged in a dive;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the notification, investigation and reporting of
    incidents under section 64;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the protocols for communicating with the relevant
    federal, provincial and municipal authorities and Indigenous
    governing bodies during an emergency response; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} coordination and liaison with all relevant
    emergency response organizations during an emergency response.

### [Implementation of Plans and Other Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1502248 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must ensure that there are sufficient financial and human
  resources to implement and continually improve the safety plan,
  environmental protection plan and emergency management plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accountable person

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator must designate one of its employees as
  the accountable person for the safety plan, environmental protection
  plan and emergency management plan and ensure that the accountable
  person has the necessary authority for assigning financial and human
  resources to develop, implement and make ongoing improvements to those
  plans.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change --- accountable person

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If there is any change to the name, position or
  contact information of the accountable person that was submitted under
  paragraph 51(2)(a), the operator must provide updated information to
  the Regulator as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conduct of authorized work or activities

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must carry on all authorized work or activities in a manner
  that ensures safety, security and environmental protection by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meeting the requirements of the safety plan,
    environmental protection plan and emergency management plan and
    ensuring that all employees, suppliers, service providers and other
    persons involved in the authorized work or activities meet those
    requirements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} monitoring the compliance of employees, suppliers,
    service providers and other persons involved in the authorized work
    or activities with all conditions of authorization;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensuring the safe and reliable operation of
    facilities, equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable
    energy project or offshore power line;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} taking all reasonable measures to minimize damage
    to property;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} taking all reasonable measures to minimize adverse
    effects for other persons in the offshore area;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} using the best available technologies for
    mitigating adverse effects on persons and the environment that are
    economically feasible to implement;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} complying with prevailing industry standards and
    best practices;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} taking all reasonable measures to prevent debris
    and to prevent or, if prevention is not possible, minimize the
    introduction into the environment of any substance or form of energy
    that is likely to have an adverse effect on the environment; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} taking all reasonable measures to minimize any
    adverse effects on persons and the environment when restoring the
    operations site after the decommissioning and abandonment of the
    facilities, equipment and systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equipment and systems

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all equipment and systems that are used during the
    authorized work or activities are tested, inspected, maintained,
    operated and handled in such a way as to ensure safety, security and
    environmental protection, taking into consideration the
    manufacturer's instructions and any applicable standards; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any defective components of the equipment or
    systems are promptly repaired or replaced, taking into consideration
    the manufacturer's instructions and any applicable standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assignment of duties

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that persons who are
  assigned duties or who carry out authorized work or activities

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have the necessary experience, training,
    qualifications and competence to perform the duties or carry on the
    work or activities in accordance with these Regulations and in a
    manner that ensures safety, security and environmental protection;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are sufficient in number and receive the
    supervision that is necessary for them to perform the duties or
    carry on the work or activities in a manner that ensures safety,
    security and environmental protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigational safety zones

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must take measures to protect facilities, equipment and
  systems at an operations site against collision with vessels,
  vehicles, aircraft and other conveyances operating in the vicinity of
  the facilities, equipment and systems from the time their construction
  begins until their decommissioning and abandonment is completed,
  including by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} establishing the navigational safety zones referred
    to in paragraph 51(2)(e) that are approved by the Regulator; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensuring that the following information is made
    available to other persons in the offshore area and brought to the
    attention of the Canadian Coast Guard, the Department of Transport
    and the Canadian Hydrographic Service of the Department of Fisheries
    and Oceans:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the location of the operations site and of the
      facilities, equipment and systems,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the boundaries of the navigational safety zones,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the risks associated with
      navigation in the vicinity of the facilities, equipment and
      systems.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundaries of navigational safety zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The establishment of the boundaries of a navigational
  safety zone must be informed by the risk assessment referred to in
  paragraph 50(l) as it relates to the risks associated with navigation
  in the vicinity of the facilities, equipment or systems at the
  operations site, but the distance between the outer extremity of the
  facilities, equipment or systems and the perimeter of the safety zone
  must not exceed 500 m in any direction, except as permitted under
  paragraph 5 of Article 60 of Part V of the United Nations Convention
  on the Law of the Sea.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routing systems and measures

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A navigational safety zone must not interfere with
  the use of established routing systems and routing measures related to
  navigational safety.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design requirements

- **[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must ensure that all facilities, equipment and systems
  related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power
  line are designed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in such a way as to ensure safety, security and
    environmental protection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} using the best available technologies for
    minimizing adverse effects on persons, infrastructure and the
    environment that are economically feasible to implement; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with prevailing industry standards
    and best practices.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors to be taken into account

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The design of the facilities, equipment and systems
  must take into account their intended use, their location and any
  site-specific physical and environmental conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical and environmental conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that every facility related
  to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line is
  designed to withstand or avoid all reasonably foreseeable
  site-specific physical and environmental conditions, including any
  reasonably foreseeable combination of those conditions, without its
  structural integrity or any associated equipment or systems that are
  critical to safety, security and environmental protection being
  compromised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rescue boat

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that a rescue boat is available and ready for use in the event of an
emergency at all times when personnel are present in the offshore area
for the purposes of carrying on authorized work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements for vessels

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that every vessel that is used to carry on authorized work or activities
is suitable for the operations site at which it is used and for the
nature of the work or activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evacuation systems and equipment

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** In determining the number
of persons that any lifeboat, life raft or marine evacuation system can
accommodate, the operator must take into consideration each person's
weight while the person is wearing an immersion suit, as well as the
space they require while wearing such a suit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to documents and information

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** The operator must ensure
that a copy of the following documents and information is readily
accessible at each operations site when personnel are present and at all
times at every associated control centre and emergency response
operations centre:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} every authorization issued in respect of work or
  activities related to the offshore renewable energy project or
  offshore power line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all information and documents submitted to the
  Regulator to meet a condition of authorization, including the safety
  plan, environmental protection plan and emergency management plan, as
  well as supporting documentation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all procedures and other documents that are necessary
  to carry on authorized work or activities in a manner that ensures
  safety, security and environmental protection.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Notification, Investigation, Reporting and Record-Keeping Requirements for Operators]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1502309 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of all offshore renewable energy projects and offshore power
lines.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of incidents

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must notify the Regulator of any reportable incident as soon
  as the circumstances permit after the incident occurs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator must ensure that every reportable
  incident is investigated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The operator must, within 14 days after the day on
  which a reportable incident occurs, submit a report to the Regulator
  that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the reportable incident, where and
    when it occurred and who was involved;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the impact of the reportable
    incident on the facilities, equipment and systems related to the
    offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line, as well as
    on vessels, persons and the environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the results of the investigation into the
    reportable incident, including details regarding the root cause of
    the reportable incident and causal and contributing factors;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any repairs undertaken to restore
    any damaged structures to their original design strength or to
    restore the functionality of any impacted equipment or systems;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures put in place to
    prevent the occurrence of similar reportable incidents;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if emergency response procedures were implemented,
    a description of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any communications that occurred with the
      relevant federal, provincial and municipal authorities and
      Indigenous governing bodies, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any coordination and liaison with relevant
      emergency response organizations; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periodic reports

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must submit periodic reports to the Regulator summarizing the
  following in respect of the period that has elapsed since the day on
  which the authorization was issued in respect of the work or
  activities in question or the day after the last day included in the
  preceding periodic report, as the case may be:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the work or activities performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the status of the offshore renewable energy project
    or offshore power line, by reference to the execution plan referred
    to in paragraph 6(e) or 50(e), as the case may be;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any deviations from or changes to the execution
    plan;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any emergency response drills or exercises referred
    to in paragraph 11(1)(g) or 54(1)(g), as the case may be, that were
    carried out;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any instances of non-conformity with these
    Regulations and the corrective actions taken; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any reportable incidents and any situations that
    had the potential to result in a reportable incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beginning of commercial production

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The periodic reports must also set out the planned
  date for the beginning of commercial production of renewable energy,
  if applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of submission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The periodic reports must be submitted at the
  following frequency unless the Commission requires them to be
  submitted more frequently as a condition of authorization:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an offshore renewable energy project
    or offshore power line to which Part 1 applies,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} monthly, in respect of authorized work or
      activities in relation to the construction or the decommissioning
      and abandonment of facilities, equipment and systems related to
      the project or power line, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} annually, in respect of authorized work or
      activities in relation to site assessment or the operation and
      maintenance of facilities, equipment and systems relate to the
      project or power line; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an offshore renewable energy project
    or offshore power line to which Part 2 applies, annually.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** The operator must, within
six months after the day on which all authorized work or activities
related to the offshore renewable energy project or offshore power line
are completed, submit to the Regulator a final report that includes the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to whether the work or activities were
  carried on as planned and as authorized;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the work or activities were not carried on as
  planned and as authorized, a description of the manner in which the
  carrying on of the work or activities deviated from what was planned
  and authorized; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the state of the operations site as
  of the completion of all authorized work and activities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

- **[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator must keep, at the operator's principal place of business in
  Canada, electronic records of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any documents and information submitted to the
    Regulator in support of an application for authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the surveys and programs referred to
    in subparagraph 6(f)(i) or 50(f)(i), as the case may be, and, if
    applicable, the surveys referred to in paragraph 12(a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all documents and information submitted to the
    Regulator to meet a condition of an authorization; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all documents and information that demonstrate that
    the authorized work or activities were carried on

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that is in keeping with any
      information provided and any approach, methodology or technology
      proposed in an application in respect of the authorized work or
      activities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in conformity with these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure of records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator must, on request, disclose the records
  to the Regulator in the form and manner specified by the Regulator.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration of retention

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The records must be retained for five years after the
  day on which the decommissioning and abandonment of the facilities,
  equipment and systems related to the offshore renewable energy project
  or offshore power line ends, or, if the project or power line is
  terminated before any construction takes place, for five years after
  the day on which the site assessment ends.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1502363 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
