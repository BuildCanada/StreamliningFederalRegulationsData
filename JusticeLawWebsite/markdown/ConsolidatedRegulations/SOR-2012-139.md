## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782099 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of a wastewater system that, when it
  deposits effluent via one or more final discharge points, deposits a
  deleterious substance prescribed in section 5 in any water or place
  referred to in subsection 36(3) of the Act and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is designed to collect an average daily volume of
    100 m^3^ or more of influent; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during any calendar year, collects an average daily
    volume of 100 m^3^ or more of influent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), these Regulations do not,
  for the subsequent calendar year, apply in respect of a wastewater
  system referred to in that paragraph that, during a calendar year,
  collected an average daily volume of less than 100 m^3^ of influent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non‑applica­tion --- areas

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a
  wastewater system located

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the Northwest Territories;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Nunavut; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} north of the 54th parallel in Quebec or
    Newfoundland and Labrador.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non‑applica­tion --- industrial,
  commercial or institutional influent

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a
  wastewater system that is located on the site of an industrial,
  commercial or institutional facility if the wastewater system is
  designed to collect influent whose volume consists of less than 50%
  blackwater and greywater combined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non‑applica­tion --- pulp and paper
  mills

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of a mill
  as defined in section 2 of the [Pulp and Paper Effluent
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-269).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of wastewater systems

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of these
Regulations, there are two types of wastewater systems, as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an intermittent wastewater system, namely, one with a
  hydraulic retention time of at least 90 days that deposits effluent
  via its final discharge point during at most four periods per calendar
  year, each of which is separated from every other period by at least
  seven clear days during which no deposit occurs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a continuous wastewater system, namely, any
  wastewater system other than an intermittent wastewater system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidated wastewater systems

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  owner of at least two original wastewater systems --- none of which
  treats its wastewater in a manner that would permit the deposit of
  effluent, via its final discharge points, that meets the condition set
  out in paragraph 6(1)(a) or (b) --- plans to consolidate those
  original wastewater systems into a future consolidated wastewater
  system, the original wastewater systems that will be consolidated
  constitute a fictional consolidated wastewater system during the
  period beginning on the day on which a consolidation plan that meets
  the requirements of subsection (3) is received by an authorization
  officer and ending on the day on which the future consolidated
  wastewater system is put into service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final discharge point

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The final discharge point of the fictional
  consolidated wastewater system is considered to be the final discharge
  point of the original wastewater systems that is allocated the
  greatest number of points under the table to Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The consolidation plan must contain a description of
  the modifications to be made to each of the original wastewater
  systems, including a description of the modifications to be made to
  their processes, so that the effluent deposited via the final
  discharge point of the future consolidated wastewater system will not
  be acutely lethal and will meet the conditions for authorization set
  out in subsection 6(1), along with a schedule for implementation of
  the plan.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Authorization to Deposit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-782130 .Part}

### [Effluent Containing Deleterious Substances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-782131 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed deleterious substances

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[deleterious substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection
34(1) of the Act, the following substances or classes of substances are
prescribed as deleterious substances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carbonaceous biochemical oxygen demanding matter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} suspended solids;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} total residual chlorine; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} un-ionized ammonia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorization to deposit

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 36(4)(b) of the Act, the owner or operator of a
  wastewater system may --- during a given calendar year, quarter or
  month, as determined in accordance with subsection (2) --- deposit or
  permit the deposit of effluent that contains any of the deleterious
  substances prescribed in section 5 of these Regulations via each of
  the system's final discharge points in any water or place referred to
  in subsection 36(3) of the Act if the effluent is not acutely lethal
  as determined in accordance with section 15, the maximum concentration
  of un-ionized ammonia in the effluent is less than 1.25 mg/L,
  expressed as nitrogen (N), at 15˚C ± 1˚C, and --- during the previous
  calendar year, previous quarter or previous month, as the case may be
  --- the effluent met the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average carbonaceous biochemical oxygen demand
    due to the quantity of CBOD matter in the effluent did not exceed 25
    mg/L;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average concentration of suspended solids in
    the effluent did not exceed 25 mg/L; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the average concentration of total residual
    chlorine in the effluent did not exceed 0.02 mg/L, if chlorine, or
    one of its compounds, was used in the treatment of wastewater.

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-97, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Averaging periods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum concentration and the averages referred
  to in subsection (1) must be determined on the following basis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each calendar year, if the average daily volume of
    effluent deposited via all of the final discharge points during the
    previous calendar year was

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} less than or equal to 17 500 m^3^, for an
      intermittent wastewater system, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} less than or equal to 2 500 m^3^, for a
      continuous wastewater system with a hydraulic retention time of
      five or more days or for a continuous wastewater system that is
      the subject of a transitional authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each quarter, if the average daily volume of
    effluent deposited via all of the final discharge points during the
    previous calendar year was

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} greater than 2 500 m^3^ and less than or equal to
      17 500 m^3^, for a continuous wastewater system with a hydraulic
      retention time of five or more days, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} less than or equal to 17 500 m^3^, for any other
      continuous wastewater system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each month, if the average daily volume of effluent
    deposited via all of the final discharge points during the previous
    calendar year was greater than 17 500 m^3^.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of averages

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The averages referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b)
  must be determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an intermittent wastewater system, based on
    samples of effluent referred to in subsection 10(1) and, if
    applicable, subsection 10(2), in accordance with subsection 10(6);
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a continuous wastewater system, based on
    samples of effluent referred to in, as the case may be, subsection
    10(3) or (4), in accordance with subsection 10(6).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determinations for additional samples

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The determination of averages made in accordance with
  subsection (3) must take into account the results of the
  determination, by a laboratory referred to in section 16, of the
  elements referred to in subsection 10(6) for any sample in excess of
  the number of samples required by subsections 10(1) to (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average of SS during certain months

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For an intermittent wastewater system or a continuous
  wastewater system with a hydraulic retention time of five or more
  days, the determination of the average referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  is not to take into account the result of any determination of the
  concentration of suspended solids in a sample of effluent referred to
  in paragraph 10(6)(b) that was taken during any four months during the
  period beginning on May 1 and ending on November 30, if that result is
  greater than 25 mg/L and is caused by a bloom of algae or
  proliferation of aquatic invertebrates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}SS average deemed 0 mg/L

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If subsection (5) applies to every sample referred to
  in paragraph (3)(a) or (b) that is used to determine the average
  referred to in paragraph (1)(b), that average is deemed to be 0 mg/L.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The authorization granted to an owner or operator
  under subsection (1) is conditional on the owner or operator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determining the average daily volume of effluent
    deposited annually via the final discharge point in accordance with
    section 7;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an intermittent wastewater system, either
    installing, maintaining and calibrating the monitoring equipment
    referred to in subparagraph 7(2)(a)(i) or establishing a method of
    estimation referred to in subparagraph 7(2)(a)(ii) and applying and
    updating it in accordance with subsection 7(4);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a continuous wastewater system, either
    installing, maintaining and calibrating the monitoring equipment
    referred to in subparagraph 7(2)(b)(i) or establishing a method of
    estimation referred to in subparagraph 7(2)(b)(ii) and applying and
    updating it in accordance with subsection 7(4);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} monitoring effluent in accordance with sections 10
    and 11 and sending a monitoring report in accordance with section
    19;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} keeping the record required under section 17;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} sending an identification report in accordance with
    section 18;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} sending the overflow report, if applicable,
    required under section 20 in accordance with subsections 19(4) and

### [Volume of Effluent]{.HTitleText2} {#h-782173 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average daily volume deposited annually

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or operator of a wastewater system must, for each calendar year,
  calculate the average daily volume of effluent deposited via all of
  the system's final discharge points by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determining, in accordance with subsection (2), for
    each day during that calendar year when effluent was deposited, the
    volume of effluent deposited via each of the final discharge points,
    expressed in m^3^;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculating the sum of the daily volumes of
    effluent of all of the discharge points referred to in paragraph
    (a); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dividing that sum by the number of days in that
    calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Daily volumes

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The volume of effluent during each day referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a) must be determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an intermittent wastewater system,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by using monitoring equipment referred to in
      section 9 that provides

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a continuous measure of the volume of influent,
        or effluent deposited via the final discharge point, during that
        day, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a measure of the rate of flow of the influent
        or that effluent upon which that daily volume may be estimated,
        or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by using a method of estimation in accordance
      with subsection (4);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a continuous wastewater system,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by using monitoring equipment referred to in
      section 9 that provides

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a continuous measure of the volume of influent,
        or effluent deposited via the final discharge point, during that
        day, if the average daily volume of effluent deposited via all
        final discharge points during the previous calendar year was
        more than 2 500 m^3^, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a continuous measure of the volume of influent
        or effluent deposited over this day, or a measure of the rate of
        flow of that influent or effluent, that allows for the daily
        volume of effluent to be estimated, if the average daily volume
        of effluent deposited via all final discharge points during the
        previous calendar year was 2 500 m^3^ or less, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by using a method of estimation in accordance
      with subsection (4) if

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a transitional authorization has been issued
        under subsection 26(1) in respect of the continuous wastewater
        system, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the average daily volume of effluent deposited
        via all final discharge points during the previous calendar year
        was less than 2 500 m^3^.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Daily volume estimate --- rate of flow

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), if the owner or
  operator estimates the daily volume of effluent deposited via the
  final discharge point based on the measured rate of flow referred to
  in clause (2)(a)(i)(B) or (b)(i)(B), the estimation is to be done as
  follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} measure the rate of flow of influent, or effluent
    deposited via the final discharge point, in any chosen unit of
    volume for any chosen unit of time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate that volume based on that rate of flow
    for the duration of the deposits of effluent made on that day and,
    if applicable, convert it to m^3^.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of estimation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The owner or operator of a wastewater system who has
  established a method of estimation of the volume of effluent must
  ensure that it is based on generally accepted engineering practices
  and use it to estimate the daily volume of effluent deposited via the
  final discharge point with a margin of error of ±15%.

### [Temporary Authorization to Deposit Un-ionized Ammonia]{.HTitleText2} {#h-782577 .Subheading}

#### [Requirements and Duration]{.HTitleText3} {#h-782578 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or operator of a wastewater system whose effluent deposited via
  its final discharge point is acutely lethal because of the
  concentration of un-ionized ammonia in it may apply to an
  authorization officer for a temporary authorization to deposit
  effluent that contains un-ionized ammonia via the final discharge
  point if the concentration of un-ionized ammonia in the water,
  determined in accordance with subsection (3), at all points that are
  100 m downstream from the point of entry where effluent is deposited
  in that water via the final discharge point is less than or equal to
  0.016 mg/L, expressed as nitrogen (N), and if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the acute lethality of the effluent --- as
    determined in accordance with Reference Method EPS 1/RM/13 using the
    procedure set out in section 6 of that Method and the Procedure for
    pH Stabilization EPS 1/RM/50 --- is primarily because of the
    concentration of un-ionized ammonia in the effluent; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effluent is acutely lethal because the
    concentration of un-ionized ammonia in the effluent deposited via
    the final discharge point is greater than or equal to 1.25 mg/L,
    expressed as nitrogen (N) at 15°C ± 1°C, determined in accordance
    with section 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Samples

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The determination referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an intermittent wastewater system, be based on
    at least one sample of effluent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a continuous wastewater system, be based on at
    least two samples of effluent taken 7 days apart.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of NH~3~ concentration in
  water

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of un-ionized ammonia in the water
  referred to in subsection (1) must be determined in accordance with
  the following formula:

  ::: {#782590 .Subsection}
  total ammonia × 1 ÷ (1 +10^pKa\ --\ pH^)

  where

  [total ammonia]{.dfn}
  :   is the concentration of total ammonia in that water --- namely,
      un-ionized ammonia (NH~3~) plus ionized ammonia (NH~4~^+^) ---
      determined in accordance with subsection (4), expressed in mg/L as
      nitrogen (N);

  [pKa]{.dfn}
  :   is 0.09018 + 2729.92/T, where T is the ambient water temperature
      in kelvin; and

  [pH]{.dfn}
  :   is the pH of that water.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of concentration of total
  ammonia in water

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The concentration of total ammonia in the water
  referred to in subsection (3) must be determined using a total ammonia
  test.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Periods for application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} An initial application for a temporary authorization
  to deposit effluent that contains un-ionized ammonia must be made
  within 30 days after, as the case may be, the determination referred
  to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b) has been made. Applications to extend
  the temporary authorization must be made at least 90 days before the
  expiry of the authorization.

### [Temporary Bypass Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-782671 .Subheading}

#### [Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-782672 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deposit without or with partial treatment

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  owner or operator of a wastewater system may apply to an authorization
  officer for a temporary authorization to bypass at least one of the
  treatment processes normally applied to wastewater in the system and,
  as a result, to deposit effluent that contains any deleterious
  substance prescribed in section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions precedent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a temporary bypass authorization
  is not to be made unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirement to bypass those treatment processes
    arises from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} construction work to make changes to the system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance of the system, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the response to an anticipated event that is
      beyond the control of the owner or operator of the system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bypass is designed, within the constraints of
    technical and economic feasibility, to minimize the volume of
    effluent deposited and the concentration of deleterious substances
    prescribed in section 5 in the effluent deposited;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notice of the proposed bypass has been given to
    members of the public and to any community or Indigenous governing
    body, if the owner or operator has reasonable grounds to believe
    that the member, community or governing body could be affected by
    the bypass or may have used or may use the receiving environment
    before, during or after the bypass; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notice of the proposed bypass has been given to the
    National Manager of the Shellfish Water Classification Program,
    Department of the Environment, if the proposed bypass will result in
    the deposit of effluent in [[open marine
    waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[marine port
    waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in Schedule 2, or a
    shellfish harvesting area or within a radius of 20 km of such an
    area or such waters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period for application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application for a temporary bypass authorization
  must be made at least

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 21 days before the day on which the requirement
    referred to in paragraph (2)(a) is to arise, if the bypass is a
    Category 1 bypass as determined in accordance with sections 43.2 to
    43.4;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 45 days before the day on which the requirement
    referred to in paragraph (2)(a) is to arise, if the bypass is a
    Category 2 bypass as determined in accordance with sections 43.2 to
    43.4; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 90 days before the day on which the requirement
    referred to in paragraph (2)(a) is to arise, if the bypass is a
    Category 3 bypass as determined in accordance with sections 43.2 to
    43.4.

#### [Bypass Categories for Temporary Bypass Authorizations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1454674 .Subheading}

[[Physical or biological treatment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[43.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 43.2 and 43.3, [[physical or biological
treatment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any treatment process, other than
preliminary treatment, applied to wastewater for the purpose of removing
suspended solids or CBOD matter, or both, from the influent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bypass category determination ---
physical or biological treatment

- **[[43.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the effluent deposited during the proposed bypass has undergone a
  physical or biological treatment, the determination of the bypass
  category for the purpose of obtaining a temporary bypass authorization
  is made in accordance with subsections (2) to (4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Category 1 bypass

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The bypass is a Category 1 bypass if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated volume of the deposits is equal to or
    less than 25 000 m^3^ and the approximate duration referred to in
    paragraph 44(1)(g) is equal to or less than 240 hours; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the bypass is at a final discharge point or at one
    or more overflow points, and all of those points of entry are in a
    water or place that regularly receives wastewater during normal
    conditions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Category 3 bypass

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The bypass is a Category 3 bypass if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated volume of the deposits is greater
    than 500 000 m^3^ or the approximate duration referred to in
    paragraph 44(1)(g) is greater than 2 160 hours; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of the following conditions apply:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a shellfish harvesting area is within 1 500 m of
      any point of entry where effluent is deposited during the bypass
      via any final discharge point or one or more overflow points, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an identified critical habitat for species
      referred to in paragraph (a) of the definition [[protected aquatic
      species]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} is within 500 m of any point of
      entry where effluent is deposited during the bypass via any final
      discharge point or one or more overflow points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Category 2 bypass

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The bypass is a Category 2 bypass if it does not meet
  the requirements set out in subsection (2) or (3).

#### [Conditions on Temporary Bypass Authorizations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1454831 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized deposit

**[[45.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.1 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a
temporary bypass authorization in respect of a wastewater system is
authorized to deposit effluent if the mitigation and monitoring measures
referred to in paragraphs 44(1)(h.1) and (h.2) and subparagraph
44(2)(b)(iv), as applicable, are implemented.

#### [Compliance Obligations]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1454836 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General requirements

- **[[45.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  owner or operator of a wastewater system who holds a temporary bypass
  authorization must, during the period of authorization, comply with,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the bypass occurs only at one or more overflow
    points,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sections 5 to 19, 21, 22 and 48 for the effluent
      deposited via all the final discharge points of the wastewater
      system; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 17(b) and section 20 for the effluent
      deposited via all the combined sewer overflow points of the
      wastewater system,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the bypass occurs at at least one final
    discharge point,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sections 7 to 10, subsections 11(1) to (3) and
      (7), sections 12 to 19, 21, 22 and 48 for the effluent deposited
      via all the final discharge points of the wastewater system; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 17(b) and section 20 for the effluent
      deposited via all the combined sewer overflow points of the
      wastewater system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- daily volume

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if, during the period of
  authorization, the volume or rate of flow of the influent or effluent
  cannot be measured in accordance with section 9, the holder of a
  temporary authorization may determine the daily volume of effluent by
  using a method of estimation in accordance with subsection 7(4).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a temporary bypass authorization must,
  within 90 days after the last day of the period of the authorization,
  send to the authorization officer a final report on the temporary
  bypass that includes the following information, as applicable:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the actual duration of the deposits;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the actual or estimated volume of the deposits;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any actual treatment applied to
    effluent;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of how the mitigation and monitoring
    measures referred to in paragraphs 44(1)(h.1) and (h.2) have been
    implemented;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the results of any monitoring referred to in
    subparagraph 44(2)(b)(iv) conducted during the bypass period; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for a Category 2 bypass as determined in accordance
    with sections 43.3 or 43.4 or a Category 3 bypass as determined in
    accordance with sections 43.2 to 43.4, a confirmation of the
    existence of a plan that describes modifications to be made to the
    wastewater system and any other measures to be taken to reduce the
    need for future temporary bypass authorizations and to limit any
    adverse effects on fish, fish habitat or the use by man of fish when
    performing work referred to in paragraph 43(2)(a), including a
    schedule for implementation of the plan.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a temporary bypass authorization must
  ensure that the plan referred to in paragraph (3)(f) is available for
  examination by the public.

for the construction work, for the maintenance of the system or for the
response referred to in paragraph 43(2)(a) and the authorization must be

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction of information

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  holder of the temporary authorization becomes aware that the
  information provided in their application for a temporary bypass
  authorization contains errors, they must, without delay, send a notice
  to the authorization officer that indicates the reason for the errors
  and provides corrections, along with a corrected statement referred to
  in paragraph 44(1)(i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Corrected authorization

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the notice and the corrections, the
  authorization officer may only issue a corrected temporary bypass
  authorization if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corrected bypass category, as determined in
    accordance with sections 43.2 to 43.4, remains the same as that of
    the original application; or,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the corrected bypass category, as determined in
    accordance with sections 43.2 to 43.4, changes from that in the
    original application as a result of the corrections, and the
    additional information required under the corrected bypass
    application has been provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The authorization officer may revoke a temporary
  bypass authorization if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information contained in the application
    referred to in subsections 44(1) to (3), as the case may be, is
    false or misleading in a material respect;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period of authorization, the holder has
    failed to comply with the conditions set out in section 45.1 or any
    obligation set out in subsection 45.2(1); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} new information indicates that a deposit under the
    temporary bypass authorization has had or is likely to have an
    effect on fish, fish habitat or the use by man of fish that is more
    adverse than the worst of the effects that were anticipated when the
    authorization was issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The authorization officer must not revoke a temporary
  authorization unless they have provided the holder with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} written reasons for the proposed revocation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to make written representations in
    respect of the proposed revocation.

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-782726 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic applications

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a transitional authorization or a temporary
  authorization must be sent electronically in the form and format
  specified by the Minister of the Environment and must bear the
  electronic signature of the owner or operator or their authorized
  representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application on paper

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister of the Environment has not specified
  an electronic form and format or if it is not feasible to send the
  application electronically in accordance with subsection (1) because
  of circumstances beyond the control of the owner or operator, or their
  authorized representative, the application must be sent on paper,
  signed by the owner or operator, or their authorized representative,
  and in the form and format specified by that Minister.
