## Human Pathogens and Toxins Regulations {#human-pathogens-and-toxins-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-44
:::

[HUMAN PATHOGENS AND TOXINS ACT](/eng/acts/H-5.67)

Registration 2015-02-20

Human Pathogens and Toxins Regulations

P.C. 2015-203 2015-02-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 66.1 of the [Human Pathogens and Toxins
Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, the Minister of Health has
caused a copy of the proposed [Human Pathogens and Toxins
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-44) to be laid before each House
of Parliament, substantially in the annexed form;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 66 of the
[Human Pathogens and Toxins Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDABA}, makes the annexed [Human
Pathogens and Toxins Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-44).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823186 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Human Pathogens and Toxins
    Act](/eng/acts/H-5.67). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual in
    a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
    least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scientific research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scientific research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following
    types of systematic investigation or research that are carried out
    in a field of science or technology by means of controlled
    activities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} basic research, when the controlled activities
      are conducted for the advancement of scientific knowledge without
      a specific practical application;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} applied research, when the controlled activities
      are conducted for the advancement of scientific knowledge with a
      specific practical application; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} experimental development, when the controlled
      activities are conducted to achieve scientific or technological
      advancement for the purpose of creating new --- or improving
      existing --- materials, products, processes or
      devices. ([recherche scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823195 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Period during which licence in effect ---
factors

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  determining the period during which a licence is in effect, the
  Minister must take the following factors into consideration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the history of the applicant's compliance, during
    the previous 10 years, with the provisions of the Act and the
    regulations, the [Department of Health Act](/eng/acts/H-3.2), the
    [Human Pathogens Importation
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-558), the [Health of Animals
    Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3) and the [Health of Animals
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._296);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the risks associated with the controlled activities
    authorized by the licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other factor that is relevant to the protection
    of the health or safety of the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum period during which a licence may be in
  effect is the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} five years, if the licence authorizes controlled
    activities in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a human pathogen that falls into Risk Group 2,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a prion that falls into Risk Group 3, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a toxin that is not prescribed under section
      10;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three years, if the licence authorizes controlled
    activities in respect of a human pathogen that falls into Risk Group
    3 --- other than a prion --- or a toxin that is prescribed under
    section 10; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} one year, if the licence authorizes controlled
    activities in respect of a human pathogen that falls into Risk Group
    4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Renewal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may renew a licence on application of
  the licence holder, for further periods set out in subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition on issuance --- risk management
plan

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the applicant for a
licence is a person who intends to carry out scientific research, the
Minister must, before issuing the licence, determine that the person has
developed a plan that sets out administrative measures for managing and
controlling biosafety and biosecurity risks during the period in which
the licence is in effect.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions of licence

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  licence is subject to the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence holder and any person who is conducting
    controlled activities authorized by the licence must not obstruct
    the biological safety officer when the officer is exercising their
    powers or carrying out their functions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who intends to conduct any of the
    following controlled activities must communicate that intention to
    the biological safety officer before they make any arrangements to
    do so:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} importing or exporting a human pathogen or toxin,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} possessing a human pathogen or toxin as a result
      of receiving from another licence holder or from a person who is
      conducting controlled activities authorized by another licence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} transferring a human pathogen or toxin to
      another licence holder or to a person who is conducting controlled
      activities authorized by another licence;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who intends to transfer a human pathogen
    or toxin must, before the transfer, take reasonable care to be
    satisfied of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the intended recipient is exempt from the
      requirement to hold a licence, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the intended recipient will conduct
      controlled activities in relation to that human pathogen or toxin
      in a facility that is set out in a licence that authorizes those
      controlled activities with respect to that human pathogen or
      toxin;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who intends to export a human pathogen or
    toxin must, before they export it, take reasonable care to be
    satisfied that the intended recipient will conduct any activities in
    respect of the human pathogen or toxin in accordance with any
    applicable biosafety and biosecurity standards and policies in the
    foreign jurisdiction;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the intended recipient of a human pathogen or toxin
    must make reasonable efforts to locate it if it is not received
    within a reasonable time after it was expected to be received, and
    must inform the biological safety officer of the situation without
    delay; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who discovers during the conduct of a
    controlled activity that they are inadvertently in possession of a
    human pathogen or toxin that is not listed in Schedule 5 to the Act
    in respect of which that controlled activity is not authorized by
    the licence must take all of the following steps:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} inform the biological safety officer of the
      inadvertent possession without delay,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure that the human pathogen or toxin is
      handled and stored appropriately while it is in their possession,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days, dispose of it or transfer it to
      a facility where controlled activities in relation to that human
      pathogen or toxin are authorized.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional condition --- prescribed
  human pathogens and toxins

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every licence that authorizes controlled activities
  in respect of a human pathogen or toxin that is prescribed under
  section 10 is subject to the further condition that, if the intended
  recipient of such a human pathogen or toxin does not receive it within
  24 hours after the expected date and time, they must take all of the
  following steps:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make reasonable efforts to locate it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform the biological safety officer without delay
    that they have not received it; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide the biological safety officer with any
    other information that is relevant to preventing any undue risk to
    the health or safety of the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification to licence holder and
biological safety officer

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person who is conducting
controlled activities authorized by a licence must notify the licence
holder and the biological safety officer, in writing, whenever they
intend to do any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} increase the virulence or pathogenicity of a human
  pathogen;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} increase the communicability of a human pathogen;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} increase the resistance of a human pathogen to
  preventive or therapeutic treatments; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} increase the toxicity of a toxin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister before making change

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licence holder must --- if their licence authorizes controlled
  activities in respect of a human pathogen that falls into Risk Group 3
  or Risk Group 4 or in respect of a prescribed toxin --- notify the
  Minister before they make any change to the physical structure of the
  facility, to any equipment or to the standard operating procedures
  that could affect biocontainment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister after name change

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence holder must notify the Minister, in
  writing, within a reasonable time after they make any change to their
  name.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 32 of Act

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A licence holder must, when
informing the Minister under section 32 of the Act of a decision to
prohibit the holder of a security clearance from having access to the
facility to which the licence applies, include their reasons for the
decision.

## [Biological Safety Officers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823249 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A biological safety officer
must have the following qualifications:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} knowledge of microbiology appropriate to the risks
  associated with the controlled activities authorized by the licence,
  attained through a combination of education, training and experience;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} knowledge of the provisions of the Act and the
  regulations and any applicable federal or provincial legislation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} knowledge of the applicable biosafety and biosecurity
  policies, standards and practices appropriate to the risks associated
  with the controlled activities authorized by the licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Functions

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  biological safety officer has the following functions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} verify the accuracy and completeness of licence
    applications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicate with the Minister on behalf of the
    licence holder;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} promote and monitor compliance with the provisions
    of the Act and the regulations, with the licence and with the
    applicable biosafety and biosecurity standards by, among other
    things,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} arranging for and documenting appropriate
      training related to applicable biosafety and biosecurity policies,
      standards and practices for all persons who conduct controlled
      activities authorized by the licence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} informing the Minister, without delay, of all
      occurrences of inadvertent possession that they are informed of
      under paragraph 4(1)(f),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} informing the Minister, without delay, of every
      situation that they are informed of under subsection 4(2),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} conducting periodic inspections and biosafety
      audits and reporting the findings to the licence holder, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} informing the licence holder in writing of any
      non-compliance by a person conducting controlled activities
      authorized by the licence that is not being corrected by that
      person after they have been made aware of it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} assist in the development and maintenance of the
    licence holder's biosafety manual and standard operating procedures
    related to biosafety and biosecurity; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} assist with internal investigations of incidents
    described in subsection 12(1) or (2), or section 13 or 14, of the
    Act or of any incident that results in a failure of or compromise to
    biocontainment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power to require records to be provided

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A biological safety officer may require any person
  who conducts controlled activities authorized by the licence to
  provide them with any records that are necessary to assist them in
  carrying out their functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Section 33 of Act

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), toxins that are set out

### [Security Clearances]{.HTitleText2} {#h-823288 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligibility

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may only issue a security clearance to an individual who is
  18 years of age or older.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal or revocation in past five
  years

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual is not eligible for a security
  clearance in either of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has refused to issue a security
    clearance to them within the past five years, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has revoked their security clearance
    within the past five years.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or inaccurate information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply if any of the
  information that formed the basis of the refusal or revocation proves
  to be false or inaccurate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who wishes to obtain a security clearance must submit an
  application to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application for a security clearance must be
  signed and dated by the applicant and must contain all of the
  following information and documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's full name, all other names used by
    them and the details of any name changes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their gender, height, weight and eye and hair
    colour;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they were born in Canada, a copy of their
      birth certificate, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they were born outside Canada, the entry
      point and date of entry into Canada, or the intended entry point
      and date of entry into Canada, and, in the case of a naturalized
      Canadian citizen or a permanent resident of Canada, the number of
      the applicable certificate that was issued under the [Citizenship
      Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} their address and telephone number, at home and at
    work, and their email address;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the addresses where they have lived during the past
    five years;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of their employers during
    the past five years and of any post-secondary educational
    institutions that they attended during that period;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} their fingerprints, taken by any of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian police force,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a private company that is accredited by the
      Royal Canadian Mounted Police to submit fingerprints to it for the
      purpose of a criminal record check, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a department or agency of the Government of
      Canada;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each of two pieces of valid
    government-issued identification, one of which must be photo
    identification;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if they are not a Canadian citizen or a permanent
    resident of Canada, the following documents:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of their [curriculum vitae]{.otherLang
      lang="la"} that sets out their professional qualifications and
      work history,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a valid visa, if applicable, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the results of a police record check from every
      jurisdiction where they have lived during the past five years, if
      that jurisdiction provides police record checks;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the dates, destination and purpose of any travel
    for periods longer than 90 days outside their country of residence,
    excluding business travel for the Government of Canada, during the
    five years before the date of their application;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if they have a spouse or common-law partner, all of
    the following information with respect to that spouse or common-law
    partner:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their gender, full name, all other names used by
      them and the details of any name changes,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} their date and place of birth,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if they were born in Canada, a copy of their
      birth certificate,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if they were born outside Canada, the
      information described in subparagraph (c)(ii), and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their current address, if known;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a former spouse or common-law
    partner who died or with whom the relationship ended within the past
    five years, the information described in subparagraphs (k)(i), (ii)
    and (v) and, if applicable, their date of death; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), a statement that
    certifies that they require the security clearance and the part of
    the facility for which it is required, signed and dated by both the
    applicant and either the licence holder or an applicant for a
    licence for that facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One statement per facility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must include a statement described in
  paragraph (2)(m) in respect of each separate facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- inspectors

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An inspector who is designated under section 40 of
  the Act need not include the statement described in paragraph (2)(m)
  in their application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record checks

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a completed application, the Minister must conduct the
  following record checks for the purpose of making a determination of
  risk under section 14:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a criminal record check;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a check of the relevant files of law enforcement
    agencies, including intelligence gathered for law enforcement
    purposes;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian Security Intelligence Service security
    assessment; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a credit check.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, in writing, request any further
  relevant information from the applicant for the purpose of making the
  determination of risk under section 14.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance --- risk assessment

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must issue a
security clearance if the Minister determines that the applicant does
not pose an undue risk to the health or safety of the public after
considering the information obtained under sections 12 and 13 and taking
the following factors into account:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relevance of the information, including the
  circumstances of the underlying events or convictions, their
  seriousness, number and frequency, the date of the last event or
  conviction and any sentence or other disposition;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether it is known or there are reasonable grounds
  to suspect that the applicant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is or has been involved in --- or contributes or
    has contributed to --- activities that are directed toward or in
    support of the use of human pathogens or toxins to commit either
    criminal offences or acts of violence against persons or property,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is or has been involved in --- or contributes or
    has contributed to --- activities that constitute threats to the
    security of Canada within the meaning of the [Canadian Security
    Intelligence Service Act](/eng/acts/C-23),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is or has been a member of a terrorist group as
    defined in subsection 83.01(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46) or is or has been involved in --- or
    contributes or has contributed to --- the activities of such a
    group,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} is or has been a member of a criminal organization
    as defined in subsection 467.1(1) of that Act or participates or has
    participated in --- or contributes or has contributed to ---
    activities of such an organization as described in subsection
    467.11(1) of that Act,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} is or has been a member of an organization that is
    known to be involved in or to contribute to --- or in respect of
    which there are reasonable grounds to suspect involvement in or
    contribution to --- activities that are directed toward or in
    support of either the use of human pathogens or toxins to commit
    criminal offences or the threat of or the use of acts of violence
    against persons or property, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} is or has been associated with an individual who
    is known to be involved in or to contribute to --- or in respect of
    whom there are reasonable grounds to suspect involvement in or
    contribution to --- activities referred to in subparagraph (i) or
    (ii), or who is a member of a group or organization referred to in
    any of subparagraphs (iii) to (v);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether there are reasonable grounds to suspect that
  the applicant is in a position in which there is a risk that they
  could be induced to commit an act or to assist or abet any person to
  commit an act that might constitute an undue risk to the health or
  safety of the public;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant has previously had a security
  clearance suspended or revoked;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant has provided false or
  misleading information in or in connection with their application;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} whether any foreign jurisdiction has refused to issue
  the equivalent of a security clearance to the applicant --- or has
  suspended or revoked one --- and the reason for the refusal,
  suspension or revocation; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant information to enable the Minister
  to assess the risk.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponement --- outstanding criminal
charges

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** If a criminal charge is
outstanding against the applicant for a security clearance that would,
if they were found guilty of it, be considered by the Minister under
paragraph 14(a), the Minister may postpone processing their application
until the charge is disposed of by the courts and must notify the
applicant in writing of the postponement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity period

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must
establish the validity period of a security clearance in accordance with
the level of risk posed by the applicant as determined under section 14.
The validity period must not exceed five years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of issuance or refusal

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** When the Minister issues
or refuses to issue a security clearance, the Minister must notify in
writing every licence holder or applicant for a licence who has signed a
statement described in paragraph 12(2)(m) with respect to the
application for that security clearance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional facility or part of facility

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a security
clearance who wishes to access a part of a facility described in section
33 of the Act not included in their application for the security
clearance must provide the Minister with a statement described in
paragraph 12(2)(m) in respect of each new facility or part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in writing

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a security
clearance must notify the Minister in writing without delay if they are
found guilty of a criminal offence after the issuance of their security
clearance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may suspend
a security clearance on receipt of any of the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} new information described in any of sections 12 to 14
  that was not available for consideration when the security clearance
  was issued;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a notice from the holder of the security clearance
  under section 19; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the decision of a licence holder, under section 32 of
  the Act, to prohibit the holder of the security clearance from having
  access to the facility to which the licence applies, and their reasons
  for the decision under section 7.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must revoke a security clearance if the Minister determines
  that the holder of the security clearance poses an undue risk to the
  health or safety of the public after considering any of the
  information described in paragraphs 20(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to licence holders

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On the revocation, the Minister must notify in
  writing every licence holder who has signed a statement described in
  paragraph 12(2)(m) with respect to that security clearance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reasons in writing

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A written notice required
under subsection 34(1) or 35(5) of the Act must contain the Minister's
reasons for the decision.

### [Accompaniment and Supervision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-823379 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One person at a time

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purpose of section 33 of the Act, a person who holds a security
  clearance may at any time accompany and supervise only one person who
  does not hold a security clearance.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuous supervision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who accompanies and supervises another
  person must at all times be in the same room as them and must monitor
  their activities at all times.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No access in certain circumstances

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 33 of the Act, a person must not enter a part of a facility even
under accompaniment and supervision in either of the following
circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their security clearance is suspended, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} they have previously been refused a security
  clearance or their security clearance has previously been revoked, and
  a new security clearance has not been issued to them since the refusal
  or revocation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The licence holder must
keep a record of the full name of every person who enters a facility
under accompaniment and supervision, together with the date on which
they entered and the full name of the person who accompanied and
supervised them.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823391 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from Risk Group 2 --- risk
reduction

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  human pathogen that is listed in Schedule 2 to the Act is exempt from
  the application of the definition [[Risk Group 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  in subsection 3(1) of the Act if it has been modified to the extent
  that it no longer meets the risk profile described in that definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from Risk Group 3 --- risk
  reduction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A human pathogen that is listed in Schedule 3 to the
  Act is exempt from the application of the definition [[Risk Group
  3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 3(1) of the Act if it has been
  modified to the extent that it no longer meets the risk profile
  described in that definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from Risk Group 4 --- risk
  reduction

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A human pathogen that is listed in Schedule 4 to the
  Act is exempt from the application of the definition [[Risk Group
  4]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 3(1) of the Act if it has been
  modified to the extent that it no longer meets the risk profile
  described in that definition.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the circumstances described in subsections (1) to
  (3), the licence holder must notify the Minister without delay after
  such a modification.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from licence requirement ---
laboratories

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who carries out laboratory analyses or diagnostic testing with
  a human pathogen that is neither a prion nor a prescribed human
  pathogen is exempt from the application of section 7 of the Act on
  condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they do not cultivate or otherwise produce a human
    pathogen; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is any production, it is done using a
    sealed container that prevents the release of the human pathogen and
    that is decontaminated before its disposal or reuse.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from licence requirement ---
  veterinary practices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A veterinarian who is registered under the laws of a
  province --- and any persons under their supervision --- who carry out
  laboratory analyses or diagnostic testing with a human pathogen that
  falls into Risk Group 2 are exempt from the application of section 7
  of the Act on condition that any controlled activities that they
  conduct in respect of that pathogen are conducted in the course of
  providing care to animals in a clinical practice in that province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- section 33 of Act

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A person is exempt from
the application of section 33 of the Act with respect to a part of a
facility on either of the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is no prescribed human pathogen or toxin
  present in that part of the facility, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any prescribed human pathogen or toxin that is
  present is locked up and inaccessible to that person.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823412 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Document retention

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Documents that are required under
  the Act to be prepared must be maintained for five years after the day
  on which they are prepared and must be provided to the Minister on
  request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- incidents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the retention period is 10
  years for documents that contain information that relates to the
  following incidents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an incident that is described in subsection 12(1)
    or (2), or section 13 or 14, of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any incident that results in a failure of or
    compromise to biocontainment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of documents

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Any document that is sent
by the Minister under the Act is considered to have been received on the
earlier of the following days:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day that is five days after it was sent, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which it is received.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823424 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of controlled activities

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who, on the day on which these Regulations come into force,
  conducts controlled activities in respect of a human pathogen or toxin
  is exempt from the application of section 7 of the Act if they submit
  an application for a licence under subsection 18(2) of the Act within
  90 days after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies until the Minister issues or
  refuses to issue the licence under subsection 18(1) or (3) of the Act,
  respectively.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Continuation of controlled activities ---
prescribed human pathogens and toxins

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual who, on the day on which these Regulations come into force,
  conducts controlled activities in respect of a prescribed human
  pathogen or toxin is exempt from the application of section 33 of the
  Act if they submit an application for a security clearance under
  section 12 within 90 days after that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) applies until the Minister issues or
  refuses to issue the security clearance under subsection 34(1) of the
  Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-823435 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}December 1, 2015

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on December 1, 2015.
