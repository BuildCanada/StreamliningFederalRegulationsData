### [Close Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565446 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3) and (5), no person who is on board a foreign
  fishing vessel of a flag state set out in Schedule II shall fish for
  or catch and retain fish of a species set out in column I of an item
  of Schedule X in a stock area set out in column II of that item during
  the close time set out in column III of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A close time set out in column III of an item of
  Schedule X applies individually and separately to each flag state set
  out in Schedule II.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), subsection (1) does not
  apply to a person fishing for squid by means of squid jiggers.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fish for squid by means of squid
  jiggers in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Subarea 3 from December 30 to December 31; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Subarea 4 from December 30 to December 31.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to subsection (1), fishing for a
  species of fish is prohibited in a stock area, a person fishing in
  that stock area for a species of fish other than a species to which
  the prohibition applies may catch and retain an incidental catch of
  the prohibited species if the quantity of the incidental catch does
  not exceed the quantities referred to in section 9.

- SOR/82-771, s. 3
- SOR/87-185, s. 3

### [Swordfish Size Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565457 .Subheading}

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[length]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
  swordfish, means the distance, measured in a straight line, from the
  tip of the lower jaw to the fork of the tail.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no person shall be in
  possession of any swordfish that is less than 125 cm in length.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply to a person fishing
  from a foreign fishing vessel for swordfish under the authority of a
  licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the catching of swordfish that are less than 125 cm
    in length is incidental to the catching of longer swordfish; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of swordfish that are less than 125 cm
    in length that are retained during any one fishing trip does not
    exceed a fishing quota of 15 per cent of the number of longer
    swordfish that are retained during that fishing trip.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-293, s. 2
:::

### [Tuna Size Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565466 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall be in possession of any
  bigeye or yellowfin tuna that weigh less than 3.2 kg.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a person fishing
  from a foreign fishing vessel for bigeye tuna under the authority of a
  licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the catching of bigeye tuna weighing less than
    3.2 kg is incidental to the catching of larger bigeye tuna; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of bigeye tuna weighing less than 3.2 kg
    that are retained during any one fishing trip does not exceed a
    fishing quota of 15 per cent of the number of larger bigeye tuna
    that are retained during that fishing trip.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a person fishing
  from a foreign fishing vessel for yellowfin tuna under the authority
  of a licence where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the catching of yellowfin tuna weighing less than
    3.2 kg is incidental to the catching of larger yellowfin tuna; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of yellowfin tuna weighing less than
    3.2 kg that are retained during any one fishing trip does not exceed
    a fishing quota of 15 per cent of the number of larger yellowfin
    tuna that are retained during that fishing trip.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall be in possession of any bluefin tuna
  that weighs less than 30 kg.

subsection 10(2), where a person is fishing for a species of fish in a
stock area under the authority of a licence, that person shall not
retain an incidental catch of a species of fish, other than a species
set out in the licence, that exceeds a fishing quota of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of haddock

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any species in any stock area not
  mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b), 10 per cent

authority of the licence.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
Canadian fisheries waters adjacent to the Pacific coast of Canada to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a foreign fishing vessel other than a foreign fishing
  vessel engaged exclusively in scientific research; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person on board, being a member of the crew of,
  attached to or employed on a vessel described in paragraph (a).

of Schedule VII shall fish for the species of fish set out in column II
of that item in the stock area set out in column III of that item during
the close time set out in column V of that item.

### [Incidental Catch Limits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565535 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Where a person is fishing
for Pacific hake in sub-zone 5-1 or 5-2, he shall not catch and retain
an incidental catch of a species of fish other than Pacific hake that
exceeds 20 per cent of the weight of the Pacific hake on board the
vessel or that exceeds:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of walleye pollock, 10 per cent,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of rockfish, two per cent,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of Pacific cod and ling cod in the
  aggregate, one per cent, or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of sablefish, one per cent,

of the said weight.

### [Gear Restrictions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565545 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person on board or operating from a foreign fishing vessel of a flag
  state set out in Column I of an item of Schedule VII shall fish for a
  species of fish set out in Column II of that item in a stock area set
  out in Column III of that item except with the gear type set out in
  Column IV of that item.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall fish for, or catch and retain,
  Pacific hake by means of a trawl net that has a mesh size

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} greater than 100 mm or less than 76 mm in the
    cod-end; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} less than 89 mm in any part of the net that is not
    the cod-end.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person fishing for a species of fish other than
  Pacific hake shall use a trawl net that has a mesh size of less than
  89 mm.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-565568 .Part}

### [Avoidance of Set Gear]{.HTitleText2} {#h-565569 .Subheading}

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  master of a foreign fishing vessel in Canadian fisheries waters shall
  ensure at all times that there is maintained a distance of at least
  1/2 nautical mile between his vessel and any equipment and gear
  attached thereto and any set fishing gear that has been marked so as
  to be visible to persons on board vessels navigating in the vicinity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,
  [[set]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of fishing gear, means that
  the fishing gear has been placed in the sea in such manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each end of the gear is anchored in the sea;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one end of the gear is anchored in the sea and the
    other end of the gear is attached to a fishing vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the gear is drifting in the sea; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} one end of the gear is drifting free in the sea
    while the other end of the gear is attached to a fishing vessel.
