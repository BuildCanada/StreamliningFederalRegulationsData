## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-482263 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Controlled Drugs and
      Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[advertisement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any representation
      by any means for the purpose of promoting, directly or indirectly,
      the sale or other disposal of a
      narcotic. ([publicité.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
      individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual
      in a conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year. ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a public
      authority of a foreign country that is authorized under the laws
      of the country to approve the importation or exportation of
      narcotics into or from the country. ([autorité
      compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[compound]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[compound]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a
      preparation. ([composé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving the financing of terrorism
        against any of sections 83.02 to 83.04 of the [Criminal
        Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving fraud against any of
        sections 380 to 382 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offence of laundering proceeds of crime
        against section 462.31 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving a criminal organization
        against any of sections 467.11 to 467.13 of the [Criminal
        Code](/eng/acts/C-46); or

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a conspiracy or an attempt to commit, being an
        accessory after the fact in relation to, or any counselling in
        relation to, an offence referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to
        (d). ([infraction désignée en matière
        criminelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[destroy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[destroy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a narcotic,
      to alter or denature it to such an extent that its consumption is
      rendered impossible or improbable. ([destruction]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[diacetylmorphine (heroin)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[diacetylmorphine (heroin)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the
      salts of diacetylmorphine. ([diacétylmorphine
      (héroïne)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hospital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hospital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is licensed, approved or designated by a
        province in accordance with the laws of the province to provide
        care or treatment to persons or animals suffering from any form
        of disease or illness; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is owned or operated by the Government of
        Canada or the government of a province and that provides health
        services. ([hôpital]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[international obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      obligation in respect of a narcotic set out in a convention,
      treaty or other multilateral or bilateral instrument that Canada
      has ratified or to which Canada adheres. ([obligation
      internationale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[licensed dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[licensed dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the holder of a
      licence issued under section 10.1. ([distributeur
      autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[methadone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[methadone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the salts of
      methadone. ([méthadone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[midwife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[midwife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
      1 of the [New Classes of Practitioners
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([sage-femme]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a controlled substance set out in the schedule;
        or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a midwife, nurse practitioner or
        podiatrist, a controlled substance set out in the schedule that
        the midwife, nurse practitioner or podiatrist may prescribe,
        possess or conduct an activity with, in accordance with sections
        3 and 4 of the [New Classes of Practitioners
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([stupéfiant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nurse practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 1 of the [New Classes of Practitioners
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([infirmier
      praticien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is entitled
      under the laws of a province to practise pharmacy and who is
      practising pharmacy in that
      province. ([pharmacien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[podiatrist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 1 of the [New Classes of Practitioners
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-230). ([podiatre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an authorization given
      by a practitioner that a stated amount of a narcotic be dispensed
      for the person named in it or the animal identified in
      it. ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[qualified person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualified person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      individual designated under subsection 9.2(1). ([responsable
      qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Security Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Security Directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Directive on
      Physical Security Requirements for Controlled Substances and Drugs
      Containing Cannabis, as amended from time to time and published by
      the Government of Canada on its website. ([Directive en matière de
      sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[senior person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[senior person in charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      individual designated under section 9.1. ([responsable
      principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[test kit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a kit

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that contains a narcotic and a reagent system
        or buffering agent;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is used during the course of a chemical or
        analytical procedure to test for the presence or quantity of a
        narcotic for a medical, laboratory, industrial, educational, law
        administration or enforcement, or research purpose; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the contents of which are not intended or
        likely to be consumed by, or administered to, a person or an
        animal. ([nécessaire d'essai]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[verbal prescription narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[verbal prescription narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      narcotic that is contained in medication that may be prescribed
      verbally and that has the following characteristics:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it contains two or more medicinal ingredients
        that are not narcotics, in a recognized therapeutic dose;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is not intended for parenteral
        administration; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it does not contain diacetylmorphine (heroin),
        hydrocodone, methadone, oxycodone or pentazocine. ([stupéfiant
        d'ordonnance verbale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Possession]{.HTitleText1} {#h-482339 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized persons

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  is authorized to possess a narcotic if the person has obtained the
  narcotic in accordance with these Regulations, in the course of
  activities conducted in connection with the administration or
  enforcement of an Act or regulation, or from a person who is exempt
  under section 56 of the Act from the application of subsection 5(1) of
  the Act with respect to that narcotic, and the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} requires the narcotic for their business or
    profession and is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a licensed dealer,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pharmacist, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a practitioner who is registered and entitled
      to practise in the province in which they possess the narcotic;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a practitioner who is registered and entitled to
    practise in a province other than the province in which they possess
    the narcotic for emergency medical purposes only;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a hospital employee or a practitioner in a
    hospital;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has obtained the narcotic for their own use

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from a practitioner,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a prescription that was not
      issued or obtained in contravention of these Regulations, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} from a pharmacist under section 36;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is a practitioner of medicine who received the
    narcotic under subsection 68(1) or (2) and their possession is for
    the purpose of providing or delivering it to a person referred to in
    subsection 68(3);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is an agent or mandatary of a practitioner of
    medicine who received the narcotic under subsection 68(1) and their
    possession is for the purpose of providing or delivering it to a
    person referred to in subsection 68(2);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} is employed as an inspector, a member of the Royal
    Canadian Mounted Police, a police constable, a peace officer or a
    member of the technical or scientific staff of the Government of
    Canada, the government of a province or a university in Canada and
    their possession is in connection with that employment;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is not a practitioner of medicine referred to in
    paragraph (e) or an agent or mandatary referred to in paragraph (f),
    is exempted under section 56 of the Act with respect to the
    possession of that narcotic and their possession is for a purpose
    set out in the exemption; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent or mandatary

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person is authorized to possess a narcotic if the
  person is acting as the agent or mandatary of any person who is
  authorized to possess it in accordance with any of paragraphs (1)(a)
  to (e), (h) and (i).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agent or mandatary --- person referred
  to in paragraph (1)(g)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is authorized to possess a narcotic if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is acting as the agent or mandatary of a
    person who they have reasonable grounds to believe is a person
    referred to in paragraph (1)(g); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} their possession of the narcotic is for the purpose
    of assisting that person in the administration or enforcement of an
    Act or regulation.

## [Test Kits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-482365 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized activities

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person may sell, possess
or otherwise deal in a test kit if the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registration number has been issued for the test
  kit under section 6 and has not been cancelled under section 7;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the test kit bears, on its external surface,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer's name,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the trade name or trademark, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the registration number; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the test kit will be used for a medical, laboratory,
  industrial, educational, law administration or enforcement, or
  research purpose.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for registration number

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  manufacturer of a test kit may obtain a registration number for it by
  submitting to the Minister an application containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the design and
    construction of the test kit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the narcotic and other
    substances, if any, contained in the test kit, including the
    qualitative and quantitative composition of each component; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed use of the test kit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the person authorized by the
    applicant for that purpose; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation by that person that all of
    the information submitted in support of the application is correct
    and complete to the best of their knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information or document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must, not later than the date specified
  in the Minister's written request to that effect, provide the Minister
  with any information or document that the Minister determines is
  necessary to complete the review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of registration number

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** On completion of the review
of the application for a registration number, the Minister must issue a
registration number for the test kit, preceded by the letters "TK", if
the Minister determines that the test kit will only be used for a
medical, laboratory, industrial, educational, law administration or
enforcement, or research purpose and that it contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a narcotic and an adulterating or denaturing agent in
  such a manner, combination, quantity, proportion or concentration that
  the preparation or mixture has no significant drug abuse potential; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} such small quantities or concentrations of a narcotic
  as to have no significant drug abuse potential.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of registration number

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must cancel
the registration number for a test kit if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the test kit is removed from the market by the
  manufacturer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the test kit is used or is likely to be used for any purpose other
  than a medical, laboratory, industrial, educational, law
  administration or enforcement, or research purpose; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  the cancellation is necessary to protect public health or safety,
  including to prevent a narcotic from being diverted to an illicit
  market or use.

### [Authorized Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1207498 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed dealer may produce, assemble, sell, provide, transport, send,
  deliver, import or export a narcotic if they comply with these
  Regulations and the terms and conditions of their dealer's licence and
  any permit issued under these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified person in charge present

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer may conduct an activity in relation
  to a narcotic at their site only if the qualified person in charge or
  an alternate qualified person in charge is present at the site.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit --- import and export

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer must obtain a permit in order to
  import or export a narcotic.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Possession for export

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer may possess a narcotic for the
  purpose of exporting it if they have obtained it in accordance with
  these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opium poppy --- production

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer may cultivate, propagate or harvest
  opium poppy only for scientific purposes.

### [Dealer's Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1207514 .Subheading}

#### [Preliminary Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1207515 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible persons

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The following persons may
apply for a dealer's licence:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who ordinarily resides in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that has its head office in Canada or
  operates a branch office in Canada; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a position that includes responsibility
  for narcotics on behalf of the Government of Canada or of a government
  of a province, a police force, a hospital or a university in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior person in charge

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a
dealer's licence must designate only one individual as the senior person
in charge, who has overall responsibility for management of the
activities with respect to narcotics that are specified in the licence
application. The applicant may designate themself if the applicant is an
individual.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualified person in charge

- **[[9.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant for a dealer's licence must designate only one individual as
  the qualified person in charge, who is responsible for supervising the
  activities with respect to narcotics that are specified in the licence
  application and for ensuring that those activities comply with these
  Regulations. The applicant may designate themself if the applicant is
  an individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternate qualified person in charge

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a dealer's licence may designate an
  individual as an alternate qualified person in charge, who is
  authorized to replace the qualified person in charge when that person
  is absent. The applicant may designate themself if the applicant is an
  individual.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Qualifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Only an individual who meets the following
  requirements may be designated as a qualified person in charge or an
  alternate qualified person in charge:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} they work at the site specified in the dealer's
    licence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} they

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are entitled or, if applicable, registered and
      entitled by a provincial professional licensing authority or a
      professional association in Canada and entitled to practise a
      profession that is relevant to their duties, such as pharmacist,
      practitioner, pharmacy technician or laboratory technician,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} hold a diploma, certificate or credential
      awarded by a post-secondary educational institution in Canada in a
      field or occupation that is relevant to their duties, such as
      pharmacy, medicine, dentistry, veterinary medicine, pharmacology,
      chemistry, biology, pharmacy technician, laboratory technician,
      pharmaceutical regulatory affairs or supply chain management or
      security, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} hold a diploma, certificate or credential that
      is awarded by a foreign educational institution in a field or
      occupation referred to in subparagraph (ii) and hold

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an [[equivalency
        assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection
        73(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an equivalency assessment issued by an
        organization or institution that is responsible for issuing
        equivalency assessments and is recognized by a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} they have sufficient knowledge of and experience
    with the use and handling of the narcotics specified in the dealer's
    licence to properly carry out their duties; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} they have sufficient knowledge of the provisions of
    the Act and these Regulations that are applicable to the activities
    specified in the dealer's licence to properly carry out their
    duties.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An applicant for a dealer's licence may designate an
  individual who does not meet any of the requirements of paragraph
  (3)(b) as a qualified person in charge or an alternate qualified
  person in charge if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no other individual working at the site meets those
    requirements;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} those requirements are not necessary for the
    activities specified in the licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual has sufficient knowledge ---
    acquired from a combination of education, training or work
    experience --- to properly carry out their duties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ineligibility

**[[9.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.3 .sectionLabel}** An individual is not
eligible to be a senior person in charge, a qualified person in charge
or an alternate qualified person in charge if, during the 10 years
before the day on which the dealer's licence application is submitted,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a designated substance offence or a
  designated criminal offence or a [[designated
  offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5), the individual

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was convicted as an adult, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} was a [[young person]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} who
    received an [[adult sentence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms
    are defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Youth Criminal Justice
    Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an offence committed outside Canada
  that, if committed in Canada, would have constituted a designated
  substance offence or a designated criminal offence or a [[designated
  offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
  [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the individual was convicted as an adult, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if they committed the offence when they were at
    least 14 years old but less than 18 years old, the individual
    received a sentence that was longer than the maximum [[youth
    sentence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as that term is defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Youth Criminal Justice
    Act](/eng/acts/Y-1.5), that could have been imposed under that Act
    for such an offence.

#### [Issuance of Licence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1207564 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who intends to conduct an activity referred to in section 8
  must obtain a dealer's licence for each site at which they intend to
  conduct activities by submitting an application to the Minister that
  contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the licence is requested by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an individual, the individual's name,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation, its corporate name and any other
      name registered with a province under which it intends to conduct
      the activities specified in its dealer's licence or by which it
      intends to identify itself, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the holder of a position described in paragraph
      9(c), the applicant's name and the title of the position;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the municipal address, telephone number and, if
    applicable, the email address of the proposed site and, if different
    from the municipal address, its mailing address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, date of birth, telephone number and email
    address of the proposed senior person in charge;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to each of the proposed qualified
    person in charge and any proposed alternate qualified person in
    charge,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, date of birth, telephone number and
      email address,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the title of their position at the site,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the position of their
      immediate supervisor at the site,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the profession they practise that
      is relevant to their duties, the name of the province that
      authorizes them to practise it and their authorization number, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their education, training and work experience
      that are relevant to their duties;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the activities that are to be conducted and the
    narcotics in respect of which each of the activities is to be
    conducted;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the licence is requested to manufacture or
    assemble a product or compound that contains a narcotic, other than
    a test kit, a list that includes, for each product or compound,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the brand name of the product or the name of the
      compound,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the drug identification number that is assigned
      to the product under section C.01.014.2 of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), if any,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name of the narcotic in the product or
      compound,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the strength per unit of the narcotic in it, the
      number of units per package and the number of packages,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if it is to be manufactured or assembled by or
      for another licensed dealer under a custom order, the name,
      municipal address and licence number of the other licensed dealer,
      and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant's name appears on the label of
      the product or compound, a copy of the [[inner
      label]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section A.01.010 of
      the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the licence is requested in order to produce a
    narcotic other than a product or compound that contains a narcotic,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the narcotic,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity that the applicant expects to
      produce under their licence and the period during which that
      quantity would be produced, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if it is to be produced for another licensed
      dealer under a custom order, the name, municipal address and
      licence number of the other licensed dealer;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the licence is requested for an activity that is
    not described in paragraph (f) or (g), the name of the narcotic for
    which the activity is to be conducted and the purpose of the
    activity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the security measures in
    place at the site, determined in accordance with the Security
    Directive; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the method for recording
    information that the applicant proposes to use for the purpose of
    section 28.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a dealer's licence must be
  accompanied by the following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant is a corporation, a copy of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the certificate of incorporation or other
      constituting instrument, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any document filed with the province in which
      its site is located that states its corporate name and any other
      name registered with the province under which the applicant
      intends to conduct the activities specified in its dealer's
      licence or by which it intends to identify itself;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} individual declarations signed and dated by each of
    the proposed senior person in charge, and qualified person in charge
    and any proposed alternate qualified person in charge, attesting
    that the person is not ineligible for a reason specified in section
    9.3;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a document issued by a Canadian police force in
    relation to each person referred to in paragraph (b), indicating
    whether, during 10 years before the day on which the application is
    submitted, the person was convicted as specified in subparagraph
    9.3(a)(i) or received a sentence as specified in subparagraph
    9.3(a)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if any of the persons referred to in paragraph (b)
    has ordinarily resided in a country other than Canada during the 10
    years before the day on which the application is submitted, a
    document issued by a police force of that country indicating whether
    in that period that person was convicted as specified in
    subparagraph 9.3(b)(i) or received a sentence as specified in
    subparagraph 9.3(b)(ii);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} declaration, signed and dated by the proposed
    senior person in charge, attesting that the proposed qualified
    person in charge and any proposed alternate qualified person in
    charge have the knowledge and experience required under paragraphs
    9.2(3)(c) and (d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the proposed qualified person in charge or any
    proposed alternate qualified person in charge does not meet the
    requirement of subparagraph 9.2(3)(b)(i), either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the person's diploma, certificate or
      credential referred to in subparagraph 9.2(3)(b)(ii) or (iii), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the education,
      training and work experience that is required under paragraph
      9.2(4)(c), together with supporting evidence, such as a copy of a
      course transcript or an attestation by the person who provided the
      training.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the proposed senior person
    in charge; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation by that person that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all of the information and documents submitted in
      support of the application are correct and complete to the best of
      their knowledge, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they have the authority to bind the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must, not later than the date specified
  in the Minister's written request to that effect, provide the Minister
  with any information or document that the Minister determines is
  necessary to complete the review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section
10.3, on completion of the review of the licence application, the
Minister must issue a dealer's licence, with or without terms and
conditions, that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence number;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensed dealer, their corporate name
  or the title of the position they hold;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the activities that are authorized and the names of
  the narcotics in respect of which each activity may be conducted;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the municipal address of the site at which the dealer
  may conduct the authorized activities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the security level at the site, determined in
  accordance with the Security Directive;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of the licence;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date of the licence, which must be not
  later than three years after its effective date;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any terms and conditions that the Minister has
  reasonable grounds to believe are necessary to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} ensure that an international obligation is
    respected,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ensure conformity with the requirements associated
    with the security level that is referred to in paragraph (e), or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} reduce a risk to public health or safety,
    including the risk of a narcotic being diverted to an illicit market
    or use; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the licensed dealer produces a narcotic, the
  quantity that they may produce and the authorized production period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

- **[[10.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a
  dealer's licence if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant may not apply for a licence under
    section 9;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the 10 years before the day on which the
    licence application is submitted, the applicant has contravened

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the Act, the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or their regulations, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a term or condition of a licence or permit
      issued to the applicant under any regulations made under the Act
      or issued to the applicant under the [Cannabis
      Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or its regulations;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} during the 10 years before the day on which the
    licence application is submitted, the proposed senior person in
    charge or qualified person in charge or any proposed alternate
    qualified person in charge was convicted as specified in
    subparagraph 9.3(a)(i) or (b)(i) or received a sentence as specified
    in subparagraph 9.3(a)(ii) or (b)(ii);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an activity for which the licence is requested
    would contravene an international obligation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an activity for which the licence is requested is
    the cultivation, propagation or harvesting of opium poppy other than
    for scientific purposes;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not have in place at the site
    the security measures set out in the Security Directive in respect
    of an activity for which the licence is requested;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the method referred to in paragraph 10(1)(j) does
    not permit the recording of information as required by section 28;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has not complied with the
    requirements of subsection 10(4) or the information or documents
    that they have provided are not sufficient to complete the review of
    the licence application;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the applicant has submitted false or misleading information or false
    or falsified documents in or in support of the licence application;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} information received from a competent authority or
    the United Nations gives the Minister reasonable grounds to believe
    that the applicant has been involved in the diversion of a narcotic
    to an illicit market or use or has been involved in an activity that
    contravened an international obligation; or

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the issuance of the licence would likely create a risk to public
    health or safety, including the risk of a narcotic being diverted to
    an illicit market or use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not refuse to issue a licence under
  paragraph (1)(b) or (i) if the applicant meets the following
  conditions, unless the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
  it is necessary to do so to protect public health or safety, including
  to prevent a narcotic from being diverted to an illicit market or use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not have a history of
    non-compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or
    their regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has carried out, or signed an
    undertaking to carry out, the necessary corrective measures to
    ensure compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)
    and their regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before refusing to issue a licence, the Minister must
  send the applicant a notice that sets out the Minister's reasons and
  gives the applicant an opportunity to be heard.

### [Import Permits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1207898 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed dealer must submit to the Minister, before each importation
  of a narcotic, an application for an import permit that contains the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their name, municipal address and dealer's licence
    number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the narcotic to be imported,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, as specified in the dealer's licence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it is a salt, the name of the salt,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its quantity, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a raw material, its purity and
      its anhydrous content;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the narcotic is contained in a product to be
    imported,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the brand name of the product,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the drug identification number that has been
      assigned to the product under section C.01.014.2 of the [Food and
      Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), if any, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the strength per unit of the narcotic in the
      product, the number of units per package and the number of
      packages;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and municipal address of the exporter in
    the country of export from whom the narcotic is being obtained;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the customs office where the
    importation is anticipated; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} each proposed mode of transportation and any
    proposed country of transit or transhipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature and attestation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed and dated by the qualified person in
    charge or an alternate qualified person in charge; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include an attestation by that person that all of
    the information submitted in support of the application is correct
    and complete to the best of their knowledge.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The licensed dealer must, not later than the date
  specified in the Minister's written request to that effect, provide
  the Minister with any information or document that the Minister
  determines is necessary to complete the review of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

- **[[18.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue an
  import permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer is not authorized by their
    dealer's licence to import the relevant narcotic or their licence
    will expire before the date of importation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the importation would contravene an international obligation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer does not have in place at the
    site the security measures set out in the Security Directive in
    respect of the importation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has not complied with the
    requirements of subsection 18(3) or the information or documents
    that they have provided are not sufficient to complete the review of
    the permit application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the licensed dealer has submitted false or misleading information or
    false or falsified documents in or in support of the permit
    application;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has been notified that their
    application to renew or amend their licence will be refused;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the importation would contravene the laws of the country of export
    or any country of transit or transhipment; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the issuance of the permit would likely create a risk to public
    health or safety, including the risk of a narcotic being diverted to
    an illicit market or use.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before refusing to issue the import permit, the
  Minister must send the licensed dealer a notice that sets out the

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister must revoke an import permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer requests the Minister to do so
    or informs the Minister of the loss or theft of the permit or the
    actual or potential unauthorized use of the permit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer does not carry out the
    corrective measures specified by the Minister under paragraph
    19(2)(c) by the specified date;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has contravened a term or
    condition of the permit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the licensed dealer submitted false or misleading information or
    false or falsified documents in or in support of the application for
    the permit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information received from a competent authority or
    the United Nations gives the Minister reasonable grounds to believe
    that the licensed dealer has been involved in the diversion of a
    narcotic to an illicit market or use; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the dealer's licence has been revoked.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not revoke an import permit for a
  ground set out in paragraph (1)(d) or 17(1)(e) or (g) if the licensed
  dealer meets the following conditions, unless the Minister has
  reasonable grounds to believe that it is necessary to do so to protect
  public health or safety, including to prevent a narcotic from being
  diverted to an illicit market or use:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer does not have a history of
    non-compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5) or
    their regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer has carried out, or signed an
    undertaking to carry out, the necessary corrective measures to
    ensure compliance with the Act, the [Cannabis Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)
    and their regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before revoking an import permit, the Minister must
  send the licensed dealer a notice that sets out the Minister's reasons
  and gives the dealer an opportunity to be heard.

### [Destruction of Narcotics]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1208256 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Destruction at site

**[[27.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27.5 .sectionLabel}** A licensed dealer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer obtains the prior approval of the
  Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the destruction is carried out in the presence of two
  of the following persons, at least one of whom must be a person
  referred to in subparagraph (i):

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the senior person in charge, the qualified person
    in charge or an alternate qualified person in charge, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person who works for or provides services to the

  method that complies with all federal, provincial and municipal
  environmental protection legislation applicable to the place of
  destruction; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as soon as the destruction is completed, the person
  who carried out the destruction and each of the two persons referred
  to in paragraph (b) who were present at the destruction sign and date
  a joint declaration attesting that the narcotic was completely
  destroyed, to which each signatory must add their name in printed
  letters.

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the form and quantity of the narcotic and, if
    applicable, the strength per unit of the narcotic in the product or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name and municipal address of the person to
    whom it was sold or provided, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it was sold or provided;

  number of packages;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of any narcotic that the dealer

  compound was placed in stock;

  at the end of each month;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name, form and quantity of any narcotic that the

  of the consignee and the date on which it was delivered, transported

  municipal address of the importer, the country of final destination

## [Hospitals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483034 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General obligations

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** A person who is in charge
of a hospital shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep or cause to be kept in a book, register or other
  record maintained for such purposes,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of any narcotic received,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person from whom any
    narcotic was received and the date received,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of any narcotic used in the
    making or assembling of a product or compound containing that
    narcotic,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of any product or compound
    that was made or assembled and that contains that narcotic and the
    date on which the product or compound was made or assembled,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name of the patient for whom a narcotic, other
    than dextropropoxyphene or a verbal prescription narcotic, was
    dispensed,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the name of the practitioner ordering or
    prescribing a narcotic, other than dextropropoxyphene or a verbal
    prescription narcotic, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the date a narcotic, other than
    dextropropoxyphene or a verbal prescription narcotic was ordered or
    prescribed and the form and quantity thereof;

  - [(viii) to (x)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-147, s.
    23\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain the recorded information in such form as to
  enable an audit to be made from time to time for a period of not less
  than two years from the making thereof;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take all necessary steps to protect narcotics in the
  hospital against loss or theft, and report to the Minister any loss or
  theft of narcotics within 10 days of his discovery thereof.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selling, providing or administering
narcotic

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person in charge of a hospital shall permit a narcotic to be sold,
  provided or administered except in accordance with this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written order or prescription

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a written order or prescription signed
  and dated by a practitioner, the person in charge of a hospital may
  permit a narcotic, other than diacetylmorphine (heroin), to be
  administered to a person or an animal under treatment as an in-patient
  or out-patient of the hospital, or to be sold or provided for the
  person or to the person in charge of the animal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verbal prescription narcotic

  [(2.1)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a verbal prescription given by a
  practitioner, the person in charge of a hospital may permit a verbal
  prescription narcotic to be administered to a person or an animal
  under treatment as an in-patient or out-patient of the hospital, or to
  be sold or provided for the person or to the person in charge of the
  animal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency --- other hospital

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5.1), the person in charge of
  a hospital may permit a narcotic to be provided, for emergency
  purposes, to a hospital employee or practitioner in another hospital
  on receipt of a written order signed and dated by a pharmacist in the
  other hospital or a practitioner authorized by the person in charge of
  the other hospital to sign the order.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-147, s. 24\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Emergency --- pharmacist

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5.1), the person in charge of
  a hospital may permit a narcotic to be sold or provided, for emergency
  purposes, to a pharmacist on receipt of a written order signed and
  dated by the pharmacist.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-37, s. 7\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signature

  [(5.1)]{.lawlabel} No person in charge of a hospital shall permit a
  narcotic to be sold or provided under subsection (3) or (4) unless the
  signature of the pharmacist in the other hospital or of the
  practitioner authorized by the person in charge of the other hospital
  to sign an order is known to the person who sells or provides the
  narcotic or has been verified.

- [(5.2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2018-147, s. 24\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research purposes

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A person in charge of a hospital may permit a
  narcotic to be provided to a person who is exempted under section 56
  of the Act with respect to the possession of the narcotic and who is
  employed in a research laboratory in the hospital for the purpose of
  research.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Heroin

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a hospital may permit
  diacetylmorphine (heroin) to be sold, provided or administered to a
  person under treatment as an in-patient or out-patient of the hospital
  on receipt of a written order or prescription signed and dated by a
  practitioner of medicine or dentistry or a nurse practitioner.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2012-230, s. 28

  - ###### Definition {#definition .MarginalNote}

    - [28]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[former
      Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Narcotic Control
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041) as they read
      immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
      force.

    - ###### Application {#application .MarginalNote}

      [(2)]{.lawlabel} This section applies to a preparation referred to
      in section 36 of the former Regulations.

    - ###### Former Regulations --- para. 36(1)(b) {#former-regulations-para.-361b .MarginalNote}

      [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite these Regulations, if the main panel of
      the label of a preparation or any outer container of the
      preparation displays information in accordance with paragraph
      36(1)(b) of the former Regulations, that paragraph continues to
      apply to the main panel of the label or any outer container during
      the period of six months after the day on which these Regulations
      are published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/),
      Part II.

- --- SOR/2013-172, s. 13

  - [13]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 11(2)(a) of the
    [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22), these Regulations apply
    before they are published in the [Canada
    Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
