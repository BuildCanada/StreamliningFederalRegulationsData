## Ontario Asparagus Pricing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-asparagus-pricing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-175
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-03-12

Regulations Respecting the Pricing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Asparagus Produced in Ontario
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3 of
the [Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-859)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Regulations respecting the pricing in interprovincial and export trade
of asparagus produced in Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-175).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/78-859, 1978 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4217
  :::

Dated at London, Ontario, this 7th day of March, 1980
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868511 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Asparagus Pricing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-175).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868513 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act
    (Ontario); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means asparagus produced in
    Ontario that is used by a processor for processing;
    ([asperges]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Asparagus
    Growers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes canning, dehydrating,
    drying, freezing or processing with sugar or sulphur dioxide or any
    other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, on the first
    day of May in any year,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the beneficial owner of property on which
      asparagus is growing, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the tenant of property on which asparagus is
      growing. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868522 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of asparagus in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868524 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a producer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the property on which asparagus is growing is
  less than one-half acre; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any asparagus that has been planted on
  that producer's property for less than two years.

## [Minimum Price]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868528 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or offer to sell, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} buy

asparagus at a price that is less than the minimum price determined by
the Commodity Board from time to time.
