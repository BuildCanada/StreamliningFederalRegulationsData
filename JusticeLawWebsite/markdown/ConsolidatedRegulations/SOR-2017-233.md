## Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Regulations {#justice-for-victims-of-corrupt-foreign-officials-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-233
:::

[JUSTICE FOR VICTIMS OF CORRUPT FOREIGN OFFICIALS ACT (SERGEI MAGNITSKY
LAW)](/eng/acts/J-2.3)

Registration 2017-11-03

Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Regulations

P.C. 2017-1346 2017-11-02
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that one or more of
the circumstances described in subsection 4(2) of the [Justice for
Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei Magnitsky
Law)](/eng/acts/J-2.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3244-E_hq_20705){#fn_81000-2-3244-E_hq_20705-ID0EBACA}
have occurred;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to sections
4 and 14 of the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act
(Sergei Magnitsky Law)](/eng/acts/J-2.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3244-E_hq_20705){#fn_81000-2-3244-E_hq_20705-ID0EDABA},
makes the annexed [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-233).

## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842583 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed foreign national

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An individual whose name is
listed in the schedule is a foreign national who, in the opinion of the
Governor in Council, has committed an act set out in subsection 4(2) of
the [Justice for Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei
Magnitsky Law)](/eng/acts/J-2.3).

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-842586 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to engage in any
activity set out in paragraphs 4(3)(a) to (e) of the [Justice for
Victims of Corrupt Foreign Officials Act (Sergei Magnitsky
Law)](/eng/acts/J-2.3) if the activity involves an individual whose name
is listed in the schedule.
