## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1338784 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the coronavirus disease
    2019. ([COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[COVID-19 drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19 drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drug for human use
    that is manufactured, sold or represented for use in relation to
    COVID-19. ([drogue utilisée en lien avec la
    COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[COVID-19 drug authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19 drug authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    authorization to do any of the following activities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} import or sell a COVID-19 drug that is to be
      tested in a clinical trial;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct a clinical trial in respect of such a
      drug. ([autorisation relative à une drogue utilisée en lien avec
      la COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[COVID-19 medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19 medical device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical
    device that is manufactured, sold or represented for use in relation
    to COVID-19. ([instrument médical utilisé en lien avec la
    COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[COVID-19 medical device authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[COVID-19 medical device authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    an authorization to do any of the following activities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} import or sell a COVID-19 medical device that is
      to be tested in a clinical trial;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} conduct a clinical trial in respect of such a
      device. ([autorisation relative à un instrument médical utilisé en
      lien avec la COVID-19]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any incident that involves a
    COVID-19 medical device tested in a clinical trial and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is related to a failure of the device or a
      deterioration in its quality or effectiveness or any inadequacy in
      its labelling or its directions for use; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has led to the death or a serious deterioration
      in the state of health of a clinical trial subject, user or other
      person or could do so were it to
      recur. ([incident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    a member in good standing of a professional association of persons
    entitled under the laws of a province to provide health care under
    their licence in that province and who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} conducts a clinical trial; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a clinical trial conducted by a
      team, is the responsible leader of that team. ([chercheur
      compétent]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[research ethics board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[research ethics board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body that is
    not affiliated with an applicant for a COVID-19 medical device
    authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization, or a holder of such
    an authorization, and whose principal mandate is to approve the
    initiation of, and conduct periodic reviews of, biomedical research
    involving human subjects in order to ensure the protection of their
    rights, safety and well-being. ([comité d'éthique de la
    recherche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  the context requires otherwise, words and expressions used in these
  Regulations have the same meaning as in the [Medical Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) and the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[clinical
  trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, [[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
  the same meaning as in section 2 of the Act, except as otherwise
  provided.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1338809 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to the importation and sale of a COVID-19 medical
  device, other than a Class I device, that is to be tested in a
  clinical trial, to the importation and sale of a COVID-19 drug, other
  than a drug that is described in Schedule C to the Act, that is to be
  tested in a clinical trial and to the conduct of a clinical trial in
  respect of such a device or drug.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- COVID-19 medical
  device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Medical Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282), other than sections 6 and 7
  and Schedule 1, do not apply to the importation or sale of a COVID-19
  medical device that is to be tested in a clinical trial or to the
  conduct of a clinical trial in respect of such a device if a COVID-19
  medical device authorization has been issued for that device and the
  authorization has not been revoked in whole.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- COVID-19 drug

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following regulations do not apply to the
  importation or sale of a COVID-19 drug that is to be tested in a
  clinical trial or to the conduct of a clinical trial in respect of
  such a drug if a COVID-19 drug authorization has been issued for that
  drug and the authorization has not been revoked in whole:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), other than sections
    A.01.022 to A.01.024, A.01.026, A.01.041, A.01.042, A.01.050,
    A.01.067, A.01.068, C.01.015, C.01.036, C.01.037 to C.01.040,
    C.01.040.2, C.01.051, C.01.064 to C.01.067, C.01.070, C.01.131,
    C.01.133 to C.01.136 and C.01.435 and Divisions 2 and 4 of Part C;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Blood
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2013-178).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[COVID-19 Medical Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1338819 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a COVID-19 medical device authorization must be
  submitted to the Minister in the form and manner specified by the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must contain sufficient information
  and material to enable the Minister to determine whether to issue the
  authorization and must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the applicant
    and, if applicable, the importer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and class of the device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the device and of the materials
    used in its manufacture and packaging;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the features of the device that
    permit it to be used for the medical conditions, purposes and uses
    for which it is manufactured, sold or represented, including its
    performance specifications if those specifications are necessary for
    proper use;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the identifier of the device, including the
    identifier of any medical device that is part of a system, test kit,
    medical device group, medical device family or medical device group
    family;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    manufacturer as they appear on the device label;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the address where the device is manufactured, if
    the address is different from the one provided in the contact
    information under paragraph (f);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the diagnosis, treatment, mitigation or prevention
    for which the device is required;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of the countries other than Canada where the
    device has been sold, the total number of units sold in those
    countries and a summary of any reported problems with the device and
    of any recalls of the device in those countries;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the known information in relation to the quality,
    safety and effectiveness of the device;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the directions for use, unless directions are not
    required for the device to be used safely and effectively;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the applicant that documented
    procedures are in place in respect of distribution records,
    complaint handling, incident reporting and recalls;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the label of the device;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the name of the qualified investigator and their
    qualifications, including their training and experience;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the institution
    at which the clinical trial is proposed to be conducted;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the protocol of the proposed clinical trial,
    including the number of clinical trial subjects, the number of units
    of the device proposed to be used for the clinical trial, the
    hypothesis for and objective of the clinical trial, the period of
    time during which the clinical trial will be conducted and a copy of
    the informed consent form;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} a written undertaking from the qualified
    investigator to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} conduct the clinical trial in accordance with the
      protocol provided by the applicant,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inform each clinical trial subject of any risks
      and benefits associated with the use of the device and obtain the
      subject's informed consent for its use, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} not permit the device to be used by any other
      person except under the direction of the qualified investigator;
      and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class III or IV device, for each
    clinical trial site, the name and contact information of the
    research ethics board that approved the protocol and informed
    consent form referred to in paragraph (p), if known at the time of
    submitting the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Class II devices

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), if the application for the
  authorization is in respect of a COVID-19 medical device that is a
  Class II device, the information and material set out in paragraphs
  (2)(c), (h) to (j), (n) and (q) may be omitted from the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister must issue a
COVID-19 medical device authorization if the following requirements are
met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted to the Minister an
  application that meets the requirements set out in section 4;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted to the Minister any
  additional information or material requested under subsection 10(1) in
  the time, form and manner specified under subsection 10(2); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the use of the device that is to be tested in the
    clinical trial will not unduly affect the health or safety of
    clinical trial subjects, users or other persons,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial is not contrary to the best
    interests of clinical trial subjects, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the objectives of the clinical trial are
    achievable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research ethics board approval

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization must not import or sell a COVID-19 medical
device for which the authorization has been issued or conduct a clinical
trial in respect of such a device unless the holder has obtained, for
each clinical trial site, the approval of a research ethics board in
respect of the protocol and informed consent form referred to in
paragraph 4(2)(p).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, at any
time, impose terms and conditions on a COVID-19 medical device
authorization or amend those terms and conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if the substance of any of the information or
  material referred to in paragraphs 4(2)(a) to (d), (f), (h), (j), (k)
  and (p) and, if applicable, submitted under subsection 10(1) is
  significantly different from the substance of the information or
  material contained in the application for a COVID-19 medical device
  authorization,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conduct of the clinical trial referred to in
    the authorization that is issued as a result of that application is
    no longer authorized for the purposes of section 3.1 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the authorization must not import or
    sell the COVID-19 medical device for which the authorization was
    issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the COVID-19 medical device
    authorization has submitted to the Minister an application to amend
    the authorization;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that the requirements set
    out in subparagraphs 5(c)(i) to (iii) are met;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder has submitted to the Minister any
    additional information or material requested under subsection 10(1)
    in the time, form and manner specified under subsection 10(2); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister amends the authorization.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may suspend, in whole or in part, a COVID-19 medical device
  authorization by notice, giving reasons, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that any of the
    requirements set out in subparagraphs 5(c)(i) to (iii) is no longer
    met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the authorization has not submitted
    to the Minister any additional information or material requested
    under subsection 10(1) in the time, form and manner specified under
    subsection 10(2); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the authorization has contravened
    these Regulations or any provisions of the Act relating to the
    device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must reinstate a COVID-19 medical device
  authorization if the holder of the authorization submits to the
  Minister, in the time, form and manner specified by the Minister,
  information or material that demonstrates that the situation giving
  rise to the suspension did not exist or has been corrected.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information and material

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may request that an applicant for a COVID-19 medical device
  authorization or a holder of such an authorization submit any
  additional information or material, including samples, that is
  necessary to enable the Minister to determine whether to issue, amend
  or suspend the authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time, form and manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant or holder must submit the information
  or material in the time, form and manner specified by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization discontinues, in whole or in part, the
clinical trial for which the authorization has been issued, the holder
must, without delay,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister, in writing, of the
  discontinuance and the reasons for it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform all qualified investigators, in writing, of
  the discontinuance and the reasons for it and advise them, in writing,
  of any potential risks to the health of clinical trial subjects, users
  or other persons; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each clinical trial site at which the
  trial is discontinued, stop the importation or sale of the device as
  of the date of the discontinuance and take all reasonable measures to
  ensure the recovery of all unused quantities of the device that have
  been sold.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discretionary revocation

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may revoke, in whole or in part, a COVID-19 medical device
  authorization by notice, giving reasons, if the holder of the
  authorization has not submitted to the Minister, in the time, form and
  manner specified by the Minister, the information or material referred
  to in subsection 9(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must revoke, in whole or in part, the
  authorization if the Minister has received a notice of discontinuance
  referred to in paragraph 11(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not import or sell a medical device for which a COVID-19
  medical device authorization has been issued or conduct a clinical
  trial in respect of such a device unless the device has a label that
  sets out the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the device;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    manufacturer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identifier of the device, including the
    identifier of any medical device that is part of a system, test kit,
    medical device group, medical device family or device group family;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a Class III or IV device, the
    control number;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the contents are not readily apparent, an
    indication of what the package contains, expressed in terms
    appropriate to the device, such as the size, net weight, length,
    volume or number of units;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the device is sterile,
    if the device is to be sold in a sterile condition;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the expiry date of the device, if applicable;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the medical conditions, purposes and uses for which
    the device is manufactured, sold or represented, including its
    performance specifications if those specifications are necessary for
    proper use;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the directions for use, unless directions are not
    required for the device to be used safely and effectively;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any special storage conditions applicable to the
    device;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the device is an
    investigational device;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating that the device is to be
    used by qualified investigators only; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an in vitro diagnostic device, a
    statement indicating that the performance specifications of the
    device have not been established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presentation of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information required by subsection (1) to be set
  out on the label must be in English and in French.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident reporting

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization must, within 10 days after becoming aware
of an incident inside or outside Canada involving a COVID-19 medical
device for which the authorization has been issued, report the incident
to the Minister and specify the nature of the incident and the
circumstances surrounding it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a COVID-19 medical device authorization must record, handle
  and store all information in respect of the clinical trial for which
  the authorization has been issued in a manner that allows for the
  complete and accurate reporting as well as the interpretation and
  verification of the information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the authorization must maintain
  complete and accurate records, which include the information and
  material referred to in subsection 4(2), in respect of a COVID-19
  medical device for which the authorization has been issued in order to
  establish that the clinical trial is conducted in accordance with
  these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the authorization must retain all
  records for the period during which the authorization has not been
  revoked in whole.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Distribution records

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a COVID-19 medical device authorization must maintain a
  distribution record in respect of each device that is imported or sold
  under the authorization or tested in a clinical trial under the
  authorization.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The distribution record must contain sufficient
  information to permit complete and rapid withdrawal of the COVID-19
  medical device for which the authorization has been issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder --- or former holder, in the case of an
  authorization that has been revoked in whole --- of the authorization
  must retain the distribution record for the longer of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the projected useful life of the device, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} two years after the date on which the holder took
    possession, care or control of the device in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timely retrieval

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Distribution records must be maintained in a manner
  that will allow their timely retrieval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaint handling

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization must, in respect of a COVID-19 medical
device for which the authorization has been issued, maintain records of
the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any reported problems relating to the performance
  characteristics or safety of the device that are received by the
  holder after the device was first sold in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all actions taken by the holder in response to those
  problems.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documented procedures

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization must, in respect of a COVID-19 medical
device for which the authorization has been issued, establish and
implement documented procedures that will enable the holder to carry out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an effective and timely investigation of the problems
  referred to in paragraph 17(a); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an effective and timely recall of the device.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- recall

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  holder of a COVID-19 medical device authorization must, on or before
  recalling a COVID-19 medical device for which the authorization has
  been issued, provide the Minister with the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the device and its identifier,
    including the identifier of any medical device that is part of a
    system, test kit, medical device group, medical device family or
    medical device group family;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
    manufacturer and, if applicable, the importer, as well as the name
    and contact information of the establishment where the device was
    manufactured, if different from that of the manufacturer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the recall, the nature of the
    defectiveness or possible defectiveness and the date on and
    circumstances under which it was discovered;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the risk associated with the
    defectiveness or possible defectiveness;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the number of affected units of the device that
    were manufactured or sold in Canada or imported into Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the affected units of the
    device were sold in Canada;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person to whom the affected device
    was sold and the number of units sold to each person;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any communication issued with respect to
    the recall;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed strategy for conducting the recall,
    including the date for beginning the recall, information as to how
    and when the Minister will be informed of the progress of the recall
    and the proposed date for its completion;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the proposed action to prevent a recurrence of the
    problem; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the name, title and telephone number of the
    representative of the holder of the authorization to contact for any
    information concerning the recall.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- after completion of
  recall

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the authorization must, as soon as
  feasible after the completion of the recall, prepare and submit to the
  Minister a report on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the recall; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the action taken to prevent a recurrence of the
    problem.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission on holder's behalf

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), the holder of the
  authorization may permit the importer of the COVID-19 medical device

  material required under those subsections if the importer has the same

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The holder of the authorization must notify the
  Minister, in writing, if the holder has permitted the importer to

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[COVID-19 Drugs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1338962 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a COVID-19 drug authorization must be signed and dated
  by the applicant's senior medical or scientific officer in Canada and
  their senior executive officer and must be submitted to the Minister
  in the form and manner specified by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must contain sufficient information
  and material to enable the Minister to determine whether to issue the
  authorization and must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the protocol of the proposed clinical trial;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the statement, as it will be set out in
    each informed consent form, that states the risks and anticipated
    benefits arising to the health of clinical trial subjects as a
    result of their participation in the clinical trial;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a clinical trial attestation containing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the title of the protocol and the clinical trial
      number,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the brand name, chemical name or code for the
      drug,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the therapeutic and pharmacological
      classifications of the drug,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the medicinal ingredients of the drug,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the non-medicinal ingredients of the drug,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the dosage form of the drug,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the
      applicant,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} if the drug is to be imported, the name and
      contact information of the applicant's representative in Canada
      who is responsible for the sale of the drug,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the name and
      contact information of the qualified investigator, if known at the
      time of submitting the application,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the name and
      contact information of the research ethics board that approved the
      protocol referred to in paragraph (a) and the informed consent
      form containing the statement referred to in paragraph (b), if
      known at the time of submitting the application, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} a statement that

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial will be conducted in
        accordance with good clinical practices and these Regulations,
        and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} all information and material contained in, or
        referenced by, the application is complete and accurate and is
        not false or misleading;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of any research
    ethics board that has previously refused to approve the protocol
    referred to in paragraph (a), its reasons for doing so and the date
    on which the refusal was given, if known at the time of submitting
    the application;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the physical, chemical and pharmaceutical
    properties of the drug;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacological aspects of the drug, including
    its metabolites in all animal species tested;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacokinetics of the drug and the drug
    metabolism, including the biological transformation of the drug in
    all animal species tested;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any toxicological effects of the drug in any animal
    species tested under a single dose study, a repeated dose study or a
    special study;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any results of carcinogenicity studies in any
    animal species tested in respect of the drug;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any results of clinical pharmacokinetic studies of
    the drug;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any of the following information that was obtained
    from previous clinical trials in humans in respect of the drug;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} safety,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pharmacodynamics,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} efficacy, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} dose responses;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if the drug contains a human-sourced excipient,
    including any used in the placebo,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the excipient has been assigned
      a drug identification number under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of the
      [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or,
      in the case of a new drug, has been issued a notice of compliance
      under subsection C.08.004(1) of those Regulations, a statement to
      that effect, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, sufficient information to
      support the identity, purity, potency, stability and safety of the
      excipient;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} if the drug has not been assigned a drug
    identification number under subsection C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food
    and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or, in the
    case of a new drug, a notice of compliance has not been issued under
    section C.08.004 or C.08.004.01 of those Regulations, the chemistry
    and manufacturing information in respect of the drug, including its
    site of manufacture; and

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date for the commencement of the
    clinical trial at each clinical trial site, if known at the time of
    submitting the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marketed drug

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the application for the authorization is in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the label of the drug; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the proposed use of
    the drug is consistent with the standard of medical practice.

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the objectives of the clinical trial are
    achievable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Good clinical practices

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A holder of a COVID-19
drug authorization must ensure that the clinical trial for which the
authorization has been issued is conducted in accordance with good
clinical practices and, without limiting the generality of the
foregoing, must ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial is scientifically sound and
  clearly described in the protocol;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial is conducted, and the drug is
  used, in accordance with the protocol and these Regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} systems and procedures that assure the quality of
  every aspect of the clinical trial are implemented;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the approval of a
  research ethics board is obtained before the clinical trial begins;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} at each clinical trial site, there is no more than
  one qualified investigator;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} at each clinical trial site, medical care and medical
  decisions, in respect of the clinical trial, are under the supervision
  of a health care professional who is authorized to provide medical
  care and make medical decisions;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} each person who is involved in the conduct of the
  clinical trial is qualified by education, training and experience to
  perform their respective tasks;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} informed consent, given in accordance with the
  applicable laws governing consent, is obtained from every person
  before they participate in the clinical trial, but only after that
  person has been informed of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the risks and anticipated benefits to their health
    arising from participation in the clinical trial, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all other aspects of the clinical trial that are
    necessary for that person to make the decision to participate in the
    clinical trial; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the drug is manufactured, handled and stored in
  accordance with the applicable good manufacturing practices referred
  to in Divisions 2 and 4 of Part C of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), with the exception of
  sections C.02.019, C.02.025 and C.02.026 of those Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** If a holder of a COVID-19

for which the authorization has been issued, the holder must, without

  discontinuance and the reasons for it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform all qualified investigators, in writing, of

  other persons; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each clinical trial site at which the
  trial is discontinued, stop the importation or sale of the drug as of
  the date of the discontinuance and take all reasonable measures to
  ensure the recovery of all unused quantities of the drug that have
  been sold.

    natural health product is suspended or cancelled in respect of that
    site.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this section.

  [[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 63 of the [Natural Health Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([essai
      clinique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in subsection 1(1) of the [Natural Health Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([produit de santé
      naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- medical devices

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that is authorized under Part 3 of the [Medical Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282) to sell a medical device for
  investigational testing --- and any person that conducts the testing
  of the device --- is, in respect of the testing, exempt from section
  3.1 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exemption does not apply in respect of any person
  to whom the medical device can no longer be sold as a result of a
  measure taken by the Minister under subsection 85(2) of the [Medical
  Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension --- deemed holder

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorization to conduct a clinical trial in respect of a drug is
  suspended if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder is deemed to hold the authorization
    under any of sections 180 to 182 of the [Budget Implementation Act,
    2019, No. 1](/eng/acts/B-9.8593); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder's authorization to sell or import the
    drug for the purposes of the clinical trial is suspended under

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} section C.03.317, C.05.016 or C.05.017 of the
      [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} section 80 or 81 of the [Natural Health Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reinstatement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorization that is suspended under
  subsection (1) is reinstated if the authorization referred to in
  paragraph (1)(b) is reinstated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation --- deemed holder

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An authorization to conduct a clinical trial in
  respect of a drug or medical device is revoked if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder is deemed to hold the authorization
    under any of sections 180 to 183 of the [Budget Implementation Act,
    2019, No. 1](/eng/acts/B-9.8593); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder's authorization to sell or import the
    drug or device for the purposes of the clinical trial is cancelled

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a drug, under

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} section C.03.319 or paragraph C.05.016(4)(b) or
        C.05.017(3)(b) of the [Food and Drug
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 82(b) of the [Natural Health Products
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a device, under paragraph
      85(2)(b) of the [Medical Devices
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Partial suspensions or revocations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the authorization referred to in paragraph (1)(b)
  or (3)(b) is only partially suspended or cancelled, the authorization
  to conduct the clinical trial is suspended or revoked to the same
  extent.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1339176 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Interim Order No.
2]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** In sections 43 to 52,
[[Interim Order No. 2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Interim Order No.
2 Respecting Clinical Trials for Medical Devices and Drugs Relating to
COVID-19, made by the Minister on May 3, 2021, and published in Part I
of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on May 22, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Applications

**[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization
that was submitted to the Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the
day on which this section comes into force and in respect of which no
decision has been made before that day is deemed to be an application
for a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19 drug
authorization submitted under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorizations

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** A COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization that was issued or
reinstated by the Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on
which this section comes into force and that has not been revoked in
whole before that day is deemed to be a COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization issued or reinstated
under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval --- research ethics board

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** Any approval of a
research ethics board that was obtained by a holder of a COVID-19
medical device authorization or a holder of a COVID-19 drug
authorization under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on which this
section comes into force is deemed to be an approval of the research
ethics board obtained by the holder of a COVID-19 medical device
authorization or the holder of a COVID-19 drug authorization under these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terms and conditions

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** Any terms and conditions
that were imposed on a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a
COVID-19 drug authorization by the Minister under Interim Order No. 2
before the day on which this section comes into force are deemed to be
terms and conditions imposed on the COVID-19 medical device
authorization or the COVID-19 drug authorization under these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment applications

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** An application to amend a
COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization
that was submitted to the Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the
day on which this section comes into force and in respect of which no
decision has been made before that day is deemed to be an application to
amend a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19 drug
authorization submitted under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amended authorization

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** A COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization that was amended by the
Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on which this section
comes into force and that has not been revoked in whole before that day
is deemed to be a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19
drug authorization amended to the same extent under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suspension

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** A COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization that was suspended in
whole or in part by the Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the
day on which this section comes into force and that has not been revoked
in whole before that day is deemed to be a COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization suspended in whole or in
part to the same extent under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request --- additional information and
material

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** A request for additional
information or material, including samples, that was made by the
Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on which this section
comes into force is deemed to be a request for additional information or
material under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** A notice of
discontinuance provided to the Minister, or information in respect of
discontinuance provided in writing to a qualified investigator, by the
holder of a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a COVID-19 drug
authorization under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on which this
section comes into force is deemed to be a notice of discontinuance or
information provided under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation in part

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** A COVID-19 medical device
authorization or a COVID-19 drug authorization that was revoked in part
by the Minister under Interim Order No. 2 before the day on which this
section comes into force and that has not been revoked in whole before
that day is deemed to be a COVID-19 medical device authorization or a
COVID-19 drug authorization revoked in part to the same extent under
these Regulations.
