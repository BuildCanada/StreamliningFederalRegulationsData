## Romania Claims Fund Regulations {#romania-claims-fund-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/72-91
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

[TREATIES OF PEACE (ITALY, ROMANIA, HUNGARY AND FINLAND) ACT,
1948](/eng/acts/Z-041)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 5, 1963

Registration 1972-03-29

Regulations Establishing the Treaty of Peace (Romania) Claims Fund and
Authorizing Payments Out of the Fund

P.C. 1972-572 1972-03-28
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Secretary of State for External Affairs,
pursuant to the [Treaties of Peace (Italy, Romania, Hungary and Finland)
Act, 1948](/eng/acts/Z-041), and Vote 57a of the Appropriation Act No.
5, 1963, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations establishing
the Treaty of Peace (Romania) Claims Fund and authorizing payments out
of the Fund](/eng/regulations/SOR-72-91).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860988 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Romania Claims Fund
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-72-91).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860990 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Custodian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Custodian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Custodian appointed by
    the Revised Regulations Respecting Trading with the Enemy
    (1943); ([séquestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Foreign Claims Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Foreign Claims Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Commission established by Order in Council P.C. 1970-2077 of
    December 8, 1970; ([Commission des réclamations
    étrangères]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
    Finance. ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Fund Established]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860995 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** There shall be established
in the Consolidated Revenue Fund a special account to be known as the
Treaty of Peace (Romania) Claims Fund, hereinafter referred to as the
"Fund".

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** There shall be credited to
the Fund all moneys representing the proceeds and earnings from Romanian
property that remain vested in the Custodian in respect of World War II
and that have been transferred by the Custodian to the Minister.

## [Payments from the Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-860998 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may pay out of the Fund such amounts, not exceeding in the
  aggregate \$40,000, to such persons as the Foreign Claims Commission
  pursuant to the [Foreign Claims (Romania) Settlement
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-72-90) may recommend.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 5, 8, 10 and 11 of the [Foreign Claims
  (Romania) Settlement Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-72-90) apply,
  with such changes as the circumstances require, with respect to
  payments made pursuant to subsection (1).

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 7, the Minister may pay out of the Fund, on a [pro
  rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis, to persons who notified the
  Government of Canada on or before December 14, 1971 of their holdings
  of bonds forming part of the Romanian external debt, and acknowledged
  by the Government of the Socialist Republic of Romania to be part of
  such debt, the difference between the money credited to the Fund and
  \$40,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the value of the
  bonds described in that subsection issued in a currency described in
  Column I of the Table to this subsection shall be calculated on the
  basis of the Canadian currency equivalent described in Column II
  thereof.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No payment out of the Fund
shall be made by the Minister, pursuant to section 6, in respect of a
bond described in that section unless the holder thereof surrenders that
bond and any coupons attached thereto to the Minister on or before
December 31, 1972.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may transfer
from the Fund to the Foreign Claims Fund referred to in section 2 of the
[Foreign Claims (Romania) Settlement
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-72-90) an amount equal to one-half of
the expenses that the Foreign Claims Commission is estimated to have
incurred in dealing with claims as defined in paragraph (b) of the
definition [claim]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} in that section.
