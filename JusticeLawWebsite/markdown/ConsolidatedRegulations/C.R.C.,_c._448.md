## Canadian Cultural Property Export Control List {#canadian-cultural-property-export-control-list .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 448
:::

[CULTURAL PROPERTY EXPORT AND IMPORT ACT](/eng/acts/C-51)

[INTERPRETATION ACT](/eng/acts/I-21)

Order Establishing a Canadian Cultural Property Export Control List
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556745 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Cultural Property Export Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._448).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-556747 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies only to
an object that is 50 or more years old and was made by a natural person
who is no longer living.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-329, s. 1
- SOR/2005-260, s. 1
:::

## [GROUP I]{.scheduleLabel}[Objects Recovered from the Soil or Waters of Canada]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556752 .scheduleLabel}

::: HistoricalNote

\]

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL2}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2005-260, s. 2(F)

\]
:::

- **1** In this Group,

  [[Aboriginal peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Aboriginal peoples of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,
      collectively, those persons of Indian or Inuit ancestry, including
      Métis persons, or persons recognized as being members of an
      Indian, Inuit or Métis group by the other members of that group,
      who at any time ordinarily resided in the territory that is now
      Canada; ([Autochtones du Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[artifact]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[artifact]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an object made or reworked
      by a person or persons and associated with historic or prehistoric
      cultures; ([objet façonné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[described mineral specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[described mineral specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mineral
      specimen for which scientific data, illustrations or descriptions
      appear in a professional publication; ([spécimen minéral
      décrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the preserved remains or
      traces of animals or plants that lived in the geological past but
      does not include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fossil fuels or fossiliferous rock intended for
        industrial use, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a carving or sculpture made by a person or
        persons from fossiliferous or fossilized matter;
        ([fossile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fossil amber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fossil amber]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fossil resin with or
      without inclusions; ([ambre jaune]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[invertebrate fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[invertebrate fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fossilized
      remains of an animal that did not possess a backbone; ([invertébré
      fossile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[meteorite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[meteorite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any naturally-occurring
      object of extraterrestrial origin; ([météorite]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mineral]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an element or chemical
      compound that occurs naturally in soil or water and includes
      crystals and naturally occurring metals, and gemstones whether or
      not polished or facetted by a person or persons. It does not
      include minerals, ores and concentrates intended for industrial
      use, or a carving or sculpture made by a person or persons from
      minerals; ([minéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[plant fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[plant fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fossilized remains
      of vegetable matter; ([plante fossile]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[recovered from the soil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[recovered from the soil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      object or specimen, means that the object or specimen has
      originated in or has been excavated from bedrock or sediments, has
      been retrieved as a find from the surface of bedrock or sediments
      or has been recovered from snow or ice; ([trouvé sur ou dans le
      sol]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tektite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tektite]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any natural form of silicate
      glass of non-volcanic origin; ([tectite]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[type fossil specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[type fossil specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any fossil
      specimen or portion thereof of a biological species used in the
      original scientific study and published description of that
      species; ([spécimen fossile type]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[type mineral specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[type mineral specimen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any mineral
      specimen or portion thereof of a mineral species used in the
      original scientific study and published description of that
      species; ([spécimen minéral type]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vertebrate fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vertebrate fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fossilized
      remains of an animal that possessed a backbone; ([vertébré
      fossile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[vertebrate trace fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[vertebrate trace fossil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      fossilized trace of a vertebrate. ([empreinte fossile de
      vertébré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 1
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 3
  :::

#### [Mineralogy]{.HTitleText2} {#mineralogy .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** Mineral specimens, whether composed of a single mineral, a part
  of a mineral or an aggregate of minerals, recovered from the soil of
  Canada, the territorial sea of Canada or the inland or other internal
  waters of Canada, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a type mineral specimen or a described mineral
    specimen of any value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a single mineral specimen of a fair market value in
    Canada of more than \$2,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a collection of 10 or more mineral specimens of a
    fair market value in Canada of more than \$5,000 recovered from a
    specific mine, quarry or locality;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} mineral specimens in bulk, recovered from a
    specific mineral occurrence, weighing 225 kg (500 pounds) or more of
    any value; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} meteorites and tektites of any value.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 1
  - SOR/97-159, s. 2
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 4(F)
  :::

#### [Palaeontology]{.HTitleText2} {#palaeontology .SchedHeadL2}

- **3** Palaeontological specimens recovered from the soil of Canada,
  the territorial sea of Canada or the inland or other internal waters
  of Canada, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a type fossil specimen of any value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fossil amber of any value;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a vertebrate fossil specimen of a fair market value
    in Canada of more than \$500;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an invertebrate fossil specimen of a fair market
    value in Canada of more than \$500;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} specimens in bulk weighing 11.25 kg (25 pounds) or
    more of vertebrate fossils or vertebrate trace fossils of any value;
    and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} specimens in bulk weighing 22.5 kg (50 pounds) or
    more, recovered from a specific outcrop, quarry or locality, that
    include one or more specimens of any value of the following, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} invertebrate fossils,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} plant fossils, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fossiliferous rock containing plant fossils or
      invertebrate fossils.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 1
  - SOR/97-159, s. 3
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 5(F)
  :::

#### [Archaeology]{.HTitleText2} {#archaeology .SchedHeadL2}

- - **4** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An archaeological object of any value
    recovered from the soil of Canada, the territorial sea of Canada or
    the inland or other internal waters of Canada not less than 75 years
    after its burial, concealment or abandonment if the object is an
    artifact or organic remains, including human remains, associated
    with or representative of historic or prehistoric cultures.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of subitem (1),
    archaeological objects described in that subitem include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} artifacts that relate to the Aboriginal peoples
      of Canada, namely,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} arrow heads, harpoon heads and such other
        projectile points used as hunting implements,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} adzes, axes, awls, celts, chisels and such
        other tools and agricultural implements,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} clubs, tomahawks and such other weapons,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} harpoon heads, fish hooks, sinkers, and such
        other fishing implements,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} pipes, vessels, potsherds and such other
        pottery,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} effigies, rock drawings, wampum and such other
        ceremonial and religious articles, and

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} beads, articles of adornment and such other
        objects used as trading goods;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} artifacts that relate to the progressive
      exploration, occupation, defence and development of the territory
      that is now Canada by non-aboriginal peoples, namely,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} arms, accoutrements, fragments of uniforms,
        buckles, badges, buttons, and such other objects related to
        military activity,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beads, articles of adornment and such other
        objects used as trading goods associated with the fur trade,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} hunting, fishing and trapping implements,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} ordnance, ship's gear, anchors and such other
        objects related to naval activity,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} religious paraphernalia and such other objects
        related to missionary activity,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} coins, cargo from shipwrecks or sunken ships
        and such other objects related to transportation, supply and
        commerce,

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} utensils, implements, tools, weapons,
        household articles and such other objects related to early
        settlement and pioneer life, and

      - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} machinery and such other objects related to
        manufacture and industry; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} organic remains associated with or representative
      of historic or prehistoric cultures.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/86-329, s. 2
  - SOR/97-159, s. 4
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 6
  :::

## [GROUP II]{.scheduleLabel}[Objects of Ethnographic Material Culture]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556826 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-1 .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The definitions in this section apply in this Group.

  [[Aboriginal person of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Aboriginal person of Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
      of Indian or Inuit ancestry, including a Métis person, or a person
      recognized as being a member of an Indian, Inuit or Métis group by
      the other members of that group, who at any time ordinarily
      resided in the territory that is now Canada. ([Autochtone du
      Canada]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[object of ethnographic material culture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[object of ethnographic material culture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      means an object that was made, reworked or adapted for use by a
      person who is an Aboriginal person of Canada, or an aboriginal
      person of a country other than Canada, that may

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} incorporate features reflecting contact with
        non-aboriginal cultures; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be a single object or an object together with
        its component parts that form a single unit. ([objet de culture
        matérielle ethnographique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 2
  - SOR/97-159, s. 5
  :::

#### [Objects of Ethnographic Material Culture]{.HTitleText2} {#objects-of-ethnographic-material-culture .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** An object of ethnographic material culture that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a fair market value in Canada of more than
    \$3,000 and was made, reworked or adapted for use by an Aboriginal
    person of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a fair market value in Canada of more than
    \$10,000 and was made, reworked or adapted for use by an aboriginal
    person of the territory that is now

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the United States,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Greenland, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the part of the Russian Federation east of 135°
      longitude; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a fair market value in Canada of more than
    \$20,000 and was made, reworked or adapted for use by a person who
    is an aboriginal person of a territory other than a territory
    mentioned in paragraph (a) or (b).

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/86-329, ss. 3, 4
  - SOR/95-170, s. 3
  - SOR/97-159, s. 5
  :::

## [GROUP III]{.scheduleLabel}[Military Objects]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556847 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-2 .SchedHeadL2}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2005-260, s. 7(F)

\]
:::

- **1** The definitions in this section apply in this Group.

  [[accoutrement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[accoutrement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a military accessory
      that is associated with the wearing of or use of a specific
      hand-carried weapon or piece of ordnance. It includes magazines,
      loading tools, belts, straps, holsters, mounts, telescopic or
      other sights, powder horns or flasks, bullet pouches, molds or
      starters, ramrods or wiping sticks, bayonets, scabbards and
      carrying cases. ([accessoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[dress]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[dress]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an object that is armour, a
      head-dress, pantaloons, a tunic with trappings, accessories or any
      other associated articles that form a part of military apparel.
      ([costume]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hand-carried weapon or piece of ordnance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hand-carried weapon or piece of ordnance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
      includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a small arm, staff-weapon or an edged weapon;
        and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cannon or artillery piece that is
        muzzle-loaded or breech-loaded, whether originally mounted or
        unmounted, that was used or designed to be used for a warlike
        purpose. ([arme portative ou pièce
        d'artillerie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[military]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[military]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} shall be construed as relating to
      any warlike force. ([militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 6
  :::

#### [Military Objects]{.HTitleText2} {#military-objects .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** Military objects made within or out of the territory that is now
  Canada if they relate to military activities that took place in the
  territory or if they relate to a person who at any time ordinarily
  resided in the territory and who participated in military activities
  that took place out of the territory, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an order, decoration, medal, insignia, including a
    ribbon, collar or sash normally associated with such order,
    decoration, medal or insignia, that has a fair market value in
    Canada of more than \$3,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a flag, colour, banner or pennant that has a fair
    market value in Canada of more than \$3,000;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} dress that has a fair market value in Canada of
    more than \$3,000; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any hand-carried weapon or piece of ordnance, or
    their associated accoutrements, that has a fair market value in
    Canada of more than \$3,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/86-329, s. 5
  - SOR/95-170, s. 4
  - SOR/97-159, s. 7
  :::

## [GROUP IV]{.scheduleLabel}[Objects of Applied and Decorative Art]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556870 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-3 .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** In this Group, [[object of applied and decorative
  art]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means art in which the principles of design,
  ornamentation, enrichment and decoration are applied to the production
  of functional and utilitarian objects or architectural features.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 8
  :::

#### [Objects of Applied and Decorative Art]{.HTitleText2} {#objects-of-applied-and-decorative-art .SchedHeadL2}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/97-159, s. 9

\]
:::

- - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Objects of applied and decorative art that
    are more than 100 years old made in the territory that is now
    Canada, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} glassware, ceramics, textiles, woodenware and
      works in base metals that have a fair market value in Canada of
      more than \$1,000; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} furniture, sculptured works in wood, other than
      sacred or religious carvings, works in precious metals and other
      objects of applied and decorative art that have a fair market
      value in Canada of more than \$4,000.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of
    paragraph (1)(a), objects of applied and decorative art described in
    that paragraph include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} utensils, tools, earthenware and such other
      household articles;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} costumes, embroidery, lace and such other objects
      related to dress;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} personal weapons; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} objects of folk art.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of
    paragraph (1)(b), objects of applied and decorative art described in
    that paragraph include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} articles made of silver or gold;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} jewellery;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} decorative sculpture;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} architectural features;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} furnishings, including carpets, tapestries and
      lighting fixtures; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} objects of folk art.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 10
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 8(F)
  :::

- **3** An object of applied and decorative art that was made in the
  territory that is now Canada, that is not less than 50 years old and
  that has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$3,000, namely, a
  sacred or religious carving.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 5
  - SOR/97-159, s. 11
  :::

- **4** An object of applied and decorative art that is not less than
  50 years old, that was made within or outside the territory that is
  now Canada by a person who at any time ordinarily resided in that
  territory and that has a fair market value in Canada of more than
  \$6,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 6
  - SOR/97-159, s. 11
  :::

- - **5** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An object of applied and decorative art that
    is made outside the territory that is now Canada, that is not less
    than 50 years old and that has a fair market value in Canada of more
    than \$3,000, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian pattern coin;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trial strike of a Canadian coin;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a medal or medallion intended for or awarded to a
      person who at any time ordinarily resided in the territory that is
      now Canada; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a pre-production proof of a Canadian postage
      stamp.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} An object of applied and decorative art, other than
    an object described in subsection (1), that is made outside the
    territory that is now Canada, that is not less than 50 years old,
    that has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$8,000 and that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was commissioned by a person who at any time
      ordinarily resided in the territory that is now Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incorporates a Canadian theme or subject; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is identified with a prominent person,
      institution or memorable event that relates to the art history,
      history or national life of Canada.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/86-329, ss. 6 to 9
  - SOR/97-159, s. 11
  :::

- **6** Any object of applied and decorative art, other than an object
  described in section 5, that was made outside the territory that is
  now Canada, that is not less than 50 years old and that has a fair
  market value in Canada of more than \$15,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 11
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 9(F)
  :::

## [GROUP V]{.scheduleLabel}[Objects of Fine Art]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556919 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-4 .SchedHeadL2}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2005-260, s. 10(F)

\]
:::

- **1** The definitions in this section apply in this Group.

  [[drawing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drawing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unique artistic
      representation or work including calligraphy, usually on paper,
      parchment or vellum, executed in media such as pen and ink, ink
      wash, black or colour chalk, pastels, charcoal, graphite, water
      colour, gouache or metal-point. ([dessin]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[painting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[painting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a unique artistic
      representation or work executed in oil-base pigments, fresco,
      collage, tempera, encaustic, synthetic or other media on stretched
      canvas, mounted paper, cardboard or other manufactured board,
      metal, glass, wood, silk or other support.
      ([peinture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[print]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[print]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an artistic representation or
      work usually on paper or vellum, executed in media such as
      woodcut, metalcut, wood engraving, engraving, etching, drypoint,
      mezzotint, aquatint, soft ground, lithography, monotype,
      cliché-verre or silk screen. ([estampe]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[sculpture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sculpture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an artistic representation
      or work in three dimensions that is carved, modeled or constructed
      and includes such a representation that has subsequently been cast
      in plaster, metal or other substance that will take on a rigid
      form. ([sculpture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 7
  - SOR/97-159, s. 12
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 11(F)
  :::

#### [Objects of Fine Art]{.HTitleText2} {#objects-of-fine-art .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** An object of fine art that is made within or outside the
  territory that is now Canada by a person who at any time ordinarily
  resided in the territory that is now Canada and that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of drawing or print, has a fair market
    value in Canada of more than \$5,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a painting or sculpture, has a fair
    market value in Canada of more than \$15,000; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of works of fine art in media other
    than those listed in paragraph (a) or (b), or works of art in
    multi-media, has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$5,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 8(F)
  - SOR/97-159, s. 13
  :::

- - **3** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Objects of fine art made within or outside
    the territory that is now Canada, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a drawing or print that has a fair market value
      in Canada of more than \$7,500,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a painting or sculpture that has a fair market
      value in Canada of more than \$20,000, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a work of fine art in media other than those
      listed in paragraph (a) or (b), or works of fine art in
      multi-media, that has a fair market value in Canada of more than
      \$7,500,

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} The object referred to in paragraph (1)(a), (b)
    or (c) must

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have been commissioned by a person who at any
      time ordinarily resided in the territory that is now Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} incorporate a Canadian theme or subject, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be identified with a prominent person,
      institution or memorable event that relates to the art history,
      history or national life of Canada.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 9
  - SOR/97-159, s. 13
  :::

- **4** Objects of fine art, other than the objects described in
  section 2 or 3, that are made outside the territory that is now
  Canada, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a drawing or print that has a fair market value in
    Canada of more than \$15,000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a painting or sculpture that has a fair market
    value in Canada of more than \$30,000; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a work of fine art in media other than those listed
    in paragraph (a) or (b), or works of fine art in multi-media, that
    has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$20,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 13
  :::

- **5** For prints executed since 1880, a distinction shall be made

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/80-855, s. 1
  :::

- **6** The date of a print is established by the date of the execution

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/80-855, s. 1
  - SOR/86-329, ss. 10 to 13
  :::

- **7** Photographs are included in Group VII of this List.

  ::: HistoricalNote

## [GROUP VI]{.scheduleLabel}[Scientific or Technological Objects]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-556965 .scheduleLabel}

#### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#interpretation-5 .SchedHeadL2}

::: HistoricalNote

- SOR/2005-260, s. 12(F)

- **1** In this Group,

  [[machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[machine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contrivance that is used
      in the performance of some kind of work or activity and that
      consists of inter-related parts and uses any source of energy
      including animal power, manpower, air, water, light, steam,
      gravity, friction, combustion or electricity, but does not include
      scrap metal intended for industrial purposes;
      ([machine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[patent model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[patent model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the model constructed
      for the purpose of obtaining a patent for an invention, discovery
      or process; ([modèle breveté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prototype model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prototype model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any original working
      model on which subsequent production was based;
      ([prototype]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[scale model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[scale model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a model reduced in size
      according to a fixed scale or proportion; ([modèle
      réduit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[scientific apparatus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[scientific apparatus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an assembly of
      objects forming a unit constructed for the purpose of research in
      any scientific discipline; ([appareil
      scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[scientific instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[scientific instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an implement,
      tool or device used for practical or scientific purposes as an
      instrument for examining or measuring. ([instrument
      scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 15
  :::

#### [Scientific or Technological Objects]{.HTitleText2} {#scientific-or-technological-objects .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** An object that was made, designed or invented in the territory
  that is now Canada, or that was made, designed or invented out of the
  territory by a person who at any time ordinarily resided in the
  territory, and that has a fair market value in Canada of more than
  \$3,000, as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a scientific instrument, other than a scientific
    instrument that is intended to be used for any scientific or
    technological purpose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an original scientific apparatus; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a scale model, patent model or prototype model of a
    scientific instrument, original scientific apparatus or machine.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 10
  - SOR/97-159, s. 16
  :::

- **3** A machine, other than a machine that is intended to be used for
  a manufacturing, industrial or commercial purpose, that was made,
  designed or invented in the territory that is now Canada or that was
  made, designed or invented outside that territory by a person who at
  any time ordinarily resided in that territory, and that has a fair
  market value in Canada of more than \$3,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 11
  - SOR/97-159, s. 17
  :::

- **4** The following objects made out of the territory that is now
  Canada if they relate to the history of science and the development of
  technology in Canada, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a scientific instrument, other than a scientific
    instrument that is intended to be used for any scientific or
    technological purpose, that has a fair market value in Canada of
    more than \$5,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a machine, other than a machine that is intended to
    be used for a manufacturing, industrial or commercial purpose, that
    has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$5,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 18
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 13(F)
  :::

- **5** The following objects made out of the territory that is now
  Canada other than the objects described in item 4, if they are related
  to the history of science and the development of technology, namely,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a scientific instrument, other than a scientific
    instrument that is intended to be used for any scientific or
    technological purpose, that has a fair market value in Canada of
    more than \$8,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a machine, other than a machine that is intended to
    be used for a manufacturing, industrial or commercial purpose, that
    has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$8,000.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 19
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 14(F)

## [GROUP VII]{.scheduleLabel}[Textual Records, Graphic Records and Sound Recordings]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-557000 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Textual Records]{.HTitleText2} {#textual-records .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#interpretation-6 .SchedHeadL3}

- **1** The definitions in this section apply in this section and
  section 2.

  [[broadsheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[broadsheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a publication that
      consists of a single sheet printed on both sides, sometimes also
      referred to as a handbill. ([prospectus]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[broadside]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[broadside]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a publication that
      consists of a single sheet bearing information on one side only
      that is intended to be affixed to a surface and that is more
      textual than graphic. ([placard]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
      [[manuscript]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-260,
      s. 15\]]{.Repealed}

  [[leaflet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[leaflet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a publication that consists
      of one or more sheets, printed on both sides, that is folded but
      not stitched or bound. ([feuillet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manuscript, record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manuscript, record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      textual material in holograph or typescript, excluding printed
      materials and other works intended for public distribution such as
      printed books, pamphlets, or serials. Nevertheless, objects
      considered to be a manuscript, record or document include

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a diary, ledger or letterbook;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a literary manuscript;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a leaflet, broadsheet, broadside or poster;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a letter and enclosures;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a cover or sheet that contains postal markings
        or that incorporates postage or revenue stamps;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum, report or account;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} sheet music;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} printed ephemera; and

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a single newspaper. ([manuscrit, pièce
        d'archives ou document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[printed book or pamphlet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[printed book or pamphlet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a work of
      at least two pages, exclusive of cover pages, made up of sheets
      bound, stitched or fastened together so as to form a material
      whole under an established date of printing. It includes each work
      in a set issued under a single title. ([livre imprimé ou
      brochure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[serial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a publication that appears at
      intervals for an indefinite period. ([série]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[serial title]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serial title]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a run of a serial,
      irrespective of its continuity. ([titre de série]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[textual record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[textual record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2005-260,
      s. 15\]]{.Repealed}

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/97-159, s. 20
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 15
  :::

##### [Textual Records]{.HTitleText3} {#textual-records-1 .SchedHeadL3}

- - **2** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Textual records if they were made in the
    territory that is now Canada, were made outside that territory by a
    person who at any time ordinarily resided in that territory, or were
    made outside that territory and relate to the history or national
    life of Canada, namely,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a manuscript, record or document that has a fair
      market value in Canada of more than \$1,000;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a collection of associated manuscripts, records
      or documents that has a fair market value in Canada of more than
      \$2,000;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a printed book or pamphlet, a set of printed
      books or pamphlets under a single title or a serial title, other
      than a newspaper, that has a fair market value in Canada of more
      than \$3,000; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a collection of associated printed books or
      pamphlets or serial titles, other than a collection of newspapers,
      that has a fair market value in Canada of more than \$15,000.

  - [(2)]{.lawlabel} Without restricting the generality of
    subsection (1), examples of paper money considered to be a
    manuscript, record or document include card money, ordonnances of
    the French regime, army bills of the years 1813 to 1815 and
    provincial treasury notes made before the year 1818 for circulation
    in the territory that is now Canada.

  - [(3)]{.lawlabel} The age of any printed material is determined by
    the established date of printing for the material.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/80-855, s. 2
  - SOR/95-170, s. 12

  - SOR/2005-260, s. 16(E)

#### [Graphic Records]{.HTitleText2} {#graphic-records .SchedHeadL2}

##### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText3} {#interpretation-7 .SchedHeadL3}

- **3** The definitions in this section apply in this section and
  sections 4 to 6.

  [[cartographic record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cartographic record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a collection of cartographic representations
        composed of manuscript or printed material that is bound,
        stitched or fastened together to form a unit that has no
        established date of printing for the unit as a whole;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a single sheet on which are cartographic
        representations, irrespective of folding; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cartographic material that is

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} unattached and issued in a combination other
          than a printed book, such as boxed maps, irrespective of the
          date of printing, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a loose format that clearly forms a
          single unit of visual information. ([pièce d'archives
          cartographiques ou document cartographique]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

  [[cinematographic film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cinematographic film]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means positive or
      negative film, with or without sound, that contains continuous
      images designed to create the illusion of motion when projected in
      rapid succession, and includes film issued in any form in a set
      under a single title that may or may not be part of the film.
      Cinematographic films issued as a serial are considered to be a
      collection of associated cinematographic films. ([film
      cinématographique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[design]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[design]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a preliminary sketch or
      drawing or an outline sketch, drawing, print or plan of a work of
      art, an edifice, a machine or any other subject to be constructed
      or made. ([dessin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[graphic record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[graphic record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a book, manuscript,
      record, document, photographic positive or negative,
      cinematographic film, map or any designs or material whose primary
      object is the communication of information in a visual form, other
      than written or printed language. ([pièce d'archives
      graphiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[iconographic object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[iconographic object]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a graphic
      representation such as a design, illustration or other document
      that consists primarily of graphic representations or pictures.
      ([objet iconographique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[map]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[map]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document that is a
      cartographic representation and includes a topographic,
      hydrographic, military, cadastral, aeronautic or survey map,
      cartogram, chart or plan. ([carte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[photographic record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[photographic record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a collection of photographs attached or mounted
        to form a unit, other than a printed book such as a photograph
        album; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a collection of loose or unattached photographs
        that clearly form a single unit of visual information. A
        photograph may take the form of a daguerrotype, ambrotype,
        calotype, talbotype, tintype, transparency, cinegraph or
        hologram. ([pièce d'archives photographiques ou document
        photographique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pictorial record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pictorial record or document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bound or attached collection of designs such
        as a sketch book; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a collection of loose or unattached designs
        that clearly form a single unit of visual information, such as a
        set of architectural plans or blueprints. ([pièce d'archives
        picturales ou document pictural]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[printed atlas or cartographic book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[printed atlas or cartographic book]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      bound work with an established date of printing and includes each
      volume of a set of bound works issued under a single title.
      ([atlas ou recueil de cartes imprimés]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[printed book of photographs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[printed book of photographs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[printed
      book of pictures or designs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bound
      work with an established date of printing and includes each volume
      of a set of bound works issued under a single title. ([recueil de
      photographies imprimé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}ou[recueil
      d'illustrations ou de dessins imprimé]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/95-170, s. 13
  - SOR/97-159, s. 20
  - SOR/2005-260, s. 17(F)
  :::
