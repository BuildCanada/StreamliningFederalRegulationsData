## Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 25 (RCMP) {#exempt-personal-information-bank-order-no.-25-rcmp .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-272
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1993-05-25

Order Respecting the Designation of the National Security Investigations
Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, as an Exempt Personal Information Bank

P.C. 1993-1034  1993-05-25
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada and the President of the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsection 18(1) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the designation of
the National Security Investigations Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, as an
exempt personal information bank.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952396 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Exempt Personal Information Bank Order, No. 25
(RCMP)](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-272).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952398 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The National Security
Investigations Records, No. CMP/P-PU-025, a personal information bank
under the control of the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, is hereby
designated as an exempt bank on the basis of sections 21 and 22 of the
[Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21).

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 18(3)(b) of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21), the laws
concerned, in relation to those files that are contained in the exempt
bank referred to in section 2 that consist predominantly of personal
information described in subparagraph 22(1)(a)(ii) of that Act, are the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), the [Foreign Interference and Security
of Information Act](/eng/acts/O-5), the [Security Offences
Act](/eng/acts/S-7), the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Act](/eng/acts/R-10) and the [Canadian Security Intelligence Service
Act](/eng/acts/C-23).
