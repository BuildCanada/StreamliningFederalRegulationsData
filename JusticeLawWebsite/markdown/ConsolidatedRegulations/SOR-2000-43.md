## Gasoline and Gasoline Blend Dispensing Flow Rate Regulations {#gasoline-and-gasoline-blend-dispensing-flow-rate-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-43
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2000-02-01

Gasoline and Gasoline Blend Dispensing Flow Rate Regulations

P.C. 2000-79 2000-02-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 5, 1999, a copy of
the proposed [Gasoline and Gasoline Blend Dispensing Flow Rate
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-43), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the Regulations or to file a notice of objection requesting
that a board of review be established and stating the reasons for the
objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA){#footnotea_e}R.S., c. 16
  (4th Supp.)
  :::

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 34(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EEBCA} of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act^a^, the federal-provincial
advisory committee has been given an opportunity to provide its advice
under section 6 of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e-ID0EEBCA){#footnoteb_e}S.C. 1989, c.
  9, s. 2
  :::

And whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 34(3) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act^a^, the
proposed Regulations do not regulate an aspect of a substance that is
regulated by or under any other Act of Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Health, pursuant to subsection 34(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Gasoline and Gasoline Blend
Dispensing Flow Rate Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-43).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663846 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a fuel that is sold or represented as gasoline;
      or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a petroleum distillate, or a mixture of petroleum
      distillates, oxygenates or additives, that is suitable for use in
      a spark ignition engine and that has the following
      characteristics, as determined by the applicable test method
      listed in the National Standard of Canada standard
      CAN/CGSB-3.5-94, Unleaded Automotive Gasoline, as amended from
      time to time:

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a vapour pressure of at least 38 kPa,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an antiknock index of at least 80,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a distillation temperature, at which 10% of
        the fuel has evaporated, of not less than 35°C and not greater
        than 70°C, and

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a distillation temperature, at which 50% of
        the fuel has evaporated, of not less than 65°C and not greater
        than 120°C. ([essence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gasoline blend]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gasoline blend]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any mixture of gasoline
    and oxygenate that is suitable for use in a spark ignition engine.
    ([mélange d'essence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heavy-duty vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heavy-duty vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an on-road vehicle
    that has

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a maximum design loaded weight rating specified
      by a manufacturer of more than 3856 kg;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a curb weight of more than 2722 kg; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an area enclosed by the geometric projection of
      the basic vehicle, which includes tires but does not include
      mirrors or air deflectors, along the longitudinal axis of the
      vehicle onto a plane perpendicular to that axis, of more than 4.2
      m^2^. ([véhicule lourd]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[on-road vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on-road vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any self-propelled
    vehicle that is designed to be driven on roads. ([véhicule
    routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oxygenate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oxygenate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an oxygen-containing,
    ashless, organic compound that, when added to gasoline, increases
    the oxygen content in the gasoline. ([produit
    oxygéné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who owns or
    operates any establishment at which gasoline or a gasoline blend is
    sold or offered for sale for use in an on-road vehicle.
    ([détaillant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wholesale purchaser-consumer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wholesale purchaser-consumer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    person that is not a retailer and that stores gasoline or a gasoline
    blend in a storage tank of at least 2100 L for use in an on-road
    vehicle. ([grossiste acheteur-consommateur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663864 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the flow rate from any nozzle that is used to dispense gasoline, or a
gasoline blend, that contains benzene, but do not apply to the flow rate
from any nozzle that is used exclusively to dispense gasoline or a
gasoline blend into heavy-duty vehicles.

## [Maximum Dispensing Flow Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663866 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No retailer or wholesale
purchaser-consumer shall use or offer for use a nozzle to dispense
gasoline or a gasoline blend into an on-road vehicle if the flow rate
from the nozzle exceeds 38 L/min.

## [Method of Determining Dispensing Flow Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663868 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  shall determine the flow rate referred to in section 3 by measuring
  the amount of time it takes to dispense 10.0 L of gasoline or a
  gasoline blend from the nozzle with the nozzle operated at the maximum
  flow rate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making the determination referred to in subsection
  (1), the person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use a digital stopwatch that reads to at least 0.01
    seconds to measure the dispensing time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the dispenser's volume meter to measure the
    dispensed volume of gasoline or gasoline blend; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} start the stopwatch when the dispenser's volume
    meter indicates that 2.0 L of gasoline or gasoline blend have been
    dispensed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person shall use the following formula to
  calculate the dispensing flow rate of gasoline or a gasoline blend
  from the nozzle, in litres per minute:

  ::: {#663876 .Subsection}
  Flow Rate = 60 × \[V~f~ - V~i~\]/\[T~f~ - T~i~\]

  where

  [T~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the reading of the stopwatch, in seconds, when the person
      starts the stopwatch;

  [T~f~]{.dfn}
  :   is the reading of the stopwatch, in seconds, when the person stops
      the stopwatch;

  [V~i~]{.dfn}
  :   is the reading of the dispenser's volume meter, in litres, when
      the person starts the stopwatch; and

  [V~f~]{.dfn}
  :   is the reading of the dispenser's volume meter, in litres, when
      the person stops the stopwatch.
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663882 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force 12 months after the day on which they are registered.
