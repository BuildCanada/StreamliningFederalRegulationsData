## Locally Engaged Staff Exclusion Approval Order {#locally-engaged-staff-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2024-7
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2024-02-14

Locally Engaged Staff Exclusion Approval Order

P.C. 2024-86 2024-02-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada, under subsections
20(1) and (3) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367){#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367-ID0EBDFA},
approves

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367-ID0EBDFA){#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application by the Public Service Commission
  of the provisions of that Act to the positions excluded under Order in
  Council P.C. 1967-444 of March 10, 1967, and to the persons occupying
  them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion by the Public Service Commission from
  the application of the Act of a person who is appointed and employed
  outside Canada, who is performing duties that are directly related to
  diplomatic or consular operations or a military support unit of the
  Government of Canada located outside Canada and who is subject to the
  laws of the host country where they are employed.

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided, under subsection
20(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367){#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367-ID0EBAEA},
that it is neither practicable nor in the best interests of the public
service to apply certain provisions of that Act to a person who is
appointed and employed outside Canada, who is performing duties that are
directly related to diplomatic or consular operations or a military
support unit of the Government of Canada located outside Canada and who
is subject to the laws of the host country where they are employed;

And whereas, under subsection 20(2) of that Act, the Public Service
Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, under subsections 20(1) and
(3) of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367){#fn_81000-2-4385_hq_28367-ID0EBCCA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} re-applies the provisions of that Act to the
  positions excluded under Order in Council P.C. 1967-444 of March 10,
  1967 and to the persons occupying them; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes from the application of that Act a person
  who is appointed and employed outside Canada, who is performing duties
  that are directly related to diplomatic or consular operations or a
  military support unit of the Government of Canada located outside
  Canada and who is subject to the laws of the host country where they
  are employed.

Gatineau, November 21, 2023
