## Order Designating the Commission that is Named the Joint Public Inquiry into the Nova Scotia April 2020 Tragedy as a Department for the Purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-commission-that-is-named-the-joint-public-inquiry-into-the-nova-scotia-april-2020-tragedy-as-a-department-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2020-70
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2020-11-11

Order Designating the Commission that is Named the Joint Public Inquiry
into the Nova Scotia April 2020 Tragedy as a Department for the Purposes
of that Act

P.C. 2020-823 2020-10-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687-ID0EGABDA}
  of the definition [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688-ID0EBABDA},
  designates the commission that is named the Joint Public Inquiry into
  the Nova Scotia April 2020 Tragedy as a department for the purposes of
  that Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689-ID0EGAADA}
  of the definition [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  section 2 of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)^b^,
  designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect
  to the commission named in paragraph (a).
