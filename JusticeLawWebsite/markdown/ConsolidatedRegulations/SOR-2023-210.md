## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[beneficiary country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beneficiary country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country or
    territory that is a beneficiary of the General Preferential
    Tariff. ([pays bénéficiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[beneficiary-plus country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[beneficiary-plus country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country or
    territory that is a beneficiary of the General Preferential Tariff
    Plus. ([pays bénéficiaire plus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any good referred to in heading
    50.07, 51.11 through 51.13, 52.08 through 52.12, 53.09 through
    53.11, 54.07 through 54.08, 55.12 through 55.16, 58.01 through
    58.07, 58.09 through 58.11, 59.01 through 59.11 or 60.01 through
    60.06 of the List of Tariff Provisions. ([tissu]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[least developed country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[least developed country]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a country or
    territory that is a beneficiary of the Least Developed Country
    Tariff. ([pays parmi les moins développés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Origin of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424468 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A good originates in a
beneficiary country, beneficiary-plus country or least developed country
if the good is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mineral product extracted from the soil or the
  seabed of the country;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vegetable product harvested in the country;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a live animal born and raised in the country;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a good obtained in the country from a live animal;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a good obtained from hunting or fishing in the
  country;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a good obtained from sea fishing or any other marine
  good taken from the sea by a vessel of the country;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a good produced on board a factory ship of the
  country exclusively from a good referred to in paragraph (f);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} waste and scrap derived from manufacturing operations
  of the country;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a used good of the country imported into Canada for
  use only for the recovery of raw materials; or

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a good produced in the country exclusively from a
  product or good referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (h).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Beneficiary country or beneficiary-plus
country

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods,
  other than those set out in Chapters 61 to 63 of the List of Tariff
  Provisions, originate in a beneficiary country or beneficiary-plus
  country if the value of the materials, parts or products originating
  outside that country or in an undetermined location and used in the
  manufacture or production of the goods is no more than 40% of the
  ex-factory price of the goods as packed for shipment to Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed origin

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following are deemed to have originated in the
    beneficiary country:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any materials, parts or products that were used
      in the manufacture or production of the goods referred to in that
      subsection and that originated in any other beneficiary country or
      Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any packing required for the transportation of
      the goods referred to in that subsection, not including packing in
      which the goods are ordinarily sold for consumption in the
      beneficiary country; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the following are deemed to have originated in the
    beneficiary-plus country:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any materials, parts or products that were used
      in the manufacture or production of the goods referred to in that
      subsection and that originated in any other beneficiary-plus
      country, any beneficiary country or Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any packing required for the transportation of
      the goods referred to in that subsection, not including packing in
      which the goods are ordinarily sold for consumption in the
      beneficiary-plus country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapter 61 and 62 goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Goods set out in Chapters 61 and 62 of the List of
  Tariff Provisions originate in a beneficiary country or in a
  beneficiary-plus country if they are cut, or knit to shape, and sewn
  or otherwise assembled in that beneficiary country or beneficiary-plus
  country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chapter 63 goods

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Goods set out in Chapter 63 of the List of Tariff
  Provisions originate in a beneficiary country or in a beneficiary-plus
  country if they are cut, or knit to shape, and sewn or otherwise
  assembled in that beneficiary country or beneficiary-plus country from
  fabric produced in any beneficiary country, beneficiary-plus country
  or Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fabric or parts that determine tariff
  classification

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining whether a good set out
  in Chapters 61 to 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions originates in a
  beneficiary country or in a beneficiary-plus country, subsections (3)
  and (4) apply only to the fabric or the parts knit to shape that
  determine the tariff classification of the good, identified in
  accordance with the General Rules for the Interpretation of the
  Harmonized System set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Least developed country

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods,
  other than those set out in Part A1, A3 or B of the schedule,
  originate in a least developed country if the value of the materials,
  parts or products originating outside that country or in an
  undetermined location and used in the manufacture or production of the
  goods is no more than 80% of the ex-factory price of the goods as
  packed for shipment to Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed origin

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the following are
  deemed to have originated in the least developed country:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any materials, parts or products that were used in
    the manufacture or production of the goods referred to in that
    subsection and that originated in any other least developed country
    or Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any packing required for the transportation of the
    goods referred to in that subsection, not including packing in which
    the goods are ordinarily sold for consumption in the least developed
    country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part A1 or A2 goods

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Goods set out in Part A1 or A2 of the schedule
  originate in a least developed country if they are cut, or knit to
  shape, and sewn or otherwise assembled in that least developed
  country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part A3 goods

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Goods set out in Part A3 of the schedule originate in
  a least developed country if they are sewn or otherwise assembled in
  that least developed country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Part B goods

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Goods set out in Part B of the schedule originate in
  a least developed country if they are cut, or knit to shape, and sewn
  or otherwise assembled in that least developed country from fabric
  produced in any least developed country or Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fabric or parts that determine tariff
  classification

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining whether a good set out
  in Part A1, A2, A3 or B of the schedule originates in a least
  developed country, subsections (3) to (5) apply only to the fabric or
  the parts knit to shape that determine the tariff classification of
  the good, identified in accordance with the General Rules for the
  Interpretation of the Harmonized System set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of originating status

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** When a good that qualifies
as originating is transported outside a beneficiary country,
beneficiary-plus country or least developed country, the good retains
its originating status only if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the good remains under customs control outside the
  beneficiary country, beneficiary-plus country or least developed
  country; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the good does not undergo any further production or
  other operation outside the beneficiary country, beneficiary-plus
  country or least developed country, other than unloading, reloading,
  separation from a bulk shipment, storing or any other operation
  necessary to transport the good to Canada or to preserve it in good
  condition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate consideration

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of determining the origin of goods, each good in a shipment
  must be considered separately, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a group, set or assembly of goods is classified
    in one tariff item, the group, set or assembly is considered to be
    one good; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} tools, parts and accessories are considered to form
    a whole with a good if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} they are imported with the good,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} they constitute the equipment customarily
      included in the sale of a good of that kind, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} their price is included in that of the good and
      no separate charge is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unassembled goods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An unassembled good whose components are imported in
  more than one shipment because it is not feasible for transport or
  production reasons to import them in one shipment is considered to be
  one good.

## [Direct Shipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1424527 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Preferential Tariff

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods
  are entitled to the General Preferential Tariff only if they are
  shipped directly to Canada, with or without transhipment, from a
  beneficiary country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General Preferential Tariff Plus

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods are entitled to the General Preferential Tariff
  Plus only if they are shipped directly to Canada, with or without
  transhipment, from a beneficiary-plus country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Least Developed Country Tariff

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Goods are entitled to the Least Developed Country
  Tariff only if they are shipped directly to Canada, with or without
  transhipment, from a least developed country.
