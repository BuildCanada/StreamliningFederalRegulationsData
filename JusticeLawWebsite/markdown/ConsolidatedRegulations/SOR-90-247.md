## Gasoline Regulations {#gasoline-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-247
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1990-04-26

Gasoline Regulations

P.C. 1990-740 1990-04-26
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 48(1) of the Canadian Environmental
Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBCA}, the
Minister of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/)Part I on July 15, 1989 a copy of the
annexed Regulations respecting concentrations of lead and phosphorus in
gasoline, substantially in the form annexed hereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBCA){#footnote1star_e}R.S., c.
  16 (4th Supp.)
  :::

And Whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, the
concentrations of lead and phosphorus in gasoline prescribed in the
annexed Regulations, if exceeded, would, on the combustion of the
gasoline in ordinary circumstances, result in a significant contribution
to air pollution;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section
47 and subsection 87(1) of the Canadian Environmental Protection
Act^\*^, is pleased hereby to revoke the Lead-Free Gasoline Regulations,
C.R.C., c. 408 and the Leaded Gasoline Regulations, C.R.C., c. 409 and
to make the annexed Regulations respecting concentrations of lead and
phosphorus in gasoline, in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922657 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    lang="fr"})

[[competition vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competition vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vehicle or boat
    that is used exclusively for competition and does not include a
    vehicle that is used on a highway or a vehicle or boat that is used
    for recreational purposes; ([véhicule de
    compétition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[gasoline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/94-355, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a common or public road,
    street, avenue, parkway, driveway, square, place, bridge or viaduct,
    any part of which is intended for or used by the general public for
    the passage of vehicles, and includes the area between the lateral

    lang="fr"})

## [Maximum Concentrations of Lead in Gasoline]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922676 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The maximum concentration
of lead in gasoline produced, imported or sold in Canada is 5 mg/L.

## [Analysis]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922685 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of lead
in gasoline produced in, imported into or sold in Canada shall be
measured in accordance with the National Standard of Canada method
CAN/CGSB-3.0 No. 19.5-2011, Methods of Testing Petroleum and Associated
Products: Determination of Lead in Automotive Gasoline (Atomic
Absorption), as amended from time to time.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Maintenance of Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-922693 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/2008-126, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who produces, sells or imports for use or sale in Canada leaded
  gasoline for use in competition vehicles shall make and retain a
  record of the quantities of leaded gasoline produced, sold or imported
  for use or sale in Canada each year and that record shall show

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline's brand name;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the gasoline's octane rating and the test method by
    which the rating was determined;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the average lead concentration in milligrams of
    lead per litre of gasoline on a yearly basis;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the distributor or reseller of the gasoline, if
      the gasoline was sold for distribution or resale, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the track or event location where the gasoline
      was used, if the gasoline was sold at a particular track or event
      location; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of leaded gasoline sold to each
    of the entities specified in subparagraph (d)(i) or at each of the
    locations specified in subparagraph (d)(ii).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record shall be submitted annually to the
  Minister on or before March 31 of the year following each year during
  which leaded gasoline was produced, sold or imported for use or sale
  in Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The record shall be retained in Canada for a period
  of five years beginning on the date the record is made.

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record that is required under these Regulations shall be sent
  electronically in the form and format specified by the Minister and
  shall bear the electronic signature of an authorized official.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister has not specified an electronic form
  and format or if it is impractical to send the record electronically
  in accordance with subsection (1) because of circumstances beyond the
  control of the person sending the record, they shall send it on paper,
  signed by an authorized official, in the form and format specified by
  the Minister. However, if no form and format have been so specified,
  the record may be in any form and format.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), an
  [[authorized official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a corporation, an officer of the
    corporation who is authorized to act on its behalf;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other person, that person or a
    person authorized to act on behalf of that person; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other entity, a person authorized
    to act on its behalf.
