::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section

Whereas the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs has consulted
with the government of each province and such organizations
representative of workers, organizations representative of employers and
organizations representative of suppliers as the Minister deemed
appropriate;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs,
pursuant to paragraph 48(1)(a) of the [Hazardous Materials Information
Review Act](/eng/acts/H-2.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-88-456_e_hq_5344){#fn_SOR-88-456_e_hq_5344-ID0EBEAA},
is pleased hereby:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1987-2720 of December
  31, 1987[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-88-456_e_hq_5345){#fn_SOR-88-456_e_hq_5345-ID0EBADAA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make the annexed [Regulations respecting hazardous
  materials information review](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-456).

## [Criteria for Determining the Validity of a Claim for Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913355 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), for the purpose of determining whether a claim for
  exemption from disclosing information in accordance with section 11 of
  the Act is valid, the Minister must have regard exclusively to the
  following criteria:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the information is not publicly available;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the claimant has taken measures that are
    reasonable in the circumstances to ensure that the information
    remains not publicly available; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the information has actual or potential
    economic value to the claimant or to the claimant's competitors
    because it is not publicly available and its disclosure would result
    in a material financial loss to the claimant or a material financial
    gain to the claimant's competitors.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining that a claim for
  exemption from disclosing information in accordance with section 11 of
  the Act is valid, but not for the purpose of determining that such a
  claim for exemption is not valid, the Minister must also, as a
  criterion, have regard to whether the money expended and the other
  business resources employed by the claimant to develop the information
  are substantial in the circumstances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), information does
  not cease to be not publicly available by reason only that it is known
  to persons who have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtained the information in confidence, express or
    implied, and are obligated by contract or trust, express or implied,
    or otherwise by law or equity to maintain the confidentiality of the
    information; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} possession of the information without colour of
    right or lawful authority.

## [Fees for Claims for Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913367 .Part}

### [Original Claims]{.HTitleText2} {#h-913368 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 7, the
fee required under subsection 11(3) of the Act to accompany an original
claim is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1800, when only one original claim is filed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when more than one original claim is filed at the
  same time,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$1800 for each of the first 15 original claims
    filed,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$400 for each of the next 10 original claims
    filed, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} \$200 for each original claim filed in addition
    to those referred to in subparagraphs (i) and (ii).

### [Manner of Filing a Claim and Information to be Contained in a Claim]{.HTitleText2} {#h-913396 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A claim
  for exemption must be in writing, must bear the written or electronic
  signature of the claimant or a facsimile of that signature, must be
  dated by the claimant and must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and, if
    applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of the
    claimant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and, if
    applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of an
    individual who can be contacted in respect of the claim, if the
    information is different from that required under paragraph (a);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement identifying the claim as either an
    original claim or a refiled claim;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether the claimant is a
    supplier or an employer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is made by a supplier, a statement
    identifying the subject matter of the information for which the
    claim is made as being one or more of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a material or substance that is a
      hazardous product,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of the material or substance,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the CAS registry number, or any other unique
        identifier, of the material or substance, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of any impurity, stabilizing
        solvent or stabilizing additive in the material or substance
        that is known to the supplier, that, in accordance with the
        provisions of the [Hazardous Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3), is
        classified in a category or subcategory of a health hazard class
        and that contributes to the classification, in accordance with
        those provisions, of the material or substance in the health
        hazard class,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an ingredient in a mixture that
      is a hazardous product,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of the ingredient,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the CAS registry number, or any other unique
        identifier, of the ingredient, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the concentration or concentration range of the
        ingredient, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a material, substance or mixture
      that is a hazardous product, the name of any toxicological study
      that identifies the material or substance or any ingredient in the
      mixture;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is made by an employer, a statement
    identifying the subject matter of the information for which the
    claim is made as being one or more of the following:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a material or substance that is a
      hazardous product,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of the material or substance,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the CAS registry number, or any other unique
        identifier, of the material or substance, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of any impurity, stabilizing
        solvent or stabilizing additive in the material or substance
        that is known to the employer, that, in accordance with the
        provisions of the [Hazardous Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3), is
        classified in a category or subcategory of a health hazard class
        and that contributes to the classification, in accordance with
        those provisions, of the material or substance in the health
        hazard class,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an ingredient in a mixture that
      is a hazardous product,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name of the ingredient,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the CAS registry number, or any other unique
        identifier, of the ingredient, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the concentration or concentration range of the
        ingredient,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a material, substance or mixture
      that is a hazardous product, the name of any toxicological study
      that identifies the material or substance or any ingredient in the
      mixture,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the product identifier of a hazardous product,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} information in respect of a hazardous product,
      other than the product identifier, that constitutes a means of
      identification, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} information that could be used to identify a
      supplier of a hazardous product; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the following in respect of the hazardous product
    that is the subject of the claim:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its product identifier,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the claim is a refiled claim, the registry
      number of the preceding claim filed in respect of that hazardous
      product,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the claim relates to a material or
      substance, or an ingredient, an impurity, a stabilizing additive
      or a stabilizing solvent of the hazardous product, the generic
      chemical name of the material, substance, ingredient, impurity,
      stabilizing additive or stabilizing solvent, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the chemical name and, if any, the CAS registry
      number and any other unique identifier of all materials,
      substances, ingredients, impurities, stabilizing additives and
      stabilizing solvents in the hazardous product and their
      concentrations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A claim for exemption submitted under subsection (1)
  must not contain any false or misleading information.

## [Filing a Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913446 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A claim for exemption must
be filed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by sending it by registered mail to the office
  responsible for the administration of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in person by the claimant or the claimant's agent by
  delivering it to the office responsible for the administration of the
  Act; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by transmitting it by any electronic means, including
  facsimile or electronic mail, to the office responsible for the
  administration of the Act, if it has the necessary facilities for
  accepting the secure transmission of the claim by that means.

## [Assigning Registry Numbers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913453 .Part}

a registry number to a claim for exemption as soon as possible after

  section 8;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the safety data sheet or label to which the claim for
  exemption relates; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the required fee.
