## Order Transferring from the Minister of Social Development to the Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development the Powers, Duties and Functions Relating to the Canada Millennium Scholarships Foundation {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-social-development-to-the-minister-of-human-resources-and-skills-development-the-powers-duties-and-functions-relating-to-the-canada-millennium-scholarships-foundation .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-204
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Minister of Social Development to the
Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development the Powers, Duties
and Functions Relating to the Canada Millennium Scholarships Foundation

P.C. 2003-2039  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Minister of Human Resources Development to be styled
Minister of Social Development to the Minister of State to be styled
Minister of Human Resources and Skills Development the powers, duties
and functions relating to the Canada Millenium Scholarship Foundation
conferred upon the Minister of Human Resources Development under Part 1
of the Budget Implementation Act 1998[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EBBAA}, effective December 12,
2003.
