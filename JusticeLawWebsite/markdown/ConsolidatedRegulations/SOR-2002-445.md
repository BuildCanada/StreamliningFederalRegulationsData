## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698610 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [International Boundary Waters
    Treaty Act](/eng/acts/I-17). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-commercial project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-commercial project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a project
    involving the removal of boundary waters in bulk in which no one is
    required to pay for the waters. ([projet non
    commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[removal of boundary waters in
  bulk]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the removal of water from boundary
  waters and taking the water, whether it has been treated or not,
  outside the water basin in which the boundary waters are located

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by any means of diversion, including by pipeline,
    canal, tunnel, aqueduct or channel; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by any other means by which more than 50,000 L of
    boundary waters are taken outside the water basin per day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The removal of boundary waters in bulk does not
  include taking a manufactured product that contains water, including
  water and other beverages in bottles or packages, outside a water
  basin.

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698619 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence or for the renewal or amendment of a licence must be submitted
to the Minister and contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the intended project;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the place where the project is to be
  located and the waters it will likely affect;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the project involves the use, obstruction or
  diversion of boundary waters and the project requires a licence under
  subsection 11(1) of the Act, a description of the increase or decrease
  in the natural flow or level of the boundary waters on the other side
  of the international boundary that would be caused by the project;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the project consists of the construction or
  maintenance of any remedial or protective work, dam or other
  obstruction in waters flowing from boundary waters, or in downstream
  waters of rivers flowing across the international boundary and the
  project requires a licence under subsection 12(1) of the Act, a
  description of the increase in the natural level of waters on the
  other side of the international boundary that would be caused by the
  project;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a concise economic analysis of the direct and
  indirect benefits and costs of the project;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a concise environmental analysis of the project that
  includes a description of the measures to be taken to protect the
  integrity of the ecosystem in which the project is located;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any permit or other authorization issued in
  respect of the project; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other details concerning the project that
  indicate how the project would be compatible with the management of
  the resources, environment and economy of Canada.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The licence must include
the conditions to which the licence is subject.

## [Water Removal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698631 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 13(1) of the Act
applies only in respect of the Canadian portion of the following water
basins:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Great Lakes --- St. Lawrence Basin, which is the area
  of land from which water drains into the Great Lakes or the St.
  Lawrence River;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Hudson Bay Basin, which is the area of land from
  which water drains into Hudson Bay; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Saint John --- St. Croix Basin, which is the area of
  land from which water drains into the St. John River or the St. Croix
  River.

<!-- -->

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 13(1) of the Act does not
  apply to the removal of boundary waters other than the removal of
  boundary waters in bulk.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), subsection 13(1) of the Act
  does not apply to boundary waters used in a conveyance, including a
  vessel, aircraft or train,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as ballast;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the operation of the conveyance; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for people, animals or goods on or in the
    conveyance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), subsection 13(1) of the Act
  does not apply to boundary waters used in a non-commercial project on
  a short term basis for firefighting or humanitarian purposes.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-698643 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 9, 2002.
