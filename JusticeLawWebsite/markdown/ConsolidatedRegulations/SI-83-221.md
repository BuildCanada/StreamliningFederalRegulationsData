## Air Transportation Tax Remission Order {#air-transportation-tax-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/83-221
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1983-12-14

Order Respecting the Remission of the Air Transportation Tax Imposed
Under the Excise Tax Act on Transportation of a Person by Air

P.C. 1983-3776 1983-11-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, the Minister of Finance, the Minister
of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of the air
transportation tax imposed under the](/eng/regulations/SI-83-221)
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on transportation of a person by air.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639294 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Air Transportation Tax Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-221).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639296 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[technical landing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical landing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a landing of an
    aircraft made solely to obtain ground services required for the
    aircraft; ([escale technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer stop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer stop]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a stop at an airport by
    an aircraft for which the passenger deplanes solely for the purpose
    of emplaning on a connecting flight. ([escale de
    correspondance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639300 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the air transportation tax paid or payable under subsections 10(1)
and 11(1) of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) for transportation of
a person by air where such transportation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} begins at a point in the taxation area outside Canada
  and ends at a point in the taxation area outside Canada, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} begins at a point in the taxation area outside Canada
  and ends at a point outside the taxation area,

and does not include an intermediate stop at a point in Canada other
than a transfer stop or a technical landing.
