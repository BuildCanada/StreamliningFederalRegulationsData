## Order Transferring to the Department of External Affairs from the Department of the Solicitor General, the Control and Supervision of the Passport Office {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-external-affairs-from-the-department-of-the-solicitor-general-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-passport-office .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-203
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-11-17

Order Transferring to the Department of External Affairs from the
Department of the Solicitor General, the Control and Supervision of the
Passport Office

P.C. 1993-1903  1993-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service Rearrangement
and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), the control and supervision
of the Passport Office was transferred from the Department of External
Affairs to the Department of the Solicitor General by Order in Council
P.C. 1993-1485 of June 25, 1993[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-203_e_hq_6538){#fn_SI-93-203_e_hq_6538-ID0EBBBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-203_e_hq_6538-ID0EBBBA){#fn_SI-93-203_e_hq_6538}SI/93-139,
  1993 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3231
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the
[Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased hereby to transfer to the Department of
External Affairs the control and supervision of that portion of the
public service in the Department of the Solicitor General known as the
Passport Office.
