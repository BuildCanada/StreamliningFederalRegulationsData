## Government of Jamaica Remission Order {#government-of-jamaica-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2001-93
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2001-09-12

Government of Jamaica Remission Order

P.C. 2001-1519  2001-08-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2001-93_e_hq_6425){#fn_SI-2001-93_e_hq_6425-ID0EDBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby remits to
the Government of Jamaica the amount of \$0.2 million, representing
accrued interest owing to the Government of Canada on a loan made to the
Government of Jamaica.
