## Interest and Administrative Charges Regulations {#interest-and-administrative-charges-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-188
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1996-04-01

Interest and Administrative Charges Regulations

T.B. 823947 1996-03-28
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 155.1(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-96-188_e_hq_6219){#fn_SOR-96-188_e_hq_6219-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed Regulations respecting interest on overdue accounts and
administrative charges for dishonoured instruments, effective April 1,
1996.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979832 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[average bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[average bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the weighted
    arithmetic average of the bank rates that are established during the
    month before the month in respect of which interest is being
    calculated; ([taux d'escompte moyen]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rate of interest
    established periodically by the Bank of Canada as the minimum rate
    at which the Bank of Canada makes short term advances to members of
    the Canadian Payments Association; ([taux
    d'escompte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[concessionary loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[concessionary loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan bearing no
    interest or a rate of interest that is three or more percentage
    points below the average cost of borrowing of the Government of
    Canada on the day the loan agreement is entered into; ([prêt assorti
    de conditions privilégiées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[demand for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[demand for payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a demand for
    payment in writing and includes an invoice, bill, statement and
    assessment; ([demande de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[due date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[due date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in relation to an amount
    owing to Her Majesty

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the provision of goods or services or the use
      of facilities,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the due date specified in a demand for payment
        for, or included in the terms and conditions of, the provision
        of the goods or services or the use of the facilities, as the
        case may be, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where no date has been specified or included
        as indicated in subparagraph (i), the day that is 30 days after
        the day on which a demand for payment is issued,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a repayable contribution,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} every day on which a scheduled repayment is to
        be made in accordance with the terms and conditions of the
        contribution, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where no repayment schedule for the
        contribution has been specified, the day that is 30 days after
        the day on which a demand for payment is issued,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the recovery of an overpayment or
      erroneous payment,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} every day on which a scheduled repayment is to
        be made, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where no repayment schedule has been arranged,
        the day that is 30 days after the day on which a demand for
        payment is issued,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} with respect to a licence, lease, loan, including
      a hypothecary loan, or a mortgage, every day on which a payment is
      to be made in accordance with the licence, lease, loan or mortgage
      agreement,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} with respect to an accountable advance, the day
      on which repayment of any balance owing to Her Majesty is to be
      made in accordance with the [Accountable Advances
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-438), and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for any other reason,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any day on which payment is to be made in
        accordance with the applicable Act of Parliament, regulation,
        order, contract or arrangement, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where a day referred to in subparagraph (i)
        cannot be determined, the day that is 30 days after the day on
        which a demand for payment is issued; ([date
        d'échéance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity,
    incorporated, continued or formed by or under an Act of Parliament
    or the legislature of a province or the laws of a foreign state or
    political subdivision of that state, that holds deposits and honours
    cheques and other payment instructions on behalf of its clients and
    includes a bank, a trust company and a cooperative credit
    society; ([institution financière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[in writing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes in any electronic form
    that is capable of being reproduced in intelligible, written form
    within a reasonable time; ([par écrit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pre-authorized debit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pre-authorized debit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a debit to a
    debtor's credit card account or other account with a financial
    institution that is initiated by the creditor, in accordance with a
    prior authorization given by the debtor to the
    creditor. ([prélèvement automatique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979859 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts owed to a Crown corporation; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts owing by a department.

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979863 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979864 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} capital market transactions and, except as provided
  in section 7, interest-bearing loans, including hypothecary loans, or
  mortgages;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} amounts owing to Her Majesty pursuant to the [Income
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), or any Act of Parliament that imposes a tax
  or duty; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} amounts referred to in any other Act of Parliament or
  regulation, or any order, contract or arrangement, that provides for
  the charging of interest on those amounts.

### [Rate of Interest]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979869 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 155.1(1) of the Act and subject to sections 6 to
  8, in all cases where an amount is owing to Her Majesty, interest
  calculated and compounded monthly at the average bank rate plus 3% is
  payable on that amount and accrues during the period beginning on the
  due date and ending on the day before the day on which payment is
  received by Her Majesty or an authorized agent of Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a partial payment is made, the period for which
  interest is payable in respect of the amount paid ends on the day
  before the day on which the partial payment is received by Her Majesty
  or an authorized agent of Her Majesty.

- SOR/99-30, s. 2
- SOR/2018-89, s. 7

### [Overpayments and Erroneous Payments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979875 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person has received an overpayment or erroneous payment, interest is
  payable by the person on any amount that is not repaid by its due date
  and accrues as specified in section 5, during the period beginning on
  the due date and ending on the day before the day on which repayment
  is received by Her Majesty or an authorized agent of Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person has received an overpayment or erroneous
  payment as a result of fraud, falsification of documents, wilful
  misrepresentation or any other offence committed by that person or by
  any other person acting on behalf of that person, unless provided
  otherwise by an order of a court, interest is payable by that person
  on the amount owing on account of the overpayment or erroneous payment
  and accrues as specified in section 5, during the period beginning on
  the day on which the overpayment or erroneous payment is made and
  ending on the day before the day on which repayment is received by Her
  Majesty or an authorized agent of Her Majesty.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in subsection (1) shall prevent a deputy head
  from entering into an arrangement with a person that calls for
  repayment to be made in instalments and interest to accrue and be paid
  on the total amount outstanding.

- SOR/99-30, s. 3

### [Concessionary Loans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979882 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in the case of a late or missed payment on a
  concessionary loan, interest is payable on the amount of the late or
  missed payment and accrues during the period beginning on the due date
  and ending on the day before the day on which payment is received by
  Her Majesty or an authorized agent of Her Majesty, at a rate equal to
  the difference between the rate set out in section 5 and the
  concessionary rate set out in the loan agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} concessionary loans that are made to sovereign
    states and to which a multilateral debt relief agreement or
    undertaking to which Canada is a party applies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} concessionary loans that are made to international
    organizations or sovereign states for the purpose of developmental
    assistance.

- SOR/99-30, s. 4
- SOR/2018-89, s. 7

### [Exceptions for Small Amounts, Errors and Breakdowns]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979890 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
provision of this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no interest is payable to the extent that the
  interest accrued as a result of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any error made or delay caused by Her Majesty, or
    an authorized agent of Her Majesty, in processing a payment or
    establishing an amount payable, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a breakdown or other failure in the system or
    communication links customarily used by the Government of Canada to
    process payments; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no interest is payable on an outstanding amount due
  for a period in respect of a program or service if the total amount
  payable for the period, including accrued interest, is less than the
  minimum amount for which a demand for payment would normally be
  issued, as established by the deputy head responsible for the program
  or service and published for the purpose of this paragraph on a
  Government of Canada website.

- SOR/2018-89, s. 5

### [Waiver]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979898 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister of a department or any public officer authorized
  in writing by that Minister may waive or reduce interest provided for
  by this Part where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the administrative costs of assessing, billing and
    collecting the interest would exceed the amount of interest owing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the interest is in respect of an amount in dispute
    that has been settled in whole, or in part, in favour of the debtor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an overpayment or erroneous payment of salary,
    wages or recurring benefits or allowances is to be recovered from a
    subsequent payment of salary, wages or recurring benefits or
    allowances, as the case may be, except in the circumstances
    described in subsection 6(2);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interest is payable under subsection 6(2) and a
    fine or penalty that takes into account interest on the overpayment
    or erroneous payment has been imposed; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the interest is payable for a period during which
    there exists an urgent and critical situation of a temporary nature
    that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is so serious as to be a national or provincial
      emergency,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} causes substantial financial hardship,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is caused by an actual or imminent fire, flood,
      drought, storm, earthquake or other natural phenomenon, a disease
      in human beings, animals or plants, an accident, pollution or an
      act of sabotage or terrorism, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} results, or may result, in danger to the lives,
      health or safety of individuals, danger to property, social
      disruption or a breakdown in the flow of essential goods, services
      or resources.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the appropriate Minister
  of a department or any public officer authorized in writing by that
  Minister may waive or reduce interest provided for by this Part where
  the debtor was prevented from making a payment by circumstances beyond
  the debtor's control, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} death or incapacity of the debtor or of the person
    who is directly responsible for making the payment on behalf of the
    debtor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a postal disruption that can reasonably be
    considered to have delayed the payment or made it impossible, where
    no alternative means of making payment that would not cause undue
    inconvenience to the debtor are available; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a delay in the processing of a pre-authorized debit
    that is caused by a breakdown or other failure in the external
    systems or communication links customarily used to process such
    debits.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When contemplating a waiver or reduction of interest
  under subsection (2), the appropriate Minister or the public officer
  shall take into consideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the case presented by the debtor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the debtor's payment history and history of
    voluntary compliance; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the debtor's diligence in conducting the debtor's
    affairs in relation to the debt.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Administrative Charges for Dishonoured Instruments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979913 .Part}

### [Charges]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979914 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an instrument tendered in payment or settlement of an amount due to
  Her Majesty is, for any reason, dishonoured, an administrative charge
  of \$15 is payable by the debtor to Her Majesty.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a payment is made by Her Majesty to a financial
  institution in order to reimburse that institution for an amount
  initially credited to the Receiver General on the basis of a
  dishonoured instrument, an administrative charge of \$10 is payable by
  the debtor to Her Majesty, in addition to the charge referred to in
  subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a financial institution charges an amount to
  Her Majesty for monitoring the account of a debtor who has tendered a
  dishonoured instrument and for subsequently certifying or clearing
  that dishonoured instrument, an administrative charge in that amount
  is payable by the debtor to Her Majesty, in addition to the charge
  referred to in subsection (1).

### [Exception for Errors and Breakdowns]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979919 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section
10, no administrative charge becomes payable as a result of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any error made or delay caused by Her Majesty, or an
  authorized agent of Her Majesty, in processing a payment or an
  instrument used to make a payment; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a breakdown or other failure in the system or
  communication links customarily used by the Government of Canada to
  process payments or instruments used to make payments.

### [Waiver]{.HTitleText2} {#h-979925 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  appropriate Minister of a department or any public officer authorized
  in writing by that Minister may waive an administrative charge where
  the administrative costs of assessing, billing and collecting the
  administrative charge would exceed the amount of the administrative
  charge.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate Minister of a department or any
  public officer authorized in writing by that Minister may waive or
  reduce an administrative charge if the administrative charge is
  payable for a period during which there exists an urgent and critical
  situation of a temporary nature that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is so serious as to be a national or provincial
    emergency;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} causes substantial financial hardship;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is caused by an actual or imminent fire, flood,
    drought, storm, earthquake or other natural phenomenon, a disease in
    human beings, animals or plants, an accident, pollution or an act of
    sabotage or terrorism; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} results, or may result, in danger to the lives,
    health or safety of individuals, danger to property, social
    disruption or a breakdown in the flow of essential goods, services
    or resources.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the appropriate Minister
  of a department or any public officer authorized in writing by that
  Minister may waive or reduce an administrative charge where
  circumstances beyond the debtor's control, including an error made by
  the financial institution or other institution on which the instrument
  tendered in payment by the debtor is drawn, resulted in the instrument
  being dishonoured.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} When contemplating a waiver or reduction of an
  administrative charge under subsection (2), the appropriate Minister
  or the public officer shall take into consideration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the case presented by the debtor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the debtor's diligence in taking action to remedy
    the situation.

## [PART III]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-979932 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Administrative charges
payable to Her Majesty under Part II are in addition to any interest
payable to Her Majesty under Part I or any other Act of Parliament or
regulation, or any order, contract or arrangement.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Where an instrument is
tendered in payment, in whole or in part, of an amount owing to Her
Majesty, including any interest payable under Part I, and that
instrument is subsequently dishonoured, the interest continues to accrue
without taking into account the purported payment.
