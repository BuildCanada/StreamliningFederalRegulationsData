Specifications Relating to the Design, Composition, Construction and
Performance of Metering Assemblies Used to Deliver Liquid Propane to
Motor Vehicles and Relating to the Installation and Use Thereof

10(1)(i) of the [Weights and Measures Act](/eng/acts/W-6) and sections

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643332 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Specifications may be
cited as the [Propane Dispenser
Specifications](/eng/regulations/SI-91-110).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643334 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Specifications,

  [[ATC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ATC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means automatic temperature
      compensator; ([CAT]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[propane dispenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[propane dispenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a metering
      assembly that consists of a meter and its accessories and that is
      designed to measure in the liquid state propane delivered to the
      fuel tanks of motor vehicles; ([distributeur de
      propane]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[supply tank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[supply tank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tank that is connected
      to a propane dispenser and that provides propane for delivery to
      the fuel tanks of motor vehicles. ([réservoir
      d\'alimentation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions used in these
  Specifications have the same meaning as in the [Weights and Measures
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1605).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643341 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Specifications apply
in respect of all propane dispensers for use in trade.

## [Design, Composition and Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643343 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A propane dispenser shall
be equipped with an electronic ATC.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a propane dispenser
has a vapour separator with a vapour release valve of the float-operated
type, the vapour release valve shall have a continuous bleed or other
means to ensure satisfactory operation at differential pressures up to
700 kPa.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  temperature sensor of the electronic ATC and the test thermal well of
  a propane dispenser shall be installed adjacent to each other

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the meter chamber;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the vapour separator housing; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the inlet or outlet piping to the meter.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A temperature sensor referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be installed so that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sensing end of the sensor is in the main flow
    of liquid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sensor is located no more than 0.5 m from the
    meter as measured along the piping; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no valves, pumps, flow-control valves or other
    equipment that may alter the temperature of the liquid are located
    between the meter chamber and the sensor.

## [Installation and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-643355 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When a propane dispenser is
installed, a 1 3/4 inch Acme thread male fitting shall be installed on
the supply tank or its piping to allow the propane to be returned to the
supply tank during an inspection carried out by an inspector.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where the fitting required
by section 7 is located more than 30 m (100 feet) from the propane
dispenser, the owner of the propane dispenser shall provide means to
allow propane to be circulated through the propane dispenser to ensure
temperature stabilization of the meter prior to and during the tests
carried out by an inspector,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by installing a vapour return line that includes a 1
  3/4 inch Acme thread male fitting installed in the propane dispenser
  and easily accessible to the inspector to return liquid propane to the
  supply tank; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by providing the inspector with a hose that is fitted
  with a 1 3/4 inch Acme thread male fitting, that has an appropriate
  diameter and that is of sufficient length to reach the fitting
  required by section 7.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The static pressure of a
supply tank, the diameter and configuration of the inlet piping to the
pump supplying the propane dispenser and the capacity of that pump shall
be such as to ensure the proper operating pressure and prevent any
cavitation under normal conditions of use of the propane dispenser.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The vapour return line
that connects the vapour separator of a propane dispenser to a supply
tank shall be of a sufficient diameter to permit the venting of all the
vapour in the propane dispenser under normal conditions of use of the
propane dispenser.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a
vapour return line connected from a propane dispenser or supply tank to
the fuel tank of a motor vehicle during a propane delivery transaction.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No pressure relief valve
shall be installed on the downstream side of a meter in a propane
dispenser other than a hydrostatic pressure relief valve required for
the relief of excessive pressure caused by the thermal expansion of
propane.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any propane dispenser
submitted for an initial inspection pursuant to paragraph 8(b) of the
[Weights and Measures Act](/eng/acts/W-6) after the day on which these
Specifications come into force shall not be certified as meeting the
requirements of these Specifications unless the propane dispenser
incorporates an electronic ATC.
