## Railway Employee Qualification Standards Regulations {#railway-employee-qualification-standards-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-150
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1987-03-16

Regulations Respecting the Minimum Qualification Standards for
Locomotive Engineers, Transfer Hostlers, Conductors and Yard Foremen
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Canadian Transport Commission, pursuant to section 46 of the
National Transportation Act and section 227 of the Railway Act, hereby
makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the minimum qualification
standards for locomotive engineers, transfer hostlers, conductors and
yard foremen](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-150).

Hull, Quebec, in the National Capital Region, March 12, 1987
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900803 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Railway Employee Qualification Standards
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-150).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900805 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[candidate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[candidate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who is required
    to undergo examination and on-job training in accordance with these
    Regulations in order to be qualified for an occupational category,
    but does not include an employee who undergoes re-examination;
    ([candidat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document issued by a
    railway company pursuant to subsection 13(2) and identifying the
    employee to whom it has been issued and the occupational category
    for which the employee is qualified; ([certificat]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[classroom training instructor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[classroom training instructor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    qualified under these Regulations to give classroom instruction;
    ([moniteur de formation théorique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transport
    Commission; ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Railway Transport
    Committee of the Commission; ([Comité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[examiner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[examiner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee or officer of a
    railway company who is qualified under these Regulations to examine
    employees; ([examinateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[occupational category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[occupational category]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the occupational
    category of locomotive engineer, transfer hostler, conductor or yard
    foreman; ([catégorie d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[on-job training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on-job training]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means instruction provided
    to an employee by a railway company in the work environment
    appropriate to the employee's occupational category; ([formation en
    cours d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[on-job training instructor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[on-job training instructor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee
    or officer of a railway company who is qualified under these
    Regulations to instruct employees; ([moniteur de formation en cours
    d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[required subject]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[required subject]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    occupational category, means a subject required by subsection 14(1)
    in order to qualify for the category; ([sujet
    requis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer hostler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer hostler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who is
    assigned to move motive power units over yard tracks and main tracks
    between the locations where the units are maintained and repaired
    and the locations where the units are put into or removed from train
    or yard services. ([mécanicien de manoeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900818 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to all railway companies
  operating in Canada under the jurisdiction of the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The requirements of these Regulations, except in so
  far as they involve the subject of Regulations No. 0-8, Uniform Code
  of Operating Rules, do not apply in respect of a railroad crew that
  originates from a home terminal in the United States and enters Canada
  to reach a terminal or turnaround point that is within 24 km
  (15 miles) of the boundary between Canada and the United States.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900822 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A railway company shall
provide employee training necessary for the purposes of these
Regulations.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  railway company shall permit any employee to work as a locomotive
  engineer, transfer hostler, conductor, or yard foreman unless the
  employee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has qualified for that occupational category in
    accordance with section 14; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a locomotive engineer or transfer
    hostler, has received a passing mark for on-job training in that
    occupational category.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) comes into force on July 1, 1990.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A railway company shall
provide to its locomotive engineer candidates and transfer hostler
candidates sufficient on-job training in respect of the required
subjects to enable them to demonstrate to instructors and examiners that
they are competent to perform their required duties.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No examiner shall issue a
passing mark for on-job training to a locomotive engineer candidate or
transfer hostler candidate unless the examiner

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is satisfied that the candidate is competent to
  perform his required duties by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} obtaining an evaluation of the candidate's
    competency from the locomotive engineer or transfer hostler with
    whom the candidate has made student on-job training trips, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assessing the candidate's competency in actual
    locomotive or train operation, or both, depending on the
    requirements of the occupational category for which the candidate is
    being examined; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has completed, signed and placed on the candidate's
  personnel file a document indicating that the candidate has passed the
  on-job training.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An examiner shall determine
the overall mark for a candidate based on written or oral classroom
examinations, or both, dealing with the required subjects.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An employee undergoing
on-job training in order to qualify as a locomotive engineer or transfer
hostler may perform the duties of the occupational category for which he
is a candidate under the direction of an on-job training instructor for
the duration of the employee's training period.

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company shall, at intervals of not more than three years, have
  each employee in an occupational category re-examined on the required
  subjects.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The overall pass mark for re-examination is 80 per
  cent.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An employee who is
transferred from one occupational category to another shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have qualified in accordance with section 14 for the
  occupational category to which he is transferred; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transfer to the occupational
  category of locomotive engineer or transfer hostler, have received
  within the preceding three years a passing mark for on-job training in
  the required subjects for the occupational category to which he is
  transferred.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  90 days after the coming into force of these Regulations, a railway
  company shall file with the Committee two copies of each type of
  classroom examination and two copies of a detailed description of each
  method of assessing on-job competence used by the company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A railway company shall notify the Committee of a
  change to a type of classroom examination format or method of
  assessing on-job competence within 90 days after implementing the
  change.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the period of employment of each employee examined in accordance with
  these Regulations, a railway company shall maintain an examination
  record for the employee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A railway company shall issue a certificate in the
  form of a wallet-sized card to each employee who has qualified for the
  employee's occupational category.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employee who receives a certificate shall carry it
  on his person while on duty and shall produce the certificate when
  requested to do so by an officer of the railway company that employs
  him or by an officer of the Commission.

## [Qualification Standards for Candidates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900850 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  subjects required for a person to qualify for an occupational category
  are the subjects listed in those items of the schedule marked with an
  "X" under the heading that corresponds to the occupational category,
  excluding those subjects or portions of subjects dealing with
  equipment that is not used by the railway company that employs the
  person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No railway company shall qualify a person for an
  occupational category unless the person obtains an overall mark of at
  least 80 per cent in the required subjects.

## [Qualification Standards for On-job Training Instructors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900854 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No railway company shall
qualify a person as an on-job training instructor for the occupational
category of locomotive engineer unless the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the qualification requirements for a locomotive
  engineer with an overall mark of at least 90 per cent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} completes not less than two years service as a
  locomotive engineer, including at least three months service in the
  area where the locomotive engineer is to give the on-job training.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** No railway company shall
qualify a person as an on-job training instructor for the occupational
category of transfer hostler unless the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meets the qualification requirements for a transfer
  hostler with an overall mark of at least 90 per cent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} completes not less than one year of service as a
  transfer hostler, including at least three months service in the area
  where the transfer hostler is to give the on-job training.

## [Qualification Standards for Classroom Training Instructors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900861 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** No railway company shall
qualify as a classroom training instructor for a required subject a
person who has not obtained a mark of at least 90 per cent in a written
examination on that subject.

## [Qualification Standards for Examiners]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900863 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** An employee or officer of
a railway company who is an on-job training instructor or a classroom
training instructor is qualified to act an an examiner on the subjects
on which the employee or officer is qualified to give instruction.

## [Training Programs and Consultation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900865 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company shall establish employee training programs for each
  occupational category.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A railway company shall establish and modify its
  employee training programs in consultation with the trade unions
  representing its employees in the occupational categories.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 90 days after the coming into force of these
  Regulations, a railway company shall file with the Committee a
  description of all employee training programs relating to each
  occupational category.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 90 days after any change is made to an
  employee training program required by subsection (1), a railway
  company shall file with the Committee a description of the change.

## [Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900871 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  each calendar year a railway company shall submit to the Committee,
  not later than March 31 of the following year, a comprehensive report
  on its employee training programs.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report referred to in subsection (1) shall specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the total number of employees in each occupational
    category;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the total number of employees who received training
    in each occupational category;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees who received training and
    met the training requirements for each category and the number who
    failed to meet the training requirements; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any new or improved techniques or devices adopted
    in the company's employee training programs.
