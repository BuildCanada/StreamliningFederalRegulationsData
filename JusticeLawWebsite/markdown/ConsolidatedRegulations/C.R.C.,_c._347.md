## Ship Construction Subsidy Regulations {#ship-construction-subsidy-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 347
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 3, 1970

Regulations Respecting Capital Subsidies for the Construction of
Commercial and Fishing Vessels
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550090 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ship Construction Subsidy
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._347).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550092 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of an eligible ship means the
    cost of the eligible ship determined by the Minister pursuant to
    subsection 6(7); ([frais approuvés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[completed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an eligible ship,
    means that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a certificate of registry has been issued for the
      ship pursuant to the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9),

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where required by the [Canada Shipping
      Act](/eng/acts/S-9), a steamship inspection certificate has been
      issued for the ship pursuant to that Act, and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship is a fishing vessel, any
      fishing trials of the ship deemed necessary by the Minister of
      Fisheries and Oceans have been carried out to the satisfaction of
      that Minister; ([terminé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
    declared by the Minister, pursuant to subsection 4(3), to be an
    eligible ship for the purposes of these Regulations; ([navire
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fishing vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship equipped for and
    to be used in the catching or trapping of fish for sale; ([bateau de
    pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeds of disposition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeds of disposition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
    assigned to that phrase in paragraph 13(21)(d) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([produit d\'une
    disposition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipbuilder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen of Canada or a
    company incorporated in Canada that is engaged in Canada in the
    business of building ships in Canada; ([constructeur de
    navires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipowner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipowner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen of Canada, a
    company incorporated in Canada, a Canadian municipal corporation or
    Her Majesty in right of any province of Canada; ([propriétaire de
    navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount of capital subsidy
    payable in respect of an eligible ship pursuant to these
    Regulations; ([subvention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel that is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} customarily engaged in the transportation of
        goods or passengers,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} used in the construction, servicing or
        maintenance of other vessels or marine works, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} used in the exploration or exploitation of
        offshore mineral resources,

      and that is at least of

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} 100 tons gross tonnage if self-propelled,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} 200 tons gross tonnage if not self-propelled,

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} 50 tons gross tonnage if a tug, or

      - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} two tons gross tonnage if a submersible
        capable of operating at depths of at least 1,000 feet, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a fishing vessel over 75 feet in overall length
      in respect of which a certificate of preliminary eligibility has
      been obtained from the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans.
      ([navire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Payment of Subsidy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550117 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  money is voted by Parliament for the purpose of capital subsidies for
  the construction of commercial and fishing vessels, a subsidy may be
  paid for an eligible ship the construction of which did not commence
  before January 1, 1966 and was not the subject of a shipbuilding
  contract entered into before January 1, 1966.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of a subsidy shall be determined in
  accordance with section 6 and may, subject to these Regulations, be
  paid in accordance with an agreement entered into pursuant to section
  4.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  application for a subsidy is made in respect of a vessel, the Minister
  may, subject to any terms or conditions established by the Treasury
  Board and subject to subsection (2), enter into an agreement with the
  shipowner and the shipbuilder thereof or, where the vessel is built by
  the owner for his own use, with the shipowner thereof, providing for
  the payment of a subsidy.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No agreement shall be entered into pursuant to
  subsection (1), until the Minister has

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declared the vessel referred to in the application
    for a subsidy to be an eligible ship pursuant to subsection (3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determined the maximum amount of the approved cost
    of the eligible ship.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister is satisfied that the vessel
  referred to in the application for a subsidy is intended for use in a
  commercial enterprise and is to be constructed in Canada by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a shipbuilder for a shipowner, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shipowner for his own use,

  the Minister may declare the vessel to be an eligible ship.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an application for a subsidy shall be in the
  prescribed form and shall be made within 60 days after the day on
  which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contract for the construction of the eligible
    ship is entered into, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the construction of the hull of the eligible ship
    is commenced,

  whichever is the earlier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may extend the time for making an
  application for a subsidy where, in his opinion, the circumstances of
  the case require an extension.

## [Amount of Subsidy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550136 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  subsidy for an eligible ship that is not a fishing vessel shall be an
  amount not exceeding

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 25 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing January 1, 1966 and
    ending May 31, 1969;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 24½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing June 1, 1969
    and ending August 31, 1969;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} 24 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing September 1, 1969 and
    ending November 30, 1969;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} 23½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing December 1,
    1969 and ending February 28, 1970;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} 23 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing March 1, 1970 and
    ending May 31, 1970;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} 22½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing June 1, 1970
    and ending August 31, 1970;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} 22 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing September 1, 1970 and
    ending November 30, 1970;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} 21½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing December 1,
    1970 and ending February 28, 1971;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 21 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing March 1, 1971 and
    ending May 31, 1971;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} 20½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing June 1, 1971
    and ending August 31, 1971;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} 20 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing September 1, 1971 and
    ending November 30, 1971;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} 19½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing December 1,
    1971 and ending February 29, 1972;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} 19 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing March 1, 1972 and
    ending May 31, 1972;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} 18½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing June 1, 1972
    and ending August 31, 1972;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} 18 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed during the period commencing September 1, 1972 and
    ending November 30, 1972;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} 17½ per cent of the approved cost where the
    eligible ship is completed during the period commencing December 1,
    1972 and ending February 28, 1973; and

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} 17 per cent of the approved cost where the eligible
    ship is completed after February 28, 1973.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The subsidy for an eligible ship that is a fishing
  vessel shall be an amount not exceeding 35 per cent of the approved
  cost.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where, owing to causes beyond the control of the
  shipowner and shipbuilder, an eligible ship is completed later than
  the date on which it might reasonably be expected to have been
  completed, the Minister may, in his discretion, declare the ship to
  have been completed on a day earlier than the day on which it was
  completed and the amount of the subsidy therefor shall be determined
  in accordance with that declaration.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (5) and (6), where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the whole or any part of the
    proceeds of disposition of any vessel described in subsection 13(15)
    of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) is used by a person for
    replacement in accordance with subparagraph (a)(i) of that
    subsection, the approved cost of the replacement shall, if the
    replacement is an eligible ship in respect of which a subsidy is to
    be paid under these Regulations, be reduced by an amount equal to
    the amount used for replacement; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel is acquired as a replacement under such
    circumstances that the Minister may pay out all or any part of a
    deposit under subsection 13(19) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), the approved cost of such replacement shall,
    if the replacement is an eligible ship in respect of which a subsidy
    is to be paid under these Regulations, be reduced by the amount that
    bears the same relationship to the proceeds of disposition of the
    vessel disposed of as the amount that may be paid out by the
    Minister under subsection 13(19) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) bears to the amount of the deposit described
    in that subsection.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply where a disposition
  referred to therein

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was by way of a hire purchase agreement entered
    into prior to May 12, 1961; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} was of a vessel that was an eligible ship in
    respect of which a subsidy under the Ship Construction Assistance
    Regulations has not been paid, and was made by way of a hire
    purchase agreement entered into between May 12, 1961 and August 8,
    1962, both dates inclusive.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply where the disposition
  referred to therein was of a vessel that was an eligible ship in
  respect of which a subsidy was not claimed, was not paid by reason of
  the use of the proceeds of a disposition described in paragraph
  (5)(b), or was reduced to that proportion of the proceeds of
  disposition of the eligible vessel that the proceeds of disposition
  described in paragraph 5(b) that were used in the eligible vessel in a
  manner satisfactory to the Canadian Transport Commission under
  paragraph (1)(a) or paragraph 4(1)(c) of the Canadian Vessel
  Construction Assistance Act chapter 43 of the Revised Statutes of
  Canada, 1952, are of the cost of the eligible vessel.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (8), the Minister shall before
  paying

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a subsidy, where the subsidy is payable in a lump
    sum, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the balance of a subsidy, where the subsidy is
    payable by progress payments,

  determine the cost of the eligible ship in respect of which the
  payment is to be made for the purpose of calculating the subsidy or
  balance of subsidy, as the case may be.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The cost of an eligible ship determined pursuant to
  subsection (7) shall not exceed the maximum amount of the approved
  cost determined by the Minister pursuant to paragraph 4(2)(b).

## [Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550170 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every agreement entered
into pursuant to these Regulations shall provide for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} payment of the subsidy by a lump sum or by progress
  payments not exceeding in the aggregate 80 per cent of the subsidy
  before the eligible ship is completed and the balance thereafter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the keeping of accurate and complete accounts of
  expenditures and records of related matters by the shipowner and the
  shipbuilder and the audit of the accounts and the inspection of the
  records by persons authorized by the Minister for that purpose at any
  time before the second anniversary of the final payment of the
  subsidy;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking by the shipowner

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that for at least the five-year period immediately
    following the day the eligible ship is completed

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the eligible ship will be owned by a shipowner
      and kept in Canadian registry, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where the eligible ship is a fishing vessel, the
      eligible ship

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} will be kept actively engaged in fishing
        operations to the satisfaction of the Minister of Fisheries for
        Canada, and

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} will not be sold or chartered without the
        prior consent in writing of the Minister of Fisheries and
        Oceans,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the event of a failure to carry out the
    undertaking contained in clause (i)(A), to pay to Her Majesty, on
    demand, an amount equal to that proportion of the subsidy paid that
    the period during which the undertaking is not carried out is of the
    five-year period, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the event of a failure to carry out either of
    the undertakings contained in subclauses (i)(B)(I) and (II), to pay
    to Her Majesty, on demand, an amount equal to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} that proportion of the subsidy paid, less the
      amount of subsidy that would have been payable under subsection
      6(1) had the eligible ship not been a fishing vessel, that the
      period during which the undertaking is not carried out is of the
      five-year period, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} such lesser amount as the Minister may fix; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} such other matters as the Minister considers
  necessary or appropriate to protect the interests of Her Majesty or to
  carry out the purposes and provisions of these Regulations.

## [Ship Construction Assistance Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550185 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  assistance may be paid under the Ship Construction Assistance
  Regulations in respect of the construction of an eligible ship for
  which a subsidy may be paid under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided in subsection (1),
  nothing in these Regulations affects the payment of assistance under
  the Ship Construction Assistance Regulations.

## [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550189 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, for the
purposes of these Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorize persons to sign agreements for him;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} investigate the financial responsibility of a
  shipowner or shipbuilder and such other matters as he considers
  necessary to determine questions relating to eligibility or approved
  cost; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prescribe forms and make rules relating to the
  application for and the payment of subsidies.
