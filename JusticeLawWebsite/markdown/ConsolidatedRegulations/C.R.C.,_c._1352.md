## Public Service Pension Adjustment Regulations {#public-service-pension-adjustment-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1352
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE PENSION ADJUSTMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.6)

Regulations Made Pursuant to the Public Service Pension Adjustment Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503966 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Service Pension Adjustment
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1352).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503968 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Pension
    Adjustment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[re-employed recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[re-employed recipient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a recipient who
    holds any office or position or performs any services, the
    remuneration for which is payable out of the Consolidated Revenue
    Fund or by an agent of Her Majesty in right of Canada.
    ([bénéficiaire employé de nouveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503972 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The increase in the pension
payable to a re-employed recipient pursuant to the Act shall be
suspended where, under the enactment or pension plan pursuant to which
the pension is payable, the pension of the recipient is suspended in
whole or in part.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where, under the enactment
or pension plan pursuant to which the pension of a re-employed recipient
is payable, no part of the pension of the recipient is suspended, the
increase in the pension payable to the recipient pursuant to the Act
shall be paid to the extent that it would be payable if the increase
were part of the pension payable to the recipient under the said
enactment or pension plan.
