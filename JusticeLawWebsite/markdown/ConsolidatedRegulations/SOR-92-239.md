## LASMO Drilling Rig Remission Order {#lasmo-drilling-rig-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-239
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1992-04-30

Order Respecting the Remission of a Portion of the Customs Duties on a
Drilling Rig for the Cohasset/Panuke Oil Development Project

P.C. 1992-782  1992-04-30
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 101 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_OICe){#fn_OICe-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of a portion of the
customs duties on a drilling rig for the Cohasset/Panuke Oil Development
Project](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-239).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-941285 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [LASMO Drilling Rig Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-239).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-941287 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, remission is hereby granted of a portion of the customs duties paid
or payable under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the
drilling rig Rowan Gorilla III imported by LASMO Nova Scotia Limited for
use in the Cohasset/Panuke Oil Development Project.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission of the duty
on the rig referred to in section 2 shall be an amount equal to the
difference between:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties on 1/120th of the value for duty
  of the rig calculated in accordance with section 4 of the [Vessel
  Duties Reduction or Removal
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-304)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBBA} and,

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1e-ID0EBBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/90-304, 1990
    [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2246
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the customs duties on 1/120th of the value for duty
  of the rig that would apply if the rig were imported on January 1 of
  each year that it remains in Canadian customs waters.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-941292 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in sections 2 and 3 is granted on condition that a claim for the
remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue no later than two
years following the end of each year the rig remains in Canadian customs
waters.
