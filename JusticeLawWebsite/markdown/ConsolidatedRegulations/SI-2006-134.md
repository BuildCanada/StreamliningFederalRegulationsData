## Order Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans Involved in Chemical Warfare Agent Testing {#order-respecting-ex-gratia-payments-to-veterans-involved-in-chemical-warfare-agent-testing .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-134
:::

Registration 2006-12-13

Order Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans Involved in Chemical
Warfare Agent Testing

P.C. 2006-1350 2006-11-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Defence, hereby makes the annexed [Order
Respecting Ex Gratia Payments to Veterans Involved in Chemical Warfare
Agent Testing](/eng/regulations/SI-2006-134).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612418 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Defence. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a former member of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the naval, army or air forces of Canada or
      similar forces raised in Newfoundland and Labrador; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian Forces. ([ancien
      combattant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612424 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is hereby
authorized, on application under section 4, to make an ex gratia payment
of \$24,000 to a veteran who was a volunteer subject in chemical warfare
agent testing during the period beginning on January 1, 1940 and ending
on December 31, 1974, and who is alive on the day on which the payment
is to be made.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a veteran
who has died, the Minister is authorized, on application under section
4, to make the payment set out in section 2

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran died testate and the distribution of
  the estate or the liquidation of the succession of the veteran is not
  completed, to the executor or liquidator of the succession;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran died testate and the distribution of
  the estate or the liquidation of the succession of the veteran has
  been completed, to the natural person who is entitled under the will
  to the residue of the estate or, if there are two or more such
  persons, to those persons on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis
  of their share of the residue; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the veteran died intestate, to the adult who was
  ordinarily living with the veteran at the time of the veteran's death
  and was primarily responsible for providing care to the veteran
  without remuneration or, if there are two or more such adults, to
  those adults on a [pro rata]{.otherLang lang="la"} basis.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612430 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for an ex
gratia payment made under section 2 or 3 shall be submitted to the
Minister on or before February 28, 2007 by the veteran or by the
applicable person or persons referred to in section 3, shall be in the
form approved by the Minister and shall be supported by any evidence
that he or she considers necessary.

## [Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612432 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  payment under section 2 or 3 consists of a one-time lump sum payment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made by the Minister under this
  Order after March 31, 2007.

## [No Crown Liability]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612436 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A payment made under this
Order does not constitute an admission of liability on the part of the
Crown.

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-612438 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to a veteran in respect of whom an ex gratia payment has been made under
the Chemical Warfare Agent Testing Recognition Program.
