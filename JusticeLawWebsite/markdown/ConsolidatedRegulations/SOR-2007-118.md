## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731650 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an unexpected event that is
    not attributable to a deviation from the standard operating
    procedures or applicable laws and that could adversely affect the
    safety of a transplant recipient or the safety, efficacy or quality
    of cells, tissues or organs. ([accident]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
    Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an undesirable
    response in the recipient to transplanted cells, tissues or organs,
    including the transmission of a disease or disease agent. ([effet
    indésirable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[banked]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[banked]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to cells and tissues,
    means processed cells and tissues that have been determined safe for
    transplantation and that are stored by the source establishment in
    its inventory and available for distribution or
    transplantation. ([mise en banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fundamental biological unit
    of a human organism that is for use in
    transplantation. ([cellule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribute]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include to
    transplant. ([distribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a living or deceased person from
    whom cells, tissues or organs are
    retrieved. ([donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donor assessment record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor assessment record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the donor
    screening, any available donor testing results, information obtained
    from the donor's medical records and a copy of the donor
    consent. ([dossier de l'évaluation du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unique
    numeric or alphanumeric designation that is assigned by the source
    establishment to a donor under section 56 and that associates each
    cell, tissue and organ, or part of one, to that donor. ([code
    d'identification du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donor screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor screening]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an evaluation based on
    the donor's medical and social history and physical examination, the
    results of any diagnostic procedures performed, and, if applicable,
    the autopsy. ([évaluation préliminaire du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor suitability assessment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    evaluation based on the donor screening and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of lymphohematopoietic cells, tissues
      and organs retrieved from live donors and of tissues retrieved
      from deceased donors, all donor testing results; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of fresh skin, islet cells and organs
      retrieved from deceased donors, the donor testing results that are
      necessary at the time of transplantation. ([évaluation de
      l'admissibilité du donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[donor testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[donor testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the laboratory tests and
    measurements done on a donor or donor specimen to determine all of
    the following:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the donor has or ever had a transmissible
      disease or is or ever was infected with a transmissible disease
      agent;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} donor compatibility; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the degree of functionality of the cell, tissue
      or organ that is to be retrieved. ([examen du
      donneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[error]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a deviation from the standard
    operating procedures or applicable laws that could adversely affect
    the safety of a transplant recipient or the safety, efficacy or
    quality of cells, tissues or organs. ([manquement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, a partnership
    or an unincorporated entity, or a part of any of them, that carries
    out any of the following activities in respect of cells, tissues or
    organs:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} importation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} processing;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} distribution; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} transplantation. ([établissement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[exceptional distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exceptional distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    distribution under sections 40 to 42 of cells, tissues or organs
    that have not been processed under these Regulations. ([distribution
    exceptionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exterior label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exterior label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the label that is
    affixed to the exterior package. ([étiquette
    extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exterior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the outermost package
    in which a cell, tissue or organ is delivered, transported or
    shipped. ([emballage extérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[general standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[general standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National Standard of
    Canada CAN/CSA-Z900.1 entitled Cells, Tissues, and Organs for
    Transplantation and Assisted Reproduction: General Requirements, as
    amended from time to time. ([norme générale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[homologous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[homologous]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a cell, tissue or
    organ, means that the cell, tissue or organ performs the same basic
    function after transplantation. ([homologue]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[interior label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interior label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the label that is
    affixed to the interior package. ([étiquette
    intérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interior package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the innermost package
    of a cell, tissue or organ that has a non-sterile
    exterior. ([emballage intérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lymphohematopoietic standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lymphohematopoietic standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National
    Standard of Canada CAN/CSA-Z900.2.5 entitled Lymphohematopoietic
    Cells for Transplantation, as amended from time to time. ([norme sur
    les cellules lymphohématopoïétiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medical director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    establishment, means a physician or dentist who is licensed under
    the laws of the jurisdiction in which the establishment is situated
    to provide health care or dental care and who is responsible for the
    application of the standard operating procedures and for all medical
    or dental procedures carried out there, as the case may
    be. ([directeur médical]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minimally manipulated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minimally manipulated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a structural tissue, that the
      processing does not alter the original characteristics that are
      relevant to its claimed utility for reconstruction, repair or
      replacement; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of cells and nonstructural tissue,
      that the processing does not alter the biological characteristics
      that are relevant to their claimed utility. ([manipulation
      minimale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[ocular standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[ocular standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National Standard of
    Canada CAN/CSA-Z900.2.4 entitled Ocular Tissues for Transplantation,
    as amended from time to time. ([norme sur les tissus
    oculaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[organ]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[organ]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a perfusable human organ for use
    in transplantation, whether whole or in parts, and whose specific
    function is intended to return after revascularization and
    reperfusion. It includes any adjunct vessels that are retrieved with
    the organ for use in organ
    transplantation. ([organe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[organ standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[organ standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National Standard of
    Canada CAN/CSA-Z900.2.3 entitled Perfusable Organs for
    Transplantation, as amended from time to time. ([norme sur les
    organes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[package insert]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package insert]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document that is
    prepared by the source establishment to accompany a cell, tissue or
    organ. ([encart informatif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of cells, tissues and
    organs, means any of the following activities:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} donor screening;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} donor testing;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} donor suitability assessment;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} retrieval, except for organs and islet cells;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} testing and measurements performed on the cells,
      tissues or organs after they are retrieved;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} preparation for use in transplantation, except
      for organs;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} preservation;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} quarantine;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} banking; and

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} packaging and
      labelling. ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quality assurance system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quality assurance system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    co-ordinated activities of an establishment that relate to the
    safety of cells, tissues and organs. It includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the standard operating procedures;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} records to demonstrate that the standard
      operating procedures have been implemented; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} audit processes to verify that the standard
      operating procedures are being implemented. ([système d'assurance
      de la qualité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scientific director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scientific director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    establishment, means an individual who is responsible for the
    application of the standard operating procedures and for all
    technical procedures carried out there. ([directeur
    scientifique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an adverse
    reaction that results in any of the following consequences for the
    recipient:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} their in-patient hospitalization or its
      prolongation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persistent or significant disability or
      incapacity;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} medical, dental or surgical intervention to
      preclude a persistent or significant disability or incapacity;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a life-threatening condition; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} death. ([effet indésirable
      grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[source establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[source establishment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), in the case of an organ
      from a deceased donor, the relevant organ donation organization;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of adjunct vessels that are retrieved
      with an organ and not used immediately in the organ
      transplantation, the relevant tissue bank;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an organ from a living donor or
      lymphohematopoietic cells that are not banked, the relevant
      transplant establishment;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of tissues or banked
      lymphohematopoietic cells, the relevant cell or tissue bank; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of islet cells, the establishment
      that prepares the cells for use in
      transplantation. ([établissement central]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[standard operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[standard operating procedures]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    component of the quality assurance system that comprises
    instructions that set out the processes and procedures to follow in
    carrying out the activities of an establishment. ([procédures
    d'opération normalisées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tissue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tissue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a functional group of human
    cells for use in transplantation. It includes the cells and tissues
    listed in the definition [[tissue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section
    3.1 of the general standard, except for paragraphs (g) and
    (l). ([tissu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tissue standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tissue standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means National Standard of
    Canada CAN/CSA-Z900.2.2 entitled Tissues for Transplantation, as
    amended from time to time. ([norme sur les tissus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[transplant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transplant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to implant cells, tissues
    or organs into a recipient. ([transplantation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731724 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scope of Regulations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to organs and minimally manipulated cells and tissues.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- various therapeutic
products

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations do not apply to any of the following therapeutic products:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cells, tissues and organs that are for
    non-homologous use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cells, tissues and organs that are for autologous
    use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} heart valves and dura mater;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} tissues and cells --- except for islet cells, and
    except for lymphohematopoietic cells that are derived from bone
    marrow, peripheral blood or cord blood --- that have a systemic
    effect and depend on their metabolic activity for their primary
    function;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} medical devices that contain cells or tissues and
    that are the subject of investigational testing involving human
    subjects under Part 3 of the [Medical Devices
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} cells, tissues and organs that are the subject of
    clinical trials under Division 5 of Part C of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} Class IV medical devices that are regulated under
    the [Medical Devices Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-282);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} blood components, blood products and whole blood,
    except for cord blood and peripheral blood for use in
    lymphohematopoietic cell transplantation;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cells and tissues that are regulated under the
    [Assisted Human Reproduction Act](/eng/acts/A-13.4) or any of its
    regulations.

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-192, s. 92\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- regulations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No other regulation made under the Act applies to
  cells, tissues or organs that are the subject of these Regulations.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731742 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transplantation

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 40 to 42, no establishment shall transplant a cell, tissue
  or organ unless it is processed by a registered establishment under
  these Regulations and determined safe for transplantation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation --- cells and tissue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 40 to 42, no establishment shall
  import tissue or a cell, other than a lymphohematopoietic cell, unless
  it is processed by a registered establishment under these Regulations
  and determined safe for transplantation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation --- organs and
  lymphohematopoietic cells

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment may import an organ or
  lymphohematopoietic cells from an establishment that is not
  registered.

## [Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731750 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to register

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  establishment must be registered under these Regulations, except a
  retrieval establishment and, subject to subsection (2), a transplant
  establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A transplant establishment that distributes cells,
  tissues or organs must be registered under these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for registration of an establishment must be made in the
  form established by the Minister, be dated and signed by the medical
  director or scientific director, and contain all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's name and civic address, and its
    postal address if different, and the name and telephone number of a
    person to contact for further information with respect to the
    application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an establishment that previously
    carried out its activities under another name, that other name;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of cells, tissues and
    organs that the establishment processes, distributes or imports;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the types of processing,
    distribution or transplantation activities that the establishment
    carries out or for which it is responsible;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the period during which the establishment has been
    in operation; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a statement dated and signed by the medical
    director or scientific director that certifies that the
    establishment is in compliance with these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information on request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must provide the Minister, on
  written request, with any relevant information necessary to complete
  the application, by the date specified in the Minister's request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration number

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  review of an application for registration, if the Minister determines
  that the information provided in the application is complete, the
  Minister must register the establishment and issue a registration
  number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 9, a registration is valid until
  December 31 in the year after the year in which the registration
  number is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may refuse to
register an establishment if he or she has reason to believe that any of
the information provided by the establishment in its application is
false, misleading, inaccurate or incomplete.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When registration may be cancelled

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may cancel a
registration in the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the application for registration contains false or
  misleading information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister receives a notice under section 13 that
  states that the establishment has ceased an activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the establishment has not complied with a request for
  additional information made under section 14; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reason to believe that the
  establishment is not in compliance with these Regulations or that the
  safety of cells, tissues or organs has been or could be compromised.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Actions before cancellation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must take all of the following actions before cancelling a
  registration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} send a written notice to the establishment that
    sets out the reasons for the proposed cancellation and specifies the
    corrective action, if any, that the establishment must take and the
    time within which it must be taken; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give the establishment an opportunity to be heard
    in writing with respect to the cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment does not carry out the
  corrective action to the Minister's satisfaction, or does not carry it
  out within the required time, the Minister must send a notice of
  cancellation of the registration that includes the reasons for the
  cancellation and the effective date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by establishment when registration
cancelled

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** On the cancellation of
its registration, the establishment must immediately take both of the
following steps:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cease carrying out the activities that were
  authorized by the registration; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the establishments to whom it has distributed
  a cell, tissue or organ or made a donor referral, during the period
  specified in the notice, of the cancellation, the reasons for the
  cancellation and the effective date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation in urgent circumstances

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 10, the Minister may
  cancel a registration immediately if he or she considers it necessary
  to do so in order to prevent injury to the health or safety of the
  public, by giving the establishment a notice of the cancellation in
  writing that states the reasons for the cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request to reconsider

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment may, in writing, request the
  Minister to reconsider the cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Opportunity to be heard

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must, within 45 days after receiving a
  request for reconsideration, provide the establishment with an
  opportunity to be heard in writing with respect to the cancellation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ongoing requirement to notify Minister

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  establishment must notify the Minister in writing of any change in the
  information provided in its application for registration, within 30
  days after the change is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of activity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an establishment ceases to process, distribute or
  import cells, tissues or organs, it must notify the Minister in
  writing of that fact, within 90 days after it ceases that activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be dated and signed by the medical
  director or scientific director and include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's name and civic address, and its
    postal address if different;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the establishment's registration number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the change or cessation became
    effective; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the cessation of an activity, the
    disposition of the cells, tissues and organs in the establishment's
    possession.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
provide the Minister, on written request, with any additional relevant
information to demonstrate that the activities it carries out are in
compliance with these Regulations, by the date specified in the
Minister's request.

## [Source Establishment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responsibility

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A source establishment is
responsible for the processing of cells, tissues and organs, whether the
processing is carried out by the source establishment itself or by
another establishment, and for determining whether the cells, tissues
and organs are safe for transplantation.

## [Processing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731814 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731815 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documented evidence

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
have documented evidence that demonstrates that the activities,
processes and technical procedures that it uses in processing cells,
tissues and organs will consistently lead to the expected results.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When pooling permitted

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** An establishment may only
pool cells, tissues or organs from different donors during processing to
create a therapeutic dose for a single recipient.

### [Donor Suitability Assessment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731820 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- cell, tissue and organ
donors

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** In assessing the
suitability of a donor of cells, tissues or organs, except a donor of
lymphohematopoietic cells, an establishment must perform all of the
following steps:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain the donor information and history in
  accordance with sections 12.2 and 12.3 of the general standard;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine that the donor is not unsuitable to donate
  on the basis of the contraindications or exclusion criteria set out in
  section 13.1.3 of the general standard and in Annex E to that
  standard;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} perform a physical examination of the donor in
  accordance with section 13.2 of the general standard; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} perform appropriate and effective tests for the
  diseases or disease agents specified in section 14.2.6 of the general
  standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plasma dilution algorithm

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** In assessing the
suitability of a donor, an establishment must apply a plasma dilution
algorithm if a donor pre-transfusion or pre-infusion blood sample is
unavailable.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional exclusion criteria --- tissue
donors

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** In assessing the
suitability of a tissue donor, except an ocular tissue donor, an
establishment must perform both of the following steps:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine that the donor is not unsuitable to donate
  on the basis of the contraindications or exclusion criteria set out in
  section 13.1.2 of the tissue standard; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} perform appropriate and effective tests for the
  diseases or disease agents specified in section 14.2.6 of the tissue
  standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional exclusion criteria --- ocular
tissue donors

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In assessing the
suitability of an ocular tissue donor, an establishment must determine
that the donor is not unsuitable to donate on the basis of the
contraindications or exclusion criteria set out in sections 13.1.3 to
13.1.6 of the ocular standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements --- organ and
islet cell donors

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  assessing the suitability of an organ or islet cell donor, an
  establishment must perform all of the following steps:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain the donor information and history in
    accordance with sections 12.2.2.3, 12.2.2.4, 12.2.3.4 and 12.2.3.7
    of the organ standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine that the donor is not unsuitable to
    donate on the basis of the contraindications or exclusion criteria
    set out in section 13.2.2 of the organ standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} perform the tests specified in sections 14.1.2 and
    14.3.2 of the organ standard; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} perform appropriate and effective tests for the
    diseases or disease agents specified in sections 14.2.6.3 and
    14.2.6.6 of the organ standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- imported organs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), in the case of an imported
  organ, the transplant establishment need only have the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} documentation of the donor suitability assessment
    according to the requirements of the jurisdiction where the
    assessment was performed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} documentation that the tests specified in sections
    14.1.2 and 14.3.2 of the organ standard have been performed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} documentation that appropriate and effective tests
    for the diseases or disease agents specified in sections 14.2.6.3
    and 14.2.6.6 of the organ standard have been performed; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of those of the tests for the diseases
    or disease agents specified in section 14.2.6.3 of the organ
    standard that must be performed before transplantation and the blood
    group test for ABO, a copy of the test results.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- lymphohematopoietic
cells

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  assessing the suitability of a donor of lymphohematopoietic cells, an
  establishment must perform all of the following steps:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} obtain the donor information and history in
    accordance with sections 12.2.2.2 and 12.2.2.3 of the
    lymphohematopoietic standard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} perform a physical examination of the donor in
    accordance with section 13.2 of the lymphohematopoietic standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determine that the donor is not unsuitable to
    donate on the basis of the contraindications or exclusion criteria
    set out in section 13.1.3 of the lymphohematopoietic standard;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} perform the tests specified in section 12.2.2.4 of
    the lymphohematopoietic standard; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} perform appropriate and effective tests for the
    diseases or disease agents specified in section 14.2.3 of the
    lymphohematopoietic standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- imported
  lymphohematopoietic cells

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), in the case of imported
  lymphohematopoietic cells, the source establishment must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have documentation of the donor suitability
    assessment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} perform the tests specified in section 12.2.2.4 of
    the lymphohematopoietic standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} perform appropriate and effective tests for the
    diseases or disease agents specified in section 14.2.3 of the
    lymphohematopoietic standard; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} determine that the donor is not unsuitable to
    donate on the basis of the exclusion criteria set out in section
    13.1.3.4 of the lymphohematopoietic standard.

### [Retrieval]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731862 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retrieval interval --- tissues

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
retrieves tissue from a deceased donor must carry out the retrieval
within the scientifically based maximum interval between the cardiac
asystole of the donor and the retrieval of the tissue.

### [Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731865 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensed diagnostic devices

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), [in
  vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic devices that are used by an
  establishment in the testing of donor blood for transmissible disease
  agents or markers under these Regulations must be licensed either

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, if the testing is performed in Canada;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Canada or the United States, if the testing is
    performed outside Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- lymphohematopoietic cells

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of lymphohematopoietic cells that are
  imported into Canada for transplantation into a specific recipient,
  the [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic devices may be
  licensed in Canada or any other jurisdiction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"}
diagnostic devices --- cells and tissues

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of cells and tissues, [in vitro]{.otherLang lang="la"} diagnostic
  devices that are used by an establishment in the testing of donor
  blood for transmissible disease agents or markers under these
  Regulations must be licensed for screening donors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- syphilis

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), [in vitro]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} diagnostic devices that are used in the testing of donor
  blood for syphilis may be licensed either for diagnosis or screening
  donors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bacteriological testing --- tissues

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
retrieves tissue, except ocular tissue, must perform bacteriological
testing in accordance with section 14.3 of the tissue standard, except
for section 14.3.2.8.

### [Packaging and Labelling]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731880 .Subheading}

#### [Packaging]{.HTitleText3} {#h-731881 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaging materials

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
packages cells, tissues or organs must ensure that it uses appropriate
packaging materials that are free from damage and capable of maintaining
the integrity of the cells, tissues and organs.

### [Quarantine]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731934 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quarantine --- cells and tissues

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  source establishment must ensure that cells, except islet cells, and
  tissues are quarantined until all of the following processing
  activities are completed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor is found to be suitable after completion
    of the donor suitability assessment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of fresh skin, bacteriological
    test results are reviewed and found to be acceptable, if applicable;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all processing records are reviewed for
    completeness and compliance with the standard operating procedures.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirement --- live donors
  of tissue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the requirements set out in subsection
  (1), the source establishment must quarantine tissues that are
  retrieved from live donors in accordance with section 17.2 of the
  tissue standard.

## [Storage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731943 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage limits

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes cells, tissues or organs and that stores cells, tissues and
adjunct vessels that were not used at the time of transplantation of the
organ with which they were retrieved must observe scientifically based
maximum storage periods.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage location

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes cells, tissues or organs must store them in a location that
has environmental conditions that maintain their safety and that is
secure against the entry of unauthorized persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Segregation --- tissues

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
stores tissues must ensure that those that are intended for autologous
use are segregated from those intended for allogeneic use.

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that

## [Exceptional Distribution]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731954 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** A source establishment
may distribute cells, tissues or organs that have not been determined
safe for transplantation if all of the following conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a cell, tissue or organ that has been determined safe
  for transplantation is not immediately available;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transplant physician or dentist, based on their
  clinical judgement, authorizes the exceptional distribution; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transplant establishment obtains the informed
  consent of the recipient.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in source establishment's records

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  source establishment that distributes cells, tissues or organs under
  section 40 must keep a copy of the notice of exceptional distribution
  in its records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in transplant establishment's
  records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The transplant establishment must keep a copy of the
  notice of exceptional distribution in its records.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice of exceptional distribution must contain all
  of the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the transplanted cell, tissue or organ;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of these Regulations with which the
    cell, tissue or organ is not in compliance at the time of its
    distribution;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the justification for the distribution that formed
    the basis for the transplant physician's or dentist's decision to
    authorize it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the source establishment that
    distributed the cell, tissue or organ;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the transplant establishment and of the
    transplant physician or dentist who authorized the distribution; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the time and date of the written authorization of
    the distribution and a copy of the authorization signed by the
    transplant physician or dentist.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Follow-up

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** A source establishment
that distributes a cell, tissue or organ under section 40 before the
donor suitability assessment is complete must, after the distribution,
complete the assessment, carry out any other appropriate follow-up
testing and notify the relevant transplant establishment of the results.

## [Error, Accident and Adverse Reaction Investigation and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-731975 .Part}

### [Errors and Accidents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-731976 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required action

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  establishment that is not a source establishment and that has
  reasonable grounds to believe that the safety of a cell, tissue or
  organ that is or was in its possession has been compromised by the
  occurrence of an error or accident during processing must immediately
  take all of the following steps:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the donor identification codes of all
    implicated cells, tissues and organs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any other implicated cells,
    tissues and organs in its possession; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the following establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the relevant source establishment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the cells, tissues or organs were imported,
      the establishment that imported them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- importers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the establishment that receives a notice under
  subsection (1) is the establishment that imported the implicated
  cells, tissues or organs, it only has to notify the source
  establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the establishment's belief that the
    safety of cells, tissues or organs has been compromised;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the safety of the implicated
    cells, tissues or organs may have been compromised, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification codes of all implicated
    cells, tissues and organs; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any suspected transmissible disease or
    disease agent, if known.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the notice is given verbally, a confirmatory
  written notice must be sent as soon as possible afterwards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by source establishment

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  source establishment that has reasonable grounds to believe that the
  safety of cells, tissues or organs for whose processing it is
  responsible has been compromised by the occurrence of an error or
  accident during processing must immediately take all of the following
  actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine any implicated cells, tissues and organs
    in its possession;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send a notice described in subsection (2) to all of
    the following establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the cells, tissues or organs were imported,
      the establishment that imported them,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any source establishment from which it received
      the donor referral, if applicable,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any source establishment to which it made a
      donor referral, if applicable, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any establishment to which it distributed
      implicated cells, tissues or organs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} initiate an investigation into the suspected error
    or accident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for its belief that the safety of the
    cells, tissues or organs has been compromised;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the safety of the implicated
    cells, tissues or organs may have been compromised, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification codes of all implicated
    cells, tissues and organs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any suspected transmissible disease or
    disease agent, if known; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement requiring all implicated cells, tissues
    and organs to be quarantined immediately and until further notice
    from the source establishment and specifying any other corrective
    action that must be taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When no investigation necessary

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** If, on receipt of a
notice under subsection 43(1), the source establishment does not have
reasonable grounds to believe that an investigation is necessary, it
must notify the establishment to that effect in writing and provide its
reasons for the decision not to conduct an investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action on receipt of notice

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that is
not a source establishment and that receives a notice under section 44
or a copy of such a notice under this section must immediately take both
of the following actions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine all implicated cells, tissues and organs
  in its possession; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} forward a copy of the notice to every establishment
  to which it distributed implicated cells, tissues or organs.

### [Adverse Reactions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732018 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required action

- **[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), an
  establishment that is not a source establishment and that has

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify and quarantine any other cells, tissues

    reaction in the same way as the transplanted cells, tissues or
    organs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the following establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the relevant source establishment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the cells, tissues or organs were imported,

  establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse reaction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification codes of all implicated
    cells, tissues and organs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of any suspected transmissible disease or
    disease agent, if known.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Written notice

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action by source establishment

- **[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  unexpected adverse reaction has occurred that involves cells, tissues
  or organs for whose processing it is responsible must immediately take
  all of the following actions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine any implicated cells, tissues and organs

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} send a notice described in subsection (2) to all of
    the following establishments:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the implicated cells, tissues or organs were
      imported, the establishment that imported them,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any source establishment from which it received
      the donor referral, if applicable,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any source establishment to which it made a
      donor referral, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any establishment to which it distributed
      implicated cells, tissues or organs; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} initiate an investigation into the adverse
    reaction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must include all of the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the adverse
    reaction;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an explanation of how the safety of the implicated
    cells, tissues or organs may have been compromised, if known;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification codes of all implicated
    cells, tissues and organs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any suspected transmissible disease or
    disease agent, if known; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement requiring all implicated cells, tissues
    and organs to be quarantined immediately and until further notice
    from the source establishment and specifying any other corrective
    action that must be taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Action on receipt of notice

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that is
not a source establishment and that receives a notice under section 48

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} quarantine all implicated cells, tissues and organs
  in its possession; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} forward a copy of the notice to every establishment

### [Investigations and Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732057 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to cooperate

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
provide the source establishment that is conducting an investigation
with any relevant information in its possession with respect to cells,
tissues or organs that it distributed or transplanted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports to Minister

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  source establishment that is conducting an investigation into either
  of the following subject-matters must provide the Minister with the
  reports described in subsection (2):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a suspected error or accident that is identified
    after distribution of cells, tissues or organs that could lead to a
    serious adverse reaction involving the transmission of an infectious
    disease or disease agent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an unexpected serious adverse reaction that is
    thought to involve the transmission of an infectious disease or
    disease agent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents and timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The reports must include the following information
  and be provided at the following times:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 24 hours after the start of the
    investigation, a preliminary report that includes all relevant
    information that is available at that time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the start of the investigation
    and every 15 days after that until the final report is made, an
    update on any new information about the suspected error or accident
    or serious adverse reaction, on the progress made in the
    investigation during those 15 days and on the steps taken to
    mitigate further risks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When investigation shows no contamination
or compromise

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  results of the investigation show that the implicated cells, tissues
  or organs are not contaminated or compromised, the source
  establishment must notify every establishment that was notified under
  section 44 or 48 to that effect in writing and that they may be
  released from quarantine.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forwarding of copies of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a notice under subsection (1), an
  establishment that is not a source establishment must forward a copy
  of the notice to every establishment to whom it distributed implicated
  cells, tissues or organs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When investigation inconclusive or shows
contamination or compromise

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  results of the investigation show that some or all of the implicated
  cells, tissues or organs are contaminated or compromised, or the
  results are inconclusive, the source establishment must notify every
  establishment that was notified under section 44 or 48 to that effect
  in writing and that they may not be released for distribution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forwarding of copies of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a notice under subsection (1), an
  establishment that is not a source establishment must forward a copy
  of the notice to every establishment to whom it distributed implicated
  cells, tissues or organs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report to Minister

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  completion of an investigation , the source establishment must submit
  a detailed final report to the Minister that contains at least all of
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the investigation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the final disposition of the cells, tissues and
    organs that were the subject of the investigation and the reasons
    for that disposition; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any corrective actions taken.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Summaries of final reports

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The source establishment must send a summary of the
  final report to every establishment that was notified under section 44
  or 48.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Forwarding of summaries

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that receives a summary under
  subsection (2) must send a copy of it to every establishment to which
  it distributed implicated cells, tissues or organs.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732089 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record quality

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** Records kept by an
establishment must be accurate, complete, legible and indelible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor identification code --- source
establishment

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  source establishment must assign a donor identification code to each
  donor of a cell, tissue or organ for which it has responsibility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Donor identification code --- all
  establishments

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every registered establishment and transplant
  establishment must ensure that the donor identification code is a
  component of its records system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

**[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** An establishment's
records must contain information with respect to all cells, tissues and
organs that it processes, distributes, imports or transplants that
identifies

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the establishment from which it receives the cells,
  tissues and organs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all establishments to which it distributes the cells,
  tissues and organs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shipping documents

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** An establishment's
records must include all shipping documents with respect to cells,
tissues and organs that it ships to another establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Source establishment records

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** The source establishment
must keep records with respect to cells, tissues and organs that it
processes that contain at least all of the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification code;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} documentation showing completion of the donor
  suitability assessment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the cells, tissues and organs
  retrieved from the donor;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name of any source establishment
  from which it received a donor referral or to which it made a donor
  referral;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the retrieval establishment;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} documentation of all processing activities;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the notice of exceptional distribution, if any; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} documentation of any reported errors, accidents and
  adverse reactions and their investigation, if any, in connection with
  cells, tissues or organs retrieved from the donor that it banked or
  distributed and any corrective action taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transplant establishment records

**[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** The transplant
establishment must keep records with respect to cells, tissues and
organs that it transplants that contain at least all of the following
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the transplanted cells, tissues or
  organs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the donor identification code;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the registration number of the source establishment;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the notice of exceptional distribution, if any, and
  confirmation that the donor suitability assessment was completed as
  required by section 42;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} information that allows the identification of the
  recipient; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} documentation of any errors, accidents and adverse
  reactions and their investigation in connection with those cells,
  tissues or organs and any corrective action taken.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Establishments to cooperate

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
provide the source establishment and the transplant establishment with
all of the information described in sections 59 and 60, respectively,
that it possesses to complete the establishment's records.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention --- 10 years after
transplantation

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must keep the following records for at least 10 years
  after the date of transplantation, if known, or for at least 10 years
  after the date of distribution, final disposition or expiry of the
  cell, tissue or organ, as the case may be, whichever is the latest:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the records described in section 57;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the records described in section 59, except
    paragraph (h);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the records described in section 60, except
    paragraph (f); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the record of destruction or other disposition of
    the cell, tissue or organ, if applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention --- 10 years after record
  creation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment must keep the following records for
  10 years after the date of their creation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the records described in paragraphs 59(h) and
    60(f); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reports of audits conducted under section 76, if
    applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention --- employee records

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that distributes cells, tissues or
  organs must keep records of the qualifications, training and
  competency of its employees for 10 years after the time an individual
  ceases to be an employee of the establishment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention --- standard operating
  procedures

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that distributes cells, tissues or
  organs must keep a copy of every version of its standard operating
  procedures for 10 years after they are superseded by a new version.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of records

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes cells, tissues or organs must store records in a location
that has appropriate environmental conditions and that is secure against
the entry of unauthorized persons.

## [Personnel, Facilities, Equipment and Supplies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732140 .Part}

### [Personnel]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732141 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sufficient number and qualifications

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment that distributes cells, tissues or organs must have
  sufficient personnel who are qualified by education, training or
  experience to perform their respective tasks to carry out the
  establishment's activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Competency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that distributes cells, tissues or
  organs must have a system for the orientation and training, both
  initial and ongoing, of personnel and for the evaluation of their
  competency.

### [Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732147 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** The facilities of an
establishment that distributes cells, tissues or organs must be
constructed and maintained to permit all of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the carrying out of all of its activities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the efficient cleaning, maintenance and disinfection
  of the facilities in a way that prevents contamination and
  cross-contamination;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} appropriate monitoring and control of environmental
  and microbiological conditions in all areas where its activities are
  carried out; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} controlled access to all areas where its activities
  are carried out.

### [Equipment and Supplies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732154 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- equipment

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes cells, tissues or organs, in carrying out its processing and
storage activities, must use equipment that is cleaned and maintained
and, whenever applicable,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} qualified for its intended purpose;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} calibrated;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} disinfected or sterilized before each use; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} requalified or recalibrated, as appropriate, after
  any repair or change is made to it that results in a change to its
  specifications.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements --- storage equipment

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
distributes cells, tissues or organs that uses equipment to store cells,
tissues or adjunct vessels that are not used immediately in organ
transplantation must ensure that the equipment maintains appropriate
environmental conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Processing supplies

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
processes cells, tissues or organs must use qualified supplies for those
activities that could affect the safety of the cells, tissues or organs,
and must store solutions, reagents and other supplies under appropriate
environmental conditions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cleaning supplies

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** An establishment that
processes cells, tissues or organs must ensure that it uses supplies for
cleaning, maintenance, disinfection or sterilization that do not react
with, or that are not absorbable by, the cells, tissues or organs.

## [Quality Assurance System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732167 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732168 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** Sections 71 to 76 apply
only to establishments that distribute cells, tissues or organs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quality assurance system required

**[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
ensure that it has a quality assurance system in place that complies
with the requirements of these Regulations for all activities that it
carries out.

### [Standard Operating Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-732173 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard operating procedures required

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
have standard operating procedures with respect to the safety of cells,
tissues and organs for all activities that it carries out.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** The standard operating
procedures must meet all of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be in a standardized format;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be approved by the medical director or scientific
  director;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be available for use at all locations where the
  relevant activities are carried out;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have any changes to the procedures approved by the
  medical director or scientific director before being implemented; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be kept up-to-date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Routine review

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  establishment must review its standard operating procedures every two
  years and again after any amendment to these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment that receives a summary of a final
  report of an error, accident or adverse reaction investigation or the
  report of an audit either of which reveals a deficiency in a standard
  operating procedure must review that procedure.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of compliance

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
keep records that demonstrate that it has implemented its standard
operating procedures.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audits

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** An establishment must
conduct an audit every two years of the activities that it carries out
to verify that those activities comply with these Regulations and with
its standard operating procedures, by a person who does not have direct
responsibility for the activities being audited.

## [Powers of Inspectors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732192 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking photographs

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** An inspector may, in the
administration of these Regulations, take photographs of any of the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any article that is referred to in subsection 23(2)
  of the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any place where the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds that any article referred to in paragraph (a) is processed;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything that the inspector believes on reasonable
  grounds is used or is capable of being used in the processing of any
  article referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-732198 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Processed within 5 years before
registration

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  following establishments may import, distribute or transplant, as the
  case may be, cells and tissues that were processed within five years
  before the day on which these Regulations are registered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a registered establishment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transplant establishment that does not distribute
    cells or tissues.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An establishment may not import, distribute or
  transplant, as the case may be, cells or tissues under subsection (1)
  unless the requirements of subsection 56(2) and section 57 are met.
