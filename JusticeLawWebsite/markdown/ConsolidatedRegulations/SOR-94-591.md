## Teslin Airport Zoning Regulations {#teslin-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-591
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1994-09-13

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Teslin Airport

P.C. 1994-1525 1994-09-13
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed [Regulations respecting
zoning at Teslin Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-591), substantially in
the form set out in the schedule hereto, was published in two successive
issues of the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on
June 11th and 18th, 1994, and in two successive issues of The Whitehorse
Star on May 27th and 30th, 1994, and a reasonable opportunity was
thereby afforded to interested persons to make representations to the
Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Teslin
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-591).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964657 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Teslin Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-591).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964659 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Teslin Airport, in the
      vicinity of Teslin, in the Yukon Territory;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which planes are more particularly described in Part II of
      the schedule; ([surfaces d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary plane
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      plane is more particularly described in Part III of the schedule;
      ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which portion is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surfaces]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the imaginary
      inclined planes that extend upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which planes are more
      particularly described in Part V of the schedule. ([surfaces de
      transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 692.2 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964669 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all land, including public road allowances, that is adjacent to or in
the vicinity of the airport, which land is more particularly described
in Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964671 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Teslin Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.3012, dated August 25, 1993, is a point located on the centre line
of runway 08-26 distant 838.2 m from the threshold of runway 26.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Teslin Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3012,
dated August 25, 1993, are planes abutting each end of the strip
associated with runway 08-26 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 08 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 345 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an inclined plane abutting the end of the strip
  associated with the approach to runway 26 having a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 345 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Teslin Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3012, dated
August 25, 1993, is an imaginary plane established at a constant
elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the airport reference point,
except that, where that plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the
ground, the outer surface is located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 08-26, shown on Teslin Airport Zoning
Plan No. E.3012, dated August 25, 1993, is 90 m in width, 45 m being on
each side of the centre line of the runway and 1 796.4 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Transitional Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-the-transitional-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Teslin Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.3012, dated August 25, 1993, is an inclined plane rising at a
ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right
angles to the centre line and projected centre line of the strip,
extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and
its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Land to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-land-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The outer boundary of the land to which these Regulations apply, shown
on Teslin Airport Zoning Plan No. E.3012, dated August 25, 1993, is a
circle with a radius of 4 000 m centred on the airport reference point.
