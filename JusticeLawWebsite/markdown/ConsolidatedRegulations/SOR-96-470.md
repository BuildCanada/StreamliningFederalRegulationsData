P.C. 1996-1590 1996-10-23

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 41(3) of the [Employment Equity
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA}, the Minister
of Labour has consulted with the Treasury Board on the annexed
[Employment Equity Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-470) as they
apply to the public sector;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  44
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Labour and the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 9(1), section 17,
subsections 18(1) and (5), subparagraph 39(4)(b)(i) and subsection 41(1)
of the [Employment Equity Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Employment Equity
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-470).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001198 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Employment Equity
      Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[bonus pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bonus pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a private sector
      employer, means any additional remuneration paid to an employee as
      a result of profit sharing, productivity, performance, commissions
      or any other incentives. ([prime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[designated CMA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[designated CMA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2020-236, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[employment equity report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employment equity report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a report
      that a private sector employer is required to file under
      section 18 of the Act. ([rapport sur l'équité en matière
      d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2020-236, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[overtime hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[overtime hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a private
      sector employer, means the hours worked by an employee, in excess
      of the standard hours of work, for which the employee received
      overtime pay. ([heures supplémentaires]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[overtime pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[overtime pay]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a private
      sector employer, means any remuneration paid for the hours worked
      by an employee in excess of the standard hours of work. ([paie
      d'heures supplémentaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permanent full-time employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanent full-time employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who is employed for an indeterminate period by a private
      sector employer to regularly work the standard number of hours
      fixed by the employer for employees in the occupational group in
      which the person is employed. ([salarié permanent à plein
      temps]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[permanent part-time employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[permanent part-time employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      person who is employed for an indeterminate period by a private
      sector employer to regularly work fewer than the standard number
      of hours fixed by the employer for employees in the occupational
      group in which the person is employed. ([salarié permanent à temps
      partiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reporting period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the calendar year
      in respect of which an employment equity report is filed.
      ([période de rapport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[temporary employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[temporary employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
      employed on a temporary basis by a private sector employer for any
      number of hours within a fixed period or periods totalling
      12 weeks or more during a calendar year, but does not include a
      person in full-time attendance at a secondary or post-secondary
      educational institution who is employed during a school break.
      ([salarié temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the Act,

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a private sector employer, means a person who
        is employed by the employer, but does not include a person
        employed on a temporary or casual basis for fewer than 12 weeks
        in a calendar year;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the federal public administration
        referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) or (c) of the Act to which the
        [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies,
        means a person who has been appointed or deployed to that
        portion pursuant to that Act, but does not include

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person appointed as a casual worker under
          subsection 50(1) of that Act, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person appointed for a period of less than
          three months; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the federal public administration
        referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) or (c) of the Act to which the
        [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) does not
        apply, means a person appointed to that portion in accordance
        with the enactment establishing that portion, but does not
        include a person employed on a temporary or casual basis for a
        period of less than three months. ([salarié]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[hired]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hired]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed by a private sector
        employer, means engaged by the employer;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed in a portion of the
        federal public administration referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b)
        or (c) of the Act to which the [Public Service Employment
        Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies, means initially appointed to
        the federal public administration in accordance with that Act
        except in the case of a person appointed as a casual worker
        under subsection 50(1) of that Act; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed in a portion of the
        federal public administration referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b)
        or (c) of the Act to which the [Public Service Employment
        Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) does not apply, means initially
        appointed in the manner provided in the enactment establishing
        that portion. ([recrutement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[occupational group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[occupational group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the workforce of a private sector employer or a
        portion of the federal public administration referred to in
        paragraph 4(1)(c) of the Act, means an occupational group set
        out in column I of Schedule II; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the federal public administration
        referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) of the Act, means an
        occupational group set out in column I of Schedule
        III. ([catégorie professionnelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[promoted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[promoted]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed in a portion of the
        federal public administration referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b)
        or (c) of the Act to which the [Public Service Employment
        Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) applies, has the same meaning as the
        definition [[promotion]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 3 of
        the [Definition of Promotion
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-376);

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed in a portion of the
        federal public administration referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b)
        or (c) of the Act to which the [Public Service Employment
        Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) does not apply, has the meaning
        customarily used by that portion; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an employee employed by a private sector
        employer, means permanently moved from one position or job in
        the employer's organization to another position or job that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a higher salary or a higher salary range
          than the salary or salary range of the position or job
          previously held by the employee, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} ranks higher in the organizational hierarchy
          of the employer,

        and includes a reclassification of the employee's position or
        job where the reclassified position or job meets the
        requirements of subparagraphs (i) and (ii).
        ([avancement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[salary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a private sector employer, means remuneration
        paid for work performed by an employee, before deductions, in
        the form of basic pay, pay for piecework, shift premiums, bonus
        pay and overtime pay, but does not include benefits, securities,
        severance pay or termination pay, vacation pay, payment in kind,
        supplementary payments, allowances, retroactive payments,
        reimbursements for employment expenses or compensation for
        extra-duty services other than overtime pay;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the federal public administration
        referred to in paragraph 4(1)(b) of the Act, means the rate of
        pay paid to an employee under a collective agreement or the rate
        approved by Treasury Board under any other applicable pay
        authority; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a portion of the federal public administration
        referred to in paragraph 4(1)(c) of the Act, means the rate of
        pay paid to an employee under a collective agreement or any
        other applicable pay authority. ([rémunération]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[terminated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terminated]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an employee,
      means retired, resigned, laid off, dismissed or otherwise having
      ceased to be an employee, but does not include laid off
      temporarily or absent by reason of illness, injury or a labour
      dispute. ([cessation de fonctions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001237 .Part}

### [Calculation of Number of Employees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001238 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
determining when an employer is considered to employ 100 or more
employees,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees of a private sector employer
  shall be calculated on the basis of the number at the time in a
  calendar year when the number of employees is the greatest; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of employees employed in a portion of the
  federal public administration referred to in paragraph 4(1)(c) of the
  Act shall be calculated on the basis of the number at the time in a
  fiscal year when the number of employees employed in that portion is
  the greatest.

### [Collection of Workforce Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001242 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (8) and (9), before preparing an employment equity plan
  referred to in section 10 of the Act, an employer shall conduct a
  workforce survey by providing to each employee a workforce survey
  questionnaire that asks the employee whether the employee is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of a visible minority;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person with a disability; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an Aboriginal person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The questionnaire shall contain the definitions
  [[Aboriginal peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[members of visible
  minorities]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[persons with
  disabilities]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} set out in section 3 of the Act
  to help the employee respond to the questionnaire.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2020-236, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall inform each employee, either on
  the questionnaire or in a notice accompanying the questionnaire, that
  a person may be a member of more than one designated group.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The questionnaire may include additional questions
  relating to employment equity.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The questionnaire shall indicate that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} responses to the questions on the questionnaire are
    voluntary; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information collected in the questionnaire is
    confidential and will only be used by or be disclosed to other
    persons within the employer's organization in order for the employer
    to carry out its obligations under the Act.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph (6)(a) shall not be construed as precluding
  an employer from requiring each employee to return the questionnaire
  to the employer.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} An employer is not required to conduct a workforce
  survey in respect of all or part of its workforce under subsection (1)
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the coming into force of these Regulations,
    the employer has already conducted a survey in respect of all or
    that part of its workforce to determine whether the employees belong
    to any of the designated groups referred to in that subsection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the previous survey had questions and was conducted
    in a manner that achieved results that are likely to be as accurate
    as the results that would be achieved using a workforce survey
    questionnaire under this section;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} responses to the questions in the previous survey
    were voluntary; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the survey results have been kept up to date in
    accordance with section 5.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where an employer replaces its employment equity plan
  with a new plan, the employer is not required to conduct a new
  workforce survey if the previous survey results have been kept up to
  date in accordance with section 5.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The employer shall ensure
that there is a means of identifying, on the workforce survey
questionnaire, the employee who returns it, whether by name or
otherwise.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The employer shall keep the
workforce survey results up to date by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing a workforce survey questionnaire

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to an employee when the employee begins employment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to an employee who wishes to change any

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to an employee who requests it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} making necessary adjustments to the survey results to
  take into account the responses to the questionnaires referred to in
  paragraph (a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} making necessary adjustments to the survey results to
  take into account members of designated groups who have been
  terminated.

### [Workforce Analysis]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001270 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Based on
  the information collected under sections 3 to 5, and on relevant
  information contained in any other employment records maintained by
  the employer, the employer shall conduct an analysis of its workforce
  in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the following for each occupational group
    of the employer's workforce, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons who are Aboriginal peoples,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons who are persons with
      disabilities,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of persons who are members of
      visible minorities, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the number of women; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determine the degree of underrepresentation of the
    persons referred to in paragraph (a) by comparing the representation
    of each designated group in each occupational group of the
    employer's workforce to their representation in each occupational
    group in whichever of the following is the most appropriate as a
    basis of comparison, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canadian workforce as a whole, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} those segments of the Canadian workforce that
      are identifiable by qualification, eligibility or geography, and
      from which the employer may reasonably be expected to draw
      employees.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In making a determination under paragraph (1)(b), the
  employer shall use the labour market information made available by the
  Minister under section 42(3) of the Act, or information from other
  sources that is determined by the Minister to be relevant labour
  market information, in order to determine the representation, in the
  geographic area or areas from which the employer may reasonably be
  expected to draw employees, of workers who are members of designated
  groups and who are qualified or eligible for the jobs within each
  occupational group of the employer's workforce.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An employer who has already conducted an analysis of
  all or part of its workforce before the coming into force of these
  Regulations is not required to conduct another analysis of all or that
  part of its workforce, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the results of the previous analysis are up to date
    as a result of periodic revisions that have taken into account the
    updating of the workforce survey results in accordance with
    section 5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the previous analysis are likely to
    be the same as the results that would be achieved by an analysis
    undertaken pursuant to subsections (1) and (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an employer replaces its employment equity plan
  with a new plan, the employer is not required to conduct a new
  workforce analysis if the results of the previous analysis have been
  kept up to date by means of periodic revisions that have taken into
  account the updating of the workforce survey results in accordance
  with section 5.

### [Review of Employment Systems, Policies and Practices]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001287 .Subheading}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where, based on the
workforce analysis conducted pursuant to section 6, underrepresentation
of persons in designated groups has been identified in any occupational
group of the employer's workforce, the employer shall conduct a review
of its employment systems, policies and practices in order to determine
whether any of those employment systems, policies and practices is an
employment barrier against persons in designated groups.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 10, for the purposes of making a determination referred to
  in section 8, the employer shall, in relation to each occupational
  group in which underrepresentation referred to in section 8 has been
  identified, review its employment systems, policies and practices with
  respect to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recruitment, selection and hiring of employees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development and training of employees;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the promotion of employees;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the retention and termination of employees; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the reasonable accommodation of the special needs
    of members of designated groups.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, following a review under subsection (1), new
  employment systems, policies or practices relating to the matters
  referred to in that subsection are implemented by the employer, the
  employer shall also review the new employment systems, policies or
  practices with respect to those matters.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An employer who, before
the coming into force of these Regulations, has conducted a review of
its employment systems, policies and practices with respect to the
matters referred to in subsection 9(1) in relation to all or part of its
workforce is not required to conduct another review with respect to the
matters already reviewed if the results of the previous review are
likely to be the same as the results that would be achieved by a review
undertaken pursuant to subsection 9(1).

### [Employment Equity Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001298 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An employer shall
establish and maintain the following records:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a record of each employee's designated group
  membership, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of each employee's occupational group
  classification;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a record of each employee's salary and salary
  increases;

- [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} for each employee employed by a private sector
  employer, a record containing the information referred to in
  paragraphs 25.1(a) to (f);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a record of each employee's promotions;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the workforce survey questionnaire that was
  provided to the employees and any other information used by the
  employer in conducting its workforce analysis;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the summary of the results of the workforce analysis
  required by section 7;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of the activities undertaken by the
  employer in conducting its employment systems review;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the employer's employment equity plan;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a record of the employer's monitoring of the
  implementation of its employment equity plan, undertaken in accordance
  with paragraph 12(b) of the Act; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a record of activities undertaken by the employer and
  information provided to employees in accordance with section 14 of the
  Act.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Private Sector Employer Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001316 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001317 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of the report required to be filed by private sector employers
under section 18 of the Act.

### [Prescriptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001319 .Subheading}

**[[14.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14.1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
a reference to any of Forms 1 to 6 is to be read as a reference to a
Form set out in the document entitled Employment Equity Forms 2021,
prepared by the Department of Employment and Social Development and
