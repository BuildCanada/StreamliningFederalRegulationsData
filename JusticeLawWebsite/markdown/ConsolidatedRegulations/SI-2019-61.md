:::

[COLLEGE OF IMMIGRATION AND CITIZENSHIP CONSULTANTS
ACT](/eng/acts/C-33.6)

Registration 2019-07-24

Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a
federal minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act

P.C. 2019-1049 2019-07-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [College of Immigration
and Citizenship Consultants Act](/eng/acts/C-33.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851){#fn_81000-3-1811_hq_24851-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a federal
minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.
