## Railway Passenger Services Contract Regulations {#railway-passenger-services-contract-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-286
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1977

Registration 1978-03-28

Regulations Respecting a Contract Between the Minister and Via Rail
Canada Inc. with Respect to Railway Passenger Services

P.C. 1978-952 1978-03-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport and the President of the Treasury Board,
pursuant to the Department of Transport Vote 52d of Appropriation Act
No. 1, 1977, is pleased hereby to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting a Contract between the Minister and Via Rail Canada Inc. with
respect to Railway Passenger Services](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-286).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861978 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Railway Passenger Services Contract
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-286).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861980 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[CN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CN]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian National Railway Company;
    ([CN]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[CP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canadian Pacific Limited;
    ([CP]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[railway passenger service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway passenger service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means passenger
    services operated over railway lines, and other passenger services
    in substitution for or ancillary to those services, that do not
    accommodate principally the carriage of goods or persons who
    regularly commute between points on a railway line; ([service
    ferroviaire voyageurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[railway passenger service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[railway passenger service contract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    contract between the Minister and VIA entered into pursuant to
    Transport Vote 52d of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1977; ([contrat de
    service ferroviaire voyageurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidiary service request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidiary service request]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a request
    in writing by the Minister to VIA with respect to the manner in
    which and the extent to which the provision, management and
    operation of railway passenger services shall be carried out by VIA;
    ([demande de services complémentaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[VIA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[VIA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means VIA Rail Canada Inc.
    ([VIA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Conditions Precedent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861989 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall not
enter into a railway passenger service contract unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Treasury Board has approved the contract;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} VIA has provided the Minister with an implementation
  plan approved by CN and CP relating to the involvement of VIA in the
  provision, management and operation of railway passenger services; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} VIA has reached an agreement with CN and CP whereby,
  in determining payments by VIA to CN and CP that may include provision
  for financial incentive measures, the proposed contract may be subject
  to a review approved by the Minister to ascertain that the costs do
  not exceed, without the approval of the Minister, costs determined by
  the application of CTC Order R-6313, subject to indexing supplied by
  the Canadian Transport Commission.

## [Conditions Relating to a Subsidiary Service Request]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861994 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every railway passenger
service contract shall provide, with respect to a subsidiary service
request, undertakings whereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} prior to the issuance of a subsidiary service request
  by the Minister, the Minister shall require VIA to provide plans and
  financial projections with respect to the services to be provided by
  VIA, including proposed fare structures if requested by the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may, before making a subsidiary service
  request, consult with provincial governments, members of the motor
  carrier industry and other organizations with respect to any proposed
  fare structure;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no subsidiary service request

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} requiring the payment of an amount in excess of ten
    million dollars or such other amount as Treasury Board may direct in
    any one year, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} relating to passenger services in substitution for
    or ancillary to passenger services operated over railway lines

  shall be made by the Minister without the approval of the Treasury
  Board;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary service request shall not contain
  undertakings that are inconsistent with the contract; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Minister may impose limitations with respect to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum total payment that may be made to VIA,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pricing conditions within which fares may be set
    by VIA, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the minimum level and quality of service to be
    provided by VIA.

## [Undertakings of Railway Passenger Service Contract]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862007 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every railway passenger
service contract shall contain, in addition to such other undertakings
as the Minister may deem advisable, the following undertakings by VIA:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} VIA shall, after consultation with the Minister,
  comply with any subsidiary service request made by the Minister;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} VIA shall make its accounts and records open to audit
  and inspection by an authorized representative of the Minister;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} VIA shall provide reports to the Minister at such
  times and containing such information as the Minister may from time to
  time require; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} VIA shall adopt a contract policy for public
  invitation to tender acceptable to the Minister.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No railway passenger
service contract shall contain any express or implied undertakings that
have the effect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} requiring the Minister to make payments to VIA in
  respect of costs incurred by VIA, other than costs by application of
  the compensation method provisions set forth in section 7; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} precluding the Minister from participating in the
  establishment of or requiring the implementation of objectives,
  service concepts, pricing policies and other related matters.

## [Payments to Via]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862016 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every railway passenger
service contract shall provide, with respect to payments to VIA pursuant
to the contract or a subsidiary service request, undertakings whereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment by the Minister may include provision for
  financial incentive measures;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application of financial incentive measures will
  be undertaken so as to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} encourage cost-effective decision-making by VIA in
    making the trade-off between attracting passengers and reducing
    costs, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} vary the terms of payment from route to route as
    may be required in accordance with the competitive conditions and
    government objectives on each route;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} payments will not be based on the actual costs
  incurred by VIA of providing, managing or operating the service but
  will be determined at the time of the contract or request, as the case
  may be, on the basis of estimated costs less estimated revenues,
  having regard to the minimum level and quality of service and the
  pricing conditions prescribed by the Minister; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in determining payments the estimate of costs related
  to the operation of passenger services will not exceed, without
  approval of the Minister, costs determined by the application of CTC
  Order R-6313, subject to indexing supplied by the Canadian Transport
  Commission.
