## Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 143 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1 {#order-fixing-the-day-for-the-purposes-of-section-143-of-the-economic-action-plan-2014-act-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-15
:::

[ECONOMIC ACTION PLAN 2014 ACT, NO. 1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)

Registration 2015-01-30

Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 143 of the Economic
Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1

P.C. 2015-38 2015-01-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 143 of the Economic Action
Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDBAA}, makes the annexed Order
Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 143 of the Economic Action
Plan 2014 Act, No. 1.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EDBAA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2014, c. 20
  :::
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817874 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Day for purposes of section 143

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The day for the purposes of
section 143 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1){#fn_1-ID0EBBA} is fixed as December 1, 2018.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_1-ID0EBBA){#fn_1}S.C.
  2014, c. 20
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817877 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndE9B3_hq_15213){#fn_IndE9B3_hq_15213-ID0EBDA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the first day on which
sections 111, 139 and 140 of the [Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No.
1](/eng/acts/E-0.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_IndE9B3_hq_15214){#fn_IndE9B3_hq_15214-ID0EBCA}
are all in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
