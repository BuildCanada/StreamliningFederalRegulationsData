## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258477 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Railway Safety
    Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conductor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conductor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who is in charge
    of the operation of a movement of railway equipment. ([chef de
    train]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[controlling locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlling locomotive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the railway
    equipment from which an operating employee controls the movement of
    the locomotive and any other railway equipment connected to the
    locomotive. ([locomotive de commande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[locomotive engineer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[locomotive engineer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who is
    in control of the locomotive. ([mécanicien de
    locomotive]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[LVVR system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[LVVR system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a locomotive voice and
    video recorder system. ([système d'EAVL]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operating employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operating employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who
    performs the duties of a conductor or locomotive engineer. ([membre
    du personnel de l'exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[TSB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TSB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Transportation
    Accident Investigation and Safety Board. ([BST]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a company that meets at least one of the following criteria:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company realized gross revenues of at least \$250
  million for the provision of rail services in Canada in each of the
  two preceding calendar years and operates controlling locomotives on
  five miles or more of track in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the company operates a passenger train service within
  a municipality or between adjacent municipalities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the company has 15 or more operating employees,
  operates controlling locomotives on 20 miles or more of track in
  Canada, operates at least one controlling locomotive at a speed of
  more than 25 miles per hour, and, if the company transports freight,
  generates more than 10% of its gross ton miles on track in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Installation of LVVR system

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that an LVVR system is installed in every
  controlling locomotive that it operates.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a company is not required to ensure that an
  LVVR system is installed in a controlling locomotive that is a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} steam locomotive;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} heritage locomotive that is used exclusively for
    tourist railway services that travels no further than a round trip
    of 150 miles at a speed of no more than 25 miles per hour;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} locomotive in yard service that is used exclusively
    in switching, marshalling, humping, trimming and industrial
    switching;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} locomotive that is being used for testing and
    evaluation purposes; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} locomotive that the company intends to decommission
    within 18 months after the day on which these Regulations come into
    force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage requirement

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A company must ensure that
the interior of every controlling locomotive fitted with an LVVR system
has signage, written in both official languages of Canada and in a font
that is clear and readable from the furthest point from the signage in
the controlling locomotive, that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} notifies persons in the controlling locomotive that
  they are subject to audio and video recording; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} includes the following statements: "It is prohibited
  by law for any person to do anything, including altering the
  locomotive voice and video recorder system, with the intent to prevent
  information from being recorded, collected or preserved." and "[Il est
  interdit par la loi à quiconque de prendre une quelconque mesure,
  notamment d'altérer le système d'enregistrement audio et vidéo de
  locomotive, dans l'intention d'empêcher l'enregistrement, la collecte
  ou la conservation de renseignements.]{.otherLang lang="fr"}"

## [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258506 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}LVVR system

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A company must ensure that
an LVVR system

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with at least one crash-protected memory
  module;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} continuously records voice and video data from the
  time the controlling locomotive engine is turned on until it is turned
  off;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not record data when the controlling locomotive
  engine is turned off;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} stores 48 hours of data on at least one
  crash-protected memory module;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} automatically and permanently erases from any
  crash-protected memory module any data other than the most recent 48
  hours of data that was stored by the LVVR system on the module;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with an alternate power supply or with a
  controlled shutdown feature that allows for a complete shutdown of the
  LVVR system in the event of a power interruption; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} restarts automatically when power is restored to the
  controlling locomotive engine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Environmental standards

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A company must ensure that,
on installation, an LVVR system meets the environmental criteria
respecting vibration, shock, bump and temperature testing set out in
standard EN 50155:2017, Railway applications --- Rolling stock ---
Electronic equipment, published by BSI Standards Limited, on October 31,
2017, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Crashworthiness

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A company must ensure that
a crash-protected memory module

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is installed in a controlling locomotive in the
  location that offers the most protection; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets, on installation or replacement, the
  crashworthiness requirements set out in standard 1482.1-2013, IEEE
  Standard for Rail Transit Vehicle Event Recorders, published by the
  Institute of Electrical and Electronic Engineers on March 6, 2014, as
  amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Microphones --- voice quality

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that an LVVR system is equipped with one or more
  microphones that, collectively, are capable of clearly recording

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the voices of the operating employees and their
    communications; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} aural warnings in the controlling locomotive,
    including alarms.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that every microphone

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has its own recording channel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is used to record the voices of operating
    employees, records frequencies from 150 Hz to 3.5 kHz; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is used to record aural warnings in the
    controlling locomotive, records frequencies from 150 Hz to 6 kHz.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement of microphones

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that the microphones are
  positioned in the controlling locomotive in a manner that allows the
  recording of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the voice of the locomotive engineer, clearly and
    distinctly from the voice of the conductor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the voice of the conductor, clearly and distinctly
    from the voice of the locomotive engineer; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} safety-related sounds and aural warnings in the
    controlling locomotive, including alarms.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cameras --- video quality

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that an LVVR system is equipped with one or more
  cameras that record at a minimum frame rate of 15 frames per second

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} clear video in all lighting conditions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} video with a resolution sufficient to determine the
    status of instrument displays in the controlling locomotive; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} video with a resolution sufficient to determine the
    reactions of the operating employees, including their facial
    features and expressions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement of cameras

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that the cameras are
  positioned in the controlling locomotive in a manner that allows the
  recording of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the interior of the controlling
    locomotive where the operating employees carry out their work;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an unobstructed view of the instruments and
    controls required to operate the controlling locomotive; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an unobstructed view of the faces and upper bodies
    of operating employees at a distance close enough to discern their
    facial features and expressions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional cameras

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a company that has satisfied
  the requirements set out in subsections (1) and (2) may equip an LVVR
  system with additional cameras that do not meet those requirements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coordinated Universal Time

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that the voice and video data recorded by an LVVR
  system has accurate date and time stamps that are expressed in
  Coordinated Universal Time (UTC).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Synchronization requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time stamps of the voice data that is
    recorded by each microphone of an LVVR system are synchronized, to
    the second, with the date and time stamps of the video data that is
    recorded by each camera of that system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time stamps of the voice and video
    data recorded by an LVVR system are synchronized, to the second,
    with the date and time stamps of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} locomotive event recorder data,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} global positioning system (GPS) data, if
      applicable, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the video data recorded by any outward-facing
      camera.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Playback synchronization

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that the voice and video data
  recorded by an LVVR system is capable of being automatically
  synchronized on a playback system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Synchronization testing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The company must carry out a test of each LVVR system
  at least once every 12 months to verify compliance with the
  synchronization requirements set out in subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep a record of each test carried
  out under subsection (4) for a period of six years after the day of
  the test and provide a copy to the Minister on request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Test recordings

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that test recordings are carried out for each LVVR
  system installed in a controlling locomotive operated by the company
  to demonstrate that the system meets the requirements set out in
  section 8, paragraphs 9(1)(b) and (c) and subsections 9(2), 10(3) and
  23(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The test recordings must be carried out in respect of
  an LVVR system once per year and at each of the following times:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on installation of the system in the controlling
    locomotive, if the company itself installs the system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prior to the company's initial operation of the
    controlling locomotive, if the company itself did not install the
    system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} each time a component of the system is repaired,
    replaced or updated.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of test recordings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the test recordings required
  under subsection (1) for a period of six years after the day on which
  they are carried out and provide a copy to the Minister on request.

## [Data Management Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258576 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Policy and procedures

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must develop, implement and make available to all employees a
  written policy that includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the purposes under section 17.91
    of the Act for which the company intends to record, collect or
    preserve voice and video data;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} procedures for collecting, communicating, accessing
    and using voice and video data; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} procedures for record keeping.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of policy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the policy while it is in
  effect and for a period of six years after the day on which it is
  replaced and provide a copy to the Minister on request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accountable executive

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must designate a member of senior management who is
  responsible for the operations of the company as the accountable
  executive to be accountable for meeting the requirements of these
  Regulations, including implementing the policy referred to in section
  12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must submit to the Minister, within 30
  days after the designation, the name and title of the accountable
  executive and a statement signed by that person confirming that they
  accept the responsibilities of the position.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized persons --- voice and video
data

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must designate the persons it authorizes to take one or more
  of the following actions and make a record of their names and
  positions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collecting voice and video data from a
    crash-protected memory module or from any other storage location
    where such data is automatically stored;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicating voice and video data; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} accessing and using voice and video data for the
    purposes of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) or (b) or subsection 17.91(3) of
    the Act, as applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), the company
  must not designate any person who directly manages operating employees
  to access or use voice and video data under paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of
  the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must ensure that only a person authorized
  to take the action referred to in paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c) takes
  that action.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep any record required under
  subsection (1) while it is current and for a period of six years after
  the day on which it is revised and provide a copy to the Minister on
  request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized persons --- training

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must provide training on the policy required under section 12
  to each person designated under subsection 14(1) before the person
  takes any actions referred to in paragraphs 14(1)(a) to (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional training

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the company makes any changes to the policy, the
  company must provide training on the changes to each person designated
  under subsection 14(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safeguards --- LVVR system

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that an LVVR system is equipped with physical
  safeguards that are designed to prevent tampering, including reducing
  the field of view of the cameras.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safeguards --- voice and video data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company must ensure that crash-protected memory
  modules and any other storage locations containing voice or video data
  that was recorded in a controlling locomotive operated by the company
  are subject to safeguards that are designed to prevent unauthorized
  access to the data, including physical safeguards such as locked
  filing cabinets and restricted access to offices, organizational
  safeguards such as requiring a continuous chain of custody and
  technological safeguards such as the use of passwords and encryption.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
even if a company uses a storage provider operated by a third party to
back up or store voice or video data that was recorded in a controlling
locomotive operated by the company, the company is responsible for
complying with the provisions of the Act and these Regulations in
respect of that data.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Virtual storage provider

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a company uses a
virtual storage provider, including a cloud storage provider, to back up
or store voice or video data that was recorded in a controlling
locomotive operated by the company, the company must ensure that the
provider is accredited under a current internationally recognized
standard respecting information security management systems.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of data access or download

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that any technological means it uses to access or
  download voice or video data from a crash-protected memory module, or
  any other storage location where such data is automatically stored,
  automatically generates an unalterable record each time such data is
  accessed or downloaded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an electronic signature that uniquely identifies
    the person who accessed or downloaded the voice or video data;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time the data was accessed or
    downloaded;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time stamps of the data; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number or other identifier that uniquely
    identifies the controlling locomotive in which the data was
    recorded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Monthly report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company must, on a monthly basis, make available
  to all operating employees a report that includes the information
  referred to in subsection (2) that was generated during the preceding
  month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the records and reports
  described in subsections (1) and (3) for a period of six years after
  the day on which they are created and provide a copy to the Minister
  on request.

## [Communication Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258629 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request from Minister or inspector

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of a request
to obtain, for use by the Minister under paragraph 17.92(1)(b) or (c) of
the Act or for use by a railway safety inspector under subsection
17.93(1) of the Act, voice or video data that was recorded in a
controlling locomotive operated by the company, the company must
communicate the data to the Minister or the railway safety inspector, as
the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secure communications

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If a company communicates
voice or video data that was recorded in a controlling locomotive
operated by the company, it must communicate the data in a format that
prevents unauthorized access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications with TSB

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company communicates to the TSB voice or video data that was recorded
  in a controlling locomotive operated by the company, it must provide
  the software and equipment required to use the data, either in advance
  of or at the time that it communicates the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications with Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a company is required to communicate voice or
  video data that was recorded in a controlling locomotive operated by
  the company to the Minister, the company must transfer the data to the
  Minister and, on request, provide the software and equipment required
  to use the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communications with railway safety
  inspector

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a company is required to communicate voice or
  video data that was recorded in a controlling locomotive operated by
  the company to a railway safety inspector, the company must make the
  data available to the inspector.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Playback of data

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must ensure that voice and video data is capable of being
  played back in such a way that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the communications of the operating employees are
    understandable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the voice of each operating employee is
    identifiable and clearly distinguishable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Playback system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company must ensure that any playback system used
  to review voice or video data allows for an uninterrupted review of
  the data and includes play, pause, rewind and fast-forward functions.

## [Accident and Incident Investigations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258648 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessing and using data --- conditions

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must not access or use, for the purpose of paragraph
  17.91(1)(b) of the Act, voice or video data that was recorded in a
  controlling locomotive operated by the company unless it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has identified that the controlling locomotive was
    involved in the accident or incident; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has reason to believe that activities in the
    controlling locomotive caused or contributed to the accident or
    incident.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessing and using data --- types

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph 17.91(1)(b) of the Act,
  the voice and video data that a company may access and use is the data
  from the controlling locomotive involved in the accident or incident
  that was recorded

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the shift of any operating employee who was
    present when the accident or incident occurred; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during a shift immediately preceding a shift
    referred to in paragraph (a) if, prior to accessing or using the
    data, the company has notified all operating employees who are
    present on the recording, and any bargaining agents representing
    those employees, that it intends to use the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if, at the time of an accident
  or incident, a company cannot identify which controlling locomotive
  was involved, the company must not use voice or video data for the
  purpose of making that determination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data collection

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of a request
for voice or video data related to an accident or incident for use by
the Minister under subsection 17.92(1) of the Act or for use by a
railway safety inspector under subsection 17.93(1) of the Act, a company
must ensure that, before the data is permanently erased, it is collected
from the crash-protected memory module or any other storage location
where the data is automatically stored, and preserved in another storage
location.

## [Random Selection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258662 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Process

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  randomly select voice and video data as required by subsection
  17.91(2) of the Act, a company must use a random number generator to
  select, in the following order,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day of the month from which the data will be
    selected;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the controlling locomotives from which the data
    will be selected; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a maximum of three start times, to the minute, for
    each of the controlling locomotives.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Random selection

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must ensure that, when making the
  selections set out in subsection (1), no day, controlling locomotive
  or start time has a greater likelihood of being selected than another.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selecting the day of the month

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(a), the company must
  use the random number generator during the week preceding the
  beginning of the month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selecting controlling locomotives

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), the company must
  select at least two controlling locomotives from among all of the
  controlling locomotives that the company is scheduled to operate in
  Canada on the day selected under paragraph 1(a). The number of
  controlling locomotives selected must not exceed 10% of the
  controlling locomotives that the company is scheduled to operate in
  Canada on that day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Selecting start times

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(c), the company may
  include parameters to ensure that the selected start times are chosen
  from among the times that the controlling locomotives are scheduled to
  operate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to download

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A company must download 30 minutes of voice and video
  data beginning from each selected start time before the data is
  permanently erased from the crash-protected memory module or any other
  storage location where such data is automatically stored.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- maximum of 30 minutes

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} A company must not download more than 30 minutes of
  voice and video data beginning from each selected start time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- repeated selection
  process

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} A company must not perform the selections set out in
  paragraph (1)(a) to (c) more than once in respect of a given month.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Random selection policy

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} A company must develop and implement a written random
  selection policy that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identifies the persons in the company who are
    authorized to perform the selections set out in subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensures that no person who schedules or manages
    employees performs the selections set out in subsection (1) or
    influences any person who does; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} identifies and describes the random number
    generator that the company uses to perform the selections set out in
    subsection (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of voice and video data

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of the Act, a company must use all voice and video
data selected in accordance with section 26 within 30 days after
downloading the data.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of random selection policy to
Minister

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company must submit a copy of the random selection policy required
  under subsection 26(9) to the Minister before selecting any voice and
  video data for the purpose of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes to policy

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a company changes its random selection policy
  after submitting it to the Minister, the company must submit a copy of
  the updated policy to the Minister before selecting any voice and
  video data for the purpose of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request by Minister

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a request to obtain, for use by the Minister under
  paragraph 17.92(1)(a) of the Act, voice or video data that was
  recorded in a controlling locomotive operated by the company, the
  company must communicate to the Minister data that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company has randomly selected in accordance
    with any methodology provided by the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has randomly selected; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the company has randomly selected for the purpose
    of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of controlling locomotives

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of paragraph (1)(b), a company must
  provide to the Minister, on request, a list of its controlling
  locomotives that are scheduled to be in operation in Canada on any day
  or in any location selected by the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Downloading data

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company must download all voice and video data
  requested under subsection (1) before it is permanently erased from
  the crash-protected memory module or any other storage location where
  such data is automatically stored.

## [Threats to Safety of Railway Operations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258705 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed threats

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 17.91(3) of the Act, a company may use voice and video data
to address the following threats to the safety of railway operations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an operating employee who uses a cellular telephone
  while on duty when normal railway radio communication systems are
  available, except as provided for in company policies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an operating employee who assumes a sleeping position
  while on duty, except as provided for in company policies;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an operating employee who uses a personal
  entertainment device while on duty, except as provided for in company
  policies;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the presence of an unauthorized person in the
  controlling locomotive;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an operating employee who is consuming or using
  intoxicants or impairing drugs;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an operating employee who reads materials not
  required in the performance of their duties while on duty, except as
  provided for in company policies; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} operating employees who are within hearing range of
  each other but who are not verbally communicating, in a clear and
  audible manner, information they are required to verbally communicate
  in accordance with rules approved or established by the Minister under
  sections 19 and 20 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Employee access to data

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** If a company identifies a
threat set out in section 30 when it is using voice or video data for
the purposes of subsection 17.91(1) of the Act, the company must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, notify all identifiable persons
  present on the recording of the data that the company has identified a
  threat on that recording;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the notice is provided under
  paragraph (a), advise any employee the company has deemed responsible
  for the threat as to whether the data will be used to address the
  threat;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} make the data available to any employee whom the
  company has deemed responsible for the threat, on request of the
  employee; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} before the data is made available under paragraph
  (c), notify any other person referred to in paragraph (a) that the
  data will be made available to the employee deemed responsible for the
  threat.

## [Preservation and Erasure of Voice and Video Data]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258721 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Erasure of data --- storage locations

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A company must ensure
that voice and video data is permanently erased from any storage
location where such data is automatically stored, other than a
crash-protected memory module, before or at the same time as the
corresponding data is permanently erased from a crash-protected memory
module in accordance with paragraph 5(e).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Erasure of data --- when purpose achieved

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company preserves voice or video data for any purpose set out in
  sections 17.91 to 17.93 of the Act, the company must permanently erase
  the data from all storage locations as soon as the data is no longer
  required for that purpose.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of
  the Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if a company uses voice or
  video data for the purpose of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) of the Act, the
  company must permanently erase the data from all storage locations
  within 30 days after downloading the data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- threat to safety of
  railway operations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (2), if a company
  preserves voice and video data for any purpose set out in subsection
  17.91(1) of the Act and uses the data to address a threat to the
  safety of railway operations set out in section 30, the company must
  preserve the data for at least two years after the day on which the
  decision to address the threat was made and permanently erase the data
  from all storage locations as soon as the following conditions have
  been met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two years have passed since the decision to address
    the threat was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the data is no longer required for the purpose for
    which it was preserved.

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1258733 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessing or using voice or video data

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  time a company accesses or uses voice or video data, the company must
  make a record that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who accessed or used the
    data;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time the data was accessed or used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time stamps of the data;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number or other identifier that uniquely
    identifies the controlling locomotive on which the data was
    recorded; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the data was accessed or
    used.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication of voice or video data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each time a company communicates voice or video data
  to the TSB, the Minister or a railway safety inspector, the company
  must make a record that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the data was communicated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time the data was communicated;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person who communicated the data;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any individual who received or, as
    applicable, viewed the data and the organization they represent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the records referred to in
  subsections (1) and (2) for a period of six years after the day on
  which they are created and provide a copy of any record to the
  Minister on request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of random selection

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  time a company randomly selects voice and video data for the purposes
  of paragraph 17.91(1)(a) or 17.92(1)(a) of the Act, the company must
  make a record that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each selection made in accordance with subsection
    26(1) or any methodology provided by the Minister under paragraph
    29(1)(a), including the full data set from which each selection was
    made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and position of the person who performed
    each selection; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the voice and video data that was downloaded.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the records referred to in
  subsection (1) for a period of six years after the day on which they
  are created and provide a copy of any record to the Minister on
  request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data use --- threats to safety of railway
operations

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  time a company uses voice or video data to address a threat to the
  safety of railway operations set out in section 30, the company must
  make a record that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the purpose for which the data was used under
    subsection 17.91(1) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the threat to the safety of railway operations that
    was addressed; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which the decision to address the threat
    was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the record referred to in
  subsection (1) for a period of six years after the day on which it is
  created and provide a copy of any record to the Minister on request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Erasure of voice or video data

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  time a company permanently erases voice or video data in accordance
  with section 33, the company must make a record that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the method used to erase the data;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date and time the data was erased;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all storage locations from which the data was
    erased; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name and position of the person who erased the
    data.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company must keep the record referred to in
  subsection (1) for a period of six years after the day on which it is
  created and provide a copy of any record to the Minister on request.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copies kept in Canada

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** The company must keep at
its principal place of business in Canada a copy of any record, test
recording, policy and report that it is required to keep under these
Regulations.
