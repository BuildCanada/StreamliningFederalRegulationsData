Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development To
Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Early Learning and Child
Care Act

P.C. 2025-422 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Canada Early Learning and
Child Care Act](/eng/acts/C-3.55)[[Footnote

  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2024-345 of April 12,
  2024[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646){#nbp_81000-3-2846_hq_1741731646-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
