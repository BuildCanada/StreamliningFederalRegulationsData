## Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Assessment of Financial Institutions Regulations {#financial-consumer-agency-of-canada-assessment-of-financial-institutions-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-474
:::

[FINANCIAL CONSUMER AGENCY OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.1)

Registration 2001-11-01

Financial Consumer Agency of Canada Assessment of Financial Institutions
Regulations

P.C. 2001-2018  2001-11-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 18(3) and section 32 of
the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2001-474_e_hq_5936){#fn_SOR-2001-474_e_hq_5936-ID0EDBAA},

of Financial Institutions Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-474).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676747 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Consumer Agency of
    Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized foreign bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([banque
    étrangère autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2 of
    the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([banque]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[foreign life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 571 of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société d'assurance-vie
    étrangère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a company or foreign company to which the
      [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Green Shield Canada. ([société
      d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[life company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([société d'assurance-vie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[retail association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retail association]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 2 of the [Cooperative Credit Associations
    Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([association de détail]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trust and loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company to
    which the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)
    applies. ([société de fiducie et de prêt]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Determination of Assets, Net Revenues and Net Premiums]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676760 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by the Commissioner

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner shall,
before December 31 in each calendar year, ascertain

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the average total assets in Canada during the fiscal
  year ending on March 31 of that year of each bank, authorized foreign
  bank and trust and loan company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the average total assets in Canada during the
  immediately preceding calendar year of each retail association;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of the net revenue received in
  Canada during the immediately preceding calendar year by Green Shield
  Canada from its prepayment plans other than administrative services
  only plans; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total amount of net premiums received in Canada
  during the immediately preceding calendar year by each company and
  foreign company to which the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies.

## [Determination of Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676767 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to be assessed

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 18(3) of the Act and subject to subsection (2),
  the amount assessed by the Commissioner against each financial
  institution in respect of any fiscal year shall be equal to the base
  assessment determined for the financial institution in accordance with
  sections 4 to 8, less any interim assessment prepared against the
  financial institution pursuant to subsection 18(4) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No assessment is payable in any fiscal year by a
  financial institution with respect to which the Minister has approved
  an application for voluntary liquidation and dissolution before the
  beginning of that fiscal year or with respect to which a court has
  made a winding-up order under the [Winding-up and Restructuring
  Act](/eng/acts/W-11) before the beginning of that fiscal year.

## [Determination of Base Assessment for Banks and Authorized Foreign Banks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676773 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The base assessment of a
financial institution that is a bank or an authorized foreign bank shall
be equal to, for any fiscal year, the aggregate of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$1,000 and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#676778 .Paragraph}
  (A - B) × C / D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the expenses that are ascertained pursuant to
      subsection 18(1) of the Act as having been incurred during the
      fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
        attributable to banks and authorized foreign banks, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (a) of
        the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
        section 2 of the Act,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of all the amounts assessed under paragraph (a)
      against all financial institutions that are banks or authorized
      foreign banks,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the average total assets in Canada of the financial
      institution, ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(a), and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the average total assets in Canada for all
      financial institutions that are banks or authorized foreign banks,
      other than an institution referred to in subsection 3(2),
      ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(a).
  :::

## [Determination of Base Assessment for Trust and Loan Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The base assessment of a
financial institution that is a trust and loan company shall be equal
to, for any fiscal year,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount determined by the formula A × B / C
  is equal to or less than \$1,000, the amount of \$1,000, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the aggregate of \$1,000 and the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#676791 .Paragraph}
  (B - D) × A / E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the average total assets in Canada of the trust and loan
      company, ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(a),

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the expenses that are ascertained pursuant to
      subsection 18(1) of the Act as having been incurred during the
      fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
        attributable to trust and loan companies, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (d) of
        the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
        section 2 of the Act,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the average total assets in Canada for all
      trust and loan companies, other than an institution referred to in
      subsection 3(2), ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(a),

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the product obtained by multiplying \$1,000 by the number of
      trust and loan companies assessed pursuant to subsection 18(3) of
      the Act in respect of that fiscal year, and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the average total assets in Canada,
      ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(a), for each trust and loan
      company, other than an institution referred to in subsection 3(2),
      in respect of which the amount determined by the formula A × B / C
      is greater than \$1,000.
  :::

## [Determination of Base Assessment for Retail Associations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676800 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The base assessment of a
financial institution that is a retail association shall be equal to,
for any fiscal year,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount determined by the formula A × B / C
  is equal to or less than \$1,000, the amount of \$1,000, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the aggregate of \$1,000 and the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#676805 .Paragraph}
  (B - D) × A / E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the average total assets in Canada of the retail association,
      ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(b),

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the expenses that are ascertained pursuant to
      subsection 18(1) of the Act as having been incurred during the
      fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
        attributable to retail associations, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (b) of
        the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
        section 2 of the Act,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the average total assets in Canada for all
      retail associations, other than an institution referred to in
      subsection 3(2), ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(b),

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the product obtained by multiplying \$1,000 by the number of
      retail associations assessed pursuant to subsection 18(3) of the
      Act in respect of that fiscal year, and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the average total assets in Canada,
      ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(b), for each retail
      association, other than an institution referred to in subsection
      3(2), in respect of which the amount determined by the formula A ×
      B / C is greater than \$1,000.
  :::

## [Determination of Base Assessment for Insurance Companies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676814 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The base assessment of a
financial institution that is a life company or a foreign life company
shall be equal to, for any fiscal year,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount determined by the formula A × C / E
  is equal to or less than \$1,000, the amount of \$1,000, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the aggregate of \$1,000 and the amount
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#676819 .Paragraph}
  (C - D) × A / B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the total amount of net premiums received in Canada by the
      financial institution, ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(d),

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined for A, in respect of
      all financial institutions that are life companies or foreign life
      companies, other than an institution referred to in subsection
      3(2), in respect of which the amount determined by the formula A ×
      C / E is greater than \$1,000,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the expenses that are ascertained pursuant to
      subsection 18(1) of the Act as having been incurred during the
      fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of
      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
        attributable to life companies and foreign life companies, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (c) of
        the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
        section 2 of the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
        attributable to life companies and foreign life companies,

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the product obtained by multiplying \$1,000 by the number of
      life companies and foreign life companies assessed pursuant to
      subsection 18(3) of the Act in respect of that fiscal year, and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined for A, in respect of
      all financial institutions that are life companies or foreign life
      companies, other than an institution referred to in subsection
      3(2).
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Green Shield Canada

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of this section, Green Shield Canada shall be considered to be
  a property and casualty company within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
  of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Base assessment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The base assessment of a financial institution that
  is an insurance company, other than one referred to in section 7,
  shall be equal to, for any fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount determined by the formula A × C /
    E is equal to or less than \$1,000, the amount of \$1,000, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} otherwise, the aggregate of \$1,000 and the amount
    determined by the formula

    ::: {#676835 .Paragraph}
    (C - D) × A / B

    where

    [A]{.dfn}
    :   is
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the insurance company is Green Shield
          Canada, the total amount of net revenue received in Canada
          from its prepayment plans other than administrative services
          only plans, ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(c), and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the insurance company is not Green Shield
          Canada or an insurance company referred to in section 7, the
          total amount of net premiums received by it in Canada,
          ascertained pursuant to paragraph 2(d),

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined for A, in respect of
        all financial institutions that are insurance companies, other
        than those referred to in subsection 3(2) and section 7, in
        respect of which the amount determined by the formula A × C / E
        is greater than \$1,000,

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the amount of the expenses that are ascertained pursuant to
        subsection 18(1) of the Act as having been incurred during the
        fiscal year for or in connection with the administration of
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Act, to the extent that those expenses
          are attributable to insurance companies other than ones
          referred to in section 7,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (c)
          of the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
          in section 2 of the Act, to the extent that those expenses are
          attributable to insurance companies other than ones referred
          to in section 7, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the provisions referred to in paragraph (e)
          of the definition [[consumer provision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
          in section 2 of the Act,

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the product obtained by multiplying \$1,000 by the number of
        insurance companies, other than those referred to in section 7,
        assessed pursuant to subsection 18(3) of the Act in respect of
        that fiscal year, and

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the aggregate of the amounts determined in A, in respect of
        all financial institutions that are insurance companies, other
        than those referred to in subsection 3(2) and section 7.
    :::

## [Notice of Assessment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676847 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice in writing

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner shall send
to each financial institution a notice in writing of the assessment
against it.
