## Order Transferring from the President of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada to the President of the Treasury Board the Control and Supervision of the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of Canada and Ordering the President of the Treasury Board to Preside Over the Agency {#order-transferring-from-the-president-of-the-queens-privy-council-for-canada-to-the-president-of-the-treasury-board-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-public-service-human-resources-management-agency-of-canada-and-ordering-the-president-of-the-treasury-board-to-preside-over-the-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-90
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2004-08-11

Order Transferring from the President of the Queen's Privy Council for
Canada to the President of the Treasury Board the Control and
Supervision of the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of
Canada and Ordering the President of the Treasury Board to Preside Over
the Agency

P.C. 2004-854 2004-07-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
  Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers
  from the President of the Queen\'s Privy Council for Canada to the
  President of the Treasury Board the control and supervision of the
  portion of the public service known as the Public Service Human
  Resources Management Agency of Canada, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the President of the Treasury Board shall
  preside over the Public Service Human Resources Management Agency of
  Canada,

effective July 20, 2004.
