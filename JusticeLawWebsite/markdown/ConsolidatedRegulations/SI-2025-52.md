## Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons as the Minister for the Purposes of the Referendum Act {#order-designating-the-leader-of-the-government-in-the-house-of-commons-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-referendum-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-52
:::

[REFERENDUM ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.7)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons
as the Minister for the Purposes of the Referendum Act

P.C. 2025-433 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-83 of February 12,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746879){#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746879-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746879-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746879}SI/2025-13
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Leader of the Government in the House
  of Commons, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of the [Referendum
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4.7)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746995){#fn_81000-3-2859_hq_1741746995-ID0EBBAAA}.
