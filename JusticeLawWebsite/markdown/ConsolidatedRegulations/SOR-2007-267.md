## Interest Rates (Air Travellers Security Charge Act) Regulations {#interest-rates-air-travellers-security-charge-act-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-267
:::

[AIR TRAVELLERS SECURITY CHARGE ACT](/eng/acts/A-10.5)

Registration 2007-11-22

Interest Rates (Air Travellers Security Charge Act) Regulations

P.C. 2007-1774 2007-11-22
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 84(1) and paragraph
84(2)(d) of the [Air Travellers Security Charge
Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Interest Rates (Air Travellers Security Charge Act)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-267).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2002, c.
  9, s. 5
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations

[[basic rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[basic rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a particular
    quarter, means the rate that is the simple arithmetic mean,
    expressed as a percentage per year and rounded to the next higher
    whole percentage where the mean is not a whole percentage, of all
    amounts each of which is the average equivalent yield, expressed as
    a percentage per year, of Government of Canada Treasury bills that
    mature approximately three months after their date of issue and that
    are sold at auctions of Government of Canada Treasury bills during
    the first month of the quarter preceding the particular
    quarter. ([taux de base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any period of three
    consecutive months beginning on January 1, April 1, July 1 or
    October 1. ([trimestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed interest rate

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the [Air
Travellers Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5), the prescribed rate
of interest in effect during a particular quarter that begins after
March 2002 and that ends before April 2007 is the rate (expressed as a
percentage per month and rounded to the nearest one tenth of a
percentage or, if the percentage is equidistant from two consecutive
multiples of one tenth of a percentage, to the higher of them)
determined by the formula

::: {#737121 .Section}
A/12

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the simple arithmetic mean of all amounts, each of which is the
    average equivalent yield (expressed as a percentage per year) of
    Government of Canada Treasury bills that mature approximately three
    months after their date of issue and that were sold at auctions of
    Government of Canada Treasury bills during the first month of the
    immediately preceding quarter.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed interest rates

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the [Air
Travellers Security Charge Act](/eng/acts/A-10.5), the prescribed rate
of interest in effect during a particular quarter that begins after
March 2007 is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid to the Receiver
  General, the sum of the basic rate in respect of the particular
  quarter and 4%;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid or applied on an
  amount payable by the Minister to a person other than a corporation,
  the sum of the basic rate in respect of the particular quarter and 2%;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid or applied on an
  amount payable by the Minister to a corporation, the basic rate in
  respect of the particular quarter.
