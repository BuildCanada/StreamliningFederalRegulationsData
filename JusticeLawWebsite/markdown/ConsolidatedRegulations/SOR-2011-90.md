## Release and Environmental Emergency Notification Regulations {#release-and-environmental-emergency-notification-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-90
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2011-03-25

Release and Environmental Emergency Notification Regulations

P.C. 2011-454 2011-03-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EIADA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EFADA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 19, 2009, a
copy of the proposed [Release and Environmental Emergency Notification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-90), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the proposed Regulations or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 200(1) of that Act, the National
Advisory Committee has been given an opportunity to provide its advice
under section 6[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_ce){#fn_ce-ID0EBACA}
of that Act;

And whereas, in accordance with subsection 209(3) of that Act, the
Minister of the Environment has, before recommending the proposed
Regulations, offered to consult with the governments of territories to
which the proposed Regulations apply and with the members of the
National Advisory Committee who are representatives of aboriginal
governments that have jurisdiction over aboriginal land to which the
proposed Regulations apply;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
paragraph 97(a), subsection 200(1) and paragraph 209(1)(d) of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EDDAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Release and Environmental Emergency Notification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-90).

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraphs 95(1)(a), 169(1)(a), 179(1)(a), 201(1)(a) and
  212(1)(a) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31), the following persons are designated:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those providing 24-hour emergency telephone service
    provided by the office set out in column 2 of the schedule for the
    province, set out in column 1, where the occurrence of the release
    of a substance, the likelihood of such a release, or the occurrence
    of the environmental emergency takes place; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pollution prevention officer referred to in
    paragraph 5(8)(a) or subsection 5(9) of the [Pollutant Discharge
    Reporting Regulations, 1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-351).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person required by paragraph 95(1)(a), 169(1)(a),
  179(1)(a), 201(1)(a) or 212(1)(a) of the [Canadian Environmental
  Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) to provide notification of
  the occurrence of a release of a substance, the likelihood of such a
  release, or the occurrence of an environmental emergency shall, as
  soon as possible in the circumstances, notify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in all cases other than those mentioned in
    paragraph (b), an enforcement officer or a person referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) at the applicable telephone number set out in
    column 3 of the schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the master of a vessel, the owner of
    a vessel or the operator of an oil handling facility to whom the
    [Pollutant Discharge Reporting Regulations,
    1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-351) apply, an enforcement officer or
    the person referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
