## Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons for the Purposes of the Canada Elections Act {#order-designating-the-leader-of-the-government-in-the-house-of-commons-for-the-purposes-of-the-canada-elections-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-53
:::

[CANADA ELECTIONS ACT](/eng/acts/E-2.01)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons
for the Purposes of the Canada Elections Act

P.C. 2025-434 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada
Elections Act](/eng/acts/E-2.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699900){#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699900-ID0EBDAA},

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699900-ID0EBDAA){#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699900}S.C.
  2000, c. 9
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-86 of February 12,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699962){#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699962-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699962-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2823_hq_1738699962}SI/2025-16
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Leader of the Government in the House
  of Commons, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, for the
  purposes of that Act.
