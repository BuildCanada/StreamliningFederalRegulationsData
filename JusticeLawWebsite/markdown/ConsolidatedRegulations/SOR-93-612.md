:::

[EUROPEAN BANK FOR RECONSTRUCTION AND DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
ACT](/eng/acts/E-13.5)

Registration 1993-12-15

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities in Canada of the European
Bank for Reconstruction and Development

P.C. 1993-2114  1993-12-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Secretary of State for External Affairs,
pursuant to section 4 of the [European Bank for Reconstruction and
Development Agreement Act](/eng/acts/E-13.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-612_e_hq_6280){#fn_SOR-93-612_e_hq_6280-ID0EDBAA},

and immunities in Canada of the European Bank for Reconstruction and
Development](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-612).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958427 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Privileges and Immunities of the European Bank for Reconstruction
and Development Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-612).

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958429 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Bank shall have in
Canada the privileges and immunities set out in Chapter VIII of the
Agreement and, in particular,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the status set out in Article 45 of the Agreement;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position with regard to judicial process, as set
  out in Article 46 of the Agreement;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the immunity of its property and assets from seizure,
  as set out in Article 47 of the Agreement;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the inviolability of its archives, as set out in
  Article 48 of the Agreement;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the freedom from restrictions of all property and
  assets to the extent set out in Article 49 of the Agreement;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the same treatment accorded to its official
  communications as is accorded to the official communications of any
  other member, as set out in Article 50 of the Agreement;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the immunities and privileges of its officers and
  employees, as set out in Articles 51 and 52 of the Agreement; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the exemption from direct taxes of its assets,
  property and income, as set out in Article 53 of the Agreement.
