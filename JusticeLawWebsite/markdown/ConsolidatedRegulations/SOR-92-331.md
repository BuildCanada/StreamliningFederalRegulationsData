## Insurance Business (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#insurance-business-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-331
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-05-21

Insurance Business (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations

P.C. 1992-1107  1992-05-21
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 416 of the [Trust and Loan
Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed Regulations respecting the extent to which a company
may undertake the business of insurance and relations between companies
and entities that undertake the business of insurance, insurance agents
and insurance brokers, effective June 1, 1992.

  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 45

:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944730 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit or charge card-related insurance,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} creditors' disability insurance,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creditors' life insurance,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} creditors' loss of employment insurance,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} creditors' vehicle inventory insurance,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} export credit insurance,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} mortgage insurance, or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} travel insurance; ([assurance
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[company web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[company web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a web page that a
    company uses in relation to its business in Canada, including any
    information provided by the company that is accessible on a
    telecommunications device. It does not include a web page that is
    only accessible by employees or agents of the company; ([page Web de
    société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company, means a policy of an insurance company that
    provides insurance to the holder of a credit or charge card issued
    by the company as a feature of the card, without request and without
    an individual assessment of risk,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against loss of, or damage to, goods purchased
      with the card,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which the insurance company undertakes to
      extend a warranty provided by the manufacturer of goods purchased
      with the card, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} against any loss arising from a contractual
      liability assumed by the holder when renting a vehicle, when the
      rental is paid for with the card; ([assurance carte de crédit ou
      de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
    of a company, means a group insurance policy that will pay all or
    part of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the company, or to a
    provincial trust or loan company that is an affiliate of the
    company, in the event of bodily injury to, or an illness or
    disability of,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, any
      director or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the company or to the provincial trust or
      loan company; ([assurance-invalidité de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    company, means a group insurance policy that will pay to the
    company, or to a provincial trust or loan company that is an
    affiliate of the company, all or part of the amount of a debt of a
    debtor or, where a debt is in respect of a small business or a farm,
    fishery or ranch, all or part of the amount of the credit limit of a
    line of credit, in the event of the death of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a director
      or officer of the body corporate, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the company or to the provincial trust or
      loan company; ([assurance-vie de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company, means a policy of an insurance company that
    will pay, without any individual assessment of risk, all or part of
    the amount of a debt of a debtor to the company, or to a provincial
    trust or loan company that is an affiliate of the company, in the
    event that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the debtor, if the debtor is a natural person,
      becomes involuntarily unemployed, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt becomes involuntarily unemployed; ([assurance
      crédit en cas de perte d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of a company, means a policy of an insurance company that
    provides insurance against direct and accidental loss or damage to
    vehicles that are held in stock for display and sale purposes by a
    debtor of the company, some or all of which have been financed by
    the company; ([assurance crédit pour stocks de
    véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to an exporter of goods or
    services against a loss incurred by the exporter due to a
    non-payment for exported goods or services; ([assurance crédit des
    exportateurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    company, means a contract of insurance between an insurance company
    and the company, or between an insurance company and a provincial
    trust or loan company that is an affiliate of the company, that
    provides insurance severally in respect of a group of identifiable
    persons who individually hold certificates of insurance; ([police
    d'assurance collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    approved, registered or otherwise authorized to insure risks under
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province; ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commitment on the
    part of a company to lend to a debtor, without a predetermined
    repayment schedule, one or more amounts, where the aggregate amount
    outstanding does not exceed a predetermined credit limit, which
    limit does not exceed the reasonable credit needs of the debtor;
    ([marge de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a company,
    means an insurance policy that provides insurance to the company, or
    to a provincial trust or loan company that is an affiliate of the
    company, against loss caused by a default on the part of a debtor
    who is a natural person under a loan from the company or from the
    provincial trust or loan company that is secured by a mortgage on
    real property or on an interest in real property; ([assurance
    hypothèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
    insurance policy that provides insurance to a natural person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay
      one or more sums of money in the event of bodily injury to, or the
      death of, the person that is caused by an accident, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay a
      certain sum for each day that the person is hospitalized in the
      event of bodily injury to the person that is caused by an accident
      or in the event of an illness or disability of the person;
      ([assurance accidents corporels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial trust or loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial trust or loan company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    trust corporation or loan corporation incorporated by or under an
    Act of the legislature of a province; ([société de fiducie ou de
    prêt provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business that is or,
    if it were incorporated, would be a small business corporation
    within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a policy of an insurance company that provides
      insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
      away from the place where the person ordinarily resides, without
      any individual assessment of risk, against

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss that results from the cancellation or
        interruption of the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss of, or damage to, personal property that
        occurs while on the trip, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} loss that is caused by the delayed arrival of
        personal baggage while on the trip, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group insurance policy that provides insurance
      to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person away from
      the province in which the person ordinarily resides

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from an illness or the disability of the person that
        occurs on the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from bodily injury to, or the death of, the person
        caused by an accident while on the trip,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to
        pay one or more sums of money in the event of an illness or the
        disability of the person that occurs on the trip, or of bodily
        injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused by an
        accident while on the trip,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred by the person for
        dental care necessitated by an accident while on the trip, or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the person dies while on the
        trip, against expenses incurred for the return of that person's
        remains to the place where the person was ordinarily resident
        before death, or for travel expenses incurred by a relative of
        that person who must travel to identify that person's remains.
        ([assurance voyage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-195, s. 1
- SOR/2002-270, s. 3
- SOR/2011-186, s. 1
:::

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
these Regulations, a web page is not a company web page by reason only
that the web page provides access to a company web page or promotes the
business of a company in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-186, s. 2
:::

## [Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944787 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A company may carry on any
aspect of the business of insurance, other than the underwriting of
insurance, outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-186, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company may administer an authorized type of insurance and personal
  accident insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company may administer a group insurance policy for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its employees or the employees of an entity that
    the company holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    section 453 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employees of another company that controls the
    company or of an entity that the other company holds control of, or
    a substantial investment in, under section 453 of the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employees of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a bank or a bank holding company, that controls
      the company, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an entity that the bank or the bank holding
      company, referred to in subparagraph (i), holds control of, or a
      substantial investment in, under section 468 or 930 of the [Bank
      Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the employees of an entity that an affiliate of the
    company holds control of, or a substantial investment in, in
    accordance with section 522.07 or 522.08 or subsection 522.32(1)
    or (3) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the employees in Canada of an authorized foreign
    bank that is an affiliate of the company; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the company is controlled by a foreign bank, the
    employees in Canada of an affiliate of the foreign bank that is an
    entity referred to in section 522.17 or 522.18 of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and that carries on a business described in
    one of those sections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-270, s. 4
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company may provide advice regarding an authorized type of insurance
  or a service in respect of an authorized type of insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company may provide advice in respect of an
  insurance policy that is not of an authorized type of insurance, or
  services in respect thereof, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advice is general in nature;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advice is not in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a specific risk, proposal in respect of life
      insurance, insurance policy or service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a particular insurance company, agent or broker;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the company does not thereby refer any person to a
    particular insurance company, agent or broker.

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944812 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A company shall not, in
relation to its business in Canada, promote an insurance company, agent
or broker unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker deals only in
  authorized types of insurance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
  company and is directed to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued by
    the company who regularly receive statements of account or notices
    of those statements,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the company's customers who are natural
    persons and who regularly receive statements of account or notices
    of those statements, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-186, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-6-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 6"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall not, in relation to its business in Canada, promote an
  insurance policy of an insurance company, agent or broker, or a
  service in respect of such a policy, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policy is of an authorized type of insurance or
    the service is in respect of such a policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is to be provided by a corporation
    without share capital, other than a mutual insurance company or a
    fraternal benefit society, that carries on business without
    pecuniary gain to its members and the policy provides insurance to a
    natural person in respect of the risks covered by travel insurance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the policy is a personal accident insurance policy
    and the promotion takes place outside a branch of the company;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the service is in respect of a policy referred to
    in paragraph (b) or of a policy referred to in paragraph (c) that is
    promoted as described in that paragraph; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
    company and is directed to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued
      by the company who regularly receive statements of account or
      notices of those statements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the company's customers who are natural
      persons and who regularly receive statements of account or notices
      of those statements, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) and section 6, a
  company may exclude from a promotion referred to in paragraph (1)(e)
  or 6(b) persons

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the promotion would contravene
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who have notified the company in writing that they
    do not wish to receive promotional material from the company; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who are holders of a credit or charge card that was
    issued by the company and in respect of which the account is not in
    good standing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-171, s. 7(E)
- SOR/2011-186, s. 5
:::

## [Web Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944837 .Part}

- **[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  promotion referred to in paragraph 6(b) may take place on a company
  web page if it relates to an insurance company, agent or broker that
  deals only in authorized types of insurance and the promotion referred
  to in paragraph 7(1)(c) or (e) may take place on a company web page if
  it relates to only an authorized type of insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a company shall not, on a company web page,
  provide access to a web page --- directly or through another web page
  --- through which there is promotion of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company, agent or broker that does not
    deal only in authorized types of insurance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent
    or broker, or a service in respect of such a policy, that is not of
    only an authorized type of insurance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-186, s. 6
:::

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944845 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, an insurance
    company, agent or broker with any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the company in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the company in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit any of its subsidiaries to provide, directly
    or indirectly, an insurance company, agent or broker with any
    information that it receives from the company respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the company in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the company in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} provide an affiliate of the company that is not a
    subsidiary of the company with information unless the company
    ensures that the affiliate does not directly or indirectly provide
    the information to an insurance company, agent or broker if the
    information is in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the company in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the company in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the company is a partnership
      with partners in Canada, any such partner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a subsidiary of the company that is a
    provincial trust or loan company to provide, directly or indirectly,
    an insurance company, agent or broker with any information
    respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the subsidiary in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the subsidiary in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is a
      partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a company
  or a subsidiary of a company that is a provincial trust or loan
  company where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company or the subsidiary has established
    procedures to ensure that the information referred to in that
    subsection will not be used by an insurance company, agent or broker
    to promote in Canada the insurance company, agent or broker or to
    promote in Canada an insurance policy, or a service in respect
    thereof; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, as the case
    may be, has given an undertaking to the company or to the
    subsidiary, in a form acceptable to the Superintendent, that that
    information will not be used to promote in Canada the insurance
    company, agent or broker or to promote in Canada an insurance
    policy, or a service in respect thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-270, s. 5
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No company shall provide a
telecommunications device that is primarily for the use of customers in
Canada and that links a customer with an insurance company, agent or
broker.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A company shall not carry
on business in Canada on premises that are adjacent to the premises of
an insurance company, agent or broker unless the company clearly
indicates to its customers that its business and the premises on which
its business is carried on are separate and distinct from those of the
insurance company, agent or broker.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-186, s. 7
:::

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944877 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where a company
administered a policy on May 6, 1992 the administration of which is not
permitted under section 4, the company may continue to administer the
policy in respect of any person in respect of whom coverage was provided
under the policy on that date.
