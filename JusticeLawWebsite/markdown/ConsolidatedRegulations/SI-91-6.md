## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Canadian Space Agency Act, the Canadian Space Agency as a Department, and the President of the Agency as Deputy Head {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-canadian-space-agency-act-the-canadian-space-agency-as-a-department-and-the-president-of-the-agency-as-deputy-head .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-6
:::

[CANADIAN SPACE AGENCY ACT](/eng/acts/C-23.2)

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 1991-01-02

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as
Minister for Purposes of the Canadian Space Agency Act, the Canadian
Space Agency as a Department, and the President of the Agency as Deputy
Head

P.C. 1990-2712  1990-12-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby, effective December 14, 1990,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canadian Space
  Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-23.2)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-6_e_hq_6473){#fn_SI-91-6_e_hq_6473-ID0EBBEAA},
  to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a
  member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for
  the purposes of that Act;

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-91-6_e_hq_6473-ID0EBBEAA){#fn_SI-91-6_e_hq_6473}R.S.
    1990, c. 13.
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the Canadian Space
  Agency as a department for the purposes of that Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to designate the
  Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as the appropriate
  Minister with respect to the Canadian Space Agency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to designate the Canadian
  Space Agency as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to designate the President of the
  Canadian Space Agency as the deputy head of the Canadian Space Agency
  for the purposes of that Act.
