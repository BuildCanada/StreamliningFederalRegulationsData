## Coral Harbour Airport Zoning Regulations {#coral-harbour-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-68
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1992-01-23

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Coral Harbour Airport

P.C. 1992-67 1992-01-23
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Coral Harbour Airport substantially in the form set out in
the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on March 9th and
16th, 1991 and in two successive issues of News/North on June 24th and
July 1st, 1991 and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to
interested persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport
with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Coral Harbour
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-68).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949699 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Coral Harbour Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-68).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949701 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Coral Harbour Airport,
      in the vicinity of Coral Harbour, in the Northwest Territories;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 56 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949711 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described in
Part VI of the schedule.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949713 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Coral Harbour Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2730 dated June 16, 1989, is a point located on the centre line of
runway 155T-335T distant 762 m from the threshold of runway 335T.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Coral Harbour Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2730 dated June 16, 1989, are surfaces abutting each end of the
strip associated with the runway designated 155T-335T, and are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 155T consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 335T consisting of an inclined plane having a
  ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 40 m measured horizontally rising
  to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 525 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 75 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Coral Harbour Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2730
dated June 16, 1989, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the
airport reference point, except that, where that common plane is less
than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is an
imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strip .SchedHeadL1}

The strip associated with runway 155T-335T, shown on Coral Harbour
Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2730 dated June 16, 1989, is 150 m in width,
75 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and 1 644 m in
length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Coral Harbour Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2730 dated June 16, 1989, is a surface consisting of an inclined
plane rising at a ratio of 1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured
horizontally at right angles to the centre line and projected centre
line of the strip and extending upward and outward from the lateral
limits of the strip and its approach surfaces to an intersection with
the outer surface.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Lands to Which These Regulations Apply]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-lands-to-which-these-regulations-apply .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits of lands, shown on Coral Harbour
Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2730 dated June 16, 1989, is a circle with a
radius of 4 000 m centered on the airport reference point.
