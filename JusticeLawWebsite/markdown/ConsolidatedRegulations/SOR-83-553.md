## Privacy Act Extension Order No. 1 {#privacy-act-extension-order-no.-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/83-553
:::

[PRIVACY ACT](/eng/acts/P-21)

Registration 1983-06-24

Order Extending the Right To Be Given Access to Personal Information
under Subsection 12(1) of the Privacy Act

P.C. 1983-1836 1983-06-23
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, is pleased hereby, pursuant to subsection 12(3)
of the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_star_e){#fn_star_e-ID0EDBAA}, to make the
annexed [Order extending the right to be given access to personal
information under subsection 12(1) of the Privacy
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-553).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878813 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Privacy Act Extension Order No. 1](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-553).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-878815 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The right to be given
access to personal information under subsection 12(1) of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21) is extended to include an
[[inmate]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
[Corrections and Conditional Release Act](/eng/acts/C-44.6), who is not
a Canadian citizen or a [[permanent resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5).
