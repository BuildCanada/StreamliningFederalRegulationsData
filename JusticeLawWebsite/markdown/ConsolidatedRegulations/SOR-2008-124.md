## Bowie Seamount Marine Protected Area Regulations {#bowie-seamount-marine-protected-area-regulations .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743677 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Bowie Seamount
  Marine Protected Area designated under section 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD 83) geodetic reference system.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the schedule, the lines connecting the points are
  rhumb lines.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743682 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The area of the Pacific
Ocean --- consisting of the seabed, the subsoil and the water column
above the seabed --- that is bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn
from a point 53º03′07.6″ N, 135º50′25.9″ W, to a point 53º16′20.9″ N,
134º59′55.4″ W, then to a point 53º39′49.2″ N, 135º17′04.9″ W, then to a
point 53º39′18.0″ N, 135º53′46.5″ W, then to a point 53º52′16.7″ N,
136º30′23.1″ W, then to a point 53º49′19.6″ N, 136º47′33.1″ W, then to a
point 53º40′02.5″ N, 136º57′03.5″ W, then to a point 53º13′59.2″ N,
136º10′00.0″ W, then back to the point of commencement (which points are
shown as points 1 to 8, respectively, of the schedule) is designated as
the Bowie Seamount Marine Protected Area.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743684 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In the Area, no person
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy, or remove from the Area,
  any living marine organism or any part of its habitat;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} disturb, damage or destroy or remove from the Area,
  any part of the seabed; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity --- including depositing,
  discharging or dumping any substance, or causing any substance to be
  deposited, discharged or dumped --- that is likely to result in the
  disturbance, damage, destruction or removal of a living marine
  organism or any part of its habitat.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-39, s. 27(F)

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743691 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3, the
following activities may be carried out in the Area:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} commercial fishing that is carried out in accordance
  with the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and its regulations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} recreational fishing that is carried out in
  accordance with the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and its
  regulations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fishing that is carried out in accordance with the
  [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} vessel travel carried out in accordance with the
  [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and its regulations;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} foreign ship travel carried out in accordance with
  the [Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3) and its regulations;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any movement or other activity of a ship, submarine
  or aircraft if the movement or other activity is carried out for the
  purpose of public safety, law enforcement or national security or for
  the exercise of Canadian sovereignty and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the ship, submarine or aircraft is owned or
    operated by or on behalf of Her Majesty in right of Canada or by a
    foreign military force acting in cooperation with, or under the
    command or control of, the Canadian Forces; or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the movement or other activity is carried out for
    the purpose of an emergency response under the direction, command or
    control of the Canadian Coast Guard;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} marine scientific research activities that are
  carried out or sponsored by a foreign government and in respect of
  which that government has received the consent of the Minister of
  Foreign Affairs under paragraph 3(2)(c) of the [Coasting Trade
  Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3), if the activities are carried out in a manner
  that complies with the terms and conditions of the consent; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an activity approved under section 6.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-39, s. 28(F)
- SOR/2017-58, s. 54

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743705 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every person who proposes
to carry out a scientific research, monitoring, educational or
commercial marine tourism activity in the Area shall submit to the
Minister for approval, not less than 60 days before the day on which the
activity is proposed to begin, a plan that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number and, if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of a
  person who can be contacted in respect of the plan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity that
  sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its purpose,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the period or periods during which it is to be
    carried out,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its location on a map;

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the types of data that are to be collected, if
    any, and the sampling protocols or other techniques to be used to
    collect the data,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the types of equipment, if any, that are to be used
    during the proposed activity, including those for gathering data
    and, if any of the equipment is to be anchored or moored in the
    Area, the methods by which the anchoring or mooring is to be
    conducted,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the type and identity of every ship that is to be
    used to carry out the proposed activity, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} every substance, if any, that is to be deposited,
    discharged or dumped within the Area during the proposed activity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the environmental effects that are
  likely to occur within the Area as a result of the proposed activity;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a list of every licence, permit, authorization or
  consent obtained or applied for in respect of the proposed activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-39, s. 29(F)
:::

Version](section-5-20080417.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister shall, within 30 days after the day on
  which a plan that is submitted in accordance with section 5 is
  received, approve the plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of scientific research and monitoring,
    the activity is for the purpose of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} understanding the ecology of the Area,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contributing to the management of the Area, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} monitoring the effectiveness of the
      conservation measures being implemented within the Area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of educational activities or commercial
    marine tourism activities, the activity is for the purpose of
    increasing public awareness of the Area.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not approve a plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the activity is likely to damage or destroy the
    habitat of a living marine organism within the Area; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
    activities carried out within the Area, are likely to damage or
    destroy the habitat of living marine organisms in the Area.

## [Reporting of Accidents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743732 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every person involved in an
accident that is likely to result in any disturbance, damage,
destruction or removal prohibited under section 3 shall, within two
hours after its occurrence, report the accident to the Canadian Coast
Guard.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743734 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
