Softwood Lumber Products Charge on Duty Deposit Refunds Remission Order,

P.C. 2007-105 2007-01-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue and the Minister for International Trade, pursuant
to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote

Remission Order, No. 2](/eng/regulations/SI-2007-14).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Softwood Lumber Products
    Export Charge Act, 2006. ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[duty deposit refund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[duty deposit refund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 18(1) of the Act. ([remboursement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[specified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[specified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 18(1) of the Act. ([intéressé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a specified person of that portion of the charge paid or payable by
the person under section 18 of the Act on the amount of a duty deposit
refund that relates to interest that accrued after October 11, 2006, and
any interest paid or payable in respect of that portion of the charge,
if the person does not sell their rights to the duty deposit refund by
agreement to Her Majesty in right of Canada.
