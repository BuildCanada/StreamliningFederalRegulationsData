## Electrolux Remission Order {#electrolux-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-59
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2005-06-29

Electrolux Remission Order

P.C. 2005-1137 2005-06-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of certain amounts of duty is unjust, on the recommendation
of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Electrolux Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2005-59).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611918 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[eligible dryers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible dryers]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means gas or electric
    laundry dryers, excluding those with controls at the front,
    removable tops and chassis designed to be stacked on top of washers,
    imported by Electrolux. ([sécheuses admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible refrigerators]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible refrigerators]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means top-mounted
    electric refrigerators, in sizes greater than 14.5 cubic feet
    (410.59 litres) and less than 18.5 cubic feet (524 litres), imported
    by Electrolux. ([réfrigérateurs admissibles]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[imported by Electrolux]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[imported by Electrolux]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means imported by
    Electrolux Canada Corp. (formerly known as WCI Canada Inc.),
    originating in or exported from the United States and bearing a
    trademark owned by Electrolux Home Products, Inc. (formerly known as
    White Consolidated Industries, Inc.), its affiliates, successors, or
    assigns. ([importé par Electrolux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611923 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of anti-dumping duties paid under the [Special Import Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-15) by Electrolux Canada Corp. on importations of
eligible refrigerators and eligible dryers during the period beginning
on April 3, 2000, and ending on October 31, 2001.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of the remission
is the portion of the anti-dumping duties paid that exceeds the margin
of dumping of the imported goods calculated on the basis of the
methodology employed in the re-determinations made by the Commissioner
of Customs and Revenue on December 5, 2003 pursuant to paragraph
59(1)(e) of the [Special Import Measures Act](/eng/acts/S-15) with
respect to certain eligible refrigerators and certain eligible dryers
imported after October 31, 2001.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611926 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on the
conditions that the claim for remission

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is made to the Canada Border Services Agency within
  two years after the date on which this Order is made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is accompanied by the documentation required to
  determine the eligiblity for remission and the specific amount of
  anti-dumping duties to be remitted.
