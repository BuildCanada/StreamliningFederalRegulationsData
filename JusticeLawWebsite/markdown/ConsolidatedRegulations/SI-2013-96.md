## Transfer of Duties Order (International Experience Canada Program Unit) {#transfer-of-duties-order-international-experience-canada-program-unit .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2013-96
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2013-09-11

Transfer of Duties Order (International Experience Canada Program Unit)

P.C. 2013-904 2013-08-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Department of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Development
to the Department of Citizenship and Immigration the control and
supervision of the portion of the federal public administration, in the
Global Business Opportunities Bureau, known as the International
Experience Canada Program Unit, effective August 31, 2013.
