## Canada Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification Regulations {#canada-travelling-exhibitions-indemnification-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-467
:::

[CANADA TRAVELLING EXHIBITIONS INDEMNIFICATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.5)

Registration 1999-12-09

Canada Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification Regulations

P.C. 1999-2163 1999-12-09
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 5 of the [Canada
Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification Act](/eng/acts/C-10.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Canada Travelling Exhibitions Indemnification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-467).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  29

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032418 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Travelling Exhibitions
    Indemnification Act](/eng/acts/C-10.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an institution situated in
    Canada that organizes or hosts a travelling exhibition, and that
    applies for an indemnity agreement on its behalf, or on behalf of
    all institutions that host the exhibition in Canada;
    ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indemnity agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indemnity agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    entered into under subsection 3(1) of the Act; ([accord
    d'indemnisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[owner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the individual, corporation,
    partnership, association, trust, government, or other entity that
    holds full and unconditional title to an object or appurtenance, or
    their designated representative; ([propriétaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application for Indemnity Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032424 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may seek an
indemnity agreement in respect of objects and appurtenances in a
travelling exhibition if the minimum total fair market value of all
objects and appurtenances in the exhibition is \$500,000.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An applicant who seeks an
indemnity agreement shall submit an application to the Minister that
sets out the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title, schedule, list of venues and an abstract
  or summary of the travelling exhibition in respect of which an
  indemnity agreement is sought, and the name of its project director
  with a list of his or her professional qualifications;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of each object and appurtenance to be
  indemnified, including its

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} name,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} creation date or age,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} material or medium, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} dimensions;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the owner of each object and
  appurtenance to be indemnified, and written evidence from each owner
  that they are willing to lend the object or appurtenance to the
  proposed travelling exhibition and are prepared to be bound by the
  terms of an indemnity agreement;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value of each object and appurtenance
  to be indemnified and the source and justification of the fair market
  value;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the total fair market value of all objects and
  appurtenances in the travelling exhibition;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a description of any other insurance or
  indemnification coverage that will be applicable to the objects or
  appurtenances to be indemnified during any period in respect of which
  an indemnity agreement is sought;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of any loss or damage that occurred at
  the premises of the applicant or other institution hosting the
  travelling exhibition that involved an object or appurtenance during
  the three years before the application, if the amount of loss or
  damage was more than \$5,000, including the date of loss or damage,
  the nature and cause of the loss or damage, and the fair market value
  of each lost or damaged object or appurtenance both before and after
  the loss or damage;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} information that shows how the travelling exhibition
  would meet the assessment criteria set out in section 5; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other information that is relevant in order to
  allow the Minister to determine whether to enter into an indemnity
  agreement.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may have the
fair market value of an object or appurtenance that is the subject of an
application for an indemnity agreement reviewed, in which case the
procedure set out in section 14 shall apply, with any modifications that
the circumstances require.

## [Assessment Criteria]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032441 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall apply
the following criteria when assessing whether to enter into an indemnity
agreement in respect of an object or appurtenance:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the theme and contents of the travelling
  exhibition are Canadian, they must enable present and future
  generations of Canadians to be exposed to their human, natural,
  artistic and scientific heritage, and thereby enhance their awareness,
  understanding, and enjoyment of the richness of that heritage;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when the theme and contents of the travelling
  exhibition are of a global significance, the travelling exhibition
  must display objects of cultural property that are of significance in
  the areas of archaeology, prehistory, history, sociology, ethnography,
  art or science;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the travelling exhibition must maintain high
  museological standards;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the travelling exhibition must further knowledge
  concerning the theme or material presented;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} public awareness of and access to the travelling
  exhibition must be maximized at each venue;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the risk of damage to the object or appurtenance,
  because of its size or the fragile nature of its composition or
  material, must be minimal;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the object or appurtenance must be transported by
  appropriate means of transportation and by competent shippers, in
  order to minimize the risk of loss or damage;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the object or appurtenance must be packed and crated
  in such a manner as to withstand the normal hazards associated with
  the applicable means of transportation, and must be packed, unpacked
  and installed by competent professionals;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the facilities of all institutions hosting the
  travelling exhibition must meet museum industry standards with regard
  to arrangements for security, fire protection and collections
  preservation, including environmental control;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} appropriate arrangements must be made with respect to
  enhanced security measures that are necessary for the object or
  appurtenance while on display, in storage and in transit;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} when the object or appurtenance has particular
  environmental requirements, it must be subject to appropriate
  environmental control, including lighting, temperature, relative
  humidity and shock or vibration, while on display, in storage and in
  transit; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the object or appurtenance must be safe and secure
  while on display, in storage and in transit.

## [Scope of Indemnity Agreements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032455 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An indemnity agreement
shall apply only in respect of the portion of the fair market value of
an object or appurtenance that is not insured or indemnified, during the
period in which the agreement is in effect, by other insurance or
indemnification coverage not provided for under these Regulations.

## [Deductible]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032457 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The deductible amount of
indemnity coverage for each travelling exhibition, based on the total
fair market value of all the objects and appurtenances in it, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$30,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$500,000 to \$3,000,000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$40,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$3,000,001 to \$10,000,000;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} \$50,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$10,000,001 to \$50,000,000;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} \$75,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$50,000,001 to \$100,000,000;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} \$200,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$100,000,001 to \$200,000,000;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} \$300,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$200,000,001 to \$300,000,000;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} \$500,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$300,000,001 to \$450,000,000; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} \$700,000, if the total fair market value is from
  \$450,000,001 to \$600,000,000.

## [Maximum Coverage for Single Conveyance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032469 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Indemnity coverage for
objects and appurtenances transported in a single conveyance may not
exceed \$100,000,000.

## [Period of Coverage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032471 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The period of coverage in
an indemnity agreement shall not exceed two years, but the Minister may
extend the period for an additional year if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an extension of the loan of an object or appurtenance
  is necessary to accommodate additional venues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} delays in transit occur that are beyond the control
  of the applicant; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has undertaken to restore a damaged
  object or appurtenance under subparagraph 12(b)(ii).

## [Condition Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032476 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Within 15 days after the
applicant assumes responsibility for the safeguarding of an object or
appurtenance that is the subject of an indemnity agreement, the
applicant shall file with the Minister a condition report describing the
physical condition of the object or appurtenance as of the time that the
applicant accepts responsibility for its safeguarding.

## [Indemnification Claims Procedure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032480 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  event of loss or damage to an object or appurtenance that is the
  subject of an indemnity agreement, the owner

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall notify the Minister within two business days
    after the discovery of the loss or damage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may, where the owner is seeking to be indemnified
    under an indemnity agreement, file with the Minister a claim in
    writing within 90 days after the date specified in an indemnity
    agreement by which an object or appurtenance is to be returned to
    the place designated by the owner, or within 60 days after the date
    on which the object or appurtenance is so returned, whichever date
    is earlier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The claim must be accompanied by written proof of the
  loss or damage that is supported by a statutory declaration that
  includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a complete and detailed inventory of the lost or
    damaged object or appurtenance with detailed information regarding
    its fair market value before and after the loss or damage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of the object or appurtenance at the
    time of the loss or damage;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} when and how the loss or damage occurred, and if it
    was caused by fire or explosion, how the fire or explosion started,
    as far as the owner knows or believes;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the loss or damage did not occur
    through any wilful act or negligence of the owner or with the
    owner's connivance;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all condition reports that, under the indemnity
    agreement, have been completed in respect of the object or
    appurtenance throughout the travelling exhibition; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other documentation or information that is
    relevant in order to allow the Minister to determine the validity of
    the claim.

## [Validity of Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032492 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister
determines that a claim is valid and that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loss is total, the Minister shall pay the amount
  of the claim to the owner in accordance with the terms of the
  indemnity agreement; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the loss or damage is partial and the Minister agrees
  with the consequent reduction in the fair market value of the object
  or appurtenance as set out in the claim, the Minister shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pay the amount of the claim to the owner, in
    accordance with the terms of the indemnity agreement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} subject to the written agreement of the owner,
    have the object or appurtenance restored to the condition described
    in the report referred to in section 10.

<!-- -->

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines that a claim is not valid, the Minister shall
  advise the owner of the object or appurtenance of this in writing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An owner may have the determination of the Minister
  reviewed by a competent and disinterested arbitrator selected jointly
  by the Minister and the owner.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator's determination is final and binding
  on the parties.

## [Disagreement on Fair Market Value]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032502 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister and the owner fail to agree on the extent of the partial loss
  or damage to, or on any consequent reduction in the fair market value
  of, the object or appurtenance, each shall select a competent
  appraiser or appraisers who shall independently consider the extent of
  the partial loss or damage and each determine any consequent reduction
  in the fair market value of the object or appurtenance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister and the owner agree on the extent of
  the partial loss or damage and the consequent reduction in the fair
  market value of an object or appurtenance, based on the determinations
  of the appraisers made under subsection (1), the Minister shall act in
  accordance with subparagraph 12(b)(i) or (ii).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister and the owner continue to disagree on
  the extent of the partial loss or damage and the consequent reduction
  in the fair market value of an object or appurtenance the Minister and
  owner shall jointly select a competent and disinterested arbitrator to
  determine the matter.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator's determination is final and binding
  on the parties.

## [Costs of Appraisal and Arbitration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032508 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  appraiser and all expenses incurred during the preparation of an
  appraisal shall be paid by the party selecting him or her.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The arbitrator and all expenses incurred during the
  preparation of an arbitration shall be paid by the parties in equal
  shares.

## [Subrogation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032512 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** After compensation for
loss or damage has been paid under these Regulations, the Minister shall
be subrogated to all the owner's rights of recovery against any person
or entity.

## [Buy-Back]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1032514 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  compensation has been paid under these Regulations in respect of an
  indemnified object or appurtenance that was lost and then recovered,
  the object or appurtenance becomes the property of the Minister on its
  recovery.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner may buy back the object or appurtenance and
  if the owner does so, its price shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is not damaged, the amount of compensation
    paid to the owner under these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is damaged, the fair market value of the
    object or appurtenance in its damaged state.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister and the owner do not agree on the
  fair market value of an object or appurtenance in its damaged state,
  the procedure set out in section 14 shall apply, with any
  modifications that the circumstances require.
