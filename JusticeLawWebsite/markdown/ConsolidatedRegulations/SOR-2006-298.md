## Virtual Elimination List {#virtual-elimination-list .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2006-298
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2006-11-24

Virtual Elimination List
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1598){#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1598-ID0EICBA}
of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599){#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599-ID0EFCBA},
the Minister of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on August 16, 2003 a copy
of the proposed [Virtual Elimination
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-298), substantially in the form set out
in the annexed List, and persons were given an opportunity to file
comments with respect to the proposed List or to file a notice of
objection requesting that a board of review be established and stating
the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1598-ID0EICBA){#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1598}S.C.
  2004, c. 15, s. 31
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599-ID0EFCBA){#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health,
pursuant to subsection 65(2) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection
Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599){#fn_SOR-2006-298_e_hq_1599-ID0EDBAA},
hereby compile the annexed [Virtual Elimination
List](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-298), effective on the date on which it
is registered.

+-----------------------------+---------------------------+
| Ottawa, August 21, 2006     | Ottawa, November 22, 2006 |
|                             |                           |
| Rona Ambrose                | Tony Clement              |
|                             |                           |
| Minister of the Environment | Minister of Health        |
+-----------------------------+---------------------------+
