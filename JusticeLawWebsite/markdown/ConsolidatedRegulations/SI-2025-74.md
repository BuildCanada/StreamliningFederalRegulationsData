## Order Designating the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to Provide Support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience {#order-designating-the-department-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-provide-support-to-the-minister-of-emergency-management-and-community-resilience .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-74
:::

[SALARIES ACT](/eng/acts/S-3)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Designating the Department of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to Provide Support to the Minister of Emergency Management
and Community Resilience

P.C. 2025-480 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under subsection 5(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835572){#fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835572-ID0EECAA}
of the [Salaries Act](/eng/acts/S-3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835594){#fn_81000-3-2906_hq_1746835594-ID0EBCAA},
designates the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to
provide support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community
Resilience in the carrying out of that Minister's responsibilities.
