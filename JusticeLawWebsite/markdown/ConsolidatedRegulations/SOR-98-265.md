## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021251 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Permit.

[[CWC toxic chemicals and precursors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[CWC toxic chemicals and precursors]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    goods referred to in Group 7 of the schedule to the [Export Control
    List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202) that are described in any of
    items 7-3.3 to 7-3.6 of the Guide. ([produits chimiques toxiques et
    précurseurs CAC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Export Controls Division]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Export
    Controls Division of the Department of Foreign Affairs and
    International Trade. ([Direction des contrôles à
    l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 1
    of the [Export Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-202).
    ([Guide]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021258 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3, this
Permit authorizes the exportation of mixtures of CWC toxic chemicals and
precursors to any country other than to a country listed on the [Area
Control List](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-543) or to Iraq or North Korea.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021260 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada may,
under the authority of and in accordance with this Permit, export from
Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} mixtures that contain less than 10 per cent by weight
  of any CWC toxic chemical or precursor described in item 7-3.3 or
  7-3.4 of the Guide; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mixtures that contain less than 25 per cent by weight
  of any CWC toxic chemical or precursor described in item 7-3.5 or
  7-3.6 of the Guide.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021266 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is a condition of this
Permit that the exporter

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep at the exporter's place of business or residence
  any documents in respect of each export made under this Permit for a
  period of six years after the date of the export;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, make the documents referred to in
  paragraph (a) available to an officer of the Export Controls Division;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where mixtures of CWC toxic chemicals and precursors
  are required to be reported in a prescribed form under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), insert the expression "GEP-38" or "LGE-38" in
  the appropriate field of the prescribed form.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1021271 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Permit comes into
force on April 23, 1998.
