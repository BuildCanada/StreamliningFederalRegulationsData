## Order Designating the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for the Queens Quay West Land Corporation for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-transport-as-appropriate-minister-for-the-queens-quay-west-land-corporation-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-26
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Designating the Minister of Transport as Appropriate Minister for
the Queens Quay West Land Corporation for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 2006-65 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), and pursuant to
subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 83(1) of that Act, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2004-871 of July 20,
  2004[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBBA}, and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EBBBBA){#footnotea_e}SI/2004-104
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Transport, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister for the
  Queens Quay West Land Corporation for the purposes of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),

effective February 6, 2006.
