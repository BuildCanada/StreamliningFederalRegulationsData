## Indian Reserve Traffic Regulations {#indian-reserve-traffic-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 959
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Regulations Governing the Operation of Vehicles within Indian Reserves
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599769 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Indian Reserve Traffic
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._959).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599771 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[road]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any roadway, driveway, street,
    lane or other place open to the public for the passage of vehicles;
    ([chemin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any wagon, cart, motor car,
    motor truck, trailer, motorcycle, traction engine, tractor,
    road-making machinery or other conveyance that is driven, propelled
    or drawn by any kind of power. ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599775 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply on
all roads within Indian reserves.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-599777 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  driver of any vehicle shall bring such vehicle to a full stop when
  ordered to do so by any person authorized by the Minister of Indian
  Affairs and Northern Development to enforce these Regulations, and
  shall obey all directions issued by such authorized person in respect
  of the routing or control of traffic, including the parking of
  vehicles.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The driver of any vehicle shall comply with the
  direction of any mechanical or other device or sign installed for the
  control or routing of traffic.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The driver of any vehicle shall not drive such
  vehicle over any road the use of which for public traffic is
  prohibited by any sign or other device.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The person in charge of any
vehicle shall not drive or ride such vehicle at any rate of speed that
is excessive or dangerous, having regard to the conditions then
prevailing, and the person shall keep the vehicle in such control when
approaching a road intersection, or crossing for pedestrians or other
purposes, as will enable him to prevent a collision with, or damage to,
all other persons and vehicles.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The driver of any vehicle
shall comply with all laws and regulations relating to motor vehicles,
which are in force from time to time in the province in which the Indian
reserve is situated, except such laws or regulations as are inconsistent
with these Regulations.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person shall park or
station any vehicle upon any road unless permission to do so is
designated by signs erected over or marked on the roadway.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No vehicle in a dangerous
or unsafe condition shall be operated on any road.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any person who violates any
of the provisions of these Regulations shall be guilty of an offence and
shall be liable, upon summary conviction, to a penalty of not less than
\$1 and not more than \$50, or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding
two months.
