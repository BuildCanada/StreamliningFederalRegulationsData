## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1497649 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Head of government institution

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The person holding a
position set out in column 2 of the schedule is designated, for the
purposes of the [Access to Information Act](/eng/acts/A-1), as the head
of the government institution set out in column 1.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1497652 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [Access to Information
Act Heads of Government Institutions Designation
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-83-113)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1719410079){#fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1719410079-ID0EBBA}
is repealed.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1719410079-ID0EBBA){#fn_81000-2-5350_hq_1719410079}SI/83-113
  :::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1497654 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}30th day after making

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the 30th day after the day on which it is made.
