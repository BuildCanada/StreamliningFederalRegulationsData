## Order Transferring to the Minister of Health the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food and from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency to the Public Health Agency of Canada the control and supervision of that portion of the Federal Public Administration known as the Domestic Terrestrial Animal Pathogen Unit {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-health-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-agriculture-and-agri-food-and-from-the-canadian-food-inspection-agency-to-the-public-health-agency-of-canada-the-control-and-supervision-of-that-portion-of-the-federal-public-administration-known-as-the-domestic-terrestrial-animal-pathogen-unit .Title-of-Act}

Order Transferring to the Minister of Health the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food and from the

::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEFAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBFAA},
transfers

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEFAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7867}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBFAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7868}R.S., c.
  P-34

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Health the powers, duties and
  functions of the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food set out in
  section 160[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13888){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13888-ID0EKFCAA}
  of the [Health of Animals
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._296)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13889){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13889-ID0EHFCAA}
  in relation to permits referred to in paragraphs 51(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890-ID0EFFCAA}
  and 51.1(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890-ID0EDFCAA}
  and (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890-ID0EBFCAA}
  of those Regulations as they relate to terrestrial animal pathogens,
  including those from avian and amphibian animals, except for those
  pathogens

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13888-ID0EKFCAA){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13888}SOR/2006-147, s.
    19
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13889-ID0EHFCAA){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13889}C.R.C., c.
    296
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890-ID0EFFCAA){#fn_81000-3-513e_hq_13890}SOR/95-54, s.
    2
    :::

  <!-- -->

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that cause an animal disease included on the list
    of the World Organisation for Animal Health, entitled OIE-Listed
    diseases, infections and infestations, as amended from time to time,
    that is not indigenous to Canada, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that cause an emerging animal disease; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} from the Canadian Food Inspection Agency to the
  Public Health Agency of Canada the control and supervision of that
  portion of the federal public administration in the Canadian Food
  Inspection Agency known as the Domestic Terrestrial Animal Pathogen
  Unit,

effective April 1, 2013.
