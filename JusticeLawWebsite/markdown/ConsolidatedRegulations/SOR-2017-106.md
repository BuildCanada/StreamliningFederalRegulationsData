## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838859 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[Marine Protected
Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations, [[Marine Protected Area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
  area of the sea that is designated by section 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In Schedules 1 and 2, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD83) reference system.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838865 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Area

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The area
  depicted in Schedule 1 that is bounded by a series of rhumb lines
  drawn from points 1 to 10, the coordinates of each of which are set
  out in that Schedule, and back to point 1 is designated as the St.
  Anns Bank Marine Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of five metres and the water column above the
  seabed.

## [Management Zones]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838871 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boundaries

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Marine Protected Area
consists of the following management zones:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Zone 1, as depicted in Schedule 2, bounded by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from point 18, then to point 20, then to
  point 2, then to point 8, passing through points 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7,
  then to point 17, then to point 16, then to point 15, then to point 9,
  then to point 13, then to point 12, then to point 11, then to point
  14, then to point 19, the coordinates of each of which are set out in
  that Schedule, and back to point 18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Zone 2, as depicted in Schedule 2, bounded by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from point 1, then to point 2, then to
  point 20, then to point 18, then to point 19, then to point 10, the
  coordinates of each of which are set out in that Schedule, and back to
  point 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Zone 3, as depicted in Schedule 2, bounded by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from point 14, then to point 11, then to
  point 12, then to point 13, the coordinates of each of which are set
  out in that Schedule, and back to point 14;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Zone 4, as depicted in Schedule 2, bounded by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from point 8, then to point 15, then to
  point 16, then to point 17, the coordinates of each of which are set
  out in that Schedule, and back to point 8.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838878 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 5 to 8,
it is prohibited in the Marine Protected Area to carry out any activity
that disturbs, damages, destroys or removes from the Marine Protected
Area any living marine organism or any part of its habitat or is likely
to do so.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838881 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fishing

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following activities
may be carried out in the Marine Protected Area if they are carried out
in accordance with the provisions of the [Fisheries
Act](/eng/acts/F-14), the [Coastal Fisheries Protection
Act](/eng/acts/C-33) and their regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing, other than commercial fishing, that is
  authorized under the [Aboriginal Communal Fishing Licences
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} fishing for seals and any related activity that is
  authorized under the [Marine Mammal
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-56) or the [Aboriginal Communal
  Fishing Licences Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-332);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Zone 2, commercial or recreational fishing by
  means of a pot, trap, rod and reel, harpoon, bottom longline,
  handline, gill net or by diving;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in Zones 3 and 4, commercial or recreational fishing
  by means of a pot, trap, rod and reel, harpoon, bottom longline or
  handline.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Navigation

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Navigation may be carried
out in the Marine Protected Area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Safety or emergency

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An activity may be carried
out in the Marine Protected Area if it is carried out for the purpose of
public safety, national defence, national security, law enforcement or
to respond to an emergency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity plan

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Any activity that is part
of an activity plan that has been approved by the Minister may be
carried out in the Marine Protected Area.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838894 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission and contents

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Any person who proposes to
carry out a scientific research or monitoring activity, educational
activity or commercial marine tourism activity in the Marine Protected
Area must submit to the Minister an activity plan that contains the
following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number and
  email address;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the activity plan is submitted by an institution
  or organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible
  for the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number
  and email address;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
  use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
  registration number, its radio call sign and the name, address,
  telephone number and email address of its owner, master and any
  operator;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
  its purpose, the methods or techniques that are to be used to carry
  out the activity and the data to be collected;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographical coordinates of the site of the
  proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
  within the boundaries of the Marine Protected Area;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates and alternative dates on which the
  activity is to be carried out;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a list of the equipment that is to be used, the means
  by which it will be deployed and retrieved and the methods by which it
  is to be anchored or moored;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a list of the samples --- including their quantities
  --- that are to be collected;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited during
  the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Area --- other than
  substances that are authorized under the [Canada Shipping Act,
  2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) to be deposited in the navigation of a vessel
  --- and the quantity and concentration of each substance;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the adverse environmental effects
  that are likely to result from carrying out the proposed activity and
  of any measures that are to be taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or
  mitigate those effects;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
  monitoring activity, educational activity or commercial marine tourism
  activity that the person has carried out or anticipates carrying out
  in the Marine Protected Area;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
  work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity and its
  anticipated date of completion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of activity plan

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are not likely to destroy the habitat of any
    living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area and will serve
    to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} increase knowledge of the biodiversity or the
      biological productivity of the Marine Protected Area or the
      habitat of any living marine organism in the Marine Protected
      Area, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the management of the Marine Protected
      Area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational activities or commercial marine
    tourism activities set out in the plan

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} will serve to increase public awareness of the
      Marine Protected Area, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are not likely to damage, destroy or remove in
      the Marine Protected Area any living marine organism or any part
      of its habitat;

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister must not approve
  an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the
    substance is authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
    activities carried out in the Marine Protected Area, are such that
    the activity is likely to adversely affect the biodiversity or the
    biological productivity of the Marine Protected Area or destroy the
    habitat of any living marine organism in the Marine Protected Area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 60 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Post-activity report

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If an activity plan has
been approved by the Minister, the person who submitted the plan must,
within 90 days after the last day of the activity, provide the Minister
with a post-activity report that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of the samples --- including their quantities
  --- and the data that were collected during the activity, including
  the dates on which and the geographical coordinates of the sites where
  the sampling was done;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an evaluation of the effectiveness of any measures
  taken to monitor, avoid, minimize or mitigate the adverse
  environmental effects of the activity; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any event that occurred during the
  activity and that was not anticipated in the activity plan, if the
  event could result in the disturbance, damage, destruction or removal
  in the Marine Protected Area of any living marine organism or any part
  of its habitat.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-838931 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
