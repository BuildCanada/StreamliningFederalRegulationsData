## Order Designating the Indian Residential Schools Truth and Reconciliation Commission as a Department and the Chairperson as the Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-indian-residential-schools-truth-and-reconciliation-commission-as-a-department-and-the-chairperson-as-the-deputy-head-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2009-46
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2009-06-24

Order Designating the Indian Residential Schools Truth and
Reconciliation Commission as a Department and the Chairperson as the
Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 2009-935 2009-06-09
::::

::::: section
:::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
[[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and paragraph (b) of the definition
[[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBFBA} hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2008-799 of April 25,
  2008[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_SI-2009-45_e_hq_13550){#fn_SI-2009-45_e_hq_13550-ID0EBAEBA},
  effective July 1, 2009;

- [(b) and (c)]{.lawlabel} \[Repealed, SI/2009-46\]

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeals paragraphs (b) and (c), effective January 1,
  2016.
