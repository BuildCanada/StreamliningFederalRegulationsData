## National Energy Board Rules of Practice and Procedure, 1995 {#national-energy-board-rules-of-practice-and-procedure-1995 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-208
:::

[NATIONAL ENERGY BOARD ACT](/eng/acts/N-7)

Registration 1995-04-26

Rules Respecting the Practice and Procedure of the National Energy Board
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The National Energy Board, pursuant to section 8 of the [National Energy
Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), hereby revokes the National Energy Board
Rules of Practice and Procedure, C.R.C., c. 1057, and makes the annexed
[Rules respecting the practice and procedure of the National Energy
Board](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-208), in substitution therefor.

Calgary, Alberta, April 25, 1995
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971955 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Rules may be cited as
the [National Energy Board Rules of Practice and Procedure,
1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-208).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971957 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Rules,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [National Energy Board
    Act](/eng/acts/N-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affidavit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a written affirmation;
    ([affidavit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an application made to the
    Board under the Act or any regulations made under the Act and
    includes a complaint; ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[complaint]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a complaint made to the
    Board that alleges anything to have been done or omitted to have
    been done contrary to or in contravention of the Act or any
    regulations made under the Act; ([plainte]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[hearing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hearing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person authorized to
    act on behalf of the Secretary at an oral hearing; ([agent
    d'audience]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an interested person who establishes an interest
      in a proceeding pursuant to section 28,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who files an answer to a complaint
      pursuant to section 19,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where two or more applications are the subject of
      a single proceeding, an applicant with respect to each other
      application in that proceeding, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a purchaser of gas who files an objection with
      the Board pursuant to section 29; ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a proceeding, a
    person who makes an application or an intervenor;
    ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceeding]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a written or oral hearing,
    whether the hearing is public or non-public, brought before the
    Board under the Act or any regulations made under the Act, from the
    time an application is filed with the Board or from the time the
    Board issues directions on procedure in respect of an inquiry or
    hearing into any matter by the Board under subsection 15(3) of the
    Act. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-971971 .Part}

### [Application of This Part]{.HTitleText2} {#h-971972 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to proceedings referred to in subsection 15(3) of
    the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to proceedings brought under subsection 21(1) of
    the Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 48, to proceedings brought under
    Part III of the Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to proceedings brought under Part IV of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} to proceedings brought under section 104 of the
    Act;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} except section 18, to proceedings brought under
    Division II of Part VI of the Act; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} to proceedings brought under the [National Energy
    Board Act Part VI (Oil and Gas)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-244), respecting applications
    for orders authorizing the exportation or importation of oil or gas
    and applications for amendments to gas export sales contracts or gas
    import purchase contracts.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any regulations made pursuant to sections
  58.39, 107, 119.01, 119.094 and 127 of the Act, at any time in a
  proceeding, where considerations of public interest and fairness so
  require, the Board may, by order, provide that this Part applies to
  any proceedings other than those referred to in subsection (1).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-380, s. 1

### [Dispensing with or Varying Rules]{.HTitleText2} {#h-971985 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time in a proceeding, where considerations of public interest and
  fairness so require, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dispense with or vary these Rules or any part
    thereof; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} extend or abridge the time fixed by these Rules or
    otherwise fixed by the Board, and may do so of its own volition or
    in response to a motion by any party whether or not the motion to
    extend or abridge the time is made after the time so fixed has
    expired.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board dispenses with or varies the Rules or
  extends or abridges the time fixed by the Rules or by the Board under
  subsection (1), the Board shall forthwith notify all parties and any
  interested persons and shall issue directions in respect of the
  procedure appropriate to the proceedings or fix the time in which to
  conduct the proceedings.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/99-380, s. 2
:::

### [Computation of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-971993 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where under these Rules the
Board fixes a time limit, it shall do so taking into consideration the
fair conduct of the proceeding.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where reference is made to
a number of days for the doing of a thing, it shall mean calendar days.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the time fixed for
the doing of a thing expires or falls on a holiday or a Saturday, the
thing may be done on the day next following that is not a holiday or a
Saturday.

### [Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-971997 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (7) and (8), service of any document may be effected by
  hand delivery, mail, courier, telex, facsimile or other means of
  written or electronic communication, if the person being served has
  the facilities for receiving a document in such manner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any document that must be served shall set out the
  name of each person or group of persons on whom it is to be served,
  the Board's file number and hearing order number and a short phrase
  summarizing the nature of the proceeding.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the date of service of a
  document is the date on which the person being served or the person's
  authorized representative receives the document.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is received by the person being
  served or the person's authorized representative after five o'clock in
  the afternoon at the place of service shall be considered to be served
  on the next business day.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a person serves a document, the person shall,
  at the Board's request, file an affidavit with the Board that sets out
  on whom the document was served and the means taken to effect service.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is required to serve a document on
  more than one party, service on each party shall be effected on the
  same day.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where an oral hearing is in progress, service of any
  document in respect of the hearing may be effected by making a copy of
  the document available to each party present at the hearing and by
  giving a copy of the document to any other party who requests one.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The service that is required under paragraph 34(1)(a)
  and subsection 104(2) of the Act and under subsection 40(4) of these
  Rules shall be personal service and shall be effected in accordance
  with Part 3 of the Federal Court Rules, 1998, except that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal service on Her Majesty in right of Canada
    shall be effected in the manner prescribed in the [Crown Liability
    and Proceedings (Provincial Court)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-604); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} personal service on Her Majesty in right of a
    province shall be effected by leaving a copy of the document with a
    lawyer employed at the principal office of either the provincial
    Department of Justice or the Attorney General, as the case may be.

- [(8.1)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a reference in the Federal
  Court Rules, 1998 to the word "Court" is deemed to be a reference to
  the Board.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Where a person serves a document by electronic means,
  the person shall provide an original hard copy of the document to the
  person served within a reasonable period after the document is served.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-573, s. 1
- SOR/2000-361, s. 1
:::

### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972013 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the filing of any document with the Secretary by
  hand delivery, mail, courier, telex, facsimile or other means of
  written or electronic communication, if the Board has the facilities
  for receiving a document in such manner, shall constitute filing with
  the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an oral hearing is in progress, the filing of
  any document with the hearing officer shall constitute filing with the
  Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the date of filing a
  document with the Board is the day on which the Secretary or the
  hearing officer receives the document in accordance with
  subsection (1) or (2), respectively.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is received by the Board after
  regular business hours, which are from eight o'clock in the forenoon
  to five o'clock in the afternoon, shall be considered to be filed on
  the next business day.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every document that is filed with the Board in
  respect of a proceeding shall set out the Board's file number and
  hearing order number and a short phrase summarizing the nature of the
  proceeding.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where a person files a document less than five days
  prior to the commencement of an oral hearing, the person shall bring
  to the hearing a copy of the document for the Board and, if service of
  the document is required, for any party present.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where a person is required to file a document with
  the Board and serve the document on one or more parties, the filing
  and the service shall be effected on the same day.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} Where a person files a document with the Board by
  electronic means, the person shall provide an original hard copy of
  the document to the Board within a reasonable period after the
  document is filed.

### [Amending of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972023 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In a
  proceeding, a party may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make minor amendments to any documents filed with
    the Board; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} with leave of the Board, make substantive
    amendments to any documents filed with the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In a proceeding, where the Board finds that a
  document or any part thereof may prejudice, embarrass or delay the
  fair conduct of the proceeding, the Board shall so notify the parties
  and may order that, unless the document or part thereof is amended
  within the time limit set out in the notice, the document or part
  thereof be struck out.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a party, in accordance with this section,
  amends any document or part thereof

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each amended page shall indicate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the amendment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the portion of the page amended by means of a
      vertical line, an asterisk or other similar marking, in the margin
      opposite to the amendment; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each amendment shall be accompanied by a statement
    explaining the nature of the amendment.

### [Affidavits]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972034 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person files a document with the Board, the Board may require the
  person to verify the document or any part thereof by affidavit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board requires verification in accordance
  with subsection (1), the Board shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an oral hearing that is in progress,
    orally inform the person of the document or part thereof that must
    be verified and the time within which verification is to be
    provided; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, serve a notice on the person
    indicating the document or part thereof that must be verified and
    the time within which verification is to be provided.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person does not comply with a requirement to
  verify made in accordance with subsection (1), the Board may strike
  out the document or part thereof that was not verified.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  affidavit in a proceeding shall be filed with the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an affidavit is made as to belief, the grounds
  on which the belief is based shall be set out in the affidavit.

### [Questions of Law]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972044 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  in the opinion of the Board, a question or issue of law, of
  jurisdiction or of practice or procedure should be decided at any time
  in a proceeding, the Board may direct that the question or issue

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be raised for a determination by the Board; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be referred to the Federal Court of Appeal for
    hearing and determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may, pending the determination of the
  question or issue referred to in subsection (1), stay the whole or any
  part of the proceeding.

### [Contents and Form of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972050 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every application shall
be in writing, signed by the applicant or the applicant's authorized
representative and filed with the Board.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  application shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contain a concise statement of the relevant facts,
    the provisions of the Act or any regulations made under the Act
    under which the application is made and the nature of, and
    justification for, the decision or order sought;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contain, in addition to the information that is
    required by the Act and any regulations made under the Act, any
    other information that explains or supports the application,
    including information referred to in published policies and
    guidelines of the Board; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the name, address, telephone number and any
    other telecommunications numbers of the applicant and the
    applicant's authorized representative, if any.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every application shall be divided into consecutively
  numbered paragraphs, each of which shall be confined as nearly as is
  practicable to a distinct portion of the subject-matter of the
  application.

<!-- -->

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any information referred to in section 15 is not filed with the
  application, the applicant shall attach to the application a statement

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the information is already in the Board's
    possession, identifying the information and the circumstances under
    which it came into the possession of the Board;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the information is unavailable to the
    applicant at the time of filing, identifying the date on which the
    applicant intends to file the required information; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the applicant objects to the filing of the
    information, setting out the objection and the grounds therefor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board determines that the grounds for the
  objection referred to in paragraph (1)(c) are not sufficient, the
  Board shall so inform the applicant and the applicant shall file the
  information.

### [Production of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972064 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Unless leave of the Board
is granted, where, at any time in a proceeding, a person intends to rely
on a document other than a decision or order of or document released by
the Board, the person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} file the document with the Board and serve a copy of
  it on all parties; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the document is already in the Board's
  possession, file a statement identifying the document and the
  circumstances under which it came into the possession of the Board.

### [Additional Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972068 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** At any time in a
proceeding, the Board may require a party to provide such further
information, particulars or documents as may be necessary to enable the
Board to obtain a full and satisfactory understanding of the
subject-matter of the proceeding.

### [Complaints]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972070 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A complaint shall be
served by the complainant on any person whose conduct is the subject of
the complaint and that person may, within 20 days after receipt of the
complaint, file a written answer with the Board and shall serve a copy
of any answer on the complainant.

### [Failure to Comply]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972072 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an applicant fails to provide the information described in sections 15
  and 16 or does not respond to a request for information from the Board
  or any other party, the Board may stay the application until the
  information is provided, whether or not the application has been set
  down for a hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a party does not comply with these Rules or any
  directions issued by the Board, the Board may stay the proceeding
  until the Board is satisfied that the Rules or directions have been
  complied with or may take such other steps as are just and reasonable
  in order to conduct a fair proceeding.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where for any reason an application or proceeding has
  been stayed for five years pursuant to subsection (1) or (2)
  respectively, the application that has been stayed or the application
  giving rise to the proceeding that has been stayed shall be considered
  to have been withdrawn and shall be returned to the applicant.

## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Public Hearings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-972077 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972078 .Subheading}

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 48,
this Part applies to the conduct of public hearings before the Board
that are required by the Act or held pursuant to subsection 15(3) or
24(3) of the Act, whether the evidence in respect of the hearing is in
written or oral form.

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may determine that a proceeding be disposed of by an oral or a
  written public hearing and may invite submissions from interested
  persons in order to make that determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where necessary at
  any time in a proceeding, the Board may order that the proceeding be
  disposed of by an oral public hearing in substitution for a written
  public hearing.

### [Hearing Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972083 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall issue a hearing order, containing the
    procedural details applicable to the hearing, accompanied by a
    notice of public hearing, where an application is to be disposed of
    by means of an oral public hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} may, if necessary, issue a hearing order,
    containing the procedural details applicable to the hearing, which
    may be accompanied by a notice of public hearing, where an
    application is to be disposed of by means of a written public
    hearing or where a proceeding is commenced by the Board under
    subsection 15(3) of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board issues a notice of public hearing in
  accordance with subsection (1), other than where the notice is issued
  pursuant to section 35 of the Act, the applicant shall, within such
  time limit as the Board may direct,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} publish the notice in such publications as the
    Board may direct; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} serve a copy of the notice on such persons as the
    Board may direct.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An applicant referred to in subsection (2) shall file
  with the Board an affidavit that sets out the title and date of each
  publication in which the notice of public hearing was published
  pursuant to paragraph (2)(a) and the means taken to effect service
  pursuant to paragraph (2)(b).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board does not issue a hearing order, it
  shall notify interested persons of the hearing and the procedure to be
  followed in respect thereof.

### [Public Inspection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972093 .Subheading}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Where the Board issues a
hearing order,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant shall keep available at the applicant's
  business address, for public inspection during regular business hours,
  a copy of the application and the record as described in subsections
  36(7) and 37(5); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board shall keep available for public inspection
  in the Board's library

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the application and all documents related
    thereto, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Board acting on its own motion
    under subsection 15(3) of the Act, all documents related to the
    proceeding.

### [Formulation of Issues]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972099 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may formulate issues to be considered in a proceeding and shall
  notify parties in respect thereof if, in the opinion of the Board,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the documents filed with the Board do not
    sufficiently raise the matters in issue in the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the formulation of issues would assist the Board in
    the conduct of the proceeding; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the formulation of issues would assist the parties
    in participating more effectively in the proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} To assist the Board in formulating issues in
  accordance with subsection (1), the Board may invite parties to
  propose issues or to suggest amendments to any formulated issues.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any party who proposes an issue pursuant to
  subsection (2) shall explain the issue's relevance and the
  justification for considering it in the proceeding.

### [Written Submissions or Conference]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972107 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** The Board may direct
parties to make written submissions for the purpose of considering
matters that relate to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clarification or simplification of issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the necessity or desirability of amending an
  application, notice of motion, answer, intervention or reply for the
  purpose of clarification, amplification or limitation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the admission of certain facts or the verification of
  those facts by affidavit, or the use by any party of documents of a
  public nature;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the settling of matters that relate to information
  requests;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the procedure to be adopted in the proceeding;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of documents among the parties; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other matter that could aid in the conduct and
  disposition of the proceeding.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The Board may convene the
parties to a conference

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to formulate issues as described in section 25; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to consider the matters described in section 26.

### [Interventions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972119 .Subheading}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a hearing order has been issued pursuant to section 23, any interested
  person may intervene in the proceeding by filing with the Board and
  serving on the applicant, if any, on or before the date set out in the
  order, a written intervention that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an oral hearing, states whether the
    person intends to appear at the hearing and the official language in
    which the person wishes to be heard;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sets out the name of the person and any authorized
    representative of the person and the mailing address, address for
    personal service, telephone number and any other telecommunications
    numbers of the person or the person's authorized representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} establishes that the person's interest justifies
    intervenor status in the proceeding; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), states the issues that
    the person intends to address at the hearing or, where the person
    does not intend to participate actively at the hearing, states the
    reasons why the person's interest justifies intervenor status in the
    proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, by reason of an inability or insufficient time
  to study an application, a person is unable to include in the written
  intervention the information required by paragraph (1)(d), the person
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide a statement in the written intervention
    explaining why the person was unable or why there was insufficient
    time to study the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 15 days after the date of service of a copy
    of the application or within 15 days after the date of filing of the
    written intervention, whichever is the later, file with the Board
    and serve on the applicant, if any, a supplement to the written
    intervention containing the information required by paragraph
    (1)(d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may accept or disallow an intervention
  depending on whether the requirements set out in subsection (1) have
  been met and in either case the Board shall notify the applicant, if
  any, and the person who filed the intervention of its decision in
  respect of the intervention.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972258 .Subheading}

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  application for review or rehearing pursuant to subsection 21(1) of
  the Act shall be in writing, signed by the applicant or the
  applicant's authorized representative, filed with the Board and served
  on all parties to the original proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for review or rehearing shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the facts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the grounds that the applicant considers

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the prejudice or damage that has
    resulted or will result from the decision or order; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the relief sought.

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dismiss the application if the Board is of the view

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for review, raised
      a doubt as to the correctness of the Board's decision or order, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for rehearing,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} issue an order granting a review or rehearing and

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before making a determination under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue directions on procedure giving interested

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any request referred to in this section shall be in
  writing and the person or company making the request shall send, on
  the same day by registered mail, a copy of the request to the Board
  and to the company or person, as the case may be.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may appoint a member of its staff to
  mediate between the company and the person involved in a request
  referred to in this section with a view to obtaining an agreement as
  to the amount of costs to be paid by the company.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where no agreement is reached within 20 days after
  the appointment of a mediator, the Board shall give notice to the
  person and the company, and commence proceedings to fix the amount of
  costs to be paid.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Right of Entry to Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#h-972341 .Part}

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972342 .Subheading}

- **[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} To
  apply for a right of entry order under section 104 of the Act, a
  company shall, after serving the owner of the lands with the notice
  described in subsection 104(2) of the Act, file an application with
  the Board not less than 30 days and not more than 60 days after the
  date of service of the notice on the owner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be served on the owner of the
  lands on the same day that the application is filed with the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the notice described in subsection 104(2)
    of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the notice has been served on the
    owner of the lands

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} not less than 30 days and not more than 60 days
      prior to filing the application with the Board, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with subsection 8(8) or in any
      manner ordered by the Board under the [National Energy Board
      Substituted Service Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-191);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a schedule that is proposed to be made part of the
    order sought and that contains, in a form suitable for depositing,
    registering, recording or filing against lands in the land registry
    or land titles office in which land transactions affecting those
    lands may be deposited, registered, recorded or filed, a description
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the lands in respect of which the order is
      sought,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the rights, titles or interests applied for in
      respect of the lands, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any rights, obligations, restrictions or terms
      and conditions that are proposed to attach

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} to the rights, titles or interests applied for
        in respect of the lands,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} to any remaining interest or interests, or

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} to any adjacent lands of the owner;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a current abstract of title to the lands, a
    certified copy of the certificate of title to the lands or a
    certified statement of rights registered in the land registers for
    the lands;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of section 56; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the application, including the
    information set out in paragraphs (a) to (e), has been served on the
    owner of the lands.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-30, s. 1
:::

### [Written Objection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-972363 .Subheading}

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  owner of lands for which a right of entry order is sought who wishes
  to object to the application shall file the objection with the Board
  no later than ten days after the date that the application is served
  on the owner by the company.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an owner of lands files an objection in
  accordance with subsection (1), the owner shall, on the same day that
  the objection is filed with the Board, serve the objection on the
  company at the address shown in the notice served on the owner by the
  company.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A company that receives an objection under
  subsection (2) shall file with the Board a reply to the objection, or
  a statement that it does not wish to respond to the objection, within
  seven days after the date that the objection is served on the company
  by the owner of the lands.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a company files a reply to an objection, the
  company shall serve the reply on the owner of the lands on the same
  day that the reply is filed with the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-30, s. 1
