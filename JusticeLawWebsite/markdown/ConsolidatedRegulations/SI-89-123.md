## Order Declaring May 1, 1989 as the Day on Which the Third Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Comes into Force {#order-declaring-may-1-1989-as-the-day-on-which-the-third-supplement-to-the-revised-statutes-of-canada-1985-comes-into-force .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/89-123
:::

[REVISED STATUTES OF CANADA, 1985 ACT](/eng/acts/R-7.7)

Registration 1989-05-10

P.C. 1989-735 1989-04-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Statute Revision Commission has reported in writing the
completion of the 3rd Supplement to the Revised Statutes of Canada,
1985;

And Whereas the Governor General has caused a printed copy of that
Supplement, attested under the signature of the Governor General,
countersigned by the Minister of Justice and certified correct by the
Chairman of the Statute Revision Commission, to be deposited in the
office of the Clerk of the Parliaments on April 4, 1989.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 13(2)
of the [Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 Act](/eng/acts/R-7.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased hereby to
declare May 1, 1989 as the day on and from which the 3rd Supplement to
the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985 shall come into force and have
operation and effect as law as though the enactments contained therein
were expressly embodied in and enacted by an Act of Parliament.
