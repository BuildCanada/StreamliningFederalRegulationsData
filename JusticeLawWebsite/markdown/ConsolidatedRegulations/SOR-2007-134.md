## Public Opinion Research Contract Regulations {#public-opinion-research-contract-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-134
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2007-06-07

Public Opinion Research Contract Regulations

P.C. 2007-945 2007-06-07
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
42(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_908_hq_1807){#fn_908_hq_1807-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Public Opinion Research Contract
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-134).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report form

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** A person entering into a
contract for public opinion research with Her Majesty must provide the
report referred to in subsection 40(2) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11) in electronic or print format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report contents --- research not for the
exclusive use of Her Majesty

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In respect of public
opinion research that is not for the exclusive use of Her Majesty, the
report must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the methodology used; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the results of the research.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report contents --- research for the
exclusive use of Her Majesty

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In respect of public
opinion research that is for the exclusive use of Her Majesty, the
report must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the covering page, the title of the project, the
  name of the person who entered into the contract, the contract number
  and the award date;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a narrative executive summary consisting of, at a
  minimum,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the research purpose and objectives,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a summary of key findings, except where the person
    who entered into the contract is not responsible for the design,
    development of the methodology and analysis of the research,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the methodology used, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to the extent to which the findings
    can be extrapolated to a broader audience;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for quantitative research, appendices containing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a full set of tabulated data,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sample size, sampling procedures and dates of
    research fieldwork,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, weighting procedures, the
    confidence interval and the margin of error,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the response rate and method of
    calculation,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the research instruments used, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} all other information about the execution of the
    fieldwork that would be needed to replicate the research initiative;
    and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for qualitative research, appendices containing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the research instruments used and, if applicable,
    the test material, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all other information about the recruitment or
    execution of the fieldwork that would be needed to replicate the
    research initiative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of contract term --- research
for the exclusive use of Her Majesty

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The contents of the
contract term referred to in subsection 40(2) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), if the public opinion research is
for the exclusive use of Her Majesty, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide for the notification of the person entering
  into the contract that the report will be sent to the Librarian and
  Archivist of Canada in accordance with section 15.1 of the [Library
  and Archives of Canada Act](/eng/acts/L-7.7); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} require that the person consent to the Librarian and
  Archivist of Canada publishing, in both official languages, the
  narrative executive summary referred to in paragraph 3(b) on the
  Library and Archives of Canada web site.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
