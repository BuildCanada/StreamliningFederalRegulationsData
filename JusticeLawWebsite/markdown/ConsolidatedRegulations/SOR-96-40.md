## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985876 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ships' Stores Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985878 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[bar-box]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[bar-box]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sealable boxes or
      compartments into which ships' stores are placed during the flight
      of an international aircraft; ([compartiment à
      boissons]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[biodiesel fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biodiesel fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means renewable fuel that
      is suitable for use in internal combustion engines of the
      compression-ignition type; ([carburant biodiesel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian coast guard ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian coast guard ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a coast
      guard ship owned by, or operated by or on behalf of, the
      Government of Canada; ([navire garde-côte
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian fishing ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian fishing ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fishing ship

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is registered or licensed in Canada under
        the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) and is owned by one or
        more persons each of whom is a Canadian citizen, a person
        resident and domiciled in Canada or a corporation incorporated
        under the laws of Canada or of a province and having its
        principal place of business in Canada, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is not required by the [Canada Shipping
        Act](/eng/acts/S-9) to be registered or licensed in Canada and
        is not registered or licensed elsewhere but is owned as
        described in paragraph (a); ([navire de pêche
        canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Canadian warship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian warship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship of war, a
      military transport or a military supply ship owned by, or operated
      by or on behalf of, the Government of Canada; ([navire de guerre
      canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fishing ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fishing ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship used for

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing, sealing or whaling outside Canada, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisioning, servicing, repairing or
        maintaining ships engaged in fishing, sealing or whaling outside
        Canada; ([navire de pêche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[foreign warship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[foreign warship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship of war, a
      military transport or a military supply ship owned by, or operated
      by or on behalf of, the government of a country other than Canada;
      ([navire de guerre étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inland waters ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inland waters ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship, other
      than an eligible ship as defined in subsection 68.5(1) of the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), engaged in trade on an inland
      voyage, as defined in that subsection; ([navire d'eaux
      internes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[international aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an aircraft
      operating internationally in the transportation of passengers or
      goods, or both, for reward; ([aéronef
      international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[military aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[military aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a military
      aircraft owned by, or operated by or on behalf of, the government
      of a country; ([aéronef militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reasonable quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reasonable quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to
      domestic or imported cigars or domestic stamped manufactured
      tobacco or imported manufactured tobacco, means a quantity not
      exceeding 200 cigarettes, 50 cigars, 200 grams of manufactured
      tobacco and 200 tobacco sticks; ([quantité
      raisonnable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[renewable fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[renewable fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes fuel that is
      produced from biomass, waste material or feedstock of biological
      origin, whether or not the fuel is blended with a petroleum
      product; ([carburant renouvelable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship registered in
      any country and used for the purpose of obtaining scientific data
      outside Canada; ([navire de service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[telegraph cable ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[telegraph cable ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ship
      registered in any country and used exclusively for the laying or
      repairing of oceanic telegraph cables. ([navire poseur de câbles
      télégraphiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, the words
  [[alcohol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[beer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
  [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},

  tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[stamped]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have
  the same meaning as in the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1).

## [Ships' Stores]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985902 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  classes of goods described in column II of an item of the schedule are
  hereby designated as ships' stores for use on board a conveyance
  within any class of conveyance described in column I of that item,
  subject to any applicable limitation set out in a note at the end of
  the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), goods are
  considered to be used on board a conveyance if they are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods that are consumed in the operation and
    maintenance of the conveyance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are consumed by a person on board the
    conveyance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} souvenirs, gifts, edible supplies, wines, spirits,
    ales, beers, domestic or imported cigars or domestic stamped
    manufactured tobacco or imported manufactured tobacco that are sold
    on board the conveyance, unless it is a Canadian warship that is
    described in column I of subitem 3(1) of the schedule, for
    consumption outside Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} goods described in column II of subitem 3(1) of the
    schedule that are sold on board a Canadian warship that is described
    in column I of that subitem 3(1)

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the date on which the
      conveyance arrived in a Canadian port, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for consumption outside Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A master of a Canadian warship described in subitem
  3(1) of the schedule shall, on the 31st consecutive day of the period
  in which that Canadian warship is docked in a Canadian port,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} advise the nearest customs office in writing that
    the Canadian warship is remaining in port longer than 30 consecutive
    days; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare a written inventory of the wines, spirits,
    ales, beers, and domestic or imported cigars or domestic stamped
    manufactured tobacco or imported manufactured tobacco remaining on
    board at the end of the 30^th^ day of that period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-394, s. 2
:::

## [Sealing Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985917 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** When a ship arrives at a
Canadian port, the master of the ship shall place alcohol, tobacco and
other goods for sale on board the ship under lock or seal, and shall
keep them there while the ship is in the port, except where otherwise
authorized by an officer.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), when an international aircraft is on the ground,
  the carrier shall ensure that bar-boxes on board are sealed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Seals on bar-boxes on an international aircraft may
  be broken when passengers begin boarding and, provided that no
  domestic passengers are carried, may remain unsealed where the
  aircraft is progressively boarded at more than one airport in Canada.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Stores for use on board a
conveyance shall remain sealed when the goods are moved to the
conveyance from a bonded warehouse or to a bonded warehouse from the
conveyance.

## [Provisioning]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985923 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** While in Canada, a
conveyance described in column I of item 2 of the schedule shall not
take on ships' stores that are to be transferred to a ship engaged in
fishing, sealing or whaling outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-394, s. 3(F)
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Authorization of delivery
of ships' stores to a conveyance may be refused by an officer where the
master, an officer or a crew member of the conveyance has failed to
comply with these Regulations.
