## Indian Timber Harvesting Regulations {#indian-timber-harvesting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-109
:::

[INDIAN ACT](/eng/acts/I-5)

Registration 2002-02-28

Indian Timber Harvesting Regulations

P.C. 2002-280  2002-02-28
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
section 57 and subsection 73(3) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5),
hereby makes the annexed [Indian Timber Harvesting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-109).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679960 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[first nation lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first nation lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the reserve lands
    and surrendered lands of a band named in the schedule. ([terres des
    premières nations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence granted under
    section 5. ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([agent de la
    paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Removal of Timber from a Reserve]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679965 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations shall be interpreted so as to negate the requirement to
obtain permission under section 93 of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5)
before removing timber from a reserve.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679967 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of the cutting of timber on first nation
  lands.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The [Indian Timber
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._961) do not apply in respect
  of the cutting of timber on first nation lands.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679971 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall cut timber on first nation
  lands without a licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person acting on behalf of the council of a band
  may, without a licence, cut timber on behalf of that council of the
  band on any reserve lands of the band that have not been allotted by
  the council of the band or designated by the band and that are not
  already subject to a licence, if the timber and any product made from
  the timber is intended for use on first nation lands of the band.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a Certificate of Possession in respect
  of land that forms part of reserve lands of a band may, without a
  licence, cut timber on the land described in the Certificate of
  Possession that is not already subject to a licence, if the timber and
  any product made from the timber is intended for use on the first
  nation lands of the band.

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679976 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister may grant a licence

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to any person, entitling that person, or another
    person acting on behalf of that person, to cut timber on first
    nation lands; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the council of a band, entitling any person
    acting on behalf of the council of the band to cut timber on the
    first nation lands of the band.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may grant a licence in respect of
  reserve lands of a band only with the consent of the council of the
  band.

## [Seizure of Timber]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679982 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or a peace officer may seize any timber cut on first nation
  lands that was not cut under the authority of a licence or in
  accordance with subsection 4(2) or (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Seizure of timber may be achieved by posting on or
  near the timber a notice stating that the timber has been seized under
  the authority of these Regulations.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** When timber has been seized
and no claim to challenge the seizure or to recover the timber is made
within 30 days after the seizure, the timber is forfeited to Her
Majesty.

## [Penalty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679987 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every person who
contravenes a provision of these Regulations is liable on summary
conviction to a fine not exceeding \$100 or to imprisonment for a term
not exceeding three months, or to both.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679989 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679991 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
