## Authorizations to Carry Restricted Firearms and Certain Handguns Regulations {#authorizations-to-carry-restricted-firearms-and-certain-handguns-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-207
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Authorizations to Carry Restricted Firearms and Certain Handguns
Regulations

P.C. 1998-482 1998-03-24
::::

:::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had a copy of the proposed [Authorizations to Carry
Restricted Firearms and Certain Handguns
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-207), substantially in the annexed
form, laid before each House of Parliament on November 27, 1996, which
date is at least 30 sitting days before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 117 of
the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Authorizations to Carry Restricted Firearms and Certain Handguns
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-207).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019748 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Firearms
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holster]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holster]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a holster that can be worn on
    a belt or attached to a body and that is equipped with a fastening
    device that can securely hold a firearm. ([étui]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prohibited handgun]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}means a handgun under
    paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
    firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([arme de poing prohibée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Circumstances in Which an Individual Needs Restricted Firearms or Prohibited Handguns for the Purpose of Section 20 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019755 .Part}

### [Protection of Life]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019756 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
20 of the Act, the circumstances in which an individual needs restricted
firearms or prohibited handguns to protect the life of that individual
or of other individuals are where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the life of that individual, or other individuals, is
  in imminent danger from one or more other individuals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} police protection is not sufficient in the
  circumstances; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the possession of a restricted firearm or prohibited
  handgun can reasonably be justified for protecting the individual or
  other individuals from death or grievous bodily harm.

### [Lawful Profession or Occupation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019761 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of section
20 of the Act, the circumstances in which an individual needs restricted
firearms or prohibited handguns for use in connection with his or her
lawful profession or occupation are where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's principal activity is the handling,
  transportation or protection of cash, negotiable instruments or other
  goods of substantial value, and firearms are required for the purpose
  of protecting his or her life or the lives of other individuals in the
  course of that handling, transportation or protection activity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual is working in a remote wilderness area
  and firearms are required for the protection of the life of that
  individual or of other individuals from wild animals; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the individual is engaged in the occupation of
  trapping in a province and is licensed or authorized and trained as
  required by the laws of the province.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Authorizations to Carry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019766 .Part}

### [Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019767 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commissioner or the
chief firearms officer, as the case may be, shall not issue to an
individual an authorization to carry a particular restricted firearm or
prohibited handgun in the circumstances described in section 2 or
paragraph 3(a), unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual has successfully completed training in
  firearms proficiency and the use of force that is appropriate for
  using the firearm in those circumstances; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is appropriate in those circumstances.

### [Renewal]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019773 .Subheading}

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 67(1) and (1.1) of the Act, the manner in which an
authorization to carry is renewed in the same as the manner in which it
can be issued.

### [Number of Firearms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019777 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An authorization to carry
may authorize the possession of one or more restricted firearms or
prohibited handguns for the purposes of section 20 of the Act.

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019779 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorization to carry issued to an individual for the purposes of
  paragraph 20(a) of the Act shall have the following conditions
  attached to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the individual is authorized to possess more
    than one restricted firearm or prohibited handgun, that the
    individual carry not more than one of them at a time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the individual carry the restricted firearm or
    prohibited handgun in a holster.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorization to carry issued to an individual for
  the purposes of paragraph 20(b) of the Act shall have the conditions
  set out in subsection (1) and the following conditions attached to it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the individual notify the chief firearm
    officer if the individual ceases to be employed or engaged in the
    lawful profession or occupation or changes employers; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the individual needs the restricted firearms or
    prohibited handguns in the circumstances described in paragraph
    3(a), that the individual wear a uniform.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019785 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An individual's
authorization to carry shall be revoked if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual's licence to possess any of the
  firearms referred to in the authorization is revoked or has expired;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the individual's physical or mental state has
  deteriorated to an extent that may affect the safety of the individual
  or of any other person; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it was issued for the purpose of a lawful profession
  or occupation and the individual ceases to be employed or engaged in
  the lawful profession or occupation.

### [Notice of Refusal or Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019791 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Commissioner or a chief firearms officer refuses to issue an
  authorization to carry or revokes an authorization to carry, they
  shall give written notice of the decision to the applicant for or
  holder of the authorization. The notice is to include reasons for the
  decision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner or a chief firearms officer need not
  disclose any information the disclosure of which could endanger the
  safety of any person.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  of a decision to refuse to issue an authorization to carry is
  sufficiently given if the notice is addressed to the applicant for the
  authorization to carry at their address that is set out in the
  application for the authorization or, if the individual has advised
  the Commissioner or the chief firearms officer of a change of that
  address, at the new address, and it is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sent by mail; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of a decision to revoke an authorization to
  carry is sufficiently given if the notice is addressed to the holder
  of the authorization at their address that is set out in the
  application for the authorization or, if the individual has advised
  the Commissioner or the chief firearms officer of a change of that
  address, at the new address, and the notice is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} delivered personally, at any time that is
    reasonable in the circumstances;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent by registered mail or by courier; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transmitted by electronic means that can produce a
    paper record.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice is deemed to be received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of delivery, if delivered personally;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the fifth working day, excluding Saturdays and
    holidays, after

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the postmark date, if it is sent by mail, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment on the waybill, if it is
      sent by courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day of transmission, if sent by electronic
    means.
