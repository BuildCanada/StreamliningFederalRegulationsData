## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851517 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cannabis
    Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriate officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the RCMP, the Assistant
      Commissioner of the RCMP in charge of drug enforcement;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a police force other than the
      RCMP, the member of the police force who is the most senior
      officer responsible for operations; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the military police, the Canadian
      Forces Provost Marshal. ([officier compétent]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a police force
    other than the RCMP, the senior police officer in charge of the
    police force. ([chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[military police]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military police]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means military police that
    are designated under subsection 2(2). ([policier
    militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[particular investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[particular investigation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a primary
    investigation conducted under the Act or any other Act of Parliament
    and includes any investigation that arises from the primary
    investigation. ([enquête particulière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[police force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[police force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police force that is
    designated under subsection 2(1). ([corps policier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[provincial minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provincial
    minister responsible for policing in a province. ([ministre
    provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designation of Police Forces and Military Police]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851531 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to designate --- police forces

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness and every
  provincial minister are authorized to designate any police force
  within the jurisdiction of the Minister or the provincial minister, as
  the case may be, for the purposes of any or all provisions of these
  Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authority to designate --- military
  police

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister of National Defence is authorized to
  designate military police for the purposes of any or all provisions of
  these Regulations.

## [General Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851537 .Part}

### [Sections 8 and 9 to 14 of the Act]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851538 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences --- general --- police

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A member of a police force
or of the military police is exempt from the application of any of
sections 8 and 9 to 14 of the Act, as applicable, if the member engages
in any activity referred to in those sections involving cannabis ---
other than cannabis that has been forfeited to Her Majesty, that is
imported or exported under the exemption provided for in section 17 of
these Regulations or that is produced under the exemption provided for
in section 19 of these Regulations --- of which the member has come into
possession during a particular investigation, if the member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in active service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is acting in the course of the member's
  responsibilities for the purposes of the particular investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}RCMP notification --- importation or
exportation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A member of a police force
or of the military police who is exempt under section 3 of these
Regulations from the application of section 11 of the Act must notify,
in written or electronic format, the Assistant Commissioner of the RCMP
in charge of drug enforcement of the importation or exportation of
cannabis, in respect of which the exemption applies, by the member ---
or by a person under the member's direction and control who is exempt

not feasible to do so before the importation or exportation, as soon as
feasible after that time.

### [Other General Exemptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851567 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conspiracy, etc. --- police

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A member of a police
force or of the military police is exempt from the application of the
provisions that create the offence of conspiracy to commit, an attempt
to commit, being an accessory after the fact in relation to, or any
counselling in relation to, an offence under section 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or
14 of the Act if the member

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in active service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is acting in the course of the member's
  responsibilities for the purposes of a particular investigation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} engages in any activity that, but for the application
  of this section, would constitute a conspiracy to commit, an attempt
  to commit, being an accessory after the fact in relation to, or any
  counselling in relation to, an offence under section 8, 9, 10, 11, 12
  or 14 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conspiracy, etc. --- direction and
control

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person is exempt from
the application of the provisions that create the offence of conspiracy
to commit, an attempt to commit, being an accessory after the fact in
relation to, or any counselling in relation to, an offence under section
8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 14 of the Act if the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acts under the direction and control of a member of a
  police force or of the military police who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is in active service, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is acting in the course of the member's
    responsibilities for the purposes of a particular investigation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acts to assist the member in the course of the
  particular investigation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} engages in any activity that, but for the application
  of this section, would constitute a conspiracy to commit, an attempt
  to commit, being an accessory after the fact in relation to, or any
  counselling in relation to, an offence under section 8, 9, 10, 11, 12
  or 14 of the Act.

### [Other Regulations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851580 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Regulations --- police

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A member of a police
force or of the military police who is exempt under these Regulations
from the application of section 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 14 of the Act in
respect of engaging in any activity referred to in that section is
exempt, in respect of engaging in that activity, from the application of

the direction and control of a member of a police force or of the
military police and who is exempt under these Regulations from the
application of section 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 14 of the Act in respect of
engaging in any activity referred to in that section is exempt, in
respect of engaging in that activity, from the application of the
[Cannabis Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144) and the
[Industrial Hemp Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-145).

## [Exemptions with Certificate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851585 .Part}

### [Section 9 or 10 of the Act --- Distribution or Sale]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851586 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}State cannabis --- distribution or sale

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 8 of these
  Regulations, a member of a police force or of the military police is
  exempt from the application of section 9 or 10 of the Act, as
  applicable, if the member engages in any activity referred to in those
  sections involving cannabis that has been forfeited to Her Majesty,
  that is imported under the exemption provided for in section 17 of

  certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuing certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate officer may issue a certificate for a
  period not exceeding six months for the purposes of subsection (1) to
  a member of a police force or of the military police, as the case may
  be, if the member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in active service; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is acting in the course of the member's
    responsibilities for the purposes of a particular investigation.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9 of
these Regulations, a person is exempt from the application of section 9
or 10 of the Act, as applicable, if the person engages in any activity
referred to in those sections involving cannabis that has been forfeited
to Her Majesty, that is imported under the exemption provided for in
section 17 of these Regulations or that is produced under the exemption
provided for in section 19 of these Regulations, if the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acts under the direction and control of a member of a
  police force or of the military police who meets the conditions set
  out in paragraphs 14(2)(a) and (b) of these Regulations; and

  particular investigation.

### [Section 11 of the Act --- Importation or Exportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851598 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- controlled deliveries

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 17(1) and section 18, cannabis requested of and obtained
directly from a foreign state does not include cannabis that has, for
the purpose of identifying any person involved in the commission of an
offence under the Act or any other Act of Parliament or a conspiracy to
commit such an offence, been allowed to pass out of or through a foreign
state, under the supervision of that state's competent authorities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}State cannabis --- importation or
exportation

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  member of a police force or of the military police is exempt from the
  application of section 11 of the Act if the member engages in any
  activity referred to in that section involving cannabis that has been
  forfeited to Her Majesty, that is produced under the exemption
  provided for in section 19 of these Regulations or that has been
  requested of and obtained directly from a foreign state, if the member
  has been issued a certificate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuing certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Assistant Commissioner of the RCMP in charge of
  drug enforcement may issue a certificate to a member of a police force
  or of the military police, as the case may be, for a period not
  exceeding six months for the purposes of subsection (1) if the member

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is in active service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member of a police force, is
    acting in the course of their responsibilities for the purposes of a
    particular investigation in which the RCMP participates; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a member of the military police, is
    acting in the course of their responsibilities for the purposes of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a particular investigation in which the RCMP
      participates, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a particular investigation other than one
      referred to in subparagraph (i).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction and control --- importation or
exportation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A person is exempt from
the application of section 11 of the Act if the person engages in any
activity referred to in that section that involves cannabis that has
been forfeited to Her Majesty, that is produced under the exemption
provided for in section 19 of these Regulations or that has been
requested of and obtained directly from a foreign state, if the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} acts under the direction and control of a member of a
  police force or of the military police who meets the applicable
  conditions set out in paragraphs 17(2)(a) to (c) of these Regulations;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} acts to assist the member in the course of the
  particular investigation.

### [Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851627 .Subheading}

### [Revocation of Certificate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-851630 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  certificate issued under section 14, 17 or 19 to a member of a police
  force or of the military police is revoked on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the appropriate officer who
    issued the certificate revokes it,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the member is no longer in active
    service,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the member is no longer acting in
    the course of their responsibilities for the purposes of the
    particular investigation to which the certificate relates,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the particular investigation to
    which the certificate relates is completed, or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the certificate expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The appropriate officer must notify the member of the
  revocation on the day on which the certificate is revoked under
  paragraph (1)(a), (c) or (d).

## [Detention and Disposal of Forfeited Cannabis]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851641 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- cannabis required

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate officer must, as soon as feasible but not later
  than 60 days after the day on which cannabis that has been forfeited
  to Her Majesty is no longer required for the preliminary inquiry,
  trial or other proceeding, under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament, in respect of which it was seized, if the cannabis is
  required for the purposes of conducting investigations under the Act
  or any other Act of Parliament, inform the Minister in writing that
  the cannabis is required for those investigations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secure location

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Cannabis referred to in subsection (1) must be kept
  in a secure location while not being used for the purposes of
  conducting investigations under the Act or any other Act of
  Parliament.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate officer is exempt from the
  application of section 9 of the Act if they transfer any cannabis
  referred to in subsection (1) to another chief or another appropriate
  officer who requests the transfer for the purposes of a particular
  investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister --- transfer

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a transfer is made under subsection (3), the chief
  or appropriate officer who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} makes the transfer of the cannabis must inform the
    Minister of the transfer, as soon as feasible after receipt of the
    request for the transfer; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} receives the cannabis must inform the Minister of
    its receipt, as soon as feasible after the receipt.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions --- cannabis not required

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If cannabis referred to in subsection (1) is no
  longer required for the purposes of conducting investigations under
  the Act or any other Act of Parliament, the chief or appropriate
  officer must seek the directions of the Minister and dispose of or
  otherwise deal with the cannabis in accordance with the Minister's
  directions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposal --- cannabis not required

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If cannabis that has been forfeited to Her Majesty is
  not required for the purposes of conducting investigations under the
  Act or any other Act of Parliament, the chief or appropriate officer
  must, as soon as feasible,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in writing seek directions from the Minister
    respecting the disposal of or otherwise dealing with the cannabis,
    unless the Minister has previously given such directions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dispose of or otherwise deal with the cannabis in
    accordance with the Minister's directions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Minister

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The chief or appropriate
officer must, within 60 days after the day on which cannabis is disposed
of or otherwise dealt with under section 105 of the Act, notify the
Minister in writing to that effect.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-851661 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate officer must submit to the Minister of Public
  Safety and Emergency Preparedness and to the Minister, within three
  months after the end of every calendar year, a report in written or
  electronic format containing the information referred to in subsection
  (4), in respect of cannabis that is imported or exported under the
  exemption provided for in section 17, cannabis that is produced under
  the exemption provided for in section 19 and cannabis that is referred
  to in section 23, that came into the possession of the police force or
  of the military police in the course of a particular investigation
  completed during the calendar year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report --- police force other
  than RCMP

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate officer of a police force
  other than the RCMP must send a copy of the report to the provincial
  minister responsible for the police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report --- military police

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Forces Provost Marshal must send a copy
  of the report to the Minister of National Defence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the type of particular investigation in which the
    cannabis came into the possession of the police force or of the
    military police;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the dates on which the particular investigation
    began and ended;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of cannabis that was forfeited
    to Her Majesty, imported, exported, produced or disposed of in the
    course of the particular investigation, as applicable; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, any other detail that is pertinent
    to describing the law enforcement activities undertaken by the
    police force or by the military police in the course of the
    particular investigation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate officer of a police force
  must submit to the Minister, on the Minister's request, a report in
  written or electronic format respecting the cannabis as required for
  the following purposes:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to ensure the protection of the public against
    potential public health risks caused by the cannabis, including the
    risk of it being diverted to an illicit market or activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to collect data required for studies and research;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to meet international obligations of the Government
    of Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} to monitor compliance with these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- cannabis no longer in
possession

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chief or appropriate officer must submit to the Minister of Public
  Safety and Emergency Preparedness and to the Minister a report in
  written or electronic format containing the information referred to in
  subsection (4), respecting any cannabis referred to in section 23 that
  is lost, stolen or otherwise no longer in the possession of the police
  force or of the military police, as soon as feasible after such a
  situation occurs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report --- police force other
  than RCMP

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The chief or appropriate officer of a police force
  other than the RCMP must send a copy of the report to the provincial
  minister responsible for the police force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy of report --- military police

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Canadian Forces Provost Marshal must send a copy
  of the report to the Minister of National Defence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of cannabis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of its forfeiture to Her Majesty or its
    importation, exportation or production, as applicable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which, and an explanation of the
    circumstances in which, it was lost or stolen or ceased to be in the
    possession of the police force or of the military police.
