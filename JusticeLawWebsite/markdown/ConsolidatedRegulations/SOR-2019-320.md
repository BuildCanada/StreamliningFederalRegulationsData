## [Water Control Structures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197591 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works --- water control structures

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following water control
structures are designated as major works:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} dams that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are capable of impounding at least 30 000 m^3^ of
    water, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are at least 2.5 m high, measured from the bed of
    the navigable water; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} structures for the diversion of water if they

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are placed across a navigable water,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} divert water from a navigable water into another
    navigable water, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} change the water level or water flow of at least
    one of those navigable waters.

## [Ferry Cables]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197601 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works --- ferry cables

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Ferry
  cables are designated as major works.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[ferry
  cable]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[ferry
  cable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any cable, rod, chain or other
  device put across, over, under or in any navigable water for working a
  ferry.

## [Bridges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197607 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works --- bridges

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following bridges are designated as major works:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable span bridges;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} floating span bridges; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fixed span bridges with one or more piers below the
    ordinary high water mark.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[floating span
  bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[floating span
  bridge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bridge that is built on floating
  anchored foundations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary works

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Temporary works that are
installed for a period of at least 30 consecutive days for the
construction, placement, alteration, rebuilding, removal,
decommissioning, repair or maintenance of a bridge are designated as
major works, unless they are installed during a period when navigation
is not possible.

## [Causeways]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197618 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works --- causeways

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Causeways that are placed
across a navigable water are designated as major works.

## [Aquaculture Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197621 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Works --- aquaculture facilities

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Aquaculture facilities are
designated as major works.
