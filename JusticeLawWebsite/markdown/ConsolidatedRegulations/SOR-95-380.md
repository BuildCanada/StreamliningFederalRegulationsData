## Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-2 {#safeguard-surtax-regulations-1995-2 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-380
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1995-08-01

Regulations Prescribing Agricultural Goods in Respect of Any Country for
the Purposes of Imposing a Safeguard Surtax, 1995-2

P.C. 1995-1278  1995-07-26
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.01(7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6357){#fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6357-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6358){#fn_SOR-95-380_e_hq_6358-ID0EDCAA},
is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing
agricultural goods in respect of any country for the purpose of imposing
a safeguard surtax, 1995-2, effective August 1, 1995.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975307 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Safeguard Surtax Regulations,
1995-2](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-380).

## [Goods Subject to Safeguard Surtax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975309 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
68 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), the following
agricultural goods, classified under the tariff item numbers that follow
them, are prescribed in respect of any country:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} cream (0401.30.10, 0401.30.20);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} powdered whey (0404.10.21, 0404.10.22); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} butter and other fats and oils derived from milk
  (0405.10.10, 0405.10.20, 0405.90.10, 0405.90.20).
