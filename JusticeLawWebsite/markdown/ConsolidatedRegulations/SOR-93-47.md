## [Registry and Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954863 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall establish
one or more offices for the activities of the Adjudication Division,
each of which is to be known as a registry.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Board shall designate a
person to be registrar at each registry.

## [Request for Inquiry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954866 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  inquiry is caused to be held pursuant to the Act, the senior
  immigration officer shall forward to the Adjudication Division a
  request for inquiry that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, sex, date of birth, civil status and
    citizenship of the person concerned;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the address and telephone number in Canada of the
    person concerned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any report and direction that relate to the person
    concerned;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to whether the person concerned
    claims to be a Convention refugee;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement as to whether the person concerned is
    detained and if so, the place of detention;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where any members of the family who are dependent
    on the person concerned and who are in Canada are included for the
    purposes of subsection 33(1) of the Act, the name, sex, date of
    birth, civil status, citizenship, address and telephone number of
    those dependent family members;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the official language chosen by the person
    concerned for communication with the Adjudication Division;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the language or dialect of the
    interpreter required by the person concerned for any proceeding;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission
    Client I.D. Number relating to the person concerned;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the names of the family members
    of the person concerned who have been referred to the Adjudication
    Division for an inquiry and the file number of the Canada Employment
    and Immigration Commission relating to those family members;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the senior immigration officer
    forwards the request for inquiry; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the name of the senior immigration officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The senior immigration officer shall forward the
  information referred to in subrule (1) to the registry designated by
  the Adjudication Division.

## [Joinder]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954882 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Adjudication Division may order that two or more inquiries be held
  jointly where the Adjudication Division believes that no injustice is
  thereby likely to be caused to any party.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), inquiries in respect of
  the legal or [de facto]{.otherLang lang="la"} spouse, dependant
  children, father, mother, brothers or sisters of the person concerned
  shall be held jointly.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On application by a party, or on the adjudicator's
  own motion at the time of the inquiry, the adjudicator may order that
  inquiries be held separately, where the adjudicator believes that
  holding hearings jointly is likely to cause an injustice to any party.

## [Change of Venue]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954887 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  may apply in accordance with rule 19 to the Adjudication Division to
  have a conference or hearing held at a place other than that which has
  been set.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application made under subrule (1) shall be
  accompanied by a statement of facts to support the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudication Division shall grant the application
  made under subrule (1) if it is satisfied that doing so will not
  adversely affect the proper operation of the Adjudication Division,
  will provide for a full and proper hearing and will dispose of the
  inquiry expeditiously.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where an application made under subrule (1) is
  granted, the file relating to the inquiry shall be transferred to the
  registry of the place where the conference or hearing is to be held.

## [Postponement and Adjournment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954893 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before
  the commencement of a hearing, a party may apply in accordance with
  rule 19 to the Adjudication Division to have the hearing postponed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before the resumption of a hearing, a party may apply
  in accordance with rule 19 to the Adjudication Division to have the
  hearing adjourned.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A party whose application for a postponement or
  adjournment was denied may reapply orally at the commencement or
  resumption of the hearing.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudication Division, in determining whether a
  hearing shall be postponed or adjourned, may take into consideration,
  where applicable,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether the postponement or adjournment would
    unreasonably impede the proceeding;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made by the parties to proceed
    expeditiously;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the nature and complexity of the issues relevant to
    the proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the evidence to be presented, and the
    likelihood of causing an injustice to any party by proceeding in the
    absence of that evidence;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} counsel's knowledge of, and experience with,
    similar proceedings;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the amount of time already afforded the parties for
    preparation of the case;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made by the parties to be present at
    the hearing;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the efforts made by the parties to make an
    application for a postponement or adjournment of the hearing at the
    earliest opportunity;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the number of, and reasons for, any previous
    postponements or adjournments;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} whether the hearing was set peremptorily; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} any other relevant facts.

## [Notice to Appear]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954910 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Adjudication Division directs a party to appear before it at a
  conference or hearing, the Adjudication Division shall, in a notice to
  appear, set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and place set for the conference or
    hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address in Canada of the person
    concerned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission
    Client I.D. Number relating to the person concerned;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the names and addresses of the family members of
    the person concerned referred to in paragraph 5(1)(f);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the conference or hearing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the right of the person concerned and of any person
    referred to in paragraph (d) to be represented by counsel at their
    own expense;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the requirement that the person concerned inform
    the Adjudication Division forthwith, in writing, of any change of
    address of the person concerned or of counsel of the person
    concerned; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} that the person concerned could be arrested for
    failing to appear at the date, time and place set for the conference
    or hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudication Division shall serve the parties and
  any person referred to in paragraph (1)(d) with the notice to appear
  and a copy of any report and direction that relate to the person
  concerned who is subject of the inquiry.

## [Official Languages]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954922 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** At least 15 days before
the date set for a conference or hearing, as the case may be, the person
concerned shall notify the Adjudication Division, in writing, of the
official language in which the person concerned chooses the conference
or hearing to be conducted.

## [Interpreter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954924 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Adjudication Division shall provide an interpreter to assist a party
  or a party's witness where the party advises the Adjudication
  Division, in writing, at least 15 days before the date set for a
  conference or hearing, as the case may be, that the party or witness
  does not understand or speak the language in which the conference or
  hearing is to be conducted, or is hearing impaired.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interpreter shall take an oath or make a solemn
  affirmation to interpret accurately any statements made, and to
  translate accurately any documents that the Adjudication Division may
  require to be translated, in the course of the conference or hearing.

## [Conferences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954928 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Adjudication Division may direct the parties, by a notice to appear,
  to attend a conference, that is to be held before or during a hearing,
  on any matter related to the inquiry, in order to provide for a full
  and proper hearing and to dispose of the inquiry expeditiously.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the conference the participants shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exchange the names and addresses of witnesses that
    they intend to call, copies of any document that they intend to
    produce and of any statement of the facts, information and opinions
    of which they are in possession and that they intend to produce as
    evidence at the hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} attempt to agree on means of simplifying the
    hearing and, as far as possible, define any pertinent issues, agree
    upon certain facts and receive documents.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An agreement concluded at a conference shall be in
  writing and signed by the participants or given orally at the hearing
  and shall govern the hearing to the extent that the Adjudication
  Division considers appropriate in order to provide for a full and
  proper hearing and to dispose of the inquiry expeditiously.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Forthwith after a conference, the adjudicator shall
  forward to the registry any written agreement concluded and a true
  copy of any documents that have been received during the conference.

## [Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954936 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A party who intends to
call an expert witness at a hearing shall, at least 20 days before the
date set for the hearing, serve on the other party a report signed by
the expert witness, setting out the name, address and qualifications of
the expert witness and a brief summary of the substance of the proposed
testimony of the expert witness.

## [Applications for Confidentiality]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954938 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An application referred
to in subsection 29(2) or 103(10) of the Act shall be made to the
Adjudication Division by motion in accordance with subrules 20(2) to
(7).

## [Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954940 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A party who calls a
witness shall pay the witness the applicable fees and allowances set out
in the Federal Court Rules.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Adjudication Division may, at the request of a party or of its own
  motion, exclude witnesses from a hearing until they are called to
  testify.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person may communicate to a witness who has been
  excluded pursuant to subrule (1) any evidence or testimony that is
  given during the course of a hearing until after the witness has
  testified.

<!-- -->

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who makes an application to summon a witness shall do so in
  writing and shall file it at the registry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a person fails to comply with a summons, the
  party who applied for the issuance of the summons may make an
  application in writing, accompanied by an affidavit setting out the
  facts on which the application is based, to the Adjudication Division
  for a warrant and shall file the application at the registry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An application made under to subrule (2) shall
  contain information to establish that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person named in the summons was served with the
    summons and was paid or offered a reasonable amount of attendance
    money but failed to comply with the requirements of the summons; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the appearance of the person named in the summons
    is necessary to ensure a full and proper hearing.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A warrant issued on an application made under
  subrule (2) that directs a peace officer to cause the person named
  therein to be apprehended anywhere in Canada shall indicate the
  measures to be taken by the peace officer in respect of the detention
  or release of the person.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The measures referred to in subrule (4) may include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} detaining the person in custody and forthwith
    bringing the person before the Adjudication Division; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} releasing the person on a recognizance, with or
    without sureties, conditional on the person's appearance at the
    date, time and place specified, to give or produce evidence at a
    hearing before the Adjudication Division.

## [Person Concerned in Custody]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954955 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where a person concerned
is detained, the Adjudication Division may order the person who detains
the person concerned to bring the latter in custody to a conference or
hearing held in respect of the person concerned.

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954957 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), every
  application that is provided for in these Rules, except an application
  referred to in rules 14 and 17 and subrule 21(3), shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} made by a party in writing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filed at the registry; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} served on the other party.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party may make an application that is provided for
  in these Rules orally at a hearing where the adjudicator is satisfied
  that no injustice is likely to be caused to any party thereby.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudication Division may determine an
  application when it is satisfied that all parties have been given a
  reasonable opportunity to make representations.

## [Motions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954965 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  application that is not provided for in these Rules shall be made by a
  party to the Adjudication Division by motion, unless, where the
  application is made during a hearing, the adjudicator decides that, in
  the interests of justice, the application should be dealt with in some
  other manner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The motion shall consist of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a notice specifying the grounds on which the motion
    is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an affidavit setting out the facts on which the
    motion is based; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the law and of the arguments
    that are relied on by the applicant.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The motion shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} served on the other party to the proceeding; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filed in duplicate, together with proof of service
    thereof, at the registry within five days after the date of service.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Evidence in support of a motion shall be introduced
  by affidavit, unless the Adjudication Division decides that, in the
  interests of justice, the evidence should be introduced in some other
  manner.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The other party may, within seven days after being
  served with a motion, file at the registry a reply stating concisely
  the law and arguments relied on by the party, accompanied by an
  affidavit setting out the facts on which the reply is based.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The applicant may, within seven days after being
  served with a reply, file a response thereto at the registry.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the reply and affidavit filed pursuant to
  subrule (5) and of the response filed pursuant to subrule (6) shall be
  served on the other party within seven days after the date of service
  of the motion or reply, as the case may be.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The Adjudication Division, on being satisfied that no
  injustice is likely to be caused, may dispose of a motion without a
  hearing.

## [Decisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954980 .Part}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  at the conclusion of an inquiry, the adjudicator decides to make a
  removal order or conditional removal order against a person concerned,
  the adjudicator shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} date and sign the order and decision that sets out
    the basis on which the order was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person concerned does not have grounds to
    appeal the decision to the Appeal Division pursuant to section 70 of
    the Act, inform the person and the person's counsel by a notice in
    writing of the person's right to file an application for leave to
    commence an application under section 18.1 of the [Federal Courts
    Act](/eng/acts/F-7).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of the documents referred to in paragraphs
  (1)(a) and (b) shall be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the parties or their counsel, or both, are
    present when the Adjudicator makes the order, given forthwith to the
    parties or their counsel or both, as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the parties or their counsel, or both, are
    absent when the adjudicator makes the order, served on the parties
    or their counsel or both, as the case may be.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The adjudicator shall, upon a request in writing of a
  party that is filed within 10 days after the conclusion of an inquiry,
  provide written reasons for the adjudicator's decision.

## [Service and Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-954991 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (5), service of
  any document in the course of any proceeding under these Rules shall
  be effected by personal service, by prepaid regular mail, by any mail
  service whereby the sender is provided with an acknowledgement of
  receipt, or by the telephone transmission of a facsimile of the
  document in accordance with subrule (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of any document served on a party pursuant to
  subrule (1) shall also be served on that party's counsel, if any.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by telephone transmission
  of a facsimile shall include a cover page setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sender's name, address and telephone number;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the addressee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including
    the cover page;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the telephone number from which the document is
    transmitted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of a person to
    contact in the event of transmission problems.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Service by prepaid regular mail shall be considered
  to be effected on the date that is seven days after the date of
  mailing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where service cannot be effected in accordance with
  subrule (1), the Chairperson may direct that service be effected by
  such means as will, to the extent possible, provide a party with the
  document and as will not likely cause injustice to any party.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} After service of a document has been effected by a
  party, the party shall file proof of service thereof at the registry.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The person concerned and
any family members of the person concerned referred to in paragraph
5(1)(f) shall notify the Adjudication Division forthwith in writing of
their addresses and the name and address for service of their counsel,
and any changes thereto.

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document shall be filed at the registry by serving it on an employee
  of the registry at which the file relating to the inquiry or the
  application referred to in subrule 29(1) is held.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The filing of a telephone transmission of a facsimile
  shall be considered to be effected on the date on which it is received
  by the registrar, as stamped on the facsimile.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All documents submitted in the course of any
  proceeding under these Rules in a language other than English or
  French shall be accompanied by a translation in English or French that
  is certified to be correct, unless the Adjudication Division decides
  that a translation is not necessary to provide for a full and proper
  hearing.

## [Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955009 .Part}

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The Adjudication Division
may, on application by a party made in accordance with rule 19 either
before or after a time limit set out in these Rules has expired, shorten
or extend the time limit, in order to provide for a full and proper
hearing.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955011 .Part}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** These Rules are not
exhaustive and, where any matter that is not provided for in these Rules
arises in the course of any proceeding, the Adjudication Division may
take whatever measures are necessary to provide for a full and proper
hearing and to dispose of the matter expeditiously.

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** Where a party does not
comply with a requirement of these Rules, the Adjudication Division, on
application made by the party in accordance with rule 19, may permit the
party to remedy the non-compliance or may waive the requirement, where
it is satisfied that no injustice is thereby likely to be caused to any
party or the proceeding will not be unreasonably impeded.

## [Review of Reasons for Detention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955014 .Part}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  pursuant to subsection 103(6) of the Act, a person concerned must be
  brought before an adjudicator for a review of the reasons for the
  continued detention of the person concerned, a senior immigration
  officer shall forward to the Adjudication Division forthwith the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date and time before which the hearing to
    review the reasons for the continued detention must be held;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and place of detention of the person
    concerned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Canada Employment and Immigration Commission
    Client I.D. Number relating to the person concerned;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the official language chosen by the person
    concerned for the hearing for the purposes of rule 10;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the language or dialect of the
    interpreter required by the person concerned for the purposes of
    rule 11;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, address for service and telephone number
    of the counsel of the person concerned, if any;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date and time at which the person concerned was
    detained;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the purpose of the detention; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} whether the review is being held after 48 hours,
    seven days or 30 days of detention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The senior immigration officer shall forward the
  information referred to in subrule (1) to the registry designated by
  the Adjudication Division.

<!-- -->

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a person concerned referred to in subrule 28(1) wishes to be brought
  before an adjudicator for a review of the reasons for detention
  pursuant to subsection 103(6) of the Act, the person concerned shall
  so apply to the Adjudication Division in accordance with rule 19.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application referred to in subrule (1) shall be
  accompanied by information referred to in paragraphs 28(1)(d) and (e)
  and a statement that sets out the reasons in support of the
  application.

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** Rule 7 and the time
limits referred to in rules 10 and 11 do not apply in respect of the
review of the reasons for detention referred to in rules 28 or 29.
