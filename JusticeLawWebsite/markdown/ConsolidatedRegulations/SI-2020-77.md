[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is granted to any person who is a
[[licensee]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of the [Radio
Regulations, 1986](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-982), section 2 of the
[Television Broadcasting Regulations, 1987](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-49)
or section 1 of the [Discretionary Services
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-159) of an amount equal to the
licence fees paid or payable under paragraph 3(b) of the [Broadcasting
Licence Fee Regulations, 1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-144), including
any interest on those fees, for the period beginning on April 1, 2020
and ending on March 31, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on the
condition that the licensee referred to in section 1

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides an attestation to the Canadian
  Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission, on or before
  January 31, 2021, that the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19)
  pandemic has had a significant financial impact causing an estimated
  reduction of 25% or more in revenues related to the broadcasting
  undertaking;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} continues to operate the broadcasting undertaking for
  at least two years after the day on which the remission is granted;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} complies with the requirements under the
  [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) and the conditions of their
  licence, in respect of the exhibition of Canadian content, for at
  least two years after the day on which the remission is granted.
