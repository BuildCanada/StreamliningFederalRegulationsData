## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1334877 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include a product for
      use on humans or animals or any product with an adhesive
      incorporated onto or in an inert
      substrate. ([adhésif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[fragrance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[fragrance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance or mixture of
      chemicals, natural essential oils or other components, that has a
      combined vapour pressure that is less than or equal to 0.267 kPa
      when measured at 20°C, the sole purpose of which is to impart a
      scent or to mask an unpleasant odour. ([parfum]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[high vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[high vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
      antiperspirant or deodorant for the human axilla, means a VOC that
      has a vapour pressure of greater than 10.67 kPa when measured at
      20°C. ([COV à pression de vapeur élevée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[low vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[low vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      product other than an antiperspirant or deodorant for the human
      axilla, means a VOC that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a vapour pressure of less than 0.013 kPa
        when measured at 20°C;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a boiling point that is greater than 216°C;
        or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} contains more than 12 carbon atoms per
        molecule. ([COV à faible pression de vapeur]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[medium vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medium vapour pressure VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
      an antiperspirant or deodorant for the human axilla, means a VOC
      that has a vapour pressure of greater than 0.267 kPa but less than
      or equal to 10.67 kPa when measured at 20°C. ([COV à pression de
      vapeur moyenne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reformulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reformulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that
      belongs to a product category set out in column 1 of the table to
      Schedule 1 and that has been reformulated to reduce its VOC
      concentration to a level that is less than the maximum VOC
      concentration set out in column 3 for that product category or, if
      applicable, the subcategory set out in column 2 to which it
      belongs. ([produit à composition modifiée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a volatile organic compound that
      participates in atmospheric photochemical reactions and that is
      not excluded under item 65 of Schedule 1 to the [Canadian
      Environmental Protection Act,
      1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([COV]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}VOC concentration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, the VOC
  concentration of a product is measured and expressed as a percentage
  of the product's net weight (% w/w).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Net quantity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the quantity
  of a product or the quantity of VOCs in a product is the net quantity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Product category or subcategory

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a product
  belongs to a product category set out in column 1 of the table to
  Schedule 1 or column 1 of Schedule 2 or, if applicable, a subcategory
  set out in column 2 of the table to Schedule 1 if, according to
  information on its container or included in any documentation relating
  to the product that is supplied by the product's manufacturer or
  importer or their authorized representative, the product may be used
  as a product that belongs to that product category or, if applicable,
  subcategory.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Design

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, any reference
  to a product that is designed for a particular purpose includes a
  product that, according to information on its container or included in
  any documentation relating to the product that is supplied by the
  product's manufacturer or importer or their authorized representative,
  may be used for that purpose.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1334899 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Products

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of any product that contains VOCs and
  belongs to a product category set out in column 1 of the table to
  Schedule 1 or column 1 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- certain products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of products
  that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} designed to be used solely in a manufacturing or
    processing activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to be used solely in a laboratory for analysis, in
    scientific research or as a laboratory analytical standard;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} regulated under the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} manufactured or imported for export only;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} adhesives that are to be sold in containers of 30
    ml or less;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} regulated under the [Volatile Organic Compound
    (VOC) Concentration Limits for Architectural Coatings
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-264) or the [Volatile Organic
    Compound (VOC) Concentration Limits for Automotive Refinishing
    Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-197);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} used in or on a new car at the time of its
    manufacture; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in transit through Canada, from a place outside
    Canada to another place outside Canada.

## [Maximum VOC Concentrations and Maximum VOC Emission Potentials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1334913 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), a person must not manufacture or import a product
  that belongs to a product category set out in column 1 of the table to
  Schedule 1 and, if applicable, a subcategory set out in column 2 that
  has a VOC concentration that is greater than the applicable maximum
  VOC concentration set out in column 3, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product is, according to the instructions set
    out in both official languages on the product's container or in any
    accompanying documentation, to be diluted before use to a level at
    which the VOC concentration is less than or equal to the applicable
    maximum VOC concentration set out in column 3; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a permit has been issued under section 9, 16 or 19
    in respect of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Product categories set out in Schedule
  2

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a person must not
  manufacture or import a product that belongs to a product category set
  out in column 1 of Schedule 2 and has a VOC emission potential that is
  greater than the applicable maximum VOC emission potential set out in
  column 2, unless a permit has been issued under section 19 in respect
  of the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of paragraph (1)(a)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The exception set out in paragraph (1)(a) does not
  apply to a multi-purpose solvent or paint thinner referred to in items
  48 and 52, respectively, of the table to Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Start date for prohibitions

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The prohibitions set out in subsections (1) and (2)
  apply beginning on

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a disinfectant referred to in item
    31 of the table to Schedule 1, January 1 of the year following the
    calendar year of the third anniversary of the day on which these
    Regulations are registered; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, January 1 of the year following
    the calendar year of the second anniversary of the day on which
    these Regulations are registered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Product belonging to more than one
product category

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if a product belongs to more than one product
  category set out in column 1 of the table to Schedule 1, the
  applicable maximum VOC concentration is the lowest of the maximum VOC
  concentrations set out in column 3 for the product categories to which
  the product belongs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for certain categories

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum VOC concentration for a product that
  belongs to one of the following product categories is the maximum VOC
  concentration set out in column 3 of the table to Schedule 1 for the
  applicable product category set out in column 1 or, if applicable,
  subcategory set out in column 2, regardless of whether the product
  also belongs to a product category with a lower maximum VOC
  concentration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} antiperspirant for the human axilla referred to in
    item 2 of the table to Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deodorant for the human axilla referred to in item
    3 of that table;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} hair products referred to in item 6 of that table;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} general-purpose cleaner referred to in item 42 of
    that table.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of VOC concentration

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of these Regulations and subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the VOC concentration of a product that belongs to a product category
  set out in column 1 of the table to Schedule 1 is determined by the
  formula

  ::: {#1334939 .Subsection}
  \[(W~S~ − W~EX~) ÷ W~P~\] × 100

  where

  [W~S~]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight in grams of all of the substances contained in the
      product that volatize when the product is tested to determine its
      VOC concentration for the purposes of these Regulations;

  [W~EX~]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight in grams of all of the substances to be excluded
      when determining the VOC concentration, namely, any of the
      following substances contained in the product that volatize when
      the product is tested to determine its VOC concentration for the
      purposes of these Regulations:
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} water, ammonia and any other inorganic
        substances;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} compounds that are excluded under item 65 of
        Schedule 1 to the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
        1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31);

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an antiperspirant or deodorant
        for the human axilla referred to in items 2 and 3, respectively,
        of the table to Schedule 1,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} VOCs that have a vapour pressure of less than
          or equal to 0.267 kPa when measured at 20°C or, if the vapour
          pressure is unknown, that contain more than 10 carbon atoms
          per molecule,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} colourants and fragrances that, combined,
          constitute 2% or less of the product's net weight, and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} ethanol;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a personal fragrance product
        referred to in item 11 of that table,

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} low vapour pressure VOCs, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fragrances;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a pressurized gas duster
        referred to in item 53 of that table, low vapour pressure VOCs;
        and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a product other than a product
        referred to in paragraph (c), (d) or (e),

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} low vapour pressure VOCs, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fragrances that, combined, constitute 2% or
          less of the product's net weight; and

  [W~P~]{.dfn}
  :   is the net weight of the product, expressed in grams.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aerosol products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the descriptions W~S~ and W~EX~,
  if the product in question belongs to a subcategory set out in column
  2 of the table to Schedule 1 that includes aerosol products, the
  propellant and non-propellant fractions are determined separately then
  added together to give the total value for each of those elements.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Antiperspirants and deodorants

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the product in question is an antiperspirant or
  deodorant for the human axilla referred to in items 2 and 3,
  respectively, of the table to Schedule 1, the VOC concentration is
  determined separately for medium vapour pressure VOCs and high vapour
  pressure VOCs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of VOC emission potential

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** When determining the VOC
emission potential for the purposes of these Regulations, the following
substances are excluded:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} low vapour pressure VOCs; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of charcoal lighter products referred to
  in item 1 of Schedule 2, fragrances that, combined, constitute 2% or
  less of the product's net weight.

## [VOC Compliance Unit Trading System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1334965 .Part}

### [Participation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1334966 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose of compliance unit trading system

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person that manufactures
or imports a product that belongs to a product category set out in
column 1 of the table to Schedule 1 may elect to participate in a
compliance unit trading system that allows participants to do one or
more of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} generate compliance units in accordance with section
  11 in respect of all of the reformulated products for which they have
  elected to participate in the compliance unit trading system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfer unused compliance units to another person in
  accordance with section 12; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} use compliance units that are generated by them or
  transferred to them to compensate for the excess quantity of VOCs
  determined in accordance with paragraph 13(d) in respect of a product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permit --- participants in compliance
unit trading system

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  that elects to participate in the compliance unit trading system may
  apply for a permit authorizing them to manufacture or import a product
  that belongs to a product category set out in column 1 of the table to
  Schedule 1 and, if applicable, a subcategory set out in column 2 that
  has a VOC concentration greater than the applicable maximum VOC
  concentration set out in column 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be submitted to the Minister and
  must contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address of their
    authorized representative, if applicable;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each product in respect of which a permit is
    sought,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its common or generic name and its trade name, if
      any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the product category set out in column 1 of the
      table to Schedule 1 and, if applicable, the subcategory set out in
      column 2 to which it belongs, as well as the information used to
      categorize it,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the VOC concentrations at which the applicant
      expects to manufacture or import the product,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for each VOC concentration referred to in
      subparagraph (iii), the quantity of the product, expressed in
      kilograms, that the applicant expects to manufacture or import per
      calendar year, excluding any quantity that is manufactured or
      imported for export only, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for each VOC concentration referred to in
      subparagraph (iii), the quantity of VOCs in the product that are
      in excess of the applicable maximum VOC concentration, determined
      by the formula

      ::: {#1334985 .Subparagraph}
      (A − B) × W

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is the VOC concentration of the product,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the maximum VOC concentration set out in column 3 of the
          table to Schedule 1 for the product category set out in column
          1 or, if applicable, the subcategory set out in column 2 to
          which the product belongs, and

      [W]{.dfn}
      :   is the quantity of the product, expressed in kilograms, that
          the applicant expects to manufacture or import per calendar
          year, excluding the quantity that is to be manufactured or
          imported for export only; and
      :::

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a plan indicating how the applicant intends to
    compensate for the excess quantity of VOCs determined in accordance
    with subparagraph (c)(v) for all of the products in respect of which
    a permit is sought by using compliance units generated by them or
    transferred to them in accordance with sections 11 and 12.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on receiving the application,
  require any clarifications that are necessary for the application to
  be processed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of change to information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The applicant must notify the Minister in writing of
  any change to the information provided under this section --- other
  than that provided under subparagraph (2)(c)(iv) --- within 30 days
  after the day on which the change occurs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister must issue the permit referred to in
  subsection 8(1) if the applicant has demonstrated how they will
  compensate for the excess quantity of VOCs determined in accordance
  with subparagraph 8(2)(c)(v).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue the permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the applicant has provided false or misleading information in
    support of their application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information required under subsections 8(2)
    to (4) and the certification required under section 26 have not been
    provided or are insufficient to enable the Minister to process the
    application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation --- grounds

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must revoke a permit issued under subsection 9(1) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the permit holder has not submitted the annual
    report referred to in section 13 within the prescribed time limit;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the permit holder has not compensated for the excess quantity of
    VOCs determined in accordance with paragraph 13(d); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the permit holder has provided false or misleading information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before revoking a permit, the Minister must provide
  the permit holder with written reasons and an opportunity to make
  written representations concerning the revocation.

### [Generation, Use and Transfer of Compliance Units]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1335010 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of participation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that intends to generate compliance units in respect of a
  reformulated product that they manufacture or import must, no later
  than October 1 of the first year in which they elect to participate in
  the compliance unit trading system in respect of that product, submit
  a notice to the Minister that contains the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
    telephone number and, if any, fax number and email address of their
    authorized representative, if applicable; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} respecting the product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its common or generic name and its trade name, if
      any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the product category set out in column 1 of the
      table to Schedule 1 and, if applicable, the subcategory set out in
      column 2 to which it belongs, as well as the information used to
      categorize it,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its lowest VOC concentration prior to
      reformulation, the date of reformulation and its VOC concentration
      after reformulation, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product, expressed in
      kilograms, that the person expects to manufacture or import during
      the period beginning on the day on which the notice is submitted
      and ending on December 31 of the same year, excluding the quantity
      that is to be manufactured or imported for export only.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Generation of compliance units

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person that has submitted a notice under
  subsection (1) may generate compliance units, at a rate of one
  compliance unit per kilogram, for all of the reformulated products
  that they manufacture or import during a given calendar year. The
  number of kilograms is determined by the formula

  ::: {#1335023 .Subsection}
  Σ \[(B~*i*~ − C~*i*~) × W~*i*~\]

  where

  [B~*i*~]{.dfn}
  :   is, for each reformulated product, the maximum VOC concentration
      set out in column 3 of the table to Schedule 1 for the product
      category set out in column 1 or, if applicable, the subcategory
      set out in column 2 to which the product belongs;

  [C~*i*~]{.dfn}
  :   is, for each reformulated product, the product's VOC concentration
      after reformulation; and

  [W~*i*~]{.dfn}
  :   is, for each reformulated product, the quantity, expressed in
      kilograms, that was manufactured or imported during the year in
      question, excluding the quantity that was manufactured or imported
      for export only.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First year of participation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the first calendar year in which the person
  participates in the compliance unit trading system, the value of the
  element W~*i*~ in the formula set out in subsection (2) is the
  quantity of the reformulated product that was manufactured or imported
  during the period beginning on the day on which the notice referred to
  in subsection (1) was submitted or the day on which the product was
  reformulated, whichever is later, and ending on December 31 of that
  year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Availability confirmed by Minister

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must provide the person with written
  confirmation of the number of compliance units that are available to
  them within 60 days after the day on which the person submits a report
  in accordance with section 14.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Valid for two years

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Compliance units whose availability is confirmed by
  the Minister are valid for two years beginning on January 1 of the

  **1** The following definitions apply in this Schedule.

  [[cleaner or duster for energized equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cleaner or duster for energized
      equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that is designed
      to clean or dust equipment while there is an electrical current in
      the equipment or while there is a residual electrical potential
      from a component such as a capacitor. ([nettoyant ou dépoussiéreur
      d'équipements sous tension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contact adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contact adhesive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means non-aerosol
      adhesive -- other than rubber cement that is designed for use on
      paper substrates and vulcanizing fluid that is designed solely for
      tire repair --- that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is designed for application to surfaces to be
        bonded together;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is to dry before the surfaces are placed in
        contact with each other;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} forms an immediate bond that makes it difficult
        or impossible to reposition the adhesive-coated surfaces after
        they are placed in contact with each other; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} does not require sustained pressure or the
        clamping of surfaces to establish an adherence between the
        surfaces after the adhesive-coated surfaces have been brought
        together using momentary pressure. ([adhésif de
        contact]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aerosol

  **2** For greater certainty, in this Schedule, a reference to
  "aerosol" does not include pump sprays.
