## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697465 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods referred to in
    Chapters 61 and 62 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are cut or
    knit to shape and are sewn or otherwise assembled in Costa Rica from
    fabric or yarn produced or obtained outside the free trade area.
    ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cotton or man-made fibre fabric and cotton or
      man-made fibre made-up textile goods referred to in Chapters 52 to
      55 of the List of Tariff Provisions, other than goods containing
      36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair, and in
      Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions that are
      woven or knitted in Costa Rica from yarn produced or obtained
      outside the free trade area or that are knitted in Costa Rica from
      yarn spun in Costa Rica from fibre produced or obtained outside
      the free trade area;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods of subheading 9404.90 that are finished and
      cut and sewn or otherwise assembled from fabrics referred to in
      any of subheadings 5208.11 to 5208.29, 5209.11 to 5209.29, 5210.11
      to 5210.29, 5211.11 to 5211.29, 5212.11, 5212.12, 5212.21,
      5212.22, 5407.41, 5407.51, 5407.71, 5407.81, 5407.91, 5408.21,
      5408.31, 5512.11, 5512.21, 5512.91, 5513.11 to 5513.19, 5514.11 to
      5514.19, 5516.11, 5516.21, 5516.31, 5516.41 or 5516.91 that are
      produced or obtained outside the free trade area; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} wool fabric and wool made-up textile goods
      referred to in Chapters 51 to 55 of the List of Tariff Provisions,
      containing 36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair, and
      in Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of the List of Tariff Provisions that
      are woven or knitted in Costa Rica from yarn produced or obtained
      outside the free trade area or that are knitted in Costa Rica from
      yarn spun in Costa Rica from fibre produced or obtained outside
      the free trade area. ([tissus et articles
      confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada and Costa Rica. ([zone de libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cotton or man-made fibre
    yarns referred to in any of headings 52.05 to 52.07 or 55.09 to
    55.11 that are spun in Costa Rica from fibre referred to in any of
    headings 52.01 to 52.03 or 55.01 to 55.07 that is produced or
    obtained outside the free trade area. ([filés]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission of Customs Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697474 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duty paid or payable at the
Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff rates of customs duty on apparel, fabric and
made-up goods and spun yarn imported from Costa Rica on or after the day
on which section 37 of the [Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement
Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-1.9), and chapter 28 of the Statutes of
Canada, 2001, come into force, in an amount equal to the difference
between the customs duty payable at those rates and the customs duty
that would be payable if the imported goods were entitled to the
applicable Costa Rica Tariff rates of customs duty.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697476 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  remission granted under section 2 applies only to an amount not
  exceeding the annual quantitative limits for each of the goods
  referred to in that section that are set out in Schedules 6.B.1, 6.B.2
  and 6.B.3 to Appendix III.1.6.1 of Annex III.1 to Chapter III of the
  Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission under section 2 is granted on the condition
  that the importer or owner of the goods provides to a customs officer,
  at the request of the officer,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a certificate issued under the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19) and the [Import Certificate
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._603), identifying the
    quantity available for purposes of remission or refund under
    Chapter III of the Canada-Costa Rica Free Trade Agreement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are accounted for under
    subsection 32(1), (3) or (5) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
    or are the subject of an application for a refund of the customs
    duty, a written declaration, in English or French, indicating that
    the certification referred to in subparagraph (c)(i) is in the
    possession of the importer or owner; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the goods are released, or at any
    other time after the importer or owner provides the declaration
    referred to in paragraph (b),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a certification of the exporter, in English,
      French or Spanish, in the form set out in the schedule,
      accompanied by a copy of the applicable invoice or sales
      agreement, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the certification is in Spanish, an English
      or French translation of the certification.
