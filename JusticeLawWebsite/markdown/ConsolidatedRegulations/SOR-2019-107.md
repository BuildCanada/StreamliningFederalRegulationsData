## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1123253 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1123254 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
      Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[carton]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[carton]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a secondary package in which
      two or more primary packages containing the same type of tobacco
      product are placed. ([cartouche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a roll or tubular construction
      intended for smoking, other than a little cigar, that contains a
      filler composed of natural or reconstituted tobacco and has a
      wrapper, or a wrapper and a binder, composed of natural or
      reconstituted tobacco. ([cigare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any roll or tubular
      construction that contains tobacco and is intended for smoking,
      other than a cigar, little cigar or tobacco
      stick. ([cigarette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[display area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[display area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the portion of the
      surface area of a tobacco product package, leaflet or tobacco
      product on which a labelling element is to be displayed. ([zone
      d'affichage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[drab brown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[drab brown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the colour known as
      Pantone 448. ([brun terne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gray]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gray]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the colour known as Pantone
      Cool Gray 2. ([gris]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[health warning]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[health warning]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a tobacco
      product, means the applicable health warning

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} referred to in subsection 88(1) and displayed
        on a tobacco product package; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} referred to in subsection 118(1) and displayed
        directly on a tobacco product. ([avertissement
        sanitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lower slide-flap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lower slide-flap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      slide and shell package, the lower extremity of the slide that can
      be folded and is concealed by the shell when the package is
      closed. ([rabat inférieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include an
      individual or entity that only packages or distributes tobacco
      products on behalf of a
      manufacturer. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[primary package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[primary package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any package ---
      other than an overwrap --- that is intended for retail sale in
      Canada and in which a tobacco product is directly
      placed. ([emballage primaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[secondary package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[secondary package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any package ---
      other than an overwrap --- that is intended for retail sale in
      Canada and in which a primary package is placed. ([emballage
      secondaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[slide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[slide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sliding portion of a slide
      and shell package. ([tiroir]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[top edge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[top edge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the edge that is in the
      horizontal plane and forms the upper limit of the package when the
      package is used in the customary manner to gain access to the
      tobacco product. ([arête supérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[upper slide-flap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[upper slide-flap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      slide and shell package, the upper extremity of the slide that can
      be folded and is concealed by the shell when the package is closed
      and that is visible when the package is used in the customary
      manner to gain access to the tobacco product. ([rabat
      supérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- common name

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to a common name is
  a reference to the common name that is shown on a label in accordance
  with subparagraph 10(b)(ii) of the [Consumer Packaging and Labelling
  Act](/eng/acts/C-38).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- declaration of net
  quantity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to a declaration of
  net quantity is a reference to the declaration of net quantity that is
  contained on a label in accordance with section 4 of the [Consumer
  Packaging and Labelling Act](/eng/acts/C-38).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- exterior surface

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to an exterior
  surface of a package that contains a tobacco product is a reference,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a package that has a rectangular
    cuboid shape when it is closed, to the part of the package that
    forms, as the case may be, the exterior surface of the front, back,
    top, bottom or any of the sides of the package;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cylindrical package,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the part of the package that forms, as the
      case may be, the exterior surface of the top or bottom of the
      package when it is closed, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the part of the package that forms, as the
      case may be, the exterior curved surface that makes up the front
      or back of the package around its circumference; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other package, to the part of
    the package that forms, as the case may be, the exterior surface of
    the front, back, top, bottom or any of the sides of the package when
    it is closed.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1123281 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tobacco product and package --- retail
sale

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to every tobacco product that is intended for retail
  sale in Canada, as well as to any package that contains such a tobacco
  product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Package that does not contain tobacco
  products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Part 1 also applies, in accordance with section 60,
  to a package that does not contain a tobacco product but is furnished
  by a manufacturer of a tobacco product so that a tobacco product,
  primary package or secondary package may be placed in it after the
  tobacco product, primary package or secondary package is sold at
  retail in Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a package that contains a tobacco product and that is intended
to be distributed to a manufacturer or retailer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interior surfaces

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, a provision of these Regulations that applies to
  any interior surface of a primary package that contains cigarettes
  applies to each surface of the slide of a slide and shell package ---
  including the upper slide-flap and lower slide-flap --- with the
  exception of any surface that forms the exterior surface of the top or
  bottom of the package when it is closed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carton

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a provision of these
  Regulations that applies to any interior surface of a secondary
  package applies to each surface of a carton that forms its flaps and
  that is visible only when the carton is opened.

### [Purpose]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1123294 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plain and standardized packaging

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 23 of the Act, these Regulations set out the
  requirements that manufacturers of tobacco products must meet in
  respect of the plain and standardized appearance, shape and content of
  tobacco product packages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- package

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 15.3 of the Act, these
  Regulations set out the requirements that manufacturers of tobacco
  products must meet in respect of the manner of displaying information
  on tobacco product packages and on leaflets, including the form and
  placement of the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Product standards and markings

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 5 and 5.3 of the Act, these Regulations also establish
standards that manufacturers of tobacco products must meet in respect of
the plain and standardized appearance and shape of tobacco products and
set out requirements that manufacturers must meet in respect of the
markings that may be displayed on tobacco products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling --- required information

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 15 and 15.2 of the Act, these Regulations set out the labelling
requirements that manufacturers of tobacco products must meet in respect
of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} information that is required under the Act to be
  displayed about tobacco products and their emissions and about the
  health hazards and health effects arising from the use of the products
  and from their emissions; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manner of displaying that information on tobacco
  products, on packages that contain tobacco products and on leaflets.

### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1123303 .Subheading}

#### [Package Contents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1123304 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary package

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary package may contain only a tobacco product, a lining and a
  leaflet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secondary package

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A secondary package may contain only a primary
  package or other secondary package, as well as any overwrap covering
  those packages.

#### [Standardized Physical Design Features]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1123310 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Secondary package shape

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every secondary package
must have a rectangular cuboid shape when it is closed, having six
surfaces that meet at right angles and edges that are rigid and
straight, without any rounding or bevelling.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand element, colour or information

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided by or under the Act, any other Act of Parliament or any Act of
the legislature of a province, the interior and exterior surfaces of a
primary package and of a secondary package must not display any brand
element or any colour or information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standardized colour

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided by these Regulations, every interior and
  exterior surface of a primary package and of a secondary package must
  be drab brown.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- required or authorized
  information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Other colours may be used on the interior and
  exterior surfaces to display a health warning or other information
  that is required or authorized by or under the Act, any other Act of
  Parliament or any Act of the legislature of a province, in accordance
  with the requirements imposed by or under that Act, other Act of
  Parliament or Act of the legislature of a province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- package made of metal or
  wood

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An interior surface of a primary package that is made
  of metal or wood may be the natural colour of the metal or wood.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except

  or any Act of the legislature of a province, every interior and
  exterior surface of a primary package and of a secondary package must
  have a matte finish.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The interior surfaces and exterior surfaces of a

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adhesive substance

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any adhesive substance
that is used on an interior or exterior surface of a primary package or
of a secondary package or on any lining or overwrap must be transparent
and colourless.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Texture

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  interior and exterior surface of a primary package and of a secondary
  package, as well as any overwrap covering the package, must have a
  smooth texture, without any raised features, embossing, decorative
  ridges, bulges or other irregularities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- carton

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, a carton may include a perforated strip for
  opening that, once torn, may leave serrations on the carton's interior
  and exterior surfaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Features

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  interior and exterior surfaces of a primary package and of a secondary
  package, as well as any lining, leaflet and overwrap, must not include
  any feature that is designed to change the appearance (such as
  heat-activated ink) or to change the surface area (such as a fold-out
  panel) of the package, lining, leaflet or overwrap.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of opening

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A primary package must be designed in such a manner
  that it may be opened by only one means when the package is used in
  the customary manner to gain access to the tobacco product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inner box

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A primary package must not be designed in such a
  manner that it forms an inner box that can be removed without being
  damaged.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alphanumeric code

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary package and a secondary package may display an alphanumeric
  code that is composed of no more than 10 numeric characters and either
  the abbreviation "CA" that represents Canada or one of the following
  internationally approved alpha codes that represents the province or
  territory where the product is sold:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Ontario, "ON";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Quebec, "QC";

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Nova Scotia, "NS";

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of New Brunswick, "NB";

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Manitoba, "MB";

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of British Columbia, "BC";

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Prince Edward Island, "PE";

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Saskatchewan, "SK";

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Alberta, "AB";

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Newfoundland and Labrador, "NL";

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Yukon, "YT";

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Northwest Territories, "NT"; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Nunavut, "NU".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alphanumeric code --- manner of display

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code may be displayed on a primary
  package and a secondary package if it is displayed in such a manner
  that the code

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a matte finish and is printed in a regular
    weight and width Lucida Sans Serif font style, without italics, and
    in black or gray;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is printed only once on the primary package and
    once on the secondary package, in characters of no more than 8
    points; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a package that has a rectangular
    cuboid shape when it is closed, is displayed on only the exterior
    surface of the top, bottom or any one of the sides of the package.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- characteristics

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code must not convey any information
  relating to characteristics of the tobacco product that the primary
  package or secondary package contains or the tobacco product's
  emissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand element

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code must not evoke the tobacco
  product's brand elements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bar code

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  primary package and a secondary package may display a bar code that is
  rectangular in shape, does not contain any image or design and is no
  more than 40 mm by 20 mm in size.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bar code --- manner of display

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The bar code may be displayed on a primary package
  and a secondary package if it is displayed in such a manner that the
  code

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has a matte finish and is printed in either black
    and white or in drab brown and white;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is displayed only once on the primary package and
    once on the secondary package; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a package that has a rectangular
    cuboid shape when it is closed, is displayed on only the exterior
    surface of the top, bottom or any one of the sides of the package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Means of electronic communication

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  interior and exterior surfaces of a primary package and of a secondary
  package, as well as any lining, leaflet and overwrap, must not include
  any feature that is designed to permit access remotely to any
  promotion by a means of electronic communication.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other types of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The surfaces, lining, leaflet and overwrap may
  include a feature that is designed to permit access remotely to other
  types of information by a means of electronic communication if the
  feature becomes visible only through technological means or meets the
  requirements imposed by or under the Act, any other Act of Parliament
  or any Act of the legislature of a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feature to prevent counterfeiting

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  feature that is designed to prevent counterfeiting may be displayed on
  an exterior surface of a primary package or of a secondary package if
  the feature

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is required under an enactment of a foreign
    government;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is a QR code, DotCode or Data Matrix;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is rectangular in shape and does not contain any
    image or design;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is no more than 40 mm by 20 mm in size;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has a matte finish and is printed in either black
    and white or in drab brown and white;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is displayed only once on each package; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a package that has a rectangular
    cuboid shape when it is closed, is displayed on only the exterior
    surface of the top, bottom or any one of the sides of the package.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 62,
every tobacco product that is made of tobacco that is rolled in paper or

texture, without any raised features, embossing, decorative ridges,
bulges or other irregularities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filter

**[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** A filter may have holes
or recesses that are not visible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alphanumeric code

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  tobacco product may display an alphanumeric code described in
  subsection 15(1), printed in black or gray.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsection 5.3(3) of the Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 5.3(3) of the Act,
  black and gray colouring agents used to display the alphanumeric code
  on a tobacco product are prescribed additives.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be printed only once on the tobacco product, in
    characters of no more than 8 points;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a matte finish and be printed in a regular
    weight and width Lucida Sans Serif font style, without italics;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cigarettes that have tipping paper,
    little cigars, cigars and tobacco products that are made in whole or
    in part of tobacco, rolled in paper and intended for use with a
    device, be oriented parallel to and displayed no more than 38 mm
    from the end of the tobacco product that is not designed to be lit
    or heated;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cigarettes that do not have tipping
    paper, be oriented parallel to and displayed no more than 38 mm from
    the end of the cigarette that is closest to the health warning's
    display area; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} be positioned in such a manner that it does not
    obscure or conceal any health warning, or any attribution referred
    to in subsection 83(1), that is displayed on the tobacco product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- cigars

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (3)(a), instead of being printed on
  a cigar, the alphanumeric code may be printed on a cigar band that
  meets the requirements of subsection 71(4) if the code is displayed
  only once on the band and is printed in characters of no more than 10
  points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- characteristics

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code must not convey any information
  relating to characteristics of the tobacco product or its emissions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Brand element

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The alphanumeric code must not evoke the tobacco
  product's brand elements.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- primary package and

  primary package and every secondary package that contains a tobacco

  of the following components:

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the explanatory text of the warning or related

    pan-Canadian toll-free telephone number and Government of Canada web
    address relating to tobacco cessation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- overwrap

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every overwrap in which two or more cigars are placed
  must also display a health warning that consists of three or more of
  the components referred to in subsection (1).

  in the source document being applicable during a period of 24 months.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First rotation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2), the first rotation of health
  warnings applies during the period that begins on the day on which
  these Regulations come into force and ends on July 31, 2026.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rotation --- transition

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The health warnings set out in a rotation in the
  source document may be used during the period of 90 days before the
  day on which that rotation applies in accordance with subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equal use

**[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** Every manufacturer
must, to the extent possible, use each health warning set out in each
applicable rotation of health warnings in the source document in respect
of an equal number of each size of each brand name of cigarettes, little
cigars and tubes that they package in a year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Placement --- display area

- **[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  health warning that is displayed on a cigarette, little cigar or tube
  must be displayed on a display area that consists of the surface of
  the tobacco product's tipping paper.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- cigarette without tipping
  paper

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, in the case of a cigarette that does not
  have tipping paper, the health warning must be displayed on a display
  area that consists of the surface of the paper that covers the rolled
  tobacco.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display area --- length

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The display area must be at least 24 mm in length,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} measured, in the case of a cigarette, little cigar
    or tube, from the end of the cigarette, little cigar or tube that is
    not designed to be lit; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} measured, in the case of a cigarette referred to in
    subsection (2), from one of the ends of the cigarette.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Space occupied

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The health warning and any attribution of the health
  warning must occupy the entire length of the display area and at least
  80% of the display area around the circumference of the cigarette,
  little cigar or tube.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of display --- official languages

- **[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  health warning must be displayed on cigarettes, little cigars or tubes
  in both official languages in such a manner that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the English and French versions each occupy at most
    40% of the display area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one version is below the other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alignment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The health warning must be centered horizontally
  along the length of the display area of the cigarette, little cigar or
  tube.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The text of the health warning must be printed on two
  white backgrounds, with the English version on one and the French
  version on the other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The health warning must be displayed on the
  cigarette, little cigar or tube in "Landscape" format.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format --- length of tipping paper

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} However, if the tipping paper of the cigarette,
  little cigar or tube is 30 mm or more in length, the health warning
  must be displayed in "Elongated Landscape" format.

health warning or an attribution on a cigarette, little cigar or tube
are prescribed additives.
