[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[specified
firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[specified
firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prohibited firearm referred to in
any of items 201 to 240 of Part 1 of the schedule to the [Regulations
Prescribing Certain Firearms and Other Weapons, Components and Parts of
Weapons, Accessories, Cartridge Magazines, Ammunition and Projectiles as
Prohibited or Restricted](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-462).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which this Order comes into force,
      owns or possesses a specified firearm and holds a licence that was
      issued under the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of the specified firearm,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold
      the licence while in possession of the specified firearm, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the specified firearm was, on the day before
      the day on which this Order comes into force, a restricted
      firearm,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} held on that day a registration certificate for
        the specified firearm that was issued under the [Firearms
        Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} is issued, on or after the day on which this
        Order comes into force, a registration certificate for the
        specified firearm that they would have been eligible to hold
        under section 13 of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) had the
        firearm remained a restricted firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on the day on which this Order comes into force,
      holds a licence that was issued under the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on or before the day on which this Order comes
      into force, entered into an agreement for the
      [[transfer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 21 of
      the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), to them of a specified
      firearm that was, immediately before the day on which this Order
      comes into force, a restricted firearm,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on or after the day on which this Order comes
      into force, is issued a registration certificate for the specified
      firearm that they would have been eligible to hold under section
      13 of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) had the firearm
      remained a restricted firearm,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of the specified firearm, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold the
      licence while in possession of the specified firearm;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm for the sole purpose of storing
      it on behalf of an owner who is a person referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b), if storage by them is a condition of that
      owner's licence, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence that was issued under the
      [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) that would have permitted them to
      possess the specified firearm according to its classification on
      the day before the day on which this Order comes into force;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is, for
      the sole purpose of carrying out a repair or adjustment referred
      to in subparagraph (2)(c)(i), in temporary possession of a
      specified firearm referred to in that subparagraph that is owned
      by a person referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} holds a licence that was issued under the
      [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6) that would have permitted them to
      possess the specified firearm according to its classification on
      the day before the day on which this Order comes into force;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm for the sole purpose of
      destroying it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has entered into an agreement with the
      Government of Canada to destroy specified firearms or is acting in
      the course of their duties, including as an employee or an agent
      or mandatary, on behalf of a person who has entered into such an
      agreement;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm for the sole purpose of
      delivering it to a person referred to in paragraph (e) to be
      destroyed, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} an organization, within the meaning of
        paragraph (a) of the definition
        [[organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the
        [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), that is authorized under an Act
        of Parliament to transport firearms, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an employee, a contractor or an agent or
        mandatary of such an organization --- or a subcontractor engaged
        by such a contractor --- if they are acting in the course of
        their duties or under a contract; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} at any time during the amnesty period, is in
      possession of a specified firearm owned by a person referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) for the sole purpose of deactivating it so
      that it is no longer a firearm, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is a [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Firearms
      Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6), that holds a licence issued under that Act
      authorizing it to possess prohibited firearms for the purpose of
      deactivating them or is an employee of such a business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(a)
    or (b) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} deactivate the specified firearm so that it is no
      longer a firearm,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm to a police
      officer for destruction or other disposal,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the person is not the owner of the specified
      firearm, deliver it to its owner,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} export the specified firearm in accordance with
      all applicable legal requirements, including the legal
      requirements of the country to which it is exported,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if the person is a
      [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
      of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6),

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} return the specified firearm to the
        manufacturer,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm to a person
        referred to in paragraph (1)(f) for the purpose of shipping the
        specified firearm to be destroyed, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm to a
        [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1)
        of that Act, for the purpose of shipping the specified firearm
        to be destroyed or deactivated,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm by vehicle, for
      the purpose of doing any of the things described in
      subparagraphs (i) to (v), by a route that, in all the
      circumstances, is reasonably direct, as long as, during
      transportation,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is unloaded and no ammunition is
        present in the vehicle,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the firearm is in the trunk of the vehicle or,
        if there is no trunk, the firearm is not visible from outside
        the vehicle, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the vehicle is not left unattended,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm by vehicle, in
      the manner referred to in subparagraph (vi), for the purpose of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} storing it in accordance with subparagraph (x),
        or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} having it stored by or, following storage,
        retrieving it from a person referred to in paragraph (1)(c), if
        the owner first notifies the chief firearms officer for the
        province in which they reside of the transport,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in
      paragraph (1)(a), if the specified firearm was, on the day before
      the day on which this Order comes into force, a non-restricted
      firearm, use it to hunt in the exercise of a right recognized and
      affirmed by section 35 of the [Constitution Act,
      1982](/eng/Const/) or to sustain the person or their family, until
      they are able to obtain another firearm for that use,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in paragraph
      (1)(a), transport the specified firearm whose use is permitted
      under subparagraph (viii) in accordance with section 10 of the
      [Storage, Display, Transportation and Handling of Firearms by
      Individuals Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209) for the
      purpose of doing the thing described in that subparagraph or
      giving temporary possession of the firearm to a person referred to
      in paragraph (1)(d), and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm before doing any of
      the things described in subparagraphs (i) to (ix), as long as it
      is stored in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} section 5 or 6 of the [Storage, Display,
        Transportation and Handling of Firearms by Individuals
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209), according to the
        classification of the firearm on the day before the day on which
        this Order comes into force, if the person is an individual, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} section 5 of the [Storage, Display and
        Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
        Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210), if the person is a
        [[business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
        of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(c) to
    do the things described in subparagraphs (a)(iii) and (x);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(d) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carry out repairs or adjustments, as necessary
      for safety reasons, of the specified firearm whose use is
      permitted under subparagraph (a)(viii), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} do the things described in subparagraphs
      (a)(iii) and (x);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(e) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm for the purpose
      of destroying it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm before
      transporting it under subparagraph (i) or destroying it, as long
      as it is stored in accordance with section 6 of the [Storage,
      Display and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by
      Businesses Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} permit a person referred to in paragraph (1)(f) to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} deliver the specified firearm to a person
      referred to in paragraph (1)(e) for the purpose of destroying it,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transport the specified firearm for the purpose
      of delivering it in accordance with subparagraph (i), and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} possess the specified firearm before doing any
      of the things described in subparagraph (i) or (ii); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} permit a business referred to in paragraph (1)(g)
    to possess the specified firearm before deactivating it, as long as
    it is stored in accordance with section 6 of the [Storage, Display
    and Transportation of Firearms and Other Weapons by Businesses
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-210).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on the day on which this
  Order comes into force and ends on March 1, 2026.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is made.
