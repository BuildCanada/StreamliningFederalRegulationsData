## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953187 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fisheries in Canadian fisheries waters in and
    adjacent to Ontario, Quebec, Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince
    Edward Island, Newfoundland and the Northwest Territories;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fisheries in the tidal waters in and adjacent to
    Manitoba;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fisheries in tidal waters in and adjacent to the
    Yukon Territory and fisheries in the Yukon Territory for fish of an
    anadromous stock of chum salmon, coho salmon, chinook salmon, pink
    salmon, sockeye salmon, rainbow trout, the family
    [Coregonidae]{.otherLang lang="la"} (whitefish and cisco) or Arctic
    char; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fisheries in the waters of the Areas enumerated and
    described in Schedule II to the [Pacific Fishery Management Area
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-215) and salmon fisheries in
    British Columbia.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of national
  parks.

## [Communal Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953197 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue a communal licence to an aboriginal organization to
  carry on fishing and related activities.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may designate, in the licence,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the persons who may fish under the authority of the
    licence, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessels that may be used to fish under the
    authority of the licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not designate the persons who
  may fish under the authority of the licence, the aboriginal
  organization may designate, in writing, those persons.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister does not designate the vessels that
  may be used to fish under the authority of the licence, the aboriginal
  organization may designate, in writing, those vessels.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-225, s. 12
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  proper management and control of fisheries and the conservation and
  protection of fish, the Minister may specify in a licence any
  condition respecting any of the matters set out in paragraphs 22(1)(b)
  to (z.1) of the [Fishery (General)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53) and any condition respecting
  any of the following matters, without restricting the generality of
  the foregoing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the species and quantities of fish that are
    permitted to be taken or transported;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the method by which and when the licence holder is
    to notify the Minister of designations, the documents that
    constitute proof of designation, when, under what circumstances and
    to whom proof of designation must be produced, the documents or
    information that designated persons and vessels must carry when
    carrying on fishing and related activities, and when, under what
    circumstances and to whom the documents or information must be
    produced;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the method to be used to mark and identify vessels
    and fishing gear;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the locations and times at which landing of fish is
    permitted;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the method to be used for the landing of fish and
    the methods by which the quantity of the fish is to be determined;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the information that a designated person or the
    master of a designated vessel is to report to the Minister or a
    person specified by the licence holder, prior to commencement of
    fishing, with respect to where and when fishing will be carried on,
    including the method by which, the times at which and the person to
    whom the report is to be made;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the locations and times of inspections of the
    contents of the hold and the procedure to be used in conducting
    those inspections;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of persons or vessels that may
    be designated to carry on fishing and related activities;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the maximum number of designated persons who may
    fish at any one time;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the type, size and quantity of fishing gear that
    may be used by a designated person;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances under which fish are to be marked
    for scientific or administrative purposes; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the disposition of fish caught under the authority
    of the licence.

  ::: HistoricalNote

  - SOR/2002-225, s. 13
  :::

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953225 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No person carrying on
fishing or any related activity under the authority of a licence shall
contravene or fail to comply with any condition of the licence.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No person other than a
designated person may fish under the authority of a licence.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person who is authorized to fish under the authority of a licence
  shall fish for or catch and retain any species of fish in any area of
  the waters referred to in subsection 3(1) during the close time
  beginning on December 29 and ending on December 31.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The close time established by subsection (1) is
  considered to be fixed separately and individually with respect to any
  species of fish found in any of the waters referred to in subsection
  3(1).
