## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660181 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of non-alloy steel
stranded wire, having a diameter of 5 mm or less, of subheading No.
7312.10, imported into Canada during the period commencing on July 10,
1996, and ending on June 16, 1999, for use in the manufacture of tires.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-73, s. 17(F)
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660185 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date on which this Order
  comes into force; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer files such evidence as may be required
  by the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency to determine eligibility for
  remission.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-660189 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
