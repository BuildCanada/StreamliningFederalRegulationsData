:::

[CANADA DISABILITY SAVINGS ACT](/eng/acts/C-3.5)

Registration 2019-12-11

Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a
Minister of the Crown, to be "the Minister" for the purposes of that Act

P.C. 2019-1364 2019-11-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [Canada Disability
Savings Act](/eng/acts/C-3.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBCCA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2018-1021 of July 18,
  2018[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1906_hq_26064){#fn_81000-3-1906_hq_26064-ID0EBABCA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Employment and Social
  Development, a Minister of the Crown, to be "the Minister" for the
  purposes of that Act.
