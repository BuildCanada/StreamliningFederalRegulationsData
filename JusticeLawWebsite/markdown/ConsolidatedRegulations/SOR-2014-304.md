## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809935 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Criminal
    Code](/eng/acts/C-46). ([Code]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified medical practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified medical practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person duly qualified under provincial law to practise
    medicine. ([médecin qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualified technician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified technician]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person or
    person of a class of persons designated by the Attorney General as
    being qualified to take samples of blood for the purposes of
    sections 254, 256 and 258 of the Code. ([technicien
    qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Samples of Bodily Substances Provided in Compliance with Probation Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-809941 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies in
respect of any samples of bodily substances that are provided by
offenders in compliance with the conditions of a probation order
prescribed by a court under paragraph 732.1(3)(c.1) or (c.2) of the
Code.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designations and specifications

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the Attorney General of
a province or the minister of justice of a territory proposes to make
designations or specifications under subsection 732.1(8) of the Code,
they must notify the Attorney General of Canada in writing that the
province or territory has the technical and operational capability to
take, analyze, store, handle and destroy any samples of bodily
substances that are to be provided.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed bodily substances

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following are
prescribed bodily substances for the purposes of paragraphs
732.1(3)(c.1) and (c.2) of the Code:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} breath;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} urine;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} blood;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} hair; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} saliva.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of persons to take blood
samples

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Only
  qualified medical practitioners and qualified technicians may be
  designated for the purposes of taking blood samples.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Medical opinion required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A blood sample may be taken from a person only if a
  qualified medical practitioner is satisfied that taking the sample
  would not endanger the person's life or health.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis of breath samples

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A breath sample must be
analyzed either by using an instrument that has been approved under the
[Approved Breath Analysis Instruments Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-201)
or by using a screening device that has been approved under the
[Approved Screening Devices Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-200).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of blood samples

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A blood sample must be
stored in a container that has been approved under the [Order Approving
Blood Sample Containers](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-37).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period for destruction of
samples

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 732.1(11) of the Code, a sample of a bodily substance must be
destroyed within one year after the day on which the sample is provided.
