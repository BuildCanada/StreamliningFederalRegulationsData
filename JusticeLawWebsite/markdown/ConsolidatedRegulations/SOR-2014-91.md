## Regulations Establishing Conditions for Making Regulations Under Subsection 36(5.2) of the Fisheries Act {#regulations-establishing-conditions-for-making-regulations-under-subsection-365.2-of-the-fisheries-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-91
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 2014-04-11

Regulations Establishing Conditions for Making Regulations Under
Subsection 36(5.2) of the Fisheries Act

P.C. 2014-435 2014-04-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and the Minister of the
Environment, pursuant to subsection 36(5.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EGCAA} of the [Fisheries
Act](/eng/acts/F-14)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EDCAA}, makes the annexed
[Regulations Establishing Conditions for Making Regulations under
Subsection 36(5.2) of the](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-91) [Fisheries
Act](/eng/acts/F-14).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
      Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[biochemical oxygen-demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biochemical oxygen-demanding matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      any organic matter that contributes to the consumption of oxygen
      that is dissolved in waters or sediment. ([matière exerçant une
      demande biochimique en oxygène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Procedure for pH Stabilization EPS 1/RM/50]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Procedure for pH Stabilization
      EPS 1/RM/50]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Procedure for pH
      Stabilization During the Testing of Acute Lethality of Wastewater
      Effluent to Rainbow Trout (EPS 1/RM/50), March 2008, published by
      the Department of the Environment, as amended from time to
      time. ([procédure de stabilisation du pH SPE
      1/RM/50]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Reference Method EPS 1/RM/13]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Reference Method EPS 1/RM/13]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Biological Test Method: Reference Method for Determining Acute
      Lethality of Effluents to Rainbow Trout (EPS 1/RM/13 Second
      Edition), December 2000 (with May 2007 amendments), published by
      the Department of the Environment, as amended from time to
      time. ([méthode de Référence SPE 1/RM/13]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[research activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[research activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means activities
      during which a deposit of a deleterious substance is made for the
      sole purpose of acquiring scientific knowledge.  ([activités de
      recherche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acutely lethal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a deposit is
  acutely lethal if, at 100% concentration, it kills more than 50% of
  the rainbow trout subjected to it over a 96- hour period when tested
  to determine lethality in accordance with Reference Method EPS 1/RM/13
  and -- if applicable -- in conjunction with the Procedure for pH
  Stabilization EPS 1/RM/50.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aquaculture, pests and invasive species

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The power that is conferred
by subsection 36(5.2) of the Act may be exercised by the Minister of
Fisheries and Oceans in relation to aquaculture, to aquatic species that
are a pest to a fishery or to aquatic invasive species, if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister is satisfied that the Regulations are
  required for the proper management and control of fisheries or the
  conservation and protection of fish; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the deleterious substance whose deposit is to be
  authorized

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is a drug whose sale is permitted or otherwise
    authorized, or whose importation is not prohibited, under the [Food
    and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is a pest control product that is registered under
    the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) or whose use is
    authorized under that Act, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is biochemical oxygen-demanding matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aquatic research

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The power that is conferred
by subsection 36(5.2) of the Act may be exercised by the Minister of the
Environment in relation to research activities if the persons authorized
by the Regulations to deposit deleterious substances have processes in
place or are subject to processes

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to verify that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the research activities will contribute to the
    development of knowledge for the purpose of managing, conserving,
    protecting or restoring fish populations or waters frequented by
    fish,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the research activities will be conducted under
    the supervision of a person who has expertise in conducting aquatic
    research under similar conditions, as evidenced by a substantial and
    relevant peer-reviewed publication record,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the research activities will be designed so as to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} avoid harmful effects on fish, fish habitat and
      the use by man of fish, other than the effects that are necessary
      to achieve results that are scientifically valid, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} confine any harmful effects on fish, fish habitat
      or the use by man of fish to the waters in respect of which the
      deposit is authorized, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if research activities render any waters
    frequented by fish unsuitable to sustain fish populations or render
    fish populations unsuitable for human consumption, no later than 20
    years following the day on which the research activities are
    completed, the waters will be suitable to sustain fish populations
    and the fish populations will be suitable for human consumption;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to evaluate whether the research activities will be
  carried out in accordance with their design and to document any
  deviations from that design; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to make research findings available to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other subject matters

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The power that is conferred
by subsection 36(5.2) of the Act may be exercised by the Minister of the
Environment in relation to any other subject matter if the following
conditions are met:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the deleterious substance to be deposited, its
  deposit or the work, undertaking or activity that results in the
  deposit is authorized under federal or provincial law, or is subject
  to guidelines issued by the federal or provincial government, and is
  subject to an enforcement or compliance regime;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the federal or provincial law or guidelines set out
  conditions that result in a deposit that is not acutely lethal and
  contains a quantity or concentration of deleterious substance that
  when measured in the deposit, or in the relevant waters frequented by
  fish, satisfies

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the recommendations of the Canadian Water Quality
    Guidelines for the Protection of Aquatic Life that were published in
    1999 by the Canadian Council of Ministers of the Environment, as
    amended from time to time, or the recommendations that were derived
    from those guidelines on their site-specific application, as amended
    from time to time, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the recommendations of any peer-reviewed
    guidelines that are established for the purpose of protecting
    aquatic life and adopted by a federal or provincial body; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effects of such a deposit on fish, fish habitat
  and the use by man of fish have been evaluated in accordance with
  generally accepted standards of good scientific practice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional conditions

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The power that is conferred
by subsection 36(5.2) of the Act may be exercised if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the proposed Regulations are published in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) at least 30 days before the day on
  which they are made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the proposed Regulations are published, the
  Minister of Fisheries and Oceans or the Minister of the Environment,
  as applicable, provides the President of the Treasury Board with an
  estimate of the costs of their implementation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
