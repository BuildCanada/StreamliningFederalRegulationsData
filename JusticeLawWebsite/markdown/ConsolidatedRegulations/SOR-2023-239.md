## Order Issuing Directions to the CRTC (Sustainable and Equitable Broadcasting Regulatory Framework) {#order-issuing-directions-to-the-crtc-sustainable-and-equitable-broadcasting-regulatory-framework .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-239
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 2023-11-09

Order Issuing Directions to the CRTC (Sustainable and Equitable
Broadcasting Regulatory Framework)

P.C. 2023-1125 2023-11-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Canadian Heritage has, in accordance with
subsection 7(6) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913){#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913-ID0EBBDA},
consulted with the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913-ID0EBBDA){#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913}S.C.
  1991, c. 11
  :::

Whereas, in accordance with paragraph 8(1)(a) of that Act, the Minister
had a copy of the proposed [Order Issuing Directions to the CRTC
(Sustainable and Equitable Broadcasting Regulatory
Framework)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-239), substantially in the annexed
form, published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part
I, on June 10, 2023 and the period given to interested persons to make
representations has ended;

And whereas, in accordance with paragraph 8(1)(b) of that Act, the
Minister had a copy of the proposed Order laid before each House of
Parliament;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Canadian Heritage, makes the annexed
[Order Issuing Directions to the CRTC (Sustainable and Equitable
Broadcasting Regulatory Framework)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2023-239) under
subsection 7(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913){#fn_81000-2-4376_hq_1696449913-ID0EBAAA}.
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Broadcasting
    Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[social media creator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[social media creator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    creates programs that are primarily intended for online distribution
    as user-uploaded programs through social media services. ([créateur
    pour les médias sociaux]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Diversity among Indigenous peoples

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In applying this Order, the
Commission is directed to consider the diversity among Indigenous
peoples.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427160 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Meaningful participation of Indigenous
persons

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In furtherance of the
objectives of the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous
Peoples, the Commission is directed to support the meaningful
participation of Indigenous persons in the Canadian broadcasting system,
including by supporting

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their ability to create and produce a wide range of
  programs;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} access to those programs; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their ownership and control of broadcasting
  undertakings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supporting Canadian programming

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is directed
to impose requirements on broadcasting undertakings that ensure that the
Canadian broadcasting system --- which is to be effectively owned and
controlled by Canadians and includes foreign broadcasting undertakings
that provide programming to Canadians --- strongly supports a wide range
of Canadian programming and Canadian creators. The requirements, both
financial and non-financial, must be equitable given the size and nature
of the undertaking and equitable as between foreign online undertakings
and Canadian broadcasting undertakings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Community broadcasters and broadcasters
of exceptional importance

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is directed
to consider how to foster collaboration and encourage innovation by, and
support the sustainability of, community broadcasters and broadcasting
undertakings that are of exceptional importance to the achievement of
the objectives of the broadcasting policy set out in subsection 3(1) of
the Act.

## [Regulation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discoverability and showcasing

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is directed
to consider both established and emerging means of discoverability and
showcasing to promote a wide range of Canadian programming. In making
regulations or imposing conditions in respect of discoverability and
showcasing requirements, the Commission is directed to prioritize
outcome-based regulations and conditions that minimize the need for
broadcasting undertakings to make changes to their computer algorithms
that impact the presentation of programs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is directed
to regulate and supervise the Canadian broadcasting system with a view
to supporting the provision of programming that is accessible without
barriers to persons with disabilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flexible and adaptable regulatory
framework

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** To support flexibility and
adaptability in its regulatory framework, the Commission is directed to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, minimize the regulatory burden on
  the Canadian broadcasting system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} avoid disruptions to programs and undertakings to
  which the Act does not apply;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} respect audience choice and, where possible, increase
  the options available;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, use tools that are based on
  incentives and outcomes;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, use digital tools and solutions;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} consider other Canadian or foreign regulatory regimes
  that affect online undertakings; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate, foster collaboration between
  Canadian and foreign broadcasting undertakings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of Canadian human resources

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** In its regulation of the
Canadian broadcasting system, the Commission is directed to ensure that
the system maximizes the use of Canadian creative and other human
resources in the creation, production and presentation of programming in
the Canadian broadcasting system, taking into account the effects of
broadcasting undertakings, including online undertakings, on economic
opportunities and remuneration for Canadian creators.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Social media creators and video games

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
directed not to impose regulatory requirements on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} online undertakings in respect of the programs of
  social media creators, including podcasts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} broadcasting undertakings in respect of the
  transmission of video games.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations --- section 4.2 of the Act

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** In exercising its powers
under section 4.2 of the Act, the Commission is directed to set out
clear, objective and readily ascertainable criteria, including criteria
that ensure that the Act only applies in respect of programs that have
been broadcast, in whole or in significant part, by a broadcasting
undertaking that is required to be carried on under a licence or that is
required to be registered with the Commission but does not provide a
social media service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulations and orders --- section 11.1
of the Act

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** In exercising its powers
under section 11.1 of the Act, the Commission is directed to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} regularly review expenditure requirements to ensure
  that they are proportional to their objectives and that those
  objectives are clear;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} recognize the diversity of services provided by
  broadcasting undertakings;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} consider providing flexibility for all broadcasting
  undertakings in meeting expenditure requirements;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} consider demographic data --- including that
  concerning the participation in the Canadian broadcasting system of
  members of equity-seeking and ethnocultural groups, including Black or
  other racialized communities --- collected from various sources,
  including broadcasting undertakings, industry organizations,
  production funds certified by the Commission and information published
  under the [Employment Equity Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where appropriate for a given business model and set
  of objectives, prioritize the imposition of requirements to make
  expenditures directly on the creation, production and presentation of
  Canadian programming;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensure that expenditure requirements support the
  creation and availability of programming in French, in recognition of
  the minority context of French in Canada and North America and the
  specific challenges involved with creating and making available
  original French language programming;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} ensure that expenditure requirements support the
  creation and availability of programming

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by Indigenous creators and broadcasting
    undertakings, including programming distributed by Indigenous
    broadcasting undertakings and programming in Indigenous languages,
    taking into consideration the importance of reconciliation with
    Indigenous peoples and the challenges and obstacles that they face,
    including those stemming from historical injustices or the legacies
    of colonialism,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by creators who are members of equity-seeking and
    ethnocultural groups, including Black or other racialized
    communities, taking into consideration the challenges and obstacles
    that they face, including systemic racism and the obstacles faced by
    those whose first language is not an official language, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in both official languages, including programming
    by creators from official language minority communities, taking into
    consideration the challenges and obstacles faced by those creators;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} consider the need for sustainable and predictable
  funding to support participation by persons, groups of persons or
  organizations representing the public interest in proceedings before
  the Commission under the Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} support broadcasting undertakings that offer
  programming services that are of exceptional importance to the
  achievement of the broadcasting policy set out in subsection 3(1) of
  the Act;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} consider the importance of sustainable support by the
  entire Canadian broadcasting system for news and current events
  programming, including a broad range of original local and regional
  news and community programming; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} support activities and services --- including
  training and development activities, conferences, the activities of
  organizations that represent creators and the development of digital
  and open-source tools and solutions --- that support and promote
  Canadian creators of audio or audio-visual programs for broadcasting
  by broadcasting undertakings, including social media creators.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of Canadian programming

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** In its determination of
what constitutes Canadian programming, the Commission is directed to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} consult Canadians, the creative and production
  sectors and other interested parties;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} support Canadians holding a broad range of key
  creative positions, in particular those with a high degree of creative
  control or visibility;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} support Canadian ownership of intellectual property;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} recognize the distinctions between broadcasting
  undertakings that distribute audio programs and those that distribute
  audio-visual programming;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} recognize that the Act applies to foreign
  broadcasting undertakings;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} consider, as it relates to audio-visual programming,
  the vital role of Canadian independent producers and of the Canadian
  creative resources that are being used by both Canadian and foreign
  broadcasting undertakings; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} consider whether its determination of what
  constitutes a Canadian program complements other Canadian content
  policies that are applicable to the Canadian broadcasting system,
  including those pertaining to audio-visual tax credits or government
  funding.

## [Engagement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427217 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Indigenous peoples

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In its regulation of the
Canadian broadcasting system, the Commission is directed to engage with
Indigenous peoples and Indigenous partners, governing bodies,
broadcasters, creators, producers, industry organizations and community
members and, in doing so, collaborate with relevant federal departments
where possible to solicit comments on, among other things,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} how to best support Indigenous broadcasting
  undertakings to help ensure the viability of the Indigenous
  broadcasting sector;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use of regulatory conditions that foster the
  success of business models that provide and reflect Indigenous
  perspectives;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} how to support the discoverability of programs by
  Indigenous creators;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the most appropriate tools, including funding
  mechanisms, for supporting Indigenous storytelling and production as
  well as Indigenous-led organizations that could manage and be
  responsible for that support; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the measures that are necessary to ensure its
  regulatory approach is in furtherance of the objectives of the United
  Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples and supports
  narrative sovereignty in the Canadian broadcasting system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equity-seeking and ethnocultural groups

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** In its regulation of the
Canadian broadcasting system, the Commission is directed to engage with
members of equity-seeking and ethnocultural groups, including Black or
other racialized communities, to solicit comments regarding

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the most appropriate tools, including funding
  mechanisms, to support those groups; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the development of a framework of measurable targets
  to support the creation, availability and discoverability of
  programming made by and broadcasting undertakings carried on by
  members of those groups.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official language minority communities

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
directed to engage with official language minority communities to
solicit comments regarding the creation of and access to programming by
and for those communities, including with respect to funding,
distribution and discoverability.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation support

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
directed to consider how to make the participation in the engagements
referred to in sections 14 to 16 as accessible as possible.

## [Information and Implementation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427233 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- achievement of policy
objectives

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
directed to provide information to the public on a periodic basis
regarding the progress made in achieving the objectives of the
broadcasting policy set in subsection 3(1) of the Act, including
progress on the inclusion and participation of Indigenous persons,
members of official language minority communities and members of
equity-seeking groups and ethnocultural groups in the Canadian
broadcasting system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is
directed to make any changes to its regulatory framework that are
necessary for the purposes of the implementation of this Order within
two years after the day on which it comes into force. In doing so, the
Commission is directed to prioritize the implementation of sections 13
to 16 and to ensure that any changes to its regulatory framework are
made as soon as feasible and on a continual basis during that two-year
period.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1427238 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
