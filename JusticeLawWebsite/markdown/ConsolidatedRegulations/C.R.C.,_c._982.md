## International River Improvements Regulations {#international-river-improvements-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 982
:::

[INTERNATIONAL RIVER IMPROVEMENTS ACT](/eng/acts/I-20)

Regulations Respecting International River Improvements
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-602667 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [International River Improvements
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._982).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-602669 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [International River
    Improvements Act](/eng/acts/I-20); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[associated works]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[associated works]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-198, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[international river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international river]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means water flowing from
    any place in Canada to any place outside Canada; ([cours d'eau
    international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[international river improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[international river improvement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dam,
    obstruction, canal, reservoir or other work the purpose or effect of
    which is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to increase, decrease or alter the natural flow
      of an international river, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to interfere with, alter or affect the actual or
      potential use of the international river outside Canada; ([ouvrage
      destiné à l'amélioration d'un cours d'eau
      international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of the
    Environment. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 1
- SOR/93-198, s. 1
:::

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-602680 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  international river improvement is excepted from the operation of the
  Act if:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the improvement has or will have in its operation
    an effect of less than 3 cm on the level or less than 0.3 m^3^/s on
    the flow of water at the Canadian boundary; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the improvement is of a temporary nature, to be
    operated for a period not exceeding two years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who intends to construct an international
  river improvement that is excepted from the application of the Act
  under subsection (1) shall notify and provide the Minister in writing
  with the information referred to in paragraphs 6(a) to (e).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 2
:::

**[[4 and 5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4_and_5 .sectionLabel}** [\[Revoked,
SOR/87-570, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-602689 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence under the Act shall be addressed to the Minister and shall
contain the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and occupation of the applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and a clear description of the international
  river on which an international river improvement is to be made;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the place where the said improvement is to be made
  and a description of the improvement;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} details as to the effect of the improvement on the
  level or flow of water at the Canadian boundary;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} details as to the effect of the improvement on the
  use of water outside Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} details of the adverse effects of the improvement on
  flood control and other uses of water together with information as to
  plans to minimize such effects;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a brief economic analysis of the direct and indirect
  benefit and costs of and resulting from the improvement; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any further details concerning the improvement
  tending to indicate that it is compatible with a sound development of
  the resources and economy of Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 3(F)
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
licence shall be accompanied by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of any agreement if it is intended to sell
  outside Canada, any part of the Canadian share of down-stream power
  resulting from a proposed international river improvement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the licence for the project issued by the
  appropriate provincial authority.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may, on
receipt of an application for a licence, require that such further
information pertaining to the international river improvement be
submitted as is necessary to enable the Minister to dispose of the
application.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 4
- SOR/93-198, s. 2
:::

## [Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-602709 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  an applicant for a licence has supplied all the information required
  by these Regulations the Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue to him a licence for a period not exceeding
    50 years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon the expiration of any licence issue a further
    licence for a period not exceeding 50 years.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each licence shall stipulate the terms and conditions
  under which the international river improvement may be constructed,
  operated and maintained, and the period for which it is issued.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Any person who assigns or
transfers a licence shall, at least one month before the assignment or
the transfer, inform the Minister in writing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 6
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Upon the request of a
licensee, his heirs, executors, administrators, successors or assigns,
the Minister may vary the terms and conditions of a licence.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), where a
  licensee has failed to comply with the terms and conditions of the
  licence within six months after being requested in writing by the
  Minister to do so, the Minister may cancel the licence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a licence is cancelled, the Minister shall
  cause to be given to the licensee

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reasonable notice in writing of the proposed
    cancellation, explaining the grounds on which the Minister proposes
    to cancel the licence; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable opportunity to respond and to make
    representations as to why the licence should not be cancelled.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/87-570, s. 7
:::
