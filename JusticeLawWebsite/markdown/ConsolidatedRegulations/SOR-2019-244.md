## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1175901 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
      Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[assistive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[assistive device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any medical device,
      mobility aid, communication aid or other aid that is specially
      designed to assist a person with a disability with a need related
      to their disability. ([dispositif d'assistance]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[barrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-244, s.
      238\]]{.Repealed}

  [[CATSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CATSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Air Transport
      Security Authority established under subsection 5(1) of the
      [Canadian Air Transport Security Authority
      Act](/eng/acts/C-11.2). ([ACSTA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[CBSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CBSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canada Border Services
      Agency established under subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Border
      Services Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-1.4). ([ASFC]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[CSA B651-18]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CSA B651-18]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Standard of
      Canada CAN/CSA B651-18, entitled Accessible design for the built
      environment, published in November 2018 by the Canadian Standards
      Association, as amended from time to time. ([norme CAN/CSA
      B651-18]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[curbside zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[curbside zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an area that is
      located outside of a terminal where passengers are picked up or
      dropped off and that is owned, operated, leased or otherwise
      controlled by the terminal operator. ([aire d'arrêt
      minute]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2019-244, s.
      238\]]{.Repealed}

  [[ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is used for the
      transportation of passengers and that operates on a fixed schedule
      between two or more points. ([traversier]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mobility aid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mobility aid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any manual or electric
      wheelchair, scooter, boarding chair, walker, cane, crutch,
      prosthesis or other aid that is specially designed to assist a
      person with a disability with a need related to mobility. ([aide à
      la mobilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a carrier, a
      terminal operator, CATSA or the CBSA, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any employees of that carrier or terminal
        operator or of CATSA or the CBSA, as the case may be;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any persons, except a travel agency, that have
        entered into an agreement or arrangement with that carrier or
        terminal operator or with CATSA or the CBSA, as the case may be,
        to provide services on their behalf; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any employees of the persons referred to in
        paragraph (b). ([personnel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[service dog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[service dog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dog that has been
      individually trained by an organization or person specializing in
      service dog training to perform a task to assist a person with a
      disability with a need related to their disability. ([chien
      d'assistance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[support person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[support person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person referred to
      in subsection 50(1) whose assistance is needed by a person with a
      disability. ([personne de soutien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an airport, a railway
      station, a ferry terminal or a bus station, or a port that permits
      the mooring of cruise ships, and any related facilities, whether
      inside or outside of the terminal, including any amenities or
      equipment used in a terminal or in its related facilities, such as
      washrooms, waiting or rest areas, boarding platforms, curbside
      zones and parking. ([gare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[terminal operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[terminal operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that
      owns, operates or leases a terminal. ([exploitant de
      gare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transportation-related service or facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transportation-related service or
      facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any service or facility,
      including any amenity or equipment used in a facility, that is
      related to the transportation of passengers within the
      transportation network. ([service ou installation liés au
      transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      the World Wide Web Consortium Recommendation, dated December 2008,
      entitled Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.0,
      published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C), as amended from
      time to time. ([Règles pour l'accessibilité des contenus
      Web]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- no effect on existing

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
nothing in these Regulations is to be construed as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} limiting the duty to accommodate under the [Canadian
  Human Rights Act](/eng/acts/H-6) or any other Act of Parliament; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requiring any person to do anything that jeopardizes
  security, public health or public safety.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Requirements Applicable to Transportation Service Providers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1175933 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1175934 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carriers, terminal operators, CATSA and
CBSA

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies to the following entities (each of which is referred to
  in this Part as a "transportation service provider"):

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every carrier to which Part 3 applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every terminal operator to which Division 1 of Part
    4 applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} CATSA; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the CBSA.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of paragraph 5(a)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Paragraph 5(a) applies to every carrier to which Part
  2 applies and every terminal operator to which Division 2 of Part 4
  applies, in addition to the entities mentioned in paragraphs (1)(a) to
  (d).

### [Communication of Information to Persons with Disabilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1175944 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General information --- alternative
formats

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  transportation service provider makes available to the public
  information about any transportation-related service or facility, the
  transportation service provider must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the information is made available in an
    electronic format, the format is compatible with adaptive technology
    that is intended to assist persons with disabilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the information is made available only in a
    paper format, it is made available, on request, in large print, in
    Braille or in an electronic format;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the information is made available in an audio
    format, it is made available, on request, in a visual format; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the information is made available in a visual
    format, it is made available, on request, in an audio format.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person with a disability makes a request
  referred to in any of paragraphs (1)(b) to (d), the transportation
  service provider must provide the information in the requested format
  without delay.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be published

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the services that it offers to persons with

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the complaint resolution services that it offers
    and how a passenger may access those services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A transportation service
provider must ensure that members of personnel who interact with
passengers in the course of carrying out their functions take in account
the following when communicating with a person with a disability:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the person's disability, particularly
  if the person is blind or deaf or has any other visual or hearing
  impairment or if the person has a communication impairment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether the person uses an assistive device to assist
  them to hear, see or communicate; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether there are methods of communication that may
  be used by the person or that may facilitate communication with the
  person, such as an augmentative or alternative communication system,
  sign language or clear, concise and plain language.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Telephone system

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If a transportation service
provider makes a telephone number available to the public that may be
used to make travel reservations or obtain information about the
provider's transportation-related services or facilities, it must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} offer to persons who are deaf or have any other
  hearing impairment, or who have a communication impairment, the option
  of doing those things by means of an email or a third party's
  telephone relay or video relay service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish, in every instance that it publishes a
  telephone number that may be used to do those things, along with that
  telephone number, a description of how a person may access the
  services referred to in paragraph (a), including the transportation
  service provider's email address and the third party's telephone
  number for telephone relay or video relay service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Website

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If a transportation service
provider makes a website available to the public that may be used to
access a client account, travel itinerary, travel schedule or trip
status, to obtain contact information for the transportation service
provider, to make or modify a reservation or to check in, it must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} offer to persons with disabilities the option of
  doing those things by means of a communication system that does not
  require the use of a website, such as by means of a telephone, an
  email or a third party's telephone relay or video relay service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} publish, in every instance that it publishes the
  address of the website that may be used to do those things, along with
  that website address, a description of how a person may access the
  services referred to in paragraph (a), including the transportation
  service provider's telephone number and email address and the third
  party's telephone number for telephone relay or video relay service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Website --- requirements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A transportation service
provider must ensure that every website, mobile website and application
that it owns, operates or controls and that is made available to the
public meets the requirements for a Level AA conformance that are set
out in the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public announcements inside terminals

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must ensure that any public announcement relating to a
  departure or a gate or track assignment that is made for passengers
  waiting at a boarding area inside a terminal is made in an audio
  format and in a visual format.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public announcement --- safety or
  security

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a transportation service provider makes any public
  announcement relating to safety or security inside a terminal, they
  must make that announcement in an audio and visual format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automated self-service kiosks

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  transportation service provider owns, operates or controls the
  hardware components of an automated self-service kiosk that is
  available for public use in a terminal, or owns, operates or controls
  the software components of such a kiosk, the transportation service
  provider must ensure that the hardware components or the software
  components, as the case may be, meet the requirements set out in
  clauses 1.4 and 3 to 7 and Annexes B and C, excluding the notes
  accompanying those clauses, of the National Standard of Canada
  CAN/CSA-B651.2-07 (R2017), entitled Accessible design for self-service
  interactive devices, published in January 2007 by the Canadian
  Standards Association, as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a transportation service provider owns, operates
  or controls the hardware components of an automated self-service kiosk
  referred to in subsection (1), the transportation service provider
  must ensure that the kiosk is visually and tactilely discernible by an
  International Symbol of Access that is affixed to the front of it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary application

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For a period of two years
beginning on the day on which this section comes into force, if a
transportation service provider owns, operates or controls the hardware
components of an automated self-service kiosk that is available for
public use in a terminal and meets the requirements of section 11, that
kiosk must be marked with signage that specifies that persons with
disabilities have priority access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistance with use of self-service
kiosks

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A transportation service
provider must, on the request of a person with a disability, assist the
person, without delay, to use any automated self-service kiosk referred
to in section 11.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessible self-service kiosks

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  transportation service provider must ensure that any automated
  self-service kiosk referred to in section 11 is in good working order
  and is properly maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the automated self-service kiosk is not in good
  working order, the transportation service provider must ensure that it
  is repaired as soon as possible and, until it is repaired, the
  transportation service provider must provide the following services to
  a person with a disability:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directing the person to the nearest working
    automated self-service kiosk that offers the same service as that
    provided by the kiosk that is not in good working order and, on the
    request of the person, assisting the person in using that kiosk; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permitting the person to advance to the front of
    the line at a counter where they will be provided the same service
    as that provided by the automated self-service kiosk that is not in
    good working order.

### [Personnel Training for the Assistance of Persons with Disabilities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1175995 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A transportation service
provider must ensure that members of personnel receive the training that
is required under sections 16 to 19.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interaction with public, etc.

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  member of personnel may be required to interact with the public or to
  participate in making decisions or in developing policies or
  procedures in relation to the requirements of these Regulations, they
  must receive training that provides an adequate level of knowledge and
  skills to carry out those functions, including training with respect
  to the requirements of these Regulations and the policies and
  procedures of the transportation service provider with respect to
  persons with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of training

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The training referred to in subsection (1) must
  provide an adequate level of knowledge in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following principles:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the principle that all persons must be treated
      with dignity regardless of their disabilities,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the principle that all persons must have the
      same opportunity to make for themselves the lives that they are
      able and wish to have regardless of their disabilities or of how
      their disabilities interact with their personal and social
      characteristics,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the principle that all persons must have
      barrier-free access to full and equal participation in society,
      regardless of their disabilities, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the principle that all persons must have
      meaningful options and be free to make their own choices, with
      support if they desire, regardless of their disabilities;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the different types of barriers that may hinder
    equal access to transportation services for persons with
    disabilities;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the various types of assistance that may be needed
    by persons with disabilities and the duties of the transportation
    service provider in relation to those needs, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type of assistance that they must provide to
      persons with disabilities, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the assistive devices that are commonly used by
      persons with disabilities and the methods of communication that
      may be used by, or may facilitate communication with, persons with
      disabilities, such as an augmentative or alternative communication
      system, sign language or clear, concise and plain language;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} communication with persons with disabilities in
    accordance with section 6 and how to interact with them in a manner
    that respects their autonomy and dignity;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the role of a support person; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the role and needs of a service dog.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Physical assistance

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If a member of personnel
may be required to provide physical assistance to a person with a
disability in the course of carrying out their functions, they must
receive training that provides an adequate level of knowledge and skills
to carry out those functions, including training on how to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} seek information from the person with respect to
  their preferred method of assistance and any other measures they may
  require to ensure their safety and their comfort;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} manoeuvre mobility aids through doors and on
  irregular and multi-level surfaces, steps, curbs and elevators;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transfer the person between their own mobility aid
  and a mobility aid provided by the transportation service provider and
  between a mobility aid and the person's passenger seat, including
  performing appropriate lifting techniques to perform various types of
  transfers of the person with maximum consideration for their dignity,
  safety and comfort;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} guide and orient a person whose impairment affects
  their mobility; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} assist a person who has limitations in balance,
  agility or coordination that affect their mobility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handling mobility aids

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a member of personnel
may be required to handle mobility aids in the course of carrying out
their functions, they must receive training that provides an adequate
level of knowledge and skills to carry out those functions, including
training with respect to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the different types of mobility aids; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the requirements and appropriate methods for
  transporting and storing mobility aids, including the disassembling,
  packaging, unpackaging and reassembling of mobility aids.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Using or assisting with special equipment

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  member of personnel may be required to use, or to assist a person with
  a disability in using, any special equipment in the course of carrying
  out their functions, they must receive training that provides an
  adequate level of knowledge and skills to carry out those functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[special
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[special
  equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a telecommunication device for persons who are deaf
    or who have any other hearing impairment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a lift, a ramp and any other level-change device;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an on-board electrical power supply;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a device for the connection of on-board auxiliary
    respirator systems;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an on-board entertainment system that is accessible
    to persons with disabilities; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an automated self-service kiosk that is accessible
    to persons with disabilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial training --- timeline

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  transportation service provider must ensure that a member of personnel
  has completed training suitable to the requirements of their functions
  within 60 days after the day on which that member assumes those
  functions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supervision of untrained personnel

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Until a member of personnel has completed the
  training that is suitable to the requirements of their functions, the
  transportation service provider must ensure that they carry out their
  functions under the direct supervision of a person who has completed
  that training.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refresher training

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A transportation service
provider must ensure that members of personnel who have received
training that is required by this Part also receive, at least once every
three years, refresher training that is suitable to the requirements of
their functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform personnel

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** If a transportation
service provider introduces any new policy, procedure or technology with
respect to persons with disabilities or offers any new
transportation-related service or facility to assist persons with
disabilities, the transportation service provider must, as soon as
feasible, inform members of personnel of that new information, unless it
is not relevant to the requirements of their functions.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparation of training programs

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  transportation service provider must implement and maintain each
  training program for members of personnel in accordance with the
  following requirements:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the training program must include the information
    set out in Schedule 1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the training program must be available for
    inspection by the Agency; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any new information that is referred to in section
    22 must be incorporated in the training program as soon as feasible.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consultation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A transportation service provider must consult
  persons with disabilities in the development of each training program
  and the principal teaching methods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information about training program

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A transportation service provider must, as soon as
  feasible, make available any information about a training program that
  is set out in Schedule 1, except any personal information or
  confidential business information, to any person who requests that
  information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions

    subsection 55(1) of the Act. ([licencié]{.DefinedTermLink

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176062 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Air, rail, marine and bus carriers

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise specified, this Part applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every large air carrier that provides a service for
    the transportation of passengers

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} between points within Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from a point of origin in Canada to a point of
      destination in a foreign country, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} from a point of origin in a foreign country to
      a point of destination in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} every rail carrier that provides a service for the
    transportation of passengers

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} between three or more provinces,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} between three or more provinces and from a point
      of origin in Canada to a point of destination in a foreign
      country, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} from a point of origin in a foreign country to
      a point of destination in three or more provinces;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} every marine carrier that provides a service for
    the transportation of passengers between two or more provinces or
    between Canada and a foreign country if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the service is provided by means of a ferry of
      1,000 gross tonnage or more, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the carrier offers on-board services to
      passengers; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} every bus carrier that provides a service for the
    transportation of passengers between two or more provinces and from
    a point of origin in Canada to a point of destination in a foreign
    country.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[large air
  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[large air
  carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an air carrier that transported a
  worldwide total of 1,000,000 passengers or more during each of the two
  preceding calendar years.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of this Part

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  air carrier transports a passenger between Canada and a foreign
  country and that trip comprises a series of flights, this Part does
  not apply in respect of any flight in that series except a flight in
  that series that departs from, or arrives in, a point in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charter flights

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply to an air carrier in respect
  of a charter flight except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a charter flight within Canada if one or more seats
    on that flight are purchased for resale to the public; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a charter flight to or from Canada if one or more
    passengers began their itinerary in Canada and one or more seats on
    that flight are purchased for resale to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Licensee obligations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must include the obligation to comply with
  these Regulations in its contracts with a charterer with respect to
  flights referred to in subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- certain aircraft

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The following provisions
do not apply to an air carrier in respect of a transportation service
provided by means of an aircraft that has a certificated maximum
carrying capacity of not more than 29 passengers:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 35(k); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the aisle width between passenger seats is not
  sufficient to accommodate the use of an on-board wheelchair, paragraph
  35(m).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- certain ferries

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Section 53 does not apply to a
  marine carrier in respect of a transportation service provided by
  means of a ferry if the carrier does not offer assigned passenger

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- adjacent passenger

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections 50(2) and 51(4) and section 52,

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- certain buses

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** This Part does not apply
to a bus carrier in respect of any of the following transportation
services:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a transportation service provided by means of a bus
  that has not more than 39 passenger seats;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transportation service that is a charter service.

### [Advance Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176108 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}At least 48 hours

- **[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person with a disability makes a request for a service set out in this
  Part at least 48 hours before the scheduled time of departure, the
  carrier must provide the service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if a person with a disability makes a
  request for a service referred to in any of paragraphs 35(a), (b),
  (g), (i) to (l) and (n) to (r) or section 37 or 38 less than 48 hours
  before the scheduled time of departure, the carrier must provide the
  service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- certain conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person with a disability makes a request for a
    service set out in this Part, other than a service referred to in
    subsection (2), less than 96 hours before the scheduled time of
    departure;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the request is made, the carrier
    informs the person that information or documents are required to
    permit the carrier to assess their request and the person must
    provide them within 48 hours; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person does not provide the information or
      documents required by the carrier within the period of 48 hours or
      the information and documents provided are not reasonably
      sufficient to permit the carrier to assess the request, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the carrier is not able to complete its
      assessment of the request within a period of 48 hours because it
      includes at least one day that is not a business day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No advance notice

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (1) and (3), the carrier must
  make every reasonable effort to provide a service requested by a
  person with a disability even if the person does not comply with a
  requirement set out in those subsections.

### [Services]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176129 .Subheading}

#### [Assistance for Persons with Disabilities]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176130 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for priority boarding

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must permit a person with a disability, on request, to board
  in advance of other passengers if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person has requested assistance with boarding,
    locating their passenger seat or cabin, transferring between a
    mobility aid and their passenger seat or storing carry-on baggage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the person is blind or has any
    other visual impairment, the person has requested a description of
    the layout of the aircraft, train, ferry or bus, as the case may be,
    or of the location and operation of operating controls at the
    person's passenger seat; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case where the person is disabled due to a
    severe allergy, the person has requested to clean their passenger
    seat to remove any potential allergens.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement to board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person with a disability requests the assistance
  referred to in paragraph (1)(a) or (b), a carrier may require the
  person, in order for members of personnel to provide that assistance,
  to board before the other passengers or, if the person arrives at the
  boarding area after the end of priority boarding, to board after the
  other passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services to be provided on request

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must, on the
request of a person with a disability, provide the following services to
the person without delay:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person with checking in at the check-in
  counter;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permitting the person, if they are unable to use an
  automated self-service kiosk or other automated check-in or ticketing
  process, to advance to the front of the line at a check-in counter or
  ticket counter;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in proceeding through any
  security screening process at the terminal, including by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} providing members of personnel to assist the person
    to proceed through the security screening process, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} collaborating with the relevant security authority
    or security personnel at the terminal to permit a person who is not
    travelling with the person with a disability to have access to the
    security screening checkpoint so that they may assist the person
    with a disability to proceed through the security screening process;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in proceeding to the boarding
  area after check-in;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} before boarding, transferring the person between the
  person's own mobility aid and a mobility aid provided by the carrier;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in boarding and disembarking
  and, in the case of a person travelling on a ferry, assisting the
  person in moving from a vehicle deck to a passenger deck or from a
  passenger deck to a vehicle deck;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in storing and retrieving their
  carry-on baggage;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} before departure and on arrival at the destination,
  transferring the person between a mobility aid and the person's
  passenger seat;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in moving in and out of a
  mobility aid space;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who is blind or has any other
  visual impairment, describing to the person, before departure or, if
  impossible because of time constraints, after departure, the layout of
  the aircraft, train, ferry or bus, as the case may be, including the
  location of washrooms and exits, and the location and operation of any
  operating controls at the person's passenger seat;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in accessing any entertainment
  content that is offered on board, such as by providing them with a
  personal electronic device and assisting them in using that device;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} before departure, providing the person with an
  individualized safety briefing and demonstration;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aircraft, train or ferry, providing
  the person with an on-board wheelchair;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aircraft, train or ferry, assisting
  the person in moving between their passenger seat and a washroom,
  including by assisting them in transferring between their passenger
  seat and an on-board wheelchair;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aircraft, permitting a person to
  use the washroom that has the most amount of space, regardless of
  where the washroom is located, if the person needs an on-board
  wheelchair or the assistance of a support person or service dog to use
  a washroom;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person who is blind or has any other
  visual impairment, describing to the person, if a meal is offered on
  board, all the food and beverages that are offered for consumption or
  providing a menu in large print or in Braille;

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} if a meal is served on board to the person, assisting
  the person with the meal by opening packages, identifying food items
  and their location and cutting large food portions;

- [(r)]{.lawlabel} if the person is not able to access a food-service
  car on a train, permitting the person and any support person to order
  a meal, and be served the meal, at their passenger seats or in their
  cabin;

- [(s)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in proceeding through border
  clearance;

- [(t)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person in retrieving their checked
  baggage;

- [(u)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person, after disembarkation, in
  proceeding to the general public area;

- [(v)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person, after disembarkation, in
  proceeding to a location where the person may receive assistance to
  proceed to the curbside zone from a member of the terminal operator's
  personnel; and

- [(w)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person, if they are transferring to
  another segment of their trip at the same terminal, in proceeding to a
  location where the person may receive assistance from a member of the
  receiving carrier's personnel.

#### [Transportation of Mobility Aids and Other Assistive Devices]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176184 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority baggage

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to sections 44 to 47, a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Option to retain

    at the destination without delay.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disassembly and reassembly

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A carrier may require a person who requests the
  service referred to in paragraph (1)(b) to do any of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the carrier with written instructions for

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} check in or arrive at the boarding area before the
    scheduled time for check-in or boarding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refused transportation --- aircraft

**[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** An air carrier may refuse
to transport a person's mobility aid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the size of the door to the aircraft's baggage
  compartment or the size of the aircraft's baggage compartment is not
  large enough to accommodate the mobility aid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it would jeopardize aircraft airworthiness; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the weight or size of the mobility aid exceeds the
  capacity of the lift or ramp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refused transportation --- trains

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A rail carrier may refuse
to transport a person's mobility aid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the doorways and turning radii of every passenger
  rail car and the doors of every baggage car are not large enough to
  accommodate the mobility aid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the weight or size of the mobility aid exceeds the
  capacity of the lift or ramp; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person must remain in the mobility aid in order
  to board the train and the combined weight of the person and the
  mobility aid exceeds the capacity of the lift or ramp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refused transportation --- ferries

**[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** A marine carrier may
refuse to transport a person's mobility aid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the weight or size of the mobility aid exceeds the
  capacity of the gangway or ramp; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person must remain in the mobility aid in order
  to board the ferry and the combined weight of the person and the
  mobility aid exceeds the capacity of the gangway or ramp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refused transportation --- buses

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** A bus carrier may refuse
to transport a person's mobility aid if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the size of the door to the bus's baggage compartment
  or the size of the bus's baggage compartment is not large enough to
  accommodate the mobility aid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the weight or size of the mobility aid exceeds the
  capacity of the baggage compartment or of the lift, ramp or bridge
  plate; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person must remain in the mobility aid in order
  to board the bus and the combined weight of the person and the
  mobility aid exceeds the capacity of the lift, ramp or bridge plate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Explanation of refusal and alternatives

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** If a carrier refuses to
transport the mobility aid of a person with a disability, the carrier
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time of the refusal, inform the person of the
  carrier's reasons for refusal and, not later than 10 days after the
  day of the refusal, provide the person with a written notice setting
  out the reasons for the refusal; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} advise the person of the availability of alternative
  trips that are provided by the carrier to the same destination and on
  which the mobility aid will not be refused for transport, and offer to
  book any such alternative trip for the person at the lesser of the
  fare for the original trip and the fare for the alternative trip.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small assistive devices

**[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must permit a
person with a disability to bring on board and to retain any small
assistive device that the person needs during travel, including a cane,
crutches, a communication device, an orthotic positioning device or a
portable oxygen concentrator.

#### [Transportation of Support Persons]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176229 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to transport

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must, on the request of a person with a disability, accept a
  support person for transport if, because of the nature of their
  disability, the person, after departure and before arrival, needs

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assistance with eating meals, taking medication,
    using the washroom;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assistance with transferring to and from a
    passenger seat;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assistance with orientation or communication; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} physical assistance in the event of an emergency,
    including in the case of an evacuation or decompression.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjacent passenger seat

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The carrier must provide a passenger seat for the
  support person that is adjacent to the passenger seat of the person
  with a disability.

#### [Transportation of Service Dogs]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176239 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to transport

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  carrier must, on the request of a person with a disability who needs
  to travel with a service dog, accept the service dog for transport and
  permit the animal to accompany the person on board, subject to
  subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A carrier may require a person with a disability who
  requests to travel with a service dog to control the dog with a leash,
  tether or harness during travel and to provide

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at the time that the person with a disability makes
    the reservation with the carrier, a declaration attesting that the
    service dog has been individually trained by an organization or
    person specializing in service dog training to perform a task to
    assist the person with a disability with a need related to their
    disability; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} before departure, an identification card or other
    document that is issued by an organization or person specializing in
    service dog training that identifies the person with a disability
    and attests that the service dog has been individually trained by
    the organization or person to perform a task to assist the person
    with a disability with a need related to their disability.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic copy

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The condition in paragraph (2)(b) is met if the
  person has provided the card or other document referred to in that
  paragraph to the carrier for the purpose of a previous request for a
  service and the carrier has retained an electronic copy of it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional space for service dog

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If, because of the size of the service dog, the
  passenger seat of the person with a disability does not provide
  sufficient floor space for the service dog to lie down at the person's
  feet in a manner that ensures the safety and well-being of the dog and
  the person, the carrier must provide to the person any passenger seat
  adjacent to their passenger seat that is needed to provide sufficient
  floor space for the service dog.

#### [Additional Passenger Seat]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176251 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to provide additional seating space

**[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** On the request of a
person with a disability who needs more than one passenger seat because
of the nature of their disability, the carrier must provide to the
person any passenger seat adjacent to their passenger seat that is
needed by the person.

#### [Allergy Buffer Zone]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176254 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to establish buffer zone

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On the
  request of a person who has a disability due to a severe allergy, a
  carrier must ensure that a buffer zone is established around the
  passenger seat of the person to assist them in avoiding the risk of
  exposure to the allergen by taking the following measures:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} providing the person with a passenger seat that is
    in a bank of seats other than the bank of seats in which the source
    of the allergen is located and other than the bank of seats facing
    that bank of seats; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notifying the passengers who are sitting in the
    same bank of seats as the person that a passenger with a severe
    allergy is present and informing them of the allergen.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[bank of
  seats]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[bank of
  seats]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means passenger seats that are immediately
  adjacent to each other and does not include passenger seats that are
  across the aisle.

#### [Communication of Information]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176262 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservations --- identification of needs

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person with a disability identifies the nature of their disability
  when making a reservation with a carrier, the carrier must engage in a
  dialogue with the person for the purpose of identifying their needs in
  relation to their disability and the services offered by the carrier
  in relation to those needs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reservations --- bus

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person with a disability identifies the nature
  of their disability when making a reservation with a bus carrier, the
  bus carrier must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide information about any stops and transfer
    points; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advise the person in advance if the route does not
    have a regularly scheduled stop at a rest stop that has a washroom
    that accommodates a person using a mobility aid and any support
    person and offer the person their choice of alternative rest stops
    on the route.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessible seats or cabins

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** If a person with a
disability identifies the nature of their disability when making a
reservation with a carrier, the carrier must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before assigning a passenger seat or cabin to a
  person with a disability, inform the person of the passenger seats and
  cabins that are available in the class of service that the person has
  requested and that have equipment and facilities that best meet the
  accessibility needs of that person, such as a wheelchair-accessible
  washroom or a passenger seat that has additional leg room, a larger
  seat pitch or movable armrests; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in assigning a passenger seat or cabin to a person
  with a disability, take into account the person's opinion with respect
  to which seats or cabins would best meet the accessibility needs of
  that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility aids --- maximum weight and
dimensions

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must publish in
electronic format on its website, or in another format if it does not
have a website, information about the maximum weight and dimensions of
mobility aids that each make and model of its aircraft, trains, ferries
or buses, as the case may be, is capable of transporting.

#### [Procedures Applicable to Requests for Services]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176278 .Subheading}

### [Refusal of Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176283 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for a carrier to refuse to transport a person with a
  disability unless the transportation of the person would impose an
  undue hardship on the carrier.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Explanation of refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a carrier refuses to transport a person with a
  disability, the carrier must, at the time of the refusal, inform the
  person of the carrier's reasons for the refusal and, not later than 10
  days after the day of the refusal, provide the person with a written
  notice setting out the reasons for the refusal, including the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the evidence of undue hardship, such as an
    engineering report, a medical report or an expert opinion that
    demonstrates that the risk is significant enough that it would be
    unreasonable to waive or modify a requirement;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any relevant rule, policy or procedure or
    regulation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the refusal and the conditions, if
    any, under which the carrier would accept the person for transport.

### [Damaged, Destroyed or Lost Mobility Aids]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176292 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of carrier

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** If the mobility aid of a
person with a disability is not retained by the person during transport
and it is damaged, destroyed or lost during transport or is not made
available to the person at the time of their arrival at their
destination, the carrier must, without delay and at the carrier's own
expense,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide them with a temporary replacement mobility
  aid that meets their needs in relation to their mobility and that they
  are permitted to use until their mobility aid is returned to them or
  is repaired or replaced or until they are reimbursed by the carrier
  for the loss of the mobility aid;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reimburse the person for any expenses they have
  incurred because the mobility aid was damaged, destroyed or lost or
  because it was not made available to them at the time of their arrival
  at their destination;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a damaged mobility aid, arrange for
  the repair of the mobility aid and return it to the person without
  delay or, in the case of a damaged mobility aid that cannot be
  adequately repaired,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} replace the damaged mobility aid with the same
    model of mobility aid or, if the same model is not available, a
    model that has equivalent features and qualities as the damaged
    mobility aid and that meets the person's needs in relation to their
    mobility, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} reimburse the person for the full replacement cost
    of the mobility aid; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a destroyed mobility aid or a mobility
  aid that is not made available to the person at the time of their
  arrival at their destination and that is not returned to them within,
  in the case of an air carrier, 96 hours after that arrival or, in the
  case of a train, marine or bus carrier, 72 hours after that arrival,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} replace the destroyed or lost mobility aid with the
    same model of mobility aid or, if the same model is not available, a
    model that has equivalent features and qualities as the destroyed or
    lost mobility aid and that meets the person's needs in relation to
    their mobility, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} reimburse the person for the full replacement cost
    of the mobility aid.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unavailability of compliant amenity or

#### [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176351 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of carrier

**[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must ensure
that any aircraft that it owns, operates or leases and any related
facilities, including any amenities and equipment used in them, meet the
requirements set out in this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lift

**[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** A lift that is used to
assist a person with a disability, including a person in a mobility aid,
to board or disembark from an aircraft must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that are located on both sides of the
  lift and a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 363 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ramp

**[[70]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-70 .sectionLabel}** A ramp that is used to
assist a person with a disability, including a person in a mobility aid,
to board or disembark from an aircraft must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a contrasting colour strip that runs the full
  width of its bottom edge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have raised edges to prevent a mobility aid from
  rolling off the edge of the ramp;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 363 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stairs

- **[[71]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-71 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  stairs that are used to board or disembark from an aircraft, and any
  interior stairs on an aircraft, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.4.1 and
    5.4.2 of CSA B651-18;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on both sides of the stairs,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at a uniform height of 860 mm to 920
      mm, measured vertically from the leading edge of the tread, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.3.1
      of CSA B651-18; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have tactile attention indicator surfaces that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses
      4.3.5.2(a) and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at the top of the stairs, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commence one tread length before the top of the
      stairs, extend the full width of the stairs and measure from 600
      mm to 650 mm in length.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delayed application --- portable stairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For a period of two years beginning on the day on
  which this section comes into force, subsection (1) does not apply in
  respect of stairs that are portable and not integrated with the
  aircraft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board wheelchair

**[[72]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-72 .sectionLabel}** An on-board wheelchair
that is available on an aircraft must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} footrests, movable armrests, an occupant restraint
  device and wheel locks;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a backrest of a height that permits the person using
  the wheelchair to be safely and easily transferred to and from a
  passenger seat; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a seat of a width that permits the wheelchair to be
  safely and easily manoeuvred on board the aircraft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory on-board wheelchair

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** At least one on-board
wheelchair must be available on an aircraft that has a
wheelchair-accessible washroom.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger seats

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** Passenger seats on an
aircraft must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable armrests on at least 50% of the seats, which
  seats are evenly distributed throughout the aircraft; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a call button that is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tactilely discernible and colour-contrasted with
    its background, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile row markers

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Aircraft must be equipped
with tactile row markers, in raised characters and Braille, that are
installed, either permanently or temporarily, on the sides of passenger
seats and above the armrests or adjacent to the mechanisms for opening
overhead bins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger safety features cards

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** An aircraft must have
available on board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least five passenger safety features cards that
  are in large print and at least five passenger safety features cards
  that are in Braille; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least one electronic device that provides
  passenger safety information in an audio and visual format and permits
  a person with a disability to adjust the volume of the audio output
  and the size of the font.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard washroom

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** A washroom on an
aircraft, other than a wheelchair-accessible washroom, must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} doors that are equipped with handles, pulls, latches,
  locks and other devices that are operable using minimal force, with
  one hand in a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation
  that does not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the
  wrist;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} toilets that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are equipped with an automated flush control or a
    flush control that is operable using minimal force, with one hand in
    a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation that does
    not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the wrist, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have a back support if the toilet has no seat lid;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} faucets that are equipped with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} handles or other controls that are tactilely
    discernible unless the water temperature is fixed to eliminate the
    risk of scalding,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of non-automated faucets, handles or
    other controls that are operable using minimal force, with one hand
    in a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation that
    does not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the wrist,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of automated faucets, a water
    activation motion sensor that turns the water on automatically when
    a hand is below the faucet;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} grab bars that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on a wall beside the toilet,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are rounded, slip-resistant and free of any sharp
    or abrasive elements,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} have an exterior diameter and a clearance from
    the wall surface to which they are attached that permit them to be
    easily grasped, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} are capable of supporting a minimum weight of
    113.4 kg;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} toilet paper dispensers that are located so as to not
  interfere with the use of the grab bars;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} soap dispensers that are operable using minimal
  force, with one hand in a closed-fist position, or by another method
  of operation that does not require tight grasping, pinching or
  twisting of the wrist; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} at least one call button or other device for
  signalling an emergency that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is colour-contrasted with its background and
    identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
washroom on an aircraft must meet the requirements set out in section 77
and must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a door or other opening to the washroom that has
  sufficient space in front of it and that is wide enough to allow the
  entry of a person using an on-board wheelchair;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have sufficient space to allow a person, with
  assistance, to transfer to and from an on-board wheelchair and the
  toilet;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have sufficient privacy, including with the use of
  retractable curtains or walls, to allow a support person or service
  dog to remain in the washroom with a person using the on-board
  wheelchair; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with faucets that are positioned to be
  easily usable by a person using an on-board wheelchair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** Every washroom on an
aircraft must be a wheelchair-accessible washroom, unless the washroom
does not have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a door or other opening to the washroom that has
  sufficient space in front of it and that is wide enough to allow the
  entry of a person using an on-board wheelchair; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sufficient space to allow a person, with assistance,
  to transfer to and from an on-board wheelchair and the toilet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board entertainment system

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** An on-board entertainment
system on an aircraft must be equipped to permit a person with a
disability to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} access entertainment content that offers closed
  captioning and audio description and that is the same entertainment
  content that is offered to the other passengers or, if that is not
  possible, that is comparable entertainment content; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} start, stop and pause the entertainment content, turn
  on and off the closed captioning and audio description, change the
  language of choice, control the volume and select channels

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} through an interface that meets the requirements
    for an interface that are set out in Web Content Accessibility
    Guidelines, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the interface has not been designed to be used
    with the on-board entertainment system, through a tactile template
    overlay affixed to the interface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing aircraft

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pre-existing aircraft referred to in subsection 66(4) has an on-board
  entertainment system that does not offer closed captioning and audio
  description, the aircraft must be equipped with personal electronic
  devices in a number that is sufficient to accommodate the number of
  persons with disabilities who have made a request for such a device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal electronic devices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The personal electronic devices must be pre-loaded
  with --- or the aircraft must provide wireless streaming of ---
  entertainment content that offers closed captioning and audio
  description and that is the same entertainment content that is offered
  to the other passengers or, if that is not possible, that is
  comparable entertainment content.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Floors

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** The floors of an aircraft
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.1 to 4.3.3 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile attention indicator surfaces

**[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** Tactile attention
indicator surfaces on an aircraft must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be located at the top of stairs and at any other
  location where there is an unprotected drop-off edge and a change in
  elevation greater than 250 mm or where the ratio of the slope to
  surface is steeper than 1:3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.5.2(a)
  and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Signage on an aircraft must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate any services, facilities, amenities or
    equipment that are offered and the direction to follow in order to
    access them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
    have a glare-free surface;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of electronic signage, meet the
    requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to 4.5.7 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Electronic signage must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have letters, numbers, symbols and pictographs that
    slowly scroll across the screen and are colour-contrasted with their
    background but are not in red on a black background; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
    4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  aircraft and any related facilities, including any amenities and
  equipment used in them, that are subject to the requirements of this
  Division must be in good working order and must be properly
  maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any facilities, amenities or equipment that are
  referred to in subsection (1) are not in good working order, they must
  be repaired as soon as possible and, until they are repaired, measures
  must be taken that will result in a substantially equivalent or
  greater level of accessibility for persons with disabilities.

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Rail Carriers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176460 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176461 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rail carriers

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified, this Division applies to every rail carrier that provides a
service for the transportation of passengers

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} between three or more provinces; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} between three or more provinces and from a point of
  origin in Canada to a point of destination in a foreign country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing trains

- **[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the provisions of this Division, except sections 97, 98, 108 and 117,
  do not apply to a pre-existing train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- portable equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 90 to 93 apply to a pre-existing train with
  respect to any step box, lift or ramp that is portable and not
  integrated with the pre-existing train.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a carrier makes a modification --- other than a
  modification to a mechanical, electrical or plumbing system or a
  modification carried out for an aesthetic purpose or for maintenance
  or repair --- to any amenity or equipment that is used in a
  pre-existing train, the carrier must ensure that the modified amenity
  or equipment meets the requirements of this Division, except in the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dimensions of the train or of the amenity or
    equipment are unalterable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the structural integrity or safe operation of the
    train or of the amenity or equipment would be materially affected;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the principal purpose of the amenity or equipment
    would be fundamentally altered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[pre-existing
  train]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[pre-existing
  train]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a train that was

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier before the day
    on which this section comes into force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier on or after the
    day on which this section comes into force, if the carrier submitted
    the call for tenders in respect of that train before that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- temporary leases

**[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply in respect of a train that is leased by a carrier on a short-term
basis in order to respond to an emergency or to replace a train that
requires repairs due to an unforeseen mechanical failure.

#### [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176482 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of carrier

**[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must ensure
that any train that it owns, operates or leases and any related
facilities, including any amenities and equipment used in them, meet the
requirements of this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Step box

**[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** A step box that is used
to assist a person with a disability to board or disembark from a train
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be of sound construction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a tread surface that is firm and slip-resistant;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a glare-free surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a top outer edge that is marked by a contrasting
  colour strip that runs the full width of the box.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory step box

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** A rail car must be
equipped with a step box if the riser height of the first or last step
of any stairs that are used by passengers to board or disembark is
greater than the riser height of the other steps.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lift

**[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** A lift that is used to
assist a person with a disability, including a person in a mobility aid,
to board or disembark from a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that are located on both sides of the
  lift and a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 363 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ramp

**[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** A ramp that is used to
assist a person with a disability, including a person in a mobility aid,
to board or disembark from a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a contrasting colour strip that runs the full
  width of its bottom edge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have raised edges to prevent a mobility aid from
  rolling off the edge of the ramp;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 363 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No level boarding

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** If a rail terminal does
not permit level boarding of a train and there is no lift or ramp
available at the rail terminal, the train must be equipped with a lift
or ramp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other stops

**[[95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-95 .sectionLabel}** If a train makes a stop
at any place that is not at a terminal and where passengers are
permitted to board and disembark, the train must be equipped with a lift
or ramp.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stairs

**[[96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-96 .sectionLabel}** Any stairs that are used
to board or disembark from a train, and any interior stairs on a train,
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.4.1 and
  5.4.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on both sides of the stairs,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at a uniform height of 860 mm to 920
    mm, measured vertically from the leading edge of the tread, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.3.1 of
    CSA B651-18; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have tactile attention indicator surfaces that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meet the meet the requirements set out in clauses
    4.3.5.2(a) and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at the top of the stairs, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commence one tread length before the top of the
    stairs, extend the full width of the stairs and measure from 600 mm
    to 650 mm in length.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board wheelchair

**[[97]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-97 .sectionLabel}** An on-board wheelchair
that is available on a train must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} footrests, movable armrests, an occupant restraint
  device and wheel locks;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a backrest of a height that permits the person using
  the wheelchair to be safely and easily transferred to and from a
  passenger seat; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a seat of a width that permits the wheelchair to be
  safely and easily manoeuvred on board the train.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory on-board wheelchairs

**[[98]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-98 .sectionLabel}** A train must have
available on-board wheelchairs in a number that is equal to at least
half of the sum of the number of mobility aid spaces on the train and
the number of wheelchair-accessible cabins on the train.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessible path of travel

- **[[99]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-99 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A path
  of travel on a train is accessible to persons with disabilities if it
  meets the requirements set out in subsections (2) and (3) and in
  clauses 4.4.2, 5.1.1(a) to (d) and 5.1.2 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hall, path or turning space

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If access to the path of travel from an exterior door
  requires passage through a hall or path or requires a 90-degree or
  similar turn, then that hall, path or turning space must have a
  minimum clear width of 1,115 mm and a minimum clear diameter of 1,500
  mm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aisle width

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The aisle width between passenger seats must be
  sufficient to accommodate an on-board wheelchair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory accessible path of travel

- **[[100]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-100 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  train must have a path of travel that is accessible to persons with
  disabilities and that provides access to, from and between all of the
  following points on the train:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any exterior door that is used by passengers to
    board or disembark;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any mobility aid space or transfer seat; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any wheelchair-accessible washroom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible area

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any area of a train that is intended for use by a
  person using a wheelchair, including a wheelchair-accessible cabin or
  a wheelchair-accessible food service area, must have a path of travel
  that is accessible to persons with disabilities from that area to any
  adjacent rail car, if that adjacent rail car contains a mobility aid
  space, transfer seat or wheelchair-accessible washroom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shower facility and cabin

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A train must have a path of travel that is accessible
  to persons with disabilities between a wheelchair-accessible shower
  facility and a wheelchair-accessible cabin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Train --- diaphragm

- **[[101]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-101 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  diaphragm on a train must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.1.1(a)
    to (d) of CSA B651-18; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have vertical handholds that are located on both
    sides of the diaphragm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[diaphragm]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[diaphragm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the passageway between rail cars.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger seats

**[[102]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-102 .sectionLabel}** Passenger seats on a
train must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable armrests on at least 10% of the seats, which
  seats are evenly distributed throughout each rail car, except a food
  service rail car;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} handholds on the back of all aisle passenger seats;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are call buttons, call buttons that are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tactilely discernible and colour-contrasted with
    their background, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile row markers

**[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** Trains must be equipped
with tactile row markers, in raised characters and Braille, that are
installed, either permanently or temporarily, on the sides of passenger
seats and above the armrests or adjacent to the mechanisms for opening
overhead bins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility aid space

**[[104]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-104 .sectionLabel}** A mobility aid space on
a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area of at least 815 mm by 1,650
  mm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with a call button that is in a location
  within the reach of the person who is seated in the mobility aid
  space, that is tactilely discernible and colour-contrasted with its
  background and that is operable using minimal force; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with a table or counter for food service
  which, if located in a food service rail car that contains tables,
  meets the requirements set out in clauses 6.7.1.2 and 6.7.1.3 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer seat

**[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** A transfer seat on a
train must be equipped with a table or counter for food service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory mobility aid space and transfer
seat

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  train must, in each class of service, have at least two adjacent
  mobility aid spaces and two adjacent transfer seats, as well as a
  number of additional mobility aid spaces and a number of additional
  transfer seats equal to one mobility aid space and one transfer seat
  for every 75 passenger seats.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum of two mobility aid spaces

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} There must be no more than two mobility aid spaces in
  each rail car.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food service rail car

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A food service rail car that offers passenger seats
  must have at least two mobility aid spaces. In the case of a bi-level
  food service rail car, there must be at least two mobility aid spaces
  that are located on the lower level of the rail car.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of mobility aids

- **[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At
  least one rail car of a train must have a space for the storage of at
  least one mobility aid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection from damage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mobility aid must be stored on a train in a manner
  that protects it from damage, including by using an anchoring system
  or by placing it in an area that is not intended for use by
  passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger safety features cards

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** A train must have
available on board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least five passenger safety features cards that
  are in large print and at least five passenger safety features cards
  that are in Braille; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least five electronic devices that provide
  passenger safety information in an audio and visual format and permit
  a person with a disability to adjust the volume of the audio output
  and the size of the font.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Window emergency exits

**[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** If a rail car is
equipped with a window emergency exit, the window emergency exit must be
identified by a contrasting colour strip around the perimeter and by
tactile and Braille signage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard washroom

**[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** A washroom on a train,
other than a wheelchair-accessible washroom, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.3 of CSA
  B651-18, except clause 6.3.1.1(a) and except that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 4.2" in clause 6.2.4.1
    must be read as excluding clause 4.2.2,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 5.2" in clause 6.3.2 must
    be read as excluding clause 5.2.2, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 6.2.3" in clause
    6.3.1.1(b) must be read as excluding clauses 6.2.3.1(c) and (d); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located such that at least one button or device
    is not more than 450 mm above the floor and at least one other
    button or device is 800 mm to 1,100 mm above the floor,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are colour-contrasted with their background and
    identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[111]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-111 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
washroom on a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.3 of CSA
  B651-18, except clause 6.3.1.1(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a clear area of at least 1,500 mm by 1,500
  mm; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that meet the requirements
  set out in paragraph 110(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible washroom

- **[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  rail car on a train that has a mobility aid space, except a food
  service rail car, must have at least one wheelchair-accessible
  washroom.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food service rail car

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a food service rail car has one or more washrooms,
  at least one of them must be a wheelchair-accessible washroom.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Menu display board

**[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** A menu display board in
a food service rail car on a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be positioned at a height that allows it to be easily
  seen by a person using a wheelchair;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
  have a glare-free surface;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
  4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counter

**[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114 .sectionLabel}** A counter in a food
service rail car on a train must meet the requirements set out in
clauses 6.7.1.2 and 6.7.1.3 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Location of food service rail car

**[[115]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-115 .sectionLabel}** A food service rail car
on a train must be adjacent to a rail car that has a mobility aid space
or a rail car that has a wheelchair-accessible cabin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board entertainment system

**[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** An on-board
entertainment system on a train must be equipped to permit a person with
a disability to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} access entertainment content that offers closed
  captioning and audio description and that is the same entertainment
  content that is offered to the other passengers or, if that is not
  possible, that is comparable entertainment content; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} start, stop and pause the entertainment content, turn
  on and off the closed captioning and audio description, change the
  language of choice, control the volume and select channels

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} through an interface that meets the requirements
    for an interface that are set out in the Web Content Accessibility
    Guidelines, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the interface has not been designed to be used
    with the on-board entertainment system, through a tactile template
    overlay affixed to the interface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing train

- **[[117]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-117 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pre-existing train referred to in subsection 87(4) has an on-board
  entertainment system that does not offer closed captioning and audio
  description, the train must be equipped with personal electronic
  devices in a number that is sufficient to accommodate the number of
  persons with disabilities who have made a request for such a device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal electronic device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The personal electronic devices must be pre-loaded
  with --- or the train must provide wireless streaming of ---
  entertainment content that offers closed captioning and audio
  description and that is the same entertainment content that is offered
  to the other passengers or, if that is not possible, that is
  comparable entertainment content.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard cabin

- **[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  cabin on a train, other than a wheelchair-accessible cabin, must be
  equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating controls that meet the requirements set
    out in clause 4.2 of CSA B651-18, except clause 4.2.2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} doors that meet the requirements set out in clause
    5.2 of CSA B651-18, except clauses 5.2.1, 5.2.2 and 5.2.9.2 and
    except that the reference to "clause 4.2" in clause 5.2.7.1(a) must
    be read as excluding clause 4.2.2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least two call buttons or two other devices for
    signalling an emergency that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located such that at least one is not more
      than 450 mm above the floor, and at least one other is 800 mm to
      1,100 mm above the floor,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are colour-contrasted with their background and
      identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are operable using minimal force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protruding objects

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there are objects that protrude more than 100 mm
  from the wall in a standard cabin, the objects must be cane-detectable
  at or below 680 mm from the floor or their undersides must be at a
  height of at least 2,030 mm from the floor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible cabin

**[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
cabin on a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area that meets the requirements
  set out in clause 4.1(b) of CSA B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with operating controls that meet the
  requirements set out in clause 4.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 4.4.2 of CSA
  B651-18;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with an entry door that is identified by
  the International Symbol of Access and meets the requirements set out
  in clause 5.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that meet the requirements
  of paragraph 118(1)(c); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} be adjacent to a wheelchair-accessible washroom or
  have a path of travel adjacent to the cabin that is accessible to
  persons with disabilities and that provides access to a
  wheelchair-accessible washroom.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible cabin

**[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** A train that has cabins
must have at least two wheelchair-accessible cabins that are adjacent to
each other.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard shower facilities

**[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** A shower facility on a
train, other than a wheelchair-accessible shower facility, must meet the
requirements set out in clause 6.5 of CSA B651-18, except clauses
6.5.5.1, 6.5.5.2 and 6.5.5.3(c)(i) and except that the reference to
"clause 4.2" in clause 6.2.3.3(a) must be read as excluding clause
4.2.2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible shower facility

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
shower facility on a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.5 of CSA
  B651-18 except clauses 6.5.5.1 and 6.5.5.2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an interior clear area of at least 900 mm by
  1,500 mm;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area in front of the shower
  entrance of at least 900 mm by 1,500 mm, with the 1,500 mm dimension
  parallel to the shower entrance; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be located near to a wheelchair-accessible cabin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible shower
facility

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** A train that has a
standard shower facility must also have at least one
wheelchair-accessible shower facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interior door and doorway

- **[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interior door and doorway on a train must meet the requirements set
  out in clauses 5.2.1 and 5.2.6 to 5.2.8 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interior door must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an automatic or semi-automatic door,
    incorporate devices that prevent the door from closing while a
    person is in the doorway and, in the case of an automatic door that
    connects rail cars, open and close the door automatically on
    detecting the movement of a person toward or away from the door;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be marked with visual indicators if 75% of the
    door's surface is composed of a transparent material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door to an enclosed space in an
    area that has no other exit doors, have a safety mechanism that is
    not a deadbolt and is capable of being manipulated from the outside;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door that is intended for use by a

    clauses 4.2.2 and 5.2.2 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exterior door

**[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125 .sectionLabel}** An exterior door on a

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.2.1 of CSA
  B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a handrail

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on each side of the door, fitted internally as
    close as is feasible to the outer wall of the rail car, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on only one side of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a doorway that is fitted with a device for
      boarding or disembarking, such as a lift, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a trap door to which stairs are attached; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the door is intended to be used by a person using
  a mobility aid, meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.2.2(a)
  and (b) of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handrails

**[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126 .sectionLabel}** Handrails on a train
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.3.1 and 5.3.2 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Floors

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** The floors of a train
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.1 to 4.3.3 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile attention indicator surfaces

**[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** Tactile attention
indicator surfaces on a train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be located at the top of stairs and at any other
  location where there is an unprotected drop-off edge and a change in
  elevation greater than 250 mm or where the ratio of the slope to
  surface is steeper than 1:3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.5.2(a)
  and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating controls

**[[129]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-129 .sectionLabel}** Operating controls on a
train must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.2.1, 4.2.3
  to 4.2.6 and 4.2.8 of CSA B651-18; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are intended to be used by a person using a
  wheelchair, meet the requirements set out in clause 4.2.2 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

- **[[130]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-130
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Signage on a train, other than a
  menu display board referred to in section 113, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate any services, facilities, amenities or
    equipment that are offered and the direction to follow in order to
    access them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
    have a glare-free surface;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of electronic signage, meet the
    requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to 4.5.7 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Electronic signage must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have letters, numbers, symbols and pictographs that
    slowly scroll across the screen and are colour-contrasted with their
    background but are not in red on a black background; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
    4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevator

**[[131]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-131 .sectionLabel}** If a train is equipped
with an elevator, the elevator must meet the requirements set out in
clause 5.6.1 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alarm system

**[[132]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-132 .sectionLabel}** If a train is equipped
with an alarm system, including in a cabin, the alarm system must
consist of both a visual alarm and an audible alarm. A visual alarm must
meet the requirements set out in clause 5.7.1 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

- **[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  train and any related facilities, including any amenities and
  equipment used in them, that are subject to any requirements of this
  Division must be in good working order and properly maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any facilities, amenities or equipment that are
  referred to in subsection (1) are not in good working order, they must
  be repaired as soon as possible and, until they are repaired, measures
  must be taken that will result in a substantially equivalent or
  greater level of accessibility for persons with disabilities.

### [DIVISION 3]{.HLabel2}[Marine Carriers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176705 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176706 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine carriers

**[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified, this Division applies to every marine carrier that provides a
service for the transportation of passengers between two or more
provinces or from a point of origin in Canada to a point of destination
in a foreign country if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the service is provided by means of a ferry of 1,000
  gross tonnage or more; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the carrier offers on-board services to passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing ferries

- **[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the provisions of this Division, except sections 143, 144, 153, 164
  and 178, do not apply to a pre-existing ferry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- portable equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 138, 140 and 142 apply to a pre-existing
  ferry with respect to any step box, ramp, gangway or stairs that are
  portable and not integrated with the pre-existing ferry.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a carrier makes a modification --- other than a
  modification to a mechanical, electrical or plumbing system or a
  modification carried out for an aesthetic purpose or for maintenance
  or repair --- to any amenity or equipment that is used in a
  pre-existing ferry, the carrier must ensure that the modified amenity
  or equipment meets the requirements of this Division, except in the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dimensions of the ferry or of the amenity or
    equipment are unalterable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the structural integrity or safe operation of the
    ferry or of the amenity or equipment would be materially affected;
    or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the principal purpose of the amenity or equipment
    would be fundamentally altered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[pre-existing
  ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[pre-existing
  ferry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a ferry that was

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier before the day
    on which this section comes into force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier on or after the
    day on which this section comes into force, if the carrier submitted
    the call for tenders in respect of that ferry before that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- temporary leases

**[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply in respect of a ferry that is leased by a carrier on a short-term
basis in order to respond to an emergency or to replace a ferry that
requires repairs due to an unforeseen mechanical failure.

#### [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176727 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of carrier

**[[137]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-137 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must ensure
that any ferry that it owns, operates or leases and any related
facilities, including any amenities and equipment used in them, meet the
requirements set out in this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Step box

**[[138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-138 .sectionLabel}** A step box that is used
to assist a person with a disability to board or disembark from a ferry
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be of sound construction;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a tread surface that is firm and slip-resistant;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a glare-free surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a top outer edge that is marked by a contrasting
  colour strip that runs the full width of the box.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory step box

**[[139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-139 .sectionLabel}** A ferry must be
equipped with a step box if the riser height of the first or last step
of any stairs that are used by passengers to board or disembark is
greater than the riser height of the other steps.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ramp or gangway

**[[140]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-140 .sectionLabel}** A ramp or gangway that
is used to assist a person with a disability, including a person in a
mobility aid, to board or disembark from a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a contrasting colour strip that runs the full
  width of its bottom edge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have raised edges to prevent a mobility aid from
  rolling off the edge of the ramp;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 363 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No level boarding

**[[141]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-141 .sectionLabel}** If a ferry terminal
does not permit level boarding of a ferry, the ferry must have a vehicle
deck that is used for the boarding and disembarking of passengers or be
equipped with a ramp or gangway.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stairs

- **[[142]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-142 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  stairs that are used to board or disembark from a ferry, and any
  interior stairs on a ferry, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.4.1 and
    5.4.2 of CSA B651-18;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on both sides of the stairs,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at a uniform height of 860 mm to 920
      mm, measured vertically from the leading edge of the tread, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.3.1
      of CSA B651-18; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} have tactile attention indicator surfaces that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses
      4.3.5.2(a) and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at the top of the stairs, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commence one tread length before the top of the
      stairs, extend the full width of the stairs and measure from 600
      mm to 650 mm in length.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Delayed application --- portable stairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For a period of two years beginning on the day on
  which this section comes into force, subsection (1) does not apply in
  respect of stairs that are portable and not integrated with the ferry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board wheelchair

**[[143]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-143 .sectionLabel}** An on-board wheelchair
that is available on a ferry must have footrests and wheel locks.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory on-board wheelchair

**[[144]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-144 .sectionLabel}** At least one on-board
wheelchair must be available on each deck of a ferry, except a deck that
is not accessible to a person using a wheelchair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessible paths of travel

- **[[145]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-145 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  path of travel on a ferry is accessible to persons with disabilities
  if it meets the requirements set out in subsections (2) and (3) and in
  clauses 4.4.2, 5.1.1 and 5.1.2 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hall, path or turning space

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If access to the path of travel from an exterior door
  requires passage through a hall or path or requires a 90-degree or
  similar turn, then that hall, path or turning space must have a
  minimum clear width of 1,115 mm and a minimum clear diameter of 1,500
  mm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aisle width

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The aisle width between passenger seats must be
  sufficient to accommodate an on-board wheelchair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory accessible path of travel

**[[146]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-146 .sectionLabel}** Every path of travel
that is on a ferry and that is intended for use by passengers must be a
path of travel that is accessible to persons with disabilities.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger seats

**[[147]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-147 .sectionLabel}** Passenger seats on a
ferry must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable armrests on at least 10% of the seats, which
  seats are evenly distributed throughout each passenger seating area,
  except a food service area or lounge area; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there are call buttons, call buttons that are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tactilely discernible and colour-contrasted with
    their background, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile row markers

**[[148]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-148 .sectionLabel}** If a marine carrier
offers assigned passenger seats to passengers, the assigned passenger
seats must be equipped with tactile row markers, in raised characters
and Braille, that are installed, either permanently or temporarily, on
the sides of the seats and above the armrests.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility aid space

**[[149]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-149 .sectionLabel}** A mobility aid space on
a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area of at least 815 mm by 1,650
  mm;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned such that a person using a mobility aid
  has adequate manoeuvring space to enter it; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a table or counter that meets the requirements
  set out in clauses 6.7.1.2 and 6.7.1.3 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer seat

**[[150]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-150 .sectionLabel}** A transfer seat on a
ferry must be equipped with a table or counter for food service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory mobility aid space and transfer
seat

- **[[151]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-151 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  ferry must, in each passenger seating area, have at least two adjacent
  mobility aid spaces and two adjacent transfer seats.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food service and lounge areas

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A food service area or a lounge area that offers
  passenger seats must have at least two adjacent mobility aid spaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of mobility aids

- **[[152]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-152 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  deck of a ferry must have a space for the storage of at least one
  mobility aid.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protection from damage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mobility aid must be stored on a ferry in a manner
  that protects it from damage, including by using an anchoring system
  or by placing it in an area that is not intended for use by
  passengers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger safety features cards

**[[153]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-153 .sectionLabel}** Each passenger deck of
a ferry must have available on board

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least five passenger safety features cards that
  are in large print and at least five passenger safety features cards
  that are in Braille; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least one electronic device that provides
  passenger safety information in an audio and visual format and permits
  a person with a disability to adjust the volume of the audio output
  and the size of the font.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Window emergency exits

**[[154]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-154 .sectionLabel}** If a ferry is equipped
with a window emergency exit, the window emergency exit must be
identified by a contrasting colour strip around the perimeter and by
tactile and Braille signage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard washroom

**[[155]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-155 .sectionLabel}** A washroom on a ferry,
other than a wheelchair-accessible washroom, must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.3 of CSA
  B651-18, except clause 6.3.1.1(a) and except that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 4.2" in clause 6.2.4.1
    must be read as excluding clause 4.2.2,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 5.2" in clause 6.3.2 must
    be read as excluding clause 5.2.2, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "clause 6.2.3" in clause
    6.3.1.1(b) must be read as excluding clauses 6.2.3.1(c) and
    6.2.3.1(d); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located such that at least one button or device
    is not more than 450 mm above the floor and at least one other
    button or device is 800 mm to 1,100 mm above the floor,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are colour-contrasted with their background and
    identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[156]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-156 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
washroom on a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.3 of CSA
  B651-18, except clause 6.3.1.1(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide a clear area of at least 1,500 mm by 1,500
  mm; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that meet the requirements
  set out in paragraph 155(b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[157]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-157 .sectionLabel}** If a deck, a food
service area or a lounge area of a ferry has one or more washrooms, at
least one of them must be a wheelchair-accessible washroom.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Menu display board

**[[158]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-158 .sectionLabel}** A menu display board in
a food service area on a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be positioned at a height that allows it to be easily
  seen by a person using a wheelchair;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
  have a glare-free surface;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
  4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Counter

**[[159]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-159 .sectionLabel}** A counter in a food
service area or a lounge area on a ferry must meet the requirements set
out in clauses 6.7.1.2 and 6.7.1.3 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Table

**[[160]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-160 .sectionLabel}** At least 5% of the
total number of tables in a food service area or lounge area on a ferry
must be identified by the International Symbol of Access and meet the
requirements set out in clauses 6.7.1.1 to 6.7.1.3 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Food service counter

- **[[161]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-161 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In a
  food service area or a lounge area on a ferry, a handrail must be
  provided along the side of a food-service counter and the floor area
  adjacent to the counter must meet the requirements set out in clauses
  4.4.2, 5.1.1 and 5.1.2 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lineup guide

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Lineup guides in a food service area or a lounge area
  on a ferry must meet the requirements set out in clause 5.1.3 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service counter

**[[162]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-162 .sectionLabel}** A counter that is used
for serving passengers on a ferry, except a food-service counter
referred to in subsection 161(1), must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have at least one section that meets the requirements
  set out in clauses 6.7.1.2 and 6.7.1.3 of CSA B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be free of any object that could impede verbal or
  visual communications between a passenger and a member of personnel;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a glare-free surface and be colour-contrasted
  with its background.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board entertainment system

**[[163]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-163 .sectionLabel}** An on-board
entertainment system on a ferry must be equipped to permit a person with
a disability to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} access entertainment content that offers closed
  captioning and audio description and that is the same entertainment
  content that is offered to the other passengers or, if that is not
  possible, that is comparable entertainment content; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} start, stop and pause the entertainment content, turn
  on and off the closed captioning and audio description, change the
  language of choice, control the volume and select channels

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} through an interface that meets the requirements
    for an interface that are set out in the Web Content Accessibility
    Guidelines, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the interface has not been designed to be used
    with the on-board entertainment system, through a tactile template
    overlay affixed to the interface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing ferry

- **[[164]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-164 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pre-existing ferry referred to in subsection 135(4) has an on-board
  entertainment system that does not offer closed captioning and audio
  description, the ferry must be equipped with personal electronic
  devices in a number that is sufficient to accommodate the number of
  persons with disabilities who have made a request for such a device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal electronic device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The personal electronic devices must be pre-loaded
  with --- or the ferry must provide wireless streaming of ---
  entertainment content that offers closed captioning and audio
  description and that is the same entertainment content that is offered
  to the other passengers or, if that is not possible, that is
  comparable entertainment content.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard cabin

- **[[165]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-165 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  cabin on a ferry, other than a wheelchair-accessible cabin, must be
  equipped with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operating controls that meet the requirements set
    out in clause 4.2 of CSA B651-18, except clause 4.2.2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} doors that meet the requirements set out in clause
    5.2 of CSA B651-18, except clauses 5.2.1, 5.2.2 and 5.2.9.2 and
    except that the reference to "clause 4.2" in clause 5.2.7.1(a) must
    be read as excluding clause 4.2.2; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least two call buttons or two other devices for
    signalling an emergency that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located such that at least one button or
      device is not more than 450 mm above the floor and at least one
      other button or device is 800 mm to 1,100 mm above the floor,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are colour-contrasted with their background and
      identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are operable using minimal force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Protruding object

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there are objects that protrude more than 100 mm
  from the wall in a standard cabin, the objects must be cane-detectable
  at or below 680 mm from the floor or their undersides must be at a
  height of at least 2,030 mm from the floor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible cabin

**[[166]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-166 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
cabin on a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area that meets the requirements
  set out in clause 4.1(b) of CSA B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with operating controls that meet the
  standards set out in clause 4.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 4.4.2 of CSA
  B651-18;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with an entry door that is identified by
  the International Symbol of Access and meets the requirements set out
  in clause 5.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with at least two call buttons or two
  other devices for signalling an emergency that meet the requirements
  of paragraph 165(1)(c); and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} be adjacent to a wheelchair-accessible washroom or be
  near a wheelchair-accessible washroom on the same deck.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible cabin

**[[167]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-167 .sectionLabel}** At least five percent
of the total number of cabins on the ferry must be wheelchair-accessible
and at least two of the wheelchair-accessible cabins must be adjacent to
each other.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard shower facility

**[[168]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-168 .sectionLabel}** A shower facility on a
ferry, other than a wheelchair-accessible shower facility, must meet the
requirements set out in clause 6.5 of CSA B651-18, except clauses
6.5.5.1, 6.5.5.2 and 6.5.5.3(c)(i) and except that the reference to
"clause 4.2" in clause 6.2.3.3(a) must be read as excluding clause
4.2.2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible shower facility

**[[169]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-169 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
shower facility on a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 6.5 of CSA
  B651-18 except clauses 6.5.5.1 and 6.5.5.2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an interior clear area of at least 900 mm by
  1,500 mm;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a clear floor area in front of the shower
  entrance of at least 900 mm by 1,500 mm, with the 1,500 mm dimension
  parallel to the shower entrance; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be located on the same deck as a
  wheelchair-accessible cabin.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible shower
facility

**[[170]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-170 .sectionLabel}** A ferry that has a
standard shower facility must also have at least one
wheelchair-accessible shower facility.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interior door and doorway

- **[[171]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-171 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interior door and doorway on a ferry must meet the requirements set
  out in clauses 5.2.1 and 5.2.6 to 5.2.8 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An interior door must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an automatic or semi-automatic door,
    incorporate devices that prevent the door from closing while a
    person is in the doorway and that open and close the door
    automatically on detecting the movement of a person toward or away
    from the door;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be marked with visual indicators if 75% of the
    door's surface is composed of a transparent material;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door to an enclosed space in an
    area that has no other exit doors, have a safety mechanism that is
    not a deadbolt and is capable of being manipulated from the outside;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a door that is intended for use by a
    person using a mobility aid, meet the requirements set out in
    clauses 4.2.2 and 5.2.2 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exterior door

**[[172]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-172 .sectionLabel}** An exterior door on a
ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.2.1 of CSA
  B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have a handrail

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on each side of the door, fitted internally as
    close as is feasible to the outer wall of the ferry, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on only one side of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a doorway that is fitted with a device for
      boarding or disembarking, such as a lift, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a trap door to which stairs are attached; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the door is intended to be used by a person using
  a mobility aid, meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.2.2(a)
  and (b) of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Handrails

**[[173]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-173 .sectionLabel}** Handrails on a ferry
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.3.1 and 5.3.2 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Floors

**[[174]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-174 .sectionLabel}** The floors of a ferry
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.1 to 4.3.3 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile attention indicator surfaces

**[[175]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-175 .sectionLabel}** Tactile attention
indicator surfaces on a ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be located at the top of stairs and at any other
  location where there is an unprotected drop-off edge and a change in
  elevation greater than 250 mm or where the ratio of the slope to
  surface is steeper than 1:3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.5.2(a)
  and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating controls

**[[176]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-176 .sectionLabel}** Operating controls on a
ferry must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.2.1, 4.2.3
  to 4.2.6 and 4.2.8 of CSA B651-18; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are intended to be used by a person using a
  wheelchair, meet the requirements set out in clause 4.2.2 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

- **[[177]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-177
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Signage on a ferry, other than a
  menu display board referred to in section 158, must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate any services, facilities, amenities or
    equipment that are offered and of the direction to follow in order
    to access them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
    have a glare-free surface;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of electronic signage, meet the
    requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to 4.5.7 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Electronic signage must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have letters, numbers, symbols and pictographs that
    slowly scroll across the screen and are colour-contrasted with their
    background but are not in red on a black background; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
    4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated relief areas

- **[[178]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-178 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  ferry on which passengers are travelling for four consecutive hours or
  more must have a designated area for service dogs to relieve
  themselves that a person with a disability may reach by means of a
  path of travel that is accessible to persons with disabilities and
  that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} identified by tactile and Braille signage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cleaned and maintained on a regular basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ferry must have signage that indicates the
  direction to follow in order to access a designated relief area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elevator

**[[179]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-179 .sectionLabel}** A ferry that has more
than one deck must have at least one elevator that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provides service from a vehicle deck to all passenger
  decks, except to a deck that does not have a housing to protect the
  elevator from the weather; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements set out in clause 5.6.1 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-operational elevator

**[[180]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-180 .sectionLabel}** Each elevator on a
ferry must have signage that warns passengers that the elevator may not
be operational when the ferry's roll exceeds the safe operating criteria
established by the manufacturer of the elevator.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alarm system

**[[181]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-181 .sectionLabel}** If a ferry is equipped
with an alarm system, including in a cabin, the alarm system must
provide both a visual alarm and an audible alarm. A visual alarm must
meet the requirements set out in clause 5.7.1 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

- **[[182]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-182 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  ferry and any related facilities, including any amenities and
  equipment used in them, that are subject to any requirements of this
  Division must be in good working order and properly maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any facilities, amenities or equipment that are
  referred to in subsection (1) are not in good working order, they must
  be repaired as soon as possible and, until they are repaired, measures
  must be taken that will result in a substantially equivalent or
  greater level of accessibility for persons with disabilities.

### [DIVISION 4]{.HLabel2}[Bus Carriers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1176944 .Subheading}

#### [Application]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176945 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bus carriers

**[[183]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-183 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified, this Division applies to every bus carrier that provides a
service for the transportation of passengers between two or more
provinces and from a point of origin in Canada to a point of destination
in a foreign country.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- certain buses

**[[184]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-184 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply to a bus carrier in respect of any of the following buses:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any bus that has a maximum carrying capacity of not
  more than 39 passengers;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any bus while it is being used in a charter service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing buses

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  the provisions of this Division, except sections 199 and 205, do not
  apply to a pre-existing bus.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification --- portable equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 188 and 189 apply to a pre-existing bus with
  respect to any lift, ramp or bridge plate that is portable and not
  integrated with the pre-existing bus.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modifications

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a carrier makes a modification --- other than a
  modification to a mechanical, electrical or plumbing system or a
  modification carried out for an aesthetic purpose or for maintenance
  or repair --- to any amenity or equipment that is used in a
  pre-existing bus, the carrier must ensure that the modified amenity or
  equipment meets the requirements of this Division, except in the
  following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dimensions of the bus or of the amenity or
    equipment are unalterable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the structural integrity or safe operation of the
    bus or of the amenity or equipment would be materially affected; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the principal purpose of the amenity or equipment
    would be fundamentally altered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[pre-existing
  bus]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[pre-existing
  bus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a bus that was

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier before the day
    on which this section comes into force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} purchased or leased by the carrier on or after the
    day on which this section comes into force, if the carrier submitted
    the call for tenders in respect of that bus before that day.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- temporary leases

**[[186]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-186 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply in respect of a bus that is leased by a carrier on a short-term
basis in order to respond to an emergency or to replace a bus that
requires repairs due to an unforeseen mechanical failure.

#### [Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1176966 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of carrier

**[[187]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-187 .sectionLabel}** A carrier must ensure
that any bus that it owns, operates or leases and any related
facilities, including any amenities and equipment used in them, meet the
requirements set out in this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lift

**[[188]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-188 .sectionLabel}** A lift that is used to
assist a person with a disability, including a person in a mobility aid,
to board or disembark from a bus must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that are located on both sides of the
  lift and a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 272 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ramp or bridge plate

**[[189]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-189 .sectionLabel}** A ramp or bridge plate
that is used to assist a person with a disability, including a person in
a mobility aid, to board or disembark from a bus must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a contrasting colour strip that runs the full
  width of its bottom edge;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have raised edges to prevent a mobility aid from
  rolling off the edge of the ramp;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have a slip-resistant surface; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be capable of supporting a minimum weight of 272 kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No level boarding

**[[190]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-190 .sectionLabel}** If a bus terminal does
not permit level boarding of a bus and there is no lift, ramp or bridge
plate available at the bus terminal, the bus must be equipped with a
lift, ramp or bridge plate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other stops

**[[191]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-191 .sectionLabel}** If a bus makes a stop
at any place that is not at a terminal and where passengers are
permitted to board and disembark, the bus must be equipped with a lift,
ramp or bridge plate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stairs

**[[192]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-192 .sectionLabel}** Any stairs that are
used to board or disembark from a bus, and any interior stairs on a bus,
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 5.4.1 and
  5.4.2 of CSA B651-18;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have handrails that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on both sides of the stairs,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at a uniform height of 860 mm to 920
    mm, measured vertically from the leading edge of the tread, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clause 5.3.1 of
    CSA B651-18;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have tactile attention indicator surfaces that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.5.2(a)
    and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located at the top of the stairs, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} commence one tread length before the top of the
    stairs, extend the full width of the stairs and measure from 600 mm
    to 650 mm in length; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with lighting that accentuates the steps
  and handrails.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger seats

**[[193]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-193 .sectionLabel}** Passenger seats on a
bus must be equipped with

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} movable armrests on all aisle and middle passenger
  seats;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} handholds on the back of all aisle passenger seats;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if there are call buttons, call buttons that are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} tactilely discernible and colour-contrasted with
    their background, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile row markers

**[[194]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-194 .sectionLabel}** A bus must be equipped
with tactile row markers, in raised characters and Braille, that are
installed, either permanently or temporarily, on the sides of passenger
seats and above the armrests or adjacent to the mechanisms for opening
overhead bins.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Priority seats for persons with
disabilities

**[[195]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-195 .sectionLabel}** The first row of
passenger seats on each side of a bus must be designated as priority
seats for persons with disabilities and there must be signage that
indicates that a person, other than a person with a disability, must
vacate a priority seat if the seat is needed by a person with a
disability.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mobility aid space

**[[196]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-196 .sectionLabel}** A mobility aid space on
a bus must have a clear floor area of at least 1,220 mm by 760 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory mobility aid space

**[[197]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-197 .sectionLabel}** A bus must have at
least two mobility aid spaces.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage of mobility aid

**[[198]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-198 .sectionLabel}** A bus must have a
baggage compartment that allows for the storage of at least two mobility
aids, each of which weighing no more than 227 kg and having a width of
no more than 1,092 mm, a height of not more than 736 mm (with the seat
and steering column collapsed) and a length of not more than 2,260.6 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Passenger safety features cards

**[[199]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-199 .sectionLabel}** A bus that has any
passenger safety features cards available on board must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least two passenger safety features cards that are
  in large print and at least two passenger safety features cards that
  are in Braille; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} at least one electronic device that provides
  passenger safety information in an audio and visual format and permits
  a person with a disability to adjust the volume of the audio output
  and the size of the font.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Window emergency exits

**[[200]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-200 .sectionLabel}** If a bus is equipped
with a window emergency exit, the window emergency exit must be
identified by a contrasting colour strip around the perimeter and by
tactile and Braille signage.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard washroom

**[[201]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-201 .sectionLabel}** A washroom on a bus,
other than a wheelchair-accessible washroom, must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} doors that are equipped with handles, pulls, latches,
  locks and other devices that are operable using minimal force, with
  one hand in a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation
  that does not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the
  wrist ;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} toilets that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are equipped with an automated flush control or a
    flush control that is operable using minimal force, with one hand in
    a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation that does
    not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the wrist, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} have a back support if the toilet has no seat lid;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} faucets that are equipped with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} handles or other controls that are tactilely
    discernible, unless the water temperature is fixed to eliminate the
    risk of scalding,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of non-automated faucets, handles or
    other controls that are operable using minimal force, with one hand
    in a closed-fist position, or by another method of operation that
    does not require tight grasping, pinching or twisting of the wrist,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of automated faucets, a water
    activation motion sensor that turns the water on automatically when
    a hand is below the faucet;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} grab bars that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located on a wall beside the toilet,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are rounded, slip-resistant and free of any sharp
    or abrasive elements,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} have an exterior diameter and a clearance from
    the wall surface to which they are attached that permit them to be
    easily grasped, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} are capable of supporting a minimum weight of
    113.4 kg;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} toilet paper dispensers that are located so as to not
  interfere with the use of the grab bars;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} soap dispensers that are operable using minimal
  force, with one hand in a closed-fist position, or by another method
  of operation that does not require tight grasping, pinching or
  twisting of the wrist; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} at least one call button or other device for
  signalling an emergency that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is colour-contrasted with its background and
    identified by tactile and Braille signage, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is operable using minimal force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[202]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-202 .sectionLabel}** A wheelchair-accessible
washroom on a bus must meet the requirements set out in section 201 and
must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a door or other opening to the washroom that has
  sufficient space in front of it and that is wide enough to allow the
  entry of a person using a wheelchair;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have sufficient space to allow a person, with
  assistance, to transfer to and from a wheelchair and the toilet;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} have sufficient privacy, including with the use of
  retractable curtains or walls, to allow a support person or service
  dog to remain in the washroom with a person using a wheelchair; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} be equipped with faucets that are positioned to be
  easily usable by a person using a wheelchair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mandatory wheelchair-accessible washroom

**[[203]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-203 .sectionLabel}** Every washroom on a bus
must be a wheelchair-accessible washroom, unless the washroom does not
have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a door or other opening to the washroom that has
  sufficient space in front of it and that is wide enough to allow the
  entry of a person using a wheelchair; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sufficient space to allow a person, with assistance,
  to transfer to and from a wheelchair and the toilet.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}On-board entertainment system

**[[204]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-204 .sectionLabel}** An on-board
entertainment system on a bus must be equipped to permit a person with a
disability to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} access entertainment content that offers closed
  captioning and audio description and that is the same entertainment
  content that is offered to the other passengers or, if that is not
  possible, that is comparable entertainment content; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} start, stop and pause the entertainment content, turn
  on and off the closed captioning and audio description functions,
  change the language of choice, control the volume and select channels

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} through an interface that meets the requirements
    for an interface that are set out in the Web Content Accessibility
    Guidelines, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the interface has not been designed to be used
    with the on-board entertainment system, through a tactile template
    overlay affixed to the interface.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing bus

- **[[205]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-205 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pre-existing bus referred to in subsection 185(4) has an on-board
  entertainment system that does not offer closed captioning and audio
  description, the bus must be equipped with personal electronic devices
  in a number that is sufficient to accommodate the number of persons
  with disabilities who have made a request for such a device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personal electronic devices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The personal electronic devices must be pre-loaded
  with --- or the bus must provide wireless streaming of ---
  entertainment content that offers closed captioning and audio
  description and that is the same entertainment content that is offered
  to the other passengers or, if that is not possible, that is
  comparable entertainment content.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grab bars, handholds and stanchions

**[[206]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-206 .sectionLabel}** Grab bars, handholds
and stanchions on a bus must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be sturdy, rounded and free of any sharp or abrasive
  element;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} have an exterior diameter and a clearance from the
  surface to which they are attached that permit them to be easily
  grasped;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with their background;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} have a slip-resistant surface;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be located

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where passengers are required to pay fares or show
    proof of payment,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} close to mobility aid spaces,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in priority seating areas, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} on each side of a door;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} be evenly distributed throughout the bus to assist
  persons with disabilities to safely board and disembark and to sit and
  stand;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} be located so as to not interfere with the turning
  and manoeuvring space that may be required by a person using a
  mobility aid to access a mobility aid space; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if they are located at a door, be within the reach of
  a person standing at ground level and installed in a manner that
  ensures that they are inside the bus when the doors are closed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Floors

**[[207]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-207 .sectionLabel}** The floors of a bus
must meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.1 to 4.3.3 of CSA
B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tactile attention indicator surfaces

**[[208]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-208 .sectionLabel}** Tactile attention
indicator surfaces on a bus must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be located at the top of stairs and at any other
  location where there is an unprotected drop-off edge and a change in
  elevation greater than 250 mm or where the ratio of the slope to
  surface is steeper than 1:3; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.3.5.2(a)
  and (b), 4.3.5.3.1 and 4.3.5.3.4 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Operating controls

**[[209]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-209 .sectionLabel}** Operating controls on a
bus must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.2.1, 4.2.3
  to 4.2.6 and 4.2.8 of CSA B651-18; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if they are intended to be used by a person using a
  wheelchair, meet the requirements set out in clause 4.2.2 of CSA
  B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

- **[[210]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-210
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Signage on a bus must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} indicate any services, facilities, amenities or
    equipment that are offered and of the direction to follow in order
    to access them;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be positioned to avoid shadow areas and glare and
    have a glare-free surface;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be colour-contrasted with its background; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} except in the case of electronic signage, meet the
    requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to 4.5.7 of CSA B651-18.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Electronic signage must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have letters, numbers, symbols and pictographs that
    slowly scroll across the screen and are colour-contrasted with their
    background but are not in red on a black background; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} meet the requirements set out in clauses 4.5.3 to
    4.5.5 of CSA B651-18.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

- **[[211]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-211 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  bus and any related facilities, including any amenities and equipment
  used in them, that are subject to any requirements of this Division
  must be in good working order and properly maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any facilities, amenities or equipment that are
  referred to in subsection (1) are not in good working order, they must
  be repaired as soon as possible and, until they are repaired, measures
  must be taken that will result in a substantially equivalent or
  greater level of accessibility for persons with disabilities.

  to in paragraph 25(1)(b);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a ferry terminal that serves a marine carrier

### [DIVISION 2]{.HLabel2}[Technical Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1177131 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[218]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-218 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
specified, this Division applies to every terminal operator referred to
in section 212 and to every terminal operator who is the owner, operator
or lessee of a port that permits the mooring of cruise ships.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application -- certain areas or
facilities

**[[219]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-219 .sectionLabel}** This Division does not
apply in respect of any area or any facility of a terminal that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not intended for use by the public;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not under the control of the terminal operator; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a commercial establishment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pre-existing terminals

- **[[220]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-220
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), sections
  222, 226, 228 and 230 do not apply to a pre-existing terminal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Modifications

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a terminal operator makes a modification --- other
  than a modification to a mechanical, electrical or plumbing system or
  a modification carried out for an aesthetic purpose or for maintenance
  or repair --- to any amenity or equipment that is used in a
  pre-existing terminal, the terminal operator must ensure that the
  modified amenity or equipment meets the requirements of sections 222,
  226, 228 and 230, except in the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the dimensions of the terminal or of the amenity or
    equipment are unalterable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the structural integrity or safe operation of the
    terminal or of the amenity or equipment would be materially
    affected;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the principal purpose of the amenity or equipment
    would be fundamentally altered; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Heritage Railway Stations Protection
    Act](/eng/acts/H-3.5) or any other Act of Parliament related to
    heritage protection would be contravened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[pre-existing
  terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section, a [[pre-existing
  terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a terminal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the terminal operator became
    the owner, operator or lessee before the day on which this section
    comes into force; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the terminal operator became
    the owner, operator or lessee on or after the day on which this
    section comes into force if the terminal operator submitted the call
    for tenders for the purchase, lease or construction of that terminal
    before the day on which this section comes into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty of terminal operator

**[[221]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-221 .sectionLabel}** A terminal operator
must ensure that any terminal that it owns, operates or leases and any
related facilities, including any amenities or equipment used in them,
meet the requirements set out in this Division.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terminal --- requirements

**[[222]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-222 .sectionLabel}** A terminal must meet
the requirements set out in CSA B651-18, excluding clauses 5.6.2, 6.5.6,
6.6.2.2, 6.6.2.7.1, 6.7.3, 7 and 8.5 and all Annexes, commentary and
figures.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lift, ramp or stairs --- requirements

**[[223]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-223 .sectionLabel}** A lift, ramp or stairs
that are used at a terminal for the boarding or disembarkation of
persons with disabilities must meet the requirements for a lift, ramp or
stairs, as the case may be, that are set out in section 69, section 70
or subsection 71(1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No level boarding --- airports

**[[224]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-224 .sectionLabel}** In the case of an
airport, if the terminal does not permit level boarding of an aircraft,
the terminal must be equipped with a lift, a ramp or portable stairs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Wheelchairs

- **[[225]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-225 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  terminal must have wheelchairs available for use by passengers in a
  number that is sufficient to accommodate the number of persons with
  disabilities who are likely to use them at any one time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A wheelchair that is available for use by passengers
  at a terminal must have

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} footrests and wheel locks; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a wheelchair used for boarding,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} movable armrests and an occupant restraint
      device, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a backrest of a height that permits the person
      using the wheelchair to be safely and easily transferred to and
      from a seat.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seats

**[[226]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-226 .sectionLabel}** A terminal must have

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} seats that are located along paths of travel at
  regular intervals of approximately 30 m; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in every boarding area, designated priority seats for
  persons with disabilities that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} are located so as to be close to members of
    personnel who are stationed at the boarding gate,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are located so as to permit them to view screens
    or other boards that display information relating to departures or
    gate or track assignments, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are marked with signage that specifies that
    persons with disabilities have priority access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated relief area

- **[[227]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-227 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated area for service dogs to relieve themselves must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be identified by tactile and Braille signage; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} be cleaned and maintained on a regular basis.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The terminal must have signage that indicates the
  direction to follow in order to access a designated relief area for
  service dogs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated relief area outside terminal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A terminal must have a designated area for service
  dogs to relieve themselves that is located outside of the terminal and
  that a person with a disability may reach from the terminal by means
  of a path of travel that is accessible to persons with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct access from restricted area

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A terminal must have a designated area for service
  dogs to relieve themselves that a person with a disability may reach,
  from the area of the terminal into which access is strictly
  controlled, by means of a path of travel that is accessible to persons
  with disabilities and that does not require the person to exit and
  re-enter that area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Light-rail trains and shuttle buses

**[[228]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-228 .sectionLabel}** Sections 90 to 93, 96,
102, 109, 127 to 130, 190, 195, 197 and 206 apply, with any
modifications that the circumstances require, in respect of any
light-rail train and any shuttle bus that operates between any
facilities of a terminal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obstruction due to repairs or maintenance

**[[229]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-229 .sectionLabel}** If there is any object
that is obstructing a path of travel inside or outside of a terminal
because of repairs or maintenance, it must be detectable by a person
using a cane.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-accessible path of travel

**[[230]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-230 .sectionLabel}** If a path of travel
inside or outside of a terminal is not accessible to a person with a
disability, including because there are stairs or escalators, there must
be an alternative path of travel that is accessible to persons with
disabilities and that allows them to access the desired service or reach
the desired destination.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance

- **[[231]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-231 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  terminal or any related facility that is subject to any requirement
  under this Division --- including a shuttle bus or a light-rail train
  that operates between any facilities of a terminal --- must be in good
  working order and properly maintained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Repairs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If any facilities that are referred to in subsection
  (1), including any amenities or equipment used in those facilities,
  are not in good working order, they must be repaired as soon as
  possible and, until they are repaired, measures must be taken that
  will result in a substantially equivalent or greater level of
  accessibility for persons with disabilities.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Requirements Applicable to CATSA and CBSA]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1177198 .Part}

### [Security Screening]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1177199 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Services to assist persons with
disabilities

**[[232]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-232 .sectionLabel}** During the security
screening process, CATSA must, on the request of a person with a
disability, provide the following services without delay:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} expediting the security screening process by
  directing the person, and any support person travelling with them, to
  the front of a line or to any other line that is intended to expedite
  the security screening process;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permitting a representative of an air carrier or an
  individual with a security pass issued by an air carrier or the
  airport to accompany the person through the security screening
  checkpoint;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person with proceeding through the
  steps of the security screening process, including by providing verbal
  or visual cues or additional instructions; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting the person with the placement of carry-on
  baggage and personal items on a screening belt and with their
  retrieval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assistive device, support person or
service dog

- **[[233]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-233
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} CATSA must, when screening a person
  with a disability who uses an assistive device or who is travelling
  with a support person or a service dog, make every reasonable effort
  to carry out the screening simultaneously with the screening of the
  person's assistive device, support person or service dog, as the case
  may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate screening of assistive device

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If CATSA removes an assistive device from a person
  with a disability for a separate screening, CATSA must return the
  assistive device to the person without delay after it has been
  screened.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate screening of mobility aid

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If CATSA removes an assistive device that is a
  mobility aid from a person with a disability for a separate screening,
  CATSA must offer a chair to the person while the mobility aid is being
  screened.
