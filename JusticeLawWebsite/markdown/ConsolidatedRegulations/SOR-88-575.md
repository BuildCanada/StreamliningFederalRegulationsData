## Commonwealth of Learning, an Agency, Privileges and Immunities Order, 1988 {#commonwealth-of-learning-an-agency-privileges-and-immunities-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-575
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1988-11-01

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities in Canada of the
Commonwealth of Learning, an Agency

P.C. 1988-2473 1988-10-31
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section
3[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBAA}
of the Privileges and Immunities (International Organizations) Act, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed Order respecting the privileges and
immunities in Canada of the Commonwealth of Learning, an Agency.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914084 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Commonwealth of Learning, an Agency, Privileges and Immunities
Order, 1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-575).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914086 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commonwealth of Learning;
    ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agency Members]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency Members]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the states that are
    members of the Commonwealth; ([membres de l'Agence]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    Agency and the Government of Canada with respect to the headquarters
    of the Agency; ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[representatives]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[representatives]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means representatives of
    states and governments that are members of the Agency.
    ([représentants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities Granted to the Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914093 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Agency shall have the
legal capacities of a body corporate.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding any other
section of this Order, the Agency shall have in Canada the privileges
and immunities set forth in Articles II and III of the Convention, to
the extent that those privileges and immunities are contained in the
Agreement.

## [Privileges and Immunities Granted to Representatives]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914096 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Representatives shall have in Canada
  the privileges and immunities set forth in Article IV of the
  Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The immunity from legal process in respect of words
  spoken or written and all other acts done by representatives in
  discharging their duties shall continue to be accorded,
  notwithstanding that the persons concerned are no longer
  representatives, to the extent that this immunity is specified in
  paragraph III(1)(a) of the Agreement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the incidence of any form of taxation depends
  upon residence, periods during which representatives are present in
  Canada for the discharge of their duties shall not be considered as
  periods of residence.

## [Privileges and Immunities Granted to Officials]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914101 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The President, the
Vice-President and all Assistant Vice-Presidents of the Agency shall
have in Canada the privileges and immunities set forth in Article V of
the Convention to the extent that these are set forth in the Agreement,
as applicable to senior officials of the Agency and, without restricting
any other privileges or immunities accorded under this section, the
President and Vice-President of the Agency shall be accorded in respect
of themselves, their spouses and minor children the privileges and
immunities, exemptions and facilities accorded to diplomatic envoys in
accordance with international law.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Officials of the Agency
shall have in Canada the privileges and immunities set forth in Article
V of the Convention to the extent that theses are set forth in the
Agreement.

## [Privileges and Immunities Granted to Experts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-914104 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Experts performing missions
for the Agency shall have in Canada, to such extent as may be required
for the exercise of their functions in Canada in relation to the Agency,
the privileges and immunities set forth in Article VI of the Convention.
