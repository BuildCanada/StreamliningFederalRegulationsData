## Order Declaring that the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired Generation of Electricity Regulations Do Not Apply in Nova Scotia {#order-declaring-that-the-reduction-of-carbon-dioxide-emissions-from-coal-fired-generation-of-electricity-regulations-do-not-apply-in-nova-scotia .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-265
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2014-11-21

Order Declaring that the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide Emissions from
Coal-fired Generation of Electricity Regulations Do Not Apply in Nova
Scotia
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2){#fn_a2-ID0EICCA} of the [Canadian
Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EFCCA}, the Minister of the
Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 28, 2014, a copy of
the proposed Order Declaring that the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide
Emissions from Coal-fired Generation of Electricity Regulations Do Not
Apply in Nova Scotia, substantially in the annexed form, and persons
were given an opportunity to file comments with respect to the proposed
Order or to file a notice of objection requesting that a board of review
be established and stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2-ID0EICCA){#fn_a2}S.C.
  2004, c. 15, s. 31
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2-ID0EFCCA){#fn_b2}S.C.
  1999, c. 33
  :::

And whereas, on May 26, 2014, the Minister of the Environment and the
Government of Nova Scotia have entered into a written agreement referred
to in subsection 10(3) of that Act to the effect that there are in force
by or under the laws of Nova Scotia

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are equivalent to the provisions of
  the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired Generation
  of Electricity Regulations made under subsection 93(1) of that Act,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions that are similar to sections 17 to 20 of
  that Act for the investigation of alleged offences under environmental
  legislation of Nova Scotia;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to
subsection 10(3) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EDAAA}, makes the annexed Order
Declaring that the Reduction of Carbon Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired
Generation of Electricity Regulations Do Not Apply in Nova Scotia.
:::

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807345 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Reduction of Carbon
Dioxide Emissions from Coal-fired Generation of Electricity Regulations
do not apply in Nova Scotia.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807348 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}July 1, 2015

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on July 1, 2015.
