## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458793 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Feeds
      Act](/eng/acts/F-9). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Food Inspection
      Agency established by section 3 of the [Canadian Food Inspection
      Agency Act](/eng/acts/C-16.5). ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[biotechnology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[biotechnology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the application of
      science and engineering to the direct or indirect use of living
      organisms or parts or products of living organisms in their
      natural or modified forms. ([biotechnologie]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single ingredient feed or
      water used to facilitate the handling of a feed and its
      incorporation into ingredient market formulations, premixes, feed
      or water. The use of a carrier does not alter the feed's intended
      effect and purpose. ([support]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cattle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means animals of the species [Bos
      taurus]{.otherLang lang="la"} or [Bos indicus]{.otherLang
      lang="la"}. ([bœuf]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[caution statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[caution statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a statement
      concerning animal health hazards or safe product handling or
      storage. ([précaution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[complete feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[complete feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed that,
      when used for the species and class of livestock stated on the
      label, provides all the nutrients necessary for the maintenance of
      life or for promoting production, except water and, in the case of
      ruminants and horses, roughage. ([aliment
      complet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[contaminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[contaminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any micro-organism,
      chemical substance, extraneous material or other substance that
      may present a risk of harm to human or animal health or the
      environment, including any substance that is not permitted under
      these Regulations or that does not comply with any limits or
      levels provided under these
      Regulations. ([contaminant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[custom medicated feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[custom medicated feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medicated
      feed that is manufactured in accordance with a veterinary
      prescription that prescribes a medicating ingredient

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is not set out in the Compendium of
        Medicating Ingredient Brochures for the species of livestock for
        which the feed is intended; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is of a brand, level or compatibility or
        that has a caution statement or warning statement that differs
        from that set out in the Compendium of Medicating Ingredient
        Brochures in respect of that particular ingredient, for the
        species of livestock for which the feed is intended. ([aliment
        médicamenté sur mesure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[customer formula feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[customer formula feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed
      that is manufactured by a feed manufacturer

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for feeding their livestock; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a written order that is
        signed by a purchaser if

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the order states the name of each single
          ingredient feed, medicating ingredient set out in the
          Compendium of Medicating Ingredient Brochures or type of
          product set out in the Compendium of Non-Feed Products, and
          their respective amounts, to be used in the manufacture of
          that feed or to be added to other mixed feeds that conform to
          the standards prescribed in these Regulations,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the feed does not contain a [[pest control
          product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1)
          of the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), and

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the feed is not intended for
          resale. ([aliment préparé selon la formule du
          client]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disposed of in a safe manner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disposed of in a safe manner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      disposed of, other than by human or animal consumption, in a
      manner that does not present a risk of harm to human or animal
      health or the environment. ([éliminer de façon
      sécuritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[facilitating agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[facilitating agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a single
      ingredient feed in an amount not exceeding the amount set out in
      the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table or a specialty feed used
      according to its directions for use that is added to another feed
      for the purpose of improving or conserving some or all of the
      nutrient content or of aiding or improving the manufacturing or
      handling properties of that other feed. The use of a facilitating
      agent does not alter the purpose of any feed into which it has
      been mixed. ([substance auxiliaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[holder of the approval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[holder of the approval]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      has been provided with a written notice under subsection 9(1)
      confirming that approval of a feed that is manufactured by or for
      a government, academic or private research establishment for
      research or experimental purposes has been given. ([titulaire de
      l'approbation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[identification code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any combination
      of letters or figures or letters and figures by which a lot of
      feed can be traced during manufacture and distribution. ([code
      d'identification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[medicated feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medicated feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed that
      contains a medicating ingredient. ([aliment
      médicamenté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[medicating ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[medicating ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drug for
      which a drug identification number is assigned under subsection
      C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or for which a
      letter of authorization for use in emergency treatment has been
      issued to a veterinarian under subsection C.08.010(1) of those
      Regulations and that is intended

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for use in the prevention or treatment of
        disease in livestock; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to affect the structure or any function of the
        body of the livestock. ([substance médicatrice]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[micro-ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[micro-ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vitamin,
      micro-mineral, flavour ingredient, medicating ingredient and any
      type of product set out in the Compendium of Non-Feed Products
      that is required in small amounts typically measured in
      milligrams, micrograms, international units or parts per
      million. ([micro-ingrédient]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mineral feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mineral feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed that is
      composed predominantly of mineral ingredients. ([aliment
      minéral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[mixed feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mixed feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a feed that contains at
      least two single ingredient feeds. ([aliment
      mélangé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[novel feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[novel feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a feed that is an
      organism, a part of an organism or a product of an organism, or
      that consists of parts or products of an organism, and that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not listed in the Canadian Feed Ingredients
        Table; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a novel trait. ([aliment
        nouveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[novel product of biotechnology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[novel product of biotechnology]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      product that is not listed in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table
      and that is a product of biotechnology. ([nouveau produit de la
      biotechnologie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[novel trait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[novel trait]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a feed that is
      an organism, a part of an organism or a product of an organism, or
      that consists of parts or products of an organism, means a
      characteristic of the feed that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been intentionally selected, created or
        introduced into the feed through a specific genetic change; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} based on valid scientific rationale, is not
        substantially equivalent, in terms of its specific use and
        safety for human or animal health or the environment, to any
        characteristic of a similar feed that is listed in the Canadian
        Feed Ingredients Table. ([caractère nouveau]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[novel viable organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[novel viable organism]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organism
      that is alive or capable of sustaining life and that is not listed
      in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table. ([nouvel organisme
      viable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[percentage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[%]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm},
      with respect to any product, means the percentage by mass of the
      product. ([pourcentage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
      [%]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[poultry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[poultry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means chickens, turkeys, ducks and
      geese. ([volaille]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[premix]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[premix]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed that provides a
      significant source of nutrients and is a uniform mixture that may
      contain micro-ingredients, macro-minerals, facilitating agents,
      specialty feeds and carriers, and that is intended to be further
      diluted and mixed with other feeds to produce a mineral feed,
      another premix, a supplement or complete
      feed. ([prémélange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[President]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the President of the
      Agency. ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of
      the label applied to all or part of any side or surface of a feed
      or feed package that is displayed or visible under normal or
      customary conditions of sale or use. ([espace
      principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes the use of a feed for
      the purpose of manufacturing another feed or the mixing of a feed
      with another feed. ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[registrant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[registrant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has been
      issued a registration certificate under section 11. ([titulaire de
      l'enregistrement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ruminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ruminant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal of the suborder
      [Ruminatiae]{.otherLang lang="la"} and includes an animal of the
      family [Camelidae]{.otherLang
      lang="la"}. ([ruminant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[single ingredient feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[single ingredient feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any substance
      or mixture of substances that has been approved for feeding to
      livestock and that is listed in the Canadian Feed Ingredients
      Table. ([aliment à ingrédient unique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specialty feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specialty feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed the
      purpose of which is to improve or facilitate

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the preservation of any feed with which it is
        mixed prior to feeding or during storage;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the characteristics, qualities, flowability,
        pelleting or tracing of any feed with which it is mixed;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ingredient dispersion or distribution in any
        feed with which it is mixed;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} ingestion, acceptability, nutrient utilization
        or digestion by livestock of any feed with which it is mixed;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quality or availability of nutrients in any
        feed with which it is mixed;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the absorption of nutrients by livestock of any
        feed that is fed to it;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the modification of characteristics of food of
        animal origin; or

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the mitigation of environmental impacts related
        to livestock production. ([aliment spécialisé]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

  [[supplement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[supplement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixed feed that
      provides a significant source of nutrients in order to improve the
      nutritional value of the total diet and that is intended to be

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fed undiluted in addition to other feeds;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} offered on a free-choice basis with other feeds
        that are available separately; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} further diluted and mixed to produce the total
        diet. ([supplément]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[total diet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[total diet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ration consisting of
      all feeds, including roughage, that are offered daily to
      livestock. ([ration totale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[trace mineral salt feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[trace mineral salt feed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mineral
      feed that is composed of salt and trace minerals. ([complément
      d'oligo-éléments et de sel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[veterinary prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[veterinary prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order
      prescribing a medicated feed issued by a veterinarian licensed to
      practice in the province in which the feed is to be fed to the
      livestock to be treated. ([ordonnance du
      vétérinaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[warning statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[warning statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a statement
      concerning human health hazards. ([mise en garde]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458887 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions from Act and Regulations

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following feeds are exempt from the application of the Act and these
  Regulations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a feed that contains a new drug, the sale of which
    is permitted by section C.08.005 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) for the purpose of
    clinical testing or by section C.08.013 of those Regulations for the
    purpose of conducting experimental studies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a feed, other than a novel feed, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is grown on a farm, it is sold by the grower,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it does not contain any substance that presents
      a risk of harm to human or animal health or the environment,
      including any deleterious substance listed in the List of
      Prescribed Deleterious Substances;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a complete feed, other than a novel feed, that is
    packaged in containers having a net mass of not more than 10 kg and
    that is intended for feeding to livestock, other than livestock
    intended for human consumption as food;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a feed, other than a novel feed, that is in the
    form of samples having a net mass of not more than 1 kg and that is,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} intended for the purpose of marketing promotion
      at exhibits, conferences or trade shows, if the samples are
      labelled "Not for Sale / [Non destiné à la vente]{.otherLang
      lang="fr"}" and are disposed of in a safe manner or are exported,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} intended for the purpose of testing at a
      laboratory in Canada, if the samples are labelled as such and are
      disposed of in a safe manner or are exported;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a feed, other than a novel feed, that is imported
    with imported livestock, if that feed is consumed only by that
    livestock while the livestock is temporarily in Canada and any
    remaining feed accompanies the livestock when the livestock is
    subsequently exported or is disposed of in a safe manner;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a feed, other than a novel feed, that is imported
    and that is intended only for consumption by imported livestock
    while the livestock is temporarily in Canada, but does not accompany
    that livestock when the livestock is imported, if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the importer has provided to the Minister, before
      the feed is imported, the following information in respect of the
      feed:

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its name,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a list of its ingredients,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the address where it will be sent,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the species and number of livestock for which
        it is intended,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the quantity required to be sent to the
        imported livestock,

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it is to be imported and its
        port of entry into Canada,

      - [(G)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the livestock for which it is
        intended is to be imported and the livestock's port of entry
        into Canada, and

      - [(H)]{.lawlabel} the date on which any remaining feed is to be
        exported and its port of exit from Canada or the date on which
        it is to be disposed of in a safe manner,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any remaining feed is exported or disposed of in
      a safe manner, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister confirms in a written notice
      provided to the importer that the information and documents have
      been received and that the feed is exempt from the Act and these
      Regulations; and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the establishment has and implements a plan in

## [Approval and Registration of Feeds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1458929 .Part}

### [Feeds to Be Approved or Registered]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1458930 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feeds that must be approved

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act, the following feeds must be
  approved:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any feed that has a novel trait;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any feed, other than a mixed feed, that is not
    listed in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any single ingredient feed that is listed in the
    Canadian Feed Ingredients Table and whose description differs from
    its description in that Table, including with respect to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its purpose,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its composition, including any hazard inherent
      in the feed, its structure, its nutritional quality or its
      physiological effects,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its manufacturing process, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the species or class of livestock for which it
      is intended and instructions on how it is to be used; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any feed that is manufactured by or for a
    government, academic or private research establishment for research
    or experimental purposes, other than a feed that is exempted under
    paragraph 3(1)(g).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any feed that is not referred to in subsection (1) is
  exempt from approval.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feeds that must be registered

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), all feeds must be registered.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from registration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following feeds are exempt from registration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any mixed feed, other than a specialty feed or
    custom medicated feed, that is manufactured in Canada and that is
    not

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designed to be administered in water for drinking
      by livestock,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a free-choice supplement, free-choice mineral
      feed or free-choice trace mineral salt feed that contains one or
      more medicating ingredients, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a feed that contains a [[pest control
      product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      the [Pest Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any mixed feed, other than a specialty feed or
    custom medicated feed, that is imported for sale and for which a
    licence has been issued under subsection 5.2(1) of the Act, and that
    is not referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any specialty feed that is listed in column 1 of
    Table 3 of the Tables of Permissible Claims for Feed Labels that
    meets the conditions set out in columns 3 and 4 of that Table for
    the approved claim set out in column 2;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any custom medicated feed manufactured in Canada if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the sale of such feed is authorized under section
      C.08.012 of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of feed manufactured does not exceed
      the amount that would normally be consumed by the number of
      animals prescribed to receive the feed during the prescribed
      period of medication,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the veterinary prescription contains
      information respecting

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the veterinary prescription
        is issued,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person for whom the
        feed is to be manufactured and of the person by whom it is
        intended to be used,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the name and level of each medicating
        ingredient in the feed,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the type and amount of feed to be manufactured,

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the number, species, production type and age or
        weight of the livestock for which the feed is intended,

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} any special manufacturing instructions,
        including any necessary facility clean-up warnings,

      - [(G)]{.lawlabel} directions for use of the feed, including
        feeding instructions and the period of medication during which
        it is to be fed to the livestock, and

      - [(H)]{.lawlabel} caution statements and warning statements, if
        applicable, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the veterinary prescription is in the
      possession of the manufacturer of the feed prior to the delivery
      of the feed; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any feed that is listed in Part 1 of the Canadian
    Feed Ingredients Table.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The exemption of the feeds referred to in
  subsection (2) applies only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mixed feed, the guarantees shown
    on the label are set out in the Tables of Nutrient Guarantees and
    Conditions for Feed Labels and, in the case of a single ingredient
    feed, in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the claims shown on the label are set out in the
    Tables of Permissible Claims for Feed Labels; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any information shown on the label of the feed does
    not appear in a language other than English or French.

### [Applications and Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1458974 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for approval or registration

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for approval or registration of a feed must be made to the
  Minister in a form approved by the President.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Elements required

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (4) and (5), the application
  must be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} three copies of a proposed label to be used with
    the feed if the application is submitted by mail, or one copy of
    such label if the application is submitted electronically;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the feed that complies with sections 50
    and 51, as well as any other name or registration or reference
    number used to identify the feed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the feed, including a list of
    ingredients;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the intended purpose of the feed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} directions for use of the feed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each location where the
    feed is manufactured;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} specifications, concerning, among other things,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the usefulness, purity and quality of the feed
      and the homogeneity of the mix, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any component, the amount of which must be
      guaranteed in the feed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the identification and description of any
    contaminant in the feed, as well as the methodology and analysis for
    the detection of the amounts of those contaminants;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} reports of any analysis conducted on representative
    samples drawn from three different and recent lots of the feed, to
    support any information referred to in paragraphs (g) and (h);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of the production methods, including
    details of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the formulae used in manufacturing the feed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the steps of the feed manufacturing process and
      any variation in the process resulting from the source of any
      ingredient or additive in the feed or the location where it is
      manufactured,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type and capacity of the equipment used in
      manufacturing the feed, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} quality control procedures to assure mix
      uniformity and to prevent contamination of subsequent lots of feed
      manufactured in the same place;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} evidence, including the results of any scientific
    investigation,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating the safety of the feed for the
      species of livestock for which it is intended, as well as other
      species of livestock that may be exposed to it, and for humans and
      the environment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating the efficacy of the feed when used
      for its intended purposes and according to its directions for use,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating the stability of the feed under
      normal storage conditions,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} demonstrating that any guarantee shown on the
      label, other than those set out in the Tables of Nutrient
      Guarantees and Conditions for Feed Labels, conveys useful
      information to the purchaser of the feed, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} supporting any claim on the label of the feed
      that is not set out in the Tables of Permissible Claims for Feed
      Labels;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} scientific analysis that shows whether there are
    significant changes in the chemical, biological or physical
    composition of livestock products when the feed is used; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} a summary of all the elements provided in support
    of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information --- novel trait

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a feed with a novel trait, an
  application for approval must be accompanied by the following
  additional information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identification and characterization of the
    novel trait and, if the novel trait is introduced from another
    species, details of the host and donor organisms and the methods of
    introduction of the novel trait into the feed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identification and characterization of the feed
    with a novel trait resulting from the introduction of the novel
    trait, including details relating to expression of the novel trait
    and the stability of the introduction of the novel trait into the
    feed, as well as a comparison of the characteristics of the feed
    with those of any comparable feed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information, including the results of any
    scientific investigation and test data in respect of the novel trait
    that are relevant in identifying whether the feed presents a risk of
    harm to human or animal health or the environment, that is in the
    applicant's possession or to which the applicant ought reasonably to
    have access; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of any Canadian government agency, of a
    government of a foreign state or of a subdivision of a foreign state
    or of an international organization, or association, of states that
    has been provided with information in respect of the feed with a
    novel trait and the purpose for which the information was provided.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sample

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may require that a sample of the feed be
  provided in support of an application referred to under subsection (2)
  or (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- research or
  experimental purposes

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a feed that is manufactured by or for
  a government, academic or private research establishment for research
  or experimental purposes, an application for approval referred to in
  subsection (1) must be accompanied by the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the feed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the feed, including a list of
    ingredients;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the intended purpose of the feed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the species of livestock for which it is intended
    and the number of livestock required to conduct the research;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of feed required to conduct the
    research;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the research protocol, including a statement of the
    purpose, objectives and duration of the research and a justification
    of the quantity of feed and number of animals required;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the establishment where the
    research will take place, including the name and contact information
    of the person supervising the research;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the establishment has a written plan that
    provides that any remaining feed and livestock products produced
    from the feed must be disposed of in a safe manner,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the precautions and confinement
      measures to be taken with respect to the feed and livestock
      products,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a monitoring plan to be applied for the duration
      of the project, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a contingency plan in case of accidental
      interaction of the feed or livestock products with any other feed
      or livestock products that are not the subject of the research;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the establishment does not have a written plan
    that complies with paragraph (h), the information referred to in
    paragraphs (2)(g) to (j), subparagraph (2)(k)(i) and paragraph
    (2)(l);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} if it is an imported feed, the date of its
    importation and its port of entry into Canada; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a summary of all the elements provided in support
    of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Scientific investigation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If any scientific investigation has been conducted
  under paragraphs (2)(k) or (3)(c), the applicant must provide the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research methods used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the statistical methods used to analyze the results
    of the investigation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conditions under which the investigation was
    conducted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quality control and quality assurance measures
    taken in conducting the research and analysis.

### [Evaluation and Decision of Minister]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459034 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval or registration of feed

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Once an
  application for approval or registration of a feed under subsection
  7(1) is evaluated, the Minister must approve or register the feed if
  the following requirements are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the elements referred to in subsections 7(2) to
    (6), as applicable, have been provided to the Minister;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted to the Minister a label
    that complies with the requirements of these Regulations; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the feed is found to be in compliance with the Act
    and these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation of risk

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), the Minister
  must evaluate the risk of harm presented by the feed to human or
  animal health or the environment, including whether the feed is toxic
  or capable of becoming toxic.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxic feed

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A feed is toxic or capable of becoming toxic if it
  enters or may enter the environment in a quantity or concentration or
  under conditions that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have or may have a harmful effect on the
    environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} constitute or may constitute a danger to the
    environment on which human or animal life depends; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} constitute or may constitute a danger in Canada to
    human or animal life or health.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of refusal

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the requirements set out in subsection (1) are not
  met, the Minister must notify the applicant in writing of the refusal
  to approve or register the feed and provide the reasons for the
  refusal.

### [Approval --- Listing and Cancellation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459050 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of approval

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listing in Canadian Feed Ingredients
  Table

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is approved by the Minister, other than a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the feed must be listed in the Canadian Feed

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the feed is already listed in that Table, it

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is approved and listed in Part 2 of the
  Canadian Feed Ingredients Table, it must be registered under section
  8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (5),
  the Minister may cancel the approval of a feed that is manufactured by
  or for a government, academic or private research establishment for
  research or experimental purposes if there are reasonable grounds to
  believe that, in respect of that feed, there has been a contravention
  of the Act or these Regulations.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In deciding whether to cancel an approval, the
  Minister must take into account the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether cancellation of the approval is necessary
    in order to respond to a risk of harm to human or animal health or
    the environment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether cancellation of the approval is unnecessary
    because corrective action has been taken or is expected to be taken
    in a timely manner without risk of harm to human or animal health or
    the environment; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether there have been contraventions of the Act
    or these Regulations in the past in respect of the feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for cancellation

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may cancel an approval only if a written
  notice has been provided to the holder of the approval that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sets out the grounds for the cancellation and the
    period within which corrective action must be taken in order to
    avoid the cancellation of the approval; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} specifies that the holder of the approval must,
    within 30 calendar days after the date of mailing of the notice,
    notify the Minister in writing that they wish to have an opportunity
    to be heard respecting the cancellation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of hearing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 calendar days after the day on which the
  Minister receives the notice that the holder of the approval wishes to
  have an opportunity to be heard, the Minister must notify the holder
  of the approval in writing of the time and place of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for cancellation

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not cancel the approval unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the approval has been provided with
    an opportunity to be heard in respect of the proposed cancellation;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder of the approval has failed to take
    corrective action in respect of the feed within the period set out
    by the Minister in the notice provided under paragraph (3)(a) or
    within any longer period that is granted by the Minister at the
    hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must notify the holder of the approval
  in writing of the cancellation of the approval and the date on which
  it takes effect.

### [New Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459118 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision to Minister

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  after approval or registration of a feed, a person becomes aware of
  any new information that the feed may present a risk of harm to human
  or animal health or the environment, the person must immediately
  provide the new information to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evaluation and decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, on the basis of the new information, the Minister
  determines, in re-evaluating the feed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that it does not present a risk of harm to human or
    animal health or the environment, the Minister must maintain the
    approval or registration of the feed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that it presents a risk of harm to human or animal
    health or the environment, the Minister must

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a feed that has been approved,
      other than a feed that is manufactured by or for a government,
      academic or private research establishment for research or
      experimental purposes, amend the description of the feed in the
      Canadian Feed Ingredients Table to respond to the risk, or remove
      the feed from that Table, in which case the feed is no longer
      approved under subsection 8(1),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a feed that has been approved for
      research or experimental purposes by or for a government, academic
      or private research establishment, cancel the approval in
      accordance with section 10, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a feed that has been registered,
      cancel the registration in accordance with section 15.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of amendment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must give public notice of the
  determination under subparagraph (2)(b)(i) to amend the description of
  the feed in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table or remove the feed

## [Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1459209 .Part}

### [General Provisions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459210 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No risk of harm

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A feed
  must not present a risk of harm to human or animal health or the
  environment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mixing of feed presenting a risk

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} It is prohibited for a person to mix a feed that
  presents a risk of harm to human or animal health or the environment
  with another feed for the purpose of bringing the feed into compliance
  with the requirements of the Act and these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (2) does not apply in the following
  circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person referred to in subsection (2) makes a
      written request for authorization to mix the feed to the Minister,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the request is accompanied by information
      respecting the risk and the measures that will be taken to
      mitigate the risk,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines, on the basis of the
      information provided, that the mix does not present a risk of harm
      to human or animal health or the environment, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Minister provides to the person a written
      authorization to mix the feed; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the feed that is being mixed is the result of a
    formulating error and the risk of harm is posed by a medicating
    ingredient, a single ingredient feed that does not contain a
    contaminant, a nutrient or a product type set out in the Compendium
    of Non-Feed Products.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited content

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** A feed must not contain
or present, as the case may be,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} weed seeds at a level

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that would individually or cumulatively exceed the
    maximum percentage set out in column 2 of the List of Weed Seeds and
    Maximum Levels for Feeds for the species of weed referred to in
    column 1, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that could present a risk of harm to animal health
    or the environment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} screenings sold or offered for sale that would
  individually or cumulatively exceed the maximum percentage of weed
  seeds set out in column 3 of the List of Weed Seeds and Maximum Levels
  for Feeds for the species of weed referred to in column 1;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} mould or damage from heat or any other cause that
  would

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} render it unfit for consumption by livestock, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cause it to present a risk of harm to human or
    animal health when fed in proportions commonly used;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any product of animal origin, including a fish or a
  bird, that is not fresh or sound or that has not been properly
  processed in accordance with good manufacturing practices;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} proteins in any form derived in Canada

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from [[specified risk
    material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 6.1 of the
    [Health of Animals Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._296),
    except in accordance with a permit issued under section 160 of those
    Regulations for the purposes of section 6.4 of those Regulations, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the carcasses of any ruminants, other than
    cattle, that died or were condemned before they otherwise would have
    been slaughtered for human consumption as food;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} proteins in any form derived from the carcass of an
  animal other than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a fish, crustacean, mollusc or insect, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a [[food animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
    defined in section 1 of the [Safe Food for Canadians
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-108), that was slaughtered
    for human consumption as food or was raised for slaughter for human
    consumption as food;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} any nutrient referred to in the Tables of Maximum
  Nutrient Values for Feeds in an amount exceeding the maximum amount
  set out in those Tables for the species of livestock for which the
  nutrient is intended;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a fat that is or may be derived from a ruminant and
  contains more than 0.15% insoluble impurities;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} any extraneous substance except in amounts that are
  unavoidable even if good manufacturing practices are followed;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} bacteria of the genus [Salmonella]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} that present a risk of harm to human or animal health;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} any contaminant referred to in the Tables of Maximum
  Contaminant Levels for Feeds at a level exceeding the maximum level
  set out in those Tables for the species or class of livestock for
  which the feed is intended;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a single ingredient feed that was formulated for a
  different species;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} any substance that could, when fed in quantities
  commonly used or as specified in the directions for use and in
  consideration of the total diet, result in the production of an
  article of [[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of
  the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27), that is prohibited from sale
  under section 4 of that Act; or

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} any substance, other than those referred to in
  paragraphs (a) to (m), that presents a risk of harm to human or animal
  health or the environment when fed in quantities commonly used or as
  specified in the directions for use and in consideration of the total
  diet.

### [Feed Names]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459373 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Suitable name

**[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** The name of a feed
required to be shown on the label of a feed must not tend to deceive or
mislead a purchaser.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

- **[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  mixed feed must have as part of its name the purpose for which it is
  intended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Species and class

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A mixed feed must have as part of its name each
  species and class of livestock for which it is intended, unless the
  feed is formulated for all species or classes of livestock, in which
  case it may have as part of its name the word "livestock" or the words
  "[animaux de ferme]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identical names

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the name of a mixed feed is identical to that of
  another feed of the same manufacturer but is different in terms of its
  guaranteed level of protein, the brand of the feed must be different
  from the brand of the other feed or the percentage of protein content
  must form part of the name of each feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Premix

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is a premix, the word "premix" or
  "[prémélange]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" must form part of the name of the
  feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplement

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is a supplement, the word "supplement" or
  "[supplément]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" must form part of the name of the
  feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Customer formula feed

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is a customer formula feed, the words
  "customer formula" or "[formule du client]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" must
  form part of the name of the feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Single ingredient feed

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is a single ingredient feed, the name of
  the feed must be one of the names approved and set out for that
  ingredient in the Canadian Feed Ingredients Table.

## [Preventive Controls]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1459412 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459413 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this section and sections 56 to 60.

[[acceptable level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[acceptable level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a level of a
    biological, chemical or physical hazard that does not present a risk
    of contamination of a feed. ([niveau acceptable]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[control measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control measure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a measure that can be
    applied to prevent or eliminate any biological, chemical or physical
    hazard that presents a risk of contamination of a feed or to reduce
    the hazard to an acceptable level. ([mesure de
    contrôle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[critical control point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[critical control point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a step at which
    the application of a control measure is essential to prevent or
    eliminate any biological, chemical or physical hazard that presents
    a risk of contamination of a feed or to reduce the hazard to an
    acceptable level. ([point de contrôle critique]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment, in a facility,
    that is used in connection with an activity that is regulated under
    the Act. ([équipement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a location, excluding a
    conveyance, where a feed is manufactured, stored, packaged, labelled
    or sold. ([installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Biological, Chemical and Physical Hazards]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459421 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identification and analysis of hazards

- **[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who manufactures, stores, packages, labels, sells or exports a
  feed must identify the biological, chemical and physical hazards that
  are present in that feed and analyze those hazards to determine
  whether they present a risk of contamination of that feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control of hazards

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person must prevent, eliminate or reduce to an
  acceptable level the hazards identified as being present in the feed
  by using control measures that are shown by evidence to be effective,
  including any treatment or process that is necessary for the feed to
  comply with section 35.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Factors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In identifying and analyzing the hazards referred to
  in subsection (1), the person must consider the effect of any factor
  relevant to the safety of the feed, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the formulation of the feed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the ingredients of the feed, including raw
    materials;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the level of any inherent contaminant in the feed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for manufacturing, processing,
    packaging and labelling the feed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the storage and distribution of the feed;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} transportation practices;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the intended or reasonably foreseeable use of the
    feed;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the condition, function, design and sanitation of
    the facility and equipment;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} employee hygiene; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} meteorological conditions.

### [Preventive Control Plan]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1459439 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Preparing, keeping and maintaining

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who manufactures, stores, packages, labels, sells or exports a
  feed must prepare, keep and maintain a written preventive control plan
  that meets the requirements set out in section 59 for any of those
  activities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), a preventive control plan is
  not required to be prepared, kept or maintained for any activity
  conducted by that person in respect of a feed that is a cultivated
  farm crop that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is unprocessed and will be manufactured, processed
    or treated for use as a grain, oil, pulse or sugar; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a label applied or attached to it that bears
    the words "For Further Preparation Only" or "[pour conditionnement
    ultérieur seulement]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Implementation

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** Any person that is
required to prepare, keep and maintain a preventive control plan must
implement that plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- hazards

- **[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  preventive control plan must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the biological, chemical and
    physical hazards that are identified under section 56 as presenting
    a risk of contamination of a feed, the control measures for
    preventing or eliminating those hazards or reducing them to an
    acceptable level, and the evidence that shows that the control
    measures are effective;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of any critical control point,
    including, for each,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the related control measures and the evidence
      that shows that the control measures are effective,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the critical limits,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for monitoring the critical
      control points in relation to their critical limits, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the corrective action procedures; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the procedures for verifying that the
    implementation of the preventive control plan results in compliance
    with the provisions of the Act and these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- measures to be taken

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The preventive control plan must describe the
  measures that will be taken to ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning and sanitation of a facility and of
    any equipment are conducted in a manner that does not present a risk
    of contamination of a feed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a facility is protected against the entry of any
    animal that presents a risk of contamination of a feed and that the
    measures do not present a risk of contamination of that feed;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} no substance in a facility presents a risk of
    contamination of a feed and no substance is handled or used in a
    manner that presents such a risk;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any sanitizer or non-feed chemical or biological
    agent in a facility

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is properly and clearly identified,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is suitable for its intended use, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is handled and used in accordance with the
      manufacturer's instructions;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any agronomic input, pet food or veterinary drug in
    a facility is properly and clearly identified;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any conveyance that is used to manufacture or
    convey a feed to or from a facility and that is unloaded or loaded
    at the facility

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is designed, constructed, used and maintained to
      prevent, eliminate or reduce to an acceptable level any risk of
      contamination of the feed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is constructed of, and maintained using,
      materials that are suitable for their intended use and do not
      present a risk of contamination of the feed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is equipped with instruments to control,
      indicate and record any parameters that are necessary to prevent,
      eliminate or reduce to an acceptable level any risk of
      contamination of the feed,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} functions as intended,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} is accessible for cleaning, sanitizing,
      maintenance and inspection,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} is of sound construction and in good repair,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} if necessary, is calibrated in accordance with
      the manufacturer's instructions,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} does not contain or has not contained any
      animal, any [[pest control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
      defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products
      Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01) or any other material or substance that
      presents a risk of contamination of the feed unless the conveyance
      is adequately sanitized, and

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} is clean and in a sanitary condition to prevent,
      eliminate or reduce to an acceptable level any risk of
      contamination of the feed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any equipment that is used in the manufacturing,
    storing, packaging or labelling of a feed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} meets the requirements set out in subparagraphs
      (f)(i) to (vii), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is appropriate for the feed and for the activity
      being conducted;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any equipment or conveyance in a facility that is
    used to handle contaminated materials, waste or any other thing that
    is not intended for a feed, unless that equipment or conveyance does
    not come into contact with those materials, that waste or those
    things,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is used only for that purpose and identified as
      being reserved for that purpose, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} meets the applicable requirements set out in
      paragraph (g);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any risk is prevented, eliminated or reduced to an
    acceptable level for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any land that forms part of a facility and that
      presents a risk of contamination of a feed, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any facility located near any place or thing
      that presents a risk of contamination of a feed;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the interior of a facility is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} designed to minimize the accumulation of
      materials that present a risk of contamination of a feed and to
      permit effective maintenance, cleaning and, if applicable,
      sanitizing,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} designed, constructed and maintained in such a
      manner that it does not present a risk of contamination of a feed,
      including

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the size and layout are adequate to accommodate
        the activity being conducted and the equipment used in the
        activity,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the entry of insects, rodents and other vermin
        is minimized, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} any floors, walls, ceilings, windows and doors
        are smooth, non-absorbent and impervious to moisture, except if
        those floors, walls, ceilings, windows or doors do not present a
        risk of contamination of a feed,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} constructed of, and maintained using, materials
      that are suitable for their intended use and appropriate for the
      feed and for the activity being conducted, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} of sound construction and in good repair;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a facility or conveyance where a feed is
    manufactured, stored, packaged or labelled is designed, constructed
    and maintained in such a manner that the movement of persons and
    things within, into and out of it is controlled;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the movement of persons and things referred to in
    paragraph (k) does not present a risk of contamination of a feed;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} physical or other effective means are used to
    separate incompatible activities in order to prevent contamination
    of a feed;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} physical or other effective means are used to
    separate a feed from

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} anything that presents a risk of contamination of
      the feed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any feed that does not meet the requirements of
      the Act or these Regulations, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} anything that is manufactured, stored, packaged
      or labelled in a facility and not intended or sold for use as a
      feed;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any feed that does not meet the requirements of the
    Act or these Regulations is identified as such and placed in a
    designated area when it arrives at a facility;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} the feed referred to in paragraph (o) does not
    contaminate any other feed that is in the facility;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} a facility has means for the removal and disposal
    of contaminated materials and waste and, if necessary to prevent
    contamination of a feed, is equipped with a drainage, sewage and
    plumbing system that functions as intended;

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} contaminated materials and waste are removed and
    disposed of at a frequency that is sufficient to prevent
    contamination of a feed and in a manner that does not present a risk
    of contamination of the feed;

  - [(s)]{.lawlabel} any water that might come into contact with a feed
    must not present a risk of contamination of that feed;

  - [(t)]{.lawlabel} any steam or ice that might come into contact with
    a feed must not present a risk of contamination of the feed;

  - [(u)]{.lawlabel} any system that supplies water is not
    cross-connected with any other system, unless measures are taken to
    prevent, eliminate or reduce to an acceptable level any risk of
    contamination of a feed as a result of the cross-connection;

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any treatment of water, steam or ice is conducted
    in a manner that does not present a risk of contamination of a feed;

  - [(w)]{.lawlabel} any unloading and loading of a feed from or onto a
    conveyance at a facility is conducted in a manner that does not
    present a risk of contamination of the feed;

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} any storing of a feed is conducted in a manner that
    does not present a risk of contamination of the feed;

  - [(y)]{.lawlabel} any storing of conveyances, equipment, sanitizers,
    agronomic inputs, veterinary drugs, chemical agents, packaging
    materials, labels or any other thing that is used in the
    manufacturing, storing, packaging or labelling of a feed is
    conducted in a manner that does not present a risk of contamination
    of the feed;

  - [(z)]{.lawlabel} any person who is involved in the manufacturing,
    storing, packaging or labelling of a feed has the competencies and
    qualifications that are necessary to carry out their duties;

  - [(z.1)]{.lawlabel} any person who enters or is in an area where a
    feed is manufactured, stored, packaged or labelled wears clothing,
    footwear and protective coverings that will prevent, eliminate or
    reduce to an acceptable level any risk of contamination of the feed;
    and

  - [(z.2)]{.lawlabel} a facility and any equipment are clean and in a
    sanitary condition to prevent, eliminate or reduce to an acceptable
    level any risk of contamination of the feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content --- other requirements

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The preventive control plan must include a
  description of the measures for ensuring that the applicable
  requirements set out in sections 34 to 54, 61 to 64, 69 and 71 are
  met.

## [Investigation, Complaints and Recall]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1459523 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Investigation --- risk of harm

- **[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures, stores, packages, labels, sells or exports a
  feed and who suspects on reasonable grounds that the feed presents a
  risk of harm to human or animal health or the environment or does not
  meet the requirements of the Act or these Regulations must immediately
  investigate the matter.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the investigation establishes that the feed
  presents a risk of harm to human or animal health or the environment,
  the person must immediately notify the Minister and immediately take
  action to mitigate the risk.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints --- procedure

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures, stores, packages, labels, sells or exports a
  feed must prepare, keep and maintain a document that sets out a
  procedure for receiving, investigating and responding to complaints
  regarding the feed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Complaints --- implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a complaint, the person must implement
  the procedure, prepare a document that sets out the details of the
  complaint, the results of the investigation and the actions taken
  based on those results and keep the document for two years after the
  day on which the actions are completed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall procedure

- **[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures, stores, packages, labels, sells or exports a
  feed must prepare, keep and maintain a document that sets out a recall
  procedure that enables the effective recall of the feed, the name of a
  contact person who is responsible for the procedure and the name of a
  contact person who is responsible for conducting recalls.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recall --- implementation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a feed is recalled because it presents a risk of
  harm to human or animal health or the environment, the person must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately implement the recall procedure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prepare a document that sets out the details of the
    recall, including any information that substantiates its
    effectiveness, and keep the document for two years after the day on
    which the recall is initiated.

## [Samples for Analysis]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1459628 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

- **[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sample of feed taken by an inspector for analysis must be
  representative of the lot of feed from which it is taken and be of
  sufficient size to be satisfactory for analytical purposes.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaged feed --- more than 5 kg

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the feed to be analyzed is in packages of more
  than 5 kg, samples approximately equal in size must be taken from not

  fewer than 10 packages, samples approximately equal in size must be
  taken from each of the packages.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Packaged feed --- 5 kg or less

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the feed to be analyzed is in packages of 5 kg or
  less, one unbroken package may constitute a sample.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Damaged feed

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a portion of the feed that is to be analyzed
  appears mouldy or otherwise damaged in a manner to affect its
  suitability for feeding purposes, separate samples may be taken of the
  undamaged portion and the damaged portion.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bulk feed

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the feed to be analyzed is in bulk, samples of
  approximately equal size must be taken from not fewer than 10 separate
  sections of that bulk feed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Validity of registration certificate

**[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** A registration
certificate issued under section 9 of the former Regulations that is
valid immediately before the day on which this section comes into force
is deemed to have been issued under section 11 of these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of registration certificate

**[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** Section 12 of the former
Regulations continues to apply in respect of a registration certificate
on or after the day on which this section comes into force if, before
that day, the Minister has sent the notice referred to in subsection
12(2) of the former Regulations to the registrant by registered mail.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents --- manufacture
before coming into force

- **[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subsections 15(1) and (4) of the
  former Regulations continue to apply in respect of any of the feeds
  referred to in those subsections if they were manufactured before the
  day on which this section comes into force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents --- manufacture
  on or after coming into force

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 64 applies in respect of any of the feeds
  referred to in that section only if they are manufactured on or after
  the day on which this section comes into force.
