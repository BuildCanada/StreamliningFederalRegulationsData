## [Maximum Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028708 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 73(2.1)(b) of the [Nunavut Act](/eng/acts/N-28.6), an amount
of \$15,000,000 is hereby prescribed for agreements with governments.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1028710 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
