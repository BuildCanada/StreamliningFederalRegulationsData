## Order Designating the Minister of Health to Be the Minister for the Purposes of the Physical Activity and Sport Act in Respect of Physical Activity and the Minister of State (Sport) to Be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act in Respect of Sport {#order-designating-the-minister-of-health-to-be-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-physical-activity-and-sport-act-in-respect-of-physical-activity-and-the-minister-of-state-sport-to-be-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-that-act-in-respect-of-sport .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-83
:::

[PHYSICAL ACTIVITY AND SPORT ACT](/eng/acts/P-13.4)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Designating the Minister of Health to Be the Minister for the
Purposes of the Physical Activity and Sport Act in Respect of Physical
Activity and the Minister of State (Sport) to Be the Minister for the
Purposes of that Act in Respect of Sport

P.C. 2025-494 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 2 of the [Physical Activity and Sport
Act](/eng/acts/P-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484){#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484-ID0EBEAA},

- ::: Footnote

  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484-ID0EBEAA){#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484}S.C.
  2003, c. 2
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-427 of March 14,
  2025[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2911_hq_1746976651){#fn_81000-3-2911_hq_1746976651-ID0EBBCAA};

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2911_hq_1746976651-ID0EBBCAA){#fn_81000-3-2911_hq_1746976651}SI/2025-46
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Health, a member of the
  King's Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes
  of the [Physical Activity and Sport Act](/eng/acts/P-13.4)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484){#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484-ID0EBABAA}
  in respect of physical activity; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of State (Sport), a member of
  the King's Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the
  purposes of the [Physical Activity and Sport
  Act](/eng/acts/P-13.4)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484){#fn_OrderdesignatingministerforanAct_hq_1746827484-ID0EBAAAA}
  in respect of sport.
