## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831669 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[cup]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cup]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[mug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
    small hollowware that is used for the consumption of
    liquids. ([tasse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou
    [chope]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drinking vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drinking vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hollowware that is
    used for the consumption of liquids. ([récipient à
    boire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[flatware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[flatware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has an
    internal depth not exceeding 25 mm, measured vertically from the
    lowest interior point to a horizontal plane passing through the
    point of overflow. ([vaisselle plate]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has an
    internal depth greater than 25 mm, measured vertically from the
    lowest interior point to a horizontal plane passing through the
    point of overflow. ([vaisselle creuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[large hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[large hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hollowware that
    has a capacity of 1.1 L or more. ([grande vaisselle
    creuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent warning]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent warning]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a warning that
    remains legible for the lifetime of the product under normal
    conditions of use and cleaning. ([mise en garde
    permanente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pitcher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pitcher]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any large hollowware that is
    commonly used for storing and dispensing liquids but does not
    include a creamer, coffeepot or teapot. ([pichet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that meets the
    following requirements:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is completely or partly made of ceramic or
      glass;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is completely or partly covered with a
      coating, glaze or decoration that contains lead or cadmium; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its use to store, prepare or serve food, as
      defined in section 2 of the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27),
      is reasonably foreseeable. ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small hollowware]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any hollowware that
    has a capacity of less than 1.1 L. ([petite vaisselle
    creuse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831685 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Migratable limits for lead and cadmium
--- general

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, a
product of a product category set out in column 1 of the table to this
section, when tested in accordance with the method set out in section 1
of the schedule, must not release lead in excess of the migratable limit
set out in column 2 or cadmium in excess of the migratable limit set out
in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Migratable limits for lead and cadmium
--- drinking vessel

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any drinking vessel with a
distinctive exterior decorative pattern within 20 mm of the rim, when
tested in accordance with the method set out in section 2 of the
schedule, must not release lead in excess of 4 mg/L or cadmium in excess
of 0.4 mg/L.

## [Identification and Warning]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831696 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A product that is not
intended to be used for food may release lead or cadmium in excess of
the migratable limits set out in the table to section 2 if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is identified by a design feature, such as a hole
  or mounting hook, that renders the product unsuitable for the storing,
  preparing or serving of food; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it displays, in English and French and in capital
  letters of not less than 3 mm in height, a permanent warning
  consisting of one of the following texts or a similar text, as may be
  appropriate:

  DANGER!

  CONTAINS LEAD --- [CONTIENT DU PLOMB]{.otherLang lang="fr"}

  DO NOT USE FOR FOOD --- [NE PAS UTILISER POUR LES ALIMENTS]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

  DANGER!

  CONTAINS CADMIUM --- [CONTIENT DU CADMIUM]{.otherLang lang="fr"}

  DO NOT USE FOR FOOD --- [NE PAS UTILISER POUR LES ALIMENTS]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

  DANGER!

  CONTAINS LEAD AND CADMIUM --- [CONTIENT DU PLOMB ET DU
  CADMIUM]{.otherLang lang="fr"}

  DO NOT USE FOR FOOD --- [NE PAS UTILISER POUR LES ALIMENTS]{.otherLang
  lang="fr"}

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 3)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test Methods]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831716 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method, which is based on the International
  Organization for Standardization standard ISO 6486-1:2019, entitled
  Ceramic ware, glass ceramic ware and glass dinnerware in contact with
  food --- Release of lead and cadmium --- Part 1: Test Method, third
  edition, August 2019, is to be used to determine whether a product
  releases lead or cadmium:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} handwash the product using a non-acidic detergent
    solution, rinse with distilled water and air dry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fill the product to within 5 mm of the level of
    overflowing with an extraction solution of 4% (volume/volume) of
    acetic acid in water;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cover the product with an inert opaque cover and
    allow to stand for 24 hours at 22 ± 2°C;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} stir the extraction solution to ensure homogeneity
    taking care not to abrade the surface of the product;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} take an aliquot of the extraction solution and,
    within eight hours, analyze the solution using an analytical
    technique that is in accordance with good laboratory practices; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} express the results as the quantity of the lead or
    cadmium in milligrams per litre of the extraction solution.

- **2** The following method, which is based on the American Society for
  Testing and Materials standard ASTM C927-80 (2019)e1, entitled
  Standard Test Method for Lead and Cadmium Extracted from the Lip and
  Rim Area of Glass Tumblers Externally Decorated with Ceramic Glass
  Enamels, is to be used to determine whether a drinking vessel bearing
  a distinctive exterior decorative pattern within 20 mm of the rim
  releases lead or cadmium:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} handwash the drinking vessel using a non-acidic
    detergent solution, rinse with distilled water and air dry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} measure the internal volume of the drinking vessel
    in millilitres by filling it with distilled water to within 5 mm of
    the level of overflowing and record the internal volume (V~2~);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} discard the water and invert the drinking vessel in
    an appropriate laboratory glassware container whose diameter is a
    minimum of 1.25 times and a maximum of 2 times the external diameter
    of the drinking vessel as measured at the rim;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} add to the laboratory glassware container the
    volume of an extraction solution of 4% (volume/volume) of acetic
    acid in water that is sufficient to submerge the rim of the drinking
    vessel in 20 mm of the solution and record the volume of extraction
    solution used (V~1~);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} cover the laboratory glassware container with an
    inert opaque cover and allow to stand for 24 hours at 22 ± 2°C;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} remove the drinking vessel and stir the extraction
    solution to ensure homogeneity;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} take an aliquot of the extraction solution and,
    within eight hours, analyze the solution using an analytical
    technique that is in accordance with good laboratory practices; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} express the results as the quantity of the lead or
    cadmium in milligrams per litre of the extraction solution relative
    to the internal volume of the drinking vessel as follows:

    ::: {#831734 .Paragraph}
    (C × V~1~) / V~2~

    where

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the concentration of lead or cadmium in milligrams per litre
        in the extraction solution used,

    [V~1~]{.dfn}
    :   is the volume in millilitres of the extraction solution used,
        and

    [V~2~]{.dfn}
    :   is the internal volume in millilitres of the drinking vessel.
    :::
