## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663912 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deposit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning that would be given
    to it by the schedule to the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-3) for the purposes of deposit insurance if that
    schedule were read without reference to subsections 2(2), (5)
    and (6) of that schedule. ([dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first deposit transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first deposit transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the first
    deposit transaction, in relation to a prescribed deposit referred to
    in section 2, between a person and an authorized foreign bank.
    ([première opération de dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Deposits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663916 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 545(3) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), a prescribed
deposit is a deposit of less than \$150,000 that is taken by an
authorized foreign bank from

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada or in right of a
  province, an agent of Her Majesty in either of those rights, including
  a municipal or public body empowered to perform a function of
  government in Canada, or an entity controlled by Her Majesty in either
  of those rights;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of a foreign country or any political
  subdivision of that country, an agency of the government of a foreign
  country or any political subdivision of that country, or an entity
  that is controlled by the government of a foreign country or any
  political subdivision of that country;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an international agency of which Canada is a member,
  including the Inter-American Development Bank, the Asian Development
  Bank, the Caribbean Development Bank, the European Bank for
  Reconstruction and Development and any other international regional
  bank of which Canada is a member, and an international agency of which
  Canada is a member that is a member of the World Bank Group;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a financial institution;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a pension fund that is maintained in respect of a
  pension plan registered for income tax purposes and that has total
  assets under administration of more than \$100 million at the time the
  first deposit transaction is made;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a mutual fund that is regulated under an Act of the
  legislature of a province or under the laws of a jurisdiction outside
  Canada and the assets of which are managed by a person that has total
  assets under their management of more than \$10 million at the time
  the first deposit transaction is made;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} an entity that, at the time the first deposit
  transaction is made, has for the fiscal year immediately preceding
  that deposit transaction, gross revenues of more than \$5 million; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other entity, where the deposit facilitates the
  provision of the following services by the authorized foreign bank to
  the entity, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} lending money,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} dealing in foreign exchange, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, other than debt
    obligations of the authorized foreign bank.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-663929 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
