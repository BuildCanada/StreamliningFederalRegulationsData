## Atomic Energy of Canada Limited Authorization Order, 1988 {#atomic-energy-of-canada-limited-authorization-order-1988 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-500
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-09-23

Order Authorizing Atomic Energy of Canada Limited to Incorporate a
Corporation and to Hold and Acquire Shares Thereof and to Sell or
Otherwise Dispose of the Property of the Medical Products Division to
that Corporation

P.C. 1988-2230 1988-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 101(6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EIDBA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is satisfied that Atomic Energy of
Canada Limited, a corporation continued under section 181[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EEDBA}, of the [Canada
Business Corporations Act](/eng/acts/C-44)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_3e){#fn_3e-ID0EBDBA}, is empowered to
incorporate a corporation and to hold and acquire the shares thereof;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EIDBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1984, c. 31, s. 11
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e-ID0EEDBA){#fn_2e}S.C. 1978-79, c. 9,
  ss. 1(F), 57(1)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_3e-ID0EBDBA){#fn_3e}S.C. 1974-75-76, c.
  33(E); S.C. 1978-79, c. 9, s. 1(F)
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources and the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraphs 101(1)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EJAAA} and (b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHAAA} and 108(2)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFAAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order Authorizing Atomic Energy of Canada Limited to incorporate
a corporation and to hold and acquire the shares thereof and to sell or
otherwise dispose of the property of the Medical Products Division to
that corporation.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913613 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Atomic Energy of Canada Limited Authorization Order,
1988](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-500).

## [Authorized Transaction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913615 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Atomic Energy of Canada
Limited is hereby authorized

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to procure the incorporation of a corporation to be
  known as "Theratronics International Limited" all the shares of which,
  on incorporation, will be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Atomic
  Energy of Canada Limited;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to acquire shares of that corporation that, on
  acquisition, will be held by, on behalf of or in trust for Atomic
  Energy of Canada Limited; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to sell or otherwise dispose of the property of the
  Medical Products Division, a division of Atomic Energy of Canada
  Limited, to that corporation.
