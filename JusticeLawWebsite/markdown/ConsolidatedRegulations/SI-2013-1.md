## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-624470 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[declaration of Subtrust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[declaration of Subtrust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      declaration, dated January 3, 2012, that sets out the terms and
      conditions of the Subtrust. ([déclaration de
      sous-fiducie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an eligible
      taxpayer, means an amount paid by the Subtrust in respect of the
      eligible taxpayer's Eligible Income Tax, within the meaning
      assigned by section 2.02 of the declaration of Subtrust, that is
      attributable to an FRT Contractor Mitigation Amount. ([montant
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[eligible taxpayer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[eligible taxpayer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by section 2.04 of the declaration of Subtrust. ([contribuable
      admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[FRT Contractor Mitigation Amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[FRT Contractor Mitigation Amount]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the
      meaning assigned by section 2.01 of the declaration of
      Subtrust. ([montant compensatoire de la FRT]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Subtrust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Subtrust]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 2011 Contractor
      Mitigation Account Subtrust that was established on January 3,
      2012, and is a subtrust of the BC Forestry Revitalization Trust
      II. ([sous-fiducie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of meanings in Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the Act.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-624482 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Eligible amount

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to
each eligible taxpayer of the income tax paid or payable under Part I of
the Act for a taxation year, and of any related interest and penalties,
that are attributable to an eligible amount paid in the year by the
Subtrust to, or for the benefit of, the eligible taxpayer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}FRT Contractor Mitigation Amount

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted to
each eligible taxpayer in respect of whom an eligible amount has been
paid by the Subtrust of the amount of any interest and penalties paid or
payable to the extent that they relate to income tax under Part I of the
Act that is attributable to an FRT Contractor Mitigation Amount paid to,
or for the benefit of, the eligible taxpayer.
