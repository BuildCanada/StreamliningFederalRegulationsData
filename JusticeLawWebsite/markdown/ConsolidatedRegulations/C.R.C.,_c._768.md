## Goods for Emergency Use Remission Order {#goods-for-emergency-use-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 768
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed Under Division III of
Part IX and Under any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act on Goods for Use
in Cases of Emergency
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561728 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Goods for Emergency Use Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._768).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561730 .Part}

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an urgent and critical situation
of a temporary nature that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is of such proportions or nature as to exceed the
  capacity or authority of a province or municipality to deal with it;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is caused by an actual or imminent

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fire, flood, drought, storm, earthquake or other
    natural phenomenon,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} disease in human beings, animals or plants,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} accident or pollution, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} act of sabotage or terrorism; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} results or may result in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} danger to the lives, health or safety of
    individuals,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} danger to property,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} social disruption, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a breakdown in the flow of essential goods,
    services or resources. (*urgence*)

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/93-157, s. 1
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561745 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of all taxes payable under Division III of
Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
on goods that are temporarily imported into Canada that are required for
an emergency.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2(E)
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-14, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All goods on which
remission is granted pursuant to section 2, except goods that are
consumed or destroyed during the emergency referred to in that section
shall, when they are no longer required, be exported from Canada.
