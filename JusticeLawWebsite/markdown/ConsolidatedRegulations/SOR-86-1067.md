## Customs Brokers Licensing Regulations {#customs-brokers-licensing-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1067
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-11-06

Customs Brokers Licensing Regulations

P.C. 1986-2487 1986-11-06
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA}, a copy of proposed
[Regulations respecting the licensing of customs
brokers](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1067), substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 10, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of National Revenue with respect
thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1986, c. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
subsection 9(5), paragraph 164(1)(j) and subsection 166(1) of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EGAAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the licensing of customs
brokers](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1067), effective on the coming into
force of subsection 9(5), paragraph 164(1)(j) and subsection 166(1) of
the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EBAAA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889409 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a
    customs broker, means the manager of a local customs office or
    customs offices that serve the area in which the broker transacts
    business, or proposes to transact business, as a customs broker;
    ([agent en chef des douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence to transact business
    as a customs broker issued pursuant to section 9 of the Act.
    ([agrément]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Qualifications for Customs Brokers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889414 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual is qualified under these Regulations if the individual

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a citizen or permanent resident of Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is of good character;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is at least 18 years of age;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} has sufficient financial resources to conduct his
    business in a responsible manner; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} has a sufficient knowledge of the laws and
    procedures relating to importations and exportations determined in
    accordance with section 4.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A partnership is qualified under these Regulations if
  the partnership

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership composed of
    individuals,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is composed of individuals each of whom meets the
      qualifications prescribed in paragraphs (1)(a) to (c),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has sufficient financial resources to conduct
      its business in a responsible manner, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has at least one partner who has a sufficient
      knowledge of the laws and procedures relating to importations and
      exportations, determined in accordance with section 4; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership composed of
    corporations,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is composed of corporations each of which meets
      the qualifications prescribed in paragraphs (3)(a) to (c),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has sufficient financial resources to conduct
      its business in a responsible manner, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has at least one partner that meets the
      qualification prescribed in paragraph (3)(d).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A corporation is qualified under these Regulations if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the corporation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is incorporated in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is of good reputation, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has sufficient financial resources to conduct
      its business in a responsible manner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all of the directors of the corporation are of good
    character;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a majority of the directors of the corporation are
    citizens or permanent residents of Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} at least one officer of the corporation has a
    sufficient knowledge of the laws and procedures relating to
    importations and exportations, determined in accordance with
    section 4.

<!-- -->

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a sufficient knowledge of the laws and procedures
  relating to importations and exportations is established, for the
  purposes of paragraph 3(1)(e), subparagraphs 3(2)(a)(iii) and (b)(iii)
  and paragraph 3(3)(d), if the individual, partner or officer has
  written the Customs Brokers Professional Examination, given under
  section 15, and attained a grade of at least 60 per cent on it

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not more than one year before the day on which the
    application for a licence was made; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than one year before the day on which the
    application for a licence was made if the individual, partner or
    officer transacted business as a customs broker, whether on their
    own behalf or on behalf of a licensee, from within one year after
    the day on which the examination was written until not more than one
    year before the day on which the application for a licence was made.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 3(1)(e),
  subparagraphs 3(2)(a)(iii) and (b)(iii) and paragraph 3(3)(d), a
  sufficient knowledge of the laws and procedures relating to
  importations and exportations is established, if the individual,
  partner or officer

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prior to the coming into force of these
    Regulations, met the requirement under the Custom-House Brokers
    Licensing Regulations of having a sufficient knowledge of the law
    relating to customs matters to discharge the obligations of a
    customs broker; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} transacted business as a customs broker, whether on
    his own behalf or on behalf of a person to whom a licence was issued
    under subsection 118(1) of the former [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), being chapter C-40 of the Revised Statutes
    of Canada, 1970, until not more than six months before the date of
    the application for a licence.

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889479 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Before a licence is
issued or renewed, the applicant must give security in accordance with
the requirements of the [Financial Security (Electronic Means)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-42) and in the amount of
\$50,000.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889494 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A fee
  of \$600 must be paid before a licence is issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A renewal fee of \$600 must be paid before a licence
  is renewed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A renewal fee must be refunded if, before the
  15th day of February preceding the day on which the licence will
  expire, the customs broker withdraws the request for renewal by
  written notice.

## [Duration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889501 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A licence, including a
licence that has been renewed, expires on the 31st day of March next
following the date on which the licence or its renewal is expressed to
be effective.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889503 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A licence authorizes the
holder to transact business as a customs broker at any customs office if
the holder maintains at least one business office in Canada.

## [Transaction of Business as a Customs Broker]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889511 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Every customs broker
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} display prominently at each business office at which
  he transacts business as a customs broker

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} where the customs broker is a partnership or a
    corporation, a sign bearing the name under which the partnership or
    corporation is authorized to transact business as a customs broker,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the licence or a copy thereof;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately notify the Minister in writing of any
  change

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the address of a business office at which he
    transacts business as a customs broker,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the legal or business name of the partnership
    or the corporation, where the customs broker is a partnership or a
    corporation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the membership of the partnership, where the
    customs broker is a partnership,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the officers or directors of the corporation,
    where the customs broker is a corporation,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2002-149, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in the ownership of the business or corporation,
    where the customs broker is an individual or corporation, and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} in the individuals meeting the knowledge
    requirement determined in accordance with section 4;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} furnish to the importer or exporter, in respect of
  each transaction made on their behalf, a copy of the customs
  accounting documents bearing the customs accounting number and
  official customs stamp, or a copy of the information transmitted by
  electronic means to the Agency; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} promptly account to a client importer or exporter for
  funds received

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for the client from the Receiver General for
    Canada, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} from the client in excess of the duties or other
    charges payable in respect of the client's business with the Canada
    Customs and Revenue Agency.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889541 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  customs broker shall keep the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} records and books of account indicating all
    financial transactions made while transacting business as a customs
    broker;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of each customs accounting document made
    while transacting business as a customs broker, a copy of all
    supporting documents and a copy of the information transmitted by
    electronic means to the Agency while transacting business as a
    customs broker;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} copies of all correspondence, bills, accounts,
    statements and other papers received or prepared by the customs
    broker that relate to the transaction of business as a customs
    broker; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} separately, all of the records, books of account
    and copies referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c) relating to business
    transacted under section 13.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every customs broker shall retain the information for
  a period of six years after the importation of the goods to which the
  information relates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information required by subsection (1) shall be
  kept in such a manner as to enable an officer to perform detailed
  audits and to obtain or verify the information.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The information required by subsection (1) shall be
  kept for the period prescribed by subsection (2)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an original document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a copy made by means of any photographic,
    microphotographic or image-processing process that is in accordance
    with National Standard of Canada CAN/CGSB-72.11-93, Microfilm and
    Electronic Images as Documentary Evidence, published by the Canadian
    General Standards Board in November 1993, as amended from time to
    time; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on machine-sensible data media if the media can be
    related back to the supporting source documents and are supported by
    a system capable of producing accessible and readable copy.

## [Cancellation or Suspension]{.HTitleText1} {#h-889556 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister or a person designated by the Minister for the purposes of
  section 9 of the Act may suspend or cancel the licence of a customs
  broker if the customs broker

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contravened the provisions of an Act of Parliament
    or a regulation made thereunder relating to the importation or
    exportation of goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acted to defraud Her Majesty or a client;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} suggested a plan for the evasion of any duties or
    other debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada that involves the
    contravention of the provisions of an Act of Parliament or a
    regulation made thereunder;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} failed to comply with these Regulations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} became insolvent or bankrupt;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} engaged in any dishonest conduct while transacting
    business as a customs broker;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} ceased to carry on business as a customs broker or
    failed to carry out his duties and responsibilities as a customs
    broker in a competent manner; or

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} is no longer qualified under these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a licence is cancelled or suspended the
  Minister or a person designated by him for the purposes of section 9
  of the Act shall cause to be given to the customs broker

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days notice of the proposed cancellation or
    suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reasonable information concerning any allegations
    with respect to any of the grounds referred to in paragraphs (1)(a)
    to (h) on which the Minister proposes to cancel or suspend the
    licence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reasonable opportunity to respond and make
    representations as to why the licence should not be cancelled or
    suspended.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-121, s. 3
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Every customs broker that
ceases to transact business as a customs broker or whose licence has
been cancelled must immediately stop displaying their licence.
