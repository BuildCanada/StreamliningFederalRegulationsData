## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833787 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada--Newfoundland and Labrador
Atlantic Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.5).

## [Financial Resources]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833790 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of financial resources

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 162.1(1) of the Act, the proof that an
  applicant has the necessary financial resources is to be made by the
  applicant providing the Board with a statement of its net assets or of
  funding arrangements that it has made that demonstrates to the Board's
  satisfaction that it is able to pay the applicable amount referred to
  in that subsection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Substantiating documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement must be accompanied by one or more of
  the following documents that substantiate it:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's most recent audited annual
    financial statement and, if the applicant has been given a credit
    rating by a credit rating agency that is current at the time the
    application is made, a document that indicates that credit rating;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy or a certificate of insurance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an escrow agreement;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a letter of credit;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a line of credit agreement under which funds
    identified in the statement are available;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee agreement;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a security bond or pledge agreement or an indemnity
    bond or suretyship agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audited statement and documents

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Board may require that the
  statement and substantiating documents be audited by a qualified
  independent auditor and that the applicant provide it with a report of
  the audit that is signed by that auditor.

## [Financial Responsibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833806 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements of pooled fund

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A pooled
  fund that is established for the purposes of subsection 163(1.01) of
  the Act must be located and administered in Canada.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted uses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fund is to be used only to make payments under
  subsection 163(2) of the Act. However, the fund may be used to make
  payments

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 158(2) of the Provincial Act, if
    it is also established for the purposes of subsection 158(1.1) of
    that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 168(2) of the [Canada-Nova Scotia
    Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8), if it is also established for the purposes of
    subsection 168(1.01) of that Act;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 160(2) of the Canada-Nova Scotia
    Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation (Nova Scotia)
    Act, SNS 1987, c. 3, if it is also established for the purposes of
    subsection 160(1A) of that Act; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} under subsection 27(2) of the [Canada Oil and Gas
    Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7), if it is also established for the
    purposes of subsection 27(1.01) of that Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment on demand

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Moneys that are required to be paid out of the fund
  must be paid by the administrator of the fund on demand.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligations of administrator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The administrator of the fund must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every year, provide the Board with an audited
    financial statement that demonstrates that the fund has been
    maintained at a minimum of \$250 million or at the higher minimum
    amount set by regulation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the Board within 24 hours of the addition of
    a participant to, or withdrawal of a participant from, the fund or
    of any change in the amount of the fund, other than one that is
    solely attributable to an interest charge or a banking fee;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the Board of a contravention by a
    participant in the fund of their obligation under subsection
    163(1.1), (1.2) or (5) of the Act within 24 hours after they become
    aware of the contravention; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provide the Board with the phone number, email
    address and mailing address of their contact person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement into pooled fund

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 163(5) of the Act, the reimbursement into the pooled fund of
an amount that is paid out of it must be made within seven days after
the day on which the payment is made.

## [Board Recommendation Regarding Lesser Financial Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-833826 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Circumstances relating to recommendation

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 163.1(1) of the Act, the Board may make a
  recommendation to the Federal Minister in respect of an applicant if
  the Board is satisfied that the estimated total of the losses,
  damages, costs and expenses --- other than losses of non-use value ---
  for which the applicant may be liable under paragraphs 162(1)(b) and
  (2)(b) of the Act in connection with the proposed work or activity to
  which the application pertains is less than the amount referred to in
  paragraph 162(2.2)(a) or (b) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recommendation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The recommendation must identify the hazards that are
  relevant to the proposed work or activity and must include an
  assessment of the risks associated with each event that could occur in
  connection with each of those hazards and that could result in debris,
  in a spill or in an authorized discharge, emission or escape of
  petroleum.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The following information must accompany the
  recommendation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated total of the losses, damages, costs
    and expenses referred to in subsection (1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recommended amount that is less than the amount
    referred to in paragraph 162(2.2)(a) or (b) or 163(1)(a) of the Act,
    as the case may be;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the reasons for the recommendation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of any information provided by the
    applicant to the Board that the Board considers to be pertinent;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any information concerning the recommendation that
    the Board provided to the Provincial Minister in connection with
    that Minister's approval under subsection 163.1(1) of the Act; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any information requested by the Federal Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may submit to the Federal Minister any
  other information that it considers to be pertinent.
