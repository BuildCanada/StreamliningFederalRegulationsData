## Public Service Part-time Regulations {#public-service-part-time-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-33b
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33)

Registration 1980-12-22

Regulations Respecting Part-time Work in the Public Service

P.C. 1980-3475 1980-12-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is not
practicable nor in the best interests of the Public Service to apply the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to persons who are
not ordinarily required to work more than one-third of the normal
scheduled daily or weekly hours of work established for persons doing
similar work;

Whereas the Public Service Commission has further decided that it is not
practicable nor in the best interests of the Public Service to apply the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) to persons who were
employed under the authority of the Part-time Employees Exclusion
Approval Order, C.R.C., c. 1350 and the Part-time Employment
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1351, and who are ordinarily required to work
more than one-third of the normal scheduled daily and weekly hours of
work established for persons doing similar work but not more than
15 hours per week;

And Whereas the Public Service Commission is of the opinion that it is
desirable to make the annexed Regulations prescribing how such persons
shall be dealt with.

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Public Service Commission, is pleased hereby,
effective January 1st, 1981,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 39 of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to revoke the Part-time Employees
  Exclusion Approval Order, C.R.C., c. 1350 and to make the annexed
  Order approving the exclusion by the Public Service Commission of
  persons who are not ordinarily required to work more than one-third of
  the normal scheduled daily or weekly hours of work established for
  persons doing similar work and of certain other persons from the
  operation of the Public Service Employment Act in substitution
  therefor; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to subsection 35(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), to revoke the Part-time Employment
  Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1351, and to make the annexed Regulations
  respecting Part-time work in the Public Service in substitution
  therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870797 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Public Service Part-time
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-33b).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870799 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[former part-time worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former part-time worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    employed under the authority of the Part-time Employees Exclusion
    Approval Order, C.R.C., c. 1350 as it read before January 1, 1981
    and the Part-Time Employment Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1351 as it read
    before January 1, 1981, who is ordinarily required to work more than
    one-third of the normal scheduled daily or weekly hours of work
    established for persons doing similar work but not more than
    15 hours per week; ([ancien travailleur à temps
    partiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Part-time Work Exclusion
    Approval Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-33a);
    ([Décret]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[part-time worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[part-time worker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is not
    ordinarily required to work more than one-third of the normal
    scheduled daily or weekly hours of work established for persons
    doing similar work. ([travailleur à temps partiel]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870804 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only in respect of persons to whom the Order applies.

## [Delegation of Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870806 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A deputy head may delegate
any of his powers, functions or duties under these Regulations to one or
more persons under his jurisdiction.

## [Part-time Workers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870808 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 6, where
a department requires the services of a part-time worker, the deputy
head

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall recruit and select the persons from Canada
  Employment Centres or from Public Service Commission Staffing Offices;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon selecting the person, may appoint him to the
  position he is to occupy.

## [Former Part-time Workers and Part-time Workers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870812 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A department in which
former part-time workers and part-time workers are employed may:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a call-back list for such period as the
  deputy head may establish;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} place on that call-back list the names of former
  part-time workers and part-time workers who have performed their
  duties satisfactorily before the termination of their services; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} call back former part-time workers and part-time
  workers whose names are on the call-back list when positions at a
  level for which they are qualified become available.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A former part-time worker
and a part-time worker shall be granted a least one day's notice before
their services are terminated.
