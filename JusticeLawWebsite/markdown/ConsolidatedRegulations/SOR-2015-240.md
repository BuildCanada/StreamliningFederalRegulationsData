## Simultaneous Programming Service Deletion and Substitution Regulations {#simultaneous-programming-service-deletion-and-substitution-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-240
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 2015-11-19

Simultaneous Programming Service Deletion and Substitution Regulations
::::

::::::::: section
:::::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 10(3) of the [Broadcasting
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371){#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371-ID0EFBDA},
a copy of the proposed [Simultaneous Programming Service Deletion and
Substitution Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-240), substantially
in the annexed form, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on August 1, 2015, and a
reasonable opportunity was given to licensees and other interested
persons to make representations to the Canadian Radio-television and
Telecommunications Commission with respect to the proposed Regulations;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371-ID0EFBDA){#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371}S.C.
  1991, c. 11
  :::

Therefore, the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission, pursuant to subsection 10(1) of the [Broadcasting
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371){#fn_SOR-2015-240_e_hq_16371-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Simultaneous Programming Service Deletion and
Substitution Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-240).

Gatineau, November 17, 2015

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Canadian television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Canadian television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      television programming undertaking that is licensed as a
      television station or that provides its Canadian programming
      service by way of a transmitting antenna, and includes an
      educational authority responsible for an educational television
      programming service and the stations CTV Two Atlantic, and CTV Two
      Alberta. ([station de télévision canadienne]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[local television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[local television station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a
      licensed area of a distribution undertaking, means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licensed television station that

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a Grade A official contour or digital
          urban official contour that includes any part of the licensed
          area, or

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if there is no Grade A official contour or
          digital urban official contour, has a transmitting antenna
          that is located within 15 km of the licensed area;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an educational authority responsible for an
        educational television programming service; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the station CTV Two Atlantic or CTV Two
        Alberta. ([station de télévision locale]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
a person that is licensed to carry on a distribution undertaking, other
than a person that is licensed to carry on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a subscription television system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a relay distribution undertaking; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an undertaking that only rebroadcasts the
  radiocommunications of one or more other licensed undertakings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Terrestrial distribution undertaking

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  operator of a Canadian television station may ask a licensee that
  carries on a terrestrial distribution undertaking to delete the
  programming service of another Canadian television station or a
  non-Canadian television station and substitute for it the programming
  service of a local television station or regional television station.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}DTH distribution undertaking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The operator of a Canadian television station may ask
  a licensee that carries on a DTH distribution undertaking

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to delete the programming service of a non-Canadian
    television station and substitute for it the programming service of
    the Canadian television station; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subscribers located within the Grade
    B official contour or noise-limited bounding official contour of the
    Canadian television station, to delete the programming service of
    another Canadian television station and substitute for it the
    programming service of the Canadian television station.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to carry out request

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided under these Regulations or in a condition of its
  licence, a licensee that receives a request referred to in section 3
  must carry out the requested deletion and substitution if the
  following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the request is in writing and is received by the
    licensee at least four days before the day on which the programming
    service to be substituted is to be broadcast;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the programming service to be deleted and the
    programming service to be substituted are comparable and are to be
    broadcast simultaneously;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the programming service to be substituted has the
    same format as, or a higher format than, the programming service to
    be deleted; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the licensee carries on a terrestrial
    distribution undertaking, the programming service to be substituted
    has a higher priority under section 17 of the [Broadcasting
    Distribution Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555) than the
    programming service to be deleted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Late request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a request that is not received within
  the period referred to in paragraph (1)(a) but that meets the
  conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(b) to (d), the licensee may carry
  out the requested deletion and substitution, except as otherwise
  provided under these Regulations or in a condition of its licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision by Commission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must not delete a programming service and
  substitute another programming service for it if the Commission
  decides under subsection 18(3) of the [Broadcasting
  Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) that the deletion and substitution are not in
  the public interest.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deletion and substitution by operator

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee and the operator of the local television
  station or the regional television station may agree to have the
  operator carry out the deletion and substitution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one request

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If a licensee that carries on a terrestrial
  distribution undertaking receives a request for deletion and
  substitution from more than one operator of a Canadian television
  station, it must give preference to the programming service of the
  television station that has the highest priority under section 17 of
  the [Broadcasting Distribution
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-555).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuation of substitution

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} A licensee may discontinue a deletion and
  substitution if the deleted and substituted programming services are
  not, or are no longer, comparable and broadcast simultaneously.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimal disruption

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee that deletes a programming service and substitutes another
  programming service for it must exercise due diligence to ensure that
  the deletion and substitution do not result in errors in the provision
  of the service and that they cause only minimal disruption of the
  service to its subscribers.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee must provide compensation to its customers
  if the Commission decides under subsection 18(3) of the [Broadcasting
  Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01) that the licensee deleted and substituted a
  programming service in a manner that, through its own actions,
  resulted in recurring substantial errors and did not establish that it
  exercised due diligence to avoid those errors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Errors

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, an error occurs if
  the deletion and substitution of the programming service are not
  carried out simultaneously or the video or audio components of the
  programming service are affected as a result of the deletion and
  substitution.
