## Air Transportation Tax Regulations, 1992 {#air-transportation-tax-regulations-1992 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-294
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1993-06-08

Regulations Respecting the Air Transportation Tax

P.C. 1993-1204  1993-06-08
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, the Minister of Transport and the
Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 21[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-294_e_hq_6256){#fn_SOR-93-294_e_hq_6256-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to revoke the
Air Transportation Tax Regulations, C.R.C., c. 583, and to make the
annexed [Regulations respecting the air transportation
tax](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-294), in substitution therefor.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952538 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Air Transportation Tax Regulations,
1992](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-294).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952540 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an air transportation tax
    licence granted by the Minister under section 17 of the Act.
    ([licence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952544 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every return that is
required under section 20 of the Act to be made by a licensed air
carrier shall be delivered or mailed to the Chief of Collections or the
Chief of Compliance at the Regional Excise Office where the licence was
issued.

## [Evidence of Payment of Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952546 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Payment
  of the tax imposed on an amount paid or payable for the transportation
  of a person by air, made to a licensed air carrier or the agent of a
  licensed air carrier before the person emplanes at an airport in
  Canada, shall be indicated and identified as the Canadian tax on the
  ticket for air transportation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The ticket referred to in subsection (1) shall, at
  the time of a person's emplanement at an airport in Canada for an
  international flight, be submitted to a person who is authorized by
  the emplaning air carrier to accept tickets for the transportation of
  persons by air and to allow emplanement for the purpose of the flights
  described in the tickets.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(2) of the Act, evidence of the prepayment of tax imposed
on an amount paid or payable for the transportation of a person by air
that begins at a point in the taxation area and ends at a point in the
taxation area shall be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the form of a ticket, boarding pass or sales
  invoice that indicates the prepayment; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submitted, at the time of emplanement in Canada, to a
  licensed air carrier or the agent of a licensed air carrier.

## [Exemption of Groups of Commercial Air Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952553 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The transportation of a
person by air is exempt from the operation of Part II of the Act on
commercial air services provided by a certified air carrier on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rotary-wing aircraft; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any fixed-wing aircraft that has a maximum
  certificated take-off weight (MCTOW) of not more than 8 000 kg.

## [Chartered Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952557 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the tax
imposed under section 10 of the Act, where the amount charged for
transportation by air includes the transportation of persons and of
goods, the amount charged for

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of persons shall be the amount
  charged for transportation by air less the amount that is attributed
  to the charge for the transportation of goods, other than goods that
  are the baggage or equipment of the persons being transported; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transportation of goods shall be the amount
  charged for transportation by air attributable to the segment of the
  journey during which the goods are transported, multiplied by the
  percentage of passenger seats that are unoccupied during that segment.

## [Payment for Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-952561 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of Part II
of the Act, where an air carrier agrees to provide the transportation of
a person by air on credit, the amount payable for the transportation is
deemed to be paid or payable at the place where, and at the time when,
the air carrier agrees to provide the service.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where the transportation of
a person by air is subject to an [ad valorem]{.otherLang lang="la"} air
transportation tax imposed by the United States, the amount of the tax
determined in accordance with subsection 11(1) of the Act in respect of
an amount paid or payable outside Canada for the transportation is
reduced to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the amount is paid or payable after December
  31, 1997 for emplanements after February 28, 1998, the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of \$2 and 3 per cent of the amount paid
    or payable for the transportation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$15; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the total of \$4 and 5 per cent of the amount paid
    or payable for the transportation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$27.50.
