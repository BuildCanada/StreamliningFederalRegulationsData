## Nautical Charts and Related Publications Fees Order {#nautical-charts-and-related-publications-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-281
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1994-04-13

Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid for the Provision of Nautical
Charts and Related Publications
::::

:::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1988-440 of March 10, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-281_e_hq_5261){#fn_SOR-94-281_e_hq_5261-ID0EDBCA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the fees to be paid for the
provision of nautical charts and related
publications](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-281).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963496 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Nautical Charts and Related Publications Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-281).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963498 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[chart sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chart sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means each side of a sheet of
    paper on which a nautical chart is printed; ([feuille de
    carte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[GEBCO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[GEBCO]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means General Bathymetric Charts of
    the Oceans. ([GEBCO]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963502 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of retail sales only.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963504 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where the Department of
Fisheries and Oceans provides a person with a nautical chart or related
publication set out in column I of an item of the schedule, that person
shall pay to the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans the fee set out in
column II of that item.
