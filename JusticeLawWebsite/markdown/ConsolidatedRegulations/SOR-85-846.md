## Canadian Ownership and Control Determination Forms Order, 1985 {#canadian-ownership-and-control-determination-forms-order-1985 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/85-846
:::

[CANADIAN OWNERSHIP AND CONTROL DETERMINATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-20)

Registration 1985-08-29

Order Respecting the Form and Manner in which an Application for a
Certificate under the Canadian Ownership and Control Determination Act
Shall be Filed on or After August 29, 1985 and the Information and
Documentation that is to be Contained in or to Accompany that
Application

"prescribed" in subsection 35(1) and to subsection 38(1) of the
[Canadian Ownership and Control Determination

application for a certificate under the Canadian Ownership and Control
Determination Act shall be filed on or after August 29, 1985 and the
information and documentation that is to be contained in or to accompany
that application](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-846).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887896 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Ownership and Control Determination Forms Order,
1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-85-846).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887898 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In this
  Order,

  [[Annex]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Annex]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an annex set out in Schedule
      II; ([appendice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Ownership and
      Control Determination Act](/eng/acts/C-20);
      ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[class of capitalization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[class of capitalization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a class of
      formal equity within the meaning of the Regulations read without
      regard to section 6 thereof or a class of forward equity;
      ([catégorie de capitalisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a form set out in Schedule I;
      ([formule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Ownership
      and Control Determination Regulations,
      1984](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-431). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All other words and expressions have the same meaning
  as in the [Canadian Ownership and Control Determination
  Act](/eng/acts/C-20) and the Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where any provision of this Order provides for the
  making of an application in a particular Form, the application may be
  made in that Form or in the form of a letter or other appropriate
  form, but, in any case, the application shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} include the information indicated in the Form;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include or be accompanied by any other information
    or documentation required by this Order to be included in or to
    accompany the application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} be certified as indicated in the Form.

## [Application of Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887911 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies to all
applications filed on or after August 29, 1985.

## [Applications by Individuals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887913 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application by an
individual shall be in Form A-I.

## [Applications by Small Applicants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887915 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application by a small
applicant shall be in Form A-S.

## [Applications by Other Applicants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887917 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, an application by an entity that is not a small applicant shall be in
Form A.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An application by a
corporation that makes an election pursuant to subsection 30(7) of the
Regulations may be in Form A-AML instead of Form A.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An application by an entity
that meets the requirements specified in Form A-C may be in Form A-C
instead of Form A.

## [Additional Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887921 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** An application by a
cooperative corporation that by reason of paragraph 12(1)(g) of the
Regulations is considered to have a Canadian ownership rate of 100 per
cent shall include or be accompanied by the information indicated in
Annex 1 and the certificate set out in that Annex completed as indicated
therein.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An application by a life
insurance company shall include or be accompanied by the information
indicated in Annex 2.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** An application by a
mutual company, other than a life insurance company, shall include or be
accompanied by the information indicated in Annex 3.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9, an
application by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an entity that by reason of subsection 12(1) of the
  Regulations is considered to have a Canadian ownership rate of 100 per
  cent, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity referred to in subsection 16(5) of the
  Regulations,

shall include or be accompanied by such of the information indicated in
Annex 4 as is relevant and the relevant certificate set out in that
Annex completed as indicated therein.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where, in the case of any
application, any of the following information, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of each class of capitalization,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a summary of nominee client information,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of measured block information,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the beneficial Canadian ownership
  calculation for each class of formal equity measured,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the Canadian ownership rate calculation,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other information that is relevant in determining
  the Canadian ownership rate of the applicant, including, but not
  limited to, the following:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information identifying a class of capitalization,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} information relating to the exclusion of a class
    of capitalization from measurement,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} information regarding the beneficiaries of a
    trust,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} information relating to the status of an investor
    as a primary investor, as a secondary investor or as an investor
    equity-related to an applicant,

is required in respect of the applicant or an investor in the applicant
in order to make, in accordance with the Act and Regulations, a
determination of the Canadian ownership rate of the applicant for the
purposes of the application, such information shall be included in the
application in respect of the applicant or investor, as the case may be.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where,
  with respect to an application that includes or is accompanied by the
  information required by Form A or Form A-AML, it is relevant to
  determine the Canadian ownership rate or control status of the
  applicant, the application shall be accompanied by such of the
  following documentation as is relevant in the making of that
  determination:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} every prospectus filed by or on behalf of the
      applicant with a securities regulatory authority, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} every other offering document,

    pursuant to which securities were issued by the applicant on or
    after October 28, 1980;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the financial statements of the applicant
    for its most recently completed fiscal period, if the applicant is
    required to measure its Canadian ownership rate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of that portion of the unit holders list of
    the applicant and of any unit holders list of an investor in the
    applicant that shows all entries therein that have a number of units
    equal to or exceeding 10 per cent of the measured block threshold
    for each class of formal equity of the applicant or the investor, as
    the case may be, the beneficial Canadian ownership rate of which is
    calculated in accordance with section 25 of the Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an applicant makes an application in Form A-S
  or an application that includes or is accompanied by the information
  indicated in that Form, the application may include or be accompanied
  by one copy of financial statements that show the assets that would be
  included in making a determination of the applicant's total assets
  pursuant to section 9 of the Regulations.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
section 39 of the Act, an application is made by a person for a
certificate stating the person's Canadian ownership rate and control
status or for a certificate stating the person's control status, the
application shall include or be accompanied by such information and
documentation as are required in order to determine the control status
of the person.

## [Filing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-887951 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** An application shall be
filed by sending it by mail or delivering it to the Central Registry of
the Petroleum Incentives Administration in the City of Calgary or the
City of Ottawa.
