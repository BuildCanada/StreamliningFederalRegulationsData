## Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.W.T.) {#reservation-to-the-crown-waiver-order-great-slave-lake-n.w.t. .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2010-54
:::

[TERRITORIAL LANDS ACT](/eng/acts/T-7)

Registration 2010-08-18

Reservation to the Crown Waiver Order (Great Slave Lake, N.W.T.)

P.C. 2010-972 2010-08-04
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Territorial Lands Act](/eng/acts/T-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612442-E_hq_8262){#fn_612442-E_hq_8262-ID0EBBAA},
hereby orders that the lands described in the annexed schedule lying
within 30.48 metres (100 feet in width) of the ordinary high water mark
along the shoreline of Great Slave Lake, in the Northwest Territories,
be included in any grant of those lands.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel} {#h-615067 .scheduleLabel}
:::

All that portion of Lot 4, Block C, in the Northwest Territories, as
that lot is shown on a plan of survey of plan number 93648 in the Canada
Lands Surveys Records at Ottawa, a copy of which is filed in the Land
Titles Office for the Northwest Territories Land Registration District
at Yellowknife under number 4172.
