## [Identified Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826714 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Identified provisions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The provisions set out in
the schedule are the identified provisions of the laws of the
Legislature of Yukon for the purposes of section 65 of the [Yukon
Surface Rights Board Act](/eng/acts/Y-4.3).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826717 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
