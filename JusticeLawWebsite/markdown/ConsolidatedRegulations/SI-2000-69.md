## Indian Settlements Remission Order (2000) {#indian-settlements-remission-order-2000 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2000-69
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2000-08-16

Indian Settlements Remission Order (2000)

P.C. 2000-1112  2000-07-27
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2000-69_e_hq_5124){#fn_SI-2000-69_e_hq_5124-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed [Indian Settlements Remission Order
(2000)](/eng/regulations/SI-2000-69).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604885 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian Settlement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a settlement named
    in column 1 of Schedule 1 and described in column 2 of that
    Schedule. ([établissement indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
    ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604889 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order applies in
respect of an Indian Settlement until all or part of the lands
constituting that Indian Settlement are set apart as a reserve by an
order of the Governor in Council.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Income Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604891 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-604892 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tax imposed under
  Part I, I.1 or I.2 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other words and expressions not otherwise defined
  in section 1 have the same meaning as in the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

### [Remission of Income Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-604896 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to a taxpayer whose income is situated on an Indian Settlement, in
respect of each taxation year or fiscal period beginning during or after
the year set out in column 2 of Schedule 2 in respect of that Indian
Settlement, of any amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties paid or payable by
  the taxpayer for the taxation year or fiscal period

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties that would have
  been payable by the taxpayer for the taxation year or fiscal period if
  the Indian Settlement had been a reserve throughout that taxation year
  or fiscal period.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-604901 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-604902 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and
  services tax imposed under Division II of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} all other words and expressions not otherwise defined
  in section 1 have the same meaning as in Part IX of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15).

### [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText2} {#h-604906 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 7 and
8, remission is hereby granted to a recipient of a taxable supply made
on or delivered to an Indian Settlement on or after the date set out in
column 3 or 4 of an item of Schedule 2, as the case may be, of tax paid
or payable by that person in an amount equal to any amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable by the recipient

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the recipient
  if the Indian Settlement had been a reserve.

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-604911 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 6
is granted to an individual if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable has not otherwise been
  rebated, credited, refunded or remitted under Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) or the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for the remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the day on which the tax was
  paid or became payable.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 6
is granted to a person other than an individual if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid or payable has not otherwise been
  rebated, credited, refunded or remitted under Part IX of the [Excise
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) or the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of tax paid on or after the date set out
  in column 4 of an item of Schedule 2 but before the date set out in
  column 3 of that item, a claim for the remission is made to the
  Minister of National Revenue within two years after the latter date;
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of tax paid on or after the date set out
  in column 3 of an item of Schedule 2, a claim for the remission is
  made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years after the
  date on which the tax was paid.
