## Import of Dairy Products for Personal Use Permit {#import-of-dairy-products-for-personal-use-permit .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 623
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

General Import Permit No. 1
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559079 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Permit may be cited as
the [Import of Dairy Products for Personal Use
Permit](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._623).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-559081 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any person may, under the
authority of this General Import Permit, import into Canada from any
country any dairy products for the personal use of the importer and his
household not exceeding \$20 in value for each importation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/82-116, Sch. I, s. 1
- SI/83-180, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Where completion and
validation of a customs entry form is required with respect to any dairy
product that is imported under the authority of this General Import
Permit, that form shall be endorsed "Imported under the authority of
General Import Permit No. 1" or "Importé en vertu de la Licence générale
d'importation n^o^ 1".
