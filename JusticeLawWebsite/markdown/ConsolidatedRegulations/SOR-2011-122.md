## Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in Benefits Order {#royal-canadian-mounted-police-dependants-pension-fund-increase-in-benefits-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-122
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[ROYAL CANADIAN MOUNTED POLICE PENSION CONTINUATION
ACT](/eng/acts/R-10.6)

Registration 2011-06-10

Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in
Benefits Order

T.B. 836109 2011-06-09
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas it appears from a report made under section 56 of the [Royal
Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EBBBA} that the Royal Canadian
Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund is substantially in excess of
the amount required to make adequate provision for the prospective
payments out of it;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae-ID0EBBBA){#fn_ae}R.S.C. 1970, c. R-10
  :::

Therefore, the Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the Minister of
Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, pursuant to subsection 57(1)
of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police Pension Continuation
Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EGBAA} and paragraph 7(2)(e) of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Royal Canadian Mounted Police (Dependants) Pension Fund Increase in
Benefits Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-122).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be-ID0EDBAA){#fn_be}R.S., c. F-11
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following pension
benefits, as provided in Part IV of the [Royal Canadian Mounted Police
Pension Continuation Act](/eng/acts/R-10.6) R.S.C. 1970, c. R-10 shall
be increased in the following manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pensions for current and prospective widows and
  children shall be increased by 2% in each of the 2011, 2012 and 2013
  plan years, as of April 1 of each plan year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} lump sum benefits payable on the death of a member
  shall be increased by 2% in each of the 2011, 2012 and 2013 plan
  years, as of April 1 of each plan year; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} residual amounts payable on the death of a widow of a
  member shall be calculated with a deemed increase in the member's
  contributions, without crediting the contributions with interest, of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 1,173% if the widow's death occurs in the 2012 plan
    year,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1,198% if the widow's death occurs in the 2013
    plan year, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 1,224% if the widow's death occurs in the 2014
    plan year.
