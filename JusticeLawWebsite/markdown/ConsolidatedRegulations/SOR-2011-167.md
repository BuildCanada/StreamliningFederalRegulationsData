## Special Economic Measures (Democratic People's Republic of Korea) Regulations {#special-economic-measures-democratic-peoples-republic-of-korea-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-167
:::

[SPECIAL ECONOMIC MEASURES ACT](/eng/acts/S-14.5)

Registration 2011-08-11

Special Economic Measures (Democratic People's Republic of Korea)
Regulations

P.C. 2011-893 2011-08-11
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the situation in
the Democratic People's Republic of Korea constitutes a grave breach of
international peace and security that has resulted or is likely to
result in a serious international crisis;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsections 4(1) to (3) of the [Special Economic Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-14.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-874-E_hq_10578){#fn_81000-2-874-E_hq_10578-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Special Economic Measures (Democratic People's
Republic of Korea) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-167).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772788 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[DPRK]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DPRK]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Democratic People's Republic
    of Korea and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government and departments and the government
      and departments of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its agencies and those of its political
      subdivisions. ([RPDC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a benefit paid under the [Old
    Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9), the [Canada Pension
    Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) or an Act respecting the Québec Pension Plan,
    R.S.Q. c. R-9, any superannuation, pension or benefit paid under or
    in respect of any retirement savings plan or under any retirement
    plan, any amount paid under or in respect of the [Garnishment,
    Attachment and Pension Diversion Act](/eng/acts/G-2) or the [Pension
    Benefits Division Act](/eng/acts/P-6.7), and any other payment made
    in respect of disability. ([pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772797 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 10, it
is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
to export, sell, supply or ship any goods, wherever situated, to the
DPRK or any person in the DPRK or to deal in any goods destined for the
DPRK or any person in the DPRK.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 10, it
is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
to import, purchase, acquire or ship any goods that are exported,
supplied or shipped from the DPRK after the day on which these
Regulations come into force, whether the goods originated in the DPRK or
elsewhere.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to make an investment
in any entity in the DPRK that involves a dealing in any property,
wherever situated, held by or on behalf of the DPRK, any person in the
DPRK, or a national of the DPRK who does not ordinarily reside in
Canada.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 11, it
is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
to provide or acquire financial services --- including those that are
intended to facilitate an investment in any entity --- to, from or for
the benefit of or on the direction or order of the DPRK or any person in
the DPRK.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 12, it
is prohibited for any person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada
to transfer, provide or communicate, directly or indirectly, technical
data to the DPRK or any person in the DPRK.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to dock in Canada or pass through Canada any ship that is
registered in the DPRK unless such docking or passage is necessary to
safeguard human life.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person to land in or fly over Canada an aircraft that is registered in
the DPRK, unless such landing or flying is necessary to safeguard human
life.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do anything
that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to cause,
facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 2 to 8.

## [Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772806 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 2 and 3 do not apply in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods consigned to the Canadian Embassies in
    Beijing or Seoul or their consular missions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods whose provision is set out in accordance with
    any agreement or arrangement between Canada and the DPRK and for
    which an export permit, if required, has been issued under the
    [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods consigned to one of the following
    organizations for the purpose of safeguarding human life, disaster
    relief, stabilization or the providing of food, medicine, medical
    supplies or equipment, and for which an export permit, if required,
    has been issued under the [Export and Import Permits
    Act](/eng/acts/E-19):

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} international organizations with diplomatic
      status,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} United Nations agencies,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the International Red Cross and Red Crescent
      Movement, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} non-governmental organizations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} personal or settlers' effects that are taken or
    shipped by an individual and that are solely for the use of the
    individual or the individual's immediate family; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} personal correspondence, including lettermail,
    printed papers and postcards, of a weight not exceeding 250 g per
    item of correspondence.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Section 2 does not apply in respect of goods whose
  export is prohibited under the [Regulations Implementing the United
  Nations Resolutions on the Democratic People's Republic of Korea
  (DPRK)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-287).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Section 3 does not apply in respect of goods whose
  import is prohibited under the [Regulations Implementing the United
  Nations Resolutions on the Democratic People's Republic of Korea
  (DPRK)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-287).

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Section 5 does not apply
in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any transaction in respect of the accounts at a
  financial institution that are used for the regular business of the
  Embassies of Canada in Beijing or Seoul or their consular missions;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a transaction by the Government of Canada that is
  provided for in any agreement or arrangement between Canada and the
  DPRK;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} non-commercial remittances of less than \$1,000 sent
  to or from the DPRK or any person in the DPRK, if the person providing
  the financial services keeps a record of the transaction;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any services provided by or to one of the following
  organizations for the purpose of safeguarding of human life, disaster
  relief, stabilization or the providing of food, medicine, medical
  supplies or equipment:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} international organizations with diplomatic status,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} United Nations agencies,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the International Red Cross and Red Crescent
    Movement, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a non-governmental organization that has entered
    into a grant or contribution agreement with the Department of
    Foreign Affairs and International Trade or the Canadian
    International Development Agency;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any pension payable to a person in the DPRK; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} services set out in section 4 of the [Regulations
  Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on the Democratic People's
  Republic of Korea (DPRK)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2006-287).

## [Application Before Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772843 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply according to their terms before they are
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-772845 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
