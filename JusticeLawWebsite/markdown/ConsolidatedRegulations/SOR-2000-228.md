## New Brunswick Primary Forest Products Order {#new-brunswick-primary-forest-products-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-228
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 2000-06-08

New Brunswick Primary Forest Products Order

P.C. 2000-868 2000-06-08
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to section
2[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes
the annexed [New Brunswick Primary Forest Products
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-228).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1991, c.
  34, s. 2
  :::
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661334 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  definitions in this subsection apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Product Act, S.N.B.
      1999, c. N-1.2, as amended from time to time.
      ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Forest Products Marketing
      Board set out in column 1 of the schedule.
      ([office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a Board set out
      in column 1 of an item of the schedule, the Marketing Plan
      established by the Regulation, as amended from time to time, set
      out in column 2 of that item. ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[primary forest product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[primary forest product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any
      unmanufactured product of forest trees of hardwood or softwood
      species, except coniferous trees cut for sale as Christmas trees
      and products from the sap of maple trees. ([produit forestier de
      base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this Order, [[private
  woodlot]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
  [[regulated area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a Board referred
  to in column 1 of an item of the schedule, have the meanings assigned
  to them by the Regulation, as amended from time to time, set out in
  column 2 of that item.

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661342 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every Board is authorized
to regulate the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of primary
forest products produced by producers on private woodlots within its
regulated area and for those purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within New Brunswick, exercise all or any powers like
the powers exercisable by the Board under its Plan and the Act in
relation to the marketing of primary forest products locally within New
Brunswick.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-316, s. 1
:::

## [Levies and Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661346 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every Board may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix, impose and collect levies or charges from
  persons in New Brunswick who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of primary forest products produced by producers on private
  woodlots within its regulated area and for those purposes may classify
  those persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the
  members of the different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Board, including the creation of reserves, the payment of expenses and
  losses resulting from the sale or disposal of primary forest products
  and the equalization or adjustment among producers of primary forest
  products of moneys realized from the sale of those products during
  such period or periods of time as the Board may determine.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661350 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
