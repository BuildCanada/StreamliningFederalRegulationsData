## Alberta Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations {#alberta-sex-offender-information-registration-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-239
:::

[SEX OFFENDER INFORMATION REGISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/S-8.7)

Registration 2004-10-26

Alberta Sex Offender Information Registration Regulations
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Lieutenant Governor in Council of Alberta, pursuant to subsection
18(1) of the [Sex Offender Information Registration
Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7), hereby makes the annexed [Alberta Sex Offender
Information Registration Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-239).

October 19, 2004

Lieutenant Governor of Alberta

Lois Hole
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712979 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[aboriginal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aboriginal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a first
    nations police service established in accordance with
    subsection 5(1) of the Alberta Police Act. ([service de police
    autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Alberta Police Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Alberta Police Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Police Act,
    Revised Statutes of Alberta 2000, c. P-17, as amended from time to
    time. ([loi albertaine sur la police]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[municipal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[municipal police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police
    service established under section 27 of the Alberta Police Act.
    ([service de police municipal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peace officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed as a
    peace officer under the Peace Officer Act or any other enactment of
    Alberta and to whom all or part of the Peace Officer Act or any of
    its regulations apply. ([agent de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Peace Officer Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Peace Officer Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Peace Officer
    Act, S.A. 2006, c. P-3.5, as amended from time to time. ([loi
    albertaine sur les agents de la paix]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regional police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional police service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a police
    service established under section 24 of the Alberta Police Act.
    ([service de police régional]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[special constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[special constable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2012-52,
    s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712992 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Alberta to collect information for the
purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP or a peace officer appointed to,
  or employed by, the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer or peace officer appointed to, or
  employed by,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a regional police service,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a municipal police service, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an aboriginal police service.

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2012-52, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713002 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Persons who are registrars
at the Sex Offender Information Registration Centre Alberta,
administered by RCMP "K" Division, are authorized in the Province of
Alberta to register information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713004 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following places are designated as registration centres in the
  Province of Alberta:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each RCMP detachment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the headquarters of each regional police service or
    municipal police service, other than that of the municipal police
    service of the City of Calgary;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the administration office of the Calgary police
    service; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the headquarters of each aboriginal police service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following are designated as the areas served by
  the registration centres:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an RCMP detachment, the area served by the
    detachment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the headquarters of regional police services or
    municipal police services, the municipalities served by those police
    services;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the administration office of the Calgary police
    service, the City of Calgary; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the headquarters of an aboriginal police
    service, the area served by that police service.
