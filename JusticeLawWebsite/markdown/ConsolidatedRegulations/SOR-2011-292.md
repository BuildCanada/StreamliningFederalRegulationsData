## Internet Child Pornography Reporting Regulations {#internet-child-pornography-reporting-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-292
:::

[AN ACT RESPECTING THE MANDATORY REPORTING OF INTERNET CHILD PORNOGRAPHY
BY PERSONS WHO PROVIDE AN INTERNET SERVICE](/eng/acts/I-20.7)

Registration 2011-12-06

Internet Child Pornography Reporting Regulations

P.C. 2011-1526 2011-12-06
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to section 12 of [An Act respecting
the mandatory reporting of Internet child pornography by persons who
provide an Internet service](/eng/acts/I-20.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Internet Child Pornography Reporting
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-292).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775153 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means [An Act respecting the mandatory
    reporting of Internet child pornography by persons who provide an
    Internet service](/eng/acts/I-20.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    organization named in section 2. ([organisme
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Internet address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Internet address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Internet Protocol
    address or a Uniform Resource Locator. ([adresse
    Internet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who provides
    an Internet service to the public. ([fournisseur de
    services]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Designated Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775160 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation of organization

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 2 of the Act, the designated organization is the Canadian Centre
for Child Protection.

## [Role, Functions and Activities of Designated Organization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775163 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Online Internet address reporting system

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The designated organization
must, for the purpose of receiving reports of Internet addresses under
section 2 of the Act, maintain a secure online system that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assigns each service provider a unique identifier for
  the purpose of making reports;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} allows a service provider to report only Internet
  addresses; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} issues to a service provider, for each report they
  make, a receipt that indicates the incident number assigned to the
  report, the service provider's name and unique identifier and the date
  and time of the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Analysis and communication of findings

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** As soon as feasible after
receiving a report under section 2 of the Act, the designated
organization must determine whether any material found at the reported
Internet address appears to constitute child pornography and, if so,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine, if possible, the geographic location of
  the server that the reported Internet address points to and the
  geographic location of the server hosting the material that appears to
  constitute child pornography; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make available to every appropriate Canadian law
  enforcement agency by secure means

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the reported Internet address,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any geographic location that the
    designated organization was able to determine under paragraph (a),
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any other information in the designated
    organization's possession that might assist the agency's
    investigation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of records

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For each report received in
accordance with section 2 of the Act, the designated organization must
retain the reported Internet address and a copy of the receipt issued
under paragraph 3(c) for two years after the day on which the report is
received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security measures

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The designated organization
must take measures to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure its continued ability to discharge its role,
  functions and activities under the Act, including measures relating to
  the protection of its physical facilities and technical
  infrastructure, risk prevention and mitigation, emergency management
  and service resumption;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} protect from unauthorized access any information
  obtained or generated by the designated organization in the course of
  discharging its role, functions or activities under the Act; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ensure that its personnel are capable of fulfilling
  their duties in the discharge of the designated organization's role,
  functions and activities under the Act, including measures relating to
  their selection and training.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incident: notification of Ministers

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The designated organization
must notify the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Public Safety
and Emergency Preparedness within 24 hours of becoming aware of any
incident that jeopardizes the designated organization's ability to
discharge its role, functions or activities under the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The designated organization
must take any measures necessary to avoid a conflict of interest in
respect of its role, functions and activities under the Act, and must
address any such conflict that does arise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual report

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The designated organization
must, not later than June 30 of each year, submit to the Minister of
Justice and the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness a
report on the discharge of its role, functions and activities under the
Act for the 12-month period beginning on April 1 of the preceding year.
The report must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the number of reports received under section 2 of the
  Act and, of those, the number that led the designated organization to
  make information available to a law enforcement agency under
  paragraph 4(b);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that the designated
  organization had in place in accordance with section 6;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of any incident referred to in
  section 7 that occurred and the steps taken in response to the
  incident;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the measures that the designated
  organization had in place in accordance with section 8, any conflict
  of interest that arose and the steps taken to address it; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other information that may affect the designated
  organization's current or future ability to discharge its role,
  functions or activities under the Act.

## [Obligations of Service Providers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-775198 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Method of reporting

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 2 of the Act, an Internet address must be reported by a service
provider using the online system referred to in section 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content of notification

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
section 3 of the Act, a notification from a service provider must be in
writing and must include the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the child pornography offence that the service
  provider has reasonable grounds to believe is being or has been
  committed using their Internet service;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the material that appears to
  constitute child pornography, including its format;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances under which the service provider
  discovered the alleged offence, including the date and time of
  discovery;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of any other evidence relating to the
  alleged offence in the possession or control of the service provider;
  and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} contact information of the service provider's
  representative for the purpose of investigating the matter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Security measures for preserved data

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A service provider that
is required to preserve computer data under section 4 of the Act must
retain a copy of that data in a secure offline location.
