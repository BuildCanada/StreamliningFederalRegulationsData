## Certain Marine Carriers Remission Order {#certain-marine-carriers-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2008-99
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2008-09-17

Certain Marine Carriers Remission Order

P.C. 2008-1671 2008-09-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Fisheries and Oceans and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_2412_hq_4561){#fn_2412_hq_4561-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610991-E_hq_4570){#fn_610991-E_hq_4570-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Certain Marine Carriers Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2008-99).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_2412_hq_4561-ID0EGCAA){#fn_2412_hq_4561}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 7(2)
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610991-E_hq_4570-ID0EDCAA){#fn_610991-E_hq_4570}R.S., c.
  F-11
  :::
:::

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the fees for marine navigation services provided by the Canadian
Coast Guard that are fixed under subsection 47(1) of the [Oceans
Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4) and that are paid or payable for the years 2008,
2009 and 2010 by marine carriers in respect of ships operating in
Canadian waters between locations situated north of 60° North latitude
and those situated south of 60° North latitude.
