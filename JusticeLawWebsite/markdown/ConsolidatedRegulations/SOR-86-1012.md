## Special Services (Customs) Regulations {#special-services-customs-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1012
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-10-02

Special Services (Customs) Regulations

P.C. 1986-2261 1986-10-02
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 15, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
sections 166 and 167 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EGBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting special customs
services](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1012), effective on the coming into
force of sections 166 and 167 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EBBAA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888371 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
offices that serve that area or place. (*agent en chef des douanes*)

## [Special Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888373 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the following services performed by an officer at
  the request of a person in charge of imported goods or goods destined
  for exportation shall be considered to be special services:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any services performed outside the hours fixed by
    the Deputy Minister for the performance of that service;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any service performed in a place outside the area
    served by a customs office; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any service performed outside the ordinary course
    of the officer's duties.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following services performed by an officer are
  not special services:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the examination of or acceptance of reports in
    respect of a military conveyance, unless that conveyance is being
    used in a commercial operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the release of human remains;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the release of perishable goods needed for the
    preservation of human life or health; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the release of non-perishable goods urgently needed
    for the preservation of human life or health.

## [Special Services Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888384 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every person for whom a
special service is performed by an officer other than an officer
referred to in section 5 shall pay \$25 for the performance of that
service.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where an
  officer is called in on duty to perform a special service for a
  person, that person shall pay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$54 for the first two hours or portion thereof
    spent in the performance of that service; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$27 for each additional hour or portion thereof
    spent in the performance of that service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No charge is payable for the time spent by an officer
  referred to in subsection (1) on meals and rest.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where more than one officer referred to in
  subsection (1) is required to perform a special service, the
  chargeable time in respect of that service shall be the aggregate of
  the time spent by all those officers in the performance of that
  service.

## [Transportation, Accommodation and Meals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888392 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/95-200, s. 1(F)

\]
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), where a special service is to be performed
  by an officer, the person requesting that service shall provide the
  officer with such transportation, accommodation and meals as may be
  required for that officer to perform that service.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the
  transportation, accommodation and meals provided shall be comparable
  in quality to those that would otherwise be authorized by the Treasury
  Board Travel Directive in effect at the time that the special service
  is provided.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a person requesting a special service does not
  provide an officer with accommodation, meals or transportation and the
  officer is required to obtain accommodation, meals or commercial
  transportation, the person requesting the special service shall pay,
  in respect thereof, an amount based on the rates and allowances set
  out in the Treasury Board Travel Directive in effect at the time that
  the special service is provided.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a person requesting a special service does not
  provide an officer with transportation and the officer is required to
  use a privately owned conveyance, or a conveyance owned or leased by
  the Government of Canada, the person requesting the service shall pay,
  in respect of the use of that conveyance, the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an amount based on the allowances for the use of
    privately owned conveyances that are set out in the Treasury Board
    Travel Directive in effect at the time that the special service is
    provided; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} five dollars.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-200, s. 2
:::

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-888404 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If the chief officer of
customs for the place or area in which a special service is to be
performed is of the opinion that security is required to guarantee the
payment of charges incurred in providing that service, the person
requesting that service must give security in accordance with the
requirements of the [Financial Security (Electronic Means)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-42) and in an amount determined
by the Minister.
