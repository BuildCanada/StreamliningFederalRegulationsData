[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Deposit Insurance
    Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fall return]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fall return]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a return referred to in
    paragraph 5(1)(a). ([déclaration automnale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to a member institution,
    means that the member institution has been operating as a member
    institution for less than two full premium years and, if it was
    formed by an amalgamation, none of its amalgamating institutions
    were member institutions immediately before the
    amalgamation. ([nouvelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spring return]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spring return]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a return referred to in
    paragraph 5(1)(b). ([déclaration printanière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[stage of intervention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stage of intervention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a stage of
    intervention that is assigned as a result of an assessment carried
    out in accordance with the Guide to Intervention for Federally
    Regulated Deposit-Taking Institutions, published by the Office of
    the Superintendent of Financial Institutions, as amended from time
    to time. ([stade d'intervention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsidiary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([filiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
