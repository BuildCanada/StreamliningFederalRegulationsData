## National Parks Wildlife Regulations {#national-parks-wildlife-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-401
:::

[CANADA NATIONAL PARKS ACT](/eng/acts/N-14.01)

Registration 1981-05-22

Regulations Respecting Wildlife in the National Parks of Canada other
than Wood Buffalo National Park

P.C. 1981-1331 1981-05-21
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 7(1) of the
National Parks Act, is pleased hereby to revoke the National Parks Game
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 1122, and to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting wildlife in the National Parks of Canada other than Wood
Buffalo National Park](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-401), in substitution
therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870990 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [National Parks Wildlife
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-401).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870992 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/91-421, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[exotic wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exotic wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all wildlife that is
    not indigenous to a park or that has been declared, pursuant to
    these Regulations, to be exotic wildlife; ([faune
    exotique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any weapon from which any
    shot, bullet or other missile can be discharged and that is capable
    of causing injury or death to wildlife and includes anything that
    can be adapted for use as a firearm and anything that is designed
    for or capable of being used for the purpose of causing injury or
    death to wildlife; ([arme à feu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guide]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who holds a valid
    licence issued pursuant to the National Parks Businesses Regulations
    to carry on the business of a guide; ([guide]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[hunt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hunt]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to trap, chase, pursue, worry,
    follow after or on the trail of, search for, shoot at, stalk, lie in
    wait for, or to attempt, in any manner, to capture, kill or injure
    any wildlife whether or not the wildlife is captured, killed or
    injured; ([chasser]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hunting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hunting permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/91-421, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[loaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[loaded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to a firearm, means that
    a live shell or cartridge is present in the breach, chamber or
    attached magazine of the firearm or that a projectile is present in
    the barrel of the firearm; ([chargée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/91-421, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[possession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means possession within the
    meaning of subsection 4(3) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
    ([possession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/91-421, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[through highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[through highway]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Trans-Canada Highway in Banff, Yoho, Glacier
      and Mount Revelstoke National Parks,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Yellowhead Highway in Jasper National Park,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Banff-Windermere Highway in Banff and
      Kootenay National Parks,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Highways 10 and 19 in Riding Mountain National
      Park,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Trans-Canada Highway, the Charlottetown
      Highway, the East Port area Highway 310 and the Terra Nova area
      Highway 301 in Terra Nova National Park,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Highway 114 and the unnumbered roads leading to
      Teahans Corner in Fundy National Park,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the Cabot Trail (Highway 19) in Cape Breton
      Highlands National Park,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} Highway 132 between Cap-aux-Os and
      Cap-des-Rosiers in Forillon National Park,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Highway 117 in Kouchibouguac National Park, or

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} Highway 93 in Banff National Park and Jasper
      National Park; ([route de transit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s.
    16\]]{.Repealed}

[[trap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trap]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure, contrivance or
    device that is capable of capturing wildlife by affording entrance
    but not exit, and includes a snare; ([piège]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means every device in, on or by
    which any person or property is or may be transported or drawn on
    land; ([véhicule]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any boat, canoe, raft,
    amphibious vehicle or any other structure, other than a sea plane,
    used or capable of being used for transportation on water;
    ([bateau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wildlife]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all wild mammals, amphibians,
    reptiles, birds, fish, insects and other invertebrates and any part
    thereof, and includes their eggs and young; ([faune ou animaux
    sauvages]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871022 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 40 and 41 of the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01), these Regulations apply to all parks other
  than Wood Buffalo National Park of Canada and to all park reserves as
  if they were parks.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In the event of an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and the [National Parks of Canada Fishing
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1120) or the [Mingan
  Archipelago National Park Reserve of Canada Snowshoe Hare
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-301), the [National Parks of
  Canada Fishing Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1120) and the
  [Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve of Canada Snowshoe Hare
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-301) prevail, respectively, to
  the extent of the inconsistency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a superintendent,
  park warden, peace officer or any other person authorized by the
  superintendent to carry out functions related to the management of a
  park while any of those persons is engaged in carrying out such
  functions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-301, s. 21\]]{.Repealed}

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-40, s. 2
- SOR/92-441, s. 4(E)
- SOR/97-102, s. 2
- SOR/2004-301, s. 21
:::

## [General Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871030 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  as otherwise provided in these Regulations, no person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hunt, disturb, hold in captivity or destroy any
    wildlife within, or remove any wildlife from, a park;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in La Mauricie National Park, Georgian Bay Islands
    National Park, St. Lawrence Islands National Park or the Mingan
    Archipelago National Park Reserve, have in the person's possession
    any wildlife;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any park other than a park referred to in
    paragraph (b), or outside a park, be in possession of any wildlife
    killed or procured within a park, unless the wildlife is in that
    person's possession in accordance with subsection (4);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s. 17\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} disturb or destroy a nest, lair, den or beaver
    house or dam in a park;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} touch or feed wildlife in a park or entice wildlife
    that is in a park to approach by holding out or setting out decoys
    or any such devices, foodstuffs or bait of any kind; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} release any exotic wildlife within a park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall shine a movable light having a
  voltage greater than 4.5 volts in any area frequented by wildlife
  between sunset and sunrise.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may, in the Mingan Archipelago National Park
  Reserve, have in the person's possession wild ducks, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ducks have been hunted in accordance with these
    Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the request of a park warden or other park
    officer, the person in possession of the ducks produces a hunting
    permit issued pursuant to section 13.1.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person may, in any park other than a park referred
  to in paragraph (1)(b), have in the person's possession any wildlife
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (5), the person in possession
    of the wildlife produces, on the request of a park warden or other
    park officer,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an export permit as issued pursuant to the Game
      Export Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a hunting licence or permit or a trapping
      licence or permit issued by the province in which the wildlife was
      procured,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a hunting tag issued by the province in which
      the wildlife was procured and the tag is properly placed on the
      wildlife as required by the laws of that province, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where the wildlife has been obtained as a gift,
      a valid licence or permit issued by the province in which the
      wildlife was procured permitting a person to give such wildlife to
      another person or a statement signed by the donor of the gift that
      includes the date of the gift, the type and number of wildlife,
      the name of the person receiving the gift and the licence or
      permit number under which the wildlife was procured; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (5), the person is in
    possession of the wildlife in the park for the purpose of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} storing it at a permanent residence in the park,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} transporting it directly on a through highway to
      a permanent residence within the park,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} transporting it directly through the park to
      private property outside the park on a road that is the sole means
      of access to that property, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Prince Edward Island National
      Park and where entrance onto the Gulf Shore Parkway was made
      solely for the purpose of travelling the most direct route to a
      private or public road outside the Park, transporting it on the
      Gulf Shore Parkway.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (4) does not apply to a person who is in
  possession of wildlife that the person is authorized under paragraph
  15(1)(a) to remove, relocate or destroy.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), reasonable protection
  from biting or stinging insects is permitted.

## [Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871061 .Part}

- **[[13.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) to (5),
  the superintendent of the Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve
  may, upon receiving an application, issue a hunting permit, to the
  person named in the application permitting that person to hunt Eider
  and Long-tailed duck within the boundaries of that Reserve during the
  open season set out for those species in that area in the [Migratory
  Birds Regulations, 2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person is eligible for a hunting permit unless
  that person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is a permanent resident of the communities of
    Longue Pointe de Mingan, Mingan, Havre St-Pierre or Baie John Beetz
    in the Province of Quebec; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} holds a valid migratory game bird hunting permit
    issued under the [Migratory Birds Regulations,
    2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall hunt for duck unless the person is
  the holder and is in possession of a hunting permit issued under
  subsection (1).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may specify, in the hunting permit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the period of time for which the permit is valid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the locations in the Mingan Archipelago National
    Park Reserve where the hunting of duck may take place; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} such other conditions as are necessary to ensure
    public safety and the protection of resources in the Mingan
    Archipelago National Park Reserve.

- [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-322, s. 18\]]{.Repealed}

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a hunting permit may hunt Eider and
  Long-tailed duck

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} between one-half hour before sunrise and one-half
    hour after sunset during the period referred to in subsection (1);
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in an amount not exceeding the daily bag limit
    prescribed by the [Migratory Birds Regulations,
    2022](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-105) for the Province of Quebec.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to any aboriginal peoples
  who have existing aboriginal or treaty rights to hunt in the lands
  constituting the Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  superintendent may authorize

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal, relocation or destruction of wildlife
    for scientific purposes or park management purposes;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sale or public disposal of wildlife or wildlife

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the introduction into a park of wildlife that is or

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  superintendent may, where it is necessary for the protection,
  conservation or management of the wildlife in a park, by notice in
  writing, prohibit the possession of firearms or other devices for
  capturing or destroying wildlife during specified periods and in
  specified areas of a park.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of prohibition issued pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall be posted at

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} park warden offices;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information bureaus in the park; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all entrances to the park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A notice posted in accordance with subsection (2)
  shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the areas in the park to which the
    prohibition applies; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the period of time during which the prohibition
    applies.

## [Dogs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871104 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a dog
for the purpose of hunting wildlife.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/91-421, s. 6
:::

## [Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871108 .Part}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall be in possession of a firearm in a park unless the
  firearm is not loaded and is transported in a case or is wrapped and
  tied securely in such a manner that no part of the firearm is exposed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person in Mingan Archipelago National Park
    Reserve who is hunting pursuant to a hunting permit issued under
    subsection 13.1(1); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bonded armoured car service guard who is carrying
    out assigned duties in the park.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} have a loaded firearm in or on a vehicle, vessel or
    aircraft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} discharge a firearm from a vehicle, vessel or
    aircraft.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The superintendent may issue a permit authorizing a
  person to carry a firearm or trap where the firearm or trap is to be
  carried through the park for use outside the park.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall carry a firearm or trap outside a
  vehicle, vessel or aircraft unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that person

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has a permit from the superintendent authorizing
      the carrying of a firearm or trap, whichever is applicable, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} complies with these Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the firearm or trap is being carried directly

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} from a vehicle, vessel or aircraft to that
      person's premises, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to a vehicle, vessel or aircraft from that
      person's premises.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No person, other than a bonded armoured car service
  guard in the performance of that person's duties in a park, shall
  discharge a firearm, except on a rifle range or when shooting wild
  ducks in the Mingan Archipelago National Park Reserve in accordance
  with these Regulations.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No person shall possess a firearm or other device for
  capturing or destroying wildlife in Kejimkujik, La Mauricie or Point
  Pelee National Parks, or in Forillon National Park except when
  traversing the park along Highway 132, unless the person has been
  authorized under paragraph 15(1)(a) to remove, relocate or destroy
  wildlife.

- SOR/82-463, s. 1(F)
- SOR/84-874, s. 3
- SOR/88-40, ss. 6, 7(F)
- SOR/91-421, s. 7
- SOR/92-441, s. 4(E)

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every person who is in
possession of a firearm or trap shall, on the request of a park warden
or other peace officer, allow the park warden or peace officer to
inspect

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the firearm or trap; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the permit required for carrying the firearm or trap.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-102, s. 9(E)
:::

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Every person acting as a
guide for any individual or group carrying firearms or traps shall
ensure that they are carried in accordance with these Regulations.

## [Poison]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871135 .Part}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** No person shall possess
any poison, poisonous substance, gas, narcotic or drug that can be used
for the purpose of capturing, injuring or killing wildlife.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/97-102, s. 10
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871139 .Part}

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** No person shall by radio,
telephone, walkie-talkie or any other means communicate the location of
wildlife within a park to any other person within or outside a park if
either of the persons is hunting.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell or
offer for sale in a park any firearm or trap.
