## Order Designating New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-new-brunswick-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-40
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2017-03-24

Order Designating New Brunswick for the Purposes of the Criminal
Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2017-251 2017-03-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas New Brunswick has legislative measures that protect recipients
of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of
borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of New Brunswick has
requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the
purposes of section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EEABA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBABA};

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Order Designating New Brunswick for the Purposes of
the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of
the](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-40) [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Province Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-843140 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** New Brunswick is designated
for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-843142 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind7E94_hq_19927){#fn_Ind7E94_hq_19927-ID0EBEA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m. Atlantic time
on the first day on which the following are both in force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} An Act Respecting Payday Loans, S.N.B. 2008, c. 3;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Payday Lending Regulation -- Cost of Credit
  Disclosure and Payday Loans Act.
