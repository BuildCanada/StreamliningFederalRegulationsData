## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858118 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Foreign Missions and
    International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement Between the
    Government of Canada and the United Nations University Concerning
    the United Nations University International Network on Water,
    Environment and Health set out in Schedule IV to the
    Act. ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[United Nations Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations
    set out in Schedule III to the Act. ([Convention des Nations
    Unies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[the Director]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in Article I of
    the Agreement. ([Directeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in Article I
    of the Agreement. ([experts]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[International Network on Water, Environment and Health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[International Network on Water, Environment and
    Health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[INWEH]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
    the same meaning as in Article I of the Agreement. ([Réseau
    international pour l'eau, l'environnement et la
    santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [RIEES]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[officials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officials]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in Article
    I of the Agreement. ([fonctionnaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personnel of INWEH]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personnel of INWEH]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in Article I of the Agreement. ([personnel du
    RIEES]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[University]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[University]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
    definition [[the University]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in Article I of
    the Agreement. ([Université]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the
    Act. ([Convention de Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-858131 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}INWEH

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} INWEH
  shall have the legal capacity of a body corporate and the privileges
  and immunities set out in Articles II and III of the United Nations
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Director and family

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Director and members of his or her family that
  form part of his or her household, unless they are Canadian citizens
  or permanent residents in Canada as defined by applicable Canadian
  legislation, shall have privileges and immunities comparable to those
  accorded to diplomatic agents, and members of their families forming
  part of their households, under the Vienna Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officials of the University -- Section
  19

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the University within the scope of
  Section 19 of Article V of the United Nations Convention and members
  of their families that form part of their households shall have, to
  the extent set out in Sections 18 and 19 of Article V and Article VII
  of the United Nations Convention, the privileges and immunities
  comparable to those accorded to diplomatic agents, and members of
  their families forming part of their households, under the Vienna
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officials of the University

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Officials of the University shall have the privileges
  and immunities set out in Section 18 of Article V of the United
  Nations Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Experts of the University shall have the privileges
  and immunities set out in Article VI of the United Nations Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Personnel of INWEH

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} Personnel of INWEH shall have, to the extent set out
  in section 4 of Article IX of the Agreement, privileges and immunities
  comparable to those accorded to diplomatic agents under Articles 31
  and 35 and paragraph (1)(b) of Article 36 of the Vienna Convention.
