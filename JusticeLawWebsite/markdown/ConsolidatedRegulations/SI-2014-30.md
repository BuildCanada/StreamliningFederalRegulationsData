## Arctic Star Order {#arctic-star-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2014-30
:::

Registration 2014-04-09

Arctic Star Order

P.C. 2014-299 2014-03-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} authorizes Canadians to accept and wear the Arctic
  Star in recognition of honourable service during the Second World War;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} directs that the Arctic Star follow the Air Crew
  Europe Star in the order of precedence in the Canadian Honours System.
