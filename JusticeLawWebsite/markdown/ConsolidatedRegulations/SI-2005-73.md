## Order Transferring Functions from the Department of Human Resources Development to the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency of the National Collection Services and of the Collections Litigation and Advisory Services {#order-transferring-functions-from-the-department-of-human-resources-development-to-the-canada-customs-and-revenue-agency-of-the-national-collection-services-and-of-the-collections-litigation-and-advisory-services .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2005-73
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2005-08-10

Order Transferring Functions from the Department of Human Resources
Development to the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency of the National
Collection Services and of the Collections Litigation and Advisory
Services

P.C. 2005-1355 2005-07-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDFAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDFAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2003, c.
  22, s. 207
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} from the Department of Human Resources Development to
  the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency the control and supervision of
  the portions of the federal public administration in the Department of
  Human Resources Development known as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} National Collection Services, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Collections Litigation and Advisory Services,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} from the Minister of State styled Minister of Human
  Resources and Skills Development and Minister responsible for
  Democratic Renewal to the Minister of National Revenue the powers,
  duties and functions of the Minister of State styled Minister of Human
  Resources and Skills Development and Minister responsible for
  Democratic Renewal relating to the collection of debts due to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada under

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Education Savings
      Act](/eng/acts/C-3.6) and any regulations made under that Act, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23),
      the [Canada Student Financial Assistance Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7)
      and any regulations made under those Acts, if the debts are not
      being collected by a service provider referred to in the [Canada
      Student Financial Assistance Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7), and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of a Province under

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Education Savings
      Act](/eng/acts/C-3.6), and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Student Financial Assistance
      Act](/eng/acts/S-22.7), if the debts are not being collected by a
      service provider referred to in that Act, and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} from the Minister of Human Resources Development
  styled Minister of Social Development to the Minister of National
  Revenue the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Human
  Resources Development styled Minister of Social Development relating
  to the collection of debts due to Her Majesty in right of Canada under
  the following Acts:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Pension Plan](/eng/acts/C-8) other than
    subsections 66(2.1) and (3) and section 107 of that Act, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the [Old Age Security Act](/eng/acts/O-9) other
    than subsections 37(2.1) and (4) and section 40 of that Act,

effective at 00:00:02, August 1, 2005.
