## [Prescribed Manner]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675328 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portion of entity's activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[financial services entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 507(1) of the Act, the prescribed manner of determining the
portion of the activities that an entity engages in that consists of one
or more of the activities referred to in paragraphs (a) to (h) of that
definition (in this section referred to as "particular activities") is
to determine it as a percentage in accordance with the formula

::: {#675331 .Section}
A × 100%

where

[A]{.dfn}

:   is the greater of the fraction B/C and the fraction D/E,

    where

    ::: {#675335 .NestedFormula}

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total assets of the entity that is in respect
        of the particular activities,

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the total assets of the entity,

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total revenue of the entity that is derived
        from the particular activities, and

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the total revenue of the entity.
    :::
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-71, s. 2(F)
:::

Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Portion of entity's business in Canada
--- par. 522.05(a) of the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 522.05(a) of the Act, the prescribed manner of determining the
portion of the business in Canada, of a foreign bank or other entity,
that consists of one or more of the activities referred to in that
paragraph (in this section referred to as "particular activities") is to
determine it as a percentage in accordance with the formula

::: {#675344 .Section}
A × 100%

where

[A]{.dfn}

:   is the greater of the fraction B/C and the fraction D/E,

    where

    ::: {#675348 .NestedFormula}

    [B]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total assets of the foreign bank or other
        entity that is in respect of the particular activities that the
        foreign bank or other entity engaged in or carried on in Canada,

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total assets of the foreign bank or other
        entity that is in respect of its business that it engaged in or
        carried on in Canada,

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total revenue of the foreign bank or other
        entity that is derived from the particular activities that the
        foreign bank or other entity engaged in or carried on in Canada,
        and

    [E]{.dfn}
    :   is the part of the total revenue of the foreign bank or other
        entity that is derived from its business that it engaged in or
        carried on in Canada.
    :::
:::

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2010-71, s. 2(F)
:::
