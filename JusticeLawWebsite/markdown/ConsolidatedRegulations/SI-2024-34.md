## Order Designating the Minister of Natural Resources as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-natural-resources-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-canadian-sustainable-jobs-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CANADIAN SUSTAINABLE JOBS ACT](/eng/acts/C-23.25)

Registration 2024-07-17

Order Designating the Minister of Natural Resources as the Minister for
the Purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act

P.C. 2024-851 2024-06-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Canadian Sustainable Jobs
Act](/eng/acts/C-23.25)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#nbp_81000-3-2726_hq_1718834525){#nbp_81000-3-2726_hq_1718834525-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Natural Resources, a member of the King's
Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
