## [Income Support Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1241170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of income support payment

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 7(1) of the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit
Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7), the amount of an income support payment for any
week is \$500.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1241173 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
