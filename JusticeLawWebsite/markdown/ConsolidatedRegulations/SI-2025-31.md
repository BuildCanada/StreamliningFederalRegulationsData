## Order Transferring from the Minister of Indigenous Services to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-indigenous-services-to-the-minister-of-industry-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-northern-ontario .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-31
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of Indigenous Services to the
Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the
Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario

P.C. 2025-407 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2870_hq_1741749327){#fn_81000-3-2870_hq_1741749327-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2870_hq_1741749346){#fn_81000-3-2870_hq_1741749346-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of Indigenous Services to the Minister of
Industry the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Indigenous
Services with respect to the Federal Economic Development Agency for
Northern Ontario.
