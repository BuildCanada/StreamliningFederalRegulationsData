## Critical Habitat of the Western Chorus Frog (Pseudacris triseriata) Great Lakes / St. Lawrence -- Canadian Shield Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-western-chorus-frog-pseudacris-triseriata-great-lakes-st.-lawrence-canadian-shield-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2018-169
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2018-07-31

Critical Habitat of the Western Chorus Frog (Pseudacris triseriata)
Great Lakes / St. Lawrence -- Canadian Shield Population Order
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Western Chorus Frog ([Pseudacris triseriata]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Great Lakes / St. Lawrence -- Canadian Shield population, is
a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species in Part 3 of
Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBAHA};

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 58(5) of that Act, the competent
minister must consult with every other competent minister and whereas
the Minister of the Environment is also the Minister responsible for the
Parks Canada Agency;

And whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a
place referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBAEA} of that Act and, under
subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the
annexed Order;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDADA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Western Chorus Frog (Pseudacris triseriata) Great Lakes /
St. Lawrence -- Canadian Shield Population
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-169).

Gatineau, July 24, 2018

::: FlushLeft
Catherine McKenna
:::

::: FlushLeft
Minister of the Environment

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Western Chorus Frog ([Pseudacris triseriata]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Great Lakes / St. Lawrence -- Canadian Shield population,
which is identified in the recovery strategy for that species that is
included in the Species at Risk Public Registry, other than the portions
of that critical habitat that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a place referred to in subsection 58(2) of the
  Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} found on a reserve or any other lands that are set
  apart for the use and benefit of a band under the [Indian
  Act](/eng/acts/I-5).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
