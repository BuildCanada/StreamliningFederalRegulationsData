## Construction Starts Fee Order {#construction-starts-fee-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-552
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1981-07-03

Order Respecting the Fees to be Charged for Issuing Multiple-Unit
Residential Building Construction Starts Certificates
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister responsible for the Canada Mortgage and Housing
Corporation, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1981-1437 of 28 May,
1981[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-81-552_e_hq_10892){#fn_SOR-81-552_e_hq_10892-ID0EFBCA},
made pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), makes the annexed Order respecting the fees to be
charged for the issuance by the Corporation of certificates of
multiple-unit residential building construction starts.

  :::

Ottawa, Ontario, this 30th day of June, 1981

::: FlushRight

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871446 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Construction Starts Fee Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-552).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871448 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Where the Canada Mortgage
and Housing Corporation carries out an inspection for the purpose of
determining if a certificate may be issued by the Corporation certifying
that the construction of a multiple-unit residential building has
commenced, the person requesting the inspection and certificate shall
pay a fee of \$60 to the Corporation for each certificate requested
after August 1, 1981.
