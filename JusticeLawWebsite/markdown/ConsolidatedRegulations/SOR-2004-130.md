[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-130

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 2004-05-11

Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Certificate Regulations

P.C. 2004-629 2004-05-11

the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 7.92[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709048 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2).

## [Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709050 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certificate referred to in section 7.92 of the Act shall be in the
  form set out in Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The certificate referred to in paragraph 8(b) of the
  Act shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The certificate referred to in subsection 8.1(4) of
  the Act shall be in the form set out in Schedule 3.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709055 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** \[Repeal\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-709057 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
