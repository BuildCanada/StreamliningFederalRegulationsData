## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299468 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
    Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    paragraph (d) of the definition [[vaping
    product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act. ([substance
    de vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299473 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retail sale --- vaping products

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to every vaping product that is intended for retail
  sale in Canada, as well as to its packaging.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vaping products --- otherwise furnished

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations also apply to every vaping product
  that is intended to be otherwise furnished, in Canada, at a point of
  sale that is a retail establishment where vaping products are
  ordinarily sold, as well as to the packaging of such a vaping product.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a vaping product that is the subject of an authorization,
including a licence, issued under the [Food and Drugs
Act](/eng/acts/F-27) authorizing its sale.

## [Nicotine Concentration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299481 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standard --- maximum nicotine
concentration

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 7.2 of the Act, a vaping product must not contain
  nicotine in a concentration that exceeds 20 mg/mL when the vaping
  substance is tested using the International Organization for
  Standardization standard ISO 20714, entitled E-liquid ---
  Determination of nicotine, propylene glycol and glycerol in liquids
  used in electronic nicotine delivery devices --- Gas chromatographic
  method, as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conversion of units of measure

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The nicotine concentration expressed in mg/mL must be
  obtained by multiplying the results of the test conducted in
  accordance with ISO 20714, expressed in mg/g, by the density of the
  vaping substance, expressed in g/mL.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- vaping substance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a reference to
  "e-liquid" in ISO 20714 must be read as a reference to "vaping
  substance".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibitions --- packaging and sale

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
30.45 of the Act, a vaping product must not be packaged or sold in a
package that displays a nicotine concentration statement, referred to in
section 5 of the [Vaping Products Labelling and Packaging
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-353), that indicates that the
nicotine concentration in the vaping substance exceeds 20 mg/mL.

## [Transitional Provision, Consequential Amendments and Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1299491 .Part}

### [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1299492 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retailers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Despite these Regulations,
a retailer may, until the day that is the 15th day after the day on
which these Regulations come into force, sell a vaping product that is
packaged in a manner that is contrary to section 5.
