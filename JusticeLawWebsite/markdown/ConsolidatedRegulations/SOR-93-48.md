## Revenue Canada Technical Publication Subscription Service Fees Order {#revenue-canada-technical-publication-subscription-service-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-48
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-02-01

Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by a Person for Subscription
Services Provided by the Department of National Revenue in Respect of
Technical Publications
::::

::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955042 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Revenue Canada Technical Publication Subscription Service Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-48).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-496, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955046 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of National
    Revenue; ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[technical publications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical publications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means interpretation
    bulletins and information circulars in respect of taxation, and
    advance tax rulings, published by the Department. ([publications
    techniques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-496, s. 2
:::

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-955052 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Any person to whom the
Department provides, at that person's request, subscription services in
respect of technical publications published in the year or two years
immediately following the request shall pay to the Receiver General a
fee of \$20.00 for a one-year subscription or \$35.00 for a two-year
subscription, as the case may be.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any person to whom the
Department provides, at that person's request, the complete set of
current technical publications published prior to the request shall pay
to the Receiver General a fee of \$65.00.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-496, s. 3
