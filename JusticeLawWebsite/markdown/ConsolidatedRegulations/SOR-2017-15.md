## Hecate Strait and Queen Charlotte Sound Glass Sponge Reefs Marine Protected Areas Regulations {#hecate-strait-and-queen-charlotte-sound-glass-sponge-reefs-marine-protected-areas-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-15
:::

[OCEANS ACT](/eng/acts/O-2.4)

Registration 2017-02-13

Hecate Strait and Queen Charlotte Sound Glass Sponge Reefs Marine
Protected Areas Regulations

P.C. 2017-110 2017-02-13
::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsection 35(3) of
the [Oceans Act](/eng/acts/O-2.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDABA}, makes the annexed [Hecate
Strait and Queen Charlotte Sound Glass Sponge Reefs Marine Protected
Areas Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-15).

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EDABA){#fn_a}S.C.
  1996, c. 31
  :::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840942 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[glass sponge reefs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[glass sponge reefs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the glass sponge
      reefs that lie within the Marine Protected Areas. ([récifs
      d'éponges siliceuses]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Marine Protected Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Marine Protected Areas]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the areas of
      the sea that are designated by section 2. ([zones de protection
      marine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Geographical coordinates

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the Schedules, all geographical coordinates
  (latitude and longitude) are expressed in the North America Datum 1983
  (NAD 83) reference system.

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840950 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Marine Protected Areas

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following areas of the sea are designated as the Hecate Strait and
  Queen Charlotte Sound Glass Sponge Reefs Marine Protected Areas:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Northern Reef Marine Protected Area, depicted
    in Schedule 1 and bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn from
    points A to L, the coordinates of which are set out in that
    Schedule, and then back to point A;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Central Reefs Marine Protected Area, depicted
    in Schedule 1 and bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn from
    points M to Y, the coordinates of which are set out in that
    Schedule, and then back to point M; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Southern Reef Marine Protected Area, depicted
    in Schedule 1 and bounded by a series of rhumb lines drawn from
    points Z to EE, the coordinates of which are set out in that
    Schedule, and then back to point Z.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Seabed, subsoil and water column

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each Marine Protected Area consists of the seabed,
  the subsoil to a depth of 20 m and the water column above the seabed.

## [Marine Protected Areas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840959 .Part}

### [Northern Reef Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-840960 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Northern Reef Marine Protected Area

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Northern Reef Marine Protected Area consists of a core protection
  zone, a vertical adaptive management zone and an adaptive management
  zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Core protection zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The core protection zone consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 20 m and the water column above the seabed to a
  depth of 100 m below the sea surface. The boundaries of the core
  protection zone are represented by a series of rhumb lines drawn from
  points 1 to 12, the coordinates of which are set out in Schedule 2,
  and then back to point 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vertical adaptive management zone

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The vertical adaptive management zone consists of the
  water column that extends above the core protection zone to the sea
  surface.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptive management zone

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The adaptive management zone consists of the seabed,
  subsoil and waters of the Northern Reef Marine Protected Area that are
  not part of the core protection zone or the vertical adaptive
  management zone.

### [Central Reefs Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-840970 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Central Reefs Marine Protected Area

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Central Reefs Marine Protected Area is composed of two core protection
  zones (Zone A and Zone B), two vertical adaptive management zones and
  an adaptive management zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Core protection zone (Zone A)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Zone A consists of the seabed, the subsoil to a depth
  of 20 m and the water column above the seabed to a depth of 120 m
  below the sea surface. The boundaries of Zone A are represented by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from points 13 to 23, the coordinates of
  which are set out in Schedule 3, and then back to point 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Core protection zone (Zone B)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Zone B consists of the seabed, the subsoil to a depth
  of 20 m and the water column above the seabed to a depth of 120 m
  below the sea surface. The boundaries of Zone B are represented by a
  series of rhumb lines drawn from points 24 to 32, the coordinates of
  which are set out in Schedule 3, and then back to point 24.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vertical adaptive management zones

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The vertical adaptive management zones consist of the
  water columns that extend above Zone A and Zone B to the sea surface.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptive management zone

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The adaptive management zone consists of the seabed,
  subsoil and waters of the Central Reefs Marine Protected Area that are
  not part of the core protection zones or the vertical adaptive
  management zones.

### [Southern Reef Marine Protected Area]{.HTitleText2} {#h-840982 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Southern Reef Marine Protected Area

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Southern Reef Marine Protected Area is composed of a core protection
  zone, a vertical adaptive management zone and an adaptive management
  zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Core protection zone

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The core protection zone consists of the seabed, the
  subsoil to a depth of 20 m and the water column above the seabed to a
  depth of 146 m below the sea surface. The boundaries of the core
  protection zone are represented by a series of rhumb lines drawn from
  points 33 to 39, the coordinates of which are set out in Schedule 4,
  and then back to point 33.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vertical adaptive management zone

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The vertical adaptive management zone consists of the
  water column that extends above the core protection zone to the sea
  surface.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adaptive management zone

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The adaptive management zone consists of the seabed,
  subsoil and waters of the Southern Reef Marine Protected Area that are
  not part of the core protection zone or the vertical adaptive
  management zone.

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840992 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited in the
Marine Protected Areas to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out any activity that disturbs, damages,
  destroys or removes any living marine organism or any part of its
  habitat or is likely to do so; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} carry out any scientific research or monitoring, or
  any educational activity, unless it is part of an activity plan that
  has been approved by the Minister.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-840997 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permitted activities

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraph 6(a), the
following activities may be carried out in the Marine Protected Areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fishing in the adaptive management and vertical
  adaptive management zones if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the fishing is not likely to result in the damage,
    destruction or removal of any part of the glass sponge reefs,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the fishing is carried out in accordance with the
    [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) and its regulations,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the fishing is carried out in accordance with the
    [Coastal Fisheries Protection Act](/eng/acts/C-33) and its
    regulations,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in the case of commercial fishing in a vertical
    adaptive management zone, the fishing is carried out by means of
    midwater trawl, midwater hook and line, troll, seine or gillnet and
    the gear does not enter a core protection zone, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of recreational fishing in a vertical
    adaptive management zone, the fishing is carried out by means of
    midwater hook and line and the gear does not enter a core protection
    zone;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} navigation of vessels in the adaptive management and
  vertical adaptive management zones that is carried out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and its regulations, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} without any anchor entering a core protection
    zone;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the laying, maintenance or repair of cables in the
  adaptive management zones if the laying, maintenance or repair is not
  likely to result in the damage, destruction or removal of any part of
  the glass sponge reefs;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is carried out for the purpose of
  public safety, national defence, national security, law enforcement or
  responding to an emergency; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any activity that is part of an activity plan that
  has been approved by the Minister.

## [Activity Plan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-841012 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submission of activity plan

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may submit to the Minister an activity plan for the carrying
  out of scientific research or monitoring, or educational activities,
  in the Marine Protected Areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity plan

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The activity plan must contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person's name, address, telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the plan is submitted by an institution or
    organization, the name of the individual who will be responsible for
    the proposed activity and their title, address, telephone number and
    email address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of each vessel that the person proposes to
    use to carry out the activity, its state of registration and
    registration number, its radio call sign and the name and address of
    its owner, master and any operator;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed dates of the vessel's first entry into
    and final exit from the Marine Protected Areas, and any proposed
    alternative dates;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the geographic coordinates of the site of the
    proposed activity and a map that shows the location of the activity
    within the boundaries of the Marine Protected Areas;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of any substances that may be deposited
    during the proposed activity in the Marine Protected Areas --- other
    than substances that are authorized by the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and its regulations to be deposited in the
    navigation of a vessel --- and the quantity and concentration of
    each substance;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the proposed activity and
    its purpose;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a general description of any study, report or other
    work that is anticipated to result from the proposed activity, and
    its anticipated date of completion;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of any scientific research or
    monitoring, or educational activities, that the person has
    previously carried out in the Marine Protected Areas or anticipates
    carrying out in those areas in the future; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a description of any measures to be taken to
    monitor, avoid, minimize or mitigate any environmental effects of
    the proposed activity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval of activity plans

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must approve an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the scientific research or monitoring activities
    set out in the plan are not likely to adversely impact the
    biological, chemical or oceanographic processes that maintain or
    enhance the biodiversity, structural habitat or ecosystem function
    of the glass sponge reefs and will serve to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} increase knowledge of the biodiversity,
      structural habitat or ecosystem function of the Marine Protected
      Areas,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the management of the Marine Protected
      Areas, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} assist in the evaluation of the effectiveness
      of any measures taken to conserve and protect the Marine Protected
      Areas; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the educational activities set out in the plan are
    not likely to result in the damage, destruction or removal of any
    part of the glass sponge reefs and will serve to increase public
    awareness of the Marine Protected Areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Approval prohibited

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister must not approve
  an activity plan if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance that may be deposited during the
    proposed activity is a [[deleterious
    substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 34(1) of
    the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), unless the deposit of the
    substance is authorized under subsection 36(4) of that Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cumulative environmental effects of the
    proposed activity, in combination with any other past and current
    activities carried out in the Marine Protected Areas, are likely to
    adversely impact the biological, chemical or oceanographic processes
    that maintain or enhance the biodiversity, structural habitat or
    ecosystem function of the glass sponge reefs; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any educational activity within a core protection
    zone is proposed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline for approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister's decision in respect of an activity
  plan must be made within

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the day on which the activity plan is
    received; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if amendments to the plan are made, 60 days after
    the day on which the amended plan is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Studies, reports or other work

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  activity plan has been approved by the Minister, the person who
  submitted the plan must provide the Minister with a copy of any study,
  report or other work that results from the activity and that is
  related to the conservation and protection of the Marine Protected
  Areas.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The study, report or other work must be accompanied
  by the data that was obtained during the activity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadline

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The study, report or other work, together with the
  data, must be provided to the Minister within 90 days after the day on
  which the study, report or other work is completed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Data

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the study, report or other work is not completed
  within a period of three years after the last day of the scientific
  research or monitoring activity or educational activity, the person
  must submit the data that was obtained during the activity to the
  Minister within 90 days after that period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
