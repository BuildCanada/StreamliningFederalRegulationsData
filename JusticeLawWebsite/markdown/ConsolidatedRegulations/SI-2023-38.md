## Order Transferring from the Minister for International Development to the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-for-international-development-to-the-president-of-the-kings-privy-council-for-canada-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-pacific-economic-development-agency-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-38
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Transferring from the Minister for International Development to
the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada the Powers, Duties
and Functions in Relation to the Pacific Economic Development Agency of
Canada

P.C. 2023-763 2023-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister for International Development to the
President of the King's Privy Council for Canada the powers, duties and
functions of the Minister for International Development in relation to
the Pacific Economic Development Agency of Canada.
