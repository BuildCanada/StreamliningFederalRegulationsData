## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713455 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
    Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[NSSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NSSOIRC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Nova Scotia Sex Offender
    Information Registration Centre administered by RCMP "H" Division.
    ([Bureau d'inscription]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
    Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Report and Notification by Telephone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713460 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A sex
  offender whose main residence is located in the Province of Nova
  Scotia may make any report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or
  section 4.3 of the Act, or provide any notification required under
  section 6 of the Act, by telephone to the NSSOIRC.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, subsection (1) does not remove
  the requirement for the sex offender to report in person under
  paragraph 4.1(c) of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713464 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of Nova Scotia to collect information for the
purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer appointed to or employed by a
  municipal police force.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713468 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Persons employed at the
NSSOIRC whose duties include the registration of information are
authorized in the Province of Nova Scotia to register that information
for the purposes of the Act.

## [Registration Centres]{.HTitleText1} {#h-713470 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following locations and
each RCMP detachment in Nova Scotia are designated as registration
centres for the area of that province that they serve:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the NSSOIRC;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Office of the Cape Breton Regional Police,
  Central Division;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Office of the Halifax Regional Police Department;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Office of New Glasgow Police Service.
