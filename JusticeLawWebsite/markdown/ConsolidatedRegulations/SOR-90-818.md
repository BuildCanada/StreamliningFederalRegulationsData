## Moncton Airport Zoning Regulations {#moncton-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-818
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1990-11-29

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Moncton Airport

P.C. 1990-2542 1990-11-29
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Moncton Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on March 17th and 24th, 1990,
and in two successive issues of The Times-Transcript on March 14th and
15th, 1990, and in two successive issues of [L'Acadie
Nouvelle]{.otherLang lang="fr"} on March 13th and 14th, 1990, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 5.4 of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Moncton Airport Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-818),
C.R.C., c. 94 and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at
Moncton Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-818), in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931217 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Moncton Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-818).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931219 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Moncton Airport, in the
      Parish of Moncton, in the County of Westmorland, in the Province
      of New Brunswick; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 60 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931230 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931234 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Aeronautical Facilities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931239 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used or developed in a manner that causes interference with
any signals or communications to and from any aircraft or to and from
any facilities used to provide services relating to aeronautics.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931241 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-931243 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point, shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan
Numbers S-2021-3, S-2021-4, S-2021-9 and S-2021-10, dated March 12,
1988, is determined by measuring 655 m northerly along the centre line
of runway 02-20 from the southerly end of runway 02 and thence measuring
183 m easterly at right angles from the said centre line.

The said airport reference point has New Brunswick Double Stereographic
Grid Co-ordinates of N 758513.10 m and E 440478.38 m.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan Numbers
S-2021-0 to S-2021-12 inclusive, dated March 12, 1988, are surfaces
abutting each end of the strips associated with runways designated
06-24, 11-29 and 02-20 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 06 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 24 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 11 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 29 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m measured vertically above the elevation at
  the end of the strip;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 02 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at its intersection with the outer
  surface; said imaginary horizontal line being at right angles to the
  projected centre line of the strip and distant 1 029 m measured
  horizontally from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the said
  imaginary horizontal line being 147.9 m from the projected centre
  line; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 20 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 30 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary line drawn at its intersection with the outer surface; said
  imaginary horizontal line being at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 1 338 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 178.8 m from the projected centre line.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan Numbers
S-2021-0, S-2021-2 to S-2021-5 inclusive and S-2021-8 to S-2021-11
inclusive, dated March 12, 1988, is an imaginary surface located at a
common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that common
plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer
surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan Numbers S-2021-3,
S-2021-4, S-2021-9 and S-2021-10, dated March 12, 1988, are described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 06-24 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 995.0 m in length;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 11-29 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  2 559.0 m in length; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 02-20 is 90 m in
  width, 45 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway, and
  1 239.0 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan Numbers
S-2021-0, S-2021-3, S-2021-4, S-2021-9 and S-2021-10 dated March 12,
1988, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of
1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to
the centre line and projected centre line of the strip and extending
upward and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
transitional surface of an adjoining strip.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-outer-limits .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits of the lands to which these Regulations
apply, shown on Moncton Airport Zoning Plan Numbers S-2021-0, S-2021-2,
S-2021-6, S-2021-8, S-2021-9 and S-2021-11, dated March 12, 1988, is a
circular area with a radius of 4 000 m centred on the airport reference
point.
