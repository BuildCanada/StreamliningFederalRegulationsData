## Specified Tangible Personal Property (GST/HST) Regulations {#specified-tangible-personal-property-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-20
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Regulations Prescribing the Threshold Amount Respecting Specified
Tangible Personal Property

P.C. 1990-2729 1990-12-18
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#a_e){#a_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations
prescribing the threshold amount and percentage of the tax respecting
specified tangible personal property.

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#a_e-ID0EDBAA){#a_e}S.C.

## [Prescribed Amount]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933284 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 183 and 184 of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), the
prescribed amount in respect of specified tangible personal property is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the property is a print, an etching, a drawing,
  a painting, a sculpture or other similar work of art, \$2,000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the property is jewellery, \$2,000;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the property is a rare folio, a rare manuscript
  or a rare book, \$2,000;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the property is a stamp, the face value of the
  stamp; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the property is a coin, zero.

  ::: HistoricalNote
  - SOR/94-368, s. 4
  - SOR/99-172, s. 3
  :::
