## Order Designating the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be the Minister referred to in the Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-indian-affairs-and-northern-development-to-be-the-minister-referred-to-in-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-35
:::

[CANADIAN HIGH ARCTIC RESEARCH STATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-17.8)

Registration 2015-06-03

Order Designating the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development to be the Minister referred to in the Act

P.C. 2015-581 2015-05-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the [Canadian High Arctic
Research Station Act](/eng/acts/C-17.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947){#fn_81000-3-859-E_hq_13947-ID0EBBAA},
designates the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be
the Minister referred to in that Act.
