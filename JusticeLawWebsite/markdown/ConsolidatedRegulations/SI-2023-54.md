## Order Transferring from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department of Employment and Social Development the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Digital Service {#order-transferring-from-the-treasury-board-secretariat-to-the-department-of-employment-and-social-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-digital-service .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2023-54
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Transferring from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department
of Employment and Social Development the Control and Supervision of the
Canadian Digital Service

P.C. 2023-784 2023-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department of
Employment and Social Development the control and supervision of that
portion of the federal public administration in the Treasury Board
Secretariat known as the Canadian Digital Service.
