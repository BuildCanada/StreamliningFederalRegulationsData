## Competition Tribunal Rules {#competition-tribunal-rules .Title-of-Act}

:::

[COMPETITION TRIBUNAL ACT](/eng/acts/C-36.4)

Registration 2008-05-01

Competition Tribunal Rules

P.C. 2008-818 2008-05-01
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Competition Tribunal, pursuant to subsection 16(1) of the
[Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBDA} and subject to the
approval of the Governor in Council, hereby makes the annexed
[Competition Tribunal Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-290).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBDA){#fna_e}R.S., c. 19 (2nd Supp.),
  Part I
  :::

February 11, 2008

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 17(a) of the [Competition Tribunal
Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EFABA}, a copy of the proposed
[Competition Tribunal Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-290), substantially
in the form set out in the annexed Rules, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on May 26, 2007 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Registrar with respect to the proposed
Rules;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to section 16 of
the [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby approves the
annexed [Competition Tribunal Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-290) made
by the Competition Tribunal.
:::

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-743819 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743820 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Competition
    Act](/eng/acts/C-34). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who files an
    application under Part VII.1 or Part VIII of the Act or a person who
    files a reference under section 124.2 of the
    Act. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified copy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a document means a copy of
    the document certified by the Registrar or a person designated by
    the Registrar. ([copie certifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chairperson]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the judicial member
    designated to be Chairman of the Tribunal under subsection 4(1) of
    the [Competition Tribunal
    Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4). ([président]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commissioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commissioner of
    Competition appointed under subsection 7(1) of the
    Act. ([commissaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[consent agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[consent agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement made
    under section 74.12, 105 or 106.1 of the Act, the terms of which
    have been agreed on by the parties. ([consentement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[counsel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member of the bar of a
    province. ([avocat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes pleadings, affidavits and
    any correspondence, memorandum, book, plan, map, drawing, diagram,
    pictorial or graphic work, photograph, film, microform, sound
    recording, videotape, machine readable record, and any other
    documentary material, regardless of physical form or
    characteristics, and any copy or portion of that
    material. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hearing in which
    documents are provided in an electronic form to the registry and are
    presented electronically in the course of the hearing. ([audience
    électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[electronic transmission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[electronic transmission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    transmission by electronic mail (e-mail) or via the Tribunal
    website. ([transmission électronique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[file]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to file with the
    Registrar. ([déposer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intervenor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person granted leave to intervene by the
      Tribunal in accordance with rule 46;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an attorney general who intervenes under
      section 88 or 101 of the Act; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commissioner who intervenes under
      section 103.2 or subsection 124.2(3) of the
      Act. ([intervenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[originating document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[originating document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means either a notice
    of application, a notice of reference, or an application for leave
    under section 103.1 of the Act. ([acte introductif
    d'instance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[paper hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[paper hearing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hearing in which
    documents are provided in paper form to the registry and are
    presented in paper form in the course of the hearing. ([audience sur
    pièces]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant or a
    respondent. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a corporation, a partnership
    and an unincorporated association. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reference]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the reference of a question
    to the Tribunal for determination under section 124.2 of the
    Act. ([renvoi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Registrar of the
    Tribunal. ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registry]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Registry of the
    Tribunal. ([greffe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[respondent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is named as a
    respondent in a notice of application. ([défendeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Rules Applicable to All Proceedings]{.HTitleText2} {#h-743846 .Subheading}

#### [Dispensing with Compliance]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743847 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may dispense with, vary or supplement the application of any
  of these Rules in a particular case in order to deal with all matters
  as informally and expeditiously as the circumstances and
  considerations of fairness permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Urgent matters

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a party considers that the circumstances require
  that an application be heard urgently or within a specified period,
  the party may request that the Tribunal give directions about how to
  proceed.

#### [Time Limits]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743853 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided
in these Rules, time limits under these Rules or under an order of the
Tribunal shall be calculated under sections 26 to 30 of the
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculating time limits

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  time for doing an act expires on a holiday or a Saturday, the act may
  be done on the next day that is not a holiday or a Saturday.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit less than six days

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a time limit is less than six days, holidays and
  Saturdays shall not be included in the calculation of the time limit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying time limits

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The time limits prescribed
by these Rules may only be shortened or extended by an order or a
direction of a judicial member.

#### [Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743863 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Memorandum of fact and law

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where in these Rules a
reference is made to a memorandum of fact and law, the memorandum of
fact and law shall contain a table of contents and, in consecutively
numbered paragraphs,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of fact;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the points in issue;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the submissions;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the order sought, including
  any order concerning costs;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a list of the authorities, statutes and regulations
  to be referred to; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an appendix, and if necessary as a separate document,
  a copy of the authorities (or relevant excerpts) as well as a copy of
  any statutory or regulatory provisions cited or relied on that have
  not been reproduced in another party's memorandum.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar or the person designated by the Registrar may issue a writ
  of subpoena for the attendance of witnesses and the production of
  documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In blank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar may issue a writ of subpoena in blank
  and the person to whom it is issued shall complete it and may include
  any number of names.

#### [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743877 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Originating document

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service
  of an originating document shall be effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, by leaving a
    certified copy of the originating document with the individual;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a partnership, by leaving a
    certified copy of the originating document with one of the partners
    during business hours;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by leaving a
    certified copy of the originating document with an officer of the
    corporation or with a person apparently in charge of the head office
    or of a branch of the corporation in Canada during business hours;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Commissioner, by leaving a
    certified copy of the originating document at the Commissioner's
    office during business hours; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person referred to in any of
    paragraphs (a) to (d) who is represented by counsel, by leaving a
    certified copy of the originating document with the counsel who
    accepts service of the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative manner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is unable to serve an originating
  document in a manner described in subrule (1), the person may apply to
  a judicial member for an order setting out another manner for
  effecting service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person who obtains an order made under
  subrule (2) shall serve the order on each person named in the
  originating document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed served

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a document has been
served in a manner not authorized by these Rules or by an order of the
Tribunal, the Tribunal may, on motion, order that a document be deemed
validly served if it is satisfied that the document came to the notice
of the person to be served or that it would have come to that person's
notice except for the person's avoidance of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other documents

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document, other than an
  originating document, on a person who is not represented by counsel
  shall be effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a manner set out in any of paragraphs 8(1)(a) to
    (d), except that the copy of the document need not be certified;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the document at the usual or
    last known address of the person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the document to the person by
    facsimile transmission in accordance with subrule (3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the document to the person by
    registered mail or by messenger or courier service, and by obtaining
    an acknowledgement of receipt signed and dated by the person or by
    someone on behalf of the person;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by electronic transmission of the document to the
    person, if the person agrees and, within 24 hours, sends an
    acknowledgement of receipt; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in any other manner that the Tribunal may order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person represented

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Service of a document, other than an originating
  document, on a person who is represented by counsel shall be effected

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the manner set out in paragraph 8(1)(e), except
    that the copy of the document need not be certified;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by leaving a copy of the document at the counsel's
    business address;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the document to the counsel by
    facsimile transmission in accordance with subrule (3);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of the document to the counsel by
    registered mail and by obtaining an acknowledgement of receipt
    signed and dated by the counsel or by someone on behalf of the
    counsel;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} by electronic transmission to the counsel, who
    shall within 24 hours send an acknowledgement of receipt; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in any other manner that the Tribunal may order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by fax

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by facsimile transmission
  shall include a cover page setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number of the
    sender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person or the counsel to whom a
    copy of the document is sent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages transmitted, including
    the cover page; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and telephone number of the person to
    contact if transmission problems occur.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by electronic transmission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A document that is served by electronic transmission
  shall be accompanied by an electronic message setting out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and e-mail
    address of the sender;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person or the counsel to whom the
    document is sent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the title of the document transmitted; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and e-mail address of
    the person to contact if transmission problems occur.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Proof
  of service shall be made by an affidavit of service in accordance with
  the form set out in Schedule 1, for an originating document, or
  Schedule 2, for a document other than an originating document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}By certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Proof of service of a document other than an
  originating document can be made by a certificate by a counsel or the
  person designated by the counsel in accordance with the form set out
  in Schedule 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service by mail

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of service by registered mail, a signed
  and dated acknowledgement of receipt shall be attached to the
  affidavit of service or the counsel's certificate, as the case may be.

#### [Filing of Documents]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743930 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic filing

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  parties shall file their documents by electronic transmission.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, if it considers that it is
  justified in the circumstances, allow paper filing and facsimile
  filing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Original document

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents filed by electronic transmission shall
  constitute the original for both electronic and paper hearings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paper copies

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Parties to paper hearings shall provide to the
  registry five paper copies of the documents filed by electronic
  transmission within 24 hours after that filing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing by intervenor

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** An intervenor shall use
the same medium (electronic or paper) as the parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paper filing

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), only those
  documents that are printed on 21.5 cm x 28 cm (letter size) paper and
  that have numbered pages may be filed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-standard format

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A document that is not printed on 21.5 cm x 28 cm
  paper and that cannot reasonably be converted to that format by the
  person filing it may be filed in its existing format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facsimile filing

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  document is filed by facsimile transmission, it is deemed to be the
  original.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing by facsimile not allowed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following documents shall not be filed by
  facsimile transmission:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an originating document and any documents
    accompanying it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document that is filed in multiple copies; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a document that contains, to which is appended or
    that is accompanied by a document containing confidential
    information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cover page

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A document filed by
facsimile transmission shall include a cover page that satisfies the
requirements of subrule 10(3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing after 17:00 hours

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A document filed by
facsimile transmission after 17:00 hours Ottawa local time is deemed to
be filed on the next day that is not a holiday or Saturday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format for electronic filing

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  electronic version of a document in PDF (Portable Document Format) or
  any other format allowed by the Tribunal shall be filed in a manner
  directed by the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filed electronically

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All documents filed by electronic transmission shall
  be electronically time stamped.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filed after 17:00 hours

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any document transmitted electronically after
  17:00 hours Ottawa local time is deemed to be filed on the next day
  that is not a holiday or Saturday.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Irregularity or defect

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** At any time before
judgment is given in a proceeding, the Tribunal may draw the attention
of a party to any irregularity or defect relating to an electronic
version of a document and permit the party to remedy it on any
conditions that the Tribunal considers fair.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic sworn statement or solemn
affirmation

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  statement made under oath or solemn affirmation may be filed
  electronically, by filing a scanned version of the document that
  includes a handwritten signature and the following: "*The document
  that is being electronically submitted to the Tribunal is an
  electronic version of a paper document that has been signed by the
  affiant. The signed document in paper copy is available and will be
  produced if requested by the Tribunal.*"

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maintenance of document

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The document referred to in subrule (1) must be
  maintained in paper form by the party or intervenor filing the
  document until one year after all periods for appeals expire.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provision of original

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Upon request of the Tribunal, the party or intervenor
  filing the statement made under oath or solemn affirmation must
  provide the original signed document for review.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative means for filing

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Upon the request of a party or intervenor, the
  Tribunal may order a different method for the electronic filing of a
  statement made under oath or a solemn affirmation, or may order other
  means for filing the document.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic certified copy

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** If a document is filed
electronically, and a certified copy of the document is requested from
the Tribunal, the Tribunal may provide an electronic copy of the
document stamped "certified".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public access

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any
confidentiality order under rule 66, the public is entitled to access
the documents filed or received in evidence on the public record, in the
format in which they were received by the registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Without confidentiality order

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A party or intervenor who
wishes to assert confidentiality in a document to be filed that is not
covered by a confidentiality order shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} file a public version of the document that does not
  include the confidential information;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the registry with a version of the document
  marked "confidential" that includes and identifies the confidential
  information that has been deleted from the public version filed under
  paragraph (a); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} bring a motion under rule 66 for an order allowing it
  to file the confidential version.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}With confidentiality order

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A party or intervenor who
wishes to file a document containing information that has already been
made subject to a confidentiality order under rule 66 shall file a
public version that does not include the confidential information and a
confidential version with each page clearly marked "confidential". The
confidential version shall identify the confidential material that has
been deleted from the public version and the date of the relevant
confidentiality order.

#### [Publication of Notice]{.HTitleText3} {#h-743988 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Registrar shall, as soon as the notice of application under Part VIII
  of the Act has been filed, publish a notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/);
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} over a period of two weeks, in at least two issues
    of at least two daily newspapers designated by the Chairperson or a
    judicial member designated by the Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice referred to in subrule (1) shall state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that an application for an order has been made to
    the Tribunal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of each person against whom or in respect
    of whom the order is sought;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the order sought;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the notice of application and accompanying
    documents may be examined at the office of the Registrar; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which a motion for leave to
    intervene must be filed.

#### [Discontinuance or Withdrawal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744001 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Discontinuance

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  applicant may discontinue all or part of an application at any time
  before a final determination of the application by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of discontinuance

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall serve a notice of discontinuance
  on each party and on each intervenor and file the notice with proof of
  service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent who has filed a response may withdraw all or part of the
  response at any time before a final determination of the application
  by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of withdrawal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The respondent shall serve a notice of withdrawal on
  each party and on any intervenor and file the notice with proof of
  service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** If a party withdraws from
or discontinues the proceedings, the Tribunal may award costs in
accordance with section 8.1 of the [Competition Tribunal
Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4).

#### [Hearings]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744014 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hearings open to the public

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** Subject to rule 30,
hearings shall be open to the public.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}In-camera hearings

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party, an intervenor or a person interested in the proceedings may
  request that all or a portion of a hearing not be open to the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of request

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who makes the request shall advise the
  Tribunal of the reasons for the request, including details of the
  specific, direct harm that would allegedly result from conducting the
  hearing or a portion of the hearing in public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of the Tribunal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, if it is of the opinion that there
  are valid reasons for a hearing not to be open to the public, make any
  order that it deems appropriate.

#### [Practice and Procedure]{.HTitleText3} {#h-744024 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Composition of the Tribunal

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 10
and 11 of the [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4) and for the
purposes of these Rules, the Tribunal shall consist of one or more
members designated by the Chairperson, at least one of whom is a
judicial member.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case record in electronic format

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** The original and official
case record of an electronic hearing shall be kept by the Tribunal only
in electronic format.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Practice directions

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may issue practice directions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technology

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may give directions requiring the use of
  any electronic or digital means of communication, storage or retrieval
  of information, or any other technology it considers appropriate to
  facilitate the conduct of a hearing or case management conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Questions as to practice or procedure

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If, in
  the course of proceedings, a question arises as to the practice or
  procedure to be followed in cases not provided for by these Rules, the
  practice and procedure set out in the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106) may be followed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal may direct

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a person is uncertain as to the practice or
  procedure to be followed, the Tribunal may give directions about how
  to proceed.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Contested Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744039 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744040 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Part

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to all
applications to the Tribunal, except applications for interim or
temporary orders (Part 4), applications for specialization agreements
(Part 5), applications for leave under section 103.1 of the Act (Part 8)
and applications for a loan order (Part 9).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of application

- **[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application shall be made by filing a notice of application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of application shall be signed by or on
  behalf of the applicant and shall set out, in numbered paragraphs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sections of the Act under which the application
    is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of each person against whom an
    order is sought;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds for the
    application and of the material facts on which the applicant relies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the economic theory of the
    case, if any, except in the case of an application made under
    Part VII.1 of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the particulars of the order sought; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the applicant intends to
    use in the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of notice

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant shall, within five days after a notice of application is
  filed, serve the notice on each respondent.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall, within five days after the
  service of the notice of application, file proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  respondent who wishes to oppose the application shall, within 45 days
  after being served with the notice of application,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve a response on the applicant and on any other
    respondent; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the response with proof of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The response shall set out, in numbered paragraphs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds on which the
    application is opposed and of the material facts on which the person
    opposing the application relies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial of each ground and of each
    material fact relevant to each ground set out in the notice of
    application;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the economic theory of the
    case, if any, except in the case of an application made under
    Part VII.1 of the Act; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the person opposing the
    application intends to use in the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant may, within 14 days after being served with a response in
  accordance with subrule 38(1), serve a reply on the respondent and on
  each other party and shall file the reply with proof of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A reply shall set out an admission or denial of each
  ground and of each material fact relevant to each ground set out in
  the response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to file

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant does not file a reply, the applicant
  is deemed to have denied each ground and each material fact relevant
  to each ground set out in the response.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timetable for disposition of application

- **[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  party shall, within 14 days after the expiry of the period for filing
  a response, consult with the other parties and, if a timetable is
  agreed, file the proposed timetable for the disposition of the
  application, including a suggested start date, duration and place for
  the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timetable not agreed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the parties cannot agree on a timetable, each
  party shall serve on the other parties a proposed timetable and file
  it with proof of service within the period set out in subrule (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order in default of response

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person has not filed a response within the period set out in
  subrule 38(1), the applicant may move that the Tribunal issue the
  order sought in the notice of application against the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On a motion in accordance with subrule (1), the
  Tribunal shall, if it is satisfied that the notice of application was
  served in accordance with these Rules and it has heard any evidence
  that it may require, make any order that it deems appropriate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall, as soon as an order is made,
  serve the order on the respondent and on each other party.

### [Intervention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744089 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for leave to intervene

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** A motion under
subsection 9(3) of the [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4) for
leave to intervene shall be filed within 10 days after the end of the
period for filing a response.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service and filing motion

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion for leave to intervene shall be made by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serving on each of the parties a motion for leave
    to intervene and an affidavit setting out the facts on which the
    motion is based; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} filing the motion and the affidavit with proof of
    service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A motion for leave to intervene shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the proceedings in which the person
    making the motion wishes to intervene;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of that person;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the matters in issue that
    affect that person and the unique or distinct perspective that the
    person will bring to the proceeding;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the competitive consequences
    arising from the matters referred to in paragraph (c) with respect
    to which that person wishes to make representations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party, if any, whose position that
    person intends to support;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the official language to be used by that person at
    the hearing of the motion and, if leave is granted, in the
    proceedings; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a description of how that person proposes to
    participate in the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition without hearing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person filing a motion for leave to intervene may
  request in writing that the Tribunal dispose of the motion without a
  hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[44]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-44 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party served with a motion for leave to intervene may, within 14 days
  after that service, serve a response to the motion on the person
  making the motion and on each of the parties and shall file any
  response to the motion with proof of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response to a motion for leave to intervene shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal with the matters raised in the motion; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} state whether the party filing the response
    considers that a hearing should be held to determine the motion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

**[[45]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-45 .sectionLabel}** A person making a motion
for leave to intervene may, within seven days after the service of the
response referred to in rule 44, serve a reply on each of the parties
and shall file the reply with proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition

- **[[46]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-46 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Tribunal is of the opinion that a hearing should be held to determine
  a motion for leave to intervene, the motion shall be disposed of at a
  time and in a manner determined by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may allow a motion for leave to
  intervene, with or without conditions, or refuse the motion.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervention allowed

**[[47]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-47 .sectionLabel}** If a motion for leave to
intervene is allowed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar shall send to the intervenor a list of
  all documents filed in the proceedings before or on the day on which
  the motion for leave to intervene was allowed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, the intervenor may obtain copies of the
  documents on the list from the Registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} each party and each other intervenor shall serve on
  the intervenor any document that is filed by them after the day on
  which the motion for leave to intervene was allowed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} access by an intervenor to a document filed or
  received in evidence is subject to any relevant confidentiality order
  of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[48]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-48 .sectionLabel}** Any document to be filed
by an intervenor shall be served on each party and each other intervenor
and shall be filed with proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervention by attorney general of a
province

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  notice of application relating to an application under section 86, 87
  or 92 of the Act is filed, the Registrar shall serve the notice on the
  attorney general of each province.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date for filing notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall inform the attorney general of
  each province of the date on or before which any notice of
  intervention under rule 50 must be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of intervention

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  attorney general of a province who decides to intervene in any
  proceedings before the Tribunal under section 86, 87 or 92 of the Act
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve a notice of intervention on each party; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the notice with proof of service within
    10 days after the expiry of the period for filing a response.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of intervention shall set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the proceedings in which the attorney
    general is intervening;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the nature of the interest
    of the attorney general in the proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the matters in respect of
    which the attorney general will make representations on behalf of
    the province;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the party, if any, whose position the
    attorney general intends to support; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the attorney general
    intends to use in the proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve the notice of intervention
  on each other intervenor as soon as it is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents

**[[51]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-51 .sectionLabel}** If a notice of
intervention is filed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar shall send to the attorney general a
  list of all documents filed in the proceedings before or on the day on
  which the notice of intervention was filed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, the attorney general may obtain copies of
  the documents on the list from the Registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} each party and each other intervenor shall serve on
  the attorney general any document filed by them after the day on which
  the notice of intervention was filed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} access by the attorney general to a document filed or
  received in evidence is subject to any relevant confidentiality order
  of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Participation of an attorney general

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 57(4) of the
  [Federal Courts Act](/eng/acts/F-7), the participation of an attorney
  general who has filed a notice of intervention is restricted to
  attending and making submissions at motions, case management
  conferences and the hearing of the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion for leave to participate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The attorney general may at any time serve and file
  with proof of service a motion for leave to participate in the
  proceedings in a manner other than that set out in subrule (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of documents

**[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** Any document to be filed
by an attorney general shall be served on each party and any other
intervenor and shall be filed with proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Intervention by the Commissioner

- **[[54]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-54 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Commissioner intervenes in a proceeding under section 103.2 or
  subsection 124.2(3) of the Act, the Commissioner shall file a notice
  of intervention that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the title of the proceedings in which the
    Commissioner is intervening; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the matters on which the
    Commissioner wishes to make representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve the notice of intervention
  on each party as soon as it is filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Commissioner's access to documents

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** If a notice of
intervention is filed by the Commissioner,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Registrar shall, within five days after the
  filing of the notice, send to the Commissioner a list of all documents
  filed in the proceedings before or on the day on which the notice of
  intervention was filed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on request, the Commissioner may obtain copies of any
  of the documents on the list from the Registrar;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} each party shall serve on the Commissioner any
  document that is filed by them after the day on which the notice of
  intervention is filed; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} access by the Commissioner to a document filed or
  received in evidence is subject to any relevant confidentiality order
  of the Tribunal.

### [Admissions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744175 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requests for admissions

**[[56]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-56 .sectionLabel}** A party may, after
pleadings have been closed but not later than 25 days before the
commencement of the hearing, request that another party admit a fact or
the authenticity of a document by serving a request to admit, in
form 255 of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106), on
that party, with any modifications that the circumstances require.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed admissions

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party who is served with a request to admit is deemed to admit the
  truth of a fact or the authenticity of a document set out in the
  request to admit unless that party serves a response to the request in
  form 256 of the [Federal Courts Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106)
  within 20 days after service of the request and denies the admission,
  setting out the grounds for the denial, with any modifications that
  the circumstances require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Admissions for purposes of the
  proceedings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party is deemed, for the purposes of the proceeding
  only, to admit the truth of the facts or the authenticity of the
  documents mentioned in the request, unless the party's response

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} specifically denies the truth of a fact or the
    authenticity of a document mentioned in the request; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} refuses to admit the truth of a fact or the
    authenticity of a document and sets out the reason for the refusal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

**[[58]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-58 .sectionLabel}** If a party denies or
refuses to admit the truth of a fact or the authenticity of a document
after receiving a request to admit, and the fact or authenticity of the
document is subsequently proved at the hearing, the Tribunal may take
the denial or refusal into account in exercising its discretion
respecting costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of admission

**[[59]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-59 .sectionLabel}** An admission made in
response to a request to admit, a deemed admission under rule 57 or an
admission in a pleading may be withdrawn on consent of the other party
or with leave of the Tribunal.

### [Discovery]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744189 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit of documents

- **[[60]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-60 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant and each respondent who has filed a response shall, within
  the time prescribed at a case management conference, serve an
  affidavit of documents on each other party.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An affidavit of documents shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list identifying the documents that are relevant
    to any matter in issue and that are or were in the possession, power
    or control of the party;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any claim that a document is confidential or
    contains confidential information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any claim that a document is privileged; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of the grounds for each claim of
    privilege.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of the Tribunal

**[[61]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-61 .sectionLabel}** Upon the motion of a
party who has served an affidavit of documents and who opposes a claim
for privilege of another party, the Tribunal may inspect the document
and determine the validity of the claim.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of deemed undertaking

- **[[62]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-62 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  rule applies to evidence obtained during documentary, written and oral
  discovery and information obtained from that evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed undertaking

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} All parties and their counsel are deemed to undertake
  not to use evidence or information to which this rule applies for any
  purposes other than those of the proceeding in which the evidence was
  obtained.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subrule (2) does not prohibit

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a use to which the person who disclosed the
    evidence consents;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the use, for any purpose, of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} evidence that is filed with the Tribunal,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} evidence that is given or referred to during a
      hearing; or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} information obtained from evidence referred to
      in subparagraph (i) or (ii),

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the use of evidence obtained in one proceeding, or
    information obtained from such evidence, to impeach the testimony of
    a witness in another proceeding, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the use of evidence or information in a subsequent
    Tribunal proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If satisfied that the interest of justice outweighs
  any prejudice that would result to a party who disclosed evidence, the
  Tribunal may, on motion, order that the deemed undertaking referred to
  in subrule (2) does not apply to the evidence or to information
  obtained from it, and may impose any terms and give any directions
  that are just.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Supplementary affidavit

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** A party who has served an
affidavit of documents and who comes into possession or control of or
obtains power over a relevant document, or who becomes aware that the
affidavit of documents is inaccurate or deficient, shall as soon as
possible serve a supplementary affidavit of documents listing the
document or correcting the inaccuracy or deficiency.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination for discovery

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Examination for discovery shall
  occur as of right.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of the Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may, in case management, make rulings to
  deal with the timing, duration, scope and form of the discovery as
  well as the appropriate person to be discovered.

### [Access to Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744224 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to documents

**[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any
confidentiality order under rule 66, a party who has served an affidavit
of documents on another party shall allow the other party to inspect and
make copies of the documents listed in the affidavit, unless those
documents are subject to a claim for privilege or are not within the
party's possession, power or control.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Confidentiality order

- **[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may order that a document or information in a document be
  treated as confidential and make any order that it deems appropriate,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} upon the motion of a party who has served an
    affidavit of documents; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} upon the motion of a party or intervenor who has
    filed or will file the document.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, the Tribunal may issue a
  single confidentiality order to cover the documents or information
  under paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of motion

**[[67]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-67 .sectionLabel}** The party or intervenor
making a motion referred to in rule 66 shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} include in the grounds for the motion details of the
  specific, direct harm that would allegedly result from unrestricted
  disclosure of the document or information; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} include in the motion a draft confidentiality order
  including the following elements, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the document or information or the
    category of documents or information for which the person seeks the
    confidentiality order,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the person or category of
    persons who are entitled to have access to the confidential document
    or information,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any document or information or category of
    documents or information to be made available to the person or
    category of persons referred to in subparagraph (ii),

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any written confidentiality agreement to be signed
    by the person or persons referred to in subparagraph (ii) and the
    provisions of that agreement,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the number of copies of any confidential document
    to be provided to the person or persons referred to in
    subparagraph (ii) and any limitation on subsequent reproduction of
    that document by that person or those persons, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the disposal of the confidential document
    following the final disposition of the proceeding.

### [Pre-hearing Disclosure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744244 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List of documents and witness statements

- **[[68]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-68 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant shall, at least 60 days before the commencement of the
  hearing, serve on every other party and on all intervenors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of documents on which the applicant intends
    to rely at the hearing, noting any waivers of privilege claimed in
    regard to those documents; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} witness statements setting out the lay witnesses'
    evidence in chief in full.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of witness statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the parties otherwise agree, the witness
  statements shall include only fact evidence that could be given orally
  by the witness together with admissible documents as attachments or
  references to those documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[69]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-69 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  respondent shall, at least 30 days before the commencement of the
  hearing, serve in response on every other party and on all intervenors

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a list of documents on which the respondent intends
    to rely at the hearing, noting any waivers of privilege claimed in
    regard to those documents; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} witness statements setting out the lay witnesses'
    evidence in chief in full.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of witness statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the parties otherwise agree, the witness
  statements shall include only fact evidence that could be given orally
  by the witness together with admissible documents as attachments or
  references to those documents.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

least 15 days before the commencement of the hearing, serve in reply on
every other party and on all intervenors

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any additional reply documents on which the applicant
  intends to rely at the hearing as a result of the respondent's
  disclosure, and any waiver of privilege; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any new reply witness statements or additions to
  existing witness statements to deal with any issues raised in the
  reply.

### [Evidence at the Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744263 .Subheading}

orders otherwise.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records to be admitted in evidence

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information under par. 11(1)(a) of the

obtained under paragraph 11(1)(a) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in chief

- **[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  documents.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness statements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Witness statements of lay witnesses shall be provided

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal may read

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may read the material provided, unless a

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Witness must attend

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A witness statement may be received in evidence at
  the hearing only if the witness is in attendance and available for

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence in chief

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The lay witness may be examined in chief for the
  purpose of summarizing or highlighting the evidence contained in the
  statement.

### [Expert Evidence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744289 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report

- **[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At

  any intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Responding report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 30 days before the commencement of the
  hearing, a respondent may serve a responding expert report on each
  other party and any intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply report

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The applicant may, at least 15 days before the
  commencement of the hearing, serve an expert reply report on each
  other party and any intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of report

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A report referred to in any of subrules (1) to (3)
  shall include a full statement of the evidence of the expert witness,
  the expert's qualifications as an expert and a list of the sources and
  documents relied upon in the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expert report provided to the registry

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise ordered during case management, a report referred to in
  rule 77 shall be provided to the registry with proof of service at
  least 10 days before the commencement of the hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal may read

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may read the report provided, unless a
  party makes a valid objection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The report shall not form part of the record until it
  is received in evidence at the hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of expert witness

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** A report referred to in
rule 77 shall not be read aloud at the hearing but the expert witness
may be examined in chief for the purpose of summarizing or highlighting
the evidence contained in the report and may be cross-examined and
re-examined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal-appointed expert

- **[[80]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-80 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may, at any time, by order appoint one or more independent
  experts to inquire into and report on any question of fact or opinion
  relevant to an issue in a proceeding.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agreed upon expert

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The parties may jointly recommend an expert to the
  Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submissions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The parties may make submissions about the terms of
  the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of order

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The order shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the expert being appointed and the
    expert's qualifications;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the instructions given to the expert with respect
    to the preparation of the report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the questions to be posed to the expert;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the report of the expert is to be
    provided to the Tribunal;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the nature and extent of the expert's participation
    in the proceeding; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the remuneration to be paid to the expert.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of report

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar shall serve a copy of the report on
  every party and any intervenor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case record

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The report shall be made part of the case record.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} Any party may file a written response to the expert's
  report and may examine the expert. The order and nature of such
  examinations shall be determined by the Tribunal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Further or supplementary report

  [(8)  The]{.lawlabel} Tribunal may order the expert to make a further
  or supplementary report, and subrules (4) to (7) apply to that report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability for payment

  [(9)]{.lawlabel} The liability for payment of the remuneration of the
  expert shall be determined by the Tribunal at any time after the
  conclusion of the hearing following receipt of submissions on that
  issue.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Motions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744333 .Part}

### [Informal Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744334 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informal procedure

- **[[81]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-81 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Except
  in the case of a motion for summary disposition, if these Rules
  provide that relief shall be sought by way of motion, a party may
  begin by informally requesting relief by sending a letter to the
  registry and serving the letter on the other parties and on any
  intervenor, who shall respond promptly.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal direction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may issue a direction resolving a matter
  for which relief is requested under subrule (1) or direct the party to
  proceed by way of motion.

### [Formal Procedure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744340 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[82]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-82 .sectionLabel}** Rules 83 to 88 apply to
all motions except for a motion for leave to intervene referred to in
rule 42 or a motion for summary disposition referred to in rule 89.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

- **[[83]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-83 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion shall be commenced by notice of motion, setting out the grounds
  for the motion and the order sought.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of motion shall be accompanied by any
  supporting affidavits and other admissible evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The moving party shall serve the notice of motion on
  each other party and any intervenors and file the notice of motion
  with proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition without hearing

- **[[84]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-84 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  moving party may request in writing that the Tribunal dispose of the
  motion without a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disposition with a hearing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a hearing is to be held, the notice of motion
  shall indicate that the motion is returnable at a date and time and in
  a manner directed by the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service of response

- **[[85]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-85 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party or intervenor served with a notice of motion may, no later than
  seven days after being served, serve on the moving party and on each
  other party and intervenor a response stating the grounds on which the
  motion is opposed and any supporting affidavits.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The response and supporting affidavits, if any, shall
  be filed with proof of service within the period set out in
  subrule (1).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without a hearing

**[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** The responding party may
request in writing that the Tribunal dispose of the motion without a
hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence and memorandum

**[[87]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-87 .sectionLabel}** The moving party and the
responding party shall, no later than 10 days after the service of the
response, serve on each other party and on any intervenors and file with
proof of service

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} any supplementary evidence to be relied on by the
  party, including transcripts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of fact and law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony by affidavit

- **[[88]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-88
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrule (2), testimony on
  a motion shall be by affidavit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Oral testimony

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The judicial member designated to preside at the
  hearing of a motion may, before or during the hearing, grant leave for
  oral testimony in relation to an issue raised in the notice of motion.

### [Motion for Summary Disposition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-744371 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of motion

- **[[89]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-89 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  motion for summary disposition under subsection 9(4) of the
  [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4) shall be commenced by a
  notice of motion, which shall set out the grounds for the motion and
  the order sought.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party to an application may bring a motion for
  summary disposition under subsection 9(4) of the [Competition Tribunal
  Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4) after the respondent has filed a response to
  the application and at a time that enables the moving party to meet
  the deadline for the hearing of motions prescribed during a case
  management conference.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of motion shall be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of fact and law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any supporting affidavits and other admissible
    evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The moving party shall serve the notice of motion and
  the accompanying documents referred to in subrule (3) on each other
  party and any intervenors and shall file those documents with proof of
  service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  party served with a motion for summary disposition may, no later than
  10 days after being served, serve a response on the moving party and
  on each other party and any intervenors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The response shall be accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of fact and law; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any supporting affidavits and other admissible
    evidence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The response and accompanying documents referred to
  in subrule (2) shall be filed with proof of service within the period
  set out in subrule (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Genuine basis for application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A response to a motion for summary disposition shall
  not rest merely on allegations or denials of the pleadings of the
  moving party, but must set out specific facts showing that there is a
  genuine basis for the application or the response to the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Testimony

**[[91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise ordered,
there shall be no oral testimony on a motion for summary disposition.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of the Tribunal

**[[92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-92 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may dismiss
or allow the application in whole or in part in accordance with
subsection 9(5) of the [Competition Tribunal Act](/eng/acts/C-36.4).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion --- refused or granted in part

**[[93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-93 .sectionLabel}** If a motion for summary
disposition is refused or is granted only in part, the Tribunal may make
an order specifying any issues that are not in dispute and defining the
issues to be determined.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Motion refused

**[[94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-94 .sectionLabel}** If a motion for summary
disposition is refused, the moving party may not make a further motion
under rule 89 without leave of the Tribunal.

- **[[103]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall file the reply with proof of
  service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deemed denied

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a memorandum of fact and law.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to the Commissioner

**[[112]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-112 .sectionLabel}** The parties shall,
within five days after a notice of application for leave to refer a
question is filed, serve the notice of the application for leave on the
Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Tribunal

- **[[113]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-113 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may grant an application for leave to refer a question, with
  or without conditions, or refuse the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave granted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If leave is granted, a notice of reference shall be
  filed, accompanied by the reference record, in accordance with
  rule 108.

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Private Access]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744522 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Part

**[[114]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-114 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
applications for leave under subsection 103.1(1) of the Act and to
consent agreements filed by persons other than the Commissioner.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for leave

- **[[115]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-115 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application under subsection 103.1(1) of the Act for leave to make an
  application under section 75 or 77 of the Act shall be made by filing
  an application for leave including an affidavit setting out the facts
  in support of the proposed application, a proposed notice of
  application and a memorandum of fact and law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proposed notice of application under section 75
  or 77 of the Act shall set out the information referred to in
  subrule 36(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

- **[[116]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-116 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  applicant shall, within five days after the application for leave is
  filed, serve a copy of the application for leave on each person
  against whom an order is sought and on the Commissioner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall, within five days after the
  service of the copy of the application for leave, file proof of
  service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification by the Commissioner

**[[117]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-117 .sectionLabel}** The certification by
the Commissioner under subsection 103.1(3) of the Act shall be made by
filing a letter.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice by the Tribunal

**[[118]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-118 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal shall,
within five days after receiving the Commissioner's certification,
notify the applicant, the Commissioner and any person against whom an
order is sought under section 75 or 77 of the Act as to whether the
hearing of the application for leave is precluded by the operation of
subsection 103.1(4) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations in writing

- **[[119]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-119 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person served with an application for leave referred to in rule 115
  who wishes to oppose the application shall, within 15 days after
  receiving the Tribunal's notice under rule 118,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve a copy of their representations in writing on
    the applicant, on any other person against whom the order is sought
    and on the Commissioner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the representations with proof of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representations in writing shall contain a memorandum
  of fact and law and shall set out the official language the person
  opposing the application intends to use.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit evidence

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Representations in writing shall not contain
  affidavit evidence, except with leave of the Tribunal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reply

**[[120]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-120 .sectionLabel}** The person making an
application for leave under section 103.1 of the Act may serve a reply
on each person against whom an order is sought and on the Commissioner
within seven days after being served with the representations in writing
under rule 119 and shall file the reply with proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision without oral hearing

**[[121]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-121 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may render
its decision on the basis of the written record without a formal oral
hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of Tribunal

**[[122]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-122 .sectionLabel}** The Tribunal may grant
the application for leave to make an application, with or without
conditions, or refuse the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

**[[123]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-123 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar shall
serve the decision without delay on the applicant, on each person
against whom an order is sought and on the Commissioner who may
intervene under section 103.2 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave granted

- **[[124]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-124 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  leave is granted in full, the notice of application that the applicant
  proposed to file is, for the purposes of the proceedings, deemed to
  have been filed and served on the date on which the order granting
  leave was made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leave granted in part

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If leave is granted in part, an amended notice, in
  accordance with the order granting leave, shall be filed and served
  within five days after the order is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[125]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-125 .sectionLabel}** The filing of a consent
agreement by parties to a private action under section 106.1 of the Act
shall be made in accordance with rule 106.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

- **[[126]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-126
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After the filing of a consent
  agreement by parties to a private action under section 106.1 of the
  Act, the Registrar shall publish without delay a notice in the [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall state

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that a consent agreement has been filed for
    registration;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of each party to the agreement;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the text of the agreement;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that access to the agreement and any documents
    filed in the matter may be obtained at the office of the Registrar;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date on or before which an application made
    under subsection 106.1(4) of the Act for the cancellation or
    replacement of the agreement must be filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

**[[127]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-127 .sectionLabel}** A copy of a notice of
application made by a third party to cancel or replace a consent
agreement between parties to a private action shall be served on the
Commissioner within five days after the notice of application is filed.

## [PART 9]{.HLabel1}[Application for a Loan Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744575 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

**[[128]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-128 .sectionLabel}** Before filing an
application with the Tribunal for a loan order under subsection 30.19(2)
of the Act, the Commissioner or the representative of the Commissioner
shall give notice in writing to the Chairperson and to the parties to
the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Filing

- **[[129]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-129 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner or the representative of the Commissioner shall file the
  notice referred to in rule 128 at least 10 days before filing the
  application for a loan order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner or the representative of the
  Commissioner shall, within five days after the notice is filed, serve
  it on the parties to the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of application

- **[[130]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-130 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a loan order by the Commissioner or the representative
  of the Commissioner shall be made by filing a notice of application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of application shall be in accordance with
  the requirements set out in subsection 30.19(3) of the Act and shall
  set out, in numbered paragraphs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sections of the Act under which the application
    is made;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of the parties to the proceedings;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds on which the
    application for a loan order is made and of the material facts on
    which the Commissioner relies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms of the loan order sought; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the Commissioner intends
    to use in the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service

- **[[131]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-131 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commissioner shall, within five days after a notice of application for
  a loan order is filed, serve the notice on the parties to the
  proceedings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proof of service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commissioner shall, within five days after the
  service of the notice of application, file proof of service.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Response

- **[[132]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-132 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person served with a notice of application for a loan order under
  subsection 30.19(2) of the Act and who wishes to oppose the
  application shall, within 15 days after receiving the notice of
  application,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve a response on the Commissioner and the other
    parties to the proceedings; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file the response with proof of service.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form and content

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response shall set out, in numbered paragraphs,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a concise statement of the grounds on which the
    application for a loan order is opposed and of the material facts on
    which the person opposing the application relies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an admission or denial of each ground and of each
    material fact relevant to each ground set out in the application for
    a loan order; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the official language that the person opposing the
    application for a loan order intends to use in the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

- **[[133]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-133 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Tribunal may render its decision on the basis of the written record
  without a formal oral hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Power of the Tribunal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Tribunal may grant the application for a loan
  order, with or without conditions, or refuse the application.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Case Management]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744613 .Part}

Definition of [[judicial member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

- **[[134]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-134 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Part, [[judicial member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  means the Chairperson or a judicial member designated by the
  Chairperson.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of a judicial member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Case management duties do not preclude a judicial
  member from presiding at the hearing of an application or reference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Case management conferences

- **[[135]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-135
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subrules (2) and (3), the
  judicial member shall conduct one or more case management conferences
  as soon as is practicable after the end of the period for filing a
  reply, or after the filing of a notice of reference, or sooner if
  indicated by the circumstances.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specialization agreements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an application for the registration of
  a specialization agreement, the judicial member shall conduct a case
  management conference as soon as is practicable after the Commissioner
  has filed proof of service of a notice of appearance in accordance
  with subrule 101(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Loan order

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an application for a loan order, if
  the judicial member deems that a hearing is necessary, the judicial
  member shall consult the parties with respect to any case management
  procedures within seven days after proof of service of the response to
  the application for a loan order has been filed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directions re scheduling

**[[136]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-136 .sectionLabel}** The judicial member
shall issue directions with respect to the scheduling of case management
conferences.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direction regarding list of matters to be
considered

- **[[137]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-137 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  judicial member may include in the directions referred to in rule 136
  a list of the matters to be considered at the case management
  conference and may require the filing of memoranda regarding any of
  those matters.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Matters to be considered

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Those matters may include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the start date, duration and location of the
    hearing, as well as the medium for the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any pending or anticipated motions, and a deadline
    date for the hearing of motions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any issues of confidentiality;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the clarification, simplification and elimination
    of issues;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the possibility of obtaining admissions of
    particular facts or documents, including an agreed statement of
    facts;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a deadline for the completion of discovery, related
    motions and answering undertakings;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the official language to be used for the pleadings
    and the hearing, as well as the official language in which each
    witness shall testify;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a reference, the determination of
    whether there shall be oral evidence;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for the exchange or serving and filing
    of the various documents related to the hearing, including
    affidavits of documents, joint briefs of authorities and agreed
    books of documents;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any matter relating to pre-hearing disclosure;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a timetable to be followed by the intervenors;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} all matters related to expert witnesses, including
    the possibility of experts meeting before a hearing to answer
    questions posed by the Tribunal;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} any amendments to the pleadings;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the advisability of a pre-hearing reference or
    determination of a question of law;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any requirement for a notice of a constitutional
    question;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} a timetable for the subsequent case management
    conferences; and

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} any other matters that may aid in the disposition
    of the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order

**[[138]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-138 .sectionLabel}** After a case management
conference, the Tribunal shall issue an order stating any rulings by the
Tribunal relating to the matters considered at the case management
conference.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Firm requirements

- **[[139]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-139 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  dates set and other requirements established by case management orders
  are firm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Variation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for a variation must be made by motion
  showing that compelling reasons exist for a change in the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Tribunal may amend

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the Tribunal is satisfied that compelling reasons
  exist for a change in the order, it may amend it.

    represented), at (address where service was made).
