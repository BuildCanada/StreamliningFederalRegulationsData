## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128526 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Forum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Forum]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Challenges Annual Forum, to
    be held in Montreal, Quebec, from June 10 to 12,
    2019. ([Forum]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on June 8, 2019 and ending on June 14, 2019. ([période
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128532 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives of foreign states

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the relevant period, representatives of a foreign state that
  participates in the Forum have, to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in relation to the Forum, the privileges
  and immunities set out in paragraphs 11(a) to (f) and section 12, to
  the extent set out in sections 14 to 16, of Article IV of the
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Senior Officials

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, senior officials of an
  international organization have, to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in relation to the Forum, the privileges
  and immunities comparable to the privileges and immunities accorded to
  diplomatic agents under the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations,
  other than duty and tax relief privileges.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other Officials

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, other officials of an
  international organization have, to the extent required for the
  exercise of their functions in relation to the Forum, the privileges
  and immunities set out in paragraphs 18(a), (d) and (e) of Article V
  of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, experts who perform
  missions for an international organization have, to the extent
  required for the exercise of their functions in relation to the Forum,
  the privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128542 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
