## Ontario Soya-Bean Order {#ontario-soya-bean-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-183
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-03-21

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Soya-Beans Produced in Ontario

P.C. 1980-665 1980-03-20
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural
Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order granting authority to regulate the marketing in
interprovincial and export trade of soya-beans produced in Ontario.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868763 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Soya-Bean Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-183).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868765 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Soya-Bean
    Growers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    soya-beans, established and amended from time to time pursuant to
    the Act and any regulations made pursuant to the Act to give effect
    to the plan; ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of soya-beans in Ontario; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[soya-beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[soya-beans]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means soya-beans produced in
    Ontario. ([soja]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868773 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of soya-beans in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by each of them
in relation to the marketing of soya-beans locally within that Province
under the Act and the Plan.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/86-165, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868777 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3 with
respect to the marketing of soya-beans in interprovincial and export
trade to make orders fixing, imposing and collecting levies or charges
from persons situated in the Province of Ontario who are engaged in the
production or marketing of soya-beans and for such purpose classifying
such persons into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the
members of the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity
Board may use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the
creation of reserves, and the payment of expenses and losses resulting
from the sale or disposal of soya-beans and the equalization or
adjustment among producers of soya-beans of moneys realized from the
sale thereof during such period or periods of time as it may determine.
