## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831984 .Part}

Definition of [[infant feeding bottle nipple]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[infant feeding bottle nipple]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a similar
product that is put in the mouth of an infant during use.

## [Requirement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-831987 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit of volatile N-nitrosamines

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An infant feeding bottle
nipple must not contain more than 10 µg/kg total volatile
N-nitrosamines, as determined by dichloromethane extraction.
