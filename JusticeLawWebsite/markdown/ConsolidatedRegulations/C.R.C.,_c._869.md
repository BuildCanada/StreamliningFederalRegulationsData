## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567393 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Cosmetic Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._869).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567395 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
      Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Assistant Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
      SOR/2004-244, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[botanical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[botanical]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an ingredient that is
      directly derived from a plant and that has not been chemically
      modified before it is used in the preparation of a
      cosmetic. ([substance végétale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[child-resistant container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[child-resistant container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 2 of the Consumer Chemicals and Containers
      Regulations, as they read on September 30, 2001. ([contenant
      protège-enfants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flame projection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flame projection]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ability of the
      pressurized contents of an aerosol container to ignite and the
      length of that ignition, when tested in accordance with official
      method DO-30, Determination of Flame Projection, dated October 15,
      1981. ([projection de flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[flashback]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that part of the flame
      projection that extends from its point of ignition back to the
      aerosol container when tested in accordance with official method
      DO-30, Determination of Flame Projection, dated October 15,
      1981. ([retour de flamme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ICI Dictionary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ICI Dictionary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2024-63, s.
      1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who imports a
      cosmetic into Canada for the purpose of selling
      it. ([[importateur]{.otherLang lang="fr"}]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[INCI name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[INCI name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
      Nomenclature Cosmetic Ingredient name assigned to an ingredient in
      the *International Cosmetic Ingredient Dictionary and Handbook*
      published by the Personal Care Products Council on its website, as
      amended from time to time. ([appellation INCI]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ingredient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any substance that is one
      of the components of a cosmetic and includes colouring agents,
      botanicals, fragrance and flavour, but does not include substances
      that are used in the preparation of the cosmetic but that are not
      present in the final product as a result of the chemical
      process. ([ingrédient]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inner label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inner label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label on or affixed to
      the immediate container of a cosmetic. ([étiquette
      intérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[leave-on product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[leave-on product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any cosmetic that
      is intended to stay in prolonged contact with the skin, hair or
      mucous membranes. ([[produit sans rinçage]{.otherLang
      lang="fr"}]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a cosmetic,
      means one of the following persons:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person in Canada who sells the cosmetic under
        the person's own name or under a trademark, design, trade name
        or other name or mark owned or controlled by the person; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person in Canada who is authorized to act in
        Canada on behalf of another person who is not in Canada, if the
        other person sells the cosmetic under the other person's own
        name or under a trademark, design, trade name or other name or
        mark owned or controlled by the other
        person. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[official method]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[official method]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a method of analysis
      or examination designated as such by the Minister for use in the
      administration of the Act and these Regulations. ([méthode
      officielle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[ornamental container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[ornamental container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a container
      that, except on the bottom, does not have any promotional or
      advertising material on it other than a trademark or common name,
      and that appears to be a decorative ornament because of a design
      that is on its surface or because of its shape or texture, and is
      sold as a decorative ornament in addition to being sold as the
      container of a cosmetic. ([contenant décoratif]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[outer label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label on or affixed to
      the outside package of a cosmetic. ([étiquette
      extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[practitioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
      registered and licensed under the laws of a province to practise
      the profession of medicine in that
      province. ([praticien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a direction given by a
      practitioner. ([ordonnance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in the [Consumer Packaging and Labelling
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._417). ([espace
      principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[rinse-off product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[rinse-off product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any cosmetic that
      is intended to be removed after application to the skin, hair or
      mucous membranes. ([[produit à rincer]{.otherLang
      lang="fr"}]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package having a
      security feature that provides reasonable assurance to consumers
      that the package has not been opened prior to
      purchase. ([emballage de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a cosmetic has more than one name, a reference
  in these Regulations to the cosmetic by any of its names is deemed to
  be a reference to the cosmetic by all of its names.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1492297 .Part}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** If, in respect of a
cosmetic, no person meets the definition
[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, a reference in these Regulations
to "manufacturer" is to be read as a reference to:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an importer of the cosmetic; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no person meets the definition
  [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, any person that manufactures or
  processes the cosmetic in Canada on behalf of another person.

## [Inspectors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567419 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  inspector shall perform the functions and duties and carry out the
  responsibilities in respect of cosmetics prescribed by the Act and
  these Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An inspector may, for the proper administration of
  the Act or these Regulations, take photographs of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any cosmetic;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any place where, on reasonable grounds, he believes
    any cosmetic is manufactured, prepared, preserved, packaged or
    stored;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} anything that, on reasonable grounds, he believes
    is used or capable of being used for the manufacture, preparation,
    preservation, packaging or storing of any cosmetic; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any labelling or advertising material relating to a
    cosmetic.

## [Importation into Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567428 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 9, no
person shall import into Canada for sale a cosmetic the sale of which in
Canada would constitute a violation of the Act or these Regulations.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An inspector may examine
and take samples of any cosmetic sought to be imported into Canada.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where an inspector examines
or takes a sample of a cosmetic pursuant to section 6, he may submit the
cosmetic or sample to an analyst for analysis or examination.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where an inspector, on
examination of a cosmetic or sample thereof or on receipt of a report of
an analyst of the result of an analysis or examination of the cosmetic
or sample, is of the opinion that the sale of the cosmetic in Canada
would constitute a violation of the Act or these Regulations, the
inspector shall so notify in writing the collector of customs concerned
and the importer.

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  person seeks to import a cosmetic into Canada for sale and the sale
  would constitute a violation of the Act or these Regulations, that
  person may, if the sale of the cosmetic would be lawful in Canada
  after relabelling or modification of the cosmetic, import the cosmetic
  into Canada on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he gives to an inspector notice of the proposed
    importation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the cosmetic will be relabelled or modified under
    the supervision of an inspector in such a manner as to enable the
    sale of the cosmetic to be lawful in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell a cosmetic that has been
  imported into Canada under subsection (1) unless the cosmetic is
  relabelled or modified in accordance with the Act and these
  Regulations within three months after its importation.

## [Sampling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567441 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** When an inspector takes a
sample of a cosmetic under subsection 23(1) of the Act, the inspector
shall inform the owner of the cosmetic or the person from whom the
sample is taken that the inspector proposes to submit the sample or a
part of it to an analyst for analysis or examination and

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where, in the opinion of the inspector, division of
  the procured quantity would not interfere with analysis or
  examination, he shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} divide the quantity into three parts,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} identify the three parts as the owner's portion,
    the sample and the duplicate sample and where only one part bears
    the label, identify that part as the sample,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} seal each part in such a manner that it cannot be
    opened without breaking the seal, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} deliver the part identified as the owner's portion
    to the owner or the person from whom the sample was obtained and
    forward the sample and the duplicate sample to an analyst for
    analysis or examination; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where, in the opinion of the inspector, division of
  the procured quantity would interfere with analysis or examination, he
  shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} identify the entire quantity as the sample,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} seal the sample in such a manner that it cannot be
    opened without breaking the seal, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} forward the sample to an analyst for analysis or
    examination.

## [Sales]{.HTitleText1} {#h-567454 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell a
cosmetic if any label or advertisement of the cosmetic contains any
symbol or statement that implies that the cosmetic has been compounded
in accordance with a prescription.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell a
cosmetic recommended for removing stains from the teeth that has a
measure of acidity greater than that represented by a pH of 4.

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall sell a cosmetic for use in the area of the eye that
  contains any coal tar dye, coal tar dye base or coal tar dye
  intermediate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1) and section 15.1,
  "area of the eye" means the area bounded by the supraorbital and
  infraorbital ridges and includes the eyebrows, the skin underlying the
  eyebrows, the eyelids, the eyelashes, the conjunctival sac of the eye,
  the eyeball and the soft tissue that lies below the eye and within the
  infraorbital ridge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/89-228, s. 1
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** No manufacturer or
importer shall sell a cosmetic that contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} chloroform as an ingredient; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an estrogenic substance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-506, s. 1
- SOR/85-928, s. 1
- SOR/92-663, s. 1
:::

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** No manufacturer or
importer shall sell a cosmetic that contains mercury or a salt or
derivative thereof unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the cosmetic is intended for use in the area of the
  eye;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mercury or its salt or derivative thereof is used
  in the cosmetic as a preservative; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer or importer

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has in his possession evidence demonstrating that
    the only satisfactory way to maintain the sterility or stability of
    the cosmetic is to use mercury or a salt or derivative thereof as a
    preservative, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at the Minister's request, provides the Minister
    with the evidence described in subparagraph (i).

### [List of Ingredients]{.HTitleText2} {#h-567504 .Subheading}

**[[21.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.1 .sectionLabel}** Sections 21.2 to 21.5
do not apply to any product whose ingredient labelling is regulated
under the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870)
or the [Natural Health Products
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-244, s. 11
:::

- **[[21.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a list of
  ingredients must appear on the outer label of a cosmetic, with each
  ingredient listed only by its INCI name.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of makeup and nail polish and enamel sold
  in a range of colour shades, all colouring agents used in the range
  may be listed if they are preceded by the symbol "+/--" or "±" or the
  phrase "may contain/peut contenir".

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Botanicals must be listed by specifying at least the
  genus and species portions of the INCI name.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An ingredient that is included in the schedule may be
  listed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by its European Union (EU) technical name set out
    in column 1 of the schedule;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by its appropriate INCI name set out in column 2 of
    the schedule and its appropriate French equivalent set out in column
    3 of the schedule; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by all three names.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-244, s. 11
- [SOR/2024-63, s. 5](#1450366-1492318){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1450366-1492318.html"
  role="button"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-21.2-20061116.html "Link to previous version of section 21.2"){.PITLink}

**[[21.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.3 .sectionLabel}** An ingredient that
has no INCI name must be listed by its chemical name.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-244, s. 11
:::

- **[[21.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and (3),
  ingredients must be listed in descending order of predominance, in
  their concentration by weight.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Ingredients that are present at a concentration of 1%
  or less and all colouring agents, regardless of their concentration,
  may be listed in random order after the ingredients that are present
  at a concentration of more than 1%.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of fragrance and flavour, the words
  "parfum" and "aroma", respectively, may be inserted at the end of the
  list of ingredients to indicate that such ingredients have been added
  to the cosmetic to produce or to mask a particular odour or flavour.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-244, s. 11
:::

- **[[21.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 21.2(1), in the
  case of a cosmetic whose immediate container or outside package is too
  small for the label to comply with paragraph 18(b), the list of
  ingredients may appear

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on a tag, tape or card affixed to the container or
    package; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on a website, provided that the outer label
    contains a statement specifying that the list of ingredients appears
    on that website and includes the location of the website.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 21.2(1), in the case of a cosmetic
  in an ornamental container that has no outside package, the list of
  ingredients may appear on a tag, tape or card affixed to the
  container.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 21.2(1), in the case of a cosmetic
  that has no outside package and whose size, shape or texture, or that

      - [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of fragrance and flavour, other
        than fragrance allergens, the words "parfum" and "aroma",
        respectively, may be inserted at the end of the list of
        ingredients to indicate that such ingredients have been added to
        the cosmetic to produce or to mask a particular odour or
        flavour.

      - [(4)]{.lawlabel} A fragrance allergen must be listed if

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rinse-off product, the
          concentration of the fragrance allergen in the cosmetic is
          greater than 0.01%; or

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a leave-on product, the
          concentration of the fragrance allergen in the cosmetic is
          greater than 0.001%.

      - [(5)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (4), a fragrance allergen is
        not required to be listed in a cosmetic's list of ingredients
        before the later of

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which a provision of a regulation,
          published by the European Union on its website and amending
          the Regulation (EC) No. 1223/2009 of the European Parliament
          and of the Council of 30 November 2009 on cosmetic products by
          adding the fragrance allergen to the European Restricted
          Substances List, comes into force; and

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the day on which the fragrance
          allergen must be listed in the list of ingredients of a
          cosmetic in order for the cosmetic to be made available on the
          European Union market, as set out or referred to in the entry
          in the European Restricted Substances List for that fragrance
          allergen.
