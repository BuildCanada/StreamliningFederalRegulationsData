## Issuance of Certificates Regulations {#issuance-of-certificates-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-587
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1993-12-15

Issuance of Certificates Regulations

P.C. 1993-2087  1993-12-15
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, is pleased hereby, pursuant
to paragraph 12(c.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-93-587_e_hq_6269){#fn_SOR-93-587_e_hq_6269-ID0ELCAA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), to revoke the
[Issuance of Certificates Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-587),
made by Order in Council P.C. 1988-2869 of December 30, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-93-587_e_hq_6270){#fn_SOR-93-587_e_hq_6270-ID0EFCAA},
and to make the annexed Regulations providing for considerations to be
taken into account by the Minister in the issuance of certificates under
section 9.01 of the Export and Import Permits Act, effective on the day
on which section 154 of [An Act to implement the North American Free
Trade Agreement](/eng/acts/N-23.8), being chapter 44 of the Statutes of
Canada, 1993, comes into force.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958173 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[apparel goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[apparel goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following goods in
    the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are both cut or knit to
    shape and sewn or otherwise assembled in Canada from fabric or yarn
    produced or obtained outside of the free trade area:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} goods provided for in Chapters 61 and 62; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods classified under heading 96.19 that are of
      textile material other than wadding; ([vêtements]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[ \[Repealed, SOR/99-57, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

[[export development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export development plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2020-71, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[export performance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export performance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the volume and
    value of exports from Canada of an exporter to the United States or
    Mexico of apparel goods, fabrics and made-up goods, spun yarn or
    metallized yarn, as applicable; ([performance à
    l'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who exports or
    intends to export from Canada to the United States or Mexico apparel
    goods, fabrics and made-up goods, spun yarn or metallized yarn, as
    applicable; ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fabric and made-up goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cotton or man-made fibre fabric and cotton or
      man-made fibre made-up textile goods referred to in Chapters 52 to
      55 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) --- excluding goods
      containing 36% or more by weight of wool or fine animal hair ---
      and in Chapters 58, 60 and 63 of that schedule, that are woven or
      knit in Canada from yarn produced or obtained outside the free
      trade area, or from yarn produced in the free trade area from
      fibre produced or obtained outside the free trade area, or knit in
      Canada from yarn spun in the free trade area from fibre produced
      or obtained outside the free trade area, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods under subheading Nos. 9404.40 and 9404.90
      in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are finished, cut and
      sewn or otherwise assembled in Canada from fabrics of subheading
      Nos. 5208.11 to 5208.29, 5209.11 to 5209.29, 5210.11 to 5210.29,
      5211.11 to 5211.20, 5212.11, 5212.12, 5212.21, 5212.22, 5407.41,
      5407.51, 5407.71, 5407.81, 5407.91, 5408.21, 5408.31, 5512.11,
      5512.21, 5512.91, 5513.11 to 5513.19, 5514.11 to 5514.19, 5516.11,
      5516.21, 5516.31, 5516.41 or 5516.91 of that schedule that are
      produced or obtained outside the free trade area; ([tissus et
      articles confectionnés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[free trade area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area comprising
    Canada, Mexico and the United States; ([zone de
    libre-échange]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[metallized yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[metallized yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means yarn under subheading
    No. 5605.00 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule
    to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that are formed in
    Canada from fibres obtained outside the free trade area; ([filé
    métallique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} or [fil
    métallisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Mexico]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Mexico]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2.2 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);
    ([Mexique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spun yarn]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cotton or man-made fibre
    yarn that is classified under heading Nos. 52.05 to 52.07 or 55.09
    to 55.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
    the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that is spun in Canada from
    fibre classified under heading Nos. 52.01 to 52.03 or 55.01 to 55.07
    of that schedule and produced or obtained outside the free trade
    area; ([filé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[United States]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[United States]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2.1 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).
    ([États-Unis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Considerations in the Issuance of Certificates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-958189 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following
considerations must be taken into account by the Minister in the
issuance of a certificate under section 9.1 or 9.2 of the [Export and
Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) in respect of goods that are the
subject of an export allocation:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the export performance of the exporter, if any, over
  a recent representative base period;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the degree of demand for certificates; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the effective utilization and preservation of the
  benefits of CUSMA and any intergovernmental arrangements made under
  it.

## [Application for Certificate of Eligibility]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1256930 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a certificate under section 9.1 or 9.2 of the [Export
  and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19) must include the following
  information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and email
    address of the exporter and of any agent or mandatary applying on
    the exporter's behalf;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the exporter or agent or mandatary is a
    corporation, the name of a contact person for the corporation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exporter's identifier number assigned by the
    Minister;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the official language to be used for communications
    with the agent or mandatary and the exporter;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether the exporter and, if
    applicable, the agent or mandatary are residents of Canada;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the good;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the tariff classification of the good;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the good and the unit of
    measurement;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the value of the good in Canadian dollars;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the date of shipment of the good;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the port of exit of the good;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the date of exit of the good;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the importer or consignee;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the good's country of origin; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} any other information requested by the Minister for
    clarification purposes related to the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate holder may, in writing, before the
  expiry date of the certificate, make a request to the Minister to
  amend the certificate. The request must state the terms and conditions
  of the certificate and the reasons for the amendment.
