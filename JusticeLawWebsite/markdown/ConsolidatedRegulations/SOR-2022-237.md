## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Haiti {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-on-haiti .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-237
:::

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2022-11-10

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on Haiti

P.C. 2022-1208 2022-11-10
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under Article
41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Resolution 2653 (2022)
on October 21, 2022;

And whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to
make regulations for enabling the measures set out in that resolution to
be effectively applied;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, makes the annexed
[Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolution on
Haiti](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-237) under section 2 of the [United
Nations Act](/eng/acts/U-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4976_hq_1667297069){#fn_81000-2-4976_hq_1667297069-ID0EBBAA}.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1378927 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    weapon, ammunition, military equipment --- including military
    vehicles --- or paramilitary equipment, and their spare
    parts. ([armes et matériel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen within the meaning
    of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or an entity established,
    incorporated or continued by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Committee of the Security Council established under paragraph 19 of
    Security Council Resolution 2653. ([Comité du Conseil de
    sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is
    designated by the Committee of the Security Council. ([personne
    désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a corporation, trust,
    partnership, fund, unincorporated association or organization or
    foreign state. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Haiti]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Haiti]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Republic of Haiti and
    includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government, any of its departments and any
      government and department of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies and any agency of its
      political subdivisions. ([Haïti]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Humanitarian Response Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Humanitarian Response Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a strategic
    plan prepared by the United Nations that addresses a protracted or
    sudden onset emergency in Haiti that requires international
    humanitarian assistance. ([Plan d'aide humanitaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activities
    conducted by state or non-state armed forces and any activities that
    support the operational capabilities of an armed group. ([activités
    militaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or was employed in the service of His Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a province;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} occupies or occupied a position of responsibility
      in the service of His Majesty in right of Canada or of a province;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is or was engaged by or on behalf of His Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a
      province. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or an
    entity. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security Council
    of the United Nations. ([Conseil de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Security Council Resolution 2653]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council Resolution 2653]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    Resolution 2653 (2022) of October 21, 2022, adopted by the Security
    Council. ([résolution 2653 du Conseil de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
    assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
    services or technical advice or transferring know-how or technical
    data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not a
    Saturday or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1378951 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property, wherever situated, that is
  owned --- or that is held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by
  a designated person or by a person acting on behalf of or at the
  direction of a designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services in respect
  of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any property, wherever situated, or
  provide any financial or related services to a designated person, to a
  person acting on behalf of or at the direction of a designated person
  or to an entity that is owned --- or that is held or controlled,
  directly or indirectly --- by a designated person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} make available any property, wherever situated, or
  provide any financial or related services for the benefit of any
  person or entity referred to in paragraph (d).

- [SOR/2023-134, s. 14](#1407502-1407735){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- humanitarian assistance

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not
prohibit the provision, processing or payment of funds, other financial
assets or economic resources or the provision of goods and services that
are necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance
or to support other activities that support basic human needs in Haiti
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds and
  other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies and
  related organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
  observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members of
  those humanitarian organizations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded non-governmental
  organizations that are participating in any United Nations
  humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other United
  Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
  organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
  Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
  grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
  non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other person authorized for that purpose by a
  United Nations committee established by the Security Council acting
  within its mandate.

- [SOR/2023-134, s. 14](#1407502-1407744){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- interest

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not
prohibit the payment of interest or other earnings if the payment is the
result of a dealing or transaction that occurred before the designated
person referred to in that section became a designated person. However,
that payment then becomes subject to section 2.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2023-134, s. 14](#1407502-1407748){.wb-lbx

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Embargo --- arms and related material

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} export, sell, supply or transfer, directly or
  indirectly, arms and related material, wherever situated, to Haiti or
  any person in Haiti; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, technical assistance
  or financial assistance related to the sale, supply, transfer,
  manufacture, maintenance or use of arms and related material to Haiti
  or any person in Haiti.

related to military activities to Haiti or any person in Haiti.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Embargo --- transport

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for the
owner or master of a [[Canadian vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in section 2 of the [Canada Shipping Act,
2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15), any operator of an aircraft registered in
Canada, any Canadian owner or master of a vessel or any Canadian
operator of an aircraft to knowingly carry, cause to be carried or
permit to be carried arms and related material that are destined for
Haiti or a person in Haiti.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- various entities

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 to 5 do not apply to an
  activity that is intended solely for the support of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations or a mission authorized by the
    United Nations, including the Multinational Security Support (MSS)
    Mission and the United Nations Integrated Office in Haiti (BINUH);
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a security unit that operates under the command of
    the Government of Haiti, including the Haitian National Police and
    the Armed Forces of Haiti.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- advance approval

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 to 5 do not apply to an activity that has
  been approved in advance by the Committee of the Security Council.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- non-lethal military
  equipment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 to 5 do not apply to non-lethal military
  equipment that is intended solely for humanitarian or protective use
  or to related technical assistance.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2025-78, s. 4](#1516718-1523047){.wb-lbx
  ajax-after="1516718-1523047.html"}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assisting in prohibited activity

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada and any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly do
anything that causes, facilitates or assists in, or is intended to
cause, facilitate or assist in, any activity prohibited by sections 2 to
5.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1378978 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property that is owned --- or that is held or controlled,
directly or indirectly --- by a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} banks regulated by the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
  and, in respect of their business in Canada, [[authorized foreign
  banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 2 of that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any business described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the business involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity set
  out in section 7 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the Canadian
  Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned --- or that is
    held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by a designated
    person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) and no civil proceedings lie against a person for
  a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1378998 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under these
  Regulations must, before engaging in that activity, apply to the
  Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the activity from the
  application of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that such an activity be prohibited or
  if the Security Council or the Committee of the Security Council has
  approved the activity in advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption for property

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose property is affected by the application of section 2 may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the
  property from the application of that section if the property is
  necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses or is subject to a lien,
  mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
  charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established in accordance with Security
  Council Resolution 2653 that the property is necessary for basic or
  extraordinary expenses or is subject to a lien, mortgage or security
  interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a charge or to a judicial,
  administrative or arbitral decision, the Minister must issue a
  certificate within the following time periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 15 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is necessary for basic
    expenses, if the Committee of the Security Council does not oppose
    the application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is necessary for
    extraordinary expenses, if the Committee of the Security Council
    approves the application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 90 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is subject to a lien,
    mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
    charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was created or issued before the person became a
      designated person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not for the benefit of a designated person,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been brought to the attention of the
      Committee of the Security Council by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- parties to contract

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is a party to a contract or a gratuitous transfer may apply
  to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt property from
  the application of section 2 to permit the receipt of payments or a
  transfer from a designated person or to permit a designated person to
  make payments or to carry out the transfer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- time period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue a certificate within 90
  working days after receiving the application and at least 10 working
  days after advising the Security Council of the Minister's intention
  to issue the certificate, if it is established that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contract was entered into or the transfer
    carried out prior to any party becoming a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payments or transfer are not to be received,
    directly or indirectly, by a designated person, by a person acting
    on behalf of, or at the direction of, a designated person or by an
    entity that is owned --- or that is held or controlled, directly or
    indirectly --- by a designated person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of a
  designated person and who claims not to be that person may apply to
  the Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they are not
  that designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 45 working days after receiving the
  application, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if it is established that the applicant is not the
    designated person, issue the certificate; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if it is not so established, provide notice to the
    applicant of the determination.

## [Disclosure of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379029 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure by official

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  official may, for the purpose of responding to a request from the
  Security Council, disclose personal information to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, for the purpose of administering or
  enforcing these Regulations or fulfilling an obligation under a
  resolution of the Security Council, disclose personal information to
  the Security Council.

## [Legal Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379035 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- legal proceedings

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** No legal proceedings lie
in Canada at the instance of the Government of Haiti, of any person in
Haiti, of a designated person or of any person claiming through or
acting on behalf of any such person in connection with any contract or
other dealing if its performance was prevented in any way by these
Regulations.

## [Application Before Publication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379038 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 11(2)(a) of the [Statutory Instruments Act](/eng/acts/S-22),
these Regulations apply according to their terms before they are
published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1379041 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
