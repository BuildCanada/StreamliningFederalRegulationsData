## [Maximum Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-675962 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum fee

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 85(2) of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) and 89(2) of the
[Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), the maximum fee that may be
charged for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer is
\$5.
