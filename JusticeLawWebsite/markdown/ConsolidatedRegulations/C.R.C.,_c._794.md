## Syncrude Remission Order {#syncrude-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 794
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax for the Syncrude Project
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563839 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Syncrude Remission Order.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563841 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[barrels]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[barrels]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means barrels of synthetic crude oil
    from Leases 17 and 22 pursuant to the Syncrude Project;
    ([barils]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means that the fiscal programs as
    they relate to the Syncrude Project in effect at the commencement of
    the Syncrude Project have been revised in such a manner as to have
    significant adverse economic effect on the Syncrude Project;
    ([condition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Crown]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Her Majesty in right of the
    Province of Alberta; ([Couronne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[leased substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[leased substances]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means all substances the
    participant has recovered pursuant to Leases 17 and 22; ([matières
    louées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Leases 17 and 22]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Leases 17 and 22]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Government of Alberta
    Bituminous Sands Leases Nos. 17 and 22, excluding that portion of
    Lease No. 17 that is subject to an Agreement dated September 20,
    1972 as amended by an Agreement dated September 26, 1972 whereby
    Great Canadian Oil Sands Limited was granted a sublease of lands
    contained in Lease No. 17, and includes any other documents or
    titles that extend the duration of Leases 17 and 22; ([concessions
    17 et 22]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Canada-Cities Service Ltd., a body corporate,
      incorporated under the laws of Canada and having its head office
      at the City of Calgary, in the Province of Alberta,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Imperial Oil Limited, a body corporate,
      incorporated under the laws of Canada and having its head office
      at the municipality of Metropolitan Toronto, in the Province of
      Ontario,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Gulf Oil Canada Limited, a body corporate,
      incorporated under the laws of Canada and having its head office
      at the City of Toronto, in the Province of Ontario,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Crown as represented by the Minister of
      Energy and Resources for the Province of Alberta,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Her Majesty in right of Canada as represented by
      the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources for Canada, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Ontario Energy Corporation, a body corporate,
      incorporated by Special Act of the Legislature of the Province of
      Ontario and having its head office at the City of Toronto, in the
      Province of Ontario,

    or any or all of them or their successors or assignees as long as
    they retain a share in the Syncrude Project;
    ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[royalty provisions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[royalty provisions]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the provisions
    contained in paragraphs 12(1)(o) and 18(1)(m), and subsections 69(6)
    to (10) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([dispositions
    relatives aux redevances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Syncrude Project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Syncrude Project]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the scheme of the
    participant for the recovery of leased substances from Leases 17 and
    22; ([projet Syncrude]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[synthetic crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[synthetic crude oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a mixture, mainly
    of pentanes and heavier hydrocarbons, that may contain sulphur
    compounds, that is derived from crude bitumen and that is liquid at
    the time its volume is measured or estimated. ([pétrole brut
    synthétique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563859 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is hereby granted to each participant of
  any tax payable for a taxation year pursuant to Part I of the [Income
  Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) as a result of the royalty provisions being
  applicable to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} amounts receivable and the fair market value of any
    property receivable by the Crown as a royalty, tax, rental or levy
    with respect to the Syncrude Project, or as an amount however
    described, that may reasonably be regarded as being in lieu of any
    of the preceding amounts;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} dispositions of leased substances to the Crown by
    the participant; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} acquisitions of leased substances from the Crown by
    the participant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No remission shall be granted pursuant to this Order
  to a participant in respect of a taxation year of that participant
  that commences after

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of 1.1 billion barrels, where the
    Governor in Council revokes this Order upon being satisfied on the
    report of the Minister of Finance that the condition exists prior to
    the recovery of 1.1 billion barrels,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of the number of barrels recovered on
    the date the Governor in Council revokes this Order upon being
    satisfied on the report of the Minister of Finance that the
    condition exists if that date is after the recovery of more than 1.1
    billion barrels and less than 2.1 billion barrels,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of 2.1 billion barrels, or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} December 31, 2003,

  whichever first occurs.
