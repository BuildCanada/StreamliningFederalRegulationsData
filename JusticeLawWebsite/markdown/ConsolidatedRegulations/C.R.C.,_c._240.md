## P.E.I. Vegetable Information (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#p.e.i.-vegetable-information-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 240
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Information Required from Persons Engaged in
the Production in Prince Edward Island of Certain Vegetables and the
Marketing of them in Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537367 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [P.E.I. Vegetable Information (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._240).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537369 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Vegetable Commodity Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual, firm,
    partnership, association or corporation that grows or markets the
    vegetable and includes any servants or agents thereof;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vegetable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any turnip produced in the
    Province of Prince Edward Island. ([légumes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537374 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to vegetable when marketed in interprovincial and export trade and
to persons and property situated in the Province of Prince Edward
Island.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537376 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person engaged in the production or marketing of vegetable shall keep
  complete and accurate records of all matters relating to such
  production or marketing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person referred to in subsection (1) shall,
  upon the request of the Commodity Board or any member or authorized
  representative of the Commodity Board, produce to the Commodity Board,
  member or representative all records referred to in that subsection.

## [Inspection and Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537380 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  member or authorized representative of the Commodity Board may at any
  reasonable time inspect any place or premises used for the production
  or marketing of vegetable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every person in possession or control of any place or
  premises referred to in subsection (1) shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} permit any member or authorized representative of
    the Commodity Board to inspect the place or premises; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} furnish any member or authorized representative of
    the Commodity Board with such information in respect of the
    production and marketing of vegetable as he may reasonably require.

## [Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537386 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any person aggrieved by any
act or decision of any member or authorized representative of the
Commodity Board may appeal to the Commodity Board and, if not satisfied
with the decision of the Commodity Board, may appeal to the Prince
Edward Island Marketing Board.
