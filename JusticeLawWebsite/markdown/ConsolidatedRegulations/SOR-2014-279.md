## Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in Yukon {#order-declaring-that-the-wastewater-systems-effluent-regulations-do-not-apply-in-yukon .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2014-279
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 2014-11-28

Order Declaring that the Wastewater Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not
Apply in Yukon

P.C. 2014-1300 2014-11-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of the Environment has caused to be published in
the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on June 28,
2014, a copy of the proposed [Order Declaring that the Wastewater
Systems Effluent Regulations Do Not Apply in
Yukon](/eng/regulations/SOR-2014-279), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the proposed order;

And whereas on October 29, 2014, the Minister of the Environment and the
Government of Yukon entered into an equivalency agreement under section
4.1[[Footnote

## [Declaration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 2 and 3, [[effluent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[final discharge
point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[wastewater
system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meanings as in section 1 of
the [Wastewater Systems Effluent
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- Regulations

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The [Wastewater Systems
Effluent Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139), made under
subsection 36(5) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14), do not apply in
respect of a wastewater system in Yukon that would otherwise have been
subject to those regulations and that is subject to the Waters Act, S.Y.
2003, c. 19; 2007, c. 6 and the Waters Regulation, Yukon O.I.C. 2003/58.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- subsection 36(3) of
the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 36(3) of the
[Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) does not apply in respect of any deposit
of effluent from the final discharge point of a wastewater system
referred to in section 2 if the effluent would otherwise have been
regulated under the [Wastewater Systems Effluent
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-139).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808280 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
