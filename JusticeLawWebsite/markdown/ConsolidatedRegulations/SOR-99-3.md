## Portions of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration Divestiture Regulations {#portions-of-the-department-of-citizenship-and-immigration-divestiture-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-3
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 1998-12-14

Portions of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration Divestiture
Regulations

T.B. 826779 1998-12-10
::::

::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-99-3_e_hq_4109){#fn_SOR-99-3_e_hq_4109-ID0EHBAA}
of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph
7(2)(a) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby
makes the annexed [Portions of the Department of Citizenship and
Immigration Divestiture Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-3).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031367 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or body who, by
    reason of an agreement with Her Majesty in right of Canada, carries
    out the activities that were formerly carried out by a portion of
    the Department of Citizenship and Immigration, and includes a person
    who acts for or on behalf of that person or body. ([nouvel
    employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031371 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 3, these Regulations apply to a person
  who, by reason of an agreement between Her Majesty in right of Canada
  and a new employer, ceases to be employed in the public service and
  becomes employed by the new employer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  subsequently becomes re-employed by the new employer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 9 do not apply to a person who has
  exercised an option under subsection 3(2).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 65(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031378 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12 to 13.01 of the Act only apply to a person on and after the day on
  which they cease to be employed by the new employer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, if on or after April 1, 1998, were it not
  for these Regulations, a person would be entitled to a return of
  contributions under subsection 12(3) or 12.1(4) of the Act, they may
  request, in writing, the return of contributions within one year after
  the day on which they cease to be employed in the public service and
  become employed by the new employer, and if, in the same
  circumstances, a person would be entitled to exercise an option in
  favour of the transfer value referred to in section 13.01 of the Act,
  they may exercise the option within the same period.

- SOR/2016-203, s. 63

## [Survivor and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031384 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The survivor and children
of a person who dies while employed by the new employer are entitled to
one of the following benefits to which they would have been entitled if
the person had been employed in the public service:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the death benefit under subsection 12(8) or 12.1(8)
  of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the allowances referred to in subsection 13(3) or
  13.001(3) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 63

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person during the period that
begins on the date on which the person ceases to be employed in the
public service and that ends on the date on which that person ceases to
be employed by the new employer is entitled to an allowance under Part I
of the Act.

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031394 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act shall begin on the date on which the
person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031396 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with the new employer that begins on the day on which the
person ceases to be employed in the public service and that ends on the
day on which they cease to be employed by the new employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 64
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, the age of the person when they cease
to be employed in the public service is their age on the day on which
they cease to be employed by the new employer.
