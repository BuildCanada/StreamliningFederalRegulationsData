## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-49
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2021-08-18

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2021-840 2021-08-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, effective August 12, 2021,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECBAA}
  of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCBAA},
  transfers from the Department of Industry to the Federal Economic
  Development Agency for Northern Ontario the control and supervision of
  that portion of the federal public administration in the Department of
  Industry known as the Federal Economic Development Initiative for
  Northern Ontario; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECBAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7867}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCBAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7868}R.S., c.
    P-34
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Economic Development and
  Official Languages is to preside over the Federal Economic Development
  Agency for Northern Ontario.
