## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). ([ Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[distribution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a trade by or on behalf of an insurance holding
      company in securities of the insurance holding company that have
      not previously been issued; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trade in previously issued securities of an
      insurance holding company from the holdings of any person or group
      of persons who act in concert and who hold in excess of 10% of the
      shares of any class of voting shares of the insurance holding
      company. ([mise en circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated securities entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    financial institution whose primary business is dealing in
    securities and that is regulated in its securities dealings by an
    Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province or by the laws
    of a foreign country. ( [entité de valeurs mobilières
    réglementée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Holding of Insurance Holding Company's Shares by Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676547 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Where subsidiary may hold shares

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 753(c) of the Act, an insurance holding company may, subject
to section 3, permit a subsidiary of the insurance holding company that
is a regulated securities entity to hold shares of the insurance holding
company if the aggregate value of shares of the insurance holding
company held by all subsidiaries of the insurance holding company that
are regulated securities entities, other than shares referred to in
section 755 of the Act, does not exceed 1% of the regulatory capital of
the insurance holding company.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception for securities underwriter

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If a subsidiary of an
insurance holding company is a regulated securities entity and is acting
as a securities underwriter in connection with a distribution of shares
of the insurance holding company, the insurance holding company may
permit the subsidiary to hold shares of the insurance holding company in
excess of the limit set out in section 2 until the distribution is
ended.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676552 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote3_e){#footnote3_e-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day on which
section 753 of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), as
enacted by section 465 of the [Financial Consumer Agency of Canada
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1), chapter 9 of the Statutes of Canada, 2001, comes
into force.
