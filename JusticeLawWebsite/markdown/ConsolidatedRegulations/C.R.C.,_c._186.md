## Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-flue-cured-tobacco-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 186
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Flue-Cured Tobacco Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536215 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Marketing (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._186).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536217 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who buys tobacco;
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured
    Tobacco Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act and
    the Plan; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[licensed buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licensed buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a buyer to whom a
    licence to buy tobacco has been issued by the Commodity Board but
    does not include a buyer whose licence has been suspended or revoked
    by the Commodity Board; ([acheteur agréé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[marketing quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing quota]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a quota fixed and
    allotted to a person by the Commodity Board to market the quantity
    of tobacco specified by the Commodity Board; ([contingent de
    vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco
    Growers' Marketing Plan established pursuant to the Act and any
    regulations made thereunder to give effect to the Plan;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means unmanufactured flue-cured
    tobacco produced in Ontario. ([tabac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536226 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of tobacco in interprovincial and export trade and
to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536228 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall buy tobacco from any person other than the Commodity
  Board or a person to whom the Commodity Board has fixed and allotted a
  marketing quota.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall offer to sell or sell tobacco to any
  person other than the Commodity Board or a licensed buyer.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall offer to
sell, sell or buy tobacco at any place other than a tobacco exchange
operated by the Commodity Board.

## [Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536233 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall ship or
transport tobacco to any place other than a tobacco exchange operated by
the Commodity Board unless he has prior written consent from the
Commodity Board.

## [Marketing Quotas]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536235 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  tobacco shall be marketed on a quota basis.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person to whom a marketing quota has not been
  fixed and allotted or whose quota has been cancelled shall market any
  tobacco.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person to whom a marketing quota has been fixed
  and allotted shall market any tobacco in excess of such marketing
  quota.
