## Organizations in the Province of British Columbia Exemption Order {#organizations-in-the-province-of-british-columbia-exemption-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-220
:::

[PERSONAL INFORMATION PROTECTION AND ELECTRONIC DOCUMENTS
ACT](/eng/acts/P-8.6)

Registration 2004-10-12

Organizations in the Province of British Columbia Exemption Order

C.P. 2004-1164 2004-10-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that the Personal
Information Protection Act, S.B.C. 2003, c. 63, of the Province of
British Columbia, which is substantially similar to Part 1 of the
[Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea){#footnotea-ID0EBBBA}, applies to the
organizations described in the annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea-ID0EBBBA){#footnotea}S.C. 2000, c. 5
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to paragraph
26(2)(b) of the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic
Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6)^a^, hereby makes the annexed
[Organizations in the Province of British Columbia Exemption
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-220).
:::

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712835 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An organization, other than
a federal work, undertaking or business, to which the Personal
Information Protection Act, S.B.C. 2003, c. 63, of the Province of
British Columbia, applies is exempt from the application of Part 1 of
the [Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents
Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6), in respect of the collection, use and disclosure
of personal information that occurs within the Province of British
Columbia.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-712837 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
