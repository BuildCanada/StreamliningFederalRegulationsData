## Remission Order in Respect of a Transfer of a Sahtu Dene and Metis Settlement Corporation's Assets under a Self-Government Agreement {#remission-order-in-respect-of-a-transfer-of-a-sahtu-dene-and-metis-settlement-corporations-assets-under-a-self-government-agreement .Title-of-Act}

Remission Order in Respect of a Transfer of a Sahtu Dene and Metis
Settlement Corporation's Assets under a Self-Government Agreement

P.C. 2015-637 2015-05-28

in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister

annexed Remission Order in Respect of a Transfer of a Sahtu Dene and
Metis Settlement Corporation's Assets under a Self-Government Agreement.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628370 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim
      Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Comprehensive Land Claim
      Agreement entered into by Her Majesty the Queen in right of Canada
      and the Sahtu Dene and Metis, as represented by the Sahtu Tribal
      Council, signed on September 6, 1993, as amended from time to
      time. ([Entente sur la revendication territoriale globale des
      Dénés et Métis du Sahtu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Sahtu Dene and Metis First Nation Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Sahtu Dene and Metis First Nation
      Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by 2.1
      of Appendix B of the Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land Claim
      Agreement. ([gouvernement de la première nation des Dénés et Métis
      du Sahtu]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[self-government agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
      assigned by 2.1 of Appendix B of the Sahtu Dene and Metis
      Comprehensive Land Claim Agreement. ([entente sur l'autonomie
      gouvernementale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[settlement corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[settlement corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning
      assigned by 11.3.1 of the Sahtu Dene and Metis Comprehensive Land
      Claim Agreement. ([société de gestion des
      indemnité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of meanings in Act

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any word or expression used in this Order and not
  defined in subsection (1) has the same meaning as in the Act.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628381 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax payable under the Act by a settlement
  corporation in respect of the settlement corporation ceasing to exist
  and all of its assets being transferred to, by way of vesting in, a
  Sahtu Dene and Metis First Nation Government under the self-government
  agreement that creates the Sahtu Dene and Metis First Nation
  Government;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any penalties payable under the Act in respect of the
  settlement corporation ceasing to exist and all of its assets being
  transferred to, by way of vesting in, a Sahtu Dene and Metis First
  Nation Government under the self-government agreement that creates the
  Sahtu Dene and Metis First Nation Government; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any interest payable in respect of that tax and those
  penalties.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-628387 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of an
amount under this Order to a person on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount has not otherwise been rebated, credited,
  refunded or remitted to the person or any other person under the Act,
  the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) or any other Act of
  the Parliament of Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person applies in writing to the Minister of
  National Revenue for remission under this Order in respect of the
  transfer before the later of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the day that is eighteen months after the day on
    which this order is made, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the filing-due date of the person's taxation year
    in which the transfer occurs.
