## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605951 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada National Parks
    Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Parks Lease
    and Licence of Occupation Regulations (1991).
    ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission --- Ten-year Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605955 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount set out in section 3 to every person who has a leasehold
interest in public lands that are situated in a national park set out in
Schedule 1 to the Act if the amount of rent payable in respect of those
lands has been fixed in the year 2000 for a ten-year period beginning in
that year, in accordance with the terms of the applicable lease and the
Regulations.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 2 shall be equal to the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the first four months of the
  third year of the ten-year period beginning in the year 2000

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the last four months of the last
  year of the ten-year period ending in the year 2000.

## [Remission --- Two-year Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605961 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount set out in section 5 to every person who has a leasehold
interest in public lands that are situated in a national park set out in
Schedule 1 to the Act if the amount of rent payable in respect of those
lands has been or is due to be fixed in the year 2002 for a two-year
period beginning in that year, in accordance with the terms of the
applicable lease and the Regulations.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 4 shall be equal to the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the first four months of the
  first year of the two-year period beginning in the year 2002

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the rental rate for the last four months of the
  second year of the two-year period ending in the year 2000.

## [Remission --- Town of Jasper]{.HTitleText1} {#h-605967 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the amount set out in section 7 to every person who has a leasehold
interest in public lands that are situated in the Town of Jasper, in
Jasper National Park of Canada as set out in Schedule 1 to the Act, if
the amount of rent payable in respect of those lands is fixed in the
year 2002 at \$1.00 per annum in accordance with the Agreement for the
Establishment of Local Government in the Town of Jasper between Her
Majesty the Queen in right of Canada and the Municipality of Jasper, the
terms of the applicable lease and the Regulations.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The amount of remission
referred to in section 6 shall be equal to the amount of the annual
rental rate under the Regulations for the period April 1, 2002 to July
31, 2002.
