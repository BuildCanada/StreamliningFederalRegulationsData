## [Prescribed Supervisory Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678954 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 607 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the
  following information or any component of that information is
  prescribed supervisory information in relation to an authorized
  foreign bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any rating assigned by the Superintendent to the
    authorized foreign bank to assess the soundness of its business in
    Canada and any other such rating that is substantially based on
    information obtained from the Superintendent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any stage of intervention assigned to the
    authorized foreign bank on the basis of the principles set out in
    the Guide to Intervention for Federal Financial Institutions;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any order made in respect of the authorized foreign
    bank under section 617 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), any
    prudential agreement entered into by the authorized foreign bank
    under section 614.1 of that Act or any direction issued to it under
    section 615 of that Act;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any report prepared by or at the request of the
    Superintendent or any recommendation made by the Superintendent as a
    result of an annual or special examination or other supervisory
    review of the authorized foreign bank, including any related
    correspondence to or from the principal officer, directors or other
    officers of the authorized foreign bank.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 607 of the [Bank
  Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), the information prescribed by the following
  regulations is prescribed supervisory information in relation to an
  affiliate of an authorized foreign bank:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Banks)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-59), if the affiliate is a
    bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Cooperative Credit
    Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-57), if the
    affiliate is an association to which the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01) applies;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Companies)
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-56), if the affiliate is a
    company, society, foreign company or provincial company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Trust and Loan
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-55), if the
    affiliate is a company to which the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8) applies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Bank Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-480), if the
    affiliate is a bank holding company to which the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01) applies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the [Supervisory Information (Insurance Holding
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-484), if the
    affiliate is an insurance holding company to which the [Insurance
    Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies.

## [Prohibited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678970 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 3 and
4, an authorized foreign bank shall not, directly or indirectly,
disclose information referred to in section 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Limited Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678974 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An authorized foreign bank
may disclose information referred to in section 1 to its affiliates or
to its directors, officers, employees, auditors, securities underwriters
or legal advisors, or to those of its affiliates, if the authorized
foreign bank ensures that the information remains confidential.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An authorized foreign bank
or any of its affiliates may disclose information referred to in
paragraph 1(1)(c) if the authorized foreign bank or affiliate considers
the information to contain a material fact or material change that is
required by the securities laws of the relevant jurisdiction to be
disclosed.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-196, s. 3(E)
:::

[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-678981 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
