## Lower St. Lawrence Region Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#lower-st.-lawrence-region-wood-producers-levy-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1111
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1986-12-02

Order Respecting the Fixing and Imposing of Levies On and the Collecting
of Levies from Producers of Wood in the Lower St. Lawrence Area in the
Province of Quebec for Wood Marketed in Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Lower St. Lawrence Wood Producers' Syndicate, pursuant to section 4
of the Quebec Wood Order, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBBA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order respecting the fixing and imposing of levies on and
the collecting of levies from producers of wood in the lower
St. Lawrence area in the Province of Quebec for wood marketed in
interprovincial and export trade](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1111).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBBA){#footnote_e}SOR/83-713,

  :::

Rimouski, Quebec, November 25, 1986

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890196 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Lower St. Lawrence Region Wood Producers' Levy (Interprovincial and
Export Trade) Order.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890198 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of wood; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Syndicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Syndicate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Lower St. Lawrence Wood
    Producers' Syndicate; ([Syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wood produced in the Province of
    Quebec in the municipal counties of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Bonaventure in the municipalities of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} L'Ascension-de-Patapédia,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} St-Alexis-de-Matapédia,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} St-André-de-Restigouche,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} St-François-d'Assise, and

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} St-Laurent-de-Matapédia,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Matane,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} Matapédia,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} Rimouski,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Rivière-du-Loup except the municipalities of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Notre-Dame-du-Portage,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} St-Antonin, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} St-Patrice-de-la-Rivière-du-Loup, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} Témiscouata. ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890217 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  producer shall pay to the Syndicate for wood produced by him and
  marketed in interprovincial and export trade a general administration
  levy calculated at one of the following rates:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in cords of 128
    apparent cubic feet, \$0.75 per cord;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in cubic feet,
    \$0.88 per 100 cubic feet;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in foot-board
    measures, \$1.76 per 1,000 foot-board measure;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in cubic-foot
    tree lengths, \$0.0088 per cubic-foot tree length;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in apparent
    cubic metres, \$0.21 per apparent cubic metre; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} where the wood marketed is measured in solid cubic
    feet, \$0.32 per solid cubic foot.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the levy imposed by subsection (1), a
  producer shall pay to the Syndicate for wood produced by him and
  marketed in interprovincial and export trade the following research
  levy:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wood from coniferous trees, \$0.04
    per apparent cubic metre; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of wood from deciduous trees, \$0.02
    per apparent cubic metre.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the levies imposed by subsections (1)
  and (2), a producer shall pay to the Syndicate for wood produced by
  him and marketed in interprovincial and export trade for production
  into pulp, paper or chipboard the following working fund levy:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of pulpwood from coniferous trees and
    debarked pulpwood from deciduous trees, \$0.62 per apparent cubic
    metre; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of undebarked pulpwood from deciduous
    trees, \$0.41 per apparent cubic metre.

## [Method of Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890232 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a producer shall pay any levy payable by
  him pursuant to section 3 to the Syndicate at its headquarters in
  Rimouski, Quebec, within 30 days after the marketing of the wood in
  respect of which the levy is payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Syndicate markets wood in interprovincial
  or export trade on behalf of a producer, the Syndicate may deduct from
  any money payable to the producer by the Syndicate any levy payable by
  the producer, pursuant to section 3, in respect of that wood.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a producer does not pay the levy set out in
  section 3 directly to the Syndicate pursuant to subsection (1) and
  where the Syndicate has not deducted the levy pursuant to
  subsection (2), any person who markets that producer's wood in
  interprovincial or export trade shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} deduct from any money payable to the producer by
    that person any levy payable by the producer to the Syndicate
    pursuant to section 3; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pay over and account to the Syndicate for the levy
    within 30 days after the marketing of the wood in respect of which
    the levy is payable.
