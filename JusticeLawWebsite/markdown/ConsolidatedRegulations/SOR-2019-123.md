## Critical Habitat of the Western Brook Lamprey (Lampetra richardsoni) Morrison Creek Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-western-brook-lamprey-lampetra-richardsoni-morrison-creek-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-123
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2019-05-06

Critical Habitat of the Western Brook Lamprey (Lampetra richardsoni)
Morrison Creek Population Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Western Brook Lamprey ([Lampetra richardsoni]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Morrison Creek population is a wildlife species that is
listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species
at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBFA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBFA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the action plan that identified the critical habitat of that
species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

And whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBDA} of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBDA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Western Brook Lamprey (Lampetra richardsoni) Morrison
Creek Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-123).

Ottawa, May 3, 2019

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Western Brook Lamprey ([Lampetra richardsoni]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) Morrison Creek population, which is identified in the action
plan for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public
Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
