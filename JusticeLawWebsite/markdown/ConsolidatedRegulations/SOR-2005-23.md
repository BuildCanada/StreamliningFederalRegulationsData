## Reporting of Exported Goods Regulations {#reporting-of-exported-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-23
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 2005-02-01

Reporting of Exported Goods Regulations

P.C. 2005-46 2005-02-01
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Solicitor General of Canada, pursuant to section 95[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCAA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Reporting of Exported Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-23).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717572 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bulk goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bulk goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that are loose or in
    mass, such that they are confined only by the permanent structures
    of a large container or a transport unit, without intermediate
    containment or intermediate packaging. ([marchandises en
    vrac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of goods that are
    exported, means the person, other than the exporter of the goods,
    who transports them from Canada. ([transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place. ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that are
    exported for sale or for any industrial, occupational, commercial,
    institutional or other similar use. ([marchandises
    commerciales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    goods that are exported, means a person who provides to the exporter
    customs services relating to the exportation of the goods, other
    than the sole service of transporting the goods from Canada, and
    includes an agent or other representative of the exporter, a customs
    broker and a freight forwarder. ([prestataire de services
    douaniers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[exporter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of goods that are
    exported, means the holder of a business number for the purposes of
    the Act who exports commercial goods or causes them to be exported.
    ([exportateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export reporting office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export reporting office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a customs
    office designated under section 5 of the Act for the purpose of
    reporting goods that are exported. ([bureau de déclaration des
    exportations]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[homogeneous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[homogeneous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} closely resemble each other in respect of their
      component materials and characteristics; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are intended to be used for the same purpose.
      ([marchandises homogènes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[restricted goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[restricted goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods the exportation
    of which is prohibited, controlled or regulated under the Act or any
    other Act of Parliament. ([marchandises d'exportation
    restreinte]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[time-sensitive goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[time-sensitive goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} would lose their value or principal utility if
      not immediately exported for use within a limited time after the
      exportation; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are part of a manufacturing and stock control
      system in which goods are produced and delivered as they are
      required. ([marchandises d'utilité temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Classes of Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717589 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 95 of the Act, the following classes of persons are required to
report goods that are exported:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} exporters;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} carriers; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} customs service providers.

## [Reporting by the Exporter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717594 .Part}

### [Reporting at an Export Reporting Office]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717595 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and sections 4, 6 and 7, all goods that are exported
  shall be reported in writing by the exporter at an export reporting
  office

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by mail, not less than
    two hours before the goods are delivered to the post office where
    the goods are mailed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by vessel, not less than
    48 hours before the goods are loaded onto the vessel;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by aircraft, not less
    than two hours before the goods are loaded on board the aircraft;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by rail, not less than
    two hours before the railcar on which the goods have been loaded is
    assembled to form part of a train for export; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by any other means,
    immediately before the exportation of the goods.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Live animals, bulk goods, homogeneous goods or
  time-sensitive goods may, unless they are restricted goods, be
  reported immediately before they are exported.

### [Summary Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717604 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  exporter may, in writing on a monthly basis, report bulk goods or
  homogeneous goods that are exported if, before the exportation,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exporter has received written confirmation from
    the chief officer of customs at the appropriate regional office of
    the Agency that the goods qualify as bulk goods or homogeneous goods
    that may be reported in accordance with this section; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the goods are restricted goods, the exporter
    has received written confirmation from an appropriate official
    responsible for the administration of the Act of Parliament under
    which the exportation of the goods is restricted that the goods may,
    under that Act, be reported in accordance with this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  sent to the Chief Statistician of Canada within five business days
  after the end of the month in which the goods are exported.

### [Restricted Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717610 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If goods
  that are exported are restricted goods, the exporter of the goods
  shall, in addition to making the report referred to in section 3 or 4,
  as applicable, provide evidence that the exportation of the goods
  complies with the Act of Parliament under which the exportation of the
  goods is restricted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The evidence referred to in subsection (1) and a copy
  of the report made under section 3 shall be provided to the chief
  officer of customs, within the applicable time set out in that
  section, and the goods made available for inspection, at

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the place specified in the permit authorizing the
    exportation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no place is specified in that permit, at the
    export reporting office located closest to the place of exit of the
    goods from Canada.

### [Exceptions to Reporting by the Exporter]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717616 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8, the
following classes of goods may, if the goods are not restricted goods,
be exported without being reported by the exporter:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal and household effects, other than those of
  an emigrant, that are not for resale or commercial use;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} commercial goods having a value of less than \$2,000
  Canadian;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} conveyances that would, if they were imported, be
  classified at the time of importation under tariff item
  No. 9801.10.00, 9801.20.00 or 9801.30.00 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} cargo containers that would, if they were imported,
  be classified at the time of importation under tariff item
  No. 980l.10.00 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the
  schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} reusable skids, drums, pallets, straps and similar
  goods used by a carrier in the international commercial transportation
  of goods;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} goods exported by diplomatic embassy or mission
  personnel for their personal or official use;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} personal gifts and donations of goods, excluding
  conveyances;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} goods that were imported into Canada and are exported
  from Canada after being transported in transit through Canada en route
  to a non-Canadian destination;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} goods that were manufactured or produced in Canada
  and that are exported from Canada for the purpose of being transhipped
  through another country to another Canadian destination;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} goods exported for repair or warranty repair that
  will be returned to Canada;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} goods for use as ships' stores by a Canadian carrier;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} goods manufactured or produced outside Canada and
  removed for export from a bonded warehouse or sufferance warehouse;
  and

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} goods, other than goods exported for further
  processing, that will be returned to Canada within 12 months after the
  date of exportation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 8, goods
that are described in or come within the scope of a written arrangement
made between the Government of Canada and the government of another
country may be exported to that country without being reported by the
exporter if, under that arrangement, information respecting the
exportation will be collected by that country as information relating to
importations into that country and will be shared with Canada.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Goods that are exported and
are of a class described in section 6 or of a type referred to in
section 7 shall be reported in writing by the exporter before the goods
leave Canada if an officer, at the time of the exportation, suspects on
reasonable grounds that they are being exported contrary to an Act of
Parliament and, for that reason, requests that they be reported.

## [Reporting by a Carrier]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717633 .Part}

### [Conveyances Used to Export Goods]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717634 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if goods are exported by a carrier by means of a
  conveyance, the exportation of the conveyance shall be reported in
  writing by the carrier before the exportation

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel, at the export reporting
    office located closest to the place where the goods are loaded
    aboard the vessel for export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a railcar, at the export reporting
    office located closest to the place where the railcar on which the
    goods are loaded is assembled to form part of a train for export;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, at the export reporting office
    located closest to the place of exit of the conveyance from Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  conveyance that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a regularly scheduled aircraft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a highway conveyance, except if the conveyance is
    used to export goods from Canada in the circumstances described in
    paragraph 6(i).

### [Goods Other Than the Conveyances Used to Export Them]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717643 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 11 to
13, all goods that are exported by a carrier, other than the conveyance
used to export them, shall be reported in writing by the carrier, before
the exportation, at the export reporting office located closest to the
place where the goods are loaded on board the conveyance for export.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Goods that are imported
into Canada and are exported from Canada after being transported in
transit through Canada en route to a non-Canadian destination shall be
reported in writing by the carrier before the goods leave Canada

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by mail, at the export
  reporting office located closest to the post office where the goods
  are mailed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by vessel, at the export
  reporting office located closest to the place where the goods are
  loaded aboard the vessel for export;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by aircraft, at the export
  reporting office located closest to the place of departure of the
  aircraft from Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by rail, at the export
  reporting office located closest to the place where the railcar on
  which the goods are loaded is assembled to form part of a train for
  export; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by any other means, at the
  export reporting office located nearest the place of exit of the goods
  from Canada.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Goods that are exported
by a carrier by means of a highway conveyance, other than goods
described in section 11, are not required to be reported by the carrier
unless an officer, at the time of the exportation, suspects on
reasonable grounds that they are being exported contrary to an Act of
Parliament and, for that reason, requests that they be reported.

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), goods
  that are exported by a carrier by means of a conveyance other than a
  highway conveyance and that have been or will be reported by the
  exporter in accordance with these Regulations may be reported by the
  carrier after their exportation if the carrier

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has, before the exportation, given an undertaking
    in writing to an officer that the carrier is exporting only such
    goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has on that basis been authorized in writing by
    that officer to report the goods in accordance with this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods referred to in subsection (1) shall be reported
  by the carrier in writing at an export reporting office

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by vessel, within three
    business days after the departure of the vessel from the place in
    Canada where it is loaded;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by rail, within one
    business day after the day on which the railcar on which the goods
    are loaded is assembled to form part of a train for export; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the goods are exported by aircraft, within one
    business day after the day on which the aircraft departs from the
    place in Canada where it is loaded.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of goods referred to in paragraph (2)(a),
  the carrier must also report the goods in writing to the Chief
  Statistician of Canada within five business days after the end of the
  month in which the conveyance departs from Canada.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Goods referred to in subsection (1) shall be reported
  in writing by the carrier at an export reporting office before the
  goods leave Canada if an officer, at the time of the exportation,
  suspects on reasonable grounds that they are being exported contrary
  to an Act of Parliament and, for that reason, requests that they be
  reported.

## [Reporting by a Customs Service Provider]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717662 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Goods that are exported
by a customs service provider shall be reported in writing by the
customs service provider at an export reporting office before the goods
leave Canada if an officer, at the time of the exportation, suspects on
reasonable grounds that they are being exported contrary to an Act of
Parliament and, for that reason, requests that they be reported.

## [Oral Reporting of Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717664 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding anything
in these Regulations, the following goods may, if they are exported, be
reported orally at the export reporting office located closest to the
place of exit of the goods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian military conveyance that does not contain
  any goods or cargo, if the conveyance is not a restricted good;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods that are the property of and are for the
  exclusive use of the Department of National Defence and that are being
  exported by that Department to its defence bases abroad or in support
  of Canadian Forces deployment operations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods that are being exported because of a medical
  emergency, fire, flood or other disaster that threatens life, property
  or the environment.

## [Manner of Reporting Fishing Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717671 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Each exportation, during
a fishing season, of a commercial fishing vessel that is registered or
licensed under the [Canada Shipping Act](/eng/acts/S-9) need not be
reported if the carrier, before the first exportation of the vessel
during that fishing season, reports the vessel in writing to the chief
officer of customs at an export reporting office.

## [Manner of Reporting Catch]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717673 .Part}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** All catch that is caught
by a Canadian registered commercial fishing vessel in Canadian
territorial waters and then delivered by that vessel to a port outside
Canada, or delivered from that vessel to a foreign registered commercial
fishing vessel bound for a port outside Canada, shall be reported in
writing by the exporter to the chief officer of customs at an export
reporting office, immediately on the return of the vessel to Canada.

## [Manner of Reporting Ferries]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717675 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If a vessel is used by a
carrier on a particular day solely or principally for the transportation
of motor vehicles or passengers across international waters, the carrier
shall, immediately on the return of the vessel to Canada after its last
trip on that day, report the vessel in writing to the chief officer of
customs at the export reporting office located closest to the place at
which the vessel lands in Canada.

## [Manner of Reporting Conveyances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717677 .Part}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** Any person who intends to
permanently export a conveyance shall, before the exportation and in
addition to making any other report required by these Regulations,
provide documentation indicating the vehicle identification number (VIN)
of the conveyance to the chief officer of customs at the export
reporting office located closest to the place of exit of the conveyance.
