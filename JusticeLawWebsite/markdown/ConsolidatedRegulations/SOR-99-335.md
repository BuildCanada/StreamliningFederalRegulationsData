## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031806 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[private buoy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}means a buoy that is not owned by
the federal government, a provincial government or a government agency.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031808 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
every private buoy other than private buoys used to mark fishing gear.

## [Placement Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031810 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place in
any Canadian waters a private buoy that interferes with or is likely to
interfere with the navigation of any vessel, or that misleads or is
likely to mislead the operator of any vessel.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall place a private buoy in any Canadian waters unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the part of the buoy that shows above the surface
    of the water is at least 15.25 cm wide and at least 30.5 cm high;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the buoy displays, on opposite sides, the capital
    letters "PRIV" that are

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as large as is practical for the size of the
      buoy, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} white when the background colour is red, green
      or black;

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} black when the background colour is white or
      yellow;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the buoy complies with the requirements set out in
    Canadian Aids to Navigation (TP 968) published by the Canadian Coast
    Guard in 1995, as amended from time to time;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the buoy displays, in a conspicuous location and in
    a permanent and legible manner, the name, address and telephone
    number of its owner;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the buoy is constructed and maintained in a manner
    and with materials that ensure that it remains in position and
    retains the characteristics specified in paragraphs (a) to (d); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the buoy's anchor is constructed and maintained in
    a manner and with materials that ensure that it remains in position.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner of a private buoy placed in any Canadian
  waters shall ensure that the information required by paragraph (1)(d)
  is accurate at all times.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If there is a need for
increased visibility or better identification of a buoy for safety and
the prevention of accidents, the Minister of Transport may order the
owner of the buoy to modify it according to the requirements set out in
the Procedures Manual for Design and Review of Short-range Aids to
Navigation Systems (TP 9677), published in March 1989 by the Canadian
Coast Guard, as amended from time to time.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No person shall place in
any Canadian waters a private buoy that has a light unless the light
remains lit throughout the night and meets the requirements referred to
in paragraph 4(1)(c).

## [Removal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1031828 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Transport
may remove from any Canadian waters a private buoy that does not comply
with these Regulations.
