## Related Party Transactions (Insurance Companies) Regulations {#related-party-transactions-insurance-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

Regulations Prescribing Transactions that a Company May Enter Into with

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 533 and 703 of the
[Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981532 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Related Party Transactions (Insurance Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-276).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981534 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8). (*Loi*)

## [Prescribed Related Party Transactions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-981536 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
533 of the Act, a company may, directly or indirectly, enter into any of
the following transactions with a related party of the company:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an allocation of an amount by or to the company made
  pursuant to subsection 18(2.3), 125(3), 127(10.3), 181.5(2),
  190.15(2), 190.16(2) or 191.1(3) of the [Income Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an allocation of an amount by or to the company made
  pursuant to subsection 74.1(10) of the Corporations Tax Act of
  Ontario;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the company agrees to pay all or a portion of the
    tax of the related party, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the related party agrees to pay the company an
    amount at least equal to the additional costs incurred by the
    company, including additional taxes payable under the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), as a result of the company entering into the
    agreement or amended agreement;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an agreement or amended agreement described in
  subsection 191.3(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3) under
  which the related party agrees to pay all or a portion of the tax of
  the company;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the issue by or to the company of conversion
  privileges, options or rights to acquire securities the issuance of
  which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which a security is being or has been issued;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the purchase, for the purpose of cancellation, of any
  shares or debt obligations issued by or to the company the purchase of
  which is not otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions
  pursuant to which the shares or debt obligations were issued;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the redemption of any redeemable shares or any debt
  obligations issued by or to the company the redemption of which is not
  otherwise provided for in the terms and conditions pursuant to which
  the shares or debt obligations were issued; or

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the payment or delivery of money or property by or to
  the company as a consequence of a reduction of the stated capital of
  the company or related party.
