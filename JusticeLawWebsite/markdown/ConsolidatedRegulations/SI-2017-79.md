## Order Designating the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as a Department for the purposes of the Act {#order-designating-the-department-of-indigenous-services-canada-as-a-department-for-the-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-79
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2017-12-13

Order Designating the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as a
Department for the purposes of the Act

P.C. 2017-1469 2017-11-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, effective November 30, 2017,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133){#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBBBAA},
  designates the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as a
  department for the purposes of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_608630-E_hq_133}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133){#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBAAAA},
  designates the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous
  Services Canada as the deputy head of that entity for the purposes of
  that Act.
:::
