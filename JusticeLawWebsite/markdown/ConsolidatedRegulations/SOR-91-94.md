## Taxation Statistics Diskette Fee Order {#taxation-statistics-diskette-fee-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-94
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-01-11

Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by a Person for a Computer Diskette
Containing Tables of Taxation Statistics
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to Order in Council P.C.
1988-2270 of September 29, 1988[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-94_e_hq_5226){#fn_SOR-91-94_e_hq_5226-ID0EFBCA},
made pursuant to paragraph 19(b) of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed [Order prescribing the
fee to be paid by a person for a computer diskette containing tables of
taxation statistics](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-94).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-94_e_hq_5226-ID0EFBCA){#fn_SOR-91-94_e_hq_5226}SI/88-188,
  1988 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4364
  :::

Ottawa, January 10, 1991

::: FlushRight
OTTO JELINEK
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of National Revenue*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-940876 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Taxation Statistics Diskette Fee
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-94).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-940878 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A person who makes a
request to the Minister of National Revenue for a computer diskette
containing taxation statistics shall pay a fee of \$27 for each diskette
supplied to the person.
