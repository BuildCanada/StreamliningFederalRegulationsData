## Uranium Mines (Ontario) Employment Exclusion Order {#uranium-mines-ontario-employment-exclusion-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-181
:::

[CANADA LABOUR CODE](/eng/acts/L-2)

Registration 1987-03-26

Order Excluding in Whole From the Application of Part IV of the Canada
Labour Code Employment on or in Connection With Uranium Mines in The
Province of Ontario that are Regulated Pursuant to the Atomic Energy
Control Act

P.C. 1987-583 1987-03-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 80.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e){#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBAA} of
the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Order excluding in whole from the application of Part IV of the
Canada Labour Code employment on or in connection with uranium mines in
the Province of Ontario that are regulated pursuant to the Atomic Energy
Control Act, effective April 2, 1987.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1star_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote1star_e}S.C.
  1984, c. 39, s. 19
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900895 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Uranium Mines (Ontario) Employment Exclusion
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-181).

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-900897 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Employment on or in
connection with uranium mines in the Province of Ontario that are
regulated pursuant to the Atomic Energy Control Act is hereby excluded,
in whole, from the application of Part IV of the [Canada Labour
Code](/eng/acts/L-2).
