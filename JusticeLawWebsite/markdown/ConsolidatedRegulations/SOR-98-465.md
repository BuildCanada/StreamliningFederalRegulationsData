## Regulations Prescribing Exclusions from Certain Definitions of the Criminal Code (International Sporting Competition Handguns) {#regulations-prescribing-exclusions-from-certain-definitions-of-the-criminal-code-international-sporting-competition-handguns .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-465
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 1998-09-16

Regulations Prescribing Exclusions from Certain Definitions of the
Criminal Code (International Sporting Competition Handguns)

P.C. 1998-1665 1998-09-16
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Justice, pursuant to the definitions
[[prescribed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0ENBAA}, [[prohibited
device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EKBAA} and
[[cm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHBAA} in subsection
84(1) and to subsection 117.15(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby makes the annexed [Regulations
Prescribing Exclusions from Certain Definitions of the Criminal Code
(International Sporting Competition
Handguns)](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-465).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0ENBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1995, c.
  39, s. 139
  :::
:::

## [Excluded Handguns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024370 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The handguns listed in the
schedule are excluded from paragraph (a) of the definition [[prohibited
firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Excluded Handgun Barrels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024372 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A handgun barrel that is a
component or part of a handgun referred to in section 1 is excluded from
paragraph (b) of the definition [[prohibited
device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 84(1) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024374 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on December 1, 1998.
