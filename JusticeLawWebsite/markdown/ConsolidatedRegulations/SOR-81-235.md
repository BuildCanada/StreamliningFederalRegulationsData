## Northern Pipeline Notice of Objection Regulations {#northern-pipeline-notice-of-objection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-235
:::

[NORTHERN PIPELINE ACT](/eng/acts/N-26)

Registration 1981-03-12

Regulations Prescribing the Notice of Objection Form To Be Used by a
Company that Objects to an Assessment Under Section 26 of the Northern
Pipeline Act

P.C. 1981-674 1981-03-12
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister responsible for the Northern Pipeline Agency[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-81-235_e_hq_6624){#fn_SOR-81-235_e_hq_6624-ID0EGACA},
pursuant to subsection 26(3) of the [Northern Pipeline
Act](/eng/acts/N-26), is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations
prescribing the Notice of Objection from to be used by a Company that
objects to an assessment under section 26 of the Northern Pipeline
Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-81-235_e_hq_6625){#fn_SOR-81-235_e_hq_6625-ID0EBACA}.

### [Notice of Objection]{.HTitleText1} {#notice-of-objection .SchedHeadL1}

For use by a company that objects to an assessment under subsection
26(1.1) of the [Northern Pipeline Act](/eng/acts/N-26).

A separate Notice of Objection must be served on the Minister in respect
of each ASSESSMENT to which objection is taken. If the facts and reasons
set out in one Notice are identical to the facts and reasons in another,
this may be indicated and one statement of facts and reasons will be
sufficient.

TWO completed copies of this Notice (signed by an authorized officer of
the company) are to be sent by REGISTERED MAIL to the Minister
responsible for the Northern Pipeline Agency, Post Office Box 1605,
Station B, Ottawa, Ontario, K1P 5A0. The envelope containing the Notices
must be postmarked within 30 days from the date of mailing of the Notice
of Assessment to which objection is being taken.
