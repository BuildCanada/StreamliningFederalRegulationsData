## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ferry-boats

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted of the
customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of ferry-boats, classified under
subheading No. 8901.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the
schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), excluding those
produced in Canada that have been exported and then subsequently
re-imported into Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted on
the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ferry-boat was permanently imported into Canada
  for the first time on or after October 1, 2015;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to the
  Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years
  after the date of importation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer files, on request, all evidence that is
  required by the Canada Border Services Agency to determine eligibility
  for remission.
