## Licensed Dealers for Controlled Drugs and Narcotics (Veterinary Use) Fees Regulations {#licensed-dealers-for-controlled-drugs-and-narcotics-veterinary-use-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-5
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1997-12-17

Licensed Dealers for Controlled Drugs and Narcotics (Veterinary Use)
Fees Regulations

P.C. 1997-1897  1997-12-17
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Health and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph
19.1(a)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EHCAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) and, considering that it
is in the public interest to remit certain debts, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EDCAA} of that
Act, hereby makes the annexed Licensed Dealers for Controlled Drugs and
Narcotics Fees Regulations (1056).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024827 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[controlled drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[controlled drug]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section G.01.001 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870). ([drogue
    contrôlée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer's licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer's licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued under section G.02.007 of the
      [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued under section 10.1 of the
      [Narcotic Control
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041). ([licence de
      distributeur autorisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dealer's licence for controlled drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer's licence for controlled
    drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-83, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[dealer's licence for narcotics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dealer's licence for narcotics]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2011-83, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[narcotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the [Narcotic Control
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041). ([stupéfiant ]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[package/label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package/label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to put a controlled drug
    or narcotic in its immediate container or to affix the inner or
    outer label to the controlled drug or
    narcotic. ([emballer-étiqueter]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sell]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes offer for sale, expose for
    sale, have in possession for sale and distribute, whether or not the
    distribution is made for consideration. ([vente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a building specified in a dealer's licence and
      located more than one kilometre from any other building specified
      in the licence; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than one building specified in a dealer's
      licence, all of which are located within one kilometre of each
      other. ([site]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024844 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to applicants for dealer's licences that relate only to controlled
drugs for veterinary use or to narcotics for veterinary use.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-83, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-83, s. 4\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any branch or agency of the Government of Canada or
  of the government of a province; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person or organization engaged only in scientific
  investigation.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-83, s. 4
:::

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024856 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dealer's licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The fee
  for a dealer's licence or its renewal is \$1,750 for each site at
  which the licensed activities are to be conducted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First year of activities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant has not completed their first
  calendar year of conducting activities under a dealer's licence, the
  fee for the licence or its renewal is \$875 for each site at which the
  licensed activities are to be conducted.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- conditions

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection 5(2), remission is granted to an applicant in respect of
  their dealer's licence or its renewal if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee payable is greater than an amount equal to
    1.5% of the applicant's actual gross revenue from the sale, testing
    and packaging/labelling of controlled drugs and narcotics for the
    previous calendar year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant provides a statement to the Minister
    of Health signed by the individual responsible for the applicant's
    financial affairs that sets out the actual gross revenue

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has not completed a calendar
      year following the issuance of their first licence, within one
      month after the end of that year, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, if applicable, with the
      application for the renewal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission --- amount

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of the remission is equal to the
  difference between the fee payable for the licence and the amount
  calculated in accordance with paragraph (1)(a).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional Information

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister of Health determines, on the basis of any available
  information, that the statement provided under paragraph 4(1)(b) is
  not sufficient to determine the actual gross revenue, the Minister
  must require, in writing, the applicant to provide additional
  information that is necessary to make that determination, within 90
  days after the day on which the request is made.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission not granted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Remission is not granted if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant fails to provide the additional
    information within the period specified; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister determines that the additional
    information, provided within the period specified, is not sufficient
    to determine the actual gross revenue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of payment

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the fee for the dealer's licence is
  payable on issuance by the Minister of Health of a written notice
  requiring payment once the licence has been issued or renewed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deferred payment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an applicant referred to in subsection
  3(2), the fee is payable on issuance of the notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} once the statement referred to in subparagraph
    4(1)(b)(i) is provided; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, during the second month after
    the end of the calendar year following the issuance of the first
    licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment --- additional information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If additional information is required under
  subsection 5(1), the fee is payable on issuance of the notice once the
  licence has been issued or renewed

  - [a)]{.lawlabel} after the Minister has assessed any additional
    information, if the applicant has provided it; or

  - [b)]{.lawlabel} after the expiry of the period set out in subsection
    5(1), if the applicant has not provided the information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An applicant for a dealer's
licence is not entitled to a remission under subsection 4(1) if, in
order to conduct an activity referred to in the application, the
applicant must also hold an establishment licence under Division 1A of
Part C of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

- SOR/2011-83, s. 5
