## Dogfish Exemption Notice {#dogfish-exemption-notice .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 836
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Notice Excepting Dogfish from the Operation of Section 29 of the
Fisheries Act
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566238 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Notice may be cited as
the [Dogfish Exemption Notice](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._836).

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-566240 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Dogfish and other fish
caught incidental to fishing for dogfish in the Goose Island Banks of
Hecate Straits are hereby excepted from the operation of section 29 of
the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14).
