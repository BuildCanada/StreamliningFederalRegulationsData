## Personnel Training for the Assistance of Persons with Disabilities Regulations {#personnel-training-for-the-assistance-of-persons-with-disabilities-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-42
:::

[CANADA TRANSPORTATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10.4)

Registration 1994-01-13

Regulations Respecting the Training of Personnel Employed in
Transportation-Related Facilities or Premises or by Carriers, to Assist
Persons with Disabilities Travelling Within the Transportation Network

P.C. 1994-21  1994-01-13
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 63.1(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-94-42_e_hq_5253){#fn_SOR-94-42_e_hq_5253-ID0EICAA}
of the National Transportation Act, 1987[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-94-42_e_hq_5254){#fn_SOR-94-42_e_hq_5254-ID0EFCAA},
is pleased hereby to approve the annexed Regulations respecting the
training of personnel employed in transportation-related facilities or
premises or by carriers, to assist persons with disabilities travelling
within the transportation network, made by the National Transportation
Agency on October 28, 1993, effective on the first anniversary of the
date of their publication in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964016 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Personnel Training for the Assistance of Persons with
Disabilities Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-42).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964018 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Transportation
    Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[attendant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[attendant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who travels with a
    person with a disability to provide a service related to a
    disability that is not usually provided by a carrier;
    ([accompagnateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Canadian citizen or a
    permanent resident within the meaning of subsection 2(1) of the
    Immigration Act, a government in Canada or an agent thereof, or any
    other person or entity that is controlled in fact by Canadians and,
    where applicable, of which at least 75 per cent of the voting
    interests are held and controlled by Canadians, that operates a
    passenger transportation service within or from Canada;
    ([transporteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[commuter rail service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commuter rail service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    passenger-train service that accommodates principally persons who
    commute between points on the railway of the company that provides
    the service; ([service ferroviaire de banlieue]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, or employee of
    that person, who performs services pursuant to a contract or an
    arrangement with a carrier or a terminal operator, and who is not an
    employee of the carrier or the terminal operator, but does not
    include a travel agency; ([entrepreneur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical, sensory,
    developmental, mental health or medical functional limitation or
    restriction that affects a person in such a manner that the person
    is a person with a disability; ([déficience]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[lodge operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lodge operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an operation that
    involves a commercial air service of a small aeroplane, as defined
    in section 2 of the Air Carriers Using Small Aeroplanes Order;
    ([hôtel pavillonnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mobility aid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mobility aid]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes wheelchairs, scooters,
    transfer chairs, walkers, canes, crutches and braces; ([aide à la
    mobilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person with a disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person with a disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    is, has been or will be a passenger on a service operated by a
    carrier and who, because of a disability, requires services that are
    not usually extended to other passengers, including assistance

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when making travel arrangements,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} when embarking and disembarking,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on board a vehicle, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} during any movements between facilities inside or
      outside the terminal buildings that involve transportation-related
      services; ([personne ayant une déficience]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[service animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service animal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an animal that is
    required by a person with a disability for assistance and is
    certified, in writing, as having been trained to assist a person
    with a disability by a professional service animal institution;
    ([animal aidant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[terminal operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[terminal operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the owner, operator
    or lessee of facilities or premises related to the transport of
    passengers within the transportation network governed by the Act;
    ([exploitant de terminal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transportation-related services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transportation-related services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes
    passenger security screening, baggage handling, vehicle rental,
    public parking and, in the case of air terminals, all ground
    transportation from the terminal. ([services liés au
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-700, s. 3
- SOR/98-197, s. 13
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964038 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to every carrier except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air carrier

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that provides service only to air terminals where
      there were less than 10,000 enplaned and deplaned passengers in
      each of the two preceding calendar years,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} whose gross annual revenue was less than
      \$500,000 in either of the two preceding calendar years, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whose operations are limited to serving the
      needs of a lodge operation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a rail carrier in respect of commuter rail services
    provided by that carrier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a carrier that operates an extra-provincial bus
    undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations apply to every terminal operator
  except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air terminal operator at whose terminal there
    were less than 10,000 enplaned and deplaned passengers in each of
    the two preceding calendar years; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an extra-provincial bus terminal operator.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-197, s. 14
:::

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 3,
these Regulations do not apply to any carrier or terminal operator that
is subject to Part 1 of the [Accessible Transportation for Persons with
Disabilities Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-244).

## [Employees and Contractors Who Interact with the Public]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964052 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier and terminal
operator shall ensure that, consistent with its type of operation, all
employees and contractors of the carrier or terminal operator who
provide transportation-related services and who may be required to
interact with the public or to make decisions in respect of the carriage
of persons with disabilities receive a level of training appropriate to
the requirements of their function in the following areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policies and procedures of the carrier or
  terminal operator with respect to persons with disabilities, including
  relevant regulatory requirements;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the needs of those persons with disabilities most
  likely to require additional services, recognition of those needs, and
  the responsibilities of the carrier or terminal operator in relation
  to those persons, including the level of assistance, methods of
  communication and aids or devices generally required by persons with
  disabilities; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the necessary skills for providing assistance to
  persons with disabilities, including the role of the attendant, and
  the needs of persons with disabilities travelling with a service
  animal, including the role and the needs of that animal.

## [Employees and Contractors Who Provide Physical Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964057 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier shall ensure
that, consistent with its type of operation, all employees and
contractors of the carrier who may be required to provide physical
assistance to a person with a disability receive the training described
in section 4 and a level of training appropriate to the requirements of
their function in the following areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} assisting with mobility aids through doors and on
  irregular and multi-level surfaces, steps, curbs and elevators;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transferring a person with a disability between the
  person's own mobility aid and a mobility aid provided by a carrier and
  between a mobility aid and the person's passenger seat, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} seeking information from a person with a disability
    with respect to the person's preferred method of transfer and
    information with respect to any other special measures required to
    ensure the safety and comfort of the person with a disability, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} performing appropriate lifting techniques to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} execute various types of transfer with maximum
      consideration for the dignity, safety and comfort of the person
      with a disability, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} avoid injury to the employee or contractor making
      the transfer;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} guiding and orienting a person who is blind or whose
  visual impairment affects that person's mobility; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} assisting a person who has limitations in balance,
  agility or coordination that affect that person's mobility.

## [Employees and Contractors Who Handle Mobility Aids]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964067 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier shall ensure
that, consistent with its type of operation, all employees and
contractors of the carrier who may be required to handle mobility aids
receive the training described in section 4 and a level of training
appropriate to the requirements of their function in the following
areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} different types of mobility aids;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} requirements, limitations and procedures for
  securing, carrying and stowing mobility aids in the passenger
  compartment of a vehicle; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} proper methods of carrying and stowing mobility aids
  in the baggage compartment of a vehicle, including the disassembling,
  packaging, unpackaging and assembling of the mobility aids.

## [Employees and Contractors Who Assist with Special Equipment or Aids]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964072 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier shall ensure
that, consistent with its type of operation, all employees and
contractors of the carrier who may be required to assist with special
equipment or aids receive the training described in section 4 and a
level of training appropriate to the requirements of their function in
the following areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} telephone devices for persons who are deaf or hard of
  hearing, electronic signage, and audio or video equipment;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mechanical lifts, ramps and other level-change
  devices; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on-board oxygen, on-board electrical supply,
  connection of auxiliary respirator systems and installation of
  stretchers.

## [Time Limit for Completion of Training]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964077 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier and terminal
operator shall ensure that all employees and contractors of the carrier
or terminal operator who are required by these Regulations to receive
training complete their initial training within 60 days after the
commencement of their duties.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier and terminal
operator shall ensure that all employees and contractors of the carrier
or terminal operator receive periodic refresher training sessions
appropriate to the requirements of their function.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier and
terminal operator shall keep its training program current by
incorporating, at the earliest opportunity, any new information on
procedures and services offered or any specific technologies introduced
by the carrier or terminal operator to assist persons with disabilities.

## [Training Program Description]{.HTitleText1} {#h-964081 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every carrier and
terminal operator shall keep available for inspection by the Agency and
the general public a copy of its current training program prepared in
the form set out in the schedule and containing the information required
therein.
