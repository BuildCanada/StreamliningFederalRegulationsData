## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832153 .Part}

Definition of [[pacifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[pacifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pacifier that has a nipple and
includes a similar product that is intended for use in a baby's mouth.

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832156 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Toxic substance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any pacifier that contains
a toxic substance must comply with section 25 of the [Toys
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-17) in relation to that
substance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit of volatile N-nitrosamines

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A pacifier must not contain
more than 10 µg/kg total volatile N-nitrosamines, as determined by
dichloromethane extraction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hazards

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A pacifier must be free
from any part that may cause, under reasonably foreseeable conditions of
use, injury to the user due to the following hazards:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} obstruction of the pharyngeal orifice;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} strangulation;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ingestion or aspiration of the pacifier or any of its
  parts; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} wounding.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional requirements

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A pacifier must meet the
following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nipple must be attached to a guard of such
  dimensions that it cannot pass through the opening in the template
  illustrated in Schedule 1 when the nipple is centered on the opening
  and a force of 9.8 N is applied axially to the nipple in such a way as
  to induce the guard to pull through the opening in the template;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when tested in accordance with Schedule 2,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the nipple must remain attached to the guard, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any part that separates from the pacifier must
    not, in a non-compressed state, fit entirely into the small parts
    cylinder illustrated in Schedule 3;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any loop of cord or other material that is attached
  to the pacifier must not be more than 356 mm in circumference; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any ring or handle must be hinged, collapsible or
  flexible.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-832175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to [Canada Consumer Product
Safety Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or Regulations

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Written
  information that appears on a pacifier, that accompanies one or that
  is in any advertisement for one must not make any direct or indirect
  reference to the [Canada Consumer Product Safety
  Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68) or these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representations --- use or modification

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any written information that appears on or that
  accompanies a pacifier, and any advertisement of one, must not make
  representations in respect of the use of or modification to the
  pacifier that would result in the failure of the pacifier to meet a
  requirement of these Regulations.

## [SCHEDULE 2]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 5(b))]{.OriginatingRef}[Structural Integrity Test]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-832190 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of the pacifier:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hold the nipple of the pacifier in a fixed position
    and apply to the ring or handle of the pacifier a force of 44.5
     ± 1.0 N in the plane of the axis of the nipple at a rate of
    4.5 ± 1.0 N/s and then maintain the final force for 10 ± 0.5 s;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} hold the guard of the pacifier in a fixed position
    and apply to the ring or handle of the pacifier a force of  44.5
    ± 1.0 N in the plane perpendicular to the axis of the nipple at a
    rate of 4.5 ± 1.0 N/s and then maintain the final force for
    10 ± 0.5 s;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repeat the procedure described in paragraph (b)
    with the force applied to the nipple of the pacifier;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immerse the pacifier in boiling water for
    10 ± 0.5 min, then remove the pacifier from the boiling water and
    allow to cool in air at 21 ± 5° C for 15 ± 0.5 min;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the procedures described in paragraphs (a)
    to (c); and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the procedures described in paragraphs (d)
    and (e) nine times.
