## Return of Packaged Alcohol to an Excise Warehouse Regulations {#return-of-packaged-alcohol-to-an-excise-warehouse-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-200
:::

[EXCISE ACT, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)

Registration 2003-06-05

Return of Packaged Alcohol to an Excise Warehouse Regulations

P.C. 2003-854 2003-06-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(o) of the
[Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Return of Packaged Alcohol to an Excise Warehouse
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-200).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702308 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations, "Act"
means the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702310 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 90(d) of the Act, a licensed user may return non-duty-paid
packaged alcohol to the excise warehouse licensee who supplied it if, at
the time of its return to the warehouse, the alcohol is packaged in the
same container in which it was packaged when it was removed from the
warehouse and the container

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been opened; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if opened, was opened by the licensed user solely for
  the purpose of analysis in accordance with paragraph 90(f) of the Act.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 91(c) of the Act, a registered user may return non-duty-paid
packaged spirits to the excise warehouse licensee who supplied them if,
at the time of their return to the warehouse, the spirits are packaged
in the same container in which they were packaged when they were removed
from the warehouse and the container

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been opened; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if opened, was opened by the registered user solely
  for the purpose of analysis in accordance with paragraph 91(b) of the
  Act.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
152 of the Act, duty-paid packaged alcohol that has been removed from an
excise warehouse may be entered into that warehouse as non-duty-paid
packaged alcohol if, at the time of its return to the warehouse, it is
packaged in the same container in which it was packaged when it was
removed from the warehouse and the container

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been opened; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if opened, was opened by the licensed user or the
  registered user solely for the purpose described in paragraph 2(b) or
  3(b), as applicable.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
153 of the Act, non-duty paid packaged alcohol that has been removed
from an excise warehouse may be entered into that warehouse as
non-duty-paid packaged alcohol if, at the time of its return to the
warehouse, it is packaged in the same container in which it was packaged
when it was removed from the warehouse and the container

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has not been opened; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if opened, was opened by the licensed user or the
  registered user solely for the purpose described in paragraph 2(b) or
  3(b), as applicable.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 185(2) of the Act, the Minister may refund the special duty
that has been paid on imported packaged spirits that are returned by a
licensed user to the excise warehouse of the excise warehouse licensee
referred to in that subsection if, at the time of their return to the
warehouse, the spirits are packaged in the same container in which they
were packaged when they were removed from the warehouse and the
container has not been opened.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702324 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations take
effect on July 1, 2003.
