## Order Authorizing the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources to Prescribe Fees {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-energy-mines-and-resources-to-prescribe-fees .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-2
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-01-11

Order Authorizing the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources to
Prescribe Fees

P.C. 1994-2131  1994-12-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraphs 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598){#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598-ID0EFDAA}
and 19.1(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598){#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598-ID0EDDAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources to prescribe by
order the fees or charges to be paid

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598-ID0EFDAA){#fn_SI-95-2_e_hq_6598}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 6
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a service or the use of a facility provided by
  the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources on behalf of Her Majesty
  in right of Canada, by the users or classes of users of the service or
  facility; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a right or privilege conferred by the Minister of
  Energy, Mines and Resources on behalf of Her Majesty in right of
  Canada by means of a licence, permit or other authorization, by the
  persons or classes of persons on whom the right or privilege is
  conferred.
