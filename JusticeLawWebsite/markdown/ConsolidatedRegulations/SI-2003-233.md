## Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department of Transport of Certain Portions of the Directorate-General of Maritime Programs {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-fisheries-and-oceans-to-the-department-of-transport-of-certain-portions-of-the-directorate-general-of-maritime-programs .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-233
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the
Department of Transport of Certain Portions of the Directorate-General
of Maritime Programs

P.C. 2003-2090  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Department of Fisheries and Oceans to the Department
of Transport the control and supervision of those portions of the
Directorate-General of Marine Programs that are responsible for policy
related to pleasure craft safety, marine navigation services, pollution
prevention and response, and navigable waters, effective December 12,
2003.
