## International Telecommunication Union Privileges and Immunities Order {#international-telecommunication-union-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-230
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 2000-06-08

International Telecommunication Union Privileges and Immunities Order

P.C. 2000-870  2000-06-08
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Minister of Finance, pursuant to
paragraphs 5(1)(a), (b), (c), (f), (g) and (h) of the [Foreign Missions
and International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2000-230_e_hq_5547){#fn_SOR-2000-230_e_hq_5547-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [International Telecommunication Union
Privileges and Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-230).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661370 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International
    Telecommunication Union. ([(Union)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Vienna Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Vienna
    Convention on Diplomatic Relations set out in Schedule I to the
    [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention de Vienne]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661375 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Union shall have the
legal capacities of a body corporate and the privileges and immunities
set out in Articles II and III of the Convention.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Representatives of foreign
states that are members of the Union shall have, during their journey to
and from the place of a meeting of the Union in Canada, as well as in
Canada,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the following privileges and immunities set out in
  Section 11 of Article IV of the Convention, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} immunity from personal arrest or detention and from
    seizure of their personal baggage and, in respect of words spoken or
    written and all acts done by them in their capacity as
    representatives, immunity from legal process of every kind,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} inviolability for all papers and documents,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the right to use codes and to receive papers or
    correspondence by courier or in sealed bags,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} exemption from immigration restrictions, alien
    registration and national service obligations in Canada when
    visiting or passing through Canada in the exercise of their
    functions,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the same immunities and facilities in respect of
    their personal baggage as are accorded to diplomatic envoys, and

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} such other privileges, immunities and facilities
    not inconsistent with the foregoing as diplomatic envoys enjoy,
    except that they shall have no right to claim exemption from customs
    duties on goods imported (otherwise than as part of their personal
    baggage) or from excise duties or sales taxes; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the privileges and immunities set out in Sections 12,
  13 and 14 of Article IV of the Convention, subject to Sections 15 and
  16 of that Article.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Senior officials of the
Union who are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents shall have
the privileges and immunities comparable to the privileges and
immunities accorded under the Vienna Convention to diplomatic agents.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Officials of the Union who
are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents shall have the
following privileges and immunities set out in Section 18 of Article V
of the Convention:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from legal process in respect of words
  spoken or written and all acts performed by them in their official
  capacity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} exemption from taxation on the salaries and
  emoluments paid to them by the Union;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immunity from national service obligations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} immunity from immigration restrictions and alien
  registration;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the same privileges in respect of exchange facilities
  as are accorded to the officials of comparable ranks forming part of
  diplomatic missions to the Government of Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the same repatriation facilities in time of
  international crisis as diplomatic envoys; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the right to import free of duty their furniture and
  effects at the time of first taking up their post in Canada.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Senior officials and
officials of the Union who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents
shall have the following privileges and immunities set out in Section 18
of Article V of the Convention:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} immunity from legal process in respect of words
  spoken or written and all acts performed by them in their official
  capacity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the same privileges in respect of exchange facilities
  as are accorded to the officials of comparable ranks forming part of
  diplomatic missions to the Government of Canada.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Experts performing missions
for the Union who are not Canadian citizens or permanent residents shall
have the privileges and immunities set out in Article VI of the
Convention.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Experts performing missions
for the Union who are Canadian citizens or permanent residents shall
have, during their journey to and from the place of mission in Canada,
as well as in Canada, the following privileges and immunities set out in
Section 22 of Article VI of the Convention, subject to the restrictions
set out in Section 23 of that Article:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of words spoken or written and acts done
  by them in the course of the performance of their mission, immunity
  from legal process of every kind, which immunity shall continue to be
  accorded notwithstanding that the persons concerned are no longer
  employed on missions for the Union; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inviolability for all papers and documents.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-661402 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
