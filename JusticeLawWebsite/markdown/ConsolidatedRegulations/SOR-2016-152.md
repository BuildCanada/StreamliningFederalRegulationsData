## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-830316 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[accessory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accessory]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product, including a
    change table accessory and a sleep accessory, that is designed or
    advertised to be used with a crib, cradle or bassinet and that has
    the following characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is designed to support the weight of a child;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it can be placed on or fixed to the crib, cradle
      or bassinet; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its primary function is

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a change table accessory, to
        allow the changing of a child's diaper or the dressing of a
        child,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a sleep accessory that has
        sides to confine the child, to provide sleeping accommodation
        for a child, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, to provide a play area for
        a child or an area to enable caregiver-child
        interaction. ([accessoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[access side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[access side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a side of a crib, cradle,
    bassinet or sleep accessory the bottom part of which is stationary
    and the top part of which has one or more adjustment
    positions. ([côté d'accès]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[adjustment position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adjustment position]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a position in
    which the movable part of an access side latches or locks and from
    which it cannot be moved without being unlatched or
    unlocked. ([position de réglage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bassinet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bassinet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has the
    following characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its primary function is to provide sleeping
      accommodation for a child and it has sides to confine the child;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a sleeping surface area that is less than
      or equal to 4 000 cm^2^; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it can be used alone, or both alone and as an
      accessory. ([moïse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cradle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cradle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has the
    following characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its primary function is to provide sleeping
      accommodation for a child and it has sides to confine the child;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a sleeping surface area that is greater
      than 4 000 cm^2^ but less than or equal to 5 500 cm^2^; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it can be used alone, or both alone and as an
      accessory. ([berceau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[crib]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[crib]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that has the following
    characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its primary function is to provide sleeping
      accommodation for a child and it has sides to confine the child;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a sleeping surface area that is greater
      than 5 500 cm^2^; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it can be used alone, or both alone and as an
      accessory. ([lit d'enfant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fastener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fastener]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes all of the following
    items:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} items that, once installed on a product, are
      capable of being joined or unjoined without the use of any tool or
      equipment, such as

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} string, cords, straps, wire and other similar
        items, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} zippers, buttons, snaps and other similar
        items; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} items that join two objects together on being
      installed on a product, such as screws, bolts, hinges, nails and
      other similar items. ([attaches]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good laboratory practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means practices
    that are in accordance with the principles set out in the
    Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development document
    entitled OECD Principles of Good Laboratory Practice, Number 1 of
    the OECD Series on Principles of Good Laboratory Practice and
    Compliance Monitoring, ENV/MC/CHEM(98)17, the English version of
    which is dated January 21, 1998 and the French version of which is
    dated March 6, 1998. ([bonnes pratiques de
    laboratoire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[key structural component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[key structural component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    crib, cradle, bassinet or accessory, means a side, mattress support,
    stabilizing bar, structural cover and stand. ([composant structurel
    essentiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mattress support]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mattress support]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes, in the case of a
    crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory that is designed to be
    used without a mattress, the surface that supports the weight of the
    child. ([support du matelas]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[occupant retention area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[occupant retention area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory, means the space that is
    bounded by the sleeping surface and the interior surfaces of the
    sides. ([espace de confinement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manufacturer, in the case of a crib, cradle,
      bassinet, accessory or stand that is manufactured in Canada for
      commercial purposes; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer, in the case of a crib, cradle,
      bassinet, accessory or stand that is imported for commercial
      purposes. ([responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[slat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[slat]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a post, bar, rod or other
    similar part of a crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep
    accessory. ([barreau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stand]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product that is designed or
    advertised to elevate a crib, cradle or bassinet but not to
    transport a child. ([soutien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[stationary side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[stationary side]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a side of a crib,
    cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory that has no adjustment
    positions. ([côté fixe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[structural cover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[structural cover]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a key structural
    component of a crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory that has
    the following characteristics:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is made of a textile or other pliable
      material;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it attaches to the frame of the product with a
      zipper, snaps, buttons or other similar fasteners;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it completely or partially defines the occupant
      retention area; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it is removable. ([recouvrement
      structurel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplied with]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplied with]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an accessory, a
    stand or a mattress, means designed to be used and sold together
    with a specific crib, cradle, bassinet or accessory that is made by
    the same manufacturer. ([fourni]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-830361 .Part}

### [Requirements for Cribs, Cradles, Bassinets, Accessories and Stands]{.HTitleText2} {#h-830362 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Key structural components --- assembly

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every key structural
component must meet one of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be manufactured so that it can only be
  assembled correctly one way according to the manufacturer's
  instructions; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the component can be assembled in more than one
  way, it must have permanent markings on it that indicate how to
  assemble it and that remain conspicuous when the crib, cradle,
  bassinet, accessory or stand is not correctly assembled.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stickers, films and surface coating
materials

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sticker, film or other similar material that can be removed, or a
  surface coating material, that is applied to an accessible part of a
  crib, cradle, bassinet, accessory or stand must not contain, when it
  is tested in accordance with a method that conforms to good laboratory
  practices,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} more than 90 mg/kg total lead;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any compound of antimony, arsenic, cadmium,
    selenium or barium if more than 1 000 mg/kg of the compound migrates
    from the material; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} more than 10 mg/kg total mercury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), [[accessible
  part]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[surface coating
  material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the same meanings as in section 1
  of the [Surface Coating Materials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-193).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Angle

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  crib, cradle, bassinet and sleep accessory must be manufactured so
  that the angle of the mattress support does not exceed 7° from the
  horizontal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Angle --- stands

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every stand must be manufactured so that the angle of
  the mattress support of a crib, cradle or bassinet that is placed on
  or fixed to it does not exceed 7° from the horizontal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Angle --- products that rock or swing

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every crib, cradle,
bassinet, sleep accessory and stand that rocks or swings must meet the
following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it does not rock or swing beyond a 20° angle from the
  horizontal; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its angle at rest does not exceed 7° from the
  horizontal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shearing and pinching

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A crib, cradle, bassinet,
accessory or stand must not have any part that is located above the
mattress support when the mattress support is in any position, that is
accessible to the child and that may cause injury to the child due to
shearing or pinching.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Types of sides

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A crib, cradle, bassinet
and sleep accessory must have only stationary sides and access sides.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Strength and integrity of slats

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The slats of a side of a
crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory must not turn or dislodge or
become deformed or otherwise damaged when tested in accordance with
Schedule 1.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posts

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a post of a crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep
  accessory must not extend more than 1.5 mm --- measured within 76 mm
  from the outermost surface of the post --- above the lowest point on
  the upper surface of the higher of the sides that adjoin the post.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A post may extend more than 406 mm above the highest
  point on the upper surface of the higher of the sides that adjoin the
  post if the crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory meets the
  requirements of the cut-out test method set out in section 7.9 of the
  ASTM International standard F1169, entitled Standard Consumer Safety
  Specification for Full-Size Baby Cribs, as amended from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extensions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A post whose height is extended by the attachment of
  one or more removable extensions must meet the requirements of
  subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entrapment --- cribs

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  crib is tested in accordance with the following provisions of Schedule
  2, there must not be any completely bounded opening through which a
  solid rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm is
  capable of passing in any orientation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 1, if the crib has a rigid side; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 2, if the crib has a side that is made of a
    textile or other pliable material.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entrapment --- other products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory is tested
  in accordance with the following provisions of Schedule 2, there must
  not be any completely bounded opening through which a solid
  rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm is
  capable of passing in any orientation:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 3, if the product has a rigid side; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} section 4, if the product has a side that is made
    of a textile or other pliable material.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Openings formed or exposed --- structural
covers --- cribs

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule 2, a structural cover
  of a crib must not form or expose any completely bounded opening that
  permits the passage of a solid rectangular block with the dimensions
  60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm in any orientation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Openings formed or exposed ---
  structural covers --- other products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When tested in accordance with section 4 of Schedule
  2, a structural cover of a cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory must
  not form or expose any completely bounded opening that permits the
  passage of a solid rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm ×
  100 mm × 100 mm in any orientation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mesh --- strength and integrity

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  mesh that is made of a textile or other pliable material and that
  forms part of the sides or bottom of a crib, cradle, bassinet or
  accessory must not, when tested in accordance with Schedule 3, tear or
  become detached from its supporting structure or anything to which it
  is attached.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Size of openings in mesh

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The openings in mesh that is made of a textile or
  other pliable material and that forms part of the sides or bottom of a
  crib, cradle, bassinet or accessory must be of such a size that the
  hemispherical tip of a probe described in Schedule 4 is unable to pass
  through the openings when tested in accordance with that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flammability

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Any part of a crib,
cradle, bassinet, accessory or stand that is made of a textile or any
other pliable material must have a flame spread time greater than seven
seconds, when tested in accordance with the Canadian General Standards
Board standard CAN/CGSB-4.2 No. 27.5, entitled Textile Test Methods:
Flame Resistance --- 45° Angle Test --- One-Second Flame Impingement, as
amended from time to time, in either of the following circumstances:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it does not have a raised fibre surface; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it has a raised fibre surface and, when tested,
  exhibits ignition or fusion of its base fibres.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entanglement --- cribs and stands

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  crib or stand is tested in accordance with section 1 of Schedule 5,
  the instrument illustrated in the figure to that Schedule must not
  catch on any mechanism, fastener or projection of the crib or stand
  that is located above the mattress support or that protrudes above it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entanglement --- other products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a cradle, bassinet, sleep accessory or stand is
  tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule 5, the instrument
  illustrated in the figure to that Schedule must not catch on any
  mechanism, fastener or projection of the cradle, bassinet, sleep
  accessory or stand that is located inside the occupant retention area
  or that protrudes into it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Presumption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsections (1) and (2), a post
  that is described in subsections 9(2) and (3) is deemed to not be a
  projection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- wood, plastic or similar hard
material

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  exposed part of a crib, cradle, bassinet, accessory or stand that is
  made of wood, plastic or a similar hard material must be free from
  cracks, burrs and other defects and be smoothly finished to eliminate
  sharp edges and points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Parts --- metal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every exposed part of a crib, cradle, bassinet,
  accessory or stand that is made of metal must be smoothly finished to
  eliminate sharp edges and points.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Metal tubing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every cut edge of any metal tubing that is part of a
  crib, cradle, bassinet, accessory or stand must meet one of the
  following requirements if the tubing is located above the mattress
  support when the mattress support is in any position and is accessible
  to the child:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be smoothly finished to eliminate sharp
    edges and points; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be protected by a cap that remains in place
    when it is subjected to a force of 90 N applied in any direction.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bolts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The threaded end of every bolt of a crib, cradle,
  bassinet, accessory or stand must, if the end is located above the
  mattress support when the mattress support is in any position and is
  accessible to the child, be covered by an acorn nut or a device that
  offers equivalent protection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small parts

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Every part of a crib,
cradle, bassinet, accessory or stand that is small enough to be totally
enclosed in a small parts cylinder illustrated in Schedule 6 must be
affixed to the product so that the part does not become detached when it
is subjected to a force of 90 N applied in any direction.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Openings

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every slot, notch, groove
or other opening in a wooden, plastic or metal part of a crib, cradle,
bassinet, accessory or stand --- or in a part of one made of a similar
hard material --- that is located above the mattress support when the
mattress support is in any position and that is accessible to the child
must meet one of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be of such a size and shape that, if it
  admits a probe that is 5.33 mm in diameter, it will also admit a probe
  that is 9.53 mm in diameter; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must have a depth that is not greater than the
  minor span dimension across the opening, if that minor span dimension
  is greater than or equal to 5.33 mm and less than 9.53 mm.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restraint systems

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A crib, cradle, bassinet
or sleep accessory must not have a child restraint system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- length --- general

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  cord, strap or other similar item that is attached by only one of its
  ends to a crib, cradle, bassinet, sleep accessory or stand must not
  measure more than 188 mm in length when it is stretched by the gradual
  application of a force of 22 N.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- length --- change
  table accessory

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a cord, strap or other similar item
  that is attached by only one of its ends to a change table accessory
  that is placed on or fixed to a crib, the portion of the cord, strap
  or item that extends into the crib's occupant retention area must not
  measure more than 188 mm in length when it is stretched by the gradual
  application of a force of 22 N.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- loops --- general

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  tested in accordance with section 1 of Schedule 7, a cord, strap or
  other similar item that is attached to a crib, cradle, bassinet, sleep
  accessory or stand must not be capable of forming either of the
  following loops:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a loop inside the occupant retention area that
    permits the passage of the small head probe illustrated in the
    figure to that Schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loop outside the occupant retention area that
    permits the passage of that probe and that, when the loop is pulled
    into the occupant retention area, remains there.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cords and straps --- loops --- change
  table accessories

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When tested in accordance with section 2 of Schedule
  7, a cord, strap or other similar item that is designed to restrain a
  child in a change table accessory that is placed on or fixed to a crib
  must not be capable of forming a loop that can be pulled into the
  occupant retention area of the crib and that permits the passage of
  the small head probe illustrated in the figure to that Schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coil springs

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Every coil spring of a
crib, cradle, bassinet or accessory that is located above the mattress
support when the mattress support is in any position and is accessible
to the child must be covered or manufactured so as to prevent injury to
the child.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mattress

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A mattress that is
supplied with a crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep accessory must meet all
of the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it must be not more than

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 150 mm thick, in the case of a crib mattress, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 38 mm thick, in the case of a cradle, bassinet or
    sleep accessory mattress;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it must be of such a size that, when it is pushed
  firmly against any side of the crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep
  accessory, it does not leave a gap of more than 30 mm between the
  mattress and any part of any other side; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it must be stitched using lock-stitching.

### [Additional Requirements for Cribs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-830464 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Structural integrity

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A crib must not exhibit
any damage, its latching or locking mechanisms must not disengage or
become permanently deformed and its fasteners must not loosen, when the
crib is tested in accordance with sections 1 to 3 of Schedule 8.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mattress support

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** The mattress support of a
crib must not dislodge, its mechanisms must not disengage or become
permanently deformed and its fasteners must not loosen, when the
mattress support is tested in accordance with section 1 of Schedule 9.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Height of sides --- mattress support in
lowest position

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Every side of a crib must
meet the following requirements when the mattress support is in its
lowest position:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} its lower surface must be lower than the upper
  surface of the mattress support;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} its upper surface must be at least 660 mm higher than
  the upper surface of the mattress support; and

## [SCHEDULE 1]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 8)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Strength and Integrity of Slats]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-830826 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the strength and
  integrity of slats:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep
    accessory according to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting any
    attachments that could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure the crib, cradle, bassinet or sleep
    accessory in the manufacturer's recommended use position to a
    horizontal surface in a manner that will not interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} apply a torque of 8 N•m to one of the slats at its
    midpoint and maintain that torque for 10 seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} record whether the slat turns or dislodges or
    becomes deformed or otherwise damaged;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    for all the other slats;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} apply a vertical upward force of 500 N at the
    midpoint of the top rail on one of the sides to which slats are
    attached and maintain the force for 30 seconds;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} record whether any of the slats dislodge from the
    top or bottom rail or become damaged; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (f) and (g)
    for all the other sides to which slats are attached.

#### [Cribs with Rigid Sides]{.HTitleText2} {#cribs-with-rigid-sides .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing completely
  bounded openings that are located above the mattress support of a crib
  that has rigid sides:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the mattress support is adjustable, adjust it to
    its lowest position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the metallic loading wedge illustrated in the
    figure to this section in the opening between two adjacent slats,
    midway along the greatest dimension of the opening;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} apply a pull force of 90 N to the eyebolt ---
    either open or closed --- of the loading wedge in a direction
    perpendicular to a plane passing through the points of contact of
    the wedge with the two slats and maintain the force for 10 seconds;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} while applying that force, attempt to pass, without
    forcing, a solid rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm ×
    100 mm × 100 mm through the opening, anywhere above or below the
    loading wedge;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (e) with the
    block in all other possible orientations;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (f)
    for all the other openings between adjacent slats; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} attempt to pass, without forcing, a solid
    rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm
    through all the other completely bounded openings that are located
    above the mattress support.

#### [Cribs with a Structural Cover or a Side that is Made of a Textile or Other Pliable Material]{.HTitleText2} {#cribs-with-a-structural-cover-or-a-side-that-is-made-of-a-textile-or-other-pliable-material .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing completely
  bounded openings that are located --- or that are created or exposed
  --- above or in the mattress support of a crib that has a structural
  cover or a side that is made of a textile or other pliable material:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the mattress support is adjustable, adjust it to
    its lowest position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} inside the occupant retention area, place a solid
    rectangular block with the dimensions 60 mm × 100 mm × 100 mm
    against a side of the crib that is made of a textile or other
    pliable material in a location on the side that is above the
    mattress support where the application of force is likely to create
    or expose a completely bounded opening;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply a force of 90 N to the block over a
    period of five seconds and maintain the force for an additional
    10 seconds;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    for all the other locations that are likely to create or expose a
    completely bounded opening in that side;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (e)
    for all the other sides of the crib that are made of a textile or
    other pliable material;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} by gradually applying a force of 90 N over a period
    of five seconds and maintaining the force for an additional
    10 seconds, attempt to pass the block, in all possible orientations,
    through any completely bounded opening that is located --- or that
    was created or exposed as a result of the steps set out in
    paragraphs (c) to (f) --- above or in the mattress support;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the crib has a structural cover, undo any
    zippers, snaps and other similar fasteners that attach it to the
    crib; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (g) for all
    the other completely bounded openings that are created or exposed
    above or in the mattress support.

## [SCHEDULE 3]{.scheduleLabel}[(Subsection 12(1))]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Strength of Mesh and Integrity of Attachment]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-830883 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the strength of
  mesh and the integrity of its attachment to a crib, cradle, bassinet
  or accessory:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the product according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the product on a horizontal surface on its
    side so that the side with the mesh panel that is being tested is
    positioned above the opposite side and, if necessary, secure it by
    using any blocking or support that maintains its position and that
    does not act directly on the frame of the side that is being tested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} apply a load of 9.18 kg that has a rectangular base
    that measures 150 mm × 75 mm to the geometric centre of the mesh
    panel or, if any exterior framing interferes with the test, as close
    as possible to the geometric centre, so that the 150-mm dimension of
    the load is aligned parallel to the top rail of the side that is
    being tested, in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply the load over a period of five
      seconds,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} leave the load in place for 10 seconds,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} gradually remove the load over a period of five
      seconds, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} allow a recovery time of 10 seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraph (c) nine
    times for a total of 10 applications;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    on the top portion of the mesh panel in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} align the loading block so that the 75-mm
      dimension is parallel with the top edge of the mesh panel, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply the load to the mesh panel so that the
      centre of the 75-mm dimension of the loading block is 25 mm below
      the centre of the top edge of the mesh panel;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    on the bottom portion of the mesh panel in the following way:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} align the load so that the 75-mm dimension is
      parallel with the bottom edge of the mesh panel, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} apply the load to the mesh panel so that the
      centre of the 75-mm dimension of the load is 25 mm above the
      centre of the bottom edge of the mesh panel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the bottom of the product has a mesh panel,
    place the product upside down on a horizontal surface and, if
    necessary, secure it by using any blocking or support that maintains
    its position and that does not act directly on the frame of the
    bottom of the product, and repeat the steps set out in
    paragraphs (c) to (f); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (f)
    on all the other mesh panels of the product.

## [SCHEDULE 7]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 20 and 42)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Loops]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-830938 .scheduleLabel}

#### [Cribs, Cradles, Bassinets, Sleep Accessories and Stands]{.HTitleText2} {#cribs-cradles-bassinets-sleep-accessories-and-stands .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing a loop formed by
  a cord, strap or other similar item that is attached to a crib,
  cradle, bassinet, sleep accessory or stand:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the product according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test and, if the product is a
    stand, put a crib, cradle or bassinet that is designed or advertised
    to be used with the stand on that stand according to the stand
    manufacturer's instructions;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the product is a sleep accessory, place the
    sleep accessory in the manufacturer's recommended use position on
    the product or fix it in that position to the product;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} select a loop to be tested and, while holding the
    loop open to its maximum diameter, attempt to pass the small head
    probe illustrated in the figure to this Schedule through the loop;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if a loop that allows the passage of the probe is
    outside the occupant retention area, apply a pull force of 22 N to
    the loop to attempt to pull it into the occupant retention area;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} release the force and observe whether the loop
    remains inside the occupant retention area; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (e)
    for all the other loops.

#### [Change Table Accessories]{.HTitleText2} {#change-table-accessories .SchedHeadL2}

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing a cord, strap or
  other similar item that is designed to restrain a child and that is
  attached to a change table accessory that is placed on or fixed to a
  crib:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib and the change table accessory
    according to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting any
    attachments that could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} place the change table accessory in the
    manufacturer's recommended use position on the crib or fix it in
    that position to the crib;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the change table accessory has at least one
    opening, select a cord, strap or other similar item to be tested
    and, with it unfastened, apply a push or pull force to it of at most
    111 N and attempt to pass it into the occupant retention area
    through the opening in the change table accessory and, if a loop
    forms inside the occupant retention area, hold the loop open to its
    maximum diameter and attempt to pass the small head probe
    illustrated in the figure to this Schedule through the loop;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (c) for all
    the other cords, straps or similar items;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if two cords, straps or other similar items pass
    into the occupant retention area, adjust them to their greatest
    length and fasten them together according to the manufacturer's
    instructions to form a loop and, while holding the loop open to its
    maximum diameter, attempt to pass the small head probe illustrated
    in the figure to this Schedule through the loop;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} unfasten the cords, straps or other similar items
    and pull them from the opening referred to in paragraph (c);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (f)
    for all the other possible combinations of openings and cords,
    straps or similar items;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} adjust the cords, straps or similar items to their
    maximum length, fasten them together and then drape the loop that is
    formed over the side of the change table accessory so that it hangs

## [SCHEDULE 8]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 23, 28 and 32, paragraph 36(c), section 38 and paragraph 41(c))]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Structural Integrity]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-830965 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [Cribs]{.HTitleText2} {#cribs .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of a crib under dynamic conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a mattress is supplied with the crib, remove it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} secure the crib to a horizontal surface in a manner
    that will not interfere with the conduct of the test and place the
    mattress support in its lowest position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} place on the mattress support a test mattress that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is made of polyurethane foam that is 76 mm thick
      and that has a density of 30 kg/m^3^ ± 6 kg/m^3^ and a 25%
      indentation force deflection (IFD) of 144 N ± 30 N,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has dimensions that are no more than 25 mm
      narrower than the interior dimensions of the crib, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is covered with a tight-fitting 8- to 12-gauge
      vinyl material;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} using the 20-kg load illustrated in the figure to
    this section, cause the load to fall freely onto the geometric
    centre of the surface of the test mattress from a height of 150 mm
    above its upper surface, measured at the outset of the test;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} remove the load after two seconds;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (e) and (f)
    149 times at the rate of one impact every four seconds for a total
    of 150 impacts;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} record any damage to the crib, any disengagement or
    permanent deformation of its latching or locking mechanisms and any
    loosening of its fasteners;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} without readjusting the mattress support, repeat
    the steps set out in paragraphs (e) to (g) near a mattress support
    mechanism, causing the load to fall at a point that is 150 mm from
    the innermost surface of each of the nearest crib sides, measured
    from the geometric centre of the load, and repeat the step set out
    in paragraph (h); and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraph (i) for all
    the other mattress support mechanisms; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} if the crib has any access sides, repeat the steps
    set out in paragraphs (e) to (h) at the midpoint along the edge of
    the mattress support beside each access side, causing the load to
    fall at a point that is 150 mm from the side, measured from the
    geometric centre of the load.

- **2** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of a crib under horizontal force conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure the crib to a horizontal surface in a manner
    that will not interfere with the conduct of the test and place the
    mattress support in its lowest position;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the movable part of any access sides of the
    crib in the adjustment position that is designed to provide
    accommodation for an unattended child;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} apply a horizontal force of 120 N perpendicularly
    to any point midway along the length of one of the sides of the
    crib, not more than 50 mm from its upper edge, in a back-and-forth
    motion at a frequency of 150 cycles per minute for a total of 9,000
    cycles;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} record any damage to the crib, any disengagement or
    permanent deformation of its latching or locking mechanisms and any
    loosening of its fasteners; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (e)
    for all the other sides of the crib.

- **3** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of a crib under vertical force conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} secure the crib to a horizontal surface in a manner
    that will not interfere with the conduct of the test and place the
    mattress support in its lowest position and;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} place the movable part of any access sides of the
    crib in the adjustment position that is designed to provide
    accommodation for an unattended child;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} apply a vertical force of 120 N to the midpoint of
    the upper edge of one of the sides, in an up-and-down motion at a
    frequency of 150 cycles per minute for a total of 9,000 cycles;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} record any damage to the crib, any disengagement or
    permanent deformation of its latching or locking mechanisms and any
    loosening of its fasteners; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (e)
    for all the other sides of the crib.

#### [Cradles]{.HTitleText2} {#cradles .SchedHeadL2}

- **4** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of a cradle under dynamic conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the cradle according to the manufacturer's
    instructions, omitting any attachments that could interfere with the
    conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a mattress is supplied with the cradle, remove
    it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} secure the cradle to a horizontal surface in a
    manner that will not interfere with the conduct of the test and
    place the mattress support in its lowest position;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} place on the mattress support a test mattress that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is made of polyurethane foam that is 76 mm thick
      and that has a density of 30 kg/m^3^ ± 6 kg/m^3^ and a 25%
      indentation force deflection (IFD) of 144 N ± 30 N,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has dimensions that are no more than 25 mm
      narrower than the interior dimensions of the cradle, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is covered with a tight-fitting 8- to 12-gauge
      vinyl material;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} using a load of 13.7 kg that has a square base that
    measures 305 mm × 305 mm, cause the load to fall freely onto the
    geometric centre of the surface of the test mattress from a height
    of 150 mm above its upper surface, measured at the outset of the
    test;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} remove the load after two seconds;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (e) and (f)
    499 times at the rate of one impact every four seconds for a total
    of 500 impacts; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} record any damage to the cradle, any disengagement
    or permanent deformation of its latching or locking mechanisms and
    any loosening of its fasteners.

#### [Bassinets]{.HTitleText2} {#bassinets .SchedHeadL2}

- **5** The following method is to be used for testing the structural
  integrity of a bassinet under static conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the bassinet according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if a removable mattress is supplied with the
    bassinet, remove it;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set up the bassinet in one of the following ways:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the bassinet is elevated by an integral
      supporting structure, place it on a horizontal surface,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the bassinet is designed or advertised to be
      used with a stand, put the bassinet on the stand according to the
      stand manufacturer's instructions and place the stand on a
      horizontal surface,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if the bassinet has one or more handles, hang
      the bassinet from its handles above a horizontal surface, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if the bassinet has no handles, no integral
      supporting structure and no stand, elevate the bassinet above a
      horizontal surface in a way that the blocking or other support
      used to hold it in the elevated position does not act directly on
      the bassinet's mattress support;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} identify the areas on the mattress support that are
    most likely to be damaged by the application of the load and mark
    them;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} apply a load of 24 kg that has a square base that
    measures 152 mm × 152 mm to one of the marked areas and maintain the
    load for 60 seconds;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (e) for all
    other marked areas;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} record any damage to the bassinet, including to its
    handles; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the bassinet can be set up in more than one way
    set out in paragraph (c), repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (d)
    to (g) for each possible way.

## [SCHEDULE 9]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 24 and 33)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test of Mattress Support]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831027 .scheduleLabel}

    loosening of its fasteners;

    position, if necessary;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) to (e)

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (c) for all of
    the mattress support mechanisms simultaneously;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} record any dislodging of the mattress support, any
    disengagement or permanent deformation of the mechanisms and any
    loosening of its fasteners;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} readjust the mattress support to its original
    position, if necessary;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} apply a force of 200 N in any direction to one of
    the mattress support mechanisms in a manner that could cause
    disengagement or permanent deformation of the mechanism or loosening
    of the mattress support fasteners;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} record any dislodging of the mattress support, any
    disengagement or permanent deformation of the mechanisms and any
    loosening of its fasteners;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} readjust the mattress support to its original
    position, if necessary; and

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (j) to (l)

## [SCHEDULE 10]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 29)]{.OriginatingRef}[Static Load Test of Slat Strength]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831055 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the strength of
  crib slats under a static load:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} select and mark for testing slats that make up 25%
    of the slats of the crib, rounded up to the nearest whole number of
    slats, and that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} based on their geometry, offer the least
      resistance to deflection,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} are not adjacent to another slat being tested,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} are not vertical rails that are joined to both
      the top and bottom horizontal rails of a side;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} select a marked slat and place the side of the crib
    to which the slat belongs in such a way that the side is rigidly
    supported at a point located within 76 mm of each end of its top and
    bottom horizontal rails in a manner that does not interfere with any
    deflection of the slats under applied force;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} over a period of at least two seconds but not more
    than five seconds, using a force-measuring device that has a contact
    area with a width of 25.4 mm ± 1.6 mm and a length at least equal to
    the width of the slat being tested, gradually apply a force of 356 N
    perpendicular to the horizontal rails, at the midpoint of the slat,
    and maintain the force for 10 seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} record whether the slat breaks or separates from
    the horizontal rails under a force of 267 N or less;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (d)
    for all the other slats marked for testing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} record whether more than one slat breaks or
    separates from the horizontal rails under a force of more than 267 N
    but less than 356 N;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} if only a single slat breaks or separates from the
    horizontal rails under a force of more than 267 N but less than
    356 N, select a further 25% of the slats of the crib for testing
    that meet the criteria set out in subparagraphs (a)(i) to (iii) and
    repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (e); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} record whether any single slat of the additional
    25% of slats selected under paragraph (g) breaks or separates from
    the horizontal rails under a force of 356 N or less.

## [SCHEDULE 11]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 31 and 37)]{.OriginatingRef}[Test for Stability of Cradles, Bassinets and Stands]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831070 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing the stability of
  a cradle or bassinet or of a stand when it is used with such a
  product:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the product according to the
    manufacturer's instructions, omitting any attachments that could
    interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set up a horizontal test surface with an aluminum
    angle stop that is, subject to paragraph (c), 25 mm high and 25 mm
    wide and that is at least 150 mm longer than the product that is
    being tested;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the cradle or bassinet has a rocking base, the
    angle stop must be of a height that, when the product is tilted, no
    part of its rounded base moves on top of the angle stop;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} position the product in one of the manufacturer's
    recommended use positions and place it against the angle stop;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} place a CAMI Newborn Dummy with its arms and legs
    straightened and face up on the geometric centre of the appropriate
    mattress or, in the case of a product that is designed to be used
    without a mattress, on the geometric centre of the surface that
    supports the weight of the child;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} gradually apply a 10-kg static vertical load over a
    period of five seconds to the location along the upper horizontal
    rail on the side that is closest to the angle stop and that is most
    likely to tip the product over;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} while maintaining the vertical load, gradually
    apply a horizontal force of 22 N in the direction of the angle stop,
    over a period of five seconds, to the same location where the
    vertical load is applied;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} maintain both the vertical load and the horizontal
    force for a further 10 seconds;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} rotate the product 90° and repeat the steps set out
    in paragraphs (d) to (h);

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} repeat the step set out in paragraph (i) twice
    more;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (d) to (j)
    for all the other manufacturer's recommended use positions; and

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} if the cradle or bassinet is designed or advertised
    to be used with a stand, put the product on the stand according to
    the stand manufacturer's instructions and repeat the steps set out
    in paragraphs (b) to (k).

## [SCHEDULE 12]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 42)]{.OriginatingRef}[Entrapment in Accessories Test Method]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-831086 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- **1** The following method is to be used for testing openings that are
  created when an accessory is placed on or fixed to a crib:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assemble the crib and the accessory or accessories,
    according to the manufacturer's instructions, omitting any
    attachments that could interfere with the conduct of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix the accessory or combination of accessories to
    the crib --- or place it or them on the crib --- in one of the
    manufacturer's recommended use positions and identify any openings
    that are created and that are likely to permit the passage of the
    small head probe illustrated in the figure to Schedule 7 but not the
    large head probe illustrated in the figure to this section;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} rotate the small head probe to the orientation that
    is most likely to permit its passage through one of the identified
    openings and, from inside the occupant retention area of the crib,
    gradually apply an upward or outward force of 111 N to the probe
    over a period of five seconds, then maintain the force for 10
    seconds;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the small head probe can pass entirely through
    the opening, repeat the steps set out in paragraph (c) using the
    large head probe and attempt to pass it, without forcing it, through
    the opening in any orientation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (c) and (d)
    for all the other identified openings; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} repeat the steps set out in paragraphs (b) to (e)
    for all the other manufacturer's recommended use positions.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} fix the accessory or combination of accessories to
