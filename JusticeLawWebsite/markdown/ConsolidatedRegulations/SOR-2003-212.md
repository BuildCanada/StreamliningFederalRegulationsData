## Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Cost Recovery Fees Regulations {#canadian-nuclear-safety-commission-cost-recovery-fees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-212
:::

[NUCLEAR SAFETY AND CONTROL ACT](/eng/acts/N-28.3)

Registration 2003-06-05

Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Cost Recovery Fees Regulations

P.C. 2003-869 2003-06-05
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission, with the approval of the
Governor in Council, pursuant to subsection 44(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGCDA} of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDCDA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Cost Recovery Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-212).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EGCDA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 2001, c.
  34, s. 61
  :::

May 22, 2003

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 44(12) of the [Nuclear Safety and
Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EFABA}, a copy of the
proposed [Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission Cost Recovery Fees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-212), substantially in the form
set out in the annexed Regulations, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on February 1, 2003, and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby given to interested persons to make
representations to the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission with respect
to the proposed Regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources and the Treasury
Board, pursuant to subsection 44(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EGAAA} of the
[Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDAAA}, hereby
approves the making of the annexed [Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission
Cost Recovery Fees Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-212) by the
Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702802 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702803 .Subheading}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Nuclear Safety and Control
    Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[actual full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[actual full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the full cost
    verified by audited financial statements. ([coût entier
    réel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class I nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class I nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Class I
    nuclear facility as defined in the Class I Nuclear Facilities
    Regulations. ([installation nucléaire de
    catégorie I]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class II nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Class II
    nuclear facility as defined in the Class II Nuclear Facilities and
    Prescribed Equipment Regulations. ([installation nucléaire de
    catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class II prescribed equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Class II
    prescribed equipment as defined in the Class II Nuclear Facilities
    and Prescribed Equipment Regulations. ([équipement réglementé de
    Catégorie II]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those
    activities, such as assessing applications, issuing licences and
    certificates, granting approvals and authorizations, verifying and
    enforcing compliance and providing information, products and
    services, that are required for the Commission to fulfil its
    regulatory responsibilities. ([activités de réglementation
    directes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dosimetry services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dosimetry services]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means dosimetry services
    within the meaning of the [Radiation Protection
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-203). ([services de
    dosimétrie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fee period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fee period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the 12-month period
    beginning on the date of issuance of a licence and, after that date,
    beginning on each anniversary date of the licence. ([période
    d'application des droits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one calendar year and ending on March 31 in the next
    calendar year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the AECB Cost
    Recovery Fees Regulations, 1996. ([ancien
    règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[full cost]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sum of the costs of the
    Commission's direct regulatory activities and indirect regulatory
    activities, including salaries and benefits, rental of office
    accommodation, supplies and equipment, professional services,
    communications, travel and training. ([coût entier]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[indirect regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indirect regulatory activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means those
    activities that are in support of direct regulatory activities, such
    as management, training, administration, human resources, finance,
    information technology services and the preparation of documents,
    including policies, standards, guides, procedures and notices.
    ([activités de réglementation indirectes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    mine or mill as defined in the [Uranium Mines and Mills
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-206).
    ([mine]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} *ou* [usine de
    concentration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste nuclear substance activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste nuclear substance activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    activities in relation to waste nuclear substances that are not
    located at a Class I or a Class II nuclear facility or at a mine or
    mill. ([activités liées aux déchets de substances
    nucléaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702819 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a secondary school or a specified educational
  institution as defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Student Loans
  Act](/eng/acts/S-23);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit organization that carries out
  research and is wholly owned by an institution referred to in
  paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit institution that receives funds from
  the federal government, a provincial government or the government of a
  city, town or regional municipality and that provides medical services
  prescribed by a medical practitioner for the purpose of maintaining
  health, preventing disease or diagnosing or treating injury, illness
  or disability in patients;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit organization that responds to
  accidents and incidents, such as a fire department, a police
  department, an emergency response service, an emergency medical
  service or an ambulance service;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a department or agency of the federal government, a
  provincial government or the government of a city, town or regional
  municipality if the department or agency that applies for or holds a
  licence from the Commission in respect of a contaminated site that is
  abandoned on the coming into force of these Regulations and the
  contamination did not result from the activities of the applicant or
  licensee; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a department as defined in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11).

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Regulatory Activity Plan Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702827 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702828 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
applicants and licensees in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class I nuclear facilities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mines and mills; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} waste nuclear substance activities.

### [Estimated Annual Fee]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702833 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Before the beginning of
each fiscal year, the Commission shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} calculate the estimated annual fee payable by an
  applicant or a licensee for that fiscal year using the estimated full
  cost of the regulatory activity plan prepared by the Commission for
  the applicant's or licensee's facility or activity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify each applicant or licensee, in writing, of the
  regulatory activity plan and the estimated annual fee payable.

### [Quarterly Invoicing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702837 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On a
  quarterly basis, the Commission shall send each applicant or licensee
  an invoice for an amount equal to 25% of the estimated annual fee
  payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the date of the invoice, the
  applicant or licensee shall pay to the Commission the amount invoiced.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If changes occur in the Commission's regulatory
  activity plan for any facility or activity for a fiscal year, the
  Commission may re-calculate the estimated fee for that facility or
  activity for the fiscal year and adjust the amount invoiced
  accordingly.

### [Annual Fee Adjustment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702842 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  year, following the end of the fiscal year, the Commission shall, for
  each facility or activity,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} calculate the actual full cost;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} calculate the fee adjustment by subtracting the
    estimated annual fee as calculated under section 4 from the actual
    full cost; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notify the applicant or licensee in writing of the
    amount of the actual full cost and the amount of the fee adjustment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the fee adjustment calculated under
  paragraph (1)(b) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} less than the estimated annual fee, the Commission
    shall refund the difference to the applicant or licensee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} greater than the estimated annual fee, the
    Commission shall invoice the applicant or licensee for an amount
    equal to the difference, and the applicant or licensee shall pay to
    the Commission that amount within 30 days after the date of the
    invoice.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2008-123, s. 1
:::

### [Initial Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702853 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In the
  case of an initial application for a facility or activity for which an
  estimated annual fee has not been calculated, the applicant shall pay
  to the Commission, with the application, a deposit of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$25,000, if the application is in respect of a
    facility; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$5,000, if the application is in respect of a
    waste nuclear substance activity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the application and deposit, the
  Commission shall calculate the estimated annual fee payable for the
  current fiscal year in accordance with paragraph 4(a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On a quarterly basis over the remaining quarters of
  the fiscal year after receipt of the application, the Commission shall
  invoice the applicant for the amount of the estimated annual fee
  payable, which is calculated on the basis of the number of quarters
  remaining in the fiscal year and is reduced by the amount of the
  deposit.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Within 30 days after the date of the invoice, the
  applicant shall pay to the Commission the amount invoiced.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} After the end of the fiscal year, the estimated
  annual fee shall be adjusted in accordance with section 6.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Formula Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702863 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702864 .Subheading}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** This Part applies to
applicants and licensees in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Class II nuclear facilities;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Class II prescribed equipment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} dosimetry services; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} nuclear substances and radiation devices to which the
  [Nuclear Substances and Radiation Devices
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-207) apply, except with respect
  to applications and licences for waste nuclear substance activities.

### [Formulas]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702870 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Fees
  under this Part shall be calculated using the formulas set out in
  Part 2 of Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The formulas comprise

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} base hours as described in section 11;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} variable hours as described in section 12;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a compliance coefficient as described in
    section 13; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an hourly rate as described in section 14.

### [Base Hours]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702878 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For each type of
application or licence, the base hours are the number of hours spent by
the Commission

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the assessment of applications; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to verify the licensee's compliance with regulatory
  requirements.

### [Variable Hours]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702882 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For each type of
application or licence, the variable hours are the additional number of
hours of direct regulatory activities as a result of the number of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} treatment rooms, bunkers, laboratories and locations
  with separate postal addresses;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} devices;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} device manufacturers; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} types of Class II prescribed equipment as defined in
  the Class II Nuclear Facilities and Prescribed Equipment Regulations.

### [Compliance Coefficient]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702888 .Subheading}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For each type of licence,
the compliance coefficient is derived from the additional number of
hours of direct regulatory activities spent by the Commission as a
result of non-compliance by a licensee with regulatory requirements.

### [Hourly Rate]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702890 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The hourly rate is the
full cost divided by the total number of hours spent by the Commission
on its direct regulatory activities.

### [Publication]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702892 .Subheading}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Before the beginning of
each fiscal year, the Commission shall publish, by electronic or other
means likely to reach applicants and licensees, for each type of
application or licence for a facility or activity set out in Part 1 of
Schedule 1, the base hours, variable hours, compliance coefficient and
hourly rate.

### [Payment of Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702894 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On an
  initial application for a licence in respect of an activity or a
  facility listed in Part 1 of Schedule 1, the applicant shall pay to
  the Commission the assessment fee and the annual fee in accordance
  with subsections (2) and (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assessment fee payable for a licence in respect
  of an activity or a facility listed in column 1 of Part 1 of
  Schedule 1 shall be calculated using the applicable fee formula set
  out in Part 2 of that Schedule, which is determined by the applicable
  formula number set out in column 2 of Part 1 of that Schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual fee payable for a licence in respect of an
  activity or a facility listed in column 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 1
  shall be calculated using the applicable fee formula set out in Part 2
  of that Schedule, which is determined by the applicable formula number
  set out in column 3 of Part 1 of that Schedule.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On an initial application for a licence for an
  activity or a facility that is not listed in Part 1 of Schedule 1, the
  applicant shall pay the deposit and fees in accordance with Part 5.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an initial application is withdrawn by the
  applicant before the assessment of the application by the Commission
  has begun, the assessment fee and annual fee paid shall be refunded to
  the applicant.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If an initial application is withdrawn by the
  applicant or rejected by the Commission after the assessment of the
  application by the Commission has begun, the assessment fee paid shall
  not be refunded and the annual fee paid shall be refunded to the
  applicant.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} A re-application after withdrawal by the applicant or
  rejection by the Commission shall be treated as a new initial
  application.

- SOR/2008-123, s. 3

### [Invoicing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702905 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  year before the licence anniversary date, the Commission shall issue
  to the licensee an invoice for the annual fee payable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall pay the fee to the Commission by
  the later of 30 days after the date of the invoice and the licence
  anniversary date.

### [Fee Not Affected]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702909 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Changes in the number of
any of the items referred to in paragraphs 12(a) to (d) during the fee
period do not affect the fee payable for that period.

### [Revocation of Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702911 .Subheading}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  licence is revoked within the first year after its issuance, there
  shall be no refund of the annual fee paid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licence is revoked after the first year of its
  issuance and the licensee has paid the annual fee for the current fee
  period,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that fee shall be reduced by an amount equal to the
    product obtained by multiplying 25% of the annual fee paid by the
    number of full quarterly periods remaining in the fee period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Commission shall refund that amount to the
    former licensee.

the fee payable under this Part is equal to the sum of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product obtained by multiplying the hourly rate
  referred to in section 14 by the number of hours of direct regulatory
  activities

and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Commission obtains professional and special
  services under contract, the cost of those services.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall apply the deposit against the
  fee payable.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} On completion of a special project, any remaining
  balance of the deposit paid shall be refunded to the applicant.

## [PART 6]{.HLabel1}[Transitional, Repeal and Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-702963 .Part}

### [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText2} {#h-702964 .Subheading}
