## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806725 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to section 2, these Regulations apply to any product that contains
  mercury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mercury and its compounds

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, mercury
  includes any of its compounds.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} waste;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a product that is at the end of its useful life and
  that is intended to be recycled;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a food, drug, or cosmetic as defined in section 2 of
  the [Food and Drugs Act](/eng/acts/F-27);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a veterinary biologic as defined in subsection 2(1)
  of the [Health of Animals Act](/eng/acts/H-3.3);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a surface coating material as defined in subsection
  1(1) of the [Surface Coating Materials
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-193) or a surface coating
  material applied to a toy regulated under the [Toys
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-17);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a [[pest control product]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products
  Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), other than a device referred to in paragraph
  2(a) of the [Pest Control Products
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1253) or a [[treated
  article]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 1(1) of those
  Regulations;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a feed as defined in section 2 of the [Feeds
  Act](/eng/acts/F-9);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a fertilizer as defined in section 2 of the
  [Fertilizers Act](/eng/acts/F-10);

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} an explosive regulated under the [Explosives
  Act](/eng/acts/E-17);

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} ammunition and explosives under the direction or
  control of the Minister of National Defence;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} a product, other than a battery, that has a mercury
  concentration of 0.1% or less by weight in homogeneous materials;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} a battery that has a mercury concentration of 0.0005%
  or less by weight;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} a measuring device that is imported solely to be
  displayed in a public exhibition for cultural or historical purposes;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} cold cathode tubing, or an electrode for use in cold
  cathode tubing, that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured in or imported into Canada after
    December 31, 2025,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is needed to repair signage or cove lighting that
    is manufactured in, imported into or installed in Canada before
    December 31, 2025, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} contains a total quantity of mercury that is less
    than or equal to

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in the case of cold cathode tubing, 100 mg per
      2.44 m (8 feet), and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an electrode for use in cold
      cathode tubing, 100 mg;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} ores, concentrates and by-products of metallurgic
  operations; and

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} an on-road vehicle as defined in subsection 1(1) of
  the On-Road Vehicle and Engine Emission Regulations that is of the
  2016 model year or of a previous model year as determined under
  section 5 of those Regulations.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806749 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture or import

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import a product that contains mercury unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product belongs to a category set out in column
    1 of Schedule 1, contains a total quantity of mercury that is less
    than or equal to the maximum total quantity set out in column 2 and
    is manufactured or imported by the person on or before the end date
    set out in column 3;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the product is a replacement part;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the product is a replacement lamp; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person holds a permit issued under subsection
    5(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale or offer for sale

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not sell or offer for sale a
  replacement lamp that belongs to a category set out in any of items 1
  to 4, column 1, of Schedule 2 after the second anniversary of the end
  date set out in column 4.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement part

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A replacement part is a part that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} will replace a component that a product contained
    before November 8, 2015, and is required for that product to
    function;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be replaced by a mercury-free alternative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} does not belong to a category set out in column 1
    of Schedule 1 or 2; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a cold cathode fluorescent lamp or
    an external electrode fluorescent lamp, is for use in an electronic
    display panel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Replacement lamp

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A replacement lamp is a lamp that belongs to a
  category set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 and

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contains a total quantity of mercury that is less
    than or equal to the maximum total quantity set out in column 2;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is manufactured in or imported into Canada on or
    after the start date set out in column 3 but no later than the end
    date set out in column 4; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} will be used in a lighting fixture installed before
    the start date set out in column 3 or, in the case of a bulb for an
    automobile headlamp, in an on-road vehicle that was manufactured in
    or imported into Canada before that date.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** A person that
manufactures or imports a product in contravention of section 3 must
ensure that the product is sent for final disposal or recycling at a
facility that is authorized, by the authorities of the jurisdiction in
which it is located, to dispose of or recycle hazardous materials.
However, an imported product may instead be returned to the person or
facility from which it was imported.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806754 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for a permit
to manufacture or import a product that contains mercury must be
submitted to the Minister and must contain the following information and
documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the applicant,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
    number and, if any, fax number and email address, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, title, civic and postal
    addresses, telephone number and, if any, fax number and email
    address of their authorized representative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the product,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its common or generic name and its trade name, if
    any,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of mercury contained in the
    product, expressed in milligrams,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated quantity to be manufactured or
    imported by the applicant in a calendar year, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an identification and description of each known
    use;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that, at the time of the application, there
  is no technically or economically feasible alternative to or
  substitute for the product that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} achieves a similar result, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a less harmful effect on the environment or on
    human health;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of a plan that identifies and describes the
  measures that the applicant will take to minimize or eliminate any
  harmful effect that the mercury contained in the product has or may
  have on the environment and human health, including measures to ensure
  that the mercury is handled safely and is not released into the
  environment during normal use of the product and at the end of its
  useful life;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the plan is to be implemented within
  30 days after the day on which the permit is issued; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the civic and postal addresses of the location where
  the information and supporting documents are kept.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister must issue the permit if the following
  conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has established that, at the time of
    the application, there is no technically or economically feasible
    alternative to or substitute for the product that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} achieves a similar result as would be achieved by
      using the product containing mercury, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a less harmful effect on the environment or
      on human health than the product containing mercury; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has submitted the plan referred to in
    paragraph 4(d), and that plan sets out measures that can reasonably
    be regarded as measures that will minimize or eliminate any harmful
    effect that the mercury contained in the product has or may have on
    the environment and human health.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Refusal

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the applicant has provided false or misleading information in
    support of their application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information and documents required under
    section 4 have not been provided or are insufficient to enable the
    Minister to process the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit expires three years after the day on which
  it is issued, unless it is renewed in accordance with section 6.

## [Labelling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806796 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Label --- products containing mercury

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (3), (4) and (5), any person that manufactures or
  imports a product that contains mercury must indicate the following
  information in a readily visible location on the product and, if
  applicable, on its package by means of a stamp, label or other mark:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the product contains mercury;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} if the mercury is contained in a component of the
    product, a statement indicating which component contains it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} safe handling procedures and the measures to be
    taken in case of accidental breakage, the address of a website where
    that information is available, or contact information for a person
    who can provide that information;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the options available for the disposal and
    recycling of the product in accordance with the laws of the
    jurisdiction where the disposal or recycling is to take place, the
    address of a website where that information is available, or contact
    information for a person who can provide that information; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the product should be disposed of
    or recycled in accordance with the applicable laws.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2024-109, s. 6\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Small product

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the product is too small to accommodate the
  information, the information must be indicated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a readily visible location on the product's
    package; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if there is no package or the package is too small
    to accommodate the information, in a notice attached to the product
    or a manual that accompanies the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Component of a product

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the mercury is contained in a component of the
  product, the information must be indicated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a readily visible location on the product; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a notice attached to the product or a manual
    that accompanies the product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Referral to website

  [(4.1)]{.lawlabel} The person referred to in subsection (1) may,
  instead of indicating the information in a notice or manual in
  accordance with paragraph (3)(b) or (4)(b), indicate in the notice or
  manual the address of a website where the information is set out.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

  [(4.2)]{.lawlabel} The information must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the information is indicated on the product or
    the product's package,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} be in both official languages,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} be presented in a font size of at least 10
      points with characters that are at least 3 mm in height, are
      legible and indelible and are impressed, embossed or in a colour
      that contrasts with the label's background or the colour of the
      product or its package, as applicable,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} be enclosed by a border, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} be easily distinguishable from other graphic
      material on the product or its package;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the information is indicated in a notice or on a
    website, be in both official languages; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the information is indicated in a manual, be in
    English, French or both official languages, as requested by the
    first retail purchaser.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) to (4.2) do not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} replacement parts referred to in subsection 3(3);
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} products that are manufactured for export.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Hg Symbol

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports a product belonging to a category
  set out in any of items 2 to 13, column 1, of Schedule 1 or in any

  contrasts with the label's background or the colour of the product, as
  applicable.

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if a product is too small to
  accommodate the symbol Hg in a font size of at least 10 points with
  characters that are at least 3 mm in height, the symbol Hg must be
  indicated in the closest possible font size and character height and
  be, at a minimum, in a font size of 7 points with characters that are
  2 mm in height.

## [Determination of Total Quantity of Mercury]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806833 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  analysis performed to determine the total quantity of mercury for the
  purposes of these Regulations must be performed by a laboratory that
  meets the following conditions at the time of the analysis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
      Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
      requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
      laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
      International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
      Recognition Arrangement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environment Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the scope of its
    accreditation includes the analysis performed to determine the total
    quantity of mercury.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Standards of good practice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If no method has been recognized by a standards
  development organization in respect of the analysis performed to
  determine the total quantity of mercury and the scope of the
  laboratory's accreditation does not therefore include that analysis,
  the analysis must be performed in accordance with standards of good
  scientific practice that are generally accepted at the time that it is
  performed.

## [Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806842 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting requirements

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports a product that contains mercury,
  other than a replacement part referred to in subsection 3(3), must
  submit a report to the Minister

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 2025 calendar year, on or before
    March 31, 2026;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the 2027 calendar year, on or before
    March 31, 2028; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of every third calendar year after the
    2027 calendar year, on or before March 31 of the calendar year
    following the year in respect of which the report is prepared.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the person,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, telephone number, fax number and
      email address, as well as the civic and postal addresses of their
      principal place of business in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name, title, telephone
      number, fax number and email address of their authorized
      representative, as well as the civic and postal addresses of their
      authorized representative's principal place of business in Canada;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its common or generic name and its trade name, if
      any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name of the category set out
      in column 1 of Schedule 1 or 2 to which the product belongs or the
      number of the permit issued under subsection 5(1),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of mercury contained in the
      product, expressed in milligrams,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity manufactured during the calendar
      year in question, if applicable,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the quantity imported during the calendar year in
      question, if applicable, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the quantity exported during the calendar year
      in question, if applicable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Component subject to these Regulations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a person that
  manufactures a product the mercury content of which is contained in a
  component that is itself a product that was subject to these
  Regulations at the time of its manufacture or import.

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806865 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports a product that contains mercury
  must maintain records that demonstrate that the product was
  manufactured or imported in accordance with the Act and these
  Regulations and that include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that manufactures a
    product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the common or generic name of the product
      manufactured, and its trade name, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name of the category set out
      in column 1 of Schedule 1 or 2 to which the product belongs or the
      number of the permit issued under subsection 5(1),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product manufactured at
      each manufacturing plant,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of mercury contained in the
      product, expressed in milligrams,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date of manufacture, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product that is exported;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person that imports a product,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the common or generic name of the product
      imported, and its trade name, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, the name of the category set out
      in column 1 of Schedule 1 or 2 to which the product belongs or the
      number of the permit issued under subsection 5(1),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product imported,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the total quantity of mercury contained in the
      product, expressed in milligrams,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
      number and, if any, the fax number and email address of the
      principal place of business of the shipper,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry at which the product was
      imported,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the date of import,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the Harmonized Commodity Description and
      Coding System tariff classification number for the product, as set
      out in the schedule to the Customs Tariff,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the person by
      the Minister of National Revenue,

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} copies of the bill of lading, invoice and all
      documents submitted to the Canada Border Services Agency for the
      product shipped, and

    - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the product that is exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record of compliance with section 3.1

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} If the person has manufactured or imported a
  product in contravention of section 3, they must maintain records that
  demonstrate compliance with section 3.1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records and supporting documents must be kept for
  a period of at least five years after the day on which the records are
  made.
