## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780817 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780818 .Subheading}

- **[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Part II of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approved organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approved organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      organization that is approved by any province for the teaching of
      first aid. ([organisme agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[CSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[CSA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Standards
      Association. ([CSA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[oxygen-deficient atmosphere]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oxygen-deficient atmosphere]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
      atmosphere in which there is less than 18% by volume of oxygen at
      a pressure of one atmosphere or in which the partial pressure of
      oxygen is less than 135 mm Hg. ([air à faible teneur en
      oxygène]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[protection equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[protection equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes safety
      materials, equipment, devices and clothing. ([équipement de
      protection]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[qualified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[qualified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      specified duty, a person who, because of their knowledge, training
      and experience, is qualified to perform that duty safely and
      properly. ([personne qualifiée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[readily available]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[readily available]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
      document, present and easily accessible at the work place at all
      times. ([facilement accessible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[safety restraining device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[safety restraining device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a safety
      harness, seat, rope, belt, strap, chain or lifeline designed to be
      used by a person to protect them from falling and includes every
      fitting, fastening or accessory attached to it. ([dispositif de
      retenue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The incorporation by reference of any standard in
  these Regulations is an incorporation of the standard as amended from
  time to time.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite any provision in any standard incorporated by
  reference in these Regulations, a reference to another publication in
  that standard is a reference to the publication as amended from time
  to time.

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780832 .Subheading}

**[[1.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations
apply to any person who is not an employee but who performs for an
employer to which these Regulations apply activities whose primary
purpose is to enable the person to acquire knowledge or experience, and
to the employer, as if that person were an employee of the employer, and
every provision of these Regulations must be read accordingly.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-211, s. 5
:::

**[[1.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
in respect of employees employed on board aircraft while in operation
and in respect of persons granted access to those aircraft by the
employer.

available for examination by the Head of Compliance and Enforcement, the

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Levels of Sound]{.HTitleText1} {#h-780843 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780844 .Subheading}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[A-weighted sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[A-weighted sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    sound pressure level as determined by a measurement system that
    includes an A-weighting filter that its manufacturer represents as
    meeting the requirements set out in the International
    Electrotechnical Commission International Standard IEC
    61672-1:2002(E), 1st edition 2002-2005 Electroacoustics -- Sound
    Level Meters. ([niveau de pression acoustique pondérée
    A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dBA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dBA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means decibel A-weighted and is a unit
    of A-weighted sound pressure level. ([dBA]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[noise exposure level (L~ex,8~)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[noise exposure level (L~ex,8~)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 10
    times the logarithm to the base 10 of the time integral over any
    24-hour period of a squared A-weighted sound pressure divided by 8,
    the reference sound pressure being 20 μPa. ([niveau d'exposition
    (L~ex,8~)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sound level meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sound level meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device for
    measuring sound pressure level that its manufacturer represents as
    meeting the performance requirements for a Type 2 instrument as
    specified in the International Electrotechnical Commission
    International Standard IEC 61672-1:2002(E), 1st edition 2002-2005
    Electroacoustics -- Sound Level
    Meters. ([sonomètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means 20 times the
    logarithm to the base 10 of the ratio of the root mean square
    pressure of a sound to the reference sound pressure of 20 μPa,
    expressed in decibels. ([niveau de pression
    acoustique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Measurement and Calculation of Exposure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780851 .Subheading}

- **[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this Part, the exposure of an employee to sound shall
  be measured using an instrument that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is recommended for that measurement in clause 4.3
    of CSA Standard CAN/CSA-Z107.56-13, Measurement of Noise Exposure;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is certified, by a certification body accredited by
    the Standards Council of Canada to grant the certification, as
    meeting the requirements for such an instrument set out in clause 4
    of that Standard.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The exposure of an employee to sound shall be
  measured in accordance with clauses 5, 6.4.1, 6.4.4, 6.5.2, 6.5.4,
  6.6.2 and 6.6.4 of the Standard referred to in paragraph (1)(a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, the measurement and
  calculation of the noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) to which an employee
  is exposed shall take into account their exposure to A-weighted sound
  pressure levels of 74 dBA and greater.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this Part, the measurement and
  calculation of the noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) may also take into
  account the exposure of the employee to A-weighted sound pressure
  levels that are less than 74 dBA.

### [Hazard Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780861 .Subheading}

- **[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an employee may be exposed, to an A-weighted sound pressure level
  equal to or greater than 84 dBA for a period that is likely to
  endanger the employee's hearing, the employer shall, without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
    investigation of the degree of potential exposure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the work place committee or the health and
    safety representative of the investigation and the name of the
    person appointed to carry out the investigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The measurement of the A-weighted sound pressure
  level shall be performed instantaneously, during normal working
  conditions, using the slow response setting of a sound level meter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the investigation referred to in subsection (1),
  the following matters shall be considered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sources of sound on board the aircraft;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the A-weighted sound pressure levels to which the
    employee is likely to be exposed and the duration of such exposure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the methods being used to reduce the exposure;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the exposure of the employee is likely to
    exceed the limits established by paragraph 2.4(a); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the employee is likely to be exposed to a
    noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) equal to or greater than 84 dBA.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On completion of the investigation and after
  consultation with the work place committee or the health and safety
  representative, the person appointed to carry out the investigation
  shall write, sign and date a report setting out their

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} observations respecting the matters considered
    under subsection (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommendations respecting the measures that should
    be taken in order to comply with sections 2.4 to 2.8; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recommendations respecting the use of hearing
    protectors by employees who are exposed to a noise exposure level
    (L~ex,8~) equal to or greater than 84 dBA but not greater than 87
    dBA.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The report shall be kept by the employer at a
  location accessible to affected employees for a period of 10 years
  after the date of the report.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If it is stated in the report that an employee is
  likely to be exposed to a noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) equal to or
  greater than 84 dBA, the employer shall, without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide the employee with written information
    describing the hazards associated with exposure to high levels of
    sound;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the report readily available to the employee;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} post and keep posted in a conspicuous place at a
    location accessible to the employee a notice stating where the
    report may be reviewed.

### [Limits of Exposure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780882 .Subheading}

**[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** No employee shall, in
any 24-hour period, be exposed to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an A-weighted sound pressure level set out in column
  1 of the schedule to this Part for a duration of exposure exceeding
  the applicable duration set out in column 2; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) that exceeds 87 dBA.

### [Reduction of Sound Exposure]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780886 .Subheading}

**[[2.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.5 .sectionLabel}** If feasible, every
employer shall, by engineering controls or other physical means other
than hearing protectors, reduce an employee's exposure to a sound level
that does not exceed the limits referred to in section 2.4.

### [Hearing Protection]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780896 .Subheading}

- **[[2.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an employer is required to make a report in accordance with section
  2.6, the employer shall, as soon as feasible, provide every employee
  who is exposed to the sound level referred to in section 2.4 with a
  hearing protector that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is certified, by a certification body accredited by
    the Standards Council of Canada to grant the certification, as
    meeting the requirements set out in CSA Standard CAN/CSA--Z94.2-02
    (R2007), Hearing Protection Devices --- Performance, Selection, Care
    and Use; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} prevents the exposure to the sound level referred
    to in section 2.4.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an employer provides a hearing protector to an
  employee under subsection (1), the employer shall, in consultation
  with the work place committee or the health and safety representative,
  formulate and implement a program to train the employee in the
  fitting, care and use of the hearing protector.

### [Warning Signs]{.HTitleText2} {#h-780902 .Subheading}

**[[2.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.8 .sectionLabel}** If an employee may be
exposed to an A-weighted sound pressure level that is likely to exceed
the limits established by paragraph 2.4(a), the employer shall ensure
that employees are warned, in writing and by signage on board the
aircraft, of a potentially hazardous level of sound on board the
aircraft.

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

generation, distribution or use of electricity.

performed on electrical equipment on board an aircraft shall be
performed by a qualified person.

**[[3.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.4 .sectionLabel}** If there is a risk that

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the equipment is separated or disconnected from every
  source of electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic or other kind of energy
  that is capable of making electrical equipment dangerous.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Hazardous Substances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781004 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781005 .Subheading}

**[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[airborne asbestos fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airborne asbestos fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means asbestos
    fibres that are longer than 5 μm (micrometres) with an aspect ratio
    equal to or greater than 3:1 and that are carried by the
    air. ([fibres d'amiante aéroportées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asbestos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asbestos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means actinolite, amosite,
    anthophyllite, chrysotile, crocidolite and tremolite in their
    fibrous form. ([amiante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asbestos-containing material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asbestos-containing material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any article that is manufactured and contains 1%
      or more asbestos by weight at the time of manufacture or that
      contains a concentration of 1% or more asbestos as determined in
      accordance with Method 9002 set out in the document entitled NIOSH
      Manual of Analytical Methods, published by the National Institute
      for Occupational Safety and Health, as amended from time to time,
      or in accordance with a scientifically proven method used to
      collect and analyze a representative sample of the material; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any material that contains a concentration of 1%
      or more asbestos as determined in accordance with Method 9002 set
      out in the document entitled NIOSH Manual of Analytical Methods,
      published by the National Institute for Occupational Safety and
      Health, as amended from time to time, or in accordance with a
      scientifically proven method used to collect and analyze a
      representative sample of the material. ([matériau contenant de
      l'amiante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearance air sampling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearance air sampling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the action of
    taking samples to determine if the concentration of airborne
    asbestos fibres inside an enclosure is below the limit referred to
    in section 5.16 to permit the dismantling of a containment
    system. ([échantillonnage de l'air après
    décontamination]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[containment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[containment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an isolation system
    that is designed to effectively contain asbestos fibre within a
    designated work area where asbestos-containing material is handled,
    removed, encapsulated or enclosed. ([confinement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[encapsulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[encapsulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treatment of an
    asbestos-containing material with a sealant that penetrates the
    material and binds the asbestos fibres together, and the treatment
    of the surface of the asbestos-containing material with a sealant
    that creates a membrane on the surface, to prevent the release of
    asbestos fibres into the air. ([encapsulation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical barrier such as
    drywall, plywood or metal sheeting that, as part of the containment
    system, isolates asbestos-containing material from adjacent areas in
    a building to prevent the release of airborne asbestos fibres into
    those areas. ([encloisonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[friable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[friable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of
    asbestos-containing material, that the material, when dry, can be
    easily crumbled or powdered by hand
    pressure. ([friable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[glove bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[glove bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a polyethylene or polyvinyl
    chloride bag that is affixed around an asbestos-containing source
    and that permits asbestos-containing material to be removed while
    minimizing the release of airborne asbestos fibres into the work
    place. ([sac à gants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazard information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazard information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    hazardous substance, information on the proper and safe storage,
    handling, use and disposal of the hazardous substance, including
    information relating to the health and physical hazards that it
    presents. ([renseignements sur les risques]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[HEPA filter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HEPA filter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a high-efficiency
    particulate air filter that has been tested to ensure efficiency
    equal to or exceeding 99.97% for removal of airborne particles
    having a mean aerodynamic diameter of 0.3 µm (micrometres) from the
    air. ([filtre HEPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[high-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[high-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that
    involves the handling or disturbance of friable asbestos-containing
    material or is carried out in proximity to friable
    asbestos-containing material, that requires a high level of control
    to prevent exposure to excessive concentrations of airborne asbestos
    fibres and that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal or disturbance of more than 1 m^2^ of
      friable asbestos-containing material in a work place, even if the
      activity is divided into smaller jobs,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the spray application of a sealant to a friable
      asbestos-containing material,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning or removal of air-handling
      equipment, other than filters, in a building that has sprayed-on
      fireproofing or sprayed-on thermal insulation that is
      asbestos-containing material,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the repair, alteration or demolition of all or
      part of a kiln, metallurgical furnace or similar structure that
      contains asbestos-containing material,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the activity is carried out by means of power tools
      that are not attached to dust-collecting devices equipped with
      HEPA filters, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the repair, alteration or demolition of all or
      part of a building in which asbestos is or was used in the
      manufacture of products, unless the asbestos was cleaned up and
      removed. ([activité à risque élevé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lower explosive limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lower explosive limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lower limit
    of flammability of a chemical agent or a combination of chemical
    agents at ambient temperature and pressure, expressed

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a gas or vapour, as a percentage in air by
      volume; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for dust, as the weight of dust per volume of
      air. ([limite explosive inférieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[low-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[low-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that
    involves the handling of asbestos-containing material or is carried
    out in proximity to non-friable asbestos-containing material and
    that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the installation or removal of ceiling tiles that
      are made of non-friable asbestos-containing material and cover an
      area of less than 7.5 m^2^,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the installation or removal of other non-friable
      asbestos-containing material, if the material is not being broken,
      cut, drilled, abraded, ground, sanded or vibrated and dust is not
      being generated,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the material is wetted to control the spread of dust
      or fibres and the activity is carried out only by means of
      non-powered hand-held tools, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the removal of less than 1 m² of drywall in which
      joint cement containing asbestos has been used. ([activité à
      faible risque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[moderate-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[moderate-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    that involves the handling of asbestos-containing material or is
    carried out in proximity to friable asbestos-containing material,
    that is not otherwise classified as a low-risk activity or high-risk
    activity and that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal of all or part of a false ceiling to
      gain access to a work area, if asbestos-containing material is
      likely to be found on the surface of the false ceiling,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the removal or disturbance of 1 m^2^ or less of
      friable asbestos-containing material during repair, alteration,
      maintenance or demolition work in a work place,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the enclosure of friable asbestos-containing
      material,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application of tape, sealant or other
      covering to pipe or boiler insulation that is asbestos-containing
      material,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the removal of ceiling tiles that are
      asbestos-containing material, if the tiles cover an area of
      greater than 2 m^2^ and are removed without being broken, cut,
      drilled, abraded, ground, sanded or vibrated,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the material is not wetted to control the spread of
      dust or fibres and the activity is carried out only by means of
      non-powered hand-held tools,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the removal of 1 m^2^ or more of drywall in which
      joint cement that is asbestos-containing material has been used,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the activity is carried out by means of power tools
      that are attached to dust-collecting devices equipped with HEPA
      filters,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of insulation that is
      asbestos-containing material from a pipe, duct or similar
      structure using a glove bag, and

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning or removal of filters used in
      air-handling equipment in a building that has sprayed-on
      fireproofing that is asbestos-containing material. ([activité à
      risque modéré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    in subsection 1(1) of the [Hazardous Products

    ancillary to that activity, and the supervision of that activity and

##### [Hazard Investigation]{.HTitleText4} {#h-781057 .Subheading}

- **[[5.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the health or safety of an employee is likely to be endangered by
  exposure to a hazardous substance in a work place, the employer shall,
  without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
    investigation in that regard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the work place committee or the health and
    safety representative of the proposed investigation, and of the name
    of the qualified person appointed to investigate, so that they may
    participate in the investigation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In an investigation, the following criteria shall be
  taken into consideration:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the chemical, biological and physical properties of
    the hazardous substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the routes of exposure to the hazardous substance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acute and chronic effects on health of exposure
    to the hazardous substance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the hazardous substance to be
    handled;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the hazardous substance is
    stored, used, handled and disposed of;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the control methods used to eliminate or reduce
    exposure of the employees to the hazardous substance;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the concentration or level of the hazardous
    substance to which an employee is likely to be exposed; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} whether the concentration of an airborne chemical
    agent or the level of ionizing or non-ionizing radiation is likely
    to exceed 50% of the values referred to in section 5.16 or the
    limits referred to in subsection 5.19(2).

##### [Medical Examinations]{.HTitleText4} {#h-781079 .Subheading}

- **[[5.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the report recommends a medical examination for the employees likely
  to be exposed to a hazardous substance, the employer shall consult a
  physician to ascertain the necessity for that medical examination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a physician shall
  conclude that a medical examination is necessary only if, in light of
  the factors referred to in subsection 5.4(2), the likelihood and
  consequences of exposure to the hazardous substance outweigh the
  intrusiveness of the medical examination or the disruption to bodily
  integrity that might be necessary.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a physician considers that a medical examination
  is necessary, the employer shall not permit an employee to handle the
  hazardous substance unless a physician acceptable to the employee has
  examined the employee and declared the employee fit, or fit with
  restrictions, to handle the hazardous substance.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of determining whether an employee
  is fit, or fit with restrictions, a physician shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take into account

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the factors referred to in subsection 5.4(2), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the likely effects of handling the hazardous
      substance on the employee's health and that of other employees and
      the capacity of the employee to perform the work; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} perform only those tests or examinations that are
    necessary to make that determination.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the physician examining an employee under
  subsection (3) declares the employee fit with restrictions to handle
  the hazardous substance, the employer shall not permit the employee to
  handle the hazardous substance except in accordance with the specified
  restrictions.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If an employer consults a physician under subsection
  (1), the employer shall keep a copy of the physician's decision with
  the report referred to in section 5.5.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The cost of a medical examination referred to in
  subsection (3) shall be borne by the employer.

##### [Control of Hazards]{.HTitleText4} {#h-781132 .Subheading}

- **[[5.16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  employee shall be exposed to a concentration of an airborne chemical
  agent, other than airborne asbestos fibres, in excess of the value for
  that chemical agent adopted by the American Conference of Governmental
  Industrial Hygienists in its publication entitled Threshold Limit
  Values (TLVs) and Biological Exposure Indices (BEIs), as amended from
  time to time.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall ensure that an employee's
  exposure to a concentration of airborne asbestos fibres is as close to
  zero as possible, but in any event the employer shall ensure that the
  concentration does not exceed the value for airborne asbestos fibres
  adopted by the American Conference of Governmental Industrial
  Hygienists in its publication entitled Threshold Limit Values (TLV)
  and Biological Exposure Indices (BEI), as amended from time to time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the concentration of an airborne chemical agent is
  likely to exceed the value referred to in subsection (1) or if the
  concentration of airborne asbestos fibres is likely to exceed zero,
  air samples shall be taken by a qualified person and the concentration
  of the chemical agent or the airborne asbestos fibres shall be
  determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the standards set out by the
    United States National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
    in the NIOSH Manual of Analytical Methods; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no specific standards for the chemical agent are
    listed in the document referred to in paragraph (a), in accordance
    with a scientifically proven method used to collect and analyze a
    representative sample of the chemical agent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A paper or electronic record of each determination
  made under subsection (2) shall be kept by the employer at a location
  accessible to affected employees, for a period of 3 years from the
  date of the determination.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The record shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the analysis;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the hazardous substance in respect of which the
    analysis was made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sampling and testing method used;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the result obtained; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and occupation of the person who made the
    analysis.

###### [Asbestos Exposure Control Plan]{.HTitleText5} {#h-781167 .Subheading}

**[[5.19.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.19.2 .sectionLabel}** Before
undertaking any work activity that involves asbestos-containing
material, an employer shall, in consultation with the policy committee
or, if there is no policy committee, the work place committee or the

asbestos exposure control plan that requires the employer to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that a hazard investigation under section 5.4

  prepared as a result of the investigation and, if necessary, have a

  identified by signs and labels or by any other effective manner;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all friable asbestos-containing material
  present in the work place is controlled by removal, enclosure or
  encapsulation or by any other effective manner to prevent employee
  exposure to asbestos;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} ensure that procedures and control measures for
  moderate-risk activities and high-risk activities are developed and
  implemented; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} develop and implement an employee education and
  training program that is specific to asbestos-containing material.

###### [Asbestos Dust, Waste and Debris Removal]{.HTitleText5} {#h-781182 .Subheading}

- **[[5.19.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.19.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During any work activities that
  involve friable asbestos-containing materials, an employer shall
  ensure that the following activities are carried out frequently and at
  regular intervals as determined by a qualified person, at the end of
  each work shift and immediately after the work activity is completed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all asbestos dust, waste and debris are removed by
    vacuuming with a vacuum cleaner that is equipped with a HEPA filter,
    damp-mopping or wet-sweeping the area that is contaminated with the
    asbestos dust, waste or debris; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any drop sheets that are contaminated with asbestos
    dust, waste or debris are wetted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All asbestos dust, waste or debris and any drop
  sheets that are contaminated with asbestos dust, waste or debris shall
  be placed in a container referred to in section 5.19.11.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-132, s. 19
:::

**[[5.19.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.19.5 .sectionLabel}** If a glove bag is
used for the removal of asbestos insulation from pipes, ducts and
similar structures, an employer shall ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is sealed to prevent the release of
  asbestos fibres into the work area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is inspected for damage or defects
  immediately before it is attached to the pipe, duct or similar
  structure and at regular intervals during its use;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all waste from asbestos-containing material that is
  on surfaces is washed to the bottom of the glove bag and all exposed
  asbestos-containing material is encapsulated when it is inside the
  glove bag;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is evacuated using a vacuum cleaner
  that is equipped with a HEPA filter to remove the air inside the bag
  prior to the removal of the glove bag; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} after the glove bag is removed, all exposed surfaces
  are cleaned with a damp cloth and a vacuum cleaner that is equipped
  with a HEPA filter.

###### [Decontamination]{.HTitleText5} {#h-781198 .Subheading}

  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before leaving a work area that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if their protective clothing is to be reused,

###### [Air Sampling]{.HTitleText5} {#h-781209 .Subheading}

- **[[5.19.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.19.8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall ensure that a
  qualified person takes air samples to test for airborne asbestos
  fibres

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the vicinity of the containment system during
    any work activity that involves asbestos-containing material and, in
    the case of a work activity that lasts longer than 24 hours, at
    least daily;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the clean room during removal and clean-up
    operations and, in the case of removal and clean-up operations that
    last longer than 24 hours, at least daily; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in contaminated areas that are inside the
    containment system as necessary during removal and clean-up
    operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall ensure that the following air
  samples are taken:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two samples for every area in an enclosure that is
    10 m^2^ or less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three samples for every area in an enclosure that
    is more than 10 m^2^ and not more than 500 m^2^; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} five samples for every area in an enclosure that is
    more than 500 m^2^.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 24 hours after obtaining the air sampling test
  results, the employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a copy of the results in a conspicuous place
    in the work place; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the results available to the policy committee,
    if any, the work place committee and the health and safety
    representative.

###### [Clearance Air Sampling]{.HTitleText5} {#h-781224 .Subheading}

- **[[5.19.9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.19.9
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before dismantling a containment
  system and after all asbestos dust, waste and debris have been cleaned
  up, removed or encapsulated, an employer shall ensure that clearance
  air samples are taken inside the enclosure and that the concentration
  of airborne asbestos fibres is determined in accordance with Method
  7400 set out in the document entitled NIOSH Manual of Analytical
  Methods, published by the National Institute for Occupational Safety
  and Health, as amended from time to time, or in accordance with a
  scientifically proven method used to collect and analyze a
  representative sample of airborne asbestos fibres.

  shall ensure that forced air is used inside the enclosure to dislodge
  any asbestos fibres from all surfaces and keep them airborne.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Clearance air sampling shall be taken until the
  concentrations of airborne asbestos fibres do not exceed the values
  referred to in subsection 5.16(1.1).

## [PART 8]{.HLabel1}[Materials Handling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781397 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781398 .Subheading}

**[[8.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[materials handling equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[materials handling equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment
    used to transport, lift, move or position persons, materials, goods
    or things including auxiliary equipment and rigging devices and
    includes mobile equipment used to lift, hoist or position persons,
    but does not include equipment that is affixed to the exterior of an
    aircraft. ([appareil de manutention des matériaux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employee who controls the
    operation of materials handling equipment and who has received
    training in the procedures referred to in subsection 8.5(1).
    ([opérateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[safe working load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[safe working load]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the maximum load
    that materials handling equipment is designed and constructed to
    handle or support safely under particular operating conditions.
    ([charge de travail admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781403 .Subheading}

- **[[8.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  feasible, materials handling equipment used on board an aircraft shall
  be designed and constructed so that failure of any of its parts will
  not result in loss of control of the equipment or create a hazardous
  condition.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All glass and other transparent materials used in
  doors, windows and other parts of materials handling equipment used on
  board an aircraft shall be of a type that does not shatter into sharp
  or dangerous pieces on impact.

### [First Aid Attendants]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781469 .Subheading}

**[[9.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.3 .sectionLabel}** If there are three or
more employees on board an aircraft, at least one of whom is not a
flight crew member, one of them shall be a first aid attendant.

**[[9.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.4 .sectionLabel}** A first aid attendant
shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} have access to a first aid kit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} render first aid to employees who are incapacitated;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if required, accompany an incapacitated employee to a
  health unit or a medical treatment facility and render first aid in
  transit;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in providing care as a first aid attendant, have
  precedence over anyone not trained in first aid; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} be responsible for providing care for an
  incapacitated employee until the treatment is complete or the employee
  is under the care of an equally or more qualified caregiver.

### [Training Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781477 .Subheading}

- **[[9.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the time required to reach a health unit or a medical treatment
  facility is two hours or less, the first aid attendant shall have
  successfully completed a basic first aid course, the elements of which
  are set out in section 1 of Schedule 1 to this Part, or a first aid
  course for flight attendants, the elements of which are set out in
  section 3 of that Schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the time required to reach a health unit or a
  medical treatment facility is more than two hours, the first aid
  attendant shall have successfully completed a standard first aid
  course, the elements of which are set out in section 2 of Schedule 1
  to this Part, or a first aid course for flight attendants, the
  elements of which are set out in section 3 of that Schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall determine, in consultation with
  the policy committee or, if there is no policy committee, the work
  place committee or the health and safety representative, the elective
  lesson elements, if any, of the flight attendant first aid courses
  required on any particular flight, taking into account the likelihood
  of their use.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} First aid courses for flight attendants shall be
  given by a qualified person who holds a valid certification from an
  approved organization attesting that they are competent to deliver
  first aid courses.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Basic and standard first aid certificates and first
  aid certifications for flight attendants are valid for a maximum of
  three years from the day on which they are issued.

## [PART 10]{.HLabel1}[Hazardous Occurrence Investigation, Recording and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-781601 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781602 .Subheading}

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[disabling injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disabling injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employment injury
    or an occupational illness that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevents an employee from reporting for work or
      from effectively performing all the duties connected with the
      employee's regular work on any day subsequent to the day on which
      the injury or illness occurred, whether or not that subsequent day
      is a working day for that employee;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} results in the loss of, or complete loss of the
      use of, a body member or part of a body member by an employee; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} results in the permanent impairment of a body
      function of an employee. ([blessure invalidante]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employment injury or
    an occupational illness for which medical treatment is provided and
    excludes a disabling injury. ([blessure légère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[regional office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regional office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the office of the
    Department of Transport that is administratively responsible for an
    employee's assigned base. ([bureau régional]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-781612 .Subheading}

**[[10.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.3 .sectionLabel}** If an employer
becomes aware of an accident, occupational illness or other hazardous
occurrence affecting any of their employees in the course of employment,
the employer shall, without delay,

  investigation of the hazardous occurrence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the work place committee or the health and
  safety representative of the proposed investigation, and of the name
  of the qualified person appointed to investigate, so that they may
  participate in the investigation; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} take necessary measures to prevent a recurrence of
  the hazardous occurrence.
