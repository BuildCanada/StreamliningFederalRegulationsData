## Annex Code 9075 Sales Tax Remission Order {#annex-code-9075-sales-tax-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 795
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Sales Tax on Goods Classified Under
Code 9075 of the Customs Tariff
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563878 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Annex Code 9075 Sales Tax Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._795).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563882 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of sales tax on all machinery and other goods imported into Canada, the
customs duties on which are subject to drawback under Code 9075 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in an amount equal to the
difference between the amount of sales tax paid or payable on all
machinery and other goods and the amount of sales tax that would be
payable in respect of all machinery and other goods if the duty-paid
value used to calculate that sales tax were reduced by the amount of the
drawback.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::
