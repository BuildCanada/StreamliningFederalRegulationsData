## Machinery Sales Tax Remission Order {#machinery-sales-tax-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 797
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Sales Tax on Machinery on which
Customs Duties are Remitted Pursuant to Certain Tariff Items
::::

::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563893 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Machinery Sales Tax Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._797).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

## [Remission of Sales Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-563897 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15)
on goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which customs duties have been remitted
  in accordance with orders made after November 5, 1980, pursuant to
  tariff items 42700-1, 42700-2, 42700-3, 42700-4, 42700-5, 42700-9,
  42700-10, 42700-11, 42700-12, 42700-13, 42700-14, 42700-15, 42700-16,
  42701-1, 42701-2 or 42701-3 of Schedule II to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), R.S.C. 1985, c. C-54, which orders
  continue in force under section 133 of the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), R.S., c. 41 (3rd Supp.), and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that are released and accounted for under the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) on or after November 5, 1980,

in an amount equal to the difference between the amount of sales tax
paid or payable on the goods and the amount of sales tax that would be
payable in respect of the goods if the duty paid value used to calculate
the sales tax on the goods were reduced by the amount of the remission
of customs duties granted under this Order.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
- SI/92-203, s. 2
:::
