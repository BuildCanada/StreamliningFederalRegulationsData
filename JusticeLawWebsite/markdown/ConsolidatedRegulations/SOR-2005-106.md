## Order Suspending the Application of Concessions On Imports of Certain Products Originating in the United States {#order-suspending-the-application-of-concessions-on-imports-of-certain-products-originating-in-the-united-states .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-106
:::

[WORLD TRADE ORGANIZATION AGREEMENT IMPLEMENTATION
ACT](/eng/acts/W-11.8)

Registration 2005-04-19

Order Suspending the Application of Concessions On Imports of Certain
Products Originating in the United States

P.C. 2005-615 2005-04-19
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister for International Trade, pursuant to subsections 13(1) and
(3) of the [World Trade Organization Agreement Implementation
Act](/eng/acts/W-11.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Order Suspending the Application of Concessions On Imports
of Certain Products Originating in the United
States](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-106).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714857 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, [[U.S.
goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods that are classified under the
following tariff item Nos. in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) that originate
in the United States, as determined in accordance with sections 1 and 2
of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Rules of Origin Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 0103.10.00;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 0103.91.00;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 0103.92.00;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 0301.10.00;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} 0303.79.00;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} 0307.10.10;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} 0307.10.20; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} 2402.20.00.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714867 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to U.S. goods that are in transit to Canada on May 1, 2005.

## [Surtax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714869 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Rights and privileges on
U.S. goods under the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) are partially
suspended by imposing a surtax on those goods in an amount of 15% [ad
valorem]{.otherLang lang="la"} on the value for duty as determined in
accordance with sections 45 to 55 of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Effective Period]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714871 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order shall have
effect for the period commencing on May 1, 2005 and ending on April 30,
2006.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714873 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on May 1, 2005.
