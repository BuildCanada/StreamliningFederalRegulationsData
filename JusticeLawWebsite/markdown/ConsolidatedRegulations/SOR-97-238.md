## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005630 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Administration
    Act](/eng/acts/F-11). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[medium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[medium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that is designed for
    storing and archiving information and includes a magnetic tape, disk
    and diskette. ([support]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[paid instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[paid instrument]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a record after the
    amounts specified in the instructions have been paid or the claim
    has been settled. ([effet payé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[record]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an original

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} instruction for payment or instrument for
        settlement, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} claim for settlement; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a medium that contains an original instruction or
      claim or that is used to store that instruction or claim.
      ([registre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Custody and Retention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005640 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Receiver General shall
have the care and custody of any paid instrument that is provided to the
Receiver General in accordance with subsection 36(1) of the Act and
shall retain the instrument until it is destroyed in accordance with
section 5.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  paid instrument referred to in section 2 is in the form of a medium
  containing an original instruction or claim, the data contained in it
  may be reproduced onto another medium.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The medium that contains the original instruction or
  claim may be reused once the data contained in it have been completely
  erased.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A paid instrument retained
in accordance with section 2 must meet the following requirements:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the data contained in it must be accessible so as to
  be usable for later reference; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the instrument is in the form of a medium, the
  data contained in it must:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} represent accurately the information generated,
    transmitted or received,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} enable the identification of the origin and
    destination of the instruction or claim and the date and time of its
    transmission or reception, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an electronic instruction for
    payment, include the digital signature.

## [Destruction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005651 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 3 and 6, at any time during the seventh year after an
  instrument has been paid, the Receiver General or the minister who
  issued the payment or settled the claim, as the case may be, shall
  destroy the paid instrument in accordance with subsection (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A paid instrument shall be destroyed by shredding,
  pulping, burning, crushing, erasing or any other means that will
  ensure that the paid instrument cannot be reused.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** If a paid instrument is
required for the purposes of any litigation, claim, inquiry,
investigation or other examination, the Receiver General or the
appropriate minister shall delay destruction of the instrument until it
is no longer required for that purpose.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005656 .Part}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005658 .Part}
