## Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Licensing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-flue-cured-tobacco-licensing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 185
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Licensing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Persons Engaged in the Marketing of Flue-Cured Tobacco Produced in
Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536191 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco Licensing (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._185).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536193 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured
    Tobacco Growers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act and
    the Plan; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Flue-Cured Tobacco
    Growers' Marketing Plan established pursuant to the Act and any
    regulations made thereunder to give effect to the Plan;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means unmanufactured flue-cured
    tobacco produced in Ontario. ([tabac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536199 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of tobacco in interprovincial and export trade and
to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Licence to Buy Tobacco]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536201 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall act as a buyer of tobacco unless he holds a licence
  issued by the Commodity Board in the form set out in the schedule.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence referred to in subsection (1) shall expire
  on June 30th of the year next following the year in which the licence
  was issued.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licence to buy tobacco may be refused where the applicant is not
  qualified by experience, financial responsibility and equipment to
  engage in the business of buying tobacco.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licence to buy tobacco may be suspended, revoked or
  refused for failure to observe, perform or carry out the provisions of
  the Act, the regulations made pursuant to the Act or any order or
  direction of the Commodity Board.
