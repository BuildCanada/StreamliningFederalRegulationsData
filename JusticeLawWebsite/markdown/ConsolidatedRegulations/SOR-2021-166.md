## [Board of Directors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1303170 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Number of directors

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(2) of the [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents
Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7), the number of directors is fixed at nine.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Directors to be appointed

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The number of directors
that are to be appointed to the Board under subsection 13(3) of the
[College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7) is
fixed at five.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1303175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}S.C. 2018, c. 27, s. 247

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-2021-166_e_hq_29407){#fn_SOR-2021-166_e_hq_29407-ID0EBCA}[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force on the day on which section
13 of the [College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents
Act](/eng/acts/C-33.7) comes into force, but if it is registered after
that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
