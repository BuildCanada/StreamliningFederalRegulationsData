## Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of Human Resources Development the Control and Supervision of the Voluntary Sector Affairs Directorate {#order-transferring-from-the-department-of-canadian-heritage-to-the-department-of-human-resources-development-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-voluntary-sector-affairs-directorate .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-231
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the
Department of Human Resources Development the Control and Supervision of
the Voluntary Sector Affairs Directorate

P.C. 2003-2088  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Department of Canadian Heritage to the Department of
Human Resources Development the control and supervision of the portion
of the public service in the Department of Canadian Heritage known as
the Voluntary Sector Affairs Directorate, effective December 12, 2003.
:::
