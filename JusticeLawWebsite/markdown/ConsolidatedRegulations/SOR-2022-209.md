## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1377641 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[goods that originate in Belarus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods that originate in Belarus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods
    that are eligible to be marked as goods of Belarus in accordance
    with the Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of
    Marking Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries) Regulations. ([marchandises
    originaires du Bélarus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods that originate in Russia]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods that originate in Russia]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods
    that are eligible to be marked as goods of Russia in accordance with
    the Determination of Country of Origin for the Purpose of Marking
    Goods (Non-CUSMA Countries) Regulations. ([marchandises originaires
    de la Russie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Extension of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1377645 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff is extended in respect of goods that
originate in Belarus and goods that originate in Russia.

## [Withdrawal of Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1377647 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff is withdrawn in respect of goods that
originate in Belarus and goods that originate in Russia.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1377649 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Section 3 does not apply to
goods of tariff item No. 2844.43.00.
