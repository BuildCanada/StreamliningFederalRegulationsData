## Returned Contributions Interest Regulations {#returned-contributions-interest-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 345
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 2, 1965

Regulations Respecting Interest on Returned Public Service
Superannuation Contributions
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550072 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Returned Contributions Interest
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._345).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550074 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
\"contribution\" means an amount paid by or on behalf of a contributor
to the Superannuation Account pursuant to subsection 6(1) of the [Public
Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been on deposit in the Superannuation Account for
  at least two years from the date of its receipt; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cannot be applied for the purpose for which it was
  originally paid into the Account because the election pursuant to
  which it was made is void.

## [Payment of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550078 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, interest is payable on a contribution
  calculated in respect of each quarter in each fiscal year as of the
  last day of June, September, December and March at the rate of one per
  cent of the amount of the contribution on deposit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Interest is payable, pursuant to subsection (1), from
  the first day of the first quarter commencing after the contribution
  was paid into the Superannuation Account until the last day of the
  quarter immediately preceding the day the contribution is paid out of
  that Account.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No interest is payable on a
contribution pursuant to section 3, where an amount in respect of the
contribution was paid to the contributor before April 1, 1967, unless
application is made therefor to the President of the Treasury Board.
