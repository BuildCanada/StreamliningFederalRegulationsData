## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744867 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).

## [Disclosure Requirements]{.HTitleText1} {#h-744870 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-bank affiliate and foreign bank

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 983(4)(g) of the Act, the word "bank", "banker"
  or "banking" may be used in a description of the corporate
  relationship between a non-bank affiliate of a foreign bank, within
  the meaning of subsection 507(1) of the Act, and a foreign bank that
  controls the non-bank affiliate if the description includes a
  statement that the foreign bank is not regulated in Canada as a
  financial institution, a bank holding company or an insurance holding
  company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the foreign bank is
  an authorized foreign bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-bank affiliate and entity associated
with foreign bank

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of paragraph 983(4)(h) of the Act, the word "bank", "banker"
  or "banking" may be used in a description of the corporate
  relationship between a non-bank affiliate of a foreign bank, within
  the meaning of subsection 507(1) of the Act, and an entity associated
  with a foreign bank that controls the non-bank affiliate if the
  description includes a statement that the entity associated with a
  foreign bank is not regulated in Canada as a financial institution, a
  bank holding company or an insurance holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the entity
  associated with a foreign bank is one described in any of
  paragraphs 468(1)(c) to (i) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Term and condition re: use of name of
foreign bank

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 983(10) of the Act, the Canadian entity must
  disclose, in conjunction with the use of the name or identifying mark
  of the foreign bank, that the foreign bank is not regulated in Canada
  as a financial institution, a bank holding company or an insurance
  holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the foreign bank is
  an authorized foreign bank.
