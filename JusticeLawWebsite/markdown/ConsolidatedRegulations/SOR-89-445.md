## Manitoba Pullet Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#manitoba-pullet-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-445
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1989-09-18

Order Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of
Pullets Raised in the Province of Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Egg Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3 of
the [Manitoba Pullet Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-352), made by Order
in Council P.C. 1980-1276 of May 15, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA} pursuant to section 2 of
the [Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes
the annexed [Order respecting the marketing in interprovincial and
export trade of pullets raised in the Province of
Manitoba](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-445).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBBA){#fn_1e}SOR/80-352, 1980 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1873
  :::

Winnipeg, Manitoba, September 6, 1989
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920376 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the Manitoba Pullet Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Order.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920378 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing Act
    of the Province of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Manitoba Egg
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person who raises pullets in the Province of
      Manitoba and includes an employee of the person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person who is entitled, under a lease or
      agreement, to a share of the pullets raised by a person referred
      to in paragraph (a), or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who, as security for a debt, takes
      possession of the pullets raised by a person referred to in
      paragraph (a); ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the female of any class of
    domestic chicken belonging to the species [Gallus
    domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"}, that is less than 20 weeks of age
    and is raised in the Province of Manitoba for the purpose of
    producing eggs for human consumption. ([poulette]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920387 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No producer shall market
pullets in interprovincial or export trade unless the Commodity Board
authorizes the producer to do so.
