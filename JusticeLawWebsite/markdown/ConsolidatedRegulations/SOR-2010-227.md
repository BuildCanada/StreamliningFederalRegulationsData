## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765398 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cargo vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is not a
      passenger vessel and is of 300 gross tonnage or more. ([bâtiment
      de charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international voyage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a voyage
      between a port in one country and a port in another country, but
      does not include a voyage solely on the Great Lakes, the St.
      Lawrence River and their connecting and tributary waters as far
      east as the lower exit of the St. Lambert Lock at
      Montréal. ([voyage international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[LRIT equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[LRIT equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      information-transmitting equipment for the long-range
      identification and tracking of a vessel. ([équipement
      LRIT]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[LRIT information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[LRIT information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the information
      referred to in section 5. ([renseignements LRIT]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of
      Transport. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[passenger vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[passenger vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that
      carries more than 12 passengers. ([bâtiment à
      passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sea area A1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sea area A1]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[sea area
      A2]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, [[sea area A3]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and
      [[sea area A4]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have the meanings assigned by
      regulation 2.1 of Chapter IV of SOLAS. ([zone océanique
      A1]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}, [zone océanique
      A2]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}, [zone océanique
      A3]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} et [zone océanique
      A4]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention
      for the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, and the Protocol of 1988
      relating to the Convention, as amended from time to
      time. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}When vessel is constructed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, a vessel is
  constructed on the earliest of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the day on which its keel is laid,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the day on which construction identifiable with a
    specific vessel begins, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the day on which assembly of the vessel reaches the
    lesser of 50 tonnes and 1% of the estimated mass of all structural
    material.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765415 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cargo vessels and passenger vessels

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of Canadian vessels everywhere if they

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} are engaged on international voyages; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} are cargo vessels or passenger vessels.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} pleasure craft; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} government vessels.

## [Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765425 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Authorized representative

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The authorized
representative of a vessel shall ensure that the requirements of
sections 4 to 10 are met.

## [LRIT Equipment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765428 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Vessels to be fitted

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  vessel shall be fitted with LRIT equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- sea area A1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a vessel
  that operates exclusively in sea area A1 if it is fitted with an
  automatic identification system that meets the requirements of section
  118 of the [Navigation Safety Regulations,
  2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216) and is operated in accordance
  with that section.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Automatic transmission

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The LRIT equipment fitted
on a vessel to meet the requirements of section 4 shall automatically
transmit the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's identity;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the vessel's position, particularly its latitude and
  longitude; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type approval or certification

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The LRIT
  equipment fitted on a vessel to meet the requirements of section 4
  shall be type-approved or certified by the Minister as meeting the
  performance standards and functional requirements set out in section 4
  of the Revised performance standards and functional requirements for
  the long-range identification and tracking of ships, the annex to
  International Maritime Organization Resolution MSC.263(84), as amended
  from time to time.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of interpreting section 4 of the
  annex referred to in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "should" shall be read to mean "shall"; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "Administration" shall be read to mean "Minister".

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Switching off equipment

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The LRIT
  equipment fitted on a vessel to meet the requirements of section 4
  shall be capable of being switched off on board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The vessel's master may switch off the LRIT equipment

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when international agreements, rules or standards
    provide for the protection of navigational information; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in exceptional circumstances and for the shortest
    duration possible when the equipment's operation is considered by
    the vessel's master to compromise the safety or security of the
    vessel.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing authorities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the master switches off the LRIT equipment in the
  case provided for by paragraph (2)(b), the master shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay inform a Marine Communications and
    Traffic Services Centre of the Canadian Coast Guard and, if the
    vessel is in the waters of a contracting government, the relevant
    maritime authority of that government; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an entry, in the record of navigational
    activities and events maintained under section 138 of the
    [Navigation Safety Regulations,
    2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216), setting out the reasons for
    the decision and indicating the period during which the equipment
    was switched off.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reducing frequency of or temporarily
stopping transmission

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The LRIT
  equipment fitted on a vessel to meet the requirements of section 4
  shall be capable of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} being configured to transmit the LRIT information
    at a reduced frequency of once every 24 hours; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} temporarily stopping the transmission of LRIT
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} While a vessel is undergoing repairs, modifications
  or conversions in dry dock or in port or is laid up, the vessel's
  master may, on his or her own initiative, and shall, if directed to do
  so by the Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reduce the frequency of the transmission of LRIT
    information to once every 24 hours; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} temporarily stop the transmission of LRIT
    information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Informing authorities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the master reduces the frequency of or temporarily
  stops the transmission of LRIT information under subsection (2), the
  master shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without delay inform a Marine Communications and
    Traffic Services Centre of the Canadian Coast Guard and, if the
    vessel is in the waters of a contracting government, the relevant
    maritime authority of that government; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an entry, in the record of navigational
    activities and events maintained under section 138 of the
    [Navigation Safety Regulations,
    2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216), indicating the period during
    which the transmission of LRIT information was reduced in frequency
    or temporarily stopped and whether or not the Minister directed the
    action.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure of system

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If the Minister or the
Canadian Coast Guard informs the master of a vessel that any part of the
system used to receive LRIT information from the vessel or to
disseminate the information has failed, the master shall make an entry
in the record of navigational activities and events maintained under
section 138 of the [Navigation Safety Regulations,
2020](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-216) setting out the date and time the
master was informed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electromagnetic interference

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** LRIT equipment shall be
installed so that electromagnetic interference does not affect the
proper functioning of navigational equipment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of proof of type approval or of
certificate

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  application, the Minister shall issue a proof of type approval or a
  certificate for LRIT equipment if the Minister determines that the
  equipment meets the performance standards and functional requirements
  referred to in subsection 6(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Carry proof or certificate on board

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A vessel's master shall ensure that a proof of type
  approval or a certificate issued under subsection (1) for the LRIT
  equipment fitted on the vessel to meet the requirements of section 4
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a proof of type-approval, carried on
    board in the form of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a label that is securely affixed to the equipment
      in a readily visible location, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a document that is kept in a readily accessible
      location; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a certificate, carried on board in a
    readily accessible location.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765487 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration date

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on the day on which they are registered.
