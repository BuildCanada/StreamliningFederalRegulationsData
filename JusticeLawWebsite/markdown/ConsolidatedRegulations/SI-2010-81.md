## Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order {#statistics-canada-census-and-survey-related-term-employment-exclusion-approval-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2010-81
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2010-11-10

Statistics Canada Census and Survey Related Term Employment Exclusion
Approval Order

P.C. 2010-1341 2010-10-28
::::

:::::::::: section
::::::::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Canadian Heritage, pursuant to section 20 of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a){#fn_1a-ID0EBDGA}, hereby approves

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a-ID0EBDGA){#fn_1a}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the re-application of the provisions of that Act to
  the positions excluded pursuant to the Statistics Canada
  Census-Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval Order[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_1b){#fn_1b-ID0EBBBGA} and the persons
  occupying them, and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_1b-ID0EBBBGA){#fn_1b}SI/2010-45
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exclusion from the application of the definitions
  [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[internal appointment
  process]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of paragraphs
  22(2)(a) to (c)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_1c){#fn_1c-ID0EOEAGA}, and sections 40,
  41[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_1d){#fn_1d-ID0EMEAGA}, 48, 51 to
  53[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EKEAGA}, 57, 59
  and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is
  solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform
  data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population
  census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EHEAGA}, or the National
  Household Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EEEAGA} when the survey
  replaces the population census long-form questionnaire and is
  conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the persons
  appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis,
  except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the Statistic
  Act[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EBEAGA}.

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_1c-ID0EOEAGA){#fn_1c}S.C. 2006, c. 9, s.
    100
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_1d-ID0EMEAGA){#fn_1d}S.C. 2006, c. 9, s.
    103
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_1e-ID0EKEAGA){#fn_1e}S.C. 2006, c. 9, s.
    104
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f-ID0EHEAGA){#fn_1f}R.S., c. S-19
    :::

Whereas the Public Service Commission has decided that it is neither
practicable nor in the best interests of the public service to apply
certain provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a){#fn_1a-ID0EKAFA} to positions in Statistics
Canada whose function is solely to provide administrative or management
support or to perform data processing duties for the purpose of
conducting the population census and the agriculture census as described
in the [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EHAFA}, or the National Household
Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics
Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EEAFA} when it replaces the
population census long-form questionnaire and is conducted in
conjunction with the population census and to the persons appointed or
deployed to those positions on a specified term basis, except for other
persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the [Statistics
Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EBAFA};

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 20(2) of the [Public Service
Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a){#fn_1a-ID0EBAEA}, the Public Service
Commission has consulted with the employer;

Therefore, the Public Service Commission, pursuant to section 20 of the
[Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_1a){#fn_1a-ID0EBCDA}, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} reapplies the provisions of that Act to the positions
  excluded pursuant to the Statistics Canada Census-Related Term
  Employment Exclusion Approval Order[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_1b){#fn_1b-ID0EBABDA} and the persons
  occupying them, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} excludes from the application of the definitions
  [[deployment]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[internal appointment
  process]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) and of paragraphs
  22(2)(a) to (c)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_1c){#fn_1c-ID0EOAADA}, and sections 40,
  41[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_1d){#fn_1d-ID0EMAADA}, 48, 51 to
  53[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EKAADA}, 57, 59
  and 62 of that Act to positions in Statistics Canada whose function is
  solely to provide administrative or management support or to perform
  data processing duties for the purpose of conducting the population
  census and the agriculture census as described in the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EHAADA}, or the National
  Household Survey made pursuant to sections 7 and 8 of the [Statistics
  Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EEAADA} when the survey
  replaces the population census long-form questionnaire and is
  conducted in conjunction with the population census and to the persons
  appointed or deployed to those positions on a specified term basis,
  except for other persons mentioned in subsection 5(1) of the
  [Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#fn_1f){#fn_1f-ID0EBAADA}.

This Order may be cited as the [Statistics Canada Census and Survey
Related Term Employment Exclusion Approval
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2010-81).

Ottawa, September 29, 2010
