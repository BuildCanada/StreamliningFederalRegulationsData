## Order Designating Nova Scotia for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-nova-scotia-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2009-177
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2009-06-11

Order Designating Nova Scotia for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest
Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2009-946 2009-06-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas Nova Scotia has legislative measures that protect recipients of
payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost of borrowing
under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of Nova Scotia has
requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the
purposes of section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EEABA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBABA};

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating Nova Scotia for the Purposes
of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal
Code](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-177).

## [Province Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749073 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Nova Scotia is designated
for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-749075 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind660_hq_8213){#fn_Ind660_hq_8213-ID0EBFA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m., Atlantic
Time, on the first day on which all of the following are in force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 18B to 18S and 18U of the Consumer
  Protection Act, R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 92, as enacted by section 2 of An
  Act to Amend Chapter 92 of the Revised Statutes, 1989, the Consumer
  Protection Act, S.N.S. 2006, c. 25;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Payday Lenders Regulations, made pursuant to
  section 18U of the Consumer Protection Act, R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 92, as
  enacted by section 2 of An Act to Amend Chapter 92 of the Revised
  Statutes, 1989, the Consumer Protection Act, S.N.S. 2006, c. 25; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Order dated July 31, 2008 made by the Nova Scotia
  Utility and Review Board under section 18T of the Consumer Protection
  Act, R.S.N.S. 1989, c. 92, as enacted by section 2 of An Act to Amend
  Chapter 92 of the Revised Statutes, 1989, the Consumer Protection Act,
  S.N.S. 2006, c. 25.
