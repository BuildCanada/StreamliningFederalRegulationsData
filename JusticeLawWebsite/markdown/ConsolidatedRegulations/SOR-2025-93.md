## Order Establishing Supplementary Rules Respecting the Sale of Natural Health Products Containing Ephedrine or Pseudoephedrine {#order-establishing-supplementary-rules-respecting-the-sale-of-natural-health-products-containing-ephedrine-or-pseudoephedrine .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2025-93
:::

[FOOD AND DRUGS ACT](/eng/acts/F-27)

Registration 2025-03-07

Order Establishing Supplementary Rules Respecting the Sale of Natural
Health Products Containing Ephedrine or Pseudoephedrine
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Health believes on reasonable grounds that the
use of a therapeutic product, other than the intended use, may present a
risk of injury to health;

Therefore the Minister of Health makes the annexed [Order Establishing
Supplementary Rules Respecting the Sale of Natural Health Products
Containing Ephedrine or Pseudoephedrine](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-93)
under section 30.01‍[[Footnote

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[combination product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[combination product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural health
      product that contains, among its ingredients, ephedrine or any of
      its salts, pseudoephedrine or any of its salts, or any combination
      of those ingredients in combination with one or more other
      substances listed in Schedule 1 of the Regulations. ([produit
      combiné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-combination product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-combination product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a natural
      health product that contains, as its only medicinal ingredients,
      ephedrine or any of its salts, pseudoephedrine or any of its
      salts, or any combination of those ingredients. ([produit non
      combiné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} )

  [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection C.01.001(1) of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870). ([pharmacien]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Natural Health
      Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context requires otherwise, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the
  Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), this Order applies to the sale of combination
  products and non-combination products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale of combination products or non-combination
    products for the purposes of a clinical trial that is subject to
    Part 4 of the Regulations; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the distribution of combination products or
    non-combination products as samples under section 103.4 of the
    Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of Regulations

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
unless otherwise provided for by this Order, the Regulations apply to
combination products and non-combination products, except that the
reference to "these Regulations" in paragraphs 18(1)(a) and 39(1)(a) and
section 92 of the Regulations are to be read as including a reference to
this Order.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- retail sale

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), a person must not sell a combination
  product or non-combination product at the retail level.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- non-combination products

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A pharmacist or a person working under the
  supervision of a pharmacist may sell, at the retail level, a
  non-combination product that is not accessible to the public for
  self-selection.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- combination products
  offered for self-selection

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A pharmacist or any other person working in a retail
  location serviced by a pharmacist may sell, at the retail level, a
  combination product that is accessible to the public for
  self-selection in the case where a pharmacist is available, at the
  request of a purchaser or consumer, to discuss the product prior to
  purchase.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- combination products not
  offered for self-selection

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A pharmacist or a person working under the
  supervision of a pharmacist may sell a combination product at the
  retail level without making it accessible to the public for
  self-selection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- further sale

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A person must not sell a
combination product or non-combination product to another person, other
than a pharmacist, for further sale at the retail level.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on May 18, 2025, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
