## Rules of Procedure for Boards of Review {#rules-of-procedure-for-boards-of-review .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-28
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2003-01-15

Rules of Procedure for Boards of Review
::::

:::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFAEA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on July 27, 2002, a copy of
the proposed [Rules of Procedure for Boards of
Review](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-28), substantially in the annexed
form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments with
respect to the Rules or to file a notice of objection requesting that a
board of review be established and stating the reasons for the
objection;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to section 341 of
the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)^a^,
hereby makes the annexed [Rules of Procedure for Boards of
Review](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-28).

Ottawa, Ontario, January 13, 2003

David Anderson

Minister of the Environment

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFAEA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  33
  :::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703300 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
    Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has filed a
    notice of objection under the Act. ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a board of review established
    under section 333 of the Act. ([commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[interested person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interested person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a government,
    association or person having an interest in the outcome of an
    inquiry for a purpose that is neither frivolous nor vexatious.
    ([personne intéressée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister or Ministers who
    establishes a Board under section 333 of the Act.
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an interested person who has filed an application
      for leave to intervene under section 10, and to whom the Board
      grants the opportunity to appear under section 335 of the Act.
      ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Non-Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703310 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These rules do not apply in
an inquiry to the extent that they are inconsistent with any particular
rules that may be made with respect to that inquiry by the Minister
under section 341 of the Act.

## [Powers of Board to Vary Rules]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703312 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At any
  time during an inquiry, if it is just, fair and in the public interest
  to do so, the Board may vary or dispense with these Rules.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Board varies or dispenses with these Rules,
  the Board must inform the Minister and the parties without delay and
  issue directions concerning the procedures to be followed at the
  inquiry.

## [Quorum]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703316 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A majority of the members
of a Board constitutes a quorum and the absence of a member of the Board
does not impair the right of the quorum to act.

## [Calculation of Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703318 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Any period of time set out
in these Rules or set by the Board in the course of an inquiry, shall be
calculated as a number of consecutive calendar days.

## [Conference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703320 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Board may direct the
Minister and the parties to attend a conference for the purpose of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} clarifying and simplifying issues;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} admitting facts or verifying facts by affidavit;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} discussing the use of documents of a public nature;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} discussing the procedure to be followed at the
  inquiry;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} exchanging written submissions, exhibits and other
  material;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} identifying submissions, documents or testimony that
  contains or may contain confidential information;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} identifying who may be given access to any
  confidential information;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} determining the number of witnesses, or the time for
  presenting evidence, rebutting evidence or cross-examining;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} discussing the use of electronic communication,
  including teleconferences and videoconferences, for the hearing; or

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} dealing with any other relevant matter.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Notice of Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703334 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board shall give written notice of a hearing to the Minister and the
  parties at least 30 days before the commencement of the hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall include the subject matter, time and
  place of the hearing and information on how to present evidence or
  make representations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall state whether all or part of the
  hearing will be conducted orally or in writing.

## [Written Submission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703339 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may require the Minister or a party to file a written submission
  with the Board containing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the facts and other evidence that they
    intend to present; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the names of any witnesses that they intend to call
    during the hearing and a summary of the evidence to be presented by
    each of those witnesses.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister or the party shall file the written
  submission with the Board at least seven days before the hearing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 2
:::

## [Expert Witnesses]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703347 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A party
  who intends to introduce the evidence of an expert witness shall, at
  least 20 days before the hearing begins, serve a report on the
  Minister and the other parties and file the report with the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A party on whom a report described in subsection (1)
  has been served and who wishes to use the evidence of an expert
  witness to rebut a matter set out in the report shall, at least 10
  days before the hearing begins, serve a report of the expert on the
  Minister and the other parties and file the report with the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A report referred to in subsection (1) or (2) shall
  include the qualifications of the expert and a full statement of the
  evidence to be presented.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister intends to introduce the evidence of
  an expert witness, the Minister shall serve on the parties and file
  with the Board the report referred to in subsection (1) or (2) , as
  applicable, within the number of days set out in that subsection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 3(F)
:::

## [Intervenors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703355 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  interested person who intends to intervene in an inquiry shall file
  with the Board and serve on the Minister and every party a written
  application for leave to intervene that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} states whether the person intends to appear at the
    hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} establishes that the person\'s interest justifies
    intervenor status; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} states the issues that the person intends to
    address at the inquiry.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board, in determining whether to grant intervenor
  status and the opportunity to appear before it in accordance with
  section 335 of the Act, shall provide the parties and the Minister the
  opportunity to make representations on whether the person should be
  granted intervenor status and take into consideration any other
  relevant matter, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the inquiry;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issues;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the person has a genuine interest in the
    issues;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the likelihood of the person being able to make a
    useful and different contribution to the Board\'s understanding of
    the issues; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any delay or prejudice that may be caused.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board must notify the Minister and the parties of
  its decision with respect to an application for leave to intervene.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board may direct the Minister or a party to
  provide to an intervenor information or evidence that they provided to
  the Board prior to the filing of the application for leave to
  intervene.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 4(F)
:::

## [Representation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703371 .Part}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister, a party or a witness may be represented at a hearing by
  counsel.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board, on motion, may authorize an agent to
  represent a party or a witness if the agent is competent to properly
  represent or advise the party or witness.

## [Evidence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703375 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board may accept evidence or representations in person, in writing or
  in electronic form, including by teleconference or videoconference.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require a party that has filed evidence
  with the Board to serve copies of that evidence on the other parties
  or the Minister.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require the Minister to serve copies of
  evidence that the Minister has filed with the Board on the parties.

## [Public Nature of Inquiry]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703380 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Except if information or
evidence is treated as confidential for any reason referred to in
paragraphs 52(a), (b) or (c) of the Act or contains personal
information, as defined in section 3 of the [Privacy
Act](/eng/acts/P-21), hearings conducted by the Board shall be public
and information provided to the Board for the purpose of an inquiry
shall be placed on the public record.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 5
:::

## [Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703384 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  document that is required by these Rules to be served or filed may be
  served or filed by hand, mail or any other means in which the
  recipient is capable of receiving it, including electronic format.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person who serves a document electronically shall,
  within seven days after the date of service, provide a paper copy of
  the document to the recipient, unless the recipient waives that right
  in writing.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The date of service of a document is the date that it
  is received by the person being served or their authorized
  representative.

## [Costs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-703389 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  awarding costs under section 338 of the Act, the Board shall consider
  whether any conduct or step taken during the proceedings was improper,
  vexatious or in bad faith.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, failure to comply with these
  Rules or a direction of the Board constitutes improper conduct.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before awarding costs, the Board shall consider the
  seriousness of the misconduct.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 6
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-15-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 15"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Board may direct that
costs be paid to the Minister or a party or that costs be paid by a
party or the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 6
:::

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** If the Board awards
costs, it shall consider the following criteria in fixing the costs:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of additional work required as a result of
  the conduct or steps taken during the proceedings that were improper,
  vexatious or in bad faith;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs incurred for the preparation of written
  submissions required or permitted by the Board and for the presence at
  the hearing of counsel that are necessary as a result of the conduct
  or steps taken during the proceedings that were improper, vexatious or
  in bad faith, with the exception of the costs of travel, accommodation
  and meals;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the reasonableness of the amounts claimed in the bill
  of costs; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the Board directs costs to be paid at the request
  of a person, any conduct of or steps taken by that person during the
  proceedings that were improper, vexatious or in bad faith.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 6
:::

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} After
  the hearing, the Minister or a party may make a written application
  for costs. The application shall be accompanied by a bill of costs and
  supporting receipts and set out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person from whom costs are claimed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reasons for the application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the conduct or steps taken during the proceedings
    by the person from whom costs are claimed that were improper,
    vexatious or in bad faith.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 days after the last day of the hearing, the
  application, accompanied by the bill of costs and receipts, shall be
  served on the person from whom costs are claimed and filed with the
  Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 10 days after service of the application,
  accompanied by the bill of costs and receipts, the person from whom
  costs are claimed and who wishes to contest the application, bill of
  costs or receipts shall serve a response on the person who filed the
  application and file the response with the Board.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-1, s. 6
- SOR/2016-34, s. 1
:::
