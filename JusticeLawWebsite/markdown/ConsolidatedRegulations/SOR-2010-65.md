## Telecommunications Fees Regulations, 2010 {#telecommunications-fees-regulations-2010 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-65
:::

[TELECOMMUNICATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/T-3.4)

Registration 2010-03-17

Telecommunications Fees Regulations, 2010
::::

::::::::: section
:::::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 69(1) of the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_3928_hq_2623){#fn_3928_hq_2623-ID0EFBDA}, a
copy of the proposed [Telecommunications Fees Regulations,
2010](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-65), substantially in the annexed form,
was published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part
I, on November 7, 2009 and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded
to interested persons to make representations to the Canadian
Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission with respect to the
proposed Regulations;

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_3928_hq_2623-ID0EFBDA){#fn_3928_hq_2623}S.C.
  1993, c. 38
  :::

Therefore, the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications
Commission, with the approval of the Treasury Board, pursuant to
subsection 68(1) of the [Telecommunications
Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_3928_hq_2623){#fn_3928_hq_2623-ID0EDACA},
hereby makes the annexed [Telecommunications Fees Regulations,
2010](/eng/regulations/SOR-2010-65).

Gatineau, Quebec, March 16, 2010

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767549 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Canadian telecommunications services revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian telecommunications services
    revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in Part B of
    the Telecom Circular CRTC 2007-15, The Canadian revenue-based
    contribution regime, published on June 8, 2007.([revenus des
    services de télécommunication canadiens]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[contribution-eligible revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[contribution-eligible revenues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    revenues calculated in accordance with the formula set out in Part A
    of the Appendix to the Telecom Information Bulletin CRTC 2019-396,
    entitled The Canadian revenue-based contribution regime, effective 1
    January 2020 and published on December 4, 2019. ([revenus
    admissibles à la contribution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} with respect to telecommunications
    service providers, has the same meaning as [[related
    party]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the International Accounting
    Standard 24 in the CPA Handbook --- Accounting, as amended from time
    to time. ([apparentés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[telecommunications service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[telecommunications service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
    meaning as in section 23 of the [Telecommunications
    Act](/eng/acts/T-3.4). ([service de
    télécommunication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} )

## [Fees and Adjustments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767555 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A telecommunications
service provider shall, in every calendar year and within 30 days after
the date of the invoice sent by the Commission, pay to the Commission
the annual fee calculated in accordance with subsection 3(1), the
supplementary fee calculated in accordance with subsection 3(2) and the
annual adjustment calculated in accordance with subsection 3(5) if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the telecommunications service provider was in
  operation on April 1 of the year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the telecommunications service provider had at least
  \$10 million in Canadian telecommunications services revenues for its
  fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar year or is one of a group
  of related telecommunications service providers which in the aggregate
  had at least \$10 million in Canadian telecommunications services
  revenues for its fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar year.

<!-- -->

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  annual fee shall be the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#767561 .Subsection}
  A/B × C

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the telecommunications service provider's contribution-eligible
      revenues for its fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar
      year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the contribution-eligible revenues for the
      fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar year of the
      telecommunications service providers that are required to pay
      under section 2; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated total regulatory costs of the Commission for the
      fiscal year as set out in the Commission's Expenditure Plan
      published in Part III of the Estimates of the Government of
      Canada.
  :::

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The supplementary fee shall be the amount determined
  by the formula

  ::: {#767567 .Subsection}
  A/B × D

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the telecommunications service provider's contribution-eligible
      revenues for its fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar
      year;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the contribution-eligible revenues for the
      fiscal year ending in the preceding calendar year of the
      telecommunications service providers that are required to pay
      under section 2; and

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the supplementary estimated regulatory costs of the Commission
      for the fiscal year as set out in the Supplementary Estimates of
      the Government of Canada.
  :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulatory costs of the Commission for the fiscal
  year is the sum of the following amounts:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the costs of the Commission's Telecommunications
    Activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the share

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the costs of the Commission's administrative
      activities that is attributable to its Telecommunications
      Activity, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} of the other costs that are taken into account
      to arrive at the net cost of the Commission's program that is
      attributable to its Telecommunications Activity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Commission shall publish, each year, the
  estimated total regulatory costs referred to in subsection (1) and the
  supplementary estimated regulatory costs referred to in subsection (2)
  in a public notice in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/),
  Part I.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The annual adjustment shall be determined by the
  following formula:

  ::: {#767579 .Subsection}
  A/B × E

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the telecommunications service provider's contribution-eligible
      revenues;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of contribution-eligible revenues of the
      telecommunications service providers that are required to pay
      under section 2; and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the difference between the sum of the estimated regulatory
      costs referred to in subsections (1) and (2) and the actual total
      regulatory costs for the fiscal year.
  :::

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The annual adjustment amount shall be charged or
  credited to the telecommunications service provider in the following
  year's invoice and shall not result in a disbursement of monies on the
  part of the Commission.

## [Transitional]{.HTitleText1} {#h-767585 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A telecommunications
service provider who is required to pay an annual adjustment under the
[Telecommunications Fees Regulations, 1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-157)
for the fiscal year ending on March 31, 2010 shall pay that adjustment
within 30 days after the date of the invoice sent by the Commission.
