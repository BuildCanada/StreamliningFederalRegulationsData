## Order Designating the Province of Manitoba for the Purposes of the Definition "applicable guidelines" in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act {#order-designating-the-province-of-manitoba-for-the-purposes-of-the-definition-applicable-guidelines-in-subsection-21-of-the-divorce-act .Title-of-Act}

:::

[DIVORCE ACT](/eng/acts/D-3.4)

Registration 1998-05-14

Order Designating the Province of Manitoba for the Purposes of the
Definition "applicable guidelines" in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act

P.C. 1998-835  1998-05-14

Whereas the Province of Manitoba has established comprehensive
guidelines for the determination of child support that deal with the
matters referred to in section 26.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EECBA} of the [Divorce
Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EBCBA};

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EECBA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1997, c. 1, s. 11
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e-ID0EBCBA){#fnb_e}R.S., c. 3 (2nd Supp.)
  :::

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection
2(5)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fnc_e){#fnc_e-ID0EGBAA} of the
[Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fnb_e){#fnb_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order Designating the Province of Manitoba for the Purposes of the
Definition "applicable guidelines" in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-288).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022283 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The Province of Manitoba is
hereby designated for the purposes of the definition [[applicable
guidelines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Divorce
Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4).

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022285 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 2(5) of the [Divorce Act](/eng/acts/D-3.4), the law that
constitutes the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of Manitoba is
the Child Support Guidelines Regulation, Man. Reg. 58/98.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1022287 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on June 1, 1998.
