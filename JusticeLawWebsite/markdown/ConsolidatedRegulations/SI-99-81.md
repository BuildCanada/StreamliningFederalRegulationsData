## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655105 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission for
    Environmental Cooperation established by Article 8(1) of the North
    American Agreement on Environmental Cooperation between the
    Government of Canada, the Government of the United States of America
    and the Government of the United Mexican States that was concluded
    on September 14, 1993, and that came into force on January 1, 1994.
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tax imposed under Part IX of
    the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([taxe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655109 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to the Director of the Commission of tax in an amount equal to the
amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid by the Director of the Commission during
  the period beginning on September 4, 1994 and ending on September 22,
  1997

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tax that would have been payable by the Director
  of the Commission in respect of the period if the Director of the
  Commission had been granted the exemptions comparable to those
  accorded in Canada under the Vienna Convention to diplomatic agents.
