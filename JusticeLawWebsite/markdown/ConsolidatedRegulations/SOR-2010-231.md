## [Contents of Annual Statement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-765517 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed statements
referred to in subsection 292(2) of the Cooperative Credit Associations
Act are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a balance sheet as at the end of the financial year;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement of income for the financial year;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a statement of cash flows for the financial year;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a statement of changes in equity for the financial
  year; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement of comprehensive income for the financial
  year.
