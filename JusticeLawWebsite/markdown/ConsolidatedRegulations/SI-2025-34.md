## Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to the Minister for International Trade the Control and Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-public-safety-and-emergency-preparedness-to-the-minister-for-international-trade-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-canadian-intergovernmental-conference-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-34
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency
Preparedness to the Minister for International Trade the Control and
Supervision of the Canadian Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat

P.C. 2025-413 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness
to the Minister for International Trade the control and supervision of
that portion of the federal public administration known as the Canadian
Intergovernmental Conference Secretariat.
