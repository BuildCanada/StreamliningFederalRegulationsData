## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807598 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Advisory Speed Tab sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Advisory Speed Tab sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign
      referred to in article 8.2.1 of the Grade Crossings
      Standards. ([panonceau Vitesse recommandée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[average annual daily railway movements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average annual daily railway
      movements]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the number of movements of
      engines, or engines coupled with railway equipment, across a grade
      crossing in a year, divided by the number of days in that
      year. ([moyenne annuelle de mouvements ferroviaires
      quotidiens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[average annual daily traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[average annual daily traffic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      number of motor vehicles that cross a grade crossing in a year,
      divided by the number of days in that year. ([débit journalier
      moyen annuel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[crossing surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[crossing surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a road
      that lies between the ends of a railway tie and that has the width
      shown in Figure 5-1 of the Grade Crossings Standards. ([surface de
      croisement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cross-product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cross-product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the product of the
      average annual daily railway movements and the average annual
      daily traffic. ([produit vectoriel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[design vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[design vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the vehicles
      shown in Figures 1.2.4.1 to 1.2.4.11 of the Geometric Design Guide
      for Canadian Roads, published by the Transportation Association of
      Canada and dated September 1999. ([véhicule type]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Emergency Notification sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Emergency Notification sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign
      referred to in article 8.5 of the Grade Crossings
      Standards. ([panneau Avis d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[existing grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[existing grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grade
      crossing for which actual construction is started before November
      28, 2014. ([passage à niveau existant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a road crossing at
      grade, or two or more road crossings at grade where the lines of
      railway are not separated by more than 30 m. ([passage à
      niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Grade Crossings Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Grade Crossings Standards]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Grade
      Crossings Standards published by the Department of Transport and
      dated January 1, 2019. [(Normes sur les passages à
      niveau)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"}

  [[high-priority grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[high-priority grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      public grade crossing with average annual daily railway movements
      of 10 or more and a railway design speed of 97 km/h (60 mph) or
      more. ([passage à niveau de priorité élevée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[new grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grade crossing
      for which actual construction is started on or after November 28,
      2014. ([nouveau passage à niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Number of Tracks sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Number of Tracks sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign
      referred to in article 8.1.2 of the Grade Crossings Standards and,
      for the purposes of sections 58 and 73, the sign referred to in
      article 4 of Part B of those Standards. ([panneau Nombre de voies
      ferrées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Prepare to Stop at Railway Crossing sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Prepare to Stop at Railway Crossing
      sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign referred to in article
      18.1 of the Grade Crossings Standards. ([panneau Préparez-vous à
      arrêter à un passage à niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[private authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[private authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person, other
      than a road authority, who has a right with respect to a private
      grade crossing. ([autorité privée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[private grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[private grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grade
      crossing that is not a public grade crossing. ([passage à niveau
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[public grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[public grade crossing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a grade
      crossing whose road is opened or maintained by a road authority
      and is designed for public use. ([passage à niveau
      public]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Railway Crossing Ahead sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Railway Crossing Ahead sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign
      referred to in article 8.2.1 of the Grade Crossings
      Standards. ([panneau Signal avancé d'un passage à
      niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Railway Crossing sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Railway Crossing sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign and
      post referred to in article 8.1.1 of the Grade Crossings Standards
      and, for the purposes of sections 58 and 73, the sign referred to
      in article 4 of Part B of those Standards. ([panneau Passage à
      niveau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[railway design speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[railway design speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the railway
      equipment speed that corresponds to the current design of the
      grade crossing. ([vitesse de référence sur la voie
      ferrée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[road approach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[road approach]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the part of a road,
      other than the crossing surface, that lies between the point that
      marks the start of the stopping sight distance and the point that
      marks the front of a design vehicle when it is past the clearance
      point as shown in Figure 10-1 of the Grade Crossings
      Standards. ([abord routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[road crossing design speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[road crossing design speed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the motor
      vehicle speed that corresponds to the current design of the grade
      crossing. ([vitesse de référence au franchissement
      routier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[sightlines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[sightlines]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sightlines referred
      to in sections 20 and 21, as applicable. ([lignes de
      visibilité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Stop Ahead sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Stop Ahead sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign referred to
      in article 8.3.1 of the Grade Crossings Standards. ([panneau
      Signal avancé d'arrêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Stop sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Stop sign]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the sign referred to in
      article 8.4.1 of the Grade Crossings Standards. ([panneau
      Stop]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[stopping sight distance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[stopping sight distance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the distance
      calculated in accordance with article 7.2 of the Grade Crossings
      Standards. ([distance de visibilité d'arrêt]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[storage distance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[storage distance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, on a road that
      crosses a grade crossing, the shortest distance between the rail
      nearest the road approach of the grade crossing and the edge of
      the nearest intersecting road, measured along the centre line of
      the road, as represented by D in Figure 11-1 of the Grade
      Crossings Standards. ([distance de stockage]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[traffic control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[traffic control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Stop sign;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Stop Ahead sign;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a Railway Crossing Ahead sign;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an Advisory Speed Tab sign;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a Prepare to Stop at Railway Crossing sign,
        including the interconnection with the warning system; or

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a traffic signal, including the interconnection
        with the warning system. ([dispositif de contrôle de la
        circulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[warning system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[warning system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an automated system,
      other than an interconnected traffic signal, that indicates the
      approach or presence of railway equipment at a grade crossing and
      that is composed of any combination of light units, bells, gates,
      operating mechanisms and circuits. ([système
      d'avertissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Separate grade crossings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, two adjacent
  and separate roads that are used by motor vehicles and that cross one
  or more lines of railway are considered to be separate grade
  crossings.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807636 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of public grade crossings and private
  grade crossings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Railway company

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), these Regulations do not
  apply in respect of a private grade crossing if the road is opened or
  maintained by a railway company that is the sole private authority at
  the grade crossing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), sections 19 to 96 do not
  apply in respect of the following grade crossings:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a public grade crossing where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the average annual daily railway movements is
      less than three,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the railway design speed is 17 km/h (10 mph) or
      less,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it does not cross more than one track,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the storage distance is 30 m or more, except if,
      at the road approach, access is for the exclusive use of
      pedestrians and non-motor vehicles,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} whistling is required or permitted when railway
      equipment is approaching the grade crossing, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the cross-product is less than 2 000;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a private grade crossing where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the railway design speed is 17 km/h (10 mph) or
      less,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it does not cross more than two tracks, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the cross-product is less than 100;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a private grade crossing where

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the railway design speed for freight trains is
      41 km/h (25 mph) or less,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the railway design speed for passenger trains is
      49 km/h (30 mph) or less,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} it does not cross more than one track,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the storage distance is 30 m or more, except if,
      at the road approach, access is for the exclusive use of
      pedestrians and non-motor vehicles,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the cross-product is less than 100, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} there is no sidewalk; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a grade crossing where the average annual daily
    railway movements is zero.

## [Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807642 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public grade crossing

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise specified in an order of the Agency or in an agreement filed
  with the Agency under subsection 101(1) of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), in the case of a public grade crossing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a railway company must ensure compliance with the
    requirements of these Regulations respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Railway Crossing sign, a Number of Tracks sign
      and an Emergency Notification sign,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance of a Stop sign that is installed
      on the same post as a Railway Crossing sign,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a warning system,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a crossing surface, other than its design, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} sightlines within the railway right-of-way and
      over land adjoining the railway right-of-way, including the
      removal of trees and brush that obstruct the sightlines; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a road authority must ensure compliance with the
    requirements of these Regulations respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the design, construction and maintenance of a
      road approach,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} traffic control devices, except for the
      maintenance of a Stop sign that is installed on the same post as a
      Railway Crossing sign,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the design of a crossing surface, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} sightlines within the land on which the road is
      situated and over land in the vicinity of the grade crossing,
      including the removal of trees and brush that obstruct the
      sightlines.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Private grade crossing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise specified in an order of the Agency
  under section 103 of the [Canada Transportation
  Act](/eng/acts/C-10.4), in the case of a private grade crossing,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a railway company must ensure compliance with the
    requirements of these Regulations respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a Railway Crossing sign, a Number of Tracks sign
      and an Emergency Notification sign,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the maintenance of a Stop sign that is installed
      on the same post as a Railway Crossing sign,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a warning system,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a crossing surface and a road approach within
      the railway right-of-way, including the selection of the road
      crossing design speed and design vehicle, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} sightlines within the railway right-of-way and
      over land adjoining the railway right-of-way --- other than
      sightlines over land owned by a private authority --- including
      the removal of trees and brush that obstruct the sightlines; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a private authority must ensure compliance with the
    requirements of these Regulations respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a road approach outside the railway right-of-way,
      other than the road crossing design speed and design vehicle,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} traffic control devices on land owned by the
      private authority, except for the maintenance of a Stop sign that
      is installed on the same post as a Railway Crossing sign, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} sightlines over land owned by the private
      authority up to the railway right-of-way, including the removal of
      trees and brush that obstruct the sightlines.

## [Information Sharing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-807669 .Part}

### [Railway Company]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807670 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must provide a road authority, in writing, with the
  following information in respect of a public grade crossing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the precise location of the grade crossing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of tracks that cross the grade crossing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the average annual daily railway movements;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the railway design speed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the warning system in place at the grade crossing;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether a Stop sign is installed
    on the same post as the Railway Crossing sign; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether or not whistling is
    required when railway equipment is approaching the grade crossing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information must be provided on receipt of a
  notice under section 3 of the [Notice of Railway Works
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-103).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing --- existing grade crossing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the case of an existing grade crossing, the
  information must be provided not later than the day that is two years
  after November 28, 2014, if a notice under section 3 of the [Notice of
  Railway Works Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-103) has not been
  received within that two-year period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** In the case of a change
referred to in paragraph 28(a) or (b) or section 87, a railway company
must provide a road authority, in writing, not later than 60 days before
the day on which the change begins, with the details of the change and
with the information referred to in subsection 4(1) relating to the
change.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Railway design speed

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
notify a road authority in writing of an increase in the railway design
speed at a public grade crossing not later than 60 days before the day
on which the increase takes effect and must specify in the notice the
precise location of the grade crossing and the new railway design speed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 5 and 6, a
railway company may make a change referred to in those sections at any
time if the road authority has advised the railway company that the
requirements of these Regulations with which the road authority must
comply with respect to the change are met.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average annual daily railway movements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
provide a road authority with the average annual daily railway movements
when that value is three or more and the value increases by 50% or more
relative to the previous value provided to the road authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Whistling

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** If a railway company stops
requiring the use of a whistle at a grade crossing, it must notify the
road authority in writing of that change not later than 30 days after
the day on which the change is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of line of railway

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** If a line of railway at a
public grade crossing is transferred from one railway company to
another, the railway company to which the line of railway is transferred
must, within seven days after the day on which the transfer takes
effect, provide the road authority with the name, address, telephone
number and email address of a contact person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date and contact information

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The information referred
to in sections 4 to 6, 8 and 9 must include the date on which it is
sent, the name and address of the railway company, and the name,
telephone number and email address of the person who provides the
information.

### [Road Authority]{.HTitleText2} {#h-807699 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A road
  authority must provide a railway company, in writing, with the
  following information in respect of a public grade crossing:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the precise location of the grade crossing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number of traffic lanes that cross the crossing
    surface;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the average annual daily traffic;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the road crossing design speed;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the specifications set out in columns A, B and C of
    Table 10-2 of the Grade Crossings Standards to which the road

  railway operations;

  the malfunction, failure or condition.

  those Standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a gate is not indicated as being required in Table
  D-1 of the Grade Crossings Standards, it is nonetheless required if
  the grade crossing corresponds to the applicable specifications set
  out in article 9.2 of those Standards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public grade crossing --- sidewalk, path
or trail

- **[[106]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-106 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  public grade crossing set out in column B of Table D-1 of the Grade
  Crossings Standards and located in an area referred to in section 104
  must be equipped with the warning system that corresponds to the
  number of tracks and the railway design speed set out in that Table,
  and the warning system must meet the applicable standards set out in
  articles 12 to 16 of those Standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Gate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a gate is not indicated as being required in Table
  D-1 of the Grade Crossings Standards, it is nonetheless required if
  the grade crossing corresponds to the applicable specifications set
  out in article 9.6 of those Standards.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guide fencing

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a warning system without a gate is indicated as
  being required in Table D-1 of the Grade Crossings Standards, guide
  fencing must be installed to deter persons from crossing the line of
  railway other than at the grade crossing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Guide fencing and barriers

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a warning system is not indicated as being
  required in column 5 of Table D-1 of the Grade Crossings Standards,
  guide fencing must be installed, as well as a barrier that is intended
  to slow a person's approach to the grade crossing and to encourage a
  person to look both ways before crossing the grade crossing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stop and proceed

**[[107]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-107 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 105
and 106, if railway equipment must stop before proceeding across a
public grade crossing that is located in an area referred to in section
104 and that is used by motor vehicles,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a warning system with flashing lights and bells must
  be installed at the grade crossing and must meet the applicable
  standards set out in articles 12 to 16 of the Grade Crossings
  Standards; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the railway company must manually protect the grade
  crossing.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-808132 .Part}

### [Information Sharing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808133 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Railway company

**[[108]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-108 .sectionLabel}** A railway company must
keep the most recent information provided to a road authority under
sections 4 to 11 and the most recent information received from a road
authority under sections 12 to 18.

### [Inspection, Testing and Maintenance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808136 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content

- **[[109]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-109 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  the day on which a railway company inspects, tests or maintains a
  warning system, it must record the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the person who conducts the
    inspection, testing or maintenance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date of the inspection, testing or maintenance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the precise location of the warning system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the inspection, testing or
    maintenance;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a description of the inspection, testing or
    maintenance that is conducted;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of any malfunction or failure of a
    component of the warning system; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} an indication of any deviation from the Grade
    Crossings Standards and the action taken to remedy it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Integrity of record

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must not be changed once it has been
  created.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The record must be kept for two years after the day
  on which it is created. However, if the Grade Crossings Standards
  specify an interval of two or more years between each inspection, each
  test or each maintenance activity, the record of the two latest
  inspections, tests or maintenance activities must be kept.

### [Temporary Protection Measures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-808151 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Malfunction, failure or condition

- **[[110]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-110 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  railway company must keep a record of any warning system malfunction
  or failure, or any condition that exists that may cause a malfunction
  or failure, of which it has been advised or has become aware under
  section 103 --- even if the existence of the malfunction, failure or
  condition is not confirmed --- and the record must contain the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature of the malfunction, failure or
    condition;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the precise location of the grade crossing;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that the railway company was
    advised or became aware of the malfunction, failure or condition;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all of the measures taken by the railway company to
    address any threat to, or interference with, the safety of railway
    operations;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that a representative of the
    railway company arrived at the grade crossing to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} take the measures referred to in paragraph (d),
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} remedy the malfunction, failure or condition;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} all the measures taken by the railway company to
    restore the grade crossing to use or to remedy the malfunction,
    failure or condition, or the reason why no remedial action was
    taken, if applicable; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that the grade crossing was
    restored to use or the malfunction, failure or condition was
    remedied.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The record must be kept for two years after the day
  on which the railway company was advised or became aware of the
  malfunction, failure or condition.
