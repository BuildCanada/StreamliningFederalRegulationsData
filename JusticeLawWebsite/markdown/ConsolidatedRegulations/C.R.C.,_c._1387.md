## Regional Development Incentives Designated Regions Order, 1974 {#regional-development-incentives-designated-regions-order-1974 .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1387
:::

[REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT INCENTIVES ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.9)

Order Designating Regions for the Purposes of the Regional Development
Incentives Act
::::

:::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508468 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Regional Development Incentives Designated Regions Order,
1974](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1387).

## [Designation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508470 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The regions set out in the
schedule are hereby designated as designated regions for the purposes of
the [Regional Development Incentives Act](/eng/acts/R-4.9) until
December 31, 1984.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-858, s. 1
- SOR/81-805, s. 1
- SOR/82-481, s. 1
:::
