[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2017-170

Registration 2017-09-01

Regulations Specifying Territories and Indicating International
Registers

P.C. 2017-1119 2017-08-31
::::

:::::::::::::::: section

the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraphs 7(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_2-197e_hq_17211){#fn_2-197e_hq_17211-ID0EICAA}
and (b)[[Footnote

]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_2-197e_hq_12052){#fn_2-197e_hq_12052-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Regulations Specifying Territories and Indicating
International Registers](/eng/regulations/SOR-2017-170).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Territories in Schedule 1

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The territories set out in
Schedule 1 are not referred to in the definition [[territory of the
European Union]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the
[Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International registers

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The registers set out in
Schedule 2 are international registers for the purpose of paragraph
3(2.2)(c) of the [Coasting Trade Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3).
