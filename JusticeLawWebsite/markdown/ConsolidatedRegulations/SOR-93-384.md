## Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United States and Families Privileges and Immunities Order {#administrative-and-technical-staff-of-the-embassy-of-the-united-states-and-families-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/93-384
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1993-07-16

Order Extending the Privileges and Immunities of Members of the
Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United States,
and Members of their Families, in Canada
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to subsection 4(1)
of the Foreign Missions and International Organizations Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDCDA}, hereby
revokes the Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the
United States of America Privileges and Immunities Order[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBCDA}, made on
December 15, 1992, and makes the annexed Order extending the privileges
and immunities of members of the administrative and technical staff of
the Embassy of the United States, and members of their families, in
Canada, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDCDA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 41
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EBCDA){#footnote2_e}Not
  published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II
  :::

Ottawa, July 14, 1993

PERRIN BEATTY

*Secretary of State for External Affairs*
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953630 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Administrative and Technical Staff of the Embassy of the United
States and Families Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-384).

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-953632 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the privileges and immunities accorded to
  diplomatic agents under section 3 of the [Foreign Missions and
  International Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4), other than duty
  and tax relief privileges, shall extend to the members of the
  administrative and technical staff of the Embassy of the United
  States, and to the members of their families forming part of their
  household, in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The privileges and immunities referred to in
  subsection (1) shall not extend to the members of the administrative
  and technical staff, or to the members of their families forming part
  of their households, who are nationals or permanent residents of
  Canada.
