## Order Transferring to the Minister of Finance the Powers, Duties and Functions of the Minister of Supply and Services under Part I of the Currency Act {#order-transferring-to-the-minister-of-finance-the-powers-duties-and-functions-of-the-minister-of-supply-and-services-under-part-i-of-the-currency-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-122
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1988-08-03

P.C. 1988-1537 1988-07-21
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 2 of the Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act, is pleased hereby to transfer
to the Minister of Finance the powers, duties and functions of the
Minister of Supply and Services under Part I of the Currency Act as
transferred by Order in Council P.C. 1976-2857 of November 18, 1976.
