## Accountable Advances Regulations {#accountable-advances-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-438
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-04-14

Regulations Respecting the Issuing of, Accounting for, Repayment and
Recovery of Accountable Advances

T.B. 801447 1986-04-10
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to section 31[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EHBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby revokes the
[Accountable Advances Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-438), C.R.C.,
c. 668 and makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the issuing of,
accounting for, repayment and recovery of accountable
advances](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-438) in substitution therefor.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EHBAA){#footnote_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 9
  :::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896997 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Accountable Advances
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-438).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-896999 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[accountable advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accountable advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a sum of money advanced to a person from an
      appropriation, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a sum of money advanced to a person from the sum
      of money described in paragraph (a)

    for which the person is accountable and includes imprest funds and
    working capital advances administered under an imprest system;
    ([avance comptable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[appropriation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appropriation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a revolving fund;
    ([crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cash]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cash]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means bank notes and coins;
    ([espèces]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[change fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[change fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cash provided to a cashier
    or other individual for the purpose of making change; ([fonds
    d'appoint]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collective agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in the [Public Service Staff Relations Act](/eng/acts/P-35);
    ([convention collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an account with a
    supplier that is funded by an accountable advance and from which the
    supplier deducts the value of material as it is shipped; ([compte de
    dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deputy head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
    [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01);
    ([sous-chef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual appointed to a
    position in a department or other portion of the public service of
    Canada listed in Schedule I to the [Public Service Staff Relations
    Act](/eng/acts/P-35); ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[holder or custodian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[holder or custodian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person to whom
    an accountable advance has been issued or who is responsible for an
    accountable advance; ([détenteur ou dépositaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a corporation,
    an organization and any branch or division of a department;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petty cash expenditure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petty cash expenditure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a disbursement
    made from a petty cash fund; ([dépense de petite
    caisse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petty cash fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petty cash fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means cash kept on hand
    under an imprest system for the purpose of making small payments in
    cash; ([fonds de petite caisse]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[standing advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[standing advance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an accountable
    advance issued in a specified amount for an indeterminate period and
    replenished to that specific amount each time an accounting for
    expenditures is made. ([avance permanente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897017 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** An individual to whom an
accountable advance is issued is personally responsible and accountable
for that advance and any loss or shortage in respect of that advance may
be recovered from that individual.

## [Issuing of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897019 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A deputy head may issue an
accountable advance

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a person

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} as a travel, relocation or posting advance where
    that person is entitled to such an advance pursuant to a contract, a
    collective agreement or a Treasury Board directive,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of establishing a deposit account
    where a contract or the customary terms of trade require prepayment
    for goods or services,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of establishing a change fund,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of establishing a petty cash or
    other imprest fund,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of funding a departmental bank
    account established pursuant to Part III of the Cheque Issue
    Regulations, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} for any other purpose where payment has to be made
    by that person on the receipt of goods or on the rendering of a
    service and the usual payment facilities are not immediately
    available or their use would be impractical; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to an employee as an emergency salary advance where
  the issuing of such an advance is otherwise authorized or required by
  statute, by a collective agreement or by a Treasury Board directive.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-258, s. 2(F)
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An accountable advance
issued under these Regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 4(a)(i) or (vi) shall not be
  issued as a standing advance unless the person to whom the advance is
  issued is required to incur expenditures or make payments or
  disbursements in respect thereof on a continuing basis;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall not be issued for any type of payment owing on
  the termination of employment;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a specific purpose shall not exceed the amount
  required to cover the expenditures that can reasonably be expected to
  be made for that purpose;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} as a standing advance shall not exceed the amount
  required for expenditures expected to be made from the advance within
  a fixed period determined on the basis of operational requirements and
  the turnover of the advance; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of establishing a petty cash fund
  shall not exceed \$2000 and no single expenditure from that fund shall
  exceed \$200 without the prior approval of the Treasury Board.

## [Accounting for Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897037 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  deputy head shall establish and maintain records of all accountable
  advances issued by him and all expenditures made therefrom.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records referred to in subsection (1) shall
  include a separate account receivable for every accountable advance
  and those accounts receivable shall be controlled by a control account
  maintained within the principal accounting system of the department.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every standing advance shall be accounted for by the
  holder or custodian thereof not later than 10 working days after the
  end of any month in which expenditures are incurred or, where a
  standing advance has been used to establish a deposit account required
  by contract, in accordance with the terms of that contract, and every
  other accountable advance shall be accounted for not later than 10
  working days after the purpose for which the advance was made is
  fulfilled

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an accountable advance issued for
    the purpose of establishing a petty cash or other imprest fund, by
    showing that the cash on hand or bank balance plus receipts or paid
    vouchers equals the amount of the advance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, by submitting receipts or
    vouchers covering the expenditures made from the advance where proof
    of payment is required for those expenditures under a Treasury Board
    directive.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The deputy head shall at the end of every fiscal year

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} require every holder or custodian of a standing
    advance to provide, within 30 days after the end of the fiscal year,
    written confirmation of the amount of the advance and his continuing
    responsibility therefor; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide to the Receiver General such reports and
    certificates in respect of all accountable advances as may be
    required for the purposes of the Public Accounts.

## [Repayment and Recovery of Advances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897047 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  unexpended balance of any accountable advance, other than a standing
  advance, shall be repaid to the department by the holder or custodian
  thereof not later than 10 working days after the purpose for which the
  advance was made has been fulfilled, unless the Treasury Board has
  authorized recovery by deduction from salary or wages or by deduction
  from a subsequent advance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1), where the deputy head
  has issued an accountable advance, he may at any time, by notice in
  writing to the holder or custodian of the advance, demand an
  accounting thereof and repayment of any unexpended balance and the
  holder or custodian shall, not later than 30 days after receiving such
  notice, provide an accounting and make the repayment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-258, s. 2(F)
:::

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  accountable advance or any part thereof that is required to be repaid
  shall be repaid in full by the holder or custodian thereof in a single
  payment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An accountable advance or any part thereof that is to
  be recovered by a department shall, wherever possible, be recovered in
  full in a single payment.
