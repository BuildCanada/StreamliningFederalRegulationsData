The National Energy Board, pursuant to subsection 48(2) of the [National
Energy Board Act](/eng/acts/N-7), hereby makes the annexed [National

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-39).

Board.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707337 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[abandon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[abandon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to permanently cease operation
    such that the cessation results in the discontinuance of service.
    ([cessation d'exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Commission referred to
    in subsection 26(1) of the Act. ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[construction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes clearing or grading.
    ([construction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deactivate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deactivate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to remove temporarily from
    service. ([désactivation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[decommission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[decommission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to permanently cease
    operation such that the cessation does not result in the
    discontinuance of service. ([désaffectation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[environment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the components of the
    Earth and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} air, land and water;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all layers of the atmosphere;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all organic and inorganic matter and living
      organisms; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the interacting natural systems that include
      components referred to in paragraphs (a) to (c).
      ([environnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an occurrence that results or
    could result in a significant adverse effect on property, the
    environment or the safety of persons. ([incident]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[operate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes repair, maintain,
    deactivate, reactivate and decommission.
    ([exploitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pressure piping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pressure piping]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an assembly of pipes,
    pipe fittings, valves, safety devices, pumps, compressors and other
    equipment that is designed for the transport of fluids and is
    connected to a boiler or pressure vessel. ([tuyauterie sous
    pression]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pressure vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pressure vessel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a vessel that is
    designed for the containment of fluids at internal gauge pressures
    of more than 103 kPa, has an internal volume of more than 42.5 L and
    has an internal diameter of more than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 152 mm, if it is not in water service; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 610 mm, if it is in water service. ([appareil
      sous pression]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a plant used for the
    processing, extraction or conversion of fluids and all structures
    located within the boundaries of the plant, including compressors
    and other structures integral to the transportation of fluids.
    ([usine de traitement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[release]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes discharge, spray, spill,
    leak, seep, pour, emit, dump and exhaust. ([rejet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707359 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of hydrocarbon processing plants that
  fall within the definition of "pipeline" in section 2 of the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} well-site facilities; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} field facilities.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The duties imposed on a
company under these Regulations are duties in respect of a processing
plant that is designed, constructed, operated or abandoned by the
company.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707366 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall ensure that its processing plant is designed,
  constructed, operated or abandoned in accordance with the provisions
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the [Canada Occupational Health and Safety
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-304).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If there is an inconsistency between these
  Regulations and the [Canada Occupational Health and Safety
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-304), the latter Regulations
  prevail to the extent of that inconsistency.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A company shall develop,
implement and maintain quality control and quality assurance programs in
respect of the design, construction, operation and abandonment of its
processing plant.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A company shall conduct a

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that its employees at its processing plant have the knowledge and
training appropriate to the tasks to which they are assigned.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A company shall develop
and implement a safety program to anticipate, prevent, manage and
mitigate potentially dangerous conditions and exposure to those
conditions during all construction, operations and emergency activities.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A company shall develop
and implement an environmental protection program to anticipate,
prevent, manage and mitigate conditions that have a potential to affect
the environment significantly and adversely.

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company contracts for the provision of services in respect of the
  construction, operation or abandonment of its processing plant, the
  company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the contractor of all conditions or features
    that are special to the construction, operation or abandonment;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform the contractor of all special safety
    practices and procedures to be followed as a result of those
    conditions or features;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} take all reasonable steps to ensure that the
    construction, operation or abandonment activities are conducted in
    accordance with the manuals developed under sections 27 and 30; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} authorize a person to halt a construction,
    operation or abandonment activity in circumstances where, in the
    person's judgment, the construction, operation or abandonment
    activity is not being conducted in accordance with the manuals
    developed under sections 27 and 30 or is creating a hazard to the
    safety of anyone at the construction, operation or abandonment site.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company shall ensure that the person it
  authorizes under paragraph (1)(d) has sufficient expertise, knowledge
  and training to carry out competently the obligations set out in that
  paragraph.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Design]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707403 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A company shall develop,
implement and maintain detailed designs of its processing plant.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that its processing plant is equipped with a source of emergency power.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that each tank, bullet, sphere or other container that contains any
fluid, other than fresh water, is designed, constructed and maintained
to restrict and contain fluids and to minimize the risk to the safety of
persons and to the environment in the event of an unintentional release.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that equipment that has a source of ignition with which gas at explosive
levels may come into contact is not located in the same building as any
process vessel or other source of flammable vapour, unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} air intake flues are located outside the building in
  an area where gas is unlikely to be present;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} relief valves, burst plates and other sources of
  flammable fluids are vented from the building or discharged to a flare
  header, or other location that is environmentally safe;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a specific risk analysis is conducted to determine
  what active, reactive or passive safety devices should be installed on
  that equipment and the company installs those devices; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the building is cross-ventilated.

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that all process vessels and equipment from which any flammable fluid or
toxic substance may be released are safely vented to a flare header or
to other locations where the protection of the environment and the
safety of persons are maintained.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that all flare headers are equipped with a means by which the flame from
the flare header is prevented from entering into piping or a vessel from
which the flammable vapour is being released.

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that hydrocarbon storage vessels or buildings used for the processing of
hydrocarbons are equipped with reliable fire suppression systems
appropriate to the risk that the vessels or buildings pose to the safety
of persons or to the environment if the vessels or buildings catch fire
or come into contact with fire.

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** A company shall equip its
processing plant with systems that are appropriate to its buildings or
structures and that are designed for the detection of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} explosive and flammable gases;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} toxic or noxious gases; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} fire, the products of combustion or temperature rise.

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A company shall equip its
processing plant with alarm devices that are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} located where they can be heard or seen from all
  locations within the plant; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designed in a manner that will allow a timely warning
  of danger to be given to persons in the plant or in the vicinity of
  the plant in order to permit safe evacuation or actions to control the
  danger.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that all pressure-relief piping and systems are designed and constructed
so that an emergency pressure release does not create a detriment to
property or to the environment or a hazard to the safety of persons.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Construction]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707423 .Part}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** A company shall, during
the construction of its processing plant, take all reasonable steps to
ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction activities at its processing plant
  do not create a detriment to property or to the environment or a
  hazard to the safety of persons that is greater than the detriment or
  hazard normally associated with identical activities carried on
  elsewhere in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons at the construction site are informed of the
  practices and procedures that are to be followed for their safety and
  for the protection of the environment; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} persons on the work site are made fully aware of any
  escalation of risk when, during the final phases of construction,
  equipment testing and acceptance procedures begin at the processing
  plant.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Testing and Examination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707433 .Part}

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall ensure that each pressure test conducted at its
  processing plant is performed under its direct supervision or the
  direct supervision of its agent or mandatary.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall ensure that the agent or mandatary
  referred to in subsection (1) is independent of any contractor that
  conducts the pressure test or any contractor that participated in the
  fabrication of the work to be tested.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company, or the agent or mandatary who supervised
  the test, shall prepare, date and sign a record of the test.

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall develop and implement a program for the non-destructive
  examination of welded joints for the purpose of determining their
  suitability and shall ensure that the program meets the requirements

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Operation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707446 .Part}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop, implement and regularly review and update
    operations manuals that provide information and procedures to
    promote safety and environmental protection in the operation of its
    processing plant,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep a current copy of the operations manuals or
    the relevant parts of them at its processing plant, in a location
    where they are readily accessible to every person engaged in
    operations at the plant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall ensure that the operations manuals
  contain safe-work procedures for all tasks that present risks to the
  safety of persons or that may be detrimental to the environment.

<!-- -->

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, "safe-work permit" means a written authorization granted
  by a company that permits an activity to be carried on in or around
  its processing plant, subject to any conditions on the authorization.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A company shall develop and implement a safe-work
  permit system to manage and regulate all work at its processing plant.

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A company shall ensure
that its processing plant is staffed at all times with at least the
number of employees set out in the report referred to in section 50.

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** A company shall take all
reasonable steps to ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintenance activities at its processing plant do not
  create a detriment to the environment or a hazard to the safety of
  persons that is greater than the detriment or hazard normally
  associated with identical activities carried on elsewhere in Canada;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons at a maintenance site are informed of the
  practices and procedures that are to be followed for their safety and
  for the protection of the environment.

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** A company shall take all

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** A company shall develop

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** A company shall

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if practical, clearly mark the open and closed
  positions of main emergency shutdown valves;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} post along the boundaries of its processing plant
  signs indicating the name of the company and the telephone number to
  call in the event of an emergency at the processing plant; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} post signs warning of potential hazards.

- **[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  company proposes to deactivate a processing plant or part of one for

- **[[49]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-49 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  this section, [[suspend]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to interrupt the
  operation of the whole or a portion of a processing plant due to an
  unforeseen event or to conduct scheduled maintenance, repairs or
  replacements.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A company shall, as soon as practicable, notify the
  Regulator and the appropriate authorities of the province in which its
  processing plant is located of any decision made by the company to
  suspend

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the whole plant for a period
    exceeding 24 hours; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operation of any portion of the plant for a
    period exceeding seven days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In addition to the notification under subsection
  (1.1), the company shall provide the following information to the
  Regulator and to the appropriate authorities of the province in which
  its processing plant is located:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of the operations to be suspended;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the suspension;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the duration of the suspension; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the effect of the suspension on the throughput of
    the plant, on the safety of persons or on the environment.

## [PART 7]{.HLabel1}[Audits, Inspections and Record Retention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707528 .Part}

- **[[52]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-52 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  company shall conduct audits and inspections on a regular basis to
  ensure that its processing plant is designed, constructed, operated or
  abandoned in compliance with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} Parts 2 and 3 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} Part 6 of the Act as it relates to the safety and
    security of persons and for the protection of property and the
    environment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} these Regulations; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of any certificate or
    order issued by the Commission, as they relate to the safety and
    security of persons and for the protection of property and the
    environment.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company shall ensure that an audit sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all cases of non-compliance that have been noted;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any corrective action taken or planned to be taken.

- **[[53]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-53 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When a
  company constructs, operates or abandons a processing plant, the
  company or its agent or mandatary shall inspect the construction,
  operation or abandonment to ensure that the requirements of these
  Regulations are met and that the terms and conditions of any
  certificate or order issued by the Commission are complied with.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The company shall ensure that an inspection is
  performed by a person who has sufficient expertise, knowledge and
  training to perform the inspection competently.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The company shall ensure that its agent or mandatary
  is independent of any contractor hired to construct, operate or
  abandon the processing plant.
