## Order Transferring to the Department of External Affairs the Control and Supervision of the Investment Development Division in Investment Canada {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-external-affairs-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-investment-development-division-in-investment-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-146
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Transferring to the Department of External Affairs the Control and
Supervision of the Investment Development Division in Investment Canada

P.C. 1993-1492  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Department of External Affairs the control and
supervision of that portion of the public service in Investment Canada
known as the Investment Development Division.
