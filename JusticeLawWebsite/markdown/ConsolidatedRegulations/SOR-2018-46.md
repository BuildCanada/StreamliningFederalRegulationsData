## Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2018) {#order-declaring-an-amnesty-period-2018 .Title-of-Act}

Order Declaring an Amnesty Period (2018)

P.C. 2018-301 2018-03-20

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[firearm]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any of the following prohibited
firearms:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Classic Green Sniper rifle;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Ver rifle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Aestas rifle;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Autumnus rifle; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a SAN Swiss Arms Model Hiemis rifle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amnesty period set out in subsection (3) is declared under section
  117.14 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) for a person who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day before the day on which this Order is
    registered, possessed a firearm and held a licence that was issued
    under the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the amnesty period, continues to hold a
    licence while in possession of the firearm.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of the amnesty period is to permit the
  person to do any of the following during that period:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} possess the firearm;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver the firearm to a peace officer, firearms
    officer or chief firearms officer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell or give the firearm to a business -- including
    a museum -- authorized to acquire and possess prohibited firearms;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} transport the firearm for the purposes of
    paragraph (b) or (c).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amnesty period

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amnesty period begins on the day on which this
  Order is registered and ends on February 28, 2021.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
