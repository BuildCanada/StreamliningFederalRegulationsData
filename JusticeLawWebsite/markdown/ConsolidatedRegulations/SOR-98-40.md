## Order Designating Certain Countries and Military Service Agencies for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00 {#order-designating-certain-countries-and-military-service-agencies-for-the-purposes-of-tariff-item-no.-9810.00.00 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-40
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Order Designating Certain Countries and Military Service Agencies for
the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00

P.C. 1997-2011  1997-12-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 132(1)(g) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order Designating Certain Countries and Military Service Agencies for
the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-40).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA){#fn_ae}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following military
service agencies are designated for the purposes of tariff item No.
9810.00.00 of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} German Air Force Tactical Training Command, Goose
  Bay, Labrador, Newfoundland

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Post Exchanges - AC & W Sites authorized by the
  Minister of National Revenue

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Royal Air Force Detachment, Goose Bay, Labrador,
  Newfoundland

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} United States Military Services Agencies at Goose Bay
  Airport, Labrador, Newfoundland

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} United States Property Accountability Office,
  Headquarters ADC Detachment No. 1, Ottawa, Ontario

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023178 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on January 1, 1998.
