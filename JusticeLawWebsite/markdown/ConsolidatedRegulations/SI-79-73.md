## Canadian Exploration Expense Remission Order {#canadian-exploration-expense-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/79-73
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1979-04-11

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Tax in Respect of Canadian
Exploration Expense Incurred by Taxpayers that Are Not
Principal-Business Corporations

P.C. 1979-1100 1979-03-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed Order respecting the remission of income tax in
respect of Canadian exploration expense incurred by taxpayers that are
not principal-business corporations.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634685 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Canadian Exploration Expense Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-79-73).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634687 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[taxation year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[taxation year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any taxation year ending
    after May 25, 1976. ([année d'imposition]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-634691 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to any taxpayer who was liable for tax under Part I of the Act in a
taxation year of an amount that is equal to the amount, if any, by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of tax payable under the Act for the year

exceeds

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of tax that would be payable under the Act
  for the year if, in its application to that taxation year,
  subparagraph 66.1(3)(a)(i) of the Act read as follows:

  > ::: ReadAsText
  > - ["(i)]{.lawlabel} the amount by which his Canadian exploration
  >   expense incurred after May 25, 1976 and before 1980 exceeds the
  >   aggregate of all amounts claimed by virtue of this paragraph in a
  >   previous taxation year, and".
  > :::
