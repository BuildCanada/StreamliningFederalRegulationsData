## Electronic Alternatives Regulations for the Purposes of Subsection 254(1) of the Canada Labour Code {#electronic-alternatives-regulations-for-the-purposes-of-subsection-2541-of-the-canada-labour-code .Title-of-Act}

254(1) of the Canada Labour Code

Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2) in accordance with the definition

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742863 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    166 of the [Canada Labour
    Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pay statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pay statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the statement that an
    employer shall furnish to an employee in accordance with subsection
    254(1) of the [Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2). ([bulletin de
    paie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Electronic Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742868 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
application of section 41 of the [Personal Information Protection and
Electronic Documents Act](/eng/acts/P-8.6) and the application of that
section to subsection 254(1) of the[Canada Labour Code](/eng/acts/L-2),
in order to satisfy the requirement that a pay statement shall be in
writing, an electronic document must satisfy the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the document must be provided to the employee by
  making it available only to the employee through an electronic source,
  such as a web site, that is accessible to the employee and whose
  location is made known to the employee;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a period of at least three years from the day on
  which the document is first provided to the employee, the document
  must be readable and printable on a computer and printer to which the
  employer shall provide the employee with private access.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pay statement considered furnished to the
employee

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A pay statement is
considered to be furnished to the employee when the electronic document
is provided to the employee in accordance with paragraph 2(a) and the
conditions set out in paragraph 2(b) are satisfied.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742875 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
