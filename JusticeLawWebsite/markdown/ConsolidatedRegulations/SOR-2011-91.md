## Deposit Out of the Normal Course of Events Notification Regulations {#deposit-out-of-the-normal-course-of-events-notification-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2011-91
:::

[FISHERIES ACT](/eng/acts/F-14)

Registration 2011-03-25

Deposit Out of the Normal Course of Events Notification Regulations

P.C. 2011-455 2011-03-25
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to paragraph 38(9)(a) of
the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Deposit Out of the Normal Course of Events Notification
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-91).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae-ID0EDBAA){#fn_ae}R.S., c. F-14
  :::
:::

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 38(4) of the [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14),
  the following persons are prescribed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} those providing 24-hour emergency telephone service
    provided by the office set out in column 2 of the schedule for the
    province, set out in column 1, where the occurrence of the deposit
    of a deleterious substance out of the normal course of events or the
    serious and imminent danger of such a deposit takes place; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pollution prevention officer referred to in
    paragraph 5(8)(a) or subsection 5(9) of the [Pollutant Discharge
    Reporting Regulations, 1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-351).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any person required by subsection 38(4) of the
  [Fisheries Act](/eng/acts/F-14) to report the occurrence of a deposit
  of a deleterious substance out of the normal course of events or the
  serious and imminent danger of such a deposit shall without delay
  notify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in all cases other than those mentioned in
    paragraph (b), an inspector or a person referred to in paragraph
    (1)(a) at the applicable telephone number set out in column 3 of the
    schedule; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the master of a vessel, the owner of
    a vessel or the operator of an oil handling facility to whom the
    [Pollutant Discharge Reporting Regulations,
    1995](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-351) apply, an inspector or the person
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b).

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
