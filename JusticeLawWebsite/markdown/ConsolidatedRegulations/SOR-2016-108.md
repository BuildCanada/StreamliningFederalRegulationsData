## [List]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826552 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The list set out in the
schedule is established for the purposes of the definition
[[spill-treating agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Canada
Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7).

## [Coming into force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826554 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
