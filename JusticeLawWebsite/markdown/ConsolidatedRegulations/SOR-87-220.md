Regulations Prescribing the Terms and Conditions of the Loan to Ford New
Holland, Inc.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904866 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ford New Holland, Inc. Loan
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-220).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904868 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[FNH]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[FNH]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Ford New Holland, Inc., or any of
    its subsidiaries or affiliates; ([FNH]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Winnipeg Facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Winnipeg Facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the farm equipment
    business located at Winnipeg, Manitoba of Versatile Corporation, a
    company continued under the Canadian Business Corporations Act, with
    its principal place of business at Vancouver, British Columbia.
    ([établissement de Winnipeg]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Loan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904873 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The loan made by the
Minister to FNH to promote the growth and efficiency of FNH by allowing
FNH to acquire the Winnipeg Facility and to install a paint system at
that Facility shall be subject to the terms and conditions set out in
sections 4 to 7.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-267, s. 1(E)
:::

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904877 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The loan referred to in
section 3 shall be for an amount not exceeding \$45,500,000.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The loan referred to in
section 3 is repayable as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a repayment of \$9,300,000, less any and all monthly
  amounts in the amount of \$265,000 paid on or after October 30, 2007,
  shall be made no later than July 31, 2008;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Buhler Industries Inc. shall provide the Minister of
  Industry, by no later than July 31, 2008, with valid and enforceable
  security in a supplemental amount of \$5,000,000, which security shall
  be in effect until October 21, 2017;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an immediate repayment of \$5,000,000 is due in the
  event that the tractor factory of Buhler Industries Inc. moves or
  ceases its operations in Canada prior to October 22, 2017 and, if not
  paid, the security held by the Minister of Industry will be realized
  on to obtain that payment; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the loan agreement is extended to October 22, 2017.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-267, s. 2
- SOR/2008-53, s. 1
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The loan referred to in
section 3 is made on condition that FNH has entered into an Asset
Purchase Agreement for the Winnipeg Facility.
