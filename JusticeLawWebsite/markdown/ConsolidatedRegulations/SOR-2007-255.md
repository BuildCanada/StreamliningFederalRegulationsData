## Eligible Financial Contract Regulations (Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act) {#eligible-financial-contract-regulations-canada-deposit-insurance-corporation-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2007-255
:::

[CANADA DEPOSIT INSURANCE CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-3)

Registration 2007-11-15

Eligible Financial Contract Regulations (Canada Deposit Insurance
Corporation Act)

P.C. 2007-1730 2007-11-15
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 39.15(8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610301-e_hq_2945){#fn_610301-e_hq_2945-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), hereby
makes the annexed [Eligible Financial Contract Regulations (Canada
Deposit Insurance Corporation Act)](/eng/regulations/SOR-2007-255).

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[derivatives agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[derivatives agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a financial
    agreement whose obligations are derived from, referenced to, or
    based on, one or more underlying reference items such as interest
    rates, indices, currencies, commodities, securities or other
    ownership interests, credit or guarantee obligations, debt
    securities, climatic variables, bandwidth, freight rates, emission
    rights, real property indices and inflation or other macroeconomic
    data and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a contract for differences or a swap, including a
      total return swap, price return swap, default swap or basis swap;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a futures agreement;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a cap, collar, floor or spread;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an option; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a spot or forward. ([contrat
      dérivé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[financial intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[financial intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a clearing agency; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person, including a broker, bank or trust
      company, that in the ordinary course of business maintains
      securities accounts or futures accounts for
      others. ([intermédiaire financier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following kinds of
financial agreements are prescribed for the purpose of the definition
"eligible financial contract" in subsection 39.15(9) of the [Canada
Deposit Insurance Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a derivatives agreement, whether settled by payment
  or delivery, that

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} trades on a futures or options exchange or board,
    or other regulated market, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is the subject of recurrent dealings in the
    derivatives markets or in the over-the-counter securities or
    commodities markets;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} borrow or lend securities or commodities, including
    an agreement to transfer securities or commodities under which the
    borrower may repay the loan with other securities or commodities,
    cash or cash equivalents,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} clear or settle securities, futures, options or
    derivatives transactions, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} act as a depository for securities;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a repurchase, reverse repurchase or buy-sellback
  agreement with respect to securities or commodities;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a margin loan in so far as it is in respect of a
  securities account or futures account maintained by a financial
  intermediary;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any combination of agreements referred to in any of
  paragraphs (a) to (d);

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a master agreement in so far as it is in respect of
  an agreement referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (e);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a master agreement in so far as it is in respect of a
  master agreement referred to in paragraph (f);

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee of, or an indemnity or reimbursement
  obligation with respect to, the liabilities under an agreement
  referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (g); and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} an agreement relating to financial collateral,
  including any form of security or security interest in collateral and
  a title transfer credit support agreement, with respect to an
  agreement referred to in any of paragraphs (a) to (h).
