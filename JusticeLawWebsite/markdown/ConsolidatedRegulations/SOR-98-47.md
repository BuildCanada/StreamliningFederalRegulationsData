## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024480 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods of tariff item No. 9971.00.00
or 9992.00.00 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule
to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

## [Accounting for Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1024482 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2013-213, s. 5(F)

\]
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** An importer or owner of
goods, or any person authorized to account for goods under paragraph
32(6)(a) or under subsection 32(7) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), shall, when accounting for the goods under
section 32 of that Act, submit

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an invoice or written statement from the person who
  performed the repair or alteration, setting out a detailed description
  of and the value of the repair or alteration; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} proof of exportation of the goods to the United
  States, Mexico, Chile, Costa Rica, Israel or another CIFTA
  beneficiary, an EFTA state, Peru, Colombia, Jordan, Panama or
  Honduras.
