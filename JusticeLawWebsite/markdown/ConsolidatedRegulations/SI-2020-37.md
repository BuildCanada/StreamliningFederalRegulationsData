## Order to Remit Part I License Fees Paid or Payable by all Broadcasting Licensees {#order-to-remit-part-i-license-fees-paid-or-payable-by-all-broadcasting-licensees .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2020-37
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2020-06-10

Order to Remit Part I License Fees Paid or Payable by all Broadcasting
Licensees

P.C. 2020-338 2020-05-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Canadian Heritage and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27206){#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27206-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27207){#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27207-ID0EEDAA},
remits the Part I licence fees paid or payable by all licensees under
paragraph 3(a) of the [Broadcasting Licence Fee Regulations,
1997](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-144)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27210){#fn_81000-2-4182_hq_27210-ID0EBDAA},
in respect of the fiscal year 2020--2021.
