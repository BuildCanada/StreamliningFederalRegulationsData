## Contractor and Homeowner Course Registration Fees Order {#contractor-and-homeowner-course-registration-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/84-156
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1984-02-07

Order Prescribing the Fees To Be Paid in Respect of a Contractor and
Homeowner Course Offered as Part of a Program Established Pursuant to
the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation Act
::::

:::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Consumer and Corporate Affairs, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)
and Order in Council P.C. 1984-324 of 2nd February, 1984[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EECCA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Order prescribing the fees to be paid in respect of a Contractor and
Homeowner Course offered as part of a program established pursuant to
the Urea Formaldehyde Insulation
Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-156)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EBCCA}.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882106 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Contractor and Homeowner Course Registration Fees
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-84-156).

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-882108 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable by a person
who applies for registration in the course in respect of contractors and
homeowners offered as part of a program established pursuant to the Urea
Formaldehyde Insulation Act shall be as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Contractor's enrolment fee ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}\$ 20.00

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Training Package (Homeowners and Contractors) ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}25.00

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Course fee (Contractors only) ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}100.00

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Re-examination fee (Contractors only) ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}15.00

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Contractor's registration fee ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot}195.00
