## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834840 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this By-law.

[[amalgamated member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amalgamated member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a member formed by
    the amalgamation or merger of members or of members and
    non-members. ([membre fusionné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[common services costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common services costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the costs
    incurred by the Association to provide services for the benefit of
    all members and participants, including management services --- such
    as services in relation to human resources, accounting or finances
    --- the development and revision of policies and rules and research
    services provided in collaboration with members and
    stakeholders. ([coût des services communs]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[common services dues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common services dues]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the dues
    determined under subsection 5(1). ([cotisation pour services
    communs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[direct operating costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[direct operating costs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} mean the costs
    incurred by the Association to operate a system. ([frais
    d'exploitation directs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-182, s. 55\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
      defined in section 1 of the Canadian Payments Association By-law
      No. 9 --- Lynx;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a [[direct clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or
      [[group clearer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as those terms are defined
      in section 1 of the Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 3 ---
      Payment Items and Automated Clearing Settlement System; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a party to any other
      system. ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fees that are
    referred to in section 7. ([droits de service]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [[Automated Clearing Settlement
      System]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in section 1 of the
      Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 3 --- Payment Items and
      Automated Clearing Settlement System;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2021-182, s. 55\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[Lynx]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      section 1 of the Canadian Payments Association By-law No. 9 ---
      Lynx; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other clearing and settlement system, payment
      system or system or arrangement that is established or operated by
      the Association. ([système]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transaction fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transaction fees]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the fees that are
    referred to in subsection 6(1). ([droits de
    transaction]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Budgets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834851 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common services dues and transaction fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must calculate common services dues and transaction fees for a
  fiscal year on the basis of the Association's operating budget and
  capital expenditures budget for the fiscal year that the Board
  prepares under subsection 22(1) of the [Canadian Payments
  Act](/eng/acts/C-21).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of budgets

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of determining the common services
  dues and transaction fees payable for a fiscal year, the operating
  budget and capital expenditures budget for the fiscal year must
  specify common services costs and direct operating costs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserves

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board, in establishing the operating budget and
  capital expenditures budget for a fiscal year, may include amounts for
  reserves in the Association's costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Changes to budgets

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board makes a significant amendment to the operating budget or capital
  expenditures budget for a fiscal year, it must adjust the common
  services dues and transaction fees payable for the fiscal year on the
  basis of that amendment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of adjustment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must provide notice to members and
  participants at least 60 days before making an adjustment to common
  service dues and transaction fees under subsection (1).

## [New and Amalgamated Members]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834864 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First year of membership

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A new
  member, other than an amalgamated member or a member that is
  designated as a bridge institution under the [Canada Deposit Insurance
  Corporation Act](/eng/acts/C-3), must, in respect of its first year of
  membership, pay the full amount of the common services dues for the
  fiscal year in which it became a member.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amalgamated member

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An amalgamated member formed in a fiscal year must
  pay the common services dues, transaction fees and service fees for
  the fiscal year in an amount that is the sum of all the combined
  unpaid common services dues, transaction fees and service fees of the
  members and non-members that were amalgamated or merged.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of name

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a member that changes its
  name, other than as part of a merger or amalgamation, is not a new
  member.

## [Common Services Dues]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834872 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of amount

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For each
  fiscal year, each member must pay dues in respect of the common
  services costs, in an amount determined in accordance with the formula

  ::: {#834876 .Subsection}
  A/B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount estimated to be the costs, for the Association, of
      providing the services that are of equal benefit to all of the
      members, as determined by the Board in the previous fiscal year;
      and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated number of members on the first day of the fiscal
      year, as determined by the Board in the previous fiscal year.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline and notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must, no later than the day that is eight
  months before the first day of a fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the common services dues payable for the
    fiscal year under subsection (1); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform members of the amount of those dues and
    explain how they were determined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Surplus

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, after the end of a fiscal year, the total amount
  of common services dues collected is greater than the actual costs,
  for the Association, of providing the services that are of equal
  benefit to all of the members, the Board may allocate the surplus to a
  research and development reserve fund, subject to a maximum amount
  established for the fiscal year by the Board.

## [Fees for Services]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834886 .Part}

### [Transaction Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-834887 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees payable for each transaction

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Each
  participant in a system must pay a fee in respect of each of its
  transactions in the system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of transaction fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For each fiscal year, the Board must determine the
  transaction fees for each system on the basis of the estimated costs,
  for the Association, to operate that system and one of, or both of,
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of all transactions by all
    participants in that system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the estimated value of those transactions.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Considerations --- determination of
  transaction fees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Board, in deciding whether to select paragraph
  (2)(a) or (b), or both, must consider, in respect of a particular
  system, the nature of the system, the number of participants and the
  number and value of transactions by each participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs of operating system

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Board's estimate of a system's operating costs
  under subsection (2) must take into account, among other things,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the direct operating costs;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the portion of common services costs not included
    in the determination of the common services dues or in the
    determination of transactions fees for other systems;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the recovery of capital investment in the system;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a fee-stabilizing charge to be held in reserve,
    subject to a maximum amount established for the fiscal year by the
    Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee-stabilizing charge

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (4)(d), [[fee-stabilizing
  charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a charge intended to account for
  minor variations between the estimates referred to in subsection (2)
  of a system's operating costs and of the number and value of
  transactions and the actual values of those items.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline and notice

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Board must, no later than the day that is eight
  months before the first day of a fiscal year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determine the transaction fees payable for the
    fiscal year under subsection (2); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform participants of the amount of those fees and
    explain how they were determined.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Adjustment of transaction fees

  [(7)]{.lawlabel} If a participant withdraws from participation in a
  system in a fiscal year, and the President determines that reserves or
  surplus for the fiscal year are not sufficient to make up for any
  resulting loss in transaction fees, the President must adjust the
  transaction fees payable for the fiscal year.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of adjustment

  [(8)]{.lawlabel} The President must provide notice to participants at
  least 15 days before making an adjustment to transaction fees under
  subsection (7).

### [Service Fees]{.HTitleText2} {#h-834913 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination of service fees

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The fees --- other than
transaction fees --- to be paid for services performed by or on behalf
of the Association for the benefit of a member, participant or other
person must be the costs, for the Association, of providing the
services.

## [Termination of Membership or Participation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834916 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reimbursement and unpaid dues or fees

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A member that ceases to be
a member or a participant that withdraws from participation in a system

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not entitled to a reimbursement of any common
  services dues, transaction fees or service fees that have been paid;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} continues to be liable for unpaid amounts of those
  dues and fees.

## [Payment of Dues or Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834921 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common services dues

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Common
  services dues are payable by a member on or before the first day of
  each fiscal year or, if another date is established by the Board under
  subsection (3), by that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transaction fees

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Transactions fees are payable by a participant for
  each preceding three-month period, within 15 days after an invoice is
  issued or, if another date is established by the Board under
  subsection (3), by that date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other dates

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of operational efficiency, the Board
  may establish dates on which common services dues or transaction fees
  are payable other than those mentioned in subsection (1) or (2).

## [Non-payment of Dues or Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834929 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interest on unpaid dues and fees

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  addition to any other action that may be taken under another by-law, a
  member, participant or other person that fails to pay common services
  dues, transaction fees or service fees on or before the day on which
  they are due must pay interest, at a rate of not more than 0.125% of
  the amount payable for each day that the payment is overdue,
  calculated in accordance with the method set out in a resolution of
  the Board.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General revenue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of any interest paid under this By-law
  accrues to the general revenue of the Association.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834935 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Common services dues and transaction fees
--- determination and notice

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 2(2), 5(2)
and 6(6) do not apply in respect of the fiscal year that begins on
January 1, 2017.
