## Insurance Business (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#insurance-business-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-300
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 2003-08-13

Insurance Business (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

P.C. 2003-1222  2003-08-13
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 381 of the [Cooperative
Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-2003-300_e_hq_5822){#fn_SOR-2003-300_e_hq_5822-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Insurance Business (Cooperative Credit
Associations) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-300).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704451 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
      Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[association web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[association web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a web page that
      an association uses in relation to its business in Canada,
      including any information provided by the association that is
      accessible on a telecommunications device. It does not include a
      web page that is only accessible by employees or agents of the
      association. ([page Web d'association]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit or charge card-related insurance,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} creditors' disability insurance,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creditors' life insurance,

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} creditors' loss of employment insurance,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} creditors' vehicle inventory insurance,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} export credit insurance,

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} mortgage insurance, or

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} travel insurance. ([assurance
        autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[credit or charge card-related
      insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an insurance
      company that provides insurance to a holder of a credit or charge
      card issued by a body corporate that is part of the cooperative
      credit system as a feature of the card, without request and
      without an individual assessment of risk,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against loss of, or damage to, goods purchased
        with the card,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which the insurance company undertakes to
        extend a warranty provided by the manufacturer of goods
        purchased with the card, or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} against any loss arising from a contractual
        liability assumed by the card holder when renting a vehicle,
        where the rental is paid for with the card. ([assurance carte de
        crédit ou de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      group insurance policy that will pay to a body corporate that is
      part of the cooperative credit system, all or part of the amount
      of a debt of a debtor to the body corporate in the event of bodily
      injury to, or an illness or disability of,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the
        debtor or the spouse or common law partner of the debtor;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
        part of the debt;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a
        director or an officer of the body corporate; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural
        person who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the
        debtor's financial obligations to the body corporate that is
        part of the cooperative credit system. ([assurance-invalidité de
        crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
      insurance policy that will pay to a body corporate that is part of
      the cooperative credit system, all or part of the amount of a debt
      of a debtor to the body corporate, or where the debt is in respect
      of a small business or a farm, fishery or ranch, all or part of
      the amount of the credit limit of a line of credit, in the event
      of the death of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the
        debtor or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
        part of the debt;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a
        director or an officer of the body corporate; or

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural
        person who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the
        debtor's financial obligations to the body corporate that is
        part of the cooperative credit system. ([assurance-vie de
        crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[creditors' loss of employment
      insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an insurance
      company that will pay to a body corporate that is part of the
      cooperative credit system, without any individual assessment of
      risk, all or part of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the body
      corporate in the event that

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the
        debtor becomes involuntarily unemployed, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
        part of the debt becomes involuntarily unemployed. ([assurance
        crédit en cas de perte d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[creditors' vehicle inventory
      insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an insurance
      company that provides insurance against direct and accidental loss
      of, or damage to, vehicles that are held in stock, for display and
      sale, by a debtor to a body corporate that is part of the
      cooperative credit system, some or all of which have been financed
      by the body corporate. ([assurance crédit pour stocks de
      véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of
      an insurance company that provides insurance to an exporter of
      goods or services against loss incurred by the exporter as a
      result of nonpayment for exported goods or services. ([assurance
      crédit des exportateurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract of
      insurance between an insurance company and any body corporate that
      is part of the cooperative credit system that provides insurance
      severally in respect of a group of identifiable persons who
      individually hold certificates of insurance. ([police d'assurance
      collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
      approved, registered or otherwise authorized to insure risks under
      an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a
      province. ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commitment on the
      part of a body corporate that is part of the cooperative credit
      system, to lend to a debtor of the body corporate, without a
      predetermined repayment schedule, one or more amounts, where the
      aggregate amount outstanding does not exceed a predetermined
      credit limit, which limit does not exceed the reasonable credit
      needs of the debtor. ([marge de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance
      policy that provides insurance to a body corporate that is part of
      the cooperative credit system, against loss caused by a default on
      the part of a debtor who is a natural person under a loan from the
      body corporate that is secured by a mortgage on real property or
      on an interest in real property. ([assurance
      hypothèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
      insurance policy that provides insurance to a natural person,
      whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one or more amounts in the event of bodily
        injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused by an
        accident, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certain amount for each day that the person
        is hospitalized, in the event of bodily injury to the person
        that is caused by an accident or in the event of an illness or
        disability of the person. ([assurance accidents
        corporels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business that is
      or, if it were incorporated, would be a small business corporation
      within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
      Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a policy of an insurance company that provides
        insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
        away from the place where the person ordinarily resides, without
        any individual assessment of risk, against

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss that results from the cancellation or
          interruption of the trip,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss of, or damage to, personal property
          that occurs during the trip, or

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} loss that is caused by the delayed arrival
          of personal baggage during the trip, or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group insurance policy that provides
        insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
        away from the province in which the person ordinarily resides

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred during the trip
          that result from an illness or disability of the person that
          occurs during the trip,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred during the trip
          that result from bodily injury to, or the death of, the person
          that is caused by an accident during the trip,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to
          pay one or more sums of money in the event of an illness or
          disability of the person that occurs during the trip, or of
          bodily injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused
          by an accident during the trip,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred by the person for
          dental care necessitated by an accident that occurs during the
          trip, or

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the person dies during the
          trip, against expenses incurred for the return of the person's
          remains to the place where the person was ordinarily resident
          before death, or against travel expenses incurred by a
          relative of that person who must travel to identify that
          person's remains. ([assurance voyage]{.DefinedTermLink
          lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the following
  bodies corporate are part of the cooperative credit system:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a local cooperative credit society;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate in which an association or a local
    cooperative credit society has a substantial investment and which is
    primarily engaged in the business of providing financial services;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a body corporate that is primarily engaged in
    providing financial services and in which two or more associations
    or local cooperative credit societies hold, in aggregate, sufficient
    voting rights which, if exercised, would constitute control of the
    body corporate within the meaning of paragraph 3(1)(a) of the Act;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative corporation, the membership of which
    is primarily composed of associations, central cooperative credit
    societies or local cooperative credit societies, and which is
    primarily engaged in the business of providing financial services.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a web page is
  not an association web page by reason only that the web page provides
  access to an association web page or promotes the business of an
  association in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 1

## [Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704512 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A retail association may
carry on any aspect of the business of insurance, other than the
underwriting of insurance, outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association may administer an authorized type of insurance and
  personal accident insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An association may administer a group insurance
  policy for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its employees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the employees of any body corporate that the
    association has control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    section 390 of the Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the employees of a member of the association.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An association may provide
advice or a service in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an authorized type of insurance;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy that is not of an authorized type
  of insurance referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b); or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy that is not of an authorized type
  of insurance, other than a policy referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b), if

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the advice is general in nature,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the advice is not in respect of

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a specific risk, proposal in respect of life
      insurance, insurance policy or service, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a particular insurance company, agent or broker,
      and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the association does not thereby refer any person
    to a particular insurance company, agent or broker.

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704531 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An association shall not,
in relation to its business in Canada, promote an insurance company,
agent or broker unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company, agent or broker deals only in authorized
  types of insurance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside an office of a
  local cooperative credit society or a branch of a retail association
  and is directed to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all holders of credit or charge cards issued by the
    association or by any body corporate that is part of the cooperative
    credit system,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all customers of the association and all members
    of local cooperative credit societies that are members of the
    association, who are natural persons and who regularly receive
    statements of account or notices of those statements, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-5-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 5"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association shall not, in relation to its business in Canada, promote
  an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent or broker, or a
  service in respect of such a policy, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policy is of an authorized type of insurance or
    the service is in respect of such a policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is not of an authorized type of
    insurance and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is restricted to insuring the risks of the
      association, a local cooperative credit society that is a member
      of the association or an affiliate of the association, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not in any way insure the risks of a
      customer arising from a transaction with the customer.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the policy is to be provided by a corporation
    without share capital, other than a mutual insurance company or
    fraternal benefit society, that carries on business without
    pecuniary gain to its members and the policy provides insurance to a
    natural person in respect of the risks covered by travel insurance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the policy is a personal accident insurance policy
    and the promotion takes place outside an office of a local
    cooperative credit society or a branch of a retail association;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the service is in respect of a policy referred to
    in paragraph (c) or a policy referred to in paragraph (d) that is
    promoted in the manner described in that paragraph; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside an office of a
    local cooperative credit society or a branch of a retail association
    and is directed to a group of persons referred to in any of
    subparagraphs 5(b)(i) to (iii).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1) and section 5, an association
  may exclude from a promotion referred to in paragraph (1)(f) or 5(b)
  any person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the promotion would contravene
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who has given written notice to the association or
    to a body corporate that is part of the cooperative credit system
    and that is involved in the promotion, that the person does not wish
    to receive promotional material from the association or the body
    corporate;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who is the holder of a credit or charge card
    referred to in subparagraph 5(b)(i) and in respect of which the
    account is not in good standing; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} who is a member of a local cooperative credit
    society that has notified the association in writing that it does
    not wish its members to receive any promotional material from the
    association.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 4

## [Web Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704557 .Part}

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  promotion referred to in paragraph 5(b) may take place on an
  association web page if it relates to an insurance company, agent or
  broker that deals only in authorized types of insurance and the
  promotion referred to in paragraph 6(1)(d) or (f) may take place on an
  association web page if it relates to only an authorized type of
  insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, an association shall not, on an association
  web page, provide access to a web page --- directly or through another
  web page --- through which there is promotion of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company, agent or broker that does not
    deal only in authorized types of insurance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent
    or broker, or a service in respect of such a policy, that is not of
    only an authorized type of insurance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 5
:::

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704565 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  association shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly or indirectly provide to an insurance
    company, agent or broker any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer in Canada of a body corporate that is
      a member of the association,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the association in Canada, if it
      is a retail association,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer referred to in
      subparagraphs (i) or (ii),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if a customer referred to in subparagraphs (i)
      or (ii) is an entity with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if a customer referred to in subparagraph (i)
      or (ii) is a partnership with partners in Canada, any such
      partner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit any of its subsidiaries to directly or
    indirectly provide to an insurance company, agent or broker any
    information that the subsidiary receives from the association
    respecting a person referred to in any of subparagraphs (a)(i) to
    (v); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a subsidiary of the association that is a
    trust or loan corporation incorporated by or under an Act of the
    legislature of a province to directly or indirectly provide to an
    insurance company, agent or broker any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer in Canada of the subsidiary,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer referred to in
      subparagraph (i),

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if a customer referred to in subparagraph (i)
      is an entity with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if a customer referred to in subparagraph (i) is
      a partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  association, or a subsidiary of an association that is a trust or loan
  corporation incorporated by or under an Act of the legislature of a
  province, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the association or subsidiary has established
    procedures to ensure that the information referred to in that
    subsection will not be used by an insurance company, agent or broker
    to promote, in Canada, the company, agent or broker, an insurance
    policy or a service in respect of an insurance policy; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, as the case
    may be, has given an undertaking to the association or subsidiary,
    in a form acceptable to the Superintendent, that the information
    will not be used to promote in Canada the insurance company, agent
    or broker, an insurance policy or a service in respect of an
    insurance policy.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No association shall
provide a telecommunications device that is primarily for the use of
customers or members in Canada of a local cooperative credit society or
a retail association and that links a customer or member with an
insurance company, agent or broker.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A retail association shall
not carry on business in Canada on premises that are adjacent to the
premises of an insurance company, agent or broker unless the retail
association clearly indicates to its customers that its business and the
premises on which its business is carried on are separate and distinct
from those of the insurance company, agent or broker.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-185, s. 6
