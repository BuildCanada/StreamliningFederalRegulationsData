## Treasury Board Delegation of Powers Order {#treasury-board-delegation-of-powers-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-1123
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1986-12-04

Order Authorizing the Treasury Board to Exercise the Powers of the
Governor in Council under Paragraph 34(1)(a) of the Financial
Administration Act

P.C. 1986-2746 1986-12-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 5(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Order authorizing the Treasury Board to exercise the
powers of the Governor in Council under paragraph 34(1)(a) of the
Financial Administration Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1123).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890274 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Treasury Board Delegation of Powers
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1123).

## [Delegation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-890276 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Treasury Board may
exercise the powers of the Governor in Council under paragraph 34(1)(a)
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) thereby directing
that no contract by the terms of which payments are required in excess
of such amount or amounts as the Treasury Board may prescribe shall be
entered into or have any force or effect unless entry into the contract
has been approved by the Treasury Board.
