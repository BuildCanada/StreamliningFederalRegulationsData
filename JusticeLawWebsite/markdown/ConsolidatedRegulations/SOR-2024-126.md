## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455834 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[international civilian personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international civilian personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}  means
      personnel --- recruited from among the nationals of a party to the
      North Atlantic Treaty --- who are appointed to the Organisation,
      assigned to international posts appearing on the approved
      establishment of a subsidiary body of the Organisation and subject
      to the Organisation's Civilian Personnel Regulations, as amended
      from time to time. ([personnel civil
      international]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[military personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[military personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means military
      personnel of a force of a party to the North Atlantic Treaty,
      including its civilian component, who are attached for
      administrative service with the Organisation.  ([personnel
      militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Organisation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Organisation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the North Atlantic
      Treaty Organisation and subsidiary bodies set up by the Council
      under Article 9 of the North Atlantic Treaty, other than military
      headquarters.  ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Status of Forces Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Status of Forces Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      Agreement between the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty
      regarding the Status of their Forces, done at London on June 19,
      1951. ([Convention sur le statut des forces]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[temporary personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[temporary personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means personnel who
      are recruited from among the nationals of a party to the North
      Atlantic Treaty to satisfy temporary requirements of the
      Organisation and who are subject to the Organisation's Civilian
      Personnel Regulations, as amended from time to time. ([personnel
      temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation --- [[military
  personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of the definition [[military
  personnel]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection (1),
  [[force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[civilian
  component]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} have, respectively, the same meaning
  as in sub-paragraphs 1(a) and (b) of Article I of the Status of Forces
  Agreement.

## [Rights, Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455845 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Legal capacity

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organisation has the legal capacity of a corporation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Privileges and immunities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Organisation has the privileges and immunities
  set out in Articles 5 to 11 of the Ottawa Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Foreign representatives of Member States

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Foreign
  representatives of Member States of the Organisation have, to the
  extent required for the discharge of their duties, the privileges and
  immunities set out in Article 13 of the Ottawa Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official clerical staff

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Official clerical staff accompanying a foreign
  representative of a Member State of the Organisation have, to the
  extent required for the discharge of their duties, the privileges and
  immunities set out in Article 14 of the Ottawa Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}International civilian personnel and
military personnel

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} International civilian personnel and
  military personnel have, to the extent required for the exercise of
  their functions, the privileges and immunities set out in Articles 18
  and 19 of the Ottawa Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Article 20 of Ottawa Agreement

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} International civilian personnel of grade G22 and
  above and military personnel of equivalent military rank have, to the
  extent required for the exercise of their functions, the privileges
  and immunities set out in Article 20 of the Ottawa Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Temporary personnel

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Temporary personnel assigned to DIANA have, to the
  extent required for the exercise of their functions, the privileges
  and immunities set out in Articles 18 and 19 of the Ottawa Agreement.

- Definition of [[DIANA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (3),
  [[DIANA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Defence Innovation Accelerator
  for the North Atlantic, a subsidiary body of the Organisation, set up
  under Article 9 of the North Atlantic Treaty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Family members

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The family members forming part of the household of
  the temporary personnel referred to in subsection (3), of the
  international civilian personnel and of the military personnel have
  the privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs (b) and (d) of
  Article 18 of the Ottawa Agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Military personnel

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The privileges and immunities enjoyed by military
  personnel and their family members under subsections (1) and (5),
  respectively, do not affect the privileges and immunities enjoyed by
  them under the Status of Forces Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts on mission

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Experts
  have, to the extent required for the exercise of their functions, the
  privileges and immunities set out in Article 21 of the Ottawa
  Agreement.

- Definition of [[experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1),
  [[experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means experts referred to in Article
  21 of the Ottawa Agreement who are nationals of a party to the North
  Atlantic Treaty and who are employed on missions on behalf of the
  Organisation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian citizens

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Canadian citizens have,
under this Order, only the privileges and immunities set out in Articles
19 and 23 of the Ottawa Agreement.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interests of the Organisation

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  privileges and immunities granted to individuals under this Order are
  granted not for their personal benefit but rather in the interests of
  the Organisation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Waiving of immunity granted

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In accordance with Article 22 of the Ottawa
  Agreement, the Organisation has the right and the duty to waive the
  immunity that has been granted if, in its opinion,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the immunity would impede the course of justice;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the immunity can be waived without prejudice to the
    interests of the Organisation.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1455883 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
