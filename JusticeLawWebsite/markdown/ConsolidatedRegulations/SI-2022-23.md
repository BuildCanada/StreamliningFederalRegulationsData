## Order Designating (1) the Commission that is Named Public Inquiry into the 2022 Public Order Emergency as a Department for the Purposes of that Act; and (2) the Prime Minister as the Appropriate Minister with Respect to the Commission {#order-designating-1-the-commission-that-is-named-public-inquiry-into-the-2022-public-order-emergency-as-a-department-for-the-purposes-of-that-act-and-2-the-prime-minister-as-the-appropriate-minister-with-respect-to-the-commission .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2022-23
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2022-05-11

Order Designating (1) the Commission that is Named Public Inquiry into
the 2022 Public Order Emergency as a Department for the Purposes of that
Act; and (2) the Prime Minister as the Appropriate Minister with Respect
to the Commission

P.C. 2022-393 2022-04-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687-ID0EECBAA}
  of the definition "department" in section 2 of the [Financial
  Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688-ID0EBCBAA},
  designates the commission that is named Public Inquiry into the 2022
  Public Order Emergency as a department for the purposes of that Act;
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687-ID0EECBAA){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27687}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69(3)
    :::

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688-ID0EBCBAA){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688}R.S., c.
    F-11
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} under paragraph (b)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689-ID0EEBAAA}
  of the definition "appropriate Minister" in section 2 of the
  [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27688-ID0EBBAAA},
  designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect
  to the commission referred to in paragraph (a).

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689-ID0EEBAAA){#fn_81000-3-2058_hq_27689}S.C.
    1992, c. 1, s. 69(1)
    :::
