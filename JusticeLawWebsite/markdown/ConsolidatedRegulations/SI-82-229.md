## Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order, 1982 {#satellites-and-satellite-subsystems-remission-order-1982 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-229
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-12-08

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Goods Used in the
Manufacture of Satellites and Satellite Subsystems for Export

P.C. 1982-3470 1982-11-18
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to section 17
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to revoke the Electronic Subsystems for Communication Satellites
Remission Order, made by Order in Council P.C. 1980-1677 of 19th June,
1980[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA},
and to make the annexed [Order respecting the remission of customs duty
on goods used in the manufacture of satellites and satellite subsystems
for export](/eng/regulations/SI-82-229).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote_e}SI/80-119,
  1980 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2395
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638662 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Satellites and Satellite Subsystems Remission Order,
1982](/eng/regulations/SI-82-229).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638664 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duty paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on goods, other than goods
classified under tariff items 42700-1 and 42701-1 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), imported during the period commencing on
March 30, 1980 and ending on December 31, 1987 for use in the
manufacture of satellites and satellite subsystems for export.

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-638666 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission referred to
in section 2 is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made
to the Minister of National Revenue within three years of the date of
importation.
