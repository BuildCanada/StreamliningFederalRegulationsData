## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788739 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[incorporated laws]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the statutes,
    regulations and other legislative instruments of British Columbia,
    or the portions of them, set out in Schedule 2, as amended from time
    to time and as adapted by sections 13 to 43. ([textes législatifs
    incorporés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[project lands]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lands set out in
    Schedule 1. ([terres du projet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Provincial [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21)

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The incorporated laws are
to be interpreted in accordance with the British Columbia
[Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21) (R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 238), as amended
from time to time, and, for that purpose, references to "enactment" in
that Act are to be read to include the incorporated laws.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other expressions

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
adaptations in sections 13 to 43 are to be interpreted to be part of the
incorporated laws to which they apply.

## [Application of Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788748 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5, the
incorporated laws apply to the project lands.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provision of an incorporated law applies only if the provision of the
  law of British Columbia that it incorporates is in force.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restriction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, an incorporated law applies
  only to the extent that it is within the limits of federal
  constitutional authority.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation of procedural matters

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided and subject to any adaptations set out in sections
  13 to 43, the following are to be carried out in accordance with the
  laws of British Columbia, whether or not those laws have been set out
  in Schedule 2:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the enforcement of incorporated laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the prosecution of an offence, or any other
    proceedings, in relation to the contravention of an incorporated
    law;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the review or appeal of an action or decision
    taken, or of a failure to take an action that could have been taken,
    under an incorporated law;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any requirements for notice or service in relation
    to an action to be taken under an incorporated law.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Related powers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), a person or body
  that has a power, duty or function under a law of British Columbia has
  the same power, duty or function in respect of any actions taken under
  that subsection.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Offences and penalties

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If contravention of a law
of British Columbia that is incorporated in these Regulations is an
offence under the laws of British Columbia, contravention of the
incorporated law is also an offence and is subject to the same penalties
as under the laws of British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Financial requirements under lease

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** If the incorporated laws
require a cash deposit or other financial security to be given, those
requirements do not displace, but instead apply in addition to, the
requirements of any lease of the project lands in relation to cash
deposits or other financial security.

## [Inapplicable Federal Regulations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788769 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The [Indian Reserve Waste
Disposal Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._960) do not apply to
the project lands.

## [Construction Standards]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conformity with British Columbia
standards

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Construction of and
alterations to buildings on the project lands must be done in accordance
with the British Columbia Building Code and British Columbia Fire Code,
as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Occupancy permit

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  building on the project lands is to be occupied unless the Haisla
  Nation, or a person authorized by the Haisla Nation, has issued an
  occupancy permit for that building.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Occupancy permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Haisla Nation, or a person authorized by the
  Haisla Nation, must issue an occupancy permit for a building within 15
  days after it receives written confirmation by a person registered or
  licensed as a professional engineer under the Engineers and
  Geoscientists Act (R.S.B.C. 1996, c. 116) that the building was
  constructed in accordance with section 10.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788780 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Survival of rights

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Any permits,
authorizations, orders or exemptions --- including any amendments to
them --- issued by the British Columbia Oil and Gas Commission in
relation to the project lands prior to the coming into force of these
Regulations are considered to have been issued under these Regulations
and to be valid for the purposes of these Regulations.

## [Adaptations Applicable to All Incorporated Laws]{.HTitleText1} {#h-788783 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}British Columbia statutes and regulations

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise
indicated, the statutes and regulations referred to in sections 18 to 43
are statutes and regulations of British Columbia.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of incorporated laws

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  incorporated laws are to be read without reference to any of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} spent provisions and provisions making
    consequential amendments to other enactments that are not
    incorporated laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provisions appointing a person, providing for the
    remuneration of a person, or establishing or continuing a provincial
    body, program or fund;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provisions relating to the internal management of a
    provincial body;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} provisions requiring or authorizing monies to be
    paid from the Consolidated Revenue Fund of British Columbia;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} provisions authorizing the Lieutenant-Governor in
    Council, a Minister or a provincial body to make regulations of
    general application, except to the extent required to make the
    regulations set out in Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of incorporated laws

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person appointed to a position under a law of
    British Columbia that has been incorporated by reference in these
    Regulations is considered to have been appointed to the same
    position for the purposes of these Regulations, for so long as that
    person remains in that position under the law of British Columbia;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a provincial body, program or fund established or
    continued under a law of British Columbia that has been incorporated
    by reference in these Regulations is considered to have been
    established or continued for the purposes of these Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specified officials and bodies

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, a person or body that has a
  power, duty or function under a law of British Columbia incorporated
  by reference in these Regulations has the same power, duty or function
  under these Regulations, subject to the adaptations set out in
  sections 18 to 43.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation of adapted laws

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if a law of British Columbia
  is adapted by these Regulations, a reference to that law in an
  incorporated law or in any notice, form, instrument or other document
  issued under an incorporated law is to be read as a reference to that
  law as adapted by these Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Unless otherwise provided
by these Regulations, a provision of an incorporated law that imposes an
obligation, liability or penalty on an owner, occupier, public
authority, public body or unspecified person or entity does not apply to
Her Majesty in right of Canada or federal ministers or officials.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on searches and inspections

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A power to search or make
inspections under an incorporated law, including the power to enter a
place, does not include a power to enter or search, or to inspect
anything in, a federal government office, without the consent of the
person who is or appears to be in charge of that office.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation on production of documents

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A power to seize, remove
or compel the production of documents under an incorporated law does not
include a power to seize, remove or compel the production of a document
in the possession of the federal government, without the consent of the
person in possession of the document.
