## Manitoba Milk Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Order {#manitoba-milk-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-530
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1990-08-07

Order Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of
Milk Produced in Manitoba
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Milk Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section
3[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBBA}
of the [Manitoba Milk Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._155), C.R.C.,
c. 155, made pursuant to section 2 of the [Agricultural Products
Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6), hereby makes the annexed Order respecting
the marketing in interprovincial and export trade of milk produced in
Manitoba.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFBBA){#footnote1_e}SOR/90-271,
  1990 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 1998
  :::

Winnipeg, Manitoba, August 1, 1990
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926477 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Milk Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-530).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926479 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}means a person who produces milk in
    the Province of Manitoba. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926482 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Order applies to the marketing of milk in interprovincial and export
  trade, and in relation to persons and property situated in the
  Province of Manitoba.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Order does not apply to the marketing of milk in
  a container that has a capacity of 20 litres or less.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926486 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall buy or
purchase milk except from or through the Commodity Board.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No producer shall sell milk
except to or through the Commodity Board.

## [Payment to Producers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926489 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commodity Board may conduct pools for the distribution of all moneys
  received from the sale of milk by producers to or through the
  Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall, after deducting all
  necessary and proper disbursements and expenses and after making
  allowances for reserves, distribute the remainder of the moneys
  received from the sale of milk in such manner that each producer
  receives a share thereof relative to the quantity, quality and grade
  of the milk delivered to the Commodity Board by the producer.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board may make an initial payment to a
  producer on delivery of milk by the producer to the Commodity Board
  and may make subsequent payments until that producer has received the
  remainder of that producer's share.

## [Transportation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926494 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall transport milk except to the Commodity Board or to a
  person retained by the Commodity Board to transport milk.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall deliver milk for transport except to
  a person retained by the Commodity board to transport milk.
