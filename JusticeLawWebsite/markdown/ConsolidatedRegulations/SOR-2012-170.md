## Order Establishing a List of Foreign State Supporters of Terrorism {#order-establishing-a-list-of-foreign-state-supporters-of-terrorism .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2012-170
:::

[STATE IMMUNITY ACT](/eng/acts/S-18)

Registration 2012-09-07

Order Establishing a List of Foreign State Supporters of Terrorism

P.C. 2012-1067 2012-09-07
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Foreign Affairs made after consultation with the Minister of Public
Safety and Emergency Preparedness, is satisfied that there are
reasonable grounds to believe that each of the foreign states set out on
the list established by the annexed Order supported or supports
terrorism;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to
subsection 6.1(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-1460-E_hq_13094){#fn_81000-2-1460-E_hq_13094-ID0EGCAA}
of the [State Immunity Act](/eng/acts/S-18)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-1460-E_hq_13095){#fn_81000-2-1460-E_hq_13095-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Order Establishing a List of Foreign State Supporters
of Terrorism](/eng/regulations/SOR-2012-170).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}List

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The list set out in the
schedule is established for the purposes of section 6.1 of the [State
Immunity Act](/eng/acts/S-18).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on September 7, 2012.
