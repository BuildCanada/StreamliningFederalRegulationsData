## Eastern Townships Wood Producers' Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#eastern-townships-wood-producers-levies-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/94-356
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1994-05-16

Order Fixing and Imposing Levies on, and the Collecting of Levies from,
Producers in the Eastern Townships Region in the Province of Quebec With
Respect to the Marketing of Wood in Interprovincial and Export Trade
::::

:::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Eastern Townships Region Wood Producers' Syndicate, pursuant to
sections 3[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFCBA} and 4 of the
[Quebec Wood Order, 1983](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-713), made by Order in
Council P.C. 1983-2885 of September 22, 1983[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBCBA}, hereby
makes the annexed Order fixing and imposing levies on, and the
collecting of levies from, producers in the Eastern Townships Region in
the Province of Quebec with respect to the marketing of wood in
interprovincial and export trade.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFCBA){#footnote1_e}SOR/85-1067,
  1985 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 4651
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EBCBA){#footnote2_e}SOR/83-713,
  1983 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 3444
  :::

Rock Forest, Quebec, May 13, 1994
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963863 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Syndicat des
    producteurs de bois de l'Estrie]{.otherLang lang="fr"}.
    ([Syndicat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Plan conjoint des producteurs
    de bois de l'Estrie, R.Q., c. M-35.1, r. 102.
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a producer who is subject to
    the Plan and engaged in the production or marketing of wood;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wood]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means wood that is produced in the
    Eastern Townships Region and that is subject to the Plan.
    ([bois]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963871 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board, for all wood marketed in interprovincial and export
trade, the applicable levies fixed or imposed in the following
Regulations, as amended from time to time:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur les contributions des producteurs de
  bois de l'Estrie]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, R.Q. c. M-35.1, r. 75.1;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur le fonds de recherche et de protection
  des producteurs de bois de l'Estrie]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, R.Q.,
  c. M-35.1, r. 99; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur le fonds de roulement du Syndicat des
  producteurs de bois de l'Estrie]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, R.Q.,
  c. M-35.1, r. 100.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-269, s. 3
- SOR/2011-328, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Collection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-963878 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The producer shall pay all
levies payable under section 3 to the Commodity Board at its
headquarters in Sherbrooke, Quebec, within the time limits set out in
the applicable Regulations referred to in that section.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-269, s. 3
