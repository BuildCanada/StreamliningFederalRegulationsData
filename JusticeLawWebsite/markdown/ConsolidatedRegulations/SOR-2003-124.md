## CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Remission Order {#ccrfta-sugar-aggregate-quantity-limit-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

:::

CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Remission Order

P.C. 2003-415 2003-03-27
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [CCRFTA Sugar Aggregate Quantity Limit Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-124).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699799 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means refined sugar of tariff item
    Nos. 1701.91.90, 1701.99.90, 1702.90.11, 1702.90.12, 1702.90.13,
    1702.90.14, 1702.90.15, 1702.90.16, 1702.90.17, 1702.90.18 and
    1702.90.89. ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Costa Rica Free Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Costa Rica Free Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a geographic area
    within Costa Rica specified by the Minister of National Revenue in
    accordance with subsection 16(2.1) of the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011). ([zone franche du Costa
    Rica]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699805 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable
pursuant to the Costa Rica Tariff under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on the following aggregate quantities of
goods for the following periods:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 20,000 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2003 and ending on December
  31, 2003;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 23,200 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2004 and ending on December
  31, 2004;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 26,680 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2005 and ending on December
  31, 2005;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} 29,615 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2006 and ending on December
  31, 2006;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} 32,280 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2007 and ending on December
  31, 2007;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} 34,863 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2008 and ending on December
  31, 2008;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} 37,652 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2009 and ending on December
  31, 2009; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} 40,000 tonnes of the goods imported into Canada
  during the period commencing on January 1, 2010 and ending on December
  31, 2010.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699815 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The remission is granted
pursuant to section 2 on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods originate in Costa Rica pursuant to the
  [CCRFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-395);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods have not undergone operations in a Costa
  Rica Free Zone;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within four years after the day on which the goods
  were imported; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} no claim for refund or drawback under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) has
  been made with respect to the goods.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-699821 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
