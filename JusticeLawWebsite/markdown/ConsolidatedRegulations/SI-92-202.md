## Importations by Certain Processing Service Companies (GST) Remission Order {#importations-by-certain-processing-service-companies-gst-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/92-202
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1992-12-16

Order Respecting the Remission of Tax Paid or Payable under Division III
of Part IX of the Excise Tax Act by Certain Processors in Respect of
Goods Imported for Processing and Subsequent Export

P.C. 1992-2375  1992-11-19

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest, is pleased hereby, on the recommendation of the
Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-92-202_e_hq_6504){#fn_SI-92-202_e_hq_6504-ID0EFBAA}

export](/eng/regulations/SI-92-202).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645223 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Importations by Certain Processing Service Companies (GST)
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-92-202).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645225 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of goods, includes the
    assembly, manufacture, modification, production, packaging,
    repackaging and repair of the goods; ([traitement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means A.R.D. Industries Ltd.,
    Automotive Technologies Inc., Dart Machine Ltd., Tube-Mac
    Installations Ltd. or U.H.V. Instruments Ltd.
    ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645229 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted to each processor of the tax paid or payable
by the processor under Division III of Part IX of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15), to the extent that the tax has not otherwise been
rebated, refunded or remitted, in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported goods that are processed in Canada and
  subsequently exported without being used in Canada, or that are used
  in, wrought into or attached to goods that are processed in Canada and
  subsequently exported without being used in Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported materials, other than fuel, lubricants or
  plant equipment, consumed directly in the processing in Canada of
  goods that are subsequently exported without being used in Canada.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645233 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The remission under section
3 in respect of goods or materials imported by a processor is on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} from the time the goods or materials are imported
  until the time the products resulting from the processing of the goods
  or materials are exported, the processor does not have any proprietary
  interest in the goods, materials or products;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the exportation referred to in section 3 occurs
  within six months after the day on which the goods or materials are
  accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6);
  and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the processor does not claim an input tax credit
  under Part IX of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of
  the tax to be remitted.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-645238 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** This Order

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Automotive Technologies Inc. and
  U.H.V. Instruments Ltd., is effective in respect of importations after
  July 3, 1991 and before October 1, 1992; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Tube-Mac Installations Ltd., A.R.D.
  Industries Ltd. and Dart Machine Ltd., is effective in respect of
  importations after August 14, 1991 and before October 1, 1992.
