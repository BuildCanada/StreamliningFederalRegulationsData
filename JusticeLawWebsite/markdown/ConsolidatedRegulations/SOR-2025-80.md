## [Definitions and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517083 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[annual charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[annual charge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount, in Canadian
    dollars, that a designated manufacturer is required to pay during a
    fiscal year for the purpose of allowing His Majesty in Right of
    Canada to recover the annual cost base for the previous fiscal
    year. ([redevance annuelle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[annual cost base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[annual cost base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount, in
    Canadian dollars, representing the costs incurred by His Majesty in
    right of Canada during a fiscal year in relation to the carrying out
    of the purpose of the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
    Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5). ([frais de base annuels]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a roll or tubular construction
    intended for smoking, other than a little cigar, that contains a
    filler composed of natural or reconstituted tobacco and has a
    wrapper, or a wrapper and a binder, composed of natural or
    reconstituted tobacco. ([cigare]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cigarette]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes any roll or tubular
    construction that contains tobacco and is intended for smoking,
    other than a cigar, little cigar, tobacco stick or
    bidi. ([cigarette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[civic address]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for an address in Canada, the unit number, civic
      number, street name, municipality name, province name and postal
      code; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for an address outside Canada, the unit number,
      civic number, street name, municipality name, province or state
      name, postal or ZIP code and country name. ([adresse
      municipale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity in
    Canada that manufactures or imports tobacco products for retail sale
    in Canada. It does not include an entity that only packages, labels
    or distributes those tobacco products. ([fabricant
    désigné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next year.
     ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[heated tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[heated tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tobacco
    product intended for use with a device that is necessary for its use
    and that heats the tobacco. ([produit du tabac
    chauffé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net sales revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net sales revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the value, in
    Canadian dollars, of a designated manufacturer's sales of tobacco
    products --- excluding any taxes or duties --- during a fiscal year
    minus the value of any returns of tobacco products during the same
    fiscal year. It includes the value, in Canadian dollars, of monetary
    and non-monetary consideration received by the designated
    manufacturer for sales of tobacco products. ([recette de ventes
    nettes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[smokeless tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[smokeless tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means chewing tobacco or
    snuff. ([tabac sans fumée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tobacco product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} does not include papers,
    tubes or filters intended for use with a tobacco product, a device
    that is necessary for the use of a tobacco product or the parts that
    may be used with that device. ([produit du tabac]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
designated manufacturers in respect of tobacco products sold

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at retail in Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} as ships' stores, in accordance with the [Ships'
  Stores Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-40); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to an [[accredited
  representative]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1).

## [Sales and Revenue]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517104 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of sales and revenue

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated manufacturer must submit to the Minister, no later than
  April 30 of each fiscal year, a statement of sales and revenue for the
  previous fiscal year that includes the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the designated manufacturer's name, telephone
    number, email address and the civic address of its principal place
    of business in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    individual who prepared the statement of sales and revenue and the
    civic address of their workplace;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    individual who is responsible for the designated manufacturer's
    financial affairs and the civic address of their workplace and, if
    that individual works for an entity other than the designated
    manufacturer, the name of the entity, as well as the telephone
    number, email address and civic address of its principal place of
    business in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, telephone number and email address of the
    individual responsible for communications respecting payment of the
    annual charge and the civic address in Canada of their workplace;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the business number assigned to the designated
    manufacturer by the Minister of National Revenue;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date of the statement of sales and revenue and
    the fiscal year to which it relates;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the designated manufacturer's net sales revenue for
    each of the following categories as well as its total net sales
    revenue:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} cigarettes,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cigars,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} little cigars,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} cigarette tobacco,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} leaf tobacco,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} pipe tobacco,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} water pipe tobacco,

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} smokeless tobacco,

    - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} heated tobacco products, and

    - [(x)]{.lawlabel} all other types of tobacco product; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for each category referred to in subparagraphs
    (g)(i) to (x):

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tobacco product sold by weight,
      the total weight in kilograms of the tobacco products sold during
      the fiscal year minus the total weight in kilograms of the tobacco
      products returned during the same fiscal year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a tobacco product sold as a unit
      or as a package containing more than one unit, the total number of
      units sold during the fiscal year minus the total number of units
      returned during the same fiscal year, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the total value, in Canadian dollars, of any
      excise duty imposed during the fiscal year on the tobacco products
      sold minus the total value, in Canadian dollars, of any excise
      duty imposed during the same fiscal year on any returns of the
      tobacco product.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attestations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The statement of sales and revenue must include the
  following signed and dated attestations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the individual who prepared the
    statement that the information in the statement is correct and
    complete to the best of their knowledge and belief and is provided
    in good faith; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an attestation by the individual who is responsible
    for the designated manufacturer's financial affairs that the
    statement was prepared in accordance with generally accepted
    accounting principles.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic submission

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The designated manufacturer must submit the statement
  of sales and revenue using the form established by the Minister
  entitled Statement of Tobacco Sales and Revenue, as amended from time
  to time, and must send it electronically in the manner specified in
  the form.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Clarification

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A designated manufacturer must, within ten days of
  the day on which the Minister makes a request, submit to the Minister
  any information that is necessary to clarify information in the
  statement of sales and revenue.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Estimated total net sales revenue

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  designated manufacturer did not submit a statement of sales and
  revenue for a given fiscal year or if the Minister has reasonable
  grounds to believe that the information provided by a designated
  manufacturer in its statement is incorrect or incomplete, the Minister
  may estimate the designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue
  using any relevant information that is available to the Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to total net sales revenue

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a designated manufacturer's total net sales
  revenue has been estimated, a reference in these Regulations to that
  designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue is to be read as a
  reference to its estimated total net sales revenue.

## [Annual Charge]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517142 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculation

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A designated manufacturer's
annual charge for each fiscal year is to be calculated according to the
formula

::: {#1517145 .Section}
A ÷ B × C

where

[A]{.dfn}
:   is the designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue for the
    previous fiscal year;

[B]{.dfn}
:   is the aggregate of the total net sales revenue of all designated
    manufacturers for the previous fiscal year; and

[C]{.dfn}
:   is the annual cost base for the previous fiscal year.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Previous annual charges

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister becomes aware that an annual charge was not previously
  calculated for a designated manufacturer but should have been
  calculated, the Minister must calculate that annual charge according
  to the formula in section 5.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, based on new information, the Minister has
  reasonable grounds to believe that it is necessary to correct an
  annual charge that was previously calculated for a designated
  manufacturer, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} use the new information to recalculate the annual
    charge according to the formula in section 5; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subtract the amount of the annual charge that was
    previously calculated from the amount of the annual charge that was
    recalculated under paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply if more than
  seven years have elapsed since November 30 of the fiscal year
  following the fiscal year to which the new information relates.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment due date

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A designated manufacturer
must, no later than November 30 of each fiscal year,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pay the annual charge that was calculated according
  to the formula in section 5 and any annual charge that was calculated
  under subsection 6(1) if, in the calculation of the annual charge, the
  designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue was at least 0.001%
  of the aggregate of the total net sales revenue of all designated
  manufacturers; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an annual charge that was recalculated
  under paragraph 6(2)(a) and where the subtraction made under paragraph
  6(2)(b) results in a positive amount,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} pay that positive amount if, in the previous
    calculation of the annual charge, the designated manufacturer's
    total net sales revenue was at least 0.001% of the aggregate of the
    total net sales revenue of all designated manufacturers, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} pay the recalculated annual charge if,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} in the previous calculation of the annual charge,
      the designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue was less
      than 0.001% of the aggregate of the total net sales revenue of all
      designated manufacturers, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} in the recalculation of the annual charge, the
      designated manufacturer's total net sales revenue was at least
      0.001% of the aggregate of the total net sales revenue of all
      designated manufacturers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount credited

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** No later than November 30
of each fiscal year, the Minister must credit to a designated
manufacturer any negative amount resulting from the subtraction made
under paragraph 6(2)(b) if, in the previous calculation of the annual
charge referred to in subsection 6(2), the designated manufacturer's
total net sales revenue was at least 0.001% of the aggregate of the
total net sales revenue of all designated manufacturers.

## [Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517175 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be kept

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  designated manufacturer must keep the documents it used in order to
  submit the statement of sales and revenue required under subsection
  3(1) and any clarifying information requested by the Minister under
  subsection 3(4) for a period of at least seven years after the day on
  which the statement to which the documents relate was submitted to the
  Minister.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accessibility of documents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents must be kept in paper or electronic
  form at the designated manufacturer's principal place of business in
  Canada in such a way that they are readily accessible at all times and
  can be submitted to the Minister on written request.
