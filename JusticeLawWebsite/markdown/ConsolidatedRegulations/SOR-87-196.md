## Canadian Arsenals Limited Pension Protection Regulations {#canadian-arsenals-limited-pension-protection-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-196
:::

[CANADIAN ARSENALS LIMITED DIVESTITURE AUTHORIZATION
ACT](/eng/acts/C-11.4)

Registration 1987-03-26

Regulations Respecting Pension Protection for Employees of Canadian
Arsenals Limited

P.C. 1987-591 1987-03-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Supply and Services and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
section 12 of the [Canadian Arsenals Limited Divestiture Authorization
Act](/eng/acts/C-11.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting pension protection
for employees of Canadian Arsenals
Limited](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-196).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904632 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Arsenals Limited Pension Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-196).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904634 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Arsenals Limited
Divestiture Authorization Act](/eng/acts/C-11.4). (*Loi*)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904636 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All the provisions of the
[Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36), the [Supplementary
Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) and the regulations made under
those Acts apply to persons who make the election referred to in
paragraph 12(1)(e) of the Act except to the extent that those provisions
are adapted in accordance with sections 4 and 5.

## [Adapted Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904638 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 12 of the Act, subparagraphs 9(7)(b)(i) and (ii) and sections 11
and 12 of the [Public Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) are
adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 9(7)(b)(i) and (ii), the year in
  which a person ceased to be employed in the Public Service shall be
  considered to be the year in which the person ceased to be employed by
  the Corporation or the amalgamated corporation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in sections 11 and 12,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} except for the purposes of subsection 11(4), the
    number of years of pensionable service and any periods of employment
    in respect of the person shall be considered to include any such
    service or employment with the Corporation or the amalgamated
    corporation during the period beginning on the day the person ceases
    to be employed in the Public Service and ending on the day
    immediately preceding the day on which the person ceases to be
    employed by the Corporation or the amalgamated corporation, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} except for the purposes of subparagraph (i) of
    these Regulations the day on which a person ceases to be employed in
    the Public Service shall be considered to be the day on which the
    person ceases to be employed by the Corporation or the amalgamated
    corporation.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 12 of the Act, subsection 4(3) and subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and
(ii) of the [Supplementary Retirement Benefits Act](/eng/acts/S-24) are
adapted as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in subsection 4(3), the retirement year or retirement
  month of a person shall be considered to be 1986 or May 1986, as the
  case may be; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in subparagraphs 6(2)(b)(i) and (ii), the year in
  which a person ceased to contribute in respect of current service to
  the Supplementary Retirement Benefits Account shall be considered to
  be the year in which the person ceased to be employed by the
  Corporation or the amalgamated corporation.

## [Application Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-904647 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
applicable on and after May 10, 1986, a day that is before their
publication in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).
