## Order Designating British Columbia for the Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code {#order-designating-british-columbia-for-the-purposes-of-the-criminal-interest-rate-provisions-of-the-criminal-code .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2009-278
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2009-10-01

Order Designating British Columbia for the Purposes of the Criminal
Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal Code

P.C. 2009-1629 2009-10-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas British Columbia has legislative measures that protect
recipients of payday loans and that provide for limits on the total cost
of borrowing under a payday loan agreement;

And whereas the Lieutenant Governor in Council of British Columbia has
requested that the Governor in Council designate that province for the
purposes of section 347.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EECBA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBCBA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EECBA){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365}S.C.
  2007, c. 9, s. 2
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EBCBA){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366}R.S., c.
  C-46
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice and the Minister of Industry,
pursuant to subsection 347.1(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3365){#fn_610628-e_hq_3365-ID0EGAAA}
of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610628-e_hq_3366){#fn_610628-e_hq_3366-ID0EDAAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Order Designating British Columbia for the
Purposes of the Criminal Interest Rate Provisions of the Criminal
Code](/eng/regulations/SOR-2009-278).
:::

## [Province Designated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750942 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** British Columbia is
designated for the purposes of section 347.1 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750944 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndE917_hq_8060){#fn_IndE917_hq_8060-ID0EBFA}[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2
.sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force at 12:00 a.m., Pacific time
or --- in that part of the province in the Mountain time zone ---
Mountain time, on the first day on which all of the following are in
force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Business Practices and Consumer Protection
  (Payday Loans) Amendment Act, 2007, S.B.C. 2007, c. 35, except for the
  following:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sections 112.12 to 112.15 of the Business Practices
    and Consumer Protection Act, S.B.C. 2004, c. 2, as enacted by
    section 4,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sections 5 and 6, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 189(4)(f.6) of the Business Practices
    and Consumer Protection Act, S.B.C. 2004, c. 2, as enacted by
    section 8;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 5.1 and 7.3 of the Business Practices and
  Consumer Protection Regulation, B.C. Reg. 294/2004, as enacted by B.C.
  Reg. 57/2009; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Payday Loans Regulation, as enacted by B.C. Reg.
  57/2009.
