## L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission Order {#l-5-hydroxytryptophan-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 773
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on L-5
Hydroxytryptophan
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561907 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._773).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561909 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duties paid or payable under the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on L-5 hydroxytryptophan imported on or
after October 1, 1975.
