## International North Pacific Fisheries Commission Privileges and Immunities Order {#international-north-pacific-fisheries-commission-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1316
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting Privileges and Immunities for the International North
Pacific Fisheries Commission and for its Representatives, Officials and
Experts
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503329 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [International North Pacific Fisheries Commission Privileges and
Immunities Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1316).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503331 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International North
    Pacific Fisheries Commission. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503335 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Organization shall have the legal capacities of a body corporate and
  shall, to such extent as it may require, have the privileges and
  immunities set forth in Articles II and III of the Convention.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Representatives of states and governments that are
  members of the Organization shall, to such extent as may be required
  for the performance of their functions, have the privileges and
  immunities set forth in Article IV of the Convention for
  representatives of members.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} All officials of the Organization shall, to such
  extent as may be required for the performance of their functions, have
  the privileges and immunities set forth in Article V of the Convention
  for officials of the United Nations.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} All experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall, to such extent as may be required for the performance of their
  functions, have the privileges and immunities set forth in Article VI
  of the Convention for experts on missions for the United Nations.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in this Order
exempts a Canadian citizen residing or ordinarily resident in Canada
from liability for any taxes or duties imposed by any law in Canada.
