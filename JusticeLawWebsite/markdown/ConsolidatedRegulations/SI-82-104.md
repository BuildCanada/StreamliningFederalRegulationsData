## Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-communications-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/82-104
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1982-05-26

Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C 1982-1394 1982-05-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11),
is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Communications to
prescribe, by order, the fees to be paid by persons in the private
sector

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for services provided in relation to the
  certification of Canadian films and videotapes the principal
  photography or taping of which was commenced on or after January 1,
  1982; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for services provided in relation to the
  certification of Canadian films and videotapes, the principal
  photography or taping of which was commenced at any time after May 25,
  1976, where the services relate to a review of an application for an
  advance ruling, a provisional approval or a certificate which the
  Minister of Communications intends to deny.
