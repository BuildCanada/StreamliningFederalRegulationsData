## Quebec Sheep Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order {#quebec-sheep-marketing-levies-interprovincial-and-export-trade-order .Title-of-Act}

Quebec Sheep Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
[Les Éleveurs d'ovins du Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, formerly known
as the [Office des producteurs d'agneaux et de moutons du
Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, pursuant to sections 3 and 4 of the
[Quebec Sheep and Wool Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-91)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-3134_hq_20178){#fn_81000-2-3134_hq_20178-ID0EDBBA},

Export Trade) Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-14).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-847486 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who raises or produces
sheep on their own behalf or on behalf of another person or offers sheep
for sale.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-847488 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Board, for each sheep sold by the producer in interprovincial and
international trade, the levies fixed or imposed in the following
regulations, as amended from time to time:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur les contributions des producteurs
  d'ovins]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 242;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} [Plan conjoint des producteurs d'ovins du
  Québec]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 245; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [Règlement sur la vente en commun des agneaux
  lourds]{.otherLang lang="fr"}, RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 246.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-847493 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
