## Temporary Importation of Conveyances by Residents of Canada Regulations {#temporary-importation-of-conveyances-by-residents-of-canada-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-840
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1982-09-07

Regulations Respecting Temporary Importation of Conveyances by Residents
of Canada

P.C. 1982-2677 1982-09-03
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to tariff item 70306-1 of
Schedule A to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting the temporary
importation of conveyances by residents of Canada.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874893 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Temporary Importation of Conveyances by Residents of
Canada Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-840).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874895 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[commuter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commuter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a resident who is employed by an employer who
      operates a business in the United States and for whom the employer
      supplies a conveyance, on which duties have not been paid, for use
      in connection with that business and for travelling between the
      resident's place of employment in the United States and the
      resident's residence, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a resident who operates a business in the United
      States and who uses a conveyance, on which duties have not been
      paid, in connection with that business and for travelling between
      the resident's place of business in the United States and
      residence; ([travailleur migrant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle, aircraft or
    other contrivance that is used to move persons or goods, but does
    not include water-borne craft; ([moyen de
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hire or reward]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hire or reward]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any payment,
    consideration, gratuity or benefit directly or indirectly charged,
    demanded, received or collected by a person for the carriage of
    passengers or goods; ([rémunération ou salaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of National
    Revenue; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, in the settled
    routine of the person's life, makes that person's home, resides and
    is ordinarily present in Canada. ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-84
- SOR/93-42, s. 1
- SOR/99-233, s. 2
:::

## [Terms and Conditions of Importation of Conveyances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874906 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A conveyance may be
imported if,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance, while in Canada is to be used solely
  for the transportation of a resident and accompanying persons from the
  point of arrival in Canada directly to a specified destination in
  Canada and from the specified destination to a destination outside
  Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commuter, the conveyance while in
  Canada is to be used for the personal transportation of the commuter
  and accompanying persons from the point of arrival in Canada to
  specified destinations in Canada and from a specified destination in
  Canada to a destination in the United States;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a resident other than a commuter, the
  conveyance is to be imported only for the purpose of transporting his
  household or personal effects into or out of Canada, or for personal
  transportation as a result of an emergency or unforeseen contingency;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a commuter, the conveyance is to be
  imported for the purpose of personal transportation of the commuter
  and accompanying persons between his place of residence in Canada and
  a point in the United States or for the purpose of visiting clients in
  Canada on behalf of his employer;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is not to be used in Canada for the
  purpose of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} touring or other leisure activity,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} carrying passengers or goods for hire or reward,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} transporting goods for sale, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} soliciting sales or subscriptions on behalf of an
    employer who operates a business in Canada;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} at the time of importation, the person importing the
  conveyance specifies to the customs officer the date on which the
  person intends to export the conveyance from Canada; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is exported from Canada within the
  time limit specified in section 5.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-84
- SOR/93-42, s. 2
:::

## [Time Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874922 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  conveyance may remain in Canada until the expiration of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the intended date of exportation specified pursuant
    to paragraph 3(f), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 30 days from the date of its importation,

  whichever is the earlier.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where it is impossible or impracticable for a
  resident to comply with the requirements referred to in subsection
  (1), the Minister may extend the time during which a conveyance may
  remain in Canada for any additional period not exceeding 60 days from
  the date of its importation into Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-84
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may require
security with respect to a conveyance that is temporarily imported in
the form of cash or a certified cheque in an amount not exceeding the
duties that would be payable if tariff item No. 9802.00.00 did not apply
to that conveyance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-84
- SOR/98-62, s. 3
:::
