## Charlottetown Airport Zoning Regulations {#charlottetown-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-649
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1992-11-19

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Charlottetown Airport

P.C. 1992-2321 1992-11-19
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 5.5 of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Charlottetown Airport, substantially in the form set out in
the schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the
[Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I, on March 16th and
23rd, 1991, and in two successive issues of the Charlottetown Guardian
and The Evening Patriot on April 25th and 26th, 1991, and in two
successive issues of [La Voix Acadienne]{.otherLang lang="fr"} on
May 1st and 8th, 1991, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded
to interested persons to make representations to the Minister of
Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Acting Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 5.4 of the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby
to make the annexed [Regulations respecting zoning at Charlottetown
Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-649).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949122 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Charlottetown Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-649).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949124 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Charlottetown Airport,
      in the County of Queens, in the Province of Prince Edward Island;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 47.2 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949135 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the Schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949139 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949144 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in paragraphs 4(a) to (c), the Minister
may require that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object
is growing remove the excessive growth.

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-949146 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or occupier of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning Plan
Nos. S-1857-3 to S-1857-6 inclusive, dated February 12, 1988, is
determined by measuring l 200 m northerly along the centre line of
runway 03-21 from the southerly end of runway 03 and thence measuring
easterly at right angles from the said centre line, 300 m from the
centre line of said runway. The said Airport Reference Point has Prince
Edward Island Double Stereographic Grid Co-ordinates of N 293 283.042 m
and E 690 665.396 m.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning Plan
Nos. S-1857-0 to S-1857-12 inclusive, dated February 12, 1988, are
surfaces abutting each end of the strips associated with runways
designated 03-21 and l0-28 and are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 03 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 21 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach l0 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant l5 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 28 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant l5 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning Plan
Nos. S-1857-0, S-1857-2 to S-1857-6 inclusive, S-1857-8, S-1857-9 and
S-1857-11, dated February 12, 1988, is an imaginary surface located at a
common plane established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the
elevation of the airport reference point, except that, where that common
plane is less than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer
surface is an imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the
ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning Plan Nos. S-1857-0 and
S-1857-3 to S-1857-6 inclusive, dated February 12, l988, are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 03-21 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  2 253.6 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 10-28 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  2 253.6 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning Plan
Nos. S-1857-0 and S-1857-3 to S-1857-6 inclusive, dated February 12,
1988, is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of
1 m measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to
the centre line and projected centre line of each strip and extending
upward and outward from the lateral limits of each strip and its
approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
transitional surface of an adjoining strip.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Limits]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-the-outer-limits .SchedHeadL1}

The boundary of the outer limits, shown on Charlottetown Airport Zoning
Plan Nos. S-1857-0, S-1857-2 to S-1857-6 inclusive, S-1857-8, S-1857-9
and S-1857-11 dated February 12, l988, is a circle with a radius of
4 000 m centred on the airport reference point.
