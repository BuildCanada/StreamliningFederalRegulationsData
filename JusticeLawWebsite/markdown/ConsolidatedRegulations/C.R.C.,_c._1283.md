## Materials for the Use of the Blind Regulations {#materials-for-the-use-of-the-blind-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1283
:::

[CANADA POST CORPORATION ACT](/eng/acts/C-10)

Regulations Respecting the Conditions Under Which Materials for the Use
of the Blind may be Sent by Post Free of Postage
::::

:::::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502235 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Materials for the Use of the Blind
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1283).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502237 .Part}

::: HistoricalNote
\[

- SOR/2000-199, s. 3(F)

\]
:::

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[material for the use of the blind]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material for the use of the blind]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} matter impressed in Braille or similar raised
      type;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} plates for manufacturing matter described in
      paragraph (a),

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} tapes, records and other sound recordings posted
      by the blind in Canada; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} tapes, records, other sound recordings and
      special paper intended solely for the use of the blind if mailed
      by or addressed to a recognized institution for the blind.
      ([documentation à l'usage des aveugles]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[postage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[postage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the charge payable for the
    handling and conveyance of material for the use of the blind.
    ([affranchissement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/83-800, s. 1
- SOR/86-245, s. 1
- SOR/94-203, s. 1
- SOR/2000-199, s. 4
- SOR/2003-379, s. 1
:::

## [Limits of Size]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502249 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Material for the use of the
blind sent by post in Canada may be sent free of postage if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for delivery in Canada,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the combined length, width and depth of the
    material does not exceed 2 m with the greatest measurement not
    exceeding 1 m, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the material is not less than 10 cm in length and
    7 cm in width;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for delivery outside Canada,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the combined length, width and depth of the
    material does not exceed 90 cm with the greatest measurement not
    exceeding 60 cm, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the material is not less than 14 cm in length and
    9 cm in width.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-194, s. 1
- SOR/2003-379, s. 1
- SOR/2008-285, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Maximum Weight]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502260 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The maximum weight of any
item of material for the use of the blind that may be sent by post under
these Regulations is 7 kg.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-194, s. 3
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-502264 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  item of material for the use of the blind shall, if for delivery in
  Canada,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of marking, printing or labelling, bear
    the words "Literature for the blind" or "Documentation à l'usage des
    aveugles" in the upper right corner on the address side of the item;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear on the address side of the item a label in a
    form approved by the Corporation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every item of material for the use of the blind
  shall, if for delivery outside of Canada,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by means of marking, printing or labelling, bear
    the words "Cécogrammes" or "Cécogrammes (Literature for the blind)"
    in the upper right corner on the address side of the item; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bear on the address side of the item a label in a
    form approved by the Corporation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every item referred to in subsection (2) shall meet
  all applicable customs documentation requirements as described in the
  [International Letter-Post Items
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-83-807).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every item of material for the use of the blind shall
  be prepared for posting in such a way that its contents are
  sufficiently protected while permitting quick and easy verification.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Every item of material for the use of the blind shall
  be accepted for registered mail or, in Canada, Xpresspost^TM^ mail at
  no charge.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-387, s. 1
- SOR/94-203, s. 2
- SOR/2003-379, s. 2
- SOR/2004-297, s. 2
:::
