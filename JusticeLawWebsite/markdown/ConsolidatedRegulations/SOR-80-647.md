## British Columbia Mushroom Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#british-columbia-mushroom-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-647
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-08-11

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Mushrooms Produced in British Columbia
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The British Columbia Mushroom Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3 of
the [British Columbia Mushroom Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-444) made
by Order in Council P.C. 1978-1544 of 11th May, 1978[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-80-647_e_hq_7914){#fn_SOR-80-647_e_hq_7914-ID0EDCAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations respecting the marketing in
interprovincial and export trade of mushrooms produced in British
Columbia](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-647).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-80-647_e_hq_7914-ID0EDCAA){#fn_SOR-80-647_e_hq_7914}SOR/78-444,
  1978, [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2284
  :::

Dated at Langley, British Columbia, this 6th day of August, 1980
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869473 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [British Columbia Mushroom Marketing (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-647).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869475 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing
    (British Columbia) Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Fraser Valley Mushroom Growers'
    Co-operative Association; ([Agence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Mushroom
    Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[caterer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[caterer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who uses the
    regulated product on his premises in serving food to the public;
    ([traiteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[grower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who produces the
    regulated product to which the scheme applies;
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence referred to in
    section 10 or 11; ([permis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[marketing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of the regulated
    product, means buying, offering for sale, selling, shipping for sale
    or storage and includes its transportation;
    ([placement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[peddler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[peddler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who sells or offers
    for sale the regulated product on the street or on premises other
    than a fixed commercial establishment operated by him; ([vendeur
    itinérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any individual, corporation,
    firm or partnership and includes the servants, agents and employees
    of any individual, corporation, firm or partnership;
    ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who cans,
    freezes, preserves, dehydrates or changes the nature of the
    regulated product by mechanical means or otherwise and markets the
    regulated product so canned, frozen, preserved, dehydrated or
    changed; ([transformateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regulated product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means mushrooms grown in
    the Province of British Columbia; ([produit
    réglementé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[retailer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who sells or
    offers for sale the regulated product direct to consumers from a
    fixed commercial establishment operated by that person but does not
    include a licensed grower who sells or offers for sale the regulated
    product

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} directly to consumers,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a caterer, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to a processor

    if the regulated product is produced by that grower and is sold or
    offered for sale by the grower at a mushroom plant owned by him or
    on the property on which the mushroom plant is situated;
    ([détaillant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scheme]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scheme]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the British Columbia Mushroom
    Scheme established under the Act; ([plan]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[transport order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transport order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order, issued and
    signed by the Board, in a form approved by the Board, that
    authorizes the moving or transporting of the regulated product from
    one place to another place; ([autorisation de
    transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[trucker]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, other than a
    grower who uses a truck owned by him to haul the regulated product
    produced by him, who, in respect of a vehicle, holds or is required
    by law to hold a public or limited freight licence issued by the
    Public Utilities Commission of the Province of British Columbia;
    ([camionneur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[warehouseman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[warehouseman]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, other than
    the Agency, who stores the regulated product for another person;
    ([entreposeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wholesaler]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person, other than the
    Agency, who sells or offers for sale a regulated product to any
    retailer, peddler, caterer, processor or wholesaler or otherwise
    than directly to the consumer. ([grossiste]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869498 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of the regulated product in interprovincial and
export trade and to persons and property situated within the Province of
British Columbia.

## [Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869500 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency is hereby designated as the Agency through which the regulated
  product shall be packed, stored and marketed in accordance with these
  Regulations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (4), the Agency shall
  receive, pack, store and market all regulated products delivered to
  the Agency in accordance with orders issued by the Board under the
  Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Agency shall not receive, pack, store or market a
  regulated product grown by a grower who is not the holder of a
  Grower's Licence referred to in section 10.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may refuse to accept, pack, store or
  market a regulated product delivered to it by a grower where,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the opinion of the Agency, the regulated product
    is not of marketable quality; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the regulated product, any provision
    of these Regulations or any order made by the Board under the Act is
    contravened by the grower.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Agency is authorized to conduct a pool or pools
  for the distribution of all proceeds received from the sale of the
  regulated product through it for such period or periods of time and
  covering such of the regulated product or types or grades thereof as
  it sees fit and, subject to approval by resolution of the Board before
  distribution thereof, shall distribute the proceeds of sale for each
  pool, after deducting necessary and proper disbursements, expenses and
  charges, in such manner that each person receives a share of the total
  proceeds in relation to the amount, variety, size, grade and class of
  the regulated product delivered by that person to the Agency and shall
  make payments in respect thereof until the total net proceeds are
  distributed.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Agency may establish an office or warehouse at
  any place approved by the Board and shall establish offices or
  warehouses at such places as the Board may direct.

## [Charges by the Agency]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869510 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency is authorized to collect from each grower such charges as are
  approved by the Board in respect of the packing, grading, regrading,
  storing, washing, handling, transporting or marketing of the regulated
  product of that grower by the Agency.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where, pursuant to paragraph 4(4)(a), the Agency
  refuses to accept, pack, store or market a regulated product, the
  Agency may make and collect such charges as are approved by the Board
  for services rendered by the Agency in respect of any handling or
  regrading of the regulated product.

## [Disposal of Regulated Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869514 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
subsection 4(4), the Agency refuses to accept, pack, store or market a
regulated product, that product shall only be disposed of in accordance
with directions issued by the Board.

## [Grievance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869516 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the Agency has

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} refused to accept, pack, store or market a regulated
  product pursuant to subsection 4(4), or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} made charges referred to in subsection 5(2),

the grower may appeal the refusal or the charges, as the case may be, to
the Board.

## [Grading of Regulated Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869521 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Subject to subsection 4(4),
all regulated products delivered to the Board shall, before sale, be
graded and packed by the Agency unless exempted under the authority of
an order issued by the Board under the Act.

## [Licences and Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869523 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** No person, other than the
Agency, shall engage in the production, packing, transporting, storing
or marketing of the regulated product unless that person has registered
with the Board.

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall operate
as a caterer, grower, peddler, processor, retailer, warehouseman or
wholesaler unless that person applies to and receives from the Board a
Caterer's Licence, Grower's Licence, Peddler's Licence, Processor's
Licence, Retailer's Licence, Warehouser's Licence or Wholesaler's
Licence, as the case may be, for each premise, building, cellar or pit
used by that person in carrying out his operation.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** No trucker shall
transport the regulated product unless he applies to and receives from
the Board a Trucker's Licence for each truck operated by him in
transporting the regulated product.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Every licence shall
expire on December 31 next following its date of issue.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The fee payable to the
Board in respect of licences are as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Caterer's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} no fee

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Grower's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$ 1.00

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Peddler's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} no fee

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Processor's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$10.00

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Retailer's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} no fee

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Trucker's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$ 1.00

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Warehouser's Licence ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} no fee

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} Wholesaler's Licence![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.LeaderRightJustified-dot} \$ 5.00

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A licence may be
cancelled by the Board for violation of any provision of the scheme, any
order issued by the Board under the Act or any provision of these
Regulations.

## [Records and Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869538 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** It shall be a condition
of any licence that the person to whom the licence is issued shall keep
and retain for at least three years, records and accounts in respect of
all matters relating to the production, packing, processing, storing,
transporting and marketing of the regulated product by that person.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-193, s. 1
:::

## [Information, Inspection and Search]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869542 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
ascertaining whether any orders of the Board under the Act or any
provisions of these Regulations are being complied with, every grower or
any person transporting, packing, storing or marketing a regulated
product shall, on request, produce for inspection by the Board or by any
member or employee of the Board or by any person authorized by the Board
for that purpose, all records and accounts kept by that grower or person
respecting all matters relating to the regulated product.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Every person engaged in
the production, packing, processing, storing, transportation or
marketing of the regulated product shall, at the request of the Board,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} furnish full information to the Board in respect of
  such production, packing, processing, storing, transportation or
  marketing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit the inspection of the premises used by him in
  such production, packing, processing, storing, transportation or
  marketing by any member or employee of the Board or any other person
  authorized by the Board for that purpose.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Where the Board or any
member or employee of the Board has reasonable grounds to believe that a
provision of these Regulations is not being complied with by a person in
charge of a vehicle that contains the regulated product or that the
Board or member or employee has reason to believe contains the regulated
product, the Board, member or employee may stop and detain the vehicle
and, if accompanied by a peace officer, may, without warrant, enter and
search the vehicle.

## [Conditions of Sale and Marketing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869548 .Part}

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} buy, sell or offer for sale the regulated product

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in combination with any other commodity at a
      combined price or at prices not applicable to the purchase of such
      commodities separately, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} contemporaneously with any gift or sale of any
      other thing of value;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to section 20, transport the regulated
    product in a vehicle unless the trucker or person transporting the
    regulated product has a valid transport order and the transport
    order is carried in the vehicle during the period the regulated
    product is being transported; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell or offer for sale the regulated product at any
    auction or be in possession of the regulated product in or about any
    premises where an auction is taking place or may reasonably be
    expected to take place.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 21, no grower shall sell, offer
  for sale or deliver to any person, or directly negotiate the sale or
  terms of sale with any person, of any regulated product required to be
  delivered to the Agency in accordance with orders issued by the Board
  under the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A wholesaler shall not buy, accept or receive the
  regulated product from any person other than the Agency.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No wholesaler shall sell or offer for sale the
  regulated product to any person other than a licensed retailer,
  peddler, caterer or processor or another licensed wholesaler.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No peddler shall buy, accept or receive the regulated
  product from any person other than a licensed wholesaler.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} No retailer shall buy, accept or receive the
  regulated product from any person other than a licensed wholesaler or
  the Agency.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} No caterer shall buy, accept or receive the regulated
  product from any person other than a licensed wholesaler, retailer or
  peddler or the Agency.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} No processor shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sell or offer for sale the regulated product except
    in a processed or manufactured form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} buy, accept or receive the regulated product from
    any person other than a licensed wholesaler or the Agency.

## [Exceptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869565 .Part}

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Paragraph 19(1)(b) does
not apply

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a person transporting the regulated product if
  that person establishes that a transport order in respect of the
  regulated product being transported has been issued;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a person transporting the regulated product
  purchased from a licensed grower pursuant to section 21; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to any person transporting bed run mushrooms from a
  licensed grower to the Agency.

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A licensed grower may
sell, offer for sale or deliver the regulated product at his mushroom
plant or on the property on which the plant is situated to any person
purchasing the regulated product for consumption if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regulated product was grown by him; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the regulated product being sold,
  offered for sale or delivered does not exceed five pounds.

## [Sale Price of the Regulated Product]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869573 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Agency shall not sell or offer for sale any type, variety or grade of
  the regulated product to a wholesaler or processor at a price less
  than the minimum price fixed by resolution of the Board from time to
  time for that type, variety or grade.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No wholesaler or processor shall buy the regulated
  product at a price less than the minimum price referred to in
  subsection (1).

## [Seizure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869577 .Part}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  any employee of the Board or any individual authorized by resolution
  of the Board has reasonable grounds to believe that any provision of
  these Regulations or any order of the Board in respect of the packing,
  transportation, storage or marketing of the regulated product is not
  being complied with, that employee or individual may seize the
  regulated product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated product seized pursuant to
  subsection (1) shall, subject to directions of the Board, be delivered
  to the Board.

<!-- -->

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the regulated product is seized pursuant to subsection 23(1), the
  employee or individual referred to in that subsection shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} attach a seizure tag or detention tag on, at or
    near the location of the regulated product; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} deliver a notice of seizure, in such form as may be
    approved by the Board, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the adult person who is or appears to be in
      charge of or in possession of the regulated product, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any adult person who is or appears to be in
      charge of any place, land, premises or vehicle where the regulated
      product is located.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a member or employee of the Board or an
  individual authorized by the Board to effect the seizure of the
  regulated product

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} attaches a seizure or detention tag pursuant to
    paragraph 24(1)(a), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} delivers a notice of seizure pursuant to paragraph
    24(1)(b),

  no person, other than a member or employee of the Board shall move,
  destroy, sell, offer for sale or otherwise dispose of the regulated
  product without authority in writing from the Board.

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The regulated product
seized pursuant to subsection 23(1) may be disposed of by the Board and
such disposal may be made within twenty-four hours of the time of the
seizure.

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the Board, in disposing of the regulated product pursuant to section
  25 directs that it be sold by the Agency, the Agency may charge and
  collect out of the proceeds of the sale

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the marketing and handling of the
    regulated product, ten cents for each pound of the regulated product
    sold;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs incurred by the Agency in respect of the
    packing, grading, storing, washing, handling, transportation or
    marketing of the regulated product; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any expenses of the Board arising out of or
    relating to the seizure and sale of the regulated product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The proceeds of the sale of the regulated product
  referred to in subsection (1), minus the charges and expenses referred
  to in that subsection may be paid by the Agency to the person from
  whom the regulated product was seized.

<!-- -->

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a regulated product is seized pursuant to subsection 23(1), the owner
  of the regulated product may make representations to the Board by
  forwarding such representations to the Secretary of the Board who
  shall place the representations before the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Board, on receiving the representations
  referred to in subsection (1), determines that the seizure of the
  regulated product was improper, the Board may order that the charges
  made pursuant to subsection 26(1), if any, be refunded and direct that
  the person from whom the regulated product was seized be paid by the
  Agency for the regulated product so seized.
