## Input Tax Credit Information (GST/HST) Regulations {#input-tax-credit-information-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Input Tax Credit Information (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2755  1990-12-18
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsections
169(4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-45_e_hq_6147){#fn_SOR-91-45_e_hq_6147-ID0EHBAA}
and 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-91-45_e_hq_6147){#fn_SOR-91-45_e_hq_6147-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the
annexed Regulations prescribing the information that a registrant is to
obtain before filing a return in which an input tax credit is claimed.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936874 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intermediary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} of a person, means, in respect
    of a supply made by the person, a registrant

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that, acting as agent of the person or under an
      agreement with the person, causes or facilitates the making of the
      supply, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is deemed under subsection 177(1.11) of the
      Act to have acted as agent of the person in making the
      supply; ([intermédiaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial sales tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial sales tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tax prescribed
    by paragraph 3(b) of the Taxes, Duties and Fees (GST) Regulations;
    ([taxe de vente provinciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[provincial sales tax paid or payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[provincial sales tax paid or payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    provincial sales tax that became payable or, if it had not become
    payable, was paid; ([taxe de vente provinciale payée ou
    payable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a supply,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} exempt,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} taxable and zero-rated, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} taxable and not zero-rated;
      ([catégorie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supporting documentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supporting documentation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the form in
    which information prescribed by section 3 is contained, and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an invoice,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a receipt,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a credit-card receipt,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a debit note,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a book or ledger of account,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a written contract or agreement,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any record contained in a computerized or
      electronic retrieval or data storage system, and

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any other document validly issued or signed by a
      registrant in respect of a supply made by the registrant in
      respect of which there is tax paid or payable; ([pièce
      justificative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax paid or payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax paid or payable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tax that became
    payable or, if it had not become payable, was paid. ([taxe payée ou
    payable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prescribed Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-936896 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 169(4)(a) of the Act, the following information is prescribed
information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the total amount paid or payable shown on the
  supporting documentation in respect of the supply or, if the
  supporting documentation is in respect of more than one supply, the
  supplies, is less than \$100,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the supplier or the intermediary in
    respect of the supply, or the name under which the supplier or the
    intermediary does business,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} where an invoice is issued in respect of the
    supply or the supplies, the date of the invoice,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where an invoice is not issued in respect of the
    supply or the supplies, the date on which there is tax paid or
    payable in respect thereof, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the total amount paid or payable for all of the
    supplies;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the total amount paid or payable shown on the
  supporting documentation in respect of the supply or, if the
  supporting documentation is in respect of more than one supply, the
  supplies, is \$100 or more and less than \$500,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the supplier or the intermediary in
    respect of the supply, or the name under which the supplier or the
    intermediary does business, and the registration number assigned
    under section 241 of the Act to the supplier or the intermediary, as
    the case may be,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in subparagraphs (a)(ii)
    to (iv),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where the amount paid or payable for the supply
    or the supplies does not include the amount of tax paid or payable
    in respect thereof,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the amount of tax paid or payable in respect of
      each supply or in respect of all of the supplies, or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} where provincial sales tax is payable in respect
      of each taxable supply that is not a zero-rated supply and is not
      payable in respect of any exempt supply or zero-rated supply,

      - [(I)]{.lawlabel} the total of the tax paid or payable under
        Division II of Part IX of the Act and the provincial sales tax
        paid or payable in respect of each taxable supply, and a
        statement to the effect that the total in respect of each
        taxable supply includes the tax paid or payable under that
        Division, or

      - [(II)]{.lawlabel} the total of the tax paid or payable under
        Division II of Part IX of the Act and the provincial sales tax
        paid or payable in respect of all taxable supplies, and a
        statement to the effect that the total includes the tax paid or
        payable under that Division,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where the amount paid or payable for the supply or
    the supplies includes the amount of tax paid or payable in respect
    thereof and one or more supplies are taxable supplies that are not
    zero-rated supplies,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a statement to the effect that tax is included in
      the amount paid or payable for each taxable supply,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the total (referred to in this paragraph as the
      "total tax rate") of the rates at which tax was paid or payable in
      respect of each of the taxable supplies that is not a zero-rated
      supply, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the amount paid or payable for each such supply
      or the total amount paid or payable for all such supplies to which
      the same total tax rate applies, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} where the status of two or more supplies is
    different, an indication of the status of each taxable supply that
    is not a zero-rated supply; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the total amount paid or payable shown on the
  supporting documentation in respect of the supply or, if the
  supporting documentation is in respect of more than one supply, the
  supplies, is \$500 or more,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information set out in paragraphs (a) and (b),

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the recipient's name, the name under which the
    recipient does business or the name of the recipient's duly
    authorized agent or representative,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the terms of payment, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of each supply sufficient to
    identify it.
