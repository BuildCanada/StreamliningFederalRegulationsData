## Order Revoking the New Brunswick Translated Documents Regulations, SOR/93-9 {#order-revoking-the-new-brunswick-translated-documents-regulations-sor93-9 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2023-5
:::

[CRIMINAL CODE](/eng/acts/C-46)

Registration 2023-01-09

Order Revoking the New Brunswick Translated Documents Regulations,
SOR/93-9
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Lieutenant-Governor in Council of New Brunswick, pursuant to section
533 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46), hereby revokes the [New
Brunswick Translated Documents Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-9),
SOR/93-9, and makes the annexed Regulation respecting the status and
admissibility into evidence of documents translated by Official
Translators in New Brunswick, in substitution therefor.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Citation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Regulation may be
cited as the New Brunswick Translated Documents Regulation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Regulation.

[[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a transcript of any
    proceedings. ([document]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Justice of New Brunswick,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Justice of The Court of King's Bench of
      New Brunswick,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a judge of The Court of Appeal of New Brunswick,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a judge of The Court of King's Bench of New
      Brunswick, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a judge of the Provincial Court of New
      Brunswick. ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Official Translator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Official Translator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed
    by the Lieutenant-Governor in Council under section 44 of the
    [Official Languages Act](/eng/acts/O-3.01). ([traducteur
    officiel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[proceedings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[proceedings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any proceedings relating
    to a preliminary inquiry or trial in a criminal matter that is under
    an Act of Parliament and that is before the Provincial Court of New
    Brunswick, The Court of King's Bench of New Brunswick or The Court
    of Appeal of New Brunswick. ([procédure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[translation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[translation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a translation from one of
    the official languages of Canada into the other official language of
    Canada by an Official Translator. ([traduction]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  Official Translator signs or purports to sign a certificate certifying
  that a translation of a document is correct, the certificate is
  evidence of the statement contained therein without proof of the
  appointment, signature or official character of the Official
  Translator.

- [3 (2)]{.lawlabel} A translation of a document, in whole or in part,
  that is certified in accordance with subsection (1) is admissible in
  evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  translation of a document that has been certified in accordance with
  subsection 3(1) is equally authentic and of equal weight in evidence
  as the document of which it is a translation.

- [4 (2)]{.lawlabel} A party to the proceedings who objects to the
  translation of a word or words in a document shall by motion, orally
  or in writing, at the first available opportunity, request that the
  judge who presides over the preliminary inquiry or trial rule on the
  objection.
