## Tetrachloroethylene (Use in Dry Cleaning and Reporting Requirements) Regulations {#tetrachloroethylene-use-in-dry-cleaning-and-reporting-requirements-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-79
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2003-02-27

Tetrachloroethylene (Use in Dry Cleaning and Reporting Requirements)
Regulations

P.C. 2003-262 2003-02-27

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on August 18, 2001, a copy
of the proposed [Tetrachloroethylene (Use in Dry Cleaning and Reporting
Requirements) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-79), and persons
were given an opportunity to file comments with respect to the
Regulations or to file a notice of objection requesting that a board of
review be established and stating the reasons for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBDA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  33
  :::

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 93(3) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBACA}, the National
Advisory Committee has been given an opportunity to provide its advice
under section 6 of that Act;

And whereas, in the opinion of the Governor in Council, pursuant to
subsection 93(4) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBABA}, the proposed
Regulations do not regulate an aspect of a substance that is regulated
by or under any other Act of Parliament in a manner that provides, in
the opinion of the Governor in Council, sufficient protection to the
environment and human health;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, pursuant to
subsection 93(1) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDAAA}, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Health, hereby makes the annexed [Tetrachloroethylene (Use in Dry
Cleaning and Reporting Requirements)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-79).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708219 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[refrigerated condenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[refrigerated condenser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a closed
    tetrachloroethylene vapour recovery system that uses mechanical
    refrigeration to cool the tetrachloroethylene vapour to a liquid for
    re-use in a dry-cleaning machine. ([condenseur
    réfrigéré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[residue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any solid, liquid or sludge
    waste containing tetrachloroethylene, other than waste water, that
    is produced at a dry-cleaning facility. ([résidus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[spotting agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[spotting agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance or mixture
    of substances that is applied directly to a stain for its removal
    from textile and apparel goods, rugs, furs, leathers and other
    similar articles. ([agent détachant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste management facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility
    that is authorized under applicable laws to handle, recycle or
    dispose of tetrachloroethylene, waste water and residue.
    ([installation de gestion des déchets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[waste water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[waste water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means waste water containing
    tetrachloroethylene, that is produced by a dry-cleaning machine or
    during the regeneration of a carbon adsorber. ([eaux
    résiduaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Tetrachloroethylene Used in Dry Cleaning]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708226 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-708227 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  Part applies in respect of the use of tetrachloroethylene for the dry
  cleaning of textile and apparel goods, rugs, furs, leathers and other
  similar articles, except in the course of their manufacture.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of the use of
  tetrachloroethylene in a textile mill.

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-708231 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use a
spotting agent that contains tetrachloroethylene for commercial or
institutional dry-cleaning purposes.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall use
tetrachloroethylene for dry cleaning unless the tetrachloroethylene,
waste water and residue are stored in closed containers at all times,
except when access is required for operation or maintenance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-216, s. 1(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall sell
tetrachloroethylene to the owner or operator of a dry-cleaning machine
or use tetrachloroethylene for dry cleaning unless the dry-cleaning
machine

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} uses the same drum for the washing, extraction,
  drying and aeration cycles;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has an integral refrigerated condenser that recovers
  tetrachloroethylene vapour in the recirculated air from the drum of
  the machine;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} prevents tetrachloroethylene vapour in the drum from
  being vented into the atmosphere during the washing, extraction,
  drying and aeration cycles;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} has an integral tetrachloroethylene-water separator
  that recovers tetrachloroethylene from waste water;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} has a manufacturer's design rating for
  tetrachloroethylene consumption equal to or less than 10 kg or 6.2 L
  of tetrachloroethylene per 1000 kg of clothing cleaned or,
  alternatively, was installed or in use prior to August 1, 2003; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} is operated within a dry-cleaning facility that is
  equipped with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a tetrachloroethylene-impermeable secondary
    containment system encompassing at least the entire surface under
    each dry-cleaning machine, tank or other container containing
    tetrachloroethylene, waste water or residue and capable of
    containing at least 110% of the capacity of the largest tank or
    container within the containment system, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} tetrachloroethylene-resistant drain plugs that are
    readily available to seal all floor drains into which
    tetrachloroethylene, waste water or residue may flow in the event of
    a spill.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-216, s. 2(F)

### [Waste Water and Residue]{.HTitleText2} {#h-708251 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or operator of a dry-cleaning machine shall have all waste water

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} transported to a waste management facility no less
    than once every 12 months; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} treated by the dry-cleaning machine's or the carbon
    adsorber's integral tetrachloroethylene-water separator and an
    on-site waste water treatment system that contains the following
    equipment:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a second tetrachloroethylene-water separator that
      recovers tetrachloroethylene from the waste water exiting the
      integral tetrachloroethylene-water separator,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an initial filter containing activated carbon
      that removes the tetrachloroethylene from the waste water exiting
      the second tetrachloroethylene-water separator,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a monitor-alarm that automatically shuts down
      the waste water treatment system when the initial filter becomes
      saturated with tetrachloroethylene, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a second filter containing activated carbon that
      removes tetrachloroethylene from the waste water after it passes
      through the initial filter and past the monitor-alarm.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When having waste water transported pursuant to
  paragraph (1)(a), the owner or operator shall have transported all of
  the waste water that is within the dry-cleaning facility at the time
  of the transport that has not been treated by the waste water
  treatment system described in paragraph (1)(b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2018-11, s. 16

  owner or operator of a dry-cleaning machine shall have all residue
  transported to a waste management facility no less than once every 12
  months.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When having residue transported pursuant to
  subsection (1), the owner or operator shall have transported all of
  the residue that is within the dry-cleaning facility at the time of

### [Transfer Requirements]{.HTitleText2} {#h-708268 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** No person shall transfer
tetrachloroethylene into a dry-cleaning machine, tank or other container
at a facility where a dry-cleaning machine is used unless a closed
direct-coupled delivery system is used during the transfer process that
prevents the release of tetrachloroethylene.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Tetrachloroethylene Reporting --- Importation, Recycling, Sale and Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708270 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A person who, in a
calendar year, imports tetrachloroethylene into Canada shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain books and records respecting the
  importation; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a report to the Minister, no later than April
  30 of the following calendar year, in a form determined by the
  Minister and containing the information specified in Schedule 1.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-216, s. 5

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who, in a calendar year, recycles tetrachloroethylene in Canada
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain books and records respecting the recycling
    activity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit a report to the Minister, no later than
    April 30 of the following calendar year, in a form determined by the
    Minister and containing the information specified in Schedule 2.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1),
  [[recycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to tetrachloroethylene,
  means recover and clean and includes reclaiming but does not include
  recycling or reclaiming tetrachloroethylene in a dry-cleaning machine.

a dry-cleaning facility who, in a calendar year, uses
tetrachloroethylene shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain books and records respecting the purchase of
  tetrachloroethylene for dry cleaning, the transport of waste water or
  residue to a waste management facility or the treatment of waste water
  under paragraph 8(1)(b); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} submit to the Minister a separate report for each
  dry-cleaning facility, no later than April 30 of the following
  calendar year, in a form determined by the Minister and containing the
  information specified in Schedule 4.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Any report submitted
under this Part shall be signed and dated

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, by a person authorized
  to do so; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by the person submitting the
  report or by a person authorized to act on behalf of that person.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A person who is required
under this Part to maintain books and records and to submit a report
shall keep the books, records, a copy of the report and any
documentation, including shipping records, supporting the information
provided to the Minister, at the person's principal place of business in
Canada, or at the principal place of business in Canada of a
representative of that person, for a period of five years after the end
of the year in which the report is made.

## [SCHEDULE 4]{.scheduleLabel}[(Paragraph 14(b))]{.OriginatingRef}[Information Related to the Purchase of Tetrachloroethylene for Dry-Cleaning, the Transport of Waste Water and Residue and the Treatment of Waste Water]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-708346 .scheduleLabel}
:::

- [1]{.lawlabel} Information respecting owner or operator of a
  dry-cleaning machine:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses of principal place
    of business, e-mail address, if any, telephone number and fax
    number, if any

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} name, title, civic and postal addresses, e-mail
    address, if any, telephone number and fax number, if any, of the
    person authorized to act on behalf of the owner or operator

- [2]{.lawlabel} Confidentiality

  Indicate if a request for confidentiality is being made under section
  313 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and the reason for the request.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Information respecting dry-cleaning machines

  The following information must be given for each machine:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make, model, serial number and cleaning capacity of
    the machine

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether the machine is equipped with an
    integral refrigerated condenser

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether the waste water treatment system
    described in paragraph 8(1)(b) of these Regulations was used for the
    treatment of waste water produced by the machine

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-216, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} indicate whether the machine was installed or in
    use prior to August 1, 2003

- [4]{.lawlabel} Information respecting purchase of tetrachloroethylene
  and transport of waste water and residue during a calendar year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} calendar year

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each purchase, the quantity, in kilograms, of
    tetrachloroethylene purchased and the date of the purchase

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each transport, the quantity, in kilograms or
    litres, of waste water transported to a waste management facility
    and the date of the transport

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each transport, the quantity, in kilograms or
    litres, of residue transported to a waste management facility and
    the date of the transport

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} for each transport, the quantity, in kilograms or
    litres, of waste water and residue mixed together transported to a
    waste management facility and the date of the transport

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses and telephone
    number of each person that transported waste water, residue or waste
    water and residue mixed together from each dry-cleaning facility and
    the date of each transport

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} name, civic and postal addresses and telephone
    number of each waste management facility to which waste water,
    residue or waste water and residue mixed together was transported
