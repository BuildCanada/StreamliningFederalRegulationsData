## Canadian Forces Dental Treatment by Civilians Regulations {#canadian-forces-dental-treatment-by-civilians-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1045
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Regulations Respecting Dental Treatment by Civilian Practitioners for
Personnel of the Canadian Forces
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483516 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canadian Forces Dental Treatment by Civilians
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1045).

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483518 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Dental treatment for
personnel of the Canadian Forces by a civilian dental practitioner
selected by the Command Dental Officer of the Canadian Forces Dental
Services in the region concerned, may be given

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a Canadian Forces Dental Services clinic is not
  available, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where Canadian Forces Dental Services personnel lack
  the specialized equipment or facilities or are otherwise unable to
  render the required treatment,

but such treatment shall not be commenced by a practitioner without
prior authorization of the Command Dental Officer.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Treatment shall be
sufficient to establish and maintain dental fitness and to provide
reasonable assurance of masticatory efficiency and freedom from pain for
a period of 12 months.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding section 2,
where Canadian Forces Dental Services facilities are not available,
civilian dental practitioners may undertake treatment, without prior
authorization, in the following emergencies only:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the relief of pain or acute infection, or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the repair of broken dentures,

but the reconstruction or alteration of any dental appliances is not
permitted without prior authorization.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of National
Defence may prescribe the procedure to be followed and the forms to be
used in arranging and making payment for treatment given under the
authority of these Regulations.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Civilian dental
practitioners who treat personnel of the Canadian Forces under the
authority of these Regulations shall be paid in accordance with the
scale of fees authorized from time to time for that type of dental
treatment when made in respect of persons under the care of the
Department of Veterans Affairs.

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Command Dental Officer may, if in his opinion it is in the interest of
  economy, employ a civilian practitioner on a [per diem]{.otherLang
  lang="la"} basis rather than on a fee basis.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The civilian dental practitioner shall receive
  payment at the rates prescribed from time to time by the Treasury
  Board for civilian medical practitioners similarly employed by the
  Department of National Defence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No payment shall be made unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the civilian dental practitioner submits a signed
    certificate certifying the specific days of the month on which
    services were rendered and for which payment is claimed, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the officer who authorized the work certifies that
    the certificate is correct.
