[EXCISE ACT](/eng/acts/E-14)

Departmental Regulations Respecting Breweries
::::

::::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558084 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Brewery Departmental
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._566).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558086 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Act](/eng/acts/E-14);
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[light]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[light]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[near

[[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any container in which beer is
    placed for shipment, sale or consumption; ([paquet]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[process stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[process stock]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means beer in any process of
    manufacture prior to duty assessment; ([stock en voie de
    fabrication]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    brewery, a period not exceeding 24 consecutive hours during which
    beer is produced. ([journée de production]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Supervision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558096 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an excise officer shall be present in every brewery
  to supervise the following operations:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} gauging and recording the capacities of vessels and
    packages;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} determining the quantities of beer for duty
    assessment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} determining the quantities of duty paid beer
    returned to process stock for repackaging; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} destroying quantities of process stock.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An excise officer is not required to be present in a
  brewery to supervise the operations in subsection (1) where the
  operations are carried out in a manner approved by the Department and
  are subject to controls and accounting procedures acceptable to the
  Department.

## [Particulars to Be Recorded]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558104 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
31 of the Act, the particulars required to be recorded in every brewery
are as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantities of raw materials and packaging
  materials used, brought into or removed from the brewery;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each type of beer fermented,
  transferred to storage or otherwise disposed of;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of any material added to beer after
  fermentation;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each type of beer transferred to a
  tank from which it is to be packaged;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each type of beer packaged;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the details of disposition of the packaged beer;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} where beer has been exported from Canada, documentary
  evidence of the export; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the beer, any allowance made under
  subsection 170(2) of the Act, any drawback of excise duty granted and
  any excise duty credited to the brewer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-572, s. 1
:::

## [Computation of Duty]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558116 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The excise duty on beer
shall be charged and computed on the quantities of beer produced during
each production day and may be reduced by an amount equal to the amount
of duty imposed on

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} beer for which the brewer is entitled to a drawback
  pursuant to section 173 of the Act or a refund pursuant to subsection
  174(2) of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} beer returned to process stock.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/79-949, s. 2
- SOR/80-8, s. 2
- SOR/80-285, s. 2
- SOR/85-1161, s. 2
- SOR/91-129, s. 2
:::

## [Drawback]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558122 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A brewer shall be allowed a
drawback of the excise duty paid on beer that has been shipped for
export if he has made a declaration pursuant to subsection 173(2) of the
Act setting out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of beer exported;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the consignee; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the details of the documentary evidence of the
  export.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-380, s. 2
:::

## [Returns]{.HTitleText1} {#h-558129 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  return required by section 175 of the Act shall be made

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a licensed brewer authorized by the
    Minister to make returns for six-month periods under subsection
    36.1(2) of the Act, for each six-month period; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, for each month.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The return shall set out the following particulars:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of beer produced;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of beer exported;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of beer on which excise duty was paid
    that has been destroyed or returned to process stock; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the amount of excise duty paid on beer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-380, s. 2
- 2010, c. 25, s. 141
:::
