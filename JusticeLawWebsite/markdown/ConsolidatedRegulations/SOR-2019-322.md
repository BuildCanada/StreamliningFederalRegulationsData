## Critical Habitat of the Atlantic Salmon (Salmo salar) Inner Bay of Fundy Population Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-atlantic-salmon-salmo-salar-inner-bay-of-fundy-population-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2019-322
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2019-08-29

Critical Habitat of the Atlantic Salmon (Salmo salar) Inner Bay of Fundy
Population Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Atlantic Salmon ([Salmo salar]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Inner
Bay of Fundy population is a wildlife species that is listed as an
endangered species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBGA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a place
referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBEA} of that Act and, under
subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBEA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 58(5) of that Act, the Minister of
Fisheries and Oceans has consulted with the Minister responsible for the
Parks Canada Agency, namely the Minister of the Environment, with
respect to the annexed Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Atlantic Salmon ([Salmo salar]{.otherLang lang="la"})
Inner Bay of Fundy Population Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-322).

Ottawa, August 27, 2019

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Atlantic Salmon ([Salmo salar]{.otherLang lang="la"}) Inner Bay
of Fundy population --- which is identified in the recovery strategy for
that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry ---
other than the portion of that critical habitat that is in a place
referred to in subsection 58(2) of that Act, more specifically, in Fundy
National Park of Canada as described in Part 7 of Schedule 1 to the
[Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
