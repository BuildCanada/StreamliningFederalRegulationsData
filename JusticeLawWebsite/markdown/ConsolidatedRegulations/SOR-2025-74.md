[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Department of Employment and
    Social Development Act](/eng/acts/H-5.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who is the
    subject of a reconsideration decision of the Commission, or the
    employer of the claimant who is the subject of such a decision, who
    appeals that decision under section 113 of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6). ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6). ([prestataire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Employment Insurance
    Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6). ([employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an appellant, the Commission or
    any person added to the appeal under section
    16. ([partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reconsideration decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reconsideration decision]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a decision
    made by the Commission under section 112 of the [Employment
    Insurance Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6), including a decision in relation to
    further time to make a reconsideration request. ([décision de
    révision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})
::::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General principle

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations must be
interpreted and applied in a manner that ensures that the appeal process
is as simple and efficient as the principles of natural justice allow.
