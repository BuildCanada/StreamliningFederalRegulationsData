## Lethbridge Airport Zoning Regulations {#lethbridge-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 92
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Lethbridge Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-583158 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Lethbridge Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._92).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-583160 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Lethbridge Airport,
    Lethbridge, in the Province of Alberta; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane the lower end of which is a horizontal line at right angles to
    the centre line of a strip and passing through a point at the strip
    end on the centre line of the strip; ([surface
    d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    horizontal plane centering on and located 150 feet above the
    assigned elevation of the airport reference point; ([surface
    horizontale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, 1,200 feet in width, including the
    runway, especially prepared for the take-off and landing of aircraft
    in a particular direction; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending upward and outward from the outer lateral
    limits of a strip and its approach surface to an intersection with
    the horizontal surface or other transitional surfaces. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-401, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 3,002 feet
above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-583172 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, as more particularly described in Part II of
the schedule.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-583183 .scheduleLabel}

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

COMMENCING at the intersection of the centre lines of Runways 05-23 and
12-30 of Lethbridge Airport, THENCE, Northwesterly along the centre line
of Runway 12-30 a distance of One Thousand Six Hundred (1,600) feet to a
point; THENCE, at right angles Southwesterly a distance of One Thousand
Six Hundred (1,600) feet to a point henceforth designated as the airport
reference point, the said point having an assigned elevation of Three
Thousand and two (3,002) feet above sea level.

situate, lying and being in the Municipal District of Lethbridge No. 25
in the Province of Alberta and composed of part of Sections One (1),
Twelve (12), and Thirteen (13), Township Eight (8), Range Twenty-two
(22), West of the Fourth (4th) Meridian; the whole of Sections three
(3), Six (6), Seven (7), Ten (10), Fifteen (15), and Eighteen (18) and

outermost boundaries are described as the horizontal surface limits in

enclosed by the boundaries of the description are affected by these
