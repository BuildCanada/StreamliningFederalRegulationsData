## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700322 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Food and Drugs
      Act](/eng/acts/F-27). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[added sulphites]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[added sulphites]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one or more of the
      food additives that are set out item 21, column 1, of table 2 of
      the Common Names for Ingredients and Components Document and are
      present in a natural health product as a result of being added to
      the product. ([sulfites ajoutés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a noxious and
      unintended response to a natural health product that occurs at any
      dose used or tested for the diagnosis, treatment or prevention of
      a disease or for modifying an organic function. ([réaction
      indésirable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[brand name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[brand name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a name in English or
      French, whether or not it includes the name of a manufacturer,
      corporation, partnership or individual

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is used to distinguish the natural health
        product; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which a natural health product is sold or
        advertised. ([marque nominative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[case report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[case report]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a detailed record of all
      relevant data associated with the use of a natural health product
      in a subject. ([fiche d'observation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Common Names for Ingredients and Components Document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Common Names for Ingredients and Components
      Document]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document entitled Common
      Names for Ingredients and Components, prepared by the Canadian
      Food Inspection Agency and published on its website, as amended
      from time to time. ([document sur les noms usuels d'ingrédients et
      de constituants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Compendium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Compendium]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Compendium of
      Monographs published by the Department of Health and as amended
      from time to time. ([Compendium]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[distributor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who sells a
      natural health product to another person for the purpose of
      further sale by that other
      person. ([distributeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[expiry date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[expiry date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the earlier of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, expressed at minimum as a year and
        month, that is the end of the stability period determined under
        section 52; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date, expressed at minimum as a year and
        month, after which the manufacturer recommends that the natural
        health product should not be used. ([date limite
        d'utilisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[food allergen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[food allergen]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any protein from any
      of the following foods, or any modified protein, including any
      protein fraction, that is derived from any of the following foods:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} almonds, Brazil nuts, cashews, hazelnuts,
        macadamia nuts, pecans, pine nuts, pistachios or walnuts;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} peanuts;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sesame seeds;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} wheat or triticale;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} eggs;

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} milk;

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} soybeans;

      - [(h)]{.lawlabel} crustaceans;

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} molluscs;

      - [(j)]{.lawlabel} fish; or

      - [(k)]{.lawlabel} mustard seeds. ([allergène
        alimentaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[food allergen source, gluten source and added sulphites statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[food allergen source, gluten source and added sulphites
      statement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a statement appearing on the
      label of a natural health product that indicates the source of a
      food allergen or gluten that is present in the product or the
      presence in the product of added sulphites in a total amount of
      10 p.p.m. or more. ([mention des sources d'allergènes alimentaires
      ou de gluten et des sulfites ajoutés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[gluten]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[gluten]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any gluten protein from the grain of any of the
        following cereals or from the grain of a hybridized strain that
        is created from at least one of the following cereals:

        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} barley,

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} oats,

        - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} rye,

        - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} triticale,

        - [(v)]{.lawlabel} wheat; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any modified gluten protein, including any
        gluten protein fraction, that is derived from the grain of any
        of the cereals referred to in paragraph (a) or from the grain of
        a hybridized strain referred to in that
        paragraph. ([gluten]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[immediate container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the container
      that is in direct contact with a natural health
      product. ([contenant immédiat]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who imports a
      natural health product into Canada for the purpose of
      sale. ([importateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[inner label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[inner label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the label on or affixed
      to an immediate container of a natural health product. ([étiquette
      intérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[List A]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[List A]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the document entitled List of
      Certain Natural Health Products for Distribution as Samples, dated
      March 2020 and published by the Government of Canada on its
      website. ([Liste A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[lot number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[lot number]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any combination of
      letters, figures, or both, by which a natural health product can
      be traced in manufacture and identified in distribution. ([numéro
      de lot]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who fabricates
      or processes a natural health product for the purpose of sale, but
      does not include a pharmacist or other health care practitioner
      who, at the request of a patient, compounds a natural health
      product for the purpose of sale to that
      patient. ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance
      set out in Schedule 1 or a combination of substances in which all
      the medicinal ingredients are substances set out in Schedule 1, a
      homeopathic medicine or a traditional medicine, that is
      manufactured, sold or represented for use in

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the diagnosis, treatment, mitigation or
        prevention of a disease, disorder or abnormal physical state or
        its symptoms in humans;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} restoring or correcting organic functions in
        humans; or

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} modifying organic functions in humans.

      However, a natural health product does not include a substance set
      out in Schedule 2, any combination of substances that includes a
      substance set out in Schedule 2 or a homeopathic medicine or a
      traditional medicine that is or includes a substance set out in
      Schedule 2. ([produit de santé naturel]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[outer label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the label on or affixed
      to the outside of a package, other than the immediate container,
      of a natural health product. ([étiquette
      extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the unit of measurement for
      type size that is known as a PostScript point and is equal to
      0.3527777778 mm. ([point]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[principal display panel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in the [Consumer Packaging and Labelling
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._417). ([espace
      principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[probiotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[probiotic]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a monoculture or
      mixed-culture of live micro-organisms that benefit the microbiota
      indigenous to humans. ([probiotique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[proper name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[proper name]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of an
      ingredient of a natural health product, one of the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the ingredient is a vitamin, the name for
        that vitamin set out in item 3 of Schedule 1;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the ingredient is a plant or a plant
        material, an alga, a bacterium, a fungus, a non-human animal
        material or a probiotic, the Latin nomenclature of its genus
        and, if any, its specific epithet; and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the ingredient is other than one described
        in paragraphs (a) or (b), the chemical name of the
        ingredient. ([nom propre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[recommended conditions of use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[recommended conditions of use]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
      respect of a natural health product,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its recommended use or purpose;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its dosage form;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} its recommended route of administration;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its recommended dose;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} its recommended duration of use, if any; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} its risk information, including any cautions,
        warnings, contra-indications or known adverse reactions
        associated with its use. ([conditions d'utilisation
        recommandées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[security package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[security package]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a package having a
      security feature that provides reasonable assurance to consumers
      that the package has not been opened prior to
      purchase. ([emballage de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serious adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a noxious
      and unintended response to a natural health product that occurs at
      any dose and that requires in-patient hospitalization or a
      prolongation of existing hospitalization, that causes congenital
      malformation, that results in persistent or significant disability
      or incapacity, that is life threatening or that results in
      death. ([réaction indésirable grave]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[serious unexpected adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[serious unexpected adverse reaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a serious adverse reaction that is not identified in nature,
      severity or frequency in the risk information set out on the label
      of the natural health product. ([réaction indésirable grave et
      imprévue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[specifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[specifications]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a description of a
      natural health product that contains the information described in
      subsection 44(2). ([spécifications]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), the words and expressions
  used in the provisions of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) that are incorporated by
  reference by these Regulations shall have the meanings assigned to
  them by these Regulations, but if no meanings are assigned, they shall
  have any meaning assigned to them by the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The word [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
  the provisions of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) that are incorporated by
  reference by these Regulations shall have the meaning assigned to it
  by the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700368 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sale of natural health products;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the manufacture, packaging, labelling and
    importation for sale of natural health products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the distribution of natural health products; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the storage of natural health products for the
    purposes of any of the activities referred to in paragraphs (b) and
    (c).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a substance or
  combination of substances or a traditional medicine is not considered
  to be a natural health product if its sale, under the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), is required to be
  pursuant to a prescription when it is sold other than in accordance
  with section C.01.043 of those Regulations.

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except where otherwise
indicated in these Regulations, the provisions of the [Food and Drug
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) do not apply to natural
health products.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Product Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700379 .Part}

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700380 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), no person shall sell a natural health
  product unless a product licence is issued in respect of the natural
  health product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No product licence holder, manufacturer, importer or
  distributor of a natural health product for which a product licence is
  issued shall sell the natural health product during any period that
  the sale of that natural health product is directed to be stopped
  under section 17.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell a natural health product for
  which a product licence is issued

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period of any suspension of the licence
    under section 18 or 19; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after cancellation of the licence under
    paragraph 20(b).

### [Licence Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700387 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
product licence shall be submitted to the Minister and shall contain the
following information and documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and telephone number, and if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of the
  applicant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the address submitted under paragraph (a) is not a
  Canadian address, the name, address and telephone number, and if
  applicable, the facsimile number and electronic mail address of the
  applicant's representative in Canada to whom notices may be sent;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each medicinal ingredient of the natural health
  product,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its proper name and its common name,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its quantity per dosage unit,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} its potency, if a representation relating to its
    potency is to be shown on any label of the natural health product,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a description of its source material, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a statement indicating whether it is synthetically
    manufactured;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a qualitative list of the non-medicinal ingredients
  that are proposed for the natural health product and for each
  ingredient listed, a statement that indicates the purpose of the
  ingredient;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} each brand name under which the natural health
  product is proposed to be sold;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the recommended conditions of use for the natural
  health product;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} information that demonstrates the safety and efficacy
  of the natural health product when it is used in accordance with the
  recommended conditions of use;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the text of each label that is proposed to be used in
  conjunction with the natural health product;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the specifications to which the natural
  health product will comply; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} one of the following attestations, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the natural health product is imported, an
    attestation by the applicant that the natural health product will be
    manufactured, packaged, labelled, imported, distributed and stored
    in accordance with the requirements set out in Part 3 or in
    accordance with requirements that are equivalent to those set out in
    Part 3, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the natural health product is not imported, an
    attestation by the applicant that the natural health product will be
    manufactured, packaged, labelled, distributed and stored in
    accordance with requirements set out in Part 3.

### [Sixty-Day Disposition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700408 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the Minister shall dispose of an application
  submitted under section 5 within 60 days after the day on which it is
  submitted if, in support of the application, the only information
  submitted by the applicant under paragraph 5(g) is that which is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application respecting a natural
    health product that has only one medicinal ingredient, contained in
    a monograph for that medicinal ingredient in the Compendium; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application respecting a natural
    health product that has more than one medicinal ingredient,
    contained in a monograph for that combination of medicinal
    ingredients in the Compendium.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Minister requests that additional information
  or samples be submitted under section 15, the 60-day period referred
  to in subsection (1) does not include the number of days beginning on
  the day on which the request is made and ending on the day on which
  the additional information or samples are received.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, the Minister
  disposes of an application on the earlier of the day on which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licence is issued in accordance with section 7;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is sent a notice under
    subsection 9(1).

### [Issuance and Amendment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700417 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall issue or
amend a product licence if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant submits an application to the Minister
  that is in accordance with section 5 or subsection 11(2), as the case
  may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant submits to the Minister all additional
  information or samples requested under section 15;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not make a false or misleading
  statement in the application; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance or amendment of the licence, as the case
  may be, is not likely to result in injury to the health of a purchaser
  or consumer.

### [Product Number]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700423 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall assign a product number to each natural health product
  in respect of which a product licence is issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a natural health product that is a
  drug for which a drug identification number is assigned in accordance
  with subsection C.01.014.2(1) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), the product number
  assigned under subsection (1) shall be the drug identification number.

### [Refusal to Issue or Amend]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700429 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister refuses to issue or amend a product licence, the Minister
  shall send the applicant a notice that sets out the reason for the
  refusal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice shall indicate that the applicant may,
  within 30 days after the date of the notice, request that the Minister
  reconsider the application.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant makes a request in accordance with
  subsection (2), the Minister shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} give the applicant an opportunity to be heard in
    respect of the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reconsider the application after giving the
    applicant that opportunity.

### [Amendment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700439 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  licensee makes any of the following changes in respect of the natural
  health product, the licensee shall not sell any lot or batch of the
  natural health product affected by the change unless the product
  licence is amended accordingly:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to its recommended dose;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change to its recommended duration of use;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the deletion or modification of risk information
    shown on any of its labels, including the deletion or modification
    of a caution, warning, contra-indication or known adverse reaction
    associated with its use;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a change of its recommended use or purpose;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change of the source material of any of its
    medicinal ingredients;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} changing any of its medicinal ingredients to or
    from being synthetically manufactured;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a change to the potency of any of its medicinal
    ingredients;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a change affecting its safety or efficacy that does
    not arise as a result of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a change to the quantity of a medicinal
      ingredient per dosage unit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the addition or substitution of a medicinal
      ingredient,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a change to its dosage form, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a change to its recommended route of
      administration; or

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} one or more of the following changes to its
    specifications, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of a test method set out in the
      specifications,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the modification of a test method set out in the
      specifications in a manner that widens the purity tolerances of
      the natural health product or the quantity, identity or potency
      tolerances of any of its medicinal ingredients, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the modification of a test method set out in
      the specifications in a manner that renders it less precise,
      accurate, specific or sensitive.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application to amend a product licence shall be
  submitted to the Minister and shall contain the following information
  and documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the product number of the natural health product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a statement identifying each change described in
    subsection (1) that has been made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the natural health
    product is safe and efficacious after the change;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the text of each label to be used in conjunction
    with the natural health product after the change, if the change is
    any of those described in paragraphs (1)(a) to (h); and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the revised specifications, if the change
    is any of those described in paragraph (1)(g) or (i).

### [Notification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700464 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  licensee makes any of the changes described in subsection (2) in
  respect of the natural health product, the licensee shall, within
  60 days after the day on which the change is made,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister of the change; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister with the text of each label
    used in conjunction with the natural health product since the
    change, if the change is any of those described in paragraphs (2)(d)
    to (f).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), changes in
  respect of a natural health product are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to any of the information submitted under
    paragraph 5(a) or (b);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a change to any of the information provided under
    section 22;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the addition or substitution of a non-medicinal
    ingredient, the addition or substitution of which does not affect
    its safety or efficacy;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} its sale under a brand name other than one
    submitted under paragraph 5(e);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a change of the common or proper name of any of its
    medicinal ingredients; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the addition of risk information to any of its
    labels, including the addition of a caution, warning,
    contra-indication or known adverse reaction associated with its use.

### [Fundamental Change]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700476 .Subheading}

of the natural health product, the licensee shall not sell the natural
health product affected by the change unless a product licence is issued
in accordance with section 7 for the natural health product as changed:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to the quantity of a medicinal ingredient
  per dosage unit;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the addition or substitution of a medicinal
  ingredient;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a change to its dosage form; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a change to its recommended route of administration.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Clinical Trials Involving Human Subjects]{.HTitleText1} {#h-700807 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700808 .Subheading}

**[[63]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-63 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[adverse event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[adverse event]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any adverse occurrence
    in the health of a clinical trial subject who is administered a
    natural health product, that may or may not be caused by the
    administration of the natural health product, and includes an
    adverse reaction, a serious adverse reaction and a serious
    unexpected adverse reaction. ([incident
    thérapeutique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clinical trial]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an investigation in
    respect of a natural health product that involves human subjects and
    that is intended to discover or verify its clinical, pharmacological
    or pharmacodynamic effects, to identify any adverse events that are
    related to its use, to study its absorption, distribution,
    metabolism and excretion, or to ascertain its safety or
    efficacy. ([essai clinique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[good clinical practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[good clinical practices]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means generally
    accepted clinical practices that are designed to ensure the
    protection of the rights, safety and well-being of clinical trial
    subjects and other persons, and the good clinical practices referred
    to in section 74. ([bonnes pratiques cliniques]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[import]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means to import a natural health
    product into Canada for the purpose of sale in a clinical
    trial. ([importer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investigator's brochure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investigator's brochure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document
    containing the preclinical and clinical information in respect of
    the natural health product that is described in paragraph
    66(e). ([brochure du chercheur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protocol]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a document that describes the
    objectives, design, methodology, statistical considerations and
    organization of a clinical trial. ([protocole]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualified investigator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person
    responsible to the sponsor for the conduct of the clinical trial at
    a clinical trial site, who is entitled to provide health care under
    the laws of the province where the clinical trial site is located
    and who is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a clinical trial respecting a
      natural health product to be used for dental purposes only, a
      physician or dentist and a member in good standing of a
      professional medical or dental association; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, a physician and a member in
      good standing of a professional medical association. ([chercheur
      qualifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[research ethics board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[research ethics board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a body that is
    not affiliated with the sponsor, and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the principal mandate of which is to approve the
      initiation of, and conduct periodic reviews of, biomedical
      research involving human subjects in order to ensure the
      protection of their rights, safety and well-being; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that has at least five members, that has a
      majority of members who are Canadian citizens or permanent
      residents under the Immigration Act, that is composed of both men
      and women and that includes at least

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} two members whose primary experience and
        expertise are in a scientific discipline, who have broad
        experience in the methods and areas of research to be approved
        and one of whom is from a medical discipline or, if the clinical
        trial is in respect of a natural health product to be used for
        dental purposes only, is from a medical or dental discipline,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one member knowledgeable in complementary or
        alternative health care,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} one member knowledgeable in ethics,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} one member knowledgeable in Canadian laws
        relevant to the research to be approved,

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} one member whose primary experience and
        expertise are in a non-scientific discipline, and

      - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} one member who is from the community or is a
        representative of an organization interested in the areas of
        research to be approved and who is not affiliated with the
        sponsor or the site where the clinical trial is to be
        conducted. ([comité d'éthique de la recherche]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sponsor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, corporate body,
    institution or organization that conducts a clinical
    trial. ([promoteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700831 .Subheading}

- **[[64]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-64
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), this Part
  applies to the sale or importation of natural health products to be
  used for the purposes of clinical trials involving human subjects.

  section 68, paragraphs 74(a) to (i), subsections 75(1) and 76(1) and

### [Prohibition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700835 .Subheading}

- **[[65]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-65
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite section 4 and subject to
  subsection (2), no person shall sell or import a natural health
  product for the purposes of a clinical trial unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized under this Part;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person complies with this Part and section
    C.01.064 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the natural health product is to be imported,
    the person has a representative in Canada who is responsible for the
    sale of the natural health product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell a natural health product for the
  purposes of a clinical trial

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} during the period of any suspension of the
    authorization under section 80 or 81; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} after cancellation of the authorization under

### [Application for Authorization]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700844 .Subheading}

**[[66]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-66 .sectionLabel}** An application by a

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the protocol for the clinical trial;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the statement, as it will be set out in

  benefits arising to the health of clinical trial subjects as a result
  of their participation in the clinical trial;

  sponsor, containing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the title of the protocol and the clinical trial

    product,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for each medicinal ingredient of the natural
    health product

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the ingredient per dosage unit of

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the dosage form of the natural health product,

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the name, address and
    telephone number and, if applicable, the facsimile number and
    electronic mail address of the research ethics board that approved

    consent form containing the statement referred to in paragraph (b),

  - [(xi)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the name, address

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacokinetics of the natural health product

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the toxicological effects, if any, in any animal

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the results of carcinogenicity studies in any
    animal species tested in respect of the natural health product, if
    any,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the results of clinical pharmacokinetic studies
    of the natural health product, if any,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} the information regarding natural health product
    safety, pharmacodynamics, efficacy and dose responses of the natural
    health product that were obtained from previous clinical trials in
    humans, if any,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the known contra-indications for and the
    precautions to be taken in respect of the natural health product,
    and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the recommended treatment in the event of an
    overdose of the natural health product, if any; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date for the commencement of the
  clinical trial at each clinical trial site.

### [Sponsor's Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-700940 .Subheading}

#### [Good Clinical Practices]{.HTitleText3} {#h-700941 .Subheading}

**[[74]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-74 .sectionLabel}** Every sponsor shall
ensure that a clinical trial is conducted in accordance with good
clinical practices and, without limiting the generality of the
foregoing, shall ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial is scientifically sound and
  clearly described in a protocol;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the clinical trial is conducted, and the natural
  health product is used, in accordance with the protocol and this Part;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} systems and procedures that assure the quality of
  every aspect of the clinical trial are implemented;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, the approval of a
  research ethics board is obtained before the clinical trial begins at
  the site;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} at each clinical trial site, there is no more than
  one qualified investigator;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} at each clinical trial site, medical care and medical
  decisions, in respect of the clinical trial, are under the supervision
  of the qualified investigator;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} each individual involved in the conduct of the
  clinical trial is qualified by education, training and experience to
  perform his or her respective tasks;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} written informed consent, given in accordance with
  the applicable laws governing consent, is obtained from every person
  before that person participates in the clinical trial but only after
  that person has been informed of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the risks and anticipated benefits to his or her
    health arising from participation in the clinical trial, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all other aspects of the clinical trial that are
    necessary for that person to make the decision to participate in the
    clinical trial;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the requirements respecting information and records
  set out in section 76 are met; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the natural health product is manufactured and stored
  in accordance with the requirements set out in Part 3 except for
  section 61.

#### [Records]{.HTitleText3} {#h-700969 .Subheading}

- **[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  sponsor shall record, handle and store all information in respect of a

  reporting as well as its interpretation and verification.

  records to demonstrate that the clinical trial is conducted in
  accordance with good clinical practices and these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The sponsor shall maintain complete and accurate

  clinical trial, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of all versions of the investigator's

    use and purpose of the natural health product at the time of the
    adverse event;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} records respecting the enrolment of clinical trial

    trial subjects to be identified and contacted in the event that the
    sale of the natural health product may endanger the health of the
    clinical trial subjects or other persons;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} records respecting the shipment, receipt,
    disposition, return and destruction of the natural health product;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, an undertaking from

    investigator prior to the commencement of his or her

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the qualified investigator will immediately, on

      entirety or at a clinical trial site, notify both the clinical
      trial subjects and the research ethics board of the
      discontinuance, provide them with the reasons for the
      discontinuance and advise them in writing of any potential risks
      to the health of clinical trial subjects or other persons;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, a copy of the
    protocol, informed consent form and any amendment to the protocol or
    informed consent form that have been approved by the research ethics

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial site, an attestation,

    trial site, stating that it has reviewed and approved the protocol

    functions in a manner consistent with good clinical practices; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for each clinical trial referred to in an
    application submitted under section 66 or subsection 71(3), the
    information referred to in subparagraph 66(c)(ix) and paragraph
    66(f).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The sponsor shall maintain all records referred to in
  this Part for a period of 15 years.

- **[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During

#### [Discontinuance of a Clinical Trial]{.HTitleText3} {#h-701001 .Subheading}

- **[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  sponsor discontinues a clinical trial in its entirety or at a clinical
  trial site, the sponsor shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notify the Minister of the discontinuance within
    15 days after the day of the discontinuance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Minister with the reason for the
    discontinuance and its impact on the proposed or ongoing clinical
    trials in respect of the natural health product conducted in Canada
    by the sponsor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} as soon as possible, notify all qualified
    investigators of the discontinuance and of the reasons for the
    discontinuance, and advise them in writing of any potential risks to
    the health of clinical trial subjects or other persons; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of each discontinued clinical trial
    site, stop the sale or importation of the natural health product as
    of the day of the discontinuance and take all reasonable measures to
    ensure the recovery of all unused quantities of the natural health
    product that have been sold.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the sponsor discontinues a clinical trial in its
  entirety or at a clinical trial site, the sponsor may resume selling
  or importing the natural health product for the purposes of the
  clinical trial in its entirety or at the clinical trial site if, in
  respect of each clinical trial site where the sale or importation is
  to be resumed, the sponsor submits to the Minister the information
  referred to in subparagraphs 66(c)(ix) to (xi) and paragraphs 66(d)
  and (f).

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the natural health product meets the requirements
    of these Regulations.

  [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} have the same meaning as in
  subsection C.01.001(1) of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

subsection 103.4(1), receives an order for and distributes or causes to
be distributed a natural health product as a sample shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain records showing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name, address and description of each person to

**[[103.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-103.6 .sectionLabel}** A person may

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in a month that follows the month in which it is

  these Regulations.

## RELATED PROVISIONS {#related-provisions .scheduleTitleText}

- --- SOR/2022-146, s. 24

  - - [24]{.lawlabel} [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply
      in this section.

      [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Natural
          Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196) as
          they read immediately before the day on which sections 17 to
          22 of these Regulations come into force. ([ancien
          règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[natural health product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
          meaning as in subsection 1(1) of the [Natural Health Products
          Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([produit de
          santé naturel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

      [[new Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

      :   [[new Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Natural
          Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196) as
          they read on the day on which sections 17 to 22 of these
          Regulations come into force. ([nouveau
          règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    - [(2)]{.lawlabel} A natural health product for which a product
      licence has been issued under section 7 of the former Regulations
      is not required, within three years after the day on which
      sections 17 to 22 of these Regulations come into force, to be
      labelled in accordance with the new Regulations, if the product is
      labelled in accordance with the former Regulations.

    - [(3)]{.lawlabel} If, during the three-year period referred to in
      subsection (2), a person that holds a product licence under the
      former Regulations in respect of a natural health product referred
      to in that subsection makes any modification of risk information
      referred to in paragraph 11(1)(c) of the former Regulations in
      respect of the product for the purpose of complying with the new
      Regulations,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} section 11 of the former Regulations does not
        apply in respect of the modification of risk information; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the modification is to be considered as a
        change to which section 12 of the former Regulations applies.
