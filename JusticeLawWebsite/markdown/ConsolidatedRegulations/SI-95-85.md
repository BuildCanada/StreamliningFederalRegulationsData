## Order Transferring to the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food the Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Public Service known as the Retail Food Program of the Department of Industry {#order-transferring-to-the-department-of-agriculture-and-agri-food-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-portion-of-the-public-service-known-as-the-retail-food-program-of-the-department-of-industry .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-85
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 1995-08-09

Order Transferring to the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food the
Control and Supervision of the Portion of the Public Service known as
the Retail Food Program of the Department of Industry

P.C. 1995-1245  1995-07-26
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), is pleased
hereby to transfer to the Department of Agriculture and Agri-Food the
control and supervision of the portion of the public service in the
Department of Industry known as the Retail Food Program.
