## Prairie Farm Rehabilitation Administration Employees Regulations {#prairie-farm-rehabilitation-administration-employees-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-442
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 1978-05-12

Regulations Respecting the Application of the Public Service Employment
act to Employees of the Prairie Farm Rehabilitation Administration

P.C. 1978-1589 1978-05-11
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Regional Economic Expansion, pursuant to
subsection 34(1) of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
[Regulations respecting the application of the Public Service Employment
Act to employees of the Prairie Farm Rehabilitation
Administration](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-442).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862231 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Prairie Farm Rehabilitation Administration Employees
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-442).

## [Application of the Public Service Employment Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-862233 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding
subsection 6(2) of the [Prairie Farm Rehabilitation
Act](/eng/acts/P-17), the provisions of the [Public Service Employment
Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) apply to all officers and employees, other than
seasonal or term employees, required for the carrying out of the
[Prairie Farm Rehabilitation Act](/eng/acts/P-17).
