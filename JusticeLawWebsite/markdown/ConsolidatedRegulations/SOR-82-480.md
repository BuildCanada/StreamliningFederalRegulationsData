## Charter Flights Airport Facilities Reservation Regulations {#charter-flights-airport-facilities-reservation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-480
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1982-05-07

Regulations Respecting Reservations for Airport Apron and Terminal
Facilities for Passenger Charter Flights

P.C. 1982-1379 1982-05-06
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 6(1) of the
[Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to approve the
annexed [Regulations respecting reservations for airport apron and
terminal facilities for passenger charter
flights](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-480) made by the Minister of Transport.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873766 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Charter Flights Airport Facilities Reservation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-480).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873768 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[air carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who operates a
    commercial air service; ([transporteur aérien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport manager]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person in charge
    of an airport or the authorized representative of that person;
    ([directeur d'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an air carrier who applies
    for a reservation referred to in section 4;
    ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[regular charter flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regular charter flight]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a passenger
    charter flight that is operated by an air carrier on a program basis
    and for which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} schedules are provided six months in advance in
      respect of international flights and three months in advance in
      respect of domestic flights to the airport manager of each airport
      listed in the schedule that the air carrier uses for the purposes
      of the flight, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the apron and terminal facilities of each airport
      are reserved through established scheduling procedures; ([vol
      d'affrètement régulier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[reservation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reservation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a reservation of the apron
    and terminal facilities of an airport listed in the schedule.
    ([réservation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-251, s. 2
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873779 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of a passenger charter flight that is operated during
the times, if any, specified in the current Notice to Airmen (NOTAM)
issued by the Department of Transport, Civil Aeronautics Branch.

## [Reservations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-873781 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  air carrier shall, before operating a passenger charter flight, other
  than a regular charter flight, with an aircraft that has a maximum
  certified take-off weight of 35,000 pounds or over, apply to the
  airport manager of each airport listed in the schedule that will be
  used for the purposes of the flight to reserve the airport apron and
  terminal facilities needed to accommodate the arrival or departure of
  the flight.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An air carrier shall apply for a reservation by
  submitting an application therefor to each airport manager

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least 30 days before the date of the proposed
    flight; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where a permit in respect of the proposed flight is
    issued under the Air Carrier Regulations less than 30 days before
    that date, as soon as possible following the receipt by the air
    carrier of the permit.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application referred to in subsection (2) shall
  be made in writing and shall contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the applicant;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of a responsible officer or
    agent of the applicant in Canada to whom notices may be sent;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the identification number or name of the proposed
    flight;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the estimated number of passengers on the proposed
    flight;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the type of aircraft that will be used for the
    proposed flight;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the origin and destination of the proposed flight
    including intermediate stops; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date and time of arrival and date and
    time of departure of the aircraft at the airport.

<!-- -->

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  airport manager who receives an application for a reservation shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} make the reservation at the time requested by the
    applicant if, taking into account all other prearranged aircraft and
    passenger activities and the facilities and services available at
    the airport, the airport is capable of accommodating the proposed
    flight, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} reject the application, if the airport is not
    capable of accommodating the proposed flight

  and notify the applicant in writing in respect of the making of the
  reservation or rejection of the application not later than two working
  days after the day he receives the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an airport manager rejects an application under
  subsection (1), he shall, at the same time that he notifies the
  applicant pursuant to that subsection, propose an alternate time or
  times for the reservation and the applicant shall reply promptly to
  the proposal so that a reservation may be made as soon as possible.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every air carrier shall
schedule the arrival and departure of an aircraft in respect of which
the air carrier has obtained a reservation at the time specified in the
reservation.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where an air carrier
obtains a reservation and subsequently determines that the time
specified in the reservation cannot be met, the air carrier shall
forthwith notify the airport manager who made the reservation and, where
the proposed flight is to be re-scheduled to arrive at or depart from an
airport listed in the schedule at another time, the air carrier shall
apply for a new reservation.
