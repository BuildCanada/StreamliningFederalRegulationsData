## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1494497 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[components and accessories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[components and accessories]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means items
      that are intended to be used with a tent, such as side panels,
      curtains, footprints, flies and gear lofts, whether they are sold
      with the tent or separately, but does not include the following:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} packaging such as bags for tents, poles or
        stakes;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} threads, zippers, ropes, hook and loop
        fasteners and webbing;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} labels and logos with a surface area of less
        than or equal to 1 000 cm^2^, calculated in a manner set out in
        Annex A of the safety standard; and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} fabric and other pliable material with a
        surface area less than or equal to 1 000 cm^2^. ([composants et
        accessoires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[safety standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[safety standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian General
      Standards Board standard CAN/CGSB-182.1-2020, entitled
      Flammability and labelling requirements for tents, as amended from
      time to time. ([norme de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[tent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[tent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a structure that meets the
      following conditions:

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is portable;

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is intended to shelter persons from outdoor
        environmental elements such as precipitation, sun, wind or
        insects;

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} it is made, in whole or in part, of fabric or
        other pliable material;

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} it has a top;

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} it has at least one side that constrains
        egress; and

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} it is not subject to the National Building Code
        of Canada 2020, published by the Canadian Commission on Building
        and Fire Codes, National Research Council of
        Canada. ([tente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference in the safety standard

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, a reference to
  "tent" in the safety standard has the same meaning as in subsection
  (1).

## [Specifications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1494516 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Performance and testing requirements

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  tested in accordance with the safety standard, fabric and other
  pliable material of a tent or of its components and accessories must
  meet the requirements set out in section 5.2.1 of the safety standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Flooring materials

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When tested in accordance with the safety standard,
  flooring materials of a tent, as defined in section 4.3 of the safety
  standard, that do not meet the requirements set out in section 5.2.1
  of that standard must meet the requirements set out in section 5.3.1
  of that standard.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Labelling requirements

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A tent
  must meet the labelling requirements set out in section 6 of the
  safety standard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Children's tent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a reference to
  "children's tent" in the safety standard means a tent that is intended
  to be used by a child under 14 years of age and that is not intended
  to be used with a cooking or heating device.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appliance

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, a reference to
  "appliance" in the safety standard means a cooking or heating device.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance period

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 2 and 3, a
tent that meets a requirement referred to in those sections, as it read
immediately before the day on which a new version of the safety standard
is published, may continue to meet that requirement:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the manufacture or import of the tent,
  for a period of one year that begins on that day; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the advertisement or sale of the tent,
  for a period of two years that begins on that day.

## [Transitional Provision]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1494545 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consumer products deemed to meet
applicable requirements

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A consumer product referred
to in the [Tents Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2024-217) or the
[Toys Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-17), as those Regulations
read immediately before the day on which these Regulations come into
force, that, immediately before that day, met the applicable
requirements for that consumer product is deemed to meet the applicable
requirements for that consumer product under these Regulations for a
period of two years, beginning on the day on which this section comes
into force.
