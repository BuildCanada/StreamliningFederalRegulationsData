## Imported Demonstration Aircraft Remission Order {#imported-demonstration-aircraft-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 771
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting Remission of the Tax Imposed Under Division III of Part
IX of the Excise Tax Act on Imported Aircraft Used for Demonstration to
Prospective Customers
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561854 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Imported Demonstration Aircraft Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._771).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561856 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes helicopters;
    ([aéronef]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[demonstration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[demonstration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the act of showing,
    displaying or exhibiting an aircraft in Canada for the purpose of
    explaining or proving its qualities and capabilities to prospective
    customers; ([démonstration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Deputy Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Deputy Minister of
    National Revenue for Customs and Excise;
    ([sous-ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, a person who
      sojourns in Canada in the year in which he imports an aircraft for
      demonstration for a period of, or periods the aggregate of which
      is, 183 days or more, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation, a corporation
      incorporated in Canada. ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of the Tax Imposed Under Division III of Part IX of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561865 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the tax paid or payable pursuant to Division III of Part IX of the
Act in respect of aircraft imported into Canada after December 31, 1990
for demonstration if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer is a resident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the importer certifies, at the time the aircraft is
  imported, that the aircraft in respect of which the remission is
  requested will be used solely for the purpose of demonstration;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the importer keeps complete records of the aircraft's
  use and makes those records available for inspection when requested by
  the officers of the Department of National Revenue, Customs and
  Excise; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the Deputy Minister determines that the
  importer has ceased to use the aircraft for demonstration, the
  aircraft is exported from Canada.
