## Concentration of Phosphorus in Certain Cleaning Products Regulations {#concentration-of-phosphorus-in-certain-cleaning-products-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-501
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 1989-10-19

Concentration of Phosphorus in Certain Cleaning Products Regulations

P.C. 1989-2098 1989-10-19
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsection 50(2) of the
Canadian Environmental Protection Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased
hereby to revoke the Phosphorus Concentration Control Regulations,
C.R.C., c. 393, and to make the annexed Regulations respecting the
concentration of phosphorus in laundry detergents, in substitution
therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^ \*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1988, c. 22
  :::
:::

## [Non-application of Section 117 of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1122449 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Section 117 of the
[Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) does
not apply to a cleaning product that is in transit through Canada, from
a place outside Canada to another place outside Canada.

::: HistoricalNote

## [Prescribed Nutrients]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920513 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 118(1)(a) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31), phosphorus and its compounds thereof are
nutrients.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2000-106, s. 1
:::

## [Laundry Detergents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920517 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of
phosphorus in any household laundry detergent must not exceed 1.1% by
weight expressed as phosphorus pentoxide or 0.5% by weight expressed as
elemental phosphorus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of
phosphorus in any commercial or industrial laundry detergent must not
exceed 5% by weight expressed as phosphorus pentoxide or 2.2% by weight
expressed as elemental phosphorus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3
:::

## [Household Dish-washing Compounds]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920524 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of
phosphorus in any household dish-washing compound must not exceed 1.1%
by weight expressed as phosphorus pentoxide or 0.5% by weight expressed
as elemental phosphorus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3
:::

## [Household Cleaners]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920528 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The concentration of
phosphorus in any household cleaner, other than a laundry detergent,
dish-washing compound, metal cleaner or de-greasing compound, must not
exceed 1.1% by weight expressed as phosphorus pentoxide or 0.5% by
weight expressed as elemental phosphorus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3
:::

## [Concentration of Phosphorus]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920532 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Any analysis performed to
determine the concentration of phosphorus for the purposes of these
Regulations must be performed by a laboratory that meets the following
conditions at the time of the analysis:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
    Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
    requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
    laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
    International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
    Recognition Arrangement, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environment Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
    and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the scope of its accreditation includes the analysis
  performed to determine the concentration of phosphorus.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3

## [Record Keeping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920536 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person who, during any year, manufactures for use or sale in Canada or
  imports any laundry detergent, household dish-washing compound or
  household cleaner within the meaning of section 6 containing
  phosphorus must maintain records containing the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the manufacture for use or sale

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the brand name of each laundry detergent,
      household dish-washing compound or household cleaner containing
      phosphorus,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of the principal
      place of business of the manufacturer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each of the cleaning products
      shipped from each manufacturing plant; and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of the recipient of
      each cleaning product that was shipped or sold;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the import

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the brand name of each laundry detergent,
      household dish-washing compound or household cleaner containing
      phosphorus,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of the principal
      place of business of the importer,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the port of entry where each cleaning product
      is imported,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name and civic address of the sender of each
      cleaning product,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the date of import,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding
      System number for the cleaning product that is imported, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the importer number for the shipment of the
      cleaning product that is imported.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records must be kept for a period of five years
  after the day on which the records are made, at the person's principal
  place of business in Canada or at any other place in Canada where they
  can be inspected. If the records are kept at any place other than the
  person's principal place of business, the person must provide the
  Minister with the civic address of the place where they are kept.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-178, s. 3
