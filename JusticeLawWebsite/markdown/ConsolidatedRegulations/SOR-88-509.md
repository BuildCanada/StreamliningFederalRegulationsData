## Brandon Airport Zoning Regulations {#brandon-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/88-509
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1988-09-29

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Brandon Airport

P.C. 1988-2250 1988-09-29
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 4.5[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), a copy of the proposed Zoning Regulations
respecting Brandon Airport, substantially in the form set out in the
schedule hereto, was published in two successive issues of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on August 22nd and 29th,
1987, and in two successive issues of the Brandon Sun on October 7th and
8th, 1987, and a reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to
interested persons to make representations to the Minister of Transport
with respect thereto;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBBA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to
section 4.4[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHAAA} of
the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to revoke the
[Brandon Airport Zoning Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-509),
C.R.C., c. 76, and to make the annexed [Regulations respecting Zoning at
Brandon Airport](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-509), in substitution therefor.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913823 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Brandon Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-509).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913825 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Brandon Airport, in the
      vicinity of Brandon, in the Province of Manitoba;
      ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
      described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
      l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from each end of a
      strip, which approach surface is more particularly described in
      Part II of the schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
      ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
      located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
      outer surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
      schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
      landing area of the airport, including the runway, prepared for
      the take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction,
      which strip is more particularly described in Part IV of the
      schedule; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
      inclined plane that extends upward and outward from the lateral
      limits of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional
      surface is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule.
      ([surface de transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the elevation
  of the airport reference point is 405 m above sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913836 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, which lands are more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the lands within the outer limits of lands described
  in Part VI of the schedule; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the lands directly under that portion of the approach
  surfaces that extend beyond the said outer limits.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913840 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation at the
location of that point

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913845 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 4, the Minister may require
that the owner or lessee of the land on which that object is growing
remove the excessive growth.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-670, s. 1
:::

## [Disposal of Waste]{.HTitleText1} {#h-913849 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** No owner or lessee of any
land to which these Regulations apply shall permit such land or any part
of it to be used for the disposal of any waste that is edible by or
attractive to birds.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-670, s. 2
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-idescription-of-the-airport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

The airport reference point shown on Brandon Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2307 dated January 2, 1987 is a point lying perpendicular distant
152.4 m northerly from a point on the centre line of runway 08-26 and
distant westerly 904 m along the said centre line from the easterly end
of the said runway.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Approach Surfaces]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-the-approach-surfaces .SchedHeadL1}

The approach surfaces, shown on Brandon Airport Zoning Plan No. E. 2307
dated January 2, 1987, are surfaces abutting each end of the strips
associated with the runways designated 08-26 and 14-32 and are described
as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 08 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 26 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 50 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 15 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 2 400 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 300 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 14 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 330 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 120 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 32 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of 1 m measured vertically to 25 m measured horizontally rising to an
  imaginary horizontal line drawn at right angles to the projected
  centre line of the strip and distant 3 000 m measured horizontally
  from the end of the strip; the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal
  line being 330 m from the projected centre line; the said imaginary
  horizontal line being 120 m above the elevation at the end of the
  strip.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

The outer surface, shown on Brandon Airport Zoning Plan No. E. 2307
dated January 2, 1987, is an imaginary surface located at a common plane
established at a constant elevation of 45 m above the elevation of the
airport reference point, except that, where that common plane is less
than 9 m above the surface of the ground, the outer surface is an
imaginary surface located at 9 m above the surface of the ground.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Strips]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-strips .SchedHeadL1}

The strips shown on Brandon Airport Zoning Plan No. E.2307 dated
January 2, 1987 are described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 08-26 is 300 m in
  width, 150 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  2 101 m in length; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway 14-32 is 60 m in
  width, 30 m being on each side of the centre line of the runway and
  1 034.5 m in length.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Each transitional surface, shown on Brandon Airport Zoning Plan
No. E.2307 dated January 2, 1987, is described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with runway 08-26
  is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 7 m measured horizontally at right angles to
  the centre line and centre line produced of the strip extending upward
  and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
  surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surface associated with runway 14-32
  is a surface consisting of an inclined plane rising at a ratio of 1 m
  measured vertically to 5 m measured horizontally at right angles to
  the centre line and centre line produced of the strip extending upward
  and outward from the lateral limits of the strip and its approach
  surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface or another
  transitional surface of an adjoining strip.
