## Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the Control and Supervision of the Regulatory Affairs and Orders in Council Secretariat Except Orders in Council Division {#order-transferring-from-the-privy-council-office-to-the-treasury-board-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-regulatory-affairs-and-orders-in-council-secretariat-except-orders-in-council-division .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-75
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-05-31

Order Transferring from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board
the Control and Supervision of the Regulatory Affairs and Orders in
Council Secretariat Except Orders in Council Division

P.C. 2006-362 2006-05-10
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA} of the [Public
Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34),
hereby transfers from the Privy Council Office to the Treasury Board the
control and supervision of the portion of the federal public
administration within the Privy Council Office known as the Regulatory
Affairs and Orders in Council Secretariat, with the exception of the
Orders in Council Division, effective July 1, 2006.
