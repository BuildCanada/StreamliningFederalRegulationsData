## Quebec Flue-Cured Tobacco Order {#quebec-flue-cured-tobacco-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 243
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Flue-Cured Tobacco Produced in Quebec
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537395 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Flue-Cured Tobacco Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._243).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537397 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Quebec Flue-Cured
    Tobacco Producers' Board constituted under the Plan; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[flue-cured tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[flue-cured tobacco]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means flue-cured tobacco
    produced in the Province of Quebec; ([tabac jaune]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Quebec Flue-Cured Tobacco
    Producers' Plan sanctioned and declared in force by the Quebec
    Agricultural Marketing Board as published in the Quebec Official
    Gazette of June 21, 1958, pursuant to the Agricultural Marketing Act
    of Quebec. ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537402 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of flue-cured tobacco in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Quebec, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of flue-cured tobacco locally within the Province of Quebec
under the heading "Obligations, powers and attributions" of the Plan,
excepting the powers exercisable under paragraphs (g) and (o) in
subsection "And particularly" of the said heading of the Plan.
