## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827950 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Summit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental conference
known as the North American Leaders' Summit, to be held in the National
Capital Region on June 29, 2016.

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827952 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** On June 29, 2016,
representatives of foreign states that participate in the Summit shall
have in Canada, to the extent required for the exercise of their
functions in Canada in relation to the Summit, the privileges and
immunities set out in sections 11(b) and (e) and 14 to 16 of Article IV
of the Convention on the Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations
set out in Schedule III to the [Foreign Missions and International
Organizations Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-827954 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
