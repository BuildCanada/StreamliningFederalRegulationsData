## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449539 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Agreement between the
    United Nations as Represented by the United Nations Environment
    Programme and the Government of Canada Regarding the Fourth Session
    of the Intergovernmental Negotiating Committee to Develop an
    International Legally Binding Instrument on Plastic Pollution,
    including in the Marine Environment
    (INC-4). ([Accord]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[meetings]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the regional preparatory consultations to be held
      in Ottawa on April 21, 2024; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the meeting of the Fourth Session of the
      Intergovernmental Negotiating Committee to Develop an
      International Legally Binding Instrument on Plastic Pollution,
      including in the Marine Environment (INC-4), to be held in Ottawa
      from April 23 to 30, 2024. ([réunions]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[relevant period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on April 19, 2024 and ending on May 3, 2024. ([période
    visée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449548 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inviolability

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** During the relevant period,
the [[premises]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in Article 3 of the
Agreement, and the archives and documents of the United Nations
Environment Programme are inviolable within the meaning of section 3 of
the Convention.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representatives

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During
  the relevant period, the following persons who have been invited to
  attend the meetings as [[participants]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in Article 2 of the Agreement, have, to the extent required
  for the exercise of their functions in relation to the meetings, the
  privileges and immunities set out in sections 11 and 12 of Article IV
  of the Convention, to the extent set out in sections 14 to 16 of that
  Article:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} representatives of foreign states;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} representatives of members of the United Nations;
    and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} representatives of members to specialized agencies
    or related organizations of the United Nations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}United Nations officials

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, officials of the United
  Nations have, to the extent required for the exercise of their
  functions in relation to the meetings, the privileges and immunities
  set out in paragraphs 18(a) and (c) to (f) of Article V of the
  Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Officials of specialized agencies or
  related organizations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, officials of specialized
  agencies or related organizations of the United Nations have, to the
  extent required for the exercise of their functions in relation to the
  meetings, the privileges and immunities set out in paragraphs
  18(a), (c) and (d) of Article V of the Convention.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Experts

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} During the relevant period, experts, other than the
  officials referred to in subsections (2) and (3), who are performing
  missions for the United Nations or its specialized agencies or related
  organizations have, to the extent required for the exercise of their
  functions in relation to the meetings, the privileges and immunities
  set out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1449563 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
