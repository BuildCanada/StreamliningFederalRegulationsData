## Resolute Bay Airport Zoning Regulations {#resolute-bay-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 106
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Resolute Bay Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-486122 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Resolute Bay Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._106).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-486124 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Resolute Bay Airport at
    Resolute, in the Northwest Territories; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    determined in the manner set out in Part I of the schedule; ([point
    de repère de l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane extending upward and outward from each end of a strip along
    and at right angles to the projected centre line thereof, which
    approach surface is more particularly described in Part III of the
    schedule; ([surface d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Transport;
    ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[outer surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary surface
    located above and in the immediate vicinity of the airport, which
    outer surface is more particularly described in Part IV of the
    schedule; ([surface extérieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, including the runway prepared for the
    take-off and landing of aircraft in a particular direction, which
    strip is more particularly described in Part V of the schedule;
    ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending upward and outward from the lateral limits
    of a strip and its approach surfaces, which transitional surface is
    more particularly described in Part VI of the schedule. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 200 feet above
sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-486134 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all the lands and lands under water adjacent to or in the vicinity of
the airport, the outer limits of which lands are described in Part II of
the schedule, other than such lands as from time to time form part of
the airport.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-486136 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** No person shall erect or
construct, on any land to which these Regulations apply, any building,
structure or object or any addition to any existing building, structure
or object, the highest point of which will exceed in elevation, at the
location of that point, any of the surfaces hereinafter set out that
project immediately over and above the surface of the land at that
location, namely,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the approach surfaces;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the outer surface; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the transitional surfaces.

## [Natural Growth]{.HTitleText1} {#h-486141 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where an object of natural
growth on any land to which these Regulations apply exceeds in elevation
any of the surfaces referred to in section 5, the Minister may make a
direction that the owner or occupier of the land on which that object is
growing remove the excessive growth.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Sections 2 and 4)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-486147 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

COMMENCING at the intersection of the runways designated 10-28 and
16-34, THENCE northerly along the centre line of runway designated 16-34
for a distance of four thousand (4,000) feet, THENCE westerly and
perpendicular to the centre line of runway 16-34 a distance of five
hundred (500) feet to the airport reference point.

### [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Description of Outer Limits of Lands]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iidescription-of-outer-limits-of-lands .SchedHeadL1}

A circular area having a radius of thirteen thousand (13,000) feet, the
centre of which is the airport reference point.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Approach Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiidescription-of-each-approach-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface abutting each end of the strips associated with the
runways designated 10-28 and 16-34, and more particularly described as
follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 10 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 28 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 16 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line, and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a surface abutting the end of the strip associated
  with runway approach 34 consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio
  of one (1) foot measured vertically to fifty (50) feet measured
  horizontally rising to an imaginary horizontal line drawn at right
  angles to the projected centre line of the strip one thousand (1,000)
  feet measured vertically above the elevation at the end of the strip
  and fifty thousand (50,000) feet measured horizontally from the end of
  the strip, the outer ends of the imaginary horizontal line being eight
  thousand (8,000) feet from the projected centre line,

which approach surfaces are shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. E. 1247, dated September 1, 1976.

### [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Description of the Outer Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-ivdescription-of-the-outer-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being an imaginary surface consisting of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a common plane established at a constant elevation of
  one hundred and fifty (150) feet above the assigned elevation of the
  airport reference point, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the common plane described in paragraph (a) is
  less than thirty (30) feet above the surface of the ground, an
  imaginary surface located thirty (30) feet above the surface of the
  ground,

which outer surface is shown on Department of Public Works Plan
No. E. 1247, dated September 1, 1976.

### [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Description of Each Strip]{.HTitleText1} {#part-vdescription-of-each-strip .SchedHeadL1}

Each strip is described as follows:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway designated 10-28 is
  eight thousand four hundred (8,400) feet in length and one thousand
  (1,000) feet in width, five hundred (500) feet being on each side of
  the centre line of the runway, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the strip associated with runway designated 16-34 is
  eleven thousand four hundred (11,400) feet in length and one thousand
  (1,000) feet in width, five hundred (500) feet being on each side of
  the centre line of the runway,

which strips are shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E. 1247,
dated September 1, 1976.

### [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Description of Transitional Surface]{.HTitleText1} {#part-videscription-of-transitional-surface .SchedHeadL1}

Being a surface consisting of an inclined plane having a ratio of one
(1) foot measured vertically to seven (7) feet measured horizontally at
right angles to the centre line and centre line produced of each strip
and extending upward and outward from the lateral limits of each strip
and its approach surfaces to an intersection with the outer surface, an
approach surface or another transitional surface of an adjoining strip,
all as shown on Department of Public Works Plan No. E. 1247, dated
September 1, 1976.
