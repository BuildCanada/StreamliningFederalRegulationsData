## Ministerial Regulations Authorization Order {#ministerial-regulations-authorization-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-631
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Registration 1986-06-05

Order Pursuant to Section 3.4 of the Aeronautics Act Authorizing the
Minister of Transport to Make Regulations Imposing Certain Charges

P.C. 1986-1348 1986-06-05
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 3.4[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EHBAA} of the [Aeronautics
Act](/eng/acts/A-2), is pleased hereby to revoke the [Ministerial
Regulations Authorization Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-631), C.R.C.,
c. 126 and to make the annexed [Order pursuant to section 3.4 of the
Aeronautics Act authorizing the Minister of Transport to make
regulations imposing certain charges](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-631), in
substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EHBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1985, c. 28, s. 1
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897649 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ministerial Regulations Authorization
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-631).

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897651 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister of Transport
is authorized to make regulations imposing charges

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the use of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any facility or service provided by or on behalf of
    the Minister for or in respect of any aircraft, whether or not,
    where the facility or service is provided during flight, the flight
    originates or terminates in Canada or any portion of the flight is
    over Canada,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other facility or service provided by or on
    behalf of the Minister at any aerodrome, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any aerodrome operated by or on behalf of Her
    Majesty in right of Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the issue, renewal, amendment or
  endorsement of any document issued or to be issued under Part I of the
  [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) or any action preparatory thereto,
  whether or not the document is issued, renewed, amended or endorsed.
