His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry and of the Treasury Board, pursuant to section
6[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EZFAA} of
the Radiocommunication Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EVFAA} and section
19.1[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0ETFAA}

to repeal the General Radio Regulations, Part I, C.R.C., c. 1371, the
General Radio Regulations, Part II, C.R.C., c. 1372, the
Interference-causing Equipment Regulations, made by Order in Council
P.C. 1993-408 of March 9, 1993[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EJFAA}, and the Radio
Operators′ Certificate Regulations, made on March 9, 1978[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e){#footnotee_e-ID0EFFAA}, and to make
the annexed Regulations respecting radiocommunication, radio
authorizations, exemptions from authorizations and the operation of
radio apparatus, radio-sensitive equipment and interference-causing
equipment in substitution therefor, effective on the date of publication
in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001594 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Radiocommunication
    Act](/eng/acts/R-2); ([loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aeronautical service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aeronautical service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for the safety and
    navigation and other operations of aircraft, and that may also
    include the exchange of air-to-ground messages on behalf of the
    public; ([service aéronautique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[amateur radio service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[amateur radio service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service in which radio apparatus are used for the
    purpose of self-training, intercommunication or technical
    investigation by individuals who are interested in radio technique
    solely with a personal aim and without pecuniary interest; ([service
    de radioamateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicable standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicable standard]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2001-533, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[developmental service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[developmental service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for research and
    development, experimentation or demonstration of radio apparatus, or
    the assessment of the marketability of radio apparatus, new
    technology or telecommunication services; ([service de
    développement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[equipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means radio apparatus,
    interference-causing equipment and radio-sensitive equipment;
    ([matériel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fixed point-to-point service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fixed point-to-point service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for communications on radio
    frequencies above 30 MHz between two fixed stations that are each
    authorized to operate at a specific point, other than fixed stations
    that also operate within the land mobile service on the same radio
    frequency as the one assigned to the land mobile service; ([service
    point à point fixe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fixed service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fixed service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radiocommunication
    service that provides for communications between fixed stations or
    between fixed stations and space stations; ([service
    fixe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fixed station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fixed station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radio station
    authorized to operate at a fixed point; ([station
    fixe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[interconnected radio-based transmission facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[interconnected radio-based transmission
    facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any radio apparatus that is
    used for the transmission or reception of intelligence to or from
    anywhere on a public switched network; ([installation de
    transmission radio d'interconnexion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[intersatellite service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[intersatellite service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for communications between
    space stations; ([service intersatellite]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an association of two or
    more persons, if the relationship among those associated persons
    does not, under the laws in Canada, constitute a corporation, a
    partnership or a trust, and if all the undivided ownership interests
    in the assets of the radiocommunication user and radiocommunication
    service provider or in the voting interests of the
    radiocommunication user and radiocommunication service provider are
    or will be owned by all the persons that are so associated;
    ([coentreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[land mobile service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[land mobile service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for communications between
    mobile stations and

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} fixed stations,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} space stations, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} other mobile stations; ([service mobile
      terrestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[manufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person specified as the manufacturer in the
      applicable standard, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no applicable standard exists or where the
      manufacturer is not specified in any applicable standard, the
      person, other than a person whose function is solely to install
      equipment, who

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} with respect to radio apparatus and
        interference-causing equipment, carries out the last assembly of
        or last modification to the model of equipment which could
        affect its capacity to cause interference to radiocommunication,
        or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} with respect to radio-sensitive equipment,
        carries out the last assembly of or last modification to the
        model of equipment which could affect its sensitivity to
        electromagnetic energy; ([fabricant]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

[[maritime service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[maritime service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radiocommunication
    service that provides for the safety and navigation and other
    operations of ships or vessels, and that may also include the
    exchange of ship-to-shore messages on behalf of the public;
    ([service maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2021-40, s.
    3\]]{.Repealed}

[[mobile station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mobile station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radio station
    intended to be used while in motion and during stops; ([station
    mobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[model]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment identified by, and
    permanently marked with, a unique brand, trade name, symbol or logo
    and an identification code, comprised of letters, numbers or a
    combination thereof; ([modèle]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a corporation, partnership,
    trust and joint venture; ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[public information service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public information service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for communications in which
    the transmissions are intended for the public, but does not include
    transmissions by a broadcasting undertaking; ([service d'information
    publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiocommunication carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiocommunication carrier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2014-34, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[radiocommunication service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiocommunication service provider]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    person who operates radio apparatus used by that person or another
    person to provide radiocommunication services for compensation;
    ([fournisseur de services radio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiocommunication user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiocommunication user]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
    operates radio apparatus for personal or government use or for a
    business other than the business of a radiocommunication service
    provider; ([usager radio]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[radiodetermination service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[radiodetermination service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    radiocommunication service that provides for the determination of
    the position, velocity or other characteristics of an object or
    physical phenomenon, or for the obtaining of information relating to
    these parameters, by means of the propagation properties of radio
    waves; ([service de radiorepérage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[renewable radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[renewable radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radio
    licence that is issued for a period of one year or less, that
    expires on March 31 and that can be renewed for a period of one
    year; ([licence radio renouvelable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[space station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[space station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radio station where
    radio apparatus that is used for any radiocommunication service is
    installed in a place located outside the major portion of the
    earth's atmosphere or is intended to travel beyond the major portion
    of the earth's atmosphere; ([station spatiale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[TAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[TAC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a technical acceptance
    certificate; ([CAT]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[temporary radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary radio licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a radio
    licence that is issued for a period of 11 months or less and that
    cannot be renewed. ([licence radio temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

set out in the Category I Equipment Standards List, as amended from time

## [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Radio Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001634 .Part}

### [Radiocommunication Services and Stations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001635 .Subheading}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is a term of a radio
licence that the holder of the licence may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} install, operate or possess radio apparatus to
  perform any of the following services, as authorized by the radio
  licence, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} aeronautical service,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} amateur radio service,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} public information service,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} developmental service,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} fixed service,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} intersatellite service,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} land mobile service,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} maritime service,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} radiodetermination service, and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} fixed point-to-point service; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} install, operate or possess radio apparatus at a
  fixed station, mobile station or space station as authorized by the
  radio licence.

### [Restriction Relating to Holders of Radio Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001648 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** It is a term of a radio
licence that the holder of the radio licence shall restrict the
activities of the station to those radiocommunication services referred
to in paragraph 3(a) that are specified in the licence.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** It is a term of a radio
licence that the holder of the radio licence who is a radiocommunication
service provider shall provide its radiocommunication services without
unjust discrimination.

### [Restrictions Relating to the Aeronautical Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001651 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Use of radio apparatus in
the aeronautical service is restricted to communications relating to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety and navigation of aircraft;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general operation of aircraft; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of messages on behalf of the public.

### [Restrictions Relating to the Developmental Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001658 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Use of radio apparatus

tests, research or demonstrations being carried out in relation to that
service.

### [Restrictions Relating to the Maritime Service]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001660 .Subheading}

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the safety and navigation of ships or vessels;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the general operation of ships or vessels; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the exchange of messages on behalf of the public.

### [Eligibility]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001667 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons are eligible to be issued radio licences or spectrum
  licences as radiocommunication users or radiocommunication service
  providers in all services except the amateur radio service:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a citizen within the meaning of subsection 2(1)
      of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29),

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident within the meaning of
      subsection 2(1) of the Immigration Act, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a non-resident who has been issued an
      employment authorization under the [Immigration and Refugee
      Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that is incorporated or continued
    under the laws of Canada or a province;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partnership, joint venture or trust if each
    partner, co-venturer or trustee is eligible to be issued a radio
    licence under this subsection;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian government, whether federal, provincial
    or local, or an agency thereof;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the Government of a country other than Canada,
    which is a signatory to the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic
    Relations, done at Vienna, April 18, 1961;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any person who is the registered owner of an
    aircraft that is registered in Canada, for the establishment and
    operation of a station on board the aircraft;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any person who is the registered or licensed owner
    of a ship or vessel that is registered under the [Canada Shipping
    Act](/eng/acts/S-9) or licensed under the [Coasting Trade
    Act](/eng/acts/C-33.3), for the establishment and operation of a
    station on board the ship or vessel; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} any person who is a resident of a country other
    than Canada, who

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} seeks to establish and operate a radio station
      designed for interconnection with a public switched network, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} requires a radio licence for radio apparatus
      used for a special event of a limited duration.

### [Non-Assignability of Radio Licences]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001688 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** It is a term of a radio
licence that the licence not be transferred or assigned without the
authorization of the Minister.

### [Stations Licensed or Exempted in Another Country]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001690 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Radio apparatus used in a
mobile station that is licensed or exempted by the responsible
administration of another country is exempt from the application of
subsection 4(1) of the Act if the mobile station is used for
communications with stations licensed or exempted in Canada or that
other country and if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operator is a citizen of that other country; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a reciprocal agreement that allows similar privileges
  to Canadians exists between that other country and Canada.

### [Radio Licences of Radiocommunication Service Providers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001694 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  a term of a radio licence of a radiocommunication service provider
  that a subscriber to the services or a lessee of radio apparatus of
  the radiocommunication service provider may install, operate or
  possess radio apparatus to communicate with other radio apparatus to
  which that licence applies.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Use of the services or radio apparatus of a
  radiocommunication service provider is restricted to communications
  with radio apparatus to which the radio licence referred to in
  subsection (1) applies.

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  radiocommunication service provider shall provide to each of its
  subscribers and lessees of its radio apparatus a copy of the terms and
  conditions of its radio licence that are applicable to those
  subscribers or lessees, as the case may be.

### [Exemption of Radio Apparatus on Board an Aircraft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001707 .Subheading}

- **[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This section applies in respect of
  an aircraft that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} registered or licensed under an Act of Parliament;
    or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} owned by, or under the direction or control of, Her
    Majesty in right of Canada or a province.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A radio apparatus that is operated on board an
  aircraft in the performance of the aeronautical service or the
  radiodetermination service is exempt from subsection 4(1) of the Act,
  in respect of a radio licence, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the radio apparatus occurs when

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is within Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is outside Canada and the territory
      of another country, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the aircraft is in the territory of another
      country with which Canada has entered into a reciprocal agreement
      that confers similar privileges on Canadians; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the operation of the radio apparatus is in
    accordance with the technical requirements for mobile stations
    operating in the aeronautical service that are specified in section
    34.1.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2011-47, s. 5\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the ship or vessel is outside Canada and the

## [PART VI]{.HLabel1}[Interference]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1001922 .Part}

### [Determination of Interference for a Model of Equipment]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1001923 .Subheading}

- **[[50]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-50 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} equipment whether or not it complies with
    applicable standards; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment for which no applicable standard exists.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where the Minister, taking into account the factors
  mentioned in subsection (5), determines that a model or several models
  of equipment cause or are likely to cause interference to
  radiocommunication or suffer from or are likely to suffer from adverse
  effects of electromagnetic energy, the Minister shall give notice of
  the determination to persons who are likely to be affected thereby.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall manufacture, import, distribute,
  lease, offer for sale, sell, install or use equipment in respect of
  which a notice referred to in subsection (2) has been given.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (3) does not apply in respect of equipment
  that is manufactured or imported solely for export purposes.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A determination pursuant to subsection (2) shall
  include the consideration of the following factors:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the electromagnetic environment in which the
    equipment is being used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the circumstances under which it is being used;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the technical characteristics of the devices being
    interfered with or being adversely affected by electromagnetic
    energy; and

    purposes of international affairs or national defence or security.

  the circumstances referred to in subsection (2), the following persons

    Canada, where the officer or servant makes use of or divulges a
    radio-based telephone communication, or intercepts and makes use of,

    be, in the course of radio frequency spectrum management for the
    purpose of identifying, isolating or preventing an unauthorized or
    interfering use of a frequency or of a transmission; or

    Canada or a person providing a communication service, where the
    officer, servant or person makes use of or divulges a radio-based
    telephone communication, intercepts and makes use of, or intercepts
    and divulges, a radiocommunication, as the case may be, in the
    course of monitoring radiocommunications for the purpose of ensuring
    the security and integrity of communications and communication
    systems.

payable by a radiocommunication service provider in respect of radio

personal communications services radio frequencies is the applicable fee

### [Space Station]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002058 .Subheading}

**[[73]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-73 .sectionLabel}** The radio licence fee
payable in respect of radio apparatus installed in a space station that
communicates with a fixed station or space station is, for each
transmitter and each receiver installed at the station, the sum of the
applicable fees set out in Part VI of Schedule III that corresponds to
the number of telephone channels per radio frequency assigned to that
transmitter or receiver.

### [Radio Licence Amendments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002061 .Subheading}

**[[75]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-75 .sectionLabel}** Where a licensee requests
an amendment to a radio licence that results in a higher fee, the radio
licence fee payable is the difference between the existing fee and the
new fee.

### [Additional Radio Frequency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002063 .Subheading}

**[[76]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-76 .sectionLabel}** Where a licensee operates
on an additional radio frequency for which a fee is prescribed, the
radio licence fee payable is the difference between the existing fee and
the new fee.

### [Increase in the Number of Telephone Channels per Radio Frequency]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002065 .Subheading}

**[[77]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-77 .sectionLabel}** Where a licensee
increases the number of telephone channels of a radio frequency assigned
to a transmitter or receiver installed at a fixed station or space
station described in section 61, 65 or 73, the radio licence fee payable
in respect of that amendment is the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the new fee for the amended total number of telephone
  channels per radio frequency assigned to that transmitter or receiver,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the existing fee for the total number of telephone
  channels per radio frequency assigned to that transmitter or receiver.

### [Relocation of a Station]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1002069 .Subheading}

**[[78]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-78 .sectionLabel}** Where a fixed station
referred to in section 67 or 68 is relocated into a congestion zone
where the radio licence fee is higher, the radio licence fee payable is,
for each assigned transmit or receive radio frequency, the difference
between the new fee in the congestion zone to which the station is
relocated and the corresponding existing fee applicable in the
congestion zone in which the station was previously located.

**[[79]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-79 .sectionLabel}** Where a fixed station
referred to in section 63 or 66 is relocated from an area into a
metropolitan area, the radio licence fee payable is, for each assigned
transmit or receive radio frequency, the difference between the new fee
in the metropolitan area to which the station is relocated and the
corresponding existing fee applicable in the area in which the station
was previously located.
