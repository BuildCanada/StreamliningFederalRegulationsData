## Sinai Multinational Force and Observers Privileges and Immunities Order, 1990 {#sinai-multinational-force-and-observers-privileges-and-immunities-order-1990 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-700
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1990-10-04

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities in Canada of the Sinai
Multinational Force and Observers

P.C. 1990-2170  1990-10-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section 4 of
the Privileges and Immunities (International Organizations) Act, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the privileges and
immunities in Canada of the Sinai Multinational Force and
Observers](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-700).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930725 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Sinai Multinational Force and Observers Privileges and Immunities
Order, 1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-700).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930727 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Sinai Multinational
    Force and Observers. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-930731 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Organization shall have
in Canada the legal capacities of a body corporate.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Organization shall have
in Canada the privileges and immunities set out in Article II of the
Convention.
