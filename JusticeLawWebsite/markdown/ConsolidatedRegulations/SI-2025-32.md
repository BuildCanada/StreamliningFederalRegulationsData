## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario) to the Minister of Industry the Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Federal Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-federal-economic-development-agency-for-southern-ontario-to-the-minister-of-industry-the-powers-duties-and-functions-in-relation-to-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-southern-ontario .Title-of-Act}

:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Federal Economic
Development Agency for Southern Ontario) to the Minister of Industry the
Powers, Duties and Functions in Relation to the Federal Economic
Development Agency for Southern Ontario

P.C. 2025-409 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EECAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBCAA},
transfers from the Minister of State (Federal Economic Development
Agency for Southern Ontario) to the Minister of Industry the powers,
duties and functions of the Minister of State (Federal Economic
Development Agency for Southern Ontario) in relation to the Federal
Economic Development Agency for Southern Ontario.
