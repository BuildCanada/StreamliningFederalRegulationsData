## NAFTA and CCFTA Verification of Origin Regulations {#nafta-and-ccfta-verification-of-origin-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-333
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1997-07-05

NAFTA and CCFTA Verification of Origin Regulations

P.C. 1997-964 1997-07-04
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the proposed [NAFTA and CCFTA Verification of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-333)implement provisions of free
trade agreements (NAFTA and CCFTA) and otherwise make no material
substantive change in an existing regulation and are therefore, by
virtue of paragraphs 164(4)(a.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EEAHA} and (d) of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EBAHA}, not required
to be published under subsection 164(3) of that Act;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to section
42.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EOAGA}, subsection
42.4(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#footnotec_e){#footnotec_e-ID0EMAGA}, paragraph
164(1)(i)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#footnoted_e){#footnoted_e-ID0EKAGA} and
subsections 164(1.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^e^](#footnotee_e){#footnotee_e-ID0EIAGA} and
(1.2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^f^](#footnotef_e){#footnotef_e-ID0EGAGA} of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#footnoteb_e){#footnoteb_e-ID0EDAGA}, hereby makes
the annexed [NAFTA and CCFTA Verification of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-333).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006272 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the competent
    authority that is responsible under the law of a NAFTA country or of
    Chile, as the case may be, for the administration of customs laws
    and regulations. ([administration douanière]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has
    the meaning assigned to that expression by Schedule XII of the
    [NAFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-14) or
    by Schedule XI of the [CCFTA Rules of Origin
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-340), as the case may be.
    ([principes comptables généralement reconnus]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under NAFTA or preferential tariff treatment under
    CCFTA is claimed. ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good, and includes a part or ingredient.
    ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information with respect to the origin of goods that are
    the subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information with respect to the origin
    of goods that are the subject of a verification of origin.
    ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into a
    place or premises for the purposes of conducting a verification of
    origin of goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006282 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, an officer may conduct a verification of origin of
goods in any of the following manners:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing a verification questionnaire completed
  by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material that is used
    in the production of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing a written response received from a
  person referred to in paragraph (a) to a verification letter; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by reviewing any other information received from a
  person referred to in paragraph (a).

## [Prescribed Premises]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006289 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following premises of
persons in a NAFTA country other than Canada or in Chile are prescribed
premises for the purposes of a verification visit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premises of an exporter or a producer of goods;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the premises of a producer or a supplier of a
  material that is used in the production of goods.

## [Conditions for Conducting a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006293 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer may conduct a verification visit on the condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the officer sends a written notice of the intention
    to conduct the verification visit to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are the subject of the
      verification visit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the customs administration of Chile or of the
      NAFTA country in the territory of which the verification visit is
      to occur, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the customs administration of
      Chile or of the NAFTA country in the territory of which the
      verification visit is to occur, the embassy in Canada of Chile or
      of that NAFTA country, as the case may be; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are the subject of the
    verification visit consents to the verification visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in paragraph (1)(a) shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration of the
    officer sending the notice;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are the
    subject of the verification visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and place of the verification visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the verification visit,
    including specific reference to the goods that are the subject of
    the verification of origin or to the materials that are used in the
    production of those goods;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the verification visit.

## [Postponement of a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006308 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  customs administration that receives a notice under subparagraph
  4(1)(a)(ii) may postpone the verification visit by sending a written
  request to the officer who sent the notice, within 15 days after
  receiving the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A postponement of a verification visit under
  subsection (1) shall be for a period not exceeding 60 days beginning
  on the date of receipt of the notice, or for a longer period, where
  the longer period is agreed to by the customs administration of Canada
  and the customs administration of Chile or the NAFTA country in the
  territory of which the verification visit is to take place, as the
  case may be.

## [Observers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006312 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Any person who receives a
notice under subparagraph 4(1)(a)(i) may designate two observers to be
present during the verification visit.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An observer designated
under section 6 may not participate in the verification visit in a
manner other than as an observer.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person who designates an
observer under section 6 shall identify that observer to the officer
conducting the verification visit.

## [Generally Accepted Accounting Principles]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006316 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where an officer
determines, during the course of a verification of origin, that a
producer of goods has failed to record costs in accordance with
Generally Accepted Accounting Principles applied in the territory of the
NAFTA country or Chile in which the goods that are the subject of the
verification of origin are produced, the officer shall send a written
notice to the producer stating that the producer has not recorded its
costs in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles and
that the producer must record those costs in accordance with those
principles within 60 days after receiving that written notice.

## [Verification Letters and Verification Questionnaires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006318 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire shall specify

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration of the
  officer sending the verification letter or verification questionnaire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods that are the subject of
  the verification of origin or of the materials that are used in the
  production of those goods; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the verification letter must
  be answered or the verification questionnaire must be completed and
  returned, which period, except in the case of a verification letter
  under section 11, shall be not less than 30 days after the date on
  which the verification letter or the verification questionnaire was
  sent.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Where, pursuant to
section 11 of the [NAFTA Rules of Origin
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-94-14) or pursuant to section 10 of
the [CCFTA Rules of Origin Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-340), a
producer of a motor vehicle elects to average its regional value content
calculation over the producer's fiscal year, an officer may, in a
verification letter, request that a cost submission reflecting the
actual costs incurred in the production of the category of motor
vehicles for which the election was made be submitted to the officer by
the producer not later than the later of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 180 days after the end of the producer's fiscal year,
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 60 days after the date on which the verification
  letter was sent.

## [Subsequent Verification Letters and Verification Questionnaires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006326 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Where a verification
letter or verification questionnaire has been sent to a person and that
person does not respond to the verification letter, or complete and
return the verification questionnaire, within the period specified
therein, an officer shall send a subsequent verification letter or
subsequent verification questionnaire to that person.

## [Denial or Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under NAFTA or of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under Ccfta]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006328 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, preferential tariff treatment under NAFTA
or preferential tariff treatment under CCFTA may be denied or withdrawn
from the goods that are the subject of a verification of origin where
the exporter or producer of the goods

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not consent to a verification visit within 30
  days after receiving a notice referred to in paragraph 4(1)(a);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who is required to maintain records of goods in
  accordance with the applicable laws of the country in which the
  verification of origin is conducted

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fails to maintain those records in accordance with
    those laws, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} denies the officer conducting the verification of
    origin access to those records; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to whom a subsequent verification letter or
  subsequent verification questionnaire is sent under section 12 does
  not respond to the subsequent verification letter or complete the
  subsequent verification questionnaire and return it to an officer
  within 30 days after the date on which the subsequent verification
  letter or subsequent verification questionnaire is

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} received, where the subsequent verification letter
    or subsequent verification questionnaire is sent in accordance with
    paragraph 18(a), or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sent, where the subsequent verification letter or
    subsequent verification questionnaire is sent in accordance with
    paragraph 18(b).

<!-- -->

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer shall send a written notice of the intent to re-determine the
  origin of goods under subsection 59(1) of the Act with any statement
  that is provided to an exporter or producer of goods pursuant to
  subsection 42.2(1) of the Act and that states that the goods that are
  the subject of a verification of origin are not eligible for
  preferential tariff treatment under NAFTA or preferential tariff
  treatment under CCFTA, as the case may be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice referred to in subsection (1) shall include
  the date after which preferential tariff treatment under NAFTA or
  preferential tariff treatment under CCFTA, as the case may be, may be
  denied or withdrawn in respect of the goods that are the subject of
  the verification of origin and the period during which the exporter or
  producer of the goods may provide written comments or additional
  information regarding the origin of those goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-53, s. 12
:::

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A re-determination of the
origin of goods shall not be made earlier than 30 days after the date on
which the notice referred to in subsection 14(1) is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} received, where the notice is sent in accordance with
  paragraph 18(a); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sent, where the notice is sent in accordance with
  paragraph 18(b).

## [Methods of Sending Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1006345 .Part}

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire may be sent by any method.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** A notice referred to in
subparagraph 4(1)(a)(i) shall be sent by any method that produces a
confirmation of receipt.

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** A subsequent verification
letter, subsequent verification questionnaire or a notice referred to in
section 14 shall be sent

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if requested by the customs administration of Chile
  or of a NAFTA country from whose territory the goods were exported as
  the case may be, by any method that produces a confirmation of
  receipt; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, by any method.
