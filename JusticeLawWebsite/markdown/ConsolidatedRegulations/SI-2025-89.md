## Order Designating the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada as the Minister for the Purposes of the Building Canada Act {#order-designating-the-president-of-the-kings-privy-council-for-canada-as-the-minister-for-the-purposes-of-the-building-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-89
:::

[BUILDING CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.89)

Registration 2025-07-16

Order Designating the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada
as the Minister for the Purposes of the Building Canada Act

P.C. 2025-567 2025-07-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Building Canada
Act](/eng/acts/B-9.89)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2939_hq_1750341153){#fn_81000-3-2939_hq_1750341153-ID0EDBAA},
designates the President of the King's Privy Council for Canada, a
member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the
purposes of the [Building Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-9.89).
