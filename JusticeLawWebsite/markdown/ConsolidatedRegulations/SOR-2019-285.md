Physical Activities Regulations

P.C. 2019-1182 2019-08-07

::::::::::: section

the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to sections 109 and 188 of the
[Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75)[[Footnote

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2019-285).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[aerodrome]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 3(1) of the [Aeronautics
      Act](/eng/acts/A-2). ([aérodrome]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[area of mining operations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[area of mining operations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the area
      at ground level occupied by any open-pit or underground workings,
      mill complex or storage area for overburden, waste rock, tailings
      or ore. ([aire d'exploitation minière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[boundary water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[boundary water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      the definition [[boundary waters]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada Water Act](/eng/acts/C-11). ([eaux
      limitrophes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[canal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a waterway constructed for
      navigation. ([canal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Class IA nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Class IA nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same
      meaning as in section 1 of the [Class I Nuclear Facilities
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-204). ([installation
      nucléaire de catégorie IA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[disposal at sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[disposal at sea]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      [[disposal]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection
      122(1) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
      1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31), but does not include any omission that
      constitutes a disposal in paragraph (g) of the definition of that
      term. ([immersion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exploratory well]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exploratory well]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in subsection 101(1) of the [Canada Petroleum Resources
      Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), but does not include a [[delineation
      well]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or [[development
      well]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as those terms are defined in that
      subsection. ([puits d'exploration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[hazardous waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[hazardous waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned
      by paragraphs 1(a) to (e) of the definitions [[hazardous
      waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and [[hazardous recyclable
      material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in sections 2 and 4,
      respectively, of the Cross-border Movement of Hazardous Waste and
      Hazardous Recyclable Material Regulations, but does not include
      nuclear substances, domestic waste water or anything collected
      from households in the course of regular municipal waste
      collection services. ([déchets dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[international electrical transmission line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[international electrical transmission
      line]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by the
      definition [[international power line]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
      section 2 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator
      Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([ligne internationale de transport
      d'électricité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[marine terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[marine terminal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility,
      including its areas, structures and equipment, that is used for
      berthing ships and that is

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} related to the movement of goods between ships
        and shore; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} used for the receiving, holding, regrouping,
        embarkation or landing of passengers transported by
        water. ([terminal maritime]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national marine conservation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national marine conservation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      [[marine conservation area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or a
      [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada National Marine Conservation Areas
      Act](/eng/acts/C-7.3), or the Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine Park
      established under section 5 of the [Saguenay-St. Lawrence Marine
      Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3). ([aire marine nationale de
      conservation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[national park]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      [[park]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or a [[park
      reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as those terms are defined in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada National Parks
      Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([parc national]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[navigable water]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
      Act](/eng/acts/N-22). ([eaux navigables]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[new right of way]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new right of way]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means land that is to be
      developed for an international electrical transmission line, a
      [[pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
      [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1), a railway line
      or an all-season public highway, and that is not alongside and
      contiguous to an area of land that was developed for an electrical
      transmission line, oil and gas pipeline, railway line or
      all-season public highway. ([nouvelle emprise]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[nuclear substance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
      in section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control
      Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3). ([substance nucléaire]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[offshore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[offshore]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, except in respect of an
      offshore area, anything that is located in

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an area referred to in paragraph 3(d) or (e) of
        the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7) in
        respect of which an authorization under that Act is required to
        conduct an activity that is related to the exploration and
        drilling for, or the production, conservation, processing or
        transportation of, oil or gas; or

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area in respect of which an authorization
        under the [Canada--Newfoundland and Labrador Atlantic Accord
        Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.5) or the [Canada-Nova Scotia
        Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord Implementation
        Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8) is required to conduct an activity that is
        related to the exploration and drilling for, or the production,
        conservation, processing or transportation of, oil or gas. ([au
        large des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[offshore area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[offshore area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      section 2 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator
      Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([zone extracôtière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[oil and gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[oil and gas pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pipeline that
      is used, or is to be used, for the transmission of oil or gas
      alone or with any other commodity. ([pipeline
      d'hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[park community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[park community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
      subsection 2(1) of the [Canada National Parks
      Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01). ([collectivité]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[uranium mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[uranium mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      the definition [[mill]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 1 of the
      [Uranium Mines and Mills
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-206). ([usine de
      concentration d'uranium]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[uranium mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[uranium mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
      the definition [[mine]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 1 of the
      [Uranium Mines and Mills
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-206). ([mine
      d'uranium]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[water body]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any body of water,
      including a canal, a reservoir, an ocean and a wetland, up to the
      high-water mark, but does not include a sewage or waste treatment
      lagoon or a mine tailings pond. ([plan d'eau]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aircraft Group Number

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, an Aircraft
  Group Number refers to the Aircraft Group Number set out in Transport
  Canada's publication, TP 312, 5th edition entitled Aerodrome Standards
  and Recommended Practices.

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to a physical
  activity that is a [[project]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  section 66 of the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
  2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21), if, before the coming into force of the
  [Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75), an
  [[authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in that section, has
  made a determination under section 67 of the [Canadian Environmental
  Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) or has indicated in writing
  that it has started to make its determination for the purpose of that
  section of whether or not the carrying out of the project is likely to
  cause significant adverse environmental effects.

period is five years after the day on which these Regulations come into
force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Project on federal lands or outside
Canada

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  authority has, before the coming into force of the [Impact Assessment
  Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75), indicated in writing that it has started to
  make its determination, for the purpose of section 67 or 68 of the
  [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21), of
  whether or not the carrying out of a project is likely to cause
  significant adverse environmental effects, that determination is made
  under the [Canadian Environmental Assessment Act,
  2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) as if that Act had not been repealed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of sections 81 to 91 of
  the [Impact Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75)

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, before the coming into force of the [Impact
  Assessment Act](/eng/acts/I-2.75), an authority has made a
  determination under section 67 or 68 of the [Canadian Environmental
  Assessment Act, 2012](/eng/acts/C-15.21) with respect to a project,

  not apply to that project.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef}[Physical Activities]{.scheduleTitleText}  {#h-1194153 .scheduleLabel}
:::

#### [National Parks and Protected Areas]{.HTitleText2} {#national-parks-and-protected-areas .SchedHeadL2}

- **1** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment in
  a [[wildlife area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of
  the [Wildlife Area Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1609), a
  [[migratory bird sanctuary]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Migratory Bird Sanctuary
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1036) or a protected marine
  area established under subsection 4.1(1) of the [Canada Wildlife
  Act](/eng/acts/W-9), of one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new electrical generating facility or electrical
    transmission line;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new structure for the diversion of water,
    including a new dam, dyke or reservoir;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new oil or gas facility or oil and gas pipeline;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a new mine or mill;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a new industrial facility;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a new canal or lock;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a new marine terminal;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a new railway line;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a new public road or parkway that is intended for
    the passage of motor vehicles;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} a new aerodrome or runway;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} a new waste management facility;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} a new aquaculture facility.

- **2** The construction, in a national marine conservation area, of a
  new physical work if the construction is contrary to the management
  plan for that area tabled in or laid before each House of Parliament
  under subsection 9(1) of the [Canada National Marine Conservation
  Areas Act](/eng/acts/C-7.3) or subsection 9(1) of the [Saguenay-St.
  Lawrence Marine Park Act](/eng/acts/S-1.3).\

- **3** The disposal at sea, in a national marine conservation area, of
  [[waste or other matter]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
  subsection 122(1) of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
  1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) at a new disposal at sea site or a new part
  of an existing disposal at sea site.

- **4** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment, in
  a national marine conservation area, of a new pipeline for carrying a
  substance other than water.\
  \

- **5** The construction, on land that is managed or administered by the
  Parks Canada Agency, of a new physical work, if the construction is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} contrary to the management plan for that land that
    is tabled in each House of Parliament under subsection 32(1) of the
    [Parks Canada Agency Act](/eng/acts/P-0.4), subsection 11(1) of the
    [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01), or subsection 9(1)
    of the [Rouge National Urban Park Act](/eng/acts/R-8.55), or to a
    similar plan for the land that is approved by the Minister
    responsible for the Parks Canada Agency; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contrary to one of the following guidelines that is
    published by the Parks Canada Agency and that applies to that land:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Marmot Basin Ski Area Site Guidelines for
      Development and Use dated February 2008,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Mt. Norquay Ski Area Site Guidelines for
      Development and Use dated July 2011,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the Lake Louise Ski Area Site Guidelines for
      Development and Use dated July 2015,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the Site Guidelines for Development and Use,
      Sunshine Village Ski Resort dated December 14, 2018.

- **6** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment, in
  a national park, of a new dam or structure for the diversion of water
  for the purpose of supplying water outside the park, of recreation or
  of electrical power generation.

- **7** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment, in
  a national park, of a structure that is required to implement a new
  agreement made under paragraph 10(2)(b) of the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

- **8** The expansion, in a national park, of the water supply capacity
  of a structure that was constructed to implement an agreement made
  under paragraph 10(2)(b) of the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) by more than 20%.

- **9** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment, in
  Yoho National Park of Canada, Kootenay National Park of Canada, Banff
  National Park of Canada or Jasper National Park of Canada, outside of
  a commercial ski area referred to in Schedule 5 to the [Canada
  National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01) and of a park community, of a
  new commercial development that requires the disposal or occupation of
  land that was not previously disposed of for the purpose of a
  commercial development with the same or a similar purpose or occupied
  by such a commercial development, if that new commercial development
  has not been subject to strategic environmental assessment and public
  review as part of the park management plan tabled in each House of
  Parliament under subsection 11(1) of the [Canada National Parks
  Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

- **10** The expansion, in Yoho National Park of Canada, Kootenay
  National Park of Canada, Banff National Park of Canada or Jasper
  National Park of Canada, outside of a commercial ski area referred to
  in Schedule 5 to the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01)
  and of a park community, of an existing commercial development that
  requires the disposal or occupation of land that was not previously
  disposed of for the purpose of a commercial development with the same
  or a similar purpose or occupied by such a commercial development, if
  that existing commercial development has not been subject to strategic
  environmental assessment and public review as part of a park
  management plan tabled in each House of Parliament under subsection
  11(1) of the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

- **11** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment,
  in a national park, of either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new railway line;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new public road or parkway that is intended for
    the passage of motor vehicles.

#### [Defence]{.HTitleText2} {#defence .SchedHeadL2}

- **12** The low-level flying of military fixed-wing jet aircraft, for
  more than 150 days in a calendar year, as part of a training program,
  at an altitude below 330 m above ground level on a route or in an area
  that was not established before October 7, 1994 by or under the
  authority of the Minister of National Defence or the Chief of the
  Defence Staff as a route or area set aside for low-level flying
  training.

- **13** The construction and operation of a new military base or
  military station that is established for more than 12 consecutive
  months.

- **14** The expansion of an existing military base or military station,
  if the expansion would result in an increase in the area of the
  military base or military station of 50% or more.

- **15** The decommissioning and abandonment of an existing military
  base or military station.

- **16** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment,
  outside an existing military base, of a new military training area,
  range or test establishment for training or weapons testing that is
  established for more than 12 consecutive months.

- **17** The testing of military weapons for more than five days in a
  calendar year in an area other than a training area, range or test
  establishment established before October 7, 1994 by or under the
  authority of the Minister of National Defence for the testing of
  weapons.

#### [Mines and Metal Mills]{.HTitleText2} {#mines-and-metal-mills .SchedHeadL2}

- **18** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new coal mine with a coal production capacity of
    5 000 t/day or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new diamond mine with an ore production capacity
    of 5 000 t/day or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new metal mine, other than a rare earth element
    mine, placer mine or uranium mine, with an ore production capacity
    of 5 000 t/day or more;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a new metal mill, other than a uranium mill, with
    an ore input capacity of 5 000 t/day or more;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a new rare earth element mine with an ore
    production capacity of 2 500 t/day or more;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a new stone quarry or sand or gravel pit with a
    production capacity of 3 500 000 t/year or more.

- **19** The expansion of an existing mine, mill, quarry or sand or
  gravel pit in one of the following circumstances:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing coal mine, if the
    expansion would result in an increase in the area of mining
    operations of 50% or more and the total coal production capacity
    would be 5 000 t/day or more after the expansion;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing diamond mine if the
    expansion would result in an increase in the area of mining
    operations of 50% or more and the total ore production capacity
    would be 5 000 t/day or more after the expansion;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing metal mine, other than a
    rare earth element mine, placer mine or uranium mine, if the
    expansion would result in an increase in the area of mining
    operations of 50% or more and the total ore production capacity
    would be 5 000 t/day or more after the expansion;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing metal mill, other than a
    uranium mill, if the expansion would result in an increase in the
    area of mining operations of 50% or more and the total ore input
    capacity would be 5 000 t/day or more after the expansion;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing rare earth element mine
    if the expansion would result in an increase in the area of mining
    operations of 50% or more and the total ore production capacity
    would be 2 500 t/day or more after the expansion;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an existing stone quarry or sand or
    gravel pit if the expansion would result in an increase in the area
    of mining operations of 50% or more and the total production
    capacity would be 3 500 000 t/year or more after the expansion.

- **20** The construction, operation and decommissioning, outside the
  licensed boundaries of an existing uranium mine, of a new uranium mine
  with an ore production capacity of 2 500 t/day or more.

- **21** The expansion of an existing uranium mine, if the expansion
  would result in an increase in the area of mining operations of 50% or
  more and the total ore production capacity would be 2500 t/day or more
  after the expansion.

- **22** The construction, operation and decommissioning, outside the
  licensed boundaries of an existing uranium mill, of a new uranium mill
  with an ore input capacity of 2 500 t/day or more.

- **23** The expansion of an existing uranium mill, if the expansion
  would result in an increase in the area of mining operations of 50% or
  more and the total ore input capacity would be 2 500 t/day or more
  after the expansion.

- **24** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new oil sands mine with a bitumen production capacity of
  10 000 m^3^/day or more.

- **25** The expansion of an existing oil sands mine, if the expansion
  would result in an increase in the area of mining operations of 50% or
  more and the total bitumen production capacity would be
  10 000 m^3^/day or more after the expansion.

#### [Nuclear Facilities, Including Certain Storage and Long-term Management or Disposal Facilities]{.HTitleText2} {#nuclear-facilities-including-certain-storage-and-long-term-management-or-disposal-facilities .SchedHeadL2}

- **26** The construction, operation and decommissioning of one of the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the processing, reprocessing or
    separation of isotopes of uranium, thorium, or plutonium, with a
    production capacity of 100 t/year or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the manufacture of a product
    derived from uranium, thorium or plutonium, with a production
    capacity of 100 t/year or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the processing or use, in a
    quantity greater than 10^15^ Bq per calendar year, of nuclear
    substances with a half-life greater than one year, other than
    uranium, thorium or plutonium.

- **27** The site preparation for, and the construction, operation and
  decommissioning of, one or more new nuclear fission or fusion reactors
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that activity is located within the licensed
    boundaries of an existing Class IA nuclear facility and the new
    reactors have a combined thermal capacity of more than 900 MWth; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that activity is not located within the licensed
    boundaries of an existing Class IA nuclear facility and the new
    reactors have a combined thermal capacity of more than 200 MWth.

- **28** The construction and operation of either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the storage of irradiated
    nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, outside the licensed boundaries of an
    existing [[nuclear facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
    section 2 of the [Nuclear Safety and Control Act](/eng/acts/N-28.3),
    other than a facility for the on-site storage of irradiated nuclear
    fuel or nuclear waste associated with one or more new fission or
    fusion reactors that have a combined thermal capacity of less than
    200 MWth;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the long-term management or
    disposal of irradiated nuclear fuel or nuclear waste.

- **29** The expansion of an existing facility for the long-term
  management or disposal of irradiated nuclear fuel or nuclear waste, if
  the expansion would result in an increase in the area of the facility,
  at ground level, of 50% or more.

#### [Oil, Gas and Other Fossil Fuels]{.HTitleText2} {#oil-gas-and-other-fossil-fuels .SchedHeadL2}

- **30** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new fossil fuel-fired power generating facility with a production
  capacity of 200 MW or more.

- **31** The expansion of an existing fossil fuel-fired power generating
  facility, if the expansion would result in an increase in production
  capacity of 50% or more and a total production capacity of 200 MW or
  more.

- **32** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new [in situ]{.otherLang lang="la"} oil sands extraction facility
  that has a bitumen production capacity of 2 000 m^3^/day or more and
  that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not within a province in which provincial
    legislation is in force to limit the amount of greenhouse gas
    emissions produced by oil sands sites in the province; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within a province in which provincial legislation
    is in force to limit the amount of greenhouse gas emissions produced
    by oil sands sites in the province and that limit has been reached.

- **33** The expansion of an existing [in situ]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  oil sands extraction facility, if the expansion would result in an
  increase in bitumen production capacity of 50% or more and a total
  bitumen production capacity of 2 000 m^3^/day or more, if the facility
  is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} not within a province in which provincial
    legislation is in force to limit the amount of greenhouse gas
    emissions produced by oil sands sites in the province; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within a province in which provincial legislation
    is in force to limit the amount of greenhouse gas emissions produced
    by oil sands sites in the province and that limit has been reached.

- **34** The drilling, testing and abandonment, in an area set out in
  one or more exploration licences issued in accordance with the [Canada
  Petroleum Resources Act](/eng/acts/C-8.5), the [Canada--Newfoundland
  and Labrador Atlantic Accord Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.5) or
  the [Canada-Nova Scotia Offshore Petroleum Resources Accord
  Implementation Act](/eng/acts/C-7.8), of offshore exploratory wells in
  the first [[drilling program]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  subsection 1(1) of the [Canada Oil and Gas Drilling and Production
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1517), SOR/2009-315.

- **35** The construction, installation and operation of a new offshore
  floating or fixed platform, vessel or artificial island used for the
  production of oil or gas.

- **36** The decommissioning and abandonment of an existing offshore
  floating or fixed platform, vessel or artificial island used for the
  production of oil or gas that is proposed to be disposed of or
  abandoned offshore or converted on site to another role.

- **37** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new oil refinery, including a heavy oil upgrader,
    with an input capacity of 10 000 m^3^/day or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the production of liquid
    petroleum products from coal with a production capacity of
    2 000 m^3^/day or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new sour gas processing facility with a sulphur
    inlet capacity of 2 000 t/day or more;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a new facility for the liquefaction, storage or
    regasification of liquefied natural gas, with a liquefied natural
    gas processing capacity of 3 000 t/day or more or a liquefied
    natural gas storage capacity of 136 000 m^3^ or more;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a new petroleum storage facility with a storage
    capacity of 500 000 m^3^ or more;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a new natural gas liquids storage facility with a
    storage capacity of 100 000 m^3^ or more.

- **38** The expansion of one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an existing oil refinery, including a heavy oil
    upgrader, if the expansion would result in an increase in input
    capacity of 50% or more and a total input capacity of
    10 000 m^3^/day or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an existing facility for the production of liquid
    petroleum products from coal, if the expansion would result in an
    increase in production capacity of 50% or more and a total
    production capacity of 2 000 m^3^/day or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an existing sour gas processing facility, if the
    expansion would result in an increase in sulphur inlet capacity of
    50% or more and a total sulphur inlet capacity of 2 000 t/day or
    more;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an existing facility for the liquefaction, storage
    or regasification of liquefied natural gas, if the expansion would
    result in an increase in the liquefied natural gas processing or
    storage capacity of 50% or more and a total liquefied natural gas
    processing capacity of 3 000 t/day or more or a total liquefied
    natural gas storage capacity of 136 000 m^3^ or more, as the case
    may be;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an existing petroleum storage facility, if the
    expansion would result in an increase in storage capacity of 50% or
    more and a total storage capacity of 500 000 m^3^ or more;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an existing natural gas liquids storage facility,
    if the expansion would result in an increase in storage capacity of
    50% or more and a total storage capacity of 100 000 m^3^ or more.

#### [Electrical Transmission Lines and Pipelines]{.HTitleText2} {#electrical-transmission-lines-and-pipelines .SchedHeadL2}

- **39** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new international electrical transmission line
    with a voltage of 345 kV or more that requires a total of 75 km or
    more of new right of way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new interprovincial power line designated by an
    order under section 261 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1).

- **40** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new offshore oil and gas pipeline, other than a
  [[flowline]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Canada Oil and Gas Installations
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118).

- **41** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new [[pipeline]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of
  the [Canadian Energy Regulator Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1), other than an
  offshore pipeline, that requires a total of 75 km or more of new right
  of way.

#### [Renewable Energy]{.HTitleText2} {#renewable-energy .SchedHeadL2}

- **42** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new hydroelectric generating facility with a
    production capacity of 200 MW or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new in-stream tidal power generating facility
    with a production capacity of 15 MW or more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new tidal power generating facility that is not
    an in-stream tidal power generating facility.

- **43** The expansion of one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an existing hydroelectric generating facility if
    the expansion would result in an increase in production capacity of
    50% or more and a total production capacity of 200 MW or more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an existing in-stream tidal power generating
    facility, if the expansion would result in an increase in production
    capacity of 50% or more and a total production capacity of 15 MW or
    more;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an existing tidal power generating facility that is
    not an in-stream tidal power generating facility, if the expansion
    would result in an increase in production capacity of 50% or more.

- **44** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment in
  an offshore area or in boundary water of a new wind power generating
  facility that has 10 or more wind turbines.

- **45** The expansion in an offshore area or in boundary water of an
  existing wind power generating facility, if the expansion would result
  in an increase in production capacity of 50% or more and a total
  number of wind turbines of 10 or more.

#### [Transport]{.HTitleText2} {#transport .SchedHeadL2}

- **46** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  one of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new aerodrome with a runway length of 1 000 m or
    more;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new aerodrome that is capable of serving aircraft
    of Aircraft Group Number IIIA or higher;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a new runway at an existing aerodrome with a length
    of 1 000 m or more.\

- **47** The operation of an existing runway

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was not capable of serving aircraft of
    Aircraft Group Number IIIA and becomes capable of serving aircraft
    of Aircraft Group Number IIIA or higher; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that was capable of serving aircraft of an Aircraft
    Group Number IIIA or higher and becomes capable of serving aircraft
    of any higher Aircraft Group Number.

- **48** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new international or interprovincial bridge or
    tunnel;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new bridge over the St. Lawrence Seaway.

- **49** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new canal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new lock or associated structure that controls
    water levels in navigable water.

- **50** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new permanent causeway with a continuous length of 400 m or more
  through navigable water.

- **51** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new all-season public highway that requires a total of 75 km or more
  of new right of way.

- **52** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new marine terminal designed to handle ships larger than 25 000 DWT.

- **53** The expansion of an existing marine terminal, if the expansion
  requires the construction of a new berth designed to handle ships
  larger than 25 000 DWT and, if the berth is not a permanent structure
  in the water, the construction of a new permanent structure in the
  water.

- **54** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  either of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a new railway line that is capable of carrying
    freight or of carrying passengers between cities and requires a
    total of 50 km or more of new right of way;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a new railway yard with a total area of 50 ha or
    more.

- **55** The expansion of an existing railway yard, if the expansion
  would result in an increase of its total area by 50% or more and a
  total area of 50 ha or more.

#### [Hazardous Waste]{.HTitleText2} {#hazardous-waste .SchedHeadL2}

- **56** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new facility that is not more than 500 m from a natural water body
  and is used exclusively for the treatment, incineration, disposal or
  recycling of hazardous waste.

- **57** The expansion of an existing facility that is not more than
  500 m from a natural water body and is used exclusively for the
  treatment, incineration, disposal or recycling of hazardous waste, if
  the expansion would result in an increase in hazardous waste input
  capacity of 50% or more.

#### [Water Projects]{.HTitleText2} {#water-projects .SchedHeadL2}

- **58** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new dam or dyke on a natural water body, if the new dam or dyke
  would result in the creation of a reservoir with a surface area that
  would exceed the annual mean surface area of the natural water body by
  1 500 ha or more.

- **59** The expansion of an existing dam or dyke on a natural water
  body, if the expansion would result in an increase in the surface area
  of the existing reservoir of 50% or more and an increase of 1 500 ha
  or more in the annual mean surface area of that reservoir.

- **60** The construction, operation, decommissioning and abandonment of
  a new structure for the diversion of 10 000 000 m^3^/year or more of
  water from a natural water body into another natural water body.

- **61** The expansion of an existing structure for the diversion of
  water from a natural water body into another natural water body, if
  the expansion would result in an increase in diversion capacity of 50%
  or more and a total diversion capacity of 10 000 000 m^3^/year or
  more.
