## Order Designating the Minister of Health as the Minister for the purpose of that Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-health-as-the-minister-for-the-purpose-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2019-125
:::

[CANNABIS ACT](/eng/acts/C-24.5)

Registration 2019-12-11

Order Designating the Minister of Health as the Minister for the purpose
of that Act

P.C. 2019-1363 2019-11-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the [Cannabis
Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_320_hq_10667){#fn_320_hq_10667-ID0EBCCA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2018-1417 of November
  21, 2018[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-1906_hq_26064){#fn_81000-3-1906_hq_26064-ID0EBABCA};
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Health, a member of the
  Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of
  that Act.
