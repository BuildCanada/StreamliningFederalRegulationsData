## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826743 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the authorization
    referred to in subsection 335(1) or paragraph 335(2)(a) of the
    [Canadian Energy Regulator
    Act](/eng/acts/C-15.1). ([autorisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any structure, highway,
    private road, railway, irrigation ditch, drain, drainage system,
    sewer, dike, telephone line, telegraph line, telecommunication line,
    line for the transmission of electricity or pipe for the
    transmission of hydrocarbons or any other
    substance. ([installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pipe]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a pipe that is part of a pipeline
    and that is used or is to be used for the transmission of
    hydrocarbons or any other commodity. ([conduite]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[prescribed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    section 2 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage
    Prevention Regulations --
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124). ([zone
    réglementaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any day that is not a
    Saturday or a Sunday or other holiday. ([jour
    ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [One-call Centre]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826751 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Obligation to be a member

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pipeline company operates a pipeline within a geographical area where
  a one-call centre exists, the pipeline company must be a member of
  that centre.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}One-call centre

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A one-call centre is an organization that, for the
  purposes of protecting the underground infrastructures of its members
  from damage and ensuring public safety,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} receives locate requests from within a defined
    geographical area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notifies its members that may be affected by any
    proposed construction or any proposed activity that would cause a
    ground disturbance and that are the subject of a locate request of
    that construction or activity.

## [Consent]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826759 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inform person of decision

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  pipeline company receives a request for the written consent referred
  to in paragraph 7(1)(a) or 10(1)(a) or section 12 of the [Canadian
  Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
  Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124), the pipeline company
  must, within 10 working days after receiving the request, inform the
  person that made the request whether the consent has been granted or
  refused and, in the case of a refusal, the reasons for the refusal.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of consent

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} When a person makes a request to the pipeline company
  for the written consent referred to in paragraph 7(1)(a) or 10(1)(a)
  of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention
  Regulations -- Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) that
  contains the technical and other information that enable the pipeline
  company to determine whether the construction or activity would impair
  the pipeline's safety and security, the pipeline company may grant its
  consent subject to any conditions necessary for the protection of
  property and the environment, the safety and security of the public
  and of the company's employees or the pipeline's safety and security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment or addition of conditions

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} At any time during the construction of a facility or
  the activity that causes a ground disturbance, the pipeline company
  may add conditions or amend the conditions referred to in
  subsection (2) if it determines that additions or amendments are
  necessary to ensure the pipeline's safety and security.

## [Obligation to Provide Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826767 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information -- application for
authorization

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If a person that intends to
construct a facility across, on, along or under a pipeline, engage in an
activity that would cause a ground disturbance within the prescribed
area of a pipeline or operate a vehicle or mobile equipment across a
pipeline has made a request to the pipeline company for the information
that is necessary to make an application to the Regulator for
authorization, the pipeline company must, within 10 working days after
receiving the request, give the person all the information, and provide
all reasonable assistance, that is necessary to prepare the application.

## [Obligations Following Request to Locate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826772 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timeline

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), if a pipeline company receives a request to locate
  its pipes from a person that intends to construct a facility across,
  on, along or under a pipeline or engage in an activity that would
  cause a ground disturbance within the prescribed area, the pipeline
  company must, within three working days after the day on which the
  request is made, or any longer period agreed to by the pipeline
  company and that person,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} inform the person, in writing, of safety practices
    to be followed while working in the vicinity of its pipes and, in
    case of a ground disturbance, within the prescribed area;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} mark the location of its pipes in the vicinity of
    the proposed facility or the prescribed area at maximum intervals of
    10 m along each pipe using markings that are clearly visible and
    distinct from any other markings that may be in the vicinity of the
    proposed facility or the prescribed area; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide information to the person that clearly
    explains the significance of the markings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Markings

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The markings must be consistent with the standards
  for locating a pipeline that are set out in the pipeline company's
  damage prevention program.

## [Obligations Respecting Certain Locations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826781 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Agricultural activity

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Even if the condition set
out in paragraph 13(1)(a) of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline
Damage Prevention Regulations --
Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) is met, when the
operation of vehicles or mobile equipment across a pipeline at specific
locations for the purposes of performing an agricultural activity could
impair the pipeline's safety or security, the pipeline company must
identify those locations and notify the following persons in writing of
those locations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} landowners of the specific locations in question; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons that are engaged in agricultural activities,
  rent or lease the land or work as service providers or employees at
  the specific locations in question.

## [Inspections]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826786 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspections and field observations

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The pipeline company must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} carry out any inspections that are necessary to
  ensure the pipeline's continued safety and security during an activity
  that causes a ground disturbance within the prescribed area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inspect all exposed pipe prior to any backfilling
  over a pipe to ensure that no damage to the pipe has occurred; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any inspection carried out under
  paragraphs (a) and (b), make field observations relating to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if a pipe was exposed, the clearance between the
    pipe and the facility and the condition of the pipe at the time of
    backfilling over the pipe,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the compliance with the measures set out in the
    [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the method used to engage in the activity that
    caused a ground disturbance, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the occurrence of any unusual events that are
    related to the construction or activity and that may have had an
    effect on the pipeline's safety or security.

## [Suspension]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826801 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  pipeline company may suspend the consent that it granted to construct
  a facility or to engage in an activity that causes a ground
  disturbance within the prescribed area if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person carrying out the construction of a
    facility does not comply with the technical details and the
    conditions referred to in paragraph 7(3)(a) of the [Canadian Energy
    Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) or the instructions
    referred to in paragraph 7(3)(c) of those Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person engaging in an activity that causes the
    ground disturbance does not comply with the technical details and
    the conditions referred to in paragraph 10(3)(a) of the [Canadian
    Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) or the instructions
    referred to in paragraph 10(3)(d) of those Regulations; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} work practices might impair the pipeline's safety
    or security.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notify Regulator

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a pipeline company suspends its consent pursuant
  to subsection (1), it must immediately notify the Regulator in writing
  of the suspension and give its reasons for the suspension.

## [Obligation to Report]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826810 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report to Regulator

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  pipeline company must immediately report to the Regulator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} every contravention of the [Canadian Energy
    Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
    Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all damage to its pipe caused or identified during
    the construction of a facility across, on, along or under a
    pipeline, the operation, maintenance or removal of a facility, an
    activity that caused a ground disturbance within the prescribed area
    or the operation of vehicles or mobile equipment across the
    pipeline; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any activity related to the construction of a
    facility across, on, along or under a pipeline, an activity that
    caused a ground disturbance within the prescribed area or the
    operation of vehicles or mobile equipment across a pipeline that the
    pipeline company considers could impair the safety or security of
    the pipe.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} details of any contravention or of any damage,
    including, in the case of damage, the cause and nature of the
    damage;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any concerns that the pipeline company may have
    regarding the pipeline's safety or security as a result of the
    construction of the facility, the activity that caused a ground
    disturbance or the operation of vehicles or mobile equipment across
    the pipeline; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any action the pipeline company intends to take or
    request.

## [Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facilities and ground disturbances

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  pipeline company must keep a record of all construction of facilities
  across, on, along or under a pipeline and of all activities that cause
  a ground disturbance within the prescribed area for the life of the
  pipeline.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records must include, for each facility or each
  activity that causes a ground disturbance, as the case may be,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person that carries out
    the construction or engages in an activity that causes a ground
    disturbance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the nature and location of the facility or the
    activity that causes a ground disturbance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the dates of commencement and termination of the
    construction of the facility or the activity that causes a ground
    disturbance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed facility, submitted
    with the request for the consent;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the pipeline company's written consent;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the inspections referred to in
    paragraphs 8(a) and (b), all findings and observations, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person that conducted the
      inspection,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the inspection, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any field observations referred to in paragraph
      8(c);

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a statement of whether the person that carried out
    the construction or the person that engaged in an activity that
    caused a ground disturbance has complied with the measures set out
    in the [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention
    Regulations -- Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124); and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the details of any abandonment, removal or
    alteration of the facility.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consent --- crossings

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The pipeline company must keep a record that contains
  a copy of the written consents granted by the pipeline company for the
  purposes of section 12 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Pipeline
  Damage Prevention Regulations --
  Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) for the life of the
  pipeline or, if there is an expiry date set out in the consent, for a
  period of 12 months from the day on which the consent expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Locations

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The pipeline company must keep a record of the
  locations that are identified under section 7.

## [Guidelines]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826850 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for consent

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Every pipeline company
must develop and maintain detailed guidelines setting out the technical
and other information to be included in the requests for the written
consent referred to in paragraph 7(1)(a) or 10(1)(a) of the [Canadian
Energy Regulator Pipeline Damage Prevention Regulations --
Authorizations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-124) and must make those
guidelines public.

## [Damage Prevention Program]{.HTitleText1} {#h-826853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minimum content

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The damage prevention
program that a pipeline company is required to develop, implement and
maintain under section 47.2 of the [Canadian Energy Regulator Onshore
Pipeline Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-99-294) must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an ongoing public awareness program to inform the
  public

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} of the presence of a pipeline,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on how to work safely near a pipeline,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} on how to report an unexpected situation related
    to a pipeline that could endanger life or cause substantial property
    or environmental damage that requires immediate action,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} on how to report any contact with a pipe or its
    coating, whether or not the pipe was damaged,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} on how to report any damage to a pipe,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} of the services of a one-call centre if one exists
    within the relevant geographical area,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} on the necessity for authorization when
    constructing a facility across, on, along or under a pipeline,
    engaging in an activity that causes a ground disturbance within the
    prescribed area or operating vehicles or mobile equipment across a
    pipeline,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} of the information to be provided in a request
    for the consent to construct a facility across, on, along or under a
    pipeline, to engage in an activity that causes a ground disturbance
    within the prescribed area or to operate a vehicle or mobile
    equipment across a pipeline, and

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} on the requirement to make a locate request and
    how to make a locate request in the relevant geographical area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} ongoing monitoring of any changes in the use of the
  land on which a pipeline is located and the land that is adjacent to
  that land;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} ongoing monitoring of any change in the landowner of
  the land on which a pipeline is located;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a process to ensure a timely response to locate
  requests;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} standards for locating a pipeline; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a process for managing requests for the consent to
  construct a facility across, on, along or under a pipeline, to engage
  in an activity that causes a ground disturbance within the prescribed
  area or to operate a vehicle or mobile equipment across the pipeline.
