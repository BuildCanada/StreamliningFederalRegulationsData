## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1328983 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Accessible Canada
      Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
      2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person employed by a
      regulated entity described in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act
      and includes a [[dependent contractor]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as
      defined in subsection 3(1) of the [Canada Labour
      Code](/eng/acts/L-2), but excludes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person employed under a program designated by
        the employer as a student employment program; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a student employed solely during the student's
        vacation periods. ([employé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[planning and reporting cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[planning and reporting cycle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      period of three consecutive years the first year of which is a
      year in which there is a date by which a regulated entity is
      required to publish a version of an accessibility plan under
      section 47, 56, 65 or 69 of the Act, as the case may be and in
      which the second and third years are years in which there is a
      date by which a regulated entity is required to publish a progress
      report under section 49, 58, 67 or 71 of the Act, as the case may
      be. ([cycle de planification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[WCAG]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the most recent version that is
      available in both English and French of the Web Content
      Accessibility Guidelines, published by the World Wide Web
      Consortium. ([WCAG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a calendar
      year. ([année]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average number of employees --- year

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the average
  number of employees during a year is the sum of the number of
  employees at the time in each month during that year when the number
  of employees is the greatest, divided by 12 and rounded to the nearest
  whole number or, if the value is equidistant between two whole
  numbers, rounded up to the nearest whole number.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Average number of employees ---
  planning and reporting cycle

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, the average
  number of employees during a planning and reporting cycle is the sum
  of the average number of employees during each year of the planning
  and reporting cycle, divided by three and rounded to the nearest whole
  number or, if the value is equidistant between two whole numbers,
  rounded up to the nearest whole number.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1328999 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Band

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a band, the
following entities and persons are exempted from the application of
sections 47 to 49, 56 to 58, 65 to 67 and 69 to 71 of the Act and
sections 3 to 19 of these Regulations until the day before the fifth
anniversary of the day on which these Regulations come into force:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity or person --- other than one that carries
  on a business --- that carries on an undertaking that is not within
  the legislative authority of a province for or on behalf of the
  [[council of the band]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5), or on the band's
  reserve lands.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Planning, Feedback and Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1329004 .Part}

### [Exemptions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1329005 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fewer than 10 employees --- year

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  regulated entity described in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act is
  exempt from the application of sections 47 to 49, 56 to 58, 65 to 67
  and 69 to 71 of the Act if that entity has an average of fewer than 10
  employees during the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the year before the year in which the entity's
    first planning and reporting cycle would have started but for this
    exemption, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the year in which the regulated entity is
    established.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fewer than 10 employees --- planning
  and reporting cycle

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulated entity described in paragraph 7(1)(e)
  or (f) of the Act that has an average of fewer than 10 employees
  during a planning and reporting cycle is exempt from the application
  of sections 47 to 49, 56 to 58, 65 to 67 and 69 to 71 of the Act,
  effective the last day of the planning and reporting cycle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cessation of exemption

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An exemption under subsection (1) or (2) ceases to
  have effect during any year in which the regulated entity has an
  average of 10 employees or more.

### [Accessibility Plans]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1329015 .Subheading}

#### [Dates for Preparation, Publication and Updates]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1329016 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Initial accessibility plan

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), for the purposes of subsections 47(1),
  56(1), 65(1) and 69(1) of the Act, the fixed day is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a regulated entity described in any of
    paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (d) of the Act, December 31, 2021;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a regulated entity described in paragraph
    7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 100 employees or
    more during the year in which these Regulations come into force,
    June 1, 2022; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a regulated entity described in paragraph
    7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 10 to 99 employees
    during the year in which these Regulations come into force, June 1,
    2023.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}New regulated entity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated entity is established or becomes
  subject to the Act during any year after the year in which these
  Regulations come into force, the fixed day for that entity is June 1
  of the year following the year in which the entity is established or
  becomes subject to the Act, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entity no longer exempt

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated entity ceases to be exempt in
  accordance with subsection 3(3) and has never prepared and published
  an initial accessibility plan under subsection 47(1), 56(1), 65(1) or
  69(1) of the Act, the fixed day for that entity is the later of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} June 1 of the second year after the year in which
    these Regulations come into force, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} June 1 of the first year in which the regulated
    entity has an average of 10 employees or more after the year in
    which it has ceased to be exempted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Updated accessibility plan

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (5), for the purposes of
  subsections 47(2), 56(2), 65(2) and 69(2) of the Act, the applicable
  period is 36 months beginning on the date by which the plan was last
  required to be published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Entity no longer exempt

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The applicable period for a regulated entity that has
  ceased to be exempt in accordance with subsection 3(3) and that,
  before becoming exempt, had completed at least one planning and
  reporting cycle is 12 months after June 1 of the first year in which
  the entity has an average of 10 employees or more after the year in
  which it has ceased to be exempted.

#### [Form]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1329033 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Language and headings

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  accessibility plan that is required by subsection 47(1) or (2), 56(1)
  or (2), 65(1) or (2), or 69(1) or (2) of the Act must be written in
  simple, clear and concise language and must include the following
  headings:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "General";

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a heading for each area identified under paragraph
    47(1)(a), 56(1)(a), 65(1)(a) or 69(1)(a) of the Act, as the case may
    be; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "Consultations".

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"General" heading

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information that is contained under the heading
  "General" must include the position title of the person designated to
  receive feedback on behalf of the regulated entity and the manner and
  information by which the public can communicate with the regulated
  entity, including the mailing address of its publicly accessible
  businesses, a telephone number and an email address in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} request the entity's accessibility plan in a format
    referred to in subsection 8(2);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} request the description of the entity's feedback
    process in a format referred to in subsection 9(5); or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide feedback.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}"Consultations" heading

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information that is contained under the heading
  "Consultations" is the information referred to in subsection 47(5),
  56(5), 65(5) or 69(5) of the Act, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  accessibility plan that is required by subsection 47(1) or (2), 56(1)
  or (2), 65(1) or (2) or 69(1) or (2) of the Act must be published

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements for Level
    AA conformance that are set out in the WCAG;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the regulated
    entity owns, operates or controls and that it uses to communicate
    with the public; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that makes the plan accessible on the
    digital platform either directly on the home screen or homepage or
    by way of a hyperlink from that home screen or homepage.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No digital platform accessible to
  public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulated entity that does not have a digital
  platform that is made available to the public must publish its
  accessibility plan by displaying a printed copy of the plan in a
  location where it is clearly visible and accessible to the public in
  the reception area or entrance of each of its places of business.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Accessibility Commissioner

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 47(3), 56(3), 65(3) and 69(3) of the Act, within 48 hours
after a version of the accessibility plan is published, a regulated
entity must notify the Accessibility Commissioner of the publication by
email or other electronic means and provide in the notice to the
Accessibility Commissioner either the URL address of the plan, the
hyperlink to that URL address or the mailing addresses of the places of
business where that version of the plan is published.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of request

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for an accessibility plan referred to in subsection 47(8),
  56(8), 65(8) or 69(8) of the Act must be made by mail, telephone,
  email or by any other means the regulated entity uses to communicate
  with the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may make a request under subsection 47(7),
  56(7), 65(7) or 69(7) of the Act that a regulated entity make its
  accessibility plan available to them in print, large print, Braille,
  audio format or an electronic format that is compatible with adaptive
  technology that is intended to assist persons with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadlines --- accessibility plan

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections 47(7), 56(7), 65(7)
  and 69(7) of the Act, the accessibility plan must be made available to
  the person making a request as soon as feasible but, at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 45 days after the day on which the request is
    received, in the case of a request for a plan in Braille or audio
    format; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for a plan in any other
    format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 15 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in any of
      paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (d) of the Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 15 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in paragraph
      7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 100 or more
      employees during the year preceding the day on which the request
      is received, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 20 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in paragraph
      7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 99 or fewer
      employees during the year preceding the day on which the request
      is received.

### [Feedback Process]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1329069 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designated person to receive feedback

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} When
  establishing a feedback process under subsection 48(1), 57(1), 66(1)
  or 70(1) of the Act, a regulated entity must designate a person
  responsible for receiving feedback on behalf of the entity and provide
  the position title of that person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Anonymous feedback

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The regulated entity must ensure that feedback
  process allows a person to provide feedback anonymously.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Feedback --- means

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The regulated entity must ensure that feedback
  process allows a person to provide feedback by mail, telephone, email
  and by any other means the regulated entity uses to communicate with
  the public.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Acknowledgment of feedback

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The regulated entity must ensure that acknowledgment
  of receipt is included in the feedback process, unless the feedback is
  received anonymously, in the same means by which it is received.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} A person may request that a regulated entity make the
  description of its feedback process referred to in section 48, 57, 66
  or 70 of the Act available to them in print, large print, Braille,
  audio format or an electronic format that is compatible with adaptive
  technology that assists persons with disabilities.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deadlines --- description of feedback
  process

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The regulated entity must make the description of its
  feedback process available to the person in the requested format as
  soon as feasible but, at the latest,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 45 days after the day on which the request is
    received, in the case of a request for the description in Braille or
    audio format; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a request for the description in any
    other format,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 15 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in any of
      paragraphs 7(1)(a) to (d) of the Act,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 15 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in paragraph
      7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 100 or more
      employees during the year preceding the day on which the request
      is received, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} 20 days after the day on which the request is
      received, in the case of a regulated entity described in paragraph
      7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that has an average of 99 or fewer
      employees during the year preceding the day on which the request
      is received.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsections 48(2), 57(2), 66(2) and 70(2) of the Act,
  the description of the feedback process must be published

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in simple, clear and concise language;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a format that meets the requirements for Level
    AA conformance that are set out in the WCAG;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the main digital platform that the regulated
    entity owns, operates or controls and that it uses to communicate
    with the public;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in a manner that makes the description accessible
    on the digital platform either directly on the home screen or
    homepage or by way of a hyperlink from that home screen or homepage;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the same time as the regulated entity publishes
    its initial accessibility plan under subsection 47(1), 56(1), 65(1)
    or 69(1) of the Act, as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No digital platform accessible to
  public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A regulated entity that does not have a digital
  platform that is made available to the public must publish a
  description of its feedback process by displaying a printed copy of
  the description together with its accessibility plan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amendment of feedback process

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** If a regulated entity
amends its feedback process established under subsection 48(1), 57(1),
66(1) or 70(1) of the Act, it must publish a description of the new
version of the process in accordance with section 10 other than
paragraph 10(1)(e), as soon as feasible.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to Accessibility Commissioner

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 48(3), 57(3), 66(3) and 70(3) of the Act, within 48 hours

publication by email or other electronic means and provide in the notice
to the Accessibility Commissioner either the URL address of the
description, the hyperlink to that URL address or the mailing addresses

### [Document Retention]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1329143 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Plan and report

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  regulated entity has prepared and published a version of an
  accessibility plan under section 47, 56, 65 or 69 of the Act or a
  progress report under section 49, 58, 67 or 71 of the Act and it has a
  digital platform that is accessible to the public, the regulated
  entity must retain that version of the plan or that report, as the
  case may be, on the platform for a period of seven years beginning on
  the date by which that version of the plan or that report, as the case
  may be, was required to be published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No digital platform accessible to
  public

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated entity has prepared and published a
  version of an accessibility plan under section 47, 56, 65 or 69 of the
  Act or a progress report under section 49, 58, 67 or 71 of the Act and
  it does not have a digital platform that is accessible to the public,
  the regulated entity must retain an electronic or print copy of that
  version of the plan or of the report, as the case may be, in such a
  manner that it is accessible to the public for a period of seven years
  beginning on the date by which that version of the plan or that
  report, as the case may be, was required to be published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Description

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated entity has published a description of
  its feedback process under subsection 48(2), 57(2), 66(2) or 70(2) of
  the Act and it has a digital platform that is accessible to the
  public, the regulated entity must retain the most recent version of
  the description on the platform for a period of seven years beginning
  on the day on which the description is published or, if it is longer,
  for a period beginning on the day on which the description is
  published and ending on the day on which a new description of the
  feedback process is published.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}No digital platform accessible to
  public

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a regulated entity has published a description of
  its feedback process under subsection 48(2), 57(2), 66(2) or 70(2) of
  the Act and it does not have a digital platform that is accessible to
  the public, the regulated entity must retain an electronic or print
  copy of the most recent version of the description in such a manner
  that it is accessible to the public for a period of seven years
  beginning on the day on which the description is published or, if it
  is longer, for a period beginning on the day of its publication and
  ending on the day on which a new description of the feedback process
  is published.

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A regulated entity must
retain an electronic copy or print copy of any feedback it receives
under section 9 for a period of seven years beginning on the day on
which it is received.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Service of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1329155 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service --- individual

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** Service of any notice or
order made under the Act on an individual named in the notice or order
may be made

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} personally, by leaving a copy of it with the
  individual at any place or, if it is not feasible to find the
  individual, with someone who appears to be an adult member of the same
  household at the individual's last known address or usual place of
  residence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it by registered mail or courier
  to the individual's last known address or usual place of residence; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} by sending a copy of it to the individual by fax,
  email or other electronic means, followed by a copy of the notice or
  order to be sent either by registered mail or courier within 48 hours
  after the day of the original transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Service --- regulated entity

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** Service of any notice or
order made under the Act on a regulated entity named in the notice or
order may be made by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} leaving a copy of it at the entity's head office or
  place of business with its agent or mandatary or with an officer or
  other individual who appears to manage or be in control of the head
  office or place of business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by registered mail or courier to
  the head office or place of business of the entity or its agent or
  mandatary; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} sending a copy of it by fax, email or other
  electronic means to any individual referred to in paragraph (a),
  followed by a copy of the notice or order to be sent either by
  registered mail or courier within 48 hours after the day of the
  original transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of service --- signature

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  certificate of service of a notice or order on a regulated entity or
  individual named in the certificate states the means of service, the
  individual who signs the certificate is deemed to have served the
  notice or order on the date that is determined under subsection (2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Date of deemed service

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice or order served in accordance with section
  20 or 21 is deemed to have been served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service made under paragraph 20(a)
    or 21(a), on the day on which it is left with an individual in
    paragraph 20(a) or 21(a), as the case may be;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service made under paragraph 20(b)
    or 21(b), on the 10th day after the date indicated on the receipt
    issued by the post office or courier; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of service made under paragraph 20(c)
    or 21(c), on the day of its original transmission.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1329174 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[small
business]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of this
Part and Schedule 2, [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
regulated entity referred to in paragraph 7(1)(e) or (f) of the Act that
has an average of fewer than 100 employees during the year before the
year in which it is served with a notice of violation under the Act or,
if the regulated entity has been established for less than one year, an
average of fewer than 100 employees on the day on which the notice of
violation is issued.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Classification

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** A violation of a
provision that is set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 1 is
classified as a minor, serious or very serious violation as set out in
column 2 of that Part.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalty range

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  range of penalties in respect of a violation within a classification
  set out in column 1 of a Part of Schedule 2 that is committed by a
  person other than a regulated entity, a small business or a regulated
  entity that is not a small business is set out in column 2 of that
  Part for a first violation, in column 3 for a second violation, in
  column 4 for a third violation and in column 5 for a fourth or
  subsequent violation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prior violations

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), only prior
  violations that the regulated entity or person is deemed or determined
  to have committed during the five years before the day on which the
  notice of violation is served are taken into account.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determining penalty amount

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The

  ::: {#1329187 .Subsection}
  ((A -- B) × C ÷ 28) + B

  [A]{.dfn}

      of Schedule 2;

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the minimum amount in the applicable range of penalties set out
      in column 2, 3, 4 or 5, as the case may be, of the applicable Part
      of Schedule 2; and

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the gravity value.
  :::
