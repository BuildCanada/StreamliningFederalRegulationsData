## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517376 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501).

## [Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517379 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and manner

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A declaration under
subsection 39.02(1) of the Act must be made at the same time and in the
same manner as the reporting of imported or exported goods under
sections 12 or 95 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Person in charge of conveyance

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 39.02(3)(a) of the Act, goods imported on board a
[[non-commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
defined in subsection 1(2) of the Cross-border Currency and Monetary
Instruments Reporting Regulations, may be declared by the person in
charge of the conveyance if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reporting of the goods under the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) is done by radio or telephone; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods are in the actual possession, or form part
  of the baggage, of a person who presents themselves by telephone in
  accordance with the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- imported goods

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or entity that imports goods or causes or arranges for goods to
  be imported for a purpose referred to in subsection 39.02(6) of the
  Act must keep, until the sixth anniversary of the day on which the
  goods were imported,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the origin, marking,
    purchase, importation, costs and value of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to payment for the goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the sale or other disposal
    of the goods in Canada; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all records and books of account that must be kept
    in respect of the goods under section 230 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- CSA importer

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity is a [[CSA
  importer]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 2 of the
  [Accounting for Imported Goods and Payment of Duties
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-1062), the person or entity must
  also keep, until the sixth anniversary of the day on which the goods
  were imported,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records describing the goods or specifying
    their quantity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the receipt of the goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all records showing the credits and adjustments
    used to determine the amount paid for the goods;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the accounting for the
    goods;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to any refund, drawback or
    re-determination of duties in respect of the goods;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a list of all vendors and consignees of the goods;
    and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the information needed to
    complete the form entitled "CSA Revenue Summary" in respect of the
    goods and to the payment of duties on the goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sufferance warehouse

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity has been issued a licence
  under paragraph 24(1)(a) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) for
  the operation of a place as a sufferance warehouse, the person or
  entity must also keep, until the sixth anniversary of the day on which
  the imported goods were received into the warehouse, all records
  containing information relating to the receipt of the goods into the
  warehouse and their removal from it.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Bonded warehouse

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity has been issued a licence
  under subsection 91(1) of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) for
  the operation of a place as a bonded warehouse, the person or entity
  must also keep, until the sixth anniversary of the day on which the
  imported goods were removed from the warehouse, all records relating
  to the goods received into the warehouse or their removal from it,
  including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information describing the
    goods at the time of receipt into the warehouse or removal from it
    for export;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information accounting for
    the goods at the time of removal from the warehouse;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information relating to the
    inventory of goods that are stored in the warehouse and the
    transactions that occur while the goods are stored in the warehouse;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information relating to the
    transfer of ownership of the goods;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information relating to the
    transfer of the goods to another bonded warehouse, to a sufferance
    warehouse or to a duty free shop; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} all records containing information relating to the
    unpacking, packing, manipulation or alteration of the goods, or any
    combining of the goods with other goods.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of record keeping

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The records referred to in subsections (1) to (4)
  must be kept

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of records and books of account
    referred to in paragraph (1)(d), in the manner specified in section
    230 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, in the manner prescribed under
    subsection 40(3) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- exported goods

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or entity that exports goods or that causes or arranges for
  such goods to be exported for a purpose referred to in subsection
  39.02(6) of the Act must keep, until the sixth anniversary of the day
  on which the goods were exported,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the origin, marking,
    purchase, importation, costs and value of the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to payment for the goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the usage to which the
    goods are put in Canada;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the exportation of the
    goods;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the source of all
    materials, including indirect materials, used in the production of
    the goods; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} all records and books of account that must be kept
    in respect of the goods under section 230 of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exportation to free trade partner

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity has completed and signed a
  certificate in accordance with subsection 97.1(1) of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the person or entity must also keep, until the
  sixth anniversary of the day on which the certificate was signed,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to an application for or
    receipt of any [[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
    in section 1 of the [Exporters' and Producers' Records
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-71), by the customs
    administration of the applicable free trade partner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person or entity is not the producer of the
    goods, all written representations received from the producer
    stating that the goods meet the rules of origin set out in or
    contemplated by the applicable free trade agreement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of record keeping

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The records referred to in subsections (1) and (2)
  must be kept

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of records and books of account
    referred to in paragraph (1)(f), in the manner specified in section
    230 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, in the manner prescribed under
    subsection 97.2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- producers

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or entity that produces goods for a purpose referred to in
  subsection 39.02(6) of the Act and that has completed and signed a
  certificate in accordance with subsection 97.1(1) of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) must keep, until the sixth anniversary of the
  day on which the certificate was signed, all records relating to the
  production of the goods, including

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the purchase, cost of,
    value of, and payment for, the goods;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to the purchase, cost of,
    value of, and payment for, all materials, including indirect
    materials, used in the production of the goods; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all records relating to an application for or
    receipt of any [[advance ruling]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined
    in section 1 of the [Exporters' and Producers' Records
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-71), by the customs
    administration of the applicable free trade partner.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of record keeping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records referred to in subsection (1) must be
  kept in the manner prescribed under subsection 97.2(1) of the [Customs
  Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Record keeping --- suppliers,
distributors and consumers

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person or entity that supplies, distributes or consumes goods for a
  purpose referred to in subsection 39.02(6) of the Act and that has
  completed and signed a certificate in accordance with subsection
  97.1(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) must keep, until the
  sixth anniversary of the day on which the certificate was signed, all
  records and books of account that must be kept in respect of the goods
  under section 230 of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of record keeping

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The records and books of account referred to in
  subsection (1) must be kept in the manner specified in section 230 of
  the Income Tax Act.

## [Retention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517446 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of notice

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 39.03(1) of the Act, the retention notice must
  be given

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a person, by handing the notice to
    the person or sending it by registered mail to the person's last
    known address; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity, by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} leaving the notice at the entity's head office or
      place of business, with an officer or other person who appears to
      be in control of or managing the head office or place of business,
      or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} sending the notice by registered mail to the
      entity's head office or place of business.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Importation or exportation by courier
  or as mail

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 39.03(2) of the Act,
  the retention notice must be given within 60 days after the day on
  which the goods are imported or exported, as the case may be.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention period

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 39.03(1) of the Act, the retention period is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of goods imported or exported by courier
  or as mail, 30 days after the day on which the retention notice is
  sent or otherwise given; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, seven days after the day on which
  the retention notice is sent or otherwise given.

## [Administrative Monetary Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517460 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Violations

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Any person or entity that
contravenes subsection 39.02(1), (5), (6) or (8) of the Act commits a
violation and is liable to a penalty determined in accordance with
section 14.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of violation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  officer has reasonable grounds to believe that a person or entity has
  committed a violation, the officer may issue a notice of violation and
  cause it to be served on the person or entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of violation must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} name the person or entity believed to have
    committed the violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} identify the violation and the provision at issue;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the amount of the penalty for the violation
    and the manner of paying the penalty;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the person or entity has the right to
    apply for a review with respect to the violation or the amount of
    the penalty, and the manner for doing so;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} indicate that the person or entity must pay the
    penalty or apply for a review within 30 days after the day on which
    the notice is served, or any longer period that the President
    specifies; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} indicate that, if the person or entity does not pay
    the penalty or apply for a review within the time and manner set out
    in the notice, the person or entity will be deemed to have committed
    the violation and will be liable to pay the penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Administrative corrections

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the notice of violation contains any error or
  omission, the officer may issue a corrected notice of violation and
  cause it to be served on the person or entity at any time during the
  period referred to in paragraph (2)(e).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of penalty

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  person or entity pays the penalty in accordance with the notice of
  violation, the person or entity is deemed to have committed the
  violation and proceedings in respect of it are ended.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity applies for a review in
  accordance with the notice of violation, the President must decide, on
  a balance of probabilities, whether the person or entity committed the
  violation and, if so, may impose the penalty set out in the notice, a
  lesser penalty or no penalty.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to act

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person or entity neither pays the penalty nor
  applies for a review within the time and manner set out in the notice
  of violation, the person or entity is deemed to have committed the
  violation and is liable to the penalty in the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of decision

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The President must cause notice of any decision made
  under subsection (2) or penalty imposed under subsection (3) to be
  served on the person or entity together with notice of the right of
  appeal under section 13.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appeal to Federal Court

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person or entity on which a notice of a decision made under subsection
  12(2) is served may appeal the decision to the Federal Court within 30
  days after the day on which the notice is served, or within any longer
  period that the Court allows.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Powers of Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On appeal, the Court may confirm, set aside or,
  subject to section 14, vary the decision.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Penalties

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  range of penalties in respect of a violation is from \$150 to \$500 if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person or entity that committed the violation
    makes a full disclosure of the relevant facts when the person or
    entity is made aware of the violation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} there are no reasonable grounds to believe that the
    violation was intentionally committed.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of the goods or financial
  transaction

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In any other case, the penalty is the greater of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fair market value of the imported or exported
    goods,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the declared value of the imported or exported
    goods, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the value of the financial transaction purporting
    to pay for the goods being imported or exported.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Multiple violations

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If multiple violations are committed with respect to
  goods imported or exported on board the same
  [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 2(1) of
  the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), the violations are treated as a
  single violation for the purposes of subsections (1) and (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Criteria

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The amount of a penalty
referred to in subsection 14(1) in respect of the violation is to be
established having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the value of the goods or of the financial
  transaction involved; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the history of the person or entity that committed
  the violation with respect to any prior violation within the five-year
  period immediately before the violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Due diligence defence not available

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Due diligence is not a
defence in a proceeding in relation to a violation.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debts due to His Majesty

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following amounts constitute a debt due to His Majesty in right of
  Canada that may be recovered in the Federal Court:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the penalty set out in a notice of
    violation, beginning on the day on which it is required to be paid
    in accordance with the notice, unless the person or entity applies
    for a review in accordance with the notice of violation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the penalty imposed by the President
    under subsection 12(2), beginning on the day on which the period
    specified in the notice of decision expires or the day specified in
    the notice of decision; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the penalty determined by the Federal
    Court under subsection 13(2), beginning on the day on which the
    period specified in the decision for the payment of that amount
    expires or the day specified in the decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Proceedings to recover a debt referred to in
  subsection (1) may be commenced no later than the fifth anniversary of
  the day on which the debt becomes payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Proceeds payable to Receiver General

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A debt referred to in subsection (1) that is paid or
  recovered is payable to and must be remitted to the Receiver General.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  unpaid amount of any debt referred to in subsection 17(1) may be
  certified by the Governor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration in Federal Court

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Registration in the Federal Court of a certificate
  has the same effect as a judgment of that Court for a debt of the
  amount specified in the certificate and all related registration
  costs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Evidence

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** In a proceeding in
respect of a violation, a notice of violation purporting to be issued
under subsection 11(1), a notice of decision purporting to be issued
under subsection 12(4) or a certificate purporting to be made under
subsection 18(1) is admissible in evidence without proof of the
signature or official character of the person appearing to have signed
it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limitation Period

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** No proceedings in respect
of a violation may be commenced after the expiry of two years after the
day on which the subject matter of the proceedings arose.
