## Affiliated Persons (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#affiliated-persons-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-327
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Specifying the Circumstances under which a Natural Person is
Affiliated with a Company

P.C. 1992-1102  1992-05-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 166 of the [Trust and Loan
Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations specifying the circumstances under which a
natural person is affiliated with a
company](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-326), effective June 1, 1992.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944448 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Affiliated Persons (Trust and Loan Companies)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-327).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944450 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[indebtedness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes indebtedness in
    respect of commercial paper, acceptances, lines of credit to the
    extent drawn on and margin loans made to a director or officer of a
    company; ([dette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[not in good standing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[not in good standing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a loan,
    means a loan in respect of which

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any payment of principal or interest is 90 days
      or more overdue,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} interest is not being accrued on the books of the
      lender because it is doubtful whether the principal or interest
      will be paid or recovered, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the rate of interest is reduced by the lender
      because the borrower is financially weak; ([en
      souffrance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[significant borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[significant borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    company, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who has indebtedness for money
      borrowed from the company or from an affiliate of the company,
      other than a loan secured by a mortgage on the principal residence
      of that person, the total principal of which exceeds the greater
      of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$200,000, and

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one fiftieth of one per cent of the regulatory
        capital of the company, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that has indebtedness for money
      borrowed from the company or from an affiliate of the company the
      total principal of which exceeds the greatest of

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$500,000,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one twentieth of one per cent of the
        regulatory capital of the company, and

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} twenty-five per cent of the value of the
        assets of the entity. ([emprunteur important]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

## [Circumstances Under Which a Natural Person Is Affiliated]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944466 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
166 of the Act, a natural person is affiliated with a company where the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is an officer or employee of the company or of an
  affiliate of the company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has a significant interest in a class of shares of
  the company;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} has a substantial investment in an affiliate of the
  company;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is a significant borrower in respect of the company;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} is an officer or employee of an entity that is a
  significant borrower in respect of the company;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} controls one or more entities of which the total
  indebtedness to the company or to an affiliate of the company would
  cause those entities, if treated as a single entity, to be a
  significant borrower of the company;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} provides goods or services to the company, or is a
  partner or an employee in a partnership that provides goods or
  services to the company or an officer or employee of, or a person who
  has a substantial investment in, a body corporate that provides goods
  or services to the company, if the total annual billings to the
  company in respect of the goods and services provided exceeds ten per
  cent of the total annual billings of the person, partnership or body
  corporate, as the case may be;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} has a loan that is not in good standing from the
  company or from an affiliate of the company or is a director, an
  officer or an employee of, or a person who controls, an entity that
  has a loan that is not in good standing from the company or from an
  affiliate of the company; or

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} is the spouse or common-law partner of a person
  described in any of paragraphs (a) to (h).
