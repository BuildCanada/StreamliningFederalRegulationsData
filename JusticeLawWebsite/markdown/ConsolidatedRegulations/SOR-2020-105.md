## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Emergency Student
    Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-3.75). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a student, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a child (including an adopted child, a stepchild
      or a foster child) who is under 12 years of age and is wholly
      dependent on the student or the student's spouse or common-law
      partner for their care and upbringing; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person with a disability who is wholly
      dependent on the student or the student's spouse or common-law
      partner for their care and upbringing. ([personne à
      charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disability]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any impairment, including a
    physical, mental, intellectual, cognitive, learning, communication
    or sensory impairment --- or a functional limitation --- whether
    permanent or episodic in nature, or evident or not, that, in
    interaction with a barrier, as defined in section 2 of the
    [Accessible Canada Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6), hinders a person's full
    and equal participation in society. ([handicap]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Post-secondary educational program

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of the
definition [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act, a
post-secondary educational program includes a series of courses taken
over a period of at least 12 weeks at the post-secondary level at

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an educational institution set out in the Master List
  of Designated Educational Institutions, published by the Government of
  Canada on its website;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an educational institution set out in the Master List
  of Certified Educational Institutions, published by the Government of
  Canada on its website;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an educational institution set out in the Directory
  of Educational Institutions and Programs, published by the Government
  of Quebec on its website; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an Indigenous institution recognized by a province.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Student who is to graduate

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A person who is to graduate
from secondary school in 2020, has applied for enrollment in a
post-secondary educational program that leads to a degree, diploma or
certificate that is scheduled to begin before February 1, 2021 and plans
to enroll in the program if their application is accepted is prescribed
for the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition
[[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act.

## [Canada Emergency Student Benefit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247032 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed period

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 5(1) of the Act, the prescribed period is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a student described in paragraph (a) of the
  definition [[student]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in section 2 of the Act,
  the period beginning on May 10, 2020 and ending August 29, 2020; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for a student described in paragraph (b) or (c) of
  that definition

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who graduates before May 10, 2020, the period
    beginning on May 10, 2020 and ending on August 29, 2020,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who graduates on or after May 10, 2020 but before
    June 7, 2020, the period beginning on June 7, 2020 and ending on
    August 29, 2020, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who has not graduated before June 7, 2020, the
    period beginning on July 5, 2020 and ending on August 29, 2020.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum allowable income

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  amount of income of \$1000.01 is determined for the purposes of
  paragraph 6(1)(a) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded income

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any income received by a student from employment or
  self-employment is excluded from the application of subparagraph
  6(1)(b)(i) of the Act if the total of such income received in respect
  of the four-week period for which they apply for the Canada emergency
  student benefit is \$1000 or less.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of benefit

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
subsection 7(1) of the Act, the amount of a Canada emergency student
benefit for any week is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} \$500, in the case of a student with a dependant or a
  student with a disability; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} \$312.50, in any other case.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum number of weeks

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 8(1) of the Act, the maximum number of weeks for which a
student may receive a Canada emergency student benefit is 16 minus the
number of weeks, if any, for which the student receives an income
support payment under the [Canada Emergency Response Benefit
Act](/eng/acts/C-3.7) or an employment insurance emergency response
benefit referred to in section 153.7 of the [Employment Insurance
Act](/eng/acts/E-5.6).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1247051 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}May 10, 2020

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on May 10, 2020.
