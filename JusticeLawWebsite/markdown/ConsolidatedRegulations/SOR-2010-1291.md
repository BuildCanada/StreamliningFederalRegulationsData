## Input Tax Credit Allocation Methods (GST/HST) Regulations {#input-tax-credit-allocation-methods-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

2010, c. 12, s. 91
:::

EXCISE TAX ACT

Registration 2010-07-12

Input Tax Credit Allocation Methods (GST/HST) Regulations

::: ReaderNote
\[Enacted by section 91 of chapter 12 of the Statutes of Canada, 2010,
deemed to have come into force on April 1, 2007.\]
:::
:::::

::: section
[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a fiscal year does not
    include a person that is at any time in the fiscal year an
    insurer. ([banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a fiscal year means a
    person that is an insurer (as defined in subsection 123(1) of the
    Act) and that carries on at any time in the fiscal year an insurance
    business as the principal business of the person in
    Canada. ([assureur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in respect of a fiscal
    year means a person that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} carries on at any time in the fiscal year a
      business as a trader or dealer in, or as a broker or salesperson
      of, securities as the principal business of the person in Canada;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is registered under the laws of Canada or a
      province to carry on in Canada at any time in the fiscal year a
      business as a trader or dealer in, or as a broker or salesperson
      of, securities; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not a bank or an insurer at any time in the
      fiscal year. ([courtier en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed classes

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following classes of
financial institutions are prescribed for the purposes of the definition
[[qualifying institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 141.02(1)
of the Act and for the purposes of subsections 141.02(3), (8), (9), (24)
and (30) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} banks;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} insurers; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} securities dealers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed amounts

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following amounts are
prescribed for the purposes of the definition [[qualifying
institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 141.02(1) of the Act
and for the purposes of subsection 141.02(24) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of banks, \$500,000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of insurers, \$500,000; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of securities dealers, \$500,000.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentages

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following percentages
are prescribed for the purposes of the definition [[qualifying
institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 141.02(1) of the Act
and for the purposes of subsections 141.02(8), (9) and (30) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of banks, 12%;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of insurers, 10%; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of securities dealers, 15%.
