## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799924 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the *Ministerio de
    Comercio Exterior y Turismo*, the *Superintendencia Nacional de
    Administración Tributaria* or, if Peru provides written notice to
    Canada of their replacement, their successors. ([autorité
    compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the authority
    responsible under the laws of Canada for the administration of
    customs laws and regulations. ([administration
    douanière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under the CPFTA is claimed.
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a good that is used in the
    production of another good, and includes a part or ingredient.
    ([matière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    contains a request for information regarding the origin of goods
    that are the subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that contains requests for information regarding the
    origin of goods that are the subject of a verification of origin.
    ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into
    premises or a place for the purpose of conducting a verification of
    origin of goods under paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799934 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods may be conducted
by reviewing the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification questionnaire completed by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the exporter or producer of the goods, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a producer or supplier of a material;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written response received from a person referred to
  in paragraph (a) to a verification letter; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information received from a person referred
  to in paragraph (a).

## [Verification Letters and Verification Questionnaires]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799941 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter or
verification questionnaire must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} identify the customs administration and the name and
  title of the officer sending the letter or questionnaire;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe the goods or materials that are the subject
  of the verification of origin; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} specify the period within which the letter must be
  answered or the questionnaire must be completed and returned, with the
  period being a minimum of 30 days beginning on the day on which the
  letter or the questionnaire is received.

## [Extension of Time]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799946 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person who receives a
verification letter or verification questionnaire may, on a single
occasion and within the period specified in the letter or questionnaire,
make a written request to the customs administration, asking for an
extension of that period. The extension shall be granted for a further
period of not more than 30 days.

## [Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799948 .Part}

### [Premises]{.HTitleText2} {#h-799949 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following premises in
Peru may be entered for the purposes of a verification visit:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the premises of an exporter or producer of goods; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the premises of a producer or supplier of a material.

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-799953 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for an officer to conduct a verification visit until the
  following conditions are satisfied:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an officer sends a written notice of the intention
    to conduct the visit to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are to be the subject
      of the visit,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the competent authority, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if requested by Peru, the Embassy of Peru
      located in Canada; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person whose premises are to be the subject of
    the visit consents in writing to the visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice of the verification visit shall
  specify

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the identity of the customs administration on
    behalf of which the notice is sent;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and title of the officer who will conduct
    the visit;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person whose premises are the
    subject of the visit;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date and the place of the visit;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the object and scope of the visit, including a
    description of the goods or materials that are the subject of the
    verification of origin; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the legal authority for the visit.

### [Postponement]{.HTitleText2} {#h-799968 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  verification visit is postponed if, within 15 days after the day on
  which it receives a notice under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(ii), Peru sends
  a written request to the officer who sent the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The postponement of the visit shall be for a period
  not exceeding 60 days, beginning on the day on which the notice is
  received, or for any longer period agreed to by Peru and the customs
  administration.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who receives a notice under subparagraph
  6(1)(a)(i) may, on a single occasion and within 15 days after the day
  on which the notice is received, request the postponement of the
  verification visit by sending a written request for postponement to
  the officer who sent the notice.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A postponement of the visit shall be for a period not
  exceeding 60 days, beginning on the day on which the notice is
  received, or for any longer period agreed to by the customs
  administration.

### [Observers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-799974 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who receives a notice under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) may designate two
  observers to be present during the verification visit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Participation of the observers during the visit is
  limited to observation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who designates observers must identify them
  to the officer conducting the verification visit.

## [Denial or Withdrawal of Preferential Tariff Treatment Under Cpfta]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799979 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act, the exporter and the
  producer of goods are, in relation to a verification of origin under
  paragraph 42.1(1)(a) of the Act, required to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain, in accordance with the applicable laws of
    the country in which the verification of origin is conducted,
    records relevant to the determination of origin with respect to the
    goods that are required to be maintained under those laws;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the officer conducting the verification of
    origin with access to those records; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} reply to each verification letter or complete and
    return each verification questionnaire to the officer within the
    period specified under paragraph 3(c) or section 4, as the case may
    be.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 42.1(2) of the Act,
  consent to the verification visit shall be provided in writing by the
  exporter or producer within 30 days after the day on which the notice
  sent under subparagraph 6(1)(a)(i) is received.

<!-- -->

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  officer must send a written notice of the intent to re-determine the
  origin of goods under subsection 59(1) of the Act with any statement
  that is provided to an exporter or producer of goods pursuant to
  subsection 42.2(1) of the Act and that states that the goods that are
  the subject of a verification of origin are not eligible for
  preferential tariff treatment under the CPFTA.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The written notice must specify the date after which
  preferential tariff treatment under the CPFTA may be denied or
  withdrawn in respect of the goods that are the subject of the
  verification of origin and the period during which the exporter or
  producer of the goods may provide written comments or additional
  information regarding the origin of those goods.

## [Means of Communication]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799989 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A verification letter, a
verification questionnaire and the notices referred to in paragraph
6(1)(a) and section 10 must be sent by any means that produces
confirmation of receipt.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799991 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations are
deemed to have come into force on August 1, 2009.
