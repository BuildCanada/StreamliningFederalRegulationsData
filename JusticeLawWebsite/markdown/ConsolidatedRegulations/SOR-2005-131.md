## Plum Pox Virus Compensation Regulations, 2004 {#plum-pox-virus-compensation-regulations-2004 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2005-131
:::

[PLANT PROTECTION ACT](/eng/acts/P-14.8)

Registration 2005-05-10

Plum Pox Virus Compensation Regulations, 2004

P.C. 2005-803 2005-05-10
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to paragraph 47(q)
of the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Plum Pox Virus Compensation Regulations,
2004](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-131).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715071 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[certified tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tree that meets

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements set out in a document referred
      to as D-83-44 that was prepared by the Food Production and
      Inspection Branch of Agriculture Canada and dated December 21,
      1983, which document is available in both official languages from
      the Canadian Food Inspection Agency on request; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equivalent requirements. ([arbre
      certifié]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tested tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tested tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a tree propagated from
    budwood and clonal rootstock that originated from plants tested
    annually by the Canadian Food Inspection Agency and found negative
    for the presence of plum pox virus. ([arbre testé]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tree]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an apricot, nectarine, peach or
    plum tree that is planted in an orchard for commercial production of
    tender fruit. ([arbre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715078 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (4), the Minister may order that compensation be paid
  under subsection 39(1) of the [Plant Protection Act](/eng/acts/P-14.8)
  to a person who has received notice, issued by an inspector under that
  Act or the [Plant Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-212)
  between January 1, 2004 and March 31, 2011, to dispose of one or more
  trees as a result of the presence of the plum pox virus, if the person

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has incurred a loss as a result of costs related to
    the

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} removal and disposition of the tree or trees,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} preparation of soil for the planting of a tree
      of a type set out in column 1 of the schedule to replace a tree
      that has been disposed of, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} replacement of trees that have been disposed of
      if the replacement trees have been or will be planted in the same
      location or in another location that the person owns or has the
      possession, care or control of;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has reported farm income for tender fruit sales in
    the year immediately preceding the year in which the notice was
    issued, the year in which the notice was issued or the year in which
    the loss occurred; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} submits an application for compensation to the
    Minister within two years after the date of issuance of the notice.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person may amend an application for compensation
  at any time before the end of the period referred to in paragraph
  (1)(c).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may submit an application after the end of
  the period referred to in paragraph (1)(c) if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there were exceptional circumstances beyond the
    person's control that prevented the person from submitting an
    application during that period; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application is submitted within 14 days after
    the day on which those circumstances cease to exist.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No compensation shall be paid for a loss referred to
  in paragraph (1)(a) for which compensation has already been paid under
  these Regulations or has been paid or is payable under

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any other program or measure, under any federal or
    provincial law, for the purpose of compensating for losses resulting
    from the presence of the plum pox virus;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other compensation or insurance program; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an insurance contract.

## [Amount of Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715097 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount of compensation
payable for each tree shall not exceed

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} for the removal and disposition of a tree of a type
  set out in column 1 of the schedule, the applicable amount set out in
  column 2;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for the preparation of soil for planting a tree of a
  type set out in column 1 of the schedule to replace a tree that has
  been disposed of, the applicable amount set out in column 3 of the
  schedule in respect of the replacement tree, if the preparation has
  been done by the applicant or the applicant has undertaken to do it in
  the undertaking required by paragraph 4(h); and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for the replacement of a tree that has been disposed
  of by a tree of a type set out in column 1 of the schedule, the amount
  from column 4 of the schedule that corresponds to that type of
  replacement tree, if the applicant has planted the replacement tree or
  has undertaken to plant it in the undertaking required by paragraph
  4(h).

## [Application for Compensation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715102 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An application for
compensation must be on a form provided by the Minister and signed by
the applicant and must include the following information and documents,
together with any other information and documents that are necessary for
the Minister to determine whether the application meets the requirements
of these Regulations:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name, address and telephone number
  and facsimile number and e-mail address, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the legal description of the land on which the tree
  is or was located;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} whether the applicant is a sole proprietorship,
  corporation, partnership, cooperative, association or organization and
  the names, addresses and telephone numbers of the owners or directors,
  as applicable;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the Statement of Farming Activities (Form
  T2042) or Statement A --- CAIS Program Information and Statement of
  Farming Activities for Individuals (Form T1163), whichever form was
  submitted with the applicant's income tax return for one of the
  following years for which the applicant reported farm income for
  tender fruit sales, namely, the year immediately preceding the year in
  which the notice referred to in subsection 2(1) was issued, the year
  in which the notice was issued or the year in which the loss occurred;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} evidence to substantiate the number of trees, the
  type or types of trees and the number of trees per tree type that were
  required to be disposed of;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} with respect to the disposition of trees

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} copies of all documents received by the applicant
    that relate to the disposition of the trees,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} evidence to substantiate the numbers of trees of
    each type set out in column 1 of the schedule that have been planted
    to replace the trees that were required to be disposed of and the
    location where the replacement trees were planted, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} evidence to substantiate that the replacement
    trees were tested trees or certified trees;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant has ordered a replacement tree that
  has not yet been delivered to the applicant, evidence to substantiate
  the number of trees ordered and the location from which the trees were
  ordered; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} with respect to compensation for the preparation of
  soil for the planting of a tree of a type set out in column 1 of the
  schedule that will be planted to replace a tree that was disposed of,
  an undertaking by the applicant

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant will prepare the soil and plant
    the replacement tree within three years after the date of the notice
    referred to in subsection 2(1) that required the disposition of the
    tree that was disposed of,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the applicant will notify the Minister
    without delay after the planting of the replacement tree that the
    soil has been prepared and the tree has been planted and of the
    location where the tree was planted, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that specifies the number of replacement trees of
    a type set out in column 1 of the schedule that will be planted, the
    location at which the trees will be planted and the type of each
    tree that will be planted.

## [Other Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715118 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The payment to an applicant
of compensation ordered in accordance with these Regulations is subject
to the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant must comply with any undertaking
  referred to in paragraph 4(h) that the applicant has given;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant must keep books and records,
  accompanied by supporting documents, that are necessary to
  substantiate the information contained in the application for at least
  three years after the date on which the replacement trees of a type
  set out in column 1 of the schedule are planted; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant must make those books, records and
  supporting documents available on request, within the period during
  which they must be kept, for inspection or audit.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715123 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** An applicant may fulfil an
undertaking to prepare soil and plant replacement trees during the
period referred to in subparagraph 4(h)(i) by preparing the soil and
planting the replacement trees after the end of that period if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} there were exceptional circumstances beyond the
  applicant's control that prevented the applicant from purchasing
  tested trees or certified trees during that period; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trees were purchased and planted as soon as they
  became available.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-715127 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
