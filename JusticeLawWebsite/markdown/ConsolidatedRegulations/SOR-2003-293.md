## Assessor's Rules of Procedure {#assessors-rules-of-procedure .Title-of-Act}

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704086 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Rules.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pesticide Residue
      Compensation Act](/eng/acts/P-10). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[appellant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who brings an
      appeal under section 15 of the Act. ([appelant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Assessor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Assessor, acting
      assessor or Deputy Assessor appointed under section 14 of the
      Act. ([évaluateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[business day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day other than a
      Saturday or a Sunday or other holiday. ([jour
      ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2007-104,
      s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

  [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a registrar of appeals
      appointed under section 19 of the
      Act. ([greffier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Rules are to be liberally construed in order to
  permit the fairest, least expensive and most expeditious proceedings.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704100 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Procedural matters not provided for

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If any question of
procedure arises during a proceeding that is not covered, or not fully
covered, in these Rules, the Assessor must decide the question in a
manner that is consistent with these Rules and with the principles of
procedural fairness and natural justice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Unfairness

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** If the application of any
Rule would cause unfairness to a party, the Assessor may waive
compliance with the Rule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Technical defects

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A defect in form or a
technical irregularity may be overlooked by the Assessor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-104, s. 2(F)
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Taking notice

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Assessor may take
judicial notice of any matter and receive and accept any evidence and
other information that the Assessor determines will assist in the
resolution of the issues.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-104, s. 2(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Calculating time limits

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), in calculating time limits under these Rules, all
  days must be counted, except that if a time limit ends on a Saturday
  or a Sunday or other holiday, the time limit must be extended until
  the next business day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Five-day time limits

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a time limit is five days, a Saturday or a Sunday
  or other holiday must not be included in the calculation of the time
  limit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of time limits

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** If adhering to a time limit
fixed by these Rules would cause unfairness to a party, the Assessor may
extend the time limit either before or after the end of the time limit.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Production of documents

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  party to an appeal may give a notice in writing to any other party to
  the appeal

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to provide to the party giving notice, or make
    available for inspection by the party giving notice, any document in
    the possession of the other party, or to which the other party has
    access, that is intended to be used in evidence by the other party
    at the hearing; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to permit the party giving notice to make
    photocopies of any documents referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limits for notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must be given at least 21 days before the
  date fixed for the hearing and the documents must be provided or made
  available for inspection within 10 days after the receipt of the
  notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy for the Registrar

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The party giving the notice must at the same time
  send a copy of the notice to the Registrar.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Varying time limits

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The periods of time referred to in subsection (2) may
  be varied by agreement between the parties or by order of the
  Assessor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order to provide or make available

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the party to whom the notice is given fails to
  comply with the notice, the party giving the notice may apply to the
  Assessor for an order directing the party to whom notice was given to
  provide the requested documents or make them available for inspection.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-104, s. 3
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public nature of filed documents

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document filed with the Assessor by a party to an appeal is a public
  document unless the party requests that the document be treated as
  confidential.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for confidential treatment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request that a document be treated as confidential
  must provide reasons for the request, including details of the nature
  and extent of the harm that disclosure of the document would cause to
  the party making the request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision to grant request

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor must grant a party's request for
  confidential treatment if the Assessor considers that disclosure of
  the document would cause harm to the party.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be in duplicate

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  documents sent to the Registrar or the Assessor must be sent in
  duplicate.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Submitting documents to the Registrar
  or Assessor

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Documents may be submitted to the Registrar or the
  Assessor by hand or by registered mail, courier or facsimile.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Facsimiles sent to the Registrar or
  Assessor

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The original of any document sent by facsimile must
  be sent by mail without delay after the facsimile transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be sent to parties

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must ensure
that copies of all documents submitted to the Registrar by a party are
sent to the other parties.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Correspondence sent by the Registrar or
Assessor

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** All correspondence sent
by the Registrar or Assessor may be sent by facsimile as long as the
original is sent by mail without delay.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-104, s. 4
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-12-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 12"){.PITLink}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Change of address or facsimile number

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A party to an appeal must
notify the Registrar without delay of a change of address or facsimile
number.

## [Appeals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704157 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Request for appeal hearing

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who wishes to appeal to the Assessor must provide the Registrar
  with a notice of appeal. The notice must state the reasons for the
  appeal and must indicate the language in which the appellant wishes
  the appeal to be heard.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to be signed

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of appeal must be signed by the appellant
  or the appellant's counsel or representative. If a representative
  signs the notice, a copy of the appellant's written authorization of
  the representative must be filed with the notice.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to be sent to Minister

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** The Registrar must send a
copy of the notice of appeal to the Minister within five days after
receiving it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister to send information

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Within 30 days after the
day on which the Minister receives a copy of the notice of appeal, the
Minister must send to the Registrar the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the decision or award of compensation being appealed;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the evidence on which the Minister relied to reach
  the decision or award of compensation being appealed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Minister's reply to the notice of appeal,
  admitting or denying any facts alleged in the notice of appeal and
  containing a statement of any facts on which he or she intends to
  rely.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Minister's documents to be sent to other
parties

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of the documents required by section 16, the Registrar must,
  without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} forward a copy of each of them to the appellant;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} give notice in writing to the parties other than
    the Minister that they have 30 days after the date of the notice to
    submit any additional information or representations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information or representations received
  from other parties to be sent to Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of any additional information or
  representations from the other parties, the Registrar must forward a
  copy of the information or representations to the Minister without
  delay.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2007-104, s. 5
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Consolidation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** If there is a common
question of fact in two or more appeals for which Notices of Appeal have
been filed in accordance with these Rules, the Assessor may, on his or
her own motion or on application by a party, order

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the appeals be consolidated on such terms as the
  Assessor may direct;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the appeals be heard at the same time;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} that the appeals be heard consecutively; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that any of the appeals be stayed until the
  determination of any of the other appeals.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Withdrawal of appeal

- **[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appellant may withdraw a notice of appeal at any time by notifying the
  Registrar in writing to that effect.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of the notice of withdrawal, the Registrar
  must without delay notify all other parties to the appeal.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time and place for hearing

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Assessor must fix the time and place for the hearing of the appeal,
  which must not be earlier than 30 days, and not later than 60 days,
  after the period referred to in subsection 17(1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notification of parties

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Registrar must notify the parties to the appeal
  of the time and place for the hearing of the appeal at least 25 days
  before the date of the hearing.

## [Conduct of an Appeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704195 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Representation of parties

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A party to an appeal may
be represented by counsel or by another representative authorized in
writing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[In camera]{.otherLang lang="la"}
hearings

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  hearing of an appeal before the Assessor may, on the request of any
  party, be held [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} if the Assessor is
  satisfied that the party has established that the circumstances so
  require.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion of witnesses

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor may order a witness at a hearing to be
  excluded from the hearing until called to give evidence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recording of hearings

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The hearing of an appeal
must be recorded.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Order of proceeding

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** Unless the order of
proceeding has been agreed to by all parties to an appeal in advance,
the Assessor must establish the order of proceeding at the start of the
hearing.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subpoena

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On
  receipt of a written request by a party, the Registrar may, on behalf
  of the Assessor, issue a subpoena for the attendance of a witness or
  the production of a document or other material at a hearing.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Form of subpoena

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A subpoena must be in the form set out in the
  schedule. It may be issued in blank and completed by a party or a
  party's counsel or representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination of witnesses

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Witnesses at a hearing may be examined orally on oath
  or solemn affirmation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Examination, cross-examination and
  re-examination

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} Either party at a hearing is entitled to examine
  their own witnesses, to cross-examine any witnesses of the other
  party, and to re-examine their own witnesses.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affidavit evidence

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Affidavit evidence is not
admissible without the consent of the party against whom the affidavit
evidence is tendered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Failure to appear

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a party to an appeal
does not appear at a hearing and the Assessor is satisfied that notice
of the hearing was sent to the party in accordance with section 20, the
Assessor may proceed in the party's absence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Postponements and adjournments

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** A hearing may be
postponed or adjourned by the Assessor from time to time on any terms
that the Assessor considers appropriate.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision

- **[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Assessor may render a decision orally at the end of the hearing or may
  render the decision at a later date.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Decision in writing

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Assessor's decision and the reasons for the
  decision must be in writing. The Registrar must send a copy of the
  decision and reasons to all parties without delay.
