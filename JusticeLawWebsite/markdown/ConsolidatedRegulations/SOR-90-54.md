## Unemployment Insurance Benefits Order, 1990 {#unemployment-insurance-benefits-order-1990 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/90-54
:::

[EMPLOYMENT INSURANCE ACT](/eng/acts/E-5.6)

Registration 1989-12-27

Order Authorizing the Maximum Amounts That May Be Paid in the Year 1990
as Benefits Under Sections 24, 25 and 26 of the Unemployment Insurance
Act

P.C. 1989-2577  1989-12-21
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Employment and Immigration and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to subsections 24(4), 25(10) and 26(8) of the Unemployment
Insurance Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order authorizing
the maximum amounts that may be paid in the year 1990 as benefits under
sections 24, 25 and 26 of the Unemployment Insurance Act, effective
January 1, 1990.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926507 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Unemployment Insurance Benefits Order,
1990](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-54).

## [Maximum Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-926509 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The maximum amounts that
may be paid in the year 1990 as benefits under sections 24, 25 and 26 of
the Unemployment Insurance Act, are \$130,000,000, \$120,000,000 and
\$376,000,000 respectively.
