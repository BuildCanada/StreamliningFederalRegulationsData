## Charitable Food Donations Anti-dumping and Countervailing Duty Remission Order {#charitable-food-donations-anti-dumping-and-countervailing-duty-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-536
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1998-10-22

Charitable Food Donations Anti-dumping and Countervailing Duty Remission
Order

P.C. 1998-1889  1998-10-22
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 115 of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
Charitable Food Donations Anti-dumping and Countervailing Duty Remission
Order.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025094 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[food]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means food for human consumption but does
not include alcoholic beverages or spirits.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025096 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted of all anti-dumping and countervailing
duties paid or payable under the [Special Import Measures
Act](/eng/acts/S-15) on food donated by a non-resident of Canada to a
"registered charity", within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the
[Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025098 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the food is imported into Canada on or after the day
  on which this Order comes into force;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the registered charity is the importer of the food;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the food is for charitable distribution in Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the food is not for sale in Canada; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date on which the food is
  accounted for under section 32 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1025105 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
