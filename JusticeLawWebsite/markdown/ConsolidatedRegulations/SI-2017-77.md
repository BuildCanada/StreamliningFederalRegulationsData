## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-77
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2017-12-13

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2017-1465 2017-11-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBEAA},
effective November 30, 2017,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EEEAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7867}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, s. 207
  :::

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EBEAA){#fn_612645E_hq_7868}R.S., c.
  P-34
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Department of Health to the
  Department of Indigenous Services Canada the control and supervision
  of that portion of the federal public administration in the Department
  of Health known as the First Nations and Inuit Health Branch; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} transfers from the Department of Indian Affairs and
  Northern Development to the Department of Indigenous Services Canada
  the control and supervision of those portions of the federal public
  administration in the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
  Development known as

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Education and Social Development Programs and
    Partnership Sector, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Regional Operations Sector.
