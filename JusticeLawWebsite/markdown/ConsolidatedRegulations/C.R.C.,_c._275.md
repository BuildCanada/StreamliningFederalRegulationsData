## Saskatchewan Hog Farm Registration (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#saskatchewan-hog-farm-registration-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 275
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Registration of the Farm of Certain Persons
Engaged in the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade of Hogs
Produced in Saskatchewan
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537730 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Saskatchewan Hog Farm Registration (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._275).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537732 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Natural Products Marketing
    Act, 1972 of Saskatchewan; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog
    Marketing Commission established pursuant to the Act and the Plan;
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in
    Saskatchewan; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Hog Marketing
    Plan established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act;
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person engaged in producing or feeding hogs
      and making them available for slaughter and includes the employer
      of any such person,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any person who, under any lease or agreement, is
      entitled to a share of the hogs produced or fed by a person
      referred to in paragraph (a), and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person who takes possession of any hogs from
      any person under any form of security for a debt.
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537742 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export trade and to
persons and property situated in the Province of Saskatchewan.

## [Farm Unit Registration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537744 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer shall market hogs unless he has been allocated a farm unit
  registration number by the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A producer may apply to the Commission for a farm
  unit registration number at the time of marketing his first shipment
  of hogs through the Commission.
