## Cannabis Fees Order {#cannabis-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

:::

[CANNABIS ACT](/eng/acts/C-24.5)

Registration 2018-10-01

Cannabis Fees Order
::::

:::::::::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 143 of the [Cannabis
Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBADA}, the Minister of Health has
consulted with any persons that the Minister considers to be interested
in the matter;

The Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection 142(1) of the [Cannabis
Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Cannabis
Fees Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-198).

Ottawa, September 28, 2018

Ginette C. Petitpas Taylor

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cannabis
    Act](/eng/acts/C-24.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cannabis drug licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cannabis drug licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
    referred to in paragraph 8(1)(f) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence relative aux
    drogues contenant du cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[cannabis revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cannabis revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the amount by which
    the amount received from the sale of cannabis exceeds the amount
    paid for the purchase of cannabis from the holder of a licence for
    micro-cultivation, a licence for standard cultivation, a licence for
    a nursery, a licence for micro-processing, a licence for standard
    processing or a licence for sale for medical purposes. ([recettes
    tirées de la vente de cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[client]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    1(2) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([client]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(2) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([permis
    d'exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fiscal year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning on
    April 1 in one year and ending on March 31 in the next
    year. ([exercice]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 1(2) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([permis
    d'importation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for analytical testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for analytical testing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    licence referred to in paragraph 8(1)(c) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence d'essais
    analytiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for a nursery]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for a nursery]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
    referred to in paragraph 8(3)(c) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de culture en
    pépinière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for micro-cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for micro-cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    licence referred to in paragraph 8(3)(a) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de
    micro-culture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for micro-processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for micro-processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
    referred to in paragraph 8(4)(a) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de
    micro-transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for research]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a licence
    referred to in paragraph 8(1)(e) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de
    recherche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for sale for medical purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for sale for medical purposes]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a licence referred to in subsection 8(5) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de vente à
    des fins médicales]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for standard cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for standard cultivation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    licence referred to in paragraph 8(3)(b) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de culture
    standard]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[licence for standard processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[licence for standard processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    licence referred to in paragraph 8(4)(b) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([licence de
    transformation standard]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[named responsible adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[named responsible adult]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in subsection 264(1) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([responsable
    nommé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    1(2) of the [Cannabis
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-144). ([lieu]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual adjustment of fees

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The fees set out in
sections 3 to 5 are to be adjusted in each fiscal year on April 1 by the
percentage change over 12 months in the April All-items Consumer Price
Index for Canada, as published by Statistics Canada under the
[Statistics Act](/eng/acts/S-19), for the previous fiscal year and
rounded to the next highest dollar.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening of an application for one
licence

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The fee
  for the screening by the Minister of an application for the issuance
  of only one licence set out in column 1 of Schedule 1 is set out in
  column 2 and is payable within 30 days of submitting the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Screening of an application for more
  than one licence

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The fee for the screening by the Minister of an
  application for the issuance of more than one licence set out in
  column 1 of Schedule 1 is the following and is payable within 30 days
  of submitting the application:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case where one of the licences referred to
    in the application is a licence for sale for medical purposes, the
    lowest applicable fee set out in column 2 of Schedule 1 with respect
    to the licences referred to in the application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, the highest applicable fee set
    out in column 2 of Schedule 1 with respect to the licences referred
    to in the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for security clearance

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The fee for the
consideration by the Minister of an application with respect to a
security clearance referred to in section 67 of the Act is \$1,654 and
is payable within 90 days of submitting the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for import or export permit

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The fee for the
consideration by the Minister of an application for an import permit or
an export permit is \$610 and is payable within 30 days of submitting
the application.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions --- analytical testing,
research, etc.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Sections 4 and 5 do not
apply with respect to an application for a security clearance, import
permit or export permit with respect to a
[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the meaning of the [Industrial
Hemp Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-145), a licence for
analytical testing, a licence for research or a cannabis drug licence.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual fee --- entry year

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of one or more licences set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 with
  respect to the same site must, not later than 60 days after the
  earliest effective date of those licences, pay the following fee:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if they hold only one of the following licences,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$2,500, for a licence for micro-cultivation, a
      licence for a nursery or a licence for micro-processing, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$23,000, for a licence for standard
      cultivation, a licence for standard processing or a licence for
      sale for medical purposes; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds more than one licence,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where one of the licences is a
      licence for sale for medical purposes, the lowest applicable fee
      referred to in subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii) with respect to the
      licences that the person holds, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, the highest applicable fee
      referred to in subparagraph (a)(i) or (ii) with respect to the
      licences that the person holds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual fee --- first fiscal year

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder must, with respect to the fiscal year
  following the fiscal year during which the licence with the earliest
  effective date was issued for the same site, pay the following fee,
  not later than September 30 of that year,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds only one licence set out in
    column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site, the fee determined
    in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 with respect to the
    licence, based on the pro-rated cannabis revenue for the fiscal year
    during which the licence with the earliest effective date was
    issued, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds more than one licence set out
    in column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where one of the licences is a
      licence for sale for medical purposes, the lowest applicable fee
      determined in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 with respect
      to the licences that the person holds, based on the pro-rated
      cannabis revenue for the fiscal year during which the licence with
      the earliest effective date was issued, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, the highest applicable fee
      determined in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 with respect
      to the licences that the person holds, based on the pro-rated
      cannabis revenue for the fiscal year during which the licence with
      the earliest effective date was issued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of revenue and annual fee ---
  subsequent years

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The holder must, for the same site and with respect
  to each fiscal year subsequent to the fiscal year to which
  subsection (2) applies, pay the following fee, not later than
  September 30 of the subsequent year:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds only one licence set out in
    column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site, the fee determined
    in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 for the licence, based on
    the cannabis revenue set out in the statement of cannabis revenue
    referred to in subsection 8(1) for the fiscal year, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds more than one licence set out
    in column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where one of the licences is a
      licence for sale for medical purposes, the lowest applicable fee
      determined in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2, based on the
      cannabis revenue set out in the statement of cannabis revenue,
      with respect to the licences that the person holds, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, the highest applicable fee
      determined in accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2, based on the
      cannabis revenue set out in the statement of cannabis revenue,
      with respect to the licences that the person holds.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension for 2020--2021 fiscal year

  [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsections (2) and (3), if a holder is
  required to pay a fee under one of those subsections not later than
  September 30, 2020, the holder may pay the fee not later than March
  31, 2021.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[pro-rated cannabis revenue]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means the cannabis revenue set out in the statement of cannabis
    revenue referred to in subsection 8(1) for a fiscal year, multiplied
    by the number of days in the fiscal year divided by the number of
    days during which the licence was effective during the fiscal year.

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the effective date of a licence that has been
    continued in force under subsection 158(1) or 159 (1) of the Act is
    deemed to be October 17, 2018;

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Statement of cannabis revenue

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of one or more licences set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 with
  respect to the same site must, no later than April 30 of a fiscal
  year, submit to the Minister a statement of the cannabis revenue for
  the preceding fiscal year with respect to those licences, including
  the amount received from the sale of cannabis and the amount paid for
  the purchase of cannabis that were used to determine the cannabis
  revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reconciliation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder must pay the following fee, not later than
  September 30 following the fiscal year during which the licence with
  the earliest effective date was issued with respect to the same site,
  if the amount is positive:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds only one licence set out in
    column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site, an amount equal to
    the difference between the fee determined in accordance with column
    2 of Schedule 2 with respect to the licence, based on the cannabis
    revenue set out in the statement of cannabis revenue for the fiscal
    year during which the licence was issued, for the applicable licence
    set out in column 1 and the fee paid under paragraph 7(1)(a); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person holds more than one licence set out
    in column 1 of Schedule 2 with respect to the site,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case where one of the licences is a
      licence for sale for medical purposes, an amount equal to the
      difference between the lowest applicable fee determined in
      accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 with respect to the
      licences that the person holds, based on the cannabis revenue set
      out in the statement of cannabis revenue for the fiscal year
      during which the licence with the earliest effective date was
      issued and the fee paid under subparagraph 7(1)(b)(i).

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases, an amount equal to the
      difference between the highest applicable fee determined in
      accordance with column 2 of Schedule 2 with respect to the
      licences that the person holds, based on the cannabis revenue set
      out in the statement and the fee paid under subparagraph
      7(1)(b)(ii).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accounting standard for statements

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Statements that are
submitted to the Minister for the purposes of this Order must be
prepared in writing in accordance with the generally accepted accounting
principles established by the Accounting Standards Board, the primary
source of which is, in Canada, the CPA Canada Handbook --- Accounting,
and must be accompanied by a written document signed by the person
responsible for the financial affairs of the holder of the licence
certifying that the records were prepared in accordance with the
standards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemptions --- sale for medical purposes

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a licence for sale for medical purposes and any other
  licence set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 , if any, is not required to
  pay the applicable fee referred to in subsection 7(1) and paragraphs
  7(2)(a) and (b) and (3)(a) and (b) and subsection 8(2) for a fiscal
  year and for the same site if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all the cannabis sold during the applicable fiscal
    year under those licences is sold in Canada to a person that is a
    client or a named responsible adult or to which an exemption has
    been granted under section 140 of the Act for a medical purpose and
    in relation to the cannabis or class of cannabis that is sold;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (1.1), the holder signs and
    submits to the Minister a written document indicating that the
    holder intends to sell all cannabis during the applicable fiscal
    year only in Canada to a person that is a client or a named
    responsible adult or to which an exemption has been granted under
    section 140 of the Act for a medical purpose and in relation to the
    cannabis or class of cannabis that is sold

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the fee referred to in subsection
      7(1), within 60 days after the earliest effective date of the
      licences, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, not later than September 30
      of every fiscal year.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SI/2024-38, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- written document

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a licence for sale for medical
  purposes is not required to submit to the Minister the written
  document referred to in paragraph (1)(b) during the applicable fiscal
  year in any of the following cases:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder's licence is revoked or expires not
    later than September 30;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the holder notifies the Minister not later than
    September 30 that the holder intends to cease conducting all the
    activities authorized by their licence; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the holder requests a revocation of their licence
    not later than September 30.

- [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a holder of a licence for sale for
  medical purposes who notifies the Minister in accordance with
  paragraph (1.1)(b), the holder is not required to submit the written
  document referred to in paragraph (1)(b) until the holder resumes
  conducting any activity authorized by their licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- separate sites

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The holder of a licence for sale for medical purposes
  with respect to a site and any other licence set out in column 1 of
  Schedule 2 with respect to another site is not required to pay a fee
  referred to in subsection 7(1) and paragraphs 7(2)(a) and (b) and
  (3)(a) and (b) and subsection 8(2) for a fiscal year and for any of
  the licences that the person holds if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the holder does not hold any other licence set out
    in column 1 with respect to the site referred to in the licence for
    sale for medical purposes; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (1.1), the holder meets the
    conditions set out in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents and information

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The holder of a licence
set out in column 1 of Schedule 2 must retain the following for not less
than seven years after the day on which they are prepared and in a
manner that will enable an audit to be made in a timely manner:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} documents or information stating the amount received
  from the sale of cannabis, the amount paid for the purchase of
  cannabis and the name of the person from which cannabis was purchased;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if section 10 applies to the holder, documents or
  information confirming that all sales of cannabis under the licence
  were made in Canada to a person that was, at the time of the sale, a
  client or a named responsible adult or to which an exemption had been,
  at the time of the sale, granted under section 140 of the Act for a
  medical purpose and in relation to the cannabis or class of cannabis
  that was sold.
