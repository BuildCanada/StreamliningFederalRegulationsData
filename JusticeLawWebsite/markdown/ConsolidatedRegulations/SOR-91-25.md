## Artists' Representatives (GST/HST) Regulations {#artists-representatives-gsthst-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-25
:::

[EXCISE TAX ACT](/eng/acts/E-15)

Registration 1990-12-18

Artists' Representatives (GST/HST) Regulations

P.C. 1990-2734  1990-12-18
::::

:::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA} of the [Excise
Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15), is pleased hereby to make the annexed
Regulations prescribing registrants who make supplies of intangible
personal property on behalf of artists.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e-ID0EFBAA){#footnote1_e}S.C. 1990, c.
  45, s. 12
  :::
:::

## [Prescribed Registrants]{.HTitleText1} {#h-933407 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Any registrant listed in
the schedule is prescribed for the purposes of subsection 177(2) of the
[Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15).
