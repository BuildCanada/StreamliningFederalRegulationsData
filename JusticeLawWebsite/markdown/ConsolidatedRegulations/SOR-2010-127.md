## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757656 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Shipping Act,
    2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[berth]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a wharf, a pier, an anchorage
    and a mooring buoy. ([poste d'amarrage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dangerous goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the substances,
    materials and articles covered by the International Maritime
    Dangerous Goods Code. ([marchandises dangereuses]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[NORDREG Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[NORDREG Zone]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Northern Canada
    Vessel Traffic Services Zone established under section 2. ([zone
    NORDREG]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[oil]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 165
    of the Act. ([hydrocarbures]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pollutant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    185 of the Act. ([polluant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[SOLAS]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the International Convention for
    the Safety of Life at Sea, 1974, and the Protocol of 1988 relating
    to the Convention, as amended from time to
    time. ([SOLAS]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Northern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757666 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed zone

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Northern Canada Vessel
Traffic Services Zone is established. It consists of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the shipping safety control zones prescribed by the
  [Shipping Safety Control Zones
  Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._356);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Ungava Bay, Hudson Bay and Kugmallit
  Bay that are not in a shipping safety control zone;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the waters of James Bay;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Koksoak River from Ungava Bay to
  Kuujjuaq;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Feuilles Bay from Ungava Bay to
  Tasiujaq;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the waters of Chesterfield Inlet that are not within
  a shipping safety control zone, and the waters of Baker Lake; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the waters of the Moose River from James Bay to
  Moosonee.

## [Prescribed Classes of Vessels]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757676 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsections 126(1) and (3) of the Act

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following vessels are
prescribed as classes of vessels for the purposes of subsections 126(1)
and (3) of the Act in respect of the NORDREG Zone:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} vessels of 300 gross tonnage or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} vessels that are engaged in towing or pushing another
  vessel, if the combined gross tonnage of the vessel and the vessel
  being towed or pushed is 500 gross tonnage or more; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} vessels that are carrying as cargo a pollutant or
  dangerous goods, or that are engaged in towing or pushing a vessel
  that is carrying as cargo a pollutant or dangerous goods.

## [Obligation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757682 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Master

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The master of a vessel of a
class prescribed by section 3 must ensure that the requirements of
sections 5 to 10 are met in respect of the vessel.

## [Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-757685 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Type of report

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  report required by any of sections 6 to 9 must begin with the term
  "NORDREG" and be followed by whichever of the following two letters
  corresponds to the report:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} "SP", in the case of a sailing plan report;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} "PR", in the case of a position report;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} "FR", in the case of a final report;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} "DR", in the case of a deviation report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The report must include the applicable designators
  required by sections 6 to 9 and set out in column 1 of the schedule,
  followed by the information that is about the subject set out in
  column 2 and that is specified in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sailing plan report

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  sailing plan report must be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when a vessel is about to enter the NORDREG Zone;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} more than one hour but not more than two hours
    before a vessel departs from a berth within the NORDREG Zone, unless
    the vessel is moving to another berth in the same port; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} immediately before a vessel gets underway within
    the NORDREG Zone, if the vessel

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has been stranded,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has stopped as a result of a breakdown in the
      main propulsion or steering system, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been involved in a collision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designators

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The sailing plan report must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraph (1)(a),
    the designators A, B, either C or D, E, F, G, H, I, L, O, P, Q, S,
    T, W and X;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraph (1)(b),
    the designators A, B, either C or D, H, I, L, O, P, Q, S, T, W and
    X; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the circumstances set out in paragraph (1)(c),
    the designators A, B, either C or D, I, L, O, P, Q, S, T, W and X.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to paragraph (2)(a)

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the vessel is about to enter the NORDREG Zone
  directly from the Eastern Canada Vessel Traffic Services Zone and has
  obtained a clearance issued under paragraph 126(3)(a) of the Act with
  respect to that zone, the designators O, Q and T do not need to be
  included.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception to paragraphs (2)(b) and (c)

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a report required by paragraph (1)(b)
  or (c), the designators O, P, Q, S, T, W and X do not need to be
  included if the corresponding information specified in column 3 of the
  schedule has not changed since the previous sailing plan report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Position report

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  position report must be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immediately after a vessel enters the NORDREG Zone;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} daily at 1600 Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), if
    a vessel is underway within the NORDREG Zone, unless the information
    required by regulation 19-1, Long-range identification and tracking
    of ships, of Chapter V of SOLAS, is being transmitted in accordance
    with that regulation.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional position report

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A position report must also be provided as soon as
  feasible after a vessel's master becomes aware of any of the
  following, if the vessel is within or about to enter the NORDREG Zone:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} another vessel in apparent difficulty;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any obstruction to navigation;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an aid to navigation that is not functioning
    properly or is damaged, out of position or missing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any ice or weather conditions that are hazardous to
    safe navigation; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a pollutant in the water.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designators

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The position report must include the following
  designators:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} A, B, either C or D, E, F and S; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} X, in the case of a position report required by
    subsection (2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Final report

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A final
  report must be provided

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the arrival of a vessel at a berth within the
    NORDREG Zone; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} immediately before a vessel exits the NORDREG Zone.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designators

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The final report must include the designators A and
  K.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Deviation report

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  deviation report must be provided when

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a vessel's position varies significantly from the
    position that was expected based on the sailing plan report; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a vessel's intended voyage changes from the sailing
    plan report.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designators

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The deviation report must include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the designators A, B, and either C or D; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the other designators included in the sailing plan
    report, if the corresponding information specified in column 3 of
    the schedule has changed since that report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Address of report

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Every report must be
addressed to NORDREG CANADA and be provided to one of the Marine
Communications and Traffic Services Centres that is designated by the
Canadian Coast Guard to receive the report.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}July 1, 2010

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on July 1, 2010.
