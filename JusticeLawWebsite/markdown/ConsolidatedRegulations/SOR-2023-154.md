## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1411825 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Motor Vehicle Safety
Act](/eng/acts/M-10.01).

## [Overview]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1411828 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Overview

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations complete
the administrative monetary penalty scheme set out in the Act for the
contravention of certain of its provisions and of certain provisions of
its regulations and of the orders made under the Act.

## [Designated Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1411831 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- Act and Regulations

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  provision of the Act or of its regulations that is set out in column 1
  of the schedule is designated as a provision the contravention of
  which may be proceeded with as a violation in accordance with sections
  16.13 to 16.23 of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount payable in respect of a violation
  referred to in subsection (1) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, the amount set out in
    column 2 of the schedule; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation or a company, the
    amount set out in column 3 of the schedule.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation --- orders

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An order
  made under any of subsections 8.1(1), 10(2.1), 10(4), 10.1(4), 10.1(7)
  and 10.4(4), section 10.5, subsections 10.6(1), 10.61(1) and 13(1),
  section 13.1, paragraph 15(4)(e) and section 15.1 of the Act is
  designated as a provision the contravention of which may be proceeded
  with as a violation in accordance with sections 16.13 to 16.23 of the
  Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum amount payable

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The maximum amount payable in respect of a violation
  referred to in subsection (1) is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an individual, \$4,000; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a corporation or a company,
    \$200,000.
