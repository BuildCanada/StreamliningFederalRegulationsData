## Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on South Sudan {#regulations-implementing-the-united-nations-resolutions-on-south-sudan .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2015-165
:::

[UNITED NATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/U-2)

Registration 2015-06-19

Regulations Implementing the United Nations Resolutions on South Sudan

P.C. 2015-849 2015-06-18
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Security Council of the United Nations, acting under Article
41 of the Charter of the United Nations, adopted Resolution 2206 (2015)
on March 3, 2015;

And whereas it appears to the Governor in Council to be necessary to
make regulations for enabling the measures set out in that resolution to
be effectively applied;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to section 2
of the [United Nations Act](/eng/acts/U-2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-2479-E_hq_15721){#fn_81000-2-2479-E_hq_15721-ID0EDBAA},
makes the annexed [Regulations Implementing the United Nations
Resolution on South Sudan](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-165).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-817981 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[armed mercenary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[armed mercenary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is specially recruited to use arms and related
      material in South Sudan or uses arms and related material in South
      Sudan;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is motivated essentially by the desire for
      private gain;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not a member of the armed forces of South
      Sudan; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is not sent to South Sudan by a state on official
      duty as a member of that state's armed forces. ([mercenaire
      armé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[arms and related material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any type of
    weapon, ammunition, military equipment including military vehicles,
    or paramilitary equipment, and their spare parts. ([armes et
    matériel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Canadian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a citizen within the meaning
    of the [Citizenship Act](/eng/acts/C-29) or an entity established,
    incorporated or continued by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province. ([Canadien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Committee of the Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Committee of the Security Council of the United Nations established
    under paragraph 16 of Resolution 2206 (2015) of March 3, 2015,
    adopted by the Security Council. ([Comité du Conseil de
    sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that is
    designated by the Committee of the Security Council under paragraph
    6 of Resolution 2206 (2015) of March 3, 2015, adopted by the
    Security Council. ([personne désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a corporation, trust,
    partnership, fund, unincorporated association or organization or a
    foreign state. ([entité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Focal Point for De-listing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Focal Point for De-listing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2020-121, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any activities
    conducted by state armed forces, non-state armed forces or armed
    mercenaries and any activities that support the operational
    capabilities of an armed group. ([activités
    militaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Foreign
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is or was employed in the service of Her Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a province;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} occupies or occupied a position of responsibility
      in the service of Her Majesty in right of Canada or of a province;
      or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is or was engaged by or on behalf of Her Majesty
      in right of Canada or of a
      province. ([fonctionnaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Panel of Experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Panel of Experts]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2020-121, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual or
    entity. ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[property]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2020-121, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Security Council
    of the United Nations. ([Conseil de sécurité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Security Council Resolution 2206]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Security Council Resolution 2206]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2020-121, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[South Sudan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[South Sudan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Republic of South
    Sudan and includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any of its political subdivisions;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} its government and any of its departments or a
      government or department of its political subdivisions; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of its agencies or any agency of its
      political subdivisions. ([Soudan du Sud]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[technical assistance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any form of
    assistance, such as providing instruction, training, consulting
    services or technical advice or transferring know-how or technical
    data. ([aide technique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[working day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a day that is not Saturday
    or a holiday. ([jour ouvrable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818003 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} deal in any property in Canada that is owned --- or
  that is held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by a designated
  person or by a person acting on behalf of, or at the direction of, a
  designated person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} enter into or facilitate any transaction related to a
  dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} provide or acquire any financial or related services
  in respect of a dealing referred to in paragraph (a);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} make available any property to, or for the benefit
  of, a designated person, a person acting on behalf of or at the
  direction of a designated person or an entity that is owned --- or
  that is held or controlled, directly or indirectly --- by a designated
  person; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} provide any financial or related services to, or for
  the benefit of, any person or entity referred to in paragraph (d) or
  acquire any such services from or for the benefit of any such person
  or entity.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- humanitarian assistance

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** Section 2 does not
prohibit the provision, processing or payment of funds, other financial
assets or economic resources or the provision of goods and services that
are necessary to ensure the timely delivery of humanitarian assistance
or to support other activities that support basic human needs in South
Sudan by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the United Nations, including its programs, funds and
  other entities and bodies, as well as its specialized agencies and
  related organizations;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international organizations;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} humanitarian organizations that have been granted
  observer status by the United Nations General Assembly and members of
  those humanitarian organizations;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} bilaterally or multilaterally funded non-governmental
  organizations that are participating in any United Nations
  humanitarian response plan, refugee response plan or other United
  Nations appeals for assistance or in groups of humanitarian
  organizations coordinated by the United Nations Office for the
  Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs, including the employees,
  grantees, subsidiaries and implementing partners of those
  non-governmental organizations while acting in those capacities;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other person authorized for that purpose by a
  United Nations committee established by the Security Council acting
  within its mandate.

person referred to in that section became a designated person. However,

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Embargo --- arms and related material

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** It is prohibited for any
person in Canada or any Canadian outside Canada to knowingly

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} export, sell, supply or transfer, directly or
  indirectly, arms and related material, wherever situated, to South
  Sudan or any person in South Sudan; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, technical assistance
  or financial assistance related to the sale, supply, transfer,
  manufacture, maintenance or use of arms and related material to South
  Sudan or to any person in South Sudan.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- United Nations personnel

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  3 and 5 do not apply if the arms and related materials are intended

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the Committee of the Security Council has been
  notified in advance, sections 3 and 5 do not apply to an activity that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the forces of a state that is taking action, in

    regional operations to counter the Lord's Resistance Army.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advance approval

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 3 and 5 do not apply to an activity that is
  intended solely to ensure the implementation of the terms of the Peace
  agreement, that is the 27 June 2018 Khartoum Declaration or to any
  other activity if the Committee of the Security Council has given
  advance approval.

## [Obligations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1249659 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to determine

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The following entities must
determine on a continuing basis whether they are in possession or
control of property that is owned, held or controlled by or on behalf of
a designated person:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [[authorized foreign banks]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef},
  as defined in section 2 of the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), in
  respect of their business in Canada, and banks regulated by that Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} cooperative credit societies, savings and credit
  unions and caisses populaires regulated by a provincial Act and
  associations regulated by the [Cooperative Credit Associations
  Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} [[foreign companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as
  defined in subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), in respect of their insurance business in
  Canada;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} [[companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, [[provincial
  companies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} and
  [[societies]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as those terms are defined in
  subsection 2(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8);

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} fraternal benefit societies regulated by a provincial
  Act in respect of their insurance activities and insurance companies
  and other entities regulated by a provincial Act that are engaged in
  the business of insuring risks;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} companies regulated by the [Trust and Loan Companies
  Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8);

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} trust companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} loan companies regulated by a provincial Act;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} entities that engage in any activity described in
  paragraph 5(h) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
  Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) if the activity involves
  the opening of an account for a client; and

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} entities authorized under provincial legislation to
  engage in the business of dealing in securities or to provide
  portfolio management or investment counselling services.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to disclose --- RCMP or CSIS

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  person in Canada, every Canadian outside Canada and every entity set
  out in section 9 must disclose without delay to the Commissioner of
  the Royal Canadian Mounted Police or to the Director of the Canadian
  Security Intelligence Service

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the existence of property in their possession or
    control that they have reason to believe is owned, held or
    controlled by or on behalf of a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any information about a transaction or proposed
    transaction in respect of property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Immunity

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No proceedings under the [United Nations
  Act](/eng/acts/U-2) and no civil proceedings lie against a person for
  a disclosure made in good faith under subsection (1).

## [Applications]{.HTitleText1} {#h-818066 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person that wishes to engage in any activity that is prohibited under
  these Regulations must, before doing so, apply to the Minister in
  writing for a certificate to exempt the activity from the application
  of the Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue the certificate if the
  Security Council did not intend that the activity be prohibited or if
  the Security Council or the Committee of the Security Council has
  approved the activity in advance.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Basic or extraordinary expenses

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose property is affected by the application of section 2 may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt the
  property from the application of that section if the property is
  necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses or is subject to a lien,
  mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
  charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is established in accordance with Resolution
  2206 (2015) of March 3, 2015, adopted by the Security Council, that
  the property is necessary for basic or extraordinary expenses or that
  it is subject to a lien, mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec
  or prior claim, to a charge or to a judicial, administrative or
  arbitral decision, the Minister must issue a certificate within the
  following time periods:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 15 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property necessary for basic expenses,
    if the Committee of the Security Council did not oppose the
    application;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property necessary for extraordinary
    expenses, if the Committee of the Security Council approved the
    application; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} within 90 working days after receiving the
    application, in the case of property that is subject to a lien,
    mortgage or security interest, to a hypothec or prior claim, to a
    charge or to a judicial, administrative or arbitral decision that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} was created or issued before the person became a
      designated person,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is not for the benefit of a designated person,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has been brought to the attention of the
      Committee of the Security Council by the Minister.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- parties to contract

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is a party to a contract or a gratuitous transfer may apply
  to the Minister in writing for a certificate to exempt property from
  the application of section 2 to permit them to receive payments or a
  transfer from a designated person or to permit a designated person to
  make payments or to carry out the transfer.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certificate --- time period

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must issue a certificate within 90
  working days after receiving the application and at least 10 working
  days after advising the Committee of the Security Council of the
  intention to issue the certificate, if it is established that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contract was entered into or the transfer was
    carried out prior to any party becoming a designated person; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the payments or transfer are not to be received,
    directly or indirectly, by a designated person, by a person acting
    on behalf of, or at the direction of, a designated person or by an
    entity that is owned, held or controlled, directly or indirectly, by
    a designated person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Mistaken identity

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person whose name is the same as or similar to the name of a
  designated person and that claims not to be that designated person may
  apply to the Minister in writing for a certificate stating that they
  are not that designated person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Determination by Minister

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Within 45 working days after receiving the
  application, the Minister must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} issue the certificate, if it is established that
    the applicant is not the designated person; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide notice to the applicant of his or her
    determination, if it is not so established.

## [Personal Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1249722 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication by Minister

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may, for the purpose of enforcing these Regulations or
  fulfilling any obligation under a resolution of the Security Council
  with respect to South Sudan or to respond to a request from the
  Security Council or any of its subsidiary organs, disclose any
  personal information to an official or the Security Council or any of
  its subsidiary organs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Receipt of information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An official may receive any personal information
  disclosed to them under subsection (1).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Communication by official

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} An official may, for the purpose of enforcing these
  Regulations or fulfilling any obligation under a resolution of the
  Security Council with respect to South Sudan or to respond to a
  request from the Security Council or any of its subsidiary organs,
  disclose any personal information to the Minister.
