## Saskatchewan Alfalfa Seed Order {#saskatchewan-alfalfa-seed-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2001-345
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 2001-09-20

Saskatchewan Alfalfa Seed Order

P.C. 2001-1641 2001-09-20
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Agriculture and Agri-Food, pursuant to subsections 2(1)
and (2) of the [Agricultural Products Marketing Act](/eng/acts/A-6),
hereby makes the annexed [Saskatchewan Alfalfa Seed
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2001-345).
:::

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672883 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[alfalfa seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alfalfa seed]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seed produced from
    the alfalfa plant, including [medicago sativa]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}, [medicago media]{.otherLang lang="la"} and [medicago
    falcata]{.otherLang lang="la"}. ([semence de
    luzerne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Alfalfa
    Seed Producers Development Commission.
    ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Saskatchewan Alfalfa Seed
    Producers Development Plan Regulations, established by Order in
    Council 513/1997 of the Lieutenant Governor in Council of
    Saskatchewan on July 23, 1997 as amended from time to time, pursuant
    to The Agri-Food Act of Saskatchewan. ([Programme]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672888 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Commission is hereby
authorized to regulate the marketing of alfalfa seed in interprovincial
and export trade, and for those purposes may, with respect to persons
and property situated within the province of Saskatchewan, exercise all
or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of alfalfa seed locally within that province under section 7
of the Plan.

## [Levies or Charges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672890 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commission may, in
relation to the powers granted to it by section 2,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} fix, impose and collect levies or charges from
  persons referred to in section 2 who are engaged in the production or
  marketing of alfalfa seed and, for that purpose, classify those
  persons into groups and fix the levies or charges payable by the
  members of the different groups in different amounts; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} use the levies or charges for the purposes of the
  Commission, including the creation of reserves.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-672894 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
