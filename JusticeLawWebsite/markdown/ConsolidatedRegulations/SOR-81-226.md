## Interim Payments and Recovery of Overpayments Regulations {#interim-payments-and-recovery-of-overpayments-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-226
:::

[PAYMENTS IN LIEU OF TAXES ACT](/eng/acts/M-13)

Registration 1981-03-09

Interim Payments and Recovery of Overpayments Regulations
::::

:::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of Public Works, pursuant to subsection 8(5) of the
Municipal Grants Act, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBCA}, hereby makes
the following Regulations respecting the making of an interim payment in
respect of a grant in lieu of a real property tax or a frontage or area
tax and the recovery of any overpayments made to a taxing authority by
way of set-off against other grants.

## [Interim Payments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870579 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** When, in respect of an
application made by a taxing authority under section 3 of the Act, a
final determination of the amount of the payment cannot be made within
50 days after receipt of the application, or within 90 days in the case
of an application made for the first time, the Minister may

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} estimate, on the basis of the information available
  to the Minister, the amount that may be paid to the taxing authority
  under section 3 of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} make an interim payment to the taxing authority in an
  amount that does not exceed the amount referred to in paragraph (a).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-487, s. 5
:::

## [Recovery of Overpayments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-870585 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** If any payment made to a
taxing authority under the Act or these Regulations is greater than the
amount that may be paid to the taxing authority under section 3 of the
Act, the amount of the overpayment and interest on that amount
prescribed for the purpose of section 155.1 of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11) may be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} set off against other payments that may otherwise be
  paid to the taxing authority under section 3 of the Act or these
  Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} recovered as a debt due to Her Majesty in right of
  Canada by the taxing authority.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-487, s. 5
:::
