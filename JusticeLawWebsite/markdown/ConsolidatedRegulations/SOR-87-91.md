## Order Authorizing the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory Affairs) to Exercise the Powers Given to the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans Under the Act in Respect of Certain Companies {#order-authorizing-the-minister-of-state-privatization-and-regulatory-affairs-to-exercise-the-powers-given-to-the-minister-of-fisheries-and-oceans-under-the-act-in-respect-of-certain-companies .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-91
:::

[ATLANTIC FISHERIES RESTRUCTURING ACT](/eng/acts/A-14)

Registration 1987-02-12

Order Authorizing the Minister of State (Privatization and Regulatory
Affairs) to Exercise the Powers Given to the Minister of Fisheries and
Oceans Under the Act in Respect of Certain Companies

P.C. 1987-256 1987-02-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 7 of the [Atlantic Fisheries
Restructuring Act](/eng/acts/A-14)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCA}, is pleased
hereby

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EBBCA){#footnote1_e}S.C.
  1980-81-82-83, c. 172
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1985-2003 of June 20,
  1985[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EBBBA}; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e-ID0EBBBA){#footnote2_e}SOR/85-594,
    1985 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2961
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to authorize the Minister of State (Privatization and
  Regulatory Affairs) to exercise the powers given to the Minister of
  Fisheries and Oceans pursuant to the [Atlantic Fisheries Restructuring
  Act](/eng/acts/A-14) in respect of 125459 Canada Limited, Fishery
  Products International Limited and 125457 Canada Limited known as
  NSHOLDCO Limited.
