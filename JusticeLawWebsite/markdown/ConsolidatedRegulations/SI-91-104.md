## Secretary of State Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Student Loans) {#secretary-of-state-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order-student-loans .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/91-104
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1991-07-17

Secretary of State Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Student Loans)

P.C. 1991-1210 1991-06-24
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19.1(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI_91-104_EN_hq_20089){#fn_SI_91-104_EN_hq_20089-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Secretary of State of Canada to prescribe, by order, a
fee based on a percentage of the amount of the loan, to be paid by
full-time students to whom guaranteed student loans are made after July
31, 1991 under the [Canada Student Loans Act](/eng/acts/S-23).
