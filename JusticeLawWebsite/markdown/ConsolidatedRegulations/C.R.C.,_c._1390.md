## Returned Soldiers' Insurance Regulations {#returned-soldiers-insurance-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1390
:::

[RETURNED SOLDIERS' INSURANCE ACT](/eng/acts/R-7.4)

Regulations Respecting Returned Soldiers' Insurance
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508822 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Returned Soldiers' Insurance
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1390).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508824 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In these
  Regulations,

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Returned Soldiers'
      Insurance Act](/eng/acts/R-7.4); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a valid contract of insurance
      entered into under the Act; ([police]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the net premium value of the
      policy computed on the basis of the British Offices Life Tables,
      1893, Om (5), with interest at four per cent per annum;
      ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Superintendent of
      Veterans' Insurance. ([surintendant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any other word or phrase that is defined in the Act
  and used in these Regulations shall, for the purposes of these
  Regulations, have the same meaning as that given to it in the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where under any term or condition of a policy issued
  under the Act any power or authority is to be exercised or anything is
  to be done by the [[Board of Pension Commissioners for
  Canada]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, such
  power or authority shall be exercised or such thing shall be done by
  the Superintendent.

## [Payment of Premiums]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508833 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** All moneys due under any
policy are payable in the City of Ottawa, in the Province of Ontario.

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Premiums
  under any policy are due and payable monthly and the due date of all
  monthly premiums is the first day of the month.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Premiums may be paid quarterly, semi-annually or
  annually in advance and where so paid are respectively three, six and
  12 times the monthly premium.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where the insured dies during any period for which a
  premium has been paid, there shall be refunded in the settlement of
  the policy the portion of the premium paid for the unexpired portion
  of that period calculated from the end of the calendar month in which
  the insured dies.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where during any period for which a premium has been
  paid the policy is surrendered, there shall be refunded in the
  settlement of the policy the cash surrender value of that policy and
  that portion of the premium paid for the unexpired portion of the
  period, calculated from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the beginning of the calendar month in which the
    policy is surrendered, or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the end of the calendar month in which the policy
    is surrendered

  whichever calculation produces the greater amount.

## [Paid-up Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508843 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the insured does not pay, within the prescribed period of grace, a
  premium due under the policy and applies in writing, within three
  months from the date on which the premium fell due, for paid-up
  insurance, he is entitled to paid-up insurance for a reduced amount.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of such paid-up insurance is,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a policy requiring that premiums be
    payable for 20 years, that proportion of the original amount of
    insurance that the total premiums paid is to the total of premiums
    originally payable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in all other policies, an amount that the reserve
    on the policy at the date of application will provide when applied
    as a single net premium, computed on the same basis as the reserve,
    at the attained age of the insured.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Paid-up insurance is payable on the same conditions
  as the original insurance.

## [Cash Surrender Value]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508850 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A cash surrender value
based on the reserve on the paid-up insurance to which the insured would
have been entitled under section 5 is, where the Superintendent is
satisfied as to the circumstances of the case, payable to the insured
where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written application therefor is made to the
  Superintendent

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by the insured, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} by the beneficiary or beneficiaries, except where
    his or their whereabouts is unknown or cannot be ascertained by the
    insured after a search satisfactory to the Superintendent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is returned to the Superintendent for
  cancellation or retention.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where the insured does not
pay, within the prescribed period of grace, a premium due under the
policy and no proper application has been made for paid-up insurance or
a cash surrender value, the policy shall be automatically continued in
force for the full amount of insurance for the extension period,
disregarding fractional parts of a month, that the cash surrender value
at the date on which the premium fell due will provide if applied as a
net single premium at the attained age of the insured.

## [Disability Benefits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508857 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the insured applies for any benefit provided by section 9 of the Act,
  he shall, if required by the Superintendent, furnish the report of a
  competent physician on an examination form provided by the
  Superintendent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each claim shall be referred to a medical referee
  appointed by the Superintendent and the referee's decision is final.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The fees of the physician shall be paid by the
  insured.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where a disability is
caused by the mental derangement of the insured, the disability benefit
may be paid to such person or persons on his behalf as the
Superintendent may deem fit.

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-508863 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where the insured does
not pay a premium by the end of the period of grace and the policy has
not been changed to provide paid-up insurance, has not been surrendered
for its cash surrender value or the automatic extension period has not
expired, the insured may, with the consent of the Superintendent, and
after such medical examination and other evidence of insurability as the
Superintendent may deem necessary, restore the policy to a
premium-paying basis at any time within five years from the due date of
the first premium in default by payment of the arrears of premiums
together with interest thereon at six per cent per annum compounded
annually.

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  age, identity, existence or death of persons shall be proved by such
  documents or other evidence as the Minister of Veterans Affairs may
  require.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where proof of age is furnished during the lifetime
  of the insured, the age may be admitted by an authorized endorsement
  on the policy.

<!-- -->

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} There
  shall be an account in the Consolidated Revenue Fund to be called the
  Returned Soldiers' Insurance Fund (in this section referred to as
  [[the Fund]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}) to which shall be credited all
  moneys received and to which shall be charged all moneys paid under
  the Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At the end of each fiscal year, the liability
  outstanding arising out of contracts entered into under the Act shall
  be calculated by the Superintendent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the liability calculated under subsection (2) is
  greater than the balance of the Fund at the date of such calculation,
  there shall be credited to the Fund an amount equal to the excess of
  the liability over the balance of the Fund.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the liability calculated under subsection (2) is
  less than the balance of the Fund at the date of such calculation,
  there shall be charged to the Fund an amount equal to the amount by
  which the balance of the Fund exceeds the liability.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} In this section,
  [[Superintendent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in the
  Department of Insurance Act.
