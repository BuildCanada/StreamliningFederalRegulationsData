## Order Transferring from the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and Associate Minister of Health to the Minister of Health the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-mental-health-and-addictions-and-associate-minister-of-health-to-the-minister-of-health-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-the-canadian-centre-on-substance-abuse-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-37
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Transferring from the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions and
Associate Minister of Health to the Minister of Health the Powers,
Duties and Functions Under the Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act

P.C. 2025-417 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJEAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGEAA},
transfers to the Minister of Health the powers, duties and functions
under the [Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse
Act](/eng/acts/C-13.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1741730666-ID0EDEAA}
that were transferred to the Minister of Mental Health and Addictions
and Associate Minister of Health by Order in Council P.C. 2021-938 of
October 26, 2021[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078){#fn_81000-3-2853_hq_1741737078-ID0EBEAA}.
