## Goods and Services Tax Builders Remission Order {#goods-and-services-tax-builders-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/95-33
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1995-03-22

Order Respecting the Remission of the Tax Under Part IX of the Excise
Tax Act Paid or Payable by Certain Builders in Respect of Additions to
Multiple Unit Residential Complexes

P.C. 1995-317  1995-02-28
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection
23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFABA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of the tax under Part IX of the Excise
Tax Act paid or payable by certain builders in respect of additions to
multiple unit residential complexes](/eng/regulations/SI-95-33).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649010 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Goods and Services Tax Builders Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-95-33).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649012 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[estimated federal sales tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[estimated federal sales tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    an addition to a multiple unit residential complex, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where, in an application for a remission under
      section 5 in respect of the addition, the amount applied for is
      based on the interior floor space of the addition, the amount
      determined by the formula

      ::: {#649017 .Paragraph}
      A × B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is \$50, and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the number of square meters of the interior floor space of
          the addition, and
      :::

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 4.25 per cent of the fair
      market value of the addition at the time the builder of the
      addition is deemed, under subsection 191(4) of the Act, to have
      collected tax in respect of the addition. ([taxe de vente fédérale
      estimative]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interior Floor Space]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649022 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** In this Order, \"interior
floor space\" of an addition to a multiple unit residential complex
includes the width of the enclosing walls of the addition that are not
adjacent to other residential complexes or residential units and half of
the width of the enclosing walls of the addition that are adjacent to
other residential complexes or residential units, but does not include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} storage rooms, attics or basements, unless they are
  finished by a builder of the addition to a standard comparable to the
  living areas of the complex;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} parking areas; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} areas set aside for the placement of equipment for
  the heating of, or the supply of water, gas or electricity to, the
  complex.

## [Basis of Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649027 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A builder of an addition to
a multiple unit residential complex who applies for a remission under
section 5 in respect of the addition shall base the amount applied for
on either the interior floor space or the fair market value of the
addition.

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649029 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a builder of an
addition to a multiple unit residential complex is deemed under
subsection 191(4) of the Act to have collected tax after 1990 in respect
of the addition and the construction of the addition was, on January 1,
1991, more than 25 per cent completed, remission of the tax is, subject
to section 6, hereby granted to the builder in an amount equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the construction of the addition was, on
  January 1, 1991, not more than 50 per cent completed, the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 50 per cent of the estimated federal sales tax in
    respect of the addition, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the tax deemed to have been so
    collected; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the construction of the addition was, on
  January 1, 1991, more than 50 per cent completed, the lesser of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 75 per cent of the estimated federal sales tax in
    respect of the addition, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the tax deemed to have been so
    collected.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-649037 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The remission granted under
section 5 is on condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the builder applies to the Minister of National
  Revenue for the remission before January 1, 1996;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no rebate under section 121 of the Act in respect of
  the complex was paid; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} no remission under section 5 in respect of the
  addition was granted to any other person.
