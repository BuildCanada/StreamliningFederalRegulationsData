## Fresh Fruits and Vegetables (United States Tariff) Import Regulations {#fresh-fruits-and-vegetables-united-states-tariff-import-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/89-58
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1988-12-30

Regulations Respecting the Importation of Certain Fresh Fruits and
Vegetables Entitled to the Benefit of the United States Tariff

P.C. 1988-2882 1988-12-30
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.2(10)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EGCAA} of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_2e){#fn_2e-ID0EDCAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the importation of certain
fresh fruits and vegetables entitled to the benefit of the United States
Tariff](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-58), effective January 1, 1989.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920729 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Fresh Fruits and Vegetables (United States Tariff) Import
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-89-58).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920731 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a receptacle, package,
    wrapper, or confining band used in connection with the goods;
    ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a fresh fruit or vegetable of
    heading No. 07.01, 07.02, 07.03, 07.04, 07.05, 07.06 (other than
    turnips), 07.07, 07.08, 07.09 (other than truffles), 08.09 or 08.10
    (other than cranberries or blueberries) or subheading No. 0806.10 or
    0808.20 that is entitled to the United States Tariff;
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of production]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of the
    places where the goods were produced, the state of the United
    States, the District of Columbia or Puerto Rico; ([point de
    production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 1
:::

## [Statement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-920738 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every person who accounts
for goods under subsection 32(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)
or who makes an interim accounting under subsection 32(2) of that Act
shall provide, at the time of accounting or interim accounting and
before the goods are released, a statement, in duplicate, signed by the
vendor and the purchaser of the goods, or any authorized representative
of the vendor or the purchaser, setting out

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the vendor, purchaser and
  ultimate consignee and their authorized representatives;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the carrier of the goods and
  the identification number of the conveyance in which the goods were
  transported;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the goods, including the tariff item
  under which they are classified;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the weight of the goods in each container and the
  number and size of all containers in the shipment;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the point of production of the goods;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the value for duty, determined in accordance with the
  [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), of the goods in each container;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the F.O.B. purchase price of the goods in each
  container;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the delivered price of the goods in each container,
  if available; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of purchase of the goods and the date of
  shipment from the point of production.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-28, s. 2
