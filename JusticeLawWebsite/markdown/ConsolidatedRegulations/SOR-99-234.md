## Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1 {#regulations-designating-certain-countries-for-the-purposes-of-tariff-item-no.-9810.00.00-1999-1 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/99-234
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1999-06-03

Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes of Tariff
Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1

P.C. 1999-1013 1999-06-03
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 132(1)(g) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes
of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1997, c.
  36
  :::
:::
