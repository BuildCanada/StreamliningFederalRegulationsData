## Canadian Tourism Commission Divestiture Regulations {#canadian-tourism-commission-divestiture-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-15
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

[PUBLIC SERVICE SUPERANNUATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-36)

Registration 2004-02-03

Canadian Tourism Commission Divestiture Regulations
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EHBAA} of the [Public
Service Superannuation Act](/eng/acts/P-36) and paragraph 7(2)(a) of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the annexed
[Canadian Tourism Commission Divestiture
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-15).

## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711568 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commission]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Canadian Tourism
    Commission established by section 3 of the [Canadian Tourism
    Commission Act](/eng/acts/C-23.3). ([Commission]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711572 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), these Regulations apply to a person who
  ceases to be considered to be employed in the public service for the
  purposes of the Act as a result of the Commission ceasing, on January
  2, 2004, to form part of the public service for the purposes of the
  Act, and who

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is employed by the Commission on January 1, 2004;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} remains employed by the Commission for at least one
    day following January 1, 2004.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  subsequently becomes re-employed by the Commission.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Sections 4 to 9 do not apply to a person who has
  received a return of contributions pursuant to subsection 3(3) or has
  exercised an option in accordance with subsection 3(4).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 84(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711581 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12 to 13.01 of the Act only apply to a person on and after the date on
  which that person ceases to be employed by the Commission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person, other than a person who exercises an option
  in accordance with subsection (4), is deemed, for the purposes of the
  application of section 13.01 of the Act, to be employed by a new
  employer as that term is defined in subsection 83(1) of the [Public
  Service Superannuation Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if on January 2, 2004 a
  person would be entitled, were it not for these Regulations, to a
  return of contributions under subsection 12(3) of the Act, that person
  may request, in writing, the payment of that entitlement within one
  year after that date.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), if on January 2, 2004 a
  person would be entitled, were it not for these Regulations, to
  exercise an option under section 13.01 of the Act, that person may
  exercise that option within one year after that date.

## [Survivor and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711587 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(8) of the Act, the survivor and children of a person who
dies while employed by the Commission are entitled to a death benefit
equal to a return of contributions.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(3) of the Act, the survivor and children of a person who
dies while employed by the Commission are entitled to the allowances
described in paragraphs 12(4)(a) and (b) of the Act, subject to the
limitations set out in subsections 12(4) and (5) of the Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a person is deemed to cease to be employed
in the public service on the day on which the person ceases to be
employed by the Commission.

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711593 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one-year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act begins on the date on which the person
ceases to be employed by the Commission.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711595 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 to 13.01 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with the Commission that begins on January 2, 2004 and that
ends on the date on which a person ceases to be employed by the
Commission.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when the person
ceases to be employed in the public service is the age of the person on
the day on which that person ceases to be employed by the Commission.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 84(E)
:::

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** A contributor to whom
these Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 83

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-711603 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on January 2, 2004.
