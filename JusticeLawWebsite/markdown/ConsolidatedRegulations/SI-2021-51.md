## Order Designating the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern Ontario as a Department and the President as Deputy Head of that Agency for the Purposes of that Act {#order-designating-the-federal-economic-development-agency-for-northern-ontario-as-a-department-and-the-president-as-deputy-head-of-that-agency-for-the-purposes-of-that-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2021-51
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2021-08-18

Order Designating the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern
Ontario as a Department and the President as Deputy Head of that Agency
for the Purposes of that Act

P.C. 2021-844 2021-08-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, effective August 12, 2021,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  "department" in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133){#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBBBAA},
  designates the Federal Economic Development Agency for Northern
  Ontario as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_608630-E_hq_133}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition "deputy
  head" in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608630-E_hq_133){#fn_608630-E_hq_133-ID0EBAAAA},
  designates the President of the Federal Economic Development Agency
  for Northern Ontario as the deputy head of that Agency for the
  purposes of that Act.
:::
