## Television Broadcasting Regulations, 1987 {#television-broadcasting-regulations-1987 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/87-49
:::

[BROADCASTING ACT](/eng/acts/B-9.01)

Registration 1987-01-09

Regulations Respecting Television Broadcasting
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a copy of proposed [Regulations respecting television
broadcasting](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-49), substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on August 9, 1986 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to licensees and other
interested persons to make representations with respect hereto;

Therefore, the Canadian Radio-Television and Telecommunications
Commission, on the recommendation of the Executive Committee, pursuant
to subsection 16(1) of the [Broadcasting Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01), hereby
revokes sections 5 to 7 and 9 to 24 and Schedules I and II of the
Television Broadcasting Regulations, C.R.C., c. 381, effective January
9, 1987, and sections 1 to 4 and 8 of the said Regulations, effective
October 1, 1987, and makes the annexed [Regulations respecting
television broadcasting](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-49), effective January
9, 1987.

Hull, Quebec, January 9, 1987
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906180 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Broadcasting
    Act](/eng/acts/B-9.01); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[advertising material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any commercial
    message and programming that promotes a station, network or program,
    but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a station or network identification,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the announcement of an upcoming program that is
      voiced over credits,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a program that consists exclusively of classified
      announcements, if the program is broadcast not more than once
      during a broadcast day and has a duration of not more than one
      hour, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a promotion for a Canadian program or a Canadian
      feature film, notwithstanding that a sponsor is identified in the
      title of the program or the film or is identified as a sponsor of
      that program or that film, where the identification is limited to
      the name of the sponsor only and does not include a description,
      representation or attribute of the sponsor's products or
      services; ([matériel publicitaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[affiliation agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[affiliation agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an agreement
    between one or more stations and another party according to which
    programs provided by the other party will be broadcast by the
    stations at a predetermined time; ([contrat
    d'affiliation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[alcoholic beverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    commercial message, means an alcoholic beverage the sale of which is
    regulated by the law of the province in which the commercial message
    is broadcast; ([boisson alcoolisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[baseband]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[baseband]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means signals in the frequency
    range 0 to 120 kHz that are used as input to the sound transmitter

:   [[broadcast day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a licensee,

    the licensee; ([journée de radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[broadcast month]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[broadcast year]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a licensee,

    radiodiffusion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[Canadian program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program

      subsection 125.4(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), has
      been issued; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that qualifies as a Canadian program in
      accordance with the criteria established by the Commission in

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} Appendices 1 and 2 to Broadcasting Regulatory

[[clock hour]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[commercial message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial message]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an advertisement

[[election period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[election period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906228 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply in respect of programming that is broadcast by a licensee using a
signal contained in a second audio program channel or a multiplex
channel or during the vertical blanking interval.

## [Programming Content]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906257 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee shall not broadcast

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} anything in contravention of the law;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any abusive comment or abusive pictorial
    representation that, when taken in context, tends to or is likely to
    expose an individual or a group or class of individuals to hatred or
    contempt on the basis of race, national or ethnic origin, colour,
    religion, sex, sexual orientation, age or mental or physical
    disability;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any obscene or profane language or pictorial
    representation; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any false or misleading news.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(b), sexual
  orientation does not include the orientation towards any sexual act or
  activity that would constitute an offence under the [Criminal
  Code](/eng/acts/C-46).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph (1)(c), material is
  obscene if it has as a dominant characteristic the undue exploitation
  of sex or the combination of sexual content with one or more of the
  following subjects, namely, crime, horror, cruelty and violence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall provide the record required by
  subsection (2) to the Commission or to an inspector designated

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The approval of the script of a commercial message or
  an endorsement referred to in subsection (1) does not indicate that

## [Political Broadcasts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906288 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** During an election period,
a licensee shall allocate time for the broadcasting of programs,
advertisements or announcements of a partisan political character on an
equitable basis to all accredited political parties and rival candidates
represented in the election or referendum.

## [Ethnic Programs]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906290 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} On or
  before January 31 of each year, the licensee of an ethnic station
  shall submit to the Commission, for approval, a calendar that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} covers a period of 52 or 53 weeks;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} begins on the Monday of the week during which the
    next broadcast year begins; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is divided into 12 periods of four or five weeks.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The licensee of an ethnic station shall devote to
  ethnic programs not less than 60 per cent of the total number of hours
  devoted to broadcasting during the aggregate of the broadcast days in
  each of the four or five week periods in the calendar approved by the
  Commission and referred to in subsection (1).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a licensee's
  condition of licence, the licensee of an ethnic station shall devote
  to third language programs at least 50% of the total number of hours
  devoted to broadcasting during the aggregate of the broadcast days in
  each of the four or five week periods in the calendar approved by the
  Commission and referred to in subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a licensee's
  condition of licence to devote up to 40% of a broadcast month to third
  language programs, the licensee of a station other than an ethnic
  station shall devote not more than 15% of its broadcast month to third
  language programs.

## [Non-Disclosure]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906301 .Part}

- **[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensee whose programming services are being distributed by a
  licensed distribution undertaking or that is negotiating terms of
  carriage with such an undertaking for its programming services,
  including new programming services, shall sign and provide to the
  licensee of the distribution undertaking an agreement that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reproduces the non-disclosure provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains its consent to comply with the
    non-disclosure provisions for the benefit of the licensee of the
    distribution undertaking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensee whose programs are being broadcast by a
  licensed video-on-demand undertaking or that is negotiating terms of
  carriage with such an undertaking for its programs shall sign and
  provide to the licensee of the video-on-demand undertaking an
  agreement that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reproduces the non-disclosure provisions; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} contains its consent to comply with the
    non-disclosure provisions for the benefit of the licensee of the
    video-on-demand undertaking.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsections (1) and (2), the
  non-disclosure provisions are those provisions set out in the Appendix
  to Broadcasting Regulatory Policy CRTC 2013-578, dated October 31,
  2013 and entitled Standard clauses for non-disclosure agreements.

- SOR/2014-206, s. 1

## [Logs and Records]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906312 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to any condition of licence,
  a licensee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep, in a form acceptable to the Commission, a
    program log or a machine readable record of its programming;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} retain the log or record for a period of one year
    after the date when the programming was broadcast; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cause to be entered in the log or record each day
    the following information:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the call letters, location and channel of the
      licensee's station,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the time at which each station identification
      announcement is made,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the time of commencement of advertising
      material, its duration and, in the case of a commercial message,
      the name of the person selling or promoting goods, services,
      natural resources or activities, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in relation to each program broadcast,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its title and any additional information that
        is to be included by the appropriate subitem of Schedule I,

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (4), the key figure set
        out in Schedule I describing the program,

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the time at which the program begins and ends,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the code set out in Schedule II indicating the
        language, type or group, as applicable, and

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the code set out in Schedule
        II indicating an accessible program, and

      - [(F)]{.lawlabel} where applicable, the code set out in Schedule
        II indicating programming that is locally relevant.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The times required to be entered pursuant to
  subparagraphs (1)(c)(iii) and (iv) and clause (1)(c)(v)(C) are local
  times.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall furnish to the Commission, within 30 days
  after the end of each month, the program log or machine-readable
  record of the licensee for that month, together with a certificate
  signed by or on behalf of the licensee attesting to the accuracy of
  the contents of the log or record.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where more than one subitem of Schedule I applies to
  a program, a licensee may, in respect of that program, cause to be
  entered in its program log or machine readable record

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the key figures indicating the subitems that apply
    to each segment of the program, in the order in which the segments
    are broadcast; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the start time and duration of each segment of the
    program.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall retain a clear and intelligible
  audio-visual recording of all of its programming

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for four weeks from the date of broadcast; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Commission receives a complaint from any
    person regarding programming or for any other reason wishes to
    investigate it and so notifies the licensee before the expiration of
    the period referred to in paragraph (a), for eight weeks from the
    date of the broadcast.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} Where, before the expiry of the applicable period
  referred to in subsection (5), the Commission requests from a licensee
  a clear and intelligible audio or audiovisual recording of its
  programming, the licensee shall furnish it to the Commission
  forthwith.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} Where a program is broadcast during reserved time by
  a station operator who operates as part of a television network,
  subsection (5) applies only to the network operator.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} This section does not apply to the licensee of a
  remote station where logging or record-keeping requirements are set
  out in a condition of licence.

## [Transfer of Ownership or Control]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906361 .Part}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of this section,

  [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[associate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, when used to indicate a
      relationship with any person, includes

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a partner of the person,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a trust or an estate in which the person has a
        substantial beneficial interest or in respect of which the
        person serves as a trustee or in a similar capacity,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of the person,

      - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} a child of the person or of their spouse or
        common-law partner, including a child adopted in fact by the
        person or by the spouse or common-law partner,

      - [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} the spouse or common-law partner of a child
        referred to in paragraph (c.1),

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a relative of the person, or of the spouse or
        common-law partner referred to in paragraph (c), if that
        relative has the same residence as the person,

      - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which the person alone, or a
        person together with one or more associates as described in this
        definition, has, directly or indirectly, control of 50 per cent
        or more of the voting interests,

      - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a corporation of which an associate, as
        described in this definition, of the person has, directly or
        indirectly, control of 50 per cent or more of the voting
        interests, and

      - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person, with whom the person has entered into
        an arrangement, a contract, an understanding or an agreement in
        respect of the voting of shares of a licensee corporation or of
        a corporation that has, directly or indirectly, effective
        control of a licensee corporation, except where that person
        controls less than one per cent of all issued voting shares of a
        corporation whose shares are publicly traded on a stock
        exchange; ([liens]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
      person, means an individual who is cohabiting with the person in a
      conjugal relationship, having so cohabited for a period of at
      least one year; ([conjoint de fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[common shares]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the shares that
      represent the residual equity in the earnings of a corporation,
      and includes securities that are convertible into such shares at
      all times at the option of the holder and the preferred shares to
      which are attached rights to participate in the earnings of the
      corporation with no upper limit; ([actions
      ordinaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes an individual, a
      partnership, a joint venture, an association, a corporation, a
      trust, an estate, a trustee, an executor and an administrator, or
      a legal representative of any of them;
      ([personne]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a corporation with share capital, means the
        vote attached to a voting share,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation without share capital, means an
        interest that entitles the owner to voting rights similar to
        those enjoyed by the owner of a voting share,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a partnership, a trust, an association or a
        joint venture, means an ownership interest in the assets of it
        that entitles the owner to receive a share of the profits of it,
        to receive a share of the assets of it on dissolution and to
        participate directly in the management of it or to vote on the
        election of the persons to be entrusted with the power and
        responsibility to manage it, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a not-for-profit partnership, trust,
        association or joint venture, means a right that entitles the
        owner to participate directly in the management of it or to vote
        on the election of the persons to be entrusted with the power
        and responsibility to manage it; ([intérêt avec droit de
        vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[voting share]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a share in the capital
      of a corporation, to which is attached one or more votes that are
      exercisable at meetings of shareholders of the corporation, either
      under all circumstances or under a circumstance that has occurred
      and is continuing, and includes any security that is convertible
      into such a share at all times at the option of the holder.
      ([action avec droit de vote]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, control of a voting
  interest by a person includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is, directly or indirectly, the
    beneficial owner of the voting interest; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person, by means of an arrangement, a contract,
    an understanding or an agreement, determines the manner in which the
    interest is voted but the solicitation of proxies or the seeking of
    instructions with respect to the completion of proxies in respect of
    the exercise of voting interests is not considered to be such an
    arrangement, contract, understanding or agreement.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section, effective control
  of a licensee or its undertaking includes situations in which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person controls, directly or indirectly, other
    than by way of security only, a majority of the voting interests of
    the licensee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person has the ability to cause the licensee or
    its board of directors to undertake a course of action; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Commission, after a public hearing of an
    application for a licence, or in respect of an existing licence,
    determines that a person has such effective control and sets out
    that determination in a decision or public notice.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided pursuant to a condition
  of its licence, a licensee shall obtain the prior approval of the
  Commission in respect of any act, agreement or transaction that
  directly or indirectly would result in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change by whatever means of the effective control
    of its undertaking;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person alone

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 30 per cent of the voting
      interests of the licensee, having control of 30 per cent or more
      of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 30 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 30 per cent or more of
      those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who owns less than 50 per cent of the issued
      common shares of the licensee, owning 50 per cent or more of those
      shares but not having, directly or indirectly, effective control
      of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} who owns less than 50 per cent of the issued
      common shares of a person who has, directly or indirectly,
      effective control of the licensee, owning 50 per cent or more of
      those shares but not having, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person together with an associate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 30 per cent of the voting
      interests of the licensee, having control of 30 per cent or more
      of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 30 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 30 per cent or more of
      those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who own less than 50 per cent of the issued
      common shares of the licensee, owning 50 per cent or more of those
      shares but not having, directly or indirectly, effective control
      of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} who own less than 50 per cent of the issued
      common shares of a person who has, directly or indirectly,
      effective control of the licensee, owning 50 per cent or more of
      those shares but not having, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensee shall notify the Commission, within 30
  days thereafter, of the occurrence of any act, agreement or
  transaction that, directly or indirectly, resulted in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person alone

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 20 per cent of the voting
      interests of the licensee, having control of 20 per cent or more
      but less than 30 per cent of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 20 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 20 per cent or more but
      less than 30 per cent of those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 40 per cent of the
      voting interests of the licensee, having control of 40 per cent or
      more but less than 50 per cent of those interests but not having,
      directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} who controls less than 40 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 40 per cent or more but
      less than 50 per cent of those interests but not having, directly
      or indirectly, effective control of the licensee; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person together with an associate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 20 per cent of the voting
      interests of the licensee, having control of 20 per cent or more
      but less than 30 per cent of those interests,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 20 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 20 per cent or more but
      less than 30 per cent of those interests,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 40 per cent of the voting
      interests of the licensee, having control of 40 per cent or more
      but less than 50 per cent of those interests but not having,
      directly or indirectly, effective control of the licensee, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} who control less than 40 per cent of the voting
      interests of a person who has, directly or indirectly, effective
      control of the licensee, having control of 40 per cent or more but
      less than 50 per cent of those interests but not having, directly
      or indirectly, effective control of the licensee.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} A notification referred to in subsection (5) shall
  set out the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person or the names of the person
    and the associate;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of the voting interests controlled
    by the person or by the person and the associate; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy or a complete description of the act,
    agreement or transaction.

## [Emergency Alerts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-906438 .Part}

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this section.

  [[community station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[community station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a station that is
      licensed as a community station. ([station
      communautaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[issuing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[issuing authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who is
      authorized by a Canadian governmental authority --- including the
      federal Department of the Environment, federal and provincial
      government departments and agencies that are responsible for
      emergency management and public safety, and municipal authorities
      --- to issue warnings to the public, and to the National Alert
      Aggregation and Dissemination System, announcing danger to life or
      property. ([autorité compétente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[National Alert Aggregation and Dissemination System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[National Alert Aggregation and Dissemination
      System]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the alert message aggregation
      system established and operated by Pelmorex Communications Inc.
      ([système d'agrégation et de dissémination national
      d'alertes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[native station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[native station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a station that is
      licensed as a native station. ([station
      autochtone]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Except as otherwise provided under a condition of its
  licence, a licensee shall implement on all stations that it is
  licensed to operate, by no later than March 31, 2015, a public
  alerting system that broadcasts without delay, on a given station, any
  alert that it receives, in a form including both text and audio
  content, from the National Alert Aggregation and Dissemination System
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} announces an imminent or unfolding danger to life;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is designated by the applicable issuing authority
    for immediate broadcast in all or part of the area within the
    station's Grade B official contour or noise-limited bounding
    official contour, as the case may be.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (2) and subject to any condition
  of licence, the deadline for implementing the public alerting system
  in the case of a community station or native station is March 31,
  2016.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall implement the public alerting
  system for each of its transmitters.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall broadcast the alert on
  transmitters that serve the area that is targeted by the alert.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The licensee shall take all reasonable measures to
  ensure that the alerts that it broadcasts are in conformity with the
  specifications and recommended practices set out in the document
  entitled National Public Alerting System Common Look and Feel
  Guidance, produced at the request of the
  Federal/Provincial/Territorial Public Alerting Working Group of Senior
  Officials Responsible for Emergency Management with the support of
  Defence Research and Development Canada, Centre for Security Science,
  Canadian Safety and Security Program, and in consultation with the
  public-private Common Look and Feel Working Group, as that document is
  amended from time to time.
