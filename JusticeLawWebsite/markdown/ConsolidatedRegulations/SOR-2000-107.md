## Persistence and Bioaccumulation Regulations {#persistence-and-bioaccumulation-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2000-107
:::

[CANADIAN ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION ACT, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)

Registration 2000-03-23

Persistence and Bioaccumulation Regulations

P.C. 2000-348 2000-03-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 332(1) of the [Canadian Environmental
Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EFBCA}, the Minister
of the Environment published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, on December 11, 1999, a
copy of the proposed [Persistence and Bioaccumulation
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-107), substantially in the
annexed form, and persons were given an opportunity to file comments
with respect to the Regulations or to file a notice of objection
requesting that a board of review be established and stating the reasons
for the objection;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EFBCA){#footnotea_e}S.C. 1999, c.
  33
  :::

And whereas, pursuant to subsection 67(2) of that Act, the Minister of
the Environment and the Minister of Health are of the opinion that the
natural occurrence, properties and characteristics of minerals and
metals in the environment have been taken into consideration;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of the Environment and the Minister of
Health, pursuant to section 67 of the [Canadian Environmental Protection
Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31)^a^, hereby makes the annexed [Persistence
and Bioaccumulation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-107).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655337 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[bioaccumulation factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bioaccumulation factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ratio of
    the concentration of a substance in an organism to the concentration
    in water, based on uptake from the surrounding medium and food.
    ([facteur de bioaccumulation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bioconcentration factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bioconcentration factor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the ratio of
    the concentration of a substance in an organism to the concentration
    in water, based only on uptake from the surrounding medium.
    ([facteur de bioconcentration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[half-life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[half-life]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period it takes the
    concentration of a substance to be reduced by half, by
    transformation, in a medium. ([demi-vie]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[octanol-water partition coefficient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[octanol-water partition coefficient]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    the ratio of the concentration of a substance in an octanol phase to
    the concentration of the substance in the water phase of an
    octanol-water mixture. ([coefficient de partage
    octanol-eau]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655343 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
any substance, other than a living organism within the meaning of Part 6
of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31).

## [Persistence and Bioaccumulation Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655345 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A substance is persistent
when it has at least one of the following characteristics:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in air,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its half-life is equal to or greater than 2 days,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} it is subject to atmospheric transport from its
    source to a remote area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in water, its half-life is equal to or greater than
  182 days;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in sediments, its half-life is equal to or greater
  than 365 days; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in soil, its half-life is equal to or greater than
  182 days.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A substance is
bioaccumulative

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} when its bioaccumulation factor is equal to or
  greater than 5 000;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if its bioaccumulation factor cannot be determined in
  accordance with a method referred to in section 5, when its
  bioconcentration factor is equal to or greater than 5 000; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} if neither its bioaccumulation factor nor its
  bioconcentration factor can be determined in accordance with a method
  referred to in section 5, when the logarithm of its octanol-water
  partition coefficient is equal to or greater than 5.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The determination of
persistence and bioaccumulation with respect to a substance under
sections 3 and 4 must be made in accordance with generally recognized
methods of the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development
(OECD) or of some other similar organisation or, if no such methods
exist, in accordance with generally recognized methods within the
scientific community and taking into account the intrinsic properties of
the substance, the ecosystem under consideration and the conditions in
the environment.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-655358 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on March 31, 2000.
