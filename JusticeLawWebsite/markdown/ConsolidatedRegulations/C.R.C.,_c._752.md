## Computer Generated Mailing List Remission Order {#computer-generated-mailing-list-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 752
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty and Sales Tax on Computer
Generated Mailing Lists
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561520 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Computer Generated Mailing List Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._752).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561522 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the customs duty paid or payable under Schedule A to the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) on computer generated mailing lists imported
during the period commencing on February 14, 1972 and ending on June 30,
1985.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/80-158, s. 1
- SI/83-137, s. 1
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
of the sales tax paid or payable under the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) on the goods referred to in section 2 in the amount
equal to the difference between the sales tax calculated on the
duty-paid value of the goods and the sales tax calculated on the value
for duty of the goods.
