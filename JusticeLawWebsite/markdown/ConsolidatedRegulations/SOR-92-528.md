## Regulatory Capital (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#regulatory-capital-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-528
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-08-31

Regulations Defining the Regulatory Capital of an Association

P.C. 1992-1847  1992-08-27
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to the definition [[regulatory
capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 and section 463 of the
[Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations defining the regulatory capital of an
association](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-528).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946616 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Regulatory Capital (Cooperative Credit Associations)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-528).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946618 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Cooperative Credit
    Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by an insurance company or
      securities dealer; ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    that is incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or of a
    province; ([société d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minority interest]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an equity interest,
    in an entity that is controlled by an association, that is held by a
    person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the association, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by the association;
      ([participation minoritaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the Act.
    ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-422, s. 1
:::

## [Regulatory Capital]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946632 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 4, the regulatory capital of an
  association, at a particular time, is the aggregate of the amounts of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} members' equity and shareholders' equity,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} minority interests, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} subordinated indebtedness

  that would be reported in the financial statements of the association
  prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
  and specifications of the Superintendent referred to in subsection
  292(4) of the Act, less the amount of goodwill that would be included
  in those financial statements.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In calculating the amount of regulatory capital under
  subsection (1), an amount may be included in respect of a security
  only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the security is, by its terms, subordinate in right
    of payment to all deposit liabilities and all other liabilities of
    the entity that issued the security other than liabilities that, by
    their terms, rank equally with, or are subordinate to, that
    security;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the security is issued and fully paidup; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of subordinated indebtedness or a
    preferred share, the security

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has an initial minimum term of five years or more
      or has no term, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} cannot be redeemed or purchased for cancellation
      in the first five years after it is issued.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-63, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 401 and 402 of the Act, the regulatory capital of
  an association is the amount determined under section 3, less the
  aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of
    shareholders' equity and subordinated indebtedness of an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the association, or of an
    entity controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the association, that is included in the amount of
    regulatory capital of the association determined under section 3,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of
    investments, other than shareholders' equity or subordinated
    indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a), of the association or a
    designated entity controlled by the association in an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the association, or in an
    entity controlled by an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the association, if the investments are part of the
    capital of the insurance company or securities dealer,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of loans,
    other than subordinated indebtedness referred to in paragraph (a),
    made by the association or by a designated entity controlled by the
    association to an insurance company or securities dealer controlled
    by the association, or to an entity controlled by an insurance
    company or securities dealer controlled by the association, if the
    loans are part of the capital of the insurance company or securities
    dealer, and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all amounts each of which is the amount of a
    minority interest in an insurance company or securities dealer
    controlled by the association, or in an entity controlled by an
    insurance company or securities dealer controlled by the
    association, that is included in the amount of regulatory capital of
    the association determined under section 3.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraphs (1)(b) and (c),
  [[capital]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an insurance company to which the
    [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8) applies, regulatory
    capital within the meaning of the [Regulatory Capital (Insurance
    Companies) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-529); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any other insurance company or a
    securities dealer, an amount that would be treated as capital by a
    regulatory body that regulates the insurance company or securities
    dealer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-422, s. 2
