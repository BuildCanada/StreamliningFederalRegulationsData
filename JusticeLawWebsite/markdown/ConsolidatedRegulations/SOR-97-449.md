## Experts on Missions for the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons Privileges and Immunities in Canada Order {#experts-on-missions-for-the-organization-for-the-prohibition-of-chemical-weapons-privileges-and-immunities-in-canada-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-449
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1997-09-23

Experts on Missions for the Organization for the Prohibition of Chemical
Weapons Privileges and Immunities in Canada Order

P.C. 1997-1345  1997-09-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to paragraph 5(1)(h) of the
[Foreign Missions and International Organizations
Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR-97-449_e_hq_5757){#fn_SOR-97-449_e_hq_5757-ID0EDBAA},

the Prohibition of Chemical Weapons Privileges and Immunities in Canada
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-449).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009361 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Chemical Weapons Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chemical Weapons Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    Convention on the Prohibition of the Development, Production,
    Stockpiling and Use of Chemical Weapons and on Their Destruction,
    done at Paris on January 13, 1993. ([Convention sur les armes
    chimiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Organization for the
    Prohibition of Chemical Weapons established by Article VIII of the
    Chemical Weapons Convention. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009366 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Inspectors, inspection
assistants and observers, as experts performing missions for the
Organization, shall have in Canada, to the extent required for the
exercise of their functions in Canada, the privileges and immunities set
out in Article VI of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1009368 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on September 23, 1997.
