## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750534 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[architectural coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[architectural coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product to
      be applied onto or impregnated into a substrate, for use on
      traffic surfaces such as streets and highways, curbs, berms,
      driveways, parking lots, sidewalks and airport runways, or
      stationary structures, including temporary buildings and their
      appurtenances, whether installed or detached. ([revêtement
      architectural]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[excluded compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[excluded compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the compounds
      that are excluded under item 65 of Schedule 1 of the [Canadian
      Environmental Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31) and
      includes acetic acid, 1,1--dimethylethyl ester
      (C~6~H~12~0~2~). ([composés exclus]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[pigment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[pigment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means finely ground insoluble
      powder that provides an architectural coating with any of the
      following properties: colour, corrosion inhibition, conductivity,
      opacity, sheen, gloss or improved mechanical
      properties. ([pigment]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[volatile organic compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[volatile organic compounds]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or
      [[VOC]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means volatile organic compounds that
      participate in atmospheric photochemical reactions and that are
      not excluded compounds. ([composés organiques
      volatils]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"} ou [COV]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Incorporation by reference

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any standard or method that is incorporated by
  reference in these Regulations is incorporated as amended from time to
  time.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750546 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of the architectural coatings set out in
  the schedule, except if they are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for application to a product or a component of a
    product, in or on the premises of a factory or a shop, as part of a
    manufacturing, processing or repairing activity;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be used in scientific research;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported, offered for sale or sold to
    be used as a laboratory sample or analytical standard; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} manufactured, imported or sold for export or for
    shipment to other persons for processing or repackaging.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply in respect of the
  following coatings:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} adhesives;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} aerosol coatings --- pressurized coatings,
    containing pigments or resins, whose ingredients are dispensed by
    means of a propellant and are packaged in a disposable can for
    either

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} hand-held application, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} use in specialized equipment for the marking of
      streets, highways or other traffic surfaces including curbs,
      berms, driveways, parking lots, sidewalks and airport runways;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} antifouling coatings --- coatings for application
    to submerged stationary structures and their appurtenances, whether
    installed or detached, to prevent or reduce the attachment of marine
    or freshwater biological organisms, registered under the [Pest
    Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01); and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} wood preservatives --- coatings to protect exposed
    wood from decay or insect attack, registered under the [Pest Control
    Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application --- 1 L or less

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations, except for sections 17 and 19, do
  not apply in respect of the following architectural coatings set out
  in the schedule if their container has a capacity of one litre or
  less:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} faux finish;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other high-temperature coating;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other lacquer, including lacquer sanding
    sealers;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other varnish;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} low solids coating;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} quick-dry enamel;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} interior wiping stain;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} exterior wood stain;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other stain; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} rust preventive coating.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750574 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manufacture or import

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not manufacture or import any architectural coating set out in
  column 1 of the schedule if its concentration of volatile organic
  compounds exceeds the limit set out in column 2 for that architectural
  coating unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dilution of the architectural coating before its
    use is required and in accordance with the written instructions of
    the manufacturer, importer or seller, to a VOC concentration equal
    to or less than that limit set out in column 2 and that coating is
    either labelled with or accompanied by those instructions in both
    official languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has been issued a permit for that
    architectural coating under section 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition takes effect in respect of each
  architectural coating set out in column 1 of the schedule beginning on
  the corresponding anniversary of the day on which these Regulations
  come into force as set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Use of traffic marking coating

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not use, during the period beginning on May 1 and ending on
  October 15, any traffic marking coating set out in column 1 of the
  schedule in which the VOC concentration exceeds 150 g/L.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition takes effect in respect of the
  traffic marking coating beginning three years after the day on which
  these Regulations come into force.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale or offer for sale

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not sell or offer for sale any architectural coating set out in
  column 1 of the schedule if its VOC concentration exceeds the limit
  set out in column 2 for that product unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} dilution of the coating before its use is required
    and in accordance with the written instructions of the manufacturer,
    importer or seller, to a VOC concentration equal to or less than
    that limit set out in column 2 and that coating is either labelled
    with or accompanied by those instructions in both official
    languages; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the coating was manufactured or imported under a
    permit issued under section 10 and the sale or offer for sale occurs
    no later than two years after the day on which the permit expires.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Effective date

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prohibition takes effect in respect of each
  architectural coating set out in column 1 of the schedule beginning
  two years after the corresponding anniversary of the day on which
  these Regulations come into force as set out in column 3.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Dilution instructions

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty, the
instructions referred to in sections 3 and 5 cannot provide for any
dilution of the architectural coating before its use to a VOC
concentration greater than the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule
for that coating.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combination of multiple components

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  greater certainty, if the written instructions of the manufacturer,
  importer or seller require the combination of multiple components
  before an architectural coating is to be used, the VOC concentration
  in the architectural coating resulting from the combination of the
  multiple components must not exceed the VOC concentration limit set
  out in column 2 of the schedule for that architectural coating.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Combination instructions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If an architectural coating requires that components
  be combined, the manufacturer, importer or seller must set out on the
  architectural coating's label or in accompanying documentation the
  recommended combination instructions in both official languages.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Most restrictive VOC concentration limit

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  anywhere on the container of an architectural coating set out in the
  schedule, or in any documentation relating to the architectural
  coating supplied by the architectural coating's manufacturer,
  importer, seller or their duly authorized representative, it is
  indicated that the architectural coating may be used for the purpose
  of a different coating category set out in column 1 of the schedule,
  then the most restrictive VOC concentration limit applies.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the following
  architectural coatings set out in column 1 of the schedule:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} antenna coating;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} bituminous roof primer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} calcimine recoater;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} conjugated oil varnish;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} fire retardant coating;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} flow coating;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any other high-temperature coating;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} impacted immersion coating;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any other industrial maintenance coating;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} any other lacquer, including lacquer sanding
    sealers;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} low solids coating;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} metallic pigmented coating;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} nuclear coating;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} pre-treatment wash primer;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} shellac;

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} specialty primer, sealer and undercoater;

  - [(q)]{.lawlabel} temperature-indicator safety coating; and

  - [(r)]{.lawlabel} thermoplastic rubber coating and mastic.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750624 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-750625 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement for permit

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person that manufactures or imports an architectural coating set out
  in column 1 of the schedule, other than an architectural coating
  referred to in paragraph 3(1)(a), or the components of an
  architectural coating that must be combined together before their use
  in which the VOC concentration exceeds the limit set out in column 2
  of the schedule, must hold a permit issued under section 10.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} An application for a permit must be submitted to the
  Minister and contain the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} respecting the applicant,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} their name, civic and postal addresses, telephone
      number and, if any, fax number and e-mail address,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the name, title, civic and postal addresses,
      telephone number and, if any, fax number and e-mail address of
      their duly authorized representative, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} respecting the architectural coating,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its trademark and trade name, if any,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} its VOC concentration,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the estimated quantity to be manufactured,
      sold, offered for sale or imported in a calendar year and the unit
      of measurement,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} its category as set out in the schedule and the
      information on which the selection of the product category was
      made, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application for renewal of a
      permit under subsection 10(3), the number of the existing permit
      that was issued under section 10;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} evidence that it is not technically or economically
    feasible for the applicant at the time of the application to reduce
    the VOC concentration in the architectural coating to the limit set
    out in column 2 of the schedule for that coating;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a description of the plan prepared identifying the
    measures that will be taken so that the VOC concentration in the
    architectural coating to be manufactured or imported will be within
    the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that coating;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} identification of the period within which the plan
    is to be fully implemented; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the civic and postal addresses of the location
    where information, supporting documents and certification, as set
    out in subsection (3), are kept.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certification

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application must be accompanied by a
  certification dated and signed by the applicant or by their duly
  authorized representative stating that the information contained in
  the application is accurate and complete.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Format for submission

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The application and certification may be submitted
  either in writing or in an electronic format that is compatible with
  the format that is used by the Minister, and the documents must bear
  the written or electronic signature, as the case may be, of the
  applicant or their duly authorized representative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Additional information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may, on receiving an application made
  under this section, require further details that pertain to the
  information contained in the application and that are necessary for
  the application to be processed.

### [Conditions of Issuance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-750650 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for issuing permit

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister must issue the permit if the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has provided evidence that, at the
    time of the application, it is not technically or economically
    feasible for the applicant to reduce the VOC concentration in the
    product to the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that
    product;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the applicant has prepared a plan to identify the
    measures that will be taken by the applicant such that the VOC
    concentration in the architectural coating for manufacture or import
    is within the limit set out in column 2 of the schedule for that
    coating; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the period within which the plan is to be fully
    implemented does not exceed four years after the day on which the
    first permit is issued to the applicant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for refusing permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must refuse to issue a permit if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    the applicant has provided false or misleading information in
    support of their application; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} information required under subsections 9(2) or (5)
    has not been provided or is insufficient to enable the Minister to
    process the application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Expiry and permit renewal

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A permit expires 24 months after the day on which it
  is issued unless, within the period of 30 days before the day on which
  the permit expires, the applicant submits a new application in
  accordance with section 9. The validity of the first permit may only
  be extended once for an additional 24 months for a given architectural
  coating and the same use.

### [Revocation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-750665 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Grounds for revocation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister must revoke a permit if the Minister has reasonable grounds
  to believe that the implementation of the plan set out in paragraph
  10(1)(b) will not be completed within the specified period or that the
  permit holder has provided false or misleading information.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions for revocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must not revoke a permit unless the
  Minister has provided the permit holder with

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written reason for the revocation; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an opportunity to be heard, by written
    representation, in respect of the revocation.

## [Determination of VOC Concentration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750673 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}General formula

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the VOC
  concentration of an architectural coating set out in the schedule,
  diluted to the maximum recommendation of the manufacturer, importer or
  seller, excluding the volume of any water and excluded compounds, must
  be determined using the following equation:

  ::: {#750677 .Subsection}
  VOC concentration = (Ws - Ww - Wec) ÷ (Vm - Vw - Vec)

  where

  [VOC concentration]{.dfn}
  :   is the VOC concentration of an architectural coating, in grams of
      VOC per litre of coating;

  [Ws]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of volatiles, in grams;

  [Ww]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of water, in grams;

  [Wec]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of excluded compounds, in grams;

  [Vm]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of architectural coating, in litres;

  [Vw]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of water, in litres; and

  [Vec]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of excluded compounds, in litres.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Low solids coating

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The VOC concentration of a low solids coating set out
  in the schedule, diluted to the maximum recommendation of the
  manufacturer, importer or seller, including the volume of any water
  and excluded compounds, must be determined using the following
  equation:

  ::: {#750688 .Subsection}
  VOC~ls~ concentration = (Ws - Ww - Wec) ÷ Vm

  where

  [VOC~ls~ concentration]{.dfn}
  :   is the VOC concentration of a low solids (ls) coating, in grams of
      VOC per litre of coating,

  [Ws]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of volatiles, in grams,

  [Ww]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of water, in grams,

  [Wec]{.dfn}
  :   is the weight of excluded compounds, in grams, and

  [Vm]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of architectural coating, in litres.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Colourant

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The VOC concentration of an architectural coating
  must be determined excluding any colourant that is added after the
  tint base is manufactured or imported, as the case may be, and
  packaged for sale.

## [Test Methods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-750699 .Part}

### [Drying Times]{.HTitleText2} {#h-750700 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Quick-dry enamel

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  set-to-touch, tack-free and dry-hard times of a quick-dry enamel set
  out in the schedule must be determined in accordance with ASTM D
  1640--03, entitled Standard Test Methods for Drying, Curing, or Film
  Formation of Organic Coatings at Room Temperature, except that the
  standard must be read as excluding references to any agreement between
  the purchaser and the seller as well as the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} sections 2, 2.1, 4 and 4.1 with the exception of
    Table 1 of section 4.1;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sections 7.3, 7.3.1, 7.5, 7.5.1, 7.5.2, 7.7, 7.7.1,
    7.8 and 7.8.1;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sections 10, 10.1, except the expression "duplicate
    determinations should agree within 10% of the time of drying", and
    10.2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Excluded methods

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations, standard ASTM D
  1640--03 must be read as excluding the following referenced standards:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} ASTM D 823; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ASTM D 1005.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reference to standards

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any reference to a standard in this section is a
  reference to the version of that standard that is in effect at the
  time the determination under subsection (1) is made.

### [Surface Chalkiness]{.HTitleText2} {#h-750715 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Specialty primer, sealer or undercoater

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  chalkiness of a surface to be conditioned by the application of a
  specialty primer, sealer or undercoater set out in the schedule must
  be determined in accordance with ASTM D 4214--07, entitled Standard
  Test Methods for Evaluating the Degree of Chalking of Exterior Paint

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#interpretation .SchedHeadL1}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

  - **1** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in this
    schedule.

    [[high-temperature coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[high-temperature coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
        architectural coating for application to surfaces exposed
        continuously or intermittently to temperatures above
        204°C. ([revêtement haute température]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[industrial maintenance coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[industrial maintenance coating]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
        architectural coating for application to substrates exposed to
        any of the following conditions:

        - [(a)]{.lawlabel} immersion in water, wastewater or chemical
          solutions or chronic exposure of interior surfaces to moisture
          condensation;

        - [(b)]{.lawlabel} acute or chronic exposure to corrosive,
          caustic or acidic agents or to chemicals, chemical fumes or
          chemical mixtures or solutions;

        - [(c)]{.lawlabel} repeated exposure to temperatures above
          121°C;

        - [(d)]{.lawlabel} repeated, frequent, heavy abrasion, including
          mechanical wear and scrubbing with industrial solvents,
          cleansers or scouring agents; or

        - [(e)]{.lawlabel} exterior exposure of metal structures and
          structural components. ([revêtement d'entretien
          industriel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[primer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[primer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an architectural coating to
        be applied to a substrate to provide a firm bond between the
        substrate and architectural coatings subsequently
        applied. ([apprêt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[sanding sealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[sanding sealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a clear or
        semi-transparent architectural coating for application to bare
        wood to seal the wood and to provide a coat that can be sanded
        to create a smooth surface for architectural coatings
        subsequently applied. ([enduit à poncer]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[sealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[sealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an architectural coating to
        be applied to a substrate to prevent architectural coatings
        subsequently applied from being absorbed by the substrate or to
        prevent them from being harmed by materials in the
        substrate. ([produit de scellement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[shellac]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[shellac]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an architectural coating
        formulated solely with the resinous secretions of the lac beetle
        ([Laccifer lacca]{.otherLang lang="la"}), diluted with alcohol
        and formulated to dry by evaporation without a chemical
        reaction. ([gomme-laque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

    [[stain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[stain]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an architectural coating
        formulated to change the colour of a surface but not to conceal
        its grain pattern or texture. ([teinture]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[undercoater]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[undercoater]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an architectural
        coating used to provide a smooth surface for architectural
        coatings subsequently applied. ([sous-couche]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})

    [[varnish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    :   [[varnish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a clear or
        semi-transparent architectural coating, excluding lacquers,
        formulated to dry by chemical reaction. Varnishes may contain
        small amounts of pigment to colour a surface or to control the
        final sheen or gloss of the finish. ([vernis]{.DefinedTermLink
        lang="fr"})
