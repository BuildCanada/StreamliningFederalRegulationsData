## Quebec Family Allowances (1989) Remission Order {#quebec-family-allowances-1989-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/90-116
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1990-10-10

Order Respecting the Remission of Income Taxes Payable in Respect of
Quebec Family Allowances Received in 1989

P.C. 1990-2091  1990-09-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 23 of the [Financial Administration
Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed [Order respecting the
remission of income taxes payable in respect of Quebec family allowances
received in 1989](/eng/regulations/SI-90-116).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642438 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Quebec Family Allowances (1989) Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-90-116).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642440 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
(*Loi*)

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-642442 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby
granted, to every individual who received family allowances in 1989
under the Family Allowances Act of the Province of Quebec, of an amount
equal to the amount by which

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties payable by the
  individual under Parts I and I.1 of the Act for the 1989 taxation year

exceed

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the taxes, interest and penalties that would have
  been payable by the individual under Parts I and I.1 of the Act for
  the 1989 taxation year if no amount were included in computing the
  individual's income for that year in respect of family allowances
  received under the Family Allowances Act of the Province of Quebec.
