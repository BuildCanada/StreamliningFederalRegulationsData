## Allowance Exclusion Order {#allowance-exclusion-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-535
:::

[PUBLIC SECTOR COMPENSATION ACT](/eng/acts/P-31.6)

Registration 1992-08-31

Order Specifying the Circumstances in Respect of which any Allowance,
Bonus, Differential, Premium or Other Emolument or Benefit Paid to
Employees of the Public Sector Is Not Included for the Purposes of the
Definition "Wage Rate" in Subsection 2(1) of the Public Sector
Compensation Act

T.B. 818994 1992-08-24
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, pursuant to the definition [[wage
rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Sector
Compensation Act](/eng/acts/P-31.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotestar_e){#footnotestar_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby
makes the annexed Order specifying the circumstances in respect of which
any allowance, bonus, differential, premium or other emolument or
benefit paid to employees of the public sector is not included for the
purposes of the definition [[wage rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
subsection 2(1) of the [Public Sector Compensation
Act](/eng/acts/P-31.6).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnotestar_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnotestar_e}S.C.
  1991, c. 30
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946807 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Allowance Exclusion Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-535).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946809 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Sector Compensation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-31.6); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[allowance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any allowance, bonus,
    differential, premium or other emolument or benefit referred to in
    the definition [[wage rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1)
    of the Act; ([allocation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group of employees identified
    by a code and listed in Schedule II. ([groupe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-946814 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
definition [[wage rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the
Act, the allowances that are not included are the allowances set out in
column I of an item of Schedule I that are paid to an employee of a
group set out in column II of that item in the circumstances set out in
the provision referred to in column III of that item.
