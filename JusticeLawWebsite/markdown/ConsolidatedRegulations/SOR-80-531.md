## Manitoba Pullet Marketing Administration Levies Order {#manitoba-pullet-marketing-administration-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-531
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-07-10

Order Providing for the Fixing, Imposing and Collection of Levies Upon
Certain Pullet Producers
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Manitoba Egg Producers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 4 of
the [Manitoba Pullet Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-352) hereby makes
the annexed [Order providing for the fixing, imposing and collection of
levies upon certain pullet producers](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-531).

Dated at Winnipeg, Manitoba, this 3rd day of July, 1980
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869368 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Manitoba Pullet Marketing Administration Levies
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-531).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869370 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Natural Products Marketing Act
    of Manitoba; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Manitoba Egg
    Producers' Marketing Board established pursuant to the Act;
    ([Office]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/90-77, s.
    1\]]{.Repealed}

[[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pullet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the female of any class of
    domestic chicken belonging to the species [Gallus
    Domesticus]{.otherLang lang="la"} that is less than 20 weeks of age
    and produced for the purpose of laying eggs for human consumption;
    ([poulette]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who raises or
    keeps pullets in the Province of Manitoba.
    ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-77, s. 1
:::

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869379 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Every producer shall pay to
the Commodity Board a levy of one cent per pullet marketed by him in
interprovincial and export trade.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-366, s. 1
:::

## [Method of Payment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-869383 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A producer shall pay to the
Commodity Board all levies payable in respect of pullets marketed by
that producer in any week, not later than seven days after the last day
of the week, together with a report indicating the purchasers and
recipients of those pullets and the number of pullets each received.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/90-77, s. 2
:::
