## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725143 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [First Nations Fiscal
    Management Act](/eng/acts/F-11.67). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[defaulting member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[defaulting member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a borrowing member
    whose failure to make a payment payable under a borrowing agreement
    with the Authority has led to a reduction in the debt reserve
    fund. ([membre en défaut]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Replenishment of Fund]{.HTitleText1} {#h-725150 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Debt reserve fund balance

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraphs 84(5)(a) and (b) of the Act, the amount is equal to the
balance of the debt reserve fund immediately before the first of the
payments from that fund were made, not taking into account any payments
that previously led the Authority to require replenishment of the fund
under those paragraphs.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2024-255, s. 3](#1499441-1506296){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1499441-1506296.html"
  role="button"}
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} At least
  90 days before the day on which a payment is to be made to replenish
  the debt reserve fund, the Authority must send to the council of every
  borrowing member with an unpaid loan a notice setting out the amount
  of the shortfall in the debt reserve fund and the amount required to
  be paid by that borrowing member as determined under section 3.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Content of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must identify all defaulting members and
  the amount of the shortfall attributable to each of them.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount to pay

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The amount to be paid under
subsection 2(1) is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a defaulting member, an amount that is
  determined by the Authority that must not exceed the amount of the
  shortfall attributable to that defaulting member; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other borrowing member with an
  unpaid loan, the amount determined by the formula

  ::: {#1506304 .Paragraph}
  \[A ÷ (B -- C)\] × (D -- E)

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate amount of the borrowing member's unpaid loans,

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate amount of all borrowing members' unpaid loans,

  [C]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate amount of all defaulting members' unpaid loans,

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is the amount of the shortfall in the debt reserve fund, and

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is the aggregate of the amounts payable by the defaulting members
      under paragraph (a).
  :::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liability

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
despite the payment of an amount under paragraph 3(a), a defaulting
member remains liable for the payment to the Authority of all amounts
required to be paid under a borrowing agreement with the Authority.
