## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008921 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[aboriginal peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aboriginal peoples]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in section 3 of the [Employment Equity Act](/eng/acts/E-5.401).
    ([Autochtones]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[employment equity program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[employment equity program]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a program to
    enhance the recruitment of aboriginal peoples to positions in the
    Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development agreed to by
    the Public Service Commission on May 1, 1995 and March 5, 1997 at
    the request of the deputy head of that Department and implemented in
    accordance with section 5.1 of the [Public Service Employment
    Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01). ([programme d'équité en matière
    d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Exclusions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008925 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), appointments of aboriginal peoples to positions in
  the Department of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under the
  employment equity program are excluded from the operation of
  subsections 29(3), 30(1) and (2) and 39(3) and (4) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and from the operation of
  any regulations made under paragraph 35(2)(a) of that Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to aboriginal peoples
  with appointment priority entitlements under subsections 29(3), 30(1)
  and (2) and 39(3) and (4) of the [Public Service Employment
  Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) and regulations made under paragraph 35(2)(a)
  of that Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1008929 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 16, 1997.
