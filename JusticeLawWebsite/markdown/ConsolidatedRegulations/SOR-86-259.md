## Extra-billing and User Charges Information Regulations {#extra-billing-and-user-charges-information-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-259
:::

[CANADA HEALTH ACT](/eng/acts/C-6)

Registration 1986-02-27

Regulations Prescribing the Types of Information that the Minister of
National Health and Welfare may Require under Paragraph 13(a) of the
Canada Health Act in Respect of Extra-Billing and User Charges and the
Times at which and the Manner in which such Information shall be
Provided by the Government of each Province

P.C. 1986-499 1986-02-27
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of National Health and Welfare has consulted with
the Ministers responsible for health care in the provinces respecting
proposed Regulations prescribing the types of information that the
Minister may require under paragraph 13(a) of the Canada Health Act in
respect of extra-billing and user charges and the times at which and the
manner in which such information shall be provided by the government of
each province.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Health and Welfare, pursuant
to paragraph 22(1)(c) of the [Canada Health
Act](/eng/acts/C-6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing the types of
information that the Minister of National Health and Welfare may require
under paragraph 13(a) of the Canada Health Act in respect of
extra-billing and user charges and the times at which and the manner in
which such information shall be provided by the government of each
province, effective April 1, 1986.

## [Types of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892975 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 13(a) of the Act, the Minister may require the government of a
province to provide the Minister with information of the following types
with respect to extra-billing in the province in a fiscal year:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the aggregate amount that, at the time
  the estimate is made, is expected to be charged through extra-billing,
  including an explanation regarding the method of determination of the
  estimate; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial statement showing the aggregate amount
  actually charged through extra-billing, including an explanation
  regarding the method of determination of the aggregate amount.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 13(a) of the Act, the Minister may require the government of a
province to provide the Minister with information of the following types
with respect to user charges in the province in a fiscal year:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the aggregate amount that, at the time
  the estimate is made, is expected to be charged in respect of user
  charges to which section 19 of the Act applies, including an
  explanation regarding the method of determination of the estimate; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a financial statement showing the aggregate amount
  actually charged in respect of user charges to which section 19 of the
  Act applies, including an explanation regarding the method of
  determination of the aggregate amount.

## [Times and Manner of Filing Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-892982 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  government of a province shall provide the Minister with such
  information, of the types prescribed by sections 3 and 4, as the
  Minister may reasonably require, at the following times:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the estimates referred to in
    paragraphs 3(a) and 4(a), before April 1 of the fiscal year to which
    they relate; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the financial statements referred to
    in paragraphs 3(b) and 4(b), before the sixteenth day of the
    twenty-first month following the end of the fiscal year to which
    they relate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The government of a province may, at its discretion,
  provide the Minister with adjustments to the estimates referred to in
  paragraphs 3(a) and 4(a) before February 16 of the fiscal year to
  which they relate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in subsections (1)
  and (2) shall be transmitted to the Minister by the most practical
  means of communication.
