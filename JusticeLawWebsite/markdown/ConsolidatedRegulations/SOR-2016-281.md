## Critical Habitat of the Roseate Tern (Sterna dougallii) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-roseate-tern-sterna-dougallii-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2016-281
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2016-10-28

Critical Habitat of the Roseate Tern (Sterna dougallii) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Roseate Tern ([Sterna dougallii]{.otherLang lang="la"}) is a
wildlife species that is listed as an endangered species in Part 2 of
Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2){#fn_a2-ID0EBBHA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2-ID0EBBHA){#fn_a2}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas a portion of the critical habitat of that species is in a place
referred to in subsection 58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2){#fn_b2-ID0EBBFA} of that Act and, under
subsection 58(5) of that Act, that portion must be excluded from the
annexed Order;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b2-ID0EBBFA){#fn_b2}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

Whereas, pursuant to subsection 58(5) of that Act, the competent
minister must consult with every other competent minister and whereas
the Minister of the Environment is also the Minister responsible for the
Parks Canada Agency;

And whereas the Minister of the Environment is of the opinion that the
annexed Order would affect land that is under the authority of the
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and, pursuant to subsection 58(9) of
that Act, has consulted with that Minister with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a2){#fn_a2-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed
[Critical Habitat of the Roseate Tern (Sterna dougallii)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-281).

Gatineau, October 26, 2016

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834822 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the portion of the
critical habitat of the Roseate Tern ([Sterna dougallii]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) --- which critical habitat is identified in the recovery
strategy for that species that is included in the Species at Risk Public
Registry --- that is located within the following areas:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Country Island in Nova Scotia and an area extending
  200 m seaward from the mean high tide line;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area extending 200 m seaward from the mean high
  tide line of each of North Brother Island and South Brother Island in
  Nova Scotia; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any area that is within a 200 m perimeter of each
  polygon encompassing each individual nesting colony of any species of
  tern on Sable Island in Nova Scotia, excluding any area that is within
  the Sable Island National Park Reserve of Canada, as described in
  Schedule 2 of the [Canada National Parks Act](/eng/acts/N-14.01).

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-834827 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
