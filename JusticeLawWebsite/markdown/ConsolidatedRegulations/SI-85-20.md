## Computer Carrier Media Remission Order {#computer-carrier-media-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-20
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-02-20

Order Respecting the Remission of the Customs Duty and the Sales Tax on
Computer Carrier Media

P.C. 1985-277 1985-01-31
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of the customs duty and the sales tax on
computer carrier media](/eng/regulations/SI-85-20).

  [[Return to footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639844 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Computer Carrier Media Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-20).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639846 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods capable of storing
    instructions or data to be processed by data processing equipment;
    ([support de transmission]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[computer carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[computer carrier media]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means software, but
    does not include sound or image recordings, integrated circuits,
    semi-conductors and similar devices or articles incorporating such
    recordings, circuits, semi-conductors or devices; ([support de
    transmission de données]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[software]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means carrier media on which
    instructions or data to be used by data processing equipment have
    been stored. ([logiciel]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639851 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and the sales tax paid or
payable under the [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) in respect of
computer carrier media in an amount equal to the difference between

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of customs duties and sales tax paid or
  payable on computer carrier media; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of customs duties and sales tax paid or
  payable on the value of the carrier media excluding the value of its
  instructions or data content but including the cost of reproducing the
  instructions or data on it.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639857 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 3
is granted on the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the computer carrier media are imported on or after
  January 1, 1985;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years of the date of importation of the
  computer carrier media for which remission is claimed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person claiming remission pursuant to this Order
  shall provide such reports and information as the Minister of National
  Revenue may require for the administration of this Order.
