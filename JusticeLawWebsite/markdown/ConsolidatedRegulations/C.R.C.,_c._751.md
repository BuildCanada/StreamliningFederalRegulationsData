## Commercial Samples Remission Order {#commercial-samples-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 751
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Order Respecting the Remission of Taxes Imposed under Division III Of
Part IX and under any Other Part of the Excise Tax Act and Customs
Duties Imposed under Section 21 of the Customs Tariff, Paid or Payable
on Commercial Samples Temporarily Imported for Exhibition or
Demonstration
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561459 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Commercial Samples Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._751).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561461 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[carnet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[carnet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an A.T.A. (Admission Temporaire
    --- Temporary Admission) Carnet referred to in the Customs
    Convention on the A.T.A. Carnet for the Temporary Admission of
    Goods; ([carnet]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[chief officer of customs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with respect to an
    area or place, means the manager of the customs office or customs
    offices that serve that area or place; ([agent en chef des
    douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[collector]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SI/88-18, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

[[commercial sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[commercial sample]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any goods that are representative of a particular
      category of goods produced outside Canada and that are imported
      solely for the purpose of being exhibited or demonstrated to
      solicit orders for similar goods to be supplied from outside
      Canada, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any films, charts, projectors and scale models,
      and similar items, imported solely for the purpose of illustrating
      a particular category of goods produced outside Canada to solicit
      orders for similar goods to be supplied from outside Canada;
      ([échantillon commercial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[foreign supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[foreign supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a non-resident of
    Canada. ([fournisseur étranger]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561472 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 and
5, remission is hereby granted of all taxes imposed under Division III
of Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and customs duties imposed under section 21 of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), paid or payable on any commercial
sample imported into Canada on or after January 1, 1991 for a temporary
period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/98-10, s. 2
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  remission mentioned in section 3 shall be granted only if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a non-resident of Canada, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada who is an employee or agent
      of a foreign supplier, acts on behalf of the foreign supplier and
      negotiates sales contracts only in the name of the foreign
      supplier;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the commercial sample, while in Canada, will remain
    in the ownership of a non-resident of Canada;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a commercial sample whose value
    exceeds \$1,000, the importer

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} indicates, at the time of importation, the places
      in Canada where he intends to exhibit or demonstrate the sample
      and, on demand, satisfies the Minister of National Revenue that
      the sample is at the places indicated, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} maintains records of the commercial sample
      pursuant to section 40 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) and
      regulations made thereunder, while it is in Canada and produces
      the records for inspection on request by an officer employed in
      the administration of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6) or the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the commercial sample will not be exhibited or
    demonstrated in Canada by anyone other than the importer;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} goods ordered as a result of the exhibition or
    demonstration of the commercial sample will not be supplied from
    within Canada; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), the commercial sample
    will be exported from Canada within one year of the date of
    importation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Deputy Minister of National Revenue may extend
  the one-year period mentioned in paragraph (1)(f) by an additional six
  months in any case where, in his opinion, it is impracticable or
  impossible for the importer to comply with that one-year period.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/98-10, s. 3
:::

## [Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561491 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  the commercial sample being imported into Canada for a temporary
  period is not accompanied by a valid carnet, the chief officer of
  customs may require the importer to give security to ensure compliance
  with the conditions set out in paragraphs 4(1)(a) to (f), in an amount
  not exceeding the aggregate of the taxes imposed under Division III of
  Part IX and under any other Part of the [Excise Tax
  Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and the customs duties imposed under section 21
  of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), that would be payable if
  this Order did not apply.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Security given under subsection (1) shall be
  deposited with the chief officer of customs and shall be in the form
  of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cash;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a certified cheque;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a transferable bond issued by the Government of
    Canada; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a bond issued by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is licensed or otherwise
      authorized under the laws of Canada or of a province to carry on
      the fidelity or surety class of insurance business and that is
      recommended to the Treasury Board by the Office of the
      Superintendent of Financial Institutions as an entity whose bonds
      may be accepted by the Government of Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a member of the Canadian Payments Association
      referred to in section 4 of the Canadian Payments Association Act,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits insured by
      the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation or the Régie de
      l'assurance-dépôts du Québec to the maximums permitted by the
      statutes under which those institutions are established,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a credit union, as defined in subsection 137(6)
      of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3), or

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that accepts deposits from the
      public, if repayment of the deposits is guaranteed by Her Majesty
      in right of a province.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Security given pursuant to this section shall be
  refunded or cancelled when the commercial sample in respect of which
  it was given has been

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} released and accounted for under the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6),

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} destroyed, with such destruction certified by a
    customs officer, police officer or fire marshall, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} exported, with such exportation certified by a
    customs officer on a form entitled "Temporary Admission Permit"
    obtained from the Department of National Revenue,

  within the time period allowed in respect of the article under section
  4.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/78-127, s. 1
- SI/86-162, s. 1
- SI/87-187, s. 1(E)
- SI/88-18, s. 2
- SI/91-8, s. 2
- SI/96-44, s. 1
- SI/98-10, s. 4
:::

## [Non-Compliance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-561511 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where there is
non-compliance with any of the conditions set out in paragraphs 4(1)(a)
to (f), the commercial sample in respect of which the violation occurs
shall forthwith be entered for consumption.
