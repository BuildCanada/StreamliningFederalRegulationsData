## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697697 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in the Act and these Regulations.

  [[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[courier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial carrier that is
      engaged in scheduled international transportation of shipments of
      goods other than goods imported or exported as mail.
      ([messager]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[monetary instruments]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[monetary instruments]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the following
      instruments in bearer form or in such other form as title to them
      passes on delivery, namely,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} securities, including stocks, bonds, debentures
        and treasury bills; and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} negotiable instruments, including bank drafts,
        cheques, promissory notes, travellers' cheques and money orders,
        other than warehouse receipts or bills of lading.

      For greater certainty, this definition does not apply to
      securities or negotiable instruments that bear restrictive
      endorsements or a stamp for the purposes of
      clearing. ([effets]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
      Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501).
      ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cargo ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cargo ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial vessel that
      is engaged in international transportation of shipments of goods
      other than goods imported or exported as mail. ([navire de
      charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
      conveyance that is used to carry passengers who have paid for
      passage. ([moyen de transport commercial de
      passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any vehicle, aircraft or
      water-borne craft, or other contrivance that is used to move
      persons, goods, currency or monetary instruments. ([moyen de
      transport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[cruise ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commercial vessel that
      has sleeping facilities for over 70 persons who are not crew
      members but does not include a vessel engaged in passenger or
      cargo ferry service. ([navire de croisière]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[currency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[currency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means coins referred to in
      section 7 of the [Currency Act](/eng/acts/C-52), notes issued by
      the Bank of Canada under the [Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2)
      that are intended for circulation in Canada or coins or bank notes
      of countries other than Canada. ([espèces]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[emergency]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a medical emergency, fire,
      flood or other disaster that threatens life, property or the
      environment. ([urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[non-commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[non-commercial passenger conveyance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
      a conveyance that does not have aboard any person who has paid for
      passage and includes corporate aircraft, private aircraft and
      marine pleasure craft. ([moyen de transport non commercial de
      passagers]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[transfer agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[transfer agent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person or entity
      appointed by a corporation to maintain records of stock, debenture
      and bond owners, to cancel and issue certificates and to send out
      dividend cheques. ([agent de transfert]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Reporting of Importations and Exportations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697716 .Part}

### [Minimum Value of Currency or Monetary Instruments]{.HTitleText2} {#h-697717 .Subheading}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of subsection 12(1) of the Act, the prescribed amount is
  \$10,000.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount is in Canadian dollars, or in its
  equivalent in a foreign currency using

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the exchange rate that is published by the Bank of
    Canada for that foreign currency and that is in effect at the time
    of the importation or exportation; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if no exchange rate is published by the Bank of
    Canada for that foreign currency, the exchange rate that the person
    or entity would use in the ordinary course of business at the time
    of the importation or exportation.

### [Importation Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-697735 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (5) and section 9, a report with respect to
  currency or monetary instruments transported by a person arriving in
  Canada shall be submitted without delay by the person at the customs
  office located at the place of importation or, if it is not open for
  business at the time of importation, at the nearest customs office
  that is open for business at that time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A report with respect to currency or monetary
  instruments transported by a person arriving in Canada on board a
  commercial passenger conveyance who has as their destination another
  place in Canada at which there is a customs office may be submitted
  without delay by the person at that customs office or, if it is not
  open for business at the time of importation, at the nearest customs
  office that is open for business at that time, on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person does not disembark from the conveyance
    at the place of arrival in Canada and the currency or monetary
    instruments are not removed from the conveyance at that place, other
    than to be transferred under customs control directly to a
    commercial passenger conveyance for departure to the other place in
    Canada or directly to a holding area designated as such for the
    purposes of the Presentation of Persons (Customs) Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person and currency or monetary instruments
    are transferred under customs control directly to a designated
    holding area, the person does not leave and the currency or monetary
    instruments are not removed from that area, other than to board or
    to be loaded on board a commercial passenger conveyance for
    departure to the other place in Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A report with respect to currency or monetary
  instruments transported by a person arriving in Canada on board a
  non-commercial passenger conveyance at a customs office where, under
  the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), customs reporting may be done by
  radio or telephone may be submitted by radio or telephone to an
  officer by that person or the person in charge of the conveyance at
  that location, on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the person informs the officer of their
    arrival for the purposes of section 11 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), they provide the information referred to in
    Schedule 1, 2 or 3, as applicable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the officer's request, they present themselves
    and make available for examination the currency or monetary
    instruments at the time and place specified by the officer.

- [(3.1)]{.lawlabel} A report with respect to currency or monetary
  instruments transported by a person arriving in Canada on board a
  non-commercial passenger conveyance, at a customs office where the
  person is authorized in accordance with the [Presentation of
  Persons (2003) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323) to present
  in an alternative manner, may be submitted to an officer by telephone,
  by that person or the person in charge of the conveyance before
  arriving in Canada, on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} when the person informs the officer of their
    arrival for the purposes of section 11 of the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), they provide the information referred to in
    Schedule 1, 2 or 3, as applicable; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the officer's request, they present themselves
    and make available for examination the currency or monetary
    instruments on arrival in Canada at the time and place specified by
    the officer.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A report with respect to currency or monetary
  instruments transported by a freight train crew member arriving in
  Canada on board the freight train shall be submitted without delay by
  the crew member at the customs office specified by the officer when
  the crew member presents himself or herself in accordance with
  section 11 of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A report with respect to currency or monetary
  instruments that are transported by courier into Canada on board an
  aircraft and that have as their destination another place in Canada at
  which there is a customs office, shall be submitted at the customs
  office located at the airport of destination shown on the air waybill,
  on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the currency or monetary instruments are not
    removed from the aircraft at the place of arrival, other than to be
    transferred under customs control directly to a holding area
    designated as such for the purposes of the Presentation of Persons
    (Customs) Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the currency or monetary instruments are
    transferred under customs control directly to a designated holding
    area, they are not removed from that area, other than to be loaded
    on board an aircraft for departure to the other place in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-412, s. 20
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 10, a
report made by an exporter with respect to the importation of currency
or monetary instruments by mail shall be made by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} including inside the mail item an importation report
  with respect to the currency or monetary instruments; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} affixing the customs declaration form required by the
  Universal Postal Convention, as amended from time to time, to the
  outside of the mail item and indicating that it contains currency or
  monetary instruments.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A report made with respect
to the importation of currency or monetary instruments that have been
retained under section 14 of the Act shall be submitted by the person or
entity to whom the notice was given at the customs office indicated on
the notice.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A report with respect to
the importation of currency or monetary instruments, other than one
referred to in sections 4 to 6, shall be submitted without delay at the
customs office that is open for business at the time of the importation
and that is nearest to the place of importation.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In an emergency, the person
in charge of a conveyance who must unload currency or monetary
instruments from the conveyance before being able to make or submit an
importation report in accordance with these Regulations may submit the
importation report by telephone or other expedient means and, as soon as
possible after that, shall make or submit a report in accordance with
these Regulations.

### [Exceptions to Importation Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-697759 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), currency or monetary instruments
  transported by a person arriving in Canada on board a commercial
  passenger conveyance who has as their destination a place outside
  Canada are not required to be reported under subsection 12(1) of the
  Act, on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person does not disembark from the conveyance
    in Canada and the currency or monetary instruments are not removed
    from the conveyance in Canada other than to be transferred under
    customs control directly to a commercial passenger conveyance for
    departure to the place outside Canada or directly to a holding area
    designated as such for the purposes of the Presentation of Persons
    (Customs) Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person and currency or monetary instruments
    are transferred under customs control directly to a designated
    holding area, the person does not leave and the currency or monetary
    instruments are not removed from that area other than to board or be
    loaded on board a commercial passenger conveyance for departure to
    the place outside Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), currency or monetary
  instruments that are transported by courier into Canada on board a
  conveyance and that have as their destination a place outside Canada
  are not required to be reported under subsection 12(1) of the Act, on
  condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the currency or monetary instruments are not
    removed from the conveyance at the place of arrival, other than to
    be transferred under customs control directly to a holding area
    designated as such for the purposes of the Presentation of Persons
    (Customs) Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the currency or monetary instruments are
    transferred under customs control directly to a designated holding
    area, they are not removed from that area, other than to be loaded
    on board a conveyance for departure to the place outside of Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Currency or monetary instruments that are transported
  into Canada on board a cruise ship or cargo ship and that have as
  their destination a place outside Canada are not required to be
  reported under subsection 12(1) of the Act, on condition that the
  currency or monetary instruments are not removed from the cruise ship
  or cargo ship while it is in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-358, s. 26
:::

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person or entity is not
required to make a report under subsection 12(1) of the Act with respect
to the importation of currency or monetary instruments that are mailed
from a location outside Canada to a destination outside Canada but that
transit through Canada in the course of post, on condition that they
will not leave the course of post until after they have left Canada.

### [Exportation Reporting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-697771 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A report with respect to
currency or monetary instruments transported by a person departing from
Canada shall be submitted without delay by the person at the customs
office located at the place of exportation or, if it is not open for
business at the time of exportation, at the nearest customs office that
is open for business at that time.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A report required to be
made by an exporter with respect to the exportation by mail of currency
or monetary instruments shall be made by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} including an exportation report inside the mail item;
  and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} mailing or submitting, at or before the time when the
  currency or monetary instruments are mailed, a copy of the exportation
  report to the customs office that is located nearest to the point at
  which the item was mailed.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A report made with
respect to the exportation of currency or monetary instruments that have
been retained under section 14 of the Act shall be submitted by the
person or entity to whom the notice was given at the customs office
indicated on the notice.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A report with respect to
the exportation of currency or monetary instruments, other than one
referred to in sections 11 to 13, shall be submitted without delay at
the customs office that is open for business at the time of exportation
and that is nearest to the place of exportation.

## [Exception Applicable to the Bank of Canada]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697778 .Part}

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** A person or entity is not
required to make a report under subsection 12(1) of the Act with respect
to the importation or exportation of currency by or on behalf of the
Bank of Canada for the purposes of the distribution, processing, or
testing of banknotes intended for circulation in Canada.

## [Exemption Applicable to Imported Shares]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697780 .Part}

**[[15.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15.1 .sectionLabel}** A person or entity is
not required to make a report under subsection 12(1) of the Act with
respect to stocks, bonds and debentures imported into Canada by courier
or as mail if the importer is a financial entity or a securities dealer
as defined in subsection 1(2) of the [Proceeds of Crime (Money
Laundering) and Terrorist Financing
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-184) or a transfer agent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-358, s. 27
:::

## [Retention]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697784 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 14(1) of the Act, an officer shall give the
  person or entity written notice in person or, if the person is not
  present, shall send the notice by registered mail to the person's
  latest known address.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 14(2) of the Act, the
  notice is to be given within 60 days after the day on which the
  currency or monetary instruments are imported or exported, as the case
  may be.

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed retention
period, for the purposes of subsection 14(1) of Act, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of importation or exportation by courier
  or as mail, 30 days after the day on which the retention notice is
  given or sent; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, seven days after the day on which
  the retention notice is given or sent.

## [Penalties]{.HTitleText1} {#h-697791 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 18(2) of the Act, the prescribed amount of the penalty is
equal to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 5% of the value of the seized currency or monetary
  instruments, up to a maximum of \$2,500, in the case of a person or
  entity who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has not concealed the currency or monetary
    instruments,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has made a full disclosure of the facts concerning
    the currency or monetary instruments on their discovery, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} has no previous seizures under the Act;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 25% of the value of the seized currency or monetary
  instruments, in the case of a person or entity who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has concealed the currency or monetary instruments,
    other than by means of using a false compartment in a conveyance, or
    who has made a false statement with respect to the currency or
    monetary instruments, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a previous seizure under the Act, other than
    in respect of any type of concealment or for making false statements
    with respect to the currency or monetary instruments; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} 50% of the value of the seized currency or monetary
  instruments, in the case of a person or entity who

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} has concealed the currency or monetary instruments
    by using a false compartment in a conveyance, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has a previous seizure under the Act for any type
    of concealment or for making a false statement with respect to the
    currency or monetary instruments.
