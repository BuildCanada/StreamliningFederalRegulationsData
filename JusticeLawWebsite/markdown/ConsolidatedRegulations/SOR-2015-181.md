## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820321 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

    Act](/eng/acts/S-6.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

:   [[boarding pass]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes a ticket or other

    passenger on a flight. ([carte d'embarquement]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820326 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Sections 2.1 to 11 apply in
respect of the following passenger-carrying flights --- or in respect of
air carriers conducting such flights --- if the passengers, the property
in the possession or control of the passengers and the personal
belongings or baggage that the passengers give to the air carrier for
transport are subject to screening that is carried out --- in Canada
under the [Aeronautics Act](/eng/acts/A-2) or in another country by the
person or entity responsible for the screening of such persons, property
and belongings or baggage --- before boarding:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} domestic flights that depart from Canadian aerodromes
  and that are conducted by air carriers under Subpart 5 of Part VII of
  the [Canadian Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} international flights that depart from or will arrive
  at Canadian aerodromes and that are conducted by air carriers

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under Subpart 1 of Part VII of the [Canadian
    Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433) using aircraft
    that have a maximum certificated take-off weight of more than
    8 618 kg (19,000 pounds) or have a seating configuration, excluding
    crew seats, of 20 or more, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under Subpart 5 of Part VII of the [Canadian
    Aviation Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-433).

## [Information Collected by Air Carrier]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1284908 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of information

- **[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  air carrier must, no later than 72 hours before the scheduled time of
  departure of a domestic flight or international flight, collect the
  information set out in paragraphs 6(2)(a) to (c) of the Act about each
  person who is expected to be on board the aircraft for the flight.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Canadian Travel Number

  [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An air carrier must provide to each person referred
  to in subsection (1) an opportunity to provide their Canadian Travel
  Number at the time of reservation and at check-in.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Collection of Canadian Travel Number

  [(1.2)]{.lawlabel} If a Canadian Travel Number is provided in
  accordance with subsection (1.1), the air carrier must collect it as
  soon as it is provided to them.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to retain information

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The air carrier must retain the information for as
  long as is necessary to comply with its obligations under subsection
  6(2) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed flight

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed information

**[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 6(2)(d) of the Act, the prescribed information about each
person referred to in subsection 6(2) of the Act is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} their citizenship or nationality;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the number and expiry date of their passport and the
  name of the country or entity that issued it or the number and expiry
  date of their permanent resident card issued by Canada or the United
  States;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} their Canadian Travel Number, if they have one;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} their reservation record locator number;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the unique passenger reference that is assigned to
  them by the air carrier;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the flight code that identifies the air carrier and
  the flight number;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} if the person is expected to be on board the
  aircraft, the scheduled date, time and place of departure of the
  aircraft;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} if the person is on board the aircraft, the date,
  time and place of departure of the aircraft; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the scheduled date, time and place of arrival of the
  aircraft.

  (f), (h) and (i) of these Regulations must be provided about each

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} An air carrier must, no later than 30 minutes after
  the cancellation of a flight for which information has been provided
  to the Minister under subsection 6(2) of the Act, inform the Minister
  of the cancellation.

## [Listed Persons]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1284961 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Listed person

- **[[2.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} This
  section applies if, before a person has obtained a boarding pass for a
  domestic flight or international flight, the Minister informs the air
  carrier that information referred to in paragraphs 6(2)(a) to (c) of
  the Act or paragraphs 2.3(a) to (c) of these Regulations that the air
  carrier has provided about the person under subsection 6(2) of the Act
  is the same as the information of a listed person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Self-service machine or Internet

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The air carrier must not allow the person to obtain a
  boarding pass for the flight in question at a self-service machine or
  from the Internet.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Verification of identity

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the person presents themselves at the air
  carrier's check-in desk to obtain a boarding pass for the flight in
  question, the air carrier must, before providing information about the
  person to the Minister in accordance with paragraph 2.4(1)(c), verify
  the person's identity using a piece of identification that is
  acceptable under subsection 3(1) or section 4, as the case may be, and
  must compare

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the information on the person's identification with
    the information in their reservation record; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the person presents a piece of photo
    identification, the person's entire face with the face displayed in
    the photograph.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information discrepancy

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the comparison reveals a discrepancy between the
  information on the person's identification and the information in
  their reservation record, the air carrier must not issue a boarding
  pass to the person pending a response from the Minister with respect
  to the information that has been provided in accordance with paragraph
  2.4(1)(a) or (c), as the case may be.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to refuse boarding pass

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The air carrier must not, in either of the following
  circumstances, issue a boarding pass to the person unless the air
  carrier has contacted the Minister of Transport by telephone and been
  informed that a direction will not be made under subsection 9(1) of
  the Act --- other than under paragraph 9(1)(b) --- in respect of the
  person:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the comparison performed under paragraph (3)(a)
    does not reveal a discrepancy between the information on the
    person's identification and the information in their reservation
    record,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the comparison performed under paragraph (3)(a)
    reveals a discrepancy between the information on the person's
    identification and the information in their reservation record and
    the Minister, in response to the information provided by the air
    carrier regarding the discrepancy, informs the air carrier that
    information referred to in paragraphs 6(2)(a) to (c) of the Act or
    paragraphs 2.3(a) to (c) of these Regulations about the person is
    still the same as that of a listed person.

## [Verification of Identity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820333 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Boarding gate --- domestic flight

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), an air carrier must, at the boarding gate for a
  domestic flight, verify the identity of each passenger who appears to
  be 18 years of age or older using

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one piece of valid photo identification issued by a
    government authority in Canada that shows the passenger's surname,
    first name and any middle names and their date of birth, except any
    document, however described and regardless of format, issued by a
    government authority for the purposes of fishing, hunting or
    boating;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} one of the following pieces of photo identification
    issued by a government authority that shows the passenger's surname,
    first name and any middle names and their date of birth and that is
    valid:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a passport issued by the country of which the
      passenger is a citizen or a national,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a NEXUS card,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a permanent resident card issued by the United
      States,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} an enhanced driver's license issued in the
      United States,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} any document referred to in subsection 50(1) or
      52(1) of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} two pieces of valid identification that are issued
    by a government authority in Canada --- at least one of which shows
    the passenger's surname, first name and any middle names and their
    date of birth --- except any document, however described and
    regardless of format, issued by a government authority for the
    purposes of fishing, hunting or boating; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a valid [[restricted area identity
    card]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in section 3 of the
    [Canadian Aviation Security Regulations,
    2012](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-318).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Alternative identification --- loss or
  theft

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event that a passenger's identity cannot be
  verified in accordance with subsection (1) because of the loss or
  theft of one or more pieces of their identification, the air carrier
  may verify their identity using other pieces of valid identification
  such as their employee identity card, public transit pass or baptismal
  certificate if the passenger presents the identification in
  conjunction with documentation issued by a government authority or a
  police service that attests to the loss or theft.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibition --- transporting passenger

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} It is
  prohibited for an air carrier to transport a passenger if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the passenger presents a piece of photo
    identification and does not resemble the photograph; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-325, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2019-325, s. 9\]]{.Repealed}

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the passenger presents more than one form of
    identification and there is a significant discrepancy between those
    forms of identification.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), an air carrier may
  transport a passenger who presents a piece of photo identification but
  does not resemble the photograph if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the passenger's appearance changed for medical
    reasons after the photograph was taken and the passenger presents
    the air carrier with a document signed by a health care professional
    that attests to that fact; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the passenger's face is bandaged for medical
    reasons and the passenger presents the air carrier with a document
    signed by a health care professional that attests to that fact.

## [Interruption of the Electronic Communications System]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1284998 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to inform

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** An air carrier and the
Minister must inform each other of any interruption of their respective
electronic communications systems referred to in subsection 2.5(1)

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a planned system interruption, as soon
  as feasible in advance of the scheduled date of the interruption and
  in writing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an unplanned system interruption,
  immediately after they become aware of the interruption.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic system interruption ---
check-in

**[[10.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.1 .sectionLabel}** In the event of an
interruption of an air carrier's or the Minister's electronic
communications system, the air carrier must, at the time of check-in for
a flight, refer to the most recent information received from the
Minister about any person who is expected to be on board the aircraft
before issuing a boarding pass to that person.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Duty to provide information

**[[10.11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.11 .sectionLabel}** In the
circumstances referred to in section 10.1, the air carrier must, at the
time of check-in of each person who is expected to be on board the
aircraft, immediately provide to the Minister of Transport, about the
person, the information referred to in paragraphs 6(2)(a) to (c) of the
Act --- and the information referred to in paragraphs 2.3(a) to (g) and
(i) of these Regulations that is in the air carrier's control --- if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no information has been received from the Minister
  about the person;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the information received from the Minister fails to
  indicate that the information referred to in paragraphs 6(2)(a) to (c)
  of the Act or paragraphs 2.3(a) to (c) of these Regulations that has
  been provided about the person is not the same as that of a person
  whose name is on the list established under subsection 8(1) of the
  Act;

- [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian Travel Number is provided;

- [(b.2)]{.lawlabel} in the case where a Canadian Travel Number has been
  provided, there has been a change to it since it was provided to the
  Minister; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any of the information about the person that is
  provided under paragraphs 6(2)(a) and (b) of the Act has significantly
  changed since it was provided to the Minister.

## [Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-820386 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information --- removal and destruction

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Every air carrier must,
by March 4, 2023,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} permanently remove all versions of the list and any
  information respecting listed persons from

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any system it used to comply with these Regulations
    as they read immediately before the day on which this section comes
    into force, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any electronic communications system it uses to
    comply with these Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} permanently destroy any document or record in its
  control containing information respecting a listed person.
