## Reporting of Imported Goods Regulations {#reporting-of-imported-goods-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/86-873
:::

[CUSTOMS ACT](/eng/acts/C-52.6)

Registration 1986-08-14

Regulations Respecting the Reporting of Imported Goods

P.C. 1986-1842 1986-08-13
::::

:::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 164(3) of the [Customs
Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), a copy of proposed Regulations, substantially in
the form annexed hereto, was published in Part I of the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) on March 29, 1986 and a reasonable
opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to make
representations with respect thereto to the Minister of National
Revenue.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to
section 12, subsections 14(2) and 18(2) and paragraph 164(1)(i) of the
[Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the reporting of imported
goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-873), effective on the coming into force
of section 12, subsections 14(2) and 18(2) and paragraph 164(1)(i) of
the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6).

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a shipment for which a carrier is responsible
  consists of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a specified good or collection of specified goods
    that is listed in a single bill of lading, waybill or similar
    document that is issued by the carrier and that relates to the
    carriage of those goods by the carrier, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a specified good that is an empty cargo container
    that is not for sale and that is transported by the carrier but is
    not listed in a bill of lading, waybill or similar document; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a shipment for which a freight forwarder is
  responsible consists of a specified good or collection of specified
  goods that is listed in a single bill of lading, waybill or similar
  document that is issued by the freight forwarder and that relates to
  the carriage of those goods.

## [Time for Reporting Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898456 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Except as otherwise
provided in these Regulations, all goods that are imported shall be
reported under section 12 of the Act without delay after arrival in
Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-156, s. 2(F)
- SOR/2015-90, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink

**[[3.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 8
and 9, specified goods that are imported by water shall be reported
under section 12 of the Act without delay after the vessel that is
transporting them lands at a customs office following arrival in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 2
:::

**[[3.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.2 .sectionLabel}** Specified goods that
are imported by air shall be reported under section 12 of the Act
without delay after the aircraft that is transporting them is cleared by
NAV CANADA to land at an airport following arrival in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 2
:::

## [Manner of Reporting Goods]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898466 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Unless a person is required
to report goods in writing under section 5 or is permitted to report
them orally under that section or in writing under section 12, they
shall report the goods to the Agency by electronic means in accordance
with the technical requirements, specifications and procedures that are
set out in the Electronic Commerce Client Requirements Document.

## [Exceptions to Reporting at the Nearest Customs Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898490 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Goods in
  the actual possession of a person arriving in Canada aboard a
  commercial passenger conveyance who has as his destination a place
  outside Canada, and any goods being carried on board the same
  conveyance and forming part of his baggage, do not have to be
  reported, on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person does not disembark from the conveyance
    in Canada and the goods are not removed from the conveyance in
    Canada other than to be transferred under customs control directly
    to another commercial passenger conveyance for departure to a place
    outside Canada or directly to a designated holding area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person and goods are transferred under
    customs control directly to a designated holding area, the person
    does not leave and the goods are not removed from that designated
    holding area other than to board or to be loaded on board a
    commercial passenger conveyance for departure to a place outside
    Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Goods in the actual possession of a person arriving
  in Canada aboard a commercial passenger conveyance who has as his
  destination another place in Canada at which there is a designated
  customs office, and any goods being carried on board the same
  conveyance and forming part of his baggage, may be reported at that
  designated customs office on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person does not disembark from the conveyance
    at the place of arrival in Canada and the goods are not removed from
    the conveyance at the place of arrival in Canada other than to be
    transferred under customs control directly to another commercial
    passenger conveyance for departure to that other place in Canada or
    directly to a designated holding area; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the person and goods are transferred under
    customs control directly to a designated holding area, the person
    does not leave and the goods are not removed from that designated
    holding area other than to board or to be loaded on board a
    commercial passenger conveyance for departure to that other place in
    Canada.

- SOR/2006-155, s. 4(F)

## [Periodic Reporting]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898507 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Canadian-built commercial
fishing vessels and duty-paid fishing vessels that are registered under
the [Canada Shipping Act, 2001](/eng/acts/C-10.15) and that are imported
during a fishing season may be reported at the close of that fishing
season if the vessels do not, after last having been reported under
section 9 of the [Reporting of Exported Goods
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2005-23),

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} take on ships stores other than diesel fuel;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} land in a country other than Canada; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} pick up goods that are not the product of Canada and
  that have not been reported under section 12 of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 6
:::

## [Information Required --- Other Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898700 .Part}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  marine pleasure craft will arrive in Canada with no specified goods on
  board and the person in charge of the marine pleasure craft is
  authorized to present themselves in the alternative manner described
  in paragraph 11(e) of the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323), that person is required
  under subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the time and
  place at which the marine pleasure craft is scheduled to land
  following arrival in Canada and a description of all of the goods
  carried on board, including their value and quantity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the marine pleasure craft
  shall give the information by telephone to an officer at a designated
  customs office at least 30 minutes but no more than four hours before
  the marine pleasure craft arrives in Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the marine pleasure craft
  shall, before it arrives in Canada, notify an officer at a designated
  customs office by telephone of any change to the information given
  unless there are emergency circumstances, in which case they shall
  notify an officer at a designated customs office of the change, and
  explain the circumstances, by telephone when the marine pleasure craft
  arrives in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  corporate aircraft or private aircraft will arrive in Canada with no
  specified goods on board and the person in charge of the aircraft is
  authorized to present themselves in an alternative manner described in
  paragraph 11(b) or (c) of the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323), that person is required
  under subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the time and
  place at which the aircraft is scheduled to land following arrival in
  Canada and a description of all of the goods carried on board,
  including their value and quantity.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the aircraft shall give the
  information by telephone to an officer at a designated customs office
  at least two but no more than 48 hours before the aircraft arrives in
  Canada.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the aircraft shall, before it
  arrives in Canada, notify an officer at a designated customs office by
  telephone of any change to the information given unless there are
  emergency circumstances, in which case they shall notify an officer at
  a designated customs office of the change, and explain the
  circumstances, by telephone when the aircraft arrives in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  highway conveyance that is ordinarily used to transport specified
  goods to or from Canada will arrive in Canada with no specified goods
  on board, the carrier that operates the conveyance is required under
  subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the information set
  out in Part 3 of Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the carrier is not required
  to give the information if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the conveyance is a tractor that is not towing a
    trailer; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the carrier is a CSA carrier and the person in
    charge of the conveyance is a commercial driver who holds an
    authorization under section 6.2 or 6.21 of the [Presentation of
    Persons (2003) Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The carrier shall give the information at least one
  hour before the highway conveyance arrives in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  rail conveyance that is ordinarily used to transport specified goods
  to or from Canada will arrive in Canada with no specified goods on
  board and either with no rail car or with all of its rail cars empty,
  the carrier that operates the conveyance is required under
  subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the information set
  out in Part 4 of Schedule 1.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The carrier shall give the information at least two
  hours before the rail conveyance arrives in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  rail conveyance that is ordinarily used to transport specified goods
  to or from Canada will arrive in Canada, a crew member on board the
  conveyance who will have goods in their actual possession or that form
  part of their baggage on arrival in Canada is required under
  subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the Agency the time and place at
  which the conveyance is scheduled to arrive in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The crew member shall give the information by radio
  or telephone to the chief officer of customs at the place at which the
  rail conveyance is scheduled to arrive in Canada at least two hours
  before it arrives.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  vessel, aircraft or rail conveyance is or will be used to transport 30
  or more persons to Canada other than on a regular schedule or
  predetermined charter schedule, the carrier that operates the
  conveyance is required under subsection 12.1(1) of the Act to give the
  Agency the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a vessel, the time and place at
    which it is scheduled to land following arrival in Canada;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aircraft, the time and place at
    which it is scheduled to land following arrival in Canada; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a rail conveyance, the time and
    place at which it is scheduled to arrive in Canada.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (1)(a) and (b), the carrier is not
  required to give the information when the person in charge of the
  conveyance is required to give it in the circumstances set out in
  subsection 22(1) or 23(1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The carrier shall give the information in writing to
  the chief officer of customs at the place referred to in
  paragraph (1)(a), (b) or (c), as the case may be, at least 72 hours
  before the conveyance arrives in Canada.

## [Carrier Code]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898752 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** The requirements and
conditions that are to be met before the Minister may issue a carrier
code are the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the application is for a carrier code that would
  be held by the applicant as a carrier in connection with a mode of
  conveyance,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not already hold a carrier code
    as a carrier in connection with that mode of conveyance,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a carrier code previously held by the applicant
    as a carrier in connection with that mode of conveyance was
    cancelled, the matter that gave rise to the cancellation has been
    corrected, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant expects to operate at least one
    conveyance of that mode that would ordinarily be used to transport
    specified goods to or from Canada; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the application is for a carrier code that would
  be held by the applicant as a freight forwarder,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not already hold a carrier code
    as a freight forwarder,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if a carrier code previously held by the applicant
    as a freight forwarder was cancelled, the matter that gave rise to
    the cancellation has been corrected, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant expects to cause specified goods to
    be transported to Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A person that holds a
carrier code shall notify the Agency without delay of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a change to the information that is given in the
  application for the carrier code;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a merger or amalgamation with another person that
  holds a carrier code; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cessation of a business activity to which the
  carrier code relates.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  circumstances in which the Minister may suspend a carrier code held by
  a person are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person contravenes

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a provision of a federal Act or regulation if the
      provision relates to the importation or exportation of goods,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
      Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5) or a regulation made under it, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the [Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and
      Terrorist Financing Act](/eng/acts/P-24.501) or a regulation made
      under it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person fails to pay an amount that is payable
    under the Act; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person provided false or misleading information
    in the application for the carrier code.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall notify the person that holds the
  carrier code without delay and in writing of the suspension of the
  carrier code, the period during which the suspension applies and the
  reasons for the suspension.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The person may make representations to the Minister
  within the period of suspension as to why the carrier code should be
  reinstated.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may reinstate a carrier code that is
  suspended under subsection 12.1(5) of the Act only if the matter that
  gave rise to the suspension has been corrected during the period of
  suspension.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  circumstances in which the Minister may cancel a carrier code held by
  a person are the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the matter that gave rise to a suspension has not
    been corrected within the period of suspension;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person has ceased all business activities to
    which the carrier code relates;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person holds more than one carrier code as a
    carrier in connection with a particular mode of conveyance;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person holds more than one carrier code as a
    freight forwarder;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the carrier code was issued before the coming into
    force of subsection 12.1(4) of the Act and the person holds it in a
    capacity other than that of a carrier or a freight forwarder, such
    as an agent or a person in charge of a conveyance; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the person requests the cancellation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before cancelling a carrier code, the Minister shall
  send written notice of the proposed cancellation and the reasons for
  it to the last known address of the person that holds the carrier code
  and, unless the carrier code is being cancelled in the circumstances
  set out in paragraph (1)(e) or (f), shall give the person the
  opportunity to make representations in writing as to why the carrier
  code should not be cancelled.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The cancellation of a carrier code is not effective
  until the earlier of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 30 days after the day on which the person that
    holds the carrier code receives the notice, and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 45 days after the day on which the notice is sent.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
exempted from holding a valid carrier code:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person in charge of a marine pleasure craft in the
  circumstances set out either in subsection 22(1) or in
  subsection 17(1) of the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person in charge of a corporate aircraft or private
  aircraft in the circumstances set out either in subsection 23(1) or in
  subsection 15(1) of the [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a crew member of a rail conveyance that is ordinarily
  used to transport specified goods to or from Canada in the
  circumstances set out in subsection 26(1);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a carrier that operates a vessel, aircraft or rail
  conveyance in the circumstances set out in subsection 27(1); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a person in charge of a non-commercial passenger
  conveyance in the circumstances set out in subsection 4(1) of the
  [Presentation of Persons (2003)
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-323).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-90, s. 8
:::
