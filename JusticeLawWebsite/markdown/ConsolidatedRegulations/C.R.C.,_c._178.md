## Ontario Berry-for-Processing Order {#ontario-berry-for-processing-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 178
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Berries Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536061 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Ontario Berry-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._178).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536063 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act of
    Ontario; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[berries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[berries]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means strawberries and raspberries
    produced in Ontario that are used by a processor for processing;
    ([petits fruits]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing
    Board of Ontario; ([Régie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Berry
    Growers' Marketing Board established by Order in Council of the
    Lieutenant Governor of Ontario pursuant to the Act; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Provincial Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the O. Reg.
    260/57 made by the Board pursuant to the Act. ([règlement
    provincial]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536070 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Board and the Commodity
Board are each authorized to regulate the marketing of berries in
interprovincial and export trade and for such purposes may, with respect
to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario,
exercise all or any powers like the powers exercisable by the Board
under sections 4 to 6 of the Provincial Regulations and by the Commodity
Board under sections 7 to 14 of the Provincial Regulations in relation
to the marketing of berries locally within that Province.
