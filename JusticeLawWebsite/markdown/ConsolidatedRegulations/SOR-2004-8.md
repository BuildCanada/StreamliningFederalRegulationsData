## Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations {#canada-customs-and-revenue-agency-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2004-8
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2004-01-23

Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations

P.C. 2004-25 2004-01-23
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 36(1)(b) of the [Public
Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01), hereby makes the annexed
[Canada Customs and Revenue Agency
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2004-8).
:::

## [Block Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714174 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsections 37.3(1) and (2)
of the [Public Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01) apply to the
portion of the Public Service known as Canada Customs and Revenue
Agency.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714176 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations cease to
have effect at 00:00:03, January 24, 2004.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-714178 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force at 00:00:01, January 24, 2004.
