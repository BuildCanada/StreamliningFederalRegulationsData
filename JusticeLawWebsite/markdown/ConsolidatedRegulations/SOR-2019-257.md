[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Payment Clearing and
      Settlement Act](/eng/acts/P-4.4). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[affiliate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an affiliated person or
      entity within the meaning of subsection
      (2). ([groupe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Affiliated persons or entities

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} one person or entity is affiliated with another
    person or entity if one of them is the subsidiary of the other or
    both are subsidiaries of the same person or entity or each of them
    is controlled by the same person or entity; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if two persons or entities are affiliated with the
    same person or entity at the same time, they are deemed to be
    affiliated with each other.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Control

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of these Regulations,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity controls a corporation if shares
    of the corporation to which are attached more than 50% of the votes
    that may be cast to elect directors of the corporation are held,
    directly or indirectly, by the person or entity and the votes
    attached to those shares are sufficient, if exercised, to elect a
    majority of the directors of the corporation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity controls a trust, fund or
    partnership (other than a limited partnership) or an unincorporated
    association or organization, if more than 50% of the ownership
    interests, however designated, into which the trust, fund,
    partnership, association or organization is divided are held,
    directly or indirectly, by the person or entity and the person or
    entity is able to direct the business and affairs of the trust,
    fund, partnership, association or organization; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the general partner of a limited partnership
    controls the limited partnership.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Subsidiary

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person or entity is a subsidiary of another person
  or entity if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is controlled by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that other person or entity,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that other person or entity and one or more
      persons or entities, each of which is controlled by that other
      person or entity, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} two or more persons or entities, each of which
      is controlled by that other person or entity; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is a subsidiary of a subsidiary of that other
    person or entity.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[[Limited clearing
  member]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 11.11(4) of the Act, a
  [[limited clearing member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a participant in
  a clearing and settlement system designated under subsection 4(1) of
  the Act who, under the rules of the system,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not required to contribute to a default fund;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not required to absorb any losses arising from
    the default of another participant in the system other than a
    reduction in the amount of variation margin gain that is returnable
    to the participant if the gain results from a transaction that was
    entered into by the participant and the defaulting participant and
    subsequently submitted for clearing to the clearing house of the
    system; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is subject to additional initial margin.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Ownership interest

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, a reference to an ownership
  interest in a clearing house does not include shares of the clearing
  house.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conflict of interest

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 11.04(6) of the Act, no member of the committee shall
beneficially own, directly or indirectly, shares of a clearing house of
a clearing and settlement system designated under subsection 4(1) of the
Act or of an entity that controls such a clearing house.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of resolution plan

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resolution plan for a
clearing and settlement system referred to in section 11.05 of the Act
must include

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a risk profile of the system that describes

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the design of the system,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the corporate governance or organizational
    structure of its clearing house,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} by way of a diagram or otherwise, the
    intercorporate relationships between the clearing house of the
    system and each of its affiliates, including with respect to each
    affiliate

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} its name and the law by or under which it was
      incorporated, continued or formed, and

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the percentage of votes attaching to all voting
      shares of the affiliate that are held, directly or indirectly, by
      another affiliate,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the critical clearing and settlement functions
    performed by the system or its clearing house,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the critical services referred to in paragraph
    11.07(1)(f) of the Act that are provided to its clearing house by a
    service provider,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the critical operational resources of the system
    and its clearing house, including staff and information technology,
    and

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} any contractual or operational arrangements
    between the system or its clearing house with other clearing houses
    or systems;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a resolution strategy that sets out the principal
  measures that could be taken in the event of a declaration of
  non-viability;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an assessment of the resources that may be necessary
  to implement the resolution strategy including the financial resources
  and loss allocation tools provided for in the rules, by-laws or
  related arrangements of the system or its clearing house;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an operational plan that sets out

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which the resolution strategy could
    be implemented, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any potential barriers to a resolution; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} a statement that the plan has been approved by the
  Governor of the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Review and modifications

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Governor of the Bank
must review and make any necessary modifications to the resolution plan

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least once during each 12-month period following
  the development or modification of the plan; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} without delay after any change referred to in any of
  paragraphs 9(2)(a) to (c) of the Act is made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Persons from whom costs may be recovered

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 11.18 of the Act, the Bank may recover the costs
  of a resolution from

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a clearing house that has been declared non-viable
    under section 11.06 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the clearing house of a clearing and settlement
    system that has been declared non-viable under section 11.06 of the
    Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person or entity who was a participant in a
    clearing and settlement system on the day on which a declaration of
    non-viability was made in respect of the system or its clearing
    house or on any subsequent day.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Costs

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The costs of a resolution of a clearing and
  settlement system or clearing house are the costs incurred, and the
  amounts paid, by the Bank in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} address the financial losses, or shortfalls in
    liquidity or capital adequacy, of the clearing house;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} maintain the pre-funded financial resources at the
    level provided for in the by-laws, rules or arrangements that govern
    the clearing and settlement system or clearing house;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} operate the clearing and settlement system or
    clearing house;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} meet a participant's financial obligations to the
    clearing house;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} administer the compensation process referred to in
    sections 6 to 18; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} generally execute the resolution.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limited clearing member

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The costs of a resolution that the Bank may recover
  from a limited clearing member are limited to the costs incurred, and
  the amounts paid, by the Bank in order to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} operate the clearing and settlement system or
    clearing house;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} administer the compensation process referred to in
    sections 6 to 18; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} meet the member's financial obligations to the
    clearing house.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amounts not included

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The amounts paid under sections 13 and 18 are not
  included in subsections (2) or (3).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of certain costs ---
  participant

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} If the Bank incurs a cost or pays an amount in order
  to meet a participant's financial obligations to a clearing house, it
  must attempt to recover that cost or amount from the participant
  before attempting to recover it from a clearing house or other
  participant.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of certain costs --- clearing
  house

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the Bank incurs a cost or pays an amount referred
  to in any of paragraphs (2)(a) to (c) and (f) in order to meet the
  obligations of a clearing house, it must attempt to recover that cost
  or amount from the clearing house before attempting to recover it from
  a participant.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed persons and entities

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following persons or entities are prescribed for the purposes of
  subsection 11.26(1) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any person or entity that was a creditor of a
    clearing house immediately before a declaration of non-viability was
    made in respect of the clearing house or its clearing and settlement
    system;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any participant that is subject to an order made
    under subsection 11.11(1) of the Act and was a participant in a
    clearing and settlement system immediately before a declaration of
    non-viability was made in respect of the system or its clearing
    house;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any shareholder of a clearing house that is subject
    to an order made under paragraph 11.09(1)(b) of the Act and that was
    a shareholder of the clearing house immediately before a declaration
    of non-viability was made in respect of the clearing house or its
    clearing and settlement system;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any holder of an ownership interest in a clearing
    house that is subject to an order made under paragraph 11.09(1)(b)
    of the Act and that was a holder of an ownership interest in the
    clearing house immediately before a declaration of non-viability was
    made in respect of the clearing house or its clearing and settlement
    system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exclusion --- creditors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(a), the following persons or
  entities are not prescribed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity that continues to receive,
    during the period beginning at the time the declaration of
    non-viability is made and ending on the day specified in the notice
    published under section 11.24 of the Act, payment in respect of the
    debt in accordance with the terms of the debt; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person or entity to which the clearing house is
    indebted as a result of the clearing house

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} acting as a central counter-party, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} providing clearing or settlement services for a
      clearing or settlement system.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Successors, assignees and transferees

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A prescribed person or entity referred to in
  subsection (1) includes the successor of the person or entity but does
  not include an assignee or transferee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation --- creditors

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The amount of compensation
to which a prescribed person or entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(a)
is entitled is the total of any amounts owing that were not paid by or
on behalf of the clearing house to the person or entity in accordance
with the terms of their contract during the period beginning at the time
the declaration of non-viability was made in respect of the clearing
house or its clearing and settlement system and ending on the day the
day specified in the notice published under section 11.24 of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation --- participants

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of compensation to which a participant referred to in paragraph
  6(1)(b) is entitled is the amount equal to the estimated value of the
  net losses incurred by the participant as a result of the making of an
  order under subsection 11.11(1) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assumption

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the net losses referred to in
  subsection (1) must be estimated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as if the measures to address financial loss, or
    shortfalls in liquidity or capital adequacy, that were specified in
    the by-laws or rules of the clearing house, or in an arrangement
    that relates to its clearing and settlement system, were exhausted;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without taking into consideration any assistance,
    financial or other, that is or may be provided to the participant,
    directly or indirectly, by the Bank or by Her Majesty in right of
    Canada or a province after the declaration of non-viability was
    made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution and liquidation values

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of determining the amount of compensation to which a
  prescribed person or entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(c) or (d) is
  entitled, the Bank must estimate the applicable resolution and
  liquidation values referred to in this section.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution value in respect of a share

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The resolution value in respect of a share is the
  total of the estimated value of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the share on the day specified in the notice
    published under section 11.24 of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any dividend paid after the declaration of
    non-viability was made with respect to the share, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other cash, securities or other rights or
    interests that are received or are to be received with respect to
    the share as a direct or indirect result of the making of an order
    under paragraph 11.09(1)(b) of the Act and any actions taken in
    furtherance of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Resolution value in respect of an
  ownership interest

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The resolution value in respect of an ownership
  interest in a clearing house is the total of the estimated value of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the ownership interest on the day specified in the
    notice published under section 11.24 of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any amount received after the declaration of
    non-viability was made with respect to the ownership interest, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} any cash, securities or other rights or interests
    that are received or are to be received with respect to the
    ownership interest as a direct or indirect result of the making of
    an order under paragraph 11.09(1)(b) of the Act and any actions
    taken in furtherance of the order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquidation value in respect of a share
  or ownership interest

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The liquidation value in respect of a share or
  ownership interest in a clearing house is the estimated value that the
  shareholder or holder of the ownership interest would have received in
  respect of the share or ownership interest if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the clearing house had become bankrupt and been
    liquidated at the time a declaration of non-viability was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the measures to address financial losses, or
    shortfalls in liquidity or capital adequacy, that were specified in
    its by-laws or rules, or in an arrangement that relates to the
    clearing and settlement system, were exhausted.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assumptions

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The liquidation value referred to in subsection (4)
  must be estimated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} as if no declaration of non-viability was made; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} without taking into consideration any assistance,
    financial or other, that is or may be provided to the clearing
    house, directly or indirectly, by the Bank or by Her Majesty in
    right of Canada or a province after the declaration of non-viability
    was made.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation --- shareholders

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  amount of compensation to which a shareholder referred to in paragraph
  6(1)(c) is entitled is determined by the formula

  ::: {#1178880 .Subsection}
  A -- B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated liquidation value in respect of their shares; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated resolution value in respect of their shares.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compensation --- holders of ownership
  interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of compensation to which a holder of an
  ownership interest referred to in paragraph 6(1)(d) is entitled is
  determined by the formula

  ::: {#1178886 .Subsection}
  A -- B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated liquidation value in respect of the ownership
      interest; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the estimated resolution value in respect of the ownership
      interest.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Negative value

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if the amount of compensation
  determined under this section is a negative value, the amount of
  compensation is zero.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice --- offer of compensation

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Within
  a reasonable period of time after the day specified in the notice
  published under section 11.24 of the Act, the Bank must give each
  person or entity that was prescribed in respect of the clearing and
  settlement system or clearing house a notice containing an offer of
  compensation in an amount equal to, or of a value estimated by the
  Bank to be equal to, the compensation to which that person or entity
  is entitled.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Contents of notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} state that an order has been made under paragraph
    11.09(1)(b) or subsection 11.11(1) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} describe the order's effects;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} state that the prescribed person or entity has 45
    days beginning on the day on which a summary of the notice is
    published in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) to
    notify the Bank of their decision to accept or object to the offer,
    or to object to the absence of an offer;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} state that if the offer or the absence of an offer
    is objected to by the prescribed person or entity, the compensation
    to be paid will be determined by an assessor; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} state that if the prescribed person or entity does
    not notify the Bank of their objection within the period set out in
    paragraph (c), the person or entity will receive the compensation
    offered or no compensation, as the case may be, and will not be
    permitted to contest its amount or value or the fact that no
    compensation is offered.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Publication

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A summary of the notice
given under subsection 11(1) must be published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) and on the website of the clearing
house.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of compensation

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Bank must pay a
prescribed person or entity referred to in section 6 any compensation
offered under subsection 11(1) if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person or entity notifies the Bank of its
  acceptance of the offer within the period set out in paragraph
  11(2)(c); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person or entity does not notify the Bank of its
  acceptance of, or objection to, the offer within that period.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appointment of assessor

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** An assessor must be
appointed under section 11.28 of the Act if the person or entity
notifies the Bank that it objects to the offer, or the absence of an
offer, within the period set out in paragraph 11(2)(c).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of appointment

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Within 45 days after the
day on which an assessor is appointed, the Bank must provide, to each
person and entity whose compensation is to be determined by the
assessor, a notice of the appointment that states that the person or
entity is bound by the assessor's determination of the amount of
compensation to be paid which may be lower or higher than that contained
in the offer.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessor's determination

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The assessor must
determine the amount of compensation to be paid in accordance with
sections 7 to 10.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Assessor's notice

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  assessor must provide, to every prescribed person or entity whose
  compensation is determined by the assessor, a notice

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} setting out the assessor's determination of the
    amount of compensation to which the person or entity is entitled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} stating that the person or entity is bound by the
    assessor's determination as to the amount of compensation to be
    paid; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} stating that the Bank must pay the compensation
    within 90 days after the date of the notice.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Copy to Bank

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The assessor must provide a copy of each notice to
  the Bank.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Payment of compensation

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The Bank must pay a
prescribed person or entity the compensation that it is entitled to
receive within

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if a notice referred to in subsection 17(1) is
  provided to the person or entity, 90 days after the date of that
  notice; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, 135 days after the day on which a
  summary of the notice given under subsection 11(1) is published in the
  [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}[[Oversight
information]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 2 of the Act, [[oversight information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
means any information contained in the following documents:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} directives issued by the Governor of the Bank under
  subsection 6(1) or (2) of the Act to a clearing house or a participant
  and any correspondence to or from the directors or officers of the
  clearing house or the participant that is related to the directive;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} reports prepared by or at the request of the Bank,
  including any written recommendations prepared by the Bank, as a
  result of an audit, inspection or other oversight review of a clearing
  and settlement system;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any correspondence to or from the directors or
  officers of a clearing house that is related to a report or
  recommendation referred to in paragraph (b);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} resolution plans referred to in subsection 11.05(1)
  of the Act;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} documents that are prepared by or at the request of
  the Bank for the purpose of advising the Governor of the Bank on
  whether to make a declaration of non-viability under section 11.06 of
  the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to affiliates

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** A clearing house may
disclose oversight information in respect of its clearing and settlement
system to its affiliates, its directors, officers, employees, auditors,
securities underwriters and legal advisors and those of its affiliates,
if the clearing house ensures that the information remains confidential.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Disclosure to government or regulatory
body

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** A clearing house may
disclose oversight information in respect of its clearing and settlement
system to any government authority or regulatory body that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} regulates the system; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} has entered into an agreement or arrangement with the
  Bank under section 13.3 of the Act in respect of the system and
  oversight information.
