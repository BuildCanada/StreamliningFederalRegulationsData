## Indian Economic Development Guarantee Order {#indian-economic-development-guarantee-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/78-21
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1970

Registration 1977-12-29

Terms and Conditions Respecting the Guaranteeing of Loans for the
Purpose of the Economic Development of Indians

P.C. 1977-3608 1977-12-22
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development and the Treasury Board, pursuant to Vote L53b of
Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970, is pleased hereby to approve the
revocation of the Indian Economic Development Regulations made by Order
in Council P.C. 1972-1498 of 4th July, 1972[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^1^](#fn_SOR_78-21_EN_hq_10306){#fn_SOR_78-21_EN_hq_10306-ID0EDCAA},
as amended[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^2^](#fn_SOR_78-21_EN_hq_10307){#fn_SOR_78-21_EN_hq_10307-ID0EBCAA},
and to approve the annexed terms and conditions respecting the
guaranteeing of loans for the purpose of the economic development of
Indians.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861458 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Terms and Conditions
may be cited as the [Indian Economic Development Guarantee
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-21).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861460 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual, a group of
    Indians, an Indian band, a partnership or a corporation in respect
    of whom an application is made by a bank pursuant to section 3;
    ([requérant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a bank to which the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01)
      applies,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a credit union, caisse populaire or other
      cooperative credit society that has been designated as a bank for
      the purposes of the [Farm Improvement Loans Act](/eng/acts/F-3),
      the Small Businesses Loans Act or the [Fisheries Improvement Loans
      Act](/eng/acts/F-22) or is designated by the Minister on the
      application of that society as a bank for purposes of this Order,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} carries on the business of a trust company
        within the meaning of the Trust Companies Act, the business of a
        loan company within the meaning of the Loan Companies Act or the
        business of insurance within the meaning of the Canadian and
        British Insurance Companies Act,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} has been designated as a bank for the purposes
        of the [Farm Improvement Loans Act](/eng/acts/F-3), the Small
        Businesses Loans Act or the [Fisheries Improvement Loans
        Act](/eng/acts/F-22), or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is designated by the Minister on the
        application of that corporation as a bank for the purposes of
        this Order, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the Province of Alberta Treasury Branch
      established pursuant to the Treasury Branches Act as enacted by
      the Province of Alberta; ([banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[borrower]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an applicant who has received
    a guaranteed loan pursuant to this Order;
    ([emprunteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business enterprise]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, regardless of a
    change in ownership or control,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any business carried on in Canada for gain or
      profit,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a cooperative association incorported under a law
      of the Parliament of Canada or the legislature of a province, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the business of a profession recognized as such
      under a law of the Parliament of Canada or the legislature of a
      province,

    but does not include

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a business having as its object the furtherance
      of a charitable or religious purpose, or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a business carried on for the purpose of money
      lending, insurance or finance; ([entreprise
      commerciale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guaranteed loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guaranteed loan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a loan, including a
    loan pursuant to a line of credit, made by a bank and guaranteed by
    the Minister pursuant to section 4; ([prêt garanti]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an Indian as defined in the
    [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5); ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[responsible officer of the bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible officer of the bank]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the manager or assistant manager of a bank or a
      branch thereof,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person for the time acting as manager of a
      bank or a branch thereof,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the credit committee of a bank or a branch
      thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any person duly authorized by a bank to supervise
      the granting of loans. ([préposé compétent de la
      banque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/84-4, s. 1
:::

## [Applications by Banks]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861489 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A bank may apply to the
Minister, in a form satisfactory to him, for the guarantee by the
Minister of a loan to be made by the bank to an applicant whose
activities contribute or may contribute to the economic development of
Indians.

## [Guarantee by the Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861491 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) to (4), the Minister may guarantee, in whole or in
  part, a loan in respect of which an application is made pursuant to
  section 3 where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} he is satisfied that the loan is required to
    finance a business enterprise that will or may contribute to the
    economic development of Indians;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the application for the loan was scrutinized and
    checked by a responsible officer of the bank, with the care required
    in the conduct of its ordinary business, prior to the making of the
    loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the terms and conditions of the loan were fixed by
    the bank in consultation with the borrower; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the loan is to be repaid within a period that does
    not exceed 15 years and repayments are to be made not less
    frequently than annually.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not, without the approval of the
  Minister of Finance, guarantee to a bank a loan in excess of
  \$100,000.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall not guarantee to a bank any loan
  where the aggregate of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the principal amount of the guaranteed loan,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed amount
    in respect of all other guaranteed loans, made pursuant to Vote L53b
    of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1970,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed amount
    of all direct loans made pursuant to Vote L53b of Appropriation Act
    No. 1, 1970,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} grants, contributions or other payments made or
    authorized by the Minister for the purposes of Indian economic
    development,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the amount outstanding and the undisbursed amount
    in respect of all loans, grants, contributions and development
    incentives made under statutory authority vested in the Minister of
    Regional Economic Expansion, and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} loans made or guaranteed under statutory authority
    by any federal Department, Crown Corporation or Agency

  to or on behalf of the applicant or in respect of the business
  enterprise for the purpose of which the guaranteed loan is required
  exceeds \$500,000 or may, in the opinion of the Minister be reasonably
  expected to exceed \$500,000, unless he has obtained, in addition to
  any approval required under subsection (2), the approval of the
  Treasury Board.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on giving any guarantee without
  the approval of the Treasury Board, certify to the Treasury Board that
  the aggregate referred to in subsection (3) does not exceed \$500,000.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Where a guaranteed loan is made by a bank other than
  to an Indian, an Indian band or a corporation controlled by one or
  more Indians, the Minister shall not guarantee more than 90 per cent
  of any loss sustained by the bank as a result of the guaranteed loan.

## [Security by Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861509 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister shall require that a bank take security for a guaranteed loan
  in one or more of the following forms:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a promissory note;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any security instrument pursuant to section 88 of
    the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an assignment of book debts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a chattel mortgage or commercial pledge;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a mortgage or hypothecation on real property;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an assignment of rights and interest of the
    borrower under an agreement for sale; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any security in place of or in addition to that
    referred to in paragraphs (a) to (f) that the Minister deems
    appropriate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every bank shall, throughout the term of a guaranteed
  loan, keep and maintain in accordance with normal banking practice,
  the security taken for the guaranteed loan under subsection (1).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No bank shall release the security taken for a
  guaranteed loan under subsection (1)

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} without the prior approval of the Minister; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the Minister of Finance originally approved
    the loan, without the prior approval of the Minister and the
    Minister of Finance.

## [Revision of Loan Terms and Default]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861523 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a borrower is in
default in respect of a guaranteed loan, other than a guaranteed loan
made by way of a line of credit, or informs the bank that any terms of
the agreement in respect of such a loan are such that he may have to
default, and the bank is of the opinion that a revision or alteration of
any of the terms of the agreement may enable the borrower to meet his
obligations thereunder, the bank may, with the consent of the borrower,
revise or alter the terms of the agreement by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} increasing or decreasing the periods between
  repayments, except that repayments shall be due no less frequently
  than annually; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} extending the time within which the guaranteed loan
  is to be repaid, so long as such extension

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} does not, without the approval of the Minister,
    exceed one year from the date on which the loan was originally
    scheduled to be repaid,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} does not, without the approval of the Minister and
    the Minister of Finance, exceed 15 years from the date on which the
    guarantee was approved, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} is not granted without the approval of the
    Minister of Finance, where that Minister originally approved the
    guarantee.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Where a borrower is in
default in respect of a loan made by way of a line of credit, or the
bank reasonably believes that the borrower will have to default in
respect of such a loan, the bank may, with the consent of the borrower,
revise or alter the terms of the loan to enable the borrower to meet his
obligation, except that no extension of the expiry date or no revision
of the security requirements and the interest rate shall be given
without the approval of the Minister and, where the Minister of Finance
has originally approved the line of credit under subsection 4(2), the
approval of the Minister of Finance.

## [Claim by Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861531 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A claim
  by a bank for a loss as a result of a guaranteed loan shall be made to
  the Minister at any time not less than 90 days after the entire amount
  of the balance outstanding becomes due and repayable and shall
  comprise

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the unpaid principal amount of the guaranteed loan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any uncollected taxed costs for or incidental to
    legal proceedings in respect of the guaranteed loan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the uncollected earned interest outstanding at the
    time the claim is approved for payment by the Minister at the full
    rate of interest specified in the loan terms for

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a period of 180 days after the entire amount of
      the balance outstanding becomes due and repayable and for such
      further period as, in the opinion of the Minister, is justified
      for reasons beyond the control of the bank, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} thereafter, at one-half the rate of interest
      specified in the loan terms;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} legal fees, legal costs and disbursements, whether
    taxable or not, actually incurred by the bank, whether with or
    without litigation, in collecting or endeavouring to collect the
    outstanding balance of the guaranteed loan or in protecting the
    interests of the Minister but only to the extent that the Deputy
    Minister of Justice taxes or allows; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other disbursements actually incurred by the
    bank in collecting or endeavouring to collect the outstanding
    balance of the guaranteed loan or in protecting the interests of the
    Minister but only to the extent that the Minister allows.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the expression
  "loan terms" includes any agreement entered into by the bank with the
  borrower on a promissory note.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A valid claim by a bank under subsection (1) shall be
  approved for payment by the Minister within 60 days from the
  acceptance thereof and shall thereupon be paid forthwith unless a
  longer period has been mutually agreed to by the bank and the
  Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/84-4, s. 2
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 11, the
Minister shall not be obligated to accept a claim under section 8 unless
he is satisfied that the steps required by section 12 have been taken.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/84-4, s. 2
:::

## [Assignment by Bank]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861548 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where, under this Order,
the Minister has paid to a bank the amount of a loss sustained by a bank
as a result of a guaranteed loan, the bank shall forthwith execute an
assignment in a form satisfactory to the Minister of all the rights of
the bank in respect of that loan and, without limiting the generality of
the foregoing, shall execute an assignment of all rights and powers of
the bank in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the loan;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any judgment obtained by the bank in respect of the
  loan; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any security taken by the bank for the repayment of
  the loan.

<!-- -->

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a loan has been guaranteed in part and where the Minister has
  instructed a bank not to realize on its securities pursuant to
  subparagraph 12(2)(a)(iii), the Minister shall become liable for the
  total loss of the bank as if the loan had been guaranteed by the
  Minister for the full amount thereof, and, on payment of the claim by
  the Minister, the bank shall execute an assignment, in a form
  satisfactory to the Minister, of all the rights of the bank in respect
  of that loan as described in section 10.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a loan has been guaranteed in part and the bank
  is unable to realize on any or all of its securities, the bank may
  submit a claim for loss pursuant to section 8 for the estimated amount
  of the net loss as mutually agreed between the bank and the Minister,
  and the bank on payment of a valid claim shall, at the option of the
  Minister,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} execute an assignment in a form satisfactory to the
    Minister of all rights of the bank in respect of that loan as
    described in section 10; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} continue to take all reasonable steps to realize on
    the outstanding securities.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a loan has been guaranteed in part and funds
  are recovered from any source, including the disposal of assets,
  subsequent to the payment of a claim in respect of the guaranteed
  loan, such funds, less realization costs, shall be divided between the
  Minister and the bank in proportion to their respective shares of the
  net loss as agreed between the bank and the Minister.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/84-4, s. 3
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861561 .Part}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where
  a borrower is in default in respect of any term or condition of a
  guaranteed loan, the bank shall, within 30 days thereafter, advise the
  Minister of the default of the borrower and, subject to sections 6 and
  7, the entire amount of the balance outstanding on the loan shall, at
  the option of the bank and the Minister, become repayable.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a guaranteed loan becomes repayable pursuant to
  subsection (1), the bank shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take such steps, whether by legal proceedings or
    otherwise, as it considers necessary to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} effect collection of the balance outstanding,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} obtain additional security,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} realize on any or all of the security it has
      taken unless it is instructed by the Minister not to do so, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} effect any compromise with, or grant any
      concession to, any person other than the borrower with the
      approval of the Minister; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days of receipt by the Minister of advice
    of the date the loan becomes repayable, take such additional steps
    for the realization or protection of the security as the Minister
    may reasonably require.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/84-4, s. 3
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall
maintain a registry for the purpose of recording loans made under this
Order.

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** A bank that makes a
guaranteed loan under this Order shall furnish to the Minister such
reports or information as the Minister may from time to time require.

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Where an application for
a guaranteed loan has been scrutinized and checked as described in
paragraph 4(1)(b) and it is subsequently discovered that a false
material statement has been made in the application or that the proceeds
of the loan have been or are being used otherwise than for the purpose
specified in the application, the liability of the Minister under the
guarantee issued under this Order is not affected thereby.

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** The Minister shall in
January of each year prepare a report with respect to the administration
of this Order during the immediately preceding fiscal year and shall
include that report in the report submitted by him to Parliament
pursuant to section 7 of the [Department of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development Act](/eng/acts/I-6).
