## Order Transferring from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department of Public Works and Government Services Certain Portions of the Chief Information Officer Branch of the Treasury Board Secretariat {#order-transferring-from-the-treasury-board-secretariat-to-the-department-of-public-works-and-government-services-certain-portions-of-the-chief-information-officer-branch-of-the-treasury-board-secretariat .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-232
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department
of Public Works and Government Services Certain Portions of the Chief
Information Officer Branch of the Treasury Board Secretariat

P.C. 2003-2089  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers from the Treasury Board Secretariat to the Department of
Public Works and Government Services the control and supervision of
those portions of the public service in the Chief Information Officer
Branch of the Treasury Board Secretariat known as

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} GOL Analysis,

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} Gateways and Clusters,

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} GOL Assistant Secretary's Office,

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Service Delivery Improvement Division,

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} Secure Channel Client Engagement and Secure Channel
  Project Management Office,

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} Organizational Readiness Division, and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} Communications and Public Relations,

effective December 12, 2003.
