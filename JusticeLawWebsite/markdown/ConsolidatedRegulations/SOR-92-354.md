## Protection of Assets (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations {#protection-of-assets-cooperative-credit-associations-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-354
:::

[COOPERATIVE CREDIT ASSOCIATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/C-41.01)

Registration 1992-06-04

Regulations Respecting the Protection and Maintenance of Assets of
Associations

P.C. 1992-1183  1992-06-04
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 463(g) of the
[Cooperative Credit Associations Act](/eng/acts/C-41.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA}, is pleased hereby to
revoke the Protection of Securities (Cooperative Credit Associations)
Regulations, C.R.C., c. 420, and to make the annexed [Regulations
respecting the protection and maintenance of assets of
associations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-354) in substitution therefor.

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EFBAA){#fn_1e}S.C. 1991, c. 48
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945192 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Protection of Assets (Cooperative Credit Associations)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-354).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945194 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bond]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a contract of insurance by which
    one party agrees to indemnify another party for loss arising out of
    the act of a third party; ([caution]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an association,
    means a negotiable security owned or held by the association or held
    by the association in trust, but does not include a security issued
    by the association; ([titre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[security transaction]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a purchase, sale,
    redemption, exchange, transfer or assignment of, or other
    transaction in respect of, a security. ([opération sur
    titres]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-527, s. 1(F)
:::

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945201 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to an association

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is carrying on business in a jurisdiction in
  compliance with a law of that jurisdiction, to the extent that that
  law is inconsistent with these Regulations; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the extent that they are inconsistent with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a power conferred or a duty imposed on the
    association in respect of the administration of an estate or trust,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the discharge of the association's
    responsibilities in acting as an agent.

## [Safeguarding of Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945207 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An association shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} maintain a record in writing of procedures to be
  followed in the handling and safeguarding of assets owned or held by
  the association; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} inform every director, officer, employee and agent of
  the association who has access to, or is involved in the handling and
  safeguarding of, assets owned or held by the association of those
  procedures.

## [Safeguarding of Securities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945211 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An association shall
maintain an up-to-date record that identifies every security.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2) and section 7, an association shall ensure that
  every security is kept securely, in a manner that prevents
  unauthorized access to the security, in the custody of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the association; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an entity that is authorized to act as a custodian
    of securities or as a depository or clearing agency for securities
    by a law of the jurisdiction in which the entity is carrying on
    business.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} under the control of the government of a
    jurisdiction in which the association is carrying on business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pledged as collateral for the indebtedness or
    potential indebtedness of the association;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} loaned to a person pursuant to a written agreement;
    or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in transit.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** An association shall not
place a security in the custody of an entity referred to in paragraph
6(1)(b) unless the association has entered into a written custodial
agreement with that entity.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An association shall, on a
daily basis, deposit any net amount received by the association as a
result of any security transaction in an account kept by the association

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} with a financial institution that is authorized to
  accept deposits by a law of the jurisdiction where the financial
  institution is carrying on business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} with a trust company that is authorized to hold money
  in trust by a law of the jurisdiction where the trust company carries
  on business;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} with the government of the jurisdiction in which the
  association is carrying on business, or with an agency thereof that is
  authorized to act as a custodian of securities; or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} with CDS Clearing and Depository Services Inc.

## [Registration of a Security]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945231 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), an association shall ensure that every
  security is registered in the association's name in the register of
  the entity that issued the security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  security that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cannot be registered in the association's name for
    any reason that is beyond the control of that association;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is under the control of the government of a
    jurisdiction in which the association is carrying on business;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is held by the association as collateral or for
    safekeeping;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is registered in the name of a nominee of the
    association or of an entity referred to in paragraph 6(1)(b);

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} is held under a book entry certificateless or
    immobilization system; or

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} is held temporarily by an agent of the association,
    a liquidator, a trustee or the issuer of the security for purposes
    of reorganization, amalgamation, liquidation or voting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a security is otherwise protected against loss,
  fraud, theft and destruction, the association may hold the security

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in bearer form; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in registered form in a name other than the
    association's name.

## [Bonding and Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-945244 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  association shall acquire and at all times maintain one or more bonds
  issued by an entity authorized to insure risks under the laws of the
  jurisdiction in which the entity carries on business to indemnify the
  association for any loss in respect of assets owned or held by that
  association arising out of a dishonest or criminal act of an officer
  or employee of that association.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An association shall, in respect of assets owned or
  held by the association, acquire and at all times maintain one or more
  insurance policies to indemnify that association for loss arising out
  of damage to, or the destruction or mysterious disappearance of, those
  assets or out of any other usual contingency.

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall
provide that the bond or insurance policy shall not be cancelled or
terminated by the insurer or the insured until at least 30 days after
the receipt by the Superintendent of a written notice from the insurer
or the insured, as the case may be, of its intention to cancel or
terminate the bond or insurance policy.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A bond or an insurance
policy acquired and maintained in accordance with section 10 shall be in
an amount that is established by the directors of the association,
having regard to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the nature and value of the assets owned or held by
  the association;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the arrangements and procedures applicable to the
  handling and safeguarding of the assets owned or held by the
  association; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other factors that might affect the extent of any
  loss that the association might sustain.
