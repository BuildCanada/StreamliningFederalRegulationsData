## Regulations Exempting Certain Precursors and Controlled Substances from the Application of the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act {#regulations-exempting-certain-precursors-and-controlled-substances-from-the-application-of-the-controlled-drugs-and-substances-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-229
:::

[CONTROLLED DRUGS AND SUBSTANCES ACT](/eng/acts/C-38.8)

Registration 1997-04-22

Regulations Exempting Certain Precursors and Controlled Substances from
the Application of the Controlled Drugs and Substances Act

P.C. 1997-627  1997-04-22
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Health, pursuant to paragraph 55(1)(z) of the
[Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR_97-229_EN_hq_5324){#fn_SOR_97-229_EN_hq_5324-ID0EDBAA},
hereby makes the annexed [Regulations Exempting Certain Precursors and
Controlled Substances from the Application of the Controlled Drugs and
Substances Act](/eng/regulations/SOR-97-229).

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SOR_97-229_EN_hq_5324-ID0EDBAA){#fn_SOR_97-229_EN_hq_5324}S.C.
  1996, c. 19
  :::
:::

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005344 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The substances set out in
Schedule I are exempt from the application of the [Controlled Drugs and
Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8).

**[[2 and 3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2_and_3 .sectionLabel}** [\[Repealed,
SOR/97-514, s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1005347 .Part}

**[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_Ind1E3F_hq_8836){#fn_Ind1E3F_hq_8836-ID0EBCA}[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4
.sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into force on the day which the
[Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8), chapter 19 of
the Statutes of Canada, 1996, comes into force.
