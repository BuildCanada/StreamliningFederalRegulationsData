[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Export and Import Permits
      Act](/eng/acts/E-19). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[current softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[current softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      products referred to in item 5104, Group 5, of the schedule to the
      Export Control List. ([produits de bois d'oeuvre
      courants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[exported]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning set out in
      section 6.4 of the Act. ([exporté]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
      the case of Quebec, the products referred to in item 5105, Group
      5, of the schedule to the Export Control List as it existed during
      the period beginning on November 1, 2004 and ending on October 11,
      2006. ([anciens produits de bois d'oeuvre]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Manitoba quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
      current softwood lumber products that may be exported from
      Manitoba to the United States during a month under subsection
      6.3(2) of the Act. ([quantité pour le Manitoba]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Ontario quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
      current softwood lumber products that may be exported from Ontario
      to the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
      Act. ([quantité pour l'Ontario]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[primary producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      produces current softwood lumber products from softwood sawlogs
      and, in the case of Quebec, includes a person who produced former
      or Quebec softwood lumber products from softwood
      sawlogs. ([entreprise de première transformation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[primary producer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      current softwood lumber products that the primary producer
      produced at any time that were not remanufactured in Canada and,
      in the case of Quebec, includes former softwood lumber
      products. ([produits d'une entreprise de première
      transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Quebec quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity of
      current softwood lumber products that may be exported from Quebec
      to the United States during a month under subsection 6.3(2) of the
      Act. ([quantité pour le Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Quebec softwood lumber products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      softwood lumber products produced in Quebec that were reported to
      the Minister of Natural Resources and Wildlife under the Forest
      Act, R.S.Q., c. F-4.1. ([produits de bois d'oeuvre du
      Québec]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[reference period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Ontario, the period beginning on
        January 1, 2007 and ending on October 31, 2007,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Quebec, the period beginning on
        November 1, 2004 and ending on October 31, 2007, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of Manitoba, the period beginning
        on November 1, 2006 and ending on October 31, 2007. ([période de
        référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remanufacturer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who
      remanufactures --- within the meaning of subsection 13(1) of the
      [Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act,
      2006](/eng/acts/S-12.55) --- current softwood lumber products and,
      in the case of Quebec, includes a person who remanufactured former
      softwood lumber products. ([entreprise de seconde
      transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[remanufacturer's products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
      current softwood lumber products that the remanufacturer
      remanufactured at any time that were not further remanufactured in
      Canada and, in the case of Quebec, includes former softwood lumber
      products. ([produits d'une entreprise de seconde
      transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Saskatchewan quantity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the quantity
      of current softwood lumber products that may be exported from
      Saskatchewan to the United States during a month under subsection
      6.3(2) of the Act. ([quantité pour la
      Saskatchewan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Transfer of export allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a primary producer or remanufacturer transfers,
  with the consent of the Minister under subsection 6.3(4) of the Act, a
  portion of its export allocation for a month, together with the
  corresponding volume of its products, that volume is deemed to be
  included in the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's
  products that were exported in that month to the United States under
  export permits and not in the volume of the transferee.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order establishes the
method for allocating the monthly quantity of current softwood lumber
products that may be exported from Ontario, Quebec, Manitoba and
Saskatchewan in 2008 for the purposes of paragraph 6.3(3)(a) of the Act.

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Ontario]{.HTitleText1} {#h-739788 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Ontario quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies for
an export allocation is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#739791 .Section}
OQ × (EO/TEO)

where

[OQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Ontario quantity;

[EO]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
    that were exported from Ontario to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period; and

[TEO]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Ontario to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who indicate in
    writing to the Minister before December 14, 2007 that they
    relinquish their allocation of the Ontario quantity for 2008.
:::

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Quebec]{.HTitleText1} {#h-739796 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers with an export history

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If some
  or all of a primary producer's products were exported from Quebec to
  the United States under export permits during the reference period and
  the primary producer applies for an export allocation, the primary
  producer must choose to receive an allocation of the Quebec quantity
  calculated using the method described in section 5 (which is based on
  their historic export volume) or the method described in subsection
  6(1) (which is based on their historic production volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Primary producers without an export
  history

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If none of a primary producer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and if the primary producer produced Quebec
  softwood lumber products during the period beginning on January 1,
  2004 and ending on December 31, 2006 and they apply for an export
  allocation, the primary producer may only receive the allocation
  calculated using the method described in subsection 6(1) (which is
  based on their historic production volume).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remanufacturers

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If some or all of a remanufacturer's products were
  exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits during
  the reference period and the remanufacturer applies for an export
  allocation, the remanufacturer may only receive the allocation
  calculated using the method described in section 9 (which is based on
  their historic export volume).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic export volume

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based on their historic
export volume is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#739806 .Section}
(PPE/TPPE) ×{\[QQ × (TPPE/TEQ) × 94%\] + RNA}

where

[PPE]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's products that were exported
    from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period;

[TPPE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producer's products that were
    exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
    during the reference period;

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[TEQ]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who indicate in
    writing to the Minister before December 14, 2007 that they
    relinquish their allocation of the Quebec quantity for 2008; and

[RNA]{.dfn}
:   is the residual non-allocated quantity of the reserve pool
    calculated in accordance with section 8.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to primary producers ---
historic production volume

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  allocation of the Quebec quantity to a primary producer that is based
  on their historic production volume is calculated in accordance with
  the formula

  ::: {#739816 .Subsection}
  RP × (PPPV/TPV)

  where

  [RP]{.dfn}
  :   is the reserve pool calculated in accordance with section 7;

  [PPPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the volume of Quebec softwood lumber products that the primary
      producer produced during the period beginning on January 1, 2004
      and ending on December 31, 2006; and

  [TPV]{.dfn}
  :   is the total volume of Quebec softwood lumber products produced
      during the same period by primary producers whose allocations are
      based on their historic production volume.
  :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Maximum allocation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The allocation may not exceed 40% of the primary
  producer's average monthly volume of Quebec softwood lumber products
  produced during the period beginning January 1, 2004 and ending on
  December 31, 2006.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Records of production volumes

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The volume of Quebec softwood lumber products
  produced by a primary producer is based on records provided, with the
  consent of the primary producer, by the Government of Quebec to the
  Government of Canada.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reserve pool

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The reserve pool is
calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#739827 .Section}
QQ × (TPPE/TEQ) × 6%

where

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[TPPE]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of primary producers' products that were
    exported from Quebec to the United States under export permits
    during the reference period; and

[TEQ]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who indicate in
    writing to the Minister before December 14, 2007 that they
    relinquish their allocation of the Quebec quantity for 2008.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Residual non-allocated quantity

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** The residual non-allocated
quantity of the reserve pool is calculated in accordance with the
formula

::: {#739834 .Section}
RP - AP

where

[RP]{.dfn}
:   is the reserve pool; and

[AP]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of current softwood lumber products allocated to
    primary producers calculated in accordance with section 6.
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation to remanufacturers

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Quebec quantity to a remanufacturer that is based on their historic
export volume is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#739840 .Section}
QQ × (RE/TEQ)

where

[QQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Quebec quantity;

[RE]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the remanufacturer's products that were exported
    from Quebec to the United States under export permits during the
    reference period; and

[TEQ]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Quebec to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who indicate in
    writing to the Minister before December 14, 2007 that they
    relinquish their allocation of the Quebec quantity for 2008.
:::

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Manitoba]{.HTitleText1} {#h-739845 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Manitoba quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer who applies
for an export allocation is calculated in accordance with the formula

::: {#739848 .Section}
MQ × (EM/TEM)

where

[MQ]{.dfn}
:   is the Manitoba quantity;

[EM]{.dfn}
:   is the volume of the primary producer's or remanufacturer's products
    that were exported from Manitoba to the United States under export
    permits during the reference period; and

[TEM]{.dfn}
:   is the total volume of the primary producers' and remanufacturers'
    products that were exported from Manitoba to the United States under
    export permits during the reference period, excluding the volume of
    products of primary producers and remanufacturers who indicate in
    writing to the Minister before December 14, 2007 that they
    relinquish their allocation of the Manitoba quantity for 2008.
:::

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Saskatchewan]{.HTitleText1} {#h-739853 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Allocation

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The allocation of the
Saskatchewan quantity to a primary producer or remanufacturer is based
on the order of receipt of applications for the export allocation, with
each applicant receiving the volume applied for until the Saskatchewan
quantity is fully allocated.

## [PART 5]{.HLabel1}[Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-739856 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
