## Order Designating Shared Services Canada as a Department and the President as Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act {#order-designating-shared-services-canada-as-a-department-and-the-president-as-deputy-head-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2011-69
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE EMPLOYMENT ACT](/eng/acts/P-33.01)

Registration 2011-08-17

Order Designating Shared Services Canada as a Department and the
President as Deputy Head for Purposes of the Act

P.C. 2011-881 2011-08-03
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition
  [[department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public
  Service Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBACBA}, designates Shared
  Services Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition [[deputy
  head]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the [Public Service
  Employment Act](/eng/acts/P-33.01)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBABBA}, designates the President
  of Shared Services Canada as the deputy head of that entity for the
  purposes of that Act,

effective August 4, 2011.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBACBA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13
