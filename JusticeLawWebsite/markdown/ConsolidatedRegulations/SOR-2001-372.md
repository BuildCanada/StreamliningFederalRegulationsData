## [Provision of Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673357 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be provided

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 604(4) and 605(1) of the [Insurance Companies
Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8), the prescribed information is that the person
may contact the Agency

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} at its office at 427 Laurier Ave. West, 6th Floor,
  Ottawa, Ontario K1R 1B9; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} through its website at
  [www.fcac-acfc.gc.ca](http://www.fcac-acfc.gc.ca).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-62, s. 1
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Manner of providing information

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 605(1) of the [Insurance Companies Act](/eng/acts/I-11.8),
the prescribed manner of providing the information referred to in
section 1 is by providing it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in a brochure, statement of account or written
  statement that contains other information that is required, under that
  Act, to be disclosed in respect of an arrangement referred to in
  subsection 601(4) of that Act, a payment, credit or charge card, the
  cost of borrowing or any other obligation of the foreign company under
  a consumer provision; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a separate document.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-62, s. 1
