## [Total Assets]{.HTitleText1} {#h-676577 .Part}

Definition of [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsections 54.1(4), 386(3) and 895(3) of the [Bank
Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01), [[total assets]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
of a bank or a bank holding company, at a particular time, means the
total value of the assets that would be reported on its balance sheet,
prepared as at that time in accordance with the accounting principles
and specifications of the Superintendant referred to in
subsection 308(4) or 840(4), as the case may be, of that Act.
