## Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction {#civilian-crews-on-auxiliary-vessels-direction .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1046
:::

[NATIONAL DEFENCE ACT](/eng/acts/N-5)

Direction Made Pursuant to Section 224 of the National Defence Act
Respecting Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483543 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Direction may be cited
as the Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-483545 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** It is hereby directed that
the Government Vessels Discipline Act shall apply to civilian crews
engaged for service on auxiliary vessels of the Canadian Forces.
