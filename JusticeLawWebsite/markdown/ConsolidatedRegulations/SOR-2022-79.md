## [Definitions and Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343229 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[1985 Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[1985 Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits
    Standards Regulations, 1985](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-19) ([Règlement
    de 1985]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Pension Benefits Standards
    Act, 1985](/eng/acts/P-7.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[solvency special payment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a special
    payment required under paragraph 9(4)(c) or (d) of the 1985
    Regulations. ([paiement spécial de solvabilité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Defined benefit plan

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
the Canada Post Corporation's defined benefit plan in respect of which
certificate of registration number 57136 has been issued by the
Superintendent under the Act.

## [Payment Relief]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343237 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvency special payments

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Despite paragraphs 9(4)(c)
and (d) of the 1985 Regulations, no solvency special payment is required
in respect of a plan year ending after the coming into force of these
Regulations.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvency ratio

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Despite subsections 9.3(1)
and (3) of the 1985 Regulations, the prescribed solvency ratio level for
the purposes of paragraphs 10.1(2)(c) and (d) of the Act is 1.05.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The following information
is prescribed, in addition to the information referred to in subsections
23(1) and (1.1) of the 1985 Regulations, for the purposes of
subparagraphs 28(1)(b)(iv) and (b.1)(ii) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the plan's solvency deficiency as shown
  in the last actuarial report filed with the Superintendent;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any payments that are required to be
  made for the plan year covered by the statement; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the amount of any solvency special payments that, but
  for section 3, would have been required for the plan year.

## [Cessation of Effect]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343247 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}December 31, 2024

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations cease to
have effect on December 31, 2024.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1343250 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
