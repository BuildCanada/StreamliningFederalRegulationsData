## [PART II]{.HLabel1}[Elevating Devices]{.HTitleText1} {#h-901640 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901641 .Subheading}

**[[2.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,
[[elevating device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an escalator, elevator or
other device for moving passengers or freight and that is installed on
rolling stock. (*appareil de levage*)

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-105, s. 5(E)
:::

### [Use and Operation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901645 .Subheading}

**[[2.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.2 .sectionLabel}** No elevating device
shall be used or operated with a load in excess of the load that it was
designed and installed to move safely.

- **[[2.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no
  elevating device shall be used or operated while any safety device
  attached to it is inoperative.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), no safety device attached
  to an elevating device shall be altered, interfered with or rendered
  inoperative.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsections (1) and (2) do not apply to an elevating
  device or a safety device that is being inspected, tested, repaired or
  maintained by a qualified person.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2021-122, s. 12(E)](#1299291-1300155){.wb-lbx
  ajax-after="1299291-1300155.html"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2.3-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 2.3"){.PITLink}
:::

### [Inspection and Testing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901651 .Subheading}

**[[2.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.4 .sectionLabel}** Every elevating device
and every safety device attached thereto shall be inspected and tested
by a qualified person to determine that it is in a safe operating
condition and is capable of carrying any load that is likely to be
imposed on it

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} before the elevating device and the safety device
  attached to it are operated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} after an alteration to the elevating device or a
  safety device attached thereto; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} once every 12 months.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2019-246, s. 157(F)](#1177765-1189029){.wb-lbx
  ajax-after="1177765-1189029.html"}
- [SOR/2021-122, s. 13(E)](#1299293-1300157){.wb-lbx
  ajax-after="1299293-1300157.html"}
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-2.4-20190625.html "Link to previous version of section 2.4"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[2.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  record of each inspection and test made in accordance with section 2.4
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the qualified person who made the
    inspection and test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include the date of the inspection and test and the
    identification and location of the elevating device and safety
    device that were inspected and tested; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} set out the observations of the qualified person
    inspecting and testing the elevating device and safety device on the
    safety of the devices.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every record referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  kept by the employer for a period of two years after the date on which
  it is signed in accordance with paragraph (1)(a).

### [Repair and Maintenance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901662 .Subheading}

**[[2.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2.6 .sectionLabel}** Repair and maintenance
of elevating devices and safety devices attached thereto shall be
performed by a qualified person appointed by the employer.

### [Lighting --- VDT]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901698 .Subheading}

- **[[3.4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.4.1
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The average level of lighting at a
  task position set out in Column I of Schedule III to this Part shall
  not be more than the level set out in Column II.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Reflection glare on a VDT screen shall be limited so
  that an employee at a task position is able to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} read every portion of any text displayed on the

### [Emergency Lighting]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901706 .Subheading}

- **[[3.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where there is a failure in the
  lighting system in an area through which an employee passes in
  carrying out procedures referred to in paragraph 13.7(2)(a), emergency
  lighting shall be available in the area for immediate use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The emergency lighting referred to in subsection (1)
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if feasible, operate automatically in the event of
    a failure of the lighting system; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide an average level of lighting of not less
    than 10 lx.

## [PART IV]{.HLabel1}[Levels of Sound]{.HTitleText1} {#h-901744 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901745 .Subheading}

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[A-weighted sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[A-weighted sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    sound pressure level as determined by a measurement system which
    includes an A-weighting filter that meets the requirements set out
    in the International Electrotechnical Commission's International
    Standard IEC 61672-1, first edition 2002-05, entitled
    Electroacoustics -- Sound Level Meters. ([niveau de pression
    acoustique pondérée A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dBA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dBA]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means decibel A-weighted and is a unit
    of A-weighted sound pressure level. ([dBA]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[noise exposure level (L~ex,8~)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[noise exposure level (L~ex,8~)]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the
    level equal to 10 times the logarithm to the base 10 of the time
    integral over any 24-hour period of a squared A-weighted sound
    pressure divided by 8, the reference sound pressure being 20 µPa.
    ([niveau d'exposition (L~ex,8~)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sound level meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sound level meter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an instrument for
    measuring levels of sound and impulse sound that meets the
    requirements set out in the International Electrotechnical
    Commission's International Standard IEC 61672-1, first edition
    2002-05, entitled Electroacoustics -- Sound Level Meters.
    ([sonomètre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sound pressure level]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the level equal
    to 20 times the logarithm to the base 10 of the ratio of the root
    mean square pressure of a sound to the reference sound pressure of
    20 µPa, expressed in decibels. ([niveau de pression
    acoustique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Hazard Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901764 .Subheading}

- **[[4.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an employee in a work place may be exposed to an A-weighted sound
  pressure level of 84 dBA or more for a duration that is likely to
  endanger the employee's hearing, the employer shall, without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
    investigation of the degree of exposure; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the work place committee or the health and
    safety representative of the investigation and the qualified
    person's name.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection (1), the measurement
  of the A-weighted sound pressure level in a work place shall be
  performed instantaneously, during normal working conditions, using the
  slow response setting of a sound level meter.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In the investigation referred to in subsection (1),
  the following matters shall be considered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sources of sound in the work place;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the A-weighted sound pressure levels to which the
    employee is likely to be exposed and the duration of such exposure;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the methods being used to reduce this exposure;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} whether the exposure of the employee is likely to
    exceed the limits set out in section 4.4; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the employee is likely to be exposed to a
    noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) of 84 dBA or more.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On completing the investigation and after consulting
  with the work place committee or the health and safety representative,
  the qualified person shall make, sign and date a written report that
  contains their

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} observations respecting the matters set out in
    subsection (3);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} recommendations respecting the measures that should
    be taken in order to comply with sections 4.4 to 4.8; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} recommendations respecting the use of hearing
    protectors by employees who are exposed to a noise exposure level
    (L~ex,8~) of 84 dBA or more but not more than 87 dBA.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep the report at a location that
  is accessible to affected employees for a period of 10 years after the
  date of the report.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the report states that an employee is likely to be
  exposed to a noise exposure level (L~ex,8~) of 84 dBA or more, the
  employer shall, without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post and keep posted a copy of the report in a
    conspicuous place accessible to the employee; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the employee with written information that
    describes the hazards associated with exposure to high levels of
    sound.

## [PART V]{.HLabel1}[Electrical Safety]{.HTitleText1} {#h-901827 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901828 .Subheading}

[[control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[control device]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a device that will
    safely disconnect electrical equipment from its source of energy;
    ([dispositif de commande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[guarded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[guarded]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of electrical
    equipment, equipment that is

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} covered, shielded, enclosed or otherwise
      protected in a manner that prevents injury to an employee who
      touches or goes near the equipment, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a place inaccessible to employees.
      ([protégé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

### [Safety Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901834 .Subheading}

- **[[5.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.2
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where work, other than the work
  referred to in subsection (2), is performed on electrical equipment,
  the employer shall adopt and implement Part VIII of the [Canada
  Occupational Health and Safety
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-304).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where an employee is required to throw switches,
  change light bulbs or fuses or perform other work that requires no
  electrical training, the following procedures apply:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where electrical equipment is live or may become
    live, no employee shall work on the equipment unless the employer
    has instructed the employee in procedures that are safe for work on
    live conductors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (c), where an employee is
    working on or near electrical equipment that is live or may become
    live, the electrical equipment shall be guarded; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if it is not feasible for electrical equipment
    referred to in paragraph (b) to be guarded, the employer shall take
    measures to protect the employee from injury by insulating the
    equipment from the employee or the employee from the ground.

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-901992 .Subheading}

**[[7.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[airborne asbestos fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airborne asbestos fibres]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means asbestos
    fibres that are longer than 5 μm (micrometres) with an aspect ratio
    equal to or greater than 3:1 and that are carried by the
    air; ([fibres d'amiante aéroportées]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asbestos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asbestos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means actinolite, amosite,
    anthophyllite, chrysotile, crocidolite and tremolite in their
    fibrous form; ([amiante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asbestos-containing material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asbestos-containing material]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any article that is manufactured and contains 1%
      or more asbestos by weight at the time of manufacture or that
      contains a concentration of 1% or more asbestos as determined in
      accordance with Method 9002 set out in the document entitled NIOSH
      Manual of Analytical Methods, published by the National Institute
      for Occupational Safety and Health, as amended from time to time,
      or in accordance with a scientifically proven method used to
      collect and analyze a representative sample of the material, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any material that contains a concentration of 1%
      or more asbestos as determined in accordance with Method 9002 set
      out in the document entitled NIOSH Manual of Analytical Methods,
      published by the National Institute for Occupational Safety and
      Health, as amended from time to time, or in accordance with a
      scientifically proven method used to collect and analyze a
      representative sample of the material; ([matériau contenant de
      l'amiante]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[clearance air sampling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[clearance air sampling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the action of
    taking samples to determine if the concentration of airborne
    asbestos fibres inside an enclosure is below the limit referred to
    in section 7.20 to permit the dismantling of a containment
    system; ([échantillonnage de l'air après
    décontamination]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[containment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[containment system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an isolation system
    that is designed to effectively contain asbestos fibre within a
    designated work area where asbestos-containing material is handled,
    removed, encapsulated or enclosed; ([système de
    confinement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[encapsulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[encapsulation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the treatment of an
    asbestos-containing material with a sealant that penetrates the
    material and binds the asbestos fibres together, and the treatment
    of the surface of the asbestos-containing material with a sealant
    that creates a membrane on the surface, to prevent the release of
    asbestos fibres into the air; ([encapsulation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[enclosure]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical barrier such as
    drywall, plywood or metal sheeting that, as part of the containment
    system, isolates asbestos-containing material from adjacent areas in
    a building to prevent the release of airborne asbestos fibres into
    those areas; ([encloisonnement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[friable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[friable]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of
    asbestos-containing material, that the material, when dry, can be
    easily crumbled or powdered by hand
    pressure; ([friable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[glove bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[glove bag]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a polyethylene or polyvinyl
    chloride bag that is affixed around an asbestos-containing source
    and that permits asbestos-containing material to be removed while
    minimizing the release of airborne asbestos fibres into the work
    place; ([sac à gants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazard information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazard information]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a
    hazardous substance, information on the proper and safe storage,
    handling, use and disposal of the hazardous substance, including
    information relating to the health and physical hazards that it
    presents; ([renseignements sur les risques]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[HEPA filter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[HEPA filter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a high-efficiency
    particulate air filter that has been tested to ensure efficiency
    equal to or exceeding 99.97% for removal of airborne particles
    having a mean aerodynamic diameter of 0.3 µm (micrometres) from the
    air; ([filtre HEPA]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[high-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[high-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that
    involves the handling or disturbance of friable asbestos-containing
    material or is carried out in proximity to friable
    asbestos-containing material, that requires a high level of control
    to prevent exposure to excessive concentrations of airborne asbestos
    fibres and that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal or disturbance of more than 1 m^2^ of
      friable asbestos-containing material in a work place, even if the
      activity is divided into smaller jobs,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the spray application of a sealant to a friable
      asbestos-containing material,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning or removal of air-handling
      equipment, other than filters, in a building that has sprayed-on
      fireproofing or sprayed-on thermal insulation that is
      asbestos-containing material,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the repair, alteration or demolition of all or
      part of a kiln, metallurgical furnace or similar structure that
      contains asbestos-containing material,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the activity is carried out by means of power tools
      that are not attached to dust-collecting devices equipped with
      HEPA filters, and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the repair, alteration or demolition of all or
      part of a building in which asbestos is or was used in the
      manufacture of products, unless the asbestos was cleaned up and
      removed; ([activité à risque élevé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[lower explosive limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[lower explosive limit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the lower limit
    of flammability of a chemical agent or a combination of chemical
    agents at ambient temperature and pressure, expressed

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for a gas or vapour, as a percentage in air by
      volume, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for dust, as the weight of dust per volume of
      air; ([limite explosive inférieure]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[low-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[low-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity that
    involves the handling of asbestos-containing material or is carried
    out in proximity to non-friable asbestos-containing material and
    that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the installation or removal of ceiling tiles that
      are made of non-friable asbestos-containing material and cover an
      area of less than 7.5 m^2^,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the installation or removal of other non-friable
      asbestos-containing material, if the material is not being broken,
      cut, drilled, abraded, ground, sanded or vibrated and dust is not
      being generated,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the material is wetted to control the spread of dust
      or fibres and the activity is carried out only by means of
      non-powered hand-held tools, and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the removal of less than 1 m² of drywall in which
      joint cement containing asbestos has been used; ([activité à
      faible risque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[moderate-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[moderate-risk activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an activity
    that involves the handling of asbestos-containing material or is
    carried out in proximity to friable asbestos-containing material,
    that is not otherwise classified as a low-risk activity or high-risk
    activity and that includes

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the removal of all or part of a false ceiling to
      gain access to a work area, if asbestos-containing material is
      likely to be found on the surface of the false ceiling,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the removal or disturbance of 1 m^2^ or less of
      friable asbestos-containing material during repair, alteration,
      maintenance or demolition work in a work place,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the enclosure of friable asbestos-containing
      material,

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the application of tape, sealant or other
      covering to pipe or boiler insulation that is asbestos-containing
      material,

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the removal of ceiling tiles that are
      asbestos-containing material, if the tiles cover an area of
      greater than 2 m^2^ and are removed without being broken, cut,
      drilled, abraded, ground, sanded or vibrated,

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the material is not wetted to control the spread of
      dust or fibres and the activity is carried out only by means of
      non-powered hand-held tools,

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the removal of 1 m^2^ or more of drywall in which
      joint cement that is asbestos-containing material has been used,

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the breaking, cutting, drilling, abrading,
      grinding, sanding or vibrating of non-friable asbestos-containing
      material, if the activity is carried out by means of power tools
      that are attached to dust-collecting devices equipped with HEPA
      filters,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the removal of insulation that is
      asbestos-containing material from a pipe, duct or similar
      structure using a glove bag, and

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the cleaning or removal of filters used in
      air-handling equipment in a building that has sprayed-on
      fireproofing that is asbestos-containing material; ([activité à
      risque modéré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[product identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product identifier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 1(1) of the [Hazardous Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17); ([identificateur de
    produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[readily available]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[readily available]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed,
    SOR/2025-79, s. 10\]]{.Repealed}

[[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section
    2 of the [Hazardous Products
    Act](/eng/acts/H-3); ([fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[work activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any low-risk activity,
    moderate-risk activity or high-risk activity or any activity that is
    ancillary to that activity, and the supervision of that activity and
    that ancillary activity. ([activité de travail]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

#### [Hazard Investigation]{.HTitleText3} {#h-902050 .Subheading}

- **[[7.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  the health or safety of an employee is likely to be endangered by
  exposure to a hazardous substance in a work place, the employer shall,
  without delay,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
    investigation in that regard; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notify the work place committee or the health and
    safety representative of the investigation and the qualified
    person's name.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the investigation, the following matters shall be
  considered:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the chemical, biological and physical properties of
    the hazardous substance;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the routes of exposure to the hazardous substance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the acute and chronic effects on health of exposure
    to the hazardous substance;

#### [Control of Hazards]{.HTitleText3} {#h-902159 .Subheading}

- **[[7.20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  employee shall be exposed to a concentration of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an airborne chemical agent, other than airborne
    asbestos fibres, in excess of the value for that chemical agent
    adopted by the American Conference of Governmental Industrial
    Hygienists in its publication entitled Threshold Limit Values (TLVs)
    and Biological Exposure Indices (BEIs), as amended from time to
    time; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an airborne hazardous substance, other than a
    chemical agent, that is hazardous to the health or safety of the
    employee.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall ensure that an employee's
  exposure to a concentration of airborne asbestos fibres is as close to
  zero as possible, but in any event the employer shall ensure that the
  concentration does not exceed the value for airborne asbestos fibres
  adopted by the American Conference of Governmental Industrial
  Hygienists in its publication entitled Threshold Limit Values (TLV)
  and Biological Exposure Indices (BEI), as amended from time to time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the concentration of an airborne chemical agent is
  likely to exceed the value referred to in paragraph (1)(a), or the
  concentration of airborne asbestos fibres is likely to exceed zero or
  that there is a concentration of an airborne hazardous substance that
  is hazardous to the health and safety of the employee, air samples
  shall be taken by a qualified person and the concentration of the
  chemical agent, airborne asbestos fibres or hazardous substance shall
  be determined

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with the standards set out in the
    United States National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
    publication entitled NIOSH Manual of Analytical Methods,
    5th edition, dated 2003;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in accordance with a method used to collect and
    analyze a representative sample of the chemical agent with accuracy
    and with detection levels at least equal to those which would be
    obtained if the standards referred to in paragraph (a) were used; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if no specific standards for the chemical agent are
    set out in the publication referred to in paragraph (a) and no
    method that meets the requirements of paragraph (b) exists, in
    accordance with a scientifically proven method used to collect and
    analyze a representative sample of the chemical agent.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A record of each test made pursuant to subsection (2)
  shall be kept by the employer for three years after the date of the
  test.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A record referred to in subsection (3) shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date, time and location of the test;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the chemical agent for which the test was made;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sampling and testing method used;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the result obtained; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and occupation of the qualified person who
    made the test.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} signs to advise employees of the hazards and required

##### [Asbestos Dust, Waste and Debris Removal]{.HTitleText4} {#h-902228 .Subheading}

- **[[7.23.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.23.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} During any work activities that
  involve friable asbestos-containing materials, an employer shall
  ensure that the following activities are carried out frequently and at
  regular intervals as determined by a qualified person, at the end of
  each work shift and immediately after the work is completed:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all asbestos dust, waste and debris are removed by
    vacuuming with a vacuum cleaner that is equipped with a HEPA filter,
    damp-mopping or wet-sweeping the area that is contaminated with the
    asbestos dust, waste or debris; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any drop sheets that are contaminated with asbestos
    dust, waste or debris are wetted.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} All asbestos dust, waste or debris and any drop
  sheets that are contaminated with asbestos dust, waste or debris shall
  be placed in a container referred to in section 7.23.11.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2017-132, s. 8
:::

**[[7.23.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.23.5 .sectionLabel}** If a glove bag is
used for the removal of asbestos insulation from pipes, ducts and
similar structures, an employer shall ensure that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is sealed to prevent the release of
  asbestos fibres into the work area;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is inspected for damage or defects
  immediately before it is attached to the pipe, duct or similar
  structure and at regular intervals during its use;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} all waste from asbestos-containing material that is
  on surfaces is washed to the bottom of the glove bag and all exposed
  asbestos-containing material is encapsulated when it is inside the
  glove bag;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the glove bag is evacuated using a vacuum cleaner
  that is equipped with a HEPA filter to remove the air inside the bag
  prior to the removal of the glove bag; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} after the glove bag is removed, all exposed surfaces
  are cleaned with a damp cloth and a vacuum cleaner that is equipped
  with a HEPA filter.

##### [Decontamination]{.HTitleText4} {#h-902244 .Subheading}

- **[[7.23.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.23.6
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before leaving a work area that is
  contaminated with asbestos-containing material, an employee shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if their protective clothing is to be reused,

    that is equipped with a HEPA filter before taking the clothing off;

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall provide employees with a facility

  their hands and face using that facility before leaving a work area
  that is contaminated with asbestos-containing material.

##### [Air Sampling]{.HTitleText4} {#h-902255 .Subheading}

- **[[7.23.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.23.8
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall ensure that a
  qualified person takes air samples to test for airborne asbestos
  fibres

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the vicinity of the containment system during
    any work activity that involves asbestos-containing material and, in
    the case of a work activity that lasts longer than 24 hours, at
    least daily;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the clean room during removal and clean-up
    operations and, in the case of removal and clean-up operations that
    last longer than 24 hours, at least daily; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in contaminated areas that are inside the
    containment system as necessary during removal and clean-up
    operations.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall ensure that the following air
  samples are taken:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} two samples for every area in an enclosure that is
    10 m^2^ or less;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} three samples for every area in an enclosure that
    is more than 10 m^2^ and not more than 500 m^2^; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} five samples for every area in an enclosure that is
    more than 500 m^2^.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Within 24 hours after obtaining the air sampling test
  results, the employer shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} post a copy of the results in a conspicuous place
    in the work place; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} make the results available to the policy committee,
    if any, the work place committee and the health and safety
    representative.

##### [Clearance Air Sampling]{.HTitleText4} {#h-902270 .Subheading}

- **[[7.23.9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.23.9
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Before dismantling a containment
  system and after all asbestos dust, waste and debris have been cleaned
  up, removed or encapsulated, an employer shall ensure that clearance
  air samples are taken inside the enclosure and that the concentration
  of airborne asbestos fibres is determined in accordance with Method
  7400 set out in the document entitled NIOSH Manual of Analytical
  Methods, published by the National Institute for Occupational Safety
  and Health, as amended from time to time, or in accordance with a
  scientifically proven method used to collect and analyze a
  representative sample of airborne asbestos fibres.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} When conducting clearance air sampling, the employer
  shall ensure that forced air is used inside the enclosure to dislodge
  any asbestos fibres from all surfaces and keep them airborne.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Clearance air sampling shall be taken until the
  concentrations of airborne asbestos fibres do not exceed the values
  referred to in subsection 7.20(1.1).

**[[7.26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7.26 .sectionLabel}** The following

:   [[bulk shipment]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 5.5(1) of the [Hazardous Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17). ([expédition en
    vrac]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any package or receptacle,
    including a bag, barrel, bottle, box, can, cylinder, drum and
    storage tank. ([contenant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hazardous waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hazardous waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a hazardous product
    that is acquired or generated for recycling or recovery or is
    intended for disposal. ([résidu dangereux]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[significant new data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[significant new data]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 5.12(1) of the [Hazardous Products
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2015-17). ([nouvelles données
    importantes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplier label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label prepared by a
    supplier that discloses any information elements required by the
    [Hazardous Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3). ([étiquette du
    fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[supplier safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[supplier safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a safety
    data sheet prepared by a supplier that discloses any information
    elements required by the [Hazardous Products Act](/eng/acts/H-3).
    ([fiche de données de sécurité du fournisseur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[work place label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work place label]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a label prepared by
    an employer in accordance with this Division. ([étiquette du lieu de
    travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[work place safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[work place safety data sheet]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a safety
    data sheet prepared by an employer in accordance with subsection
    7.30(1). ([fiche de données de sécurité du lieu de
    travail]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- **[[10.21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10.21
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every machine on rolling stock that

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes the machine inoperative if the employee or
    any part of his clothing is in or near a part of the machine that is
    likely to cause injury.

  subsection (1) shall not be removable.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A machine guard shall be so constructed, installed
  and maintained that it meets the requirements of subsection (1).

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902646 .Subheading}

**[[11.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.1 .sectionLabel}** In this Part,

[[disabling injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disabling injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employment injury
    or an occupational illness that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} prevents an employee from reporting for work or
      from effectively performing all the duties connected with the
      employee's regular work on any day subsequent to the day on which
      the injury or illness occurred, whether or not that subsequent day
      is a working day for that employee,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} results in the loss by an employee of a body
      member or part of one or in the complete loss of the usefulness of
      a body member or part of one, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} results in the permanent impairment of a body
      function of an employee; ([blessure invalidante]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[minor injury]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an employment injury or
    an occupational illness for which medical treatment is provided and
    excludes a disabling injury. ([blessure légère]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

### [Investigation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902659 .Subheading}

- **[[11.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  an employer becomes aware of an accident, occupational illness or
  other hazardous occurrence that affects an employee in the course of
  their work, the employer shall without delay

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} take necessary measures to prevent a recurrence of
    the hazardous occurrence;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} appoint a qualified person to carry out an
    investigation of the hazardous occurrence; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} report the occurrence and the name of the person
    appointed to investigate it to the work place committee or the
    health and safety representative.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the hazardous occurrence referred to in
  subsection (1) is an accident that involves a motor vehicle on a
  public road and that is investigated by a police authority,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigation shall be carried out by obtaining
    from that police authority a copy of its report respecting the
    accident; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as soon as feasible after receipt of the report,
    the employer shall provide a copy of the report to the work place
    committee or the health and safety representative.

## [PART XII]{.HLabel1}[First Aid]{.HTitleText1} {#h-902738 .Part}

### [Interpretation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902739 .Subheading}

**[[12.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.1 .sectionLabel}** The following
definitions apply in this Part.

[[approved organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approved organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an organization
    that is approved by any province for the teaching of first aid.
    ([organisme agréé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first aid certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first aid certificate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the certificate
    issued by either a qualified person or the organization that
    developed the training, as the case may be, for successful
    completion of a first aid course of at least one day that includes
    the subjects set out in subsection 12.7(1). ([certificat de
    secourisme]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[first aid station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[first aid station]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a place at which
    first aid supplies or equipment are stored. ([poste de
    secours]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/88-200, s. 13(E)
- SOR/2015-143, s. 67

### [First Aid Stations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902746 .Subheading}

- **[[12.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer shall ensure that there is at least one first aid station
  located in every work place and that every first aid station is
  clearly identified by a conspicuous sign and is readily available and
  accessible during all working hours.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall inspect every first aid station at
  least monthly and ensure that its contents are maintained in a clean,
  dry and serviceable condition.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/95-105, s. 55(F)

### [Communication of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902752 .Subheading}

**[[12.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.3 .sectionLabel}** The employer shall
post and keep posted, or have readily available, in a conspicuous place
accessible to every employee in each work place

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a description of the first aid to be rendered for any
  injury or illness, including an occupational illness;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the location of first aid stations; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transport procedures for employees who have an injury
  or illness, including an occupational illness.

- **[[12.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.4
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall provide, for
  every first aid station, a first aid kit that includes the first aid
  supplies and equipment set out in the schedule to this Part and shall
  replenish the kit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Medication shall not be stored in first aid kits.

  there is a hazard of skin or eye injury because of the presence of a
  hazardous substance in a work place, the employer shall ensure that
  shower and eye-wash facilities are readily available and accessible to
  employees.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If it is not feasible to comply with subsection (1),

### [Emergency Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902771 .Subheading}

**[[12.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.6 .sectionLabel}** Before assigning
employees to a work place, the employer shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} ensure that, for that work place, there is an
  ambulance service or other suitable means of transporting an employee
  who has an injury or illness, including an occupational illness, to a
  hospital, medical clinic or physician's office, at which emergency
  medical treatment can be dispensed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide for that work place a means of quickly
  summoning the ambulance service or other suitable means of
  transportation.

### [Training]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902777 .Subheading}

- **[[12.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer shall provide their employees with a one-day first aid course
  that includes the following subjects and that is given by a qualified
  person who holds a valid certification from an approved organization
  attesting that the person is competent to deliver first aid training:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the provision of basic first aid and the employee's
    role and obligations in relation to basic first aid;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} emergency scene management, including
    anti-contamination procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cardiopulmonary resuscitation;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} medical emergencies;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} shock and unconsciousness; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} wounds and bleeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An employer shall ensure that all employees hold a
  valid first aid certificate.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} First aid certificates are, for the purposes of this
  Part, valid for a maximum of three years from the date of issue.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-143, s. 67
:::

### [Records]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902790 .Subheading}

- **[[12.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12.8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employee who renders first aid in accordance with this Part shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} enter the following information in a first aid
    record:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date and time that the injury or illness,
      including an occupational illness, was reported,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the full name of the employee who received first
      aid,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which, and the time and location at
      which, the first aid was rendered,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the injury or illness,
      including an occupational illness,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the first aid rendered,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a brief description of the arrangements made for
      the treatment or transportation of the employee who received first
      aid, and

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the names of witnesses, if applicable;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} sign the first aid record beneath the information
    entered; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} store the first aid record in the first aid kit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep the first aid record for a
  period of two years beginning on the day of the last entry.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Persons who have access to first aid records shall
  keep the information that is contained in the records confidential,
  except as required for the purposes of meeting reporting obligations
  under Part XI.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receiving a written request from a workers'
  compensation authority for the province where the work place is
  located or from a physician, the employer shall provide an employee
  with a copy of the first aid record pertaining to the employee's
  treatment.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The employer shall keep a record of the expiry date
  of each employee's first aid certificate and shall make the record
  readily available to them.

### [Fire Extinguishers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902826 .Subheading}

- **[[13.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer shall install at least one portable fire extinguisher in
  rolling stock, other than rolling stock that is used to transport

### [Emergency Procedures]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902844 .Subheading}

- **[[13.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  employer shall, after consulting with the work place committee or the
  health and safety representative, prepare emergency procedures to be
  implemented

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if any person commits or threatens to commit an
    act, other than an occurrence of harassment and violence, that may
    be hazardous to the health or safety of the employer or any
    employees;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where there is a possibility of an accumulation,
    spill or leak of a hazardous substance in a work place controlled by
    the employer, in the event of such an accumulation, spill or leak;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the event of an accident involving a train; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the event of the failure of the lighting system
    of a train.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The emergency procedures referred to in
  subsection (1) shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a full description of the procedures to be

### [Moving and Coupling]{.HTitleText2} {#h-902856 .Subheading}

## [PART XIV]{.HLabel1}[Hazard Prevention Program]{.HTitleText1} {#h-902864 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Despite section 1.4 of
the [Canada Occupational Health and Safety
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-304), Part XIX of those Regulations
applies in respect of employees on trains while in operation and in
respect of all persons granted access to such trains by the employer,
with the following modifications:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "section 15.3" in paragraphs
  19.3(1)(e), 19.4(f) and 19.6(1)(c) is to be read as a reference to
  section 11.2 of these Regulations; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the reference to "these Regulations" in paragraph
  19.6(1)(d) is to be read as a reference to these Regulations.
