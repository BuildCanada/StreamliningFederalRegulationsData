## Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Infrastructure and Communities) to the Minister of Transport the Control and Supervision of the Office of Infrastructure of Canada {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-of-state-infrastructure-and-communities-to-the-minister-of-transport-the-control-and-supervision-of-the-office-of-infrastructure-of-canada .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2006-22
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2006-02-22

Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Infrastructure and
Communities) to the Minister of Transport the Control and Supervision of
the Office of Infrastructure of Canada

P.C. 2006-60 2006-02-06
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBBA} of the
  [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
  Act](/eng/acts/P-34), transfers from the Minister of State styled
  Minister of State (Infrastructure and Communities) to the Minister of
  Transport the control and supervision of the portion of the federal
  public administration known as the Office of Infrastructure of Canada,
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e-ID0EDBBBA){#footnotea_e}S.C.
    2003, c. 22, s. 207
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} orders that the Minister of Transport shall preside
  over the Office of Infrastructure of Canada,

effective February 6, 2006.
