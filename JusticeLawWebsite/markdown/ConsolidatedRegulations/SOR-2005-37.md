## [Approved Containers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-721124 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following containers,
being containers of a kind that is designed to receive a sample of blood
of a person for analysis, are hereby approved as suitable, in respect of
blood samples, for the purposes of section 258 of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46):

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Vacutainer® XF947;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} BD Vacutainer™ 367001;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Vacutainer® 367001;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Tri-Tech Inc. TUG10;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} BD Vacutainer® REF 367001; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} TRITECHFORENSICS TUG10.
