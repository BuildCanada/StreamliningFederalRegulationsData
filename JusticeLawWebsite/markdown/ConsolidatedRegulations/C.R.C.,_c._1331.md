## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503564 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Formal Documents
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1331).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503566 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Privy Seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Privy Seal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the seal adopted by the
    Governor General or the Administrator for the sealing of official
    documents that are to be signed by him, or with his authority by his
    deputy, and that do not require to be sealed with the Great Seal;
    ([sceau privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[permanent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[permanent]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any commission,
    board or corporation, means established without any limitation of
    time as to its continuance; ([permanent]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[regular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[regular]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means appointed on a permanent
    basis; ([régulier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[temporary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to any commission,
    board or corporation, means established for a specified period of
    time or until the occurrence of a stated event;
    ([temporaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[temporary or ad hoc]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[temporary or ad hoc]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means appointed
    specially for a specified short period of time or until the
    occurrence of a stated event. ([temporaire ou spécial (ad
    hoc)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503573 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  document under seal issued in accordance with these Regulations shall
  bear the signature of the person holding the office indicated in each
  case, or the signature of the acting holder of such office, except
  that any such document required to be signed by the deputy head of a
  department may in any case be signed by the head or the acting head of
  such department.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in these Regulations shall be construed as
  requiring the issuance of a Commission to any person on re-appointment
  to an office unless the commission constitutes the instrument of
  re-appointment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any document in respect of the appointment of the
  Attorney General or the Minister of Industry, or in respect of the
  appointment of a person holding one of those offices to concurrently
  hold another office, that is required by these Regulations to be
  issued under seal and to be signed by the Attorney General or
  Registrar General may be signed by the Prime Minister.
