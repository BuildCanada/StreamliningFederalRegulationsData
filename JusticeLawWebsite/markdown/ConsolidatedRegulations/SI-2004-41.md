## Order Respecting the Remission of Equalization Overpayments Made to Certain Provinces under the Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements Act {#order-respecting-the-remission-of-equalization-overpayments-made-to-certain-provinces-under-the-federal-provincial-fiscal-arrangements-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2004-41
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2004-04-21

Order Respecting the Remission of Equalization Overpayments Made to
Certain Provinces under the Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements Act

P.C. 2004-356  2004-03-30
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of Finance and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2004-41_e_hq_5148){#fn_SI-2004-41_e_hq_5148-ID0EFBAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), hereby makes the
annexed Order Respecting the Remission of Equalization Overpayments Made
to Certain Provinces under the Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements
Act.

  :::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-611230 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission is hereby granted
to the province set out in column 1 of the Schedule for the amount set
out in column 2 in respect of equalization overpayments made by the
Government of Canada under the [Federal-Provincial Fiscal Arrangements
Act](/eng/acts/F-7.8), for the fiscal years beginning April 1, 1993 and
ending March 31, 2001, by reason of the overstatement of the amount of
mutual fund trust capital gains refunds in the calculation of
equalization payments.
