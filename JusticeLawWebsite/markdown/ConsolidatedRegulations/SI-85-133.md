## Investigation Drugs, Placebos and Emergency Drugs Remission Order {#investigation-drugs-placebos-and-emergency-drugs-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-133
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-08-07

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duty on Investigation Drugs,
Placebos and Emergency Drugs

P.C. 1985-2279 1985-07-24
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, is pleased hereby, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Finance and the Treasury Board,
pursuant to section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EFBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), to make the annexed
[Order respecting the remission of customs duty on investigation drugs,
placebos and emergency drugs](/eng/regulations/SI-85-133).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639648 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Investigation Drugs, Placebos and Emergency Drugs Remission
Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-133).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639650 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[emergency drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[emergency drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means new drugs for
    emergency treatment referred to in section C.08.010, Division 8 of
    the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870);
    ([drogue pour traitement d'urgence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[investigation drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[investigation drugs]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means new drugs referred
    to in section C.08.005, Division 8 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), and includes
    radio-pharmaceuticals; ([drogue expérimentale]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[placebos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[placebos]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means placebos imported with
    investigation drugs for use in studies involving the investigation
    drugs. ([placebo]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639655 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) with respect to investigation
drugs, placebos and emergency drugs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/88-17, s. 2
:::

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639659 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Remission under section 3
is granted on the following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the investigation drugs, placebos or emergency drugs
  are imported on or after January 1, 1985;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made to the Minister of
  National Revenue within two years after the date of importation of the
  investigation drugs, placebos and emergency drugs for which remission
  is claimed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any person claiming remission pursuant to this Order
  shall provide such reports and information as the Minister of National
  Revenue may require for the administration of this Order.
