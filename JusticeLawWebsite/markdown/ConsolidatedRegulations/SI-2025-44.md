[ACCESSIBLE CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/A-0.6)

Registration 2025-03-26

Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development as
the Minister for the Purposes of the Accessible Canada Act

P.C. 2025-424 2025-03-14
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the [Accessible Canada
Act](/eng/acts/A-0.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734725){#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734725-ID0EBDAA},

  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2023-1064 of October
  27, 2023[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734704){#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734704-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734704-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-2849_hq_1741734704}SI/2023-72

  Development, a member of the King's Privy Council for Canada, as the
  Minister for the purposes of that Act.
