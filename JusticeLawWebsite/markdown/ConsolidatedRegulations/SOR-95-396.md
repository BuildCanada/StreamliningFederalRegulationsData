## General Import Permit No. 3 --- Wheat and Wheat Products and Barley and Barley Products for Personal Use {#general-import-permit-no.-3-wheat-and-wheat-products-and-barley-and-barley-products-for-personal-use .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/95-396
:::

[EXPORT AND IMPORT PERMITS ACT](/eng/acts/E-19)

Registration 1995-08-03

General Import Permit No. 3 --- Wheat and Wheat Products and Barley and
Barley Products for Personal Use
::::

:::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
Whereas wheat and wheat products, and barley and barley products have
been included on the [Import Control
List](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._604)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5282){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5282-ID0EBBEA},
C.R.C., c. 604;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5282-ID0EBBEA){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5282}SOR/89-251,
  1989 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2569
  :::

And Whereas the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsection
6.2(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5283){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5283-ID0EDBDA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), has determined
an import access quantity for wheat and wheat products and barley and
barley products;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5283-ID0EDBDA){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5283}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 106
  :::

Therefore, the Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsection
8.3(3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5284){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5284-ID0EFBCA}
of the [Export and Import Permits Act](/eng/acts/E-19), hereby issues
the annexed General permit authorizing the importation for personal use
of wheat and wheat products and barley and barley products.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*\*^](#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5284-ID0EFBCA){#fn_SOR-95-396_e_hq_5284}S.C.
  1994, c. 47, s. 109
  :::

Ottawa, August 3, 1995

::: FlushRight
ANDRÉ OUELLET
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of Foreign Affairs*
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975381 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Permit,

[[barley and barley products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[barley and barley products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods set
    out in items 22 to 31 and 33 of the schedule; ([orge et
    sous-produits de l'orge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[wheat and wheat products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[wheat and wheat products]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods set out
    in items 1 to 21 and 32 of the schedule. ([froment (blé) et
    sous-produits du froment (blé)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-52, s. 1
:::

## [General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975387 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A resident of Canada may,
under the authority of this Permit, import into Canada for the personal
use of the importer and the importer's household wheat and wheat
products and barley and barley products.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where goods imported under
the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the
prescribed form under the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6), that form
shall contain the statement "Imported under the authority of [General
Import Permit No. 3 --- Wheat and Wheat Products and Barley and Barley
Products for Personal Use](/eng/regulations/SOR-95-396)" or "[Importé en
vertu de la]{.otherLang lang="fr"}Licence générale d'importation n^o^ 3
--- Froment (blé) et sous-produits du froment (blé) et orge et
sous-produits de l'orge pour usage personnel".

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-975390 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Item 9
  of the schedule comes into force on the day on which tariff item No.
  1902.11.11 of Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)
  comes into force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Item 19 of the schedule comes into force, in respect
  of goods that are classified under tariff item No. 1905.90.23 of
  Schedule I to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), on the day on
  which that tariff item No. comes into force.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 2)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-975395 .scheduleLabel}
:::

### [Wheat]{.HTitleText1} {#wheat .SchedHeadL1}

- [1]{.lawlabel} Wheat and meslin that are classified under tariff item
  No. 1001.11.10, 1001.19.10, 1001.91.10 or 1001.99.10 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

### [Wheat Products]{.HTitleText1} {#wheat-products .SchedHeadL1}

- [2]{.lawlabel} Wheat or meslin flour that is classified under tariff
  item No. 1101.00.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the
  schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [3]{.lawlabel} Wheat groats, meal and pellets, that are classified
  under tariff item No. 1103.11.10 or 1103.20.11 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [4]{.lawlabel} Wheat grains otherwise worked (for example, hulled,
  rolled, flaked, pearled, sliced or kibbled) that are classified under
  tariff item No. 1104.19.11 or 1104.29.11 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [5]{.lawlabel} Wheat germ, whole, rolled, flaked or ground, that is
  classified under tariff item No. 1104.30.11 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [6]{.lawlabel} Wheat starch that is classified under tariff item No.
  1108.11.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [7]{.lawlabel} Wheat gluten, whether or not dried, that is classified
  under tariff item No. 1109.00.10 in the List of Tariff Provisions set
  out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [8]{.lawlabel} Mixes and doughs, for the preparation of bread, pastry,
  cakes, biscuits and other bakers' wares classified under heading No.
  19.05 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), containing 25 per cent or more
  by weight of wheat, that are classified under tariff item No.
  1901.20.13 or 1901.20.23 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [9]{.lawlabel} Uncooked pasta, containing 25 per cent or more by
  weight of wheat, not stuffed or otherwise prepared, containing eggs,
  that is classified under tariff item No. 1902.11.10 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [10]{.lawlabel} Uncooked pasta, containing flour and water only (where
  the flour content is 25 per cent or more by weight of wheat), that is
  classified under tariff item No. 1902.19.21 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [11]{.lawlabel} Uncooked pasta, containing 25 per cent or more by
  weight of wheat, not stuffed or otherwise prepared, that is classified
  under tariff item No. 1902.19.11 or 1902.19.91 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [12]{.lawlabel} Cooked or pre-cooked pasta, containing 25 per cent or
  more by weight of wheat, not stuffed and without meat, that is
  classified under tariff item No. 1902.30.11 or 1902.30.20 in the List
  of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [13]{.lawlabel} Prepared foods, containing 25 per cent or more by
  weight of wheat, obtained by the swelling or roasting of cereals or
  cereal products, that are classified under tariff item No. 1904.10.10
  in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [13.1]{.lawlabel} Prepared foods, containing 25 per cent or more by
  weight of wheat, obtained from unroasted cereal flakes or from
  mixtures of unroasted cereal flakes and roasted cereal flakes or
  swelled cereals, that are classified under tariff item No. 1904.20.10
  or 1904.20.61 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule
  to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [14]{.lawlabel} Cereals, containing 25 per cent or more by weight of
  wheat, in grain form or in the form of flakes or other worked grains
  (except flour and meal), pre-cooked or otherwise prepared, not
  elsewhere specified or included, that are classified under tariff item
  No. 1904.30.10, 1904.30.61, 1904.90.10 or 1904.90.61 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [15]{.lawlabel} Crispbread, containing 25 per cent or more by weight
  of wheat, that is classified under tariff item No. 1905.10.10,
  1905.10.40 or 1905.10.71 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [16]{.lawlabel} Sweet biscuits, waffles and wafers, containing 25 per
  cent or more by weight of wheat, that are classified under tariff item
  No. 1905.31.21, 1905.31.91 or 1905.32.91 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [17]{.lawlabel} Rusks, toasted bread and similar toasted products,
  containing 25 per cent or more by weight of wheat, that are classified
  under tariff item No. 1905.40.20 or 1905.40.50 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [18]{.lawlabel} Bread (other than bread leavened with yeast and
  unleavened bread for sacramental purposes), containing 25 per cent or
  more by weight of wheat, that is classified under tariff item No.
  1905.90.31 or 1905.90.34 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in
  the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [19]{.lawlabel} Biscuits, containing 25 per cent or more by weight of
  wheat, that are classified under tariff item No. 1905.90.41 or
  1905.90.42 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [20]{.lawlabel} Pretzels, containing 25 per cent or more by weight of
  wheat, that are classified under tariff item No. 1905.90.61 in the
  List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [21]{.lawlabel} Wheat bran, sharps and other residues, whether or not
  in the form of pellets, derived from the sifting, milling or other
  working of wheat, that are classified under tariff item No. 2302.30.10
  in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

### [Barley]{.HTitleText1} {#barley .SchedHeadL1}

- [22]{.lawlabel} Barley that is classified under tariff item
  No. 1003.10.11, 1003.10.91, 1003.90.11 or 1003.90.91 in the List of
  Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

### [Barley Products]{.HTitleText1} {#barley-products .SchedHeadL1}

- [23]{.lawlabel} Barley flour that is classified under tariff item No.
  1102.90.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [24]{.lawlabel} Barley groats, meal and pellets, that are classified
  under tariff item No. 1103.19.11 or 1103.20.21 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [25]{.lawlabel} Barley grains otherwise worked (for example, hulled,
  rolled, flaked, pearled, sliced or kibbled) that are classified under
  tariff item No. 1104.19.21 or 1104.29.21 in the List of Tariff
  Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [26]{.lawlabel} Malt, whether or not roasted, that is classified under
  tariff item No. 1107.10.11, 1107.10.91, 1107.20.11 or 1107.20.91 in
  the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [27]{.lawlabel} Barley starch that is classified under tariff item No.
  1108.19.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [28]{.lawlabel} Malt extract that is classified under tariff item No.
  1901.90.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [29]{.lawlabel} Prepared foods obtained by the swelling or roasting of
  barley that are classified under tariff item No. 1904.10.30 in the
  List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [29.1]{.lawlabel} Prepared foods, of barley, obtained from unroasted
  cereal flakes or from mixtures of unroasted cereal flakes and roasted
  cereal flakes or swelled cereals, that are classified under tariff
  item No. 1904.20.30 or 1904.20.63 in the List of Tariff Provisions set
  out in the schedule to the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [30]{.lawlabel} Barley cereals, in grain form or in the form of flakes
  or other worked grains (except flour and meal), pre-cooked or
  otherwise prepared, not elsewhere specified or included, that are
  classified under tariff item No. 1904.90.30 or 1904.90.63 in the List
  of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
  Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

- [31]{.lawlabel} Barley bran, sharps and other residues, whether or not
  in the form of pellets, derived from the sifting, milling or other
  working of barley, that are classified under tariff item No.
  2302.40.11 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to
  the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011).

### [Goods Classified Under Heading No. 98.04 in the List of Tariff Provisions Set Out in the Schedule to the Customs Tariff]{.HTitleText1} {#goods-classified-under-heading-no.-98.04-in-the-list-of-tariff-provisions-set-out-in-the-schedule-to-the-customs-tariff .SchedHeadL1}

- [32]{.lawlabel} Goods that are classified under heading No. 98.04 or
  98.26 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and that are otherwise
  classifiable under a tariff item referred to in any of items 1 to 21
  of this schedule.

- [33]{.lawlabel} Goods that are classified under heading No. 98.04 or
  98.26 in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
  [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and that are otherwise
  classifiable under a tariff item referred to in any of items 22 to 31
  of this schedule.
