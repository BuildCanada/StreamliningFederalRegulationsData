## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806270 .Part}

Definition of [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Conflict of Interest
Act](/eng/acts/C-36.65).

## [Designations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806273 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public office holders

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (e) of the definition [[public office
holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act, the
following persons are designated as public office holders:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Governor and the Deputy Governor appointed under
  subsection 6(1) of the [Bank of Canada Act](/eng/acts/B-2);

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Directors appointed under subsection 23(1) of the
  [Museums Act](/eng/acts/M-13.4);

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Chief Executive Officer appointed under section
  17 of the [Canadian Centre on Substance Abuse Act](/eng/acts/C-13.4);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the President appointed under subsection 6(1) of the
  [National Arts Centre Act](/eng/acts/N-3); and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the chief executive officers appointed under
  subsection 13(1.1) of the [Pilotage Act](/eng/acts/P-14).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reporting public office holders

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph (f) of the definition [[reporting public office
holder]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 2(1) of the Act, the
following persons are designated as reporting public office holders:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons who, under section 2, are public office
  holders as defined in subsection 2(1) of the Act and exercise their
  official duties and functions on a part-time basis but receive an
  annual salary and benefits; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who, under section 2, are public office
  holders as defined in subsection 2(1) of the Act and exercise their
  official duties and functions on a full-time basis.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-806285 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}30 days after registration

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
30 days after the day on which it is registered.
