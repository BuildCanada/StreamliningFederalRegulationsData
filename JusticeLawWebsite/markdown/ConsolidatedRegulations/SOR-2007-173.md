General Preferential Tariff Withdrawal Order (Bulgaria and Romania)

the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 34(1)(b) of the [Customs
Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote

## [Withdrawal of Entitlement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734924 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Entitlement to the benefit
of the General Preferential Tariff is hereby withdrawn in respect of all
goods that originate in Bulgaria and Romania.

## [Exception]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734926 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Section 1 does not apply to
goods that were in transit to Canada before August 1, 2007.

## [Amendments to the Customs Tariff Schedule]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734928 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** \[Amendment\]

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-734930 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on August 1, 2007.
