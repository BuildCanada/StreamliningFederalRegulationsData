[PROHIBITION ON THE PURCHASE OF RESIDENTIAL PROPERTY BY NON-CANADIANS

Registration 2023-08-16

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the
Minister for the Purposes of the Prohibition on the Purchase of
Residential Property by Non-Canadians Act

:::: section
::: {.section .order}

the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the [Prohibition on the Purchase
of Residential Property by Non-Canadians
Act](/eng/acts/P-25.2)[[Footnote

  19, 2022[[Footnote
