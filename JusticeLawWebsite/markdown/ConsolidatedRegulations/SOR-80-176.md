## Ontario Asparagus Marketing (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-asparagus-marketing-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/80-176
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Registration 1980-03-12

Regulations Respecting the Marketing in Interprovincial and Export Trade
of Asparagus Produced in Ontario
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3 of
the [Ontario Asparagus Growers' Marketing-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-78-859)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDACA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Regulations respecting the marketing in interprovincial and export
trade of asparagus produced in Ontario](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-176).

Dated at London, Ontario, this 7th day of March, 1980

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868543 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Ontario Asparagus Marketing (Interprovincial and Export)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-80-176).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868545 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Farm Products Marketing Act
    (Ontario); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[asparagus]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means asparagus produced in
    Ontario that is used by a processor for processing;
    ([asperges]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Asparagus
    Growers' Marketing Board, established pursuant to the Act; ([Office
    de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[processing]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} includes canning, dehydrating,
    drying, freezing or processing with sugar or sulphur dioxide or any
    other chemical; ([transformation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who, on the first
    day of May in any year,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is the beneficial owner of property on which
      asparagus is growing, or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the tenant of property on which asparagus is
      growing. ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868554 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of asparagus in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated within the Province of Ontario.

## [Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868556 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations do not
apply to a producer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the property on which asparagus is growing is
  less than one-half acre; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of any asparagus that has been planted on
  that producer's property for less than two years.

## [Purchase and Sale]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868560 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  producer shall sell or offer to sell the asparagus produced by him
  through the Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall buy asparagus except through the
  Commodity Board.

## [Transportation and Packing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868564 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall transport asparagus from a producer to any place other
  than to a receiving station designated by the Commodity Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall assemble asparagus at any place other
  than at a receiving station designated by the Commodity Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} No person shall buy asparagus that has not been
  transported to a receiving station designated by the Commodity Board.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No person shall sell asparagus that has not been
  transported to a receiving station designated by the Commodity Board.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No person shall process, pack or package any
  asparagus that has not been purchased through the Commodity Board.

## [Contracts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868571 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** All asparagus shall be
bought and sold on the terms of a contract in writing between the
Commodity Board and the purchaser of the asparagus and the contract
shall be in the form set out in the schedule.

## [Pool]{.HTitleText1} {#h-868573 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Commodity Board shall, for the purpose of distributing the proceeds
  from sales by it of asparagus delivered to it by producers pursuant to
  these Regulations, conduct one pool.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board shall distribute the proceeds of
  the sales in the pool referred to in subsection (1), after deducting
  therefrom all the necessary and proper disbursements and expenses, in
  such a manner that each producer receives his share of the total
  proceeds in the pool in proportion to the quantity and taking into
  account the class, grade and size of asparagus delivered by him to the
  Commodity Board.

## [SCHEDULE]{.scheduleLabel}[(Section 7)]{.OriginatingRef} {#h-868578 .scheduleLabel}

THIS AGREEMENT made this ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} day
of ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}, 19![blank
line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader}.

BETWEEN:

(Hereinafter called the purchaser)

--- and ---

THE ONTARIO ASPARAGUS GROWERS' MARKETING BOARD

(Hereinafter called the Commodity Board)

WITNESSETH as follows:

- [1]{.lawlabel} The purchaser agrees to purchase through the Commodity
  Board and the Commodity Board as the marketing agency for asparagus
  agrees to sell the purchaser approximately ![blank
  line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} pounds of asparagus of the
  following grades:

  Approximately ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} pounds No. 1

  Approximately ![blank line](/img/ii_spacer.gif){.Leader} pounds No. 2

- [2]{.lawlabel} In addition to the above stated poundage of No. 1 grade
  asparagus, the purchaser agrees, if and when requested by the
  Commodity Board, to purchase and take delivery of a poundage of No. 2
  grade asparagus up to 10 per cent of the above-stated poundage of No.
  1 grade asparagus.

- [3]{.lawlabel} The purchaser agrees to accept deliveries of asparagus
  at the Commodity Board's receiving station upon reasonable notice to
  the purchaser that the asparagus is ready for delivery.

- [4]{.lawlabel} The purchaser and the Commodity Board agree that the
  grades respecting asparagus for processing established under The Farm
  Products Grades and Sales Act (Ontario) shall apply to this Agreement
  and be binding upon the purchaser and the Commodity Board.

- [5]{.lawlabel} In the case of a dispute as to the grade of the
  asparagus, the decision of a fruit and vegetable inspector appointed
  under The Farm Products Grades and Sales Act (Ontario) shall be
  accepted.

- [6]{.lawlabel} The purchaser shall pay the local board at its office,
  504 Newbold Street, London, Ontario, the purchase price of asparagus
  purchased by him as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} for asparagus delivered to the purchaser to and
    including the 26th day of May of any year to be paid for by the
    purchaser on or before the 8th day of June of that year;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for asparagus delivered to the purchaser after the
    26th day of May to and including the 9th day of June of any year to
    be paid for by the purchaser on or before the 22nd day of June of
    that year; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for asparagus delivered to the purchaser after the
    9th day of June of any year to be paid for by the purchaser on or
    before the 13th day of July of that year.

- [7]{.lawlabel} The Commodity Board or the purchaser shall be excused
  for non-fulfilment of this agreement caused by fire, flood,
  earthquake, wind, hail, acts of God, invasion, order of a civil or of
  military authorities, lack of transportation facilities or other
  causes beyond the control of either party to the extent that the
  fulfilment of the agreement has been hindered or prevented by such
  cause or causes.
