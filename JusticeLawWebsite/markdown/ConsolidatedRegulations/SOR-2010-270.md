## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766215 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Fisheries
    Act](/eng/acts/F-14). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[aquaculture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[aquaculture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the cultivation of fish.
    ([aquaculture]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[nuisance fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[nuisance fish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means fish that represent an
    imminent danger to the equipment used in the operation of an
    aquaculture facility, the safety of persons in the facility or the
    fish cultivated in the facility. ([poisson
    nuisible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prescribed activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prescribed activities]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the catching of fish for the purpose of
      cultivation;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the catching of fish that is incidental to the
      operation of an aquaculture facility;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the catching of fish for the purpose of complying
      with any monitoring condition specified in an aquaculture licence;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the catching of fish that escape from an
      aquaculture facility for the purpose of returning them to the
      aquaculture facility or otherwise disposing of them; and

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the catching of nuisance fish. ([activités
      réglementaires]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shellfish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shellfish]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means [Mollusca]{.otherLang
    lang="la"}, [Echinodermata]{.otherLang lang="la"} and
    [Crustacea]{.otherLang lang="la"}. ([mollusques et
    crustacés]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2015-96, s. 1

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766229 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of aquaculture and prescribed activities in

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the territorial sea of Canada off the coast of
  British Columbia;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the internal waters of Canada off the coast of
  British Columbia that are not in that province;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the internal waters of Canada in British Columbia;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} any facility in British Columbia from which fish may
  escape into Canadian fisheries waters.

## [Aquaculture Licences]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766235 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may issue an aquaculture licence authorizing a person to
  engage in aquaculture and prescribed activities on payment of the flat
  fee and the annual fee for the licence for the first year of the
  period during which it is valid.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The flat fee is payable for each aquaculture licence
  application and is determined in accordance with the formula

  ::: {#766239 .Subsection}
  A × B

  where

  [A]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aquaculture licence for fish
        cultivated for non-commercial purposes, \$0, and

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of all other aquaculture licences,
        \$100; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the annual adjustment factor determined in accordance with the
      formula
      ::: {#766245 .NestedFormula}
      \[(C -- 2015) × .02\] + 1

      where

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the year of the payment of the fee.
      :::
  :::

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The annual fee is payable in respect of each year of
  the period during which the licence is valid and is determined in
  accordance with the formula

  ::: {#766249 .Subsection}
  D × E × B

  where

  [D]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aquaculture licence for fish
        cultivated for commercial purposes in a marine environment other
        than those fish referred to in paragraph (b), the number of
        tonnes of fish authorized to be cultivated under the terms of
        the licence,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aquaculture licence for
        shellfish cultivated for commercial purposes in a marine
        environment, the number of hectares on which the shellfish are
        authorized to be cultivated under the terms of the licence, and

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aquaculture licence for fish
        cultivated for commercial purposes in a freshwater facility or
        in a land-based facility, zero, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an aquaculture licence for fish
        cultivated for non-commercial purposes, zero;

  [E]{.dfn}
  :   is
      - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the licence referred to in
        paragraph (a) of the description of D, \$2.50,

      - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the licence referred to in
        paragraph (b) of the description of D, \$5.00,

      - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the licence referred to in
        paragraph (c) of the description of D, \$0, and

      - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the licence referred to in
        paragraph (d) of the description of D, \$0; and

  [B]{.dfn}
  :   is the annual adjustment factor determined for B in
      subsection (2).
  :::

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An aquaculture licence must not be issued to an
  applicant who has not paid in full the flat and annual fees for
  aquaculture licences that were previously issued to that applicant.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-96, s. 2
:::

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the proper management
and control of fisheries and the conservation and protection of fish,
the Minister may specify, in addition to the conditions respecting the
matters set out in subsection 22(1) of the [Fishery (General)
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-53), conditions in an aquaculture
licence respecting any of the following matters:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the species and quantities of fish that are permitted
  to be cultivated and their place of origin;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the age, sex, stage of development or size of fish
  that are permitted to be cultivated;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the waters in which aquaculture and prescribed
  activities are permitted to be engaged in;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the fish feed that is permitted to be used in
  aquaculture, as well as the storage of fish feed in the aquaculture
  facility;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the harvesting of fish in the aquaculture facility;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the measures that must be taken to control and
  monitor the presence of pathogens and pests in the aquaculture
  facility;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the measures that must be taken to monitor the
  presence of pathogens and pests in wild fish in the waters that may be
  affected by the operations of the aquaculture facility;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the measures that must be taken to minimize the
  escape of fish from the aquaculture facility and to catch the fish
  that escape;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the catching of nuisance fish;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the measures that must be taken to minimize the
  impact of the aquaculture facility's operations on fish and fish
  habitat;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the measures that must be taken to monitor the
  environmental impact of the aquaculture facility's operations;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the equipment that is permitted to be used in the
  operation of the aquaculture facility and the manner in which it is
  permitted to be used;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} the notice that must be given to the Minister before

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a substance is used to treat fish for pathogens or
    pests,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} fish are transferred to the aquaculture facility,
    or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} fish are harvested;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} the verification by an observer of any activity that
  is part of the aquaculture facility's operations;

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} the records that must be kept in relation to any
  matter referred to in paragraphs 61(2)(a) to (f) of the Act, including
  records of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the species, quantity, age and sex of fish
    transferred to the aquaculture facility and the date of their
    transfer and harvest,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the species and quantity of any fish found in the
    aquaculture facility that were not transferred to the facility under
    the authority of the licence,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} any diagnosis or treatment of a fish pathogen or
    pest present in the aquaculture facility, including the extent to
    which the pathogen or pest affects the fish in the facility,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any substance used to treat fish for pathogens or
    pests, including the quantity used and the date and method of its
    administration,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the number and species of fish that die prior to
    harvest, and the cause of death,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the number and species of nuisance fish that die
    as a result of the aquaculture facility's operations,

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the inspection and maintenance of the equipment
    used in the operation of the aquaculture facility,

  - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} any major failure of the aquaculture facility's
    containment structures and the quantity of any fish that escape from
    the facility,

  - [(ix)]{.lawlabel} the data collected in the monitoring of the
    environmental impact of the aquaculture facility's operations, and

  - [(x)]{.lawlabel} the data collected in the monitoring of the health
    of fish in the aquaculture facility and in the waters that may be
    affected by its operations;

- [(p)]{.lawlabel} the manner and form in which the records are to be
  kept, the times at which and the person to whom the records are to be
  produced and the period for which the records are to be retained; and

- [(q)]{.lawlabel} the place and time at which and the manner by which
  the annual licence fee must be paid.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2015-96, s. 3

- **[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If,
  during a given year, a condition in an aquaculture licence is amended
  to modify the quantity of fish authorized to be cultivated or the area
  within which shellfish is authorized to be cultivated, the annual fee
  payable for that particular year must be recalculated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In the event that the annual fee is recalculated, the
  Minister must amend the conditions of the licence respecting the
  payment of the fee to reflect any increase if the specific amount of
  each annual fee is set out in the licence.

- SOR/2015-96, s. 4

## [Incidental Catch]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766303 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Unless the retention of
incidental catch is expressly authorized by an aquaculture licence,
every person who catches a fish incidentally must immediately return it,
if it is alive, to waters outside the aquaculture facility in a manner
that causes it the least harm.

## [Keeping and Producing Licence]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766305 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Every holder of an
aquaculture licence must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} keep the licence or a copy of it in the aquaculture
  facility and produce it on the demand of a fishery officer or fishery
  guardian; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} when engaged in activities authorized by the licence
  outside the facility, produce the licence or a copy of it on the
  demand of a fishery officer or fishery guardian.

## [Prohibition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766309 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person must not engage in
aquaculture or prescribed activities except under the authority of an
aquaculture licence.
