## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897390 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Fee
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-547).

## [Fee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-897392 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), the fee to be paid by a person who files an
  application for divorce and in respect of whom a service is provided
  under section 5 of the [Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-600) is \$10 per application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No fee shall be paid by a person who receives legal
  aid from a province in respect of the person's application for divorce
  where, pursuant to the law of the province, payment by that person of
  the fees established by the province for filing an application for
  divorce is waived.
