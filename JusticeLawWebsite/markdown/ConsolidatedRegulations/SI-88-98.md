## Minister of National Health and Welfare Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-national-health-and-welfare-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/88-98
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1988-06-22

Minister of National Health and Welfare Authority to Prescribe Fees
Order

P.C. 1988-1066 1988-06-02
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Governor in Council is of the opinion that the cost of a
drug submission evaluation service provided by the Department of
National Health and Welfare should be borne by the person who uses the
service.

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of National Health and Welfare and the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 13(b) of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to authorize the
Minister of National Health and Welfare to prescribe, by order, the fees
payable by a person to whom a drug submission evaluation service is
provided by the Department of National Health and Welfare, in accordance
with the annexed terms and conditions.
