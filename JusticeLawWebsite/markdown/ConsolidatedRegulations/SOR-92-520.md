## Merchant Seamen Compensation Order, 1992 {#merchant-seamen-compensation-order-1992 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-520
:::

[MERCHANT SEAMEN COMPENSATION ACT](/eng/acts/M-6)

Registration 1992-08-27

Order Respecting an Increase in Compensation for Merchant Seamen and
their Dependants, 1992

P.C. 1992-1866  1992-08-27
::::

::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the annexed order does not increase the amount or maximum rate
of earnings payable to merchant seamen as compensation to an amount or
maximum rate of earnings that exceeds the highest equivalent amount or
maximum rate of earnings presently specified in the enactments of the
Legislature of the Province of Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward
Island or Newfoundland relating to compensation of workers and their
dependants for accidents occurring to workers during the course of their
employment;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 35 of the
[Merchant Seamen Compensation Act](/eng/acts/M-6), is pleased hereby to
revoke the Merchant Seamen Compensation Order, 1986, made by Order in
Council P.C. 1986-1051 of May 1, 1986[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, and to make the annexed
Order respecting compensation for merchant seamen and their dependants,
1992, in substitution therefor.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA){#fn_1e}SOR/86-491, 1986 [Canada
  Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 2081
  :::
:::
