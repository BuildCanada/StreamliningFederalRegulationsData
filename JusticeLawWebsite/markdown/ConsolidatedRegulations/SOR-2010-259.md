## Ontario Grapes-for-Processing Marketing Levies Order {#ontario-grapes-for-processing-marketing-levies-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2010-259
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

ONTARIO GRAPES-FOR-PROCESSING ORDER

Registration 2010-11-15

Ontario Grapes-for-Processing Marketing Levies Order
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Grape Growers of Ontario, formerly known as The Ontario Grape
Growers' Marketing Board, pursuant to section 3 and paragraph 4(b) of
the [Ontario Grapes-for-Processing
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._201)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612707-E_hq_8143){#fn_612707-E_hq_8143-ID0EDBBA},
hereby makes the annexed Ontario Grapes-for-Processing Marketing Levies
Order.

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612707-E_hq_8143-ID0EDBBA){#fn_612707-E_hq_8143}C.R.C., c.
  201
  :::

St. Catharines, Ontario, November 10, 2010
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766082 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[buyer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that buys grapes.
    ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Grape Growers of
    Ontario, formerly known as The Ontario Grape Growers' Marketing
    Board, established under the Farm Products Marketing Act, R.S.O.
    1990, c. F.9. ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is engaged in the production or marketing of
      grapes;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} employs a person referred to in paragraph (a);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is entitled under any lease or agreement

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to a share of the grapes produced or marketed
        by a person referred to in paragraph (a), or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the proceeds from the sale of those grapes;
        or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} takes possession of any grapes under any form of
      security or legal proceedings for a debt.
      ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766093 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Every producer situated in
Ontario must pay to the Commodity Board, for all grapes of a variety set
out in column 3 of the schedule that are marketed by that producer in
interprovincial and export trade, the total of the General Levy and the
Research and Industry Development Levy set out respectively in columns 4
and 5 of that item.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  buyer must deduct the levies referred to in section 2 from every
  payment made to a producer for grapes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The buyer must forward the levies to the Commodity
  Board not later than 30 days after the end of the month in which they
  are deducted, together with a statement on a form provided by the
  Commodity Board that sets out

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount and variety of grapes bought from the
    producer;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the amount of the levies being forwarded on the
    producer's behalf;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the producer's name; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the producer's Commodity Board licence number.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-766102 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
