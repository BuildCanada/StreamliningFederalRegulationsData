## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128461 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Greenhouse Gas Pollution
    Pricing Act](/eng/acts/G-11.55). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bio-aviation fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bio-aviation fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a particular
    substance

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is derived entirely from biological matter
      available on a renewable or recurring basis;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that may contain water if the proportion of the
      water does not exceed 1% of the particular substance;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that may contain other substances, materials or
      things that are not described in paragraph (a) or (b) if the
      combined proportion of those other substances, materials or things
      does not exceed 6% of the particular substance; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that is suitable for generating power by means of
      an aircraft engine when used

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} on its own,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} after being blended with aviation gasoline or
        aviation turbo fuel,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} after being blended with an aviation
        gasoline-like blendstock to produce aviation gasoline, or

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} after being blended with an aviation turbo
        fuel-like blendstock to produce aviation turbo
        fuel. ([bio-carburant d'aviation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible greenhouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible greenhouse]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a greenhouse all
    or substantially all of which is used for the growing of vegetables,
    fruits, bedding plants, flowers, ornamental plants, tree seedlings,
    medicinal plants or other plants. ([serre
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible greenhouse activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible greenhouse activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the use
    of a qualifying greenhouse fuel to heat an eligible greenhouse or to
    supplement carbon dioxide in an eligible greenhouse in order to grow
    or produce plants. ([activité de serre admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible heating activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible heating activity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the use of
    light fuel oil exclusively for providing heat to a home, building or
    similar structure but not for generating heat in an industrial
    process, including a commercial process that involves removing
    moisture from a good. ([activité de chauffage
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means export from
    Canada. ([exportation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fuel charge system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fuel charge system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 168(1) of the Act. ([régime de redevance sur les
    combustibles]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greenhouse operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greenhouse operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person that
    carries on a business of growing vegetables, fruits, bedding plants,
    flowers, ornamental plants, tree seedlings, medicinal plants or
    other plants in eligible greenhouses with a reasonable expectation
    of profit. ([exploitant de serre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[main electrical network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[main electrical network]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a network for
    the distribution of electricity that is subject to the standards of
    the North American Electric Reliability Corporation. ([réseau
    électrique principal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying greenhouse fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying greenhouse fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a type of
    fuel that is marketable natural gas or propane. ([combustible de
    serre admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[qualifying power plant fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[qualifying power plant fuel]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a type of
    fuel that is light fuel oil or marketable natural gas. ([combustible
    de centrale électrique admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote community]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a geographic area
    that is not serviced by a main electrical network and that is not
    serviced by a distribution system. ([collectivité
    éloignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[remote power plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote power plant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a power plant that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} generates electricity for general distribution to
      the public in a remote community;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is not connected to a main electrical network;
      and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is not connected to a distribution
      system. ([centrale électrique éloignée]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[remote power plant operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[remote power plant operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    that operates a remote power plant. ([exploitant de centrale
    électrique éloignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Interest Rate]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1128464 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Part.

[[basic rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[basic rate]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a particular
    quarter, means the rate that is the simple arithmetic mean,
    expressed as a percentage per year and rounded to the next higher
    whole percentage if the mean is not a whole percentage, of all
    amounts each of which is the average equivalent yield, expressed as
    a percentage per year, of Government of Canada Treasury Bills that
    mature approximately three months after their date of issue and that
    are sold at auctions of Government of Canada Treasury Bills during
    the first month of the quarter preceding the particular
    quarter. ([taux de base]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[quarter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any period of three
    consecutive months beginning on January 1, April 1, July 1 or
    October 1. ([trimestre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed rates of interest

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of Part 1
of the Act, the prescribed rate of interest in effect during a
particular quarter is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid to the Receiver
  General, the sum of the basic rate in respect of the particular
  quarter and 4%;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid or applied on an
  amount payable by the Minister to a person (other than a corporation),
  the sum of the basic rate in respect of the particular quarter and 2%;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of interest to be paid or applied on an
  amount payable by the Minister to a corporation, the basic rate in
  respect of the particular quarter; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the sum of the basic rate in
  respect of the particular quarter and 4%.

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Rebates]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1187292 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Rebate --- fuel exported by non-resident

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 48 of the Act, if at a particular time a quantity
  of fuel is exported by a person that is not resident in Canada and not
  registered for the purposes of Part 1 of the Act and if the quantity
  of fuel was delivered at an earlier time to the person in a listed
  province by a registered distributor in respect of that type of fuel,
  the Minister must pay a rebate to the registered distributor in
  respect of the quantity of fuel, the listed province and the reporting
  period of the registered distributor that includes the particular time
  if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a charge under section 17 of the Act was payable by
    the registered distributor at the earlier time in respect of the
    quantity of fuel and the listed province and that charge was taken
    into account in the determination of the net charge for the
    reporting period of the registered distributor that includes the
    earlier time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} during the period that begins at the earlier time
    and ends at the particular time, the quantity of fuel is not further
    processed, transformed or altered in Canada except to the extent
    reasonably necessary or incidental to its transportation; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person provides to the registered distributor,
    and the registered distributor retains, evidence satisfactory to the
    Minister of the exportation of the quantity of fuel by the person.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amount of rebate

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 48 of the Act, the amount
  of the rebate under subsection (1) is equal to the amount of the
  charge referred to in paragraph (1)(a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The rebate under subsection (1) is not payable to the
  extent that the fuel is exported in a supply tank of a vehicle.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The rebate under subsection (1) is not payable if the
  type of fuel is gasoline, kerosene, light fuel oil or propane and the
  quantity of the fuel that is exported otherwise than in a supply tank
  of a vehicle does not exceed 1000 L.
