## Chrysler Canada Ltd. Regulations, 1982 {#chrysler-canada-ltd.-regulations-1982 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/82-926
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 1, 1980-81

Registration 1982-10-14

Regulations Respecting the Insurance of Loans Made to Chrysler Canada
Ltd., 1982

P.C. 1982-3146 1982-10-14
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Industry, Trade and Commerce, pursuant to Industry,
Trade and Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, is
pleased hereby to revoke the Chrysler Canada Ltd. Regulations made by
Order in Council P.C. 1980-1621 of 13th June, 1980[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA} and to make
the annexed [Regulations respecting the insurance of loans made to
Chrysler Canada Ltd., 1982](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-926).

  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e-ID0EDBAA){#footnote1_e}SOR/80-453,

  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874974 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Chrysler Canada Ltd. Regulations,
1982](/eng/regulations/SOR-82-926).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874976 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Chrysler Canada Ltd.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chrysler Canada Ltd.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated under that name under the laws of Canada and having its
    registered office in the City of Windsor in the Province of Ontario;
    ([Chrysler Canada Ltée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Chrysler Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chrysler Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated under that name under the laws of the State of Delaware
    in the United States of America and having its head office in the
    City of Highland Park in the State of Michigan; ([Chrysler
    Corporation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[DCI Diesel Canada Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[DCI Diesel Canada Inc.]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a company
    incorporated under that name under the laws of Canada and in which
    the initial equity interest of Chrysler Canada Ltd. is 97% and of
    Perkins Engines Canada Ltd. is 3%; ([DCI Diesel Canada
    Inc.]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Industry,
    Trade and Commerce; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender approved by
    the Minister other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that, in the opinion of the
      Minister, is effectively controlled by any such government or any
      agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a municipal corporation. ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-874987 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to sections 4 and 5, the Minister may, pursuant to Industry, Trade and
  Commerce Vote 1a of Appropriation Act No. 1, 1980-81, provide
  insurance of any loans, not exceeding two hundred million dollars in
  the aggregate, made by a private lender to Chrysler Canada Ltd.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any agreement to insure between Her Majesty in right
  of Canada and Chrysler Corporation, Chrysler Canada Ltd. and DCI
  Diesel Canada Inc. shall be in form and content satisfactory to the
  Minister and shall include the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that the private lender pays Her Majesty an
    insurance fee of one per cent per annum based on the amount of
    insurance in effect from time to time;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that the insured loan is used

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to the extent of \$150 million maximum to provide
      for the conversion and retooling of the Windsor Assembly Plant
      into a van/wagon plant in the years 1983 and 1984 so as to produce
      a van/wagon for sale to the public in the autumn of 1983, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to the extent of \$50 million maximum to finance
      the conversion, retooling and re-equipping of the Windsor Engine
      Plant into a diesel engine plant capable of producing 240,000
      engines per year at straight time by December 31, 1985;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} that any loan insurance agreement between Her
    Majesty and a private lender shall be entered into on or after
    January 1, 1983 but not later than September 1, 1984;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} that the insured loan be repaid over a period of
    not more than five years commencing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the insured loan referred to in
      subparagraph (b)(i), on April 30, 1986, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the insured loan referred to in
      subparagraph (b)(ii), on December 31, 1986;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Canada Ltd. conducts its business in
    accordance with an Operating and Financing Plan approved by the
    Minister;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} that the employment level of Chrysler Canada Ltd.
    and DCI Diesel Canada Inc. shall be,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} nine per cent during the calendar years 1980 and
      1981,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at least 11 per cent during each calendar year
      commencing in the year 1982 and ending in the year 1986, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an average of at least 11 per cent for the
      calendar years 1987 and 1988 and in no case shall the employment
      be less than nine per cent in the calendar year 1987 or 1988,

    of the employment level of Chrysler Corporation in the United States
    for the same periods;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} that the employment in the diesel engine plant
    located in the Windsor Engine Plant referred to in subparagraph
    (b)(ii) shall, subject to demonstrated productivity improvements and
    market factors, reach a level of 1,000 persons in 1988 and that such
    level shall be maintained until the later of 1990 and the repayment
    of the insured loan referred to in subparagraph (b)(ii);

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} that during the years 1980, 1981, 1982, 1983, 1984,
    1985 and 1986, Chrysler Canada Ltd. shall invest a total of \$821
    million in the Windsor Assembly Plant, the Windsor Engine Plant
    referred to in paragraph (g) and its other Canadian operations;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Canada Ltd. reaches an agreement with
    its bankers on a restructuring of any loans outstanding and such
    restructuring shall provide that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any repayment of the principal outstanding is
      postponed until September 30, 1986 and is repayable in equal
      quarterly instalments over a period of five years commencing on
      that date,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the principal outstanding referred to in
      subparagraph (i) does not accrue interest at more than 12.5 per
      cent per annum, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} seven per cent of the interest referred to in
      subparagraph (ii) is deferred until the fourth anniversary of the
      date of the restructuring;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Canada Ltd. and DCI Diesel Canada
    Inc. enter into a trust indenture with The Canada Trust Company and
    a security sharing agreement with the private lenders, The Canada
    Trust Company, the Minister, Her Majesty in Right of Ontario and the
    Ontario Development Corporation, the combined effects of which will
    be

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to secure, by way of a first charge on all the
      fixed and the non-fixed assets of Chrysler Canada Ltd. and DCI
      Diesel Canada Inc.,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the restructured loans referred to in paragraph
        (i),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the insured loans referred to in paragraph (b),

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the loans guaranteed by the Ontario Development
        Corporation in the amount of \$33 million,

      - [(D)]{.lawlabel} the repayable contributions made by the Ontario
        Development Corporation in the amount of \$10 million, and

      - [(E)]{.lawlabel} the repayable contributions made by Her Majesty
        in Right of Canada in the amount of \$12 million, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to distribute the proceeds from the realization
      of

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the fixed assets contained in the Pillette Road
        Plant in the following manner:

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} firstly, that the proceeds be used to reduce
          the insured loans to the extent of \$50 million,

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} secondly, any of the proceeds be used to
          retire [pari passu]{.otherLang lang="la"} the restructured
          loans referred to in paragraph (i) and the insured loans
          referred to in paragraph (b), and

        - [(III)]{.lawlabel} thirdly, any proceeds remaining be used to
          retire the debt guaranteed by the Ontario Development
          Corporation referred to in clause (i)(C) and the repayable
          contributions referred to in clauses (i)(D) and (E),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the fixed assets of Chrysler Canada Ltd., other
        than those referred to in clause (A), and of DCI Diesel Canada
        Inc. in the following manner:

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} firstly, that the proceeds be used to retire
          [pari passu]{.otherLang lang="la"} the restructured loans
          referred to in paragraph (i) and the insured loans referred to
          in paragraph (b), and

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} secondly, any remainder of the proceeds be
          used to retire the loans guaranteed by the Ontario Development
          Corporation referred to in clause (i)(C) and the repayable
          contributions referred to in clauses (i)(D) and (E), and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the non-fixed assets of Chrysler Canada Ltd.
        and DCI Diesel Canada Inc. in the following manner:

        - [(I)]{.lawlabel} firstly, that the proceeds be used to retire
          [pari passu]{.otherLang lang="la"} the restructured loans
          referred to in paragraph (i), and

        - [(II)]{.lawlabel} secondly, that any of the proceeds be used
          to retire the insured loans referred to in paragraph (b), the
          loans guaranteed by the Ontario Development Corporation
          referred to in clause (i)(C) and the repayable contributions
          referred to in clauses (i)(D) and (E);

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Corporation grants Chrysler Canada
    Ltd.

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an exclusive world-wide licence to produce the
      T115 van/wagons, such van/wagons to be manufactured in Canada, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an exclusive licence to market such van/wagons
      in Canada;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Corporation grants to DCI Diesel
    Canada Inc. an exclusive licence to manufacture, use and sell,
    commencing on January 1, 1983 and until the later of January 1, 1994
    and the date of the repayment of the loan described in subparagraph
    (b)(ii), the diesel engines described in the schedule;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Corporation delivers to Her Majesty
    warrants to acquire 2,034,000 shares of Chrysler Corporation common
    stock of a par value of \$6.25 U.S. per share at any time up to and
    including December 31, 1990 at \$13.00 U.S. per share;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} that the government of the United States has made a
    commitment to guarantee the payment of up to \$1.5 billion U.S. in
    loans to Chrysler Corporation and Chrysler Corporation has drawn
    down loans in the amount of at least \$1.2 billion U.S. pursuant to
    such commitment before the loan insurance agreement referred to in
    paragraph (c) comes into effect;

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} that Chrysler Canada Ltd. shall carry out its
    obligations with respect to the conversion, retooling and
    re-equipping of the Windsor Engine Plant referred to in subparagraph
    3(2)(b)(ii) through DCI Diesel Canada Inc.; and

  - [(p)]{.lawlabel} that Perkins Engines Canada Ltd., with the consent
    of Massey-Ferguson Ltd., Massey-Ferguson-Perkins Limited and Perkins
    Engines Ltd., enter into an agreement with the Minister to the
    effect that Perkins Engines Canada Ltd. will not use its
    shareholdings in DCI Diesel Canada Inc. to prevent DCI Diesel Canada
    Inc. from fulfilling the terms of any agreement between the
    Minister, Chrysler Corporation, Chrysler Canada Ltd. and DCI Diesel
    Canada Inc.

## [Application for Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875039 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  insurance shall be provided to a private lender pursuant to section 3
  unless that private lender has made an application for the insurance
  to the Minister.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a private lender makes an application referred
  to in subsection (1), he shall furnish the Minister with such
  information relating to the application as the Minister may require.

## [Payment of Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-875043 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender
demands, prior to the maturity of a loan referred to in section 3 in
respect of which the Minister has provided insurance pursuant to that
section, repayment of the loan and the borrower does not repay the loan,
the amount of such insurance payable to the private lender shall not
exceed the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the amount of insurance in effect on the date of such
  demand, and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender,

after compliance with the terms and conditions of the loan insurance
agreements including the provisions respecting security for the loan.

### [Indirect Injection Diesel Engines]{.HTitleText1} {#indirect-injection-diesel-engines .SchedHeadL1}

- **1** 3.7 Litre 6-cylinder Turbo

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} automotive version meeting U.S. federal and
    Canadian emission standards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} automotive version meeting California emission
    standards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} industrial version.

- **2** 2.2 Litre 4-cylinder Turbo

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} automotive version meeting U.S. federal and
    Canadian emission standards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} industrial version.

- **3** 2.2 Litre 4-cylinder Natural Aspiration

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} automotive version meeting federal U.S. and
    Canadian emission standards;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} automotive version meeting California emission
    standards;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} industrial version.

- **4** 2.2 Litre 4-cylinder Turbo automotive version meeting California
  emission standards.
