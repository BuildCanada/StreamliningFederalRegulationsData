## Great Lakes Fishery Commission Privileges and Immunities Order {#great-lakes-fishery-commission-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/81-690
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Registration 1981-09-04

Order Respecting the Granting of Certain Privileges to the Great Lakes
Fishery Commission

P.C. 1981-2359 1981-09-03
::::

:::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Secretary of State for External Affairs, pursuant to section 3 of
the Privileges and Immunities (International Organizations) Act, is
pleased hereby to make the annexed [Order respecting the granting of
certain privileges to the Great Lakes Fishery
Commission](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-690).
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871806 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Great Lakes Fishery Commission Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-81-690).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/85-1018, s. 1
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871810 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Great Lakes Fishery
    Commission. ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges]{.HTitleText1} {#h-871814 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Organization shall have
in Canada the legal capacities of a body corporate and shall, to such
extent as may be required for the performance of its functions, have the
immunities and privileges set forth in sections 2, 5, 6, 7 and 8 of
Article II of the Convention.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/84-143, s. 1(F)
- SOR/85-1018, s. 2
