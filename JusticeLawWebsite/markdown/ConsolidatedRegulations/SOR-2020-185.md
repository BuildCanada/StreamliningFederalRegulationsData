## Critical Habitat of the Northern Wolffish (Anarhichas denticulatus) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-northern-wolffish-anarhichas-denticulatus-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2020-185
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2020-08-28

Critical Habitat of the Northern Wolffish (Anarhichas denticulatus)
Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Northern Wolffish ([Anarhichas denticulatus]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) is a wildlife species that is listed as a threatened species
in Part 3 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a-ID0EBBGA){#fn_a}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b){#fn_b-ID0EBBEA} of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_b-ID0EBBEA){#fn_b}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans is of the opinion that
the annexed Order would affect an area in respect of which a wildlife
management board is authorized by a land claims agreement to perform
functions in respect of wildlife species and, pursuant to subsection
58(8) of that Act, has consulted the wildlife management board in
question with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_a){#fn_a-ID0EDACA}, makes the annexed [Critical
Habitat of the Northern Wolffish (Anarhichas denticulatus)
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2020-185).

Ottawa, August 25, 2020

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Northern Wolffish ([Anarhichas denticulatus]{.otherLang
lang="la"}), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that
species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
