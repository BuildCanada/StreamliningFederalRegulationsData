## Canada Communication Group Divestiture Regulations {#canada-communication-group-divestiture-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/97-127
:::

Canada Communication Group Divestiture Regulations

T.B. 825053 1997-02-20
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
The Treasury Board, on the recommendation of the President of the
Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 42.1(1)(u)[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003409 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Public Service Superannuation
    Act](/eng/acts/P-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new employer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Canada Communication
    Group Inc., the wholly-owned subsidiary of St. Joseph Corporation
    that will carry on the business of the Canada Communication Group in
    accordance with the sales agreement. ([nouvel
    employeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[sales agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[sales agreement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the agreement of
    purchase and sale relating to the transfer of the Canada
    Communication Group, a part of the Department of Public Works and
    Government Services, made between Her Majesty the Queen in right of
    Canada represented by the Minister of Public Works and Government
    Services and St. Joseph Corporation on December 13, 1996.
    ([convention de vente]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[St. Joseph Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[St. Joseph Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation
    established under the laws of Ontario that is referred to as the
    purchaser in the sales agreement. ([St. Joseph
    Corporation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transfer date]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the date on which a
    person who is an employee within the meaning of the sales agreement,
    having accepted an offer of employment from St. Joseph Corporation,
    ceases to be employed in the public service. ([date de
    cession]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- SOR/2016-203, s. 52(E)

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003418 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply to a person who, having
  received and accepted an offer of employment from St. Joseph
  Corporation pursuant to the sales agreement, on the transfer date
  ceases to be employed in the public service and becomes employed by
  the new employer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to a person who
  subsequently becomes re-employed by the new employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 52(E)

## [Applicable Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003424 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections
  12, 13 and 13.01 of the Act only apply to a person on and after the
  date on which that person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), where on the transfer date a
  person, on ceasing to be employed in the public service, would have
  been eligible, were it not for these Regulations, to exercise an
  option under section 12, paragraph 13(7)(a) or (b) or section 13.01 of
  the Act, the person may exercise such an option

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in paragraphs 13(7)(a)
    and (b) of the Act, no later than June 20, 1998; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the cases referred to in sections 12 and 13.01
    of the Act, no later than December 1, 1998.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person who has exercised an option under
  subsection (2) is deemed not to have become employed by the new
  employer for the purposes of these Regulations and of sections 83 to
  99 of the [Public Service Superannuation
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1358) and is not entitled to
  exercise an option pursuant to subsection (1) when the person ceases
  to be employed by the new employer.

## [Surviving Spouse and Children]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003433 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 12(8) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by the new employer are entitled to a
death benefit equal to a return of contributions.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 13(3) of the Act, the surviving spouse and children of a
person who dies while employed by the new employer are entitled to an
allowance as described in paragraphs 12(4)(a) and (b) of the Act,
subject to the limitations set out in subsections 12(4) and (5) of the
Act.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, a child who was born to or adopted by a
person or became the stepchild of the person during the period that
begins on the date on which the person ceases to be employed in the
public service and that ends on the date on which that person ceases to
be employed by the new employer is entitled to an allowance under Part I
of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 52(E)

## [Adaptation of Subsection 10(5) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003439 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 10(5) of the Act, the one year period referred to in
paragraph 10(5)(a) of the Act shall begin on the date on which the
person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

## [Adaptation of Sections 12 and 13 of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003441 .Part}

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, pensionable service includes the period
of service with the new employer that begins on the transfer date and
that ends on the date on which the person ceases to be employed by the
new employer.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 12 and 13 of the Act, the age of a person when the person
ceases to be employed in the public service is the age of the person on
the day on which that person ceases to be employed by the new employer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2016-203, s. 52(E)

these Regulations apply is considered to be a Group 1 contributor.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1003449 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come
into force on March 7, 1997.
