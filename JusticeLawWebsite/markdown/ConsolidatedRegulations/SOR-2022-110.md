## [Definitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345678 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[air-conditioning system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[air-conditioning system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} or [[refrigeration
    system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system that has a compressor,
    condenser, evaporator and expansion valve, and that contains or is
    designed to contain a halocarbon refrigerant. ([système de
    climatisation ou de réfrigération]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certified person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} holds a valid certificate recognized by at least
      one province indicating completion of an environmental awareness
      course in recycling, recovery and handling procedures in respect
      of halocarbon refrigerants; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is recognized by the Canadian Forces or under the
      laws of Canada or a province as qualified to work on
      air-conditioning systems or refrigeration systems. ([personne
      accréditée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[container]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a container that contains or
    is designed to contain a halocarbon for the purpose of storage or
    transportation. ([récipient]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[fire-extinguishing system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[fire-extinguishing system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a portable
    or fixed system that contains or is designed to contain a halocarbon
    fire-extinguishing agent. ([système d'extinction
    d'incendie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[halocarbon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[halocarbon]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a substance set out in
    Schedule 1, whether existing alone or in a mixture, and includes
    isomers of any such substance. ([halocarbure]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an air-conditioning system or
      refrigeration system or solvent system, joining two or more
      components that contain or are designed to contain a halocarbon
      essential to the operation of the system;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fixed fire-extinguishing system,
      adding a component --- that contains or is designed to contain a
      halocarbon --- to the other components of the system in order to
      make the system operational; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a new portable fire-extinguishing
      system, making it available. ([installation]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[large]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[large]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an air-conditioning
    system or refrigeration system, describes a system that consists of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a single refrigerant circuit that contains or is
      designed to contain more than 10 kg of halocarbon; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} multiple refrigerant circuits one or more of
      which contains or is designed to contain more than 10 kg of
      halocarbon. ([de grande capacité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[military vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[military vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    [[ship]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or
    [[aircraft]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 122(1)
    of the [Canadian Environmental Protection Act,
    1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31), or a land vehicle, that is designed to be
    used in combat or in a combat support role. ([véhicule
    militaire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[recycling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[recycling]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a halocarbon, means
    recovering and, if needed, cleaning by a process such as filtering
    or drying, and reusing to charge an air-conditioning system or
    refrigeration system, solvent system, fire-extinguishing system or
    container. ([recyclage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[responsible person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, solvent system,
    fire-extinguishing system or container, means the person responsible
    for its service and operation. ([personne
    responsable]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any work that is carried out
    on an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, solvent
    system, fire-extinguishing system or container and that involves the
    components that contain or are designed to contain a halocarbon,
    such as the charging of a system or container with a halocarbon, the
    removal or reassembly of one or more components of the circuit
    containing a halocarbon or the detection and repair of
    leaks. ([entretien]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an air-conditioning
    system or refrigeration system, describes a system that consists of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a single refrigerant circuit that contains or is
      designed to contain 10 kg or less of halocarbon; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} multiple refrigerant circuits each of which
      contains or is designed to contain 10 kg or less of
      halocarbon. ([de petite capacité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[solvent system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[solvent system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a system that uses or
    is designed to use a halocarbon as a solvent, including in a
    cleaning application. It does not include a system or application
    that uses a halocarbon as a laboratory analytical standard or
    laboratory reagent or in a process in which the halocarbon is
    converted into another substance or is generated but ultimately
    converted into a different substance. ([système de
    solvants]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345703 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to air-conditioning systems or refrigeration
  systems, solvent systems, fire-extinguishing systems and containers
  located in Canada that are

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} owned by Her Majesty in right of Canada, a board or
    agency of the Government of Canada, a [[Crown
    corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, as defined in subsection 83(1)
    of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), or a federal
    work or undertaking; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} located on aboriginal lands or federal lands.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to foam products.

## [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345711 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release of halocarbon

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not release, or allow or cause the release of, a halocarbon that
  is contained in

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system,
    fire-extinguishing system or container; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} equipment used in the reuse, recycling, reclamation
    or storage of a halocarbon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the release of a
  halocarbon

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is for the purpose of calibrating leak-detecting
    devices with equipment designed specifically for that purpose and
    the manufacturer\'s recommended procedures are followed;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} results from connecting or disconnecting hoses that
    are less than 1 m in length and used for charging with, or
    recovering, a halocarbon;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} results from a purge system on an air-conditioning
    system or refrigeration system, including any associated recovery
    equipment, that emits less than 0.1 kg of halocarbon per kilogram of
    air purged to the environment; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} is from a fire-extinguishing system for the purpose
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} fighting a fire that is not set for training
      purposes, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} testing the system in a military vehicle as
      authorized by a permit issued under subsection 20(2).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[reclamation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} In this section,
  [[reclamation]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}, in respect of a halocarbon,
  means its recovery, reprocessing and upgrading through a process such
  as filtering, drying, distilling and treating chemically in order to
  restore the halocarbon to industry-accepted reuse standards.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Installation or activation

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A person must not install
or activate an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system or
fire-extinguishing system that contains or is designed to contain a
halocarbon listed in any of items 1 to 9 of Schedule 1 unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is reactivating the system at the same
  site;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person is installing a fire-extinguishing system
  in a military vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person is installing a fire-extinguishing system
  during the manufacture of a new civilian aircraft in accordance with
  the Convention on International Civil Aviation, signed at Chicago on
  December 7, 1944, as amended from time to time; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a fire-extinguishing system, the
  person is authorized to do so by a permit issued under subsection
  20(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvent system --- items 1 to 9 of
Schedule 1

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not install or use a solvent system that uses or is designed to
  use a halocarbon listed in any of items 1 to 9 of Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Solvent system --- items 11 and 12 of
  Schedule 1

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person must not install or use a solvent system
  that uses or is designed to use a halocarbon listed in item 11 or 12
  of Schedule 1 unless they are authorized to do so by a permit issued
  under subsection 20(2).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage or transport

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not store or transport a halocarbon unless it is in a container
  that is designed and manufactured to be refilled and to contain that
  specific type of halocarbon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of a
  halocarbon that is used as a laboratory analytical standard or a
  laboratory reagent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Chiller

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  must not operate a chiller that contains a halocarbon listed in any of
  items 1 to 9 of Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
  [[chiller]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[chiller]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}
  means an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system that has a
  compressor, an evaporator and a secondary coolant, but does not
  include an absorption chiller.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purge system

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** A person must not install
or operate a purge system, including any associated recovery equipment,
unless the system emits less than 0.1 kg of halocarbon per kilogram of
air purged to the environment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging --- leak detection

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person must not charge an
air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, solvent system,
fire-extinguishing system or container with a halocarbon for the purpose
of leak testing the system or container.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging with a halocarbon

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 11, a
person must not charge an air-conditioning system or refrigeration
system, fire-extinguishing system or container with a halocarbon unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person has previously leak tested the system or
  container; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case where a leak is detected, the person has
  notified the owner of the system or container of the leak and the leak
  has been repaired.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of section 10

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  leak is detected from an air-conditioning system or refrigeration
  system or fire-extinguishing system and it is necessary to charge the
  system with a halocarbon to prevent an immediate danger to the
  environment or to human life or health, section 10 does not apply
  during the period in which the danger persists.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a system is charged under the circumstances
  described in subsection (1),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person who charged it must immediately notify
    the owner; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the owner must, within seven days after being
    notified, submit a report to the Minister in a paper format or in an
    electronic format compatible with that used by the Minister,
    containing the information set out in Part 1 of Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging --- air-conditioning or
refrigeration

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person must not charge an air-conditioning system or refrigeration
  system with a halocarbon listed in any of items 1 to 9 of Schedule 1.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if the charge replaces
  a halocarbon that is recovered to service the system and the charge
  does not result in a net gain in the amount of halocarbon contained in
  the system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Charging --- fire-extinguishing system

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A person must not charge
a fire-extinguishing system with a halocarbon listed in any of items 1
to 9 of Schedule 1 unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the charge replaces a halocarbon that is recovered to
  service the system and the charge does not result in a net gain in the
  amount of halocarbon contained in the system;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the system is for use in a military vehicle;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the system is for use in a civilian aircraft; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person is authorized to charge the system by a
  permit issued under subsection 20(2).

## [Installation, Servicing, Recovery and Withdrawal from Use]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345774 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certified person

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** Only a certified person
may install, service or recover a halocarbon from an air-conditioning
system or refrigeration system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery of halocarbon

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certified person who installs or services an air-conditioning system
  or refrigeration system, and the responsible person who installs or
  services a solvent system, fire-extinguishing system or container,
  must recover any halocarbon that would otherwise be released during
  those procedures into a container designed to contain that specific
  type of halocarbon.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Recovery equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The equipment used to recover a halocarbon from a
  fire-extinguishing system must have a rated transfer efficiency of at
  least 99%.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Permanent withdrawal from use

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certified person who permanently withdraws from use an
  air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, and the responsible
  person who permanently withdraws from use a solvent system,
  fire-extinguishing system or container, must first

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} recover all halocarbon contained in the system or
    container into a container designed to contain that specific type of
    halocarbon; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} affix a notice to the system or container
    containing the information set out in Part 2 of Schedule 2.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a small
  air-conditioning system or refrigeration system if the system is being
  transferred to a new owner, it is in operating condition and its
  transfer does not result in the release of a halocarbon.

## [Leaks and Leak Tests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345789 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Frequency of leak tests

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** The certified person in
respect of a large air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, and
the responsible person in respect of a fire-extinguishing system or
container that contains or is designed to contain more than 10 kg of
halocarbon, must conduct a leak test of its components that contain a
halocarbon at least once every calendar year and no more than 15 months
since the previous test.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leak --- system

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** The certified person in
respect of an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, and the
responsible person in respect of a fire-extinguishing system, must, as
soon as possible in the circumstances after a leak from the system is
detected, and in any case within seven days after the leak is detected,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repair the leak;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} isolate the leaking portion of the system and recover
  the halocarbon from it; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} recover the halocarbon from the system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Leak --- container

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** As soon as possible in
the circumstances after a leak from a container is detected, and in any
case within seven days after the day on which the leak is detected, the
responsible person in respect of the container must repair the leak or
recover the halocarbon from the container.

## [Permits]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345799 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application for permit

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of a solvent system or fire-extinguishing system must submit to
  the Minister an application for a permit, on a form that the Minister
  provides, containing the information set out in Part 3 of Schedule 2,
  if the owner proposes to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} test a fire-extinguishing system in a military
    vehicle in accordance with subparagraph 3(2)(d)(ii);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} install a fire-extinguishing system mentioned in
    section 4;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} install or use a solvent system mentioned in
    subsection 5(2); or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} charge a fire-extinguishing system mentioned in
    section 13.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Issuance of permit

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the information required under subsection (1) has
  been provided and the Minister determines that no technically or
  financially feasible alternative to the use of the halocarbon exists
  that could have a less harmful impact on the environment or on human
  life or health, the Minister must issue a permit that is valid for one
  year beginning on the day of issuance in the case of a solvent system
  or for three years beginning on that day in the case of a
  fire-extinguishing system.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cancellation of permit

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may cancel a permit if any false or misleading information
  has been submitted in support of the application for the permit.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of cancellation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before cancelling a permit, the Minister must notify
  the permit holder of the proposed cancellation and the reasons for it
  and give them an opportunity to make verbal or written representations
  in respect of the cancellation.

## [Inventory, Activity Logs and Reports]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345814 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Creation of inventory

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** Before the first
anniversary of the day on which these Regulations come into force, the
owner of a large air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, or of
a solvent system, fire-extinguishing system or container that contains
or is designed to contain more than 10 kg of halocarbon, must establish
and maintain an inventory containing the information set out in Part 4
of Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity log --- large system

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  responsible person in respect of a large air-conditioning system or
  refrigeration system, or a solvent system, fire-extinguishing system
  or container that contains or is designed to contain more than 10 kg
  of halocarbon, must maintain an activity log containing the
  information set out in Part 5 of Schedule 2 in which an entry is
  recorded for each time the system or container is installed, serviced
  or permanently withdrawn from use or ownership has been transferred.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Activity log --- small system

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person in respect of a small
  air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, or a solvent system,
  fire-extinguishing system or container that contains or is designed to
  contain 10 kg or less of halocarbon, must maintain an activity log
  containing the information set out in Part 5 of Schedule 2 in which an
  entry is recorded for each time the system or container is serviced.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Reports --- release of 100 kg or more

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a release
of 100 kg or more of halocarbon from an air-conditioning system or
refrigeration system, solvent system, fire-extinguishing system or
container, the owner of the system or container must submit the
following reports to the Minister, within the applicable period:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} within 24 hours after the release is detected, a
  verbal report or a report in a paper format or in an electronic format
  compatible with that used by the Minister containing the information
  set out in Part 6 of Schedule 2; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the release is detected, a
  report in a paper format or in an electronic format compatible with
  that used by the Minister, containing the information set out in Part
  7 of Schedule 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Report --- release of less than 100 kg

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** In the event of a release
of more than 10 kg but less than 100 kg of halocarbon from an
air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, solvent system,
fire-extinguishing system or container, the owner of the system or
container must submit a report in a paper format or in an electronic
format compatible with that used by the Minister, containing the
information set out in Part 7 of Schedule 2 within the applicable
period:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} no later than July 31 for releases detected during
  the period that begins on January 1 and ends on June 30 of the same
  year; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} no later than January 31 of the following year for
  releases detected during the period that begins on July 1 and ends on
  December 31.

## [Retention of Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1345830 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention of documents

- **[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner of an air-conditioning system or refrigeration system, solvent
  system, fire-extinguishing system or container must retain on the site
  where the system or container is located all documents or copies of
  those documents required under these Regulations for a period of at
  least five years after the day on which they are prepared or
  submitted, and must provide them to the Minister on request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Retention at principal place of
  business

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The owner must retain at their principal place of
  business in Canada a copy of the documents for each system or
  container that is located in a means of transportation, on a site to
  which access is limited for at least four months in a year or on a
  site where the owner is not present regularly, and must provide them
  to the Minister on request.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electronic format

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Documents referred to in subsection (1) or (2) may be
  retained in electronic format compatible with that used by the
  Minister.
