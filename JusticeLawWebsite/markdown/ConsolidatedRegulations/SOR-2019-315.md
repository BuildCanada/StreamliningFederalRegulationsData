## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197139 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission of surtax

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), remission is granted of the surtax, or any portion
  of the surtax, paid under the [Order Imposing a Surtax on the
  Importation of Certain Steel Goods](/eng/regulations/SOR-2018-206) in
  respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the goods described in paragraphs 1(c) to (i) of
    the schedule to that Order; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the goods expressly excluded from item 2 of the
    schedule to that Order.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Conditions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The remission referred to in subsection (1) is
  granted on condition that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no other claim for relief of the surtax, or the
    portion of the surtax, as applicable, has been granted under the
    [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) in respect of those goods; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is made by the importer to
    the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two
    years after the date of the importation of those goods.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1197149 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Registration

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
