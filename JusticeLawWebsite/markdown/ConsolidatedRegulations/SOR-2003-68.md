## [Prescribed Purpose or Circumstance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707989 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed purpose or circumstance

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 441(1)(d.1)(iii) of the Act, the prescribed purpose or the
prescribed circumstance, in the case of a property and casualty company
or a marine company, is a purpose or circumstance that is materially
related to the provision of financial products or services by the
company or a member of the company's group.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-296, s. 38

## [Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-707994 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed activity

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 495(4)(f) of the Act and subject to
  subsections (2) and (3), a prescribed activity in relation to an
  entity is designing, developing, holding, managing, manufacturing,
  selling or otherwise dealing with any data transmission system,
  information site, communication device or information platform or
  portal that is used to provide information services.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Limit on size of investment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Neither a property and casualty company nor a marine
  company may acquire control of, or hold, acquire or increase a
  substantial investment in, an entity engaging in an activity described
  in subsection (1) if the sum of the following exceeds 5% of the
  company's regulatory capital:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests that the company and its subsidiaries, whether
    individually or jointly, would acquire in the entity under paragraph
    495(4)(f) of the Act,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate balance sheet value of the shares and
    ownership interests held by the company and its subsidiaries,
    whether individually or jointly, in entities engaging in an activity
    described in subsection (1) that the company holds control of, or a
    substantial investment in, under paragraph 495(4)(f) of the Act, and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate value of outstanding loans made by
    the company and its subsidiaries, whether individually or jointly,
    to entities engaging in an activity described in subsection (1) that
    the company holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under
    paragraph 495(4)(f) of the Act.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Restricted activities

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Neither a property and casualty company nor a marine
  company may acquire control of, or hold, acquire or increase a
  substantial investment in, an entity engaging in an activity described
  in subsection (1) if the entity engages in the business of accepting
  deposit liabilities or if the activities of the entity include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} activities that a company is not permitted to
    engage in under section 466 or 469 of the Act or that a property and
    casualty company or a marine company is not permitted to engage in
    under section 478 of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any financial intermediary activity that exposes
    the entity to material market or credit risk, including the
    activities of a finance entity, a factoring entity and a financial
    leasing entity;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the activities of a specialized financing entity;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} dealing in securities, except as may be permitted
    under paragraph 495(4)(e) of the Act or as may be permitted to a
    company under paragraph 440(2)(b) of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} dealing in goods, wares or merchandise that a
    company is not permitted to deal in under subsection 441(3) of the
    Act, other than as permitted under subsection (1);

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} acquiring control of or acquiring or holding a
    substantial investment in another entity unless

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is controlled by
      the property and casualty company or the marine company, the
      company itself would be permitted under Part IX of the Act to
      acquire a substantial investment in the other entity, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an entity that is not controlled
      by the property and casualty company or the marine company, the
      company itself would be permitted to acquire a substantial
      investment in the other entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph
      493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection 495(1) or (4) of the Act; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} any activity prescribed under paragraph 495(5)(e)
    of the Act.

## [Exemption from Restrictions on Investments]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708016 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption from restrictions

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subparagraph 3(3)(f)(ii), subsections 495(6) to (8) of the Act do not
apply in determining whether a property and casualty company or a marine
company would be permitted to acquire a substantial investment in an
entity under subsection 493(2), paragraph 493(3)(b) or (c) or subsection
495(1) or (4) of the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-296, s. 40

## [Non-application of Subsection 495(7) of the Act]{.HTitleText1} {#h-708021 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 495(7) of the
Act does not apply if, under paragraph 495(4)(f) of the Act, a property
and casualty company or a marine company acquires control of, or
acquires or increases a substantial investment in, an entity whose
business is limited to activities described in subsection 3(1). Nothing
in this section limits the operation of subsections 3(2) and (3).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-296, s. 40
