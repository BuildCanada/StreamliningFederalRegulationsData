## Postal Imports Remission Order {#postal-imports-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/85-181
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1985-10-16

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties, Sales and Excise Taxes
on Certain Goods Imported by Mail

P.C. 1985-2954 1985-10-03
::::

:::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of National Revenue and the Treasury Board and pursuant to
section 17[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote_e){#footnote_e-ID0EHBAA} of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby to
revoke the [Postal Imports Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-181),
C.R.C., c. 779, and, considering that it is in the public interest to do
so, is pleased to substitute therefor, the annexed Order respecting the
remission of customs duties, sales and excise taxes on certian goods
imported by mail.

  1980-81-82-83, c. 170, s. 4
  :::
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639775 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Postal Imports Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-85-181).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639777 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[cannabis product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1); ([produit du
    cannabis]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, with the exception of a publication
    or book classified under tariff item No. 9812.00.00 in the List of
    Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011), does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} alcoholic beverages, cannabis products, vaping
      products, cigars, cigarettes and manufactured tobacco;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} goods classified under tariff item No. 9816.00.00
      in the List of Tariff Provisions set out in the schedule to the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) and goods for which the value
      for duty is reduced by the application of section 85 of the
      [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} books, newspapers, magazines, periodicals and
      other similar publications, where the supplier is required to
      register under Subdivision d of Division V of Part IX of the
      [Excise Tax Act](/eng/acts/E-15) and is not so
      registered; ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[vaping product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    section 2 of the [Excise Act, 2001](/eng/acts/E-14.1). ([produit de
    vapotage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639786 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order does not apply
to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} imported goods that are purchased from a retailer in
  Canada and mailed to the purchaser directly from a place situated out
  of Canada;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported goods that are purchased or ordered through
  or from an address, a post office box or a telephone in Canada; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} goods imported by a person other than the person in
  Canada who ordered or purchased the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-129, s. 1
:::

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639793 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 5,
remission is hereby granted of the customs duties and excise taxes paid
or payable on goods imported by mail and having a value for duty not
exceeding \$20.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/92-129, s. 1
:::

## [Condition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-639797 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Where the benefit of
remission is not received at the time of importation, the remission
granted under section 4 is granted on the condition that a claim for
remission is made to the Minister of National Revenue within two years
after the date of importation of the goods for which remission is
claimed.
