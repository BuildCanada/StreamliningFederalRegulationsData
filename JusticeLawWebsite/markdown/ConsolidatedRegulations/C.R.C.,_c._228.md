## Prince Edward Island Hog Order {#prince-edward-island-hog-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 228
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Order Granting Authority to Regulate the Marketing in Interprovincial
and Export Trade of Hogs Produced in Prince Edward Island
::::

:::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537044 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Prince Edward Island Hog Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._228).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537046 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward Island Natural
    Products Marketing Act; ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Prince Edward
    Island Hog Commodity Marketing Board established pursuant to the
    Act; ([Office de commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[hog]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any member of the species [Sus
    Scrofa L.]{.otherLang lang="la"} (domestic pig) produced in Prince
    Edward Island; ([porc]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Plan]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any plan for the marketing of
    hogs established and amended from time to time pursuant to the Act.
    ([Plan]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Interprovincial and Export Trade]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537052 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized to regulate the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and
export trade and for such purposes may, with respect to persons and
property situated within the Province of Prince Edward Island, exercise
all or any powers like the powers exercisable by it in relation to the
marketing of hogs locally within that Province under the Act and the
Plan.

## [Levies]{.HTitleText1} {#h-537054 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The Commodity Board is
authorized,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Revoked, SOR/80-90\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in relation to the powers granted to it by section 3
  with respect to the marketing of hogs in interprovincial and export
  trade,

to make orders fixing levies or charges and imposing them upon and
collecting them from persons described in section 3 who are engaged in
the marketing of hogs, and for such purposes classifying such persons
into groups and fixing the levies or charges payable by the members of
the different groups in different amounts, and the Commodity Board may
use such levies or charges for its purposes, including the creation of
reserves, the payment of expenses and losses resulting from the sale or
disposal of hogs, and the equalization or adjustment among producers of
hogs of moneys realized from the sale thereof during such period or
periods of time as it may determine.
