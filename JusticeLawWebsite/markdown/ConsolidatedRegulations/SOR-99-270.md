## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030274 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01).
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[authorized type of insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} credit or charge card-related insurance;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} creditors' disability insurance;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} creditors' life insurance;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} creditors' loss of employment insurance;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} creditors' vehicle inventory insurance;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} export credit insurance;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} mortgage insurance; or

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} travel insurance. ([assurance
      autorisée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[bank web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[bank web page]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a web page that an
    authorized foreign bank uses in relation to its business in Canada,
    including any information provided by the authorized foreign bank
    that is accessible on a telecommunications device. It does not
    include a web page that is only accessible by employees or agents of
    the authorized foreign bank. ([page Web de banque]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[credit or charge card-related insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of an authorized foreign bank, means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to the holder of a credit
    or charge card issued by the authorized foreign bank as a feature of
    the card, without request and without an individual assessment of
    risk,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} against loss of, or damage to, goods purchased
      with the card;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} under which the insurance company undertakes to
      extend a warranty provided by the manufacturer of goods purchased
      with the card; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} against any loss arising from a contractual
      liability assumed by the holder when renting a vehicle, when the
      rental is paid for with the card. ([assurance carte de crédit ou
      de paiement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' disability insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect
    of an authorized foreign bank, means a group insurance policy that
    will pay all or part of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the
    authorized foreign bank, in the event of bodily injury to, or an
    illness or disability of,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, any
      director or officer of the body corporate; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the authorized foreign bank.
      ([assurance-invalidité de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' life insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    authorized foreign bank, means a group insurance policy that will
    pay to the authorized foreign bank all or part of the amount of a
    debt of a debtor or, where a debt is in respect of a small business
    or a farm, fishery or ranch, all or part of the amount of the credit
    limit of a line of credit, in the event of the death of

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a natural person, the debtor
      or the spouse or common-law partner of the debtor;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is a body corporate, a director
      or officer of the body corporate; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} where the debtor is an entity, any natural person
      who is essential to the ability of the debtor to meet the debtor's
      financial obligations to the authorized foreign bank.
      ([assurance-vie de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' loss of employment insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of an authorized foreign bank, means a policy of an
    insurance company that will pay, without any individual assessment
    of risk, all or part of the amount of a debt of a debtor to the
    authorized foreign bank, in the event that

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the debtor, if the debtor is a natural person,
      becomes involuntarily unemployed; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a natural person who is a guarantor of all or
      part of the debt becomes involuntarily unemployed. ([assurance
      crédit en cas de perte d'emploi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[creditors' vehicle inventory insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in
    respect of an authorized foreign bank, means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance against direct and
    accidental loss or damage to vehicles that are held in stock for
    display and sale purposes by a debtor of the authorized foreign
    bank, some or all of which have been financed by the authorized
    foreign bank; ([assurance crédit pour stocks de
    véhicules]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[export credit insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a policy of an
    insurance company that provides insurance to an exporter of goods or
    services against a loss incurred by the exporter due to a
    non-payment for exported goods or services. ([assurance crédit des
    exportateurs]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[group insurance policy]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    authorized foreign bank, means a contract of insurance between an
    insurance company and the authorized foreign bank that provides
    insurance severally in respect of a group of identifiable persons
    who individually hold certificates of insurance. ([police
    d'assurance collective]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    approved, registered or otherwise authorized to insure risks under
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province. ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[line of credit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a commitment on the
    part of an authorized foreign bank to lend to a debtor, without a
    predetermined repayment schedule, one or more amounts, where the
    aggregate amount outstanding does not exceed a predetermined credit
    limit, which limit does not exceed the reasonable credit needs of
    the debtor. ([marge de crédit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mortgage insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of an
    authorized foreign bank, means an insurance policy that provides
    insurance to the authorized foreign bank against loss caused by a
    default on the part of a debtor who is a natural person under a loan
    from the authorized foreign bank that is secured by a mortgage on
    real property or on an interest in real property. ([assurance
    hypothèque]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[personal accident insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a group
    insurance policy that provides insurance to a natural person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay
      one or more sums of money in the event of bodily injury to, or the
      death of, the person that is caused by an accident; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to pay a
      certain sum for each day that the person is hospitalized in the
      event of bodily injury to the person that is caused by an accident
      or in the event of an illness or disability of the person.
      ([assurance accidents corporels]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[small business]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a business that is or,
    if it were incorporated, would be a small business corporation
    within the meaning of subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3). ([petite entreprise]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[travel insurance]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a policy of an insurance company that provides
      insurance to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person
      away from the place where the person ordinarily resides, without
      any individual assessment of risk, against

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} loss that results from the cancellation or
        interruption of the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} loss of, or damage to, personal property that
        occurs while on the trip, or

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} loss that is caused by the delayed arrival of
        personal baggage while on the trip; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a group insurance policy that provides insurance
      to a natural person in respect of a trip by the person away from
      the province in which the person ordinarily resides

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from an illness or the disability of the person that
        occurs on the trip,

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred while on the trip
        that result from bodily injury to, or the death of, the person
        caused by an accident while on the trip,

      - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} whereby the insurance company undertakes to
        pay one or more sums of money in the event of an illness or the
        disability of the person that occurs on the trip, or of bodily
        injury to, or the death of, the person that is caused by an
        accident while on the trip,

      - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} against expenses incurred by the person for
        dental care necessitated by an accident while on the trip, or

      - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in the event that the person dies while on the
        trip, against expenses incurred for the return of that person's
        remains to the place where the person was ordinarily resident
        before death, or for travel expenses incurred by a relative of
        that person who must travel to identify that person's remains.
        ([assurance voyage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2001-189, s. 1
- SOR/2002-268, s. 1
- SOR/2011-184, s. 1
:::

**[[1.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1.1 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
these Regulations, a web page is not a bank web page by reason only that
the web page provides access to a bank web page or promotes the business
of an authorized foreign bank in Canada.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-184, s. 2
:::

## [Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030330 .Part}

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorized foreign bank may administer an authorized type of insurance
  and personal accident insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorized foreign bank may administer a group
  insurance policy for

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} its employees or the employees of an entity that it
    holds control of, or a substantial investment in, under section
    522.07 or 522.08 or subsection 522.32(1) or (3) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employees of an entity that an affiliate of the
    authorized foreign bank holds control of, or a substantial
    investment in, under section 522.07 or 522.08 or subsection
    522.32(1) or (3) of the Act; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employees in Canada of an affiliate of the
    authorized foreign bank that is an entity referred to in section
    522.17 or 522.18 of the Act and that carries on a business described
    in one of those sections.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-268, s. 2
:::

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorized foreign bank may provide advice regarding an authorized
  type of insurance or a service in respect of an authorized type of
  insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An authorized foreign bank may provide advice in
  respect of an insurance policy that is not of an authorized type of
  insurance, or services in respect thereof, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the advice is general in nature;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advice is not in respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a specific risk, proposal in respect of life
      insurance, insurance policy or service, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a particular insurance company, agent or broker;
      and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the authorized foreign bank does not thereby refer
    any person to a particular insurance company, agent or broker.

## [Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030347 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An authorized foreign bank
shall not, in relation to its business in Canada, promote an insurance
company, agent or broker unless

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the company, agent or broker deals only in authorized
  types of insurance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
  authorized foreign bank and is directed to

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued by
    the authorized foreign bank who regularly receive statements of
    account or notices of those statements,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the authorized foreign bank's customers who
    are natural persons and who regularly receive statements of account
    or notices of those statements, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-184, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-4-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 4"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authorized foreign bank shall not, in relation to its business in
  Canada, promote an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent or
  broker, or a service in respect of such a policy, unless

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the policy is of an authorized type of insurance or
    the service is in respect of such a policy;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the policy is to be provided by a corporation
    without share capital, other than a mutual insurance company or a
    fraternal benefit society, that carries on business without
    pecuniary gain to its members and the policy provides insurance to a
    natural person in respect of the risks covered by travel insurance;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the policy is a personal accident insurance policy
    and the promotion takes place outside a branch of the authorized
    foreign bank;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the service is in respect of a policy referred to
    in paragraph (b) or of a policy referred to in paragraph (c) that is
    promoted as described in that paragraph; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the promotion takes place outside a branch of the
    authorized foreign bank and is directed to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} all the holders of credit or charge cards issued
      by the authorized foreign bank who regularly receive statements of
      account or notices of those statements,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} all the authorized foreign bank's customers who
      are natural persons and who regularly receive statements of
      account or notices of those statements, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the general public.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Notwithstanding subsection (1) and section 4, an
  authorized foreign bank may exclude from a promotion referred to in
  paragraph (1)(e) or 4(b) persons

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of whom the promotion would contravene
    an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} who have notified the authorized foreign bank in
    writing that they do not wish to receive promotional material from
    the authorized foreign bank; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} who are holders of a credit or charge card that was
    issued by the authorized foreign bank and in respect of which the
    account is not in good standing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-184, s. 4
:::

## [Web Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030372 .Part}

- **[[5.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  promotion referred to in paragraph 4(b) may take place on a bank web
  page if it relates to an insurance company, agent or broker that deals
  only in authorized types of insurance and the promotion referred to in
  paragraph 5(1)(c) or (e) may take place on a bank web page if it
  relates to only an authorized type of insurance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} However, an authorized foreign bank shall not, on a
  bank web page, provide access to a web page --- directly or through
  another web page --- through which there is promotion of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company, agent or broker that does not
    deal only in authorized types of insurance; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance policy of an insurance company, agent
    or broker, or a service in respect of such a policy, that is not of
    only an authorized type of insurance.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-184, s. 5
:::

## [Prohibited Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1030380 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  authorized foreign bank shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} provide, directly or indirectly, an insurance
    company, agent or broker with any information respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the authorized foreign bank in
      Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the authorized
      foreign bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is an entity with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is a partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} permit any of its subsidiaries to provide, directly
    or indirectly, an insurance company, agent or broker with any
    information that it receives from the authorized foreign bank
    respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the authorized foreign bank in
      Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the authorized
      foreign bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is an entity with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is a partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner;

  - [(b.1)]{.lawlabel} provide an affiliate of the authorized foreign
    bank that is not a subsidiary of the authorized foreign bank with
    information unless the authorized foreign bank ensures that the
    affiliate does not directly or indirectly provide the information to
    an insurance company, agent or broker if the information is in
    respect of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the authorized foreign bank in
      Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the authorized
      foreign bank in Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is an entity with members in Canada, any such member,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the authorized foreign bank
      is a partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} permit a subsidiary of the authorized foreign bank
    that is a trust or loan corporation incorporated by or under an Act
    of the legislature of a province to provide, directly or indirectly,
    an insurance company, agent or broker with any information
    respecting

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a customer of the subsidiary in Canada,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a customer of the subsidiary in
      Canada,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is an entity
      with members in Canada, any such member, or

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} where a customer of the subsidiary is a
      partnership with partners in Canada, any such partner.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of an
  authorized foreign bank or a subsidiary of an authorized foreign bank
  that is a trust or loan corporation incorporated by or under an Act of
  the legislature of a province where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the authorized foreign bank or the subsidiary has
    established procedures to ensure that the information referred to in
    that subsection will not be used by an insurance company, agent or
    broker to promote in Canada the insurance company, agent or broker
    or to promote in Canada an insurance policy, or a service in respect
    thereof; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the insurance company, agent or broker, as the case
    may be, has given an undertaking to the authorized foreign bank or
    to the subsidiary, in a form acceptable to the Superintendent, that
    that information will not be used to promote in Canada the insurance
    company, agent or broker or to promote in Canada an insurance
    policy, or a service in respect thereof.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2002-268, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** No authorized foreign bank
shall provide a telecommunications device that is primarily for the use
of customers in Canada and that links a customer with an insurance
company, agent or broker.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** An authorized foreign bank
shall not carry on business in Canada on premises that are adjacent to
the premises of an insurance company, agent or broker unless the
authorized foreign bank clearly indicates to its customers that its
business and the premises on which its business is carried on are
separate and distinct from those of the insurance company, agent or
broker.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2011-184, s. 6
:::
