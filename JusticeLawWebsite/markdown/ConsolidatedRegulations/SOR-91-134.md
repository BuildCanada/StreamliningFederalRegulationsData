## Prisoner-of-War Status Determination Regulations {#prisoner-of-war-status-determination-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-134
:::

[GENEVA CONVENTIONS ACT](/eng/acts/G-3)

Registration 1991-02-01

Regulations Respecting the Determination of the Entitlement of Persons
Detained by the Canadian Forces to Prisoner-of-War Status
::::

::::::::::::::: section
::::: {.section .order}
The Minister of National Defence, pursuant to section 8 of the [Geneva
Conventions Act](/eng/acts/G-3), hereby makes the annexed Regulations
respecting the determination of the entitlement of persons detained by
the Canadian Forces to prisoner-of-war status.

January 25, 1991

::: FlushRight
BILL MCKNIGHT
:::

::: FlushRight
*Minister of National Defence*
:::
:::::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932693 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Prisoner-of-War Status Determination
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-134).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932695 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[detainee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[detainee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person in the custody of a
    unit or other element of the Canadian Forces who has committed a
    belligerent act; ([personne détenue]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[non-commissioned member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[non-commissioned member]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning
    as in section 2 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);
    ([militaire du rang]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in section 2
    of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5);
    ([officier]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[prisoner-of-war status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[prisoner-of-war status]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means prisoner-of-war
    status under Article 4 of the Geneva Convention relative to the
    Treatment of Prisoners of War of August 12, 1949 or under Section II
    of Part III of the Protocol additional to the Geneva Conventions of
    12 August 1949, and relating to the protection of victims of
    international armed conflicts; ([statut de prisonnier de
    guerre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tribunal]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a prisoner-of-war status
    tribunal established pursuant to section 4.
    ([tribunal]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Authorities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932702 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The following authorities
may establish a tribunal:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Minister of National Defence;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief of the Defence Staff;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} an officer commanding a command;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an officer commanding a formation; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} any other authority that the Chief of the Defence
  Staff may prescribe or appoint.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 1
:::

## [Prisoner-of-war Status Tribunals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932711 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A tribunal shall consist of
one officer of the Legal Branch of the Canadian Forces who shall be
appointed, with the concurrence of the Judge Advocate General, by the
authority who establishes the tribunal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 2
:::

## [Function of Tribunal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932715 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A tribunal shall, when
directed to do so by the authority who established the tribunal, hold a
hearing to determine whether a detainee brought before it is entitled to
prisoner-of-war status.

## [Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932717 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} In
  carrying out its function, a tribunal may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to subsection (2), order the appearance of
    any witness subject to the Code of Service Discipline, as defined in
    section 2 of the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), and request
    the appearance of any other witness;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} require the production of documentary and real
    evidence under the control of the Canadian Forces; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} require each witness to testify under oath or
    solemn affirmation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A tribunal may not compel the detainee whose
  entitlement to prisoner-of-war status is to be determined by the
  tribunal to testify.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 3
:::

## [Screening of Detainees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932726 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The commanding officer of a
unit or other element of the Canadian Forces shall ensure that each
detainee is screened as soon as is practicable after being taken into
custody by the unit or element, to determine

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} whether or not the detainee is entitled to
  prisoner-of-war status; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} whether there is doubt with respect to the detainee's
  entitlement to prisoner-of-war status.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 4
:::

## [Request for Determination of Status]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932732 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where a
  commanding officer believes that there may be doubt whether a detainee
  is entitled to prisoner-of-war status, the commanding officer shall
  request, as soon as is practicable, an authority referred to in
  section 3 to direct that a tribunal hold a hearing to determine the
  detainee's entitlement to that status.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  accompanied by a summary of the information on which it was determined
  that there was doubt with respect to the detainee's entitlement to
  prisoner-of-war status.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The authority shall review the summary referred to in
  subsection (2) and shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where the authority is satisfied that the detainee
    is entitled to prisoner-of-war status, direct the commanding officer
    to accord that status to the detainee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where the authority is satisfied that the detainee
    is not entitled to prisoner-of-war status, direct the commanding
    officer not to accord that status to the detainee; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} where the authority is in doubt with respect to the
    entitlement of the detainee to prisoner-of-war status, direct a
    tribunal to hold a hearing to determine whether the detainee is
    entitled to prisoner-of-war status and inform the commanding officer
    of the direction.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Where a direction is made pursuant to paragraph
  (3)(c), the commanding officer shall cause the detainee to be informed
  of the direction and to be given reasonable notice of the time, date
  and place of the hearing.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 5
:::

## [Investigating Member]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932743 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  authority who directs a tribunal to hold a hearing to determine a
  detainee's entitlement to prisoner-of-war status shall appoint an
  officer or a non-commissioned member to act as investigating member
  for the tribunal.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The investigating member shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} perform the clerical and preliminary work for the
    tribunal hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} arrange for the attendance of witnesses;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} gather, and present to the tribunal during the
    hearing, all available evidence relevant to the prisoner-of-war
    status of the detainee; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} obtain such additional evidence as the tribunal
    directs.

## [Representation of the Detainee]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932751 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  detainee has the right to be represented before a tribunal by an
  assisting member appointed pursuant to subsection (2) or (3).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as is practicable after a direction pursuant
  to paragraph 8(3)(c) is made, an officer or a non-commissioned member
  shall be appointed, by or under the authority of the commanding
  officer, as an assisting member.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A particular assisting member may be requested by the
  detainee and shall be appointed where exigencies of the service permit
  and the member is willing to act in that capacity.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The assisting member shall, to the extent requested
  by the detainee,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} assist the detainee in preparing for the hearing;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} represent the detainee during the hearing; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} assist the detainee in preparing any written
    representations where, under subsection 17(1), the detainee requests
    a review of the tribunal's determination.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Nothing in this section affects the discretion of a
  tribunal to permit additional assistance or representation in
  appropriate circumstances.

## [Interpreter]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932761 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** A detainee who does not
understand the language in which a hearing is to be conducted is
entitled

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the assistance of a qualified interpreter to
  enable the detainee to prepare for and participate in the hearing; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to make written representations to the authority who
  directed the tribunal to make a determination, where, pursuant to
  subsection 17(1), the detainee requests a review of the determination.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 6
:::

## [Interim Status]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932767 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** A detainee whose
entitlement to prisoner-of-war status is in doubt shall be treated as a
prisoner of war until a tribunal determines that the detainee is not
entitled to that status and, where a review of the tribunal's
determination is requested under subsection 17(1), until the completion
of the review.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 7(F)
:::

## [Hearing]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932771 .Part}

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** A tribunal shall hold a
hearing to determine a detainee's entitlement to prisoner-of-war status
in accordance with the following procedure:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the direction to hold the hearing shall be read;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the tribunal shall ask the detainee whether further
  time is required to prepare for the hearing and shall grant any
  reasonable adjournment requested for that purpose;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the investigating member shall present the relevant
  evidence without regard to whether the evidence tends to prove or
  disprove that the detainee is entitled to prisoner-of-war status;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the detainee may present relevant evidence;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} at the conclusion of the presentation of the
  evidence, the tribunal may recall witnesses or call additional
  witnesses;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} after all the evidence has been presented, an address
  to the tribunal may be made first by the investigating officer and
  then by the detainee;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} after hearing the addresses, the tribunal shall

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the evidence establishes on a balance of
    probabilities that the detainee is not entitled to prisoner-of-war
    status, determine that the detainee is not entitled to that status,
    or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, determine that the detainee is
    entitled to prisoner-of-war status; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the tribunal shall announce its determination, give
  the reasons therefor and inform the detainee of the detainee's right
  under subsection 17(1) to request a review of the determination.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 8(F)
:::

## [Special Cases]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932785 .Part}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** In cases not provided for
in these Regulations, the tribunal may, in carrying out its function,
determine the procedure to be followed.

## [Evidence in Camera]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932787 .Part}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  tribunal may hear evidence [in camera]{.otherLang lang="la"} and in
  the absence of the detainee where the tribunal considers it necessary
  to do so in the interest of national security.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The tribunal shall give the detainee an oral or a
  written summary of any evidence heard in the absence of the detainee
  that, in the opinion of the tribunal, would not be injurious to
  national security.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/96-85, s. 9(F)
:::

## [Minutes of Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932793 .Part}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  tribunal shall keep, or cause to be kept, minutes of proceedings in
  respect of each hearing.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The minutes of proceedings shall contain

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the direction to determine the status of
    the detainee;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a record of the time and place of the hearing and
    the names of the persons present;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a summary of the testimony of all witnesses heard
    by the tribunal;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all real and documentary evidence considered by the
    tribunal; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a record of the tribunal's determination and the
    reasons therefor and the time and date when the determination was
    announced.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The tribunal shall forward the minutes of proceedings
  to the authority who directed the tribunal to make a determination.

## [Review of Determination]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932803 .Part}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  detainee whose prisoner-of-war status has been determined pursuant to
  section 13 may, within 24 hours after the tribunal announces its
  determination, request a review of the determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The request referred to in subsection (1) shall be in
  writing and shall be given to the commanding officer of the unit or
  element that has custody of the detainee.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Where a request is made under subsection (1), the
  authority who directed the tribunal to make a determination shall
  review the determination.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Any written representations to the authority that the
  detainee wishes to make shall be submitted to the commanding officer
  within seven days after the determination is announced.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The commanding officer shall forward the request and
  any written representations to the authority.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The review shall consist of a review of the minutes
  of proceedings and any written representations made by the detainee.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The authority may, on completion of the review,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} substitute the authority's own determination of the
    detainee's entitlement to prisoner-of-war status for the
    determination of the tribunal;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} set aside the determination of the tribunal and
    direct that another tribunal hold a hearing to determine whether the
    detainee is entitled to prisoner-of-war status; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} confirm the determination of the tribunal.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} The authority shall give written reasons for the
  decision made under subsection (7).

## [Witness Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932816 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** Any person who is not
subject to the Code of Service Discipline, as defined in section 2 of
the [National Defence Act](/eng/acts/N-5), and who attends as a witness
at a tribunal hearing is entitled to compensation for such expenses as
the tribunal considers reasonable in the circumstances.
