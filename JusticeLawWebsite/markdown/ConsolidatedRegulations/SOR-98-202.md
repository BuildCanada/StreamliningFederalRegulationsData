## Conditions of Transferring Firearms and Other Weapons Regulations {#conditions-of-transferring-firearms-and-other-weapons-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-202
:::

[FIREARMS ACT](/eng/acts/F-11.6)

Registration 1998-03-24

Conditions of Transferring Firearms and Other Weapons Regulations

P.C. 1998-477 1998-03-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 118 of the [Firearms
Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDABA}, the Minister
of Justice had the proposed [Conditions of Transferring Firearms and
other Weapons Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-202), substantially
in the annexed form, laid before each House of Parliament on November
27, 1996 and October 30, 1997, which dates are at least 30 sitting days
before the date of this Order;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to paragraphs 23(e)
and 24(2)(d), section 26, paragraphs 27(d), 30(c) and 32(a) and (c) and
section 117 of the [Firearms Act](/eng/acts/F-11.6)^a^, hereby makes the
annexed [Conditions of Transferring Firearms and other Weapons
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-202).

## [Transfers of Firearms]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019208 .Part}

### [Conditions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019209 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of paragraph 23.2(1)(f) of the Act, a transferor must meet
  the condition that they provide the Registrar with the names and the
  licence numbers of the transferor and the transferee.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-279, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of paragraph 23.2(1)(f) of the Act,
  the transferee must meet the following conditions:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the transferee is an individual and the firearm
    is a restricted firearm or a handgun referred to in subsection
    12(6.1) of the Act, the transferee must inform the chief firearms
    officer of their reasons

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} for needing the restricted firearm or handgun

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} to protect the life of that individual or of
        other individuals, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} for use in connection with his or her lawful
        profession or occupation, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for wishing to acquire the restricted firearm or
      handgun

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} for use in target practice, or a target
        shooting competition, under conditions specified in an
        authorization to transport or under the auspices of a shooting
        club or shooting range that is approved under section 29 of the
        Act, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} to form part of a gun collection of the
        individual, in the case of an individual who satisfies the
        criteria described in section 30 of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the transferee wishes to acquire a restricted
    firearm or a handgun referred to in subsection 12(6.1) of the Act to
    form part of their gun collection, the transferee must provide the
    chief firearms officer with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} information regarding the transferee's knowledge
      of the historical, technological or scientific characteristics
      that relate to or distinguish the restricted firearms or handguns
      that he or she possesses,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the transferee's signed consent to the periodic
      inspection, conducted in a reasonable manner, of the premises in
      which the restricted firearms or handguns are to be kept, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} details of the transferee's understanding of
      the requirements set out in the [Storage, Display, Transportation
      and Handling of Firearms by Individuals
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-209) respecting the secure
      storage of restricted firearms or handguns.

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2004-279, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

### [Condition Precedent Before Authorizing a Transfer]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019228 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The chief firearms officer
may only authorize the transfer of a restricted firearm or prohibited
firearm if the officer determines that it is not contrary to the
interests of the safety of the transferee or any other person to
transfer the firearm.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2004-279, s. 4
:::

### [Handguns]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1378321 .Subheading}

**[[4.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4.1 .sectionLabel}** The chief firearms
officer may authorize the transfer of a handgun to an individual only if
the individual needs the handgun

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to protect the life of that individual or of other
  individuals;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} for use in connection with his or her lawful
  profession or occupation; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} to train, compete or coach in a handgun shooting
  discipline that is on the programme of the International Olympic
  Committee or the International Paralympic Committee, and the
  individual provides a letter to a chief firearms officer from a
  provincial or national sport shooting governing body indicating

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that the individual trains, competes or coaches in
    such a discipline,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the specific discipline in which the individual
    trains, competes or coaches, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the handgun that the individual seeks to
    acquire is necessary for training, competing or coaching in that
    specific discipline.

### [Information Relating to Transferee's Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1349463 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The prescribed information
for the purpose of subsection 23(2) of the Act is the information set
out on the front of the transferee's licence, including the photograph.

::: HistoricalNote
- [SOR/2022-91, s. 7](#1344084-1349484){.wb-lbx
  aria-controls="centred-popup" ajax-after="1344084-1349484.html"
  role="button"}
:::

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of the
issuance of a reference number under section 23 of the Act, the
transferor must

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} confirm, when making a request referred to in
  paragraph 23(1)(b) of the Act to the Registrar, that they have taken
  reasonable steps to verify that the transferee is the holder of the
  licence, including

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a transfer that is completed in
    person, whether in whole or in part, by comparing the photograph on
    the licence with the person presenting themselves as the transferee,
    and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in all other cases,

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} by using the method set out in paragraph (a), or

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the comparison cannot be undertaken using that
      method, by comparing the information on the transferee's licence
      with that on another piece of photo identification that has been
      issued by the Government of Canada or a provincial or municipal
      government; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} provide the Registrar with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the transferee's licence number, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any other information requested by the Registrar.

- [SOR/2022-91, s. 7](#1344084-1349495){.wb-lbx

### [Transfers of Firearms]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019236 .Subheading}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(1) of the Act, a transferor must comply with the following
conditions to transfer a firearm to Her Majesty in right of Canada or a
province, to a police force or to a municipality:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the transferor must provide the Registrar with

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and licence number of the transferor,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the transfer,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the number of the registration certificate for
    the firearm and its firearms identification number, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the name and public agency identification number
    of the transferee; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the transferor must obtain a receipt from the
  transferee that identifies the date of the transfer and describes the
  firearm transferred.

### [Transfers of Prohibited Weapons, Restricted Weapons, Prohibited Devices, Ammunition and Prohibited Ammunition]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1019246 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 26(2) of the Act, to transfer a prohibited weapon, a
restricted weapon, a prohibited device, ammunition or prohibited
ammunition to Her Majesty in right of Canada or a province, to a police
force or to a municipality, a transferor must obtain a receipt from the
person accepting the transfer on behalf of Her Majesty in right of
Canada or a province or on behalf of a police force or a municipality
that identifies the date of the transfer and describing the goods
transferred.

## [Transfers by Mail]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1019250 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 32 of the Act, before transferring a firearm by mail, a
transferor must obtain all copies of registration certificates and
authorizations that the Act requires for the transfer to occur.
