## Order Respecting the Interim Federal Health Program, 2012 {#order-respecting-the-interim-federal-health-program-2012 .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2012-26
:::

Registration 2012-04-25

Order Respecting the Interim Federal Health Program, 2012

::: ReaderNote
\[Declared to be of no force or effect in Canadian Doctors For Refugee
Care v. Canada (Attorney General), 2014 FC 651, \[2015\] 2 FCR 267.\]
:::

P.C. 2012-433 2012-04-05
:::::

:::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, hereby

\(a\) repeals Order in Council P.C. 157-11/848 of June 20, 1957; and

\(b\) makes the annexed [Order Respecting the Interim Federal Health
Program, 2012](/eng/regulations/SI-2012-26).
:::

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618257 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Immigration and Refugee
    Protection Act](/eng/acts/I-2.5). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[condition of public safety concern]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[condition of public safety concern]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    mental health condition in a person who has been examined by a
    physician licensed in Canada and for which the physician is of the
    opinion that the person will likely cause harm to others. ([état
    préoccupant pour la sécurité publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[disease posing a risk to public health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[disease posing a risk to public health]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    a communicable disease

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that is on the list of national notifiable
      diseases of the Public Health Agency of Canada, as amended from
      time to time,

      - [(i)]{.lawlabel} which is subject to human-to-human transmission
        and requires public health intervention in accordance with
        provincial legislation, or

      - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for which immunization has been recommended
        under Canadian medical standards; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} that is referred to in the Interim Federal Health
      Program Policy of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration,
      as amended from time to time. ([maladie présentant un risque pour
      la santé publique]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[expanded health care coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[expanded health care coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means coverage
    for the following services and products provided in Canada, as
    defined in the Interim Federal Health Program Policy of the
    Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as amended from time to
    time:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hospital services;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} services of physicians licensed in Canada,
      registered nurses licensed in Canada and other health care
      professionals licensed in Canada;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} translation services for health purposes;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} laboratory, diagnostic and ambulance services;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} supplemental services; and

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} supplemental products. (*couverture des soins de
      santé élargie*)

[[health care coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[health care coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means coverage for the
    following services and products provided in Canada only if they are
    of an urgent or essential nature as defined in the Interim Federal
    Health Program Policy of the Department of Citizenship and
    Immigration, as amended from time to time:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hospital services;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} services of physicians licensed in Canada and
      registered nurses licensed in Canada;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} laboratory, diagnostic and ambulance services;
      and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immunization and medication, only if required to
      prevent or treat a disease posing a risk to public health or to
      treat a condition of public safety concern. ([couverture des soins
      de santé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[immigration medical examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[immigration medical examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    medical examination requested under paragraph 16(2)(b) of the Act
    and has the meaning assigned to the term [[medical
    examination]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 29 of the [Immigration
    and Refugee Protection Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).
    ([visite médicale d'immigration]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Citizenship
    and Immigration. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[person whose refugee claim has been rejected]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[person whose refugee claim has been rejected]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means a person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} whose claim for refugee protection has been
      finally rejected by the Immigration and Refugee Board and whose
      right to judicial review, or any appeal of that judicial review,
      in respect of that claim has been exhausted; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} whose claim is deemed to be rejected under
      subsections 105(3), 108(3) or 109(3) of the Act. ([personne dont
      la demande d'asile a été rejetée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[protected person]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 95(2) of the Act. ([personne protégée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[public health or public safety health care coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[public health or public safety health care
    coverage]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means coverage for the following
    services and products provided in Canada, only if required to
    diagnose, prevent or treat a disease posing a risk to public health
    or to diagnose or treat a condition of public safety concern:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} hospital services;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} services of physicians licensed in Canada and
      registered nurses licensed in Canada;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} laboratory and diagnostic services; and

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} immunization and medication. ([couverture des
      soins de santé pour la santé ou la sécurité
      publiques]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[refugee claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[refugee claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person whose claim
    for refugee protection is eligible to be referred to the Immigration
    and Refugee Board and who is awaiting a final determination of that
    claim by that Board, including a person whose right to judicial
    review of that determination, or appeal of that judicial review, has
    not been exhausted. This definition does not include a person whose
    refugee claim was determined to be abandoned or withdrawn.
    ([demandeur d'asile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resettled refugee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resettled refugee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person on whom
    refugee protection has been conferred under paragraph 95(1)(a) of
    the Act. ([réfugié réétabli]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, ss. 1, 6(F)
:::

## [Authorization]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618293 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The Minister is hereby
authorized to administer the Interim Federal Health Program in
accordance with this Order.

## [Powers of Minister]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618295 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may pay the cost of health care coverage incurred for
  protected persons, other than resettled refugees referred to in
  subsection 6.1(2), for a period set out in the Interim Federal Health
  Program Policy of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as
  amended from time to time.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister may pay the cost
  of health care coverage incurred for protected persons who are
  resettled refugees only while they are under sponsorship under the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-3-20120405.html "Link to previous version of section 3"){.PITLink}
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), the Minister may pay the cost of health
  care coverage and immigration medical examinations incurred for
  refugee claimants.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to pay, under
  subsection (1), the cost of health care coverage incurred for refugee
  claimants while their refugee claims are suspended under
  subsection 103(1) or 105(1) of the Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to pay, under
  subsection (1), the cost of health care coverage incurred for refugee
  claimants who are nationals of a country that is, when services or
  products are provided, designated under subsection 109.1(1) of the
  Act.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay the
cost of public health or public safety health care coverage incurred for
persons whose refugee claims have been rejected except for those whose
removal order has been enforced pursuant to section 240 of the
[Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227).

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay the
cost of public health or public safety health care coverage and
immigration medical examinations incurred for refugee claimants

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} while their refugee claims are suspended under
  subsection 103(1) or 105(1) of the Act; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} who are nationals of a country that is, when services
  or products are provided, designated under subsection 109.1(1) of the
  Act.

<!-- -->

- **[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister may pay the cost of expanded health care coverage and
  immigration medical examinations incurred in Canada for the following
  persons for a period set out in the Interim Federal Health Program
  Policy of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as amended
  from time to time:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} persons for whom the Minister exercises a power
    conferred under subsection 25.1(1) or 25.2(1) of the Act, if they
    are or were in receipt of governmental resettlement assistance in
    the form of income support as defined in the Interim Federal Health
    Program Policy of the Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as
    amended from time to time; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} persons who have been issued a temporary resident
    permit under section 24 of the Act, if it has been determined, in
    accordance with ministerial instructions made under subsection 24(3)
    of the Act, that they are or may be victims of human trafficking.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may pay the cost of expanded health care
  coverage incurred for resettled refugees, if they are or were in
  receipt of governmental resettlement assistance in the form of income
  support as defined in the Interim Federal Health Program Policy of the
  Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as amended from time to
  time, for a period set out in that Policy.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 3
:::

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Despite sections 3 to 6,
the Minister may, in exceptional and compelling circumstances, including
when the Minister exercises a power conferred under subsection 25.2(1)
of the Act, on his or her own initiative and for the period that he or
she determines, pay the cost incurred in Canada of any of the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} health care coverage;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} public health or public safety health care coverage;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} immigration medical examinations; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} immunization and medication, only if required to
  prevent or treat a disease posing a risk to public health or to treat
  a condition of public safety concern.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-7-20120405.html "Link to previous version of section 7"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** In order to protect the
health and safety of Canadians, the Minister may, on his or her own
initiative, in exceptional and compelling circumstances, pay the cost of
immigration medical examinations, medication or immunization incurred
outside Canada that are, in the opinion of the Minister, required prior
to departure for Canada for the following persons:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} resettled refugees; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} foreign nationals in respect of whom the Minister
  exercises a power conferred under subsection 25.2(1) of the Act.

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The Minister may pay the
cost of health care services and products incurred, in accordance with
the Interim Federal Health Program Policy of the Department of
Citizenship and Immigration, as amended from time to time, for foreign
nationals or permanent residents who are detained under the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 6(F)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-9-20120405.html "Link to previous version of section 9"){.PITLink}

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** Any payment referred to
in sections 3 to 9 shall be made in accordance with the benefit grids
referred to in the Interim Federal Health Program Policy of the
Department of Citizenship and Immigration, as amended from time to time.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 4

## [Restrictions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618332 .Part}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Minister is not authorized to make a payment under sections 3 to 6 for
  persons who are or were eligible under any provincial health insurance
  plan or program, whether or not an application has been made to the
  plan or program.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the Minister may pay the cost
  of immunization and medication incurred for protected persons who are
  resettled refugees while they are under sponsorship under the
  [Immigration and Refugee Protection
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227), but only if required to
  prevent or treat a disease posing a risk to public health or to treat
  a condition of public safety concern.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to make a payment
  under section 6.1 for persons referred to in that section for any
  services or products for which they are eligible under any provincial
  health insurance plan or program, without regard to the amount that
  may be recovered under that plan or program for those services or
  products.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to make a payment
  under section 9 for persons referred to in that section who are
  eligible under any provincial health insurance plan or program,
  whether or not an application has been made to the plan or program.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to make a payment
  under this Order for any services or products for which a person may
  make a claim under any private insurance plan, without regard to the
  amount that may be recovered under that plan for those services or
  products.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not authorized to make a payment
  under this Order for persons who are Canadian citizens.

::: HistoricalNote
- SI/2012-49, s. 5
:::

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618342 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** For greater certainty,
Order in Council P.C. 157-11/848 of June 20, 1957 continues to apply in
respect of health care services and products provided until June 29,
2012 and the Minister may pay the cost of health care services and
products provided after that date only in accordance with this Order.

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 4(3) and
paragraph 6(b) do not apply to a refugee claimant whose claim for
refugee protection was made prior to the day on which section 12 of the
Balanced Refugee Reform Act, chapter 8 of the Statutes of Canada, 2010,
comes into force.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-618345 .Part}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), this
  Order comes into force on June 30, 2012.

- [[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_IndCDD6_hq_12336){#fn_IndCDD6_hq_12336-ID0EBBBA}[(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection 4(3),
  paragraph 6(b) and section 12 come into force on the day on which
  section 12 of the Balanced Refugee Reform Act, chapter 8 of the
  Statutes of Canada, 2010, comes into force.
