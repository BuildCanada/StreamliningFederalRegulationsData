## [Qualification Required of Evaluating Officer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745310 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An evaluating officer must
be a certified drug recognition expert accredited by the International
Association of Chiefs of Police.

## [Physical Coordination Tests]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745312 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The physical coordination
tests to be conducted under paragraph 254(2)(a) of the [Criminal
Code](/eng/acts/C-46) are the following standard field sobriety tests:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the horizontal gaze nystagmus test;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the walk-and-turn test; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the one-leg stand test.

## [Evaluation Tests and Procedures]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745317 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The tests to be conducted
and the procedures to be followed during an evaluation under subsection
254(3.1) of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46) are

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a preliminary examination, which consists of
  measuring the pulse and determining that the pupils are the same size
  and that the eyes track an object equally;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} eye examinations, which consist of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the horizontal gaze nystagmus test,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the vertical gaze nystagmus test, and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the lack-of-convergence test;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} divided-attention tests, which consist of

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Romberg balance test,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the walk-and-turn test referred to in paragraph
    2(b),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the one-leg stand test referred to in paragraph
    2(c), and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the finger-to-nose test, which includes the test
    subject tilting the head back and touching the tip of their index
    finger to the tip of their nose in a specified manner while keeping
    their eyes closed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} an examination, which consists of measuring the blood
  pressure, temperature and pulse;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an examination of pupil sizes under light levels of
  ambient light, near total darkness and direct light and an examination
  of the nasal and oral cavities;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an examination, which consists of checking the muscle
  tone and pulse; and

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a visual examination of the arms, neck and, if
  exposed, the legs for evidence of injection sites.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-745333 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on July 2, 2008.
