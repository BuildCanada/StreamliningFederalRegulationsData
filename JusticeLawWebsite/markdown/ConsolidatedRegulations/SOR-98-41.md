## Prison Manufactured or Produced Goods Regulations, 1998 {#prison-manufactured-or-produced-goods-regulations-1998 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/98-41
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1997-12-29

Prison Manufactured or Produced Goods Regulations, 1998

P.C. 1997-2012  1997-12-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subparagraph 132(1)(m)(i) of the
[Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e){#fna_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes the annexed
[Prison Manufactured or Produced Goods Regulations,
1998](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-41).

  [[Return to footnote ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fna_e-ID0EDBAA){#fna_e}S.C.
  1997, c. 36
  :::
:::

## [Exclusion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1023212 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Tariff item 9897.00.00 of
the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011) is amended to exclude goods
manufactured or produced wholly or in part by prison labour, on the
condition that the goods are imported solely for personal use and not
for sale or for any business or occupational use, by:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a non-resident of Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a resident of Canada, reporting the goods at the time
  of his or her return to Canada.
