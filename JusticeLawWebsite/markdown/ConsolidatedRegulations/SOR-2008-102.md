## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742830 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations set out in Schedule
    III to the [Foreign Missions and International Organizations
    Act](/eng/acts/F-29.4). ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the meeting of the
    Organization to be held in Banff, Alberta from September 2 to
    6, 2008. ([réunion]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the intergovernmental
    conference known as the Conference of Defence Ministers of the
    Americas (CDMA). ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Preparatory Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Preparatory Meeting]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the planning and
    preparatory meeting of the Organization to be held in Banff, Alberta
    from May 14 to 16, 2008. ([réunion préparatoire]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742836 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** During the period beginning
on May 11, 2008 and ending on May 19, 2008 and the period beginning on
August 31, 2008 and ending on September 9, 2008, representatives of
states and governments that are members of the Organization shall have
in Canada, to the extent that may be required for the exercise of their
functions in Canada in relation to the Preparatory Meeting and the
Meeting, the privileges and immunities set out in Sections 11, 12 and 14
to 16 of Article IV of the Convention.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-742838 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
