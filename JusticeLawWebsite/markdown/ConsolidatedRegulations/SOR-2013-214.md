## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799861 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs administration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the competent
    authority that is responsible under the law of an EFTA state for the
    administration of its customs laws and regulations. ([administration
    douanière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[goods]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means goods for which preferential
    tariff treatment under the CEFTA is claimed.
    ([marchandises]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification letter]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a letter that
    requests information with respect to the origin of goods that are
    the subject of a verification of origin. ([lettre de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification questionnaire]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    questionnaire that requests information with respect to the origin
    of goods that are the subject of a verification of origin.
    ([questionnaire de vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[verification visit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the entry into any
    premises or place for the purpose of conducting a verification of
    origin of goods under subsection 97.201(1) of the Act. ([visite de
    vérification]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Manner of Conducting a Verification of Origin]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799869 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In addition to a
verification visit, a verification of origin of goods exported from
Canada to an EFTA state may be conducted by a review of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a verification questionnaire completed by the
  exporter or the producer of the goods;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written response, received from the exporter or the
  producer, to a verification letter; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} any other information received from the exporter or
  the producer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-214, s. 7
:::

## [Prescribed Premises or Place]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799876 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 97.201(2)(a) of the Act, the premises or the place that may be
entered for the purpose of conducting conduct of a verification visit is
that of the exporter or the producer of the goods.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-214, s. 7
:::

## [Condition for Conducting a Verification Visit]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799880 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** An officer may conduct a
verification visit only if they send a written notice of their intention
to do so to the exporter or the producer whose premises or place is the
subject of the verification visit.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2013-214, s. 7
:::

## [Observer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799884 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** If requested by the customs
administration of the EFTA state of import, the exporter whose premises
or place is subject of the verification visit must permit the
participation of an observer from that customs administration.

## [Manner of Sending Documents]{.HTitleText1} {#h-799886 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purpose of
paragraph 97.201(3)(a) of the Act, the opinion and supporting documents
are to be provided by any means of transmission that produces
confirmation of receipt.
