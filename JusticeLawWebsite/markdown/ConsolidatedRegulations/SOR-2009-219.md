[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Interpretation

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Companies' Creditors
    Arrangement Act](/eng/acts/C-36). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[initial order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[initial order]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an order that is made on
    the initial application in respect of a debtor company. ([ordonnance
    initiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Stock exchange

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A stock exchange that is
regulated by an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province is
prescribed for the purposes of the definition [[income
trust]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 2(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Pension plan

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A pension plan that is
regulated by an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province is
prescribed for the purposes of subsection 6(6) of the Act.

in the table to this section are prescribed for the purposes of

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Regulatory bodies

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A stock exchange that is
regulated by an Act of Parliament or of the legislature of a province,
the Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada and the Mutual
Fund Dealers Association of Canada are prescribed for the purposes of
subsection 11.1(1) of the Act.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be published in newspapers

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The following information
is prescribed for the purposes of subparagraph 23(1)(a)(i) of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of each applicant and the name under which
  the applicant carries on business, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and judicial district of the court that made
  the initial order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the court file number for the proceedings;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the initial order was made; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the monitor,
  including its Web site address.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Making initial order and list of
creditors publicly available

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of clauses
23(1)(a)(ii)(A) and (C) of the Act, the prescribed manner for making the
initial order and list publicly available is by posting them on a Web
page created by the monitor for the proceedings.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice that initial order is publicly
available

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of clause
23(1)(a)(ii)(B) of the Act, the prescribed manner of sending the notice
is by personal service, mail, courier, facsimile or other electronic
transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Paragraph 23(1)(f) of the Act

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The following paragraphs
set out the documents that are specified for the purposes of paragraph
23(1)(f) of the Act and the prescribed manner and time for filing them:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} Form 1 of the schedule, entitled "Information
  Pertaining to Initial Order", to be filed by facsimile or other
  electronic transmission within one business day after the day on which
  the initial order is made;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the initial application, the initial order and any
  amendments to that order, to be filed by facsimile or other electronic
  transmission within two business days after the day on which the
  monitor receives them;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} Form 2 of the schedule, entitled "Debtor Company
  Information Summary (Commencement of Proceedings)", to be filed by
  facsimile or other electronic transmission within five business days
  after the day on which the monitor receives the initial order;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} Form 3 of the schedule, entitled "Debtor Company
  Information Summary (Following the Order Discharging the Monitor)", to
  be filed by facsimile or other electronic transmission within five
  business days after the day on which the court makes an order
  discharging the monitor; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the documents posted on the Web page created by the
  monitor under section 7, to be filed by submitting them on a compact
  disc, digital versatile disc or other data storage medium within 30
  days after the day on which the court makes an order discharging the
  monitor.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Documents to be publicly available

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following documents are prescribed for the purposes of paragraph
  23(1)(j) of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} reports of the monitor, including exhibits, and
    cash-flow statements that are filed with the court, other than those
    -- or any part of them -- that are subject to a court order
    prohibiting their release to the public;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} proposed compromises or arrangements that are filed
    with the court, including amendments to them;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} court orders; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} written communications and notifications that the
    monitor sends to all creditors.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Posting on Web page

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prescribed manner for making the documents
  publicly available is by posting them on the Web page created by the
  monitor under section 7.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Time limit for posting

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The prescribed time for posting the documents is, in
  respect of the documents referred to in paragraphs (1)(a) and (b),
  within two business days after the day on which they are filed, in
  respect of the documents referred to in paragraph (1)(c), within two
  business days after the day on which the monitor receives them and, in
  respect of the documents referred to in paragraph (1)(d), within two
  business days after the day on which they are sent.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Public record

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsection 26(1) of the Act, the prescribed
  information is the information set out on Form 1 of the schedule, and
  the prescribed period is 10 years after the day on which the
  information is received by the Superintendent of Bankruptcy.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other records

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subsection 26(2) of the Act, the
  prescribed period is 10 years after the day on which the record is
  received by the Superintendent of Bankruptcy.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of delegation

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 31(2) of the Act, the prescribed manner of giving notice of
the delegation is by personal service, mail, courier, facsimile or other
electronic transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice to disclaim or resiliate

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 32(1) of the Act, Form 4 of the schedule, entitled "Notice by
Debtor Company to Disclaim or Resiliate an Agreement", is prescribed and
the prescribed manner of giving notice is by personal service,
registered mail or courier or, if the recipient agrees, by facsimile or
other electronic transmission.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information to be published in newspapers

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
paragraph 53(b) of the Act, the prescribed information is the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of the foreign
  representative;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the debtor company and the name under
  which it carries on business in Canada;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the following information respecting the order,
  namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the court that made it,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the legislative provision under which it was made,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date on which it was made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the country in which the foreign proceeding is filed;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} whether the proceeding is a foreign main or foreign
  non-main proceeding; and

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name and contact information of legal counsel for
  the foreign representative.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[Footnote

Act, to amend the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act and the Companies'
Creditors Arrangement Act and to make consequential amendments to other
Acts, chapter 47 of the Statutes of Canada, 2005, comes into force, but
if they are registered after that day, they come into force on the day
on which they are registered.
