## Order Designating the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous Services Canada as Deputy Head in respect of that Entity {#order-designating-the-deputy-minister-of-the-department-of-indigenous-services-canada-as-deputy-head-in-respect-of-that-entity .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-78
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2017-12-13

Order Designating the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous
Services Canada as Deputy Head in respect of that Entity

P.C. 2017-1468 2017-11-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_608651-E_hq_152){#fn_608651-E_hq_152-ID0EECAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_610261-E_hq_4314){#fn_610261-E_hq_4314-ID0EBCAA},
designates the Deputy Minister of the Department of Indigenous Services
Canada as deputy head in respect of that entity, effective November 30,
2017.
