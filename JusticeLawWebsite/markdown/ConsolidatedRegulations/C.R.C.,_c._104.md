## Quebec Airport Zoning Regulations {#quebec-airport-zoning-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 104
:::

[AERONAUTICS ACT](/eng/acts/A-2)

Regulations Respecting Zoning at Quebec Airport
::::

::::::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-481236 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Quebec Airport Zoning
Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._104).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-481238 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means Quebec Airport, L'Ancienne
    Lorette, in the Province of Quebec; ([aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[airport reference point]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the point
    described in Part I of the schedule; ([point de repère de
    l'aéroport]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[approach surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary inclined
    plane the lower end of which is a horizontal line at right angles to
    the centre line of a strip and passing through a point at the strip
    end on the centre line of the strip; ([surface
    d'approche]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[horizontal surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    horizontal plane centering on and located 150 feet above the
    assigned elevation of the airport reference point; ([surface
    horizontale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Revoked, SOR/93-401,
    s. 2\]]{.Repealed}

[[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[strip]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a rectangular portion of the
    landing area of the airport, especially prepared for the take-off
    and landing of aircraft in a particular direction and being

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 1,200 feet, including the runway, in the case of
      the runway designated as 12-30, and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 1,500 feet, including the runway, in the case of
      the runway designated as 06-24; ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[transitional surface]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an imaginary
    inclined plane extending upward and outward from the outer lateral
    limits of a strip and its approach surface to an intersection with
    the horizontal surface or other transitional surfaces. ([surface de
    transition]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/93-401, s. 2
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of these
Regulations, the airport reference point is deemed to be 200 feet above
sea level.

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-481252 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply to
all lands, including public road allowances, adjacent to or in the
vicinity of the airport, as more particularly described in Part II of
the schedule.

### [PART I]{.HLabel1}[Airport Reference Point]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iairport-reference-point .SchedHeadL1}

Premising that the bearings hereinafter mentioned are astronomical and
are referred to the centre line of strip 06-24, Quebec Airport,
L'Ancienne Lorette, Province of Quebec, as North forty-four degrees
forty-nine minutes East (N. 44°49′ E.):

BEGINNING at the intersection of the centre line of strip 06-24 with the
centre line of strip 12-30; THENCE North forty-four degrees forty-nine
minutes East (N. 44°49′ E.) along the centre line of strip 06-24 a
distance of two thousand feet (2,000′) to a point; THENCE, North
forty-five degrees eleven minutes West (N. 45°11′ W.) a distance of
seven hundred and fifty feet (750′) to a point henceforth designated as
the airport reference point, said airport reference point being also at
one thousand eight hundred and ninety-nine feet and one tenth (1,899.1′)
measured along a line having a bearing of South twenty-one degrees seven
minutes West (S. 21°07′ W.) from a concrete monument at the intersection
of the concession line between Concessions V and VI and the lot line
between lots 376 and 383, the said airport reference point having an
assigned true elevation of two hundred feet (200′) above sea level.
