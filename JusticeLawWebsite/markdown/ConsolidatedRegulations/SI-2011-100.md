## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-615233 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in this Order.

[[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registered charity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
    in subsection 248(1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
    ([organisme de bienfaisance enregistré]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[revocation tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[revocation tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned by
    subsection 188(1.1) of the [Income Tax Act](/eng/acts/I-3.3).
    ([impôt de révocation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-615237 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Remission of the revocation
tax paid or payable is granted to a person on the condition that the
person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} was a registered charity before the coming into force
  of this Order;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} was a participant in the Financial Assistance Program
  for Domestic Help Services, established by the Quebec Department of
  Health and Social Services, before the coming into force of this
  Order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} makes a written application for revocation of its
  registration as a registered charity to the Minister of National
  Revenue within nine months after the day on which this Order comes
  into force; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} makes a written application for remission under this
  Order to the Minister of National Revenue within nine months after the
  day on which this Order comes into force.
