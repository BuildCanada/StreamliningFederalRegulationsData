## [Definition]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1251926 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Tobacco and Vaping Products
Act](/eng/acts/T-11.5).

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Advertising and Point of Sale Promotion]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1251929 .Part}

### [Advertising]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1251930 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertising --- young persons

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.701 of the Act, a vaping product or a vaping
  product-related brand element must not be promoted by means of
  advertising done in a manner that allows the advertising to be seen or
  heard by young persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exceptions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to the following types
  of advertising:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} subject to paragraph (b), visual advertising that
    is located at the point of sale, if the conditions set out in
    paragraphs 6(1)(a) to (d) are met;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} advertising on a sign that is located at a point of
    sale that is a retail establishment where vaping products are sold
    if

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the conditions set out in paragraphs 7(1)(a)
      to (e) are met, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the sign indicates only the availability at the
      establishment and price of vaping products and provincial
      legislation that governs the retail establishment applies to signs
      promoting vaping products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} advertising in a publication that is addressed and
    sent to an adult who is identified by name; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} advertising in a publication that is provided on
    request to an adult at a point of sale that is a retail
    establishment where vaping products are sold.

### [Point of Sale Promotion]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1251942 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display --- vaping product

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.8 of the Act, a vaping product must not be
  displayed, at the point of sale, in a manner that allows it to be seen
  by young persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial legislation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a point of sale that
  is a retail establishment where vaping products are sold if provincial
  legislation that governs the retail establishment prohibits vaping
  products from being displayed in a manner that allows them to be seen
  by young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display --- package

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.8 of the Act, the package of a vaping product
  must not be displayed, at the point of sale, in a manner that allows
  the package to be seen by young persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial legislation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a point of sale that
  is a retail establishment where vaping products are sold if provincial
  legislation that governs the retail establishment prohibits the
  packages of vaping products from being displayed in a manner that
  allows the packages to be seen by young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Display --- brand element

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.8 of the Act, a thing that displays a vaping
  product-related brand element must not be displayed, at the point of
  sale, in a manner that allows the brand element to be seen by young
  persons.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial legislation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to a point of sale that
  is a retail establishment where vaping products are sold if provincial
  legislation that governs the retail establishment prohibits, directly
  or indirectly, vaping product-related brand elements from being
  displayed in a manner that allows them to be seen by young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Visual advertising --- general conditions

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.8 of the Act, a vaping product or a vaping
  product-related brand element must not, subject to subsection (2) and
  section 7, be promoted by means of a visual advertisement at the point
  of sale unless the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} only one such advertisement is located at the point
    of sale;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement indicates only the availability
    at the point of sale and price of vaping products;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement does not include any visual,
    sound or other effects that are likely to draw attention to it; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the advertisement uses only black characters on a
    white background.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- visibility

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of visual
  advertising done in a manner that does not allow the advertising to be
  seen by young persons.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Signs --- specific conditions

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.8 of the Act, a vaping product or a vaping
  product-related brand element must not, subject to subsections (2) and
  (3), be promoted by means of advertising on a sign that is located at
  a point of sale that is a retail establishment where vaping products
  are sold, unless the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the sign indicates only the availability at the
    establishment and price of vaping products;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} only one such sign is located at the establishment;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the sign does not include any visual, sound or
    other effects that are likely to draw attention to it;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the sign uses only black characters on a white
    background; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the sign is rectangular and does not exceed 3 600
    cm^2^ in area.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- provincial legislation

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply if provincial
  legislation that governs the retail establishment applies to signs
  promoting vaping products.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception --- visibility

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply in respect of
  advertising on a sign and that is done in a manner that does not allow
  the advertising to be seen by young persons.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Required Information in Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1251979 .Part}

### [Health Warning]{.HTitleText2} {#h-1251980 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Advertising --- list of health warnings

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of section 30.7 of the Act, a vaping product or a vaping
  product-related brand element must not be promoted by means of
  advertising unless it conveys one of the health warnings set out in
  the document entitled List of Health Warnings for Vaping Product
  Advertising, as amended from time to time and published by the
  Government of Canada on its website.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Amended list

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the List of Health Warnings for Vaping Product
  Advertising is amended, advertising may convey a health warning that
  was set out in the previous version of the List for a period of 60
  days after the day on which the new version of that List is published
  by the Government of Canada.

#### [Visual Advertising]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1252003 .Subheading}

##### [General Requirements]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1252004 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 14 to 23

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 24,
sections 14 to 23 apply to all forms of visual advertising.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of [[display
area]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
section 15, subsection 17(1) and sections 18 and 19, [[display
area]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of visual advertising, means the
portion of the surface area of an advertisement on which the information
required under this Part may be displayed and that,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of visual advertising by video, occupies
  100% of the surface area of the advertisement; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in every other case, occupies --- from the edge that
  is in the horizontal plane, that forms the upper limit of the
  advertisement and that extends from the left edge to the right edge of
  the advertisement --- at least 20% of the surface area of the
  advertisement that is visible at first sight to consumers.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- placement

**[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** Required information that
is conveyed in visual advertising must be displayed on the display area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- visibility and
legibility

**[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** Required information that
is conveyed in visual advertising

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be clear and legible; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not be concealed or obscured.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirement --- rectangular border

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Required information that is
  conveyed in visual advertising must be enclosed within a rectangular
  border that must be displayed on the display area in such a manner
  that it demarcates the information from any other information
  displayed on the advertisement.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Appearance --- rectangular border

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The border must be the same colour as the type of the
  health warning and form a continuous line that has a uniform width of
  3% of the length of the shortest side of the rectangle.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Health warning --- legibility

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  health warning that is conveyed in visual advertising must be
  displayed in a standard sans serif type that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is not compressed, expanded or decorative;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} as illustrated in the schedule, has a large
    x-height relative to the ascender or descender of the type; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} is black, on a background that is white, or white,
    on a background that is black.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Characters in text --- health warning

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each character in the text must have the same font
  and type size.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text of health warning

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The health warning must be displayed in such a manner
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the first word is in upper case letters and bold
    type;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the remaining text is capitalized in the same
    manner as in the List of Health Warnings for Vaping Product
    Advertising and it is not in bold type; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if the health warning is displayed on more than one
    line of text, the letters in each word appear on the same line of
    text.

##### [Advertising by Means of Telecommunication]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1252056 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of sections 14 to 23

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Sections 14 to 23 do not apply to
  required information that is conveyed in visual advertising
  transmitted by a means of telecommunication that does not allow the
  display of required information in accordance with the requirements
  set out in those sections.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 25 to 30

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Sections 25 to 30 apply to required information that
  is conveyed in visual advertising transmitted by a means of
  telecommunication that does not allow the display of required
  information in accordance with the requirements set out in sections 14
  to 23.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- display

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** Required information must
be displayed at the beginning of the advertising.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- visibility and
legibility

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** Required information

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be clear and legible; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not be concealed or obscured.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Official languages --- display

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** If a health warning is
conveyed in both official languages, each language version must be
displayed before or after the other version.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Health warning --- legibility

  health warning must be displayed in a standard sans serif type that is
  not compressed, expanded or decorative.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Characters in text

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each character in the text of the health warning must
  have the same font and type size.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Text of health warning

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The health warning must be displayed in such a manner
  that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the text is presented in a consolidated manner,
    without any intervening words or images;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the first word is in upper case letters;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the remaining text is capitalized in the same
    manner as in the List of Health Warnings for Vaping Product
    Advertising; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if the health warning is displayed on more than one
    line of text, the letters in each word appear on the same line of
    text.

##### [Video Advertising]{.HTitleText4} {#h-1252087 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** Required information that
is conveyed in visual advertising by video must be displayed at the end
of the advertising.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Health warning --- minimum duration

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A health warning that is
conveyed in visual advertising by video must be displayed for at least

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} four seconds, if the health warning is displayed in
  only one official language; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} eight seconds, if the health warning is displayed in
  both official languages.

#### [Audio Advertising]{.HTitleText3} {#h-1252094 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application of sections 34 to 36

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** Sections 34 to 36 apply
to all kinds of audio advertising.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- placement

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Required information that
is conveyed in audio advertising must be conveyed at the end of the
audio advertising and must not be combined with any other audio
information.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Requirements

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** The following
requirements apply to the health warning that is conveyed in audio
advertising:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the health warning must be conveyed in its entirety
  at the same speed, volume and tone as the main message, without any
  word being emphasized more than any other;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the health warning must be conveyed at the same
  speed, volume and tone in both official languages, if the advertising
  uses both official languages or another language; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the health warning must be conveyed without any music
  or background sound.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Attribution

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** The attribution "This is
a Health Canada warning:" must immediately precede the English version
of the health warning that is conveyed in audio advertising and the
attribution "[Ce message est une mise en garde de Santé
Canada :]{.otherLang lang="fr"}" must immediately precede the French
version of the health warning.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Required information --- audio-visual
