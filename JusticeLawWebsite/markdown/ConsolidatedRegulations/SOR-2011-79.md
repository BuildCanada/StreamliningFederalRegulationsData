Whereas the requirements of section 4 of the [User Fees
Act](/eng/acts/U-3.7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_ae){#fn_ae-ID0EBABA} have been complied with in
respect of the fees fixed in the annexed Regulations;

Therefore His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Treasury Board and the Minister of Health,
pursuant to subsection 19(1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EKEAA} and section 19.1[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_be){#fn_be-ID0EIEAA} of the [Financial
Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_ce){#fn_ce-ID0EFEAA} and, considering that it
is otherwise in the public interest, subsection 23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_de){#fn_de-ID0EDEAA} of that Act, hereby makes
the annexed [Fees in Respect of Drugs and Medical Devices
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2011-79).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose --- fees

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  purpose of these Regulations is to prescribe the fees for the
  examination of an application for, or the renewal of, a dealer's
  licence under Part G of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) or under the [Narcotic
  Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Purpose --- remission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The purpose of these Regulations is also to remit, in
  whole or in part, certain of those fees.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee --- Dealer's licence

- **[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  fee payable by an applicant for the examination of an application for
  either a dealer's licence or the renewal of a dealer's licence is
  \$4,510 for each of the premises at which activities are to be
  conducted under the licence.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection 33(2), if the fee is greater
  than an amount equal to 1% of the applicant's actual gross revenue
  from activities conducted under a dealer's licence during the previous
  calendar year, remission is granted of the difference between those
  amounts if the applicant provides with their application a statement
  signed by the individual responsible for the applicant's financial
  affairs that sets out the actual gross revenue.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Timing of payment

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), the fee is payable at the
  time of submitting the application for a dealer's licence or for the
  renewal of a dealer's licence under section 10 or 11 of the [Narcotic
  Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041) or section
  G.02.006 or G.02.010 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}First year of activities

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant has not completed their first
  calendar year of conducting activities under the licence, the payment
  of the fee is deferred until the end of that year.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Audited sales records

- **[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Minister determines, on the basis of any information available to the
  Minister, that the statement provided under subsection 31(2) is not
  adequate to determine the applicant's actual gross revenue referred to
  in that subsection, the Minister may require, for the purpose of
  determining the fee payable or the amount of the remission, the
  applicant to provide sales records that have been audited by a
  qualified independent auditor.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Omission

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, within 60 days after the day of the Minister's
  request for the audited sales records, the applicant has not provided
  the Minister with them, the difference between the fee payable under
  subsection 31(1) and the amount already paid is immediately payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Difference payable

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} If the audited sales records establish that the
  amount already paid is less than the amount payable under section 31,
  the difference between those amounts is immediately payable.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Difference remitted

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the audited sales records establish that the
  amount already paid is greater than the amount payable under section
  31, remission is granted of an amount equal to the difference between
  those amounts, which the Minister must repay to the applicant.
