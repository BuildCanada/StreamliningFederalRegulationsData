## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905111 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [[Nardeux Canada Ltée]{.otherLang lang="fr"}
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-272).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905113 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Regional
    Industrial Expansion; ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[[Nardeux Canada Ltée]{.otherLang lang="fr"}]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[[Nardeux Canada Ltée]{.otherLang lang="fr"}]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
    means a company incorporated with that name under federal laws and
    having its principal place of business in Montreal, Quebec;
    ([Nardeux Canada Ltée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[private lender]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any lender, or the
    assignee of any lender, other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Government of Canada,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the government of any province of Canada,

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an agency of any government referred to in
      paragraph (a) or (b) or any company that is effectively controlled
      by any such government or any agency thereof, or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a municipal corporation. ([prêteur
      privé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Insurance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905122 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Subject to sections 4 to 6,
the Minister may provide insurance for up to 85 per cent of each and
every loss, not exceeding in aggregate \$475,000, that may result in
respect of loans made by a private lender to [Nardeux Canada
Ltée]{.otherLang lang="fr"} to permit the implementation of high
technology in the areas of radio-protection and
radio-telecommunications.

## [Terms and Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-905124 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The amount of insurance
provided by the Minister to a private lender pursuant to section 3 may
be reduced at the request of the private lender.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** A private lender shall pay
to the Minister by semi-annual payments in advance a fee not exceeding
two per cent per annum of the amount of insurance in force.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Where a private lender has
demanded repayment from [Nardeux Canada Ltée]{.otherLang lang="fr"} for
a loan insured under these Regulations, the amount payable by the
Minister to the private lender in the event that the private lender has
incurred a loss shall not exceed the lesser of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} 100 per cent of the amount of insurance in force on
  the date of the demand; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} 85 per cent of the loss incurred by the private
  lender up to a maximum loss of \$475,000.
