## Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order {#minister-of-communications-authority-to-prescribe-fees-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-32
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 1993-03-24

Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

P.C. 1993-428  1993-03-09
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(b)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-32_e_hq_6512){#fn_SI-93-32_e_hq_6512-ID0EDDAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11), is pleased hereby
to authorize the Minister of Communications to prescribe, by order, the
fees or charges to be paid by a user for whom the Department of
Communications provides any of the following services :

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-32_e_hq_6512-ID0EDDAA){#fn_SI-93-32_e_hq_6512}S.C.
  1991, c. 24, s. 6
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the technical assessment, reassessment, testing or
  certification of telecommunications apparatus; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the labelling or marking of telecommunications
  apparatus as evidence of ongoing compliance with the technical
  requirements, specifications, standards or procedures of the
  Department of Communications.
:::
