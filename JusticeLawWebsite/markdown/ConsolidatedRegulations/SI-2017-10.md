## Transfer of Duties Order {#transfer-of-duties-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2017-10
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2017-02-22

Transfer of Duties Order

P.C. 2017-76 2017-02-01
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612481-E_hq_7374){#fn_612481-E_hq_7374-ID0EHDAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612481-E_hq_7375){#fn_612481-E_hq_7375-ID0EEDAA},
transfers from the Minister of Industry to the Minister of State styled
the Minister of Science the powers, duties and functions of the Minister
of Industry under Part I of the [Budget Implementation Act,
1997](/eng/acts/B-9.81)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_81000-3-1338-E_hq_19113){#fn_81000-3-1338-E_hq_19113-ID0EBDAA}.
