## European Communities Privileges and Immunities Order {#european-communities-privileges-and-immunities-order .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 1308
:::

[FOREIGN MISSIONS AND INTERNATIONAL ORGANIZATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/F-29.4)

Order Respecting the Privileges and Immunities of the European
Communities in Canada
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503216 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [European Communities Privileges and Immunities
Order](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1308).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503218 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Convention]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Convention on the
    Privileges and Immunities of the United Nations;
    ([Convention]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Organization]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the European Communities;
    ([Organisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[senior official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[senior official]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person designated as
    a senior official of the Organization by the President of the
    Commission of the Organization and accepted as such by the Secretary
    of State for External Affairs. ([fonctionnaire
    supérieur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Privileges and Immunities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-503223 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** The Organization shall have
in Canada the legal capacities of a body corporate and shall, to such
extent as may be necessary for the performance of its functions, have
the privileges and immunities set forth in Articles II and III of the
Convention.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Unless in any particular
case a privilege or immunity is waived.

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} senior officials shall have in Canada, to such extent
  as may be necessary for the performance of their functions, the
  privileges and immunities referred to and specified in section 19 of
  the Convention.

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} officials of the Organization, other than senior
  officials, shall have in Canada, to such extent as may be necessary
  for the performance of their functions, the privileges and immunities
  specified in section 18 of the Convention; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} experts performing missions for the Organization
  shall have in Canada, to such extent as may be necessary for the
  performance of their functions, the privileges and immunities set
  forth in Article VI of the Convention for experts on missions for the
  United Nations.
