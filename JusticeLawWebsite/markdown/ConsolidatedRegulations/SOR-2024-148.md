## [Application for Approval]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1485051 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fees

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** An owner who submits an
application for an approval under subsection 5(1) or paragraph 10(1)(a)
of the [Canadian Navigable Waters Act](/eng/acts/N-22) to construct,
place, alter or rebuild a work must pay to the Minister the fee set out
in the table to this section that corresponds to the period set out in
column 1 during which the application is submitted and to the category
of the work specified, as the case may be, in column 2, in column 3 or
in column 4.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}More than one work

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** If an application for an
approval concerns more than one work, the fee payable is the sum of the
fees required for each of the works.

## [Application for Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1485062 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fee and payment

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** A fee of \$66,000 must be
paid to the Minister at the time an application for an exemption is
submitted under subsection 24(1) of the [Canadian Navigable Waters
Act](/eng/acts/N-22).

<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(c)</span> Log boom</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Boom designed to collect or contain floating
logs.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(d)</span> Protective barrier</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Barrier designed to protect an area from
tides, vessels or other hazards.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(e)</span> Safety boom</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Safety and security boom that restricts the
circulation of vessels or swimmers or acts as a safety barrier for dams
or against obstructions.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">45</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Breakwater</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure designed to protect an anchorage,
harbour or other work from the effects of weather conditions and
waves.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Floating breakwater</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Floating wave attenuator used to protect an
area from waves.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">46</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Bridge</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Elevated structure carrying a road, path,
railroad or any other similar thing across a navigable water.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">47</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Building</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure having a roof or walls that is
constructed on a navigable water and is not otherwise identified in this
Schedule.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Floating building</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Building with a flotation system that is
moored or secured and not used for navigating.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(b)</span> Heliport or landing
pad</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure constructed in a navigable water
used for the landing and takeoff of helicopters.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(c)</span> Observation tower</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure used to observe the
surroundings.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">48</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Canal</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Artificial watercourse typically constructed
in a size suitable for navigation.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">49</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Causeway</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Raised path, railway or road across a
navigable water, typically made of compacted earth, sand and rocks.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">50</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Dam</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure designed to hold back water and
raise its level, forming a reservoir.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">51</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Dolphin</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure consisting of closely driven piles
used as a fender for a dock or as a mooring or guide for vessels or
other works.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">52</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Drilling platform</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure with facilities for well drilling
and deep-sea mining for minerals or other resources beneath the
seabed.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Oil production
platform</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure with facilities for well drilling
used to explore, extract, store and process petroleum and natural gas
that are contained in rock formations beneath the seabed.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">53</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Dyke</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure typically constructed parallel to a
shore to contain water along low-lying land and regulate its effects, or
to guide its flow.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">54</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Ferry cable</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Cable connected to two shores of, and used to
guide a ferry across, a navigable water.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">55</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Harbour</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Group of structures that shelter anchored or
moored vessels from rough waters and bad weather and allow for the
loading or unloading of goods or the boarding or disembarking of
passengers.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">56</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Infill</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Dumping of fill in a concentrated area for
construction or development.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">57</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Lock</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Confined section of a canal or other
navigable water in which the water level can be changed using gates and
sluices for the purpose of raising or lowering vessels between two
gates.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">58</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Marina</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Group of structures that provide mooring or
berthing capacity for recreational vessels and may have supply, repair
and other facilities required for the use of the vessels.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">59</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Mooring facility</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Group of more than eight mooring systems
situated within a concentrated area and belonging to the same owner,
offering mooring services to vessels.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">60</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Pilings</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Posts driven vertically into the bed of a
navigable water to support the foundations of a structure.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">61</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Pipeline</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Pipe for conveying any type of matter.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">62</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Power project</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Any type of facility that generates
power.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Hydroelectric
station</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure used to produce electrical energy
from the flow of water, typically through the use of a dam.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(b)</span> Tidal turbine</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure used to produce electrical energy
from the tides.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(c)</span> Wind turbine</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure used to produce electrical energy
from the wind.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">63</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Scuttled vessel</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Vessel that has been deliberately sunk by
allowing water to flow into the hull.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">64</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Spud barge</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Flat-bottomed vessel moored by steel shafts
or through-deck piling that is used for the construction, placement,
alteration, rebuilding, removal, repair or decommissioning of another
work.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">65</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Terminal</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure with integrated machinery used to
load and unload container vessels, bulk carriers, tanker ships or
roll-on roll-off vessels, or to allow passengers to board or disembark
from vessels.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Deep water terminal</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Terminal used to load and unload, or to allow
passengers to board or disembark from, vessels that require water depth
of 9.144 m (30 ft) or more.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(b)</span> Ferry terminal</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure that typically has integrated
mechanisms used primarily to board or disembark passengers from
ferries.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">66</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Tunnel</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Underground passageway that allows crossing
beneath a navigable water.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Underwater corridor</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Underwater passageway containing supporting
structures for cables and joints or other elements of wiring systems and
whose dimensions allow people to pass freely throughout its entire
length.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">67</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Water control structure</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure designed to manage water levels and
flows in channels and pipes.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Water diversion</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">System of structures that diverts water from
an area upstream to an area downstream of a navigable water or toward
another body of water.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">68</td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom">Weir</td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Structure constructed across a navigable
water used to slightly raise the water level on the upstream side and
allow a steady flow of water over parts of that structure.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight noBorderBottom"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(a)</span> Fish weir</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td class="noBorderBottom">Weir, typically made of rock or concrete,
constructed entirely or partially across a navigable water and used to
direct the passage of or trap fish.</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="noBorderRight"></td>
<td class="noBorderRight"><ul>
<li><p><span class="tablelabel">(b)</span> Submerged weir</p></li>
</ul></td>
<td>Weir constructed entirely below the surface of a navigable
water.</td>
</tr>
