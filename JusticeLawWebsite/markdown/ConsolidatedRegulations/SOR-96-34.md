## Exported Motor Vehicles Drawback Regulations {#exported-motor-vehicles-drawback-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-34
:::

[CUSTOMS TARIFF](/eng/acts/C-54.011)

Registration 1996-01-01

Regulations Respecting the Drawback of Duties Paid on Imported New Motor
Vehicles, or on Imported Goods where they, or the Same Quantity of
Domestic or Imported Goods of the Same Class, Are Used or Consumed in
the Processing in Canada of New Motor Vehicles, where the New Vehicles
Are Used Temporarily in Canada and Subsequently Exported

P.C. 1995-2242  1995-12-28

the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection
100(5)[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985732 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Exported Motor Vehicles Drawback
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-34).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985734 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Customs
    Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[motor vehicle]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an automobile,
    motorcycle, motor bicycle, truck, motor bus or motor home, but does
    not include any truck that has a gross vehicle weight in excess of
    4.5359 tonnes; ([véhicule automobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[purchaser]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who purchases a
    motor vehicle; ([acheteur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[resident]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who is ordinarily
    resident in Canada. ([résident]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985740 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), these Regulations apply in respect of the grant,
  under subsection 113(1) of the Act, of a drawback of the duties paid
  in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an imported motor vehicle that is subsequently
    exported;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} imported goods used in the processing in Canada of
    a motor vehicle that is subsequently exported;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} imported goods, other than goods described in the
    schedule, directly consumed or expended in the processing in Canada
    of a motor vehicle that is subsequently exported;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} imported goods, where the same quantity of domestic
    or imported goods of the same class is used in the processing in
    Canada of a motor vehicle that is subsequently exported; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} imported goods, other than goods described in the
    schedule, where the same quantity of domestic or imported goods of
    the same class is directly consumed or expended in the processing in
    Canada of a motor vehicle that is subsequently exported.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The drawback referred to in subsection (1) shall be
  granted only where the motor vehicle is sold to a purchaser in Canada
  before it is exported and it is, at the time of the sale, new.

- SOR/98-62, s. 11

## [Persons Who May Claim]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985751 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** A drawback may be claimed
by

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the importer of the motor vehicle or the person who
  sold the motor vehicle to the purchaser, in the case of an imported
  motor vehicle; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the processor of the motor vehicle, in the case of a
  motor vehicle processed in Canada.

## [Circumstances]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985755 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
drawback may be made where

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is exported by the purchaser

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} within 30 days after the date of its delivery to
    the purchaser, if the purchaser is a resident, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} within 12 months after the date of its delivery to
    the purchaser, if the purchaser is not a resident;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} before the motor vehicle is exported, it remains
  owned by the purchaser, it is used by the purchaser, and it is not
  used

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} by any person other than the purchaser,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} for carrying passengers or goods for compensation,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} for the transport of goods for sale, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} for soliciting sales or subscriptions on behalf of
    a Canadian business;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle was exported before the application
  is filed;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of imported goods referred to in
  paragraph 3(1)(d) or (e),

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the goods were used, consumed or expended in the
    same plant in Canada in which the domestic or imported goods of the
    same class were used, consumed or expended in the processing of the
    motor vehicle, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the motor vehicle is exported within two years
    after the date that those imported goods were imported; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the applicant provides a waiver from all other
  persons entitled to claim a drawback, refund or remission of the
  duties, waiving their right to do so.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** A drawback shall not be
granted where the domestic or imported goods of the same class that are
referred to in subparagraph 5(d)(i) are used, consumed or expended
before the imported goods that are the subject of the application for a
drawback are used, consumed or expended in the processing of the motor
vehicle.

## [Goods of the Same Class]{.HTitleText1} {#h-985771 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Domestic and imported goods
shall be considered to be of the same class if the goods are so similar
that they may be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} used interchangeably in the processing in Canada of a
  motor vehicle; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} directly consumed or expended interchangeably in the
  processing in Canada of a motor vehicle.
