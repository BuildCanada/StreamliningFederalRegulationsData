## Exemption Order in Respect of Labelling Requirements for Certain Natural Health Products {#exemption-order-in-respect-of-labelling-requirements-for-certain-natural-health-products .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2025-94
:::

[FOOD AND DRUGS ACT](/eng/acts/F-27)

Registration 2025-03-07

Exemption Order in Respect of Labelling Requirements for Certain Natural
Health Products
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Minister of Health believes on reasonable grounds that the
annexed Order is necessary for a health or safety purpose or is
otherwise in the public interest;

And whereas the Minister of Health believes on reasonable grounds that,
having regard to its benefits and condition, the annexed Order is
unlikely to result in unacceptable health, safety or, if applicable,
environmental risks or an unacceptable degree of uncertainty respecting
health, safety or, if applicable, environmental risks;

Therefore, the Minister of Health makes the annexed [Exemption Order in
Respect of Labelling Requirements for Certain Natural Health
Products](/eng/regulations/SOR-2025-94) under section 30.05[[Footnote

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517811 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in this Order.

  [[commencement day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[commencement day]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the day on which
      sections 17 to 22 of the Regulations Amending the Natural Health
      Products Regulations, made by Order in Council P.C. 2022-710 of
      June 20, 2022 and registered as SOR/2022-146, come into
      force. ([date de référence]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[former Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Regulations
      as they read immediately before the commencement day. ([ancien
      règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[new Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[new Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Regulations as
      they read on the commencement day. ([nouveau
      règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[product licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[product licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a product licence
      issued under section 7 of the Regulations. ([licence de mise en
      marché]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Natural Health
      Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196). ([Règlement]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Words and expressions

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless the context otherwise requires, words and
  expressions used in this Order have the same meaning as in the
  Regulations.

## [Conditional Exemption]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517822 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exemption --- certain natural health
products

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to the condition set out in subsection (2), every natural health
  product for which a product licence has been issued on or after the
  day on which this Order comes into force and that is not labelled in
  accordance with the new Regulations is exempt from the application of
  sections 86 to 94 of the new Regulations as they apply to labelling.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Condition --- former labelling
  requirements

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The natural health product must, when it is sold, be
  labelled in accordance with the former Regulations.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application of condition

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The condition does not apply if the sale of the
  natural health product is to a manufacturer or distributor.

## [Repeal]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517830 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** This Order is repealed on
June 21, 2028.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-1517832 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on June 21, 2025, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into
force on the day on which it is registered.
