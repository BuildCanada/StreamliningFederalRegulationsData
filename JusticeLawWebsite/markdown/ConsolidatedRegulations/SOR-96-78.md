## Proclamation Designating Certain Countries as Designated States for Purposes of the Act {#proclamation-designating-certain-countries-as-designated-states-for-purposes-of-the-act .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-78
:::

[VISITING FORCES ACT](/eng/acts/V-2)

Registration 1996-01-05

Proclamation Designating Certain Countries as Designated States for
Purposes of the Act
::::

::::::::: section
:::::::: {.section .order}
ROMÉO LEBLANC

\[L.S.\]

Canada

Elizabeth the Second, by the Grace of God of the United Kingdom, Canada
and Her other Realms and Territories QUEEN, Head of the Commonwealth,
Defender of the Faith.

To All to Whom these Presents shall come or whom the same may in anyway
concern,

*Greeting*:

::: FlushLeft
GEORGE THOMSON
:::

::: FlushLeft
*Deputy Attorney General*
:::

A Proclamation

Whereas section 4 of the [Visiting Forces Act](/eng/acts/V-2), chapter
V-2 of the Revised Statutes of Canada, 1985, provides that the Governor
in Council may by proclamation designate any country as a designated
state for the purposes of the Act and declare the extent to which that
Act is applicable in respect of any designated state;

Now Know you that We, by and with the advice of Our Privy Council for
Canada and pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1995-1969 of November 23,
1995, do by this Our Proclamation

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} designate the Republic of Benin, the Republic of El
  Salvador, the Republic of Estonia, the Republic of Latvia, the
  Republic of Lithuania, the Republic of Nicaragua and Romania as
  designated states for the purposes of the [Visiting Forces
  Act](/eng/acts/V-2); and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} declare that the [Visiting Forces
  Act](/eng/acts/V-2), with the exception of Part VI, is applicable in
  respect of the designated states referred to in paragraph (a).

Of All Which Our Loving Subjects and all others whom these Presents may
concern are hereby required to take notice and to govern themselves
accordingly.

In Testimony Whereof, We have caused these Our Letters to be made Patent
and the Great Seal of Canada to be hereunto affixed. Witness: Our Right
Trusty and Well-beloved Roméo A. LeBlanc, a Member of Our Privy Council
for Canada, Chancellor and Principal Companion of Our Order of Canada,
Chancellor and Commander of Our Order of Military Merit, Governor
General and Commander-in-Chief of Canada.

At Our Government House, in Our City of Ottawa, this twenty-eighth day
of December in the year of Our Lord one thousand nine hundred and
ninety-five and in the forty-fourth year of Our Reign.

::: FlushRight
By Command,
:::

::: FlushRight
KEVIN G. LYNCH
:::

::: FlushRight
*Deputy Registrar General of Canada*
