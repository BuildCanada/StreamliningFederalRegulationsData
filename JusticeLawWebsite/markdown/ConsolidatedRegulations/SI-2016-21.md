## Certain Unauthorized Payments in Respect of Travel Expenses and Other Expenses, Allowances and Benefits (Canadian Forces) Remission Order {#certain-unauthorized-payments-in-respect-of-travel-expenses-and-other-expenses-allowances-and-benefits-canadian-forces-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2016-21
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2016-05-04

Certain Unauthorized Payments in Respect of Travel Expenses and Other
Expenses, Allowances and Benefits (Canadian Forces) Remission Order

P.C. 2016-231 2016-04-15
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that the
collection of debts for certain unauthorized payments in respect of
travel expenses and other expenses, allowances and benefits is
unreasonable and unjust, on the recommendation of the Minister of
National Defence and the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_3708_hq_11819){#fn_3708_hq_11819-ID0EGCAA} of
the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_3708_hq_11820){#fn_3708_hq_11820-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Certain Unauthorized Payments in Respect of Travel
Expenses and Other Expenses, Allowances and Benefits (Canadian Forces)
Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2016-21).

## [Remission]{.HTitleText1} {#h-629043 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 2,
remission is granted, in a total amount of \$147,487,513 including
interest, to any beneficiary of unauthorized payments in respect of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} separation expenses paid during the period beginning
  on September 1, 2001 and ending on January 31, 2011

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} to an officer or non-commissioned member who was a
    member of the reserve force on Class "B" Reserve Service and who
    would have been entitled to separation expenses under instruction
    209.997 of the Compensation and Benefits Instructions for the
    Canadian Forces had they been a member of the reserve force on Class
    "C" Reserve Service during the relevant period, in respect of any
    category of separation expense, up to the amounts that were
    authorized at that time under that instruction or fixed at that time
    under the authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} to an officer or non-commissioned member, in
    respect of lodging, to the extent that the expenses exceeded amounts
    authorized at that time by instruction 209.997, up to the amounts
    that were fixed at that time under the authority of the Chief of the
    Defence Staff,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} to an officer or non-commissioned member
    returning from temporary duty or attached posting, in respect of
    interim lodging and meal costs, up to the amounts that were fixed at
    that time under the authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} to an officer or non-commissioned member, in
    respect of lease liability costs, furniture rental, and one-time
    connection fees for cable, Internet, cellular telephones and
    land-line telephones, up to the amounts that were fixed at that time
    under the authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff, and

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} to an officer or non-commissioned member who
    married another officer or non-commissioned member at a time when
    the two were not posted to the same location;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} travel expenses for next of kin of an ill, injured,
  deceased or released officer or non-commissioned member paid from the
  Canadian Forces Contingency Fund during the period beginning on April
  1, 2007 and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} transportation on leave expenses of an officer or
  non-commissioned member that were purported to be authorized under
  instruction 209.50 of the Compensation and Benefits Instructions for
  the Canadian Forces, up to the amounts that were fixed at that time
  under the authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff, paid during the
  period beginning on February 1, 2007 and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} expenses for the storage of a private motor vehicle
  of an officer or non-commissioned member while they were absent on
  duty, up to the amounts that were fixed at that time under the
  authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff, paid during the period
  beginning on April 1, 2006 and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} travel expenses of a person holding an honorary
  appointment under article 3.06 of the Queen's Regulations and Orders
  for the Canadian Forces, paid during the period beginning on January
  1, 2005 and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} expenses for the maintenance and safeguarding of a
  vacant residence of an officer or non-commissioned member while absent
  on duty, up to the amounts that were fixed at that time under the
  authority of the Chief of the Defence Staff, paid during the period
  beginning on September 1, 2001 and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} temporary duty travel expenses and allowances of an
  officer or non-commissioned member, while on an attachment, up to the
  amounts that were fixed at that time under the authority of the Chief
  of the Defence Staff, paid during the period beginning on July 1, 2006
  and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} financial assistance for an ill, injured or released
  officer or non-commissioned member, or their family members, or for
  the family members of a deceased officer or non-commissioned member,
  paid during the period beginning on April 1, 1999 and ending on
  January 31, 2011;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} compassionate travel assistance for an officer or
  non-commissioned member that was purported to be authorized under
  instruction 209.51 of the Compensation and Benefits Instructions for
  the Canadian Forces, paid during the period beginning on April 1, 2006
  and ending on January 31, 2011;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} recruitment allowances for an officer or
  non-commissioned member, or for an applicant to the Canadian Forces,
  that were purported to be authorized under instruction 205.525 of the
  Compensation and Benefits Instructions for the Canadian Forces, paid
  during the period beginning on May 1, 2002 and ending on October 31,
  2005; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} vacation travel allowances paid during the period
  beginning on January 1, 2004 and ending on April 30, 2010, to Brian
  Donohue in the amount of \$20,317, to Maria Groenewege in the amount
  of \$16,732 and to Kathreen Siberry in the amount of \$9,562.

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Amounts that have been
recovered are not remitted.

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Remission is granted to any
  accounting officer for the unrecovered amount of any unauthorized
  payments referred to in section 1, including payable interest, for
  which the officer is responsible.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In this section, [[accounting
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any current or former [[accounting
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, as defined in article 1.02 of the
  Queen's Regulations and Orders for the Canadian Forces, or any other
  person who has performed the functions of such an officer.
