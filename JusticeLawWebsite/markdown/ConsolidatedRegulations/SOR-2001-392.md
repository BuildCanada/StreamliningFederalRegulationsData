## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673919 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bank
    Act](/eng/acts/B-1.01). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a joint venture;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of an insurance company or
      securities dealer. ([entité désignée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[insurance company]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an insurance company
    incorporated by or under the laws of Canada or a province. ([société
    d'assurances]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[joint venture]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a real property entity

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} that was formed by a bank holding company, or by
      a designated entity controlled by a bank holding company, and one
      or more other persons for the purpose of a specific business
      undertaking;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in which the bank holding company or designated
      entity has a substantial investment; and

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of which the persons who formed it
      have agreed on joint control, regardless of the distribution of
      their equity. ([coentreprise]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity that is
    primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares of a body corporate that is primarily
      engaged in holding, managing or otherwise dealing with real
      property, including shares of a body corporate that is another
      real property entity; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} ownership interests in an unincorporated entity
      that is primarily engaged in holding, managing or otherwise
      dealing with real property, including ownership interests in an
      unincorporated entity that is another real property
      entity. ([entité immobilière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[related real property entity]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of
    a bank holding company, means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity, other than a designated
      entity controlled by the bank holding company, in which the bank
      holding company or a designated entity controlled by the bank
      holding company beneficially owns sufficient shares or ownership
      interests to cause the bank holding company or designated entity
      to have a substantial investment in the real property entity; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in paragraph (a). ([entité
      immobilière apparentée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[securities dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an entity described
    in paragraph (g) of the definition [[financial
    institution]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
    Act. ([négociant en valeurs mobilières]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[third party]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a bank holding
    company, means a person other than

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the bank
      holding company; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
      holding company. ([tierce partie]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Book value of interest in real property

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 8 to 11, the book value of an asset that is an interest of a
bank holding company in real property, at a particular time, is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is real property, the gross book value
  of the real property, less any accumulated depreciation on the real
  property, that would be reported on a balance sheet of the bank
  holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with the
  accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
  referred to in subsection 840(4) of the Act; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the asset is a security or loan, the book value of
  the security or loan that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
  bank holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with the
  accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
  referred to in subsection 840(4) of the Act.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673949 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exempt bank holding companies

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Sections 938 to 940 of the
Act do not apply to

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank holding company with equity of one
  billion dollars or more; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a bank holding company with equity of one billion
  dollars or more that is controlled by

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a widely held bank holding company,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a widely held insurance holding company,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an eligible financial institution, as defined in
    subsection 370(1) of the Act, other than a foreign bank, or

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a foreign bank that is widely held.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-25, s. 1
:::

## [Prescribed Subsidiary]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673960 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed subsidiary

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 938 to 940 of the Act, a prescribed subsidiary of a bank
holding company is a subsidiary of the bank holding company other than

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an insurance company;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a subsidiary of an insurance company or securities
  dealer.

## [Prescribed Percentage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673966 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of sections 938 and 939 of the Act, the prescribed percentage
  is 70%.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prescribed percentage

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of section 940 of the Act, the
  prescribed percentage is 100%.

## [Valuation of Equity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673972 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value equals book value

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 939 and 940 of the Act, the value of the participating shares
and ownership interests referred to in those sections that are
beneficially owned by a bank holding company and its subsidiaries
prescribed by section 4 is equal to their book value as reported on the
consolidated balance sheet of the bank holding company.

## [Interests of a Bank Holding Company in Real Property]{.HTitleText1} {#h-673975 .Part}

### [General]{.HTitleText2} {#h-673976 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition and value of interest in real
property

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purposes of Division 9 of Part XV of the Act, other than
  subsection 937(3) of the Act, sections 8 to 12 define the interests of
  a bank holding company in real property and set out how those
  interests are to be valued.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Exception

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection 937(3) of the Act, the
  interests of a bank holding company in real property means real
  property referred to in paragraph 11(1)(a) and shares and ownership
  interests referred to in paragraph 11(1)(b) that become beneficially
  owned by the bank holding company, or by a designated entity
  controlled by the bank holding company, as a result of the realization
  of a security interest on a loan or debt obligation referred to in
  subsection 11(1).

### [Direct Interests --- Real Property and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-673982 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Direct interests

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a bank holding company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by the bank
    holding company or by a designated entity controlled by the bank
    holding company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation, for which the bank holding
    company or a designated entity controlled by the bank holding
    company is liable, that was issued for the purpose of acquiring or
    improving real property referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of direct interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a bank holding company
  in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), any amount by which the book value of the debt
    obligation exceeds the book value of the real property referred to
    in that paragraph.

### [Indirect Interests --- Real Property, Shares and Ownership Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-673992 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a bank holding company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at a particular time, real property

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that is beneficially owned by

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
        holding company that is a joint venture, or

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} an entity in which an entity described in
        clause (A) has a substantial investment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that would be reported on a balance sheet of the
      bank holding company prepared as at that time in accordance with
      the accounting principles and specifications of the Superintendent
      referred to in subsection 840(4) of the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares and ownership interests beneficially owned
    by the bank holding company, or by a designated entity controlled by
    the bank holding company, in any related real property entity of the
    bank holding company other than a related real property entity
    referred to in paragraph (a).

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a bank holding company
  in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of real property referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the book value of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of shares and ownership interests
    referred to in paragraph (1)(b), the book value of the shares and
    ownership interests.

### [Indirect Interests --- Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674006 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a bank holding company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a related real property
    entity of the bank holding company and beneficially owned by the
    bank holding company or by a designated entity controlled by the
    bank holding company;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the bank holding company, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the bank holding company, to a
    related real property entity of the bank holding company;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by the bank holding company, or by a
    designated entity controlled by the bank holding company, to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity in which a securities
      dealer or insurance company controlled by the bank holding company
      has a substantial investment, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity that is controlled by a
      real property entity described in subparagraph (i);

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation issued by a real property entity
    described in subparagraph (c)(i) or (ii) and beneficially owned by
    the bank holding company or by a designated entity controlled by the
    bank holding company;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a debt obligation that is

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} issued by a related real property entity of the
      bank holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} beneficially owned by a third party, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} guaranteed by the bank holding company or by a
      designated entity controlled by the bank holding company; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a loan made by a third party to a related real
    property entity of the bank holding company and guaranteed by the
    bank holding company or by a designated entity controlled by the
    bank holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a bank holding company
  in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a debt obligation referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a) or (d), the book value of the debt obligation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b) or (c), the book value of the loan; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guaranteed debt obligation referred
    to in paragraph (1)(e) or a guaranteed loan referred to in
    paragraph (1)(f),

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is issued by, or the loan
      is made to, an entity that is a related real property entity of
      the bank holding company and the entity beneficially owns real
      property that is an interest of the bank holding company in real
      property under paragraph 9(1)(a), any amount by which the face
      value of the guarantee exceeds the value of the real property
      determined in accordance with paragraph 9(2)(a), and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the face value of the
      guarantee.

### [Indirect Interests --- Secured Loans and Debt Obligations]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674028 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Certain indirect interests

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  bank holding company or a designated entity controlled by a bank
  holding company makes a loan to, or beneficially owns or guarantees
  the debt obligation of, a third party, the loan or debt obligation is
  an interest of the bank holding company in real property if it is
  secured by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} real property beneficially owned by a third party
    in conjunction with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the designated entity,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
      holding company,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a securities dealer or insurance company
      controlled by the bank holding company,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an entity controlled by a securities dealer or
      insurance company referred to in subparagraph (iv), or

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a real property entity described in
      subparagraph 10(1)(c)(i) or (ii); or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by
    a third party in

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an entity that beneficially owns real property in
      conjunction with the bank holding company, a related real property
      entity of the bank holding company or a designated entity
      controlled by the bank holding company, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
      holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a bank holding company
  in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by real property referred to in paragraph (1)(a), the lesser
    of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in the real property at the time that the security
      interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674047 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the bank holding
            company or a designated entity controlled by the bank
            holding company or the debt obligation is beneficially owned
            by the bank holding company or a designated entity
            controlled by the bank holding company, the book value of
            the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            bank holding company or a designated entity controlled by
            the bank holding company, the face value of the guarantee,
            and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation;
      :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in an entity referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i), the
    lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674056 .Subparagraph}
      A - (B - (C × D/E))

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the bank holding
            company or a designated entity controlled by the bank
            holding company or the debt obligation is beneficially owned
            by the bank holding company or a designated entity
            controlled by the bank holding company, the book value of
            the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            bank holding company or a designated entity controlled by
            the bank holding company, the face value of the guarantee,

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of all security interests
          that were given for the loan or debt obligation,

      [C]{.dfn}
      :   is the net realizable value of the entity's interest in the
          real property referred to in subparagraph (1)(b)(i),

      [D]{.dfn}
      :   is the value of the shares or ownership interests that are
          given as the security interest, and

      [E]{.dfn}
      :   is the total value of the outstanding shares or ownership
          interests in the entity; and
      :::

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a loan or debt obligation that is
    secured by shares or ownership interests beneficially owned by a
    third party in a related real property entity referred to in
    subparagraph (1)(b)(ii), the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the net realizable value of the third party's
      interest in those shares or ownership interests at the time that
      the security interest was given, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the amount determined by the formula

      ::: {#674068 .Subparagraph}
      A - B

      where

      [A]{.dfn}
      :   is
          - [(A)]{.lawlabel} if the loan was made by the bank holding
            company or a designated entity controlled by the bank
            holding company or the debt obligation is beneficially owned
            by the bank holding company or a designated entity
            controlled by the bank holding company, the book value of
            the loan or debt obligation, or

          - [(B)]{.lawlabel} if the debt obligation is guaranteed by the
            bank holding company or a designated entity controlled by
            the bank holding company, the face value of the guarantee,
            and

      [B]{.dfn}
      :   is the total net realizable value of any other security
          interests that were given for the loan or debt obligation.
      :::

### [Other Interests]{.HTitleText2} {#h-674074 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Other interests

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following are interests of a bank holding company in real property:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a guarantee given by the bank holding company, or
    by a designated entity controlled by the bank holding company, to an
    entity other than the bank holding company or designated entity for
    the purpose of completing the development of real property that is
    beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the bank
      holding company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
      holding company; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agreement made by the bank holding company, or
    by a designated entity controlled by the bank holding company, to
    support a third party's cost of operating or financing real property
    that is beneficially owned by

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the bank holding company,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated entity controlled by the bank
      holding company, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a related real property entity of the bank
      holding company.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Value of those interests

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The value of the interests of a bank holding company
  in real property is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a guarantee referred to in
    paragraph (1)(a), the estimated cost of completing the development
    of the real property; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an agreement referred to in
    paragraph (1)(b), the amount of any funds advanced by the bank
    holding company, or by a designated entity controlled by the bank
    holding company, under the agreement.
