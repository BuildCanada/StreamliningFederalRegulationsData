## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550738 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in these Rules.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Bankruptcy and Insolvency
    Act](/eng/acts/B-3). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[business hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[business hours]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to a Division
    Office, means the hours during which the Division Office is open to
    the public from Monday to Friday, holidays excepted, as posted by
    that Division Office. ([heures d'ouverture]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[directive]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a directive issued by the
    Superintendent pursuant to subsection 5(4) of the Act.
    ([instructions]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Division Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Division Office]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the office of the
    Superintendent, Department of Industry, for the bankruptcy division
    in which the proceedings were commenced. ([bureau de
    division]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[judge]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a judge of a court having
    jurisdiction in bankruptcy under sections 183 to 186 of the Act.
    ([juge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[petition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[petition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2007-61,
    s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

[[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[registrar]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person appointed or
    assigned as a registrar in bankruptcy under section 184 of the Act.
    ([registraire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tariff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tariff]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the tariff of costs set out in
    the schedule. ([tarif]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[taxing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[taxing officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the registrar or other
    officer appointed or assigned under section 184 of the Act for the
    taxation or fixing of costs or the passing of accounts.
    ([fonctionnaire taxateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-579, s. 2
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2007-61, s. 1
:::

are to be prescribed must be in the form prescribed, with any
modifications that the circumstances require and subject to any
deviations permitted by section 32 of the [Interpretation
Act](/eng/acts/I-21), and must be used in proceedings under the Act.

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (2), a notice or other document that is received by a
  Division Office outside of its business hours is deemed to have been
  received

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the next business day of that Division Office,
    if it was received

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} between the end of business hours and midnight,
      local time, on a business day, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} on a Saturday or holiday; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} at the beginning of business hours of that Division
    Office, if it was received between midnight and the beginning of
    business hours, local time, on a business day.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subsection (1) does not apply to documents related to
  proceedings under Part III of the Act that are filed by facsimile.

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise provided in the Act or these Rules, every notice or other
  document given or sent pursuant to the Act or these Rules must be
  served, delivered personally, or sent by mail, courier, facsimile or
  electronic transmission.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Unless otherwise provided in these Rules, every
  notice or other document given or sent pursuant to the Act or these
  Rules

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be received by the addressee at least four
    days before the event to which it relates, if it is served,
    delivered personally, or sent by facsimile or electronic
    transmission; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must be sent to the addressee at least 10 days
    before the event to which it relates, if it is sent by mail or by
    courier.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A trustee, receiver or administrator who gives or
  sends a notice or other document shall prepare an affidavit, or obtain
  proof, that it was given or sent, and shall retain the affidavit or
  proof in their files.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The court may, on an [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"}
  application, exempt any person from the application of subsection (2)
  or order any terms and conditions that the court considers
  appropriate, including a change in the time limits.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2007-61, ss. 3(E), 63(E)

## [Court Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550791 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} All
  proceedings used in court must be dated and entitled in the name of
  the court in which they are used, together with the words "in
  Bankruptcy and Insolvency".

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every document used in the filing of a bankruptcy
  application or used after the filing of an assignment must be entitled
  "In the Matter of the Bankruptcy of \...".

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every document used in the filing of a proposal
  before bankruptcy must be entitled "In the Matter of the Proposal
  of \...".

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Every document used in the course of a receivership
  must be entitled "In the Matter of the Receivership of \...".

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} Unless the Chief Justice, Associate Chief Justice or
  Commissioner, as the case may be, referred to in section 184 of the
  Act otherwise directs, every document that is required to be filed in
  court must first be filed at the office of the registrar.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} If the court deems necessary that any notice be sent
  to the Superintendent in any proceeding before it, a copy of that
  notice shall be sent to the Division Office.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2007-61, s. 63(E), 64

## [Motions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550804 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Subject to these Rules,
every application to the court must be made by motion unless the court
orders otherwise.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The Superintendent may
intervene in any application to the court by filing a notice of
intervention with the court.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Subject to any order of
the court given in exigent circumstances, a party who makes a motion
must, at least one day before the day set for the hearing of the motion,
file with the court

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the original of the notice of motion, or the motion,
  as the case may be;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} every affidavit in support of the notice of motion or
  the motion, as the case may be; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} proof of service, if any, of the documents described
  in paragraphs (a) and (b).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2005-284, s. 2
:::

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  bankrupt or other person who is apprehended under section 166 of the
  Act shall be kept in the place of custody set out in the warrant,
  pending the order of the court.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} As soon as a bankrupt or other person has been handed

## [Costs and Taxation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550839 .Part}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** All bills of costs for
legal services -- other than those that do not exceed \$2,500 in
aggregate, excluding applicable federal and provincial taxes -- must be
taxed by the taxing officer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2009-218, s. 2
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-18-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 18"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** A bill of costs must
describe, in a fair, reasonable and detailed manner, the nature of the
legal services rendered.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** The bill of costs shall
not be taxed unless the trustee is represented at the taxation or the
bill of costs has attached to it a declaration, signed by the trustee,
stating that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2009-218, s. 3\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the services have been duly authorized and duly
  rendered; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the charges are reasonable in the trustee's opinion.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2009-218, s. 3
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-20-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 20"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** In determining the amount
of costs to be allowed, the taxing officer shall determine whether

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the legal services have been duly rendered;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the charges are reasonable and, if applicable, are in
  accordance with the tariff;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the legal services rendered are accounted for, and
  are not services that should have been rendered by the trustee; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the legal services have been authorized and approved
  in accordance with the Act, if the Act so requires.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2007-61, s. 63(E)
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-21-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 21"){.PITLink}
:::

**[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** When a bill of costs has
been taxed, the taxing officer shall make a statement to that effect on
the bill and shall sign that statement, and a bill of costs so signed
has the same effect as a judgment of the court and may be enforced in
the same manner as a judgment.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** The court may, on [ex
parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application by a trustee, order a barrister
or solicitor or, in the Province of Quebec, an advocate to submit a bill
of costs to the trustee, or to bring the bill of costs into court to be
taxed, and, if the barrister, solicitor or advocate fails to comply, the
court may, on [ex parte]{.otherLang lang="la"} application by the
trustee, order that the trustee distribute the proceeds of the estate
that the trustee possesses without regard to the barrister, solicitor or
advocate's bill of costs.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
- SOR/2007-61, s. 7
:::

::: PITLink
[Previous
Version](section-23-20060322.html "Link to previous version of section 23"){.PITLink}
:::

  payment of the bill of costs and, on request, to the Division Office,

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certified copy of the bill of costs to be presented

## [Appeals from Decisions of the Registrar]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550884 .Part}

- **[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  appeal from an order or decision of the registrar must be made by
  motion to a judge.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of motion or a motion, as the case may be,
  must be filed at the office of the registrar and served on the other
  party within 10 days after the day of the order or decision appealed
  from, or within such further time as the judge stipulates.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The notice of motion or the motion must set out the
  grounds of the appeal.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

  appeal.

## [Code of Ethics for Trustees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-550904 .Part}

**[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** Every trustee shall
maintain the high standards of ethics that are central to the
maintenance of public trust and confidence in the administration of the
Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
sections 39 to 52, [[professional engagement]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
any bankruptcy or insolvency matter in respect of which a trustee is
appointed or designated to act in that capacity pursuant to the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** Trustees shall perform
their duties in a timely manner and carry out their functions with
competence, honesty, integrity and due care.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** Trustees shall cooperate
fully with representatives of the Superintendent in all matters arising
out of the Act, these Rules or a directive.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/78-389, s. 2
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** Trustees shall not
assist, advise or encourage any person to engage in any conduct that the
trustees know, or ought to know, is illegal or dishonest, in respect of
the bankruptcy and insolvency process.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/98-240, s. 1
:::

**[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** Trustees shall be honest
and impartial and shall provide to interested parties full and accurate
information as required by the Act with respect to the professional
engagements of the trustees.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/81-646, s. 2
- SOR/98-240, s. 1

  of the Act.

## [Mediation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-551367 .Part}

- **[[105]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-105 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For
  the purposes of subsections 68(8) and 170.1(2) of the Act, the
  procedures governing a mediation are as set out in this section.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of this section,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the bankrupt and the trustee are always parties to
    the mediation;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the trustee may act either personally or through a
    representative;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an opposition to discharge made by a creditor or
    the trustee, referred to in subsection 170.1(1) of the Act, is
    deemed to be a request by the creditor or the trustee, as the case
    may be, for mediation; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a creditor who requests mediation is a party to the
    mediation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of conducting a particular mediation,
  the Superintendent shall designate as mediator

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} an employee of a Division Office, including
    Division Offices other than the one for the bankruptcy division in
    which the proceedings were commenced; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other person with training or experience in
    mediation and whom the Superintendent considers qualified.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} On receipt of a request for mediation from a trustee
  under subsection 68(6) or (7) or 170.1(1) of the Act, accompanied by
  the most recent income and expense statement in prescribed form
  completed by the bankrupt, the official receiver shall refer the
  matter to the mediator, who shall set the time and place for the
  mediation. The time set for the mediation must be within 45 days after
  the official receiver received the request for mediation.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The mediator shall conduct the mediation with all
  parties physically present, unless the mediator decides to conduct the
  mediation by telephone conference call or by means of any other
  communication facilities that permit all persons participating in the
  mediation to communicate with each other.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The mediation must be held at the Division Office, at
  any other place that is designated by the mediator, or, if the
  mediation is conducted otherwise than with all parties physically
  present, at any combination of places necessary for that purpose.

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The mediator shall send a copy of the notice of the
  mediation, in prescribed form, to the bankrupt, to the trustee and to
  any creditor who requested mediation, at least 15 days, or any shorter
  period that may be agreed to by all the parties concerned, before the
  date set for the mediation.

- [(8)]{.lawlabel} If, at any time before the mediation has started, the
  mediator believes on reasonable grounds that the mediation cannot
  proceed at the time scheduled, the mediator shall reschedule it,
  setting a new time and place.

- [(9)]{.lawlabel} Except when it would constitute a second adjournment,
  the mediator shall, subject to subsection (13), adjourn the mediation
  at any time during the mediation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a party requests an adjournment and the mediator
    believes on reasonable grounds that the mediation would benefit from
    further negotiations or the provision of additional information;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mediator believes on reasonable grounds that
    one of the parties, other than the trustee in the case of a
    mediation requested by a creditor under subsection 170.1(1) of the
    Act, cannot continue the mediation for a certain period of time;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} all the creditors who were informed of the
    mediation in accordance with subsection (7) or (11) fail to appear
    at the mediation and the mediator believes on reasonable grounds,
    with respect to at least one of those creditors, that the
    non-appearance is neither a delaying tactic nor intended to bring
    the mediation into disrepute;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mediation requested by a creditor
    under subsection 170.1(1) of the Act, a party, other than the
    trustee, who was informed of the mediation in accordance with
    subsection (7) or (11) fails to appear at the mediation and the
    mediator believes on reasonable grounds that the non-appearance is
    neither a delaying tactic nor intended to bring the mediation into
    disrepute; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} in any case other than the one referred to in
    paragraph (d), a party, other than a creditor, who was informed of
    the mediation in accordance with subsection (7) or (11) fails to
    appear at the mediation and the mediator believes on reasonable
    grounds that the non-appearance is neither a delaying tactic nor
    intended to bring the mediation into disrepute.

- [(10)]{.lawlabel} If a mediation is rescheduled or adjourned, the new
  date set must be within 10 days after the date on which the
  rescheduling or adjournment occurs.

- [(11)]{.lawlabel} If a mediation is rescheduled or adjourned, the
  mediator shall inform the parties of the new time and place.

- [(12)]{.lawlabel} At any time during the mediation, the mediator
  shall, subject to subsection (13), cancel the mediation if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is an outstanding opposition to the discharge
    of the bankrupt by a creditor or the trustee on a ground referred to
    in paragraphs 173(1)(a) to (l) or (o) of the Act;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the mediator believes on reasonable grounds that a
    party is abusing the rescheduling procedures;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} there has already been an adjournment and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} there is a request for adjournment under
      paragraph (9)(a), or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} one of the circumstances referred to in
      paragraphs (9)(b) to (e) occurs;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the mediator believes on reasonable grounds that
    one of the parties, other than the trustee in the case of a
    mediation requested by a creditor under subsection 170.1(1) of the
    Act, cannot continue the mediation at all;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} all the creditors who were informed of the
    mediation in accordance with subsection (7) or (11) fail to appear
    at the mediation and the mediator believes on reasonable grounds,
    with respect to all of those creditors, that the non-appearance is a
    delaying tactic or is intended to bring the mediation into
    disrepute;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a mediation requested by a creditor
    under subsection 170.1(1) of the Act, a party, other than the
    trustee, who was informed of the mediation in accordance with
    subsection (7) or (11) fails to appear at the mediation and the
    mediator believes on reasonable grounds that the non-appearance is a
    delaying tactic or is intended to bring the mediation into
    disrepute; or

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} in any case other than the one referred to in
    paragraph (f), a party, other than a creditor, who was informed of
    the mediation in accordance with subsection (7) or (11) fails to
    appear at the mediation and the mediator believes on reasonable
    grounds that the non-appearance is a delaying tactic or is intended
    to bring the mediation into disrepute.

- [(13)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraphs (9)(b) and (d) and (12)(d) and
  (f), the absence of one or more creditors who requested mediation, or
  the inability of one or more creditors who requested mediation to
  continue the mediation, is not a ground for adjourning or cancelling
  the mediation if at least one creditor who requested mediation is
  present at the mediation, or is able to continue the mediation, as the
  case may be.

- [(14)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a mediation under section 170.1 of
  the Act, if all of the creditors who requested the mediation cause the
  cancellation of the mediation under paragraph (12)(e),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the opposition to discharge on the part of each of
    those creditors on a ground referred to in paragraph 173(1)(m)
    or (n) of the Act is deemed withdrawn; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the issues submitted to mediation are deemed to
    have been thereby resolved for the purposes of subsection 170.1(3)
    of the Act.

- [(15)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a mediation under section 68 of the Act is
    cancelled under any of paragraphs (12)(a) to (g), or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a mediation under section 170.1 of the Act is
    cancelled otherwise than under paragraph (12)(e),

  the issues submitted to mediation are deemed to have not been thereby
  resolved for the purposes of subsection 68(10) or 170.1(3), as the
  case may be, of the Act.

- [(16)]{.lawlabel} If a mediation is cancelled, the mediator shall send
  to the Division Office and the parties a notice of the cancellation,
  in prescribed form, setting out the grounds for the cancellation.

- [(17)]{.lawlabel} No mediator or party to a mediation shall disclose
  to the public any confidential information concerning an issue
  submitted to mediation, unless the disclosure is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} required by law; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} authorized by the person to whom the confidential
    information relates.

- [(18)]{.lawlabel} If agreement is reached by all parties at the
  mediation, a mediation settlement agreement, in prescribed form and
  including all terms and conditions of the settlement reached, must be
  signed by the parties, and the mediator shall send copies of the
  agreement to the Division Office and the parties. The agreement is
  binding on the parties, subject to any subsequent court order.

- [(19)]{.lawlabel} All payments made by a bankrupt under a mediation
  settlement agreement must be made to the trustee and deposited into
  the estate account.

- [(20)]{.lawlabel} If the parties fail to reach agreement at the
  mediation, the mediator shall issue a notice in prescribed form to the
  effect that the issues submitted to mediation under subsection 68(6)
  or (7) or 170.1(1), as the case may be, of the Act were not resolved,
  and shall send that notice to the Division Office and the parties.

### [PART III]{.HLabel1}[Scale of Trustee's Disbursements for Work in other than Summary Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#part-iiiscale-of-trustees-disbursements-for-work-in-other-than-summary-administration .SchedHeadL1}

The trustee shall, in other than summary administrations, be entitled to
be paid his disbursements, and in taxing such disbursements the taxing
officer may allow as disbursements the following:

- [1]{.lawlabel} For taking possession, verifying the bankrupt's
  statement of affairs, and making an inventory of his assets and a list
  of his liabilities:

  The actual disbursements of the trustee in connection with the said
  work.

- [2]{.lawlabel} For preparing and mailing to all creditors the
  following documents:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} notice of first meeting,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} documents accompanying notice of first meeting,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} explanatory circular letter,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} statement of assets and liabilities,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} minutes of first meeting or synopsis thereof,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} interim report from statement,

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} notice of application for discharge,

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} notice of application to pass accounts,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} statement of receipts and disbursements,

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} dividend sheet,

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} other documents necessary or desirable in the
    opinion of the taxing officer to inform the creditors,

  the cost of printing plus postage or if it is less,

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} on the first 100 notices and other documents,
    \$0.07 per folio,

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} on the next 200 notices and other documents, \$0.05
    per folio,

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} on any excess over 300 notices and other documents,
    \$0.02 per folio,

  plus postage.

- [3]{.lawlabel} Printed proof of debt and proxy forms at cost:

  If the court considers that the amount of remuneration allowed to the
  trustee is sufficient to compensate him adequately for all services
  rendered to the estate, the court may disallow the foregoing
  disbursements either in whole or in part.

  If a court officer performs a service for which no fee is provided in
  this tariff, the court may allow a fee in an amount equal to the fee
  in this tariff for the service most nearly analogous or comparable to
  the services rendered, or if no fee can be found herein applicable to
  the particular service rendered, according to the tariff in effect in
  other civil matters in the court.

  No disbursements are payable to trustees in respect of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} collection notices,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} notices of sale,

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} notices under section 120,

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} notices respecting goods in storage,

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} notices of stay of proceedings,

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} any other notice that is not sent to all creditors.
