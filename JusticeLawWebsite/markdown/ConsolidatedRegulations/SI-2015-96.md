## Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs as the appropriate Minister for the Windsor-Detroit Bridge Authority {#order-designating-the-minister-of-infrastructure-communities-and-intergovernmental-affairs-as-the-appropriate-minister-for-the-windsor-detroit-bridge-authority .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2015-96
:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2015-11-18

Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure, Communities and
Intergovernmental Affairs as the appropriate Minister for the
Windsor-Detroit Bridge Authority

P.C. 2015-1237 2015-11-04
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (d) of the definition
[[appropriate Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
[Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-558-e_hq_13162){#fn_81000-3-558-e_hq_13162-ID0EDDAA}
and subparagraph (a)(ii) of the definition [[appropriate
Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in subsection 83(1) of that Act,
effective November 4, 2015,

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-558-e_hq_13162-ID0EDDAA){#fn_81000-3-558-e_hq_13162}R.S., c.
  F-11
  :::

<!-- -->

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} repeals Order in Council P.C. 2012-1352 of October 9,
  2012[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569-ID0EBBBAA){#fn_81000-3-829-e_hq_13569}SI/2012-78
    :::

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} designates the Minister of Infrastructure,
  Communities and Intergovernmental Affairs, a member of the Queen's
  Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister for the
  Windsor-Detroit Bridge Authority.
