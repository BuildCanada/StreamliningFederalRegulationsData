## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805475 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Status of the Artist
    Act](/eng/acts/S-19.6). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[applicant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an artist, an artists'
    association or a producer that has filed an application.
    ([demandeur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any application or
    complaint made to the Board under the Act.
    ([demande]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[application for reconsideration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[application for reconsideration]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    application for review of a Board determination or order, other than
    an application for review of a Board determination of a sector
    referred to in subsection 35(1) or an application for review of a
    certification order referred to in section 36. ([demande de
    révision]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[participant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any applicant, respondent
    or intervenor. ([participant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Returning Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Returning Officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who is
    appointed by the Board to conduct a representation vote. ([directeur
    du scrutin]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [General Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805483 .Part}

### [Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805484 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of all proceedings before the Board under the Act.

### [Computation of Time]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805486 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Unless
  otherwise stated by the Board, time limits must be calculated in
  calendar days.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Whenever a time limit or deadline calculated under
  these Regulations falls on a Saturday or a holiday, as defined in
  subsection 35(1) of the [Interpretation Act](/eng/acts/I-21), the time
  limit is extended to the next working day.

### [ Order or Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805490 .Subheading}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any member of the Board may
sign an order or a determination of the Board.

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805492 .Subheading}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An application must be
filed with the Board in writing and include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the respondent, if any;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the application and full particulars
  of the relevant facts;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
  requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their authorized
  representative; and

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

### [Notice of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805502 .Subheading}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** On receipt of an
application, other than an application referred to in section 24, 29 or
35, the Board must, to the extent possible, give notice of the
application in writing to a person whose rights may be directly affected
by the application.

### [Responses]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805504 .Subheading}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  response to an application must be filed with the Board in writing
  within 15 days after the day on which notice of the application is
  received and include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the respondent and of their authorized
    representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number of the application to which
    the response relates;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a full response to any allegations or issues raised
    in the application and full particulars of any additional relevant
    facts;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the respondent's position with respect to the
    determination or order sought by the applicant;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought by the
    respondent;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the respondent or of their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the response.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time to respond must be
  made to the Board in writing and set out the grounds for the requested
  extension.

### [Replies]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805517 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A reply
  to a response must be filed with the Board in writing within 10 days
  after the day on which the response is filed and include the
  following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number of the proceeding to which
    the reply relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a full reply to any allegations or issues raised in
    the response and full particulars of any additional relevant facts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's position with respect to the
    determination or order sought by the respondent;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of the reply.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time to reply must be
  made to the Board in writing and set out the grounds for the requested
  extension.

### [Requests for Leave to Intervene]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805528 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for leave to intervene under subsection 19(3) of the Act must
  be filed with the Board in writing within 15 days after the day on
  which notice of the application is received or within the time period
  set out in any public notice referred to in subsections 24(1) and
  35(2) and include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the person requesting leave to intervene and of
    their authorized representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number of the application that is
    the subject of the request for leave to intervene;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for intervention and a description of
    the person's interest in the matter, including an explanation of any
    prejudice that the person would suffer if the request was denied and
    an explanation of whether their interest is different from that of
    any other participant;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an indication of how the intervention will assist
    the Board in furthering the objectives of the Act;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the person requesting leave to
    intervene or of their authorized representative; and

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date of the request.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response to the request for leave to intervene must
  be filed within 10 days after the day on which notice of the request
  is received.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A reply to the response to the request for leave to
  intervene must be filed within five days after the day on which the
  response is filed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time for filing any
  document under this section must be made to the Board in writing and
  set out the grounds for the requested extension.

### [Interventions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805541 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  request for leave to intervene is granted, the intervenor must file a
  written submission with the Board on the merits of the case within
  10 days after the day on which notice is received that the request has
  been granted and it must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number of the application to which
    the submission relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} full particulars of the facts, relevant dates and
    grounds for the submission;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the intervenor's position with respect to any
    determination or order sought;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought by the
    intervenor; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A response to the intervenor's submission on the
  merits of the case must be filed within 10 days after the day on which
  the submission is filed.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time for filing any
  document under this section must be made to the Board in writing and
  set out the grounds for the requested extension.

### [Filing and Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805551 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 16, a
person who files a document with the Board, other than an application,
must serve without delay a copy of that document on all participants and
any other person named in any notice that the person has received and
must inform the Board of the time and manner of service.

### [Manner of Filing and Service of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805553 .Subheading}

- **[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  document that is required by these Regulations to be filed with the
  Board or served on any person, or the person's authorized
  representative, must be filed or served

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} by hand, to the recipient;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} by mail to the address for service, as described in
    subsection (2);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} by transmission through electronic means that
    provides proof of receipt of the document; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} by any other manner that the Board authorizes.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In paragraph (1)(b), the address for service is

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of the Board, the address of an office
    of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada that is
    identified as a Board office; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any other person, the address of the
    person that appears in any notice issued by the Board in the
    proceeding in respect of which service is being made or, if no
    address appears in the notice, the last known address of the person.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A document that is transmitted by electronic means in
  accordance with paragraph (1)(c) must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the person transmitting the document;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the person to whom the document is being
    transmitted;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of transmission; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the total number of pages being transmitted.

### [Filing of Documents --- Oral Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805570 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant that intends to present evidence at an oral hearing must
  file with the Board six copies, or the number of copies that the Board
  may specify, of the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} all documents that the participant intends to
    present as evidence, including any document filed with the
    application, response or reply, as the case may be, in one or more
    tabbed books; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a list of witnesses expected to be called that
    includes their names and occupations, along with a summary of the
    information that is expected to be provided on issues raised in the
    application, response or reply.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subsection (1) must be
  filed

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} no later than 10 days before the day on which the
    hearing is scheduled, in the case of the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} no later than eight days before that day, in the
    case of the respondent and the intervenor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in subsection (1) must be
  served on all other participants within the applicable time period as
  set out in subsection (2).

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a participant does not comply with
  subsection (1), (2) or (3), the Board may refuse to consider any
  document or hear any witness that is presented by the participant at
  the hearing.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Board may require that the participant submit to
  it, in advance of the hearing, the authorities and arguments on which
  the participant intends to rely.

### [Date of Filing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805581 .Subheading}

**[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** The date of filing of any
document with the Board is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a document sent by registered mail,
  the date on which it is mailed; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in any other case, the date on which the document is
  received by the Board.

### [Production of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805585 .Subheading}

- **[[15]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-15 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  participant may, at any time before a hearing, request that another
  participant produce any document relevant to the proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If that other participant fails to produce the
  document within 10 days after the day on which the request is
  received, the participant that made the request may apply to the Board
  for an order requiring its production.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If that other participant fails to comply with a
  request made under subsection (1) or an order referred to in
  subsection (2), the Board may order the participant to pay the costs
  of any adjournment of the proceedings that results from the failure.

### [Confidentiality of Documents]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805590 .Subheading}

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the Board
  must place a document on the public record if the document is relevant
  to the proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board, on its own initiative or at the request of
  a participant, may declare that a document is confidential.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In determining whether a document is confidential,
  the Board must consider whether disclosure of the document would cause
  specific direct harm to a person and whether the specific direct harm
  would outweigh the public interest in its disclosure.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If the Board declares that a document is
  confidential, the Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} order that the document or any part of it not be
    placed on the public record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} order that a version or any part of the document
    from which the confidential information has been removed be placed
    on the public record;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} order that any portion of a hearing, including any
    argument, examination or cross-examination, that deals with the
    confidential document be conducted in private;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} order that the document or any part of it be
    provided to the participants, or only to their legal counsel or
    authorized representative, and that the document not be placed on
    the public record; or

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} make any other order that it considers appropriate.

### [Confidentiality of Artists' Wishes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805601 .Subheading}

**[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17 .sectionLabel}** Despite any other
provision of these Regulations, the Board, or an employee of the
Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada who is authorized to
act on behalf of the Board, must not disclose evidence that could reveal
membership in an artists' association, opposition to the certification
of an artists' association or the wish of any artist to be represented,
or not to be represented, by an artists' association, unless the
disclosure would further the objectives of the Act.

### [Evidence of Artists' Wishes]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805605 .Subheading}

**[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18 .sectionLabel}** An artist's membership in
an artists' association is evidence that the artist wishes to be
represented by that artists' association for the purposes of the Act.

### [Consolidation and Severance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805607 .Subheading}

**[[19]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-19 .sectionLabel}** The Board may order, in
respect of two or more proceedings, that they be consolidated, heard
together, heard consecutively or severed.

### [Notice of Oral Hearing]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805609 .Subheading}

- **[[20]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-20 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board determines that an oral hearing is necessary, it may give notice
  of the hearing by any available means.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must give not less than 15 days' notice of
  an oral hearing to the participants, unless the participants consent
  to a shorter notice.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a participant fails to appear after having been
  given notice, the Board may proceed and dispose of the matter in that
  participant's absence.

### [Summons]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805614 .Subheading}

- **[[21]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-21 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  request for a summons must be made to the Board in writing and include
  the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number of the proceeding to which
    the summons relates;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person to be summoned;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the person is required to appear;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the reason for the summons; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of any document or item that
    the person being summoned must bring with them to the oral hearing,
    and an indication of how the document or item is relevant to the
    proceeding.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The participant that requests a summons must serve
  the summons by hand on the person to be summoned at least five days
  before the person is required to appear, unless the Board directs
  otherwise.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The participant that requests a summons is
  responsible for paying the witness's expenses and fees, in accordance
  with section 64 of the Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A person who is summoned to an oral hearing must
  attend at the time and date specified, as well as each day of the
  hearing, unless the Board directs otherwise.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If an oral hearing is adjourned and the date on which
  it is to resume is not indicated at the time of the adjournment, the
  participant that requested the summons must notify the person summoned
  of the date on which the oral hearing is to resume

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} at least five days before the day on which the
    person summoned is to appear; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the Board has given less than five days' notice
    of the date on which the oral hearing is to resume, within a time
    period that is fair and reasonable in the circumstances.

### [Notice of Constitutional Question]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805628 .Subheading}

- **[[22]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-22 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  participant intends to question the constitutional validity,
  applicability or operability of a statute or regulation, the
  participant must, as soon as the circumstances giving rise to the
  question become known and no later than 10 days before the day on
  which the question is to be argued

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} serve a notice of a constitutional question on the
    other participants, the Attorney General of Canada and the attorney
    general of each province; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} file a copy of the notice with the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice of a constitutional question must be in
  the form required by the [Federal Courts
  Rules](/eng/regulations/SOR-98-106).

## [Certification Proceedings]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805634 .Part}

### [Application for Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805635 .Subheading}

- **[[23]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-23 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for certification must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
    representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a general description of the sector for which
    certification is sought;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the number of professional freelance
    artists working in the proposed sector;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an estimate of the number of members of the
    applicant who work in the proposed sector;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a current copy of the applicant's membership list
    that is certified by the applicant's authorized representative and
    that indicates

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each member's name and current postal address,
      and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if the applicant also represents individuals who
      do not work within the proposed sector, a list of the members who
      work within the proposed sector;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any scale agreement in force affecting
    the proposed sector;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the applicant's constitution and its
    by-laws both of which are certified by their authorized
    representative;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} evidence that the membership authorizes the
    applicant to apply for certification;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The information referred to in paragraphs (1)(d)
  and (e) must be filed with the Board as separate documents marked
  "Confidential/[Confidentiel]{.otherLang lang="fr"}".

### [Public Notice]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805652 .Subheading}

- **[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  Board must publish a notice of the application for certification in
  the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I, or provide
  the notice through any other means that the Board considers
  appropriate.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The notice must indicate the name of the applicant, a
  description of the proposed sector, and the time period for filing
  competing applications and expressions of interest from artists,
  artists' associations, producers and other interested persons in
  respect of the proposed sector.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The time period referred to in subsection (2) must be
  at least 30 days after the day on which the notice is published or
  provided.

### [Notice of Intervention as of Right]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805657 .Subheading}

- **[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  artist, artists' association or producer that intervenes in an
  application for certification under subsection 26(2) or 27(2) of the
  Act must file a notice of intervention with the Board.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A notice of intervention must be filed in writing
  within the time period specified in the notice published or provided
  under subsection 24(1) and include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the intervenor and of their authorized
    representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number indicated in the notice
    referred to in subsection 24(1);

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a written submission of the intervenor that
    includes a description of their interest in the matter and their
    position with respect to the determination sought;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the intervenor or of their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the date of filing of the notice of intervention.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A response to the intervenor's submission must be
  filed within 10 days after the day on which the submission is filed.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} A request for an extension of time for filing any
  document under this section must be made to the Board in writing and
  set out the grounds for the requested extension.

### [Subsequent Application for Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805668 .Subheading}

**[[26]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-26 .sectionLabel}** If the Board rejects an
application to certify an artists's association, that association may
not submit a new application for certification in respect of the same or
substantially the same sector until six months after the day on which
the previous application was rejected.

## [Representation Votes]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805670 .Part}

- **[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  Board orders that a representation vote be taken, the Board must
  appoint a Returning Officer.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Returning Officer may give directions to ensure
  the proper conduct of the vote and must report the results of the vote
  to the Board.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Returning Officer may appoint one or more
  employees of the Administrative Tribunals Support Service of Canada to
  assist in the conduct of the vote.

## [Revocation of Certification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805677 .Part}

### [Application for Revocation of Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805678 .Subheading}

**[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** An application for the
revocation of an artists' association's certification must include the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the artists' association that holds the
  certification that the applicant seeks to revoke;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a description of the sector within which the
  applicant works and in respect of which the artists' association has
  been certified;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the application and full particulars
  of the relevant facts;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
  requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their authorized
  representative; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

### [Notice of Application for Revocation of Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805689 .Subheading}

**[[29]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-29 .sectionLabel}** The Board must provide
the affected artists' association with a copy of the application for
revocation of certification.

### [Subsequent Application for Revocation of Certification]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805691 .Subheading}

**[[30]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-30 .sectionLabel}** If the Board rejects an
application for the revocation of an artists' association's
certification, a new application for revocation in respect of the same
sector may not be submitted until six months after the day on which the
previous application was rejected.

## [Joint Application to Change Termination Date of Scale Agreement]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805693 .Part}

**[[31]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-31 .sectionLabel}** A joint application to
change the termination date of a scale agreement must include the
following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of each joint applicant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a copy of all scale agreements between the parties,
  whether in force or expired, and any other document that the Board
  requires;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the signature of each joint applicant or of their
  authorized representative; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

## [Complaints]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805699 .Part}

**[[32]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-32 .sectionLabel}** A complaint made under
section 53 of the Act must include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the complainant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the person or organization that is the object of
  the complaint, or of any person who may be affected by the complaint;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the provision of the Act on which the complaint is
  based;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the complainant first knew of the
  actions or circumstances giving rise to the complaint;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} full particulars of any measures taken by the
  complainant to resolve the situation that gave rise to the complaint;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the complaint and full particulars of
  the relevant facts;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the date and description of any order or decision of
  the Board relating to the complaint;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the remedy sought by the
  complainant;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
  requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the complainant or of their
  authorized representative; and

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} the date of the complaint.

## [Declaration Respecting Pressure Tactic]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805713 .Part}

**[[33]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-33 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
declaration of an unlawful pressure tactic under subsection 47(1) or
section 48 of the Act must include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of any artist, artists' association or producer that,
  in the opinion of the applicant, could have an interest in the
  application;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a reference to the provision of the Act under which
  the application is being made;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the question that the applicant wishes to have the
  Board determine or the nature of the declaration that the applicant is
  seeking;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the application and full particulars
  of the relevant facts;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date and description of any order or decision of
  the Board relating to the application;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} the effective date and expiry date of any scale
  agreement in force or expired that applies to the artists in the
  existing sector;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
  requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their authorized
  representative; and

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

## [Referral of Question by Arbitrator or Arbitration Board]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805726 .Part}

- **[[34]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-34 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If an
  arbitrator or arbitration board refers a question to the Board under
  section 41 of the Act, the Board must give notice to the parties to
  the arbitration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Each party to the arbitration must file written
  submissions within 15 days after the day on which the notice of the
  referral of a question is received and must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the party and of their authorized representative,
    if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the position of the party respecting the question
    referred to the Board, the grounds for the party's position and full
    particulars of the relevant facts;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the party or of their authorized
    representative; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the date of filing of the submissions.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Each party must serve a copy of its submissions and
  documents on the other party.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Each party has the opportunity to respond to the
  other party's submissions within 10 days after the day on which those
  submissions are filed.

## [Application for Review of Board Determination or Order]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805738 .Part}

### [Determination of Sector]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805739 .Subheading}

- **[[35]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-35 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for review of a Board determination of a sector for the
  purpose of enlarging, modifying or clarifying the scope of the sector
  may be filed at any time by a certified artists' association or
  producer affected by the determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Board must publish a notice of the application
  for review in the [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/), Part I,
  or provide the notice through any other means that the Board considers
  appropriate if the review might result in an enlargement of the
  sector.

### [Certification Order]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805743 .Subheading}

**[[36]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-36 .sectionLabel}** An application for review
of a certification order may be filed by a certified artists'
association at any time in order to update that order for any purpose,
including making a change to the name of that association.

### [Reconsideration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805745 .Subheading}

- **[[37]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-37 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for reconsideration must be filed within 30 days after the
  day on which the determination or order is issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The application must be served on all persons who
  were participants to the determination or order that is the subject of
  the application.

### [Content of Application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805749 .Subheading}

**[[38]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-38 .sectionLabel}** An application for a
review referred to in sections 35 to 37 must include the following:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
  representative, if any;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
  and fax numbers of any artists' association or producer affected by
  the determination or order;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Board's file number and the date of the
  determination or order;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the application and full particulars
  of the relevant facts;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the determination or order sought;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
  requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their authorized
  representative; and

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

## [Filing Determination or Order in Federal Court]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805760 .Part}

- **[[39]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-39 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application to file a copy of a determination or order in the Federal
  Court under section 22 of the Act must include the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of the applicant and of their authorized
    representative, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name, postal and email addresses and telephone
    and fax numbers of any person named in, or directly affected by, the
    determination or order;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the determination or order to be filed;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the grounds for the applicant's belief that the
    determination or order should be filed, including why the applicant
    believes that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} any person named in the determination or order
      has failed or is likely to fail to comply with it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} filing the determination or order would serve a
      useful purpose;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} an indication of whether an oral hearing is being
    requested and, if so, the reason for the request;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a copy of any supporting document;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the signature of the applicant or of their
    authorized representative; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the date of the application.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the application states that a person named in the
  determination or order has failed or is likely to fail to comply with
  it, the applicant must serve a copy of the application on the person.

## [General Powers]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805774 .Part}

### [Other Procedural Matters]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805775 .Subheading}

**[[40]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-40 .sectionLabel}** If a procedural matter
that is not provided for by these Regulations arises, the Board may take
any action that is consistent with these Regulations and the Act and
that it considers necessary to resolve the matter.

### [Non-compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805777 .Subheading}

- **[[41]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-41 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If a
  participant, after being given an opportunity to comply by the Board,
  fails to comply with a rule of procedure under these Regulations, the
  Board may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} summarily dismiss or refuse to hear the
    application, if the non-complying participant is the applicant; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} decide the application without further notice, if
    the non-complying participant is the respondent or intervenor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a participant does not attend a pre-hearing
  proceeding or a hearing after having been given notice, the Board may
  decide the matter in the participant's absence.

### [Dispensing with Compliance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-805783 .Subheading}

**[[42]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-42 .sectionLabel}** The Board may, of its own
motion or on application, excuse a participant from complying with any
provision of these Regulations in order to ensure that a proceeding is
dealt with informally and expeditiously.

## [Transitional Provisions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805785 .Part}

- **[[43]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-43 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply in respect of all proceedings before the Board that
  are pending on the day on which these Regulations come into force.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Any proceeding commenced or document filed in
  accordance with the [Canadian Artists and Producers Professional
  Relations Tribunal Procedural
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-343) before the day on which
  these Regulations come into force is not invalid merely because the
  proceeding or document does not conform to these Regulations.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-805791 .Part}

into force on the day on which they are registered.
