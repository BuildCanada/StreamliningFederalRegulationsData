## Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Handling (Interprovincial and Export) Regulations {#ontario-greenhouse-vegetable-handling-interprovincial-and-export-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[C.R.C.]{.abbr title="Consolidated Regulations of Canada"}, c. 212
:::

[AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS MARKETING ACT](/eng/acts/A-6)

Regulations Respecting the Handling and Shipping in Interprovincial and
Export Trade of Greenhouse Vegetables Produced in Ontario
::::

::: section
## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536682 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the Ontario Greenhouse Vegetable Handling (Interprovincial and
Export) Regulations.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536684 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Commodity Board]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means The Ontario Greenhouse
    Vegetable Producers' Marketing Board; ([Office de
    commercialisation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[greenhouse vegetables]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means tomatoes,
    cucumbers and lettuce produced in the Province of Ontario in a
    greenhouse or any other enclosure under glass, plastic or other
    material used for the purpose of controlling temperature and
    providing protection for growing plants; ([légumes de
    serre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[producer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person engaged in the
    production of greenhouse vegetables; ([producteur]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any quantity of greenhouse
    vegetables; ([produit]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[shipper]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any person who offers for
    sale, sells, receives, assembles, packs, ships for sale or
    transports a product but does not include

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a servant employed by and driving a vehicle owned
      by a producer or a shipper,

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a railway company, or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person who transports the product by motor
      vehicle as agent of the producer. ([expéditeur]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536694 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply
only to the marketing of a product in interprovincial and export trade
and to persons and property situated in the Province of Ontario.

## [Handling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536696 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  producer shall deliver to a shipper a product that does not comply
  with the grades established by the Canada Agricultural Products
  Standards Act.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No shipper shall accept delivery of a product that
  does not comply with the grades established by the Canada Agricultural
  Products Standards Act.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every person packing a product shall mark all
  containers as required by the Farm Products Grades and Sales Act of
  Ontario and the regulations made thereunder.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper shall be
responsible for making an inspection of a product received by him to
insure that the product complies with the provisions of section 4.

## [Cooling]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536702 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  shipper shall cool a product received by him and not shipped on the
  day of receipt to a temperature between 13 and 18 degrees Celsius
  within 12 hours of receipt of the product.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Every shipper shipping a product in a railway car
  shall load the product in accordance with the loading and
  refrigeration instructions recommended by the railway company and
  required for the safe arrival of the product at its destination.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Every shipper transporting or shipping a product by
  means of a motor vehicle to a destination beyond a radius of 100 miles
  shall keep such product at a temperature between 13 and 18 degrees
  Celsius until the safe arrival of the product at its destination.

## [Shipping]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536707 .Part}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** Every shipper shall ship
each variety of a product received by him in the order in which the
variety is received.

## [Delivery]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536709 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Every
  shipper shall, upon the written direction of the Commodity Board,
  deliver to any other shipper designated in that direction such
  quantity of the product in his possession at the time he receives the
  direction as is specified in the direction.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Where a product is delivered by a shipper to another
  shipper pursuant to the direction set out in subsection (1), the
  shipper's remuneration shall be divided between the shipper who
  delivered the product and the shipper who received the product as
  determined by the Commodity Board.

## [Inspection]{.HTitleText1} {#h-536713 .Part}

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** The platform and storage
facilities of every shipper and the records of every shipment of a
product shall be available for inspection by authorized representatives
of the Commodity Board during normal business hours.
