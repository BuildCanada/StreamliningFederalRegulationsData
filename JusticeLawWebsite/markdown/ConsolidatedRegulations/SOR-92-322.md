## Disclosure of Interest (Trust and Loan Companies) Regulations {#disclosure-of-interest-trust-and-loan-companies-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/92-322
:::

[TRUST AND LOAN COMPANIES ACT](/eng/acts/T-19.8)

Registration 1992-05-21

Regulations Respecting the Disclosure of Interest Rates Relating to
Deposit Accounts and Debt Obligations

P.C. 1992-1097  1992-05-21
::::

::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Finance, pursuant to sections 427 to 429 of the [Trust
and Loan Companies Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_1e){#fn_1e-ID0EDBAA}, is pleased hereby to
make the annexed [Regulations respecting the disclosure of interest
rates relating to deposit accounts and debt
obligations](/eng/regulations/SOR-92-321), effective June 1, 1992.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944255 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Trust and Loan Companies
    Act](/eng/acts/T-19.8); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[deposit account]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an interest-bearing
    deposit account. ([compte de dépôt]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[point of service]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a physical location
    to which the public has access and at which a company carries on
    business with the public and opens or initiates the opening of
    deposit accounts through natural persons in Canada. ([point de
    service]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Disclosure in Respect of Deposit Accounts]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944262 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsection (1.1), a company shall disclose to a person who requests
  the company to open a deposit account the rate of interest applicable
  to the deposit account and how the amount of interest to be paid is to
  be calculated

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} where a statement of account is provided in respect
    of the deposit account, by means of a written statement delivered to
    that person, at or before the time the deposit account is opened; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} where no statement of account is provided in
    respect of the deposit account, by means of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a written statement copies of which are available
      and displayed in each branch of the company where deposit accounts
      are kept, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a general notice that is displayed in each of
      the company's branches where deposit accounts are kept, at each of
      the company's points of service and on the company's websites
      through which it offers deposit accounts in Canada.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} In the case of the opening of a deposit account on
  a telephone request as contemplated by subsection 431(3) of the Act, a
  company must, not later than seven business days after the account is
  opened, provide to the customer a written statement that sets out the
  rate of interest applicable to the account and how the amount of
  interest to be paid is to be calculated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A written statement and a general notice referred to
  in subsection (1) or a written statement referred to in subsection
  (1.1) shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the annual rate of interest;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the frequency of payment of interest;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the manner, if any, in which the balance in the
    deposit account affects the rate of interest; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} any other circumstance that affects the rate of
    interest.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A written statement referred to in subsection (1.1)
  that is sent to a customer by mail is deemed to be provided to the
  customer on the fifth day after the postmark date on the statement.

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Where there is a change in
the rate of interest, or in the manner of calculating the amount of
interest, that is applicable to a deposit account, the company shall
disclose the changes by means of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a written statement delivered to the person in whose
  name the account is maintained;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a written statement copies of which are available and
  displayed in each branch of the company where deposit accounts are
  kept; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a general notice that is displayed in each of the
  company's branches where deposit accounts are kept, at each of the
  company's points of service and on the company's websites through
  which it offers deposit accounts in Canada.

## [Disclosure in Respect of Advertising]{.HTitleText1} {#h-944285 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of section
428 of the Act, in all advertisements in respect of interest-bearing
deposits or debt obligations, a company shall disclose how the amount of
interest applicable to each deposit and debt obligation is to be
calculated by means of a statement in the advertisement that clearly
discloses

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in respect of interest-bearing deposits, the manner,
  if any, in which the balance of a deposit account will affect the rate
  of interest; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} any other circumstance that will affect the rate of
  interest.
