## Notice of Railway Works Regulations {#notice-of-railway-works-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/91-103
:::

[RAILWAY SAFETY ACT](/eng/acts/R-4.2)

Registration 1991-01-17

Regulations Respecting Notice of Proposed Railway Works

P.C. 1991-37 1991-01-17
::::

::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to section 50 of the Railway Safety Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_f){#footnote1_f-ID0EDABA}, a copy of
the proposed Regulations respecting notice of proposed railway works,
substantially in the form set out hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on December 24, 1988 and a
reasonable opportunity was thereby afforded to interested persons to
make representations to the Minister of Transport with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to subsection 8(1)
and paragraph 47(a) of the Railway Safety Act[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_f){#footnote1_f-ID0EBBAA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed Regulations respecting notice of proposed
railway works.

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932547 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Notice of Railway Works
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-91-103).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932549 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[municipality]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a municipality listed in Part
1 section 5 of the Canadian Almanac and Directory, published by Copp
Clark Pitman Limited, Toronto, Ontario, as amended from time to time.
(*municipalité*)

## [Prescribed Kinds of Works]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932551 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** For the purposes of
subsection 8(1) of the [Railway Safety Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2), the
following are prescribed as railway works of a kind for which notice
shall be given:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration of a line of railway
  involving the acquisition of land in addition to land on which an
  existing line of railway is situated;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration in a municipality of
  railway bridges and culverts having an overall span greater than six
  metres and railway tunnels;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration of structures located
  above or below a line of railway by a party other than a railway
  company, but excluding a mine or an oil or gas well;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration of road crossings for
  public use, including the installation or alteration of road crossing
  warning systems, but excluding the installation or alteration of road
  crossing signs; and

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} the construction or alteration of any line works that
  may affect drainage on land adjoining the land on which a line of
  railway is situated.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/94-692, s. 4
:::

## [Time and Content of Notice]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932560 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The notice referred to in
section 3 shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} be given at least 60 days before the proposed date of
  commencement of the proposed railway works;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} set out the period, not less than 60 days, during
  which objections, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the [Railway Safety
  Act](/eng/acts/R-4.2), may be filed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} contain the following information, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a drawing showing the location of the proposed
    works,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed works with general
    plans, including elevations of proposed structures,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a description of any impact that the proposed
    works may have on the safety of persons and property, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the proposed date of commencement and the
    projected time for completion of the proposed works.

## [Persons to Whom Notice Is to Be Given]{.HTitleText1} {#h-932569 .Part}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A notice
  referred to in section 3 shall be given

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a railway company proposing to
    construct or alter line works, to the municipality, or
    municipalities, in which the line works will be or are located and
    to any owner of land immediately abutting land on which the line
    works are situated;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any party proposing to construct a
    road crossing, excluding the installation of a road crossing warning
    system, to the following, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the railway company whose line is to be crossed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the municipality in which the crossing works are
      to be located,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the authority having responsibility for the
      road in question, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} any owner of land immediately abutting land on
      which the crossing works are situated; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of any party proposing to alter a road
    crossing or to install or alter a road crossing warning system, to
    the following, namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the railway company whose line is crossed,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the municipality in which the crossing works are
      located, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the authority having responsibility for the
      road in question.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A copy of a notice referred to in subsection (1)
  shall be sent forthwith to the Director of the regional Railway Safety
  Directorate office having jurisdiction over the railway at the
  location of the proposed works.
