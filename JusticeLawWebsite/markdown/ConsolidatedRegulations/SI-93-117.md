## Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Standards Council of Canada Act {#order-designating-the-minister-of-industry-science-and-technology-as-minister-for-purposes-of-the-standards-council-of-canada-act .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/93-117
:::

[STANDARDS COUNCIL OF CANADA ACT](/eng/acts/S-16)

Registration 1993-07-14

Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as
Minister for Purposes of the Standards Council of Canada Act

P.C. 1993-1463  1993-06-25
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition
[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the [Standards Council
of Canada Act](/eng/acts/S-16), is pleased hereby

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1982-15 of
  January 12, 1982[[Footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-117_e_hq_6517){#fn_SI-93-117_e_hq_6517-ID0EBCAAA};
  and

  - ::: Footnote
    [[Return to footnote
    ]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#fn_SI-93-117_e_hq_6517-ID0EBCAAA){#fn_SI-93-117_e_hq_6517}SI/82-28,
    1982 [Canada Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part II, p. 424
    :::

  <!-- -->

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and
    Technology, a member of the Queen's Privy Council for Canada, as the
    Minister for the purposes of the [Standards Council of Canada
    Act](/eng/acts/S-16).
