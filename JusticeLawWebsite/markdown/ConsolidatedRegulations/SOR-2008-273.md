## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[General]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746925 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definitions

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canadian Environmental
      Protection Act, 1999](/eng/acts/C-15.31). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[authorized facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[authorized facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a facility,
      including a transfer site, that is authorized by the authorities
      of the jurisdiction in which it is located to process PCBs or
      products containing PCBs or to conduct laboratory analysis or
      research with PCBs or products containing PCBs. ([installation
      agréée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[National Fire Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[National Fire Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the National Fire
      Code of Canada 2005, NRCC No. 47667, issued by the Canadian
      Commission on Building and Fire Codes, National Research Council
      of Canada, as amended from time to time. ([Code national de
      prévention des incendies]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[PCB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[PCB]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any chlorobiphenyl described in
      item 1 of the List of Toxic Substances in Schedule 1 to the
      Act. ([BPC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

  [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[process]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-301, s.
      3\]]{.Repealed}

  [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[product]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}[\[Repealed, SOR/2011-301, s.
      3\]]{.Repealed}

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Concentration --- several matrices

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, if a solid or
  a liquid containing PCBs is composed of several matrices, the
  concentration of PCBs is based on the mass of the matrix in which the
  PCBs are located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Accredited laboratory

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} Any analysis performed to determine the concentration
  of PCBs for the purposes of these Regulations must be performed by a
  laboratory that meets the following conditions at the time of the
  analysis:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} it is accredited

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} under the International Organization for
      Standardization standard ISO/IEC 17025, entitled General
      requirements for the competence of testing and calibration
      laboratories, by an accrediting body that is a signatory to the
      International Laboratory Accreditation Cooperation Mutual
      Recognition Arrangement, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} under the Environment Quality Act, CQLR, c. Q-2;
      and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the scope of its accreditation includes the
    analysis performed to determine the concentration of PCBs in the
    matrix in which the PCBs are located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling method

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of these Regulations, other than
  section 13, the concentration of PCBs in a matrix is determined using
  a provincially, nationally or internationally recognized sampling
  method for PCBs in the matrix in which the PCBs are located.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sampling method --- bulk solid products

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of section 13, the concentration of
  PCBs is determined using a sampling method for bulk solid products,
  which is set out in either federal or provincial legislation, as
  amended from time to time, or approved by the United States
  Environmental Protection Agency for compliance with the Resource
  Conservation and Recovery Act or with the regulations made under that
  Act, as amended from time to time.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

- **[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} These
  Regulations apply to PCBs and to any products containing PCBs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} These Regulations do not apply to the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the import and export of PCBs that are or are
    considered to be [[hazardous waste]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} or
    [[hazardous recyclable material]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} within the
    meaning of sections 2 to 5 of the Cross-border Movement of Hazardous
    Waste and Hazardous Recyclable Material Regulations;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the sale, importation or advertising of liquids
    containing PCBs for use in microscopy, including immersion oils but
    not including refractive index oils, which is prohibited under
    section 5 of the [Canada Consumer Product Safety
    Act](/eng/acts/C-1.68); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offer for sale, sale and use of land
    contaminated with PCBs or with products containing PCBs.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Sale of property

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations shall be construed as preventing the sale of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} personal property or movables that contain PCBs, or
  real property or immovables that have PCBs or products containing
  PCBs, and that form part of the sale of the whole or part of a
  business, including a manufacturing or a processing business;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} real property or immovables that have products
  containing PCBs if the products continue to be used after the sale for
  the same purpose at the same place and are an integral part of the
  property or immovable; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} real property or immovables on which a PCB storage
  site is located.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Compliance

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** In addition to the persons
who must comply with the requirements set out in these Regulations, a
person who owns PCBs or products containing PCBs shall ensure that the
requirements of these Regulations with respect to those PCBs or products
are met.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Prohibitions and Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText1} {#h-746964 .Part}

### [Prohibitions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-746965 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release into the environment

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person shall release PCBs into the environment, other than from the
  equipment referred to in subsection (2), in a concentration of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 2 mg/kg or more for a liquid containing PCBs; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 50 mg/kg or more for a solid containing PCBs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Release from equipment

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person shall release more than one gram of PCBs
  into the environment from equipment referred to in section 16 that is
  in use or from equipment in use for which an extension has been
  granted under section 17.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Prohibited activities

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** Except as provided in these
Regulations, no person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} manufacture, export or import PCBs or a product
  containing PCBs in a concentration of 2 mg/kg or more;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} offer for sale or sell PCBs or a product containing
  PCBs in a concentration of 50 mg/kg or more; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} process or use PCBs or a product containing PCBs.

### [Permitted Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-746978 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Laboratory analysis

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A person may manufacture,
export, import, offer for sale, sell, process and use PCBs or products
containing PCBs for the purpose of laboratory analysis if the analysis
is conducted

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} in an authorized facility that is authorized for that
  purpose; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in a facility that conforms to internationally
  recognized guidelines on best laboratory practices, if the authorities
  of the jurisdiction in which the facility is located do not have a
  mechanism in place to authorize the facility to conduct the analysis.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Research

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may process and use the PCBs or products

  paragraph (1)(a) or (b).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electrical capacitor

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** A person may offer for
sale, sell and use an electrical capacitor containing PCBs if the
electrical capacitor

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} is an integral part of a consumer product;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is fusion sealed; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} would be rendered inoperable and irreparable if the
  PCBs were removed from it.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Aircraft, ships, trains and other
vehicles

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** A person may export,
import, offer for sale, sell and use for transportation purposes
aircraft, ships, trains and other vehicles that contain PCBs only in
their communication, navigation or electronic control equipment or
cables.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Colouring pigment

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may manufacture, export, import, offer for sale, sell, process
  and use a colouring pigment containing PCBs produced incidentally if
  the concentration of the PCBs is less than 50 mg/kg.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Annual average concentration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection (1), the annual average
  concentration of PCBs produced incidentally in colouring pigment that
  a person may manufacture, export, import, offer for sale, sell,
  process and use shall not exceed 25 mg/kg.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Cables, pipelines, electrical capacitors
and other equipment

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person may use the following products containing PCBs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} cables, if they remain in place on September 5,
    2008;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pipelines that transport natural gas, petroleum or
    petroleum products and any associated equipment that is in contact
    with the natural gas, petroleum or petroleum products if the
    pipelines and the equipment remain in place on September 5, 2008;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} fusion sealed capacitors if they are used in
    relation to communication equipment or electronic control equipment;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the following equipment containing PCBs in a
    concentration of less than 50 mg/kg if the equipment is used for the
    purpose for which it was manufactured:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} electrical capacitors, light ballasts, electrical
      transformers and their auxiliary electrical equipment, including
      pole-top electrical transformers and their pole-top auxiliary
      electrical equipment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} electromagnets that are not used in the handling
      of food, feed or any additive to food or feed, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} heat transfer equipment, hydraulic equipment,
      vapour diffusion pumps and bridge bearings.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Electrical capacitors

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may import fusion sealed capacitors
  containing PCBs for use in relation to communication tactical
  equipment or electronic control tactical equipment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquids for servicing --- concentration

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may use liquids containing PCBs in a

  until December 31, 2009.

### [End-of-use dates and Extension]{.HTitleText2} {#h-747040 .Subheading}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Equipment --- subparagraphs 14(1)(d)(i)
to (iii)

- **[[16]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-16
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (2) and
  (2.1), a person may use the equipment referred to in

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of equipment containing PCBs in a

      in a child care facility, preschool, primary school, secondary

      property on which the plant or facility is located and within 100
      m of it, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} December 31, 2025, if the equipment is located

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Light ballasts and pole-top electrical

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} A person may use the following equipment containing

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pole-top electrical transformers and their pole-top

  facility and contain PCBs in a concentration of 500 mg/kg or more if
  that equipment is in use on September 5, 2008.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Liquid --- authorized concentration

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person may use a liquid containing PCBs in a
  concentration of 2 mg/kg or more but less than 50 mg/kg in equipment
  until the day on which the liquid is removed from the equipment.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Extension of end-of-use date

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Despite subsection 15(2), paragraph
  16(1)(a) and subparagraph 16(1)(b)(i), a person may use the equipment
  and the liquids used for servicing that equipment, referred to in
  those provisions, until the date set out in an extension granted by
  the Minister under subsection (2) for that equipment and those
  liquids.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall, on receiving a written
  application containing the information set out in subsection (3),
  grant an extension up to the date applied for but no later than
  December 31, 2014, if either of the following conditions are met:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the equipment is being replaced with equipment that
    is engineered to order, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not technically feasible to replace the
      equipment on or before December 31, 2009,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is taking all necessary measures
      to minimize or eliminate any harmful effect of the PCBs in the
      equipment on the environment and on human health,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a plan has been prepared, along with timelines,
      to end the use of the equipment by the date applied for,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a plan has been prepared for inspecting the
      equipment on a monthly basis for the period of the extension for
      damage that could lead to the release of PCBs, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the equipment bears the label required under
      section 29; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the equipment is located at a facility that is
    scheduled for permanent closure on or before December 31, 2014, and

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is taking all necessary measures to
      minimize or eliminate any harmful effect of the PCBs in the
      equipment on the environment and on human health,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a plan has been prepared, along with timelines,
      to end the use of the equipment by the date applied for,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a plan has been prepared for inspecting the
      equipment on a monthly basis, for the period of the extension, for
      damage that could lead to the release of PCBs, and

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the equipment bears the label required under
      section 29.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Information

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application shall contain the following:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, civic and mailing addresses, telephone
    number, fax number, if any, and e-mail address, if any, of the
    applicant and of any person authorized to act on the applicant's
    behalf;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a technical description of the equipment which is
    the subject of the application, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the type and function of the equipment,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of liquid containing PCBs that is
      in the equipment and the quantity of liquid needed for servicing
      that equipment, expressed in litres,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the concentration of PCBs in the liquid,
      expressed in milligrams of PCBs per kilogram of liquid,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of PCBs in the liquid that is in
      the equipment, expressed in kilograms, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the name-plate description, if any, and the
      manufacturer's serial number, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the unique identification number that is on the
    label required under section 29;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name, if any, and civic address of the facility
    where the equipment is located, or, if there is no civic address,
    the location using the owner's site identification system, and the
    function and technical description of the facility;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} it is not technically feasible to replace the
      equipment on or before December 31, 2009, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the facility where the equipment is located is
      scheduled for permanent closure on or before December 31, 2014;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} information demonstrating that the applicant is
    taking all necessary measures to minimize or eliminate any harmful
    effect of the PCBs that are contained in the equipment on the
    environment and on human health;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the plan, along with timelines, for ending the use
    of the equipment; and

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the plan for inspecting the equipment.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Notice of change to information

  [(4)]{.lawlabel} The applicant shall notify the Minister in writing of
  any change to the information provided under subsection (3) within 30
  days after the day on which the change occurs.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}False or misleading information

  [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall refuse to grant an extension if
  the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that the applicant has
  provided false or misleading information in support of its
  application.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Revocation

  [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister shall revoke the extension if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements set out in subsection (2) are no
    longer met during the period of the extension; or

## [PART 3]{.HLabel1}[Storage]{.HTitleText1} {#h-747105 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- concentration of 50 mg/kg
or more

- **[[18]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-18
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), this Part
  applies to all solids and liquids containing PCBs in a concentration
  of 50 mg/kg or more that are located at a particular site if the
  liquids or solids are present in a quantity greater than or equal to

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} 100 L, in the case of liquids; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} 100 kg, in the case of solids.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application --- quantity of 1 kg or
  more

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (3), this Part also applies to
  all solids and liquids containing PCBs in a concentration of 50 mg/kg
  or more that are located at a particular site in a quantity of less
  than 100 L, in the case of liquids, and 100 kg, in the case of solids,
  if the total quantity of PCBs contained in the liquids or the solids
  or in the liquids and solids combined, determined in accordance with
  the following formulas, as applicable, is 1 kg or more:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of liquids,

    ::: {#747114 .Paragraph}
    Σ((V × D × C)) ÷ 1 000 000

    where

    [V]{.dfn}
    :   is the volume of each liquid containing PCBs in a given
        concentration, expressed in L,

    [D]{.dfn}
    :   is the density of PCBs in each liquid as follows:
        - [(i)]{.lawlabel} 0.9 kg/L, if the concentration of PCBs in the
          liquid is less than 10 000 mg/kg, and

        - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} 1.5 kg/L, if the concentration of PCBs in
          the liquid is 10 000 mg/kg or more, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the concentration of PCBs in each liquid, expressed in mg/kg;
        and
    :::

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of solids,

    ::: {#747122 .Paragraph}
    Σ((M × C)) ÷ 1 000 000

    where

    [M]{.dfn}
    :   is the weight of each solid containing PCBs in a given
        concentration, expressed in kg, and

    [C]{.dfn}
    :   is the concentration of PCBs in each solid, expressed in mg/kg.
    :::

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Non-application

  [(3)]{.lawlabel} This Part does not apply in respect of the following
  products containing PCBs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} solid or liquid products that are processed daily
    or used;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pipelines that transport natural gas, petroleum or
    petroleum products, and any associated equipment that is in contact
    with the natural gas, petroleum or petroleum products, if they
    remain in place on September 5, 2008; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} cables, if they remain in place on September 5,
    2008.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}PCB storage site

**[[24]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-24 .sectionLabel}** PCBs or products
containing PCBs shall be stored at a site that is

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a building, room, shipping container or other
  enclosed structure; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an area that is enclosed by a woven mesh wire fence
  or any other fence or wall with similar security characteristics, and
  the fence or wall shall be at least 1.83 m high.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Storage requirements

**[[25]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-25 .sectionLabel}** The owner or operator of
a PCB storage site shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} store all PCBs or products containing PCBs that are
  in liquid form in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} sealed containers, other than drums, that are made
    of steel or other metals that provide sufficient durability and
    strength to prevent those PCBs or products from being affected by
    the weather or released, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} drums that are

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} of a capacity not greater than 205 L,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a closed-head double-bung drum made of steel
      having a gauge of 16 or heavier, and

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} painted or treated to prevent rusting;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} store all PCBs or products containing PCBs that are
  in solid form in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} containers, other than drums, that are made of
    steel or other materials that provide sufficient durability and
    strength to prevent those PCBs or products from being affected by
    the weather or released, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} drums that are

    - [(A)]{.lawlabel} of a capacity not greater than 205 L,

    - [(B)]{.lawlabel} made of steel having a gauge of 18 or heavier,

    - [(C)]{.lawlabel} equipped with a securely attached, removable
      steel lid and a gasket made of material that is resistant to the
      PCBs or the products containing PCBs that are stored in the drums,
      and

    - [(D)]{.lawlabel} painted or treated to prevent rusting;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} store equipment containing PCB liquids in

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} containers, other than drums, that are made of
    steel or other materials that provide sufficient durability and
    strength to prevent the equipment from being affected by the weather
    and to prevent any PCB liquid that leaks from the equipment from
    being released, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} drums described in subparagraph (b)(ii);

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} store all equipment that is not in a container, other
  than drained equipment, if that equipment contains PCB liquid, and all
  containers of PCB liquid, on a floor or surface that is made of steel,
  concrete or any other similar durable material and that is constructed
  with curbing or sides that are capable of containing

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} if one piece of equipment or one container is being
    stored, 125% of the volume of the PCB liquid in the equipment or
    container, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if more than one piece of equipment or more than
    one container is being stored, the greater of twice the volume of
    the PCB liquid in the largest piece of equipment or the largest
    container and 25% of the volume of all the PCB liquid stored on the
    floor or surface;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} if the material of the floor or surface or the
  curbing or sides referred to in paragraph (d) are capable of absorbing
  any PCB liquid or other product containing PCBs, seal the floor,
  surface, curbing or sides with an impervious, durable, PCB-resistant
  coating;

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all floor drains, sumps or other openings
  in the floor or surface referred to in paragraph (d) are

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} closed and sealed to prevent the release of
    liquids, or

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} connected to a drainage system suitable for liquid
    dangerous goods that terminates at a location where any spilled
    liquids will be contained and recovered and where the spilled
    liquids will not create a fire hazard or a risk to public health or
    safety;

- [(g)]{.lawlabel} place on skids or pallets all equipment containing
  PCBs and containers of PCBs or products containing PCBs that are not
  permanently secured to the floor or a surface;

- [(h)]{.lawlabel} stack containers of PCBs and products containing
  PCBs, other than drums, only if the containers are designed for
  stacking, and stack containers of PCB liquid not more than two
  containers high;

- [(i)]{.lawlabel} if drums containing PCBs or products containing PCBs
  are stacked, separate the drums from each other with pallets and, in
  the case of drums of PCB liquid, stack the drums not more than two
  drums high;

- [(j)]{.lawlabel} store equipment containing PCBs, and containers of
  PCBs or products containing PCBs, in a manner that makes them
  accessible for inspection;

- [(k)]{.lawlabel} store PCBs or products containing PCBs in a manner
  that prevents them from catching fire or being released;

- [(l)]{.lawlabel} store PCBs or products containing PCBs together, and
  separate them from other stored materials;

- [(m)]{.lawlabel} if reasonably practicable, equip any indoor PCB
  storage site having a mechanical exhaust system with heat or smoke
  sensory controls that stop the fan and close the intake and exhaust
  dampers in the event of a fire;

- [(n)]{.lawlabel} if equipment or containers of PCB liquid are stored
  outdoors, cover all PCB equipment that is not in a container, other
  than drained equipment, if that equipment contains PCB liquid, and all
  containers of PCB liquid, with a weatherproof roof or barrier that
  protects the equipment and containers and prevents rain or snow from
  entering the curbing and the sides of the floor and the surface under
  them; and

- [(o)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all drained PCB equipment and all
  containers of any PCB solid or PCB equipment are structurally sound
  and weatherproof if stored outdoors.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Access to PCB storage site

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Inspection and maintenance of a PCB
storage site

**[[27]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-27 .sectionLabel}** The owner or operator of
a PCB storage site shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} inspect all floors, curbing, sides, drains, drainage
  systems, weatherproof roofs and barriers, fences and walls of the PCB
  storage site, any fire alarm system, fire extinguishers and fire
  suppression system and all equipment containing PCBs, containers used
  for the storage of PCBs or products containing PCBs and materials for
  clean-up at the PCB storage site

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} each month,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} at intervals of more than one month, if the

    determines that it is not reasonably practicable to inspect the site
    each month, due to its remote location, or

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} at intervals of less than one month, if more
    frequent inspections are necessary for the safe operation of the
    site; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} keep in good condition and, if damaged, immediately
  repair or replace the floors, curbing, sides, drains, drainage
  systems, weatherproof roofs or barriers, fences, walls, fire alarm
  system, fire extinguishers, fire suppression system, equipment
  containing PCBs and containers and immediately clean up any
  contaminated area.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Fire protection and emergency procedures

- **[[28]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-28 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  owner or operator of a PCB storage site shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} develop and implement at the PCB storage site a
    fire protection and emergency procedures plan and shall

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} update and test the plan once per year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} keep a written copy of the latest plan at the
      PCB storage site and another at their principal place of business,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} make the latest plan readily available to
      persons who implement the plan and to the local fire department or
      to the local officer appointed by the provincial Fire Marshall if
      there is no local fire department or to any other local authority
      responsible for fire protection;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all employees who are authorized to
    enter the PCB storage site are familiar with the contents of the
    latest plan;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} equip the indoor PCB storage site with a fully
    operative fire alarm system that is maintained, inspected and tested
    in accordance with articles 6.3.1.1 and 6.3.1.2 of the National Fire
    Code and with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} portable fire extinguishers that are selected and
      installed in accordance with article 2.1.5.1 of the National Fire
      Code and maintained, inspected and tested in accordance with
      article 6.2.1.1 of that Code, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} an automatic fire suppression system that meets
      the requirements of article 3.2.7.9 of the National Fire Code, if
      required;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} keep a copy of the records referred to in sections
    43 and 44 at the PCB storage site and make a copy readily available
    to the local fire department and, if there is no local fire
    department, to the local officer appointed by the provincial Fire
    Marshall or to any other local authority responsible for fire
    protection;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} ensure that all employees who are authorized to
    enter the PCB storage site are made aware of the hazards of PCBs and
    are familiar with the use of protective equipment and clothing and
    the clean-up procedures referred to in the Guidelines for the
    Management of Wastes Containing Polychlorinated Biphenyls (PCBs),
    CCME-TS/WM-TRE008, September 1989, as amended from time to time,
    issued by the Canadian Council of Ministers of the Environment; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} store absorbent materials for clean-up near the PCB
    storage site.

- [Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Shipping containers

  [(2)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (1)(c), if the indoor PCB storage
  site is a shipping container, the owner or operator of the site does
  not have to equip that site with a fire alarm system.
