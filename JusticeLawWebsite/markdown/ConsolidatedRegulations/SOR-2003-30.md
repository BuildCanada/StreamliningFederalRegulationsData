## Consular Fees (Specialized Services) Regulations {#consular-fees-specialized-services-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2003-30

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Consular Fees (Specialized Services) Regulations

P.C. 2003-4  2003-01-23
::::

:::::::::::: section

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to
paragraph 19(1)(a)[[Footnote

Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-30).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704416 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[locally engaged employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as
the definition [[employee]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 2 of the
Locally-Engaged Staff Employment Regulations.

## [Fees]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704418 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 4, the
fee to be paid for a specialized service set out in column 1 of the
schedule that is provided at a mission or outside a mission by a
consular officer or locally engaged employee is the fee set out in
column 2.

## [Exemptions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704423 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** No fee is payable in
respect of a specialized service that is provided

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to a person who is destitute or to whom overriding
  humanitarian or compassionate considerations apply;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to a person who is a Canadian or foreign official, if
  the service is requested in their official capacity;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} for a naval vessel or service aircraft of a
  Commonwealth country;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} for the purposes of the [Citizenship
  Act](/eng/acts/C-29); or

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} for the purpose of the payment of any remuneration,
  pension or allowance by the Government of Canada or the government of
  a province, or for the purposes of any other official business of
  those governments.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-704432 .Part}

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
