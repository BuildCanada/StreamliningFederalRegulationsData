## Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency to the Canada Border Services Agency {#order-transferring-certain-portions-of-the-canada-customs-and-revenue-agency-to-the-canada-border-services-agency .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-216
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2003-12-31

Order Transferring Certain Portions of the Canada Customs and Revenue
Agency to the Canada Border Services Agency

P.C. 2003-2064  2003-12-12
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a) of the [Public Service
Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act](/eng/acts/P-34), hereby
transfers to the Canada Border Services Agency, effective December 12,
2003, the control and supervision of the following portions of the
public service in the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Customs Branch;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Customs Operations Division of the Investigations
  Directorate of the Compliance Programs Branch;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the Customs Appeal Directorate of the Appeals Branch;
  and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} that portion within each of the Agency's regional
  divisions responsible for the administration of the Customs program,
  other than the portions of those regional divisions that relate to
  collections.
