## Precursor Control Regulations {#precursor-control-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2002-359
:::

[CONTROLLED DRUGS AND SUBSTANCES ACT](/eng/acts/C-38.8)

Registration 2002-09-24

Precursor Control Regulations

P.C. 2002-1615 2002-09-24
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas a provision of the annexed Regulations deals with the
communication of information obtained under the Regulations to certain
classes of persons, and in the opinion of the Governor in Council,
pursuant to paragraph 55(1)(s) of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances
Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EBABA}, it is
necessary to communicate the information to those classes of persons for
the proper administration of the Act and the Regulations;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection 55(1)
of the [Controlled Drugs and Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#footnotea_e){#footnotea_e-ID0EDBAA}, hereby makes
the annexed [Precursor Control
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-359).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-695007 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Controlled Drugs and
    Substances Act](/eng/acts/C-38.8). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Class A export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class A export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to
    export one or more Class A precursors, issued under section 33.
    ([permis d'exportation de catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class A import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class A import permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to
    import one or more Class A precursors, issued under section 26.
    ([permis d'importation de catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class A precursor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class A precursor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance set out in Part 1 of Schedule VI to
      the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any preparation or mixture referred to in Part 3
      of Schedule VI to the Act that contains a substance referred to in
      paragraph (a). ([précurseur de catégorie A]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Class B export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class B export permit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a permit to
    export one or more Class B precursors, issued under section 70.
    ([permis d'exportation de catégorie B]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Class B precursor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Class B precursor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any substance set out in Part 2 of Schedule VI to
      the Act; and

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any preparation or mixture referred to in Part 3
      of Schedule VI to the Act that contains a substance referred to in
      paragraph (a). ([précurseur de catégorie B]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[competent authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a public authority
    of a foreign country that is authorized under the laws of the
    country to approve the importation or exportation of precursors into
    or from the country. ([autorité compétente]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[customs officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[customs officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an officer as defined
    in subsection 2(1) of the [Customs Act](/eng/acts/C-52.6). ([agent
    des douanes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated criminal offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any offence involving the financing of terrorism
      against sections 83.02 to 83.04 of the [Criminal
      Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any offence involving fraud against sections 380
      to 382 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the offence of laundering proceeds of crime
      against section 462.31 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an offence involving organized crime against
      sections 467.11 to 467.13 of the [Criminal Code](/eng/acts/C-46);
      or

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a conspiracy or an attempt to commit, being an
      accessory after the fact in relation to, or any counselling in
      relation to, an offence referred to in paragraphs (a) to (e).
      ([infraction désignée en matière criminelle]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[designated drug offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[designated drug offence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an offence against section 4, 5, 6, 19.1 or 19.2

      before May 14, 1997;

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} an offence under Part I of the Act, except

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a conspiracy or an attempt to commit, being an

      lang="fr"})

[[drug in dosage form]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    lang="fr"})

[[extract]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

    ([extraire]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Harmonized System Code]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[international obligation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[licence]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pharmacist]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an individual who

[[prescription]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

[[registered dealer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Class A Precursors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-695066 .Part}

### [Exemption]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695067 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a drug in
dosage form, a person who sells or provides or possesses for the purpose
of sale or provision, or who conducts an activity mentioned in section 9
or 47, is exempt from the requirements of these Regulations in respect
of that activity if the drug contains

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a Class A precursor that is a prescription drug as
  defined in section A.01.010 of the [Food and Drug
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870); or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Class A precursor and one or more controlled
  substances listed in Schedule I, III or IV to the Act.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 2
- SOR/2013-122, s. 19
:::

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a Class A
precursor that is a preparation or mixture, a person who conducts an
activity mentioned in section 6, 9, 10 or 47 is exempt from the
requirements of these Regulations, except requirements in respect of the
production of the preparation or mixture or the possession of a
precursor for the purpose of producing the preparation or mixture, if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the preparation or mixture is a fragrance or
  flavouring

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} containing anthranilic acid, N-anthranilic acid,
    gamma butyrolactone, phenylacetic acid, piperonal or piperidine in a
    total concentration equal to or less than 20% by weight or volume in
    the case of a solid or liquid, respectively, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} intended to be used in a food, drug, cosmetic or
    household product;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} it is a silicone product that is a sealant, adhesive
  or coating containing acetic anhydride in a concentration equal to or
  less than 1% by weight or volume in the case of a solid or liquid,
  respectively; or

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} it contains gamma butyrolactone or 1,4-butanediol in
  a total concentration equal to or less than 20% by weight or volume in
  the case of a solid or liquid respectively, and is intended to be used
  in the following products or processes:

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a control product as defined in the [Pest Control
    Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), R.S., c. P-9, before the coming
    into force of subsection 2(1) of the [Pest Control Products
    Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), S.C. 2002, c. 28,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a pest control product as defined in the [Pest
    Control Products Act](/eng/acts/P-9.01), S.C. 2002, c. 28, after the
    coming into force of subsection 2(1) of that Act,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} cleaning or etching preparations for electronic
    devices, components and parts,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} biofermentation for polyester production,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} melamine coatings,

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} automotive coatings, or

  - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} resin systems for manufacturing polyurethane.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 3
:::

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A person
  who uses a Class A precursor that is described in section 3 or is the
  subject of an authorization certificate under section 49, to produce
  another Class A precursor that is a preparation or mixture, is exempt
  from the requirements of these Regulations in respect of that
  production.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} With respect to a preparation or mixture produced
  under subsection (1), a person who conducts any activity mentioned in
  section 6, 9, 10 or 47 is exempt from the requirements of these
  Regulations in respect of the activity.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 4
:::

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** With respect to any Class A
precursor, a person who only sells, provides or possesses for the
purpose of sale or provision is exempt from the requirements of these
Regulations in respect of the activity if the person

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} sells or provides a selection of goods that is not
  limited to chemicals or chemicals and equipment used in the chemical
  industry for the production, processing or storage of chemicals; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} sells or provides Class A precursors

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} only on a retail basis,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a precursor set out in column 1 of
    the schedule, only in a quantity, per transaction, that does not
    exceed the maximum quantity, expressed as an absolute amount or per
    package, specified for the precursor in column 2 of the schedule,
    and

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a preparation or mixture
    containing a precursor set out in column 1 of the schedule, only in
    a quantity, per transaction, that does not exceed the maximum
    quantity, expressed as an absolute quantity or per package,
    specified for the contained precursor in column 2 of the schedule.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2003-153, s. 1
- SOR/2005-365, s. 4

### [Limitation on Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695101 .Subheading}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person other than a licensed dealer may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} produce a Class A precursor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} package a Class A precursor; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} sell or provide a Class A precursor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No person may possess a Class A precursor for the
  purpose of an activity mentioned in subsection (1), except to the
  extent necessary to conduct the activity in relation to the precursor,
  as authorized by the person's licence.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer may import or export a Class A
  precursor or possess a Class A precursor for the purpose of export if
  the dealer complies with the conditions set out in section 7.

**[[6.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6.1 .sectionLabel}** No person may possess a
Class A precursor for the purpose of producing a controlled substance
unless the person is the holder of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licence issued under section G.02.007 or J.01.015
  of the [Food and Drug Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870),
  section 17.1 of the [Benzodiazepines and Other Targeted Substances
  Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2000-217) or section 10.1 of the
  [Narcotic Control Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1041) that
  authorizes the production of the substance; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} an exemption issued under section 56 of the Act.

### [Conditions Applicable to Licensed Dealers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695114 .Subheading}

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** A licensed dealer may carry
out an activity referred to in section 6 if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer is licensed to carry out the
  activity with respect to the Class A precursor;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the licensed dealer carries out the activity in
  accordance with all conditions set out in the licence;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of import, the licensed dealer is the
  holder of a Class A import permit for the precursor and acts in
  accordance with all conditions set out in the permit; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in the case of export, the licensed dealer is the
  holder of a Class A export permit for the precursor and acts in
  accordance with all conditions set out in the permit.

### [End-use Declaration]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695120 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed dealer who intends to sell or provide, to a person who is not
  a licensed dealer, a Class A precursor set out in column 1 of the
  schedule in a quantity, per transaction, greater than the maximum
  quantity, expressed as an absolute quantity or per package, specified
  for the precursor in column 2 of the schedule shall, before entering
  into the transaction, obtain an end-use declaration signed and dated
  by the person acquiring the precursor.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} The end-use declaration shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensed dealer, being, in the case
    of a corporation, its corporate name, as well as their address and
    telephone number and facsimile transmission number, if any;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person acquiring the precursor,
    being, in the case of a corporation, its corporate name, as well as
    their address and telephone number and facsimile transmission
    number, if any;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of all Class A precursors involved in the
    transactions mentioned in the end-use declaration;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} all uses for which the precursor is being acquired;
    and

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a certification by the signatory stating that the
    person is acquiring the precursor as the end user, for the uses
    mentioned in the end-use declaration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensed dealer intends to sell or provide, to a
  person who is not a licensed dealer, a Class A precursor that is a
  preparation or mixture containing a precursor set out in Part 1 of
  Schedule VI to the Act, an end-use declaration under subsection (1) is
  required if the quantity of the contained precursor, per transaction,
  is greater than the maximum quantity, expressed as an absolute
  quantity or per package, specified for the contained precursor in
  column 2 of the schedule.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} An end-use declaration obtained under subsection (1)
  or (2) applies to any subsequent transaction between the licensed
  dealer and the signatory to the declaration that occurs in the same
  calendar year as the transaction mentioned in the declaration if the
  subsequent transaction involves the same Class A precursor and the
  same end-uses.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} For greater certainty, another end-use declaration
  must be obtained in respect of a subsequent transaction between the
  licensed dealer and the signatory to the declaration that occurs in
  the same calendar year as the transaction mentioned in the declaration
  if the transaction involves a different Class A precursor or a
  different end-use.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer required to obtain an end-use
  declaration must take reasonable steps to verify the identity of the
  signatory to the declaration if that person or their signature is
  unfamiliar to the licensed dealer.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 6
:::

### [Limitation on Transportation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695135 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person may send, transport or deliver a Class A precursor, or possess
  the precursor for such a purpose, except

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a licensed dealer, to the extent necessary to
    conduct an activity permitted by the licence in respect of the
    precursor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} an agent or mandatary of the licensed dealer;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the end user of the precursor; or

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} an agent or mandatary of the end user.

- [(1.1)]{.lawlabel} A person sending, transporting or delivering a
  Class A precursor set out in column 1 of the schedule in a quantity
  greater than the maximum quantity, expressed as an absolute quantity
  or per package, specified for the precursor in column 2 of the
  schedule, shall ensure that the precursor is accompanied by
  documentation indicating

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of the precursor;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name of the licensed dealer selling or
    providing the precursor;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name of the person to whom the precursor is
    being sent, transported or delivered; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the date the precursor was sent.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer must, when transporting an imported
  Class A precursor between the port of entry and the site set out in
  the licence, or when sending, delivering or transporting a Class A
  precursor to a destination, including the port of exit, take all steps
  necessary to ensure the safekeeping of the precursor during
  transportation or ensure that all steps are taken so as to prevent the
  diversion of the precursor to an illicit market or use.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 7
- SOR/2018-69, s. 79(E)

**[[9.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9.1 .sectionLabel}** If a rail car, reusable
container, intermodal tank container or other container, having a
capacity of 9,000 L or more, was used to provide or send red or white
phosphorus and the rail car or container is returned to the person who
produced the phosphorous with residual amounts that cannot be offloaded
under normally accepted operating procedures in the chemical industry,
the person returning the rail car or container in that state is exempt
from the requirements of these Regulations in respect of that activity.

### [Individuals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695161 .Subheading}

- **[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  individual entering or returning to Canada and having in their
  possession a Class A precursor that is a preparation or mixture may
  import the preparation or mixture if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the preparation or mixture is intended to treat a
    medical condition of the individual or an accompanying person for
    whom the individual is responsible;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a preparation or mixture containing
    ephedra, ephedrine or pseudoephedrine, the preparation or mixture is
    packaged and labelled as a consumer product and the total quantity
    imported of the precursor contained in the preparation or mixture
    does not exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in the case of ephedra, 20 g,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of ephedrine, 0.4 g, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in the case of pseudoephedrine, 3 g; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a preparation or mixture containing
    ergometrine or ergotamine, the preparation or mixture is packaged in
    a container with a label showing that it was dispensed under
    prescription in a pharmacy or hospital or by a physician and the
    total quantity imported of the precursor contained in the
    preparation or mixture does not exceed the lesser of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a single prescribed course of treatment, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a 90-day supply, based on the normal daily dose
      for the precursor.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An individual leaving Canada and having in their
  possession a Class A precursor that is a preparation or mixture may
  export the preparation or mixture if the requirements of
  subsection (1) are met, with any modifications that the circumstances
  require.

### [Licence]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695173 .Subheading}

#### [Eligibility for a Licence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-695174 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** To be eligible to apply
for a licence, a person must be

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} an individual who ordinarily resides in Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a corporation that has its head office or a branch
  office in Canada.

#### [Senior Person in Charge and Responsible Person in Charge]{.HTitleText3} {#h-695178 .Subheading}

- **[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed dealer must designate a senior person in charge, who may, if
  appropriate, be the licensed dealer, to have overall responsibility
  for management of the licensed dealer's Class A precursor activities
  at the licensed site.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer must designate a responsible person
  in charge, who may, if appropriate, be the senior person in charge, to
  work at the site set out in the licence and have responsibility for
  supervising, on behalf of the licensed dealer, the licensed dealer's
  Class A precursor activities at the site and for ensuring that the
  activities comply with these Regulations.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A licensed dealer may designate an alternate
  responsible person in charge to work at the site set out in the
  licence and have authority to replace the responsible person in charge
  when that person is absent.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The senior person in charge, responsible person in
  charge and the alternate responsible person in charge

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} must be familiar with the provisions of the Act and
    these Regulations that apply to the licence held by the licensed
    dealer by whom they are designated; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} must not have been convicted, as an adult, within
    the previous 10 years, of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a designated drug offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated criminal offence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an offence committed outside of Canada that, if
      committed in Canada, would have constituted an offence referred to
      in subparagraph (i) or (ii).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The responsible person in charge and the alternate
  responsible person in charge must have sufficient knowledge concerning
  the use and handling of Class A precursors to which the licence
  applies, including the risk of those precursors being diverted to an
  illicit market or use, to enable them to properly perform their
  responsibilities.

#### [Application for a Licence]{.HTitleText3} {#h-695190 .Subheading}

- **[[14]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-14 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} An
  application for a licence or to renew a licence pertaining to a Class
  A precursor shall be made to the Minister and contain the following
  information

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant's name or, if the applicant is a
    corporation, its corporate name, any other registered name with a
    province and any other trade name under which the applicant intends
    to carry out the activities set out in the licence or intends to
    identify itself;

  - [(a.1)]{.lawlabel} a description of the nature of the business
    conducted or intended to be conducted by the applicant;

  - [(a.2)]{.lawlabel} the length of time, if any, the applicant has
    been in business;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} for each Class A precursor sought to be licensed,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} its name, if any, otherwise a description of its
      chemical composition,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} if it is a salt, the name of the salt,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} if it is a preparation or mixture, its brand
      name, if any, and the names of all precursors set out in Part 1 of
      Schedule VI to the Act that it contains;

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if it is a drug containing a precursor for which
      a drug identification number has been assigned under section
      C.01.014.2 of the [Food and Drug
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870), the drug
      identification number, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} if it is a natural health product containing a
      precursor for which a product number has been assigned under
      section 8 of the [Natural Health Products
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196), the product number;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} for each precursor mentioned in the application,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the Class A precursor activity referred to in
      section 6 that is sought to be licensed and that would be
      conducted at the site to which the licence would apply,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the names of the persons from whom the applicant
      intends to obtain the precursor, if applicable, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the type of clientele to whom the applicant
      intends to supply the precursor;

  - [(c.1)]{.lawlabel} if an establishment licence under section
    C.01A.008 of the [Food and Drug
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._870) has been issued to the
    applicant in respect of a Class A precursor, the number of the
    licence;

  - [(c.2)]{.lawlabel} if a site licence under section 29 or 36 of the
    [Natural Health Products Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2003-196)
    has been issued to the applicant in respect of a Class A precursor,
    the number of the licence;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the address, telephone number, facsimile
    transmission number and e-mail address of the site sought to be
    licensed and each building within the site where the activity sought
    to be licensed is to be carried out;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the postal address for the site and each building
    referred to in paragraph (d), if different from the address provided
    under that paragraph;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the name, date of birth, gender, position with the
    applicant, telephone number, facsimile transmission number and
    e-mail address of the senior person in charge for the site;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the name, date of birth and gender of the
    responsible person in charge for the site and the alternate
    responsible person in charge, if any;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a description of the proposed security measures to
    be used at the site and when a precursor is transported, sent or
    delivered, including the measures required under subsection 9(2),
    section 83 and subsection 85(3);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a description of the internal controls proposed
    with respect to precursor activities at the site; and

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} in the case of an application to renew, the number
    of the licence sought to be renewed.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the applicant intends to engage in an activity
  referred to in section 6 respecting Class A precursors at more than
  one site, a separate application must be made for each site.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The application for a licence or its renewal must

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} be signed by the senior person in charge for the
    site mentioned in the application; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} include a statement that

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the information and documents provided in the
      application are correct and complete to the best of the knowledge
      of the signatory, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the signatory has the authority to bind the
      applicant.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An application for a licence or its renewal must be
  accompanied by

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} declarations signed by the senior person in charge,
    the responsible person in charge and the alternate responsible
    person in charge, if any, stating that they have not, as an adult,
    been convicted within the previous 10 years of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a designated drug offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated criminal offence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an offence committed outside of Canada that, if
      committed in Canada, would have constituted an offence referred to
      in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a document issued by a Canadian police force with
    respect to each of the persons referred to in paragraph (a), setting
    out the person's criminal record for the previous 10 years, as an
    adult, in respect of designated drug offences and designated
    criminal offences, or indicating that the person has no such record;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if any of the persons mentioned in paragraph (a)
    has ordinarily resided in a country other than Canada in the
    preceding 10 years, a document issued by a police force of that
    country setting out the person's criminal record for the previous 10
    years, as an adult, in respect of an offence that would have
    constituted a designated criminal offence or a designated drug
    offence if committed in Canada, or indicating that the person has no
    such record; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, a copy of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the certificate of incorporation or other
      constituting instrument for the applicant, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} any document filed with the province in which
      the site sought to be licensed is located that states the
      applicant's corporate name and any other name registered with the
      province under which the applicant will carry out the activities
      set out in the licence or will identify itself.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The documents referred to in paragraphs (4)(b)
  and (c) are not required if the persons referred to in those
  paragraphs consent in writing

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} to having a criminal record check carried out for
    them, as an adult, in respect of the offences mentioned in those
    paragraphs during the preceding 10 years;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} to provide all information and to submit to any
    means of identification required to obtain the criminal record
    check; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} to pay the fee established by the [Royal Canadian
    Mounted Police, Criminal Record Verification for Civil Purposes Fee
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-93-485).

#### [Grounds for Refusal]{.HTitleText3} {#h-695259 .Subheading}

- **[[17]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-17
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (2), the
  Minister shall, in accordance with subsection 84(1), refuse to issue,
  renew or amend a licence if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is not eligible under section 12;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister has reasonable grounds to believe that
    any information or document included in the application is false or
    misleading;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} additional information required under section 15
    has not been provided or is insufficient to enable the Minister to
    process the application;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the issuance, renewal or amendment of the licence
    would permit an activity that would not be in compliance with an
    international obligation of Canada;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} information received from a competent authority,
    the United Nations or a peace officer raises a reasonable belief
    that the applicant has been or will be involved in the diversion of
    a controlled substance or precursor to an illicit market or use;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the applicant does not have in place the security
    measures required under subsection 9(2), section 83 and subsection
    85(3) with respect to the precursors sought to be licensed;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the applicant is not, or within the previous 10
    years has not been, in compliance with

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a provision of the Act or any regulation made or
      continued under the Act, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a term or condition of a licence, registration
      or import or export permit issued to the applicant under any
      regulation made or continued under the Act;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the senior person in charge for the site, the
    responsible person in charge or the alternate responsible person in
    charge, if any, has been convicted, as an adult, within the previous
    10 years, of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a designated drug offence,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a designated criminal offence, or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an offence committed outside of Canada that, if
      committed in Canada, would have constituted an offence referred to
      in subparagraph (i) or (ii);

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the proposed internal controls referred to in
    paragraph 14(1)(i) are not sufficient to

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} support a reliable system of record-keeping in
      respect of Class A precursor activities and Class A precursor
      inventory at the site, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} permit the Minister to audit these precursor
      activities; or

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the issuance, renewal or amendment of the licence
    would likely create a risk to public health, safety or security,
    including the risk of a Class A precursor being diverted to an
    illicit market or use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is not required to refuse to issue,
  renew or amend a licence under paragraph (1)(b) or (g) if the
  applicant

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} does not have a history of non-compliance with the
    Act and any regulation made or continued under the Act; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has carried out, or signed an undertaking to carry
    out, specified corrective measures to ensure compliance with the Act
    and these Regulations.

    carrying out the destruction and each person mentioned in
    paragraph (d) sign and date a joint declaration certifying that the
    precursor was completely destroyed, to which each signatory shall
    add their name in printed letters.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A person is qualified to witness the destruction of a
  Class A precursor if

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person is the responsible person in charge for
    the site or, if applicable, the alternate responsible person in
    charge for the site set out in the licence; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the person works for or provides services to the
    licensed dealer and acts in a senior position.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a Class A precursor is to be destroyed elsewhere
  than at the licensed site, the licensed dealer must ensure that

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} appropriate measures are taken to ensure security
    of the precursor during transit in order to prevent the diversion of
    a Class A precursor to an illicit market or use;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the destruction is carried out by a business that
    specializes in the destruction of dangerous goods;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the destruction is conducted in accordance with a
    method that complies with all federal, provincial and municipal
    environmental protection legislation applicable to the place of
    destruction; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} once the destruction is completed, the individual
    within the business carrying out the destruction provides the
    licensed dealer with a declaration attesting to the complete
    destruction of the precursor, showing

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name of the precursor, if any, otherwise a
      description of its chemical composition, and

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the precursor destroyed and, in
      the case of a preparation or mixture, the quantity of all
      precursors set out in Part 1 of Schedule VI to the Act that it
      contains;

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of destruction,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the method of destruction, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} their name and signature as well as the name and
      signature of another person within the business who witnessed the
      destruction.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Class B Precursors]{.HTitleText1} {#h-695680 .Part}

### [Exemption]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695681 .Subheading}

**[[55]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-55 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a Class B
precursor that is a preparation or mixture, a person who conducts an
activity mentioned in section 57 is exempt from the requirements of
these Regulations, except requirements applying to production for the
purpose of sale or provision, if the preparation or mixture contains a
precursor set out in Part 2 of Schedule VI to the Act and the contained
precursor, either alone or with any other precursor of the same type,
does not constitute more than 30% of the preparation or mixture by
weight or volume, in the case of a solid or liquid, respectively.

B precursor that is described in section 55 or is the subject of an
authorization certificate under section 77, to produce another Class B
precursor that is a preparation or mixture, is exempt from the
requirements of these Regulations in respect of that production.

### [Limitation on Activities]{.HTitleText2} {#h-695688 .Subheading}

- **[[57]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-57 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} No
  person other than a registered dealer may produce a Class B precursor
  for the purpose of sale or provision.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A registered dealer may import a Class B precursor.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsection (4), a registered dealer may
  export a Class B precursor and may possess the precursor for the
  purpose of the export.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} In the case of a country named in a table referred to
  in paragraph (a) or (b) and a Class B precursor identified in the
  table as one requiring notice of pre-exportation to that country, a
  registered dealer may only export the Class B precursor to the country
  if the registered dealer holds a Class B export permit in respect of
  the precursor and complies with the terms and conditions of the
  permit:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the table entitled "Governments that have requested
    pre-export notifications pursuant to article 12, paragraph 10(a), of
    the 1988 Convention", contained in the most recent INCB annual
    report concerning the implementation of article 12 of the United
    Nations Convention against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and
    Psychotropic Substances of 1988; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the table entitled Supplementary Table of Countries
    Requiring Pre-Export Notification With Regard to Class B Precursors,
    published by the Department of Health, as amended from time to time,
    listing the countries that, according to notification from the INCB
    to the Minister since publication of the most recent annual report
    referred to in paragraph (a), have made a formal request to the
    United Nations to receive notice of pre-exportation in respect of
    the shipment to the country of Class B precursors.

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} an offence committed outside of Canada that, if

      in subparagraph (i) or (ii).

### [Suspicious Transactions]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696023 .Subheading}

- **[[86]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-86 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  licensed dealer and a registered dealer shall, on becoming aware of
  the transaction, make a record of every transaction occurring in the
  course of their activities in respect of which there are reasonable
  grounds to suspect that the transaction is related to the diversion of
  a precursor to an illicit market or use.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} For the purpose of subsection (1), the factors to be
  considered in assessing if a transaction or group of transactions give
  rise to reasonable grounds to suspect that the transaction or group of
  transactions is related to the diversion of a precursor to an illicit
  market or use include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the composition and chemical properties of the
    precursor involved, the illicit use that may be made of it and the
    risk of its diversion to an illicit market or use having regard to
    those factors;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the precursor involved and, in the
    case of a preparation or mixture, the quantity of all precursors set
    out in Parts 1 or 2 of Schedule VI to the Act that it contains;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the intended use of the precursor stated by the
    other party to the transaction;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the proposed means of transportation, route of
    delivery, place of origin or place of destination;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the method of payment involved; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} in the case of prior dealings between the dealer
    and the other party to the transaction, any suspicious departure
    from the pattern of the prior dealings.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A suspicious transaction record shall include

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name, address, telephone number and position
    with the dealer of the individual making the record;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the other party to the
    transaction;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} details of the transaction involved, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the date and time of the transaction,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the type of transaction, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of the precursor and, in
      the case of a preparation or mixture, the quantity of all
      precursors set out in Parts 1 or 2 of Schedule VI to the Act that
      it contains; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a detailed description of the reasons for
    suspecting that the transaction is related to the diversion of a
    precursor to an illicit market or use.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} No licensed dealer or registered dealer shall
  disclose that they have made a suspicious transaction record under
  this section or disclose the contents of the record, with the intent
  to prejudice a criminal investigation, whether or not a criminal
  investigation has begun.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} No criminal or civil proceedings lie against a
  licensed dealer or registered dealer for making a suspicious
  transaction record under this section in good faith.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister is authorized to receive information
  that is provided voluntarily by a dealer referred to in subsection (1)
  in respect of a transaction mentioned in that subsection.

  activities during the calendar year.

Minister in writing of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date of the intended closure or removal;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the site to which the precursors will be moved; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of each precursor to be moved.

- SOR/2005-365, s. 54

### [Security and Reporting of Loss or Theft]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696076 .Subheading}

- **[[90]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-90 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  holder of a licence, a registration or authorization certificate or an
  import or export permit issued or granted under these Regulations
  shall maintain measures necessary to ensure the security of any
  precursor in their possession, as well as the licence, certificate or
  permit.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If a licensed dealer or registered dealer experiences
  a theft of a precursor or an unusual waste or disappearance of a

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} shall provide notice of the occurrence to a member

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} shall provide notice of the occurrence to the

    has been provided.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If a licence, a registration or authorization
  certificate, an import or export permit or a permit for transit or
  transhipment issued under these Regulations is lost or stolen, the
  holder of the document shall provide notice of the occurrence to the
  Minister, in writing, within 24 hours after becoming aware of the
  occurrence.

## [PART 4]{.HLabel1}[Pharmacists, Practitioners and Hospitals]{.HTitleText1} {#h-696141 .Part}

### [Non-application]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696142 .Subheading}

**[[91.1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.1 .sectionLabel}** With respect to a
Class A precursor, the requirements of paragraph 6(1)(c) and subsections
6(2) and 9(1) concerning sale or provision, possession for the purpose
of sale or provision and transport do not apply to a pharmacist,
practitioner or hospital that sells or provides preparations or mixtures
containing Class A precursors only on a retail basis.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

### [Pharmacists]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696146 .Subheading}

#### [Activities Authorized Under a Prescription]{.HTitleText3} {#h-696147 .Subheading}

**[[91.2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.2 .sectionLabel}** A pharmacist may,
pursuant to a prescription, compound a preparation or mixture using a
precursor.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

- **[[91.3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pharmacist may sell or provide a preparation or mixture that contains
  a Class A precursor set out in column 1 of the schedule, in a
  quantity, per transaction, exceeding the maximum quantity, expressed
  as an absolute quantity or per package, specified for the contained
  precursor in column 2 of the schedule, if the pharmacist sells or
  provides preparations or mixtures containing Class A precursors
  exclusively on a retail basis and the transaction is made under a
  prescription.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The prescription must be

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} written, dated and signed by the practitioner who
    issued it;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provided verbally by a practitioner; or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} transferred to the pharmacist in accordance with
    the requirements of section 91.7.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

#### [Information to Be Recorded]{.HTitleText3} {#h-696159 .Subheading}

- **[[91.4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pharmacist who receives a verbal prescription for a preparation or
  mixture containing a Class A precursor must, before filling it, record
  the following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the individual or owner of
    the animal for whose benefit the prescription is provided;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the prescription was provided;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of the preparation or mixture
    specified in the prescription;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the name of the recording pharmacist and the name
    of the practitioner who issued the prescription;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the directions for use specified by the
    practitioner; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} if the prescription is to be refilled, the number
    of times it may be refilled and, if applicable, the interval between
    refills.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The pharmacist must retain, in accordance with
  section 91.95, a hard copy of the prescription or a written record of
  it.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

- **[[91.5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.5
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject to section 91.6, a
  pharmacist who fills a written or verbal prescription for a
  preparation or mixture containing a Class A precursor must record the
  following information:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the date the prescription was filled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the quantity of the preparation or mixture provided
    when the prescription was filled;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacist's name or initials; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number assigned to the prescription.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A pharmacist who fills a written prescription shall
  retain the prescription in accordance with section 91.95.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

#### [Refill of Prescription]{.HTitleText3} {#h-696180 .Subheading}

**[[91.6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.6 .sectionLabel}** A pharmacist may only
refill a prescription for a preparation or mixture containing a Class A
precursor if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the practitioner who issued the prescription
  expressly directs that it may be refilled and specifies the number of
  refills;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the pharmacist makes a record of each refill in
  accordance with subsection 91.5(1), with any modifications that the
  circumstances require;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} at least one refill remains on the prescription; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} if an interval between refills has been specified by
  the practitioner, the interval has expired.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

#### [Transfer of Prescription]{.HTitleText3} {#h-696188 .Subheading}

- **[[91.7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  pharmacist may transfer a prescription for a preparation or mixture
  containing a Class A precursor to another pharmacist or to a hospital.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Before a pharmacist sells or provides a preparation
  or mixture containing a Class A precursor to an individual under a
  prescription that is transferred under subsection (1), the pharmacist
  shall

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a verbal transfer, record the
    information required by subsection 91.4(1);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a written transfer, obtain from the
    transferring pharmacist a copy of

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the prescription written by the practitioner, or

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the written record, made in accordance with
      subsection 91.4(2), of the practitioner's verbal prescription; and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in all cases, record

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the transferring
      pharmacist,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the number of authorized refills remaining and,
      if applicable, the specified interval between refills, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the date of the last refill.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

### [Practitioners]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696202 .Subheading}

**[[91.8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.8 .sectionLabel}** A practitioner may,
with respect to a preparation or mixture containing a Class A precursor,
prescribe it for or administer it to an individual or animal, or sell or
provide it to or for an individual or for the benefit of an animal, only
if

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the individual or animal is being treated by the
  practitioner in their professional capacity; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the preparation or mixture is required to treat the
  individual's or animal's medical condition.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

**[[91.9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.9 .sectionLabel}** A practitioner who
sells or provides a preparation or mixture containing a Class A
precursor set out in column 1 of the schedule to an individual for
self-administration or administration to an animal in a quantity that
exceeds the maximum quantity, expressed as an absolute quantity or per
package, specified for the contained precursor in column 2 of the
schedule, may do so only if the transaction is made under a prescription
and shall make a record of the following information:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of the preparation or mixture;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person to whom the
  preparation or mixture was provided; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the preparation or mixture was
  provided.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

### [Hospitals]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696214 .Subheading}

**[[91.91]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.91 .sectionLabel}** A hospital that
sells or provides a preparation or mixture containing a Class A
precursor exclusively on a retail basis may do so subject to sections
91.92 to 91.94.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

- **[[91.92]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.92
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of a hospital
  shall not permit a preparation or mixture containing a Class A
  precursor set out in column 1 of the schedule to be sold or provided
  to a patient or for the benefit of an animal under treatment as an
  in-patient or an out-patient of the hospital in a quantity, per
  transaction, that exceeds the maximum quantity, expressed as an
  absolute quantity or per package, specified for the contained
  precursor in column 2 of the schedule, unless the transaction is made
  under a prescription or another authorization of a practitioner
  practising in the hospital.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The person in charge of the hospital shall keep the
  following information or ensure that it is kept:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the name and quantity of the preparation or mixture
    provided when the prescription was filled;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the name and address of the person for whom the
    prescription was issued;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the date on which the prescription was filled; and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the number assigned to the prescription.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

**[[91.93]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.93 .sectionLabel}** The person in
charge of a hospital shall not permit a prescription for a preparation
or mixture containing a Class A precursor to be refilled at the hospital
unless the requirements of section 91.6 are satisfied, with any
modifications that the circumstances require.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

**[[91.94]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.94 .sectionLabel}** The person in
charge of a hospital shall not permit a preparation or mixture
containing a Class A precursor to be sold or provided pursuant to a
prescription that has been transferred to the hospital, unless the
requirements of subsection 91.7(2) are satisfied, with any modifications
that the circumstances require.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

**[[91.95]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.95 .sectionLabel}** Information to be
recorded and records to be kept under this Part shall be retained for at
least two years after the date the information was obtained or the
record was made.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
:::

### [Security Measures and Communication of Information]{.HTitleText2} {#h-696236 .Subheading}

**[[91.96]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-91.96 .sectionLabel}** A pharmacist,
practitioner or hospital that sells or provides only on a retail basis,
or possesses for the purpose of sale or provision, preparations or
mixtures containing Class A precursors, shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} take reasonable steps to ensure the security of the
  Class A precursors;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on discovering an unusual waste or disappearance of a
  Class A precursor that cannot be explained on the basis of normally
  accepted business practices, notify

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a member of a police force within 24 hours after
    the discovery, and

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the Minister, in writing, within 72 hours after
    making the discovery, and confirm that the notice required under
    subparagraph (i) has been provided;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on discovering a theft of a Class A precursor set out
  in column 1 of the schedule, if the package in which it is contained
  is not intended for retail sale and contains a quantity of the
  precursor exceeding the maximum quantity specified for the precursor
  in column 2 of the schedule, notify the persons mentioned in
  subparagraphs (b)(i) and (ii) within the applicable time-periods; and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on request, provide to the Minister or make available
  for examination by the Minister, any information or record required to
  be kept or made under this Part.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2005-365, s. 58
