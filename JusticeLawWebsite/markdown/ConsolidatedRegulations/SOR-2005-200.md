## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716974 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The following definitions
apply in these Regulations.

[[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[common-law partner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in relation to an
    individual, means a person who is cohabiting with the individual in
    a conjugal relationship at the time of the individual's death,
    having so cohabited for a period of at least one year. ([conjoint de
    fait]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the corporation
    incorporated under the name Last Post Fund, on June 22, 1921, under
    the Companies Act. ([société]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Department]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Department of Veterans
    Affairs. ([ministère]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependent child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependent child]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a dependent child as
    defined in subsection 2(1) of the [War Veterans Allowance
    Act](/eng/acts/W-3). ([enfant à charge]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to a disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in
    respect of a person, that the person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has received a [[disability
      award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of the
      [Veterans Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} has not received a [[disability
      award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in subsection 2(1) of
      that Act because one of the conditions set out in section 53 of
      that Act, as it read immediately before April 1, 2019, had not
      been met as of that day, unless a decision regarding the
      application for pain and suffering compensation referred to in
      subsection 174(1) of that Act has been made; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} but for subsection 54(1) of that Act as it read
      immediately before April 1, 2019, would have received a
      [[disability award]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} as defined in
      subsection 2(1) of that Act. ([droit à une indemnité
      d'invalidité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to a pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to a pension]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}, in respect of a
    deceased person, means that, at the time of death, the person was
    receiving a pension under the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6) or had
    received a final payment of a pension, or that the Minister had
    determined that the person was entitled to a pension. ([droit à une
    pension]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[entitled to pain and suffering compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[entitled to pain and suffering
    compensation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, in respect of a person,
    that the person

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} has received pain and suffering compensation
      under subsection 45(1) or 47(1) of the [Veterans Well-being
      Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8);

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} is referred to in subsection 45(1) of that Act
      but whose extent of disability has not yet been assessed; or

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} but for subsection 56.4(1) of that Act, would
      have received pain and suffering compensation under subsection
      45(1) or 47(1) of that Act. ([droit à une indemnité pour douleur
      et souffrance]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[income-qualified civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[income-qualified civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a civilian
    as defined in subsection 56(1) of the [Civilian War-related Benefits
    Act](/eng/acts/C-31) who

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} was at the time of death in receipt of an
      allowance under subsection 57(1) of that Act; or

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} would have been eligible at the time of death for
      an allowance under that subsection were it not for payments made
      to the civilian or the civilian's spouse or common-law partner, or
      for which the civilian or the civilian's spouse or common-law
      partner was eligible under the [Old Age Security
      Act](/eng/acts/O-9) or similar legislation of another
      country. ([civil au revenu admissible]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Veterans
    Affairs. ([ministre]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net assets of the succession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net assets of the succession]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the net
    value of the portion of succession to a deceased person's estate
    that, in accordance with the laws of the country or the province in
    which the person was domiciled at the time of death, can be
    transferred to the heirs by way of bequest or inheritance after the
    payment of all debts owed by the person or the estate, including the
    costs of the following: the deceased person's last sickness,
    funeral, burial, cremation and gravemarker. ([actif net
    successoral]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[net assets of the survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[net assets of the survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the net
    worth of the survivor. ([actif net du survivant]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[overseas service civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[overseas service civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person
    referred to in paragraphs (e) to (i) of the definition
    [[civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in subsection 56(1) of the
    [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31). ([civil ayant
    servi outre-mer]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pensioned condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pensioned condition]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a disability in
    respect of which a person is entitled to a pension. ([état
    indemnisé]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who at the time of
    death was entitled to a pension in respect of a disability payable
    under, or because of,

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), including a
      supplementary pension under section 64, 65 or 66 of that Act, but
      not including a pension that is awarded under subsection 22(2) of
      that Act or continued under subsection 83(4) of that Act;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} any of Parts I to III and VI to X of the
      [Civilian War-related Benefits Act](/eng/acts/C-31);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} paragraph 3(c) of the Special Operators War
      Service Benefits Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 256;

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} paragraphs 3(d) and (e) of the [Women's Royal
      Naval Services and the South African Military Nursing Service
      (Benefits) Act](/eng/acts/W-11.4), R.S.C. 1952, c. 297;

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} Veterans Affairs Vote 815 of Schedule C to The
      Appropriation Act, No. 4, 1950, S.C. 1950, c. 55 or any other
      provision enacted by Parliament for the continuation of certain
      Newfoundland pensions that were not payable under the terms of the
      union of Canada and Newfoundland; or

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the Civilian Government Employees (War)
      Compensation Order. ([pensionné]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[survivor]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a survivor as defined in
    subsection 2(1) of the [War Veterans Allowance
    Act](/eng/acts/W-3). ([survivant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means one of the following persons:

    - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a former member of the Canadian Forces or any
      predecessor naval, army or air forces of Canada or Newfoundland;

    - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian merchant mariner of World War I or
      World War II within the meaning of subsection 21.1(3) or (4),
      respectively, of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6);

    - [(c)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-74, s. 1\]]{.Repealed}

    - [(d)]{.lawlabel} a Canadian merchant mariner of the Korean War
      within the meaning of subsection 21.1(5) of the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6);

    - [(e)]{.lawlabel} a special operator who is deemed to be a veteran
      under paragraph 3(a) of the Special Operators War Service Benefits
      Act, R.S.C. 1952, c. 256;

    - [(f)]{.lawlabel} a supervisor who is deemed to be a veteran under
      paragraph 3(a) of the Supervisors War Service Benefits Act, R.S.C.
      1952, c. 258;

    - [(g)]{.lawlabel} a person who is deemed to be a veteran under
      section 3 of the [Women's Royal Naval Services and the South
      African Military Nursing Service (Benefits)
      Act](/eng/acts/W-11.4), R.S.C. 1952, c. 297;

    - [(h)]{.lawlabel} a person on whom the benefits of the [Pension
      Act](/eng/acts/P-6) may be conferred under section 64, 65 or 66 of
      that Act;

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a person who, as a member of His Majesty's naval,
      army or air forces or of similar forces allied with His Majesty,
      served on active service during World War I or World War II and
      was resident in Canada at the time he or she became a member of
      those forces; or

    - [(j)]{.lawlabel} an allied veteran within the meaning of
      subsection 37(4), (4.1) or (4.2) of the [War Veterans Allowance
      Act](/eng/acts/W-3). ([ancien combattant]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

[[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[World War I]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6). ([Première
    Guerre mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[World War II]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the meaning assigned in
    subsection 3(1) of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6). ([Seconde
    Guerre mondiale]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [PART 1]{.HLabel1}[Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717016 .Part}

### [Eligibility for Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717017 .Subheading}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Financial assistance
towards last sickness, funeral, burial and cremation costs in accordance
with section 3 is payable

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} if there is an insufficiency of funds, as determined
  under section 4, for the following deceased persons, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pensioner who dies in Canada or elsewhere,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a veteran or an income-qualified civilian or an
    overseas service civilian who dies in Canada,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a veteran who is buried or cremated in Canada,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a veteran referred to in any of paragraphs (a)
    to (g) of the definition [[veteran]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    section 1 who dies outside Canada,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an overseas service civilian who dies outside
    Canada, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a veteran or a civilian who dies outside Canada
    and on the date of death was undergoing a medical examination
    required by the Minister or by the Veterans Review and Appeal Board;

  - [(vii) and (viii)]{.lawlabel} [\[Repealed, SOR/2014-74, s.
    2\]]{.Repealed}

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Canada or elsewhere, for the following deceased
  persons, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a pensioner, referred to in any of paragraphs (b)
    to (f) of the definition of [[pensioner]{.dfn}]{.DefinitionRef} in
    section 1, whose death was as a result of an injury or disease or an
    aggravation of an injury or disease that was attributable to or was
    incurred during the pensioner's service,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a person in respect of whom a pension is awarded
    in accordance with paragraph 21(1)(b) or (2)(b) of the [Pension
    Act](/eng/acts/P-6),

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} a pensioner who on the date of death was
    receiving treatment benefits under Part I of the [Veterans Health
    Care Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-594) in respect of a
    period of acute care for a pensioned condition,

  - [(iii.1)]{.lawlabel} a person who, on the date of death, was
    receiving treatment benefits under Part I of the [Veterans Health
    Care Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-594) in respect of a
    period of acute care for a pensioned condition or for an injury or
    disease in respect of which they are entitled to a disability award
    or entitled to pain and suffering compensation,

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a person who, on the date of death, was undergoing
    a medical examination required by the Minister or by the Veterans
    Review and Appeal Board,

  - [(v)]{.lawlabel} a pensioner who on the date of death was in receipt
    of treatment benefits under Part I of the [Veterans Health Care
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-90-594) in respect of
    hospitalization for a condition in respect of which it is uncertain
    whether the condition was a pensioned condition, or

  - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} a person who dies as a result of a service-related
    injury or disease or a non-service-related injury or disease that
    was aggravated by service in accordance with the [Veterans
    Well-being Act](/eng/acts/C-16.8).

### [Amounts of Financial Assistance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717037 .Subheading}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3
  .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Financial assistance is payable for
  the following costs:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} any medical costs of the deceased person's last
    sickness that were not otherwise paid by the Minister and that are
    not insured under a private or provincial health care plan;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the costs of the following funeral services:

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} preparation of the remains,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} provision of a casket that meets the standards
      specified by the Minister or provision of an urn,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} provision of ceremonial services,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} provision of death notices,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} transportation of the remains from the place
      where the death occurred to a funeral home and to a place of
      burial or cremation, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} transportation of the remains that requires the
      services of two funeral homes because the funeral home or place of
      burial or cremation are not in the same locality as the place
      where the death occurred;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} if necessary, the cost of any special preparation
    of the remains for viewing;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} if required, the cost of providing a single grave
    and reasonable costs of opening and closing the grave and its
    perpetual care;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} if a grave liner is required by the authorities of
    the cemetery, municipality or province, the cost of a decomposable
    or non-decomposable grave liner; and

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the cost of cremating the remains.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The amount of financial assistance is the least of

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the amounts for the costs
    described in subsection (1), adding any goods and services tax,
    harmonized sales tax or provincial sales tax. In this aggregate
    amount the costs, before tax, do not exceed

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the costs referred to in
      paragraph (1)(a), \$75,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the costs referred to in
      subparagraphs (1)(b)(i) to (v), \$7,376,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the cost referred to in
      subparagraph (1)(b)(vi), \$500,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the cost of any special
      preparation of the remains under paragraph (1)(c), \$210,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the cost of a decomposable grave
      liner under paragraph (1)(e), \$200, and

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the cost of a non-decomposable
      grave liner under paragraph (1)(e), \$570;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the cases described in paragraph 2(a), the
    amount of any insufficiency of funds, determined under section 4.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Despite paragraph (2)(a), an additional amount may be
  paid to cover the difference between the cost of a normal casket and
  the cost of an oversized casket or a hermetically sealed one.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} Financial assistance is payable only if no similar
  payment has been made by the Government of Canada in respect of the
  same deceased person.

### [Determination of Insufficiency of Funds]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717065 .Subheading}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  determination in respect of an insufficiency of funds to meet costs,
  for the purpose of the provision of financial assistance, must be made
  by the Corporation as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} if the deceased person has a survivor or a
    dependent child, the following amounts are deducted from the sum of
    the net assets of the survivor and the net assets of the succession,
    namely,

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} \$35,279 for the survivor or, if there is no
      survivor, for the dependent child,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} \$700 for each additional dependent child,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the aggregate of the amounts under paragraphs
      3(2)(a) minus any amount already paid for the costs set out in
      subsection 3(1), if these amounts have not already been considered
      in the determination of the net assets of the succession,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} if a gravemarker is not provided under section
      5,

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} the lesser of the actual cost of a gravemarker
        and the value of the gravemarker that would have been provided
        under that section minus the amount already paid for the
        gravemarker, if this amount has not already been considered in
        the determination of the net assets of the succession, and

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} a reasonable amount for the perpetual care of
        the gravemarker, if this amount has not already been considered
        in the determination of the net assets of the succession,

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the person's monthly income
      before their death,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, an amount equal to the market
      value of a motor vehicle that is ordinarily used by the deceased
      person's family as a mode of transportation and that is chosen by
      the person making the application for financial assistance,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} if applicable, an amount equal to the market
      value of the residence where the deceased person's family lives,
      as chosen by the person making the application for financial
      assistance, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} an amount equal to the market value of the
      household effects of the deceased person's family; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the deceased person does not have a survivor or
    dependent children, the deductible amounts described in
    paragraph (a) do not apply, and the net assets of the succession as
    a whole are to be taken into consideration.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the amount determined under paragraph (1)(a)
  or (b) is nil or negative, there is an insufficiency of funds.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The amount set out in subparagraph 4(1)(a)(i) is to
  be adjusted on January 1 of each year in accordance with the
  percentage increase to the Consumer Price Index for the year ending on
  September 30 of the previous year.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The Consumer Price Index is the average all-items
  Consumer Price Index for Canada (not seasonally adjusted) published by
  Statistics Canada.

### [Gravemarkers]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717085 .Subheading}

- **[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If
  neither a foreign government, nor the Commonwealth War Graves
  Commission nor any other agency provides a gravemarker, a gravemarker
  must, subject to subsection (2), be provided and installed for the
  following persons:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in Canada or elsewhere, if there is an
    insufficiency of funds, as determined under section 4, on
    application, a person described in any of subparagraphs 2(a)(i)
    or (iv) to (vi);

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in Canada, if there is an insufficiency of funds,
    as determined under section 4, on application, a person described in
    subparagraph 2(a)(ii) or (iii); and

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} in Canada or elsewhere, a person described in
    paragraph 2(b).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The provision of a gravemarker under these
  Regulations is subject to the condition that the gravemarker must not
  be altered or replaced without the prior consent of the Minister.

gravemarkers provided under these Regulations.

## [PART 2]{.HLabel1}[Administration]{.HTitleText1} {#h-717099 .Part}

### [Applications]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717100 .Subheading}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Any
  person may apply for a gravemarker and for financial assistance for
  last sickness, funeral, burial and cremation costs on behalf of a
  deceased person.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} No financial assistance is payable unless an
  application for it is made within one year after the date that the
  remains of the deceased person become available for interment.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} If an application for financial assistance is
  received but cannot be acted on as a result of a pending decision as
  to whether the person is entitled to a disability award or entitled to
  pain and suffering compensation or as to whether a pension may be
  awarded under paragraph 21(1)(b) or (2)(b) of the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6), the application is suspended until a decision has
  been rendered.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} If a person is determined to be entitled to a
  disability award or entitled to pain and suffering compensation or a
  decision is made to award a pension under paragraph 21(1)(b) or (2)(b)
  of the [Pension Act](/eng/acts/P-6), the application for financial
  assistance must be acted on as if the initial decision with respect to
  eligibility under subparagraph 2(b)(i), (ii) or (vi) had been
  favourable.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} If more than one person applies for financial
  assistance in respect of the same death, the order of priority for an
  application, to the exclusion of all others, is as follows:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the liquidator of the succession of the deceased;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the survivor of the deceased;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the person who is best able to ensure the burial;
    and

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the person who lives closest to the place of burial
    of the deceased.

### [Agreement with the Last Post Fund]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717113 .Subheading}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} For the
  purpose of facilitating the provision of financial assistance and
  gravemarkers under Part 1, as well as the administration of these
  Regulations, the Minister may enter into an agreement with the
  Corporation by which

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the Corporation determines the eligibility for and
    provides, on behalf of the Government of Canada, that assistance and
    gravemarkers in accordance with these Regulations; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Minister reimburses the Corporation for any
    costs incurred under paragraph (a), by Parliamentary appropriation.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} An agreement must address

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the requirements for an application for financial
    assistance and gravemarkers under Part 1 and the procedure by which
    the application may be received by the Corporation, including any
    declarations and undertakings that the Corporation may require the
    applicant to make;

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the regular channels of communication to be used
    between the Corporation and the Department;

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} the procedure to be followed for obtaining or
    verifying, from the records of the Department, relevant information
    concerning a person's eligibility for financial assistance and
    gravemarkers under Part 1 or any other information necessary for the
    administration of these Regulations;

  - [(d)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed by the Corporation
    when making determinations concerning a person's eligibility under
    paragraph 2(a), including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the identification of the representatives of the
      Corporation duly authorized to render those determinations,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of any determination to the
      applicant, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of a notice to the applicant
      respecting any internal appeal process established by the
      Corporation;

  - [(e)]{.lawlabel} the procedures to be followed by the Corporation in
    respect of applications under paragraph 2(b), including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} referrals to the Department,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the provision of any determination to the
      applicant, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the provision to the applicant of any review of
      a determination;

  - [(f)]{.lawlabel} the documentation and records to be kept by the
    Corporation in respect of applications, and the transfer of those
    documents and records to the Department;

  - [(g)]{.lawlabel} the determination and reimbursement to be made of
    the Corporation's administrative and operating expenses by
    Parliamentary appropriation;

  - [(h)]{.lawlabel} the financial procedures to be used for reimbursing
    the Corporation, including

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the determination of the amounts and frequency of
      reimbursements to the Corporation, including refunds for
      expenditures made in a previous fiscal year,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} the statistical and accounting information on
      which the reimbursements are to be based and the manner, form and
      frequency with which the Corporation must report that information,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} the manner in which deductions may be made from
      reimbursements to the Corporation in respect of overpayments of
      financial assistance that are being recovered by the Corporation,

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the conditions and manner in which
      reimbursements to the Corporation may be made in advance, and

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the standards by which the estimates and the
      budget of the Corporation must be prepared and its accounts must
      be kept;

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the business plans, operating budgets, capital
    budgets and performance reports to be submitted by the Corporation
    to the Minister;

  - [(j)]{.lawlabel} the audit of the Corporation's accounts, the access
    that auditors and evaluators may have to the Corporation's records
    relating to its functions under the agreement and the evaluation of
    the Corporation's performance in light of the agreement;

  - [(k)]{.lawlabel} the safeguarding of any personal information as
    defined in the [Privacy Act](/eng/acts/P-21) that may be collected
    by the Department or the Corporation for the purposes of these
    Regulations;

  - [(l)]{.lawlabel} the exercise by the Corporation of the rights or
    powers that the Minister has in respect of burial in any cemeteries
    and burial plots owned or controlled by the Government of Canada;

  - [(m)]{.lawlabel} the assumption or exclusion of any obligations or
    liabilities that might be incurred by the Corporation in connection
    with any function or procedure under the agreement;

  - [(n)]{.lawlabel} the notice to be given and the formalities required
    for a termination of the agreement; and

  - [(o)]{.lawlabel} the specification of which law governs the
    agreement and its interpretation.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The Minister may

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} terminate an agreement at any time after the notice
    and formalities required by the agreement have been given and may
    enter into similar agreements with one or more other bodies, in
    which case a reference to the corporation in section 4, 9 and 10
    shall be read as a reference to the other body or bodies; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} assign any of the functions referred to in this
    section to be carried out by employees of the Department.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} An agreement may be amended or replaced by subsequent
  agreements.

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must determine whether the conditions
  set out in paragraph (b) of the definition [[income-qualified
  civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in section 1 or under
  subparagraph 2(b)(i), (ii) or (vi) are met and inform the Corporation
  of the determination.

- [(6)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must advise the Corporation whether any
  deceased person was, at the time of death,

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} a person entitled to a disability award, entitled
    to pain and suffering compensation or entitled to a pension; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} a person referred to in paragraph (a) of the
    definition [[income-qualified civilian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} in
    section 1 or a person referred to in any of subparagraphs 2(b)(ii)
    to (iv).

- [(7)]{.lawlabel} The Minister must make available on request to the
  public

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the agreements in force and of any amendments to
    them; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the standards specified under section 7.

### [Review of Determination]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717158 .Subheading}

- **[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} A
  person who is dissatisfied with a final determination of the
  Corporation, or with a determination with respect to eligibility made
  under subparagraph 2(b)(i), (ii) or (vi), may, within 60 days after
  receiving that determination or, if circumstances beyond the control
  of the person necessitate a longer period, within the longer period,
  apply in writing to the Minister for a review of that determination.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If, after the review, the Minister concludes that the
  final determination made by the Corporation was in error, the Minister
  may take any steps to remedy the error that the Minister considers
  necessary in the circumstances.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} In lieu of the review referred to in subsection (1),
  if a person is dissatisfied with a determination with respect to
  eligibility referred to in subparagraphs 2(b)(i) or (ii), the person
  may within 60 days after receiving the determination or, if
  circumstances beyond the control of the person necessitate a longer
  period, within the longer period, apply to the Minister for a decision
  under paragraph 21(1)(b) or (2)(b) of the [Pension
  Act](/eng/acts/P-6), unless an application for that purpose has
  previously been made.

### [Other Activities of the Corporation]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717165 .Subheading}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** Nothing in these
Regulations precludes the Corporation from providing financial
assistance and a gravemarker similar to the financial assistance and
gravemarker referred to under Part 1 for any deceased person who is
ineligible under these Regulations

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the Corporation's own initiative and at its own
  expense; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on the request and at the expense of any department
  of the Government of Canada, the government of any province, any
  municipality, any foreign government or agency, or any other person,
  corporation or agency.

### [Gravemarker Maintenance]{.HTitleText2} {#h-717169 .Subheading}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** If a gravemarker or
financial assistance towards funeral, burial and cremation costs have
been provided under these Regulations or under predecessor enactments
dealing with the same subject-matter, all necessary maintenance of the
gravemarker must be provided by the Minister, including the replacement
or refurbishment of

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the gravemarker in any cemetery or block of graves
  owned by the Government of Canada and allocated to former members of
  the armed forces or the merchant navy or any persons who engaged in
  pursuits relating to war on behalf of Canada; or

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a gravemarker, of the standard referred to in
  subsection 6(1), in the cemetery where the original gravemarker or
  burial was provided.
