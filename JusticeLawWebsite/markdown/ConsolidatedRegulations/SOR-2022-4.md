## Critical Habitat of the Gattinger's Agalinis (Agalinis gattingeri) Order {#critical-habitat-of-the-gattingers-agalinis-agalinis-gattingeri-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2022-4
:::

[SPECIES AT RISK ACT](/eng/acts/S-15.3)

Registration 2022-01-18

Critical Habitat of the Gattinger's Agalinis (Agalinis gattingeri) Order
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas the Gattinger's Agalinis ([Agalinis gattingeri]{.otherLang
lang="la"}) is a wildlife species that is listed as an endangered
species in Part 2 of Schedule 1 to the [Species at Risk
Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732){#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732-ID0EBBGA};

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732-ID0EBBGA){#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732}S.C.
  2002, c. 29
  :::

Whereas the recovery strategy that identified the critical habitat of
that species has been included in the Species at Risk Public Registry;

Whereas no portion of the critical habitat of that species that is
specified in the annexed Order is in a place referred to in subsection
58(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27733){#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27733-ID0EBBEA}
of that Act;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27733-ID0EBBEA){#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27733}S.C.
  2015, c. 10, s. 60
  :::

And whereas the Minister of the Environment is of the opinion that the
annexed Order would affect land that is under the authority of the
Minister of Fisheries and Oceans and, pursuant to subsection 58(9) of
that Act, has consulted with that Minister with respect to the Order;

Therefore, the Minister of the Environment, pursuant to subsections
58(4) and (5) of the [Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732){#fn_81000-2-4301_hq_27732-ID0EDACA},
makes the annexed [Critical Habitat of the Gattinger's Agalinis
([Agalinis gattingeri]{.otherLang lang="la"})
Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-2022-4).

Gatineau, January 14, 2022

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Application

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Subsection 58(1) of the
[Species at Risk Act](/eng/acts/S-15.3) applies to the critical habitat
of the Gattinger's Agalinis ([Agalinis gattingeri]{.otherLang
lang="la"}), which is identified in the recovery strategy for that
species that is included in the Species at Risk Public Registry.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
