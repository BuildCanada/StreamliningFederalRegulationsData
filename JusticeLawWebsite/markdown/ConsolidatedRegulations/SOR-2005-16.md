## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716830 .Part}

- **[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following definitions apply in these Regulations.

  [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Sex Offender Information
      Registration Act](/eng/acts/S-8.7). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink
      lang="fr"})

  [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

  :   [[RCMP]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Royal Canadian Mounted
      Police. ([GRC]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} In these Regulations, [[police
  officer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} and [[police force]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}
  have the same meaning as in section 1 of the Police Act, chapter P-9.2
  of the Acts of New Brunswick, 1977, as amended from time to time.

## [Report and Notification]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716836 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** A sex offender whose main
residence is located in the Province of New Brunswick may make any
report required under paragraph 4.1(a) or (b) or section 4.3 of the Act,
or provide any notification required under section 6 of the Act, by
telephone.

## [Persons Authorized to Collect Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716838 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Members of the RCMP and
police officers are authorized in the Province of New Brunswick to
collect information for the purposes of the Act.

## [Persons Authorized to Register Information]{.HTitleText1} {#h-716840 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** The following persons are
authorized in the Province of New Brunswick to register information for
the purposes of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} a member of the RCMP;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} a police officer; and

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} a person employed at the New Brunswick Sex Offender
  Information Registration Centre, administered by RCMP "J" Division,
  whose duties include the registration of information.
