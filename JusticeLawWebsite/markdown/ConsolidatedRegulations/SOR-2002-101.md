## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679447 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Definition of Act

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,
[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Financial Consumer Agency of
Canada Act](/eng/acts/F-11.1).

## [Designated Violations]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679450 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Designation

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** The following are
designated as violations that may be proceeded with under sections 20 to
31 of the Act:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the contravention of any consumer provision; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the non-compliance with any compliance agreement
  entered into under an Act listed in Schedule 1 to the Act.

## [Coming into Force]{.HTitleText1} {#h-679455 .Part}

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations come into
force on the day on which they are registered.
