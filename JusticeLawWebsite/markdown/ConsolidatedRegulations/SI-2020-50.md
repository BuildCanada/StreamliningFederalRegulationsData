## Fees Paid for the Provision of Services in Relation to an Assessment from the Department of Employment and Social Development Remission Order {#fees-paid-for-the-provision-of-services-in-relation-to-an-assessment-from-the-department-of-employment-and-social-development-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

:::

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Registration 2020-07-22

Fees Paid for the Provision of Services in Relation to an Assessment
from the Department of Employment and Social Development Remission Order

P.C. 2020-517 2020-06-29
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Treasury
Board, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration and the Minister of
Employment and Social Development, pursuant to subsection
23(2.1)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27176){#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27176-ID0EGCAA}
of the [Financial Administration Act](/eng/acts/F-11)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27177){#fn_81000-2-4172_hq_27177-ID0EDCAA},
makes the annexed [Fees Paid for the Provision of Services in Relation
to an Assessment from the Department of Employment and Social
Development Remission Order](/eng/regulations/SI-2020-50).

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Remission

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Remission of a fee paid
under section 315.2 of the [Immigration and Refugee Protection
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-2002-227) for the provision of
services in relation to an assessment from the Department of Employment
and Social Development requested by an employer or group of employers,
in respect of an offer of employment, is granted to persons meeting the
following conditions:

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the fee was paid in respect of an assessment
  requested before April 4, 2020;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the employer or group of employers no longer requires
  the assessment or, if the assessment has already been made, it cannot
  be used;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the employer or group of employers has withdrawn the
  offer of employment due to the impact of the coronavirus disease 2019
  (COVID-19); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} in respect of the assessment, no work permit has been
  issued under section 200 or 201 of those Regulations and no
  application for such a work permit is being processed.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
