## Teslin Exploration Limited Order {#teslin-exploration-limited-order .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/74-431
:::

[APPROPRIATION ACTS](/eng/acts/Z-01)

APPROPRIATION ACT NO. 9, 1966

Registration 1974-07-22

Order Authorizing the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern
Development to Consent to the Transfer of Certain Territorial Coal
Licences and to Postpone the Repayment of Amounts Payable Under the
Northern Mineral Exploration Assistance Regulations

P.C. 1974-1561 1974-07-16
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
His Excellency the Administrator in Council, on the recommendation of
the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
Vote No. 7a of Appropriation Act No. 9, 1966, is pleased hereby to make
the annexed Order authorizing the Minister of Indian Affairs and
Northern Development to consent to the transfer of certain Territorial
Coal Licences and to postpone the repayment of amounts payable under the
Northern Mineral Exploration assistance regulations.
:::

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861130 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** This Order may be cited as
the [Teslin Exploration Limited Order](/eng/regulations/SOR-74-431).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861132 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In this Order,

[[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Minister]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Minister of Indian
    Affairs and Northern Development; ([Ministre]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Regulations]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Northern Mineral
    Exploration Assistance
    Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._332).
    ([règlement]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Consent to Transfer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861136 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding paragraph
11(c) of the Regulations, the Minister may consent to the transfer by
Teslin Exploration Limited of Territorial Coal Licences numbered 10, 11
and 12 to Vittorio Ghitti.

## [Postponement of Repayment]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861138 .Part}

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Notwithstanding paragraph
11(e) of the Regulations, and subject to section 5 of this Order, the
obligation to repay to Her Majesty in right of Canada the balance of the
grant paid to Teslin Exploration Limited pursuant to the Regulations
together with simple interest thereon at the rate of 10 per cent per
annum is postponed until the disposition of Territorial Coal Licences
numbered 10, 11, 12, 15, 16 and 17.

## [Undertaking]{.HTitleText1} {#h-861140 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** Teslin Exploration Limited
shall give a written undertaking to repay to Her Majesty in right of
Canada the balance of the grant owing under the Regulations together
with simple interest thereon at the rate of 10 per cent per annum out of
any of Territorial Coal Licences numbered 10, 11, 12, 15, 16 and 17.
