## Lockdown Regions Designation Order (COVID-19) {#lockdown-regions-designation-order-covid-19 .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/2021-275
:::

[CANADA WORKER LOCKDOWN BENEFIT ACT](/eng/acts/C-11.01)

Registration 2021-12-24

Lockdown Regions Designation Order (COVID-19)

P.C. 2021-1061 2021-12-23
::::

::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas "lockdown orders", as defined in section 2 of the [Canada Worker
Lockdown Benefit Act](/eng/acts/C-11.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235){#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235-ID0EBBCA},
have been made with respect to COVID-19 outbreaks in regions set out in
the annexed Order, imposing measures referred to in subparagraphs (a)(i)
and (ii) of that definition that apply to those regions for at least 14
consecutive days;

- ::: Footnote
  [[Return to footnote
  ]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235-ID0EBBCA){#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235}S.C.
  2021, c. 26, s. 5
  :::

And whereas the Minister of Employment and Social Development is of the
opinion that it is in the public interest that an order designating
those regions as lockdown regions be made for the benefit periods set
out in the annexed Order;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Employment and Social Development,
pursuant to section 3 of the [Canada Worker Lockdown Benefit
Act](/eng/acts/C-11.01)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235){#fn_81000-3-2291_hq_30235-ID0EDAAA},
makes the annexed Lockdown Regions Designation Order (COVID-19).
:::

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Lockdown regions and benefit periods

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** Each region of Canada set
out in column 1 of the schedule is designated as a lockdown region for
the benefit period set out in column 2.

[Marginal note:]{.wb-invisible}Coming into force

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** This Order comes into force
on the day on which it is registered.
