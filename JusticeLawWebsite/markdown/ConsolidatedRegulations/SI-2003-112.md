## Non-signatory Yukon First Nations Remission Order {#non-signatory-yukon-first-nations-remission-order .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2003-112

[FINANCIAL ADMINISTRATION ACT](/eng/acts/F-11)

Non-signatory Yukon First Nations Remission Order

P.C. 2003-764  2003-05-29
::::

::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, considering that it is
in the public interest to do so, on the recommendation of the Minister
of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 23(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_SI-2003-112_e_hq_5143){#fn_SI-2003-112_e_hq_5143-ID0EFBAA}

annexed Non-signatory Yukon First Nations Remission Order.

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-606787 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** The definitions in this
section apply in this Order.

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Excise Tax
    Act](/eng/acts/E-15). ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[band]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([bande]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[eligible claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible claimant]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a Yukon first nation
    named in the schedule and Indians who are members of that band.
    ([demandeur admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[eligible period]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the period beginning
    on February 15, 1998 and ending on March 31, 2002. ([période
    admissible]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Indian]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in subsection
    2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5). ([Indien]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[reserve]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} has the same meaning as in
    subsection 2(1) of the [Indian Act](/eng/acts/I-5).
    ([réserve]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[tax]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the goods and services tax imposed
    under Division II of Part IX of the Act. ([taxe]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Yukon first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Yukon first nation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a band named in the
    schedule. ([première nation du Yukon]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

## [Remission of the Goods and Services Tax]{.HTitleText1} {#h-606797 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** Subject to section 3,
remission is hereby granted to an eligible claimant of tax paid on a
supply of tangible personal property acquired outside a reserve during
the eligible period that was not delivered to a reserve within the
eligible period.

## [Conditions]{.HTitleText1} {#h-606799 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** Remission is granted on
condition that

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} the property was acquired by an eligible claimant
  outside a reserve for the personal use and consumption of the eligible
  claimant;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} the property was not delivered to a reserve by the
  supplier or the supplier's agent;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} the tax paid has not otherwise been rebated, credited
  or remitted under the Act or the [Financial Administration
  Act](/eng/acts/F-11); and

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} a claim for remission is submitted in writing to the
  Minister of National Revenue within two years after the day on which
  this Order is made, together with the following documentation, namely,

  - [(i)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the Certificate of Indian Status issued
    to the eligible claimant by the Department of Indian Affairs and
    Northern Development,

  - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} a copy of the invoice relating to the supply of
    the property stating the name and address of the supplier, the date
    of the supply, the name of the eligible claimant, the value of
    consideration paid for the supply of the property and a sufficiently
    detailed description of the property,

  - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} proof that the tax payable on the supply of the
    property has been paid, and

  - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} a statement from the supplier indicating that,
    upon request, the supplier or the supplier's agent would normally
    have delivered the property to a reserve but did not do so during
    the eligible period.
