## Inuk of Fort George Observer Regulations {#inuk-of-fort-george-observer-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[NASKAPI AND THE CREE-NASKAPI COMMISSION ACT](/eng/acts/C-45.7)

Registration 1986-08-14

Regulations Respecting the Inuk of Fort George Observer

P.C. 1986-1837 1986-08-13

the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to
subsection 36(2) of the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act[[Footnote

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898288 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Inuk of Fort George Observer
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-86-868).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898290 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Cree-Naskapi (of Quebec) Act;
    ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[landholding corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[landholding corporation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Kiggaluk
    Corporation incorporated pursuant to section 5 of An Act respecting
    the land regime in the James Bay and New Quebec territories (Quebec)
    and as designated by Quebec Order in Council 2210-82 dated September
    22, 1982; ([corporation foncière]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[observer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[observer]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the observer referred to in
    section 36 of the Act; ([observateur]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Secretary of the Council of the Kativik Regional Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Secretary of the Council of the Kativik Regional
    Government]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the person appointed as
    secretary pursuant to section 298 of An Act respecting Northern
    Villages and the Kativik Regional Government (Quebec). ([secrétaire
    du conseil de l'administration régionale Kativik]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Selection of Observer]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898296 .Part}

- **[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} there is a general election of the council of the
    Chisasibi Band and the council elected does not include an Inuk of
    Fort George,

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 69 of the Act, the office of a
    council member of the Chisasibi Band who is an Inuk of Fort George
    becomes vacant and, after his successor in office is elected, the
    council of the Chisasibi Band does not include an Inuk of Fort
    George, or

  - [(c)]{.lawlabel} pursuant to section 12 or 13, the position of
    observer becomes vacant,

  within 30 days after the election or the vacancy, the landholding
  corporation shall call a meeting in the manner set out in subsection
  (2).

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The landholding corporation shall call the meeting
  referred to in subsection (1) by posting in a public place in
  Chisasibi, at least three days prior to the date fixed for the
  meeting, a notice in English, Cree and Inuttituut setting out the
  purpose, date, time and place of the meeting, the place where
  nominations will be received by the landholding corporation and
  stating that nominations for the position of observer will be received
  by the landholding corporation up until 24 hours prior to the time set
  for the meeting.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} The landholding corporation shall receive nominations
  for the position of observer up until 24 hours prior to the time set
  for the meeting referred to in subsection (1).

**[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** Any Inuk of Fort George who
is eligible to nominate a candidate for observer may, in writing,
nominate an Inuk of Fort George who is eligible to be a candidate for
observer by submitting to the landholding corporation a nomination
signed by the Inuk nominated.

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** An Inuk of Fort George, if
18 years of age or over and not declared mentally incompetent under the
laws of the Province, is eligible at a meeting to select an observer, to
vote for, nominate a candidate for, or be a candidate for observer
unless he

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} has been convicted of contravening a regulation made
  under paragraph 67(1)(b) of the Act within the four year period
  preceding the date fixed for the meeting for the selection of the
  observer;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is the band secretary or band treasurer of the
  Chisasibi Band referred to in section 41 of the Act;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is a judge to which the [Judges Act](/eng/acts/J-1)
  applies or is a Crown prosecutor; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is, on the day fixed for the meeting of the selection
  of the observer, undergoing a term of imprisonment as a result of
  having been convicted of an indictable offence.

**[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** The landholding corporation
shall preside over the voting for the observer and the voting shall take
place with a show of hands.

**[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** The candidate for observer
who receives the largest number of votes at the meeting is selected
observer if at least 15 eligible voters attend the meeting.

**[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** Where two or more
candidates receive an equal number of votes and an additional vote cast
for one of the candidates would enable that candidate to be declared to
have obtained the largest number of votes, the landholding corporation
shall hold another vote, in accordance with section 6, in respect of
only those candidates.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/92-452, s. 1
:::

**[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** Where fewer than 15
eligible voters attend a meeting, the landholding corporation shall call
another meeting by posting a notice in accordance with section 3.

## [Announcement of Results]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898317 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** The landholding
corporation shall, within seven days after the selection of the
observer,

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} post the name of the person selected as observer in a
  public place in Chisasibi; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} communicate, in writing, the name of the observer to
  the band secretary of the Chisasibi Band, to the Secretary of the
  Council of the Kativik Regional Government and to the Minister.

## [Term of Office]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898321 .Part}

**[[11]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-11 .sectionLabel}** The term of office of the
observer expires

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} on the day of a general election of the Chisasibi
  Band;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} on any day that an Inuk of Fort George is elected a
  council member of the Chisasibi Band;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} on the day that the position of the observer becomes
  vacant pursuant to section 12; or

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} on the day that, pursuant to section 13, the
  landholding corporation declares the position vacant.

## [Vacancy]{.HTitleText1} {#h-898327 .Part}

**[[12]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-12 .sectionLabel}** The position of observer
becomes vacant if the observer

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} dies or submits his resignation in writing to the
  landholding corporation;

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} is convicted of contravening a regulation made under
  paragraph 67(1)(b) of the Act in respect of an election where no Inuk
  of Fort George was elected;

- [(c)]{.lawlabel} is appointed band secretary or band treasurer of the
  Chisasibi Band pursuant to section 41 of the Act;

- [(d)]{.lawlabel} is appointed a judge to which the [Judges
  Act](/eng/acts/J-1) applies or a Crown prosecutor;

- [(e)]{.lawlabel} commences a term of imprisonment as a result of
  having been convicted of an indictable offence; or

- [(f)]{.lawlabel} is declared mentally incompetent in accordance with
  the laws of the Province.

**[[13]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-13 .sectionLabel}** Where the observer has,
without permission of the landholding corporation, been absent from
three or more consecutive council meetings otherwise than by reason of
illness or incapacity, the landholding corporation may by resolution
declare the office of observer vacant.
