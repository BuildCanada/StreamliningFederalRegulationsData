## Canada Oil and Gas Certificate of Fitness Regulations {#canada-oil-and-gas-certificate-of-fitness-regulations .Title-of-Act}

[SOR]{.abbr title="Statutory Orders and Regulations"}/96-114
:::

[CANADA OIL AND GAS OPERATIONS ACT](/eng/acts/O-7)

Registration 1996-02-13

Regulations Respecting the Issuance of Certificates of Fitness for
Offshore Oil and Gas Production, Drilling, Accommodation and Diving
Installations

P.C. 1996-163  1996-02-13
::::

::::::::::::::::::::::::: section
::: {.section .order}
Whereas, pursuant to subsection 15(1) of the [Canada Oil and Gas
Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EFADA}, a copy of
proposed [Regulations respecting the issuance of certificates of fitness
for offshore oil and gas production, drilling, accommodation and diving
installations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-114), substantially in the form
annexed hereto, was published in the [Canada
Gazette](http://www.gazette.gc.ca/) Part I on May 14, 1994 and
interested persons were afforded an opportunity to make representations
with respect thereto;

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the
recommendation of the Minister of Natural Resources and the Minister of
Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to section
14[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*\*^](#footnote2_e){#footnote2_e-ID0EGACA} of the
[Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act](/eng/acts/O-7)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^\*^](#footnote1_e){#footnote1_e-ID0EDACA}, is pleased
hereby to make the annexed [Regulations respecting the issuance of
certificates of fitness for offshore oil and gas production, drilling,
accommodation and diving installations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-114).

## [Short Title]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977262 .Part}

**[[1]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-1 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations may be
cited as the [Canada Oil and Gas Certificate of Fitness
Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-114).

## [Interpretation]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977264 .Part}

**[[2]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-2 .sectionLabel}** In these Regulations,

[[accommodation installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[accommodation installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an
    installation that is used to accommodate persons at an offshore
    production site or offshore drill site and that functions
    independently of an offshore production installation, offshore
    drilling installation or diving installation, and includes any
    associated dependent diving system; ([installation
    d'habitation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Act]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the [Canada Oil and Gas Operations
    Act](/eng/acts/O-7); ([Loi]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certificate of fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certificate of fitness]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a certificate
    issued by a certifying authority in accordance with section 4;
    ([certificat de conformité]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[certifying authority]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means, for the purposes
    of section 5.12 of the Act, the American Bureau of Shipping, Bureau
    Veritas, Det norskeVeritas Classification A/S, Germanischer Lloyd or
    Lloyd's Register North America, Inc. ([autorité]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[Chief]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the Chief Safety Officer;
    ([(délégué)]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[dependent diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependent diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving
    system that is associated with an offshore installation other than a
    diving installation and that does not function independently of the
    installation; ([système de plongée non autonome]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[dependent personnel accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[dependent personnel accommodation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means
    personnel accommodation that is associated with an offshore
    installation other than an accommodation installation and that does
    not function independently of the offshore installation; ([logement
    du personnel connexe]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[diving installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diving installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving system
    and any associated vessel that functions independently of an
    accommodation installation, offshore production installation or
    offshore drilling installation; ([installation de
    plongée]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[diving system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plant or equipment
    used in or in connection with a diving operation, and includes the
    plant and equipment that are essential to a diver or to a pilot of a
    manned submersible; ([système de plongée]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[drilling base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drilling base]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the stable foundation on
    which a drilling rig is installed, and includes the seafloor, an
    artificial island, an ice platform, a platform fixed to the ground
    or seafloor and any other foundation specially constructed for
    drilling operations; ([base de forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drilling unit
    or a drilling rig and its associated drilling base, and includes any
    associated dependent diving system; ([installation de
    forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drilling rig]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drilling rig]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plant used to make a
    well by boring or other means, and includes a derrick, draw-works,
    rotary table, mud pump, blowout preventer, accumulator, choke
    manifold and other associated equipment, including power, control
    and monitoring systems; ([appareil de forage]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[drilling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drilling unit]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drillship,
    submersible, semi-submersible, barge, jack-up or other vessel that
    is used in a drilling program and is fitted with a drilling rig, and
    includes the drilling rig and other facilities related to the
    drilling program that are installed on a vessel; ([unité de
    forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a location where a drilling
    rig is or is proposed to be installed; ([emplacement de
    forage]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a diving installation, a
    drilling installation, a production installation or an accommodation
    installation; ([installation]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[mobile installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[mobile installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offshore
    installation that is designed to operate in a floating or buoyant
    mode or that can be moved from place to place without major
    dismantling or modification, whether or not it has its own motive
    power; ([installation mobile]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[new installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[new installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offshore
    installation that is constructed after the coming into force of
    these Regulations; ([nouvelle installation]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[offshore drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore drilling installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    drilling installation at an offshore drill site; ([installation de
    forage au large des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[offshore drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore drill site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a drill site that
    is in a region that is covered by water and is not an island,
    artificial island or ice platform; ([emplacement de forage au large
    des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[offshore installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means an offshore
    drilling installation, an offshore production installation, an
    accommodation installation or a diving installation; ([installation
    au large des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[offshore production installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore production installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a
    production installation at an offshore production site;
    ([installation de production au large des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[offshore production site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[offshore production site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a production
    site in a region that is covered by water and is not an island,
    artificial island or ice platform; ([emplacement de production au
    large des côtes]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[operator]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a person who has applied for
    or has been granted a Production Operations Authorization, a
    Drilling Program Authorization or a Diving Program Authorization
    pursuant to paragraph 5(1)(b) of the Act;
    ([exploitant]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production facility]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment for the
    production of oil or gas located at an offshore production site,
    including separation, treatment and processing facilities, equipment
    and facilities used in support of production operations, landing
    areas, heliports, storage areas or tanks and dependent personnel
    accommodations, but not including any associated platform,
    artificial island, subsea production system, drilling equipment or
    diving system; ([matériel de production]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[production installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production installation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a production
    facility and any associated platform, artificial island, subsea
    production system, offshore loading system, drilling equipment,
    facilities related to marine activities and dependent diving system;
    ([installation de production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production operation]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means any operation
    that is related to the production of oil or gas from a pool or
    field; ([travaux de production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[production site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[production site]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means a location where a
    production installation is or is proposed to be installed;
    ([emplacement de production]{.DefinedTermLink lang="fr"})

[[scope of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[scope of work]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means the plan of activities
    carried out by a certifying authority and submitted to the Chief for
    approval pursuant to section 6, for the purposes of issuing a
    certificate of fitness; ([plan de travail]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

[[subsea production system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm}

:   [[subsea production system]{.dfn}]{.DefinedTerm} means equipment and
    structures that are located on or below or buried in the seafloor
    for the production of oil or gas from, or for the injection of
    fluids into, a field under an offshore production site, and includes
    production risers, flow lines and associated production control
    systems. ([système de production sous-marin]{.DefinedTermLink
    lang="fr"})

## [Application]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977297 .Part}

**[[3]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-3 .sectionLabel}** These Regulations apply in
respect of those submarine areas not within a province, adjacent to the
coast of Canada and extending throughout the natural prolongation of the
land territory of Canada to the outer edge of the continental margin or
to a distance of two hundred nautical miles from the baselines from
which the breadth of the territorial sea of Canada is measured,
whichever is the greater.

## [Issuance of Certificates of Fitness]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977299 .Part}

- **[[4]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-4 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  following installations are prescribed for the purposes of
  section 5.12 of the Act:

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} each production installation, accommodation
    installation and diving installation at an offshore production site;
    and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} each drilling installation, diving installation and
    accommodation installation at an offshore drill site.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} Subject to subsections (3) and (5) and section 5, a
  certifying authority may issue a certificate of fitness in respect of
  the installations referred to in subsection (1), if the certifying
  authority

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} determines that, in relation to the production or
    drill site or region in which the particular installation is to be
    operated, the installation

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} is designed, constructed, transported and
      installed or established in accordance with

      - [(A)]{.lawlabel} Parts I to III of the [Canada Oil and Gas
        Installations Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118),

      - [(B)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of the [Oil and Gas Occupational
        Safety and Health Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-87-612)
        listed in Part 1 of the schedule to these Regulations, and

      - [(C)]{.lawlabel} the provisions of the [Canada Oil and Gas
        Diving Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-88-600) listed in
        Part 2 of the schedule to these Regulations, if the installation
        includes a dependent diving system,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} is fit for the purpose for which it is to be
      used and can be operated safely without polluting the environment,
      and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} will continue to meet the requirements of
      subparagraphs (i) and (ii) for the period of validity that is
      endorsed on the certificate of fitness if the installation is
      maintained in accordance with the inspection, maintenance and
      weight control programs submitted to and approved by the
      certifying authority under subsection (5); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} carries out the scope of work in respect of which
    the certificate of fitness is issued.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} For the purposes of subparagraph (2)(a)(i), the
  certifying authority may substitute, for any equipment, methods,
  measure or standard required by any Regulations referred to in that
  subparagraph, equipment, methods, measures or standards the use of
  which is authorized by the Chief or Chief Conservation Officer, as
  applicable under section 16 of the Act.

- [(4)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority shall endorse on any
  certificate of fitness it issues details of every limitation on the
  operation of the installation that is necessary to ensure that the
  installation meets the requirements of paragraph (2)(a).

- [(5)]{.lawlabel} The certifying authority shall not issue a
  certificate of fitness unless, for the purpose of enabling the
  certifying authority to determine whether the installation meets the
  requirements of paragraph (2)(a) and to carry out the scope of work
  referred to in paragraph (2)(b),

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the person applying for the certificate

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} provides the certifying authority with all the
      information required by the certifying authority,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} carries out or assists the certifying authority
      to carry out every inspection, test or survey required by the
      certifying authority, and

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} submits to the certifying authority an
      inspection and monitoring program, a maintenance program and a
      weight control program for approval; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} if the programs are adequate to ensure and maintain
    the integrity of the installation, the certifying authority approves
    the programs referred to in subparagraph (a)(iii).

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-315, s. 92

## [Conflict of Interest]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977323 .Part}

**[[5]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-5 .sectionLabel}** The certifying authority
shall not issue a certificate of fitness in respect of an installation
if the certifying authority has been involved, other than as a
certifying authority or a classification body, in the design,
construction or installation of the installation.

## [Approval of Scope of Work]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977327 .Part}

- **[[6]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-6 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certifying authority shall, for the purposes of issuing a certificate
  of fitness in respect of an offshore installation, submit a scope of
  work to the Chief for approval.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} The Chief shall approve a scope of work where the
  Chief determines that the scope of work

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} is sufficiently detailed to permit the certifying
    authority to determine whether the installation meets the
    requirements of paragraph 4(2)(a); and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} provides for the means for determining whether

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} the environmental criteria for the region or site
      and the loads assumed for the installation are correct,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an offshore production
      installation, the concept safety analysis required by section 44
      of the [Canada Oil and Gas Installations
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118) meets the requirements
      of that section,

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of a new installation, the
      installation has been constructed in accordance with a quality
      assurance program referred to in section 4 of the [Canada Oil and
      Gas Installations Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118),

    - [(iv)]{.lawlabel} the operations manual meets the requirements of
      section 64 of the [Canada Oil and Gas Installations
      Regulations](/eng/regulations/SOR-96-118),

    - [(v)]{.lawlabel} the construction and installation of the
      installation has been carried out in accordance with the design
      specifications,

    - [(vi)]{.lawlabel} the materials used in the construction and
      installation of the installation meet the design specifications,

    - [(vii)]{.lawlabel} the structures, facilities, equipment and
      systems critical to safety, and to the protection of the natural
      environment, are in place and functioning appropriately, and

    - [(viii)]{.lawlabel} in respect of an offshore drilling
      installation or an offshore production installation, the
      structures, facilities, equipment and systems to meet the
      requirements of the provisions of the [Canada Oil and Gas Drilling
      and Production Regulations](/eng/regulations/C.R.C.,_c._1517)
      listed in Part 3 of the schedule to these Regulations are in place
      and functioning appropriately.

## [Expiration Date]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977343 .Part}

- **[[7]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-7 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} If the
  certifying authority determines that, when the installation is
  maintained in accordance with the programs submitted to it under
  subparagraph 4(5)(a)(iii), the installation will meet the requirements
  of paragraph 4(2)(a) for a period of at least five years, the
  certifying authority shall endorse on the certificate of fitness an
  expiration date that is five years after the date of issuance.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} If the period of time referred to in subsection (1)
  is less than five years, the certifying authority shall endorse on the
  certificate of fitness an expiration date that is the number of years
  or months in that lesser period after the date of issuance.

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of fitness shall expire on the
  expiration date that is endorsed on it.

## [Area of Validity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977350 .Part}

- **[[8]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-8 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} The
  certifying authority shall endorse on the certificate of fitness a
  description of the site or region in which the installation is to be
  operated.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} A certificate of fitness is valid for the operation
  of the installation at the site or in the region that is endorsed on
  it.

## [Invalidity]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977356 .Part}

- **[[9]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-9 .sectionLabel}** [(1)]{.lawlabel} Subject
  to subsections (2) and (3), a certificate of fitness ceases to be
  valid where

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} the certifying authority or the Chief determines

    - [(i)]{.lawlabel} that any of the information submitted under
      subsection 4(5) was incorrect and that the certificate of fitness
      would not have been issued if that information had been correct,

    - [(ii)]{.lawlabel} that the installation no longer meets the
      requirements of paragraph 4(2)(a), or

    - [(iii)]{.lawlabel} that the installation has not been inspected,
      monitored and maintained in accordance with any limitations
      endorsed on the certificate of fitness; or

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} the Chief determines that the certifying authority
    has failed to carry out the scope of work relating to the
    installation in respect of which the certificate of fitness was
    issued.

- [(2)]{.lawlabel} At least 30 days before a determination is made
  pursuant to subsection (1), notice, in writing, that a determination
  is going to be made shall be given

  - [(a)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a determination by the certifying
    authority, by the certifying authority to the Chief and the person
    to whom the certificate of fitness in respect of which the
    determination is to be made has been issued; and

  - [(b)]{.lawlabel} in the case of a determination by the Chief, by the
    Chief to the certifying authority and the person referred to in
    paragraph (a).

- [(3)]{.lawlabel} Before making a determination pursuant to
  subsection (1), the certifying authority or the Chief, as the case may
  be, shall consider any information in relation to that determination
  that is provided by any person notified pursuant to subsection (2).

## [Change of Certifying Authority]{.HTitleText1} {#h-977370 .Part}

**[[10]{.sectionLabel}]{#s-10 .sectionLabel}** Where a person to whom a
certificate of fitness has been issued intends to change the certifying
authority in respect of an installation, the person shall

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} where possible, notify the Chief at least 90 days
  before the change is made; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} where it is not possible to notify the Chief in
  accordance with paragraph (a), notify the Chief as soon as the person
  changes the certifying authority.

::: HistoricalNote
- SOR/2009-315, s. 96(F)
