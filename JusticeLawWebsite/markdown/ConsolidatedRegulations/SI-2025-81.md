## Order Transferring from the Minister for International Trade to the Minister of State (U.S. Trade) and the Minister of Foreign Affairs the Powers, Duties and Functions Under Subsection 53(2) of the Customs Tariff {#order-transferring-from-the-minister-for-international-trade-to-the-minister-of-state-u.s.-trade-and-the-minister-of-foreign-affairs-the-powers-duties-and-functions-under-subsection-532-of-the-customs-tariff .Title-of-Act}

[SI]{.abbr title="Statutory Instruments"}/2025-81
:::

[PUBLIC SERVICE REARRANGEMENT AND TRANSFER OF DUTIES
ACT](/eng/acts/P-34)

Registration 2025-06-04

Order Transferring from the Minister for International Trade to the
Minister of State (U.S. Trade) and the Minister of Foreign Affairs the
Powers, Duties and Functions Under Subsection 53(2) of the Customs
Tariff

P.C. 2025-492 2025-05-13
::::

:::: section
::: {.section .order}
Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of
the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^a^](#fn_612645E_hq_7867){#fn_612645E_hq_7867-ID0EJGAA}
of the [Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties
Act](/eng/acts/P-34)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^b^](#fn_612645E_hq_7868){#fn_612645E_hq_7868-ID0EGGAA},
transfers from the Minister for International Trade the powers, duties
and functions of the Minister of International Trade under subsection
53‍(2)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^c^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831656){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831656-ID0EEGAA}
of the [Customs Tariff](/eng/acts/C-54.011)[[Footnote
]{.wb-invisible}^d^](#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831634){#fn_PSRTDA-pdfprecedent_hq_1746831634-ID0EBGAA},

- [(a)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of State (U.S. Trade), specifically
  in relation to Canada-U.S. trade; and

- [(b)]{.lawlabel} to the Minister of Foreign Affairs, in relation to
  all other trade.
